# ============================================================================
# CHAOS REDUX - EVENT 040 LAWRENCE OF ARABIA
# ============================================================================
# Britain recalls a man believed dead and sends him into one Arabian
# government at a time. The target decides the terms, both sides play out the
# contest through decisions and missions, and each target ends in one
# settlement. Event 40 is Minor Fire-Once: later targets are internal stages.
#
#   .1  hidden entry on ENG                .2  opening report (target)
#   .3  recall report (ENG)                .4  terms of access (target)
#   .5  weekly pulse (hidden, target)      .6  next target job (hidden, ENG)
#   .7  Embedded report (target)           .8  Ascendant report (target)
#   .9  protectorate terms (target)        .10 independent treaty (ENG)
#   .11 protectorate notice (ENG)          .12 shipment on its way (target)
#   .13 shipment landed (target)           .15 bases for aid (target)
#   .16 exchange offer (target)            .17 .18 .19 contact reports
#   .20 .21 recovery success               .22 .23 recovery failure
#   .24 .25 detention reports              .26 local agents decision (ENG)
#   .27 watchdog (hidden, ENG)             .28 campaign closed (ENG)
#   .30 Lawrence's choice (target)         .40 news (all news kinds)
#   .45 retaliation choice (ENG)           .50 .51 neighbour reactions
#   .60 .61 .62 incidents                  .71 to .78 settlement reports
# ============================================================================
