# Blender authoring, processing, and PDX export

Read the relevant complete sections before skeletal authoring, Blender processing, material or geometry repair, action review, export, or reimport. Read the linked rebuild workflow only for explicitly requested repair or rebuild work.

## Skeletal authoring gate

Every skeleton, skin weight set, and skeletal action that the Meshy provider route does not supply is authored live in Blender by the model agent through the Blender MCP bridge, under `Skeleton, skinning, and action policy`. Work iteratively on the approved working checkpoint, review the real deformation and the real motion visually, and save the accepted result as a job checkpoint before the export and reimport proof steps.

Use `chaosx_3d_model_pipeline` for this authoring work. The session may create or repair anatomy-appropriate bones, hierarchy, bind pose, skin weights, controls, constraints, and articulated keyed actions, then bake, inspect deformation and contacts, synchronize sound, export, and reimport. Preserve immutable provider geometry; work on checkpoints. Verify live schemas, bridge health, and the supported adapter operations before authoring. Record the exact authoring session, the target rig and action names, the affected bones and channels, and every phase or bone change. Keep inspection and render routes read-only and retain the allowlisted adapter boundary.

Require genuine role motion and role-appropriate multi-frame evidence. Attack or fire actions must visibly aim, discharge, recoil, and recover where the role requires it. Death actions must visibly articulate collapse, impact, and settling. Validate meaningful start, middle, and end phases plus any loop phases required by the role, and reject an action that only poses, rotates, translates, or aliases another role.

If the live session cannot produce a required role, report the exact capability blocker. Never alias, omit, or simplify a role, and never substitute a static pose, a whole-rig transform, or a companion preview for requested skeletal motion. Live rigging and animation are held to `No shortcuts in model work` without exception.

### Live Blender authoring session

Bones, skin weights, and keyframes are created and edited only inside the live bridge session, on the approved working checkpoint. After appending a pose source, resolve its rig from the selected body's exact Armature modifier and active action slot rather than the first enumerated armature. Verify parent/rest and posed world-joint position/orientation parity before transferring channels, applying a measured location-unit conversion exactly once; extra exported UV/normal/material vertex records alone do not prove different source lineage. A provider-eligible bipedal body receives its skeleton and skinning from the Meshy rig task, and every asset the provider route does not cover is rigged and skinned there. The no-shortcuts rule governs that session: build the complete bone set the anatomy and the actions require, author the influence of every deforming vertex, and key the real motion, reviewing each result on the deformed mesh.

For every asset on the live authoring route, the session owns the complete articulated build: bone set and hierarchy, bind pose, explicit skin weights, controls and constraints, rigid component parenting, firearm fitting and attachment, weapon and prop controls, locators, and every keyed action. A measured muzzle, attachment, or effect locator is authored as one job-owned bone-parented Empty through `author_locator`, which never creates geometry or motion.

Author skin weights deliberately and verify them by deformation review in representative poses. An unreviewed bulk assignment is not accepted skinning, and every deforming vertex must carry real authored influence before export.

Mechanical assets preserve rigid components and deliberate pivots so turrets, barrels, recoil, propellers, rotors, doors, wheels, and tracks do not bend from blended weights.

Nonhumanoid anatomy — winged, digitigrade, quadrupedal, multi-limbed, or otherwise nonhumanoid — requires the dedicated custom-rig route, its written rig map, an anatomy-appropriate skeleton, and a recorded route status. Never route such an asset through a humanoid armature, and never accept a humanoid armature merely because a tool returned one.

For firearm-bearing units, ingest the Meshy rig and its receipt-verified provider actions for the body, bring in the separately generated firearm geometry, fit and attach it, and establish trigger-hand, support-hand, shoulder/stock, muzzle, recoil, and ground contacts in the same session. Keep the firearm rigid and separately controlled, and add the weapon and prop bones or rigid parenting and constraints the assembly needs.

Save the accepted session result as a job checkpoint, and checkpoint each accepted action in the PDX export coordinate system through `prepare_export_coordinate_checkpoint` before export.

### Action acceptance

For every animated runtime consumer, require substantive skeletal animation for every requested role. Missing, unverified, static, transform-only, whole-rig-only, or semantically aliased actions cannot pass. An action ingested from a provider result is accepted only when its provenance receipt verifies and its motion passes the same role, contact, and ground-contact review as an action authored live.

Inspect every authored action for per-frame trigger-hand, secondary-hand or foregrip, and shoulder/stock contact, weapon retention, muzzle-axis continuity, genuine role motion, and substantive articulated death where required. Firearm actions must visibly aim, discharge, recoil, and recover, and energy-weapon actions must react appropriately to their design. Reject clips that unintentionally swing, sling, detach, drop, or misorient the weapon.

Repair rejected roles in the live Blender session while preserving accepted geometry and passing actions.

Hold loop coverage, root policy, ground contact, export, and reimport evidence to `Action evidence and ground contacts`, and record the authoring or ingestion session, the accepted checkpoint, the authored or ingested bones and channels, and the role phases for every action.

## Blender processing and checkpoints

Use a versioned scene template with protected provider-source, working, rig, action, export, reference, and evidence collections. Unattended work must use the verified repository-owned allowlisted adapter. Do not expose unrestricted Blender Python, shell commands, arbitrary URLs, or paths outside the job and approved reference roots. A development-only Blender route is allowed only in the isolated profile and only after the parent verifies its installation and actual callable interface.

`prepare_candidate` prepares provider geometry inside the job root from `source_rel`, `asset_kind`, `target_height_m`, `runtime_stem`, `runtime_entity_scale`, `target_triangles`, `excluded_provider_objects`, `gfx_references`, `texture_source_rels`, `preserve_geometry_topology`, `repair_before_reduction`, `topology_weld_distance`, `max_runtime_footprint_m`, `runtime_footprint_policy`, and `output_namespace_rel`. Every rig-binding payload key is rejected outright, because preparation keeps provider geometry, applies the calibrated height and entity scale exactly once, and never creates a skeleton, binds geometry to a rig, or produces skin weights. A Meshy-rigged body arrives with its skeleton and skinning inside the downloaded provider model, so preparation imports that provider result as authored data and sanitizes provider scale channels on humanoid assets. Call `save_checkpoint` for each stable stage the adapter does not already checkpoint.

Required stages:

1. import the approved GLB or FBX and preserve provider objects
2. duplicate into the working collection
3. import named vanilla references read-only. Humanoid land units must include the installed vanilla infantry reference and its entity scale, and building jobs must include the installed mesh and exact map-building entity scale
4. inspect scene and geometry
5. normalize orientation, scale, origin, and ground/water contact
6. measure and enforce the profile runtime footprint budget for static map buildings
7. perform only bounded local repairs
8. triangulate before final rig and export QA
9. convert PBR inputs to the locally verified PDX material convention
10. process textures and record DDS paths
11. obtain the skeleton, bind pose, and skin weights from the Meshy rig route for a provider-eligible bipedal body, or author them in the live bridge session for every asset the provider route does not cover, and review the deformation
12. ingest the receipt-verified provider actions onto the rig, author or repair the remaining roles in the live bridge session, then bake and checkpoint the required actions, preserving accepted motion
13. save checkpoints, export, and build evidence

Save stable checkpoint stages in `blender/checkpoints/` for source import, normalization, component modeling/repair, materials, rig/weights, props/attachments, actions, and pre-export. Preserve immutable source checkpoints, versions, and stage transitions. Complete missing required anatomy, equipment, weapons, tools, and other model components under the standing authorization in `Route selection and component completion`. Record source-informed design and added geometry separately, preserve the intended identity, and report an exact capability blocker if required modeling operations are unavailable.

For a humanoid recovery with detached or loose components, use the verified repository-owned `chaosx_blender_hoi4_review_humanoid_components` operation with `render_group = true` before selecting any component for reuse. Require a grouped visual review render with explicit component ids and named views against the dim whole-body reference. Bounds-only guessing is invalid. The review reads the source checkpoint without saving or mutating it and writes only job-root-bounded previews and a review report. Do not isolate a component until the parent has reviewed that evidence.

### Rebuilding existing units with the review tools

Follow `references/unit_rebuild_workflow.md` for every rebuild or new provider-eligible biped. It is the ordered route: free triage of the shipped model, identity sources (portraits plus renders of the current model), portrait-grounded reference, provider generation, intake, action review and grounding, export, and runtime wiring.

Use the repository tools instead of ad hoc scripts:

- `.tools/3d_pipeline/review/review_cli.py` — read-only background-Blender triage, reference renders, action review with floor contact and phase measurement, and comparison sheets.
- `.tools/3d_pipeline/review/provider_intake.py` — downloads, calibrates, writes verified provenance, and chains provider action imports from a manifest.
- `.tools/3d_pipeline/review/lab_actions.py` — live Lab helpers for grounding, slot repair, NLA cleanup, and authored bone-direction keys.
- `.tools/3d_pipeline/review/pdx_textures.py` — profile-driven PDX material preparation from provider PBR maps; use `.tools/3d_pipeline/textures/cli.py` for final compressed model-texture delivery under the contract below.
- `.tools/3d_pipeline/review/export_package.py` — mesh and action export with a reimport proof per role and optional hash-checked runtime install.

A reference that looks generic beside the unit's portrait is rejected before any geometry credit is spent. Provider actions are ingested, grounded, reviewed from at least two views, and re-authored in Blender when a role reads as the wrong character.

## Geometry, materials, rigging, and actions

Record triangles, vertices, objects, material slots, loose components, non-manifold and boundary edges, holes, degenerates, normals, UV layers and relevant overlap, transforms, negative scale, bounds, origin, ground/water contact, reference comparison, and profile semantic checks. Repair or reject holes, loose components, non-manifold edges, and degenerate geometry before acceptance. Any intentional open surface must be named by the profile and marked for review. Final topology is triangular unless a verified local engine path says otherwise.

Retain provider source maps unchanged. Convert through the exact local PDX material precedent and record shader, channel mapping, color space, alpha behavior, texture dimensions, DDS paths, and unsupported maps. For the installed `PdxMeshAdvanced` and `PdxMeshAdvancedSkinned` routes, `gfx/FX/pdxmesh.shader` enables `PDX_IMPROVED_BLINN_PHONG`: green supplies specular strength, blue metalness, and alpha glossiness; keep red at the locally verified unused/mask value. Convert provider roughness to glossiness with `A = 255 - roughness` for 8-bit channels (`1 - roughness` when normalized), and verify the inversion against the source roughness after resizing and DDS round-trip. Never use a raw grayscale roughness map as the PDX specular map. Final model textures follow the shared compressed delivery contract below; the uncompressed 2D asset converter is not the model delivery route. Preserve large source atlases as evidence and record the runtime resize. If the provider diffuse is too dark, derive a documented deterministic grade from the immutable provider base and rebuild the runtime derivative from that base on every run. Never compound a grade from an older processed or runtime texture. Do not pass materials QA with missing, black, magenta, invisible, accidentally transparent, or implausibly reflective surfaces.

Resize packed RGBA material-data textures by splitting R, G, B, and A into independent scalar channels, resampling each channel, then merging them without color conversion or alpha premultiplication. Alpha stores glossiness in these packed specular maps and normal data in packed normal maps, not opacity; ordinary RGBA color-image resizing can corrupt RGB as alpha varies. Verify channel ranges and required constant channels after resizing and the DDS round-trip before accepting the material. The current `io_pdx_mesh` importer displays packed specular alpha as Blender Roughness, which differs from the engine shader's glossiness interpretation. Keep the engine-packed artifact correct and treat any corrected Blender preview as a separate interpretation; a plausible importer preview does not validate runtime channel packing. For actual-byte reimport visual QA, preserve the imported mesh and action but rebind the verified PDX decoding node graph to the same final DDS maps, with Blender roughness computed as `1 - specular.A` and the locally verified packed normal decoding; archive both the raw importer preview and the corrected material review when their appearance differs. If a pale patch appears only on a posed jacket or other layered surface, map its rendered pixels back to exported triangle ids, rest coordinates, UVs, and diffuse/specular texels before deciding whether it is texture projection, shader interpretation, or a hole. A UV triangle thinner than one texel can sample a bad gutter even when a pixel-center raster mask finds no texels; include the triangle's bounded UV footprint and the verified atlas filtering gutter in a targeted texture repair, then rerender the affected phase and view from the exported files.

Use one profile's calibrated axes, scale, triangle range, material/texture limits, rig route, action roles, root policy, instance density, semantic checks, export preset, and runtime pattern. A profile must be calibrated before paid or export work begins.

For map buildings, the profile's footprint budget is a hard runtime gate. The default policy is `reject`. `fit_to_budget` is allowed only when the job explicitly requests it and applies a uniform X/Y fit after height normalization. The adapter report must retain `runtime_dimensions_before_fit_m`, `runtime_dimensions_after_fit_m`, `fit_factor_xy`, `runtime_footprint_before_m`, and `runtime_footprint_after_m`.

The reusable pilot runner treats `building` and `static_building` as static routes, prepares their `Image_0`/`Image_1`/`Image_2` texture sources, and never sends them through humanoid rig or action work. A building job without a named installed vanilla reference is rejected before Blender preparation.

For static map-building materials, use the shader from the installed vanilla building consumer. With the current vanilla surface this is `PdxMeshAdvancedSnow`. Verify packed PDX normal and specular channel statistics before synchronizing runtime DDS files.

Take the skeleton from the route fixed in `Route selection and component completion`, and hold the rig-map, mechanical-pivot, deformation-review, and humanoid-armature rejection requirements to `Live Blender authoring session`.

For every firearm-bearing unit, the route and the input rules are the ones in `Route selection and component completion`, `Equipped items are generated separately and animated as an assembly`, and `Exactly one reference image per Meshy geometry task`; download and preserve the body and each item task independently, and keep repository code from generating a skeleton, skin weights, or keyframes. When firearm geometry generation is part of the requested work, guns enter the assembly as separate Meshy-generated geometry; a repair request does not by itself authorize or require replacing the body. Add weapon/prop bones or rigid parenting and constraints, and establish trigger-hand, support-hand, shoulder/stock, muzzle, recoil, and ground contacts. Final animation must preserve all required components, and a declared `non_firing` unit still gets its equipment but no firing effects.

Action inspection, rejection, and repair follow `Action acceptance` without exception. Recording fields for authored and ingested actions are listed under `Action evidence and ground contacts` and `Evidence package and handoff`.

For every runtime state that visibly fires or discharges, the model handoff must independently record the action and exact discharge frame/time, verified muzzle or weapon locator/node, weapon-appropriate muzzle flash, smoke, cartridge, impact particle, beam, or other discharge effect as applicable, light where applicable, legally usable weapon-identity-matched sourced `soundeffect`, audio provenance/license, runtime entity consumer, synchronization evidence, and status. Cover `attack`, `defend`, `support_attack`, and every other firing state separately. A firing state without synchronized particles or sound is incomplete. A firearm-bearing unit explicitly declared `non_firing` must have no firing state, discharge effect, muzzle locator requirement, or firing sound, and the manifest must record that zero-state/effect/sound audit. The worker supplies this crosswalk. The parent owns final entity, particle, sound-definition, and runtime wiring.

Nonhumanoid anatomy follows the dedicated custom-rig route and the humanoid-armature rejection in `Live Blender authoring session`, with the exact blocker and route status recorded.

For `nonhumanoid_creature` jobs, inspect every fragment's face count in the live authoring session and again in the export collection. Discard and report every zero-face fragment by object or node name, exclude it from the export collection and runtime candidate, and fail closed if any empty mesh node reaches export or reimport.

### Shared model texture delivery

Use `.tools/3d_pipeline/textures/cli.py` (`--help` for the current arguments) to prepare a hash-bound manifest, convert into a separate staging root, review the decoded output and semantic gates, then let the parent install through its compare-and-swap checks. Record source, target preimage, staged output, masks, provenance, and registration SHA-256 values; never regenerate from an already graded runtime derivative. Native BC1 diffuse and BC3 normal/specular DDS with complete mip chains are the model contract. Default to 512 pixels, never upscale a smaller source, and require a concrete size justification for 1024; the source atlas size is not a runtime-size exemption.

Resolve the actual active `pdxmesh`/`meshsettings` texture slot before deriving its UV occupancy mask with `.tools/3d_pipeline/runtime/uv_masks.py`. Bind masks to the selected mesh, registration, and texture bytes. Apply the material-class recipe in `textures/conventions.py` only through explicit reviewed class masks and immutable provider roughness/metalness provenance; do not replace a mixed atlas with one blanket specular value or synthesize missing detail. Keep packed channels as scalar data and validate occupied texels and the mip chain after encoding. Use occupancy-driven atlas dilation, normally 12 pixels within the supported 8-16 range, or record the explicit reason for skipping it.

For an installed packed normal with unproven source tangent handedness, preserve occupied G and A, mirror G into R, clear B, and repair only flat padding/gutters through the verified mask. Provider RGB repacking or green-sign inversion requires source-layout and handedness evidence bound by hash; never infer a sign flip from a Blender preview. Retain the pre-encoding G/A preservation report and decoded BC3 error/normal-detail measurements separately, since lossy compression cannot promise byte-identical channels.

### Family consumer coverage

Before claiming family-wide model or sound coverage, enumerate every `common/units` subunit that resolves the custom `sprite` token. An entity-state sound package reaches only consumers that resolve that entity, so intended shared-family consumers must share the binding and deliberate exclusions must be recorded in the handoff.

When an armored or other variant is defined as the visual derivative of a specialized parent unit, bind its `sprite` to that parent's specialized entity token unless the variant has a separately approved entity package. Preserve the variant's gameplay and `map_icon_category` settings, and audit the exact `common/units` mapping before handoff. Never leave a documented parent variant on the generic base sprite.

### Weight and topology validation

Use `.tools/3d_pipeline/adapter/geometry_delivery_checks.py` on the decoded delivered mesh. Combine all PDX material/vertex streams beneath each logical object for topology and winding analysis, while reporting each stream's export capacity separately; stream borders alone are not holes. Preserve the complete read-only topology and winding report even when non-manifold geometry blocks repair. Non-manifold, duplicate, or degenerate input remains ambiguous for automatic orientation repair, and a produced report does not authorize a flip, weld, cap, or acceptance.

Require no zero-weight deforming vertices, normalized weights, influence counts within local precedent, no unapproved opposite-side stretch, rigid assignment for rigid parts, and deformation tests in representative poses. Skin weights come from the deliberate live authoring required by `Live Blender authoring session`.

`sanitize_runtime_candidate` creates the runtime checkpoint and enforces the engine weight limits: it removes invalid and zero-weight influences, keeps the strongest influences within the maximum influence count, renormalizes the survivors, and reports the result per mesh while preserving geometry, the rig, weapons, and materials in `weight_only` mode. An unweighted vertex is a rigging defect: the pipeline raises on it and reports it for real authoring in Blender rather than pinning it to the root bone.

Nearest-N bone weighting and axis-aligned bounding-box selections can produce abrupt weight seams despite full coverage and nonzero weights. Render articulation extremes and inspect stretched edges; repair affected deforming regions with locally smooth, explicit normalized weights, verify the selected vertices receive the intended assignment, and confirm unselected weights remain unchanged.

A complete-looking surface with backface culling disabled does not prove reversed faces: the visible surface may be a far wall seen through a hole. Distinguish rest-topology defects from posed foldovers using neutral and posed views, exact affected edges, and face/corner normals before choosing a local repair; do not blanket-flip faces, weld vertices, or cap openings.

Before changing source normals, inspect the actual exported normal buffers or decoded native normal buffers and compare corresponding corners in a common coordinate space. A source-space normal discrepancy alone does not justify a repair. Blender's native split-normal encoding can introduce measurable angular drift; retain the default 0.25-degree guard. A reviewed per-job tolerance up to 0.5 degrees is supported only with the exact source hash, per-corner before/after and reopened-checkpoint evidence, and final visual review; never replace those checks with a broad normal reset.

### Action evidence and ground contacts

Every requested action must have a semantic role, its authoring-session provenance, the accepted rig and weight checkpoints, final name, FPS, frame range, loop state, root policy, preview, exported `.anim`, proposed runtime binding, and validation result. Preserve accepted authored motion through cleanup, baking, and export, and record the authored bones, channels, and role phases with before/after visual and deformation evidence. Inspect and sanitize scale F-curves and scale keyed location channels only when the provider and calibrated mesh units differ. Define in-place or root-motion policy before processing, apply any location conversion exactly once, and record the factor and before/after channels. Check foot and ground contacts at representative frames and validate every required role as real skeletal motion. Do not replace a missing action with a static pose, simplistic procedural stand-in, whole-rig transform, or another role's action. For loop actions, first/middle/last reimport screenshots are insufficient because the midpoint may intentionally return to neutral: sample at least the first, quarter, middle, three-quarter, and last phases. Retain decoded-pixel or pose/bounds comparisons that prove the quarter phases differ as intended and that the loop endpoints return appropriately, and record contact and actor-bounds checks at every sampled phase. For attack or fire roles, retain aim, discharge, recoil, and recovery evidence where applicable. For death roles, retain articulated collapse, impact, and settling evidence. For non-loop terminal actions, retain start/mid/end samples or other role-appropriate phases. A skeleton change invalidates weights, actions, exports, and downstream evidence.

Author ground contact from each frame's uncorrected posed contacts in the live session, preserve rotation modes, and prevent lateral drift; require contact preservation in the actual exported bytes after reimport, since a grounded Blender object alone does not prove exported grounding. Hold every creature action to a positive 1 mm ground clearance at its lowest contact point. Accept at most 10 mm of measured ground-contact tolerance after authoring, record the per-action measurements, and reject any action outside that limit. Require a grounded pass for every verified action before export.

Normalize quaternion sign continuity with `.tools/3d_pipeline/adapter/animation_delivery_checks.py` by negating all four existing quaternion key values and their matching handle values when consecutive quaternion dot products are negative. Preserve equivalent orientation, key times, interpolation settings, and key count; this cleanup never authors or inserts keys. Review half-frame and other interpolated motion for quaternion branch flips, joint snaps, and floor excursions; integer-frame poses alone do not pass this check.

Run the read-only delivered-animation gates in `animation_delivery_checks.py` against the actual `.anim` and matching skeleton, using a hash-bound role/loop/root policy and explicit strike windows where relevant. Retain pose-delta/spike, initial-root and drift, loop-seam, and stride results rather than treating parser success as motion acceptance. Measure sole/contact points in world coordinates against a declared ground plane, with contact/stationary tolerances and uncertainty, before accepting plant counts or proposing footstep times. Native ankle positions and nominal quarter-cycle timings are insufficient sole-contact proof; an unmeasured gate stays unresolved.

The per-frame limits come from `role_motion_limits` in `animation_delivery_checks.py`, calibrated from the installed vanilla `GER_infantry_*` clips measured in source-mesh units with the exact `GER_infantry.mesh` skeleton: idle 10 deg and 0.10 u per frame (vanilla idle peaks 6.0 deg, 0.052 u), move and retreat 25 deg and 0.36 u per frame (vanilla walk peaks 21.9 deg, 0.314 u at the toes), every other role outside declared strike windows 30 deg and 0.41 u per frame (vanilla strikes peak 21.3 deg, 0.353 u). Re-derive them from vanilla if the reference set or the measurement space changes; do not waive a flag or widen a window instead.

Global minimum-Z clearance alone does not prove anatomical support: a rigid prop, finger, or coat hem can touch the ground while the torso visibly floats. Inspect actual intended support/contact groups across intermediate collapse, impact, and settle frames as well as the terminal death pose, and each limb's stance/swing contacts through gait phases. Reject floating body groups that should be supported and interpolation that raises a settled body back toward standing. Correct articulation and prop contacts in the authored motion rather than relying only on whole-body Z translation.

## PDX export and reimport evidence

Before export, ensure the export collection contains only approved objects and that transforms, topology, materials, armature, actions, exporter version, and preset pass. Export `.mesh` and required `.anim` files with `export_mesh` and `export_animation` through the checksum-locked verified `io_pdx_mesh` route. Capture every warning, output path, and byte size.

Apply any required assembly yaw through `rotate_existing_assembly_yaw`, then reimport the final actual `.mesh`/`.anim` bytes with `reimport_export` and verify frame-1 facing and rest-bounds parity against the accepted source, allowing only the intended action pose; grounding and source-yaw equivalence alone cannot prove exported orientation. Use the locked `animation_root_export.py` correction to align initial root translation/quaternion with the samples' exact POSE-to-WORLD coordinate conversion, preserving child records and sample payload and verifying serialized readback. Do not bake the rig or retry paid generation to mask an initial-root export mismatch.

Keep the complete assembled body and all added props within the calibrated runtime triangle/material/texture budgets. Required equipment and components must not be omitted to hit the target. Modest, controlled reduction of low-value body or prop topology is authorized without renewed approval when needed to accommodate required additions; preserve silhouette, identity, joints, grips, UV/material quality, and required details, record before/after counts, and recheck deformation and export limits. If required quality and runtime limits cannot both pass, report the exact blocker rather than shipping an incomplete model.

For a static building, the final working checkpoint must pass the allowlisted `bake_static_mesh_transforms` gate before export: bake location, rotation, and scale into mesh data without changing world bounds, ground contact, UVs, materials, normals, or object name, then require exact identity object transforms. Reject armatures/actions, protected or shared source data, non-finite or negative transforms, bounds drift, and any retained non-identity transform. Do not compensate through entity scale.

When an accepted static-building silhouette would exceed the PDX per-stream vertex/index envelope after UV and normal seam splitting, do not destroy the shape through aggressive decimation. After transform baking, use `partition_static_mesh_export_batches` to keep the same object, geometry, UVs, normals, textures, bounds, and transforms while emitting several bounded PDX mesh streams. Default to a conservative worst-case 24,000 vertices per batch unless fresh installed-vanilla calibration proves another limit, parse the actual text export, and fail if any individual stream exceeds either 65,535 vertices or 65,535 triangle indices. Texture-identical batch slots are render streams rather than distinct logical materials, but every exported stream index must have a matching `meshsettings` block in the active `.gfx`. An index-0-only registration is invalid even when the locked importer can rejoin and render every stream.

A skeletal export that would exceed the same per-stream envelope uses `partition_skeletal_mesh_export_batches` on the hashed skeletal checkpoint after the export coordinate checkpoint, keeping the exact rig, meshes, materials, image consumers, and retention while emitting bounded material-backed PDX mesh streams under the same 65,535 vertex and triangle-index limits.

For every output, retain the export log and reimport or parse the actual `.mesh` or `.anim` bytes through the locked stack, saving the proof scene or parser report, measured geometry/action facts, and any warnings. If the verified stack cannot re-import or parse that format, record an explicit `required installation/verification` or `blocked` result. A Blender viewport, provider preview, file existence, or plausible filename is not reimport evidence. Do not silently ignore exporter warnings, missing actions, unsupported material channels, or an absent parser.

Before final runtime wiring for every custom model consumer, including articulated units, resolve the active `.gfx` `pdxmesh`/`meshsettings` texture overrides and distinguish them from stale embedded `.mesh` texture names. Effective overrides are the texture binding to audit and rebind for material review; an obsolete embedded filename alone is not a missing active texture. Decode or reimport the selected actual `.mesh` bytes and compare each `pdxmesh` `meshsettings.name` and `index` against the decoded object-node names and material-stream names and indices, then verify the shader and texture references. Provider labels, job slugs, prior-candidate names, file existence, and successful `.mesh`/`.anim`/DDS decoding alone do not prove material binding. Retain the byte identity and binding comparison in the runtime handoff, and repeat the check when the selected runtime mesh changes. The parent may perform this read-only decoding and binding review without reopening model production.

For installed-byte checkpoints, `.tools/3d_pipeline/runtime/reimport_runtime.py` snapshots and verifies the exact runtime mesh and bound texture hashes, checks the mesh SHA-256 in the adapter receipt, and records the saved proof checkpoint's hash. Its `preview_views=[]` route establishes native geometry/skeleton import only: no rendered material review, skin deformation, action acceptance, or live-game pass is implied. Keep parser decoding, native checkpoint import, rendered review, and user live-consumer evidence as distinct results.

`promote_accepted_reimport` promotes one hash-bound accepted reimport proof with metadata-only working approval: it requires the complete receipt, the immutable source `.blend`/`.mesh`/`.anim` hashes, one exact rig and 1-512 exact meshes, and a new sibling checkpoint, and it preserves geometry, materials, weights, bones, actions, and scale through save and reopen.

For static map buildings, also audit the runtime consumer after reimport: the `.gfx` scale and shader, meshsettings object name, runtime mesh and DDS paths, building definition, and spawn policy must agree. A custom map building must not use vanilla `special_project_facility_spawn`. Use a dedicated `type = province` spawn pool for a direct map consumer, and define the matching `building_<spawn_point>` entity in the active `.asset` file. Different meshes must never share one spawn point because HOI4 resolves one map entity per spawn point. When every gameplay building level must appear, wire the gameplay building directly and provide one explicit spawn position per possible rendered level. Use a hidden provincial anchor only for an intentional single visual independent of gameplay level, place it with state-scoped `set_building_level`, and explicitly clean it up on conversion, dismantlement, annexation, or deletion. Leave automatic nudging enabled unless complete `map/buildings.txt` coverage is maintained. If nudging fails, preserve every vanilla row in a deterministic generated override and add complete custom coverage.
