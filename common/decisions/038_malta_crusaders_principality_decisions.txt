# ============================================================================
# The decisions of the principality category (Evolution II). Seven charters
# create a principality from the held states of a region. The management rows
# are aimed at one principality of global.malta_principalities (FROM is the
# principality): request a levy, send relief, enforce the charter, lighten or
# burden the tribute, arbitrate a succession four ways, begin integration and
# dissolve a failed principality. One row forms the federation of all of them.
# Every row has a payment check that reads the published cost, an effect
# tooltip and a score for the AI.
# ============================================================================
