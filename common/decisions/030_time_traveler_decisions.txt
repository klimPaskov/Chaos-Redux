# ============================================================================

# ============================================================================
# Custodian category (time_traveler_category): six decisions and the consultation
# missions.
#   consult     opens the family picker, chaosx.nr30.20
#   revise      reopens the access arrangement, chaosx.nr30.14
#   relocate    moves the traveler to another secure state
#   guard       a guard detail around the traveler
#   sting       a counterintelligence sting against one interested country
#   share       one forecast shared with an ally
# Foreign category (time_traveler_foreign_category): the four operations of an
# interested country. AI countries act through chaosx.nr30.30 and never through
# these decisions, so their weights are zero.
# Costs are cached in the time_traveler_cost_* country variables, refreshed by
# time_traveler_refresh_costs and time_traveler_refresh_rival_costs. Missions
# complete on their own when the objective is observed.
# ============================================================================
