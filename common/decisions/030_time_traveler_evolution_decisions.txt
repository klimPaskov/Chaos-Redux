# ============================================================================
# CHAOS REDUX - EVENT 030 TIME TRAVELER GOVERNMENT DECISIONS
# ============================================================================
# The country the traveler leads. A traveler-led government is strong in
# coordination and intelligence and carries specific risks: preventive
# detention based on predicted crimes, hostility towards officials whose future
# conduct he remembers, premature wars and a growing distance from the public.
# The five preventive choices of Evolution I:
#   watch      surveillance of a predicted threat (can become public)
#   restrict   remove future offenders before the remembered offense
#   change     an early deterrent or a mobilization (chaosx.nr30.130)
#   build      a permanent institution imported from the remembered future
#   suppress   reform an institution that later failed
# Costs are cached by time_traveler_refresh_costs. No decision grants a war
# goal. Detainees of the restriction are held in ordinary custody.
# ============================================================================
