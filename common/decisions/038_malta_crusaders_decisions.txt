# CHAOS REDUX - EVENT 038 MALTA CRUSADERS DECISIONS AND MISSIONS
# ============================================================================
# The Crusade Council: the three answers to an open order demand, Joint
# Command, the arbitration of Jerusalem and the seizure of an order treasury.
# Mediterranean Campaigns: escort the sea road, repair the Holy Land rail,
# fortify a port, the emergency air bridge, the ultimatum and the regional
# crusade (both aimed at a country), the three settlements of a held state and
# four missions. The Banners: one row per formation family. The Eleventh
# Crusade: recovery rows by phase and three missions.
#
# Merged design rows (reported in docs/events/038_malta_crusaders.md):
# Select Regional Objective is the target list of the two campaign rows, the
# Port Project mission is the timer of Fortify a Port, Convert Captured
# Equipment, Request Foreign Equipment and Seek Temporary Refuge need systems
# of later phases.
#
# Every row has a payment check that reads the published cost, an effect
# tooltip and an AI score from common/mtth/038_malta_crusaders_mtth.txt.
# ============================================================================
