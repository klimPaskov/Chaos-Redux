# CHAOS REDUX - EVENT 040 LAWRENCE OF ARABIA FEDERATION FOCUS TREE
# ============================================================================
# Loaded onto the federation core when Evolution III forms a federation (a
# transformed member, no new country tag). Opening group: one provisional
# government, then four lanes of choice: the political settlement of the
# route, the federal centre, the army and the economy. The remaining lanes
# open behind them.
#
#   Lane              Root (x)             Filters
#   Economy, oil      customs line (2)     industry, stability, research
#   Army              member armies (8)    army, manpower
#   Air, coast        airfields (14)       air, navy
#   British compact   imperial compact (18) political (British Arabia)
#   Sovereign         congress (22)        political (Independent Federation)
#   Personal union    oath (26)            political (Lawrence's Kingdom)
#   Federal centre    census (31)          internal affairs, industry
#   Diplomacy         recognition (39)     trade, political
#   Integration       accession (43)       annexation, internal affairs
#
# The three political lanes are mutually hidden by route flags
# (allow_branch). The starting spirits, Uneven Administration, Rival
# Commands and Promise Debt, Federal Mistrust or Personal Settlement, have a
# lifecycle through these focuses and the federal decisions. Authority gains
# use the lawrence_fed_authority_* effects and the values are in
# lawrence_focus (common/script_constants/040_lawrence_of_arabia_federation_
# constants.txt).
# ============================================================================
