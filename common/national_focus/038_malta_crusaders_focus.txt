# CHAOS REDUX - EVENT 038 MALTA CRUSADERS FOCUS TREE
# ============================================================================
# The playable identity of the Malta Crusader state. The tree begins after the
# crusade has started: a short opening (the crusade is underway, the four
# anchors, the council) and then six lanes of choice. Loaded by
# malta_initialize_country_package through load_focus_tree.
#
#   Lane            Root (x)                       Filters
#   Logistics       Secure the Sea Road (2)        industry, trade
#   Army            Unleash the Knight Banners (8) army experience, research
#   Orders          Charter the Orders (17)        political, military
#   Settlement      Frame the Settlement (26)      political, internal affairs
#   Holy Land       Fortify the Holy Land (35)     annexation, stability
#   Diplomacy       Appeal to Catholic Governments (43) trade, political
#   Principalities  Renew the Latin East (26, y 10) Evolution II
#   Eleventh        Gather the Survivors (6, y 10) after a collapse
#   Church          The Western Question (43, y 8)  the Holy See and the Kingdom
#
# The three settlement routes exclude each other (Grand Magistry, Assembly of
# the Langues, civil settlement). The six dominant-order focuses are open only
# on the Grand Magistry route and only until one order is named. The tree adds
# no national spirits: it swaps the three of the opening package along their
# lifecycle (fortress, orders line, army). Values come from the malta_focus
# constants.
# ============================================================================
