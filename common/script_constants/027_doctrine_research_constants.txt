# Native subdoctrine AI preferences mirrored from vanilla
# common/doctrines/subdoctrines/**/*.txt ai_will_do. Each subdoctrine score is
# multiplied by its base factor, its country factor when the country's
# original tag is one that vanilla lists for it, and its minor or major factor.
# A product below the base weight leaves the candidate at the base weight
# instead, so every valid choice keeps a positive weight. Subdoctrines without
