# Event 012 response views

Purpose: organize the existing human host's decision category without changing payments, target selection, gameplay rewards or timers.
Scope: COUNTRY on the host; all helpers take no parameters and use boolean calls.
The private `gods_of_africa_response_view` enum selects overview, priorities, partners or judgment.
Absent or invalid state displays overview.

## Navigation and precedence

`gods_of_africa_open_priorities`, `gods_of_africa_open_partner_relations`, `gods_of_africa_open_judgments` and `gods_of_africa_return_to_overview` recheck human control, current phase and visible child context before changing only the presentation enum.
Use each as `helper_name = yes` from its matching navigation decision.
Selecting a priority returns to overview after its existing priority owner executes.
Navigation never pays, chooses a target, answers an offer, changes doctrine or edits a deadline.

Current substitute offers, paid reconciliation offers and valid victor-renewal requests take precedence over discretionary controls without changing the stored view.
Context helpers inspect only the maintained participant and candidate arrays.
Unaffordable substitutes still receive accept-with-reason/reject controls, and non-member victors retain their renewal exception.
When the last valid answer closes, the saved view returns, or doctrine selection if none is selected.

The three reply rows prewarm their existing arrays by country existence in daily `target_trigger`.
Exact current-answer predicates run in per-frame `visible`; existing `available` payment checks and effect owners remain.
A new offer can therefore appear without another daily target match for an already cached country.
A previously uncached country remains subject to native target-refresh timing.
`ROOT` stays the host inside bounded array checks, while `THIS` is the member; decision `FROM` remains the selected target.

## Surfaces and cleanup

- Pending answers: accept/reject substitute, accept reconciliation and eligible final settlement.
- Doctrine: the three existing choices and eligible final settlement.
- Overview: three context-sensitive navigation choices and eligible final settlement.
- Priorities: five core needs and at most one existing advanced lane, plus return navigation.
- Partners: leniency, pardon, protection and expedition, plus return.
- Judgment: offender marking, escalation, coalition support and occupier coercion, plus return.

Final commitment remains on overview, doctrine and pending-answer surfaces, avoiding a seventh primary choice beside six priorities.
Settlement delivery and separate recovery decisions are never view-gated.
Discretionary human choices recheck their view in visibility, availability and hidden execution.
AI hosts bypass navigation and retain their existing campaign conditions and numeric willingness factors.

Fresh host generation, unifier loss, final system cleanup and residual original-host recovery clear the private view.
Ordinary ticks and temporary pending-answer priority do not reset it.
It is not transferred as gameplay authority.

## Participant obligations

The generic substitute shortcut is hidden; the three named alternatives and return-to-demand retain every offer outcome.
While a final ultimatum is pending, the four optional preparations and the untimed defiance entry are deferred, with matching click-time preflights.
Previously purchased preparation effects remain, and eligible unused choices return after the ultimatum closes.
Reconciliation responses, paid receipts and every applicable ultimatum answer remain accessible.
Neither mission's activation, due date, timeout, payment or outcome changes.
No view transition pauses, restarts, answers or forfeits either obligation.

## Presentation and limits

Navigation reuses the priority, protection and offender icons in `interface/012_africa.gfx`.
English labels and qualitative phase/deadline notes use the existing localisation file and `012_africa_gods_response_scripted_localisation.txt`.
No public number, category, scripted GUI, artwork or event-catalog entry is added.
This private API remains documented with its owner rather than expanding the cross-system dynamic registry.
Acceptance basis: the full-Gods action-budget goal and parent adoption of the October 7 bounded phase proposal.
The response-context handoff records native/offline scope references and the preserved target predicates.
Source/model counts do not establish native row geometry, cache timing or campaign AI cadence.
No broader phase expansion is proposed.
