| Symbol after `gods_of_africa_` | Scope and input | Output and side effects | Default |

All symbols use ordinary Boolean calls.
No temporary variable is read through ROOT/PREV or another scope.
The quote calculator owns industry-specific scratch names and does not clobber equipment-floor arithmetic.
No global target, periodic hook or world scan is created.

## History, reports and cleanup

Terminal service facts enter the original participant's existing aligned kind/day history using `industry_service_ended`, with no compliance, refusal or failure increment.
Five frozen identity fields prevent repeated publication, and final money/day/site facts remain in that actor's bounded `last_industry_retired_*` receipt.
This historical identity proof is independent of live Gods permissions and does not require the donor to remain a current participant.
No growing active programme registry is introduced.

The report bridge runs before terms are cleared, so the existing `.604` wrapper can describe the named site, actual new paid service and exact financial balance.
Report caches are cleared by the existing receipt-cleanup owner.
Financial term cleanup clears the industrial unresolved latch only after its owned in-flight transaction is absent, while preserving host-owned unresolved evidence and earned time.
The CXT package only receives an inert readiness flag through its existing idempotent extension.

These are source contracts, not observations of native repair assignment, timer refresh, monetary precision or expiry.
The source models and MCP command checks retain their explicit `SourceNoNativeProof` limits.
