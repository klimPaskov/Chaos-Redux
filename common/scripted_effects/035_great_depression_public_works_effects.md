# Event 035 regional works contracts

The original full-package user instruction is the acceptance basis for the identified regional works in specification Part 3 and center recovery in Part 4.
These owner helpers implement that bounded surface without a world scan, factory grant, political-power substitute, extra public meter, or undocumented negative-damage effect.
Engine execution, complete balance, resource refunds, lasting-capacity closure, all assets and full-event acceptance remain separate requirements.

## Scope, inputs and outputs

All helpers except their explicit nested country blocks run in STATE under the crisis-country ROOT.
`great_depression_prepare_public_works_plan` requires current center registration, ownership/control, an open project slot and an actual need.
The targeted action calls it before payment, then checks the existing four-type Public Works cost contract, performs the existing debit once, and calls the common project authorizer.
Preparation records site, original payer, live owner, episode, previous authorization and the next expected paid-action count.
Authorization checks the exact preparation, payer/site, fresh action count and same-day payment witness.
`great_depression_bind_public_works_plan` also verifies current registration, crisis, project episode/type, unspent fresh authorization and the same witness before committing its paid receipt.
All normal state work and certification fields remain owned by the shared condition-scaled project lifecycle.

The frozen kind has priority: infrastructure repair, railway repair, port repair, inadequate infrastructure, an eligible neighboring railway connection, missing coastal port facilities, wartime anti-air facilities, abandoned-site conversion, or locally needed housing recovery.
Repairs freeze damaged levels and total levels; the required measured reduction is one damaged level or the remaining fractional damage when less than one.
They accelerate the documented native state repair families while controlled work remains valid.
Proof requires both reduced damage and preserved total capacity, so dismantling a damaged building cannot certify a repair.
No negative damage command or same-level reset is used.

Physical construction freezes the original and target levels, grants at most one level after paid controlled work, and checks actual total and non-damaged getters against the target.
External completion of that target grants no extra level.
Bombing, ineffective construction, or subsequent destruction cannot cause a second issuance within that episode.
Rail planning stores the first eligible adjacent owned/controlled partner with a capital railway or the capital itself.
Preparation, issuance and proof use the same allied-only, fallback-disabled, owner-only controller priority.
Completion rechecks the frozen adjacency, partner authority and capital network, then the actual source-to-partner connection.
Port proof establishes coastal facilities, not sea access, convoy availability or a usable international shipping route.

Housing and abandoned-site conversion freeze the exact local additive demand contribution already used by the national hidden-component rebuild.
The single `great_depression_measure_center_local_demand` helper owns that measurement for both consumers.
The paid result changes the physical center to Reopened, writes its existing stage ledger, and remeasures the same contribution before certifying a genuine reduction.
National demand movement alone and self-written outcome flags do not satisfy the result.
This is represented state recovery with a construction/supply legacy, not factory capacity or building slots.
Distressed plus protection alone has no positive local housing burden and is not a housing need.

## Lifecycle, side effects and defaults

`great_depression_finish_public_works_result` runs before generic completion counters and requires exact current paid work, coherent duration, unspent authorization and completed controlled work.
Other project types retain their existing contract through `great_depression_project_has_public_works_result`.
An active paid Public Works plan cannot be reinterpreted as another project type to bypass its result proof; legitimate subsequent projects enter only after transient plan cleanup.
Unproved results retain a frozen 28-day proof grace after their project due date.
The native center objective uses the same date for a bound Public Works project, including a valid paid plan at a Distressed or already Reopened selected center.
Existing main/support mission slot and once-per-episode terminal gates remain in force.
The project failure resolver retires its exact bound native mission; native timeout dispatches to that resolver for matching Public Works, preventing a second failure charge.

Suspension, lost control and invalid actors remove repair administration.
Legitimate resume/adoption revalidates the exact current receipt before restoring it.
Transfer preserves the original payer, accepted need, physical baseline, authorization and required work while moving the live owner and episode into the successor's existing center row.
Already issued work remains spent after adoption.
Cleanup removes transient plans and emergency repair terms but retains durable issuance and local-recovery history.
Issuance is limited by the globally allocated episode, not by a bare authorization serial that can restart after cleanup.
A later episode may pay for a fresh genuine need; infrastructure/port/air thresholds and existing recovery/legacy gates prevent an unbounded repeated map grant.
Malformed, unmatched or missing plan fields fail closed and have no default successful outcome.

## Usage

```text
# STATE selected under paying ROOT; the existing action supplies the whole transaction.
great_depression_launch_regional_works_program = yes
# Existing bounded active-country receiver processes registered centers:
great_depression_process_center_project = yes
```

## Presentation and assets

`GetGreatDepressionWorksNeed` gives frozen work priority over changing preview need in the state-targeted tooltip.
Repair administration uses `GFX_modifier_035_great_depression_center_public_works`; housing/site recovery uses `GFX_modifier_035_great_depression_center_reopened`, both registered in the existing Event 035 modifier icon family.
No artwork is resized into another family and no new bitmap is claimed.
Full per-asset source/PNG/final-DDS/native-consumer inspection remains required by the user's separate asset repair request.
The central tuning groups are `great_depression_public_works_kind` and `great_depression_public_works` in `common/script_constants/035_great_depression_constants.txt`.
Other player-facing numbers remain quoted costs and accepted work dates, not new persistent management meters.

It covers named eligibility, all nine work kinds, exact paid binding, local burden, 512 additive stage/treatment combinations, capacity-preserving repair, fractional damage, demolition, bombing, external completion, one-shot output, missing authority, transfer, repeat episode, cleanup and either callback order.
These models do not establish native repair queues, railway path selection, province port placement, consumer rendering, save/reload or campaign execution.
The architect and probability-owner handoffs retain precise reference revisions, partial MCP results and current limits.
The complete refund/reserved-input ledger and lasting-capacity closing transaction remain required and are not replaced by this result proof.
