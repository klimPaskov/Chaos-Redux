# Overview:
# - Defines dedicated roles for protected, chemical-assault, armored-delivery,
#   and containment formations without replacing any vanilla role template.
# - Every target is country-gated by its unlocks, complete standing-equipment
#   bill and, for offensive roles, current use policy and real payload stock.
# - Target templates describe standing formations only. They never authorize
#   or dispatch an exposure operation.
# ============================================================================
