# ============================================================================
# CHAOS REDUX - CBRN REGIMENTAL SUPPORT AI TEMPLATES
# ============================================================================
# Overview:
# - Defines protected-line, hazard-assault, chemical-artillery,
#   armored-delivery, protected-garrison, coercive-suppression, and
#   biosecurity formations without replacing any vanilla template file.
# - Every target is country-gated by its unlocks, complete standing-equipment
#   bill and, for offensive roles, current use policy and real payload stock.
# - Native enable and can_upgrade_in_field gates respond to doctrine, threat,
#   HQ preparation, stock, fuel, resistance, contamination, and outbreak state.
#   No scripted pulse, destructive template deletion, or inferred operation
#   target is used.
# - Target templates describe standing formations only. They never authorize
#   or dispatch an exposure operation.
# ============================================================================
