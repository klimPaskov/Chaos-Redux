# Migration pressure trigger API

`migration_pressure_triggers.txt` publishes two read-only STATE-scope predicates for the decision owner and bounded crisis adapters.

`migration_state_has_live_departure_pressure` accepts a valid current famine-to-migration safety receipt at sustained supply strain (the existing 14-day exposure or two-food-incident threshold) or acute shortage and worse, current owner-proven blockade, active persecution or bombing, tracked chemical or biological contamination, a live disaster aftermath with direct population pressure, an established neighboring Black Plague or live standard/weaponized zombie outbreak, or an adjacent state whose actual controller is at war with this state's actual controller. It does not scan active registries or infer a front from an owner-only relation.

`migration_state_has_real_migration_issue` accepts live departure pressure or positive state-local flight, trapped, reception, and live-cohort load, plus a currently valid matching reception-front identity. Positive cohorts require aligned registries and unambiguous local selection in their consuming operation; this trigger never mutates those registries.

Both triggers fail closed when the state is not a valid civilian-system state. Durable history, raw outbreak flags without an established outbreak predicate, stale food projections, and unpaired reception-front identity rows do not qualify.
