## Runtime derivative changes

All WAVs are mono 44.1 kHz PCM16 and retain the documented normalization.
The electrical pulse is additionally trimmed to one second with entry/exit fades; the ten-second original is preserved in the source evidence.
Electrical source and derivative license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
Death source and derivative license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/.
Other listed sources use https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.
Exact runtime filenames, hashes and transformations are recorded in docs/assets/chaos_redux_3d_model_pilots/models_3d/portal_raider/evidence/audio/runtime_proposal/runtime_sound_copy_manifest.json.
