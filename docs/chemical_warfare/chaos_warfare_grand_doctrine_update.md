# Chaos Warfare Grand Doctrine

## Purpose

Chaos Warfare is the aggressive CBRN grand doctrine. It is available only to a country with a real protective, agent, command, historical-program, or scenario capability. Adoption initializes the national CBRN institution, opens four specialized tracks, grants 15% planning speed, and grants 35% soft attack, breakthrough, and defense to chemical support companies.

The doctrine makes prepared CBRN deployment faster and more destructive. It can reduce the Condemnation impact of recorded use, but it never erases payload consumption, evidence, attribution, deaths, contamination, medical saturation, resistance trauma, history, or responsibility.

## Tracks

- Hazard Infantry rewards mask discipline, protected assault formations, the Chaos Battalion, and hazard-pioneer operations.
- Contaminant Fire Support rewards projector batteries, ammunition trains, artillery coordination, chemical operational effect, and persistent contamination.
- Armored Hazard Delivery rewards sealed armor, chemical delivery detachments, protected maneuver, and coordinated breakthrough.
- Integrated CBRN Command rewards reconnaissance, headquarters logistics, paid air and exact-state operations, ordinary biological lifecycle potency, and lower Condemnation impact at high mastery.

Each adoption and mastery tier has large visible unit or command bonuses in addition to its systemic receipt. Current mastery multipliers are 12 for infantry, 16 for combat support, 14 for armor, and 14 for operations.

## Shared-pipeline effects

The Combat Support ladder reaches 1.65 operational effect and 1.80 contamination output. The Integrated Operations ladder reaches 1.70 chemical and biological operational effect, 1.60 chemical-air dose, and 1.50 chemical-air duration. Its Condemnation-only ladder is 0.75, 0.55, and 0.35. Offensive officer-corps postures can further increase dose, casualties, contamination, duration, medical saturation, and camp killing efficiency while lowering Condemnation impact; those bonuses do not conceal or delete consequence records.

Every active chemical route still has to provide an exact target, real payload debit or authoritative native equipment receipt, target protection, positive release conditions, and an accepted action record before the shared dispatcher applies disruption, deaths, contamination, medical saturation, evidence, attribution, history, treaty response, and Condemnation.

## Tactics

`tactic_gas_mask_defense` remains a defensive battlefield tactic unlocked by mask research. The legacy chemical offensive tactics are synchronized for save compatibility, but their release weight is fail-closed because the current combat-tactic scope cannot prove an exact selected state, a paid payload receipt, and verified release conditions. No free chemical release or estimator replaces that missing receipt.
