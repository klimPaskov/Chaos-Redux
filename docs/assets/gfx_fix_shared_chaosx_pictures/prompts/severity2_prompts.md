## `GFX_report_railway_sovereignty`

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: source art for a Hearts of Iron IV report event image; the repository will apply its standard 210x176 tilted paper-card treatment. Scene/backdrop: a 1930s Eastern European railway junction with switch tracks, a signal cabin, telegraph poles, and low hills under overcast skies. Subject: a heavy interwar armored train moving slowly through the junction, its locomotive and riveted cars clearly period-accurate, no identifiable insignia. Style/medium: candid 1930s press photograph, monochrome tonal base with natural fine grain, documentary realism. Composition/framing: medium-wide view down the tracks, train centered and readable, keep the locomotive and junction within a safe central crop with ample edge room for later report-card processing. Mood: strategic control of transport infrastructure. Constraints: opaque full-canvas photograph only; no tilted card, border, paper mat, frame, shadow, readable writing, logos, flags, modern vehicles, extra focal people, or watermark.
