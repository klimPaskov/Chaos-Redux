## Notes

- No final gameplay flag should be invented for historical polities that still lack an attested symbol. Those rows stay blocked until a real archival flag, seal, coat, banner, regalia photo, or portrait is found.
- The Kongo and Merina flag rows are usable as research leads, but both are modern vector files built from older references rather than period cloth photographs.
- The self-made `Flag of the Sokoto Caliphate.svg` and `Ajuran Empire flag.png` Commons files were rejected as primary leads because their pages do not provide reliable historical sourcing. The Burmi flag scan and Mareeg ruin photo are the stronger replacements.
- The Zanzibar row is better than the old 1963-only lead, but it is still a modern vectorization from a cited heraldic source book rather than a direct period artifact scan.
- Kabalega remains blocked because this pass did not surface a rights-clean portrait or period illustration specific to him; modern tomb and memorial photography is not an acceptable substitute for the intended historical dossier/portrait use.
