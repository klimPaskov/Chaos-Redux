# Disaster Wardens audio source research

Recorded before download on 2026-08-06. Runtime wiring is parent-owned.

## Approved sources

- `rain_thunder.ogg`: “Rain and thunder,” Caesar, public domain dedication. Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_and_thunder.ogg. Direct download: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/Rain_and_thunder.ogg. Modification and redistribution are unrestricted.
- `signal_bell.ogg`: “WWS Signalbell,” Work With Sounds / Konrad Gutkowski / Julian Blaschke, CC BY 4.0. Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWS_Signalbell.ogg. Direct download: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/file/WWS_Signalbell.ogg. Modification and redistribution are allowed with attribution and a change notice.

Selection rationale: real weather for idle/containment/death and an emergency signal device for selection, movement, and rescue cues. No generated or placeholder audio is used.
