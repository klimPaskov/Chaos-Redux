## Shared generation notes

- Each source PNG was generated as a single symbolic subject on a flat chroma-key background through the built-in `image_gen` path.
- Local post-processing removed the chroma key, cropped the subject, added a dark outline and soft shadow, and composited the result onto a shared medal-style backplate.
- The shared backplate was derived from the earlier discarded medal draft at `/mnt/c/Users/klimp/.codex/generated_images/019ed796-5f89-74e3-80a2-b57de38344c8/ig_0ec47d002bf8d8e2016a3318d2d8548191b7733805d39284c5.png`.
- `_grey` variants were derived locally from the completed variants through desaturation and contrast tuning.
- `_not_eligible` variants were derived locally from the grey variants with a red crossed overlay.
- Final icons use a dark full-frame achievement presentation. No flat white background or white matte was introduced.
