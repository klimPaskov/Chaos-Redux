## Scope

This package replaces the generic African placeholder portrait for the ten human regional authority tags:

- `WAC` - West African Congress
- `SAH` - Sahel Caravan
- `MAG` - Maghreb Coast
- `NHR` - Nile-Horn League
- `EAC` - East African Railway Congress
- `GLK` - Great Lakes Council
- `CBC` - Congo Basin Charter
- `ZSC` - Zambezi-Stone Cities
- `SLC` - South African Liberation Congress
- `IOC` - Indian Ocean Congress

These are fictional institutional leader portraits for human political bodies. They are not portraits of real historical people and are separate from the explicit nonhuman / supernatural high-chaos actor portraits.

## Source Notes

An asset subagent was spawned for this package, but it stopped without a final handoff after producing unusable compass/seal sheets. Those temporary sheets were rejected and not used as final portraits. Parent generation produced the final source images with built-in `$imagegen`, preserved the generated originals under the Codex generated-image directory, and copied the reviewed sources into this package.
