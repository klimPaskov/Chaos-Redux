## Generation prompts

Shared style target for all prompts: detailed painted HOI4 achievement medal source art with bronze-gold laurel relief, dark enamel field, one clear central subject, strong silhouette, and square framing readable at `64x64`. All prompts forbade text, portraits, watermarks, and modern logos.

| Achievement ID | Exact source prompt summary |
| --- | --- |
| `ACH_AFR_CHARTER_WITH_TEETH` | Armed Charter League seal with crossed rifles and a bayonet behind a stamped treaty plaque, reading as a defended charter rather than chains. |
| `ACH_AFR_ARCHIVE_OF_OLD_SEATS` | Archive cabinet and stacked old-seat ledgers with Africa-shaped wax seals and record tabs, clearly archival and governmental. |
| `ACH_AFR_BESTIARY_HAS_A_SEAT` | Fictional nonhuman Bestiary delegation in a charter chamber, with an okapi, a horned forest beast, and an impossible avian delegate around a treaty table. |
| `ACH_AFR_NOT_PAPER_ANYMORE` | Paper territorial claims transforming into living cores, with treaty papers unfolding into railway lines, factories, and city towers across an Africa-shaped foundation. |
| `ACH_AFR_ALLIES_MADE_PEACE` | South African and Allied rupture resolving into an armistice, shown as broken artillery and split flags lowered beside a signed ceasefire plaque and clasped gauntlets. |
| `ACH_AFR_WORLD_IS_ONE_ONLY_AFTER_AFRICA` | Globe and continent seals under a final charter, with a monumental union gate framing the world and Africa at the center. |

The same subagent pass also generated `ACH_AFR_CONTINENTS_HAVE_A_CONGRESS` with cross-continent congress rings over a charter field and interlocking continental seals around a central treaty emblem; that generated source was assigned to batch 2 because that icon already belonged to the batch 2 package.

## Shared processing notes

- White or pale source backdrops were removed or neutralized during local processing so the final icons do not carry the white matte issue.
- Normal variants keep the bronze medal finish.
- `_grey` variants are locally derived desaturated medal versions.
- `_not_eligible` variants are locally derived from the grey icons with a red crossed-out overlay matching the existing achievement reference pattern.
- No real people, real flags, or final sourced historical state symbols were used in final art.
