## Shared generation and processing notes

- Final art is generated fictional and symbolic medal art only. No real photographs, archival images, or real state emblems were sourced.
- The usable source renders came from the built-in `image_gen` workspace under `/mnt/c/Users/klimp/.codex/generated_images/019ed7ac-50f4-79f3-a214-766496eec937/`.
- Initial mistaken copies from a different generated-images cache were replaced before processing; only the source files listed above were used for final outputs.
- Completed icons were resized directly from the generated medal art to exact `64x64`.
- `_grey` variants were derived locally by grayscale conversion from the completed icon.
- `_not_eligible` variants were derived locally from the grey icon with a repo-style red crossed overlay.
- Achievement icons intentionally keep full-frame dark medal backplates. No flat white background or white matte was introduced.
- Transparency was not used for the final achievement icons because the existing Chaos Redux achievement family is full-frame medal art rather than transparent cutout iconography.
