# Event 012 Tier A identity icon prompt record

These six fictional Tier A package icons were generated as original symbolic art for focus and decision surfaces. They contain no words, letters, numbers, banners, human councils, or readable text, and they do not reuse the eight strange-force unit motifs.

The first native ImageGen sheet placed six square tiles in this order: Pan, Gorilla Kingdom, The Green, Living Rivers, Stoneborn, and Ancient Hosts. The Pan tile was regenerated after review so that Pan is a lean great-ape Pan-genus engineer with subtle horns and pointed ears, dexterous hands, tools, and fieldworks; it is deliberately distinct from the broad, heavily armored Gorilla Kingdom ape.

The approved art directions are:

- Pan: agile great-ape engineer, tool satchel, pliers, compact fieldworks, forest sabotage.
- Gorilla Kingdom: massive nonhuman gorilla, heavy infantry armor, mountain forest, broad shoulders.
- The Green: ancient living tree and forest intelligence, roots, canopy, defensive ecology, no humanoid person.
- Living Rivers: braided serpentine river guardian, impossible flowing channels, reeds, water bargaining.
- Stoneborn: living carved stone headless sentinel or monolith, masonry cracks, fortress and siege geometry.
- Ancient Hosts: supernatural ancient nonhuman host formation, relic standards, ruins, no human soldiers.

The six source tiles are retained under `sources/focus/` and `sources/decision/`; the alpha intermediates under `processed_alpha/` preserve source-scale canvases for inspection. Exact target PNGs are under `processed/focus/` and `processed/decision/`.
