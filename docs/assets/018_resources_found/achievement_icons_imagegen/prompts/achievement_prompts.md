# Event 018 Achievement Icon Prompts

## Production mode

- Source mode: built-in `$imagegen`, one independent generation per achievement.
- Use case: `stylized-concept`.
- Asset type: completed-state Hearts of Iron IV custom-achievement semantic master, intended for exact `64x64` runtime export.
- Cross-type rule: every source is achievement-specific artwork. No focus, idea, decision, category, portrait, flag, or event-picture source is reused or resized.

## Shared prompt foundation

Create exactly one square, fully opaque custom-achievement icon. Use a dark gunmetal and tarnished-bronze beveled square frame with small rivets and a restrained inner bronze laurel or medal rim. Inside the frame, paint one centered semantic tableau in a 1930s to 1940s grand-strategy achievement style: aged hand-painted realism, crisp silhouette, strong light-dark separation, deep charcoal shadows, controlled brass highlights, and enough simplified detail to remain unmistakable after reduction to `64x64`. Keep the principal subject large and within the safe center. The source is the completed/color state, not a grey or disabled state.

The Oth-Kesh, when present, are an original organized nonhuman subterranean species with heavy low bodies and layered mineral carapaces grown as part of their anatomy. They are not goblins, kobolds, humanoid soldiers in costumes, gemstones with faces, or a real-world ethnic or religious analogue.

No text, letters, words, signatures, handwriting, labels, runes, glyphs, readable maps, digits, numerals, tally marks, currency symbols, flags, national emblems, political insignia, logos, watermark, UI caption, fake checkerboard, white sticker rim, transparent canvas, or disabled-state red cross. Any paper, seal, border post, dial, crate, or plaque must be blank. Do not produce a contact sheet, multiple panels, mockup, or alternate variants.

## Per-achievement subjects

### `018_resources_found_one_vein_market`

One immense luminous ore vein dominates a deep mine cut and rises through a compact junction of ore carts, rail lines, and industrial gears, making the single geological seam visibly more powerful than the surrounding trade machinery. Use iron grey, burnished copper, amber ore light, and dark coal shadows. Do not show coins, banknotes, price tags, or currency symbols.

### `018_resources_found_all_resources_one_state`

One mine headframe and drill complex sits at the center while six materially distinct resource motifs converge into it: a black oil flow, pale aluminium ingot, raw rubber and tapping cup, dark tungsten cutting tool, steel beam, and blue-silver chromium crystal. Arrange the six motifs as a balanced ring feeding one field. Keep every motif visual and unlabeled.

### `018_resources_found_every_worker_home`

A safely closed mine shaft with an intact steel gate and warm lamps. An orderly arc of returned miners' helmets and hand lamps rests in front, every lamp lit, while a clean rail exits toward dawn. The mood is relief and disciplined safety. No memorial wreath, grave, casualty imagery, skull, cross, or abandoned equipment.

### `018_resources_found_full_seal_evolution_three`

A massive reinforced concrete-and-steel shaft bulkhead under visible subterranean pressure, held by heavy braces and freshly driven anchors. Two period engineers at the lower edge operate a wrench and sealing pump while the center door bows slightly but holds. Use safety red only as a small warning accent. No creature is fully visible.

### `018_resources_found_contract_of_century`

A blank folded period contract closed with a large unmarked wax-and-brass seal, layered over a mine headframe, a loaded rail wagon, and a rail line reaching outward. The seal, rail, and resource field must read as one stable international industrial agreement. The paper must contain no marks or writing.

### `018_resources_found_resolve_field_dispute`

Two opposing blank border posts stand on either side of a shared mineral seam. Their rifles are lowered and angled outward while a neutral unmarked gear-and-stone commission seal bridges the center, with a straight inspection road passing beneath it. The composition communicates settlement and demilitarization, not victory.

### `018_resources_found_thirty_from_below`

A massed organized column of many mineral-armored Oth-Kesh silhouettes emerges in disciplined ranks from one gigantic breach. In the foreground, a much smaller period anti-tank gun and sandbag defense holds its line. Emphasize overwhelming formation depth through repeated silhouettes and receding ranks without any readable count or numeral.

### `018_resources_found_last_shaft_closed`

The final battle-scarred cave entrance is closed by a new steel-and-concrete seal at first light. A damaged anti-tank gun, spent engineering braces, cable reels, and a single upright shovel frame the sealed opening. No living Oth-Kesh remain. The image communicates the last regional threat source being physically closed after hard fighting.

### `018_resources_found_ten_from_one_state`

One richly veined geological anchor rises from a single captured non-origin landscape and feeds a complete circular brood lattice of many small mineral nodes. At the center, one newly formed Oth-Kesh brood silhouette steps from the anchor. Show maximum capacity through a full, evenly occupied ring without digits, numerals, tally marks, or a readable map.

### `018_resources_found_no_men_no_guns`

An empty human barracks and empty rifle rack occupy the dim upper background, with no soldiers and no weapons in use. Beneath them, bright mineral veins organize several disciplined Oth-Kesh broods into formation from the ground itself. Make the contrast between abandoned human manpower and resource-born formations immediately readable.

### `018_resources_found_moving_mountain`

A colossal Stone Phalanx of interlocking layered Oth-Kesh carapaces advances like a moving mountain beneath incoming period anti-tank fire. The central low armored body is supported by overlapping shield-like mineral plates and a dense formation behind it. Show impact sparks and dust but no gore and no human insignia.

### `018_resources_found_front_has_a_floor`

A dramatic cutaway of a fortified surface line, bunker, and supply depot above ground while a disciplined armored Oth-Kesh force drives through a prepared tunnel directly beneath the defenses and rises behind them. The underground route must be the central subject and the surface fortification must remain readable at small size.

### `018_resources_found_hills_begin_to_move`

A broad rocky ridgeline fractures into a fast tide of many lighter mineral-armored Oth-Kesh silhouettes descending together through dust and loose scree. The hill itself seems to become the advancing formation. Favor sweeping diagonal motion and repeated clean silhouettes over tiny individual detail.

### `018_resources_found_continental_appetite`

A generic, non-identifiable continent-shaped stone slab fills the center. A connected luminous underground vein network crosses it from one deep origin chamber to several physical breach craters at distant coasts, while a heavy Oth-Kesh command silhouette anchors the lower edge. Avoid any recognizable real continent or political borders.

### `018_resources_found_ground_quiet_again`

A silent sealed chasm lies beneath a repaired period industrial skyline at calm dawn. Multinational reconstruction is suggested by varied engineering equipment rather than flags. A subdued globe-like stone arc with several healed seams sits behind the skyline, while the foreground seal is intact and free of weapons or living Oth-Kesh. The mood is costly global survival and lasting quiet.

## Variant derivation

For each accepted completed master, the processed completed image is a centered `64x64` opaque square. The grey variant is an exact grayscale conversion of that completed image. The not-eligible variant is the grey image with `.agents/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/assets/achievements/overlay.png` composited at its native `64x64` alignment. No variant is independently generated, red-tinted, or redrawn.
