# Event 091 SE01 archival Lenin scene source

## Selection and source record

Selected for a separate archival Lenin identity plate: **Lenin addressing Red Army troops in Moscow on 5 May 1920**, photographed by **Grigory Petrovich Goldstein (1870–1941)**. The image shows Lenin speaking from a wooden platform above a crowd. At the 457x328 consumer size his face and speaking pose remain readable, and the crowd and platform supply an actual historical public-address scene.

## Fit and remaining limits

The parent visually reviewed this 457x328 crop on 2026-10-13 and passed its readable Lenin/crowd context and lack of introduced artifacts. It is an archival source placeholder, not a final image of the fictional Worldwide Opening. The photograph depicts Lenin addressing troops preparing for the Polish front; it has no organic third eye, tools, or distinct revolutionary regions. It must not be presented as documentary evidence of the alternate-history global event. The accepted SE01 brief still requires a separate composition covering those event-specific elements.

The repository already has a separate Lenin leader-portrait source package under `docs/assets/portraits/091_the_great_revolution/leader_vladimir_lenin/` recorded in the 2026-10-13 progress note. This scene source is a different Goldstein photograph for a distinct 457x328 composition; it does not copy, replace, restyle, or wire the portrait source package or current portrait consumer.
