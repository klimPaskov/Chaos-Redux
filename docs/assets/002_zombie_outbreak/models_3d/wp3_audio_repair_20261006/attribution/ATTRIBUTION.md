## Level contract

The WP3 source brief says shared zombie moans should be targeted at -20 LUFS as loops, while the WP3 shared-voice line requests a -3 dBFS sample peak. Section 3.5 of `unit_fix_plan.md` also requires at most -1 dBTP and the shared loop edge gate. These measurements are compatible for the accepted installed moan02 and the staged peak-limited moan01 and Colodical candidates. The parent installed moan01's -3 dBFS derivative after the worker's continuation; it is reproducible from the retained accepted preimage with a 1.988527 dB maximum recorded-sample attenuation and retains -20.5 LUFS and a 2.769 dB loop-edge delta. The current runtime SHA is `76661F92D59F934FD5D9AA66EFDB401B2718F674A8AA5053BDCFBCB63A33C0CE`; parent CAS and installed-inspection receipts are in `docs/plans/unit_fix_2026-10/wp3_evidence/`. The additional Colodical candidate retains -20.7 LUFS, -3.0 dBFS sample peak, -2.1 dBTP, and a 0.801 dB edge delta. Moan03 remains unchanged because recorded-sample crossfades did not produce a loop-valid file. Exact limiter commands, paths, hashes, and receipts are in `evidence/wp3_audio_repair_resumed_receipts.json`.
