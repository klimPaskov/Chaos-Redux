## Source and visual notes

- The report source is an original, fictional alternate-history documentary scene. Generation is appropriate because no real event or real person must be represented. Its 1936–1945 era fit comes from the period quay, uniforms, caps, steam cargo vessel, harbor crane, customs procedure, masonry, and black-and-white press-photograph treatment.
- The focus, decision, and idea families were prompted and generated independently for their actual UI sizes. Coordinated concepts share lore symbols, but no processed icon is a resized copy of another family.
- `P-F08` records one rejected source candidate and the selected corrective edit. The rejected candidate remains outside the repository and is not a runtime asset.
- Runtime DDS files are uncompressed 32-bit BGRA with one image level and exact processed-PNG pixel equality.
- No fallback, placeholder, sourced substitute, flat monogram, or transform-only derivative was used.
