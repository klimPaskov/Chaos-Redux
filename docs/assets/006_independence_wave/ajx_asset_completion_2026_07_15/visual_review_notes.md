# Event 006 AJX asset visual review

Review date: `2026-07-15`

## Advisor dossiers

All three cards were inspected at native `65x67`, at 5x nearest-neighbour
scale, beside all three canonical vanilla dossier references, and after
decoding the actual final DDS.

- Mine-and-rail dispatch superintendent: approved. The narrow ash-blond face,
  asymmetrical brows, alert gaze, and slate dispatch jacket remain readable.
- Cross-border accounts comptroller: approved. The gaunt face, deep-set eyes,
  narrow moustache, and half-rim spectacles remain distinct from the other
  Event 006 advisors.
- Factory-security inspector: approved. The broad civilian face, dark low bun,
  direct gaze, and herringbone coat remain readable without suggesting a
  soldier, commander, police poster, or collective institution.

Every face remains clear above and to the left of the paper slip. The paper is
small enough not to become a generic UI panel. The worn dark bevel, angled
paper, seal, transparent corners, restrained value range, and head-and-
shoulders silhouettes remain consistent with the canonical family.

## Municipal Neutral Commission focus

The focus icon was inspected at native `94x86`, at 5x nearest-neighbour scale,
beside all three canonical vanilla focus examples, beside three installed
Event 006 focus precedents, and after decoding the final DDS.

Approved findings:

- the level scales remain the first readable subject;
- the civic hall distinguishes municipal authority from a generic legal icon;
- the rail arc and coal facets preserve the Saar industrial identity;
- the small blue-white-red civic shield remains subordinate to the central
  commission emblem;
- the icon has transparent unused canvas and clean corners, with no green
  fringe, opaque square panel, white halo, checker pixels, text, weapons,
  crown, eagle, or military insignia;
- the native silhouette is distinct from every shared Event 006 focus icon
  inspected.
