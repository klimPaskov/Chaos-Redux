Source masters, design-reference inputs, processed PNG ladders, contact sheets, validation JSON, and SHA-256 records are retained under this package directory. All final TGAs are uncompressed true-colour 32-bit BGRA, bottom-left origin, with dimensions `82x52`, `41x26`, and `10x7`.

COX and CQX are generated civic syntheses from regional/heraldic references rather than claims of an attested 1936 national standard. CVX is a generated script-free civic adaptation of the documented Najdi green/sword relationship. CXX is a generated Hadhramaut civic synthesis from the Kathiri regional palette and stars. The other five designs follow the cited historical/regional geometry as recorded in `metadata/flag_validation.json` and `manifest.md`.
