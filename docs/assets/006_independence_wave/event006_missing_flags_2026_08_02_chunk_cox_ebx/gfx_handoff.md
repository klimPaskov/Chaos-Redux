COX, CQX, CVX, CXX, DAX, DFX, HYX, DKX, DLX, DPX, DVX, and EBX are generated civic or regional syntheses rather than claims of attested 1936 national standards. DBX, CUX, CWX, CYX, and DHX follow cited historical flag geometry. CPX uses Pontic heraldic reconstruction. The design basis and uncertainty notes for every tag are recorded in `metadata/flag_validation.json` and `manifest.md`.
