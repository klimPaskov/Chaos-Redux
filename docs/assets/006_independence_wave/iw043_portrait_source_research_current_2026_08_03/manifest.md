## New candidate: Hadi Atlasi

**Muhammed-Hadi Miftakhutdinovich Atlasi (Hadi Atlasi, 1876-1938)** was a Tatar public figure, historian-Turkologist, pedagogue, publicist, and deputy of the Second State Duma in 1907. The available biography identifies him as Tatar and records that he was alive during the 1936-centered scenario until his death on 15 February 1938. Those civic, educational, and political roles fit the Bolgar civic-presidium consumer better than the river-security role, although the fictional office transfer remains a parent design decision rather than a historical appointment.
