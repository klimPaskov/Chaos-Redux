# Event 006 EEX-IAX flag design research

This chunk covers the eighteen missing base flag ladders `EEX`, `EHX`, `ERX`, `ESX`, `EWX`, `FAX`, `FBX`, `FDX`, `FLX`, `FNX`, `FOX`, `FSX`, `FUX`, `FVX`, `FXX`, `GBX`, `GCX`, and `IAX`. The Event 006 registry and research-resolution matrix are the identity authority:

- `docs/specs/006_independence_wave_specs/matrices/006_candidate_country_registry.csv`
- `docs/specs/006_independence_wave_specs/research/006_package_research_resolution.csv`
- `docs/plans/006_independence_wave_plans/asset_research/006_package_asset_coverage.md`

All final designs are `$imagegen` flat graphic source masters. The linked pages below are research references for motif ownership, regional context, and date fit only; no source pixels are shipped or traced. The generated masters are original ImageGen outputs and the generated-art rights/provenance record is the prompt file plus the SHA-256 metadata. Wikipedia pages are used under their page licenses (CC BY-SA/GFDL as applicable); this does not transfer a license to generated art or turn a modern flag into a 1936 historical standard.
