# Saved source-image rights and limits

Only files with an explicit redistribution licence are stored here. Their SHA-256 hashes and exact attribution text are in `../source_manifest.csv`.

## Important authority limits

- The Sardinian manuscript image is a historical artifact, but the official regional history warns that the Sardinian entry may have been added after the manuscript's 1370-1386 compilation.
- The second Sardinian file is a modern, self-published reconstruction. Its CC0 status makes it easy to redistribute, not historically authoritative.
- The Sicilian SVG is a modern reconstruction. Its flesh-and-gold Trinacria does not match the Ministry of Culture catalog description of the surviving 1848 S.015 flag, whose central Trinacria is embroidered in gold.
- The Trieste SVG is a modern reconstruction with a disputed file history. The specialist source's Plate 45, figure 20 shows a simpler centred corsesca and must control the later reconstruction.
- The Banat image is the full Romanian royal arms. Its bridge-and-lion quarter is Romanian state heraldry from 1921, not a Banat Republic flag and not a neutral multiethnic standard.

Official regional images, Italian catalog-object images, the State Archive of Palermo scans, the FIAV paper, and the Banat heraldry article were not copied into this folder where their image-reuse rights were unclear, limited, or all-rights-reserved. They remain link-only evidence in `../historical_evidence.md`.

Share-alike files may impose obligations on visually derived output. A later production pass must either comply with those licences, use the files only for human research while reconstructing from public-domain historical facts, or obtain another cleared source. The source researcher does not decide the mod's distribution licence.
