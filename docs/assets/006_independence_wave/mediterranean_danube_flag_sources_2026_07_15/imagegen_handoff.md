# Event 006 Mediterranean and Danube ImageGen handoff

## Commissioning gate

This is a prompt and review packet for a later asset-producing agent. It does not authorize runtime production by itself. The later agent must use `chaos-redux-event-assets`; use `chaos-redux-frame-animation` only if animation is separately requested. Each final flag must be produced through ImageGen even when the goal is a strict flat reconstruction.

**No flag or package covered here is content-ready.** Trieste has the strongest executable candidate, Sardinia requires approval of a declared fictional treatment, Sicily's exact-object candidate is route-specific, and Banat is blocked pending a parent decision.

## Universal generation prompt

Append the package-specific brief to this exact control language:

> Create one flat orthographic flag design that fills the entire frame. Use a clean vector-like construction with hard edges, a limited flat palette, and no antialias-dependent fine detail. Show no flagpole, waving, folds, cloth texture, lighting, gradients, shadows, bevel, embroidery texture, room, sky, mockup, caption, letters, numerals, date, signature, watermark, or sheet of alternatives. Do not invent any additional emblem, seal, star, crown, wreath, weapon, sacred symbol, border, or text. Preserve the requested symbol count, placement, and orientation exactly. Keep every critical stroke and gap thick enough to remain recognizable at 10 by 7 pixels. Generate only the flag face.

A working 41:26 or 3:2 field may be used for exploration when the historical ratio is unresolved, but it must be logged as a production normalization. The final source master should be reviewed before conversion to 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7.

## IW-018 Sardinia / ARX

### Approval state

Do not execute until the parent approves the fictional baseline treatment. The generated result must be catalogued as an alternate-history civic synthesis, never as an attested 1936 flag.

### Reference inputs

- `source_images/sardinia_gelre_armorial_folio_62r.png`: primary-artifact view of the motif; do not reproduce manuscript texture.
- `source_images/sardinia_traditional_four_moors_reference.png`: modern geometry aid showing a different historical interpretation; do not copy its blindfolded, hoist-facing arrangement as authoritative.
- Official history and catalog evidence in `historical_evidence.md` control the prompt.

### Candidate prompt

> Create an explicitly fictional 1936 Sardinian civic flag synthesis based on attested Four Moors motifs. Use a plain white field with one medium-width red St George cross reaching all four edges. Place exactly one simplified black profile head in each of the four white quarters, four heads total. Pair the two left heads facing right and the two right heads facing left so all four face inward toward the cross. Give each head a simple white band tied around the forehead while leaving the eyes visibly open. Keep all four heads identical in scale and treatment and centered within their quarters. Use only white, red, black, and the minimum flat skin/band separation needed for readability. Do not use blindfolds, Savoy arms, a crown, shield, Italian tricolour, eagle, stars, lettering, or ornamental manuscript texture.

### Mandatory review rejects

- any head faces away from the cross or has a band over the eyes;
- any number other than four heads or one cross;
- Savoy/SPM, Italian, royal, religious, or generic ideology additions;
- a claim in the manifest that the design is historically attested in 1936;
- facial detail that collapses into noise at 10x7.

## IW-019 Sicily / ASX

### Approval state

The brief below is executable only for the constitutional-independence route represented by the 1848 S.015 national colour. It is not approved as an ideology-neutral baseline. Confirm the normalized ratio and the Trinacria rotation/silhouette before retention.

### Reference inputs

- `source_images/sicily_1848_national_flag_reference.svg`: modern layout aid only. Its flesh-and-gold emblem treatment does not match the catalogued object's all-gold embroidery description.
- The Palermo archive decree chronology and the Ministry S.015 catalog record in `historical_evidence.md` control the reconstruction.

### Exact-object normalization prompt

> Reconstruct the flat field of the surviving 1848 Sicilian national flag S.015 as a clean orthographic flag. Divide the field into exactly three equal vertical bands: green at the hoist, warm ecru-white in the centre, and red at the fly. Center exactly one simplified Trinacria emblem within the middle band. Render the entire Trinacria as one flat gold shape, including its central head and three bent legs, with no flesh colour and no separate multicolour details. Keep the three legs evenly spaced and preserve the reviewed source orientation. Omit the surviving object's fringe, tassels, pole, cravat, inscription, and embroidery texture. Do not use the post-2000 diagonal red-yellow field, a Bourbon/TTS shield, crown, eagle, fasces, star, lettering, or extra symbols.

### Mandatory review rejects

- flesh-coloured or multicolour Trinacria instead of the catalogued all-gold treatment;
- horizontal bands, a diagonal red-yellow field, or green anywhere except the hoist band;
- fringe, tassels, cravat, or text treated as part of the flat field;
- use for crown, labor, military, patron-client, or neutral baseline without a separate approved ownership decision;
- unreviewed leg rotation or detail too fine for the HOI4 small flag.

## IW-021 Trieste Free State / ICX

### Approval state

This is the strongest exact civic candidate, but retention still requires silhouette review against Fabretto Plate 45, figure 20, plus documented ratio and red-colour normalization.

### Reference inputs

- `source_images/trieste_free_territory_flag_reference.svg`: licensed layout aid only; its elongated emblem silhouette is disputed in its own file history and is not sole authority.
- Fabretto's FIAV study and the treaty/civic sources linked in `historical_evidence.md` control form and function.

### Exact civic reconstruction prompt

> Reconstruct the traditional City of Trieste civic flag as a flat orthographic design suitable for the Free Territory package. Use one solid red field and exactly one centered white-to-silver corsesca emblem. Match the compact historic silhouette in Fabretto Plate 45, figure 20: a slender vertical central spear, two symmetrical side blades curving upward from a compact joint, and a short lower shaft. Keep the emblem upright, centered, balanced, and large enough to remain recognizable at 10 by 7 pixels. Use only red and white/silver. Do not turn the corsesca into an elongated modern logo, anchor, fleur-de-lis, trident, sword, or shield. Do not add a United Nations emblem, eagle, crown, stars, stripes, Italian or Yugoslav colours, lettering, border, or full civic arms.

### Mandatory review rejects

- silhouette not checked against Fabretto figure 20;
- any emblem count other than one or any tilt away from vertical;
- a UN overlay, full shield, Habsburg device, Italian tricolour, or Yugoslav star;
- narrow side blades or gaps that disappear at 10x7;
- ratio or digital red presented as a dated historical fact without source support.

## IW-024 Banat / AXX

### Approval state

**Blocked. Do not execute a Banat ImageGen prompt yet.** No attested flag was located in the consulted authoritative sources for the 1918 Banat Republic. Never feed an internet reconstruction into ImageGen and then describe the output as authentic.

The parent must choose and document one of the following politically owned strategies before a production agent writes the final prompt.

### Option GATE: declared fictional federal/commission synthesis

Potential basis: the Forforosa Gate/fortress motif from Habsburg Banat administrative heraldry. This can proceed only after explicit approval to strip imperial and confessional ownership and declare the result fictional.

If approved, the eventual brief should require one highly simplified, symmetric gate or fortress with two openings/towers on a flat field; a restricted period palette; no crown, eagles, motto, eye, religious apparatus, palisade clutter, modern border, or text. The exact structure, field colour, and ownership statement must be approved before generation. It cannot be called the Banat Republic's historical flag.

### Option BRIDGE_LION: Romanian-aligned route only

Potential basis: the 1921 Romanian state quarter with natural waves, a gold two-arched Trajan bridge, and a gold emerging lion on red. This is not suitable for a neutral multiethnic baseline. If an explicitly Romanian-aligned route is later approved, the design must contain no sword, because the sword is a post-1992 addition. The saved `source_images/banat_1921_romanian_arms_reference.jpg` is reference evidence, not a flag face to crop.

### Permanently rejected shortcuts

- any web image labelled as a 1918 Banat Republic flag without an archive, museum, surviving object, law, or scholarly chain of evidence;
- the 1849 Serbian Voivodeship sword-and-severed-head lion used as a neutral emblem;
- the Romanian bridge-and-lion quarter stripped of its Romanian political ownership;
- the 1992 sword added to a 1921-derived lion;
- flags built from generic Romanian, Serbian, Hungarian, Habsburg, communist, fascist, international, or religious colour coding;
- generated text, mottos, dates, maps, shields full of tiny quartering, or generic sacred symbols.
