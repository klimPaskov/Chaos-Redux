# ARX Event 006 role evidence v15

## Emilio Lussu

[Italian Wikipedia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilio_Lussu) identifies Emilio Lussu as a Sardinian politician, soldier, writer, and Partito Sardo d'Azione / Partito d'Azione figure who was alive in 1936.

The current ARX civic route already owns the `ARX_emilio_lussu` consumer.

The Senate-sourced headshot is a clearer, unobstructed male head-and-shoulders identity source than the prior 1916 trial source, but the Commons record only says `before 1958` and does not establish a 1936 capture date.

## Luigi Arborio Mella di Sant'Elia

[Italian Wikipedia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luigi_Arborio_Mella_di_Sant%27Elia) identifies him as born in Sassari, son of Alessandro Arborio Mella (military governor of Sassari), Grand Master of Court Ceremonies for Vittorio Emanuele III at age twenty-five, and confidant of Queen Margherita.

The same biography records his First World War service as a major, later parliamentary service, and life through 1936; his Senate appointment began in 1939, so the 1936 role should be described as a Sardinian-born royal-court or conservative council representative rather than retroactively calling him a 1936 senator.

Wikidata records his birth as 1873-12-02 and death as 1955-06-26.

The image itself is a small but clear formal head-and-shoulders Senate portrait with court sash and decorations, and its Commons date is bounded only before his death.

## Giuseppe Valle

[Italian Wikipedia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Valle) identifies Valle as born in Sassari and serving as Chief of Staff of the Regia Aeronautica from 22 March 1934 to 10 November 1939, making him alive and active in the 1936 start window.

The 1936 *Grande enciclopedia aeronautica* source is period-matched and visually strong, but the exact Kaiserreich `SRD_giuseppe_valle` owner blocks direct reuse without a guarded transfer outside this scope.

## Giuseppe Pizzorno

[Italian Wikipedia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Pizzorno) identifies Pizzorno as born in Cagliari in 1891, a Regio Esercito general, commander of Brigata Sassari, participant in East Africa operations, and a 1935-1936 operational commander.

The Commons source is rights-tagged `PD-Italy` plus `PD-1996` and has no owner collision, but the only portrait is a 145x160 side-profile thumbnail with an unknown capture date, so it is blocked for production-quality identity preservation.

## Vittorio Vernè

[Generals.dk](https://generals.dk/general/Vern%C3%A8/Vittorio/Italy.html) and [Italian Wikipedia](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vittorio_Vern%C3%A8) document Vernè as a real Italian major-general commanding formations during the 1936 East African war, including the 5th Blackshirt Division from 12 August 1936 until his death in January 1937.

The prior ARX source audit documents a Sardinia-linked formation connection, but Vernè was born in Rome and must not be labelled Sardinian-born.

If the parent accepts a Sardinia-linked territorial/military commander, Vernè is the only presently source-ready collision-free command candidate in this package.
