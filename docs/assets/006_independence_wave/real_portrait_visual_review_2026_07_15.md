## Josef Friedrich Matthes — approved

Identity invariants surviving the final crop: broad square face, compact jaw
and cheeks, deep brow, direct serious gaze, nose and mouth proportions, dark
beret shape/angle, black bow tie, white shirt, textured tweed lapels, and the
source-visible breast card. The presentation is tight at 156x210 and remains
within the canonical vanilla leader value range and quiet-background framing.

## Rupprecht of Bavaria — approved after correction

The first edit was rejected because the moustache had too much vertical
volume. The corrected edit restores the source's thin, close-trimmed horizontal
moustache and preserves the long narrow face, high receding hairline, ear and
eye placement, straight nose, stern expression, high embroidered collar,
epaulettes, shoulder chain, neck orders, and upper medal row. The 156x210 crop
keeps the face dominant while retaining the Bavarian dynastic/military route
identity.

## Francois Debeauvais — blocked

The rights-cleared 1928 source fails identity fidelity at portrait scale. The
sharper 1932 candidate lacks a defensible US rights basis and the 1933
candidate's rights record is internally contradictory. No image edit was
attempted and the rejected processed PNG, runtime DDS, and sprite registration
were removed.
