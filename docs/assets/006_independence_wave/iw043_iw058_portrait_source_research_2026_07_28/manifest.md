## Repaint and processing boundary

The retained crops remain immutable identity evidence. Five rows are still source-only because their status gates are blocked or need review. Galimzhan is the exception: the parent-authorized repaint and processor candidate are retained above as no-wire evidence, but no DDS or runtime package was produced and the candidate remains blocked on independent audit and prompt-provenance review.

If a parent-authorized producer advances a `source_ready` row, use the exact crop as the identity reference, the canonical leader references under `.agents/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/assets/vanilla_reference/portraits/leaders/` for style only, and the mandatory source-locked identity-preserving HOI4 leader workflow. The producer must preserve facial geometry, asymmetry, age, expression, hair/facial hair, visible hat or uniform, and source-visible medals; it must not invent hidden clothing, rank marks, religious symbols, or institutional insignia.

The later pipeline must retain the unchanged master, this exact crop and equality JSON, raw repaint result, original-size repaint master, deterministic 156x210 candidate, review sheet, independent likeness/style/provenance audit, and final DDS in separate paths. A source-only row is not runtime-ready until a reviewer other than the producer passes identity, style, provenance, rights, and era gates.
