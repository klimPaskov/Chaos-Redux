Accepted for runtime installation on 2026-07-16. ## Review criteria

- Male-only presentation and distinct identity: passed.
- Painted, matte HOI4 character rather than glossy photographic finish: passed.
- Period-appropriate civilian or military clothing: passed.
- Restrained background that supports, but does not compete with, the face: passed.
- Clear silhouette, face placement, and contrast at runtime dimensions: passed.
- Commander-small derivation from the matching new master with dossier transparency preserved: passed.
- No advisor-icon production or wiring: passed.
