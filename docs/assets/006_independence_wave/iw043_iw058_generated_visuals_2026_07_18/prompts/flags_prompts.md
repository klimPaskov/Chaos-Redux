# IW-043 / IW-058 ImageGen flag prompts

All ten masters were generated independently with the built-in ImageGen path. Each prompt required a clean, orthographic, flat flag rectangle with solid geometry, no fabric, folds, pole, perspective, lighting, shadows, gradients, scenery, text, watermark, or modern flag copy. Masters were then cover-cropped and resized mechanically into the HOI4 82x52 / 41x26 / 10x7 ladder; no emblem was locally redrawn.

## CHU_independence_wave_middle_volga_congressX

Fictional 1936 Middle Volga civic-congress flag: deep river-blue field; two or three restrained horizontal limestone-white and muted-green river lines; one small neutral open civic ring/seal; equal-weight geometry expressing plural negotiation. No medieval crown, khan/tamga, universal crescent, ethnic hierarchy, or military eagle.

## CHU_independence_wave_volga_bulgariaX

Fictional Volga Bulgaria restoration-route flag: warm limestone/cream field with river teal, muted leaf green, and a narrow amber accent; one small simplified arch/minaret or masonry-ring motif as a civic archaeological reference, never a literal monument or ancient flag claim. No crown, khan portrait, tamga, pan-Tatar claim, or copied reconstruction.

## CHU_independence_wave_volga_federationX

Fictional inclusive Volga federation flag: cool blue field; equal cream and forest-green modules; four or five linked river segments/nodes around an open circle. Equal institutions and waterways only; no one-people emblem, single religion symbol, military banner, or disputed Idel-Ural tamga.

## VOLGA_URAL_FEDERATIONX

Distinct fictional Volga-Ural consent federation flag: dark blue field; interlocking double-river knot or four equal bars forming an architectural ring; cream, muted green, and quiet rust accents. Broader and more architectural than the CHU federal seal; no pan-Turkic or imperial shorthand, crown, exclusive crest, or copied 1918 reconstruction.

## IDEL_URAL_COMPACTX

Fictional negotiated Idel-Ural Compact flag informed only by the contested blue/river discussion: neutral blue field; open double-river circle or compact civic bridge in cream and muted green. The FOTW/Wikimedia reconstruction is explicitly not authentic and was not copied; no recognizable tamga, medieval emblem, crown, or modern Tatarstan design.

No cross-like motif, church attribution, Levies badge, winged disk, Ashur, current Assyrian flag copy, or post-1968/1973 AUA design.

## MESOPOTAMIAN_FEDERATIONX

No winged disk, Lamassu, Ashur, direct 1921 Iraqi tricolor, Arab-only or Assyrian-only symbol, cuneiform text, or post-1968/1973 AUA flag.
