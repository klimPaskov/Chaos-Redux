## Role and identity evidence

- The archived Chuvash Encyclopedia entry identifies Spasov as a state official, records Red Army service in 1919–1926, dates his Chuvash ASSR SNK chairmanship to February 1931–February 1932 and September 1937–July 1938, and records his deputy-chairman role in the Nizhny Novgorod/Gorky regional executive committee in 1932–1937: https://web.archive.org/web/20240518043403/http://enc.cap.ru/?t=prsn&lnk=4532
- The same official entry places him in Middle Volga territorial administration during the 1936 baseline and records participation in the First World War, Civil War, and Great Patriotic War.
- A secondary biography records his wartime political/military ranks as battalion commissar, senior battalion commissar, and lieutenant-colonel of the quartermaster service. These facts support a security/defense-administration consumer without claiming that he held a literal river-police post: https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%A1%D0%BF%D0%B0%D1%81%D0%BE%D0%B2,_%D0%9B%D1%83%D0%BA%D0%B0_%D0%A1%D0%B5%D0%BC%D1%91%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87
- The Chuvash ASSR lies on the east bank of the Volga. The regional administrative and military record therefore meets the requested Chuvash/Middle Volga territorial-administration, military, and regional-defense tie for the River Security Directorate role without inferring personal ethnicity.
