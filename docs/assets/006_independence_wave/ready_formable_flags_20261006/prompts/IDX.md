# IDX — Indus Federation flag

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original alternate-history national flag for the Indus Federation, intended to remain recognizable at HOI4 flag sizes 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7. Primary request: create a clean, flat, full-canvas rectangular flag with a teal river-blue field and one original, balanced civic emblem: a golden geometric river braid crossing a narrow abstract rail bridge, integrating two parallel land-and-water strokes into a compact, distinctive federation mark. The braid and bridge must be visibly separate but connected, using clear heraldic geometry that survives small-size reduction. Composition: exact wide flag proportion close to 82:52 (about 1.58:1), full-bleed opaque field to all four edges, centered emblem within generous safe margins and occupying roughly two-thirds of canvas height. Style: crisp 2D graphic flag, restrained period-compatible colors, solid color fills, clean strong edges. Constraints: fictional negotiated federation symbol, not an attested historical flag; no chakra, religious, or national cues and no copying of real flags or symbols. No text, letters, numbers, stars, borders, frames, badges, extra symbols, flagpole, fabric, folds, waving, perspective, shadows, gradients, vignettes, painterly rendering, modern objects, or surrounding scene.
