# MRX — Maritime Federation flag

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original alternate-history national flag for the Maritime Federation, intended to remain recognizable at HOI4 flag sizes 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7. Primary request: create a clean, flat, full-canvas rectangular flag on a dark navy-ocean field, with one simple, balanced emblem: a warm-gold sun above a chain of exactly three simplified island silhouettes, joined by one continuous elegant arc that can read as both a sail and a wave. Keep the three islands distinct and the connecting arc singular, with a compact heraldic silhouette suitable for a maritime federation. Composition: exact wide flag proportion close to 82:52 (about 1.58:1), full-bleed opaque field to all four edges, emblem centered within generous safe margins and occupying roughly two-thirds of canvas height. Style: crisp 2D graphic flag, restrained period-compatible colors, solid color fills, clean strong edges, no unnecessary detail. Constraints: fictional negotiated federation symbol, not an attested historical flag; original island and wave shapes, no copied national or religious symbols. No text, letters, numbers, stars, borders, frames, badges, extra islands, flagpole, fabric, folds, waving, perspective, shadows, gradients, vignettes, painterly rendering, modern objects, or surrounding scene.
