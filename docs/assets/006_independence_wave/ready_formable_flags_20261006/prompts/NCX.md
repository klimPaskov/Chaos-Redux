# NCX — North Caucasian Federation flag

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original alternate-history national flag for the North Caucasian Federation, intended to remain recognizable at HOI4 flag sizes 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7. Primary request: create a clean, flat, full-canvas rectangular flag design with a deep slate-blue field and one centered, distinctive civic emblem: three pale snow peaks linked by a warm amber ridge that also reads as a defensive arc, with a small off-white starless sun above the central mountain pass. The mountain forms and linked valleys should be angular and balanced, with enough precise heraldic construction to read as an authored federation symbol. Composition: exact wide flag proportion close to 82:52 (about 1.58:1), full-bleed opaque field to all four edges, emblem centrally placed within generous safe margins and occupying roughly two-thirds of canvas height. Style: crisp 2D graphic flag, restrained period-compatible colors, solid color fills, clean strong edges, no distressed texture. Constraints: fictional negotiated federation symbol, not an attested historical flag; do not copy any real national or religious glyph. No text, letters, numbers, stars, borders, frames, badges, extra symbols, flagpole, fabric, folds, waving, perspective, shadows, gradients, vignettes, painterly rendering, modern objects, or surrounding scene.
