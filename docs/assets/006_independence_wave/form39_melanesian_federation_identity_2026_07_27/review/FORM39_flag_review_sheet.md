# FORM-39 flag review sheet

Review asset: `FORM39_flag_contact_sheet.png`.

Candidate status: `needs_user_review`.

The ImageGen source is a single flat, orthographic flag face with no readable text, fabric, pole, perspective, scene, or watermark. The retained source master is shown beside the processed 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7 ladder outputs. The normal and medium panels preserve the centered three-lobed emblem and gold maritime band. The small panel is enlarged with nearest-neighbour solely for inspection; it retains a compact light three-lobed mark over the gold band but should be independently checked at native 10x7 before runtime admission.

Processing did not redraw, trace, recolor, or replace the generated emblem. Runtime TGAs use the vanilla bottom-left origin convention and are byte-identical to the package copies.

The only open acceptance issue is identity/wiring: FORM-39 remains registry-only and `MFX` is a proposed reserved X-tag candidate pending the FIJ/PNG/WPG consent-led adapter, member research gates, final tag choice, and collision tests. No gameplay or `.gfx` wiring is claimed by this package.
