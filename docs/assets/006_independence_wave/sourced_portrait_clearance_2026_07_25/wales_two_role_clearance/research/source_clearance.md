# Wales alternative portrait source clearance research

## Research decision

The failed David Grenfell and George Cornwallis-West source photographs were not retried. A different civic photograph with stronger face geometry and a different commander photograph with unmistakable period uniform detail were required. The selected pair is W. J. Gruffydd and Lewis Pugh Evans.

## W. J. Gruffydd source evidence

- Commons file page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:W._J._Gruffydd.jpg
- Unchanged master URL: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a8/W._J._Gruffydd.jpg
- Commons metadata snapshot: `source_page_snapshots/w_j_gruffydd_commons_api.json`.
- Commons describes the image as Cardiff University Welsh Department academic staff, 1946, and links archive item `https://archive.org/details/CUA00764`.
- Commons records Cardiff University as the artist/attribution and `CC BY-SA 4.0` as the license. Attribution is required; retain the Cardiff University and archive credit in downstream provenance.
- The source is 3,070×3,962 and evenly readable. The head, glasses, brow, nose, mouth, ear geometry, shoulders, pipe and civilian jacket are visible without reconstruction. The source is postwar, so it is a strong identity reference but not a 1936 documentary photograph.

## Lewis Pugh Evans source evidence

- Commons file page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lewis_Pugh_Evans_VC_IWM_HU_93411.jpg
- Unchanged master URL: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c1/Lewis_Pugh_Evans_VC_IWM_HU_93411.jpg
- Commons page snapshot: `source_page_snapshots/lewis_pugh_evans_commons_file_page.html`.
- Commons describes a photograph of Lieutenant Colonel Lewis Pugh Evans VC, circa 1918, sourced from Imperial War Museums collection no. 2500-02, HU 93411. The page attributes the photograph to H. Walter Barnett and marks it `{{PD-Old}}` / Public Domain.
- Identity evidence: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Pugh_Evans records Evans as born at Abermad, Cardiganshire, Wales, and records later brigadier service plus Military Liaison Officer duty at the Headquarters of the Wales Region during the Second World War.
- The source is 605×800 and shows a single person in period uniform with clear eyes, ears, jaw, moustache, cap, tunic, shoulder details and rank/medal bars. The crop retains the face and shoulders without resampling. The image is circa 1918, so later treatment must age the subject for the 1936 scenario without changing identity geometry.

## Processing evidence

Both decoded PNG masters were produced as lossless RGB decodes of the downloaded JPEG masters. Both head-and-shoulders crops were made exclusively with `.agents/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/tools/extract_portrait_source_crop.py`. The retained JSON files prove exact decoded-pixel equality between each decoded master rectangle and its crop output. The contact sheet compares each immutable master and exact crop for review.

No ImageGen, resizing, enhancement, recolouring, DDS conversion, advisor/dossier processing, or runtime wiring was performed in this package.
