# IW-006 Walloon commander source clearance retry 03

This package supplies a sourced real-person commander candidate for the Walloon Defence Council mountain/commander portrait request. The candidate is Louis Hubert baron Ruquoy, also spelled Louis Rucquoy in period sources.

## Selection

Ruquoy was born on 3 November 1861 at Frasnes-lez-Buissenal in Hainaut and died on 24 January 1937 at Braine-l'Alleud, so he was alive throughout 1936. He was a Belgian lieutenant-general, commanded the 5th Army Division, became Chief of the General Staff on 6 January 1917, and later commanded Belgian occupation forces in the Rhineland. His Hainaut birthplace provides the Walloon connection required for this route.

The military fit is strong for a senior council commander or veteran strategist. He was retired by 1927, so this package does not claim that he was an active Belgian staff chief in 1936; the event should frame him as an elder commander or security council figure if the parent selects him.

## Selected archival master and rights

The selected immutable master is `source_masters/ruquoy_rol_1923_group.jpg`, an 8419 x 6051 Agence Rol press photograph from Brussels dated 12 March 1923 and preserved by the Bibliotheque nationale de France in Gallica as item `btv1b531010537`. The full caption identifies the center figure as general Rucquoy, between general Henri Maglinse and Defence Minister Albert Devèze. The exact source crop isolates that center figure.

The Commons Gallica record marks the source public domain under PD France, PD-1996, and PD US expired. Preserve this attribution in any later distribution: `Agence Rol / Bibliotheque nationale de France, Gallica, item btv1b531010537.` The agency attribution is the available photographer/provenance record; the original negative photographer is not separately named.
