## Historical evidence

The French-language identity snapshot records Ruquoy's Hainaut birthplace, 1937 death, 1917 General Staff appointment, 5th Division command, and later Belgian occupation-force command. The 1923 Agence Rol/BnF image is period-matched and shows him as a senior uniformed military figure. His retirement in 1927 means the event should use a veteran or council-commander framing rather than claim active General Staff service in 1936.

The 1917 *Le Pays de France* and *Le Miroir* views are retained as independent appearance and uniform corroboration. The former is a CC BY-SA 3.0 Garitan scan; the latter carries a disputed-copyright-information warning and is not a selected rights-cleared source.
