# ASX Sicily grounded army-command source retry

Date: 2026-07-22  
Scope: IW-019 (ASX Sicily), separate male army/corps-command portrait  
Owner: sourced visual-asset subagent  
Disposition: source research and ownership gate complete; the only legally
cleared identity is carried forward to the separate `sicily_di_benedetto_trial_01`
refinish package. No runtime, GFX, character, localisation, or gameplay file is
changed here.

## Gate and outcome

Sicily is a grounded historical package. A candidate must be a real male person,
have a defensible Sicilian birth or Sicily-command connection, be alive and
plausibly usable in the 1936 setting, have a direct source URL and explicit
reuse basis, and not already be an active character/portrait owner in installed
vanilla or current Chaos Redux. The package must not use a generated, generic,
female, advisor, dossier, or `_small` substitute.

No exact, rights-clear military-uniform replacement survived all gates. The
parent has approved Vincenzo Di Benedetto as a deliberate emergency-role
adaptation: a retired Sicilian-region general recalled for the independence
crisis. That is not a claim that he held an active 1936 command. His unchanged
public-domain civilian suit portrait is preserved for the separate source-locked
refinish package; the source itself is not altered here.

## Ownership checks

Exact and variant names were searched in current Chaos Redux and installed
vanilla character definitions, country-history recruitment, leader/commander
portrait roots, interface/GFX, and localisation. The Di Benedetto scan returned
no active character or portrait owner. Zingales and Santini returned the vanilla
owners described above. The broader reference-mod search is not an exclusivity
claim and no image was copied from a reference mod.

## Crop and asset contract

The Di Benedetto source is preserved byte-for-byte. The explicit source crop is
recorded in the downstream refinish package as a face-visible head-and-shoulders
crop targeting the full leader canvas ratio `156x210` (0.742857). The civilian
suit, tie, grayscale tonal range, and visible face must remain source-anchored;
no uniform, insignia, medals, hat, or invented military detail is authorised.
The final treatment, if independently approved, must be a full `156x210`
leader/commander portrait only—never an advisor card, dossier, or `_small`
asset.

This package intentionally contains no processed DDS, no runtime texture, and
no `.gfx` edit. Those are parent-owned wiring surfaces; the separate refinish
package records any approved output and its conversion evidence.

## Simplifications and blockers

- Zingales and Santini are legally sourced but unavailable because vanilla owns
  active characters with those identities.
- Scimeca, Corselli, Maltese, Pagano, and La Ferla remain blocked by unclear or
  unavailable image licences (and, for Maltese, weaker active-1936 timing).
- Di Benedetto is a deliberate role adaptation, not a historical claim of a
  1936 field command; his civilian source appearance is preserved.
- No generated identity, generic substitute, female portrait, advisor/dossier
  card, miniature, runtime DDS, GFX edit, or gameplay change was made.
