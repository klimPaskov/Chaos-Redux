# Event 6 western-gap portrait source retry

Source-only manifest for the 2026-07-22 retry of unresolved grounded Event 6 portraits. This folder contains unchanged archival/source masters only. No image was cropped, resized, retouched, re-encoded, converted to PNG/DDS, or wired into gameplay. The existing BAY Rupprecht and RHI Josef Friedrich Matthes assets are protected and were not touched.

## Status vocabulary

- `source_ready`: the exact original upload/archive binary is present locally, its dimensions/bytes/SHA-256 are recorded, the subject and role fit are defensible, and a rights basis is documented.
- `needs_review`: a face-visible binary is present, but a human must resolve a rights, date, branch, regional-fit, or provenance question before processing.
- `blocked`: no acceptable attributable and rights-defensible original was acquired; no generated or generic substitute is permitted.
- `rejected_derivative`: a smaller/cropped derivative is retained only as provenance history and must not be processed when a direct original exists.
