# ARX grounded portrait-source retry 01

Date: 2026-07-22  
Scope: sourced real male portrait research for Event 006 IW-018 Sardinia
(`ARX`). This package covers the unresolved crown/council role and the
Sardinian territorial or military-command role without changing gameplay,
GFX, localisation, or runtime textures.

The package preserves the downloaded masters under `source_masters/` and
contains only mechanical 156x210 crop previews under `crop_previews/`. The
contact sheet is a comparison aid; it is not a finished HOI4 portrait. No
ImageGen, painterly finish, DDS conversion, sprite registration, or GFX edit
was performed. See [manifest.md](manifest.md) for status and uncertainty,
[gfx_handoff.md](gfx_handoff.md) for deferred consumer paths, and
[search_notes/ownership_and_candidate_log.md](search_notes/ownership_and_candidate_log.md)
for the identity and ownership gate.

Disposition summary:

- Prince Eugenio di Savoia-Genova, Duke of Ancona / fifth Duke of Genoa is a
  strong crown-route identity and has an attributable 1920s face-usable source,
  but Commons supplies only the Italian `PD-Italy` claim and leaves the US
  publication/URAA condition unresolved. Status: `needs_user_review`.
- Taddeo Orlando is a period-plausible military lead: active in Libya in 1936
  and later commander of the 21st Infantry Division "Granatieri di Sardegna".
  The only face crop is a 173x234 Generals.dk-derived image with unclear US
  status; an alternate 1942 Digital Library of Slovenia group photograph is
  mechanically cropped for comparison but is not a 1936 portrait. Status:
  `needs_user_review` pending role and rights review.
- No source-ready replacement is claimed. Ubaldo Soddu and Carlo Geloso were
  rejected from this package because vanilla already owns their active
  identities; Carlo Sanna is Sardinian and historically important but died in
  1928; Luigi Efisio Marras remains the previously documented postwar-image
  lead and is not duplicated here.

If review rejects the remaining candidates, the correct disposition is to keep
the ARX roles blocked rather than inventing a face or borrowing a vanilla
portrait.
