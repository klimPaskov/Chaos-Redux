## Source records

### Prince Eugenio di Savoia-Genova, Duke of Ancona / fifth Duke of Genoa

- Commons file page: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eugenio_di_savoia,_quinto_duca_di_genova.jpg>
- Direct original bitstream: <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e9/Eugenio_di_savoia%2C_quinto_duca_di_genova.jpg>
- Commons record: 287x368 JPEG, `63,752` bytes; description identifies Eugenio
  (1906-1996) as fifth Duke of Genoa; date `between 1920 and 1930`; author
  `Unknown author`; source is the uploaded original. The local unchanged
  master is the 287x368 file, not the separate 2435x3122 `FXD` derivative
  marked on Commons as AI-upscaled.
- Identity and era evidence: the 1936 *Enciclopedia Italiana* entry names him
  as `Savoia (Savoia-Genova), Eugenio di, duca di Ancona`, born in Turin on
  13 March 1906; it records his 1927 naval-academy commission, 1929 and 1932
  promotions, and command of a San Marco battalion during the Italo-Ethiopian
  conflict. See <https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/savoia-eugenio-di-duca-di-ancona_%28Enciclopedia-Italiana%29/>.
- Rights: Commons applies `PD-Italy` for a simple photograph after Italy's
  20-year term, then expressly states that US status depends on unknown US
  registration and pre-1978 publication evidence. No independent US
  publication record or `PD-1996` tag was located. Status remains
  `needs_user_review`; do not call the source redistribution-clear.
- Visual note: the face is unobstructed and the 1920s naval uniform is period
  appropriate for a 1936 crown-route portrait. The preview is a mechanical
  ratio crop and Lanczos resize only; no painting, denoising, recolouring, or
  identity alteration was performed.
- Ownership gate: exact/variant scans over the current Chaos Redux tree,
  installed vanilla, Kaiserreich `1521695605`, and approved reference mods
  `2265420196`/`1458561226` found no active `Eugenio` or `Duke of Genoa`
  character. A Kaiserreich localisation line mentions Eugenio in a marriage
  event, but no active character or portrait consumer was found. Cross-mod
  historical mention is recorded, not treated as an ownership blocker.

### Taddeo Orlando

- Main Commons file page: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Taddeo_Orlando.jpg>
- Main direct original: <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1f/Taddeo_Orlando.jpg>
- Main Commons record: 173x234 JPEG, `14,393` bytes; author field `Regio
  Esercito`; Commons source field points to
  <http://www.generals.dk/general/Orlando/Taddeo/Italy.html>. The page itself
  is a low-resolution military-portrait extraction and gives no original
  creation date. Commons applies `PD-Italy` but includes the unresolved US
  registration/publication warning; no `PD-1996` determination was found.
- Alternate Commons file page: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Taddeo_Orlando_and_Mario_Robotti.jpg>
- Alternate direct original: <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bd/Taddeo_Orlando_and_Mario_Robotti.jpg>
- Alternate record: 301x554 JPEG, date `1942`, a two-person field photograph
  credited to the Digital Library of Slovenia. The right-person crop in this
  package is mechanical comparison evidence only; it is visibly degraded and
  is not a 1936-era portrait.
- Identity and role evidence: the Italian Carabinieri biography gives Taddeo
  Orlando's birth in Gaeta on 23 June 1885, artillery career, service in
  Tripolitania from 1936, command of the 21st Infantry Division `Granatieri di
  Sardegna` from 1 March 1940, and death on 1 September 1950. See
  <https://www.carabinieri.it/chi-siamo/ieri/storia/i-comandanti-generali/i-cti-generali/gen-c-a-taddeo-orlando>.
  The Italian Army commander roll lists Orlando among the 1934-43 division
  commanders: <https://www.esercito.difesa.it/en/organization/the-chief-of-general-staff-of-the-army/comfoter/southern-operational-forces-command/grenadiers-of-sardinia-mechanized-brigade/the-commanders/123885.html>.
- Rights and fit: the main face file is attributable to a period Italian Army
  source but lacks a creation/publication record and US status; the alternate
  is later than the scenario and not a solo portrait. The candidate therefore
  remains `needs_user_review`, not source-ready.
- Ownership gate: exact/variant scans over the current Chaos Redux tree,
  installed vanilla, and the reference mod roots found no active Taddeo
  Orlando character or portrait consumer. No external portrait bitstream was
  copied.
