## Gate policy

Only a real male person with a defensible period source, a role appropriate to the grounded polity on 1936-01-01, and a redistribution basis compatible with the project could be accepted. Modern photographs, Generals.dk/NPG/rights-ambiguous sources, non-free scans, dead-before-start candidates, civilian officeholders for a commander slot, and any person already actively owned by vanilla or the current project were rejected. No fallback is authorized.
