# Real-person portrait eligibility

Event 006 portraits are male-only, and the accepted IW-043/IW-058 design calls for separately authored, all-male institutional group portraits by default. No advisor art is in scope. This review covers only the optional real-person question and does not approve any final portrait.

## Optional candidate: `ASY_shimun_eshai` — Mar Eshai Shimun XXIII

**Identity and period fit.** The Mar Shimun Memorial Foundation identifies Mar Eshai Shimun XXIII as the Catholicos Patriarch of the Church of the East, born 26 February 1908 at Qudchanis and ordained patriarch in 1920 at age twelve. The biography records his political and religious representation of Assyrians, the family’s 1933 exile, and later conferences including 1937. He is therefore a real male leader whose role can fit an IW-058 church/council branch in the 1936 setting, subject to route-specific approval.

**Exact image status.** The Foundation’s photo archive contains a “Mar Eshai 1920” formal archival image credited to MS Archives. The page does not expose a clear public reuse license, image hash, or stable direct-download permission. The Qatar Digital Library/British Library record for Eshai Shimun is later (1945) and documentary rather than a cleared portrait source. The Library of Congress image “Shemasha (Deacon) Ishai d'Mar Shimun” is a different, older family member from 1890–1895; it is not a substitute even though the LOC page states no known restrictions.

**Eligibility decision:** `needs_user_review` / research-only. No source file was downloaded, no crop was made, and no portrait PNG/DDS is approved. A separate producer and reviewer must clear an exact source image and record source URL, archive, date, license/permission, native crop rectangle, candidate hash, and visual approval before any HOI4 processing. The producing agent cannot self-approve.

**Crop guidance if a licensed source is later supplied.** Prefer a 1930–1936 archival photograph, head-and-shoulders framing, face centered with clerical headwear and robe visible, no text or identifying props obscuring the face. The circa-1920 formal image is potentially crop-feasible only if permission is granted; it is roughly 8–12 years younger than the 1936 scenario and should be labelled as such. Preserve facial likeness, nose/eye/ear relationships, hair/headwear silhouette, and robe details through the native advisor/leader pipeline; do not substitute a modern actor, generic priest, resized leader portrait, or generated likeness.
