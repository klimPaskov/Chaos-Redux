## Definition and playback wiring

Each audio ID has six music definitions in `music/chaosx_super_event_music.asset`, one zero-chance station record in `music/chaosx_super_event_music.txt`, one base WAV sound and six sound-effect wrappers in `sound/chaosx_sound.asset`, and all thirty wrappers are registered in the `Effects` category. The shared settings-aware playback helper resolves every `0.5`–`3.0` volume setting in both music and sound modes.

- `chaosx_super_event_camp_global_discovery_track`

Audio production and wiring have no blockers or substitutions. The original implementation handoff is retained in `audio_handoff.md` as a completed provenance record.
