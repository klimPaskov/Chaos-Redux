# Event 016 achievement-icon ImageGen prompts

Source mode: built-in `$imagegen` (`gpt-image-2` path), one call per achievement identity.

Every row is expanded with this shared production brief:

> Create an original completed-state Hearts of Iron IV achievement icon source master at no less than 512x512. Render a compact nearly square dark steel-and-bronze achievement plaque with an aged 1930s-1940s grand-strategy finish, integrated laurel or riveted edge, crisp readable silhouette, high contrast, and the requested challenge-specific subject. The complete plaque should fill about 88% of a square canvas and remain legible at 64x64. Place it on a perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` chroma-key background with no shadows, gradients, texture, reflections, floor plane, lighting variation, or vignette; do not use `#00ff00` in the plaque. No cast shadow outside the plaque, text, letters, numbers, watermark, logo, checkerboard, white halo, sticker rim, or modern UI. Avoid a generic Kruger portrait and avoid making the identity depend on tiny details.

The grey and not-eligible states are derived mechanically from the approved completed-state PNG. The latter composites the repository overlay at `.agents/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/assets/vanilla_reference/icons/achievements/overlay.png` without redrawing or substituting the treatment.
