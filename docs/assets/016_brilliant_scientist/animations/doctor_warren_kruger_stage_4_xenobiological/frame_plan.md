# Stage 4 Xenobiological Frame Plan

| Frame | Separately authored visual state |
| --- | --- |
| 000 | Human Kruger examines a sealed luminous organism with a probe. |
| 001 | A broad leaf-like tendril presses against the glass and he recoils one shoulder. |
| 002 | He transfers a small living sample between two vessels using forceps. |
| 003 | A vine curls around his gloved forearm while he studies it rather than panicking. |
| 004 | Kruger leans over an opened containment cradle as two asymmetric tendrils rise. |
| 005 | He raises a specimen lamp; the organism orients a newly shaped sensory fan toward it. |
| 006 | A different chamber vents and Kruger shields his face with a distinct hand pose. |
| 007 | He steadies a pulsing biological conduit with both hands; the apparatus anatomy changes. |
| 008 | Multiple contained lifeforms surround him while he records their reactions. |
| 009 | Kruger reseals the primary vessel and returns toward the frame-000 analytical pose. |

The frames must preserve a clearly human Warren Kruger. Alien physiognomy belongs only to the `alien_revealed` subvariant.
