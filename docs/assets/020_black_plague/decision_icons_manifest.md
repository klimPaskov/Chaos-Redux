## Processing and validation record

Source PNGs were generated or preserved at 1254×1254 and retained under `source_png/`. The official imagegen `remove_chroma_key.py` helper produced full-resolution RGBA intermediates under `alpha_intermediate/`. A deterministic Pillow pass trimmed transparent margins, fit each silhouette inside a 31×30 safe area, and centered it on a 33×32 RGBA canvas under `processed_png/decisions/`. The repository `convert_to_dds.py` tool produced one-level uncompressed BGRA DDS files at 33×32 with no mipmaps. A checkerboard contact sheet and review preview are at `contact_sheets/decision_icons_contact_sheet.png` and `previews/decision_icons_preview.png`.

The seven new response prompts are recorded in `prompts/decision_icons_2026-07-24.md`. The weapon-delivery prompt and provenance are recorded in `prompts/rat_identity_weaponization_prompts.md`; its source, chroma-key alpha intermediate, processed PNG, and runtime DDS are retained under the Event 020 asset workspace. The eight existing source concepts are preserved unchanged; no source file was overwritten.

## Crown and burrow action provenance

The two action icons were generated on 2026-08-01 with the official built-in ImageGen workflow. The retained source PNG dimensions are 1246×1262 (`decision_strike_the_crown_imagegen_source.png`) and 1254×1254 (`decision_seal_royal_burrows_imagegen_source.png`) under `source_png/`; the local chroma-key helper produced RGBA intermediates under `alpha_intermediate/`, and a deterministic Pillow pass trimmed transparent margins, fit each silhouette inside a 31×30 safe area, and centered it on the exact 33×32 RGBA canvas. The final DDS files are one-level, uncompressed BGRA at 33×32 with transparent unused pixels.

These are fictional, symbolic assets generated for Chaos Redux. No internet or user-provided source was used, so there is no third-party image license or attribution requirement; the project may distribute the generated files under its normal mod license. ImageGen provenance is retained in the source PNGs and the prompt entries above.
