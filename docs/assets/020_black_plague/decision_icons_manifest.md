## Processing and validation record

Source PNGs were generated or preserved at 1254×1254 and retained under `source_png/`. The official imagegen `remove_chroma_key.py` helper produced full-resolution RGBA intermediates under `alpha_intermediate/`. A deterministic Pillow pass trimmed transparent margins, fit each silhouette inside a 31×30 safe area, and centered it on a 33×32 RGBA canvas under `processed_png/decisions/`. The repository `convert_to_dds.py` tool produced one-level uncompressed BGRA DDS files at 33×32 with no mipmaps. A checkerboard contact sheet and review preview are at `contact_sheets/decision_icons_contact_sheet.png` and `previews/decision_icons_preview.png`.

The seven new source prompts are recorded in `prompts/decision_icons_2026-07-24.md`. The eight existing source concepts are preserved unchanged; no source file was overwritten.
