# Event 020 Rat identity and weaponization generation prompts

All prompts used the official built-in image-generation workflow. Symbolic icons requested a flat `#00ff00` chroma-key source for local alpha removal, with no green in the subject, no text, no watermark, no cast shadow, and crisp HOI4-style painterly edges. The special-project icon used a wide sealed plague canister, rat skull, and laboratory glass motif. Decision prompts covered containment (rat tails around a sealed flask), field adaptation (rat paw, microscope, specimen vial), covert delivery (sealed satchel, railway crate, tail), and strategic deterrence (plague vial inside iron shield and rat teeth).

Decision prompts matched the live keys `safety_first`, `military_acceleration`, `dual_use`, and `defensive_conversion`: sealed canister containment, armored rail acceleration, split plague/medical shield, and fortified medical conversion. The dedicated `weapon_delivery` prompt uses a sealed transport canister inside a railway cargo crate with a geometric containment seal, so the delivery operation no longer aliases the Military Acceleration icon. Focus prompts used separate compositions for first warren, urban sewer network, field grain brood, dock harbor brood, war trench brood, distributed brood signal, royal basin, absolute crown, brood council, Black Breath hierophancy, and terminal route readiness. Each prompt explicitly required a distinct central subject and prohibited reuse or recolor-only variants.

Portrait prompts classified the four brood leaders as fictional high-chaos institutional collective identities and the Rat King as a fictional high-chaos impossible non-human individual. They requested subdued painterly 156x210 upper-torso framing, distinct setting and regalia for the sovereign, no human face, no comedy, no gore, and no text.

Flag prompts requested a clean flat graphic-design rectangle with no pole, cloth folds, waving fabric, 3D scene, text, or watermark. Motifs were unique per tag: burrow spiral, ash chevrons, carrion teeth, sump drain, grain sheaf, harbor hook, broken rail, drowned arch, field maw, sewer crown, trench line, salt dock, bone warren, and Rat King crown/tail knot.
