## Flags

Flags are root-only engine assets by vanilla convention. Every registered base Rat tag has a distinct ImageGen design and all three TGA sizes: `gfx/flags/<TAG>.tga` (82x52), `gfx/flags/medium/<TAG>.tga` (41x26), and `gfx/flags/small/<TAG>.tga` (10x7). Tags covered are `RTA`, `RTB`, `RTC`, `RTD`, `RTE`, `RTF`, `RTG`, `RTH`, `RTI`, `RTJ`, `RTK`, `RTL`, `RTM`, and `RTX`.

## Gaps

This tranche intentionally covers critical Rat route/focus identities only. The remaining base and Rat King focus nodes, the wider Rat decision icon family, route-specific/world-end cosmetic flags, animated Rat King portrait/seal, and the 14 achievement icon triplets are not substituted with generic art; they remain explicit follow-up asset work.
