## Flags

Flags are root-only engine assets by vanilla convention. Runtime Event 020 identity is limited to `RTA` as the reusable Rat Nation carrier and `RTX` as the separate Rat King. The `RTB` through `RTM` flag set is archival unused production retained for provenance and must not be read as a runtime country pool. Every listed asset has the three TGA sizes: `gfx/flags/<TAG>.tga` (82x52), `gfx/flags/medium/<TAG>.tga` (41x26), and `gfx/flags/small/<TAG>.tga` (10x7). Runtime tags covered are `RTA` and `RTX`; archival tags covered are `RTB`, `RTC`, `RTD`, `RTE`, `RTF`, `RTG`, `RTH`, `RTI`, `RTJ`, `RTK`, `RTL`, and `RTM`.
