# Natural Disaster Achievement Regen Prompts

Package: `docs/assets/achievement_icon_regen_hoi4_style_natural_disaster_2026_06_21/`

Shared direction:
- full square HOI4-style achievement tile
- bronze medal/frame composition
- dark enamel background
- strong contrast and one readable central scene at 64x64
- no transparency, no text, no white square background, no focus-icon framing
