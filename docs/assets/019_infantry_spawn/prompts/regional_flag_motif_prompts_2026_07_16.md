Source mode: built-in ImageGen, one generation call per regional motif. The
seven outputs are original fictional secondary emblems. They are not sourced
or traced from real-world flags, coats of arms, religious marks, corporate
marks, or national insignia.

## Shared production brief

Each call used the `logo-brand` prompt taxonomy and requested an original
secondary heraldic motif for a flat Hearts of Iron IV cosmetic flag. Shared
constraints were:

- one isolated, centered, fully visible emblem with generous padding
- clean flat orthographic vexillology and crisp vector-like edges
- warm ivory and antique gold fills with a thick deep-charcoal contour
- a perfectly flat `#00ff00` chroma background for official local removal
- a bold silhouette intended to survive as a 3–4-pixel secondary mark
- no text, letters, numbers, real-world insignia, flag field, fabric, folds,
  pole, waving, perspective, gradients, lighting, texture, shadow, glow,
  border frame, extra objects, or watermark

The final regional flags retain each existing Event 019 identity source. The
generated motif is a secondary fly-side emblem and is not used to recolor or
redraw the identity layer.
