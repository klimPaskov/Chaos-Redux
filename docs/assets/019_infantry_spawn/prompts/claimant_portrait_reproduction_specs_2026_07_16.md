## Shared generation contract

- One original vertical army/host identity scene per call, composed to survive a centered 156x210 cover crop.
- Hand-painted late-1930s/1940s grand-strategy treatment with documentary atmosphere, restrained contrast, period matériel, strong mass silhouette, and no UI frame.
- Claimant scenes show a distinct regional muster, formation geometry, terrain, logistics method, or operational posture. Soldiers may appear only as anonymous massed ranks; no individual person, face, bust, officer, commander, or human focal subject.
- Derivative scenes show armies or councils-as-massed-entities. No commander portrait, councillor portrait, anthropomorphic close-up, or single dominant individual.
- The technical unassigned scene has no claimant, family, country, continent, ideology, or route identity. It exists only for a UI or scripted-localisation state that has not yet resolved a valid profile.
- No real-person likeness, copied vanilla identity, real national/state/party emblem, extremist insignia, readable text, watermark, collage, or modern equipment.
- Gameplay identities, male-only name pools, male-default leader metadata, archetype profiles, region gates, filenames, and sprite IDs remain unchanged; the artwork represents the forces that embody those identities.
