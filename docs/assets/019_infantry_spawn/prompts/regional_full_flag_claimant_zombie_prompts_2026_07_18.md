## Shared full-flag prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: flat HOI4 cosmetic flag design. Create one full-bleed, orthographic rectangular flag integrating exactly one identity contract with exactly one regional heraldic motif. Use bold authored heraldic geometry and full colour that remains readable at 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7 after the approved deterministic spot-colour normalisation pass. Keep the source uncropped and unmodified.

Required constraints: no fabric, folds, poles, scenery, people, faces, text, letters, perspective, lighting, shadows, gradients, vignettes, painterly surface, watermark, or UI artifacts. Do not redraw the design locally or substitute primitive shapes. Preserve the generated emblem construction and all identity/region cues in the raw master.

Canonical prompt references: generated_icon_and_flag_prompts.md under Cosmetic flag prompts; regional_flag_motif_prompts_2026_07_16.md under Shared production brief and Region-specific prompts and frozen sources.

## Identity contracts

- CLAIMANT_BREAKAWAY: Claimant breakaway: oxblood field, charcoal diagonal command sash, sealed open muster ledger, unequal brass batons, and muted-olive helmet canton.
- ZOMBIE_BASE: Zombie base: bone tally standard and broken rifle on black and dried-blood burgundy.
- ZOMBIE_CLAIMANT: Zombie claimant: ivory crown, skull, and single gold baton on crimson and charcoal.
- ZOMBIE_COLLECTIVE: Zombie collective: three linked helmeted skull profiles around a central ring, with no crown.
- ZOMBIE_SPECIES: Zombie species: bone-white devouring spiral on near-black with a restrained burgundy border.

## Region contracts

- EUROPE: Europe split heraldic chevron: two interlocked asymmetrical heraldic wings with a central shield-notch and forked lower point.
- MIDDLE_EAST: Middle East eight-point interlaced knot: one continuous over-under ribbon, open octagonal heart, and eight spear-leaf terminals.
- AFRICA: Africa stepped half-sun and spearhead: terraced rays on both sides, inward hook-notches, and a broad downward spearhead.
- ASIA: Asia three-peaked mountain, curling cloud, and open gate: stepped lintel ends and one interlocked heraldic badge.
- AUSTRALIA: Australia asymmetric navigation star and interlocking waves: one long compass ray and an open eye-shaped center.
- NORTH_AMERICA: North America broken star with rail chevron: three rail ties, a broken upper point, and industrial-heraldic geometry.
- SOUTH_AMERICA: South America angular condor-step around a maize diamond: compact wings, stepped diamond, and five-kernel maize ear.
