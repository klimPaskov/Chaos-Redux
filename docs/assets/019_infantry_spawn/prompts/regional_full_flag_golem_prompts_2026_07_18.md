# Event 019 Golem Regional Full-Flag ImageGen Prompts

Date: 2026-07-18

Source mode: exactly one independent built-in ImageGen call per runtime tag (28 calls total). Every generated result is retained unmodified under `source_png/flags/regional_full_flag_raw/golem/`; no API/CLI, batch generation, local compositing, or transformed source is used.

## Shared production brief

All prompts use `logo-brand` for a fictional flat cosmetic flag. The source is a full-bleed horizontal 16:10 rectangle intended to remain readable at 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7. Artwork is original mineral/turquoise heraldry, not a real flag or insignia.

Shared constraints: intentional flat orthographic vexillology; bold vector-like hard edges; no fabric, folds, pole, waving, scenery, people, faces, human silhouettes, text, letters, numbers, watermark, UI, perspective, lighting, shadows, depth highlights, gradients, painterly surface, texture, glow, bevels, 3D rendering, or photographic treatment. Full-colour palette uses turquoise/blue-green, basalt charcoal/coal black, slate/mineral grey, and restrained ivory stone, plus a region-specific accent. Identity and regional motifs must be integrated as one authored heraldic design.

## Identity contracts

- **GOLEM_BASE:** a turquoise binding rune between two stone fists and a broken chain.
- **GOLEM_CLAIMANT:** a master-builder compass/crown above a marching stone boot.
- **GOLEM_COLLECTIVE:** nine interlocking rune tiles forming one larger living pattern.
- **GOLEM_SPECIES:** three fantastical stone colossi advancing from a mountain arch; abstract non-human mineral silhouettes with no faces.

## Regional motif contracts

- **EUROPE:** a split heraldic chevron made from two interlocked asymmetrical heraldic wings, a tiny central shield-notch, and a forked lower point.
- **MIDDLE_EAST:** an eight-point interlaced knot built from one continuous geometric ribbon with alternating over-under crossings, an open octagonal heart, and eight spear-leaf terminals.
- **AFRICA:** a stepped half-sun rising behind a broad downward spearhead, with three terraced rays per side and inward hook-notches.
- **ASIA:** a three-peaked mountain rising through a curling cloud inside an open gate-like arch with stepped lintel ends.
- **AUSTRALIA:** an asymmetric eight-ray navigation star cradled by two interlocking ocean waves, one long compass ray, and an open eye-shaped center.
- **NORTH_AMERICA:** a deliberately broken five-point star whose lower arms become a rail chevron, with three rail ties and a gap through the upper point.
- **SOUTH_AMERICA:** an angular condor-step forming a diamond around a five-kernel maize ear, a compact civic-heraldic badge rather than a realistic bird.
