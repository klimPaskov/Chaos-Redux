# Canal Schedule report art prompt

Create a fictional HOI4 report-event illustration for a South Asian Canal Schedule during Air Winter and Fallout recovery. Wide 4:3 composition, no text, no logos, no flags, no real people. Foreground: a cracked earthen irrigation embankment and a weathered sluice gate with a small hand-painted measurement board, several bundled anonymous workers and village delegates checking paper ledgers. Midground: narrow irrigation channels leading toward dormant wheat and rice fields with frost, dead vegetation, thin ash and soot on the water, a few patches of gray meltwater. Background: hazy foothills under dim blue-gray light with a restrained ash cloud, faint cold rain and a pale thaw horizon. The scene should feel specific to South Asian agricultural water management, practical and human, not generic apocalypse. Muted charcoal, dusty ochre, cold teal, and faded green palette. Painterly historical alternate-history news illustration, crisp focal detail, atmospheric depth, suitable for a 210x176 report card crop.
