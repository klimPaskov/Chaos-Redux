## Provenance and Processing

Every preserved source master in this package is original fictional imagery generated with OpenAI `image_gen`. No web image, archival photograph, real-person likeness, copyrighted character reference, or third-party source image was used. Accordingly, no third-party licence is asserted or required for these generated sources. The exact creative brief is preserved in `docs/assets/015_utopia_manifesto/prompts/generated_event_art_final_prompts.md`.

Local processing was deterministic and limited to crop/composition treatment, restrained colour grading, transparency where required, exact-size resize, contact-sheet construction, and DDS export:

- Reports use the repository's canonical report-card processor, producing a tilted sepia documentary card on transparent canvas at `210x176`.
- News images use a wide monochrome press treatment at `397x153` with fully opaque alpha.
- Super-event images use a restrained cinematic grade at `457x328` with fully opaque alpha.
- Runtime DDS files are uncompressed legacy 32-bit BGRA with explicit alpha masks and one image level.
