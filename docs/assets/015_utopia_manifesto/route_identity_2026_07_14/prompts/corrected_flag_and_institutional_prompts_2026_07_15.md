## Shared flag direction

Every flag call requested an original fictional landscape flag filling the canvas edge-to-edge, with a single optically centered civic composition, broad negative space, and a deliberately limited palette. The required finish was a clean flat vector-like design with hard solid fills, no fabric, folds, lighting, texture, brushwork, scene presentation, pole, mockup, text, letters, numbers, real flag resemblance, real party marks, or political/extremist symbols. Each prompt explicitly required the composition to remain recognizable after reduction to `10x7`.

Generated source masters sometimes retained low-level tonal variation despite that direction. The deterministic finishing pass therefore reduced each active source to no more than six solid colors without dithering and collapsed connected border shading. It did not add, redraw, move, or replace motifs.
