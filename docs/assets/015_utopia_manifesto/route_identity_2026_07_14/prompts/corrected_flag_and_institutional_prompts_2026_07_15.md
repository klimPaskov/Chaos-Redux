Every call requested one fictional `3:2` landscape flag filling the canvas, one dominant civic seal with substantial internal heraldic construction, broad negative space, and a route-specific palette. The prompts required buildings, tools, ledgers, bridges, stores, gardens, instruments, gates, locks, reserves, or networks to carry the identity. They explicitly rejected a result reducible to a few circles, stars, stripes, chevrons, diamonds, blobs, or generic political marks.

The generated masters are preserved as generated. The finishing script performs only aspect fitting, restrained colour finishing, sharpening, resize, validation, and bottom-left TGA export. It does not quantize, flatten, trace, redraw, simplify, or substitute the generated heraldry.
