# Utopia Balance to Assignment — Frame Plan

All eight frames use the same front-on orthographic camera, the same shallow horizontal instrument, the same center anchor, and the same civic-ledger palette. Each state is a separate built-in ImageGen output. The visual change is physical mechanism and token behavior, not transform, recolor, filter, brightness, or glow.

| Frame | Motion state | Genuine authored visual change | Prompt delta | Anchor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 000 | unsorted measure | Five loose brass tokens rest at uneven positions on an open measuring rail; grid comb retracted. | Establish the exact instrument and camera. | center |
| 001 | gauge engages | A calibrated brass comb begins extending from the central carriage; loose tokens remain irregular. | Change only comb deployment and nearest token contact. | center |
| 002 | first alignment | Outer guide rails slide inward; two tokens enter the first measured cells. | Add guide movement and two aligned token positions. | center |
| 003 | row forming | A second pair of tokens moves into adjacent measured cells while the comb extends farther. | Continue physical sorting into a visible row. | center |
| 004 | grid rising | Perpendicular dividers lift, turning the row into a shallow matrix; final loose token approaches its cell. | Add cross-dividers and final token travel. | center |
| 005 | measured placement | All tokens occupy separate cells; the carriage applies equal spacing but clamps remain open. | Complete token placement with distinct visible cells. | center |
| 006 | assignment lock | Small brass retainers close around the occupied cells; the central measure settles level. | Close retainers and settle the gauge. | center |
| 007 | Assignment reached | Final orderly measured grid of tokens with all retainers seated and spacing visibly uniform. | Refine completed state; closest continuity to 006 without duplication. | center |

Frame 007 is the static fallback. The sequence is not designed as a loop.
