# Utopia Balance to Choice — Frame Plan

All eight frames use the same front-on orthographic camera, the same shallow horizontal instrument, the same center anchor, and the same civic-ledger palette. Each state is a separate built-in ImageGen output. The visual change is physical mechanism and token behavior, not transform, recolor, filter, brightness, or glow.

| Frame | Motion state | Genuine authored visual change | Prompt delta | Anchor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 000 | neutral measure | Closed central carriage; three tokens retained on one measured rail; branch gates shut. | Establish the exact instrument and camera. | center |
| 001 | first release | Left latch lifts and the first small branch gate opens; retained tokens remain seated. | Change only latch and first gate state. | center |
| 002 | fork emerging | A second hinged path opens from the center; the lead token rolls clear of its detent. | Add emerging fork and displaced lead token. | center |
| 003 | paths separating | Three mechanical route channels become visibly distinct; two tokens leave the common rail. | Widen authored branch geometry and token separation. | center |
| 004 | free distribution | The central divider folds down; tokens occupy different branch mouths with open spacing. | Open divider and distribute tokens physically. | center |
| 005 | routes fully open | Brass route leaves extend farther and stops retract; tokens advance independently. | Complete branch deployment and token travel. | center |
| 006 | voluntary settlement | Tokens reach three separate open rests; no clamp, cage, or grid holds them. | Settle tokens at individual rests; keep mechanisms open. | center |
| 007 | Choice reached | Final balanced branching tableau with the carriage unlocked and three independent token positions. | Refine completed state; closest continuity to 006 without duplication. | center |

Frame 007 is the static fallback. The sequence is not designed as a loop.
