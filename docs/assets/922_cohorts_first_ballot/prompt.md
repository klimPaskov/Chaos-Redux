# The Cohort's First Ballot report art prompt

Create one fictional alternate-history report-event illustration for the Congo Green Basin after Fallout and Air Winter. Show young adult delegates from river and hill settlements at a river ferry canopy with a simple wooden ballot box and waterproof paper ledgers. Include an older guardian, a clinic worker, and a teacher. Ash settles on corrugated roofs, cold rain crosses the river, dead vegetation stands beyond the pump road, and the light is dim gray. Use documentary painted realism with a restrained charcoal, rust, moss green, and river blue palette. Keep the ballot box and delegates centered for a 210x176 report-event crop. Do not show readable text, logos, national flags, real people, radiation symbols, sci-fi machinery, split panels, collage, or a watermark.
