# The Repair Charter report art prompt

Create a dedicated fictional report-event painting of a post-Fallout East Asian Technate repair corridor. A cold ash-covered rail road runs between a damaged clinic and a compact generator workshop. Fictional repair crews carry stamped parts cases, measuring tools, route ledgers, and a hand-cranked radio while a senior mechanic points to a charter map. Show dim winter light, pale ash haze, frost on pipes, dead vegetation, and a small warm work lamp.

The image must contain no text, logos, flags, real people, identifiable public figures, gore, zombies, modern branding, or watermark. Keep the action centered for the 210 by 176 report-event crop.
