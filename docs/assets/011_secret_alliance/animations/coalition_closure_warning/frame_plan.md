# Coalition Closure Warning Frame Plan

Every row is a separately generated source frame. Mechanical processing may align, crop, resize, pad, sheet, preview, and convert these frames, but it may not create the motion state.

| Frame | Motion state | Visual change already present in source artwork | Prompt delta | Anchor note | Loop note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 000 | guarded rest | arms remain wide; cords slack but visible; central seal already cracked | widest opening, lowest tension | seal center fixed | loop destination |
| 001 | first closure | upper arm lowers one joint; side cords become taut | one deliberate inward step | seal center fixed | easing in |
| 002 | tightening | both side arms close; cord knots pull toward the crack | visibly tighter geometry | seal center fixed | rising pressure |
| 003 | near closure | arm tips enter the inner ring; crack is boxed in | narrow aperture | seal center fixed | approach peak |
| 004 | full closure | three arms lock around the broken seal; cords form a tight enclosing triangle | fully closed warning state | seal center fixed | static fallback |
| 005 | held pressure | arms stay locked but cord knots and clasp positions differ from frame 004 | second separately illustrated peak state | seal center fixed | one-frame hold |
| 006 | partial release | lower arm releases one notch; side cords remain taut | slight physical retreat | seal center fixed | easing out |
| 007 | return guard | arms reopen close to frame 000 but retain more tension and different cord lay | near-rest return pose | seal center fixed | connects to 000 |

Each frame keeps one camera, one palette, one 4:3 canvas, and a centered broken seal with consistent scale. The source frames must visibly redraw arm joints, cord lay, knots, and clasp positions; lighting variation alone is insufficient.
