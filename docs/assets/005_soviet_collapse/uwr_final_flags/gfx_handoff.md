No `.gfx` edit is required or authorized for country flags. The parent can rely on the exact tag and ideology filenames above; no sprite-definition guesswork is needed.

Use notes: keep the normal, medium, and small ladders synchronized when making future UWR flag changes. The family is intentionally opaque and uses a bottom-origin TGA header matching the validated flag export contract. The source masters, processed PNG previews, DDS QA artifacts, prompts, contact sheets, and hashes remain under this event-scoped evidence package.
