# UWR final flag family ImageGen prompts

Source mode: `$imagegen` generated fictional flag designs.

All five source masters were generated as separate built-in ImageGen results and retained in `source_png/`. The masters were used only as generated flat design sources; final flag exports use mechanical resizing and TGA encoding, not local redrawing or emblem substitution. ImageGen returned native masters between 1576x997 and 1577x998 pixels.

## Base flag — `UWR_master.png`

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: fictional Hearts of Iron IV country flag source master. Primary request: Create one original fictional horizontal national flag for the Unconventional Warfare Republic (UWR), a high-chaos Soviet successor built around the Tver pathogen directorate, laboratory security, and chemical/biological warfare command. The flag must be a single clean orthographic rectangular flag filling the entire image, suitable for later export to HOI4 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7 TGA files. Style/medium: flat 2D heraldic flag design, crisp printed geometry, restrained mid-century institutional design, no fabric. Composition/framing: full flag rectangle with a deep midnight teal field, a strong centered shield-like laboratory containment emblem, and a clear outer silhouette; use a sealed glass sample ampoule inside an angular containment frame, with a compact three-lobed pathogen/leaf glyph and small industrial latch details. The emblem must remain legible when reduced to 10x7. Color palette: midnight teal, near-black navy, bone white, oxidized copper-red, and a small amount of cold cyan; high contrast. Materials/textures: flat solid colour fields only, clean edges, no texture. Text (verbatim): none. Constraints: one flag only, full-bleed rectangular design, invented fictional heraldry, balanced margins, simple but authored emblem, no readable text. Avoid: waving fabric, folds, flagpole, scenery, sky, perspective, lighting, cast shadows, gradients, 3D render, painterly brushwork, watermarks, UI, labels, letters, numbers, Soviet hammer-and-sickle, orange gear, crossed weapons, generic skull, copied real-world flag, and any background outside the flag.
