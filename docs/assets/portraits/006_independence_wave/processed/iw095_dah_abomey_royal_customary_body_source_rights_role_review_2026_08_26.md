# IW-095 Dahomey Abomey royal/customary body source review

Status: `research_only_fail_closed_source_candidate_hold`.

This evidence packet is for IW-095 Dahomey (`DAH`), whose current anchor is state `776` in the 1936 opening and whose former host is France. It does not create a character, runtime portrait, or central package admission.

## Proposed institutional subject

Subject: the Abomey royal/customary court as an authentic institutional body, not a named person and not an independent restored Dahomey government.

The proposed body is grounded in a dated photograph of the royal thrones and reception court at the palace of Guézo in Abomey. The source record names Guézo, Glélé, Béhanzin, and Agoliagbo and identifies the place as Abomey, Dahomey (current Benin).

The source predates the 1936 opening by six years and therefore supports period-authentic institutional material. It does not prove which person held authority in 1936, that the court was an independent state in 1936, or that a named leader can be assigned to the image.
