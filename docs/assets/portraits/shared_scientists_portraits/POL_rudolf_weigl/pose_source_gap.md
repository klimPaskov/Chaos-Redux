# Rudolf Weigl source-pose gap

Status: candidate retained as an unpromoted study; the requested three-quarter frontal source requirement remains unresolved.

Parent review on 2026-10-06 passed identity and provenance and failed the requested framing because the pose does not satisfy the table's frontal or three-quarter requirement.

The candidate remains at `portrait_POL_rudolf_weigl_source_crop.png` and is not installed.

Bounded searches covered `Rudolf Weigl portrait photo`, `Rudolf Weigl frontal portrait`, `Rudolf Weigl 1930s portrait`, `Rudolf Weigl official portrait`, the [Wikimedia Commons Rudolf Weigl category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rudolf_Weigl), and National Digital Archives catalog references 1-N-620 through 1-N-623.

An authentic frontal or three-quarter source with public-domain or clear reuse rights is required before this row can be promoted.

No face was generated or substituted, and the existing runtime DDS and sprite wiring remain unchanged.
