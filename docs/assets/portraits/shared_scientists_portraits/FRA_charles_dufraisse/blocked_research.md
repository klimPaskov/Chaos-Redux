The bounded search covered `Charles Dufraisse portrait photo`, `Charles Dufraisse chemist photograph`, `Charles Dufraisse laboratory photograph`, `Charles Dufraisse frontal portrait`, the Wikimedia Commons file search, the official Collège de France photo library, the FranceArchives record for the 1954 College report, and the BnF portrait index.

The Commons file [Charles Moureu Laboratoire parisien.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Charles_Moureu_Laboratoire_parisien.jpg) is marked public domain, and the College de France historical account [describes Dufraisse in the laboratory team photograph](https://www.college-de-france.fr/media/lettre-du-college-de-france/UPL1523606604030198575_CDF_L01_Interieur.pdf); the Commons description identifies him on the right in the laboratory scene.

The downloaded Commons original was reviewed as a multi-person laboratory scene; Dufraisse is seated on the right, wearing a cap and long beard, looking down and sideways rather than presenting the requested frontal or three-quarter bust.

Its source file SHA-256 was `50e1a230730df86a50b4594bd63a6e9573b54abcd61062ea7ab5bae4054ac060`; it is retained only as a rejected-source research note and was not copied into the asset archive or installed.

The official Collège de France [photo-library record 002279_027](https://phototheque.college-de-france.fr/tl/image/info/13590/002279_027.html) identifies “Le professeur Charles Dufraisse menant une expérience en laboratoire” and credits photographer Pierre Jahan.

Its preview was visually checked: the image shows a laboratory profile with a prominent beard and repeated Collège de France watermark, and the record does not state a reuse license; its displayed `01/01/1900` date conflicts with the archival series context and is not treated as a reliable date.

The FranceArchives [1954 College de France photographic report record](https://francearchives.gouv.fr/facomponent/6410786c5e935e75b1752ec3518e5039bd9e8811) lists portraits of Dufraisse in his office, group and laboratory photographs, and Pierre Jahan as the photographer, but does not grant a reuse license for the photographs.

The BnF [portrait index, letter D](https://multimedia-ext.bnf.fr/pdf/fichier_portraits_dept_estampes_lettre_D.pdf) lists “Dufraise, Charles” under NE-101 with `Communication différée`; no accessible cleared image was available from that record.

The candidate that satisfies both historical identity and reuse rights still fails the required pose and headwear gate, while the official photo-library candidate has unresolved rights and also fails framing.

No candidate portrait PNG, crop, or DDS was created or promoted, and `gfx/leaders/scientists/portrait_FRA_charles_dufraisse.dds` remains unchanged until an eligible historical source is verified.
