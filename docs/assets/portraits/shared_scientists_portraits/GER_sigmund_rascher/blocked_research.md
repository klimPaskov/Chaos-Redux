# Sigmund Rascher portrait source blocker

Status: blocked because the only standalone likeness located is explicitly non-free, while rights-cleared archival photographs found in the bounded search depict prisoners or experiment scenes rather than a usable single-person portrait.

The existing historical character and roster identity remain unchanged; no fictional or substitute face is permitted.

The Wikimedia Commons [Sigmund Rascher category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Sigmund_Rascher) contains only the [Dachau High Altitude Experiments subcategory](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Dachau_High_Altitude_Experiments), whose three files show prisoners in experimental settings rather than Rascher as a portrait subject.

The [Commons pressurization-experiment file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pressurization_experiment_at_Dachau.jpg) is marked public domain and attributed to Rascher, but it depicts a prisoner in the pressure chamber, not Rascher.

The U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum [“A victim of a Nazi medical experiment” record](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/photo/a-victim-of-a-nazi-medical-experiment) identifies Rascher as overseeing an ice-water experiment, but the photo is a multi-person scene credited to Bildarchiv Preussischer Kulturbesitz and is not a single-bust portrait with a documented reuse license.

The Harvard Law School Library [Nuremberg Trials record for photographs of Rascher's experiments](https://nuremberg.law.harvard.edu/documents/4094-photographs-of-sigmund-raschers?q=issue%3Ahigh+altitude+experiments) is an archival source for the experiment photographs, not a standalone portrait source.

The only standalone likeness found is [File:Sigmund Rascher child.jpeg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Sigmund_Rascher_child.jpeg), credited to Timeline; its file page states that it is copyrighted and not under a free license, and warns that other uses may infringe copyright.

Although a crop above the infant could improve composition, it cannot cure the rights restriction, so the image was not copied, cropped, or installed.

The Commons [“(Familie) Rascher, Außenaufnahmen” file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:(Familie)_Rascher,_Au%C3%9Fenaufnahmen_-_LABW_-_Staatsarchiv_Freiburg_W_134_Nr._033062.jpeg) is licensed CC BY 4.0, but its archive title and collection context do not identify Sigmund Rascher, and the finding aid places it among “Portraits Rumänien”; surname similarity is insufficient to establish identity.

Search terms included `Sigmund Rascher portrait`, `Sigmund Rascher photograph`, `Sigmund Rascher file photo`, `Rascher with child photograph`, and `Rascher Holzlöhner photograph`, along with the Commons category and archival entries above.

No candidate portrait PNG, crop, or DDS was created or promoted, and the existing runtime file `gfx/leaders/scientists/portrait_GER_sigmund_rascher.dds` remains unchanged pending a rights-cleared authentic portrait with framing suitable for the scientist set.
