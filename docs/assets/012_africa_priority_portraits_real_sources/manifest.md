# Event 012 Africa sourced-real portrait manifest

This manifest is the source of truth for the real-person portrait tranche. It records the exact identity, source, provenance, license position, date and era fit, visible regalia or objects, immutable crop evidence, ownership disposition, and parent-owned runtime target. A row is not runtime-complete until the source-locked repaint and an independent likeness/style/provenance audit are recorded outside this folder.

## Gate vocabulary

`source_ready_repaint_pending_audit` means the archival master and exact crop are complete, the identity and license are defensible, and the parent may request a source-locked repaint. It does not authorize runtime use.

`blocked_ownership` means the source is defensible but the exact person is already owned by another event or system, so a transfer/reuse contract is required before any repaint or wiring.

`needs_user_review_rights` means a real identity is plausible but the source license, archive permission, or provenance is not strong enough for the runtime gate.

`needs_user_review_identity` means the archival record names the person or group, but the exact individual position in a group image or other identity link still requires an independent review before repaint.

`blocked_source_gap` means no named, era-appropriate, defensible archival photograph was found for the requested polity identity.

`blocked_identity_substitution` means a real photograph exists, but it depicts a different person or successor than the requested identity and cannot be silently substituted.

## Portrait source-mode policy

Grounded historical African countries and named historical officeholders use attributed real-person sources, exact crops, source-locked identity-preserving repaint, and independent review before runtime wiring. A missing source therefore remains a documented block rather than an invitation to invent a historical face.

Fictional, alternate-history, nonhuman, or supernatural high-chaos identities may use ImageGen-created portraits. Those portraits must be labelled `fictional_high_chaos`, use a one-person HOI4 composition with a plain background, avoid caricaturing any real African identity, and remain dormant behind their accepted country, formation, model, or package gates until gameplay wiring exists. Fictional art must never be relabelled as a historical ruler or used to silently fill a source gap for a real polity.
