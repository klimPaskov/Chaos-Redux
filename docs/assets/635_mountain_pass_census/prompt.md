# Mountain Pass Census report art prompt

Create a fictional HOI4 report-event illustration for a Middle East and North Africa Mountain Pass Census during Air Winter and Fallout recovery. Use a wide 4:3 composition with no text, logos, flags, or recognizable real people. In the foreground, show a rough shelter entrance, an unmarked wooden census table, blank paper registers, bundled anonymous families, and two practical pass guards. In the midground, show a narrow highland route under snow, frost, ash, dead vegetation, and a few covered water jars. In the background, show stark mountain ridges, a dim cold sky, light ash haze, and a distant thaw band. The scene should feel specific to a highland refuge census and contested passage, not a generic apocalypse. Use restrained black, charcoal, winter blue, faded clay, and dusty sepia. Painterly historical alternate-history news illustration with crisp focal detail and atmospheric depth, suitable for a 210x176 report card crop. Keep every register blank and every person fictional and anonymous.
