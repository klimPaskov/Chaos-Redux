## Rights and NoAI record

Rights status for all sixteen rows is reference_only_user_authorized. The reviewed pages attribute the concept artwork to Adrian Smith and credit the image family to CoolMiniOrNot/CMON, but do not assert a permissive redistribution license or public-domain status. Installation is therefore recorded as the user-authorized source-art branch for this mod, not as a claim of unrestricted third-party licensing.

No explicit NoAI or AI-prohibition statement was found on the reviewed Alkony source page or the direct image URLs as of 2026-08-24. The workflow used the archived source directly for bounded 2D crop, aspect-preserving resize, and format conversion; it did not use the images as ImageGen inputs, did not generate a redesign, and used no Meshy, Blender, or other 3D portrait pipeline.
