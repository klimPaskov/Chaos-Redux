## Processing and DDS

Source PNGs are opaque RGB at `1080x1456` or `1081x1455`. `crops/` retains each lossless crop and JSON with source dimensions, exact crop box, crop dimensions, hashes, and resize method. Crops use the largest centered `156:210` aspect rectangle, then a deterministic Pillow LANCZOS resize produces opaque RGB `156x210` candidates in `processed_png/`; no repainting, face edits, color filters, or synthetic fill were used.

All eight candidate DDS files are in `candidate_dds/` and were produced with `.agents/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/tools/convert_to_dds.py`. Each has `156x210` pixels, 32-bit BGRA channel masks (`B8G8R8A8_UNORM`), a `624`-byte row pitch, a `131040`-byte base-level payload, and a `131168`-byte DDS file. The DDS header has no mip chain and carries one base image surface. Every decoded DDS RGB image matches its processed PNG exactly, and every decoded alpha sample is opaque. Per-file hashes and pixel checks are in `evidence/dds_verification.json`; the complete source/crop/PNG/DDS and existing-path crosswalk is `evidence/output_inventory.json`.
