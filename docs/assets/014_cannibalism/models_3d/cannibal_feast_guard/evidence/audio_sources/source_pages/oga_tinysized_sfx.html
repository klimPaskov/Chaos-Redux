Selection of sounds suitable for (and not limited to) fantasy games.

Included sounds:

- cuts

- arrows + quiver

- books / paper

- boots / steps

- bottles / vials / cork

- pouch

- coins

- creaks

- handcuffs

- keys / lockbox

- knife / seax / sword

- leather sheath

- metal hits and scrapes

- plastic bags

- whooshes

- water drops / pouring

... and more

 

All sounds are organic and unprocessed, apart from normalization.

96 files, 44.1kHz, 16bit.

More information and content at http://www.janschupke.eu
