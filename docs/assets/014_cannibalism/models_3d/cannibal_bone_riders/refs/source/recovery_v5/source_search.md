# Event 014 Bone Riders source recovery v5

Research date: 2026-08-24.

Status: `blocked_exact_gate_unmet`.

Required exact source: one complete modern fictional artwork showing a living horse wearing conspicuous skull, bone, or rib barding and one living painted feral cannibal rider visibly using a sling and carrying a stone pouch. The rider must show exposed flesh, rough hides or scavenged cloth, skull/bone gear, and culturally unanchored fictional styling. Lance, spear, sword, pig, elephant, camel, undead mount, riderless armor, generic knight, plate/lamellar, grayscale/unpainted, culturally identifiable, composite, and redesign-dependent sources are ineligible.

No exact pass was found. No source in this tranche is approved for ImageGen, Meshy, compositing, redesign, or runtime use.

## Search coverage

Queries covered `fantasy concept art cannibal rider sling horse bone armor skull barding`, `tabletop miniature feral rider sling horse skull bone armor`, `game art savage rider sling mounted horse bone armor`, `fantasy mounted slinger horse concept art`, `horse slinger fantasy miniature rider sling`, `sling cavalry fantasy miniature`, `slinger rider horse tabletop miniature`, `fantasy living horse bone armor rider concept art skull barding`, `tabletop bone barding horse rider miniature`, `bone armor horse rider fantasy art -skeleton -undead`, and `skull barding horse rider concept art`. Search surfaces included game concept/promotional art, tabletop/miniature catalogs, portfolio sites, ModDB, official/fan game archives, and broad image search.

The search consistently separated into bone-barded horse designs without riders and mounted slinger imagery without a horse, bone gear, painted cannibal identity, or culture-neutral fiction. No single artwork joined the required components.
