Long since in development, Faerun Total War is finally going to make a first release. A mod for M2TW Kingdoms, set in the world of Forgotten Realms, in the vast continent of Faerun. While our campaign is being reworked, the first release is focusing on custom battles.

Post news Report RSS Faerun: Total War - The Empire of Calimshan Updated Preview!

The Empire of Calimshan has gotten 21 additional units since the 2020 demo all added in 2023-2024, all of which are previewed earlier and were also created between late October 2017 and late May 2018, and they were all Human units - since then modeller Assurbanippal also finished 7 additional units, by rigging the two camel mounts which allowed 4 new units, and by creating the Djinns & other fantasy units - 7 new units - I coded them in May 2025 - 28 additional units in total.

Posted by Mr_Nygren on Dec 17th, 2025


The Empire of Calimshan has gotten 21 additional units since the 2020 demo all added in 2023-2024, all of which are previewed earlier and were also created between late October 2017 and late May 2018, and they were all Human units - since then modeller Assurbanippal also finished 6 additional models, by rigging the two camel mounts which allowed 4 new units, and by creating the Djinns & other fantasy units - 7 new units - I coded them in May 2025 - 28 additional units in total.

The original creator for the mod, TWC User Vladyvid - WizardofTurmish here on the MODDB, the creator of this MODDB-page, he had coded/implemented all the units of the Empire of Calimshan included within the demo released in June 2020, and later updated in October 2020 - I believe that many of the demo-units were his work as well, such as skins, models and other stuff - but not every single unit - Assurbanippal certainly created the Minotaurs and a few of the units, but the rest I think were a collaboration between them.

The new units are Assurbanippals work alone I believe, but 21 of these units already existed in 2018 but didn't get implemented into the demo of the mod due to the policy of keeping rosters smaller, but also due to lack of free time.

I was tasked to implement two factions when I joined the team in July 2020 after trying out the demo, and those two factions were the Thayan Nation and the Empire of Shadovar - I received both of the rosters back then, then ended up implementing 15 Thayan units I believe before I actually got tired, had been modding other mods too - TWC User WarcrafrtHero came in as a surprise, and he had been my team-mate on the other mod Warcraft: Total war between 2017-2019 - and he implemented four factiosn into the custom battles - Rashemen, Aglarond, Cormanthyr and Damara - it was only later in the fall of 2023 that I came back to this mod, and I had access to his work that he had shared in October 2020.

During the fall of 2023 I got the information that the Empire of Calimshan had 21 units outside of the game, so my first goal became to add the missing Calim units to complete the faction, after which I implemented/coded about 11 units in the fall of 2023, then later in February 2024 I implemented the rest of Thay's units, the rest of Calims units, all the custom mounts of the older factions, the captains/generals of all the factions that were in the mod and lastly the Shadovar roster - so I was only done with all of this in February 2024, and not in 2020 as originally planned.

In August 2024 Assurbanippal agreed on my idea to rig the old camel models also made in 2018, one of which was very unique and armored - these models were only sent later and I believe allowed for four new units for Calimshan - three very basic militia units even worse than the Ammlakar police forces - and one elite cavalry unit usingt he armored camel - besides these units the Genasi and Djinns were made in August 2024, and all of this coded/implemented by me in late May 2025 7 years after the bigger portion of the Calim roster had been both created and rigged by Assurbanippal, but not implemented into the game - the demo released in 2020 by Vladyvid/WizardofTurmish only included a third of the models as the Calim roster is the third or fourth largest roster.

The development on the Empire of Calimshan started in early October 2017 (after the Thayan Nation had been completed) and the faction was finished in May 2018 (when development on the Magocracy of Unther started). Later in 2024 additional fantastical units were created for the faction, and the camel mounts were rigged - this was because the Calim faction whilst having a huge roster already, mostly had historical arabic units and none of the planned fantastical units except maybe the Minotaurs and the wizards - so they did get the Djinns and Genasi later. All of their nonfantastical units already existed since May 2018.

The Empire of Calimshan as such is more recent than many of the unreleased factions, but got coded/implemented earlier - at least a third of their roster is already released.

Only their new units plus a few elites will be shown in this article, I didn't bother to screenshot the already released units unless they were very fantastical or elite.

Unit Roster:


The Empire of Calimshan - El-Ghandaban - Already Released but elite!

This unit is not new and is already in the released demo for the faction of the Empire of Calimshan, it's name is El-Ghandaban - but as the unit was used as the base for the Ogres I just thought that it deserved a mention, as it is quite big. It has the same size as the Ogres and the same animation.


As the unit weren't previewed here I thought I'd upload a picture as it's one of their better units.



Empire of Calimshan - El-Pajjabar - Heavily Armored Minos or Minotaurs!

This unit is not new and is already in the released demo for the faction of the Empire of Calimshan, it's name is El-Pajjabar - but as the unit was used as the base for the Thay Minos I just thought that it deserved a mention, as it is an even more impressive Minotaur. It is a an identical unit but with all of the units being clad in golden armor.


As the unit weren't previewed here I thought I'd upload a picture as it's one of their better units. It's probably their best infantry unit.



Empire of Calimshan - Amlakkar Skirmishers


The Empire of Calimshan has a huge roster of 40 units, out of which only 13 units made it into the 2020 demo - I've implemented the rest of their units into the mod, and here is the Amlakkar Skirmishers.


It is a new javelin-throwing unit - the reason the unit is named differently on the picture is because the file of the unit was named like on the picture, but the units with that body had been made the Amlakkar units - the police/militia forces of Calim.


The demo didn't have the marka horse animations in use by all the custom mounts, so I also did put the animations into the mod.


The first 13 units of the faction were coded into the game by WizardofTurnish/Vladyyvid - the mod creator/leader.



Empire of Calimshan - Beduine Slingers, Archers, Spearmen and Swordsmen!


The Beduine Slingers - a unique unit of Calim and one out of few units of this type in the mod, actually it's the only one currently inside the mod.


The reason the name is "Levy" instead of "Beduine" on the picture is because I implemented these not knowing that WizardofTurnish/Vladyviid had already coded the same units for the rebels/slave faction - so they were called "Beduine" there. There exists also the Archers, Swordsmen and the Spearmen using the same base-model with different weapons.


These units are like the common people rising up.



Empire of Calimshan - Bedine Levy Cavalry


This camel did exist as a model since 2018, but wasn't actually rigged for the mod because it was written that the Empire of Calimshan didn't necessarily use Camels.


We decided (Me and Assurbanippal) to go back to the model and rig it, as the model did exist in his files, and instead of using it for the Calim roster it's the mount for the Bedine Rebels of Calimshan.


The bedine rebels were implemented into the mod back in February 2024 and are now using the light camel, and there will be one melee unit, one archer and one mounted slinger unit. The Minor Factions in the campaign can recruit any unit available to the "slave"-faction, so it's like a roster for the Minor Factions in rebel areas near Calimshan. The units are also currently available for Calim which may or may not change.


The Bedine units consist of Cavalry, spearmen, swordsmen, slingers, javelinmen and archers since before.


They are also implemented for Calimshan as their weakest most basic troops - weaker than the Amlakkar militia, they are essentially armed commoners or civilians.


The idea was one that the Camel shouldn't go to waste, and two that it's more interesting with some variation.

The Amlakkar Archers of Memnon, a unique Amlakkar/Police/Militia unit available only from the city of Memnon, and there are also the Amlakkar Spearmen of Memnon, Amlakkar Swordsmen of Memnon and the Amlakkar Cavalry of Memnon.


These variations are using the same base-model with different weapons - they are better than the ordinary Amlakkar units - they are more like mid-tier or elite militia unique from one city.



Empire of Calimshan - Amlakkar of Memnon Heavy Cavalry


This heavy camel did exist as a model since 2018, but wasn't actually rigged for the mod because it was written that the Empire of Calimshan didn't necessarily use Camels.


We decided (Me and Assurbanippal) to go back to the model and rig it, as the model did exist in his files, and frankly I've never ever seen an armored Camel in Medieval 2 before, so it was just too cool to not include in some capacity.


The rare Amlakkar Cavalry of Memnon unit that is unique from the city of Memnon - there are unique Memnon mid-tier Militia from the city of Memnon including Cavalry, spearmen, archers and swordsmen - so it fit this unit the best.


The Amlakkar of Memnon were implemented by me in February 2024, and the Armoured Camel fit their cavlary unit the best. The Amlakkar of Memnon are better than the ordinary militia, and have just now been boosted (all four units) to be quite a bit stronger than the ordinary Amlakkar militia of the Calimshan faction, and are using leather armor and better shields than the other unit as well.


The idea was one that the Camel shouldn't go to waste, and two that it's more interesting with some variation.

The El-Sikaena elite female assassin unit, throwing knives at enemies. The only such unit in the mod currently.

The Dismounted Cataphract. It is a Phalanx elite unit.

The Farisha Horse Archers. It is a new elite heavily armored ranged cavalry unit.



Empire of Calimshan - Bodyguard Cataphract


The Bodyguard Cataphract. It is replacing the vanilla bodyguard used in the demo.



Empire of Calimshan - Qyshaganni Warriors


The Qyshaganni Warriors - an elite heavy infantry unit of Calim, it's probably their best infantry unit only 2nd to the dismounted cataphract.


Edit: No, i forgot - the Empire of Calim also has the Minotaurs called El-Pajjabar and another heavy infantry unit similar to the Ogres but with big humans instead.


They have since also gotten the Djinns.


Empire of Calimshan - Qyshaganni Wizards


The Qyshaganni Wizards, the new dismounted mage unit - while it is using the same models as the ones on carpets I thought the picture would be nice more up-front.



Empire of Calimshan - Qyshaganni Wizards on Carpets


The Qyshaganni Wizards mounted on carpets - called the Qyshaganni Carpet Wizards, their elite mounted mage unit.


So these Wizards fly on magical carpets and shoot fireballs at enemies, they're more powerful than the dismounted unit.



Empire of Calimshan - Fire Genasi


The Fire genasi isn't a mage as previously stated, instead it's an elite archer unit using the fire arrow projectile as it's magical projectile - it's shooting a fire arrow, but has equal stats to the dismounted Sunshaft Elves at the moment, a unit which like the Fire Genasi have chainmail armor, and is an elite ranged unit.


Assurbanippal suggested this projectile.


"Genasi (pronounced: /dʒɛnˈɑːsi/ jen-AH-see[12]) were planetouched humans,[13][5] infused with the power of the elements.[14][5][9] Along with aasimar and tieflings, genasi were the most common planetouched found on the world of Toril.[15]


Technically speaking, the genasi were not a race but rather a general classification of humans who had a heritage (usually unknown) that included some planar being from one of the elemental planes,[13] most often a genie,[13][5] (dao, djinn, efreet, marid, or jann,[6]) from whence their name is derived.[14]"


The Genasi are humans with some Djinni heritage.



Empire of Calimshan - Storm Genasi


The Storm Genasi is similar to the Fire Genasi but using a different projectile. It's uncertain which one yet.

I personally can't see the facial features very well and hope the texture will be improved, for example the eyes of the Fire Genasi were blue but with the face red, which made it easy to see/find the eyes on the model. But with this model everything is blue so I have a hard time seeing anything.



Empire of Calimshan - Djinni/Genie


The Djinni or more commonly known as "Genie" is one of the best infantry units of the Empire of Calimshan, armed with two Scimitars and their passive magical powers, the Djinns will smack a punch onto any hostile force, they're not mages as stated earlier but elite infantry.


And with this unit the Empire of Calimshan has been 100% finished, hopefully the texture for the Storm genasi will be improved as it looked bad compared to the first iteration of the model.



Empire of Calimshan - New Captain!


So I've added all the captains and generals of the factions inside of the demo, and of the new factions that are currently implemented - this I did back in February 2024.


They are replacing the old vanilla Medieval 2 generals and captains - of course the generals are using a different model.


So this means I do have some pictures of the new captains from custom battle.


On this picture is the new Captain of the Empire of Calimshan.



The Empire of Calimshan - Strat-Model Overhaul Finished!


On this picture we have the new strat-map models for the Empire of Calimshan - I finished them June 28th, 2025.


We have:


Soldier = Amlakkar of Memnon (Unique mid-tier Amlakkar units only from the City of Memnon, they do look cooler than the regular Amlakkar and Sidimmin units and does include heavy camel cavalry).


General = Qyshaganni Wizard (Red variant using a staff, the leaders of Calimshan are Wizards after the expulsion of the Djinni masters ages ago)


Diplomat = Qyshaganni Wizard (Blue variant using no staff nor other weapon).


Spy = Sidimmin of Keltar (A unique unit of the city of Keltar, used as their Spy/Thief).


Assassin = El-Sikaena (Female Assassins of the Calim Empire).


It's also possible to add a ship which we will borrow from other mods with permission.


And then you can have a unique model for a Leader, heir and 8 heroes.
