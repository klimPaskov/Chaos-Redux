# Event 014 Bone Riders source research addendum

Research date: 2026-08-24.

Scope: modern designed game, fantasy, horror, or tabletop artwork only.

Archival, historical, museum, archaeological, ethnographic, documentary, reenactment, and generated imagery were excluded.

Copyrighted artwork is retained only as `reference_only_user_authorized` source material.

Explicit NoAI, do-not-train, or equivalent restrictions are rejected.

## Mandatory gate

The source must show one coherent living horse and one mounted feral warrior in the same artwork.

The horse must have skull, rib, or long-bone barding with readable tack and four-leg anatomy.

The rider must have a painted skull helmet, visible sling, and visible stone ammunition pouch.

No spear is allowed.

No living Indigenous material is allowed.

No single candidate found in this search pass satisfies all requirements.

The asset is blocked rather than using an undead horse, riderless sheet, generic cavalry, spear-bearing substitute, separate miniature components, or a composite of multiple sources.

## Search evidence

Queries included `fantasy bone armored horse rider sling concept art`, `dark fantasy mounted slinger horse bone armor artwork`, `game concept art skull barding horse rider`, and `tabletop miniature bone armored horse rider sling`.

These results were dominated by skeletal horses, spear cavalry, riderless barding sheets, or separate miniature components.

Queries included `fantasy mounted skirmisher sling horse game art`, `horse rider using sling fantasy art`, `mounted slinger fantasy horse artwork`, and `fantasy game concept art mounted slinger horse sling stone pouch bone armor`.

These results returned generic cavalry, historical or ethnographic-inspired skirmishers, modern slingshot imagery, and no living horse with bone barding plus a mounted sling and pouch.

Queries included `mounted slinger fantasy horse artwork ArtStation`, `mounted slinger fantasy horse MobyGames`, `mounted slinger fantasy horse DeviantArt`, and `sling stone pouch mounted warrior game`.

No defensible exact source was found; near-misses lacked a living mount, rider, sling, pouch, bone armor, or a no-spear composition.

Queries included `sling mounted fantasy warrior horse art`, `sling horseman fantasy concept art`, `stone pouch horse rider fantasy illustration`, and `sling pouch mounted warrior game`.

Results were historical references, generic mounted warriors, or text-only discussions rather than eligible modern-designed source art.

Historical, ethnographic, documentary, and reenactment imagery was not archived or used.

Queries included `bone barding horse rider concept art`, `skull armor horse rider game art sling`, and `living horse bone armor mounted warrior fantasy`.

The strongest visual results remained the Awakened Horseman and Diablo mount sheet, both failing multiple mandatory gates.

Explicit NoAI-restricted or equivalent material was not selected.
