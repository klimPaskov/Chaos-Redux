    DescriptionWWS Clatterofhorseshoesonthepavement.ogg
    English: The sound of shod horse running on the pavement. The recording was realized in the National Museum of Agriculture and Agricultural-Food Industry in Szreniawa (Poland).

    Apart from historical exhibits, the museum consists also of a small agricultural farm, where animals are bred and cereals are grown. The livestock includes a mare of Polish primitive horse Konik called Humana. Shod mare is running on the pavement of the museum yard.

    Sound recordist: Monika Widzicka
    Tags: clatter/farm/Horse/Horseshoes/livestock/Szreniawa
    Date 25 June 2015
