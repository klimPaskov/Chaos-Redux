## Six fully bald repairs

Final review found hair on six otherwise accepted regional cells. Those exact source, processed, and DDS files were replaced with newly generated, fully hairless portraits while preserving the slot action and regional/origin identity:

- Europe CBB: ration-biscuit tearing on a storm-battered coastal ration structure.
- Asia CBB: brass bosun whistle held beside and away from the jaw on a cyclone breakwater.
- Asia CBH: blank prison ration token bent in one fist in an empty ration-intake room.
- Africa CBE: inverted canteen shaken over an empty palm beside a dust-choked lorry road.
- Africa CBF: cracked looped engine fan belt stretched between both hands in a vehicle yard.
- Africa CBG: ripped cast-iron gate hinge scraped along empty cell bars.

The rebuilt Europe/Asia/Africa source, processed, actual-size, per-region, and DDS-decoded contacts include these repairs. Direct review confirmed fully hairless scalps, feral asymmetry, visible dark burgundy/crimson staining, distinct actions, and readable portrait-scale silhouettes.

## Source and processing status

Every accepted source is a built-in image-generation output for a fictional anonymous subject. No protected portrait or real-person likeness was used. No accepted portrait uses national, sacred, ceremonial, tribal, Indigenous, religious, or living-cultural regalia.

Processing was limited to source preservation, centered cover crop, Lanczos resize to 156x210 RGBA, contact assembly, and DDS conversion with the signed DirectXTex May 2026 texconv backend through .tools/convert_to_dds.py. No face, stain, prop, or identity was painted locally.
