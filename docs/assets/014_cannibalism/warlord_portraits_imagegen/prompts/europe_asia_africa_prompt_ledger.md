## Shared generation constraints

- One fictional adult human-origin subject only; no real-person likeness.
- Extreme facial asymmetry, mismatched eyes, canted jaws, damaged teeth, and
  readable feral behavior; no conventional stoic-officer fallback.
- Period 1930s-1940s materials and setting-specific origin cues.
- No flags, national propaganda, readable text, sacred or ceremonial motifs,
  living Indigenous motifs, modern tactical kit, supernatural anatomy, visible
  victim, or violence in progress.
- Stains in two-stage portraits were added only through imagegen surface edits.
  Clean bases and expression intermediates were never promoted to runtime.
