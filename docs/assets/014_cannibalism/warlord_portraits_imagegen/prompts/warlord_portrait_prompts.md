# Event 014 E14-POR-WARLORD-01 Imagegen Prompts

Generation mode: built-in OpenAI `image_gen`, one independent generation per portrait.

Generation date: 2026-07-11.

Status: creative brief and first-pass prompt record. Exact prompts for the eight accepted source images are preserved in `accepted_production_prompts.md`. The built-in output safety system rejected several injury-specific drafts; the accepted prompts kept conspicuous blood on skin and heavy blood staining on clothing while explicitly excluding wounds, exposed tissue, victims, and violence in progress.

All subjects are fictional adult male-presenting characters. No input image was supplied and no real person, historical leader, existing portrait, or protected Event 014 portrait was used as a reference. The eight slots deliberately vary facial structure, body type, pose, lighting, scavenged gear, backdrop, and origin cues.

## Shared constraints

- Vertical Hearts of Iron IV country-leader portrait source, composed for a final 156 by 210 crop.
- Semi-realistic hand-painted 1936-1945 grand-strategy portrait finish, subdued and tactile rather than glossy or cinematic.
- One fictional adult male-presenting warlord, visibly bald, chest-up or upper-torso framing, head fully inside frame, face readable at thumbnail size.
- Explicit blood and gore on skin and clothing, with an adult-only wartime-horror presentation.
- Invented rough hide, raw cloth, damaged canvas, rope, leather, chain, and scavenged period military components only.
- Regionally adaptable design: no racial, ethnic, national, Indigenous, tribal, or religious label; no living-cultural costume, ceremonial regalia, sacred motif, copied heraldry, or stereotyped body paint.
- No supernatural element, antler, horn, skull mask, magic, glowing eye, monster anatomy, ritual aura, or occult symbol.
- No formal suit, polished dress uniform, grand mantle, crown, throne, supreme-command iconography, fixed flag, national insignia, extremist symbol, medal, readable text, watermark, modern equipment, extra person, severed head, or identifiable victim.
- Strongly distinct from the other seven specified designs.
