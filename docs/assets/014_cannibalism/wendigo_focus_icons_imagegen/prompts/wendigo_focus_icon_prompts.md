# Event 014 Wendigo Focus Icon Imagegen Prompts

## Production contract

Each entry below is a separate built-in `$imagegen` generation request. The generated source is project-bound and becomes one, and only one, 94 by 86 national-focus icon. No entry is a recolour, crop, transform, or reuse of another entry.

Shared prompt scaffold applied to every request:

> Use case: stylized-concept  
> Asset type: Hearts of Iron IV-style national focus icon source art, final use at 94 by 86 pixels  
> Style/medium: original painterly 1930s-1940s grand-strategy horror emblem, aged painted metal and bone textures, antique dark-bronze focus-medallion framing, subtle laurel or industrial framing only where it supports the subject, crisp black outer silhouette, compact high-contrast composition, unsettling fictional body horror  
> Composition/framing: one centered emblem with generous padding, large readable shapes, no tiny background scene, no separate panels  
> Lighting/mood: cold blue-grey frost against charcoal iron, bone white and saturated fresh or dried blood, severe directional light  
> Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat solid #ff00ff chroma-key background for local background removal; one uniform color with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor, reflection, or lighting variation  
> Gore requirement: visible fictional blood and butchered flesh or bone integrated into the central subject, legible after reduction to game size; no identifiable real victim  
> Constraints: fully original source art; subject separated cleanly from the chroma background; crisp edges; no #ff00ff in the emblem; no readable text; no letters; no numbers; no watermark; no modern UI; no logos; no real-world political or military insignia; no antlers or deer skull; no dreamcatcher, totem pole, headdress, medicine wheel, sacred object, tribal rune, ceremonial regalia, or insignia borrowed from any living Indigenous tradition; no fake transparency; no opaque square backdrop; no white sticker rim; no neon glow; no generic skull-only icon
