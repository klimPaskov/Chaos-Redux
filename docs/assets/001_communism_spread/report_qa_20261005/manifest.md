# Event001 generated report-art package

Production ran from 2026-10-05 into 2026-10-06 Europe/Kiev for the 15 unique communist-insurgency report scenes named by the parent brief. Every row is generated fictional or alternate-history documentary art. Generation fits because each scene is a staged event-specific moment rather than a claim about a real person, battle, place, or archival object. No character portrait, historical likeness, readable text, real insignia, or modern prop was created.

The accepted report-image reference family is the canonical event_art/report family at .agents/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/assets/gfx_references/event_art/report/. I reviewed its native contact sheet and catalog, whose report references are Vanilla HOI4 DDS extractions at gfx/event_pictures/report_event_001.dds, report_event_african_soldiers.dds, report_event_airplane_crash.dds, report_event_soldiers_marching.dds, and report_event_soldiers_parade.dds. The family establishes the 210x176 tilted archival-photo canvas and transparent outer corners. The current Event001 runtime report DDSs were also inspected before production.

All 15 accepted source PNGs are the full-frame edge-filled ImageGen edits listed below. The first ImageGen calls requested native transparency, but the provider returned full scenes with translucent or feathered perimeters. Those initial images are retained in source_png/ as lineage evidence. A targeted ImageGen edge-fill edit made each accepted source a continuous opaque rectangular period photograph. The official report-image processor then applied the shared black-and-white/sepia treatment, grain, card tilt, softened photo edge, soft shadow, and final transparent outer corners. This repair used no background-removal tool; the original native-alpha candidates remain untouched.

The exact initial and repair prompts, tool modes, and per-scene intent are in prompts/imagegen_prompt_log.md. Default ImageGen artifact IDs and their workspace copies are mapped in notes/generation_lineage.md. Source hashes, source and alpha metadata, processed-preview metadata, final DDS hashes and headers, alpha corners, and pixel-exact DDS round-trip results are in notes/asset_metadata_and_dds_validation.json.

## Reviews and technical fit

I reviewed every source generation and the completed 210x176 cards in the native-size and 4x contact sheets. The parent approved both sheets and all 15 scenes on 2026-10-06. The repeated ritual scenes have separate compositions: the dark-worker image is a night gathering beneath a press, while the worker-ritual image is a raised view of a floor ring of machine oil, chalk, a gear, and bolts. The whisper and foreshadowing scenes are also distinct: a quiet tool shop with folded cloth versus a large abandoned hall with a hanging cloth and its shadow.

All processed previews and DDS files are 210x176 RGBA. The previews were produced with .agents/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/tools/process_report_event_image.py and the runtime textures with .agents/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/tools/convert_to_dds.py. Every final DDS uses the repository converter's uncompressed 32-bit BGRA layout, one mip level, the expected legacy header, and transparent outer corners. Each DDS decodes pixel-for-pixel to its processed PNG. Detailed values are in the JSON evidence file.
