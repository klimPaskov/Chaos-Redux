# Stage 7 Biological Countermeasure Icon Prompts

All twelve prompts used the official built-in ImageGen workflow with a square 1254x1254 source master, a flat `#00ff00` chroma-key background, generous transparent-margin padding, no text, no watermark, no flag, no national emblem, no protected real-world symbol or logo, no medical cross or caduceus, and a dark charcoal HOI4-style outline.

## Decision prompts

| Asset | Prompt-specific visual brief |
|---|---|
| `decision_bio_activate_surveillance_network` | A compact brass epidemiological monitoring gauge above a parchment signal map with three linked reporting nodes, muted parchment, brass, charcoal, desaturated teal, and warning amber, designed for 32x32 readability. |
| `decision_bio_quarantine_state` | A sealed cordon barrier across a roadway, a plain amber warning seal, and one small shuttered doorway or abandoned suitcase as a civilian hardship undertone, designed for 32x32 readability. |
| `decision_bio_border_control` | A closed steel transport gate spanning a disinfected rail and road corridor with a small sanitation canister and pale vapor cue, designed for later close and reopen variants. |
| `decision_bio_anthrax_antibiotics` | Two sealed glass antibiotic ampoules with amber contents in front of a compact dark clustered spore silhouette, with the ampoules and spore cluster kept visually distinct at 32x32. |
| `decision_bio_plague_antibiotics` | Two sealed treatment ampoules with pale contents in front of an unmistakable segmented black flea/vector silhouette, kept visually distinct from the other agent-specific icons at 32x32. |
| `decision_bio_tularemia_antibiotics` | Two sealed treatment ampoules with warm contents in front of a small tick on a simple grass-and-leaf field sprig, kept visibly distinct from the anthrax spore and plague flea silhouettes at 32x32. |
| `decision_bio_international_medical_mission` | Two plain field trucks approaching an open inspection clipboard and table, suggesting multilateral medical cooperation without any real-world organization mark, flag, initials, or emblem. |
| `decision_bio_sustain_containment` | Layered cordon barrier in front of a small hospital silhouette with a descending outbreak curve and three fading dots, designed as one compact containment-state symbol. |
| `decision_bio_expand_medical_capacity` | A protected canvas field-hospital tent behind a heavy medical satchel and a small utility truck, suggesting stored beds and mobile treatment capacity at 32x32. |
| `decision_bio_expand_biosecurity_capacity` | A sealed metal sample cabinet with glass specimen containers, a compact brass microscope, and a blank generic security shield with no emblem or heraldic device. |

## Shared negative constraints

The prompts excluded modern UI, photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, neon colors, unreadable micro-labels, opaque square backdrops, fake checkerboards, white halos, national insignia, real-world organization logos, and cross-type reuse.

No source master was created by editing or resizing another icon type.
