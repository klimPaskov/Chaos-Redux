- The 12 chlorine, phosgene, mustard, and lewisite source/processed pairs were retained and inspected; they were not regenerated for stylistic preference.
- The interrupted `chem_air_bomb_sarin_long_range` source/processed pair was retained after inspection because its physical streamlined pod and transparent cutout met the same readability bar.
- The remaining 14 missing sources were independently generated one image at a time through imagegen. No still was reused across agents or variants, and no palette-only or resize-only variant was used.
- The official Chaos Redux tech-icon references and the named vanilla aircraft-module references (`airplane_drop_tanks_icon`, `airplane_extra_fueltank_icon`, `airplane_large_bomb_bay_icon`, and `airplane_torpedo_icon`) were inspected before generation.
