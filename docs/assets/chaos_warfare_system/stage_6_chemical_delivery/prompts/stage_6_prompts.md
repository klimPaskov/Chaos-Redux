# Stage 6 chemical-delivery equipment prompts

All 18 source masters were generated independently with the built-in official imagegen workflow. Each prompt requested one sober WWII-era industrial visual on a flat `#00ff00` chroma-key field for local alpha extraction. Prompts prohibited readable text, logos, people, victims, injury, explosions, gas clouds, modern materials, and cartoon treatment. The equipment visual target was a compact hand-painted HOI4 equipment card with worn olive-drab metal, dark iron, brass fittings, aged wood, strong silhouette, and readability at `131x52`; the separate idea icon targets the compact `64x64` HOI4 idea-icon contract.
