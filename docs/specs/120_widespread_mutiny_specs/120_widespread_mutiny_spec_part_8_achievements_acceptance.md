# 120 Widespread Mutiny, Part 8: Achievements and acceptance

## Four achievements with substantial campaign histories

The achievement keys below are semantic working IDs awaiting shared-registry allocation. Their labels and descriptions are direction only. Each achievement uses the project's current campaign eligibility rules and a specific Event 120 history. Triggering the opening, reaching Chaos 600 or clicking a focus is insufficient.

The tracked history follows the actual country or council lineage, recorded player-control transitions, originating formations and relevant diplomatic relationships. Country release, tag reuse, focus bypass, save loading and repeated callbacks cannot manufacture a clean attempt. Ordinary inherited saves preserve their genuine eligible history.

### E120_A01, working label: An army kept intact

**Eligible country:** A human-controlled original government facing a naturally selected Event 120 incident. Its opening deployed army must contain at least 20,000 personnel. The achievement is visible once the event is known, with final text describing the substantial recovery challenge.

**Required history:** Enter with Disaffection at least 60, an active long-war factor, and either the supply or heavy-loss factor. At least two organically admitted cohorts must together involve 15% of the incident's opening deployed manpower. Resolve at least two distinct primary grievance families through actual commitments, including at least one real rest or supply delivery. Complete every promised milestone on time. Finish country recovery, then sustain lawful service and Agreement confidence of at least 70 for a further 90 days.

At least 90% of the original affected formations must remain in lawful service or their agreed rest period, with identity continuity proved. Their remaining manpower and equipment must be genuine. The achievement does not require the event to restore ordinary combat losses. Integer formation counts round the required surviving share upward, so a small eligible set usually requires all its formations.

**Disqualifiers:** Scripted or debug outbreak setup, a deliberate government promise breach used to cause spread, suppression, defection, paid or immediate discharge of a qualifying formation, substitution of fresh formations for the original set, or an unrecorded control transfer. New unrelated wars do not automatically invalidate a delivered agreement, but breaking the protected service terms does.

**Why it is hard:** The country must preserve an exhausted, meaningfully large part of its real army through two different practical remedies while a relevant war continues. The recovery period prevents an instant unlock after buying an agreement.

**Icon direction:** Two recognizable service helmets beside a repaired leave roster and intact unit pennant, expressing continuity and delivered obligations. Keep the roster textless. Required art is the completed, grey and not-eligible triplet.

**Tracking:** Opening pressure flags and denominator, original formation set, actual primary demands, organic spread provenance, delivery deadlines and receipts, excluded responses, recovery close date and the subsequent 90-day compliance window.

### E120_A02, working label: Leave carried home

**Eligible country:** A human-controlled government with a natural linked 114 and 120 incident and actual Event 009 settlement of the affected war relationship. Visible after a compatible Desertion Crisis connection is discovered.

**Required history:** Reconcile at least 5,000 actual Event 114 departed personnel into the combined crisis without a second source loss. Complete a single consistent political settlement with distinct material dispositions. After Event 009 ends the matching war, execute the promised repatriation or agreed discharge, recover at least 90% of equipment recorded as recoverable and accessible under that agreement, and honor every protected-personnel obligation for 180 days.

The country must remain engaged in a different real war for at least 30 days after the matching peace. Its unrelated deployed formations must remain outside this event's discharge and ceasefire operations. This makes exact war scoping part of the accomplishment. The recovered-equipment denominator is frozen from the verified agreed recoverable property, excludes genuinely inaccessible property before agreement, and cannot be reduced by deliberate disposal afterward.

**Disqualifiers:** A duplicated debit, refund, return or transfer, invented equipment, missed promised transport, forced remobilization during protection, using a national peace proxy to clear the wrong front, manual scenario setup, or a later destroyed record being re-created for the count. Honest recorded combat deaths cannot become surviving returnees.

**Why it is hard:** The outcome requires three event owners, a real war settlement and a long delivered discharge program while another military commitment continues.

**Icon direction:** A service kit and secured rifle beside a homeward rail ticket shape, with a second distant front marker remaining on a small map. Avoid tiny numbers and generated lettering.

**Tracking:** Originating 114 liability IDs, accepted 120 custody, actual settled pair and war scope, unrelated-war duration, secured-property denominator, measured returns and the 180-day protected period.

### E120_A03, working label: A council becomes a country

**Eligible country:** A human-controlled joint armed council originating naturally from opposing formations in Brothers Across the Trenches. Hidden until a viable joint country is created, then its requirements become factual and readable.

**Required history:** Found the council with at least 5,000 surviving personnel from each of two opposing legal owners, preserving their actual formation and equipment histories. Choose the civilian territorial republic route directly. Establish civilian control of recruitment and public institutions, resolve the founding armed dispute through separate accepted agreements with both former authorities, and maintain an independent civilian settlement for 180 days.

The country must sustain its actual supply and at least one functioning civilian factory or the completed contracted reconstruction alternative. Both origin groups receive their promised service or repatriation outcomes. Territory remains within the frozen founding footprint plus the one lawful indispensable adjacent supply state when that objective is used. Foreign recognition can be achieved through the route's alternative diplomacy, but both founding armed relationships must reach an actual lawful settlement for this achievement.

**Disqualifiers:** Emergency-command route first, fabricated or duplicated forces, imposed civilian mandate, annexing unrelated territory, a war of opportunistic expansion, free reconstruction grants counted as production, manual or debug setup, or a carrier reset used to restart the 180-day period.

**Why it is hard:** Surviving armed separation is only the beginning. The player must turn a two-origin military coalition into a supplied civilian country and end both founding conflicts without an expansion shortcut.

**Icon direction:** Two differently shaped service caps set below a civilian assembly table and regional seal. The institution is the subject, with no portrait of an invented person.

**Tracking:** Both founding owners, proved personnel and property transfers, chosen route, civilian mandate, lawful territorial set, the two settlements, supply and reconstruction facts, and the 180-day institutional history.

### E120_A04, working label: Temporary means temporary

**Eligible country:** A human-controlled domestic or joint council that naturally chose emergency defensive command during an actual attack. Hidden until the emergency mandate is accepted.

**Required history:** Accept a single 90-day emergency mandate, commit the route's real Army XP and force obligations, and preserve the valid capital or declared backup capital plus at least 75% of the founding civilian population under lawful administration through the emergency. Repel or obtain an accepted end to at least one actual hostile attempt to eliminate the council. The threat must be a real military relationship with combat or a verified imminent attack, with no arranged mock war solely for eligibility.

Transfer authority to a civilian mandate by the original deadline, remove emergency recruitment and command powers, and complete the promised review and release obligations. Sustain that civilian settlement for 180 days with Agreement confidence at least 70 at the final check and no overdue protected-personnel duty. The operational-recovery benefit ends on schedule and cannot be carried into the permanent government.

**Disqualifiers:** Extending or resetting the mandate, focus bypass used to skip its sacrifice, any deliberate founding-footprint recapture loop, a second emergency used to preserve the original temporary benefit, free-force reconstruction, scripted setup, or survival bought by abandoning the promised civilian handover.

**Why it is hard:** The player must survive a real emergency, accept its economic tradeoff and relinquish temporary powers while the institution still has the capacity to keep them.

**Icon direction:** An officer's field baton placed beside an open civilian ledger, with a restrained clock motif. The transfer of authority remains clear at native size.

**Tracking:** Original mandate start and deadline, actual hostile threat, capital succession, founding population denominator, committed costs, temporary powers and their removal, civilian transition, subsequent promises and the 180-day compliance window.

## Shared achievement safeguards

Each unlock is evaluated from recorded actual outcomes. A setup flag is a disqualifier for that attempt and cannot vanish on ordinary cleanup. Official player transfer into an event-created council is allowed when the originating campaign and incident were eligible. A console switch or an unproved inherited tag cannot supply the same proof.

The system records why an attempt has become ineligible and exposes that through the existing achievement interface where appropriate. It does not add another event currency or a public audit dashboard. Ordinary event prose concentrates on the current military and political situation.

## Evidence needed for completion

The formation, protected-personnel, local ceasefire and limited-settlement scenarios are native feasibility requirements. They must pass before those behaviors are described as implemented. A saved variable bearing the desired name, an AI order preference, a generated template or a screenshot of a popup is insufficient evidence of the physical result.

Source-level checks can establish registration, references, authored arithmetic, accepted route coverage, explicit guards, cleanup ownership and asset lookup. A complete probability audit needs actual candidate pools and implemented helper evaluation. Rendered UI and native-size art review establish presentation behavior within their tested limits. A live campaign or a relevant user-provided save can establish particular actual state transitions, with game version and setup recorded.

The current package is a completed planning handoff. Its formula audit and full source reading are documented separately. It does not claim that these implementation scenarios were run. Future implementers report which exact scenarios passed, the evidence used, remaining defects and any still-unproved native capability.
