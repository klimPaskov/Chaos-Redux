# 120 Widespread Mutiny, Part 3: Brothers Across the Trenches

## What changes at Chaos 600+

The evolution gives soldiers an alternative source of military agreement across the front. An exhausted formation can discover that the troops opposite it are refusing the same war. Contact can lead to mutual restraint, a joint demand, a bounded local ceasefire and, after a failed crackdown, one armed council that resists both governments.

The historical dossier separates local fraternization from the different institutions and authority claims of soldiers' councils. The bilateral armed country in this evolution is a deliberate fictional extrapolation from those separate precedents. [Historical research, S03, S05 and S08](120_widespread_mutiny_research.md)

The evolution changes the choices and the source of authority. It does not merely enlarge a national combat penalty. The ordinary government can still deliver leave, repair supplies, agree discharge, regain reliable service or use a properly prepared loyal force.

## Eligibility, entry and persistence

Eligibility requires the evolution to be enabled, Chaos at least 600, an active eligible incident or valid evolved opening, and two opposing human-compatible military constituencies on the same actual front. At least one has Disaffection of 60 or more. The counterpart must have its own unresolved incident or satisfy ordinary low-war-support admission with a real affected formation.

An exact front record identifies the actual war relationship, both legal owners, any commanding hosts, the participating formations and a contiguous local boundary. The initial sector covers the smallest coherent frontage around contact, normally one state on each side and never more than three participating states in total. States are descriptive boundaries for this contract, not permission to transfer all their inhabitants, factories or unrelated units.

The record is not inferred merely from two countries being at war or owning adjacent territory. The formations must face each other in that operational sector. A country fighting several enemies needs separate exact records. A shared faction front cannot speak for every allied army.

| Entry path | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| An ordinary incident reaches eligibility during play | Expose the contact possibility immediately and start the contextual opportunity timer without another Event 120 draw |
| A new Event 120 occurrence begins at 600+ with a valid opposing pair | Use an evolved opening that identifies both constituencies and begins the contact stage directly |
| A compatible recorded fraternization already exists | Adopt its exact actors and surviving commitments, then continue from the proven contact state |
| The conditions disappear before contact | Suspend or retire that opportunity without inventing a pact or removing ordinary mutiny consequences |

The active opportunity has the 90-day contextual MTTH anchor in Part 6. Evolved entry can start at contact, but neither path skips reciprocal consent, force ownership or local enforcement requirements. Eligibility, activation, a branch unlock and the evolution history entry each give zero Chaos.

After a pact exists, a fall below 600 or a disabled future-evolution setting stops new opportunities. It does not erase an agreement, return defected soldiers, dissolve a real council or refund a historical loss. Existing commitments reach a lawful outcome. Re-enabling the evolution cannot issue another activation reward or duplicate the same front record.

## Contact and reciprocal commitment

Contact begins with a bounded report of observed behavior. Soldiers can exchange a burial arrangement, a message, a warning about an unwanted offensive or a delegation. The government knows what its own representatives observed. It does not automatically know the opponent's exact confidence, plans or hidden supply state.

The first proposal has a 10-day response window. Each party receives the same immutable terms, its own formations and obligations, and the other side's claimed commitments. A reply can accept, ask for one verifiable guarantee, or reject. A request for a guarantee has one disclosed extension of up to 10 days. It cannot be repeated to freeze the front indefinitely.

An expired or unattended proposal grants no consent. A human player never loses authority through another player's open popup. The accepting actor rechecks the same proposal at the moment of response. A later front, government or carrier incarnation cannot inherit that acceptance.

The first joint compact chooses at most two practical demands, such as reciprocal rotation and safe recovery of wounded personnel, or local stand-down and protected discharge. Recognition of delegates is part of administering those demands. A country-level peace demand can be referred to Event 009 or ordinary diplomacy, but it is not already satisfied by this compact.

If only one side accepts, that cohort can continue its domestic negotiation or withdraw from contact. It does not become subordinate to the enemy. A government may reject its soldiers' proposal while those soldiers continue their refusal. That disagreement is a visible military crisis, rather than a hidden automatic diplomatic signature.

## The local ceasefire contract

The first valid compact lasts 30 days. It can be renewed twice after a new check of actual delivery and continued consent, for a maximum of 90 continuous days under this temporary mechanism. A longer arrangement needs an actual political settlement, agreed rotation of the participating formations or a lawful end to their service.

Within the agreed sector, participating forces stop offensive combat and hostile movement against each other. They can hold positions, treat and recover wounded, receive permitted supplies and leave along agreed safe routes. They cannot exploit the pause for a surprise breakthrough, transfer an attack to a renamed template, or send fresh units through the same sector under an unrecorded order.

The game must enforce this for human-controlled and AI-controlled participants. If it cannot prevent the relevant hostile order, the design requires a verified mechanism that detects and resolves the first attempted breach before the ceasefire can be represented as effective. Merely lowering organization, discouraging AI attacks or writing a political promise does not complete this behavior. The handoff records the unresolved native capability gate.

The same governments continue fighting elsewhere. The compact does not apply `white_peace` or a national truce. It does not freeze unrelated allies, naval combat, air operations or other wars. Operations originating outside the sector that strike a protected participant are breaches by the responsible authority where that authority is part of the agreement. An unbound third party remains a real external danger and cannot be silently forced into the compact.

A new allied formation entering the sector must accept the applicable commitments or remain outside its protection. If a third party makes the arrangement physically impossible, the affected corridor is suspended with a factual notice and a protected withdrawal attempt. No side receives an invulnerability bonus or a free teleport.

| Change | Required resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Orderly expiry | End the temporary restrictions after the disclosed notice and resolve each formation's next duty |
| Voluntary withdrawal | Give 10 days of notice where safe, complete agreed movements and retain delivered benefits |
| Deliberate surprise attack | End protection for the responsible actor, reduce its relevant confidence by 30 once and process actual combat outcomes |
| Front moves beyond the recorded sector | Revalidate the surviving footprint, preserve exact obligations and offer a new compact only for new geography |
| One participant is destroyed, annexed or changes ownership | Resolve its surviving obligations through the lawful successor, without treating a recycled tag as the original signatory |
| Event 009 settles the relevant war relationship | Close only the matching combat restraint and begin the remaining stand-down or discharge work |

The counterpart sees the fact and responsibility of a breach when established, together with uncertainty if attribution is unresolved. The event does not manufacture perfect intelligence to justify retaliation.

## A joint soldiers' council

A council can form as a negotiating institution without acquiring a country. It represents the participating soldiers, records both origins and asks each government for a feasible settlement. The council can fail to persuade its own soldiers. The Agreement confidence value reflects whether its commitments are believed, not merely whether a government has signed a paper.

The two country incidents retain their own values and exact people. The paired front has one common phase and terms. The UI can show the two existing values for each actual actor when relevant. It does not create a third combined numerical gauge. For checks requiring both parties' confidence, use the lower of the two values. For joint escalation pressure, use the higher Disaffection. These derived comparisons are not additional public resources.

The council has three broad choices. It can broker reciprocal return to service under delivered guarantees, arrange demobilization or repatriation separately for each origin, or maintain the local compact while seeking a wider lawful settlement. A government accepting one origin's return does not obtain the other origin's troops or equipment.

One cohort can leave the joint institution peacefully after its own obligations are settled. The remaining cohort continues under its own history if viable. A joint council does not require permanent ideological unity, a new faction or an automatic merger of countries.

## Crackdown and armed separation

A government can attempt containment against its own resistant contingent with the ordinary loyal-force preparation rules. The preview explicitly identifies that the target belongs to a paired compact and that a crackdown may unite both contingents. It cannot borrow the other government's troops or debit the other country's resources without an accepted separate commitment.

When a crackdown fails, the remaining contingents decide whether to stand together. The joint armed outcome requires both constituencies to reject renewed government authority, actual surviving formations from both, a viable local footprint, an accepted institutional mandate and a fully prepared country package. A high Chaos value alone cannot supply missing troops or land.

Before creating a country, evaluate a compatible established Event 116 warlord. A single recipient is preferred when it can safely receive both contingents, honor the joint protection terms and sustain them. If only one side wants that recipient, the two dispositions separate rather than forcing a merger. The same formation has one final physical outcome.

When the joint-country gate passes, the armed council inherits only the actual committed formations and measured property. It becomes hostile to both founding governments where the failed crackdown and actual war context justify that relationship. It does not automatically enter every war of either former owner, confiscate their navy or receive their full technology union. Its sovereign or provisional future is played through Part 4.

If the gate fails, the game retains the live refusal, withdrawal and negotiated-release possibilities. It never deletes the original formations in anticipation of a country that did not activate. A stranded contact group can dissolve politically while its surviving soldiers remain accounted for under their actual owners.

## Rare variations with complete exits

| Variation | Why it occurs | Meaningful choice | Ending and guard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet reciprocal stand-down | Both cohorts want rest and mistrust wider politics | Deliver rotation and let the local contact end | No country forms, no permanent alliance or recurring contact reward |
| Unequal concessions | One government can deliver leave while the other cannot | Separate the returns, fund a consensual aid delivery, or continue only the unresolved compact | The successful side is not punished twice, and aid has an actual donor debit |
| Delegates lose their mandate | A council breaks its promise or cannot control its own contingent | Replace the negotiating institution, accept discharge, or attempt valid containment | Replacing delegates does not reset the force or payment ledger |
| A mixed civilian district rejects military rule | Armed council control exceeds civilian acceptance | Negotiate jurisdiction, protect civilian representation, or retreat | Military occupation gives no automatic core or free manpower |
| A former enemy becomes the only viable protector | A nearby recipient can sustain the force after both original authorities fail | Accept a limited treaty or retain a precarious independent position | Treaty terms specify authority and preserve repatriation rights, without automatic puppet or faction effects |

## One campaign-scale presentation milestone

The package accepts one rare super-event concept, working label `SE120_SOLDIERS_CONGRESS`. This is a semantic label awaiting allocation, not a reserved presentation or audio number.

It marks a real sustained change in military authority. Require at least three distinct reciprocal compacts on disjoint recorded sectors, involving at least three countries and two hostile war relationships. At least two involved countries must be majors or have at least 15 functioning factories each. The compacts must have survived 30 days. In addition, either a joint council has sustained a civilian-backed independent settlement for 90 days, or two founding governments have delivered national service settlements through their ordinary institutions.

The milestone describes this limited movement accurately. It does not announce peace everywhere or close unrelated wars. It fires once per campaign after actual conditions are committed. A new event draw, country release, reused carrier or renewed local compact cannot trigger it again. Its presentation grants no separate Chaos, equipment, manpower or political concession.

The image direction is a soldiers' and civilian delegates' meeting linked to several fronts, using contrasting uniforms and service objects without invented historical leaders. Text direction concerns the changed source of authority, the agreements actually achieved and the uncertainty that remains. A real attributed quotation and a unique cleared musical recording are production requirements. Part 7 and the super-event prompt define that work. Ordinary contact and a first local truce use event or news presentation.
