# 120 Widespread Mutiny, Part 7: Presentation, assets and text direction

## What the player needs to understand

The event presents soldiers as organized people with immediate military leverage and specific demands. The opening emphasizes the actual duty being refused, the affected formations and the government's next decision. Later reports distinguish a promise, partial delivery, resumed service, discharge, defection and combat loss. A picture or dramatic title must never imply that a country has collapsed when one battalion has requested leave.

The ordinary category uses a static documentary picture above a basic formatted value table and the normal decision list. It shows Disaffection, Agreement confidence, the selected cohort and its next deadline. Native war support, force strength, supply and costs appear as supporting facts and tooltips. The council uses the same meanings and visual vocabulary in its ordinary country decision category.

At most five primary actions and three missions appear at once. Phase replacement keeps delivery and recovery visible. Contextual target choice uses native selection and reports, with a clear current-country and cohort label. The paired-front report can show both actors' two existing values when information is legitimately known. Unknown opponent information is described qualitatively. The player does not manage another meter.

The proposed static picture fits ordinary categories with a basic table. A later implementation that adds rich controls, a custom interactive panel or animated meters must reassess that presentation choice. The proposal does not depend on such controls.

## Text direction for decisions, focuses and history

All labels introduced in this specification are working labels. The implementation writer supplies final titles, names, descriptions, buttons, tooltip prose, focus descriptions, party and country names, achievement copy and catalog wording from these directions. No sentence here is a localisation template.

Decision names should express the actual commitment. The description gives the relevant actor, the problem being addressed and the military tradeoff. Requirements and costs remain factual and complete. A player should be able to distinguish a negotiation fee from the practical work that follows it. An unavailable action names the missing force, authority, route or resource.

The compact route uses the language of service obligations, credible guarantees and limited institutional rights. The civilian republic route concerns representation, courts, reconstruction and control of recruitment. The emergency command route emphasizes its immediate defense task, named oversight, deadline and transfer of power. All three share the same underlying force and property facts.

Focus descriptions connect an institutional action to a concrete consequence. A supply focus explains the locality or route it prepares. A political focus names the authority that changes. An external focus identifies whose acceptance remains necessary. Completion tooltips disclose visible effects, while concealed future opportunities remain undisclosed until their own reveal conditions pass.

Country names combine the actual region or legally recognized state identity with the current institutional form. A joint origin uses a geographically credible shared label. It cannot use an unrelated revolutionary name because a historical council once existed elsewhere. Ideology and party naming follow the selected local context and route mandate. An institutional leader is described as an office or collective body, without an invented personal biography.

Event Details gives the premise and current actors without listing hidden mechanics or future surprises. Event Log records actual occurrences, settlements and evolved behavior with the correct actor and locality. The evolution entry follows concrete activation context and gives no Chaos by itself. The super-event milestone has a separate factual record.

Use clear, connected English. Avoid em dashes, semicolons, staged contrasts, terse dramatic fragments and generic paperwork language. The tone can convey apprehension, relief or uncertainty through specific observed details. Ordinary event text does not advertise achievements.

## Identity and source modes

Historical photographs need an attributable collection item, creator when known, date, subject, rights status and a crop that preserves meaning. The research dossier supplies leads from the National Army Museum and Bundesarchiv. Those leads are not cleared assets. A reused archival image cannot be captioned as an event it did not depict.

Fictional event scenes can use authored period documentary imagery. They must be classified as fictional or alternate history in the manifest. Source and generation prompts should specify period uniforms, weapons, environment and the actual dramatic action. Generated text, fabricated insignia passed off as genuine, modern equipment and theatrical crowds are rejected.

The collective portrait is an institutional composition with no person. If a particular country later uses a real commander already in the game, preserve that person's identity and ownership, inspect the existing portrait, and source any approved replacement through the portrait workflow. The accepted package does not authorize invented faces for grounded historical offices. A small advisor portrait is a separate consumer and cannot be inferred from the existence of a leader.

For a new council flag, first establish the actual regional identity, relevant heraldry and the absence or presence of an attested movement flag. An attested design is reconstructed faithfully. A fictional institutional variant records its regional source motifs and its alternate-history synthesis. Each visually distinct flag uses a separate flat image-generation source. Preserve the authored emblem through export and compare geometry, color fields, symbol count, orientation and heraldic details against the approved references.

A living original country's no-suffix base flag remains its own asset. Event 120 applies a route-specific cosmetic identity only when the actual political transformation justifies it. The small flag must remain recognizable. Final TGA dimensions and origin conventions match native consumers, with no upside-down copies or UI workaround for a bad encoding.

## Why the soldiers retain conventional unit art

The event changes military authority and service obligations. Its human soldiers use the existing rifles, artillery, armor, transport and training they actually possess. Their mutiny does not create a different biological or technical class. Their formations therefore retain appropriate conventional templates, equipment identities, counters, voices and models.

A council can standardize a mixed inherited force through the paid military branch in Part 4. That reorganization changes actual battalions, equipment and training through supported ordinary systems. It does not justify a new custom subunit simply to place a council emblem on the map. Country flags, institutional art and the decision presentation communicate the new authority.

## Super-event production direction

The single accepted presentation corresponds to the exact sustained milestone in Part 3. Its title direction concerns the established movement's reach and new source of military consent. Its description direction identifies what has actually happened, which constituencies are represented, and the wars or obligations that remain. Button direction is acknowledgment of this campaign fact. None of the working labels supplies final text.

The verified Kiel council sentence in historical source S08 is a research candidate concerning claimed political power. It is not selected final copy. A researcher must check the complete context, precise attribution, language, translation rights and suitability for a multi-origin fictional movement. A cultural remark must likewise come from a sourced, relevant tradition or contemporary observation, with no invented quotation or lyric fragment.

Audio direction is restrained collective resolve and the uncertainty of a military institution becoming political. Research exact compositions, arrangements and recordings, including creator, performer, date, source URL, rights and thematic fit. A composition's age does not establish rights to a recording. The selected recording must be unique within the shared presentation inventory and suitable for the actual milestone. No synthetic test tone or unspecified song can satisfy the final asset.

The current audio owner contract uses an OGG music cue under the shared music system, with a unique playback identifier, the current volume variants from mute through the supported upper level, station registration and replacement playback. A muted replacement still stops the previous presentation. Playback must work for the setting active when the event appears and when the user changes it. The coding prompt names the exact current skill as the authority.

The super-event number, audio number and any cultural-reference final text remain unallocated production requirements. Event number 120 is not a safe presentation number. The repository evidence records that presentation 120 already belongs to another event.

## Asset acceptance and practical review

Each asset row keeps its exact consumer, source mode, source references, generation request when applicable, selected master, conversions and final filename. Save the actual request before production. Missing historical prompt data stays marked unavailable. Existing candidates are reviewed before fresh generation.

Review source size and native size, then the actual DDS or TGA round trip. The report card needs transparent corner space, a subtle consistent tilt and a soft shadow. News needs a readable central subject in monochrome. Flags need all three sizes and correct orientation. Icons need a distinct silhouette and correct engine lookup. Portraits need the actual institutional or personal subject. The super-event image needs room for its surrounding UI and a readable composition.

The family reference roots supplied by the current assets skill are authoritative. Exact paths and current canonical examples are verified during production. Record the consumer-specific resolution where an older source differs. Completion requires a row-to-runtime crosswalk and a readable in-context preview where a renderer is available, with missing live-game evidence stated accurately.
