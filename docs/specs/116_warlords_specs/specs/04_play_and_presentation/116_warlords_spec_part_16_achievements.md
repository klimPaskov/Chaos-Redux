# Part 16: Difficult achievements across the campaign

Achievements should recognize distinct difficult ways of navigating the warlord era. They use actual campaign history and current outcomes. Clicking a formation button, changing a name, or opening a scenario is not an accomplishment by itself.

The identifiers and descriptions below are structural design records. Final achievement titles, descriptions, and flavor remain direction-only. All icons use the shared 64 by 64 consumer and current approved template/overlay.

An achievement may be unreachable in a campaign that never creates its required route. Do not fabricate extra warlords or force a third government into a peace treaty to make the collection complete. The achievement design should encourage hard campaigns without making a successful smaller settlement look like failure.

## Tracking rules

Freeze relevant natural-origin baselines at the actual committed creation or entry moment. Record political lineage rather than tag alone, exact owner receipts rather than a UI index, and actual player participation under the shared achievement rules. Count a campaign from real war and military contribution evidence. A mere declaration, ally's unaided victory, or renamed old war does not establish player accomplishment.

Each achievement has one durable completion receipt. Current-state conditions such as independent government or civilian authority must hold through the stated period. Historical preconditions such as having once been a genuine subordinate remain recorded after liberation. Do not confuse those two kinds of evidence.

Achievement tracking observes the existing owner events and bounded participant records. It must not add recurring scans of every country or army. If the needed participation or continuity proof is not available, that achievement remains unimplemented until the shared owner supports it. A convenient approximate flag is not sufficient.

Ordinary achievements exclude manual scenario and debug/CXT origin, inherited fabricated completion, and test-country participation. A future explicit manual challenge can have its own separate record, but no such challenge is required to unlock these achievements or access a scenario. Seat changes chosen as part of a lawful ordinary event use the shared player-history rules and do not automatically imply debug origin.

Achievements do not add Chaos, restore resources, or erase costs. Their purpose is to recognize outcomes. In particular, there is no civilian-death threshold, atrocity score, or reward for repeatedly releasing and conquering the same population.

## Text and asset handoff

Final wording should make the role, difficult accomplishment, and important exclusion understandable without reproducing implementation logic. Keep identities and tone distinct across civilian recovery, independent development, patronage, unification, terminal conquest, liberation, continuity, and settlement.

Each icon uses a clear small silhouette and the approved achievement overlay. Avoid embedding text, tiny country labels, flags too detailed to read, or a decorative image that implies a different route. The achievement prompt includes the exact proof and anti-exploit rules so the icon and wording cannot accidentally celebrate a generic button click.
