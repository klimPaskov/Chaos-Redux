# Part 15: Assets, localisation, and super-event production

The visual identity should show military authority taking root in actual places. Use recognizable regional settings, ordinary armed forces, improvised or established administrative spaces, and visible changes in political scale. The escalation moves from a local command to an international order through what the campaign has accomplished.

This part is a complete production plan. No art, portrait, flag, audio, animation, or 3D binary was produced or verified in this planning turn.

## Art direction

Early warlord imagery should be specific to the parent and region. A local headquarters, depot, railway station, road junction, or government building can convey the seizure without pretending to be an exact game screenshot. The founder's portrait must be the actual character. Do not depict a newly invented face as a known historical general.

Later imagery can show rival command centers, political negotiations under military pressure, reconstruction, and the contrast between a personal retinue and a functioning state administration. Avoid generic explosions as a substitute for political meaning. Civilian suffering is treated with restraint and concrete consequences.

Supreme War State imagery should communicate coordinated military government and a consolidated chain of orders. It should not imply global conquest before that has happened. Final Warlord imagery can show the international extent of that command, with geographically grounded theaters and a sense of remaining opposition. Defeat imagery should show a broken central order and surviving governments or commands, with damage and loss still present.

## Flags and dynamic country coverage

Reuse an appropriate existing flag when the carrier is genuinely that country and no new political identity requires a new asset. For a new personal military regime, approve a regional identity and its finite set of prepared flag variants before enabling that country package. A dynamic country cannot rely on runtime image generation.

Research real regional symbols, color conventions, and historical context before reconstructing a grounded design. New flags are clean, opaque, flat flag graphics. Do not use cloth folds, dramatic lighting, slogans, or photographic backgrounds as the usable flag texture. Compare symbol placement, proportions, geometry, and colors against the approved reference. Review all three native sizes, especially the 10 by 7 silhouette.

A country package specifies valid behavior for every reachable political identity. If several identities intentionally share a flag, document that choice. Avoid an unrelated fallback flag because a carrier's ideology changed. Supreme War State uses its own new approved flag family while retaining the host's actual country continuity.

Country color, flag, noun, definite form, adjective, ideology, leader, and focus package must agree about which actor exists. Political renaming must not make two live governments indistinguishable or make an independent rival appear subordinated.

## Portrait ownership

The actual founder's existing game portrait is the first choice and remains tied to his actual character. A required new grounded portrait needs attributed source imagery, an identity-preserving crop, and the current portrait-creator workflow. The grounded styled-final RunPod step belongs to the user alone. A source placeholder remains explicitly pending until an approved final exists.

Native ImageGen portrait generation is reserved for a wholly fictional leader of a wholly fictional polity under the current rules. 067's canonical Generalissimo remains 067-owned, so 116 does not generate another face. A council or regency can use a people-free institutional composition rather than inventing a roster of historical officers.

Keep source identity, crop, rights/provenance, provider task where applicable, final review, and consumer mapping. Do not call a generated approximation of a real general a faithful transferred portrait.

## Units, models, and sound

The country uses ordinary human military units and appropriate existing base-game models, counters, and sound. Preserve the originating military culture where the native setup supports it. The implementation brief must specify the country culture/entity mapping and verify infantry, cavalry, motorized, armor, air, and naval consumers that are actually used.

No new custom sub-unit, equipment family, uniform model, custom voice bank, or bespoke animated soldier is part of this design. Personal loyalty and command are political mechanics rather than a new battlefield species. Appropriate verified native visual reuse is the planned solution, not an unfinished custom-unit placeholder.

If a later accepted design genuinely introduces a distinct unit family, it becomes a separate full production scope. It must follow the current unit-sound provenance and licensing rules and the current 3D pipeline. That pipeline requires an item-free body, separate single-item geometry tasks, eligible provider rig/action intake with receipts, and live Blender fitting and action review. The legacy direct-Blender route cannot be silently substituted. No such production was run here.

Frame animation and native raids are also not required by the accepted mechanic. A static super-event and ordinary campaign systems communicate the designed actions. If an animated surface is later accepted, it requires actual frames/actions, state logic, a static fallback, and consumer verification. Moving or filtering one still does not constitute completed animation.

## Localisation direction matrix

| Surface | Actor perspective and information | Tone and required variation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First parent report | Loss of the actual general, region, forces, and authority | Concrete and immediate, proportionate to the parent's size and current war |
| Founder report | Seized command, available resources, political claim, immediate threats | Ambitious but materially constrained, no omniscient prediction of eventual world conquest |
| Later defection report | The same origin country's widening command problem | Escalate through observed changes and consequences, avoid identical repeated copy |
| Parent choices | Recovery, security, concessions, detention, and emergency government | Clear political intent and cost without revealing hidden outcomes |
| Warlord routes | Personal loyalty, institutions, regional government, patronage, succession | Distinct political futures and tradeoffs, no generic administrative jargon |
| Treaties | Actual signatories, authority, territory, forces, and duration | Precise enough to understand the bargain, with dynamic actors and named places |
| Unification | Current rivals and actual military integration | Achievement through campaigns, without implying a title itself compels obedience |
| Final Warlord | Cross-regional military obedience and remaining opposition | Restrained global scale, researched historical allusion where approved, no generic apocalyptic filler |
| Defeat and aftermath | Broken central command, surviving governments, present losses | Serious and specific, neither instant peace nor restoration of the dead |
| Event Details | Current identity, context, status, and history | No effect dump, no helper names or internal receipts |
| Achievements | Distinct difficult accomplishment | Concise route identity after text approval, no body-count humor |

Dynamic placeholders include actual country names, general and ruler names, relevant regions and capitals, signatories, current dates, and the correct owner state. They must not expose nonexistent actors or private data through stale saved scopes. Use grammar-safe localization for noun, definite form, adjective, and plural participants.

No sample localisation is included. The research document contains attributed candidate quotations only as research evidence, not as approved player copy. Final text requires the appropriate research and editorial pass after route and actor context are fixed.

## Super-event text and audio gate

The completed text-research handoff supports a distinction between domestic consolidation, international military dominance, and the reversibility of conquest. It offers primary-source candidates and describes their context and limitations. Parent acceptance is of the direction and evidence, not a final pasted quotation or button label.

The audio owner must select a source recording appropriate to each accepted cue, document composition and recording rights separately, listen to the actual source, record the intended excerpt and edit, and verify the encoded consumer. A historical composition or old recording date alone is not a complete rights or suitability check. No final recording, license clearance, listening result, excerpt, mix, or OGG was approved in this turn.

Review each new asset at native size and in its actual consumer. Check alpha, compression/export mode, clipping, highlight/shine behavior, country identity, political variants, scaling, localization length, and visual contrast. Shared GUI and super-event framework changes remain with their owners.

Keep temporary event asset evidence while production is active or blocked. Before an implementation is declared complete, promote durable evidence, prove there are no runtime references to the temporary workspace, and remove the temporary event workspace under the project rules. The present planning package contains directions and evidence maps, not a falsely completed asset workspace.
