## Engineering gates that remain open

The selected source evidence does not prove live cross-country character transfer preserving every required changing attribute, exact ordinary-army subset transfer, class-specific stockpile conservation, or reliable custody of every borrowed air/naval asset. Those capabilities must be established before their corresponding birth paths can ship.

The inspected 019 reconstruction path is a private exact owner operation with narrower guarantees. It is not a generic substitute for the selected actual general or an ordinary division. Existing 019 breakaways can be preserved in place, but new cross-country operations still require their own proofs.

The inspected old 067 event and the newer 067 specification require owner reconciliation. The acknowledged Cohesion transition, canonical removal, country/focus integration, and separate world-end competition must be implemented against the actual owner version.

Genuine subject command must cover current and future troops and independent military policy. Native subject flags and war-goal restrictions do not automatically establish that. Current global command, field-strength comparison, sustained replacement capacity, usable routes, and comprehensive change invalidation require verified observations and bounded owner integration. Unknown evidence fails readiness.

The existing release coordinator is registered for other owners and has an explicit irreversible frontier. 116 requires an accepted extension. Exact 019 provider and management helpers, relevant history/achievement owners, treaty helpers, retry queues, and other transitive definitions must be fully read when implementation uses them. Reading their call sites does not replace reading their definitions.

The shared identifier registry returned 404 at the inspected revision. Current event, scenario, callback, country, and owner identifiers remain provisional until the authoritative registry or successor, workbook, active definitions, and installed mod collisions are checked. No tag or scenario ID is certified by this package.
