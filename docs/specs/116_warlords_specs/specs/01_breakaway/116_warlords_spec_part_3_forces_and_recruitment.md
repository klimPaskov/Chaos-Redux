# Part 3: Forces, equipment, and a military future

The warlord's opening force is a defection from an existing military system. His later force is an army supported by the country he actually governs. Keep that distinction visible in every transfer, recruitment action, and reward.

## Sizing the opening command

Estimate an opening band from four inputs: the parent's current size, current internal instability, eligible local military strength, and the founder's ability. Local force availability and the survival of the parent are hard constraints. Ability and instability shape a request inside those constraints.

The baseline request normally represents about 6 to 18 percent of the parent's current field manpower, with a 25 percent hard ceiling. The stronger evolution can request about 12 to 28 percent, with a 35 percent ceiling. These are starting tuning bands rather than guaranteed allocations. A high-skill founder in a large disintegrating country may reach the upper band. A small eligible parent with few local formations should receive a smaller credible split or fail viability, never an invented army.

Count trained troops and their actual equipment, not just division names. A depleted formation does not become full strength when it defects. Parent survival also reserves a meaningful force to hold its remaining territory and continue current wars. Recompute after each successful split. Do not derive the sixth revolt from the original country's army size.

## The transfer manifest

| Asset | Permitted source | Must survive the transfer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founding general | The exact selected live character | Identity, applicable traits, earned state, portrait, history, valid army and ruler roles |
| Defecting divisions | Explicit existing formations in or able to reach the seized command | Current manpower, equipment, experience, strength, legal template identity, and liabilities |
| Military cadres | Actual eligible officers or a paid training and appointment process | Current ownership and uniqueness of any named person |
| Stockpiles | Specific available parent or owner-held stocks | Exact variant quantities, source debit, destination credit, recorded transit loss if any |
| Unassigned manpower | Actual accessible recruitable population and mobilized reserves | No duplication with manpower already serving in transferred units |
| Fuel | Real stored fuel moved through a plausible captured depot or route | Source loss and destination gain within capacity |
| Industry | Transferred controlled factories and infrastructure | Damage, construction state, and location |
| Foreign support | Delivered shipments or native loan relationships | Sender cost, route, contract, and ownership of borrowed assets |

Physical custody follows a conservation identity: source loss equals destination gain plus proved loss. A fee discount may reduce an administrative payment. It cannot turn a 500-rifle debit into a 1,000-rifle delivery. The universal cost system applies to eligible prices and fees. It does not discount the amount of manpower or equipment required to build a real unit.

A transferred formation retains its condition. If the engine cannot transfer an ordinary subset while preserving its material state and necessary identity, that is an implementation blocker for the affected path. The special recorded reconstruction method in 019 is limited to the exact formations and guarantees owned by 019. It does not authorize reconstruction of arbitrary vanilla formations or a replacement founding general.

## Where the army goes

Prefer existing formations inside the region or on a continuous friendly withdrawal route into it. Defection does not teleport an army through a hostile front or across an ocean. An isolated follower may try to withdraw through normal movement, remain a trapped belligerent, surrender under ordinary rules, or fail to join. Its assets are not also issued in the new capital.

Prevent unavoidable overlap errors before commit. The war relationship, ownership change, and actual unit custody must agree about which forces are enemies and where they can stand. Do not give the new state free dislodgment of hostile divisions through a province ownership effect. Explicit engine fixtures must cover units on a future border, units in a pocket, expeditionary forces, volunteers, and a formation already in a strategic redeployment.

Parent equipment loans, licensed production, foreign expeditionaries, and sponsor-owned units retain their real owners and obligations. A general cannot seize an allied expeditionary force merely because it was assigned to his front. A native loan or contract must be honored, renegotiated, or lawfully ended by its owner.

## Ordinary unit families are sufficient

The regime fields ordinary infantry, cavalry, motorized troops, artillery, engineers, logistics units, and whatever more advanced equipment it actually obtains. Personal loyalty is a political and command relationship. It does not require a new battalion with invented combat physics.

| Template role | Military purpose | Entry and expansion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local line formation | Hold the initial connected front with affordable infantry support | Starts with inherited lawful templates, expands through normal training |
| Mobile command reserve | Secure routes, counterattack, and concentrate the founder's best troops | Cavalry in a poor economy, motorized only with vehicles and fuel |
| Depot and route guard | Protect productive centers and the links that make the country viable | Actual field forces or native garrison arrangements, never invisible free divisions |
| Veteran retinue | A limited personal core with inherited experience | Drawn from real loyal formations, replacement training does not copy veteran experience |
| Professional expansion army | Sustain a larger country beyond the founder's initial following | Requires staff reform, production, training time, and service support |

Template access is not a unit grant. Changing a template creates its ordinary equipment and training consequences. Retinue status must not produce an uncapped stacking modifier. New soldiers joining a veteran unit receive normal reinforcement dilution unless a verified native training mechanic says otherwise.

## Recruitment and replacements

Recruit from the population and laws of territory the regime actually holds. Native manpower laws remain the main mobilization system. An event recruitment action can coordinate training or allocate equipment, but it must prove the native manpower and equipment debit and must not issue a free fully supplied division.

Use captured industry, ordinary production lines, trade, licensed equipment where available, captured stocks, and delivered foreign aid. Inherited technology reflects what the source military genuinely knows and what the transferred state can employ. Do not transfer unreached technologies, completed special projects, nuclear stockpiles, or future unlocks simply because the parent owns one advanced item.

Keep donor reservations, cargo in transit, recipient stock, equipment committed to training, and equipment already in field units as distinct custody states. A promised shipment cannot equip a formation before delivery. If a reserve is captured, it cannot also be refunded to its former owner. A lawful successor can inherit the same paid pending training or repair contract after owner acknowledgment. Founder death does not refresh the work, and country defeat cannot redirect a completed unit into an unrelated actor. Cancellation returns only what actually remains recoverable.

In a poor region, the realistic alternatives are a smaller army, an infantry-heavy force, patron support, or conquest of a supply base. A viable opening need not support indefinite expansion without strategic effort. A landlocked state without military industry must have an actual accessible supplier and an adequate initial reserve if it is to pass viability. A promise that an AI might later send aid is insufficient.

Factory construction, repair, infrastructure, and railway work use real locations and native economic costs or a verified reservation adapter. Losing a construction site ends its benefit. No repeating focus can create equipment from a historical stockpile flag.

## Air and naval inheritance

The default warlord receives no proportional navy or air force. A coastal or aviation-heavy region can seize specific service assets only when ownership, personnel, equipment, facilities, fuel, and legal basing can all be transferred coherently. A ship away on a foreign patrol is not in the captured harbor. Aircraft physically based in a region do not automatically include every trained pilot and all foreign equipment there.

An approved air package needs actual aircraft variants, an operating airfield, support capacity, pilots or the relevant native manpower accounting, and fuel. Its AI assigns realistic missions near the command's front. It cannot patrol the world from one seized base.

An approved naval package needs exact hulls, service manpower, usable ports, fuel where applicable, legal orders, and repaired custody. Its initial role is coastal defense, convoy protection, or local transport according to the vessels actually acquired. A state without these assets remains a land power and can seek a naval patron or build a fleet later.

## Expansion without duplication

Conquest can add real manpower, industry, captured stocks, usable equipment, and officers who actually submit. Annexing a rival does not recreate every formation it once commanded. Disarmed troops can become accounted veterans or a native manpower return only once. Prisoners, refugees, demobilized soldiers, and serving manpower are different populations and cannot each contain the same people.

019 formations retain claimant, lot, template, generation, provider, and liability ownership throughout defection, subordination, and annexation. 116 requests an owner transfer or cleanup receipt. It never erases those records because the flag or overlord changed. Later ordinary recruitment by the same country is still ordinary recruitment unless 019 independently authorizes its own issuance.

Defeated or unsupported warlords may shrink, demobilize, surrender, or survive with a small army. There is no minimum-force replenishment tick. Their military future develops from their success and circumstances, as the user brief requires.
