# Part 2: Targets, regions, and the first breakaway

The event chooses a country that can lose a region and continue to function. It then chooses a general who can form a viable local command. Geography and military reality constrain the outcome before random selection is allowed to commit it.

## Country and general selection

Build a deduplicated pool containing all valid major countries and all valid player-controlled countries. A human-controlled major appears once. Select uniformly from that complete eligible country pool. Within the selected country, select uniformly from the complete pool of valid actual generals with at least one valid birth plan. General ability changes the strength of a breakaway. It does not secretly make a skilled general more likely to be chosen.

An eligible parent is an active human polity with a functioning government and a substantial owned and controlled land base. Exclude landless actors, nonhuman provider countries, the test country, countries in an uncompleted release transaction, and countries whose current surrender or occupation leaves no credible surviving government. Being at war does not itself exclude a country. Severe internal instability can make an otherwise eligible split stronger.

A valid founder is a living, recruited, available army general whose current country ownership can be proved. A general can be assigned or unassigned. His candidacy still needs an actual viable local military constituency. Exclude a current head of government whose departure would contradict the parent package, captured or removed characters, actors reserved by an incompatible character transaction, and the protected canonical Generalissimo unless 067 explicitly owns the operation. Admirals are not substitute generals. The presence of a face in a portrait pool does not create an eligible person.

Live-character movement is a hard capability gate before any birth is admitted. A verified mechanism must preserve the same actual character ID and its relevant traits, attributes, history, portrait, and valid ruler/general roles in the destination, without retaining an active duplicate in the parent. If that capability is unproved, reject the affected birth path before mutation. A failed reversible staged proof requires exact restoration. A failure after an irreversible step requires quarantine and owner repair, not a claimed rollback or replacement profile. This capability remains unverified in the present planning package.

An unassigned general may gain support at a real depot or garrison concentration. Do not invent a hometown, ethnic constituency, or personal unit bond to justify the selection. Named links created by 019 or another owner require that owner's acknowledgment. If no valid general and region combination exists, this parent is absent from the eligible pool.

## Viability is checked on the resulting countries

| Test | Starting balance intent | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parent before selection | Normally at least 6 eligible connected states, 6 million accessible population, and a meaningful industrial and field-army base | Filters token majors and nominally large but unusable countries |
| New state | Normally 2 or more adjacent states, about 0.8 million accessible population, a working capital, a defendable supply position, and a real replacement route | A breakaway must be able to survive more than its opening equipment issue |
| Large single-state exception | A substantial state with about 2 million accessible population and strong military, supply, and economic capacity | State granularity must not make a genuinely large region unusable |
| Remaining parent | Normally at least 3 connected eligible states, 3 million accessible population, 6 usable factories including a military factory, and a meaningful surviving army | One protected capital is not an adequate surviving country |
| War capacity | Both parties retain supplied forces, deployable manpower, equipment or a credible immediate supply arrangement | Prevents a scripted empty-country execution |
| Geography | Both governments have an owned and controlled capital in their viable component | Ownership on a map cannot substitute for governing access |

These anchors are evaluated together and scaled against the actual campaign. Large population alone cannot compensate for no accessible capital or no military. A country with unusually few large states can pass through an explicit large-state profile only when both resulting countries meet the equivalent capacity tests. The UI must not pretend a rejected plan is viable because its state count passed.

Do not lower these standards for a human target, a high intensity scenario, a late evolution, or a very skilled founder. If no honest configuration fits, do not create the state.

## Building a coherent region

Begin with a controlled military concentration, supply hub, defensible interior district, or connected frontier command. Grow through passable state adjacency toward a compact region with population and supply. Favor useful internal connections over a thin chain of states selected to maximize industry. Rivers, mountains, rail links, ports, existing front lines, and pockets should influence which otherwise valid plans are plausible.

Only states that the parent both owns and effectively controls are candidates for initial territorial seizure. Occupying an enemy state does not give the parent authority to release it as a new sovereign possession. Overseas colonies can be considered if they form a coherent local polity with real access, but temporary occupation and unrelated distant enclaves are not a free territorial reservoir.

Use one component test for both countries. From each preserved or proposed capital, traverse owned and controlled states through passable land borders or usable native straits, with operating supply along the required connections. The ordinary seized set must lie wholly in that viable component. Compute the parent's surviving thresholds from its capital component after the proposed cut and after every later split. Unconnected possessions remain real possessions but do not pad the minimums. A specifically reviewed regional maritime exception can join nearby islands through actual controlled ports, available transport, legal access, and a proved usable sea route. It must remain one coherent regional polity. A separate overseas breakaway still needs its own local capital, internal supply, real replacement source, and usable external delivery route if it depends on imports.

The normal first breakaway aims at roughly 12 to 25 percent of the parent's usable local base, subject to absolute viability and force conservation. The hard territory ceiling is a proposed 25 percent of current eligible states or equivalent capacity, with an explicitly disclosed large-state exception. The stronger evolution can raise the upper share to 35 percent. Round a plan by meaningful geography, then rerun both viability tests. Do not use a percentage rounding rule that silently takes the only remaining industrial or supply center.

The parent capital is protected in the ordinary first split. A different, later government overthrow belongs to an actual campaign or an owning civil-war system. The new capital should be a defensible administrative or military center in the seized region, with a ranked list of valid backup capitals. A captured port without a secure hinterland is a poor default.

## Ownership, cores, and local legitimacy

Transferred ownership and transferred control must refer to the same frozen state list. Existing cores and claims do not all become warlord cores. Parent home-core populations can support a carefully bounded home-region core grant when it represents continuity of local citizenship and recruitment. Occupied noncore populations retain their existing political status unless a separately earned regional settlement changes it.

Do not grant cores over the whole former country. The founder can claim national authority without receiving its manpower. Later integration requires control, time, administration, and a real political route. Local occupation, resistance, competing national claims, and civilian mobility remain meaningful after the flag changes.

## The birth sequence

| Moment | Result visible in play | Required proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selection | A specific parent, general, and region are chosen | Complete valid pools and a viable frozen plan |
| Reservation | Nothing is yet announced as accomplished | Exact carrier, states, character, forces, stocks, and owner permissions reserved |
| Preparation | A valid provisional government can exist | Capital, identity, laws, research, military templates, and supply setup validated |
| Transfer | The selected territory and assets change custody | Conservation and actual character continuity proved |
| Finalization | The new country becomes the political actor | Parent and breakaway survive, ownership/control and army custody agree |
| Publication | Parent, founder, and relevant neighbors receive their information | One committed event history and one ordinary world consequence |

Before irreversible finalization, recovery must restore the exact original state. After it, a failed proof retains the transaction ledger and stops success presentation while the responsible owner repairs or diagnoses the partial result. It is unacceptable to display a successful revolt, consume the ordinary firing, or pay an achievement while the selected general remains in the parent army or both countries contain the same equipment.

For any 019-linked person or asset, freeze the actual owner, claimant UID, exact formation and lot identity, and associated liabilities. Require the accepted 019 owner's fresh acknowledgment and transfer receipt before finalizing its participation. An existing derivative keeps its current country and actual character. A refused or unproved 019 asset contributes nothing to birth viability, and no free substitute is issued. Cancel the birth if the remaining proved package is inadequate. Calling an existing private 019 helper directly is not an accepted shared interface.

## War and the first response

The first ordinary breakaway normally creates an actual war with the parent. The participants fight with their real alliances, forces, borders, and supply. If 021 already owns a compatible conflict between the same institutional actors, it must reconcile the front and obligations before 116 publishes anything. Never create a redundant war, add uninvolved signatories, or resolve a civil-war front by cosmetic relabeling.

The parent receives immediate choices to prioritize defense, recovery, negotiation, or emergency military government. These do not undo the split for a political-power fee. An armistice requires both real parties and the relevant war owner. A demand for submission is an offer or a war objective, not a hidden annexation effect.

After the first publication, neighboring countries receive information proportionate to their involvement. Bordering powers can assess intervention, actual sponsors can review their contracts, and distant observers receive a concise report. Intelligence may obscure exact stockpiles and intent. It must not conceal which state owns a visible province or whether a war exists.

## Failure and fairness

Freeze selection once a transaction begins. Closing a window, reloading a save, changing a selected UI row, or nesting another owner's helper must not reroll the founder. A genuine invalidation, such as the general dying before commit or another war taking the proposed capital, cancels that plan cleanly. A later ordinary scheduler attempt can construct a fresh eligible plan without awarding progress for the failure.

The probability review must distinguish country selection, general selection, region construction, and continuation timing. It must verify the full candidate pool and show that major status plus human control does not double the chance. No exact percentage may be claimed from a sampled or incomplete pool.
