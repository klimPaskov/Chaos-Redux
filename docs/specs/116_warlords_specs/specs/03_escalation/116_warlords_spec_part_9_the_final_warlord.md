# Part 9: The Final Warlord

The Final Warlord begins after the Generalissimo has converted a unified warlord order into a sustained international hierarchy of military obedience. The Supreme War State has already been formed. It must now hold major conquests across several regions, command their armies, protect the routes connecting them, and possess enough usable strength to continue against the remaining opposition.

The transition follows present control. A map of past victories, a list of former subjects, or a large alliance is insufficient.

## International campaigns come after domestic formation

The first international readiness baseline is recorded after actual Supreme War State formation, when the order undertakes its first qualifying campaign beyond the origin crisis. It records the relevant independent great-power lineages, broad world regions, and world capacity needed for later comparisons. This is a one-time evidence operation, not a new recurring world scan.

Conquests made during domestic unification still provide their actual resources. They do not count as later international great-power victories. Countries fabricated from the origin parent, a released client, a recycled tag, or a deliberately promoted puppet cannot become separate great-power victories through renaming.

A qualifying great-power lineage is an independently established major military country at the international baseline, or a later independently developed power that passes an equivalent substantial-power test before the campaign begins. The starting equivalent test is at least 10 percent of the baseline world's usable military industry, with an independent armed government and a real geographic base. Qualification must be frozen before subordination and survive tag changes as lineage history. It must never be created retroactively from the victorious root's assets.

## Proposed readiness profile

The following is a complete starting balance profile for implementation review. The user's fixed requirements are later international conquest, multiple world regions, current effective command, sustainable global dominance, strict Chaos greater than 1000, the enabled branch, and exclusive world-end ownership. The numerical thresholds below are proposed tuning rather than user-specified constants.

| Requirement | Starting operational threshold | What cannot substitute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Root and ruler | Actual Supreme War State with the canonical living Generalissimo in command | A historical formation flag or an unrelated military leader |
| Domestic unity | Every current origin rival resolved or genuinely subordinated | Old capitulations, a shared flag, or a nominal subject list |
| Foreign great powers | At least 4 qualifying foreign great-power lineages currently conquered or genuinely commanded through post-formation campaigns | Several released pieces of one defeated country |
| Campaign geography | Qualifying great-power control spans at least 3 macroregions | Four neighbors in one region |
| Sustained world presence | Meaningful command anchors in at least 4 macroregions | One ungoverned island or a temporary expeditionary landing |
| Military industry | At least 55 percent of the world comparison capacity is currently usable under effective command | Paper ownership of destroyed, inaccessible, or independently controlled factories |
| Population under government | At least 40 percent of the world comparison population under sustained effective government | Claims, temporary front-line occupation, or unheld former conquests |
| Field strength | At least 1.25 times the effective field strength of all remaining independent armed opposition in the comparison cohort | Empty templates, depleted paper divisions, or armies the root cannot command |
| Operational links | Every counted anchor and great-power contribution has a usable military command and supply route | Nominal access without an operating route |
| Sustained campaign capacity | Real replacement production or stocks, available manpower, functioning logistics, and supplied theater forces | A brief statistical lead with an army unable to reinforce or reach its fronts |
| Consolidation | All required current proof maintained for 180 continuous days | A single favorable screenshot or a timer whose prerequisites have failed |

The macroregions are Europe, Africa, Asia, North America, South America, and Oceania. Their installed state assignment must be reviewed and explicit, including transcontinental boundaries. Regions are geographic coverage categories, not additional public values.

A meaningful anchor is a defensible controlled capital or command center and its accessible hinterland, with a functioning supply base and a substantial governed population or industrial base. The starting floor is either 5 million governed people or 10 usable factories, together with a real operating military presence and replacement access. Small-state map granularity cannot turn an empty island into a global theater. A wholly unusual campaign that lacks enough genuine qualifying powers or regions can leave this branch unreachable. Do not silently lower the standard or fabricate targets.

## Measuring real capacity

Count each state, industry asset, population cohort, and field formation once. A subordinate country's contribution belongs to the order only while the military relationship is genuinely enforceable and its relevant assets are accessible. Annexed and subject records must not both contain the same population or army after an ownership change.

The industry and population comparison denominators use the greater of the recorded international baseline and the current valid world total. This prevents destruction or civilian deaths from making the order appear globally dominant simply by shrinking the denominator. Actual world growth can raise the requirement. The numerator contains only current usable controlled capacity.

Effective field strength is a private evidence estimate based on actual field manpower, equipment fulfillment, and operational supply, with a documented conservative method for relevant military quality. It is not a displayed power score. Its exact native queries, comparison cohort, rounding, and uncertain inputs must be proved before this gate can be implemented. Independently governed allies remain outside the hierarchy and count among potential remaining military opposition for this comparison. Friendship is not obedience.

The military evidence must also show that the order can replace losses and operate its major theaters. The initial reserve target is about 90 days of recent ordinary replacement consumption for its critical equipment and fuel needs, supported by sustainable production and imports. A quiet front cannot reduce the requirement to zero. The implementation must use a declared minimum operating requirement appropriate to the actual formations. Scaled supply interruptions and very short fluctuations can affect pressure without instantly destroying a state, but an unproved critical route or army contribution cannot remain credited to eligibility.

These observations require owner-supported engine capabilities. A guessed supply route, unsupported army-control inference, or incomplete external-strength census returns unknown, which fails readiness. This package does not claim that the current tools or inspected implementation already provide those queries.

## Current military obedience

The hierarchy is a set of real command relationships. The root must direct its own army and every counted subordinate military through enforceable native or verified owner mechanisms. A subject can retain local administration, but it cannot retain an independent army and government while being counted as already subdued.

Qualification covers current and future recruited forces, obligatory participation in the order's campaigns, strategic access, and a usable chain of orders. A one-time transfer of several divisions does not qualify the rest of an independent country's military. A puppet label does not close loopholes in independent war policy or recruitment. If the necessary subject system is unsupported, only fully proved alternative control, such as annexation and army integration, can count.

Cycles, orphaned subjects, conflicting owners, suspended command, and stale country references fail the affected relationship. When a subordinate becomes independent, its assets leave the numerator and its armed strength returns to the outside cohort immediately. Previous victories remain in history but stop supplying present credit.

The factual readiness roster records dependencies: which current country, army contribution, region anchor, and route depend on each command relationship. One liberation can invalidate several dependent requirements, but it remains one attributed loss of control rather than five separate Chaos rewards. Reveal only information appropriate to the viewer. The root can restore a route, establish a genuinely usable alternate center, negotiate renewed obedience, redeploy real troops, or accept a smaller order. Designating another port does not move stock or preserve a broken consolidation period. An alternate route already proved before the old one fails can preserve uninterrupted connectivity. A real interruption restarts the required proof.

## Readiness, warning, and transition

Once all military and territorial requirements are first satisfied, begin the 180-day consolidation objective. Inform the root and the relevant opposing countries through their proper perspectives. Show the facts they can act on: commanded great powers, controlled regions, critical routes, unresolved rivals, and the consolidation period. The information is a campaign warning rather than omniscient access to private stockpiles.

Successful liberation, an independent rival, loss of a necessary anchor, removal of the Generalissimo, a broken effective-command relationship, or failure of a required capacity threshold invalidates readiness. Reset the uninterrupted proof period when a material requirement ceases to hold. An observation service failure pauses and invalidates proof rather than granting invisible grace. Presentation may show why readiness was lost without revealing secret opponent intentions.

Use native aggregate evidence or registered owner change notifications where supported. The world baseline, a player-requested readiness examination, and final commit can perform bounded one-off proof jobs. Do not implement a monthly or daily whole-world sweep under the name of a readiness registry. If comprehensive change coverage is unavailable, the continuous-control claim remains an explicit implementation blocker.

At the terminal commit, revalidate a fresh snapshot and all global rules together:

1. Chaos is strictly greater than 1000. Exactly 1000 fails.
2. World-end scenarios are enabled.
3. The Final Warlord's own branch is enabled in the shared world-end registry.
4. No world-end scenario is already active or has won the simultaneous claim.
5. The Generalissimo, Supreme War State, domestic unity, international command, routes, and consolidation proof are currently valid.

Only a successful exclusive claim launches the terminal presentation and branch history. A stale preview cannot authorize the transition after a liberation or a settings change. Political formation, a focus completion, a Chaos tier, or an achievement cannot launch it separately.

## The final struggle

The remaining world confronts the actual military order. Existing wars continue. Opposing countries can coordinate, offer access, supply one another, protect threatened routes, recognize restored governments, support surviving rival commands, or conduct direct campaigns against the root and its subordinate armies. Shared world threat can make coordination more attractive. It does not compel a universal faction or annex independent defenders into one side.

The Supreme War State receives its terminal political and command presentation and can sustain a visibly powerful final campaign. Its advantages remain linked to the existing hierarchy, supply, and real force base. The branch does not manufacture a second army equal to every conquered country's original strength or give it military access across every neutral border.

The defender's best strategy can be to break obedience rather than race directly to the root capital. Freeing a major power removes its usable capacity from the order. Reopening a supply theater can divide the order's fronts. Supporting a real rival can force the Generalissimo to choose between external conquest and internal control. None of these actions guarantees success without the underlying campaign.

If no independent opposition remains, do not invent countries or troops merely to populate a final battle. The terminal owner follows the actual remaining governments, forces, and aftermath conditions. It still cannot treat prior human losses as reversible.

## Defeat and persistence

After launch, losing a launch requirement does not undo the world end. The ordinary automatic event stop remains in force. The active terminal conflict continues until its real military and political outcome is resolved under the shared rules.

The Generalissimo's death can disrupt the order and close his personal leadership, but it does not dissolve every surviving army. A remaining command directorate can continue fighting with the assets it actually controls. It cannot launch a replacement world end or clone the canonical leader.

The order is defeated when its central command no longer exercises effective authority over a viable continuation of the terminal hierarchy and its surviving independent military centers have been resolved through actual defeat, subordination to opponents, dissolution, or recognized successor government outcomes. Capturing one capital without breaking the armies is insufficient. Conversely, a cosmetic country name cannot preserve a defeated order after its real command has ended.

The resulting map may contain restored countries, independent successor governments, ordinary warlords, occupied districts, and ongoing wars. Part 10 specifies these outcomes. The terminal flag is not cleared to restart ordinary automatic Chaos event firing.
