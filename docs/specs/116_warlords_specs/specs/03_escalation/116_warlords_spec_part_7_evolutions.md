# Part 7: The two evolutions

The Generals Follow changes how many rival commands can emerge from the origin country. Stronger Warlords changes the size and strength of newly emerging commands. Neither evolution is a substitute for a viable country, an actual general, or real military assets.

## Stage structure

| Evolution | Proposed Chaos tier | Persistent change | What activation alone does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Generals Follow | First assigned evolution tier, starting anchor 200 | Enables further warned breakaways from the same surviving origin parent | Opens the continuation system and its information, without creating a country or adding Chaos |
| Stronger Warlords | Second assigned evolution tier, starting anchor 400 | Raises the prospective region and defection bands for later valid births | Changes future birth planning, without restoring or enlarging existing armies |

These are the only two evolution stages. Exact tier registration must follow the shared evolution registry and collision review. Each occupies one tier. Political recovery, a founder's death, Generalissimo unification, and world-end readiness are lifecycle states, not additional evolutions.

## The Generals Follow during an active crisis

After a committed first breakaway, a sufficiently large surviving parent can suffer another command crisis. The system examines that parent's registered generals and eligible regions, not every country in the world. It uses the current remaining military, population, and industrial base. Previous losses reduce later possibilities.

The next crisis begins with a warning about one actual command. The starting warning duration is 120 days, scaled by administrative reach, the size of the command, current wars, supply, and instability. It must not fall below 90 days for the ordinary prevention mission. A separate minimum interval of 120 days between successful births prevents instant fragmentation through multiple queued warnings. The initial stochastic continuation anchor is approximately 180 days once a new warning can legitimately be scheduled. This is a tuning target for probability inspection, not a claimed exact mean in every campaign.

Only one continuation warning is active for the parent at a time. Freeze its actor and intended region, then revalidate both as circumstances change. The parent can supply, negotiate with, reassign, contain, or lawfully detain the actual command. A rival or patron can exploit the crisis through real support. Payment without completed delivery or changed command does not resolve the mission.

Refreshing an unlocked material envelope preserves the same warned person and completed containment facts. It cannot silently replace the region with a better target. A genuinely different region requires an explicit replanning result and a renewed relevant response opportunity. Death, actual detention, lawful loss of the command, or an incompatible legitimate owner change closes that person's warning. A failed evolved first birth cannot leave a continuation warning from a country that was never created.

If the warning expires with a still-valid independent command, the defection proceeds through the same full transaction as the first birth. If the plan is no longer viable, it closes or returns to a bounded replanning state with a clear reason. It does not seize a random other region to guarantee the evolution's spectacle.

The starting new-creation capacity is adaptive: around 2 active breakaway countries for the smallest eligible parent, 4 for a substantial parent, and 6 for a very large one. A configurable upper creation ceiling of 8 protects performance and readability. Actual viability is usually the tighter constraint. Existing independent 019 or other acknowledged rival actors remain in the political register even if this exceeds the new-creation ceiling. A capacity ceiling must never delete an already living country.

## Stronger Warlords during an active crisis

New birth plans may claim a larger coherent region and attract a larger share of eligible real formations, stocks, and officers. Founder ability, local military concentration, parent instability, Desertion Crisis, and actual mutiny increase the request within the stronger bands. Parent survival, current asset availability, and supply remain hard limits.

The stronger band normally requests 12 to 28 percent of current parent field manpower with a 35 percent ceiling. Territory follows the proposed 35 percent ceiling and the same compact-region rules. A stronger warlord may begin with more experienced real cadres or a better supplied reserve when these actually defect. It does not receive free levels, full equipment, or extra generals generated to match a percentage.

Existing warlords immediately gain relevant information about the changed environment and can react politically. Their manpower, equipment, territory, existing officer roster, and experience remain current. A warning that has not yet frozen its material manifest can use the stronger planning band after an explicit visible refresh. A transfer already in its locked commit phase cannot be silently enlarged.

## Evolved first firing

If the event has not yet fired when an evolution becomes active, build the opening with the applicable evolved rules. The first event still creates one warlord state, one founder, one region, and one committed history entry.

The Generals Follow then makes later warnings available after the normal response window and minimum spacing. It does not create a batch of countries in the opening report. Stronger Warlords can strengthen this first birth because it is a new birth, using only available physical assets and the stronger viability constraints.

If only Stronger Warlords is enabled, the first valid breakaway can be stronger while further automatic births remain disabled. Disabling one evolution must not silently disable its sibling, remove existing countries, refund committed assets, or reverse a completed political outcome.

## Continuation, settlement, and the terminal period

An acknowledged settlement can close the origin parent's continuation pressure when no live command crisis remains and the actual military arrangements have stabilized. Crossing another Chaos threshold does not reopen a settled crisis or reset the fire-once event. A later real defection from an owning system can interact with surviving lineages as a new material fact.

Once a world end is active, ordinary automatic event and evolution dispatch obeys the shared terminal stop. Already active wars, warnings, treaties, owner cleanup, and the Final Warlord's internal counterplay continue where the world-end rules permit ongoing crisis resolution. A new terminal-order defection must be an actual local consequence of that active conflict. It cannot be an ordinary global event fired through a renamed queue.

## Chaos and anti-exploit behavior

Activation, logging, a timer completing, a warning appearing, and an evolution checkbox changing grant zero Chaos. The abnormal consequence of a committed additional fragmentation can have its own guarded Chaos contribution. The ordinary war, annexation, deaths, and other shared events retain their own accounting.

The same actor or territory cannot repeatedly create a full new-breakaway premium through annex-release cycling. Use the origin and current political lineage, exact consequence receipts, and diminishing first-area significance. Preventing a real threatened split can remove part of its current instability. It cannot refund casualties or destruction that already occurred.
