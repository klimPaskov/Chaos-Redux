# Part 10: Settlement, liberation, and successor governments

Warlord crises end in different ways. A parent can recover its territory. A partition can stabilize into independent countries. A military state can become civilian. The Supreme War State can lose provinces, subjects, its ruler, or its entire command structure. Each outcome follows the actual governments, armies, people, and territory that survive.

## Ordinary outcomes

| Outcome | What changes | What remains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parent reconquest | Ownership and control return through the actual war settlement, independent command ends or submits | Damage, casualties, surviving people, current equipment, and reconstruction needs |
| Negotiated reintegration | Specified regional authority and army arrangements enter a common state | Real promises, residual institutions, and any unresolved military obligations |
| Recognized partition | War ends on an actual agreed border, independent governments remain | Claims or guarantees permitted by the treaty, rival interests, and separate armies |
| Warlord conquest of the parent | The winning regime governs what it actually conquers or commands | Its political route, existing country lineage, and the administration required by expansion |
| Civilian transition | Personal military rule ends through a real government and command settlement | The country's ordinary army, economy, citizens, and historical origin |
| Foreign domination | A patron or intervener obtains actual agreed or imposed authority | The precise autonomy and military obligations of that settlement |
| Fragmented defeat | Several actual successor governments or ordinary warlords remain | Their current territorial and military bases and any continuing wars |

No outcome triggers a universal return to the pre-event map. A historical parent can recover its former land only through the actual settlement and valid actor restoration rules. A state controlled by a third participant cannot be distributed as though it were an unowned event reward.

## Treaties bind actual participants

Before peace or partition changes anything, freeze each real signatory's obligations: territory, control, military custody, debt or aid commitments, subject status, access, and prisoners or demobilized personnel where an owner models them. Require current consent or an actual imposed settlement under the relevant war rules.

Each signatory supplies its own performed-obligation proof against one shared agreement hold date. One government's repaired route cannot stand in for another's military handover or a patron's promised delivery. Freeze completed evidence before front records, temporary flags, or a participating government disappear. Bind an actual successor only when the settlement and lineage prove that inheritance. An in-period breach invalidates durability. A later new crisis records recurrence against the old completed agreement before replacing its live crisis identity, rather than retroactively erasing fulfilled history.

021 owns the institutional civil-war settlement when it is the active war owner. 116 supplies its exact actor and command facts. A sponsor cannot sign away a recipient's independent army. Empty signatory lists, stale government references, and nonexistent recipients invalidate the operation. A treaty preview must be revalidated at confirmation, especially after a capital changes hands or a participant is annexed.

Keep ordinary war settlement distinct from scripted peace authority. If the current owner cannot lawfully terminate or partition the relevant war, do not fake a complete peace. The parties may still receive a negotiation opportunity, but the specification's promised outcome requires a verified settlement path before that route ships.

## Liberation changes current control

A country regains effective independence when it has a functioning government, actual territory or a valid owner-supported exile base, and military authority outside the Generalissimo's command. A displayed autonomy value or a request to leave is insufficient until the real relationship changes. Conversely, once independence is real, the order cannot retain credit because a cached member flag remains.

On a proved liberation, remove the affected current command relationship and the liberated assets from domestic or global readiness immediately. Retain the conquest history. Invalidate only the contributions that actually depended on that relationship, then recompute the order's current proof through the bounded owner system.

The liberated government chooses its next policy according to its actual interests and condition. It may join an opposing coalition, remain neutral, recover territory, settle with a neighbor, or become another military regime. Liberation does not automatically place it under the liberator's command or summon a full new army.

An existing national identity may return through its accepted carrier and owner rules. A new successor must have a real territorial and administrative basis. The strict entry-size thresholds for the original random event do not forbid an ordinary war from leaving a small existing country alive. They do prevent inventing a new viable military power without people, forces, and resources.

## A defeated command can leave survivors

Separate loss of a military campaign, loss of hierarchy membership, loss of the current government, and final destruction of an actor. A defeated 019 derivative can retain a remnant and history under its owner. A surviving founder can serve a successor, go into a valid exile, retire, or remain a rival according to actual events. A killed or permanently removed actor cannot return through a saved profile.

Every surviving formation keeps a real custody outcome: incorporated, disarmed with measured returns, surrendered, destroyed in ordinary combat, lawfully transferred to a successor, or still independent. Do not assign the same soldiers both to an integrated army and a demobilized manpower reward. Do not issue the former maximum equipment to every successor.

If a country disappears, its owner's cleanup must prove what happened to its people, formations, ledgers, and obligations before clearing identity records. Failed cleanup keeps diagnostic evidence and schedules bounded repair. A missing tag is not permission to delete all records with a similar flag.

## Defeat of the Supreme War State

The political formation can fracture without the whole country being annexed. A root holding a reduced but functioning country may keep a historical identity while losing its special command benefits and terminal readiness. Its unification roster reflects newly independent rivals. Rebuilding it requires real recovery.

During The Final Warlord, defeat means the central order no longer has a viable coordinated command capable of continuing the terminal campaign. Inspect the actual root, functioning successor command, commanded armies, major routes, and remaining subordinated military centers. Capturing the capital or killing the Generalissimo can be decisive evidence, but neither alone proves that every theater has ceased to obey.

Former members that have become independent successors are no longer pieces of the central order merely because their armies survive. The defenders need not annihilate every ordinary successor government to defeat the terminal hierarchy. They must break or defeat the continuing central command and resolve whatever still genuinely fights under it. An isolated command that becomes an independent ordinary warlord retains its real country and wars.

Record the terminal defeat once through its owner, then resolve the remaining map through actual control and settlement. The shared world-end flag remains active and ordinary automatic event firing stays stopped. The branch's defeated aftermath is not a restart of the ordinary Chaos scheduler.

## Successor formation priorities

1. Preserve a living legitimate government already controlling the area when the settlement recognizes it.
2. Restore an eligible displaced government through its actual owner and surviving institutions where the war outcome supports it.
3. Recognize an existing independent military command with a real regional base as an ordinary warlord or military government.
4. Create a new regional successor only through a validated country package with an actual leader or institution, territory, population, resources, and lawful military custody.

These are settlement priorities, not a script that overrides victor choices or creates all four outcomes. Borders follow actual control, agreements, viable administration, and relevant claims. A disconnected military enclave may require access, evacuation, surrender, or a negotiated link. It does not gain a corridor through neutral territory automatically.

## Civilian losses and recovery

Use the shared civilian accounting for actual modeled deaths. State population uses the established thousands-based representation. Debits must respect the state floor and record the actual amount once in the shared Deaths ledger. The act of changing ownership or autonomy does not kill anyone by itself.

If a retreat or settlement moves civilians, use an actual origin, destination, route, capacity, and accepted migration owner. Origin debit equals destination credit plus actual route deaths. A failed delivery cannot both restore all people at origin and credit them at destination. Current famine and migration owner blockers remain blockers to the affected scripted operation, not excuses to invent substitute population effects.

Reconstruction can repair a railway, restore a factory, settle accounted veterans, or rebuild administrative capacity. It does not subtract past deaths from history. Humanitarian assistance requires an actual donor, goods or service capacity, and delivery. There is no achievement for maximizing civilian losses and no population reward for repeating a collapse.

## Cleanup and future play

End obsolete warning missions, release unused reservations with exact receipts, retire inapplicable temporary spirits, and close actor-specific decision rows. Keep surviving political routes, ordinary recruitment, current treaties, reconstruction, and legitimate country history.

Release command relationships only through their owners. Do not clear 019 claimant or provider ledgers, 067 canonical history, 021 treaty obligations, Hunger Lines host/network state, or shared world-end flags as generic Warlords cleanup. The same country may still be meaningful to another event after the 116 crisis has ended.

A stable postwar state can build, research, trade, recruit, and fight normally under the world's current rules. Its future need not remain a permanent special-event minigame. The lasting consequences are the governments and armies that exist, the land they hold, and the losses that cannot be undone.
