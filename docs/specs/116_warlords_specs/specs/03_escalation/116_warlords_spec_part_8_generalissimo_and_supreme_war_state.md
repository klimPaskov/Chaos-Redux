# Part 8: Generalissimo and the Supreme War State

Generalissimo 067 offers a special way to unify the warlord crisis. He must become the actual political and military leader of a participating country and then bring the rival commands under real authority. The formation of the Supreme War State recognizes that accomplished domestic order. It does not accomplish it on the player's behalf.

## One canonical Generalissimo

067 owns the unique Generalissimo's identity, validity, ruler and commander roles, removal outcomes, Influence, post-takeover Command Cohesion, and its independent world-end branch. 116 resolves that actual character through the accepted owner interface. It does not create a second copy, choose an arbitrary skilled general as a substitute, or resurrect an actor permanently removed by 067.

The inspected older 067 event and its newer nine-part specification describe different implementation states. The future integration must reconcile the owner representation explicitly. A planning document cannot prove that its proposed actor lifecycle already exists. The implementation appendix records this as a gate.

The Generalissimo can enter the crisis through an actual 067 takeover or another owner-approved continuity path. He may lead the surviving parent, an existing warlord state, or another country able to campaign into the crisis. The host remains the same country. Existing 019 actors, formations, and meaningful country content retain their identities and owners.

## Who must be unified

Maintain a bounded origin roster of the independent military regimes created by this crisis and acknowledged pre-existing breakaways that actually participate in it. Record the current government and army of each lineage. A change of name, tag, ideology, or founder does not remove a surviving actor from the roster.

The roster includes a civilian successor if it still governs independently with armed forces. Civilian reform is a valid outcome for that country, but it cannot be used to pretend that an independent rival has submitted. A defeated actor is resolved only when its real government and independent command have been dissolved, integrated, or genuinely subordinated. A surviving exile government and army require their actual owner's treatment.

The original parent need not disappear if it remains an ordinary independent state outside the warlord union. Its surviving territory and government are real external actors. If it is itself a rival military regime within the claimed unification, it belongs in the roster. The Generalissimo cannot claim control of the parent's territory or troops merely because he has united the breakaways.

New actual defections and successful liberations update this roster. An arbitrary creation cap does not cap the number of living rivals that count. Remote unrelated military governments do not all become domestic rivals merely because they resemble warlords.

## Campaigns and submission

At least one real campaign must establish the Generalissimo's authority over a relevant rival. Meaningful combat, a valid war settlement, or coerced subordination backed by an actual campaign can qualify. A scripted ultimatum accepted without an armed contest or a material surrender can create an ordinary diplomatic arrangement, but it cannot supply the required campaign record by itself.

Every rival credited toward this special unification must have its own annexation or subordination receipt linked to an actual campaign under the Generalissimo's leadership, that campaign's war settlement, or a material coerced surrender credibly caused by it. One conquered rival does not make unrelated voluntary associations qualify. A single campaign can force several commands to submit, so separate wars against every rival are unnecessary. Ordinary voluntary cooperation remains a diplomatic relationship until the required campaign-linked submission is established.

| Rival's current condition | Counts as unified? | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annexed, government ended, forces incorporated or lawfully disarmed, territory governed | Yes | Independent political and military command has actually ended |
| Local government retained under verified central command over existing and future armies, war policy, and access | Yes | Genuine military subordination is compatible with local administration |
| Nominal puppet with independently controlled army or independent war policy | No | Subject status alone does not establish obedience |
| Faction ally, treaty partner, recognized neighbor, or shared cosmetic identity | No | Cooperation is not command |
| Capitulated while an independent government or effective army continues elsewhere | Not yet | Capitulation alone does not prove resolution of the whole actor |
| Leader killed while country and army remain independent | No | A dead founder does not dissolve the regime |
| Previously conquered, now successfully liberated or released | No | Historical conquest is not current control |
| Fully dissolved actor with no independent successor government or armed command | Resolved | There is no surviving rival to command, with owner proof required |

Subject qualification must cover current forces and later recruits. A one-time expeditionary transfer is not enough if the subject can immediately raise an independent army, refuse the order's wars, or retain autonomous diplomacy that defeats central command. Where the engine cannot enforce the required relationship, that subject does not count. Annexation with proved military integration remains a separate valid route.

## Integration after victory

A conquered rival strengthens the order through what actually survives: usable industry, controlled population, experienced officers who submit, supplied formations, stockpiles, routes, and administrative capacity. Integration has costs in time, industrial allocation, staffing, and political concessions. A ruined province and a destroyed army cannot supply the same reward as an intact surrender.

The central command chooses between direct military administration and a genuinely subordinate regional command. Direct administration gives firmer command but places more occupation and reconstruction work on the root. Regional command can preserve local expertise and ease administration but creates a relationship that must remain enforceable. Neither route grants blanket cores or unlimited recruitment from occupied populations.

Officer integration is an actual appointment and owner-approved character transfer where needed. Army integration uses exact surviving units or an accounted disarmament and rebuilding process. It never issues a new full army equal to the rival's historic maximum. Capturing the same lineage twice can restore its current assets to the order, but it cannot stack the same permanent integration reward twice.

## Formation requirements

The Supreme War State formation becomes available when the canonical living Generalissimo is the actual ruler of the root, at least one qualifying unification campaign is recorded, every current relevant rival is resolved or genuinely commanded, and the root can govern a connected practical military order.

The starting consolidation period is 180 days. During it, the root must retain its capital, a viable military-economic base, functional Command Cohesion, usable command routes, and the actual control arrangements described above. The period begins only after the last required rival submits. Losing a required relationship immediately invalidates the completed set. A new eligible rival cannot be ignored because the timer began earlier.

The consolidation objective is a mission with visible factual requirements. It completes when those facts have held for the required period. A final formation decision then rechecks current proof and pays any remaining declared administrative cost. It changes the host's political identity, not the world map. No hidden annexation, universal coring, army grant, or world-end claim occurs during this decision.

Formation can happen below Chaos 1000 and while world ends or this particular branch are disabled. It is an earlier political achievement. Conversely, Chaos above 1000 cannot replace a missing rival's submission or the consolidation period.

## The earned military order

Supreme War State should feel substantially stronger than a loose collection of warlords. It can direct the forces that it truly commands, plan integrated offensives, coordinate production and replacement priorities, develop theater supply, and concentrate a command reserve. These are useful strategic capabilities tied to controlled resources and verified native army relationships.

Its advanced command spirit can provide a strong bounded package, initially around improved planning, organization recovery, and coordinated military production. Exact modifiers belong in the tuning profile after engine review. Upgrade the command constitution spirit rather than adding a new permanent bonus for every conquered tag. Effective command and real assets do most of the work.

The focus and decision routes now shift toward integration, theater logistics, strategic industry, external campaigns, and preserving or resisting the Generalissimo's personal authority. The player still chooses fronts and fights wars. A theater program must identify real armies and supply routes. It cannot start with a global army teleport or automatic obedience from neutral major powers.

## Breaking and rebuilding the union

A liberated subject, renewed defection, successful rival restoration, independent army, or broken command route can remove a member from effective control. Reopen the affected rivalry and remove its contribution from current military-order proof. Paid integration costs and history remain. Benefits that depended on the lost capability shrink or become inactive.

Losing unity need not immediately erase the country's displayed historical name. The UI distinguishes a historical formation milestone from present command. Reconstituting the order requires restoring actual relationships and completing the relevant consolidation requirements. It does not grant the formation reward again.

If the Generalissimo dies or is permanently removed before The Final Warlord begins, his unique terminal eligibility closes. The country and its actual hierarchy can survive under a military council or successor government. That successor can fight, reform, fragment, or retain the historical state, but it does not become a newly invented canonical Generalissimo.

## Relationship to 067's own ending

067's separately specified ending, The Generalissimos' World, remains an independent owner branch with its own criteria and settings. It is not renamed to The Final Warlord. The shared world-end owner arbitrates competing readiness. A successful claim by any branch blocks a second launch. Neither this formation nor a rival's defeat clears an already active world end.
