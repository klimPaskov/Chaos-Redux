# Part 13: Difficult achievements and campaign acceptance

## Reward the campaign the player actually created

The achievement set should remember difficult protection, institutional recovery, survival after failure, competent alien expansion and the defeat of supreme command. It must not reward merely seeing a portrait, choosing the first investigation or watching The Unveiling. It should also avoid teaching the player that killing many suspects is the intended measure of success.

The following identifiers and labels are working design keys. They are not final achievement titles or descriptions. The writing direction describes the accomplishment and tone, while the implementation and asset handoffs own finished localisation and icons. Every completed icon has a 64 by 64 consumer and must remain readable at that size. Hidden achievements protect later revelations rather than withholding ordinary payment conditions.

Track actual campaign outcomes with immutable original-host, person, cohort, state, brood and scenario receipts. A current country tag is insufficient because countries can be occupied, restored and merged. A human player continuing into a brood does not carry human-only achievement eligibility into the alien role. A player absorbed into a stronger brood retains valid brood provenance through the supported continuation.

All achievements require the project's normal eligible campaign mode. Explicit debug creation, CXT fixtures, direct test grants, invalid save migration and manual scenario bypasses disqualify the affected event achievement generation. The implementation must inspect the existing root achievement registry and normal eligibility pattern rather than invent a second registry or rely on a tooltip. In ordinary play, the achievements remain possible regardless of which eligible country initially receives the event.

## Planned achievement set

### 119_mistresses_containment_without_purge

**Title direction:** Disciplined protection that exposes a dangerous network while preserving civil trust. Avoid a claim that every woman has been proved innocent.

**Eligible player and conditions:** A human host that actually began with at least 100 adult infiltrators. Complete at least five independent case investigations, recover or isolate at least one real institutional connection, and eliminate that host's viable covert network through verified removal or macro-isolation. Maintain Confidence at least 75 and Reach at least 50 through the 90-day confirmation period. Finish with no territorial brood from that host at The Unveiling, or qualify when the whole covert crisis genuinely resolves before revelation.

**Disqualifiers and difficulty:** Any player-ordered lethal purge in that host generation disqualifies it. Merely beginning in an unseeded country does not qualify. Difficulty is hard because organizational containment must work without the fastest coercive option, and the result must survive a sustained confirmation period. Visibility is ordinary after the investigation response opens.

**Icon and tracking:** A protected civic archive joined to a closed network diagram, with a human silhouette safely outside it. Track initial real cohort, completed independent methods, institutional and isolation receipts, the continuous Confidence and Reach interval, and lethal-action history. No portrait beauty category belongs in the trigger.

### 119_mistresses_false_accusations_resolved

**Title direction:** Careful judgment under apparently persuasive but mistaken evidence.

**Eligible player and conditions:** A human host must spare five distinct actual humans who each received at least one adverse evidence family, complete protection of their relevant source or household, and later obtain a reassuring report from a genuinely independent evidence family through an unused ordinary method or a permitted audit replacement. At least two cases must involve an observable source-integrity concern handled through that ordinary bounded remedy. Maintain Confidence at least 65 when the fifth qualifying case closes. These are documented protective acts and reassuring observations, not a new guaranteed biological exoneration test.

**Disqualifiers and difficulty:** Reopened versions of the same person count once. A false concern can qualify when the same actual protection and independent reassuring report occur, regardless of the source's hidden integrity. Merely clicking audit or spare is insufficient. Difficulty is hard because the player must preserve uncertain people and complete real follow-up without an omniscient test. The achievement remains hidden until completion, with no live innocent-person progress count.

**Icon and tracking:** A torn accusation sheet beside an intact protected-person record. Track real person IDs and hidden human truth, adverse report provenance, the independent reassuring follow-up, actual source-protection delivery and the one-time audit lineage. Hidden truth is a retrospective achievement guard, never a case-action eligibility input or an ordinary guaranteed verdict. Do not disclose the hidden achievement progress through a live innocent-person count.

### 119_mistresses_international_containment

**Title direction:** A functioning international response that defeats a network crossing national borders.

**Eligible player and conditions:** A human country coordinates delivered independent-source exchanges with three other original human hosts spanning at least two immutable world regions. Those deliveries must materially contribute to three distinct verified institutional recoveries or macro-isolations. The player's own viable network must also be contained, and at least two assisted hosts must avoid territorial revolt from the affected network at revelation or global covert resolution.

**Disqualifiers and difficulty:** Three aliases of one successor country do not count as three partners. Repeated delivery of one copied report does not count as independent work. Exchanges completed after all targets were already resolved receive no retroactive credit. Difficulty is very hard because foreign authority, reliable sources and several national outcomes must align. Visibility is ordinary after reciprocal cooperation is available.

**Icon and tracking:** Three distinct archive seals connected across a restrained world-map motif. Track consenting partner provenance, actual delivery, source independence, linked recovery receipts and their later outbreak outcome. The same participant can contribute to multiple genuine projects, but cannot multiply a single completion.

### 119_mistresses_legitimacy_recovered

**Title direction:** Rebuilding a trustworthy response after a real institutional crisis, with responsibility rather than self-congratulation.

**Eligible player and conditions:** A human host suffers an actual major institutional takeover and a documented response breakdown that brings Confidence to 25 or below. It must then recover that institution, complete all documented restitution obligations that arose in its response, restore Confidence to at least 75 and Reach to at least 50, and maintain both for 180 days without another established wrongful purge.

**Disqualifiers and difficulty:** Deliberately lowering a meter through an unrelated debug action or a reversible policy toggle cannot create the qualifying crisis. The trigger requires the actual takeover and breakdown receipts. No minimum number of innocent deaths is required. Difficulty is hard because recovery must persist through active pressure. Visibility is hidden until the qualifying crisis has actually occurred.

**Icon and tracking:** A repaired institutional seal with visible seams and a protected household behind it. Track causal crisis identity, inherited restitution obligations, actual repair, wrongful-action outcomes and continuous recovery dates. Restitution cannot erase the historical death ledger.

### 119_mistresses_host_restored

**Title direction:** National recovery after the inside attack succeeds, emphasizing surviving people and restored institutions.

**Eligible player and conditions:** A human host loses its original capital or at least 40% of its frozen original population weight in The Unveiling. Continue through a supported human remnant, exile or legitimate restoration. Recover every original-host state seized by that host's initial brood, defeat that brood or its surviving merged successor's claim to those states, and complete civilian service restoration in the capital and at least one other affected service area where the map permits one. At least half of the host's registered protected partner cohort must remain alive through the recovery.

**Disqualifiers and difficulty:** A separate unrelated human occupation is not automatically an alien conquest. Releasing and re-annexing one's own tag does not manufacture the defeat or restoration. A one-state host uses its single real service area rather than an invented second state. Difficulty is very hard because the player must survive a substantial opening loss and finish reconstruction. Visibility is hidden until a qualifying territorial loss.

**Icon and tracking:** The human administrative emblem recovered from an alien lattice, with a lit civil-service building. Track the original snapshot, legitimate continuation, held restoration claims, protected people and completed service receipts. Use actual surviving cohorts rather than requested evacuation totals.

### 119_mistresses_small_power_contribution

**Title direction:** A small country makes a decisive practical contribution to humanity's survival.

**Eligible player and conditions:** A human country with no more than ten operating controlled civilian factories, military factories and dockyards at the original manifestation remains an independent viable human participant. During the Mother war, it completes a successful relay disruption, receives at least 500 real evacuated human survivors through a functioning corridor, and contributes its own paid resources to the successful coalition final mission. The Mother must actually be defeated.

**Disqualifiers and difficulty:** Industrial size is frozen before the crisis, so releasing factories immediately before the final mission does not qualify. Refugee round trips count each person once. Supplies must come from a real owned or delivered stock and the coalition's single commitment. Difficulty is very hard because a small responder must sustain several complementary contributions. Visibility is hidden until Mother emergence.

**Icon and tracking:** A small illuminated harbor or field office linked to a broken giant command structure. Track the initial capacity band, real arrivals, relay operation receipt and contributed final resources. Do not require the small country to own the lair or steal allied territorial credit.

### 119_mistresses_mother_defeated

**Title direction:** A costly coordinated victory over the supreme alien organism.

**Eligible player and conditions:** A human country owns the successful coalition final mission or is its documented military coordinator. The actual required relay count remains disabled through the 30-day mission, the lair is held through a valid supplied route, the guardian condition is genuinely resolved, and the unique Mother-defeat receipt commits. The player must have delivered at least one real military or paid operational contribution before the final commitment.

**Disqualifiers and difficulty:** A debug defeat, missing-entity cleanup, an orphaned objective or merely observing an ally's cinematic does not qualify. A valid absent-guardian territorial route remains eligible when it reflects the actual finite army state, rather than an implementation error. Difficulty is very hard. Visibility is hidden until the lair and relay objectives become known.

**Icon and tracking:** A severed alien command crown above a defended human position. Track the Mother generation, coalition ownership, qualifying contribution, exact final interval and one-time defeat. The icon must not use an unsourced cultural quotation.

### 119_mistresses_total_military_victory

**Title direction:** Defeating the entire alien order through a sustained military campaign.

**Eligible player and conditions:** In a genuine Mother campaign, the player's human coalition destroys every viable territorial brood, removes all viable lair and guardian positions, and resolves the Mother through the direct total-conquest rule before any paid final-containment mission succeeds. The player must supply actual fighting forces in at least two immutable world regions during that campaign.

**Disqualifiers and difficulty:** Cosmetic annexation, a tag cleanup or deleting a disconnected survivor record is not military destruction. An unrelated human faction's conquest without the player's material participation is insufficient. Difficulty is extreme because the coalition must finish the international war without relying on the shorter decisive mission. Visibility is hidden until Mother emergence.

**Icon and tracking:** Several separated alien territorial masses broken by a coherent human front. Track real war participation by region, active brood generations, complete territorial defeat and the unique Mother resolution source. It does not require a player to deliberately leave relays functioning or abandon useful counterplay.

### 119_mistresses_littoral_breakout

**Title direction:** An isolated alien foothold builds a real maritime war capability and escapes containment.

**Eligible player and conditions:** Begin as a littoral brood with no controlled land connection to another brood and no free starting naval package. Survive 180 days, preserve a valid supplied capital or port through at least one actual enemy blockade or contested sea-route interruption, and establish a permanent controlled bridgehead on another landmass through a legal native military operation. Sustain that bridgehead and its real transport route for 60 days.

**Disqualifiers and difficulty:** Sea adjacency does not count as a merger or a bridgehead. A scripted state grant, a released friendly puppet or an unsupported instant invasion is insufficient. Difficulty is very hard because transport, fuel, supply and a finite army all matter. Visibility is hidden until the player legitimately takes brood control.

**Icon and tracking:** A small alien coastal emblem crossing a visibly contested strait toward a fortified shore. Track opening geography, actual starting naval receipts, blockade or route disruption, legal operation, bridgehead control and transport continuity. No extra navy is created for the achievement.

### 119_mistresses_continental_consolidation

**Title direction:** Several independent alien revolts become a genuine continental power.

**Eligible player and conditions:** As a brood player, become or continue in the deterministic recipient of contact mergers that unite at least three different original-host outbreaks. Hold a continuous controlled land corridor across their meaningful surviving territories, sustain at least 10,000 actual adult-alien combatants across fielded forces and reserves without double counting, and keep the resulting country supplied and at war for 90 days after the third origin joins.

**Disqualifiers and difficulty:** Reused tags, empty donors, artificial friendly releases and repeat grants do not count as new origins or adults. The recipient must be the actual prescribed strongest member, including its tie-break rules. Difficulty is very hard because consolidation must survive subsequent war and logistics. Visibility is hidden until the first real brood merger.

**Icon and tracking:** Three distinct brood emblems connected by one land-shaped organism. Track original-host sets, frozen component recipient, real conserved adults, corridor and supplied interval. A human player handed to the recipient through the supported continuation keeps the relevant provenance.

### 119_mistresses_supreme_brood_victory

**Title direction:** The alien player turns a successful international uprising into an actual final conquest.

**Eligible player and conditions:** As an active brood participant, materially contribute to the sustained multiregion success that creates the Mother, continue under her supreme command, and reach the real alien victory state with no viable human resistance remaining. The Mother must remain alive at that resolution. The player must have controlled a qualifying original-host capital and fielded actual forces in at least two regions before emergence.

**Disqualifiers and difficulty:** Reaching Chaos 1,001 or hosting the cinematic is insufficient. An initial Unveiling allocation supplies no recent-conquest credit. A missing human tag, invalid exile check or observer switch cannot stand in for defeating resistance. Difficulty is extreme. Visibility is hidden until genuine Mother emergence.

**Icon and tracking:** The colossal Mother above a connected alien command map, using the approved anatomy. Track the strict emergence receipt, player contribution, continuing brood identity and the complete human-resistance defeat result. Do not add a separate requirement to kill a numerical quota of civilians.

### 119_mistresses_brood_after_severance

**Title direction:** A regional alien government survives the permanent loss of supreme command.

**Eligible player and conditions:** A brood with the distributed-command route experiences a genuine Mother defeat, survives the full 60-day command shock, restores a valid command institution, and then retains a supplied territorial perimeter for 180 further days while facing actual human war. At the end it must have at least 1,000 conserved living adult aliens and a funded fighting formation.

**Disqualifiers and difficulty:** Deliberately switching an incompatible route after the Mother dies does not count as having established distributed continuity. A peace obtained by a debug command, free replacement army or a respawned Mother disqualifies the result. Difficulty is very hard because the player must recover a real surviving country under pressure. Visibility is hidden until Mother defeat.

**Icon and tracking:** Several surviving local command nodes around a broken central organism. Track attained route, permanent Mother defeat, shared shock duration, command recovery, actual war, supplied perimeter and the surviving adult ledger. This rewards the country package's aftermath rather than undoing humanity's victory.

## What a convincing release must demonstrate

The following are playable acceptance scenarios. Their purpose is to establish the specified behavior, not to demand an unbounded testing campaign. Implementation should use the smallest controlled fixture that resolves each actual risk, then a limited set of representative campaigns for interactions. A design document or successful parser is not evidence of a working native window, preserved multiplayer controller or correct engine population change.

## Evaluating difficulty without confusing it with correctness

First establish that the same person, cohort, item and state are not credited twice. Then evaluate screening strategies on the same saved evidence sets and budgets. Compare indiscriminate purge, universal sparing, affordable investigation and selective corroboration with institutional protection. Use saved human lives, eventual revolt readiness, public order and real expenditure together. A high alien-removal count alone is not a successful policy.

The portrait-only identity classifier should perform near the relevant sampling prior on held-out fictional assignments. Any demographic group, source website, crop convention, filename or image-quality difference that predicts guilt is a design failure. Test the deliberate biography-level perfection cue against its likelihood-ratio ceiling of 1.25. These are separate checks because the raw photograph is assigned independently of truth.

Evaluate the 70% ordinary and 65% visible-emergency AI thresholds against the actual saved evidence family, source-compromise mixture and two-independent-adverse-family requirement. Check the entire legal action pool and actual resources. A reachable posterior is necessary, but does not prove that the policy protects more people or makes sensible military priorities.

For revolt and war balance, compare a peaceful small state, a wartime major, a fragmented colonial host, an occupied country, an isolated littoral brood and a connected industrial brood. Measure time to containment, opening land and forces, supplied combat effectiveness, replacement throughput, successful mergers, emergence frequency and the human time required to reach the Mother. The purpose is to adjust the declared tuning values while preserving the user's threshold and playable-victory requirements.

The ordinary equipment and adult constraints must remain meaningful at high Chaos. A Mother that becomes unbeatable through multiplying old grants is a correctness defect. A Mother that is too strong despite correct accounting is a balance problem. These require different remedies and should not be hidden behind a generic difficulty bonus.

## Completion of the design and later implementation

The companion execution map and final prompts assign the native implementation, art, rights review, localisation, audio, country, unit, UI, probability and completion work. All achievements above belong to the launch handoff, with containment and basic human victory tracking implemented early so later routes can use the same provenance. Hidden late achievements still need complete icons and tracking before release.

Native technology and doctrine grants, unit ownership transfer, exact understrength creation, multiplayer continuation, human-cohort transactions under alien occupation and legal corridor passage require capability evidence. If the chosen runtime cannot support one, the implementation must report the exact affected design result and establish a supported equivalent before claiming completion. It cannot silently replace the event with an approximate invasion, a small repeated portrait set or an automatic ending.

The resulting campaign is complete when the core relationship benefits, difficult uncertain screening, protective networks, two evolutions, simultaneous territorial war, conserved mergers, sustained Mother emergence, playable human victory and coherent aftermath all work together. The complete delivery also needs the country routes, AI, full portrait release floor, original unit assets, three super-event packages, decision and mission set, achievements and correct shared-system records described throughout these specifications.
