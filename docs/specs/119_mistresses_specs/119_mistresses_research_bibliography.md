# Mistresses: primary research and production leads

## Scope of this research

This bibliography records the external primary sources used for portrait acquisition planning, rights conditions, candidate audio and the targeted repository comparison. It supports the design handoff. It is not a manifest of 1,250 cleared identities, an audio audition record or a legal clearance for every item in an archive.

Research was performed on 23 October 2026. Where a page's current content was available only through primary-domain search indexing, that limitation is stated below. The production worker must inspect and preserve the actual item record and file before approving any runtime use. The supplied project sources have their own complete reading record in [Source evidence](../../plans/119_mistresses_plans/imported_review/119_mistresses_source_evidence.md) and [Source coverage](../../plans/119_mistresses_plans/imported_review/119_mistresses_source_coverage.json).

## Photograph discovery and item-rights guidance

| Source | What it supports | Actual research status and production implication |
|---|---|---|
| [Wikimedia Commons reuse guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia) | Reusers must check the specific file's terms and relevant non-copyright restrictions | Guidance retrieved. Use individual file pages, originals and attribution records. A Commons search result is not item clearance |
| [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | Attribution, license reference and modification notice, with rights outside the license still relevant | Primary deed retrieved. Follow the linked applicable legal text for the actual asset. Do not imply endorsement or assume the license settles the fictional sensitive-use context |
| [Library of Congress Free to Use and Reuse](https://www.loc.gov/free-to-use/) | Curated discovery routes for reusable material | Primary collection guidance retrieved. Verify each selected item and its recorded rights basis |
| [Women of Protest rights and access](https://www.loc.gov/collections/women-of-protest/about-this-collection/rights-and-access/) | A relevant portrait source with collection-specific rights detail and exceptions | Primary rights information retrieved through search. Candidate count does not equal cleared adult identity count, and protected exceptions must be excluded |
| [Smithsonian Open Access FAQ](https://www.si.edu/openaccess/faq/) | CC0-marked assets and the distinction between copyright status and other possible rights | Primary-domain indexed FAQ text was available. A direct open returned an unavailable page in this session. Treat it as a discovery lead and verify the current actual item before production |

A source's reusable status and a real person's contextual suitability are separate acceptance questions. The planning requirement is predominantly authentic internet photographs of adults used under fictional identities, with documented permission or other adequate rights basis for the intended treatment. It does not require targeting recognizable living public figures, carrying real biographies into cases or using photographs whose restrictions remain unresolved.

The portrait worker should seek a broad set of source collections, inspect originals and deduplicate actual people across them. The proposed 2,000-candidate queue, 50-identity pilot and later batches are production quantities chosen for this design. They are not measured archive yields. No external source has been cited as already providing the complete cleared pool.

The final super-event cues target 60 to 120 seconds and stay within two minutes unless separately authorized. That requirement comes from the current supplied project skill, not from the lengths of these recordings. Longer originals can supply properly documented excerpts. Source composition and recording rights both matter. The commercial Tomorrow's Girls recording and lyrics are not required by this design.

The production manifest must record creator, title, exact source URL, license/version, access date, original hash, final hash, in/out points, edits, attribution and the actual sound consumer. A discovered title alone is insufficient. If the candidate does not fit the scene, select a different researched licensed recording instead of claiming the lead was approved.

## Text and quotation research still required

The design gives directions for the three super-events and every ordinary player-facing surface. It does not include researched final quotations, cultural allusions, slogans, lyrics or complete localisation. The super-event prompt assigns that research and writing before final integration. Accepted event names are the user's supplied names. Working action, route and achievement labels are not approved final copy.

This boundary prevents a remembered quotation or attractive placeholder from being mistaken for sourced production material. It also allows the final writer to match the exact native UI length and the player's knowledge at the actual event phase.
