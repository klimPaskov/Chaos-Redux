## Time and difficult edge cases

Ordinary strategic missions normally last at least 90 days. The individual case actions, 45-day revealed local emergency, 60-day relay repair and 30-day final containment have explicit operational reasons for shorter durations. Their native timer and visible deadline must agree. No currently true stockpile threshold can auto-complete the work on the first tick.

An unresolved nonterritorial pocket keeps its baseline local production burden and one host stability penalty until real containment. Cancelling grants no progress, suppression or retreat. Its one lifetime retreat opportunity and 25% original-cohort limit belong to Part 5. A zero-Confidence, zero-war-support host cannot remove a pocket by a free start-and-cancel loop.

The local emergency uses 30 Command Power and 50/100/150 support equipment. The 90-day civil route consumes up to five current war-support points and an additional local production burden, but still needs actual protected authority and the final 30 days of maintained presence. Both operate on saved reachable pockets. No mission invents an army, second state or legal capital.

Evacuation uses Part 2's actual throughput and destination capacity. Keep origin, transit, arrival and integration receipts distinct. Supplied land throughput is at most 500 × F people per service day, the ten-convoy sea route 250 × F, and the walking route 50. These ceilings never override the smaller actual cohort, route or reception limit. An interrupted route does not automatically kill people who remain at origin.

Relay disruption uses its saved random rank and final evidence-based 25% to 85% full-success threshold, followed by the next 15% for partial success. The interface can explain observable preparation without revealing an unobserved defender action through a precise live probability. One coalition operation per relay and the 180-day start interval prevent parallel rerolls.

An operation's successful 60-day relay disablement cannot be shortened by repair. A militarily disabled relay requires restored real alien control, supply, 25 Command Power, 250 Line equipment, 50 support equipment and a maintained 60-day repair. The required number of relays for final containment is `ceil(0.60 × actual saved relay count)`. At final commitment, enough relays must have at least 30 days remaining or durable human-held disablement. Exactly 30 maintained days count before same-tick timed expiry. Real earlier control loss still fails the mission.

The final mission pays 50 Command Power plus 50/100/150 support equipment once through one coalition receipt. Allied contributions cannot create duplicates. Mother absence, defeated guardian or total military conquest must resolve through Part 8's valid territorial fallback, not an impossible orphaned entity.

Assimilation targets only eligible saved adult civilian cohorts. Its four commitments are 50 Command Power, 100 Political Power, 250 Line equipment and 50 uncommitted alien staff. Rescue can preempt the target reservation through the shared cohort owner. Interruption leaves living people in their actual place. Successful measured losses create at most half as many pending alien adults, sharing the same release ceiling as industrial maturation. A merger preserves paid sites and time, but creates no extra start allowance.
