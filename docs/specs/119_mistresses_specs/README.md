## The design in one view

The early relationship wave gives real stability and demographic benefits while weakening military participation. No country starts knowing the women are aliens. Baseline cells already protect members, place companions around institutions and maintain international routes. Replacement Wave at 600+ expands their ability to capture authority. The Unveiling at 800+ turns the surviving network into a coordinated open war, after dynamic evolution pacing and a real response window.

The screening game uses immutable fictional people, saved evidence and a large varied photographic pool. It admits both humans and aliens from finite real records. Beauty and apparent perfection remain unreliable. Investigations have source dependence and possible compromise, while wrongful purges damage actual people, cooperation and institutions. Human and AI actors pay the same costs. Exactly two persistent event-owned public values, Confidence and Reach, describe the response without exposing a national infestation percentage.

The revolt freezes all hosts before resolving people, states and armies. Territory depends on actual influence and protected institutions. Forces come from surviving adult aliens and measured absorbed adult partners. Small pockets receive a finite local emergency rather than an empty country or invented battalion. Cadre, Line and Siege have different battlefield roles, production obligations and logistical weaknesses.

All broods fight on one side. Connected land components merge into the prescribed strongest country while preserving surviving assets and original provenance. The design explicitly handles ties, several simultaneous contacts, research exclusions, paid production, characters and player continuation. It uses the Zombie system as an inspected precedent rather than assuming that the existing implementation already supplies this complete algorithm.

Mother of Mothers requires Chaos strictly above 1,000 and sustained success in multiple world regions. Durable wartime conquests can count without rewriting sovereignty, but the original uprising alone cannot provide the required recent success. Her emergence begins a playable global survival war. Humanity can sever real relays, take the lair and complete the final mission, or win through complete military conquest. The Mother cannot return after defeat. Surviving local broods and human occupation records continue coherently.

## Important design decisions and their status

The Mother territorial predicate counts qualifying durable wartime control, not just formal annexation. A recapture after any control loss requires a new 30-day admission. Initial uprising states never earn recent-gain credit. The strict Chaos threshold, region/global requirements and continuous qualification remain separate. These rules avoid both an unreachable endgame during a continuing war and an automatic Mother from the opening revolt.

The source model makes the original proposed 85% ordinary AI threshold impractical. The final launch proposal uses 70% ordinarily and 65% during a visible immediate-access emergency, with two independent adverse families and all real costs. That is a reachability correction, not a measured claim that indiscriminate purge can never be optimal. The complete policy comparison remains an implementation balance task.

The guardian and relocation escort designate existing funded formations. Relay repairs consume resources and time. A successful disruption retains its whole 60-day window, and the final mission rejects a known too-short window before payment. Small pockets retain their actual local burden if a response is cancelled. None of these features can generate an army, resource refund or containment result through a repeated interface action.
