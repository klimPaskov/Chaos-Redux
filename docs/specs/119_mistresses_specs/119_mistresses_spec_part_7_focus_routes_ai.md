# Part 7. Focus routes, strategic choices and AI

## A tree that changes how the war is fought

The brood focus tree should turn the opening revolt into a recognizable strategic campaign. At first, the player is trying to preserve the finite force created at Unveiling. Later, the player chooses how to govern the captured territory, build a replacement economy, move armies across difficult geography and exploit cooperation with other broods. The strongest route depends on the position. A continental brood with damaged railways needs a different opening from a port enclave facing a hostile fleet.

The human side has a parallel progression without losing its existing national story. A compact additive focus layer strengthens evidence institutions, prepares military continuity, supports international cooperation and helps recover from open revolt. A player pursuing the original country's industrial or diplomatic tree can still respond through decisions. Event 119 should create difficult strategic priorities, rather than confiscate the whole national focus screen.

This chapter describes routes and major anchor groups. An anchor group is a coherent strategic commitment that the implementation worker can express through a suitably paced group of focuses. It is not a fixed node count. No exact graph, coordinates or final focus names are prescribed. All labels below are working design labels, not final localisation.

The two persistent public custom values remain Public Confidence and Investigative Reach. Native stability, war support, manpower, equipment, research slots, industry, supply and map control provide the rest of the strategic information. A brood does not receive a third meter for unity, biomass, obedience, ascendance or command power under another name. Mother qualification and maturation records are shown as concrete objectives, dates and limited statuses where appropriate, without turning hidden arithmetic into a new public gauge.

## Pacing, commitments and reward discipline

Use a lean opening. Emergency commitments can take ten days, ordinary choices 35 days and major institutional or military commitments 70 days. A 70-day focus needs a substantial change in what the player can do. A small efficiency adjustment does not justify another mandatory month of clicks. Crisis decisions remain available when a focus cannot finish before the 30-day Unveiling warning expires.

A lasting mutually exclusive choice needs an actual strategic reason. Centralized and distributed authority are incompatible command settlements. Retained machinery and dedicated organic production compete for the same institutional investment. Cadre, Line and Siege development are complementary military capabilities with costs and priorities, not three permanently exclusive unit buttons. A port route is unavailable without a feasible maritime position, but its absence should not lock inland countries out of the common tree ending.

Rewards should often change a capability or solve a specific problem. Useful rewards include a legal template, a production unlock, a research bonus, a repair project, an evacuation alternative, access to a new mission, a specialist role, a reciprocal diplomatic proposal, a finite equipment conversion or a concrete occupation policy. Political power, experience and stability can accompany these rewards, but should not be the entire identity of every branch.

At most three Event 119 focus-owned national spirits can be active on any route, including the package's starting spirits. Upgrades replace an existing slot. Mother effects, lost-capital recovery and postwar aftermath obey the same cap. Existing unrelated spirits from a host's original national tree are outside the new package's ownership, but the implementation still reviews cumulative power. Moving a permanent reward into a sequence of timed ideas does not satisfy the design intent.

## Host progression remains additive

The host additions attach to the existing tree through the supported focus extension mechanism selected after inspection. Preserve the original country identity, completed focuses, mutually exclusive choices, available branches and current focus behavior. Do not load a replacement national tree as a shortcut. Where an installed country tree cannot accept the planned extension cleanly, the same crisis capability must have its normal decision adapter while the focus integration is repaired.

A single visible incident opens the response family. Replacement Wave broadens institutional targets. The Unveiling warning opens urgent continuity and protection anchors. Open alien war unlocks reconquest and coalition anchors. Mother war reveals its endgame group only when the shared registry and part 8 lifecycle permit it. The host never sees a locked future branch whose text casually reveals the unknown alien identity before the campaign has earned that knowledge.

### Evidence-led institutions

This route develops reliable records, protected testimony and independent review. Its first anchor increases practical investigative reach without claiming infallibility. A proposed 35-day investment grants Reach +5 and opens a service-circuit improvement using part 4's cost and capacity rules. Later anchors protect the provenance of evidence, improve cross-border corroboration and reduce the cost or time of a specific investigation operation by 15%.

The strategic payoff is better casework and stronger resistance to institutional compromise. The player still chooses investigate, spare or purge. Focus completion never labels the current queue's aliens, rerolls a suspect's truth or opens a hidden national infestation display. Reach thresholds continue to govern the shared queue and simultaneous investigations. The current design supports five national cases, increasing to ten at Reach 50, with one, two and three investigation slots at Reach 0, 25 and 50 respectively. Focuses improve the shared system rather than creating a private parallel case queue. [S]

A major 70-day anchor establishes independent audit and protected archive continuity. Its payoff is an additional route to restore a compromised institution and preserve evidence during capital evacuation. It can offer a one-time research bonus for an appropriate communications or administrative technology, plus a small experience reward for trained personnel. It does not produce a universal alien detector.

The challenge is time and political cost. Evidence work can be outpaced by a prepared network. Higher Confidence helps referrals, but it does not ensure that every witness is reliable. A player who ignores military continuity can still face a serious revolt despite excellent investigations.

### Emergency security

This route makes coercive capacity and immediate institutional control more available. Its opening provides a temporary patrol or access-review alternative under part 4. It can reduce the setup cost of a defined emergency operation by 15% or shorten a specific response mission. It must also create an actual administrative and legitimacy burden.

The route does not give a free purge, waive proof requirements or silently increase every suspect's alien prior. Every purge still incurs the universal Confidence −5, and a later independently established wrongful purge incurs the additional Confidence −15 and serious 60-day unrest defined by the case system. Completing an emergency focus cannot erase that receipt. [S]

A later choice either subjects the apparatus to independent review or entrenches its exceptional powers. Review costs political resources and time, restores a bounded Confidence +5 after actual restitution and improves future evidence cooperation. Entrenchment improves a concrete short-term containment or access-denial tool while leaving the confidence and unrest liabilities intact. Neither branch makes innocent deaths a sustainable resource generator.

The terminal payoff is an effective continuity institution capable of holding key depots and offices during the warning period. Its protection contributes only to the real protected-defense reductions in part 5. It cannot award the maximum reduction to every state because the country selected a focus. Defense sites still need the operation, location and capacity that the shared mission requires.

### Accommodation and exploitation

A host can attempt to preserve social benefits, negotiate access to influential networks or use compromised channels for short-term advantage. This is a risky policy route, not a hidden way to receive permanent alien technology without consequences.

The opening preserves an eligible part of the household-support benefit and can reduce the native recruitment or war-support burden by five percentage points through funded family services. A deeper commitment opens reversible access review and controlled liaison operations. These can obtain records, delay a particular confrontation or produce an opportunity for corroboration. They can also expose staff, allow a real network opportunity or lose a source. Results depend on the existing evidence and participants, not a focus-specific truth roll.

An informed host may later choose a limited tactical arrangement after alien identity is established. The agreement must specify its territory, duration, paid cost and termination. It never grants a general exemption from Unveiling, removes all hostile cells or lets the human country join the alien side through an accidental diplomatic inheritance. A secret accommodation cannot block another player's protected evacuation mission.

The valuable terminal outcome is a negotiated local evacuation or orderly separation that saves registered people and preserves infrastructure. A failed arrangement leaves a concrete institutional exposure and a recoverable continuity task. This route can be pragmatic and costly at the same time. It is not a comic punishment branch with no rational use.

### Military continuity and household protection

These support anchors remain compatible with all three response approaches. They strengthen trusted command, protected partner separation, depot security, rail evacuation and disciplined reserve deployment. A proposed 35-day anchor opens the service-family protection mission and a 50% research bonus for an appropriate support or logistics technology. The mission still requires finite eligible partners and a real protected destination.

A major anchor creates a tested command continuity arrangement. It can replace a portion of the event's military disruption penalty, grant 25 army experience and unlock the depot-protection operation. Its effect is supported planning, readiness or supply coordination. It cannot issue, cancel or draw battleplans for the player.

When the Unveiling warning begins, ten-day emergency anchors can accelerate an already prepared protection or evacuation plan. They do not create a national instant shield. A country that neglected the earlier institutions has fewer protected routes and must make sharper choices about whom and what to move.

### Coalition and reconquest

International anchors develop reciprocal source exchange before revelation and concrete military cooperation afterward. They connect to the Tin foil hat reaction without creating a second alien invasion. The host can seek access, equipment support, coordinated defensive aims and a coalition conference through the existing diplomatic framework.

A player should face choices between immediate local defense and helping another country stop a stronger brood. Aid has a measured equipment or political cost. It can prevent an enemy land connection, preserve a useful partner and improve later coalition participation. It cannot convert all human rivals into obedient allies by a focus completion.

Reconquest anchors concern known alien territory, evacuation of surviving human enclaves, restoration of administration and evidence recovery from captured offices. A major payoff is a funded reconstruction package for a limited set of actual liberated states. It repairs or accelerates repair rather than granting undamaged replacement industry everywhere. Restoration follows original-owner and controller records. A colonial or occupied state does not automatically belong to the country that happened to finish the focus.

### Host spirit slots and aftermath

Use the host's three Event 119 slots for the household relationship condition, the response institution and the current national crisis or recovery state. Upgrade the household condition as support or separation succeeds. Replace the response institution when emergency authority is reviewed or entrenched. Consolidate national warning, open-war disruption and recovery effects in the third slot where compatible.

Individual case consequences remain real, including the specified wrongful-purge unrest. They are not extra permanent focus rewards. State-level destruction, occupation and displacement remain on their actual state or cohort records. The focus cap must not erase those harms merely to make the national idea bar tidy.

After successful containment, the human country retains useful institutions and legitimate household services. After reconquest, it must restore confidence, reintegrate displaced people and investigate abuses. After Mother defeat, it still needs to defeat or contain surviving local broods. The terminal rewards include research capacity, reconstruction, diplomatic credibility and the removal of earned crisis penalties. They do not restore absorbed partners or resurrect dead case subjects.

## Brood opening and survival anchors

The brood opening is short enough to matter while war is already underway. The package from part 6 provides its finite army, laws and two starting spirits. The first choices help use that package. They do not delay host war until the player completes a ceremonial foundation focus.

| Anchor group | Unlock and problem | Concrete payoff | Continuing limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Command continuity | Active brood with a legal capital. Officers and scattered cells need a usable hierarchy. | A ten-day emergency commitment reduces the command-disruption recovery penalty from −20% to −10% and enables the first command settlement. | The planning burden and finite reserves remain until the deeper commitment. |
| Supply corridor | A real route between the capital and a useful controlled depot, port or industrial state. | Opens a paid repair or transport mission and grants 25 army experience for a verified operational reorganization. | It does not create railways through enemy territory or guarantee supply throughput. |
| Force accounting | Completed opening deployment receipt and actual survivors. | Unlocks practical paid templates, reorganization and reinforcement priorities. | No second OOB, fresh manpower or reserve equipment grant. |
| Infrastructure continuity | Controlled damaged industry or a real infrastructure bottleneck. | Replaces the initial economy penalty with a less severe tier after the associated repair commitment. | Ownership alone does not make a damaged factory usable. |
| Territorial administration | Actual occupied human communities and unresolved survival records. | Opens a coherent occupation policy and a supported enclave or evacuation settlement. | Human residents remain outside alien conscription. |

An isolated brood can prioritize local command and perimeter survival before a large rail project. A country without a valid port never receives a mandatory naval focus as the only route onward. If the original capital falls during an anchor, preserve paid work and evaluate the documented continuation condition. Do not reward a completed repair for a site the country no longer controls.

## Command settlement: concentration or distribution

Centralized authority supports a compact striking army and rapid exploitation from a strong center. Its main anchor replaces the command spirit with a centralized form, grants a singular leader role and opens a focused offensive preparation mission. Proposed national effects are planning speed +15% and organization recovery +5%, with the larger offensive benefit tied to the funded temporary operation rather than permanent universal attack.

Its cost is dependence on a working command center and a smaller set of high-value leaders. Losing the active center replaces the spirit with a command-disruption tier until a legal successor is established. This should be a meaningful operational window for humans, not an immediate collapse of every unit. The centralized terminal payoff improves a chosen army-support capability and offers a finite doctrine or research investment.

Distributed authority supports a longer front, peripheral defense and command continuity under bombing or capital loss. Its command form proposes organization recovery +10% and reduced ordinary lost-capital disruption duration by 25%. A peripheral-defense mission can prepare a limited set of real border or port sites. It does not apply a global fortification bonus to every future conquest.

Its cost is slower concentrated planning and a weaker immediate offensive preparation package. The route values cadres, reserves and local logistics, but can still build Siege formations if it pays for them. This ordinary-capital benefit does not shorten the shared 60-day shock after Mother defeat. The terminal payoff is a robust command succession institution and better access to defensive reorganization.

The settlements are mutually exclusive at the major commitment. Early emergency stabilization is common. Later crisis reform can permit a deliberate switch only through an explicit long and costly transition that replaces the previous spirit, leader role and incompatible capability. It preserves completed tangible rewards and does not replay the new route's opening grants.

Both settlements obey compulsory land-contact absorption. Distributed command does not mean independent broods can decline a merger. Centralized authority does not receive a bonus to the deterministic recipient rank merely because its leader claims supremacy.

## Economy settlement: preserve machinery or specialize organically

Retained machinery prioritizes industrial continuity, transport, ordinary supports and eventual conventional air or naval capacity. The major anchor replaces infrastructure discontinuity with a machinery spirit, proposes production-efficiency growth +10%, and opens a limited reconstruction or equipment-conversion project. A later anchor can grant a research slot when the country has retained a genuine industrial or research center.

The player gains flexibility but still needs resources, fuel, trained adults, legal variants and port access. The route cannot copy every conquered design or operate an obsolete captured fleet without maintenance. Aircraft and ships consume ordinary production time. Its advanced payoff should make a functioning combined-arms country possible, rather than turn organic troops into the only worthwhile investment.

Dedicated organic production commits industry to family equipment and adult maturation. Its spirit proposes organic-equipment production cost −15%, implemented through the supported equipment or technology modifier scope selected by the worker. If no safe family-limited national modifier exists, use the corresponding technology or equipment-cost implementation and include it in the cumulative balance budget. Do not claim an unsupported effect name.

The route unlocks the paid maturation process in part 6 and later reduces its Line-equipment input from 250 to 200. The 90-day maturation period, 100-adult batch output and country-wide release cap remain. It does not accelerate human births or reduce a child maturation timer. Ordinary machinery capability remains available at a lower priority, so an island brood can still obtain transport through real industry.

The commitments are mutually exclusive where they represent the same industrial institution. Shared repair, research foundations, resource acquisition and basic logistics remain common. A later fusion payoff can remove one carefully chosen weakness after substantial territorial and industrial development, but it must not combine every full bonus from both branches. A merged donor's factories and legal technologies survive without importing a second economy spirit.

## Military families and strategic geography

Military development offers several coherent investments that can coexist. The cost is focus time, research, factories, equipment and the finite adult reserve. A player can prepare a specialist tool without being forced to ban the others forever.

| Family | Major anchor and reward | Challenge and counterplay | AI preference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cadre operations | Develop protected movement, reconnaissance and practical small formations. Give a 50% relevant research bonus and unlock a limited reconnaissance or raid mission. | Cadres cannot ignore fronts, sea crossings, firepower or ammunition. A raid has a real target, cost, duration and retreat condition. | Higher priority for small, forested, urban or fragmented positions and equipment-poor countries. |
| Line sustainment | Standardize Line production, logistics support and defensive organization. Reward a useful template and a measured reduction in organic replacement burden. | Supply use and anti-armor weakness remain. This is the default army, not a permanent attack multiplier stack. | Highest general priority for continental fronts and countries rebuilding damaged infrastructure. |
| Siege commitment | Build the heavy production and training institution, then unlock a paid mixed breach formation. Reward a heavy-equipment research bonus and a suitable specialist. | Requires usable industry, a supplied site and a funded equipment bill. Losses are expensive. | Low priority without stable supply, adequate Line equipment and a fortified human target. |
| Conventional support | Maintain engineers, logistics, anti-air and selected captured weapons. Reward supported production access or a compatible research bonus. | Technology, manpower and equipment remain separate requirements. Captured stock is finite. | High priority when human armor or air attack presents a real weakness. |
| Maritime survival | Secure a valid port, preserve convoys and develop escort or air protection using legal conventional systems. | Naval superiority, range, fuel, transport and geography still govern operations. | Strong for isolated port countries, absent for a wholly inland country with no feasible maritime goal. |

Territorial anchors point toward concrete nearby objectives. A railway junction can enable a corridor. A port can connect an overseas possession. An industrial state can sustain replacement. An original host capital can support the shared Mother predicate. These goals have different strategic values, and the tree should not present them as interchangeable claims granting the same factories.

The expansion family should offer regional methods before global ambitions. A short corridor offensive can be more valuable than a distant capital. Conquest rewards are paid once for the actual objective, with a defensive alternative if the target becomes unavailable through a legitimate settlement. A focus should not grant an instant core on every conquered human state. Occupation, surviving residents and supply remain part of the campaign.

## Cooperation, merger inheritance and hidden development

Cooperation anchors open measured equipment transfers, shared intelligence about known human defenses and practical coordination requests. They can improve allied access and prepare administrative compatibility before land contact. They do not transfer all forces early to manipulate the eventual recipient rank, and they do not let one brood charge another's reserve without authorization from the actual owner action.

Absorption is automatic when part 10's controlled-land component contract is met. The recipient retains its command and economy settlement. Donor territorial rewards and legal capabilities transfer once, research slots use the maximum entitlement, and completion receipts prevent a second payoff. A donor's completed heavy-production institution can preserve legal Siege capability without giving the recipient the donor's incompatible full economy spirit.

Hidden development should emerge from an earned event, recovered technical archive or special terrain opportunity. A captured research center can reveal an adaptation project. A surviving institutional coordinator can provide a distinctive command option. The branch is hidden until the relevant condition is discovered, so its icon and navigation do not spoil the pre-revelation mystery.

A hidden project is neither a guaranteed optimal route nor a way to bypass the population budget. It can provide a rare equipment adaptation, a research bonus or a different crisis solution. Its documentation must state the trigger, competing uses of the recovered material, failure outcome and how the capability survives or retires on merger. No focus creates a permanent unexplained stockpile of miracle weapons.

## Crisis paths and endgame convergence

Crisis anchors are short, practical responses to actual failures. Lost capital, exhausted stores, a severed island, a destroyed maturation site and severe command disruption deserve distinct recovery conditions. They can open a retreat, reorganization, emergency production conversion or negotiated evacuation. They do not grant a free army whenever the player deliberately empties the reserve.

Emergency conversions consume actual compatible stores and return a bounded output. Reorganization preserves veteran units rather than replacing them. A transport crisis can prioritize construction and convoy allocation, but cannot launch an invasion through a blocked sea. A lost maturation site resolves the paid batch and living staff exactly once before a new project can be opened.

All viable routes converge on a mature war state. The player should have a functioning authority, an economy settlement, a chosen force mix and a way to address the country's geography. Convergence means that every route can participate in the endgame. It does not erase the costs that made those routes different.

The Mother approach becomes available only after the narrative and military conditions warrant it. Its anchors prepare command reception, secure relevant sites and choose how existing institutions serve supreme authority. Completion cannot set Chaos above 1000, fabricate a second qualifying region, alter the frozen world census or shorten part 8's sustained qualification by itself.

When the shared Mother predicate succeeds, the command spirit in slot one is replaced by the appropriate Mother command tier. The actual Mother character rules only her host polity, while regional governors elsewhere accept supreme command. Centralized institutions can emphasize offensive concentration. Distributed institutions can emphasize peripheral command continuity. The modifiers must fit inside part 8's total alien power budget and leave its discoverable human countermeasures effective. The fixed relay objectives, finite guardian, one legal connected-land relocation and permanent genuine defeat cannot be overwritten by a route payoff. This chapter adds no independent immortality, unlimited reinforcement or uncounterable global debuff.

Mother defeat opens a continuation group. The brood can stabilize a defensive perimeter, recover its command chain or support Part 8's limited civilian evacuation agreement while its local war continues. These anchors replace the command-slot aftermath after the shared 60-day shock, preserve actual territory and surviving units, and open postwar production or administration. A surviving Mother victory opens consolidation, occupied-territory administration and the management of remaining human opposition. Both outcomes leave a country worth playing.

## AI that follows the same strategic constraints

Use local focus weights, route plans and role-specific strategies. Do not alter global AI definitions simply to make one event's countries active. Dynamic brood identity belongs in strategy enable conditions with appropriate retirement or `abort_when_not_enabled` behavior. An `allowed` check evaluated only at startup is not a valid gate for a country that is created years later. Temporary phase behavior must use a removable supported mechanism, rather than accumulating permanent `add_ai_strategy` entries that have no corresponding removal effect. [A]

The AI consumes observed country and map facts. It may know its own fielded force, stores, supply and registered adult budget. A human response AI receives the evidence available through the case system. It must not inspect a suspect's hidden truth or use the global network ledger to target the perfect purge. Ordinary case-action thresholds remain the evidence-based 70% standard and 65% visible immediate-access crisis proposals, with two independent adverse evidence families required and finite monthly budgets from part 4. Those thresholds are not biological certainty. [S]

### Focus and route selection

Use a small baseline preference scale of five, ten and 20 for low, ordinary and strong relative priorities, then validate the complete candidate pool. A focus weight is not a percentage chance. Native focus selection uses competing random draws, while event choice uses its own probability model and decisions use separate score behavior. Do not report a 20-versus-ten focus choice as a 67% probability without the correct evaluator and all competing focuses. [A]

AI openings prioritize keeping a viable country. An isolated brood prefers command stabilization, port defense and low-cost cadres. A supplied industrial brood prefers Line replacement and a real offensive corridor. A country with almost no equipment should repair and produce before adding heavy templates. A threatened human host prioritizes immediate continuity and partner protection over a 70-day prestige project that cannot finish before revelation.

Command preference should consider front length, concentration of industry and exposure of the capital. Economy preference should consider usable factories, fuel access, inherited transport and the real adult replacement problem. Existing commitments are respected. The AI does not switch routes monthly to collect opening grants.

Mother preparation has no priority before eligibility is plausibly approaching. During an active Mother war, objective defense and useful attacks outrank ordinary economic vanity. After Mother defeat, command recovery and survival take precedence over continuing a focus whose premise has ceased to exist.

### Production and templates

Start with a proposed land-production allocation of 25% Cadre equipment, 60% Line equipment and 15% support and transport among the factories actually available for those roles. A qualified heavy country may shift to 20% Cadre, 50% Line, 15% Siege and 15% support. These are target shares, not extra minimum factories. A two-factory country cannot be ordered to run four separate five-factory minima.

Raise Line replacement priority when its fielded formations are under-equipped. Pause additional Siege recruitment while the ordinary army lacks equipment or the intended supply corridor is broken. Reserve a meaningful share of manpower for reinforcement rather than consuming the whole pool in queued divisions. The target share is 25% of the finite available alien manpower during ordinary active war, with a deliberate emergency exception when capital defense is at stake.

Maintain exactly one active role-level AI template preference for each role in a country. Overlapping generic and brood-specific entries can make upgrade behavior unpredictable. Role choice and deterministic highest-priority target-template selection must be reviewed separately. The AI can move from an emergency Cadre formation to its intended practical template when equipment supports it, without continually redesigning every division.

Maturation batches are funded only when Line equipment, support equipment, adult seed staff and a stable site are available. AI priority rises when replacement needs are sustained and current formations can survive the 90-day delay. There is no AI-only free equipment, manpower, industrial capacity or shortened incubation.

### War, ports and targets

Target the actual hostile host, nearby human supply nodes and useful corridors. Other broods are always allies. Existing human-war relationships are preserved where the shared transaction requires them, and no inherited guarantee can make one brood attack another.

AI strategy can influence an existing front or a legal native invasion plan. It cannot create a front, issue a battleplan, teleport units or ignore naval requirements. Coastal defense targets require actual owned ports and valid fronts. Aircraft need actual airfields, equipment, range and fuel. A strategy promising an overseas offensive without a functioning navy or air route is disabled until the native prerequisites exist.

Validate cumulative strategy weights against vanilla and other mod entries of the same type and target. Values from unlike strategy types are not comparable. A temporary defensive priority must retire when the emergency ends, and a destroyed country must not leave stale strategies pointing at a reused tag generation. All event-owned refreshes visit registered countries and changed targets, never a repeated whole-world scan.

## Presentation, navigation and assets

Organize the focus screen around a small number of readable strategic families. The host additions should remain visually connected to the crisis rather than appear as a second unrelated national tree. The brood tree can distinguish command, economy, forces, geography, cooperation and endgame through composition and palette. Exact placement follows the future rendered layout review, not an invented coordinate grid here. [F]

Add navigation only when real distance or branching makes it useful. Hidden branches receive navigation when revealed. Filters should describe actual player purposes, such as military capability, industry, security or reconstruction. Do not create a decorative custom GUI when standard focus filters, tooltips and decision objectives already communicate the choice.

Major anchor icons need distinct compositions. A command institution, maturation project, rail corridor, protected archive and siege organism should not all be the same emblem recolored. The route's icon communicates the action or capability, while the common alien visual system provides coherence. Mother and aftermath icons should communicate authority and severance without requiring body-horror detail to be readable.

Part 11 owns production and sourcing. This chapter requests focus icons, family headers where needed, leader and institution portraits, the three limited spirit families, map and decision references, and discoverable endgame objective art. The writing handoff explains intent, prerequisites, immediate results, risks and continuing obligations. It does not supply final names, descriptions, buttons or achievement text.

## Evaluation and tuning cases

| Scenario | Expected strategic behavior | Failure to correct |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human host with high Confidence and low Reach | Build capacity and protect evidence while using limited case slots. | A focus instantly exposes all aliens or ignores the shared queue. |
| Human host with high Reach and low Confidence | Protect sources, audit coercion and repair cooperation. | More investigators automatically solve the legitimacy problem. |
| Thirty-day warning during an unrelated long focus | Decisions and short eligible anchors provide practical protection. | The existing national tree is replaced or the player has no response. |
| Tiny island brood | Survival, port defense and finite Cadre use before grand expansion. | Mandatory Siege or land-corridor focus blocks all progress. |
| Large supplied brood | Line sustainment, planned corridor and selective heavy investment. | Every route rushes the same all-purpose combat spirit. |
| Equipment-starved brood | Reinforce existing formations and repair industry. | AI queues full armies or starts maturation with unpaid stores. |
| Absorption of opposite settlements | Recipient route persists, donor assets and legal capabilities survive once. | Both full spirit sets stack or completed donors pay again. |
| Mother threshold approached | Prepare useful institutions while actual territorial success remains necessary. | A focus summons her at Chaos 1000 or before sustained multi-region success. |
| Mother defeated | Command recovery, survival and remaining local war continue. | All focus access vanishes or the command replacement becomes a fourth permanent spirit. |
| No optional DLC | Complete response, brood production, war and endgame progression. | The sole unit unlock or essential mission requires a paid expansion. |

Future AI evaluation should inspect candidate pools before calculating probabilities, then sweep concrete scenarios around Reach 25 and 50, the Unveiling readiness boundaries, equipment shortages, industrial thresholds and Mother eligibility. Compare route frequencies and operational outcomes before and after a change. A source-level weight check does not prove good battlefield behavior. Optional testing stops once the named risk is resolved rather than expanding into an unrelated global AI rewrite.
