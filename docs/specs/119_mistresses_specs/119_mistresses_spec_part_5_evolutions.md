# Part 5: Replacement Wave and The Unveiling

## Escalation changes what the network can do

Ordinary infiltration already includes cross-border links, mutual protection, introductions and obstruction. Replacement Wave gives the network a more dangerous relationship with authority. The Unveiling turns accumulated private influence into an open territorial war. These are changes in the form of the threat, with new counterplay and visible consequences.

Chaos thresholds establish eligibility. They do not award free Chaos, create an automatic human defeat, replenish dead infiltrators or erase earlier containment. Every evolution reads the surviving campaign state.

## Timing and evolved openings

| State of the campaign | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Event first fires at 200 to 599 | Begin the ordinary relationship and investigation crisis |
| Active event reaches 600 | Replacement Wave enters the enabled evolution's MTTH schedule if a surviving active network exists |
| Event first fires at 600 to 799 | Begin an evolved opening with institutions already exposed to increased risk, while keeping alien identity initially unknown |
| Active event reaches 800 | After Replacement Wave has entered, The Unveiling enters its enabled MTTH schedule if a surviving network can act |
| Event first fires at 800 or higher | Begin a compressed crisis with a 90-day minimum response window from first manifestation before territorial revolt |
| Chaos falls below an already entered evolution | Retain the entered capabilities and consequences. Falling Chaos does not resurrect removed people or restore captured institutions |
| All viable networks are eliminated before eligibility | Do not manufacture an evolution from an empty event |

For a first manifestation at Chaos 600 to 799, opening seeded states use cell-presence bands 40, 50 or 60 and access bands 30, 40 or 50. At Chaos 800 or higher they use presence 60, 70 or 80 and access 40, 50 or 60. These saved evolved-opening bands represent an already established hidden network. They retain the same finite adult cohort limits and do not fabricate prior births, deaths or completed player actions. An event that was already active receives no free score increase merely for crossing a threshold.

For an already active event, crossing the threshold unlocks a dynamic evolution opportunity. It does not instantly activate the evolution. Both Replacement Wave and The Unveiling use the shared MTTH scheduler with a proposed base parameter of 90 days. Replacement Wave uses a factor of 0.50 when actual major institutions in at least two different hosts are already compromised. The Unveiling uses a factor of 0.50 when surviving territorial candidates qualify in at least two world regions. Otherwise each retains its base parameter. These are native MTTH tuning inputs, not a promise that the evolution fires on day 45 or day 90. The engine's probability semantics must be verified before translating them into an expected frequency.

The Unveiling's ordinary schedule requires Replacement Wave to have entered. A jump from Chaos 500 to 900 therefore makes the first evolution eligible, then permits the second schedule after the first commits. The separate evolved-opening rule can establish those initial capabilities immediately when the event first manifests at high Chaos. At 800 or above, that opening commits its shared revelation deadline no earlier than day 90. This is the documented first-fire exception to normal MTTH pacing.

Use the existing evolution enablement gate and shared history owner. MTTH opportunities arise only through simulation scheduling, never from a screen refresh, case click or settings preview. Falling below a not-yet-entered threshold pauses eligibility. It does not undo a committed evolution. Each successful activation has one durable receipt, so repeated callbacks, reloads and enablement refreshes cannot restart its consequences.

For an ordinary active campaign, The Unveiling has a 30-day actionable warning period after its MTTH activation commits. This is a deliberate short emergency period. The event's normal strategic missions remain longer. The late-opening minimum of 90 days takes priority if it would end later. After commitment, falling Chaos alone does not cancel the deadline. If all viable networks are genuinely eliminated before resolution, settle the empty crisis through containment and do not invent an alien force or a revelation consequence.

Warning does not mean every country suddenly receives a complete alien dossier. Before universal exposure, the public sees increasingly coordinated disruptions, missing partners and urgent but imperfect intelligence. A country that already proved an alien case can explain more to its own population. At the common revelation, all countries learn the essential truth through a global report.

The schedule has one saved deadline. Save/load, country switching and opening the investigation window cannot reset it. A country joining the crisis after the deadline inherits the open-war state rather than receiving a private rewind of the entire world.

## Evolution I: Replacement Wave

### The new threat

At Chaos 600+, surviving cells can replace or compromise officials, officers and local authorities. Some replacements are alien impersonators. Some compromised people remain human and act under pressure, attachment, deception or dependence. Their political behavior does not change their biological identity in the screening ledger.

Institutional access can now rise beyond the ordinary cap of 40, up to 80 through normal Replacement Wave activity. The difference is important. A quiet cell can survive with low access. A cell with high access can conceal its own evidence, redirect resources and prepare a coordinated seizure.

Replacement attempts require an actual institutional opening. Eligible openings include an already documented companion relationship around an office, a prior compromised subordinate, an unresolved archive breach or a failed security operation that created a useful vacancy. A high Chaos value alone is not enough to replace a leader in a country with no local network.

### Institutional targets

| Target | Alien advantage | Player-facing consequence | Effective response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Civil records office | Alters identity and relationship records | Contradictory documents and delayed casework | Independent archive comparison and protected staff |
| Local police authority | Warns cells of investigation | Failed operations and endangered witnesses | External investigators and compartmented operations |
| Military personnel office | Places or protects compromised officers | Readiness disruption and unreliable appointments | Protected rotation and audited appointments |
| Transport administration | Moves people and material | Missing consignments and unexpected travel patterns | Route audits and cooperative checks at both endpoints |
| Ministerial household or cabinet access | Shapes priorities and suppresses reports | Policy delay and an institutional crisis | Corroborated political investigation and continuity planning |
| Regional command | Prepares seizure of depots and communications | Stronger local revolt readiness | Secure depots, trusted communications and reserve deployment |

Each successful operation uses one opportunity and places a bounded consequence. A country cannot receive six full national penalties from one compromised clerk. National effects require access to a national institution. Local access produces local consequences.

### Misleading the army without promising unsupported control

The design needs military misdirection to feel consequential. A compromised command can reduce planning preparation, readiness or supply coordination for a defined period, or force a player choice between continuing an operation and pausing to restore reliable command. A false warning can consume a reserve commitment or temporarily expose a route.

Use supported modifiers, events and native AI strategy hooks. The design does not assume that an ordinary scripted effect can issue or cancel a battle plan. The player must retain direct army control. A narrative about incorrect orders must resolve to a concrete supported gameplay consequence rather than silently moving units through an unavailable engine command.

### Replacement and case truth

An innocent woman in a saved case does not become an alien because the evolution needs a surprise. If an alien later impersonates her, that is a new person record linked to the earlier identity and a new investigation. The old case's truth and result remain correct for the person it actually concerned. If an innocent official becomes compromised, the new case concerns coercion or institutional misconduct, not a retroactive biological identity change.

This rule preserves the player's ability to reason about evidence. It also allows frightening replacements without making all previous investigation meaningless.

### Counterplay and recovery

Protected records and personnel rotation reduce access and replacement opportunities at a real cost. Foreign corroboration can expose a compromised domestic source, provided the foreign endpoint is itself reliable. Removing a pivotal office holder can reduce institutional access substantially while leaving ordinary household cells intact.

Failed purges help the network indirectly. They damage Confidence, reduce legitimate cooperation, create vacancies and make warnings about abuse easier to dismiss. They do not create aliens from human victims. Every new alien placement still requires an existing adult cohort and an actual opportunity.

Recovering an institution removes its active sabotage effect, restores a bounded amount of Reach and leaves a temporary recovery burden. Restoring a compromised cabinet requires political continuity rather than deleting every existing minister or replacing the country's whole focus tree. Human leaders who survived can return through an appropriate political process. Dead leaders cannot be restored by a cleanup effect.

## Evolution II: The Unveiling

### The moment of revelation

At the shared deadline, exposed infiltrators absorb adult partners who remain within their reach, abandon their human appearances and become deformed alien creatures. The scene must connect intimate betrayal to the visible scale of the coming war. Protected partners, separated households and dismantled cells produce real exceptions.

Surviving cells then revolt simultaneously in the countries they have infiltrated. The map resolution uses the same frozen pre-revelation state for every participant. The order in which the engine processes countries cannot let an early revolt weaken a later host's snapshot, take its preassigned territory or inflate a neighboring brood's opening army.

The revelation report is global. Local outbreak reports describe the player's own losses, saved people, captured institutions and enemy position. Successful countries receive a meaningful confirmation that their preparation prevented or limited the uprising. They can still face invasion from abroad.

### State readiness

Territorial seizure requires an organized cell with access to authority and enough surviving adults to act. The launch candidate rule requires cell presence at least 40, institutional access at least 30 and at least 25 active adult infiltrators in the state. These are organizational thresholds, not a percentage of women in that state.

For eligible candidates, use a hidden readiness estimate:

`readiness = clamp(0.60 × cell presence + 0.40 × institutional access − 0.25 × Investigative Reach − protected-defense reduction, 0, 100)`

The protected-defense reduction is 0, 10 or 20 according to actual completed institutional and depot protection. It is not awarded just because a large army exists somewhere in the country. A state needs readiness at least 45 to be considered for immediate seizure. Use the saved value. Do not roll again when a player opens the map.

Readiness determines priority and the practical quality of the uprising. It is never shown as a precise public infestation score. Player-facing assessments explain the evidence, the condition of institutions and the presence of protection measures.

### How much of a host can fall at once

The immediate outbreak is bounded by the host's population-weighted cell presence at the frozen snapshot. The proposed cap uses the pre-revelation human population of its eligible territory.

| Population-weighted cell presence | Maximum population weight in immediately seized states |
|---|---:|
| Below 25 | 5% |
| 25 to below 50 | 20% |
| 50 to below 75 | 40% |
| 75 or higher | 60% |

Select whole eligible states in a deterministic priority order that favors a viable capital, connected positions, useful depots and the highest readiness. A state that would exceed the cap is not partially transferred through an invented province-ownership feature. Another fitting candidate can be selected. Unselected cells still resolve their exposed partners and can cause bounded local fighting, but they do not receive a free country or army in an invalid location.

For a one-state host, the special full-takeover case requires readiness at least 75, cell presence at least 75, institutional access at least 60, the normal candidate adult minimum and a viable funded opening force. These values define severe local exposure. The host also needs a verified government-in-exile or player-continuation route. The case is the sole explicit exception to the ordinary whole-state population cap. If any requirement fails, resolve the nonterritorial local insurrection below without manufacturing a second owned state. Explain the exception and its consequences during the warning period. A new permanent map state is not required.

Large hosts keep a territorial remnant from the initial allocation. Their remaining land can still be conquered in the subsequent war. A historical capital is eligible only if its own local network satisfies the readiness rules. It is never captured solely because it is valuable to the dramatic scene.

### Very small or nonterritorial revolts

A territorial package must fund at least 50 actual adult combatants, at least 20% of the 250-adult Emergency Cadre battalion, with its corresponding finite equipment issue and a verified supported understrength deployment. This floor is a viability test, not a grant. The 30%, 45% or 60% opening share still applies. If the measured budget cannot fund that minimum, the state allocation cannot fit, a capital is invalid or the one-state exception fails, do not create an empty country. The revealed surviving cohort remains at its actual location as a nonterritorial pocket. It has no native manpower pool, free formation or immediate new territorial roll.

If the installed creation path cannot express the intended understrength deployment exactly, it must fund a complete legal 250-adult Cadre from the same committed share or use the nonterritorial outcome. It cannot round 50 people up to 250. The affected candidate states stay with their human holder. Their registered adult-partner exposure still resolves under Part 2, but survivors and unabsorbed humans remain counted once.

At most one local-insurrection response mission per host covers its saved nonterritorial pockets. The ordinary emergency contract lasts 45 days, consumes 30 Command Power and 50, 100 or 150 support equipment using Part 4's capacity bands, and requires actual locally assigned security or protected civil authority throughout the final 30 days. A poor host can choose a 90-day civil-protection contract with no stockpile payment, a one-time war-support sacrifice of the smaller of five points and current war support, and a 10% local production burden in the affected states for 90 days. It requires a working accessible local authority and the same final 30 days of protected presence. Neither route creates its personnel by payment.

An unresolved active pocket imposes a 10% local production penalty and one host-level five-point stability penalty while it can still operate. The host penalty does not multiply with the number of pockets. These express the open local emergency and persist until real resolution. The temporary civil-protection or failed-response burden is a separate additional 10% local penalty with a maximum of one copy. Opening, cancelling or allowing a mission to fail never removes the baseline pocket burden.

Success places the actual surviving pocket under nonlethal isolation, removes its operational links and access, ends its active-pocket burden and retires its revolt contribution. It does not record those adult aliens as dead. Failure or cancellation after commitment loses ten Confidence once for that mission and applies one 90-day additional production penalty of 10%, replacing rather than stacking with the civil-protection burden. Preserve only work actually accomplished. Cancellation provides no automatic disruption, isolation, retreat or protected-presence credit.

After at least 45 real days of an unresolved emergency, or an actual military breakthrough, a pocket can make one retreat attempt. At most 25% of its originally revealed surviving adults, rounded down and bounded by the current survivors, may reach an existing brood along a real usable controlled route. Debit the source and credit that destination once. No repeated failure or cancellation renews that original retreat allowance. The remaining pocket retains its baseline disruption until contained. It cannot grow, seed a fresh territorial state or generate passive human deaths. A new response can begin 30 days after mission closure, or actual screening and network containment can resolve the cohort earlier. Inaccessible pockets wait for access or liberation instead of causing an impossible paid mission to launch.

The shorter 45-day contract is justified by an already revealed local emergency. Its failure creates a concrete temporary burden and a bounded enemy reinforcement opportunity, while preserving the user's promise that countries with successful prior containment face a smaller uprising or none. If every would-be revolt is nonterritorial, the global Unveiling can reveal the species without manufacturing an alien state to keep the Mother pathway alive.

### One outbreak package per host

Create at most one initial brood polity per pre-revelation host. Its selected states can include overseas possessions when those local cells independently qualify. Its country package reflects the actual land it receives. An overseas holding does not establish a land connection or naval capability by itself.

The local state remembers the pre-revelation sovereign, controller, region and protected human cohorts. Colonies, occupied land and subjects retain their own local records. Two different hosts cannot be assigned the same state. The immutable state allocation resolves disputes before any country is created.

If the state was occupied by another human power, the revolt is hostile to both the relevant host and the actual human occupier. The war setup must give the aliens the ability to contest their claimed territory while preserving existing wars among humans. It must not accidentally create an alliance between the brood and its host's wartime enemy.

### Simultaneous resolution contract

1. Freeze eligible countries, state readiness, household exposure, units and available stocks. Complete the one-time human-world census for later Mother qualification. Validate the whole-state allocation, every recipient and capital, and all continuation routes before irreversible losses.
2. Commit the shared deadline and prevent another declaration of the same invasion from original 158.
3. Resolve protection and partner absorption with measured losses while the normal human ownership/classification context still exists.
4. Apply the prevalidated whole-state allocation without overlap, then establish valid owned land and a usable capital for every new brood.
5. Apply nonhuman classification and its shared-system cleanup, while preserving surviving human cohorts in those states.
6. Transfer the explicitly allocated equipment and instantiate only the funded formations. Keep deployment receipts separate from template existence.
7. Put every brood on the same side and establish immediate war with its human host and other required hostile holders.
8. Publish the coordinated revelation and local reports, then unlock normal war processing and contact mergers.

This is one logical transaction even if the implementation uses bounded preparation jobs and ordered native operations. Failed validation cannot leave half of the map declared while the rest is still using a mutable snapshot. Recoverable interruption resumes committed work exactly once. The detailed save and transaction contract is in [Part 10](119_mistresses_spec_part_10_integration_transactions.md).

### People and starting strength

Use the measured adult budget defined in [Part 2](119_mistresses_spec_part_2_networks_population.md). Starting fielded strength is a share of that budget, not a fixed division grant per country. The country package uses 30%, 45% or 60% according to isolation, supply and completed network preparation. The remainder is a finite reserve.

Initial organic equipment is a declared one-time transformation entitlement associated with those funded units. Its readiness depends on preparation. Captured human equipment is a separate measured transfer from an existing stock or allocated depot. Equipment cannot remain in the host stockpile and also appear in the brood's deployed formations and reserves.

A well-contained country may lose some exposed partners but have no organized territorial revolt. A state with a few survivors can produce a contained local emergency. A deeply compromised major can produce a viable enemy country with captured industry, supply access and multiple formations. This range is the central reward for earlier play.

## The common brood war

All alien states enter a common faction or an equivalent validated common-war arrangement. Their AI treats fellow broods as allies. They share strategic goals against human resistance and do not conduct opportunistic wars against one another. The Mother later concentrates this existing alignment under a supreme command.

Human countries do not all receive a forced faction change when the uprising occurs. The response can begin with intelligence cooperation, military access, expeditionary support or a coalition conference. Existing rivalries create difficult choices. Tin foil hat can provide an especially forceful route to political or military coordination without creating a separate invasion.

Aliens must deal with supply, transport, terrain and industry. Sea contact does not merge countries. An island brood needs actual shipping or captured naval infrastructure. A continental brood benefits from connecting a corridor, and humanity benefits from cutting that corridor before consolidation.

## Contact mergers

When two or more broods obtain valid controlled land adjacency, the stronger absorbs the weaker. Evaluate a connected group from a frozen snapshot. Rank by effective surviving fielded land-force strength, then usable owned-and-controlled military industry, then the lower immutable brood registration ID. The unit-family weights are Cadre 1.00, Line 1.50 and Siege 3.00 per actual surviving adult-alien combatant. These are proposed comparison ratios rather than tested native combat statistics.

The strongest member receives the entire group's territory and surviving forces. Do not merge pairs in an order that changes who wins halfway through the same resolution. Existing completed mergers count as part of the current country in a later independent contact. Only gains created during the same batch are excluded from that batch's ranking.

Stocks, reserves, compatible research, characters and earned capabilities transfer under their conservation rules. Existing armies keep their actual strength, experience and equipment where supported. Mutually exclusive technologies require a compatible union and documented treatment of the incompatible branch. A union cannot create duplicate research slots by adding every donor's entitlement.

A human player controlling a donor needs a supported continuation in the recipient or a clearly presented observer/recipient transition. In multiplayer, two people controlling broods that merge require verified co-op or another supported continuation before the donor is removed. This is a release validation gate. It is not permission to silently discard a player's country.

## After an unsuccessful Unveiling

If all territorial revolts fail, surviving isolated cells remain subject to follow-up. They do not create a fresh global invasion under Tomorrow's Girls. Countries recover over time, with differences based on civilian protection, wrongful purges and the duration of occupation.

If some broods survive, the open war continues even if Chaos falls below 800. Their contact-merger rule remains active. Mother of Mothers is checked separately against sustained territorial success and the strict Chaos threshold. A rebellion alone never starts that world-end scenario.
