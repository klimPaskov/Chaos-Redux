# Part 8: Mother of Mothers and the surviving world

## A final war that can still be won

Mother of Mothers begins when the alien uprising has become a sustained threat across several parts of the world. A colossal matriarch emerges from the strongest surviving concentration of brood power. She takes supreme command of the alien war and coordinates the remaining broods toward the absorption or destruction of humanity.

Her arrival is a major presentation moment and a change in the war's strategic rules. It is not a victory screen for the aliens. Human armies remain controllable, occupied populations remain people, surviving governments retain choices, and the Mother has discoverable vulnerabilities. The human player can defeat her and break the alien order.

The ordinary uprising is insufficient. A small rebellion at Chaos 1,100 must not create the Mother. A huge alien empire at Chaos exactly 1,000 must not create her either. Both the strict Chaos gate and the sustained success test must be satisfied.

## The emergence test

Define six immutable world regions for this scenario: Europe, Asia, Africa, North America, South America and Oceania. Assign every relevant state once using the project's authoritative map registry and an explicit treatment of transcontinental states. A state contributes to exactly one region. The assignment is geographical and does not change when a country annexes it or moves its capital.

Immediately before The Unveiling, freeze the human civilian-population and industrial weights of the whole eligible human world, including countries with no infiltration. This is one bounded transition census. Subsequent ownership and control hooks maintain the current regional totals. The recurring Mother check reads those cached totals rather than scanning the world again.

| Condition | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Chaos | Strictly greater than 1,000 |
| Event state | The Unveiling has resolved and at least one living brood can host the Mother |
| World-end ownership | No other world-end scenario owns the active global scenario slot |
| Regional breadth | At least two of the six regions qualify at the same time |
| Qualifying region | Durable alien holdings total at least 25% of its frozen human-population weight and at least 25% of its frozen industrial weight, including at least one original human host capital in that region |
| Global scale | Durable alien holdings total at least 20% of frozen world human-population weight or at least 35% of frozen world industrial weight |
| Recent success | Within the preceding 60 days, at least one qualifying region gained a net 5 percentage points of durable population weight through actual post-Unveiling conquest, or another original human host genuinely capitulated to the alien war after the revelation |
| Durability | All conditions hold for 45 consecutive days. Resolve emergence every five days, with immediate cached invalidation when a relevant condition fails |

A region with zero frozen industry uses the population and capital tests. A region with zero frozen human population cannot qualify. If the whole frozen world's industry is zero, disable the industrial arm of the global-scale alternative and require the 20% population test. Industry means the frozen economic factory weight selected for the shared census, covering civilian factories, military factories and dockyards consistently. New construction, destruction and moving capitals do not rewrite the denominator.

Durable holdings include a state both owned and controlled by a brood, or a state continuously controlled by the alien side for 30 days with valid land and supply access throughout that admission period. The second route lets genuine wartime conquest count while its legal sovereign remains human. It records durable control without rewriting ownership, original-host identity, region or human restoration claims. Loss of alien control removes the weight immediately and resets its admission clock. After any such loss, recapture must complete a new 30-day admission even if legal ownership remained alien. This post-loss requirement takes priority over the owned-and-controlled shortcut. Loss of the required land or supply access before admission also resets that clock. Once admitted, supply interruption alone does not erase the territorial weight, though the Mother must independently have a viable supplied host and lair.

Count each state once across all broods. An interbrood merger preserves an admitted receipt and its history without adding weight or restarting its clock. The initial Unveiling allocation establishes the starting baseline. Those states are permanently excluded from recent-gain credit, including later recapture, and their opening takeover grants no capitulation credit. A nonbaseline state earns recent-gain credit only at its first genuine post-revelation durable admission. For the preceding 60 days, sum those qualifying credits and subtract actual durable-weight losses in the same region, then divide by the frozen regional denominator. A five-percentage-point net increase satisfies the gain alternative. Repeated releases, friendly transfers and recaptures provide no new positive credit. Current holdings must still satisfy every independent regional and global threshold.

For the capitulation alternative, each original host counts once, and only a genuine alien-war capitulation caused by subsequent fighting after the initial Unveiling transaction qualifies. A full one-state opening takeover is part of the baseline. Releasing and reconquering a puppet cannot manufacture repeated successes. A human ally's victory in an unrelated war does not count for the aliens.

Any failed eligibility condition resets the 45-day qualification streak. Ownership, control, supply-admission, Chaos and world-end ownership hooks invalidate it immediately from cached totals, including between the five-day resolution checks. A brief disqualifying loss cannot be hidden by retaking the state before the next check. At Chaos 1,000 the streak resets. At Chaos 1,001 it can progress if the other conditions hold. For example, a decisive wartime capture on day 10 can earn durable status on day 40 and complete a new 45-day emergence streak on day 85 if every other condition remains true. After the Mother has emerged, falling Chaos does not remove her or undo the survival war.

### Why the test uses frozen weights

The aliens must gain and retain meaningful territory. They cannot improve their share by killing civilians elsewhere, destroying human factories to shrink a live denominator or rearranging their own faction tags. Humanity can prevent emergence by holding capitals, cutting alien territory, restoring ownership and control, or lowering Chaos before the sustained test completes.

The exact percentages and durations are launch-tuning proposals, chosen to make the Mother a consequence of a successful international invasion. They require representative campaign evaluation. The strict threshold above 1,000 and the need for success across multiple world regions are fixed user requirements.

## Warning and scenario entry

Once the aliens meet the broad regional and global criteria, human intelligence can report evidence of unusual coordination and a developing central organism. This is a qualitative warning supported by the map. Do not expose a third public numerical meter or an exact hidden countdown to emergence.

On successful qualification, choose a host brood using the same frozen military-strength ranking used for mergers, restricted to broods with a valid supplied land capital and a viable controlled location for the Mother. If the strongest brood lacks a suitable site, select the next eligible brood. Never create the Mother in an invalid state, in the sea or inside an already liberated province.

Entry commits the shared world-end registry, records the responsible event and participating broods, suspends ordinary automatic chaos-event rolls worldwide, and publishes the Mother super-event. It preserves explicit player actions and already active crisis systems according to their owners. It does not cancel every war, delete national focus progress or reset the world to a new scenario map.

A different active world-end scenario blocks new Mother entry. The event can remain an open alien war until that conflict is resolved. It cannot overwrite another scenario's flags or claim ownership of its global suspension. A debug launch can test entry explicitly, but does not qualify for normal achievements.

## Supreme command without losing the campaign's geography

The Mother is the supreme leader of every brood. The host polity contains her actual leader character and lair. Other broods retain regional governors and local administration while accepting a common strategic command. Their existing forces and fronts remain on the map. All still fight on the same side.

The Mother does not grant sea transport, land adjacency or control of a human country that has not been conquered. Noncontiguous broods can follow the same war direction without being treated as physically connected. The existing land-contact merger rule continues. If the Mother's host is absorbed by a stronger brood, her leadership and lair identity move safely into the recipient record. The merger neither kills nor duplicates her.

Supreme command concentrates attacks on supply corridors, human industrial centers, evacuation routes and the remaining coalition leadership. The aliens coordinate strategic priorities through supported AI behavior and available military arrangements. The design must not assume that a scripted effect can draw battle plans or directly operate another country's units.

As launch tuning, supreme command upgrades an existing brood command spirit to provide 15% organization and 10% coordination-related benefit, using verified native modifiers. It also imposes an increased logistical burden appropriate to larger organisms and synchronized offensives. It replaces the relevant spirit tier and respects the maximum of three focus-owned spirits in any route. It is not an unlimited stack of one bonus per brood.

## The Mother's body and the lair

The Mother is visibly colossal and recognizably related to the transformed infiltrators. Her form should combine an immense reproductive or command organism with the remnants of many human silhouettes, protective plates and living communication structures. The image should convey a biological hierarchy that has become a geographic presence. It must not rely on sexual display.

The required strategic model anchors her to one real controlled state with a protected lair and a designated guardian formation. Her portrait, super-event image and map presence share the same approved anatomy. The map entity is a mandatory visual presentation consumer tied to the lair objective, not a fourth recruitable combat family. Its size does not by itself give a division infinite combat statistics.

Designate one existing funded Line or mixed Breach formation in the selected host, preferring a supplied formation defending the lair. If neither is available, an existing funded Cadre can serve. Preserve its actual adults, equipment, strength, experience and identity. Moving it to the lair uses ordinary military movement. Designation grants no free unit, refill or separate manpower pool. The guardian's distinct counter and visual variant identify the existing formation rather than duplicating it. If no eligible formation exists, record the guardian condition as absent and use the real territorial lair condition. A missing guardian cannot make the Mother invulnerable.

The lair has a real supply situation. Its defenders require finite equipment, adult-alien reserves and industry. Human armies can reach it through ordinary military operations. A heavily defended capital is an obstacle. An invulnerable decorative province is not a valid implementation.

## Command relays and the path to victory

At emergence, identify up to five command-relay states outside the lair. They must be real brood-controlled locations connected to meaningful military, industrial or biological infrastructure. Include a relay in each qualifying region when possible. The fixed set is saved. Relays do not teleport every time a player attacks one.

Let the actual relay count be `R`. Since at least two regions were required, a valid scenario must have at least one eligible state outside the lair. If it does not, entry is invalid and must be corrected before the cinematic. The number of relays that must be disabled to expose the Mother is `ceil(0.60 × R)`. Thus five relays require three, three require two and one requires one.

Display these as concrete objectives on the map and a checklist of identified sites. Do not create a permanent public command-strength percentage. A player's intelligence can initially know a relay only by region and later identify its exact state. The aliens know their own sites.

### Ways to disable a relay

| Method | Requirements | Result | Alien counterplay |
|---|---|---|---|
| Military liberation | Human control of the relay state held for 30 consecutive days | Relay remains disabled while under human control | Retake the state and complete a 60-day repair effort |
| Supported disruption operation | Verified target, trained response, a 90-day base-game mission and committed resources | Relay disabled for 60 days on success | Protect records or remove the operation's access before resolution. After success, the full 60-day disablement is honored |
| Destruction during a major offensive | Verified military capture of the relay infrastructure and a documented combat outcome | Same durable disablement as liberation, with a reconstruction burden | Rebuild only after restoring actual control and paying the repair cost |

Native raid features may enrich a supported DLC route. Every essential relay objective must also be achievable through base-game warfare and the dedicated operation system. No DLC may be required to make the Mother mortal.

The operation's details belong to the decision and mission handoff. Its outcome is determined by the saved access, preparation, defender response and an authored risk model. Reopening its interface cannot reroll success. The player is told when an operation fails or loses its target, without receiving hidden case truth as a consolation prize.

### Repair has a real cost and clock

A militarily disabled relay can begin one 60-day repair only after a living brood regains actual control and a valid supplied land site. Its owner consumes 25 Command Power, 250 Line equipment and 50 support equipment once. It must retain control and supply for the full 60 days. Control or supply loss fails that commitment, leaves the relay disabled and preserves already spent resources. Another paid attempt may start after a 30-day interval and renewed eligibility. A merger transfers the original paid repair and elapsed time without resetting or duplicating it.

Successful disruption operations guarantee their advertised 60-day disablement. There is no emergency action that shortens that interval. If the same relay is also militarily damaged, both conditions must end before it returns. An alien repair cannot complete while a human force controls the site. Mother defeat closes all remaining repair missions without another debit or a replacement relay. These rules give the human final mission a real window while allowing the alien side to recover objectives through territory, time and finite resources.

## Defeating the Mother

The normal decisive route requires enough relays currently disabled, human control of the lair state with a valid supply or supported expeditionary route, and the guardian formation defeated, absent or driven out of the lair's defended location. The player then sustains a 30-day final containment mission. This short duration is justified by a live military emergency. At commitment, at least the required number of relays must have 30 days of guaranteed remaining disablement or continued human control. A known expiring operation cannot charge for an already impossible attempt. Later military reversal can still make a properly admitted mission fail.

The final mission requires a commitment of 50 Command Power and a capacity-scaled support-equipment allocation, proposed at 50 for a small responder, 100 for a medium responder and 150 for a large responder. Equipment is charged once at commitment. Command Power is a consumed resource, not a retained threshold. Only one coalition-owned final mission can exist. Allied contributions belong to that same commitment and cannot pay for duplicate outcomes.

Losing lair control suspends the opportunity and fails the current mission. A relay repaired before the necessary isolation period also interrupts it. The briefing explains these conditions. The expedition's spent resources are not automatically refunded after a genuine failure. Cancellation before any irreversible deployment follows the shared decision contract's defined refund rule.

At successful completion, the Mother is killed or permanently disabled, her supreme-command benefit ends, and her world-end instance is marked defeated. The presentation acknowledges the human forces and survivors involved. There is no hidden immediate successor Mother that invalidates the victory.

### Direct military conquest remains valid

If humanity defeats every brood country and eliminates all viable lair and guardian locations, the Mother cannot survive solely because the relay checklist was unfinished. Total military destruction resolves her defeat. Likewise, an unsupported or missing guardian entity cannot make the scenario unwinnable. The design must have a verified territorial fallback tied to the same real campaign outcome.

### A limited retreat

Before sufficient relay isolation, the Mother can attempt one relocation if the lair falls. It requires a real, connected, controlled fallback state and at least one surviving eligible funded formation as escort. The surviving guardian can fill that role, or one existing host Cadre or Line formation can be assigned. It remains the same formation with its current adults and stores. It cannot simultaneously be reserved as incubation or assimilation staff. The escape has a 30-day telegraphed relocation period and temporarily removes the supreme-command advantage. The controlled route and surviving escort must persist through completion. Their loss defeats the escape. It transfers the unique Mother identity once and never spawns a fresh full-strength guardian army.

After the one relocation is consumed, another successful lair assault resolves through the normal final-mission or total-conquest route. If no viable fallback exists, escape fails. A disconnected island on another continent is not an instant teleport destination.

## Human strategic contributions

Major powers can provide the main offensives, air cover, supply and industrial replacement. Smaller countries can protect witnesses, identify a relay, supply support equipment, host refugees, maintain a port or complete a disruption operation. A country that prevented its own uprising has a valuable position in the global war, with intact institutions and fewer losses.

Human cooperation begins from actual diplomatic conditions. A political coalition can coordinate intelligence across existing factions. A military coalition can form where the native faction and war rules permit it. Existing faction members do not automatically acquire membership of two factions or abandon their prior commitments without a supported transition.

Tin foil hat can accelerate this response through a warning campaign, an intelligence conference or a military coalition. Its central benefit is organized action against the actual threat. It does not prove that every conspiracy claim was true, reveal all suspects or trigger a parallel alien invasion.

Coalition goals should reward protecting people and defeating the command structure. Contributions can be measured through liberated objectives, delivered supplies, completed protective missions and actual fighting. Do not award contribution for wrongful purges or for repeatedly exchanging the same state with an ally.

### Local evacuation agreements

A temporary arrangement can save a real civilian cohort without ending the alien war. It requires a human coordinator, the actual brood holder of the origin, a consenting human destination and a usable named route. Each governing party commits 25 Political Power to the same saved agreement. Settle both charges only after mutual acceptance and successful preflight. A refused proposal spends nothing. The human coordinator separately funds the ordinary corridor mission in Part 4. The agreement lasts 90 days, applies only to its assigned people and route, and cannot include military passage, territorial ownership or immunity for other alien cells.

After the first actual delivery, the brood receives a 90-day local reduction of up to five percentage points in the existing event-owned occupation burden at the origin, floored at zero. This replaces the corresponding policy tier within the strategic-settlement spirit or local modifier and cannot erase unrelated native resistance. It cannot earn the same benefit repeatedly from the same civilian assignment. Humanity gains the people actually delivered, with no promised release before physical transfer. An adult removed through this route cannot simultaneously remain reserved for assimilation.

Actual fighting, a change of holder or loss of access can interrupt the corridor. Undelivered people remain in their actual location, the surviving records continue, and already paid resources do not reappear. The arrangement does not stop native enemy armies through an unsupported battleplan command. The implementation must verify the corridor's legal passage and transfer adapter. If it cannot, that agreement is unavailable and military liberation remains the complete rescue route.

A general negotiated peace that leaves a sovereign armed brood contained is outside the launch design. The specified endings are genuine military defeat, nonterritorial cohort containment, an ongoing local war after the Mother's defeat, or actual alien victory. A temporary evacuation arrangement must not be presented as an implemented general armistice system.

## What the aliens do during the survival war

The broods defend relays, maintain supply corridors and concentrate force where human resistance can be broken. They can invest in industrial growth, mobile protection or large siege organisms through their existing strategic routes. The Mother improves coordination and allows a focused final offensive, but the same finite manpower and equipment ledgers still apply.

### Bounded wartime assimilation

While the Mother is alive, a brood may undertake one 90-day assimilation commitment at a supplied owned-and-controlled site. Its eligible captives are only surviving human adult civilian partners or individual adult case subjects already represented by a real human cohort record before capture. They must now be physically held there through an actual lost protection site or interrupted escape route. The captive record is a status of those existing people. It adds no population. Unknown residents, newborns, minors, already absorbed people, recorded deaths and all service cohorts are excluded. Occupation alone does not prove that every resident is an eligible adult or captive.

Reserve at most 250 eligible adults from those records, with no person committed to another transfer or operation. The action consumes 50 Command Power, 100 Political Power and 250 Line equipment, and reserves 50 existing uncommitted alien adults as noncombat staff. These are four commitments. The launch cost is fixed even if fewer than 250 eligible captives exist, and the quote shows the actual saved cohort. One brood has at most one active assimilation commitment, with at least 180 days between starts. All inputs and people have one durable receipt.

The site must retain alien control and supply for 90 days. Human recapture, successful evacuation of the reserved people, loss of supply or the Mother's defeat interrupts the operation before the terminal absorption. Living people remain in their actual current human or captive cohort, with no automatic mass death. Actual surviving alien staff return once. Spent inputs are not refunded as a fresh stockpile. A failed operation cannot be retried immediately against the same people because the same 180-day start interval remains in force.

At successful completion, the shared human-cohort adapter applies the actual eligible adult civilian loss under nonhuman ownership and reports measured deaths once. Never temporarily clear the country's nonhuman marker to use a helper that rejects that scope. Convert only `floor(0.50 × measured absorbed adults)` into a pending adult-alien maturation record. The output shares the monthly ceiling from Part 6 with industrial incubation. It is not granted immediately to manpower and then released again. If 250 eligible adults are actually lost, the maximum output is 125. If the measured loss is 180, output is 90. Unperformed requested losses yield nothing.

Mergers preserve already paid commitments and their physical sites, even if two arrive in the same recipient. No new assimilation starts while any is active, and the latest inherited start date controls the 180-day interval. Their completions share one monthly release allowance. Mother defeat prevents new starts and cancels unfinished absorption, while already measured historical losses and legitimately pending adults remain accounted. This policy has a finite original cohort, a costly cadence and direct rescue counterplay. It is not a passive reward for every capture or a currency paid by ordinary battlefield deaths.

The aliens can destroy refugee routes or force difficult defensive decisions within the fictional war. The player's practical response is military protection, evacuation and counterattack. The design does not require detailed real-world methods of abuse. Reports should give the human consequences and military stakes clearly.

## The Mother's defeat and continuing local war

Defeating the Mother immediately removes the supreme-command bonus and imposes a temporary 60-day command shock on the broods. Their common alignment remains. They do not all vanish or become friendly to their hosts. Local governors can still command surviving armies and defend occupied land.

The global world-end scenario enters a 30-day consolidation phase. During this phase, the game verifies the unique Mother's defeat, clears only her scenario-owned objectives and operations, settles the final mission, and restores the event's ordinary open-war processing. It then releases its own world-end ownership and automatic-event suspension if no other owner is entitled to retain them. The player's global settings remain respected.

Surviving broods continue to register a world threat through the shared threat aggregator. Their war can be finished militarily or through verified isolation and containment. The Mother never reappears in that campaign. A later unrelated world-end scenario remains the responsibility of its own system.

## Human defeat and observer continuation

A single fallen capital, an alien annexation or the death of one human faction leader is insufficient to declare total human defeat. Remaining human countries, governments in exile with supported continuation, military forces and viable refuge territory must be considered.

If no viable human resistance remains, the alien victory can resolve as a genuine end-state. A human player whose own country falls earlier receives the supported native defeat or continuation choices. A brood player can continue the conquest. An observer can inspect the outcome. The global survival war must remain playable until its actual resolution conditions are met.

## Recovery after the crisis

| Prior outcome | Recovery emphasis | Persistent consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet containment | Restore normal casework and retain useful civic institutions | Modest vigilance benefit and preserved population |
| Small local revolt | Repair damaged offices and support bereaved households | Temporary readiness and reconstruction burden |
| Long occupation | Liberate and reconcile surviving human cohorts, rebuild services, return displaced people | Substantial infrastructure and social recovery |
| Many wrongful purges | Investigate misconduct, compensate families and restore trustworthy institutions | Long-lived Confidence damage until meaningful redress |
| Strong international cooperation | Maintain compatible records and emergency planning | Shared resilience without permanent universal surveillance |
| Mother defeated | Recognize victory, repair command structures and finish local wars | The supreme alien order is permanently broken |

Recovery missions usually last 90 to 180 days and offer practical choices. Compensation spends political and material resources. Resettlement requires a real origin, destination and capacity. Reopening a military office takes personnel and time. Public trust does not jump back to 100 because an event was marked resolved.

Measured refugee transfer records origin debit, actual destination addition, deaths if any and people still in transit exactly once. Return journeys use the same individuals. Liberation restores eligibility for normal civilian systems without adding the surviving population a second time.

The final archive should let the player understand the campaign: people protected, institutions recovered, documented wrongful purges, territory liberated and the Mother's defeat. These are historical results, not new live progress meters. The event leaves a world that reflects how the player survived it.
