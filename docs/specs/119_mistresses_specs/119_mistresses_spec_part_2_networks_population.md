# Part 2: Households, covert networks and population

## The ordinary crisis is already international

The infiltrators begin as individuals who appear unusually good at meeting the emotional needs of people around them. Some form lasting partnerships. Some move through several social circles without attracting much attention. A smaller number deliberately enter military headquarters, ministries, transport offices, police records departments and influential households.

These women are not isolated random encounters. A local cell can hide a member, find her a new introduction, discredit a witness or arrange travel to another country. A well-connected cell can protect several weaker ones. At the same time, human friendships, ordinary mistakes, migration and political patronage can produce similar patterns. The government must distinguish an alien protection network from the much larger world of legitimate human relationships.

This entire system runs before Replacement Wave. The evolution expands the network's ability to replace and compromise authority. It does not unlock basic coordination.

## The hidden model and what the player can observe

| Hidden element | What it represents | Observable evidence | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adult alien cohort | Finite living infiltrators resident in a state | Individual cases and corroborated links, never a precise population count | Bounds future transformed combatants and household exposure |
| Cell presence, 0 to 100 | Local ability to shelter, recruit contacts and keep activity going | Repeated obstruction, related disappearances, recovered safe locations | Determines persistence, spread and territorial readiness |
| Institutional access, 0 to 100 | Influence over specific offices, commands and records | Reassigned officers, conflicting orders, blocked records, elite connections | Enables sabotage and prepares state seizure |
| Attachment cohorts | Registered adult civilian and service partners exposed to a living infiltrator | Leave requests, family records and investigated relationships | Bounds the people endangered by absorption |
| Network links | Actual routes or relationships between existing cells | Travel records, shared intermediaries and corroborated case edges | Allows assistance, flight and foreign placement |
| Protected or isolated cohort | People removed from the network's reach without being killed | Successful protection or containment operation | Reduces later losses and available revolt strength |

Cell presence and institutional access are measures of organization. A score of 60 does not mean that 60% of women or 60% of the population are aliens. Case probabilities likewise describe the selected referral channel, not national prevalence.

Every state has one authoritative local record. Every person represented by a case has an immutable campaign person ID. A case can reveal links to that record, but creating a second case about the same person never creates a second infiltrator. Captured archives retain their original state and country provenance through occupation, independence, merger and liberation.

## Arrival, growth and limits

The initial seed is deliberately small relative to the human population. The proposed opening adult cohort in a seeded state is the smaller of 5,000 people and 0.05% of its eligible human civilian population, rounded down to whole adults. Tiny states receive the resulting small cohort. There is no minimum grant that creates more infiltrators than the location can support.

Opening cell presence is 20, 30 or 40 according to the saved opportunity profile. Institutional access begins at 10 or 20 and cannot exceed 40 during the ordinary crisis. Useful opportunity factors are transport access, institutions that the cell has actually entered, active war disruption and the ability to maintain real links. The player's national identity or the portrait subject's demographic identity never changes guilt.

A cell's monthly review chooses a limited priority. It can deepen local protection, seek one institutional placement, repair one disrupted link, move endangered members or attempt one foreign introduction. It cannot receive the full benefit of every activity in the same review. As launch tuning, an unobstructed local success adds 5 cell-presence points or 5 access points. An ordinary failure adds nothing. Exposed activity can create evidence for the response.

New adult arrivals are bounded by the smaller of 10% of the existing active cohort and 0.005% of the state's eligible population per month. The total resident cohort cannot exceed 0.25% of the local eligible human population through ordinary infiltration. Growth requires an active cell, a viable arrival or recruitment route and an unresolved opportunity. A state reduced to zero active cells does not regenerate from a timer alone. It needs a surviving foreign or neighboring source that pays its own finite placement cost.

At initial manifestation, save one off-map adult arrival reserve equal to the total actually seeded adult alien cohort. This is a finite additional source, not human population or recruitable manpower. A monthly arrival debits that reserve by the actual adults admitted and credits one destination cohort. The reserve never regenerates, is never copied into a successor tag and closes to further arrivals at The Unveiling. Its unused adults do not appear later as a free army. Moving an already resident alien between states debits the original resident cohort instead and does not spend or replenish the off-map reserve.

To seed a previously empty state, an existing active source commits five actual resident adult aliens and its one monthly priority to a valid route. The destination receives those same five only after successful arrival, starts at presence 20 and access 10, and gains no extra native human population. Failure preserves the actual people at their recorded origin, transit or captured location. It never quietly replenishes the source. A source with fewer than five available adults cannot make this placement. These rules make new introductions, ordinary cohort growth and emergency relocation distinct conserved movements.

These are launch-tuning limits, not an instruction to add millions of invisible characters. The finite cohort is an aggregate. Only relevant case subjects, coordinators and plot-critical figures become individual records. Household benefits include ordinary human relationships as well as alien-linked ones, so the visible social improvement is larger than the small alien cohort.

### Monthly activity priorities

| Situation | Likely network priority | Counterplay | Opportunity cost for the network |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet state with weak investigations | Build local protection | Community records and corroborated casework | Less institutional penetration that month |
| Military or political access opportunity | Place a companion around a useful office | Protected command and independent record checks | Exposes a connection and consumes a finite placement opportunity |
| Identified coordinator under pressure | Hide, relocate or sever links | Protected witnesses and coordinated border evidence | Gives up access or local strength |
| Neighboring country offers a viable route | Create one foreign link | Reciprocal investigations and transport audits | Splits attention and risks both endpoint cells |
| Many wrongful purges damage cooperation | Exploit the evidence gap | Compensation and trusted investigators | Gains concealment rather than free additional people |
| Good investigation dismantles the local cell | Survive as isolated individuals or leave | Sustained containment and follow-up | Loses institutional operations and territorial readiness |

The selection is saved before it resolves. Reopening a case, changing a tab or reloading the same save cannot cause a different network activity to be rolled for an already committed review.

## Cross-border links

A link joins two actual cells. Its route can use a land border, an operating port connection, a diplomatic channel or a specific influential intermediary. A link records what it can carry. Personal protection is not automatically a military supply route, and a prewar social connection never grants an army the ability to cross a sea.

Each state can maintain at most five active external cell links at launch. A route closed by war, port loss or corroborated investigation becomes dormant or broken. It does not continue to deliver growth because a flag was set months ago. A link can help only one selected priority in a monthly review. Linking ten countries is useful because it creates alternatives and influence, not because every link multiplies every other bonus.

The network can move a finite number of endangered adults between endpoints. The origin loses those residents and their local contribution. The destination gains the same people after the travel outcome is known. Partners do not automatically travel with them. Travel casualties, captured members and isolated survivors each have a distinct outcome. An arrival is never duplicated because two countries reported it.

Human governments can share evidence about a route without granting each other perfect knowledge of every citizen. A partner with compromised institutions can withhold or falsify a report. Independent corroboration reduces this risk. Breaking one international coordinator can expose useful links in several countries, but each recipient still has to act on its own evidence and jurisdiction.

## What accurate screening accomplishes

Removing a confirmed ordinary operator disables that person and their actual incident links, and reduces local cell presence by 5. Removing a confirmed pivotal coordinator reduces it by 10. Institutional access falls by 5 only if that person genuinely supplied an institutional connection, or by 10 for a pivotal institutional coordinator. Values cannot fall below zero. A later case about the same removed person grants no second disruption.

These effects represent loss of access and organizational trust. They do not mean that killing one suspect kills an abstract percentage of the state. A larger operation requires a separate, evidenced target such as a compromised office, a meeting location or a documented travel route.

Successful macro containment can isolate a finite identified cohort and prevent it from contributing to revolt. A verified fully disconnected cell can be operationally isolated by a macro-containment receipt even though every resident infiltrator has not been individually screened. The receipt prevents that cohort from supplying territorial seizure or other cells until an actual escape or rebuilt link is resolved. Where the operation is nonlethal, those people remain living captives or isolated aliens. Where combat or a lethal action occurs, the actual losses are accounted for once. The event must never convert a generic success tooltip into an unbounded civilian death total.

An isolated individual can still be dangerous, but cannot seize a province through personal suspicion alone. A country can reduce territorial danger before it identifies every remaining infiltrator. That distinction makes successful counterplay attainable with finite casework and a portrait pool of practical size.

## Public Confidence and Investigative Reach in the simulation

Confidence changes the response's access to voluntary testimony, protected witnesses and useful records. High Confidence improves the quality and continuity of legitimate referrals. Low Confidence produces silence, hostile crowds, false accusations and flight. None of these changes the biological truth of a case already created.

Reach determines how much casework and institutional protection the country can sustain. It improves the response's capacity to join independent evidence and maintain coverage. It does not turn every investigation into a perfect laboratory test. A country can have high Reach and poor Confidence, producing a large security apparatus with unreliable cooperation. It can have high Confidence and low Reach, producing willing witnesses and a severe backlog.

As launch tuning, Confidence below 35 gives surviving cells a 5-point monthly concealment advantage when an actual response failure occurs. Confidence at least 75 enables better voluntary cooperation in the screening model. Reach at least 50 supports a larger active case queue and more simultaneous institutional work. Concrete thresholds and costs are developed in [Parts 3](119_mistresses_spec_part_3_screening.md) and [4](119_mistresses_spec_part_4_decisions_missions.md).

Do not award network strength merely because a public meter changed. The change modifies an eligible action or a real opportunity. There must still be a surviving cell, an available target and a bounded monthly result.

## Relationships and demographic benefit

The relationship wave includes genuine human couples. Its benefits should be visible in the country's social modifier and later population reports without exposing which relationships involve aliens.

Maintain a finite cohort of newly established households that are eligible for the event's additional births. This cohort includes human households and alien-linked households. Its size is bounded by 1% of the state's current human population. It is a broad demographic abstraction, not a claim about every individual's fertility or wishes.

Extra births begin after a 270-day lead-in for the relevant cohort. Every subsequent 90 days, the proposed additional birth count is the smaller of 5% of those eligible households and 0.05% of the state's human population. The request is then constrained by actual surviving households and local conditions. War displacement, deprivation and lost relationships can reduce the increment. Births already recorded are never retroactively removed by a later purge or revelation.

For example, a state with 1,000,000 people and 10,000 eligible new households can request 500 additional births after its lead-in. The measured civilian addition is the result. If the helper applies only 495 because of engine rounding, 495 is the birth ledger value. The country receives no event-granted immediate recruitable manpower from that transaction.

Use the shared positive population helper, `apply_state_population_gain_without_recruitable_manpower_gain`, whose supplied contract and inspected implementation measure the addition and remove immediate owner/controller recruitable credit. This does not create a complete age model for Hearts of Iron IV. Event newborns are never counted as alien recruitment, absorbed adult partners or trained military replacements. Ordinary long-term engine population abstraction remains outside this event's promise.

The early modifier can be reduced or replaced when the response changes policy. Supporting families and maintaining confidence can preserve some of the social benefit after infiltration is controlled. Destroying the network should not require the country to destroy its successful maternity services or ordinary human marriages.

## Military attachment and discipline

The broad military effect is reluctance to enlist, increased pressure for leave and reduced enthusiasm for prolonged war. Represent it through a bounded relationship modifier and specific, temporary incidents. Do not invisibly delete divisions during the ordinary crisis.

Family support reduces the recruitment and war-support penalties at a real economic cost. Protected rotation reduces the chance that a compromised relationship disrupts a whole command. Neglect can produce a localized incident, such as delayed readiness or a temporary organization penalty affecting a defined formation group. The player must be told which consequence applies and for how long.

Replacement Wave can damage communications, planning preparation or deployment efficiency through supported modifiers and explicit decisions. Ordinary scripted effects must not be described as directly issuing, cancelling or drawing native battle plans. If a dramatic incident implies misdirection, its actual playable consequence must be a supported readiness, logistics or command penalty.

## Partners, absorption and survivors

Every active alien household contributes at most one currently exposed adult partner to the absorption pool. The launch proposal attaches 75% of the resident cohort, with up to 20% of those partners assigned to a service-partner ledger when an actual military population exists. Other attachments remain civilian. Protection, separation, evacuation, successful investigation and the infiltrator's death reduce those pools before revelation.

At The Unveiling, an exposed living infiltrator absorbs the registered adult partner who remains within reach. The scene represents the destruction of that partner's independent life. The loss is recorded as an actual human death. Protected, separated or escaped partners survive. An imprisoned infiltrator cannot reach a partner in a protected human location simply because the two once shared a relationship.

Civilian losses use measured state population transactions before the state is transferred to or controlled by a nonhuman brood. The shared exact-loss helper refuses a state whose owner or controller fails the normal civilian-system classifier. If another nonhuman actor already holds the state, use an explicit shared adapter for the surviving human cohort. Do not temporarily misclassify that country as human to force the call through.

Service partners must be drawn from an existing service cohort and removed through a verified military-casualty transaction. They cannot be removed once from fielded manpower and again from civilian population. If the engine cannot support the intended exact fielded loss, implementation must define and validate an equivalent service-reserve accounting adapter before release. An unsupported command must not silently convert every military partner into a civilian loss.

Children, dependants, unrelated household members and human partners already protected are excluded. Remaining human civilians stay on the state ledger after alien conquest. Human ownership, civilian-system eligibility and the physical existence of human people are different facts.

### One-state accounting example

A state has 1,000 surviving adult infiltrators, 600 exposed civilian partners and 150 exposed service partners. Earlier containment protects 100 civilians and 50 service partners. The requested absorption is therefore 500 civilian and 100 service partners. Suppose the verified transactions measure 500 and 95 respectively. Actual human losses are 595, not 600 and not 1,000.

The proposed alien recruit budget is surviving adult infiltrators plus half of measured absorbed adults, rounded down. In this example it is 1,000 + floor(595 / 2) = 1,297 adult-alien combatants. The country package partitions that one budget into fielded formations and reserves. The 150 protected partners remain human survivors. The other 298 absorbed adults do not become an extra free recruitment pool. They are accounted for within the transformation's noncombat biological mass.

The conversion ratio is a balance proposal. It is not a biological claim or a reason to count children. The country and unit design can vary formation size and deployment share without changing this conservation rule.

### Evacuation capacity and real survivors

Part 4's corridor mission quotes one saved civilian assignment from at most five actual origin states. An ordinary supplied land corridor can move at most `500 × F` people per day during its 30 maintained service days, where F is that mission's frozen service-footprint multiplier. The sea alternative with ten committed convoys can move at most `250 × F` per day. The walking alternative moves at most 50 people per day through accessible neighboring controlled states. Round each daily quantity down to whole people. These are launch service-throughput proposals, not claims about historical transport capacity or native convoy mechanics.

The actual daily transfer is the smallest of that route allowance, the still-unmoved assigned people, the origin's measured eligible survivors and the receiving state's remaining reception capacity. One country or coalition cannot spend the same route allowance twice by running overlapping assignments. Reserve shared ports, convoy commitments and service crews by their real route record. A route interruption stops transfers until access is restored within the mission's deadline. It never creates an automatic death toll for people who have not moved.

An initial receiving-state capacity is 5% of its actual human civilian population at assignment admission, less all existing unsettled event refugee reception load, rounded down. Snapshot that capacity so a just-arrived cohort does not manufacture extra capacity during the same admission. Actual delivered people occupy it once. A 180-day integration obligation with continuing human authority and a functioning civil service releases that reception load when complete, while the people remain physically present. Moving them again is a new measured transfer of the same cohort. Integration is never a second population gain.

Protected partners, ordinary adult civilians, children and dependants can be evacuated, but retain their different cohort attributes. Children remain excluded from screening, absorption and military conversion. A registered adult who is later recaptured remains the same human person and can become eligible for Part 8's narrow wartime assimilation only under its explicit custody rules. Rescue preempts a conflicting absorption reservation through the one population owner.

An occupied origin requires actual human recovery of the route or a supported local evacuation agreement with its real holder. A map click cannot remove people through an enemy front. The agreement in Part 8 permits the named civilian transfer without ending the general war, granting military access to an entire country or disclosing every local cell.

## Spread, elimination and long-term resolution

An unaffected country can acquire a cell only from a real surviving source through a viable route. It receives new evidence opportunities and a fair response period. A liberated state inherits its surviving local records and protection outcomes. An annexed country does not wipe its cells, but its successor does not receive duplicate opening cohorts either.

Local covert containment succeeds when cell presence is zero, institutional access is zero, no usable external link remains and the surviving local alien cohort is removed or covered by an actual macro-isolation receipt. There is no numerical residual shortcut that makes uncontained survivors disappear from the system. Isolated people remain alive and recorded. After public revelation, Part 5's active-pocket burdens persist until that real resolution, even for a very small cohort. Sustained follow-up can resolve residual individuals without keeping a penalty after the operational threat has actually ended.

Global ordinary-crisis resolution requires no active cell capable of rebuilding, no viable arrival route from a surviving cell and no unprocessed committed placement. A 90-day monitored confirmation closes the event's covert phase. It stops new event-owned births after already accrued eligible requests are settled, removes temporary military penalties and begins recovery. It does not roll the same Fire-Once event again.

After an open revolt, isolated remnants cannot restart a second worldwide Unveiling. They can create bounded local incidents and require follow-up. Mother qualification and territorial war use the actual surviving brood states. The shared record of the first invasion remains consumed for both Mistresses and Tomorrow's Girls.

## Design checks

The network should be dangerous because it has protected people, offices and routes. It should not grow because the game forgot to stop a timer. Every new arrival needs a source. Every removed person has one identity. Every protected partner has a real exclusion from absorption. Every population change reports the measured result. Every new brood formation draws from a finite adult-alien budget.

The resulting play is understandable even without seeing the hidden arithmetic. Protecting witnesses improves evidence. Breaking a coordinator weakens connected cells. Supporting military families preserves readiness. Evacuating partners saves lives and denies future enemy strength. Ignoring a compromised office gives the aliens a better chance of taking that district when the open war begins.
