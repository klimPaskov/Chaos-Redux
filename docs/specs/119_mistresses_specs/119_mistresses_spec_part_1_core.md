# Mistresses

## A welcome change that becomes a war

Mistresses begins with people finding the companionship they thought the world had denied them. Soldiers ask for leave. Ministers acquire unusually devoted confidantes. Ordinary households become happier, marriages increase, and maternity wards report a sustained rise in births. The first public response should be relief, curiosity and argument about military duty. No country begins with proof, or even official knowledge, that the newcomers include aliens.

The player gradually discovers a second pattern. Unrelated investigations lose the same documents. A witness receives help from a woman in another country. An officer repeatedly moves reliable staff away from a particular district. Women who appear to have no connection protect one another with remarkable coordination. The crisis becomes a problem of evidence, institutions and trust before it becomes a problem of armies.

The central choice is how much a government is willing to damage the society it is trying to protect. Careful investigation consumes scarce capacity and can miss dangerous people. Indiscriminate purges remove some infiltrators while also killing innocents, destroying cooperation and driving the surviving network deeper into public life. Accepting the early benefits without building a response leaves the country more exposed when the disguise finally falls away.

The open war must remember those choices. A country that protected witnesses and dismantled influential cells faces fewer captured districts and weaker enemy formations. A government that ignored the problem can lose important institutions from inside. A government that conducted wrongful purges can enter the war with a smaller exposed network and a much less willing population. These are different strategic positions, not variations of a single punishment.

## What makes the event distinctive

The event has a real demographic and social upside. An improvement in family life is not retroactively erased because some relationships were engineered by infiltrators. Human marriages remain human marriages. Children survive as people who need protection. The government can preserve useful social institutions while removing a covert network.

Appearance invites mistakes without deciding guilt. Many aliens seem unusually attractive or perfectly suited to their partners, but ordinary women can be aliens and beautiful women can be innocent. A polished biography, a reassuring photograph or an implausibly agreeable personality can raise a question. None can settle it. The strongest usable clues involve corroborated conduct, records, access and relationships between cases. Even these can be forged or misread.

The network exists from the beginning. International protection, placement in influential circles and obstruction belong to the ordinary crisis. Replacement Wave adds the ability to capture institutions at scale. It does not introduce the network for the first time.

The late war grows out of the map that the players helped create. Revolts are simultaneous, their strength comes from accumulated influence, and adjoining alien countries unite through conquest and contact. Mother of Mothers requires demonstrable alien success. Her appearance opens another playable struggle rather than displaying an immediate defeat screen.

## Opening eligibility and reach

The event becomes eligible at Chaos 200 if it has never fired, ordinary event selection is permitted, the event is enabled, at least one eligible human country has populated land, and no active world-end scenario has suspended ordinary automatic events. It uses the shared scheduler and its existing timing rules. This event does not introduce a second timer or accelerate every other crisis merely by remaining active.

The initial distribution is a saved, one-time allocation. As launch tuning, seed actual alien cells in 75% of eligible human countries, with at least one seeded country in each world region that has eligible candidates. Countries without an initial alien cell can still experience the wider relationship boom and receive ordinary human cases. This prevents the opening notification from proving that a specific country contains an alien.

Within a seeded country, prioritize populated civilian centers, military communities, political centers and useful transport connections. Use the country's own eligible states and a bounded allocation. A larger state receives a larger finite cohort. A large empire does not receive the same number of women as a one-state microstate. Existing nonhuman countries, unpopulated locations and inaccessible invalid map locations do not receive normal civilian relationship benefits.

No ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sexuality, class origin or appearance category raises the probability of alien identity. Geographic variety is for plausible portraits, biographies and international play. Operational opportunity and state conditions determine where networks invest.

New human countries created during the event register through a country lifecycle hook. They inherit the actual local cells, households, evidence and policy consequences in their territory. They do not receive a fresh worldwide seed roll. Occupation and colonial ownership retain separate local and sovereign records so that a capital's successful investigation does not magically clear every overseas state.

## Early public effects

Initial public presentation explains increased relationships, social confidence, births, leave requests and reluctance to fight. It can mention contradictory reports and unusual coincidences. It cannot name alien infiltrators, identify a hidden brood or show a transformation image before discovery permits that knowledge.

The relationship effect is a replaceable event modifier with benefits and costs that scale with the documented local relationship wave. Launch tuning uses three tiers with stability gains of 5, 10 or 15 percentage points, recruitable-population-factor penalties of 5%, 10% or 15%, and war-support penalties of 5, 10 or 15 percentage points. These are an explicit design target. Implementation must verify the precise modifier fields and avoid stacking multiple copies of the same tier.

A country at peace may rationally accept the early tradeoff. A country already fighting an existential war is much more likely to pay for family support, staffed investigations and command protection. The player can reduce military disruption without outlawing relationships. Baseline births are handled as measured civilian population gains and never as immediate trained replacements.

The full state, cohort and population model is in [Part 2](119_mistresses_spec_part_2_networks_population.md). Screening and the uncertainty model are in [Part 3](119_mistresses_spec_part_3_screening.md).

## Two public values

| Working label | Meaning | Opening value | What changes it |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Public Confidence | Willingness to cooperate with the event response and believe its institutions | 65 | Verified successes, protection, wrongful purges, exposed obstruction, compensation and transparent recovery |
| Investigative Reach | Staffed capacity to connect records, protect evidence and sustain casework | 10 | Resource commitments, training, foreign cooperation, institution capture, war disruption and recovered archives |

Both values are bounded from 0 to 100. They are the only persistent public numbers introduced by this event. They have distinct icons and concise explanations wherever they appear. Existing national stability, war support, manpower and equipment remain visible through normal game interfaces. Individual case observations, map ownership and finite endgame objectives can be shown without inventing additional permanent meters.

Actual infiltration, institutional access, linked cells, readiness to seize a state, survivor cohorts and Mother qualification are hidden simulation data. The player receives evidence-based qualitative assessments rather than a perfectly accurate national infestation percentage. A low public meter never proves that a particular woman is an alien.

## Player routes

**Civil protection and corroboration** invests in witnesses, records and compensation. It creates slower early results but better cooperation and a resilient response. Its difficulty is maintaining military readiness while the network continues to grow.

**Protected command and targeted security** concentrates scarce capacity on officers, ministers, communications and transport hubs. It can preserve a defensible state through Replacement Wave while leaving ordinary households exposed. It is particularly useful during an existing war.

**Coercive suppression** offers immediate removal and temporary disruption. It is available as a consequential government choice, with severe costs when innocents are killed. Its apparent speed must be offset by false positives, hidden relocation, resistance to evidence collection and a weaker social base during the eventual war. It cannot be the statistically dominant universal solution.

**Accommodation and denial** preserves early benefits and postpones response costs. It is viable for a limited period or a country with little actual infiltration. Continued denial in the face of corroborated evidence allows institutional capture and stronger revolt. The route is a risky strategic commitment, not a menu option that secretly ends the event successfully.

**International containment** shares useful evidence and protects exposed countries. It creates strong counterplay against networks that cross borders. It also creates risks from compromised partners and requires practical contributions rather than cost-free universal intelligence.

Host routes, brood strategic paths and AI behavior are developed in [Part 7](119_mistresses_spec_part_7_focus_routes_ai.md). Governments can change policy after failure. A change requires a believable transition, costs and recovery time rather than an instant removal of every consequence.

## Information, tone and human agency

The early tone is intimate unease. Bureaucratic mistakes, military leave disputes and contradictory personal testimony create the tension. The later tone is biological horror and military desperation. The transformation can be grotesque, but it must retain visual continuity with the infiltrators' coordinated behavior and recurring network imagery.

Human women are central witnesses, investigators, officials, soldiers and survivors. Innocent suspects have plausible lives and reasons for inconsistent records. The event's antagonist is a fictional alien network. The game must not teach the player that real-world demographic traits, independence, attractiveness or unfamiliarity are evidence of guilt.

Every depicted partner and every screening subject is an adult. Children enter the design through family protection, births, evacuation and aftermath. They are never screening targets, transformation spectacle, a military recruitment input or an enemy unit.

The player's agency comes from priorities, evidence, policy and military preparation. Some uncertainty remains until open revolt. The game explains observable causes and consequences, including why an action is unavailable, while retaining the hidden truth of unproven cases. Archive review and postwar accountability make past decisions understandable without granting advance omniscience.

## Campaign outcomes worth supporting

A successful country can contain its cells before any territorial uprising. A partially successful country can hold most of its territory and defeat a small revolt. A poorly prepared country can survive through retreat, allies and liberation. An occupied human country can recover its government and population records. A player can continue as a brood through an explicit supported transition. A victorious brood can help create the Mother and still face a difficult global war.

Humanity can defeat Mother of Mothers. Killing or disabling her breaks supreme coordination and her special advantages. Local alien countries do not disappear without a military or containment resolution. The player's achievement is therefore meaningful while the remaining war remains coherent.

The event must also support an honest human defeat. If all playable human resistance is destroyed, the game can offer the appropriate defeat outcome or observer continuation. A threshold check, a single rebellion, a lost capital or a cinematic is never sufficient to declare that the entire world has ended.
