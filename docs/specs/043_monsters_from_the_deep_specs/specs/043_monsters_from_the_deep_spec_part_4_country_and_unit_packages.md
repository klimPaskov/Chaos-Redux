# Event 043: Monsters from the Deep

## Part 4: Monster countries, apex units, and support packages

## Country-package standard

Every full monster identity requires a complete country package before it can enter the natural or manual roster.

A package is ready only when it has:

- one collision-audited three-character tag
- country definition and dormant history shell
- map colour
- normal, medium, and small flags
- country name and adjective
- one unique nonhuman leader
- static and animated leader portrait package
- one unique focus tree
- route-specific focus AI
- one unique apex subunit and division template
- one unique 3D model and action set
- one bespoke counter package
- one sourced sound package
- three primary support-family permissions
- starting ideas with mapped lifecycles
- one event-owned decision category profile
- one lair and landing-state pool
- one surrender and remnant route
- pact preferences
- Cthulhu transfer proof
- CXT test-country setup
- event, focus, decision, and country localisation
- asset and runtime documentation

A missing visible package blocks that identity from the registered roster. The opening selector cannot silently replace a missing apex model with a generic seamonster model.

## Stable tag plan

The event needs seventeen dedicated stable tags:

- sixteen full monster tags, one for each named apex
- one terminal Cthulhu tag

Abyssal Remnants keep the defeated creature's tag. They replace its full-monster marker, leader, tree, and active package. They do not need a second parallel tag family.

Exact tag tokens remain open until implementation runs the required collision audit against:

- installed vanilla country tags
- every Chaos Redux tag
- installed Workshop mods
- sibling local mods
- protected dormant carrier registries
- reserved save-facing identities

The tags should be added through one Event 043 country-tag file. Their common country shell can be shared where engine behavior allows, while identity setup remains creature-specific.

## Starting politics and administration

All full monster countries use a fixed nonhuman ruling identity. They have no elections and no ordinary ideology contest.

The visible political package should be minimal:

- the named creature is leader
- the ruling party name reflects the creature or brood order
- the country uses one event-owned nonhuman ideology presentation or a safe existing neutral carrier presentation
- ordinary advisor and law interfaces are hidden or inert where possible
- political power is not the main event currency
- captured human administration is represented through state modifiers and occupation pressure, not a human cabinet

A shared starting idea can mark **Apex Sovereignty**. A second creature-specific idea expresses the named apex. A third temporary landing idea can cover the first survival window. The trees should upgrade or remove the landing idea after consolidation.

## Starting force standard

Baseline natural emergence gives:

- one apex division
- two to four support divisions
- one support receipt after the first named objective
- no ordinary manpower
- no ordinary equipment stockpile
- no conventional air force
- no conventional navy
- no normal reinforcement production

Evolution I, Evolution II, and manual intensity modify support count, experience, and starting route access. They never create a second apex.

## Roster profiles

## 1. Titanus Kraken

### Cultural and geographic anchor

Scandinavian and Norwegian maritime tradition. The preferred emergence is **Norwegian coast and Norwegian Sea pool**.

### Play identity

Choose between a chain of harbor-lairs, a fleet-killing strangulation route, or solitary dominion over the northern seas.

The creature's apex role is **Crushing tentacle siege, port seizure, convoy strangulation, and coastal fortress breaking**. Its primary support mixture is **Abyssal Raiders, Reefbreakers, Trench Stalkers**.

### Inland profile

Lowest ordinary inland ceiling. It gains one extra depth only through a late landward route.

### Pact and rivalry profile

Strong affinity with Hafgufa, guarded affinity with Jormungandr, rivalry with Scylla.

### Counterplay

Loss of ports and deep inland pursuit sharply reduce Sea Bond.

### AI role

The primary route AI profile is **Siege controller**. Its late campaign payoff is **A northern port network that lets spawned broods move quickly between controlled coastal hubs**.

### Visual identity

Immense cephalopod mass rising through broken quays, cold slate water, pale suction scars, and heavy fog.

Animated portrait direction: Slow mantle breathing, eye movement, water running from limbs, and restrained fog drift.


Its opening war should usually place pressure on Norway, a Nordic successor, or another state controlling the selected coast. Kraken values naval bases and ports above populated inland states. A captured major port can become a tentacle anchorage that grants defensive power and one movement link to another lair.

Kraken's apex should have high breakthrough and fort pressure near the sea. It should have lower strategic speed than Scylla or Ipupiara. Its signature power temporarily damages a selected hostile port and improves the apex's assault against that state, with a clear cooldown and no global building sweep.

Its solitary route should aim to dominate the Norwegian Sea and North Atlantic approaches. Its pact route can coordinate a northern blockade with Hafgufa. Jormungandr remains a useful partner and a dangerous terminal rival.



## 2. Titanus Scylla

### Cultural and geographic anchor

Greek tradition centered on the Strait of Messina. The preferred emergence is **Sicily or Calabria near the Strait of Messina**.

### Play identity

Choose between the Six Maws hunt, control of straits and islands, or a solitary route built around repeated decapitation attacks.

The creature's apex role is **Rapid multi-vector attacks, high pursuit, island warfare, and lethal attacks against retreating units**. Its primary support mixture is **Riptide Shoals, Abyssal Raiders, Venom Broods**.

### Inland profile

Moderate ceiling with strong performance in narrow coastal corridors and islands.

### Pact and rivalry profile

Weak pact preference, situational compact with Cetus, rivalry with Kraken and Leviathan.

### Counterplay

Lower staying power than the fortress-class apexes and poor performance in prolonged inland battles.

### AI role

The primary route AI profile is **Pursuit predator**. Its late campaign payoff is **A strait network that gives rapid reinforcement and target access across nearby islands**.

### Visual identity

Fish-tailed woman-monster silhouette with multiple canine or serpentine attack heads, grounded in classical art without copying one artifact.

Animated portrait direction: Independent head turns, small jaw movement, spray, and a controlled shift of the main gaze.


Scylla should begin in a compact theatre where speed matters. Sicily and Calabria create a chokepoint campaign, but the selector must test whether one-state transfer leaves the apex a valid front. Her support force should be light and mobile. A static heavy package would erase her identity.

The apex should use repeated attacks and pursuit pressure. The implementation can express the six-head theme through combat phases, temporary target effects, or a power that punishes retreat. It should not create six duplicate apex units.

Scylla's solitary route rewards killing important enemy formations and seizing islands before defenders consolidate. Her limited pact route is practical and temporary. She should be among the most likely monsters to betray a compact after Hunger rises.



## 3. Titanus Leviathan

### Cultural and geographic anchor

Biblical and Levantine sea-monster tradition. The preferred emergence is **Eastern Mediterranean and Levantine coast**.

### Play identity

Choose between the armored living fortress, a shore-breaking devastation path, or command over lesser deep creatures.

The creature's apex role is **Extreme armor, endurance, infrastructure crushing, and slow unstoppable coastal advance**. Its primary support mixture is **Drowned Colossi, Reefbreakers, Trench Stalkers**.

### Inland profile

Moderate ceiling. Late routes can trade speed for deeper endurance.

### Pact and rivalry profile

Affinity with Cetus and Timingila, rivalry with Jormungandr for terminal leadership.

### Counterplay

Slow redeployment and limited ability to answer separated coastal counterattacks.

### AI role

The primary route AI profile is **Armored juggernaut**. Its late campaign payoff is **A mobile fortress profile that can anchor one major offensive while broods hold captured shores**.

### Visual identity

Vast plated sea beast with ancient scars, dark Levantine water, broken stone harbor works, and no borrowed sacred lettering.

Animated portrait direction: Deep breathing, plates flexing, one eye opening, and water pressure moving across the frame.


Leviathan is the roster's strongest living fortress. Its apex should absorb concentrated fire and destroy infrastructure through selected assault outcomes. It must remain slow enough that defenders can attack another part of the coast, sever its lair network, and force redeployment choices.

The country should begin with fewer but heavier support units. Drowned Colossi and Reefbreakers hold territory while Leviathan advances. A rapid-raider build remains possible only through a secondary route.

Its pact route presents Leviathan as an anchor for eastern Mediterranean and Indian Ocean monsters. The solitary route turns controlled coastal capitals into fortified dominion seats. It should not gain unrestricted deep inland reach merely because its armor is high.



## 4. Titanus Cetus

### Cultural and geographic anchor

Greek ketos traditions linked to Aithiopia and Joppa. The preferred emergence is **Red Sea, Horn of Africa, or Levant-adjacent fallback pool**.

### Play identity

Choose between sacrificial terror, broad brood command, or a roaming ketos route that links the Red Sea and eastern Mediterranean.

The creature's apex role is **Balanced amphibious conquest, support-swarm coordination, and coastal devastation**. Its primary support mixture is **Riptide Shoals, Abyssal Raiders, Drowned Colossi**.

### Inland profile

Moderate ceiling with an early route toward dry coastal hinterlands.

### Pact and rivalry profile

High pact willingness with Leviathan and Timingila, conditional pact with Scylla.

### Counterplay

No single extreme combat advantage and greater dependence on mixed support formations.

### AI role

The primary route AI profile is **Balanced coastal conqueror**. Its late campaign payoff is **A multi-sea reinforcement web built from captured ports and regional pact access**.

### Visual identity

Classical ketos form with elongated marine body, curling tail, large jaws, and regional rock and reef cues.

Animated portrait direction: Neck and jaw motion, tail shadow, foam, and a measured shift toward the viewer.


Cetus fills the balanced expeditionary role. The Red Sea and Horn anchor should be preferred, while Joppa remains a historically linked fallback. The event must avoid presenting the Ethiopian ketos tradition as a modern national claim.

Cetus gains value from mixed support formations and can react to several theatres. Its apex should have reliable attack and endurance without matching Leviathan's armor or Scylla's speed.

The pact route is one of the strongest in the roster. Cetus can broker early compacts among Leviathan, Timingila, and selected Mediterranean monsters. Its solitary route uses fear and sacrifice themes through in-world monster behavior, with no direct imitation of a living religious ritual.



## 5. Titanus Hafgufa

### Cultural and geographic anchor

Old Norse and Icelandic descriptions of a vast feeding creature. The preferred emergence is **Iceland or Greenland Sea coast**.

### Play identity

Choose between trap-feeding lairs, an Arctic blockade network, or a vast stationary maw that draws enemies into prepared battles.

The creature's apex role is **Stationary or slow ambush power, blockade, baiting, ship swallowing, and fortified coastal lairs**. Its primary support mixture is **Trench Stalkers, Reefbreakers, Riptide Shoals**.

### Inland profile

Very low ceiling. Inland extension remains costly even late.

### Pact and rivalry profile

Strong affinity with Kraken, guarded affinity with Jormungandr, little interest in distant pacts.

### Counterplay

Slow strategic movement and a heavy reliance on defended coastal feeding grounds.

### AI role

The primary route AI profile is **Ambush fortress**. Its late campaign payoff is **Prepared coastal kill zones that reward holding selected ports instead of constant expansion**.

### Visual identity

Whale-like colossal maw presented as a moving island or paired rock mass, avoiding the false assumption that Hafgufa is another squid.

Animated portrait direction: Mouth opening, bait-cloud movement, waterline shift, and distant birds reacting.


Hafgufa should play as a prepared coastal trap. Its apex may be slow or partially stationary in strategic terms, but it must remain a real division that can be attacked and destroyed. The event cannot fake its presence only through a state modifier because the brief requires one apex unit.

Its lairs create bait zones. Enemy fleets, convoys, and landings near these zones can trigger bounded advantages. The monster earns support by holding the same coast and defeating attacks, not by racing inland.

The visual and text package must keep Hafgufa distinct from Kraken. Research links the medieval creature to whale-like trap feeding. Art that turns it into another giant squid would repeat a later conflation.



## 6. Titanus Jormungandr

### Cultural and geographic anchor

Norse World Serpent tradition. The preferred emergence is **North Atlantic or Nordic sea pool**.

### Play identity

Choose between encircling sea routes, venomous coastal collapse, or an apocalyptic solitary route built around continental reach.

The creature's apex role is **Long coastal reach, venom, environmental devastation, and strong late inland adaptation**. Its primary support mixture is **Venom Broods, Abyssal Raiders, Drowned Colossi**.

### Inland profile

Highest ordinary ceiling after Jiaolong and Cipactli. The terminal route can remove most limits.

### Pact and rivalry profile

Guarded ties with Kraken and Hafgufa, strong solitary ambition, rivalry with Leviathan.

### Counterplay

High Hunger growth when its realm becomes disconnected and elongated.

### AI role

The primary route AI profile is **Long-front serpent**. Its late campaign payoff is **A connected ring of coastal strongholds that extends reach without granting unrestricted continental conquest**.

### Visual identity

World-scale serpent coils glimpsed across storm seas, with no direct imitation of modern franchise designs.

Animated portrait direction: Slow coil movement, venom breath, storm light, and an eye tracking across the frame.


Jormungandr can eventually operate deeper than most ordinary monsters. That strength must carry a cost. Long connected coastal realms are powerful, while disconnected coils produce high Hunger and Sea Bond decay.

The apex should combine reach, venom pressure, and environmental damage. A signature action can mark a connected coastal sequence for temporary devastation. It must not attack every state along a continent through one global effect.

The solitary route has the strongest non-Cthulhu terminal ambition. It can satisfy the Apex Dominion readiness path after defeating rival apex countries and proving broad coastal control. It still cannot trigger a world end below World Collapse.



## 7. Titanus Iku-Turso

Finnish and Baltic mythic tradition. The preferred emergence is **Gulf of Finland or Baltic coast**.

Moderate ceiling. Frozen coast and lake-adjacent routes can reduce selected penalties after research.

### Pact and rivalry profile

Low baseline pact willingness, situational compact with Kraken, rivalry with Umibozu.

### Counterplay

Less raw durability and reduced advantage outside fog, cold, and coastal conditions.

### AI role

The primary route AI profile is **Fog raider**. Its late campaign payoff is **A Baltic concealment network that improves raids and defensive movement between threatened coasts**.

Deliberately non-canonical silhouette that combines sea growths, ancient armor-like ridges, and Baltic fog, since source traditions do not settle one appearance.

Iku-Turso's source traditions do not supply one settled visual anatomy. The country package should treat uncertainty as part of the identity. Different report art can show conflicting silhouettes while the final unit model chooses one coherent fictional design without claiming it is canonical.

The apex relies on fog, cold, and sudden coastal attacks. Its signature action can obscure a selected Baltic operation and reduce enemy organization for a bounded period. It should not create a general weather system that affects unrelated continents.

The focus tree can choose a hidden-raider identity or a more visible monstrous war form. Pacts remain rare. A compact with Kraken should require a shared Baltic or Nordic enemy and clear territorial separation.

Low ceiling. It can project pressure inland through storms without becoming a deep land conqueror.

### Pact and rivalry profile

Affinity with Akkorokamui, cautious cooperation with Jiaolong, rivalry with Bakunawa.

### Counterplay

Lower direct attack after storm pressure is removed and weak deep inland pursuit.

### AI role

The primary route AI profile is **Storm disrupter**. Its late campaign payoff is **A moving storm front that closes selected enemy ports for bounded periods after successful coastal objectives**.

### Visual identity

Massive dark monk-like sea silhouette, spare face, towering wave scale, and period Japanese coastal context.

Animated portrait direction: Wave rise, head tilt, eye or mouth reveal, rain, and a restrained lightning flash.


Umibozu should threaten Japan through storms and sudden appearances. The event should use Japanese yōkai research and visual archives as references, then create original fictional event art. It should not copy one historic scroll image into a country flag or model.

The apex's direct damage is secondary to disruption. Port shutdown, organization loss, planning disruption, and night attack create openings for support formations. A human defender can counter this package through port defense, air observation, and a successful tether mission.

The focus tree can deepen the apparition, storm, or brood route. Umibozu gains pact affinity with Akkorokamui because their theatres can meet without immediate identity duplication.

The ink route disrupts enemy targeting and port defense. The brood route creates a northern network. The pact route can coordinate with Umibozu and Jiaolong while preserving a distinct Ainu-associated geographical anchor.

## Shared support formations

The six support families create a common language across the roster. Each family still needs its own complete unit package.

| Family | Main role | Strategic use | Main limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Riptide Shoal | Fast amphibious screen | Port capture, island-chain objective, or successful pursuit mission | Cannot hold deep inland fronts and should not become a cheap universal line unit |
| Abyssal Raider | Mobile coastal assault | Coastal capital objective, raiding focus, or earned Hunger expenditure | Moderate durability and rising inland weakness |
| Reefbreaker | Heavy siege brute | Fortified port destruction, reef-lair focus, or heavy brood milestone | Expensive reinforcement receipt and poor pursuit |
| Trench Stalker | Ambush and defense | Lair defense, blue-hole or fog route, or successful coastal consolidation | Weak when forced into exposed inland battle |
| Venom Brood | Disruption and attrition | Poison, ink, venom, or disease-themed route milestone | Cannot independently break strong fortified lines |
| Drowned Colossus | Slow line anchor | Major feeding reserve, deep-lair capstone, or terminal reinforcement | Strict cap, high earned cost, and severe inland supply pressure |

The support-unit matrix gives the complete family brief.

## Creature access to support families

Each creature starts with three primary families. A focus route may unlock one secondary family. Cthulhu's Dominion can access all surviving family definitions through a reconciled terminal registry.

The event should preserve composition identity:

- fortress creatures use Reefbreakers and Drowned Colossi
- ambush creatures use Trench Stalkers
- rapid predators use Abyssal Raiders and Riptide Shoals
- venom, ink, and disruption routes use Venom Broods

A country should not solve every weakness by unlocking all families.

## Apex stat families

Raw combat values remain implementation work. The unit design uses five broad apex stat families.

| Family | Members | Combat direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fortress | Leviathan, Hafgufa | High defense and strength, low speed, strong coastal anchoring |
| Pursuit | Scylla, Te Wheke, Ipupiara | High speed and breakthrough, lower sustained defense |
| Disruption | Iku-Turso, Umibozu, Akkorokamui | Organization pressure, special powers, conditional defense |
| Devourer | Cetus, Timingila, Cipactli, Lusca | Feeding and reinforcement interaction with varied durability |
| Serpent and corridor | Kraken, Jormungandr, Jiaolong, Bakunawa | Reach, port or corridor control, and specialized movement |

Kraken sits between fortress and corridor roles. Lusca sits between devourer and ambush roles. The families guide tuning and AI. They are not public class labels.

## Unique apex division presentation

Every apex division needs:

- creature name as division name
- unique division icon or emblem
- unique battalion
- bespoke map counter
- unique 3D entity
- selection sound
- acknowledgement sound
- movement sound
- idle sound
- attack sound
- impact or special-action sound
- death or destruction sound
- route-specific unit tooltip
- visible current inland and Sea Bond effects
- no ordinary manpower or equipment line

The counter must remain readable at map scale and use the verified vanilla green family where that consumer requires it. A general monster portrait or flag cannot replace a unit counter.

## 3D model scale relationships

Apex creatures should appear larger than support creatures without covering an entire state or obscuring unit interaction.

Each model job needs a measured scale crosswalk against an installed vanilla entity. Nonhumanoid jobs require:

- measured reference dimensions
- provider target dimensions
- Blender dimensions
- entity scale
- effective runtime dimensions
- one named custom rig route
- action map
- ground-contact plan
- camera and selection readability
- death visibility

The exact scale is model-specific. No universal `giant monster scale` should be copied across sixteen different anatomies.

## Apex animation roles

Each apex needs substantive skeletal actions appropriate to its anatomy.

Minimum roles:

- idle
- locomotion
- attack
- special action where the unit uses one
- hit or impact reaction when the engine consumer supports it
- death or destruction

A tentacled creature needs articulated tentacle motion. A serpent needs body propagation. A whale-like ambush creature needs mouth and body actions. Whole-model translation, rotation, or static-pose aliases do not count.

## Sound ownership

Every apex and support family needs a sourced sound package with rights evidence.

No generated, synthesized, recorded, placeholder, or unlicensed audio is allowed. The 3D worker researches candidate sounds and maps them to actions. The parent implementation agent owns final sound definitions and entity wiring.

One generic roar reused for all monsters would erase identity and fail the asset contract.

## Cthulhu's Dominion country package

The terminal country is a separate stable tag.

It receives:

- public name under the final selected localisation
- Cthulhu as leader
- one fictional high-chaos animated leader portrait
- a dedicated terminal flag set
- one terminal focus tree
- all surviving apex divisions
- reconciled support formations
- reconciled controlled states and subjects
- one terminal Hunger value or terminal replacement state
- one terminal Sea Bond value
- unrestricted or nearly unrestricted inland range
- all support-family permissions
- no Cthulhu division
- no ability to restore a dead apex
- terminal AI strategy
- terminal surrender and all-apex collapse
- world-end super-event package

Cthulhu is a supernatural leader and union center. The event must never imply that a missing Cthulhu map unit is an asset gap.

## Cthulhu leader presentation

The portrait should present an impossible deep-sea ruler whose presence binds the surviving creatures. It should avoid direct copying of one commercial Cthulhu design.

The animated state can use:

- slow head and mantle motion
- distant eye light
- deep-water particulate movement
- restrained shadow movement behind the leader
- one terminal corruption state at low surviving-apex count

The portrait cannot be a transform-only loop.

## Abyssal Remnant country package

Every defeated full monster tag can load one shared remnant tree and one generic remnant leader family.

The remnant package needs:

- generic nonhuman country name variant tied to the original creature
- generic remnant leader
- static and animated leader presentation only if separately authorised
- reduced flag variant or unchanged original flag with clear remnant status
- support-only unit access
- strict support cap
- no apex unit
- no unique apex powers
- no terminal leadership
- no independent pact leadership
- shallow inland range
- surrender, dispersal, and final cleanup routes

A remnant should remain dangerous around its old lairs. It should not become a second full country campaign.

## Human population under monster rule

The state population remains human unless another event has changed it through a proven transition.

Monster control can create:

- evacuation
- feeding deaths
- resistance
- infrastructure loss
- famine pressure
- hidden survivors
- collaboration by coercion
- post-liberation trauma

It cannot convert the population into normal recruitable monster manpower.

## Country-package readiness state

Every identity should use a content-readiness proof checked by the opening selector.

A package is `ready` only after all required gameplay and visible surfaces exist. During staged implementation, an identity can be registered as `blocked` or `development_only`. It must not enter natural selection.

Manual debug setup can expose a development-only identity only through an explicit developer route that never ships as normal player UI.
