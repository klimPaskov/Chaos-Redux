## Mutual-exclusion rule

Pact and solitary commitment branches become mutually exclusive after survival. Early apex, brood, and shallow range investments remain shared unless a creature-specific route makes them incompatible.

## Completion rule

A branch does not count as implemented when it contains only flat modifiers. It needs one mechanical unlock, one consequence, and one capstone or handoff.
