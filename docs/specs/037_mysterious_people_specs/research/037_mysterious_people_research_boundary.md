# Event 037 research boundary

## Source basis

This event is a fictional global anomaly. Its design was expanded from the accepted Event 037 concept and the supplied Chaos Redux mechanics, event, decision, asset, population, Famine, Migration, Deaths, Camp and Repression, Condemnation, catalog, and subagent rules.

No external historical claim is required to establish how the population appears. The event's core rule forbids a canonical explanation.

## Appropriate implementation research

Implementation may research or inspect:

- period census administration and household registration practices
- 1930s and 1940s municipal record rooms
- period housing, schools, clinics, rail stations, farms, and public works
- wartime rationing and relief logistics
- period terminology for registration, settlement, relief, and public administration
- visual references for documentary event art
- installed vanilla and Chaos Redux presentation precedents

This research should improve wording, visuals, and institutional detail.

## Inappropriate research conclusion

Research cannot establish or imply that the newcomers are:

- aliens
- time travellers
- clones
- returned dead
- alternate-universe people
- products of a secret government project
- members of another known Chaos Redux species or event

Scientific, religious, ideological, and intelligence theories can appear in-world as interpretations. None receives canonical authority.

## Source citation rule

Any real historical wording, image, institution, or administrative claim used in final localisation or assets should be sourced according to the repository research and asset rules.

The final event does not need a bibliography for its fictional origin. It does need provenance for sourced art, period references, real quotations if any are later introduced, and every external factual claim used in permanent documentation.
