# Event 037 research boundary

## Source basis

Event 037 is a fictional global anomaly.
No historical claim is needed to explain how the newcomers appear, and the [mystery firewall in Part 1](../specs/037_mysterious_people_spec_part_1_core.md#mystery-firewall) forbids any explanation.

## Useful research

Implementation may research period detail that makes the newcomers' arrival feel lived in:

- 1930s and 1940s housing, schools, markets, factories, farms, rail stations, and public works
- wartime rationing, bread queues, and crowded-city conditions
- period terminology for registration, housing, and municipal government
- visual references for documentary report art
- installed vanilla and Chaos Redux presentation precedents

## Research limits

Research cannot establish or imply an origin for the newcomers.
Scientific, religious, ideological, and intelligence theories may appear in-world as opinions, and none receives authority.

## Citation rule

Real historical wording, images, institutions, or administrative facts used in final localisation, assets, or permanent documentation follow the repository research and asset rules for provenance.
The fictional origin needs no bibliography.
