# Event 037: Mysterious People

## Part 5: Event chain, flavour, and reactions

## Event-chain shape

Event 037 needs a compact event chain with broad flavour coverage. It should not create one popup for every state or force every country to read the same local report.

The family has four roles:

1. Global manifestation report for each firing.
2. National impact report for human countries with meaningful local results.
3. Local anomaly reports selected from a varied pool with cooldowns.
4. Crisis and policy reports tied to pressure, Famine, Migration, and atrocity outcomes.

The entry event remains `chaosx.nr37.1` during implementation. Follow-up numbering should reserve space for baseline flavour, three evolution families, international reactions, and aftermath.

## Global manifestation report

Every firing produces one global report for human players.

It should establish:

- appearance occurred across inhabited states worldwide
- world population rose by the applied amount
- newcomers possess local knowledge and records
- no transport or departure evidence exists
- current manifestation is larger or smaller than earlier ones when history exists
- governments are beginning to treat the phenomenon as recurring

The report communicates scale and public understanding. It should not list a raw effect package.

Baseline tone is restrained, curious, and uneasy. Evolution I emphasizes rapid settlement growth and strain. Evolution II emphasizes food and movement pressure. Evolution III emphasizes multiplication beyond ordinary institutional capacity.

## National impact report

A human country receives a national report when at least one condition applies:

- latest firing created a meaningful number of people in the country
- country crossed a pressure threshold
- country gained a major demographic-dividend opportunity
- a capital, core industrial state, or important island received a large increase
- active policy creates a distinct response
- country already manages Event 037-linked Famine, Migration, or repression

The report shows the national amount created and identifies one or two high-impact states. It directs the player to the decision category when action is needed.

A country with no valid state receives no empty event.

## Follow-up selection

Local flavour uses a weighted pool with diversity memory.

Selection considers evolution, pressure, integration, policy, state terrain and development, urban or rural context, island and occupation status, mysterious majority, prior flavour families, active humanitarian systems, and firing count.

A flavour event normally targets one state and one country. The same family should not repeat until several other families have had a chance.

Small local effects are valid when they follow from the story. The main role is to sustain mystery and show how population changes ordinary life.

## Baseline flavour family

### 1. The Village with Old Foundations

Trigger direction:

- rural or lightly developed state
- recent grant
- no severe active crisis

Observed situation:

A complete settlement occupies land remembered as empty. Foundations show weathering, wells contain years of deposits, and fruit trees are mature.

Gameplay direction:

- small integration or local-capacity benefit
- survey can improve registration
- demolition increases hostility without solving the mystery

### 2. Five Years of School Records

Trigger direction:

- populated state with good integration potential

Observed situation:

Teachers, pupils, attendance logs, examination results, and disciplinary files describe several school years. Long-term residents do not remember the school opening.

Gameplay direction:

- integration benefit under Open Integration
- administrative strain under Restricted Registration
- support for clinics and schools project

### 3. The Unknown Relative

Trigger direction:

- stable state
- low pressure

Observed situation:

Families receive visits from relatives whose names and histories fit family records. Private photographs show shared holidays and weddings that only one side remembers.

Gameplay direction:

- integration benefit when accepted
- hidden-population growth when authorities encourage denunciation

### 4. The Road Everyone Uses

Trigger direction:

- state with infrastructure
- new settlement present

Observed situation:

A road connects the settlement to the regional network. Drivers know its turns and danger points. Maintenance records contain years of repairs, yet recent maps omitted it.

Gameplay direction:

- small transport benefit or project discount
- survey can reveal another contradictory record

### 5. Tax Receipts from Last Winter

Trigger direction:

- country established the population office

Observed situation:

New households present valid tax receipts dated before manifestation. Payments appear in government accounts, but the money cannot be traced to an earlier deposit.

Gameplay direction:

- improves administrative confidence
- increases pressure on officials claiming fabrication

### 6. The Factory Shift

Trigger direction:

- industrial state
- spare employment capacity

Observed situation:

A factory begins a full shift staffed by people already listed in its personnel system. Managers remember hiring them, while previous production records show no shift.

Gameplay direction:

- temporary dividend increase
- higher service pressure when housing is weak

### 7. Cemeteries for Living Families

Trigger direction:

- state with a large mysterious share

Observed situation:

A cemetery contains generations of family graves connected to living newcomers. Stone age and soil disturbance appear genuine.

Gameplay direction:

- integration and mystery
- persecution or grave destruction creates evidence and resistance

### 8. The Duplicate Address

Trigger direction:

- urban state

Observed situation:

Two unrelated households possess valid deeds, keys, photographs, and utility histories for the same home. The building contains rooms arranged to support both accounts.

Gameplay direction:

- housing pressure
- emergency housing gains urgency
- fair settlement improves legitimacy

### 9. Names Across Continents

Trigger direction:

- two or more countries have recent arrivals

Observed situation:

Rare surnames and family stories appear in distant countries with no plausible migration link. Documents describe relatives separated by borders long before either settlement existed.

Gameplay direction:

- international research cooperation
- no origin conclusion

### 10. The Doctor's Patients

Trigger direction:

- state has clinic capacity or active clinic project

Observed situation:

A physician recognizes patients and their medical histories. Patients remember years of treatment. Older staff insist the consultation room did not exist last week.

Gameplay direction:

- integration and service benefit
- support for clinic expansion

## Evolution I flavour family

### 11. New Districts by Morning

Trigger direction:

- Evolution I or higher
- urban state
- recent large grant

Observed situation:

Entire residential districts fill underused land. Streets, shops, and municipal services exist, but strategic transport has not expanded with them.

Gameplay direction:

- transport and service pressure
- priority for utilities work

### 12. The Settlement Board Revolts

Trigger direction:

- Managed Settlement
- several projects active

Observed situation:

Local administrators refuse a national resettlement quota because the destination lacks water, schools, or work.

Gameplay direction:

- revise plan
- spend more capacity
- impose settlement and accept pressure and resistance

### 13. Work Without Homes

Trigger direction:

- industrial state
- strong employment
- weak housing

Observed situation:

Factories absorb the workforce while families sleep in warehouses, rail yards, and unfinished buildings.

Gameplay direction:

- production benefit continues
- housing and health pressure rises

### 14. The Quiet Rural Boom

Trigger direction:

- rural state
- adequate food and transport
- pressure below Overcrowded

Observed situation:

New farms and workshops integrate with little conflict. Nearby towns gain markets and labor.

Gameplay direction:

- strong local dividend
- priority as planned-settlement destination

### 15. The Census That Changes Overnight

Trigger direction:

- Restricted Registration
- hidden population rising

Observed situation:

Officials complete a count, then discover that archives contain a different completed count with older signatures and a larger population.

Gameplay direction:

- reported pressure becomes less reliable
- registration can restore confidence
- repression increases hidden population

### 16. Two Municipal Governments

Trigger direction:

- large mysterious share
- low integration or administrative conflict

Observed situation:

The new district possesses a council with records of past elections and budgets. Existing city government rejects its authority.

Gameplay direction:

- compromise improves integration
- dissolution creates resistance
- recognition expands capacity at political cost

## Evolution II flavour and crisis family

### 17. Grain for People Who Were Not Here

Trigger direction:

- Evolution II or higher
- food projection worsening

Observed situation:

Distribution plans were written for the previous population. Warehouses empty earlier than expected, while records claim the larger ration list has existed for years.

Gameplay direction:

- stronger population burden to Famine
- directs player to reserves, imports, or relief

### 18. The Station Never Empties

Trigger direction:

- active internal movement
- crowded transport hub

Observed situation:

Families arrive faster than trains can remove them. Many possess tickets issued before manifestation.

Gameplay direction:

- trapped-population risk
- transport project or corridor mission
- unsafe clearing can create displacement harm

### 19. A Border Full of Cousins

Trigger direction:

- neighboring countries have Event 037 populations
- one border policy is restrictive

Observed situation:

Families on both sides claim close relations and old visits. Border records support them, though guards remember no crossings.

Gameplay direction:

- reception or family reunification
- closure creates trapped people
- violent pushback can create deaths and Condemnation

### 20. Relief Convoys Change Destination

Trigger direction:

- active Famine relief
- several pressured states

Observed situation:

Convoy manifests list a destination that relief officials do not remember selecting. The listed settlement exists and is in greater need than the original route.

Gameplay direction:

- honor, split, or reject rerouting
- no proof that newcomers altered the manifest

### 21. The Empty Province Is Gone

Trigger direction:

- planned settlement or rural expansion

Observed situation:

Land reserved for future settlement is occupied by villages with property claims older than the national plan.

Gameplay direction:

- destination loses spare capacity
- alternative destination required
- compensation or recognition can preserve legitimacy

### 22. Hospitals with No Beds

Trigger direction:

- Overcrowded or worse
- service bottleneck

Observed situation:

New patients possess complete medical files, but wards and medicine stocks reflect the smaller population.

Gameplay direction:

- health and integration pressure
- clinic project gains urgency
- severe failure feeds owning mortality systems

### 23. The Night Convoy

Trigger direction:

- authoritarian policy
- forced or covert movement considered

Observed situation:

Security units begin moving families without a public order. Destination is uncertain and transport receipts are incomplete.

Gameplay direction:

- stop movement
- formalize it through Migration with proof and consequences
- conceal it and risk discovery

No untracked transfer can apply.

### 24. A City Asks for Entry Limits

Trigger direction:

- major urban state at Emergency

Observed situation:

Local authorities demand power to restrict new arrivals and redirect settlement.

Gameplay direction:

- grant local controls
- fund capacity
- impose national reception
- each route changes pressure, integration, and legitimacy

## Evolution III flavour and collapse family

### 25. The Majority Nobody Remembers Meeting

Trigger direction:

- mysterious people exceed half of state population

Observed situation:

Most residents now belong to families that appeared through Event 037. Institutions function normally, but original residents cannot explain when the balance changed.

Gameplay direction:

- strong integration opportunity
- hostile policy creates severe resistance and concern

### 26. Maps Cannot Keep Up

Trigger direction:

- repeated Evolution III manifestations
- several settlements

Observed situation:

Survey agencies publish maps outdated before distribution. Older editions sometimes contain settlements the latest edition omits.

Gameplay direction:

- planning penalty
- population office investment reduces effect

### 27. The Second Capital

Trigger direction:

- capital at high pressure
- large mysterious district

Observed situation:

A complete administrative quarter claims to have served the capital for decades. Ministries discover duplicate departments and conflicting authority.

Gameplay direction:

- integrate capacity
- dissolve duplicate offices
- move national functions
- political consequences depend on government

### 28. Cities Beyond the Railhead

Trigger direction:

- remote state
- huge grant

Observed situation:

Large settlements appear beyond practical reach of the rail and supply network.

Gameplay direction:

- new-city and transport program
- planned evacuation or settlement
- Breakdown risk if ignored

### 29. The Food Ministry's Impossible Ledger

Trigger direction:

- severe Famine projection
- Restricted Registration or data conflict

Observed situation:

Food accounts contain years of consumption by people who appeared recently. Stocks are physically missing in amounts matching records.

Gameplay direction:

- confirms a real shortage
- exposes undercounting
- does not imply theft by newcomers

### 30. A Government Counts Origins

Trigger direction:

- Evolution III
- political pressure for origin classification

Observed situation:

Government proposes formal origin registration for every resident.

Gameplay direction:

- reject classification
- record provenance for support planning with safeguards
- create coercive registry enabling repression and evidence risk

### 31. The Closed Zone

Trigger direction:

- closed settlement zone active

Observed situation:

Supply, movement, and information deteriorate. Residents organize internal markets, schools, and resistance.

Gameplay direction:

- improve access
- intensify control
- invite inspection
- conceal conditions

Famine, camps, resistance, and Condemnation own results.

### 32. Missing from the Ledger

Trigger direction:

- forced labor or killing active
- evidence discrepancy

Observed situation:

Population records show rapid decline that movement reports cannot explain.

Gameplay direction:

- investigation
- cover-up
- exposure
- survivor flight

Use existing evidence and Condemnation.

### 33. The Country That Managed

Trigger direction:

- Evolution III
- large mysterious share
- pressure below Strained for sustained period

Observed situation:

A country has absorbed a demographic transformation that broke other states. New cities, farms, schools, and industry function as ordinary institutions.

Gameplay direction:

- major dividend
- international prestige and settlement leadership
- achievement progress

### 34. The Country That Emptied Its Camps

Trigger direction:

- repression ended
- survivors released or resettled
- evidence remains

Observed situation:

Sites close, but population loss, family separation, and records remain.

Gameplay direction:

- reform and survivor support
- investigation and accountability
- incomplete cover-up
- no automatic removal of Condemnation

## Scientific and administrative reactions

International research can study document age, genetics, language, building materials, archives, population models, memory, and aerial photographs where campaign technology permits.

Research can improve registration, pressure forecasting, or cooperation. It cannot unlock a canonical explanation.

## Religious and ideological reactions

Interpretations can include providential growth, divine judgment through abundance, national renewal, historical destiny, a challenge to property and citizenship, a new working class, a threat to hierarchy, universal human status, or exclusion propaganda.

These are political interpretations. No route receives privileged truth.

## Regime reaction matrix

| Regime or condition | Likely public response | Main tendency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stable democracy | rights, registration, housing, inquiry | Open Integration, support, cooperation |
| Weak democracy | quotas, coalition dispute, local resistance | mixed policy, delayed projects, relief |
| Communist government | labor allocation, collective housing, planning | Managed Settlement, agriculture, employment |
| Fascist government | origin classification, propaganda, hierarchy | Restricted Registration, coercion, persecution risk |
| Military government | screening, logistics, strategic resettlement | Managed Settlement, military distribution |
| Monarchy or traditional regime | dynastic, religious, customary interpretation | policy follows legitimacy and institutions |
| Colonial authority | labor demand, settler conflict, unequal access | exploitation, camps, resistance |
| Occupied territory | conflict between owner and controller | relief, resistance, forced movement risk |
| Government in exile | claims about citizens and occupied population | diplomacy, relief requests |
| Actual nonhuman actor | no ordinary civilian handling | shared classifier exclusion |

## Geographic reactions

Densely populated industrial regions gain large cohorts and strong labor value, with housing and distribution strain.

Rural agricultural regions can absorb settlement when water, land, food, and transport exist.

Small islands receive bounded local ratios and face hard food, water, port, and movement limits.

Front-line and bombed states receive civilians into damaged housing and dangerous routes, creating urgent evacuation and relief.

Occupied states create disputes over registration, labor, relief, and citizenship.

Underpopulated interiors offer settlement opportunities only after real transport, water, and employment investment.

## International diplomacy

Concrete needs can create:

- food-export relief
- industrial housing aid
- settlement reception
- convoy access
- faction coordination
- ideological influence
- sanctions against persecution
- international settlement compacts led by successful countries

The event should not create a universal permanent organization without campaign support. Cooperation can use projects and temporary agreements.

## Cross-event callbacks

Useful callbacks include Event 019 soldiers recognizing a settlement name, time-travel records contradicting Event 037 archives, disasters revealing impossible foundations, resource discoveries attracting settlement, equipment windfalls supporting logistics, disease spreading through density, and civil wars recruiting integrated arrivals.

Every callback preserves separate ownership and separate explanations.

## Writing direction

Event text should use concrete observations about people, homes, records, institutions, shortages, movement, and policy.

Avoid generic denials, repeated report-versus-authority contrast, map changes as the main emotional image, sealed dossiers as the main mystery device, direct labels calling the event a warning, final exposition, and cheap comedy around mass suffering.

Baseline reactions can use restrained disbelief, practical acceptance, administrative concern, or cautious welcome. Evolution I reactions emphasize planning and capacity. Evolution II reactions emphasize food, routes, and relief. Evolution III reactions expose political and moral direction.

Final localisation should use dynamic country, state, population, pressure, and policy context. Working labels here are not final text.

## Notification control

- one global report per firing
- one national report only for significant human-country impact
- flavour selected by cooldown and diversity memory
- crisis reports only at meaningful thresholds or concrete outcomes
- no report per state grant
- no report per pressure tick
- AI processes without human popups
- multiplayer receives relevant national context without duplicating the world transaction

## Event-chain acceptance statement

The event family succeeds when repeated firings produce varied, state-specific observations while origin remains unresolved. Flavour explains why decisions matter and shows policy consequences. It cannot replace real population, pressure, Famine, Migration, camp, evidence, or Condemnation mechanics with text-only outcomes.
