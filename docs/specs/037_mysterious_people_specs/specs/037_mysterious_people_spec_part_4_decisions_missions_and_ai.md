# Event 037: Mysterious People

## Part 4: Decisions, missions, and AI

## Decision-system purpose

The Event 037 decision layer helps each country turn a global population anomaly into national policy. It should let the player answer three questions quickly:

- How severe is current Overpopulation Pressure?
- Which states create the largest immediate risk?
- Which investment or movement action will change the situation most?

The category changes by pressure stage, evolution, policy, and selected state. It must not remain a permanent list of every possible housing, food, migration, police, and atrocity action.

## Presentation

Use an ordinary decision category with:

- a distinct category icon
- a static category picture
- national living mysterious-population total
- one visual pressure meter or staged status
- active policy stance
- concise selected-state summary
- one to three active missions
- three to five primary decisions in the current phase

The category picture establishes identity through settlement, population, or municipal contradiction. It contains no fake buttons, painted values, or controls.

## Category lifecycle

The category appears when a country owns or controls living Event 037 population and has a policy, consequence, or useful action.

At low pressure it can collapse into a compact status view. At higher pressure emergency actions replace low-priority options.

It hides after the country has no living Event 037 population, no active Event 037-linked Famine or Migration consequence, no mission, no policy consequence, no atrocity site, and no pending request. Historical and achievement facts remain.

## Policy stances

Each country selects one public stance. Changing stance has a meaningful cooldown or transition cost.

### Open Integration

The government treats mysterious arrivals as ordinary residents and prioritizes registration, employment, education, and services.

Direction:

- faster integration
- stronger demographic dividend
- lower resistance and hidden population
- higher immediate demand on housing and services
- easier voluntary settlement and reception
- greater political cost if the government later persecutes the population

Favored by stable democracies, high-capacity countries, and governments with low pressure.

### Managed Settlement

The government accepts arrivals while directing where they live and work.

Direction:

- moderate integration
- strong access to planned settlement
- lower concentration in selected states
- construction and transport costs
- coercion risk when movement becomes compulsory
- good performance in large countries with uneven density and working logistics

Favored by large countries, wartime majors protecting core regions, and governments with moderate pressure.

### Restricted Registration

The government limits access, movement, and formal recognition while trying to control the anomaly.

Direction:

- lower short-term service enrollment
- slower integration and weaker dividend
- more hidden population
- resistance, black-market settlement, and documentation conflicts
- easier progression into closed zones and forced movement at high evolution
- official reports can understate actual crisis risk

Favored by authoritarian, exclusionary, low-capacity, or highly pressured regimes.

If this stance creates undercounting, warning signs should appear through events and shared humanitarian evidence. The player should understand why official pressure later changes.

## Selected-state workflow

The category focuses targeted actions on one selected state.

1. Show a compact list of high-priority Event 037 states.
2. Select one state.
3. Show mysterious population, share, pressure, main bottleneck, and active projects.
4. Replace generic actions with decisions valid for that state.
5. Clear selection to return to the national view.

Priority favors high pressure, Famine risk, trapped people, housing or transport failure, capitals, industrial regions, and states where a project can produce large recovery.

Human players see only selected-state actions. AI can evaluate all valid targets without the selector.

## Baseline decisions

### Establish the Population Office

Creates formal Event 037 administration, unlocks reliable national totals, policy stance, and integration processing.

Cost direction:

- political and administrative capacity
- temporary civilian-factory burden in larger countries

This is a one-time institutional action.

### Conduct a Registration Drive

Improves integration in weakly registered states, reduces hidden population under inclusive policy, exposes undercounting, and improves state prioritization.

Cost direction:

- political or civilian administrative burden
- support equipment or manpower when field offices are needed
- time

The action targets a meaningful group of weak states, not the entire country for one cheap payment.

### Employ the New Workforce

Converts high integration and spare capacity into demographic-dividend growth.

Requirements:

- pressure below Emergency
- adequate integration
- no active atrocity campaign against the population

The reward scales with integrated population and weakens when pressure becomes severe.

## Evolution I state projects

### Build Emergency Housing

Reduces housing pressure through a timed construction project. Completion creates durable capacity.

Cost direction:

- civilian factory commitment
- construction capacity
- manpower
- duration based on population and damage

Failure can create unfinished settlements, corruption, or higher local pressure.

### Extend Water and Transport

Improves distribution, sanitation, movement, and settlement capacity.

Cost direction:

- civilian factory commitment
- trains or trucks
- fuel where needed
- time

The result responds to actual infrastructure and supply. A remote island, urban capital, and damaged front-line state cannot receive identical effects.

### Expand Clinics and Schools

Improves integration, services, and long-term economic and military use.

Cost direction:

- civilian factory burden
- support equipment
- manpower or political capacity
- time

The project is weaker under occupation or active famine.

### Prepare a Planned Settlement

Creates a voluntary movement request through Migration from a pressured origin to a proven destination.

Requirements:

- valid origin and destination
- destination capacity
- transport and access
- compatible policy

Costs can include trains, trucks, convoys, fuel, destination construction, and time.

### Expand Agricultural Support

Publishes Event 037 burden to Famine and directs the player into agricultural expansion, reserve, import, or distribution work through the Famine owner.

The Event 037 category must not duplicate the same food-import decision under a second system.

## Evolution II emergency actions

### Open Emergency Food Channels

Directs the player into the appropriate Famine reserve, import, relief, route, or airlift action and identifies which Event 037 states drive demand.

Costs and outcomes belong to Famine.

### Mobilize Military Logistics

Uses military transport, depots, and command capacity for civilian distribution.

Cost direction:

- conservative command-power cost
- fuel
- trucks or trains
- temporary supply or readiness strain

The action creates a real tradeoff with active fronts.

### Request Foreign Relief

Asks suitable countries for grain, convoys, construction aid, or reception support.

Donor AI considers food security, distance, access, relations, ideology, faction, war, and its own Event 037 pressure. Aid can create dependency or influence.

### Redistribute the Population

Submits an internal or cross-border movement request through Migration, favoring severe origins and proven safe destinations.

Ordinary policy supports voluntary and controlled movement. Coercive movement belongs to extreme routes.

### Requisition Empty Property

Creates temporary housing through emergency use of vacant or underused property.

Consequences can include stability, legitimacy, compliance, or property conflict. It does not create free permanent construction.

## Evolution III constructive actions

### Build New Cities

Commits a major long-term construction program in selected safe states.

Requirements and costs include major civilian factory commitment, infrastructure or railway support, transport equipment, construction manpower, and long duration.

Completion should visibly change state development and enable large settlement.

### National Food Expansion Program

Creates a country-scale agricultural and distribution effort through Famine and links several vulnerable states into one response.

### International Settlement Compact

Creates multilateral reception and distribution through Migration. It requires trust, access, and capacity. It can fragment when members close borders, enter war, or persecute the population.

## Evolution III extreme actions

Extreme actions appear only under severe pressure and compatible politics. Their presentation must be distinct from support investment.

### Create Closed Settlement Zones

Concentrates and restricts Event 037 population.

Consequences include local pressure, resistance, hidden population, Famine vulnerability, security commitment, evidence, and possible Condemnation.

### Order Forced Relocation

Moves a targeted cohort through Migration. It requires a valid route and destination. Unsafe movement can create deaths. Refusal creates trapped people or diplomatic escalation.

### Impose Forced Labor

Creates or expands an Event 037-targeted site through Camp and Repression. It can raise short-term output while producing mortality, evidence, resistance, and Condemnation.

### Begin Extermination

Enters the existing systematic-killing route against Event 037 mysterious people. It uses exact population and provenance transactions and the full atrocity, discovery, Condemnation, resistance, migration, and Deaths consequences.

It cannot appear as a neutral pressure-control option.

## Missions

The event normally shows one active national mission and no more than two urgent state missions.

### Absorb the Newcomers

Recommended duration is `120` to `180` days.

Success requires active work such as completing capacity projects, raising integration, keeping pressure below failure stage, and preventing severe Event 037-linked Famine.

Success strengthens the dividend, lowers pressure, improves integration, and advances achievements.

Failure raises hidden population or pressure and can create a harder follow-up.

### Prevent Demographic Emergency

Recommended duration is about `180` days at Overcrowded pressure.

Success requires lowering pressure, containing Famine risk, and completing at least one meaningful response.

Failure creates Emergency pressure, stronger migration demand, political instability, and a crisis report.

### Keep the Corridor Open

Recommended duration is `90` to `150` days depending on route and war.

Success requires actual route security, supply access, or transport. It auto-completes when conditions are met.

Failure creates trapped population, worsened relief access, or a failed movement receipt through the owner.

### Complete the New Settlement

Recommended duration is `120` to `240` days.

Success requires housing, route access, and destination capacity. Completion moves a verified cohort and creates durable capacity.

Failure leaves people at origin or trapped in transit according to Migration. It cannot delete them.

## Cost design

One action uses no more than four spendable cost types.

Useful families include:

- civilian factory commitment
- construction capacity
- manpower
- support equipment
- trucks
- trains
- convoys
- fuel
- political power for policy and administration
- command power for military logistics
- stability or legitimacy as consequence
- time and active mission slots

Costs scale from population affected, state damage, distance, destination capacity, war, industry, previous failure, evolution, and pressure.

The same political-power price cannot cover a census office, new city, food convoy, and forced relocation.

## Visibility budget

| Pressure stage | Primary national actions | Selected-state actions | Active missions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Absorbing | `2` to `3` | `0` to `2` | `0` to `1` |
| Strained | `3` to `4` | `2` to `3` | `1` |
| Overcrowded | `3` to `5` | `2` to `4` | `1` to `2` |
| Emergency | `3` to `5` | `2` to `4` | `1` to `3` |
| Breakdown | `3` to `5` | `2` to `4` | `1` to `3` |

Basic actions are replaced by stronger stage versions where appropriate. Obsolete projects hide after completion, target loss, policy change, or crisis resolution.

## AI profiles

### High-capacity integrator

Conditions include stability, strong industry, food access, low pressure, and inclusive politics.

Behavior:

- Open Integration
- early housing and services
- voluntary settlement
- use of demographic dividend
- reception when capacity exists
- strong repression avoidance

### Planned-development state

Conditions include large territory, uneven density, adequate transport, and moderate pressure.

Behavior:

- Managed Settlement
- destination construction
- movement from dense states
- new cities and agriculture
- balanced workforce and logistics

### Wartime logistic state

Conditions include major war, pressured industry, available military transport, and front-line supply risk.

Behavior:

- protect capitals and industrial regions
- use military logistics only when fronts can bear cost
- evacuate combat zones
- avoid actions that collapse supply
- accept temporary crowding when production survival requires it

### Relief-dependent state

Conditions include weak industry, poor food access, damaged transport, and high pressure.

Behavior:

- request relief
- prioritize Famine actions
- seek controlled reception or settlement
- avoid projects it cannot finish
- accept dependency when survival requires it

### Restrictive authoritarian state

Conditions include authoritarian politics, low legitimacy or high repression, severe pressure, and fear of integration.

Behavior:

- Restricted Registration
- closed zones and compulsory settlement
- military distribution and coercive control
- higher hidden population and resistance
- possible forced labor or deportation

### Atrocity-prone regime

Systematic killing remains rare and scenario-dependent.

Strong prerequisites include Evolution III, sustained Breakdown, severe food or housing failure, exterminatory politics, low Condemnation concern, weak democratic constraints, existing atrocity capacity, no viable safer action, and high tolerance for resistance and isolation.

Strong suppressors include democracy, alliance dependence, monitoring, available relief, working settlement routes, high integration, major workforce use, and prior sanctions.

Probability evidence must show that support actions dominate whenever viable.

## Donor and destination AI

Relief donors and settlement destinations consider:

- their own pressure
- Food Security and Relief Access
- Reception Capacity and Border Policy
- distance and route
- transport stockpiles
- relations and faction
- ideology
- war
- influence goals
- Condemnation and humanitarian policy
- risk of accepting too many people

A donor cannot send missing resources. A destination cannot accept beyond proven capacity without inheriting pressure. Refusal must produce a valid outcome.

## Invalidation and cleanup

AI decisions fail closed when a state changes controller, destination disappears, route becomes impossible, country becomes nonhuman, stage changes, another owner consumed the request, cohort no longer exists, or peace and annexation invalidate a project.

AI clears stale selections and chooses a new valid target. It cannot repeatedly pay for a failed action.

## Weighted-logic evidence

Every weighted policy choice, target selector, evolution timing factor, flavour pool, and random project outcome needs named scenario evidence through the project probability workflow.

The separate probability matrix defines policy ordering, relief ordering, wartime logistics, destination and donor selection, atrocity suppression, evolution pacing, state priority, and flavour diversity.

The design prescribes outcome ordering, invalid-target exclusion, and starvation limits. Exact percentages require the complete implemented candidate pool.
