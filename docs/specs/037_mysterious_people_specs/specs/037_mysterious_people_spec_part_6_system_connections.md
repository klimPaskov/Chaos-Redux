# Event 037: Mysterious People

## Part 6: Connections to shared Chaos Redux systems

## Ownership principle

Event 037 owns the population anomaly and the facts required to describe it.

Event 037 owns:

- population creation at manifestation
- per-state mysterious provenance
- country and world mysterious totals
- integration related to the created cohort
- Overpopulation Pressure
- manifestation and mystery events
- event-owned policy and support decisions
- requests submitted to shared systems
- provenance reconciliation after applied shared transactions
- event achievements and direct Chaos milestones

Shared systems retain their responsibilities. Event 037 supplies inputs, receives proven results, and does not create a second version of an existing mechanic.

## Famine connection

Event 037 publishes population burden and support facts needed by Famine, including current population, mysterious population, recent growth, transport and housing pressure, integration conditions, settlement projects, and trapped-population demand.

Famine retains ownership of Food Security, Food Reserves, Relief Access, stage movement, blockade proof, reserve release, imports, route repair, escorted relief, airlift, invited relief, mortality, evacuation requests, decisions, missions, and map mode.

When Event 037 offers agricultural support or emergency food channels, the action should open, strengthen, or request the existing Famine route. The country cannot pay twice for one step unless the second payment represents a distinct action.

Famine applies real population loss and records the death cause once. Event 037 reduces provenance by the proportional mysterious share of applied loss. It does not add a second death entry called overpopulation.

## Migration connection

Event 037 can request voluntary internal settlement, controlled redistribution, reception, resettlement, evacuation, family reunification, forced relocation, deportation, and return.

Migration retains ownership of Displacement Load, Reception Capacity, Border Policy, cohorts, routes, corridors, trapped people, reception, transit, settlement, return, route deaths, forced-displacement deaths, decisions, missions, and map mode.

Every request includes proof of mysterious composition. Migration returns applied survivors and deaths. Event 037 adjusts origin and destination provenance from applied results only.

Event 037 can recommend low-pressure destinations, but Migration chooses from verified food safety, reception, route, and policy facts.

A closed border creates trapped people. It does not reduce Event 037 population. Violent pushback and unsafe return can create deaths through Migration.

## Deaths connection

Every real death among Event 037 populations enters the shared Deaths system through the owner that caused it.

Causes can include famine, forced displacement, camp mortality, systematic killing, bombing, conventional harm, chemical or biological warfare, nuclear attack, disease, natural disaster, cannibalism, and other event-owned loss.

Event 037 records provenance share after the applied loss. It does not replace the cause with a generic Mysterious People reason.

An Event 037 summary can show derived lifetime deaths, but those totals cannot become a second Chaos source.

## Camp and Repression connection

Camp and Repression owns forced-labor sites, concentration, extermination, evidence, discovery, reform, and site processing.

Event 037 contributes one possible targeted cohort: living mysterious population in the state.

Camp and Repression retains ownership of site creation, capacity, labor, mortality, killing, concealment, inspection, occupation discovery, evidence, reform, closure, decisions, and interface.

Event 037 receives applied death, transfer, release, and survivor facts so provenance remains correct.

## Condemnation connection

Public persecution contributes to existing atrocity and cover-up sources.

Possible sources include abusive closed zones, exposed relocation deaths, forced labor, systematic killing, destroyed records, blocked inspections, cover-ups, and repeated coercive policy.

Hidden evidence remains hidden until the owner reveals it. Event 037 should not make every restrictive policy immediately public when evidence rules say otherwise.

Condemnation consequences remain shared, including monitoring, sanctions, embargo pressure, support restrictions, and isolation.

## Resistance and politics

Event 037 pressure affects politics through concrete conditions:

- unregistered communities resist coercive registration
- forced movement creates resistance
- food failure creates unrest
- fair integration improves legitimacy
- unequal relief creates regional conflict
- a mysterious majority becomes a political constituency
- camps and killing create survivor resistance and foreign support

Use existing resistance, stability, ideology, government, and civil-conflict systems. Do not create a universal Event 037 civil war at one threshold.

## Occupation and controller responsibility

The controller normally owns immediate food distribution, movement permission, security, emergency housing, local registration, camps, and relief access.

The legal owner can retain claims, exile representation, historical responsibility for earlier policy, and relief or return demands supported by existing systems.

State provenance remains through control changes. Human players receive reports only when they can act or are materially affected.

## Special and nonhuman countries

Actual nonhuman countries do not use ordinary Event 037 civilian systems. States owned and controlled only by such actors are excluded unless the owner system proves that ordinary human civilians remain and can be processed safely.

Special but human scenario countries can remain eligible when they use normal civilian systems.

Shared civilian and nonhuman classifiers decide the boundary. Event 037 should not create a competing tag list.

When a normal country becomes nonhuman, ordinary decisions close, pending requests fail closed or transfer correctly, and human population is handled through the transforming system's contract. Event 037 does not silently delete provenance.

## Natural disasters

Disasters treat arrivals as ordinary civilians. They can kill a proportional mysterious share, damage support capacity, create Famine vulnerability, create Migration requests, destroy settlements, and reduce ability to absorb later manifestations.

Event 037 does not grant disaster immunity or a separate shelter mechanic.

## Disease and biological systems

High density and weak services can increase vulnerability through existing outbreak rules.

Disease systems own outbreak state, spread, mortality, quarantine, treatment, weapon effects, and contamination. Event 037 can provide density, movement, integration, and service conditions.

The newcomers are not inherently diseased.

## Chemical warfare, bombing, and nuclear attacks

Event 037 populations are ordinary civilians under attack.

Attack owners determine exposure, protection, deaths, fallout, contamination, building damage, displacement, Condemnation, and Chaos. Event 037 receives applied loss and updates provenance once.

## Cannibalism and population-consuming threats

Population-consuming events can target Event 037 states because arrivals are real civilians.

Untargeted consumption reduces provenance proportionally. Explicit targeting requires proof and exact accounting. Event origins remain separate.

## Population-restoring and population-losing events

Event 037 can partly reverse demographic damage by creating new people in surviving inhabited states. It does not resurrect named victims or restore erased communities as the same people.

A state below the inhabited threshold receives no grant until population rises enough.

Later growth does not erase historical Deaths totals.

## Economy and resources

Resource discoveries, industrial booms, and construction surges can help support population.

Resource-rich states can attract settlement. Industrial booms absorb workers while worsening housing. Depression removes employment. Embargos restrict food and construction. Equipment windfalls can support logistics when relevant.

Each owner keeps its own mechanics. Event 037 reads resulting capacity.

## War and manpower

Integrated Event 037 population can support larger armed forces. Benefits follow real population and integration.

War worsens crisis through mobilization, transport requisition, destroyed housing, blockade, refugees, occupation, closed borders, and persecution.

The player faces a real choice between military use and support investment.

## Event clusters

A cluster firing calls Event 037 once.

It preserves one global transaction, one history record, one repeatable cap change, one set of direct Chaos milestone checks, and normal post-transaction reports.

Other anomaly members cannot modify Event 037 provenance without an explicit contract.

## Multiplayer

World state is shared:

- population creation applies once
- ledgers and pressure are deterministic shared state
- each human receives relevant reports
- one player's option cannot reverse the transaction
- national policy remains country-owned
- agreements can involve several humans
- host or first player does not own the anomaly
- save and reload preserves policies and selected targets

No duplicate delayed report may reapply the firing.

## Performance model

Event 037 is global at the moment it fires. One bounded world pass is central.

Recurring processing remains sparse:

- register only states with living mysterious population or an active consequence
- register only countries with population, policy, mission, request, or consequence
- update pressure through bounded active registries
- update totals incrementally
- remove resolved entries
- refresh after ownership, migration, famine, and atrocity changes
- reserve full reconciliation for repair and debug use

The design does not require daily or monthly scanning of every state. It does not require one event per state.

## Shared-system acceptance statement

A connection succeeds when ownership remains clear. Event 037 creates people and remembers provenance. Famine determines food crisis. Migration determines movement. Deaths records mortality. Camps determine site abuse and killing. Condemnation determines public diplomatic consequences. Every transaction changes real population once and provenance once.
