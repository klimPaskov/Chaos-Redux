# Event 037: Mysterious People

## Part 6: Connections to shared Chaos Redux systems

## Ownership principle

Event 037 owns population creation at a manifestation, the newcomer ledger, settlement, Overpopulation Pressure, the Newcomers modifier, its stance, actions, mission, events, achievements, and its direct Chaos milestones.
Every other consequence belongs to the system that already owns it.
Event 037 sends requests through those systems' public entry points and never writes their ledgers.

| System | Connection | Owner keeps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Famine | Hotspots at state pressure `60+` submit a food-need component through `famine_apply_pressure_request` (Part 3). Event 037 reads the committed famine stage for its state pressure addition and settlement penalty. | Food Security, Food Reserves, Relief Access, stages, decisions, missions, mortality, map mode |
| Migration | Hotspots at state pressure `80+` request flight through `migration_request_flight_pressure` (Part 3). | Displacement Load, Reception Capacity, Border Policy, cohorts, routes, trapped people, reception, return, route deaths |
| Civilian transfer primitive | One Event 037 hook in finalization blends the destination's newcomer share and removes measured departures from the origin's unsettled backlog (Part 2). | Origin debit, route deaths, survivor credit, conservation |
| Deaths and the Chaos Meter | None beyond the direct milestones in Part 1. Newcomer deaths are ordinary civilian deaths recorded by the owner that caused them. | Death records, death reasons, Chaos from deaths |
| Shared civilian classifiers | `humanitarian_state_is_valid` decides which states receive newcomers (Part 1). | Special and nonhuman country rules |
| Event system and Event Logs | One history entry per firing, three evolutions, Event Details (Part 7). | Selection, repeatable weight, cluster dispatch, logging framework |

## Proportional losses need no adapters

Because the ledger stores a share (Part 2), every untargeted loss already removes newcomers in proportion.
Famine, bombing, nuclear attack, chemical and biological attack, disease, natural disasters, cannibalism, camps, war, and fallout need no Event 037 callback and no change to their own scripts.
Their deaths stay recorded under their own reasons.

## No origin-targeted actions

Event 037 offers no action that kills, imprisons, deports, or confines people because of their origin.
The shared camp system never offers a protected-class target selector, and its target pools are territorial and political.
Camps, gulags, and genocide routes that operate in a state with newcomers take people in proportion, like any other loss.
Persecution appears as politics: the Restricted Registration stance, the origin register in `chaosx.nr37.24`, and the flight that Breakdown produces.
The question of whether origin-targeted routes should exist is recorded in the README's open questions.

## Systems with no direct connection

- Condemnation: Event 037 creates no atrocity, so it adds no Condemnation source.
  Condemnation from camps or famine in newcomer states comes from those owners.
- CBRN, fallout, and Air Cleanliness: their population losses reach the ledger through the share, and their food effects reach Event 037 through the famine stage.
- World threat: no connection.

## Occupation and controller responsibility

National pressure, the Newcomers modifier, and the mission belong to the state's owner.
Occupied states settle at half speed (Part 2), and projects need the state to be owned and controlled by the acting country.
The ledger survives every change of control.

## Multiplayer

The world transaction applies once on the host.
Ledgers, counts, and pressure are shared state.
Each human player receives their own manifestation report and flavour events, and nothing in a report or option can reapply the firing.

## Shared-system acceptance statement

A connection succeeds when ownership stays clear.
Event 037 creates people and tracks their settlement, Famine decides hunger, Migration decides movement, Deaths records mortality, and every transaction changes real population once.
