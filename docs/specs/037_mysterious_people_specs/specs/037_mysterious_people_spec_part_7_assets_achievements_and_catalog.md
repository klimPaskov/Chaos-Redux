# Event 037: Mysterious People

## Part 7: Assets, achievements, and catalog alignment

## Visual identity

Event 037 should look ordinary enough to make the anomaly credible. Its visual language is documentary, municipal, domestic, and crowded. It should avoid portals, alien silhouettes, cloning equipment, science-fiction machinery, supernatural beams, and explicit alternate-reality imagery.

Useful subjects include complete villages beside familiar roads, families entering furnished districts, municipal officials facing contradictory records, schoolchildren in established settlements, dense housing around factories or farms, and overlapping deeds or photographs as secondary props.

People and settlement life should remain the main subject. Documents can support the scene without becoming the whole composition.

## Achievement set

Three difficult achievements are planned. IDs are stable working identifiers. Final title and description wording belongs to implementation.

### `chaos_redux_037_room_for_everyone`

Working title direction: Room for Everyone.

Intent:

Reward a country that makes a large Event 037 population part of ordinary life without solving pressure through death or expulsion.

Required outcome:

- at least `25%` of player-country population is living Event 037 mysterious population
- national pressure remains below `40` for `365` consecutive days
- no controlled Event 037 state has severe or catastrophic Famine during the timer
- player completes at least one housing or services project and one integration mission
- player has not used an Event 037-targeted atrocity action
- player has not caused Event 037-targeted forced-displacement deaths during the timer

Icon direction:

- dense but orderly city or town around an open civic building
- capacity and inclusion

### `chaos_redux_037_a_billion_more`

Working title direction: A Billion More.

Intent:

Reward successful management after Evolution III transforms world demographics.

Required outcome:

- Evolution III active
- living Event 037 population worldwide reaches `1,000,000,000`
- player country contains at least `100,000,000` living mysterious people
- national pressure remains below `60` for `180` consecutive days
- no controlled Event 037 state remains at Breakdown during the timer
- no active Event 037-targeted camp, extermination, violent border, or unsafe forced return
- player completes a national food or settlement program

Icon direction:

- immense crowd integrated into cities, farms, and industry
- world scale without globe-only composition

### `chaos_redux_037_three_censuses`

Working title direction: Three Censuses.

Intent:

Reward consistent management across three manifestations.

Required outcome:

- player experiences three Event 037 firings while controlling a valid state
- after each firing, player completes one meaningful support mission before the next
- no player-controlled Event 037 state reaches catastrophic Famine during the sequence
- no violent pushback, unsafe forced return, forced labor, or systematic killing during the sequence
- third firing ends with pressure below Emergency

Valid political continuation should retain progress under shared achievement rules.

Icon direction:

- three layered census books or household rolls paired with growing settlement
- no readable generated text

## Event Details direction

Event Details should explain worldwide appearance, real population increase, repeatability, possible long-term support pressure, and unresolved origin.

It should not expose exact formulas, internal variable names, AI weights, unrevealed atrocity paths, implementation history, or a definitive explanation.

Evolution previews describe changed public situation and management challenge. They should not read like modifier lists.
