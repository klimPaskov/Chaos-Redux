## `acid_rain_033_before_the_first_drop`

- Title direction: complete national civil defense before the first corrosive rain arrives.
- Description direction: explain that all four components must reach tier 4 before any controlled state receives its opening shock.
- Eligible countries: any country controlling at least one frozen eligible state at formation.
- Unlock: reach 100 Preparedness before the first national opening exposure receipt.
- Disqualifier: any controlled state records its opening shock first.
- Difficulty and reason: very high because the event can start near the player and full preparation requires sixteen paid project tiers.
- Visibility: visible from formation.
- Icon: fully shielded city beneath the first falling droplets.
- Tracking: component completion dates, first national opening-shock date, event generation.

## `acid_rain_033_shelters_held`

- Title direction: a shelter network survives a severe cell without mass casualties.
- Description direction: state the three-state minimum, two-pulse minimum, protection requirement, and 0.05 percent casualty ceiling.
- Eligible countries: a country responsible for at least three states in one severe episode.
- Unlock: complete one severe cell with at least two pulses and cumulative deaths below 0.05 percent of the combined forecast-time population.
- Disqualifier: no target state has a valid shelter protocol or evacuation receipt.
- Difficulty and reason: high because the player must prepare before the forecast closes and protect several states under the same episode.
- Visibility: visible.
- Icon: shelter door resisting dense rust-red rain.
- Tracking: episode ID, frozen population baseline, target states, actual applied state-population deaths, shelter and evacuation receipts.

- Tracking: forecast ID, severe episode ID, state ID, completion date, first-pulse date, state baseline, and actual applied state-population deaths.
