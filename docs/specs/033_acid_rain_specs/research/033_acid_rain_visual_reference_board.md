## Combined visual brief

### Formation super-event

Combine references 1, 4, 6, and 10. Show a city of stained stone and metal under a dark corrosive storm, with civilians and wardens moving into shelters. Keep the view wide enough to show scale.

### Severe-cell report

Combine references 1, 6, 7, and 8. Show heavy rain, damaged street surfaces, a masked warden, and civilians being directed away from the exposed area.

### Protected water and food

Combine references 9 and 10 with period reservoir, pump, drum, and sealed-ration forms. Avoid a modern industrial water plant.

### Environmental aftermath

Combine references 1, 2, and 3. Use dead vegetation, blackened stone, rust streaks, damaged rails, and contaminated runoff.

### Icon language

Use the mask silhouette from reference 8, the oilskin outline from reference 6, the shelter geometry from references 5 and 10, and the eroded stone texture from reference 1. Simplify heavily at 32 by 32 and 64 by 64.

## Prohibited visual shortcuts

- neon green rain
- modern hazmat suits
- modern plastic water tanks
- science-fiction weather satellites
- skull clouds as the main motif
- clean modern hospital rooms
- direct photo collage without license review
- unreadable text inside icons
