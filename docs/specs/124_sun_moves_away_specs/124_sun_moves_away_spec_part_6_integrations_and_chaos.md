# Part 6: Cross-event behavior, shared consequences and Chaos

## One global episode, several consequence owners

Sun Moves Away owns the abnormal world condition, its exposure history, allocation commitments, regional incident centers, evolution progress and Return transaction.
It uses the existing owners for food security, actual civilian losses, population movement, diplomacy, equipment, construction and canonical creature families.
The boundaries matter because a real loss or delivery must happen exactly once even when several systems describe it.

| Surface | Sun responsibility | Existing owner responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World darkness and all-hour night | Activate and remove one reversible episode contribution | Supported engine presentation and combat consumers |
| Cold exposure | Establish state-specific environmental and service facts | Supported modifiers and registered state lifecycles |
| Famine | Submit proven affected-population pressure and lawful relief facts | Food Security, Food Reserves, Relief Access, warning stages, starvation and recovery |
| Direct cold or attack deaths | Identify the physical cause, exposed cohort and one consequence receipt | Exact observed population loss, protected floor, manpower reconciliation and Deaths accounting |
| Displacement | Identify a real cause, requested cohort, lawful actors and route | Migration request validation, reception, physical transfer, route losses and settlement |
| Aid cargo | Select an affordable, lawful offer and operating plan | Exact sender debit, dispatch, delivery and recipient credit |
| Canonical nocturnal actors | Request a temporary darkness delta for verified living eligible holders | Family identity, actual survivors, recruitment, baseline bonuses and retirement |
| Buildings and transport | Identify Sun-attributed actual damage or repair work | Supported damage, construction and supply consumers |
| Shared history and settings | Publish the actual accepted episode, stages and resolution | Major pacing, event history, evolution history, settings and selectors |

The current source evidence for these owners is linked in the [research record](124_sun_moves_away_research_and_sources.md).
Exact helper inputs and native syntax belong to the implementation handoff after current owner contracts are inspected.

## Heat Wave: strict exclusion in both directions

The active world cannot contain both Event 051 Heat Wave and Event 124 Sun Moves Away.
The rule applies to ordinary selection, direct entry, linked callbacks, debug or settings dispatch, cluster force paths, manual scenarios and already saved active state.
Filtering an event out of a candidate pool is insufficient protection because other routes can bypass that pool.

### Entry behavior

Both owner entry transactions recheck the other owner's active condition before committing effects, history, costs, presentation or episode receipts.
If an incumbent valid crisis is active, the attempted start rejects without partial activation.
The already active crisis continues normally.
The rejected event does not consume a fire-once history opportunity or log a successful evolution.

When both start attempts arrive in the same update, the first valid owner commit becomes the incumbent.
The second recheck sees that committed state and rejects.
The rule must be deterministic across multiplayer clients.
Manual setup explains the actual conflict and does not silently end Heat Wave to launch Sun.

### A legacy save already contains both

Use trustworthy activation receipts to preserve the earlier valid episode and retire the later conflicting owner's temporary effects through its own cleanup.
If trusted order proves Heat Wave was active first, the inconsistent Sun activation is rolled back as an invalid episode through a dedicated reconciliation path.
That path is not a successful natural Return and cannot earn Return presentation, achievements or Chaos relief.
If Sun was first, retire Heat Wave's temporary layer while preserving its actual historical harm.

If activation receipts are absent, contradictory or tied, preserve Sun to honor its uninterrupted-darkness requirement and retire only Heat Wave-owned temporary effects.
Record a migration diagnostic identifying the uncertain legacy state and the selected owner.
The cleanup does not refund historical heat deaths, damage or famine.
It does not remove independent weather or another event's unrelated modifiers.

The current live Heat Wave root grants a two-year country idea and has no Sun guard in the inspected entry or idea.
Its proposed rework is a different source surface and does not establish this required cross-owner behavior.
The implementation must therefore update the actual current owner and its reachable entry paths, with the proposed rework reconciled if it is promoted later.

## Human systems and actual population

Environmental effects reach every state.
Humanitarian mutations require the exact current owner eligibility, which is more specific than a generic human or special-country label.
The inspected humanitarian gate accepts valid normal-civilian countries that are ordinary countries or Holy Realm, with a valid positive-population state owner and controller.
It cannot be bypassed simply by claiming a special country is human.

A currently unsupported human owner remains affected by the global environment and valid human modifiers.
If its food or displacement integration requires broader support, implement an explicit owner-reviewed contract before claiming that branch complete.
Do not toggle human classification, forge actor proof or build a second famine ledger to evade the boundary.
Pure nonhuman populations use their actual owning systems and do not create human starvation records.

### Famine

The current state endpoint `famine_request_event_pressure` accepts a proven request through the famine owner.
It converts an accepted affected-people amount into pressure and registers work.
It does not kill or move those people.
The inspected gateway does not supply a Sun episode or replay guard, so Sun reserves an in-flight attempt against one dated cause receipt before publishing the request.
The caller initializes its result storage, then finalizes that receipt only when the existing `famine_pressure_request_result` reports acceptance.
A rejected attempt preserves the same cause identity and may retry after its invalid proof is corrected, without adding pressure or inventing a fresh cause.
An uncertain outcome requires reconciliation against the owner's actual accepted state before any retry and fails closed when that proof is unavailable.

Agricultural exposure, route failure and household demand must refer to distinguishable factual contributions.
One failed delivery cannot be repeatedly submitted as newly lost harvest, lost food and lost supply for the same interval.
Pressure scales with actual affected residents and the duration and severity of the service failure, within the owner's supported units and bounds.
An unaccepted request creates no fictional outcome and remains a visible integration defect until its cause is resolved.

Famine owns reserve use, warnings, starvation and recovery.
Sun does not apply another population loss when the famine owner already recorded a death.
Food relief pays the current owner quote and improves only the food or access actually delivered.
Ordinary settlement lighting does not produce food.

### Direct losses

Direct cold exposure and verified predator attacks can cause losses outside starvation.
Every consequence identifies the actual state, valid actor, cause, requested maximum and protected population floor.
The exact state-loss owner measures actual population change and reconciles recruitable-manpower side effects under its supported contract.
Only the observed positive loss is eligible for the Deaths ledger.

A request for 500 deaths that applies only 120 cannot produce a 500-person report, a 500-person Chaos contribution or an additional 380-person debit later merely to match the original request.
A zero applied loss creates no death record.
Settings that suppress Deaths logging do not convert a real physical loss into proof that nobody died.
Achievement and presentation receipts must retain allowed observed physical facts without bypassing the owner's logging settings.

### Migration and rescue

Migration requests describe pressure or a proposed move.
They are not proof that a person has changed state.
The physical transfer owner debits the origin, treats route deaths as a portion of that debit, and credits survivors once.
The conservation relationship is actual origin debit equals route deaths plus actual destination credit.

An evacuation needs lawful origin and destination proof, custody, a feasible route, actual reception and a unique cohort identity.
The same people cannot be counted as rescued again through a different window, split shipment or subsequent return journey.
Reception capacity remains synchronized with actual received people.
Return of the Sun does not erase a living displaced cohort or automatically move it home.

## Actual nocturnal survivors

Temporary advantages require three separate proofs: a genuinely darkness-adapted family, a real surviving holder or population, and a removable supported effect in that holder's scope.
The presence of a battalion definition or old event flag supplies none of the missing proofs by itself.

| Situation | Required treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Living ordinary zombie outbreak | Verify its current owner and surviving units or population, then apply only the eligible Sun delta |
| Weaponized outbreak or derivative | Respect its separate family and provider ownership, with no borrowed parent progression or recruitment |
| Living Wendigo actor or preserved pack | Inspect its actual existing overlay and units, preserving owner-specific winter and other bonuses |
| Human army with a verified creature family | Restrict the removable effect to eligible units or supported family scope |
| Defeated actor with surviving definition or history | Grant nothing and create nothing |
| Vampire owner not implemented or unverified | Leave the conditional compatibility dormant until a real owner, survivors and API exist |
| New regional predator center from Evolution II | Use the event-owned incident population and containment record, separately from canonical actor identity |

The inspected zombie weaponized packages already contain substantial family-specific night-attack values.
All-hour night conditions can make those existing strengths continuously relevant.
The new event delta therefore needs a stacking review with actual native semantics and a matching before/after holder.
It must not become a blanket buff to an entire mixed army or every special country.

Permanent `add_unit_bonus` grants and technology unlock removal do not meet the temporary cleanup contract.
A pure eligible creature country may use its counterpart within the event's existing three spirit slots when its entire beneficiary scope is valid.
Mixed holders require a supported exact carrier.
If the installed engine or owner cannot supply one, report the missing behavior explicitly and preserve the design requirement.

Removing Sun must preserve the actor's original ideas, technology, equipment, family membership and independent winter.
Retiring an actual actor during the crisis removes its Sun binding at that time.
Later existence checks cannot recreate it for cleanup, an achievement or a delayed callback.

## Costs, service time and ownership changes

Every payment is attached to a valid actor, episode, target and generation before mutation.
The quote, affordability test, debit and refund agree on the same selected scope and current discount rules.
At most four distinct spendable cost types belong to one action, with retained reserves shown separately from payment.
A running native construction reservation is not charged a second time as if it were still free capacity.

Service benefits are measured through elapsed functioning intervals.
Starting and ending between two consequence updates still produces the correct amount of service.
Purchasing a five-day benefit just after a slow consequence pulse cannot make it useless or extend it for the whole interval.
Unpaid renewal ends future service at the correct boundary and cannot consume resources the country does not possess.

Capture leaves the physical shelter or repaired route in place.
Its operating commitment is revalidated against the new controller, route, recipient and payer.
The former payer receives only a lawful unspent-reservation release and cannot reclaim consumed fuel or installed work.
Country release and civil war create new holder identities, so inherited registration markers cannot be mistaken for the new country's own receipt.

Ordinary exposure cadence belongs to the state and episode.
Changing control cannot accelerate the next harm pulse or reset accumulated untreated exposure.
Delayed choices must verify their exact incident generation and do nothing after supersession or Return.

## Bounded global work

Initial activation and immediate Return cover the whole affected world because the feature is global.
Ongoing work uses registered affected states, active countries, centers, paid projects and consequence receipts.
It does not repeatedly rediscover every country and state through a new global daily scan.

The Return hazard needs one authoritative episode evaluation for its elapsed interval.
It does not require one independent roll per country.
State harm and service projections update through bounded owner queues and changes to their relevant dependencies.
Any batching preserves unvisited queue suffixes, valid dates and pending acknowledgements.
An unrelated loop limit cannot justify silently leaving late states unaffected.

GUI refresh reads live state or synchronously published projections without advancing time, paying resources or mutating population.
Paused changes to stock, route, target, policy or control must update price and eligibility immediately.
Global presentation aggregates update from actual registered changes and remain available to every open panel.

## Chaos impact map

The event separates the abnormal global condition from physical losses that already belong to shared accounting.
The table gives proposed event-owned magnitude anchors.
The implementation must inspect generic event awards and owner conversions before applying them, so these targets never stack accidentally with an equivalent shared award.

| Milestone or cause | Direction and intended magnitude | Dynamic factors and proof | Repetition and overlap control | Reversal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A genuine global manifestation | Target +20 abnormal-crisis Chaos | Requires committed world darkness, all-hour night and environmental episode | Once per actual episode, reduce any new award by an equivalent generic manifestation award already applied | Return has its separate limited relief, with no refund of real damage |
| Eligibility, opening strength selection or evolution activation | 0 | Thresholds, toggles and actual history entries | Never a reward for setting a flag, logging or unlocking a decision | None |
| Sustained widespread essential-service failure | +10 at each newly reached 20%, 40% and 60% affected world-population threshold, maximum +30 | Residents have failed essential delivery for at least 30 consecutive days, using actual service receipts and a fixed episode denominator | Each newly crossed threshold pays once, with no second premium for the same failure labeled as refuge collapse | Later verified service recovery can earn its own limited reduction |
| Organized predatory route denial | +5 when 3, 6 and 12 distinct state corridors have each been denied for at least 15 days, maximum +15 | Actual living center or eligible external nocturnal actor, effective denial and exposed service demand | State and corridor identity persist across borders, one shared incident cannot count repeatedly, this premium concerns organized reach | Containment removes the cause, with reduction only through verified service restoration |
| Direct cold, attack or famine deaths | 0 additional Sun award | Actual observed deaths and their correct reason reach the shared owner | The existing death conversion counts once, never requested loss and never a second manual death-to-Chaos increment | Deaths remain historical facts |
| Displacement and reception pressure | 0 additional Sun award | Actual migration and cohort facts through its owner | A request is not a move, and any existing migration Chaos is retained without a duplicate event premium | Shared recovery rules apply |
| Actual building damage, wars or territory changes | 0 additional Sun award | Their current generic owner facts | No new event award duplicates existing destruction, war or annexation accounting | Repair does not erase historical losses |
| Verified restoration of essential service for previously exposed residents | -5 at newly sustained recovery of 20%, 40% and 60% of the fixed episode world population, maximum -15 | Those residents had registered failed service and then receive at least 90 days of functioning protection and delivery | Each resident cohort contributes once, policy changes, gifts, repeated clicks and alternate countries cannot recycle a receipt | Later failure can have real consequences, but does not reopen the same reward threshold |
| Return of the Sun | Up to -10 | One genuine successful resolution after a real committed manifestation | Once, bounded by the abnormal-crisis contribution actually awarded, no relief from invalid-start rollback or debug cleanup | Does not refund deaths, damage, famine or displacement |
| Ordinary aftermath repair or report acknowledgement | 0 additional Sun award | Actual completion goes to its existing owner or achievement proof | No repeatable repair-click or popup farm | Existing owner recovery remains valid |

The fixed world denominator is the eligible human population recorded at valid manifestation.
The implementation records later lawful new population separately and uses an agreed owner extension for material additions without shrinking the denominator through cession, conversion or death.
Only currently eligible living residents can supply new service-time evidence.
Deaths cannot raise a service-recovery share by removing the people who were hardest to protect.

Chaos thresholds describe verified worldwide consequences and do not become a third public national progress meter.
Their history entries explain the actual cause qualitatively.
If a milestone combines several previously unclaimed thresholds in one update, it claims each eligible receipt once with a single coherent accounting transaction.

The service-failure premium concerns sustained inability to maintain essential life, while the predatory premium concerns organized regional reach.
If a single fact is being used to claim both without independent evidence, assign it to the one best-supported cause.
Famine mortality and physical destruction remain with their own ledgers in either case.

Chaos settings and owner guards retain authority.
The event never bypasses disabled accounting by manually changing the meter through a second route.
The [AI and balance part](124_sun_moves_away_spec_part_5_ai_balance.md) must compare these cumulative contributions alongside the shared consequences.
