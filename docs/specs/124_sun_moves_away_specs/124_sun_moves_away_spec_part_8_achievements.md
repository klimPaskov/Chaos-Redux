Their identifiers are symbolic event-owned tracking handles pending inspection of the current achievement registry and other active reservations.
All thresholds in this part are proposed tuning anchors accepted for package review.
Final names, descriptions and localisation remain a separate writing task.

## 2. Cohorts, time and abuse resistance

At entry, record eligible population cohorts, state identities, lawful ownership and control, rural classification, industrial capacity and real overseas dependencies through their current owners.
Minimum population means affected eligible human residents, with humanitarian country and state preflights applied wherever the action requires them.
Population transfers update the proof only through lawful physical receipts that preserve cohort identity and eliminate double counting.
Releasing, ceding or abandoning poorly protected states cannot remove their residents from an achievement denominator.
Death and conversion also leave the fixed entry denominator unchanged, following [Part 6](124_sun_moves_away_spec_part_6_integrations_and_chaos.md).
Only currently eligible living residents can provide new service-time evidence.
Later lawful population additions remain separately recorded until an agreed owner extension admits their contribution.
When a successor takes responsibility, the shared owner must confirm the successor and continuing service before any interval receives credit.
An unsupported transfer leaves the interval unverified and preserves the original cohort in the audit record.

Sustained national thresholds use the entry cohort and its receipt-updated lawful locations, so an improved current dashboard caused solely by territorial loss cannot manufacture success.
State conditions apply the same essential-service definition as Civilian Protection, including the weakest-service constraint.
Delivery uses actual supported demand and received quantities from the corridor owner.
Purchased routes, planned capacity, selected policy and equipment definitions provide no delivery or protection proof by themselves.

Measure continuous duration from actual service intervals, including interruptions that begin and end between consequence pulses.
Any failed required condition resets its continuous window, while earlier receipts remain available for audit.
Missing evidence earns no time and cannot be bridged by a later favorable snapshot.
Each due-window delivery counts once, regardless of how many callbacks or cargo fragments report it.
A cohort's contribution remains bound to its original episode and achievement claim across successor and alternate-country paths.
Self-inflicted damage, artificial incident creation and repeated circulation of the same civilians or supplies cannot create qualifying scale.

Return proceeds immediately under [Part 2](124_sun_moves_away_spec_part_2_evolutions_and_return.md).
An early Return leaves unmet crisis-duration achievements incomplete and never delays resolution.
Recovery eligibility uses the resolved episode's fixed history and grants as soon as its real work is complete.
Only a genuine successful Return unlocks recovery proof, and invalid-start rollback or debug cleanup cannot supply it.

## 3. Sustained distributed rural protection

### ACH124_RURAL_PROTECTION

The working concept rewards a country that keeps dispersed rural communities functioning throughout a long crisis.
The country must enter through ordinary eligible play and have at least 1,000,000 affected rural residents across at least three qualifying states.
Rural classification comes from a documented existing state-category or geography mapping fixed at entry.
An absent classification mapping is an implementation evidence gap.

Success requires 180 consecutive crisis days under the Distributed Civilian Relief priority.
During that interval, cohort-assessed national Civilian Protection stays at or above 80 and Lifeline Reliability stays at or above 75.
Each of the three qualifying rural states must also maintain protection at or above 80 for its tracked residents.
All warmth, shelter, food access, safety and routine capacity counted in protection must actually function.
Existing ordinary services can qualify when their owners supply the required proof.

A priority switch, service interruption below threshold, invalid reception transfer or early Return ends the qualifying window.
Moving exposed residents out of the tracked denominator, temporary unrecorded protection and artificial population grants disqualify the affected proof.
The tracker stores the rural cohort mapping, interval intersections, policy history, service minima and corridor receipts.
Completion can commit during the crisis when the full interval closes.

The icon brief uses a worn woollen mitten shielding a small farm lantern, with two distant roof silhouettes establishing distributed settlement life.
The lantern remains the dominant shape at native size, and the achievement template supplies the surrounding state treatment.

## 4. Scarce-capacity or island lifelines

### ACH124_SCARCE_LIFELINES

The working concept rewards dependable essential delivery despite a genuinely constrained starting position.
The country must have at least 500,000 affected eligible residents and satisfy one entry qualification.
Qualification A is five or fewer owned civilian factories at entry, counting capacity already damaged or reserved so deliberate idling cannot create eligibility.
Qualification B is a verified overseas lifeline on which at least 500,000 tracked eligible residents actually depend.
The relevant factory-count and dependency definitions must be bound to current owner evidence before implementation.

Success requires 120 consecutive crisis days with cohort-assessed Civilian Protection at least 70 and Lifeline Reliability at least 90.
At least six distinct genuine delivery due windows must close successfully during that interval.
Each qualifying window meets its owner-assessed essential delivery requirement with physical received quantities.
Existing trade, ordinary routes and paid emergency convoys can contribute through the same receipt standard.
Splitting one shipment, cycling the same stock or buying unused route capacity creates no additional successful window.

A failed essential delivery, loss of the applicable service thresholds, unsupported route proof or early Return resets or ends the duration attempt.
Country splits and factory reservations preserve the entry eligibility snapshot.
The tracker retains the frozen constraint, dependent cohorts, delivery requirements, received quantities and interval minima.
The grant commits during the crisis when both duration and six-window proof are complete.

The icon brief uses a weathered harbor bollard holding a taut rope that leads toward a small distant cargo vessel.
The composition emphasizes a dependable connection across darkness, with one readable rope curve and restrained water detail.

## 5. Paid Endless Night refuge continuity

### ACH124_PAID_REFUGES

The working concept rewards a large refuge service that remains physically funded and effective through prolonged Endless Night.
The country must have Evolution III active and operate at least three distinct lit refuges in three states.
At least 1,000,000 unique eligible residents must receive sustained functioning refuge services throughout the qualifying interval.
Capacity is bounded by each site's weakest essential service and real reception limits.

Success requires 180 consecutive days after Evolution III activation with cohort-assessed Civilian Protection and Lifeline Reliability both at least 85.
All required refuge fuel and equipment upkeep must be physically paid, and construction or maintenance reservations must remain valid.
The applicable build and sustain receipts come from A14 and A15 in [Part 4](124_sun_moves_away_spec_part_4_decisions_missions.md).
The 180-day proof spans separately authorized A15 operating periods and evaluates their complete service intervals.
Persistent proof includes actual occupied maintenance factory-days and reservation-release receipts for each authorized operating period.
Supplier promises, decorative illumination and an initial construction payment cannot establish operating continuity.

An unpaid interval, invalid resource provenance, failed essential service, excessive reception load or closure below the three-site and population thresholds resets the window.
Lawful aid can maintain the network through actual delivered quantities.
Reducing a refuge's served area preserves prior expenditure and immediately reduces the capacity eligible for future proof.
Early Return closes an unfinished attempt without extending the crisis.
Persistent proof includes every site's capacity, served cohorts, payments, reservation periods, interruptions and shared food-access status.

The icon brief uses a maintained generator beneath a lit communal building, with a visible fuel line linking the two forms.
The building and generator read as one compact service system and avoid a luminous city skyline that hides the upkeep theme.

## 6. Containment of verified living threats

### ACH124_LIVING_CONTAINMENT

The working concept rewards ending organized danger after real encounters with established living nocturnal actors or Event 124 regional predators.
The eligible country must protect an affected cohort of at least 500,000 residents across three qualifying localities.
At least three distinct source-owned incidents must have verified living threat evidence before the player's successful intervention.
Canonical actor history, an available unit definition or an extinct population cannot supply that evidence.

Success requires an episode at least 120 days old and 60 consecutive crisis days without an eligible organized incursion from the tracked sources into the protected localities.
The interval begins after the third qualifying incident has a successful legitimate containment or elimination outcome.
Sources may remain restrained in place or be legitimately destroyed through ordinary play.
The achievement never requires maintaining a dangerous actor alive or exterminating an entire canonical species.
Any continuing security or containment commitment needed to maintain the outcome must remain funded and physically valid.

A renewed eligible organized incursion resets the 60-day window.
Manufactured encounters, resurrected actors, duplicate subordinate reports from one incident and unverified absence of attacks supply no qualifying proof.
A center's legitimate withdrawal or elimination requires its owner-confirmed final record.
Early Return ends an unfinished duration attempt.
The tracker stores source identity, pre-intervention survival proof, three independent incident receipts, protected cohorts and the complete absence-of-incursion interval.

The icon brief uses a closed reinforced gate carrying three deep claw marks, with a steady guard lamp on its interior side.
The gate conveys an ended breach and intact protection without requiring an unverified creature portrait or a trophy of an entire species.

## 7. Exact civilian rescue and evacuation

### ACH124_CIVILIAN_RESCUE

The working concept rewards a substantial number of people reaching verified safety through lawful rescue or evacuation.
The country must have an affected eligible pool of at least 500,000 people and remain in a normal active crisis for at least 90 days.
Success requires at least 100,000 unique people actually received alive through three independent delivered cohorts originating in at least two states.
The qualifying A11 or A18 missions must carry distinct underlying emergency or transfer identities.
Administrative splitting of one cohort into several journeys does not create additional independent cohorts.

Destination acceptance, lawful access and functioning mandatory reception services must be confirmed at actual arrival.
Existing destination residents consume capacity before the newly arrived cohort is credited.
Partial arrivals count only the actual received survivors, with unresolved or lost travelers excluded from the successful total.
A rescued person later evacuated again retains the same identity contribution and counts once.
The tracker records origin loss, transit, destination receipt, host acceptance and deduplicated final survival for each cohort.

Forced circular transfers, debug population, self-created displacement, invalid humanitarian eligibility and credit for an uncompleted departure disqualify the affected proof.
No civilian death is required for eligibility or success.
The scale and episode-age conditions must both be met before Return for this crisis achievement.
Outstanding lawful movement can continue through its shared owner afterward without postponing Return for the award.

The icon brief uses two hands guiding a bundled civilian across a gangway toward a clearly lit doorway.
The human figure remains dignified and small enough for the hands, threshold and safe arrival to define the composition.

## 8. Rebuilding after Return

### ACH124_RECOVERY

The working concept rewards completion of real repair and reopening obligations after daylight returns.
The resolved ordinary episode must have lasted at least 90 days and affected at least 500,000 eligible residents across three states.
Its immutable shared damage record must contain at least 10 infrastructure or factory level-equivalents of actual Sun-attributed damage caused through legitimate play.
Pre-existing damage, unrelated warfare and deliberate self-destruction do not enter this achievement's damage denominator.

At Return, freeze the repairable Sun damage set and the Sun-closed essential services that can lawfully reopen.
Success requires confirmed repair of at least 90 percent of that registered repairable damage and reopening of all registered reopenable essential services in the qualifying cohort.
When the authoritative repair unit is indivisible, round the required repaired amount upward.
Repair and reopening receipts come from A19 and A20 or the equivalent accepted shared owners.
The continuing effects of famine, displacement or an unrelated crisis retain their own authoritative conditions.

Destruction or cession of a damaged site cannot remove its obligation from the frozen denominator.
A lawful successor may provide the repair receipt only when the shared owner confirms continuity and the achievement framework accepts that actor relationship.
Cancelled building orders, promised repairs, a change of displayed owner and refunded reservations supply no completed repair credit.
The tracker retains the Return snapshot, actual damage provenance, valid successor links and completed repair and service receipts.
The achievement grants immediately when real completion satisfies the proof, with no additional post-Return waiting period.
Existing operational evidence required by A20 remains part of the reopening receipt.

The icon brief uses a mason's trowel beside a repaired railway arch with a narrow band of returning daylight above it.
The repaired structure remains the dominant subject, with visible old damage confined to an adjacent edge.

Use the exact supplied achievement templates and preserve the red-cross overlay unchanged at its native alignment.

Acceptance exercises every exact threshold, one value immediately below it, interrupted qualifying time, missing source proof, reload and duplicate callbacks.
It also exercises manual starts, debug-resource invalidation, territorial denominator laundering, extinct-threat claims, repeated civilian cohorts and early Return.
Recovery testing includes partially repaired levels, lawful successor proof and immediate grant after the final genuine reopening.
The parent reviews registry uniqueness, mode eligibility, grant idempotence and the match between described success and actual persistent proof.
A missing required grant, mode-eligibility, receipt or owner capability blocks implementation of the affected achievement.
Unavailable optional checks or user-owned native observation limit the evidence claims.
Completion does not require requesting or waiting for user live-game testing.
An achievement can be described as tested only for checks actually performed with recorded evidence.
