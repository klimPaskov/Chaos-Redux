# Part 4: Decisions, missions and paid commitments

## Response choices

The response system turns the two values in [part 1](124_sun_moves_away_spec_part_1_core.md) into practical choices about people, routes and continuing service.
This part proposes twenty action families.
Their A01 to A20 references organize the specification and allocate no runtime identifiers.
All names are working labels.
Costs and durations below are authored tuning anchors.

Each response must change a real condition.
Heat reaches occupied buildings, repaired routes carry supplies, clinics support actual residents, and escorts protect a real movement.
A purchase contributes to Civilian Protection only through the weakest functioning essential service.
Lifeline Reliability improves through delivered service.
No action purchases daylight, cancels ordinary night penalties, alters the Return hazard or grants a new national currency.

## Common action contract

Every card inherits the following rules.
A stated exception changes only that rule.

**Owner and reveal.** The Sun owner controls its service commitments and presentation.
Famine, Migration, physical repair, creature families and existing stock owners retain their transactions.
A card appears for a current problem, a threatened existing commitment or an actionable recovery obligation.
The target must exist, have a valid actor and permit the proposed physical access.
Humanitarian work uses the receiving owner's full country and state eligibility.

**Quote and authorization.** Selection shows the accepted target, reachable population, resources, retained requirements, duration, total authorized upkeep and consequences of interruption.
A fresh quote is computed again at commitment.
Each action has at most four spendable cost types.
Three values may appear inline, with every cost icon and amount in the full tooltip.
Reserve floors and required facilities have separate labels.
A higher price requires a new authorization or a smaller package.

**Security capacity.** A09 and A17 reserve identified formations or existing owner-proven security capacity for their accepted workload.
Their nonoverlapping commitments share the same availability ledger.
Reassignment, combat elsewhere or loss of the assigned force ends unsupported local protection immediately.
One force cannot supply simultaneous full coverage to several places.

**Transaction.** Actor, lawful payer, episode, target, generation, quoted resources and reservation identity travel together.
The start either obtains the complete valid bundle or changes nothing.
Exact equality with available spendable stock is affordable after explicit retained requirements.
Resource deductions, delayed deliveries, progress and terminal outcomes commit once.
A second click, reopened window or reload cannot repeat payment or service.
Every continuing charge belongs to a disclosed finite authorization.

**Time and outcomes.** Service credit records the intervals when fuel, staffing, safety and access actually function.
A service that starts and ends between consequence pulses still counts.
Success completes automatically when the real objective is achieved.
Partial success retains measured service, surviving deliveries and completed repair.
Failure records the failed objective and actual consequences.
It does not fabricate casualties, erase a cohort or award the full result.
Starting another program cannot reset exposure or borrow another program's progress.

**Repeat use.** The same target cannot hold duplicate work.
The default restart cooldown is seven days after closure.
Seamless renewal of a funded service retains its receipt lineage.
A01 and delegated owner actions keep their stated cooldowns.
A16 safety closure and a distinct A18 incident may act immediately.

**Interruption and capture.** Loss of required access pauses future spending and service immediately.
A normal project has seven days to restore its prerequisites before closure.
A rescue deadline continues because stranded people remain in danger.
On capture, owner change, annexation or civil war, unpaid installments stop.
The physical asset remains where it actually exists.
Successor access requires a fresh lawful adoption check.
A former payer receives no new benefit from captured infrastructure.
Refunds cover only exact unspent reservations and provably unconsumed resources, with a durable receipt preventing replay.

**Return.** Darkness-dependent work ends immediately, future bills stop and reversible reservations release.
Consumed materials and delivered aid remain consumed or delivered.
Sun bonuses disappear from the exact holders.
A valid ongoing transfer may finish under its own owner.
Real repair, famine, reception and unresolved rescue obligations may continue through those owners.
Recovery work requires a real remaining problem and its own explicit commitment.

**AI and presentation.** AI uses the same targets, prices, stock checks and receipts.
Its preferences are specified in [part 5](124_sun_moves_away_spec_part_5_ai_balance.md).
Reports explain what worked, the remaining service gap and the next obligation.
Art direction uses the concrete operation on the card.
No card requires a finished narrative, quotation or extra persistent meter.

## Cost sizing and low-capacity work

A standard local example serves up to 100,000 reachable residents.
A reduced package serves up to 25,000.
The accepted population always fits existing buildings, staff, food access and transport.
These figures size costs and service receipts internally.
They add no public value.

For Sun-owned material costs, start from the card's standard package and scale by accepted population.
Apply a route factor of 1.00 for direct access, 1.25 for difficult access or 1.50 for severely constrained but functioning access.
Deep Freeze can multiply heating and cold-maintenance demand by 1.40.
Apply that factor only to the affected resource.
Whole equipment items round upward, and fuel quotes round upward to the next 100.
Disconnected or unsafe access fails eligibility when no receiving owner can prove a route.

Every card has a resource ceiling and a physical scope limit.
If scaling exceeds either, reduce the accepted population or work segment before quoting.
A price ceiling never grants unlimited coverage.
For example, A02's 100,000-resident package in difficult access under Deep Freeze costs 5,300 fuel and 35 support equipment.
A proposed 300,000-resident package exceeds its 12,000-fuel ceiling.
Reducing to 225,000 gives 11,900 fuel and 79 support equipment, both within its ceilings.

Priority can reduce one suitable Sun-owned material cost by at most 10 percent through better allocation.
Distributed relief favors A02 and A04, strategic continuity favors A05 and A06, and concentrated support favors A14 and A15.
Apply one priority adjustment to a quote.
Preserve minimum usable equipment, actual consumption and the common service bottleneck.
Shared Famine and Migration quotes retain their own rules.

Small countries can adapt usable existing buildings with local labor.
The reduced heating and rest packages require fewer materials and divert ten percent of the selected state's available construction and repair effort for their full thirty-seven-day setup and service interval.
Reduced repair uses the same percentage for its stated work period.
These arrangements reserve no unavailable civilian factory.
This labor arrangement improves existing service capacity.
A country with no functioning heat source still needs fuel or external assistance.
No coal, electricity, food or shelter-kit stockpile is invented.

Factory commitments use real reversible construction capacity.
Their supported reservation method remains an implementation gate.
An unsupported reservation cannot be represented as a paid factory that continues producing elsewhere.

## Presentation and mission limits

The compact panel displays three selected-state urgent actions, two national or network actions and one allocation or review action.
Selecting another state or intent replaces those rows.
A filter cannot expose several copies of the same paid operation.
Blocked rows explain their binding requirement.
Empty space is acceptable when no meaningful action exists.

A country may hold at most three Sun response mission commitments.
A delegated Famine or Migration mission shown here occupies one of those positions while retaining its original owner and clock.
It must also satisfy that owner's slot limit.
The panel creates no duplicate mission.
Existing unrelated owner work continues normally.

Immediate services and allocation choices use receipts without a mission slot.
Strategic projects generally use forty-five to ninety days.
Sustained refuge work uses ninety to one hundred eighty days.
Emergency rescue uses fifteen to thirty days because delay threatens an identified stranded group.
When all slots are occupied, a new rescue requires the player or AI to close or suspend an existing eligible commitment with its ordinary settlement.
Completion never requires another purchase.

Mission-bearing cards are A05, A06, A10, A11, A13, A14, A15, A17, A18, A19 and A20.
A16 uses the existing Migration mission when movement requires one.
Other cards use service or administrative receipts.

## Action cards

### A01. Choose Allocation Priority

**Owner and target.** Sun, national.
Reveal at entry and later through the single policy row.
The first selection is free.
A switch costs 25 political power, takes fourteen days to reorganize and has a thirty-day cooldown.
Each country keeps one priority.

**Result and exceptions.** Switching changes eligible allocation efficiency and the labor or transport emphasis described in part 1.
Existing payments, exposure, services and mission deadlines persist.
A country removed during reorganization closes that receipt.
Return cancels unfinished reorganization without refunding spent administrative effort.

**AI and direction.** Choose from observed rural need, strategic demand and accessible reception.
Show administrators assigning scarce transport and staff, with clear consequences for unserved places.

### A02. Emergency Heating

**Owner and target.** Sun, one eligible inhabited state with inadequate warmth and usable buildings.
A package costs 3,000 fuel and 20 support equipment for seven setup days followed by thirty funded service days, thirty-seven days total.
Ceilings are 12,000 fuel, 80 support equipment and 300,000 residents.
Renewal requires a fresh quote.

**Result and exceptions.** Service begins after seven days of setup.
Interruption stops warmth credit at the actual failure time.
The reduced fuel-free adaptation uses five support equipment and the common local labor diversion where an existing heat source can serve 25,000 residents.
Return ends the program.

**AI and direction.** Prioritize exposed residents with sufficient food and safe access.
Show insulation, shared heated rooms and fuel delivery.

### A03. Communal Food Distribution

**Owner and target.** Famine, presented through Sun for a real local food-access problem and available food or relief capacity.
The action invokes an eligible existing distribution or relief operation with its complete current quote and duration.
Sun adds no fee.

**Costs and outcomes.** Transport-only owner anchors include 5,000 fuel for the lowest connected tier or 10,000 fuel and five convoys for its sea equivalent, plus the underlying operation's actual resources.
Select an exact operation whose combined bundle has at most four spend types.
Otherwise present a smaller valid operation or refer to the owner's interface.
Partial delivery receives only its measured relief effect.

**AI and direction.** Favor acute deprivation and reachable beneficiaries.
Show real kitchens and distribution queues.
Return leaves Famine's genuine obligation intact.

### A04. Rest and Clinics

**Owner and target.** Sun service coordination, one inhabited state with exhausted residents and available care capacity.
The package costs 30 support equipment and a ten-percent local work diversion for seven setup days followed by thirty funded service days, thirty-seven days total.
The cap is 90 equipment for 300,000 residents.
The reduced package uses eight equipment for 25,000.

**Result and exceptions.** Seven days establish rotations and basic care.
Actual service reduces the relevant deprivation contribution within part 1's sixty-percent overall mitigation ceiling.
Nutrition still depends on food access.
Occupation or access loss ends unsupported service immediately.

**AI and direction.** Favor persistent exhaustion after warmth and food gaps are manageable.
Show staffed treatment rooms and shift rosters.
Return removes the Sun mitigation effect while real care remains with its owner.

### A05. Mark and Light a Lifeline

**Owner and target.** Sun, one usable route segment with proven demand and avoidable handling or navigation failures.
A forty-five-day commitment costs 40 support equipment and 2,000 fuel for setup and authorized maintenance.
Ceilings are 120 equipment and 8,000 fuel.

**Result and exceptions.** Markings, signals and staffed checkpoints improve the service supported by that route after ten days.
The mission succeeds when the selected corridor sustains its promised delivery for thirty days.
A damaged or blocked route needs repair or access first.
Capture invalidates the payer's authority.

**AI and direction.** Favor corridors serving several existing commitments.
Show signals, work lamps and recognizable route markers.
Return removes darkness-specific advantage and ends further maintenance charges.

### A06. Repair a Critical Route

**Owner and target.** Physical repair owner with a Sun mission reference, one damaged rail, road-handling or port segment that blocks essential delivery.
The standard sixty-day project uses 50 support equipment and one civilian factory reservation.
Ceiling is 150 equipment and two factories, with total reserved work capped at 120 factory-days.

**Result and exceptions.** Progress requires actual repair of the identified damage.
Partial completion preserves repaired capacity.
The reduced option repairs one smaller eligible defect through local work diversion over ninety days.
Undamaged infrastructure cannot qualify.

**AI and direction.** Prefer repairs unlocking food, heat or evacuation.
Show repair crews at the exact obstruction.
Return permits the genuine repair to continue with its remaining authorized reservation.

### A07. Divert Military Fuel

**Owner and target.** Sun, an emergency heating operation with valid civilian access and a country whose fuel has competing military demand.
The order costs fifteen command power and redirects 6,000 fuel across twenty-one days, charged in three disclosed 2,000-fuel installments.
Ceiling is 12,000 fuel and thirty command power.

**Result and exceptions.** The order consumes the ordinary national fuel stock and extends only the heating capacity that can receive it.
Existing protected commitments remain prerequisites.
It creates no separate military fuel reserve or new stockpile.
A failed delivery stops the next installment.

**AI and direction.** Use when immediate civilian exposure outweighs discretionary military activity.
Show a real transport reassignment and warn about the remaining national fuel margin.
Return stops unpaid installments.

### A08. Reorganize Factory Shifts

**Owner and target.** Sun, a selected industrial state with active production and exhausted workers.
A forty-five-day operation costs 20 support equipment and diverts ten percent of local industrial work for the first fourteen days.
Ceiling is 60 equipment across three selected states.

**Result and exceptions.** Safer shifts and reliable rest reduce the supported factories' deprivation contribution after the initial reorganization.
Delivered warmth and food remain prerequisites for the full benefit.
Output cannot exceed the ordinary baseline through this response.
Capture terminates the previous operator's arrangement.

**AI and direction.** Prefer important staffed industry after basic services function.
Show clocks, shorter shifts and transport home.
Return removes both Sun-specific reorganization and mitigation effects immediately.

### A09. Protect Rural Settlements

**Owner and target.** Sun security coordination, an inhabited rural state with a verified local threat.
A thirty-day patrol commitment costs 100 infantry equipment, 20 support equipment and fifteen command power.
Ceilings are 300, 60 and forty respectively.

**Result and exceptions.** Available local security personnel and access are retained requirements.
Patrols protect actual residents and routes.
Their measured coverage affects local safety and incident opportunity.
This action creates no divisions and grants no country-wide attack bonus.
Partial coverage leaves distant settlements exposed.

**AI and direction.** Favor threatened clusters with functioning warmth and food.
Show patrol routes and escorted residents.
Capture ends the old patrol receipt.
Return closes the Sun security package, leaving actual surviving threats to their owners.

### A10. Escort a Relief Convoy

**Owner and target.** Transport owner with Sun mission coordination, one confirmed sea shipment and a reachable receiving port.
The sixty-day escort commitment uses 4,000 fuel, twenty command power and a valid reservation of five available convoys.
Ceilings are 12,000 fuel, forty command power and fifteen reserved convoys.

**Result and exceptions.** Convoy availability is a reservation, with actual losses resolved by the transport owner.
Escorts and access must exist.
Success requires measured cargo delivery.
Partial arrival credits only delivered quantities.
No arrival is inferred from stationed troops or elapsed time.

**AI and direction.** Prefer indispensable island supply over redundant routes.
Show loading, escort and reception.
A valid voyage can finish after Return through its transport owner.

### A11. Organize Evacuation

**Owner and target.** Migration, an exact origin cohort and valid destination with preparation, transport, food safety and reception headroom.
Use the current general or vulnerable-person evacuation quote, batch and owner deadline.
Sun reserves one panel mission position and charges no additional fee.

**Result and exceptions.** Confirm that the complete underlying and transport costs use at most four spend types.
Preparation and movement remain separate where the owner requires them.
Success follows measured surviving arrivals.
Rejection changes no population.
Closure preserves stranded obligations, and capture uses the owner's current route validity.

**AI and direction.** Evacuate when sustainable protection is unavailable and a safer destination genuinely exists.
Show named origin and receiving places.
Return does not erase displaced people or force an unsafe return journey.

### A12. Send Bilateral Relief

**Owner and target.** Stock and transport owners, a consenting eligible recipient with a real service deficit and receiving route.
A sample shipment contains 3,000 fuel, 30 support equipment and 100 infantry equipment when security need justifies the latter.
A disclosed transit fuel allowance of 500 uses the same fuel cost type.
Ceilings are 15,000 total fuel, 150 support and 500 infantry equipment.

**Result and exceptions.** Debit consigned stock once, credit only delivered cargo and account separately for burned transit fuel.
The receiver gains no resource before delivery.
Withheld consent or invalid access cancels unissued work.
A partial shipment settles its exact balance.

**AI and direction.** Favor deliverable aid after essential domestic commitments.
Show cargo and destination.
Return allows valid aid already dispatched to complete.

### A13. Observe and Coordinate

**Owner and target.** Sun, a country with incomplete local service reports or unresolved route conditions.
A thirty-day survey uses ten support equipment and five command power, capped at thirty equipment and fifteen command power.
A reduced local survey uses two equipment and existing staff.

**Result and exceptions.** Confirmed observations improve target selection, reveal reachable unmet need and prevent duplicate requests.
The operation reports uncertainty where evidence remains incomplete.
It changes no Return hazard and reveals no sunrise countdown.
A threatened survey route may abort with partial information.

**AI and direction.** Prefer it when uncertainty blocks a useful commitment, after urgent survival work.
Show maps, radio reports and field observers.
Return closes darkness surveys and preserves valid recovery observations.

### A14. Build a Lit Refuge

**Owner and target.** Sun installation owner, available only with The Endless Night active.
Select a supplied settlement with usable buildings, lawful control, staffing, safety and independently valid reception capacity.
A ninety-day, 100,000-resident project costs 120 support equipment, 6,000 commissioning fuel and one reserved civilian factory.
Ceilings are 240 equipment, 12,000 fuel and two factories across 200,000 residents.

**Result and exceptions.** Success requires an operating warmth, lighting, food and safety package.
Partial installations remain incomplete and provide only proven services.
Construction never creates food or automatic reception headroom.

**AI and direction.** Favor a site with affordable continuing upkeep and secure corridors.
Show conversion of existing settlement infrastructure.
Return releases construction reservations and retains only genuine physical improvements.

### A15. Sustain a Lit Refuge

**Owner and target.** Sun, an operating Endless Night refuge or a bounded set of registered refuge sites.
Each ninety-day authorization for 100,000 residents costs 18,000 fuel, 90 support equipment and thirty civilian factory-days of maintenance capacity.
Disclosed installments and real construction reservations are scheduled before commitment.
Ceilings are 90,000 fuel, 450 support equipment and 150 factory-days, with at most two simultaneous factories per accepted package.
Verify the supported whole-reservation mapping and reduce scope when capacity cannot fit.
A 180-day mission consists of two separately authorized ninety-day periods.

**Result and exceptions.** Success requires at least eighty-one functioning service days in ninety, with no outage longer than three days.
A partial period keeps its actual served intervals.
Unaffordable upkeep reduces accepted coverage or closes a site safely through A16.

**AI and direction.** Existing refuge commitments precede expansion.
Show staffed power and heating rooms.
Return immediately stops the artificial refuge obligation and future installments.

### A16. Reduce or Close a Refuge

**Owner and target.** Sun with Migration where movement is necessary, an existing refuge whose upkeep or access is failing.
Local consolidation costs five support equipment per site, capped at twenty.
Closure preparation takes fourteen days.
Any civilian movement uses its separate complete Migration quote and mission.

**Result and exceptions.** Reduce coverage to genuinely supportable capacity.
Close only after occupants have viable ordinary services or a validated transfer.
If neither exists, the site becomes an unresolved humanitarian obligation with clear consequences.
Clicking closure cannot delete residents, refund consumed fuel or declare them safe.

**AI and direction.** Prefer early consolidation over an unpayable expansion.
Show orderly consolidation and receiving arrangements.
Return removes Sun obligations immediately, while unresolved human needs remain.

### A17. Contain a Predator Population

**Owner and target.** Sun incident owner for an established regional predator population, or the existing creature-family owner for actual surviving actors it permits targeting.
A sixty-day operation costs 200 infantry equipment, 40 support equipment and twenty-five command power.
Ceilings are 600, 120 and sixty.

**Result and exceptions.** Success requires the selected population's measured attack capability or access to protected settlements to fall below its incident closure condition.
Patrol presence alone is insufficient.
Losses and survivors remain owner records.
The action creates no target, resurrects no eliminated species and never grants a blanket bonus to a mixed human army.

**AI and direction.** Prefer repeated attacks on essential corridors.
Show barriers, tracking and defended approaches.
Return removes only Sun deltas and hands remaining local threats to bounded aftermath.

### A18. Rescue Missing Travelers

**Owner and target.** The incident owner and Migration when transfer is involved, one confirmed stranded group with surviving people and a possible route.
A twenty-one-day emergency mission costs 50 infantry equipment, 15 support equipment and ten command power.
Ceilings are 150, 45 and thirty.

**Result and exceptions.** The deadline reflects dwindling supplies and exposure.
Rescue succeeds on verified contact and safe arrival or protection.
Partial recovery records exactly the survivors reached.
Timeout resolves the actual incident outcome and cannot assume every missing person died.
A new rescue requires a new incident receipt.

**AI and direction.** Give urgent, feasible rescues priority over discretionary projects.
Show search parties and safe reception.
Return removes darkness while an outstanding living-person rescue may continue through its owner.

### A19. Repair Winter Damage

**Owner and target.** Physical repair owner, after Return, for verified damage caused during the episode and still unrepaired.
A ninety-day recovery project uses 40 support equipment and one reserved civilian factory.
Ceilings are 120 equipment and two factories, with 180 factory-days maximum.

**Result and exceptions.** Completion requires measured repair of the selected assets.
Earlier repair credit, a changed controller or a removed asset changes remaining scope.
Partial reconstruction remains real.
A reduced local package repairs a smaller defect through the labor arrangement when no factory is free.

**AI and direction.** Prioritize routes and facilities that constrain current food, reception or production.
Show reconstruction of the damaged place.
No Sunlight Deprivation or darkness effect persists to justify the mission.

### A20. Reopen Essential Services

**Owner and target.** The relevant service owner with Sun recovery reporting, a restored facility or corridor whose remaining disruption has an episode-linked record.
A sixty-day commitment costs 20 support equipment and 1,000 fuel, capped at 60 equipment and 4,000 fuel.

**Result and exceptions.** Validate staffing, safety, repairs and receiving demand, then record thirty continuous days of real operation.
Success retires that exact recovery obligation.
Partial reopening retains functioning capacity.
Renewed independent damage opens its own owner problem and cannot recreate the Sun episode.

**AI and direction.** Prefer reopened services that release existing humanitarian burdens.
Show actual users returning to the facility.
An unused building or empty route cannot earn recovery credit merely by waiting.

## Source and implementation boundaries

The response proposals use the current owner separation in the [Famine system](https://github.com/klimPaskov/Chaos-Redux/blob/41bc4e5045a7b5c131be69dda2f08079d478634c/docs/systems/famine_system.md), especially lines 95 to 131, and [Migration system](https://github.com/klimPaskov/Chaos-Redux/blob/41bc4e5045a7b5c131be69dda2f08079d478634c/docs/systems/migration_system.md), lines 97 to 150.
Their actual resource quotes, population conservation and receiving gates govern delegated work.
The [humanitarian validity contract](https://github.com/klimPaskov/Chaos-Redux/blob/41bc4e5045a7b5c131be69dda2f08079d478634c/common/scripted_triggers/humanitarian_validation_triggers.txt), lines 4 to 30, is narrower than generic human identity.

The new Sun actions, construction reservations, shipment adapters and mission predicates are proposals requiring verified implementation surfaces.
Existing function names and unresolved capabilities are collected in [part 6](124_sun_moves_away_spec_part_6_integrations_and_chaos.md) and the [research record](124_sun_moves_away_research_and_sources.md).
This document supplies behavior and transactions, without asserting that a future owner API already exists.
