## Three distinct roles

`SE124_MANIFEST` accompanies the once-committed global anomaly after its actual map, combat and environmental effects take hold.
Its scene and text establish the changed world and uncertainty through a recognizable period landscape and human response.

`SE124_ENDLESS_CONSEQUENCE` requires Evolution III plus one material condition from part 2.
Gate A is a functioning paid refuge network across three independently controlled countries for thirty days, with each serving at least ten percent of its eligible population and the network serving at least one percent of the fixed episode world denominator.
Gate B is at least twenty percent of that denominator experiencing local protection below 35 for thirty consecutive days after III.
An evolution flag alone cannot trigger this presentation.

`SE124_RETURN_AFTERMATH` follows immediate Return cleanup when the episode lasted at least 180 days or recorded 100,000 actual Sun-attributed civilian deaths, 1,000,000 unique displaced people, or ten Sun-caused infrastructure/factory damage level-equivalents.
Short lower-damage Return uses ordinary news.
No presentation delays Return, repeats a physical outcome or refunds historical harm.

Use `chaosx_super_event_audio_researcher` for suitable structured musical recordings.
Inspect approved repository tracks and provenance first.
Each accepted super-event needs its own final recording and audio ID unless exact reuse already has explicit approval.

Prefer one to two minutes, with exceptions handled under the current skill.
Ambient noise, a stinger or a locally synthesized primitive waveform does not satisfy a required musical cue, and generated audio is never presented as a sourced recording.
