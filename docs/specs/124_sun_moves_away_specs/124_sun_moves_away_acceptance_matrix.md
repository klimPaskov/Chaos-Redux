## Fixtures and evidence

Use existing-country fixtures for cold preparation, tropical rural populations, desert imports, scarce islands, war, occupation, pure nocturnal owners, mixed armies, and absent survivors.
Record exact tags/states/controllers, populations, DLC, resources/access, predicates, revision, episode/generation, receipts, settings, and eligible exposure.
Separate incompatible baselines and test thresholds on both sides and at equality.

S covers source, installed references, accounting, calculations, and static asset review.
M means available MCP structural, scenario, or render evidence.
L means actual engine observation only when supplied by the user or separately authorized.
The two core engine mechanisms remain unproven.
Static previews cannot establish them, and missing functionality blocks completion.
No game launch or log search is authorized.
