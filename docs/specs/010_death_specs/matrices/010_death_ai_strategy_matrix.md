## Invalid action blockers

AI must not:

- choose Black Oath before Death is revealed.
- use coastal-watch decisions without coastal states or naval/logistics capacity.
- start wasteland outpost missions in states it does not control.
- take necromancy decisions without route unlocks or recaptured wasteland/casualty pressure.
- declare war if Death does not exist, is still unknown, or has only consumed one island.
- choose Death focus branches before the stage/evolution allows them.
- keep compact decisions visible after becoming a Herald.
- fire world-end behavior before continent-consumed and Chaos-above-1000 conditions are both true.
