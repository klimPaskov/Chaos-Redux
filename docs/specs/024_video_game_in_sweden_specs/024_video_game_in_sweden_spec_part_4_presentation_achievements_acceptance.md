# Event 24 Presentation, Achievements, and Acceptance

## Writing direction

Final localisation belongs to implementation. The directions below define viewpoint, tone, visible information, and material that should remain uncertain.

### Baseline event

Viewpoint:

- Swedish government, military, and press observers encountering an unusual national invention.

Visible information:

- The machine can simulate geography, production, logistics, military deployments, and political alignments.
- Officers have already found practical uses.
- Developers and publishers disagree about access.

Tone:

- Dry institutional enthusiasm.
- Period engineering confidence.
- Mild satire through serious treatment of a strange product.

Avoid:

- Direct mention of Hearts of Iron, Paradox, players, save files, modifiers, or game rules as implementation terms.
- Modern startup, software-store, livestream, forum, or esports language.
- A joke-only popup that makes the military benefit feel fake.

Dynamic references:

- Sweden's war state.
- Current world tension.
- Whether Stockholm is secure.
- Whether Sweden is independent or a subject.

### Baseline options

Controlled Staff Trial:

- The speaker is a cautious military and civil authority.
- The option accepts the game as a supervised instrument.
- Tone should be practical and slightly skeptical.

Civilian Commercial Release:

- The speaker is a publisher, public educator, or government promoter.
- The option treats the product as a national cultural export.
- Tone can use confident administrative absurdity without modern sales language.

Competitive Officer League:

- The speaker is an ambitious officer corps.
- The option treats competition as a path to better command.
- Tone can be proud and faintly self-damning.

### Evolution I text

Viewpoint:

- Staff colleges, commanders, referees, and junior officers.

Visible information:

- Rankings are influencing prestige.
- Officers are rehearsing the same scenarios repeatedly.
- The text should distinguish useful results from cases where the game rewards narrow behavior.

Tone:

- Competitive seriousness.
- Professional rivalry.
- Dry concern about what is being measured.

Avoid:

- Treating every officer as foolish.
- Saying that the game has become dangerous before concrete problems appear.
- Modern terms such as meta, balance patch, build order, tier list, or speedrun in final text.

The event may describe equivalent behavior using period military language.

### Evolution II text

Viewpoint:

- Public clubs, teachers, newspapers, workers, political organizers, and families.

Visible information:

- Clubs and leagues are spreading.
- Public interest in military planning is rising.
- Work, education, and politics are being reorganized around the game.

Tone:

- Social satire grounded in real institutions.
- Brisk public enthusiasm.
- Increasing irritation from employers and officials.

Avoid:

- Cheap jokes about addiction.
- Medical claims.
- Dismissing the public as unintelligent.
- Modern internet-community imagery.

### Evolution III text

Viewpoint:

- Senior command, cabinet offices, production managers, diplomats, soldiers, and families affected by incorrect assumptions.

Visible information:

- Plans ignore transport or weather.
- Officials expect alignments and replacement rates to follow simplified categories.
- Real operations are being judged by simulated outcomes.

Tone:

- Concrete unease.
- Controlled official language.
- Serious consequences with restrained irony.

Avoid:

- Empty declarations about reality collapsing.
- Staccato slogans.
- A direct fourth-wall reveal.
- Language that labels the event as a warning or instructs the player how severe the situation should feel.

### Foreign reactions

Copy:

- Curious and practical.
- The foreign government sees a cheap training tool.

Study:

- Cautious intelligence or military analysis.
- The country wants the assumptions and data more than the product's public image.

Ban:

- Concern about distraction, dissent, propaganda, or foreign influence.
- Tone depends on ideology and current stability.

Ridicule:

- Press confidence or rivalry.
- The reaction should become awkward if Sweden later succeeds militarily.

## Event image direction

The main report image should show a period-authentic Swedish room containing officers, civilian engineers, and a large mechanical or projected strategy map. Relay cabinets, punched cards, teleprinters, wooden counters, cables, and military charts should establish the alternate technology.

The scene should look like a 1936 to 1945 press or documentary photograph. It must not show a modern monitor, keyboard, plastic console, neon arcade, readable generated text, or a modern military uniform.

The image should communicate invention and serious attention. It should not be a map-only diagram.

## Decision category presentation

Use one static category picture showing the simulation apparatus in active use. The picture should include:

- A large map or projection surface.
- Relay or switchboard machinery.
- Punched cards or printed scenario sheets.
- Counters or plotting tools.
- Human adjudicators and operators.

The picture is presentation. It must not paint fake buttons, a meter, dynamic numbers, or decision controls into the art.

Simulation Reliance is shown through dynamic category text and staged idea icons.

## Achievement set

Event 24 supports two achievements. The labels are working labels and are not final localisation.

### Achievement 1: Reality Check

Eligible country:

- Player-controlled principal Sweden.

Visibility:

- Visible from campaign start or from Event 24 firing. It should not reveal hidden event incidents.

Core route:

1. Event 24 fires.
2. Sweden reaches Evolution III.
3. Sweden begins the Reality Audit.
4. The audit succeeds fully.
5. Sweden keeps Simulation Reliance below Doctrine for one year.
6. Sweden does not lose control of Stockholm during that year.

Optional difficulty reinforcement:

- Require Sweden to be at war for part of the audit or to win a war after the audit without returning to Worldview Reliance.

Disqualifiers:

- Debug or force-trigger paths under the normal achievement contract.
- Restriction route instead of audit.
- Partial or failed audit.
- Reliance returning to Doctrine or Worldview during the proof period.

Why it is meaningful:

The achievement requires the player to let the event escalate, accept a real temporary military burden, complete the field objective, and then maintain discipline.

Icon direction:

A projected strategy map crossed by a muddy boot print, survey compass, or field notebook. The image should communicate evidence correcting abstraction.

### Achievement 2: According to Plan

Eligible country:

- Player-controlled principal Sweden.

Visibility:

- Visible, with wording that does not reveal the exact Evolution III incident family.

Core route:

1. Event 24 reaches Evolution III.
2. Sweden chooses Trust the Model.
3. Sweden enters or is already fighting a meaningful war against a major power or a country with clearly greater industrial strength.
4. Sweden wins or forces a favorable peace before the mandatory reassessment ends.
5. Stockholm remains under Swedish control throughout the surge.
6. Sweden completes the mandatory reassessment after victory.

Disqualifiers:

- A war created only through debug or force-trigger testing.
- An opponent that was already near capitulation when the surge began.
- Loss of Stockholm.
- Restarting or duplicating the surge.

Why it is meaningful:

The route grants strong temporary power but exposes Sweden to supply, recovery, and adaptability risks. The achievement rewards a hard campaign outcome, not a decision click.

Icon direction:

A brass plotting table with a Swedish-colored marker reaching an objective while broken rail and weather symbols remain visible around it. The image should communicate a successful plan under real friction.

## Event Log and Event Details

### History entry

The History row should show:

- Event ID 24.
- Event name.
- Date.
- Swedish actor and flag.
- Minor Fire-Once classification.

The first history row is recorded once. Sub-events do not create duplicate Event 24 history rows.

### Evolution rows

Each actual evolution records one entry with:

- Event ID 24.
- Evolution type and stage.
- Current Chaos tier.
- Swedish actor.
- Date.

The three evolution names shown in the log should match final in-game evolution wording. Baseline program stages must not appear as evolutions.

### Event Details direction

Event Details should explain:

- Sweden created an advanced strategy simulation.
- The military can use it for training and analysis.
- Broader institutional trust can reshape military and public behavior.

It should not list:

- Simulation Reliance thresholds.
- Evolution MTTH.
- Hidden incident pools.
- Exact AI weights.
- Achievement conditions.
- Future crisis routes.

### Availability display

Event 24 shows `N/A` when no principal Sweden exists. It shows ordinary fire-once weight when a valid Sweden exists and the event has not fired. After firing, it shows the normal permanent fire-once state.

## Catalog wording direction

The authoritative workbook should describe the event in player-facing terms after final localisation exists.

The details field should cover the invention, limited training value, and the risk of overconfidence without listing modifiers.

Each evolution field should describe its premise:

- Officers make the game part of professional culture.
- Public institutions turn it into a national obsession.
- Government and command begin applying game logic to reality.

Status should remain below Implemented until the full event, assets, AI, Event Log, decisions, achievements, documentation, and live test requirements are complete.

## Multiplayer behavior

- The player controlling the principal Swedish host receives all Swedish choices.
- Other human players receive only relevant foreign-response or report events.
- A human foreign country can choose Copy, Study, Ban, Ridicule, or Ignore when selected for a response.
- The foreign choice cannot open the Swedish decision system.
- Host migration during tag switching or civil war cannot duplicate the event.
- Save and reload preserve the current Swedish actor and active mission.

## DLC compatibility

The core event should work without a specific DLC. It may use ordinary events, decisions, ideas, commander traits, army experience, resources, and core military modifiers.

Do not make the event depend on a special doctrine interface, intelligence agency, market system, or licensed-production mechanic. DLC-aware flavor may be added when it has a safe core-game equivalent and does not create separate balance outcomes.

## Accessibility and clarity

- Reliance uses a number, a named stage, and a stage-specific idea icon.
- Positive and negative changes use consistent text colors and symbols.
- The stage must remain understandable without relying only on color.
- Decision tooltips should normally use two to four short lines for the main effect and a concise requirements section.
- The category should show the next review or active mission clearly.
- Foreign-response events should name Sweden and the reacting country directly.
- No hidden cost may appear after the player commits to an action.

## Acceptance scenarios

### A1: Sweden exists and Event 24 has not fired

Expected:

- Event 24 has normal fire-once availability.
- The actor is the principal Sweden.
- The entry event offers all valid release philosophies.

### A2: Sweden does not exist

Expected:

- Event 24 shows `N/A`.
- No event is queued against a dead tag.

### A3: Baseline controlled conclusion

Setup:

- Calm World.
- Controlled Staff Trial.
- Reliance remains below Habit or low Habit.
- One controlled exercise and one review are completed.

Expected:

- The trial can conclude within the intended baseline duration.
- No evolution is required.
- The category and staged idea are removed cleanly.
- A finite aftermath remains, then expires.

### A4: Officer league reaches Evolution I

Expected:

- Evolution I occurs only at Gathering Storm or higher.
- The correct evolution row appears.
- Evolution I decisions replace baseline actions.
- At most one commander can gain the mixed trait.

### A5: Civilian release reaches Evolution II

Expected:

- Evolution II requires Rising Chaos or higher and broad public reach.
- Productivity and public-interest tradeoffs are visible.
- No more than three foreign countries receive a meaningful response.
- Foreign copies remain temporary and do not gain the Swedish mechanic.

### A6: Reality Audit succeeds

Expected:

- The mission requires real divisions and committed resources.
- Success lowers Reliance, replaces Simulation Orthodoxy, corrects the trait when relevant, and closes the crisis after its finite aftermath.
- The Reality Check achievement begins or completes according to its proof period.

### A7: Reality Audit partially succeeds or fails

Expected:

- Partial success and failure use distinct results.
- The event remains resolvable.
- Restriction and narrower re-audit routes remain available.

### A8: Trust the Model

Expected:

- Strong temporary benefits and serious risks apply together.
- Invalid desperate situations block or heavily suppress AI selection.
- A mandatory reassessment occurs within the fixed maximum duration.
- The route cannot become a permanent Worldview state.

### A9: Evolutions disabled

Expected:

- Disabled evolutions do not record flags or unlock content.
- Baseline or earlier evolution phases retain a clean conclusion.
- No achievement becomes available through a disabled stage.

### A10: Save and reload

Expected:

- Route, Reliance, evolution, mission progress, foreign targets, commander target, and reassessment deadline persist.
- No event, reward, foreign response, or evolution duplicates.

### A11: Annexation and civil war

Expected:

- The principal host rule prevents two Swedish programs.
- A dead host stops progression and cleans active decisions.
- Fire-once history remains permanent.

### A12: Event Log and Event Details

Expected:

- One History row with Sweden as actor.
- Correct evolution rows with date, tier, and actor.
- Event Details shows premise without mechanics spoilers.
- Impossible availability shows `N/A`.

### A13: Asset coverage

Expected:

- Main report image, category picture, idea icons, priority decision icons, commander-trait icons, and achievement triplets exist and are wired.
- No pink texture, wrong aspect ratio, modern visual element, opaque square, or placeholder remains.

### A14: AI time progression

Expected:

- Stable AI Sweden tends toward controlled or dual-track play.
- Militarized AI Sweden can exploit the officer route when materially capable.
- Supply failure, lost cores, or near capitulation push AI toward audit or restriction.
- The AI does not maintain an ignored baseline program forever.

## Anti-bloat closure

The event design is complete at this scope. It has a useful baseline, three escalating evolutions, one readable reliance value, player agency, AI behavior, bounded foreign reactions, staged assets, achievements, recovery, and a clean conclusion.

Further expansion should stop unless implementation reveals a concrete gap in an accepted route, consequence, recovery path, AI decision, or visible payoff. The central choice remains the difference between using a model as a tool and granting it authority over reality.
