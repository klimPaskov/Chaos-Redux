# The Irish Navy

The naval lane has independent value for every strategic route.
A reunited island needs coast protection, supply security, and maintained ships before it can sustain distant ambitions.
The branch uses normal hulls, modules, doctrine, production, and repair systems, with equivalent base-game behavior where a designer or market feature is unavailable.

## N01: Naval establishment

Create the institutional and industrial basis for a navy.
An existing Irish fleet is inventoried rather than overwritten.
The player chooses immediate coastal readiness or a stronger construction program.
Readiness provides a limited procurement or refit opportunity backed by real equipment and capacity.
Construction improves the route toward a sustainable dockyard program.
Neither grants a fleet large enough to ignore the industry's later development.

## N02: Shipbuilding and repair

Invest in a finite Irish shipyard and repair network.
The Belfast project connects to U04 and T02, with duplicate building rewards prevented by shared location accounting.
Choose surface escort production or a submarine-oriented workshop and training program.
A later secondary program is possible at a higher cost.
The branch must offer a useful hull or module research opportunity and a concrete production investment.
Existing state slots and shipyard levels control the construction result.

## N03: Escort force

Develop a trained force for convoy protection and local sea control.
The program requires suitable hull technology, shipbuilding capacity, and crew preparation.
It provides doctrine support and a paid operational exercise.
An empire uses the force for the Irish Sea and distant supplies.
A federation can share access and accepted patrol responsibilities.
A primary Atlantic government can make it the center of its maritime strategy.
Existing ships qualify by their supported role, not merely by being any naval unit.

## N04: Submarine arm

Offer a coherent submarine alternative with reconnaissance, repair facilities, trained crews, and production.
The player must still maintain convoy protection and coastal defense through ordinary means.
A submarine strategy cannot count its boats as the entire escort requirement for a protected transport operation.
The reward improves relevant research, training, and production opportunities without completing every naval doctrine.
The route's mission uses sea-access and fleet-capacity checks that are actually supported by the installed engine.

## N05: Coastal defense and naval intelligence

Develop observation, port defense, patrol support, and coastal aviation coordination.
Existing naval and air intelligence mechanics can be used where supported.
A base-game alternative provides practical scouting and defensive preparation without inventing access to a DLC-only agency.
The player chooses priority among a western Atlantic approach and the Irish Sea front according to actual threats.
Only finite owned coastal locations receive construction.
No global coastline loop is required.

## N06: Transport and naval operations

The amphibious option connects to M08 marines and A05 reconnaissance.
The merchant-support option improves convoy replacement and safe expedition supply.
An operation requires actual transport capability and does not teleport troops across a sea zone.
A failed military operation loses ships and units through ordinary mechanics.
The branch offers a recovery procurement contract, not an automatic refund.
An already powerful Ireland can enter advanced training without rebuilding an invented starter fleet.

## N07: A sustainable fleet

The naval capstone requires the chosen main doctrine, working shipyards, repair capacity, a trained fleet in the intended role, and a completed maritime exercise.
It improves the selected Defense Establishment family and opens a later procurement contract.
Evolution II adds deeper joint or overseas operations.
Evolution III adds a larger sustainable program tied to actual industry and fuel.
A carrier ambition, if selected as late content, must be a demanding alternative requiring hull research, aircraft, screens, and dockyards.
It is not a free mandatory carrier spawned by an unrelated culture focus.
