# A United Ireland

The common lane establishes a state that can govern the territory it has won.
It treats northern political life as a practical settlement among citizens, employers, workers, local institutions, and national authorities.
The lane must not identify all unionists as rebels or portray every constitutional disagreement as an armed uprising.
The new government can act firmly while distinguishing opposition from violence.

## U01: Reunification administration

This immediate group creates the northern administration mission and introduces Northern Settlement.
The player chooses whether the first administrative effort prioritizes civilian services or military demobilization and supply.
Civilian services begin reconstruction sooner.
The military-first alternative reduces the immediate burden of the emergency establishment and improves the next paid retraining contract.
Both retain access to the constitutional groups.
Neither awards a second tree-unlock bonus.
A useful initial reward is a repair allocation and 50 Political Power to support the first real decision, paid once.

## U02: Constitutional settlement

Offer three constitutional policies with explicit consequences.
Regional self-government retains a northern assembly with defined domestic powers while foreign affairs and national defense belong to the Irish state.
The unitary civic policy provides equal citizenship, national representation, and enforceable local guarantees without a separate regional legislature.
Central direction places appointments, policing oversight, and reconstruction priorities under the national executive.

The first two policies can reach the best cooperative settlement.
Central direction receives earlier administrative recruitment and faster initial military preparation, but imposes the political loss and federal restrictions defined in the values file.
None automatically changes the country's ideology.
Where an ideology affects eligibility for a particular institution, provide a coherent equivalent policy and disclose the changed consequence.

## U03: Public service and security settlement

The government chooses the conditions under which existing public servants, railway workers, engineers, and local security personnel continue in service.
A reviewed-continuity policy retains expertise with a slower oversight process.
A replacement policy gives stronger central control but delays service recovery and increases the real manpower and administrative burden.
The mission offers an improvement in Northern Settlement only after its promised safeguards or staffing commitments have been fulfilled.

A separate security incident can test these commitments once during early integration.
The player investigates targeted wrongdoing, negotiates institutional compliance, or applies a costly central intervention.
It does not generate an unrequested civil war.
The result affects trust, industrial activity, or project timing through the existing value and spirit family.

## U04: Belfast reconstruction

The player chooses the first industrial priority among civilian repair, arms production, and maritime reconstruction.
Each option uses a 120-day project with a civilian factory commitment and a concrete building or repair outcome.
Later focuses can obtain the other capabilities, but the initial choice determines the cheapest and earliest development.
The original northern industrial base is inventoried before rewards are calculated.
Existing facilities can receive improved repair, conversion, or specialization instead of duplicate construction.

The arms option links directly to M02 equipment production.
The maritime option links to T02 and N01.
Civilian recovery improves the cost of U05 infrastructure and can support a higher Settlement gain if the government honors employment guarantees.
The group should leave the player with an actual change in production or construction opportunity, not solely an industrial percentage modifier.

## U05: Island infrastructure

Connect northern and southern supply, passenger movement, and industrial logistics through a finite set of Irish-owned locations.
The first project prioritizes the Dublin-Belfast connection as installed-map geography allows.
A later choice extends regional infrastructure or port approaches.
The reward targets real railway, infrastructure, or port capacity supported by the engine and the active map.
A nationally scaled research bonus is an explicit alternate only where the intended physical improvement is already complete.

This group lowers practical supply limitations for M07 mobile forces and improves Atlantic distribution.
It is not an automatic maximum-level upgrade of every Irish state.
A damaged or occupied worksite pauses the related mission.
Costs already spent on completed work remain spent, while future committed factory time is released through the documented cancellation rule.

## U06: Employment and public finance

Build a tax and procurement settlement that can fund the larger military.
One path favors civilian recovery and export production.
The other favors direct state procurement and faster arms expansion.
Both include a production investment and a condition tied to actual usable factories.
Political alignment may change writing and supported laws, but not erase the economic choice.

Civilian recovery provides a better basis for diaspora investment and cooperative federal procurement.
State procurement makes artillery, trucks, and a standing army easier to support, but has a higher short-term civilian commitment.
The player may later rebalance procurement once at a cost, replacing the original policy benefit.
Do not allow both economic spirit variants to coexist.

## U07: Relations with the defeated holder

For Britain, present border normalization, shipping and trade talks, or an armed but observed settlement.
The historic possibility of negotiating ports and economic relations is a research anchor, not a requirement that the campaign reproduce a fixed 1938 agreement.
For another holder, substitute a neutral settlement relationship and relevant actual trade or port access.
Do not describe British ministers, the Irish Sea, or treaty ports when the counterpart and location make that description false.

Normalization requires the event settlement to be complete and Ireland to honor the 365-day interval.
It can produce opinion improvement and a mutually accepted trade or access arrangement.
The hardline choice prepares later doctrine and defenses but does not instantly restart the limited war.
A human counterpart can refuse optional normalization without undoing Irish reunification.

## U08: A settled national government

This capstone requires the constitutional mission, functioning northern reconstruction, and Settlement of at least 75.
It replaces the provisional domestic spirit with the chosen mature constitutional variant and opens the most demanding external charters.
A country that already holds the North as a core still receives the institutional payoff, without another core reward.
The group should celebrate functioning national institutions, not merely a changed map.

After this point, regional disputes become occasional tests of specific commitments.
They are not a permanent random drain that forces repeated purchases of the same settlement.
A government that later breaks its promises loses the corresponding benefit and has a defined revision or restoration path.
