# The Irish Army

The army lane begins with the force that actually won reunification.
Its purpose is to support a sustainable modern military, including a useful defensive strategy and several expeditionary alternatives.
The lane must contain equipment production, research, training, command, logistics, and operational content.
A series of generic attack bonuses does not satisfy it.

## M01: The permanent establishment

Inventory event and pre-existing formations, replace emergency support with the chosen peacetime system, and select a standing professional army or a reserve-heavy establishment.
Standing forces gain readiness and training efficiency with a larger continuing equipment burden.
Reserves gain a paid mobilization contract and lower peacetime support needs, with a preparation delay before an external operation.
Event formations can be demobilized only through a player-visible choice with verified unit ownership and equipment accounting.
The group never deletes the entire army to simplify implementation.

## M02: Infantry equipment and workshops

Create a concrete production program for rifles, support equipment, and field maintenance.
Choose domestic workshops with durable capacity or an initial foreign procurement contract that provides usable equipment sooner.
Foreign procurement requires a valid supplier or an explicit market-equivalent mechanism.
The domestic alternative remains available when no supplier accepts.
A coherent technology bonus helps the next relevant equipment generation without jumping far ahead of the game year.

## M03: Artillery and fire support

Develop artillery production, trained fire-control personnel, and support equipment.
A field-artillery priority helps a conventional land army.
A mobile-support priority costs additional trucks and integrates with M07.
The player gains a paid training mission and a bounded equipment-production improvement.
Its completion is tied to actual industrial and training commitments.
Heavy artillery is a later option where supported by existing equipment families, not an invented national weapon system.

## M04: Professional infantry

Develop experienced infantry formations and officer schooling.
The first specialization chooses assault training or disciplined defensive infantry.
Assault training supports a more demanding artillery and logistics requirement.
Defensive infantry helps conserve manpower and prepare the island without obliging the player to invade.
The existing Defense Establishment family holds the selected specialization's limited modifiers.
A paid retraining contract applies to the event's eligible ordinary formations through verified mechanisms and cannot be repeated to manufacture unlimited experience.

## M05: The reserve system

A reserve-heavy establishment builds mobilization lists, regional depots, and equipment stocks.
A professional government can also establish a smaller reserve system but does not receive the full reserve doctrine payoff.
Mobilization consumes recorded reserves, manpower, and time.
Deactivation returns only surviving equipment and personnel through supported accounting.
If exact disband-return control is unavailable, use ordinary recruitment with training bonuses and transparent player-managed demobilization.
Do not create a fictional reusable manpower bank.

## M06: Logistics and medical support

Develop transport, maintenance, field support, and casualty replacement capacity.
The meaningful choice is between a larger homeland depot system and a smaller deployable support establishment.
The former improves resilience and preparedness at Irish-owned locations.
The latter supports overseas commitments but costs more trucks and fuel.
A finite supply exercise connects the choice to operational use.
The baseline includes useful logistics, while higher tiers increase the scale of paid training and support capacity.

## M07: Mobile formations

Mechanization is earned through production and research.
Begin with motorized formations and a maintenance program.
A later choice emphasizes a small mechanized force or a broader truck-supported infantry army.
The mechanized option requires actual equipment technology and industrial capacity, with more fuel and support costs.
The broader option remains useful for a smaller Irish economy.
No focus instantly converts every infantry formation into a fully equipped mechanized division.
Existing advanced technology is recognized and redundant research preparation can be bypassed.

## M08: Special forces and amphibious warfare

Choose a mountain and difficult-terrain emphasis or a marine and landing emphasis as the first special-forces investment.
Both use ordinary supported battalion families and the actual special-forces cap.
The landing route needs escorts, transport, coastal intelligence, and air preparation from the navy and air branches.
The terrain route benefits Scottish or other difficult campaigns and island defense.
Higher Evolutions expand training capacity and allow a secondary specialization, with paid preparation and the actual equipment burden.

## M09: Operational command

The army capstone turns its selected doctrine into a repeatable but bounded national capability.
A defensive government can complete an island-defense exercise using garrisons, supply, and air cover.
An empire can complete an Irish Sea expeditionary preparation.
A federation can complete an accepted joint exercise with a real partner contribution.
A primary Atlantic government can qualify a supported overseas force.
The corresponding mission grants a lasting selected-family improvement and a later training opportunity with cooldown.
It never gives all four operational rewards at once.

Officer expansion is driven by the actual roster and force needs.
Reuse verified existing characters and recruit only to fill a genuine gap.
Final traits, portraits, and dates belong in the character audit.
The military lane does not require a new national leader, an invented historical commander, or a custom 3D unit family.
