# Evolution design

## Shared entry and timed progression

| Tier | Chaos threshold | Working identity | Immediate attempt | Earned campaign expansion |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline | Below first enabled threshold | Reclamation | Five-division entitlement | Complete national campaign and all strategic destinations |
| I | 200 | Army of Reunification | Ten-division cumulative entitlement | Professional mobilization, deeper artillery and territorial preparation |
| II | 400 | Gaelic Awakening | Fifteen-division cumulative entitlement | Broader Scottish and Welsh interactions, cultural institutions, regional reach |
| III | 600 | Empire from the Isles | Twenty-division cumulative entitlement | Major imperial administration, federal coordination, wider Atlantic campaigning |

At initial commitment, use the highest enabled eligible tier under the existing Evolution-setting contract.
When a later threshold becomes eligible, progression uses sequential timed Evolution opportunities with a starting MTTH design value of 90 days.
Ninety days is a tuning input, not a promise that exactly half or any other claimed share of campaigns has upgraded by that date.
The actual timing distribution must be evaluated with the installed version's verified probability adapter.
Prepare the event context before evaluating the enabled guard as required by the supplied event skill.
Disabled Evolution settings must not write completed-stage flags or award content.

Only the active attempt and a successfully unlocked national campaign can progress.
A permanently failed attempt stops this progression.
A later drop in Chaos does not revoke a tier already earned.
It prevents a not-yet-qualified higher step from starting until eligibility returns.
Use the shared project's actual MTTH eligibility semantics for an interrupted waiting interval and document them during implementation.
Do not create a second independent timer model for this event.

## Army of Reunification

During the opening war, grant the cumulative entitlement difference and improve the single emergency Defense Establishment spirit.
Open an extra tactical preparation choice between artillery concentration and reserve coordination.
Both consume real available equipment or experience through the normal decision system.
Neither changes the territorial victory test.

After reunification, add larger paid training batches, a corps-support organization, and a deeper logistics preparation sequence.
This tier provides a better foundation for a regional campaign.
It does not issue an immediate invasion order against Britain or add foreign cores.
The baseline army branches remain usable without this tier.

## Gaelic Awakening

Expand the Gaelic institutional branch with overseas cultural links and separate Scottish and Welsh political interactions.
A Scottish language and education agreement is distinct from an independence demand.
Welsh and Breton participation has its own cultural and political basis and is not described as Irish Gaelic assimilation.
A successful cultural relationship improves the terms of later diplomacy but cannot override a human refusal.

The empire gains an additional preparatory choice between securing Scottish institutions and pressing a wider British-isles claim program.
The second choice raises External Strain and weakens federal credibility.
The federation gains support for a Breton associated partner and practical education and industrial exchanges.
Atlantic governments can organize a more developed diaspora investment round and an additional island-access negotiation.
Each addition uses the same cost, consent, and territorial rules as the baseline route.

## Empire from the Isles

The empire can move from its Irish-Scottish foundation to a larger network of dominions and Atlantic bases.
It receives late administration, shipbuilding, and expeditionary groups that require prior infrastructure and manageable External Strain.
A proposed high-tier maritime protectorate requires a concrete target, a negotiated or ordinary-war acquisition, and a separate integration choice.
It never changes every British possession to Irish ownership at proclamation.

The federation receives a standing joint defense council, larger agreed expeditionary quotas, and a common procurement program.
Members retain their tags and can decline new obligations.
A member that withdraws support reduces actual common capacity.
The Irish player cannot obtain a larger military from a partner that has refused or lost its territory.

The primary Atlantic route gains a second mature operational circuit, expanded naval aviation, and a larger but still paid expeditionary establishment.
It can support a sustained campaign beyond the immediate Irish Sea.
The cost is greater convoy exposure, fuel use, and External Strain.
A strong defensive Atlantic strategy remains valid and does not need a universal conquest objective.

## Presentation and duplication

Evolution notifications explain the new opportunities and any real delivered increment.
They do not repeat the reunification announcement or imply the tree has been unlocked again.
Use one actor-scoped popup and one existing event-log update per stage.
Do not count an Evolution as a new cluster firing or award Chaos just for upgrading.
A replay or duplicate callback must not create another package, news event, achievement award, or focus reload.
