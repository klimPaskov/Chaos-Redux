# National conditions and the three-spirit budget

## Northern Settlement

Northern Settlement is a public integer from 0 to 100.
It begins at 35 after successful reunification.
It is not used before victory and is not a prerequisite for receiving the tree.
The value changes through completed projects, policy choices, interrupted guarantees, and specific domestic incidents.
There is no automatic daily decay.

| Range | Working condition | Gameplay meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 24 | Disrupted administration | Northern projects are slower and a recovery action is prioritized |
| 25 to 49 | Provisional settlement | Reconstruction works, but full institutional payoffs are unavailable |
| 50 to 74 | Functional settlement | Broader northern recruitment, construction, and external agreements become credible |
| 75 to 89 | Established settlement | Core integration where needed, imperial charter, and federal charter can qualify |
| 90 to 100 | Mature settlement | Best voluntary cooperation and domestic recovery payoffs |

Typical completed contributions are 10 points for a funded reconstruction round, 15 for honoring a constitutional settlement, 10 for a public-service agreement, and 10 for a completed judicial and security review.
A broken explicit guarantee costs 15 points.
A failed reconstruction mission caused by renewed occupation costs 5 points, with no repeated loss every day.
Political events cannot reduce the value several times for the same breach.

Regional autonomy permits access to the strongest voluntary Scottish and Welsh agreements once its institutions are funded.
Equal-citizenship unitary rule reaches the same high Settlement range through legal guarantees and functioning representation.
Concentrated central rule offers a quicker initial 10-point administrative gain and manpower access, but starts with a 10-point political loss and blocks the best federal charter until a constitutional revision is completed.
This offset is displayed together.
The player should see the bargain before choosing it.

Do not remove an existing Irish core to simulate unrest.
If the North is not already an Irish core, a successful integration mission at Settlement 75 or higher can award the core after 180 days of Irish ownership and control.
The core is a legal and mechanical integration reward.
Local political opposition is represented through decisions and modifiers, not by repeatedly stripping and restoring cores.

## External Strain

External Strain is a public integer from 0 to 100, beginning at zero after victory.
It records commitments made by this campaign.
Ordinary international tension remains the shared game value and is not duplicated here.

Commitments have explicit event-owned contributions.
A funded expeditionary agreement adds 10 while active.
A separately administered overseas acquisition adds 10 until its administration mission is complete.
A broken partner guarantee adds 15 until its reconciliation contract is settled.
A formal imperial expansion program adds 10 during its active preparation or administration period.
These contributions are recorded individually so that ending one removes its own burden exactly once.
Do not subtract an arbitrary amount every month regardless of remaining obligations.

| Range | Working condition | Gameplay meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 24 | Supported commitments | Full route benefits and normal expansion options |
| 25 to 49 | Stretched capacity | Additional foreign projects cost 25 percent more of their primary investment |
| 50 to 74 | Heavy commitments | New optional expeditions pause and administration takes 25 percent longer |
| 75 to 89 | Overextended | Further expansion declarations from this package are blocked and a consolidation mission opens |
| 90 to 100 | Emergency retrenchment | The player must end or renegotiate one active commitment before starting new external projects |

A native defensive war is never prohibited by a campaign meter.
The blocks apply to optional Event 072 decisions and focus rewards that create new commitments.
Where a focus has already been paid for, it awards preparation and retains a visible later execution decision instead of silently losing its reward.
Costs are rounded to the project's supported increments and displayed after all modifiers.

Ending an expedition, releasing an eligible administered territory through a lawful agreement, finishing a port administration program, or renegotiating an obligation reduces the associated contribution.
A general consolidation mission can reduce a residual political burden by 10, has a 180-day cooldown, and requires an actual unresolved burden.
It cannot push the value below the sum of active obligations.

## Discrete information

Gaelic education, national cultural institutions, and a public charter are three completed milestones, not another progress meter.
Federal partner status uses named stages such as invited, accepted, chartered, and withdrawn.
Officer training uses finite mission completion flags.
The GUI may show these factual stages and native manpower, factories, experience, and equipment without calling them new custom values.
Hidden numerical helpers are permitted only for calculation, grants, timing, and ownership accounting.
They must not quietly become additional player-managed systems.

## Three national spirit families

| Family | Opening state | Postwar states | Main effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Settlement and State | Absent before victory | Provisional, autonomous, civic-unitary, centralized, revised | Northern administration, stability, construction and integration conditions |
| Defense Establishment | Emergency support | Standing force, reserve system, professional, expeditionary | Logistics, training, planning, reinforcement and selected military specialization |
| External Commitments | Absent before victory | Supported, stretched, heavy, overextended, retrenching | Foreign-project costs and administration burden |

Only one state of each family can exist at a time.
Evolution changes the existing Defense Establishment family.
Gaelic institutional rewards are included in the applicable Settlement and State family or delivered through specific unlocks and production investments.
The Gaelic Empire's national identity is expressed through staged variants of these families, its flag, cosmetic identity, claims, decisions, and focus content.
It does not add a fourth permanent imperial spirit.

The three-spirit budget applies to temporary Event 072 ideas as well as permanent ones.
Do not move a forbidden fourth spirit into an invisible idea as a bookkeeping trick.
State-specific physical reconstruction effects can remain state modifiers where they genuinely describe a location.
Do not spread a national rule across many state modifiers merely to evade the spirit limit.

## Starting balance for spirit effects

Provisional settlement can impose 5 percent lower stability and 5 percent slower northern construction through the appropriate supported scopes.
A mature civic or autonomous settlement replaces those penalties with a 5 percent stability benefit and useful northern construction access.
A centralized settlement can offer 5 percent mobilization or training improvement through supported modifiers, with the explicit diplomatic restrictions and domestic cost described above.
Exact modifier keys must be verified in installed documentation.
If a proposed northern-only modifier has no valid state scope, implement its concrete project effect instead of inventing a national modifier that claims to be local.

Military specializations should generally use 5 to 15 percent supported modifiers alongside actual capacity and equipment effects.
The strongest 20 to 25 percent emergency supply support belongs to the temporary opening and cannot remain as a permanent universal army advantage.
Final numbers require direct comparisons among all constitutional and strategic combinations.
