# Celtic partner country packages

## Existing states and new releases

Use existing Scottish, Welsh, and Breton country definitions wherever they are valid.
SCO, WLS, and BRI are candidate conventional tags that must be confirmed against the active repository and installed country data before runtime use.
Do not reserve a new tag merely because it is easier than inspecting an existing one.
A cosmetic change must not break identity checks.
A released country needs a capital, owned territory, population, coherent technologies and laws, a functioning army and economy, country localisation, flags, and appropriate content.

Existing countries retain their national trees, leaders, advisors, laws, technologies, and military.
They receive additive treaty decisions or compatible focus content only after acceptance.
An existing human partner is never forced into a generic Irish-provided tree.
A new release with no adequate native content receives the country-specific supplementary branch groups below.
Use the installed game's strongest valid baseline content where possible.
The supplement is a real playable package, not an empty generic tree with one alliance focus.

## Formation and transfer accounting

Territory must come from lawful release, a settled war, or an accepted owner agreement.
A focus cannot create a sovereign country in an uninvolved foreign state without a supported political process.
The new country inherits the territory's real buildings and population under the tested release behavior.
Do not add copies of every inherited factory as a separate reward.
Its capital is selected from its valid owned homeland using existing country history where possible, with a controlled fallback.
Do not invent a historical capital assignment without verifying the existing package.

Grant a coherent defensive technology and law floor appropriate to the date.
Do not copy every Irish technology or create an advanced doctrine tree through a release effect.
A country with fewer usable research slots than the approved release floor can be brought to that floor.
Never reduce an existing country's slots or overwrite superior equipment.
A proposed new-release floor is two research slots, with a third earned through its own reconstruction or higher-tier shared program.

A finite emergency defensive force can accompany an event-created release where the normal release has no viable army.
The grant is consumed once per country and release provenance.
Existing countries receive paid aid instead.
The proposed new-release formation ceilings by Irish tier are Scotland 3, 5, 7, 10 ordinary infantry divisions, Wales 2, 3, 4, 5, and Brittany 2, 3, 5, 7.
Use lower actual grants when the country already has a functioning army or cannot sustain the ceiling.
The supply and manpower ledger must account for filled formations separately from reserve stocks.
These are defensive startup ceilings requiring balance tests, not recurring entitlements.

Irish aid after that point consumes real Irish equipment and Political Power through a bilateral offer.
The recipient must be valid at acceptance, and the transfer occurs once.
A rejected or expired offer does not delete equipment.
No unlimited refund loop is created by release, annexation, and release again.
Permanent provenance markers survive identity changes where the engine supports them safely.

## Scotland supplement

SC01 establishes the new administration and restores the most urgent economic functions.
The player chooses civil reconstruction or immediate defense and receives a finite work program.
SC02 defines relations with Ireland and the prior holder through independent acceptance, association, neutrality, or a valid hostile stance.
Independence is not conditional on later accepting a federation unless an explicitly negotiated treaty stated that obligation before release.

SC03 develops maritime and industrial capacity using actual owned locations.
It connects to a coastal-defense program and a paid procurement opportunity.
SC04 builds a trained infantry and logistics establishment suited to Scotland's terrain and coastline.
SC05 offers its own language and cultural policy without treating all Scottish identity as Irish Gaelic culture.
SC06 chooses a federal charter, a narrower security partnership, or independent consolidation.
A country declining federation still has complete economic, military, and domestic play.
SC07 develops either a maritime contribution or an agreed expeditionary contribution based on real capacity.

A Scottish government under an imperial dominion arrangement receives distinct sovereignty choices and can object to a proposed union.
A human player's explicit acceptance is required before a transfer that ends independent country control.
An AI evaluates the actual settlement and threat instead of always surrendering its country because Ireland completed a focus.

## Wales supplement

WA01 establishes the government and basic economic administration.
WA02 focuses on industrial recovery, transport, and labor relations through real facilities.
WA03 develops a small coherent land-defense force and an equipment reserve.
WA04 develops coastal support, ports, and air defense suited to its own territory.
WA05 addresses Welsh cultural institutions and political self-government.
WA06 chooses federal membership, a limited procurement and defense pact, or an independent industrial strategy.
WA07 provides a mature contribution based on factories, supply, coastal operations, or a small expeditionary force.

Welsh participation must be useful without pretending it can field a large army immediately.
A federal procurement role may be its best contribution.
Where the country lacks the promised industrial capacity, it can accept a smaller commitment and obtain a correspondingly smaller shared benefit.
The system must not mint military equipment based solely on having a Welsh tag.

## Brittany supplement

BR01 establishes civil administration and the basic economic recovery program.
BR02 develops ports and local transport.
BR03 organizes defensive infantry and coastal support.
BR04 provides cultural and external relationship choices, including a distinct relationship with the territory's former holder.
BR05 offers association with the Celtic league, full federal participation when eligible, or a separate maritime policy.
BR06 develops a funded maritime contribution or export-oriented industrial project.

Brittany is an optional addition to the main Irish campaign.
It has enough domestic and military content to remain playable when it declines Ireland's wider ambitions.
Its absence does not block Scotland, Wales, or Ireland from completing the baseline federal route.

## Human and AI participation

Every human recipient sees the offer, territorial result, resource contribution, war obligation, and exit terms before accepting.
A human refusal does not cause an AI-only fallback to execute the same effect anyway.
An absent or disconnected multiplayer player follows the normal game's decision timeout behavior specified by the host's actual rules, not an invented automatic annexation policy.
Timeout defaults preserve current sovereignty and spend no unaccepted resources.

AI partners prioritize domestic viability, defense, and affordable commitments.
A low-manpower partner favors equipment or access agreements over troop obligations.
A country under immediate threat values credible defense support, but should reject an offer that provides no actual protection.
AI acceptance weights must be inspected with the probability tools during implementation.
No percentages are claimed for the provisional priorities in this package.

## Identity, art, and government

Retain valid native flag and portrait assets.
Create new original assets only for missing country-package surfaces or the federal emblem.
Do not generate a portrait of a real historical person without a verified source and identity.
Fictional filler leaders are not mandatory.
Character shortages are resolved through the existing country package first.
Country-specific news and treaty text use the actual government and current counterpart, with no forced dated title.
