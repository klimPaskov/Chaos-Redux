# Ireland Reclaims the North

## Catalog entry

- Event ID: `72`
- Event name: Ireland Reclaims the North
- Type: Minor Fire-Once
- Status: To Be Reworked
- Chaos level: 1
- Cluster: Formables
- Cluster role: Low member

## Core idea

> Ireland launches a sudden campaign to reclaim Northern Ireland. The event gives Ireland an immediate military force for the attempt, but the much larger reward is conditional. Ireland only unlocks its new national focus tree if it successfully takes Northern Ireland and completes reunification.

The event is a short opening war that can unlock a much larger Irish campaign.

## Availability

Event 72 requires:

- Ireland to exist
- Ireland to not already control Northern Ireland
- a valid country to control Northern Ireland
- Ireland to be capable of entering the reclamation war

If Ireland already controls Northern Ireland, the event should not fire.

## The reclamation war

When Event 72 fires, Ireland immediately begins the attempt to reclaim Northern Ireland.

Ireland receives a temporary opening military package so it has a realistic chance of winning even if it was weak before the event.

The package should include actual assets such as:

- new divisions
- infantry equipment
- support equipment
- artillery
- trucks where appropriate
- manpower
- fuel and supply support
- Army Experience
- a few capable commanders if required

The force scales substantially with the active Evolution.

The goal of the opening war is specifically **Northern Ireland**. Ireland is not required to conquer Britain or conduct a full invasion of Great Britain.

## Reclamation peace

Once Ireland controls Northern Ireland and the required territorial condition is secured, the reclamation war can end through a scripted peace.

If Britain is the opponent, peace is signed between Ireland and Britain.

If campaign circumstances placed Northern Ireland under another valid country, the same reclamation logic should work against that holder.

Ireland keeps Northern Ireland and the wider war created specifically by Event 72 ends.

The peace should prevent the event from turning into an unnecessary total conquest of Britain after Ireland has already achieved its objective.

## Success requirement

Successfully reclaiming Northern Ireland is the gateway to the rest of Event 72.

Only after Ireland secures Northern Ireland does it receive:

- the full new Irish focus tree
- the reunited national identity
- post-reunification political content
- expanded military content
- new diplomatic ambitions
- later formable routes

If Ireland fails to reclaim Northern Ireland, it **does not receive the new focus tree**.

The large Event 72 campaign therefore has to be earned through the opening war.

## Immediate success aftermath

After reunification, Ireland consolidates the North and transitions from a small reclamation campaign into a much broader national project.

The early focus tree should deal with:

- integration of Northern Ireland
- treatment of local political opposition
- rebuilding war damage
- Irish military expansion
- development of Belfast and northern industry
- national reconciliation or hardline centralization
- relations with Britain after the settlement

From there, the tree branches into much larger ambitions.

## Large Irish focus tree

### A United Ireland

The common opening branch establishes control over the reunited island.

It covers political integration, economic development, infrastructure, military reform, and the new constitutional position of Northern Ireland.

Different routes can pursue reconciliation, assimilation, regional autonomy, or much harder central rule.

### The Gaelic Revival

Ireland can turn reunification into a wider cultural transformation.

This route can emphasize:

- Irish language revival
- Gaelic institutions
- historical symbolism
- cultural nationalism
- revival of traditional military imagery
- transformation of the state around a more explicitly Gaelic identity

A strong Gaelic revival becomes one of the prerequisites for the more ambitious imperial route.

### The Gaelic Empire

Ireland can eventually proclaim a **Gaelic Empire**.

This should be a major formable path rather than simply a cosmetic rename.

The route can pursue territories and political influence connected to broader Gaelic and Celtic historical ambitions.

Possible areas of interest can include:

- Scotland
- the Isle of Man
- Celtic regions of Britain
- Atlantic islands
- other historically or culturally connected territories where the final full specification supports them

The Gaelic Empire should have its own flag, country name, national ideas, claims, military identity, and late focus branches.

### The Celtic Question

Ireland can instead pursue cooperation with other Celtic peoples.

This can involve:

- supporting Scottish independence
- supporting Welsh nationalism
- relations with Breton or other Celtic movements where relevant
- creating Celtic allies rather than directly annexing everyone
- establishing a Celtic political or military bloc

This can eventually create a more federative alternative to the Gaelic Empire.

### An Atlantic Power

A reunited Ireland can develop into a major Atlantic state.

This branch can focus on:

- naval expansion
- ports
- submarines
- convoy warfare
- merchant shipping
- airbases
- Atlantic islands
- overseas diplomacy
- relations with North America

It provides an alternative to focusing exclusively on Britain.

### The Irish Army

The military branch transforms the emergency reclamation army into a permanent modern force.

It can specialize in:

- elite infantry
- artillery
- mechanization
- special forces
- defensive warfare
- amphibious operations
- rapid mobilization

The temporary opening units should therefore become the foundation of a much larger military rather than remaining the entire Irish army.

## Evolution I: The Army of Reunification

- Chaos requirement: `200+`.

Ireland receives a substantially stronger force when the event fires.

The opening army gains:

- more divisions
- larger equipment reserves
- more artillery
- stronger support companies
- more manpower
- better commanders
- greater logistical preparation

The focus tree after victory also gains stronger military and territorial ambitions.

## Evolution II: The Gaelic Awakening

- Chaos requirement: `400+`.

The immediate reclamation force becomes large enough for Ireland to fight a serious regional war.

The later focus tree expands its Gaelic and Celtic routes.

Possible additional content includes:

- stronger Scottish interaction
- Welsh and Celtic political movements
- more aggressive Gaelic Empire claims
- additional cultural and political integration systems
- expanded Atlantic ambitions

Ireland can begin transforming from a reunited island state into a genuine regional power.

## Evolution III: Empire from the Isles

- Chaos requirement: `600+`.

Ireland receives its strongest opening military package.

If it successfully reunifies, the late focus tree becomes much more expansionist.

The Gaelic Empire can pursue a much larger imperial project, while alternative routes can build a powerful Celtic federation or Atlantic bloc.

The military tree also receives stronger elite formations, naval expansion, and the ability to support much wider campaigns beyond Ireland.

## Failure

If Ireland loses the reclamation war or is unable to secure Northern Ireland, Event 72 does not grant the large new focus tree.

Ireland keeps whatever consequences resulted naturally from the war, but the imperial restoration campaign does not begin.

This makes the initial Northern Ireland campaign the defining test of the event.

## AI direction

AI Ireland should strongly prioritize Northern Ireland once Event 72 begins.

It should not waste its opening army on unrelated fronts before completing the reclamation objective.

Once Northern Ireland is secured and peace is signed, AI behavior can follow the selected focus route.

A Gaelic Empire route should be aggressive.

A Celtic cooperation route should prefer allied or released Celtic governments.

An Atlantic route should prioritize naval and overseas strength.

## Presentation

The opening event should announce Ireland's decision to reclaim Northern Ireland.

Successful reunification should receive a larger news event because it marks the actual beginning of the Event 72 campaign.

Later major news can include:

- completion of Irish reunification
- proclamation of the Gaelic Empire
- establishment of a Celtic alliance
- Scottish or Welsh alignment with Ireland
- Ireland emerging as a major Atlantic power
