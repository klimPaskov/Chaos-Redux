The core progression is fixed by the brief.
Baseline at Chaos 1 requests two or three small minor targets.
Partition Fever at 200 requests four or five small or medium minors.
Grand Partitions at 600 requests six through eight eligible minors, including larger countries where the current campaign supports their partition.
Major powers remain immune as targets at every stage.
The actual positive target count can be smaller when a complete compatible pool is smaller.

The expanded design keeps one public Coalition Cohesion value and exact native state allocations.
Baseline concessions retain the capital and a meaningful remnant.
Targets can prevent the partition completely through diplomacy or successful defense.
Claimant victories, incomplete transfers, full multi-party partitions and unrelated annexations have distinct outcomes.
