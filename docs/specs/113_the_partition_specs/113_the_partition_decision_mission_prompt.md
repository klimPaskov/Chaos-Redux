## One authority for every action

One actor, crisis, role, phase, proposal version and quote identify a current action opportunity.
The same authority is used by the native decision path and the human dossier.
Only that authority may debit, deliver, sign, authorize or issue a terminal receipt.
Changing the selected country or state in the GUI changes navigation only.

Recheck current identity, major immunity, ownership and control, coalition membership, recipient, legal route, deadline and exact quote before any paid or irreversible step.
An invalid stale action fails before cost.
Maintain at most one pending affirmative native confirmation per actor across every Partition crisis.
Use the in-window complete review and the native safe confirmation defined in Part 8, with cancel first and the actual 13-day native timeout.
Cancellation, timeout, invalidation and consumption release the pending authority without extending the crisis deadline.

Keep territorial consent, claimant division approval and actual war authorization as different facts.
A target signature is needed for a peaceful territorial award.
A claimant signature on its desired division cannot create target consent.
A human target's refusal or silence cannot declare war on its behalf.
