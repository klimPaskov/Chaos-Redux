# 113 The Partition: Achievements and campaign examples

The achievement set rewards difficult outcomes across the target, protector, mediator, and claimant routes.
Each achievement requires a real campaign result plus a sustained consequence.
Opening a crisis, signing a document, delivering one cheap package, or watching another country win is insufficient.

All achievement names below are working labels and technical keys.
The final title and description must be written from their direction after implementation confirms the exact conditions.
The state and participant records are shared with the crisis outcome system rather than reconstructed from a single final map.

## A1 Independent defense

**Working key:** `chaosx_113_unbroken_frontier`.
The title direction should emphasize a small country keeping its frontier through its own war effort.
The description direction must communicate every major restriction without suggesting that receiving an ultimatum alone is progress.

The player must be the continuing target of a baseline opening crisis.
At opening it is an independent minor outside every faction, with a valid proposed loss and at least two accepted claimants.
At least one of those claimants is a major when it actually enters the target's partition war.
At least two original claimants must actually fight the target.

The player obtains the full-prevention-by-defense outcome and then owns and fully controls every opening state for 180 consecutive days after the last relevant aggression ends.
It remains independent and outside a faction throughout the crisis and the 180-day hold.
No foreign country may enter that war on the target's side during the qualifying defense.
The target may use its own preparations and counterproposals, but must not accept an Event 113 outside protection commitment.
Ordinary preexisting trade and equipment acquisition are not retroactively classified as prohibited event protection.

Capitulation, subject status, faction membership, accepted event protection, or an allied defensive belligerent disqualifies this attempt.
Any loss of ownership or full control of an opening state during the 180-day postwar hold permanently fails this crisis's achievement attempt rather than restarting its timer.
An outside war that happens to defeat a claimant does not count unless the target's own qualifying partition war occurred and the full-prevention outcome is proved.
If the active crisis later evolves, its baseline opening classification is retained for this achievement while the additional difficulty remains real.

Track the opening identity, opening state set, opening faction status, original claimant identities, actual war participants, major status at each claimant's entry, accepted protection history, capitulation history, and the continuous postwar hold.
A later new crisis is a new attempt, with the event's normal reselection protection still applying.
This achievement is difficult because the player must turn a genuine multi-country war into complete territorial prevention while declining the event's most direct external support route.

## A2 Three simultaneous shields

**Working key:** `chaosx_113_three_shields`.
The title direction should suggest a foreign power holding several independent countries together through a wider crisis.
The description direction must make clear that all three protected countries must survive intact.

The qualifying crises must be three distinct targets from the same wave that opened as Grand Partitions.
The player accepts a legal defensive commitment to each while each crisis is unresolved.
The ordinary three-commitment country cap applies, so these commitments use all three slots.
The player cannot also be a claimant in that wave.

Each of the three targets must enter an actual partition war against at least two recorded claimants.
At least one actual attacking claimant across those wars must be a major.
The player must enter the defense of every target and satisfy the Task D actual-war-participation standard for each crisis.
The short-war exception applies only under its exact five-day entry and continuous participation rule.
A merged war can satisfy several participation records only when each protected target and its relevant attackers are actually present in that war.

The player must also complete a credited arms delivery to at least one of the three targets after accepting its commitment.
All three targets must achieve full prevention.
Each must then remain independent with every opening state owned and fully controlled for 180 consecutive days after the last of the three qualifying wars ends.
The protector remains independent during that hold.

Any qualifying target's extinction, subjection, concluded territorial loss, or incomplete defense breaks the attempt.
A pledge without actual entry, a war against an unrelated claimant elsewhere, or three achievements attached to one target cannot satisfy the conditions.
The three outcomes are individual records even when diplomacy or wars merge.

Track the wave, the three crisis identifiers, commitment and entry dates, real rosters, the delivery receipt, each full-prevention outcome, and the common 180-day hold start.
The achievement rewards difficult allocation of a protector's army and resources across several fronts.
It does not require three concurrent Tasks, since the independent achievement tracker can observe qualifying outcomes while the one-Partition-Task limit remains intact.

## A3 Renunciation followed by protection

**Working key:** `chaosx_113_renounced_share`.
The title direction should express a demanding reversal of policy without celebrating the original coercion.
The description direction must identify both the abandoned core demand and the subsequent successful protection.

The player begins as an accepted claimant in a Partition Fever or Grand Partitions crisis.
The first valid public demand is a complete partition, with at least three claimants and at least one major claimant other than the player.
The player's initial assigned share includes at least one state that was already its core before the crisis opened.
A core created after opening cannot qualify.

The player fully withdraws every territorial demand through C4 before any award to it.
Its full withdrawal closes claimant reentry for that crisis and starts the normal renunciation period.
After at least 15 days, the target explicitly accepts the former claimant as a protector under V3.
The former claimant then delivers both a credited arms package and a credited transport package from its real reserves.

The target must obtain full prevention or a completed limited settlement that retains its capital and at least 50% of both opening population and opening physical industry.
Ignore the industrial percentage when the opening industrial total was zero.
The target must remain independent and the settlement conditions must hold for 180 consecutive days after the relevant outcome.
The player must receive none of the target's opening states by any method from opening through that hold.

Any direct award, later annexation of a qualifying state, renewed claimant role, aggression against the target, unpaid delivery, or target extinction disqualifies the attempt.
During the 180-day hold, a broken settlement, loss of the target's retained capital control, or failure of its required independence or retained-population and industry conditions permanently fails this attempt rather than restarting it.
Merely withdrawing from a doomed coalition is insufficient because the player must spend material, receive the target's consent, and help secure a successful durable result.

Track the preopening core facts, first public allocation, other major claimant, withdrawal timestamp, target's later protection acceptance, both delivery receipts, retained ratios, state recipients, and the 180-day hold.
This route uses an existing identity and ordinary diplomacy, with no free ideology change or automatic forgiveness.

## A4 Recover the opening map

**Working key:** `chaosx_113_map_restored`.
The title direction should communicate recovery from a real partition rather than an effortless cancellation of a proposal.
The description direction must identify the territorial loss, actual recovery war, deadline, and later hold.

The player is the continuing target identity of an evolved crisis that concludes with a limited or interrupted settlement leaving it independent.
The actual awarded states must contain at least 25% of its opening population or at least 50% of its opening physical industry.
The industrial branch is ignored when the denominator is zero.
At least two distinct claimants must have received target states.

Within 1,080 days of that concluded loss, the player must own and fully control every state it owned when that crisis opened.
At least one originally awarded state must return through the player's actual war against a recorded recipient and the resulting native occupation or peace outcome.
A purely diplomatic return of every state does not satisfy the military recovery condition.
The event supplies neither free cores nor an automatic restoration transfer for this achievement.

After restoration, all opening states must remain owned and fully controlled for 180 consecutive days.
The player must retain its country identity and independence from the concluded partition through that hold.
Receiving additional ordinary territory is allowed, but does not replace a missing opening state.
Restored ownership without control starts no hold timer.

Extinction and later country recreation cannot continue the original attempt.
Subject status, identity substitution, a missed 1,080-day restoration deadline, or a later loss during the hold prevents completion of that attempt.
An actual restoration before the deadline may finish its 180-day hold afterward.

Track the opening and awarded state sets, snapshot loss ratios, each original recipient, loss conclusion date, actual recovery-war provenance, restoration date, and continuous hold.
This route is intentionally a long campaign challenge that gives a wounded survivor a later ambition.

## A5 Three durable settlements

**Working key:** `chaosx_113_three_settlements`.
The title direction should evoke repeated difficult diplomacy across a regional crisis.
The description direction must avoid equating a meeting or invitation with a settled country.

The player is a target-invited mediator for three distinct crises from one wave that opened as Partition Fever or Grand Partitions.
It accepts all three roles before any of their final settlements are ratified.
At least one first valid public demand is a complete partition.
At least one actual claimant across the three crises is a major.

For each target, the player must produce the material improvement required by Task S relative to the first valid public demand, whose immutable snapshot is bound when mediation is accepted.
The proposal must remove at least one demanded state, replace complete partition with a viable limited settlement, or secure an all-claimant no-transfer withdrawal.
The first valid public demand is retained separately so a mediator cannot invent a harsher baseline after joining.
The credited proposal must also materially improve the then-current offer after the player's own mediation acceptance, so it cannot merely claim another negotiator's completed concession.

Each target must obtain a completed limited settlement or an all-party binding no-transfer withdrawal.
Every limited settlement must preserve the capital and at least 50% of opening population and physical industry, ignoring a zero industrial denominator.
No partition aggression may begin against any of the three targets from the player's mediation acceptance through final ratification of that target's agreement.
The achievement therefore rewards preventing these wars, even if unrelated ordinary campaign wars continue elsewhere.

All three results must hold for 180 consecutive days after the last final agreement is executed.
Each target remains independent and every due treaty promise is fulfilled.
The player receives none of the three targets' opening states from the first mediation acceptance through the hold.

Claimant participation anywhere in the qualifying wave, a new partition war during the specified diplomatic interval, incomplete awards, broken material promises, or target extinction disqualifies the attempt.
A no-transfer withdrawal qualifies only when every remaining claimant renounces its current demand and the outcome record proves full prevention.

Track the three invitations, exact baselines, submitted improvements, required consents, war timelines, executed agreements, actual state receipts, and the common hold.
The player has to coordinate multiple distinct signatory groups before short diplomatic deadlines while accepting the material and political cost of mediation.

## A6 Keep the division together

**Working key:** `chaosx_113_division_held`.
The title direction should be cold and political, emphasizing the durability of an aggressive coalition's division.
The description direction must plainly state the two complete partitions and the difficult collective conditions.

The player is an independent claimant in two different target crises from the same wave that opened as Grand Partitions.
Both targets reject the division and enter actual native wars against the player and at least one other recorded claimant.
At least one major power must actually enter on a target's defensive side across those two wars.
Voluntary extinction and unrelated outside conquests cannot qualify.

Each target must reach the actual complete-partition outcome with at least two independent claimant beneficiaries.
The player must receive at least one state from each target and at least five target states in total.
Every claimant that remained committed when enforcement began must receive every state assigned to it in the last unanimously ratified claimant division before that enforcement.
Those claimant signatures describe their agreed division and do not substitute for target consent to peaceful transfer.
The actual native war and conference result must match those shares for the achievement to remain possible.

The coalition must be united with Cohesion at least 75 when each final partition outcome is recorded.
No pair of recorded claimant partners may enter war against each other from the first enforcement through the final hold.
Every beneficiary must remain independent and retain its awarded states for 180 consecutive days after the second complete partition.
The player's own independence and identity must also continue throughout.

A single-power annexation, missed promised share, opportunistic seizure from a partner, subject beneficiary, coalition betrayal, or loss of an awarded state during the hold disqualifies the attempt.
The rare super-event may occur during this route, but its display does not award the achievement.
The achievement has stricter personal, treaty, war, and durability conditions.

Track both crises, original enforced allocation versions, all enforcement signatories, actual defensive major entry, final owners, actual awards, cohesion at completion, all participant war relationships, and the 180-day hold.
This is the dangerous claimant route in the set, with the challenge coming from winning wider wars while preserving the division among competing powers.

## Shared tracking behavior

Eligibility begins at the first qualifying real condition for each achievement.
It is not backfilled from a later final map when the required history is absent.
The tracker binds each attempt to the player country identity, immutable crisis or wave identifiers, and opening state snapshots.
Player switching cannot transfer credit between country identities.

Continuous holds begin only when all required conditions are simultaneously true.
A checked loss, aggression, broken obligation, subject change, or invalidating identity change resets or fails the attempt as specified above.
Save and load preserve dates and receipts without adding time or replaying completion.
The native achievement owner grants the unlock once, after every condition is true.

Historical facts needed for active achievements survive normal crisis cleanup.
Once no Task, achievement, durability check, event log, or recovery window needs a detailed record, it can be reduced to its terminal summary under the bounded history policy.
The implementation must not keep an unlimited active country scan merely because an achievement exists.

## Worked campaign examples

These examples are synthetic design cases using anonymous countries and ordinary state counts.
They are not historical predictions, scripted target lists, or engine test results.
Population shares, factory totals, and dates are declared scenario inputs for explaining the intended result.

### E1 A baseline compromise

A small independent target owns eight states and 25 physical factories.
Two neighbors want one adjoining state each.
The two states contain 30% of opening population and 40% of opening factories, while the capital, sole port, and retained capital connection remain intact.
The two-state offer fits the baseline state budget and leaves a viable remnant.

The target pays for a counterproposal that exchanges one demanded industrial state for a less damaging connected state with a real claimant interest.
Both claimants approve the new version and the target ratifies.
The first and remaining awards execute at the posted day-5 and day-10 milestones.
Actual completion gives a limited-settlement outcome, and a 45-day compliant hold makes it durable.
The target survives with a meaningful loss and a reconstruction route if actual damaged facilities exist.

### E2 A baseline offer that cannot be made

A three-state minor's capital region holds most population and the only supply connection.
Two neighbors can reach different border states, but awarding both would leave only 20% of opening population in the retained capital component.
The coarse-map minimum of two demanded states cannot override the remnant threshold.
This target has no feasible baseline package and is excluded before any ultimatum or Chaos receipt.

The event uses another feasible target when one exists during opening selection.
It does not give either neighbor the capital, invent a tiny replacement state, or force complete partition to fill a slot.

### E3 One feasible target after a larger roll

A baseline wave rolls three targets, but only one target has a jointly feasible pair of interested claimants after major immunity, shared crisis capacity, and same-wave role conflicts are applied.
The other candidate countries each fail a declared gate.
The actual firing threatens the one feasible target and records the smaller result.
It creates one opening consequence and consumes one shared firing, without inventing two more countries.

If no feasible target exists, the candidate event is unavailable and no empty firing, cost, or Chaos consequence occurs.
An interrupted selection computation cannot claim that the pool is empty.

### E4 A strong neighbor abandons its share

A target obtains an operational guarantee from a power with a real entry route and ready forces.
One claimant decides the expected frontier gain is no longer worth the war and fully withdraws.
Its proposed awards and event enforcement authorization are removed.

The remaining claimant cannot conduct a coordinated partition alone.
It can seek a new eligible partner inside the current cap and deadline or end the attempt.
Ordinary unilateral aggression remains governed by the campaign's native rules and is reported separately from a completed multi-party partition.

### E5 Poland remains an eligible larger minor

In a particular Grand opening, Poland is independent, remains a minor, owns more than 20 states, and has several interested direct neighbors.
Its size excludes it from the small and medium bands but does not exclude it from Grand Partitions.
The proposal still has to pass exact border, recipient, state ownership, shared capacity, and military conditions.

Poland can refuse, acquire actual foreign protection, and defend successfully.
Its name adds no special surrender modifier and no promised historical outcome.
If it becomes a major before execution, remaining authored partition authority is invalidated.

### E6 A signature becomes stale during handover

Every party ratifies a four-state evolved settlement.
The first two frontier states transfer at the first milestone.
Before the remaining milestone, a third power acquires one pending state through an unrelated native peace.

The pending allocation is now invalid.
The remaining awards stop, the outcome records the two actual transfers, and the dossier identifies the third-party owner as the reason for interruption.
No substitute state is taken and no resource refund restores the two completed territorial awards.
A revised settlement needs fresh approval from every affected party.

### E7 Two wars become one

Two targets from one Grand wave are attacked by overlapping coalitions.
A common protector enters and the native war system merges the relationships into a broader conflict with additional belligerents.
Each crisis continues to record its target, opening states, claimants, and relevant aggression.

The event cannot call an unsafe pairwise peace and declare both targets saved.
One target can later survive intact while the other loses states in the same conference.
Tasks and achievements resolve from the two actual country outcomes, with shared native war accounting applied by its normal owner.

### E8 A pledge fails before it matters

A protector pays the 25-CP preparation cost and promises legal defensive intervention.
Before the war, its only valid entry relationship disappears.
The pledge becomes unfulfillable and the target is informed before making its next choice.

The paid preparation is not a delivered army and does not satisfy Task D.
The target can still negotiate, seek a different available commitment, or defend.
A later unrelated war by the former protector does not repair the failed commitment retroactively.

### E9 A Task deadline and outcome coincide

A defense Task expires on the same date that the last relevant aggression ends.
The target still owns all opening states, controls its capital and every valid threatened state, and has no pending award.
The outcome observer records the actual full-prevention fact with that date.

The Task consumer then checks the outcome date and the accepted actor's contribution record.
If both meet the contract by the deadline, the Task succeeds even if its interface updates afterward.
If the actual peace occurs after the deadline, a later successful defense does not retroactively earn the expired Task.

### E10 A country loses every state but the record survives

A Grand target is actually defeated and every current owned state is awarded to two independent recorded claimants.
The capital is the final award where an authored accepted handover is used, or follows the actual native conference result in war.
The target's active country scope can no longer carry the complete crisis record.

A surviving event observer records the final owners, beneficiaries, territorial loss, and native extinction status.
A government in exile, where the native campaign actually supports one, is recorded distinctly from owning a surviving territory.
Defense Tasks fail from the actual loss of the country rather than remaining active on a dead target scope.
The complete partition counts toward the rare Grand outcome only when its full trigger conditions are met.

### E11 The claimant coalition fights itself

Two claimants remain in a material dispute for 30 days over a connected industrial corridor.
Neither accepts a feasible revision and one authorizes a legal contest against the other.
Actual declaration ends their common partition pact and changes the wider strategic situation.

The target gains an opportunity to seek withdrawal or a defensive settlement, but does not receive an automatic victory while a claimant still wages a relevant war against it.
The claimant war is an ordinary native conflict with its own consequences.
The outcome record distinguishes coalition collapse from full prevention.

### E12 Chaos rises during an accepted settlement

A baseline wave has two original targets.
One has accepted a handover and the other is still negotiating when Chaos reaches 600.
The unresolved batch may progress through its active evolution clocks, but the two original target slots remain fixed.

The accepted handover keeps its exact states, terms, and dates.
The unresolved target can face an evolved revised demand only through new legal proposals and consent rules.
A later new firing at 600 can roll six to eight eligible targets after the existing diplomatic wave has closed and shared pacing permits it.
