## Critical cross-file contracts

### Selection, capacity and phase authority

The form thresholds, requested target ranges, claimant maxima and minor-only hard gates agree across the design and final handoffs.
Requested count is separated from the actual complete compatible count, with zero feasible targets preventing an empty firing.
The compatible opening search must consider real alternative coalitions before declaring a shortage.
It cannot silently truncate candidates or use an unfinished search as proof that a smaller pool is unavoidable.

One accepted actor-to-crisis membership contains its separately approved child obligations.
The cap is three accepted claimant or outside-support memberships per actor across active waves.
An invitation alone reserves no accepted membership.
One target may have at most two distinct outside support providers, while guarantee, aid, transport, mediation and intervention within one provider relationship retain their own consent and fulfillment records.
These definitions preserve the intended A2, A3 and A5 routes without creating free capacity or permitting simultaneous claimant and protector authority over the same target.

Native mission ownership is consistently bounded to three per country across all waves, including an attached Partition Task.
The target's one phase slot moves through ultimatum, handover and reconstruction.
Incoming requests and the Partition Task use the other defined slots, while recipients and prolonged war observation use dated dossier records.

Current phase and legal authority precede AI scores or action visibility.
Fractured enforcement, premature rival contest, unavailable entry, an invalid recipient, full membership capacity and major-target promotion cannot be overcome by a high weight or a stale button.
Postrejection action availability and late invitations match the corrected review-window and unused-extension rules.

### Consent, deadlines, identity and outcome races

The baseline ultimatum is 60 days and an evolved opening ultimatum is 45 days.
Active evolution does not shorten a promised absolute deadline or change an already ratified handover.
One permitted 15-day extension requires genuine progress, and a material new offer requires its stated review opportunity rather than an unlimited resubmission loop.

The persistent dossier provides full terms before an identity-bound native confirmation.
One affirmative confirmation may be outstanding per actor across crises, with safe cancellation, explicit release and no deadline extension.
Actor identity, crisis, proposal version, recipient, legal route and current quote are revalidated before an irreversible action.
Navigation and closing the dossier carry no purchase or signature side effect.

Country-generation identity, immutable crisis keys and surviving outcome authority prevent a recycled tag, vanished target or overwritten selected row from inheriting consent or rewards.
Staged handover rechecks the remaining whole award vector and each state immediately before execution.
Actual completed awards remain history if a later award fails, with no promised atomic rollback, invented substitute state or unearned top-up.

Native war entry, actual belligerents, ended aggression and actual peace awards govern war results.
Merged wars and unrelated alliances cannot be erased through an assumed pairwise peace API.
Zero owned states, capitulation, exile, annexation and actual extinction remain separate facts.
Human voluntary complete extinction stays blocked until the native player outcome is proved.

Task D requires full prevention rather than capital survival alone.
Task S requires its actual nonextinguishing result and full 45-day compliance period within the fixed deadline.
Outside defenders and mediators need their own postacceptance contribution, while claimant moderation uses the original demand baseline.
Same-date qualifying facts take precedence over timeout, while facts first arising after the deadline cannot be backfilled.
Actor loss, actual target destruction, unrelated-war escape, major promotion and technical identity invalidation have separate failure or neutral-cancellation dispositions.
