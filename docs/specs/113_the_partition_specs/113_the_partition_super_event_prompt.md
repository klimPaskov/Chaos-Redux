## Actual trigger

The shared outcome observer may present this super-event once per campaign only when every condition below is true.

1. Both qualifying target crises belong to the same wave that opened as Grand Partitions.
2. At least two distinct targets reach actual complete multi-party partition, with every owned state lost under the accepted outcome definition.
3. At least ten distinct target states are actually awarded across the qualifying complete partitions.
4. At least one major actually participates as claimant or protector in the qualifying crisis history.
5. At least two independent recorded claimant beneficiaries receive the divided territory.
6. The second qualifying complete partition occurs within 180 days of the first.

Crossing 600 Chaos, opening a large wave, signing a demand, issuing a war goal, capitulating a country or losing a single country to one annexer does not satisfy the outcome.
An unrelated third-party conquest cannot be inserted as a qualifying partition.
A formerly baseline wave that later evolves does not meet the Grand-opening condition.
Use actual immutable outcome records and one campaign display receipt.
The super-event itself adds zero Chaos and does not cause territorial loss or award the claimant achievement.

## Historical starting references

The package's source-evidence file documents three complete treaty reads and one archive metadata source.
Use them as starting context, not as an already selected quotation or automatic image license.

- [Munich Agreement,29 September 1938](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/imt/munich1.asp), for an exact timetable, mapped zones and the language of imposed arrangements.
- [Annex to that agreement](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/imt/munich2.asp), for conditional and distinct guarantee commitments.
- [German-Soviet Boundary and Friendship Treaty,28 September 1939](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/20th_century/gsbound.asp), for a map-defined division and the need to distinguish signatory rhetoric from neutral fact.
- [Library of Congress Maps of Poland plate metadata](https://www.loc.gov/resource/g6520m.gct00401/?sp=10), for the changing extent of partitions, with direct archive image inspection still outstanding.

Do further primary-source research when a candidate quote, image or recording needs it.
