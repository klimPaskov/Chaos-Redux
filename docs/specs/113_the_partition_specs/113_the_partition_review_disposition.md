The parent accepted that recommendation.
The remaining implementation gates are already carried in the coding, asset, super-event, decision, achievement and probability handoffs.
No accepted expansion addendum remains unmerged in this planning package.

Two final achievement clarifications were folded during prompt authoring.
A5 uses the immutable first valid public demand and also requires the mediator's own postparticipation material improvement to the then-current offer.
A1 and A3 explicitly fail their attempt on a specified continuous-hold break rather than restarting the timer.
These clarify existing conditions and do not add a new achievement or reward route.

The completion auditor identified one remaining fixture definition gap in AI05.
Its former wording said that a claimant dropped an additional strategic request without naming that state or establishing the preceding offer.
The final probability handoff now defines a separate Fever branch with a direct C2 border and real junction interest on T6, an opening P3 award vector, a narrower P4 award vector, exact dates, retained-country arithmetic and fresh version-bound signatures.
It also names the actor under observation and enumerates every claimant action's relevant availability.
