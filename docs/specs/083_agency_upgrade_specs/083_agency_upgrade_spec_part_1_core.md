## The intelligence arms race

Intelligence services around the world suddenly gain trained personnel, new capabilities, and the institutional backing to use them. Governments that previously relied on a few military attachés find themselves running an intelligence agency. Established services discover that departments they intended to develop over years are ready to operate. Every government initially sees a national advantage, then begins to realize that its opponents have received the same opportunity.

The event accelerates the campaign's existing espionage game. Its lasting consequence is a larger population of functioning agencies and operatives, including agencies in countries that would otherwise struggle to establish them. Differences between countries come from the upgrades they receive, the people they recruit, their existing intelligence infrastructure, and what they choose to do next.

The origin of the simultaneous expansion remains unexplained. This uncertainty belongs in the atmosphere. It does not create a hidden sponsor, secretly compromised recruits, or a second random punishment attached to accepting the reward.

## What every country receives

The highest eligible enabled evolution determines one replacement package for the whole firing.

| Package | Chaos requirement | Random upgrade grants | Permanent operative slots | Immediately recruitable operatives | Additional temporary reward |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Baseline | Below the first eligible evolution | 1 | +1 | 1 | None |
| Evolution I: Expanded Agencies | 200+ | 2 | +2 | 2 | None |
| Evolution II: Intelligence Boom | 400+ | 3 | +3 | 3 | One random intelligence bonus for 180 days |
| Evolution III: Intelligence States | 600+ | 5 | +5 | 5 | All five specified intelligence bonuses for 180 days |

The amounts replace the smaller packages. Intelligence States grants five upgrades, five slots, and five recruits, not the sum of all four rows.

Each existing country receives its own rolls from its own eligible upgrades. A common package does not mean that every country receives the same upgrades or recruits. Country size, player control, ideology, faction membership, and subject status do not reduce the reward. Existing countries with a functioning native agency interface remain eligible during war and capitulation. A dormant country tag that does not exist is not a recipient.

The award takes effect when the event fires. The report acknowledges what has happened. Leaving the report open cannot delay the award, obtain a later evolution, reroll the result, or stop the rest of the world receiving its share.

## Establishing a new agency

A country without an intelligence agency receives one before any upgrade is selected. Agency creation is additional to the package. It never consumes the first upgrade roll, operative slot, or recruit.

The agency uses the country's existing national identity and native agency cosmetics. A country with an established or renamed agency keeps its identity. The event does not overwrite an agency name, replace its emblem, disband existing departments, reset upgrades, or move existing operatives.

The newly created agency receives every part of the current package. At 600+ Chaos this means a new agency, five valid upgrade grants or their conversions, five permanent slots, five immediately recruitable operatives, and the full temporary bonus suite. Normal native rewards caused by agency creation or reaching an upgrade milestone remain additional to Event 83's explicit grants.

A construction or upgrade already underway must not leave the country paying for an item that the event has completed. Preserve unrelated work. When the awarded item is the item under construction, its completed state must be recognized and its remaining industrial commitment released according to the native system. Do not invent a political-power refund or a second free upgrade to approximate that reconciliation.

## Random agency development

An upgrade roll chooses uniformly among the country's currently valid, unowned purchase increments. The candidate pool represents the complete effective agency-upgrade tree supported by the running installation, including valid country-specific and compatible mod additions.

An increment means one native purchase step. Where an upgrade has several levels, a roll grants its next valid unowned level. It does not grant a whole multi-level branch for the price of one roll. Prerequisites, country restrictions, and enabled features determine validity. A paid construction timer is not a reason to discard an otherwise valid increment, because Event 83 completes its awards immediately.

Rebuild eligibility after every successful grant. A newly awarded prerequisite can open a successor for the next roll in the same firing. An increment granted earlier in that firing cannot be granted again. Separate random selections are therefore conditional draws without replacement among the candidates that exist at each step.

This is a broad development event. It must not quietly prefer cryptology, passive defense, intelligence departments, or the familiar handful of upgrades used by an older helper. The same inclusion rules apply to every eligible branch.

## Operative capacity and recruitment

The operative-slot reward is permanent, cumulative capacity. It adds to the country's existing capacity from all other sources. A repeat firing does not replace an earlier Event 83 contribution. Losing a temporary bonus does not remove these slots.

The personnel reward supplies the stated number of additional, distinct, ordinary operatives who can all be recruited immediately. At Evolution III, the player can recruit all five without waiting for successive recruitment cooldowns. More names in a recruitment menu, a single cooldown reset, and five additional empty slots are each insufficient on their own.

Recruitment remains the player's choice. The report does not forcibly hire and assign the operatives. Unclaimed Event 83 recruits remain available until claimed, and claiming them cannot consume a later firing's entitlement or an existing recruitment opportunity. Operatives supplied by different firings are distinct. Ordinary recruitment outside the event retains its normal rules.

Recruits use the native pool of appropriate names, portraits, nationalities, and normal starting characteristics. There are no guaranteed elite traits, automatic experience, legendary historical agents, or undisclosed penalties. New personnel become ordinary operatives after recruitment and can be assigned, captured, injured, rescued, or lost through the campaign's normal espionage systems.

An extra slot received as an exhausted-upgrade conversion is capacity only. It does not also create an extra immediate recruit. This preserves the distinction between a staffing allocation and an additional development benefit.

## Playing the advantage

The player decides how to exploit the expansion through the agency and operative interfaces. A small country can build its first foreign network, retain somebody at home, or save an expensive operation for a favorable moment. A major country can distribute personnel across more theaters rather than simply replacing an existing operative. A mature agency can turn surplus development into additional capacity or a temporary operational push.

Existing operation costs, target restrictions, equipment commitments, network requirements, and risks remain meaningful. Agency Upgrade does not pay for every operation that follows. A larger pool of operatives creates more possible commitments and more competition for the same resources.

Defensive use is a real choice. Foreign agencies also expand, so committing every new operative abroad leaves the player exposed to a stronger international intelligence environment. The event does not dictate a preferred assignment or force a country into offensive espionage.

## Repetition and lasting change

Every genuine later firing grants a complete new package using that firing's Chaos level. Completed upgrades remain completed, Event 83's capacity accumulates, and unclaimed recruits remain available alongside new recruits. A lower-Chaos repeat never revokes an earlier reward.

There is no new event-specific cap on the permanent operative expansion. A country that has completed its available upgrade tree remains a full recipient and receives one conversion for every otherwise unusable upgrade roll. These countries are not removed from the event to make the implementation easier.

Temporary benefits have their own expiration dates. Repetition can create overlapping benefits, but it cannot overwrite an older grant, silently extend every old benefit, or remove the permanent agency expansion. The exact temporary-reward rules are defined in Part 2.
