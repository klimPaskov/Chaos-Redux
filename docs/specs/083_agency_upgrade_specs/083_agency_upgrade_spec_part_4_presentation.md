# Agency Upgrade: presentation and writing direction

## The player-facing surfaces

The existing agency screen and operative roster carry the mechanic. A country-level event report explains the award, and one consolidated national modifier shows Event 83's continuing capacity contribution and active temporary benefits. Existing shared event history records the incident and chosen evolution.

The player does not manage a new custom meter. Permanent capacity is already visible through native operative slots. Temporary effects and their expiration dates belong in the consolidated tooltip. Internal firing identifiers, selection weights, eligibility checks, and expiry bookkeeping stay out of ordinary player-facing text.

Use at most one Event 83 national-spirit or dynamic-modifier tile at a time. Keep the full native agency-upgrade results visible in the agency itself. A single consolidated presentation must not flatten separate expiration dates into a misleading single timer.

## Award receipt

The recipient's receipt distinguishes an agency created during this firing, upgrade increments actually granted, permanent slots actually added, additional recruits immediately available, converted rolls, and temporary benefits with their durations.

Show completed upgrades by their real native names and levels. Group repeated conversion results by benefit, giving both the total added magnitude and the number of contributing grants where helpful. The receipt should remain readable when all five rolls convert. Longer details can use an expanded tooltip or the existing history detail surface rather than five separate popups.

The acknowledgment option has no reward choice, refusal route, hidden penalty, or AI preference. Its writing may react sharply to the situation, but its mechanical purpose is to close an already resolved report.

## Writing direction by package

| Surface | Viewpoint and information | Tone and uncertainty | Dynamic details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline report | The recipient's government recognizes a sudden practical expansion of its service | National self-satisfaction with the first signs that the advantage is shared internationally | Recipient, existing or newly created agency, actual result |
| Expanded Agencies report | A service head or cabinet sees enough new capacity to make broader commitments | Competitive confidence, with restrained irony about other governments reaching similar conclusions | Agency identity, the package's scale, current war context when relevant |
| Intelligence Boom report | The institution is developing a particular specialty rapidly | Excitement about the visible specialty, uncertainty about why foreign services appear equally active | Actual specialty and its duration, avoiding promises about successful operations |
| Intelligence States report | Governments depend on a much larger apparatus while foreign services do the same | Grand institutional confidence undercut by the universality of the expansion | Recipient's regime and agency, current temporary suite, no assertion that all secrets are known |
| Exhausted-tree detail | An experienced service has more resources than remaining departments to develop | Dry professional satisfaction or understated absurdity | Actual converted benefits, without presenting a completed tree as a failure |
| New-agency detail | A government gains a functioning service where none existed | Sudden institutional ambition, adapted to the country's real circumstances | Native agency name and the complete award |

The human work should remain visible through technical specialists, language skills, recruitment, and field commitments. Do not reduce the event to a staff desk, a stamped memorandum, a map changing color, or a generic message arriving by radio.

## Acknowledgment option direction

The speaker is the recipient government or its intelligence leadership. At low Chaos, use confident acceptance with sarcasm aimed at its own claims of superior intelligence. At high Chaos, emphasize the irony of every government believing it has gained a unique secret advantage.

A democracy may react through public accountability and private confidence. An authoritarian government may boast about institutional reach. A small country may express disproportionate ambition. These are writing directions rather than separate effects. Do not make every ideology use the same joke with a different country name.

Cultural allusions require specific period research before final wording. Avoid modern surveillance-company language, internet jokes, unsupported national stereotypes, and references to institutions that did not yet exist in the campaign period. Do not turn interrogation, political persecution, or operative deaths into cheap comedy.

## History and catalog direction

The shared event title retains Agency Upgrade. User-provided evolution titles retain Expanded Agencies, Intelligence Boom, and Intelligence States. New text must be written during implementation, with final wording derived from this direction rather than copied from a planning placeholder.

EventDetails and catalog Details describe the premise and international intelligence expansion. Numerical rewards, exact thresholds, and duration calculations belong in effect tooltips and detailed history rather than premise-only fields. The old political-power premise must not survive this rework.

History must identify the package actually selected for the firing. A first appearance at Evolution III cannot be recorded as the baseline simply because the logger ran before package selection. Repeated awards and first evolution entry are distinct history facts.

## Visual direction

Use one period-grounded report illustration centered on people and capability: a small group of field and technical intelligence personnel preparing to enter service, with period equipment and contrasting practical roles. Let the composition suggest several departments through a few legible objects rather than a dense collage of tiny symbols.

The image is fictional event art, not an alleged photograph of a named historical agency. Nationally neutral visual cues allow it to work for every recipient. Do not show modern computers, satellite imagery, contemporary tactical equipment, or a universal American or British agency emblem.

The consolidated modifier icon uses a clear institutional-intelligence motif that remains legible at 64 by 64 pixels. Keep one visual identity across repeats and tiers. The actual evolution and active magnitudes are communicated by text, not by requiring the player to memorize many similar icons.

Two achievement motifs are defined in Part 5. Their completed, grey, and not-eligible variants must follow the existing project achievement pipeline. Asset production, reference matching, dimensions, and final filenames are specified in the separate asset prompt.
