## The military lottery

Every government receives a military advantage so excessive that ordinary estimates of strength stop being dependable. An army can suddenly advance at an impossible speed, aircraft can become extraordinarily difficult to destroy, or a fleet can acquire overwhelming firepower. The effect belongs to the country for one year. Its usefulness depends on what that country does with it.

Every valid country draws independently. A great power can receive a narrow advantage while a small neighbor acquires a decisive one. A navy without ships can become theoretically excellent. A country already strong in one field can become even stronger there. The event does not correct these outcomes.

Each roll selects one military statistic and then a magnitude appropriate to that statistic. All outcomes are beneficial in direction. The absurdity comes from their strength, uneven distribution, and combinations. A reduction in consumption is a benefit. A positive increase in consumption is not an alternative bad roll.

Repeated firings add further packages. Every package retains its own results and its own expiry. A new firing never replaces an older package or extends its clock.

## The first year

The country receives its package immediately when the worldwide occurrence is committed. A report tells the player what happened and points to the exact results. Reading, postponing, dismissing, or reopening that report does not change the reward or its start date.

The first strategic task is to recognize what the result makes possible. Exceptional speed can support encirclement and exploitation. Exceptional defense can buy time for an army that was losing. Organization recovery can support sustained operations. Lower supply consumption can make a remote front practical. Naval or air results may instead justify a longer preparation period.

The country is free to ignore the advantage. There is no compulsory campaign, prescribed doctrine, purchase, or policy choice attached to receiving it.

The benefit remains available through changes of government, peace, war, ordinary diplomatic alignment, and cosmetic national identity. It also applies to relevant forces established later during the same package.

At expiry, the temporary statistical contribution ends. Equipment already produced, experience already earned, land already captured, and wars already won remain part of the campaign. Expiry does not rewind history.

## Progression

| Military Madness | Below 200, or no higher eligible enabled stage | 1 | 1 | Broad pool |
| Evolution I: Military Insanity | 200+ | 3 | 1 | Broad and expanded pools |
| Evolution II: Impossible Warfare | 400+ | 6 | 2 | Broad, expanded, and situational pools |
| Evolution III: Gods of War | 600+ | 10 | 3 | All compatible military pools |

Every row lasts 365 days. Higher stages replace the number of rolls for that firing. They do not add their listed counts together. Gods of War therefore grants ten rolls, not twenty.

The guaranteed broad rolls are a minimum. The remaining draws can also select broad modifiers, including the same statistic already drawn.

There is no Evolution IV, additional terminal stage, or automatic world-ending outcome.

## Evolution timing

Military Madness uses the project's pre-fire evolved-opening exception. The highest enabled stage whose Chaos requirement is met is selected when the occurrence begins. There is no additional waiting period or probability gate between satisfying the threshold and receiving that firing's package.

All recipients of one occurrence use the same frozen stage. The event's own Chaos change cannot promote countries processed later in the distribution.

A rise in Chaos between firings changes the stage available to the next firing. It does not add rolls to existing packages. A fall in Chaos can produce a lower-stage future package without reducing an older one. Each package is a record of the world when it arrived.

Evolution switches control future package stages. If Evolution III is disabled while Evolution II is enabled and Chaos is 650, the new package uses Evolution II. Disabling a lower stage does not secretly disable an enabled higher stage. Each stage is a complete package profile with its own broad-roll minimum.

## Who receives a package

The recipient set contains all currently existing countries that can receive temporary national effects through the supported game and mod configuration. It is frozen for the occurrence, with existence rechecked before delivery.

Human and AI countries follow the same rules. Independent countries, subjects, majors, minors, countries at peace, countries at war, and technically valid governments in exile are included. A country does not need deployed divisions, aircraft, a fleet, a coastline, or a useful current target.

Special Chaos Redux countries remain eligible when their country carrier supports the effect system. Civilian population eligibility rules from unrelated events do not apply.

An absent or destroyed country is not a recipient. A country created after distribution waits for a later firing. A new civil-war country does not copy a predecessor's package and does not receive a retroactive draw.

## What remains random

The event never assigns countries military archetypes. There are no infantry, armored, defensive, naval, or aviation package templates.

There are no rerolls, substitutions for inconvenient results, equal-value budgets, compensation grants, guaranteed domain coverage, or player-selected rewards. Two countries can receive identical results by coincidence. One country can receive the same statistic in every slot.

A result that is technically valid but currently unhelpful stays in the pool. A result that cannot function as a supported, reversible temporary national effect does not enter a draw.

## Military Preparation

Event 087 remains a Low member of Military Preparation. This is its cluster role, even when a high-Chaos package creates an extreme military imbalance.

The cluster controls its own selection and pacing. Military Madness supplies one member occurrence and distributes that occurrence worldwide once. Recipient count and modifier count do not multiply the cluster's timer, repeat count, or event weight changes.

Its repeatable status remains meaningful after Evolution III. Later firings continue producing independent ten-roll packages while that stage is eligible.
