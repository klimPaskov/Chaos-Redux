# 03. Hidden Influence and Peace Recovery

## Purpose

Lootboxes give the country authentic advantages while gradually making the country dependent on an influence it cannot inspect. The danger belongs to the recipient country. It does not spoil the contents, change the quality of delivered equipment, reclaim a reward, or secretly bill the purchase again.

This part defines the concealed national consequence of completed openings and the country’s route back to lasting health. It uses the same Event 107 introduced in Part 1, the rewards in Part 2, and the opening completion described in Part 4. Event 107 remains a Minor Fire-Once event with three evolutions. Hidden influence adds no cluster, fourth evolution, spendable resource, or separate global Chaos meter. Private exposure and its national symptoms add no global Chaos.

The event classification in this paragraph is an imported claim. The three evolution names and thresholds are accepted high-level design; detailed hidden consequence families and effects remain in the design parts. Repository links below are pinned to the earlier planning inspection and do not describe the current checkout.

The central distinction is between burden and symptoms. Burden records unresolved exposure and can fall only through the country’s own qualifying peaceful contributions. Symptoms are temporary national conditions with finite lifetimes. A country can regain ordinary performance while still carrying latent burden. That distinction makes the influence dangerous without making another purchase or an artificial war necessary for escape.

## 1. What the country carries

The country retains several separate kinds of hidden state. Each has a specific purpose, and none becomes a visible Lootboxes resource.

| State | Meaning | What changes it |
|---|---|---|
| Lifetime completed openings | Historical use of Lootboxes | Increases once when a completed opening commits. Recovery never rewrites history. |
| Recoverable burden | Unresolved exposure that determines present susceptibility | Increases with completed openings and decreases through verified peaceful contributions. |
| Recent opening cadence | Whether recent purchases formed a concentrated run | Updates from completed openings and their dates. It can become stale with time without lowering burden. |
| Current symptom episode | The finite period of disturbance associated with a recent opening | Begins or changes only through that opening’s accepted consequence or its single eligible delayed check. |
| Temporary relief | A short reduction of an existing dependence symptom | May follow another completed opening. It does not reduce burden or erase an episode’s identity. |
| Recovery contribution record | Evidence that an actual peaceful improvement was credited once | Created by the relevant action owner and consumed under the recovery rules below. |

Lifetime count must never impose a permanent minimum severity. A country that opened many boxes and later made enough genuine peaceful contributions can return to low burden or zero burden. Its past remains relevant to historical statistics and any separately specified achievements, but it does not condemn every future opening to a severe outcome.

Recoverable burden belongs to the country, not to the human currently controlling it. Tag switching, an AI taking over, closing the window, reloading a save, or changing the global coordinator must not clear or transfer it. Another country’s luck, purchases, symptoms and recovery stay separate.

The interface reveals no burden number, dependence tier, probability, recovery balance, omen collection or causal explanation. The player can see the normal consequences in national performance. The Lootboxes category, opening window and Event Details do not identify the source. The four omen names below are design names for different behaviors, not a required collection of four visible national spirits.

## 2. Accumulation and exposure

Every successfully completed opening adds burden exactly once. A planning baseline is **5 burden per opening**. This is an internal tuning unit. It cannot be bought, sold, transferred, paid as a cost or displayed as a progress bar.

Payment alone creates no exposure. A purchased box waiting at its first or fourth click has the same unresolved purchase, with no burden increase yet. The fifth accepted click completes the opening, grants the sealed real reward, and commits one exposure contribution. Reopening the result, duplicate input and repeated resolution callbacks cannot add another reward or another exposure contribution. AI completion follows the same rule.

Rarity does not determine whether the country accumulates burden. Common openings count fully. A long series containing only Common rewards can create deep susceptibility and severe national disturbances once an evolution is active. Premium status, multiple rewards from Bonus Drops, and unusual Chaos Loot do not multiply the exposure charge within a single opening. They remain one completed box.

For initial balance work, use the following burden bands. Their boundaries should move with campaign testing rather than become fixed player promises.

| Planning band | Illustrative burden | Intended behavior after evolution |
|---|---:|---|
| Light | Greater than 0 and below 25 | Usually quiet. A small but genuine chance of an appropriate symptom remains. |
| Established | At least 25 and below 60 | Disturbances become plausible and concentrated buying starts to matter. |
| Heavy | At least 60 and below 120 | Meaningful economic, political or readiness episodes become a regular risk of further openings. |
| Deep | 120 and above | Severe episodes are possible, including after an entirely Common history. Chance and penalties still have upper limits. |

Burden continues to increase beyond the deepest named band. The consequence system limits its outward intensity rather than discarding later exposure. This preserves the value of further peaceful recovery without allowing arbitrarily large penalties.

These intervals cover every positive fractional value, including values below 5 after shared contribution allocation. Zero is the cleared state. A later completed opening adds fresh burden before evaluating its new episode. Implementation storage and accumulation must use supported bounded numeric representation without silently wrapping or losing recovery precision.

Recent cadence is a secondary amplifier. Several openings within roughly 30 to 60 days should be riskier than the same number spread across a long campaign at equal burden and Chaos. Cadence must have a capped influence, so the first opening after a long pause cannot become automatically safe and a fast sequence cannot make harm certain. Reading elapsed time may quiet cadence. It never cleanses burden.

## 3. Evolution, Chaos and chance

Before any Lootboxes evolution has activated, the system can accumulate burden quietly. It must not advertise a future curse through the chest animation, tooltips, unusual rarity flashes, audio warnings or an empty spirit slot. Rarity-neutral light during the fourth click stays a sign of the opening stage alone.

Once an evolution is active and the country has exposure, every completed opening has a genuine nonzero chance of an appropriate hidden national consequence. The initial calibration can place light-burden opening risk in the low single digits, established burden around 5% to 15%, heavy burden around 15% to 30%, and deep burden around 30% to 45%. These are broad planning bands for the opening-level chance of a consequence, not four independent rolls that accidentally multiply the overall risk.

One opening-level episode gate owns that total probability. Its declared outcome pool contains quiet, eligible immediate, eligible delayed, and, where appropriate, bounded combined episodes. A quiet outcome does not receive another independent withdrawal roll later. Selecting a delayed episode arms one opportunity whose due-time checks can cancel or weaken it as the country recovers or conditions change. Those checks do not reroll the opening or add a second probability budget. A combined episode is explicitly included in the opening-level pool and respects the family and magnitude caps.

Evaluate the new episode from the actual completion-time evolution state, current global Chaos, post-opening burden, and current cadence. This consequence context is separate from the reward evolution snapshot sealed at purchase. A box waiting through an evolution unlock retains its original prize while its eventual completed opening uses the country's then-current susceptibility. Peaceful recovery before completion reduces the existing burden normally.

Current global Chaos changes the pressure within those bands. High Chaos raises the chance of manifestation and the chance of selecting a stronger eligible package. Lower Chaos favors quiet outcomes and milder packages. An already unlocked evolution does not erase itself when the world calms, and the country’s burden does not fall merely because global Chaos falls.

No rule guarantees punishment after a fixed count, a Common drought, an unlucky streak or a specific date. Long use should be dangerous in expectation while still allowing runs without an omen. The upper probability remains below certainty. Strong outcomes are selected through chance and eligibility, rather than through an inevitable collapse counter.

Each evolution adds a distinct direction of danger alongside its genuine benefit:

| Evolution | Hidden consequence direction |
|---|---|
| Premium Boxes, Chaos 200+ | Whispered Fortune can disturb political judgment and confidence. The better prize opportunity remains real. |
| Bonus Drops, Chaos 400+ | Need for Another can create a finite episode of dependence after repeated openings and a subsequent gap. Multiple delivered rewards remain real. |
| Chaos Loot, Chaos 600+ | Borrowed Prosperity can temporarily weaken ordinary production or military performance around otherwise useful unusual rewards. |

Ill Omen is the uncommon severe branch of accumulated influence. It can become eligible at substantial burden after at least one evolution is active. It is not a fourth evolution or a Legendary-only punishment. Its particular package must suit the country’s active evolution context and actual national capabilities.

## 4. Four distinct manifestation styles

The styles differ in what the country feels, when the effect appears, and how relief works. Numerical magnitudes and durations below are tuning bands. The implementation must use supported national modifier channels and preserve the concealment contract.

### Whispered Fortune

The government becomes less effective at ordinary political work while remaining able to act. Suitable packages reduce political power generation by approximately 10% to 25% and apply a temporary stability reduction of roughly 5 to 10 percentage points. A milder version can emphasize political power alone. A stronger version combines political drag with reduced confidence for 30 to 60 days.

This should be noticeable in planning, appointments and policy timing. It cannot consume the player’s stored political power, raise the fixed 50 PP box price, interfere with a prize already awarded, or force a political choice. The effect ends on its own deadline. Any stability component should be a reversible temporary condition rather than an unexplained permanent subtraction from the country’s base stability.

### Need for Another

This style concerns the gap after repeated use. Once Bonus Drops is active, an opening at sufficient burden may establish one delayed withdrawal opportunity roughly 20 to 40 days later. If the country continues without another completed opening, that opportunity can produce reduced productivity, confidence or readiness for approximately 20 to 45 days.

Suitable packages include a 10% to 15% reduction in ordinary factory output, a comparable reduction in organization recovery, or a temporary stability reduction around 5 percentage points. Select a coherent combination that affects the country’s real activity. Avoid spreading tiny penalties across many unrelated statistics.

A subsequent opening may briefly ease an existing dependence symptom for about 5 to 10 days. The relief is temporary, and its duration cannot stack into a stored reserve. The new opening adds its full burden and receives its own consequence opportunity. It can therefore provide immediate comfort while worsening later susceptibility.

Abstinence must also work as a practical response. The withdrawal episode has a finite end and does not schedule another episode when it expires. A player who stops opening boxes eventually regains ordinary performance even if lasting burden remains unresolved.

### Borrowed Prosperity

Ordinary national systems briefly perform below expectations. The player keeps every factory, building, equipment item, resource and other reward already granted. A country can enjoy its new equipment while its normal production or preparation becomes less efficient for a time.

Suitable packages reduce ordinary factory output or production-efficiency growth by approximately 10% to 20% for 30 to 60 days. A military variant can instead affect organization recovery or routine supply demand. Select the branch that produces a real but recoverable setback for that country.

This style must not delete factories, remove construction progress, destroy stockpiles, take back manpower, reduce the quality of a delivered design or make Chaos equipment defective. A timed production penalty is a national symptom. A supposedly valuable prize that secretly fails is outside the design.

### Ill Omen

Ill Omen is uncommon and forceful. It requires substantial burden and selects an appropriate national setback, such as a 15% to 25% organization-recovery penalty, a 15% to 20% rise in supply demand, a 15% to 25% production penalty, or a temporary stability condition around 10 to 15 percentage points. A severe package can combine two related channels for approximately 45 to 75 days, with a planning maximum of 90 days.

The consequence should change the country’s immediate priorities. It can make an offensive harder to sustain, delay industrial expansion or create a serious period of political weakness. It cannot impose a forced war, surrender, irreversible country transformation, permanent loss of control or unavoidable end state.

Ill Omen needs no rare reward to justify its severity. A deeply exposed country whose boxes were all Common remains eligible. Conversely, a first Legendary prize does not automatically inflict a severe omen.

### Combined pressure

Keep no more than two active manifestation families on a country. A new accepted outcome can replace, strengthen or refresh an eligible existing condition within the current limits. It must not add another copy of the same penalty on every opening.

Initial combined caps should keep total Lootboxes penalties near 30% for political power generation, 25% for ordinary output, 25% for organization recovery, 20% for additional supply demand, and 15 temporary stability points. These are campaign-tuning limits, not promises that all maxima occur together. The package selector should prefer a coherent primary setback and a supporting effect.

Countries with few factories, unusual military systems or unavailable modifier channels require a relevant eligible package. An unsupported or meaningless branch cannot stand in for a real consequence. The chosen solution must also preserve a quiet outcome and respect the combined caps.

## 5. Finite episodes and quiet intervals

Each completed opening owns one finite exposure episode. It may cause an immediate omen and, where Need for Another is eligible, at most one delayed withdrawal check. A later completed opening supersedes the older pending withdrawal check. Past openings cannot accumulate an invisible queue that punishes the country for years after it stops.

The delayed check has an original due date and a latest valid horizon. Reopening the UI, saving, reloading, changing control or retrying delivery cannot move that date forward. An expired or already evaluated check stays terminal. The end of a symptom removes that symptom and schedules nothing new.

A planning envelope should let all consequences of the last opening expire within approximately 90 to 120 days, including a delayed withdrawal episode. Further voluntary openings can create fresh exposure and extend trouble through new episodes. A quiet country receives no indefinite rescheduling merely because its burden is high.

Some symptoms can therefore resurface after an apparently calm interval within the finite episode created by a recent opening. That possibility gives dependence its delayed character. It does not create an endless recurring penalty for waiting.

Temporary relief leaves the original burden intact and does not count as a peaceful contribution. It can suppress or soften the eligible dependence condition while its short relief window is active. The symptom’s own expiry continues to approach during relief. A relief window cannot preserve an otherwise expired symptom for a later return.

At global Chaos zero, or while the Chaos meter is disabled, passive waiting must not generate another withdrawal episode. Pending delayed opportunities that reach their evaluation point under those conditions end without creating a new symptom. Existing effects keep their finite deadlines. Burden remains available to influence a later opening after evolution, but ordinary performance returns without a purchase or a manufactured conflict.

## 6. Lasting recovery through actual peaceful contribution

Lasting recovery follows a country’s meaningful action that actually lowers shared global Chaos through an existing owner system. It is not awarded for the world becoming calmer somewhere else, for displaying a negative number, or for intending to perform an action.

Monthly natural decay, unrelated foreign changes, manual settings changes, cosmetic choices, and failed or otherwise ineffective actions are ineligible. A negative total must also accompany a genuine successful peaceful action. Issuing a native command, advancing a success counter or creating a History row cannot substitute for the owner verifying that the conflict ended, freedom changed, political transition completed or restorative work actually succeeded.

The proposed integration is a **peaceful-contribution adapter** at the authoritative Chaos change and the responsible action owner. This is a required addition for Event 107 integration, not a claim that a suitable general receipt API already exists. Its role is to carry verified action evidence into the hidden burden system without adding a player-facing mechanic.

For one committed change, the available amount is the measured reduction between the shared Chaos value immediately before and immediately after that change. A requested reduction of 3 that moves Chaos from 1 to 0 supplies only 1. A negative request that moves nothing supplies zero. The measurement belongs directly to the committed mutation, before unrelated downstream callbacks can alter the total.

The adapter must carry these meanings:

| Evidence | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| Stable action identity | This exact successful action can be recognized again without producing more recovery. |
| Action reason and causal episode | The improvement can be related to its treaty, liberation, political transition, cleanup project or other genuine source. |
| Actual negative Chaos amount | The value that really left the shared meter after its rules and limits applied. |
| Responsible contributors | The country or countries whose own actions caused the qualifying improvement. |
| Allocation | Each contributor’s share of one finite available amount. |
| Provenance and durability | Enough evidence to reject a manufactured reversal and to validate any required lasting result. |
| Terminal consumption | The contribution cannot pay again after reload, duplicate callbacks, UI changes or later History rebuilding. |

[Chaos History](https://github.com/klimPaskov/Chaos-Redux/blob/a3ef485c5da7e2337122eca04ac65c175050f251/common/scripted_effects/chaos_meter_effects.txt#L4287-L4494) remains useful evidence about the world’s record. Its displayed actor and row position are not sufficient recovery authority. A beneficiary, selected reporting country, host or last processed participant cannot automatically receive the whole improvement. Contributor identity must come from the action that caused it. A diagnostic link to a History entry may accompany the contribution record, while credit remains independent of row merging, sorting, rebuilding or eventual pruning.

A planning conversion is **5 to 15 burden removed per actual attributable negative Chaos point**, with **10** as the first balance baseline. Together with 5 burden per opening, an actual own reduction of 1 would normally offset around two openings. A larger genuine peace or liberation can remove several openings’ burden, while a small action provides proportionately less.

Recovery cannot exceed current qualifying burden or become a reserve against future purchases. If a durability check delays final credit, its maximum relief is limited to unresolved burden present when the peaceful action committed. Outstanding contributions cannot promise to remove the same old burden twice. A country at zero burden gains no banked purification balance.

## 7. Fair attribution and ordinary recovery paths

One country acting alone can receive the eligible contribution. Several actual contributors divide it. The sum of allocated negative Chaos must never exceed the actual reduction, including any source cap and the shared meter’s zero floor.

Where contribution measures exist, use the owner’s recorded measures. Where two genuine participants have equal responsibility and no better supported distinction exists, an equal split is appropriate. Preserve fractional shares internally or distribute a fixed remainder deterministically. Rounding each country up would create recovery that the world never earned.

An ineligible participant’s allocation is not automatically donated to an eligible ally. Country size, player status, major status or being the host gives no extra share. A source’s larger actual Chaos reduction already expresses its larger effect. Event 107 must not multiply the same reduction again just because a country is a major.

The launch integration must support meaningful existing routes for the affected country classes, including countries that are already peaceful. The following are source families to integrate with their actual owners, subject to their genuine result and provenance.

| Existing action family | Qualification for recovery |
|---|---|
| Genuine peace or settlement | An actual conflict ends and the relevant country contributes to a durable peaceful result. Actual participants receive only their allocated share. |
| Liberation or release as free | The action creates real freedom. Credit follows the responsible liberator or releaser rather than automatically following the liberated beneficiary. |
| Subject freedom or restored government | Credit requires evidence of the country’s actual contribution. Merely being the country whose status changed is insufficient. |
| Meaningful faction departure or disbandment | The act creates a durable peaceful change and is not part of a join/leave cycle. |
| Peaceful political transition | A durable change with meaningful peaceful consequences and an actual attributable negative source can qualify. Deliberate party cycling cannot. |
| Existing environmental or restorative projects | A completed owner action must produce a verified actual negative Chaos contribution, with its sponsor or real contributors preserved through any conversion or shared cap. |

Documented owner actions such as [Global Cleaning Day](https://github.com/klimPaskov/Chaos-Redux/blob/a3ef485c5da7e2337122eca04ac65c175050f251/docs/systems/air_cleanliness/air_cleanliness_treaty.md#L71-L77) are candidates for the last family only when their implementation, real negative result and contributor bridge are verified. Membership, signing a declaration, spending resources on an unrelated project, moving aid, improving a local state or preventing a possible future increase does not by itself establish an actual negative global contribution.

For environmental systems that accumulate fractional changes before moving whole Chaos points, the owner must preserve the origin of the contributing reduction through that conversion. Passive recovery and another country’s work cannot become the sponsor’s credit simply because its project happens to cross the final rounding boundary.

Launch acceptance requires usable existing nonwar restorative paths with verified attribution for the relevant affected country classes. A player who is already independent, democratic, outside a faction and at peace must not be expected to create a war, acquire a subject or switch parties merely to obtain a recovery opportunity. Any missing owner integration remains a dependency to resolve within those existing systems. It does not authorize a Lootboxes cleansing decision, an invented aid credit or a new currency.

## 8. Preventing manufactured recovery

Qualification must distinguish a new peaceful improvement from undoing an arrangement the same actor created to collect credit. This is a causal question. A cooldown alone cannot answer it.

For faction and subject relationships, preserve the identity of the relationship and the transition already credited. Joining and leaving the same faction, creating and releasing the same puppet, or repeatedly restoring the same deliberately reversed government must not create a new entitlement each time. A fresh technical callback or changed row number does not make the action new.

A later independent episode can still qualify. A country attacked again after a genuine peace, a new externally imposed occupation, or a distinct liberation can create new circumstances. Anti-farm rules must not permanently ban recovery from the same neighbor or punish a defender for another country’s aggression.

For settlements, preserve the conflict’s causal origin and relevant participants before the ending destroys that context. A country cannot start an artificial war, return to the previous arrangement and claim a healing contribution for the reversal. This remains true if the original positive Chaos was capped, suppressed or absent from the current History display. A war’s duration alone cannot launder its origin.

The originator of a conflict is not permanently barred from every future peaceful act. A substantive new concession, real freedom beyond the previous arrangement or a separate independently arising improvement can qualify when its own owner supplies an actual negative contribution. The design rewards the new durable benefit rather than the mere restoration of a self-created disturbance.

Reversible actions can hold their recovery contribution pending for a planning interval of roughly 30 to 90 days while the owner validates the result. That interval verifies the original action. It creates no additional Chaos reduction and earns no repeated recovery for each day of peace. The due date is fixed, and one terminal outcome consumes or rejects the original contribution.

An actor’s own reversal can invalidate pending credit. An unrelated foreign declaration must not automatically invalidate an otherwise genuine defensive peace or liberation. After credit has been consumed, the same relationship cannot pay again through a simple reversal cycle. These rules avoid both easy farming and an indefinite hidden obligation to remain untouched by foreign events.

## 9. How recovery changes symptoms

A successful contribution lowers burden immediately when its qualification becomes final. Reevaluate current symptom strength against the new burden without rolling a new omen. A substantial reduction should weaken the active condition or shorten its remaining life. Reaching zero burden removes remaining influence-owned symptoms and cancels pending withdrawal opportunities.

Partial recovery must feel useful. Crossing from deep to heavy or from heavy to established burden should reduce the severity that can remain active. It need not wipe every ongoing symptom from a minor contribution. The remaining conditions retain finite expiry, and repeated genuine peaceful contributions can finish the recovery.

Recovery never writes another negative amount to global Chaos. The peaceful owner has already made that change. Removing local burden and symptoms is the Event 107 consequence of the same contribution, and must not recursively generate another recoverable world change.

At the zero floor, a disabled meter or another state where the authoritative writer makes no reduction, lasting recovery is zero. The country can still wait out its symptoms and regain ordinary performance. A later genuine action that actually lowers Chaos can resume lasting recovery. The player is never required to raise Chaos first to keep the country functional.

Further openings add fresh burden after recovery and can create another finite episode. The country retains its genuine prizes throughout this process. Recovery changes susceptibility and national symptoms, not the ownership or quality of anything won.

## 10. Representative outcomes

**A long Common history.** A country completes 25 Common openings. With the illustrative baseline it carries 125 burden. If an evolution is active, the next opening can cause a severe appropriate omen even though no exceptional reward ever appeared. It can also remain quiet. Nothing guarantees punishment on opening 26.

**A real peace near the floor.** A qualified action requests a reduction of 3 while Chaos is 1. The world falls to zero. Only 1 is available to allocate. At the initial conversion baseline, a sole contributor can remove at most 10 eligible burden. The nominal request does not justify removing 30.

**Two actual contributors.** A jointly caused improvement lowers Chaos by 3, with equal supported responsibility. Each receives 1.5 of the negative contribution and can remove up to 15 eligible burden at the baseline. Neither receives the full 3. Uninvolved allies receive nothing.

**A country stops buying.** A heavily exposed country has an active production symptom and one delayed withdrawal opportunity. It makes no further purchase. Each opportunity reaches its original terminal outcome, all effects expire within the bounded episode, and no replacement check is scheduled. Burden stays high, but the country returns to ordinary performance. A later purchase is still risky.

**A peaceful country helps the world.** A verified existing restorative owner project completes and causes a real attributable negative Chaos change. Its sponsor can recover without a war or subject release. If that project improves only local conditions or produces no actual meter reduction, it supplies no lasting credit. Its ordinary benefit remains intact.

## 11. AI, persistence and delivery standard

AI countries follow the same exposure, probability, symptom and recovery rules. They pay the same 50 PP for a box and do not receive automatic cleansing. Their buying policy can respond to political reserves, normal priorities, recent purchase cadence and experienced national disruption. It must not read a hidden exact burden threshold as if the country understood the curse perfectly.

Ordinary AI peace, liberation, diplomacy and verified restorative work should reach the same contribution adapters used for players. There is no special AI purification action. Resource priorities should leave room for routine statecraft, especially when temporary political or production symptoms are active.

Save persistence must preserve burden, completed-opening identity, active conditions, original expiry dates, pending withdrawal identity, temporary relief and consumed or pending recovery contributions. Duplicate callbacks can confirm an already completed outcome but cannot create another effect. A change of controller or coordinator does not reset those records.

Performance follows the existing event and owner lifecycles. Completed openings, actual qualifying action commits, bounded delayed checks and finite modifier expiries provide the work opportunities. Event 107 adds no country-wide daily or monthly search for exposed countries and no repeated scan of Chaos History. Callback cleanup must terminate stale work without discarding another country’s active episode.

Delivery is acceptable when native behavior demonstrates meaningful concealed modifiers, complete removal at expiry, correct temporary relief, faithful save restoration, and equal human/AI and multiplayer attribution. The acceptance cases must include all-Common deep exposure, a quiet high-burden run, the zero floor, disabled meter, capped multi-country settlement, duplicate contribution delivery, reversible arrangements, a manufactured conflict, and a real existing nonwar recovery path. The result must preserve both sides of the event: useful genuine rewards and a hidden influence that can become severe while remaining recoverable.
