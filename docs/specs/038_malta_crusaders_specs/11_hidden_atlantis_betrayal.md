# Hidden route: Atlantis betrayal

## Route status

Atlantis is a hidden German betrayal that can follow the Teutonic Order. It is not an Event 38 evolution and does not appear in public Event Details, public world-end toggles, the public workbook, or the manual Holy World scenario setup.

The route is intentionally extreme alternate-history propaganda. German claims of Atlantean ancestry are treated as regime mythology and ideological fabrication.

## Eligibility

All required conditions must pass:

- the Teutonic Order faction has existed continuously for at least two years
- Malta or the Holy See exists and remains a faction member
- the Holy Realm exists and remains a faction member
- Germany exists and remains a faction member
- Germany is still on the required Nazi route
- Germany is the strongest founding member by the accepted industrial comparison
- no terminal world-end state is active
- Atlantis has not already occurred
- the betrayal is not permanently blocked by a prior human rejection

Industrial comparison should use a stable combined measure of civilian factories, military factories, dockyards, and active usable industry. The exact weights require tuning and probability evidence. Temporary occupation of a few factories should not create a one-day eligibility flip.

## Choice behavior

- AI Germany chooses Atlantis 90 percent of the time when every eligibility condition remains valid.
- Human Germany receives a normal choice with clear public consequences and no hidden automatic override.
- AI probability is evaluated only across the complete eligible option pool.
- A human rejection can permanently block or delay the route according to the option text. It must not be silently ignored later.

The 90 percent figure is a design target and requires `hoi4.probability_inspect`, evaluation, and comparison under named scenarios.

## Betrayal sequence

### Ideological proclamation

German leadership declares that Germans are Atlantean and therefore superior to former allies. The proclamation references Atlas, Poseidon, and a fabricated ancestral program as regime myth.

### Leadership return

Hitler returns to German leadership if he had been transferred to the Holy Realm route. The transition must remove any active duplicate leadership and install a valid Holy Realm successor or regency.

### Country transformation

Germany receives an Atlantis cosmetic identity. Berlin is renamed **Atlantus** through the accepted city-renaming system.

The transformation updates:

- country name and adjective
- flag and emblem
- ruling party and propaganda
- leader and portrait presentation
- national spirits
- focus or decision overlay
- AI strategy
- diplomatic stance
- special unit access

### Alliance rupture

Germany leaves the Teutonic Order and immediately becomes hostile to Malta or the Holy See and the Holy Realm. A dedicated Atlantis super-event fires.

War creation uses a bounded sequence. It must not duplicate existing wars or declare against invalid actors.

## Atlantean Core Program

### Engine conflict

The rough idea requests immediate cores on the former Roman Empire, selected American and Canadian states, relevant Atlantic and Caribbean islands, the Holy Realm, and all Malta or Holy See territory.

Immediate engine cores across those regions would remove much occupation resistance and undercut the route's own genocide, evidence, migration, and resistance gameplay. It would also grant full manpower and industry from territories the regime has only declared as mythic inheritance.

### Accepted design correction

Atlantis immediately receives an **Atlantean Core Program** over the full requested registry. This program provides:

- permanent ideological claims
- map highlighting
- war and decision eligibility
- propaganda and recruitment effects
- target prioritization
- settlement policy access
- route-specific tooltips

Actual engine cores are granted only through one of these paths:

1. a controlled post-conquest integration mission
2. an approved terminal escalation exception
3. a state-specific existing German core or historical integration rule

This preserves the requested universal claim fantasy while keeping resistance, occupation, atrocities, and migration meaningful.

No implementation should replace this with instant universal `add_core_of` calls without a new accepted design decision.

## Territorial registries

### Roman program

The Roman registry should be built from one exact local state collection covering the accepted maximum former Roman Empire extent. It must use current state files, not a copied outdated online list.

The registry should be divided into regions for staged war and performance:

- Italy and western Mediterranean
- Iberia and Gaul
- Britain and the Rhine frontier
- Balkans and Greece
- Anatolia and Levant
- Egypt and North Africa

### Atlantic program

A separate curated collection covers:

- selected American and Canadian Atlantic states
- relevant Atlantic islands
- selected Caribbean islands
- any accepted route-specific coastal claims

The selection must be explicit and documented. “Selected” cannot be implemented as random states.

### Former allies

The program always includes:

- Holy Realm territory
- Malta Crusader, Holy See, or Kingdom of God territory
- active crusader principalities according to route logic

## War program

### Immediate wars

Atlantis immediately declares war on:

- Malta or the Holy See
- the Holy Realm

The betrayal war is the central opening.

### Later wars

Other holders of Atlantean program territory become targets through regional decisions and rapid campaign pulses. Germany should not receive one fragile one-tick declaration against every country on Earth.

### Settlement

Atlantis normally prefers direct occupation, extermination policy, client administrations, and later integration. It does not create ordinary Malta-style principalities.

## Atlantean Supreme formations

### Initial package

Atlantis receives exactly 20 fully equipped Atlantean Supreme tank formations through a one-time transaction.

The setup freezes:

- template
- equipment stock
- manpower
- deployment states
- commander assignments
- supply capacity

It commits only when all 20 formations can be created safely. If the full transaction cannot complete, it uses a documented reduced package based on valid deployment space and records the exact reason. It must never duplicate equipment or create partial hidden formations.

### Combat role

The formations have exceptional:

- armour
- breakthrough
- speed
- organization
- reliability
- planning

They should remain vulnerable to concentrated late-game anti-tank, air superiority, logistics collapse, fuel shortages, and encirclement. Exceptional does not mean mechanically immortal.

### Replacement

The initial tanks are hard to replace. Later production requires:

- Atlantean Supreme Armour technology
- dedicated factories
- rare resources or high resource burden
- fuel
- long production time
- route-specific workshops

The player cannot repeatedly press a cheap decision to regenerate the first 20 formations.

## Extermination policy

### Scope

The route activates an extreme Nazi racial-extermination policy against populations outside its defined German racial category. The system uses existing camp, genocide, Deaths, evidence, resistance, migration, famine, and Condemnation mechanics.

The implementation must avoid bespoke population deletion when the shared atrocity system can represent the policy.

### Policy layers

- persecution and legal exclusion
- forced removal
- camps and detention
- labour extraction
- systematic killing at escalated sites
- record destruction and concealment
- occupation resistance
- refugee flight and trapped populations
- discovery and international reaction

### Population transactions

Every death uses the exact state civilian population loss contract with protected floors, recruitable-manpower reconciliation, reason code, optional target country, and one Deaths registration.

Migration transfers survivors and route deaths through the shared migration transaction. Famine deaths remain famine-owned. The systems must not debit the same people twice.

### Evidence and discovery

Atrocity evidence can remain hidden until:

- inspection
- occupation
- resistance intelligence
- witness escape
- record capture
- foreign observers
- internal disclosure

Public evidence increases Condemnation and diplomatic consequences. Hidden evidence must never appear in public country rows before disclosure.

### Resistance and instability

The policy provides short-term extraction, terror, and military control. It creates severe:

- resistance
- garrison cost
- migration
- sabotage
- international isolation
- partisan support
- internal administrative burden
- long-term population and production loss

Actual engine cores from the Atlantean program cannot erase these effects before controlled integration.

## Relation to chemical and biological systems

If Atlantis uses chemical, biological, nuclear, or other unconventional weapons, those actions use their owner systems and shared Condemnation. Event 38 does not duplicate their Chaos or casualty registration.

Restricted sites can integrate with the camp and repression network. Technology and stockpile gates remain required.

## Atlantis government and economy

### National spirits

Suggested staged ideas:

- **Atlantean Delusion** for ideological mobilization, aggression, and diplomatic isolation
- **Supreme Armoured Program** for initial elite tanks and severe production burden
- **War Against the Human Map** for the territorial program and escalating resistance

These ideas need lifecycles. Defeat, internal collapse, reform, or terminal escalation must transform or remove them.

### Economy

Atlantis gains major military production capacity through Germany's existing industry and route bonuses. It also takes on:

- extreme fuel demand
- rare resource pressure
- occupation burden
- reduced civilian capacity
- sanctions and embargo pressure
- resistance damage

The route should feel powerful and difficult to sustain.

## AI strategy

Atlantis AI should:

- destroy or isolate Malta and Holy Realm first
- preserve the 20 elite formations
- secure fuel and supply
- prioritize contiguous regional campaigns
- avoid scattering tanks into low-supply islands
- use air and conventional forces to support spearheads
- escalate occupation and extermination policy according to route settings
- counter resistance and evidence discovery
- pursue the Roman program before distant Atlantic claims unless strategic conditions favour the Atlantic

Target weights require probability inspection and named scenarios.

## Super-event

The Atlantis betrayal receives a unique hidden-route super-event.

It needs:

- a short specific title direction centered on the betrayal and mythic German transformation
- in-world description of the proclamation, rupture, and first attacks
- a sourced quote or short primary-source-compatible line about racial myth, empire, hubris, or betrayal
- a short researched button remark
- unique licensed musical cue
- generated fictional image
- unique slot, sprite, audio ID, and settings-aware sound wiring

The visual must not present Nazi imagery as aspirational. It should communicate violent regime transformation and rupture.

## Defeat and aftermath

Atlantis can be defeated through:

- German capitulation
- loss of Atlantus and the core military government
- destruction or surrender of the Supreme armoured program
- internal regime collapse
- successful anti-Atlantis settlement

After defeat:

- the Atlantean Core Program is removed
- actual route-granted cores are reviewed and removed where appropriate
- extermination policies and active sites are dismantled or transferred to the postwar accountability system
- evidence is disclosed or preserved
- survivors and displaced populations enter migration and return systems
- former allies receive settlement events
- the Teutonic Order cannot silently reform with unchanged leadership

A major global Atlantis war can justify a defeat aftermath super-event and reconstruction compact. A short failed betrayal does not automatically receive one.

## Public secrecy contract

Before the betrayal, no public Event 38 surface may reveal:

- the 90 percent AI choice
- the two-year timer
- the Atlantis cosmetic identity
- Atlantus
- the territorial program
- the 20 tank formations
- the extermination policy

After the super-event, these become visible through normal gameplay text and tooltips.
