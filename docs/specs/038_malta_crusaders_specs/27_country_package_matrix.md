# Country package matrix

## Purpose

This matrix defines every persistent national identity that Event 38 can create or transform. A package must be implemented as a real country surface when it can survive independently, fight, select focuses, use decisions, or appear as a meaningful subject. A cosmetic identity may reuse its legal country carrier only when the route preserves ownership, history, and gameplay state cleanly.

All candidate tags require a fresh collision audit against vanilla, Chaos Redux, installed Workshop mods, and sibling local mods. No tag below is locked by this planning pack.

## Principality shared-tree contract

Persistent principalities can share a base focus tree only when the shared tree remains regionally specific through scripted localisation, route modules, AI weights, leaders, starting problems, decisions, state targets, resources, and postwar handling. The shared base should contain:

- survival and administration
- charter politics
- local military development
- infrastructure and supply
- relation with Malta or the Pope
- autonomy, federation, integration, or independence route
- regional ambition appropriate to the package
- crisis and succession content

No principality may receive a copied Malta tree or a five-focus placeholder branch.

## Leader and portrait ownership

Every leader candidate must pass the portrait source-mode gate and ownership search. Real or grounded political identities require attributed real-person or authentic institutional sources. A fictional one-person portrait is allowed only for a truly fictional high-Chaos identity. The same Pope, Hitler, Grand Master, or other named person cannot be active as the ruler of two countries at once.

The Blessed Hitler concept in the hidden Teutonic route must use a guarded leadership arrangement. Germany may retain Hitler and the Holy Realm may use a symbolic institutional figure or transformed office, or Germany may enter a temporary regency while the transferred character is active elsewhere. The implementation must choose one model and prove exclusive ownership.

## Country setup acceptance

Every fighting package must include:

- legal tag or cosmetic identity strategy
- capital and fallback capital logic
- owner and controller transaction
- cores, claims, compliance, and resistance rules
- ruling party and party names
- leader, portrait, gender, and name-pool consistency
- starting laws, technology, research, production, manpower, fuel, convoys, trains, and supply
- army, navy, air, templates, equipment, commanders, and reinforcement path
- focus tree or overlay route
- decisions and AI
- normal, medium, and small flags with required variants
- defeat, annexation, release, integration, independence, and cleanup behavior
- multiplayer switching and player-country safeguards

A package cannot be marked complete because its tag and flag exist.
