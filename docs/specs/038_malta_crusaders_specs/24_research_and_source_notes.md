## Research method

Historical research is used for institutions, geography, names, political tensions, and asset direction. It does not turn the event into a history simulation and does not validate fictional route claims.

Source priority:

- official institutional sites
- museums and archives
- academic presses and university repositories
- Holocaust documentation for Nazi pseudo-science
- current HOI4 public wiki only as a provisional map aid

Exact implementation still requires local vanilla, offline wiki, and MCP evidence.

## Historical anchors

### The Order of St John and Malta

The official Order of Malta history traces the order to a hospital community in Jerusalem and its later development as a religious order. It records the move from Rhodes to Malta and the later return of the Order's central administration to Rome.

Design use:

- Hospitaller care and hospital systems
- Jerusalem as sacred origin
- Rhodes and Dodecanese as a maritime bridge
- Malta as fortress and later symbolic center
- Rome as a plausible institutional connection

The event does not claim that the modern Order historically planned a 1930s crusade.

### Fra' Ludovico Chigi Albani della Rovere

The official Grand Masters list records Fra' Ludovico Chigi Albani della Rovere as Grand Master from 1931 to 1951.

Design use:

- grounded candidate for the opening Grand Master
- source-based portrait research
- World War II humanitarian and institutional context

The character remains subject to roster ownership, source, and portrait gates.

### Ancient Langues

The Order of Malta records seven original Langues, with Castile and Portugal becoming an eighth in 1462. Each had its own head and associated high office.

Design use:

- Assembly of the Langues political route
- multinational recruitment
- council seats and office allocation

The design adapts the historical institution. It does not copy medieval legal structure as a complete twentieth-century constitution.

### Fort St Angelo

Heritage Malta records that the Order turned Fort St Angelo into its headquarters after 1530 and expanded its defences. It later served the Royal Navy and suffered heavy bombing in World War II.

Design use:

- Malta opening capital symbolism
- fortress, harbour, convoy, and coastal defence mechanics
- Royal Navy and order history overlap

### Vatican City and the Holy See

The Vatican City State official history records its creation through the Lateran Treaty signed on 11 February 1929 and ratified on 7 June 1929. The treaty provided a territorial basis for the Holy See's independence.

Design use:

- distinction between the Holy See and a territorial Papal state
- Rome and Vatican governance route
- need to handle an existing Papal institutional actor rather than invent one

The Event 38 Holy See is an alternate-history state expansion and should not be written as a historical continuation that automatically follows from the treaty.

### Crusader states

Cambridge and Smarthistory sources identify the Kingdom of Jerusalem, Principality of Antioch, County of Tripoli, and County of Edessa as the major Latin crusader states.

Design use:

- Jerusalem, Antioch, and Tripoli principality identities
- proof that the principalities were separate political structures
- need for local, Armenian, Greek, Muslim, and other community politics rather than a uniform map colour

Edessa is not automatically included in the Event 38 principality list because its inland geography and current game state may not fit the Mediterranean campaign. It can be reconsidered only through a later accepted addendum.

### Cyprus

Academic work on the Latin East treats Cyprus as a durable Latin regime and major eastern Mediterranean base after the late twelfth century.

Design use:

- Crusader Cyprus
- maritime bridge
- island principality and naval-order route

### Military orders

Academic and institutional sources support distinct roles for Hospitallers, Templars, and Teutonic Knights. Hospitallers combined religious, hospital, and military functions. Templars developed military and financial roles. The Teutonic Order developed a territorial state in the Baltic after hospital origins.

Design use:

- different order mechanics
- no generic “knight order” copies
- Hospitaller care, Templar finance, Teutonic discipline and territory

### Order of Saint Lazarus

Academic discussion connects the medieval Order of Saint Lazarus with care for lepers and the image of “leper knights.”

Design use:

- disease and contaminated-region specialist order
- field hospitals and relief
- public stigma and ethical conflict

The final implementation should verify exact claims before using specific historical titles or emblems.

### Malta in the 1930s

The University of Malta repository thesis on imperial policy from 1934 to 1939 describes Malta's return to Crown Colony government and the political consequences of British rule. Other scholarship discusses the language question and divisions over British, Italian, and Maltese identity.

Design use:

- civilian Maltese institutions
- British and Italian political pressure
- language and identity conflict
- reason to avoid portraying Malta as an empty island waiting for foreign knights

### Nazi Tibet expedition and pseudo-science

The United States Holocaust Memorial Museum documents the German anthropological expedition to Tibet and Bruno Beger's connection to the Ahnenerbe. USHMM material on racial hygiene documents Nazi pseudo-science and its role in persecution and murder.

Design use:

- factual grounding for the existing German expedition flags
- explicit treatment of the hidden origin claim as Nazi ideology and fabrication
- refusal to present racial claims as scientific truth

The Atlantis route is fictional and receives no historical validation from these sources.

## Critical historical cautions

### No uniform religious population

The Levant, Anatolia, Greece, Cyprus, and North Africa contain many communities and institutions. The event should not describe Muslims, Orthodox Christians, Catholics, Armenians, Jews, or any other population as one political actor.

### No automatic continuity

A medieval title does not create a legitimate twentieth-century successor by itself. Event 38's states are alternate-history creations whose legitimacy is contested.

### No proof of relics or miracles

Relic authenticity and miracle claims remain uncertain.

### No validation of Nazi mythology

German racial-origin claims and Atlantean ancestry are propaganda or delusion.

### Atrocity systems need serious treatment

The Atlantis route uses shared population, camp, evidence, migration, Deaths, and Condemnation mechanics. It should not use mass killing as decorative flavour or a simple production bonus.
