# Crusader principalities and territorial governance

## Purpose

Evolution II allows Malta to turn conquered territory into a network of Crusader Principalities and order states. These countries reduce direct occupation burden, raise local armies, create political variety, and give the campaign a regional structure beyond annexation.

A principality is never created because a focus name exists. It appears only after Malta controls the required region and the release transaction proves that the new country can function.

## Governance choices

Every eligible region supports one or more of these settlements:

1. direct commandery
2. order grant
3. Crusader Principality
4. local Christian restoration
5. Papal administration
6. negotiated return under treaty

The available set depends on local population, existing country identities, churches, order influence, route, war outcome, and current control.

## Common principality contract

Every persistent principality needs:

- collision-safe tag or transformation carrier
- exact origin and generation proof
- valid capital
- coherent owned and controlled territory
- public name, adjective, colour, and flags
- leader or institutional government with portrait provenance
- party and ideology setup
- starting ideas and lifecycle
- usable forces, templates, manpower, equipment, fuel, convoys, trains, and supply
- technology and production
- shared or bespoke focus content
- decisions and Malta obligations
- AI behavior
- defeat, annexation, succession, transfer, and cleanup

A principality cannot be a flag, name, and one militia division.

## Shared tree strategy

The principalities can use one deep shared **Crusader Principality** tree with country-specific overlays. The shared tree must still feel different by using:

- principality-specific opening problems
- local route names and descriptions
- different order patrons
- distinct capital and regional objectives
- different military templates
- different church and community politics
- country-specific decisions
- country-specific AI weights
- distinct flags and leader packages
- principality-specific capstones

Long-lived major principalities can receive larger overlays. Minor marches can use a smaller country package when their role and lifetime are genuinely limited.

## Kingdom of Jerusalem

### Formation area

The principality requires stable control of the Event 38 Jerusalem region and a valid capital in Jerusalem. The exact state group is resolved from the current local map.

### Identity

The Kingdom of Jerusalem is the most prestigious principality and the main rival to direct Malta rule of the Holy Land.

### Starting problem

- sacred prestige exceeds administrative capacity
- local communities reject imported rule
- several orders claim military authority
- Malta expects military and financial obligations

### Routes

- loyal crown under Malta
- order-dominated kingdom
- local Christian constitutional kingdom
- Papal kingdom
- independent claimant route after severe Malta weakness

### Military role

- Holy Land guards
- knight and crossbow recruitment
- local infantry
- fortified sacred centers

### Obligations

- defend Jerusalem
- contribute units or equipment
- maintain pilgrimage access
- accept Malta or Papal war leadership according to charter

### Failure

If Jerusalem is lost, the country can relocate temporarily, become a government in exile, merge into Malta's Eleventh Crusade, or collapse. The route must not keep a landless ordinary AI state indefinitely without purpose.

## Principality of Antioch

### Formation area

Requires coherent control of the Antioch and northern Levant or Cilician region. Exact state groups depend on current borders and local states.

### Identity

Antioch is a frontier principality with strong fortress, Armenian, Greek, Syriac, Arab, and order politics. Historical and cultural detail requires careful research and final localisation review.

### Starting problem

- exposed northern frontier
- competing church jurisdictions
- dependence on Cilician and coastal supply
- strong order garrisons

### Routes

- fortress principality
- local Christian coalition
- Teutonic or Siege Brotherhood patronage
- Papal frontier
- negotiated autonomy under a regional Christian state

### Military role

- fortress infantry
- siege engineers
- mountain and coastal defence
- armored knight cadres

## County of Tripoli

### Formation area

Requires control of the Event 38 Tripoli and central Levantine coast group. This is the Levantine County of Tripoli, not the Libyan city. Final localisation must avoid ambiguity.

### Identity

A maritime county that connects Jerusalem, Antioch, Cyprus, and Mediterranean supply.

### Starting problem

- narrow coastline
- vulnerable ports
- competing local authorities
- pressure from stronger neighbours

### Routes

- maritime county
- Hospitaller protectorate
- merchant and local elite compact
- Papal coastal administration

### Military role

- port defence
- light infantry
- naval order units
- convoy support

## Crusader Cyprus

### Formation area

Requires stable control of Cyprus.

### Identity

Cyprus is the main island principality and strategic bridge between Malta and the Holy Land.

### Starting problem

- overburdened ports
- tension between island government and order garrisons
- foreign naval threat
- dependence on convoy protection

### Routes

- autonomous island kingdom
- Naval Order state
- direct Malta command
- Papal island administration

### Military role

- marines
- naval escorts
- island fortresses

## Aegean Crusader State

### Formation area

Uses one coherent Event 38 Aegean island group. It must not combine isolated islands that cannot support one country.

### Identity

A network of fortified islands and naval chapters.

### Starting problem

- dispersed population
- weak industry
- naval dependence
- competing Greek, Italian, Turkish, and order claims

### Routes

- Naval Order confederation
- Greek Christian restoration under Malta guarantee
- direct crusader command
- Papal Aegean league

### Military role

- marines
- coastal artillery
- convoy escorts
- island militias

## Anatolian order state

### Formation area

Requires a coherent southern Anatolian or Cilician group with a valid port and land connection.

### Identity

A heavily militarized frontier state with Teutonic, Templar, or local Christian sponsorship.

### Starting problem

- exposed land border
- severe resistance
- difficult supply
- conflict over land grants

### Routes

- Teutonic march
- local Armenian or other historically grounded Christian restoration where the campaign and research support it
- Templar military colony
- Papal protectorate

### Military role

- heavy knights
- frontier infantry
- siege engineers
- anti-tank lancers later

The spec does not authorize invented ethnic or historical claims. Final identities require local historical research and a tag audit.

## North African march

### Formation area

Requires one coherent selected North African coastal group. It cannot merge distant coastal states into a single disconnected country.

### Identity

A defensive port march that protects the western sea road.

### Starting problem

- limited local legitimacy
- long supply route
- desert and coastal warfare
- conflict with existing colonial and local governments

### Routes

- fortified march
- Hospitaller relief protectorate
- local Christian or mixed administration where supported
- direct Malta naval command

### Military role

- port defence
- mounted columns
- desert logistics
- naval support

## Order grants

An order grant is not always a separate country. It can be:

- a state modifier and headquarters under direct Malta control
- an autonomous territory
- a principality with the order as patron
- an order-state country when the region and route justify persistence

The implementation should choose the least complex country surface that changes gameplay. Do not create a tag for every headquarters.

## Local Christian restorations

A local restoration returns authority to a campaign-valid local Christian government or creates a defensible historically grounded identity. It requires:

- a valid existing or researched identity
- coherent territory
- a leader or institution
- local support proof
- a complete country package
- a treaty with Malta or the Holy See

The event must not assume that all Christians in one region share one political goal or church allegiance.

## Papal administrations

Papal administrations are subject governments under the Holy See. They provide Sacred Legitimacy and terminal preparation but create church disputes and reduced local political freedom.

A Papal administration needs:

- valid Papal supreme ruler
- stable control
- local clergy or administration capacity
- no duplicate local government
- subject obligations
- route-specific law and leader setup

## Obligations system

Each principality charter defines a small set of obligations:

- troop contribution
- equipment contribution
- convoy or port support
- pilgrimage access
- sacred-site defence
- tax or industry contribution
- obedience to Malta or Papal war leadership
- local autonomy and church guarantees

The player sees a qualitative charter summary and active obligation mission. Hidden values track fulfillment and loyalty.

## Loyalty and succession

Principality loyalty is hidden and feeds public events, cohesion, and authority. It changes through:

- charter fulfillment
- military protection
- taxation
- local autonomy
- order patronage
- Papal policy
- successful wars
- abandonment during siege
- leadership succession
- forced religious policy
- atrocities and condemnation

Succession events can produce:

- loyal heir or council
- order candidate
- local elite candidate
- Papal appointee
- independence demand
- Malta intervention

A succession dispute should not automatically become civil war. It can be resolved through recognition, concessions, council arbitration, or a limited leadership crisis.

## Integration

Direct integration requires time and proof. Suggested requirements include:

- stable ownership or subject control
- minimum compliance or local cooperation
- no active famine or migration catastrophe
- resolved charter and succession
- no severe public atrocity evidence tied to the region
- sufficient Authority
- a route focus or decision
- a named integration mission

Large or culturally distinct regions should normally become cores only after staged integration. The player can choose permanent subject rule instead.

## Principality federation

The Assembly of the Langues or Papal routes can create a federation of principalities.

Requirements:

- minimum member count
- fulfilled obligations
- sufficient Authority or Sacred Legitimacy
- acceptable Order Cohesion
- no active member rebellion
- shared military or legal framework

The federation can provide:

- joint command
- shared reserves
- coordinated defence
- common pilgrimage routes
- dispute arbitration
- member confidence

It is not a free annexation mechanic.

## AI behavior

Principality AI should:

- defend its charter territory
- prioritize its distinct military role
- fulfill obligations when viable
- request relief when threatened
- resist impossible demands
- avoid independence war against overwhelming Malta without sponsors
- seek autonomy when Malta is weak, absent, or repeatedly violates the charter
- choose routes from local conditions and patron order

Malta AI should create principalities when direct occupation is costly, supply is thin, or local cooperation supports it. It should avoid creating one-state subjects with no economy or force package.

## Principalities and multiplayer

A human player controlling a region that becomes eligible for principality status must receive a clear outcome. The event cannot silently replace the human actor. Valid options include:

- negotiate subject status
- reject and fight
- accept a cosmetic or government transformation
- become the principality under the same player control

Player switching uses the project's multiplayer contract. The release transaction must not create duplicate human control or inaccessible ghost actors.

## Completion evidence

The principality system is incomplete until:

- exact region registries exist
- every country identity passes collision and provenance checks
- every created actor has a complete package
- shared-tree overlays are country-specific
- obligations, succession, loyalty, and integration work
- Malta decisions can create, manage, and dissolve subjects safely
- AI behavior is route-aware
- invalid or annexed actors clean up
- focus, decision, event-log, asset, and workbook surfaces agree
