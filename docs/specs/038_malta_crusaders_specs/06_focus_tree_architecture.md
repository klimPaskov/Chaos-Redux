# Focus tree architecture

## Tree role

The Malta Crusader focus tree is the playable identity of the country. It begins after the crusade has already started. It must not spend several years explaining why the event happened or preparing a first landing that already exists.

The tree solves immediate survival, then opens political, military, logistical, territorial, diplomatic, formable, recovery, hidden, and terminal ambitions. Route choices must alter decisions, units, leaders, flags, territorial policy, AI, and diplomacy.

## Scale and pacing

The exact focus count belongs to implementation. A full implementation will likely require 90 to 130 authored focuses because the country has several political outcomes, military-order paths, a failure route, principalities, hidden routes, and terminal preparation.

Focus duration guidance:

- 7 days for emergency release handoffs, route reveals, and narrow settlement switches
- 35 days for early strategic choices, crisis responses, and branch openers
- 56 or 70 days for substantial programs and normal reforms
- longer commitments only when the public payoff and tradeoff justify them

Do not split one reward into several short filler focuses.

## First-glance lane plan

The tree should reserve distinct visual lanes:

1. **Center opening lane:** immediate survival and Crusade Council establishment
2. **Upper center lane:** political settlement and order authority
3. **Upper left lane:** Mediterranean logistics, industry, navy, and ports
4. **Upper right lane:** Holy Land campaign, expansion, and settlement
5. **Far left lane:** military modernization and custom units
6. **Far right lane:** diplomacy, Catholic support, and Papal relations
7. **Lower center lane:** principalities and governance
8. **Lower left lane:** Eleventh Crusade failure and recovery
9. **Lower right lane:** Holy See and Kingdom of God
10. **Hidden overlay lanes:** Teutonic Order, Atlantis consequences, and Holy World preparation

Hidden lanes remain invisible until their reveal conditions exist. Space should be reserved so their later appearance does not overlap ordinary branches.

## Opening group

### The Crusade Is Underway

A short opening focus confirms the release transaction, initializes focus-controlled content, and presents the current strategic situation. It should not grant large free rewards.

### Hold the Four Anchors

The early campaign identifies Malta, Jerusalem, the maritime bridge, and the primary supply port. The focus unlocks active missions to hold or restore them.

### Convene the Crusade Council

This focus opens the Crusade Council interface, order demand loop, and the first political choice.

### Three early choices

The player chooses an early priority without permanently locking the full political route:

- **Secure the Sea Road:** immediate logistics and convoy survival
- **Fortify the Holy Land:** defensive authority and local guard organization
- **Unleash the Knight Banners:** offensive tempo at a clear equipment and supply cost

These choices change the first 180 days. They can later converge, but they must lead to different builds and missions.

## Political settlement family

### Grand Magistry route

**Narrative:** one central order government imposes command over the expedition.

**Mechanical identity:** high Authority, strict command, fewer order disputes, stronger direct administration, higher resistance and autonomy pressure.

**Key groups:**

- codify emergency command
- appoint central officers
- absorb local chapter treasuries
- standardize order law
- choose a dominant order
- establish a permanent Grand Magistry

**Tradeoff:** rapid command and direct output in exchange for weaker cohesion, principality suspicion, and a risk of authoritarian overreach.

**Payoff:** Sovereign Order of Malta or order-specific dominion identity, advanced direct-control decisions, elite dominant-order formations.

### Assembly of the Langues route

**Narrative:** the revived historic language and regional groupings become a confederated council.

**Mechanical identity:** high Cohesion, broad order access, foreign volunteer integration, slower central decisions, strong multinational legitimacy.

**Key groups:**

- restore the Langues as political constituencies
- allocate council seats
- establish shared command
- rotate offices
- arbitrate order land grants
- create a constitutional confederation

**Tradeoff:** stable cooperation and wider recruitment at the cost of slower emergency command and weaker direct extraction.

**Payoff:** Confederation of the Military Orders, mixed-order elite units, stable principality federation, route-specific faction tools.

### Civilian Maltese settlement route

**Narrative:** Maltese civilian institutions constrain the imported crusader hierarchy.

**Mechanical identity:** legitimacy through constitutional rule, stronger core economy, lower occupation extremism, weaker order dominance.

**Key groups:**

- restore civilian courts
- protect Maltese labour and municipal institutions
- define military jurisdiction
- create an elected or mixed council
- reconcile Church and civil authority
- establish a constitutional crusader kingdom

**Tradeoff:** stronger domestic stability and foreign acceptance in exchange for reduced emergency power and slower military mobilization.

**Payoff:** Crusader Kingdom of Malta or constitutional order state, lower resistance governance, more reliable democratic supporters.

This route does not require a shallow full ideology branch. It uses the country's real internal problem, military versus civilian authority.

## Military Orders family

The military-order family is compatible with several political routes, but dominant-order capstones are mutually exclusive.

### Hospitaller path

Focus groups:

- hospitals under fire
- fortress medicine
- recovery columns
- relief access charters
- contaminated-region commands
- the Grand Hospitaller's network

Rewards include Hospitaller Companies, state hospitals, relief missions, casualty recovery, famine and migration response options, and defensive formations.

Payoff: **The Hospital and the Sword**, a durable institution that turns protection and care into military staying power.

### Templar path

Focus groups:

- reopen the treasury
- foreign credit network
- war finance offices
- offensive procurement
- captured concession system
- Templar grand command

Rewards include faster procurement, breakthrough, planning, sponsor deals, and conquest finance.

Tradeoff: debt, sponsor influence, corruption risk, and aggressive decision pressure.

Payoff: **The Temple's Treasury**, a powerful war economy with a visible dependency cost.

### Teutonic traditions path

Focus groups:

- heavy armour schools
- iron discipline
- eastern chapter doctrine
- knight foundries
- order high command
- the black cross arsenal

Rewards include Armored Knight quality, heavy equipment, organization, training, and harsh-climate preparation.

The normal route is public and does not reveal the hidden Teutonic alliance. It remains a viable military specialization even when Germany or the Holy Realm cannot participate.

### Saint Lazarus path

Focus groups:

- field hospitals for the shunned
- quarantine orders
- disease wardens
- attrition commands
- the Lazarite frontier

Rewards include outbreak protection, contaminated-region operations, relief missions, field hospitals, and recovery.

Payoff: **The Green Cross Commands**, a specialist order able to fight and provide care in the worst regions.

### Naval Orders path

Focus groups:

- command of the sea road
- island chapters
- boarding companies
- convoy courts
- amphibious banners
- the Mediterranean patrol

Rewards include marines, naval invasion planning, convoy defence, escorts, island forts, and port actions.

### Siege Brotherhood path

Focus groups:

- rebuild the engines
- engineers of the walls
- captured artillery schools
- explosive counterweights
- mobile siege trains
- brotherhood of the broken gate

Rewards include Siege Hosts, engineer support, fort attack, rail and depot actions, and later special ammunition.

## Mediterranean logistics and economy family

### Fortress Malta

Develop Fort St Angelo, the Grand Harbour, air defence, repair, underground storage, coastal forts, and local workshops.

This group improves the island without placing implausible industrial mass on it.

### Chain of Fortified Ports

Develop selected ports across Cyprus, the Holy Land, Aegean islands, and North Africa. Focus rewards should name valid controlled regions and unlock targeted construction decisions.

### Convoy Republic

Build escort capacity, convoy contracts, naval repair, captured merchant fleets, and route insurance.

### Workshops of the Orders

Create distributed military workshops for knight armour, bows, siege engines, and support equipment.

### Principality Contributions

Convert subject obligations into equipment, manpower, construction, or local militia without direct annexation.

### Resource strategies

Offer distinct answers to scarcity:

- foreign contracts
- captured concessions
- local substitution
- sponsor dependency
- expansion toward oil and steel

The player should not erase all resource constraints through one early focus.

## Military modernization family

### Standardize the Banners

Creates a small readable template set and centralized provider management.

### Armour Against the Century

Develops modern knight armour equipment and improves protection.

### The Remount Service

Builds horse, truck, and cavalry logistics.

### Ranged Orders

Splits Archer and Crossbow roles and unlocks production.

### Engines of Siege

Unlocks and improves Siege Formations.

### Modern combined arms

Integrates conventional artillery, anti-air, radios, logistics, and armoured support.

### Anti-Tank Lances

A later specialization with high training and reliability costs.

### Mechanized Knight Carriers

A late-game industrial route that turns mounted identity into modern mobile warfare.

### Blessed Commands

Requires Evolution III or a valid Papal route and high Sacred Legitimacy. It creates capped elite formations.

## Holy Land and expansion family

### Secure Jerusalem

A central anchor. It requires actual stable control, not a momentary occupation.

### The Jordan Corridor

Repairs the inland route, secures supply, and defines local governance.

### Levantine frontier

Opens decisions toward Lebanon, Syria, Antioch, and Tripoli according to current borders.

### Cyprus and the Aegean

Secures island bases and offers principality or direct command choices.

### Anatolian marches

Targets southern Anatolian and Cilician routes without granting a free war against all Turkey.

### North African ports

Targets selected coastal footholds and crusader marches.

### Rome and the western question

Opens only when the Papal or Italian relationship makes the route meaningful. It can use diplomacy, invitation, crisis, or conquest depending on current conditions.

### Settlement capstones

- a network of commanderies
- a league of principalities
- direct crusader kingdom
- restored local Christian governments
- Papal administration

Every war group needs postwar decisions. War goals alone are incomplete.

## Diplomacy and religious support family

### Appeal to Catholic governments

Opens targeted aid, volunteers, recognition, and convoy support.

### The Papal legation

Creates a formal relation with the Pope and a path toward Papal rule.

### Protect the pilgrims

Improves legitimacy through route safety and sacred-site administration.

### Concord with local churches

Addresses Orthodox and Eastern Christian institutions through negotiation, autonomy, or Papal pressure.

### Foreign military chapters

Lets eligible countries host order chapters, send officers, and raise limited formations.

### International condemnation response

Provides compliance, reform, propaganda, inspection, or defiance actions when crusader policy creates condemnation.

### Believer leagues

At high Chaos and legitimacy, creates a pre-terminal diplomatic network. It does not divide the whole world until Holy World activation.

## Principalities and governance family

This family opens after stable territorial control and Evolution II where required.

Focus groups:

- charter the Kingdom of Jerusalem
- restore Antioch
- establish the County of Tripoli
- grant Cyprus to an order
- organize the Aegean marches
- create an Anatolian order state
- raise a North African march
- define obligations and succession
- create a high court of the principalities

The player chooses direct rule, order grant, principality, local restoration, or Papal administration by region. No single focus creates every possible subject.

## Holy See route

### Papal eligibility

The route requires:

- valid Pope or Papal institutional actor
- sufficient Sacred Legitimacy
- stable control of Rome
- a public settlement choice
- no incompatible political route

### Route groups

- invite or install the Pope
- secure the Lateran and Roman administration
- move the political capital to Rome
- preserve Jerusalem as sacred military center
- reform the Crusade Council under Papal supremacy
- reorganize principalities as Papal subjects
- create the Holy See identity

The route should handle a pre-existing Vatican or Papal actor without duplicating the Pope.

## Kingdom of God route

The Kingdom of God is a late public formable route. It requires major territorial success, Papal supremacy, high Authority, and high Sacred Legitimacy.

It changes:

- country identity and flag
- leader and government
- focus access
- principality obligations
- recruitment
- diplomacy
- Holy World preparation eligibility

It does not automatically trigger the world-end route.

## Eleventh Crusade failure family

The failure branch becomes visible when the Holy Land expedition collapses or Malta loses most non-core footholds.

Focus groups:

- count the survivors
- rebuild the transports
- appeal to the Catholic world
- raise emergency banners
- fortify Malta
- select a new landing site
- negotiate with local allies
- launch the Eleventh Crusade

The branch supports several outcomes:

- successful return to Jerusalem
- a smaller alternative foothold
- negotiated retreat and fortified Malta
- local Christian protectorate
- dissolution of the crusade into a humanitarian or naval order state

Failure content must not merely restore the original state for free. Recovery requires time, equipment, sponsors, and a real military plan.

## Evolution overlays

### Evolution I overlay

Adds order headquarters, volunteer chapters, stronger unit branches, and deeper order politics.

### Evolution II overlay

Adds principality formation, subject governance, church disputes, local administration, and succession.

### Evolution III overlay

Adds relic searches, foreign religious movements, Blessed formations, and high-Chaos diplomacy.

Disabled evolutions must not block baseline completion. The tree should either hide the evolved content or offer a clean baseline alternative.

## Hidden Teutonic route

The hidden branch appears only after the exact Germany, Holy Realm, Malta, and flag conditions are met. Its visible public reveal begins with the alliance invitation, not with early spoiler focuses.

The branch includes:

- reconcile order myths
- negotiate the three-state pact
- form the Teutonic Order faction
- settle transformed leadership
- build multinational knight recruitment
- begin Operation The Final Crusade
- launch regional campaigns

A player who never meets the hidden conditions still has a complete normal Teutonic traditions path.

## Holy World preparation lane

This lane appears at 800 or more Chaos only when the Pope is supreme ruler.

Focus groups:

- define believer submission
- reorganize principalities
- build the Papal alliance
- create continental campaign offices
- prepare terminal logistics
- prove control of one complete continent
- await World Collapse

The lane can reach **Holy World Ready** below 1000 Chaos. It cannot set `world_end` before 1000.

## Branch interaction

Examples of required interactions:

- Hospitaller development improves failure recovery and humane governance.
- Templar finance accelerates expansion but raises sponsor and corruption pressure.
- Naval Orders reduce the cost and risk of Aegean and North African expansion.
- Siege Brotherhoods improve fort missions and city assaults.
- Civilian constitutional rule makes local restoration and negotiated settlements easier.
- Grand Magistry improves direct rule but worsens order and principality autonomy disputes.
- Papal rule increases sacred legitimacy but narrows secular diplomacy.
- High Cohesion improves principality federation and mixed-order units.
- High Authority improves direct integration and terminal readiness.

Branches must not exist as isolated stat columns.

## Focus Navigation

The final tree needs Focus Navigation entries for:

- Council and politics
- Military Orders
- Fortress and logistics
- Army modernization
- Holy Land campaign
- Principalities
- Diplomacy and Papal relations
- Eleventh Crusade when visible
- Holy World preparation when visible

Hidden route navigation stays unavailable until reveal.

## Search filters

Every focus uses accurate filters for its primary role. Custom filters are allowed for Military Orders and Crusader Governance if current vanilla filters cannot express them cleanly. Multi-tagging does not excuse unclear branch ownership.

## Focus inlay decision

The Crusade Council is better as a separate event-owned mechanic window than a focus inlay because it must remain usable during ordinary war and decision play. A small read-only inlay summary can be considered only if it materially helps route selection and does not duplicate the full GUI.

## AI plans

Required AI strategy plans:

- survival first
- Grand Magistry centralizer
- Langues confederation
- civilian constitutional route
- Hospitaller defensive order
- Templar offensive financier
- Teutonic heavy-armour route
- naval island campaign
- siege conquest route
- Papal Holy See route
- principality federation
- Eleventh Crusade recovery
- hidden Teutonic alliance
- Holy World preparation

Each plan needs route validity, campaign-state checks, and probability evidence. Invalid routes stay hidden or receive zero weight.

## Focus completion evidence

The focus tree is incomplete until:

- the architecture is implemented with clear lanes
- every required route has a visible root, internal direction, and payoff
- route locks and convergence points work
- hidden branches do not leak
- focus filters are accurate
- Focus Navigation works
- decisions, units, ideas, countries, leaders, flags, and events unlock correctly
- idea lifecycles are complete
- AI route plans are audited
- `hoi4.focus_inspect`, `hoi4.focus_render`, and bounded rewrite comparisons show a clean full-tree layout
- a route coverage table matches this spec
