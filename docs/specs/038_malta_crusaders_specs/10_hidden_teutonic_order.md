# Hidden route: Teutonic Order

## Route status

The Teutonic Order route is a hidden cross-event easter egg. It is not an Event 38 evolution, a public world-end route, or a manual scenario option.

It becomes possible only after Malta has succeeded in its main crusader campaign and the required Germany and Holy Realm facts exist. The route must remain absent from public Event Details, public evolution previews, the event catalog's public detail fields, and normal focus navigation until the reveal begins.

## Required campaign facts

All of these are required:

- Event 38 has fired and Malta or its accepted Holy See successor remains viable
- Malta has succeeded in its main crusader route
- Malta controls or leads a stable Holy Land and Mediterranean network
- the Holy Realm exists
- Germany exists and remains on the required Nazi route
- Germany has not been replaced by an incompatible successor
- `germany_master_race_claim_established` exists
- `germany_holy_realm_contact_positive` exists
- `holy_realm_german_origin_claim_contact` exists
- no terminal state is active
- the hidden route has not been permanently rejected or broken

The German flags represent Nazi belief, fabrication, or propaganda about racial origins. They do not establish a real biological or historical fact.

## Readiness checks

The route also needs practical readiness:

- Malta, Germany, and the Holy Realm can enter the same faction
- none is already locked into an incompatible terminal faction state
- each has a valid capital and government
- Germany can contribute meaningful industrial or military support
- Malta can contribute its crusader network
- the Holy Realm can contribute its own accepted mechanics
- leadership ownership can be resolved without the same Hitler character leading two countries simultaneously

A missing practical check keeps the route hidden or delays it. It does not create a malformed faction.

## Reveal sequence

### First hint

After the exact cross-event flags exist, a limited Malta and Germany incident can refer to overlapping myths, delegations, relic claims, and order traditions. The incident should be strange and specific without explaining the final route.

### Three-state negotiations

A hidden decision chain opens for Malta, Germany, and the Holy Realm. Each actor can:

- accept negotiations
- demand ideological or territorial concessions
- delay
- reject
- expose the talks

The alliance forms only when all three provide valid assent or the accepted AI route conditions resolve them.

### Formation

The faction name is **Teutonic Order**. Formation fires a dedicated super-event and creates one faction-level command structure.

The alliance combines:

- Malta's crusader and religious institutions
- Germany's industry, military, and Nazi ideological apparatus
- the Holy Realm's religious mythology and route-specific systems

The event presentation should make the coalition intentionally absurd while showing its internal brutality and instability. It should not treat the ideological mixture as coherent history.

## Faction structure

### Leadership

Faction leadership should normally depend on the accepted formation bargain. Germany may be the strongest military member, while Malta owns religious and crusader authority.

The faction can use a shared **Triune Command** status with:

- Malta as crusade authority
- Germany as industrial and military command
- Holy Realm as spiritual-mythic authority

The player sees a qualitative command arrangement. The route should not add another persistent Event 38 public meter.

### Membership

Initial members are limited to Malta or Holy See, Nazi Germany, and the Holy Realm. Later members require:

- ideological or religious route compatibility
- an invitation or subjugation path
- no terminal conflict with an existing member
- a role in regional campaign plans

The faction cannot automatically absorb every Catholic, fascist, or Buddhist state.

### Shared capabilities

- multinational knight recruitment
- German production contracts
- Holy Realm advisers and symbols
- shared military research where compatible
- regional crusade planning
- joint elite formations
- foreign order chapters
- faction logistics and bases

Technology sharing must respect mutual exclusivity and use compatibility-aware grants or research groups.

## Blessed alliance leadership

### Transformed Hitler figure

The Holy Realm can install a transformed Blessed Hitler figure as a Catholic-Buddhist-Nazi religious symbol only through the existing Holy Realm route and accepted cross-event conditions.

The same Hitler character cannot be active leader in Germany and the Holy Realm at once.

Accepted implementation strategies:

1. transfer the character to the Holy Realm and install a named German regency
2. keep Hitler in Germany and use a separate institutional or symbolic Holy Realm leadership representation
3. use an event-owned transformed character instance only if the character system and ownership audit prove that duplication is impossible at runtime

The implementation must choose one strategy after inspecting current Holy Realm and German character ownership. A generic duplicate portrait and character is forbidden.

### Blessed Supreme Pope

Malta or the Holy See can receive a transformed Blessed Supreme Pope or equivalent Papal ruler when the Papal route and alliance bargain support it.

A real Pope requires sourced portrait handling. A supernatural visual transformation can use an animated overlay or fictional route-specific presentation only when the underlying identity remains clear and source rules are respected.

## Operation The Final Crusade

### Role

Operation The Final Crusade is a multinational regional campaign system. It does not declare war on the entire world in one effect.

### Campaign offices

The faction creates regional campaign offices for:

- the Mediterranean
- central and eastern Europe
- the Middle East
- Africa
- Asia
- the Atlantic when later conditions support it

Only one or two major regional campaigns should normally be active at a time before terminal escalation.

### Regional campaign loop

1. choose a region
2. identify valid hostile governments and local supporters
3. prepare logistics and bases
4. recruit multinational formations
5. issue alignment demands or protectorate offers
6. begin one or more bounded wars
7. create settlement, puppet, principality, or allied-government outcomes
8. evaluate faction cohesion and next target

### Malta's contribution

- religious legitimacy
- military orders
- crusader state administration
- Mediterranean ports
- knight recruitment
- Papal or sacred diplomacy

### Germany's contribution

- industrial production
- armour and air power
- logistics
- command structure
- conventional army
- coercive diplomacy

### Holy Realm contribution

- route-specific religious mythology
- advisers and transformed leaders
- local units and systems
- ideological legitimacy inside the alliance

### Player choices

Malta can push for:

- order states and principalities
- Papal administrations
- direct alliance members
- religious protectorates

Germany can push for:

- industrial clients
- occupation administrations
- racial-ideological rule
- direct military command

The Holy Realm can push for its own spiritual and political settlements.

These conflicts create events and can weaken the alliance before Atlantis.

## Final Crusade super-event

The super-event fires when the faction forms and Operation The Final Crusade is formally proclaimed. It should mark a new world-order bloc, not merely one war declaration.

Research requirements:

- verified quote candidates about crusade, alliance, fanaticism, or power
- a short researched button remark
- unique licensed musical cue
- generated fictional image showing the alliance's medieval-modern and ideological mixture
- dedicated slot, sprite, audio ID, and settings-aware sound wiring

Final wording and source choices belong to the super-event research workflow.

## AI behavior

### Malta AI

Malta accepts when:

- its crusader route is successful
- Germany is strong enough to help
- the Holy Realm relation is positive
- Papal or dominant-order politics permit the ideological compromise
- no safer ordinary route offers more value

Malta rejects when:

- civilian constitutional or humane governance routes dominate
- Germany is collapsing
- the Holy Realm is hostile
- the alliance would trigger suicidal wars
- Sacred Legitimacy would collapse from the pact

### Germany AI

Germany accepts when:

- the required Nazi route is active
- the racial-origin claim flags exist
- Malta controls useful ports and sacred territory
- the Holy Realm route is positive
- Germany can dominate or benefit from the faction

### Holy Realm AI

The Holy Realm follows its own route compatibility and prior contact outcomes. It should not join because a single flag exists while its current government is hostile.

All acceptance, bargaining, regional target, and settlement weights require named probability scenarios.

## Atlantis eligibility timer

The hidden Atlantis betrayal becomes eligible only after the Teutonic Order has existed continuously for two years.

The timer pauses or resets when:

- a required founding member leaves
- a required founding member is annexed
- the faction dissolves
- Germany leaves the Nazi route
- Germany is no longer the required actor

The timer must use one persistent start date or elapsed counter with clear save behavior. It cannot be satisfied by leaving and rejoining immediately.

## Failure and dissolution

The alliance can fail through:

- member rejection during formation
- leadership dispute
- regional campaign disaster
- Malta's Papal condemnation
- Holy Realm route break
- Germany's defeat or regime change
- member civil war
- Atlantis betrayal

A non-Atlantis dissolution produces:

- faction breakup or reduced pact
- equipment and technology settlement
- unit ownership cleanup
- former-member diplomacy
- possible order and legitimacy consequences

It does not automatically trigger a world-end route.

## Cross-event ownership

Event 38 owns:

- Malta's hidden eligibility
- faction formation from Malta's side
- crusader campaign offices
- Final Crusade presentation
- transition into Atlantis eligibility

The Holy Realm event owns its country, transformed leadership, and internal contact state. Germany owns its regime, character, and Nazi route. Shared effects should read owner-published facts rather than duplicate their systems.

## Public secrecy contract

Before reveal, no public surface may name:

- Teutonic Order faction formation
- Blessed alliance leaders
- Operation The Final Crusade
- Atlantis eligibility
- two-year betrayal timer

After formation, the faction and Final Crusade become public. Atlantis remains hidden until the betrayal begins.
