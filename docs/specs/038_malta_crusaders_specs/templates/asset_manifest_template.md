# Event 38 asset manifest template

| Requirement ID | Asset type | Runtime basename | Source mode | Source title or prompt | Creator or archive | Source URL | License or terms | Original checksum | Processed checksum | Target dimensions | Source path | Processed PNG | Final runtime path | Sprite, sound, entity, or consumer | Static fallback | Producer | Reviewer | Status | Temporary workspace disposition | Notes or blocker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Allowed status values:

- planned
- source_selected
- in_production
- needs_user_review
- blocked
- final_candidate
- wired
- reviewed
- complete

`complete` requires final runtime consumer, provenance, review, and permanent evidence. It cannot mean only that a source PNG or provider candidate exists.
