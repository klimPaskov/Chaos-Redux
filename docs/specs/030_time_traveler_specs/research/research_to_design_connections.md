# Research to Design Connections

| Research observation | Design consequence | Content rejected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Napoleonic rule combined legal and administrative reform with concentrated executive power | Napoleon receives law, administration, merit, and executive-risk routes | battlefield bonuses as the whole package |
| Caesar's rise is inseparable from civil conflict and republican breakdown | Caesar creates emergency-office and institutional-collapse choices | generic Roman restoration button |
| Alexander's empire fragmented after his death | conquest success creates succession and successor-state pressure | permanent stable empire with free cores |
| Mongol rule used communication and local administration | Genghis receives relay, federation, and adaptive-governance content | one-note horde caricature |
| Joan's public role was shaped by custody, trial, faith, and national war | Joan is primarily a resistance symbol and commander | automatic throne claimant |
| Saladin's context requires regional and Muslim perspectives | coalition and anti-occupation politics receive careful regional treatment | generic Crusade spectacle |
| Bolívar's union project faced constitutional and regional tension | Gran Colombia restoration requires federal settlement and integration | instant continental core grant |
| Ieyasu used selective trade and patient consolidation | Tokugawa content includes controlled exchange and dual authority | simple isolation modifier |
| Modern political biographies are first-party identity sources | named future packages remain neutral and require independent implementation research | partisan praise, mockery, slogans, or direct current-politics commentary |
| The source brief names three public values | hidden complexity remains internal and Temporal Stability reuses divergence | additional portal, faction, trust, and machine meters |
| The source brief calls for limited historical selection | ordinary campaign caps figures and visitors | every named person appearing in one normal campaign |
| Machine fiction inspiration must remain original | Closed Future, Consensus Engine, and Causal Monopoly define a distinct motive and look | direct Terminator plot, visual language, or catchphrases |
