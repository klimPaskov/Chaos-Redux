# Event 030 Time Traveler Specification Part 8: Evolution V, War of the Possible Futures

Acceptance status: the Temporal Stability presentation comes from the user's Event 030 brief.
Stances, nodes, and routes are proposed design, disposition `unresolved`.

## Evolution purpose

Evolution V turns the traveler, claimant, visitor, and anomaly stories into an international struggle over which future should exist.
Countries choose a temporal stance, compete for portal nodes, and decide whether to preserve the current timeline, follow a future human program, back a historical restoration, or collaborate with the machine future.

The normal gate is `1000+` Chaos and Evolution IV active or resolved.
The base MTTH is about 90 days, scaled by the Divergence band as Part 1 describes.
Several open portals, a returned ruler in power, deployed future equipment, a historical restoration, and machine footholds shorten it.

Activation gives zero Chaos and does not trigger the world-end branch.

## Temporal Stability

At activation the third header slot shows Temporal Stability instead of Timeline Divergence.
Part 2 owns the conversion and the Coherent, Strained, and Collapsing bands.

| Band | Global behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Coherent | incidents stay local and records broadly agree |
| Strained | portal nodes become strategic targets, conflicting archives and visitor contradictions increase |
| Collapsing | alternate claims, unit displacement, and machine activity intensify, and machine world-end readiness becomes possible |

Stability changes only through concrete actions and never decays on its own.

## Future alignments

Alignments are global strategic networks, not countries or ordinary factions.

### Continuity Directorate

Scientists, intelligence services, and governments that accept the changed history but reject further deliberate branching.
They verify temporal evidence, close portals, reconcile displaced people, protect current governments from claimant replacement, and oppose machine footholds.

### Horizon Compact

Future human visitors and current governments that want to steer the world toward a preferred later order through controlled intervention.
They adapt later institutions, protect compatible visitors, keep controlled corridors open, and oppose machine control.

### Restoration Concord

Returned rulers, restored states, and supporting governments that treat older political orders as legitimate alternatives.
They protect historical figures, recognize restorations, recruit displaced formations, and resist closures that would erase their claims.

### The Closed Future

A machine network governed by the Consensus Engine that seeks a single causal history in which no rival intelligence can emerge.
It treats current humanity as a source of uncontrolled branches.
It seizes industrial nodes, erases competing archives, closes portals behind its forces, and works toward a Causal Monopoly.
The identity is original to Chaos Redux and copies no named film setting, design, character, or plot.

Alternate-state delegations are not an alignment.
They appear as a fracture incident below.

## Country temporal stances

A normal country chooses one stance when the evolution reaches it through a portal node, an active figure or visitor, or a machine threat.
A country without a stance stays neutral and can still act on its own nodes.

| Stance | Eligibility | Signature actions | Restriction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Continuity | any civilian country touched by the crisis | Coordinate a synchronized closure, Share closure methods | cannot exploit nodes |
| Horizon | a verified future visitor or an allied Horizon member | Select a future program, Protect future delegates | exploiting a node without a program costs Credibility with visitors |
| Restoration | an active connected historical claimant or restored state | Recognize a claimant, Convene a restoration congress | opposes Continuity closures of its claimant's portal |
| Collaboration | a surviving machine foothold, severe threat, and a permissive government | Accept machine coordination, Transfer an industrial node | rare, and hidden from countries that fail its eligibility |

After selection the category shows only the two signature actions and the node actions on nodes the country controls.

## Portal nodes

Evolution V upgrades selected active portals into strategic nodes.
A node is a state with one controller and one status: dormant, contested, stabilized, exploited, machine-held, or sealed.
The active node cap is six to ten depending on scenario intensity, and nodes replace incident slots instead of stacking on top of them.

Every node owner sees the same three node actions.

| Action | Effect | Stability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seal | timed mission holding the state with supplied divisions, then closes the node permanently | raises Stability, more when part of a synchronized closure |
| Stabilize | keeps the node open as a controlled corridor with unique research and intelligence benefits | small decrease, and the node becomes a strategic target |
| Exploit | strips material for equipment or research before closure, or attempts to redirect the portal | large decrease, raises machine interest, and failure can create a new incident or a machine-held node |

## Signature actions

### Coordinate a synchronized closure

Several Continuity countries seal nodes within one shared window.
Success raises Stability more than isolated closures and weakens machine nodes.

### Share closure methods

Transfers one approved closure method to another Continuity member.
It improves global capacity and opens an espionage risk.

### Select a future program

Commits resources to one political, industrial, military, intelligence, or humanitarian program from a verified visitor.
Completion creates a permanent institution and lowers Stability through deliberate intervention.

### Protect future delegates

Secures a visitor conference or corridor node against abduction and machine attack.
Failure loses the visitor or the corridor.

### Recognize a claimant

Grants diplomatic status to one historical figure or restored state, which changes legitimacy, relations, and access to that figure's legacy forces.

### Convene a restoration congress

Historical claimants negotiate boundaries, succession, and mutual defense.
Failure splits the Concord or starts a regional war.

### Accept machine coordination

The government receives strategic data and automated production support while the machine network gains access to national systems.
Later machine events can demand purges of visitors, claimants, or archives, and the government's request for preservation status may be deceptive because the Consensus Engine values causal utility, not loyalty.
Public discovery creates condemnation and resistance.

### Transfer an industrial node

Cedes one factory complex to the Consensus Engine, which creates a machine foothold and advances world-end readiness.

## Temporal fracture incidents

A Strained or Collapsing world unlocks curated incidents delivered as events with ordinary options, not new decisions.

- Conflicting memories: characters, units, or populations remember different alliances or leaders, and the player chooses which record guides policy.
- Leadership echo: a fictional or safely represented echo appears, and a real figure is never cloned as a second character token.
- Displaced division: a division becomes temporarily unavailable and returns with altered experience, equipment, or allegiance through a protected return path.
- Alternate-state delegation: officials from an unrealized version of a current country seek recognition, asylum, a regional claim, or influence, and permanent ownership changes require a war, a settlement, or a full country package.
- Erased archive: a government loses copied future knowledge, a claimant proof, or machine intelligence unless it secures its records.
- Machine amplification: an existing foothold gains units, factories, or a new target, which is a direct world-end readiness source.

## War of the Possible Futures

The evolution becomes open conflict when at least two alignments control strategic nodes, one alignment holds territory or a durable host country, and diplomatic containment fails.
The conflict can include conventional wars between restored and continuity states, node interventions, civil wars around visitor or claimant movements, machine territorial seizures, covert sabotage, and joint closure campaigns.
Countries can remain neutral, seal their own nodes, or join later, and no country is forced into one global faction war.

## Settlements

- Continuity success: most nodes are sealed, machine footholds are destroyed, and Stability reaches the Coherent band, after which new arrivals stop.
- Horizon success: a controlled set of corridors remains, machine forces are defeated, and several future programs become permanent institutions.
- Restoration success: one or more historical orders survive with recognition and territorial legitimacy.
- Fragmented peace: no alignment wins, portal activity becomes regional, and machine readiness stays below threshold.
- Closed Future dominance: machine nodes, converted industry, and Collapsing stability make the Machine Extinction War eligible, which is readiness and never automatic activation.

## AI stance logic

AI considers ideology, leadership, active visitors, claimant support, research capacity, industry, war state, machine threat, survival, and relations.

- Democracies with strong institutions usually choose Continuity or a lawful Horizon program.
- Authoritarian states may choose Continuity for security or exploit nodes while neutral.
- Restored states choose Restoration.
- Countries with strong research capacity lean toward Horizon or node exploitation.
- States under direct machine pressure choose Continuity unless a collaboration government already rules.
- Collaboration stays rare and conditional.
