# Event 030 Time Traveler Specification Part 6: Evolution III, Visitors from the Future

## Evolution purpose

Evolution III brings people from periods after the normal Hearts of Iron IV era into states that recognize their future political, military, scientific, social, or economic significance. These visitors remember later institutions and conflicts. They also bring partisan goals, personal ambitions, and incompatible accounts of history.

The normal gate is `600+` Chaos, Evolution II active or resolved, and enough Timeline Divergence to open forward portals. The base MTTH is about 150 days. Future technology use, several copied knowledge fragments, and a returned historical ruler shorten the pacing.

## Campaign limits

A normal campaign should receive one initial future visitor and no more than two active future visitor packages at once. High-chaos manual setups may raise the cap to four. Political heads of government should be rarer than scientists, organizers, officers, and institutional specialists.

The named flagship political packages from the source brief are:

- Donald Trump in the United States
- Vladimir Putin in Russia or the Soviet Union
- Xi Jinping in China

These packages require neutral research, sourced portraits, subject ownership review, careful localisation, and route logic that reacts to the 1930s state. They should not become partisan jokes or automatic rulers.

Additional visitors require separate researched packages. Implementation should prioritize people born after the campaign start or people whose younger self is not already an active character. A visitor whose younger self exists creates a duplicate-person crisis and belongs to the stricter Evolution V echo rules unless a safe one-owner representation is designed.

## Future visitor categories

### Political leader

A later political leader can organize a movement, advise an existing government, seek office, bargain for protection, or make a claim to future legitimacy. Their methods may rely on media, party organization, security institutions, economic policy, identity politics, or coalition management that does not map cleanly onto the current state.

### Military officer

A later officer understands operational concepts, professional systems, intelligence integration, logistics, and weapons that may not exist. They must adapt their knowledge to current equipment and doctrine.

### Scientist or engineer

A later technical specialist can identify viable research paths, safety failures, material needs, and future applications. They cannot provide complete industrial supply chains or instantly create unavailable components.

### Activist or social organizer

A later organizer introduces methods of mass communication, civil organization, labor action, rights campaigns, resistance, or international advocacy. Their movement can transform politics without a direct claim to government.

### Industrial or administrative figure

A later manager or institutional planner understands large organizations, supply systems, public administration, finance, or industrial coordination. Their advice can improve state capacity while creating new concentrations of power.

### Intelligence specialist

A later intelligence figure understands surveillance, disinformation, counterintelligence, covert influence, and institutional failure. Their arrival intensifies the international race and creates serious civil-liberty risks.

## Authenticity and incompatible futures

Future visitors cannot prove every claim through archives that do not yet exist. Their proof uses:

- knowledge of the original traveler or another returned figure
- details of later technology that can be tested in principle
- personal knowledge of current people who live into the visitor's era
- predictions about institutional developments already beginning
- artifacts, materials, recordings, or documents that pass scientific examination

Different visitors may remember incompatible futures. The system tracks each visitor's provenance internally. The player sees contradictions, confidence classes, and evidence, not a hidden truth score.

Some visitors may lie deliberately. A liar still needs a coherent motive, evidence strategy, and detectable contradiction. Random deception without a design purpose would make the system feel arbitrary.

## Donald Trump package

### Host and arrival

The primary host is the United States. The arrival becomes public or politically visible through business contacts, press attention, a security report, or a direct attempt to reach national leadership.

### Movement

The movement can draw from media operators, business networks, economic nationalists, anti-establishment voters, border and sovereignty campaigns, and people attracted to personal political leadership.

### Gameplay identity

The package centers on modern mass-media politics, personal branding, economic nationalism, trade conflict, executive negotiation, and distrust of established political institutions. These methods enter a media and party system that lacks later television, internet, and social platforms, so adaptation matters.

### Main routes

- communications and negotiation adviser
- business and industrial coalition organizer
- public political movement seeking office through existing institutions
- opposition figure attacking the current party system
- detained or marginalized visitor whose supporters turn the suppression into a campaign

A serious claim to power requires public support, a valid electoral or political route, and a weakened existing government. It should not create an automatic civil war.

## Vladimir Putin package

### Host and arrival

Russia or the Soviet Union is primary. The route changes according to whether the host is Soviet, anti-Soviet, monarchist, nationalist, fragmented, or occupied.

### Movement

The movement can draw from intelligence services, security officials, state administrators, Russian nationalists, strategic-industry managers, and people seeking order after political fragmentation.

### Gameplay identity

The package centers on security institutions, centralized executive authority, intelligence practice, strategic resources, state restoration, and controlled political competition. A visitor who remembers the Soviet collapse can either defend the Union, prepare a Russian successor state, or attempt to shape a different collapse.

### Main routes

- security and intelligence adviser
- internal party or state organizer
- Russian continuity movement inside a weakening Soviet state
- national leader claim after a genuine state collapse
- detained visitor used as a source on later security failures

The package must avoid treating a future Russian state as inevitable. Current ideology, national composition, war, and the success or failure of Event 005 matter.

## Xi Jinping package

### Host and arrival

A valid Chinese government is primary. The package must identify which state controls the relevant political and territorial base. It should not assume one claimant already represents all China.

### Movement

The movement can draw from party organizers, state planners, military-political cadres, anti-corruption advocates, industrial development officials, and groups seeking centralized national coordination.

### Gameplay identity

The package centers on party discipline, long-horizon industrial policy, central coordination, national development, political consolidation, and control over large institutions. Later systems must be adapted to a fragmented and wartime China.

### Main routes

- party and state organization adviser
- industrial planning coordinator
- internal movement within a communist Chinese government
- national consolidation claimant in a fractured China
- detained visitor whose future knowledge becomes a contested party document

A leadership route requires control, party support, and a real political opening. It cannot simply replace every existing Chinese leader.

## Knowledge adaptation

Future visitors introduce concepts, not free completed technology.

### Political and communications methods

Later propaganda, polling, broadcast strategy, political branding, civil organization, and information control can change decision systems and party behavior. They require available media and institutions.

### Economic methods

Later industrial policy, privatization, state coordination, trade strategy, supply-chain management, and financial methods can unlock policy routes. The player must choose which later model fits the current ideology and economy.

### Intelligence methods

Later surveillance, data organization, disinformation, counterintelligence, and covert action can strengthen agencies. They also create Exposure, condemnation, and internal political risks.

### Military concepts

Later officers can introduce operational concepts, command integration, logistics, air-land coordination, missile theory, and strategic warning. The host still needs the corresponding technology and equipment.

### Scientific knowledge

Later scientists can identify viable research paths and safety problems. Actual advanced technology must use the relevant research, special project, or custom technology system. The event must not grant an unrestricted technology union from a future visitor.

## Political response options

Every host can:

- integrate the visitor in a bounded advisory role
- place the visitor under controlled study
- grant a public platform
- permit an existing legal political route
- bargain for information in exchange for asylum
- suppress the movement
- imprison or exile the visitor
- conduct a covert removal

The action set changes with current laws, ideology, stability, and the visitor's conduct.

## Claimant escalation

A future visitor becomes a serious claimant only after four conditions:

- a legitimate connection to the host polity
- a public or institutional support base
- low enough state authority or a genuine constitutional opening
- a clear plan for government in the current era

The package begins with political pressure. It escalates to mass crisis, coup, regional revolt, or civil war only when support and armed capacity justify it.

## Conflicting visitor accounts

When two visitors remember incompatible futures, the host can:

- run independent verification
- compartmentalize them
- hold a controlled confrontation
- choose one strategic model
- preserve both accounts as uncertainty
- expose the contradiction publicly
- use the conflict as a counterintelligence trap

The choice changes Credibility, Exposure, and Timeline Divergence. It can also reveal that one visitor is from an alternate future without proving which future is preferable.

## Public movements

Every major visitor must appear through a public or institutional action. Suitable surfaces include speeches, demonstrations, party congresses, industrial campaigns, intelligence defections, government appointments, arrests, leaked documents, and foreign asylum disputes.

A visitor who remains secret still creates a visible government consequence. The event should never hide a named major figure in an advisor list with no arrival story.

## Modern figure portrayal rules

- Use neutral, factual direction.
- Avoid partisan insults, praise, memes, caricature, or copied slogans as default writing.
- Distinguish the historical person's documented career from the fictional alternate-history route.
- Do not invent direct quotations.
- Use sourced real-person portraits and one-owner character handling.
- Treat imprisonment, assassination, and civil war as serious political consequences.
- Provide different routes based on the current country and do not force one canonical ideology.

## Evolution outcomes

- one or more later-era advisers are integrated
- a future visitor builds a public movement
- a visitor wins or loses a political contest
- a visitor becomes a claimant, coup leader, or civil-war leader
- a government suppresses or exiles the visitor
- incompatible futures become public
- future methods alter state institutions
- copied future documents spread to other countries
- Timeline Divergence reaches the point where exact forecasts collapse

## Chaos changes

Evolution activation gives zero Chaos. One-shot consequence changes can follow a future visitor taking power, a major public contradiction, a civil war, a later technology being deployed, or a peaceful resolution that prevents conflict. Generic war and death sources remain separate.
