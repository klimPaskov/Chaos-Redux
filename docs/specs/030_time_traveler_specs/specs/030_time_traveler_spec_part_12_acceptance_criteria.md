# Event 030 Time Traveler Specification Part 12: Implementation Acceptance Criteria

## Completion definition

Event 030 is complete only when the baseline, all accepted evolutions, the public world-end branch, the triggerable scenario, AI, assets, documentation, Event Details, event logs, catalog, cleanup, and validation surfaces agree.

A parser-clean entry event is not completion.

## Baseline event

- `chaosx.nr30.1` exists and starts the canonical chain.
- Event 030 is registered as a Major event with Chaos level `1`.
- The default enable allowlist includes Event 030 only after the rework is ready for normal selection.
- Host selection supports existing majors and player-controlled countries, including player non-majors.
- Shared special-country and actual-nonhuman exclusions are respected.
- One original traveler is created and cannot duplicate.
- The opening verifies at least two proof families.
- The host chooses one access arrangement.
- The initial national spirit is useful and restrained.
- The strongest rewards come from completed consultations.
- Baseline can reach every mapped durable outcome without an evolution.
- Traveler loss converts direct access into the correct institutional legacy.

## Public values

- Credibility, Exposure, and Timeline Divergence are the only persistent Event 030 values the player must track before Evolution V.
- Every value has a labelled range, stage, cause, consequence, and response.
- Hidden sector and operation values remain hidden.
- Evolution V displays derived Temporal Stability instead of adding a fourth meter.
- Values are centralized and persist through save and reload.
- Custody transfer recalculates Credibility without resetting global history.
- Public Exposure creates a permanent floor.

## Consultations

- All six consultation families exist.
- Only one consultation mission can be active at a time.
- Every consultation creates a real objective, target, risk, or commitment.
- Costs use no more than four spendable types per action.
- Costs are dynamic and use correct texticons.
- Objectives name states, regions, countries, or measurable conditions.
- Success, partial success, divergence failure, and forgery failure use distinct effects.
- No consultation grants unrestricted future technology.
- Rewards change play and avoid tiny standalone modifier dust.
- AI can use every consultation through an equivalent path.

## International intelligence race

- The interested-country registry is bounded and sparse.
- All seven foreign operations exist with distinct risk and purpose.
- All six host countermeasures exist with meaningful costs and consequences.
- Abduction and assassination require mature access and valid routes.
- A captured double never becomes a second traveler.
- Custody transfer moves the correct character, categories, targets, and cooldown state.
- Former custodians retain only their copied legacy and recovery options.
- Multiplayer gives every valid player an operation path without ghost copies.

## Evolution I

- The evolution uses MTTH and respects the `200+` gate.
- A confirmed traveler death closes only this evolution track.
- Hidden sector support is reflected through events and decisions, not six public meters.
- Peaceful charter, emergency authority, leadership, political contest, arrest, transfer, and removal routes exist.
- A traveler government has strengths, limits, and preventive-governance choices.
- Coup, revolt, and civil war require escalating support and territory.
- Small or unsupported movements do not force a full civil war.
- Every leadership change updates character, politics, AI, ideas, decisions, and foreign response.

## Evolution II

- The evolution uses MTTH and respects the `400+` gate.
- The initial nine figure packages are fully researched and implemented or explicitly reported as blocked.
- Normal active figure caps are enforced.
- Every figure has arrival, authenticity, custody, movement, power, and resolution stages.
- Historical knowledge limits are real gameplay constraints.
- Named figures never enter silently as free advisers.
- Real-person ownership and portrait rules are satisfied.
- Restorations include territory, recognition, resistance, integration, AI, and cleanup.
- The same historical figure cannot reappear in ordinary play.

## Evolution III

- The evolution uses MTTH and respects the `600+` gate.
- Future visitors use bounded active caps.
- Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin, and Xi Jinping receive neutral researched packages or are explicitly blocked with a source reason.
- Political, military, scientific, activist, industrial, and intelligence categories are supported.
- Knowledge adaptation requires current-era institutions and equipment.
- Incompatible futures create evidence and policy choices.
- Claimant escalation requires polity connection, support, state weakness, and a government plan.
- No real-person face is generated or substituted.

## Evolution IV

- The evolution uses MTTH and respects the `800+` gate.
- The global and country incident caps are enforced.
- All eight incident families have a complete lifecycle.
- Prehistoric fauna remains a state incident unless a later accepted unit expansion exists.
- Displaced formations require equipment and modern training.
- New civilian cohorts do not debit nonexistent source population.
- Existing-population movement uses Migration.
- Temporal disease uses the biological and humanitarian owner systems.
- Unique units and characters are protected from unexplained deletion.
- Machine scouts remain a local threat and contribute to later readiness only when durable.

## Evolution V

- The evolution uses MTTH and respects the `1000+` gate.
- Temporal Stability is derived from Timeline Divergence.
- Continuity Directorate, Horizon Compact, Restoration Concord, alternate delegations, and the Closed Future have distinct roles.
- Country stance selection changes available play.
- Strategic portal nodes use a bounded state registry.
- Low-stability incidents use curated safeguards.
- Real-person duplicates are represented without cloned character tokens.
- War of the Possible Futures can remain regional and does not force every country into one faction.
- Closed Future dominance creates world-end readiness, not automatic activation.

## Machine Extinction War

- The branch has its own public Event Details row and persistent toggle.
- All readiness conditions are checked after Evolution V.
- Activation requires Chaos above `1000` and no active world end.
- Activation sets the shared world-end state, scenario flag, super-event visibility, and world threat source.
- The Closed Future has an original country, leader, doctrine, visual identity, AI, units, industry, and cleanup.
- The Closed Future is marked special and actual nonhuman.
- Human collaborator states remain human.
- Industrial seizure, decapitation, causal lock, extinction operations, and final convergence phases exist.
- Machine production depends on converted industry and equipment.
- Human response decisions have real costs, named targets, and AI equivalents.
- Population loss, migration, famine, condemnation, and unconventional warfare route through their owners.
- Machine and human victory conditions use world state.
- A qualifying defeat aftermath exists and does not fire after a small local skirmish.

## Triggerable scenario

- The authoritative workbook and registry confirm the final scenario ID.
- The dynamic scenario list, details, confirmation flow, and four intensity stops are wired.
- Low, Medium, High, and Maximum create materially different setups.
- Maximum activates every temporal category, not every possible named figure.
- Maximum raises Chaos above the world-end threshold but does not set `world_end` during setup.
- Scenario bypass flags are tightly scoped and cleared.
- Duplicate launch is blocked.
- Normal Event 030 behavior resumes after setup.

## AI and probability

- Host access, consultations, countermeasures, foreign operations, claimant responses, incident policy, temporal stances, collaboration, and machine strategy all have AI logic.
- Invalid countries, dead figures, closed portals, absent targets, disabled evolutions, and disabled world-end branches receive zero or hidden availability.
- The ten named probability scenarios are inspected and evaluated.
- Every weight patch receives a same-scenario compare pass.
- Candidate pools and external factors are declared so exact and unresolved findings are not confused.

## Assets

- Every authorized visible asset has a manifest row and runtime consumer.
- Original traveler leader and advisor portraits are distinct consumer-ready outputs.
- Every real person has sourced provenance, crop, ownership, and review evidence.
- Decision, idea, category, achievement, faction, and machine icon families are not satisfied by resized unrelated assets.
- Every new flag is image-generated after the required source or fictional design step.
- Every super-event has final image and unique licensed audio.
- Machine models, animations, sounds, counters, materials, exports, and reimport evidence are complete for every mapped runtime consumer.
- No runtime reference points into temporary `docs/assets/` workspaces at final completion.

## Text and documentation

- Final player-facing text follows the Event 030 text direction and project style.
- No em dash or semicolon appears in player-facing prose.
- No dialectical template, staccato filler, process note, or hidden mechanic leak appears.
- Event Details describe premise and public state, not raw effects.
- Every evolution has log text across the correct surfaces.
- The world-end row is branch-specific.
- `docs/events/030_time_traveler/` contains the canonical implementation overview.
- Super-event research and audio documentation are complete.
- The authoritative XLSX is updated and the three CSVs are regenerated.

## HOI4 MCP evidence

- `hoi4.event_inspect`, `hoi4.event_render`, and `hoi4.event_compare` cover the full chain.
- `hoi4.probability_inspect` begins every weighted audit.
- Technology or doctrine changes use `hoi4.tech_inspect`, `hoi4.tech_render`, and `hoi4.tech_compare`.
- Any accepted dedicated event UI change uses the mandatory GUI inspect, render, rewrite, and compare flow. The current design does not require a dedicated full window.
- Any map rewrite uses the map MCP route.
- Missing MCP capability is reported as a blocker and source-only review does not replace it.

## Audits before completion

- decision and mission audit
- localisation audit
- country package audit for every durable new actor
- focus tree audit for the Closed Future tree
- AI probability audit
- event completion audit
- spreadsheet alignment pass
- documentation curation pass when working plans or handoffs have accumulated
- manual or subagent improvement-loop closure pass after the main implementation tranche

## Simplification reporting

The final implementation report must list every missing or weaker substitute. It must explicitly report blocked portraits, absent figure packages, missing models, pending audio, unimplemented scenario intensity, generic country content, missing AI, skipped MCP evidence, or catalog mismatch.

No unapproved fallback can be presented as completion.
