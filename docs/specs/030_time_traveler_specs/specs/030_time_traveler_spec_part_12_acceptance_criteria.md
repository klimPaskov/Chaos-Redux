# Event 030 Time Traveler Specification Part 12: Implementation Acceptance Criteria

This part is the single owner of Event 030 completion criteria.
Prompts and the [acceptance matrix](../quality/acceptance_matrix.md) point here instead of restating it.
Criteria apply to the design once the parent or user accepts it, as the [README](../README.md#status-and-acceptance) describes.

## Completion definition

Event 030 is complete only when the baseline, every accepted evolution, the public world-end branch, the triggerable scenario, AI, assets, documentation, Event Details, event logs, catalog, cleanup, and validation evidence agree.
A parser-clean entry event is not completion.

## Legacy migration

- `chaosx.nr30.1` stays the hidden entry and keeps load-aware weighted host selection with the prefire reservation.
- The legacy timed `time_traveler` idea, the unconditional Germany and United States follow-ups, and their localisation are replaced.
- The Berlin-Moscow and Pacific warnings survive only as remembered war variants of the diplomatic forecast.
- The Event 016 contact bridge and Temporal Theory gate keep working after the idea is retired.
- The individual-crisis provider reads the custody marker and its documentation is updated.

## Baseline event

- Event 030 is registered as a Major event with Chaos level `1` and no cluster.
- The default enable allowlist includes Event 030 only after the rework is ready for normal selection.
- The host pool is existing majors plus player countries, weighted by the individual-crisis curve, with the shared special and nonhuman exclusions.
- One original traveler exists and cannot duplicate through custody transfer, annexation, tag switching, save, or multiplayer.
- The opening verifies two proof families.
- The host chooses one of four access arrangements.
- The initial spirit is restrained and far below the legacy idea's strength.
- The strongest rewards come from completed consultations.
- The baseline reaches every completion state in Part 1 without an evolution.
- Traveler loss converts direct access into the correct legacy stage.

## Public values

- Credibility, Exposure, and Timeline Divergence are the only persistent values shown before Evolution V.
- Each value shows its number, one of three band names, and a tooltip with the band effect and the last three causes.
- Hidden bloc, network, and incident scores never appear as numbers.
- Evolution V shows derived Temporal Stability in the same slot.
- Values live in centralized variables and constants and persist through save and reload.
- Custody transfer recalculates Credibility without resetting global history.
- Public Exposure creates a permanent floor.

## Consultations

- All six families exist, including the remembered war variants for Germany and the United States.
- Only one consultation mission is active at a time.
- Every consultation names a recipient, objective, deadline, and reward family before payment.
- Costs use at most four types, scale dynamically, and use correct texticons.
- Success, partial, and failed results use distinct effects, and the failure tooltip names its cause.
- No consultation grants unrestricted future technology.
- Rewards change play and avoid small standalone modifiers.
- AI can use every family.

## International intelligence race

- The interested-country registry is sparse and capped by the Exposure band.
- Four foreign operations and four host countermeasures exist with distinct risk and purpose.
- Network level stays hidden and gates each operation.
- Seizure requires the Public band, a mature network, and a valid route.
- A captured double never becomes a second traveler.
- Custody transfer moves the character, category, crisis slot, targets, and cooldowns.
- Former custodians keep only their legacy and recovery options.
- Every valid player can act as a rival without ghost copies.

## Evolution I

- The evolution uses MTTH and the `200+` gate.
- A confirmed traveler death closes only this track.
- Three hidden support blocs are reflected through events and decisions.
- Charter, emergency authority, appointment, open contest, arrest, transfer, and removal routes exist.
- A traveler government has strengths, limits, and preventive-governance choices.
- Preventive detention uses the existing camp intake when a camp network exists and creates no camp of its own.
- Coup, revolt, and civil war require escalating support and territory.
- Every leadership change updates character, politics, AI, ideas, decisions, and foreign response.

## Evolution II

- The evolution uses MTTH and the `400+` gate.
- The nine figure packages share one scaffold and are implemented or explicitly reported as blocked.
- Active figure caps are enforced.
- Every figure has arrival, authenticity, custody, movement, power, and resolution stages.
- Historical knowledge limits are real gameplay constraints.
- Named figures never enter silently as free advisers.
- Real-person ownership and portrait rules are satisfied.
- Restorations include territory, recognition, resistance, integration, AI, and cleanup.
- A resolved figure cannot reappear in ordinary play.

## Evolution III

- The evolution uses MTTH and the `600+` gate.
- Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin, and Xi Jinping receive neutral researched packages or are explicitly blocked with a source reason.
- The four fictional specialist categories arrive as single bounded roles without movements.
- Active visitor caps are enforced.
- Knowledge adaptation requires current-era institutions and equipment.
- Incompatible futures create evidence and policy choices.
- No real-person face is generated or substituted.

## Evolution IV

- The evolution uses MTTH and the `800+` gate.
- Global and country incident caps are enforced.
- All seven incident families have a complete lifecycle and exactly three actions each.
- Prehistoric fauna stays a state incident.
- New civilian cohorts are never debited from a nonexistent origin, and existing-population movement uses migration requests.
- Temporal disease runs through the biological outbreak owner.
- Unique units and characters return through a recorded event.
- Machine footholds contribute to world-end readiness only when durable.

## Evolution V

- The evolution uses MTTH and the `1000+` gate.
- Temporal Stability is derived from Timeline Divergence.
- Continuity, Horizon, Restoration, and Collaboration stances each show two signature actions.
- Node owners see Seal, Stabilize, and Exploit on a bounded node registry.
- Fracture incidents, including alternate-state delegations, are events with safeguards.
- Real-person echoes never clone a character token.
- The war can stay regional and never forces every country into one faction.
- Closed Future dominance creates readiness, not activation.

## Machine Extinction War

- The branch has its own public Event Details row and persistent toggle.
- Activation requires Chaos above `1000`, Evolution V, Collapsing stability below the tuned threshold, the machine footprint, hostile intent, the enabled toggle, and no active world end.
- Activation sets the shared world-end state, the scenario flag, the super-event, and the Closed Future world-threat source.
- The Closed Future has an original country, leader, doctrine, flag, AI, focus tree, and cleanup, and is marked special and nonhuman.
- Foundry Frames reuse the shared robot family through refinement technologies, and the drone family has provider, CXT, enum, and cleanup coverage.
- Human collaborator states remain human.
- All five phases exist and machine production depends on converted industry.
- Human response decisions have real costs, named targets, and AI equivalents.
- Deaths, migration, famine, condemnation, and unconventional warfare route through their owners.
- Machine and human victory use world state, and the aftermath requires a long multi-region war.

## Triggerable scenario

- All Times at Once uses the reserved `SCN-020`, raw ID `20`, rechecked against runtime and workbook occupancy before integration.
- The scenario list, details, confirmation, and four intensity stops are wired.
- Low, Medium, High, and Maximum create materially different setups.
- Maximum raises Chaos above the world-end threshold but never sets `world_end` during setup.
- Bypass flags are scoped and cleared, duplicate launch is blocked, and ordinary Event 030 behavior resumes.

## AI and probability

- Arrangement choice, consultations, countermeasures, foreign operations, claimant responses, incident policy, stances, collaboration, and machine strategy all have AI logic.
- Invalid countries, dead figures, closed portals, absent targets, and disabled branches receive zero or hidden availability.
- The ten `TT_AI` scenarios in Part 10 are evaluated, and weight patches are compared on the same scenarios.
- Candidate pools and external factors are declared so exact and unresolved findings stay distinct.

## Assets

- Every authorized visible asset has a manifest row and a runtime consumer.
- The original traveler has distinct leader and advisor portraits.
- Every real person has sourced provenance, crop, ownership, and review evidence.
- Icon families are not satisfied by resized unrelated assets.
- Every super-event has a final image and unique licensed audio.
- The drone model, sound, and reimport evidence are complete.
- No runtime reference points into temporary `docs/assets/` workspaces at completion.

## Text and documentation

- Player-facing text follows Part 11 and the event writing style, with no em dash, no semicolon, and no mechanic leak.
- Event Details describe premise and public state, not raw effects.
- Every evolution has log text across the correct surfaces, and the world-end row is branch-specific.
- `docs/events/030_time_traveler/` holds the canonical implementation overview.
- The authoritative XLSX is updated and the three CSVs are regenerated by the exporter.

## Tool evidence

Use the HOI4 MCP event, probability, focus, technology, and map routes where they answer a concrete question about the change, within the retry limits in `CLAUDE.md`.
An unavailable or inconclusive route is recorded as a limitation of the affected claim, followed by source review and calculations from the actual script.
It does not block otherwise finished implementation and is never reported as passed.

## Audits before completion

- decision and mission audit
- localisation audit
- country package audit for the Closed Future
- focus tree audit for the Closed Future tree
- AI probability audit
- event completion audit
- spreadsheet alignment pass
- documentation curation when plans or handoffs have accumulated

## Simplification reporting

The final report lists every missing or weaker substitute, including blocked portraits, absent figure packages, the drone model, pending audio, unimplemented intensities, generic country content, missing AI, limited tool evidence, and catalog mismatch.
No unapproved fallback is presented as completion.
