# Event 030 Time Traveler Specification Part 11: Assets, Presentation, Text Direction, and Achievements

## Presentation hierarchy

Event 030 uses the least complex surface that remains clear.

1. normal event and super-event presentation for major reveals
2. one main decision category with a category icon and static category picture
3. compact attached display for the three public values
4. selected-target foreign-operation category when the target list is active
5. map highlights for portal, anomaly, and machine states
6. no separate full mechanic window

The compact display should use visual stage states, threshold markers, icons, and concise tooltips. It should not present a telemetry ledger or a row of unexplained numbers.

## Category picture direction

The category picture should depict a secure consultation setting where the traveler, guarded records, and evidence of future knowledge are visible. The emotional center is the impossible person and the institutions surrounding them. Avoid a generic map table, glowing portal in an empty room, or a collage of famous people.

A later high-divergence variant can show damaged records, conflicting clocks, altered photographs, or a portal trace. Variants should be distinct state art, not a fake animated pulse made from one still.

Animation is optional. If accepted, it requires a real frame plan, separately authored frames, a frame sheet, static fallback, and verified consumer wiring. Static state variants are sufficient for the baseline design.

## Original traveler portrait package

The traveler is fictional and uses native ImageGen through the portrait worker.

Required authorized consumers:

- one country-leader portrait at `156x210` for the power route
- one advisor or dossier portrait at the inspected native size for the baseline advisor role

These are separate consumer-specific outputs. One should not be produced by casually resizing the other. The portrait should look period-authentic while retaining one subtle impossible detail that does not become fantasy costume. Avoid modern clothing, glowing eyes, holographic interfaces, and readable text.

The character may be male or female, but the selected portrait, name pool, pronouns, metadata, and character definition must remain coherent.

## Real-person portrait packages

The nine historical figures and three named future political figures require sourced real-person or authentic institutional material. The portrait worker must:

- search installed vanilla and the project for existing character ownership
- preserve one active character owner
- archive source, crop, provenance, hashes, and review evidence
- create the exact role-specific processed output
- never generate, reconstruct, beautify, or substitute the person's face
- mark the portrait blocked when a defensible source is unavailable

A figure who can become leader or commander should use one character identity with guarded role changes. The implementation should avoid duplicate character tokens for the same real person.

## Super-events

### Baseline major reveal

Role: first verified public or governmental confirmation that a person from the future exists.

Trigger: Event 030 opening after the host has accepted the first proof, or the corresponding high-chaos compressed opening.

Image direction: the traveler under guarded examination with a verified impossible object, document, or technical demonstration. The scene should feel like a government has realized the claim may be true.

Text direction: concise public context, uncertainty about the traveler's motives, and the strategic importance of knowledge. Do not list bonuses or future evolutions.

### War of the Possible Futures

Role: global escalation when future alignments and strategic portal nodes begin open conflict.

Trigger: Evolution V reaches the first genuine international temporal conflict, not merely its log activation.

Image direction: several eras and political forces contest one visible portal or strategic city, with no collage of portraits.

### Machine Extinction War

Role: public world-end activation.

Trigger: the terminal branch commits and sets the shared world-end state.

Image direction: a major industrial region being converted by original machine forces while human infrastructure and civilians establish scale.

### Machine defeat aftermath

Role: reflective global aftermath after a long, multi-region machine war.

Trigger: all master nodes destroyed, the durable machine actor defeated, and the global-crisis duration and damage thresholds met.

Image direction: captured silent machine works, reconstruction, and survivors. Avoid an uncomplicated victory parade.

Every super-event needs a unique slot, final image, sourced quote, licensed structured musical cue, base sound, settings-volume wrappers, audio ID, settings-aware helper call, documentation, and catalog alignment. No final quote, button line, or track is selected in this planning package.

## Player-facing text direction

### Baseline

The viewpoint is the host government, intelligence service, scientific team, military staff, or public after evidence becomes visible. The writing should show the proof and the institutional reaction. Avoid describing the event as a generic warning from the future.

### Consultations

Text should state the public recommendation, target, uncertainty class, and visible consequence of acting. It should not reveal hidden AI scores, future event IDs, or guaranteed outcomes.

### Historical figures

Writing should show the mismatch between a recognizable person and a modern state. Avoid comedy based on primitive misunderstanding. Let the figure display competence in their own political and military language while learning modern limits.

### Future visitors

Use neutral and factual direction for real political people. Avoid slogans, partisan mockery, praise, memes, or present-day campaign language. The fiction concerns their arrival and choices in the 1930s, not a judgement on current politics.

### Epoch Breakdown

Describe visible anomalies, fear, missing time, displaced people, unfamiliar animals, and damaged institutions. Do not label every incident a warning or announce the future world-end route.

### Machines

Use cold procedural intent, industrial action, and strategic consequence. Avoid borrowed film dialogue and familiar catchphrases.

### Options

Option tone should match the actor and stakes. Baseline government responses can use official caution, controlled disbelief, or dry bureaucratic irony. Assassination, displacement, civil war, and mass death use serious wording. Final option text requires separate research when it uses a cultural reference.

## Event Details and spreadsheet direction

Event Details should explain the premise, the struggle over future knowledge, and the escalating temporal consequences. It should not list raw bonuses, thresholds, hidden conditions, implementation state, or every possible figure.

Evolution details should describe the public situation of each stage. The world-end row explains the Closed Future's terminal aim and the human war for survival without exposing exact readiness values.

The authoritative XLSX must replace the legacy Event 30 shorthand. The three CSVs remain export-only.

## Achievements

Working names are structural labels. Final names and descriptions require localisation review and icon production.

### The Future Refused

Requirement: complete three successful consultations, keep Credibility above `60`, then end direct access through a lawful voluntary agreement before Timeline Divergence reaches `30`.

Purpose: rewards restraint after gaining real value.

### No One Owns Tomorrow

Requirement: keep the traveler alive for two years, keep Exposure below `40`, and prevent every foreign country from obtaining a verified Future Fragment.

Disqualifier: public recognition or a successful foreign theft.

### I Have Seen This Office Before

Requirement: make the original traveler national leader through appointment, election, council vote, or another nonviolent route.

Disqualifier: coup or civil war used to take power.

### The Wrong Century

Requirement: integrate one historical figure and one future visitor into the same country as lawful advisers, commanders, or public officials while both remain alive.

### Nine Lives of History

Requirement: resolve three historical claimant crises without executing the returned figures and without creating a full civil war.

### A Controlled Breach

Requirement: maintain one protected portal corridor for a year, complete two useful adaptation projects, and keep Temporal Stability above `50`.

### Close the Door Behind You

Requirement: after Evolution V, seal every active strategic node and raise Temporal Stability above `80`.

Disqualifier: collaboration with the Closed Future.

### A Future Without Masters

Requirement: defeat the Machine Extinction War, destroy every master machine core, and refuse machine collaboration throughout the campaign.

### The Last Archive

Requirement: preserve the original traveler records, one historical archive, one future visitor account, and one captured machine core through the machine war, then choose whether to destroy or retain the collection.

### All Times Survived

Requirement: launch the Maximum All Times at Once scenario and survive three years without a machine victory, while at least one historical and one future visitor remain resolved peacefully.

## Achievement implementation rules

- Use real achievement definitions, tracking, disqualifiers, localisation, and unique icon triplets.
- Do not unlock achievements merely because the event fired.
- Scenario achievements must identify that the manual scenario was used.
- Hard achievements need visible progress or staged tracker rows when the project pattern supports them.
- Achievement routes must survive save and reload.
