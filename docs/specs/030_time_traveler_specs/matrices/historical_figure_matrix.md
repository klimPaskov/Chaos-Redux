| Figure | Primary host | Movement base | Strongest gameplay | Modernity limit | Main power route | Distinct failure risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Napoleon Bonaparte | France and valid French successors | officers, veterans, centralizers, legal reformers | operational concentration, state reform, merit promotion | industrial attrition and modern mass war | constitutional Bonapartism or imperial claim | continental overreach and personal rule |
| Julius Caesar | Italy or a state controlling Rome | officers, classical nationalists, populist networks | coalition, initiative, personal loyalty | modern logistics and mass institutions | emergency dictatorship or Roman restoration | republican collapse and assassination crisis |
| Alexander the Great | Greece and Macedonia | expansionist officers, Hellenic movements | battlefield leadership and transregional ambition | succession and modern supply | Macedonian imperial claim | empire fragments after victory |
| Genghis Khan | Mongolia and connected Inner Asian hosts | steppe communities, cavalry traditions, regional leaders | mobile command, relay networks, adaptive administration | fixed industrial borders and modern firepower | confederation or Great Khan claim | violent expansion and successor fragmentation |

## Standard content per figure

- one arrival report
- one authenticity chain
- one initial custody choice
- one movement package
- one adviser or commander route where suitable
- one serious claimant route where suitable
- one suppression and one negotiated route
- one defeat, exile, death, or retirement resolution
- one AI profile
- one source and portrait package
- one cleanup and no-repeat memory
