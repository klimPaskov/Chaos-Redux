# AI Strategy Matrix

| Actor and situation | Preferred action | Secondary action | Refusal condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| stable democratic host at peace | controlled government or scientific access, lawful charter | public contest if traveler seeks power | covert killing without proven threat |
| authoritarian host at war | military access, secrecy, guard detail | emergency authority | broad public access when security is weak |
| low-stability host | compartmentalize, investigate sectors, negotiate | arrest with loyal forces | full access without countermeasures |
| friendly major ally | request selected sharing, offer protection | observe consultations | abduction while alliance is healthy |
| hostile intelligence major | observe, recruit, steal, forge | abduction if mature | repeated low-chance attack |
| weak historical host | integrate symbolic or adviser role | foreign support or exile | full civil war without territory and force |
| strong historical claimant | political contest or negotiated office | regional revolt | instant leader replacement with no support |
| future scientist in industrial state | controlled study and adaptation | share with ally | unrestricted technology grant |
| future political visitor in stable state | advisory or legal movement route | public debate | immediate coup |
| severe fauna incident | contain and evacuate | hunt | research while capital is threatened |
| future equipment site | study if capable, destroy if weak or threatened | share with ally | unsupported deployment with no maintenance |
| Evolution V research power | Continuity or Horizon | controlled exploitation | machine collaboration without survival pressure |
| restored historical state | Restoration Concord | negotiated neutrality | Continuity closure that would erase its own route |
| machine-threatened state | emergency compact, defend nodes, evacuate | sabotage and technology sharing | isolated offensive with no supply |
| desperate collaboration candidate | bargain only with direct machine threat and low survival chance | seek human aid first | high stability, strong allies, or no contact |
| Closed Future | contiguous industry, nodes, capitals, research, strategic weapons | divide coalitions and protect cores | unsupported remote dispersion |

## Probability evidence

Every weighted surface starts with `hoi4.probability_inspect`. The baseline and post-patch passes use the same `TT_AI_01` through `TT_AI_10` scenarios. The audit reports whether each result is exact, bounded, sampled, score-only, or unresolved.
