# Possible Future Alignment Matrix

[Part 8](../specs/030_time_traveler_spec_part_8_evolution_v_possible_futures.md) owns the alignment, stance, and node rules.

| Alignment | Core belief | Preferred actions | Territorial form | Main failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Continuity Directorate | preserve the current altered timeline and stop further branching | verify, seal, reconcile, share closure methods | network of member countries | overcontrol and loss of useful future knowledge |
| Horizon Compact | guide the world toward a better later order through controlled intervention | adopt future programs, protect visitors, keep corridors | current states with future delegates | incompatible programs and dependence on visitors |
| Restoration Concord | older legitimate orders deserve survival and recognition | recognize claimants, restore states, recruit legacy forces | restored states and supporting governments | rival claims and succession conflict |
| The Closed Future | one causal sequence must eliminate rival futures | seize industry, erase archives, close portals behind its forces | durable nonhuman machine country | human resistance and loss of master nodes |

Alternate-state delegations are a fracture incident, not an alignment.

## Country stances

| Stance | Normal eligibility | Hard blocker | Signature actions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Continuity | civilian country touched by the crisis | Closed Future country or machine puppet | Coordinate a synchronized closure, Share closure methods |
| Horizon | verified future visitor or allied Horizon member | visitor hostility | Select a future program, Protect future delegates |
| Restoration | active connected claimant or restored state | no connected claimant | Recognize a claimant, Convene a restoration congress |
| Collaboration | machine foothold, severe threat, permissive government | strong Continuity commitment, no machine contact, or player-disabled route | Accept machine coordination, Transfer an industrial node |
