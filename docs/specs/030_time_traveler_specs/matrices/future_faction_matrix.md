# Possible Future Alignment Matrix

| Alignment | Core belief | Preferred actions | Territorial form | Main failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Continuity Directorate | preserve the current altered timeline and stop further branching | verify, contain, close, reconcile, share closure technology | network first, country only through members | overcontrol and loss of useful future knowledge |
| Horizon Compact | guide the world toward a better later order through controlled intervention | adapt institutions, protect visitors, keep controlled corridors | coalition of current states and future delegates | incompatible programs and dependence on visitors |
| Restoration Concord | older legitimate orders deserve survival and recognition | recognize claimants, restore states, recruit legacy forces | restored states and supporting governments | rival claims and succession conflict |
| Alternate State delegations | each unrealized polity seeks its own survival | recognition, asylum, territorial claim, intelligence | regional and package-specific | contradictory borders and weak legitimacy |
| The Closed Future | one causal sequence must eliminate rival futures | seize industry, erase archives, close portals behind invasion, remove rivals | durable nonhuman machine country | human resistance and loss of master nodes |
