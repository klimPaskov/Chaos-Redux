| Working label | Core requirement | Main disqualifier | Design skill tested |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Future Refused | three successful consultations at Trusted Credibility or higher, then a lawful voluntary end of access while the timeline is Recognizable | coercive removal or an Altered timeline | restraint after advantage |
| No One Owns Tomorrow | traveler alive for two years, Exposure never Public, no foreign Future Fragment | Public Recognition or a successful theft | secrecy and counterintelligence |
| I Have Seen This Office Before | traveler becomes leader peacefully | coup or civil war | political route management |
| The Wrong Century | one past and one future visitor lawfully integrated in one country | either killed, imprisoned, or in a claimant war | mixed visitor diplomacy |
| Nine Lives of History | three historical claimant crises resolved without execution or full civil war | any qualifying execution or civil war | nonviolent claimant control |
| A Controlled Breach | one stabilized corridor held for a year, two future programs, Stability never Collapsing | uncontrolled portal spread | risk-managed intervention |
| Close the Door Behind You | all strategic nodes sealed and Stability Coherent | machine collaboration | global closure coordination |
| A Future Without Masters | defeat the machine war and destroy every master core | any machine collaboration | terminal military victory |
| All Times Survived | Maximum scenario, survive three years, peaceful past and future resolution | machine victory | scenario mastery |
