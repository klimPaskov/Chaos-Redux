# Purity Doctrine and The Final Crusade

## One evolution, separate from ordinary development

Purity Doctrine is the event's single planned evolution. Founding a faction, creating a client, changing leaders, consolidating a region, and surviving a defeat are baseline developments. They must not occupy additional evolution slots or appear as invented tier evolutions.

Purity Doctrine first becomes eligible in Rising Chaos, from Chaos 400 upward, while the event has a living active authority capable of debating and enforcing a charter. This occupies one evolution tier. Ordinary expansion can occur below 400 and does not require the evolution. No evolution is added at every remaining tier simply to fill the five-stage allowance.

Eligibility also requires a plausible internal doctrinal faction, a supported additional target definition, and an active political situation in which expansion would change play. Reaching 400 alone does not create new demographic data, duplicate a group already targeted, or force a government to adopt the doctrine. The shared evolution flow uses its 90-day timing anchor with dynamic pressure from command concentration, conflict, existing targets, opposition, and internal resistance.

## What changes

The movement broadens the definition of enemies to one or more additional religious, racial, or ethnic groups. The expansion can threaten populations inside its own member countries and communities protected by governments that previously accepted the compact. The political crisis is therefore both external and internal.

The evolution creates a proposed charter revision with the exact old and new target definitions. Additional targets come from supported authored group identities. A new definition cannot be a disguised repetition of the old one, and a person belonging to multiple groups enters the effective union once.

The authored launch set has two concrete expansion directions. A racialized expansion adds supported Romani cohorts. A religious-authority schism in the Romanian profile can add supported Greek Catholic cohorts after an actual conflict over protection or religious authority. These are fictional decisions of the crusading government. The historical sources establish relevant communities and political contexts, not this invented expanded charter or an inevitable alliance. Source R11 informs the Romani context and R6 the religious conflict. The Slovak profile can use the Romani expansion only with supported current cohort data and a real authored doctrinal challenger. It does not inherit the Romanian religious dispute automatically.

The player response differs by direction. A racialized expansion can threaten communities across otherwise compatible members. The religious schism can divide Christian institutions and make Malta or a compatible Holy Realm route a protector or rival. The full registry remains extensible to other religious, racial or ethnic definitions, but no additional launch profile enters the pool without the same authored political and population evidence.

The immediate changes are a doctrine dispute, renewed member consent, new protection and refusal opportunities, and access to appropriate shared target-policy updates. An active camp is not automatically filled with an invented new population. Actual custody, deportation, forced labor, killing, flight and resistance require the shared owner to process valid living groups under current authority.

### Active-event application

The movement's current leadership receives the proposal. Existing members see how it affects their charter, protected communities, allies, and domestic population. The founder can endorse the expansion, negotiate a narrower compact when its route permits, or refuse and confront a doctrinal challenger. Members can assent, refuse a new campaign while staying under the old agreement if accepted, leave as independent adherents, or repudiate the movement.

A crusader client that endorses the broader doctrine can continue using it after the founder falls. A client that refuses does not have its population secretly retargeted by an inherited flag. It can become a rival compact, an opponent, or a government needing protection from the broadened movement.

Existing player controls and AI routes update in place. No second event firing, duplicate founding package, reset focus tree, free army, or repeated first-manifestation Chaos is permitted. The evolution history identifies the actual actor and charter episode.

### Pre-fire evolved opening

If the event first manifests when Purity Doctrine is already eligible and the shared evolution system authorizes an evolved opening, the founding seizure still begins with one governing country and one initial charter group. The opening immediately enters the doctrine crisis with a pending broader charter and organized internal opponents. It does not spawn several fully armed governments or begin with an unexplained completed global alliance.

The first target group remains identifiable, satisfying the baseline premise. The additional targets are adopted through the same consent and current population validation used by the active-event path. The opening can have a shorter political preparation interval when the current government already has the institutions and conflict that justify it, with at least a meaningful warning and response stage for affected players.

## Disabled, invalid, and rejected doctrine paths

If the evolution is disabled, its proposal and target expansion do not activate. The baseline charter, ordinary faction play, clients, wars and opposition continue. Focuses that would otherwise lead into the doctrine crisis converge into charter maintenance, diplomacy, or ordinary sustainment. No route is stranded at a disabled evolution.

If no valid new group definition exists, the evolution remains ineligible. It does not choose a random proxy or repeat the old target for an evolution log entry. If a proposed charter is rejected by all authorities capable of enacting it, the political episode closes without adopting new targets. Rejection can still produce a real schism if an existing capable government commits to the expanded doctrine through its own action.

Turning the evolution setting off after activation prevents new automatic evolution work according to the shared settings policy. It cannot resurrect victims, erase existing wars, remove historical evidence, or silently undo a government's enacted doctrine. A current government needs an actual repeal or repudiation to stop its orders. The implementation must reconcile this rule with the owner's observed settings semantics and document any required owner-side adjustment.

Evolution eligibility, activation, logging, or branch access gives exactly zero direct Chaos. Actual later political schism, regional spread, collapse of protection, or other distinct material consequences can use the event's Chaos map. Shared war and civilian-death contributions are not repeated.

## The Final Crusade is an independent terminal branch

The Final Crusade is the terminal commitment of a surviving coordinated crusader bloc to a global campaign against every state and population excluded from its religious, racial, or ethnic order. It is not an automatic second name for the Holy Realm's ending, Malta's Holy World, or Malta's separately specified hidden Operation The Final Crusade.

The branch has its own stable world-end identity, owner mapping, persistent public toggle, terminal state, Event Details row, history, super-event and audio identity. IDs remain unassigned until the catalog migration and shared reservation audit are complete. Existing world-end IDs are appended around, not renumbered.

Purity Doctrine can make the order broader and more unstable, but The Final Crusade does not require that particular evolution. A bloc still enforcing its original exclusionary charter can attempt the terminal transition if it satisfies every current political, territorial, operational, and global condition. Disabling Purity Doctrine must not implicitly disable a separately enabled ending.

## Global gates

Every condition must be true in the same final validation:

1. Chaos is strictly greater than 1000. Exactly 1000 is insufficient.
2. The global Disable World End setting is off.
3. The Final Crusade's own branch is enabled.
4. No other world-end scenario has committed or holds the shared terminal reservation.
5. A living coordinated crusader bloc still satisfies the current-position conditions below.
6. The proposed terminal command has the assent of the governments whose capacity is being counted.

No event reward pushes Chaos over 1000 merely to manufacture this eligibility. No prior checkpoint, old conquest total, earlier alliance, or accumulated death count substitutes for these conditions.

The inspected shared Chaos owner can accept a genuine increase from 995 to 1005, but an increase from 999 to exactly 1000 leaves ordinary subsequent changes blocked by that owner's existing cutoff. At exactly 1000, this ending remains ineligible even when every other requirement is met. The design does not guarantee a terminal outcome in every campaign. Heroic Crusade must not add a hidden point, borrow another event's exceptional update reason, reinterpret the threshold as inclusive, or change the global meter's policy without an explicit shared-owner design change. Validation must cover both genuine overshoot and the exact-threshold plateau. Part 08 also requires receipts to distinguish a requested Chaos change from one the owner actually applies.

## Current-position conditions

The numbers below are proposed balance anchors to make the requirement reviewable. They require map, military and probability validation before release. They are not existing runtime constants.

| Dimension | Proposed qualifying condition | What invalidates it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Political breadth | At least three real governing authorities in the coordinated compact, including at least two distinct from the current leader | Empty tags, dormant claimants, duplicate governments over the same capacity, repudiation, unrecognized command, or departure |
| Geographic breadth | Durable qualified holdings in at least three non-overlapping world regions | Lost territory, a broken operational route, removal of a region's sustaining government, or a merely symbolic enclave |
| Territorial depth | Each region has an authored coherent state group with political administration, a supply center or viable port, and a meaningful military-industrial base | A capital flag without functioning territory, transient control, no supply, or a isolated token province |
| Regional importance | Each qualifying base controls either at least 20 percent of that region's current usable military industry or an authored strategic state group large enough to sustain an independent campaign | Tiny client multiplication and region-boundary exploits. The strategic exception needs an explicit map-reviewed profile and equal military capability. |
| Duration | All required governments, agreements and regional bases remain valid for 120 consecutive days | A material loss or broken compact resets the affected proof. Reconstitution needs a new common command period. |
| Persecuting authority | Shared Repression and Camps verifies enforceable current targeting policy, institutions, capacity and genuine operations in the qualifying controlled territories | Repudiation, liberation, owner-confirmed cessation, loss of authority, invalid group evidence, or a decorative event marker |
| Military capability | Existing usable forces, equipment, fuel, supply and replacements can sustain credible opening operations against the remaining major opposition | Paper divisions, zero equipment, trapped forces, double-assigned formations, absent replacement capacity, or supply collapse |
| Reach | A verified theater plan exists for every remaining major opposition grouping, with real staging areas and supported land or maritime approaches | An unreachable major opponent, closed transit, missing naval capacity, or reuse of the same sole force in incompatible simultaneous theaters |
| Coordination | Bloc cohesion is at least 75 and the terminal commitments are current | Cohesion loss, refused charter, unsettled succession, or a member withdrawing its counted contribution |

The six map regions are Europe, Africa, Asia, North America, South America, and Oceania. This is a planning partition, not an assertion about engine continent IDs. A map-reviewed state manifest must assign each eligible state once and document border and island cases. The strategic state-group exception supports real archipelagic or low-industry theaters. It is not an unbounded override for any three ports.

Current usable industry excludes industry the bloc cannot operate because it lacks control or supply. A subject and its overlord cannot both claim the same factories or units. Local political control is established by governing authority and actual enforcement, not ownership alone. Occupied territory can qualify if the bloc truly governs and sustains it, but recent conquest must complete the same tenure.

The military planning anchor is a bloc field force with roughly three quarters of the remaining major opposition's usable field capability, plus credible local opening strength in each planned theater and a reserve the common command has not assigned twice. This deliberately permits a dangerous contested war. It does not require certain victory. Compare force quality, equipment, logistics, air or naval needs, and actual theater access rather than raw division count. If the engine cannot provide a sound comparison, use a documented conservative capability model and classify its uncertainty. Do not silently substitute historical conquest counts.

A native division-count strength ratio is not this capability measure. A naval-control predicate that includes friendly outsiders is also insufficient to prove that the assenting bloc owns a sustainable route. Verify each native input and count only the actual signatories' committed assets and usable agreements. Equipment still in production, unsupported resource rights and forces already assigned to another theater cannot supply current readiness.

## Complete political opposition and theater rules

The political opposition registry includes every current living human government outside the order's accepted governing charter, including neutral states, nonmember protectors, repudiating governments and independent adherents that refuse the final common order. A limited partner is not exempt. Subjects remain distinct governments for political decisions, while military and industrial capacity is assigned to its actual owner once. Supported governments in exile contribute only their real surviving forces and sponsor relationships.

Build opposition groupings from current native factions, actual war coalitions and explicit defense compacts, joining overlapping memberships into one operational grouping without duplicating states. A major grouping contains a current native major country or at least 10 percent of the excluded world's usable military industry or usable field capability. Always include the three strongest remaining operational groupings, or all if fewer than three exist, so small surviving opponents cannot vanish from the reach test. Every excluded non-major state stays in the political target registry and is assigned to an appropriate later theater.

An authoritative current-country registry and its lifecycle hooks establish completeness. A missing country, malformed row or unknown registry version blocks certification. If a fully certified pre-terminal registry contains no capable state opposition, resolve ordinary political dominance through the normal event. Do not use an empty pool to trigger a final war. After the terminal transition, the same genuinely empty opposing-government state can support a dominance outcome.

Usable military industry means currently controlled, operable military factories in the verified state manifest, excluding inaccessible or nonfunctional capacity. Usable force means real formations with current equipment, manpower, organization, supply and necessary fuel. The implementation capability model must expose its native inputs and conservative handling of unavailable information. Intelligence uncertainty cannot be replaced by fictional precise enemy values in the player UI.

Each major grouping needs at least one credible opening theater. A land approach requires a valid front or usable access through governments whose current agreement actually permits the operation. A maritime approach requires a staging port, transport, supported invasion capability, cover and sustainment appropriate to that route. An unmodeled air assault cannot stand in for missing maritime capability.

The common command assigns distinct existing formations and finite stockpile or production commitments to concurrently planned theaters. A formation can serve a later theater only after the earlier assignment is explicitly released. Later sequential operations cannot certify an impossible first contact with an otherwise unreachable major opponent. Limited partners' resources do not count unless they become assenting members and commit those exact resources to the terminal compact.

The three proposed launch client regions are not automatic strategic exceptions for terminal geography. Any low-industry regional exception must name a concrete state group and prove a comparable sustained theater role through the map and military audit. Without such a profile, the normal industry and territorial-depth rule applies. This prevents an empty exception table from becoming an unrestricted bypass.

## Persecution evidence is not a body-count quota

The terminal test recognizes the existence and operation of the persecuting political order. It never requires a minimum number of prisoners or deaths, and killing more people does not fill a readiness bar. The order needs institutions capable of enforcing its doctrine under current control, with owner-verified contemporary activity and valid target coverage.

An empty or depopulated region does not become stronger evidence because the victims were killed or displaced. Loss of the living population, industry, governance, or sustainment needed by the qualifying region can weaken it. A state that truly ends persecution removes that evidence even if its government retains a religious symbol or nominal membership.

If current aggregate civilian changes make the target registry ambiguous, the relevant proof is invalid until the shared owner reconciles it. The event cannot certify exact ongoing targeted persecution from an old cohort snapshot.

## Warning and counterplay before commitment

At Total Chaos, from 800 upward, a bloc approaching the political and geographic conditions begins visible strategic preparation. The player sees known preparations and current missing conditions appropriate to their viewpoint. Opponents receive a report when a publicly observable capability milestone makes a global campaign credible. Secret target intentions remain uncertain until exposed.

The readiness explanation names present weaknesses: a region that has not held long enough, an independent client outside the compact, a missing maritime route, a broken supply promise, an unsettled charter, or an unconfronted major opponent. There is no permanent historical progress score to grind.

Opponents can prevent the transition by liberating a qualifying region, isolating a client, securing the departure or repudiation of a counted government, breaking a real route, defeating committed forces, or ending a local persecution apparatus under an actual new authority. A dip in one numerical score is not the only counterplay.

A final commitment proposal gives affected governments a current opportunity to assent or refuse. Use a 30-day political commitment window after all durable capability conditions are met. It is an emergency-scale political decision rather than a military buildup mission. Readiness must remain true throughout. A refusal removes that government's capacity and recalculates eligibility. The order cannot claim its army first and discover the refusal after declaring war.

For a concrete warning predicate, public strategic preparation begins when Chaos is at least 800, three real compact governments exist, and two regional bases meet the local control and sustainment tests. The report describes these observable institutions and deployments, with concealed intentions identified as uncertain. A sudden Chaos jump that skips this threshold emits the warning immediately. A terminal commitment cannot conclude earlier than 90 days after that public warning, even when the bloc had previously accumulated enough tenure.

Readiness explanations are refreshed by relevant control, government, compact, route, force and owner-receipt changes and by bounded due reviews. They carry the time of the latest completed review. Stale or incomplete calculation shows unresolved status. At final commitment, every input is freshly validated. A new major theater or a lost required condition invalidates the common readiness proof and cancels the commitment window. Unaffected local historical records remain, but the complete current proof must earn its continuous 120-day validity again.

## Atomic terminal transition

At commitment, the shared terminal owner performs a fresh serial validation and claims the unique terminal state before applying global consequences. Another ending committing first makes this request fail cleanly without partial wars, target expansion, super-event playback, or Chaos.

Once committed, record the branch, commanding governments, actual charter, date, and current theaters. Latch the end of ordinary automatic event firing permanently, including normal events already queued but not yet dispatched. Show the unique terminal super-event and world-end history once. Then issue or stage the actual campaign orders through the existing war and diplomacy owners.

This permanence requires new shared-owner integration. The inspected scheduler already respects `world_end`, but queued cluster work lacks an equivalent general gate and settings can clear that flag. A terminal receipt must therefore outlive that mutable flag. Every ordinary dispatch checks the receipt, and pending batches settle their unexecuted members, history and reservations before their queue storage is cleared. Cancelled members are recorded as cancelled or skipped under the declared shared batch contract, never as fired. Normal settings and save initialization cannot restart ordinary production after commitment. The settings explanation must make this permanent consequence clear while keeping ongoing war and civilian work available.

The terminal charter commits the order against all states and populations excluded by its doctrine. Country-level opposition and population-level exclusion remain separate. The event maintains a bounded registry of current opposing governments and applicable known living cohorts. It does not invent demographic identity for every person in the world or recursively declare wars through an unbounded daily scan.

Newly appearing states and changed governments are processed through lifecycle hooks. The campaign can stage operations by theater according to real routes and force availability. Its global commitment is immediate even when physical wars cannot begin everywhere on the same day. The world-end presentation must describe that global campaign without promising instant universal military contact.

## Play after the transition

Ordinary automatic event firing stays stopped. Existing military simulation, camp and civilian maintenance, Deaths, Migration, Resistance, Condemnation, relief actions, liberation, country succession, manual in-crisis decisions, and this ending's own scheduled work continue under their established owners.

Opponents can still defeat the movement. They can shelter threatened people, defeat its armies, liberate territory, break client governments, or support real repudiation. A founding-state capitulation alone remains insufficient. Every surviving government that still sustains the order must be dealt with according to its actual territory, forces, sponsorship, doctrine and authority.

A government outside the order can seek admission only through a genuine political commitment and the order's current inclusion rules. Joining a military faction does not recategorize targeted civilians or erase responsibility. A nominal surrender without ending persecution or command remains part of the final struggle.

## Terminal outcomes

**Defeat of the order** occurs when every sustaining crusader government has been defeated, repudiated, or made incapable of continued governing and military action. The terminal history records that result. Shared population losses, displacement, custody evidence and ruined states remain. Ordinary automatic firing does not restart.

**Dominance of the order** occurs when all remaining major opposition governments have been defeated or have genuinely entered its governing order, all meaningful organized opposition has lost the capacity to sustain a rival international order, and the bloc still governs the surviving world it claims. Scattered local resistance can continue through shared systems. Dominance is a political and military outcome, not a requirement to kill every excluded person.

**Prolonged final war** is a valid continuing state. Neither side receives an arbitrary victory when a timer expires. A fragmented crusade can continue in separate theaters, with its remaining governments still responsible for the campaign they sustain. The final conflict and its aftermath remain playable after the automatic event sequence has ended.

## Universal charter at the terminal transition

Political opponents use the complete government registry defined above. Population inclusion is a separate rule. Joining a military faction or signing the final compact cannot automatically change the identity or safety of that government's residents.

The terminal charter contains an explicit positive list of recognized religious identities and the complete enacted exclusion union. The Romanian launch profile recognizes the supported Eastern Orthodox identity family by default. The Slovak launch profile recognizes the supported Roman Catholic identity family by default. These are fictional rules asserted by the crusading institutions, not statements that the actual religions endorse the movement. The implementation manifest must expand each family to the exact supported canonical identities and descendants. It may not equate Eastern Orthodox, Oriental Orthodox, Roman Catholic and Greek Catholic categories without an explicit authored recognition rule.

The negotiated-order route may recognize an additional supported religious identity through an actual agreement, disclosed during preparation and affirmed by every government whose final assent is counted. Recognition cannot remove an enacted ethnic, racial or religious exclusion. It cannot be an invisible AI exception added to make readiness pass. Concentrated command retains the founder profile's positive set unless an explicit doctrinal change is enacted. A successor inherits the current charter revision, rather than substituting its own national religion. Changing the proposed universal charter invalidates the assent window and requires renewed current proof under that revision.

| Classification | Exact rule |
| --- | --- |
| Excluded by explicit veto | The living segment has any known identity in the enacted initial or Purity exclusion union, even if its religious identity is recognized. |
| Excluded by the universal religious rule | No known explicit veto is needed, but its relevant religious identity is known and falls outside the complete recognized list. |
| Included | Its supported identity satisfies the positive rule and the relevant exclusion dimensions are known sufficiently to determine that no enacted veto applies. |
| Unclassified | Required identity dimensions are missing or cannot be reconciled. Failure to prove inclusion does not prove exclusion. |

An overlapping person appears once in the effective excluded union. Classification is scoped to the charter revision and a living segment's current supported identity record. The system does not infer ancestry, assign beliefs from residence, or manufacture a conversion. Later changes require a supported shared identity transaction and preserve prior responsibility and receipts.

The warning presents the proposed recognized set and explicit exclusions in ordinary language, including the newly threatened populations that the universal rule would add. It begins early enough to satisfy the existing 90-day public warning requirement. Every committing government affirms this same revision during the final 30-day window. A member that refuses remains a real actor but cannot contribute to the committing bloc's common proof, forces or routes. Recalculate the complete political opposition registry accordingly.

Terminal readiness requires a complete supported charter schema and valid current identity coverage for all represented living segments in every counted regional base. Unknown or unreconciled segments in a counted base block that base's qualification. Existing regional and global durability rules then apply. This requirement prevents a government from claiming an operational universal persecution system through invented demographic evidence.

Missing population data elsewhere remains explicitly unclassified. It cannot authorize a numerical civilian action or produce a fabricated global census. The complete political registry still makes the final campaign global, while newly controlled territory must pass the same shared identity and authority checks before a policy can affect known excluded populations. The commitment itself makes no automatic civilian population change. No completion condition requires exterminating all excluded people.
