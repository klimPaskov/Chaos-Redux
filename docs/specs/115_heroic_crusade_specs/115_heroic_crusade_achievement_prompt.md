## 1. Protected destination

| Field | Design |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed ID | `chaosx_hc_115_protected_destination` |
| Title direction | A place where threatened people can remain safely |
| Description direction | Complete sustained reception from several threatened origins while meeting real protection obligations. |
| Eligible countries | A living receiving government outside the persecuting order, or a government that completed a genuine repudiation before the credited rescue operations. |
