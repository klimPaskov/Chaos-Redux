## Design at a glance

A specific militant institution captures a suitable existing government, establishes a crusading order and adopts one supported target identity. The primary authored origin is a Romanian Legionary seizure. An existing compatible Slovak government is the conditional alternative. These are explicit alternate-history divergences. Government compatibility depends on current institutions, targets, choices and capacity. A shared religion is not automatic assent.

The movement can create real clients in conquered regions, with authored frontier, maritime and interior contexts. Those governments use actual territory, industry, people and funded forces. They can obey, become independent adherents or break with the order. The founding state's defeat cannot delete surviving governments or settle their responsibilities.

Opponents can offer protection, sustain real destinations, defend routes, support resistance, liberate regions and complete civilian settlement. The existing Repression and Camps, Deaths, Migration, native/shared Resistance and Condemnation owners remain authoritative. The complete selected-source read confirms that aggregate cohorts do not establish a protected-group selector or disjoint identity partitions. The plan therefore requires a shared identity extension and the missing adapters. Unknown data cannot be replaced by assumptions about a country's religion or majority.

Purity Doctrine broadens the enacted target union, including domestic populations, and reopens political consent. The Final Crusade has a separate universal charter and terminal branch. Its current governments must hold and sustain real bases in several world regions, maintain the actual persecuting system, possess sufficient forces and routes, and withstand a public warning and final consent period. Strict Chaos above 1000 and the shared world-end gates are necessary. The final war remains playable after ordinary automatic event firing permanently stops.
