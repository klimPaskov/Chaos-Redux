# Authored origins and deterministic country setup

## What is selected and what remains unverified

This supplement resolves the earlier country audit's concern that a list of archetypes alone does not define a playable origin. It selects a primary historical institutional context, one conditional alternative, and three concrete regional client contexts. The international crusading order and the new administrative governments are fictional alternate history. Exact installed state IDs, country tags, available characters, focus attachments, technologies and native country statistics remain source-verification gates. They are not invented in this plan.

The historical evidence and links are recorded in `sources_and_research.md`. History establishes why a political institution is a plausible authored origin. It does not establish that the actual government in a particular save is suitable, that all residents share its beliefs, or that historical national demographic totals can be copied into current map states.

## Founder profile A: Romanian Legionary seizure

Romania is the primary authored candidate. The historical anchor is the Iron Guard's antisemitic political movement and its contested participation in government in 1940 to 1941. The source record supports a struggle over real institutions. A victory by that movement followed by an autonomous international crusading order is the explicit divergence, not a claim about an actual historical international order. Sources R5 and R6.

| Field | Selected design |
| --- | --- |
| Origin | Existing Romania, with an authored early seizure or victory in an existing internal command confrontation |
| Authority | A Romanian Legionary directing institution. A real leader is used only when alive and appropriate to the save's political history. Otherwise use institutional representation, without resurrecting a person. |
| Initial target | Jewish people under the movement's antisemitic classification, represented only through the shared supported identity dataset |
| Religious and political direction | A specific fascist institution claiming religious authority. It does not represent the whole Romanian Orthodox Church or all Romanian Christians. |
| Native political route | Use the inspected existing fascist political family and party or institution mapping. Do not add a new global religion ideology. Preserve legitimate alternative institutions for the rupture route. |
| Initial enemy relationship | A reachable government that actually protects threatened people, rejects a current ultimatum or is already in a relevant war. A Jewish population does not imply a sovereign Jewish enemy country. |
| Political opposition | Existing state and military institutions, civic opposition and documented religious dissent can contest the movement. Rivalry with the movement is not automatic civilian protection. |
| Capital | Current valid Romanian capital. Preferred authored historical center is Bucharest only when actually controlled and viable. Fallback is the highest-priority controlled Romanian political and supply center in the verified country manifest. |
| Economy and army | Inherit the current Romanian economy, troops, stockpiles, research, laws, infrastructure and wars. Add no replacement founding army or free industrial package. |
| Focus surface | Preserve the living Romanian tree. Attach the bounded founder branch only through the verified additive method, with the same core political choices available in events and decisions. |
| Geography | Current land frontiers and any usable Black Sea access. A port does not confer a fleet or passage through controlled straits. |
| Invalid states | No surviving Romanian authority, incompatible exclusive event ownership, no viable capital, no supported target dataset, no authored institutional opportunity, or no reachable international political relationship |

For character production, the historical research distinguishes Corneliu Zelea Codreanu's earlier lifetime from Horia Sima's leadership in the 1940 context. The game must check its actual character and survival history. Their names are historical source anchors, not permission to overwrite an unrelated current leader or fabricate a survival event. A council's portrait uses sourced institutional material. Source R6.

## Founder profile B: conditional Slovak party-state

The alternative is an existing Slovak government in a compatible Hlinka Party context. Jozef Tiso's historical government provides a documented clerical political setting and anti-Jewish persecution, alongside real church protest and resistance. An autonomous crusading compact is the fictional divergence. This profile cannot automatically partition an intact Czechoslovakia. Source R7.

| Field | Selected design |
| --- | --- |
| Origin | A living Slovakia with sufficient current political authority and a valid institutional opportunity |
| Authority | Current verified Hlinka Party government or an authored institutional successor. Tiso appears only when alive and appropriate to the actual save. |
| Initial target | The same supported Jewish target identity, with this government's classification and source provenance kept distinct from the Romanian charter |
| Native politics | Existing inspected native ideology and party mapping for this government. No free independence from an overlord or faction. |
| Opposition | Eligible existing alternative institutions, documented clerical dissent and resistance, with current capacity deciding whether they can act |
| Capital and fallback | Bratislava when currently controlled and viable. Otherwise the ordered controlled political and supply centers in its verified origin manifest. |
| Economy, army and research | Inherit the living country's actual state. Dependence and shortages remain. No Romanian or sponsor army is copied into it. |
| International role | Land-based access and a real frontier unless the alternate-history map has given it a usable coast. Sponsor restrictions and existing wars remain decisive. |
| Focus surface | Preserve the existing living tree, with bounded additive founder content under verified ownership and independent event or decision responses |
| Invalid states | Country absent, no autonomy to form the required relationship, hostile occupation preventing actual government, unsupported target data, no viable approach, or incompatible exclusive event ownership |

Both profiles start from a supported persecution target. Purity Doctrine's additional categories are a separate authored target dataset and political proposal. The historical sources do not justify inventing a blanket anti-Muslim, anti-Catholic or anti-Orthodox target for either origin. Expansion must identify the additional actual community, evidence and overlap rules before that variation is enabled.

## Concrete client origin profiles

These three profiles are the authored launch set. The broader dynamic system can admit later complete region profiles without changing the core rules. Their administrative institutions are fictional imposed governments unless a legitimate existing identity is actually available through the political settlement. Source notes R5, R8, R9 and R10 provide historical context, not game geography.

| Profile | Coherent origin and capital candidate | Map and supply gate | Identity and territory rule | Distinct play |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frontier | A Bessarabian region centered on Chișinău | Verified connected controlled states, a real frontier and supply center, viable supporting industry or supply agreement | Reuse an existing appropriate regional identity only if absent and supported by the map and settlement. Otherwise use an audited new military-administration identity with claims limited to the governed region. | Sponsor support, border defense, disputed command and the choice between a limited offensive or independent consolidation |
| Maritime | Thessaloniki and a defensible connected Greek hinterland or supported port region | Verified port, usable route, convoys, fuel, landing or land approach, enough hinterland to sustain authority | A local imposed administration cannot overwrite a living Greece or claim to represent all Greeks. Greek restoration remains available where valid. | Port survival, convoy dependence, alternative routes, coastal defense and the danger of a stranded overseas client |
| Interior | Sarajevo and a coherent controlled Bosnian interior region | Verified local supply and industry or enforceable transit, sufficient territorial depth and a viable capital | A fictional council cannot stand for every community in the region. For every profile, the state manifest must list the selected capital first and at least one named in-region fallback if geography provides one. The ordered fallback rule prioritizes a remaining controlled supply and administrative center, then a viable controlled regional city. A region without any valid fallback can still exist but must face normal capitulation when its sole viable center falls. The implementation cannot invent an extra state to satisfy a template.

## Deterministic dynamic setup rules

Use these rules for the actual event-created client. Existing living countries retain their native values and do not receive a second setup pass.

| Surface | Deterministic rule |
| --- | --- |
| Territory and population | Transfer only the validated controlled state group through the native owner. Preserve actual population and all shared living, custody, displacement and responsibility records. |
| Politics | Establish the appropriate imposed institutional government in the inspected existing native political family. Begin with meaningful local opposition derived from current control and resistance. Route changes replace this authority through the defined political process. |
| Laws | Start no higher than the region's lawful current economic mobilization and the sponsor's supported administrative capacity. Use the least extreme valid conscription law that can sustain the paid starting force, subject to native eligibility. Record the exact selected law and why. |
| Factories and slots | Inherit factories and building slots physically present in transferred states, including damage. Add zero free factories or slots at creation. Construction uses actual civilian industry and later earned focus preparation. |
| Research slots | Proposed starting rule is two slots, reduced to one if usable controlled civilian plus military industry is below five. A third slot requires a later research-institution focus and sustained industry of at least ten usable factories. Never exceed the sponsor's research capacity through initial setup. |
| Technologies | Use the intersection of relevant technologies supported by the region's former owner and the current funding sponsor, constrained by the actual date and prerequisites. A paid later transfer can add a supported sponsor technology. No technologies are copied from an unrelated worldwide best country. |
| Equipment set | Ordinary infantry equipment and only locally supported artillery, support, truck, train, air and naval equipment. Select actual researched variants from the inspected providers. No custom equipment family is introduced. |
| Starting army | Use one recorded formation transfer or a funded recruitment package. The baseline small frontier envelope is 12 to 24 infantry battalions, but actual committed manpower, equipment and supply clamp the result downward. If the minimum viable defensive package cannot be funded, client creation is unavailable. |
| Army manpower | Existing transferred formations retain their actual manpower. New formations consume proven available manpower from the agreed owner. They cannot consume population removed by civilian deaths or reclassify detainees into free military manpower. |
| Templates | A small defensive infantry template is the starting family. Add support companies only when equipment and technology support them. Expand into a limited offensive or motorized template through real production and training. No empty nominal elite divisions. |
| Deployment | Place forces only in controlled supplied states, prioritizing capital, principal supply or port, then current frontier. Preserve a defensive reserve before expeditionary assignments. |
| Production | First cover infantry and support-equipment replacement, then the profile's transport need, then feasible artillery or specialist support. A two-factory client cannot run a full air, armor and navy program. |
| Stockpile and aid | Separate inherited local captured stocks permitted by native rules from the sponsor's measured contribution. Deduct each transferred item once and identify its receiving country or setup escrow. |
| Air force | Inherit only valid transferred aircraft and use a controlled usable airbase. Otherwise begin without wings and build or import them later. No airfield means no starting air wing. |
| Navy | Transfer real supported vessels only through a valid native transfer and usable home port. Otherwise the port client uses actual convoy and allied naval support until it can produce or acquire ships. No duplicate sponsor fleet. |
| Command | Use eligible existing transferred or retained commanders when their ownership permits it. Otherwise use the project's sourced or institutional command representation, with no invented historical person's identity. |
| Diplomacy | Set subject and compact relationships only from the accepted creation agreement. Preserve current wars and valid access. Independent status never supplies automatic transit or world-wide war goals. |
| Reinforcement | Apply the same production, manpower, training, supply and aid rules as ordinary countries. Aid is a material agreement with a real donor cost. |

The numerical research and army rules are proposed tuning. The implementation must document their actual outcomes against the selected state manifest and native data. If the resulting package cannot survive as a coherent government, revise the origin package openly rather than spawning unaccounted resources.

Technology inheritance validates the actual definitions as well as their IDs. A scripted grant can bypass the normal research-menu eligibility and execute `on_research_complete` rewards. Audit those callbacks before applying the sponsor intersection, so a retry or restored client cannot replay political, equipment or other one-time grants. Use one inheritance receipt and the current date, dependencies, exclusivity and equipment unlocks. Later departure removes temporary sponsor access and paid support according to their owners. It does not assume that unresearching a technology reliably revokes every database object it unlocked.

Historical character validity requires the actual campaign record. A native expiry date may apply only to the scenario's start date, and retirement or a leader-kill effect may leave a character available for later reassignment. Those mechanics are not proof that a deceased person can return. Check the supported historical calendar, current character identity and recorded death or retirement before using a real leader. Otherwise use the already selected institutional succession route. An institution is represented honestly rather than given an invented historical person's face.

## Regional focus module selection

Select a primary module at creation from the accepted origin profile. The frontier, maritime and interior authored profiles above have explicit precedence. For later dynamic profiles, maritime applies only when survival or the primary campaign actually depends on a validated port route. Frontier applies when a supplied land border is the principal strategic theater. Interior applies when local sustainment and transit determine survival. A country with both a coast and a frontier does not receive every module automatically.

Secondary preparation anchors can be enabled by real acquired capability after the primary module is established. They share resource and one-time reward receipts with any equivalent primary anchor. Losing a port, frontier, sponsor or transit agreement does not reset the whole focus tree. It invalidates dependent unfinished actions and opens a relevant recovery or withdrawal path. Completed physical construction remains where the map still contains it.

If none of the modules has a viable origin and sustaining capability, the client cannot be created. A living client that later loses all viable sustainment faces the actual political and military crisis, including collapse. It is not assigned a generic fallback army or an irrelevant naval branch.

## Interface ownership by actor

The founder receives the complete bounded founder branch while retaining its original meaningful tree. An actually event-created client receives its origin-selected event tree. Existing full members, limited partners, refusing governments and opponents keep their native trees. Invitation, command refusal, withdrawal, repudiation, protection and relief are always available through the appropriate event, decision or mission when politically and physically valid.

The authored Romanian and Slovak founder profiles receive the supported additive founder content. Existing opponents use the decision and mission progression as their complete baseline response. Additional opposition focus anchors are optional authored enhancements only for an inspected compatible tree and cannot be required to protect people or fight the crusade. This is the selected interface architecture, not a fallback that silently omits a required political route.
