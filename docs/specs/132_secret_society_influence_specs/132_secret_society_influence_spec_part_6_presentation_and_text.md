# Part 6: presentation and text direction

This part supplies presentation direction and working identifiers. Parts 1–5 own causes, choices, costs and outcomes. Preserve the uploaded event and evolution names. Other labels remain provisional, with final localisation still to be written.

## Native decision presentation

Use an ordinary decision category with its native action list, normal mission rows and one eligible static category picture. The category contains a short situation paragraph, a basic formatted summary of Network Influence and Evidence Coverage, and concise qualitative case facts. It remains eligible for a picture because it has no attached meters, custom buttons, interactive panels or animation.

Show both values as integers from 0 to 100. Influence describes the network's practical interference. Coverage describes supported investigative knowledge, with independent corroboration identified separately. Evidence is never presented as a resource, accusation strength or permission to remove an unnamed official. A value tooltip identifies its meaning, current material causes and next relevant consequence. Use natural lines and supported formatting, not pipe-separated telemetry, improvised table borders or decorative text gauges.

At most six primary actions appear together. Normal presentation should prefer three to five. Prioritize imminent deadlines, justified investigation, protection, proven removals and recovery. Phase and target conditions reveal alternatives. Active projects stay legible after their initiating decision disappears.

Use at most three active mission rows: one investigative project, one response operation and one bilateral operation. A consolidation mission replaces the completed response operation. Show a prepared takeover deadline or accommodation due date as a concise dated category statement and contextual tooltip when the three commitment slots are occupied. These dates are native timing information, not additional persistent mechanic values.

Each mission states its actual actor, target institution or country, duration, completion proof, cancellation consequence and current blocking reason. A protected source, identified contract or committed partner should be named when known. A selected country in a presentation control cannot replace the target frozen when the project began. The existing Event 039 objective binder demonstrates this distinction between actual recipient and outer event scope, but its helpers remain Event 039-owned. ## Choices, costs and consequences

Action descriptions explain the institution being changed and the meaningful risk or benefit. Tooltips carry exact costs, unavailable requirements and delayed consequences. Show every spendable cost with its correct native texticon, with no more than four distinct spendable types per action. Keep reserved civilian capacity, required equipment stock and non-consumed conditions separate from actual payment.

Before commitment, explain which paid amount is consumed, which capacity is reserved, what survives cancellation and what can be refunded. At a changed target or authority loss, display the actual neutral-cancellation result from the transaction receipt. Do not turn an invalid target into a fictional success report.

An accommodation choice must identify its administrative, procurement or command obligation, the term's due date and the consequence of refusal. Its reduced interference and continuing Influence floor belong in the spirit and action tooltips. Renewal text identifies the new concession. Switching contract family must disclose the continuing prior obligation. Controlled exit explains that benefits end, evidence remains and settlement still requires the independent pillar closures.

Blind purge text describes uncertain accusations, damaged trust and lost leads. It cannot imply that arbitrary dismissal proves membership. Command-protection text connects its officer-time and readiness cost to the interference it prevents. It cannot promise native-order cancellation or unrelated character removal.

## Spirit stages and case facts

One influence spirit changes between the below-40, 40–69 and 70+ bands. Its normal and accommodated modifier variants share the appropriate band icon, while the description states the active agreement. Only a confirmed command-access route displays the planning penalty. Refresh the native modifier and description together at each threshold.

One response-burden slot combines investigative, command or simultaneous commitments. One finite aftermath slot represents consolidation or an unsettled political transition. Do not create separate visible spirits for each lead, lodge, mission, foreign relationship or agreement. The maximum is three Event 132 slots, and obsolete stage variants are removed in the same state transition that installs their replacement.

Qualitative facts identify proven leadership, funding and access links, which remain intact and which have been severed. They also identify any deliberately retained contact and the one permitted rebuilding risk. Foreign survival does not imply an undisclosed domestic contact after true local closure. Evidence invalidation explains its actual compromised source while preserving independent facts.

## Report and news direction

The six report scene families below cover contextual reports and may be reused within the same visual purpose. Their labels identify assets, not final event titles. Match the text to the event's committed result and the player's actual information.

| Scene family | Viewpoint and visible information | Information withheld |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening irregularities | Government staff describe obstructed instructions and linked appointments or contracts. | Unproven members, supposed real-world lodge identity and future evolution conditions. |
| Corroborated records | Investigators explain the independently verified connection, affected institution and justified next step. Also supports documented closure. | Unsupported guilt, undiscovered contacts and raw registry identifiers. |
| Negotiated obligation | The responsible ministry describes the exact concession, relief and renewal pressure, including controlled exit. | Claims that agreement equals dismantlement or that refusal automatically causes a coup. |
| Rival circles | Investigators distinguish the two circles' observed access and contradictory accounts. | Treating one rival's allegation as independent corroboration. |
| Connected foreign chapter | A consenting partner or liaison explains a proven cross-border relationship and its bounded operation. | A complete world network map or automatic attribution of unrelated leaks. |
| Cabinet preparation | An announced leadership challenge names its proven civilian and participating command support and available defenses. | Secret thresholds, predetermined military obedience or guaranteed civil war. |

Two news scene families cover public governmental contest and verified institutional restoration. News uses an external correspondent's viewpoint and names the actual country and public outcome. A warned attempt, completed political capture, military split and reunification are different facts. A national report can cover a local recovery without declaring every foreign chapter destroyed.

Domestic reports may speak from the affected government's perspective. Foreign, observer and claimant versions need explicit branches, with a neutral fallback that cannot inherit another country's pronouns or accusations. Dynamic country, office, actor, partner, obligation, amount and deadline substitutions require live validity checks and an institution-level fallback when a person no longer holds the role. Do not fabricate a replacement person's identity.

## Public detail, focus and achievement surfaces

Event Details and catalog Details describe the premise and stakes without listing modifier values, rewards, penalties or hidden activation conditions. Evolution descriptions explain the new kind of problem. They do not present ordinary investigation stages as evolutions. Event log entries identify the actual host or partner and the committed result. Catalog mirror fields use the final accepted in-game wording, with catalog identity 132 (former catalog ID 150) and the registry and workbook migration left to the parent.

The temporary claimant's twelve-focus crisis tree uses the exact focus identifiers in Part 4. Its text describes continuity, command, supply, conciliation or patronage and the resulting wartime commitment. It must not imply a new ethnicity, ideology or permanent national identity. The host retains its meaningful national tree. Claimant victory describes the dependency actually left behind, and the restoration focus describes the verified return to national development content.

Achievement names and prose are authored later from the separate achievement prompt. Ordinary event, focus and decision text never advertises achievement routes. Hidden achievement conditions reveal only after their relevant evolution or political crisis becomes public. Achievement progress belongs in achievement UI, not additional category meters.

## Writing and review handoff

Use direct period-appropriate language about people, orders, payments, appointments and consequences. Keep fictional human motives clear. Avoid religious or ethnic conspiracy coding, occult shorthand, generic secret-master imagery and historical accusations inferred from a portrait. Preserve uncertainty where the evidence is uncertain. Do not supply invented quotations or source-like literary allusions.

Verify real offices, legal titles, institutions and historical actors against reliable sources and the actual country package. Otherwise use a neutral institution label. Research establishes identity and wording, not guilt.

Report descriptions on light parchment use plain names and values without colour codes, including dynamic substitutions. Supported dark tooltips and decision consumers may use restrained formatting. Preserve actual glyphs, BOM and authored newline escapes. Review normal, low-resource, no-agency, subject, AI, rival, foreign, accommodation-exit, coup and civil-war contexts. Check long names, missing targets, cancelled actions, integer formatting, crowded mission rows and the two-value limit at 1080p. Record source and available visual evidence separately.
