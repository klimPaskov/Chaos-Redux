# Secret Society Influence: the Hidden Cabinet and a divided government

## A prepared attempt to take authority

The Hidden Cabinet requires Influence 75 or higher, functioning leadership and funding, compromised access in at least two distinct civilian institutions, and an actual participating military command. An institution must have performed or enabled a recorded appointment, procurement or administrative action. A generic low-stability condition cannot supply both institutions. An agency can be one compromised institution when it really exists, but an agency is never required.

The participating command must control existing land, naval or air assets and have a current connection to the organization. Merely possessing generals, suffering a mutiny elsewhere or fighting a war is insufficient. The case records the involved command and its actual force envelope. Protected loyal headquarters remain distinct from participating officers. None of this requires a third public loyalty value.

These conditions must survive the 30-day eligibility dwell and dynamic 90-day MTTH described in Part 3. Recheck them before the announced attempt. The evolution supplies an actual coordinated preparation, not an immediate transfer of the country. High-Chaos openings need these same real facts. Influence 60 at such an opening cannot bypass the later 75-point requirement.

### The warned preparation window

The normal preparation window is 90 days. At announcement, apply the following adjustments and clamp the resulting interval to 60–120 days:

- An actual ongoing war subtracts 15 days.
- An already protected loyal command adds 15 days.
- An independently exposed coordination link adds 15 days.
- Mature coordination at Influence 90 or above subtracts 15 days.

Show the resulting deadline and the relevant endangered institutions. Freeze that date when the warning appears. Later global Chaos changes cannot silently shorten it. The first successful defense that materially disrupts coordination may earn one 15-day extension, with total preparation capped at 120 days from announcement. A defense that removes an essential prerequisite defeats the attempt instead of merely delaying it.

At most one fully announced attempt occurs per local national chapter in this episode. Its receipt survives a changed leader, tag representation and save/reload. Settings interruption supplies no victory award. Disabling this evolution before the native outcome commits stops its offensive preparation and preserves ordinary recovery. An already committed internal war follows its real war lifecycle.

## Distinct outcomes

**Defeated preparation.** Loss of leadership, funding, a necessary civilian institution or effective participating command ends the attempt. The report identifies the broken prerequisite. Preserve all ordinary pillar results and remaining society access. A 30-day aftermath can represent stability −5 points after a publicly exposed attempt, using the existing aftermath slot. It cannot restart through repeated cleanup. The country still completes ordinary dismantlement and consolidation before receiving the relevant terminal achievement.

**Dependent government.** Acceptance or an actual political takeover without a viable armed division preserves the country's identity, ordinary controls, meaningful national tree and surviving characters. The society replaces chaotic obstruction with enforceable appointments and procurement preferences. The proposed influence variant applies stability −10 points, political power gain −25%, consumer-goods factor +15%, and planning speed −15% only where participating command remains. Influence has a floor of 75 while both cabinet access and its funding dependency function. Existing compact obligations settle into this single disclosed arrangement, with no duplicate reservations or relief multiplier.

The government can investigate, protect an independent institution and sever proven pillars. Breaking either cabinet access or its financing removes the dependent-government floor and returns the remaining case to ordinary influence rules. Once all three actual pillars are severed and no residual contact, compact or local rebuilding remains, stop natural growth and cap Influence at 20. This permits recovery from a saturated 100-point crisis without an extra award. Full recovery still requires coordination and consolidation. This outcome cannot silently cancel diplomacy, research, elections, national focuses or native battle orders.

**Actual military split.** When participating command attempts to enforce the takeover and a real loyal government and force group resist from viable territory, create a two-sided native civil war. The state and force conditions below determine whether this outcome exists. A country with only one viable territorial and force group can suffer political capture or a defeated attempt according to its actual institutions. When two viable groups exist but the engine implementation is unresolved, the military-split requirement remains an explicit feasibility gap. A same-tag contest cannot replace it.

## Two governments claiming the same country

The claimant is a temporary rival government with the source country's national identity and ordinary armed forces. It is not a new ethnic state, historical nation, supernatural faction or excuse for a bespoke unit family. Its working description distinguishes its claim to government while retaining the national name and appropriate national symbols.

The source government retains its meaningful tree. The claimant receives the compact crisis tree below. Both remain within one underlying national chapter identity for the five-chapter limit. The claimant cannot create an independent outbound operation, a second foreign-establishment award or another capture receipt for the same national outcome. Each native actor nevertheless has exact country ownership for units, institutions, payments and cleanup.

### Feasible territory and conserved forces

Select connected territory actually owned and controlled by the source, with recorded institutional or command access. Preserve a viable government remnant. Each side needs a usable capital, an actual existing land-force group, and a sustainable land or maritime supply connection. Do not assign the sole functioning capital and every supply base to the claimant. A source with no two usable state groups or no forces for both sides cannot support this military outcome.

Base the claimant land share on the actual force context in the selected territory and participating command, with a proposed ordinary envelope of 25–50% of the existing national army. Protected loyal command reduces the eligible envelope. Deep command participation can support the upper end, while uncertain access cannot. The final request must leave actual usable forces on both sides. Separate naval, air and stockpile allocations depend on the real bases, assets and needs of each side.

The existing Event 021 transaction is a useful source precedent for connected territory, remnant checks, separate allocation ratios and actual-result receipts. ## Victory, reunification and national recovery

A government victory ends the claimant through the real native war and reunification path, retaining actual surviving troops, equipment and characters under verified transfer rules. A claimant victory establishes the winning rival government over the same nation. Neither grants free foreign territory or awards a second invasion-style country package.

The winning side needs a usable national development tree. The host keeps its original tree throughout. A winning claimant uses Restore Administration to recover the correct existing national content, completed-focus history where supported, valid branch locks, research and institutional continuity. The implementation must verify native inheritance and loading behavior. A generic tree, empty focus window or repeated rewards from resetting completed focuses is an unresolved defect, not an acceptable substitute.

Claimant victory through patronage initially preserves a dependent government. Conciliation opens an easier path to independent casework but does not automatically prove or sever the society's pillars. Both winners can perform the full domestic recovery. Final actual pillar severance, with no retained contact, compact or rebuilding transaction, stops growth and caps Influence at 20 before normal consolidation. Remove war-only focus modifiers, settle exact reservations, reassign surviving duties safely and continue any genuine external war.

The internal-war achievement requires the real two-country war receipt, actual reunification, verified restored national development, severed society pillars, resolved coordination and completed 90-day consolidation, extended only by the relevant-war rule. Either continuously tracked player side can qualify through legitimate native side selection. Debug tag switching, a simulated war, capital occupation alone or a leader change cannot qualify. Captured-government restoration and local dismantlement use their distinct one-shot consequence receipts without duplicating native war, annexation or casualty accounting.
