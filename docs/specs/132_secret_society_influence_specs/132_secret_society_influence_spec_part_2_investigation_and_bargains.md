# Investigation, protection and bargains

The country succeeds by identifying specific connections, protecting the people who can establish them and removing the network's practical access.
The government can undertake this work with domestic administrative resources even when it has no intelligence agency.
Its choices affect how quickly the case advances, how much disruption the country accepts and which obligations survive a settlement.
All action and mission labels below are working labels, not final localisation.

## Investigation commitments

The standard inquiry lasts 45 days.
A trusted agency reduces that by 10 days and one relevant current agency upgrade can reduce it by another 5.
A continuing external war adds 10 days, while active obstruction of the institution being examined adds 5.
The final duration stays within 30–60 days and is quoted before commitment.
Agency ownership alone does not provide the second reduction.
An agency whose leadership is currently compromised receives neither benefit until its own access route is secured.

Standard inquiries reserve civilian capacity and cost 25 political power.
The reserved capacity is one civilian factory for countries with fewer than 10 controlled civilian factories, two for countries with 10–39, and five for countries with 40 or more.
The count is taken from the actual current country and quoted once.
Existing consumer-goods obligations and other reservations still determine whether that capacity is available.
The reservation is released at every real terminal outcome.
It is not an additional permanent loss of industry.

An investigator project is directed at one uncompleted evidence relationship.
A valid completed first inquiry establishes 15 Coverage for that pillar.
A valid independent corroboration establishes the second 15.
Completion provides useful progress rather than an all-or-nothing random roll that can indefinitely block the country.
Obstruction changes duration, exposes a response choice or changes the available independent source.
It cannot erase all recovery routes.

### The low-capacity route

When a standard inquiry is unaffordable, the government can assign ordinary administrative staff to the same investigation.
This route charges no political power or equipment and requires no spare civilian factory.
It applies political power gain −10% and construction speed −10% through the shared response-burden slot for 90 days.
An external war or repeated interference can extend the duration to 120 days, but further obstruction cannot keep extending that same project.
The investigator slot remains occupied and the same natural Influence-growth reduction applies for the actual active interval.

This route produces the same properly bound 15-point evidence result for a pillar lead or its independent corroboration.
It also covers the final coordination inquiry, producing that objective's 10-point result under the same proof requirements.
Every required evidence relationship therefore remains completable without the ordinary resource quote.
It never gives a lower grade of proof merely because the country is small.
Its price is a longer period of national disruption and a much later response opportunity.
The AI must use it when all standard useful inquiries are unaffordable.
Low political power cannot make the action disappear, and a purely nominal AI affordability hint must not exclude it.

Losing reserved capacity during a paid inquiry presents a controlled transition into this slower commitment.
Retain already completed work, the original payment and the actual remaining capacity reservation.
Release capacity that can no longer be held.
The revised remaining duration cannot exceed 120 days from conversion and must not debit the original fee again.
The player can instead cancel under the disclosed cancellation policy.

### Evidence and people

Removing exposed members means removing proven organizational access and patronage.
The normal action does not kill a person or wipe an entire cabinet.
Where a named existing character occupies the relevant position, the current role, ownership and safe succession must be verified before changing that role.
Future national content that depends on the person must remain coherent.
An office can be secured through supervised authority or an institutional replacement when that preserves the intended gameplay without inventing a new person.

An unresolved historical relationship remains a valid inquiry target when the original route has genuinely ceased.
Establish its history and independently verify the absence of a functioning successor under Part 1's resolved-and-retired rule.
This closes the actual pillar without accusing a replacement, and it preserves the guaranteed route to all 100 Coverage.
Any invalidated original payment and the correctly rebound historical inquiry remain distinct transactions.

The protection mission and the insider's testimony remain distinct.
Turning an insider occupies the investigator lane for the standard 45-day inquiry with its stated adjustments, or the 90–120-day slow alternative.
Its one 25-political-power standard fee replaces the normal inquiry fee rather than adding another payment.
The slow method has no up-front political-power fee.
Both methods incur the one-time stability cost and commit one concurrent response lane to the chosen witness-protection method.
The full quote includes that method's actual equipment reservation or institutional-guarantee burden, with no separate duplicate protection charge.
Protection normally runs 90 days from commitment and extends to the slow inquiry's quoted completion date when needed, up to 120 days.
Verify the testimony and award its one target-bound lead only at the legitimate inquiry completion.
Finishing protection does not automatically corroborate everything the insider claimed.
Each lodge can supply only one consumed defection opportunity per actual insider, and renewing a protection arrangement does not produce another lead.
If a witness is lost, previously independent proof remains valid and another investigation method stays open.

The temporary Influence reduction from a blind purge expires exactly once.
It cannot be renewed continuously through repeated clicks.
That action is available only once per local case, and its permanent mistake receipt disqualifies clean-containment achievements.
The society's real leadership and funding survive unless independently proven and removed.

Exposing proven coordination can succeed only once in the same announced takeover attempt.
The disclosed institution withdraws its support for that attempt, while ordinary patronage access still requires its own targeted removal.
If fewer than two required civilian supports remain, the attempt ends immediately.
If sufficient support survives, apply the one eligible 15-day extension only when no earlier defense has used it, keeping the total preparation window within 120 days.
Disclosure grants no new Coverage, pillar reduction or independent Chaos award merely for publication.
Its action occupies the existing response role in the warned-takeover phase.

## Accommodation contracts

One local compact may be active at a time.
It has an immutable identity, a named institutional concession, a quoted start date and a 90-day term.
The society reduces its ordinary interference as described in Part 1.
The government chooses which access it is willing to preserve.

| Contract family, working label | Initial and continuing concession | What becomes easier | What remains unresolved | Renewal and exit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Administrative appointments | 25 political power at agreement and political power gain −10% throughout the term | Routine civilian obstruction falls, with ordinary command protection still available | A specified appointment channel stays open and Influence cannot fall below 35 | First renewal repeats the fee, second renewal raises it to 50 or requires a 45-day administrative commitment. Further renewals keep that disclosed ceiling |
| Procurement preference | Reserve one, two or five civilian factories according to the same footprint bands, with consumer-goods factor +10% for the term | The uncontrolled contract-disruption component ends | The identified funding route stays open and can finance a real society operation | First renewal repeats the commitment, second offers the same factories plus factor +15% or a controlled withdrawal. The term cannot reserve another overlapping factory set |
| Command patronage | 10 army experience at agreement, planning speed −10% and army experience gain −10% for the term | Routine society command interference is reduced and identified officers remain in their roles | The protected command channel stays available to the society and can later support a real takeover | First renewal repeats the experience payment, second requires 20 or a 45-day readiness sacrifice. Further renewals keep that disclosed ceiling |

Only the relevant contract obligation applies.
The government does not pay all three families together.
The reduced influence variant and the contract burden must be calculated as one complete package so the same interference is not charged twice.
Part 1's +5% residual consumer-goods factor for administrative and command bargains disappears if their separately proven funding route has actually been severed.

An advance renewal notice appears 30 days before the existing term ends.
It presents the exact due payment, the available alternative sacrifice and the controlled withdrawal route.
Ignoring the notice leads to the disclosed end of the concession and the return of ordinary interference.
It cannot immediately create a coup or a civil war.
An actual Hidden Cabinet preparation continues under Part 4's separate conditions and deadline.

Switching compact families at renewal preserves the number of prior renewals and resolves the old obligation before opening the new one.
It never resets the concession history, creates a second relief multiplier or returns consumed political power or experience.
The second-renewal ceiling gives the arrangement a predictable long-term cost instead of an infinite price ladder.

A government can investigate during a compact.
Once the three pillars can be removed, controlled withdrawal provides a costly but complete route to independent government.
The society may attempt one evidence-obstruction incident during that withdrawal if its leadership and access are still functioning.
The incident does not destroy proven independent facts.

## Ordinary incidents

Incidents use the current local facts and the normal local incident limit.
They create a new choice or observable consequence, not a generic loss every few weeks.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conflicting command instructions | An active command channel obstructs a real military institution | Protect headquarters, examine the command connection or accept a stated temporary planning burden | The protected route prevents further command acceleration during its covered interval. Investigation can establish a new access fact |
| Favored contractor | An intact funding route interferes with an actual procurement relationship | Trace the beneficiary or tolerate the contract until a current war commitment ends | Investigating opens a funding lead. Tolerance maintains the disclosed consumer-goods burden without adding a second copy |
| Witness under pressure | A real sole-source witness becomes exposed | Begin one protection mission or seek an independent source during its 90-day window | Success preserves testimony. Failure removes only the source-dependent fact and opens another investigation method |
| A compromised investigator | Evidence ties the current investigating institution to an active chapter | Transfer the inquiry to ordinary administration or examine that institution | Retain completed work and remove an unearned agency bonus. The same project cannot finish twice |
| Mistaken removal exposed | A past blind purge or proven faulty source harmed a loyal office | Rehabilitate the specific office over 45 days or accept its finite impairment | Repair restores usable administration. It does not grant an unrelated Influence reduction or erase the mistake receipt |
| A replacement carries the old connection | An unresolved pillar survives a change of person or government | Revalidate the successor access or close the proven institutional channel | Preserve old facts, require fresh proof of the successor relationship and avoid targeting the former holder |

If an institution changes during an objective, the action follows only a verified surviving relationship.
When that relationship no longer exists, the objective ends neutrally and can identify the next still-valid line of inquiry.
The game must not fabricate a replacement suspect merely to consume a pending action.

## Missions and presentation by phase

An investigative work project uses its declared 30–60 or 90–120-day duration.
It is a commitment to work, not a broad deadline requiring the player to accomplish an unrelated national task.
The witness-protection and consolidation missions normally allow 90 days.
Only the prepared takeover's explicitly urgent defenses may use the shorter 60-day end of its window.
Visible success conditions complete automatically when the real conditions have been sustained.
The player does not need a second reward-claim click.

| Current phase | Six primary action roles shown at most |
| --- | --- |
| Unproven network | Choose inquiry target, choose independent corroboration where available, protect command where relevant, consider an actual insider, examine agency where relevant, consider accommodation |
| Proven local case | Continue one useful inquiry, resolve coordination, remove the selected proven pillar, protect a live witness, protect command, review or leave a compact |
| Rival circles | Investigate a chosen circle, corroborate a rival claim, choose one bargain, expose the proven circle, protect a defector, pursue joint closure |
| Foreign connection | Continue the local inquiry, select one known partner or chapter, request cooperation, answer a received offer, resolve a known transfer, seal a documented residual contact |
| Prepared takeover | Secure command, expose proven coordination, disrupt funding, replace a compromised institution, negotiate the actual proposal, continue the most relevant investigation |
| Recovery | Consolidate, repair a proven mistake, settle an existing obligation, resolve a surviving foreign contact, restore a divided institution, inspect completed case facts |

Rows are roles, not six compulsory buttons.
Unavailable actions explain their specific missing fact or resource through a short tooltip.
Changing the selected pillar or known country updates the action in that role and does not create another simultaneous family of controls.
Native decision list actions, a concise value summary and one eligible static picture remain the main presentation.

## Payment, cancellation and cleanup

Quote each paid project once for its exact payer, target, episode and action generation.
The displayed quote, affordability check, payment and any refund must use that same record.
At exact affordability the action remains available.
Native factory reservations count once and are not charged again when completion runs.
The project cannot debit resources a second time at expiry or receive a second completion from a delayed callback.

The up-front political power and experience fees are consumed when useful work begins.
Voluntary cancellation does not refund that consumed effort.
Reserved equipment that was never used and continuing factory capacity are released to the verified payer.
Technical rejection before useful work begins refunds every actual debit and creates neither success nor a failure penalty.
Neutral invalidation after work begins releases unused reservations and preserves completed factual evidence, with no new failure cost or artificial cooldown.
Consumed fees remain recorded as consumed work, as stated when the project began.

If the payer no longer exists or the country identity has changed, the system must resolve the exact original payment record before returning resources.
It cannot refund an unrelated successor or create equipment from a nominal price.
An unresolved ownership record remains a clearly reported implementation issue and cannot be silently deleted.

Success, failure, voluntary cancellation, neutral authority loss, phase replacement and country retirement each remove their actual native mission or timed decision.
Clearing a private active flag alone is insufficient.
Stopping one project does not remove another event's modifiers, diplomacy, characters or evidence.
Completed discovery, paid-contract, mistake, closure and achievement records remain for their intended lifetime.

The decision and mission prompt applies these lifecycle requirements to implementation.
Part 5 includes exact-affordability, repeated-click, low-capacity, stale-target and transition scenarios that must be checked against the actual implemented source.
