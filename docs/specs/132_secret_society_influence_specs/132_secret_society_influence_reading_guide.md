# Secret Society Influence: package guide

Secret Society Influence begins as a domestic struggle against private control of appointments, contracts and command channels.
The government investigates particular connections, protects institutions and chooses whether to dismantle the organization or accept a costly accommodation.
Three optional evolutions introduce competing lodges, connected foreign chapters and a prepared struggle for government authority.

This package develops the uploaded Event 132 rough brief into a complete proposed design and implementation handoff.
New numerical tuning, action structure, the temporary claimant package and achievement conditions are authored proposals.
They are not claims that the user previously approved those details or that code, assets or gameplay have already been completed.
All new names and labels are working labels.
Player-facing text is specified through direction, meaning and required information, without draft localisation.

## Decisions that hold the package together

The country sees Network Influence and Evidence Coverage.
It proves leadership, funding and institutional access through independent evidence, then actively severs each pillar.
An accusation never becomes proof by spending an aggregate score.
The normal interface has at most six primary action roles and three concurrent mission lanes, while every promised response remains reachable through its current phase and selected target.

Ordinary investigations do not require an intelligence agency.
A guaranteed slow route remains available at zero political power and without spare factories or equipment, at a meaningful cost in time and administrative performance.
Agency capabilities provide bounded advantages and can themselves require scrutiny.
Accommodation uses one renewable 90-day contract whose paid concessions, due date and renewal history survive switching.

Rival Lodges, Across the Borders and The Hidden Cabinet remain independent optional mutations at 200+, 400+ and 800+ Chaos.
Actual eligible conditions and normal evolution pacing govern activation.
The episode supports at most five active national chapter identities.
A temporary native civil-war claimant belongs to its parent national chapter and does not create a sixth foreign establishment or an additional expansion source.

Complete local dismantlement is a durable victory even when foreign organizers survive.
Only a disclosed residual contact in a partial settlement can support the single delayed rebuilding attempt.
A high Chaos value does not create a coup, and a coup does not automatically create viable civil-war armies.

The host retains ordinary country control and its existing national development tree.
A genuine internal war needs actual territory, participating command and conserved forces on both sides.
The temporary claimant receives a compact twelve-focus wartime tree and a reviewed path back to usable national content after victory.
Native same-ideology splitting, exact force assignment and national-content inheritance remain explicit integration questions in the research handoff.
They cannot be resolved by silently changing the promised outcome.
