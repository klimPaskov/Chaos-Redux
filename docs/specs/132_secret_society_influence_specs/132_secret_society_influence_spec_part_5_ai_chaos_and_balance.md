# Secret Society Influence: opponents, consequences and balance

All names and labels in this section are working design labels, not final player-facing text. Network Influence and Evidence Coverage remain the only persistent public values. AI scores, payment receipts, chapter identities and consequence history are internal implementation state.

## Governments should pursue a coherent response

AI governments pursue closure, defend threatened commands or accept a justified accommodation. They use the same resources, time, proof and failures as players. When useful paid inquiries are unaffordable, the slow route takes priority over optional bargains. Agency ownership never becomes a participation gate.

Use proposed willingness scores of 200 for urgent protection, 100 for an attainable objective, 60 for preparation, 20 for a discretionary opportunity and 0 for an ineligible action. These are design priorities, not probabilities or observed behavior. Assess decision willingness, event-option weights, evolution MTTH and focus selection separately. Legal availability enforces every hard gate independently.

An AI keeps one investigation priority until it completes, becomes invalid or an announced coup threatens a more important objective. It can run the investigator, response and bilateral lanes concurrently when affordable, subject to the shared limit of three missions. It cannot reopen completed evidence work, repeatedly switch contracts, or cancel paid projects for a discount. A new priority never cancels an already paid action without the same neutral-cancellation rules available to the player.

| Actor and condition | Preferred action and proposed priority | Deliberate tradeoff and rejected behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unassisted government with a valid lead | Begin investigation at 100 and prioritize independent corroboration. When paid quotes are unaffordable, start the guaranteed slow domestic route at 100. | The slow route continues with zero political power or available factories while authority remains valid. It cannot identify a hidden culprit perfectly or earn agency-only proof. |
| Government with a proved intact pillar | Sever that pillar at 100, raised to 200 when it prevents an announced takeover. | Preserve enough resources for the operation. Evidence Coverage is not consumed, and an already severed pillar has score 0. |
| Belligerent with actual compromised command | Protect command at 200 during an announced preparation window, otherwise 100 when military interference is material. Continue a lower-cost inquiry where possible. | Officer experience, time and readiness costs still apply. War alone is not proof that a commander belongs to the society. |
| Financially constrained government facing severe interference | Compare an attainable investigation with one accommodation contract. Accommodation rises from 20 to 100 only when its specific burden is sustainable and immediate relief materially helps. | It cannot pay with negative reserves. Renewals compare the new concession against an affordable exit rather than renewing forever by default. |
| Government already accommodating | Preserve earned evidence. Prepare a proved exit at 100, meet a tolerable current obligation at 60, and renew at 20 unless the declared additional concession has a clear survival benefit. | Changing contract type keeps its original due date and renewal history. AI cannot reset the obligation by alternating choices. |
| Government facing rival lodges | Independent corroboration remains 100. Turning an insider can reach 100 where their actual access unlocks a blocked pillar. A bargain benefiting the survivor remains 20 unless immediate survival justifies it. | The same allegation cannot prove both circles. Exposing one must not make the survivor automatically innocent. |
| Foreign investigation partner | Consent and cooperate at 100 when its own chapter or attributed interference is demonstrated and the bilateral operation is affordable. Otherwise weigh diplomatic costs at 20. | No forced access to another country's institutions. A refusal closes that operation cleanly without erasing the initiator's domestic route. |
| Society chapter with leadership, funds and access intact | Protect an exposed indispensable connection before spending on expansion. An outbound operation is an opportunity, never an automatic consequence of high Influence. | Require a real connection, financing, one free outbound lane and a legal destination slot. It cannot spawn a chapter from a dead contact. |
| Society preparing a cabinet takeover | Maintain the actual participating command and financed preparation. Losing an essential prerequisite interrupts or defeats preparation according to its recorded stage. | No takeover at a Chaos threshold alone. It cannot replace lost formations with newly created bonus units. |
| Government or claimant during actual internal war | Secure a viable capital and logistics before discretionary expansion. Choose crisis-tree and wartime-decision branches that support its attainable settlement. | Ordinary national forces, current resource shortages and external wars remain real constraints. The AI cannot bypass war resolution by clicking the rival government's political outcome. |

Blind purges should be exceptional even for an authoritarian AI. They are not evidence-generating actions. Their willingness stays below a proved investigation or command defense, and becomes nonzero only in the specific desperate circumstances defined by the decision. An AI that repeatedly damages trust while another response is feasible fails the balance review.

Record available actions, calculated scores, selected targets and resulting receipts. Event-option comparisons may calculate probabilities from their real competing weights. Decision-score comparisons must not present those scores as click probabilities.

It settles its recorded payment once and grants no false success proof. A later record-based inquiry can independently verify the vanished relationship and its actual cessation. |

Run the economic cases for a weak minor, a major and a subject. Compare agency absent, useful and compromised. Change one relevant factor at a time for Influence, proof, war, reserves and chapter capacity. A result showing an illegal choice, impossible payment or permanent AI deadlock is a defect to fix. The exact numerical weights remain a future source-based validation task.

## Chaos follows material consequences

Evolution availability, evidence points, a timer ticking, a completed research action and an abstract Influence increase add no direct Chaos. Concrete harm is posted when it happens. Generic war, casualties, annexation and other already counted shared consequences stay with their owning systems.

The proposed balancing rule caps cumulative positive Event 132 postings at **75 over the entire episode**. This is parent-authored design, not existing engine behavior. Each positive posting equals the smaller of its consequence amount and the unused positive allowance. Recovery does not refill that allowance. Keep this accounting invisible to the player, with ordinary event history explaining the actual consequences.

| Actual consequence | Event 132 Chaos and dynamic scope | Receipt, reversal and overlap rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First demonstrated institutional disruption | +2 once globally for the episode, attached to the original affected country. It requires an applied disruption, not merely assignment or an unrevealed rumor. | One irreversible posting receipt. Later reports, spirit-band changes and evolution entry do not repeat it. Eligible local dismantlement may later credit against its remaining attribution. |
| Real foreign chapter established | +3 for each distinct newly affected country identity. Scale total contribution by actual successful establishments, within five active underlying national chapter identities and the remaining 75-point allowance. | Post only after the destination's chapter and access exist. Aliases, transfers between holders, recapture and the one permitted rebuild do not create a second first-establishment receipt. |
| Organized command seizure before a larger outcome | +5 once per country only when a separate earlier operation actually takes coordinated command functions and materially disrupts the government. | It must be distinct in time and consequence from the capture, coup or war transaction. If it is simply that transaction's military description, post 0 here. No refund for merely regaining one office. |
| Government actually captured | +10 for a minor or +15 for a major, using its validated status immediately before capture. Once per distinct country identity. | A threatened, attempted or failed coup gives 0 on this row. Do not add a second generic coup amount for the same capture. Recapturing the same government cannot re-award it. |
| Completed domestic dismantlement | Up to -2 once per country after all three pillars, coordination, residual-contract and consolidation requirements are satisfied. | Bound the credit by that country's attributable unreversed Event 132 positives. Rebuild and second closure cannot repeat the award. Ordinary investigations and partial settlements do not qualify. |
| Captured government restored | Up to -5 once per captured country after government authority and the specified institutional restoration are actually recovered. | Draw only against that country's remaining attributable positives. Winning a battle, changing a leader or reoccupying a capital alone does not qualify. Shared war settlement reduction is not duplicated. |
| International network genuinely ends | Up to -5 once globally when at least three real chapters existed and all chapters, rebuilding contracts and pending operations are retired. | Credit only the remaining attributable positive balance after all local credits. Local victory remains valid before this condition. Reopening a dead callback never reopens the award. |

A temporary civil-war claimant shares its parent's national chapter identity, five-chapter allowance and one outbound operation limit. It is neither a new foreign establishment nor another expansion source. Actor-specific payments, missions, commands and territory retain their actual owners. Cosmetic changes, aliases and recaptures cannot re-award establishment, capture or closure receipts.

The reversal ledger prevents two recovery rows from consuming the same positive contribution. It records actual awarded amounts, including a partial positive posting at the 75-point limit. No row promises an automatic full refund. A queued consequence rechecks that its source, destination, generation and unconsumed receipt still match before posting.

Example: the original disruption contributes 2 and two foreign establishments contribute 3 each, for 8 total. Three valid domestic closures can return 2 each. If no other Event 132 consequence occurred, only 2 remains attributable. International closure can therefore return 2, not its nominal maximum of 5. If a real government capture added further harm, the larger remaining balance could support the full international credit. Ordinary war Chaos remains outside both calculations.

## Pacing should create decisions before a collapse

Influence begins at 35, with +5 per evidenced opening vulnerability, capped at 55. An evolved 60-point opening needs its additional prerequisites. Natural growth is +5 per 30 elapsed days, rising to +10 at Influence 70+ with intact leadership and funding. A valid domestic investigation offsets 5 for its actual covered interval. Callback frequency, save loading and UI openings cannot change elapsed progress.

A normal inquiry starts at 45 days, with trusted agency assistance -10, an actual relevant upgrade -5, war +10 and continuing obstruction +5, clamped to 30–60 days. The guaranteed slow domestic route takes 90–120 days, using the declared political-power-gain and construction burden instead of an unaffordable purchase. Both offset natural growth by 5 while validly active. A compromised agency grants neither agency reduction. Native mission objectives normally allow 90 days, with a shorter 60-day coup defense only where explicitly justified.

Each pillar yields 15 Coverage for an independent lead and 15 for independent corroboration, maximum 30. The coordination link supplies the final 10. Closure work can overlap in the response lane once a pillar is proved. Severance removes 20 Influence once and ends its associated growth contribution. A low Influence band does not replace the requirement to sever all three pillars. Once all are severed and no residual contact, active compact or local rebuilding transaction remains, stop natural growth and cap Influence at 20. This final loss of functioning access prevents a saturated case from becoming stuck at 40 after three reductions. It adds no separate Chaos refund. Final coordination and independent corroboration retain the guaranteed slow route when their paid version is unaffordable.

Accommodation renews on its original 90-day cadence. Foreign operations take 60–120 days with maintained links. Cabinet preparation has its own announced 90-day central window adjusted within 60–120 days. Each evolution requires 30 continuous eligible days before its 90-day base MTTH, modified and clamped to 45–180 days as Part 3 or Part 4 specifies. MTTH is an opportunity process, not a guaranteed deadline. It never replaces the announced action clock.

Disabling an evolution prevents new expansion belonging to it. It does not confiscate evidence, remove the baseline recovery route, strand a paid project or instantly declare a live war resolved. Sparse participant scheduling and existing state transitions are sufficient. A recurrent scan of every world country is unnecessary.

## Balance walkthroughs

**A small country investigates without an agency.** Start at Influence 35 and Evidence 0. Two unobstructed 45-day inquiries establish one pillar's 30 Coverage. Their continuous growth offset holds Influence at 35 absent separate incidents. Keep the next inquiry running during the 45-day removal. Severance then removes 20, reaching 15, while the other pillars still prevent recovery. A country with no payable reserves uses the 90–120-day slow route and its response burden instead. Time still produces the first 15-point lead. Doing nothing is materially different: 210 days of unopposed +5 growth reaches 70, after which intact leadership and funding accelerate the next 60 days to 90. These calculations exclude additional incidents and expose the cost of inactivity without trapping poor countries.

**A war economy accepts temporary accommodation.** Start at 55 with real command exposure. After 90 days without an investigation offset, untreated Influence reaches 70. Halving interference through accommodation can make survival possible, but its floor of 35 prevents passive eradication and its industrial, political or military obligation remains real. Use the respite to obtain proof and prepare an exit. A second renewal with a newly declared concession must be worse than a free extension. The AI should prefer exit when it can afford actual closure and defense.

**Foreign survival does not erase domestic victory.** A country completes local dismantlement and consolidation while two foreign chapters retain legitimate connections elsewhere. It loses local interference and gets its one eligible local credit. It does not need to invade those partners. A later intrusion requires an actual residual local contact and the allowed rebuilding contract. True local closure leaves neither, so a foreign chapter cannot reset the player's victory merely by waiting.

**A prepared coup meets a real defense.** Influence at least 75 is insufficient alone. Leadership, financing, compromised civilian institutions and an actual participating command must all be present. During the announced window, command protection competes with readiness expenditure, exposure depends on proof, and funding removal attacks a specific prerequisite. A successful defense is meaningful because it defeats a prepared operation. A military split happens only when command and territory really divide. The existing Event 021 source records actual forces after a native split, while its ledger warns that rounding and callback order still require engine evidence. Exact same-ideology behavior remains an implementation feasibility item, with no forced ideology substitution or synthetic army as a silent substitute.
