# Achievement implementation and icon prompt

Implement six difficult Event 132 achievements from committed gameplay facts. These are authored planning conditions and working identifiers, not final names, descriptions or completed achievement code. Do not award anything for the opening, purchasing an action, crossing a Chaos threshold or merely viewing an evolution.

## Shared eligibility and durable tracking

All six require a natural Event 132 episode. Forced scenarios, debug initialization, artificial evolution activation and injected completion receipts permanently disqualify that episode. Record the reason. Ordinary evolution enablement and legitimate choices remain valid.

Bind progress to the episode generation, actual local chapter, stable country identity and authorized player side. A tag, current country name or visible spirit alone is insufficient. Repeated callbacks, save/reload, cosmetic changes and claimant creation cannot duplicate progress. Completed awards persist after event cleanup. Eligibility failures remain latched even if the offending agency, bargain or temporary flag later disappears.

Conditions use owner-produced evidence, action, payment, outcome and closure receipts. They never infer success from a missing category or country. Local closure means all three pillars severed, coordination resolved, Influence 20 or less, no live rebuilding transaction, active accommodation compact or retained contact, and completed consolidation. Consolidation lasts at least 90 elapsed days and includes any valid extension to 120 required by the owner. Mere timer expiry, annexation or transition to a nonhuman actor is not closure.

Store proof with the owner lifecycle and evaluate the finite candidate list at relevant transitions. Publish awards idempotently. Show criteria in achievement UI without extra category meters or reward advertising in ordinary text.

## 1. Clean containment without an agency

Working ID: `132_secret_society_influence_unassisted_containment`.
Difficulty: very hard domestic containment.
Visibility: reveal after the natural opening report.

The original affected country must begin the episode either fighting a real war or with stability below 50% before Event 132's own modifiers. Freeze that qualifying circumstance at admission. It must then achieve full local closure with Coverage 100, having never possessed an intelligence agency at any time during its own case. The no-agency check includes an agency created, inherited or granted by another event. A game without the agency DLC can qualify under the same domestic conditions.

The country must use only domestic investigative proof, never adopt an accommodation contract, never choose blind purge and never suffer political capture or a civil-war split during the case. Influence must stay below 70 from admission through consolidation. Foreign resource gifts alone are not investigative assistance, but a credited foreign casework or borrowed-service receipt breaks the domestic-only condition.

Track opening war/stability proof, agency-ever and foreign-investigation-ever latches, peak Influence, forbidden-response latches and the final closure receipt. Removing an agency later, returning from a different country or clearing old flags cannot restore eligibility.

Icon subject: an original protected public ledger and an ordinary administrative key, without an intelligence-agency insignia. Compose for a strong native-size silhouette.

## 2. Independently dismantle both rival circles

Working ID: `132_secret_society_influence_both_lodges_exposed`.
Difficulty: hard expanded investigation.
Visibility: hidden until Rival Lodges is actually revealed.

Rival Lodges must activate naturally with two real, distinct circles in the same local case. Establish each circle's leadership and actual access through independent corroboration, resolve every circle-specific branch required by Part 3, sever the three aggregate pillars, resolve coordination and complete full local closure. No active privileged-circle bargain or surviving local access may remain.

A rival's allegation can open a lead but cannot satisfy its own corroboration. If two circles share a financier or institution, one transaction may close the actual shared route, while each circle's relationship still needs its own proof. Do not require duplicate payment or grant duplicate evidence for that shared institution.

Disqualifiers are synthetic second-circle creation, counting the same circle under two names, unilateral destruction without the required evidence, and closure by authority loss. A failed investigation is recoverable if later valid independent proof replaces it. Track distinct circle identities, evidence provenance, shared-route ownership, branch closure and final consolidation.

Icon subject: two distinct interlocked rings separated by one examined public record. Use a fictional civic motif with no borrowed historical society emblem.

## 3. Close cases with two foreign partners

Working ID: `132_secret_society_influence_three_country_cooperation`.
Difficulty: very hard diplomatic recovery.
Visibility: hidden until Across the Borders is revealed and an actual foreign chapter is known.

The player's chapter and two distinct foreign countries must each have a real chapter from the same natural episode. Both partners must consent to their investigations. Each bilateral relationship must complete a material reciprocal evidence exchange, with each side contributing a valid independently sourced fact or operation result that advances the other's real case.

All three countries must then earn local closure receipts and finish their own consolidation. Other foreign chapters may remain active. This achievement does not make global eradication a requirement for ordinary domestic recovery.

A temporary claimant, cosmetic rename, tag reuse or successor of an already counted partner cannot supply a second country. Annexation, forced authority changes, unconsented searches, self-funded fake chapters and duplicated evidence receipts do not count. If a partner loses its institutions before closure, that partner's slot remains unfulfilled. Valid historical closure receipts survive normal cleanup.

Track both partners' stable identities, chapter admissions, consent generations, reciprocal contribution receipts and their closure outcomes. Award to the qualifying player country only after all three local cases have completed. Do not infer foreign success merely from low visible Influence.

Icon subject: three different administrative keys meeting over a secured dossier. It must communicate cooperation without suggesting a new military alliance.

## 4. Fulfil repeated accommodation, then leave it

Working ID: `132_secret_society_influence_paid_exit`.
Difficulty: very hard long-term recovery.
Visibility: reveal when the first accommodation contract is accepted.

Complete the initial 90-day contract and two fully paid 90-day renewal terms, with every concession required by its quoted renewal. At least 270 actual days of fulfilled obligation must therefore be recorded. Switching between administrative, procurement and command contracts preserves the original obligations and due dates and cannot manufacture a new completed term.

After those three settled terms, take the controlled-exit route, end the actual continuing benefits and obligations, and independently dismantle leadership, funding and institutional access. Retained evidence may support that work. Resolve coordination, reduce Influence to 20 or less and finish full consolidation without a residual contact.

Any missed owed payment, unpaid concession, default disguised as neutral cancellation, premature refund or switch that erases an obligation disqualifies this route. Blind purge, government capture or a military split cannot substitute for controlled exit. A paid renewal click before its term ends does not count as a fulfilled renewal.

Track original contract generation, three unique settled-term receipts, each concession, payment/debit proof, due dates, controlled-exit receipt and subsequent pillar closures. The count comes from completed terms, not how often a button was pressed.

Icon subject: a fulfilled contract with its binding ribbon deliberately released, preserving the document rather than burning the evidence.

## 5. Defeat an announced prepared coup politically

Working ID: `132_secret_society_influence_prepared_coup_defeated`.
Difficulty: very hard crisis defense.
Visibility: hidden until The Hidden Cabinet produces a valid public preparation warning.

The owner must record a fully prepared attempt with Influence 75 or more, intact leadership and funding, at least two participating civilian institutions and actual participating command. The announced 60–120-day preparation window must exist. A threshold reached without this proof does not qualify.

Complete a materially decisive defense that removes an essential prerequisite and causes the actual attempt's defeated outcome. The first decisive defense resolves the threat immediately. No government capture, accepted cabinet dependence or native civil-war mutation may occur during this case. Starting a protective project without an actual successful defensive result does not qualify.

Then finish full local dismantlement and consolidation. Disabling an evolution, deleting a country, losing the target or clearing the queued attempt is not a political victory. A rejected or refunded defense does not count. Negotiated acceptance of the cabinet's authority fails this route even if recovery succeeds later.

Track the warned-attempt generation, its actual civilian and military proof, the decisive defense receipt, owner-issued defeated outcome and later independent closure. Later routine casework cannot retroactively manufacture a defeated-threat receipt.

Icon subject: a preserved institutional chair and shield, with a broken illicit chain of command. Avoid a battlefield triumph motif.

## 6. Win the internal war and restore institutions

Working ID: `132_secret_society_influence_reunification_and_restoration`.
Difficulty: exceptional military and institutional recovery.
Visibility: hidden until the real Event 132 internal war commits.

A valid escalation of The Hidden Cabinet must produce two actual countries, coherent divided territory, participating command and a real war. Track the human player's chosen side continuously. Supported native side selection into the temporary claimant is allowed, while debug switching, artificial tag cycling or taking over an already victorious side is disqualifying.

The tracked side must win that internal war, reunify the recorded national territory, and complete the verified restoration of appropriate national development content. It must preserve the surviving country's real identity and handle ordinary institutions through Part 4's settlement. Military victory alone is insufficient, especially for a claimant that retains society patronage.

Finally sever all society pillars, resolve coordination, reach Influence 20 or less and complete full consolidation without a retained contact. The claimant's conciliation route can support recovery. Its patronage route must first fulfil and deliberately dismantle the obligations it preserves.

A fabricated war, annexation without the recorded internal conflict, cloned forces, an empty restored tree, a renamed country or a missing claimant pointer supplies no victory proof. Require the real-war, actual-reunification, verified-development-content and institutional-closure receipts. Preserve those distinct receipts through country transfer and cleanup.

Icon subject: reunited national administrative records beneath a repaired civic seal, with ordinary military equipment set aside. Do not invent a new national flag or ethnic identity.

Derive the grey subject deterministically while preserving position and alpha. Build the not-eligible subject by adding the unchanged supplied `overlay.png` at exact 64×64 alignment. The templates are workflow inputs, not reusable reference art. Verify their hashes against the `icons/achievements/template/` entries in the event-assets `CATALOG.md` and do not redraw or recolour them.

Deliver all 18 final DDS files directly under `gfx/achievements/`, named from the exact six full IDs above with base, `_grey` and `_not_eligible` suffixes. Preserve separate source triplets, verify exact compositing equality and canonical DDS decode, and review a native-size contact sheet. The registered achievement identity governs lookup and any existing sprite aliases must be recorded. Missing states, source ambiguity or a failed template check leaves the asset incomplete.

For each route, evaluate one qualifying source scenario and failures at every decisive boundary. Include agency acquired then removed, later war not present at opening, duplicated rival identity, false corroboration, revoked partner consent, reused partner tag, unpaid renewal, switched contract, cancellation refund, unresolved coup, government capture, fake civil war, claimant side selection, lost national development content and incomplete consolidation.

Review duplicate accepted callbacks and persistent ownership fields in the actual implementation. Verify in the source model that neutral authority-loss cleanup never awards success and that an earned award survives later event cleanup. Inspect a save or run the game only when the user explicitly requests that validation, with the remaining persistence evidence limit stated honestly otherwise. Report source-model and native-runtime evidence separately. Hand off exact IDs, predicates, receipt owners, eligibility latches, visibility keys, icon triplets and unresolved native-engine limitations, without claiming completion from the condition table alone.
