# Secret Society Influence: the domestic network

The government keeps issuing orders, appointing officials and supplying its forces, but a private network increasingly decides which instructions are followed and which people receive access.
The country must identify how the organization holds itself together, protect essential institutions and choose how much disruption it can bear while removing that influence.
Cooperation with the society offers relief at the price of obligations that survive the initial bargain.

Event 132 retains the uploaded concept's fictional human organization and five response families.
The three proposed evolutions remain optional developments at 200+, 400+ and 800+ Chaos.
The numerical tuning, decision structure, outcomes and country packages in these parts are authored planning proposals.
They are ready for implementation review, with acceptance and implementation evidence recorded separately.
All newly introduced route, action, focus and achievement labels are working labels, not final localisation.
The event name and evolution labels supplied in the brief identify the intended content.

## The opening country

One suitable country receives the developing Secret Society Influence spirit.
Major powers, ordinary AI minors, player countries and subjects are eligible.
The target must have a functioning human government and real institutions that the event can affect.
An actual nonhuman country is outside this human institutional premise, while an unusual human country remains eligible.
The opening requires a viable domestic investigation route and a real controlled administrative base.
A country already absorbed by another state cannot remain a live target through a stale country pointer.

Use the shared individual-crisis capacity when admitting the initial host and each foreign chapter.
Its current limit is three active participating crises per country, with lower-load candidates favored after their event-specific suitability is assessed.
This event contributes one local active-crisis provider, regardless of how many lodges or response missions it contains.
Continuation and recovery belong to an already admitted episode and do not consume fresh slots.
An accommodated network still occupies its slot because its interference and obligations remain active.
Completed recovery and exact authority-loss retirement release the slot.

The intended classification is Minor Fire-Once, available from Chaos level 1, meaning 0+ Chaos.
Intelligence is the primary proposed cluster membership at Medium severity.
Domestic Unrest is a secondary proposed membership at Low severity.
The shared Medium participation floor can postpone an Intelligence-cluster appearance until 200+ even when standalone selection is already possible.
Secondary cluster admission and standalone availability retain their own valid conditions.
Both memberships consume the same first-firing record.

The initial network receives Influence 35, increased by 5 for each applicable opening condition:

- The country is fighting a real war.
- Its stability before this event's own modifiers is below 50%.
- Its command has a current, evidenced disruption that leaves a useful institutional opening.
- A relevant earlier crisis has left an unresolved institution or patronage connection.

Opening Influence is capped at 55 under this baseline.
The same disruption cannot qualify twice through different names.
Having a military, being a subject or following a particular ideology does not itself establish compromised command.
An evolved opening may use Influence 60 only where Part 3 or Part 4's additional conditions genuinely exist.

The first report shows consequences that can be observed in the country: instructions repeatedly bypassed, trusted staff losing access, or contracts benefiting linked intermediaries.
It does not identify a real ethnic, religious or historical organization as the hidden cause.
The network's particular members, foreign relationships and ambitions emerge through this campaign's evidence.
The initial actor is prepared before the shared event history is recorded, so the affected country's flag and name are correct.

## Two public values

### Network Influence

Influence runs from 0 to 100 and represents the network's present ability to secure appointments, divert resources and obstruct government action.
It changes the strength of the developing spirit, the opportunities available to the society and the urgency of countermeasures.
Its tooltip identifies the current largest causes and the next meaningful threshold.
Influence does not reveal the identity of an unproven member or an undiscovered foreign chapter.

### Evidence Coverage

Coverage runs from 0 to 100 and describes how completely the current local organization has been established through reliable evidence.
It is knowledge, never a currency that can be spent to accuse another person.
Three pillars each contribute up to 30 points:

| Pillar | First reliable connection, 15 points | Independent corroboration, another 15 points | The action that breaks it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leadership | A verifiable connection between an organizer and an obstructed institution | A separate source or observed action confirms that organizer's role | Remove the proven organizers' access and replace their authority |
| Funding | A verified supply, payment or contracting relationship supports the network | Independent examination establishes the beneficiary and the actual flow | End the identified preference and remove the funding route |
| Institutional access | A verified appointment or command channel serves the network | A separate observation or protected insider confirms its use | Replace or isolate the proven access route and verify the successor arrangement |

The last 10 points come from a corroborated coordination link showing how those pillars act together.
That objective opens after two pillars are proven.
Coverage 100 requires all three pillars and this final link.
Repeated examination of the same evidence gives no new points.
An accusation, an anonymous unsupported allegation and a rival's uncorroborated claim give no points.

Every evidence fact retains its actual country, pillar, subject or institution, source and episode identity.
The displayed total never authorizes action against an unrelated person.
If an official has died, changed country or lost the relevant role, the decision must resolve the current institution and its successor connection.
It must not remove whoever happens to hold the office when the player clicks.

If a real relationship has ceased and no successor connection survives, its historical records and independently verified cessation can still complete that pillar's accounting.
The ordinary first inquiry establishes what actually existed or ceased, and a distinct current record or institutional check corroborates that the route has ended without a successor.
Record it as resolved and retired, with no declaration of guilt against a new person.
The standard and slow inquiry routes remain available for this purpose, including the historical coordination link.
Verified absence of every functioning route lets that pillar be recorded as severed once.
A stale paid project may still end neutrally and a correctly bound historical audit may then begin, with no invented success for the invalid project.
Whole-country authority retirement remains a neutral lifecycle outcome rather than a dismantlement victory.

Facts already established remain in the history after accommodation, leadership replacement, save and reload, and a normal change of government.
Newly discovered lodge participation can make an existing pillar incomplete without erasing old facts.
Coverage is then recalculated against the expanded local case and the reason is visible.
This is not an arbitrary loss of accumulated progress.
A proven false or compromised source can invalidate the fact that depended on it, but independent evidence remains valid.

Qualitative case facts explain which action is justified.
They do not introduce hidden extra gauges for funding, loyalty, confidence, rival favor or foreign reach that the player must manage separately.
Native stability, resources and the normal timers remain visible in their usual places.

## One developing influence spirit

The influence spirit changes stage while retaining its identity.
It carries the following proposed ordinary-disruption anchors:

| Influence | Stability modifier | Political power gain | Consumer-goods factor | Planning speed while command access is active |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0–39, an active network remains | −10 percentage points | −15% | +10% | −10% |
| 40–69 | −15 percentage points | −25% | +15% | −20% |
| 70–100 | −20 percentage points | −35% | +20% | −30% |

These are meaningful planning penalties that make investigation and bargains worth considering.
Consumer-goods factor means a relative modifier to that requirement, not a grant of 10, 15 or 20 percentage points of the country's entire economy.
The implementation agent must verify the applicable native modifier tokens and current consumer-goods rules before encoding the values.
The military component exists only when the network has an actual command-access route.
A civil procurement scandal cannot automatically impair every general.

Temporary spending and response costs are not added as an unlimited collection of national spirits.
One combined response-burden spirit displays active investigative and command commitments.
One finite aftermath spirit can represent recovery or the continuing cost of a resolved takeover.
There are at most three simultaneously visible Event 132 spirit slots, and mutually exclusive influence stages cannot stack.
Relevant commander-specific consequences need their own exact character ownership and removal conditions.

Accommodation exchanges the ordinary interference for a negotiated influence variant.
Its immediate stability, political power and command interference are reduced by approximately half, rounded to the nearest 5-point modifier step, with ties rounded toward the less severe penalty.
Contract-specific costs in Part 2 remain fully visible and cannot be halved again by this rule.
For example, an accommodated middle-band network uses stability −5 points, political power gain −10% and command planning speed −10% where applicable, alongside its chosen contract obligation.
Consumer-goods interference is replaced by the procurement contract's explicit burden where that contract is selected.
Administrative or command bargains retain consumer-goods factor +5% while the original funding route remains intact.

## The ordinary investigation

An unopposed network gains 5 Influence per 30 elapsed days.
At Influence 70 or above, intact leadership and intact funding increase that anchor to 10.
One active, valid domestic investigation reduces this natural growth by 5 for its actual covered interval.
Protected command prevents the command-access contribution from being used to accelerate an incident or takeover.
It does not conceal funding or remove civilian patrons.
Severing a pillar removes 20 Influence once and disables that pillar's associated growth or obstruction contribution.
Influence is clamped to 0–100.

Once all three functional pillars are severed and no residual contact, active compact or local rebuilding transaction remains, natural growth stops completely and residual Influence is capped at 20.
This final loss of organizational capacity makes recovery reachable even from Influence 100.
It grants no separate Chaos credit and does not replace the required evidence or consolidation period.

The elapsed-time calculation cannot depend on how often a callback runs.
Duplicate callbacks, multiple UI openings or tag switches must not accelerate either influence or recovery.
Refreshing an effect at a threshold updates its visible stage immediately through the owning lifecycle.

The country can usually run one investigative project, one response operation and one bilateral project concurrently.
A response operation includes command protection, an insider arrangement or a targeted removal.
The three-slot limit is a commitment model, not three extra public values.
The category tells the player which existing commitment must finish or be canceled before another starts.
The chosen six primary actions change with the current phase, proven case and imminent obligations.

Investigation always has a complete domestic route.
An intelligence agency can shorten relevant work and improve protection against interference, while agency compromise can remove that benefit until examined.
An agency never supplies guilt proof automatically and is never required for the final dismantlement.
Low-resource countries retain the slower administrative route in Part 2.

The ordinary course can include obstruction, recanted testimony, damaged trust, a failed replacement or worsening influence.
Those are baseline incidents with specific causes and recovery choices.
They are not evolution records.
One incident per 30-day local window is the normal pressure limit, reduced to 15 days only during an already prepared takeover or a funded operation with its own stated deadline.
Several related consequences of one incident share that incident's receipt and cannot be resolved twice.

## Four domestic outcomes

### Dismantlement

All three pillars must be severed, the coordination link resolved and local Influence reduced to 20 or less.
Any local rebuilding transaction must be canceled or resolved against its actual owner.
A 90-day consolidation period then verifies that replacement authority and procurement access remain in government hands.
The society's active interference stops when its functioning pillars have been removed.
The recovery spirit lasts through consolidation, starting at stability −5 points and political power gain −10%, then clears completely.
The period can extend to 120 days if a continuing war has disrupted the same affected institutions.
It cannot restart indefinitely because of unrelated global wars or foreign chapters.

The country succeeds locally even while a foreign chapter survives.
A fully closed local case has no remaining institutional contact through which that chapter can rebuild.
The local victory history and any earned achievement receipt survive global cleanup.

### Partial containment

One or two pillars are removed and the remaining access is constrained by protection or a negotiated restriction.
Interference falls with Influence, but the country has not completed dismantlement.
The unresolved pillar and its available next action remain visible.
This outcome is useful during a major external war and can later be completed.

If the settlement deliberately retains a specific domestic contact linked to a surviving foreign chapter, Part 3 permits one rebuilding attempt after 180 days.
The preserved contact, possible consequence and available sealing action must be visible when accepting that settlement.
No new local contact may be invented to undo a true completed dismantlement.

### Accommodation

The government accepts a concrete, renewable contract.
Interference falls immediately and the chosen 90-day obligation continues.
Influence cannot fall below 35 while that active bargain preserves the network's political access.
Evidence remains available for a later investigation.
Ending the compact removes its protection but does not reset its due date, refund consumed concessions or erase its record.
The contract family, renewal pressure and exit are fully specified in Part 2.

### Dependent government

The prepared Hidden Cabinet succeeds or the government accepts its political terms.
The country continues under player control with the existing national identity, real institutions and a larger, explicit society dependency.
This is a political outcome of the evolved struggle, not a routine accommodation upgrade.
It creates a route to recover authority, maintain the dependency or defeat an actual divided command where a real civil war occurred.
Part 4 owns that country package and its consequences.

## Continuity and ownership

Changing the country leader does not sever any pillar by itself.
A normal election, a focus reward, an adviser dismissal or an unrelated coup can change the person through whom a connection operated.
The case then follows the surviving institution and the evidence of succession.
Only a demonstrated loss of its leadership, funding or access removes that pillar.

Player control includes diplomacy, production, research, normal army management and the existing national focus tree.
The event applies documented burdens and country decisions.
It does not silently issue native battle orders, cancel arbitrary front orders, delete a leader or overwrite another event's authority.

Authority loss, annexation, capitulation, civil war, release, an actual nonhuman transformation and later human restoration each receive an explicit local lifecycle disposition.
Surviving countries preserve their own evidence and obligations.
Annexed or invalid institutions cannot execute delayed removal actions against a successor merely because a tag was reused.
A civilian-system retirement cancels active human projects neutrally through their exact owned records, retaining real history and resolving any paid obligations according to Part 2.
The same transition must not grant a successful dismantlement award.

The episode permits at most five active national chapter identities after actual foreign spread.
A temporary civil-war claimant and its original government share one national chapter identity for that limit and for foreign expansion, while each native actor retains exact local ownership.
The claimant cannot create an additional outbound lane or earn a new foreign-establishment award.

Agency Upgrade can contribute an actual relevant agency capability if the current country has received it.
Widespread Mutiny and Warlords can supply a current command vulnerability only where their own surviving facts support that connection.
Their causes and outcomes remain owned by those events.
The Book keeps its demonic government-capture identity, while this network's motives and methods remain human throughout the specified routes.
