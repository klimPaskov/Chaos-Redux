# Secret Society Influence: rival lodges and foreign chapters

## Three independent mutations

Rival Lodges, Across the Borders and The Hidden Cabinet change what the organization can do. They are independent evolutions of the same case graph. A government can face foreign connections without an internal lodge split, or a domestic Hidden Cabinet without international expansion. Ordinary discovery, obstruction, investigation, accommodation and recovery remain baseline progression.

Each evolution activates once for the episode, at a chapter whose actual circumstances support it. Its first activation produces the shared evolution record with that country as the actor. Later countries receive only the local manifestations justified by their own institutions and connections. They do not repeat the episode's activation reward, historical milestone or initial incident.

Eligibility must remain valid for 30 continuous days before its MTTH roll begins. The ordinary MTTH anchor is 90 days. The proposed multipliers below apply only when their stated facts exist, multiply together, and clamp the resulting MTTH to 45–180 days. MTTH remains a waiting distribution rather than a promised completion date.

| Evolution | Minimum Chaos and actual preconditions | Dynamic MTTH factors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rival Lodges | 200+, an active organization with leadership and funding, and two identifiable circles using different patrons or institutional access. A recent dispute or exposed connection must establish a genuine division. | ×0.75 when exposed leadership threatens both circles. ×0.75 after a recorded competing-contract or command dispute in the preceding 30 days. ×1.5 while a valid compact protects both circles' access. |
| Across the Borders | 400+, an active source chapter retaining institutional access and funding, a current usable diplomatic, subject, faction or exile connection, and an eligible counterpart with capacity for an attributable operation. | ×0.75 for an organizer actually sheltered through the connection in the preceding 60 days. ×0.75 when the chapter is using an established faction or subject institution. ×1.5 while the destination has an active cooperative inspection of that channel. |
| The Hidden Cabinet | 800+, the Influence, civilian-institution, financing, leadership and participating-command conditions in Part 4. | ×0.75 at Influence 90+. ×0.75 during an actual war. ×1.5 while an active inquiry examines a participating institution. |

A failed condition clears the uncommitted eligibility dwell. Raising Chaos, opening the category or reloading cannot preserve progress through an ineligible period. Before firing, recheck the actual country, chapter identity, evolution setting and essential facts. An evolved opening may begin at Influence 60 only when its real circumstances have been prepared. It still observes this evolution pacing. Starting at high Chaos supplies no invented foreign ally, competing circle or participating commander.

The setting for a disabled evolution blocks its new activation and expansion. Existing evidence, costs already consumed, historical consequences and viable recovery actions remain. Disabling Rival Lodges stops new competitive incidents and bargains. Disabling Across the Borders prevents new hostile operations and new chapter establishment, while agreed investigative work may finish. A hostile operation awaiting commitment retires its exact reservation and financing burden. The Hidden Cabinet's pre-war treatment appears in Part 4. None of these toggles automatically resolves a live native war.

Evolution activation, eligibility, new decisions and mutation logs generate zero direct Chaos. Actual resulting harm is accounted for once under Part 5.

## Rival Lodges changes the local case

The two circles are working identities within the fictional organization. Their names should describe their particular patrons or access rather than imply a real ethnicity, religion or historical society. They inherit the existing network's people, funding and influence. Splitting creates no additional Influence, new population, bonus income or second national spirit. The ordinary +5 or entrenched +10 growth anchor remains a single local calculation.

Use the most appropriate actual division:

| Local variant | Distinct access and conflict | Meaningful player opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Procurement patrons and command clients | One circle protects the diverted contracts. The other depends on officers receiving appointments or supplies. Their dispute concerns who receives a real benefit. | Protect command while tracing procurement, or turn a disappointed intermediary whose information reaches the other circle. |
| Civil-service patrons and agency protectors | One circle controls appointments. The other obstructs investigation through an existing agency relationship. This variant requires an actual agency. | Examine the compromised agency to restore its bounded assistance, or continue the full administrative route while securing an independent witness. |
| Domestic patrons and sheltered organizers | One circle retains local access while the other receives help through a real foreign connection. This variant requires active Across the Borders facts. | Secure foreign corroboration with consent, or sever the local connection without demanding the foreign government's cooperation. |

The circles may share a contractor or intermediary. Shared access does not make every accusation independent, and a shared contact cannot be removed twice for two rewards. The case records which actual participant each piece of evidence proves.

### Proof against both circles

The two public values stay Network Influence and Evidence Coverage. There are no lodge-favor or rivalry meters. The category can identify the currently relevant circle in the active task's purpose and tooltip.

Each pillar still contributes 15 points for its established lead stage and another 15 for independent corroboration, with the final coordination link worth 10. Once a second circle is demonstrably involved in that pillar, its lead stage requires all identified participants to be accounted for and its corroboration stage requires independent confirmation of each. A completed 30-point pillar can therefore return to 15 when new reliable participation leaves corroboration incomplete. Its original evidence remains true and recorded. The tooltip explains the expanded case, the unresolved participant and the next usable source.

A rival's unsupported allegation is a lead opportunity, with no automatic Coverage. A document independently checked against an actual transaction can establish a fact. The same testimony, copy of a document or mutually dependent witness cannot supply both proof steps. When one independently verified record genuinely covers both beneficiaries, it can satisfy those subjects without awarding duplicate Coverage.

Severing a pillar requires removing all its still-functioning local routes. Closing one circle's route gives a meaningful partial result, such as ending that route's obstruction, but the pillar's 20-point Influence reduction is awarded only when the whole local pillar is severed. This avoids farming two full reductions from one divided organization.

### Three approaches to the rivalry

**Independent casework** uses the ordinary investigation and removal actions. The investigator chooses the unresolved circle and pillar. Both can be exposed through administrative work without an agency or a bargain. Once both are corroborated, targeted removals can overlap with further investigation through the existing response lane. This approach takes longer but preserves freedom to dismantle every surviving route.

**Protected defection** uses Turn a known insider and its complete Part 2 quote. The investigator lane verifies testimony through the standard inquiry with one 25-political-power fee, or through the fee-free slow alternative. Both incur the stated one-time stability cost. The response lane concurrently protects the witness under the equipment or institutional-guarantee method for 90 days, extended to the quoted slow inquiry's completion when needed, up to 120 days. The participant must have actual access, and the one target-bound lead is awarded only when that inquiry completes with usable evidence. Independent corroboration follows through a distinct ordinary source. Finishing protection grants no second lead. Failure to protect the witness can delay corroboration or close that testimony route, while other administrative sources remain available. The consumed source identity prevents a repeated offer to the same person from creating new evidence.

**A lodge bargain** protects one circle's specified access in return for usable information about its rival. It occupies the applicable existing 90-day accommodation contract, including its stated cost and original renewal date. It cannot stack a second compact on top of another family. The grant strengthens the beneficiary by 10 Influence once because the government has actually protected an appointment or contract. Its testimony still requires independent checking. The bargain can be made once per local episode, and the information is tied to a finite set of real records.

The supported circle remains implicated where evidence proves its role. Removing its rival never makes it innocent. After the protected term, the government can complete the ordinary case against the survivor. Early repudiation follows the existing contract's declared backlash once. Switching patron, changing the contract description or cancelling a confirmation does not restart the term, recover the concession or refresh the information award.

An AI prefers independently attainable proof. It considers a bargain when a known command emergency or economic crisis makes the immediate relief material, and only when the surviving obligation is affordable. It must compare the resulting protected circle with its intended closure. The organization protects an exposed indispensable route before inventing another competitive incident. A defeated circle stays defeated, and the survivor cannot regenerate it to provide another bargain.

## Across the Borders requires an actual channel

A foreign connection must identify its source chapter, destination government, current institutional channel and purpose. A shared border, ideological similarity or high Chaos is insufficient by itself. Valid channels include a real faction or subject relationship being used by the network, a documented diplomatic contact, or an organizer who has actually received refuge. Their political relationship does not establish guilt by association. The case must show the organization's use of it.

A source retains leadership, a functioning funding route and institutional access throughout preparation. Each chapter can own one outbound operation at a time. A government can also conduct one bilateral response project through the third mission lane. Where one action belongs to both categories, it occupies both limits rather than opening a second hidden lane.

The graph supports at most five active national chapter identities, including accommodated and captured governments. A temporary claimant and original government within one internal war remain representations of their existing national chapter, not a sixth foreign establishment or a second outbound sponsor. First admission to another country also requires the shared three-crisis capacity test. Reserve a destination provisionally, then repeat both capacity checks immediately before establishment. A stale reservation cannot displace a different crisis or create an extra chapter.

### Financing and duration

Hostile society operations use a named, still-functioning diversion such as the confirmed procurement channel. They never silently spend a human government's political power. While committed, that source carries an additional consumer-goods factor burden of +5% inside its existing influence-spirit variant. This is the finite economic contribution funding the operation. It ends when the operation resolves or its funding is severed. A source without a usable civilian production base cannot claim this financing and cannot launch that operation.

The operation records the source, intended recipient, exact channel, start, due date, financing contribution and one completion receipt. Its ordinary duration is 90 days, reduced to 60 for an already functioning refuge channel and increased to 120 when an active inspection impedes that same connection. The shown date is fixed at commitment. A later changed condition can interrupt the work or provide one declared delay, with a maximum total duration of 120 days. It cannot turn into an indefinitely renewed hidden mission.

Voluntary government sponsorship has an explicit accepted quote instead. The proposed anchor is 50 political power for a 90-day interference operation against an already identified foreign chapter's institution, reduced to 60 days with an established refuge and extended to 120 by active inspection. Sponsorship preserves the source society's access and adds 10 local Influence once. It produces the same traceable case evidence as hostile society funding. Its target suffers one defined obstruction incident within the ordinary incident budget, not a fabricated war or free leader removal.

### Foreign operations and government responses

| Operation or response | Required facts and result | Failure and closure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish a chapter | A financed source uses its real channel to reach a genuine local appointment, contractor or command contact. Successful completion establishes a destination chapter at Influence 35, with normal opening vulnerabilities applied up to 55. | Severed funding, lost contact, invalid government, failed capacity or a successful interception ends the attempt. No chapter or establishment Chaos is awarded. |
| Shelter an organizer | An exposed organizer actually has access to a willing refuge through the channel. Movement preserves their identity and the evidence already proving it. | Refuge refusal or loss of the route leaves the person at their real location. Their failure to travel cannot generate a substitute organizer. |
| Cooperative case review | Both governments consent to a named case and each accepts a 35-political-power quote. Work takes 90 days without agencies, or 60 with useful uncompromised agency assistance. | Either government can refuse before commitment. Later neutral invalidation follows the recorded refund rule. Failure does not close either domestic investigation route. |
| Close the local channel | Ordinary proof and removal establish the domestic connection and sever its present access. Local authorities act only on their own institution. | A foreign refusal cannot block local severance. Historical foreign evidence remains, while this exact channel stops financing or carrying operations. |

Cooperation shares an independently corroborated fact that the participants actually established. It does not award 30 points to every pillar, reveal unrelated chapters or let one actor remove another's officials. An unassisted country can contribute procurement records, travel evidence, verified appointments or protected testimony. Agency absence changes pace and protection, not permission to participate.

The initial foreign report identifies the detected connection at the level supported by evidence. A country cannot use secret global knowledge to select a hidden target. The source government's voluntary support or its known financing channel leaves attributed evidence. This allows a later investigation to establish responsibility without automatic world opinion penalties, fabricated faction membership or a second diplomatic system.

## Local victory, residual contacts and one rebuilding attempt

A country that severs all three local pillars, resolves coordination, removes every residual contact and completes consolidation has a true local victory. Foreign chapters may survive. They cannot undo that victory by waiting, and no new local contact may be invented to justify a return.

A partial settlement can deliberately preserve one specific domestic contact linked to one surviving foreign source. Before accepting it, the player sees the surviving access, the earliest rebuilding date and the sealing action. The settlement remains partial, continues to occupy its local crisis slot and retains the remaining influence burden. It does not receive a completed-dismantlement award.

That one contact can support at most one rebuilding attempt, and never before 180 days have passed since the settlement. The named foreign chapter must still have leadership, funding, its actual connection and a free outbound lane. The local contact must still hold the relevant access. At the earliest date, begin a normal financed 60–120-day operation. Do not instantly restore the organization. The defender can investigate or remove the named contact through the normal no-agency route during the warning.

Success restores only the pillar and access that this operation actually rebuilt. Influence rises by 20, capped at the maximum appropriate to the resulting active organization. Old evidence remains true. A new participant may require fresh corroboration, with an explained Coverage change. Rebuilding cannot recreate a defeated lodge, restore every severed pillar, or begin the Hidden Cabinet directly. It earns no second first-establishment or local-victory reward.

Failure, interception or successful sealing consumes that contact's one attempt permanently. Changing its display name, chapter leader, cosmetic country identity or sponsorship cannot refresh it. A lost foreign source ends the pending attempt. A genuinely new episode is outside this fire-once case graph.

When the last functioning chapter, residual contact, funded operation and pending mutation has retired, close the graph's active lifecycle. Preserve evidence history, actual consequences, completed local victories and finite earned aftermath. International closure is a distinct result under Part 5, while every earlier valid domestic victory remains intact.
