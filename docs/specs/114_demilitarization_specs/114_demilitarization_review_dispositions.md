## Accepted tradeoffs

A border capital never becomes an exemption under Total. A country can legitimately have no legal domestic deployment state. Exact existing-army preservation is therefore a required native proof gate. Unsupported preservation does not authorise a replacement army or a weakened border rule.

Total can leave a poor country unable to afford a constructive response. Consolidation improves cohesion temporarily but does not remove geographic crowding. Scarcity is an explicit harsh gameplay outcome at this stage, with honest costs and disabled reasons.

An Event 096 overlap achievement can be ineligible when the current lock has too little time remaining or historic unit maturity cannot be established. The implementation must not fabricate age or extend the independent crisis to guarantee eligibility.

Native proof, current owner integration, numeric balance, probability results, and visual production remain implementation work. Missing evidence was retained as a gate rather than converted into a nominal flag, reserve deduction, generic modifier, or easier achievement.
