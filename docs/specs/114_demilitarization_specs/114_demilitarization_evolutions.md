# Demilitarization: evolutions and entry paths

The three evolutions form a cumulative sequence. Territorial withdrawal remains the baseline lifecycle. Ordinary war suspension, peace restoration, and recovery are not additional evolutions.

All labels are working design labels, not final localisation. The brief supplies the three evolution concepts. The thresholds and pacing below are parent-authored design choices within the requested expansion.

## Progression map

At the first firing, choose the highest enabled sequential opening stage whose Chaos floor and current country or army prerequisites are satisfied. Waive only the live stage-age requirements. There is no additional random opening-stage draw. Apply all included stages in one opening transaction and retain the normal first 14-peacetime-day review interval. The stage-specific opening descriptions below follow this selector.

| Evolution | World Chaos floor | Additional active-event conditions | Starting live MTTH | Principal change |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| I. Deeper Demilitarization | 200 | Global regime active for 30 days and at least one peaceful enrolled country controls a border state. | 90 days | Add one inward layer from the current original border set. |
| II. Desertion Crisis | 400 | Evolution I active, at least 30 days since its activation, and a peaceful enrolled country has an eligible fielded army. | 120 days | Start the cohesion, review, response, and formation-loss system. |
| III. Total Demilitarization | 800 | Evolution II active for 60 days and at least one peaceful enrolled country controls territory. | 150 days | Add all controlled non-capital states to the previous territorial footprint. |

One evolution is assigned to each used Chaos tier. The unused tier at 600 allows an extended period of combined geographic and military strain before the near-total territorial stage becomes possible.

Live MTTH is a starting pacing target, not a scheduled guarantee. The probability worker must inspect the actual shared implementation rather than assume a conversion between MTTH and a per-tick percentage.

Once activated, an evolution remains part of this event's history and behaviour even if global Chaos later falls. Successful containment changes cohesion and actual military outcomes. It does not erase the world's knowledge of an evolved territorial rule.

### Eligibility and pacing

Eligibility checks do not generate Chaos. They also do not silently apply effects.

For Evolution I, use MTTH 60 days when at least three peaceful enrolled countries would gain additional inland restricted states. Use 135 days when at most two countries gain such states and no actual formation needs relocation from that added footprint. Use 90 days otherwise. These factors come from current enrolled-actor summaries.

For Evolution II, use MTTH 80 days when at least three peaceful enrolled armies each have at least 20,000 eligible soldiers and less than one third of controlled states usable, or when at least one relevant enrolled country has an active compatible military-collapse crisis. Use 160 days when every relevant eligible army has fewer than 20,000 soldiers, stability at least 60%, and war support at least 35%. Use 120 days otherwise.

For Evolution III, use MTTH 100 days after a documented Event 114 formation dissolution in the last 180 calendar days, or when at least three peaceful enrolled countries have cohesion below 45 at both of their two latest completed reviews. Use 150 days otherwise. A recent dissolution supplies a pacing factor, not an extra evolution Chaos charge.

The fast branch has priority, the slow branch applies only when the fast branch is false, and the stated default applies otherwise. Select one MTTH for each evolution. Do not multiply these branches together. Any shared pacing adjustment must preserve a minimum live MTTH of 45 days. Do not reroll eligibility from an unrelated daily country loop.

## Evolution I: Deeper Demilitarization

The frontier withdrawal spreads into the immediate interior. A country with substantial depth loses forward camps and rail staging areas. A thin country may discover that the supposed second line is another frontier.

For an already active event, recompute the border snapshot and its one-hop inland neighbours for each peaceful country. Apply only the newly required Event 114 contributions. A state already required by baseline remains the same obligation. A state already demilitarized by another system gains an overlapping claim without being charged as a fresh physical restriction.

The local report focuses on a garrison or staging area that has actually become unusable. It distinguishes the original frontier from the newly affected interior. If the country gains no additional restricted state, show the evolved status in the category without inventing a local evacuation story.

The response system continues to use ordinary military planning. The player cannot pay to shrink the belt. Better service conditions and consolidation become more valuable because legal space can now be scarce.

Containment of this stage means maintaining a coherent, supplied army within the remaining usable geography. The territorial rule itself returns after each peace.

### Pre-fire evolved opening

If the opening selector reaches Evolution I at Chaos 200 or above, the one-hop belt is included from the start. The age requirement belongs to live progression and is waived for this opening path.

Create the actual evolution record with the true opening date. Do not fabricate an earlier activation or suggest that the country endured an unseen first withdrawal. The opening report describes the deeper extent as the initial discovered condition.

## Evolution II: Desertion Crisis

Withdrawal begins to undermine service itself. The army's nominal structure can remain intact while its real strength falls. An advanced crisis can remove a formation entirely.

On active-event activation, initialise Army Cohesion once for each eligible country, enrol its actual divisions, and schedule the first review after 14 accumulated peacetime days. Show the service-condition idea and response choices immediately. Do not inflict a surprise full-division dissolution at activation.

An enrolled island country with no land-border states can still develop service discontent. The worldwide breakdown of military purpose has spread beyond the original frontier geometry. Its absence of crowding gives it a more favourable pressure profile.

Countries currently at war receive this system when they next become peaceful. They begin with a valid country-specific cohort and the normal first review interval. Existing military losses from other events are not retroactively relabelled as desertion.

The initial report describes evidence such as incomplete musters, abandoned kit, or men failing to return. A later report identifies actual measured personnel loss. A severe formation report is reserved for a real dissolution receipt.

### Pre-fire evolved opening

At Chaos 400 or above, the opening selector includes Evolutions I and II when both are enabled and their current non-age prerequisites hold. Apply the current territorial belt and initialise cohesion from the actual opening conditions. Give the same 14 peacetime-day interval before the first review.

Starting at an evolved stage is not a licence to skip the player's first opportunity to respond. Native existing mutiny or poor national conditions can make the first review dangerous, but a whole-formation loss still requires its own notice and resolution.

## Evolution III: Total Demilitarization

Provincial military space closes. A deep inland capital can become the sole remaining domestic deployment state. A capital already inside the border or Deeper belt does not gain an exemption.

For an active event, compare the old and new territorial sets, then apply the new contribution. Keep the current cohesion, programme costs, formation notices, personnel-return receipts, cooldowns, and loss history. Do not reinitialise the country as a fresh crisis.

Total increases ongoing service pressure. It does not automatically destroy a percentage of the army. The player sees the concentration problem through restricted space, existing supply conditions, and more difficult cohesion maintenance.

The report should use the country's actual condition. Where a capital refuge exists, describe crowded accommodation and the closure of provincial barracks. Where no refuge exists, describe the comprehensive restriction without depicting a move into a supposedly usable capital.

A broad news item is appropriate only after a materially large near-total application. If every relevant country becomes belligerent before the evolution commits, revalidation defers activation. If the stage has already committed and a country enters war before its local footprint applies, preserve the global milestone and defer that local material report and Chaos until a genuine peaceful application.

### Pre-fire evolved opening

At Chaos 800 or above, the opening selector includes Total when all three sequential evolutions are enabled and their current non-age prerequisites hold. All earlier territorial rules remain part of the result. Cohesion is initialised once and the first review interval is preserved.

One opening can therefore be geographically severe without immediately erasing divisions. Its report and category must make both the extent and the available responses clear.

## Disabled and interrupted progression

The sequence has explicit predecessor requirements. If Evolution I is disabled before activation, II and III remain unavailable through this sequence. If II is disabled before activation, III remains unavailable. Preview text must identify the missing prerequisite rather than present a mysterious zero chance.

A disabled evolution must not receive a recorded flag, advance the stage, or unlock downstream behaviour. Re-enabling it restarts eligibility from the real current state and the shared system's legitimate timing rules.

An evolution that was already active remains a historical fact. Follow the shared settings contract for existing content instead of treating a future-enable checkbox as a destructive rollback command.

| Country report | Describe the actual local consequence and its relevant response. |
| Event Details | Explain the premise of widening withdrawal and unstable service. Put precise costs and consequences in tooltips. |
| Chaos History | Attribute a change to an actual new territorial footprint, measured desertion threshold, or dissolution. |

An evolved opening can produce a material opening charge, but not one separate charge for each stage variable. Country-level effects use a country actor. The global evolution and worldwide opening use the verified shared no-actor convention.

## Replay differences

A country with deep inland territory may find Evolution I manageable and struggle only when Total arrives. A compact continental country can face restricted space from the first firing and reach severe service pressure much earlier. An island state can largely bypass the geographic opening but still need to manage its army after Evolution II.

A recently demobilising country should receive contextual writing about expectations of release. A long-service army should emphasise the loss of purpose and professional continuity. These variations change narrative emphasis and pressure inputs that are already defined. They do not create separate event trees or hidden immunity rules.
