# Demilitarization: achievements

These five achievements reward sustained military management, difficult geography, recovery, and correct coexistence with another crisis. A normal opening acknowledgement or first paid decision is insufficient.

All challenge names are working design labels, not final localisation. Proposed IDs are planning identifiers that must be checked against the root achievement registry before implementation.

## Shared tracking

Evaluate achievements for a legitimate player country through the existing achievement system. AI uses the same mechanics, but does not receive player achievements. Respect the mod's existing global eligibility rules.

Record the attempt's starting country, actual controlled state set, eligible army manpower, formation identities where needed, current evolution, and start date. Use committed gameplay receipts and native observed state. Mature means 60 continuous calendar days of verified service identity, as defined in the desertion specification. Maturity qualifies an attempt at entry. Maintain each challenge's minimum current formation count at every review, while A114-02 also preserves its specifically tracked identities.

Pause rules and resets are specified per challenge. Each timed challenge must retain its stated minimum eligible army and formation count, as well as its required proportion of starting or recovered manpower, at scheduled reviews, verified army-change notifications, and the final check. Falling below an applicable floor resets the timed attempt immediately when observed. An empty or temporarily ineligible army cannot let a calendar attempt clock continue while reviews are frozen. Never infer a clean historical interval from only the final state. Console changes, administrative resets, scripted gifts used only for a validation fixture, stale targets, and duplicated receipts cannot qualify.

A tag change or annexation ends the attempt unless the shared system has a verified legitimate identity-continuity rule. Multiplayer country switching must not combine one country's completed interval with another country's army.

## A114-01: sustained service under restriction

**Proposed ID:** chaosx_114_sustained_service

**Title direction.** Emphasise an intact military institution that has endured prolonged peacetime restriction.

**Description direction.** Explain the duration, cohesion floor, substantial army requirement, and successful use of different constructive responses.

**Eligibility.** Desertion Crisis is active. The country has at least five mature eligible formations and 20,000 eligible fielded soldiers. At least one third of its controlled states are restricted by Event 114.

**Unlock.** Complete 180 consecutive peacetime days while cohesion is at least 70 at every scheduled review. Preserve at least 80% of the starting fielded manpower at the final check. Complete both service conditions and cadre retention during the interval. Suffer no spontaneous full dissolution.

**Reset conditions.** War, cohesion below the floor at a review, loss of the geographic restriction threshold for more than one review interval, a spontaneous dissolution, or an invalid country identity ends the attempt. Ordinary partial desertion is allowed and must be genuinely recovered.

**Difficulty and visibility.** Hard, visible. Long service, real resource expenditure, and maintaining an army under restriction make it more demanding than passive survival.

**Tracking.** Store the initial manpower and eligible formation count, review streak, both distinct programme receipts, and last valid geographic check. Count each completion once.

**Art.** An intact regimental standard with a repaired fastening, A114-A01.

## A114-02: successful withdrawal into the interior

**Proposed ID:** chaosx_114_inland_withdrawal

**Title direction.** Emphasise a coherent army surviving the loss of its forward military geography.

**Description direction.** Explain actual relocation from newly restricted states and preservation of substantial forces afterward.

**Eligibility.** The country receives at least three physically new Deeper DMZ states, has at least 20,000 eligible fielded soldiers, and has legal same-controller interior destinations.

**Unlock.** Preserve at least three pre-existing formations that actually moved out of newly restricted states into legal interior states. Those formations must together contain at least 10,000 soldiers before relocation. Maintain their legitimate identities for 60 consecutive peacetime days and at least 85% of their combined pre-relocation manpower at the final check.

**Reset conditions.** War during the interval, a tracked formation disappearing or transferring owner, a duplicated movement receipt, or loss of valid legal destination accounting. Routine internal movement among legal states does not reset the interval.

**Difficulty and visibility.** Hard, visible. It requires an actual geographic event and identifiable formations, rather than three fabricated units sitting in a capital.

**Tracking.** Capture the true added Deeper footprint, original formation identities and manpower, observed evacuation results, and subsequent survival. A state already demilitarized by another source does not count as physically new.

**Art.** A military boot beside a closed frontier barrier and intact field pack, A114-A02.

## A114-03: an army in the remaining capital region

**Proposed ID:** chaosx_114_capital_cantonments

**Title direction.** Emphasise the endurance of a substantial army inside a sharply constrained military space.

**Description direction.** Explain the actual capital-only condition and the duration of successful service.

**Eligibility.** Total Demilitarization leaves exactly one legal controlled deployment state, which is the current controlled capital. The country controls at least six states and has at least 30,000 eligible fielded soldiers across five mature formations.

**Unlock.** Maintain the true capital-only condition for 120 consecutive peacetime days. Keep cohesion at least 50 at every review, preserve at least 80% of the starting fielded manpower at the final check, and suffer no spontaneous dissolution.

**Reset conditions.** War, an additional legal state appearing, a capital move, losing the capital, failure of the cohesion or army requirement, or full dissolution. A country whose border capital is itself restricted is ineligible for this particular challenge.

**Difficulty and visibility.** Very hard, visible. It combines Total's geometry, sustained service pressure, and a meaningful army.

**Tracking.** The legal-state set must agree with the actual physical DMZ and source rules throughout the attempt. A UI label alone is not evidence of capital-only deployment.

**Art.** A barracks gate enclosing a folded standard with a small civic dome behind it, A114-A03.

## A114-04: recovery after a real formation loss

**Proposed ID:** chaosx_114_rebuilt_service

**Title direction.** Emphasise rebuilding military organisation after a costly breakdown.

**Description direction.** Distinguish spontaneous formation loss from a planned disband and require sustained recovery.

**Eligibility.** A committed Event 114 spontaneous dissolution removed at least 5,000 actual soldiers, or at least 10% of a pre-incident eligible army of 20,000 or more.

**Unlock.** After that receipt, restore eligible fielded manpower to the post-incident army plus 90% of the actual personnel lost in the dissolution, with the added amount rounded up to whole soldiers, through legitimately supplied forces. Then maintain at least that army level and cohesion at least 70 at every scheduled review and the final check across 90 consecutive peacetime days, with no further spontaneous dissolution.

**Reset conditions.** Falling below the recovered army target or cohesion floor at a review resets the 90-day streak. A new dissolution creates a new immutable target and restarts the streak against the highest outstanding target. It cannot lower an older recovery requirement. War resets the streak but preserves the qualifying historic incident. Administrative army reconstruction, a voluntary consolidation, a combat loss, or a unit removed by another event cannot create eligibility.

**Difficulty and visibility.** Very hard, hidden until a qualifying loss. The player must recover from a real sacrifice and maintain the result.

**Tracking.** Preserve the original loss receipt, actual manpower baseline, source of the loss, subsequent native army observations, and the clean recovery interval. Delayed personnel returns can contribute only once when paid.

**Art.** A repaired sleeve insignia on closed service kit, A114-A04.

## A114-05: maintain service under independent military restrictions

**Proposed ID:** chaosx_114_independent_conditions

**Title direction.** Emphasise competent military management while two separate institutions constrain the army.

**Description direction.** Explain that Event 114's service crisis and Event 096's template restriction must both remain genuinely active during the interval.

**Eligibility.** Desertion Crisis and Event 096's legitimate template lock are simultaneously active. The country has at least 20,000 eligible fielded soldiers and five mature formations. The 096 owner must establish more than 60 days of remaining legitimate lock duration at attempt start. The achievement cannot extend that duration or delay an owner-driven release.

A simultaneous first enrolment and 096 opening can be ineligible when formation age is unknown and must be observed conservatively. The challenge rewards a proven established army entering a sufficiently long overlap. Do not invent earlier service history to make every opening qualify.

**Unlock.** Remain at peace under both systems for 60 consecutive days. Maintain cohesion at least 60 at every review, preserve at least 80% of the starting fielded manpower, complete a cadre programme using an existing formation without editing its locked template, and suffer actual Event 114 personnel departures totaling at least 0.25% of the attempt's starting eligible fielded manpower, rounded down with a minimum of one soldier. This is attainable from one full orderly-band incident and scales with the real army.

**Reset conditions.** War, either qualifying system becoming inactive before 60 days, an unlawful lock clear, template editing that should be blocked, or a broken country identity. A legitimate owner-driven expiry simply ends the attempt, without declaring a fault.

**Difficulty and visibility.** Hard, hidden until both systems overlap. The player must manage actual attrition of service while the independent template restriction remains effective.

**Tracking.** Read the 096 owner state and legitimate expiry. Event 114 must have no write path that clears it. Preserve the personnel-loss and cadre-completion receipts. The achievement does not invent or replace Event 096's separate army-allowance condition.

**Art.** A service ledger secured by two distinct clasps, A114-A05.

## Completion and presentation

Use the single existing root achievement registry and its native completed, grey, and not-eligible states. Every achievement receives final implementation-written localisation and its own original subject.

The artwork is not a substitute for historical tracking. Validate each achievement with a positive case and a near-miss that differs by one meaningful requirement. Use the general validation matrix for war, save/load, identity, and duplicate-receipt cases.

The shared registry and real owner hooks decide whether an achievement is currently implementable. Missing history or unit-identity evidence is a blocker, not permission to weaken the condition to a final-day snapshot.
