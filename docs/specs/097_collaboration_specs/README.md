## Acceptance basis

### Explicit user decisions

These come from the user's event brief and are fixed:

- Event 097 Collaboration is Minor Repeatable at Chaos level 1, in the Intelligence cluster as a Medium member, with status To Be Reworked.
- Every country gains collaboration against every other country, globally and symmetrically, through the normal collaboration mechanic. It ignores ideology, alliances, relations, and plausibility.
- The event is repeatable. Each firing deepens the networks, and values stack only as far as the normal system allows.
- There is no separate Collaboration currency or large event-specific management system. The normal collaboration mechanic is the event.
- The four evolutions, their names, their Chaos requirements of 200, 400, 600, and 800, and their described behavior.
- The connection to Event 095 Occupation Revolt, where installed governments can become targets of a national restoration uprising if enough opposition survives.
- The connection to the Treaty of Tordesillas Restoration event, whose spheres can rely on collaborator governments.

### Recorded parent acceptance for the nested variant

The nested specification parts, matrices, handoffs and prompts expand the brief under recorded parent planning acceptance in the linked plan dispositions.
That record names the exploration, architecture, decision and probability source reviews and improvement-loop closure folded in on 6 October.
It explicitly leaves native and probability conclusions unresolved, and is not user review or live MCP evidence.
The imported variant's separate parent acceptance is recorded in its decision register.
The conflict table above limits reliance on either acceptance record until the overlap is resolved.

It includes the stance choice, layer sizes, the single network band ladder, every number, both decision categories, Collaborators Unmasked and its national version at restoration, Open Gates, Turned Regime, the Installed Administration lifecycle, local auxiliaries, the restoration pressure contract, the reports to occupiers and to original countries, the Event 052 and Event 063 links, the single Tordesillas entry point, the Chaos impact map with its rate guard, the super-event, and the achievements. All numbers are tuning anchors that must pass the balance and probability reviews named in the package. Payment rules that depend on what a country holds, such as trains or trucks for A3 and convoys or trucks for A5, are part of this accepted design and are not fallbacks.
