# 097 Collaboration
## Part 7: AI behavior and Chaos consequences

## AI and human parity

AI countries receive the same native grants, caps, thresholds, spent opportunities, and government-readiness rules as human countries. No AI choice is required to accept the universal grant. A report's acknowledgment weight is unrelated to the likelihood of the world receiving collaboration.

A country cannot opt out because it is democratic, likes the target, shares a faction, lacks an agency, or considers the target historically implausible. Those considerations may affect what it later chooses to do with a network, but not whether the network exists.

Likewise, human countries do not receive an extra layer for being players. This global event already affects their homeland directly and must remain consistent across multiplayer peers.

## Strategy does not become automatic aggression

A deep network can improve an AI's assessment of an already plausible campaign. It should not make a country declare a distant, unwinnable war solely to use its collaboration value.

Existing military strength, access, supply, naval reach, allies, threats, war goals, and native diplomatic constraints remain part of strategic planning. Event 097 does not supply free war goals or remove ideological restrictions on war declaration.

The event should avoid competing with native AI by repeatedly forcing occupation laws, changing faction membership, or replacing war plans. The specific evolved consequences are applied through their objective conditions. Broader strategy uses the native systems that already own those choices.

## Behavior by situation

| Situation | Intended AI behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Peaceful country with many networks | Preserve ordinary diplomacy and prepare according to actual threats |
| Plausible invasion with deep collaboration | Value a successful conquest more highly, without ignoring military feasibility |
| Occupier receiving an early handover | Use the improved native compliance, but maintain the garrison and policies the real occupation still requires |
| Sponsor with a Fifth Column opportunity | Receive the automatic native breakthrough when conditions are met, without paying for or selecting it |
| Defender nearing a breakthrough | Continue normal defensive and recovery behavior, with no free loyalty reset |
| Victor with government readiness | Make a deliberate native government-versus-direct-occupation choice |
| Existing collaborator regime | Follow its actual native subject, faction, defense, and occupation rules |

## Government choice policy

The government's strategic choice must be meaningful and reproducible. The following score is a design preference for adapting the native AI creation choice, not a claim about an existing engine weight field.

Begin at 50 for establishing a legally available, qualifying native collaboration government. Add 25 if the occupation's garrison fulfillment is below 90%. Add 20 if the sponsor already administers at least five other occupied political countries. Add 20 when an accepted treaty role specifically calls for indirect control in this sphere. Subtract 30 when direct control of this bundle is required for an active, identified strategic objective. Subtract 25 while disputed control or an ongoing front makes immediate government creation operationally unsafe.

Do not apply a factor twice because several states satisfy it. A single occupied-country bundle receives one score. Use only verified, available native or accepted project inputs. An unavailable input cannot silently be assumed true.

If the final score is at least 60, prefer installation. Otherwise prefer retaining direct occupation. The default score of 50 deliberately avoids creating a government automatically without a concrete administrative or strategic reason. A tie at 60 favors installation. Legality always overrides preference.

Examples of intended choices are:

| Case | Score | Preference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stable single occupation without pressure | 50 | Retain direct occupation |
| Garrison shortage only | 75 | Install |
| Broad empire of at least five other occupations | 70 | Install |
| Garrison shortage but an essential direct-control objective | 45 | Retain |
| Treaty indirect-control role with no dispute | 70 | Install |
| Treaty preference but an unsafe disputed front | 45 | Retain |

The strategic-objective penalty must identify a real objective rather than using an unqualified flag that every country has forever. Similarly, a treaty bonus requires the actual accepted partition role, not simply Spanish or Portuguese nationality.

Human players receive the real choice without having the AI score exposed as a management meter. The score does not alter the government's native costs or grant a reward for choosing the favored route.

## Reconsidering a postponed government

A declined or postponed native offer remains subject to ordinary native availability. Relevant changes such as a resolved front or a significant garrison shortage can justify reconsideration. Event 097 should not send a daily request or create a political-power loop.

If the native interface does not expose an appropriate AI choice surface, the implementation must resolve that integration explicitly. It must not invent a second hidden government that bypasses the player-visible route.

## Chaos from the universal infiltration

The new worldwide penetration is a genuine instability event even before any war is won. A normal firing that produces at least one actual native collaboration increase adds **5 Chaos once globally**.

Add a further **5 Chaos** when that firing causes at least half of the participating target countries to acquire at least one foreign network crossing from below 50 to at least 50 collaboration. Count distinct target countries, not directed pairs. The maximum direct network-manifestation impact is therefore 10 Chaos per legitimate normal firing.

For example, in a snapshot of 20 valid countries, at least 10 distinct targets must experience that crossing for the additional 5. Several sponsors crossing 50 inside one country still count as one target. An already deep target does not count again simply because another firing raises its existing deep networks further, unless a different network genuinely crosses the threshold in that firing.

This scaling reflects how widely the new grant establishes deep foreign penetration. It does not depend on ideology, which country opened a popup first, or a fixed list of major powers.

A duplicate call, all-capped no-op, report acknowledgment, or evolution activation without a new grant produces no manifestation Chaos.

## Reversal and failed consolidation

The design's reversal sources are real peace, restoration, liberation, and independence. They already have shared accounting. A restored homeland should not receive both an invented Event 097 reversal and several duplicate versions of the same shared outcome.

Failed consolidation is expressed by losing control, losing a native government, or being unable to complete an offer. There is no extra positive Chaos merely for failing a UI action. If failure produces a real war, coup, or other shared consequence, that owning system accounts for it.

This event does not directly add deaths, air contamination, or atrocity responsibility. Fictional descriptions of disloyal officials must not be converted into invented casualty totals. Actual casualties and destructive policies remain with the systems that produce them.

## Probability and pacing acceptance

The event's displayed weight remains a relative weight. Neither 1,000 nor the AI government score is a normalized probability. Exact selection chances require the complete live pool, shared timer, recovery behavior, cluster handling, and enabled settings.

The implementation review must evaluate baseline availability, repeated firings, saturated worlds, evolution timing, disabled stages, and mixed Intelligence-cluster membership. Human-readable intent is not a substitute for the required probability analysis of the implemented surfaces.
