# 097 Collaboration
## Part 5: Collaboration Governments

## An administration becomes a regime

Evolution IV turns deeply prepared collaborators into an easier route to actual native collaboration governments after a qualifying defeat. The government is tied to the power that installed it. It is not an independent country that merely has good relations with its sponsor.

The route remains part of the normal occupation and collaboration-government system. It does not create a separate class of Event 097 puppets with copied modifiers, a new loyalty resource, or a menu for assembling ministerial offices.

Ordinary collaboration governments remain available whenever native rules already permit them. Event 097 must not remove that access before Evolution IV.

## Qualifying victory

A sponsor must have at least **60 prepared collaboration** inside the target immediately before its qualifying defeat. Use a verified pre-defeat observation so that any native consumption or conversion of collaboration during capitulation does not erase eligibility. A post-defeat zero must not be mistaken for proof that no network existed. It must possess an actual eligible occupation from the relevant victory and satisfy the native route's territorial and subject-relationship conditions, apart from the specific readiness and ideology barriers the evolution is designed to address.

A country that only declared war without securing any eligible territory has no administration to install. Neither does a distant ally with a strong network but no lawful control of the proposed area.

Use the real native beneficiary and post-defeat territorial situation. The event does not assign every conquered state to the country that fired the global event, the faction leader, or whichever country has the highest collaboration value.

A target being close to defeat is not enough. Evolution III handles that stage. Government readiness follows actual defeat or an already existing, verifiable defeated occupation.

## The readiness intervention

Let Q be the installed native route's actual compliance requirement for collaboration-government readiness. Q is not assumed to be identical to the 80-point early-administration ceiling in Evolution II.

At a qualifying defeat, Evolution IV can raise compliance in the eligible sponsor-held homeland area toward the level necessary for that native route. It never lowers an already higher value. The purpose is to remove the long administrative wait where the network was already strong enough to supply a functioning replacement government.

The readiness intervention is offered once per sponsor and defeated political identity. It covers the eligible territorial bundle held at that qualifying opportunity. States gained later do not trigger a second free worldwide readiness offer. Their later inclusion follows native occupation and government rules.

This is stronger than Evolution II and has its own consequence: it prepares a native government after a real defeat. It is not a second payment of the same early-capture handover. If native collaboration conversion has already made the territory ready, no additional compliance is needed and no extra economic bonus is awarded.

The intervention does not force a government to be created. A human victor can retain direct occupation.

The harsh-rule deferral from Part 3 applies to the readiness intervention: states of the bundle held under a harsher-than-standard occupation rule or holding an occupier-run camp receive no readiness raise until that rule is relaxed or the camp closed, and the intervention stays unspent for them.
A brutal victor can still install a government through the ordinary native route once its compliance gets there on its own.

## Native eligibility still matters

Meeting a compliance value does not establish ownership of foreign-controlled states, erase another power's subject relationship, settle disputed borders, or make a nonexistent target identity valid.

The offer must identify the actual territorial bundle that the native route would use. If that route would also sweep in territory outside the sponsor's lawful control, creation must not proceed until the native constraints are satisfied. The event cannot cure that problem by transferring somebody else's land.

Where the native route uses an aggregate compliance measure, the intervention must work with that measure and its legitimate state set. It must not use one high-compliance sample state as proof that the entire government is eligible.

## Democracy and universal participation

The user's global promise includes democracies and other countries normally restricted from collaboration-government creation. Evolution IV must provide a narrowly scoped route for a qualifying Event 097 government without changing the country's ideology or broadly rewriting diplomatic rules.

A democratic sponsor should not gain general permission to justify unrelated wars, puppet any country at a peace conference, or ignore all faction restrictions. The exception is the creation of this qualifying native collaboration government from an actual deep network and a lawful defeated occupation.

A generic puppet with a similar name does not satisfy this requirement. Neither does an invisible ideology change followed by changing it back. If the installed engine cannot support the narrow native route, this remains an explicit implementation blocker. It is not permission to quietly remove democratic access from the design.

The same standard applies to DLC configurations. The target behavior is the specified native mechanic in supported project configurations. Where the native capability itself is unavailable, implementation must report the exact unsupported configuration and obtain a design decision rather than inventing a substitute.

## The victor's choice

Use the native government-creation surface where possible. A qualifying notification may direct the player to it, but it should not create a second permanent management interface.

The meaningful choice is between keeping direct occupation and installing the prepared government. Direct occupation preserves direct territorial administration and the responsibilities that come with it. Installation delegates administration to a tied local regime under the native relationship.

Use the real native costs, transfers, and benefits. Do not charge an arbitrary additional political-power fee simply to make the button feel expensive. The strategic sacrifice is the loss of the directly administered position and the consequences of governing through a subject.

Declining or postponing installation does not remove the player's network or confiscate already earned compliance. It also does not replenish the readiness intervention for another payment later.

## Government identity and inheritance

Prefer the native collaboration-government identity and creation process. Existing living countries, their meaningful focus trees, leaders, politics, and armies must not be overwritten merely to produce a convenient tag.

A new government receives the country setup that the native route legitimately creates. Event 097 does not add free armies, free equipment, cores throughout its sponsor's empire, or a custom focus tree as a reward for appearing.

Government names and sponsor references must describe the current relationship. The presentation should not remain permanently tied to an original sponsor after an actual independence, annexation, or lawful overlord change.

Any supplementary country-package work required by the installed native route must preserve those native identity constraints. A missing flag or leader asset cannot be hidden by shipping an unrelated country's identity.

## Rival powers and competing regimes

The global grant can support several powers building their own networks of collaboration governments in different conquered countries. This is the guaranteed form of competition.

Multiple rival governments claiming the same homeland may be possible only where the native system supports separate, lawful, non-overlapping administrations and sufficient identities. Event 097 must not assume it can manufacture unlimited copies of a target country.

When two powers contest the same territory or the same available government identity, use the native ownership and creation constraints. The second power retains its prepared network and legitimate occupation benefits, but does not steal the first government's territory through a duplicate creation call.

A strong network is an opportunity to govern what a power has actually conquered. It is not a global auction in which the highest collaboration value wins every defeated country.

## Existing defeated occupations at activation

When Evolution IV activates, review existing occupations with verifiable native defeat context and either a qualifying current network or a verified qualifying pre-defeat observation. Do not reconstruct missing pre-defeat collaboration from current compliance. This is a bounded activation review, not a grant of new networks against every country annexed earlier in the campaign.

Do not invent a historical victory receipt for a country merely because it owns another nation's cores. Peaceful purchases, diplomatic transfers, and unverified ancient annexations do not automatically qualify.

A completed native government remains in place without receiving a second creation reward. Existing ordinary puppets are not automatically converted unless the native route expressly supports the change and the same eligibility rules are satisfied.

## The aftermath

A collaboration government can be attacked, lose territory, become independent, be annexed, or face national restoration through other systems. Its creation is not a permanent protection against politics.

If the country displaced by a collaboration government still exists as a government in exile, it receives two short reports and nothing else.
The first arrives when the government is installed over its cores and names, from the exiles' point of view, the officials and officers who formed it.
The second arrives when that government loses its protector, because the sponsor is defeated, annexed or releases it.
Both reuse the Collaboration Governments report image and carry no effect; they tell the exiles when restoration through Event 095 becomes plausible.

Event 097 keeps only the minimal causal history needed to recognize what it helped create and to avoid paying again. The government itself belongs to the native political system. When the sponsor disappears, the event does not resurrect it or recreate the old relationship on the next firing.
