# Vanilla-doctrine biological battlefield access

## Acceptance and source correction

The user's accepted 2026-09-20 CBRN plan explicitly allows vanilla doctrines to support basic biological programs, protection, and ordinary offensive operations. This correction applies that decision to all four ordinary biological battlefield raids. The older biological specification's mandatory Theater CBRN Headquarters gate conflicted with the accepted plan because `theater_cbrn_headquarters` is unresearchable and granted only after terminal Chaos Warfare command.

`bio_battlefield_country_can_operate` now requires the researchable 50-base-day `pathogen_handling_protocols` technology instead of that doctrine-only marker. It still requires war, derived full protection readiness, battlefield-use policy, a completed ordinary agent project, and any applicable national last-resort safeguards. The native raid definitions retain their matching project, exact state, supply-node origin, assigned division, 21-day preparation, and equipment reservation. The same country trigger is used again by the hidden outcome receipt, so losing research, protection, policy, project, war, or target validity still prevents an accepted biological release. Theater CBRN Headquarters keeps its advanced headquarters-unit unlocks.

The protection snapshot also derives its cleanup contribution from real decontamination kits in stock and in armies, provided Field Decontamination Kits has been researched. The equipment requirement is `ceil(deployed_army_manpower_k × 0.25)` kits, a gameplay calibration equivalent to one kit per four thousand deployed troops; the coefficient lives in `common/script_constants/cbrn_system_constants.txt`. At complete military mask coverage, basic and improved mask training, and complete cleanup-kit coverage, a vanilla doctrine reaches 95 derived readiness points against the 80-point full threshold. With no usable cleanup kits it reaches only 75. The Chaos Warfare specialization adds five points, but the full-readiness gate also requires researched field decontamination and no cleanup-kit shortage, so those five points cannot replace the equipment. The Program Management description shows mask and cleanup shortages and explains missing cleanup training.
