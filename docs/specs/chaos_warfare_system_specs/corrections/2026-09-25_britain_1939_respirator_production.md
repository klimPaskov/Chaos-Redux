# Britain 1939 respirator production

**Acceptance basis:** The approved CBRN overhaul requires a distinct sourced 1939 equipment, civilian-protection, industrial, and personnel setup. The user's 2026-09-25 clarification keeps force-scaled military masks and a 25% military replacement reserve for both bookmarks, a separately sourced British 1939 civilian issue, and zero starting civilian coverage elsewhere without country evidence.

The [2 February 1939 parliamentary answer](https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/commons/1939/feb/02/air-raid-precautions) reported 40 million civilian respirators issued during the preceding twelve months and described continuing production of infant protection. The [8 June 1939 parliamentary answer](https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/written-answers/1939/jun/08/respirators) reported more than 46 million civilian respirators issued to local authorities by 31 May. These sources support a functioning prewar respirator supply programme, but do not identify a Hearts of Iron IV factory count or production efficiency.

`chaosx_startup_grant_eng` in `common/scripted_effects/chaosx_startup_history_effects.txt` grants basic-mask knowledge before its post-1938 branch. That branch creates one continuous `gas_mask_equipment_1` production line with one requested military factory and 100 starting efficiency in the installed history convention, using file-local tuning constants. The one-factory allocation is a conservative gameplay calibration that competes with conventional production. It is not a claim that one real factory manufactured the historical issue. The source issue of 40,000 abstract crates and the force-scaled military reserve remain separate stock transactions; the production line does not create free equipment on selection or repeat the starting grant.

The line uses the installed vanilla `add_equipment_production` effect contract and the current buildable basic mask model. Britain's vanilla 1939 order of battle creates infantry, support-equipment, and tank lines, but no respirator line. The existing one-shot startup history dispatcher and date gate limit this addition to the later historical start. No new equipment archetype, project, icon, CXT package, or localisation key is introduced; the basic-mask equipment name and icon are already registered.

Source checks can establish the date gate, one-shot dispatch, research unlock order, buildable model, and absence of a duplicate vanilla line. They cannot establish engine assignment of the requested factory, actual output under resources and modifiers, or the final civilian and military stock ledgers. Other countries' 1939 industrial distinctions remain an accepted requirement without a calibrated source change here.
