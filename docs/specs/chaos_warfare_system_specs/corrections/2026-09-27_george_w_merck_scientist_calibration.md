# George W. Merck scientist calibration

**Disposition: implemented in source; specialist-field mapping remains an informed gameplay judgment.** This correction follows the user-approved review of all 67 CBRN scientists on the installed 1–4 skill scale and the requirement to separate scientific competence from offensive-program authorization. The parent task authorizes a bounded Merck skill and trait correction while preserving his existing portrait, recruitment date, roster entry, and authorization behavior.

George W. Merck received a bachelor's degree in chemistry from Harvard in 1915, joined the family pharmaceutical company instead of pursuing a doctorate, acquired experience across its departments, and became company president in 1925. The New York Community Trust biography records that he established a laboratory for pure and applied research, advocated industry investment in research, and built a research organization that recruited academic scientists. Harvard Business School likewise identifies his role in establishing Merck's first research division in 1933. These sources support applied chemistry and research-program leadership, but do not document Merck personally conducting biological-warfare experiments or pathogen research.

The National Academies history describes Merck as a manufacturing chemist and company president when he headed the War Research Service from 15 May 1942. It says the WRS recommended biological-warfare projects to federal agencies and initiated research through Chemical Warfare Service contracts with universities and industry. This supports his documented program-administration role and confirms the existing May 15 availability date; it does not establish expert biological-warfare research competence or an individual authorization to use biological weapons.

The character remains `USA_george_w_merck` with the approved portrait `GFX_portrait_USA_george_w_merck`, and recruitment still opens on 15 May 1942 through the existing `date > 1942.05.14` gate. His biological-warfare specialization moves from skill 4 (`EXPERT`) to skill 2 (`SKILLED`). `scientist_trait_brilliant_theorist` is removed because the evidence does not show expert theoretical work in biology or biological warfare. `scientist_trait_resourceful` remains as an interpretive abstraction for his documented ability to build and organize industrial research capacity; the trait is not presented as a historical title or direct experimental credential.

This skill-2 mapping is a moderate representation of transferable chemistry and research leadership in a biological-warfare scientist slot, not a claim that Merck was a pathogen researcher. A stricter model could rate him at level 1 or omit the specialist role, but that would require revisiting the user-directed 67-person roster requirement and is outside this correction. No offensive authorization, policy, project, doctrine, or technology rules were changed. The character remains in the 67-entry roster, and recruitment itself grants no authorization.
