# Dated conduct traits for Rascher and Mengele

**Disposition: implemented in source; native startup and character-trait behavior remain unverified.** On 2026-10-09 the parent accepted the two narrow date mappings from the user-authorized review of all 67 CBRN scientists: Rascher's `scientist_trait_inhumane` begins on 15 May 1941, the date of his documented request to use human subjects; Mengele's begins on 30 May 1943, the date of his Auschwitz assignment. The Rascher date records the request and is not asserted to be the first experiment.

## Accepted historical mapping

Josef Mengele remains in the scientist roster from 1 January 1943. The U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum records that he returned to Germany in January 1943 and resumed work with Verschuer, then was assigned to Auschwitz on 30 May 1943. The existing static inhumane trait was available from the January transfer even though the role used to ground that trait began at Auschwitz later. The accepted mapping retains his earlier research availability and dates only the ethical conduct trait to the Auschwitz assignment. [USHMM, “Josef Mengele,” lines 186-189](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/josef-mengele).

Sigmund Rascher remains available as a scientist from 1 January 1941 under the existing country-specific CSH transfer. The U.S. National Archives finding aid dates his letter requesting human subjects for potentially fatal Luftwaffe research to 15 May 1941, with a 22 May reply concerning prisoner availability. The accepted trait date is 15 May 1941, the directly documented request date; it does not claim this was the first experiment or first unethical act. [U.S. National Archives, *United States of America v. Erhard Milch*, Special List 38, page 30, lines 931-939](https://www.archives.gov/files/publications/lists/special-list38.pdf).

The one-time trait additions change only when the scientist bonus becomes available. `scientist_trait_inhumane` remains restricted to biological-warfare specialization and retains its existing modifiers in `common/scientist_traits/chaosx_00_traits.txt`. Neither scientist entry, trait award, nor research bonus authorizes offensive use; national permission remains separate.

## Implementation

`common/characters/cbrn_historical_scientists.txt:292-314` no longer gives either character a static `scientist_trait_inhumane`. Their character IDs, portraits, scientist roles, and level-1 biowarfare baselines are unchanged.

The existing `chaosx_startup_recruit_dated_ger_cbrn_characters` effect in `common/scripted_effects/chaosx_startup_history_effects.txt:552-597` awards Rascher's trait when `date > 1941.05.14` and Mengele's when `date > 1943.05.29`. Each block also requires that the character is present in Germany and that its one-time country flag is not set, then applies the trait in character scope and sets the flag. The helper already runs through its German startup call in `common/scripted_effects/chaosx_startup_history_effects.txt:51` and existing `on_daily_GER` call in `common/on_actions/cbrn_historical_startup_on_actions.txt:28-31`, so the patch adds no on-action, world iteration, runtime recruitment, or alternate CSH route. The history-time CSH registrations remain at `history/general/chaosx_startup_character_recruitment.txt:100-101`.

The following accepted behavior remains unchanged: CSH attaches the 29 dated identities during history initialization; Rascher transfers after 31 December 1940; Mengele transfers after 31 December 1942; portraits and advisor roles are untouched; the other five dated inhumane-trait awards remain identical; and the Event 016 and Germany–Mengele event files are unchanged.
