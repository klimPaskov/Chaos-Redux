# International decontamination mission cost

**Acceptance basis:** The user's September 2026 CBRN overhaul requires a readable decision cost row, one visible charge per payment, and a shorter player loop. The repository's decisions-and-missions skill limits an action to four spendable cost types and three inline cost values. This correction applies that accepted direction to the existing exact-state Sponsor Decontamination mission.

The ordinary mission consumes 100 gas masks, 60 decontamination sets, and 25 trucks at dispatch, and assigns two civilian factories for 45 days. A mutually exclusive Event 026 sale variant keeps the same target and lifecycle but consumes 50/30/13 or 25/15/7 materials and assigns one factory after whole-unit rounding. The three immediate equipment payments appear with registered icons in the cost row; the factory assignment is explained in the cost hover because it persists during the mission. Field Decontamination Kits, a chemically contaminated consenting foreign-controlled state, the accepted access relationship, and peace with the controller remain prerequisites. The decontamination sets represent the specialist wash and detection stores previously charged separately as instruments and generic support. The universal convoy charge is retired because this exact-state mission can serve a landlocked partner. The separate inspection-demand and forensic-publication instrument costs retain their own payment contracts.

The ordinary and sale variants each reuse one inclusive material quote from their custom cost row and available gate. Before selection they also require two or one available factories respectively; after selection their shared payment adapter rechecks materials and exact state access without retesting capacity already reserved by the native timer. It preflights mask, decontamination, and truck models, pays them oldest first through one concrete-model transaction, refunds a failed partial activation, and starts the exact provider/recipient mission only after settlement. Cancellation after a successful start returns none of the consumed equipment and applies no cleanup; the native timer releases its assigned factory capacity. Variant-specific state markers retain the running ordinary or sale timer under its provider when the one-day sale status changes, and both markers clear on completion or cancellation. The 45-day duration, access checks, cleanup amount, cooldown, and AI weight factors stay aligned across variants. The cheaper material requirement can broaden AI eligibility, so scenario evidence is required before claiming balance acceptance.

At first-model production costs, the ordinary immediate equipment bill falls from 1,262.5 to 327.5 IC: masks 20, field decontamination sets 270, and trucks 37.5 remain; the retired instruments, support equipment, and ten convoys accounted for 935 IC. Its two-factory, 45-day commitment remains; the sale variant commits one. This is a source-cost comparison, not an engine measurement or proof that repeated missions are balanced; higher equipment models change the total.
