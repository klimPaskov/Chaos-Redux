# International decontamination mission cost

**Acceptance basis:** The user's September 2026 CBRN overhaul requires a readable decision cost row, one visible charge per payment, and a shorter player loop. The repository's decisions-and-missions skill limits an action to four spendable cost types and three inline cost values. This correction applies that accepted direction to the existing exact-state Sponsor Decontamination mission.

The mission consumes 100 gas masks, 60 decontamination sets, and 25 trucks at dispatch, and assigns two civilian factories for 45 days. The three immediate equipment payments appear with registered icons in the cost row. The factory assignment is explained in the requirement and start-effect text because it persists during the mission. Field Decontamination Kits, a chemically contaminated consenting foreign-controlled state, the accepted access relationship, and peace with the controller remain prerequisites. The decontamination sets represent the specialist wash and detection stores previously charged separately as instruments and generic support. The universal convoy charge is retired because this exact-state mission can serve a landlocked partner. The separate inspection-demand and forensic-publication instrument costs are unaffected.

The same `cbrn_country_can_pay_international_decon_cost` predicate gates the decision's available state and custom cost row. The target root remains visible after the equipment technology unlock even when stock is insufficient, so the blocked cost row can explain the shortage. Exact quantities satisfy the predicate's less-than-negated checks. `cbrn_diplomacy_pay_international_decon_cost` pays the three equipment amounts once when the mission begins; cancellation returns none of them and applies no cleanup. The 45-day timer, foreign access checks, cleanup amount, cooldown, and AI weight factors stay as before. The cheaper material requirement can broaden AI eligibility, so scenario evidence is required before claiming balance acceptance.

At first-model production costs, the immediate equipment bill falls from 1,262.5 to 327.5 IC: masks 20, field decontamination sets 270, and trucks 37.5 remain; the retired instruments, support equipment, and ten convoys accounted for 935 IC. The two-factory, 45-day commitment remains. This is a source-cost comparison, not an engine measurement or proof that repeated missions are balanced; higher equipment models change the total.
