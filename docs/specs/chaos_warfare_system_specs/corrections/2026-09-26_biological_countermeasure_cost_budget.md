# Biological Countermeasure Decision Cost Budget

Status: accepted within the user-approved CBRN simplification and presentation overhaul; surveillance, national Smallpox vaccination, International Medical Mission, and Field Hospitals are implemented in source, while quarantine and containment remain accepted and queued.

The user-approved plan requires concise native decision payments, exact inclusive affordability, and automatic readiness without another administrative payment layer. The repository decision skill limits each action to four consumed or committed types and three inline cost values. The parent accepted this narrower cost consolidation on 2026-09-26 after the read-only biological decision audit found actual payments missing from the displayed rows. The first four decisions use one custom cost contract because the installed Decision modding wiki states that native `cost` and `custom_cost_text` cannot be combined into one reliable row. Their Political Power debit is applied once at selection by the same hidden payment effect that consumes equipment; country or exact-state effects commit capacity. The shared predicate checks Political Power, paid equipment, the required on-hand equipment, and capacity with inclusive `NOT = { ... < amount }` or `greater_than_or_equals` comparisons and is called by both `available` and `custom_cost_trigger`. The inline row shows Political Power and two consumed equipment types; its hover enumerates the fourth commitment and labels the separate stock requirement as not consumed. The AI saving hints mirror the Political Power constants; their acceptance by the native decision parser still needs engine evidence.

Source evidence for the four actions: `common/decisions/biowarfare_disease_containment_decisions.txt`, `common/scripted_triggers/biological_countermeasure_triggers.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/biological_countermeasure_effects.txt`, `common/scripted_localisation/biological_countermeasure_value_localisation.txt`, and `localisation/english/chaosx_decisions_l_english.yml`. The scripted-localisation file binds cost, duration, and outcome display values to the owning constants. Their existing decision sprites are `GFX_decision_bio_activate_surveillance_network`, `GFX_decision_biowarfare_smallpox_vaccination`, `GFX_decision_deploy_field_hospitals`, and `GFX_decision_bio_international_medical_mission`; rows and hovers use registered Political Power, CBRN Instrument, Support Equipment, Medical Capacity, and motorized texticons. No new sprite is required for these actions.

Validation remains incomplete until native decision-list row and hover rendering, `ai_hint_pp_cost` parsing, exact below/at/above engine stock tests, and same-scenario weighted comparison are available. The present source checks do not establish those consumer results.
