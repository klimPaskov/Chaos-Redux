# Biological Countermeasure Decision Cost Budget

Status: accepted within the user-approved CBRN simplification and presentation overhaul; all eight listed action families are implemented in source, with native consumer and mission-outcome validation still pending.

The user-approved plan requires concise native decision payments, exact inclusive affordability, and automatic readiness without another administrative payment layer. The repository decision skill limits each action to four consumed or committed types and three inline cost values. The parent accepted this narrower cost consolidation on 2026-09-26 after the read-only biological decision audit found actual payments missing from the displayed rows. The eight implemented action families use one custom cost contract because the installed Decision modding wiki states that native `cost` and `custom_cost_text` cannot be combined into one reliable row. Their Political Power debit is applied once at selection by the same hidden payment effect that consumes equipment; country or exact-state effects commit capacity where applicable. Shared country predicates check fixed selection payments and on-hand requirements with inclusive stock and Political Power comparisons; country capacity uses `greater_than_or_equals`. Each predicate is called by both `available` and `custom_cost_trigger`; conditional state Medical Capacity is additionally checked by the targeted treatment eligibility trigger. Inline rows show at most three payments, while cost hovers enumerate any fourth commitment and label separate stock requirements as not consumed. The treatment-course hover evaluates the targeted state's service flag so it quotes a Medical Capacity commitment only when the course creates one; the existing state eligibility trigger requires that capacity before the first course is selectable. Sustain Containment also checks the additional resolution-time Support Equipment and on-hand transport and instruments before any continuing success or partial-success payment and effect. The AI saving hints mirror the Political Power constants; their acceptance by the native decision parser still needs engine evidence.

No new sprite is required for these actions.

Validation remains incomplete until native decision-list row and hover rendering, `ai_hint_pp_cost` parsing, exact below/at/above engine stock tests, and same-scenario weighted comparison are available. The present source checks do not establish those consumer results.
