The first two decisions use one custom cost contract because the installed Decision modding wiki states that native `cost` and `custom_cost_text` cannot be combined into one reliable row. Their Political Power debit is applied once at selection by the same effect that consumes the two equipment stocks and commits capacity. The shared predicate checks Political Power, both equipment stocks, transport stock, and capacity with inclusive `NOT = { ... < amount }` comparisons and is called by both `available` and `custom_cost_trigger`. The inline row shows Political Power and the two consumed equipment types; its hover enumerates the fourth commitment and labels trucks as not consumed. The AI saving hint mirrors the Political Power constant; its acceptance by the native decision parser still needs engine evidence.

Source evidence for the first two actions: `common/decisions/biowarfare_disease_containment_decisions.txt`, `common/scripted_triggers/biological_countermeasure_triggers.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/biological_countermeasure_effects.txt`, `common/scripted_localisation/biological_countermeasure_value_localisation.txt`, and `localisation/english/chaosx_decisions_l_english.yml`. The new scripted-localisation file binds cost, duration, and ongoing-effect display values to the owning constants. Existing decision icons are `GFX_decision_bio_activate_surveillance_network` and `GFX_decision_biowarfare_smallpox_vaccination`; the row uses registered Political Power, CBRN Instrument, Support Equipment, Medical Capacity, and motorized texticons. No new sprite is required for these two actions.

Validation remains incomplete until native decision-list row and hover rendering, `ai_hint_pp_cost` parsing, exact below/at/above engine stock tests, and same-scenario weighted comparison are available. The present source checks do not establish those consumer results.
