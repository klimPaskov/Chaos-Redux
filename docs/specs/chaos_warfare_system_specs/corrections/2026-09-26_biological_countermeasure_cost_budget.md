# Biological Countermeasure Decision Cost Budget

Status: accepted within the user-approved CBRN simplification and presentation overhaul; all six listed action families are implemented in source, with native consumer and mission-outcome validation still pending.

The user-approved plan requires concise native decision payments, exact inclusive affordability, and automatic readiness without another administrative payment layer. The repository decision skill limits each action to four consumed or committed types and three inline cost values. The parent accepted this narrower cost consolidation on 2026-09-26 after the read-only biological decision audit found actual payments missing from the displayed rows. The six implemented action families use one custom cost contract because the installed Decision modding wiki states that native `cost` and `custom_cost_text` cannot be combined into one reliable row. Their Political Power debit is applied once at selection by the same hidden payment effect that consumes equipment; country or exact-state effects commit capacity where applicable. The shared predicate checks Political Power, paid equipment, required on-hand equipment, and capacity with inclusive `NOT = { ... < amount }` or `greater_than_or_equals` comparisons and is called by both `available` and `custom_cost_trigger`. The inline row shows three payments; its hover enumerates the full four-type contract and labels separate stock requirements as not consumed. Sustain Containment also checks the additional resolution-time Support Equipment and on-hand transport and instruments before any continuing success or partial-success payment and effect. The AI saving hints mirror the Political Power constants; their acceptance by the native decision parser still needs engine evidence.

Source evidence for the six action families: `common/decisions/biowarfare_disease_containment_decisions.txt`, `common/scripted_triggers/biological_countermeasure_triggers.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/biological_countermeasure_effects.txt`, `common/scripted_localisation/biological_countermeasure_value_localisation.txt`, and `localisation/english/chaosx_decisions_l_english.yml`. The scripted-localisation file binds cost, duration, and outcome display values to the owning constants. Their existing decision sprites are `GFX_decision_bio_activate_surveillance_network`, `GFX_decision_biowarfare_smallpox_vaccination`, `GFX_decision_deploy_field_hospitals`, `GFX_decision_bio_international_medical_mission`, `GFX_decision_bio_quarantine_state`, and `GFX_decision_bio_sustain_containment`; rows and hovers use registered Political Power, Command Power, manpower, CBRN Instrument, Support Equipment, infantry equipment, Medical Capacity, and motorized texticons. No new sprite is required for these actions.

Validation remains incomplete until native decision-list row and hover rendering, `ai_hint_pp_cost` parsing, exact below/at/above engine stock tests, and same-scenario weighted comparison are available. The present source checks do not establish those consumer results.
