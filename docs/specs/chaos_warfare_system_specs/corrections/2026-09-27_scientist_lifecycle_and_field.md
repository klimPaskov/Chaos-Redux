# CBRN scientist lifecycle and field correction

**Disposition: implemented in source; engine recruitment and project-selection behavior remain unverified.** The user's accepted CBRN overhaul requires date-appropriate access to all 67 scientists and expertise that reflects each person's work. This correction covers source-supported historical mismatches found in the individual roster audit; the other person-specific proposals remain unresolved in the dated source handoffs.

| Character | Source-backed correction | Gameplay treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| John Scott Haldane | A [contemporary mine-research obituary](https://nmrs.org.uk/resources/obituaries-of-members/obituaries-of-members-names-beginning-with-h/john-scott-haldane/) dates his death to 14 March 1936. | Recruit him at the January 1936 start, retire him on 14 March through the existing bounded ENG callback, and do not grant him at the August 1939 start. A Royal Society death record appears to give 15 March; the day-level discrepancy remains a historical-source limit and has no effect on either bookmark. |
| Yakov Fishman | The [Soviet arrest and search order](https://docs.historyrussia.org/ru/nodes/89934-order-vydannyy-gu-gb-nkvd-sssr-na-arest-i-obysk-fishmana-yakova-moiseevicha-5-iyunya-1937-g) is dated 5 June 1937. | Recruit him at the January 1936 start but exclude him from the August 1939 bookmark. A campaign begun in 1936 may diverge; the historic arrest does not automatically fire in that campaign. |
| Ivan Velikanov | The [Russian history-of-science repression register](https://ihst.ru/projects/sohist/repress/kom/1938/velikanov.htm) and a [source-based Oxford history](https://academic.oup.com/book/4364/chapter-abstract/146298118) record his execution on 8 April 1938 after leading the Soviet biological program. | Recruit him at the January 1936 start but exclude him from the August 1939 bookmark. As with Fishman, a 1936 campaign may diverge; the execution is a historical-start bound rather than an automatic scripted purge. His scientific competence and separate advisor authorization remain otherwise unchanged. |
| Chikahiko Koizumi | The [historical chemical-warfare study](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-51664-6_14) documents chemical research and command by 1915, a chemical-weapons laboratory by 1917, and gas-mask work. | Change his sole scientist specialization from biological level 1 to chemical level 3. Level 3 is a gameplay calibration of the documented expertise, not a historical numeric claim. His later support of Ishii remains biographical, not a biological scientist skill or offensive permission. |
| Kurt Blome | A [5 June 1943 teletype presented in the Doctors' Trial](https://nuremberg.law.harvard.edu/transcripts/1-transcript-for-nmt-1-medical-case?seq=1491) links him to biological-war research near Posen, while [Schreiber's testimony](https://nuremberg.law.harvard.edu/transcripts/7?seq=16189) describes a July 1943 conference and director assignment. The exact initial appointment day is uncertain. | Open both scientist recruitment and the separate Chaos Warfare advisor appointment on 1 July 1943 as a conservative month-level gameplay floor. His scientific role does not itself grant offensive permission. |
| Franciszek Witaszek | The [Polish Institute of National Remembrance](https://ipn.gov.pl/pl/historia-z-ipn/archiwum/165376,Jerzy-Dabrowski-Dwie-matki-Alodii-Witaszek.html) places his leadership of the Poznań resistance retaliation group in mid-1940; [the Home Army Museum](https://muzeum-ak.pl/opracowanie/aresztowanie-grupy-franciszka-witaszka/43/) records a family dispute over alleged poisoning. | Keep his existing scientist access, but delay the biological-sabotage advisor appointment to 1 July 1940 as an explicit mid-year floor. The advisor still needs Chaos Warfare; this does not resolve the contested personal attribution. |
| Grigory Mairanovsky | A [reproduced Soviet archival memorandum](https://www.istmat.org/node/28192) says he entered NKVD service in 1937 and organized the secret toxicology laboratory with prisoner poison testing in 1938. | Keep 1937 scientist recruitment, but delay the toxicology-director appointment and `scientist_trait_inhumane_cw` until 1 January 1938, a year-level gameplay floor rather than an exact lab opening day. One SOV country flag prevents duplicate trait awards. |
| Frank Olson | The [CIA's released personnel file](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/FRANK%20R.%20OLSON%5B15620870%5D.pdf) recognizes his aerobiology work; a [CIA-hosted historical account](https://www.cia.gov/library/abbottabad-compound/12/129E144131F2E093FB1E441C737ACF92_SearchForTheManchurianCandidate.rtf.pdf) places his Detrick arrival in 1943 and Special Operations leadership years later. | Keep biological scientist skill 3, remove unsupported `gifted_engineer`, and replace the 1943 Program Director appointment with an Aerobiology Specialist appointment. Its scientist and advisor gates open 1 January 1943 as year-level floors. The advisor keeps +15% research and +25% project speed, adds +2% initial biological potency, and has no production-efficiency, growth, or Condemnation modifier. Offensive deployment still requires separate authorization. |
| Joachim Mrugowsky | The [Doctors' Trial judgment](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/llmlp/2011525364_NT_war-criminals_Vol-II/2011525364_NT_war-criminals_Vol-II.pdf) records his attendance at a late-December 1941 conference authorizing prisoner vaccine experiments. The [Ding diary archive](https://nuremberg.law.harvard.edu/documents/4145-work-journal-of-the-typhus) and judgment disagree on 22 versus 29 December for the meeting. | Keep his biological skill and 1939 institutional recruitment; award `scientist_trait_inhumane` once on 29 December 1941, the conservative documented bound rather than a claim about his exact first participation. |
| Gerhard Rose | The [Schilling letter](https://nuremberg.law.harvard.edu/documents/2294-letter-to-gerhard-rose) and [Doctors' Trial transcript](https://nuremberg.law.harvard.edu/transcripts/1?seq=11640) document his support of Dachau prisoner malaria experiments, with his endorsement recorded by 17 April 1942. | Retain his 1936 biological expertise and `scientist_trait_bright`; award `scientist_trait_inhumane` once on 17 April 1942 rather than at the 1936 start. The exact first date of earlier civilian-patient experiments is not established. |
| August Hirt and Otto Bickenbach | The [historical prisoner-experiment study](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-51664-6_13) dates Hirt's first documented mustard experiment to 25 November 1942 and Bickenbach's phosgene experiments to June 1943. | Keep the existing chemical skill and recruitment gates, remove the premature static `scientist_trait_inhumane_cw`, and add that trait once at the respective documented onset through the bounded GER callback. Bickenbach uses 1 June as a month-level gameplay floor, not a claimed exact day. Their earlier scientist-role eligibility still needs separate person-specific review. |

The ENG, SOV, GER, POL, and USA callbacks already run at startup and only in the relevant country's daily on action. The Haldane retirement and German/Soviet trait awards therefore need no worldwide pulse. A country flag guards each trait award so repeated callbacks cannot grant it twice. Character availability, skill, and earned trait do not set CBRN use policy, research completion, or doctrine mastery. No character portrait or equipment archetype changes.

Olson's advisor trait uses the existing native advisor sprite 9 in `common/country_leader/cbrn_historical_advisor_traits.txt`; his already wired large scientist and small advisor portraits remain unchanged. The trait label and effect text are registered in `localisation/english/chaosx_characters_l_english.yml`, so no new DDS or `.gfx` alias is needed.

Source cases: on 1 January 1936 Haldane, Fishman, and Velikanov are eligible; on 14 March Haldane retires; on 5 June 1937 a continuing 1936 campaign retains Fishman while a newly initialized later scenario cannot recruit him; on 8 April 1938 a continuing 1936 campaign retains Velikanov while a later start cannot recruit him; on 14 August 1939 none of the three is recruited. The installed `common/bookmarks/blitzkrieg.txt:5` gives the later bookmark date as `1939.8.14.12`. Mairanovsky's inhumane trait is absent before 1 January 1938; Mrugowsky's before 29 December 1941, Rose's before 17 April 1942, Hirt's before 25 November 1942, and Bickenbach's before 1 June 1943. Blome's scientist and advisor gates coincide on 1 July 1943, Witaszek's advisor begins 1 July 1940, and Olson's scientist/advisor gates coincide on 1 January 1943. Each award flag prevents a second trait operation. These are source-path checks, not engine-executed outcomes.
