# Chaos Warfare rewards and mastery contributors

Acceptance basis: the user objects to the weak individual doctrine rewards, explicitly names the 3% assault breakthrough grant and asks why only three unit types contribute to mastery.
This correction applies the accepted overhaul's meaningful specialization requirement and full legal-stack budgets to those source defects.
The parent accepts the bounded allocation below within that scope; it does not alter policy, costs, native mastery multipliers, milestone identities, existing prerequisite timing, paid operations or weapon authorization.

Hazard Assault adoption gives Protection Companies the existing 10% reliability benefit immediately.
The complete 15-point doctrine breakthrough allowance is one 15% grant at mastery III alongside the existing conditional Chaos Battalion unlock.
Adoption and mastery IV/V no longer duplicate fragments of that breakthrough allowance.
Mastery V retains its assault soft attack and hidden equipment upgrade; the latter keeps eleven organization points and uses four percent breakthrough so the possible Cadres defense and earned defense-skill layers fit the dedicated-unit ceiling.
The two projector technology breakthrough grants use 6.5% each to fit the same conservative independent-layer budget.

Armored delivery gets its existing total 5% breakthrough at adoption and total 15% track reliability at mastery I.
Fire support gives line/support artillery their existing 9.5% doctrine soft-attack allowance at adoption.
Fire support's five organization points belong to Projector Batteries; armor's five belong to the three armored delivery variants, avoiding a ten-point overlap on armored companies.
Integrated Command grants its existing 25% reconnaissance allowance at adoption.
All other stats and milestone effects remain as authored.

Native mastery uses exact retained role lists: infantry five, combat support three, armor three, operations six.
Field Medical and Biosecurity Assault contribute to the roles they actually support; all thirteen retained CBRN unit types occur in at least one track.
The three armored entries represent the three retained chassis variants rather than an engine limit.
No unrelated ordinary infantry, retired agent tank company or legacy Livens ID is added to the lists.
Existing manpower multipliers and thresholds remain; additional real companies contribute their manpower and consume their real production/supply/support-slot budgets.

Player text describes the actual night/cohesion, supply, specialist assault and command benefits rather than inert compatibility flags.
The existing deployment research and Theater Exploitation gates still apply.
Disposition: implemented in source; parent source tests/audits and native interpretation limits are recorded in the [implementation report](../../../plans/chaos_warfare_system_plans/2026-10-07_doctrine_reward_mastery_implementation.md).
