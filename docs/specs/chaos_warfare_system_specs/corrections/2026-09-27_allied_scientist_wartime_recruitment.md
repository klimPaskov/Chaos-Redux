# Allied scientist wartime recruitment and skill

**Disposition: implemented in source; engine recruitment and project effects remain unverified.** The user's accepted CBRN overhaul requires each of the 67 scientists to have date-appropriate access and a skill calibrated on the installed 1–4 scale. Individual source review found three prewar CBRN assignments that used later wartime work as their rationale, plus one prewar chemical skill at the maximum level without corresponding chemical-defence experience. The historical dates below are gameplay access floors where sources do not give exact appointment days.

Kellaway and Banting move from direct history recruitment to existing-country startup plus new `on_daily_AST` and `on_daily_CAN` callbacks. The callbacks are tag specific, so no world daily or weekly iteration is added. Henderson remains in the bounded ENG callback; Maass remains a direct recruit. The source roster remains 67 unique definitions and now has 40 direct and 27 dated recruitment paths. None of these operations grants an offensive policy, doctrine, weapon project, or equipment stock.

The parent's `hoi4.reference_context/search/read` inventory does not expose those routes, so the relevant local files were read directly; local SHA-256 revisions are `876be96cab02a3eaa9a133891bea5f963f8980aa4a794f759c1b30f87d2663c4` for the installed documentation and `6822adc85a374a1c1364f2def6f32c6d66da9fb0280319ddd6af4b84b33d882a` for the offline wiki. Source-path acceptance cases: AST has no Kellaway CBRN scientist on 1 January 1936 or 14 August 1939 but recruits him from 1 January 1942; CAN begins with Maass at skill 3, has no Banting CBRN scientist in either bookmark, and recruits Banting from 1 January 1940; ENG recruits Henderson from 1 June 1940. Repeated daily callbacks find the character already present and do not recruit twice. These checks establish source gating and one-path identity only; special-project selection and project-speed effects still require engine evidence. Existing portrait sprites and DDS files are unchanged, and no new icon is required.

Future work should resolve whether the broad `specialization_cw` and `specialization_biowarfare` project categories should explicitly describe defensive medical research as well as weapon research; the biographies alone cannot authorize a new specialty or a universal skill-rating formula.
