# Occupation cover-up Political Power scaling

**Disposition: implemented in source; native decision and payment scenarios remain unverified.** The user's approved CBRN overhaul says concealment actions keep their existing resource types and pay `1 + Condemnation / 500`, from one to three times the ordinary price. The two exact-record occupation concealment decisions previously charged fixed native Political Power, so they are included without introducing equipment or Command Power charges. The earlier accepted amendment described an equipment multiplier; the user's broader explicit instruction governs these existing Political Power payments.

| Public Condemnation | Seal State | Destroy Contaminated Records |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 40 Political Power | 60 Political Power |
| 500 | 80 Political Power | 120 Political Power |
| 1000 | 120 Political Power | 180 Political Power |

The shared Condemnation calculator clamps its score to 0–1000 and applies the same multiplier to both base prices. Political Power keeps its fixed-point value; the whole-unit upward rounding reserved for equipment is not applied. Each decision uses one custom cost row with the existing `£pol_power` vanilla texticon. The row's source-bound getter, hidden inclusive affordability trigger, and click-time debit all recompute the same amount. Exactly the displayed Political Power passes the `NOT has_political_power < quote` boundary. The former fixed native `cost` is removed so there is no second debit. Fixed AI Political Power hints retain the ordinary 40 and 60 prices so low-Condemnation AI access is not needlessly delayed; the live availability gate still requires the full scaled price, and the existing `ai_will_do` weights are unchanged.

The click effect revalidates the selected state and its exact historical chemical record, debits the quoted Political Power once, and dispatches only the selected seal or records-destruction action. If that exact-record dispatch fails, the same quote is refunded; no concealment outcome or hidden Coverup Condemnation is fabricated by the payment wrapper. Successful state effects retain their existing evidence suppression, resistance, discoverability, and Condemnation consequences. Admission and inspection remain separate actions with their existing prices and effects.

The source surfaces are `common/decisions/cbrn_occupation_decisions.txt`, `common/scripted_triggers/cbrn_occupation_triggers.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/cbrn_occupation_effects.txt`, `common/scripted_localisation/cbrn_occupation_value_localisation.txt`, `localisation/english/cbrn_occupation_l_english.yml`, and the existing `common/scripted_triggers/condemnation_sanctions_triggers.txt` calculator. The two base prices and fixed AI hints are grouped in `common/script_constants/cbrn_occupation_constants.txt`. No new sprite, DDS, equipment archetype, global iteration, or CXT grant is required; `£pol_power` resolves through vanilla `interface/texticons.gfx`.
