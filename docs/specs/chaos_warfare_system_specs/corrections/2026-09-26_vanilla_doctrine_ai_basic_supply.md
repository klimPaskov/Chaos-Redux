# Vanilla-doctrine AI basic CBRN supply path

Acceptance basis: the user's accepted CBRN overhaul permits vanilla-doctrine countries to research protection, build basic chemical and biological programs, choose authorization, equip their forces, and use native raids. The prior [ordinary battlefield AI policy correction](2026-09-26_vanilla_doctrine_battlefield_ai_policy.md) lets a prepared AI choose Battlefield Authorization and pass the ordinary raid target gate. Source review then found that its ordinary research and payload-production route still depended on `cbrn_ai_has_battlefield_program_posture`, which requires Chaos Warfare. The parent accepts this bounded supply route to complete the player-authorized ordinary path without changing advanced specialization.

`cbrn_ai_can_expand_basic_battlefield_supply` is a country-scope, side-effect-free trigger that requires exact selected Battlefield or Unrestricted policy, the existing offensive military-factory floor, no conventional equipment deficit, and the existing stock-backed stable protective base. It requires no Chaos Warfare doctrine. `cbrn_ai_can_expand_ordinary_chemical_supply` combines this basic route with the established retaliation/doctrine offensive-expansion gate, and only named and generic ordinary chemical research or agent-lot surplus strategies consume it. The original doctrine-gated `cbrn_ai_has_battlefield_program_posture` and broad `cbrn_ai_can_expand_offensive_cbrn_production` remain unchanged for HQ templates, specialized chemical pressure, nerve and exotic payloads, strategic routes, and Event 016 raid preferences.

A protected wartime country still at Retaliation Only can gain research weight for Chlorine Gas when it has no agent technology. This uses the existing normal offensive production-safety gate, so military industry, conventional reserves, and protective stock must already be adequate. Research does not change policy or grant stock. After Battlefield policy is deliberately selected, the generic ordinary agent research strategy accepts the new basic supply route and the exact policy value. Its current Chlorine and Mustard research targets remain the same. Britain, France, the Soviet Union, the United States, Italy, and Japan keep their existing national ordinary-agent research weights under the new supply route. Germany receives a separate vanilla-doctrine Mustard weight, while its existing Tabun weight stays on the old doctrine/route gate. Generic research and surplus management continue to favor Chlorine, then Phosgene or Mustard, rather than deliberately filling every possible basic family; Lewisite remains a researched national preference where configured.

The basic chemical pressure strategy is limited to chlorine, phosgene, mustard, and lewisite payload archetypes. It is enabled only under a vanilla doctrine with the new supply gate and at least one early agent technology, and uses the existing Battlefield pressure constant. Existing country-specific and generic ordinary exact-model surplus blocks accept the combined ordinary-supply helper while preserving their technology gates, stock targets, and national agent preferences. The retaliatory pressure block stands down when this basic Battlefield pressure is active, avoiding two simultaneous generic pressure layers. Nerve, exotic, filled-shell, strategic, and desperate strategy enable blocks were not changed. Native raids still reserve exact equipment and enforce their own research, project, origin, target, and assigned-division rules.

The normal biological project-research and safe-production gates accept the same basic supply helper alongside their existing retaliation, doctrine, strategic, and Japan routes. Tularemia may be chosen as a first Battlefield project, and safe Tularemia or Anthrax exact-model surplus production may continue after moving from Retaliation Only to Battlefield Authorization. Pathogen Handling, Rapid Outbreak Response, completed project-output technology, military industry, conventional supply, protective stock, and a complete Controlled or Strained arsenal-risk context remain in their respective existing gates. Plague and Smallpox safe production, Japan's campaign, desperate release, Event 016 Zombie/Black Plague stock, and advanced discovery were not opened by this correction. These strategies direct AI production only after ordinary equipment needs are met and do not grant free payload.

Source scenario expectations: a protected, adequately supplied vanilla-doctrine AI at war under Retaliation Only may prioritize Chlorine research but cannot make ordinary first-use raids without a target-specific retaliation right. Once it has basic knowledge and deliberately selects Battlefield Authorization, the basic chemical pressure and its national or generic exact-model store can activate without Chaos Warfare. A safe biological country can complete an ordinary project, adopt Battlefield Authorization, and maintain its Tularemia or Anthrax stock. Missing stable protection, the military-industry floor, conventional stock, a valid biological risk context, a completed agent project, or the selected policy keeps the corresponding route closed. No strategic or exotic production becomes available from the basic helper alone. These are source-linked conditional outcomes, not engine-measured production lines or AI choices.
