# Same-mask combined hazard protection budget

**Disposition: implemented in source; engine-effective exposure and technology comparison remain unverified.** The user's accepted CBRN budget permits issued masks as foundational equipment protection and caps the additional combined hazard-specific benefit at 50%. The [read-only combined-budget audit](../../../plans/chaos_warfare_system_plans/subagent_handoffs/2026-09-27_hazard_protection_combined_budget_audit.md) found reachable over-budget chemical cases: at 89% sealed-mask issue, full field kits and medical capacity changed choking casualty multiplier from the same-mask 0.25 to 0.06, a 76% additional reduction, and blister from 0.60 to 0.15, a 75% reduction. Later target treatment and shelter benefits could compound further because the previous death floor started from the already improved six-layer table result.

`cbrn_calculate_action_protection` now evaluates the existing class-specific casualty table once from the current issued-mask respiratory score with extra decontamination and medical inputs temporarily zeroed. That result is `cbrn_action_mask_only_casualty_mult`; it reflects actual stock, deployed demand, model quality, filter condition, and fitting, so zero or partial issue keeps its genuine shortage penalty. The normal six-layer score still drives the displayed protection, disruption, and contamination outputs, but its casualty multiplier cannot fall below half of the same-mask multiplier. Military and civilian calls preserve separate mask-only outputs. The attacker toxicological-director factor applies to both the real result and its baseline. The final military-death, civilian-death, and medical-saturation floors use those actor-adjusted mask-only baselines before target treatment, hospitals, and shelter effects, enforcing one combined additional-benefit ceiling across the full chemical path.
