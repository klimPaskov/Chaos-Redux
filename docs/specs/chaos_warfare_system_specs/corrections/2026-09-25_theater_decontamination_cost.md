# Theater decontamination assignment cost

**Acceptance basis:** The user's September 2026 CBRN overhaul calls for automatic routine protection, a shorter player loop, readable decision payments, and exact-cost boundaries. The repository decisions-and-missions skill limits one action to four spendable cost types and three inline values. This correction applies those accepted requirements to the existing exact-state theater cleanup assignment.

Deploy Decon Corridor spends 4 Command Power, 40 decontamination sets, and 300 fuel at dispatch. The cost row displays exactly these three amounts with matching registered icons. The country must also hold 100 mask crates, 20 support equipment, and 2 trucks in stock to sustain the active column; these are non-consumed operational requirements. Theater Exploitation, an active Decontamination Corridor HQ ability, a controlled state with chemical contamination, and an unused national assignment period remain required. The cleanup output, severity scaling, and 28-day country and state assignment locks remain unchanged.

The payment helper rechecks both affordability and operational support immediately before debiting. The country lock and cleanup run only after the payment flag is set. The same inclusive less-than-negated payment predicate gates the visible cost row and the decision's availability; a stockpile exactly equal to each displayed amount is sufficient. The requirement predicate independently checks support stockpiles at their exact thresholds.

At first-model production costs, the assignment's paid equipment bill falls by 105 IC: 100 masks at 0.20 IC, 20 support equipment at 4 IC, and 2 trucks at 2.5 IC become held stock instead of consumed stock. The remaining 40 decontamination sets cost 180 IC to replace, alongside 4 Command Power and 300 fuel. The five Political Power payment is retired. The change preserves the need to produce and retain protection supplies without charging the column for them on every dispatch. This is a source-cost comparison, not a measured in-game balance result.
