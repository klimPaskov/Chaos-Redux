# Ordinary battlefield AI policy and native raid access

Acceptance basis: the user's 2026-09-20 CBRN overhaul permits a vanilla-doctrine country to research and conduct basic authorized chemical and biological operations, and requires human and AI attacks to use the same native raid execution path. The prior [AI gate follow-up](../../../plans/chaos_warfare_system_plans/2026-09-26_native_battlefield_ai_policy_gate_followup.md) identified a source gap: ordinary chemical and biological native land-raid AI target gates accepted a retaliation right or exceptional first-use route, but not deliberately selected Battlefield Authorization alone. The policy decision also had a zero generic AI score. This correction implements the parent-accepted narrow route under that user authorization.

`cbrn_ai_policy_authorizes_ordinary_battlefield_first_use` returns true only for exact persistent policy value 3, Battlefield Authorization, or 5, Unrestricted Authorization. It excludes the retired value 4. The ordinary chemical and biological land-raid AI target predicates add this helper as one route beside their existing exact retaliation and exceptional checks. The broader `cbrn_ai_route_allows_first_use` is unchanged because strategic research, sabotage, occupation, and exotic routes also consume it. All native raid target, project, protection, payload, assigned-division, war, and final outcome gates remain in their existing paths. No attack adapter, payment, refund, or callback changed.

`cbrn_ai_can_choose_ordinary_battlefield_policy` gives a generic AI an explicit path to the normal 5-Command-Power decision. It requires Retaliation Only as the current policy, war, stable protective stock, limited readiness, no conventional army deficit or near-capitulation, and either ordinary chemical agent research or safe biological research plus an ordinary agent project and full readiness. The decision retains its zero base score and gains a named +12 score only when this trigger passes. The existing exceptional-route additions remain. A final zero factor keeps an AI already at Unrestricted Authorization from downgrading to Battlefield. Retaliation Only receives a final zero factor only while at war under Battlefield or Unrestricted Authorization, preventing 90-day wartime oscillation. At peace its prior base score returns and the normal reassessment interval permits deliberate de-escalation. Doctrine mastery does not change authorization automatically.

The bounded decision audit found that chemical land-raid AI already rechecks conventional army supply at target selection, while the biological counterpart only checked it when choosing the policy. The biological ordinary battlefield AI target gate now applies the same conventional-deficit safeguard. Human raid availability is unchanged. This keeps the later production tradeoff meaningful after policy adoption rather than allowing a depleted conventional army to keep selecting biological attacks.

The source gate is narrow: it enables consideration of 55 chemical and four ordinary biological native land-raid definitions through their shared AI predicates. The native raid AI weight still has to remain positive after its own factors, and raid creation still needs its exact stock, project, target, origin, and unit requirements. A generic country with Retaliation Only but no target-specific retaliation right remains unable to make a first-use strike. A country at Battlefield Authorization without the required research, protection, or paid payload still cannot create one. This correction does not grant strategic or exotic first use.

Conditional source arithmetic gives Battlefield Authorization a score contribution of 12 from its zero base when the new prepared-wartime trigger is true. If the current policy is Battlefield or Unrestricted during war, the final Retaliation factor reduces its ordinary base 4, including the enemy-use multiplier when present, to zero. At peace that factor does not apply, so Retaliation returns to its ordinary base 4 after reassessment. Under current Unrestricted Authorization the final Battlefield factor reduces any exceptional or theater additions to zero. These are scripted-score implications under stated trigger truth values, not measured AI choices or normalized probabilities.
