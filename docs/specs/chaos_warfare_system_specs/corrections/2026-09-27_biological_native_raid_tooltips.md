# Ordinary biological raid outcome language

**Disposition: implemented in localisation; native tooltip layout and raid lifecycle remain unverified.** The accepted CBRN presentation overhaul requires concise, player-facing effects and one native resource path. The four ordinary strategic biological raid definitions already reserve their exact Anthrax, Plague, Tularemia, or Smallpox payload through `essential_equipment`, and their country event bridge applies the final actor, target, and authority checks. Thirty-four actor, target, and rejected-context tooltip keys in `localisation/english/biological_strategic_raids_l_english.yml` now describe the possible release, failed-delivery evidence, actor-side accident, incubation, and countermeasures without internal record terminology.

The failure text distinguishes evidence recovered at the target from an accidental release in the raiding country. Limited success describes a possible partial seed; success describes concealed or detected incubation; critical success describes a stronger seed that countermeasures can still limit. A rejected final context tells the target that no agent is released there. The tooltip language is conditional because native outcome selection precedes the scripted final gate. It does not promise payload destruction or a refund: installed `common/raids/_documentation.md:167-180,237-291` documents equipment collection after creation and four outcome callbacks, but no cancellation/refund or outcome-destruction rule for essential equipment.
