# Hospital protection payment and quoted equipment

**Acceptance basis:** The user's September 2026 CBRN overhaul requires readable native decision cost rows, exact-cost affordability, and a single displayed and actual payment for each action. This correction applies those accepted requirements to the existing exact-state hospital response.

Protect Hospitals appears for a controlled state with a verified chemical-raid alert and no active hospital response. A stock shortage leaves that state target visible but blocks payment. The cost row quotes 10 Political Power and the alert's population-scaled respirator-crate and Support Equipment requirements, with working registered icons. It is the only player-facing payment display; the effect tooltip explains the continuing medical protection for its configured duration.

The alert creation effect calculates and stores equipment requirements on the targeted state. `cbrn_state_hospital_payment_ready` is the inclusive gate shared by decision availability, the custom cost row, and the hidden completion effect. The completion effect uses the stored amounts without recalculating them, debits real mask and support stock, then pays Political Power and applies the timed state protection only after both equipment debits complete. The AI uses a static Political Power hint because the engine's custom cost does not reserve Political Power on its own; that named file-scoped hint mirrors the shared 10-point cost constant.

At `state_population_k = 1000`, the existing ratios and clamps quote 30 respirator crates and 20 Support Equipment, alongside 10 Political Power. The strict less-than shortage tests reject 29 crates, 19 support items, or 9 Political Power; exact 30/20/10 and higher amounts satisfy the source gate. This checks the below/exact/above boundary in source arithmetic, not engine inventory execution.

The relevant files are `common/decisions/cbrn_protection_decisions.txt`, `common/scripted_triggers/cbrn_protection_triggers.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/cbrn_protection_decision_effects.txt`, `common/script_constants/cbrn_system_constants.txt`, and `localisation/english/cbrn_protection_l_english.yml`. The state mask and support helpers compare owned stock with the quoted amounts using a strict less-than shortage test, so exact equality is affordable. Native decision-list rendering and engine inventory execution remain unverified; source arithmetic alone does not establish the visual or runtime result.
