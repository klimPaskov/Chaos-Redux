# Projector Defense with a Biological Operations Veteran

Status: accepted within the user's combined CBRN modifier budget and implemented in source; engine-effective aggregation remains unverified.

The prior strict projector-defense product counted four independent 5% chemical-support sources and two 5% Livens technology tiers: `1.05^6 - 1 = 34.0096%`. It omitted the legal assignment of a commander with the earned `biological_operations_veteran` trait to an army containing Chemical Projector Batteries. That trait grants `defense_skill = 1` in `common/unit_leader/chaosx_traits.txt`. The offline Character Modding reference says one army-leader Defense skill level adds 2.5% defense. Under the same conservative independent-product method, `1.05^6 × 1.025 - 1 = 37.3598%`, above the accepted +35% dedicated-unit ceiling. The installed vanilla documentation does not state the final combat conversion or aggregation formula, so the 2.5% skill contribution is a source-informed upper-bound input rather than an observed engine stat.

Both Livens tiers now use the shared 4% projector-defense technology constant; their soft attack, hard attack, breakthrough, equipment unlocks, prerequisites, year columns, and AI scores are unchanged. The revised strict static product is `1.05^4 × 1.04^2 - 1 = 31.4692%`; adding the veteran's assumed 2.5% gives `1.05^4 × 1.04^2 × 1.025 - 1 = 34.7559%`. The static source bound therefore retains about 0.244 percentage points of headroom under the +35% budget. The two Army HQ status traits in `common/unit_leader/cbrn_hq_traits.txt` contribute -10% or -3% army attack during protective postures, so they cannot raise this positive defense maximum.
