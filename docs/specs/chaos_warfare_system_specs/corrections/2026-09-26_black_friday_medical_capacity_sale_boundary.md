# Medical Capacity Investment Sale-Cost Boundary

Status: accepted and implemented in source under the user-approved requirement that displayed payments, affordability, and deductions use one calculation; native decision and Event 026 consumer evidence remains unresolved.

`bio_expand_medical_capacity` is a three-type investment: Political Power, Support Equipment, and trucks. Event 026 owns its sale and evolution discounts through the shared quote and transaction adapter. The ordinary reference is 50 Political Power, 120 Support Equipment, and 40 trucks; the current baseline sale quotes 25/60/20, and the evolution sale quotes 13/30/10 after whole-unit rounding. These amounts are tuning values from `common/script_constants/biological_countermeasure_constants.txt` and the Event 026 discount constants, not independent localisation prices.

Before this correction, the custom row displayed the discounted quote, but the decision's `available` check and the active-sale payment effect also called `bio_countermeasure_can_expand_medical_capacity`, which required full-price support equipment and trucks. A country with exactly the displayed sale resources could not select the decision. The source now separates `bio_countermeasure_can_expand_medical_capacity_prerequisites` (Field Hospital research and room for another capacity gain) from full-price stock checks. The active-sale availability and payment paths use the prerequisite helper plus the shared discounted quote and affordability checks; the inactive path retains the full-price gate. The decision's `available` and `custom_cost_trigger` both call `black_friday_can_pay_bio_expand_medical_capacity`, and the payment is hidden from the ordinary effect tooltip. Normal-row values are bound from the same three ordinary cost constants rather than repeated literals.

The decision also supplies an `ai_hint_pp_cost` from its ordinary Political Power constant. Its native `cost = 0` avoids a second engine debit, but the AI still needs a saving hint for the custom Political Power payment. The hint is conservatively based on the ordinary cost during a sale; native parsing of the constant-valued hint remains unverified.

The custom row's hover repeats the selected normal or sale quote with all three texticons and explains selection-time payment and incomplete-transaction refund. The ordinary effect tooltip describes the later Medical Capacity gain without duplicating the upfront cost.

Source evidence: `common/decisions/biowarfare_disease_containment_decisions.txt`, `common/scripted_triggers/biological_countermeasure_triggers.txt`, `common/scripted_triggers/026_black_friday_triggers.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/026_black_friday_effects.txt`, `common/scripted_localisation/biological_countermeasure_value_localisation.txt`, and `localisation/english/chaosx_decisions_l_english.yml`. Event 026's transaction owner checks the three quoted resources again before paying, records their receipts, and refunds an incomplete transaction. The existing `GFX_decision_bio_expand_medical_capacity` decision sprite and registered Political Power, Support Equipment, and motorized texticons are reused; no new asset is required.

Source checks prove the active-sale path no longer invokes the full-price stock helper and that the normal row reads the owning constants. They do not prove the native row render, engine parser behavior for the quote, exact stock tests in game, or Event 026's delayed transaction result. Those remain validation blockers.
