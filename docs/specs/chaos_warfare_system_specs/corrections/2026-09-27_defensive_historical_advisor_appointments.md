# Defensive historical advisor appointments

Disposition: implemented in source with processed small advisor cards; AI probability and native appointment display remain under review.

The user's accepted CBRN overhaul calls for existing scientists to take date-appropriate defensive medical, protective-chemistry, biological-research, and logistics appointments without requiring Chaos Warfare or unrestricted offensive use. This correction applies that accepted scope to three scientists whose existing approved large portraits and dated work support distinct conditional roles. The source choices below are parent implementation judgments within that scope, not claims that the historical people held identical in-game titles.

Nikolay Zelinsky can be appointed by the Soviet Union after Collective Protection Systems is completed. His [institute's history](https://zioc.ru/institute/zelinskij-n.-d) documents activated-carbon respirator work and production before 1936. An active appointment reduces military chemical casualties by 3% only when the defending controller has issued military respirator crates. It does not reduce crate replacement or change civilian protection, chemical damage output, or weapon access.

Norman Heatley can be appointed by Britain from 1940 after Field Antibiotics is completed. The [Oxford History of Science Museum](https://www.mhs.ox.ac.uk/backfromthedead/exhibition/production/index.html) documents his production apparatus, a December 1940 penicillin batch, and later scale-up. An active appointment reduces medical saturation by 5% during a matching anthrax, plague, or tularemia antibiotic course; smallpox is excluded.

Stanhope Bayne-Jones can be appointed by the United States from 1942 after Mass Vaccine Production is completed. The [National Library of Medicine finding aid](https://findingaids.nlm.nih.gov/repositories/4/resources/969) documents his wartime epidemiological and preventive-medicine roles. An active appointment reduces outbreak growth by 5% only where the defending controller has an active Surveillance Network. It does not increase offensive biological potency, spread, or authorization.

All three cost 100 Political Power, occupy the existing political-advisor slot, and have AI weights that favor a defensive CBRN profile and, for the medical appointments, a real outbreak. They do not add static army combat or research bonuses. The two biological effects enter the existing response-floor calculation; the chemical effect enters the existing mask-anchored casualty floor. Source-level combinations with the existing medical pioneer are 0.95 × 0.85 = 0.8075 on active antibiotic medical saturation and 0.97 on Zelinsky's separate military chemical casualty component, before shared floors and other responses. The existing filter-replacement stack is unchanged.

Approved large scientist portraits provide the source for `GFX_idea_ENG_norman_heatley`, `GFX_idea_SOV_nikolay_zelinsky`, and `GFX_idea_USA_stanhope_bayne_jones`; the 65×67 DDS cards belong in `gfx/interface/advisors/cbrn/` with sprite definitions in `interface/cbrn_historical_advisors.gfx`.

The 1940 and 1942 date checks apply to Heatley and Bayne-Jones respectively. A live advisor selection and outbreak scenario is still needed to verify those engine outcomes.
