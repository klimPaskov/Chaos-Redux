# Emergency mask distribution cost

Status: accepted and implemented in source. The acceptance basis is the user's approved CBRN overhaul, which requires every upfront payment to appear once in a native or custom cost row, and their follow-up approval to reserve timed respirator payments at selection. Emergency Mask Distribution is immediate, so its live crate payment occurs on selection without a timed reserve.

The state card shows ten Political Power and the current rounded respirator-crate shipment, capped by real national stock across the five mask models. Its target remains visible for a verified state alert or contamination with a positive live distribution gap, including when stock is exhausted; the shared affordability gate controls selection and the blocked row. The ten-point Political Power boundary is inclusive. The ordinary effect tooltip explains the coverage and congestion consequence without repeating the payment.

The state quote and actual distributor use the same target, initial cost, emergency wastage, fitting effectiveness, filter condition, terrain, and country modifiers. The quote treats unset state mask-model and fitting ledgers as zero and an unset filter condition as the ordinary full-condition default, so a freshly controlled state can be quoted before its protection ledger is initialized. The distributor debits real models in order. The decision rechecks affordability on execution, and the state helper initializes the consumed-crate output before invoking the nested distributor. It spends Political Power, marks the one-time surge, and applies congestion only after at least one crate is consumed. No periodic world scan or separate purchase layer is introduced.
