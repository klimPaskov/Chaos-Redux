# Balance, Tuning, and Consistency Rework

## Balance philosophy

Chaos Warfare can be stronger than ordinary doctrine in prepared circumstances. It should be weaker when the user lacks payload, protection, headquarters integration, supply, or political tolerance.

The system is balanced through prerequisites, production, preparation, counterplay, self-harm, diplomacy, and aftermath. It is not balanced by making every effect too small to notice.

## Current inconsistencies to remove

### Empty doctrine milestones

Fix: give each milestone a functional unlock and readiness cap.

### Broad doctrine stat stacking

Fix: replace large permanent category modifiers with abilities, temporary exposure effects, logistics, and role-based support.

### Agent-by-agent subunit duplication

Fix: consolidate projector and chemical-tank supports into role-based subunits with selectable payload profiles.

### Chaos Battalion universal arsenal requirement

Fix: require protection and specialist equipment. Payload comes from attached support or operations.

### Chemical aircraft without payload consumption

Fix: reserve and consume payload for explicit operations. Continuous mission effects require a verified hook or approval.

### Gas-mask research without equipment

Fix: create equipment, stockpiles, military issue, civilian distribution, filter consumption, and replacement.

### Identical major starting payloads

Fix: country program formulas and differentiated profiles.

### Doctrine reduces confirmed Condemnation

Fix: doctrine affects evidence and collateral damage, not confirmed responsibility below a floor.

### Genocide terminology inside doctrine

Fix: use military CBRN terminology. Connect atrocities through evidence and Condemnation only when behavior qualifies.

### AI-disabled core mechanics

Fix: program-aware AI for research, templates, headquarters, operations, countermeasures, and sanctions.

### Placeholder art assumptions

Fix: every visible new unit, technology, decision, company, designer, and achievement receives an asset entry.

### Unsupported airborne chemical tanks

Fix: no armored chemical delivery support is parachute-capable unless a verified design and balance case exists.

### Mixed tabun support

Current content can research tabun without a full set of delivery and payload definitions comparable to sarin and soman. Fix by choosing one consistent role:

- tabun becomes a research precursor and profile modifier, not a fielded payload
- or tabun receives complete payload, delivery, effects, and countermeasure support

Do not leave it half enabled.

## Power budget

### Permanent doctrine bonuses

Recommended cumulative permanent limits:

- global army attack factor: 0 to 5 percent
- global organisation factor: 0 to 5 percent
- planning speed: 0 to 15 percent
- coordination: 0 to 8 percent
- chemical support direct combat bonus: 0 to 15 percent after full mastery
- contaminated-state attrition reduction: up to 30 percent with proper support

The decisive effects are operational and temporary.

### Temporary operation effects

Prepared operations can create:

- enemy organisation loss of 10 to 35 percent
- recovery reduction of 10 to 40 percent
- reinforcement reduction of 10 to 35 percent
- movement reduction of 5 to 25 percent
- attack or defence reduction of 5 to 25 percent
- entrenchment reduction or fort assault utility

Catastrophic nerve operations can exceed these bands briefly, with major death, evidence, and cooldown cost.

### Friendly penalties

Protective posture:

- movement: minus 2 to 8 percent
- attack or breakthrough: minus 2 to 6 percent
- supply: plus 5 to 15 percent
- heat penalty: additional burden

Low protection during own chemical operation can create equal or greater disruption than the target experiences.

## Cost budget

### Doctrine adoption

- 100 army XP remains a reasonable anchor
- establishment mission requires protection and headquarters equipment

### Headquarters ability command power

Use current vanilla scaling by battalions affected.

Target minimum and maximum after scaling:

- protective posture: 10 to 35 command power
- prepared chemical offensive: 20 to 60
- decontamination corridor: 10 to 45
- strategic or combined operation: 35 to 60

No decision or ability should exceed the project command-power ceiling of 60 unless the base game ability system imposes a different verified rule and the user approves it.

### Equipment burden

A normal major should need several military factories for sustained theater use. A minor can protect a limited army but cannot maintain mass strategic attacks without foreign support.

### Payload reserve

Operation strength uses payload ratio:

| Ratio | Effect |
| ---: | ---: |
| Below 25 percent | operation blocked |
| 25 to 49 percent | 35 percent dose, high accident risk |
| 50 to 74 percent | 65 percent dose |
| 75 to 99 percent | 90 percent dose |
| 100 to 124 percent | full dose |
| 125 percent or more | no extra dose, reserve protects against loss |

Overstock does not multiply operation power without a higher-intensity decision.

## Protection balance

### Gas masks

Gas masks strongly counter choking agents. They partially counter blister and nerve agents unless paired with clothing, decontamination, warning, and medical support.

### Opportunity cost

Protection uses:

- production lines
- support slots
- headquarters slots
- supply
- recurring replacement
- command power during alerts

A fully protected army should not also gain free offensive bonuses.

### Counter-counterplay

The attacker can respond to masks through:

- persistent agents
- higher dose
- combined conventional bombardment
- delayed exposure
- targeting supply and filter reserves

These responses cost more and create greater evidence and Condemnation.

## Death balance

### Goals

- tactical use can kill thousands in dense or prolonged operations
- strategic raids can kill tens of thousands or more in large states
- catastrophic biological outbreaks can kill hundreds of thousands over time
- protection and medical preparation can reduce deaths dramatically
- repeated use can be campaign-altering

### Safeguards

- exposed share is separate from total state population
- one continuing-death loop per state and source family
- severity and duration caps
- no negative population
- death logs aggregate small ticks
- cleanup stops future deaths
- doomsday content uses separate explicit caps

## Contamination balance

### Goals

- tactical contamination changes local combat
- serious contamination disrupts supply and construction
- severe contamination can reshape a front
- strategic repeated use can influence Air Cleanliness

### Safeguards

- global Air Cleanliness updates by class transition
- state contamination clamps to 100
- new attacks refresh or raise one state effect rather than stack unlimited modifiers
- cleanup has diminishing returns at severe classes
- friendly forces suffer without protection

## Condemnation balance

### Goals

- one limited battlefield use creates concern, not instant pariah status
- repeated battlefield use can reach formal censure and arms embargo
- strategic chemical raids and biological attacks can jump tiers
- doomsday release can create immediate pariah status

### Suggested base gains before multipliers

| Action | Base chemical or biological gain |
| --- | ---: |
| Local projector use | 2 to 5 |
| Prepared battlefield barrage | 4 to 10 |
| Persistent state fire plan | 8 to 18 |
| Nerve battlefield operation | 12 to 25 |
| Chemical strategic raid | 20 to 50 |
| Biological covert seed | 15 to 40 when confirmed |
| Biological strategic raid | 30 to 70 |
| Nerve suppression | 10 to 35 |
| Doomsday release | 150 to 400 |

Deaths, target status, repeat use, and evidence modify the result. Final values belong in shared constants.

## Doctrine mastery pacing

Current mastery multipliers vary widely and can create rapid or inconsistent progression. Normalize the four tracks so a country cannot complete one merely by fielding one cheap support company.

Mastery sources:

- fielded equipped units
- successful operations
- defensive survival under exposure
- decontamination and containment
- exercises and establishment missions
- technology and officer corps

Mastery should scale with essential equipment status. Unequipped companies do not train mastery effectively.

Target pacing:

- adoption to mastery 1: 6 to 12 months of active preparation
- mastery 3: 18 to 36 months for a committed major
- mastery 5: late-war or extremely active program

## Technology timing

- basic protection and early agents remain available from First World War legacy
- improved protection and modern logistics fit 1936 to 1939
- nerve-agent battlefield deployment requires special projects and late-1930s or later
- advanced headquarters, sealed protection, and rapid decontamination fit 1940 to 1943
- biological offensive delivery remains special-project gated

Doctrine-only technology does not bypass special-project agent requirements.

## AI balance

AI operation frequency uses a national active-operation cap.

Suggested caps:

- one major prepared chemical operation per Army HQ
- one strategic chemical raid in preparation nationally
- one biological strike or covert operation in preparation nationally
- one nerve suppression operation per selected occupation region

AI cooldowns rise after:

- failure
- friendly blowback
- severe sanctions
- low stockpile
- high enemy protection

## Exploit audit

### Token support exploit

One small support company must not grant order-wide power to an unequipped army. Essential equipment and coverage weighting prevent it.

### Disabled equipment exploit

Special effects scale with equipment status and are blocked below minimum.

### Template switching exploit

Switching a support company in immediately before an operation should require equipment delivery and a preparation delay.

### Infinite mask recycling

Civilian distribution cannot be reclaimed at full value. State ownership changes preserve distributed coverage and damaged stock rules.

### Payload duplication

Strategic cylinders, shell lots, air lots, and division support cannot all represent the same stock without conversion loss. One source is consumed per operation.

### Repeated death tick exploit

Overlapping operations use bounded severity, not independent loops.

### Condemnation concealment exploit

Doctrine cannot reduce confirmed use to zero. Latent responsibility is released on later evidence.

### AI garrison exploit

Nerve suppression requires a targeted decision and payload. A support company alone does not provide unlimited state suppression.

### Air estimator exploit

Continuous contamination requires explicit authorization and payload budget. Chemical-capable aircraft sitting at an airbase do not contaminate a region.

## Performance

- no new all-country daily, weekly, or monthly pass without permission
- use operation completion hooks, combat hooks already approved, targeted state timers, and existing pulses
- avoid scanning every strategic region for every country
- cache selected target and operation data
- clean variables after state recovery and operation completion
- aggregate death and evidence logging

## Validation scenarios

### Scenario 1: Unprotected fortified front

A prepared choking or blister barrage should create a breakthrough opportunity, deaths, and contamination. The attacker must protect follow-up troops.

### Scenario 2: Fully protected defender

The same barrage should produce much lower deaths and organisation loss. Persistent contamination still disrupts supply and movement.

### Scenario 3: Forecast failure

An attacker with low forecast confidence should risk friendly exposure and wasted payload.

### Scenario 4: Chemical air raid

Successful raid consumes payload and contaminates the selected state. Failed raid consumes payload and can create wreckage evidence.

### Scenario 5: Civilian mask distribution

A protected capital suffers substantially fewer deaths than an unprotected state under the same raid.

### Scenario 6: Nerve suppression

Resistance falls immediately, then trauma, evidence, deaths, and Condemnation create a long-term cost.

### Scenario 7: Biological seed

The target receives incubation, can detect and contain it, and does not automatically suffer a full outbreak.

### Scenario 8: Stockpile accident

Unsafe high stockpile creates domestic risk. Maximum safety prevents ordinary accident but not sabotage or bombing.

### Scenario 9: Sanctions

Repeated use reaches embargo tiers and materially affects imported program inputs.

### Scenario 10: Minor-country defence

A small country can protect priority units and its capital without adopting the full offensive doctrine.

## Acceptance criteria

- no important power comes from a free universal stat pile
- every strong effect has cost, counterplay, and aftermath
- existing contradictions are removed or explicitly migrated
- balance constants are centralized
- performance avoids broad scans
- validation covers both attacker and defender
