# Chaos Warfare Doctrine Architecture

## Doctrine role

Chaos Warfare remains a land grand doctrine. It is a specialist doctrine for countries willing to devote production, headquarters slots, research, and diplomatic capital to CBRN operations.

The doctrine retains four tracks to match the current system and vanilla grand-doctrine structure. Working labels are provided for planning only. Final localisation must be written during implementation.

| Track | Working label | Primary role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Infantry | Hazard Assault Formations | Protected assault infantry, chaos battalions, contaminated terrain exploitation. |
| Armor | Toxic Armored Warfare | Sealed crews, armored delivery, mobile suppression, protected breakthrough. |
| Combat support | Contaminant Fire Support | Chemical artillery, projectors, ammunition trains, persistent fire plans. |
| Operations | Integrated CBRN Command | Army HQ, forecast, intelligence, decontamination, biological security. |

## Adoption gate

The doctrine should remain visible to all countries but require one of these capability gates to adopt:

- basic gas-mask technology and one chemical-agent technology
- a completed chemical special project
- a national focus or event that establishes a CBRN command
- a country profile with a historical program
- a manual scenario override

The adoption cost remains army XP but also creates an initial equipment and institution requirement. The country receives a ninety-day establishment mission. It must create a minimum protective reserve, designate a CBRN headquarters, and train one protected formation. Failure leaves the doctrine active at low Chemical Readiness with offensive abilities unavailable.

## Adoption effects

Adoption should not grant a universal 20 percent attack package. It should:

- unlock Chemical Readiness
- unlock the CBRN Operations decision category
- unlock the basic CBRN Operations HQ support company
- unlock Gas Mask and Decontamination Regimental Support
- unlock doctrine-only technology visibility
- enable the chemical barrage tactic at a low weight when payload and protection conditions are met
- enable the first two national use policies
- add a small mastery gain bonus for chemical support and protected formations

Direct stat changes should be limited to modest improvements such as five percent planning speed for properly equipped CBRN headquarters and five percent organisation retention under chemical exposure.

## Grand-doctrine milestones

The current empty milestone blocks become the backbone of the doctrine.

### Milestone 1: Protective Foundation

Requirements:

- doctrine adopted
- basic protective equipment in production
- one CBRN headquarters company fielded

Unlocks:

- military protective coverage UI
- protective posture commander ability
- filter replacement decision
- basic chemical reconnaissance company
- Chemical Readiness cap rises from 39 to 59

### Milestone 2: Delivery Integration

Requirements:

- one delivery track at mastery 2
- one agent stockpile above minimum operational reserve
- one protected army order

Unlocks:

- prepared chemical offensive ability
- chemical artillery-ammunition operation
- armored delivery payload profile
- reliable raid planning interface
- Chemical Readiness cap rises to 74

### Milestone 3: Theater Exploitation

Requirements:

- two tracks at mastery 3
- decontamination capacity at least 40
- headquarters weather or intelligence cell

Unlocks:

- decontamination corridor ability
- combined delivery operations
- doctrine-only Chaos Assault Battalion
- contaminated-route exploitation bonuses
- Chemical Readiness cap rises to 89

### Milestone 4: Terminal CBRN Command

Requirements:

- all four tracks active
- one track at mastery 5
- national policy at limited battlefield authority or higher
- advanced protective equipment or equivalent special project

Unlocks:

- full readiness cap
- theater CBRN command technology
- capstone operations
- final officer-corps spirit family
- extreme use decisions, with explicit death and Condemnation warnings

## Infantry track: Hazard Assault Formations

This track replaces broad infantry buffs with protected assault formations and exploitation mechanics.

### Adoption effect

- protected infantry loses less organisation and recovery under chemical exposure
- hazard assault units gain a small urban and fort breakthrough modifier
- unlocks Hazard Assault Training mission

### Mastery 1: Mask Discipline and Assault Drills

Unlocks:

- improved military mask issue efficiency
- order-scoped organisation retention under choking-agent exposure
- reduced friendly panic during forecast failure
- division training mission that consumes protective equipment and army XP

No broad army attack bonus.

### Mastery 2: Contaminated Terrain Movement

Unlocks:

- Hazard Pioneer regimental support
- reduced movement and supply penalties in Trace, Local, and Serious contamination
- route-marking and contaminated-corridor decisions
- modest engineer and recon synergy

### Mastery 3: Chaos Assault Battalion

Unlocks the redesigned line battalion and its first doctrine-only technology.

The battalion is a protected assault cadre. It does not carry every chemical and biological weapon. It gains operational effects from attached chemical delivery support and from the Army HQ order.

Capabilities:

- high breakthrough and organisation retention in urban, fort, forest, jungle, and marsh combat
- high equipment and supply burden
- limited special-forces cap usage
- low defensive staying power in open terrain
- increased casualties when protective supply falls

### Mastery 4: Shock Exploitation Columns

Unlocks:

- follow-up assault ability after a successful chemical operation
- faster planning conversion into breakthrough for protected units
- enemy reinforcement delay in contaminated states
- higher friendly-exposure penalty if used without a decontamination corridor

### Mastery 5: Terminal Hazard Offensive

Unlocks:

- capstone Chaos Assault technology
- ability to extend a prepared operation into adjacent target states
- severe payload and protective-equipment cost
- higher military and civilian death ceiling
- strong evidence and Condemnation floor

The capstone is powerful because it combines command preparation, supply, and protected formations. It does not add a permanent 45 percent soft-attack bonus.

## Armor track: Toxic Armored Warfare

This track focuses on sealed crews, mobile delivery, and rapid exploitation.

### Adoption effect

- unlocks armored chemical delivery support
- modest breakthrough retention in contaminated terrain
- armored units consume more protective supplies but suffer less movement loss than infantry

### Mastery 1: Sealed Crew Compartments

Unlocks doctrine-only sealed-crew technology.

Effects:

- reduced inhalation exposure for tank crews
- lower reliability penalty from contaminated terrain
- higher production cost and maintenance burden
- does not protect accompanying infantry

### Mastery 2: Armored Agent Delivery

Unlocks:

- light and medium armored delivery profiles
- chemical-shell or aerosol payload stance
- short-range rapid exposure operation
- strong urban and fort role

The unit uses one armored-delivery subunit per chassis role. Agent choice comes from the operation profile.

### Mastery 3: Mobile Nerve Suppression

Unlocks the Nerve Agent Suppression Detachment and occupation-use decisions.

Benefits:

- very high suppression
- reduced garrison damage in the short term
- rapid resistance disruption

Costs:

- state deaths
- contamination
- evidence
- resistance radicalisation after the immediate effect
- severe Condemnation

This mastery does not unlock genocide infrastructure or a generic “Concentration” law.

### Mastery 4: Protected Breakthrough Logistics

Unlocks:

- mobile decontamination attachment for armored orders
- lower fuel and supply disruption in contaminated corridors
- faster replacement of sealed filters
- higher truck and decontamination-equipment requirement

### Mastery 5: Catastrophic Shock Breakthrough

Unlocks:

- synchronized armored delivery and assault ability
- short, severe enemy organisation and reinforcement penalty
- strong friendly blowback if weather, payload, or protection is inadequate
- high command-power and fuel cost
- long cooldown and international evidence spike

## Combat-support track: Contaminant Fire Support

This track handles artillery, projectors, ammunition, and persistent contamination.

### Adoption effect

- unlocks Chemical Ammunition Train regimental support
- unlocks basic choking-agent shells if the agent is researched
- increases chemical fire-plan preparation speed

### Mastery 1: Projector Fire-Control Cells

Unlocks:

- consolidated Chemical Projector Battery support company
- improved dose consistency
- lower friendly blowback from short-range projectors
- no generic soft-attack boost to all support artillery

### Mastery 2: Counterbattery Chemical Fire

Unlocks:

- defensive chemical barrage response
- enemy artillery organisation and reinforcement disruption
- ammunition expenditure scaled by enemy artillery density
- reduced effect against masked and dispersed forces

### Mastery 3: Chemical Shell Logistics

Unlocks:

- chemical artillery-shell equipment or abstract ammunition lots
- artillery fire-plan decisions
- payload stock conversion between strategic cylinders and shell lots
- ammunition-train essential equipment block

### Mastery 4: Persistent Agent Distribution

Unlocks blister-agent persistent fire plans.

Effects:

- longer state contamination
- supply-route disruption
- delayed deaths
- lower immediate breakthrough than nerve or choking agents
- stronger cleanup requirement

### Mastery 5: Deep Contamination Fire Plan

Unlocks a multi-state artillery operation.

Requirements:

- theater CBRN headquarters
- high payload stockpile
- target intelligence
- protected friendly order
- sufficient artillery and ammunition-train coverage

The effect is limited by artillery density, target frontage, weather, and operation duration. It cannot be repeated continuously without replenishment.

## Operations track: Integrated CBRN Command

This track is the headquarters, intelligence, medical, and biological-security branch.

### Adoption effect

- unlocks CBRN Operations HQ Section
- improves forecast display and operation tooltip quality
- reduces operation preparation time slightly

### Mastery 1: Chemical Intelligence and Weather Cells

Unlocks:

- weather and intelligence HQ support
- higher forecast confidence
- target protective-coverage estimate
- reduced misdelivery risk
- evidence-recovery bonus when defending against enemy use

### Mastery 2: Protected Signal Networks

Unlocks:

- order-wide warning transmission
- faster mask-on response
- lower first-use surprise penalty
- better headquarters ability reach
- higher support-equipment and radio requirement

### Mastery 3: Countercontamination Routing

Unlocks:

- Mobile Decontamination Column HQ support
- Decontamination Corridor ability
- route cleanup priority
- reduced contaminated supply penalties for assigned orders

### Mastery 4: Air-Surface and Biological Coordination

Unlocks:

- coordinated chemical raid and ground exploitation
- chemical air-interdiction technology
- Biological Security HQ Section
- improved containment after capturing infected or contaminated territory

The later accepted doctrine clarification limits mitigation to Condemnation impact. Doctrine may increase physical CBRN harm and may reduce the Condemnation result through the bounded shared multiplier, but it cannot reduce or erase friendly deaths, accidental spread, evidence, attribution, contamination, trauma, responsibility, history, or any other physical or forensic consequence.

The still-later accepted correction in specification 08 governs one narrow exception: Gas-Chamber Saturation Drills may reduce the amount of new forensic evidence produced by its existing-camp nerve method. It cannot erase evidence, discovery, responsibility, attribution, deaths, contamination, trauma, Condemnation, or history, and the exception does not apply to any other CBRN route.

### Mastery 5: Theater CBRN Overmatch

Unlocks:

- theater CBRN command technology
- combined protection, delivery, medical, intelligence, and decontamination preparation
- capstone headquarters ability that lets one order maintain a high protective posture while conducting an operation
- substantial command-power, supply, mask, filter, medical, and payload upkeep

- Chemical Artillery Shells
- Persistent Agent Shell Filling
- Armored Agent Delivery
- Sealed Tank Crews
- Nerve Suppression Detachment
- Mobile Decontamination Columns
- Chemical Air Interdiction
- Theater CBRN Headquarters
- Biological Security Assault Detachment

## Officer corps

The doctrine should provide a small family of mutually exclusive spirits rather than stacking every existing CBRN spirit.

### Army command spirits

- Controlled Retaliation Doctrine: protection, evidence recovery, low first-use willingness.
- Theater Contamination Doctrine: stronger delivery and cleanup, higher supply burden.
- Terminal Hazard Doctrine: maximum offensive effect, higher casualties and Condemnation.

### Division command spirits

- Mask Discipline: protection and organisation retention.
- Hazard Assault Cadres: Chaos Battalion and pioneer performance.
- Contaminant Fire Coordination: projector and chemical artillery reliability.

### Army chief or high-command roles

- CBRN Operations Director
- Civil Defence Coordinator
- Chemical Logistics Inspector
- Biological Security Director

Final names should follow country-specific localisation and character rules. Generic advisor roles should not create fictional historical people.

## AI doctrine adoption

AI adopts Chaos Warfare only when it has a viable program. Adoption score considers:

- researched agent and mask techs
- chemical or biological special projects
- military factories available for specialist production
- current enemy chemical use
- Condemnation tolerance
- ideology and country profile
- expected war duration
- access to air, artillery, or armor delivery
- sanctions vulnerability
- current doctrine investment

Ordinary democratic AI should prefer defensive preparation or retaliation. Radical, desperate, high-chaos, or historically aggressive profiles can pursue first use.

## Doctrine balance limits

- No branch may give more than a modest permanent global army attack factor.
- The same effect cannot be granted through doctrine, spirit, support company, and technology without a clear cap.
- Every strong operational effect needs essential equipment and a recurring cost.
- Protection must reduce offensive effect enough to justify defensive production.
- Confirmed use cannot receive a doctrine-based Condemnation discount below the public-harm floor.
- Doctrine-only units must scale with equipment status.
