## National permission and raids

The national use policies are exactly **Retaliation Only**, **Battlefield Authorization**, and **Unrestricted Authorization**. Changing policy costs 5 Command Power and starts a 90-day reassessment interval. Doctrine progression never changes policy automatically.

The raid interface has exactly two CBRN categories: **Chemical Raids** and **Biological Raids**. Native land delivery covers cylinder, projector, artillery, and armored roles. Each actual raid uses an exact selected state, a supply-node origin, an assigned division, army intelligence, a matching weapon and native preparation. Human and AI use the same executable path. Older per-agent raid-type definitions may remain only where required inside these two categories; they are not extra categories. Any earlier proposed strategic, agent-specific, or recovery category must be reconciled against this exact-two-category requirement before promotion.

The [ordinary biological raid language correction](../corrections/2026-09-27_biological_native_raid_tooltips.md) keeps four agent outcome tooltips conditional on the final release gate and does not state an equipment refund or loss that the installed raid documentation cannot prove.

The [neighboring native raid tooltip correction](../corrections/2026-09-27_native_raid_tooltip_family.md) applies the same conditional consequence and equipment-evidence boundary to chemical, Japan, doomsday, battlefield biological, sabotage, Event 016, and facility-recovery branches.

Basic authorized chemical and ordinary biological battlefield raids must remain available to a country using a vanilla grand doctrine after normal research, production, protection, and project requirements. Theater CBRN Headquarters is a Chaos Warfare specialization, not an ordinary biological-raid prerequisite.

Military and civilian mask issue is automatic and debits actual stock. Civil Defence decisions appear for a real threat, outbreak, contamination, or response need. No world-wide periodic country iteration is authorized.

The accepted real-equipment and military-priority requirements also govern civilian field cleanup through the [field-kit allocation correction](../corrections/2026-09-26_civilian_decontamination_field_kit_pool.md). Its one-kit-per-100,000-civilians demand is an implementation calibration, not a claim about historical inventories; the correction defines the eligible threatened states and bounded refresh hooks.

The user's 2026-09-25 follow-up selects click-time reservation for the timed Occupied Population and Protective Aid decisions. Each action must quote and remove its exact calculated respirator-crate commitment when selected, use no more than that reservation at completion, and refund unused crates on cancellation or target invalidation. A higher later coverage requirement cannot silently charge additional stock. The visible cost row must match the reserved amount; an estimate is not accepted as an exact payment.

The exact-state theater decontamination assignment has three upfront payments and separate non-consumed support-stock requirements, as specified in [`../corrections/2026-09-25_theater_decontamination_cost.md`](../corrections/2026-09-25_theater_decontamination_cost.md). This follows the accepted shorter-loop and readable-cost requirements without changing its contamination output.

The six headquarters roles consolidate to **Operations and Intelligence**, **Protection and Decontamination**, and **Medical and Biosecurity**. Division support uses one **Protection Company** and one **Field Medical Company**; nerve suppression is an upgrade within the accepted structure. Remove eighteen agent-specific chemical-tank companies and retain three armored chassis roles.

The fully combined legal CBRN bonuses are capped at army attack and organisation +5% each, planning +15%, coordination +8%, reinforce chance +2 percentage points, and conventional attack, defense, and breakthrough +15% per stat. Dedicated CBRN static bonuses cap at +35% and conditional hazard bonuses at +50%. Artillery doctrine conventional soft attack caps at +10%. Technology costs use the 50%, 75%, and 100% relative cost bands. These are combined caps, not a separate allowance for each source.

The [same-mask hazard budget correction](../corrections/2026-09-27_same_mask_hazard_budget.md) applies the 50% additional-benefit ceiling to chemical casualties, medical saturation, and target treatment after real issued-mask coverage, including partial-stock cases. Disruption and contamination keep their separate bounded formulas.
