# Accepted CBRN overhaul amendment

## Authority and status

The user explicitly requested implementation of this design in the 2026-09-20 task. This is the acceptance basis for the requirements below. This amendment supersedes conflicting proposals in the earlier Chaos Warfare specifications and prompts; its presence does not prove that gameplay, assets, or validation are complete. The implementation and evidence ledger is [`../../../plans/chaos_warfare_system_plans/2026-09-20_cbrn_overhaul_requirement_ledger.md`](../../../plans/chaos_warfare_system_plans/2026-09-20_cbrn_overhaul_requirement_ledger.md).

## National permission and raids

The national use policies are exactly **Retaliation Only**, **Battlefield Authorization**, and **Unrestricted Authorization**. Changing policy costs 5 Command Power and starts a 90-day reassessment interval. Doctrine progression never changes policy automatically.

The raid interface has exactly two CBRN categories: **Chemical Raids** and **Biological Raids**. Native land delivery covers cylinder, projector, artillery, and armored roles. Each actual raid uses an exact selected state, a supply-node origin, an assigned division, army intelligence, a matching weapon and native preparation. Human and AI use the same executable path. Older per-agent raid-type definitions may remain only where required inside these two categories; they are not extra categories. Any earlier proposed strategic, agent-specific, or recovery category must be reconciled against this exact-two-category requirement before promotion.

## Protection, units, and limits

Military and civilian mask issue is automatic and debits actual stock. Civil Defence decisions appear for a real threat, outbreak, contamination, or response need. No world-wide periodic country iteration is authorized.

The six headquarters roles consolidate to **Operations and Intelligence**, **Protection and Decontamination**, and **Medical and Biosecurity**. Division support uses one **Protection Company** and one **Field Medical Company**; nerve suppression is an upgrade within the accepted structure. Remove eighteen agent-specific chemical-tank companies and retain three armored chassis roles.

The fully combined legal CBRN bonuses are capped at army attack and organisation +5% each, planning +15%, coordination +8%, reinforce chance +2 percentage points, and conventional attack, defense, and breakthrough +15% per stat. Dedicated CBRN static bonuses cap at +35% and conditional hazard bonuses at +50%. Artillery doctrine conventional soft attack caps at +10%. Technology costs use the 50%, 75%, and 100% relative cost bands. These are combined caps, not a separate allowance for each source.

## Condemnation and projects

Condemnation is bounded from 0 to 1000. The equipment cost multiplier is `1 + Condemnation / 500`, with whole equipment requirements rounded up. Inspection imposes 5–15% consumer goods for 180 days. The Condemnation specification records the same accepted limits; the final owner must validate the full source and interface contract.

The eight accepted projects are **Mechanized Smoke Screens**, **Collective Protection Systems**, **Self-Renewing Filters** (fictional), **Phantom Mist** (fictional), **Mass Vaccine Production**, **Field Antibiotics**, **Regenerative Serum** (fictional), and **Broad-Spectrum Antiserum** (fictional). Existing advanced biomedical, cloning, exotic, and Mengele routes remain available. Event alternatives must be idempotent rather than duplicating rewards or grants.

## Starts, people, organizations, and traits

Historical starting profiles must be grounded in country and program evidence, including starting stock, research, facilities, and usable route state where authorized. The roster requires 67 scientists. Three generic CBRN MIO families have twelve-node trees. National organizations include ICI, IG Farben, DuPont, Rhône-Poulenc, Montecatini, and Showa Denko, plus two explicitly fictional advanced organizations. Source the historical identities and do not present the fictional organizations as historical.

The four accepted commander traits are **Biological Operations Veteran**, **Hazard Warfare Veteran**, **Protected Assault Expert**, and **Theatre Containment Organizer**. **Chemical Operations Commander** is awarded from service evidence. Native CBRN High Command must be represented. The **Field-Validated Planner** trait and its Event 24 reward route are removed by the same 2026-09-20 user instruction; Event 24 field validation may still resolve its non-trait effects.
