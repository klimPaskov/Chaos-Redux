## Technology consistency gates

- Agent technologies must unlock a complete payload and at least one delivery or research role.
- A precursor such as tabun must either have full gameplay support or be clearly marked as a precursor.
- Doctrine-only technologies cannot bypass special-project gates for advanced agents.
- AI research weights depend on posture and stockpile, not a flat factor of one for every node.
- Every new visible technology needs a unique tech icon and localisation direction.
