| Tabun | nerve precursor or agent | 4 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 4 | 5 | 5 | final role must be completed or precursor-only |
| Sarin | nerve | 5 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 4 | 5 | 5 | rapid battlefield and strategic shock |
| Soman | nerve | 5 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 5 | 5 | 5 | capstone nerve effect and suppression |

| Aphrodisiac or behavioral concept | experimental incapacitant | 1 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 4 | experimental project and propaganda, low reliability |
