# British Tax: achievements

All titles below are working labels, not final localisation. Each achievement has a proposed unique registry identifier that must be checked against the repository before implementation. The set rewards difficult campaigns and meaningful outcomes rather than simply receiving the event.

## A01: sustained revenue order

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_081_revenue_order`. **Eligibility:** ENG. **Difficulty:** Very hard. **Visibility:** Visible.

Earn the mature Revenue Empire outcome under Evolution II and sustain its actual industrial breadth and consumer-goods contribution conditions for a further 180 days. At least two foreign sovereign identities must have contributed during the qualification period, even where they subsequently became dependencies. Existing successor identity rules prevent puppet-tag multiplication.

The achievement cannot be earned by triggering the super-event manually, having zero ordinary consumer-goods requirement, receiving uncollected assessments, or starting directly with a qualification flag. Qualification requires 360 days of real mature-order conditions in total.

The completed icon depicts a central British industrial insignia receiving distinct streams of production from several smaller industrial symbols. The subject must remain legible at 64 x 64 without relying on text or a detailed world map.

## A02: small country, complete settlement

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_081_minor_settlement`. **Eligibility:** A human-controlled independent minor with at most 20 usable civilian-plus-military factories when its first resistance project begins. **Difficulty:** Very hard. **Visibility:** Visible.

Complete all twelve domain projects and all four consolidations through the peaceful route. Evolution II must be active for the entire final transition. The country must never use the hostile-exit action during this occurrence and must remain free of a British dependency relationship during its uninterrupted final transition.

Growing into a major through ordinary play does not invalidate the original small-country qualification. Starting as a major and temporarily deleting factories does not establish eligibility. Use the first legitimate tax assessment and project-start history together.

The completed icon depicts a small factory with a fully broken foreign collection chain and a closed domestic ledger. It should communicate disproportionate effort without copying another achievement's subject.

## A03: from compulsory taxpayer to sovereignty

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_081_former_subject_settlement`. **Eligibility:** A country that was a genuine British dependent at initial Event 81 assessment. **Difficulty:** Very hard. **Visibility:** Visible.

End genuine British dependency through ordinary political or military play, then complete peaceful Tax Independence. At least one full reform stage must be completed under Evolution I or II. The country must not regain exemption solely by inheriting an earlier settlement from another identity.

The initial dependent record, the first genuine independence date, and distinct consolidated stages must be tracked. Repeated puppet-release cycles cannot award missing progress.

The icon depicts a colonial collection seal broken apart beside a rising domestic industrial emblem. Avoid a generic national flag so the achievement remains appropriate to different campaigns.

## A04: the collector defeated

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_081_enforcement_defeated`. **Eligibility:** A human-controlled major that validly refused or cancelled while sovereign. **Difficulty:** Very hard. **Visibility:** Visible.

Cause the special Event 81 entitlement to apply, fight a resulting British enforcement war, and achieve an applicable settlement that abolishes taxation worldwide while ENG survives. The player must be the victorious settlement actor and have at least 20% of its victorious side's recorded war participation in that linked conflict, rather than being an uninvolved country receiving a global popup. The native participation read and peace-actor identity must be verified before implementing this condition.

Do not count an unrelated earlier British war, a forced console goal, or a peace that secures only bilateral exemption. The identity of the original refusal and the enforcement conflict must be connected.

The icon shows a defeated collection emblem beneath a treaty document and an intact industrial skyline. It represents military-diplomatic success, not the destruction of Britain as a country.

## A05: ally at the limit

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_081_allied_exit`. **Eligibility:** An independent taxpayer that is a member of a British-led faction at its first assessment. **Difficulty:** Hard. **Visibility:** Visible.

Reach Nominal while ENG still leads. Remain at Nominal for at least 180 days under that relationship. After an ordinary legitimate leadership change or faction exit, complete the entire final transition peacefully. Do not become a British subject or use hostile cancellation during the route.

The 180-day period proves the faction ceiling was actually experienced. A one-day faction join immediately before completion cannot satisfy the initial assessment requirement.

The icon depicts an allied clasp opening beside a nearly cleared industrial ledger, with sovereignty represented through composition rather than text.

## A06: replace the whole dependency

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_081_external_recovery`. **Eligibility:** An independent taxpayer under Evolution II. **Difficulty:** Hard. **Visibility:** Hidden until Evolution I.

Begin the external domain of a transition with actual British trade exposure and at least two distinct expanded external claims successfully collected in the preceding 180 days. Complete the external domain and later Tax Independence without hostile cancellation. The reform must remove real fuel, convoy, or resource obligations, not merely labels attached to zero amounts.

The icon combines a fuel drum, merchant convoy, and alternative trade route in a compact, newly controlled domestic arrangement. All three must remain readable without excessive detail.

## A07: two sovereign settlements

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_081_partner_settlements`. **Eligibility:** An independent taxpayer or a sovereign assistance partner. **Difficulty:** Very hard. **Visibility:** Visible.

Complete a real bilateral assistance project with its required costs. Both participants must then reach peaceful Tax Independence under the same Event 81 occurrence, and both final transitions must occur while Evolution II is active. Neither can be the other's subject during qualification.

Record both identities and one completed assistance commitment. Being allied, offering an unaccepted proposal, or receiving a one-day nominal trade route is insufficient. Only one award per eligible country occurs.

The icon depicts two different domestic industrial emblems linked by an equal, open trade route rather than a central collecting crown.

## A08: recover a damaged network

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_081_network_recovery`. **Eligibility:** ENG under Evolution II. **Difficulty:** Extreme. **Visibility:** Hidden until Evolution II.

First sustain meaningful collection from at least eight distinct eligible foreign identities for 180 days. Subsequently lose at least half of that recorded real industrial revenue through sovereign peaceful or hostile exits, not self-inflicted bookkeeping changes. Then rebuild to the prior real receipt level for 180 days through remaining legitimate taxpayers, their genuine economic growth, new valid countries, or explicit enforcement settlements.

Countries that earned peaceful Tax Independence cannot be silently retaxed to satisfy the recovery. Britain's own temporary surcharge expiration, a changed rounding method, destroyed domestic factories, tag splitting, or manually removing a British modifier does not establish a qualifying collapse.

The icon depicts a fractured collection mechanism rebuilt around fewer but substantial industrial supports. This is an economic recovery challenge, not a trivial repeated activation reward.

## Shared achievement asset and tracking contract

Each achievement receives one original 64 x 64 completed subject and the prescribed grey and not-eligible states produced from the project's immutable templates. Final runtime files belong directly in `gfx/achievements/` using the registry ID plus the required suffixes.

Use durable country identities, recorded genuine outcomes, and explicit disqualifiers. Do not infer a ten-year historical sequence from the current tax stage alone. Console-only debugging, duplicated event delivery, save reload, or a restored tag must not produce duplicate progress.

Visibility is a presentation rule, not permission to skip implementation. All eight achievements require final text research and localisation, distinct assets, registry collision checks, and scenario coverage before completion can be claimed.
