# British Tax: country play and event connections

## Country archetypes

Country routes adapt to actual campaign conditions. They do not depend on the 1936 diplomatic situation remaining intact or on a particular historical leader still being alive.

| Archetype | Immediate decision | Longer campaign | Main British consideration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent industrial major | Compare the meaningful tax against lost British resources and escalation | Negotiate faster reforms, secure alternative imports, or refuse from strength | Losing a large taxpayer can outweigh several minor conquests |
| Small independent mainland country | Decide whether an embargo and British goal are survivable | Preserve a little construction capacity and recover the most damaging domain first | Coercion may produce little net revenue if it ruins the target |
| Independent island or maritime minor | Evaluate British naval reach, resource access, and shipping exposure | External reform may be more urgent than factory recovery | Naval strength matters more than a crude comparison of divisions |
| British dominion or colony | Cannot refuse or start resistance | Pursue actual independence through its existing national campaign | Automatic taxation is reliable but can increase the stakes of losing political control |
| Independent British-led ally | Must accept the relationship while ENG leads | Reduce to Nominal and retain the option to finish after a legitimate leadership change | A concession can preserve the alliance while keeping a small claim |
| Former British subject | Immediate independent refusal becomes possible | Restore previous consolidated history or start the long reform route | A newly free country may be determined to exit despite weak short-term economics |
| Country already fighting Britain | Assessment exists, collection is suspended | Resolve taxation explicitly in peace or record a valid hostile exit | Announcing tax on an enemy is not a source of wartime factories |
| British government under a different ideology | Continues as beneficiary ENG | Use contemporary regime language and normal national priorities | The tax system is not contingent on a monarch or one named ministry |

These routes provide behavioural distinctions without requiring a new country, leader, ideology, or focus tree for each reaction. Existing national content remains the source of political identity and sovereignty changes.

## Relationship-sensitive flavour

The same assessment has different emotional stakes. A major notices that Britain expects meaningful industrial support despite limited leverage. A weak minor discovers that its rate is proportionately higher. A colony is informed of compliance rather than invited to decide. An ally discovers that membership protects neither its factory allocation nor its freedom to refuse.

For a country with a previous completed independence settlement, Britain has to acknowledge the exception. It must not send the same initial demand because a cosmetic tag or ideology changed. A former subject can interpret the first available resistance decision as a tangible consequence of political independence.

## Economic recovery and economic crisis

A construction boom, new factories, or acquired territory expands the next assessment base only once that capacity is usable. Britain may benefit from genuine growth, but an apparent growth figure containing Event 81 income must not feed back into the tax calculation.

A depression, inflation crisis, occupation loss, or a collapse in ordinary usable capacity reduces the amount available for collection. The tax may remain oppressive in percentage terms, but actual British revenue falls with the economy it exploits. A mission's continuing industrial commitment can become invalid and should offer the low-industry conversion rather than stranding the player.

Events that change consumer-goods rules must be applied through the native economy before calculating Event 81's additional occupation and Britain's maximum relief. Separate sources should not each claim to have moved the same consumer-goods factory elsewhere.

## Black Friday and temporary discounts

Event 26 and any universal cost adjustment must retain their own ownership of discount behaviour. British Tax affects the bilateral price relation and its own decision commitments. It must not reapply a universal discount to an already discounted cost or infer that an ordinary Black Friday price reduction abolishes the British premium.

Both systems may affect an actual transaction, but the combined native price must remain positive and its displayed cost must match what is charged. A temporary discount cannot create free fuel, negative civilian-factory trade cost, or a permanent exemption from a continuing tax.

## Event 79: The Master

A country becoming a genuine subject through the influence contest changes sovereignty and therefore its Tax Resistance permissions. If ENG becomes its master, the British-dependent schedule and hard lock apply. If another country becomes its master, the non-British dependent treatment applies after ordinary subject obligations.

Foreign Influence is not a second Event 81 resistance currency. British Tax neither gives automatic Influence to every British collector nor lets a player spend Event 79 Influence to bypass the dependency lock. A later legitimate independence outcome updates taxation normally.

## Event 72: Ireland Reclaims the North

The event-specific Irish campaign remains conditional on its own victory and focus-tree unlock. British Tax responds to Ireland's current sovereignty, faction relationship, war with ENG, and usable economy. A reclamation war suspends bilateral collection while it is fought.

Successful reunification does not automatically grant Tax Independence unless the actual peace settlement includes that outcome. The unlocked Irish campaign can improve industrial capacity, trade alternatives, or bargaining strength through its real effects. Event 81 should read those effects rather than require arbitrary completion of a named focus merely to make its own reforms available.

## Domestic political crises

A political crisis such as Parliament of Fear can reduce a country's ability to pay Political Power costs or keep a stable project running. The existing crisis remains responsible for those costs and institutions. British Tax does not clone its chamber or paranoia value into the tax category.

A coup or change of ruling party does not automatically cancel taxation, reset completed domains, or grant a new refusal reward. The country retains its economic and treaty history unless the change also creates a genuinely different sovereign identity that the normal successor rules recognise.

## Resource and transport changes

New strategic resources, a lost port, or diminished shipping can change what Britain can actually collect. Expanded fuel and convoy levies only access their defined surplus. Britain cannot take a submarine, troop transport already assigned to an invasion, or fuel that does not exist by relabelling it a tax.

If a resource deposit changes owner, British rights tied to the previous owner end unless a valid new owner's liability supplies a new lawful Event 81 claim. Tax rights do not follow the map independently of the country relationship that created them.

## Diplomacy outside Britain

Alternative trade partners give an independent taxpayer a concrete way to replace dependence. These agreements are bilateral assistance projects, not a new universal alliance that automatically unites all refusers. The partner needs a valid economy and a relevant commitment. An AI partner evaluates trade interests, relations, and the danger of antagonising Britain.

Helping one country does not waive its initial tax, complete three domains at once, or create a new spendable influence pool. A successful partner project can reduce a specific burden through the normal reform sequence. Britain can respond to that real diplomatic activity without acquiring a special war goal against every foreign helper.

## Negative Economy cluster

Event 81 is a High member of Negative Economy. The cluster selects one event outcome for the shared timer and weight process. British Tax's opening is the event firing. Later assessments, active Evolutions, individual refusals, and reform completions are consequences of that firing.

The event's fire-once identity stays consumed after worldwide abolition or the disappearance of ENG. The cluster can continue operating through its other members according to their own rules, but cannot select Event 81 again in that campaign.

## Integration priorities

The first priority is preserving sovereignty and real economic consequences across existing systems. The second is adapting descriptions and AI priorities to the changed world. Connections that merely add another small modifier, duplicate a foreign-influence mechanic, or require an unrelated focus completion are not part of this design.
