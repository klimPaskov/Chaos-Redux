# British Tax: events, visual presentation, and super-event

## Writing direction

The central humour is the gap between the outrageous demand and Britain's calm administrative treatment of it. Foreign governments should react according to their position, with anger, calculation, resignation, bargaining, or alarm. British prose can be dry and self-satisfied without treating a threatened war as harmless.

The user's supplied terms remain the names of the mechanic and its major stages. New wording is direction-only here. Do not copy table labels, event roles, asset filenames, or research candidates directly into localisation. Avoid generic filler about paperwork where a specific industrial, consumer-goods, trade, or sovereignty consequence can carry the scene.

Country display names and titles should follow the active campaign. A non-monarchist Britain does not invoke a reigning monarch unless the actual government supports that wording. The story is alternate history, and generated documentary-style images must not be presented in source notes as photographs of real events.

## News coverage

N01 covers the initial universal taxation demand. N02 covers a major hostile refusal when the opponent or resulting crisis makes it internationally important. N03 covers Evolution I's visibly expanded claims. N04 covers worldwide abolition.

Not every minor refusal deserves an international news event. Use diplomatic significance, the human participant's involvement, actual enforcement preparation, or an emerging international confrontation to prioritise news. Local reports and the established history surfaces can record quieter outcomes.

## National ideas and category appearance

A taxpayer has one consolidated persistent tax idea. Its effects change with the active regime and completed domains. The icon changes between the baseline, Evolution I, and Evolution II family. A British taxpayer cannot exist because ENG is always excluded.

ENG has one consolidated revenue idea for the active tier where an idea is needed to expose its aggregate effects. A temporary pressure or emergency overlay may occupy one further Event 81 slot. Keep Event 81 at two simultaneous visible ideas per country, beneath the project maximum of three. Do not create one national spirit for each tax type, reform domain, invoice, or trade partner.

The two ordinary categories use distinct static pictures. The resistance picture focuses on domestic reclamation of economic authority. The British administration picture focuses on central collection and foreign assessments. Use the native category layout and targeted decisions, not a separate large custom window.

## Mature Revenue Empire super-event

The super-event is the mature-order outcome in Part 5. Its role is to show that the tax demand has become a sustained international economic relationship. The image may portray a crowded British collection centre with industry, cargo, and foreign delegations brought into one imposing composition. It should feel like an international order backed by material capacity, not a joke card with an oversized coin.

The title direction is formal British self-congratulation over an established order. The description direction contrasts Britain's industrial freedom with the accumulated obligations abroad. The main quote direction concerns taxation as power over another political community. The button direction should be a researched cultural or historical response that supports the tone without requiring a modern lyric or an invented quotation.

Research notes in the separate bibliography verify two short primary-source quotation candidates. They remain candidates pending final text research, context review, and acceptance. A candidate is not approved player-facing copy simply because it appears in this package.

Audio should be an authentic recording with a ceremonial, increasingly oppressive character. A 60-to-120-second selected passage should enter cleanly, sustain the reveal, and end deliberately. Composition rights, recording rights, source attribution, and redistribution permission must be checked separately. A streaming page or freely playable recording is not automatically reusable in a mod.

Use the existing settings-aware super-event framework. A visual-disabled or audio-disabled setting changes presentation, not the underlying economic milestone. Do not reserve an unverified free slot, invent an audio ID already used elsewhere, or silently substitute default music. Required audio and final cultural text remain explicit production gates.
