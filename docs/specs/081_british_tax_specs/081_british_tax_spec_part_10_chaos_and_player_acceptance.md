# British Tax: Chaos consequences and player acceptance

## Chaos principle

Event 81's catalog Chaos level is 1. Its persistent outcomes can affect the shared Chaos system, but a popup, a logged record, an administrative tier name, or a repeated collection tick does not independently create Chaos.

The map below uses initial outcome anchors. Final calibration must fit the shared Chaos conventions and avoid counting the same world-tension change, deaths, ordinary war outcome, or state transfer again through an Event 81-specific reward.

| Outcome | Event-owned Chaos change | Guard and rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| First material international collection | +5 | Once for this occurrence, after at least two eligible foreign countries actually contribute |
| First material expanded collection under Evolution I | +5 | Once, after an expanded claim produces a real burden and benefit |
| First material expanded collection under Evolution II | +10 | Once, for the intensified regime's substantive collection |
| First major international hostile break | +5 | Once for a materially important refusal with actual trade loss or enforcement preparation, excluding any already counted world-tension contribution |
| Durable recovery of at least one quarter of the affected initial foreign industry through peaceful settlements | -5 | Once, based on completed sovereignty outcomes, not repeated project clicks |
| Durable recovery of at least one half through peaceful settlements | -5 | Once, additional to the quarter milestone |
| Worldwide abolition of a materially operating tax system | -10 | Once, provided there was actual earlier collection |

Starting directly under Evolution II uses a single initial substantive-collection award of +10 rather than adding the baseline, Evolution I, and Evolution II awards for one transaction. Later distinct active Evolution outcomes use their listed own-stage awards. A missing intermediate award is not backfilled retroactively.

Reform projects and normal consolidation supply economic relief but do not each farm negative Chaos. Annual bills, returned convoys, a pressure timeout, country creation, renamed ministries, and ceremonial acknowledgement supply no separate Chaos. The Revenue Empire super-event presents the already achieved economic milestone and does not add a second reward merely for displaying it.

A hostile break without any prior or potential economic relationship may still have its user-requested diplomatic consequences, but does not automatically qualify as the major international Chaos event. A war already counted by the shared tension and casualty systems is not charged again under a generic Event 81 war-start amount.

## Acceptance stories

### A small independent taxpayer

The country receives a clearly higher proportional assessment than a comparable major. Accepting removes the immediate enforcement crisis but visibly reduces usable capacity. The player can start a reform even when the remaining economy is too small to reserve a full factory. Completing industrial reform immediately returns part of the capacity, and the other claims remain visible. The player is neither inexplicably exempt nor permanently softlocked.

### A British colony

The country receives compulsory taxation with no refusal option, no British Tax Resistance category, and no exemption obtained through a British petition. A higher autonomy level alone changes nothing. Genuine independence unlocks the independent choices and restores valid prior consolidated history where relevant.

### A British-led independent ally

The country accepts automatically, completes three transitions, and stops at Nominal. It cannot queue final work and complete it while ENG still leads. Once a legitimate leadership or membership change ends the lock, the final transition becomes available. An already completed peaceful exemption survives a later voluntary faction join.

### An AI major and a human major

Both can refuse while sovereign and outside a British-led faction. Both receive the embargo and severe bilateral opinion consequences. Only the human-controlled major automatically creates the special Event 81 entitlement. Switching control after the fact does not rewrite the refusal history.

### Partial reform when an Evolution activates

An independent taxpayer has completed its industrial domain but not the other two. Evolution I increases the base schedules, but the industrial reduction remains. The active mission retains its quoted cost and duration. New external claims inherit the current external domain share instead of appearing as permanent unremovable taxes.

### A disappearing tax base

A large taxpayer exits, loses usable industry, changes resource ownership, or disappears. Britain's corresponding capacity, consumer-goods relief, trade privilege, and future asset claims change with it. The system does not continue collecting from a stale country account. A save and reload does not resurrect the departed contribution.

### A long peaceful campaign

The player completes twelve projects and four consolidations, seeing relief along the way. British objections can cost time and Political Power, but cannot prevent completion forever. Final consolidation removes the last tax and the temporary final floor without creating the hostile-exit embargo or goal.

### A military settlement

A refusal produces the correct entitlement. Britain chooses whether to fight through ordinary strategic rules. An applicable victorious settlement can end bilateral taxation or abolish the system globally, and these outcomes are visibly different. Unrelated diplomatic restrictions are not erased by Event 81 cleanup.

### Britain reaches its consumer-goods floor

A growing foreign tax base frees British industry up to the native floor. Further foreign consumer-goods contributions do not generate negative requirements or new factories. Removing a taxpayer reduces the credited relief only by that taxpayer's real contribution, without disrupting unrelated modifiers.

### Multiplayer disputes

Two human countries receive separate assessments, quoted costs, refusal outcomes, and targeted British relationships. A petition addressed to one cannot change the other's stage. Simultaneous exits do not overwrite the beneficiary, target the host by accident, or issue duplicate global announcements.

## Completion boundary

A complete implementation must reproduce these stories with actual game effects, not only correct descriptions. Source inspection and mathematical consistency are useful evidence, but cannot establish native trade direction, selective cleanup, multiplayer event targeting, rendering quality, or real AI probabilities without the relevant validation tools and later authorised testing.
