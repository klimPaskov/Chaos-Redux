# Part 5: Age of Heroes

## Evolution purpose and entry

Age of Heroes introduces material beings and forces from Greek myth. It builds on the ordinary Spartan state and remains compatible with every conquest route. Modernization, citizenship, armor, naval construction and normal Greek conquest are baseline development. They do not count as this evolution.

The first manifestation is fixed. A Laconian investigation culminates in the material presence of Castor and Polydeuces, the Dioscuri. Later undertakings concern Theban earthborn warriors, Talos on Crete and the birds of Stymphalus in Arcadia. Their origins are explicit so the narrative does not confuse the Spartoi with Spartans or present all Greek traditions as Laconian property.

The use of these traditions is based on ancient literary sources, with precise source notes in [Research and evidence](111_this_is_sparta_research_and_evidence.md). Their modern service agreements, combat statistics, Strain rules and political choices are authored fiction.

### Activation contract

| Requirement | Initial design |
| --- | --- |
| Global Chaos | At least 600 |
| Shared evolution setting | Age of Heroes is enabled for Event 111 |
| Country | The actual independent Spartan actor exists and is not capitulated |
| Territory | Sparta owns and controls the audited Laconia founding state |
| Institutions | Civic Settlement at least 30 and Military Adaptation at least 35 |
| State | No previous permanent seal, no duplicate active investigation, no completed current evolution receipt |

Qualifying country and Chaos callbacks make the investigation available. There is no new worldwide monthly scan and no recurring grant through the CXT daily hook. The same helper checks eligibility for a running Sparta and a high-Chaos birth. A new country receives its ordinary founding package first. It earns the civic and military prerequisites and then uses this same investigation. It never receives a second ancient host for starting in an evolved world.

The player may undertake the investigation or defer it for 180 days. Deferral leaves the baseline tree and route goals usable. The offer returns only if its requirements still hold. A disabled evolution leaves no consumed permanent eligibility flag and creates no supernatural actors.

Starting the investigation activates the evolution through the shared evolution framework and shows Mythic Strain at 0. This activation awards zero Chaos. It opens the Laconian undertaking and the visible outlines of the later mythic branch. Successful manifestation and actual service create `C111_HERO_PROOF`, with the separate +10 consequence defined in Part 1.

### The first undertaking

The Laconian investigation uses action `MY01`. Its anchor cost is 35 political power and 25 support equipment, with a 35-day duration. Existing technical education reduces the equipment requirement, while damaged supply lengthens the undertaking up to 60 days. No command power is required for the initial investigation, so a young state can enter the branch without an unrelated military-experience bottleneck.

The commitment is a real project in the controlled founding state. Loss of control pauses it for up to 30 days. If control is not restored, the project cancels, releases reserved resources and retains only the actual consumed work. It can be undertaken later after a 90-day recovery delay. The caller must not refund consumed equipment as an unspent deposit.

Successful completion creates the two unique mythic character records and offers a first service charter. The player sees what the charter permits, its Strain consequences and its withdrawal rules. Accepting a real command or training service makes the first material proof milestone eligible. Merely displaying a portrait, unlocking a focus or playing the associated scene is insufficient.

Ordinary route completion does not require any mythic site, artifact or character. The myth branch can be expanded through treaties, so a maritime commonwealth is not forced to annex Thebes or Crete solely to obtain access.

## Characters and unique identity

### Castor

Castor is a fictional mythic character with one globally unique event-owned identity. His normal role is a field commander oriented toward disciplined maneuver, cohesion and coordination. The starting skill anchor is equivalent to a competent experienced general, with a narrow mythic trait rather than maximum skill in every field. Initial trait targets are a modest organization advantage and faster planning, subject to installed modifier and stacking validation.

His service does not summon formations, create cavalry technology or confer invulnerability. He needs an actual assigned army. His offensive value falls when that army is badly supplied, even if the character himself is supernatural.

In the sacred dyarchy Castor becomes the native visible country-leader character representing joint rule. He leaves the free field-command role while holding the sovereign office. The country identity and political-spirit writing direction explicitly convey the co-rule of the twins. His portrait remains an individual image.

### Polydeuces

Polydeuces is a separate globally unique mythic character. His normal military direction concerns close combat, cohesion and training. He may serve as a commander or occupy a senior training office, with one active service assignment at a time. The training effect is narrow and capped, with no simultaneous stacking of a full commander benefit, a full advisor benefit and a tournament presence.

He is also the specific mythic entrant for the Wrestling Tournament connection. Entry requires actual manifestation, current availability, the tournament owner's Open Division and a valid invitation or entry contract. His mythic nature remains visible to that system. He cannot be entered as an ordinary human merely because his body resembles one.

In the sacred dyarchy he holds the second sovereign office and a real senior military office consumer. The native advisor surface uses a separate 65×67 composition. His commander and tournament assignments still require explicit release from conflicting duties. The UI must not imply that selecting his tournament entry leaves an equally powerful duplicate at the front.

### Shared service rules

Before the final constitutional choice, both characters can exist and be visible, but only one may provide an active field-command or national training service at a time. The other remains available for civic custody, recovery and the next rotation. This makes the pair a flexible institution without doubling every army bonus.

Assignment changes have a 30-day anchor cooldown, shortened to 21 days by a mature service institution and lengthened to 45 after a rupture. A character engaged in a tournament, a rescue undertaking or recovery is unavailable for another assignment. The actual status is checked again when the receiving action commits.

A service term lasts 120 days, with its ordinary duty cost paid when the term is accepted. The player can renew, change assignment or allow withdrawal. A term ending during a live battle must use a validated safe removal rule rather than deleting units or corrupting an army. No daily toggling between mortal and divine realms is simulated.

A defeated or withdrawn character keeps its unique identity and history. A cooldown cannot create a fresh copy of the same hero. Death, permanent withdrawal, release and temporary recovery are distinct outcomes. A completed permanent seal prevents re-summoning through the original investigation.

## Military families

There are three new mythic military families. Together with the returned host and modern Spartan cadre, this gives five complete custom military packages for the event. Each requires its real equipment, native consumer, icon and counter where applicable, model, animation, source-backed sound, supply behavior, reinforcement route and cleanup contract.

### Common representation

Manifested beings are represented by their own equipment or entity records. Human liaison and maintenance staff draw from real recruitable manpower. A battalion may not add its supernatural bodies to the general human manpower pool and then let the player disband them into ordinary infantry. The preferred representation uses creature equipment for the impossible combatants and a small explicit human support complement. Exact native manpower, equipment and wing-size semantics must be proved before that representation is accepted.

Every creation or replacement has a bounded manifestation receipt. Captured equipment, lend-lease, licensing, template copying, country annexation and conversion must not duplicate a unique entity or bypass a family cap. Where the engine permits captured creature equipment, owner callbacks must provide an exact, event-driven disposition. A broad periodic scan that deletes arbitrary foreign equipment is not acceptable.

The first operational nonhuman family produces `C111_IMPOSSIBLE_FORCE` once for the whole evolution. The second and third families do not each repeat that Chaos award.

| Family | Strategic role | Planning cap | Concrete counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spartoi | Reliable short-range assault and holding power in an infantry army | Four earthborn companies under interim custody, each representing about 500 beings and a small human liaison group | Artillery, encirclement, supply denial and loss of the Theban custody site |
| Talos | A unique heavy defensive guardian with a limited breakthrough role | One entity and one field consumer globally | Modern anti-tank weapons, aircraft, movement constraints, repair burden and disruption of its binding |
| Stymphalian birds | Short-range reconnaissance and light ground harassment | One native wing under interim custody, with about one quarter of a contemporary full CAS wing's operational effect | Fighters, anti-aircraft fire, range, weather, nesting access and fragile organization |

These caps are design maxima. The actual deployable cap is the lower of the constitutional limit, supported site capacity and the country's trained liaison capacity. It cannot be raised by temporarily moving a hero between jobs or releasing and reannexing the same territory.

The constitutional family allowances belong to Event 111 globally. They are never granted afresh to each recipient. One owner ledger counts active consumers, pending paid reservations and surviving equipment held for redeployment or recovery across all authorized countries. The sum must fit the live family allowance. A verified transfer moves the existing receipt and count. Disbanding, capture, licensing, lend-lease, annexation or template copying cannot create a second allowance. Actual destroyed beings leave the live count, but their replacements still require a new paid manifestation, valid custody access and available capacity. Reducing a constitutional cap requires the disclosed safe release of any excess before the new settlement completes. Stored surviving beings cannot conceal the excess.

General foreign draws through Event 019 are ineligible for these mythic families. A foreign derivative is permitted only by an explicitly authored transfer that preserves its Event 111 origin, real recipient custody or access to the registered site, trained human support, paid manifestation receipt and the same global count. It inherits no Spartan polity, hero, focus, constitution or achievement progression. Missing origin, unsupported transfer accounting or impossible exact cleanup rejects the transfer and blocks that consumer's registration. It does not justify deleting unrelated foreign forces or claiming an unaudited engine capability.

### Spartoi

The Theban undertaking does not reward conquest of Thebes with an automatic army. It establishes lawful access to the relevant site, a controlled manifestation area and a trained cadre capable of directing the earthborn force. The source tradition's internal fighting informs the binding risk. It does not require reproducing an ancient scene as a modern historical fact.

The first completed undertaking permits one company. Further companies use separately paid formation actions up to the live cap. A company is designed around a manifested equipment count rather than ordinary recruitable soldiers. The balancing anchor is stronger cohesion and defense than an equivalent light infantry component, with poor flexibility, modest speed and a clear disadvantage against modern concentrated firepower.

Companies use ordinary supply, transport and supporting artillery. They do not carry an entire independent industrial or recruitment package into a receiving country. The owner can register the family for documented land consumers only under the global allowance and origin contract above, with every required callback. An authorized derivative occupies the same allowance as a Spartan-held company. It cannot replenish from ordinary human recruitment or an unrelated country's Event 019 draw.

Casualties reduce the real equipment count. Replacement requires access to the site, an available manifestation allowance and a new paid ritual. It adds Strain. Disbanding a formation returns only validated surviving equipment and human support personnel under the actual receipt. It never restores the consumed manifestation allowance simply because the template disappeared.

### Talos

Talos is a unique Cretan guardian. There is one body, one persistent identity and one current location or recovery state. Control of Crete grants access to attempt the undertaking, while an accepted Cretan custodial treaty can grant the same access without transferring sovereignty. The treaty identifies the real owner, controller, site, permitted service and conditions for recall.

The restoration needs a developed technical institution, support equipment, reserved civilian capacity and a supplied site. The country must be able to maintain the guardian before it can deploy it. Its first service is coastal defense or defense of an agreed state. Offensive deployment requires a later military study and an explicit custody revision.

Its battlefield target is high local defense and powerful short attacks, with slow speed, poor recovery and meaningful vulnerability to modern anti-armor weapons and air attack. Its durability should exceed an ordinary infantry component, while its operational radius and repair burden keep it from replacing a tank army. Its representation is a unique heavy entity with a real human support complement, not a free pool of thousands of armored vehicles.


Movement between islands and continents uses a valid native transport path. If the installed heavy-land representation can embark, it needs normal convoy movement and can be intercepted. A relocation action may only reproduce a legal, paid, timed transfer after the source entity is safely withdrawn and the destination proved. It cannot teleport a fighting guardian or duplicate it in both locations.

A severe defeat places the unique body into a damaged or lost state. Recovery is a specific undertaking at the actual site or under a negotiated return agreement. A destroyed binding cannot be repaired by manufacturing another generic equipment item. Permanent destruction or release closes the creation path, while salvage can provide a limited research reward with no new Talos.

### Stymphalian birds

The Arcadian undertaking concerns containment and limited direction of a dangerous flock. It requires a compatible airfield consumer, trained handlers and a secure nesting site. Its initial role is short-range reconnaissance and light support near friendly ground forces. It does not grant strategic bombing, jet performance or automatic naval superiority.

The native wing-size rule is verified at implementation. The planning cap is one legal wing, with the flock's total performance tuned to roughly one quarter of a contemporary full CAS wing. If the engine uses a fixed wing size of 100, the family uses that legal size and adjusts statistics. It does not publish an impossible 20-aircraft wing by assumption.

The birds have limited range, low survivability against modern fighters and substantial disruption from anti-aircraft fire. The army still needs ordinary aircraft, air defense and naval escorts. Their custom model and animations must fit the real aerial consumer, with an original green-styled military icon where that consumer requires one. A land battalion counter is not created as an unused substitute for an air-wing identity.

Losses are replenished through a bounded nesting undertaking using the family receipt. A wiped-out wing does not reappear on a daily callback. A site captured by an enemy suspends replacement and can force withdrawal after the agreed response window. A treaty holder can also revoke access under its explicit terms.

## Mythic Strain

Mythic Strain ranges from 0 to 100. Its immediate band, active sources and next consequence appear in the decision category. The value changes through meaningful actions and one bounded owner cadence. It is not a hidden random punishment.

| Band | Status and consequences |
| --- | --- |
| 0–24 | Stable custody. Ordinary agreed service functions normally |
| 25–49 | Sustained pressure. Further undertakings remain available, with clear upkeep and cooldown information |
| 50–74 | Stressed bindings. New manifestation costs rise, recovery slows and the player is warned which site is most exposed |
| 75–89 | Rupture warning. New offensive overdrive is blocked, and a 90-day containment window opens for the exposed site |
| 90–100 | Active rupture where a real exposed family remains. The chosen family stands down or breaks its service, with a concrete local consequence and a recovery undertaking. If all powers are actually safe and inactive, resumption is blocked while pressure recovers and no physical incident is fabricated |

Crossing a band does not itself add Chaos. Only the real loss of control from a rupture can create `C111_RUPTURE`.

The cadence is scheduled only while this event's mythic state is active. It follows a durable due date so changing controllers or saving and loading cannot accelerate recovery. On each tick it reads the bounded registered sites and family records, records actual effects and schedules the next due date. It is not part of the registration-only CXT daily hook.

The arithmetic clamps after each committed transaction. Threshold consequences use the post-transaction value and one episode receipt. A large action that moves directly from 70 to 95 causes one rupture episode with a clear origin. It does not award both a warning and several duplicated failures.

### Exposed-site selection

The next threatened site is selected deterministically. A broken treaty has first priority, followed by an unsupplied site, a lost controlled site, and then the longest continuously overdriven operational family. A stable tie uses a documented priority order and is shown to the player before the deadline. The game does not secretly select the most expensive asset after the player has paid to protect a different site.

A rupture affects one family or registered site initially. A second affected site requires sustained continued overuse during the recovery episode and an explicit warning. This is the only upgrade from the +3 to +5 Chaos severity band. There is no chain that automatically spawns hostile countries across the map.

### Warning episode and deadline contract

Entering 75–89 with a real exposed service creates one episode with a named site, material cause, start date and due date exactly 90 days later. Exposure means an operational supernatural service or an unresolved dangerous binding with an actual entity at the site. A permanently released family or a safely withdrawn, inactive family in lawful supplied custody is not exposed. The episode keeps its original target and due date through ordinary value changes, ownership changes, save and load, and service toggles. It cannot restart as a fresh 90-day warning when Strain briefly falls below 75.

`MY11` quotes its actual scaled completion date beside the remaining warning time. Its 90-day work anchor can take 75% to 150% of that duration, rounded by the verified timer contract. The project deadline and the original warning deadline are separate. Starting repair never postpones the warning. If repair cannot finish in time, the confirmation must say so and identify safe `MY10` withdrawal as the immediate mitigation where it is feasible. The player may still undertake the repair, but the interface cannot imply that payment alone protects the exposed site.

A completed `MY10` can neutralize the episode's active exposure only after the actual service ends and the beings reach lawful supplied custody or a valid permanent release. Requesting withdrawal during a battle is insufficient. The warning clock continues until native safe removal is confirmed. A broken treaty, dangerous abandoned binding or enemy-held entity remains exposed until its real obligation is resolved. Repair that fulfills the named obligation and brings Strain below 75 can also neutralize exposure. The record then becomes dormant with its original date and history, without a rupture merely because that date later passes.

The affected service cannot reactivate while its dormant warning remains unsettled. Close and rearm the episode only after the material breach is repaired or lawfully released, all related entities have a valid disposition, and Strain has remained below 50 for 30 continuous days. A later warning needs a new material exposure after that recovery. Switching assignments, moving a border or canceling and reopening the same project cannot reset the episode or repeat its outcome receipt.

At the original due date, unresolved real exposure raises Strain to at least 90 and executes one targeted rupture. The native local consequence must occur before any Chaos receipt is eligible. A direct increase to 90 or more does not wait for this deadline. It selects a still-exposed site through the same priority rule and creates one actual rupture episode. With no exposed service, high Strain blocks resumption and follows ordinary safe recovery. It cannot invent a destroyed facility, a hostile entity or a Chaos award.

### Overdrive menu

Overdrive never grants a universal attack bonus to every unit. The player chooses one supported operational use for a currently active family. Castor can assist a specified army's planning and recovery. Polydeuces can support a short assault-training or close-combat task. Spartoi can hold a nominated supplied front. Talos can concentrate on the defense or assault of its current eligible theater. Birds can increase their local reconnaissance and harassment effort.

Each effect needs a real scoped native consumer. If the desired army or theater scope is unsupported, the implementation must redesign that individual operation within the accepted role and seek a concrete design review. It must not silently make a local promise into a country-wide combat bonus. The first implementation uses only the subset whose scoped behavior is proved and leaves no falsely advertised button for an unsupported effect.

## Custody, rupture and external counterplay

Every active family has a registered site, current custodian, active agreement, service scope and restoration state. Site records persist through changes of government and border control. A new owner does not inherit Sparta's oath, automatically gain a creature or erase a broken agreement.

Before a rupture the owner can stand down the endangered service, supply the site, honor an overdue treaty, complete relief or permanently release the power. Each response says which specific obligation it resolves. Paying a generic cost while leaving the original breach in place cannot satisfy the mission.

An active rupture removes the affected supernatural service and applies a bounded consequence at its actual location. Examples include a damaged restoration workshop, a disrupted airfield, a local supply interruption or the withdrawal of a hero. Actual building damage uses the shared helper with precise targets. Any civilian loss uses the exact civilian-loss and death-ledger path. The design does not invent casualty totals merely to make a scene dramatic.

Enemies can use conventional warfare against all deployed units. They can also capture a registered site, break a supply corridor or negotiate with a treaty custodian. A controlled site enables a scoped countermeasure undertaking that denies replacement or removes an unlawful binding. It does not transfer a free creature to the captor.

## Final constitutional overlays

All three overlays require a completed normal route charter, Civic Settlement at least 65, Military Adaptation at least 75, the twins materially present and a stable custody record. Mythic Strain must be below 50. The sacred dyarchy and coercive mandate additionally require global Chaos at least 800. A prior permanent seal blocks both.

The choice commits through `MY14`, with a cancellation-first confirmation and a full description of the new cap, leader effect, diplomatic consequences and ongoing Strain. It replaces the political spirit stage within the existing three-slot budget. The route's geography and ordinary postwar obligations continue to apply.

### Mortal custodianship

Mortal institutions retain sovereignty and make the right to refuse supernatural service part of the settlement. The twins can continue their agreed military or civic roles. The country preserves the political form it previously chose, including broader citizenship where adopted.

The limit becomes three earthborn companies, one Talos and one bird wing. Honored custody reduces Strain by an additional 2 per cadence. Coercive binding and compulsory relocation are closed. Negotiated access, voluntary service and permanent release remain available. This is the strongest option for a stable league, a maritime commonwealth or a state that wants mythic allies without an escalating cult of conquest.

Its late payoff is durable cooperation and fewer disruptions to ordinary institutions. It does not award a fourth research slot merely for choosing a moral direction. The achievement route requires completing a difficult ordinary campaign and sustaining the resulting order.

### Sacred dyarchy

The manifested twins assume the sovereign offices through an explicit constitutional settlement. Castor becomes the visible native leader. Polydeuces holds the second sovereign and senior military office. ### Coercive divine mandate

Mortal rulers claim the authority to impose a wider order through proven supernatural force. They retain the existing institutional leader identity and route flag. This path does not invent a photograph of an ancient ruler or replace the government with an unmanifested Olympian.

The cap rises to six earthborn companies and two legal bird wings, while Talos remains unique. At least one additional trained liaison institution and one supplied additional custody site are required for the higher cap to become usable. The extra forces are paid for individually. They are not free rewards at constitution completion.

Active-service pressure receives +2 Strain per cadence. Coercive site demands become available, with real refusal, war and occupation consequences. Broken treaties are more difficult to repair. The military institution can gain one narrow operational advantage through its existing spirit stage, with an explicit tradeoff in recovery or civilian cooperation.

Choosing the constitution itself adds no Chaos. The first actual coerced regional settlement using operational mythic power creates `C111_COERCIVE_MANDATE`. The route remains vulnerable to a conventional coalition and to external containment of its finite registered sites.

## Evolution presentation and writing direction

The first proof scene depicts the actual twins and a tangible service. It uses one primary image consumer because this first manifestation is fixed. The writing should make their presence and the state's choice clear without a finished quotation or a copied line from modern entertainment.

Theban material uses earth, newly emerged armed figures and controlled disorder. Talos uses a singular immense bronze body, a defined vulnerability and human-scale technical support. Stymphalian material uses a dangerous flock and the contrast in scale between birds, air defenses and modern aircraft. All are fictional depictions grounded in the cited mythic traditions.

The sacred-dyarchy leader overlay is the one planned animated portrait treatment. It appears only after the actual constitutional outcome. It requires genuinely authored frame changes, an explicitly mapped runtime consumer, a static fallback and the image and frame limits in Part 7. Repositioning or filtering a still image is not acceptable animation.

Material mythic proof, sacred sovereignty and an internationally significant defeat each have distinct presentation conditions. A small failed investigation or a local damaged workshop receives a report. It does not consume the global defeat super-event.

## Aftercare and continuity

Loss of Laconia suspends heroic assignments and the central custody institution after the native safe-removal window. It does not erase the characters' history or create a new investigation. Site treaties elsewhere can preserve a recovery location only if their actual terms permit it.

Annexation cancels unpaid requests, closes active services and moves unique entities into their specified captured, destroyed, withdrawn or protected state. The owner-side callbacks perform exact cleanup for known consumers. A country restored later can recover still-existing institutions and characters through their records, but cannot refund lost troops, reset Strain history or regain a permanently released Talos.

Permanent sealing closes the original manifestation path and starts 180 days of aftercare. Surviving human support personnel return through a conserved native transfer. Material creatures follow the selected release or containment outcome. Ordinary factories, research, route progress and human citizenship remain. Completion of the aftercare can award the one bounded Chaos reduction in Part 1.

The late success state is a country that can govern its chosen empire, league or maritime network while honoring or consciously confronting the obligations of mythic power. The late failure state remains playable through ordinary war, retreat, political compromise and containment. Every route can still end as a human Spartan state with a modern army.
