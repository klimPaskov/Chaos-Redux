## Checks used to accept the corrections

The parent inspected the structured findings and all forty original AI policy rows, reviewed the accepted equations and warning contract, and reconciled the resulting clauses across the owning parts. The cadre rule is concrete. For ordinary deployed manpower `O` and quota tier `p`, renewable capacity is `Q = max(2000, floor(p*O/(1-p)))`. Actual cadre manpower `M`, certified surviving original attribution `E` and pending contemporary admissions `P` must satisfy `M - E + P <= Q` after each proposed admission. Deduplicated gross losses consume original attribution first where individual losses cannot be distinguished. This is conservative eligibility accounting. It is not a claim that the native game identifies each historical person.

The warning check distinguishes an active dangerous service from a safely inactive family. A warning timer cannot be canceled by requesting withdrawal while the real consumer remains engaged. Conversely, the design cannot create a physical rupture or Chaos award without a real exposed entity and local consequence. This closes the timer mismatch while preserving the cost of delaying mitigation.

The shared containment episode distinguishes its paid work from its observation record. The factory reservation occupies the actor's work budget while material work is underway. The same 180-day outcome is observed by several consumers without charging a second slot, waiting another automatic period or awarding Chaos several times.

These are specification and arithmetic checks. They do not constitute implemented unit tests, native scripting validation, probability simulation, a rendered GUI check or game evidence.
