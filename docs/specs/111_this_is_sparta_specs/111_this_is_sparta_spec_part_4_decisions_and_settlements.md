# Part 4: Decisions, missions and territorial settlements

This part defines Event 111's ordinary decision systems. It uses the country and military package in [Part 2](111_this_is_sparta_spec_part_2_country_and_military.md), the route sets in [Part 3](111_this_is_sparta_spec_part_3_focus_routes.md) and the shared values and milestone accounting in [Part 1](111_this_is_sparta_spec_part_1_core.md). Age of Heroes uses the same transaction and interface rules through its own specification.

All semantic action and mission keys are planned consumers, not final installed script IDs. All labels are writing directions. The numerical costs below are first-pass design anchors. An implementation may calibrate their material value after inspection, but it must preserve the stated sacrifice, caps, affordability and outcome.

## 1. Interface and action budget

Use native decisions and missions. Four ordinary category consumers organize the work without introducing a general management window.

| Category | Main purpose | Primary actions and presentation |
|---|---|---|
| `111_category_home` | Local survival, civic government and production | Emergency supply, civic compact, industrial project and arms contract. A simple category picture is permitted. |
| `111_category_agoge` | Adult training, finite-cohort conversion and modern force development | Intake, conversion and the relevant exercise or equipment context. A simple category picture is permitted. |
| `111_category_campaign` | The selected route, its next objective and operational preparation | Objective selection, diplomatic offer, campaign preparation and route proclamation. This category contains the one compact exact-shape state-puzzle panel and no competing category picture. |
| `111_category_settlement` | Regional administration, integration and broken obligations | Postwar settlement, regional integration and the contextual charter-repair action. A simple category picture is permitted. |

Approximately six primary actions are visible in a category at once. Contextual target selection reveals the relevant actions instead of listing every foreign state. This is a readability budget, not an assumed engine row limit. The human selector prioritizes a manageable set of relevant targets and can move to another valid target. The AI evaluates all eligible targets through the same predicate.

The value table shows Civic Settlement and Military Adaptation, both from zero to 100. Mythic Strain appears only after actual activation of Age of Heroes. Public status for a port, partner, institution or project is qualitative. The UI does not reveal a fourth persistent meter under another name. Native manpower, equipment, political power and command power remain normal resources.

At most three concurrent player-accepted Event 111 work undertakings are active for the same acting country across all its categories, including evolution work. Their mission consumers share this budget. At most two may be territorial or industrial programs, and at most one may be an intake or conversion. Sparta's ordinary and mythic work shares this one budget. Outsider containment uses the acting outsider's equivalent budget, so a full Spartan queue cannot prevent an enemy undertaking. A player who selects a mythic investigation must have a free slot or explicitly finish or cancel another program. Charter problems are recorded as qualitative issues until a repair mission is selected. Nine overseas obligations do not create nine simultaneous accepted work undertakings.

This includes the 90-day regional or partner persistence, final-charter contribution period, 180-day sealing or containment durability and similar achievement observations. Unavoidable Strain warnings, service terms and cooldowns likewise remain independent durable conditions shown in concise status. A selected paid repair such as `MY11` does consume a slot. `MY10` remains freely available with all slots occupied, so the queue cannot make mitigation impossible. Starting `MY12` may require pausing or canceling an existing undertaking, with honest treatment of actually consumed resources and completed work. This distinction cannot be used to hide a fourth active construction, training or binding project.

## 2. Shared transaction contract

Every paid action follows one quote, confirmation and commit path. The quote identifies the current actor, selected target, region-set revision, actual costs, duration, expected reward and important failure conditions. Confirmation defaults to cancel. Acceptance rechecks authority, target, resource availability and the quoted version. A changed owner or exhausted stockpile creates a revised quote instead of executing stale terms.

No action spends more than four distinct cost types, and command-power cost is at most 60. At most three inline cost values appear with correct texticons. The full tooltip lists every cost, including all equipment types and reserved industrial capacity. The base, blocked and tooltip custom-cost strings use one inclusive affordability component. Equality with the quoted cost is affordable. The actual debit uses that same component.

Factory commitment means actual civilian industrial capacity reserved for a stated duration. It is not a new bankable currency or a balance of abstract factory points. If an installed native or shared mechanism cannot represent the proposed commitment faithfully, its implementation gate must choose a real construction or bilateral industrial workflow before enablement. A tooltip cannot claim a material sacrifice that the effect does not impose.

The durable receipt records:

- Event identity, acting country, selected country and selected geographic or unit role.
- Route, institutional stage and region-set revision at commitment.
- Exact quoted and actually paid amounts, including equipment identity and quality where relevant.
- Capacity reservations, start date, native deadline, objective window and promised counterpart delivery.
- Current authority, success, failure, cancellation and refund-consumed states.
- Cohort, territory, partner and milestone source receipts needed to prevent duplication.

Success is evaluated before timeout on the same tick. A mission cannot reward success without a valid setup receipt. Objective completion automatically delivers the result. It does not require another paid completion click. Native deadline changes and any helper display update together. An effect's removal duration is separately specified and never assumed to equal the mission's timeout.

Authority-loss cancellation is distinct from failure. Annexation, loss of a selected unit, a country switch or a target disappearing can invalidate a project without proving that its ordinary objective was failed. The cancellation path accounts for surviving personnel, unused equipment and remaining capacity commitments. Material actually consumed, destroyed or delivered is never refunded as if still in storage.

## 4. Survival and political obligations

Emergency supply begins with an affordable home action. It directs real local labour and industrial attention to the capital connection, existing damage, transport distribution or a small specified works project. It cannot conjure a strategic reserve or repeatedly refill divisions. A missing basic infrastructure consumer must use the real project fallback chosen in the map and country package.

The associated mission checks that the selected home connection remains functional and avoids severe shortage for a disclosed 15-day observation period. The exact installed supply predicate must be verified. If direct supply inspection cannot support a reliable objective, use the completed infrastructure and delivered-equipment condition, state the narrower promise and remove any unsupported supply claim. Failure loses 5 Civic Settlement and opens a revised smaller repair plan. It does not delete the country or create another arrival.

The civic compact records actual rights, contributions and institutional authority. Political selection determines its terms. A renewed dyarchy must settle its relationship with modern residents, civic reform must deliver its promised participation, and military supremacy must account for emergency powers and any promised return of civilian authority. Completion changes the polity stage in its existing spirit slot.

A broken promise can cost 10 Civic Settlement and replace that slot with a contested stage. The response offers mediation, a less demanding compact or an explicit coercive alternative with its consequences. There is no automatic civil war every time the value crosses a threshold. Repeated emergency compromise can restore Civic Settlement only to 40. Higher values require durable institutions and fulfilled regional obligations.

Founding compact, constitutional ratification, first admitted civic intake and distinct regional settlements are separately guarded sources. The first admitted contemporary civic intake grants 5 Civic Settlement once. First regional settlement gains are 5 each, capped at 25 across ordinary route expansion. Repeating the same province, renaming a client or replacing a lost port never creates a new first-region source. These gains complement the shared first-milestone accounting without adding independent Chaos awards.

After a valid route charter and 180 days of fulfilled civic and regional duties, the existing compact family offers a mature institutional variant. Its completed real administrative work grants 10 Civic Settlement once. The eligibility holding period is an independent receipt, followed by the paid compact undertaking. This is further development of the existing polity, not another national spirit or public value.

The shared regional pool also permits deeper civic-service work in five distinct Greek districts: the Peloponnese, Attica and Central Greece, Thessaly and Epirus, Greek Macedonia and Western Thrace, and the Greek islands. Each uses the existing compact or settlement family with a real local-service outcome, costs and deadline. Each can contribute 5 once within the same total 25-point pool. They are optional civic development within the already defined homeland, with no additional mandatory charter geography. A broad mainland or island settlement that has already credited its designated district consumes that district's source, so deeper work there does not pay again. State splits and changes of partner preserve that source identity. The other districts let Hegemony improve its civic order without inventing another foreign conquest.

| Civic source role | Geographic mapping inside `111_geo_greek_homeland` | Credit reconciliation |
|---|---|---|
| `111_civic_district_peloponnese` | The Peloponnesian mainland role, including the new Laconian state | A broad first Greek-mainland or Greek-region civic award consumes this district's credit. |
| `111_civic_district_central` | Attica and Central Greece, including Euboea where it belongs to the mapped central state package | Deeper central civic service supplies its own once-only credit. |
| `111_civic_district_thessaly_epirus` | Thessaly and Epirus within the fixed homeland | Excludes Albanian territory and all states assigned to the other districts. |
| `111_civic_district_northern` | Greek Macedonia and Western Thrace within the fixed homeland | Excludes Vardar Macedonia and Bulgarian territory. |
| `111_civic_district_islands` | Remaining Greek-homeland island states after the four mainland allocations | Includes Crete where independently represented. Excludes Rhodes and Cyprus, and cannot reuse a coastal state's islands already assigned with that whole state. |

The installed crosswalk assigns each Greek-homeland state to exactly one of these five district roles. Mixed mainland/island states stay whole and take one assignment. The island role is the exact remaining set, not another overlapping coastline mask. A required-region or basin award that uses the same assigned territory consumes the corresponding district source rather than adding a second credit. Non-Greek regional sources likewise have a non-overlapping credited-territory allocation. The source ledger and its shared 25-point cap are authoritative for both ordinary settlement and deeper civic variants.

Record an earned civic ceiling from the founding value and actual one-time institutional or regional credits. The maximum from the listed ordinary sources is 95 in weak/normal openings and 90 in severe/late openings when the full regional pool is earned. A western state using only its four required administration groups can reach 90 in weak/normal and 85 in severe/late after the mature compact. The Hegemony districts, Alexander regions, Thermopylae regions plus optional Greek civic work, and five colonial basins can each supply the full regional pool. These paths make the 85-point civic achievements reachable in every opening band without emergency farming.

Civic losses create a bounded restoration record identifying the broken promise and actual applied loss. Once the promise is materially repaired, an existing compact or regional-settlement variant can restore up to 10 lost points through paid work and a quiet observation period. Use the ordinary 50-PP and one-factory, 90-day commitment, with the appropriate 120- or 180-day mission deadline. Restoration cannot exceed the remaining documented loss or the earned ceiling. It does not establish a new civic high score from repeated emergency measures. A legitimately earned later one-time institution raises the ceiling even while an older breach remains, so unfortunate timing cannot consume the only route to an achievement. Political repression that remains in force is not a repaired civic promise.

## 5. The modern Agoge and returned-cohort conversion

The basic intake is 500 adults. A developed academy can train 1,000, and the mature network can train 2,000 per intake, with proportionate manpower and equipment requirements. These are training cohorts rather than an invented permanent manpower currency. The implementation uses native recruitment or a conserved deployment transaction. Manpower is debited once, either by the validated queue or the project, never both.

Cadre growth is limited by instructor capacity and an exact renewable quota. The founding 3,000-person contemporary local brigades are ordinary native infantry. They are not custom cadres and possess no original-returnee attribution. All opening manpower anchors in Part 2 remain unchanged.

| Quantity | Authoritative definition |
|---|---|
| `O` | Actual human manpower in Spartan-owned deployed ordinary-native land formations. Exclude returned-host and modern-cadre personnel, mythical bodies and their liaison staff, foreign-owned expeditionaries, garrisons, training queues and the unassigned manpower pool. A Spartan-owned formation does not leave accounting merely because an ally commands it. |
| `p` | Academy quota tier, 0.10, 0.20 or 0.25. |
| `Q` | Renewable contemporary cadre allowance, `max(2000, floor(p*O/(1-p)))`. The ratio preserves `p` as the intended cadre share of ordinary plus non-exempt cadre manpower. The 2,000-person floor is an explicit small-army exception. |
| `M` | Actual human manpower serving in the custom modern-cadre family under Spartan ownership. Counting semantics must match the audited native formation consumer. |
| `E` | Certified surviving original-returnee attribution presently included in `M`. This belongs to proven original people, not a template, equipment type or formation's nominal capacity. |
| `P` | All committed contemporary cadre intake and replacement manpower not yet included in `M`, including queues and reserved transfers. A person moves from `P` to `M` once and is never counted twice. |

Every proposed admission or refill must leave `M - E + P <= Q`. Evaluate the post-commit quantities, not a pre-payment snapshot. All modern recruits and all contemporary replacements count toward the renewable quota, whether they enter a new formation or replenish a converted one. A replenished division cannot retain its old exemption as if every new soldier were an original returnee. The original host and its converted survivors also cannot inflate `O` to create extra renewable capacity.

Original attribution is conserved by the founding cohort ledger. Conversion transfers only proved surviving original people from that ledger into `E`. Reorganization or a permitted physical transfer moves the same attribution with a single consumed source receipt. It never mints more. The total original-person allowance cannot grow after founding, and no replacement, template change, disbanding, ownership loop or cosmetic identity raises it.

Gross casualty evidence reduces the affected original attribution. If mixed-unit casualties cannot distinguish original and contemporary people, use the conservative rule `E_i_after = max(0, E_i_before - L_i)`, capped by the formation's actual remaining strength, where `L_i` is the deduplicated gross human loss supported by the native evidence. The rule consumes original allowance first. It records a conservative surviving attribution rather than inventing the identity of the dead. Contemporary replenishment increases ordinary quota use and never increases `E_i`.

Every automatic and decision-driven reinforcement path must enforce this rule. Net strength snapshots alone cannot do so, since losses and refill may cancel between observations. Implementation must prove supported gross-loss and admission accounting with safe native reinforcement control, or prevent automatic reinforcement and use a demonstrated conserved quota-checked admission path. The specification assumes no casualty-identity API or reinforcement command. If the installed engine cannot safely conserve and constrain the accepted consumer, that is an explicit enablement blocker.

If ordinary casualties or another real change reduce `Q` below existing non-exempt cadre strength, retain living formations and freeze further contemporary admission and refill. Reconcile unused pending capacity with its exact paid receipt, preserving actually trained people and consumed material. Do not delete living troops or refund deployed personnel to force the inequality. A returned-attribution loss similarly shrinks the inherited allowance. Contemporary recruits cannot continue filling the inherited excess after its original people have been lost. Conventional troops remain the main renewable growth path of a large empire.

Agoge training can also support ordinary contemporary formations through its general curriculum. A full elite quota therefore does not make the entire institution useless. Civilian, medical, logistics and technical contribution can satisfy the political admission framework without compulsory combat service. New recruits are adults from the existing eligible population. Wider recruitment requires the rights and settlement conditions of the relevant region.

The military adaptation ladder uses actual completed stages. Basic firearms familiarization contributes 5 once and is available below 25, using a safe site, contemporary liaison instructors and 100 existing modern rifles. Modern liaison contributes 10 once, basic technical education 10, verified support-service provision 10, the first equipped staff exercise 10 and mature staff integration 10. Intake tier completions and the first conversion contribute the amounts in the action table. The weak opening can therefore reach its first gate through 10 initial, 10 liaison and 5 familiarization, without requiring an already unlocked intake or research institution. Completing all listed stages and three intake tiers reaches at least 90 from that weak opening. Additional late equipment and doctrine milestones can bring the value toward 100 under the shared source register. Repeating a template, retraining the same cohort or equipping and unequipping one division cannot repeat a stage reward.

Conversion selects real surviving formations. The quote measures current personnel and equipment, reserves the required modern kit, records the selected unit and its certified original attribution, and withdraws it from the usable field force for the local training period. The result is the same surviving people in the modern family. Retired ancient equipment returns only if it actually survives and the design permits its reuse. Conversion never returns the soldiers to the national pool and also spawns them in a new unit. Any additional contemporary support or replacement personnel are separately accounted for within the ordinary quota and actual manpower source.

Loss of the training site suspends or cancels conversion according to the actual unit's legal location. If the selected unit was destroyed, its personnel cannot be refunded. If the operation is canceled while people and equipment remain safe, unused modern kit and surviving personnel return to their recorded source. Partial training can retain bounded experience, but it does not receive the first-completion adaptation reward. Ordinary modern replacements after conversion come from qualified contemporary manpower and always obey the renewable quota.

## 6. Construction and foreign delivery

Industrial projects choose one actual site and one concrete output. The quote displays whether the work repairs an existing asset or creates new capacity. Construction consumes the stated industrial commitment and applicable material. The first arsenal requires the preceding focus and completes a real military factory. A port project requires a legal coast and building capacity. Rail and supply projects require verified connected geography. A research facility must satisfy its institutional conditions before it helps unlock a slot.

A destroyed project does not refund completed work. Cancellation frees only remaining reserved capacity and returns only unused material. Ownership loss removes authority to continue. Partner projects require renewed consent if their government changes, with delivered equipment and sunk work retained in the history. No project adds factories simultaneously to Sparta and a partner from the same payment.

An arms supplier evaluates its own equipment deficit, current war, ideology and relations, industrial need, route safety and the proposed compensation. An AI seller never agrees solely because Sparta clicked a focus. Delivery quantity is capped by the negotiated amount and actual available stock. Late openings can negotiate more modern supplies when a real seller has them. They cannot buy technology that no supplier possesses.

If the international market or relevant industrial mechanism is unavailable, a bilateral event-owned contract preserves seller consent, exact inventory debit, real compensation and delivery delay. The fallback must be implemented with supported native or shared systems. A failed or intercepted delivery follows its actual material disposition. The same shipment cannot be refunded to the buyer, returned to the seller and delivered later.

## 7. Diplomacy and campaign preparation

The selected objective names the actual current owner, controller, required states and permitted sovereignty models. The route's legitimacy claim is described as an authored political position. Macedonia does not lose its historical identity because Sparta adopts Alexander's program. Persia does not become a generic enemy faction, and Greece is not automatically absorbed by a Hegemony focus.

Diplomatic offers state what each side gains and gives up. A league partner contributes specified territory to the common order while retaining government and accepted rights. A regional client accepts an actual subordinate or audited charter relationship. A coastal commonwealth partner accepts port-service obligations. Offers can include recognition, access, equipment, industrial service, defence or a limited state transfer. Every affected human-controlled country can accept or decline its own terms.

Existing guarantees, faction wars and unrelated opponents remain in force. A special peace action may conclude only the states and participants that the supported native mechanism and their consent allow. It cannot end an entire global war to settle one Spartan demand. If no legal local peace exists, the offer explains that limitation and normal war mechanics continue.

Preparation missions have actual objectives: repair a named port, establish a connected depot chain, place adequately equipped forces at a legal staging point, secure passage, provide the selected transports or complete an exercise. The player sees achievable material requirements. Enemy strength is a planning estimate, not a compulsory perfect-strength threshold that prevents a human from attempting a risky war.

On success, the material work remains and the selected operational receipt unlocks its action. Any temporary advantage must have a named unit or state consumer, a public duration and a supported native implementation. There is no extra nationwide fourth spirit. Failure consumes completed work, releases unused reservations and permits a reduced plan. Changing the Thermopylae corridor or Western entry approach costs 50 political power, clears the old exclusive preparation and retains only genuinely shared completed infrastructure.

## 8. Regional administration and integration

Postwar settlement begins only when its region has a valid governing relationship. An occupied capital does not settle all surrounding states. Direct administration requires actual ownership and control as prescribed by the route. Client and partner administration requires a current accepted charter and the relevant government's ownership and control. The implementation uses the same predicate as the puzzle.

A region's objective combines a functioning administrative site, supplied relief or reconstruction, the chosen legal arrangement and a quiet observation period. The normal project takes up to 180 days, with a 270-day version for a devastated or distant region. Its last 30 days require the disclosed obligations to remain fulfilled. A narrow partial result can repair infrastructure and reduce a local shortage without qualifying the complete regional receipt.

The completed receipt grants scoped production, recruitment or supply access appropriate to the region. Greek union administration can support direct citizen recruitment. A league administration coordinates real partners. Alexander's regions specialize in their actual assets and client contributions. Thermopylae's corridor and Iran settlements address expeditionary supply and occupation. Western settlements recover damaged Italian industry. Colonial basin settlements maintain ports and service agreements.

Broad conquest does not automatically grant cores. Within the authored Greek homeland, a specifically eligible state can gain a Spartan core after completed regional settlement, at least 180 days of continuous peaceful legal control, Civic Settlement at least 60 and, where native occupation applies, compliance at least 60 with resistance below 20. The citizenship program must actually include that population. Greece's political response and surviving claims remain relevant.

Outside the Greek homeland, integration grants supported native administration and recruitment improvements. It must conserve existing population and use the real occupation or state system. This package grants no extra-Greek cores. Its complete core scope is the Laconian founding core and eligible Greek-homeland states under the civic integration rule above. Implementers must not invent another core list for Iran, Italy or the Mediterranean. A player can sustain a larger modern army through subjects, accepted volunteers and lawful wider recruitment without making every conquered population a fictional ancient Spartan citizen.

Colonial service differs from territorial annexation. A treaty port can contribute only while its exact charter, access and material obligations hold. Mere docking rights do not count. A sovereign port still needs a working facility, route and administration. The Black Sea group must maintain real strait access. If access disappears, dependent service benefits suspend and the relevant puzzle requirement shows its blocked operational status.

Final charter eligibility requires every regional receipt specified in Part 3 and 90 days of continued valid contribution after its settlement takes effect. The optional Alexander eastern extension uses its Afghan, western-approach and Punjab settlements under the same rule. Historical receipts remain, while live dependent benefits suspend after loss. Restoration may require a new observation period or repair, but never repeats the original first-completion reward.

## 10. AI and acceptance criteria

The AI first protects the capital connection, obtains basic rifles, stabilizes the civic compact and develops renewable recruitment. It commits to one attainable route after evaluating actual geography, opponents, industry, access and institutions. It keeps enough resources to finish current projects before beginning another. It does not repeatedly offer a treaty a human has just refused, and a broken agreement changes its future willingness.

Target scoring values reachable objectives, legal supply, limited fronts, compatible relationships and incomplete required regions.
