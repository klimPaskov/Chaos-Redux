# Event 029: Evolutions and late crisis outcomes

## Evolution structure decision

Event 029 registers three evolutions.

The user fixed Evolution I at Chaos Tier, 600+.

The project planning rule permits one evolution stage per chaos tier.

The remaining higher tiers are Totalen Chaos at 800+ and World Collapse at 1,000+.

Evolution II therefore belongs to Totalen Chaos and Evolution III belongs to World Collapse.

The blank Evolution IV and Evolution V placeholders should not become extra registered stages at the same tiers.

Their intended depth is preserved through two late crisis outcomes inside the existing evolution tracks:

- The Gilded Sovereignty inside The Resource Curse
- The Bottomless Account inside Demons Beneath the Mine

These outcomes can have reports, decisions, modifiers, achievements, and distinct endings.

They do not create additional evolution-log rows unless the repository later changes the one-stage-per-tier rule.

This structure prevents the event from violating its planning contract while keeping the late-game political and supernatural content.

## Shared evolution rules

Each evolution is a mutation track layered onto the ordinary mine lifecycle.

The ordinary mine can continue developing, closing, transferring controller, suffering raids, and recovering while an evolution is active.

Evolution entry is paced through dynamic MTTH.

The default target is roughly 90 days after all required conditions are true.

The exact timing changes through pressure, development, order, legitimacy, policy, war, damage, prior incidents, and containment.

An evolution must not trigger simply because the global chaos value crossed a threshold.

The mine must also have a plausible local state.

A well-managed mine can delay or avoid an evolution for a long time.

An aggressively exploited mine can enter it quickly.

Each evolution has an active-event path and a pre-fire evolved-opening path.

### Active-event path

When a mine already exists, the evolution evaluates each active mine separately.

The evolved content appears for the current controller without requiring Event 029 to fire again.

The evolution records the current controller as actor and the mine state as the affected location through event-owned state tracking.

A mine that later changes controller keeps its evolved physical and local condition.

The new controller receives the related decisions and modifiers through the controller refresh.

### Pre-fire evolved-opening path

When Event 029 fires for the first time after the relevant chaos threshold, the opening package can begin with stronger risk factors.

The evolved opening should change the first incidents and starting values.

It should not replace the discovery, the exact 1,000 political-power grant, or the ordinary claim and development choices.

The evolution log should be recorded only when the evolved condition actually enters play.

### Disabled evolution behavior

Every evolution respects the Event Details evolution toggle.

A disabled evolution does not set its recorded flag, hidden pressure, decision access, modifier state, report pool, or late crisis route.

Baseline progression continues normally.

Disabling The Resource Curse does not block revenue settlement or corruption incidents that are ordinary baseline content.

It blocks the persistent evolved curse track and The Gilded Sovereignty.

Disabling Gold Disease blocks the obsessive syndrome, its movement rules, quarantine actions, and its crisis incidents.

Disabling Demons Beneath the Mine blocks supernatural pressure, bargains, and The Bottomless Account.

A save with an evolution already active should follow the repository's established toggle policy and must not be silently corrupted by removing required state.

## Evolution I: The Resource Curse

### Evolution tier

Chaos Tier, 600+.

### Core change

The mine stops functioning as one rich project and begins reshaping the controller's government, economy, and political incentives.

Revenue becomes easier to spend than to govern.

Officials, parties, officers, contractors, local bosses, and foreign intermediaries organize around access to receipts and licenses.

Ordinary productive sectors lose workers, investment, and political attention.

The state becomes dependent on mine payroll and contracts.

The national government becomes dependent on the mine's political-power and construction contribution.

### Entry conditions

The evolution becomes possible when all core conditions are true:

- global chaos is at least 600
- Event 029 and Evolution I are enabled
- at least one active Event 029 mine exists
- the mine is open, contested, or temporarily closed with a live administration
- the mine has delivered meaningful revenue or development

The evolution becomes more likely through:

- high Extraction Pressure
- high Mine Development
- low Revenue Legitimacy
- low Local Order
- central treasury monopoly
- patronage distribution
- exclusive foreign concession
- hidden beneficial ownership
- private guards
- repeated emergency contracts
- wartime requisition
- non-core or occupied extraction without local sharing
- multiple controlled mines
- poor stability
- weak institutions
- failed audit
- exposed theft without reform

The evolution becomes less likely through:

- citizens' dividend
- local revenue settlement
- stabilization fund
- transparent auction
- beneficial-owner disclosure
- published contracts
- successful audit
- diversified state development
- moderate pressure
- high legitimacy
- high order based on civil administration
- temporary controlled closure

### Pre-fire evolved opening

At 600+ chaos, a first firing can begin with a more politicized rush.

The opening should weight these incidents more heavily:

- competing ministries claim authority
- party organizations stake control over employment
- officials issue overlapping permits
- foreign intermediaries approach local power brokers
- the first shipment is undercounted
- the national budget immediately assumes future receipts
- local prices and wages detach from the rest of the country

The mine still begins in the claims phase.

The controller still has the chance to establish strong institutions before the evolution records.

### Visible mechanic changes

The evolution adds a clear curse stage to the category header and controller aggregate.

Extraction Pressure now increases the marginal reward and the rate of political capture.

Revenue Legitimacy becomes the main defense.

Mine Development can worsen dependence when the controller has not built a stabilization or diversification route.

Local Order produced through private or military force becomes less effective at containing corruption than order produced through accepted administration.

### Controller idea lifecycle

Use one staged idea or dynamic modifier.

Working lifecycle:

1. Windfall Administration
2. Captured Revenue State
3. Resource-Dependent Government
4. Reformed Resource Settlement
5. Entrenched Patronage

The idea changes through governance choices and results.

It should not stack one new spirit for each corruption event.

### Evolution decisions

#### Nationalize Extraction

This route removes private or foreign operating control and gives the state direct responsibility.

It can lower concession exposure and raise national control.

It can also concentrate corruption inside the state if oversight is weak.

A clean nationalization needs compensation, administrative capacity, contract review, and replacement management.

A violent or uncompensated nationalization can trigger sabotage, sanctions, security conflict, and lower legitimacy.

#### Distribute the Revenue

The controller creates a broad dividend or a local revenue share.

This reduces immediate contribution and raises legitimacy.

It is the strongest long-term defense when it is maintained across several payment cycles.

A one-time payment should not erase an entrenched curse.

#### Build a Stabilization and Diversification Program

The controller invests mine receipts in other industry, infrastructure, agriculture, services, or reserves.

The route should use real state projects, timed missions, and staged protection.

It should not grant a generic one-click diversification bonus.

Success lowers dependence and improves recovery after closure.

#### Tolerate Corruption

The controller accepts patronage as the price of political order.

This can stabilize a weak government in the short term.

It raises hidden capture, lowers effective receipts, and strengthens later company or elite autonomy.

#### Let Private Interests Dominate

The government grants wide discretion to concession holders, contractors, or domestic syndicates.

Development and immediate output rise.

Revenue legitimacy, sovereignty, and government control fall.

This route is the main path toward The Gilded Sovereignty.

#### Create an Independent Revenue Authority

The controller separates licensing, measurement, receipts, audit, and spending responsibilities.

The route costs administrative capacity and political support.

It is effective only when the authority is protected from removal, document destruction, and capture.

### Incident families

#### Patronage

- jobs allocated through party or officer networks
- relatives and allies receive contracts
- a local boss controls housing and payroll
- a ministry directs receipts to loyal districts
- a military commander claims emergency authority over shipments

#### Theft and accounting

- output undercounted at the assay office
- price or exchange manipulation
- missing train or convoy receipts
- forged contractor invoices
- duplicate equipment purchases
- false development projects
- hidden owners behind a winning bidder

#### Dependence

- ordinary factories lose workers
- local agriculture or services shrink
- the national budget assumes the mine will remain open
- construction projects stop when receipts are interrupted
- the controller refuses temporary closure because its political system depends on the mine

#### Political capture

- an advisor, official, or party faction becomes tied to mine interests
- an audit is blocked
- a contract is renewed without review
- private guards intimidate local authorities
- a cabinet crisis forms around nationalization or revenue sharing

#### Local resentment

- residents see roads and guards but no public benefit
- workers strike over a diverted dividend
- a colonial administration exports receipts while services deteriorate
- a local authority demands fixed control over revenue
- armed resistance targets the mine and leaves the wider government outside the immediate target set

### Containment and reform

Good management should be able to contain the curse.

Containment requires a sustained pattern of choices. One button cannot resolve it.

A credible reform path should include:

- successful audit
- public contract or state operating terms
- beneficial-owner disclosure where concessions exist
- revenue-sharing settlement
- development or stabilization fund
- moderate pressure
- removal or reform of abusive security
- replacement of captured administration
- maintenance over time

Containment can transform the controller idea into Reformed Resource Settlement.

The mine remains valuable and can still face ordinary incidents.

### Failure

Poor management can create:

- higher law or advisor costs
- political-power loss from theft and patronage
- lower stability
- weaker production outside the mine
- construction dependence
- local resistance
- blocked reforms
- stronger foreign leverage
- private-security autonomy
- recurring corruption reports
- a government crisis after closure or controller loss

Failure does not automatically remove the mine.

It makes the player more dependent on the same system causing the damage.

## Late Resource Curse outcome: The Gilded Sovereignty

### Classification

The Gilded Sovereignty is a late crisis outcome inside Evolution I.

It is not Evolution IV.

It does not receive a separate chaos-tier stage.

### Premise

The mine administration becomes a rival authority inside the state.

It controls payroll, housing, transport, claims, security, contracts, local courts, and access to the workings.

The central government still owns the state on the map.

The local authority can obey, bargain, resist, or defect depending on its interests.

No new country tag is required by default.

### Entry conditions

The outcome requires:

- The Resource Curse active
- high Mine Development
- very low Revenue Legitimacy
- high hidden capture
- high concession exposure or private-security autonomy
- prolonged private or patronage dominance
- government dependence on mine receipts
- no successful recent reform

Totalen Chaos increases the chance, but the route depends on local state.

### Visible changes

The category identifies that the government no longer controls every part of the mine system.

Some decisions become negotiations with the mine authority.

The controller's positive contribution can remain high.

The cost appears through law, sovereignty, security, foreign policy, and the risk that the authority survives a controller change.

### Player responses

#### Revoke the Charter

The government cancels the authority's legal basis.

This begins a political and security confrontation.

Success restores sovereignty and damages development.

Failure can close the mine, trigger guard resistance, or invite foreign pressure.

#### Buy Back the District

The government purchases claims, infrastructure, security contracts, and debt.

This is expensive and less destructive.

It is available only when the authority can be negotiated with and the controller has economic capacity.

#### Share Sovereignty

The government recognizes a special jurisdiction or revenue settlement.

The mine remains productive and partially autonomous.

The controller accepts a permanent political cost and lower freedom over closure, contracts, and security.

#### Recognize the Local Board

Workers, residents, local officials, or concession interests receive a formal governing body.

The outcome can improve legitimacy if the board is representative.

It can entrench capture if the board is controlled by guards or owners.

#### Violent Nationalization

The army clears the authority and seizes the works.

The route can restore control quickly.

It carries the highest risk of deaths, mine destruction, condemnation, and foreign intervention.

#### Let It Govern

The central state accepts the authority as the practical government of the mine district.

Output and private development remain strong.

The controller's national laws, labor policy, and diplomacy become constrained by the enclave.

### Transfer behavior

A new controller inherits the authority as a local actor.

The authority can offer continuity in exchange for recognition.

It can sabotage an occupier, cooperate with a concession sponsor, or negotiate with a liberated government.

The outcome should make conquest more complicated without creating a new map tag.

### End states

- sovereign authority dismantled and public administration restored
- negotiated autonomous mine district
- local representative settlement
- entrenched private enclave
- destroyed works after confrontation
- foreign-protected concession enclave
- permanent closure

### Achievement hooks

The Gilded Sovereignty supports No Man Owns the Mountain and Claim Jumper tracking.

## Evolution II: Gold Disease

### Evolution tier

Totalen Chaos, 800+.

### Nature of the syndrome

Gold Disease is a fictional obsessive greed syndrome.

It is not a biological pathogen.

It does not use the bioweapon outbreak registry, stockpile accidents, disease spread, vaccination, or shared cure systems.

It spreads through access, possession, counting, payroll, hidden material, shipments, returned workers, guard rotations, and command involvement.

The event should present behavior and consequence before giving the player a settled explanation.

### Core change

Workers hide material inside clothes and tools.

Officials divert shipments.

Guards fight over dust and specimens.

Engineers alter safety plans to reach richer ground.

Soldiers assigned to the district begin stealing from one another.

Families, gangs, and units barricade entrances and refuse relief.

Every group believes the wealth belongs to it personally.

### Entry conditions

The evolution becomes possible when:

- global chaos is at least 800
- Event 029 and Evolution II are enabled
- an active Event 029 mine exists
- the mine is open or contested
- enough workers, guards, officials, or shipments are exposed

The evolution becomes more likely through:

- high Extraction Pressure
- high hidden theft
- high-value portable commodity profile
- low Local Order
- low Revenue Legitimacy
- repeated wage or shipment disputes
- patronage
- private guards
- military control
- workers armed during an unresolved dispute
- deep mining
- multiple recent violent incidents
- refusal to close after a severe warning

The evolution becomes less likely through:

- moderate pressure
- revenue sharing
- controlled access
- secure official buying system
- workforce rotation with screening
- temporary closure
- sealed deep sections
- strong order and legitimacy

### Pre-fire evolved opening

At 800+ chaos, a new mine can open with unusually intense possession behavior.

Early incidents can include:

- the first survey team hiding specimens from one another
- guards searching workers before an official system exists
- a public fight over worthless rock believed to contain ore
- an official refusing to release the first assay
- families following shipment wagons

The full evolution should still enter through a paced incident or opening chain.

The event should not announce a disease on the discovery day.

### Visible mechanic changes

The category adds a Gold Disease condition and a concise crisis stage.

Extraction Pressure becomes the strongest accelerator.

Local Order can become misleading when armed groups impose their own control.

Revenue Legitimacy helps only when the revenue share is physically delivered and trusted.

Mine Development increases the number of people and systems exposed.

The controller sees the affected groups and current movement risk through reports and tooltips, not a complicated contagion map.

### Movement routes

Gold Disease pressure can move through bounded event-owned routes:

- worker transfer to another Event 029 mine
- shipment to the capital or a processing state
- guard or army rotation
- stolen material sold through a neighboring state
- pay transport
- concession staff returning to a sponsor

Movement creates incidents or temporary country effects.

It does not create a normal biological outbreak state.

It should not scan every state or country periodically.

Each movement occurs through a specific event, mission result, contract, shipment, or unit rotation action.

### Crisis decisions

#### Share the Revenue Immediately

The controller pays workers and local residents through a visible emergency settlement.

This can reduce the belief that every group must seize its share.

It lowers current receipts and works best with high legitimacy.

It is weaker after repeated broken promises.

#### Replace the Workforce

The controller removes affected teams and imports new workers.

This lowers immediate pressure and can move the problem through transport if screening fails.

The source conditions must also change or the replacement force becomes affected.

#### Impose Military Control

The army takes the entrances, payroll, and shipment route.

This can restore access and raise the number of armed affected people.

It is powerful and dangerous.

#### Quarantine the District

The controller restricts movement and suspends ordinary travel.

This reduces movement routes and raises shortages, smuggling, and legitimacy pressure.

It requires enforceable access control.

#### Close the Mine Temporarily

The controller stops extraction and removes current output.

This is the strongest ordinary pressure reduction.

It can trigger barricades or guard mutiny when groups refuse to leave valuable ground.

#### Seal the Deep Sections

The controller closes the areas associated with the strongest incidents.

This preserves upper workings and reduces output.

It is a main containment route when the source appears localized.

#### Controlled Rationing of Access

The controller limits shifts, guards, tools, and material handling.

This reduces pressure while preserving some output.

It requires high administration and order.

#### Destroy or Secure Confiscated Material

The controller removes seized specimens and stolen material from circulation.

Storage creates another target.

Destruction or burial lowers immediate risk and can raise worker anger or supernatural pressure when Evolution III is active.

### Incident progression

#### Early obsession

- hidden nuggets and samples
- altered records
- repeated searching and counting
- workers refusing to leave shifts
- guards stealing from lockers
- family disputes over small amounts

#### Organized capture

- gangs control a shaft
- guards create a private buying system
- officials issue false permits
- soldiers stop shipments
- workers barricade entrances
- a concession faction refuses inspection

#### Mass violence

- fighting over a shipment
- execution of suspected thieves
- guard mutiny
- worker killings
- family or community massacre
- military clearing of a barricade

Mass violence must use the Deaths and Condemnation systems when public evidence and responsibility exist.

#### Structural failure

- unsafe digging
- blocked ventilation
- sabotaged drainage
- explosives used during a dispute
- support timbers removed to reach visible material
- collapse during a barricade

### Containment

Successful containment should require several steps:

- reduce pressure
- stop uncontrolled movement
- restore legitimate payment
- remove or reform affected guards and officials
- secure records and shipments
- repair dangerous works
- maintain containment over time

A single quarantine click should not cure the crisis.

A mine can return to ordinary operation with a lasting susceptibility modifier.

A permanently closed or sealed mine ends most Gold Disease pressure.

### Failure outcomes

- temporary closure
- long-term loss of development
- damaged infrastructure
- permanent closure
- catastrophic collapse
- large civilian and military deaths
- loss or removal of officials, advisors, or commanders when the implementation has valid named targets
- armed-control crisis
- transfer of the mine to another controller
- national political scandal

Gold Disease should never be farmable for free population removal, compensation, political power, or repeated purge benefits.

## Evolution III: Demons Beneath the Mine

### Evolution tier

World Collapse, 1,000+.

### Core identity

The mine opens chambers containing a fictional supernatural force tied to valuation, possession, bargains, and the conversion of human authority into wealth.

The force is not an underground civilization.

It does not duplicate Event 018's caves or Oth-Kesh.

It has no ordinary country tag, army, or territorial invasion route by default.

Its power appears through the mine, its contracts, its workers, its ledgers, its officials, and the controller's choices.

### Cultural boundary

The design can draw broad inspiration from documented mining folklore about warning knocks, unseen underground workers, dangerous shafts, rituals, and the belief that the mine responds to conduct.

It must not copy a living mining deity, ritual, or community tradition and label it demonic.

The final supernatural entity, symbols, bargains, and incidents are fictional.

### Entry conditions

The evolution becomes possible when:

- global chaos is at least 1,000
- Event 029 and Evolution III are enabled
- an active mine exists
- the mine has opened deep workings or suffered a deep collapse
- deep-excavation pressure is high enough
- no incompatible terminal `world_end` state is active

At 1,000+ chaos, the evolution can enter while normal Event 029 progression remains active. A terminal route that has already set `world_end` blocks new Event 029 evolution jobs unless that route explicitly keeps this mine system compatible.

The evolution becomes more likely through:

- high Extraction Pressure
- repeated Drive Deeper decisions
- sealed sections reopened
- high Mine Development
- Gold Disease violence
- Resource Curse patronage and hidden contracts
- mass death inside the mine
- unsafe collapse recovery
- deliberate sacrifice or predatory policy
- refusal to close after impossible incidents
- low legitimacy
- a controller dependent on mine revenue

The evolution becomes less likely through:

- partial sealing
- permanent sealing
- low pressure
- successful evacuation
- refusal to exploit strange material
- controlled investigation
- high legitimacy and trusted administration
- destruction of dangerous records or specimens through a clean containment route

### Pre-fire evolved opening

At 1,000+ chaos, a new discovery can begin with impossible details:

- survey knocks answer the number of people underground
- a sealed sample reappears after being removed
- the first ledger balances without a recorded sale
- workers describe a shaft that is absent from the map
- an assay produces a value linked to a recent death or political loss

The player still receives the normal discovery and claim choices.

The true evolution can enter quickly through a paced investigation chain.

### Early signs

The first reports should remain uncertain.

Possible signs include:

- knocks that repeat questions or numbers
- voices naming hidden amounts
- tools returned in impossible places
- lamps extinguished in a pattern
- missing teams whose payroll continues to balance
- altered workers who stop speaking about anything except ownership
- ore appearing after violence
- support timbers marked with unfamiliar accounting symbols
- shafts that seem longer inside than outside
- sealed tunnels found open
- contracts signed in handwriting no one recognizes

The writing should describe what witnesses experience.

It should not tell the player that these incidents are a warning.

### Revealed effects

As the force becomes undeniable, incidents can cause:

- disappearances
- murders
- population loss
- removal or death of political figures when a valid target exists
- guard and army breakdown
- production accidents
- planning and organization penalties
- resistance-control failure
- political-power loss
- stability loss
- recruitable-population pressure
- local flight and evacuation
- altered contracts
- impossible shipment receipts

The mine remains extremely valuable.

Higher pressure can increase output and supernatural pressure together.

### Controller condition

The controller receives one staged supernatural modifier. Do not create a stack of small spirits.

Working lifecycle:

- Unexplained Mine Incidents
- The Opened Depths
- Infernal Accounts
- Bound Extraction
- Sealed and Remembered

The modifier changes through investigation, sealing, purges, assistance, and bargains.

### Response routes

#### Permanently Seal the Mine

The controller evacuates, closes, fills, collapses, or binds the entrances through a defined project.

This is the cleanest end to the supernatural threat and the permanent loss of the mine.

Success depends on evacuation, control of entrances, sealed-depth integrity, and resistance from affected groups.

#### Evacuate the District

The controller removes workers and nearby residents before final containment.

This lowers deaths and removes available labor and witnesses.

It creates refugees, economic loss, and a slower sealing project.

#### Military Purge

The controller clears workers, guards, officials, and sections believed to be affected.

This can restore access or intensify the force through violence.

Mass killing and forced entombment use the Deaths and Condemnation systems.

The route must never conceal its human cost.

#### Seek Religious Assistance

The controller invites valid religious authorities, clergy, or ritual specialists based on country context.

The action should use research direction and avoid invented claims about a real faith.

It can improve legitimacy, evacuation, witness cooperation, or sealing.

It does not guarantee supernatural success.

#### Seek Occult Assistance

The controller turns to scholars, secret societies, or event-created occult contacts.

This can reveal bargain terms or containment methods.

It raises political and supernatural risks.

It should remain fictional and context-specific.

#### Seek Scientific Assistance

The controller requests engineers, psychologists, physicists, or Event 016 expertise through a bounded adapter.

This can improve measurement, sealing, workforce screening, and evidence handling.

It does not grant Event 016 project history, Kruger ownership, or free advanced technology.

#### Continue Controlled Exploitation

The controller maintains upper workings and limits access to opened chambers.

This preserves part of the mine's value.

It requires low pressure, strong order, and repeated containment work.

Failure can reopen the full crisis.

#### Make an Agreement

The controller accepts explicit terms offered through the mine.

This is the route toward The Bottomless Account.

The agreement must name visible obligations, limits, and costs.

It must not hide an open-ended death-farming mechanic.

### Supernatural pressure

Supernatural pressure remains hidden as a raw variable.

The player sees stage, incidents, obligations, and current containment status.

Pressure rises through:

- extraction
- violence
- broken promises
- reopened sections
- concealed incidents
- use of affected material
- officials profiting from the bargain
- refusal to meet visible terms

Pressure falls through:

- sealing
- evacuation
- reduced extraction
- fulfilled bounded terms
- destruction or isolation of affected records and material
- successful assistance
- permanent closure

### Containment success

A contained mine can reach one of these states:

- permanently sealed
- upper workings operated under strict limits
- abandoned district under guard
- scientifically monitored closure
- religiously or ritually protected closure

The event should preserve uncertainty about what the method truly accomplished unless later incidents provide evidence.

### Failure

Failure can produce:

- mass disappearance
- catastrophic collapse
- removal of political or military figures
- persistent controller penalties
- a state that cannot be safely exploited
- a bargain imposed under worse terms
- permanent sealing after large deaths
- temporary loss of military control in the state
- a wider world-threat source only if the force clearly escapes the mine system

Ordinary supernatural pressure inside one mine should not set a global threat flag.

## Late supernatural outcome: The Bottomless Account

### Classification

The Bottomless Account is a late crisis outcome inside Evolution III.

It is not Evolution V.

It does not create a world-end scenario or a separate evolution stage.

### Premise

The mine begins paying according to a ledger that values political authority, public trust, human promises, material sacrifice, and bounded acts of violence.

The controller can receive extraordinary output by accepting terms that damage the state.

The entity does not accept arbitrary deaths as a generic currency.

Every valid payment belongs to a defined event choice with a cap, cooldown, and one-time or staged consequence.

### Entry conditions

The outcome requires:

- Demons Beneath the Mine active
- a deliberate agreement or repeated exploitation after clear contact
- very high supernatural pressure
- high Extraction Pressure
- controller dependence on the mine
- at least one accepted term

War, financial collapse, authoritarian rule, and loss of the mine can make the route more likely.

### Visible account structure

The category shows:

- current agreement stage
- next visible obligation
- current benefit
- consequence for breach
- broad closure route

It should not show a large supernatural currency shop.

The player manages one active obligation at a time.

### Bounded payment families

#### Material tribute

The controller gives equipment, fuel, trains, convoys, industrial capacity, or stored mine output.

This is the safest payment and produces the smallest reward.

#### Political authority

The controller gives political power, law flexibility, advisor access, or government legitimacy through a defined staged loss.

This can remove or lock a valid official only when the implementation has a safe target and clear visible consequence.

#### Public trust

The controller accepts stability loss, broken dividend promises, contract secrecy, or a legitimacy collapse.

This strengthens the Resource Curse and local unrest.

#### Territorial or industrial sacrifice

The controller closes a factory, abandons a project, destroys a transport asset, or gives up mine development through a bounded effect.

The mine returns a stronger temporary or long-term contribution.

#### Human sacrifice through a scripted crisis choice

This route exists only in severe high-chaos branches.

It must identify the people at risk, the actor responsible, the exact bounded population loss, the Deaths reason, and public evidence for Condemnation.

It cannot be repeated without limit.

It cannot be triggered by ordinary combat deaths, genocide systems, strategic bombing, or unrelated population loss.

### Player responses

#### Close the Account

The controller rejects further terms and begins permanent containment.

Existing benefits end.

Breach consequences can still occur unless the sealing route succeeds.

#### Bind the Terms

The controller uses legal, religious, occult, or scientific assistance to narrow the agreement.

This reduces reward and breach risk.

It can transform the mine into controlled upper-working exploitation.

#### Pay in Material

The controller chooses physical resources over political or human costs.

This is expensive and safer.

#### Spend Authority

The controller sacrifices political control or legitimacy to preserve output.

This can destabilize the government and strengthen The Gilded Sovereignty when both tracks are active.

#### Accept Predatory Prosperity

The controller continues receiving the strongest reward and accepts worsening national and local consequences.

The route can end in government capture, collapse, mass violence, or permanent loss of the mine.

### Breach consequences

A broken term can cause:

- sudden loss of mine contribution
- destruction of development
- disappearance of workers or officials
- state population loss
- infrastructure damage
- army organization or planning penalties
- forced closure
- altered controller aggregate
- a new harsher obligation

The result should follow the kind of promise that was broken.

### End states

- account closed and mine sealed
- terms bound and upper workings controlled
- material-payment arrangement
- government hollowed out by authority payments
- predatory prosperity followed by collapse
- mine lost to another controller with the account still active
- catastrophic destruction of the site

### Achievement hooks

The Bottomless Account supports Close the Account and The Last Shift tracking.

## Interaction among evolutions

The three evolutions can coexist on one mine when their conditions are met and each is enabled.

The Resource Curse changes institutions and incentives.

Gold Disease changes behavior among exposed people and groups.

Demons Beneath the Mine changes the physical and supernatural logic of extraction.

Their effects should connect without becoming one merged stage.

Examples:

- a corrupt concession hides early Gold Disease incidents
- Gold-Sick guards strengthen a private enclave
- a Gilded Sovereignty negotiates with the opened depths without central approval
- a public revenue settlement makes Gold Disease containment easier
- mass violence during Gold Disease raises supernatural pressure
- The Bottomless Account can consume political authority and worsen the Resource Curse
- permanent sealing ends ordinary output and most active pressure across all tracks

Each evolution keeps its own enable state, recorded flag, event-log identity, incident pool, and cleanup.

## Evolution log direction

Each registered evolution needs a distinct log identity.

### The Resource Curse

The log direction should represent the point when the wealth begins governing public institutions and economic choices.

It should not list modifiers or explain that corruption has reached a hidden threshold.

### Gold Disease

The log direction should represent the first confirmed pattern of obsessive seizure, hidden material, and armed refusal across several groups.

It should not call the condition a virus or use biological-war terminology.

### Demons Beneath the Mine

The log direction should represent the point when the impossible effects can no longer be treated as ordinary collapse, fraud, or panic.

It should not reveal every bargain route or late outcome.

The event-detail evolution catalog describes each premise and visible change without fake history dates or indexes.

The main Evolutions tab and selected History detail show the real date, actor, tier, and stage after recording.

## Evolution pacing factors

The implementation should centralize factors for:

- base MTTH
- pressure multiplier
- development multiplier
- order multiplier
- legitimacy multiplier
- closure multiplier

## Evolution recovery and persistence

An evolution can be contained, reformed, or closed.

Its historical record remains.

Containment can remove active penalties and decisions where the design says the pressure has ended.

Controller transfer preserves physical and local evolution state.

National corruption aftermath can remain with the old controller when it resulted from years of dependence.

A new controller should not inherit unrelated national patronage unless the local mine authority or contract carries it.

Permanent sealing should close mine-specific decision families, cancel missions, end positive contribution, and preserve a concise aftermath modifier.
