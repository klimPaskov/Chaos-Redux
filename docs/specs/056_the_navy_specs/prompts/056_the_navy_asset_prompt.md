## Report-event images

Route generated scene art to `chaosx_generated_event_art` with a context-complete prompt.

Create four full-canvas fictional documentary images:

1. Baseline manifestation, showing a crowded 1936 to 1945 harbor where a coherent mixed navy has appeared among dockworkers, sailors, cranes, warehouses, and mooring congestion.
2. Evolution I, showing an unusually large or sharply specialized fleet overwhelming a harbor or roadstead while ship roles remain readable.
3. Evolution II, showing crews inspecting unfamiliar but mechanically credible experimental ships among ordinary escorts.
4. Evolution III, showing a coherent impossible armada that combines carriers, advanced submarines, capital ships, unusual experimental hulls, and dense support groups.

Use the exact report-event consumer and reference family to confirm final dimensions. The current planning reference is 210 by 176, but consumer inspection is authoritative.

Visual rules:

- period-authentic black-and-white or restrained sepia documentary treatment
- full-canvas harbor or roadstead scenes
- clear ship silhouettes at final size
- physically credible water contact, wakes, masts, hulls, and moorings
- 1936 to 1945 crews, uniforms, cranes, warehouses, aircraft, and port infrastructure
- no readable generated text
- no modern container port, digital equipment, postwar missiles, modern uniforms, or cinematic science-fiction lighting
- no clean ceremonial fleet parade as the default composition
- no fused ships, repeated hull artifacts, impossible rigging, bad crops, bleeding, or overflow

Save source PNGs, processed previews, final DDS files, prompt records, contact sheets, and manifest evidence. Use event-scoped runtime folders under the relevant asset category. Keep the temporary `docs/assets/056_the_navy/` workspace only while work is active or blocked. Before full event completion, promote durable provenance and runtime crosswalk facts into permanent documentation, verify that no runtime path points into `docs/assets/`, and delete the complete temporary event workspace.

## Achievement icon triplets

Route icon production to `chaosx_icon_artist`.

Create one original achievement family for each ID:

- `the_navy_from_steppe_to_sea`
- `the_navy_three_gifts_one_admiralty`
- `the_navy_wrong_fleet_right_war`

Each family needs unlocked, grey, and not-eligible states using the repository's root-only achievement filename convention. Inspect the canonical achievement references, exact canvas, overlay workflow, alpha treatment, border, contrast, and final consumer before production.

Icon directions:

- From Steppe to Sea: open inland horizon or steppe transitioning into an anchor, naval bow, or ship wake. Avoid flags and literal maps.
- Three Gifts, One Admiralty: three distinct ship silhouettes organized under one admiralty star, pennant, or wreath.
- Wrong Fleet, Right War: an initially mismatched naval silhouette or tool redirected toward a victory wreath, target, or broken enemy line. Show adaptation without comic ridicule.

All icons must be designed as achievement art at final-size readability. Do not resize report images or reuse another icon family. Preserve genuine transparency only where the inspected achievement reference requires it. Use the correct not-eligible compositing workflow rather than improvising a new overlay.
