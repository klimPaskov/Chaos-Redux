# Tuning and Balance Targets

These are design targets, not fixed script constants. Final values should be centralized and adjusted through task-specific scenario tests.

## Outbreak timing

| Item | Target band | Scaling factors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incubating duration | 14 to 42 days | surveillance, Evolution I, weapon provenance, state capacity |
| Threatened exposure decay | 14 to 60 days | route closure, preparedness, neighboring source severity |
| Spread pulse | about 7 days | event performance and existing disease cadence |
| Mortality update | daily to several days | smooth population loss and performance |
| Containment confirmation | 30 to 90 sustained days | load, treatment, incoming exposure |
| Recovery period | 60 to 180 days | devastation, hospitals, cleanup, cure progress |
| Monitoring memory | 90 to 365 days | prevention law and countermeasure maturity |

## Mortality targets

| Response condition | 90-day cumulative | 180-day cumulative | 365-day cumulative |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| exceptional response | 0.5 to 2 percent | 2 to 6 percent | 5 to 15 percent |
| strong response | 1 to 4 percent | 5 to 12 percent | 10 to 25 percent |
| weak response | 3 to 8 percent | 12 to 30 percent | 25 to 50 percent |
| neglect or collapse | 5 to 15 percent | 20 to 45 percent | 40 to 70 percent |
| evolved or rat control | 8 to 20 percent | 30 to 60 percent | 60 to 85 percent or more |

### Mortality safeguards

- one population reduction effect per state update
- state cannot lose more people than currently live there
- ordinary nonterminal play preserves a small surviving population floor
- terminal world-end can override the floor
- death tracker and event total read the same loss amount
- treatment and cure reduce the curve rather than adding population back

## Spread outcomes

| Scenario | Expected behavior |
| --- | --- |
| prepared origin owner | origin often contained before three infected states |
| ordinary response | small regional outbreak over several months |
| weak response | several new states per month in middle phase |
| Evolution I | 20 to 40 percent faster effective crisis growth as a tuning starting point |
| Evolution II | low-frequency port jumps with high strategic consequence |
| rat occupation | newly occupied state becomes infected reliably |

## Rat Infestation targets

| Band | Expected behavior | Main response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| low | isolated evidence and weak local spread modifier | surveillance and ordinary sanitation |
| medium | warehouse, shelter, sewer, or port infestation | specific rat-clearing and food-store decisions |
| high | city-wide pressure, stronger mortality and transport spread | combined city clearing, shelter control, and transport purge |
| extreme | organized nests, rat basin eligibility, or rat occupation | military cordon, warren clearance, liberation cleanup |

## Countermeasure progress

| Country profile | Full progress target |
| --- | --- |
| well-funded, active cases, safe facility, cooperation | 180 to 360 days |
| strong but wartime | 270 to 540 days |
| weak or isolated | 360 to 720 days |
| no samples | negligible until access route succeeds |

## Emergency burden

| Burden level | Design effect |
| --- | --- |
| low | manageable ongoing cost |
| medium | clear consumer goods, supply, and output strain |
| high | stability, compliance, and production crisis |
| extreme | illegal movement, measure failure, protest, and forced triage |

Capacity scales with civilian factories, state count, stability, medical capacity, and war state.

## Weaponization project

| Path | Duration target | Accident risk | Countermeasure benefit | Condemnation exposure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Safety-First | 18 to 30 months | low | high | lower until discovered |
| Dual-Use | 18 to 30 months with high capacity | medium | medium to high | medium |
| Military Acceleration | 12 to 18 months | high to extreme | low | high |
| Defensive Conversion | ends offensive path at milestone | falling | high | reduced but not erased |

At least six phases and eighteen unique iteration roles should exist.

## Rat emergence

| Basin | Connected states | Death and load direction | Initial divisions |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| minimum | 3 or more | one Collapsed state and major local loss | 10 to 16 |
| severe | 4 to 7 | several Severe Crisis states | 17 to 28 |
| catastrophic | 8 to 15 | regional administrative collapse | 29 to 45 |
| world-collapse | 16 or more or equivalent map share | enormous death and high Chaos | 46 to 70 |

Final formula should use nonlinear population scaling so one huge state does not create hundreds of divisions.

## Brood pulse

| Stage | Cadence | Batch target | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| early base brood | about 30 days | 2 to 6 formations | enough to replace losses and grow slowly |
| established brood | 25 to 35 days | 4 to 10 | scales with states and nodes |
| dominant brood | 20 to 30 days | 6 to 14 | performance cap applies |
| Rat King | 20 to 30 days | 8 to 20 | doctrine changes composition |
| world-end readiness | route-dependent | large capped royal batch | should not create unlimited unit spam |

## Rat supply and counterplay

- strong organization and recovery in plague states
- major reduction outside a connected plague corridor
- armor and air should outperform ordinary infantry against concentrated swarms
- engineers and clearance decisions should matter
- rat armies remain dangerous in urban, forest, marsh, ruined, and tunnel contexts
- no single universal modifier should neutralize them

## Test scenarios

1. **Prepared minor origin**. Low industry but early surveillance. Check that one state can be contained without trivial cost.
2. **Unprepared populous major**. Large state population, weak prevention, no war. Check 180-day deaths and regional spread.
3. **Frontline outbreak**. Origin on active front with strategic redeployment. Check troop-route spread and cordon tradeoff.
4. **Island empire after Evolution II**. Check ports, convoy supply, island quarantine, and overseas jumps.
5. **Global cure coalition**. Several countries share research. Check pace, cleanup, and AI cooperation.
6. **Reckless weapon project**. Check iteration variety, accident rate, domestic return outbreak, condemnation, and deployment.
7. **Minimum rat basin**. Check that 10 to 16 divisions survive but remain containable.
8. **Catastrophic rat basin**. Check strong army, pulse growth, state infection, and human counterplay.
9. **Two adjacent broods**. Check dominance, standoff, annexation, and unit transfer without war.
10. **Player base Rat Nation**. Check no manual recruitment, readable pulse, route depth, and candidacy.
11. **Rat King transfer**. Check all states, units, nodes, values, flags, and focus systems transfer once.
12. **Rat King government routes**. Check three distinct play styles and no more than three spirits.
13. **Evolution V near success**. Check human last-response missions can interrupt the path.
14. **World-end terminal**. Check full map resolution, death accounting, super-event, and system freeze.
15. **Rat King defeat**. Check plague remains, cleanup works, restoration rules, and aftermath eligibility.
16. **Triggerable scenario Low**. Check three-continent distribution, two or more broods, one King, black mapmode, and viable containment.
17. **Triggerable scenario Medium and High**. Check independent broods remain visible, AI prioritization, disease-category clutter, and Rat Infestation decisions.
18. **Triggerable scenario Maximum**. Check every eligible continent, tag and division caps, Chaos floor 999, and no automatic world end.
19. **Scenario over active outbreak**. Check existing progress, actors, history, evolutions, and deaths are reused without duplication.
20. **Scenario repeat and reload**. Check a second launch performs reconciliation only, while save-reload preserves states, actors, decisions, pulses, and mapmode colours.
