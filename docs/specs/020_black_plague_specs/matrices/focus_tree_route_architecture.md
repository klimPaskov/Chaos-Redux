# Focus Tree Route Architecture

## Base Rat Nation tree

Target size: roughly 40 to 50 focus roles. Exact node count, names, coordinates, and connections belong to implementation.

| Lane | Opening role | Middle choices | Late payoff | Interactions | AI notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awakening and Survival | secure capital, first node, first pulse | expand now or consolidate | stable brood state | feeds every other lane | all broods prioritize first |
| Origin Adaptation | confirm Urban, Field, Dock, or War identity | archetype decisions and unit mix | strong terrain and route identity | modifies economy, military, targets | fixed by origin with situational branch weights |
| Hierarchy | address low Coherence | Dominant Beast, Distributed Instinct, Emergent Cunning | command model and candidacy ceiling | changes pulse, absorption, proto-sentience | route based on territory and strength |
| Mutation | unlock pulse template variety | Mass Swarm, Giant Mutation, Burrow Warfare | peak specialist template | limited crossover with military lane | choose from enemy and terrain |
| Territorial Plague Economy | first burrow nodes | Preserve the Herd or Consume the State | sustainable empire or explosive growth | changes Hunger, deaths, Evolution IV score | AI avoids consuming already empty territory |
| Military Method | protect first front | Flood, Strongpoint, Roads, Nest Defense | coherent campaign style | uses mutation and origin strengths | route based on human opposition |
| Rival Absorption | detect other broods | challenge, resist, integrate | absorbed brood history and dominance | feeds Coherence and candidacy | no suicidal challenge |
| Proto-Sentience | preserve patterns and knowledge | command signs, maps, communication | Rat King candidacy | requires Coherence, territory, absorbed rival | only secure broods pursue |

### Route locks

- one hierarchy route only
- one primary mutation route, limited secondary crossover late
- Preserve the Herd and Consume the State are mutually exclusive territory policies
- origin archetype is fixed at emergence
- Rat King candidacy requires proto-sentience capstone and world conditions

### Branch closure

Every lane ends in a decision unlock, unit system change, territory rule, candidacy condition, or campaign method. No lane should end with only a minor modifier.

## Rat King tree

Target size: roughly 70 to 100 focus roles.

| Lane | Opening | Main route or groups | Payoff | Failure state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coronation and Stabilization | integrate transferred package | secure capital, reconcile broods, first royal pulse | stable Rat King state and government choice | disputed crown if transfer stability fails |
| Government | choose one structure | Absolute Crown, Council of Burrows, Black Breath Hierophancy | route-specific administration and army | brittle crown, council deadlock, plague exhaustion |
| Administration and Supply | convert burrow network | rail ruins, ports, feeding provinces, regional rule | continent-scale logistics | severed network |
| Military Castes | choose pulse doctrine | Royal Swarm, Crown Brutes, Deep Roads, Tail Guard | advanced combined royal army | Hunger and pulse imbalance |
| Plague Mastery | adapt base disease dominion | occupation infection, cure adaptation, port spread | strong disease weapon and defense | human countermeasure advantage |
| Captured Knowledge | turn Sentience into action | maps, radios, archives, sabotage, scouts | strategic intelligence and world-end planning | sentience fracture |
| Human Population Policy | decide long-term treatment | Preserve and Rule, Empty the Cities, Selective Harvest | stable empire, rapid death, or flexible policy | resistance, depopulation, administrative complexity |
| Continental Campaign | choose target continent | interior, coasts, capitals, refuges | Crown the Continent | Dominion loss after failed objectives |
| World-End Path | Evolution V only | coordination break, harbor closure, capital objectives, royal integration | terminal ready flag | path interrupted by crown strike or lost threshold |

### Government branch interaction

| Route | Best lanes | Weakness | World-end style |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Absolute Crown | military castes, capital campaigns, Dominion | capital vulnerability and brittle cohesion | fastest concentrated conquest |
| Council of Burrows | administration, preservation, distributed supply | slower decisions and deadlock | most stable long global occupation |
| Black Breath Hierophancy | plague mastery, Empty the Cities, death thresholds | depopulation and Hunger | fastest infection and death route |

### National spirit cap proof

| Country | Simultaneous focus-created spirits | Lifecycle |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Base Rat Nation | 3 maximum | Uncounted Brood, Born of Pestilence, Fractured Instinct |
