## Mission design rules

- all map missions name real states, capitals, ports, corridors, or dynamic selected regions
- easy missions last at least about ninety days unless immediate danger justifies less
- medium missions last roughly one hundred twenty to one hundred eighty days
- continent and world-end missions can last six months or a year
- success and failure use distinct effects
- active mission caps keep categories readable
- AI sees all valid targets without human selector friction
- cleanup cancels missions when owner, target, selected disease, Rat Infestation, rat country, route, or world_end state becomes invalid
- the shared category hides Black Plague-specific entries when another disease is selected or no qualifying target remains
