# 020 Black Plague decision and mission matrix

This matrix records the decision and mission families defined in Part 8. It is a planning handoff, not final localisation. All names are working labels only.

| Family or mission | Owner | Board phase | Trigger direction | Cost and requirement direction | Success direction | Failure or risk direction | Cleanup | AI notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| National disease surveillance | Any human country | prepared | No local infection, or low nearby risk. | Medical capacity, industry, political effort, possibly support equipment. | Lowers first exposure severity and improves early detection. | Weak if started after infection grows. | Converts to monitoring or stronger actions when threat appears. | High for majors and countries near infection. |
| Medical stockpiles | Any human country | prepared | Prepared or threatened status. | Industry, support equipment, convoys for import, consumer burden. | Reduces death ticks and strengthens emergency treatment. | Stockpiles can be strained by large outbreaks. | Draws down or refreshes through later decisions. | High when industry exists, lower for poor minors unless aided. |
| Disease law preparation | Any human country | prepared | No local outbreak or early threat. | Stability, war support, political cost, compliance burden. | Reduces future severity and unlocks fast actions. | Overuse hurts economy and public trust. | Replaced by active emergency laws during infection. | Authoritarian states choose harsher versions more often. |
| Border health controls | Neighbor or exposed country | threatened | Adjacent infected state, rat border, refugee route, or troop route. | Trade, supply, relations, stability, border divisions. | Lowers cross-border spread. | Harms economy and relations, may fail if troop movement remains high. | Removed when neighboring threat ends. | High when border is active and population is large. |
| Port inspections | Port or coastal country | threatened or Evolution II | Port state threatened by overseas spread, infected port, or rat-held port. | Convoys, dockyard or naval burden, trade penalty, fuel, inspection staff. | Reduces sea and port jumps. | Too weak against rat-held port pressure or high traffic. | Ends when port threat disappears or becomes infected. | Island countries prioritize this. |
| Troop-route restrictions | Countries with troop movement risk | threatened | Active troop routes through infected or threatened states. | Command burden, supply, army XP, movement penalties. | Lowers military spread risk. | Hurts fronts and can reduce response speed. | Removed when route no longer threatens spread. | Avoid if in desperate front crisis unless disease risk is severe. |
| Refugee corridor management | Neighboring or infected owner | threatened and infected | Refugee pressure from infected or rat-front states. | Convoys, trains, stability, consumer goods, local capacity. | Saves population and lowers chaotic spread. | Poorly managed corridors can spread disease. | Converts to recovery support after local cure. | Democracies and aid coalitions prefer managed corridors. |
| Emergency quarantine | Infected state owner | infected | Owned or controlled infected state. | Stability, local economy, equipment, police or army presence. | Slows spread and lowers death pressure. | Harsh quarantine raises resistance and economic damage. | Ends when state cured, contained, lost, or rat-held. | Strong for severe states, weaker for small exposed states. |
| Local lockdown | Infected state owner | severe or collapse | Severe infected state or rapidly rising death pressure. | High stability, output, compliance, supply cost. | Strong spread reduction. | Large economic and political damage. | Converts to contained status or recovery path. | Authoritarian states use earlier, democracies use when severe. |
| Emergency hospital construction | Infected state owner | infected | Infected state with population and medical need. | Civilian factory burden, support equipment, manpower, trucks. | Reduces death ticks and improves treatment capacity. | Slow if infrastructure is poor or state is contested. | Remains as recovery support, then fades. | High for states with large population. |
| Field medical program | Infected state owner | infected and contained | Infected or contained state. | Support equipment, manpower, trucks, medical stockpile. | Reduces deaths and adds cure progress. | Can be overwhelmed by collapse status or rat occupation. | Ends after cure or state loss. | Strong AI preference when cure path is available. |
| Local cleanup crews | Infected or recovered state owner | contained and recovering | State is contained, recovering, or cured with residue. | Equipment, manpower, local stability, time. | Removes modifiers and relapse pressure. | Early cleanup can fail if disease load is still high. | Clears state target flags when complete. | AI waits until containment strength is sufficient. |
| Hold the quarantine line | Infected state owner | containment mission | Severe infected state has quarantine active. | Supplied divisions, equipment, command effort, mission time. | Keeps containment active and lowers spread. | Failure raises spread and local panic. | Mission cancels if state cured, lost, or rat-held. | AI uses when border states or capitals are at risk. |
| Guard the border ring | Threatened neighbor | containment mission | Bordering infected or rat-held state. | Divisions in named border states, support equipment, supply. | Lowers cross-border spread and rat breach risk. | Failure opens threatened state status and panic. | Ends when threat no longer borders. | High for majors and faction leaders near rats. |
| Keep the port clean | Port owner | containment mission | Port risk after Evolution II or infected nearby port. | Naval presence, convoys, fuel, port control, inspection burden. | Lowers port jump chance. | Failure can seed exposed or infected state. | Ends if port infected, safe, or lost. | Island and trade-heavy countries prioritize. |
| Retake and clean the warren | Human country at war with rats | rat war mission | Retaken rat-held state has warren-remnant status. | State control, divisions, equipment, engineers, time. | Removes warren pressure and lowers relapse risk. | Failure can restore threatened status or spawn remnants. | Clears remnant flags after success. | AI uses after military front stabilizes. |
| National cure program | Infected or sample-capable country | cure | Infection exists domestically or samples are available. | Industry, research capacity, medical stockpile, stability burden. | Adds cure progress over time. | Slow under war, low industry, or low medical capacity. | Progress persists if disease later returns. | Majors and infected countries prioritize. |
| Share samples and research | Cooperative countries | cure | Diplomatic access, allied or global response, sample access. | Relations, convoys, research capacity, safety risk. | Raises shared progress and helps weak countries. | Accident or theft risk if safety is poor. | Ends when cooperation invalid or cure complete. | Democracies and coalitions use often. |
| Emergency treatment deployment | Infected state owner | cure and infected | Cure progress or medical capacity exists. | Medical stockpile, support equipment, manpower, trucks. | Reduces death ticks in selected state. | Can be wasted if state falls to rats or quarantine fails. | Ends at cure, loss, or rat occupation. | AI targets high-population severe states. |
| Study Black Death samples | Bio-capable or infected country | weapon-capable | Samples from infected or rat-held state available. | Research capacity, containment safety, condemnation risk, accident risk. | Unlocks countermeasure knowledge or project entry. | Lab accident or domestic outbreak risk in gameplay terms only. | Ends when project moves forward or sample access ends. | Bio-capable radicals take more risk. |
| Weaponized payload project | Bio-capable country | weapon-capable | Black Death exists and special project prerequisites met. | Long special project, safety, resources, condemnation, accident risk. | Unlocks gameplay deployment through existing biowarfare delivery systems. | Blowback, lab leak, stockpile accident, retaliation, runaway spread. | Project flags and safety state clear or persist by project outcome. | Rare for cautious AI, higher for extremist or desperate bio powers. |
| Army cordon against warrens | Countries bordering rats | rat-threatened | Border with rat-held plague state or high warren pressure. | Divisions, support equipment, infantry equipment, supply, command burden. | Lowers rat breach and disease spread from rat border. | Weak cordon can fail and raise panic. | Ends when no rat border or state lost. | High for countries on rat front. |
| Anti-rat field operations | Human country fighting rats | rat war | Active war with rats or retaken warren state. | Equipment, army XP, manpower, supply, local control. | Damages rat pressure and supports cleanup. | Losses or disease exposure if poorly supplied. | Ends when target cleared or lost. | AI uses after retaking or before major push. |
| Fortify the rat border | Neighboring country | rat-threatened | Rat border exists and threat persists. | Civilian factories, forts, rail, supply, equipment. | Improves defensive line and reduces border panic. | Too slow if rats attack immediately. | Continues as normal defense or recovery after rats defeated. | High for defensive minors and coalition lines. |
| Evacuate threatened population | Threatened or infected owner | threatened, infected, rat-threatened | High population state threatened by disease or rats. | Trains, trucks, convoys, stability, industry disruption. | Saves population and lowers death potential. | Refugee spread risk and economic damage. | Converts to return or resettlement after cleanup. | Democracies prefer earlier, harsh regimes delay. |
| Burn out warren remnants | Retaken rat-state owner | recovering and warren-remnant | Retaken state has warren-remnant status. | Equipment, manpower, engineers, stability, infrastructure damage. | Removes hidden nest pressure. | Harsh cleanup damages state and public trust. | Removes remnant decisions after success. | AI uses when relapse risk is high. |
| Emergency medical coordination | Countries in global response | severe outbreak | Several countries infected or high shared threat. | Aid, convoys, relations, medical stockpile, research capacity. | Spreads cure and treatment support. | Can be blocked by wars or hostility. | Ends when global threat declines. | Democracies and faction leaders use most. |
| Rat containment coalition | Countries facing rat or King threat | rat threat or King threat | Rat nations control enough states or King exists. | Diplomacy, military access, equipment aid, faction coordination. | Improves joint containment and military response. | Rivalries can reduce effectiveness. | Ends after rat defeat and cleanup threshold. | World-threat logic should increase cooperation. |
| Disease board state selector | Human player and AI equivalent | all relevant phases | Many state targets exist. | No direct cost, but selected actions have costs. | Reduces UI clutter and focuses decisions on one state or zone. | Stale target risk if cleanup is weak. | Clears invalid selected state target. | AI should not rely on human selector. |
| Mapmode refresh | Shared system | all phases | Any disease, containment, cure, weaponized, rat, or cleanup status changes. | No decision cost. | Keeps visual state accurate. | Stale visuals cause player confusion. | Refresh on every state transition. | AI not applicable, but state logic must be reliable. |
