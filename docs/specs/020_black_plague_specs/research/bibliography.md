## Research cautions

- Real historical mortality estimates and transmission debates remain complex and regionally variable.
- The event uses research to create plausible mechanics, not to assert one complete historical explanation.
- The fictional strain, mutant rats, sentient governments, and world-end path are alternate-history invention.
- Final quote and audio implementation still requires source verification and project documentation.
