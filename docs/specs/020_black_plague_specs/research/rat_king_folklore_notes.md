# Rat King Folklore Notes

## Research boundary

A real-world rat king is a rare cluster of rats whose tails become entangled. The term also carries folklore and omen associations. The Event 20 Rat King is a fictional sentient sovereign inspired by the image and name. The spec does not claim that historical rat kings were intelligent monarchs or plague governments.

## Observed phenomenon

Modern reporting has documented rare rat king specimens and living entanglements. A 2021 South Estonia case involved tails bound with material that included soil, manure, straw, and feathers. Most reported specimens involved black rats, and many historical finds came from parts of Europe.

Design translation:

- the visual motif of entwined tails can appear in flags, coronation art, and the Rat King seal
- the Rat King can emerge from several broods becoming physically and politically bound
- the event text should leave early organization uncertain, then reveal actual sentience through behavior
- ordinary base broods should not begin with a fully articulate monarch

Source:

- National Geographic, Rat kings might actually be real, https://www.nationalgeographic.com/animals/article/rat-king-real-science

## Folklore role

Rat kings have often been treated as ominous or uncanny objects. The title naturally supports plague, crowding, filth, hidden nests, and the fear of many bodies acting as one.

Design translation:

- Evolution IV should feel like a political and supernatural consolidation with government, identity, and army growth
- the sovereign's symbols can use tails, knots, circles, and a crown formed from many broods
- the Council route can reinterpret the entanglement as distributed government
- the Absolute Crown route can treat it as proof of one command
- the Hierophancy route can make the knot a sacred plague sign

## Visual cautions

- avoid comedy crown imagery
- avoid pet-rat cuteness
- avoid gore as the only source of menace
- preserve a readable sentient face for the leader portrait
- use collective portraits for base Rat Nations
- use one-person portrait and actual-like nonhuman name pool for the Rat King
- do not reuse one flag with palette changes for every brood

## Text cautions

- do not claim normal plague historically produced rat governments
- do not present folklore as verified science
- do not make the first rat report explain the entire evolution
- use organized movement, captured structures, command patterns, and coordinated attacks to reveal sentience gradually
