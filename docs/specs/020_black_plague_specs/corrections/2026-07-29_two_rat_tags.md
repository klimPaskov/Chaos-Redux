# Event 20 two-tag rat-country correction

This correction supersedes every earlier requirement for a multi-tag Rat Nation pool.

Event 20 reserves exactly two rat country tags:

- `RTA` is the reusable base Rat Nation.
- `RTX` is the separate sentient Rat King.

Evolution III may establish only `RTA`.
Its four origin archetypes remain runtime identities selected from the founding basin.
Additional warrens and broods are represented through rat-controlled states, infestation, brood mass, army pulses, and focus or decision state inside `RTA`.
They are not separate countries.

Evolution IV may establish `RTX` in a separate Royal Basin.
The triggerable scenario creates or preserves one `RTA` and one `RTX`, keeps their coexistence grace period, and remains idempotent.
Scenario intensity continues to scale plague-state coverage, royal territory, opening forces, and Chaos, but it does not scale the number of Rat Nation tags.

All references to rival Rat Nation tags, finite country pools, inter-tag brood annexation, or several independently tagged broods are superseded.
Brood consolidation is internal to `RTA`.
Rat King absorption targets the `RTA` carrier after the grace period.
