# Quarantine and army cordon payment budget

Acceptance basis: the user's approved CBRN overhaul calls for one readable upfront cost row, exact-cost eligibility, and a shorter response loop. The repository decision skill limits each action to four spent or committed resource types. The parent accepts this bounded mapping for Event 020 quarantine and army cordon actions. Demolition and warren clearance still need separate payment review.

Establish Quarantine spends the selected state's fuel quote and the existing 10 Command Power. It commits two civilian factories and two Response Capacity slots during its 14-day setup. The existing personnel, support equipment, and truck quotes remain on-hand requirements. After setup, factories are released while active quarantine continues to use two capacity slots and monitoring uses one. The prior 2% Stability and 1% War Support debits and gates are retired. The state phase, access, cancellation, cooldown, monitoring, and disease outcomes remain unchanged.

Raise an Army Cordon spends the selected state's infantry-equipment quote and the existing 30 Command Power. It commits two civilian factories and three Response Capacity slots for the 21-day setup. Personnel, support equipment, trucks, and fuel remain on-hand requirements, alongside the existing qualifying-division requirement. Factories are released after setup, while the deployed cordon continues to use three capacity slots. The prior 2% Stability and 2% War Support debits and gates are retired. Force, phase, cancellation, and disease-result rules remain unchanged.

Each start effect in `common/scripted_effects/020_black_plague_response_action_effects.txt` resets every payment input, sets only its two direct debits, and calls `black_plague_pay_response_cost` once. The corresponding triggers in `common/scripted_triggers/020_black_plague_response_triggers.txt` retain the same paid-stock and Command Power gates and check each action's exact factory and capacity requirements inclusively. `common/decisions/020_black_plague_response_decisions.txt` uses the same predicate in availability and the custom cost trigger, hides duplicate raw affordability lines, and shows a concise three-icon row. Five quarantine and four cordon scripted-localisation getters bind Command Power, factories, capacity, duration, and monitoring capacity to the source constants. The full hover explains on-hand requirements, continuing capacity, and that canceled work does not refund the initial payments.

At one below either direct payment, factory count, or free capacity amount, the corresponding source gate fails. Exact equality and one above pass. This is source-level boundary evidence, not engine-executed payment proof. Quarantine's ongoing monitoring capacity and the cordon's ongoing burden are commitments of the same Response Capacity resource type already counted at selection. Both actions therefore fit the four-type cost limit.
