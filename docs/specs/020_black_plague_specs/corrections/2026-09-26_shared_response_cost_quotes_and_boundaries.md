**Disposition: implemented in source; native consumer validation unresolved.** The user's accepted 2026-09-20 CBRN overhaul requires supported localisation bindings, displayed payments that match deductions, and eligibility at the exact displayed amount. This correction applies those requirements to Event 020's shared response actions. It does not approve a new payment layer or change their response outcomes.

The Event 020 English response file previously contained 395 unsupported `[?constant:...]` expressions across its shared and dedicated action text. Supported scripted-localisation getters now read the corresponding shared script constants and expose them through temporary variables. The selected-state material getters follow the source population bands. The actual Crown Strike Response Capacity quote uses `black_plague_response_burden.crown_strike`, the same constant as the action effect. Cost hovers explain spending and commitment lifetime without repeating the visible amounts. Stronger Prevention Law's Stability loss appears in its completion tooltip, when the effect actually fires, rather than among upfront payments.

Royal Node's tooltip states its two-point Response Capacity commitment, which remains tied to the shared medium-burden constant even though the long terminal payment row itself is not visually accepted. Armored Clearance identifies its separate unspent infantry-stock requirement, and Liberation and Quarantine names its containment-force requirement.

Ordinary selected-state actions pay their scaled support equipment, motorized equipment, and fuel once when ordered. Their population-band gate is inclusive at 45 support, 23 trucks, and 450 fuel for the minimum band; 90/45/900 for the large band; and 120/60/1200 for the metropolitan band. The minimum calculation's 22.5 trucks are rounded upward to 23 whole units before debit. Separate 60/30/600 and 120/60/1200 stock floors were removed from these ordinary actions because they were neither paid nor presented as independently authorized reserve requirements. The fixed national knowledge, Royal Node, Crown Strike, and Royal Burrow payment gates remain, with inclusive equality. Individual trains, Command Power, route, phase, and state requirements remain active and are checked separately.

For a minimum-band action that carried the old standard floor, the effective pre-change material gate required at least 61 support equipment, 31 trucks, and 601 fuel despite a displayed 45/23/450 payment. The accepted source gate is now 45/23/450. Large and metropolitan bands lose the strict one-unit excess at their respective exact displayed amounts. This is a real accessibility change; it does not increase any action's paid material amount or AI willingness score.

Those pairs require joint tuning because the native fields reject the shared constant token. Shared custom rows remain dense and the four-type payment/commitment budget, especially for train, Command Power, and terminal operations, needs a separate cost redesign. The existing model-aware motorized helper now removes model 0 before model 1 for shared and dedicated response payments; the dedicated payment history records its actual completed debit.
