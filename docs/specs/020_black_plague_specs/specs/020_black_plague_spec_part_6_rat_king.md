# Event 20 Black Plague Specification, Part 6

## Rat King country package, sentient government, and deep focus tree

All names and labels in this file are working labels, not final localisation.

## Evolution IV purpose

Evolution IV creates a separate Rat King country. It is not a cosmetic name applied to the strongest base Rat Nation. The new country unifies the active broods, inherits their units and territory, replaces the shared base tree with a much deeper tree, receives a sentient leader and government, and becomes the main late-game actor for Event 20.

The transformation should feel like the moment the plague changes from a collection of nonhuman infestations into an organized world power.

## Evolution IV eligibility

The natural Evolution IV path can begin rolling when all of the following are true. The triggerable scenario in Part 9 uses a separate immediate entry that bypasses these natural requirements only during scenario bootstrap.

- Chaos is at least 800.
- Evolution III is recorded.
- At least two Rat Nations exist or one Rat Nation has already absorbed another.
- A valid candidate has high Coherence or proto-sentience progress.
- Rat-controlled territory and cumulative event deaths pass meaningful thresholds.
- The candidate is not in immediate military collapse.

The roll accelerates with:

- more absorbed broods
- a larger dominance gap
- more controlled plague states
- higher proto-sentience
- control of major cities, capitals, ports, research centers, and communication hubs
- a high ratio of rat strength to nearby human strength

It slows when:

- broods remain evenly matched
- human counteroffensives cut rat territory apart
- the leading candidate has low Coherence
- global rat territory is shrinking

## Candidate selection

The strongest candidate is chosen through a Rat King claim score.

### Claim score factors

- controlled state share among all rat countries
- current division strength and unit quality
- number of absorbed broods
- Brood Mass
- Coherence
- proto-sentience progress
- control of important human capitals or research centers
- cumulative human deaths in controlled states
- survival time
- completed candidacy focus group

A player-controlled Rat Nation receives a clear warning and a limited opportunity to challenge a stronger AI candidate if it is close enough. The challenge should require territory, brood commitment, and a timed objective rather than a cheap political power button.

## Coronation transfer

When Evolution IV resolves naturally, a new Rat King tag receives the world rat package. The triggerable scenario uses a scenario coronation variant because several Rat Nations and the Rat King must exist at the same time from launch.

### Required transfer

- all states owned or controlled by active Rat Nations
- all rat units and valid commanders
- all established burrow nodes
- accumulated event-attributed rat deaths and conquest values
- relevant focus and decision unlocks
- weaponized disease knowledge captured by rat countries
- port and overseas route permissions
- absorbed brood history
- global Rat King claim and world-threat context

### Required cleanup

- all base Rat Nation countries are annexed or deactivated safely
- base rat decisions and focus trees close
- obsolete dominance timers stop
- stale selected targets and event targets clear
- units are not duplicated or lost
- no state loses its plague status
- human liberation and disease cleanup status remains accurate

The new country must be independently playable and should not depend on a base Rat Nation tag continuing to exist invisibly.

### Triggerable scenario coronation variant

The scenario selects a separate Royal Basin and creates the Rat King there. It transfers only the Royal Basin package and any strength budget assigned to that basin. Other scenario-created Rat Nations remain independent for a short royal-consolidation grace period. The King receives its full sentient government, focus tree, mechanics, pulse, leader, and Evolution IV actor state immediately. After the grace period, ordinary dominance, allegiance, absorption, and conquest logic can unify the remaining broods.

This variant records Evolution IV once and can use the same coronation super-event. It does not weaken the natural full-transfer path.

## Rat King public identity

The public map name should be a direct country name rather than an office name. The exact name can change by government route, but it should remain short enough for the map.

### Leader

The Rat King is a sentient individual and should have:

- a generated one-person leader portrait
- a small pool of actual-like nonhuman personal names and epithets
- route-aware leader traits
- no generic office-only leader name
- no human gender pool mismatch

The generated portrait's apparent presentation determines the matching name pool and metadata. If the design uses a clearly male-presenting or female-presenting sovereign, implementation must keep name and metadata aligned. An androgynous or deliberately nonhuman portrait can use a dedicated nonhuman name pool and neutral metadata when supported.

### Portrait direction

- upper-torso HOI4 leader framing
- visibly sentient rat monarch
- period materials and captured human regalia
- no cartoon crown
- no readable text
- subdued painterly treatment
- strong eyes and recognizable silhouette
- a static fallback and an animated high-chaos version

The animated version can use subtle breathing, whisker movement, eye light, cloak movement, and background fog. Every frame must be real source art, not a transformed still.

## Rat King mechanic values

The Rat King replaces the base Brood Mass, Hunger, and Coherence presentation with four connected values.

### Dominion

Dominion measures territorial and political control over the brood world.

**Increases from**:

- controlled states
- absorbed broods
- captured capitals
- completed continental campaigns
- established royal burrow nodes

**Unlocks**:

- regional command decisions
- larger pulse caps
- government reforms
- Evolution V eligibility

**Falls from**:

- loss of capitals
- major encirclements
- rebellion or brood fragmentation
- sustained human liberation

### Sentience

Sentience measures organized thought, language, planning, and captured knowledge.

**Increases from**:

- preserved human records
- captured research centers
- emergent cunning legacy
- government focuses
- intelligence and administration decisions

**Unlocks**:

- advanced focus branches
- sentient unit command
- captured technology use
- strategic overseas campaigns
- world-end planning

**Tradeoff**:

High Sentience can reduce raw swarm growth unless the government solves the tension between instinct and organization.

### Brood Cohesion

Brood Cohesion replaces base Coherence and measures loyalty to the new crown or governing structure.

**Increases from**:

- successful unification
- route-consistent government decisions
- regular growth pulses
- victory over human coalitions
- balanced treatment of absorbed broods

**Falls from**:

- territorial loss
- Hunger crisis
- conflicting government choices
- overcentralization in the council route
- excessive decentralization in the crown route

Low Brood Cohesion can reduce pulse quality, create local brood revolts, or temporarily reactivate a base rat tag from the dormant pool.

### Hunger

Hunger remains as the material pressure that drives conquest.

The Rat King has more tools to manage Hunger through food regions, controlled human populations, stored supplies, and planned campaigns. It also faces larger consequences when a continental empire stops expanding.

## Rat King national spirit lifecycle

The Rat King uses no more than three focus-tree-created spirits at once.

### Working spirit 1: Crowned Brood

Represents unification, nonhuman manpower rules, and command over growth pulses.

- inherits no-manpower and no-equipment behavior
- improves pulse quality and transfer safety
- gains staged government upgrades
- can worsen into a disputed crown form when Brood Cohesion collapses

### Working spirit 2: Plague Dominion

Represents immunity, occupation infection, burrow supply, and advanced disease control.

- upgrades through plague mastery
- can preserve or consume populations based on route
- supports overseas spread and world-threat pressure

### Working spirit 3: Stolen Mind

Represents Sentience and captured knowledge.

- begins as a mixed spirit with administrative weakness
- upgrades through government and knowledge routes
- can specialize into royal command, distributed council intelligence, or hierophant doctrine
- becomes a final world-end identity after Evolution V

## Rat King army system

The Rat King still cannot train ordinary divisions manually. It receives a royal brood pulse with more strategic control.

### Royal pulse

The pulse uses:

- Dominion
- Brood Cohesion
- controlled plague states
- royal burrow nodes
- recent conquest
- government route
- military branch
- Hunger
- global performance cap

The player can choose a pulse doctrine before each cycle or at staged intervals.

### Pulse doctrines

#### Flood Doctrine

- more Swarm Columns
- faster front replacement
- high Hunger and supply pressure

#### Titan Doctrine

- fewer Royal Brutes
- strong breakthrough and city assault
- slower pulse

#### Tunnel Doctrine

- Burrower and infiltration units
- strong fort and river crossing
- weaker open-front staying power

#### Royal Guard Doctrine

- elite defensive and command formations
- protects capitals, focus objectives, and world-end preparations
- lower territorial coverage

The player chooses doctrine through a mechanic action with cooldown and opportunity cost. It is not a free switch every day.

## Advanced unit families

### Royal Swarm

Improved main line formation with better command and planning than base swarms.

### Crown Brutes

Large mutant shock units with high breakthrough and city assault.

### Deep Burrow Columns

Advanced tunnel units that threaten forts, supply hubs, and river lines.

### Tail Guard

Elite defensive units around the sovereign, capitals, and world-end project states.

### Plague Bearers

Special formations that increase infection pressure in occupied states. They do not deploy a separate real-world biological procedure.

### Sea Broods

Late coastal formations used through scripted port transfer and invasion support. They are not a conventional navy and should not create normal ship production requirements.

## Captured human knowledge

Sentience allows the Rat King to use selected captured systems.

### Valid uses

- maps and strategic planning
- rail and port administration
- captured radio and signals
- basic industrial salvage
- air-raid warning and concealment
- intelligence from captured archives
- use of existing infrastructure

### Restricted uses

- no broad conventional equipment economy
- no ordinary human manpower
- no instant access to every captured technology
- no free strategic bomber or navy production
- no copied full human focus tree

Captured knowledge should improve organization and campaign planning rather than erase the country's unique mechanics.

## Government route family

The Rat King chooses one of three government structures after the coronation opening.

### Absolute Crown route

**Fantasy**: one sovereign commands every brood through court, fear, and royal scent.

**Mechanical identity**:

- high Dominion
- strong Royal Guard and Crown Brutes
- fastest absorption and central command
- concentrated capital and leader vulnerability
- lower tolerance for local brood autonomy

**Key focus groups**:

- establish the throne nest
- appoint regional tail-lords
- create a royal guard
- centralize growth pulses
- punish divided broods
- proclaim one command language

**Tradeoff**:

Territorial loss near the capital causes severe Brood Cohesion damage. The route can become brittle when the crown is cut off.

**Late payoff**:

A highly centralized conquest machine with the strongest continental campaign timing.

### Council of Burrows route

**Fantasy**: sentient brood delegates coordinate through many nests.

**Mechanical identity**:

- high Brood Cohesion across wide territory
- strong local supply and recovery
- more stable growth under capital loss
- slower decisive campaigns
- risk of deadlock and weaker individual commanders

**Key focus groups**:

- recognize brood delegates
- establish regional burrow councils
- distribute captured knowledge
- vote on pulse doctrine
- create shared nest law
- balance Hunger across regions

**Tradeoff**:

High Sentience with low Dominion can create policy paralysis. Emergency powers can solve a crisis while damaging the council identity.

**Late payoff**:

The most resilient global administration and best long-term occupation system.

### Black Breath Hierophancy route

**Fantasy**: plague, death, and the Rat King become a sacred order.

**Mechanical identity**:

- highest disease load and mortality pressure
- strong Plague Bearers and world-threat acceleration
- faster Evolution V death thresholds
- lower preservation of controlled population
- greater Hunger risk after regions are emptied

**Key focus groups**:

- sanctify the first plague basin
- create a priestly brood caste
- interpret disease signs
- turn captured hospitals into plague shrines
- spread ritual fear through occupied cities
- bind the world-end route to plague mastery

**Tradeoff**:

The route burns through population and state value. It can become strategically weaker if it empties its own territory before securing new regions.

**Late payoff**:

The fastest death and infection engine, with the most dangerous world-end route.

## Rat King focus tree architecture

The Rat King tree should contain roughly seventy to one hundred focus roles. The implementation agent chooses the exact focus count and layout while preserving the route architecture.

The tree has nine major lanes.

1. coronation and transfer stabilization
2. government route
3. brood administration and supply
4. military castes
5. plague mastery
6. captured knowledge and intelligence
7. continental campaign
8. human population policy
9. Evolution V world-end path

## Coronation and stabilization lane

The opening lane repairs the instability caused by unification.

### Focus group roles

- secure the new capital
- integrate transferred units
- reconcile or suppress absorbed broods
- choose the first royal pulse doctrine
- establish Dominion and Brood Cohesion baselines
- reveal the Rat King to the world
- open the government route choice

### Rewards

- safe transfer completion
- temporary defense and supply
- mechanic values
- event and super-event trigger
- government branch access

This lane should not be bypassed by an Evolution IV pre-fire state because the Rat King cannot exist before rat emergence and unification.

## Brood administration and supply lane

### Preserve the burrow network

- converts base burrow nodes into royal nodes
- improves local supply and pulse contribution
- supports Council route strongly

### Command the rail ruins

- uses captured rail infrastructure for movement without conventional train production
- improves strategic concentration
- vulnerable to human sabotage and air attack

### Rule the ports

- strengthens overseas transfer and Dock Brood integration
- unlocks Sea Broods and port campaigns

### Maintain the feeding provinces

- supports controlled population preservation
- lowers Hunger growth
- creates resistance and revolt pressure among human survivors

### Consume the dead lands

- converts depopulated states into short-term Brood Mass
- damages future supply and state value
- supports Hierophancy

## Military caste lane

The military lane branches into three compatible but resource-limited caste families.

### Endless Swarm family

- Royal Swarm upgrades
- wider fronts
- faster pulse
- greater losses and Hunger

### Crowned Giants family

- Crown Brutes
- fort and capital assault
- slower growth
- concentrated battle power

### Deep Road family

- Deep Burrow Columns
- supply hub attack
- encirclement and infiltration
- stronger performance in difficult terrain

A late convergence can create one combined royal army package only after Dominion and Sentience reach high levels. It should remain expensive and late.

## Plague mastery lane

This lane controls infection as a strategic ecology.

### Focus group roles

- increase infection establishment after occupation
- reduce rat penalties in low-load states
- create regional plague waves
- improve resistance to human cleanup
- adapt to completed human countermeasures
- increase overseas infection after port capture
- turn weaponized stockpiles captured from humans into abstract strategic resources

### Cure adaptation

Human completion of the Black Plague countermeasure should not trivialize the Rat King. The plague mastery lane can adapt gradually, restoring some severity. It must not erase the human achievement. Adaptation should take time, cost focus access, and remain weaker against fully treated states than against unprepared states.

## Captured knowledge and intelligence lane

### Focus group roles

- decode maps and radio traffic
- identify weak borders and hospital networks
- infiltrate supply and port systems
- use captured archives to select strategic targets
- steal or destroy countermeasure research
- organize sentient scouts
- preserve human technical labor under coercion

### Intelligence actions

- expose a weak quarantine corridor
- sabotage a countermeasure project
- intercept evacuation routes
- conceal a port jump
- identify a continental capital objective

Actions require Sentience and have cooldowns. They do not provide detailed real-world biological procedures.

## Human population policy lane

The Rat King chooses how to treat surviving humans. These routes are mutually exclusive because they determine long-term state value and death pace.

### Preserve and Rule route

- slower civilian death rate in controlled states
- stable food and labor contribution
- higher resistance, intelligence leak, and revolt risk
- stronger Council and Crown administration
- slower Evolution V death threshold

### Empty the Cities route

- rapid civilian deaths
- strong Brood Mass and Hunger relief
- collapsed state value
- faster Evolution V threshold
- stronger foreign world-threat cooperation
- long-term supply weakness in emptied territory

### Selective Harvest route

- targets strategic population centers while preserving selected regions
- requires high Sentience and administrative control
- gives flexible growth and campaign planning
- creates greater mechanic complexity and internal conflict

Final text should describe consequences through state and policy rather than celebrating extermination.

## Continental campaign lane

The Rat King needs a clear expansion route that changes the map.

### Campaign preparation

- choose a primary continent
- identify regional capitals, ports, rail hubs, and supply corridors
- establish invasion and plague fronts
- set a visible continental control objective

### Campaign families

#### Break the Interior

Focuses on rail hubs, major cities, and land corridors.

#### Silence the Coasts

Focuses on ports, islands, convoy routes, and overseas staging.

#### Overrun the Capitals

Creates timed objectives against surviving continental capitals.

#### Close the Refuge

Targets isolated clean regions that support human counteroffensives.

### Post-conquest handling

- establish royal burrow nodes
- choose population policy
- convert or destroy infrastructure
- assign regional brood administration
- manage Hunger and resistance

The branch should not be a list of free war goals. It should unlock campaign decisions and state objectives based on real borders, ports, and control.

## Diplomacy

The Rat King remains hostile to human countries and cannot join ordinary factions. Sentience does not turn the country into a normal diplomatic actor.

The government can still use limited strategic interactions.

- temporary feints or local pauses through scripted events when strategically useful
- demands for evacuation or surrender that primarily create panic and movement
- manipulation of rival human coalitions through intelligence
- no stable alliance, guarantee, subject, or trade relation with human countries

The Council route can communicate more clearly, but its strategic goal remains rat dominion.

## Internal crises

A deep country needs failure states.

### Disputed crown

Low Brood Cohesion after territorial loss can create a challenger brood. The player must restore control, accept council concessions, or fight a limited internal rat split.

### Hunger collapse

A depopulated empire can lose growth and organization. The player must open a new campaign, preserve population, or sacrifice Brood Mass.

### Sentience fracture

Rapid knowledge growth can separate sentient elites from instinctive swarms. Government route actions resolve or exploit the split.

### Severed burrow network

Human offensives that cut the empire into disconnected regions reduce supply, pulse contribution, and Dominion until corridors are restored.

### Countermeasure adaptation crisis

A major human cure breakthrough can lower disease pressure. The Rat King chooses research theft, plague adaptation, rapid conquest, or deeper reliance on brute force.

## AI route selection

### Absolute Crown AI

Favored when:

- one candidate dominated Evolution IV strongly
- high Dominion and strong capital defense
- human coalition is fragmented
- rapid conquest is possible

Avoided when:

- territory is dispersed across several continents
- capital is exposed
- Brood Cohesion is already low

### Council AI

Favored when:

- territory is wide and fragmented
- several absorbed broods had similar strength
- port and overseas regions matter
- long defensive war is expected

Avoided when:

- Evolution V threshold is close and rapid conquest is needed

### Hierophancy AI

Favored when:

- Chaos is very high
- event deaths are already enormous
- global cure progress is weak
- state population remains high enough to exploit
- the world-end route is plausible

Avoided when:

- controlled territory is already depopulated
- Hunger is extreme
- human countermeasures are strong and widespread

The AI matrix defines the detailed weights.

## Evolution IV super-event

The Rat King coronation deserves a super-event because it changes the campaign from scattered outbreaks into an organized nonhuman power.

### Trigger

- natural Evolution IV transfer or triggerable-scenario coronation completes
- Rat King leader and government exist
- at least one major human country remains
- super-event has not already fired

### Role

Global transformation and threat reveal.

### Text direction

- title direction about the recognition of a single rat sovereign, avoiding generic apocalypse language
- description direction centered on organized commands, banners or symbols, coordinated movement, and captured human structures
- button remark requires source research
- main quote requires source research or use of a verified public-domain candidate from the research note

### Image direction

A fictional period-documentary or painterly scene of a sentient rat sovereign and organized brood court in a ruined civic interior. No readable text and no comic styling.

### Audio direction

A unique one to two minute processional, chant, or dark court piece with verified source and license. It must not reuse the world-end track.

## Acceptance criteria for Evolution IV and Rat King

- Evolution IV uses dynamic pacing and a real candidate score
- a separate Rat King tag is created
- all rat states and units transfer without duplication or loss
- base Rat Nation systems clean up
- the leader is a sentient individual with matching portrait and name metadata
- the country uses Dominion, Sentience, Brood Cohesion, and Hunger visibly
- no more than three focus-tree spirits are active
- the country still uses no human manpower or ordinary equipment
- the royal pulse offers meaningful doctrines and remains timed
- government routes create different administration, military, and world-end play
- the focus tree has deep interacting branches and internal crises
- human population policy creates real short-term and long-term tradeoffs
- continental conquest uses objectives and post-conquest handling
- the Rat King remains hostile to humanity
- AI route selection reacts to world state
- coronation super-event has unique complete image, text research, and audio research requirements
