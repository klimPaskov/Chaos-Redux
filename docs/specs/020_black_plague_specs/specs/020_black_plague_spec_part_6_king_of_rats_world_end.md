# 020 Black Plague spec Part 6 - King of Rats and world-end route

## Evolution IV purpose

Working label, not final localisation: `King of Rats`.

Evolution IV creates the King of Rats. The strongest rat nation unites all rat nations into a new country with a stronger identity, different focus tree, deeper government logic, and a more dangerous military path.

The King of Rats should not be a renamed ordinary rat nation. It is a separate country package. It represents sentient, organized rat civilization emerging from plague states.

## King formation conditions

The King of Rats can appear when:

- at least two rat nations exist, or one rat nation has become dominant enough to claim all warrens
- the strongest rat nation controls a meaningful state base
- rat-held states have high cumulative disease load and death totals
- the Black Death has become uncontrolled in several connected states
- chaos is high enough for Evolution IV
- no human country has recently contained the rat outbreak
- the strongest rat nation has completed enough base rat focus progress, or has absorbed weaker nests

If several rat nations exist, the strongest one becomes the source. The King country should receive all rat-held states and units. If only one rat nation exists but it has grown into a major threat, it can transform into the King through a rare path.

## Unification behavior

When the King appears:

- all rat nations are annexed or transferred into the King country
- their units transfer to the King where possible
- all rat-held states remain plague states
- rat unit growth becomes more organized
- the King receives a new leader portrait and flag
- the King receives a deeper focus tree
- human countries receive a stronger world-threat response
- the disease board marks the King as the central rat actor
- the evolution log records the actor

## King focus-tree architecture

The King of Rats tree should be a full chaos-country tree. It can be fixed-purpose, but it still needs internal branches and real play.

Architecture map:

| Lane | Role | Main content direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coronation and sentience | Establish the King and organized government. | Leader reveal, court or warren council, rat law, state control. |
| Swarm command | Make rat armies more organized. | King guard, swarm doctrine, tunnel command, unit growth, special templates. |
| Warren economy | Replace normal industry with plague-state extraction. | Scavenging, underground nests, corpse fields, rail gnawing, captured factories. |
| Plague mastery | Use Black Death as ecology and weapon. | Faster plague conversion, lower rat penalties, human death pressure, cure resistance. |
| Human terror | Break human border states and morale. | Panic, resistance, refugee spread, anti-containment attacks. |
| Rat diplomacy | Internal nonhuman order and absorption. | Absorb remaining nests, manage warren factions, prevent splintering. |
| Continental conquest | Build toward continent control. | Regional war goals, invasion priorities, supply adaptation, port capture. |
| Sentient government split | Choose how the rat state thinks. | Royal command, brood council, or hunger mind route direction. |
| World-end path | Unlock after enough territory and deaths. | Final organized conquest, continent control condition, terminal scenario. |

## Sentient government routes

The King should have internal government routes that change play without turning into normal human politics.

Working route labels, not final localisation:

| Route | Meaning | Mechanical style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Royal command | One sovereign commands all warrens. | Strong leader bonuses, faster absorption, higher army coordination, worse instability if the King is threatened. |
| Brood council | Sentient warren factions govern through a council. | More stable growth, better defense, slower conquest, stronger recovery from losses. |
| Hunger mind | The state becomes a single consuming instinct with flashes of sentience. | Extreme unit growth and plague spread, weaker administration, higher world-end acceleration. |

These routes should affect leader traits, focus availability, unit ticks, plague behavior, and AI aggression.

## World-end path and Evolution V

Working label, not final localisation: `world-end path`.

Evolution V unlocks the world-end path in the King of Rats focus tree. The King must still earn the terminal scenario. It should not trigger immediately when the King appears.

Unlock conditions:

- King of Rats exists
- chaos value is high enough for Evolution V
- King controls enough states or has killed enough people through plague and rat occupation
- at least one continent is within strategic reach or already heavily infected
- humans have failed to contain the rat threat

World-end path requirements:

- complete the required King focus path
- control a full continent or the event-defined continent-control group
- occupy enough states globally or kill enough population through plague and rat-held states
- maintain rat state unity long enough for the final path to finish
- avoid being pushed back below required state control before the terminal focus completes

When those conditions are met, the world-end scenario triggers.

## World-end scenario

World-end scenario direction: the King of Rats has taken over the world.

This is a terminal branch. It should use the existing world-end rules: set the global world-end state, set the scenario-specific flag, show the matching super-event, and gate incompatible future systems.

The end-state should show that human political order has collapsed under rat sovereignty and Black Death ecology. The player should see that ordinary recovery is no longer the campaign direction.

## Super-event roles

This event should have at least two super-event packages.

| Role | Trigger | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| King reveal super-event | Evolution IV creates the King of Rats | Marks the transition from scattered nonhuman outbreaks to organized rat sovereignty. |
| World-end super-event | Evolution V terminal condition succeeds | Marks the campaign-ending rat world scenario. |

A rat nation first emergence may also deserve a news or report event. It can receive super-event treatment only if the implementation and next design pass decide the threshold is large enough.

Super-event text must remain research-gated. The planning package does not provide final titles, final button text, final quotes, final cultural remarks, or final audio. The super-event prompt asks the research agents to find and verify those pieces.

## Defeating the rats

The event should allow human countries to defeat rat nations before the world end. This content needs more detail in the next pass, but the core direction is clear.

Defeat flow:

- retake rat-held states
- run warren cleanup decisions
- keep army cordons until disease load falls
- prevent relapse and new rat warren pressure
- destroy or contain the King of Rats if it exists
- trigger a defeat aftermath if the King was a global threat long enough

A full aftermath package should be considered in the continuation pass. It should only exist if the rat threat became global or near-global.

## Achievement direction

Achievements should cover:

- containing the first outbreak without spread
- curing Black Death after high deaths without using weaponization
- weaponizing Black Death and surviving blowback
- defeating first rat nation before a second appears
- defeating the King after it controls a large region
- forming and winning as the King of Rats
- triggering the rat world-end scenario as the King
- saving a continent through prevention after Evolution II
- allowing no rat-held states after Evolution III becomes possible

The achievement prompt expands this into implementation-ready tracking directions.
