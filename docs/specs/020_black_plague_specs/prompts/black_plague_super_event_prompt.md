# Black Plague Super-Event Research and Production Prompt

Research and prepare the complete super-event packages for Chaos Redux Event 20. Read the accepted spec, `chaos-redux-super-events`, `chaos-redux-event-assets`, and the Event 20 research notes. Do not wire gameplay, localisation, GFX, or sound definitions unless the parent explicitly grants that scope.

## Super-event 1: Rat King coronation

### Role

Global transformation. Natural Evolution IV has created a separate sentient Rat King country and unified the Rat Nations, or the triggerable scenario has created the King in a Royal Basin while independent broods still exist.

### Trigger context

- natural Evolution IV transfer or triggerable-scenario Royal Basin creation is complete
- Rat King leader, country, capital, government, and territory are valid
- the event has not already shown this super-event

### Text research

- title direction: short recognition of one sovereign over the broods, avoiding generic apocalypse wording
- description direction: organized command, captured human spaces, symbols, coordinated movement, and public realization of sentience, with wording valid for both natural unification and scenario coexistence
- button remark: research a short grim cultural allusion about a crown, rats, a piper, or a city discovering who commands it
- quote: compare several verified candidates

A verified candidate is Robert Browning's public-domain line from *The Pied Piper of Hamelin*: “And out of the houses the rats came tumbling.” Use it only if it fits better than alternatives.

For every candidate record exact wording, author, work, date or period, source URL, attribution confidence, public-domain or copyright note, and fit.

### Image

Coordinate with generated event art for a 457 by 328 fictional super-event image. The visual brief is in the asset prompt.

### Audio

Find a unique licensed or public-domain musical track.

- target final duration: one to two minutes
- role: dark processional, court, chant, or transformation
- no drone, stinger, noise bed, test tone, oscillator, or placeholder
- do not reuse the world-end track

`De profundis.ogg` on Wikimedia Commons is a research lead. Verify title, recording source, license, attribution, composition status, duration, and edit terms before use. A different track is acceptable when it fits better and has clearer metadata.

## Super-event 2: Rat King world end

### Role

Terminal scenario. The Rat King has completed the world-end focus path, controls a continent under the spec rule, Chaos is greater than 1000, and the terminal takeover begins.

### Text research

- title direction: completed rat dominion, specific and final, no generic “the end begins” language
- description direction: emptied human cities, organized global burrow rule, surviving traces, and the sovereign's command
- button remark: short, cold, final, and source-verified when it uses a cultural reference
- quote: compare several public-domain candidates

A verified candidate is the public-domain King James Version excerpt from Revelation 6:8: “And I looked, and behold a pale horse: and his name that sat on him was Death.” Compare its fit with other verified plague, judgment, or collapse texts.

### Image

Coordinate with generated event art for a 457 by 328 fictional terminal image. The brief is in the asset prompt.

### Audio

Find a unique licensed or public-domain musical track with final judgment, ritual, or terminal weight.

`Dies.irae.ogg` by Membeth on Wikimedia Commons is a strong public-domain lead. The source recording is 7 minutes 14 seconds and requires a musically coherent trim to one to two minutes, careful fades, 44.1 kHz conversion, source preservation, and full derivative documentation.

Do not reuse coronation audio.

## Optional super-event 3: Rat King defeat aftermath

Prepare this package only if implementation retains the eligibility gate for a global, long, catastrophic Rat King war.

- role: defeat aftermath and reconstruction
- text direction: cost, memory, vigilance, and survival
- quote direction: public-domain literature, scripture, speech, or poetry about rebuilding and the price of survival
- image direction: ruined throne or royal nest with exhausted relief and reconstruction
- audio direction: unique lament or memorial piece, restrained rather than triumphant
