## Shared observation contract

The player remains at war with the relevant owners during the qualifying attempt, contributes positive native sweeping removal, and the region reaches zero currently relevant hostile mines. Count each region once.

Prove the difference between active player sweeping and passive decay, peace, owner disappearance, allied-only sweeping or administrative mine deletion. A task force on a sweeping mission without a successful attributable removal does not suffice. Owner fields can contain both old and event-added mines, so record that the event increased the field and that the field was later cleared. Do not pretend to identify individual Event 088 mines inside an aggregated native field.

Positive sweeping, region ownership history and material-increase observation are unverified capabilities in the planning package. Verify real interfaces. A read-only save inspector can prove a test snapshot without creating a callable gameplay trigger. Preserve that distinction and report missing native observations instead of inventing APIs or easier progress rules.

## `chaosx_088_open_water_offensive`

Visible very-high-difficulty route for eligible human-controlled countries. Require qualifying clearance in three distinct regions. At least one must be used by a later verified player-led native naval invasion against a qualifying hostile mine owner. The landing must establish control in that enemy's coastal state. The selected enemy then capitulates to the player's side before the relevant war relationship ends, with the landing state retained under the specified control rule.

Track actual landing route, attacker, target, control transition and qualifying defeat. A coastal state captured over land, a planned invasion arrow, peace-conference transfer, allied-only landing or an unrelated event annexation is not equivalent. Implement a supported allied-control-transfer exception only if it can be distinguished from enemy recapture. Otherwise keep that clause blocked rather than claiming broad support.

Use title direction connecting clearance to an audacious overseas operation. The description should make the combined requirements understandable. The icon depicts a landing craft through a mine gap toward a coast, visually distinct from the clearance achievement. Deliver its three 64 × 64 states.
