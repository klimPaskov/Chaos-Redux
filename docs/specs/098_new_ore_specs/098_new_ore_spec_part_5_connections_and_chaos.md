# New Ore, part 5: Connections and Chaos feedback

## Riches Found, Event 029

The interaction occurs only when an actual Riches Found mine and a registered New Ore deposit occupy the same state. That place can provide economic income, industrial opportunity, ore access, and valuable foreign agreements through their respective systems.

The two benefits retain separate entitlements. Direct control of the ore grants its armor advantage. Gold concessions, mine ownership shares, or an Event 029 economic agreement do not automatically grant ore access. An ore allocation does not transfer a share of the gold income. A player can negotiate both, with each agreement explaining its own obligations.

The combined state receives a stronger strategic valuation. AI that is choosing where to defend or attack considers both the economic operation and the armor source. Use an additional 1.25 strategic-priority factor when both benefits are materially active, applied once after their ordinary value contributions. It does not multiply armor or the mine's income.

When the original ore state contains a functioning Riches Found operation, that activity can accelerate Deep Vein's investigation within the timing envelope. It does not guarantee the evolution, reveal predetermined future deposits, or allow a player to buy a second ore state.

On capture, the state remains a dual prize. Each owning event recalculates its own current beneficiaries. Neither callback deletes the other marker, overwrites its source identity, applies its income twice, or resolves its contracts on the other's behalf.

Report direction should make the region's unusual concentration of value understandable. Its military and economic significance can attract better offers, stronger protective diplomacy, and more determined enemies. Existing Event 029 security or concession routes remain their own playable systems.

## Greenland Sale, Event 092

The connection applies when the actual territory being marketed through Greenland Sale includes a registered ore state. The seller's possession of an ore state elsewhere is insufficient. Event 098 does not create a new worldwide land auction or silently expand the territory Event 092 can sell.

The lot preview identifies each ore-bearing state, current controller, current direct armor tier, and whether purchasing legal ownership would immediately give control. If a hostile occupier retains control, the buyer is purchasing a claim to an extremely valuable place while the occupier retains the immediate armor benefit.

### Valuing an ore-bearing lot

Use the auction's own economic units. Let `B` be the normal value of the territory being sold without ore. Let `R` be the auction's positive reference value for a major conventional strategic territory, derived from its existing comparable-territory valuation.

The intended minimum ore-bearing lot valuation is:

| Current material | Minimum strategic lot value |
| --- | --- |
| Baseline | Greater of `5 × B` and `3 × R` |
| Deep Vein | Greater of `8 × B` and `5 × R` |
| Super-Alloy | Greater of `15 × B` and `10 × R` |

The reference-value floor matters for sparsely populated territory whose ordinary price is very small or zero. Multiplying an insignificant base price alone would fail to make the deposit extraordinarily valuable.

If a single legitimate lot contains several deposits, apply a further factor of `1 + 0.25 × min(k − 1, 3)` for its `k` ore states. This recognizes additional sources with diminishing value. The auction's normal affordability and bidding constraints remain active.

If Event 092 lacks a usable comparable-territory provider, establishing that provider is a connection dependency. Event 098 must not invent a new currency or treat a zero reference as a completed high-value auction implementation.

AI bidders value their own expected gain. A country buying its first secure direct source should bid more aggressively than a country with several secure deposits. A buyer needing reconquest discounts immediate military usability without removing the lot's long-term strategic significance. Rival armored powers may compete fiercely, but the result depends on actual bidders and resources.

Pending or completed foreign allocations are disclosed. If the actual supplying controller voluntarily sells control through Event 092, its affected current and future prepaid periods must first settle through part 3's real unused-payment buyback. The sale owner receives a verified settlement result and does not transfer control while the required voluntary settlement is unpaid. If the auction changes controller, old allocations then terminate through the normal control rule. If only ownership changes, the current controller's valid contracts remain and no buyback is required. The sale price does not grant immediate ore benefits before the actual territorial transfer.

## World Claims, Event 086

A World Claims claim gains strategic significance when it covers an ore state. The claim identifies a political interest in the territory, while actual control continues to decide the armor benefit.

The relevant claimant receives higher priority for the ore state in its normal territorial planning. A valid claim can reduce the attention cost or preparation time of an otherwise eligible Ore Wars demand. It cannot create military reach, bypass a truce, or grant mineral access while another country controls the state.

The claim can predate discovery or appear afterward. The interaction checks the exact overlap at both times. Claimants and controllers are dynamic campaign actors, including countries affected by release, succession, or civil war. Existing source-specific limitations in Event 086 must be reconciled by its owner before claiming broader support.

Several countries may claim the same ore state. Each evaluates it under ordinary diplomacy and the ore-interest rules. The event does not select an automatic rightful winner, create free cores, or turn every claim into an immediate war.

## Asteroid Incoming, Event 028

The accepted design choice is to keep the two materials as separate advantages. A country can control an Asteroid mineral source and a New Ore deposit and receive both. There is no third hybrid material to manufacture, trade, or manage.

Event 028 retains its mineral locations, controller rules, grade rules, and contribution. Event 098 evaluates its effective armor promise against the country's otherwise equivalent armor with Event 028 still present. The New Ore extra-deposit ceiling constrains Event 098 alone. It does not cap or erase Event 028's contribution.

For example, suppose an actual controlled test shows 100 armor without either material and 200 with the relevant Asteroid benefit alone. Baseline New Ore must take that Asteroid-equipped case to at least 300. Deep Vein requires at least 350. A single Super-Alloy deposit requires at least 600. These figures illustrate the required composition using an assumed measured Asteroid result, not a claim that every engine armor consumer uses the same multiplier phase.

If a country loses its last New Ore state while retaining the Asteroid source, only the New Ore entitlement is removed. If it loses the Asteroid source while retaining New Ore, New Ore continues against the remaining equivalent baseline. Two effects must not write over one common source slot or delete one another's ideas.

A foreign New Ore agreement grants only the foreign New Ore advantage. It does not export the supplier's Asteroid minerals or a combined super-material. Likewise, a country cannot use an Asteroid fragment as an additional New Ore deposit for diminishing returns or export capacity.

The state selectors allow a New Ore deposit to coexist with an Asteroid mineral site. Neither event treats the other marker as a reason to reroll ordinary valid territory. A physically invalid state remains invalid under the map's genuine rules.

The supplied project registry describes a newer current-controller Asteroid mineral API than the repository snapshot retrieved during this planning task. The implementation must resolve that difference before choosing an integration surface. It must preserve the intended mineral contract and cannot adopt a fixed six-slot limit from older code as an unreviewed design restriction.

## Sandstorm, Event 099

The connection becomes relevant when [Sandstorm's actual or committed approach footprint](../099_sandstorm_specs/099_sandstorm_part_02_fronts_and_forecasts.md) intersects a registered ore state or a real military supply route serving its defense.
A remote storm or a country-level dust label does not change a deposit's situation.
Event 099 owns the local [military and supply penalties](../099_sandstorm_specs/099_sandstorm_part_03_military_and_air.md), exposure, responses and physical damage.
Event 098 keeps the deposit, armor entitlement and agreement terms.

The material's armor advantage remains effective through the passage.
Reduced ordinary extraction, damaged machinery or slower transport does not switch off direct or purchased ore access.
Armored forces inside the footprint still suffer Event 099's coordination, movement and supply consequences, while otherwise comparable forces in a clear theater retain their ordinary weather conditions.

The controller can prepare its threatened forces or a genuinely useful corridor through [Event 099's existing responses](../099_sandstorm_specs/099_sandstorm_part_05_responses_and_missions.md).
A multi-deposit supplier can offer a safer physical source through Event 098's consensual reassignment, and a customer can seek its one existing backup agreement before a threatened source is captured.
Reassignment preserves remaining current and paid future periods under part 3.
A new backup uses its own ordinary quote and adaptation rule, with no added Sandstorm access fee.
A storm alone cannot start the source-defense mission, which still requires a real hostile threat.

AI weighs the forecast's certainty, actual route usefulness, source exposure and alternative access through its existing defense and agreement choices.
Only an actual terminating event, such as source capture, changes contractual entitlement under part 3.
Dust clearance supplies neither an ore reliability award nor a new Chaos receipt.

## Occupation Revolt, Event 095

A conquered deposit state is the most valuable occupied territory on the map, so holding it is a political problem and not only a military one.
The [Occupation Revolt owner](../095_occupation_revolt_specs/README.md) decides whether a revolt starts, what it demands and how it resolves; New Ore contributes only the fact that the state carries a deposit, which that owner may read as added unrest pressure.

While a revolt controls the deposit state, the occupier loses the direct access that state supplied, exactly as any other loss of control does, and its agreements sourced from that state end under the ordinary rules.
Regaining control restores direct access without a second discovery, a new Chaos receipt or a refunded agreement.
Suppressing a revolt in a deposit state uses the occupier's ordinary repression and condemnation consequences; the ore grants no exemption and creates no separate atrocity path.

This gives the ore a defensive cost that scales with how it was acquired: a controller that bought or inherited its deposit faces no revolt pressure from Event 098, while a conqueror must garrison the prize it took.
Basis: 10 Oct 2026 spec audit under the user's planning-package brief, recorded as accepted and queued for whichever owner implements first.

## Positive Economy cluster behavior

New Ore is a High member of Positive Economy because its discovery creates an exceptional material advantage and can make one state a source of economic and diplomatic opportunity. The later possibility of conflict does not change its assigned cluster identity.

Cluster selection produces one initial Event 098 discovery. Evolution discoveries remain part of that event and do not impersonate extra cluster firings. A related economic incident can combine with ore only through a real shared state or valid event connection.

Cluster presentation should communicate the sudden arrival of a highly valuable material and the opportunity it offers its controller. Mechanical numbers belong in the appropriate effect and access surfaces. Event Details and the corresponding catalog premise remain descriptive.

The ore is an abnormal material with consequences beyond an ordinary resource discovery. Chaos comes from its concrete appearance, wider distribution, stronger material, and distinct military escalation. Its beneficial effect on the controller does not make it exempt from the Chaos system.

At the maximum twelve deposits, these positive Event 098 milestones sum to at most 52 Chaos before the one possible 3-point settlement reduction. Some campaigns never satisfy several rows. This is an accounting bound from the declared design, not an instruction to force all milestones to occur.

Receipts are tied to geological identity, material capability, or the specific diplomatic confrontation. Controller cycling, repeated callback delivery, save reload, contract renewal, and an evolution log refresh cannot award them again. The material remains valuable if global Chaos falls after its discovery.

## Campaign variants created by these rules

| Working variant | Triggering situation | Choices and consequences | Closure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fortunate minor | First source appears in a small country | Protect it, select a patron, use barter to equip the army, or retain monopoly | The country retains, shares, or loses its strategic position through ordinary play |
| Occupied discovery | A state is occupied when selected | Occupier gets the immediate advantage, legal owner has a new liberation objective | Control settles through combat or peace |
| Rival supplier | A new deposit appears under an independent rival | Existing customers reconsider renewals, both suppliers compete for partners | Stable competing access or an attempt to concentrate sources |
| Double-value region | New Ore and Riches Found share a state | Joint defensive priority, separate income and access bargaining | Both state systems resolve their beneficiaries independently |
| Marketed prize | The actual Greenland Sale lot contains ore | High valuation and competing bids, with occupation risk disclosed | Final territorial control determines armor entitlement |
| Claimed prize | World Claims covers an ore state | Stronger political interest, more credible demand where otherwise valid | Claim remains separate from occupation and access |
| Two materials | One country controls Asteroid minerals and New Ore | An exceptionally powerful combination with two vulnerable source entitlements | Losing either source removes only its contribution |
| Monopoly restored | A country conquers all currently known deposits | Rivals seek weak access or prepare counters, future discoveries can reopen competition | Monopoly remains contingent on control and later discoveries |

These variants emerge from actual campaign state. They do not add a second random incident lottery or require named countries to receive the ore.
