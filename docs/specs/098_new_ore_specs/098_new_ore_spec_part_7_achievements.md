# New Ore, part 7: Achievement routes

## Achievements for territory, commitment, and mastery

The achievements reward difficult uses of the material, recovery from losing it, commercial judgment, and rare event connections. Event firing, a first capture, or a single accepted agreement is insufficient.

Every title below is a working design label. Final titles and descriptions are direction-only until implementation. All routes use the project's existing achievement eligibility rules and actual campaign history. Debug-created evidence, copied ideas without entitlement, repeated callbacks, and test-country activity cannot satisfy them.

A condition requiring continuous control or access uses real dates and interruptions. A final snapshot cannot prove a year of uninterrupted control. If a route becomes structurally impossible on a particular supported map, show the correct eligibility state rather than silently changing the achievement.

## A098-01: Original-source monopoly, working label

**Title direction:** A memorable expression of protecting an irreplaceable discovery without sharing its military advantage.

**Eligible actor:** The country that controlled the original state when the initial discovery was committed and that was player-eligible at that time.

**Unlock conditions:** Retain uninterrupted direct control of the original state for 730 days. During that period, win a completed war against a country that was a major power at the beginning of the war, with at least 120 days of actual war while the original state remained controlled. The country must have used a meaningful armored force during the war, defined as at least eight deployed armor-based divisions or four active armored capital ships at a verified wartime checkpoint. It must never have granted foreign access from any New Ore source during the qualifying 730 days.

**Disqualifiers:** Any loss of the original state, any activated outgoing allocation, subject-based substitution for direct control, or a nominal war without the required duration and military use. A new deposit elsewhere does not disqualify the country, but it cannot replace the original state.

**Visibility and difficulty:** Visible after becoming the original controller, very hard.

**Why it is interesting:** The player gives up equipment income and protective access bargains while preserving a vulnerable prize through a serious war.

**Tracking:** Original controller and state identity, first-discovery date, continuous control, outgoing-access history, war participant identity and major status at start, war duration, armored-force checkpoint, verified victory outcome.

**Icon direction:** A single mineral seam enclosed by a robust defensive ring with signs of sustained fighting. Completed, grey, and not-eligible variants use the common achievement pipeline.

## A098-02: Recovered source, working label

**Title direction:** Recovery of a lost strategic advantage through a difficult return.

**Eligible actor:** Any player-eligible country that directly controlled a deposit and then lost it to an actual wartime enemy.

**Unlock conditions:** The same enemy controls that source for at least 90 consecutive days. The former controller retakes it through the continuing conflict or a later genuine war and retains it for 365 consecutive days afterward. The enemy must remain independent of the player at recapture, and the returning country must field at least eight armor-based divisions or four active armored capital ships during the recovery period.

**Disqualifiers:** Voluntary friendly transfer, subject cycling, a copied source marker, recapture before the hostile occupation period, or losing the source again during the final hold. A failed recovery can establish a later new valid attempt through another genuine hostile episode, with its own dates.

**Visibility and difficulty:** Revealed after a qualifying loss, hard.

**Why it is interesting:** The player must recover territory while its opponent enjoys the very advantage that made the source important.

**Tracking:** Exact deposit, prior controller, hostile captor, continuous hostile occupation, actual reconquest event, final hold date, military-use checkpoint. A controller flip does not automatically imply combat.

**Icon direction:** A damaged protective plate repaired around its original mineral core, with a reclaimed position motif.

## A098-03: Sustained supplier, working label

**Title direction:** A difficult achievement about turning strategic material into durable influence.

**Eligible actor:** Any player-eligible direct controller.

**Unlock conditions:** Supply three distinct independent foreign countries continuously for 360 days while retaining at least one directly controlled deposit. At least two of the agreements must include real equipment compensation, at least one must be a valid security allocation, and at least one recipient must be outside the supplier's faction. Each recipient must actually depend on foreign ore access and possess at least five armor-based divisions or three active armored capital ships at both the start and end checkpoints.

**Disqualifiers:** A recipient becomes the supplier's subject, gains direct ore access during the period, loses all valid foreign access, stops satisfying its security qualification where applicable, or has its record replaced by another country. Renewal can maintain continuity if there is no access gap. Reassignment can maintain continuity only through the valid consensual source rule.

**Visibility and difficulty:** Visible after the first paid foreign agreement, very hard.

**Why it is interesting:** It requires balancing export capacity, actual material income, military protection, and independent customers over more than one ordinary contract term.

**Tracking:** Three exact recipient identities, continuously effective access, sources, paid receipts, family qualification, outside-faction proof, independence and relevant-force checkpoints.

**Icon direction:** Three distinct alloy shipments radiating from one guarded mineral source, with a strong central supply identity.

## A098-04: Purchased strategic prize, working label

**Title direction:** The extraordinary price of securing a deposit through a genuine territorial sale.

**Eligible actor:** Any player-eligible buyer in an actual Greenland Sale ore-bearing lot.

**Unlock conditions:** Win the auction after at least two other independent countries made valid competing bids. Pay the verified ore-adjusted price. Gain actual control of an ore state within the sold lot, having controlled no ore deposit immediately before the purchase. Retain that state for 180 continuous days and deploy at least eight armor-based divisions or four armored capital ships during that period.

**Disqualifiers:** A fabricated auction, sale of a different non-ore territory, unpaid or refunded purchase, merely acquiring ownership while another country retains control, a supplier-subject shortcut, or a loss during the hold period.

**Visibility and difficulty:** Hidden until an eligible ore-bearing lot is marketed, rare and very hard.

**Why it is interesting:** The route combines genuine competition, a large economic sacrifice, territorial control, and military exploitation.

**Tracking:** Event 092 auction identity, exact lot states, valid bidder identities, actual paid receipt, control result, prior deposit count, hold date, armored-force checkpoint. Event 092 owns the auction facts.

**Icon direction:** A mineral-bearing territory seal secured through a substantial exchange, with three competing bid markers expressed as symbols rather than text.

## A098-05: Two-material arsenal, working label

**Title direction:** A rare combination of terrestrial discovery and extraterrestrial material under deliberate military control.

**Eligible actor:** Any player-eligible country with access to the relevant active Event 028 mineral route.

**Unlock conditions:** Directly control the actual Asteroid main-crater mineral site and at least two New Ore deposits after Super-Alloy activation. Maintain all of those specific sources for 180 continuous days while possessing at least fifteen armor-based divisions or eight active armored capital ships. The combined actual armor entitlement must be valid throughout the qualifying period.

**Disqualifiers:** A fragment used in place of the required main-crater site, foreign access used instead of direct control, subject holdings, loss of any required source, or an unvalidated combined effect that only exists as tooltip text.

**Visibility and difficulty:** Hidden until both mineral systems exist, exceptional difficulty.

**Why it is interesting:** The player must acquire and defend several rare sources while managing an intentionally extreme military advantage.

**Tracking:** Event 028's actual main mineral identity and controller, two exact New Ore state identities, evolution and combined-entitlement validity, continuous hold, force checkpoints. The achievement does not invent another mineral system.

**Icon direction:** Two clearly distinct mineral origins reinforcing one formidable protective surface, with the Asteroid and New Ore motifs kept separate.

## A098-06: Broken monopoly, working label

**Title direction:** Breaking another country's concentrated control of the material through a difficult military challenge.

**Eligible actor:** A player-eligible country with neither direct nor foreign ore access at the start of a qualifying war.

**Unlock conditions:** At war start, one independent enemy controls every known deposit and at least three exist. Ore Wars must be active. Capture one of those deposits through that conflict and retain it for 365 days while the former monopolist remains independent, hostile or a former opponent, and still controls at least one other deposit. The player must use at least eight armor-based divisions or four armored capital ships after obtaining the source.

**Disqualifiers:** Friendly transfer, starting with a licence, receiving a deposit through a subject, eliminating the opponent before the contested-source period, or losing the captured source. A later naturally discovered source elsewhere does not erase the recorded monopoly at war start.

**Visibility and difficulty:** Hidden until a qualifying monopoly exists, exceptional difficulty.

**Why it is interesting:** The attacker begins without the ore advantage and must seize and keep a source against an established armored power.

**Tracking:** Snapshot of known deposit set and controller at war start, actual access state of attacker, war identity, capture, continuous hold, surviving opponent and another controlled source.

**Icon direction:** A broken enclosing chain around several ore fragments, with one fragment held securely outside it.

## A098-07: Distributed Super-Alloy control, working label

**Title direction:** Sustained command of widely separated mineral sources at the material's strongest stage.

**Eligible actor:** Any player-eligible country on a map supporting at least three distinct relevant continents.

**Unlock conditions:** After Super-Alloy activation, directly control three deposits on three different continents for 365 continuous days. Possess at least fifteen armor-based divisions or eight active armored capital ships at the beginning and end of that period. Maintain valid direct entitlement throughout.

**Disqualifiers:** Counting a subject, a licence, a claim, or three states on one continent. Losing any qualifying continent's last directly controlled source interrupts the period. Another source on the same continent can preserve that continent's contribution if direct control never lapses.

**Visibility and difficulty:** Revealed after a deposit appears on a third continent, very hard and geographically rare.

**Why it is interesting:** The challenge is defending dispersed sources while the benefit of the third source is smaller than the first two.

**Tracking:** Actual map continent identities, continuous direct sources for each qualifying continent, Super-Alloy state, military checkpoints, and country identity. Map geometry is never inferred from a painted achievement icon.

**Icon direction:** Three geographically separated mineral motifs connected by a protective alloy band, with no invented exact state borders.

## A098-08: Honored settlement, working label

**Title direction:** A strong government obtaining the material through a demanding bargain and honoring that settlement.

**Eligible actor:** A player-eligible country that issued a valid Ore Wars demand after a qualifying failed negotiation.

**Unlock conditions:** Accept the supplier's permitted counteroffer, fulfil and pay a valid security allocation, then keep effective foreign access and the agreed security relationship continuously for 360 days. The two countries remain independent and at peace with one another. The recipient controls no ore state during this period and possesses at least eight armor-based divisions or four armored capital ships at both endpoints.

**Disqualifiers:** An invalid or sham demand, unpaid terms, supplier becoming the recipient's subject, direct acquisition of a deposit, war between the parties, lapse of access or the agreed commitment, or replacing the supplier with another country mid-period.

**Visibility and difficulty:** Revealed after a valid ore demand, hard.

**Why it is interesting:** The player accepts weaker access and a meaningful relationship instead of immediately pursuing conquest, then maintains the bargain through changing conditions.

**Tracking:** Original negotiation and demand episode, exact counteroffer, final payment, native commitment, supplier and source, continuous foreign entitlement, peace and independence history, force checkpoints.

**Icon direction:** A firm military gauntlet and protective material linked by a durable unbroken clasp, without a generic handshake photograph.

## Shared force and history definitions

An armor-based division must contain at least six battalions from the applicable armor-oriented combat families and have at least 80% equipment strength at the checkpoint. A single armored support component added to a trivial template is insufficient. Supported custom armored formations need an equivalent substantial combat-family definition in the implementation inventory.

An active armored capital ship must be a valid deployed capital-ship consumer with positive actual armor and at least 80% strength. A queued hull or stock icon does not qualify. The alternative land and naval thresholds allow the same achievements to work with the relevant military role and supported DLC path.

The common achievement framework owns human eligibility and campaign integrity. Event 098 supplies exact state, actor, date, contract, and confrontation evidence. Where another event owns an auction or mineral site, use its verified evidence instead of reconstructing a convenient approximation.

Each achievement has one completed icon direction and the required grey and not-eligible variants. Its final tooltip must describe real conditions without leaking internal variable names or suggesting an easier shortcut.
