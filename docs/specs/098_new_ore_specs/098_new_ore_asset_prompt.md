## Shared artistic language

Use a dark metallic ore with a restrained steel-blue crystalline seam. Finished material keeps a subdued related signature within a dense protective surface. It should read as exceptional useful metallurgy in a period setting. Keep the material distinct from the Asteroid event's own mineral identity.

Period event art should use 1936–1945 documentary composition, practical excavation, anonymous technicians, armor tests, guarded mine entrances, or prepared shipments. Required images do not depict named real people. News pictures are black and white. Report pictures use the proper processed documentary-card treatment rather than a generic rectangle renamed as a report.

Each icon family receives original subject art composed for that exact small canvas and native alpha behavior. Use the approved ImageGen route for original subjects. Generate transparent icons with actual transparent backgrounds from the start. Preserve alpha through processing and DDS conversion. Do not use chroma-key extraction or stretch one large focus-style composition into every family.

The direct and foreign ideas must remain distinguishable at native size. Direct art communicates possession of a source and protective material. Foreign art communicates a limited supplied allocation. Grade variants make raw material, the richer vein, and finished Super-Alloy legible without relying on extra text or only a color shift.

Action icons share a coherent palette and lighting while keeping distinct silhouettes. The twelve part 6 motifs are separate family-specific briefs. Report reuse within the declared agreement and control families is intentional because the underlying physical story is shared. It is not permission to collapse every event picture into one image.
