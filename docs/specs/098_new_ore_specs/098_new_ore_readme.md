## Central design decisions

One uniform global state discovery gives its actual controller at least +50% effective real armor. Deep Vein raises the direct tier to +75%. Super-Alloy raises one source to +200%, with diminishing extra gains below +300%. Foreign allocations grant +20%, +30%, or +80%, with one strongest source and no stacking. Existing applicable armor receives the same advantage as new armor.

The selected diplomacy loop has three agreement families, finite dates and places, actual equipment/XP commitments, one counteroffer, one future renewal period, and clear control-loss risks. Supplier-caused non-hostile relation changes cannot erase paid obligations. Voluntary event-controlled peaceful source transfers settle unused paid terms first. Other unsettled non-hostile transfers leave real control immediate and block automatic AI prepayment farming for 720 days.

Later deposits remain globally random, with map-sized scarcity and bounded evolving cadence. Claims, auctions, mine coexistence and asteroid minerals use the actual facts owned by their respective events. Normal diplomacy, supply, warfare, and territorial defense remain the surrounding campaign systems.
