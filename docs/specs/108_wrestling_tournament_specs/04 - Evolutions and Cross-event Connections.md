# A recognizable competition under greater Chaos

The four evolutions change the stakes, organization, preparation or admission rules of the tournament.
They do not replace registration, preparation, seeding, rounds, the final or history.
Every edition remains a complete competition when no evolution occurs.
Baseline cheating, dubious tonics, experimental offers, sponsorship, injuries and disputes remain available from the first ordinary edition.

## Independent evolution eligibility

| Track | Chaos threshold | Required campaign evidence | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| National Obsession | 200 or more | Actual political investment, major participation, returning champions or meaningful rivalries | Greater public commitments, support, publicity demands and conditional backlash |
| The Fixed Match | 400 or more | A tournament with valuable sponsorship or prestige and a plausible set of interested patrons | An organized multi-party corruption network, coordinated approaches and network-wide inquiries |
| Enhanced Athletes | 600 or more | An operational existing medical, biological, scientific or relevant industrial programme accessible to a participant | Systematic extreme enhancement with specialized benefits, instability, recovery and ethics consequences |
| Open Division | 800 or more | At least one existing suitable extraordinary being and a valid owner-supported nomination path | Admission of actual compatible extraordinary entrants under a published division rulebook |

No track requires the previous numbered track.
National Obsession can remain absent while a high-Chaos world holds an Open Division edition.
The Fixed Match does not create a research programme, and Enhanced Athletes does not generate a nonhuman population.

Use the shared evolution pacing convention with a 90-day MTTH anchor after the relevant eligibility becomes true.
Political commitment and rivalries shorten National Obsession's expected wait, valuable organized patronage shortens The Fixed Match's wait, mature operational programmes shorten Enhanced Athletes' wait, and several suitable existing extraordinary candidates shorten Open Division's wait.
An implementation must use the verified game-version MTTH adapter when reporting timing.
The anchor is not a promise that an evolution fires exactly 90 days later.

The first successful unlock is recorded once in the shared evolution history with event, track, actor where appropriate and actual date.
Each edition separately snapshots which tracks its published rules use.
An unlocked track remains known in campaign history, but a new edition must still satisfy its stated Chaos and capability gates before using its expanded rules.
A track can therefore be known but inactive in a calmer or capability-poor later edition.
Within a current edition, a fall in Chaos does not undo a committed procedure, erase a contestant or rewrite the draw.
Live capability loss still ends services that genuinely depend on the lost capability.

## Active unlock and evolved opening

When a track unlocks during entry or preparation, announce the concrete new choice and provide time to respond before lock.
If fewer than 15 preparation days remain, any new long course begins only in the next edition.
The current edition can still receive appropriate reporting and lawful short responses.
The first appearance is not an excuse to grant a free completed enhancement or replace a wrestler after lock.

When the tournament first fires into an already suitable high-Chaos campaign, the opening can include the independently qualifying evolved rules.
The opening builder evaluates each track's real conditions and a documented intensified-opening branch before publishing the invitation.
It records the same unlock receipts and shared evolution history as the normal path.
Disabled evolutions neither unlock nor set a consumed receipt.
The normal six-phase tournament still runs in full.

Division admission is fixed at roster lock.
An Open Division unlock after lock can introduce an announcement and the next-edition nomination prospect, but it cannot insert a creature into the ongoing draw.
Late National Obsession or corruption discoveries can affect present publicity, conduct and sanctions without changing positions.
Late Enhanced Athletes can expose or manage existing treatments, while a new long invasive course waits for a later preparation window.

## Evolution I: National Obsession

The player's important choice is how much public standing to stake on a fallible athlete.
The country can promote its champion heavily, support them without an extravagant promise, or lower expectations.
Those choices affect sponsor demands, support quality, reporting and the cost of defeat.

Use a public commitment category rather than another visible numerical meter.
Its states correspond to cautious support, a prominent national campaign and a government-backed victory promise.
The action preview gives the concrete support, publicity time and possible aftermath for the chosen category.
The country's current war needs, stability, recent humiliations and actual investment scale the commitment's cost.

| Choice or incident | Benefit | Price or adverse possibility | Closure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Promote the contender | More reliable support and greater recognition for a genuine run | PP, publicity time and stronger expectations | Commitment ends or changes with the actual result |
| Maintain measured support | Keeps preparation focused and preserves a smaller reward | Gives up the most lucrative sponsor terms | Normal loss does not cause national crisis |
| Make a victory promise | Greatest potential recognition and support | Highest political exposure and pressure to overtrain | A win pays only the committed reward, a loss can trigger bounded backlash |
| Resist excessive publicity | Preserves a camp or recovery interval | Sponsor may reduce future support | No refund of already consumed support, no retroactive loss of training |
| Confront an angry sponsor | Ends coercion or opens a substantiated inquiry | Administrative effort and possible political friction | Sponsor contract and case settle once |

A heavily promoted underdog run can be celebrated even after a semifinal loss.
A champion defeated narrowly by a comparable rival is treated differently from a visibly unfit favorite whose government promised certainty.
Backlash depends on the original public commitment, the credible pre-bout expectation and the actual outcome.
It is not applied to every defeat.

The strongest ordinary aftermath uses a temporary public-confidence or sporting-reputation effect, with an anchor of a small stability or political-power consequence lasting around 30 to 60 days.
The implementation should prefer one evolving event-owned recognition or embarrassment effect over several stacking national spirits.
No result creates an unrelated civil war or treats a sporting loss as automatic national collapse.

AI promotion depends on expected competitiveness, current legitimacy needs and spare capacity.
An AI fighting for survival is more likely to choose measured support or decline an expensive promise.
A stable minor with a credible contender can invest strongly.
Promotion never modifies the match resolver in the AI country's favor.

## Evolution II: The Fixed Match

An organized network connects promoters, officials and patrons across more than one team.
The network has an identity, members, approach records, known evidence, committed schemes and an adjudication state.
Its scope is a bounded set of actual participants and patrons, normally two to five countries or teams.
Do not create a political faction or a new government for it.

The player can refuse an approach, preserve evidence for an investigation, publicly expose a substantiated arrangement, or join a concrete scheme.
Each response has a visible immediate consequence and an uncertain later consequence.
Refusal leaves a clean path to victory.
Exposure is valuable because it can remove a real scheme or protect a future bout, not because clicking an accusation automatically awards the title.

| Scheme | Required timing | Possible effect | Limits and discovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Manipulated credential or favorable seed | Before roster lock only | A bounded official seeding-score distortion | Preserve the unmodified score and evidence record, keep top seed placement rules, never reshuffle after publication |
| Compromised official | Before the relevant result commits | A bounded decision advantage in a close bout | Use Part 03's one-draw clean-versus-biased threshold bands. An official-dependent changed winner receives only a narrow or long referee decision, never a decisive pin or clear submission. Timely review can neutralize the adjustment |
| Concealed assistance | A recorded pre-bout arrangement | A narrow illegal performance contribution | Uses the same bounded aid channel and detection exposure as baseline misconduct |
| Paid underperformance | The approached athlete or team accepts before tactics commit | Reduces that participant's performance or effort | Cannot set the winner directly, carries career and sanction risk, an honest opponent may still overcome a scheme intended against them |
| Coordinated false accusation | Before a real inquiry | Distracts or pressures a rival if credible evidence exists | Rumor alone cannot disqualify, proven fabrication becomes a separate offense |

The pre-lock distortion has a five-point seeding-score anchor and cannot change the published tree later.
Even a compromised official seed does not increase the wrestler's natural ability.
The bracket shows the original seed and can later mark its credential as tainted.
Future editions use corrected reputation and results.

Fixing changes bounded inputs to the ordinary resolver. The maximum signed official-bias adjustment has not been selected. The calculator's tested ±6 offsets are test coverage only, not an accepted cap; specify and review the magnitude before claiming exact fixing probabilities. Do not infer a new balance target from that test range.
It cannot assign a winner, replay an unfavorable roll, reroll after reload or force the player's country to lose.
A scheme aimed at a clean competitor can fail because the honest wrestler wins decisively or because the scheme is uncovered.
The complete AI and probability audit must compare the same bout with no scheme, an undetected scheme and a neutralized scheme.

A network inquiry uses accumulated evidence from actual approaches, payments, witnesses and official decisions.
Evidence can expose only the proven members and acts.
An innocent athlete from the same country does not automatically inherit every sponsor's guilt.
Consequences can include removal of an official, a result sanction, title vacancy, future suspension, lost support and targeted diplomatic friction.
The inquiry closes or remains explicitly unresolved at settlement, with future evidence handled as an amendment.

AI willingness to join depends on its particular patron incentives, competitive prospects, exposure and health risk.
Ideology may influence the public framing but cannot be a universal corruption switch.
Countries with strong clean prospects should usually prefer retaining their chance over buying an unreliable and dangerous scheme.

## Evolution III: Enhanced Athletes

This track expands modest ordinary experimentation into systematic extreme intervention.
Every offered course names an existing owner capability and its present service requirements.
The tournament reads those requirements and requests an eligible service through a reviewed adapter.
It never unlocks technology, creates a laboratory, manufactures a clone or grants a scientific institution to justify an offer.

| Course family | Existing capability needed | Competitive identity | Failure and recovery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Advanced restorative medicine | An operational existing regenerative or comparable medical programme | Better recovery from a manageable condition | Treatment time, incomplete response, a genuine complication and limits on repeat use |
| Extreme biological intervention | A verified existing biological or enhancement programme that actually supports the procedure | A strong improvement in one attribute with a defined weakness or instability | High Strain, health surveillance, possible suspension and a long recovery after failure |
| Industrial training apparatus | A real existing industrial or scientific device appropriate to training | More precise specialized preparation within the normal preparation cap | Expense, overuse injury and loss of support if the apparatus becomes unavailable |
| Species-specific care | A real existing owner system able to treat that admitted being | Preserves an unusual entrant's condition without giving unrelated powers | An incompatible treatment is unavailable, while losing the service can force withdrawal |

Course selection is not a generic shop of invented technologies.
The implementer first maps the actual owner API and its permitted results, then instantiates the appropriate course family.
If the owner provides no defensible operation, that row remains an unavailable integration until the owner contract exists.
This is an explicit dependency rather than a silent substitute technology.

Use a single major course per wrestler per edition.
An extreme intervention can add up to a 15-point anchor to one effective attribute before the global performance cap, paired with a meaningful weakness, instability or recovery burden.
The benefit cannot stack with several repeated payments or compound across every future edition without an owner-supported persistent transformation.
Ordinary human athletes retain credible counters through Technique, Endurance, Speed and Adaptability.

Treatment acceptance must be explicit.
A timeout chooses ordinary care or refusal.
An invasive procedure is unavailable once there is insufficient preparation time to complete its treatment and clearance.
Medical complications use a serious report and actual eligibility effects.
They do not create mass casualties because a single athlete was hurt.

If a programme is lost, services that require it stop and unused reservations settle.
A completed living clone or a completed biological change does not disappear merely because a laboratory later changes owner.
The adapter distinguishes continued service access from the existence and condition of the person already produced.
Only the originating system can decide whether its sustained effect actually ceases.

AI evaluates the specific matchup gain against the course's health burden, current condition, remaining preparation and national need.
It should reject high-risk treatment for an already competitive healthy athlete often enough to preserve varied clean fields.
The treatment pool must contain a credible ordinary alternative in every eligible state.

## Evolution IV: Open Division

Open Division admits suitable extraordinary representatives who already exist.
The athlete needs a stable identity, distinct portrait, actual owner provenance and the ability to participate under the published rules.
An uncontrolled swarm, an abstract technology, a generic military production permission or a named faction without a suitable being is insufficient.

Nomination refers to a real existing individual, or to a specific eligible member represented by an existing population or unit-family record through the owner's approved selection contract.
This does not spawn another cohort or transfer an entire division into the sporting event.
The owner retains the population, military and lifecycle systems.

| Entrant family | Possible strength | Meaningful limitation | Required proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Suitable zombie representative | Exceptional endurance or toughness | Slow reactions, limited technique and difficulty adapting | Existing compatible zombie population or actor, controllability and safe admission |
| Golem or similar constructed being | Powerful control and resistance to fatigue | Poor agility, predictable movement and technical vulnerability | Actual existing constructed being from its owner, compatible movement and rule compliance |
| Alien representative | Unfamiliar technique, agility or adaptation | Species-specific endurance, toughness or rules limitations | Existing compatible alien person or population, not contact or weapons technology alone |
| Exceptional clone | A profile derived from the actual clone's identity | Instability, narrower training or ordinary human limitations | Existing mature candidate and correct human/nonhuman classification |
| Mythological Spartan or other eligible hero | Extraordinary authored strengths | A documented weakness, unfamiliar rules or recovery cost | A real Age of Heroes actor or equivalent owner-supported being in this campaign |

These are behavioral directions rather than copied military unit statistics.
The wrestler profile must be balanced through all six attributes and the same bounded match model.
No entrant receives an automatic species victory, an immunity to technical loss or an unrestricted bonus multiplied by the number of divisions in its country.

Returned ordinary human Spartans remain eligible for the ordinary tournament.
Cloning is an origin, not by itself a species classification.
A mature compatible human clone can enter ordinary competition under the owner's human rules.
A genuinely nonhuman or extraordinary clone follows Open Division and any applicable enhancement rules.

Safety decisions address real control, fitness and conduct.
A frightened crowd can prompt a public reassurance, stricter supervision or a delayed eligibility review.
An uncontrollable entrant is refused before lock or withdrawn afterward.
Its originating invasion, army or state is neither created nor automatically destroyed by that decision.
Serious misconduct can injure an athlete or staff and must produce proportionate consequences.

Human underdogs can win through a favorable matchup, sound preparation and ordinary uncertainty.
Their achievement checks use actual contested wins against suitable extraordinary opponents.
A human does not earn that recognition from a first-round bye or a creature's withdrawal.

## Direct Chaos consequences

Tournament entry, selection, training, ordinary victory, a higher seed, an evolution unlock and the mere presence of a monster grant zero direct Chaos.
Only a concrete event-owned disruptive consequence can contribute an additional abnormal-consequence premium.
The edition keeps per-case receipts and a total event-owned positive-premium ceiling of ten Chaos as the initial balance target.
Generic shared accounting remains outside that ceiling and is never duplicated by a sporting premium.

| Milestone | Why it changes Chaos | Direction and anchor | Scaling, guard and shared overlap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary accusation, refusal, investigation or clean result | No demonstrated abnormal harm | 0 | Cannot farm Chaos through accusations, offers or selection clicks |
| Proven local violent misconduct with actual disruption | A sporting incident has created a real abnormal public disturbance | +2 | Once per case, do not add an extra death or injury count already owned elsewhere |
| National Obsession becomes actual sustained public disorder | A major public commitment produces a concrete disruptive aftermath | +2 | Requires the disorder outcome, not merely a heavily promoted defeat, once per commitment |
| Organized fixing is proven across several active delegations | A coordinated international sporting institution is materially compromised | +3, rising to +5 for broad proven participation | Scale by proven participating cases and consequences, not accusation count, once per network outcome |
| A verified enhancement scandal causes an actual serious institutional crisis | The treatment's abnormal conduct has a consequence beyond normal sporting injury | +2 to +5 | Requires actual misconduct and public consequence, no separate premium for the same shared atrocity finding |
| An admitted extraordinary being causes a real uncontrollable public incident | The competition's control failure produces a concrete disruptive event | +2 to +5 | Mere fear or nonhuman admission gives zero, account actual physical harm through its owner |
| A documented intervention resolves a tournament-owned continuing disruption | A real event-owned source of instability is contained | Reduce up to 2 of that case's outstanding event-owned premium | Cannot remove more than the recorded outstanding premium, cannot repeat for the same case |

If the event causes an actual civilian population loss, the population mutation and Deaths accounting use the shared measured-loss path exactly once.
Ordinary wrestler injury is a sporting condition, not a national manpower debit.
Actual unit loss belongs to the military or special-unit owner and is not inferred from a portrait's injury label.
Normal sports cheating is not automatically a CBRN atrocity or a shared condemnation case.
Only an actual qualifying owner-system offense can enter that subsystem.
