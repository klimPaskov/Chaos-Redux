# AI and probability handoff

## 1. Fairness and the evidence boundary

The AI uses the same eligibility, prices, camp effects, seed calculation, approach weights, result resolver, injury rules and adjudication boundaries as the player.
Changing a control flag cannot alter an entrant's probability or award.
Difficulty modifiers from unrelated crisis systems must not become an undisclosed player wrestling handicap.

AI differences come from lawful information, priorities and risk tolerance.
A country at war can prefer a modest delegation or decline.
A returning champion can receive serious support without making the same nomination inevitable.
A small technical contender can be an intelligent selection without a special hidden national bonus.

The weights below define intended policy and reference fixtures.
They are not claims about the semantics of native `ai_will_do`, GUI click weights or a normalized probability across independently visible decisions.
The implementation must inspect the actual selected execution route and audit its complete candidate set.
One event-owned choice job or one native decision admission reaches the same guarded action handler.
Do not run both routes as independent purchases.

The imported review receipts report that the supplied mathematical model was checked separately from native behavior. The bounded [107/108 probability review](../../plans/108_wrestling_tournament_plans/subagent_handoffs/107_108_probability_review_2026-10-10.md) records the later read-only formula check, the method-bin boundary correction and the unresolved official-bias magnitude. Neither review establishes native game behavior. The earlier statement that no HOI4 probability routes ran in the planning session is historical, not a current tool claim. Parts 09 and 11 map useful future evidence without making optional tools or live-game tests completion gates.

## 2. What each AI knows

| Information | Own delegation | Opponent or spectator AI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stable identity, public seed, known record, public injuries and declared style | Available | Available |
| Exact own preparation, purchased assistance and chosen tactic | Available to its actual controller | Hidden unless lawfully disclosed |
| Opponent qualitative attributes and public readiness observations | Same observations offered to the player | Same public observations |
| Actual opponent secret tactic or future roll | Unavailable | Unavailable |
| Misconduct the country's own staff knowingly authorized | Available for its response policy | Hidden until evidence is obtained |
| Investigator findings | Available at its assigned evidence access level | Only the public finding or legitimately shared evidence |
| Unrelated owner-system secrets | No new access through wrestling | No new access through wrestling |

AI analysis cannot inspect a hidden truth flag merely because it exists in the same scripted state.
It may know its own authorized wrongdoing.
It may not use an opponent's undiscovered tonic or future result receipt to select a perfect counter.
Visible forecasts are qualitative and use the same known-condition model for both control types.

## 3. Strategic profiles and spending limits

At announcement, freeze one modest preference profile for each invited country for this edition.
It can emphasize sporting continuity, cautious preparation, technical development or public prestige.
Derive bounded tendencies from actual finances, war pressure, candidate evidence, prior tournament experience and an event-owned stored variation draw.
Ideology may shape public framing and political priorities, but it does not determine guilt or physical ability.
No reroll occurs when a player opens the window or a country swaps controller.

An ordinary AI protects a PP reserve and industrial opportunity cost before buying preparation.
Use an anchor reserve of 50 PP in peace and 100 under substantial war pressure, adjusted to existing country economy conventions.
Reserve is an AI preference, not a player admission requirement or a new resource.
A country with insufficient surplus prefers Community support, rest or declining optional expenditure.
It cannot spend unavailable resources, reserve unavailable factories, or repeatedly borrow against an anticipated championship.

The ordinary total tournament budget should start around 20% of discretionary PP above that reserve, with a minimum viable entry and camp plan considered explicitly.
Publicly committed National Obsession can raise the preference up to 35%, but a negative military or construction margin still blocks reckless automatic expansion.
These are tuneable planning fractions whose actual campaign effects must be compared, not hard assumptions that every AI can afford six paid camps.
Free ordinary recovery and a valid saved tactic prevent a cash-poor delegation from stalling the tournament.

## 5. Training and approach behavior in detail

For each possible camp, estimate its legal expected improvement and post-camp condition using the same caps and diminishing returns as the player.
Reduce value when the remaining attribute headroom is small, an identical third camp would be penalized, or a known injury conflicts with that movement.
Give Conditioning additional value for a long qualifier route and Rest sharply increasing value above 60 Strain.
At the medical limit of 80 Strain, remove prohibited strenuous choices rather than merely lowering their weight.
Technique and study gain value against a known powerful field or an unusual existing entrant.
Do not know the exact future opponent before the draw.

As a complete illustrative choice fixture, a healthy balanced wrestler early in preparation can use weights 15 Strength, 20 Technique, 20 Conditioning, 10 Speed, 10 Study, 5 Rest and 20 Balanced.
For an otherwise identical strained wrestler, use 5, 10, 10, 5, 10, 50 and 10 when each option remains medically legal.
Both sum to 100.
They illustrate a intended direction of change.
They do not override stronger injury exclusions or imply a native decision's individual weight is a direct click percentage.

Approach evaluation considers the weighted six-attribute score, known condition, plausible opponent choices and expected recovery needs.
Evaluate against a small public-information distribution over opponent approaches, not the actual hidden selection.
Keep at least modest weight on approaches within a small utility margin, with a bounded stored choice draw.
Clear style disadvantages may receive zero preference while the approach remains legally available to a player.
The match equation itself never changes because the AI preferred a particular approach.

## 7. Investigation and wrongdoing reference cases

Truth, observed evidence and public judgment are separate.
A public cleared result describes the investigation's finding, not an instruction to delete the underlying actual event.
New genuinely independent evidence can justify a later case update.
The same paid inquiry cannot be rerun until it gets the desired result.

| Reference context | Substantiated violation | Cleared | Inconclusive | Scope of the example |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Actual violation with strong authentic evidence | 800 | 20 | 180 | Demonstrates effective investigation without guaranteed discovery |
| Actual violation with weak evidence | 250 | 100 | 650 | Hidden wrongdoing can remain unproven and later evidence can matter |
| Innocent entrant with strong exculpatory evidence | 0 | 850 | 150 | A loud accusation cannot by itself create actual guilt |
| Innocent entrant with weak available evidence | 0 | 450 | 550 | Inconclusive status does not impose a cheating DQ |

Each row is a complete 1,000-weight design fixture.
The resolver does not reveal the hidden truth to an opponent AI.
The last two rows intentionally exclude a cheating conviction without an actual underlying violation.
An independent travel, medical or representation failure can still make an innocent athlete unable to compete.
Deliberate fabricated evidence creates a separate actual wrongdoing record for the fabricator and needs its own proof.

Ordinary illegal tonic aid has Part 02's one-bout benefit cap of four effective points and its real health and detection exposure.
Organized official bias contributes only a bounded close-bout score adjustment. Its maximum signed adjustment is unresolved. The calculator's tested offsets from -6 through +6 are coverage inputs only, not an accepted cap.
Use Part 03's single three-branch winner pool to distinguish unaffected wins from the official-dependent difference between clean and biased probability.
An official-dependent winner can receive only a narrow or long referee decision, never a decisive pin or clear submission.
Audit both threshold orderings, zero difference and integer rounding, while keeping the complete pool at the same total weight.
When several illegal assistance sources overlap, cap their combined direct match-score advantage at six effective points, excluding an already applied physical course whose explicit attribute benefit is handled separately under Part 04.
No illegal adjustment escapes the .04 to .96 contested-bout bounds or chooses a winner directly.
If inquiry removes the assistance before freeze, the same clean input model applies.
If exposure comes after resolution, use the local adjudication rules rather than rerolling.

## 9. Probability audit protocol

Discover current probability-tool exposure and inspect the live schemas before using a supported route. Tool names and schemas are conditional, not guaranteed.
Inventory every random list, chance calculation, AI decision family, evolution opportunity and sequence-dependent state change introduced or modified by Event 108.
For each, record the full candidate set and all eligibility guards, scope bindings, base weights and modifiers. The assigned probability auditor owns the bounded weighted-logic pass for changed source; do not duplicate its audit or alter its balance targets through this handoff.
An omitted branch, impossible scope or unmodeled native callback is an audit limitation, not a zero-probability result.

Evaluate the reference fixtures and sweep the meaningful boundaries.
At minimum sweep Readiness and Strain around 35, 60, 80 and their adjacent values, attribute cap/headroom, four approaches, all injury grades, N around each draw power of two, the cost equality boundary, each Chaos threshold, and source possession/loss.
For the score approximation, target absolute probability error at most .005, exact complement symmetry after weight rounding, monotonicity and no overflow at supported extremes.

Compare matched complete models before and after a balance change.
For a new system that had no existing implementation, label the baseline as the authored reference or first implemented version.
Do not claim that an absent old Event 108 was an executable before-state.
Use sequence analysis only when every state transition, candidate pool, receipt and stopping condition in the sequence is modeled.
Use simulation only to resolve a stated remaining stochastic question, with seed, sample count and uncertainty reported.
Neither a large sample nor a passing schema check can compensate for an incomplete outcome model.

Evidence must identify what a tool actually checked, unsupported features, remaining assumptions, source hashes and artifact paths.
Use relevant probability tools when they are available and answer a concrete question. Under AGENTS.md, allow the initial request and at most one corrected or narrowed retry that addresses the failure; then calculate from the actual source and helpers or perform local checks. Distinguish source-derived results from native-tool evidence, and record any unresolved limitation without treating an unavailable optional route as a completion blocker.

## 10. Acceptance direction

Favor more often does not mean predict every tournament.
The mathematical probability floor preserves an ordinary underdog path, but the complete multi-round title distribution also depends on draw, injuries, recovery, public information and choices.
Do not multiply a single bout chance five times and present that as a measured full tournament forecast when those conditions change.

The accepted balance should support at least a careful low-budget plan, a specialized counter plan, a durable long-run plan and a stronger-funded broad plan.
No permanent champion, national-size multiplier, mandatory doping route, always-winning species, automatic bribe result or omniscient AI approach is acceptable.
Stop optional retesting after the declared invariants, complete pools and concrete remaining risks are resolved, then retain the reproducible evidence for the completion audit.
