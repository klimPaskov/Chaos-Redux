## Edition timing and bracket contract

Use the frozen shared preparation duration published for the edition. It is 70 to 110 days in five-day steps, with 90 days as the default. Entry closes on day 20 after announcement. Roster lock is entry close plus that duration. The earliest competition slot is roster lock plus 10 days, followed by reserved round slots five days apart. In the default fixture these dates are day 20, day 110 and day 120. At lock, the actual tree uses the trailing matching round-size slots reserved at entry close. Unused leading slots become rest, retaining the announced main-round and final dates. A 65-person registration reduced to 32 therefore has its first expected contest on day 130, while a reduction to two uses the final date on day 150. Distinguish earliest competition date, derived first expected contest and first actual played contest. Camps remain 15-day actions under Part 02. Settlement lasts 10 days. The next edition may open 180 days after archive. All exceptional delays share a combined maximum of 10 days and preserve minimum recovery.

At roster lock, let `N` be the final accepted and still-eligible entrant count and let `P` be the smallest power of two at least `N`. A field of zero or one closes as `insufficient_field`, without a title or fabricated opponent. For `N` from two through 32, the full `P`-leaf tree is the main draw. Its capacity is 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32. For `N > 32`, set `q = log2(P / 32)`. The first `q` levels are qualifiers and the remaining levels are the same fixed 32-slot main tree used by every larger field. An intact field contests exactly `N - 32` qualifier bouts and leaves 32 advancing routes. Byes occupy published slots and dates but are not contests, wins or random rolls.

The immutable tree is the full balanced seed tree defined in Part 03. All locked entrants appear once, seed labels are assigned once, and each advancement follows the locked links. A qualifier who advances keeps the original opening seed in the main draw. For example, seed 33 remains seed 33 if they defeat seed 32 for a main-draw route. The route changes its occupant, not its position or the graph. Do not split a large field into direct entrants and a separate hand-selected qualifier pool. Do not re-rank survivors, reseed after a result or alter a pairing to create a rivalry. Registration close reserves the qualifying dates for the field then known. If the field shrinks before lock, preserve the published main-round dates and use any unnecessary qualifier dates as rest. Do not admit a late entrant or change the calendar to fill a preferred slot.

The bracket view is generated from the locked slot, round, match and route arrays on every opening. It is a native dynamic bracket, never a fixed-draw screenshot or a static image fallback. Before a draw exists, show entry and schedule status instead of fabricated bracket lines. A missing or invalid tree is a visible data error and release blocker. Once the tree exists, vacancies, byes and unresolved branches render from their actual records.

The current official result for each match is authoritative. Preserve each original attempt receipt and append any correction. A result report shows the method, actual participants, scheduled and revised dates, winner or no-winner status, and outgoing route. Do not invent a score when no supported score was recorded. A bye is labeled as a bye. A walkover names its reason. A double withdrawal or double disqualification has no winner and outputs an explicit vacancy. A later match with one real entrant and one vacancy records vacant-branch advancement without a contested win. Two vacancies propagate as a vacant node. A no-contest retry retains the first attempt and uses one legitimate retry only when five days remain in the shared delay allowance. An exhausted or unsafe branch becomes vacant. A one-entrant final may award the published walkover title under Part 03. A final with no valid entrant leaves `champion_status = vacant`. Unsafe venue closure without a safe existing alternate is an interrupted edition with no title.

Pair and plan state freezes one day before each scheduled contest. Before that boundary, a timely ruling can change the current official output only under Part 03. After the boundary, the selected pair and saved match approaches cannot be replaced. The interface disables a late match-plan action and explains the cutoff without charging or changing the plan.

Late disqualification and other inquiry results must distinguish the original receipt from the current official standing. Before the successor's participant freeze, a justified ruling can overturn a bout and restore only its original clean opponent to its fixed outgoing slot. After the successor pair freezes, a ruling is prospective. It cannot revive an earlier loser into that frozen match, replay a legitimate successor or move another branch. During final settlement, apply the Part 03 rule for ratifying the clean final winner, awarding the direct clean finalist when a proven final violation permits it, or leaving the title vacant. A post-ratification amendment updates standing and sanctions without paying a second prize. The archive preserves the original result, ruling, current result and amendment chain. Unrelated completed bouts stay unchanged.

## Window contents and control contract

The same window presents all six ordinary phases through four local views: Bracket, Management, Profile and History. Its header always names the actual authoritative phase, including roster lock and final settlement. The player can reopen it from the event entry, the Event 108 Open bracket action and relevant Event 108 reports or match notices. An eliminated player and a spectator can continue to inspect public results and later rounds. Completed editions remain selectable in paged history.

The tournament surface exposes these working controls. They are behavior labels for design review, not final localization or verified native control bindings.

| Working control | Active state | Disabled or read-only state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Open bracket` | Opens the current Event 108 tree or its entry and schedule status | Hidden only when no Event 108 edition or archive is available |
| `Select entrant` | Initial nomination during entry, or a valid replacement for an already registered country before roster lock | After roster lock, for spectators or for ineligible candidates. Entry close prevents a new country entry but does not end the published pre-lock replacement right. |
| `Choose camp direction` | One selector contains all seven Part 02 directions: Strength, Technique, Conditioning, Speed and Reaction, Opponent Study, Rest and Rehabilitation, and Balanced | Read-only after roster lock, for spectators or when no eligible wrestler or camp interval remains |
| `Apply camp` | Applies the selected direction through the Part 02 action and resource contract | Disabled until a selection and valid quote exist, if another camp runs, if 15 days do not remain before lock or if medical rules prohibit it. The 90-day fixture permits six full camps, while the published duration and actual interruptions determine capacity. |
| `Review support offer` | Shows the real quote and effects for a permitted support choice | Disabled if the choice is unaffordable, closed by timing, or outside the player's authority |
| `Set match approach` | Stores one of the four Part 03 approaches for the player's active entrant before the one-day freeze | Disabled after pair and plan freeze, for a settled bout or for spectators. A missing response uses the saved default. |
| `Resolve case` | Opens the current case choices for an allegation or ruling owned by Event 108 | Disabled when no open case is addressable by the player or the response window is closed |
| `Open profile` | Opens a candidate, entrant or historical athlete's profile | Read-only for all spectators. Secret information is omitted according to policy below. |
| `Open match record` | Opens the full public record and current ruling for a selected match | Read-only when data has not yet been generated or when the match is not visible to the viewer |
| `Round or qualifier selector` | Selects a real round, qualifier level or main-draw view from the locked tree | Options unavailable for stages not present in the edition are omitted, not fabricated |
| `Edition history` | Pages saved Event 108 editions and opens an edition snapshot | Read-only. Paging does not discard older entrants, qualifier records or amendments. |

Only two custom management meters are numeric: **Readiness** and **Strain**, each shown as a 0 to 100 value for the player's own selected entrant. Injury, medical clearance, reputation, form, eligibility, tactical fit and the six natural attributes use named or qualitative states. Do not render six numeric bars, six scores or a six-spoke numeric dashboard. Each profile still gives all six attributes a meaningful comparative description: Strength, Technique, Endurance, Speed, Toughness and Adaptability. A single readable six-row list is preferred when it fits. Compare descriptors to an identified opponent or a stated field reference, and show `unknown` when scouting does not support a claim. Do not require paired tabs merely to hide attribute facts. Factual seed numbers, dates, match records and recorded results remain visible.

The selector contains all seven training directions, followed by one `Apply camp` action. It must not present seven independent primary buttons. The table spans several views, and only controls relevant to the current phase appear together. Contextual actions remain within the Part 02 ceiling of six simultaneous primary actions. Resource prices, refunds and awards are read from their owning transaction and result records. The window does not create a second currency, charge on opening, predict hidden rolls or perform a debit outside the verified action contract.

### Information policy

| Viewer | Visible information |
| --- | --- |
| Player with an active Event 108 team | Their own exact Readiness and Strain, permitted internal decision previews, all public bracket records, and their own committed training or treatment records |
| Other team or spectator | Public entrants, profiles, seeds, dated results, public condition disclosures, allegations and official findings, plus the full public bracket and archive |
| Any viewer without authority | No other team's unannounced tactic, private treatment choice, hidden evidence, private decision preview or exact internal six-attribute values |

Qualitative six-attribute descriptions may be public where the information policy and actual scouting support them. They never disclose hidden exact values. A concealed treatment becomes public only through an actual public disclosure or official finding. A match approach is hidden until its scheduled reveal rule permits disclosure. Information labels must distinguish `not known`, `not applicable`, `pending` and zero.

## Layout specification

The window keeps the entrant identity, selected edition, current phase and next scheduled date visible while the bracket body scrolls. Bracket pairing rows include the opening seed, country flag snapshot, portrait, name, match state and outgoing route. Selecting a wrestler opens the full profile. Selecting a match opens its dated detail and amendment history. Match connectors follow the tree data, including qualifier routes into the fixed main draw. At large fields, horizontal and vertical scrolling or real round selection exposes every node. A selected match must remain traceable through all following rounds.

## Identity, portraits and flags

Every entrant has one stable person identity, one portrait registry key and one edition affiliation snapshot. The archive keeps the wrestler name, portrait version key, country name and flag presentation key for the affiliation at entry. A changed or annexed country cannot rewrite that historic presentation. An optional current-country fact is a separate, explicitly labeled field and may include a separately mapped current flag. Do not use a live `GetFlag` result as the archived entrant flag or silently replace the historic flag with a current regime flag.

Portrait creation and source research remain with the portrait researcher and `chaosx_portrait_creator`. This handoff sets production fields and use rules only. It does not source people, approve rights, create images or certify usability. The project target is at least 80 unique usable wrestler identities and portraits. The current research handoff tracks 24 people and has produced no runtime assets, so the target is not met. Capacity must scale to the union of supported selectable nominees and actual field demand, including supported Open Division candidates. Recalculate capacity when supported countries, candidate pools or owner-supported beings expand. Do not reuse a face for different identities, silently narrow the eligible country set because portraits are short, or ship an anonymous replacement face as if it represented a named entrant. An asset shortage is a release blocker. Eligibility and field reports must show the real supported roster and missing-asset blocker without suppressing a country.

The portrait registry must support these fields before portraits can be treated as usable:

| Registry field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Stable wrestler/person ID and edition entrant ID | Prevent identity collision across selection, bracket, match records and future editions |
| Source mode | Grounded historical person, grounded period contestant needing defensible source identity, or an explicitly generation-eligible fictional high-Chaos or impossible being. Source mode is separate from species and production status |
| Historical subject or fictional identity reference | Identify exactly whose portrait is represented |
| Source citation, repository or creator reference, attribution and use-rights evidence | Preserve verifiable provenance and permitted use |
| Portrait version key, crop reference, intended display dimensions and runtime asset path | Keep selection, bracket and archive identity consistent |
| Production state | Distinguish pending research, explicit source placeholder, styled final pending approval, approved source or fictional art, native import and runtime-ready states |
| Identity review, distinct-face review and final reviewer record | Ensure the right person is shown once per identity and no face is reused for another person |
| Native rendering result and tested contexts | Confirm the portrait works in profile, bracket, match and archive contexts after import |

A `source_placeholder` exists only after an actual correctly identified and attributed source image has been acquired, reviewed, framed, resized to 156 x 210, converted to runtime DDS and wired under the portrait production contract. A researched name or URL is not a source placeholder. It remains visibly provisional and pending the user-operated historical styling workflow and final review. Count it as accepted temporary runtime coverage only when the user has accepted that specific provisional mode. Do not count it as a completed styled portrait. A historical portrait needs checked provenance and use rights. A grounded country's ordinary period contender needs a defensible sourced identity or period source under the portrait contract, otherwise an explicit unresolved source-mode requirement. Giving that contender a fictional name does not authorize a generated face. The authorized ImageGen route is limited to the current asset gate's truly fictional high-Chaos or impossible/supernatural subjects. Source-mode, styling, import and visual review remain future production steps. A registered identity without an accepted runtime portrait does not count toward the usable 80 or the live field capacity gate.

Each grounded portrait also requires a durable source package under `docs/assets/portraits/108_wrestling_tournament/` using its exact runtime basename. Keep the untouched original, lossless crop PNG, crop/equality JSON, deterministic 156 x 210 processed PNG and provenance `.txt` together. Use the current portrait extraction tool and preserve actual detector or explicit manual-crop evidence, dimensions, exact coordinates and decoded-pixel equality. The independent reviewer must be different from the producer and record identity, framing and provenance verdicts before DDS conversion or wiring. A missing or failed gate remains blocked. The archive never supplies a runtime path and survives cleanup of the separate temporary `docs/assets/108_wrestling_tournament/` workspace.
