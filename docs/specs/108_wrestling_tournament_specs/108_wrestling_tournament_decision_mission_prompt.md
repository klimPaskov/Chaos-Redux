## Complete baseline action map

| Action family | Admission and timing | Choice and completion contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Register delegation | Entry phase, actual eligible country, no existing entry, ready distinct candidate pool | One country slot and the ten-PP scaled anchor quote. No payment before a valid pool exists. Opt out cleanly if no valid saved nomination is supplied |
| Nominate or replace | Own registered entry and an eligible reserved person, before roster lock | Select one actual identity. Replacement remains possible after entry closes for that same registered country. No new nomination currency. Old training, injury, assistance and misconduct remain attached to the old person |
| Choose and start camp | Preparation active, no running camp, at least 15 days remain and medical requirements pass | Offer Strength, Technique, Conditioning, Speed, Study, Rest and Balanced inside one selector and apply control. Use the five-PP base quote and real completion receipt. Specialized supplies apply only when the actual action requires and displays them |
| Cancel camp | Own active camp | Forfeit incomplete gains and consumed administration, settle unused commitments once. A cancellation never becomes a completed training opportunity or a reroll of its incident |
| Set or renew funding | Own entry, preparation phase and valid new term | Community, Established or National support with the Part 02 costs, zero/one/two factory capacity and 30-day term. No retroactive gains. Renewal, downgrade, stop and mid-term capacity loss preserve completed gains and change only future support |
| Recover and seek ordinary care | Treatable injury, low readiness or high strain with valid available time | Display time, medical status and any paid support quote. Recovery can improve, fail or complicate according to the complete eligible pool. Severe medical invalidity cannot be bought away by an unrelated fee |
| Use final partial recovery | An earlier incident disrupted the camp schedule and five or ten days remain before lock | Proportionate recovery only under Part 02's disruption gate. Do not grant a full 15-day camp's attributes or a fresh full camp incident opportunity |
| Tactical briefing | Draw published and an actual next opponent exists | Use the five-day interval opportunity for bounded matchup knowledge. Paid scouting uses its stated modest quote. Public profile information cannot reveal a hidden tactic, undiscovered treatment or future roll |
| Choose bout approach | Own living entrant with a pending match before its one-day pair/tactic freeze | Select Overpower, Technical Counters, Conserve Energy or Balanced. No currency. Keep the saved/default tactic, initially Balanced, when a valid response is absent |
| Review allegation | Actual case and valid role as involved team, accuser or affected delegation | Investigate, cooperate, contest or refrain with the real evidence and cost. Track response per actor so one answer cannot overwrite another country's pending case |
| Respond to banned tonic | Real offer and medically valid athlete | Reject, report, accept the explicitly illegal risk, or seek lawful care. Acceptance records short-lived aid, strain, medical and detection exposure. One offer cannot stack or refresh by reopening |
| Respond to experimental offer | Actual provider, capability, proposal, compatible person and enough time | Verify, accept the bounded disclosed course, seek safer care or refuse. Track duration, cost, complication, instability, eligibility and source loss separately. The offer never grants the supplying programme |
| Handle sponsor pressure | Actual support or public commitment | Attend, renegotiate, resist or expose misconduct. Resolve lost preparation, support, promise and reputation consequences. A vague rumor cannot create a fictional paid sponsor |
| Resolve travel, weigh-in or identity dispute | Specific unresolved dispute on the current entrant | Supply authentic proof, arrange a feasible journey, take an allowed common delay or withdraw. Preserve the country registration deadline and the post-lock absence rules |
| Withdraw contestant or delegation | Current control and clear consequence preview | Resolve the current nomination/entry state under Part 01. Pre-lock substitution uses the existing country entry. Post-lock withdrawal yields the correct walkover or vacancy, with actual pending costs settled |
| Follow, inspect or change notification preference | Any observer after announcement, including eliminated countries | Viewer state only, no cost, athletic mutation, RNG or achievement grant |

Use at most one camp mission, one support contract and one urgent case mission in the ordinary flow.
Related case updates reuse the actual case.
Expose three to five useful primary actions, with six as the ceiling.
Keep all seven camps selectable without seven simultaneously competing mission cards.
Group completed actions and historical evidence into the profile and archive instead of continuing stale missions indefinitely.
