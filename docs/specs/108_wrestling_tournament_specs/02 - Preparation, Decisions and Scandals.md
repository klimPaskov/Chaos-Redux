# Preparing a contender

Preparation turns the nomination into a national commitment.
A wrestler begins with a useful natural profile, then the country decides how to improve performance without arriving exhausted, injured or compromised.
The common preparation window supports several completed choices and at least one meaningful opportunity to recover or change direction.
Baseline competition includes every controversy family requested in the brief.

## What a preparation cycle does

A normal camp occupies 15 days and only one camp can be active for a wrestler at a time.
With the 90-day preparation anchor, a country can complete six cycles.
The country can mix all seven training directions during those cycles.
An action is unavailable if it cannot finish by roster lock, with the remaining time shown.
A five- or ten-day shortened final recovery option is permitted when an earlier incident disrupted the schedule, but it grants proportional recovery and no full camp's training gain.

Healthy entrants begin around Readiness 45 and Strain 10, adjusted by their actual career condition.
Readiness below 35 makes sustained training inefficient and invites a recovery choice.
Strain above 60 makes further intensive work increasingly hazardous.
At 80 or more Strain, medical review blocks the most strenuous camp until the condition improves.
An injury can impose a stronger restriction regardless of the numbers.

Most camp gains are edition-specific preparation bonuses attached to the trained attribute.
They expire gradually after the edition and do not become permanent natural talent.
The permanent career gain from ordinary training is limited to a small improvement in the two most consistently trained attributes once per edition.
Use a one-point anchor for each, with an authored career-training cap and an age-adjusted decline path.
Match experience can improve consistency and Adaptability independently of buying more camps.

For each successful attribute camp, calculate a preparation gain from a four-point anchor, current attribute headroom, health, funding quality and repeated-specialization fatigue.
The headroom factor ranges from 0.25 to 1.0 as the underlying attribute approaches its ceiling.
The third consecutive camp in the same direction receives half the normal preparation gain and extra Strain.
No single attribute receives more than ten edition-preparation points, and the combined edition-preparation budget is capped at 30.
These internal limits keep national expenditure from replacing wrestler quality or tactics.
Explain diminishing benefit in the action preview without showing the full hidden scoring calculation.

## Seven training directions

The following changes are tuning anchors for a healthy athlete at ordinary funding.
Injury, current Readiness, Strain, previous specialization and country support adjust the result.
The confirmed action preview shows expected Readiness and Strain changes and any known health restriction.

| Working direction | Main improvement | Readiness / Strain anchor | Opportunity cost and risk | AI preference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strength camp | Strength preparation and early control | +10 / +15 | Gains less when already very strong, with extra risk under an existing joint or muscle injury | Favors an underdeveloped power profile facing a physically vulnerable field |
| Technique camp | Technique preparation and counter consistency | +10 / +10 | Less immediate physical improvement, with slower progress when changing a deeply established style | Favors weaker technicians and likely powerful opponents |
| Conditioning camp | Endurance preparation and late-round resilience | +10 / +10 | Modest first-bout advantage, better value when a long run is plausible | Favors low Endurance or a likely extended qualifier route |
| Speed and reaction work | Speed preparation and explosive entries | +10 / +15 | Repeated bursts increase strain and expose an injured lower limb | Favors slow wrestlers or opponents with poor reactions |
| Opponent study | Adaptability preparation and a bounded style-specific scouting advantage | +5 / +5 | Uses a full camp slot and loses some value if the expected opponent is eliminated | Favors known champions, recurring rivals and unfamiliar styles |
| Rest and rehabilitation | Condition and a manageable injury's recovery | +10 / -25 | Grants no attribute preparation and cannot instantly remove a severe injury | Strongly favored under high Strain, injury or poor Readiness |
| Balanced camp | Smaller Technique, Endurance and Adaptability preparation gains | +10 / +10 | Provides less specialized advantage in a single matchup | Default when needs are mixed, information is weak or a new AI candidate has no strong specialization |

Readiness and Strain are clamped to their valid range after actual effects.
The lower Strain from rest does not imply that a serious fracture or severe treatment complication has healed.
Clinical recovery dates and specific eligibility restrictions remain authoritative.

Opponent study before lock targets an available style or a known likely contender rather than a fabricated future pairing.
After lock, a short tactical briefing can target the actual next opponent, but it cannot add seeding evidence or natural attributes.
The study bonus is strongest when the information is current and the athlete has enough Adaptability to use it.
It is capped below the value of a substantial underlying skill difference.

## Funding without a separate currency

The country pays from existing resources and commits real preparation time.
Community support is a viable route for a low-industry country.
Larger support contracts buy consistency, modest training efficiency and better recovery options.
They do not purchase match wins.

| Support level | Default commitment for a 30-day term | Benefits | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Community delegation | No additional industrial reservation, ordinary camp fees and the same camp time | Full access to ordinary training, rest, tactics and fair competition | Less reliable recovery and less support against travel disruptions |
| Established coaching staff | 15 PP and one civilian factory reserved for 30 days | About 15 percent greater effective camp gain within the same caps, more reliable rehabilitation and travel preparation | Competes with civilian construction and cannot be refunded as if the term never happened |
| National training program | 35 PP and two civilian factories reserved for 30 days | About 25 percent greater effective camp gain within the same caps, a better support team and one additional medical response opportunity | Diminishing benefit, larger wartime opportunity cost and higher sponsor pressure when publicly promoted |

One or two factories are discrete native capacity commitments, which is the reason these counts do not follow five-unit resource rounding.
No country is required to possess a spare civilian factory to enter or use ordinary camps.
The quality multiplier applies to the remaining headroom rather than increasing the preparation caps.
National funding therefore cannot turn repeated camps into unlimited power.

PP anchors scale with current national capacity, competing war needs and the degree of publicity promised.
Use a capacity multiplier between 0.75 and 1.75 and a war-pressure multiplier between 1.0 and 1.5.
Ordinary costs are rounded to five PP for presentation before the shared discount quote is applied.
The final payable quote follows the shared framework's actual resource quantum and may consequently differ from a multiple of five.
It must be displayed with enough precision and accepted at an exactly equal balance.

Medical supplies can consume support equipment when a treatment actually calls for it.
A default rehabilitation supply package uses five units, scaling with injury severity and the existing country's shortages.
It does not consume weapons, divisions or national manpower for an ordinary wrestler's injury.
Actual military or civilian harm is accounted only where a real owner-system consequence occurred.

Factory-funded contracts use a native decision reservation with one owner.
The bracket may show the contract and open its decision surface, but it must not independently debit or reserve factories through an invented GUI API.
If a verified native activation bridge is later used, the same admission and receipt must govern it.
The inspected clone-maturation contract provides a relevant reservation-order precedent and the universal-cost framework requires a separate owner adapter for factory commitments.
See evidence R05 and R06.

Funding is reviewed when its 30-day term ends.
A country can renew, downgrade or stop.
Increasing support late in preparation benefits only time that remains.
There is no retroactive camp improvement, refund arbitrage or post-lock seed update.
If a country loses capacity mid-contract, release unavailable reservations, record the interruption, preserve already earned gains and reduce only future support.

At most one camp mission, one support contract and one urgent case mission should be active for a country in the ordinary flow.
Related case updates share the case rather than producing a stack of duplicate mission timers.
The interface exposes three to five context-appropriate primary controls, with six as the ceiling.
The seven camp directions remain options inside a single camp selector and an apply action.

## The ordinary incident pool

An incident opportunity occurs at a camp completion or an explicitly scheduled pre-bout checkpoint.
It is not rolled whenever the GUI refreshes or a daily all-country hook runs.
Use one complete weighted pool for each opportunity type, retaining a substantial no-incident outcome.
An initial camp-pool fixture has weights 800 for no incident, 70 for a favorable ordinary training report, 30 for strain or injury, 20 for an allegation, 20 for a tonic offer, 10 for an experimental offer, 30 for sponsor pressure, 10 for eligibility friction and 10 for travel trouble.
These sum to 1000 only in the named healthy baseline fixture with every listed branch eligible.
Actual factors and invalid branches change the denominator and require the complete pool audit.
They are design weights rather than measured incident probabilities in the game.

Risky training raises injury pressure, a vulnerable route raises travel pressure, public national promotion raises sponsor pressure, and a real scientific capability makes the relevant experimental offer eligible.
No capability means that offer has zero eligibility, not that the tournament grants the capability.
High Strain cannot be offset simply by increasing the no-incident weight through funding.
Safe training and rest must measurably reduce expected serious harm.

Ordinary reporting caps repeated nuisance incidents per country while preserving genuine injuries and critical eligibility changes.
Use a two-major-incident anchor during ordinary preparation, excluding player-initiated misconduct and necessary updates to an already open case.
The global tournament can still contain different incidents affecting other countries.
Summaries prevent that breadth from turning into a popup for every observer.

## Allegations, evidence and findings

Every case has separate truth, allegation, evidence, public status and adjudication fields.
The true underlying incident is committed once when the case is created.
An accuser's influence changes access to publicity or witnesses, not whether the accused actually committed the act.
The player-facing case states are alleged, under inquiry, cleared, unproven and proven.
Only a finding supported by the investigation contract can impose a cheating sanction.

An investigation can uncover relevant evidence, disprove the accusation or finish without enough evidence.
A false allegation does not automatically become a permanent reputation penalty for the accused.
An unsuccessful good-faith complaint is not treated as malicious fabrication.
Proof that an accuser knowingly fabricated evidence can create its own sanction.

| Case | Meaningful responses | Favorable or contained outcome | Adverse outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Suspected bribed official | Request a review, accept an impartial substitute, cooperate or conceal known payments | Official replaced, innocent athlete cleared, reliable result stands | Proven interference leads to a bout sanction, suspension or sponsor investigation |
| Illegal hold or concealed assistance | Accept the referee's ruling, appeal with evidence, disclose team misconduct or withdraw the affected athlete | Error corrected before result commitment or complaint remains unproven | Disqualification, injury responsibility and a recorded conduct sanction |
| Match-fixing rumor | Decline a contact, preserve evidence, expose the approach or enter the arrangement | A refusal protects clean eligibility, an inquiry can expose the actual network | A committed scheme raises exposure and can produce a later finding even if it failed to affect the bout |
| Banned tonic | Refuse, report, accept with known risk or seek legitimate treatment | Clean preparation remains competitive and the supplier may be removed | Short-lived performance help, extra Strain, illness and a disqualification risk |
| Unsafe training | Reduce intensity, rest, continue the plan or change support staff | A weaker immediate gain preserves condition and later-round prospects | A strain, severe accident or lost preparation time |
| Experimental medicine or device | Verify the actual provider, choose the intervention, request ordinary care or refuse | Modest legal recovery or a bounded specialized advantage | Complication, instability, eligibility challenge or exposure of unethical conduct |
| Sponsor or political demand | Attend publicity, renegotiate, resist or reveal coercion | Retain some support without an impossible victory promise | Lost training time, excess pressure, financial overcommitment or backlash |
| Travel, weigh-in or identity dispute | Provide the actual record, arrange a valid route, accept a permitted common delay or withdraw | Eligibility confirmed and the entrant reaches the scheduled bout | A pre-lock replacement or a post-lock recorded withdrawal |

The tournament does not declare real historical wrestlers guilty of historical doping, corruption or cruelty without a source.
Generated incidents are plainly events of this alternate campaign and derive from its fictional teams, offers and decisions.
Research-backed biographical text remains separate from them.

## Short-lived aid and lasting consequences

A baseline illegal tonic can improve a narrow performance contribution for one bout by a small anchor amount, such as four effective points before the matchup cap.
It also increases Strain and adds a health and detection exposure record.
The benefit cannot stack by consuming several offers or reopening the same event.
Its expiry is tied to the bout or date receipt rather than a viewer action.
No exact preparation recipe or modern drug system is required for the mechanic.

Legitimate ordinary therapy can improve recovery without being treated as cheating.
Unusual training equipment must fit the period or come from an actual existing capability.
Technology names, ownership, site requirements and revocation rules must be verified against the owning system before implementation.
A rumor about a scientist does not qualify a country for Enhanced Athletes.

Serious injury stops strenuous participation until the required recovery or medical clearance.
Treat it with a serious report, a credible recovery horizon and a real lost opportunity.
A country can withdraw an athlete rather than being forced into reckless treatment to remain competitive.
Losing a wrestler's tournament eligibility does not delete their career or replace their portrait with a new person.

## Inquiries during the bracket

Immediate refereeing decisions occur before the match result is committed.
A timely serious case can use one five-day common competition delay if the edition still has that allowance, moving dependent matches together and preserving minimum recovery.
Combined venue, inquiry and no-contest retry delays cannot exceed ten days for the edition.
The held match shows its reason, current finding status and revised date.

If the allowance is exhausted, decide only what current evidence establishes.
An unproven accusation cannot become a disqualification merely to keep the calendar moving.
A medically ineligible athlete can still be withdrawn on the independent medical rule.
Later evidence can amend official standing and impose future sanctions according to the result policy in part 03.
It never reruns unrelated legitimate matches.

## Payment and timeout behavior

Each paid action records actor, edition, wrestler, action kind, selected target where relevant, actual cost components, start, due date and a unique receipt.
Confirmation rechecks authority and the current quote before any debit.
The user sees a changed quote before authorizing a different payment.
Once paid, refunds use the recorded actual amount and consumed portion, not a new quote.

Safe timeout choices are first in irreversible confirmation events.
No response to a doping, corruption or experimental-treatment offer means refusal or ordinary care.
No response to a nomination request means decline entry if there is no valid saved choice.
No response to a tactic request uses the saved default, initially balanced.
Timeouts must not purchase a new expensive contract, admit wrongdoing, conceal evidence or treat silence as consent to invasive treatment.

Authority loss cancels pending commands and unstarted offers without running a normal reward or failure path.
Already completed training remains part of the athlete's career record.
The payment owner releases each reservation or refund exactly once, including when native decision removal later calls the same cleanup path.
