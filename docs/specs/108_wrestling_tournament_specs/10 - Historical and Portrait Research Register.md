They are not automatically the original archival item or independent proof of rights.

## Status meanings and source-mode boundary

- **Identity-confirmed** means a named item caption or separate independent athlete evidence supports who the person is. It does not mean the portrait has been visually reviewed or the image can be used.
- **Source-lead** means the photo-to-person link, date, or underlying source is incomplete. Do not treat it as a named historical portrait ready for production.
- **Portrait-reviewed** is a separate visual review state. All rows are not reviewed.
- **Rights review** is a separate state. An archive record, a Creative Commons label, or a Commons public-domain template is reported as source metadata only. No legal determination is claimed.
- **Production-cleared** is a separate owner-controlled state. All rows remain not cleared.

An unresolved source row is documentation of a content gap, not a portrait and not a source mode. `source_placeholder` may be assigned only after the correct-person attributed image has been downloaded, explicitly cropped to the correct head-and-shoulders composition, resized to 156 x 210, converted to the required runtime DDS variant, wired to the correct stable portrait identifier, and reviewed against the owner contract. No row in this register meets those conditions. `styled_final` requires the user-operated authorized provider workflow, its job and revision evidence, and independent identity, framing, and provenance review. A generated historical likeness is not a fallback. Grounded ordinary contestants need a defensible source or an explicit unresolved source-mode gate even if assigned a fictional narrative name. ImageGen eligibility is restricted to the current asset contract's truly fictional high-Chaos or impossible/supernatural subjects. This research pass produced no art.

### Actual simultaneous candidate and field capacity

Let `D_t` be the number of distinct eligible delegations in campaign `t`, including any existing eligible extraordinary country, after checking actual representation and owner conditions. Let `k_d` be the number of unique reserved candidate identities offered to delegation `d`, normally three and never below two. Its pool can contain human and extraordinary alternatives. Each person can be reserved for only one delegation at a time. The selected entrant is one of those candidates and is not counted a second time.

The simultaneous active source and portrait demand is `U_active = sum(k_d)` over those delegations. The total retained asset demand also includes distinct archived portrait versions and other committed reusable career portraits not already counted in the active union. The production capacity must therefore be at least `max(80, size(active candidates union retained archive and career portrait versions))`. A new style version for an existing person is an additional retained asset but is not a second distinct identity toward the 80-person floor. Record both counts.

The accepted field has at most `D_t` entrants because each delegation sends only one wrestler. Adding three extraordinary candidate alternatives to an already counted country increases its candidate and asset demand, not the number of entrant slots. If eight extra candidates are offered across an existing 32-country set with 96 ordinary candidates, active demand can be 104 portraits while the maximum selected field remains 32. A newly eligible separate country increases `D_t` once and requires its own meaningful pool.

The 80-person floor covers at most 26 complete three-candidate country pools, with two identities left over. It does not provide three choices for a fully selectable 32-country field, which requires 96 identities before additional alternatives and retained historical versions. Eighty can cover 40 countries only at the two-person minimum. Before claiming worldwide coverage, enumerate the actual eligible delegation set for every supported start-year range and raise the target to its calculated demand. The 32-slot main draw does not cap candidate or qualifier portrait requirements.

Candidate identities must be globally distinct within an edition. The same identity can return in a later edition if active-year evidence permits it, but one person must not be offered to two countries at once. The identity registry and portrait key must be stable across selection, bracket, match report, achievement, and archived edition views. Count historical archive-only champions separately if they do not occupy current candidate slots, but do not count the same person twice in the unique identity total.
