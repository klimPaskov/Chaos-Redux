# Asteroid Incoming: Research Basis

## Research role

The research supports the event premise, damage families, dust aftermath, close-passage presentation, and extraordinary-mineral flavor. It does not turn Event 028 into a literal impact simulator. The user's percentages and armour values remain the design authority.

## Deflection premise

NASA's Double Asteroid Redirection Test demonstrated that a high-speed kinetic impact could change an asteroid moonlet's orbit. NASA also reported that ejecta recoil substantially amplified the transferred momentum. This supports the event's core premise that a brief intervention can alter a trajectory and that fragmentation or ejecta can become part of the consequence.

**Design use:** The scientific project can redirect the main body. At higher Chaos, the alteration destabilizes the object and creates fragments. The event does not claim that a 1930s program could reproduce DART technology through ordinary historical science. The project is an alternate-history breakthrough supplied by the event premise.

## Regional impact effects

The Earth Impact Effects Program developed by Collins, Melosh, and Marcus estimates regional consequences across crater formation, ejecta, thermal radiation, seismic shaking, and atmospheric blast. The U.S. Geological Survey also describes shock waves, thermal effects, debris, damaged ground cover, runoff, water impacts, crop impacts, and air-quality effects as important impact consequences.

**Design use:** Event 028 combines center destruction with several land-adjacency rings. The rings are a map-readable abstraction of several physical effects. They are not distance calculations in kilometers.

## Atmospheric dust and climate

Research on the Chicxulub impact has modeled long-lived fine silicate dust, reduced sunlight, cooling, and disruption to photosynthesis. A 2025 Science Advances study also modeled global climate and ecological responses to dust from a smaller Bennu-type impact. The exact outcomes depend heavily on impact size, location, material, atmosphere, and model assumptions.

**Design use:** Every main impact creates a Dust Load that affects production, supply, weather quality, and Air Cleanliness. Severity scales with actual destruction. The event avoids presenting its stage values as scientific predictions.

## Downwind and downstream damage

USGS impact-preparedness work highlights debris effects on crops, drinking-water reservoirs, air quality, bare soil, runoff, and flooding. It also uses volcanic ash and wildfire behavior as useful analogs for some aftermath questions.

**Design use:** Event 028 can call supported Event 013 disaster families for secondary wildfire, landslide, flooding, or ash-like debris behavior in exact states. The main impact remains owned by Event 028.

## Metallic asteroids and impact-related ore

NASA describes S-type asteroids as containing silicate material and nickel-iron and M-type asteroids as metallic nickel-iron bodies. NASA's Earth Observatory notes that the Sudbury impact structure is associated with abundant nickel and copper ore deposits around the basin.

**Design use:** Extraordinary Minerals can use dense meteoric alloy, fused material, and shock-formed crystal as plausible flavor directions. The plus 100 and plus 20 armour effects are a deliberate Chaos Redux mechanic. They are not claims about the normal military value of a real crater.

## Period close-passage flavor

The asteroid Hermes was observed in 1937 and later linked to several close approaches, including a 1942 Earth approach. JPL describes the object's chaotic trajectory and the difficulty of reconstructing its encounters.

**Design use:** The close-passage news can use period observatory work, uncertain orbital calculations, photographic plates, and public interest as visual and writing reference. Event 028 should not identify its fictional object as Hermes unless a later accepted design deliberately does so.

## Research limits

- Impact effects vary with diameter, velocity, angle, density, target geology, land or ocean location, and atmospheric behavior.
- State adjacency is a gameplay representation of distance and terrain.
- Dust-stage values are balance choices.
- The event does not model tsunamis because the accepted design chooses a land state and land-adjacency rings.
- Extraordinary material is a high-Chaos reward with scientific flavor, not an economic forecast.
- The research does not support nuclear classification. Asteroid effects need their own source identity.
