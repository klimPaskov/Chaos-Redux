# Achievement Prompt: System Camp Repression Rework

Achievements should reward dismantlement, reform, exposure, containment, survival, and defeating blowback. Do not reward maximizing atrocities, optimizing killing, or targeting protected groups.

Working concepts:

1. Inherit the Ledger, Close the Ledger
   - Start as a country with dormant historical camp infrastructure.
   - Dismantle every active site before severe discovery or tribunal threshold.

2. Papers for the Liberated
   - As a democratic major, discover and document several severe sites before the responsible country capitulates.
   - Disqualify if the player operates an active radicalized site.

3. The Doctor Loses His War
   - As Germany, allow the Mengele Directorate revolt to fire, then defeat it and dismantle all laboratory-linked sites.

4. No Pingfang Shadow
   - As Japan, remove Ishii from program control, shut down experiment-linked sites, and avoid a major outbreak.

5. Grain Before Fear
   - As the Soviet Union, survive Union Crisis without famine reaching critical pressure.

6. Dominion Without Chains
   - As the U.K., keep the Raj loyal while dismantling expanded detention infrastructure.

7. Redress Before Victory
   - As the U.S.A., terminate relocation authority and complete a redress or court-review chain before the war ends.

8. Congo Reformed
   - As Belgium, reform the Congo concession labor system before international exposure.

9. Roads Without Camps
   - As Italy, retain Libya after dismantling colonial camp infrastructure and relying on conventional garrison and infrastructure decisions.

10. Gurs Closed
   - As France or Free France, dismantle Vichy camp legacy and prevent a tribunal severity spike.

Each achievement needs a completed 64x64 icon, grey and not-eligible variants if the achievement system requires them, tracking flags, disqualifiers, localisation, docs, and spreadsheet alignment.
