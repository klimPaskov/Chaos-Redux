# Super-Event Prompt: System Camp Repression Rework

Use this only if the implementation confirms a super-event is warranted.

Research quote, cultural remark, audio, and image direction separately. Do not invent quotes or final button text.

Candidate roles:

1. Severe Global Discovery
   - Trigger: first discovery of a severe radicalized or contaminated site with high evidence depth.
   - Tone: evidence becoming undeniable after liberation, focused on survivors and records, not generic apocalypse.
   - Image: period documentary discovery scene or symbolic archive of liberated evidence.
   - Audio: sober, documentary, and historically grounded.

2. Angel of Death Directorate Revolt
   - Trigger: existing Mengele Directorate coup or emergency laboratory revolt.
   - Tone: laboratory command breaks from Berlin and becomes an independent military threat.
   - Image: generated symbolic biomedical command hall or facility revolt scene.
   - Audio: cold, martial, scientific-horror tone.

3. Soviet Famine Catastrophe
   - Trigger: famine pressure reaches critical value during high paranoia, war, or Union Crisis.
   - Tone: state extraction and administrative fear turning into mass hunger and collapse.
   - Image: period documentary rural crisis or symbolic empty granary.
   - Audio: mournful, heavy, not triumphant.

4. Pingfang Exposure
   - Trigger: Allied or Soviet discovery of Japan's major biowarfare experiment network.
   - Tone: occupied-territory evidence and medical atrocity exposure.
   - Image: sourced or generated period evidence scene without operational biological-attack detail.
   - Audio: severe, investigative, tribunal-facing.

5. Colonial Reckoning
   - Trigger: a colonial labor network is exposed after major war, decolonization, or capital loss.
   - Tone: imperial extraction exposed through survivors, local records, and foreign pressure.
   - Image: colonial infrastructure, labor camp aftermath, or tribunal evidence.
   - Audio: somber and political.

All super-events must use settings-aware playback, unique final audio unless reuse is explicitly approved, full localisation, image, audio docs, and music track list updates.
