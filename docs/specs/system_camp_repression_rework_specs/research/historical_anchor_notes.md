# Historical Anchor Notes

These notes give implementation agents concrete historical anchors for the country packages. They are design anchors, not player-facing text and not final localisation.

## Japan and Shiro Ishii

The Japan package should use Unit 731 and Shiro Ishii as the historical anchor for the Kwantung Army biowarfare and experimentation route. The design should not add operational recipes for biological attacks. The gameplay shape is a risk-heavy research and atrocity-evidence system: research speed, special project gates, accident risk, outbreak risk, evidence depth, tribunal severity, and foreign reaction.

The U.S. Naval History and Heritage Command documents a late-war Japanese biological attack plan known as Operation Cherry Blossoms at Night. Use this only as a high-chaos historical anchor for a cancelled dossier, discovery event, or desperate doomsday liability branch. Do not turn it into an efficient weapon recipe.

Suggested Japan-specific project working labels, not final localisation:

- Pingfang Records Office: unlocks Japan-specific biowarfare research modifiers and raises evidence depth.
- Kwantung Medical Intelligence: increases special-project progress but raises outbreak accident risk and Shiro Ishii influence.
- Occupation Test Ledger: connects controlled Chinese or Manchurian state pools to experiment-site monthly Deaths and discovery severity.
- Epidemic Mapping Bureau: improves containment and countermeasure research while exposing the player to documentary evidence pressure.
- Cherry Blossom Dossier: rare high-chaos doomsday liability project that consumes stockpiles and creates uncontrollable contamination pressure through existing Chaos Redux biowarfare hooks.

## United States

The U.S. package should use Executive Order 9066 and Japanese American incarceration as the historical anchor for a democratic emergency detention route. The United States should not gain a strong economic positive from this. The design should mostly create legitimacy damage, democratic-support loss, court pressure, state-population disruption, war-support friction, and postwar redress or reckoning content. Any short-term gain should be administrative security or counter-espionage pressure, not forced-labor economic growth.

## United Kingdom and British Raj

The British Empire package should use wartime civilian internment in the Raj and imperial detention networks as the anchor. The design should connect British management to Raj autonomy pressure, Indian manpower and colonial labor extraction, dominion control, and independence resistance. The U.K. should receive limited imperial administration and manpower pressure while India receives a visible colonial extraction idea, autonomy growth pressure, and rising unrest if the system is expanded.

## France and Vichy North Africa

The France and Vichy package should use Gurs, Vichy labor camps, and North African labor-camp networks as anchors. Vichy intensification should strengthen collaborationist coercion while adding evidence, colonial unrest, resistance, and tribunal severity. Free France and democratic France should receive dismantle, inspection, and refugee-aid routes rather than an expansion loop.

## Italy

The Italy package should use Italian colonial camps in Libya and East Africa as anchors. The route should be colonial repression and logistical coercion, not a copy of Germany's genocide chain. It should give temporary colonial supply and infrastructure pressure while causing population loss, desert attrition, resistance growth, evidence depth, and postwar colonial collapse pressure.

## Belgium and Congo

The Belgium package should use forced labor and concessionary resource extraction in Congo as the anchor. This is an extraction and colonial-control system. It should connect rubber, mineral, and infrastructure pressure to Congo population loss, colonial unrest, international exposure, and postwar decolonization pressure. It should not become an extermination-camp route by default.

## Soviet Union and famine

The Soviet package should use the Gulag system, Stalinist paranoia, deportations, forced labor, and famine pressure as anchors. The Holodomor and wider Soviet famine are design anchors for a failed-management famine route. The system should tie high paranoia, grain extraction, camp overreach, purges, and republic fear into famine, population loss, old-movement grievance, Union Crisis dynamics, and collapse severity.
