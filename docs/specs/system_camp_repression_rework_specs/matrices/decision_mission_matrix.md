| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expand labor network | generic, Germany, Soviet, colonies | activate a valid state pool | equipment, trains, manpower, stability | labor output and control | deaths, evidence, resistance |

| Labor to construction | Germany, Soviet, U.K., Italy | redirect forced labor | trains, civilian-factory burden | construction speed or infrastructure | deaths, overreach |

| Evidence destruction | all active users | destroy records before retreat | manpower, equipment, time, nearby enemy | lower some evidence | extra deaths, failure worsens discovery |
| Inspection | democracies, reform routes | open sites to review | stability, hardliner anger | lower tribunal severity | loses labor output |
| Dismantlement | all users | close network stages | political cost, stability shock | stops deaths and reduces evidence growth | loss of labor, backlash |
