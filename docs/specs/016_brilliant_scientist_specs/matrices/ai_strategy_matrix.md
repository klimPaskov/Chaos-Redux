# AI strategy matrix for Brilliant Scientist

All labels are working labels and not final localisation.

## Host AI

| Host situation | Preferred posture | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stable democracy, not at major war | Public recruitment, peer review, university consortium | Early sealed projects and legal exemptions |
| Authoritarian major at war | Military lab, security, weapons, rockets | Public openness if rivals are nearby |
| Small country with low industry | Public institute and limited projects | Expensive sealed city or final device support |
| High intelligence capability | Screening, counterintelligence, controlled observers | Blind trust if Strangeness rises |
| Low stability | Avoid harsh arrest unless leverage is high | Actions that reduce stability further |
| High chaos | Stronger projects and more risk tolerance | Sending Kruger away |
| Near capitulation | Desperate military projects or surrender authority if authoritarian | Long public projects with no immediate payoff |

AI always accepts Kruger at baseline. AI should not choose send-away. AI should not try arrest if Laboratory Autonomy is high and Government Leverage is low unless desperate or scripted as a high-chaos mistake.

## Foreign AI

| Actor | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Major rival | Offers asylum, theft, rival institute, sabotage if relations are bad |
| Major ally | Requests observers and shared research, helps security if trusted |
| Neighbor | Watches borders, joins containment if Kruger secedes |
| Ideological rival | Uses propaganda, leaks, and theft |
| Desperate minor | Buys prototype fragments or seeks Kruger aid |
| Former host after defection | Tries retrieval or containment depending on strength |

Foreign AI should evaluate strength, relations, distance, ideology, intelligence capability, war state, and risk of retaliation.

## Kruger sovereign AI

| State | Priority |
| --- | --- |
| New secession | Defend lab state, defeat host, secure supply |
| Clone-heavy arsenal | Mobilize clones and overwhelm host quickly |
| Robot-heavy arsenal | Build industry, mechanized or robot armies, defensive production |
| Specimen-heavy arsenal | Shock attacks and fear weapons, but protect supply |
| Xenotech-heavy arsenal | Elite breakthroughs and target alien sites |
| Temporal-heavy arsenal | Use raids, redeployment, and prediction branches |
| Nuclear-heavy arsenal | Protect reactors and pursue final device once safe |
| Losing war with final device progress | Accelerate arming and consider terminal branch |
| Winning war | Expand to research centers and major capitals |

Kruger AI should be dangerous, but it should not ignore supply, capital defense, or host war goals. The final device route should be rare before Evolution IV and strongly weighted when the required projects exist and Kruger is either winning dramatically or facing defeat after major progress.

## AI safeguards

- AI should not open project branches whose needed project memory does not exist unless the branch itself creates that project later.
- AI should not form or recognize Kruger if the country is already in a terminal world-end state.
- AI should not pursue foreign theft against a much stronger host without intelligence capability or a sponsor.
- AI should not use final device sabotage missions if it cannot reach the target or lacks intelligence or air access.
- AI should clear obsolete decisions after Kruger dies, defects, secedes, or is contained.
