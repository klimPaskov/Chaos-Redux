# 016 Brilliant Scientist spec, part 6: Kruger State focus tree

## Binding reconciliation, 2026-07-14

The broad Bestiary lane splits into separate paleogenetic and xenobiological openers, facilities, production decisions, units, supply burdens, failures, counters, and capstones. It converges only through Synthesis. The temporal lane uses debt, synchronization capacity, anchors, authentication, bounded recovery targets, stabilization, and persistent scars.

Laboratory World is the conquest, submission, integration, and global-administration ending. Strategic Singularity is the vulnerable multi-year component race and denied-victory ending. Fail-deadly or deliberate global-use doctrine blocks Laboratory World while armed. Laboratory World consolidation requires disarmament and verified nonterminal control. Singularity firing permanently prevents Laboratory World; Laboratory World firing cancels singularity construction, arming, and activation.

## Tree purpose

The Kruger State needs a large bespoke focus tree because it is expected to survive, fight, develop several project systems, and possibly become a global threat. The tree should feel like a state built from inherited experiments rather than a normal country with scientific names pasted over generic rewards.

Target scope:

- Roughly 85 to 115 focuses when fully implemented.
- Final count is owned by the implementation agent.
- Path-level architecture is mandatory.
- Exact coordinates and final focus names are implementation work.
- Every major branch needs several focus groups, route interaction, a mechanical unlock, AI behavior, and a clear payoff.

## Tree architecture

### Opening trunk

The first focuses answer four urgent questions.

1. Can the laboratory territory survive the split?
2. Who commands the mixed project forces?
3. Which inherited portfolio is reliable enough to build around?
4. Does the state seek coexistence, recognition, submission, or conquest?

Opening focus groups:

- Secure the primary laboratory.
- Audit the inherited portfolio.
- Stabilize power and supply.
- Appoint an emergency command structure.
- Recover missing assistants and archives.
- Choose a governing doctrine.

The opening should branch based on actual formation type.

- Peaceful charter receives diplomacy, administration, and shared-research options.
- Violent rebellion receives defense, emergency mobilization, and archive-seizure options.
- Partial enclave receives survival and territorial-link options.
- Host takeover bypasses territorial-secession focuses and starts with institutional consolidation.

Branches must interact. The political route changes how economy, army, diplomacy, and project branches work.

## Opening survival branch

### Secure the laboratory perimeter

Role:

- Route opener for violent or vulnerable formations.
- Strengthen defense in actual facility states.
- Unlock named perimeter missions.
- Improve government-control status.

Reward direction:

- Fortification, anti-air, local supply, guard organization, and emergency decisions.
- No generic country-wide modifier as the only reward.

### Inventory the project vaults

Role:

- Read inherited project stages.
- Reveal which project branches are available.
- Recover missing prototypes or mark them held by the former host.

Reward direction:

- Dynamic project access.
- Targeted archive-recovery decisions.
- AI route weighting.

### Keep the lights on

Role:

- Solve immediate power and industrial collapse.

Reward direction:

- Repair infrastructure, add local power or industry support, reopen one production line, unlock emergency fuel and material decisions.

### Form the provisional command

Role:

- Unify guards, defectors, clones, robots, and handlers under a temporary command.

Choice direction:

- Human staff command.
- Kruger direct command.
- Machine coordination.
- Project-army council.

This choice influences later political and military routes.

### Recover the missing staff

Role:

- Decide whether to offer amnesty, conduct extractions, clone expertise, or rely on machines.

Reward direction:

- Assistant capacity, intelligence operations, foreign opinion, and Scientific Exodus lifecycle.

### Define the state's first purpose

Route lock between broad strategic directions:

- Secure scientific sovereignty.
- Build an open scientific commonwealth.
- Prepare a project empire.
- Pursue the singular Kruger objective.

This is a political direction, not an immediate final mutual exclusion with every support branch.

## Government and human-status branch

### Human technocracy route

Narrative role:

Kruger accepts that ordinary people, assistants, and institutions must govern daily life even if he retains final scientific authority.

Focus groups:

- Provisional civil service.
- Assistant assembly.
- Scientific law and project review.
- Citizenship for laboratory workers.
- Courts for project crimes.
- Limits on personal continuity.
- Public research charter.

Mechanical role:

- Strong stability and administration.
- Lower project accident and revolt risk.
- Better foreign recognition.
- Slower extreme Weaponization.
- Greater ability to retain human occupied territory.
- Public-science and peaceful-faction options.

Leadership possibilities:

- Kruger remains head of state with a human cabinet.
- A council becomes head of government while Kruger holds a scientific office.
- A rare settlement can reduce his personal rule without ending the country.

Failure state:

If Kruger refuses institutional limits while his personal network remains strong, a palace or laboratory crisis can reopen machine, clone, or direct-rule branches.

### Replicated sovereignty route

Narrative role:

Clones become the demographic and political foundation of the state.

Early questions:

- Are clones citizens or instruments?
- Do they receive individual names and careers?
- Can a clone refuse Kruger?
- Is one Kruger body legally superior to another?

Focus groups:

- Growth-facility stabilization.
- Clone legal status.
- Replicated officer corps.
- Memory and education transfer.
- Biological settlement.
- Clone family or cohort structure.
- The succession problem.

Mechanical role:

- Manpower generation through bounded facilities.
- Clone advisors and commanders.
- Population integration decisions.
- Reduced ordinary recruitment pressure.
- High equipment, food, and medical burden.
- Risk of clone autonomy or civil conflict.

Route variants:

- Obedient cohort state.
- Clone citizen republic under Kruger.
- Kruger-copy hierarchy.
- Mixed human-clone commonwealth.

Late payoff:

A mature replicated population that can integrate territory and sustain a large army without becoming an infinite free manpower source.

Failure state:

Growth collapse, identity revolt, rival Kruger copies, or a clone emancipation crisis.

### Machine ascendancy route

Narrative role:

Administration and military command move into an autonomous network.

Focus groups:

- Restore command nodes.
- Automate accounting and logistics.
- Machine legal personhood or instrument status.
- Replace human ministries.
- Distributed Kruger memory.
- Network self-defense.
- Machine continuity beyond biological death.

Mechanical role:

- Lower human administrative burden.
- Strong production, logistics, and command.
- Robot unit and automated defense growth.
- High power and resource dependence.
- Electronic disruption vulnerability.
- Nonhuman classification after the route actually becomes machine-majority.

Route variants:

- Kruger controls the network.
- The network models Kruger but develops an independent identity.
- Human-machine partnership.
- Fully automated machine state.

Late payoff:

A machine government that can run a global facility network and support project warfare.

Failure state:

Network schism, command-loop paralysis, machine rejection of biological Kruger, or foreign penetration.

### Temporal Continuum route

Narrative role:

Several Kruger versions and future records compete over one state.

Focus groups:

- Establish one legal timeline.
- Authenticate orders.
- Create temporal archives.
- Assign authority among Kruger variants.
- Use future information.
- Control paradox.
- Form the Continuum government.

Mechanical role:

- Forecasting, recovery, limited repeat actions, and temporal units.
- Unique leader and advisor variants.
- Strong intelligence and planning.
- Paradox pressure, instability, and contradictory AI priorities.

Route variants:

- One Prime Kruger dominates.
- A council of temporal selves.
- The earliest Kruger has legal priority.
- The future version directs conquest.

Late payoff:

A state able to recover selected losses and anticipate threats, with hard caps and escalating paradox.

Failure state:

Timeline civil war, duplicate commands, erased project stages, or a hostile future Kruger.

### Synthesis route

Narrative role:

No single population or command system dominates. Human assistants, clones, machines, engineered organisms, and Kruger variants are combined into one designed state.

Unlock condition:

- Several project families at meaningful stages.
- No exclusive political route completed.
- Strong cross-project capacity.

Mechanical role:

- Flexible combined-arms bonuses.
- Access to mixed units and institutions.
- High maintenance and internal-cohesion burden.
- Strong late-game adaptability.

Late payoff:

A deliberately absurd high-chaos polity that uses several project systems without receiving the maximum capstone of every exclusive route.

Failure state:

Project caste conflict, resource collapse, or loss of central control.

## Laboratory economy branch

### Restore the facility network

- Repair actual damaged laboratories.
- Reconnect rail, power, and communications.
- Reopen one inherited project line.

### Divide theory from production

- Build research and prototype sites in separate states.
- Reduce one accident type.
- Increase transport and espionage burden.

### Automated production route

Requires robotics or computation.

- Autonomous machine tools.
- Frame factories.
- Predictive logistics.
- Self-repairing production.

Payoff:

Strong military production and robot replacement, with power and resource pressure.

### Replicative labor route

Requires cloning or biological synthesis.

- Specialized worker cohorts.
- Rapid skill transfer.
- Biological construction teams.

Payoff:

Fast facility growth and manpower support, with ethical and supply costs.

### Portal logistics route

Requires teleportation.

- Linked depots.
- Emergency transit.
- Distributed facilities.

Payoff:

Supply resilience and global reach, with energy and terminal vulnerability.

### Foreign scientific concession route

Requires diplomacy.

- Sell limited access.
- Lease laboratory zones.
- Trade prototypes for resources.

Payoff:

Resources and recognition, with espionage and sovereignty risk.

### Closed laboratory autarky route

- Seize strategic resources.
- Build synthetic substitutes.
- Restrict foreign access.

Payoff:

Independence and security, with slower diplomatic expansion and higher occupation burden.

### Economy capstone groups

Possible capstones:

- Global Laboratory Network.
- Autonomous Industrial Ecology.
- Replicated Production Society.
- Portal-Linked Arsenal.

Each capstone must unlock a continuing decision system or production behavior.

## Conventional security branch

### Unite the guards

- Convert temporary laboratory guards into a coherent force.
- Choose officer source.
- Remove Fragmented Command in stages.

### Defector integration

- Offer rank and protection to former host officers.
- Create ordinary divisions and commanders.
- Risk infiltration.

### Engineer corps

- Repair facilities, build defenses, recover prototypes, and support special units.

### Counterintelligence office

- Protect laboratories.
- Run foreign target decisions.
- Detect assassination and sabotage.
- Create false project trails.

### Air and missile shield

Requires electronics and rocketry.

- Defend facility states.
- Intercept strategic raids.
- Protect singularity sites.

### Human general staff route

- Reliable conventional planning.
- Better occupation and integration.
- Limits Kruger direct command.

### Direct Kruger command route

- Strong project coordination and offensive planning.
- High personal-rule and succession risk.

### Machine command route

- Strong robot and mixed-force control.
- Vulnerable to network disruption.

### Project-army council route

- Lets clone, machine, bestiary, portal, and conventional commanders coordinate.
- Supports synthesis politics.

Conventional security capstone:

A durable ordinary army that prevents the country from collapsing when one project family fails.

## Machine cognition and robot corps branch

Availability and depth scale with inherited robotics stage.

Focus groups:

- Recover prototype frames.
- Standardize power and parts.
- Choose human crews or full autonomy.
- Build robot factories.
- Create repair battalions.
- Develop heavy assault frames.
- Establish machine air defense.
- Link command nodes.
- Grant or deny machine personhood.
- Create self-designing production.

Key decisions unlocked:

- Produce a bounded robot wave.
- Repair damaged frames.
- Convert captured factories.
- Deploy autonomous defense to a facility state.
- Isolate a compromised node.

Capstone choices:

- Army of Machines.
- Human-Guided Mechanical Corps.
- Distributed Kruger Network.

Do not make all capstones compatible. They represent different political and command identities.

## Replicative biology and clone corps branch

Availability and depth scale with cloning stage.

Focus groups:

- Secure growth chambers.
- Stabilize genetic lines.
- Decide clone legal status.
- Create education and memory programs.
- Build clone officer corps.
- Expand medical support.
- Create settlement cohorts.
- Develop specialized military lines.
- Address Kruger duplication.

Key decisions unlocked:

- Begin a growth cycle.
- Mature a selected unit family.
- Grant citizenship to a cohort.
- Integrate clones into a captured state.
- Investigate identity instability.
- Retire or free an obsolete cohort.

Capstone choices:

- Replicated Nation.
- Obedient Endless Army, powerful but ethically and politically dangerous.
- Many Krugers.
- Human-Clone Commonwealth.

The `Endless Army` fantasy should mean a powerful renewable pathway constrained by real production, not an uncapped weekly unit spawn.

## Paleogenetics and xenobiological synthesis branches

The branches share only a visual lane before splitting. Paleogenetics scales from restored terrestrial species and its handler and reserve economy. Xenobiological synthesis scales from designed organisms, medical fabrication, containment, and the selected control method.

Focus groups:

- Recover breeding stock.
- Build feed and containment systems.
- Train handlers.
- Choose restored species or designed organisms.
- Develop terrain-specific units.
- Create biological armor and control harnesses.
- Build ecological weapons or reject release.
- Integrate machine control if robotics exists.
- Integrate cloning if replicative biology exists.

Key decisions unlocked:

- Breed one bounded unit batch.
- Relocate a breeding facility.
- Hunt an escaped specimen.
- Deploy a creature to a named front.
- Convert captured agricultural capacity.
- Establish a protected ecosystem or war preserve.

Capstone choices:

- Dinosaur Host.
- Engineered Legion.
- Controlled Bestiary.
- Unleashed War Ecology.

Failure states:

- Feed collapse.
- Handler revolt.
- Ecological escape.
- Creatures rejecting control.

## Quantum transit and temporal branch

### Portal route

Focus groups:

- Repair terminal geometry.
- Stabilize power.
- Link core facilities.
- Build military transit teams.
- Open foreign infiltration routes.
- Develop strategic delivery.
- Create a global portal network.

Decisions:

- Link two controlled facilities.
- Move a limited unit or stockpile.
- Raid a foreign laboratory.
- Close a compromised terminal.
- Rescue an isolated enclave.

### Temporal route

Focus groups:

- Authenticate the first time signal.
- Establish paradox accounting.
- Recover a lost prototype.
- Invite or reject future Kruger.
- Create temporal guards.
- Manipulate one campaign deadline.
- Build a controlled time machine.

Decisions:

- Receive future intelligence.
- Recover one destroyed project component.
- Restore a bounded unit.
- Send a warning to an earlier stage.
- Close a paradox event.

Cross-route capstone:

A Continuum Network can combine portals and temporal displacement. It grants exceptional reach and recovery, but sharply raises paradox, energy burden, and world threat.

## Exotic energy and strategic weapons branch

Focus groups:

- Secure reactors and exotic materials.
- Harden project sites.
- Develop field weapons.
- Create long-range delivery.
- Build strategic deterrent.
- Choose public demonstration, hidden reserve, or active coercion.
- Begin singularity theory.
- Build prototype core.
- Distribute fail-deadly controls.
- Arm the final device.

This branch must take years when pursued to the terminal weapon. It should be vulnerable to foreign raids, assistant defection, resource denial, and rapid conquest.

Branch locks:

- World-end and Evolution IV settings.
- Required project synergies.
- Sovereign Kruger route.
- Sufficient facility network.

Capstone directions:

- Deterrent without immediate use.
- Strategic conquest weapon.
- Capitulation failsafe.
- Temporal escape alternative, which may disable the failsafe and create another ending.

## Diplomacy and intelligence branch

### Recognition route

- Invite observers.
- Offer limited project access.
- Seek bilateral recognition.
- Join or create a scientific compact.
- Negotiate borders with the former host.

### Patronage route

- Trade technology for resources or protection.
- Balance several major powers.
- Prevent one patron from controlling the state.

### Scientific recruitment route

- Offer asylum and laboratories.
- Recruit foreign assistants.
- Extract targeted scientists.
- Recover project knowledge from other countries.

### Espionage route

- Steal technologies.
- Sabotage containment coalitions.
- Replace or infiltrate officials with clones or machines if projects permit.
- Use portals or temporal information for operations.

### Submission network route

- Offer countries advanced technology in exchange for facilities, faction membership, or political submission.
- Create project client states.
- Establish inspection and enforcement decisions.

### Isolation route

- Close borders.
- Hide facilities.
- Develop autarky.
- Reduce foreign actions while losing access to staff and resources.

Diplomacy capstones:

- Scientific Commonwealth.
- Kruger Protection Bloc.
- Network of Submission.
- Fortress Laboratory State.

## Expansion and integration branch

### Former host settlement

Possible routes:

- Negotiate permanent borders.
- Demand all laboratory states.
- Reconcile through shared science.
- Defeat and integrate the host.
- Puppet the host as an administrative shell.

### Facility seizure doctrine

- Gain target decisions for foreign laboratories, reactors, rocket sites, and special-project facilities.
- Capture project progress and staff.
- Create diplomatic consequences and world threat.

### Resource corridors

- Target rail hubs, ports, power sites, rare-resource regions, and industrial belts needed by the portfolio.
- Use state-specific missions and postwar integration.

### Scientific protectorates

- Offer technology for subject status.
- Let local government remain while Kruger controls research and strategic production.

### Direct annexation route

- Use force to seize territory.
- High resistance, condemnation, and administrative burden.
- Requires integration decisions and a population policy.

### World conquest route

Evolution IV only.

Focus groups:

- Declare ordinary sovereignty obsolete.
- Form the project armies into a global command.
- Issue submission terms.
- Seize global scientific centers.
- Break containment coalitions.
- Establish continental laboratory networks.
- Transform subjects into project states.
- Complete global strategic delivery.
- Prepare final coercion or terminal device.

World-conquest focuses should unlock ongoing decisions, claims, war goals, submission events, facility capture, and faction rules. They should not be a straight sequence of attack bonuses.

## Postwar integration systems

Kruger conquest creates varied populations and infrastructure.

Integration options:

- Human civil administration.
- Clone settlement.
- Machine administration.
- Scientific protectorate.
- Laboratory-zone extraction.
- Open scientific citizenship.
- Forced project labor, which creates severe resistance, condemnation, and moral consequences.

Cores should be staged. Large foreign regions require compliance, infrastructure, political integration, or route-specific population transformation.

## Branch convergence

Examples of intended convergence:

- Human technocracy plus public diplomacy creates a scientific commonwealth.
- Machine politics plus automated economy and robot corps creates machine ascendancy.
- Clone politics plus replicative economy and clone corps creates replicated sovereignty.
- Temporal politics plus quantum branch creates the Continuum.
- Synthesis politics can combine several project military branches at reduced capstone strength.
- World conquest can follow any dominant project route, but its army and submission system should reflect that route.
- Strategic singularity requires energy branch plus cross-project prerequisites, not one political route only.

## Route locks

Hard mutual exclusions should be reserved for real identity conflicts.

Likely exclusive families:

- Human constitutional settlement versus absolute Kruger personal rule.
- Clone citizenship versus clones as property.
- Machine partnership versus full machine replacement.
- One Prime Kruger versus temporal council.
- Open scientific commonwealth versus global submission doctrine.
- Deterrent doctrine versus automatic capitulation failsafe, unless later research creates a deliberate override with severe cost.

Support branches such as conventional security, economy, facility restoration, and limited diplomacy should remain broadly compatible.

## Focus duration and pacing

### Early phase

- Shorter emergency focuses.
- Survival, supply, command, and first administration.
- No large world-conquest rewards.

### Middle phase

- Standard-duration institutional and project focuses.
- Branch choices, unit systems, diplomacy, and resource solutions.

### Late phase

- Longer strategic focuses.
- Global network, project capstones, conquest, singularity, and final political identities.

The tree should not let a one-state enclave arm the terminal device through a short rush while still fighting its first host war.

## Focus reward standards

Rewards should include:

- State-specific facilities and repairs.
- Project decisions and missions.
- Unit templates and bounded force-generation systems.
- Advisors, commanders, assistants, and institutional leaders.
- Technologies and special-project access.
- Laws and political identity.
- Claims, war goals, protectorates, submission events, and integration systems.
- Faction mechanics.
- Project countermeasures.
- Cosmetic names, flags, portraits, and super-event thresholds.
- Strategic abilities tied to actual project stages.

Avoid:

- Repeated political power.
- Tiny stability and war-support rewards.
- One new national spirit per focus.
- Free repeated divisions.
- Generic factory rewards detached from states and project needs.

## Focus filters

The implementation should define a clear filter taxonomy, such as:

- State survival.
- Government and citizenship.
- Laboratory economy.
- Conventional military.
- Machine projects.
- Replicative biology.
- Bestiary.
- Quantum and temporal.
- Exotic energy.
- Diplomacy and intelligence.
- Expansion and integration.
- World conquest and terminal branch.

Filter icons should be distinct and event-scoped where required.

## AI route selection

AI route choice uses inherited portfolio and formation state.

| Portfolio and state | Preferred route |
| --- | --- |
| Strong cloning, limited industry, high population pressure | Replicated sovereignty and clone corps |
| Strong robotics, advanced materials, low manpower | Machine ascendancy and automated economy |
| Paleogenetics or monster Deployment | Bestiary command and biological military |
| Teleportation with several facility sites | Portal logistics and global facility seizure |
| Temporal Deployment | Continuum politics and temporal intelligence |
| Alien arms and high energy | Exotic energy, deterrent, or conquest |
| Public charter, low Grievance, foreign recognition | Human technocracy and scientific commonwealth |
| Violent split, high Grievance, strong project army | Former-host war and submission doctrine |
| Weak enclave | Survival, conventional security, recognition, and resource access |
| Evolution IV, large state, several Weaponized projects | World conquest and singularity research |

AI should avoid a project branch when it lacks the inherited stage, facilities, or resource path needed to use it.

## Failure and crisis branches

The tree should contain reactive content for:

- Former host retaking the primary laboratory.
- Power collapse.
- Clone revolt.
- Machine-network split.
- Bestiary escape.
- Portal network compromise.
- Temporal paradox crisis.
- Foreign containment coalition.
- Loss of strategic materials.
- Kruger death or contradictory continuity.
- Singularity site sabotage.

A crisis branch should help the country respond, change route, or fail in a memorable way. It should not be a set of passive penalty-removal focuses.

## End states

### Stable scientific state

Kruger State survives as a regional, research-focused country with a coherent political identity.

### Scientific commonwealth

The country builds a recognized network that spreads research without direct world conquest.

### Project empire

The state uses one dominant project family to conquer and transform neighbors.

### Global submission order

Countries enter a Kruger-led bloc and surrender scientific sovereignty.

### Laboratory world

Kruger controls an overwhelming share of the world, all major opposition is defeated, and the world-end threshold is met. The global order becomes a terminal Kruger system.

### Strategic singularity

The final device fires after its full research and arming chain. It pushes chaos through the normal world-end threshold and triggers the Fallout terminal scenario.

### Defeated threat

The country is destroyed or dismantled after becoming a major world threat. An aftermath package governs laboratories, survivors, technology, and international rules.
