# Event 16 Brilliant Scientist, host-country overlay branch expansion

All focus and branch labels are working labels, not final localisation. This section expands the host-country overlay branch from the core spec.

## Host overlay purpose

The host overlay is not a full replacement tree for every country. It is an event branch or additive focus package for the country that recruited Kruger. The branch should work for a major power, a minor country, a democratic state, or an authoritarian state without erasing that country’s normal identity.

The overlay should do three things:

- let the host steer Kruger through public science, military science, security, restraint, sealed projects, or confrontation
- connect focuses to the decision category or scripted GUI instead of giving isolated bonuses
- preserve route memory so later secession, containment, defection, or aftermath can read what the host actually did

## Entry and reveal pacing

The host should not see the whole overlay immediately. Reveal it in layers.

| Phase | Visible branch groups | Unlock direction | Hidden content handling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline | intake, public or secret setup, first project focus | event fired and Kruger accepted | no final confrontation branch visible |
| Evolution I | public fame, universities, first policy route | fame or first institute completed | only broad danger hints visible |
| Evolution II | security, foreign pressure, anti-theft, staff protection | foreign attention or security incident | no rebel army details shown |
| Evolution III | restraint, sealed projects, emergency readiness | strange project incident or dangerous project approval | final confrontation appears as public crisis tools |
| Evolution IV | confrontation, surrender authority, siege, treaty, secession response | Kruger is rogue, escaped, or sovereign | terminal details stay in super-event and crisis branch |

## Opening anchor groups

| Anchor group | Purpose | Decision hooks | Reward families | AI notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interview the prodigy | establishes Kruger as a state actor | sets posture and first focus selector | advisor access, first research momentum | AI always accepts and chooses posture by ideology and war |
| Choose the sponsor | public university, military office, or intelligence screen | opens public, secret, or screening decisions | route memory and starting idea version | AI democracies prefer public, wartime authoritarians prefer secret |
| Assign the first field | selects civil, military, physics, biology, machine, or alien-leaning field | shows only matching project cards | field-specific research speed and project memory seed | AI uses war and industrial need |

## Public branch

The public branch keeps Kruger inside universities and visible institutions. It is strong for safe research and weaker for emergency weapons.

| Anchor group | Purpose | Rewards | Locks and tradeoffs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| University consortium | connects universities and public labs | Research Momentum, public institute memory, civilian research bonuses | raises Public Fame and Foreign Heat |
| National lectures circuit | makes Kruger famous | Public Fame, stability if country is calm, student volunteers | theft risk and assassination risk rise |
| Civil medicine program | turns biology toward recovery and hospitals | medicine memory, manpower recovery, hospital or support-equipment bonuses | blocks or delays bioweapon routes unless later overridden |
| Patent and industry exchange | spreads safe industry gains | civilian factories, production efficiency, industrial decisions | rivals can copy fragments |
| International peer review | raises legitimacy and leverage | Government Leverage, lower Strangeness growth, foreign trust | slows dangerous projects |
| Public science mobilization | capstone | strong but safer research package, achievement hooks | cannot coexist with the most extreme sealed-city route |

Public branch localisation should show enthusiasm, crowds, lectures, university competition, and practical inventions. It should avoid saying the path is safe in absolute terms.

## Military branch

The military branch uses Kruger as a strategic asset. It should be tempting during war and dangerous during peace.

| Anchor group | Purpose | Rewards | Locks and tradeoffs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| War research office | puts Kruger under uniformed command | military lab memory, special-project output, army XP hooks | raises Autonomy and secrecy |
| Prototype weapons range | unlocks field tests | military project decisions, equipment, doctrine research | accident and condemnation hooks |
| Rocket and air program | supports delivery systems | rocketry memory, air research, airbase or radar work | foreign observation and final-device prerequisites |
| Battlefield medicine board | combines war and medicine | casualty recovery, hospitals, military medicine | can drift into clone prerequisites |
| Emergency arsenal privileges | grants exemptions under wartime pressure | strong research momentum and weapon speed | large Government Leverage loss |
| Directorate of war science | capstone | military-focused idea stage, elite advisor access | easier Kruger secession if autonomy is high |

The branch should use equipment, XP, factory time, and war pressure costs. It should not rely on political power alone.

## Security branch

The security branch is compatible with public or military paths. It should protect the program and preserve state authority.

| Anchor group | Purpose | Decision hooks | Rewards | Failure state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vet the staff | reduces leaks and strange incidents | screen staff decision | Security Integrity, lower theft | staff anger if overused |
| Loyal guard detail | puts guards under the government, not Kruger | expand guard decision | Security, loyal divisions, arrest preparation | guard loyalty drift if Autonomy high |
| Counterintelligence bureau | fights foreign pressure | trace stolen notes, expose rivals | lower Foreign Heat, rival incidents | false arrests lower fame |
| Relocate families and key assistants | protects human leverage | staff protection mission | Leverage and security | kidnapping event if ignored |
| Facility evacuation drills | prepares for crisis | evacuation mission | lowers rebel asset conversion | costs trains, convoys, factories |
| Emergency chain of custody | capstone | containment readiness | makes arrest possible if other values support it | failure creates armed standoff |

The player should understand that security improves options later. It should not reveal exact arrest success thresholds.

## Restraint branch

The restraint branch creates slower but safer control. It should be a real path with good rewards, not a punishment path.

| Anchor group | Purpose | Rewards | Route effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Peer review charter | puts review between Kruger and the state | Government Leverage, lower Strangeness growth | slows project speed |
| Ethics tribunal | public accountability when concern rises | lowers Autonomy, improves public trust | risky if Public Fame is high and evidence is thin |
| Budget audit law | follows money and equipment | discovers hidden stockpiles, raises Leverage | can anger Kruger |
| Limited classification act | divides safe public work from sealed work | safer public branch, foreign trust | locks some sealed projects unless overridden |
| Destroy unsafe prototypes | removes one dangerous memory output from future rebellion strength | emergency decision upgrades | can trigger crisis if done late |
| Science under law | capstone | strong leverage, safer achievements, reduced revolt strength | blocks final-device host support |

This branch should let the host win by restraint. It should not be a flavour-only route.

## Sealed-project branch

The sealed-project branch unlocks the biggest gains and the worst future liabilities.

| Anchor group | Requires | Rewards | Hidden memory direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Restricted complex | secret or military route | special-project speed, lab state modifier | sealed lab memory |
| Private procurement network | legal exemptions or high war pressure | faster projects, reduced upfront costs | hidden stockpile memory |
| Autonomous workshop wing | automation or industry project | factory output, robot prerequisites | automation and machine memory |
| Biological annex | biology project | medicine, clone or specimen options | clone or specimen memory depending choice |
| Foreign material vault | alien, nuclear, or rare project memory | xenotech or nuclear acceleration | alien or reactor custody memory |
| Sealed city charter | high Autonomy and multiple projects | extreme research momentum and facility scaling | large rebel state foundation |

Sealed-project focuses should be costly in factories, equipment, security staff, stability, war support, and foreign pressure. The rewards can be absurd, but the state should visibly lose direct control.

## Final-confrontation branch

The final-confrontation branch opens only when Kruger is dangerous, rogue, or openly sovereign. It should contain several possible responses.

| Anchor group | Trigger direction | Outcome families | Route locks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cabinet emergency session | Kruger refuses ordinary limits or severe incident fires | choose confrontation posture | requires host still functional |
| Prepare the arrest order | enough Leverage and Security | possible containment without rebellion | weak if sealed city and private guards exist |
| Siege the laboratory | Kruger has armed facility but no independent state yet | local battle, state damage, project destruction | requires loyal divisions and supply |
| Offer a research exile | foreign pressure or low leverage | transfer, defection, or delayed crisis | blocked if Kruger already has sovereign army |
| Bargain for obedience | high Autonomy but high Momentum | keep Kruger with concessions | worsens future sovereignty risk |
| Surrender authority | desperate host or authoritarian collapse | Kruger becomes open ruler or state-within-state | opens sovereign route and super-event candidate |
| Burn the archives | post-containment or late crisis | remove dangerous future hooks | loses research benefits |
| Coalition containment call | Kruger sovereign or final-device race | invites foreign support | needs foreign relations or world-threat state |

The confrontation branch should allow partial success. A host may save staff while losing prototypes, kill Kruger but leave escaped specimens, or contain the man while foreign countries keep stolen notes.

## Host overlay AI

| Host AI type | Public | Military | Security | Restraint | Sealed projects | Confrontation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| stable democracy | high | low | medium | high | low | cautious if leverage high |
| wartime democracy | medium | medium | high | medium | low to medium | containment and coalition |
| authoritarian major | low | high | high | low | high | bargain or arrest if strong |
| authoritarian minor | low | medium | medium | low | medium if desperate | avoid arrest when weak |
| small neutral | high if affordable | low | medium | high | low | send to treaty or containment |
| high chaos AI | medium | high | medium | low | high | more likely to make severe mistakes |

AI should never send Kruger away at baseline. AI may transfer him only through crisis logic, asylum pressure, or defeat.
