# Event 16 Brilliant Scientist, mechanics and decision map

All labels are working labels and not final localisation.

## Mechanic shape

The host should receive a decision category or a custom mechanic panel called here the Kruger Institute. This is a working label. The panel should exist because the player needs to manage a living system, not because the event needs decoration.

The category should show:

- Current host.
- Kruger status: public scholar, military asset, protected celebrity, restricted detainee, rogue director, escaped, or sovereign leader.
- Research Momentum.
- Laboratory Autonomy.
- Security Integrity.
- Public Fame.
- Strangeness.
- Government Leverage.
- Active project focus.
- A compact summary of approved facility families.
- Warnings when a value is near a public threshold, without revealing hidden rebellion thresholds.

## Starting national spirits

The host should not receive a pile of unrelated ideas. Use a small staged set.

| Idea | Start or unlock | Role | Upgrade path | Failure path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kruger Research Program | Baseline acceptance | Main research acceleration and special-project access | Public institute, military directorate, or sovereign laboratory stage | Removed if Kruger defects, captured by rebels if he secedes |
| Scientific Fever | Evolution I | Public fame, university competition, research volunteers | National science mobilization or international consortium | Panic, spy attention, academic purges |
| Restricted Laboratory Complex | Secret route or Evolution II | Security, special-project throughput, secret weapon access | Hardened complex, sealed city, automated laboratory | Rebel fortress or foreign scandal |
| Anomalous Research Burden | Evolution III | Represents strange physics and safety costs | Stabilized containment if restrained | Reality breaches, monsters, time accidents, alien exposure |

## Advisor and special-project scientist plan

Kruger should be available as a special project scientist in every special project field. This should feel absurd and obviously beyond normal advisor balance. Implementation should still keep him attached to the host and remove or transfer him cleanly if he dies, defects, is imprisoned, or becomes leader of his own country.

Field behavior should vary by project family:

| Field | Kruger direction |
| --- | --- |
| Nuclear | Very strong speed and breakthrough output, heavy Strangeness and world-end hooks |
| Rockets | High speed, alien gun and final delivery system hooks |
| Electronics | Strong radar, computation, cryptography, targeting, and AI hooks |
| Industry | Strong construction, production efficiency, resource extraction, and laboratory scaling |
| Engineering | Special machinery, teleportation, containment facilities, and automation |
| Biology | Cloning, medicine, bioweapons, monster synthesis, and outbreak hooks |
| Military | Prototype weapons, special units, doctrine research, and logistics distortions |

## Decision families

The decision category should not become a political power store. Costs should use factories, equipment, XP, manpower, stability, war support, command capacity, industrial disruption, security staffing, labs, and time.

### Institutional control decisions

| Decision family | Visible purpose | Costs and requirements | Effects | Hidden memory |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Build public institute | Keep Kruger inside ordinary science | Civilian factories, time, stability, university route | Raises Research Momentum and Public Fame, lowers Strangeness growth | Public institution route |
| Create military laboratory | Improve secret military output | Military factories, command power, army XP, lower stability | Raises Security Integrity at first, raises Autonomy and weapon access | Military laboratory route |
| Establish peer review board | Slow unsafe work and build leverage | Political attention, civilian factories, lower immediate momentum | Raises Government Leverage, lowers Strangeness growth | Public restraint evidence |
| Grant legal exemptions | Let Kruger bypass ministries | Stability or war support cost, high fame or war pressure | Raises Momentum and Autonomy, unlocks risky projects | Autonomy concession |
| Audit laboratory accounts | Find private stockpiles and hidden projects | Intelligence agency or security integrity, time | Raises Leverage if successful, lowers Momentum if heavy-handed | Audit success or backlash |
| Move the laboratory | Hide or isolate the work | Trains, convoys if overseas, civilian factories, security | Improves protection and changes rebellion spawn logic | Sealed site chosen |

### Security and foreign-pressure decisions

| Decision family | Visible purpose | Costs and requirements | Effects | Failure or risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expand personal guard | Prevent kidnapping and assassination | Infantry equipment, support equipment, manpower | Raises Security Integrity, may raise Autonomy if guards answer to Kruger | Guard loyalty drift |
| Counterintelligence sweep | Catch foreign agents | Intelligence agency, command power, stability risk | Removes spy flags, can expose rivals | False arrests lower Public Fame |
| Screen laboratory staff | Reduce anomalies and leaks | Time, lower Momentum, security personnel | Lowers Strangeness and foreign theft risk | Staff anger if public fame high |
| Accept foreign observers | Trade openness for recognition | Diplomatic relations, open route | Raises Public Fame and lowers foreign hostility | Raises theft and defection risk |
| Deny foreign access | Keep the project national | Stability, opinion penalties | Lowers theft risk, raises rival institutes | Escalates sabotage chance |
| Relocate family and staff | Protect human attachments | Convoys, trains, civilian factories | Raises Security and Leverage | If ignored, kidnapping events can fire |

### Research focus decisions

Research focus should be a staged selector. The player should not see all project decisions at once. Early focus decisions unlock safe categories first. Risky categories appear through evolutions, project prerequisites, and prior concessions.

| Focus | Early effect | Later risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civil science | Industry, medicine, electronics, public legitimacy | Public fame makes foreign attention stronger |
| Military science | Weapons, rockets, doctrine, special projects | Autonomy and secret facilities grow faster |
| Theoretical physics | Nuclear, teleportation, time anomalies | Strangeness rises and world-end hooks appear |
| Biological systems | Medicine, cloning, bioweapons, monsters | Outbreaks, ethical scandals, nonhuman armies |
| Machine intelligence | Robotics, AI command, automation | Robot rebellion assets and control loss |
| Xenotechnology | Alien weapons and materials | Alien confirmation risk and diplomacy shocks |

### Emergency response decisions

Emergency decisions should appear only when values reach dangerous states.

| Emergency | Trigger direction | Player action | Outcome direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Missing staff inquiry | Staff disappear or memory gaps appear | Spend security and time to investigate | Can reveal cloning, monster, or alien evidence |
| Laboratory quarantine | Biologic or physical anomaly appears | Lock down facilities with troops and equipment | Can lower spread or create armed standoff |
| Scientific ethics tribunal | Public concern rises | Public hearing route | Lowers Autonomy if Leverage is high, may increase Fame |
| Arrest the director | High concern and enough Leverage | Attempt to imprison or kill Kruger | Succeeds only if Autonomy is low enough and project arsenal is weak |
| Evacuate the sealed city | Late strangeness or rebellion signs | Save staff and reduce rebel assets | Expensive and lowers Momentum |
| Destroy the prototypes | Extreme route | Remove a project family | Can stop a rebel army type, may trigger immediate revolt |

## Dynamic costs

Costs should scale with:

- Host industry.
- Host war state.
- Chaos tier.
- Public Fame.
- Laboratory Autonomy.
- Security Integrity.
- Project family danger.
- Whether the host is a major, minor, subject, at war, or nearly capitulated.
- Whether the host has intelligence agency capability.

A small country should pay a smaller absolute cost but a larger relative burden. A major country should be able to fund more, but the larger program should create more foreign attention and more future rebel assets.

## Project approval memory

Every approved project should write a hidden memory flag or variable in the host and on Kruger. These memories shape:

- Evolution chance.
- Rebellion strength.
- Rebel country starting units.
- Focus tree branches unlocked for Kruger if he secedes.
- World-end device prerequisites.
- Foreign intelligence events.
- Achievements.
- Asset and portrait progression.

The player should not see a raw list called rebel assets. The player should see public facility summaries such as cloned medical labor, automated foundries, specimen wing, prototype weapon range, temporal observatory, or sealed reactor.

## Initial event options and mechanical direction

| Option direction | Immediate direction | Long-term direction | AI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public recruitment | Research bonus, fame, public institute | Safer politics, more foreign attention | AI can choose if democratic or stable |
| Military recruitment | Research bonus, secret lab, military projects | Faster dangerous routes, better early security | AI prefers at war or authoritarian |
| Send away | Transfers Kruger to another eligible country | Original country can later react or regret | AI never chooses |
| Investigate first | Slower start, information gain | Better leverage and alien clues | AI rare, prefers high-intelligence states |

## Tooltip and localisation direction

Text should explain what the country is publicly doing. It should not tell the player that a decision adds rebel clone strength or moves toward world end. Visible tooltips can describe strain, public attention, security concerns, lost staff, unusual machines, and unreliable prototypes.

Cost text should be icon-first and short. Long requirements should use custom tooltips and named facilities or state groups.
