# 016 Brilliant Scientist spec, part 10: achievements and completion

## Binding reconciliation, 2026-07-14

Exactly seventeen achievements are required. `016_brilliant_scientist_public_method` and `016_brilliant_scientist_clean_break` remain separate: the former proves public governance, publication, and independent replication while Kruger remains active under a binding public compact; the latter proves a later safe end to Kruger's exclusive control through retirement, dismissal, exile, or settlement while preserving the institutional legacy. `not_from_here` requires locked extraterrestrial provenance and public proof. `yesterday_sent_help` requires a real crisis, bounded recovery, temporal debt, and completed stabilization. Laboratory World requires a verified nonterminal singularity state, not merely absence of firing.

## Achievement philosophy

Event 16 achievements should reward difficult campaign stories, portfolio mastery, safe institutional play, risky control, foreign competition, project-army use, defeat of a global threat, and terminal failure. The event firing or simple recruitment should never unlock an achievement by itself.

Working keys and labels below are not final localisation.

## Achievement set

### `016_brilliant_scientist_borrowed_century`

Working label:

Borrowed Century.

Eligible start:

Any country that receives Kruger directly or through transfer.

Challenge:

While retaining Kruger, complete a demanding set of advanced conventional technologies across electronics, industry, rocketry, atomic research, and medicine before a date or within a dynamic period after appointment.

Required conditions:

- Kruger remains employed and alive in a valid form.
- At least five distinct advanced technology categories completed after appointment.
- No research-console or triggerable-scenario disqualifier.
- The country has not granted a sovereign charter or lost Kruger.

Difficulty:

Hard.

Why it is not trivial:

The player must exploit the research advantage across breadth while funding the institution and protecting Kruger.

Icon direction:

A calendar or clock overtaken by layered scientific instruments, not just a portrait.

### `016_brilliant_scientist_every_door`

Working label:

Every Door in the Laboratory.

Eligible start:

Any Kruger host or Kruger State.

Challenge:

Reach at least Prototype in one project from every broad domain group.

Domain groups:

- Computation and electronics.
- Industry and energy.
- Biology.
- Transit or time.
- Autonomous or nonhuman systems.
- Strategic weapons.

Disqualifiers:

- Triggerable setup that grants stages directly.
- Project stages obtained only through debug or inherited scenario shortcuts.

Difficulty:

Very hard.

Why it is not trivial:

Capacity, resources, safety, foreign pressure, and evolution timing make broad completion expensive.

Icon direction:

Several distinct laboratory doors or symbols opening around one impossible central light.

### `016_brilliant_scientist_public_method`

Working label:

The Method Belongs to Everyone.

Eligible start:

A normal host country.

Challenge:

Establish public scientific governance with low Dependence, a functioning independent replication network, and no Weaponized forbidden project while Kruger remains active under a binding public compact.

Required conditions:

- Public appointment or later public-governance reform.
- Publish the Kruger Method.
- Complete at least three independent replication missions.
- Keep Kruger active under the binding public compact when the achievement unlocks.
- No violent rebellion.
- No offensive biological weapon use.

Difficulty:

Hard.

Why it is not trivial:

The player sacrifices exclusive project speed and must manage exposure while preserving enough benefit to justify the route.

Icon direction:

An open scientific book or instrument shared by several hands, with period styling.

### `016_brilliant_scientist_the_one_who_left`

Working label:

The One Who Left.

Eligible start:

A country that did not receive Kruger in the initial random event.

Challenge:

Successfully recruit Kruger away from another country through invitation, asylum, or extraction, then retain him long enough to complete a major Prototype.

Required conditions:

- Kruger previously belonged to another country.
- Recruitment is not a scripted initial transfer caused by that country choosing the normal rejection option, unless the player deliberately influenced the transfer.
- One Prototype completed under the new host.
- No duplicate Kruger character remains active in the former host.

Difficulty:

Very hard.

Icon direction:

A scientist crossing a border between two laboratory silhouettes.

### `016_brilliant_scientist_clean_break`

Working label:

A Clean Break.

Eligible start:

A Kruger host.

Challenge:

Remove, retire, or exile Kruger without rebellion, assassination, mass casualties, or total scientific collapse, while retaining an independent national research legacy.

Required conditions:

- Dependence lowered through replication.
- Binding settlement, retirement, or safe dismissal completed.
- No Kruger State formed violently.
- No assistant massacre or biological fail-deadly event.
- Permanent institutional research remnant remains.

Difficulty:

Hard.

Icon direction:

A laboratory key placed beside an intact national academy.

### `016_brilliant_scientist_approve_everything`

Working label:

Sign Here, Doctor.

Eligible start:

A normal host.

Challenge:

Approve every impossible project family through at least Prototype, retain Kruger until Evolution IV, and prevent rebellion or takeover through the final sovereignty demand.

Required conditions:

- Every impossible family visible in the campaign reaches Prototype.
- Evolution IV active.
- Kruger remains hosted after the final demand.
- Government control resolution succeeds.
- No peaceful charter or violent Kruger State before unlock.

Extreme.

Why it is not trivial:

The portfolio creates huge Mandate, Dependence, exposure, and autonomous capacity. Control requires extensive replication, security, and negotiated limits.

Difficulty:

Extreme.

## Achievement tracking principles

- Use event-owned flags and variables only where final-state checks cannot prove the route.
- Track original host, appointment route, project stages, rebellion formation type, former-host identity, dominant portfolio, nuclear-use disqualifiers, singularity stage, world-threat duration, and aftermath settlement.
- Prevent tag switching or subject release from bypassing eligibility.
- Triggerable scenarios and debug launches should disqualify achievements when they grant route progress directly.
- A peaceful charter and violent rebellion must remain distinguishable.
- Clone, machine, dinosaur, monster, portal, temporal, exotic, and biological force participation must be tracked by real unit or operational use, not ownership of an icon.

## Implementation tranches

### Tranche 1: stable appointment

- Host selection.
- Opening options.
- Transfer chain.
- Persistent Kruger character.
- `+100%` research anchor.
- Special-project scientist access.
- Stage 0 portrait and basic idea lifecycle.
- Event log and Event Details.

Exit condition:

The baseline event is fully playable without evolutions.

### Tranche 2: host management

- Main decision category.
- Core values.
- Laboratory site.
- Staff, facility, governance, replication, and security decisions.
- Basic scripted GUI or readable decision-header presentation.
- AI host behavior.

Exit condition:

A player can build a safe public program or a concentrated secret program.

### Tranche 3: portfolio and incidents

- Conventional foundations.
- Impossible project families in staged groups.
- Project capacity.
- Accidents and containment missions.
- Cross-event hooks.
- Project-specific assets.

Exit condition:

Project history changes the host and produces visible campaign variation.

### Tranche 4: Evolutions I and II

- National policy.
- University and assistant events.
- International interest.
- Foreign actions and security.
- Portrait stages I and II.
- Evolution logging.

Exit condition:

Kruger can become a national and international strategic asset.

### Tranche 5: Evolution III and sovereignty

- Forbidden project stages.
- Hidden Independent Capacity and visible warnings.
- Demands.
- Confinement, killing, defection, charter, rebellion, and takeover outcomes.
- Portrait stage III.

Exit condition:

Every host route has a readable and mechanically valid resolution.

### Tranche 6: Kruger State

- Tag and country package.
- Territory selection.
- Starting forces derived from portfolio.
- Ideas and economy.
- AI.
- Country assets.
- Former-host war and recovery.

Exit condition:

Every valid formation creates a playable and coherent country.

### Tranche 7: full focus tree and Evolution IV

- 85 to 115 focus architecture.
- Route-specific decisions and forces.
- World-conquest branch.
- World-threat integration.
- Severe animated portraits.

Exit condition:

The Kruger State has complete early, middle, and late play.

### Tranche 8: super-events, world end, achievements, aftermath

- Super-event research and final assets.
- Unique audio.
- Laboratory World.
- Strategic Singularity and Fallout.
- Defeat aftermath.
- Achievements and icons.
- Docs and catalog.

Exit condition:

All terminal, defeat, presentation, and player-record surfaces are complete.

## Completion standard

Event 16 is complete only when:

- The baseline appointment is fully implemented.
- All four evolutions work through active and pre-fire entry paths.
- Disabled evolutions have clean alternatives.
- The full project portfolio has costs, risks, AI, and visible states.
- The persistent character and all scientist surfaces stay synchronized.
- Every host resolution works.
- The Kruger State package is playable in every valid formation strength band.
- The focus tree meets route-depth and reward-diversity standards.
- Project-derived forces match actual portfolio history.
- AI can use host, foreign, Kruger, and world-end systems.
- All required static and animated assets exist, are documented, and are wired.
- Every super-event has verified text, a unique final track, image, audio ID, and complete settings-aware wiring.
- World-end branches use the normal terminal contract.
- Achievements have tracking, disqualifiers, localisation, icons, and documentation.
- Event log, Event Details, docs, catalogs, and workbook agree.
- The mandatory improvement-loop closure and completion audit report no unresolved accepted gaps.
- No simplification, fallback, placeholder, missing AI, missing asset, or missing route is hidden.

## Deliberate implementation ownership

The specification does not lock:

- Final focus coordinates.
- Final focus count inside the 85 to 115 target range.
- Final event IDs beyond the stable `chaosx.nr16` namespace.
- Final localisation text.
- Final super-event titles, quotes, button remarks, and audio.
- Final country tag if `KRG` conflicts.
- Exact numerical constants.

Those details belong to implementation and tuning. They must preserve the route architecture, effects, costs, identities, and acceptance criteria in this package.
