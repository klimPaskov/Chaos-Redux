# Event 16 Brilliant Scientist, projects and evolutions

All labels are working labels and not final localisation.

## Baseline stage versus evolution tracks

The baseline is the ordinary host relationship. It includes recruitment, early research focus, first laboratory decisions, and the first national effects. These ordinary stages should not be logged as evolutions.

Evolutions are major changes in how the Kruger relationship behaves. They should use MTTH-style pacing after conditions become possible. They should never fire instantly except in a pre-fire evolved opening where the event first appears in a high-chaos world.

## Evolution I, national scientific celebrity

Evolution I begins when Public Fame or Research Momentum rises enough that the country’s universities, ministries, and press cannot treat Kruger as a private advisor.

### Active-event entry

If Kruger already belongs to a host, Evolution I upgrades the host system:

- Unlocks university, institute, and national science mobilization decisions.
- Opens the Public Fame value fully.
- Creates foreign admiration and jealousy events.
- Lets the host choose a broad field to institutionalize.
- Adds a national spirit stage that makes Kruger feel like a national scientific project rather than one advisor.

### Pre-fire evolved opening

If the event has not fired and the world is already unstable, Kruger can arrive famous. The opening should begin with more Public Fame, immediate foreign attention, and a stronger research bonus, but lower Government Leverage because he arrives with followers and patrons.

### New project access

Evolution I unlocks safe and semi-safe projects:

| Project family | Visible direction | Example payoff | Risk memory |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| University consortium | Civil science and collaboration | Research speed, research slot progress, electronics and industry bonuses | Public fame rises |
| Applied engineering bureau | Industry, production, construction | Faster factories, resource extraction, special machinery | Autonomy rises if private contracts are allowed |
| Signals and computation group | Radar, encryption, calculation machines | Electronics tech and intelligence bonuses | Foreign theft risk rises |
| Medical acceleration program | Medicine and battlefield care | Manpower recovery, hospital or medicine hooks | Biology path memory |
| Rocket mathematics office | Rockets and air defense | Rocket and jet related bonuses | Military laboratory memory |

## Evolution II, international importance and security crisis

Evolution II begins when Public Fame, foreign attention, or breakthrough count rises enough that other states act. It can also trigger if the host refuses foreign access after Kruger becomes famous.

### Active-event entry

- Foreign countries can invite Kruger to lecture, offer him asylum, steal his notes, infiltrate the lab, sponsor rival scientists, sabotage facilities, or attempt kidnapping.
- The host can harden security, accept observers, create secret sites, or trade limited access for diplomatic concessions.
- Rival majors can receive temporary research efforts or spy missions if they fail to obtain Kruger.
- Opinion modifiers and spy-flavored events appear.
- The assassination or defection risk becomes real if Security Integrity is low.

### Security incidents

| Incident | Trigger direction | Player response | Result direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foreign invitation | High fame and open diplomacy | Allow, restrict, or stage-managed visit | Fame, theft risk, influence, and possible defection |
| Stolen notebooks | Security gap | Counterintelligence or public blame | Rival research bonus or recovered data |
| Sabotaged laboratory | Rival hostility | Repair, blame, retaliate, cover up | Security, stability, and foreign relations change |
| Bodyguard loyalty issue | Guard system tied to Kruger | Reassign guards or let Kruger choose | Leverage versus Autonomy tradeoff |
| Asylum rumor | Low host stability or high restrictions | Negotiate or clamp down | Defection or loyalty memory |

### Pre-fire evolved opening

In a high-chaos first firing, Kruger may arrive already pursued. The opening event should start with a foreign spy incident and force the host to decide whether to make him public, hide him, or accept international protection.

## Evolution III, strange and dangerous research

Evolution III begins when Strangeness, risky project approvals, or theoretical project depth crosses a threshold. This is the first evolution where the alien possibility becomes mechanically important.

### What changes

- Safe public science and risky secret projects become mutually tense.
- The host receives visible anomalies through reports, missing staff events, impossible prototype behavior, or local public fear.
- Project approvals start creating military assets that can later become Kruger’s rebel army.
- The host can try to restrict, imprison, kill, or permanently contain Kruger.
- If Government Leverage is high and Laboratory Autonomy is low, containment can succeed.
- If Laboratory Autonomy, private guards, clone staff, robot security, sealed city infrastructure, or monster facilities are strong, an arrest attempt can trigger rebellion.

### The main dilemma

The player should face a serious choice:

| Choice direction | Visible meaning | Hidden consequence direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public science only | Force Kruger into civilian, reviewed science | Slows project output and lowers disaster risk if leverage is high |
| Permit sealed projects | Keep the dangerous work hidden | Stronger bonuses and stronger future rebel arsenal |
| Restrict and investigate | Build evidence before acting | Can unlock successful containment if done early |
| Arrest or eliminate | End the threat now | Can succeed, kill him, imprison him, trigger escape, or trigger war |
| Give him command authority | Let him manage security and facilities | Massive output, rapid Autonomy growth, later rebellion likely |

## Evolution IV, Kruger sovereignty and weaponized projects

Evolution IV is the final normal evolution. It unlocks Kruger’s sovereign ambitions and the world conquest branch.

### Entry conditions

Evolution IV becomes possible when one or more of these are true:

- Laboratory Autonomy is very high.
- The host approved multiple dangerous project families.
- Kruger survived an arrest attempt.
- Security Integrity collapsed while project arsenal is high.
- Kruger’s own country already exists and has survived long enough.
- Chaos is high enough to make strange statehood plausible.

### Outcomes

Evolution IV can resolve in several ways:

| Outcome | Conditions | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sovereign laboratory inside host | Host gives in and stays nominally in control | Host becomes dependent and can slide toward puppet-like Kruger rule |
| Kruger secedes | Autonomy high, leverage low, project arsenal present | New Kruger country spawns and wars the host |
| Kruger escapes abroad | Security low and foreign path active | He transfers to another country with project memories partly preserved |
| Kruger is contained | Leverage high and dangerous arsenal low | Host keeps reduced bonuses and avoids sovereign branch |
| Kruger dies | Rare successful kill with high security | Ends the scientist system but can create martyr or stolen notes events |

### World conquest branch

Once independent or sovereign, Kruger stops behaving like an advisor and becomes a crisis actor. He should target the former host first unless another country already controls the laboratory state or he has been transferred abroad. After the host is subdued, he seeks research centers, capitals, uranium states, industrial belts, alien sites, and special-project targets.

Evolution IV should integrate with the world-threat framework if implementation has a suitable shared source flag. The source should reflect Kruger’s sovereign war machine rather than ordinary scientific fame.

## Project family map

Project families should be distinct. Each family gives a near-term reward and creates a different future risk.

| Project family | Unlock phase | Host reward | Rebel asset if Kruger turns | Interaction hooks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Electronics and computation | I | Radar, encryption, research speed, intelligence | Automated targeting, codebreakers, AI command staff | Counterintelligence, Research Investment |
| Industrial automation | I | Factory output, construction, production efficiency | Robot foundries, autonomous factories | Robot and AI routes |
| Advanced rocketry | I or II | Rocket, jet, missile, air defense boosts | Long-range strike capability | Alien Spacecraft, Special Tech |
| Nuclear theory | II or III | Nuclear research, reactor speed, special project progress | Final device prerequisites and nuclear arsenal | Fallout systems |
| Biological acceleration | II or III | Medicine, manpower recovery, hospitals | Cloning, bioweapons, specimen armies | Chemical and Biological Warfare, Super Soldiers, Crazy Scientist |
| Clone program | III | Manpower and disposable labor | Clone divisions, rapid manpower regeneration | Super Soldiers, camp and atrocity systems must be handled cautiously |
| Robotics and AI | III | Production, logistics, automated warfare bonuses | Robot divisions and automated command | AI aggression and machine routes |
| Creature synthesis | III | Fear weapons, special infantry, occupation tools | Monsters, dinosaurs, obedient beasts | Great Revolution creature themes, Mass Panic |
| Xenotechnology | III | Alien guns, aircraft, materials | Alien infantry weapons and shields | Alien Spacecraft, ZIN, Tomorrow's Girls |
| Teleportation | III or IV | Logistics bypass, strategic redeployment, surprise attacks | Teleport raids and impossible encirclement | Time Traveler, supply systems |
| Time machine | IV | Research repeats, advisor duplication, prediction | Time-displaced units and reset events | Time Traveler, Time Bomb |
| Final equation device | IV | Only for sovereign or surrendered route | World-end bomb if completed | Fallout world-end scenario |

## Project approval philosophy

The player should not be punished for every approval. Some projects should be useful and safe if paired with security and peer review. Danger should come from stacking autonomy, secrecy, private armies, and impossible fields faster than the state can contain them.

The strongest risky projects should require several earlier commitments. The player should have enough information to understand that the state is losing control without seeing exact rebel spawn formulas.

## Containment and rebellion thresholds

The exact formula belongs to implementation, but the spec should preserve these design principles:

- Government Leverage should rise through audits, peer review, public institutions, loyal security, and early restraint.
- Government Leverage should fall when Kruger gets legal exemptions, private guards, autonomous factories, secret staff, sealed cities, alien material custody, clone labor, robot security, or direct command over weapons.
- Rebellion strength should scale from project memories, not only from the host’s industrial size.
- The host should be able to stop Kruger if it acted early.
- A host that gave him everything should discover too late that ordinary arrest orders no longer reach the laboratory gates.
