# Event 16 Brilliant Scientist, super-events, world-end, and aftermath

All labels are working labels and not final localisation. Super-event titles, button remarks, quotes, slogans, and cultural references require research before implementation.

## Super-event role map

This event starts as a minor fire-once event and should not show a super-event at baseline. Super-events are justified when Kruger becomes a sovereign crisis actor, when the world faces the final device, and when a costly global defeat of Kruger changes the campaign.

| Working super-event label | Role | Trigger direction | Needed pieces |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sovereign Kruger reveal | Escalation and faction formation | Kruger secedes or the host openly surrenders authority to his laboratory state | Research-gated title, description direction, quote research, generated image, unique audio |
| Final device armed | World-end threshold race | Kruger completes the final device and is willing or forced to use it | Research-gated title, description direction, quote research, generated image, unique audio |
| Kruger defeated after world crisis | Defeat aftermath | Kruger controlled major territory or fought a long global war and was defeated before final device use | Reflective quote, aftermath image, unique audio |

## Sovereign Kruger reveal super-event

This should fire when Kruger becomes a country or when the host accepts a state of open Kruger rule. The super-event should tell the world that a laboratory network has become a government and an army.

Tone direction:

- The world sees a state organized around a scientist, private laboratories, prototype armies, and sealed facilities.
- The image should be generated, fictional, and period-authentic, showing a laboratory city or leader figure with banners, machines, and strange military silhouettes.
- The description should not reveal every project branch. It should show that state sovereignty has moved from ministries to laboratories.
- Quote direction should involve scientific hubris, forbidden knowledge, or the danger of power without restraint. Research required.
- Button remark direction can use a short, grim cultural or literary allusion about science becoming rule. Research required.
- Audio mood should be ominous, structured, and musical. It should not be a drone or generated tone.

## Final device armed super-event

This is the world-end presentation. It should fire through the normal world-end contract after the final device forces chaos over the required threshold. This reconciles the user request that the device ends the world regardless of prior chaos tier with the Chaos Redux rule that world-end scenarios require chaos above 1000.

Trigger direction:

1. Kruger has completed the final device mission chain.
2. He is close to capitulation, or he chooses the world-end route after reaching a major conquest threshold.
3. The device fires.
4. The effect raises chaos past the world-end threshold if needed.
5. The fallout world-end scenario is triggered through the existing terminal branch pattern.

Tone direction:

- The device should feel like a scientific conclusion, not a generic larger nuke.
- The public can understand only the light, the heat, the ruined instruments, and the collapse of weather, communication, and command.
- It should connect to fallout and air cleanliness systems where possible.
- Quote direction should involve final judgment, human arrogance, the limits of knowledge, or a scientific discovery turned into an extinction act. Research required.
- Button remark direction should be short and devastating. Research required.

## Defeat aftermath super-event

Use this only if Kruger became a global or near-global threat and was defeated after a long crisis. A quick host victory does not need it.

Trigger direction:

- Kruger country existed for a meaningful duration.
- Kruger controlled a significant share of industry, capitals, or major powers, or triggered world-threat state.
- The final device did not fire.
- A coalition, host, or player defeated him.

Aftermath content:

- Dismantle laboratories.
- Secure surviving prototypes.
- Hunt escaped specimens.
- Decide whether to share or bury captured notes.
- Create a limited science oversight pact if the war was global enough.
- Add remembrance or reconstruction events if deaths and devastation were severe.

## World-end branch

The final world-end branch is a fallout world-end scenario caused by Kruger’s completed final device. It is not a normal nuke. It is a scientific weapon that breaks containment and makes the world-end scenario inevitable.

Required design points:

- It should be terminal.
- It should set the global world-end state through the existing contract.
- It should set a scenario-specific Kruger or fallout final-device flag.
- It should show the final device super-event.
- It should stop incompatible Kruger progression, normal negotiations, and containment decisions.
- It should document that the event forced chaos above the world-end threshold before firing terminal logic if the world had lower chaos.

## Final device race

The final device should take time. Enemies should have a window to stop it.

Progress factors:

| Factor | Speeds progress | Slows progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nuclear project history | Nuclear theory approved, reactors controlled | No nuclear route, reactor sabotage |
| Temporal project history | Time machine approved | Reality instability incidents |
| Xenotechnology | Alien material secured | Alien material absent or stolen |
| Industry | Automated foundries and captured factories | Bombed industry and supply disruption |
| Security | Sealed city and loyal guards | Sabotage, spies, internal revolt |
| War state | Desperation if near capitulation can accelerate arming | Stable conquest can focus on construction |

Counterplay:

- Capture or bomb the final test site.
- Steal or destroy components.
- Support internal defectors.
- Force Kruger into early capitulation before arming completes.
- Use intelligence decisions to delay the mission.
- Seize alien material or reactor states first.

## Postwar science choices after containment

If Kruger is contained or defeated without world end, the host or victor should receive choices about the surviving knowledge.

| Choice direction | Effect direction | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Destroy the notes | Remove most bonuses, lower future danger | Lose rare tech opportunities |
| Archive under guard | Keep limited research benefits | Future theft events possible |
| Publish safe material | Gain public science and diplomacy | Smaller foreign research spread |
| Weaponize captured work | Strong military gains | Strangeness, condemnation, and future disaster hooks |
| Share under treaty | Allied bonuses and oversight | Rival infiltration and dependency |
