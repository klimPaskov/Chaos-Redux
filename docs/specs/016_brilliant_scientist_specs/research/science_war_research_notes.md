# Research notes for Brilliant Scientist

These notes support the event direction. They are not player-facing localisation and should not be pasted into the game.

## Wartime research as inspiration

Los Alamos provides the strongest historical model for the first safe phase of the event. The Manhattan Project moved dispersed fast-neutron research into a coordinated secret laboratory, and Oppenheimer argued for a central site where theoretical and experimental work could be exchanged quickly. Source: https://www.osti.gov/opennet/manhattan-project-history/Events/1942-1945/establishing_los_alamos.htm

MIT Radiation Laboratory provides a second model for the public effect of one research network changing a war. The laboratory grew around microwave radar and produced many radar systems, including airborne bombing radars, shipboard search radars, gun-laying radars, ground-controlled approach, identification beacons, and LORAN. Source: https://www.ll.mit.edu/about/history/mit-radiation-laboratory

Bletchley Park and the Bombe are useful for electronics, cryptography, and strange calculation projects. The public-facing design should avoid naming real figures unless sourced and should instead use the idea of machines that make impossible calculations routine. Source: https://www.bletchleypark.org.uk/our-story/6-facts-about-the-bombe/

Project Paperclip and Project Overcast provide inspiration for the foreign-reaction layer. The Smithsonian source describes the program that brought German and Austrian engineers, scientists, and technicians to the United States after World War II, and it also highlights the moral cost of recruiting specialists with Nazi records. Source: https://airandspace.si.edu/stories/editorial/project-paperclip-and-american-rocketry-after-world-war-ii

Penicillin research provides a civilian science route. Fleming discovered penicillin in 1928, while later researchers demonstrated its therapeutic value in 1940. This supports a route where Kruger can be channeled into medicine and industry instead of weapons. Source: https://www.sciencehistory.org/education/scientific-biographies/alexander-fleming/

National Archives OSRD records provide inspiration for bureaucratic science offices, contract files, field reports, and military research units without turning event text into paperwork drama. Source: https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/227.html

## How this research should shape the event

The event should begin with institutional awe rather than immediate apocalypse. The first useful question is whether the host builds a coordinated research ecosystem around Warren Kruger or lets him become a private sovereign power inside the state.

The public route should borrow from OSRD, Los Alamos, the Rad Lab, and penicillin production. It turns genius into laboratories, peer review, industrial production, medicine, radar, computation, rockets, and ordinary special-project acceleration.

The dangerous route should borrow from Paperclip and secret weapons programs. It should make governments tolerate immoral bargains because the output is too useful. Kruger’s alien nature should first appear as an explanation people avoid, not as an early reveal.

Foreign reactions should not be generic opinion penalties. They should include invitations, theft attempts, security missions, asylum offers, spy networks, rival institutes, black-market samples, and diplomatic scandals.

The final world-end weapon should feel like a perversion of big-science coordination. It should require time, facilities, project history, and an independent Kruger state or a host government that has already surrendered authority to him.
