# Super-event research prompt for Event 16 Brilliant Scientist

Use the Chaos Redux super-event workflow. Do not edit localisation, event files, sound definitions, `.gfx`, GUI, docs outside the named research note, or spreadsheets unless the parent explicitly expands scope.

## Research boundary

All super-event titles, button remarks, quotes, cultural references, slogans, lyric fragments, allusions, and audio choices are research blockers until verified. The specification uses working labels only. Do not treat a working label as final localisation.

## Required super-event roles

### Sovereign Kruger reveal

Role: global reveal or escalation. Trigger direction: Kruger secedes, becomes openly sovereign, or publicly reveals the laboratory state after the host loses control. Tone direction: strange scientific authority becoming political and military power. Image direction: Kruger and the laboratory state with prototype forces, period-authentic but fictional. Quote direction: knowledge, arrogance, forbidden inquiry, or authority escaping restraint.

### Final device armed

Role: world-end approach and terminal race. Trigger direction: Kruger advances the final device far enough that sabotage or capitulation timing becomes the central campaign issue. Tone direction: dread, irreversible scientific overreach, and a world that may be converted into fallout if the device completes. Image direction: final device facility and impossible light, not an abstract title card. Quote direction: final judgment, ruin, or the cost of forbidden knowledge.

### Defeat aftermath

Role: costly victory aftermath. Trigger direction: Kruger is defeated after the threat became global or near-global and the campaign paid a major price. Tone direction: reflective, exhausted, and wary of future scientific militarisation. Image direction: ruined laboratories, captured prototypes, exhausted soldiers, and postwar inspection.

## Output research note

Create `docs/super_events/016_brilliant_scientist_super_event_research.md`. For each role, compare several quote candidates, verify wording and attribution, recommend one quote and one backup, compare button remark or cultural reference candidates, select a remark direction only after source checks, research public domain or clearly licensed audio, document composition and recording rights, and coordinate image needs with the asset package.

## Audio requirements

Each final super-event needs a unique final track, unique audio id, source URL, creator or composer, performer or recording source when known, license, duration, source download path, final `.ogg` path, editing notes, and wiring handoff. Reject unclear licenses. Do not use generated tones, oscillator music, pure drones, abstract ambience, placeholder tracks, or undocumented reuse.
