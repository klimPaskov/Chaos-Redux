## Critical failure conditions

Do not mark complete when any of these remain:

- Research anchor reduced without accepted design change.
- Kruger missing from a special-project field.
- Duplicate or stale Kruger character after transfer or rebellion.
- A project family exists only as flavour or tiny modifiers.
- Rebellion receives an army type not supported by project history.
- A country forms without viable territory, supply, or starting forces.
- Focus branches are shallow or generic.
- AI cannot use a human-facing system.
- Required assets are placeholders, missing, unwired, or undocumented.
- An animated portrait is a transform-only still.
- Super-event text or audio is unresearched, reused without approval, or undocumented.
- Singularity bypasses the chaos threshold instead of raising it first.
- Disabled evolutions brick baseline play.
- Catalog or workbook wording is stale.
- An accepted addendum remains unresolved.
- A fallback or simplification is hidden.
