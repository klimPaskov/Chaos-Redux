# 84 · The Peace Movement
## Part 5 · International spread and Chaos evolutions

### Transmission is a relationship, not a universal daily roll

The opening creates one origin. Later countries need an identifiable contact with an active movement organization or a domestic shock strong enough to establish their own circle. Spread follows contact opportunities and social conditions, not an assumption that every neighboring population automatically agrees with the same program.

A valid contact can be a shared inhabited border with civilian passage, a functioning trade or migration link, a common war, a faction relationship with actual exchange, an accepted cultural visit, a refugee corridor, or a funded exile organization. A contact whose route is closed, whose partner no longer exists, or whose sponsoring organization is dormant cannot keep producing spread. A distant radio or press campaign needs an actual sponsor and consumes one international contact opportunity.

Store a bounded contact shortlist for each active source country. Rebuild it only after a material relationship change or the source country's scheduled review. A source may sponsor at most three unresolved foreign contact projects. No project creates a new global daily or weekly scan. Existing shared framework callbacks may be reused only after their scope and cost are inspected.

### Exposure and the first foreign cell

The first successful contact introduces a local circle at Influence 10 and Reaction 5, unless local values are already higher. A contact never overwrites a country's existing values. A country with a genuine long-war grievance can instead begin at Influence 15 and Reaction 10. The origin's opening event remains unique and does not replay for every new country.

Ordinary transmission receives one evaluation per affected-country 30-day review. Base candidate weights express which eligible contact is most plausible. A common inhabited border has weight 30, an active migration or refugee channel 30, a common war 25, a functioning trade link 20, an accepted cultural exchange 40, and an organized exile campaign 35. These weights select among contacts. They are not statements that spread occurs with that percentage probability.

After selecting a contact, a first-cell test has a base 20% chance for that review. Add 15 percentage points for a completed cultural or civic exchange, 10 for actual war weariness, 10 for legal public organizing, and 10 for an established domestic sponsor. Subtract 10 for effective border restrictions on that specific route and 15 for verified disruption of its organizing network. Clamp the result to 5% through 60%. A completely invalid route is excluded rather than granted the 5% floor. These are proposed event-roll probabilities for the implementing auditor to verify, not measured game behavior.

Only one ordinary first-cell success may originate from a given source in one review. Countries already affected receive at most the single +5 foreign-network Influence contribution specified in Part 2, regardless of how many foreign sponsors contact them. A sponsor cannot multiply that contribution by founding several duplicate committees.

### Domestic reappearance and clandestine organization

A country with no current foreign contact can develop a domestic circle after a severe, real war or repression shock. It must have an eligible civilian anchor and a concrete grievance, such as a sustained service dispute, a verified abusive crackdown, or a major veterans' protest. This route restores the movement's social character when borders close without making closed countries immune or making propagation inevitable.

Suppression can move an organization underground. Underground status reduces public festivals, official sponsorship, and ordinary visible transmission. It does not secretly grant all open-organizing bonuses. A clandestine group needs an actual surviving anchor, sympathizer, exile connection, or grievance. Without one, it decays toward dormancy rather than acting as an indestructible ghost.

Repression can strengthen a surviving organization by creating a documented grievance. The Influence increase is already bounded in Part 2. Do not add an additional hidden spread bonus every review for the same raid. A raid's aftermath expires after its specified window unless a new, separately validated incident occurs.

### Evolution structure

The three evolution stages form a cumulative sequence. Love and Liberation requires global Chaos at least 200. The Reaction requires at least 400. Armed Peace requires at least 600 and an actual history of repeated repression. Earlier stages remain part of the event after a later stage opens. Falling Chaos stops new threshold-dependent evolution checks but does not remove established laws, characters, organizations, or units.

Crossing a threshold makes the next evolution eligible. It does not force the change that day. Use the project evolution mechanism with a proposed base MTTH of 90 days for the next eligible stage. A lower stage must have resolved before the next stage's timer begins. The exact timing adapter and modifier composition require the probability auditor. Do not call an unverified 90-day MTTH a 90-day guarantee or convert it into a daily percentage by guesswork.

For an already active event, useful accelerants are three affected countries, a completed transnational congress, a high-Influence government, and a qualifying crackdown. A completed reconciliation program can slow escalation when the next stage depends on unresolved conflict. Love and Liberation need not be prevented by successful rights reforms. Its politics can develop peacefully. The Reaction can organize lawfully, even when the world has not entered a civil war. Armed Peace cannot be justified by a peaceful festival alone.

### Starting the event at elevated Chaos

At an ordinary root firing, snapshot the current Chaos tier once. At 0 through 199, start the baseline only. At 200 through 399, initialize the Love and Liberation platform as part of the opening, while still beginning with one modest local circle. At 400 through 599, that platform also has an identifiable traditionalist countercampaign. At 600 or above, an opening can know of armed self-defense only if actual qualifying repression already exists in the selected country or a valid linked exile history. Otherwise Armed Peace remains locked until repression occurs.

An evolved opening does not create several movement governments, international blocs, or an equipped army for free. Those are mature scenario features. The historical-repression predicate must use campaign evidence, not a country's real-world religion, ideology, reputation, or a fabricated atrocity. If no such campaign evidence is available, do not claim it exists.

The manual scenario deliberately bypasses these ordinary opening rules and declares its mature starting history and material grants. Its setup is defined separately in Part 11.

### Evolution I · Love and Liberation

At this stage the coalition publishes a more explicit program of legal equality, public queer culture, personal autonomy, communal living, and spiritual experimentation. It brings the family-policy congress, equality-law package, public relationship recognition, and identity-route debate into normal play.

The internal disagreement is substantive. A pluralist legal wing wants individual rights and ordinary democratic institutions. A communal wing wants civilian ownership and local assemblies. A spiritual wing wants protected communities and a public ethical council. All three can contain queer people, heterosexual allies, parents, and voluntarily childless supporters. Only an explicit policy choice activates a demographic modifier.

The government may negotiate selected reforms without adopting the entire platform. Conscientious objection does not require nationalizing industry. Equal legal recognition does not require anti-natalism. A civilian cooperative program does not require the government to join a foreign bloc. These separations provide useful bargaining space and prevent one omnibus button from solving every disagreement.

Once a movement government has lawful authority, it can select a country-appropriate constitutional identity. The selection is a political commitment with institutions and a flag package. A high Influence score alone does not rename a country against its government's decision.

### Evolution II · The Reaction

An international traditionalist response becomes possible after the threshold and a real organizing base. Reaction already exists domestically from the opening. This evolution gives it cross-border institutions, coordinated aid, and rival political programs.

Traditionalist governments can offer intelligence cooperation, funding controls, border coordination, abstract activist-list agreements, extradition treaties, public messaging, legal support for allied parties, and military aid to valid partners. Each instrument is a concrete treaty or project. It is not a global passive debuff applied to all movement supporters.

Movement governments can respond with asylum, civic funding, communications, lawful trade restrictions, and volunteers. Volunteers and military shipments obey existing war, sovereignty, equipment, transport, and diplomatic conditions. A legal refuge does not automatically create soldiers. A treaty over extradition can fracture a coalition if a member's constitution prohibits the transfer.

Religious and secular traditionalists use country-specific institutions and vocabulary. A religious conservative can oppose armed revolution while accepting a legal equality settlement. A secular militarist can favor repression while having little interest in religious law. Their common bloc emerges from negotiated strategic interests rather than a claim that these societies are interchangeable.

### Evolution III · Armed Peace

The stage requires at least two distinct qualifying repression incidents, or one failed eradication campaign that created an actual institutional rupture. The incidents must occur within 180 days in affected countries with surviving movement organizations. They cannot be duplicate reports of the same action. A reconciled, unarmed world at Chaos 600 does not receive militias merely to satisfy the stage's name.

The movement debates whether armed defense has destroyed its pacifist purpose. Three responses are available to an eligible movement authority. It can maintain organized nonviolence and invest in legal guarantees. It can authorize locally accountable Commune Guards and limited Rainbow Militias. It can create a professional defense mandate leading toward Liberation Brigades. The last two require material recruitment and training. They do not erase the civilian economy or instantly restore all pre-event mobilization capacity.

Lotus Ascendants are a limited order that offers disciplined defensive service under a fictional doctrine of transcendence. The political authority must decide whether to integrate them under civilian law, tolerate an independent order, or decline their service. Independent status increases the actual armed-group Reaction contribution. Integration trades some recruitment speed for accountable command. The order's beliefs never confer supernatural casualty immunity or literal resurrection.

Traditionalist forces are not merely targets for these new units. Their path can improve command cohesion and mobilization, protect civilian institutions, or pursue coercive restoration with growing political resistance. Part 8 defines the military tradeoffs and Part 9 defines reciprocal civil-war and bloc play.

### Evolution broadcasts and quiet outcomes

A local evolution receives an appropriate report or news item. Use a super-event only when the change establishes a global turning point with enough political reach to justify it. Do not play a world-scale cinematic when one small town adopts a new communal charter.

Evolution itself adds no Chaos. Its concrete international spread, government rupture, civil war, or terminal commitment can have a separately owned Chaos consequence. A peaceful legal settlement is not assigned Chaos merely because the law protects queer people. A government may reach the final evolutionary platform and still settle into a stable, demobilized society without either international bloc becoming a world-conquest machine.
