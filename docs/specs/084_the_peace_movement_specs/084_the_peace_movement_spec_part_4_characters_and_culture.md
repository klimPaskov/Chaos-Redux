# 84 · The Peace Movement
## Part 4 · Culture, characters, private life, and public consequences

### The movement has people rather than a single doctrine

The first organizers are a loose coalition.
Antiwar veterans want fewer funerals.
Queer legal-aid circles want protection and a public life.
Spiritual teachers want space for meditation and communal worship.
Workers want shorter military shifts and civilian necessities.
Some communes encourage parenthood and childcare.
Others defend voluntary childlessness or adopt explicitly anti-natalist politics.
These positions coexist until a decision over law, resources, command, or leadership makes the disagreement consequential.

Use recurring local organizations rather than spawning a new named association for every incident.
A country needs one civic network, one spiritual network, and one political delegation at most in the opening phase.
Their names and membership vary by country.
An organization can later become a coalition partner, exile committee, relief service, or militia sponsor.
Its earlier treatment should matter when it returns.
A legal-aid circle protected during the opening may mediate a later strike.
A retreat raided after the government promised protection will not immediately trust the same minister.

The movement includes gay, lesbian, bisexual, transgender, and other queer people, allies, and people whose private lives are never specified.
Membership is a political or social affiliation, not an orientation test.
Public queer culture is visible through legal-aid offices, couples attending festivals, period-appropriate clothing, artistic performance, and banners.
Avoid treating sexual display as the only way to make the movement recognizable.

### Cultural incidents with consequences

| Incident family | What creates the incident | Meaningful choice | Persistent consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public festival | An open organization has a safe venue and sponsor | Permit a negotiated route, sponsor civilian relief alongside it, or restrict the event | The completed event can produce the bounded cultural Influence contribution, a credible public-order agreement, or a contested repression case |
| Communal kitchen | Displaced people or shortages create a local need | Allocate real civilian capacity, permit voluntary support, or requisition its supplies | Actual relief reduces the local grievance, while requisition can damage trust without creating fictional famine casualties |
| Workplace refusal | Military production and conscription demands collide with a mobilized workforce | Negotiate alternative service, bargain over shifts, or compel production | The chosen agreement changes the relevant service or economy spirit and can split an aligned official from the government |
| Spiritual retreat | A recognized circle seeks a gathering place | Protect plural practice, require ordinary public-order terms, or close it | Peaceful recognition may lower Reaction among religious bridge-builders, while a proven violent raid creates a repression record |
| Family-policy congress | A movement government must settle its policy | Family autonomy, deferred parenthood, explicit anti-natalism, or funded inclusive family support | Apply the specific demographic policy in Part 2, never an automatic penalty for queer equality |
| Veterans' delegation | A long war and actual casualties create organized opposition | Hear the delegation, negotiate service terms, or refuse it | A surviving commander can become a broker, a sympathizer, or a critic of the movement's military policy |
| Symbols dispute | A specific incident involving a national or religious symbol has occurred | Investigate responsibility, accept a mediated repair, or exploit the dispute politically | Responsibility can belong to a provocateur or an individual rather than every movement member |
| Civic art exchange | A foreign partner and functioning contact channel exist | Host, reciprocate, or decline | The exchange creates one time-limited transmission contact rather than unlimited global Influence |

Each family should have at least a peaceful, a contested, and a wartime context where meaningful.
Do not manufacture an abuse event merely to ensure the player sees every branch.
A protected festival can simply succeed.
An investigation may conclude that an alarming rumor was false.

### Character selection and persistence

Use existing living, available characters where their supported role makes the event meaningful.
A leader's affiliation can affect cabinet authority.
A general's stance can affect one command's loyalty.
An admiral can mediate fleet neutrality.
An adviser can support a reform.
An intelligence official can uncover foreign sponsorship.
A scientist or industrial figure can sponsor a civilian research program or contest forced conversion of production.

A role is eligible only when the campaign actually contains a character or supported character-like representation for it.
Do not attach leader traits to an industrial concern or fabricate an operative system when its required content is unavailable.
A role without a suitable native character can appear as an explicitly fictional named committee in a report, with a country-level case outcome.
That does not create an extra adviser slot or a free industrial organization.

Preserve real identity, age eligibility, survival, active service, and existing unique route content.
Alternative-history political conversion may involve a real historical character, but the text must situate it in this campaign.
Do not present an invented private sexual history as a historical discovery.
Private-life cases involving unsupported identity claims use adult fictional officials.
A sourced historical queer identity may be acknowledged without making that person's orientation a random trait lottery.

Characters carry one primary Event 84 public alignment at a time and at most one unresolved case.
Distinct political and personal facts may coexist, but mechanically identical modifiers never stack through repeated events.
A commander cannot receive the same loyalty reward from separate general and field-marshal representations.
An inactive, retired, dead, captured, or transferred character cannot keep providing an active-office effect.

### Gathering and disclosure cases

A gathering case begins with an actual political connection, a protected private event, an intelligence investigation, or a fabricated allegation.
A hidden case type is selected when the incident begins and persists through save and reload.
Later choices reveal or respond to evidence rather than rerolling whether the allegation was true.

There are four evidence categories.
Confirmed public political participation establishes movement sympathy.
Verified private attendance establishes attendance, not every claim made about it.
Fabricated allegations establish a disinformation case when exposed.
Inconclusive material remains inconclusive and cannot legitimately become a confirmed orientation label.
The player-facing report must distinguish these outcomes.

The subject is an adult.
The scene is non-explicit.
It may show a doorway, invitation, confiscated correspondence, a retreat's dining hall, or a public press conference.
Do not show sexual activity or use voyeuristic imagery as the reward for investigation.

| Government response | Requirements | Resolution and tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Protect private life | The government can enforce protection and accepts a public explanation | Ends the threatened disclosure's active Stability risk, can give −5 Reaction through a credible agreement, and may alienate an existing firebrand |
| Permit voluntary disclosure | The character consents and controls the statement | Can transform a fictional character into a Queer Icon or advocate after a real public act. There is no forced conversion of real people into invented identities |
| Investigate public misconduct | A concrete claim concerns corruption, coercion, misuse of office, or an actual political operation | Outcomes follow evidence. Consensual private life is not itself misconduct |
| Remove from office | The government has authority over the post | Removes that office's active political influence. It does not delete the character, refund recruitment, or automatically establish guilt |
| Use confidential leverage | A supported intelligence case and a concrete political demand exist | An abstract, time-limited political concession may result. Exposure creates a hypocrisy or abuse-of-office case, closes the access, and can produce +10 Influence and +10 Reaction |
| Publish the allegation | The publisher has a real public channel | Confirmation, rebuttal, or fabrication exposure drives the result. False claims can damage the publisher and cannot silently become permanent historical facts |

Blackmail remains an abstract game case.
There is no operational guide to finding or targeting real queer people.
Extradition and activist-list cooperation later in the event use similarly abstract treaty commitments, not realistic methods of identifying private individuals.

### Major figures and cabinet crises

A leader case becomes a national political event only when it threatens actual authority, reveals concrete hypocrisy, exposes a foreign operation, or produces a deliberate public realignment.
Its size comes from the office and political circumstances.
Private attendance alone does not cause a government collapse.

A government that persecuted a group while secretly relying on its organizers faces a contradiction in policy and conduct.
The scandal can resolve through resignation, a legal reversal, a parliamentary inquiry, or a hardened purge.
An accepting government may experience no destabilization from a leader's voluntary coming out at all.
It can receive a cultural mobilization opportunity instead.

A converted leader cannot override every institution by personal declaration.
For a peaceful movement takeover, the cabinet or constitutional body must accept a program and the military must accept lawful authority.
An opposing parliament can force a coalition negotiation.
A disloyal command can create a command-rupture mission.
This preserves leader conversion as a requested takeover route without turning one trait into an automatic country flip.

### Local flavour and historical grounding

Historical references are illustrative, not a claim that this fictional movement existed in 1936.
The research ledger separates checked sources from research leads.
Magnus Hirschfeld's institute provides a documented precedent for rights advocacy and its repression, but Hirschfeld died in 1935 and is not inserted as a living 1936 adviser.
The movement's rainbow symbolism is a deliberate alternate-history invention, not a claim that the familiar later flag already existed in the base-game period.
Historical sources H01 and H02 are documented with their limits in `084_the_peace_movement_super_event_research.md`.

Religious pacifists, secular conservatives, queer religious believers, nationalist antiwar veterans, and family-oriented communes provide bridges across the apparent divide.
Country flavour should select institutions and arguments from researched local context.
Do not use Christianity, Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism, atheism, or nationality as an automatic hostility score.
The Lotus Ascendants are a particular fictional order with their own choices and internal dissent.

### Character aftermath

Successful reconciliation can convert an antagonist into a lawful opposition leader.
A protected officer can return to service under an alternative-service charter.
A failed infiltrator may face an inquiry or flee to a foreign sponsor.
An exile organizer can return after an amnesty.
A movement celebrity who enters government loses access to recurring outsider protest rewards against their own administration.

When a civil war ends, process allegiance, captivity, appointments, and personal survival before assigning portraits and office effects.
Do not duplicate the same historical commander across victorious and defeated governments.
A surviving opposing politician may remain a legal critic if the settlement allows it.
This is a political outcome, not an incomplete cleanup.
