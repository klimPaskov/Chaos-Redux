## U84-RM · Rainbow Militias

The silhouette is a civilian volunteer made ready for field service: practical period jacket or work shirt, sturdy trousers and boots, small field pack, minimal webbing, and a restrained movement armband. The armband must not carry miniature unreadable text. The design has no modern tactical armor, plastic accessories, digital camouflage, or mandatory flamboyant costume. Queer identity is conveyed through the institution and visual motif rather than sexualized anatomy.

The weapon is a period-plausible conventional rifle, separately generated from its approved modern concept-art reference. Its exact historical pattern is a research-and-art choice, not an invitation to add a new equipment technology. The gun must have readable major forms, correct grip and muzzle orientation, and a supported visible recoil cycle.

The body movement communicates enthusiasm and uneven professional polish without slapstick. Idle has a guarded stance and small weight shifts. Movement has a sustainable marching/run cycle appropriate to the game's speed. Attack and support attack aim and fire through the correct hand and weapon transforms. Defense emphasizes cover and bracing. Death is a non-graphic fall with a clear transition and no contorted detached weapon.

## U84-CG · Commune Guards

The silhouette is a local defender carrying limited field equipment: practical coat or workwear, a locally distinct guard cap or helmet where researched, compact satchel, and a recognizable civic-guard emblem. It must differ from the militia through posture, equipment distribution, and territorial-defense identity, not merely armband color.

Use a separately generated conventional rifle suitable to the period. The defender's animation can be more cautious and position-focused, with deliberate scanning in idle and a well-braced defensive action. Do not imply that local troops are incapable of moving or that an immobile model counts as territorial specialization. The gameplay movement penalty belongs to the unit definition, while the animation still completes a proper cycle.

The model can guard civilian districts, relief sites, and supply positions. Do not attach a temple or communal house directly to the unit mesh as a visual shortcut. Its distinct locality motif belongs in counters, icons, and environment scenes.

## U84-LA · Lotus Ascendants

The silhouette is a disciplined fictional spiritual volunteer in practical field clothing, with restrained wrapped cloth or a short mantle that does not obstruct weapon grip or exaggerate religious costume. Include a small fictional order emblem. The body must remain mechanically plausible for a soldier carrying a rifle and supplies. Avoid turning it into an undressed monk caricature or assigning one real religion's clergy clothing to a universal combat order.

Use a separately generated rifle. Any cloth elements need controlled deformation and appropriate mesh complexity for the game camera. They cannot clip through hands, gun, knees, or the ground during key poses. There are no glowing supernatural organs, levitation, resurrection animations, or implied immunity.

Idle expresses stillness through deliberate breathing and weight, not zero animation. Move has controlled pacing. Attack and support attack use calm braced firing and correct recoil. Defense communicates disciplined endurance. Death remains an ordinary mortal outcome. The spiritual story belongs in narrative and institutions, not a magic combat effect.

## U84-LB · Liberation Brigades

The silhouette is a professional movement field soldier: coherent period uniform, useful webbing, helmet or cap appropriate to the selected modern concept reference, field pack, and a small civilian-command or brigade insignia. It is not part of the minimum complete model. Do not produce an unused arsenal of weapons to inflate deliverable counts.

Animations emphasize practiced weapon handling and stable footwork. All six actions need independent appropriate motion and valid transitions. A faster animation is not automatically better. Synchronize footsteps, firing, recoil, and muzzle effects with their actual event points.

## Action and acceptance matrix

| Action | Required visible behavior | Key validation |
|---|---|---|
| Idle | Breathing, weight, ready posture, stable gun grip | No frozen alias, no sliding feet, loop seam verified |
| Move | Clear locomotion with weapon and pack movement | Foot contact, root behavior, speed, loop and terrain presentation |
| Attack | Aim, brace, fire, recoil, recovery | Hands remain on weapon, muzzle event and sound align |
| Defend | A distinct defensive posture and action | No clipping during lowered or braced stance, readable game-camera silhouette |
| Support attack | A supported attacking behavior with its own correct assignment | Not an unrelated idle alias, action naming and engine consumer verified |
| Death | Non-graphic collapse and supported end state | Weapon follows intended physics/animation, no stretching or resurrection |
