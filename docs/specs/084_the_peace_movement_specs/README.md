# Event 84 · The Peace Movement

## Planning and implementation-handoff package

This package expands the uploaded Event 84 concept into a complete campaign design, from the first counterculture circles through negotiated reform, government change, international polarization, armed defense, and The Gay World. It also defines the mature War for the Soul manual scenario.

**Original delivery status:** full-scope authored design and handoff, without implementation or independent-review proof. All 43 supplied text files were fully read. Several external source dependencies, final cultural/audio research, asset-production prerequisites, and mandatory independent tool/agent reviews remain unresolved and are documented in the source ledger. Numeric tuning is proposed rather than playtested.

## Design commitments

Influence and Reaction are independent. Equality and orientation are separate from explicit demographic policy. Peaceful takeovers and conservative pluralist settlements remain viable. The four special units need Armed Peace and actual material, while ordinary national forces handle earlier defensive emergencies. Existing national trees remain meaningful, and new authorities need complete country packages.

World-end activation uses strictly more than 1,000 Chaos and strictly more than 50% living world population, counted once per controlled state, plus the two political requirements from the brief and the shared enable/ownership gates. Activation starts the final campaign. It does not award victory. Manual intensity changes mature starting circumstances and explicitly adds Chaos without duplicating people, forces, or earned progress.
