## Verified quotation candidates, not selected localisation

This is a candidate for a nonviolent counterpoint to SE02 or the final settlement.
Do not use it to claim that the real scripture advocates the fictional order's combat doctrine.

Candidate Q02, an excerpt from the corporate testimony reproduced at H04: “to seek peace, and ensue it”.
Its precise source and religious context are known.
A short fragment by itself may be too slight for a major scene.
The text researcher must assess whether a contextually complete, properly attributed allowed excerpt is better, without inventing a quotation from a remembered slogan.

No final quotation is selected for SE01 or SE03.
Research directions are the tension between private conscience and public authority, and the difference between social victory and conquest.
They require a source, not a fabricated epigraph assigned to a historical leader.
