# 84 · The Peace Movement
## Part 13 · Presentation, narrative direction, and asset requirements

### Presentation choice

Use ordinary decision categories with the two requested public values, current institutional posture, and concise mission status. This is sufficient for the player's primary choices. No custom draggable window, bespoke map puzzle, new public currency, or animated meter is justified for the core design.

There are three mutually contextual category states: public movement and negotiations, constitutional or civil-war crisis, and international/world-order commitments. They share a consistent static category illustration family and never produce more than six primary visible actions or three active visible missions. Character cases appear through reports and the currently relevant case action. Existing shared map modes can support food, migration, or territorial context without an Event 84 map mode duplicating them.

The category picture is permitted because this design does not use the excluded interactive state-puzzle or full event-owned window. Inspect the actual category-picture consumer and reference contact sheet before selecting its final dimensions. No dimensions are invented for this consumer in the absence of those reference files.

### What the player should immediately understand

The country can have high Influence and high Reaction simultaneously. The public summary should show both independently, describe the current political institution, and identify the next material objective. A trend explanation names the major current causes and their review interval. It must not claim a guaranteed future government change because a meter is rising.

Tooltips distinguish percentage points from relative percentages, a maintained institutional total from a temporary modifier, selected contact weights from actual event-roll probabilities, and one-time commitments from daily expenditure. A demographic tooltip explicitly identifies the chosen population policy. The wording must never imply that sexual orientation inherently reduces population or military skill.

### Narrative register

The opening is curious, intimate, and politically inconvenient. Cultural disputes can have wit and irony, especially when official bureaucracy tries to classify a philosophy of personal freedom. Repression, coerced disclosure, and civilian harm use serious, clear language. The joke belongs to the institution's contradiction or the government's hypocrisy, not to queer people, religious communities, or victims of violence.

The later international story is grander but remains grounded in campaign facts. The movement's claim to peace sits beside the material decisions required to defend itself. Traditionalist arguments vary by country and route. A constitutional conservative's speech should not sound identical to an emergency junta's threat. The Lotus order should sound like a fictional disciplined institution, not a caricature of all Hindu or Buddhist practice.

### Localisation scope and research gate

All labels in this package are working design labels unless they were explicitly named in the user's brief. They are not ready-to-paste event titles, descriptions, options, focus names, achievement text, or super-event button lines. The coding and writing agents must produce final localisation after the necessary research and review. Never substitute a file name or internal asset label for a finished cultural reference.

Narrative contexts include origin and foreign arrival, the five government responses, each civic negotiation, the three evolutions, character affiliations and case outcomes, seven peaceful or coercive transfer routes plus civil-war victory, each constitution, both bloc congresses, civil-war settlement types, world-end activation and victory, reversal, and all four manual intensities. Each context needs correct actor, tense, authority, and outcome conditions rather than one generic paragraph reused everywhere.

Reports must support at least first-person government and observer news perspectives where the event warrants both. A public broadcast does not expose confidential case details before the case is resolved. A sourced real person's identity cannot be changed by a dynamic placeholder meant for a fictional official.

### Scene families and visual escalation

The opening shows small civilian gatherings, relief work, legal correspondence, a university-like public venue where locally justified, or a port receiving new ideas. Love and Liberation adds larger public festivals, legal-equality campaigning, communal buildings, and visible civic institutions. The Reaction adds rival meetings, national or religious institutions, cabinet negotiations, and official restrictions. Armed Peace introduces actual training grounds, guarded relief centers, civilian command meetings, and sober defensive positions.

Global scenes show real political space: a multinational congress, delegations crossing a ruined frontier, crowded streets after a settlement, or an industrial city whose civilian and military life has changed. Super-event art should depict a scene with people, architecture, depth, and a clear focal point. It must not collapse into an abstract rainbow poster or a collage of unrelated religious symbols.

### Flags, identities, and portraits

Existing countries keep their current flags until an actual constitutional identity change requires a new one. The movement's five identity directions need country-specific coherent variants, not one identical universal flag with a country name printed on it. Use the same design language across sizes while keeping the smallest flag readable. The familiar rainbow flag is an intentional alternate-history invention in this setting, not documented 1936 heraldry.

Grounded historical people require identity-preserving sources and the dedicated portrait worker. The user alone operates the RunPod final portrait workflow under the supplied project rule. This package does not authorize the parent or a subagent to run that service. A sourced placeholder must be clearly marked pending and cannot be passed off as a finished portrait.

Fictional officials are suitable for unsupported private-life cases and caretaker roles. They need coherent adult age, role, country context, and persistence. They are not generic substitutes for every available real historical leader. An institution can sometimes be shown through a seal or meeting-room scene rather than inventing a leader who is never used mechanically.

### 3D pipeline commitment

All four custom firearm formations use the most specific supplied 3D pipeline. Generate a weapon-free body through Meshy 7 from one approved body image. Generate each gun separately through Meshy 7 from its own single gun-only image. Assemble, rig, weight, and animate directly in Blender through the required qualified worker. Do not request Meshy rigging or action generation for these firearm units. Do not model their guns procedurally in Blender as a silent substitute for the newer separate-generation rule.

Use modern professionally designed artwork with documented rights suitable for the intended generation reference. Archival photographs, museum displays, reenactment photos, and documentary material are not the body/gun generation references under this skill, even though some are appropriate sources for other asset categories. No such production-ready licensed reference was verified during this planning turn. That remains an explicit production gate.

Every unit requires idle, movement, attack, defense, support attack, and death actions with actual skeletal motion, proper weapon grip, recoil, foot contact, weight transfer, and export/reimport checks. Sample scale from an installed vanilla reference mesh and its entity scale once. Do not guess a human height, apply scale twice, or accept a static pose aliased under six action names.

### Audio and voice

Use licensed sourced audio only. Do not synthesize voices, record substitutes, generate tones, or use temporary generic sound as a claimed final asset. Super-events need distinct final cues, each no longer than 120 seconds, with composition and recording rights both resolved, legitimate source, actual listening review, clean edit, and settings-aware playback.

Unit voice selection follows the actual country tag or original-tag routing. Do not promise that four subunits in the same country can each have an independent voicebank when the engine routes voices by country. Existing appropriate country voices can remain an intentional design choice after inspection. A genuinely new carrier may require a complete sourced bank. Distinct per-entity footsteps or weapon sounds need verified action and sound consumers.

### Presentation acceptance

The final implementation needs native-size asset previews, contact sheets, complete flag identity coverage, correct report framing, readable icons, actual in-game counters, and animation state proof. Inspect category layouts in a peaceful country, a high-polarization country, a civil war, a member of each bloc, and the terminal scenario. A static design document does not prove the UI, models, sounds, or portraits have been produced.
