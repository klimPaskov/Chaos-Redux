# 84 · The Peace Movement
## Part 13 · Presentation, narrative direction, and asset requirements

### Presentation choice

Use ordinary decision categories with the two requested public values, current institutional posture, and concise mission status.
This is sufficient for the player's primary choices.
No custom draggable window, bespoke map puzzle, new public currency, or animated meter is justified for the core design.

There are three mutually contextual category states: public movement and negotiations, constitutional or civil-war crisis, and international/world-order commitments.
They share a consistent static category illustration family and never produce more than six primary visible actions or three active visible missions.
Character cases appear through reports and the currently relevant case action.
Existing shared map modes can support food, migration, or territorial context without an Event 84 map mode duplicating them.

A static category picture is permitted; its dimensions come from the inspected consumer.

### What the player should immediately understand

The country can have high Influence and high Reaction simultaneously.
The public summary should show both independently, describe the current political institution, and identify the next material objective.
A trend line names at most the three largest current causes and the next consequential threshold.
It must not claim a guaranteed future government change because a meter is rising.

Tooltips distinguish percentage points from relative percentages, a maintained institutional total from a temporary modifier, selected contact weights from actual event-roll probabilities, and one-time commitments from daily expenditure.
A demographic tooltip explicitly identifies the chosen population policy.
The wording must never imply that sexual orientation inherently reduces population or military skill.

### Narrative register

The opening is curious, intimate, and politically inconvenient.
Cultural disputes can have wit and irony, especially when official bureaucracy tries to classify a philosophy of personal freedom.
Repression, coerced disclosure, and civilian harm use serious, clear language.
The joke belongs to the institution's contradiction or the government's hypocrisy, not to queer people, religious communities, or victims of violence.

The later international story is grander but remains grounded in campaign facts.
The movement's claim to peace sits beside the material decisions required to defend itself.
Traditionalist arguments vary by country and route.
A constitutional conservative's speech should not sound identical to an emergency junta's threat.
The Lotus order should sound like a fictional disciplined institution, not a caricature of all Hindu or Buddhist practice.

### Localisation scope and research gate

All labels in this package are working design labels unless the user's brief named them.
Final localisation is written during implementation after the necessary research; an internal label never stands in for finished text.

Narrative contexts include origin and foreign arrival, the five government responses, each civic negotiation, the three evolutions, character affiliations and case outcomes, seven peaceful or coercive transfer routes plus civil-war victory, each constitution, both bloc congresses, civil-war settlement types, world-end activation and victory, reversal, and all four manual intensities.
Each context needs correct actor, tense, authority, and outcome conditions rather than one generic paragraph reused everywhere.

Reports must support at least first-person government and observer news perspectives where the event warrants both.
A public broadcast does not expose confidential case details before the case is resolved.
A sourced real person's identity cannot be changed by a dynamic placeholder meant for a fictional official.

### Scene families and visual escalation

The opening shows small civilian gatherings, relief work, legal correspondence, a university-like public venue where locally justified, or a port receiving new ideas.
Love and Liberation adds larger public festivals, legal-equality campaigning, communal buildings, and visible civic institutions.
The Reaction adds rival meetings, national or religious institutions, cabinet negotiations, and official restrictions.
Armed Peace introduces actual training grounds, guarded relief centers, civilian command meetings, and sober defensive positions.

Global scenes show real political space: a multinational congress, delegations crossing a ruined frontier, crowded streets after a settlement, or an industrial city whose civilian and military life has changed.
Super-event art should depict a scene with people, architecture, depth, and a clear focal point.
It must not collapse into an abstract rainbow poster or a collage of unrelated religious symbols.

### Assets

The [asset register](084_the_peace_movement_asset_register.md) is the canonical list of scenes, icons, identities, portraits, counters, and native sizes.
The [3D model briefs](084_the_peace_movement_3d_model_briefs.md) own the four unit models and their actions.
Presentation rules that shape the design are kept here.

Existing countries keep their current flags until an actual constitutional identity change requires a new one.
The movement's identity directions need country-specific coherent variants, not one identical universal flag with a country name printed on it.
The familiar rainbow flag is an intentional alternate-history invention in this setting, not documented 1936 heraldry.

Fictional officials are suitable for unsupported private-life cases and caretaker roles.
They need a coherent adult age, role, country context, and persistence.
They are not generic substitutes for available real historical leaders.
An institution can sometimes be shown through a seal or meeting-room scene rather than inventing a leader who is never used mechanically.
Grounded portraits follow the portrait creator's workflow; the user alone produces their final HOI4-style versions.

Audio is licensed sourced audio only.
Each super-event has its own cue of at most 120 seconds.
Unit voices follow country-tag routing, so four subunits in one country do not get four independent voicebanks.
