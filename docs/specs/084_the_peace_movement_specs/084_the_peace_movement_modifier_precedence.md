# 84 · Modifier precedence and nonstacking contract

## One calculation, three visible spirits

This file resolves overlapping route and wartime language in the specification.
It is the authoritative composition rule for authored modifier totals, pending verification of the actual supported engine scopes.
The implementation does not get to choose a stronger stack because several descriptive paragraphs mention the same institution.

Start with the Influence-band totals in Part 2.
Add the Reaction Stability contribution and the separate genuine-polarization penalty.
Then apply only the current lawful constitution's maintained effects, then the active defense or terminal mandate.
Finish by distributing the resulting totals across Social settlement, Civilian economy, and Service and command.
No earlier tier or obsolete route spirit remains active.

## Recovery never worsens a lower band

A replacement recovery value is a limit on the severity of an existing Event 84 penalty, not a compulsory new penalty.
For negative War Support, output, and recruitable-factor contributions, use the less negative of the current Influence baseline and the eligible recovery value.
For a positive training-time penalty, use the smaller of the baseline and the eligible recovery value.
At Influence zero, a recovery program cannot create a penalty that was absent.

A republic's completed service program, an ordinary wartime defense program, and terminal integration are alternatives for overlapping recovery fields.
Use the strongest currently valid recovery once.
Do not add them together.
Terminal integration requires its funded completed mission, not merely the terminal flag.

## Maintained route effects

| Route | Additional rule |
|---|---|
| Pluralist republic | Apply its service recovery totals only after the program succeeds. A maintained constitutional settlement reduces the effective Reaction tier used for the Stability penalty by one, to a minimum of zero penalty. It does not alter stored Reaction, civil-war thresholds, or political organization |
| Communal commonwealth | Add +10% civilian construction after the supply charter. Recover up to 10 percentage points of the current Event 84 military-output penalty, stopping at zero. This recovery never creates a positive military-output bonus |
| Spiritual council | Add +10 percentage points of Stability while its pluralist nonviolent civic settlement is maintained. After the funded support project, one new limited Lotus cohort trains in 90 rather than 120 days. Consume that cohort benefit once, without changing equipment or manpower costs |
| Constitutional conservation | Recover up to 10 percentage points of the current War Support penalty and 10 of the recruitable-factor penalty, stopping at zero, while the service bargain is maintained |
| National discipline | Recover up to 30 percentage points of the current military-output penalty and 20 of the War Support penalty, stopping at zero, only after its discipline program. Apply its stated coercive Stability cost and obligations separately |
| Religious-national or secular civic compact | Benefits come from the completed selected relief and service objectives. Do not add an undefined blanket religion or military modifier |
| Negotiated mixed settlement | Apply the actually agreed reforms and service terms. No automatic full movement-route recovery or cosmetic-government bonus |

Apply specific capped route recoveries after the selected ordinary/terminal recovery floor.
For example, a commonwealth with terminal military-output contribution −5% can recover it to zero, not to +5%.
A military recovery never automatically removes consumer-goods demand, restores offensive-war willingness, or changes the demographic policy.

## Other temporary contributions

A successfully delivered co-optation project's +5 percentage-point Stability contribution lasts 180 days and is folded into the social spirit.
It is awarded once per actual organization/project and cannot stack with another representation of that same project.
Actual violent street conflict suspends the movement's civilian-construction bonus while it persists.
It does not revoke rights or erase completed buildings.

The additional high-Influence/high-Reaction polarization penalty applies only during unresolved institutional confrontation.
A completed and maintained settlement can remove that temporary confrontation penalty while peaceful organizations remain numerically strong.
Apply native final Stability and War Support limits through the verified game implementation, without changing the independently stored political values.

## Demographic and unit separation

Explicit demographic policy changes positive future growth only.
It is independent of recruitment recovery and sexual orientation.
Unit-specific organization, recovery, defense, and training belong to the actual supported unit or cohort consumer.
Do not fold them into a global country modifier that buffs unrelated armor, ships, or foreign units.

The implementation audit must show representative calculated totals for all five Influence bands, all five Reaction bands, each route, peacetime, defensive mandate, terminal integration, and an expired agreement.
A scenario or test report may enumerate these combinations.
The game need not display dozens of separate spirits to represent them.
