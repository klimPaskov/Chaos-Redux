# 84 · The Peace Movement
## Part 11 · Manual scenario: War for the Soul

### Purpose and entry

War for the Soul starts the mature ideological conflict immediately. It is selected through the shared manual-scenario controls and registered as its own scenario entry. No exact registry ID is reserved here because the complete live registry and collision audit were not supplied. The scenario name and Event 84 linkage are fixed.

The manual setup bypasses ordinary opening, transmission, and evolution waiting requirements. It declares a mature political starting history and initializes both blocs, movement cells in neutral countries, divided officials, Rainbow Militias, and Lotus Ascendants. It also starts an immediate diplomatic crisis or an explicitly configured war. It does not bypass structural map validity, player consent to their assigned government, compatible terminal ownership, or real country-package requirements.

The launch view must show intensity, proposed aligned countries, the player role, initial Chaos grant, likely military allocations, and whether the opening is a crisis or war. Launching the scenario commits these effects once. Merely moving the slider changes no country, population, equipment, or flag.

### Country selection and coalition balance

Select valid existing sovereign countries that can support functioning authority. Distribute meaningful population, industry, ports, and existing military strength across both sides. Include at least one major power or player country in each bloc where the map allows. Do not classify all countries of one religion or culture into a single camp.

Prefer a coherent strategic frontier or two understandable theaters over scattered arbitrary civil-war fragments. Some states remain neutral with active cells so diplomacy and political spread still matter. The origin player may lead the movement, lead the traditionalist response, or remain a neutral government trying to contain polarization. In multiplayer, collect each affected human's valid alignment choice before committing a transformation or use an explicitly agreed preset.

The default scenario preview aims to place movement-led governments below 45% of the living world population. That keeps the terminal majority as a campaign objective. This is a setup target, not a secret alteration of population or a universal refusal to launch. If the available map or chosen player assignments make the target impossible, show the real share and warn that terminal conditions may be close. Do not deliberately divide a country or kill population to meet the target.

### Material initialization

Political values and completed institutions are declared scenario setup grants. Initial forces are formed by reallocating suitable existing formations and available manpower and equipment first. For each coalition, record the pre-setup force and stockpile totals, conversions, actual recruitment reservations, and any explicitly enabled scenario equipment grant. No hidden manpower or population creation is allowed.

The default scenario does not conjure extra ships, aircraft, armor, research slots, or advanced technologies. Existing forces and knowledge survive the governmental changes. A shortage produces an understrength training cohort and a resupply objective rather than a fictional full-strength army. Where the mature setup cannot field even the required limited militia and Lotus presence anywhere in the bloc, reduce the target distribution to a capable member. If no member can support it, report a structural setup failure instead of presenting the scenario as complete.

An optional symmetric equipment-grant mode may be offered only as an explicit scenario setting after the actual scenario framework supports it. Its grant budget must be displayed, equal in material value across the blocs, and recorded separately from inherited assets. It is not part of the default specification and cannot be silently enabled to hide force-allocation bugs.

### Mature history without fake ongoing costs

Initialize the three evolution stages as scenario-established history. The setup states that prior disputes produced the current armed organizations. It does not fabricate detailed historical atrocities involving real people or replay opening broadcasts for every country. Constitutions, commands, and bloc commitments are established consistently with the selected intensity and route.

Apply only one root episode identity and one scenario setup ledger. The ordinary random Event 84 opening is consumed so the same movement is not founded again later. The manual Chaos addition is not followed by automatic retrospective Chaos awards for every seeded organization, congress, and army. Future actual developments use the ordinary milestone rules.

### Immediate crisis and war

Low begins with a dispute over protected organizers and a safe-passage agreement. Medium adds funding restrictions, mobilization, and a contested border or extradition crisis. High begins with a threatened member and a command deadline, making intervention likely but still leaving a meaningful diplomatic exit. Maximum begins with an actual declared conflict between valid opposing authorities, using the project's supported war setup and preserving unrelated current wars.

An initial war must have a reachable theater, valid opposing factions, and declared political aims. It cannot be a war between a country and itself, between two members of the same unresolved faction, or against a nonexistent tag. Existing allies are reconciled by a clear prelaunch choice rather than silently expelled.

### Scenario objectives and endings

The movement side can win domestic legitimacy, protect allies, reach the population majority, and pursue The Gay World. The traditionalist side can preserve member governments, break hostile intervention, restore service capacity, and negotiate or impose its own settlement. A neutral player can survive, mediate the frontier, protect civilians, and maintain independence without being forced into either bloc by score alone.

Each intensity uses the same underlying objectives and constraints. Higher intensity changes starting scale, polarization, resources already organized, and diplomatic urgency. It does not remove the requirement to train forces, conserve assets, settle institutions, or satisfy world-end gates.

### Relaunch, removal, and save continuity

The same scenario cannot be launched again while its Event 84 episode or terminal branch is active. A preview canceled before launch has no side effects. A launch interrupted by a validation failure must leave either the unchanged prelaunch state or a clearly recoverable committed setup, never half a dozen transformed countries with no blocs.

Disabling future random Event 84 appearances does not dissolve an already running manual scenario. Disabling the public world-end branch prevents automatic terminal activation but does not erase existing organizations or stop the ideological war. Save and reload preserve selected governments, intensity, allocations, treaty commitments, and crisis deadlines.
