# 84 · The Peace Movement
## Part 6 · Government transfer, political identities, and country packages

### Political authority and popular organization are different

Influence measures the movement's organizing strength and political reach. It does not grant the legal powers of government. A country becomes movement-led only after a completed transfer of authority. A country becomes movement-dominated for the world-end population test only when Influence is at least 80 and the movement also controls the executive or holds an enforceable governing-majority agreement for at least 60 days. A popular movement under an actively opposing government does not qualify.

Traditionalist-led status similarly requires a real government or governing authority, not simply high Reaction. A high-Reaction constitutional government can remain neutral or negotiate a domestic settlement. Military and civilian institutions should carry their existing history into the transfer. A new flag is the visible consequence of an established constitution, not a substitute for one.

### Peaceful and coercive transfer routes

| Route | Minimum political basis | Decisive objective | Main failure outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Election | Influence at least 60, a valid election or constitutional vote, legal participation | Win the actual event-defined governing mandate and form a cabinet accepted by the constitutional authority | Remain legal opposition, accept a coalition offer, or contest a documented procedural violation |
| Parliamentary coalition | Influence at least 60 and a negotiating government | Deliver a signed governing program and sustain it for 60 days | Coalition collapse returns lawful opposition rather than automatically starting war |
| Leader conversion | A living leader voluntarily adopts the program and has governing authority | Obtain cabinet and command acceptance of the new program | Cabinet revolt, caretaker government, or a command-rupture mission |
| Mass strike | Influence at least 60 and functioning organized workplaces | Sustain a 90-day political strike mission while maintaining civilian relief and a negotiating delegation | Partial settlement, exhaustion, loss of public support, or repression aftermath |
| Resignation | Existing government cannot sustain authority and has accepted a transition | Establish a caretaker cabinet and complete a 60-day constitutional convention | Rival claimants or prolonged caretaker rule, without granting both sides duplicate national assets |
| Military refusal | A concrete military command refuses an unlawful or contested order | Secure a lawful replacement authority or a negotiated neutral command | A faction of the military defects and can trigger a valid civil war |
| Revolution | Influence at least 80, failed peaceful authority transfer, organized seizure of institutions | Hold the capital and enough administrative anchors to establish an authority during a 90-day crisis | Failed uprising or a territorial civil war when both sides can actually exist |
| Civil-war victory | A movement belligerent exists and survives | Defeat the opposing authority and complete the postwar constitutional settlement | Exile, demobilization, or surviving territorial government under a ceasefire |

Baseline political takeovers do not require the evolution thresholds. Love and Liberation expands the program and identity options. Armed Peace expands military choices. Neither is a prerequisite for a peaceful cabinet accepting basic antiwar and equality reforms.

### Shared constitutional convention

A movement takeover opens one convention lasting 120 days. The authority must settle civil rights, service obligations, economic administration, family autonomy, religious freedom, and local representation. Three explicit core commitments establish a legitimate movement government: protection of personal and religious freedom, a civilian command authority, and a lawful way to oppose the government. Abandoning those commitments leads to a coercive movement-government variant, not a secret free stability upgrade.

The convention offers three main institutional paths and a negotiated mixed settlement. They share early legal and relief work, then diverge. A country can preserve its existing short name or adopt an appropriate cosmetic identity. No path implies that all citizens share the governing coalition's religion, orientation, or preferred family structure.

### Movement path A · Pluralist republic

The Free Soul Republic route places rights, representative government, and civilian oversight at the center. Its late civic settlement reduces the unresolved Reaction contribution by one band while the agreement is maintained, without actually deleting organized opposition or lowering its stored value by fiat. Its military program supports alternative service, professional defense, and a limited volunteer reserve.

A completed route can replace the dominant Influence service penalties with an accountable-defense variant: War Support −20 percentage points, mobilization speed −20%, training time +10%, and recruitable-population factor −15%. These totals remain meaningful handicaps compared with a fully militarized state. Defensive preparation can be competent without turning universal mobilization into a civic ideal.

Its principal international reward is a guarantees-and-asylum compact. Signatories owe protection and declared resources rather than automatic faction membership. Its principal risk is slow coalition decision-making when a foreign crisis demands immediate action. A divided parliament can extend a nonurgent mission by 30 days rather than simply erasing its democratic institutions.

### Movement path B · Communal commonwealth

The Rainbow Commonwealth or Communal State route emphasizes local assemblies, civilian cooperatives, shared housing, relief, and workplace control. It can improve civilian construction by another 10% and replace 10 percentage points of the movement's military-output penalty after a completed cooperative-production program. This is a route-level total adjustment folded into the existing economy spirit, not a fourth spirit or free factories.

The country must keep supply, wages, and industrial coordination functioning during decentralization. A 150-day supply-and-charter mission requires functioning civilian production and connected controlled population centers. Success establishes the cooperative institutions. Partial success preserves local experiments but gives no national production adjustment. Failure produces a strike or regional autonomy dispute.

Family policy is chosen independently. A communal government can support parents, defend voluntary childlessness, or pursue an explicit anti-natalist program. Childcare and housing consume real resources. The route's military strength is local defense, rapid supported militia recruitment, and resistance to hostile occupation. Its weakness is coordinating a large expeditionary army across distant theaters.

### Movement path C · Spiritual council

The Spiritual Liberation Council is the governing institution, while the country may use a shorter map name such as a Free Republic or Commonwealth. The route protects multiple forms of worship and maintains secular civic rights. It supports meditation centers, voluntary service, spiritual charities, and the fictional Lotus order. A monopoly religion imposed by the council would violate this route's pluralist settlement and create opposition.

Its completed civilian institutions offer an additional +10 percentage points of Stability during a maintained nonviolent settlement and 25% shorter training time for one limited Lotus cohort after a completed medical-and-training support project, not supernatural healing. The military expression is higher organization and recovery for properly trained Lotus formations. Ordinary divisions do not become spiritual super-soldiers because the cabinet has a new portrait.

Its difficult choice is whether an armed order answers to elected civilian authority. Civilian integration makes elite recruitment slower but removes the independent-armed-group Reaction contribution. Independent privileges accelerate one recruitment cohort and leave the political problem active. A schismatic order can refuse integration or leave the country, carrying only its own surviving personnel and equipment.

### Negotiated mixed settlement

A government can adopt a rights-and-service agreement without becoming movement-led. It retains its national identity, principal tree, and existing ruling structure while implementing the particular law changes and civic institutions it accepted. The movement becomes legal opposition or a limited coalition partner. This is a full outcome with a 180-day maintenance objective, foreign-refuge policy, veterans' reintegration, and a final assessment of military readiness.

A successful mixed settlement reduces confrontation and removes temporary crisis penalties. It does not silently abolish equal rights when Influence falls. Its success can qualify for difficult reconciliation achievements, but it does not count as movement dominance for the terminal population threshold unless the separate governing-majority criterion is met.

### Traditionalist path A · Constitutional conservation

This government protects religious and national institutions through elections, public argument, family support, and accountable policing. It may oppose anti-natalism and foreign interference while accepting legal equality and conscientious objection. Its settlement keeps independent courts and permits peaceful movement organizations.

Its completed readiness program can remove up to 20 percentage points of the movement-imposed mobilization-speed penalty and 10 percentage points of its recruitable-factor penalty when an actual service compromise is maintained. The movement's existence and equality laws persist. A country cannot receive the complete military recovery while simultaneously ordering mass persecution that reignites the service crisis.

### Traditionalist path B · National discipline

This route concentrates executive and military authority, restricts foreign political funding, and restores mobilization. It can replace up to 30 percentage points of the movement's military-output penalty and 30 percentage points of its mobilization penalty after a successful 180-day discipline program. These are recoveries of the Event 84 burden, not uncapped positive bonuses on top of an unaffected army.

The price is a continuing political security obligation, a −10 percentage-point Stability contribution while coercive emergency measures remain, and potential underground organizing. Success can suppress organizations, but military readiness does not prove that the population has abandoned their beliefs. A failed discipline campaign can cause command splits or an opposition-government uprising.

### Traditionalist path C · Religious-national compact

This route coordinates actual local religious and national institutions through a negotiated governing compact. The available institutional language must fit the country and be researched. It can sponsor family support, charities, chaplain services, and civic continuity. A secular equivalent uses veterans, civil associations, and cultural institutions rather than inventing a state religion.

The route's productive version funds voluntary institutions and accepts lawful dissent. Its coercive version attempts moral policing and forced restoration. Those choices change the government's stability, diplomatic obligations, and opposition, not the supposed moral worth of an entire religion. An international compact can include countries with different faiths through common diplomatic commitments without pretending they share theology.

### Country creation policy

Existing countries normally keep their historical tag and receive cosmetic identities only after a valid institutional change. A civil war uses an appropriate engine-supported civil-war country or an audited reserved carrier. Do not assign a permanent new tag in this planning package before the required vanilla, repository, Workshop, and local-mod collision audit. A missing collision audit is an implementation blocker, not permission to invent a three-letter tag.

The origin is not guaranteed to become the global leader. Leadership can pass through a congress to the country with the most credible support, not simply the largest starting army. No fixed extra country named after the whole movement is required. A transnational organization is represented as a bloc institution until it controls real territory and legitimately forms a state.

### Country-package completeness

| Package aspect | Existing-country transformation | Event-created civil-war authority |
|---|---|---|
| Territory and population | Preserve current borders and living population unless a separate treaty or war changes them | Partition actual controlled states and their current inhabitants, never duplicate population |
| Name and flag | Preserve current identity or apply the chosen constitutional cosmetic with its approved generated flag package | Use a locally recognizable claimant identity with complete ideology and size variants |
| Leader and cabinet | Preserve eligible people, resolve their allegiance, appoint supported or explicitly fictional replacements where necessary | Allocate surviving eligible characters once, then use a fictional caretaker where the role genuinely lacks a candidate |
| Portraits | Preserve existing assets where identity is unchanged, source grounded new role portraits through the portrait specialist | Supply leader and adviser assets separately when both roles are present |
| Army and command | Preserve deployed units and equipment, apply actual demobilization or training projects | Divide surviving formations, commanders, stockpiles, and manpower through the validated civil-war model |
| Navy and air force | Preserve ownership and basing until lawful transfer | Split whole existing assets or leave them neutral pending agreement, with a recorded conservation result |
| Industry and supply | Preserve existing buildings and damage, commit resources to projects | Inherit the buildings in controlled territory, establish an actual supply capital, and retain damage |
| Technologies and doctrines | Preserve researched content and slots | Inherit the appropriate parent's knowledge without duplication rewards or free extra slots |
| Diplomacy | Preserve treaties until a valid notice, crisis, or agreement changes them | Resolve old wars, guarantees, subjects, access, and sponsor obligations explicitly |
| Focus content | Keep meaningful national content and add the Event 84 crisis branch | Provide a complete compact governing tree plus inherited generic essentials where appropriate |
| Laws and civilian systems | Ordinary human laws, demography, famine, migration, and civilian-loss accounting remain | The new authority is an ordinary human polity, never a nonhuman exception |
| Recovery and release | Reversal restores a valid current identity, not a dead ruler or obsolete ideology snapshot | Defeat, merger, independence, exile, and peace-conference handling each have a defined exit |

The coding handoff must turn this matrix into a per-country implementation coverage table. Unfinished leader, tree, flag, startup unit, diplomacy, or technology coverage blocks release of that country package.

### Sovereignty, subjects, and faction commitments

A subject can have a strong movement and even a movement-led cabinet, but remains a subject until it legally or militarily changes that status. It cannot independently sign a world-security treaty, create an external faction, or distribute its overlord's military resources. Its available political goal may be autonomy, an agreed service exception, negotiated independence, or a supported uprising.

An existing faction membership is not silently replaced by a movement cosmetic. Joining either International requires an explicit diplomatic resolution: a permitted exit, a leadership agreement, a negotiated federation of allied states, or an acknowledged crisis. Member states already fighting together must not be randomly separated into hostile factions by a presentation event.

### Reversal and continuity

A movement government can lose an election or coalition vote without a civil war. A traditionalist government can moderate its program. A defeated militia can demobilize into civilian life. Preserve permanent legal reforms unless a subsequent explicit policy actually changes them. Restore offices according to living available characters and current political authority, not a copied opening-day cabinet.

A country that reverses alignment keeps its actual equipment, buildings, casualties, and population. Route rewards tied to maintained institutions expire or transform when those institutions end. Completed infrastructure remains real. A reversal cannot mint a second constitution reward, a new stockpile, or another set of achievement milestones.
