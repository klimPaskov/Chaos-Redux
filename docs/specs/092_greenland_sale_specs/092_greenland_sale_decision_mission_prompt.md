## Auction phases

The no-first-bid opening window then lasts 30 days.
The first accepted funded bid starts a fresh 240-hour closing period.
Every higher accepted bid by a different country restarts the full period and refunds the previous actual escrow once.
A leader cannot bid against itself or withdraw voluntarily.
Rejected, stale, unaffordable, late, or invalid offers do not change money or the clock.

At the deadline, validate the complete lot, current participants, ownership, control, required permissions, protected remainder, and paid principal.
A successful closing transfers the whole lot and credits exactly the paid escrow once.
A terminal cancellation returns only still-refundable value to its rightful live owner or follows the explicit succession/loss rule.
A no-bid outcome keeps the territory with the seller.
Do not revive old lower bids whose escrow has already been refunded.
