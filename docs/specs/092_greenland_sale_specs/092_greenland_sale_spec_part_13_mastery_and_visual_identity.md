# Greenland Sale: mastery and visual identity

## A recognizable market

The visual identity combines territorial maps, official contracts, industrial backing, and the unusual scale of the object being sold.
The founding art should make Greenland recognizable without inserting an assumed Danish seller or American winner.
Later market art should broaden to ports, connected regions, and a politically charged border handover.
The images support the actual phase and consequence rather than imply a historical transaction took place.

The category picture remains a compact, static identity for the auction system.
Bid, allocation, listing, construction redemption, and following a lot each need a legible action role at native icon size.
Industrial Reserve needs a distinct cost icon so that its payments are never mistaken for political power or the native equipment-market bank.
A currency icon must communicate backed industrial purchasing power rather than a free pile of ordinary money.

Two report illustrations cover the initial offer and the seller's productive reinvestment aftermath.
Three news illustrations distinguish a Greenland transfer, a consequential overseas or strategic transfer, and a homeland border transfer.
The ordinary outbid and no-bid notices can use the auction's established identity without commissioning an image for every possible price or bidder.
The asset prompt supplies the consumer map, required dimensions, processing workflow, and unresolved reference checks.

## Mastery through use, not merely purchase

Achievements should reward the player for making an acquisition useful, turning a sale into recovery, assembling a coherent position, or surviving a costly homeland bargain.
Simply opening the auction, making the first bid, or winning an uncontested lot is insufficient.
The achievements have proposed internal identifiers and working thematic directions.
Their final titles and descriptions require the localisation and asset workflow.

The conditions use actual completed sales, paid receipts, delivered construction, and continuing ownership.
Rejected bids, refunded offers, debug transfers, artificial same-controller opponents, and a sale that never settled cannot satisfy them.
A player's country must be the relevant actor rather than an observer in a world where an AI happened to meet the condition.

## Arctic development mastery

Proposed identifier: `092_greenland_sale_arctic_development`.
The player wins the founding Greenland lot after at least two other independent countries have made genuine, fully funded bids on that lot.
The competitors must not be subjects of the player, the player's overlord, or governments controlled by the same human participant in a way that manufactures the contest.
The achievement does not require particular historical bidders.

After purchase, the player retains ownership and control of every purchased Greenland state for 365 consecutive days.
During that period it completes at least two new useful construction increments in two different building families within the purchased holdings.
At the end, the holdings must include a usable port and a usable airfield.
Those capabilities are not granted by the achievement or by the sale.

The intended mastery is winning meaningful competition and then funding an operational Arctic position despite the acquisition cost.
A fully developed lot can make this achievement unavailable in that particular campaign if it offers no legal further construction.
The implementation must not manufacture empty building levels or credit pre-purchase construction to make the condition universally attainable.
The visual direction is a developed Arctic foothold, with a harbor and airfield motif held together by a restrained purchase-contract element.

## Productive withdrawal mastery

Proposed identifier: `092_greenland_sale_productive_withdrawal`.
The player sells a non-core overseas lot and spends at least 75 percent of that particular sale receipt on completed ordinary construction in retained, owned and controlled home territory within 180 days.
At least one of the receipt-backed completed projects must add a civilian factory.
The player must retain its protected capital and inhabited home through the period.

The receipt is real value received from another funded country.
The construction evidence must identify its actual use rather than credit a factory that another event granted for free.
One unit of spending cannot count toward several unrelated sale receipts.
A smaller sale may not generate enough proceeds to satisfy this achievement, which intentionally encourages a substantial and productive disposition.

The visual direction contrasts a relinquished outlying map parcel with a new working industrial structure at home.
The emphasis is useful recovery rather than a bag of money.
The final description should make the receipt and completed-construction requirements understandable without exposing internal transaction bookkeeping.

## Coherent acquisition mastery

Proposed identifier: `092_greenland_sale_connected_acquisitions`.
The player completes at least two separate purchases from two independent sellers whose acquired lots become part of one connected overseas holding through owned land or a verified map connection.
At least one of the acquired lots must contain a usable port, and the player must retain ownership and control of the purchased states for 180 days after the second qualifying purchase.
The connecting territory may include a possession the player already owned.

Every purchased lot must have been paid for through a valid auction.
The connection cannot run through foreign military access, an unowned enclave, an imagined sea lane, or a state temporarily occupied during a war.
Both sellers must have been independent of the player's subject or overlord relationship at publication and settlement.
The condition measures coherent peaceful acquisition rather than two unrelated bargains on opposite sides of the world.

The visual direction is a pair of differently marked territorial parcels joining into one readable coastal position.
It should remain recognizable at achievement-icon size and avoid a tiny text-heavy map.

## Homeland recovery mastery

Proposed identifier: `092_greenland_sale_homeland_recovery`.
The player completes a homeland sale while the event's severe-pressure assessment genuinely applies at publication, then completes the associated reconstruction mission.
It retains its protected capital and inhabited home for 180 days after the sale.
At the end, its stability is at least the pre-sale level and at least 50 percent.

The reconstruction success must be genuine and cannot come from an externally interrupted or expired mission.
The stability target can be achieved through ordinary political recovery, but the territorial sale alone never grants it.
The achievement therefore combines an actual sacrifice, a useful receipt-backed response, and a durable political recovery.
It does not require the player to provoke a civil war or deliberately destroy its industry.

The visual direction is a damaged national border document beside a repaired civic or industrial symbol.
The image should express a surviving country after a difficult bargain rather than celebrate total national liquidation.

## Shared achievement presentation

Each achievement requires one original colored subject and the project's normal completed, unavailable, and available state treatment.
The grayscale and red-overlay variants must be derived deterministically from the same original subject using the supplied workflow.
They are not three separately generated images with mismatched compositions.
The red overlay and other canonical elements must come from the verified project references during production.

The achievements do not pay additional Industrial Reserve, remove political costs, add free cores, or alter auction odds.
They recognize outcomes rather than provide an economic incentive to manufacture repeated sales.
Their evidence survives save and reload without awarding the same result twice.
The asset and achievement prompts specify the handoff and the validation gates, while these conditions remain the gameplay authority.
