## Main design commitments

Galicia remains a complete opening crisis. Real Portuguese aid can earn a postwar transfer, while victory, defeat, and betrayal have different consequences. The two countries must resolve their relationship before restoring the treaty. The global partition uses actual geography and a disclosed modern game convention, with fixed homeland protection and Galicia as a special settlement.

Regional coring works through coherent projects. The two powers keep their identities, their meaningful country trees, and separate spheres. Restitution respects ownership and consent. The final campaign requires both crowns to demonstrate conquest and integration, and gives the remaining world genuine resistance and negotiation paths.
