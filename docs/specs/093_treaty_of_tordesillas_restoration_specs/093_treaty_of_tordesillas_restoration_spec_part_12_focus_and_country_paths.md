# Treaty of Tordesillas Restoration: focus paths and country packages


## Preserve the country before adding the event

Spain and Portugal keep their meaningful existing trees, current leaders, parties, flags, research, army, and national history. Event 093 adds a focused political and administrative package. It does not replace a Spanish civil-war tree or Portugal's native political routes with a generic conquest ladder.

The implementation must inspect the current trees and use compatible additive branches. A government that cannot logically support imperial restoration may decline the event route without losing its native choices. The same mechanical stage should not be purchasable twice through an event decision and a focus reward.

The core diplomacy and integration rules do not require a particular DLC. Optional volunteer and intelligence support enhance the experience when available. The additive event package must be designed and tested on the declared supported no-DLC configuration rather than relying on an unavailable native focus as a hidden mandatory gate.

All branch names below are design roles, not finalized focus titles. Early preparation focuses generally use the project's 35-day scale, with major institutional commitments at 70 days. Those durations are tuning targets, not a reason to fill the tree with unnecessary nodes.

## Portugal: the price of influence

Portugal's opening event branch begins after the Galicia dispute has a clear political direction. It separates a diplomatic settlement route from a force-preparation route. Both can lead toward a later treaty, but they change the cost of reconciliation and the kind of leverage Portugal brings to the conference.

The diplomatic route strengthens the credibility of compensation, delivery planning, and border recognition. Its useful reward is a better process for fulfilling real obligations, not a focus that makes Spain automatically cede Galicia. A civil-war support side path improves the ability to organize a sustainable package and preserve domestic reserves.

The force-preparation route develops actual readiness for a possible Iberian confrontation. It can support border logistics, training, and mobilization within the existing national system. It does not immediately grant a large army, bypass declarations of war, or transform Galicia into a core. The player pays the strategic cost of preparing for force instead of deepening trust.

The routes converge at the possibility of a recognized settlement. Convergence requires the actual diplomatic or military outcome. Completing a focus cannot manufacture the territorial agreement that the event has not reached.

## Portugal: maritime administration or continental depth

After restoration Portugal chooses an initial expansion emphasis. A maritime-administration path improves the ability to sustain dispersed possessions, organize supply, and use legitimate partner access. Its targets should be selected from the current map and owned territory rather than from a fixed list of historical colonies.

A continental-depth path prepares Portugal for the larger land campaigns its assigned sphere may demand. It emphasizes supply corridors, training, production planning, and coordination with Spain. It does not convert Portugal into a duplicate of the Spanish military package.

The two emphases are an early mutually exclusive investment, not permanent bans on using land or naval forces. Later convergence allows a mature empire to remedy weaknesses through real national investment. The choice should matter during the vulnerable expansion period without making the wrong early choice ruin the whole campaign.

## Spain: sovereignty after Galicia

Spain's event branch begins from its actual post-crisis state. A government that honored the bargain can build a partnership on fulfilled obligations. One that kept Galicia through a recognized settlement emphasizes secure borders and equal sovereignty. A government repairing repudiation must complete restitution before taking the diplomatic benefits of restored trust.

A recovery side path supports the aftermath of civil war or an Iberian conflict. It focuses on restoring damaged owned infrastructure and administration, not creating factories from nowhere in an unowned state. A peaceful Spain cannot farm a civil-war reconstruction package just because the event branch exists.

A treaty-negotiation path establishes the domestic basis for accepting a two-power order. It should address the political cost of treating Portugal as a partner and accepting limits on Spanish expansion. It must not require monarchy or delete a republican identity.

The branch converges with Portugal's restoration only after both governments have actually agreed. Spain cannot annex Portugal through a focus whose visual language resembles treaty cooperation.

## Spain: overseas reach or regional consolidation

Spain's first imperial emphasis separates the capacity to reach distant assigned territory from the ability to consolidate nearer holdings. Overseas reach supports actual basing, escort, supply, and naval preparation. Regional consolidation supports a coherent land campaign and stable administration of acquired territory.

A military-support side path can help Portugal when the Portuguese Sphere is proving harder to develop. Its reward is a real contribution or a verified cooperation effect, not an automatic increase in Portugal's Consolidation. The outcome depends on what the assistance enables on the map.

A Spain already strong at sea can prioritize administration. A land-focused Spain can invest in reach. AI behavior should read those conditions rather than always selecting the historical or aesthetically preferred branch.

## Shared institutional fork

At Evolution III each country chooses between administrative capacity and accelerated administration. The capacity path raises its concurrent project limit from two to three after a substantial institutional investment. The acceleration path retains two slots but reduces eligible project duration by 20 percent, subject to the 60-day minimum and the same admission conditions.

The two benefits are mutually exclusive for that country. This is the main enduring administrative fork. Neither benefit changes sphere entitlement, legal ownership, or local security requirements. A later treaty collapse suspends their treaty-specific project use, while completed native construction or training rewards remain legitimate.

A smaller side path can improve logistical preparation for integration by reducing a specified non-transfer administrative cost or improving a verified supply capability. It must not discount the amount of equipment physically promised to another country. The exact native consumer must be inspected before final implementation.

## The universal doctrine branch

Evolution IV opens a short strategic branch about finishing the assigned sphere and sustaining the partner. It is not another long queue of generic attack bonuses. One route prioritizes remaining independent governments through pressure and coherent campaigns. Another prioritizes restitution, subject settlements, and regional integration needed for a lasting order.

These routes converge at world-end readiness only when both countries meet the actual territorial conditions. A focus cannot substitute for 70 percent reach or 40 percent integration. The final joint consent remains a diplomatic event involving both powers.

There is no hidden route that secretly eliminates the other crown. A unilateral Iberian union belongs to another established system and is treated here as the end of the two-power premise.

## Reward discipline

Prefer access to meaningful actions, bounded improvements to existing logistics, targeted reconstruction, real bilateral assistance, and administrative choices. Where a focus restores infrastructure, its target must be owned, eligible, and not already at the cap. Where a reward involves equipment, the source and actual stockpile result must be proven.

There are no new technology folders or doctrines. A research bonus should be added only when its exact native category, prerequisites, and effect have been verified with the required technology tools. This draft does not assume that a plausible sounding category token exists.

Do not award one permanent spirit per focus. At most three concurrent Event 093 country spirits may be visible, including treaty coordination, the selected administrative policy, and a temporary crisis or reconstruction effect. Related effects should update or consolidate those roles. Most regional benefits should be carried by actual state results.

## Focus acceptance criteria

Both national packages must have meaningful early choice, at least one real cross-branch dependency, a strategic side path, and a convergence that checks the actual diplomatic outcome. Search categories should make diplomacy, logistics, administration, and expansion discoverable. Exact node names, positions, icons, and native prerequisites are implementation work governed by the focus skill.

The focus tree must not hide a mandatory event action behind an unavailable DLC. It must not overlap existing controls or use a large inlay window without a justified need. No focus inlay is required by this design. Native tree rendering, logic comparison, country variants, and save/reload behavior remain mandatory verification gates.
