## Deliberate design exclusions

No automatic Iberian union, new country tags, replacement leaders or national flags, custom technology tree, native raids, 3D units, animated portraits, or decorative full-screen GUI are required. These exclusions preserve the user's two-power treaty identity. They are not hidden missing implementation features.

The focus design is path-level rather than a raw node/coordinate dump. Localization is direction-only except for the user's established names. Regional integration batches meaningful state groups. These follow the supplied planning skill rather than shortening the requested creative scope.
