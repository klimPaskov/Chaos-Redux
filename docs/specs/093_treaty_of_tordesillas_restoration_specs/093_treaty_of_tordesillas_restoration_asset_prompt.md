# Event 093 visual asset handoff

Use Parts 11, 12, 16, and 17 of the Event 093 source package, the source register, and chaos-redux-event-assets.
Read all applicable production rules and the canonical reference library under `.claude/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/` before creating art.
A missing template or consumer is a blocker to final production, not permission to approximate it.

## Scope

Create the six report roles, the Galicia and global-partition news roles, distinct collapse and completed-order aftermath news variants, one world-end scene, the actual implemented focus and decision icon inventory, at most three concurrent country-spirit icon roles, and six complete achievement triplets.
Sizes and narrative roles are in Part 16.
The final focus-icon count follows the accepted native layout, not a speculative quota.

Preserve the existing national flags, leaders, and portraits.
No new portrait, flag, unit model, skeletal animation, or animated category image is required.
Do not generate a substitute historical face or a merged Iberian national flag.
Any new treaty emblem is a neutral event emblem, not a new country identity.

## Research and source modes

Route grounded period-source research through chaosx_asset_source_researcher.
Record source, author or archive, date, rights information, uncertainty, and intended consumer.
Use appropriate original generated non-portrait art through the designated art role where permitted.
Do not call an unsourced generated scene an archival photograph.

The twentieth-century setting matters.
The old treaty can be a document or symbol in a modern diplomatic and military scene.
It is not a reason to dress the current governments as sixteenth-century rulers.
Preserve the distinction between the initial local crisis and the later global claim.

Study the exact canonical report, news, super-event, focus, decision, spirit, category-picture, and achievement families.
Match native dimensions, alpha, framing, scale, and visual language.
Request a real transparent background from the image-generation route for every alpha-backed icon or emblem, verify and preserve alpha through processing and DDS conversion, and record any fallback background removal and edge review under the current asset skill.
Use the visual-component catalog only within its allowed source roles.

## Exact geography

The sphere display, state highlights, Galicia mask, and region eligibility must come from actual installed state geometry.
Do not use image generation or a hand-drawn approximation for the authoritative map.
Coordinate with the implementation/UI owner for real state masks and dynamic qualification.
Decorative background art and a static explanatory legend are separate consumers.

The live map needs distinct sphere treatments, protected homelands, the Galicia exception, inherited disputed holdings, and readable ownership or eligibility information.
It must not imply half-state transfer where the design assigns a whole state.

## Achievement triplets

Use the canonical `achievement_template.png`, `achievement_template_grey.png`, and `overlay.png` at their exact 64-by-64 alignment.
Verify their supplied integrity requirements.
Create completed, grey, and not-eligible sources through the required deterministic template process, preserving the overlay unchanged.
Do not redraw template borders or create a color-shift-only approximation.

All six achievements in Part 17 need distinct, recognizable artwork tied to their actual gameplay.
Store runtime assets under the established `gfx/achievements/` conventions and wire the actual achievement consumers.

## Production and delivery

Use chaosx_generated_event_art and chaosx_icon_artist for their appropriate work.
Parent review must inspect actual output, not merely a filename or generation success message.
No local primitive drawing, placeholder rectangles, unapproved converter, fake DDS extension, or guessed native dimension can stand in for final assets.

Retain source masters, source-mode and prompt records, approved previews, final DDS, dimensions, alpha and header checks, round-trip previews, exact consumer mappings, and provenance.
Validate the report/news/super crops against their consumer dimensions and achievement states at native size.
Check map geometry against live state ownership and qualification.

Deliver an asset manifest and actual specialist handoff under the event's docs/plans folder.
Integrate accepted final asset direction into the source specs only through parent review.
Report blocked references or production stages directly.
