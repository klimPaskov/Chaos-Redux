# Treaty of Tordesillas Restoration: visual assets and super-event direction


## Visual identity

Begin with a small territorial dispute in a recognizable twentieth-century setting.
Desks, border maps, diplomatic folders, supply depots, and contemporary officials should carry the opening.
The older treaty enters through documents and historical symbolism, not by replacing every soldier and statesman with a sixteenth-century costume.

As the event escalates, compositions widen from Galicia and a bilateral conference to competing world maps and the administrative scale of two empires.
The visual irony is the growing mismatch between the age of the treaty and the modern power used to enforce it.

Do not generate or replace portraits of real leaders for this event.
Existing Spain and Portugal identities remain in use.
No new flag is required.
A treaty emblem may use a neutral map divider and paired seals without pretending to be a historical national flag.

## Geography assets are a different class

The sphere display and regional eligibility map must be derived from the installed game's state geometry.
Generated illustration is not acceptable as a factual ownership map.
The overlay must distinguish native ownership, assigned sphere, integration eligibility, protected homelands, and the Galicia exception.

Do not ask an image model to invent the exact shape of Galicia or a state puzzle.
Decorative background art can be generated or sourced through the proper art workflow.
The actual state masks, highlighting, and qualification must use verified map data.

A static legend can explain the meridians and symbols.
It must clearly state that the game convention is a modern reinterpretation and that whole states use the majority-land rule.
The underlying implementation needs visual comparison against actual game state, not only a pretty standalone image.

## Super-event trigger and meaning

The sole required super-event is **Two Crowns, One World**, the user-established world-end identity.
It fires only after both crowns meet the entry conditions, both consent, and the shared world-end registration succeeds.
It does not fire on Evolution IV or at exactly 1000 Chaos.

The image should communicate two distinct powers and one global doctrine.
It should not imply an Iberian union, one crown conquering the other, or the instant disappearance of every independent country.
The final campaign is beginning, so remaining opposition can be present in the composition.

The description direction is the completion of a political transformation: an old bilateral settlement has become the organizing claim of two mature empires.
It should connect to the settled Galicia history where appropriate, without writing different fabricated historical quotations for every route.

The response direction acknowledges the new strategic order.
The planning package does not supply final button text, finished flavor prose, or an invented cultural saying.

## Quote research

The best researched direction is a short passage from the 1494 treaty about peace, agreement, or the return of territory assigned to the other power.
It connects directly to the event's reciprocal logic and can be used ironically against the scale of later conquest.
The source register records a verified short candidate and its location.
[H1](093_treaty_of_tordesillas_restoration_source_and_decision_register.md)

A second direction is the treaty's shared survey and mutually agreed boundary.
A third is the later Asian settlement's effort to resolve overlapping claims.
These are source-based directions, not finished quotations.
The final researcher must verify wording against the chosen edition and retain an accurate attribution.

Do not use the often repeated 'Adam's will' remark or a supposed royal quotation without a primary-source check.
Do not attribute a line from a translator, introduction, or modern historian to a sixteenth-century monarch.
A literary counterpoint such as criticism of imperial ambition can be considered only after verifying the exact work, passage, and translation.

## Audio direction and preliminary candidates

The cue should convey ceremony, confidence, and an increasingly coercive global claim.
A restrained martial or ceremonial instrumental passage is more appropriate than a generic explosion, a fabricated speech, or a synthesized test tone.
Its exact excerpt, duration, and mix depend on the actual chosen recording.

Two real recording pages were inspected as preliminary references: the U.S. Navy Band performances of **A Portuguesa** and **Marcha Real** hosted on Wikimedia Commons.
Their pages distinguish composition and performance information and display public-domain notices.
That is source-page evidence, not an approval of every arrangement, jurisdiction, game use, or excerpt.
[A1](093_treaty_of_tordesillas_restoration_source_and_decision_register.md) [A2](093_treaty_of_tordesillas_restoration_source_and_decision_register.md)

Neither is selected as the final cue.
A purely Portuguese anthem would overweight Portugal.
A purely Spanish royal anthem would be awkward for some non-monarchical Spanish routes and would likewise underrepresent the other crown.
A musical dialogue is a possible editorial direction, but its actual arrangements, compatibility, rights, and dramatic effect require the audio research workflow.

A third research direction is a neutral period ceremonial instrumental recording with no national or regime exclusivity.
No specific recording in that category was verified during this pass.
It remains a research task, not a fabricated recommendation or an uncredited generated composition.

No recording was downloaded, listened to, edited, waveform-inspected, or checked against the repository's music reuse registry here.
The final audio selection, license evidence, source comparison, excerpt timing, loudness treatment, and unique-track check remain production gates.

## Production requirements

Read the canonical asset reference library under `.claude/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/` and its production references before creating assets.
The directions above are not reference-matched final artwork.

Use the designated source-research and asset roles where available.
Create full-resolution masters, review the actual composition, then convert and validate through the approved DDS pipeline.
Check required headers, alpha, dimensions, in-game consumer, and a round-trip image.
Do not create final art through placeholder primitives or silently substitute an unapproved conversion route.

Achievement variants must use the exact required completed, grey, and overlay template process.
Do not redraw template borders by eye.
Store final achievement assets under the established achievement path and wire their actual consumers.

## Explicitly unnecessary asset work

No new leader portraits, national flags, 3D models, skeletal animation, native raid assets, or animated portrait handoffs are part of this design.
No custom focus inlay window is required.
A static category picture is used only where it improves the opening's presentation.

Adding such content later would require a meaningful design reason and the full corresponding production and verification path.
