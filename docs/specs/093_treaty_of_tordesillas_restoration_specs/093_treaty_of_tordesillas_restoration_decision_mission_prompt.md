## Costs and delivery

The displayed quote, affordability check, actual payment, receipt, and refund must agree. Recheck on confirmation. Use the current shared discounted-cost framework only where the exact consumer is supported. Do not treat the overview document as blanket proof.

Aid and compensation physically transfer goods. Integration expenditure consumes goods. Preserve that distinction. A discount cannot create equipment in the recipient, and a native lend-lease or volunteer expense cannot be charged again. A credited delivery must have a proven actual result.

No action has more than four spendable resource components. Most have one to three. Limit inline icons to three and provide the complete transaction in the tooltip. Political consequences such as war-support changes are not incorrectly discounted as prices.

## Missions and completion

The 180-day aid commitment measures signed delivered components. Its success resolves automatically. Early victory uses the 30-day remaining-delivery settlement process. Earned repudiation differs from incomplete-contract renegotiation. A lost recipient cannot receive future goods or transfer territory.

Diplomatic reply windows are not performance missions. Reconciliation takes a genuine non-aggression or restitution period. Military theater tasks need realistic 180-day-or-longer windows. Final-campaign target responses use the proposed 120-day standard.

Regional projects use fixed original enrollment, real ownership and control, per-state security, supply, capacity, and receipt-based cancellation. New conquests cannot join a nearly finished project. Empty or cancelled projects cannot award cores, Confidence, or achievement credit. Demonstrate success, failure, pause, expiry, cancellation, and reload paths separately.

## Ownership and UI

Portugal chooses whether to declare war after refusal. The legal owner consents to territorial transfer. Enemy-owned occupation is not transferable. Core states and protected exceptions are not included in standing automatic restitution. A split subject has one native patron and a real final territorial settlement.

The map and region selector must use actual state geometry and qualification. Coordinate with the UI owner only if a new panel is justified. Run the prescribed inspector and renderer workflows for any added GUI. Source inspection alone is not a visual or click test.
