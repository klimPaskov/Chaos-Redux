Read Parts 02-11, 14-15, and 18 plus chaos-redux-decisions-missions, chaos-redux-events, chaos-redux-map-states, and the actual installed shared-cost contracts.
Part 11 maps the interaction families.
Part 14 is the tuning authority.
This package supplies design behavior, not implementation-ready effect tokens.

## Interaction scope

Implement the Galicia demand, compensation and recognition paths, live civil-war contract, quarter-package installment delivery, postwar settlement, reconciliation, treaty conferences, stage adoption, bounded campaign and external-country approaches, legal restitution, and regional projects.
Include withdrawal, invalid target, suspension, cancellation, and final failure routes.

Keep three to five primary actions per phase and never exceed six.
Use selected targets and relevant active sets instead of a worldwide decision row for every state.
Keep one to three active performance missions apart from the defined project-slot presentation.
Expose no more than the four custom values in Part 01.

## Costs and delivery

The displayed quote, affordability check, actual payment, receipt, and refund must agree.
Recheck on confirmation.
Use the current shared discounted-cost framework only where the exact consumer is supported.
Do not treat the overview document as blanket proof.

Aid and compensation physically transfer goods.
Integration expenditure consumes goods.
Preserve that distinction.
A discount cannot create equipment in the recipient, and a native lend-lease or volunteer expense cannot be charged again.
A credited delivery must have a proven actual result.

No action has more than four spendable resource components.
Most have one to three.
Limit inline icons to three and provide the complete transaction in the tooltip.
Political consequences such as war-support changes are not incorrectly discounted as prices.

## Missions and completion

The 180-day aid commitment measures signed delivered components.
Its success resolves automatically.
Early victory uses the 30-day remaining-delivery settlement process.
Earned repudiation differs from incomplete-contract renegotiation.
A lost recipient cannot receive future goods or transfer territory.

Diplomatic reply windows are not performance missions.
Reconciliation takes a genuine non-aggression or restitution period.
Military theater tasks need realistic 180-day-or-longer windows.
Final-campaign target responses use the proposed 120-day standard.

Regional projects use fixed original enrollment, real ownership and control, per-state security, supply, capacity, and receipt-based cancellation.
Settlement (negotiated or coercive) and pace (ordinary or concentrated) are chosen inside project confirmation; famine pauses, requisition conversion and the Condemnation Tier 3 block follow Part 07.
New conquests cannot join a nearly finished project.
Empty or cancelled projects cannot award cores, Confidence, or achievement credit.
Specify success, failure, pause, expiry, cancellation, and reload paths separately so the user can test each one.

## Ownership and UI

Portugal chooses whether to declare war after refusal.
The legal owner consents to territorial transfer.
Enemy-owned occupation is not transferable.
Core states and protected exceptions are not included in standing automatic restitution.
A split subject has one native patron and a real final territorial settlement.

The map and region selector must use actual state geometry and qualification.
Coordinate with the UI owner only if a new panel is justified.
Use the `chaos-redux-scripted-gui` workflow for any added GUI.
