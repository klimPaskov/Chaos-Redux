## Super-event 1: League formation

Role:

- faction formation
- international institutional reveal
- first durable league created by Event 6 countries

Trigger meaning:

- minimum founding membership exists
- the congress succeeds
- a complete charter is adopted
- leadership is selected
- the league becomes an active diplomatic or military actor

Tone variants to support:

- defensive and legalist
- hopeful and developmental
- sovereign-equality and anti-patron
- armed liberation
- radical revisionist

Image direction:

- fictional 1930s or 1940s congress scene
- strong central charter or assembly moment
- delegates from several small states
- distinct symbols without readable generated text
- no generic world map as the main subject

Approved package:

- title: `The League of New States`
- button: `Small states, one covenant.`
- quote: the short Woodrow Wilson Point XIV excerpt recorded in the research file
- audio: Jeremiah Clarke, `A Trumpet Voluntary`, with the accepted London Brass Players recording and segment held behind the documented `6001` rights blocker

The final text package can vary by charter if the existing super-event system supports dynamic localisation cleanly. Otherwise select one broad package that reflects the first durable league, then use follow-up events for charter tone.

## Super-event 2: Dangerous coordinated revisionism

Role:

- global threat escalation
- declaration that sudden sovereignty has become an organized engine of partition and war

Eligible trigger families:

- radical league adopts an offensive charter and reaches a military and membership threshold
- a high-chaos 20-country wave creates several armed or radical states with synchronized claims
- several Event 6 countries launch coordinated wars against former hosts
- a hidden formable becomes the center of an aggressive bloc
- the league sponsors enough later breakaways to create a visible cascade

Exclusions:

- normal wave
- ordinary league election
- one small regional formable
- weak consultative congress
- routine border war

Image direction:

- fictional period military-political assembly or synchronized mobilization
- clear common bloc identity
- several new-state symbols
- ominous but grounded
- no generic apocalypse image
- no world map as the sole subject

Approved package:

- title: `Every Border a Casus Belli`
- button: `They have sown the wind.`
- quote: Hosea 8:7 KJV as recorded in the research file
- audio: Tchaikovsky, `1812 Overture`, United States Marine Band recording, selected segment and source recorded in the audio file
