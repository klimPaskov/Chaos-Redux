# Independence Wave signature country research dossiers

Research completed: 2026-07-10

These dossiers resolve the design-level historical, institutional, territorial, leadership, and symbol questions for the ten Level 3 packages. They do not replace final asset provenance or the installed-game map rebind. The package resolution matrix records the binding tag, anchor, disposition, and source IDs for every candidate.

## IW-043, Volga Bulgaria

**Resolved tag and release role:** `BQX`, a new Event 6 tag. It is a high-chaos restoration package. Tatarstan, Bashkiria, Chuvashia, Mari El, and Udmurtia retain their own registered or separate packages.

**Modern political bridge:** The opening authority is a Middle Volga congress rooted in autonomous-republic institutions, cultural associations, municipal administration, Islamic institutions, labor, rail and industrial bodies, and regional national delegates. The route can borrow from Idel-Ural federal thought without declaring that every Volga people accepts one national identity.

**Historical route:** Volga Bulgaria appears as a later restoration or proclamation route. UNESCO locates medieval Bolgar south of Kazan near the Volga and Kama and describes it as a site of cultural exchange, historical continuity, and continuing importance to Tatar Muslims. That supports a Bolgar heritage route and a Kazan-Bolgar anchor. It does not justify a medieval government copied into 1936.

**Map resolution:** Reserve `249 Kazan` and optionally `256 Cheboksary`. The package cannot consume a protected host capital. Tatarstan and Chuvashia share `RG-MIDDLE-VOLGA-KAZAN`, so only one of those packages can reserve the same coarse state in one synchronized wave.

**Leadership and institutions:** Use a collective congress for the opening. Real Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, Muslim, secular, labor, and municipal figures can enter through region inserts after source and portrait review. No fictional medieval khan is the baseline leader.

**Symbols:** The automatic opening uses a modern civic seal. Bolgar archaeology, Islamic heritage, and historical motifs can appear on route art only with source notes. No reconstructed medieval flag is presented as settled fact.

## IW-058, Assyria

**Resolved tag and release role:** Reuse registered `ASY`. It is a high-chaos signature package anchored on `676 Mosul` and the Nineveh-Mosul district.

**Identity rule:** Assyrian is the state-level public identity. Assyrian, Chaldean, Syriac, Aramean, church, language, village, urban, and diaspora self-identifications are represented through guarantees, faction and community seats, route names, and local autonomy. The design cannot force every related community into one label.

**Modern political bridge:** A provisional national council combines secular national activists, clergy from relevant churches, village and urban delegates, diaspora links, and veterans of the Assyrian Levies. Recognition, security, settlement, and external guarantees are the opening crisis.

**Map resolution:** Mosul is the compact anchor. Wider claims in northern Iraq, Syria, Turkey, or Iran are never opening territory. They require diplomacy, local support, settlement, or a later federal route.

**Leadership and symbols:** Use an institutional council until period leaders and portraits are fully sourced. Flags, church symbols, Levies symbols, and movement emblems must be attributed to the correct organization or community.

## IW-059, Mesopotamia

**Resolved tag and release role:** `CGX`, a new Event 6 tag. It is a high-chaos civic or federal alternative anchored on `291 Baghdad`.

**Modern political bridge:** The package is a modern Mesopotamian civic federation, not an ancient empire. Baghdad municipal and ministerial administration, the al-Ahali reform milieu, labor, students, river provinces, army officers, tribal and customary authorities where specifically sourced, and minority guarantees compete over the constitution.

**Political routes:** A constitutional river federation, social reform republic, military developmental state, pluralist league state, patron client, and high-chaos ancient-symbol route are available. Ancient imagery never replaces the baseline institutions.

**Map resolution:** Baghdad is the anchor. Mosul, Kurdish regions, Basra, tribal districts, and minority areas require consent, federal settlement, or later expansion. If the current Iraqi host cannot retain a valid state, this package becomes a formable or scenario route instead of deleting the host.

## IW-093, Asante

**Resolved tag and release role:** `DOX`, a new Event 6 tag. It is a high-chaos signature package on `274 Ghana`, with Kumasi required as the capital location.

**Period leadership:** Prempeh II is the period Asantehene and the preferred sourced leader. The 1935 restoration of the Asante Confederacy gives the 1936 package a direct institutional bridge.

**Government structure:** The Asantehene, chiefs and confederated stools, Kumasi administration, a provisional cabinet, schools, merchants, labor, veterans, and surrounding communities form the balance. Traditional authority is neither an absolute monarchy nor decorative flavor.

**Map resolution:** The compact Asante heartland is distinct from Fante and wider Gold Coast routes. `IW-093` and `IW-094` share `RG-GHANA-ASANTE-FANTE`, so a coarse map cannot release both from the same state without a substate split.

**Symbols:** The Golden Stool and royal regalia are source-sensitive symbols. Republican, labor, military, and patron routes use distinct modern flags.

## IW-096, Edo Kingdom of Benin

**Resolved tag and release role:** Use the new Event 6 tag `DRX`. Registered `BIA` remains exclusive to the Biafran regional package. The two packages still share the coarse Nigeria reservation group and cannot both release automatically without distinct current-map substates.

**Period leadership:** Akenzua II, Oba from 1933, is the period leader. His education policy and 1936 campaign for return of looted court objects provide modern state-building and cultural property routes.

**Government structure:** The Oba, hereditary and title-holding councils, court and craft guild institutions, urban administration, schools, merchants, labor, and a constitutional cabinet form the internal system.

**Map resolution:** The baseline public map has one coarse Nigeria state. Automatic release requires a Benin City or Edo substate. Without it, the package remains high-chaos, formable, or scenario content.

**Symbols:** Court art and regalia are not generic decoration. Source every historical symbol and distinguish Edo Benin from the modern Republic of Benin and Dahomey.

## IW-097, Oyo

**Resolved tag and release role:** `DSX`, a new Event 6 tag. It is a high-chaos signature package that needs an Oyo or Ibadan substate inside the coarse Nigeria map group.

**Period leadership:** Ladigbolu I is the period Alaafin. The Oyo Mesi, Ibadan and other urban authorities, military and cavalry institutions, merchants, farmers, labor, and a modern cabinet divide power.

**Government routes:** Constitutional monarchy, stronger Oyo Mesi rule, city federation, civic republic, military oligarchy, patron client, and wider Yoruba federation routes are distinct. Oyo does not stand for all Yoruba identities by default.

**Historical treatment:** Slavery, warfare, and imperial history receive grave and concrete treatment. They are not reward themes.

## IW-098, Sokoto

**Resolved tag and release role:** Reuse registered `SOK`. It can enter the automatic pool when the tag is not living and the current map provides a viable Sokoto anchor.

**Date-aware leadership:** Hasan dan Mu'azu Ahmadu is the Sultan before 17 June 1938. Siddiq Abubakar III succeeds from that date. Ahmadu Bello can appear as a district and council figure, not as Sultan in the 1936 opening.

**Government structure:** The Sultan and kingmakers, emirate relations, Islamic legal and educational institutions, Hausa civic and commercial interests, Fulani dynastic institutions, local administration, veterans, and a federal cabinet form the balance.

**Map resolution:** The coarse Nigeria state requires a Sokoto substate or registered tag core set. The package does not claim all northern Nigeria at release.

## IW-150, Aceh

**Resolved tag and release role:** `FTX`, a new Event 6 tag. It is a high-chaos signature package on `672 Sumatra`, with Banda Aceh required as the capital location.

**Period institutions:** The 1936 authority combines uleebalang, ulama, merchants, district notables, schools, veterans, and port administration. Daud Beureueh can appear through education and religious reform content.

**Chronology rule:** PUSA was founded in 1939, so PUSA organization names and dedicated route content cannot appear in a 1936 opening unless the campaign date has reached 1939. Earlier reform and school networks provide the pre-1939 bridge.

**Map resolution:** Aceh, Minangkabau, and Riau share the coarse Sumatra state. `RG-SUMATRA` permits only one automatic package without current-map substates. The others can emerge later, form, or appear in a scenario variant.

## IW-161, Mon State

**Resolved tag and release role:** `GEX`, a new Event 6 tag. It is a high-chaos signature package that requires a Lower Burma, Tenasserim, or other distinct Mon anchor.

**Period institutions:** Monasteries, language and cultural associations, municipal notables, merchants, river and coastal communities, veterans, and a provisional federal council provide the 1936 bridge.

**Chronology rule:** Post-1947 organizations, Mon National Day institutions, and later armed movements cannot be used in the 1936 opening. They may appear only after the correct date or through alternate-history events that explicitly create them.

**Cross-border rule:** Thai Mon communities are diplomatic, cultural, refugee, or federal links. They are not automatically annexed territory.

## IW-197, Mapuche Federation

**Resolved tag and release role:** `HOX`, a new Event 6 tag. It is a high-chaos signature package. The opening public identity is a Mapuche federation. Wallmapu is reserved for a negotiated formable or route after internal and cross-border settlement.

**Period leadership:** Manuel Aburto Panguilef and the Federación Araucana are direct period anchors. The organization proposed an Indigenous Republic in 1931, held a congress in 1935, and Aburto was repressed in 1936. Herminia Aburto Colihueque supports civic, electoral, archival, and women's organization content. Later groups are date-gated.

**Internal structure:** Mapuche, Lafkenche, Williche, Pehuenche, and other represented territorial identities receive seats, autonomy rules, land and language policy, and distinct economic and military concerns. The state cannot use one undifferentiated council.

**Map resolution:** Use one south-central Chile or current-map Araucanía anchor. Argentine and wider Chilean territories require local support, diplomacy, plebiscites, or a later formable. `IW-197`, Patagonian, and Araucania and Patagonia restoration packages share `RG-SOUTHERN-CONE`.

**Symbols:** Use period organization or territorial-identity symbols only after attribution review. No single ceremonial motif is treated as a universal Mapuche flag.

## Binding outcome

All ten signature packages now have a release role, tag decision, modern political bridge, leadership rule, compact territorial rule, symbol rule, chronology constraints, and source packet. Their final portraits, flags, and exact installed-map state files remain asset and implementation work, not unresearched design.
