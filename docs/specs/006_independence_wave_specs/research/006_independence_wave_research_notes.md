# 006 Independence Wave Research Notes

These notes collect source anchors and source-mode rules for the Event 6 specification. They are design support, not final localisation. They do not approve any historical flag, symbol, leader portrait, quote, or audio file without a dedicated implementation-time source and license check.

## Source application rules

Research should guide state groups, route themes, institutions, symbols, leader direction, unit names, focus insert tone, and asset source mode. It should not become final player-facing prose by itself.

Historical flags, real court emblems, religious symbols, real leader portraits, real regalia, and well-attested ancient symbols require sourced asset work. Generated art is appropriate for fictional, alternate, symbolic, and high-chaos variants only when the asset record marks them as invented or alternate.
