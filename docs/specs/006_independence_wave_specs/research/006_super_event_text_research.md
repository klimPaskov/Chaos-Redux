# Independence Wave super-event text research

Research completed: 2026-07-10

Description verification promoted: 2026-07-14

The two required super-event text packages are approved for implementation. The titles, descriptions, buttons, and quote excerpts below are final research recommendations. The main implementation agent may adjust punctuation or dynamic country names for UI fit, but it should not replace the sourced quote without a new source note.

## Super-event 1, league formation

**Role:** First durable Event 6 league becomes an active institution after a successful congress, founding membership, charter adoption, and leadership selection.

**Final title:** The League of New States

**Final description:**

Delegates from the newest states have ratified a common charter and chosen the league's leadership. The signatories promise arbitration, mutual aid, and collective resistance to any former host or foreign patron that attempts to extinguish a member.

The league can speak for its members, bargain in their name, and coordinate shared aid or mobilization.

**Final button:** Small states, one covenant.

The button is original Event 6 wording and has no external quotation dependency.

**Final quote:** “A general association of nations must be formed under specific covenants…”

**Attribution:** Woodrow Wilson, Fourteen Points, Point XIV, 8 January 1918.

**Source:** Lillian Goldman Law Library, Yale, `https://avalon.law.yale.edu/20th_century/wilson14.asp`.

**Attribution confidence:** High. The source reproduces Point XIV and dates the document. The excerpt is short and public historical text.

**Fit:** The quotation directly joins small and great states through a covenant and mutual guarantees. It fits a first durable league without forcing one charter ideology.

**Backup direction:** If UI space rejects the excerpt, use a shorter excerpt from the same Point XIV. Do not substitute an unsourced quote.

## Super-event 2, dangerous coordinated bloc

**Role:** A radical or militarized Event 6 league converts sovereignty into coordinated claims, ultimatums, sponsorship of breakaways, and synchronized war.

**Final title:** Every Border a Casus Belli

**Final description:**

New-state governments coordinate claims, ultimatums, arms shipments, and mobilization schedules across several regions. Their border commands follow shared timetables, and governments pledge arms or troops to support one another's territorial demands.

Former hosts reinforce several fronts as they prepare for wars that may begin on the same day.

**Final button:** They have sown the wind.

The button is a short allusion to the selected verse. It does not reproduce a modern copyrighted work.

**Final quote:** “For they have sown the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind.”

**Attribution:** Hosea 8:7, King James Version.

**Source:** `https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hosea%208%3A7&version=KJV`.

**Attribution confidence:** High. The page identifies Hosea 8:7 and the King James Version. The KJV is public domain in the United States.

**Fit:** The verse connects deliberate action to an escalated consequence and matches a bloc whose sponsorship and coordinated claims create larger wars.

## Dynamic charter treatment

The league formation super-event uses one broad package so every valid charter can trigger it. Charter-specific tone belongs in the follow-up event and scripted localisation around the league name, leader, membership, and charter. The dangerous package only fires for the offensive or destabilizing thresholds already defined in Part 6.

## Copyright and quotation control

Both selected quotes are short. No modern song lyric, film line, game line, or protected literary passage is used. The descriptions and buttons are original package text. The implementation must preserve attribution in the research documentation even when the in-game quote line uses the compact super-event format.

The 2026-07-14 verification confirmed the selected source wording and attribution. It also recommended ASCII omission marks for font coverage. That punctuation-only recommendation is not promoted here because the approved quote strings are unchanged in this reconciliation.
