# 068 ZIN: achievements

## Achievement ownership

Use the existing root-only Chaos Redux achievement set. Event-owned IDs begin with `068_zin_`. The actual achievement ID, root DDS filename stem and localisation stem stay aligned. Do not create a separate set or assume a native rarity field exists. Common, Epic and Legendary are documented groupings.

The following 30 achievements describe gameplay outcomes. They are proposed, not installed. Final names and text should be written after the condition and art direction are accepted. Registration can be available to any player country while each condition still requires its specific route, origin and real action history.

## Natural and forced progression

Ordinary achievements require the relevant natural origin. Manual scenarios, debug force and setup-completed focuses do not manufacture eligibility. Record the provenance of the affected chain rather than globally disabling unrelated achievements without a reason. Switching to a naturally created succession camp preserves the participating player's history through the explicit transfer receipt.

The player must perform or legitimately inherit the actual recorded action. Country names, tags, ideology and current ownership alone are insufficient when a condition depends on past behavior. The unique ruler and colony instance identities survive valid succession and annexation without resetting historical restrictions.

No achievement rewards a repeated population debit, a fake ship capture, a duplicated army, a mapless Glo bonus or a silently skipped civil war. Political-victory achievements remain separate from terminal-super-event achievements so the explicit Chaos policy issue cannot accidentally invalidate the recorded good campaign.

## Tracking and art

Use direct existing state for simple current conditions and the smallest durable receipt for historical claims. Do not run whole-world scans every day to rediscover a completed treaty or an old player choice. A terminal or one-time achievement commit consumes its own qualifying proof once.

Every achievement needs its eligible, grey and not-eligible icon variants under the verified consumer contract. The original icon is a clear 64 x 64 image with the planned society and action identity. Variant production follows the asset skill and requires native-size review. No icon is produced by this planning package.

The implementation audit must test a positive natural case, a negative near-miss and a forced-setup case for every row. Thresholds, time intervals, actual actor identity and no-farming conditions must be visible in the proof report. A successful file parse does not demonstrate that an achievement fires at the correct moment.
