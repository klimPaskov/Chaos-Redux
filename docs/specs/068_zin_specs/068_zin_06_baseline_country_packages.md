# 068 ZIN: Baseline country packages

These 12 packages use the common settlement framework and retain their own political choices, missions, units, leaders, and AI. The core societies are developed in the dedicated Rush, baseline-core, and greater-powers parts.

Every route label below is an internal planning description. It is not approved focus, event, decision, party, or achievement localisation. New histories and leader biographies are proposed additions to the user's named geography. They are not map transcriptions.

Each three-stage workstream expands into the necessary focus nodes, connected decisions, and visible consequences. The stages normally use 70-day institutional work and 35-day commitment choices. Routes share the founding administration, supply, and Earth-relations spine, but their political commitments are not interchangeable. Mutual exclusions apply when a country accepts incompatible sovereign, patron, or civilian obligations. Every secondary country needs a complete neutral or independent continuation, not only a faction-join button.

Military access, contracts, and alliances remain subject to real routes and existing wars. No country receives another starting army after a merger, succession, patron change, or cosmetic rename. A nominally friendly route cannot ignore a recorded betrayal merely because an ideology value changed.

## Z68-C05: Gunwaith Highland Clans

**Homeland:** `north_earth`. **People:** Human highland clans. **Placement:** Scandinavia, Scotland, Alpine regions, the Rockies, Canada, and comparable highlands.

**Proposed leader:** A clan speaker who organized a joint crossing while promising that no clan would lose its own law.

### Homeland and opening problem

The clans remember the white northern lands of Gunwaith and the smaller inhabited approaches south of them. Their proposed departure dispute concerns winter stores and the right of one clan to close a shared mountain passage.

The settlement has defensible terrain but several competing local commands. Centralizing too quickly risks losing the scouts needed to defend the passes.

### Country package

The initial government uses `neutrality` with a society-specific political institution. The arrival brings 50,000 civilians in addition to its separately accounted military establishment. Its target usable economy is 3 civilian and 3 military factories, counting inherited capacity first. It has 2 research slots and the ordinary early technologies needed to operate its equipment. It does not inherit every Earth technology.

Starting army: 10 × `gunwaith_highlander`. The unit registry owns the templates, physical personnel, replacement rules, and combat targets. The country's identity spirit and one current political condition are the default two ZIN spirit slots. Glo uses the third if acquired.

### Focus route families

**Z68-C05-P1, internal workstream: Confederated defense.** Recognize the clan courts → Create a joint winter store → Ratify a shared defensive command. Unlock cooperative pass defense and a northern league role. Clans retain local law and cannot be sold into a patron pact.

**Z68-C05-P2, internal workstream: Central highland crown.** Choose a single war leader → Standardize levies and supply → Bind the border garrisons. Gain a larger standing army and a direct conquest route after activation, at the cost of local resistance to foreign campaigns.

**Z68-C05-P3, internal workstream: Exile sanctuary.** Admit displaced highland communities → Negotiate Earth protection → Maintain guarded neutrality. Keep a complete neutral route with civilian shelter, mountain defense, and paid allied logistics without compulsory global faction membership.

### Signature mission

**Internal mission direction: Keep the winter passage open.** Duration: 180 days. Required proof: Control the founding state, keep its supply route functioning, and deliver one support-equipment shipment to a named partner. Success: A second highland mustering allotment and a permanent clan agreement. Failure: The affected clan withdraws offensive service until a repaired route is held for 90 days.

### Distinct player actions

| Internal action direction | Cost | Requirement | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Assemble pass guards | 25 CP and 100 support equipment | A threatened controlled mountain state | Commit five existing formations to a 90-day defense obligation, then unlock one replacement allotment. |

| Arbitrate a clan closure | 50 PP | A recorded dispute with one clan | Choose compensation or central enforcement. Compensation preserves the league route, enforcement strengthens the crown and loses local cooperation. |

### AI and presentation

Prefer confederation when a cooperative northern neighbor exists. Prefer centralization after repeated attacks. Never launch a warm-climate expedition while the home winter route is failing.

Highland stonework, winter cloaks, and clan-specific shields. Use Gunwaith geography from the North Earth sheet, not the Cold Lands yeti map.

### Homeland and opening problem

The Trollholds remember the Lothord Fog Mountains and disputes over who could cross them in winter. Their proposed departure followed the collapse of an old toll agreement with neighboring rulers.

The army is extremely strong at home but slow, scarce, and vulnerable to being committed too far away.

### Country package

The initial government uses `neutrality` with a society-specific political institution. The arrival brings 10,000 civilians in addition to its separately accounted military establishment. Its target usable economy is 2 civilian and 2 military factories, counting inherited capacity first. It has 2 research slots and the ordinary early technologies needed to operate its equipment. It does not inherit every Earth technology.

Starting army: 5 × `fog_troll`. The unit registry owns the templates, physical personnel, replacement rules, and combat targets. The country's identity spirit and one current political condition are the default two ZIN spirit slots. Glo uses the third if acquired.

### Focus route families

**Z68-C15-P1, internal workstream: Guarded holds.** Define local passage rights → Build a protected supply compact → Refuse offensive patron levies. Remain neutral and defensively valuable in a northern league.

**Z68-C15-P2, internal workstream: Toll kingdom.** Centralize the holds → Prepare coercive passage demands → Expand along a limited mountain corridor. Open a regional expansion route based on actual connected states after activation.

**Z68-C15-P3, internal workstream: Bound host.** Accept a dark patron's supplies → Commit one host under a written obligation → Submit or break the bond. Gain support with a real loss of independence and a difficult revolt option.

### Signature mission

**Internal mission direction: Hold the agreed pass.** Duration: 180 days. Required proof: Keep a named controlled mountain state supplied and honor its passage agreement. Success: A second replacement allotment and stronger northern diplomatic ties. Failure: The client withdraws support and the affected hold refuses another distant deployment.

### Distinct player actions

| Internal action direction | Cost | Requirement | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Commit a pass host | 25 CP and 100 support equipment | A threatened mountain state and unused troll allotment | Assign the real host to a 90-day defense commitment. |

| Renegotiate passage | 50 PP | A current transit partner and a recorded dispute | Choose a paid neutral settlement or an explicit coercive demand that can provoke war. |

### AI and presentation

Value defensible terrain and sustainable supply over nominal territorial size. Avoid tropical campaigns without a paid and viable logistics agreement.

Heavy fog-weathered trolls with local stone equipment. Keep them distinct from frost trolls and the shadowed Endless host.

## Z68-C16: Eye Mountain Ogreholds

**Homeland:** `north_earth`. **People:** Northern ogres. **Placement:** Mountain states, isolated uplands, and mineral regions.

**Proposed leader:** A clan judge whose personal authority must survive disputes among shock-force chiefs.

### Homeland and opening problem

The Ogreholds remember the Eye Mountains and the need to negotiate access to mineral and trade routes. Their proposed crossing split the clans between service contracts and a new mountain kingdom.

Chiefs want raids, while the settlement needs roads, supplies, and a lawful division of contracts.

### Country package

The initial government uses `neutrality` with a society-specific political institution. The arrival brings 10,000 civilians in addition to its separately accounted military establishment. Its target usable economy is 2 civilian and 3 military factories, counting inherited capacity first. It has 2 research slots and the ordinary early technologies needed to operate its equipment. It does not inherit every Earth technology.

Starting army: 5 × `eye_ogre`, 5 × `ogre_porter`. The unit registry owns the templates, physical personnel, replacement rules, and combat targets. The country's identity spirit and one current political condition are the default two ZIN spirit slots. Glo uses the third if acquired.

### Focus route families

**Z68-C16-P1, internal workstream: Clan service council.** Set rules for military service → Open guarded trade → Join a northern defensive league. Create a neutral or defensive mercenary state with finite ogre deployments.

**Z68-C16-P2, internal workstream: Mountain strongman.** Unify the shock chiefs → Secure neighboring passes → Build a limited ogre realm. Permit regional conquest after activation, with clan disputes when campaigns fail.

**Z68-C16-P3, internal workstream: Anti-demon compact.** Reject a coercive dark levy → Protect a smaller neighbor → Accept a limited good alliance. Allow a northern good route through conduct. It does not make the Ogreholds a Rush dominion.

### Signature mission

**Internal mission direction: Deliver the mountain service.** Duration: 90 days. Required proof: A named host completes a reachable defense or siege contract without abandoning the home supply route. Success: Payment and a clan agreement for another finite host. Failure: A chief contests the ruler and blocks new offensive contracts until arbitration.

### Distinct player actions

| Internal action direction | Cost | Requirement | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Arbitrate a shock-chief dispute | 50 PP | A recorded failed or competing contract | Preserve the service council or strengthen a single ruler with a corresponding political cost. |

| Call the porter companies | 25 CP and 100 support equipment | An actual mountain supply problem | Commit the existing support force to repair and transport duties for 90 days. |

### AI and presentation

Prefer short, reachable contracts. Choose an anti-demon compact only after an actual coercive threat and successful protection pledge.

Broad ogre bodies, heavy carrying frames, and mountain weapons. Do not reuse Hiloron giant anatomy or political symbols.

Arrival news should name the homeland and explain the opening political problem. It must not imply that proposed institutions or biographies were transcribed from the map. Final text and any personal name are authored during the localisation pass.
