# 068 ZIN: evolutions, scenarios and shared consequences

## Progression and evolution are different

A new colony, a Rush charter, the king's death, a supply crisis and a court investigation are ordinary progression inside the persistent event. They are not separate evolution rows. Evolution changes the available society pool or the range of possible political action.

The numeric Chaos gates are those in the brief. The event's catalog Chaos level stays 1. Reaching a threshold makes an ordinary evolution eligible. It does not automatically fire every eligible stage on that day.

| Stage | Chaos gate | Proposed eligible MTTH | New scope |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| I Dangerous Crossings | 200+ | 90 days | Afrit's Worshipdom, Shachihata and thirteen dangerous secondary societies. Broader active policy and patron operations. |
| II Greater Societies | 400+ | 90 days | Sunhot elves, Children of Volgan and nine greater secondary societies. Regional intervention and stronger leagues. |
| III Great Lords | 600+ | 120 days | Direct Volgan and Castle of Yeldenne, with their full unique packages. Large active routes. |
| IV Reckoning | 1000+ | 90 days | The prepared three-way global alignment war, followed eventually by one of three victory branches. |

MTTH values are timing parameters, not guaranteed waits or exact expected calendar dates. Changing conditions can alter eligibility and timing. The implementation probability adapter must establish what the current engine actually does before reporting probabilities.

### Timing modifiers

For the first three stages, an exposed active crossing network or a completed relevant invitation can use a 25 percent shorter eligible timing parameter. A sustained successful containment settlement can use a 25 percent longer parameter for the affected pathway. Apply each source family once, clamp the resulting parameter to at least 30 days and keep the full modifier pool inspectable.

For the Reckoning, a real war between two organized ZIN blocs can shorten the preparation parameter by 25 percent. Missing viable anchors blocks the normal three-way setup. No timing modifier creates those anchors from defeated rulers, disables a player setting or silently adds the required Chaos.

If the global meter falls below an uncommitted stage's threshold, its normal eligibility pauses. Previously admitted societies remain real countries. Their already completed institutions and units are not erased when Chaos falls. Later aggressive actions still check their own current political permission and threat rules.

## Independent evolution controls

Each evolution has its own enabled state and source-owned readiness. Disabled stages must not mark themselves complete, populate their gated society pool or unlock downstream content through a hidden recorded flag.

The pool addition of one stage does not inherently require the previous stage to be enabled. For example, a separately enabled greater-society stage may admit Sunhot after its own requirements while the dangerous-society pool remains disabled. However, the normal Reckoning still needs its real anchors. It cannot ignore a disabled lord pathway by silently spawning Volgan anyway.

The baseline Rush assassination and civil war remain available without Evolution I. Afrit's responsibility is canonical and his covert agents can act before the Worshipdom appears on Earth. If the crown route creates his first physical ruler identity, later Worshipdom content recognizes that same identity. It does not produce a second Afrit.

## One arrival coordinator

The ordinary arrival cadence belongs to one persistent Event 068 coordinator. It is not replicated for every colony. Ten countries with related-crossing branches must not accidentally create ten independent global 180-day arrival loops.

Player and AI crossing requests enter a bounded queue with their source country, requested family, incident generation and funding receipt. The coordinator processes only admitted active requests and its ordinary arrival opportunity. It checks the society cap, available carrier and legal state before committing. A completed focus queues one request, not an unlimited monthly entitlement.

The soft active-colony limits of 15, 20, 25, 35 and 45 for baseline through Reckoning are pressure controls for ordinary new arrivals. They never delete existing colonies, block an indispensable succession government or split a unique identity. Required structural packages must be budgeted before a scenario or large transition begins.

## Four manual scenarios

The source brief says three setup types but lists four named setups. This package retains all four. To fit the existing scenario interface without inventing an extra control hierarchy, register four related scenario entries with stable logical keys. Allocate actual numeric scenario IDs only after inspecting the current registry.

Every scenario uses the existing four-stop intensity control. The launch confirmation reads the current selected type and intensity at confirmation time. It does not reuse values from the first click.

| Logical key | Scenario | Type control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `zin_first_colonies` | First Colonies | Mixed common societies, human-led common societies, or a species-diverse common pool. |
| `zin_empty_throne` | The Empty Throne | Einendil, Afrit, Golden Regency or Free Dominions opening bias. |
| `zin_open_crossings` | Open Crossings | Balanced origins, dispersed settlements or concentrated regional rivalry. |
| `zin_reckoning` | The Reckoning of ZIN | Unusurped Rush or Afrit-held Rush. Both create three real opposing blocs. |

Scenario labels listed in the brief remain intact. New subtype labels are writing directions pending localisation.

### First Colonies

Create 3, 5, 10 or 15 baseline colonies at Low, Medium, High or Maximum intensity. Include the canonical Rush Kingdom only once when selected. Common societies can repeat with distinct instance identities and leaders.

Starting army scale is 75, 100, 125 or 150 percent of the society package. Round only whole formation allocations through the tested stable allocation rule. The scale never depends on the former owner's weakness. Unique heroes and artifacts do not multiply with intensity.

The scenario starts with ordinary neutral postures and contact problems. It does not preselect the good or dark winner, force a succession or award a world-end state. Scenario force scaling is an explicit sandbox exception to the ordinary fixed founding package.

### The Empty Throne

Create the canonical Rush Kingdom and enough genuine admitted dominion settlements for a meaningful civil war. Low and Medium establish three territorial centers, High four, Maximum five, counting the royal capital and admitted outposts. Prepare the actual charter, supply and unit records before assigning camps.

The selected type biases the initial political conditions and makes the selected player's route available. It does not declare that side the winner. Proposed starting values are Unity 75 and Influence 20 for Einendil, Unity 35 and Influence 80 for Afrit, Unity 50 and Influence 35 for the Regency, and Unity 75 and Influence 35 for Free Dominions.

The setup forms the early Lost Cause with one valid external good partner, resolves the mandatory death and opens the real war. Einendil's service is accepted in his type and becomes a live choice where appropriate in other types. His title remains non-royal. Afrit is unique even when a Worshipdom already exists.

The scenario cannot overwrite an already human-controlled incompatible Rush route without consent. If an existing campaign cannot be advanced coherently to this exact crisis, show that conflict instead of resetting its history or cloning the country.

### Open Crossings

Create 10, 15, 25 or 35 colonies by intensity from baseline, Evolution I and Evolution II families. Use a target mix of 50 percent baseline, 30 percent dangerous and 20 percent greater families among the admitted valid candidates. These are scenario allocation targets, not ordinary arrival probabilities.

The mixture is resolved before state transfer, with unique rulers and available carriers checked. Keep diplomacy unresolved except for canonically established Volgan allegiance and any explicit same-origin relationship. Sunhot remains peaceful. The Worshipdom does not receive a free conquered Rush kingdom.

This setup deliberately bypasses the normal Chaos and timing gates for its admitted pool. It does not globally enable those evolutions, grant their automatic history or make all future arrivals ignore normal settings.

### The Reckoning of ZIN

Create 12, 20, 30 or 40 colony countries by intensity, including the three viable faction anchors and a broad selection of supporting families. Required anchors count within those totals. Establish the castle and Glo only when their full structural package is ready. Their inclusion is expected in the normal complete scenario design, not a license to place a placeholder gem modifier without the building.

In the unusurped type, a good Rush government leads The Lost Cause, Afrit leads the Worshipdom and Volgan leads his realm's Earth host. In the usurped type, Afrit holds the Rush crown and Worshipdom while a surviving good anchor leads The Lost Cause. Build the actual surviving or scenario-created dominion obligations to support the chosen state of the world.

The anchors retain their defined military asymmetry. Intensity changes the admitted breadth and applies the explicit 75, 100, 125 or 150 percent ordinary force scale consistently. It does not randomly make the weakest dark power strongest without the Rush acquisition.

Declare the three-way war only after valid country, state, army, faction, access and supply setup. No previous Event 068 progression or current Chaos threshold is required to launch this manual setup. Launch does not set `world_end`. The eventual lower-Chaos terminal result remains subject to the explicit policy issue in the diplomacy document.

### All manual launches

A launch is a coherent transaction. If the complete required set cannot be built, stop before any transfer. Do not create half the requested countries and report the scenario successfully launched. An explicit preview can report the available admissible count before confirmation, but a reduced setup requires a visible accepted change.

Manual bypass markers apply only during the launch transaction and are cleared after success or rollback. Reopening the window, changing intensity or repeating a click cannot replay a committed arrival. A later launch cannot duplicate Afrit, Volgan, Horos, Einendil or the castle.

## Direct event Chaos effects

Use the shared Chaos change owner. Ordinary war, peace, annexation, liberation, faction changes and tracked deaths already contribute through their existing systems. Event 068 adds only specifically authored outcomes that are not counted by those generic operations.

| Event-specific outcome | Proposed direct Chaos change | Receipt |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| First unexplained crossing | +10 | The first successful Event 068 arrival only. |
| Public confirmation of common origin | +5 | Once, unless included in the Reckoning reveal already. |
| Exposed seizure of a sovereign court by Afrit | +15 | One actual usurpation, excluding ordinary civil-war and annexation contributions. |
| A destroyed hostile crossing command | -10 | One real command permanently contained, not a temporary province occupation. |
| Einendil completes a civilian-protection settlement | -10 | One distinct conflict settlement with actual delivered protection or return. |
| Full defeat of a dark faction's Earth projection | -25 | Once per distinct rival faction, not per defeated client tag. |
| Final good political peace | -50 | Once after the full political victory proof, whether terminal commitment is enabled or not. |

These are proposed event values, not numbers copied from the current old ZIN script. They cannot be obtained by staging repeated wars between the same countries, releasing the same command repeatedly or creating and deleting puppet clients. Reductions never restore dead population or erase the historical record of harm.

Do not impose a hidden Chaos floor during the Reckoning. The explicit terminal-eligibility conflict must be resolved through a policy decision, not by undoing the player's successful peace actions.

## Connections with existing systems

| Owning system | ZIN contribution | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event 010 Death | Reactions to an actual Edvoid appearance and geographical references to his ocean or Mistake. | No new Edvoid country, leader or duplicated Death chain. |
| Independence Wave and release coordinator | Reserve state and carrier transactions before settlements or political releases. Preserve existing meaningful country content. | ZIN origin never invokes another event's package merely because it reused a carrier. |
| Shared country classification | Register created countries as special Chaos countries and classify actual nonhuman packages separately. | A human cult, Noris colony or Dondor government is not automatically nonhuman. Mixed countries need a deliberate registered policy. |
| Deaths | Record real living losses and use a verified new-civilian-death receipt for Volgan's bounded grave muster. | No duplicate debit and no undead feedback loop. |
| Migration | Request actual evacuation, reception, return and settlement of existing Earth residents. | ZIN does not subtract and recreate those people itself. External ZIN arrivals need their own proven entry adapter. |
| Famine | Register actual new settlement demand, disrupted supply or promised relief. | A country being an island or inhabited by creatures does not automatically create famine. |
| Air Cleanliness | Fire and devastation operations use an accepted source-specific adapter when they actually produce smoke or contamination. | Mere presence of demons or flame troops never creates arbitrary global pollution. |
| Condemnation and repression | Actual exposed atrocities, unlawful harm and cover-ups can create shared evidence and consequences. | Alignment or species alone is not an atrocity receipt. Do not add a protected-class targeting interface. |
| World threat | Active organized dark projection contributes a source to the shared threat framework. | No parallel global ZIN threat meter, and no baseline universal Earth coalition. |
| Event clusters | ZIN is the Severe member of Alien Invasions and remains a Major. | A cluster consumes one shared pacing event, not one timer transaction for each colony or member. |
| Shared achievements | Track actual actor origin, route and scenario provenance. | Forced setup does not silently earn ordinary progression achievements. |
| Event log and details | First arrival, actual evolutions, major succession results, public terminal branches and scenario entries use their proper shared surfaces. | Ordinary colony development is not mislabeled as an evolution, and hidden information is not published in Event Details. |

Other rough events, such as disasters, disease, defensive construction or independent unit arrivals, may later gain ZIN-aware reactions. Those bridges are not treated as existing APIs here. Their owning specification and source must be inspected before a dependency is added.

## Aftermath without a terminal ending

A contained colony war, a disabled ending or a political victory below the current terminal threshold still needs a playable aftermath. Keep surviving countries, treaties, artifact control, population changes and destroyed commands. Close obsolete missions and move the surviving governments to reconstruction, peaceful development, negotiated independence or their appropriate victorious policy.

A nonterminal outcome must not masquerade as a terminal super-event and freeze unrelated random events. Conversely, a committed world end must not allow later ordinary arrival timers to restart the defeated global crisis. The owner of the terminal state controls the final freeze and conflict rules.
