# 068 ZIN: focus blueprint and route dependency rules

## What this blueprint fixes

`registries/068_zin_focus_blueprint.json` provides individual logical focus work items, prerequisites, proposed durations, effect direction and completion policy. The shared framework is instantiated only where the country package permits it. Core countries have dedicated workstreams. Secondary societies have their own route commitments and mission-dependent payoffs.

These records are design nodes, not final native focus scripts. Coordinates, exact native IDs, localization, icons and bindings to verified effects remain implementation outputs. The country documents remain authoritative for the numerical reward, physical unit count, political choice and story gate behind a work item. A node that opens a 180-day project does not itself deliver that project's reward.

## Shared framework

The eight shared nodes cover administration, local supply, first contact, military institutions, resident settlement, a related crossing, active policy and Reckoning alignment. A country may already possess part of the starting institution. In that case, its own tree transforms the node into a meaningful next task or records a proper bypass with no duplicate grant.

The framework must not overwrite a valuable existing Earth or other-event focus tree merely because a carrier was selected. New colony packages use an admitted carrier with their own provenance. A negotiated overlay onto a living country preserves that country's meaningful content unless an explicit accepted political transformation replaces it.

## Secondary route graph

Each secondary package has three proposed routes with three logical stages. The first stage explores or prepares an option. The second commits its political direction. The third pays off the actual completed institution, treaty, campaign or signature mission. Second-stage commitments are mutually exclusive within that society's three-route set unless the country document expressly defines a later negotiated transition.

A focus completion cannot invent a willing partner, a supplied front, a captured ship or a new country to satisfy its own gate. It opens the action or accepts the actual completed proof. A missing partner gives a specific alternate eligible counterpart or a visible blocked route, never an unrelated reward.

## Duration and reward review

Ordinary institutional work uses 70 days, political commitments use 35 days and emergency succession instructions can use 7 days. These are proposed values. Mandatory events are not delayed by an unselected focus. A player cannot keep the Golden King alive by never selecting a death-related node.

Reward variety comes from new diplomatic rights, unit access, real equipment or construction, resident policy, charters, command obligations, investigations, country identity, controlled territorial outcomes and bounded Chaos consequences. Do not replace those effects with rows of generic attack, stability or PP modifiers.

Keep at most three event-created national spirits active, usually the society settlement and political-military institution plus Glo where held. Update the owned composite idea for a changed institution instead of adding a permanent spirit for every completed focus. Local effects belong to the actual state or formation where possible.

## Native tree completion gate

Before calling a tree complete, enumerate every final focus and show its prerequisite links, mutually exclusive choices, availability, bypass, reward, icon, dynamic tooltip, AI strategy and route-ending reachability. Render the actual native tree with the supported inspection route and review disconnected nodes, overlapping icons, crossed lines, unreachable commitments and localization expansion.

The machine-readable blueprint does not claim that those native checks have been performed. It supplies a concrete work inventory for the implementation and audit agents. A major route cannot be reduced to its first node with the remaining stages described only in prose.
