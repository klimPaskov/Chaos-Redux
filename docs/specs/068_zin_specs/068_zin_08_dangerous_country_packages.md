# 068 ZIN: Dangerous Crossings country packages

These 13 packages use the common settlement framework and retain their own political choices, missions, units, leaders, and AI. The core societies are developed in the dedicated Rush, baseline-core, and greater-powers parts.

Every route label below is an internal planning description. It is not approved focus, event, decision, party, or achievement localisation. New histories and leader biographies are proposed additions to the user's named geography. They are not map transcriptions.

Each three-stage workstream expands into the necessary focus nodes, connected decisions, and visible consequences. The stages normally use 70-day institutional work and 35-day commitment choices. Routes share the founding administration, supply, and Earth-relations spine, but their political commitments are not interchangeable. Mutual exclusions apply when a country accepts incompatible sovereign, patron, or civilian obligations. Every secondary country needs a complete neutral or independent continuation, not only a faction-join button.

Military access, contracts, and alliances remain subject to real routes and existing wars. No country receives another starting army after a merger, succession, patron change, or cosmetic rename. A nominally friendly route cannot ignore a recorded betrayal merely because an ideology value changed.

## Z68-C19: Pulundur Dark-Sea Raiders

**Homeland:** `dark_lands`. **People:** Sea orcs and associated human crews. **Placement:** Ports, islands, narrow seas, and isolated coasts.

**Proposed leader:** A fleet captain who escaped an exclusive levy from a stronger dark ruler.

### Homeland and opening problem

Pulundur remembers the Dark Sea and the competition between coastal rulers who demanded ships without paying crews. Its proposed crossing preserved a fleet community whose loyalty is to its captains before any distant dark throne.

A raiding fleet needs repair facilities and safe buyers. Uncontrolled attacks can close every harbor the colony needs.

### Country package

The initial government uses `neutrality` with a society-specific political institution. The arrival brings 25,000 civilians in addition to its separately accounted military establishment. Its target usable economy is 3 civilian and 2 military factories, counting inherited capacity first. It has 2 research slots and the ordinary early technologies needed to operate its equipment. It does not inherit every Earth technology.

Starting army: 5 × `pulundur_marine`, 5 × `sea_orc`. The unit registry owns the templates, physical personnel, replacement rules, and combat targets. The country's identity spirit and one current political condition are the default two ZIN spirit slots. Glo uses the third if acquired.

### Focus route families

**Z68-C19-P1, internal workstream: Independent sea compact.** Secure a home port → Negotiate lawful repair and trade → Create a limited coastal league. Remain neutral or selectively hostile with contracts that do not automatically serve Afrit.

**Z68-C19-P2, internal workstream: Ashen privateering.** Accept an Afrit charter → Attack a named enemy supply route → Negotiate autonomy or accept cult control. Provide a real naval arm for The Ashen Throne, with a revolt path if the charter is broken.

**Z68-C19-P3, internal workstream: Dark-sea conquest.** Unify the captains → Prepare a reachable coastal target → Build a chain of occupied ports. Expand only after activation and with actual transport and supply proof. Captured harbors create garrison and repair obligations.

### Signature mission

**Internal mission direction: Return the contracted fleet.** Duration: 90 days. Required proof: Complete one reachable naval-support contract and return the committed ships or surviving crews to a valid port. Success: Payment, a repair agreement, and a limited marine replacement allotment. Failure: Losses remain real and the captains can refuse another patron contract.

### Distinct player actions

| Internal action direction | Cost | Requirement | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Accept a privateering charter | 50 PP and 10 convoys | A named client, valid target, and activated external policy | Commit to a particular campaign instead of unrestricted attacks on all trade. |

| Open a repair harbor | Two civilian factories for 90 days | A controlled damaged port or a willing treaty partner | Repair actual port capacity and bind the access agreement. |

### AI and presentation

Never raid a state solely because it is coastal. Prefer reachable targets with a repair port and avoid contracts against its own guaranteed partner.

Dark-sea ship crews, boarding gear, and a distinctive sea-orc model. News uses Pulundur and the Dark Sea, not the Dead Lands.

Arrival news should name the homeland and explain the opening political problem. It must not imply that proposed institutions or biographies were transcribed from the map. Final text and any personal name are authored during the localisation pass.
