# 068 ZIN: narrative and super-event direction

## Writing ownership

This file specifies what each event must communicate, who knows it and which choices it may offer. It does not provide final localisation, invented historical quotations or finished public prose. Canonical names and scenario titles come from the supplied brief. New labels are internal writing directions.

The opening is a sequence of separate societies appearing on Earth. A government can know that a colony calls its homeland Rush without already knowing the complete ZIN cosmology. Attributed testimony, proven fact and rumor must remain distinct. Afrit's Influence is visible in Rush gameplay, while his responsibility for the king's death remains concealed until a legitimate evidence route exposes it.

Every full colony arrival has its own report direction below. The shared structure is used to avoid missing the founding state, former owner and response. The actual prose must still describe the society's history, institutions and conflict. Do not paste one generic alien description into forty-four events.

## Options and recipient logic

An Earth recipient can recognize, negotiate, prepare a border response or choose a deliberate attack when it has the authority to do so. A colony recipient decides its resident policy and internal institutions. A Rush player chooses a valid succession camp. A distant observer receives an informational report rather than an option to command a foreign army.

Acceptance text must identify the actual cost or territorial consequence. Sarcasm can fit a reckless king, confident warlord or bureaucratic response, but it must not conceal that a choice begins war, closes Einendil's route or submits a dominion. Avoid interchangeable jokes across every society.

News repetition is controlled by incident identity and topic. The first meeting between two unfamiliar societies can deserve a report. Every routine equipment shipment does not. A repeat colony from a known society should acknowledge earlier contact instead of rediscovering the entire world.

## Five required super-events

The first arrival, Reckoning and three terminal victories have separate super-events. The king's death, claimant victories and Glo transfers use substantial ordinary reports by default. Additional super-events for those moments require a specific accepted reason and cannot compete with every later colony arrival.
