# 068 ZIN: Greater Societies country packages

These 9 packages use the common settlement framework and retain their own political choices, missions, units, leaders, and AI. The core societies are developed in the dedicated Rush, baseline-core, and greater-powers parts.

Every route label below is an internal planning description. It is not approved focus, event, decision, party, or achievement localisation. New histories and leader biographies are proposed additions to the user's named geography. They are not map transcriptions.

Each three-stage workstream expands into the necessary focus nodes, connected decisions, and visible consequences. The stages normally use 70-day institutional work and 35-day commitment choices. Routes share the founding administration, supply, and Earth-relations spine, but their political commitments are not interchangeable. Mutual exclusions apply when a country accepts incompatible sovereign, patron, or civilian obligations. Every secondary country needs a complete neutral or independent continuation, not only a faction-join button.

Military access, contracts, and alliances remain subject to real routes and existing wars. No country receives another starting army after a merger, succession, patron change, or cosmetic rename. A nominally friendly route cannot ignore a recorded betrayal merely because an ideology value changed.

## Z68-C34: Forgotten Giants

**Homeland:** `dark_lands`. **People:** Ancient giants. **Placement:** Large forests, remote mountains, and defensible inland states.

**Proposed leader:** An old giant speaker whose memories are valuable but incomplete.

### Homeland and opening problem

The giants remember the Giant Forest and the region described as the Forgotten of Giants in the supplied brief. Their proposed history concerns a withdrawal from wars in which other rulers treated them as siege equipment.

Earth powers and both dark factions seek their strength, while the giants must decide who can be trusted not to consume the last of their host.

### Country package

The initial government uses `neutrality` with a society-specific political institution. The arrival brings 10,000 civilians in addition to its separately accounted military establishment. Its target usable economy is 2 civilian and 2 military factories, counting inherited capacity first. It has 2 research slots and the ordinary early technologies needed to operate its equipment. It does not inherit every Earth technology.

Starting army: 5 × `forgotten_giant`. The unit registry owns the templates, physical personnel, replacement rules, and combat targets. The country's identity spirit and one current political condition are the default two ZIN spirit slots. Glo uses the third if acquired.

### Focus route families

**Z68-C34-P1, internal workstream: Protected ancient refuge.** Refuse compulsory service → Negotiate a sanctuary → Protect smaller neighboring communities. Remain neutral or join a good defensive coalition through actual trust.

**Z68-C34-P2, internal workstream: Reclaimed giant realm.** Unify surviving kin → Prepare a limited territorial demand → Establish a defensible giant country. Open a regional expansion route after activation, without claiming that every ancient place belongs to them.

**Z68-C34-P3, internal workstream: Bound ancient host.** Accept a patron's shelter → Commit a finite host → Submit or seek release. Allow Afrit or Volgan to recruit them through coercion or bargain, with an explicit escape cost.

### Signature mission

**Internal mission direction: Honor the ancient sanctuary.** Duration: 180 days. Required proof: Protect the named refuge and complete its civilian and supply obligations without a forced levy. Success: A trusted alliance and access to one additional giant allotment. Failure: The giants withdraw service and can abandon the patron agreement.

### Distinct player actions

| Internal action direction | Cost | Requirement | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Request an ancient witness | 50 PP | A friendly relationship and a valid evidence investigation | Obtain limited testimony about old language or tradition. It does not invent the place of Einendil's freezing. |

| Commit the old host | 25 CP and 100 support equipment | A declared defense or siege obligation and unused allotment | Assign the actual giant force for 180 days. |

### AI and presentation

Respond strongly to treatment and broken promises. Favor survival over nominal faction ideology, and refuse repeated unreplaced losses.

Ancient giant anatomy, weathered equipment, and a distinct speaker portrait. Keep testimony art separate from a claim that the map proves the new biography.
