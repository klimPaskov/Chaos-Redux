# Historical grounding and limits

## Primary source consulted

H01 is the Office of the Historian edition of the Boxer Protocol, dated 7 September 1901, in *Foreign Relations of the United States, 1901, Appendix: Affairs in China*, Document 179.
Its provisions include financial obligations, restrictions on arms imports, arrangements for the foreign legation quarter, destruction of certain forts, specified foreign occupation points, and troop-withdrawal arrangements.
This supports using several separately negotiated settlement terms in the design.
It does not support automatically granting every intervener every possible privilege or transferring all Chinese territory.
The exact source locator is recorded under H01 in [sources.json](sources.json).
The source was consulted through returned excerpts, not reviewed as a complete transcription.

## How the source informs the design

The proposed settlement system lets an intervener seek a concrete, limited term and requires a defined payer, recipient, territory, duration, and termination rule.
The campaign state decides whether that term is possible.
A rescue mission, occupation, compensation demand, and general claim to Chinese territory are separate commitments.
This is design reasoning inspired by the treaty's varied provisions, not an assertion that the proposed mechanics reproduce historical negotiations.

The event concerns a fictional revival in the campaign's own period and political map.
The 1901 treaty is not evidence that an institution, concession, named person, or military unit remains present when the event fires.
The package therefore does not restore historical targets or leaders by default.

## Fictional content

The three political route families, the three ritual schools, the numeric meters, the mission catalog, the scenario presets, and the postwar progression are original proposed gameplay designs.
Their names are working directions and do not claim to identify documented historical Boxer institutions.
Genuine supernatural effects are fictional Chaos Redux content.
The package makes no historical claim that rituals provided supernatural protection.

## Research still required

Regional institutions, period-appropriate named people, authentic symbols, usable portrait sources, flag motifs, and suitable licensed recordings need a separate completed research pass.
A collective institutional label is not permission to invent historical faces or fabricate an archival source.
No quote, portrait identity, recording, license, or exact historical flag has been accepted by this package.
