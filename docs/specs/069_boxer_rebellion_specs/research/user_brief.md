# Boxer Rebellion

## Catalog entry

- Event ID: `69`
- Event name: Boxer Rebellion
- Type: Minor Fire-Once

- Cluster role: Severe member
- Additional cluster: Diplomacy
- Additional cluster role: Medium member

## Core idea

> A new Boxer movement erupts across China, attacking foreign influence, missionaries, concessions, occupation authorities, and Chinese governments seen as collaborating with outsiders. The crisis can remain an internal uprising, become a war between China and foreign powers, or produce a multinational intervention resembling the historical Eight-Nation Alliance.

The event adapts to whatever China looks like when it fires.

China may be unified, divided between several Chinese governments, occupied, partially colonized, controlled through puppets, already fighting foreign powers, or governed by an ideology completely different from the historical Qing government.

## Where the rebellion begins

Several valid Chinese states become centers of Boxer activity.

The movement prefers areas with foreign-controlled Chinese territory nearby, concessions or treaty-port style presence, occupation, foreign military access or bases, strong foreign influence, instability, weak central authority, or recent defeat by foreign powers.

## Boxer movement

The Boxers become their own organized force through state unrest, armed uprisings, militia units, sabotage, attacks against foreign institutions, attacks against collaborating Chinese authorities, and local seizures of towns and infrastructure.

A visible **Boxer Strength** value tracks the movement.

It can grow through successful uprisings, government tolerance, foreign intervention, Chinese military defeats, occupation, and popular support.

It falls when strongholds are destroyed, leaders are captured, support collapses, or Chinese authorities suppress the movement.

## Chinese government response

### Suppress the Boxers

The government treats them as rebels, improving relations with foreign countries and reducing intervention risk at the cost of fighting a domestic movement.

### Tolerate them

The government avoids openly endorsing the Boxers while allowing them to attack foreign influence.

### Support them

A Chinese government can openly align itself with the uprising.

Boxer units become allies or auxiliaries and foreign powers with interests in China become much more likely to intervene.

## Foreign targets

The movement dynamically identifies foreign countries with meaningful interests inside China.

Priority targets include countries that own or occupy Chinese territory, control treaty-port style possessions, station troops in China, fight Chinese governments, recently took Chinese territory, or maintain unusually strong political or economic influence there.

## Foreign crisis

Foreign governments can receive attacks on missions, destruction of businesses, attacks on railways, isolated garrisons, besieged foreign districts, murders of foreign nationals, and demands that Chinese authorities restore order.

They can evacuate personnel, reinforce concessions, send weapons or troops, issue ultimatums, demand compensation, demand suppression, or organize a joint intervention.

## International intervention

If the crisis becomes severe enough, affected powers can form a temporary intervention coalition, dynamically recreating the role of the historical Eight-Nation Alliance.

It can contain fewer or more than eight countries.

Its goals can include rescuing besieged nationals, protecting foreign-controlled territory, destroying Boxer forces, forcing suppression, occupying strategic cities, and imposing a postwar settlement.

Different members can pursue different ambitions.

## Boxer victory

The Boxers can succeed by driving foreign troops from much of China, seizing concessions, defeating an intervention, surviving with government support, or forcing foreign withdrawal.

A Chinese government that embraces them can gain a permanent anti-foreign political route.

## Foreign victory

Foreign intervention can defeat the uprising and impose Boxer suppression, reparations, temporary occupation, demilitarized areas, military access, restored concessions, political humiliation, or expanded privileges.

The settlement depends on the interests and military position of the actual interveners.

## Evolution I: The Righteous Fists

- Chaos requirement: `200+`.

The movement becomes much more organized through identifiable leadership networks, regional commanders, militia structures, recruitment systems, and stronger territorial control.

Boxer-held areas can begin functioning as a parallel authority.

## Evolution II: Heavenly Protection

- Chaos requirement: `400+`.

The supernatural beliefs surrounding the movement become mechanically relevant.

Boxer groups can develop extreme morale, ritual systems, unusual combat effects, and in some cases genuine Chaos Redux anomalies rather than purely psychological effects.

Different groups can develop different schools and rituals.

## Evolution III: China Against the World

- Chaos requirement: `600+`.

The movement can transcend individual Chinese governments.

Chinese rivals can temporarily cooperate against foreign intervention through joint councils, ceasefires, shared Boxer armies, coordinated attacks, and a common anti-intervention pact.

If the foreign coalition is defeated, the surviving Chinese participants can then struggle over the post-rebellion order.

## Boxer-controlled China

If the Boxers become the dominant force rather than merely supporting another government, they can establish a dedicated Chinese regime centered on anti-foreignism, militia society, traditionalist and religious Boxer ideology, and an internal struggle over whether to adopt modern weapons without accepting foreign domination.

It should receive a dedicated focus branch or adapted focus tree if it survives.

## Foreign occupation can make things worse

Heavy occupation, extreme reparations, annexations, or abusive postwar terms can create stronger long-term Chinese hostility even after the original Boxer organization is defeated.

## Connections

**Chemical and Biological Weapons Convention** can affect intervention behavior.

**The Offensive** can make both Chinese and intervention AI more aggressive.

**Independence Wave** and **Subjects Break Free** can interact with foreign-controlled Chinese territory where valid.

**The Black Market** can arm Boxer forces, Chinese governments, or collaborators.

If China is already fragmented by **Random Civil War**, support follows the actual governments, occupations, and alignments rather than assuming one unified China.

## Presentation

The opening receives a major news event.

Further news can cover attacks on foreign compounds, Chinese government support, formation of the intervention coalition, major sieges, foreign occupation, Boxer victory, and the final imposed settlement.
