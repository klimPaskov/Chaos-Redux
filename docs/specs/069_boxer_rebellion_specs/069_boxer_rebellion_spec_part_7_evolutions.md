# Evolutions of the Boxer movement

The three evolutions alter the institutions and possibilities of the crisis.
Ordinary armed mobilization, foreign intervention, siege relief, and postwar settlement remain baseline developments.
They must function when all evolutions are disabled.

Crossing a Chaos threshold makes an evolution eligible when its campaign conditions are also met.
It does not immediately apply the evolution or guarantee that it will occur.
An active crisis normally uses a dynamic mean-time-to-happen process with an initial timing anchor near 90 days.
Organization, sustained fighting, meaningful government support, and repeated relevant incidents can shorten that time.
Successful containment, broken networks, or loss of the required actors can lengthen it or remove eligibility.

An event that first fires at higher Chaos can begin with the corresponding enabled institutional form when the opening conditions support it.
That is an evolved opening, not a sequence of instant reward popups.
Equipment, territory, country setup, and foreign capacity still require the same real accounting.

## Evolution I: The Righteous Fists

**Chaos requirement: 200+.**

Local societies acquire reliable leadership networks, regional commanders, recruitment arrangements, supply routes, and a means of resolving disputes.
The movement becomes capable of acting as a parallel authority in the areas where it can actually sustain that authority.

The evolution is eligible when several societies maintain contact and at least one substantial organization survives beyond a short-lived disturbance.
A functioning supporter, linked strongholds, or a completed regional council can provide that institutional base.
A few isolated riots with no command or supplies do not qualify merely because global Chaos is high.

### Regional command styles

A council can preserve local command and coordinate a common task, or establish a stronger central staff.
The first approach keeps local support and makes cooperation easier across rival Chinese governments, but limits the speed of large military commitments.
The second makes planned operations and regularization more reliable, but creates disputes with societies that supplied their own arms and recruits.

These choices change recruitment permissions, which formations accept joint operations, how quickly reserves can be assembled, and the consequences of a policy reversal.
They do not require another Authority or Cohesion meter.
The interface identifies the adopted command arrangement and any active dispute.

### Parallel administration

Boxer-held areas can administer local security, collect supplies through a supported allocation, maintain workshops, and negotiate with neighboring governments.
Those activities require actual control and capacity.
They do not create a complete national economy from a movement flag.

A government may recognize a regional council as an auxiliary institution.
It may also attempt to replace the council with regular administration.
The council can accept, bargain for autonomy, or leave the compact.
M05 and M10 provide the playable process.

### Evolution result

The immediate unlock is a deeper command and administration system.
The full payoff is earned through council, supply, and regularization objectives.
The evolution itself gives no free national army, no automatic territory, and no Chaos award.

## Evolution II: Heavenly Protection

**Chaos requirement: 400+.**

Belief, training, and unusual phenomena become an active part of the movement's military and political organization.
The event distinguishes ordinary morale effects, claims that prove unreliable, and actual Chaos Redux anomalies.
It never presents the movement's historical beliefs as established real-world supernatural facts.

Eligibility requires a surviving organized movement, repeated use of a school or ritual practice, and enough operational continuity for the phenomenon to be observed.
Evolution I can help create those conditions, but the implementation must not make an enabled Evolution II unusable solely because Evolution I was disabled.
A well-established baseline organization can supply the required foundation.

### School A: Collective discipline

The school uses common drills, oaths, and a shared code to improve cohesion under fire.
Its ordinary form strengthens a formation's willingness to hold a prepared position and recover after disruption.
Its advantage depends on supply, intact command, and the selected formation's participation in training.

The proposed tactical window lasts 15 days after a 30-day preparation, with a 90-day school cooldown.
A starting balance target is a meaningful organization-recovery benefit around 20% for the supported consumer, paired with a visible limitation on changing the planned operation.
The exact modifier and its permitted unit or state scope require engine verification.
Do not silently apply a narrowly described effect to every division in China.

Failure can leave the formation exhausted or expose command weakness.
It cannot erase physical equipment losses.
This school remains a valid conventional institution even when no supernatural effect is confirmed.

### School B: Concealed approaches

The school develops local guides, disciplined movement, and a ritual identity around remaining unseen.
Its ordinary form helps a prepared force use a known route with better local cooperation.
A confirmed anomalous form can briefly disrupt enemy observation around one supported operation.

The effect is local and temporary.
It must not make all units invisible, remove every enemy aircraft, or disable the opponent's entire intelligence system.
It does not cross a sealed border or provide supply through an inaccessible region.
The proposed window is 15 days with a 90-day cooldown, and it requires control of the relevant approaches.

If exact unit-level detection behavior is unsupported, the effect remains blocked pending a reviewed representation.
A broad national combat bonus cannot be substituted while retaining a misleading promise of invisibility.

### School C: Warded formations

The school claims protection from particular forms of attack.
Initial reports can reflect morale, selective survival stories, or unreliable evidence.
M16 determines whether a genuine event-world anomaly has appeared.

A confirmed form can provide a bounded defensive effect to a small, explicitly participating force during one operation.
The proposed balance ceiling is a 20% defensive benefit for 15 days, subject to the installed engine's actual modifier support.
It does not provide absolute invulnerability, resurrection, unlimited reinforcement, or immunity to artillery, aircraft, starvation, or contamination.

A formation still needs ammunition, replacements, medical support, and a supply route.
Chemical and biological protection requires the relevant real equipment and shared system behavior.
A failed claim can cause an internal crisis when commanders relied on it in place of ordinary preparation.

### Schools and political choices

One society normally commits to one school for an operational cycle.
Changing schools requires training and a new commitment, preventing a stack of every benefit on the same formation.
Different societies may follow different schools.
A regional council can regulate them, permit parallel practices, or restrict military decisions to effects it can verify.

The political conflict concerns who may authorize operations and what evidence they must provide.
Religious belief by itself does not automatically make a government incapable of modernization.
Modern rifles, workshops, medicine, and logistics remain available through the appropriate routes.

A conventional outcome must remain meaningful.
If no anomaly is confirmed, the country can preserve cultural and religious institutions while building a practical army.
A confirmed anomaly opens its specific operational option without replacing the rest of the military branch.

## Evolution III: China Against the World

**Chaos requirement: 600+.**

The movement becomes a basis for cooperation across Chinese governments that normally distrust one another.
A shared intervention threat creates a reason to coordinate forces, supplies, ceasefires where possible, and negotiating positions.
The cooperation is conditional and preserves the participants' separate political identities.

Eligibility requires a credible foreign intervention or occupation challenge, surviving Chinese participants, and a feasible common objective.
Two governments with no operational contact cannot create an effective joint army merely by signing a popup.
If China is unified, the evolution creates a national council of participating regional and military institutions within the existing state.
It does not invent rival countries to satisfy a country-count condition.

### The pact

The pact defines a limited common enemy or intervention objective, a command arrangement, contributions, protection commitments, and the treatment of recovered territory.
Members can coordinate an operation, share a supply task, or appoint a conference delegation.
They remain responsible for their own units and budgets.

A Chinese participant need not endorse every Boxer society to join an anti-intervention agreement.
It can demand protection of civilians and disarmament of groups that violate the pact.
A society can reject those conditions and remain outside the common command.

### Ceasefires and existing wars

A ceasefire must correspond to an actual supported change in the relevant war relationship.
Use it only when the current war structure permits a safe limited settlement.
If a Chinese conflict is entangled with a wider war, the pact must use a feasible narrower arrangement or exclude the impossible military commitment.
Do not claim that a flag has stopped combat when the countries remain at war and their armies continue fighting.

Existing factions are not automatically dissolved.
Joint operations use compatible access, supply, aid, and command mechanisms.
An operation whose required engine behavior is unavailable remains an explicit blocker.

### Recovered territory

Before a joint offensive, the participants agree whether recovered territory returns to a prior Chinese owner, remains under temporary military control, joins a negotiated autonomous arrangement, or waits for a postwar conference.
The agreement follows the current owner, controller, claims, and local compact.
No member gains every Chinese core as an automatic reward.

### The post-intervention dispute

Defeating the intervention removes the common reason for cooperation.
Participants must then decide whether to preserve a limited defense agreement, build a negotiated federation, return to separate governments, or contest the postwar order.

The first disagreement should produce a conference, a local command dispute, or a contested territorial handover.
A new full civil war is reserved for a situation with genuinely incompatible territorial authority and military commands.
It is not an automatic punishment for victory.

## Disabled evolutions and continuing institutions

A disabled evolution cannot record itself, unlock new gated powers, or award a concealed replacement bonus.
Baseline containment, intervention, country survival, and settlement remain playable.

Disabling an evolution during the crisis prevents new gated activations.
Already created countries are not deleted, completed political agreements do not vanish, and existing timed operations follow their documented expiration or withdrawal process.
A religious route retains its conventional institutions after supernatural operations are disabled.
A Chinese pact can conclude its existing commitments without receiving further disabled evolution unlocks.

Each evolution is logged once through the shared pipeline with the correct parent event and actor where applicable.
The evolution's activation and log entry change Chaos by zero.
Any separate concrete consequence uses the event impact ledger in Part 12 and must not be counted twice.
