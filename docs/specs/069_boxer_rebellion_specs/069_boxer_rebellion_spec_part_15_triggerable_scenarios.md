# Triggerable Boxer scenarios

The shared scenario window provides a direct way to start a coherent Boxer challenge.
It does not wait for the random timer, a particular Chaos level, a normal evolution delay, or an unused natural fire-once flag.
The launch is blocked only when the requested setup is structurally impossible or conflicts with an active terminal state.

Use the existing scenario registry, confirmation flow, type controls, four-stop intensity slider, and localisation surfaces.
Do not build a separate Event 069 scenario window or request dedicated scenario art.
The confirmation reads the currently selected type, intensity, participants, and targets at the moment of launch.

## Four scenario types

### Type A: Internal uprising

Start with a group of Boxer centers inside the selected Chinese theater and give the affected governments an immediate policy choice.
Foreign intervention is possible later but is not the starting assumption.
The setup supplies a coherent local grievance, functioning organizations, and the initial material allocations declared by the selected intensity.

The type is useful for testing or playing suppression, toleration, support, local compacts, and the early country route.
It must not automatically create a foreign possession or make every outside major an enemy.

### Type B: Siege emergency

Start with one or more specific threatened sites and an urgent protection or evacuation problem.
The confirmation identifies the affected people or force, responsible authority, interested foreign actor, available route, and deadline.
Each site must have at least one structurally viable response, although success is not guaranteed.

Use actual registered interests where possible.
If the manual challenge explicitly creates a temporary foreign delegation or protected mission as its premise, record it as scenario-created and state that premise in the confirmation.
Do not misrepresent it as a historical possession or silently transfer state ownership.
No fictional civilian count may be subtracted from a country's manpower as an opening punishment.

### Type C: Intervention campaign

Start with a credible intervention commitment, a declared mandate, and a viable opposing movement.
Use multiple independent participants when available.
If only one feasible foreign power exists, the setup is a unilateral intervention and must be labeled accordingly.
It must not display an eight-country coalition that does not exist.

The setup uses legal routes and actual force ownership.
Any scenario-supplied units, equipment, or allocations are explicit one-time setup grants with receipts.
They are not normal recruitment rewards and cannot be collected again by relaunching the same active scenario.
Existing wars and factions remain intact unless a specific, supported change is part of the confirmed setup.

### Type D: Boxer government

Start with a viable Boxer country or bind the challenge to an already existing compatible Boxer government.
The confirmation identifies the carrier, founding territory, capital, forces, resources, relations, and whether a player handover is offered.
The player may keep their current country.
A change of player country is an explicit choice.

The normal institutional and Chaos gates can be bypassed for this manual setup.
The carrier, territory, ownership, and conflict checks cannot be bypassed.
Do not overwrite a living unrelated country, duplicate a meaningful national identity, or transfer the player's capital without the confirmed setup clearly requiring it.

## Intensity profiles

The values and desired center counts below are initial tuning targets.
Physical deployment and target counts are constrained by the real map and valid actors.
A smaller feasible theater uses fewer sites with appropriately scaled commitments, and the confirmation shows the actual result.
Intensity changes the challenge, not a promise that every map can provide the same number of countries or ports.

| Type | Low | Medium | High | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Internal uprising | Strength 25, Pressure 10, normally two centers and early organization | Strength 40, Pressure 25, normally three centers and armed recruitment | Strength 60, Pressure 45, normally four centers and established regional organization | Strength 80, Pressure 65, up to five initial centers, strongholds and a prepared advanced institutional challenge |
| Siege emergency | Strength 40, Pressure 35, one site with a 60-day window | Strength 55, Pressure 55, two viable sites with 60-day windows | Strength 70, Pressure 75, two serious sites with 30-day windows and higher operational demands | Strength 85, Pressure 90, up to three urgent sites with 30-day windows, with no hidden fourth mission |
| Intervention campaign | Strength 45, Pressure 55, a limited expedition and narrow mandate | Strength 60, Pressure 70, several compatible commitments where available | Strength 75, Pressure 85, stronger forces, contested supply, and an organized movement | Strength 90, Pressure 95, the strongest feasible bounded campaign with explicit advanced starting institutions |
| Boxer government | Strength 55, Pressure 25, a small viable state and a modest funded force | Strength 65, Pressure 45, a stronger founding package and a real supply problem | Strength 80, Pressure 65, regional organization and a prepared intervention risk | Strength 90, Pressure 85, a broad institutional challenge, strong opposition, and any explicitly selected advanced starting state |

All setup grants are finite and proportional to the actual challenge.
The implementation must show their material and territorial effects in the confirmation.
Do not use intensity as an excuse to grant unlimited divisions, advanced technology, a free navy, or arbitrary immunity.

## Evolution setup

A scenario can directly establish an institutional or operational state that would normally require an evolution.
Use a tightly scoped launch flag or variable for that setup and clear it afterward.
The setup does not silently raise global Chaos or change the user's global evolution toggles.

An advanced initial ritual operation can be active when explicitly included in the confirmed challenge.
Its future renewal follows the normal current settings after the launch bypass is cleared.
The scenario description must explain that distinction when the relevant evolution is disabled.
The same principle applies to an initial Chinese pact or a regional council.
Already created institutions can finish their legitimate commitments without receiving unlimited future bypasses.

Manual setup does not award ordinary random-event or evolution rewards a second time.
Use the shared scenario history and the correct evolution context for records that the framework requires.
The record must make clear that the state was scenario-created.

## Repeated launch and an existing crisis

Prior natural firing is not a reason to disable the scenario.
A currently active Boxer crisis is normally reconfigured through its existing instance and actors rather than duplicated.
The confirmation shows what will change and what existing obligations will remain.

Do not grant a second founding army, duplicate the Boxer carrier, reset every mission deadline, reroll an already paid shipment, or clear treaty debt through repeated confirmation.
An intensity increase can apply only the explicit additional setup required by the new profile, with a receipt for the difference.
A lower intensity must not confiscate unrelated units or buildings as a hidden correction.

After a closed crisis, a new manual generation can begin while preserving permanent countries, focus progress, historical outcomes, and independent obligations that still exist.
The manual generation has its own setup receipts and remains disqualified from natural Event 069 achievement credit.
A surviving Boxer government is reused through an origin-aware path when appropriate.

## Structural failure

A setup fails safely when no valid Chinese theater exists, no viable required route or actor can be built, a country carrier conflicts, or a terminal state makes launch incompatible.
The confirmation and button state must use the same structural eligibility check.
A failed setup must not partially transfer territory or consume resources.

Do not block launch merely because Chaos is below 200, 400, or 600, because the natural event already fired, because a normal evolution timer has not elapsed, or because a previous super-event was shown.
Those are normal progression conditions, not structural impossibilities.

## Required scenario evidence

Test all sixteen type and intensity combinations on a coherent map fixture, then test the important structural variants.
These include unified China, fragmented China, foreign occupation, no initial foreign privilege, an already active crisis, a surviving Boxer government, prior natural firing, disabled evolutions, a player handover, and a terminal-state block.

Confirm that displayed type and intensity match the launched state, bypass flags are cleared, setup grants are idempotent, actor references remain valid, and the normal crisis resumes after setup.
These tests were not executed in this planning run.
