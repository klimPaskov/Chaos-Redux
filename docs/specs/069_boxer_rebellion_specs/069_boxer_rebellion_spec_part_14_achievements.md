# Achievement design

Achievements should recognize difficult combinations of choices, campaign conditions, and sustained results.
They must not reward a single cheap button or a condition that is already guaranteed by the source event.
The names below describe the intended challenge and are not finished achievement titles or localisation.

Use the project's single root achievement registry and its exact ID and asset conventions.
The design references A01 through A12 are not reserved runtime IDs.
Each final achievement needs a unique full engine ID and three correctly named 64 by 64 assets in the achievement root.

## Shared eligibility and evidence

Natural Event 069 play is eligible under the project's normal achievement rules.
A manual scenario launch, debug force, or test fixture affecting this crisis disqualifies the relevant Event 069 achievement history according to a persistent receipt.
It must not be possible to clear that receipt by changing tag, reloading the category, ending the current phase, or reopening the scenario.
Unrelated achievements remain governed by their own shared rules.

Record important facts when they occur.
Do not reconstruct a former coalition, a surrendered allocation, an earlier civilian protection pledge, or the initial holder of a concession from the final map alone.
The history record distinguishes independent governments from subjects and temporary release shells.

A credible intervention needs actual theater involvement.
Its receipt must identify the participants, accepted mandate, deployed and supplied forces, relevant sites, and duration.
A declaration by an unreachable country or a one-day observer commitment is insufficient.
The final physical threshold should be calibrated against the verified unit and deployment data during implementation.

## A01 Contain the crisis while protecting civilians

Play a Chinese government that faces an established armed movement and a serious foreign crisis, with Pressure reaching at least 60 through natural play.
Resolve the relevant armed networks through suppression or disarmament, fulfill protection undertakings in at least three affected states, and maintain the settlement for 180 days.

The player must not deliberately breach those protection undertakings or expand the conflict through a new aggressive foreign war during the qualifying response.
Opening harm that occurred before the player could act is recorded but does not automatically make protection impossible.
The challenge rewards recovery and responsible control, not a claim that no violence ever occurred anywhere in China.

## A02 Complete a limited multinational rescue

Play a foreign participant in a coalition of at least two independent powers.
Help resolve three distinct threatened sites across at least two local authorities, keep the accepted mandate limited to evacuation or protection, and complete the required withdrawal within 90 days of the last relevant task.

Gain no new Chinese territorial ownership or coerced privilege through that intervention.
The record must show a real contribution to the rescues and an actual withdrawal.
Joining after the rescues are complete does not qualify.

## A03 Survive without a foreign patron

Play the Event 069 Boxer government through a credible intervention involving at least two independent powers, including a major power with a real theater commitment.
Preserve independent control over the government, complete M06, and reach a settlement or withdrawal while holding a functioning founding supply network.

Do not accept a puppet relationship, exclusive foreign base right, or a foreign-controlled security institution during the qualifying crisis.
Ordinary permitted trade or a genuinely reciprocal agreement is not automatically patronage.
The challenge concerns political independence and military sustainability.

## A04 Build a negotiated Chinese federation

Complete the federal political route and integrate or associate at least three genuinely independent Chinese governments through negotiated agreements.
Each qualifying partner must have existed with meaningful territory and institutions before its agreement.
A newly released one-state shell created solely to be reabsorbed does not count.

Complete the relevant M15 processes and maintain the resulting federal compact for 365 days.
A coerced annexation cannot be relabeled as a negotiated association after the fact.
The challenge requires both territorial progress and an enduring institution.

## A05 Modernize under a conventional religious council

Complete the religious governing route, establish a sustainable professional core through M06, and finish the relevant technical education and arsenal programs.
Reach a stable independent settlement while using no confirmed supernatural combat operation during the qualifying crisis.

The route can retain religious institutions and traditional practices.
The challenge is to make the state function through real training, production, and diplomacy.
A disabled Evolution II is a valid context, provided the remaining difficulty and crisis requirements are met.

## A06 Use three schools without abandoning logistics

Reveal the three Evolution II schools and complete three distinct qualifying operations using their separate allowed effects.
Each operation must have a valid supplied force and a real objective.
No formation may stack the schools in violation of the operational rules.

Complete the relevant investigation or verification requirements and preserve a functioning military core afterward.
Reusing an already resolved site, creating an artificial one-day enemy, or repeatedly activating a rite without an objective does not count.
The challenge tests controlled use of the schools rather than raw activation count.

## A07 Preserve the Chinese pact after victory

In a fragmented China, help establish an Evolution III pact with at least three genuine Chinese governments.
Complete a joint operation against a credible intervention, resolve the recovered-territory agreement, and preserve the pact or its agreed successor arrangement for 365 days after foreign withdrawal.

The qualifying participants must not be absorbed by an automatic unification effect.
A voluntary federal successor can count when every relevant agreement is recorded.
The challenge is deliberately specific to a divided-China campaign and does not force the unified-China variant to create fake members.

## A08 Recover from an imposed settlement

As a Chinese signatory, accept a substantial foreign protocol after a genuine defeat, fulfill or lawfully amend its obligations, and secure the end of its temporary occupation and restricted privileges.
Restore at least two affected districts through M14 and maintain the revised settlement for 365 days.

The original defeat must involve a credible campaign and real losses of control or enforceable obligations.
A token self-imposed treaty with no practical cost does not qualify.
Recovery can use negotiation and improved capacity without requiring a new aggressive foreign war.

## A09 Coordinate an actual eight-power intervention

Participate in a naturally formed coalition of exactly eight independent powers that each make a useful, recorded contribution.
Complete the agreed common objective and a workable settlement or withdrawal.

Eight is an achievement condition only.
It is not the event's membership requirement or a reason to invent participants.
Subjects do not provide extra independent slots, and temporary shell countries do not count.
The challenge is rare and depends on the actual campaign.

## A10 Reverse a deeply established uprising

As a Chinese government, inherit or naturally face an armed crisis that reaches at least 85 Boxer Strength with several functioning strongholds.
Restore authority through a combination of real operations and negotiated disarmament, while honoring the government's civilian protection commitments.

Maintain the final local settlement for 180 days and avoid claiming success through a fixed manpower deletion or an automatic country wipe.
The high Strength condition must be recorded before the final resolution sequence.
A debug or manual intensity increase does not qualify.

## A11 Recover privileges from several foreign holders

As a Chinese participant, end at least three distinct preexisting foreign territorial or institutional privileges held by different independent powers.
Each privilege must have been recorded before the relevant Event 069 negotiation or operation.
Use actual transfers, enforceable revisions, or completed withdrawals, then maintain the resulting arrangement for 180 days.

Do not create new privileges simply to revoke them for progress.
A substate privilege counts through its actual institutional record and does not require annexing the surrounding state.
The achievement can be completed through different combinations of negotiation and military success.

## A12 Build an army and economy that outlast the emergency

As the Boxer government, complete a political route, sustain M06, finish production programs at two genuine industrial sites, and keep an essential supply corridor functional.
Complete a stable settlement and a postwar institution objective without relying on a permanent free equipment or manpower source.

The industrial sites must contain actual functioning facilities funded or inherited through legitimate ownership.
A second icon, repeated focus reward, or duplicate repair receipt does not create a second site.
The achievement rewards a playable country that can continue after the rebellion.

## Tracking and review

Each achievement needs a persistent evidence record for its starting context, relevant actors, commitments, exclusions, milestones, and final verification period.
Only the owner can receive its player achievement, while shared contributors remain part of the evidence.
Changing government or country through a legitimate in-crisis handover follows the project's existing player-identity policy.
The implementation must document that policy rather than guessing.

Run exploit review for puppet multiplication, release-and-reannex loops, fake coalitions, repeated site creation, backdated privileges, manual scenario resets, duplicate repair rewards, and civilian-protection failures hidden by actor changes.
The achievement worker must align actual triggers and assets after the gameplay contracts are implemented.
