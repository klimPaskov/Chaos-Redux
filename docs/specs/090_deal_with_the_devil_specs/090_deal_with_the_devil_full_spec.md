# Deal with the Devil: identity, secrecy, and the first meeting

## The event the player experiences

A government has a problem that its ordinary institutions cannot solve quickly enough. A visitor offers a practical solution. The delivery is real: the trains arrive, the threatened research programme resumes, or the hospitals begin saving people. The visitor asks for an appointment, an exemption, a protected office, or a limited authority that appears proportionate to the emergency. Months later, the country discovers that the institution entrusted with the solution has developed loyalties of its own.

Event 090 follows this relationship from its first plausible favour to an infernal government, an open invasion from within, or an expensive escape. Its central temptation is competent assistance at the moment competence is scarce. The stranger should not behave like an obviously fraudulent merchant whose offer an informed player would never consider. His account of who supplies the assistance can be misleading, while the stated assistance itself must be delivered.

Mephistopheles is the author-facing identity. During the opening, the player sees a visitor whose profession and presentation fit the particular offer. The same recognisable face, restrained manner, and peculiar turns of phrase connect his appearances. He need not invent a new unrelated personality for every sector. A financier can introduce a medical foundation; a diplomatic intermediary can produce an industrial guarantor. This helps the later discovery feel like an interpretation of evidence that already existed.

## Catalog authority and replacement

The design identity is Event 090, **Deal with the Devil**, Minor Fire-Once, status **To Be Reworked**, Chaos level 1, outside every event cluster. The supplied brief replaces the old Suicide Craft concept in its entirety. It does not inherit aircraft rewards, kamikaze mechanics, old eligibility, or old achievement conditions merely because the number is unchanged.

Event 161's unavailable duplicate must be retired as a duplicate or receive an explicitly different future idea. Neither number may independently open another Mephistopheles parent chain. The implementation handoff must reconcile the authoritative catalog source, generated exports, event selection, player details, localisation, achievements, and any references to the old 090 concept. This package does not itself edit those sources or promote a catalog status.

The supplied brief establishes the required concept. The numerical tuning, specific offer variants, branch choices, and settlements developed here are a design proposal, not evidence that the user approved every new detail. The source/decision register separates those layers. The preserved original brief remains authoritative where the interrupted source handoff leaves an unresolved difference.

## Who can receive the visitor

Build one deduplicated pool from functioning human countries that are currently major or player-controlled. A player-controlled major receives one entry, not two. Do not prefer a fixed historical tag or assume the player is a major. Evaluate the country's current government and condition rather than its original name.

Exclude actual nonhuman states, terminal actors whose authority already replaces ordinary government, an existing open Mephistophelean parent, and countries already under another event's complete and exclusive control. A normal dictatorship, a country suffering political pressure, or a country with an unusual adviser is not automatically such an exclusive actor. The owner of the other event determines whether ordinary national authority still exists.

War, defeat, famine, disease, sanctions, research collapse, low stability, and threatened leadership are useful opening conditions. They are not reasons to discard an otherwise eligible host. A healthy player country remains eligible: its offer addresses a credible ambition or vulnerability rather than inventing an epidemic to justify a physician.

Selection is uniform within the valid pool in this proposal. Crisis intensity selects the offer after the host is chosen; it does not secretly give a desperate AI major ten tickets and make a small player almost impossible to target. A later change to weighted host selection needs an explicit design decision and a probability review.

The separate-country revolt creates a genuine territorial feasibility problem for a one-state host. Do not conceal that problem inside the word “valid.” This package proposes an additional opening viability gate only as a decision awaiting approval: reserve the ability to leave a playable host while creating a viable hostile foothold. The current shared civil-war documentation describes a same-tag solution for unsafe one-state cases, but that is not the separate Mephistophelean country required here. Until that conflict is resolved, one-state support is a release blocker. Part 6 gives the alternatives and the safe pending-state behavior. No arbitrary foreign state is stolen as an unannounced workaround.

## Choosing the opening need

Assess immediate survival, institutional failure, material shortages, political danger, diplomatic isolation, and a plausible peacetime ambition. An urgent problem with a reward the event can actually deliver takes priority over a more dramatic problem whose owning system has no safe integration.

A country losing a war because its divisions lack rifles receives a material rescue rather than a research-speed bonus. A country whose hospitals are overwhelmed receives a treatment promise that names its scope. A country with an intact army but a collapsed research establishment can receive restored capacity. Territorial ambition can produce financing, intelligence, diplomatic access, or an opportunity to negotiate; it does not justify pretending that another human player's land can be granted without that player's involvement.

Choose one primary problem and one offer. A secondary benefit can support the primary purpose, but the first event is not a basket of seven unrelated national bonuses. The visitor must sound as though he understands this government.

Before presenting the offer, establish that the promised reward can be fulfilled. A stale problem can change during the decision interval. An outbreak might already have been contained or a war might have ended. In that case the visitor offers an updated, equally explicit arrangement before a choice is committed. An obsolete offer must not charge a concession for no useful delivery.

## The opening scene

The meeting takes place in an ordinary seat of authority: a minister's office, an embassy reception room, a private railway carriage, or a hospital administration room. The visitor is composed, well dressed, and unhurried. There are no horns, pentagrams, infernal flags, glowing contract seals, or supernatural menu effects. The unsettling element is how readily he supplies something that exhausted officials have been unable to obtain.

An example military opening can describe a shipping schedule, an inventory, and an officer already waiting with the papers. An economic opening can describe factories whose owners have unexpectedly accepted a common reconstruction plan. A medical opening can include the improvement of a small, observed group before national distribution is offered. A demonstration proves only the promised capability; it does not count as accepted national authority.

The acceptance text states the delivered benefit and the visible concession. The refusal is ordinary and respectful. The first clean refusal costs no political power, stability, equipment, research, manpower, or diplomatic relation. It does not secretly prepare punitive troops. A patient departure is more effective than a threat at the first meeting.

## Information boundaries

There are three distinct knowledge conditions. **Unexplained assistance** means the government has received or considered an unusual offer. **Credible evidence** means it can investigate an organized intrusion and take concrete precautions. **Open identification** means it can name Mephistopheles and understands the supernatural confrontation. These are knowledge conditions used to govern presentation, not a new public meter.

Knowledge is not automatically global. A foreign government can have evidence from its own diplomats before the original host does. Conversely, a distant country does not become omniscient merely because the host saw a private medical report. An openly manifested infernal army is strong public evidence for the countries observing it. The public world can learn the nature of the threat through events that actually communicate that information.

The campaign's ordinary player surfaces must respect the viewer's knowledge. This includes event titles, acceptance tooltips, national-spirit names, decision categories, the Event Log, Event Details, focus visibility, portraits, unit names, achievement notifications, scenario descriptions, and foreign offer summaries. Suppressing only a corruption number is insufficient when the Event Log labels the first visitor “Mephistopheles.”

The author-facing catalog retains the real concept title. Before identification, the player's log and details use the proposed cover title **An Unusual Benefactor** and summarize only the observed arrangement. They do not list future possession stages, demon battalions, the hidden liberation branch, or the Last Signature. After identification, the same event record can explain the revealed history. An explicit debug or spoiler view may show internal design information; it is not the default campaign view.

This is a presentation exception to any general rule that Event Details must expose every evolution or terminal route. The user specifically requires secrecy. Implementing the normal details template unchanged would violate the event rather than satisfy it.

## What the player is entitled to know

The visible deal is not allowed to hide its ordinary cost. A protected office is described as protected. An adviser with authority over procurement is described as having procurement authority. A hospital programme with access to medical records discloses that access. The player is surprised by the visitor's identity and the long-term use of the authority, not by an invented concession that was absent from the agreement.

The acceptance preview contains the immediate reward, its duration when finite, and the stated concession. It does not show “demonic influence,” a hidden acceptance count, a probability of possession, a future revolt percentage, or a red warning icon whose tooltip explains the final route. Later incidents supply evidence through play.

The world does not promise perfect ignorance. A player who has read the mod's source, remembers a previous campaign, or recognises the literary allusion can know more than the government. The design still gives that player a real strategic choice: accept a large benefit and manage the eventual cost, refuse and prepare for open defiance, or deliberately pursue the possessed-state game.

## Fire-once identity and continued play

Only one original parent courtship starts from Event 090's random selection. Refusals, investigations, a civil war, the infernal tree, and foreign bargains are continuations of that event. They do not reroll the random event or create additional main-event entries.

A defeated manifestation does not automatically reappear in a fresh major country with a reset reward account. Surviving foreign institutions can continue only through a route that was already established and is explicitly described later. An Event 019 family derivative is never another Mephistopheles parent.

The event remains playable after the fifth acceptance. Possession changes the regime's political purpose and available content, while the human player keeps control of military operations, production, construction, diplomacy where permitted by the new regime, and strategic choices. It is not a disguised game-over screen or a transfer of the player's country to an autonomous AI.

# Deal with the Devil: courtship and the five main deals

## Acceptance is a choice, not a hidden score threshold

Peaceful possession requires five deliberately accepted **main arrangements**. There is no alternate path in which side favours, personal symptoms, high Chaos, unsolicited aid, or a successor's ordinary appointment silently supplies a missing main acceptance. The hidden institutional process can become more dangerous without changing this requirement.

An arrangement counts when the government explicitly adopts its visible terms and the promised principal delivery is secured. A declined invitation, an investigated demonstration, an unread popup, a failed delivery, or an unsolicited consignment does not count. The game can store the five acceptance states internally without displaying a deal counter.

A main arrangement gives Mephistopheles a wider relationship with national authority as the chain progresses. Its material subject remains tailored to current needs. Five military crises do not force five identical shipments; the relationship can move from an emergency delivery to command appointments, protected procurement, continuity of administration, and finally the leader's binding mandate. The category of reward and the reach of the concession are separate design choices.

## The five institutional steps

The first arrangement establishes a **protected point of entry**. A representative may supervise the delivered assistance in one relevant institution. The office is small enough that a rational government can treat it as a reasonable condition. Its strange influence is still local, and removing it later is expensive but manageable.

The second arrangement establishes **trusted appointments**. The visitor recommends or seconds people who must interact with ordinary ministers, officers, physicians, or scientists. Those people can help deliver a second major benefit. They also create loyalties that survive the original visitor's absence. A government that accepted only the first deal must not already have this second level of national access merely because it waited.

The third arrangement establishes **delegated national authority**. The programme can coordinate multiple institutions or issue instructions within its stated remit. This is the point at which an ordinary disagreement with the visitor can become a national crisis. The reward should accordingly solve a larger problem than the first office solved.

The fourth arrangement establishes **continuity over objections**. The trusted network receives protection against ordinary dismissal, a continuity board, a special chain of command, or an analogous power explicitly stated in the visible terms. Other officials now have concrete reasons to object. The offer is still useful, but it is no longer innocently administrative. Before the next principal bargain becomes available, the player must have received actionable evidence and a meaningful interval in which to protect something independent.

The fifth arrangement establishes **the final mandate**. The leader or the surviving lawful authority adopts the prepared network as the lasting guarantor of government. The reward is delivered and peaceful possession follows. The state has already been prepared; there is no opening civil war, no arbitrary destruction of half the army, and no compulsory loss of the capital. The ruler remains the familiar public face, visibly transformed.

These descriptions are the design's internal structure. The popup does not label them “Step 1” through “Step 5.” Each appears as a particular solution and a particular concession in that campaign.

## Clean courtship and refusal before any acceptance

The proposal uses at most five clean invitations to secure the first accepted arrangement. A refusal at this stage declines a bid for the first point of entry; it does not consume one of the five acceptances needed for possession. This resolves the otherwise ambiguous case in which the host refuses twice and then changes its mind. That host still needs five accepted main arrangements.

The first clean refusal is entirely unpunished. The second invitation returns after a substantial delay with a better version of the same help or a response to a more urgent real problem. Subsequent clean invitations remain persuasion rather than a series of small retaliations. The visitor may become more candid or impatient in the accompanying prose, but he does not acquire ministers, factories, or soldiers for free.

Improved clean bids should increase the useful assistance or reduce an ordinary visible burden. They must not remove every concession or introduce an infinite escalator that rewards waiting forever. The proposed improvement cap is 50 percent above the relevant initial package, reached in two 25-percent improvements. Relevance can improve further if the country's actual crisis deepens. The cap applies to this bargaining bonus, not to legitimately larger national scale or a different kind of emergency.

The fourth clean refusal produces an unsettling but non-punitive sign and gives the host an opportunity to prepare against an unexplained threat. The fifth clean invitation is the final attempt at persuasion. Its refusal ends courtship and leads to the weakest open manifestation after a visible mobilization interval. The host has accepted no institutions and loses no divisions to supposed collaborators who were never established.

This finite invitation limit is new proposed tuning. It does not change the brief's requirement that rejecting every attempt produces an open revolt. The source/decision register makes the limit easy to revise without changing the five-acceptance rule.

## Pacing

The ordinary first invitation arrives with Event 090. Further clean invitations have a proposed delay of 180–270 days. After an acceptance, the next main offer has a proposed delay of 240–360 days. These are tuning ranges for the normal event scheduler, not promises that a threshold immediately fires a popup.

A country accepting the first five main arrangements therefore normally develops over several years. A country refusing several initial bids and then accepting takes longer. War emergencies can justify the short end of a range; they do not compress the complete chain into several weeks. A hard proposed floor of 120 days between committed main arrangements survives all acceleration effects.

Only one main offer can be pending. A popup waiting for the player does not let a second main offer accumulate behind it. A long pause does not produce a queue of overdue signatures when the game resumes. A material change in the country's situation can cause the pending reward to be refreshed before acceptance, but it cannot duplicate the concession or award.

A player may postpone an offer once for 30 days while a current investigation or government transition is resolved. Postponement is not acceptance and is not a clean refusal. Repeated indefinite postponement is not a route to retaining a free open offer forever. At the end of the interval the government must accept, refuse, or receive a refreshed offer because the original promise has become invalid.

## Refusal after the first arrangement

One accepted arrangement gives the visitor a real office and a real claim to the service relationship. It does not justify an immediate national coup. Refusing the second main offer is not itself punished with a secret stability loss or a surprise army. The visitor can make one revised bid after at least 180 days. The host can also begin an ordinary review of the existing arrangement if concrete discrepancies have appeared.

A second refusal at this point closes further persuasion unless the host deliberately reopens it. The government must choose what to do about the continuing concession. A negotiated separation gives up the named institution, replaces the supplied service, or returns a defined material benefit. An unresolved arrangement does not pay new rewards and does not drift toward five acceptances merely with time.

The proposed quiet closeout after one arrangement is a costly breakaway route, not a consequence-free refusal bonus. The surviving foreign or domestic officials can remain as ordinary story consequences, but their network is no longer permitted to claim the authority of a surrendered main arrangement. A cleanly completed early settlement does not later generate a large army from nonexistent accepted mandates.

The visitor can contest an attempted confiscation that retains his protected institution while declaring its obligations void. That dispute must be announced. It creates an opportunity for a limited breach confrontation, with strength derived from actual surviving leverage, rather than making every refusal after a single deal immediately dangerous.

## Refusal after two or three arrangements

The country's institutions now depend on a network that has people in more than one place. Refusal brings a specific demand: honor an existing appointment, preserve an entrusted facility, complete a delivery obligation, or surrender the supplied benefit. The demand exposes the nature of the pressure. It is not a generic hidden-counter warning.

Give a proposed 90-day settlement interval in which the government can protect its command chain, recover independent records, replace the service, and choose a settlement strategy. The ordinary refusal alone does not delete previously cured people, cancel all unrelated research, or transfer random territory. The danger comes from the compromised institutions that are actually present.

The country can make a costly negotiated withdrawal, find and execute a genuine loophole, or prepare to fight. A failed or rejected settlement leads to a partial-acceptance confrontation. Two accepted arrangements create a serious but still geographically limited foothold. Three create a broader crisis in which industrial, military, or political institutions can work together. The exact opening is determined by their location and function, not by a universal half-country split.

An ultimatum is not a sixth kind of main deal that can accidentally complete possession. Agreeing merely to the settlement terms does not count as another main acceptance. A renewed principal bargain requires its own clear reward, concession, and explicit acceptance.

## Refusal after four arrangements

The fifth main offer arrives only after the government has had actionable evidence and at least 60 days to undertake a protective response. Its presentation remains faithful to the visible reward and terms, but its surrounding events make clear that an independent decision is now at stake. The final bargain cannot be the first indication that something is wrong.

Acceptance produces peaceful possession. Refusal can produce a large internal war or an immediate attempted seizure of authority. Whether a seizure succeeds depends on concrete protections already secured: an independent command chain, a protected successor or leader, surviving records, an uncompromised seat of government, and the actual loyalty of the network's personnel. The design does not grant the player automatic safety just because a refusal button exists.

A successful overt seizure after four acceptances is **usurpation**, not five-deal peaceful possession. It has a different origin record and political settlement. It cannot earn a “five freely accepted main deals” achievement or erase the struggle that occurred. The player must retain a playable side or a supported continuation as the captured regime with liberation content; an unannounced loss of human control is not an acceptable shortcut.

If the country is protected enough to prevent the cabinet seizure, the prepared network forms its territorial revolt where viable. Prevention does not dissolve every hostile facility or convert a heavily infiltrated country into the clean-defiance origin.

## Personal offers and side bargains

A personal rescue can intensify the current leader's visual and narrative symptoms within the current acceptance tier. It may make a medical examination especially revealing or leave that individual more vulnerable during a succession crisis. It cannot count twice, skip the next main bargain, or produce full possession at four acceptances.

Side bargains concern a bounded local favor: releasing an official, securing a particular convoy, accelerating one inspection, or protecting a witness. They can create a witness, an obligation, or local leverage. They cannot create the final mandate, satisfy a missing main acceptance, grant the fourth arrangement's constitutional authority, or assemble a national coup by accumulation.

Limit side-bargain content to at most two unresolved local favors at a time. Present them as ordinary events when relevant rather than a menu of farmable transactions. No side reward repeats from a reload, a leadership change, a destroyed temporary country, or cycling the same target.

## Unsolicited and unsigned assistance

An evolution or special incident can put an unsolicited gift in the government's path. The country can inspect it, quarantine it, send it back, or explicitly put it into national service under stated terms. Its physical arrival is not a main acceptance. A successful demonstration in a hospital or an unexplained shipment waiting at a port does not authorize the visitor to bind the country.

Adopting a principal unsolicited gift can become a main arrangement only through a visible choice that states the practical benefit and concession. Merely fighting a battle while the shipment happens to be present cannot sign for the player. The visitor can lie about the source of his generosity; the game must not lie about which player action constituted agreement.

These safeguards do not make the unsigned-gift variant harmless. Inspectors can be compromised, political pressure can build to use a desperately needed delivery, and a minister can advocate accepting it. Those are playable dilemmas. They are not retroactive consent.

## Failed delivery and changes of government

A failed promise cannot advance the relationship as though the promised assistance arrived. If a reward is partly delivered, the player must be told what remains outstanding and be offered the appropriate revised terms or cancellation. A later deliberate breach can create a loophole, but ordinary engine failure is not a narrative reward for Mephistopheles.

Institutional history belongs to the country and arrangement, while personal vulnerability belongs to the actual character. Replacing the leader does not reset four established ministries. It also does not make a newly arrived successor fully possessed without a new event or an existing delegated-authority basis. Part 5 separates these effects.

Annexation, capitulation, a new civil war, and a move into exile require the active relationship to be reconciled with the real surviving authority. Do not move the visitor to whichever tag happens to reuse an old numeric slot. No successor receives a fresh copy of an already paid reward solely because the government's carrier changed.

## Route guarantees

The following guarantees govern all later parts. Five valid main acceptances produce peaceful possession and no opening civil war. Zero main acceptances followed by total clean refusal produce the weakest open manifestation. Partial acceptance retains the consequences of actual surviving institutions and offers costly resistance. Personal and side content cannot change those counts. Evidence and counterplay precede the final decision. The player keeps a playable continuation.

Every implementation and test matrix should be derived from these guarantees rather than introducing an additional hidden threshold that overrides them.

# Deal with the Devil: military, economic, and political offers

## How to use the offer catalogue

Every offer below is a complete design seed with a delivered reward, a visible concession, later leverage, and a possible separation cost. The catalogue is author-facing. Its hidden consequences and loopholes must never be copied into an acceptance tooltip.

The selected benefit should materially change the host's situation. It should not require accepting four weak preliminary deals before anything interesting happens. The first principal reward must already be worth considering. Later bargains increase institutional reach and can solve larger problems without becoming seven simultaneous reward packages.

The proposed national scales are **small**, **established**, and **major-capacity**, based on controlled usable industry and the size of the problem. Starting industry bands are below 15, from 15 up to but not including 50, and 50 or more usable factories. A country does not jump to the largest aid package because the engine calls it a major. A high-capacity player suffering total occupation is assessed from what it can actually use. These are balancing bands, not a new player-facing value.

Choose the quantities that restore useful capacity, constrained by the stated package cap. Supply a real shortage rather than useless stock. A fallback that cannot deliver a major useful reward should select another offer. Do not count “the player received some political power instead” as fulfillment of a promise to save an army or suppress an outbreak.

Clean reoffer improvements from Part 2 apply to the principal useful component. They do not multiply every subreward independently or bypass safety caps. An institution's access must be stated even when the ordinary fiscal cost is reduced.

## Military offer: The Convoys That Arrived

**Situation.** The host's ordinary frontline formations are equipment-starved, and a material shortage is more urgent than a lack of technology. The visitor presents cargo manifests, not a miraculous promise that every battle will be won.

**Reward.** Deliver usable infantry equipment toward the measured shortage, with proposed principal caps of 5,000, 15,000, or 30,000 items by national scale. Up to 250, 750, or 1,500 support-equipment items can accompany it when those items address a real shortage. Do not include both support equipment and an unrelated artillery windfall merely to enlarge the tooltip. The cargo should be immediately available after the arrangement is secured. If the country cannot use ordinary rifles, select a compatible material offer rather than forcing an invalid equipment token.

**Visible concession.** A private procurement office supervises the cargo and its distribution. The stated remit names procurement records and designated depots. A first arrangement does not grant this office control of the entire army. Later tiers can expand its remit to multiple depots or protected appointments, explicitly.

**Delayed consequence.** Clerks begin bypassing ordinary requisition procedures. A depot commander receives instructions from an office the general staff cannot inspect. If the relationship breaks, those particular depots and officers can support the revolt. The grant itself does not magically make every recipient division loyal to the visitor.

**Separation.** Return an equivalent quantity of the surviving delivered cargo, surrender the protected depot, or replace the procurement structure at a material and command cost. Equipment already lost in combat cannot be resurrected for a nominal return. Its portion of the settlement must be paid with a real replacement burden, an agreed facility sacrifice, or an exposed contractual argument.

**Discoverable loophole.** The visible wording can appoint the office until the named consignment has been fully accounted for. Recovering independent manifests and proving the end of that assignment can end its authority. Burning all records destroys that evidence and may make separation harder.

## Military offer: A Front That Holds

**Situation.** The host has an army and equipment but is close to a breakthrough, exhausted, or unable to coordinate its defenses. The visitor offers an emergency command mission.

**Reward.** A proposed 180-day defensive package supplies +25 percent division defense, +25 percent organization recovery, and a 20 percent reduction to supply consumption. These are target effects requiring legal modifier verification, not asserted engine syntax. The package is powerful enough to affect the crisis but does not guarantee a victory, teleport divisions, erase encirclement, or prevent a human opponent from acting. If a qualifying temporary modifier cannot apply to the intended formations, the offer must be redesigned before being presented.

**Visible concession.** A military coordinator receives access to orders and designated staff conferences. The later national-authority version requires protected representation within the command chain. The player sees that change in authority.

**Delayed consequence.** Orders arrive before their recorded issuance. Selected officers display unusual calm or repeat the same private instructions. The future revolt gains a coherent staff and prepared positions rather than arbitrary extra tanks.

**Separation.** Dismiss the coordinator and reorganize the compromised commands. Remaining temporary command assistance ends. A proposed 60-day reorganization cost reduces planning and organization recovery, with sufficient warning to schedule a withdrawal or replacement. The country does not lose an unrelated technology or every general.

**Discoverable loophole.** Authority was granted over a specifically named emergency theater. Ending that emergency, moving the independent command seat, and proving that the original military remit no longer exists can terminate the appointment. Renaming the theater without changing its actual function is not enough.

## Military offer: The Men Who Can Return

**Situation.** The host is short of manpower and has a documented pool of living wounded personnel, displaced recruits, inaccessible reserves, or other recoverable people. The visitor offers to restore their ability to serve.

**Reward.** Restore a substantial portion of the eligible living pool, initially targeting 25 percent and never exceeding the actual recoverable amount. The offer can add a 180-day casualty-recovery improvement when the owner system supports it. It does not return people already counted as dead, create an adult population from nothing, or raise recruitment beyond the country's real population rules. A country without such a pool receives a different military offer.

**Visible concession.** A recovery and records bureau coordinates the return. Medical administration and military enrollment are named in its mandate. The programme's actual saving of lives is a legitimate reason to accept.

**Delayed consequence.** Recovered soldiers report the same dream, or their officers receive identical private recommendations. The vulnerable people remain human. Their treatment does not automatically convert them into demons or remove normal disease and famine rules.

**Separation.** End the bureau's access, replace its services, and release compromised officers from command. People who genuinely recovered remain alive and recovered. Costs fall on the military administration, remaining temporary assistance, and the state's real replacement capacity. A costly demobilization can be an explicit option, not a mandatory medical reversal.

**Discoverable loophole.** The agreement can promise the return of named personnel to ordinary military jurisdiction. Evidence that the bureau retained unlawful custody creates a limited nonperformance case. It does not grant a universal right to retain all other gifts without settlement.

## Economic offer: The Reconstruction Trust

**Situation.** A country has usable labor and sites but cannot restart industry after a collapse, bombing campaign, or severe construction crisis.

**Reward.** Repair or restore existing disabled industrial capacity first, with a proposed target of 5, 10, or 20 factories across viable controlled states. New factories are offered only when the promise explicitly includes new capacity and legal building slots exist. A proposed +25 percent construction-speed benefit for 180 days can support the restoration. The restoration must use the existing damage or crisis owner's rules rather than deleting every economic penalty on the country.

**Visible concession.** A trust receives operating authority over the named facilities and protected access to their management. Its territorial footprint is the actual set of benefiting states. The concession must not secretly include factories on another continent that received no assistance.

**Delayed consequence.** Company records refer to directors who cannot be found. Local administrators discover that ordinary inspection requires the trust's permission. During a breach, the industrial foothold gives the rebels productive territory and a supply base. It does not entitle them to all national industry.

**Separation.** Place the trust under independent receivership, surrender a specified portion of the restored capacity for a bounded period, or close its protected facilities while rebuilding ordinary management. A permanently repaired building is not simply “unrepaired” by an invisible timer. The visible cost is the loss of its use, its deliberate demolition, a replacement obligation, or the political consequences of retaining it against the settlement.

**Discoverable loophole.** A trust granted authority only for reconstruction can lose that authority once an independent completion review proves its work finished. Securing such a review is a project with witnesses and access, not a free rename button.

## Economic offer: A Season of Impossible Output

**Situation.** The host's immediate survival depends on producing enough equipment, supplies, or civil goods from the factories it still controls.

**Reward.** Grant a proposed +35 percent factory-output effect for 365 days, with an appropriate dockyard alternative only when shipbuilding is the selected problem. A limited production-efficiency retention benefit can replace part of the output increase if the existing crisis specifically destroys continuity. The package is one economic intervention, not an unlimited production law permanently exceeding every other system.

**Visible concession.** A private inspectorate controls work allocation and production records inside the participating industrial sector. At later acceptance tiers, protected labor and management appointments extend its reach. The visitor does not require a public infernal ministry while the secret phase remains plausible.

**Delayed consequence.** Factories meet targets despite impossible shipment schedules. Workers find that the inspectorate knows personal details never entered in their employment records. A later regime can consolidate the inspectorate into lasting industry, while an independent host must replace the extraordinary coordination.

**Separation.** End the remaining production surge and accept a proposed 90-day normalization burden of reduced output or construction capacity. The quantity already manufactured remains real. A confiscation route can retain it while risking a confrontation; a negotiated route pays a defined replacement or institutional cost.

**Discoverable loophole.** The programme's authorization can be tied to a stated production target. Meeting that target under independently verified accounting ends the inspectorate's mandate. Falsifying the output does not work, and a target added after acceptance is not legitimate.

## Economic offer: The Supply of Last Resort

**Situation.** A resource shortage, blockade, or broken trade relationship prevents useful production. The visitor claims access to reserves, transport, or intermediaries that ordinary officials cannot obtain.

**Reward.** Supply a bounded resource or logistics arrangement for 180 days through the existing resource, trade, or event-owner system. The offer names what it delivers: access to a reserve, concrete transport assets, relief from a particular registered shortage, or a negotiated trading opportunity. It cannot promise globally open sea lanes while hostile navies remain capable of blockade. If physical convoy delivery is promised, the arrival and its risks are represented consistently.

**Visible concession.** An import board receives access to customs, manifests, or a named port. The concession is especially attractive when it appears to solve sanctions without changing public policy.

**Delayed consequence.** The board becomes a route for foreign visitors and protected shipments. Its infrastructure can support foreign bargains later. A blockade lifted by one owner may remain active from another; the board does not overwrite either relationship.

**Separation.** Return surviving transport assets or replace the trade arrangement and remove the board's protected status. A severe but finite supply shock is a meaningful cost. Deleting unrelated convoys or every resource deposit is not.

**Discoverable loophole.** The agreement may authorize only goods of a specified kind through a named port. Documenting unauthorized passengers or military cargo can revoke that narrow permission. The port must be genuinely outside the board's control afterward.

## Political offer: A Government That Can Act

**Situation.** Low stability, a fractured cabinet, or emergency administrative paralysis prevents the government from executing otherwise viable policy.

**Reward.** A proposed +20 percentage points of stability and +200 political power provide immediate room to act. For a large, severe institutional collapse, the political-power component can increase to 400 while the stability gain remains capped by the normal national limit. Registered crisis penalties are relieved only through their owner's supported settlement. No opposition party is silently erased.

**Visible concession.** A special secretary receives authority to coordinate cabinet papers and selected appointments. The first-tier office is narrow. Later versions involve a continuity board, legal exemptions, or protected officials, and state those concessions plainly.

**Delayed consequence.** Cabinet members begin agreeing before an issue has been discussed. A dissenting minister finds that a dismissal letter predates the meeting. Political leverage supplies administrative loyalists, police cooperation, and control over records in a later breach.

**Separation.** Dismiss the network, expose its interference, and accept a political reorganization. Proposed costs include 150 political power and a 10-percentage-point temporary stability shock, moderated when independent institutions were prepared. These are a starting balance envelope, not a demand to charge every listed cost together.

**Discoverable loophole.** The office exists under the authority of a specific emergency cabinet. A lawful reconstitution that actually ends that cabinet, backed by independent records and a functioning successor, can limit the secretary's appointment. Cycling an ordinary leader portrait alone does not.

## Political offer: The Opposition's Sudden Silence

**Situation.** A particular organized political threat is close to removing the government or breaking its wartime administration. The offer must identify the real pressure rather than manufacture a new opposition faction.

**Reward.** Reduce the named crisis through its owning system and grant a proposed 180-day stability or authority benefit. The reward does not mean a promise to kill an entire political population. The visitor can obtain withdrawals, expose a real rival's scheme, secure a coalition, or bring an administrative rebellion to a halt. Any violent outcome uses the normal consequence owners and is not hidden inside a benign stability tooltip.

**Visible concession.** A legal envoy or mediator gains protected access to the relevant courts, political leadership, or negotiations. The government sees the appointment's ordinary breadth.

**Delayed consequence.** Judges or party officials respond to an authority the government cannot identify. A later confrontation can fracture the state apparatus without requiring the army to have accepted a military bargain.

**Separation.** Restore the affected institution's independence, surrender some of the political advantage obtained, or reopen the dispute under an ordinary settlement. The original opponents are not automatically resurrected from death or restored to positions they lost for unrelated reasons.

**Discoverable loophole.** The visitor's representative promised to act as a mediator rather than a permanent superior. A surviving copy of the agreement and testimony from both sides can prove that distinction. Obtaining the testimony may require releasing an opponent or making a concession the player would prefer to avoid.

## Political offer: Continuity of the State

**Situation.** The government expects a succession crisis, a forced abdication, or institutional collapse during war. This is especially suitable for the fourth arrangement because its visible benefit and dangerous concession have comparable reach.

**Reward.** Protect the defined transition against a specific registered crisis, retain a bounded amount of state capacity, and supply a proposed +25 percent political-power gain for 180 days. It does not make a leader immortal against every other event or invalidate a human opponent's victory.

**Visible concession.** The continuity authority can remain in office despite ordinary dismissals while the stated emergency lasts. The wording is deliberately consequential. The game must not conceal it as “appoint one adviser” when its real public terms bind the next government.

**Delayed consequence.** The network can remain effective after a leader is removed. The player discovers why killing or replacing one possessed person is insufficient. At the fifth arrangement this institutional continuity can become the route through which the final mandate is adopted.

**Separation.** Establish an independent lawful successor, protect the records and command chain, and dismantle the continuity authority. Losing access to the protected government's remaining temporary guarantees is a fair cost. Replacing every institution with a free new government is not.

**Discoverable loophole.** The terms may preserve a particular constitutional office, not whichever person Mephistopheles later places in it. Proving that he has abolished or usurped the protected office can undermine his claim, provided a real independent authority survives to act on the evidence.

## Campaign variation without repetitive grants

Do not offer the same principal reward twice while its first material purpose remains satisfied. A second military offer can address manpower after rifles solved the original shortage. A second economic offer can protect transport after industry recovered. Reusing a category should create a new dilemma and a distinct institution or expansion of authority.

The visitor is permitted to exploit a crisis caused by another event. He is not permitted to trigger that event secretly merely to ensure his offer remains useful unless a later, expressly designed interaction establishes that responsibility. Let the world create most of the temptation.

# Deal with the Devil: knowledge, medicine, diplomacy, and the ruler

## Scientific offer: The Missing Archive

**Situation.** Research Failure or another supported owner has recorded the loss of methods, records, current work, or the ability to reproduce known technology. The visitor presents copies that appear older than the originals.

**Reward.** Recover a substantial bounded portion of the actual lost archive and restore the associated owner-tracked progress. The proposed starting target is 50 percent of eligible recoverable archive loss, together with one genuinely lost research slot when a safe restoration path exists. Never exceed the recorded pre-crisis slot entitlement or add a permanent extra slot simply because the target now has fewer than a major country. Technologies are selected only from a verified eligible regression history. Existing equipment and buildings are not recreated as duplicate rewards.

The Research Failure specification distinguishes Scientific Capacity from Archive Recovery. This offer primarily restores records and reproducible knowledge. It cannot claim to rebuild laboratories, staff, and all national capacity merely by adding research speed. When the concrete owner implementation is unavailable, this offer remains gated until the promised restoration can be made real.

**Visible concession.** A private archive foundation coordinates access and verifies recovered documents. Its representatives receive access to the restricted records required to do that work.

**Delayed consequence.** The foundation has access to military plans, project histories, and contradictory records that can expose or conceal the visitor. During a revolt it supplies scientific staff, protected sites, or unusual equipment already established by the campaign. It does not independently unlock every Event 016 project.

**Separation.** Remove the foundation and replace its verification network, sacrificing some remaining temporary recovery assistance or surrendering a defined archive advantage. Knowledge already independently verified cannot simply vanish from every mind without an explicitly represented destruction of its institutions. A settlement can restrict access to the recovered collection, close its protected facility, or require an alternative costly reconstruction.

**Loophole.** If the foundation's authority depends on being the only surviving custodian, an independently authenticated second copy can end that exclusivity. The Brilliant Scientist can help authenticate it, but his mere presence does not automatically generate the evidence.

## Scientific offer: A Result Before the Experiment

**Situation.** A current research programme or special project is stalled and the country has the capacity to use a breakthrough. The visitor supplies a working result whose records appear to precede the experiment.

**Reward.** Grant a large owner-supported advance in one eligible project or a proposed 365-day +35 percent research-speed package for a relevant field. The concrete offer chooses one principal form. A safe, eligible technology completion can replace it when the project's graph and required prerequisites have been verified. It must not force-complete an unsafe terminal project, break prerequisite chains, or release a different event's exclusive actor.

**Visible concession.** A scientific coordinator obtains access to the project board and a protected laboratory. Its remit is described in ordinary research terms.

**Delayed consequence.** Scientists cannot reproduce part of the supplied result, or measurements depend on the coordinator being present. This creates a plausible investigation and a real chance to exploit the anomaly. Successful applications remain useful. The story is not that the original reward was secretly worthless.

**Separation.** Suspend the dependent portion of the programme, evacuate independent researchers, and give up the remaining accelerated assistance. The player can preserve reproducible work at a material and time cost. Recklessly retaining a protected laboratory can make it an anchor of the partial revolt.

**Loophole.** The visitor promised a result reproducible without his intervention. A carefully documented failure of that exact promise can open a limited legal and scientific challenge. An ordinary laboratory accident or a deliberately sabotaged demonstration does not automatically prove nonperformance.

## Scientific offer: The Institute Rebuilt

**Situation.** Institutions and specialists, rather than records alone, have collapsed. The offer is distinct from The Missing Archive.

**Reward.** Restore a substantial share of the owner-tracked institutional capacity, rebuild eligible damaged research facilities, and recover living, available specialists through their actual custody rules. A proposed capacity restoration of 50 percent of the recorded deficit is a starting point. Any additional temporary research surge must be justified by the still-existing crisis. Dead scientists do not return as ordinary staff, and a scientist currently held by another country is not silently duplicated.

**Visible concession.** A reconstruction foundation recommends administrators and controls its protected institute. At later tiers it can coordinate the national research system under explicitly stated delegated authority.

**Delayed consequence.** The rebuilt establishment becomes a strong institutional base. Kruger can notice that the new administration knows methods he never published. He may oppose it, negotiate with it, or try to appropriate it. None of those responses transfers ownership of the Scientist's existing project and clone systems to Event 090.

**Separation.** Rebuild an independent administration, dismiss protected appointees, and accept a period of reduced capacity while the institute changes hands. The rebuilding should cost real time and resources, while preserving independently trained personnel and already delivered physical repairs when the settlement permits.

**Loophole.** A foundation established to reconstruct an institute may not lawfully govern every successor institute. A genuine decentralization into independently staffed institutions can frustrate its claim, but simply renaming the same board and buildings cannot.

## Medical promises must work

A medical bargain is one of the event's strongest temptations because the beneficiary is the population rather than an abstract modifier. Its success must be visible in the owning disease or mortality system. It does not quietly create a second infection value whose removal leaves the original epidemic untouched.

Treatment, containment, immunity, and logistical relief are different promises. The text names which is supplied, for which disease or affected group, over which territory, and for how long. A temporary universal treatment effect can treat multiple supported diseases without claiming permanent immunity to all future strains, poison, starvation, and supernatural possession.

The cost comes from access to people, medical administration, records, and protected facilities. A cure is not secretly fake. Breaking the relationship does not automatically reinfect everyone who recovered. Disease later returning through a new exposure is a new event governed by the disease owner, not a retrospective cancellation of the original delivery.

An active Wu institute can provide an independent alternative only through [its actual care sequence](../089_doctor_wu_specs/medicine/089_doctor_wu_spec_part_3_universal_cures.md).
For the named patients, compare matching treatment already available and deliverable with research or manufacture that remains unfinished.
The bargain can remain tempting when extraordinary care arrives sooner or reaches people the independent network cannot yet serve.
Wu's presence alone cannot make an urgent offer irrelevant, and a research design alone cannot count as delivered care.
If independent treatment reaches the promised patients before the offer is answered, refresh or withdraw the obsolete medical promise under the existing pending-offer rule.
A government can decline and pursue independent care without starting Wu's event or receiving his progression from that refusal.
The AI evaluates this visible alternative through the existing acceptance policy, using current access, capacity, and delivery rather than an author-facing promise of eventual cure.

All of the medical content here is fictional game design. It specifies state, unit, and population outcomes, not real pathogen cultivation, laboratory protocols, or methods of biological attack.

## Medical offer: The Wards Go Quiet

**Situation.** A supported epidemic or biological exposure is causing a severe crisis in the host. The visitor offers rapid treatment of a bounded affected area.

**Reward.** Treat the most seriously affected eligible controlled states, with proposed initial maxima of 5, 10, or 20 states by national scale. The actual promise can cover fewer states more thoroughly when that is the meaningful unit of treatment. The disease owner applies a large documented suppression or cure outcome appropriate to the named disease. The offer must not promise to remove an active zombie army merely by curing civilian infection, or to erase the entire Black Plague event by treating an outbreak state.

**Visible concession.** A private hospital network obtains clinical authority and access to records within the treatment area. Its protected personnel are visible as medical staff rather than infernal agents.

**Delayed consequence.** The network can conceal unusual examinations, select vulnerable officials, and move protected personnel. A physician notices a patient who appears in two registers at once. Hospitals can later be sites of resistance, collaboration, or refuge. Patients are not automatically enemy troops.

**Separation.** Transfer the hospitals to ordinary administration, replace continuing medicines and logistics, and lose any remaining extraordinary containment assistance. Already cured people remain cured. The material and political cost is the replacement programme, possible temporary service disruption, and the conflict over the protected institution.

**Loophole.** The network's authority can be conditional on treating the named outbreak, not permanent control over the population's medical records. Proving the outbreak's resolution and securing an independent care system can terminate that authority. Doctor Wu can help verify the medical result, but cannot by that act alone expel possession.

## Medical offer: A Winter Without Funerals

**Situation.** Famine mortality, displacement, or a breakdown of medical distribution threatens living people. The offer addresses supply and survival rather than inventing a treatment for lack of food.

**Reward.** Deliver a major owner-supported relief package: food distribution, functioning medical logistics, a sustained reduction of the named mortality pressure, and protection of a defined affected population for a proposed 180 days. It must improve the actual famine and migration system. A generic manpower increase is not sufficient, and the shared death ledger never subtracts people who already died.

**Visible concession.** A relief commission runs distribution, identifies recipients, and receives protected access to transport and records. Its authority is especially plausible where ordinary administration has collapsed.

**Delayed consequence.** The commission becomes a political patron with local loyalty and a network of depots. Some communities defend it because it really saved them. This is a source of meaningful internal division during breakaway, not proof that every recipient is possessed.

**Separation.** Establish a replacement distribution network and surrender a defined share of the commission's extraordinary logistical advantage. An immediate blanket shutdown can be chosen as a harsh emergency measure, with its real consequences, but it is not the only way to resist. Keeping people alive while removing the institution should be the expensive competent route.

**Loophole.** A commission authorized to serve the public may violate its stated duty by conditioning continued ordinary relief on political obedience. Independent evidence and a replacement provider can weaken its claim. The act of investigating must not itself stop the promised initial relief.

## Medical offer: The Protected Cohort

**Situation.** The host needs a temporary means of keeping a selected military or civilian group functional during a supported disease crisis. This is appropriate for an army defending a critical theater or essential staff keeping a government alive.

**Reward.** A defined cohort receives strong temporary protection or treatment for 180 days through the disease owner. The scope is the selected population or formations, not every person with the country's flag. Disease protection does not imply chemical immunity, immunity to famine, resistance to bullets, or freedom from possession. The exact supported disease list is stated when the offer is made.

**Visible concession.** A protected treatment programme receives continuing access to the cohort and its relevant records. It can recommend medical personnel but does not automatically appoint every battlefield commander.

**Delayed consequence.** The medical network acquires detailed knowledge of the cohort and an opportunity to influence its selected officers or officials. Altered troops can be developed only through an explicit later route. The initial treatment remains a medical success.

**Separation.** End continuing access, replace treatment, and accept the loss of future extraordinary protection. Soldiers already saved do not instantly die. Wu can design a transition programme and protect selected people, at a cost in capacity, time, and risk that preserves his owner rules.

**Loophole.** The promise may guarantee that the cohort remains fit for ordinary national service. Evidence that the network has diverted those people into private service can support a challenge. The player must actually recover their ordinary chain of authority.

## Diplomatic promises and the agency of other governments

Diplomatic rescue cannot pretend that foreign governments are scenery. A negotiated peace, alliance, guarantee, or recognition requires the relevant actors and the owning system. A human target receives a real choice. Mephistopheles can pay, persuade, compromise an already established envoy, or exploit an available diplomatic opening. He cannot promise another player's unconditional agreement and then deliver only a small opinion bonus.

Before offering a diplomatic result, secure a valid deliverable: an owner-controlled sanction can be lifted, a genuine consenting guarantor can be available, an existing mediator can open negotiations, or a particular trade restriction can be relieved. Where foreign consent is still uncertain, the reward must be stated as a valuable opportunity or underwriting arrangement, not a guaranteed outcome. If that opportunity is too weak to tempt the host, select another principal category.

## Diplomatic offer: A Door Back into the World

**Situation.** The host is isolated by a particular sanction, embargo, or recognition dispute that has a supported resolution path.

**Reward.** Remove or substantially weaken the named restriction, restore its associated access for a proposed 180 days, or deliver recognition already secured from actual participating states. Unrelated embargoes and future hostile actions remain possible. The Great Embargo, condemnation, convention enforcement, and bilateral decisions retain their own authority.

**Visible concession.** A private envoy gains immunity and access to diplomatic correspondence relevant to the arrangement. At later tiers, a protected mission can coordinate the host's diplomatic response.

**Delayed consequence.** The envoy can filter warnings, isolate independent diplomats, and establish safe routes for later foreign bargains. Some apparent diplomatic successes begin relying on the same intermediary.

**Separation.** Close or expel the mission, replace its agreements, and accept the loss of remaining visitor-dependent protection. A negotiated restriction already lawfully removed does not reappear from an unrelated system simply because the visitor is angry. Its reimposition requires the real owner and relevant foreign actions.

**Loophole.** Immunity limited to the named mediation does not authorize running a private foreign policy. Captured instructions showing such an extension can justify revoking that mission's protection while preserving independently concluded agreements.

## Diplomatic offer: Someone Will Stand Beside You

**Situation.** The host faces abandonment or a hostile coalition and an actual foreign partner can be persuaded to provide meaningful assistance.

**Reward.** Deliver a concrete consenting guarantee, military-access arrangement, support package, or negotiated alliance opportunity. The offer names the form of protection and its limitations. A guarantee is not a promise that an overwhelmed ally will always win the resulting war.

**Visible concession.** The host recognizes the visitor's intermediary organization and accepts a liaison in the relevant foreign and military offices. This creates a bridge into both countries only to the extent each actually agreed.

**Delayed consequence.** Compromised diplomats may advise both governments. The relationship can become a seed for the foreign-bargain system, but one country's acceptance does not secretly count as the other's main deal.

**Separation.** Remove the liaison and either preserve the alliance through independent diplomacy or surrender the visitor-dependent portion of the arrangement. The ally chooses its response according to its own interest and knowledge.

**Loophole.** An agreement that promises independent support can be challenged if the supposed partner is demonstrably another instrument of the same authority and that independence was an explicit material term. Recognizing mere political sympathy is not sufficient proof.

## Diplomatic offer: The Conversation That Ends the War

**Situation.** A supported negotiated settlement is possible and would materially improve the host's position. This offer should be uncommon when the enemy has no credible incentive to negotiate.

**Reward.** Secure a real settlement proposal with clearly stated terms and an actual consenting or properly simulated negotiation counterpart. If the final treaty is not yet agreed, the principal promise is access to a protected negotiation and a concrete concession already secured. The host cannot be charged for a guaranteed peace that the system cannot deliver.

**Visible concession.** The visitor becomes the protected mediator and receives access to the relevant negotiations. His authority is limited by the signed mandate.

**Delayed consequence.** He possesses sensitive records, diplomatic leverage, and perhaps a role in the postwar settlement. These can matter during later isolation or client negotiations. He does not automatically own every state whose borders changed in the peace.

**Separation.** End the mediation authority and fulfill any legitimate independent settlement obligations. Territorial returns can be a meaningful cost only where the host can actually transfer the territory and the treaty terms support that choice. A free, repeatable annex-and-return reward loop is prohibited.

**Loophole.** A mandate ending when the treaty takes effect can expire. Proving the signed effective terms may require recovering an unaltered copy held abroad.

## Personal offer: One More Morning

**Situation.** A real owner-supported illness, assassination crisis, or scheduled removal threatens the current leader. A generic healthy ruler does not need to be artificially put on a death timer to enable this offer.

**Reward.** Save the leader from the specified threat through the owner system and grant a bounded recovery benefit. This is not universal immortality. Later unrelated attacks, wars, natural transitions, and other event claims retain their normal authority. The rescue must happen before the main acceptance can be treated as fulfilled.

**Visible concession.** A physician or private secretary receives protected personal access. The scope is personal at first; later principal arrangements can connect it to national continuity.

**Delayed consequence.** The leader shows symptoms sooner within the current accepted tier. A medical examination can find impossible discrepancies. The personal route creates a more vulnerable human host, while institutions still determine whether the state can be taken over.

**Separation.** Remove the protected attendant, establish independent care and security, and accept a costly period of weakness or political exposure. The already averted assassination is not replayed, and a healed illness does not automatically return solely to punish refusal. Treatment can cease prospectively when that dependence was part of the delivered arrangement.

**Loophole.** A promise to preserve the leader as a living person can conflict with replacing that person's agency or bodily identity. Proving the distinction is difficult and requires a surviving independent person capable of acting on the evidence. It is a route to contest possession, not a universal instant cure.

## Personal offer: The Country Still Loves You

**Situation.** A scandal, a loss of public support, or an actual succession challenge threatens the leader. The visitor can solve that particular problem without simply replacing the whole nation's ideology.

**Reward.** Resolve a defined owner-supported scandal or challenge, grant a proposed +15 percentage points of stability and a 180-day personal authority benefit, or preserve a legitimate position under specific terms. A personality bonus is a trait only where the character system supports it safely.

**Visible concession.** A confidential adviser manages the leader's correspondence or public schedule. The fourth-tier version can involve a consequential succession or continuity promise that is stated openly.

**Delayed consequence.** The leader becomes reliant on someone who appears to know every rival's intentions. Their smile and eyes become noticeably less natural. A change of leader may remove the most vulnerable body without removing the adviser network.

**Separation.** Publicly expose the manipulation, surrender some restored political advantage, protect a successor, or retire the compromised ruler. The government can survive while that individual loses power. The choice should feel personal and politically consequential.

**Loophole.** A promise that protects a particular office-holder does not automatically bind a successor who never held that person's personal authority. This helps only when the institutional agreements do not independently bind the state. It cannot erase four earlier national mandates.

## Personal offer: An Unbroken Reign

**Situation.** A ruler wants continuity beyond an approaching lawful transition. This is an openly ambitious offer, appropriate after the stranger has established trust or when the leader is genuinely desperate.

**Reward.** Provide the specific supported continuity protection and a powerful finite authority package. The country remains subject to real military defeat, valid external event ownership, and future challenges not covered by the promise. A claim of everlasting rule is not mechanically implemented as immunity to every system.

**Visible concession.** A personal oath and named succession arrangement connect the leader's position to the visitor's appointed representatives. At the final mandate, those institutions can become the instrument of peaceful possession. The visible text should be tempting and consequential rather than filled with unexplained occult terminology.

**Delayed consequence.** The leader's physical and political role becomes difficult to separate from the network. The distributed-government route later grows from this problem. The five-acceptance requirement remains intact.

**Separation.** Renounce the protected position, transfer lawful authority to a real independent successor, or fight to preserve the state while relinquishing the ruler's advantage. Keeping the same person, powers, institution, and benefit while declaring the oath void is not a completed sacrifice.

**Loophole.** A personal title that was never legally held may have been incapable of granting the stated power. Establishing that fact requires actual source evidence in the campaign and often costs the ruler the very legitimacy they hoped to preserve.

## Selecting among personal and public interests

A personal offer should not be selected merely because it gives the visitor faster visible symptoms. The government must have a real leader-related need or ambition. A rational AI weighs the national benefit and the visible political cost rather than optimizing the hidden possession process.

A human player can deliberately save a ruler at a dangerous price. The event should let that choice have both genuine success and lasting consequences. It should also permit a player to sacrifice the ruler's position to preserve independent government. Those alternatives supply much of the personal drama of the final stages.

# Deal with the Devil: discovery, the changing face, and resistance

## Evidence develops into a usable choice

The secret phase works when the player first notices an inconsistency, later understands that several inconsistencies connect, and finally acquires a way to act. It fails when random flavor events can indefinitely withhold the only clue that unlocks resistance.

The first acceptance schedules a rare, ambiguous incident after a proposed 60–120 days. It should relate to the actual institution: a duplicate shipment signature for a procurement bargain, an impossible patient record for a medical programme, or an appointment predating a cabinet meeting. Its presentation does not identify a demon. Dismissing it can preserve immediate calm; retaining the evidence keeps a useful witness or document.

After the second acceptance, a second independent kind of evidence becomes available. A repeated oddity alone should not be the sole route. The government can authorize a quiet ordinary inquiry into the office, audit its records, or consult a relevant specialist. These actions are framed as an investigation into an institution whose behavior has actually become suspicious.

After the third acceptance, the chain guarantees a contradiction serious enough to support meaningful precautions. The player can still deny its significance, but the game must present an actionable choice. After the fourth, identification or an equivalent unmistakable threat must arrive early enough to permit at least 60 days of counterplay before the final main offer. Random incident rolls can add variety; they cannot remove that minimum evidence path.

A government that never accepts any deal has fewer domestic clues. Repeated visits, the final clean invitation, a foreign warning, and the announced manifestation supply a different preparation path. It must not receive an elaborate purge menu for ministries that were never infiltrated.

## A small investigation surface

Before credible evidence, use occasional events and normal appointments. Do not create an always-visible “Demonic Influence” category, a disabled exorcism button, or a locked focus branch whose title gives the secret away.

After credible evidence, a compact ordinary category, proposed **The Independent Inquiry**, offers at most three relevant actions at a time. The initial choices are to protect a witness, audit the implicated institution, or seek one appropriate external assessment. A country without a scientist or useful intelligence service still has a records-and-witness route. Lack of a special character must not make liberation mathematically impossible.

An action consumes a concrete resource and has a defined purpose. Protecting a witness costs security capacity or political power and preserves testimony. An audit commits administrative capacity and exposes the office's actual reach. A medical assessment occupies a physician or treatment capacity and can separate physical symptoms from ordinary illness. An intelligence inquiry has operational risk and can identify a protected foreign contact. None simply grants “minus 20 corruption.”

A proposed ordinary inquiry takes 45 days and 75 political power. Protecting a witness can take 30 days and 50 political power. The exact provider cost replaces those figures when a specialist owns the action. Only one major inquiry and one protective measure run simultaneously in the starting design. These are tuning choices, not a requirement to add parallel progress bars.

The report names what was found, what remains uncertain, and what concrete opportunity follows. Failed investigations can lose time, a witness, or secrecy, but the mandatory late evidence path still exists. A failed roll must not convert a prepared player into an unavoidable fifth acceptance.

## Six incident patterns with distinct consequences

**The signature appears twice.** An official's signature authorizes two incompatible instructions at the same time. Preserving the original copies opens an authority challenge. Public accusation without the originals raises political conflict and may let the protected office dismiss the incident as forgery.

**The patient has no interval.** A clinical record shows a recovery and a transfer with no plausible time between them. Independent examination can identify a particular vulnerable official. Seizing every hospital immediately would sacrifice a real functioning treatment network and is a much harsher choice.

**The order was already obeyed.** A general reports executing a command that the ruler does not remember issuing. Protecting a parallel chain of command prevents a later coup from relying on that route. Ignoring the report leaves a specific headquarters vulnerable; it does not secretly lower every division's loyalty.

**The office cannot be searched.** An ordinary authority discovers the exact immunity or continuity clause granted in an earlier deal. The player sees how a concession once considered reasonable now limits state action. A legal review can establish its scope, while a forced search risks an announced breach dispute.

**The foreign ambassador refuses the meeting.** A diplomat recognizes someone in the visitor's entourage. The government can discreetly hear the warning, protect the contact, or sacrifice that relationship to preserve its current arrangement. The warning supplies knowledge, not automatic proof that every related foreign government is already possessed.

**The photograph is wrong.** A staff photograph shows an increasingly unnatural expression, while an older portrait remains normal. The incident directs attention to the living leader and can open a medical or succession inquiry. It does not require the government to possess a scientific explanation before it takes sensible precautions.

Use the patterns that arise from the chosen bargains. Repetition can reinforce an established motif, but do not replay the same mysterious clock event five times merely to fill the calendar.

## The five portrait stages

One main acceptance produces an almost imperceptible red tint around the eyes and a slight sharpening of the expression. There is no blood. Two make a faint glow, a subtly broader smile, and light shadows noticeable at close inspection. Three produce clearly red eyes, an unnatural smile, deeper shadows, and an optional restrained flicker. Four strengthen the glow and crooked expression, with a small dark or blood detail near the mouth or collar. Five show the full transformed ruler: intense eyes, a fixed unnatural smile, infernal shading, and restrained blood detail suited to the ordinary portrait scale.

Personal bargains intensify a stage's physical symptoms earlier, but cannot display the fully possessed fifth-stage package after fewer than five acceptances solely because the personal route was selected. An overt four-deal usurpation has its own revealed authority presentation, not a false fifth acceptance.

The effect should follow the actual character and current institutional relationship. The underlying portrait remains the source of identity, uniform, pose, age, and lighting. A generic demon head is not a replacement for the recognizable historical or generated ruler. The blood treatment remains restrained; the intended unease comes principally from the altered expression and eyes.

## Reusable effects and source-aware alignment

The brief requests reusable overlays rather than a library of independently repainted historical leaders. The visual plan therefore separates an immutable base portrait from a reusable effect stack and alignment metadata. Eye positions, mouth area, face angle, and relevant occlusion must be known for the selected source portrait. A fixed pair of red dots at the same coordinates for every face is not an acceptable implementation.

An expression change is more demanding than a light tint. A reusable mouth-shadow or highlight effect must not draw a second mouth or distort facial structure. Any actual deformation or animated treatment requires proof that the chosen rendering route can preserve the base identity and work on the supported portrait surfaces. This package does not assert that the game's native portrait renderer supports arbitrary facial warping.

Politics, diplomacy, important event portraits, and any other supported display of the same leader need the same stage and source ownership. Static fallbacks are required for every animated state. An external event that legitimately replaces a character or their portrait must not be overwritten by stale possession code. Unfamiliar portraits require safe alignment rather than copying coordinates from a different ruler.

The release gate is a real in-game demonstration on contrasting portraits: frontal and turned heads, glasses, facial hair, different lighting, a custom leader, and a changed successor. A baked-per-leader replacement pipeline is not silently accepted as the requested reusable solution. If the overlay contract cannot be met, record the limitation and seek an explicit presentation decision.

## Successors, former rulers, and divided vulnerability

Accepted national arrangements survive an ordinary change of leader because their institutions still exist. Personal symptoms do not automatically transfer at full strength to a successor. A protected institutional network can begin approaching the new leader, while the former ruler retains whatever personal vulnerability has been established.

A prepared succession preserves an independent lawful authority, removes the old ruler from binding decisions, and protects the relevant records. It can block a person-centered seizure and open a loophole. It cannot undo protected procurement, hospitals, and continuity boards without addressing them.

An unprepared successor can receive a newly observed sign of institutional pressure. The event explains the relationship rather than silently pasting the former ruler's fifth-stage face onto an unrelated person. During distributed possession, multiple actual hosts can exist, but they remain separately identified. A dead former ruler's portrait is not treated as the current governing body.

The possibility of split vulnerability is a narrative and political condition: the former ruler may remain personally compromised while the successor inherits a compromised administration. Do not expose two new numeric possession meters. Concrete witnesses, appointments, and protected offices are sufficient to make the split playable.

## Three ways to leave

**Negotiated separation** gives up a defined benefit, institution, or continuing service and replaces the essential work under independent authority. This is most viable after one or two main arrangements. Its competence lies in protecting the population and ordinary administration while surrendering the visitor's advantage.

**A discovered loophole** contests an actual term of a particular agreement. It needs a visible contractual basis, proof acquired through play, and a real authority capable of acting on it. It can reduce the price or dismantle a critical part of the network. It is not a magic phrase that erases all accepted history.

**Open resistance** retains or seizes disputed benefits and prepares to fight the visitor's actual institutions and forces. The player gets a larger portion of the material gains at the price of confrontation. After several acceptances, this may be the only way to preserve the country without submitting.

These methods can be combined. A country may lawfully end the archive foundation, replace the hospitals, and fight the military office that refuses to leave. Each successful action changes the concrete opening footprint. There is no single public “pay to cleanse all deals” button.

## Sacrifice should have an object

Returning surviving equipment, surrendering a trust's use of factories for a defined period, closing a protected laboratory, retiring the saved ruler, dismissing a valuable general, or accepting temporary administrative weakness are meaningful sacrifices. A flat political-power fee that leaves every extraordinary benefit and every protected office intact is not.

The object sacrificed should follow the reward. Medical separation replaces care rather than killing cured patients. Scientific separation gives up access, remaining acceleration, or dependent institutions rather than arbitrarily deleting unrelated technology. Diplomatic separation costs the visitor-dependent agreement while preserving independently valid treaties. Military separation settles delivered materiel and command access rather than disbanding an arbitrary half of the army.

When a benefit has been consumed irreversibly, the settlement names a real replacement burden. The government can repay comparable equipment from its own production, surrender an agreed industrial concession, or use proof that changes what is owed. The amount cannot increase without bound because the player took too long to click a menu item.

## Loopholes are local, earned, and fallible

A valid loophole needs four elements: the exact relevant promise, preserved evidence, a substantive fact that satisfies the loophole, and an independent capacity to enforce the result. Missing any one can produce a partial success or a contested settlement.

A contract signed by someone lacking authority is useful only if that lack of authority was real in the campaign. A new government can contest a predecessor's personal agreement without automatically repudiating valid state institutions. Two conflicting copies create an evidentiary struggle; whichever copy happens to be selected by the player is not automatically true. A destroyed named institution can end an institutional appointment while leaving the broader national mandate alive.

Mephistopheles should exploit exact language intelligently. He can dispute scope, offer a revised settlement, try to recover a document, or pressure a witness. He cannot retroactively add a clause that the visible agreement never contained merely to defeat a correctly completed player plan.

Success reduces a specific claim or prevents a particular seizure. The player sees which office has lost its authority, which person is protected, or which material obligation has been settled. They still need to deal with surviving arrangements.

## Defeating the intrusion before possession

A completed early separation removes the visitor's active authority from the surrendered arrangement, closes future unsolicited principal rewards from that relationship, and leaves the country's actual history intact. The saved people, produced equipment, and political memories remain. Scars can include a damaged institution, a retired leader, distrust of extraordinary advisers, or a modest finite reconstruction burden.

A late break can still win, but victory has to defeat or disarm the actual loyalist network as well as the visible leader. Removing a portrait overlay is the visual consequence of a successful political or personal resolution, not the mechanic that makes the government free.

After full peaceful possession, resistance moves into the shared tree's hidden liberation route. Its prerequisites and choices are developed in Part 7. The player cannot simply reopen the early investigation category and buy their country back for the first-deal price.

# Deal with the Devil: manifestation and the three country origins

## A confrontation shaped by what the country allowed

The clean-defiance manifestation, the partial-acceptance revolt, and peaceful possession must remain distinct starts. They share the later infernal tree because they express the same actor's rule, but they do not start with the same territory, army, institutions, or political problem.

Clean defiance gives Mephistopheles a supernatural foothold without a captured human administration. Partial acceptance gives him the actual collaborators, protected facilities, and organizational advantages created by the deals. Peaceful possession gives him the intact state's authority because five accepted arrangements prepared it. The last has the strongest initial political and material position precisely because it avoided the opening civil war.

## Clean defiance: the visitor abandons persuasion

After the last clean refusal, the visitor's final departure is followed by a visible warning interval, proposed at 60 days. Reports identify a gathering threat and a likely region. The host can prepare, ask for help, or protect the threatened administration. It is not required to know the name of every demon formation before it can mobilize against an armed intrusion.

Mephistopheles then appears openly and forms a hostile state on a viable foothold inside the host's territory. The new country's public identity is proposed as **The Infernal Enclave**, with Mephistopheles as its actual leader. Its flag and portraits are openly infernal, and the public reveal can name the event's true author-facing identity.

The clean host contributes no supposed collaborators merely because the event needs a civil-war split. The manifestation's initial army is summoned and sustained by its foothold. Local people remain people living in seized territory; they do not become demons through map ownership. The human army, navy, and air force are not arbitrarily halved.

The initial foothold should be the smallest viable connected region that supports a meaningful military actor while preserving a playable host. It needs a capital, supply, an initial force, and an economic route through the shared tree. A port is required for a naval enclave's military plan. A nominal one-province visual occupation without actual country territory is not the promised hostile country.

The force should be dangerous enough to require a campaign response but materially weaker than the force prepared after multiple accepted bargains at the same Chaos tier. Its limitations are dependence on a small base, scarce summoning capacity, and little knowledge of the host's command system. Those weaknesses give clean refusal a real strategic value.

## Partial acceptance: the country rises against itself

A partial revolt uses the institutions established by accepted main arrangements and surviving local favors. A procurement office supports depots and selected commanders. A trust supports its factories and administrative districts. A hospital network supplies protected sites and logistics, not an automatic army of patients. A research foundation supports specialists, laboratories, and only those unusual projects legitimately available in the campaign. Diplomatic offices supply contacts or access actually secured abroad.

The revolt's proposed public identity is **The Pactbound Directorate** while it relies on captured human institutions. It can later retain the human mask or reveal direct infernal rule through the shared tree. Its opening leadership must match the actual route: Mephistopheles can rule openly, or a known possessed authority can front the regime. It is not a randomly selected historical leader unrelated to the arrangements.

Use a connected viable core of actual network sites. Additional accepted institutions can increase available supply, stock, experienced officers, political cooperation, and the ability to coordinate the revolt. They do not entitle the actor to geographically scattered states with no connection merely to satisfy a fixed percentage.

Protective action matters. A successfully evacuated archive no longer supplies the same rebel research advantage. A depot recovered before the breach cannot also appear as fully stocked rebel territory. A protected independent headquarters prevents the corresponding command seizure. The opening remains more dangerous than clean defiance because other accepted arrangements survive, not because the script ignores what the player did.

## Proposed strength envelopes

The starting balance envelope compares the revolt to the host's **effective sustainable land strength**, not raw division count. Understrength one-battalion formations must not inflate the opponent. The metric used at implementation needs verified inputs and tests; these percentages are design targets rather than an asserted engine calculation.

At the same Chaos tier, clean manifestation targets approximately 15 percent of the host's effective land strength, with a bounded minimum sufficient for a viable actor. A one-arrangement unresolved breach targets about 20 percent. Two surviving main institutional footholds target 30 percent; three target 40 percent; four target 50 percent. These are starting force envelopes before geography, actual surviving assets, and explicit evolution preparations are considered.

Chaos can strengthen the supernatural portion in 5-percentage-point steps up to a proposed additional 20 points. That addition does not turn all accepted human divisions into free replacements. Every branch must remain capped by a viability review that preserves a playable host and avoids enormous global-army snapshots producing an impossible minor theater.

An exact absolute floor and cap require live balance testing for very small and very large countries. The release must test the same acceptance count across small players, medium powers, major powers, occupied hosts, and late-war armies. A table of percentages alone is not evidence of balanced opposition.

In a partial revolt, transferred or reconstructed human assets and genuinely summoned assets are accounted separately. Do not give the actor both a share of a stockpile and another “starting equipment” grant representing the same goods. The host cannot keep a formation that the narrative claims defected while the rebel receives a duplicate of it at full strength.

## Territory, capitals, and foreign wars

Both sides need a valid seat of authority and an operational plan. Preserve the host's capital where possible; otherwise establish a safe replacement before any transfer. The revolt's connected region must include usable supply and cannot take every controlled productive state while claiming the host remains playable.

A foreign war changes the opening. A host already facing invasion may need a smaller but strategically dangerous enclave, an explicit delay while a viable region is established, or a different prepared foothold. The event does not donate enemy-occupied states that the host cannot control. It must not restore the host's occupied industry solely so the revolt can steal it.

Neither side receives an unexplained peace with all outside belligerents. The actual war and diplomacy systems determine inherited wars, intervention, access, and settlement. Mephistopheles can exploit a foreign conflict, but a new army appearing behind the front does not automatically remove the host's original enemies.

Air and naval assets require real bases and usable existing assets or a specifically designed new force. A landlocked manifestation does not inherit half the national fleet. A naval enclave without ships cannot receive an AI plan that assumes effortless overseas conquest. Its origin branch must provide a supported, costly route to transport or a viable local defense.

## The one-state problem must remain visible

A one-state country cannot be split into two state-owning countries while both retain territory under an ordinary state-transfer design. The examined Event 021 documentation uses same-tag contests in unsafe one-state or all-island cases. That is useful evidence of a constraint, not approval to replace Event 090's required separate Mephistophelean country.

Three alternatives require an explicit decision or new engine evidence. First, add a transparent viability condition to original host selection, leaving one-state players untargeted until a supported manifestation foothold exists. Second, obtain specific approval for a same-tag internal siege as the one-state exception, acknowledging that it changes the supplied route. Third, demonstrate a supported separate-country method that preserves both the host and the requested internal origin without stealing unrelated foreign territory or inventing runtime map creation.

This package recommends the first alternative provisionally because it preserves the nature of the event for supported targets. It is **not marked accepted**. The final release must resolve whether that narrower selection is permissible. No source command for runtime creation of a new state is invented here.

If a previously viable host loses its other states before the revolt opens, do not execute an invalid transfer. Preserve the established relationship and visible threat as pending, allow the host's real war or territorial recovery to resolve the issue, and explain the deferral through an appropriate event. Do not reroll the entire parent onto a new country, reset accepted deals, or grant the promised final reward a second time.

An all-island country with several states is not automatically impossible. A single viable rebel island can be connected within itself, provided both actors have real supply and a supported naval or defensive campaign. It needs a separate live test rather than a blanket assumption that the continental setup works unchanged.

## Formation transfer and evidence limits

The examined Event 019 contract records an engine limitation: its natural derivatives use an approved recreate-prove-delete substitute because a division-scoped transfer was unavailable for that workflow. That substitute preserves recorded identity and obligations but does not preserve all live veterancy, equipment variants, decorations, assignments, or orders.

Do not treat that Event 019-specific approval as permission for Event 090 to refresh damaged host divisions or rewrite their history. The Event 090 implementation must either use a verified appropriate native split or obtain approval for a bounded transfer substitute with its losses disclosed. A narrative claim that particular commanders and formations defected must have a defensible connection to what the engine actually moves.

The initial summons are separate newly created formations and should be described as such. Their creation cannot be used to hide duplication of confiscated human units. Before committing the revolt, establish the complete territory and force plan; a failed setup leaves no half-created country, lost human army, or free cargo in either side.

## Peaceful possession: the intact state

After five accepted main arrangements, retain the country's ordinary territory, army, industry, stockpiles, and familiar ruler. Apply the full possession presentation and an appropriate cosmetic identity, proposed as **[Country] under the Covenant** where that phrasing fits the actual country name. The final wording should be localized grammatically rather than assembled into broken names.

The shared Mephistophelean tree replaces the independent political route. The player still commands the state. Existing independent focuses and decisions that directly contradict the new authority become unavailable or are replaced under an explicit transition plan. Completed historical rewards remain unless a particular benefit depends on an institution the takeover actually dissolves. Do not repay already completed focuses because the tree changed.

Production, construction, technology, ordinary military operations, and unrelated surviving country content should remain intact where compatible. A broad instruction to “replace all national content” would unnecessarily destroy the campaign. The political settlement defines which old authorities can no longer act independently.

Prepared human institutions give this origin the strongest initial resources, lowest immediate reconstruction burden, and quickest practical route to foreign bargains. The Hidden Army evolution can add preparations that were actually made, but the basic foreign, military, and client branches do not require it.

## Origin openings in the shared tree

The clean origin begins with establishing a defensible seat, sustaining the manifestation, acquiring local human cooperation, and choosing whether to rule only by force or become capable of making credible offers again. Its first strategic problem is survival with little ordinary administrative legitimacy.

The partial origin begins with joining compromised institutions into a functioning state, resolving rival officers and protected patrons, securing a connected economy, and deciding what to do with the government's remaining human collaborators. Its first strategic problem is turning a network that could sabotage a country into one that can govern territory.

The peaceful origin begins with consolidating continuity, choosing the public mask, assigning the existing army's relationship to the new authority, and using intact diplomatic channels. Its first strategic problem is selecting a form of rule without unnecessarily destroying the advantages obtained by consent.

These openings converge on shared political, military, economic, scientific, foreign, and liberation branches. They are not separate duplicate trees with minor renamed bonuses, and they must not be shortened to a single origin focus that grants everything.

## Victory and defeat

A clean host that defeats the enclave removes its actual territorial actor, summons, and surviving parent authority according to their recorded ownership. It can preserve captured evidence and choose whether to study what remains. Victory does not automatically make every foreign bargain cease if an independently established network survives abroad.

A host defeating a partial revolt must recover the compromised institutions or settle their remaining obligations. Defeating the last field division does not instantly cleanse the cabinet. Conversely, ordinary civilians in liberated territory are not permanently classified as infernal enemies merely because the revolt controlled their state.

An infernal victory can reunify the host through the appropriate origin history and shared tree. It does not masquerade as the five-deal peaceful route. A defeated human player receives the normal supported continuation choices the campaign provides; this event does not promise an extra country switch the engine or multiplayer rules cannot deliver.

Liberation after full possession has its own political struggle and scars. The restored country should retain a meaningful history of what it accepted, sacrificed, and recovered, rather than returning to an untouched pre-event save state.

# Deal with the Devil: the shared tree and the question of rule

## One tree, several genuine political problems

The shared tree belongs to the actual Mephistophelean parent regime: the clean manifestation, the partial revolt, or the fully possessed host. Its ordinary content is baseline progression. Chaos evolutions can change the conditions under which that content operates; they do not unlock the existence of diplomacy, demons, industry, or clients.

The tree has three origin openings feeding a central political spine, with a military lane to one side, economy and scientific institutions to the other, and foreign bargains below the consolidation choices. Liberation appears only when its surviving resistance basis is discovered. It must not be a conspicuously locked branch titled “Remove Mephistopheles” during the secret phase.

The architecture is branch-level design. The implementation can divide a substantial objective into appropriate focus nodes after checking the actual layout, provided it preserves the described choices, dependencies, costs, and outcomes. An unreadable grid of minor +5-percent bonuses is not a fuller interpretation of the tree.

Use mostly 35-day or 70-day focuses as starting pacing, with actions whose real situation is urgent handled by events or decisions rather than waiting through a ceremonial focus. The tree is a strategic commitment; it should not become the only button that allows an army already under attack to receive supplies.

## Clean origin: A Seat in the World

The first objective is to make the manifestation durable. **A Seat in the World** establishes the enclave's public authority and the initial site that sustains its army. It does not grant all later summoning facilities. **The First Garrison** commits resources to a defensible force and local supply. Completing it trades early expansion for a foothold less likely to collapse immediately.

The next choice concerns the people living in the seized territory. **Useful Neighbors** preserves ordinary local administration under strict supervision and improves the ability to produce credible civilian offers later. **The Uninvited Court** replaces that cooperation with direct infernal administration, improving immediate coercive control but making human recruitment, diplomatic recognition, and civilian recovery more difficult. Both retain the actual population and ordinary need for food and care.

**Terms for the Living** opens the shared bargain institutions. Clean defiance did not disprove the value of future offers: it forced Mephistopheles to establish a state capable of delivering them openly. The origin's foreign route starts later in practice because it must first create real surplus and a functioning administration, not because an evolution locks it out.

## Partial origin: The Offices Become a State

**The Offices Become a State** joins surviving protected institutions under a common authority. Its outcomes depend on what actually survived the breakaway. A hospital-based network does not receive a free military academy; an industrial trust does not automatically recover an archive the host successfully evacuated.

**Settle the Patrons** forces a choice between keeping valuable human collaborators with defined privileges and replacing them with more obedient possessed appointees. Retaining the collaborators preserves expertise and recruitment, while leaving genuine centers of self-interest. Replacing them increases direct control and supplies the distributed-host branch at the cost of disruption and more obvious supernatural rule.

**Connect the Protected Sites** repairs supply and administration between the actual rebel footholds. It does not annex disconnected neutral states as a focus reward. Where the revolt begins with only one viable region, the objective develops its internal capacity instead of manufacturing a second region.

**A Government Beyond the Breach** settles the provisional wartime authority into the shared political spine. The country carries the scars and asset losses of its opening conflict. It does not receive a peaceful-possession prosperity grant merely because the same tree is now active.

## Peaceful origin: Everything Continues

**Everything Continues** preserves the apparent continuity of the government and makes the changed political authority explicit to the player controlling it. Existing divisions, factories, and records are organized under the new mandate without a fictional reconstruction from rubble.

**The Familiar Cabinet** chooses how much ordinary administration to preserve. Keeping experienced officials improves economic continuity and foreign plausibility, but preserves people who can later assist liberation. Replacing key independent officials increases the regime's control and weakens that resistance basis while imposing a real administrative cost. This choice should be strategically meaningful rather than an obvious free purge.

**The Army Receives Its Orders** establishes the relationship with the existing armed forces. The player chooses an initial limited Pactbound conversion programme or a predominantly human officer-supervision model. Neither choice destroys the ordinary army to force demon recruitment.

**A Reputation Already Built** gives the intact state an early practical advantage in foreign offers. Its embassies, medical successes, scientific reputation, and material deliveries are usable instruments. It cannot promise more actual resources than the country possesses merely because its origin is peaceful.

## The public face: keep the mask or rule openly

**A Familiar Face** commits to the human mask. Retain the possessed ruler and familiar institutions. Domestic administrative continuity and the credibility of foreign assistance improve, while open summoning and spectacular infernal deployments undermine that advantage. The mask is a method of rule, not a guarantee that no foreign government can discover the truth.

The masked state can still field human Pactbound troops and develop limited demonic forces in controlled settings. It pays a diplomatic and internal cost when it uses those forces openly. The branch is not rendered useless by one unavoidable scripted demon appearing in its capital. The player chooses how far to compromise concealment.

**The Principal Appears** reveals Mephistopheles as the public ruler and adopts the open infernal identity. Direct authority and large-scale demon recruitment become easier to sustain, while ordinary governments are less willing to accept apparently neutral assistance. The country retains ordinary production and population needs; public revelation does not make its economy immune to war or disease.

Revelation is a one-way public fact for witnesses. A later return to a human spokesman can change presentation and diplomatic tactics, but it cannot erase every government's knowledge. Do not let the player alternate mask and revelation to collect both focus rewards repeatedly.

## The seat of authority: one host or an interchangeable government

The public-face choice is separate from the distribution of possession. **One Voice at the Table** concentrates authority in the principal ruler and a small, disciplined core. It provides coherent command and lower administrative conflict. It also leaves a meaningful vulnerability: protecting or separating that ruler can damage the regime if independent institutions survive.

**Many Hands, One Signature** spreads possession through selected ministers and commanders. It reduces the effectiveness of decapitation and allows government to survive the loss of one body. Its costs are more complex loyalties, friction among hosts, stronger visible signs, and the risk that a powerful possessed official develops a distinct claim to local authority.

Distributed possession does not clone unique historical people or give the country several simultaneous copies of the same character. It uses actual eligible people and the existing character ownership rules. A minister controlled by another exclusive actor requires a negotiated interaction or a conflict, not silent reassignment.

A later **The Cabinet Disagrees** crisis tests the distributed route. The player can subordinate rival hosts through costly administrative intervention, grant them limited institutional fiefs, or remove one and accept the resulting disruption. Each path has a real effect on the army, economy, or foreign clients those officials supervise. It is not another public loyalty meter.

## Human cooperation and the limits of coercion

**The People Who Benefited** develops support among people saved or employed by the earlier bargains. It can improve local stability and administrative cooperation in the relevant states. That support is material and political, not universal supernatural obedience. A population may defend its hospital network while opposing possession of the ruler.

**An Ordinary Civil Service** preserves a degree of independent competence. It improves reconstruction, client administration, and the continuity of medical or scientific work. The surviving institutions are also a possible basis for later liberation. This makes tolerance useful enough that a rational infernal player might accept the risk.

**No Appeal Beyond This Office** concentrates coercive authority. It reduces some immediate administrative resistance but increases foreign alarm and the danger of brittle rule. When a revolt or occupation crisis occurs, a state with no trusted ordinary institutions may find it harder to restore normal services. The tree does not pay for the purge twice with both free stability and free industry.

## The economic and scientific junction

**Promises Made Permanent** allows the state to consolidate actual earlier industrial or scientific arrangements into durable institutions. The reward depends on inherited assets, and the new regime pays the costs of reconstruction or permanent operation. It does not create a second copy of every delivered factory.

**The Price of Reliability** invests in civilian services that make future bargains credible and keep the human population functioning. **Beyond Reproducible Results** favors dangerous infernal research and stronger military applications. The branches can share prerequisites but must compete for meaningful industrial and scientific capacity. Completing both eventually should take real investment, not two free short focuses.

Kruger and Doctor Wu add substantive choices only when their actual actors and programmes are present. Their branches are not permanently visible empty references in every campaign. The parent must retain their owner state, authority, research, and risks rather than replacing them with generic +research portraits.

## Foreign strategy from a domestic settlement

**An Offer Worth Carrying** establishes a real delivery institution for foreign bargains. The regime chooses an initial specialty based on its strongest surviving assets: arms, reconstruction, medicine, science, diplomatic access, or political continuity. That specialty improves relevant delivery, not all foreign acceptance rolls indiscriminately.

**Offices Abroad** creates a bounded capacity for active foreign relationships. **Partners in Continuity** opens binding client arrangements. **The Infernal Compact** forms only after the actual membership conditions in Part 9 are satisfied. A focus can prepare the charter, but cannot create missing consenting allies by finishing a timer.

The foreign lane also supports a **Protection or Predation** choice. Protection invests in helping clients survive and increases the credibility of future deals, at a cost in resources and strategic freedom. Predation extracts more immediate industry or military support, increases resistance, and gives foreign coalitions better grounds to intervene. Neither path removes client agency or liberation content.

## Hidden liberation: a surviving independent basis

The possessed state can discover a liberation path when some independent capacity genuinely survived: protected records, a trusted command chain, an uncorrupted physician or scientist, an independent civil service, a protected successor, or an established foreign contact. No particular named special character is mandatory. Having more than one basis provides options and resilience, while having none can make the rare route unavailable until a real new opportunity emerges.

The path begins through an event, not an obviously locked public focus from the opening. **Someone Still Remembers** identifies a person or institution capable of acting independently. **Keep the Record Outside** protects evidence from the possessed administration. **Orders in a Different Hand** establishes an alternative command or legal chain. These are substantial tasks with risks and costs, not three anonymous reductions to a hidden number.

The player controlling the possessed state can decide to assist this resistance. That choice changes their strategic aim but does not immediately remove the regime. It becomes a campaign within the same country, with the risk of discovery and a possible internal war.

## Liberation choices and costs

**Save the Person** attempts to separate the human ruler from the possessing authority. It needs evidence, protection, and a competent specialist or equivalent established route. Success can preserve the person at the price of lost personal advantages and a vulnerable transition. It does not automatically free distributed ministerial hosts or dismantle the state's agreements.

**Save the Office** removes or retires the compromised ruler and restores independent lawful authority through a protected successor. It sacrifices the ruler's position and some continuity benefits. The new government still needs a functioning army and administration capable of enforcing the settlement.

**Break the Government Open** deliberately triggers an internal struggle when peaceful separation is impossible. The resistance's territory and forces come from the institutions and commands it actually preserved. It does not receive a free half-country army just because it chose the liberation branch. The Mephistophelean loyalists retain their own established support and capabilities.

The three approaches can converge on **No One Signs for Us**, the decisive settlement of the remaining principal authority. A courtroom argument alone does not win against an intact infernal army, and a military victory alone does not settle every surviving office. The route combines the relevant political and military outcomes.

## Restoration is not a rewind

A liberated country restores an appropriate independent political route or a supported restoration branch. The original tree cannot simply be loaded in a way that repays completed focuses. Its historical completion state and surviving national institutions must be reconciled.

Earlier real medical successes, verified knowledge, surviving produced equipment, and valid independent treaties remain according to their actual settlement. Extraordinary ongoing benefits end where their source is removed. National scars can include a ruined institute, a retired ruler, a costly reconstructed army, distrust of protected foreign missions, or a finite recovery burden.

Captured infernal knowledge can become a dangerous limited asset, not a free permanent copy of the entire demonic tree. The country chooses whether to preserve a strictly contained research opportunity, destroy it, or entrust it to an existing specialist system. That choice can keep the aftermath interesting without restarting the five-deal chain.

## Focus design constraints

Every substantial focus needs a specific political or material outcome, a cost or opportunity cost where appropriate, and a connection to the country's actual condition. Avoid parallel branches that merely rename identical bonuses. Never lock ordinary demon recruitment, foreign bargains, or client relations behind a Chaos evolution.

The three origin histories must remain available to localisation, AI, achievements, and settlement. A clean enclave that conquered the host is not a peacefully possessed country. A four-deal usurpation is not the full-acceptance achievement route. A liberated client is not automatically the original human host.

# Deal with the Devil: armies, industry, and infernal research

## Three military doctrines of rule

The regime can preserve and supervise a predominantly human army, invest in expensive genuine demons, or develop a combined force. Each is a baseline strategic choice with a distinct economy and diplomatic cost.

The human path uses existing divisions, supply, officers, and ordinary equipment. Pactbound conversions improve obedience and recovery without making soldiers invulnerable. It is the strongest route for preserving a peaceful possession's intact military and foreign plausibility.

The demonic path reduces dependence on human combat manpower and provides unusual assault formations, but requires functioning sites, specialist equipment, industrial investment, and limited summoning throughput. It must not be a free infinite army that appears whenever the player has political power.

The combined path pairs ordinary logistics and line troops with a limited demonic striking arm. Its tradeoff is coordination: different sustainment, officer conflict, and a difficult choice over who controls the decisive formations. The shared tree should reward a deliberate combined plan rather than make it automatically superior in every statistic.

## Pactbound infantry

Pactbound infantry are living human soldiers organized through the regime's command and institutional agreements. They use ordinary manpower and rifles, supplemented by a provider-owned binding or command equipment requirement if the final unit design genuinely needs it. They remain subject to ordinary human disease, famine, and casualty accounting.

Their intended role is dependable line infantry with improved organization recovery and resistance to command disruption, balanced by no universal armor, no magical piercing, and ordinary vulnerability to artillery and encirclement. Starting comparative targets are +15 percent organization recovery and +10 percent defense relative to the selected ordinary reference, with comparable speed and a modest additional support burden. The exact unit values require source and live balance verification.

They can be trained after the corresponding human-army programme is established. A selected conversion must pay its actual costs and preserve or explicitly reconcile the existing formation's state. An ordinary unit does not become a fully reinforced elite merely because a focus changed its name.

Presentation includes a distinct uniform treatment, visible unit equipment or command insignia, a custom counter, and an appropriate human voice set where supported. They are human collaborators, not a demon model wearing a helmet.

## Possessed elite troops

Possessed elites are a smaller human assault and security formation whose officers or soldiers have undergone an explicit later transformation. They are not the default outcome of being cured in a hospital. Their creation requires a real programme, a selected eligible cohort, equipment, and the political commitment to use altered personnel.

Their intended role is a high-organization assault reserve and regime guard. Proposed comparative targets emphasize +20 percent breakthrough and +15 percent organization, with higher supply and specialist replacement costs. They should not outperform heavy demons in every combat statistic or ordinary infantry in cheap mass defense.

Use a bounded conversion or recruitment process. Losing these troops is costly because new personnel and preparation take time. Their human component remains subject to the relevant death and medical systems. Any additional resistance to a particular effect is family-specific and supported, not a national immunity.

The visual package should make the human origin recognizable, with unnatural posture, eyes, or disciplined movement. Distinct animation and sound treatment must be assessed against the actual unit routing. A generic zombie animation is not a neutral substitute for controlled possessed soldiers.

## Infernal shock infantry

These are genuine nonhuman assault troops. They excel at breaking an defended position for a limited campaign and depend on a real summoning and sustainment base. The starting role targets strong soft attack and breakthrough, ordinary anti-armor limitations, and a substantial supply burden. A proposed comparative envelope is +35 percent soft attack, +25 percent breakthrough, and +30 percent supply consumption against the chosen reference infantry.

Their combat manpower requirement can be zero because their bodies are summoned, while human logistics or support personnel remain separately accounted for. Their creation consumes specialist material and time. A destroyed summoning site disrupts future replenishment rather than instantly erasing every creature already deployed without warning.

These troops are spawn-only through a bounded military action in the starting design. They cannot be mass-trained through an ordinary zero-cost manpower queue. The model, silhouette, locomotion, attacks, idle states, death, selection treatment, and counter must clearly identify a real demonic family.

## Demon heavy infantry

Heavy demons are slow breakthrough and defensive anchors. They possess strong armor-like protection and hard-hitting close assault capacity, balanced by low speed, substantial supply and specialist-equipment cost, and vulnerability to concentrated anti-armor or other verified counters. They are not a universal answer to terrain, logistics, air attack, and every enemy weapon.

A starting comparison targets +50 percent defensive staying power and substantial protection, with movement reduced by roughly 25 percent and a high support burden. These are role envelopes, not exact claims about armor formulas. The implementation must test how the concrete armor, piercing, hardness, organization, and equipment model interact.

Recruitment requires an advanced functioning site and more material than shock infantry. A heavy creature's special equipment cannot be refunded as an equal quantity of ordinary infantry rifles. Its footprint, counter, and selection should remain readable at map scale rather than becoming a huge decorative model that obscures the province.

## Fast raiding creatures

The raiding family is mobile, lightly protected, and useful for exploiting breaches, disrupting a poorly defended rear, or rapidly reinforcing a local struggle. It has a distinct gait and silhouette rather than a small reskin of the heavy demon.

The starting role targets approximately +35 percent speed relative to the selected infantry reference, reduced defensive staying power, and limited capability against fortified or armored opposition. Their weakness in sustained frontal combat prevents speed from becoming a universal advantage. Supply disruption from their operations must use supported game outcomes, not a separate invisible province-sabotage system.

They are summoned in bounded cohorts and consume material appropriate to their family. They do not receive free strategic teleportation. Portal movement, where another event genuinely provides it, remains that owner's capability and is not inferred from the word “demon.”

## Summoned support and human auxiliaries

Two support concepts are sufficient for the starting design. **Binding attendants** stabilize and sustain the demonic component of mixed formations, using real support equipment and, where applicable, human staff. **Infernal pioneers** provide an unusual assault or entrenchment role with explicit cost and a limited battlefield purpose. Support-only definitions do not become independent divisions with no combat body.

Possessed commanders use actual character ownership and appropriate traits. Their loss, capture, removal, or liberation affects the commands and political roles they really hold. No commander trait substitutes for a missing custom creature family.

Client auxiliaries are human formations provided through an actual client obligation. Their origin, manpower, equipment, and return or defection terms remain traceable to the client. They are not an unlimited generic demonic spawn pool. Ordinary expeditionary or supported transfer behavior should be preferred when it preserves that ownership accurately.

## Sustaining the army without a new soul economy

Use functioning sites, real industrial allocation, specialist equipment stocks, ordinary political or command capacity, and time. Do not add a public soul currency, sacrifice dashboard, corruption meter, or per-battalion contract ledger simply to make the army look supernatural.

A starting summoning programme operates one site with one batch in preparation at a time. Expansion through the tree adds a limited number of sites or throughput improvements, each with an industrial and administrative cost. A proposed routine batch takes 60 days; a heavy batch takes 90 days. Exact batch size, equipment demand, and cost belong to a single owner balance table after unit definitions are verified.

The military decision surface shows the actual available cohort, its cost, preparation time, and sustainment. At most three currently relevant recruitment choices should be visible together. A family-specific shortage explains why that cohort is unavailable. It must not substitute another family because the requested provider or equipment is missing.

A supply crisis weakens the army through supported mechanics and interrupts new recruitment. It does not convert an equipment shortage into unexplained civilian deaths. Human support staff remain vulnerable to ordinary conditions even when the combat creatures are not.

## Shared Event 019 participation

Event 090 owns its demon families, their material costs, presentation, and parent progression. Event 019 can discover eligible families through the existing owner-side registry contract. New registrations belong in Event 090's owner integration and startup path, not in a second central registry or a new hardcoded Event 019 family list.

Use the examined contract's complete provider surfaces, including exact payment and refund, presentation, eligibility, derivative setup, and cleanup. Provider IDs must be allocated after a current collision check; this package deliberately reserves no unverified numeric IDs. Support families remain attached to appropriate combat providers rather than becoming fictitious standalone divisions.

An Event 019 derivative can use the family and an appropriate neutral massed-host identity. It must not become Mephistopheles, inherit the five deals, unlock the parent tree or foreign bargains, count as a possessed signatory, or activate the Last Signature. The registry's parent-isolation proof is a required boundary.

Family visibility also respects secrecy. Registration alone does not advertise “Mephistopheles's Demons” in every human country's decision list before any relevant appearance. Eligibility and public presentation must be based on a real reveal or supported family encounter. A secret registration is not permission for Event 019 to expose the entire parent event.

Demonic zombies remain under the Zombie Outbreak owner's family. Event 090's demons are distinct. Shared clones, alien organisms, portal troops, and temporal troops retain their existing providers and material histories; an infernal collaboration does not silently rebrand them into newly owned duplicates.

Every new unit and concrete equipment type also needs the project's CXT extension coverage, separately from the Event 019 registration. Neither integration is proven simply because files exist.

## The economy of keeping promises

The regime's economic branch turns actual protected offices into institutions that can govern and produce. Industrial trusts can become permanent production networks. Procurement offices can become a reliable supply administration. Relief commissions can support ordinary population survival. Research foundations can sustain real scientific work. Their inherited footprint and condition determine their starting value.

Three strategic priorities compete. **Reliable Prosperity** preserves human welfare and normal production, making foreign offers more credible and reducing domestic disruption. **The Protected War Economy** concentrates capacity on Pactbound and demonic forces, with civilian and diplomatic costs. **Extraordinary Industry** pursues unusual methods and scientific interactions, accepting dependency on specialist facilities and a higher risk of damaging incidents.

The branches do not conjure factories independently of usable states and slots. A focus that makes an earlier temporary arrangement permanent pays the cost of continuing it or changing its institutional basis. The same delivered factory cannot be counted as a reward again under reconstruction, consolidation, client extraction, and terminal industrial share.

Supply sites and specialist production remain attackable strategic assets. The player can disperse them at additional cost, protect them, or accept concentration for efficiency. Losing one produces a concrete problem in recruitment and delivery, not an arbitrary nationwide “infernal power” number falling without explanation.

## Research with and against the specialists

A scientific regime can use the visitor's impossible methods to accelerate a limited project while accepting reliance on an unreproducible component. The interesting choice is whether to preserve human verification, use the unexplained result as a black box, or attempt to replace the visitor's contribution with reproducible work.

With the Brilliant Scientist, the player can support independent measurement, create a guarded joint project, or place him under the network's authority. Independent measurement helps reliability and potential resistance. A joint project offers powerful but bounded results and a contest over credit, access, and ownership. Coercive control can obtain short-term cooperation while increasing rivalry and the danger of losing the scientist or his institution.

With Doctor Wu, cooperation can treat altered soldiers, protect selected officials, or investigate the physical effects of possession. Weaponizing infernal biology is a dangerous fictional project with real political and institutional consequences, not an automatic extension of every medical cure. Wu's own authority, treatment systems, staff, strains, and opposition remain under his package.

A collaboration needs actual shared access and a valid owner outcome. Merely having both portraits in one country does not unlock every possible project. Cross-event projects should each answer a specific question and create a useful result, cost, failure route, and ownership settlement. No real laboratory procedures are specified here.

## What remains human

A possessed national leader does not turn every state population, hospital, factory worker, ordinary division, and client into a nonhuman entity. Disease, famine, migration, casualties, and ordinary administrative needs continue for human people. Genuine nonhuman combat bodies can have family-specific resistances where those are supported and balanced.

The open enclave likewise rules territory with a human population. Country-level identity classifiers must not be used as a shortcut that erases all civilian consequences. Distinguish the nature of the governing actor, the population affected by an outcome, and the material composition of a formation.

# Deal with the Devil: foreign bargains, clients, and the Infernal Compact

## Assistance becomes foreign policy

An open Mephistophelean state or a fully possessed host can use the same principle abroad: solve a serious problem and acquire a protected institutional relationship. Foreign bargaining is baseline content. The early cross-border evolution changes when limited contacts can begin; it does not create the basic foreign system.

The foreign game should let the infernal player choose a few consequential relationships, finance useful deliveries, and decide how much to extract from resulting clients. It should not become an enormous spreadsheet of hidden influence numbers or a global broadcast of offers every month.

Targets retain agency. A human target receives real choices. An AI target evaluates the visible proposal and what it knows about the benefactor. Countries that know Mephistopheles openly can still accept assistance, but their decision concerns a known dangerous patron rather than an implausible repeated disguise.

## A bounded diplomatic desk

The proposed normal capacity is three active foreign relationships being negotiated at once, with only one live principal offer per target. At each review, show a shortlist of at most five meaningful candidates. The player selects a target and a deliverable offer category; the actual bargain is tailored to that target's real need.

A review of the bounded active relationships occurs through the existing event scheduler or explicit outcomes. The implementation must not scan every country every day. A new candidate refresh can occur at a proposed 60-day cadence, and an individual offer normally gives its recipient 30 days to respond. A human pending popup does not create repeated identical offers.

A refused opening imposes a proposed 180-day reapproach cooldown for the same relationship. A different genuine emergency can justify reconsideration through a specific event, but changing the label on the same cargo is not a way to bypass refusal. The original host's main chain remains distinct and cannot be replaced with cheaper foreign steps while it is incomplete.

The infernal player's ordinary decision surface can show target name, current visible relationship, selected assistance, commitments already made by that target, and pending practical obligations. It does not show a continent-wide grid of hidden corruption. A target sees only its own known arrangement and evidence, not the patron's entire intelligence picture.

## Selecting relevant targets

Prioritize countries facing an actual military defeat, reconstruction need, disease or famine crisis, lost research capacity, diplomatic isolation, leadership danger, or substantial political instability. A peaceful aspirational offer is possible where the promised industrial, scientific, or diplomatic benefit is genuinely useful.

Eligibility also depends on delivery. A landlocked isolated patron cannot promise ordinary overseas transport it cannot provide. A medical intervention needs access to the target population or an explicitly supported delivery route. A scientific recovery needs the target's real loss history. An economic offer needs useful sites and an available production or resource commitment.

Exclude actual nonhuman or terminal actors from ordinary possession bargaining unless a separately designed interaction establishes that they can meaningfully consent to this kind of institutional claim. Complete control by another event is not silently displaced. Ordinary subjects can receive limited assistance, but binding sovereign commitments require their actual legal and diplomatic authority. An overlord's existing relationship is not erased by a hidden foreign-bargain counter.

A subject seeking a binding independent guarantee needs an explicit overlord response, a supported change in its status, or a visible independence conflict. A human overlord is not forced to surrender rights without an action that represents the dispute.

## Foreign rewards consume real capacity

The patron must finance a delivery through its actual stocks, industry, medical access, research institutions, or supported supernatural production. Foreign rewards are not unlimited copies of the original event's one-time generosity. Holding three active negotiations therefore creates meaningful competition for resources.

An arms offer reserves and transfers a bounded useful stock. An industrial programme commits capacity for a stated interval. A medical offer occupies real treatment or delivery capacity and applies the disease owner's outcomes. A scientific offer shares verified knowledge or rebuilds a valid target institution. A diplomatic offer provides something the patron and its partners can actually grant.

If a promised delivery fails before commitment, do not debit the target's concession or advance its binding stage. A partial delivery requires an explicit revised agreement or cancellation. A refusal releases the patron's unused reservation without producing duplicate goods. A war or loss of access can interrupt an ongoing service under the terms the target saw; it cannot retroactively make the initial accepted reward fictional.

## Three foreign commitments

The proposed accelerated foreign sequence has three principal commitments, distinct from the original host's five main acceptances. This shorter structure is new design tuning and remains recorded as a proposal.

The first, **Practical Assistance**, delivers one large useful benefit and establishes a limited local office. Its concession is narrow enough that a government can rationally accept even while distrusting the patron. The target has not yet become a client.

The second, **A Continuing Mission**, expands assistance and permits protected staff or a more durable institutional role. It creates stronger dependency and evidence. The target receives an opportunity to establish independent records, replace part of the service, or refuse further expansion. A secret system does not hide the mission's ordinary public authority.

The third, **A Guarantee of Continuity**, delivers the promised final assistance and establishes a binding client relationship under explicit visible terms. The target must have received enough evidence to understand that accepting affects its independence. The exact identity of a still-masked patron can remain uncertain until the appropriate reveal, but the game cannot describe the final public concession as a trivial one-time adviser appointment.

The steps are normally separated by 90–180 days, with an absolute proposed floor of 60 days. Foreign progression is faster than the original years-long courtship because the patron has an actual state and delivery institutions. It is not a three-click annexation in the same week.

## Client government and open possession are different conditions

A controlled client can retain a local leader and much of its domestic administration while binding policy and selected institutions to the patron. The leader may be compromised, possessed, or constrained by loyal officials according to the actual route. Do not use one portrait state to imply an individual transformation that has not occurred.

Open possession is a further revealed political settlement. It can occur through an explicit client choice, a successful supported takeover after a breach, or a route developed by the patron's political branch. It is not a free fourth acceptance that happened offscreen. The foreign sequence does not become another complete duplicate of the original five-deal narrative.

A client retains a playable local game. Where the chosen native subject relationship imposes restrictions, those restrictions are part of the stated commitment and must be tested. A custom political obligation must not be described as a native autonomy mechanic unless the implementation actually uses it.

Existing completed national content is preserved where compatible. The client receives an appropriately scoped altered political branch and obligations, rather than automatically copying every principal-state focus and world-end power. The local government's resistance route remains available under its concrete conditions.

## Obligations follow the aid received

A military client supplies access, a defined expeditionary or auxiliary contribution, or a bounded equipment commitment. The starting ceiling is approximately 20 percent of its effective sustainable land force for an ordinary ongoing contribution. The final representation needs verified transfer and return behavior. A small threatened client must not surrender its last viable defending formation merely because a fixed minimum says “one division.”

An economic client commits a bounded portion of usable industrial or resource capacity, initially targeting about 10 percent. Use the supported native or owner allocation mechanism, not a hidden destruction of its factories followed by newly created factories for the patron. The same capacity is counted once in all economic and terminal calculations.

A scientific client gives access to a defined programme, knowledge-sharing arrangement, or project capacity. It does not automatically reveal every technology, duplicate a unique scientist, or transfer an exclusive project actor. The target can preserve an independent archive as a costly source of later resistance.

A medical client provides treatment access, staff cooperation, logistics, or a supported modified-troop programme. Its obligation is not an unexplained recurring subtraction of civilian population. The people whom the patron saved remain living beneficiaries with political interests; their gratitude can support the client government without equating gratitude with possession.

A diplomatic client supports recognition, access, mediation, or a specific foreign position. Its involvement follows the actual diplomatic system and cannot automatically bind every member of its former faction. A political client can host missions or introduce the patron to neighboring authorities, but one introduction is not another country's consent.

Later accepted bargains can create a mixed obligation profile. Present the resulting practical duties clearly and cap their combined burden. Do not independently apply the maximum levy from all six categories until the client becomes unusable.

## Protection, extraction, and credibility

A protective patron spends resources to keep clients alive. It can relieve an emergency, defend a client, replace an interrupted service, or accept a temporary reduction of obligations during a genuine crisis. This strengthens its future credibility and reduces resistance without needing a public reputation meter.

An extractive patron demands the maximum supported contribution, insists on protected appointments, and treats defection as a threat to its own authority. It gains more immediate capacity and political control, while damaging the willingness of secure foreign governments to deal with it and increasing the risk of coordinated resistance.

Failure to protect a client against a specifically guaranteed threat is meaningful evidence. It can create a loophole or political movement. It does not automatically cancel every treaty because the patron lost one unrelated battle. The terms of the actual promise matter.

The patron can also make a fresh voluntary offer to a resisting client. That delivery has a real cost and creates an explicit new settlement. It must not reset all opposition and repay all original rewards from the beginning.

## Resistance and defection

A foreign government that accepted only the first assistance can replace the service, close the office, or settle the material obligation at a manageable but meaningful cost. After the second commitment, separation becomes a serious institutional struggle. After client status, it requires the local liberation route, a negotiated release, or a supported conflict.

Local resistance depends on concrete independent capacity: surviving records, officials, military commands, treatment alternatives, foreign guarantees, or a preserved lawful successor. A fully compromised local cabinet can still face popular or military opposition, but those actors need actual organization before they can produce a new playable government.

A client can seek outside help. A third country can offer replacement aid, protect a witness, guarantee the independent government, or intervene through a real war. The helper pays real costs and does not simply click “liberate client” to steal a country without the local struggle.

Defection ends or contests the actual obligations. Expeditionary units, leased capacity, and ongoing services must be returned or reconciled according to their supported ownership. A client cannot keep all transferred foreign materiel and recover its original stockpile again through cleanup. The patron cannot delete all ordinary national industry as a punitive shortcut.

## Conquest and imposed clients

Military victory can install an infernal client government through the supported peace and occupation systems. This is an imposed origin, not proof that the target accepted three foreign bargains. It has greater immediate resistance, weaker credibility as a consensual relationship, and a different route into any later ratification.

A conquered territory is not automatically a possessed signatory to the Last Signature. It must develop an actual government, durable control, sufficient capacity, and a binding political settlement. Releasing many tiny puppets on the eve of the finale does not manufacture a global order.

Foreign control by another event remains a conflict to resolve, not a flag to clear on conquest. The actor that owns a terminal or nonhuman state retains its own defeat and cleanup contract.

## Forming the Infernal Compact

The proposed initial condition is the principal regime plus at least two other meaningful participants: controlled clients, openly committed allies, or governments with binding foreign commitments. At least one participant besides the principal must have a durable binding relationship; two countries that merely received a first consignment do not form an infernal faction by accident.

Prepare the charter through the shared tree, then require the actual parties and valid diplomatic relationships before formation. A faction does not arise from one country alone. A member already constrained by another faction or overlord needs a supported transition and an actual choice or conflict.

The Compact coordinates access, defense, war planning, client obligations, and limited joint formations. It can speed practical delivery because members share institutions and transport. It does not cause every country on the same continent to begin accepting offers.

Members have different motives. A military client wants protection; an industrial partner wants markets and reconstruction; a scientific client wants access; an openly possessed state wants ideological and supernatural consolidation. Their disputes create useful events when the patron demands sacrifices inconsistent with those motives.

## The Compact under strain

A major defeat can expose the gap between guaranteed protection and the patron's actual reach. A failed medical commitment can create an independent hospital network. A distributed possessed minister can favor some clients over others. The response can be a negotiated reduction of duties, a genuine renewed rescue, a coercive intervention, or a factional rupture.

Do not create a second faction-wide corruption meter. The relevant relationships, promises, actual failures, and surviving institutions already provide the basis for consequences. The player should be deciding whom to help and whom to risk losing, not managing thirty nearly identical sliders.

## Foreign activity before the principal reveal

The cross-border evolution permits limited earlier contacts. Those contacts can create a safe house, a compromised diplomat, a sympathetic official, or a small practical favor. Their cadence and scale are below the full foreign system. They cannot produce a complete client empire before the original host has progressed, and they never count as main acceptances for that host.

An early foreign government can warn the host, cooperate with the visitor, or conceal its own involvement. When the principal state later forms, surviving contacts become genuine starting relationships with their actual commitments and history. They do not retroactively become fully binding clients simply because the interface has unlocked.

# Deal with the Devil: evolutions, the Last Signature, and other powers

## Source-preserved evolution requirements

The exact evolution labels and source requirements are preserved in `matrices/090_source_evolution_requirements.md`, extracted directly from the supplied brief. That file is authoritative for the early evolution wording not retained in the interrupted handoff. No replacement name or different early-evolution premise is invented here.

The shared integration rule is consistent across all four evolutions: reaching a Chaos threshold makes an evolution eligible through the normal project evolution system. It is not an immediate popup merely because a number crossed a line. The supplied pre-fire opening-variant exception remains applicable. The evolution state itself adds no Chaos.

Baseline already contains ordinary bargains, five-deal possession, refusal, partial revolt, the shared tree, demon armies, foreign bargains, clients, and the Compact. No implementation should move one of those basic features into an evolution unlock in order to shorten the baseline.

## Stronger persuasion and earlier access

When an early evolution improves the visitor's generosity or tailoring, increase the practical value of a relevant offer, the range of credible opportunities, or the precision with which he addresses a current crisis. Do not make the reward fake, add an acceptance that the player never chose, or hide an ordinary visible concession. The clean first refusal remains unpunished.

An earlier contact with an ordinary official can create a witness, a recommendation, or a point of suspicion. It is not an accepted main arrangement. The host starts with zero accepted deals even when a pre-fire variant already includes unexplained contact. That contact cannot supply the fourth arrangement's constitutional protection or an entire army.

The unsolicited-gift variant uses the explicit inspect, return, quarantine, or adopt choices in Part 2. Pressure to use useful assistance is the gameplay. Passive receipt, a demonstration, or the existence of stock in a port does not silently sign for the country. The exact evolution to which this variant belongs remains the one specified in the preserved source sections.

## Offers Across Borders

At the supplied 600-plus Chaos eligibility threshold, the visitor can test limited favors in other countries before the principal host is fully possessed or an open infernal state exists. Keep the original host as the main narrative target.

The proposed early capacity is two foreign contacts at once, reviewed at a slower 180-day cadence. A contact can produce a safe house, sympathetic diplomat, discreet treatment, small material assistance, or a warning from someone who recognized the visitor. It cannot complete a foreign client chain before the full parent foreign system is available.

A future revolt can receive the actual support secured through those contacts: a volunteer group, a delivery route, recognition, intelligence, or refuge. Foreign helpers are not automatically Mephistophelean countries. They can betray the visitor, bargain with the host, or expose their own earlier agreement.

If the evolution affects the opening variant, one or two foreign governments may already know the visitor under another identity. Their knowledge concerns their actual encounter. The original host does not acquire that knowledge until it receives a warning or other evidence.

## The Hidden Army

At the supplied 800-plus Chaos eligibility threshold, accepted arrangements begin producing more concrete preparations: protected weapons, compromised barracks, depots, command posts, altered personnel, or functioning summoning sites. The arrangement's subject and footprint determine what is prepared.

A military network can organize barracks and officers. An industrial trust can produce equipment and sustain a site. A diplomatic mission can secure a foreign route. Medical and scientific networks can prepare protected facilities and selected personnel only through the actual supported systems. Do not generate the same standardized underground tank army regardless of which bargains were accepted.

These preparations accelerate a later revolt and improve its cohesion. They also give a peacefully possessed state something real to inherit. Each preparation is counted once. It is not both a preexisting depot in transferred territory and a second abstract starting grant.

The pre-fire variant may include a small hidden network, but it counts as no accepted deal and grants no free political control. In clean defiance, such a network can support the supernatural appearance only within the weakest-origin constraints. It cannot secretly supply a mass of loyal human ministries after every offer was refused.

Investigations can discover and disrupt particular preparations. The evolution does not make preparation removal impossible simply because its name says “hidden.” A recovered depot, protected commander, or dismantled site has a concrete effect on the opening plan.

## When an evolution arrives during an existing chain

Apply the new behavior prospectively. Do not retroactively convert every past hospital into a fully prepared barracks or claim that a previously returned gift was accepted after all. Existing institutions can begin new preparations over a meaningful interval, with the corresponding evidence opportunities.

A stronger offer cannot overwrite a pending visible offer without an explicit refresh. A foreign contact does not bypass the one-main-offer limit. A pre-fire variant is not rerun on a country already in the fourth arrangement.

After possession or manifestation, the evolution can improve the corresponding institutions and military preparation under their real costs and capacities. It does not unlock the shared tree for a second time or repay its origin rewards.

## The Last Signature is a global political achievement

The terminal route becomes eligible only when Chaos is **strictly above 1000** and the regime has built a genuine international order. Possessing one major country, conquering a continent without local governments, or summoning a very large army is not enough.

The proposed public reveal occurs only after Mephistopheles has been identified and the basic global-order conditions are present. Before then, ordinary Event Details, locked achievements, and unexplained focus labels must not advertise the route. The author-facing files may describe it fully.

The finale's image is a world whose familiar governments continue to speak in their own voices while the same authority stands behind them. Keep recognizable leaders, local institutions, flags where the mask route warrants them, and visible possession effects. The world does not need to become a single empty red tag to demonstrate control.

## Proposed qualification for ratification

Require at least five durable human governments bound to the order, including the principal if it is a possessed human host. An open nonhuman principal can lead the order but is not counted as an additional possessed human government. At least three qualifying governments must be meaningful current majors, not newly released tiny shells. Their actual control and capacity must have remained durable for a proposed 180 days.

The order must operate across at least three represented continents and control a proposed 60 percent of global usable industrial capacity and 50 percent of verified effective military strength. The exact military measure is an implementation and balance gate: raw division count is insufficient, and unavailable engine statistics must not be replaced by a misleading proxy without review.

At least three of the qualifying governments must have reached their binding relationship through actual foreign commitments or a subsequent genuine ratification, not conquest alone. The Infernal Compact must exist with meaningful membership. The capital and governing institutions of each signatory must be operational rather than merely claimed on the map.

The shared terminal arbitration must allow this route: a stronger exclusive world-end actor or already resolved incompatible end state blocks the launch under its owner rules. This package does not invent a numerical “stronger than every other apocalypse” ranking.

These numbers are explicit proposed tuning. The irreducible requirements are broad international control, multiple possessed or binding governments, genuine industrial and military power, and the shared world-end boundary. Releasing nominal puppets or transferring the same factory between signatories cannot satisfy those requirements repeatedly.

## A ratification crisis, not an invisible instant win

Once the conditions are met, **The Last Signature** becomes an open terminal project for the involved powers, with a proposed 180-day ratification period. The ordinary target status is now meaningful public information: governments are binding themselves to an international order whose nature has been exposed.

Opponents can disrupt the process by liberating a signatory, breaking its governing control, severing an essential promise, defeating sufficient industrial or military capacity, or exposing a valid authority flaw. The patron can protect threatened members, replace interrupted aid, suppress an actual revolt, or negotiate a renewed binding commitment at a real price.

Loss of a required qualification suspends or interrupts ratification under a defined rule rather than silently completing from an old snapshot. The starting proposal pauses progress for a short recoverable interruption of up to 30 days; a longer failure cancels the current attempt and requires a new valid start. A restart does not repay preparatory focus rewards or duplicate client obligations.

A country released on the last day cannot count as a long-established signatory. A capitulated or liberated leader cannot sign through a stale pointer. An Event 019 demon derivative cannot provide the missing government. The terminal claim concerns actual political control.

## The completed order

The final event confirms the binding global order, changes the shared terminal state through the approved owner path, and applies the established super-event and aftermath conventions. Do not assume completion necessarily ends player control, annexes every member, kills all civilians, or removes every remaining war. Those would be separate substantial design claims.

The human mask can preserve a recognizable world whose diplomacy and leadership reveal a common authority. Open-rule members can display more overt identities. Local governments still matter as administrative and military actors, even if their independent political direction has been extinguished by the terminal settlement.

If the shared world-end framework has a specific irreversible completion or post-ending rule, use it. This package does not claim that a new universal liberation-after-the-ending framework already exists. Pre-completion disruption and client liberation are fully required; post-completion reversibility remains a shared-owner integration decision.

## Brilliant Scientist: measuring the impossible

Kruger can identify a gift without a plausible source, compare contradictory records, observe possession, and try to understand or exploit the method. His presence creates stronger opportunities, not automatic knowledge of every hidden arrangement. A scientific result can make him both the best investigator and a highly tempting collaborator.

Three substantial interactions are proposed. **Independent Verification** preserves reproducible work and can create loophole evidence while costing time and access. **A Joint Result** delivers a bounded unusual project and creates a contest over institutional authority. **The Researcher Under Protection** gives the visitor access to a powerful scientist while risking rivalry, compromise, or an attempt by the scientist to seize the method for himself.

The Scientist retains his project histories, authority, special forces, clone systems, and owner cleanup. Event 090 cannot duplicate the unique actor, silently clear his authority, or unlock Germany-specific refinement as generic shared cloning. An infernal scientific programme must earn and use the same actual access and ownership rules as any other consumer.

## Time Traveler: one possible future

The traveler can recognize the visitor, recall a failed bargain, preserve an alternative copy, identify a possible revolt site, or offer a method to conceal a leader or document. The warning is evidence with a provenance and a possible limitation, not guaranteed omniscience.

A future revolt location is a useful lead whose relevance can change when the host dismantles a protected facility. A known loophole still requires the actual agreement and conditions in this timeline. A manipulated timeline can supply misleading information, and the player should have a way to corroborate it rather than be forced to trust or reject the traveler blindly.

The original traveler remains a unique actor with real custody, survival, and copied-knowledge rules. Event 090 does not spawn a spare traveler merely because that would provide convenient counterplay.

## Disease, biological operations, famine, and deaths

Ordinary biological disease, smallpox, tularemia, zombie infection, Black Plague, modified strains, Wu's systems, famine mortality, and contaminated states remain under their actual owners. A bargain names a supported treatment or containment result and invokes that result once. It does not maintain a parallel infection ledger or subtract fatalities already recorded.

Native biological raids retain their own preparation, stock reservations, command costs, cancellation, outcome, and history. Event 090's medical or scientific cooperation cannot debit a payload again in a separate outcome callback. Any offensive fictional-game collaboration requires the native route and owner consequences rather than a new parallel attack decision.

Possessed human leaders and populations remain human for these systems. Genuine demons can receive explicit family-specific treatment. Disease resistance in a selected troop cohort cannot be generalized to every civilian in an infernal-owned state.

## Economic, military, and diplomatic crises

Industrial Boom collapse, Depression effects, blockade, equipment losses, major offensives, fortifications, alliance betrayal, subjects breaking away, and leadership changes can select a relevant bargain. Their existence does not authorize Event 090 to erase all related state or treaty ownership.

Relieve the actual problem through the owner's supported result. Where the owner offers only a partial supported intervention, describe that partial intervention honestly and determine whether it is strong enough to be a principal reward. Otherwise use another offer rather than pretending a generic bonus ended the crisis.

Research Failure is particularly important because its losses distinguish methods, active work, capacity, staff, and slots. Restoring one component cannot be described as restoring the whole scientific establishment. Already existing equipment and buildings do not become duplicate reward stock.

## Other supernatural or manipulative actors

Afrit, the Master, Secret Society Influence, Parliament of Fear, and comparable actors can recognize a rival method, conceal their own role, cooperate temporarily, or expose Mephistopheles. An encounter should arise from overlapping people, territory, institutions, or objectives rather than an obligatory crossover popup in every campaign.

Mephistopheles owns tailored bargains, access granted through accepted rewards, institutional intrusion, possession, and the infernal political order. Other actors retain their own methods, leaders, countries, progress, and terminal claims. No generic “supernatural control” flag silently transfers all of them to the newest event.

For a fully controlled host, the earlier exclusive owner wins the initial eligibility check unless an explicit conflict route is supported. For partial overlapping influence, the interaction can create a rival claim, a warning, or a joint project with a defined settlement. Uninspected owner specifications remain marked as future integration work rather than filled with invented exact rules.

# Deal with the Devil: AI choices, balance, and consequences

## The AI should inhabit the same uncertainty

Before identification, an AI government evaluates the offered practical benefit, its stated concession, the country's current needs, and evidence it actually possesses. It must not query the hidden institutional count or the precise future rebel strength to make a perfectly informed refusal.

Remembering that the government accepted a particular protected mission is legitimate: the government made that decision. Knowing that a hidden threshold is one point from possession is not. Use observable arrangements, visible authority, witnessed incidents, and discovered evidence rather than raw hidden progression values in the acceptance policy.

After identification, the AI can evaluate Mephistopheles as a known actor. A fully possessed regime can also act on its own established institutions and strategic goals. The uncertainty restriction does not require an infernal government to forget what it itself controls.

## A bounded acceptance policy

Start with a visible-reward assessment rather than one universal chance. A stable, secure country with a useful but nonessential offer has a proposed acceptance starting point of 30 percent. A country facing a serious matched shortage starts around 60 percent. An immediate survival crisis with a reward that actually addresses it starts around 85 percent. An irrelevant or infeasible offer is withdrawn or replaced rather than left to a very low acceptance roll.

Adjust for visible concession breadth, ordinary political compatibility, previous fulfilled promises, known failures, available independent alternatives, and evidence of hostile control. A government can value competent assistance while disliking an intrusive continuity authority. Strong scientific, religious, intelligence, or foreign resistance matters when it supplies actual knowledge or an alternative, not merely because a country has a thematic flag.

An ordinary unexposed offer should normally remain within a 10–90 percent acceptance range. Known explicit threats or a terminal military situation can justify carefully reviewed exceptions, but do not use desperation to force every AI acceptance. Conversely, a country facing certain defeat should not refuse effective help solely because an invisible variable says the next deal is dangerous.

The figures are initial behavioral targets, not a final probability formula. The implementation should use a single auditable weight policy and test representative country states. It must not add unrelated random rolls that make the effective acceptance probability impossible to explain.

## Choice profiles

**The secure government** favors clean refusal, independent review, and ordinary alternatives. It can still accept a major scientific or industrial opportunity, especially when the concession is genuinely narrow. Its refusal should not be an absolute thematic ban on unusual assistance.

**The exhausted government** places a high value on immediate survival but tries to preserve an independent command chain or archive when it has evidence. It is the strongest candidate for partial acceptance and a costly later break.

**The personally threatened ruler** values a concrete rescue, while weighing the visible cost to succession and institutions. The national AI should not sacrifice the entire country for a cosmetic trait unless the represented government actually has a reason to do so.

**The informed resister** seeks a specific feasible breakaway plan. It does not pay for three mutually exclusive investigations at once or launch a revolt with no viable force while a safe negotiated separation is available.

**The deliberate collaborator** can choose the possessed-state route when known assistance, ideological opportunity, or an extreme crisis makes collaboration plausible. That route is a valid game outcome, not an AI failure automatically “corrected” by a hidden refusal bonus.

## AI response to evidence and deadlines

When an actionable report arrives, the AI selects one protective priority according to the network it faces. A military intrusion favors command protection. An archive foundation favors independent records. A medical network favors a replacement care system. It should not investigate the most visually dramatic institution while ignoring the one that controls its survival.

Near the final offer, it considers the real cost and feasibility of refusal, separation, and collaboration. It does not assume every refusal is safe, but it also does not surrender merely because its army contains many divisions whose loyalty was never compromised.

Timers and pending popups must be respected. The AI does not accept two main offers on the same day, cycle a cancelled foreign target for repeated rewards, or spend a reserved military cost on another action while pretending the reservation still exists.

## AI for the three origins

A clean enclave prioritizes defending its foothold, establishing supply, and building a credible economic base. It should not declare several foreign wars before it can survive the host's counterattack. It can make future offers once it has genuine delivery capacity.

A partial revolt secures its connected industrial and command assets, handles collaborator conflict, and avoids wasting its opening advantage on geographically impossible objectives. Its plan should respond to the actual surviving institutions, including any that the host disrupted before the revolt.

A peacefully possessed major initially preserves the intact state's strengths. It chooses human, demonic, or combined military development based on manpower, industry, access, and enemies. It should not disband an effective human army simply because a new demon recruitment category is available.

Foreign strategy favors a few useful relationships, protects or extracts from them according to the chosen route, and responds to real defection risks. The AI does not offer a cure where it lacks access or spend the same stockpile simultaneously on three foreign deliveries.

## Chaos accounting

Chaos level 1 remains the event's catalog classification. It is not a statement that every later civil war or terminal outcome adds exactly one point. The ordinary parent firing follows the shared event scheduler's existing accounting once; this package does not invent a replacement for that system.

The proposed event-specific increments are recorded in the outcome matrix. Routine acceptance, a portrait change, an investigation, side-favor bookkeeping, and evolution activation produce no independent Chaos award. Concrete manifestation, usurpation, full possession, foreign political capture, and the terminal order can produce bounded increments under the shared owner contract.

Deaths, ordinary war consequences, biological exposure, famine, condemnation, and related outcomes remain owned by their shared systems. Do not count the same death once as a medical failure, again as a revolt event, and again when its state is transferred. Demonic bodies are not silently counted as dead human civilians.

A replayed report, reloaded focus, renamed country, or restored pending transaction cannot pay the same event-specific increment twice. The visible story can be reported to several countries while the world outcome is accounted once.

## Proposed outcome increments

For initial balance review, use +20 event-specific Chaos for clean manifestation, +25 for a one-arrangement open breach, +30 for a two-arrangement revolt, +40 for a three-arrangement revolt, and +50 for a four-arrangement revolt or overt seizure. These increments describe the supernatural political outcome separately from normal war and death accounting; the implementation must confirm the shared system does not already own that same increment.

Full five-deal peaceful possession has a proposed +40 increment. A binding foreign client established through a completed political capture has +10, while open client possession can add a further +10 only if it represents a distinct later outcome rather than the same capture being reported twice. Faction formation alone grants no additional Chaos. The completed Last Signature uses the shared terminal outcome and a proposed Event 090-specific +100 only if that is compatible with the terminal owner.

These are proposals, not inherited source constants. If the shared Chaos system already posts an equivalent political-outcome award, retain one owner and reconcile the table rather than stacking two versions. A simple policy decision belongs in the implementation register; it does not require another parallel Chaos system.

## Tension, condemnation, and diplomatic consequences

Conventional world tension, war entry, sanctions, condemnation, and diplomatic opinion are not interchangeable with Chaos. An openly aggressive revolt or a coerced foreign takeover can have a legitimate diplomatic consequence, but it must use the actual system and appropriate provenance.

A government accepting a medical cure is not automatically condemned as though it launched a biological attack. A possessed state's actual offensive use of a supported weapon receives the normal consequences. Revealing supernatural rule can alter diplomatic acceptance and alignment without fabricating an atrocity that never occurred.

## Balance goals and failure signs

The first reward must make a rational player pause. Clean refusal should preserve the strongest independent human administration and produce the weakest hostile manifestation, while still requiring a response. Early separation should be expensive enough that the first gift is not a free repeatable exploit. Partial acceptance should create the richest confrontations because the country's own saved institutions have become part of the struggle.

Full acceptance should preserve the largest initial asset base and offer a satisfying new regime game. It must not be an obviously inferior trap whose tree is much smaller than the independent country's old content. Foreign offers should be useful enough to tempt desperate targets and costly enough that the patron cannot buy the world from an infinite stockpile.

A design fails if the player can repeatedly receive the same aid by changing leader, if every AI accepts all five offers, if medical breakaway kills cured patients, if clean refusal transfers a random half-army, if the final warning arrives after the fifth choice, or if the terminal route can be completed by releasing empty puppets.

## Required play patterns

Balance testing must include deliberate full acceptance, total clean refusal, a first-deal withdrawal, two- and three-deal breaks with different institutions, a four-deal protected refusal, an unprepared overt seizure, and post-possession liberation. Each pattern needs both ordinary and high-Chaos variants.

Test small players, conventional majors, countries at war, countries with no war, leaders who change, countries affected by multiple crises, a foreign human target, existing subjects, and the unresolved one-state boundary. Source inspection and the abstract model included with this package are useful preparation. They are not substitutes for these in-game outcomes.

# Deal with the Devil: presentation, assets, and remembered outcomes

## An ordinary surface that becomes difficult to ignore

The opening uses normal event popups, neutral public ideas, ordinary appointments, and subtle portrait changes. It has no special contract window, corruption bar, seals, progress stages, or secret-phase custom dashboard. The later revelation earns a more overt visual vocabulary.

Use no more than three Event 090-owned national spirits at once in the ordinary presentation. Consolidate ongoing assistance under one neutral arrangement spirit where that does not obscure the visible current effects, use a second for the current institutional or regime condition, and reserve a third for an active resistance or reconstruction burden. Do not create a new persistent icon for every accepted office and every portrait tier. Temporary effects still need accurate tooltips even when the icon count is consolidated.

Neutral names must describe actual public benefits or institutions. A first medical programme can be an “Emergency Treatment Network”; it cannot be a “Demonic Hospital” whose tooltip silently spoils the stranger. After identification, the appropriate public names and descriptions can change without changing who owns the underlying effect.

## Event and report art

The opening image shows a private meeting with a composed stranger. The surroundings should fit the selected institution and era without portraying the stranger as a caricature of a real ethnic, religious, or national group. His menace comes from composure and impossible access.

Use a small coherent set of later images: an institutional irregularity, an independent examination or audit, a protected office confronted, an open manifestation, an intact cabinet after possession, a liberated seat of government, and a foreign agreement. Variants can reuse a coherent scene where the actual situation permits; an unrelated dramatic battlefield picture is not a suitable generic medical report.

The open reveal can support a super-event because it changes the public identity of the crisis. Peaceful possession needs a distinct treatment: continuity made uncanny rather than a city inexplicably destroyed. The Last Signature, when actually reached, has its own global image and restrained presentation of familiar leaders under one authority.

Actual image generation is not part of this planning delivery. The asset prompt specifies later ImageGen use for original images and references, with source and rights records. A reference concept is not evidence that a final in-game asset has been generated, integrated, or reviewed.

## Portraits and identities

Provide a distinct Mephistopheles portrait with compatible masked and openly revealed treatments where required. The same individual should be recognizable across those states. The possessed human ruler uses the five overlay stages and static fallbacks described in Part 5.

Country identities need appropriate names, flags, emblems, and map colors for the clean enclave, partial institutional regime, peaceful possessed host, open-rule conversion, foreign client variants, and liberation. Do not create hundreds of nearly identical hardcoded historical-country flags if a supported reusable identity treatment exists. Conversely, do not claim a generic runtime flag compositor is available without demonstrating it.

The masked identity should preserve enough national familiarity that the country still feels like the one the player developed. The open identity can be unmistakably infernal. Client presentation must distinguish political dependence from a fully possessed individual leader.

## Focus, decision, and unit art

The shared tree needs a coherent icon language for authority, the human mask, distributed hosts, ordinary administration, demon forces, industry, research, foreign offices, client obligations, and liberation. Icons should not be unique for uniqueness's sake, but major choices deserve distinguishable symbols.

Decision imagery should group the independent inquiry, breakaway, recruitment, foreign assistance, and client management without creating a separate decorative window for each. Before discovery, an invisible future category must not leak its name through a tooltip or an empty icon slot.

Every genuine demon combat family needs its own complete production package: a readable concept, model, textures, rig, skeletal actions, correct runtime entities, counter, and sourced audio treatment. At minimum, plan idle, movement, attack, hit response where supported, and death behavior. Required actions must be mapped to the actual engine's unit animation expectations.

A renamed vanilla infantry model does not satisfy a heavy demon or raiding-creature requirement. A screenshot of a 3D model does not prove that its rig and animations work in the game. A turntable or editor preview is useful evidence, but final acceptance needs the actual runtime unit and its selection, movement, combat, and death.

## Audio and rights

Use sourced and licensed sound for unit voices and effects, with creator, source, license, permitted use, and any attribution recorded. Do not assume that a film clip, commercial game voice, or online upload is reusable because it is easy to download. Original recorded or generated material also needs a clear provenance record and a supported runtime format.

The human, possessed, shock, heavy, raiding, and support families need coherent but distinct audio direction. A heavy demon should not sound like ordinary human rifle infantry. Mixed-country audio is a specific engine gate: verify whether the selection-voice route can distinguish human and demon families within the same country. A country-level voice selector might not provide the requested separation.

Where a voice distinction is unsupported, report it and evaluate a supported nonvoice family treatment or another explicit design decision. Do not mark the sound package complete by assigning one generic voice set to everything and claiming family-specific routing was tested.

Super-event audio needs the same provenance discipline. The planning package does not assert that a particular track is licensed or select a commercial recording by implication.

## Proposed achievements

**No Signature of Mine** records total clean refusal and the defeat of the resulting actual manifestation. It requires zero accepted main deals in that parent chain. A host that accepted one deal and later repaid it does not qualify as never having signed.

**The Fifth Appointment** records five valid main acceptances and peaceful possession without an opening civil war. A four-deal usurpation, a conquered client, or a generic Event 019 derivative does not qualify.

**Read the Other Copy** records a successful discovered loophole that materially ends a real institutional claim. Clicking an investigation or receiving a flavor document is insufficient.

**The Patients Stay Well** records a successful medical bargain followed by independence restored while the actual treated population remains protected through a supported replacement or completed cure outcome. It does not require preventing every future unrelated illness forever.

**A Face of Your Own** records liberation of a possessed human ruler where the actual person survives and independent authority is restored. Replacing the portrait with a different character does not meet the personal survival condition.

**A Government Without Him** records successful post-possession liberation through preserved institutions even when the original ruler cannot be retained. It rewards a distinct political solution rather than duplicating the personal rescue achievement.

**Partners of Their Own Free Will** records a meaningful Compact built through the required binding foreign bargains rather than purely imposed conquest. The exact membership count should align with the final accepted foreign design.

**The Last Signature** records completion of the genuine terminal route under its current owner conditions. Tiny released shells, duplicated actors, stale major status, and family derivatives cannot satisfy its requirements.

The titles are proposed final wording subject to localisation review. Achievement definitions and notifications must respect the campaign knowledge boundary. Before relevant discovery, display a supported hidden-achievement treatment or neutral completion presentation. Do not spoil the fifth appointment or terminal route through a visible locked description on day one.

## Localisation voice

The stranger speaks precisely, courteously, and with a quiet sense that an agreement already exists in the room. His humor can be dry. Government responses can show irony, disbelief, determination, or opportunism without making every option a joke. The first acceptance should sound like a government solving a problem, not knowingly volunteering for a villain's tutorial.

Investigation reports distinguish facts from conclusions. A record can be impossible without immediately naming the cause. Later reports become firmer as evidence accumulates. The final peaceful-possession text should emphasize how little ordinary procedure changes even though independent authority has gone.

Tooltips remain exact about visible effects, durations, and ordinary concessions. The secrecy rule is not permission to omit a real visible cost. Grammar must handle the actual country, ruler, institution, and foreign target rather than concatenating broken phrases around tags.

## Acceptance of the presentation package

The asset manifest must identify planned, generated, integrated, statically inspected, and live-tested states separately. Empty fields are work remaining, not proof of completion. Every final asset has a source or provenance and an actual in-game role.

Release requires evidence that the secret phase stays secret across the normal player surfaces, that late possession is unmistakable, that the ruler's identity remains recognizable, that the shared focus layout is usable, and that genuine demon units have the requested complete presentation. No current package file claims those assets have already been produced.
