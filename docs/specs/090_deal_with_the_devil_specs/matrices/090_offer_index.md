Every selected variant must answer five questions before implementation: what exactly arrives; what visible authority is granted; which later institution can use that authority; what an independent government sacrifices to separate; and what precise discovered fact could support its particular loophole. No generic reward fallback is permitted to stand in for an unfulfilled specific promise.
