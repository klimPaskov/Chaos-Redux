## Focus lanes

The origin lane preserves the clean enclave, partial institutional revolt, and intact peaceful possession. The political spine separates public mask from distribution of authority. The military lane offers human, genuine demonic, and combined forces. The economic/scientific lane makes inherited promises durable and chooses reliable services or dangerous dependence. The foreign lane funds credible offers, clients, and the Compact. Liberation is a discovered counter-route, not a day-one public lane.

Do not add a decision merely to mirror every focus. Focuses establish durable strategic programmes; events and decisions handle immediate bargains, investigations, emergencies, deliveries, and specific clients. The implementation may split the branch-level objectives into an appropriate graph without deleting a choice or turning the branch into repetitive minor modifiers.
