# Deal with the Devil: armies, industry, and infernal research

## Three military doctrines of rule

The regime can preserve and supervise a predominantly human army, invest in expensive genuine demons, or develop a combined force. Each is a baseline strategic choice with a distinct economy and diplomatic cost.

The human path uses existing divisions, supply, officers, and ordinary equipment. Pactbound conversions improve obedience and recovery without making soldiers invulnerable. It is the strongest route for preserving a peaceful possession's intact military and foreign plausibility.

The demonic path reduces dependence on human combat manpower and provides unusual assault formations, but requires functioning sites, specialist equipment, industrial investment, and limited summoning throughput. It must not be a free infinite army that appears whenever the player has political power.

The combined path pairs ordinary logistics and line troops with a limited demonic striking arm. Its tradeoff is coordination: different sustainment, officer conflict, and a difficult choice over who controls the decisive formations. The shared tree should reward a deliberate combined plan rather than make it automatically superior in every statistic.

## Pactbound infantry

Pactbound infantry are living human soldiers organized through the regime's command and institutional agreements. They use ordinary manpower and rifles, supplemented by a provider-owned binding or command equipment requirement if the final unit design genuinely needs it. They remain subject to ordinary human disease, famine, and casualty accounting.

Their intended role is dependable line infantry with improved organization recovery and resistance to command disruption, balanced by no universal armor, no magical piercing, and ordinary vulnerability to artillery and encirclement. Starting comparative targets are +15 percent organization recovery and +10 percent defense relative to the selected ordinary reference, with comparable speed and a modest additional support burden. The exact unit values require source and live balance verification.

They can be trained after the corresponding human-army programme is established. A selected conversion must pay its actual costs and preserve or explicitly reconcile the existing formation's state. An ordinary unit does not become a fully reinforced elite merely because a focus changed its name.

Presentation includes a distinct uniform treatment, visible unit equipment or command insignia, a custom counter, and an appropriate human voice set where supported. They are human collaborators, not a demon model wearing a helmet.

## Possessed elite troops

Possessed elites are a smaller human assault and security formation whose officers or soldiers have undergone an explicit later transformation. They are not the default outcome of being cured in a hospital. Their creation requires a real programme, a selected eligible cohort, equipment, and the political commitment to use altered personnel.

Their intended role is a high-organization assault reserve and regime guard. Proposed comparative targets emphasize +20 percent breakthrough and +15 percent organization, with higher supply and specialist replacement costs. They should not outperform heavy demons in every combat statistic or ordinary infantry in cheap mass defense.

Use a bounded conversion or recruitment process. Losing these troops is costly because new personnel and preparation take time. Their human component remains subject to the relevant death and medical systems. Any additional resistance to a particular effect is family-specific and supported, not a national immunity.

The visual package should make the human origin recognizable, with unnatural posture, eyes, or disciplined movement. Distinct animation and sound treatment must be assessed against the actual unit routing. A generic zombie animation is not a neutral substitute for controlled possessed soldiers.

## Infernal shock infantry

These are genuine nonhuman assault troops. They excel at breaking an defended position for a limited campaign and depend on a real summoning and sustainment base. The starting role targets strong soft attack and breakthrough, ordinary anti-armor limitations, and a substantial supply burden. A proposed comparative envelope is +35 percent soft attack, +25 percent breakthrough, and +30 percent supply consumption against the chosen reference infantry.

Their combat manpower requirement can be zero because their bodies are summoned, while human logistics or support personnel remain separately accounted for. Their creation consumes specialist material and time. A destroyed summoning site disrupts future replenishment rather than instantly erasing every creature already deployed without warning.

These troops are spawn-only through a bounded military action in the starting design. They cannot be mass-trained through an ordinary zero-cost manpower queue. The model, silhouette, locomotion, attacks, idle states, death, selection treatment, and counter must clearly identify a real demonic family.

## Demon heavy infantry

Heavy demons are slow breakthrough and defensive anchors. They possess strong armor-like protection and hard-hitting close assault capacity, balanced by low speed, substantial supply and specialist-equipment cost, and vulnerability to concentrated anti-armor or other verified counters. They are not a universal answer to terrain, logistics, air attack, and every enemy weapon.

A starting comparison targets +50 percent defensive staying power and substantial protection, with movement reduced by roughly 25 percent and a high support burden. These are role envelopes, not exact claims about armor formulas. The implementation must test how the concrete armor, piercing, hardness, organization, and equipment model interact.

Recruitment requires an advanced functioning site and more material than shock infantry. A heavy creature's special equipment cannot be refunded as an equal quantity of ordinary infantry rifles. Its footprint, counter, and selection should remain readable at map scale rather than becoming a huge decorative model that obscures the province.

## Fast raiding creatures

The raiding family is mobile, lightly protected, and useful for exploiting breaches, disrupting a poorly defended rear, or rapidly reinforcing a local struggle. It has a distinct gait and silhouette rather than a small reskin of the heavy demon.

The starting role targets approximately +35 percent speed relative to the selected infantry reference, reduced defensive staying power, and limited capability against fortified or armored opposition. Their weakness in sustained frontal combat prevents speed from becoming a universal advantage. Supply disruption from their operations must use supported game outcomes, not a separate invisible province-sabotage system.

They are summoned in bounded cohorts and consume material appropriate to their family. They do not receive free strategic teleportation. Portal movement, where another event genuinely provides it, remains that owner's capability and is not inferred from the word “demon.”

## Summoned support and human auxiliaries

Two support concepts are sufficient for the starting design. **Binding attendants** stabilize and sustain the demonic component of mixed formations, using real support equipment and, where applicable, human staff. **Infernal pioneers** provide an unusual assault or entrenchment role with explicit cost and a limited battlefield purpose. Support-only definitions do not become independent divisions with no combat body.

Possessed commanders use actual character ownership and appropriate traits. Their loss, capture, removal, or liberation affects the commands and political roles they really hold. No commander trait substitutes for a missing custom creature family.

Client auxiliaries are human formations provided through an actual client obligation. Their origin, manpower, equipment, and return or defection terms remain traceable to the client. They are not an unlimited generic demonic spawn pool. Ordinary expeditionary or supported transfer behavior should be preferred when it preserves that ownership accurately.

## Sustaining the army without a new soul economy

Use functioning sites, real industrial allocation, specialist equipment stocks, ordinary political or command capacity, and time. Do not add a public soul currency, sacrifice dashboard, corruption meter, or per-battalion contract ledger simply to make the army look supernatural.

A starting summoning programme operates one site with one batch in preparation at a time. Expansion through the tree adds a limited number of sites or throughput improvements, each with an industrial and administrative cost. A proposed routine batch takes 60 days; a heavy batch takes 90 days. Exact batch size, equipment demand, and cost belong to a single owner balance table after unit definitions are verified.

The military decision surface shows the actual available cohort, its cost, preparation time, and sustainment. At most three currently relevant recruitment choices should be visible together. A family-specific shortage explains why that cohort is unavailable. It must not substitute another family because the requested provider or equipment is missing.

A supply crisis weakens the army through supported mechanics and interrupts new recruitment. It does not convert an equipment shortage into unexplained civilian deaths. Human support staff remain vulnerable to ordinary conditions even when the combat creatures are not.

## Shared Event 019 participation

Event 090 owns its demon families, their material costs, presentation, and parent progression. Event 019 can discover eligible families through the existing owner-side registry contract. New registrations belong in Event 090's owner integration and startup path, not in a second central registry or a new hardcoded Event 019 family list.

Use the examined contract's complete provider surfaces, including exact payment and refund, presentation, eligibility, derivative setup, and cleanup. Provider IDs must be allocated after a current collision check; this package deliberately reserves no unverified numeric IDs. Support families remain attached to appropriate combat providers rather than becoming fictitious standalone divisions.

An Event 019 derivative can use the family and an appropriate neutral massed-host identity. It must not become Mephistopheles, inherit the five deals, unlock the parent tree or foreign bargains, count as a possessed signatory, or activate the Last Signature. The registry's parent-isolation proof is a required boundary.

Family visibility also respects secrecy. Registration alone does not advertise “Mephistopheles's Demons” in every human country's decision list before any relevant appearance. Eligibility and public presentation must be based on a real reveal or supported family encounter. A secret registration is not permission for Event 019 to expose the entire parent event.

Demonic zombies remain under the Zombie Outbreak owner's family. Event 090's demons are distinct. Shared clones, alien organisms, portal troops, and temporal troops retain their existing providers and material histories; an infernal collaboration does not silently rebrand them into newly owned duplicates.

Every new unit and concrete equipment type also needs the project's CXT extension coverage, separately from the Event 019 registration. Neither integration is proven simply because files exist.

## The economy of keeping promises

The regime's economic branch turns actual protected offices into institutions that can govern and produce. Industrial trusts can become permanent production networks. Procurement offices can become a reliable supply administration. Relief commissions can support ordinary population survival. Research foundations can sustain real scientific work. Their inherited footprint and condition determine their starting value.

Three strategic priorities compete. **Reliable Prosperity** preserves human welfare and normal production, making foreign offers more credible and reducing domestic disruption. **The Protected War Economy** concentrates capacity on Pactbound and demonic forces, with civilian and diplomatic costs. **Extraordinary Industry** pursues unusual methods and scientific interactions, accepting dependency on specialist facilities and a higher risk of damaging incidents.

The branches do not conjure factories independently of usable states and slots. A focus that makes an earlier temporary arrangement permanent pays the cost of continuing it or changing its institutional basis. The same delivered factory cannot be counted as a reward again under reconstruction, consolidation, client extraction, and terminal industrial share.

Supply sites and specialist production remain attackable strategic assets. The player can disperse them at additional cost, protect them, or accept concentration for efficiency. Losing one produces a concrete problem in recruitment and delivery, not an arbitrary nationwide “infernal power” number falling without explanation.

## Research with and against the specialists

A scientific regime can use the visitor's impossible methods to accelerate a limited project while accepting reliance on an unreproducible component. The interesting choice is whether to preserve human verification, use the unexplained result as a black box, or attempt to replace the visitor's contribution with reproducible work.

With the Brilliant Scientist, the player can support independent measurement, create a guarded joint project, or place him under the network's authority. Independent measurement helps reliability and potential resistance. A joint project offers powerful but bounded results and a contest over credit, access, and ownership. Coercive control can obtain short-term cooperation while increasing rivalry and the danger of losing the scientist or his institution.

With Doctor Wu, cooperation can treat altered soldiers, protect selected officials, or investigate the physical effects of possession. Weaponizing infernal biology is a dangerous fictional project with real political and institutional consequences, not an automatic extension of every medical cure. Wu's own authority, treatment systems, staff, strains, and opposition remain under his package.

A collaboration needs actual shared access and a valid owner outcome. Merely having both portraits in one country does not unlock every possible project. Cross-event projects should each answer a specific question and create a useful result, cost, failure route, and ownership settlement. No real laboratory procedures are specified here.

## What remains human

A possessed national leader does not turn every state population, hospital, factory worker, ordinary division, and client into a nonhuman entity. Disease, famine, migration, casualties, and ordinary administrative needs continue for human people. Genuine nonhuman combat bodies can have family-specific resistances where those are supported and balanced.

The open enclave likewise rules territory with a human population. Country-level identity classifiers must not be used as a shortcut that erases all civilian consequences. Distinguish the nature of the governing actor, the population affected by an outcome, and the material composition of a formation.
