# Deal with the Devil: the shared tree and the question of rule

## One tree, several genuine political problems

The shared tree belongs to the actual Mephistophelean parent regime: the clean manifestation, the partial revolt, or the fully possessed host. Its ordinary content is baseline progression. Chaos evolutions can change the conditions under which that content operates; they do not unlock the existence of diplomacy, demons, industry, or clients.

The tree has three origin openings feeding a central political spine, with a military lane to one side, economy and scientific institutions to the other, and foreign bargains below the consolidation choices. Liberation appears only when its surviving resistance basis is discovered. It must not be a conspicuously locked branch titled “Remove Mephistopheles” during the secret phase.

The architecture is branch-level design. The implementation can divide a substantial objective into appropriate focus nodes after checking the actual layout, provided it preserves the described choices, dependencies, costs, and outcomes. An unreadable grid of minor +5-percent bonuses is not a fuller interpretation of the tree.

Use mostly 35-day or 70-day focuses as starting pacing, with actions whose real situation is urgent handled by events or decisions rather than waiting through a ceremonial focus. The tree is a strategic commitment; it should not become the only button that allows an army already under attack to receive supplies.

## Clean origin: A Seat in the World

The first objective is to make the manifestation durable. **A Seat in the World** establishes the enclave's public authority and the initial site that sustains its army. It does not grant all later summoning facilities. **The First Garrison** commits resources to a defensible force and local supply. Completing it trades early expansion for a foothold less likely to collapse immediately.

The next choice concerns the people living in the seized territory. **Useful Neighbors** preserves ordinary local administration under strict supervision and improves the ability to produce credible civilian offers later. **The Uninvited Court** replaces that cooperation with direct infernal administration, improving immediate coercive control but making human recruitment, diplomatic recognition, and civilian recovery more difficult. Both retain the actual population and ordinary need for food and care.

**Terms for the Living** opens the shared bargain institutions. Clean defiance did not disprove the value of future offers: it forced Mephistopheles to establish a state capable of delivering them openly. The origin's foreign route starts later in practice because it must first create real surplus and a functioning administration, not because an evolution locks it out.

## Partial origin: The Offices Become a State

**The Offices Become a State** joins surviving protected institutions under a common authority. Its outcomes depend on what actually survived the breakaway. A hospital-based network does not receive a free military academy; an industrial trust does not automatically recover an archive the host successfully evacuated.

**Settle the Patrons** forces a choice between keeping valuable human collaborators with defined privileges and replacing them with more obedient possessed appointees. Retaining the collaborators preserves expertise and recruitment, while leaving genuine centers of self-interest. Replacing them increases direct control and supplies the distributed-host branch at the cost of disruption and more obvious supernatural rule.

**Connect the Protected Sites** repairs supply and administration between the actual rebel footholds. It does not annex disconnected neutral states as a focus reward. Where the revolt begins with only one viable region, the objective develops its internal capacity instead of manufacturing a second region.

**A Government Beyond the Breach** settles the provisional wartime authority into the shared political spine. The country carries the scars and asset losses of its opening conflict. It does not receive a peaceful-possession prosperity grant merely because the same tree is now active.

## Peaceful origin: Everything Continues

**Everything Continues** preserves the apparent continuity of the government and makes the changed political authority explicit to the player controlling it. Existing divisions, factories, and records are organized under the new mandate without a fictional reconstruction from rubble.

**The Familiar Cabinet** chooses how much ordinary administration to preserve. Keeping experienced officials improves economic continuity and foreign plausibility, but preserves people who can later assist liberation. Replacing key independent officials increases the regime's control and weakens that resistance basis while imposing a real administrative cost. This choice should be strategically meaningful rather than an obvious free purge.

**The Army Receives Its Orders** establishes the relationship with the existing armed forces. The player chooses an initial limited Pactbound conversion programme or a predominantly human officer-supervision model. Neither choice destroys the ordinary army to force demon recruitment.

**A Reputation Already Built** gives the intact state an early practical advantage in foreign offers. Its embassies, medical successes, scientific reputation, and material deliveries are usable instruments. It cannot promise more actual resources than the country possesses merely because its origin is peaceful.

## The public face: keep the mask or rule openly

**A Familiar Face** commits to the human mask. Retain the possessed ruler and familiar institutions. Domestic administrative continuity and the credibility of foreign assistance improve, while open summoning and spectacular infernal deployments undermine that advantage. The mask is a method of rule, not a guarantee that no foreign government can discover the truth.

The masked state can still field human Pactbound troops and develop limited demonic forces in controlled settings. It pays a diplomatic and internal cost when it uses those forces openly. The branch is not rendered useless by one unavoidable scripted demon appearing in its capital. The player chooses how far to compromise concealment.

**The Principal Appears** reveals Mephistopheles as the public ruler and adopts the open infernal identity. Direct authority and large-scale demon recruitment become easier to sustain, while ordinary governments are less willing to accept apparently neutral assistance. The country retains ordinary production and population needs; public revelation does not make its economy immune to war or disease.

Revelation is a one-way public fact for witnesses. A later return to a human spokesman can change presentation and diplomatic tactics, but it cannot erase every government's knowledge. Do not let the player alternate mask and revelation to collect both focus rewards repeatedly.

## The seat of authority: one host or an interchangeable government

The public-face choice is separate from the distribution of possession. **One Voice at the Table** concentrates authority in the principal ruler and a small, disciplined core. It provides coherent command and lower administrative conflict. It also leaves a meaningful vulnerability: protecting or separating that ruler can damage the regime if independent institutions survive.

**Many Hands, One Signature** spreads possession through selected ministers and commanders. It reduces the effectiveness of decapitation and allows government to survive the loss of one body. Its costs are more complex loyalties, friction among hosts, stronger visible signs, and the risk that a powerful possessed official develops a distinct claim to local authority.

Distributed possession does not clone unique historical people or give the country several simultaneous copies of the same character. It uses actual eligible people and the existing character ownership rules. A minister controlled by another exclusive actor requires a negotiated interaction or a conflict, not silent reassignment.

A later **The Cabinet Disagrees** crisis tests the distributed route. The player can subordinate rival hosts through costly administrative intervention, grant them limited institutional fiefs, or remove one and accept the resulting disruption. Each path has a real effect on the army, economy, or foreign clients those officials supervise. It is not another public loyalty meter.

## Human cooperation and the limits of coercion

**The People Who Benefited** develops support among people saved or employed by the earlier bargains. It can improve local stability and administrative cooperation in the relevant states. That support is material and political, not universal supernatural obedience. A population may defend its hospital network while opposing possession of the ruler.

**An Ordinary Civil Service** preserves a degree of independent competence. It improves reconstruction, client administration, and the continuity of medical or scientific work. The surviving institutions are also a possible basis for later liberation. This makes tolerance useful enough that a rational infernal player might accept the risk.

**No Appeal Beyond This Office** concentrates coercive authority. It reduces some immediate administrative resistance but increases foreign alarm and the danger of brittle rule. When a revolt or occupation crisis occurs, a state with no trusted ordinary institutions may find it harder to restore normal services. The tree does not pay for the purge twice with both free stability and free industry.

## The economic and scientific junction

**Promises Made Permanent** allows the state to consolidate actual earlier industrial or scientific arrangements into durable institutions. The reward depends on inherited assets, and the new regime pays the costs of reconstruction or permanent operation. It does not create a second copy of every delivered factory.

**The Price of Reliability** invests in civilian services that make future bargains credible and keep the human population functioning. **Beyond Reproducible Results** favors dangerous infernal research and stronger military applications. The branches can share prerequisites but must compete for meaningful industrial and scientific capacity. Completing both eventually should take real investment, not two free short focuses.

Kruger and Doctor Wu add substantive choices only when their actual actors and programmes are present. Their branches are not permanently visible empty references in every campaign. The parent must retain their owner state, authority, research, and risks rather than replacing them with generic +research portraits.

## Foreign strategy from a domestic settlement

**An Offer Worth Carrying** establishes a real delivery institution for foreign bargains. The regime chooses an initial specialty based on its strongest surviving assets: arms, reconstruction, medicine, science, diplomatic access, or political continuity. That specialty improves relevant delivery, not all foreign acceptance rolls indiscriminately.

**Offices Abroad** creates a bounded capacity for active foreign relationships. **Partners in Continuity** opens binding client arrangements. **The Infernal Compact** forms only after the actual membership conditions in Part 9 are satisfied. A focus can prepare the charter, but cannot create missing consenting allies by finishing a timer.

The foreign lane also supports a **Protection or Predation** choice. Protection invests in helping clients survive and increases the credibility of future deals, at a cost in resources and strategic freedom. Predation extracts more immediate industry or military support, increases resistance, and gives foreign coalitions better grounds to intervene. Neither path removes client agency or liberation content.

## Hidden liberation: a surviving independent basis

The possessed state can discover a liberation path when some independent capacity genuinely survived: protected records, a trusted command chain, an uncorrupted physician or scientist, an independent civil service, a protected successor, or an established foreign contact. No particular named special character is mandatory. Having more than one basis provides options and resilience, while having none can make the rare route unavailable until a real new opportunity emerges.

The path begins through an event, not an obviously locked public focus from the opening. **Someone Still Remembers** identifies a person or institution capable of acting independently. **Keep the Record Outside** protects evidence from the possessed administration. **Orders in a Different Hand** establishes an alternative command or legal chain. These are substantial tasks with risks and costs, not three anonymous reductions to a hidden number.

The player controlling the possessed state can decide to assist this resistance. That choice changes their strategic aim but does not immediately remove the regime. It becomes a campaign within the same country, with the risk of discovery and a possible internal war.

## Liberation choices and costs

**Save the Person** attempts to separate the human ruler from the possessing authority. It needs evidence, protection, and a competent specialist or equivalent established route. Success can preserve the person at the price of lost personal advantages and a vulnerable transition. It does not automatically free distributed ministerial hosts or dismantle the state's agreements.

**Save the Office** removes or retires the compromised ruler and restores independent lawful authority through a protected successor. It sacrifices the ruler's position and some continuity benefits. The new government still needs a functioning army and administration capable of enforcing the settlement.

**Break the Government Open** deliberately triggers an internal struggle when peaceful separation is impossible. The resistance's territory and forces come from the institutions and commands it actually preserved. It does not receive a free half-country army just because it chose the liberation branch. The Mephistophelean loyalists retain their own established support and capabilities.

The three approaches can converge on **No One Signs for Us**, the decisive settlement of the remaining principal authority. A courtroom argument alone does not win against an intact infernal army, and a military victory alone does not settle every surviving office. The route combines the relevant political and military outcomes.

## Restoration is not a rewind

A liberated country restores an appropriate independent political route or a supported restoration branch. The original tree cannot simply be loaded in a way that repays completed focuses. Its historical completion state and surviving national institutions must be reconciled.

Earlier real medical successes, verified knowledge, surviving produced equipment, and valid independent treaties remain according to their actual settlement. Extraordinary ongoing benefits end where their source is removed. National scars can include a ruined institute, a retired ruler, a costly reconstructed army, distrust of protected foreign missions, or a finite recovery burden.

Captured infernal knowledge can become a dangerous limited asset, not a free permanent copy of the entire demonic tree. The country chooses whether to preserve a strictly contained research opportunity, destroy it, or entrust it to an existing specialist system. That choice can keep the aftermath interesting without restarting the five-deal chain.

## Focus design constraints

Every substantial focus needs a specific political or material outcome, a cost or opportunity cost where appropriate, and a connection to the country's actual condition. Avoid parallel branches that merely rename identical bonuses. Never lock ordinary demon recruitment, foreign bargains, or client relations behind a Chaos evolution.

The three origin histories must remain available to localisation, AI, achievements, and settlement. A clean enclave that conquered the host is not a peacefully possessed country. A four-deal usurpation is not the full-acceptance achievement route. A liberated client is not automatically the original human host.
