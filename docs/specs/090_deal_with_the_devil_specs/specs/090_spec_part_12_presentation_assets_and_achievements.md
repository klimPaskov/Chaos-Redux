# Deal with the Devil: presentation, assets, and remembered outcomes

## An ordinary surface that becomes difficult to ignore

The opening uses normal event popups, neutral public ideas, ordinary appointments, and subtle portrait changes. It has no special contract window, corruption bar, seals, progress stages, or secret-phase custom dashboard. The later revelation earns a more overt visual vocabulary.

Use no more than three Event 090-owned national spirits at once in the ordinary presentation. Consolidate ongoing assistance under one neutral arrangement spirit where that does not obscure the visible current effects, use a second for the current institutional or regime condition, and reserve a third for an active resistance or reconstruction burden. Do not create a new persistent icon for every accepted office and every portrait tier. Temporary effects still need accurate tooltips even when the icon count is consolidated.

Neutral names must describe actual public benefits or institutions. A first medical programme can be an “Emergency Treatment Network”; it cannot be a “Demonic Hospital” whose tooltip silently spoils the stranger. After identification, the appropriate public names and descriptions can change without changing who owns the underlying effect.

## Event and report art

The opening image shows a private meeting with a composed stranger. The surroundings should fit the selected institution and era without portraying the stranger as a caricature of a real ethnic, religious, or national group. His menace comes from composure and impossible access.

Use a small coherent set of later images: an institutional irregularity, an independent examination or audit, a protected office confronted, an open manifestation, an intact cabinet after possession, a liberated seat of government, and a foreign agreement. Variants can reuse a coherent scene where the actual situation permits; an unrelated dramatic battlefield picture is not a suitable generic medical report.

The open reveal can support a super-event because it changes the public identity of the crisis. Peaceful possession needs a distinct treatment: continuity made uncanny rather than a city inexplicably destroyed. The Last Signature, when actually reached, has its own global image and restrained presentation of familiar leaders under one authority.

Actual image generation is not part of this planning delivery. The asset prompt specifies later ImageGen use for original images and references, with source and rights records. A reference concept is not evidence that a final in-game asset has been generated, integrated, or reviewed.

## Portraits and identities

Provide a distinct Mephistopheles portrait with compatible masked and openly revealed treatments where required. The same individual should be recognizable across those states. The possessed human ruler uses the five overlay stages and static fallbacks described in Part 5.

Country identities need appropriate names, flags, emblems, and map colors for the clean enclave, partial institutional regime, peaceful possessed host, open-rule conversion, foreign client variants, and liberation. Do not create hundreds of nearly identical hardcoded historical-country flags if a supported reusable identity treatment exists. Conversely, do not claim a generic runtime flag compositor is available without demonstrating it.

The masked identity should preserve enough national familiarity that the country still feels like the one the player developed. The open identity can be unmistakably infernal. Client presentation must distinguish political dependence from a fully possessed individual leader.

## Focus, decision, and unit art

The shared tree needs a coherent icon language for authority, the human mask, distributed hosts, ordinary administration, demon forces, industry, research, foreign offices, client obligations, and liberation. Icons should not be unique for uniqueness's sake, but major choices deserve distinguishable symbols.

Decision imagery should group the independent inquiry, breakaway, recruitment, foreign assistance, and client management without creating a separate decorative window for each. Before discovery, an invisible future category must not leak its name through a tooltip or an empty icon slot.

Every genuine demon combat family needs its own complete production package: a readable concept, model, textures, rig, skeletal actions, correct runtime entities, counter, and sourced audio treatment. At minimum, plan idle, movement, attack, hit response where supported, and death behavior. Required actions must be mapped to the actual engine's unit animation expectations.

A renamed vanilla infantry model does not satisfy a heavy demon or raiding-creature requirement. A screenshot of a 3D model does not prove that its rig and animations work in the game. A turntable or editor preview is useful evidence, but final acceptance needs the actual runtime unit and its selection, movement, combat, and death.

## Audio and rights

Use sourced and licensed sound for unit voices and effects, with creator, source, license, permitted use, and any attribution recorded. Do not assume that a film clip, commercial game voice, or online upload is reusable because it is easy to download. Original recorded or generated material also needs a clear provenance record and a supported runtime format.

The human, possessed, shock, heavy, raiding, and support families need coherent but distinct audio direction. A heavy demon should not sound like ordinary human rifle infantry. Mixed-country audio is a specific engine gate: verify whether the selection-voice route can distinguish human and demon families within the same country. A country-level voice selector might not provide the requested separation.

Where a voice distinction is unsupported, report it and evaluate a supported nonvoice family treatment or another explicit design decision. Do not mark the sound package complete by assigning one generic voice set to everything and claiming family-specific routing was tested.

Super-event audio needs the same provenance discipline. The planning package does not assert that a particular track is licensed or select a commercial recording by implication.

## Proposed achievements

**No Signature of Mine** records total clean refusal and the defeat of the resulting actual manifestation. It requires zero accepted main deals in that parent chain. A host that accepted one deal and later repaid it does not qualify as never having signed.

**The Fifth Appointment** records five valid main acceptances and peaceful possession without an opening civil war. A four-deal usurpation, a conquered client, or a generic Event 019 derivative does not qualify.

**Read the Other Copy** records a successful discovered loophole that materially ends a real institutional claim. Clicking an investigation or receiving a flavor document is insufficient.

**The Patients Stay Well** records a successful medical bargain followed by independence restored while the actual treated population remains protected through a supported replacement or completed cure outcome. It does not require preventing every future unrelated illness forever.

**A Face of Your Own** records liberation of a possessed human ruler where the actual person survives and independent authority is restored. Replacing the portrait with a different character does not meet the personal survival condition.

**A Government Without Him** records successful post-possession liberation through preserved institutions even when the original ruler cannot be retained. It rewards a distinct political solution rather than duplicating the personal rescue achievement.

**Partners of Their Own Free Will** records a meaningful Compact built through the required binding foreign bargains rather than purely imposed conquest. The exact membership count should align with the final accepted foreign design.

**The Last Signature** records completion of the genuine terminal route under its current owner conditions. Tiny released shells, duplicated actors, stale major status, and family derivatives cannot satisfy its requirements.

The titles are proposed final wording subject to localisation review. Achievement definitions and notifications must respect the campaign knowledge boundary. Before relevant discovery, display a supported hidden-achievement treatment or neutral completion presentation. Do not spoil the fifth appointment or terminal route through a visible locked description on day one.

## Localisation voice

The stranger speaks precisely, courteously, and with a quiet sense that an agreement already exists in the room. His humor can be dry. Government responses can show irony, disbelief, determination, or opportunism without making every option a joke. The first acceptance should sound like a government solving a problem, not knowingly volunteering for a villain's tutorial.

Investigation reports distinguish facts from conclusions. A record can be impossible without immediately naming the cause. Later reports become firmer as evidence accumulates. The final peaceful-possession text should emphasize how little ordinary procedure changes even though independent authority has gone.

Tooltips remain exact about visible effects, durations, and ordinary concessions. The secrecy rule is not permission to omit a real visible cost. Grammar must handle the actual country, ruler, institution, and foreign target rather than concatenating broken phrases around tags.

## Acceptance of the presentation package

The asset manifest must identify planned, generated, integrated, statically inspected, and live-tested states separately. Empty fields are work remaining, not proof of completion. Every final asset has a source or provenance and an actual in-game role.

Release requires evidence that the secret phase stays secret across the normal player surfaces, that late possession is unmistakable, that the ruler's identity remains recognizable, that the shared focus layout is usable, and that genuine demon units have the requested complete presentation. No current package file claims those assets have already been produced.
