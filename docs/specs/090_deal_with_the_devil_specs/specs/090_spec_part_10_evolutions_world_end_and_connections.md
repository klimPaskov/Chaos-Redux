# Deal with the Devil: evolutions, the Last Signature, and other powers

## Evolution rules

The exact evolution labels and source requirements are preserved in [the source evolution requirements](../matrices/090_source_evolution_requirements.md), extracted directly from the user's brief.
That file is authoritative for evolution names, thresholds and premises.

Reaching a Chaos threshold makes an evolution eligible through the normal project evolution system with MTTH-style pacing.
It is not an immediate popup merely because a number crossed a line, except that an evolution eligible before the event first fires changes the opening variant.
The evolution state itself adds no Chaos.

Baseline already contains ordinary bargains, five-deal possession, refusal, retaliation, partial revolt, the shared tree, demon armies, foreign bargains, clients, and the Compact.
No implementation moves one of those basic features into an evolution unlock in order to shorten the baseline.

When an evolution arrives during an existing chain, apply it prospectively.
Do not retroactively convert every past hospital into a prepared barracks or claim that a previously returned gift was accepted after all.
A stronger offer cannot overwrite a pending visible offer without an explicit refresh, and a pre-fire variant is not rerun on a country already in the chain.
After possession or manifestation, an evolution improves the corresponding institutions under their real costs and capacities; it does not unlock the shared tree a second time or repay its origin rewards.

## Evolution I: The Offer Learns (200+)

Accepted bargains stop being fixed gifts.
When the host's situation changes, each accepted arrangement with an ongoing component can adapt once per arrangement through an **arrangement adapts** event.
A military gift turns to a new front, an economic benefit shifts toward an industry Mephistopheles wants, a medical programme begins choosing its own patients.

The event offers two answers.
Accepting the adaptation keeps the benefit running against the new problem, and the institution's footprint grows to cover the new sector, which strengthens its contribution to retaliation and to a revolt.
Refusing ends the ongoing component early but leaves the institution in place.
Neither answer counts as a main acceptance or a refusal of a main offer.

Before the event first fires, the opening offer selects the best-matching variant with the improved second-visit value and arrives with its demonstration already delivered: a sample of the cargo, a cured ward, a signed preliminary agreement.
The demonstration is evidence, not acceptance, and the first refusal remains unpunished.

## Evolution II: Possession Moves (400+)

The influence no longer depends on one person.
In the baseline, removing a compromised official ends that person's part in the network, and only the four-deal network can pass authority on (Part 2).
With this evolution, from the first arrangement onward, removing a compromised adviser, minister or commander moves the influence to another person tied to the same arrangement, and the report event names who now holds it.
Separation therefore has to close the institution; dismissing its staff one by one no longer works.

A leader change produces one of three outcomes, chosen by which safeguards exist rather than by a hidden roll.
With no Protected Successor, the portrait stage transfers to the successor.
With a Protected Successor but a former leader still in the country, the former leader remains as a **possessed rival**: an ordinary political figure whose faction can contest decisions until removed through the inquiry.
With a Protected Successor and an active Loyal Command, control splits into **two competing centres**: the new government is free, but the old leader's network keeps its institutions and reaches the next settlement as a partial revolt.
None of these outcomes adds or removes a main acceptance.

Before the first offer, one ordinary official already has unexplained contact with the stranger.
That official is an early witness for an alert player and an early host for the network; the country still begins with zero accepted deals.

## Evolution III: Offers Across Borders (600+)

The visitor tests limited favours in other countries before the original host is fully possessed or an open infernal state exists.
The original host remains the main narrative target.

The proposed early capacity is two foreign contacts at once, reviewed at a slower 180-day cadence.
A contact can produce a safe house, sympathetic diplomat, discreet treatment, small material assistance, or a warning from someone who recognized the visitor.
It cannot complete a foreign client chain before the full parent foreign system is available, and it never counts as a main acceptance for the host.

A future revolt receives the actual support secured through those contacts: a volunteer group, a delivery route, recognition, intelligence, or refuge.
Foreign helpers are not automatically Mephistophelean countries.
They can betray the visitor, bargain with the host, or expose their own earlier agreement; a contact that exposes him becomes an Outside Witness for the host.
When the principal state later forms, surviving contacts become genuine starting relationships with their actual commitments and history.

If the evolution affects the opening variant, one or two foreign governments may already know the visitor under another identity.
Their knowledge concerns their actual encounter.
The original host does not acquire it until it receives a warning or other evidence.

## Evolution IV: The Hidden Army (800+)

Accepted arrangements begin producing concrete preparations: protected weapons, compromised barracks, depots, command posts, altered personnel, foreign volunteers, or functioning summoning sites.
The arrangement's subject and footprint determine what is prepared.

A military network organizes barracks and officers.
An industrial trust produces equipment and sustains a site.
A diplomatic mission secures a foreign route or volunteers.
Medical and scientific networks prepare protected facilities and selected personnel only through the actual supported systems.
Do not generate the same standardized underground tank army regardless of which bargains were accepted.

Each preparation appears as a named site in a state the arrangement already touches.
It makes a later revolt start faster and more coherent, adds its prepared units to the Chaos-scaled force envelope (Part 6), and gives a peacefully possessed state prepared units and facilities on day one.
Each preparation is counted once, either as a site in transferred territory or as a starting grant, never both.

Investigations can find and dismantle particular preparations, and each one dismantled shrinks the opening plan.
Before the first offer, a small hidden network can exist in the host; it counts as no accepted deal and grants no political control.
In clean defiance, such a network supports the supernatural appearance only within the weakest-origin limits; it cannot supply loyal human ministries after every offer was refused.

## The Last Signature is a global political achievement

The terminal route becomes eligible only when Chaos is **strictly above 1000** and the regime has built a genuine international order.
Possessing one major country, conquering a continent without local governments, or summoning a very large army is not enough.

The public reveal occurs only after Mephistopheles has been identified and the basic global-order conditions are present.
Before then, ordinary Event Details, locked achievements, and unexplained focus labels do not advertise the route.

The finale's image is a world whose familiar governments continue to speak in their own voices while the same authority stands behind them.
Keep recognizable leaders, local institutions, flags where the mask route warrants them, and visible possession effects.
The world does not need to become a single empty red tag to demonstrate control.

## Qualification for ratification

Require at least five durable human governments bound to the order, including the principal if it is a possessed human host.
An open nonhuman principal can lead the order but is not counted as an additional possessed human government.
At least three qualifying governments must be meaningful current majors, not newly released tiny shells.
Their actual control and capacity must have remained durable for a proposed 180 days.

The order must operate across at least three represented continents and control a proposed 60 percent of global usable industrial capacity and 50 percent of verified effective military strength.
The exact military measure is an implementation and balance gate (G07): raw division count is insufficient, and unavailable engine statistics must not be replaced by a misleading proxy without review.

At least three of the qualifying governments must have reached their binding relationship through actual foreign commitments or a subsequent genuine ratification, not conquest alone.
The Infernal Compact must exist with meaningful membership.
The capital and governing institutions of each signatory must be operational rather than merely claimed on the map.

The shared terminal arbitration must allow this route: a stronger exclusive world-end actor or already resolved incompatible end state blocks the launch under its owner rules.
This package does not invent a numerical “stronger than every other apocalypse” ranking.

The irreducible requirements are broad international control, multiple possessed or binding governments, genuine industrial and military power, and the shared world-end boundary.
Releasing nominal puppets or transferring the same factory between signatories cannot satisfy them.

## A ratification crisis, not an invisible instant win

Once the conditions are met, **The Last Signature** becomes an open terminal project for the involved powers, with a proposed 180-day ratification period.
Governments are binding themselves to an international order whose nature has been exposed.

Ratification start registers a world-threat source (proposed `world_threat_source_mephistopheles`) through the shared [world threat mechanic](../../../systems/world_threat_mechanic.md), so non-signatory governments move toward cooperation against the order.
The source clears when ratification is cancelled or the order is defeated.
The masked secret phase and an unratified Compact never register it; secrecy would otherwise leak through the world's behaviour.

Opponents can disrupt the process by liberating a signatory, breaking its governing control, severing an essential promise, defeating sufficient industrial or military capacity, or exposing a valid authority flaw.
The patron can protect threatened members, replace interrupted aid, suppress an actual revolt, or negotiate a renewed binding commitment at a real price.

Loss of a required qualification pauses ratification for a recoverable interruption of up to 30 days; a longer failure cancels the attempt and requires a new valid start.
A restart does not repay preparatory focus rewards or duplicate client obligations.
A country released on the last day cannot count as a long-established signatory, a capitulated or liberated leader cannot sign through a stale pointer, and an Event 019 demon derivative cannot provide the missing government.

## The completed order

The final event confirms the binding global order, changes the shared terminal state through the approved owner path, and applies the established super-event and aftermath conventions.
Completion does not necessarily end player control, annex every member, kill civilians, or remove remaining wars; those would be separate design claims.

The human mask can preserve a recognizable world whose diplomacy and leadership reveal a common authority.
Open-rule members can display more overt identities.
Local governments still matter as administrative and military actors, even if their independent political direction has been extinguished by the terminal settlement.

If the shared world-end framework has a specific irreversible completion or post-ending rule, use it.
Pre-completion disruption and client liberation are required; post-completion reversibility remains a shared-owner integration decision.

## Defeat and aftermath

Defeating the open Dominion does not automatically erase every accepted arrangement in every country.
Surviving possessed ministers, client governments trying to break free, captured infernal research, damaged institutions, former hosts recovering their identity, permanent portrait scars, and disputes over equipment and territory created by bargains all remain as their actual owners record them.

When the principal state capitulates or is annexed, Mephistopheles makes **one final attempt to move**.
He can move only into something that already exists: a surviving bound client government, or a possessed official or commander still holding office in a surviving signatory.
If such a host exists, that country becomes the parent with its own accepted history and no reset reward account; the attempt is announced, and the receiving country's safeguards and the liberation route can stop it.
If no such host exists, or the attempt is defeated, Mephistopheles is gone for the campaign.
A post-possession liberation that reaches **No One Signs for Us** while he still has a body serves as that final attempt.

A short and weak manifestation needs only a defeat report.
A war that involved the Compact and several clients earns a defeat super-event and longer reconstruction content for the former hosts.

## Brilliant Scientist: measuring the impossible

Kruger can identify a gift without a plausible source, compare contradictory records, observe possession, and try to understand or exploit the method.
His presence creates stronger opportunities, not automatic knowledge of every hidden arrangement, and he can serve as the host's Outside Witness.

Three substantial interactions are proposed.
**Independent Verification** preserves reproducible work and can create loophole evidence while costing time and access.
**A Joint Result** delivers a bounded unusual project and creates a contest over institutional authority.
**The Researcher Under Protection** gives the visitor access to a powerful scientist while risking rivalry, compromise, or an attempt by the scientist to seize the method for himself.

The Scientist retains his project histories, authority, special forces, clone systems, and owner cleanup.
Event 090 cannot duplicate the unique actor, silently clear his authority, or unlock Germany-specific refinement as generic shared cloning.

## Doctor Wu: the person and the institution

Wu's participation begins with an accessible living patient, implicated hospital, altered formation, or independent clinical witness.
[His medical contract](../../089_doctor_wu_specs/medicine/089_doctor_wu_spec_part_3_universal_cures.md) distinguishes a supported biological condition from a non-biological cause, and [his shared-owner rules](../../089_doctor_wu_specs/integration/089_doctor_wu_spec_part_18_shared_owners_and_consequences.md) retain exact staff, recipients, stock, and care commitments.
He can examine unusual physical, behavioral, memory, or medical changes and treat a compatible condition.
Compatible research into infernal biology remains a possible use of Wu's existing qualified projects and actual source material.
Slowing transformation or liberating a host requires an explicit Event 090 result, rather than assuming that every possession is a registered disease.

**Keep the Patient Human** commits an available qualified service to the actual person or formation.
A biological match permits the supported treatment.
An unexplained non-biological finding supplies evidence for the existing inquiry and makes Wu an Outside Witness.
A clinical result alone does not lower the main acceptance count, reset a portrait tier, or dissolve state agreements.

**Protect the Independent Staff** preserves the actual witnesses and continuing care outside the protected network.
A government separating from a medical bargain can appoint an independent provider or arrange a supported foreign service for the existing patient group.
Its real staff, medicine, route, and handover interval must cover that commitment, using Wu's ordinary domestic or foreign service rules.
Research completed elsewhere cannot replace a missing delivery, and the same team or stock cannot serve both institutions beyond its available capacity.
If the handover fails, the government can continue negotiating, reduce the supported commitment, or accept the declared service interruption without reversing completed cures.

**Separate the Host** uses the completed assessment as evidence within Event 090's existing loophole or liberation route.
Event 090 still requires the specific binding term, Independent Records, and an independent authority able to act.
A medical finding can support the case about one person or hospital mandate, while other accepted arrangements remain effective.
The AI applies the existing informed-resister policy to that actual feasible choice, and countries without Wu retain the records-and-witness route.

All three are working roles within the existing inquiry and liberation content, not additional decision families or an interface.
Medical authority, production, strain rules, custody, and institutional competition remain governed by Wu's owners.

## Time Traveler: one possible future

The traveler can recognize the visitor, recall a failed bargain, preserve an alternative copy, identify a possible revolt site, or offer a method to conceal a leader or document.
The warning is evidence with a provenance and a possible limitation, not guaranteed omniscience.

A future revolt location is a useful lead whose relevance can change when the host dismantles a protected facility.
A known loophole still requires the actual agreement and conditions in this timeline.
A manipulated timeline can supply misleading information, and the player has a way to corroborate it rather than being forced to trust or reject the traveler blindly.
The original traveler remains a unique actor with real custody, survival, and copied-knowledge rules.

Every connection below runs through the owning system; Event 090 keeps no parallel ledger.

Possessed human leaders and populations remain human for every system above.
Genuine demons can receive explicit family-specific treatment, and disease resistance in a selected cohort is never generalized to the civilians of an infernal-owned state.

## Other supernatural or manipulative actors

Afrit, the Master, Secret Society Influence, Parliament of Fear, and comparable actors can recognize a rival method, conceal their own role, cooperate temporarily, or expose Mephistopheles.
An encounter arises from overlapping people, territory, institutions, or objectives rather than an obligatory crossover popup in every campaign.
The **rival tempter** variant (Part 2) is the host-side form of this overlap.

Mephistopheles owns tailored bargains, access granted through accepted rewards, institutional intrusion, possession, and the infernal political order.
Other actors retain their own methods, leaders, countries, progress, and terminal claims.
For a fully controlled host, the earlier exclusive owner wins the initial eligibility check unless an explicit conflict route is supported.
For partial overlapping influence, the interaction can create a rival claim, a warning, or a joint project with a defined settlement.
