# Deal with the Devil: manifestation and the three country origins

## A confrontation shaped by what the country allowed

The clean-defiance manifestation, the partial-acceptance revolt, and peaceful possession must remain distinct starts.
They share the later infernal tree because they express the same actor's rule, but they do not start with the same territory, army, institutions, or political problem.

Clean defiance gives Mephistopheles a supernatural foothold without a captured human administration.
Partial acceptance gives him the actual collaborators, protected facilities, and organizational advantages created by the deals.
Peaceful possession gives him the intact state's authority because five accepted arrangements prepared it.
The last has the strongest initial political and material position precisely because it avoided the opening civil war.

## Clean defiance: the visitor abandons persuasion

After the last clean refusal, the visitor's final departure is followed by a visible warning interval, proposed at 60 days.
Reports identify a gathering threat and a likely region.
The host can prepare, ask for help, or protect the threatened administration.
It is not required to know the name of every demon formation before it can mobilize against an armed intrusion.

Mephistopheles then appears openly and forms a hostile state on a viable foothold inside the host's territory.
The new country is the Mephistophelean Dominion in its open form, cosmetically named **the Infernal Enclave** while it holds only its foothold, with Mephistopheles as its actual leader.
Its flag and portraits are openly infernal, and the public reveal names him.

The clean host contributes no supposed collaborators merely because the event needs a civil-war split.
The manifestation's initial army is summoned and sustained by its foothold.
Local people remain people living in seized territory; they do not become demons through map ownership.
The human army, navy, and air force are not arbitrarily halved.

The initial foothold should be the smallest viable connected region that supports a meaningful military actor while preserving a playable host.
It needs a capital, supply, an initial force, and an economic route through the shared tree.
A port is required for a naval enclave's military plan.
A nominal one-province visual occupation without actual country territory is not the promised hostile country.

The force should be dangerous enough to require a campaign response but materially weaker than the force prepared after multiple accepted bargains at the same Chaos tier.
Its limitations are dependence on a small base, scarce summoning capacity, and little knowledge of the host's command system.
Those weaknesses give clean refusal a real strategic value.
To keep it from collapsing in the first weeks, the enclave holds a strong temporary defensive bonus on its own territory (proposed +25 percent defense and entrenchment for 120 days), so the host must mount a real offensive rather than mop up.

The host receives emergency tools for the warning interval and the opening war: protect the threatened administration, secure the likely foothold region, and request help from allies or neighbours.
These are ordinary decisions in the emergency category (decision map), not a purge menu for ministries that were never infiltrated.

## Partial acceptance: the country rises against itself

A partial revolt uses the institutions established by accepted main arrangements and surviving local favors.
A procurement office supports depots and selected commanders.
A trust supports its factories and administrative districts.
A hospital network supplies protected sites and logistics, not an automatic army of patients.
A research foundation supports specialists, laboratories, and only those unusual projects legitimately available in the campaign.
Diplomatic offices supply contacts or access actually secured abroad.

The revolt is the Mephistophelean Dominion cosmetically named **the Pactbound Directorate** while it relies on captured human institutions.
It can later retain the human mask or reveal direct infernal rule through the shared tree.
Its opening leadership must match the actual route: Mephistopheles can rule openly, or a known possessed authority can front the regime.
It is not a randomly selected historical leader unrelated to the arrangements.

Use a connected viable core of actual network sites.
Additional accepted institutions can increase available supply, stock, experienced officers, political cooperation, and the ability to coordinate the revolt.
They do not entitle the actor to geographically scattered states with no connection merely to satisfy a fixed percentage.

Protective action matters, and it is read from the four safeguards (Part 5).
Independent Records removes the matching archive or depot from the footprint, Loyal Command keeps its headquarters and army with the host, and a Protected Successor keeps the government seat.
A successfully evacuated archive no longer supplies the same rebel research advantage.
A depot recovered before the breach cannot also appear as fully stocked rebel territory.
A protected independent headquarters prevents the corresponding command seizure.
The opening remains more dangerous than clean defiance because other accepted arrangements survive, not because the script ignores what the player did.

## Proposed strength envelopes

The starting balance envelope compares the revolt to the host's **effective sustainable land strength**, not raw division count.
Understrength one-battalion formations must not inflate the opponent.
The metric used at implementation needs verified inputs and tests; these percentages are design targets rather than an asserted engine calculation.

At the same Chaos tier, clean manifestation targets approximately 15 percent of the host's effective land strength, with a bounded minimum sufficient for a viable actor.
A one-arrangement unresolved breach targets about 20 percent.
Two surviving main institutional footholds target 30 percent; three target 40 percent; four target 50 percent.
These are starting force envelopes before geography, actual surviving assets, and explicit evolution preparations are considered.

Chaos can strengthen the supernatural portion in 5-percentage-point steps up to a proposed additional 20 points.
That addition does not turn all accepted human divisions into free replacements.
Every branch must remain capped by a viability review that preserves a playable host and avoids enormous global-army snapshots producing an impossible minor theater.

An exact absolute floor and cap require live balance testing for very small and very large countries.
The release must test the same acceptance count across small players, medium powers, major powers, occupied hosts, and late-war armies.
A table of percentages alone is not evidence of balanced opposition.

In a partial revolt, transferred or reconstructed human assets and genuinely summoned assets are accounted separately.
Do not give the actor both a share of a stockpile and another “starting equipment” grant representing the same goods.
The host cannot keep a formation that the narrative claims defected while the rebel receives a duplicate of it at full strength.

## Territory, capitals, and foreign wars

Both sides need a valid seat of authority and an operational plan.
Preserve the host's capital where possible; otherwise establish a safe replacement before any transfer.
The revolt's connected region must include usable supply and cannot take every controlled productive state while claiming the host remains playable.

A foreign war changes the opening.
A host already facing invasion may need a smaller but strategically dangerous enclave, an explicit delay while a viable region is established, or a different prepared foothold.
The event does not donate enemy-occupied states that the host cannot control.
It must not restore the host's occupied industry solely so the revolt can steal it.

Neither side receives an unexplained peace with all outside belligerents.
The actual war and diplomacy systems determine inherited wars, intervention, access, and settlement.
Mephistopheles can exploit a foreign conflict, but a new army appearing behind the front does not automatically remove the host's original enemies.

Air and naval assets require real bases and usable existing assets or a specifically designed new force.
A landlocked manifestation does not inherit half the national fleet.
A naval enclave without ships cannot receive an AI plan that assumes effortless overseas conquest.
Its origin branch must provide a supported, costly route to transport or a viable local defense.

## The one-state problem must remain visible

A one-state country cannot be split into two state-owning countries while both retain territory under an ordinary state-transfer design.
The examined Event 021 documentation uses same-tag contests in unsafe one-state or all-island cases.
That is useful evidence of a constraint, not approval to replace Event 090's required separate Mephistophelean country.

Three alternatives require an explicit decision or new engine evidence.
First, add a transparent viability condition to original host selection, leaving one-state players untargeted until a supported manifestation foothold exists.
Second, obtain specific approval for a same-tag internal siege as the one-state exception, acknowledging that it changes the supplied route.
Third, demonstrate a supported separate-country method that preserves both the host and the requested internal origin without stealing unrelated foreign territory or inventing runtime map creation.

The package recommends the first alternative because it preserves the nature of the event for supported targets.
It narrows the user's host pool, so it remains an open question for the user (README, open question 1).
No source command for runtime creation of a new state is invented here.

If a previously viable host loses its other states before the revolt opens, do not execute an invalid transfer.
Preserve the established relationship and visible threat as pending, allow the host's real war or territorial recovery to resolve the issue, and explain the deferral through an appropriate event.
Do not reroll the entire parent onto a new country, reset accepted deals, or grant the promised final reward a second time.

An all-island country with several states is not automatically impossible.
A single viable rebel island can be connected within itself, provided both actors have real supply and a supported naval or defensive campaign.
It needs a separate live test rather than a blanket assumption that the continental setup works unchanged.

## Formation transfer and evidence limits

The examined Event 019 contract records an engine limitation: its natural derivatives use an approved recreate-prove-delete substitute because a division-scoped transfer was unavailable for that workflow.
That substitute preserves recorded identity and obligations but does not preserve all live veterancy, equipment variants, decorations, assignments, or orders.

Do not treat that Event 019-specific approval as permission for Event 090 to refresh damaged host divisions or rewrite their history.
The Event 090 implementation must either use a verified appropriate native split or obtain approval for a bounded transfer substitute with its losses disclosed.
A narrative claim that particular commanders and formations defected must have a defensible connection to what the engine actually moves.

The initial summons are separate newly created formations and should be described as such.
Their creation cannot be used to hide duplication of confiscated human units.
Before committing the revolt, establish the complete territory and force plan; a failed setup leaves no half-created country, lost human army, or free cargo in either side.

## Peaceful possession: the intact state

After five accepted main arrangements, retain the country's ordinary territory, army, industry, stockpiles, and familiar ruler.
Apply the full possession presentation and the Dominion's masked cosmetic identity, proposed as **[Country] under the Covenant** where that phrasing fits the actual country name; revealing Mephistopheles later switches it to the open Dominion name and flag (Part 7).
The final wording should be localized grammatically rather than assembled into broken names.

The shared Mephistophelean tree replaces the independent political route.
The player still commands the state.
Existing independent focuses and decisions that directly contradict the new authority become unavailable or are replaced under an explicit transition plan.
Completed historical rewards remain unless a particular benefit depends on an institution the takeover actually dissolves.
Do not repay already completed focuses because the tree changed.

Production, construction, technology, ordinary military operations, and unrelated surviving country content should remain intact where compatible.
A broad instruction to “replace all national content” would unnecessarily destroy the campaign.
The political settlement defines which old authorities can no longer act independently.

Prepared human institutions give this origin the strongest initial resources, lowest immediate reconstruction burden, and quickest practical route to foreign bargains.
The Hidden Army evolution can add preparations that were actually made, but the basic foreign, military, and client branches do not require it.

## Origin openings in the shared tree

The three opening branches keep the brief's working names: **Rejected at Every Door** (clean), **Collect What Was Promised** (partial) and **Rule Through the Leader** (peaceful).
Part 7 lists their focuses.

The clean origin begins with establishing a defensible seat, sustaining the manifestation, acquiring local human cooperation, and choosing whether to rule only by force or become capable of making credible offers again.
Its first strategic problem is survival with little ordinary administrative legitimacy.

The partial origin begins with joining compromised institutions into a functioning state, resolving rival officers and protected patrons, securing a connected economy, and deciding what to do with the government's remaining human collaborators.
Its first strategic problem is turning a network that could sabotage a country into one that can govern territory.

The peaceful origin begins with consolidating continuity, choosing the public mask, assigning the existing army's relationship to the new authority, and using intact diplomatic channels.
Its first strategic problem is selecting a form of rule without unnecessarily destroying the advantages obtained by consent.

These openings converge on shared political, military, economic, scientific, foreign, and liberation branches.
They are not separate duplicate trees with minor renamed bonuses, and they must not be shortened to a single origin focus that grants everything.

## Victory and defeat

A clean host that defeats the enclave removes its actual territorial actor, summons, and surviving parent authority according to their recorded ownership.
It can preserve captured evidence and choose whether to study what remains.
Victory does not automatically make every foreign bargain cease if an independently established network survives abroad.

A host defeating a partial revolt must recover the compromised institutions or settle their remaining obligations.
Defeating the last field division does not instantly cleanse the cabinet.
Conversely, ordinary civilians in liberated territory are not permanently classified as infernal enemies merely because the revolt controlled their state.

An infernal victory can reunify the host through the appropriate origin history and shared tree.
It does not masquerade as the five-deal peaceful route.
A defeated human player receives the normal supported continuation choices the campaign provides; this event does not promise an extra country switch the engine or multiplayer rules cannot deliver.

Liberation after full possession has its own political struggle and scars.
The restored country should retain a meaningful history of what it accepted, sacrificed, and recovered, rather than returning to an untouched pre-event save state.
