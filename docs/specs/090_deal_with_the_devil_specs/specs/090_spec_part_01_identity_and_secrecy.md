# Deal with the Devil: identity, secrecy, and the first meeting

## The event the player experiences

A government has a problem that its ordinary institutions cannot solve quickly enough. A visitor offers a practical solution. The delivery is real: the trains arrive, the threatened research programme resumes, or the hospitals begin saving people. The visitor asks for an appointment, an exemption, a protected office, or a limited authority that appears proportionate to the emergency. Months later, the country discovers that the institution entrusted with the solution has developed loyalties of its own.

Event 090 follows this relationship from its first plausible favour to an infernal government, an open invasion from within, or an expensive escape. Its central temptation is competent assistance at the moment competence is scarce. The stranger should not behave like an obviously fraudulent merchant whose offer an informed player would never consider. His account of who supplies the assistance can be misleading, while the stated assistance itself must be delivered.

Mephistopheles is the author-facing identity. During the opening, the player sees a visitor whose profession and presentation fit the particular offer. The same recognisable face, restrained manner, and peculiar turns of phrase connect his appearances. He need not invent a new unrelated personality for every sector. A financier can introduce a medical foundation; a diplomatic intermediary can produce an industrial guarantor. This helps the later discovery feel like an interpretation of evidence that already existed.

## Catalog authority and replacement

The design identity is Event 090, **Deal with the Devil**, Minor Fire-Once, status **To Be Reworked**, Chaos level 1, outside every event cluster. The supplied brief replaces the old Suicide Craft concept in its entirety. It does not inherit aircraft rewards, kamikaze mechanics, old eligibility, or old achievement conditions merely because the number is unchanged.

Event 161's unavailable duplicate must be retired as a duplicate or receive an explicitly different future idea. Neither number may independently open another Mephistopheles parent chain. The implementation handoff must reconcile the authoritative catalog source, generated exports, event selection, player details, localisation, achievements, and any references to the old 090 concept. This package does not itself edit those sources or promote a catalog status.

The supplied brief establishes the required concept. The numerical tuning, specific offer variants, branch choices, and settlements developed here are a design proposal, not evidence that the user approved every new detail. The source/decision register separates those layers. The preserved original brief remains authoritative where the interrupted source handoff leaves an unresolved difference.

## Who can receive the visitor

Build one deduplicated pool from functioning human countries that are currently major or player-controlled. A player-controlled major receives one entry, not two. Do not prefer a fixed historical tag or assume the player is a major. Evaluate the country's current government and condition rather than its original name.

Exclude actual nonhuman states, terminal actors whose authority already replaces ordinary government, an existing open Mephistophelean parent, and countries already under another event's complete and exclusive control. A normal dictatorship, a country suffering political pressure, or a country with an unusual adviser is not automatically such an exclusive actor. The owner of the other event determines whether ordinary national authority still exists.

War, defeat, famine, disease, sanctions, research collapse, low stability, and threatened leadership are useful opening conditions. They are not reasons to discard an otherwise eligible host. A healthy player country remains eligible: its offer addresses a credible ambition or vulnerability rather than inventing an epidemic to justify a physician.

Selection is uniform within the valid pool in this proposal. Crisis intensity selects the offer after the host is chosen; it does not secretly give a desperate AI major ten tickets and make a small player almost impossible to target. A later change to weighted host selection needs an explicit design decision and a probability review.

The separate-country revolt creates a genuine territorial feasibility problem for a one-state host. Do not conceal that problem inside the word “valid.” This package proposes an additional opening viability gate only as a decision awaiting approval: reserve the ability to leave a playable host while creating a viable hostile foothold. The current shared civil-war documentation describes a same-tag solution for unsafe one-state cases, but that is not the separate Mephistophelean country required here. Until that conflict is resolved, one-state support is a release blocker. Part 6 gives the alternatives and the safe pending-state behavior. No arbitrary foreign state is stolen as an unannounced workaround.

## Choosing the opening need

Assess immediate survival, institutional failure, material shortages, political danger, diplomatic isolation, and a plausible peacetime ambition. An urgent problem with a reward the event can actually deliver takes priority over a more dramatic problem whose owning system has no safe integration.

A country losing a war because its divisions lack rifles receives a material rescue rather than a research-speed bonus. A country whose hospitals are overwhelmed receives a treatment promise that names its scope. A country with an intact army but a collapsed research establishment can receive restored capacity. Territorial ambition can produce financing, intelligence, diplomatic access, or an opportunity to negotiate; it does not justify pretending that another human player's land can be granted without that player's involvement.

Choose one primary problem and one offer. A secondary benefit can support the primary purpose, but the first event is not a basket of seven unrelated national bonuses. The visitor must sound as though he understands this government.

Before presenting the offer, establish that the promised reward can be fulfilled. A stale problem can change during the decision interval. An outbreak might already have been contained or a war might have ended. In that case the visitor offers an updated, equally explicit arrangement before a choice is committed. An obsolete offer must not charge a concession for no useful delivery.

## The opening scene

The meeting takes place in an ordinary seat of authority: a minister's office, an embassy reception room, a private railway carriage, or a hospital administration room. The visitor is composed, well dressed, and unhurried. There are no horns, pentagrams, infernal flags, glowing contract seals, or supernatural menu effects. The unsettling element is how readily he supplies something that exhausted officials have been unable to obtain.

An example military opening can describe a shipping schedule, an inventory, and an officer already waiting with the papers. An economic opening can describe factories whose owners have unexpectedly accepted a common reconstruction plan. A medical opening can include the improvement of a small, observed group before national distribution is offered. A demonstration proves only the promised capability; it does not count as accepted national authority.

The acceptance text states the delivered benefit and the visible concession. The refusal is ordinary and respectful. The first clean refusal costs no political power, stability, equipment, research, manpower, or diplomatic relation. It does not secretly prepare punitive troops. A patient departure is more effective than a threat at the first meeting.

## Information boundaries

There are three distinct knowledge conditions. **Unexplained assistance** means the government has received or considered an unusual offer. **Credible evidence** means it can investigate an organized intrusion and take concrete precautions. **Open identification** means it can name Mephistopheles and understands the supernatural confrontation. These are knowledge conditions used to govern presentation, not a new public meter.

Knowledge is not automatically global. A foreign government can have evidence from its own diplomats before the original host does. Conversely, a distant country does not become omniscient merely because the host saw a private medical report. An openly manifested infernal army is strong public evidence for the countries observing it. The public world can learn the nature of the threat through events that actually communicate that information.

The campaign's ordinary player surfaces must respect the viewer's knowledge. This includes event titles, acceptance tooltips, national-spirit names, decision categories, the Event Log, Event Details, focus visibility, portraits, unit names, achievement notifications, scenario descriptions, and foreign offer summaries. Suppressing only a corruption number is insufficient when the Event Log labels the first visitor “Mephistopheles.”

The author-facing catalog retains the real concept title. Before identification, the player's log and details use the proposed cover title **An Unusual Benefactor** and summarize only the observed arrangement. They do not list future possession stages, demon battalions, the hidden liberation branch, or the Last Signature. After identification, the same event record can explain the revealed history. An explicit debug or spoiler view may show internal design information; it is not the default campaign view.

This is a presentation exception to any general rule that Event Details must expose every evolution or terminal route. The user specifically requires secrecy. Implementing the normal details template unchanged would violate the event rather than satisfy it.

## What the player is entitled to know

The visible deal is not allowed to hide its ordinary cost. A protected office is described as protected. An adviser with authority over procurement is described as having procurement authority. A hospital programme with access to medical records discloses that access. The player is surprised by the visitor's identity and the long-term use of the authority, not by an invented concession that was absent from the agreement.

The acceptance preview contains the immediate reward, its duration when finite, and the stated concession. It does not show “demonic influence,” a hidden acceptance count, a probability of possession, a future revolt percentage, or a red warning icon whose tooltip explains the final route. Later incidents supply evidence through play.

The world does not promise perfect ignorance. A player who has read the mod's source, remembers a previous campaign, or recognises the literary allusion can know more than the government. The design still gives that player a real strategic choice: accept a large benefit and manage the eventual cost, refuse and prepare for open defiance, or deliberately pursue the possessed-state game.

## Fire-once identity and continued play

Only one original parent courtship starts from Event 090's random selection. Refusals, investigations, a civil war, the infernal tree, and foreign bargains are continuations of that event. They do not reroll the random event or create additional main-event entries.

A defeated manifestation does not automatically reappear in a fresh major country with a reset reward account. Surviving foreign institutions can continue only through a route that was already established and is explicitly described later. An Event 019 family derivative is never another Mephistopheles parent.

The event remains playable after the fifth acceptance. Possession changes the regime's political purpose and available content, while the human player keeps control of military operations, production, construction, diplomacy where permitted by the new regime, and strategic choices. It is not a disguised game-over screen or a transfer of the player's country to an autonomous AI.
