## Original design summary

- Event 35 remains Minor Repeatable, Chaos level 1, and a Low member of the Negative Economy cluster.
- An independent firing selects one valid random major or player-controlled country.
- `Depression Severity` is the only persistent public Event 35 number and uses a `0` to `100` scale.
- Panic and Contraction, Depression, Stabilization, and Recovery remain lifecycle phases, not evolutions.
- Six recovery doctrines use dynamic material commitments and bounded action visibility.
- One to three Depression Centers appear under normal national conditions, with a rare fourth only for a justified Evolution III corridor case.
- Permanent factory loss occurs only after sustained deep failure or through a deliberate liquidation route.
- Evolution I is Financial Contagion at Rising Chaos.
- Evolution II is Social Collapse at Chaos Tier.
- Evolution III is The Second Great Depression at Totalen Chaos.
- Event 34 transfers one frozen same-country snapshot and does not create another global pacing transaction.
- An existing Event 35 crisis is deepened and raised to the inherited evolution floor without duplicate categories, Severity values, ledgers, modifiers, or history rows.
- Chaos changes follow guarded concrete outcomes. Evolution activation alone adds no Chaos.
- The Evolution III global episode has one complete super-event production handoff.
- Six Event 35 achievement contracts cover severe recovery, center mastery, contagion containment, Social Collapse survival, liquidation-led recovery, and worldwide reconstruction.
- Event 34 owns the separate inherited-crash mastery contract.
