## Doctrine action map

| Doctrine | Main opening action | Main state project | Stabilization action | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency Public Works | National Employment Guarantee | Regional Works Program | Convert Works into Lasting Capacity | Fiscal and logistics overextension |
| Rescue Strategic Industry | Designate Strategic Plant Network | Protect and Consolidate Essential Industry | End Rescue Dependence or retain bounded arsenal institution | Regional inequality and civilian neglect |
| Stabilize Finance and Trade | Temporary Bank Holiday and Audit | Restore Local Credit and Trade Connection | Establish Durable Credit Guarantee | Expensive guarantees and foreign dependency |
| Austerity and Retrenchment | Balance Emergency Budget | Restructure or close unsupported local commitments | Restore Ordinary Budget Rules | Demand collapse and Social Collapse |
| Direct State Planning | Establish Emergency Production Board | Trusteeship, nationalization, and planned reopening | Complete National Recovery Plan | Administrative burden and low flexibility |
| Let the Market Clear | Withdraw Emergency Support | Auction or Reorganize Failed Assets | Certify the Cleared Economy | Economic Paralysis and permanent local loss |

## Baseline public-works route

```text
Opening shock
  -> choose Emergency Public Works
  -> protect the most exposed center
  -> launch Regional Works Program
  -> Halt the Panic
  -> complete employment objective
  -> reopen each required center
  -> convert completed works into lasting capacity
  -> Prevent a Relapse
  -> final recovery proof
```

## Baseline strategic-industry route

```text
Opening shock during war or strategic emergency
  -> choose Rescue Strategic Industry
  -> designate one vital plant network
  -> guarantee essential orders
  -> keep resources and freight moving
  -> consolidate failing plants
  -> resolve neglected civilian and regional pressure
  -> end rescue dependence or retain bounded strategic institution
  -> final recovery proof
```

## Baseline finance-and-trade route

```text
Opening shock with credit and trade pressure
  -> choose Stabilize Finance and Trade
  -> temporary bank holiday and audit
  -> reopen viable institutions
  -> recapitalize or restructure
  -> negotiate valid clearing and import agreements
  -> protect exposed Depression Centers
  -> establish durable guarantee
  -> final recovery proof
```

## Baseline austerity route

```text
Opening shock with dominant fiscal pressure
  -> choose Austerity and Retrenchment
  -> balance emergency budget
  -> restructure public obligations
  -> accept short-term demand and stability pressure
  -> keep centers from shuttering
  -> end temporary commitments only after Severity falls
  -> restore ordinary budget rules
  -> final recovery proof
```

## Baseline planning route

```text
Opening shock with coordination failure
  -> choose Direct State Planning
  -> establish Emergency Production Board
  -> place selected center under trusteeship or nationalization
  -> ration strategic inputs
  -> complete center reopening targets
  -> resolve political and administrative resistance
  -> complete National Recovery Plan
  -> final recovery proof
```

## Baseline market-clear route

```text
Opening shock with viable institutions and failing capacity
  -> choose Let the Market Clear
  -> withdraw selected support
  -> allow or force asset repricing
  -> reorganize one center
  -> survive closure and unemployment pressure
  -> reopen viable capacity or accept bounded liquidation
  -> remove emergency controls
  -> certify the cleared economy
  -> final recovery proof
```

## Relapse route

```text
Stabilization
  -> policy support withdrawn too early or major shock arrives
  -> fragile-recovery status
  -> Severity rises above relapse condition
  -> Prevent a Relapse fails
  -> return to Depression phase
  -> reopen or protect failed center
  -> rebuild stabilization proof
```

## Economic Paralysis route

```text
Severity reaches 100
  -> ordinary actions are hidden
  -> maximum-Severity emergency starts
  -> select one emergency philosophy based on doctrine and causes
  -> protect one center, resolve one dominant shock, and lower Severity
     -> success returns to Systemic Breakdown or Deep Depression
     -> failure shutters or damages one eligible center and can trigger political crisis
  -> receipt and cooldown prevent repeated destruction
```

## Baseline National Breakdown route

```text
Sustained Systemic Breakdown or Economic Paralysis
  -> ordinary strike, radicalization, or government crisis
  -> visible National Breakdown warning
  -> Prevent National Breakdown objective
     -> success clears warning and starts cooldown
     -> failure calls strict political-outcome validator
        -> government fall, emergency rule, policy reversal, or prolonged unrest
        -> rare civil conflict only with valid actor, territory, forces, leadership, and no recent-war block
  -> Evolution II suspends this compact route and uses Social Collapse instead
```

## Financial Contagion route

```text
Evolution I activates
  -> build sparse exposure registry
  -> exposed countries receive qualitative Economic Contagion
  -> origin and partners choose aid, ring-fence, diversification, exploitation, or abandonment
  -> exposure falls and origin recovers
     or exposed country reaches Near Depression
        -> local vulnerability and delay check
        -> consequence call starts full Event 35
  -> anti-loop receipts prevent repeated conversion
```

## Social Collapse route

```text
Evolution II activates
  -> organized protest
  -> sector strike or occupation
  -> national unrest
  -> government crisis
     -> negotiated settlement
     -> emergency government
     -> targeted force and its consequences
     -> valid coup or regional settlement
     -> rare civil conflict after full extreme gate
  -> political aftermath remains linked to recovery
```

## The Second Great Depression route

```text
Evolution III activates
  -> create one global episode
  -> apply one bounded worldwide pressure package
  -> fire one global escalation super-event
  -> register lighter Global Contraction conditions
  -> vulnerable countries convert into full Event 35
  -> supplier booms accept or restrict world orders
  -> clearing blocs, debt conferences, and reconstruction projects
  -> fragmented recovery
  -> international reconstruction proof
  -> end global pressure while national crises continue where unresolved
```

## Visibility rules

- Show no more than five ordinary primary actions.
- Show six only during the one-time doctrine selection or a justified emergency.
- Show no more than three active missions.
- Display one selected Depression Center at a time.
- Hide invalid foreign targets.
- Replace normal actions with emergency forms at Economic Paralysis.
- Hide completed doctrine actions even after doctrine switching.
- Keep AI target access separate from the player selector.
- Close every action when its crisis, source, partner, state, or mission becomes invalid.

| Foreign rescue failure | Origin and provider exposure shock | Evolution I or III registry |

| Emergency government failure | Coup, settlement, or conflict gate | Evolution II |
| Maximum emergency failure | Bounded center and political consequence | Event 35 baseline plus enabled evolution |
