# 4. Lice, Gnats, and Flies

## III. Lice and Gnats

Infestations affect people, animals, sleeping quarters, workshops, and crowded accommodation.
The phase should feel persistent and exhausting.
The army has difficulty resting, factories lose productive time, and civilian services already strained by water and cleanup problems become less reliable.

The event keeps the Lice and Gnats identity supplied in the concept.
It does not claim that a particular modern disease or insect species has been scientifically identified.
The uncertainty in the traditional insect terminology is useful atmosphere, not an excuse to replace this stage with another plague.

### Civilian and military exposure

Civilian disruption follows the actual affected population and workplaces.
Military disruption follows the affected formations, quartering, or supported national projection.
A country with most of its population and formations outside the severe state footprint should not receive the same full penalty as a completely exposed one.

The proposed ordinary exposed-country anchors are a 10% output interruption, a 10% construction slowdown, and a 15% reduction in organization recovery.
These are starting design values for the affected share, before the consolidated national limits.
Do not apply separate identical division-recovery and organization-recovery penalties that represent the same engine statistic.

The phase does not automatically destroy trained manpower.
Its initial military character is impaired recovery and disrupted training.
Any actual soldier deaths belong to the military casualty owner.
A civilian population-loss wrapper cannot be used to simulate missing military organization.

### The choice of relief priorities

The country can protect civilian accommodation and workplaces through local service teams, temporary closures, and support equipment.
A labor-only route remains available where national stockpiles are small.
Its cost is reduced production for the participating population rather than imaginary medical stores.

A second route rotates or stands down an exposed military group long enough to restore basic conditions.
It sacrifices operational tempo.
It should be attractive to an army capable of pausing and difficult for one holding a threatened front.

A third route keeps a vital civilian service operating while the broader cleanup continues.
This protects a water point, hospital district, or shelter established in an earlier plague.
It preserves the cumulative value of earlier choices without giving a new country-wide immunity flag.

Harden Our Hearts prioritizes operational continuity.
It reduces the relevant production interruption and political panic, but it cannot eliminate infestation or grant a permanent division buff.
The cost is additional Wrath and a more exhausted civilian response when Flies arrives.

### The recovery objective

The urgent objective asks the country to keep the selected civilian or military recovery commitment in place for a 10-day window.
Full success restores useful service in the named target and reduces its residual vulnerability.
Partial success preserves completed work and gives a smaller current protection result.
Failure leaves the unresolved local burden.

The player cannot complete the military route by briefly moving a token formation through a state.
The commitment must involve the actual exposed formation or a meaningful share of the selected group for the stated duration.
The AI uses the same physical and time requirements.

### Mortality

Lice and Gnats can worsen mortality in an already weakened population, but their principal baseline role remains disruption and vulnerability.
The proposed secondary-mortality allowance is 10 basis points of exposed live population across a genuine owner-managed episode.
This is a balance target for the disease integration, not a guaranteed end-of-phase debit.

If no supported disease owner can accept a proven outbreak consequence, the integration remains a required implementation item.
Do not quietly replace it with an unexplained direct death percentage and then claim the disease requirement is complete.

## IV. Flies

Flies fill populated areas, contaminate food, and worsen sanitation.
The accumulated significance of the earlier plagues becomes undeniable.
A country that preserved water distribution and civilian services can mount an organized response.
A country that repeatedly diverted those services toward the war effort faces several connected failures.

The visual focus is food and civilian life: covered market stalls, disrupted kitchens, exhausted cleaning crews, and service areas overwhelmed by swarms.
The phase should not reuse the same generic insect image as Lice and Gnats.
The third plague concerns infestation and exhaustion.
The fourth concerns contamination, sanitation collapse, and the erosion of food security.

### Protect food and essential services

The main action protects functioning food storage and distribution in an affected state.
It can temporarily reduce civilian consumption flexibility or interrupt production while stores and kitchens are secured.
Food that is actually lost is reported through the Famine owner's input contract.
Do not delete national stockpiles of rifles to represent contaminated food.

A second action restores a damaged sanitation or civilian-service district.
Earlier water and cleanup work reduces the amount of new work needed.
This is an actual inherited advantage, not a separate permanent national bonus.

A third action routes external relief to a functioning reception point.
The action must establish a real delivery destination.
Where the recipient lacks safe reception, assistance can first help restore that capacity instead of magically distributing goods to every state.

Harden Our Hearts keeps the emergency war-production commitment and suppresses the immediate political shock.
It cannot make unsafe food safe.
When the country's civilian food or disease owners report actual deterioration, the consequences remain.

### The two linked objectives

The normal phase presents one primary objective and, where the problem is large enough, a second concurrent objective.

The food-protection objective requires the threatened reserve or distribution commitment to remain usable through the contamination window.
Full success preserves the event-threatened share.
Partial success loses only the unprotected share.
Failure submits the actual loss once.

The service objective requires a selected high-risk civilian district to regain a working combination of water access and sanitation support.
Full success reduces subsequent disease vulnerability.
Partial success lowers the remaining pressure.
Failure retains the proven service deficit rather than spawning an unrelated disease everywhere.

The same material commitment cannot fully satisfy both objectives when they compete for it.
A country with limited capacity chooses which problem to address first.
The failure of the other objective causes real harm without automatically adding moral blame for an impossible choice.

### Disease and food ownership

Flies can generate a substantial disease consequence in already weakened states.
The starting balance allowance is 25 basis points of exposed live population over an actual outbreak episode.
The disease owner determines spread, duration, treatment, and deaths.
Event 085 supplies the manifestation, exposure, and relevant completed protection facts.

Food loss, famine thresholds, reserve consumption, relief access, and famine mortality remain Famine-owned.
A state may be suffering both illness and food insecurity, but each death has one proximate owner and one Deaths entry.
The event's report can summarize both categories without performing another population debit.

If the disease owner later submits hunger-driven displacement, Migration owns the movement.
The arrival of refugees does not itself prove a new outbreak in the receiving country.
Reception conditions and actual disease facts must support that connection.

## When the pattern becomes public

The phase's principal report makes the ordered resemblance to Exodus part of ordinary government knowledge.
Scientists may still dispute the cause.
Religious institutions may disagree over interpretation.
The government nevertheless has enough evidence to justify preparations for agricultural and medical disasters.

This recognition should unlock practical planning rather than a new theology meter.
The country can start preserving civilian distribution capacity and identifying safe shelter locations for later phases.
It cannot complete Observe the Passover before Darkness.

Countries that chose different explanations receive distinct narrative framing around the same material choices.
The event must not imply that a modern population deserves collective punishment because of its religion, ethnicity, or nationality.
The government's actual conduct and the fictional anomaly remain the mechanical subjects.

## After the swarms

At baseline, the physical swarms recede.
The related output interruption tapers with them.
Established outbreaks, depleted reserves, and damaged services continue under their owners.

Under Evolution I, flies can remain while livestock begins dying.
Cleanup crews must choose between ongoing urban contamination and the first rural losses.
Evolution II spreads the physical footprint across nearly all eligible populated states.
The event becomes harder because the country has fewer unaffected service centers, not because ten separate national spirits multiply the same penalty.
