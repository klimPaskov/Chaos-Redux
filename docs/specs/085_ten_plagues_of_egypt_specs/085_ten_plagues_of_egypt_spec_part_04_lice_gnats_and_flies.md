# 4. Lice, Gnats, and Flies

## III. Lice and Gnats

Infestations reach people, animals, barracks, workshops and crowded housing.
The phase feels persistent and exhausting: armies cannot rest, factories lose time and services strained by water and cleanup become less reliable.
The event keeps the Lice and Gnats name from the brief and does not identify a real species or disease.

### Effects

Anchors for a fully exposed country, scaled by the exposed share and the crisis spirit's ceilings:

| Effect | Anchor |
| --- | ---: |
| Factory output | −10% |
| Construction speed | −10% |
| Organization recovery | −15% |

Use one recovery statistic, not separate division-recovery and organization-recovery penalties for the same engine value.
The phase destroys no manpower; any soldier deaths belong to the military casualty owner.
Disease mortality allowance: 10 basis points of exposed population through the disease owner (Part 13).

### Responses

- **Heed: Infestation Control** (Part 14) restores a selected state's civilian services for 10 days, by support equipment or by local organization.
It also preserves a water point or shelter built in an earlier phase, so earlier choices keep paying off.
- **Military Stand-Down** (Part 14) rotates an exposed army out of the line long enough to restore conditions.
It costs operational tempo, suits an army that can pause and hurts one holding a threatened front.
The commitment must involve the actual exposed divisions for the full window; a token unit passing through a state does not count.
- **Harden Our Hearts** halves the production interruption and political panic but cannot remove the infestation, and the civilian response is weaker when Flies arrives.

## IV. Flies

Flies fill populated areas, contaminate food and break sanitation.
The resemblance to Exodus becomes undeniable.
A country that kept water and services running can organize; one that diverted them to the war faces several connected failures.

The visuals focus on food and civilian life: covered market stalls, disrupted kitchens, overwhelmed cleaning crews.
Lice and Gnats are about infestation and exhaustion; Flies are about contamination, sanitation collapse and food security.
They do not share one generic insect image.

### Effects

- A food shock to Famine in affected states, larger where earlier water and cleanup work failed.
- Disease allowance: 25 basis points of exposed population through the disease owner, higher in states already weakened.
- Supply loss, output disruption and Stability loss through the crisis spirit.

Each death has one cause and one Deaths entry: hunger deaths belong to Famine, disease deaths to the outbreak owner.

### Responses

- **Heed: Food and Sanitation** (Part 14) protects one selected state's food storage and sanitation for 10 days.
Earlier completed water or frog work in the same state halves its cost.
Full success cancels the state's Flies food shock and most of its disease exposure.
- Famine's own **Release Food Stores** remains the reserve action; Event 085 does not duplicate it.
- Foreign relief can be directed to a state whose reception is working (Part 10).
- **Harden Our Hearts** keeps war production and suppresses the political shock; it cannot make food safe.

## When the pattern becomes public

The Flies report makes the ordered resemblance to Exodus common knowledge.
Scientists dispute the cause and religious institutions dispute the meaning, but every government has enough evidence to prepare for the agricultural and medical disasters ahead.
Countries that chose different explanations get different framing around the same choices.
No text implies that a modern population deserves punishment for its religion, ethnicity or nationality.

## After the swarms

At baseline the swarms recede and the interruption tapers.
Outbreaks, depleted reserves and damaged services continue under their owners.
Under Evolution I flies remain while livestock begins dying, and crews must choose between urban contamination and the first rural losses.
