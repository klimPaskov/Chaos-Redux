# 1. The Global Crisis

## Ten Plagues of Egypt

Event 085 is a Major event with Chaos level 3.
It belongs only to the Natural Disasters cluster as a Severe member.
Its identity is a worldwide sequence of the ten biblical plagues, with a particularly strong Egyptian and Nile presentation.
Egypt is neither the only target nor the source of an automatic punishment applied to other countries.

The sequence is Water into Blood, Frogs, Lice and Gnats, Flies, Pestilence of Livestock, Boils, Hail and Fire, Locusts, Darkness, and Death of the Firstborn.
These are ten ordinary phases of one event.
They are not ten Evolutions, ten independent random events, or a pool from which the game chooses a few disasters.

The player must keep a country functioning while deciding how much productive capacity, equipment, transport, and political authority to spend protecting its inhabitants.
A government can preserve its war effort by hardening its heart, but repeated refusals make subsequent plagues more dangerous.
The contrast should remain visible in actual factories, working relief routes, surviving settlements, and population losses.

The catastrophe has no compulsory theological explanation.
A government may describe it through religion, supernatural emergency procedure, scientific uncertainty, or official denial.
Changing that description never changes protection by itself.
Material action and Divine Wrath determine the outcome.

## One worldwide episode

A natural firing begins one globally coordinated episode.
Every valid country receives the first warning and joins the same sequence.
A Major event's natural eligibility follows the shared Chaos-level rules, placing the ordinary level-3 opening at 400 Chaos.
The manual scenario does not wait for that threshold.

The Major event's ordinary natural or cluster entry is fire-once for the campaign.
An accepted opening consumes that member eligibility; a renewed internal cycle does not create another Major roll.
A later explicitly permitted manual scenario can begin a new episode without rearming the consumed natural entry.
Only one Event 085 episode can be active.
The root is `chaosx.nr85.1`.
Its internal warnings, plague transitions, relief outcomes, Evolutions, and repeat cycles do not create additional random-event entries or repeatedly reset the shared event timer.
A cluster firing selects Event 085 once and starts the same episode.
The member retains its own Chaos threshold, enabled state, and fire-once restriction.
The cluster commits the appropriate Major pacing transition once for the selected sequence; Event 085 does not charge a second transition through its country recipients.
Use the existing cluster history ownership, linking the plague episode rather than inserting duplicate independently paced rows.
Actual Evolution activations retain the shared framework's own Evolution history entries.

Countries participate according to their actual responsibilities.
A country with human inhabitants receives humanitarian effects, decisions, and demographic losses.
A state controlled by an occupier is still an inhabited state, and its population is not omitted because ownership and control differ.
An exile government may coordinate assistance where it has a valid route, but it is not assigned a second copy of the population living under another authority.

A genuinely nonhuman host does not acquire imaginary human firstborn or a human refugee population.
Physical hazards can still damage its exposed infrastructure when the relevant owner supports that target.
Use the shared population and country classifications rather than treating every unusual tag as either human or immune.

A newly released country joins the current plague, not Blood.
It inherits the physical situation, completed local protection, and allocated responsibility of its territory.
It receives a timely explanation of the current deadline without replaying every old popup.

## The ordinary calendar

The proposed first-cycle anchor is 168 days from Blood to the tenth plague.
The first eight intervals give the country several meaningful decisions and allow food insecurity to mature under the actual Famine system.
Darkness remains a short emergency.

| Phase | Opening day | Interval until the next opening | Main character of the interval |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| I. Water into Blood | 0 | 20 days | Water distribution and early uncertainty |
| II. Frogs | 20 | 15 days | Disruption followed by a sanitation emergency |
| III. Lice and Gnats | 35 | 20 days | Exhausted people and institutions |
| IV. Flies | 55 | 20 days | Food contamination and worsening civilian illness |
| V. Pestilence of Livestock | 75 | 25 days | Agricultural loss and food-security deterioration |
| VI. Boils | 100 | 25 days | Medical capacity and labor disruption |
| VII. Hail and Fire | 125 | 15 days | Forecast, shelter, impact, and rescue |
| VIII. Locusts | 140 | 25 days | Moving fronts and the destruction of remaining crops |
| IX. Darkness | 165 | 3 days | Final preparations under a global deadline |
| X. Death of the Firstborn | 168 | One simultaneous resolution | Demographic shock and succession consequences |

These are central tuning anchors, not historical claims about the duration of Exodus.
The opening calendar should be legible and predictable once the pattern is understood.
Randomness belongs chiefly in valid local manifestations, route pressure, and country reactions, rather than in unexpectedly deleting the preparation window.

At baseline, the acute manifestation of an early plague normally begins easing during the latter half of its interval.
Frogs can recede while cleanup continues.
The swarm can disperse while an established illness remains.
The livestock do not come back to life when Boils begins.
This distinction allows baseline tapering without pretending that food reserves, dead people, and ruined buildings reset every twenty days.

An Evolution changes future scheduling and unresolved exposure.
It never rewrites a delivered warning into an earlier deadline, repeats an already settled population transaction, or retroactively removes a completed preparation.
The minimum remaining time promised by an active objective must survive a mid-phase escalation.

## Worldwide participation and local footprints

Every country experiences the crisis, while the baseline's strongest local manifestations strike representative parts of its territory.
For local manifestation families, the starting coverage anchor is one quarter of each responsible country's eligible populated states, rounded up with a minimum of one when a valid state exists.
Evolution I increases that planning anchor to one half.
Evolution II aims to reach every eligible populated state over the phase.

The coverage rule measures the whole phase's reach.
It does not mean that one quarter of states are struck again every day.
Revisits require an explicit repeat strike, surviving exposure, and an Evolution or recurrence rule.

All physically valid states belong in the appropriate candidate pool.
After genuine geography and current-state validity have been established, equivalent candidates receive equal draw weight.
Population, capital status, industrial wealth, or player control must not quietly bias a supposedly random opening.
Population and exposure still influence consequences after a state is selected.
Player and AI relief priorities may legitimately favor dense or strategically important states.

Water dependence cannot be inferred only from a visible river province.
A landlocked state can depend on wells, reservoirs, and distributed water supplies.
Likewise, a one-state urban country remains part of the food crisis even where no field-scale locust front is possible.
Its markets, stored food, and any proved supply dependence provide the appropriate manifestation.
Do not invent an agricultural state or a cross-border trade flow merely to satisfy a target count.

Darkness and the tenth plague are global rather than representative-state draws.
Protection can greatly reduce their consequences, but countries do not randomly miss those phases.

## What a country sees

The event-owned decision category shows Divine Wrath as its only mechanic value.
The current plague, the next known deadline, and completed preparations are contextual information.
They are not additional national meters.

The player should usually choose among three to five primary actions and see no more than three concurrent urgent objectives.
Older relief belongs to its existing system or a relevant aftermath action.
The category must not accumulate ten permanent rows of plague controls.

Use one consolidated crisis national spirit for the national projection of current exposure.
A temporary policy spirit and a mourning or reconstruction spirit may accompany it when they convey different information.
Event 085 contributes no more than three simultaneous national spirits.
It never removes unrelated national spirits to satisfy that limit.

Local state damage remains local.
A damaged rail junction does not produce an identical national penalty in a vast continental country and a small single-state country.
The national projection reflects the share of relevant population or productive capacity actually exposed, with bounded totals when manifestations overlap.

## Recognizing the pattern

The first reports can be disputed.
A government may investigate the water, dismiss the frogs, or accuse its opponents of manufacturing panic.
By Flies and Livestock, successive reports explicitly connect the order to Exodus.
The exact insect identification remains flexible enough to preserve the user's Lice and Gnats phase.

Before Hail and Fire, the country receives a meaningful warning about sheltering exposed people and securing transport.
Before Darkness, institutions can prepare distribution networks and identify households that will need help.
Observe the Passover itself becomes available during Darkness.

There is no mandatory resurrected Moses, historical Pharaoh, new religious country, or world-spanning religious faction.
Clergy, community organizers, scientists, municipal officials, soldiers, and households provide the human face of the crisis through the country's existing setting.

## Surviving is an outcome

A well-prepared country should finish with a smaller demographic wound, functioning relief capacity, and stronger prospects for reconstruction.
An unprepared country should carry real population loss, ruined regions, weakened institutions, and unresolved food or displacement problems into the rest of the campaign.

The sequence can end without ending the campaign.
Evolution III can reopen it, and the separately gated world-end branch can turn recurrence into an enduring global emergency.
Neither path adds an eleventh plague.
