# 1. The Global Crisis

## Ten Plagues of Egypt

Event 085 is a Major event with Chaos level 3, a Severe member of the Natural Disasters cluster only.
It is a worldwide sequence of the ten biblical plagues with a strong Egyptian and Nile presentation.
Egypt is not the only target and carries no special guilt or punishment.

The sequence is Water into Blood, Frogs, Lice and Gnats, Flies, Pestilence of Livestock, Boils, Hail and Fire, Locusts, Darkness, and Death of the Firstborn.
These are ten ordered phases of one event, not ten Evolutions and not a random pool.

The player keeps a country functioning while deciding how much industry, equipment, transport and political authority to spend protecting its people.
Hardening the country's heart preserves the war effort and makes later plagues more dangerous.
The difference must be visible in factories, relief routes, surviving settlements and population losses.

No explanation is compulsory.
A government may call the plagues divine punishment, a supernatural anomaly, an unexplained catastrophe or mass hysteria.
The explanation never changes protection; material action and Divine Wrath decide the outcome.

## One worldwide episode

A natural firing begins one global episode, and every valid country receives the first warning.
Natural eligibility follows the shared Event Chaos Level rules for level 3.
Only one episode can be active at a time.

The natural or cluster entry is fire-once for the campaign.
A cluster selection starts the same episode and commits Major pacing once, following the Acid Rain precedent in the Natural Disasters cluster.
Warnings, phase transitions, outcomes, Evolutions and renewed cycles add no random-event entries, no extra pacing and no duplicate history rows.
Actual Evolution activations use the shared Evolution history.
A manual launch (Part 12) neither rearms nor duplicates the natural entry.

### Who takes part

A country with human inhabitants receives humanitarian effects, decisions and population loss for the states it controls.
An occupied state is still inhabited; its controller is responsible for it.
A government in exile can send relief where it has a valid route but receives no second copy of its homeland's population.
A nonhuman country (shared population classification) receives physical damage only, never firstborn deaths or refugees.

A country released mid-episode joins the current phase, inherits its territory's damage, Passover status and Wrath share (Part 9), and receives one short catch-up report.

## The ordinary calendar

The first cycle runs 168 days from Blood to the tenth plague.
Darkness is a short final emergency.

| Phase | Opening day | Interval until the next opening | Main character |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| I. Water into Blood | 0 | 20 days | Water distribution and early uncertainty |
| II. Frogs | 20 | 15 days | Disruption, then a sanitation emergency |
| III. Lice and Gnats | 35 | 20 days | Exhausted people and institutions |
| IV. Flies | 55 | 20 days | Food contamination and civilian illness |
| V. Pestilence of Livestock | 75 | 25 days | Agricultural loss and falling food security |
| VI. Boils | 100 | 25 days | Medical capacity and labor disruption |
| VII. Hail and Fire | 125 | 15 days | Forecast, shelter, impact and rescue |
| VIII. Locusts | 140 | 25 days | Moving fronts destroying the remaining crops |
| IX. Darkness | 165 | 3 days | Final preparations under a global deadline |
| X. Death of the Firstborn | 168 | Single resolution | Demographic shock and succession |

These are tuning anchors kept in script constants.
The calendar is predictable once the pattern is known; randomness belongs to local footprints and reactions, not to deleting preparation time.

At baseline an early plague's acute effect eases during the second half of its interval.
Its lasting consequences remain: dead livestock, depleted reserves, outbreaks and ruined buildings stay with their owners.

An Evolution changes future scheduling and unresolved exposure only.
It never shortens a delivered warning or an active objective's deadline, repeats a settled population loss, or removes a completed preparation.

## Local footprints

Every country experiences every phase, while the strongest local effects strike part of its territory.
At baseline the footprint is one quarter of a country's eligible populated states per phase, rounded up, minimum one.
Evolution I raises it to one half; Evolution II reaches every eligible populated state over the phase.
The footprint measures the phase's total reach, not daily strikes.

Candidate states must be physically valid for the plague.
Equivalent valid states have equal draw weight; population, capital status, wealth and player control do not bias selection.
Consequences after selection scale with the people and buildings actually exposed.

Water dependence and food dependence do not require a river or farmland province.
A landlocked or one-state country still takes part through wells, reservoirs, stored food and markets.
Do not invent agricultural states or trade flows to meet a target count.

Darkness and the tenth plague are global.
Protection reduces their harm, but no country skips them.

## What a country sees

The event category shows Divine Wrath as its only event-specific value, plus the current plague, the next deadline and the country's Passover status once Darkness begins.
Event 085 holds at most three national spirits at once: one crisis spirit, one Harden Our Hearts policy spirit and one mourning or reconstruction spirit.
The category budget and action list are in [Part 14](085_ten_plagues_of_egypt_spec_part_14_decisions_and_missions.md).

Local damage stays local.
The crisis spirit projects only the share of population or industry actually exposed, so a continental country and a one-state country are not penalized identically for one damaged junction.

## Recognizing the pattern

The first reports can be disputed; governments may investigate the water, dismiss the frogs or blame their enemies.
By Flies, reports explicitly connect the order to Exodus.
Before Hail and Fire, every country receives a shelter warning.
Before Darkness, institutions can prepare distribution networks.
Observe the Passover opens at Darkness onset.

There is no resurrected Moses, historical Pharaoh, new religious country or world religious faction.
Clergy, scientists, municipal officials, soldiers and households carry the human story through each country's existing setting.

## Surviving is an outcome

A well-prepared country finishes with a smaller demographic wound, working relief capacity and better prospects for reconstruction.
An unprepared country carries real population loss, ruined regions and unresolved hunger or displacement into the rest of the campaign.
The sequence ends without ending the campaign unless Evolution III or the world-end branch renews it (Part 9); neither adds an eleventh plague.
