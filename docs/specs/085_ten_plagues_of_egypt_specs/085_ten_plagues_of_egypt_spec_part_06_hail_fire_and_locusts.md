# 6. Hail and Fire, Then Locusts

## VII. Hail and Fire

A supernatural storm strikes exposed states with enormous hailstones, lightning, and fire.
This is the sequence's principal physical-destruction phase.
It damages places the player recognizes and can prepare to protect.

The storm footprint is formed from valid current states.
The warning names the expected state and the useful preparation deadline.
The country is not asked to defend an unidentified target while a hidden random roll decides where the storm occurs.

The biblical inspiration gives sheltering a direct role.
People brought out of exposed fields and vulnerable settlements are better protected.
The game extends that principle to civilian shelters, rail workers, depots, airfields, and damaged urban districts without treating modern technical precautions as a definitive explanation of the anomaly.

### Before impact

The baseline warning arrives five days before the first impact.
A state selected later through an Evolution or a moving continuation receives its own usable warning.
A phase transition cannot retroactively shorten an accepted warning.

The central response shelters an actual exposed population.
The player chooses between broad local sheltering, a transport-supported evacuation to an existing safe district, and protection of a critical service or transport site.
Only commitments compatible with the state's geography and available capacity appear.

Evacuation belongs to Migration.
It must finish before impact for its survivors to be outside the exposed population.
Moving a unit counter or changing a state flag does not move civilians.

Protecting equipment and airfields reduces a different loss.
It can be a rational military choice, but it does not count as full civilian sheltering.
The decision text must distinguish saved people, saved material, and continued production.

### Impact and damage

The proposed ordinary direct-mortality anchor is 250 basis points of the actually exposed live civilian population before Wrath, intensity, and shelter protection.
This is one storm-impact budget for that footprint.
If the storm contains hail and a causal fire continuation, their combined population exposure must not silently become two full copies of that budget.

Building damage prioritizes existing infrastructure, rail connections, exposed industry, air bases, and vulnerable civilian services.
The initial physical scale is substantial repairable damage to roughly a quarter of vulnerable local capacity, with more severe damage under high Wrath and later Evolutions.
Exact building damage units and rail segment operations must follow the inspected helper and map contracts.

The event does not delete buildings that do not exist, remove levels below zero, or use a generic damage helper's population branch after casualties have already been settled.
Equipment losses concern stockpiles or formations actually exposed to the impact.
There is no second global stockpile deletion presented as local storm damage.

Where military damage is supported, it applies to real formations in the footprint and uses the military casualty owner.
The civilian death calculation cannot count those same people again.

### Natural Disasters ownership

Use the public `call_natural_disaster` gateway for the physical disaster episode, its accepted target, queued impact, actual casualties, building damage, reports, and aftermath.
Event 085 supplies the ordered plague, Wrath-derived severity, preparation context, and its own episode identity.
It does not call Event 013's private effects.

Use the no-history external-call policy so the storm does not masquerade as a new random Event 013 firing.
The shared natural-disaster cause remains the proximate Deaths reason.
A correlation receipt identifies the consequence as originating from Event 085 for its report and achievement checks.

Hail and causal fire must be represented through a verified supported family or an owner-reviewed composite extension.
The public gateway's existence does not prove that it already supports every combined-plague requirement.
The extension must preserve one exposed-population budget, independent material damage where justified, owner-local cleanup, and the original warning.
A pair of uncoordinated full-strength disaster calls is not an acceptable substitute.

Existing disaster warnings and aftermath actions remain owned by Natural Disasters.
Event 085 must not create a duplicate rescue shop with cheaper costs.
A completed owner-side shelter or rescue result can be recognized once for Divine Wrath through an exact receipt.

### Rescue and the next warning

After impact, survivors need rescue, route repair, and functioning food distribution.
The shared disaster owner handles those operations.
Event 085 highlights the next strategic danger: locusts will consume the crops that survived.

The player can prioritize one remaining agricultural area and a route to a functioning reserve.
This decision competes with restoring industrial or military capacity.
A country cannot protect every vulnerable field and rebuild every air base with the same crews.

## VIII. Locusts

Locust fronts move through states and consume surviving crops and exposed food supplies.
The phase must have a geographical life.
A single national modifier lasting twenty-five days is insufficient.

The event establishes front seeds among physically eligible current states.
Each seed opens a bounded sequence of adjacent-state exposures.
Separate island and disconnected land regions can receive their own seed when the global coverage profile calls for it.
A front does not teleport from an inland state to another continent and pretend that it followed a continuous route.

The forecast identifies the next threatened state or a small adjacent set.
The exact front is then resolved from valid candidates with equal weights where no physical difference justifies another treatment.
Later Evolutions add fronts and broaden coverage rather than replacing movement with repeated whole-world rolls.

### What the locusts consume

Crop damage acts on what remains.
A state that lost much of its agriculture to Hail and Fire cannot lose that same destroyed output again.
The locust contribution is the additional loss of surviving production or exposed stores.

The largest indirect mortality phase comes from the combination of damaged transport, livestock loss, surviving crops destroyed, and disrupted relief.
Famine owns the food-security consequences and actual famine deaths.
Event 085 reports the worsening position without charging a separate locust-famine death percentage.

An urban or nonagricultural state can still suffer from a proved loss of stored food or incoming supply.
Do not invent field-scale crop destruction where the current state has no appropriate exposure.
Such a country remains part of the plague through its real food problem and the global sequence.

### Rescue the remaining harvest

The first protective action moves a committed share of threatened food into an existing protected reserve or distribution center.
It must complete before the front reaches the source.
Transport costs and capacity are real.
Food moved is debited from the source's exposed amount and credited once through the food owner.

The second action protects a surviving agricultural district and its access route.
It sacrifices local output and labor during the operation.
This reduces the locust loss in that district and preserves a base for later recovery.
It does not build an imaginary impenetrable wall around the country.

The third response establishes an actual international relief corridor where a safer source and receiving capacity exist.
The donor must also be feeding its own surviving population.
Relief cannot be generated without a source simply because every country is in danger.

Harden Our Hearts prioritizes military requisition and continuity.
It may keep a strategic army supplied for longer.
Its benefit is bounded by real stores and routes.
The resulting civilian extraction is passed to Famine, and deliberate exploitation can increase Wrath.

### The front objective

The urgent objective has a deadline tied to a specific forecast arrival.
Full success completes the selected harvest movement or protection before that arrival.
Partial success protects only what was actually moved or secured.
Failure leaves the surviving unprotected share available for the front's impact.

A route destroyed by the previous storm may make a former plan impossible.
The player receives a concrete blocked reason and can choose a viable local action if time remains.
The game does not silently replace the target or withdraw a previously delivered warning.

Once a front leaves a state, the direct crop loss is settled.
It does not keep consuming the same crop every day.
A recurrence or explicitly intensified revisit requires new surviving exposure and its own bounded contribution.

## The landscape before Darkness

By the end of Locusts, the map should show the relationship between protected refuges, damaged transport, hungry regions, and functioning relief routes.
These are existing state and humanitarian facts, not four new Event 085 meters.

The final warning should arrive against this visibly altered world.
Countries that kept productive services alive can distribute emergency household supplies efficiently.
Countries that preserved only their armies face the ninth plague with fewer civilian institutions capable of carrying out the last preparation.

The famine does not pause for Darkness.
The storm damage does not repair itself at the next phase.
The player enters the final emergency carrying the consequences of the choices made during the preceding months.
