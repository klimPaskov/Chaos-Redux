# 17. Super-Event Milestones

## Four milestones

| Slot | Trigger | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tp85_opening_sign` | First global Blood of the episode | A world-scale anomaly has begun |
| `tp85_firstborn_night` | First tenth-plague settlement of the episode | The largest single night of loss |
| `tp85_endless_return` | First activation of The Plagues Without End | The completed sequence has become an endless threat |
| `tp85_cycle_broken` | Verified escape from the endless branch | Recurrence ends; the world is not restored |

Each fires once per episode.
Slot names are production labels, not titles; global super-event IDs are allocated only after checking the live registry, and no existing slot is overwritten.
Country presentation settings and the shared super-event queue are respected; the mechanics never wait for playback, and suppressed presentation suppresses nothing else.
Later renewed cycles use ordinary reports, and a finite Evolution III renewal is never presented as the world-end.

## Opening sign

**Picture:** a broad civilian view of a river, city intake, bridge or reservoir running red with period life still going on, ominous and recognizable, not yet showing the world-end.
A Nile context may inform it without making the catastrophe look Egyptian only.

**Text:** research the Exodus water passages, later cultural references to the first plague, and a brief remark about authority facing an unmistakable sign.

**Audio:** a real recording with a restrained, ominous opening and a recognizable development.

## The firstborn night

**Picture:** sudden absence across ages and social roles in a household, civic building or workplace, with survivors beginning to understand.
No graphic bodies, no reduction to child deaths, no named real person shown as a victim; prepared households may appear as contrast.

**Text:** research the final warning and firstborn-night passages and a remark about shared loss across ranks.
The text states no death total; reports supply applied deaths.

**Audio:** a real recording that carries grief and collective shock, not a triumphant battle cue; any vocal text is translated and checked.

## The endless return

**Picture:** a repaired water system turning red while last cycle's damage remains, newly composed rather than a recolored opening image.

**Text:** research hardening, repeated warnings and refused chances to change; the wording makes clear the full ten-stage sequence is returning, with no eleventh plague and no canonical theology.

**Audio:** a real recording with a returning motif or escalation that conveys repetition without reusing the opening cue.

## Breaking the cycle

**Picture:** ordinary light, surviving people and practical repair in a visibly damaged world; no untouched city, no restored population, no divine figure.

**Text:** research a short passage or remark about restraint, mercy, endurance or returning to ordinary life; it must not claim that famine, disease, war or grief has ended or that the dead return.

**Audio:** a real recording that gives sober release, not a victory march, ending naturally within the playback window.

## Research and production gates

The text researcher returns for each slot: title, any quotation with exact translation, verse and attribution, button text, source passage and why it fits.
No translations are mixed, no quotation is invented, and working labels never ship as final text.

The audio researcher returns: work, composer, performer, exact source, composition and recording rights, acquisition availability, passage, duration and cut points.
A streaming page alone is not distribution evidence; no drone, noise track, unlicensed film cue or synthetic imitation substitutes while research is open.

The art worker supplies each 457 × 328 composition; the implementer owns IDs, queue behavior, localisation, the shipped sound, volume and duplicate guards.
