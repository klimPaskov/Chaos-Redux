# 17. Super-Event Milestones

## Four milestones

The event uses four super-event directions.
The labels here are production identifiers, not finished player-facing titles, quotations, buttons, or audio selections.

| Semantic slot | Trigger | Repeat policy | Main purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tp85_opening_sign` | First accepted global Blood onset in the episode | Once per episode | Establish that a world-scale anomaly has begun |
| `tp85_firstborn_night` | First complete tenth-plague settlement in the episode | Once per episode | Mark the largest intended single direct mortality night |
| `tp85_endless_return` | First valid activation of The Plagues Without End | Once per episode | Reveal that the completed sequence has become an endless threat |
| `tp85_cycle_broken` | Verified full-cycle escape from that endless branch | Once per episode | Mark the end of recurrence without pretending the world is restored |

Allocate actual global super-event IDs only after inspecting the current registry.
An existing numeric slot must never be overwritten because its title appears unrelated.

National settings and the shared super-event queue remain respected.
The mechanic proceeds without waiting for playback.
Suppressed presentation does not suppress casualties, deadlines, or achievements.

## Opening global sign

### Picture direction

Use a broad civilian view of a familiar water-dependent world becoming impossible: a river, city intake, bridge, or reservoir with red water and period human activity.
A distinct Nile context can inform the visual without making the catastrophe look exclusive to Egypt.

The first scene should be ominous and recognizable, with enough ordinary life remaining to show what later phases will destroy.
It should not already depict the final world-end.

### Text research direction

Research the relevant Exodus water passages, later cultural references to the first plague, and an appropriate brief remark about authority confronting an unmistakable sign.
The final title, quotation, and button require source checks.
The designer's working label is not final localization.

If a biblical translation is quoted, record the exact translation, verse, quoted words, and reproduction conditions.
Do not merge wording from several translations into an invented quotation.

### Audio direction

Seek a real structured musical recording with a restrained ominous opening and a recognizable development.
Record composition and recording rights separately.
No specific piece is selected here.

The final cue should fit the shared playback contract, generally a carefully chosen one- to two-minute passage where that consumer permits it.
It must not be a placeholder drone, a noise track, an unlicensed film excerpt, or a synthetic imitation presented as a historical recording.

## The firstborn night

### Picture direction

The image communicates sudden absence across ages and social roles.
A household, civic building, or workplace can contain empty adult and child places with survivors beginning to understand the loss.
Avoid graphic bodies and avoid reducing the plague to child mortality.

Prepared households may remain visible as part of the contrast.
Do not falsely portray a named real historical person as a confirmed victim.

### Text research direction

Research the final warning and firstborn-night passages, mourning across social ranks, and an appropriate cultural remark about loss.
The worldwide game adaptation must be distinguished from the source narrative.

The text does not claim a fixed historical or scripted death total.
The report context can provide actual applied deaths through the shared owner.
A selected quotation cannot be altered to insert a generated casualty number.

### Audio direction

Seek a real recording capable of carrying grief and collective shock without turning the moment into a triumphant battle cue.
Lyrics are not required.
Any vocal text must be translated and checked before use.

The selected excerpt must have a workable start and end, sufficient rights evidence, and a source that can actually be obtained for production.
A composition being old does not establish that a modern recording is free to distribute.

## The endless return

### Picture direction

Return to a repaired water system turning red while damage from the previous cycle remains visible.
The scene should show why recurrence is worse: there is less working capacity left to confront it.
It is a newly composed image, not simply the opening picture with a stronger red overlay.

### Text research direction

Research hardening, repeated warnings, and the idea of a refused opportunity to change.
Do not invent an eleventh plague or present an unsupported theological claim as the game's canonical explanation.

The final wording must make clear that the complete ten-stage sequence is returning.
The consequence is repeated catastrophe, not a new unrelated monster or faction.

### Audio direction

Seek a real structured recording with an identifiable returning motif or escalation that can communicate repetition without simply reusing the opening audio.
The final producer supplies the cut, levels, source, and licensing evidence.
The research role proposes sources; the implementation role confirms the actual shipped file and consumer.

## Breaking the cycle

### Picture direction

Show ordinary light, surviving people, and practical repair in a world still visibly damaged.
Do not show an untouched city, restored population, or a divine figure confirming one explanation.

The contrast is that another Blood is no longer inevitable.
Reconstruction has become possible.

### Text research direction

Research a brief sourced passage or cultural remark about restraint, mercy, endurance, or returning to ordinary life.
Its meaning must fit collective civilian protection and the game's actual escape condition.

Do not declare that all famine, disease, war, or grief has ended.
No text can imply that the dead have returned.

### Audio direction

Seek a real recording that provides release without an unconditional victory march.
The cue can remain sober.
It should finish naturally within the supported playback window rather than being cut off at an arbitrary loud point.

## Research and implementation gates

For every slot, the text researcher must provide final title, quotation if used, attribution, button, supporting passage or source, translation identity where relevant, and a reason the selection fits this exact milestone.
Unsourced cultural allusions and invented author attributions are blockers.

The audio researcher must provide source provenance, composition rights, recording rights, exact passage, duration, acquisition availability, and production instructions.
A streaming page alone is not proof of a distributable recording.

The art worker supplies a separately reviewed 457 × 328 composition.
The coding worker owns the final IDs, queue behavior, localization, shipped audio, volume, and event-trigger integration.

Do not implement working labels or unresearched prose as temporary final text.
Do not substitute generated noise when audio research is unresolved.
An unavailable licensed recording remains an open production item.

All four slots require duplicate guards, country-presentation settings, save/load integrity, and a runtime consumer review.
The second and later ordinary renewed cycles use appropriate reports rather than replaying every global super-event.
