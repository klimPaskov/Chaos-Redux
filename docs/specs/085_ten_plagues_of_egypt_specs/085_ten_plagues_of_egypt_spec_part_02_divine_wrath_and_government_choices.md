# 2. Divine Wrath and Government Choices

## The public value

Divine Wrath runs from 0 to 100.
It is an event-specific index, not a percentage of religious belief or a judgment about the ethnicity of a population.
The ordinary opening value is 25.
Scenario presets and inherited responsibility can establish another starting value.

The display distinguishes relatively restrained conditions below 25, mounting danger from 25 through 49, severe danger from 50 through 74, and extreme danger from 75 upward.
These bands help the player read the situation.
The underlying severity response is continuous so crossing one point does not suddenly double a country's casualties.

Every change must identify the concrete government action or failure responsible.
A short explanation might point to an abandoned shelter commitment, completed water distribution, or supplies delivered to a neighbor.
Do not expose an unexplained positive number after an unrelated event.

Poverty, being at war, suffering a disaster, or receiving refugees does not automatically count as wrongdoing.
The country is judged through the choices it can actually make.
A government without trucks can organize a slower local response.
A government that deliberately withholds available transport from a promised evacuation has made a different choice.

## Harden Our Hearts

Harden Our Hearts is available once per plague while that phase presents a meaningful conflict between civilian protection and national production or military activity.
It should be attractive to a government fighting for its survival, not a deliberately useless trap.

The baseline policy grants 10 percentage points of War Support, a temporary 5-point Stability cushion, and a substantial reduction in the event-owned production interruption attributable to the current plague.
The production benefit removes half of the relevant Event 085 disruption rather than adding an unrestricted industrial bonus.
It cannot restore a physically destroyed factory or cancel a Famine-owned penalty.
Military mobilization can continue without an event-created demand to lower laws.

The initial commitment raises Divine Wrath by 15.
If the country keeps the policy through the phase while an accepted civilian obligation remains knowingly unmet, the recorded refusal adds a further 5 once.
There is no invisible daily Wrath accumulation.
At the next plague, the government must choose again to obtain a new continuity benefit.

The Stability cushion expires.
It does not permanently compensate every later mourning loss.
War Support remains subject to the ordinary game and later event consequences rather than becoming an indefinitely stacking morale farm.

Hardening does not prohibit every local act of compassion.
A government can keep strategic factories running while protecting a threatened settlement.
However, the same factories or transports cannot simultaneously provide full war-continuity credit and be committed to an incompatible relief action.
The player must be shown which commitment conflicts.

During Darkness, hardening preserves a bounded emergency war effort and political calm, but it does not prevent the loss of daylight or make aircraft ignore the visibility hazard.
The final refusal remains a choice with a clear warning about the tenth plague.

## Heed the Warning

Heed the Warning opens the current plague's protective action, not a generic Political Power purchase.
Its effect comes from completed work: safe water delivered, cleanup completed, people sheltered, reserves protected, or households reached.

A fully completed major protective objective reduces Divine Wrath by 10.
A materially useful partial result reduces it by 5.
The same objective can earn credit only once for the same episode, phase, target, and responsibility assignment.
These values are proposed common anchors, with any exception justified by the action's actual reach.

Announcing relief does not lower Wrath.
Dispatching a truck does not count as a completed delivery.
Funding a project that is later captured or interrupted preserves the work actually completed but does not grant the full promised result.

Country-wide protection scales with people reached.
A small local intervention cannot lower every later mortality calculation as though the entire country was prepared.
The common Wrath reduction represents responsible conduct, while direct protection remains attached to the people and places that received it.

## Cooperation and exploitation

A verified foreign delivery that materially contributes to a recipient's active protection or relief objective reduces the donor's Wrath by 5.
A donor may earn this cooperation credit at most twice during one plague, and the same shipment cannot be credited through several bilateral wrappers.
Aid returned to its origin, ceremonial transfers, and deliberately manufactured emergencies do not qualify.

Receiving aid carries no automatic Wrath reduction.
Putting it to its documented protective use does.
A country cannot lower Wrath by accepting stores and immediately requisitioning them for an unrelated offensive.

The following conduct can raise Wrath when the relevant owner supplies an actual consequence or fulfilled policy fact:

| Conduct | Proposed change | Limitation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Deliberately abandon a feasible accepted civilian commitment | +10 | Once per failed obligation, excluding genuine impossibility |
| Divert delivered relief into a competing war commitment | +10 | Requires the same material or capacity to be traced |
| Refuse a final warning through Harden Our Hearts | +15 | Uses the phase policy entry, not an extra duplicate |
| Violently suppress an actual civilian emergency | +15 | Requires an owner-proven incident, not an ideology assumption |
| Persist with a recorded exploitative emergency policy | +5 | Once at a named phase settlement, not periodic polling |
| Complete a substantial protective objective | −10 | Actual result, one credit |
| Complete a meaningful partial response | −5 | Cannot be added to a later full credit beyond the full total |
| Deliver effective foreign relief | −5 | At most two distinct qualifying deliveries per phase |

A later completion upgrades partial credit to the full total by granting only the remaining difference.
This prevents a partial response followed by full completion from receiving both rewards in full.

Failures caused solely by a destroyed route, genuine lack of reachable capacity, or a valid hostile occupation do not receive the same blame as deliberate refusal.
They still leave people exposed.
Divine Wrath and physical preparedness are related but separate causal concepts.

## How Wrath changes harm

For ordinary pre-tenth exposure, the initial continuous Wrath multiplier is:

`0.50 + 1.50 × (Divine Wrath / 100)²`

This gives a restrained country lower severity without complete immunity and makes repeated hardening increasingly dangerous.
At Wrath 0 the multiplier is 0.50, at 50 it is 0.875, and at 100 it is 2.00.
Evolution and recurrence can intensify the manifestation, while completed physical protection reduces the relevant exposure.

This formula is a proposed tuning anchor.
It is not a claim about the existing engine or an empirical casualty model.
Part 15 supplies campaign comparisons that must determine whether it produces the intended strategic tradeoff.

Do not multiply every economic, demographic, military, and food effect by the same final number without considering its units.
Food pressure is sent as the Famine owner's defined component.
Civilian deaths use an actual population transaction.
Building damage remains bounded by what exists.
A production penalty remains within the consolidated modifier's ceiling.

The tenth plague uses its separate firstborn vulnerability calculation in Part 8.
That calculation is deliberately more sensitive to the combination of high Wrath and rejected preparation.

## Responsibility survives border changes

Civilian protection already established in a state remains there when control changes.
A new authority does not undo functioning shelters merely because it has a different tag.
A government also cannot escape its recorded conduct by releasing a puppet or changing its cosmetic name.

When responsibility splits, the original contribution to global Wrath is divided between successor responsibilities without creating or deleting weight.
When governments merge, their relevant Wrath obligations combine using the transferred responsibility weights.
The merger must not let a tiny low-Wrath tag absorb a large high-Wrath country and instantly erase the latter's history.

Refugees are not carriers of another government's moral liability.
Receiving them does not directly raise the host's Wrath.
Their real needs can strain food, housing, and disease services through the respective systems.
How the host responds to those needs can subsequently change its own Wrath.

## Recovery of conduct

Wrath does not fall automatically because the population has died or because the government waits behind closed borders.
There is always a viable constructive route while people and responsible authorities remain: local protection, relief delivery, restoring essential services, or cooperating on the next warning.

The system must not permanently trap an impoverished country at 100.
Actions can be scaled down, staged locally, or supported from abroad.
They still require actual work and elapsed time.
A low-cost local route may save fewer people at once, but it can rebuild credibility without an arbitrary factory requirement.

The player should be able to change direction halfway through the sequence.
Earlier deaths and destruction remain, and late repentance cannot retroactively complete Passover.
Nevertheless, relief before the later plagues can lower Wrath, protect survivors, and contribute to ending recurrence.
