# Narrative Source Notes — Ten Plagues of Egypt

Research date: 2026-09-23.
These sources give literary and religious context.
They do not establish the plagues as historical events, supply a medical diagnosis or set any number in the game.
The user's brief is the authority for the worldwide adaptation, universal access to protection, real population loss, owner systems and the three Evolutions.

## Boundaries

No super-event quotation, title, button, cultural remark or recording has been selected; that is the super-event researchers' work.
Verse numbering and English wording differ between editions, so the text researcher must name the exact edition before quoting.
No historical character's birth order and no historical casualty figure is adopted, and the 25% firstborn share is a game abstraction that must never be presented as research.
