# 3. Water into Blood and Frogs

## I. Water into Blood

The first plague begins with visible changes in freshwater and conflicting official explanations.
Water drawn from rivers, reservoirs, wells, or distribution points becomes blood-like, foul, or unusable.
The manifestation is supernatural in its reach, but its immediate consequences are concrete: drinking water, sanitation, cooling, food preparation, and military supply compete for functioning sources.

The Nile receives a dedicated report and a distinct visual composition where the current map contains the relevant Egyptian territory.
That report reaches the authority actually responsible for the affected area.
It does not require an independent EGY tag, move a capital, or grant Egypt a special mechanical guilt value.

Other countries receive geographically appropriate reports from their actual affected states.
A reservoir serving an industrial district, a municipal water queue, or a well supplying an isolated settlement can carry the same opening.
Do not invent named installations that the current map cannot identify.

### The water response

The primary protective choice is to establish emergency safe-water distribution.
The player selects a threatened population center or functioning route from the current affected-state list.
A motorized route commits trucks and fuel and may include support equipment.
A low-capacity local route instead diverts civilian labor and accepts a temporary production interruption.
Both routes must provide a real service, and their displayed reach follows the population they can supply.

A second choice protects civilian priority against industrial demand.
The government can shut down water-intensive work for a short period and preserve water for homes, kitchens, and clinics.
The benefit is a lower current exposure and a useful service network for later sanitation phases.
The sacrifice is immediate lost production, not a permanent consumer-goods penalty that continues after the emergency.

Harden Our Hearts maintains industrial priority and reduces the Event 085 output interruption.
It leaves civilian distribution weaker and raises Wrath.
The country can still invest in a small local water route, but it cannot claim the same industrial capacity as both uninterrupted and diverted.

A bounded investigation records the contradiction between normal explanations and the observed spread.
Its gameplay value is better targeting and earlier identification of threatened water-service states.
It does not discover a guaranteed scientific cure, award unrelated technology, or remove the biblical sequence.

### The urgent objective

The water objective asks the country to keep one selected civilian supply route or local distribution program functioning through the acute interval.
The initial window is 10 days within the twenty-day phase.
Full success supplies the committed population for the complete objective window.
Partial success recognizes actual service days or a smaller completed delivery.
Failure leaves the uncovered population exposed.

Protection reduces the phase's modest direct mortality and its sanitation pressure.
It can also reduce proved food and supply disruption.
Successful distribution lowers Wrath through the common completion rule.
The continuing physical usefulness of the distribution network is retained when frogs arrive.

The phase's proposed direct mortality budget is 5 basis points of the exposed live population before Wrath, protection, and intensity factors.
This is a whole-phase budget, not a daily percentage.
Subsequent disease deaths belong to an actual disease owner and cannot be added again as contaminated-water deaths.

### Recession

At baseline, usable sources begin returning during the second half of the phase.
The country can reopen interrupted production gradually.
The report makes clear which sources are recovering and which service problems remain.

A recovered river does not refill every food reserve or remove an established outbreak.
The owner systems retain those consequences.
Water into Blood leaves a meaningful early record, but it should ordinarily be less deadly than the later medical and storm phases.

Under Evolution I, the water manifestation can persist into Frogs and Lice.
The country must decide whether to keep a useful distribution network operating instead of reclaiming all of its trucks and labor as soon as the next title appears.

## II. Frogs

Frogs emerge in extraordinary numbers and enter homes, ministries, factories, farms, barracks, and depots.
The phase begins with disruption and disbelief rather than immediate mass death.
An invaded production hall, blocked drainage, or unusable kitchen gives the player a tangible problem.

The strongest manifestations use the phase's valid state footprint.
Each affected state records the local disruption once, then progresses toward clearance or a sanitation aftermath.
It must not damage every factory every day merely because a frog flag remains present.

### Keep services working

One response prioritizes civilian water points, kitchens, and homes.
It uses organized cleanup and temporary closure of affected facilities.
The immediate economic cost is greater than leaving the disruption untreated, but the preserved civilian services reduce later disease pressure.

Another response protects a strategic transport or production site.
It may keep an important depot, rail connection, or factory complex usable with fewer resources than a broad cleanup.
This is a valid limited response, not a moral failure.
It earns protection only for the actual site and people served.

The government can also request a neighboring cleanup or relief contribution where a route and spare capacity exist.
It does not receive new equipment from an abstract global pool.

The hardening choice directs crews toward war-critical facilities and minimizes public acknowledgment.
Its continuity benefit should be especially tempting here because the direct threat still appears absurdly small.
The later sanitation costs make repeated dismissal consequential.

### The mass die-off

Near the end of the acute frog phase, large numbers of frogs die.
The visual and report direction shifts from swarms in buildings to exhausted crews clearing streets and service areas.
This transition is an essential part of the plague, rather than an optional decorative event.

The country's earlier cleanup determines how severe the sanitation aftermath becomes.
A functioning water network and completed clearance can keep essential districts usable.
Uncleared areas create proved pressure for the disease owner during Lice and Flies.

The objective is to clear the selected civilian service district before the die-off aftermath is settled.
Full success preserves its services and prevents the avoidable part of that sanitation pressure.
Partial success leaves a smaller cleanup burden.
Failure retains the actual backlog and worsens later exposure.
There is no instant nationwide disease just because the objective timer expired.

### Mortality and equipment

Direct frog mortality is normally zero.
An independently proved accident may use a small direct-loss receipt, while a genuine secondary outbreak uses the disease owner.
The initial secondary-disease balance allowance is around 5 basis points over the relevant exposure period, subject to the disease owner's real progression.
This is not a command to remove that population at the end of the phase.

Destroyed or unusable stockpiles require a specific affected depot and a bounded material loss.
Do not randomly delete a percentage of every country's entire arsenal.
Frogs mainly interrupt access and work.
Any actual building damage should be exceptional and linked to an identified accident or service failure.

### After Frogs

At baseline, the visible swarms recede before Lice reaches full strength.
Cleanup can continue without keeping the full frog production penalty.
The country retains useful sanitation work and any unresolved local disease conditions.

At higher intensity, swarms can occupy several major sites while the water crisis persists.
The phase becomes a logistics and sanitation conflict, not a new combat system.
There are no frog divisions, frog states, giant-frog invasions, or unrelated supernatural rewards.

## The relationship between the first two plagues

These phases teach the central choice at manageable severity.
Protective work produces a useful foundation.
Denial preserves output and raises future risk.
The first two phases should not already consume the demographic scale reserved for Livestock, Boils, Hail and Fire, Locusts, and the tenth plague.

The two responses also establish recurring local actors: municipal crews, district officials, civilian kitchens, and military transport officers.
Later reports can return to those institutions when their earlier work matters.
That continuity gives the event a human narrative without creating a second political system or an additional meter.
