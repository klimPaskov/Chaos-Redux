# 16. Visual Presentation and Asset Coverage

## A world that changes from plague to plague

The visual progression begins with an unsettling environmental change and ends with recognizable mass civilian catastrophe.
Each plague needs a distinct scene and silhouette.
Ten interchangeable disaster photographs or a single red tint over the entire package would lose the sequence's identity.

The setting is the current campaign's interwar and wartime world.
Generated scenes should use plausible period clothing, vehicles, factories, homes, and civic spaces without pretending to be archival evidence of a real historical plague.
They should depict the event's fictional circumstances.

Egypt receives an additional Nile scene.
The rest of the package remains global: urban and rural districts, different climates, small countries, industrial powers, occupied areas, and refugee reception are represented without restricting the event to one region.

Civilian suffering is shown through damaged services, interrupted work, shelters, empty places, and mourning.
Named-character deaths are not illustrated with invented photographs of real people dying.
The tenth plague's imagery must not imply that all firstborn victims were children.

The known static inventory therefore contains 93 planned final outputs.
This is a coverage target, not a statement that any art has been generated.
The Darkness presentation must be resolved separately and may add runtime assets after the supported consumer is established.

Generate full-quality original sources before preparing exact final crops.
Country reports receive the skill's local sepia, tilt, border, and transparent-corner processing.
News uses its actual black-and-white consumer.
Native-transparent icons must have genuinely usable alpha, not a painted checkerboard.

Convert ordinary final DDS files with the repository event-assets skill's converter and verify its required format and dimensions.
Achievement triplets use the dedicated achievement processor, preserving its immutable templates and exact not-eligible overlay.
Do not regenerate three unrelated achievement drawings or process achievement variants through the ordinary single-image converter.

Each final output has a manifest row linking its source, processing, dimensions, transparency, exact destination, sprite or consumer, phase condition, and review evidence.
Filename counts alone do not prove that a consumer displays the correct image.

## News and super-event pictures

The six news subjects are global Blood, recognition of the ordered pattern, widespread storm destruction, global Darkness, the firstborn night, and mass Exodus.
They use wider compositions suitable for the news consumer and are not resized report-card files.

The four super-event pictures cover the opening global sign, the tenth-plague night, the first endless-cycle activation, and verified escape.
Their image directions and research gates are in Part 17.
Each is composed for 457 × 328 rather than stretched from a news crop.

Global-news distribution is guarded by episode and milestone.
An image is not a reason to generate another global popup for every country or state.

## Category pictures and interface

Provide ten phase pictures plus mourning and reconstruction pictures.
Use mutually exclusive triggered conditions so the current category state displays exactly one appropriate image.
An intermission before a known restart uses the current mourning or reconstruction picture and a contextual date; it does not require another player-managed page.

The category remains an ordinary compact decision surface with one mechanic value.
A simple picture is eligible here because it adds visual context without competing with a custom meter or full command GUI.

The global Darkness treatment is separate from this category.
If a supported event-owned interface layer is required, it is noninteractive, respects map and input boundaries, and is reviewed by the UI specialist.
It must not obscure orders, modal dialogs, or accessibility settings.
A shared interface rewrite is not authorized without evidence that a narrow solution is insufficient.

No new focus-tree inlay, formable state puzzle, country selection window, or 3D plague-unit interface is justified.
Those skill families were considered and are not part of this event's mapped content.

## Icons

The three national spirit subjects are the consolidated active crisis, government continuity policy, and mourning/reconstruction.
Each has a different silhouette and works at 64 × 64.
The crisis tooltip and modifier state change with the active consequences; it does not need ten simultaneous national spirit icons.

The sixteen decision subjects correspond in order to the families in Part 14: hardened policy, safe water, frog clearance, infestation response, food/sanitation, rural continuity, civilian care, military stand-down, storm shelter, evacuation, harvest preservation, essential services, normal Passover, emergency local Passover, foreign relief, and reconstruction coordination.

Use clear objects and actions rather than tiny text, numbered banners, or elaborate miniature scenes.
Shared Famine, Migration, and Natural Disasters actions keep their owners' established assets.
Event 085 does not commission duplicate owner icons simply to fill its own inventory.

## Achievement art

The twelve achievement subjects are defined in Part 18.
Each receives one original transparent subject, then the standard colored, grey, and not-eligible states.
All three variants must align exactly.

The standard not-eligible red cross is a deterministic processor overlay.
It is not redrawn by image generation.
Names, text, and eligibility meaning stay in localization and UI, not inside an unreadable 64-pixel picture.

## Animation and source evidence

No new 3D animals, skeletal animation, animated portraits, or moving leader assets are required.
Existing characters remain existing characters.
The event does require a convincingly global Darkness state.

If motion is later chosen for a supported 2D consumer, use the frame-animation skill with genuinely distinct authored frames and an explicit static accessibility state.
A transform-only wobble of a single image is not accepted as a meaningful new animation.
Do not add animation merely to satisfy an asset count.

Generated original art retains its generation prompt, source file, processing history, and parent review.
Historically sourced reference material retains source and rights records.
A sourced image used only for reference is distinguished from an image actually distributed in the mod.

The final asset package is complete only when every declared runtime consumer resolves, every processing requirement is met, and the visible result has been reviewed through available supported evidence.
This specification contains directions and inventory only.
