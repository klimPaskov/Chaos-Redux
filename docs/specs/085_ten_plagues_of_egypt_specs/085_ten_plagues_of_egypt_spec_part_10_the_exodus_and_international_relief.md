# 10. The Exodus and International Relief

## Flight from a world under judgment

Evolution II turns the plagues' displacement into the Exodus: people flee states whose services have collapsed toward countries that seem safer.
High-Wrath countries usually lose the most people and low-Wrath countries become destinations.

Before Evolution II, flight still happens through the owners' ordinary causes: Famine survivor flight, disaster displacement and outbreak flight.
Evolution II adds Exodus pressure on top of those causes.

### Exodus requests

At each phase opening under Evolution II, Event 085 sends Migration one flight request for each affected state that is in Famine's acute shortage or worse, struck by Hail and Fire, or in an active Boils outbreak, in a country at Wrath 50 or more.
The request uses Migration's incident adapter with the Ten Plagues cause, the state, and a share of its population scaled by Wrath and the number of those conditions present.
Migration accepts, caps and moves the people.
Wrath alone never moves anyone, and no high-Wrath state loses a flat percentage.

## Migration owns the people

Migration owns cohorts, routes, border policy, reception, transit, integration and return; Event 085 keeps no refugee inventory.
A person who leaves is not dead: if 100,000 leave, 1,000 die on the route and 99,000 arrive, the origin loses 100,000, the destination gains 99,000 and Deaths records 1,000 route deaths, with no further debit.
Closed borders do not delete people; they stay trapped, look for another route or need local protection, and their food and shelter needs go to Famine through Migration's trapped-population adapter.

Refugees carry no Wrath and are not assumed to carry disease; any outbreak needs the outbreak owner's exposure facts.
Passover status belongs to states, not people (Part 7).

## Government responses

The host's choices are Migration's existing actions:

- **Open Emergency Reception**, **Screen Incoming Civilians** and **Distribute Arrivals** for broad or bounded reception.
- **Permit Transit** toward a confirmed destination.
- **Close the Border** and **Enforce the Closure** for refusal.
- **Request Passage** with **Accept** or **Refuse Passage** for corridors between countries, including countries at war.

Event 085 does not copy these actions.
Its category shows an urgent Exodus line when a neighboring state's flight request targets the country, linking to Migration's category.
Refusing reception when capacity exists raises no Wrath by itself; violent enforcement does when it produces a Condemnation incident (Part 2).
Reception camps follow the camp system's own rules; the event neither labels every camp an atrocity nor ignores recorded abuse.

## Corridors during war

Countries at war can open a limited civilian corridor through Migration's passage actions without joining a faction, making peace or granting military access.
A corridor has an origin, a destination and a service window.
Using it to move troops or divert its supplies cancels it through Migration and counts as deliberate diversion for Wrath (Part 2).
AI willingness falls with its current offensive, transport shortage, a threatened capital or a record of violated agreements; religion never decides it.

## Effective foreign relief

**Foreign Relief Pledge** (Part 14) lets a donor fund one recipient's active Event 085 protective objective at a time, paying the material part of its cost from the donor's stockpile.
When the recipient's objective completes, the donor gets −5 Wrath, at most twice per phase, and the recipient gets its ordinary completion credit; neither receives a second bonus for the same work.
Famine, Migration and Natural Disasters relief continue under their own rules.

Aid to a recipient with no active objective, aid returned to its origin, or a pledge whose objective fails earns nothing.
A failure caused by a destroyed route or a lost state carries no blame; withdrawing the pledge's equipment for a war action counts as diversion.

## Exodus during Darkness

Crossing a border is not Passover.
Arrivals take the status of the state they are in at the deadline, and the 10% growth rule (Part 7) prevents last-hour transfers into prepared states from creating protection.
There is no last-hour national evacuation into a prepared ally; local preparation stays the realistic alternative.

## Egypt and the meaning of Exodus

Egypt receives particular news and scene treatment because of the Nile and the biblical story.
It is not the mandatory source of refugees.
The Exodus label creates no required ethnic migration, new state, territorial claim or conversion.

## After the sequence

Ending the plagues does not send everyone home.
Migration owns return, settlement, continuing reception and trapped people, and a ruined origin can stay unsafe.
Event 085 stops sending new Exodus requests when its episode ends.
