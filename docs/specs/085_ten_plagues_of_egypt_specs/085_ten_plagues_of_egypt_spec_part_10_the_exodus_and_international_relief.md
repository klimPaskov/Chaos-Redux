# 10. The Exodus and International Relief

## Flight from a world under judgment

Evolution II introduces the Exodus as a major world response.
People attempt to leave states where services have collapsed or where the next plague seems unsurvivable.
High-Wrath countries often generate the strongest pressure, while comparatively safer low-Wrath countries become sought-after destinations.

Wrath alone is not a population-transfer operation.
An Exodus request must identify actual people, an origin, a cause or proven departure episode, and a valid movement context.
Famine, disease, storm destruction, and failed civilian services can establish material causes.
Fear-driven departures require an explicit Migration-accepted incident rather than an invented percentage removed from every high-Wrath state.

Earlier plagues can already cause genuine migration through existing owner systems.
Evolution II adds the large, event-specific Exodus direction and coordination opportunities.
It does not suppress valid displacement before its unlock or retroactively claim unrelated movements.

## Migration owns the people

Migration owns cohorts, routes, border policy, reception, transit, integration, return, and exact population transfers.
Event 085 supplies its cause, warning, origin, protection context, and requested coordination.
It does not maintain a competing refugee inventory.

A person leaving a state is not automatically dead.
If 100,000 people depart, 1,000 die on the route, and 99,000 arrive, the origin loses 100,000, the destination gains 99,000, and the Deaths system records 1,000 route deaths.
There is no additional 1,000-person debit from the origin after that transfer.

Closed borders do not delete a cohort.
People can remain trapped, seek a different route, or require local protection.
Actual food and shelter pressure then reaches the appropriate owner.
Route danger and violent enforcement can cause separately settled deaths where the owner proves them.

The firstborn-survivor and Passover records travel with the surviving people through a typed extension.
Neither origin nor destination may independently regenerate those records.
Transporting protected civilians is not a way to duplicate the protected population.

## Finding a safer destination

Candidate destinations first pass actual route, reception, food-safety, and hazard checks.
Low Divine Wrath is a useful preference only among valid destinations.
It does not prove that a country has food, an open port, functioning shelters, spare reception capacity, or protection from a storm already on its way.

A low-Wrath country can become overwhelmed.
Its government faces genuine decisions about distribution, transit, relief imports, additional shelter, and international support.
Refugees are not treated as inherently diseased or morally responsible for the crisis.
Any disease consequence requires the outbreak owner's actual exposure facts.

Unknown food safety does not count as stable.
An untracked or newly released destination must obtain a valid projection from Famine rather than inheriting an optimistic default.
A route with no proven transit permission or physical capacity remains unavailable.

Landlocked and island countries use appropriate routes.
The event cannot demand convoys from a country that can use a safe overland corridor.
It cannot treat every island as automatically blockaded.

## Government responses

Existing Migration actions own accepting refugees, controlled reception, border restrictions, evacuation preparation, transit, reception districts, and integration.
Event 085 does not copy those actions into a second menu with cheaper costs or different safety rules.

Its own coordination surface can highlight an urgent Exodus request and lead to the appropriate owner action.
It can offer an event-specific international pledge that requests a valid route or relief operation.
Completion returns a receipt; the event then applies only its own Wrath and narrative consequences.

The practical choices are broad reception with a real capacity commitment, limited admission into prepared districts, safe transit toward a confirmed destination, or refusing a presently feasible humanitarian request.
Restrictions caused by genuinely absent safe capacity are distinguished from violent rejection or opportunistic exploitation.

Emergency reception districts and camps are described according to their actual function and policy.
The event does not automatically label every camp an atrocity, nor does it ignore owner-proven abuse.
Political and humanitarian consequences follow the relevant evidence.

## Cooperation during war

Countries at war can negotiate a limited civilian corridor or protected relief passage without entering the same faction, ending every war, or changing ideology.
The relevant diplomatic and Migration owners must prove permissions and route safety.

A corridor has a purpose, an origin, a destination, and a service window.
It does not become universal military access.
Using it to move combat formations or divert its supplies cancels only the affected agreement through the proper owner and can raise Wrath when deliberate exploitation is proven.

Enemy cooperation should be difficult enough to matter but possible when survival interests align.
A country's current offensive, transport shortage, threatened capital, or record of violating relief agreements can change its willingness.
Religion alone never decides acceptance.

## Effective foreign relief

A donor can maintain one Event 085 outbound coordination pledge at a time.
The pledge identifies a real recipient need and the actual transport or local delivery arrangement.
Ordinary Famine, Migration, or Natural Disasters assistance continues under those owners' own limits.

A valid completed delivery can lower the donor's Divine Wrath by 5.
At most two distinct deliveries receive this credit per phase.
The recipient receives only the ordinary credit for its own completed protective commitment; it does not gain a second automatic donation bonus on top of the same work.

Sending a nominal resource amount to a safe ally, sending it back, or transferring a stockpile without fulfilling a need gives no Wrath credit.
Two governments cannot farm the same shipment repeatedly.
A partly delivered operation credits only the meaningful accepted result.

A canceled promise does not automatically make the donor guilty.
A destroyed route or newly unsafe destination can be a legitimate failure.
Deliberately diverting already committed relief into a competing offensive is different and follows the conduct rules.

## Exodus during Darkness

A late flight may reach a safer district, but crossing a border is not itself Passover preparation.
Arrivals must be placed in a genuinely reached household or reception area, or retain a verified prepared arrangement that remains effective through the move.

The shared global deadline still applies.
A convoy unresolved at midnight cannot be treated as already received merely because its destination is protected.
The exact location and living custody cohort at the deadline determine exposure.

The event must not offer a last-hour national evacuation that teleports millions into a prepared ally.
Local shelter and emergency preparation remain meaningful alternatives where a safe transfer cannot finish.

## Egypt and the meaning of Exodus

Egypt receives particular news and scene treatment because of the Nile and the biblical association.
It does not become the mandatory source of every refugee cohort or the only country capable of understanding the warning.

The Exodus label describes this crisis's mass flight.
It does not create a required ethnic migration, a new historical state, a territorial claim, or a compulsory religious conversion.
Current map conditions and actual civilian needs determine the movement.

## After the sequence

Ending the plagues does not instantly send everyone home.
Migration owns safe return, permanent settlement, continuing reception, and unresolved trapped populations.
A destroyed origin can remain unsafe after Blood and Darkness have ended.

Event 085 stops adding new Exodus pressure when its causes end.
Existing cohorts continue to their valid outcomes.
Their durable history can support event achievements, but it does not keep an empty event coordination category open forever.
