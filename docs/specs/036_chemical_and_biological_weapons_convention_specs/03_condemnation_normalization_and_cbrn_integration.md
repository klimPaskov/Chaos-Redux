# Condemnation normalization and CBRN integration

## Governing rule

Event 036 modifies diplomatic normalization around unconventional warfare.

It does not own the physical attack, outbreak, strike, casualty, contamination, evidence, attribution, or protection transaction.

Every deliberate chemical, biological, nuclear, or thermonuclear action must continue through the shared CBRN action pipeline.

The action record remains authoritative for:

- attacker
- affected country
- exact state
- date
- weapon family
- agent or payload
- delivery method
- severity
- civilian deaths
- military casualty receipt
- contamination or outbreak change
- evidence
- attribution
- retaliation proof
- first-use proof
- repeat-use state

Event 036 reads that record after the normal action has been validated.

It applies a convention source multiplier and reaction policy, then leaves the durable record unchanged.

## Order of operations

For each public unconventional action:

1. Validate the action through the owning chemical, biological, nuclear, or Chaos weapon system.
2. Apply physical effects once.
3. Record deaths, contamination, outbreak change, evidence, attribution, retaliation, and first-use state.
4. Calculate the ordinary Condemnation source values.
5. Snapshot the attacker’s Event 036 posture, active evolution, treaty reservations, and relevant ratifications.
6. Apply the Event 036 multiplier to eligible chemical, biological, nuclear, and repeat-use source components.
7. Apply severity floors.
8. Add atrocity and cover-up sources without Event 036 relief.
9. Record the final public Condemnation transaction through the shared system.
10. Update member and non-signatory diplomatic reactions.

The treaty snapshot belongs to the action record or a linked receipt.

A later posture change must not retroactively rewrite the multiplier used for an earlier action.

Covert preparation never reduces public sources after exposure.

## Severity floors

The source multiplier can never reduce an action below its severity floor.

| Action class | Minimum share of normal source value | Reason |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Ordinary chemical action against a valid military target | Posture table controls | This is the conduct most strongly normalized by the convention |
| Chemical action with extreme civilian destruction | `0.50` | Civilian harm remains internationally serious |
| Ordinary controlled biological action | `0.20` | Biological spread retains a higher minimum than chemical use |
| Catastrophic biological outbreak | `0.70` | Runaway disease cannot become routine treaty conduct |
| Nuclear action against a valid military or industrial target | `0.50` | Nuclear relief remains bounded even at Evolution III |
| Nuclear action against a populated capital or dense civilian center | `0.80` | Civilian destruction dominates the diplomatic response |
| Thermonuclear action | `1.00` | The convention provides no thermonuclear relief |
| Chemical, biological, or nuclear doomsday effect | `1.00` | Terminal or mass-destruction conduct remains fully condemned |
| Atrocity source | `1.00` | Atrocity responsibility is outside the convention relief |
| Cover-up source | `1.00` | Concealment and falsification are outside the convention relief |

When several floors apply, use the strongest floor.

The implementation must not lower the floor because the convention is Dominant or because the attacker has repeated the action many times.

## First-use handling

First-use proof remains part of the shared CBRN action record.

At Baseline, Full Ratification reduces ordinary source values but does not erase the diplomatic distinction between first use and retaliation.

Evolution I allows countries to ratify explicit first-use articles.

Only countries that accepted those articles receive the full Evolution I first-use relief.

Countries that retained a reservation receive no first-use relief.

A country that initiates use in breach of its public policy receives:

- normal or floored Condemnation for the action
- a treaty-breach diplomatic memory
- reduced member trust
- increased withdrawal and sanction willingness among reservation members
- inspection or disclosure pressure when relevant

The breach does not create a new physical effect and does not duplicate generic Chaos.

## Repeat-use handling

The convention weakens repeat-use pressure for eligible members.

The repeat-use multiplier applies only to the repeat-use source component.

It does not reduce the underlying chemical, biological, or nuclear source twice.

A catastrophic action keeps its severity floor on every use.

The action history remains complete even when the repeat-use source becomes small.

## One-time normalization on entry

After all opening postures are recorded, the convention may reduce existing public Condemnation source components once.

The reduction is source-specific and country-specific.

| Opening posture | Chemical source reduction | Biological source reduction | Nuclear source reduction | Eligible repeat-use reduction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Full Ratification | `35%` | `25%` | `10%` | `20%` |
| Chemical Accession Only | `30%` | `0%` | `5%` | `15%` for chemical use only |
| Retaliation Reservation | `15%` | `10%` | `5%` | `10%` |
| Public Rejection | `0%` | `0%` | `0%` | `0%` |
| Public Rejection with Covert Preparation | `0%` | `0%` | `0%` | `0%` |

The one-time effect must:

- touch only public chemical, biological, nuclear, and eligible repeat-use source buckets
- preserve atrocity and cover-up values
- preserve hidden evidence
- preserve every action record
- preserve total deaths and contamination
- avoid reducing any source below its severity floor
- mark the country as normalized once
- use the opening posture snapshot

Existing bilateral restrictions and native embargoes are re-evaluated through their normal refresh rules.

Event 036 must not claim that an engine relationship has been cancelled when the engine cannot cancel it generically.

## Member and non-signatory reactions

The shared public Condemnation score remains one country score.

Different diplomatic camps react through participation willingness, opinion, treaty obligations, and sanction policy.

Convention members are less willing to sanction another member for policy-compatible ordinary use.

Reservation members remain more willing to sanction first-use breaches.

Public opponents retain normal or increased sanction willingness and may act at lower practical concern thresholds when the action violates the old prohibition.

A non-signatory does not create a second Condemnation total.

Its harsher position is expressed through bilateral and multilateral reaction logic.

## Evidence and inspections

Event 036 does not make hidden evidence public automatically.

An inspection treaty can create a valid disclosure route.

A successful inspection may reveal existing evidence through the shared evidence system.

Refusal can create suspicion, a treaty breach, and a cover-up source only when the owning evidence system proves the required conditions.

The convention must never invent evidence because an AI country chose to inspect.

## Protection

Protection, masks, decontamination, detection, and medical preparedness can reduce physical harm through their existing systems.

Protection never reduces responsibility, action history, evidence, attribution, or Condemnation by itself.

Treaty cooperation may improve access to protective equipment and research.

Any reduced casualty result must come from the protection and physical effect systems before Condemnation is calculated.

## Deaths, Air Cleanliness, famine, and migration

Every real population loss continues through the exact state civilian population transaction and Deaths registration.

Chemical contamination and biological outbreaks continue to affect Air Cleanliness through their owner systems.

Famine and migration remain separate owner systems and react only when their normal proof conditions are met.

Event 036 cannot suppress or rewrite those consequences because a use was treaty-compatible.

## Nuclear relationship

The convention weakens nuclear restraint through doctrine, research cooperation, targeting norms, and reduced ordinary nuclear Condemnation.

It does not classify nuclear warfare as ordinary chemical warfare.

Thermonuclear use remains outside convention relief.

Nuclear actions retain their existing fallout, deaths, contamination, action history, and generic Chaos behavior.

## Chaos weapon relationship

A Chaos weapon unlocked through the International Chaos Weapons Program uses its own physical, evidence, attribution, Condemnation, protection, and consequence systems when deployed.

The project unlock does not give that weapon special immunity.

Event 036 relief applies only when the weapon owner explicitly maps its use to an eligible chemical, biological, nuclear, or repeat-use source family.

A provider that lacks a safe Condemnation mapping remains unlockable only if its owner confirms that Event 036 relief does not apply.
