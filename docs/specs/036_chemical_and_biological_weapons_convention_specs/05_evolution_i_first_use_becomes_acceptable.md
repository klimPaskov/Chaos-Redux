# Evolution I: First Use Becomes Acceptable

## Availability and pacing

Evolution I becomes eligible at `200+` Chaos while Event 036 is active and the evolution is enabled.

Its base MTTH is 90 days, built through [the MTTH skill](../../../.claude/skills/chaos-redux-mtth/SKILL.md).
War between members, members with chemical or biological delivery, a recent member CBRN attack, and Established or Dominant Standing shorten it.
A reservation majority among members and Provisional Standing lengthen it.

If Event 036 first fires at `200+` Chaos, Evolution I follows 7 to 30 days after the opening responses resolve, as its own evolution event and log entry.

Activation follows the shared evolution contract (`is_current_evolution_enabled`, `record_events_log_evolution_entry`) and changes no Chaos.

## Playable change

The question moves from whether unconventional warfare may be prepared to whether it may be started.

Evolution I unlocks the First-Use Revision and the Missile and Delivery Cooperation Accord ([04](04_recurring_conventions_and_treaty_families.md#evolution-i)) and lets Retaliation Reservation members repeal through Revise Posture.

The first agenda after activation is the First-Use Revision.

## Permission line

Evolution I adds no new state.
The posture tooltip's permission line reads the country's ratified clauses: retaliation only, chemical first use, or chemical and biological first use.

The existing CBRN use policy decisions (`cbrn_choose_retaliation_only`, `cbrn_choose_battlefield_authorization`, `cbrn_choose_unrestricted_authorization`) remain the only place a country authorizes delivery.
The convention changes what each policy costs diplomatically, not what it permits.

## AI military behaviour

A ratified first-use clause raises AI willingness to choose Battlefield Authorization and to initiate a valid action.
Every existing validity gate in the CBRN delivery adapters still applies, and the AI rules are owned by [10](10_ai_behavior_and_probability_contract.md#first-use).

Chemical first use should become common among aggressive or desperate ratifiers, while biological first use stays rarer because outbreak and blowback risk remain.

## Opposition play

Opponents and reservation members can organize opposition against the First-Use Revision, publish forensic evidence through the existing CBRN action, and demand inspections under the Protocol.
A successful campaign can defeat the biological clause while the chemical clause passes, or defeat both.
It cannot remove the recorded evolution.

## Chaos outcomes

The first First-Use Revision chemical clause entering force adds `+5` Chaos once.
The first biological clause entering force adds `+5` Chaos once, even when both pass in the same resolution.
Failed votes, reservations, and posture changes add nothing, and actual weapon use keeps its generic sources.

## Event and log presentation

One evolution log entry tied to Event 036, with the coordinator or the First-Use Revision sponsor as actor.
Event Details explains that member governments may now authorize first use, without multipliers or Evolution II content.
