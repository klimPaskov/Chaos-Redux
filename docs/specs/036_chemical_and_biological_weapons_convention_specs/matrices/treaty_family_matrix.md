# Treaty family matrix

| Working treaty family | Minimum stage | Eligible postures | Normal quorum | Capability gate | Allowed reservation | Main benefits | Main obligations | Failure or breach consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Protection and Assistance Compact | Baseline | Full, Chemical, Reservation | Ordinary | At least two members able to supply protection or aid | Limit offensive research cooperation while keeping aid | Protection research, emergency aid, equipment cooperation | Deliver aid after a valid request | Trust loss and weaker guarantee support |
| Chemical Production and Delivery Articles | Baseline | Full, Chemical, Reservation with limits | Ordinary | At least two members with chemical or delivery relevance | Declaration and safety clause without transfer clause | Chemical research, production priority, delivery cooperation | Declarations, safety, or agreed quotas | Inspection pressure and treaty breach |
| Biological Laboratory Registry | Baseline | Full, Reservation with limits | Ordinary | At least two members with biological or containment relevance | Defensive and containment research only | Biological research, containment, laboratory cooperation | Declarations and confidence-building measures | Exposure risk and reduced research trust |
| Inspection and Verification Protocol | Baseline | Full, Chemical, Reservation | Ordinary | Valid evidence and inspection owner APIs | Chemical-only or declaration-only scope | Inspection requests, clarification, evidence publication | Site access or declarations when required | Cover-up source after proven falsification |
| Retaliation Guarantee | Baseline | Full, Chemical, Reservation | Ordinary | At least three members able to provide aid, sanctions, or planning | Assistance without automatic retaliatory use | Protection, intelligence, sanction support, retaliation planning | Fulfill valid requests | Guarantee credibility loss |
| Missile and Delivery Cooperation Accord | Evolution I | Full, Chemical, Reservation with limits | Ordinary | Valid missile, rocket, aircraft, or delivery owner system | Defensive and retaliatory delivery only | Research bonuses and owner-approved project progress | Industrial or research commitment | Lost benefit and sponsor trust |
| First-Use Revision | Evolution I | Full, Chemical, Reservation choosing repeal | Ordinary | At least two members with valid use capability | Chemical-only authorization or nuclear survival clause | First-use policy and eligible Condemnation relief | Public policy and implementation | Treaty-breach reaction for unauthorized use |
| Biological Mobilization Articles | Evolution I | Full, Reservation with strong limits | Ordinary | Valid biological research, containment, and delivery route | Defensive research and retaliation stockpile | Research, stockpile, delivery, protection priorities | Containment and declarations | Outbreak scrutiny and treaty breach |
| Strategic WMD Doctrine Charter | Evolution II | Full, Chemical for limited sections, Reservation for defensive sections | Strategic | Ratifiers collectively cover at least two capability families | Theater integration without nuclear or biological first use | Integration decisions, reserves, planning, targeting doctrine | Readiness, protection, and planning missions | Integration state loss and trust reduction |
| Nuclear Materials and Targeting Accord | Evolution II | Full, Chemical when general clauses permit, Reservation with survival clause | Strategic | At least one nuclear-capable member or two members with valid nuclear project relevance | Retaliation and national-survival use only | Research, materials, delivery, military-target doctrine | Security, protection, and public policy | Strong breach for populated civilian use |
| Allied CBRN Command Compact | Evolution II | Compatible ratifiers that are allied | Strategic | At least two allied ratifiers with usable capability | Protection and retaliation coordination only | Intelligence, reserves, protection, joint planning | Guarantee and assistance performance | Compact suspension |
| Arsenal Without Limits Articles | Evolution III | Full, Chemical for chemical sections, Reservation for defensive sections | Arsenal | One major or two capable industrial and scientific members | Defensive participation without unrestricted first use | Strongest ordinary relief, transfers, stockpile and doctrine priorities | Implementation and shared obligations | Withdrawal pressure and loss of advanced access |
| International Chaos Weapons Program Charter | Evolution III | Compatible active members | Arsenal | At least one eligible registered project and valid recipient set | Provider-specific exclusion of incompatible weapon families | Shared project access and contribution decisions | Pay project costs and maintain quorum | Program pause, repeal, or contribution loss |

## Quorum definitions

| Quorum class | Ratifier rule | Additional rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary | `max(3, ceiling(35% of eligible active members))` | Every treaty-specific capability gate must pass |
| Strategic | `max(4, ceiling(45% of eligible active members))` | Ratifiers must cover the treaty’s operational capability |
| Arsenal | `max(5, ceiling(50% of eligible active members))` | At least one valid major contributor or two valid industrial and scientific contributors |

## Agenda retry rules

| Result | Cooldown | Next eligible direction |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Adopted with strong majority | 240 to 360 days for equivalent agenda | Stronger or adjacent treaty family |
| Adopted narrowly | 180 to 300 days | Implementation review or compromise strengthening |
| Failed narrowly | 180 days | Narrower clause or renewed lobbying |
| Failed decisively | 300 to 540 days | Different family unless campaign state changes materially |
| Sponsor invalidated | 90 days | Reselect sponsor and preserve agenda eligibility |
| Capability gate lost | Until capability returns | Pause and avoid resolving a false vote |
