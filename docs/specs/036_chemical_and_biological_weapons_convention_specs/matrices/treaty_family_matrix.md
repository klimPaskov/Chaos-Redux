# Treaty family matrix

Process, quorum formulas, and family descriptions are owned by [04](../04_recurring_conventions_and_treaty_families.md).

| Family | Stage | Eligible postures | Quorum | Capability gate | Reservation clause | Main benefit | Obligation | Breach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Protection and Assistance Compact | Baseline | Full, Chemical, Reservation | Ordinary | Two members with protective equipment in stock | None | Protection research, decontamination access, guarantee | Assistance Guarantee after a ratifier is attacked | Suspension |
| Chemical Production Articles | Baseline | Full, Chemical | Ordinary | Two members with chemical technology | Declarations without production modifiers | Chemical research and production | Declaration Deadline every 365 days | Suspension |
| Biological Laboratory Registry | Baseline | Full, Reservation (containment only) | Ordinary | Two members with biological or containment technology | Containment research only | Biological research and containment | Declaration Deadline every 365 days | Suspension |
| Inspection and Verification Protocol | Baseline | Full, Chemical, Reservation | Ordinary | None beyond quorum | Chemical inspections only | Inspection demands against ratifiers | Accept inspections | Blocked-inspection cover-up source and suspension |
| First-Use Revision | Evolution I | Full (both clauses), Chemical (chemical clause) | Ordinary | Two members with a usable weapon of the clause's family | Each clause is ratified separately | Policy-compatible first use, relief stage I | None | Not applicable |
| Missile and Delivery Cooperation Accord | Evolution I | Full, Chemical, Reservation | Ordinary | A member with rocket or air-delivery technology | None | Delivery research and project progress | None | Not applicable |
| Strategic WMD Doctrine Charter | Evolution II | Full, Chemical (chemical sections), Reservation (defensive sections) | Strategic | Ratifiers together cover two capability families | Defensive sections only | Doctrine Integration, which brings relief stage II, and allied sharing | Doctrine Integration mission | Doctrine lost |
| Nuclear Materials and Targeting Accord | Evolution II | Full, Chemical, Reservation (retaliation use only) | Strategic | One nuclear-capable member or two with nuclear research | Retaliation use only | Nuclear research, nuclear relief stage II | None | Not applicable |
| Arsenal Without Limits Articles | Evolution III | Full, Chemical (chemical sections), Reservation (defensive sections) | Arsenal | Arsenal rule | Defensive sections only | Relief stage III, every use policy-compatible | None | Not applicable |
| International Chaos Weapons Program Charter | Evolution III | Full, Chemical, Reservation | Arsenal | One eligible registered project | None | Program and contributions | None | Not applicable |
