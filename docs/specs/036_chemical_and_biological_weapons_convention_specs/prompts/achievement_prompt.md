# Achievement prompt

Implement the seven achievements in [13](../13_achievements.md) through [the custom achievements system](../../../systems/custom_achievements.md), without weakening any requirement.

Event-specific points to verify:

- first use and retaliation come from the CBRN ledger and Retaliation Authority, never from a current idea or policy
- project participation and shares come from contribution receipts, never from membership
- the Small State snapshot is taken at project selection
- disqualifiers survive save and reload and never clear
- thermonuclear strikes cannot satisfy All Articles Invoked, valid retaliation does not disqualify The Reservation Holds, and non-contributors cannot receive Without the Incident

Route the seven masters and their normal, grey, and not-eligible states to `chaosx_icon_artist` through [the asset prompt](asset_prompt.md).
Write names and descriptions that explain the challenge without flags, internal values, or hidden routes.
