# Improvement-loop closure

## Playable promise reviewed

The event promises a world in which governments openly rewrite the rules of unconventional warfare.

The player must choose a national posture, influence repeated treaty rounds, decide whether first use and integrated WMD doctrine become acceptable, face persistent physical and diplomatic consequences, and participate in or oppose a late international Chaos weapon program.

## Gaps found and resolved during the planning loop

### Risk: the opening could become a single modifier choice

Resolution: every posture now changes treaty access, Condemnation treatment, research and production priorities, first-use policy, inspection exposure, AI behavior, and late project eligibility.

### Risk: recurring treaties could become repeated flavour popups

Resolution: conferences now have sponsors, agenda eligibility, negotiation periods, lobbying, reservations, scalable quorums, implementation missions, failure memory, retry rules, and capability gates.

### Risk: Condemnation relief could erase responsibility

Resolution: the design applies source-specific multipliers only after the durable CBRN action record exists and preserves deaths, contamination, evidence, attribution, atrocity, cover-up, thermonuclear, catastrophic outbreak, and extreme civilian floors.

### Risk: opposition could be an empty refusal option

Resolution: public opponents receive leadership, lobbying, inspection, evidence, sanctions, withdrawal recruitment, and convention-collapse play.

### Risk: covert rejection could become free hidden technology

Resolution: it changes access and priorities only, uses ordinary research and production, and connects discovery to shared evidence and cover-up consequences.

### Risk: the project could dominate the whole event

Resolution: it appears only after Evolution III and a successful charter vote, uses one active project, four contribution families, one progress display, dormancy, pause, cancellation, and provider-bounded access.

### Risk: a major power could complete the project alone immediately

Resolution: contribution floors help minors, per-action and per-period caps restrain majors, and all access still requires active contribution.

### Risk: project selection could be biased by strategy

Resolution: the candidate registry is deduplicated and project selection is uniformly random across every eligible unique ID.

### Risk: project completion could accidentally fire source events

Resolution: every provider needs a narrow grant adapter, immediate cancellation callback, and explicit before-and-after source-isolation proof.

### Risk: the player could be asked to track too many values

Resolution: the public surface is limited to National Posture, Convention Standing, and Current Agenda or project progress.

### Risk: the decision category could become a debug menu

Resolution: actions are phased, visible primary actions are capped, missions are capped, target management is bounded, and obsolete decisions disappear.

### Risk: a custom interface could add cost without clarity

Resolution: the accepted presentation uses normal decisions, a compact status header, and one static category picture.

## Anti-bloat review

Additional countries, focus trees, custom unit families, 3D models, portrait packages, animated presentation, terminal routes, and unrelated diplomatic systems would not improve the accepted playable loop.

The event already has distinct posture play, recurring diplomacy, three evolutions, opposition, covert preparation, doctrine, a shared project, AI strategy, achievements, assets, and cross-system integration.

Broad expansion is not recommended at specification stage.

## Closure recommendation

The design improvement loop can stop.

Implementation should proceed from the source specs.

After a meaningful implementation tranche, the implementation-specific improvement planner should inspect actual gameplay surfaces for gaps created by the code.

That later pass should write a closure handoff unless implementation evidence reveals a new broad design problem.

## Remaining work is implementation work

The remaining tasks are exact repository discovery, owner API design, code, AI probability inspection, localisation, asset production, workbook alignment, acceptance testing, and completion auditing.

They are not unresolved specification depth.
