# Evolution III: The Arsenal Without Limits

## Availability and pacing

Evolution III becomes eligible at `600+` Chaos after Evolution II is recorded and while it is enabled.

Its base MTTH is 150 days.
An active Strategic WMD Doctrine Charter, widespread first-use ratification, Dominant Standing, and a large war between members shorten it.
Divided Standing, a strong Opposition Leader, and a reservation majority lengthen it.

If Event 036 first fires at `600+` Chaos, the earlier evolutions run first on accelerated ordered schedules, and Evolution III never shares a transaction with Evolution II.

Activation changes no Chaos.

## Playable change

Most remaining restraint becomes optional among permissive ratifiers.
Members build large reserves, integrate delivery, and stop sanctioning one another, while the player decides whether to enter that system, keep a reservation, withdraw, or lead a final opposition effort.

Evolution III unlocks the Arsenal Without Limits Articles and the International Chaos Weapons Program Charter ([04](04_recurring_conventions_and_treaty_families.md#evolution-iii)).

## Member behaviour

Full Ratification ratifiers of the Arsenal receive relief stage III and the highest AI production and use priorities.
Chemical Accession ratifiers receive the chemical sections only.
Reservation members can ratify the defensive sections and stay retaliation-only.
Public opponents try to keep the Arsenal and the Charter below quorum, expose covert programs, and recruit withdrawals.

## Remaining hard limits

Even for Arsenal ratifiers:

- thermonuclear use, Final Silence, and doomsday releases receive no relief
- biological outbreaks and nuclear capital strikes keep their floors
- atrocity and cover-up sources stay fully active
- deaths, contamination, fallout, evidence, attribution, and action history stay exact
- generic Chaos from actual use stays active
- no posture or treaty makes an invalid use valid

## Opposition end state

A strong opposition can keep the Arsenal or the Charter below quorum and push Standing down through withdrawals.
It cannot erase completed research, action history, or granted project access.
A successful opposition leaves a durable divided order with competing blocs and active sanctions.

## Chaos outcomes

The owning table is [12](12_chaos_impact_and_cross_event_connections.md#event-owned-chaos).
Evolution III's own outcomes are the first Arsenal entering force (`+10`), the program Charter entering force (`+5`), and each completed project (`+10`).

## Event and log presentation

One Event 036 evolution log entry.
Event Details explains that member governments can accept unrestricted arsenals and pool their research to recreate weapons the world has not yet seen.
It never names a project before selection and never claims a source event has happened.
