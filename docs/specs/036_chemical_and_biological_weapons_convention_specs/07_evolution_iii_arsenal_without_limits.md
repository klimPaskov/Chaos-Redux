# Evolution III: The Arsenal Without Limits

## Availability and pacing

Evolution III becomes eligible at `600+` Chaos after Evolution II is active.

The base MTTH is 150 days.

The MTTH shortens when:

- strategic doctrine treaties are active
- first-use policy is widespread
- ordinary chemical use has become frequent
- several members possess biological, nuclear, missile, or advanced delivery capability
- public opposition is weak
- Convention Standing is Dominant
- members are fighting a large war

It lengthens when:

- the convention is divided
- strategic doctrine adoption is limited
- public opponents remain influential
- several members preserve retaliation-only policy
- the evolution is disabled

If Event 036 first fires at `600+` Chaos, the prior evolutions are queued in order on accelerated schedules.

Evolution III cannot activate in the same transaction as Evolution II.

Evolution activation gives zero Chaos.

## Playable change

Most remaining restraint becomes optional among permissive ratifiers.

Chemical warfare becomes a routine military tool when it is useful.

Biological first use becomes common among governments willing to accept outbreak risk.

Nuclear first use becomes easier against strategic targets.

Member governments build large reserves, integrate delivery systems, and reduce sanctions against one another.

The player chooses whether to enter that system fully, retain a reservation, withdraw, or organize a final opposition effort.

## Arsenal Without Limits Articles

Evolution III unlocks the Arsenal Without Limits treaty family.

The treaty can include:

- unrestricted chemical production and transfer among ratifiers
- broad biological stockpile and delivery preparation
- permissive first-use policy
- integrated missile and nuclear research
- weaker member-on-member sanctions
- large reserve targets
- expanded protection and decontamination requirements
- shared strategic planning

Ratification does not grant completed weapons.

It changes access, policy, cooperation, and AI behavior.

A country without relevant physical capabilities remains unable to use them.

## Remaining hard limits

Even at this stage:

- thermonuclear actions receive no convention Condemnation relief
- catastrophic biological outbreaks retain their severity floor
- extreme civilian destruction retains its severity floor
- atrocity and cover-up sources remain fully active
- physical casualties and population loss remain exact
- contamination and fallout remain active
- evidence and attribution remain active
- action history remains complete
- generic Chaos from actual use remains active

The evolution makes permissive policy powerful.

It does not make consequences disappear.

## International Chaos Weapons Program Charter

Evolution III unlocks the program charter when the registered project pool has at least one eligible project.

The charter requires the strongest ratification quorum and the capability gate defined in the recurring convention spec.

Ratifying the charter creates the program coordinator state, builds the eligible pool, and selects one project at equal probability.

The program then follows `08_international_chaos_weapons_program.md`.

## Member behavior

Full Ratification members that accept the Arsenal Without Limits Articles receive the strongest ordinary Condemnation relief and the highest research, production, stockpile, delivery, and use priorities.

Chemical Accession members can accept the chemical and general delivery sections but remain outside biological unlocks.

Reservation members can remain inside protection, inspection, retaliation, and defensive research agreements while refusing unrestricted first use.

Public opponents can attempt to break the ratification quorum, expose covert programs, sanction members, and recruit withdrawals.

## Routine use and AI limits

Chemical use may become routine when:

- a valid target exists
- a physical payload exists
- delivery and readiness are valid
- military benefit is meaningful
- friendly-force and self-contamination risk are acceptable

Biological use may become common, but the AI must still evaluate outbreak control, blowback, civilian spread, friendly borders, containment, and retaliation.

Nuclear use may become easier against high-value targets, but the AI must still evaluate payload scarcity, fallout, target value, civilian impact, retaliation risk, and campaign need.

No posture or treaty can turn an invalid use action into a valid one.

## Opposition end state

A strong opposition campaign can keep the convention below the Arsenal or Program quorum.

It can also reduce Dominant standing to Divided or Provisional through public withdrawals.

It cannot erase already completed research, action history, or weapon access.

A successful opposition outcome should leave a durable divided international order with strong bilateral sanctions and competing treaty blocs.

## Concrete Chaos outcomes

Evolution III activation gives zero Chaos.

The first Arsenal Without Limits treaty entering force adds `+10` Chaos once.

The International Chaos Weapons Program Charter entering force adds `+5` Chaos once.

Each completed registered project adds `+10` Chaos once.

Project cancellation adds zero Chaos.

A convention collapse caused by broad public withdrawal removes `5` Chaos once per genuine collapse episode, with an anti-farming generation gate.

Actual weapon use remains covered by shared generic sources.

## Event and log presentation

The evolution receives one Event 036 evolution log entry.

Event Details should explain that member states can accept unrestricted arsenals and begin reproducing previously unavailable Chaos weapons through an international research program.

It must not name a project until the equal-random selection has occurred.

It must not claim that source events have fired.
