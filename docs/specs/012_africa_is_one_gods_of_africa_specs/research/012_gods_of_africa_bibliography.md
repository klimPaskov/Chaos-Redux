## Religious plurality

### Rice University Boniuk Institute review of Religious Plurality in Africa

Source:

https://boniuk.rice.edu/religious-plurality-africa

Relevant themes:

- rejection of a single flattened category
- equal attention to Islam, Christianity, and African religious traditions
- plurality, historical depth, mixed practice, and local difference

Use in design:

- fictional doctrine requirement
- no universal historical pantheon
- no unrelated sacred-symbol collage
- multiple internal interpretations

### Religious Plurality in Africa: Coexistence, Conviviality, Conflict

Publisher page:

https://boydellandbrewer.com/book/religious-plurality-in-africa-9781847014108/

Editors:

- Marloes Janson
- Kai Kresse
- Benedikt Pontzen
- Hassan A. Mwakimako

Relevant themes:

- interaction between Christianity, Islam, and African religious traditions
- regional case studies
- local coexistence, competition, and cross-over

Use in design:

- cultural review direction
- varied local interpretation
- rejection of a monolithic religious identity
