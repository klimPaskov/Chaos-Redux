# Gods of Africa research notes

## Research purpose

The research was used to strengthen three parts of the design:

- continental political identity
- foreign policy and tribute logic
- cultural safeguards around religion and visual language

The system remains fictional. Research provides institutional and cultural anchors. It does not convert modern African Union structures into literal 1930s gameplay history.

## Pan-African unity and continental agency

The African Union describes the OAU as an expression of a Pan-African vision for a united and free Africa in control of its destiny. Its stated historical aims include unity, solidarity, sovereignty, territorial integrity, decolonization, cooperation, and coordinated common positions.

Design implication:

- Gods of Africa should emerge from political unification and continental self-determination
- foreign demands should focus strongly on occupation, bases, sovereignty, common positions, and material capacity
- Africa should act as an institution with its own needs and strategy
- the system should not read as a random magical curse detached from state power

## Integration, infrastructure, and common policy

Agenda 2063 links Pan-African unity with political and economic independence, trade, free movement, rail, road, sea, air, power pools, decolonization, peace, security, cultural identity, and a stronger global role.

The African Union's own overview also lists common policies on trade, defense, and foreign relations among its aims.

Design implication:

- valid tribute families should include infrastructure, trains, convoys, fuel, industry, trade, access, and security support
- Africa's current need profile should matter
- final friendship can include trade, alliance, protection, and shared infrastructure
- Event 012's liberation and foreign-base systems should be central to Wrath
- a dominant Africa should gain diplomatic leverage before it gains catastrophic punishment

## Peace, conflict prevention, and self-financing

Agenda 2063 presents peace and security, conflict prevention, operational continental security structures, and financing Africa's own security needs as linked goals.

Design implication:

- emergency wartime demands can be framed as continental security support
- compliant partners can receive reciprocal protection
- the African unifier should pay real costs when it protects another country
- the system should have a cooperative doctrine route alongside its extractive route

## Peer review and remembered conduct

The African Peer Review Mechanism describes itself as a mutually agreed voluntary African self-monitoring mechanism focused on governance at local, national, and continental levels.

Design implication:

- the hidden relationship record can be understood as an institutional memory of conduct
- public Wrath can track present anger while hidden history tracks long-term reliability
- final outcomes should use the full history and should not be solved by one payment

The gameplay system is coercive and therefore differs sharply from voluntary peer review. The research supports the idea of persistent remembered conduct, not the tribute mechanism itself.

## Religious plurality

Recent scholarship on religious plurality in Africa argues against flattening Christianity, Islam, and African religious traditions into one mass. It emphasizes diverse traditions, mixed practice, local histories, and changing boundaries.

Design implication:

- the Gods must be a newly created fictional state doctrine
- final text should avoid claiming an ancient universal African religion
- visual art should avoid a collage of unrelated sacred symbols
- different African communities can interpret the doctrine differently
- public ambiguity can include literal belief, political myth, strategic theatre, and Chaos manifestation

## Respect for religious diversity

Agenda 2063 explicitly connects cultural identity and common heritage with respect for religious diversity.

Design implication:

- the unifier may claim a common continental voice without erasing local faiths
- the Gods' doctrine can become a contested political project inside Africa
- the reciprocal doctrine route fits an inclusive continental identity
- the extractive route should create internal and foreign political costs

## Visual research direction

Useful visual anchors include:

- continental assemblies and conference chambers
- period radio and communications systems
- regional architecture chosen for a specific location
- rail, port, industrial, and military logistics
- state seals and commissioned monumental art

The asset package should not merge buildings from Ethiopia, Mali, Zimbabwe, Egypt, and other regions into one generic sacred city. A scene can use a specific regional location when the story names it. A continental institution should have a newly designed visual language.

## Historical period adaptation

Most modern institutional sources postdate Hearts of Iron IV's starting period. They are useful as design parallels for:

- continental integration
- common positions
- self-financing
- infrastructure
- peace and security
- sovereignty
- decolonization
- cultural plurality

Final gameplay should adapt these themes into the alternate-history Event 012 campaign. It should not name modern programs or copy modern organizational branding unless Event 012 deliberately moves into a later timeless phase.

## Critical design conclusions

### Conclusion 1

The concept is strongest as a state-backed doctrine with uncertain supernatural force. Pure magic would make Strength arbitrary. Pure propaganda would weaken the intended high-Chaos consequences.

### Conclusion 2

The two public values are sufficient. Strength represents capability. Wrath represents personal hostility. Long-term standing can remain hidden and communicated through conduct.

### Conclusion 3

Material demands need real African needs and protected target capacity. A long hardcoded list would become repetitive and would produce absurd requests.

### Conclusion 4

Reciprocity is required. A country that funds Africa for years needs protection and a large final settlement.

### Conclusion 5

Cultural specificity should come from particular Event 012 states, leaders, institutions, and local scenes. It should not come from a universalized religious collage.

## Research limits

The live Event 012 implementation and source documentation were not available in the active environment. This research therefore informs design direction only.

No final quote, cultural remark, track, sacred symbol, or historical image was selected. Those choices require the dedicated super-event and asset research workflow during implementation.
