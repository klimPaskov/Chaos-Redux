## Route behavior

Reciprocal Covenant should improve negotiation, protection, emergency aid memory, and final friendship while reducing immediate extraction.

Sovereign Exaction should increase tribute, offender pressure, sanctions, and retaliation while creating more defiance and isolation.

Provision should improve need matching, arsenals, fuel, transport, rail, ports, and reserves.

Oaths should unlock recognition, territory return, access, ending enemy support, and later security arrangements.

Protection should unlock real aid, reconstruction, expeditionary support, and Favored settlement tools.

Judgment should improve offense proof, sanctions, punishment variety, intervention against occupiers, and the full-gate Tier V policy. No focus may bypass the Strength ceiling.
