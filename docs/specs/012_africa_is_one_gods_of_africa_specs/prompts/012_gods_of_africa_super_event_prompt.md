# Super-event research and implementation prompt

Research and implement one Gods of Africa credibility super-event under Event `012` if the live Event 012 presentation audit confirms that the campaign threshold deserves it.

## Role

The moment marks the first time foreign powers accept that Africa's demands have global reach.

Trigger direction:

- Gods of Africa active
- African unifier valid
- Strength enters the accepted global-reach band
- one-time proof absent
- Event 012 campaign active

Weak activation uses normal news and waits for this threshold.

## Text research

Route exact quote and cultural-line research to `chaosx_super_event_text_researcher`.

Find several verifiable candidates connected to:

- Pan-African unity
- sovereignty
- anti-colonial power
- responsibility
- judgment
- continental voice

Prefer historical political writing, public-domain literature, or another traceable source.

Do not invent a proverb, misattribute a quote, or present a generic line as African wisdom.

Final title should be short and specific. Final description should show a shift in world conduct without listing mechanic effects. Final button text can use one brief researched allusion.

## Image

Route the image to `chaosx_generated_event_art`.

Show the African unifier as the source of coordinated political authority. Use one coherent fictional institution and one restrained impossible sign. Avoid giant fantasy deities, a burning globe, map arrows, modern conference equipment, or copied modern AU branding.

## Audio

Route audio to `chaosx_super_event_audio_researcher`.

Select one unique licensed musical recording with ceremonial, political, and unsettling structure.

Reject:

- generic tribal loops
- generated audio
- test tones
- drones
- ambient beds
- one-shot sound effects
- unclear recording rights

Check composition and recording rights separately. Preserve the source and prepare a game-ready WAV.

## Wiring

Complete:

- intentional super-event slot
- title, description, button, and quote keys
- image sprite and scripted localisation
- unique audio ID
- base sound definition
- required settings-volume wrappers
- `global.current_super_event_audio_id`
- `play_current_super_event_sound = yes`
- event trigger
- docs
- permanent source and license record
- `music/chaosx_music_track_list.html`
- authoritative workbook alignment

Do not reuse another super-event's audio or leave placeholder art.
