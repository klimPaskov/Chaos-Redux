# Gods of Africa focus integration specification

## Tree ownership

Gods of Africa uses an overlay branch inside the existing Event `012` African unifier focus tree.

It does not receive a separate national focus tree. The branch becomes visible only after Africa Is One Evolution I and the consolidation milestone make the doctrine relevant.

The branch should occupy reserved space near Event 012 diplomacy, continental authority, or late political consolidation content. It should not cross unrelated military, industry, or liberation connectors.

## Branch promise

The branch decides how the African unifier turns a supernatural claim into a foreign policy system.

Its focuses should change:

- demand family access
- demand severity and patience
- negotiation
- protection
- punishment method
- African priority selection
- foreign alignment
- Event 012 sanctions and liberation routes
- final relationship outcomes

A flat Strength increase can support a focus reward. It cannot be the main reward for most of the branch.

## Visibility and pacing

### Reveal condition

The branch is hidden before:

- Event 012 Evolution I
- the African unifier exists
- the consolidation delay has begun or completed

The first anchor can become visible shortly before activation if it represents institutional preparation. The foreign demand actions remain locked until activation.

### Focus duration direction

- `35` days for doctrine choices, emergency policy, or a focused diplomatic shift
- `70` days for institution building, major logistics programs, protection structures, and punishment doctrine
- longer durations only for a continental commitment that produces a major payoff

Do not split one policy into several short focuses without a new action, event, or choice between them.

## Opening anchors

### Working anchor: Prepare the Continental Proclamation

Narrative role:

- Africa decides that continental unity requires a common foreign voice

Mechanical role:

- starts or confirms the consolidation mission
- opens doctrine preparation decisions
- creates the owner-side institution carrier if Event 012 has no suitable existing institution
- reveals the Gods branch

Reward direction:

- one concrete institution effect
- one decision unlock
- no immediate foreign demands before the activation gate

### Working anchor: Establish the Office of the Voices

Narrative role:

- creates the body that records demands, interprets the Gods, and reports foreign conduct

Mechanical role:

- unlocks African priority selection
- prepares participant registry and reporting
- creates the doctrine choice event
- may improve hidden demand matching

Reward direction:

- owner helper unlock
- grouped foreign report access
- small supporting diplomatic or intelligence effect

### Working anchor: The Continental Proclamation

Narrative role:

- activates the system after all conditions are met

Mechanical role:

- calls the Event 012 activation helper
- registers participants
- records the milestone
- opens the participant categories
- triggers the proclamation report

Bypass:

- completed automatically if the system was already activated by a valid Event 012 path

## Doctrine fork

### Reciprocal Covenant

Narrative role:

- tribute is framed as a mutual compact for African security and world survival

Mechanical identity:

- lower average demand severity
- longer deadlines
- stronger substitution and extension acceptance
- larger hidden friendship gains from emergency aid
- stronger protection decisions
- stronger Favored settlement rewards
- lower Africa AI willingness to destroy useful cooperative partners

Tradeoff:

- slower immediate African stockpile growth
- fewer punitive shortcuts
- severe offenders may exploit patience until later judgment tools unlock

Decision integration:

- leniency
- reciprocal defense requests
- protection of reliable partners
- reconstruction assistance
- cooperative final settlements

AI preference:

- stable, diplomatic, trade-oriented, or coalition-building African governments
- unifier with several supportive foreign partners
- campaigns where continental control is secure enough to favor long-term diplomacy

### Sovereign Exaction

Narrative role:

- tribute is declared an owed payment for exploitation, occupation, and interference

Mechanical identity:

- larger demand bands
- shorter deadlines
- stronger priority-offender selection
- faster material gain
- higher Wrath gains from refusal and broken promises
- stronger sanctions and retaliation
- easier access to punitive demand families

Tradeoff:

- more permanent defiance
- weaker friendship growth
- greater diplomatic isolation
- higher condemnation risk from direct punishment
- more hostile final settlements

Decision integration:

- punitive tribute
- restitution demands
- offender targeting
- sanctions
- military preparation against defiant powers

AI preference:

- Africa still fighting occupiers
- several foreign bases remain
- repeated foreign refusals
- militarized or authoritarian Event 012 routes
- high Chaos and high external threat

### Fork rule

The two doctrines are mutually exclusive.

Changing doctrine later should require a major crisis or route event. It should not be a cheap reversible policy decision.

## Provision branch

This branch turns tribute into usable continental capability.

### Working focus group: Measure the Needs of the Continent

Unlocks:

- improved hidden African need profile
- priority categories
- surplus checks
- less repeated demand waste

Gameplay payoff:

- demands more often match actual equipment, fuel, transport, and repair deficits

### Working focus group: Continental Arsenals

Unlocks:

- land equipment demand variants
- storage and distribution support
- route-specific military production integration

Map and production effects:

- improve selected Event 012 arsenal or supply states
- connect tribute to real production or stockpiles

### Working focus group: Rails, Ports, and Fuel

Evolution II gate.

Unlocks:

- train, convoy, fuel, railway, port, and infrastructure support demands
- industrial reconstruction contracts
- protection through transport support

Map effects:

- named Event 012 corridor projects
- repair or construction decisions

### Working focus group: The Continental Reserve

Payoff:

- allows Africa to hold a portion of tribute as a strategic reserve
- improves emergency protection and war response
- reduces waste from receiving material beyond immediate need

Tradeoff:

- tying up stockpiles can reduce current field use

## Oaths branch

This branch governs recognition, access, agreements, and foreign alignment.

### Working focus group: Recognize the Continental Voice

Unlocks:

- recognition demands
- diplomatic status tracking
- improved final settlement participation

### Working focus group: The Return of African Soil

Unlocks:

- precise territory-return demands
- withdrawal timetables
- release and transfer settlements
- stronger hidden credit for voluntary return

Requirements:

- Event 012 authoritative African core and occupation registry

### Working focus group: End Foreign Sponsorship

Unlocks:

- demands to end aid, access, guarantees, or recognition for African enemies
- proof and verification tools

### Working focus group: Continental Security Oaths

Evolution III gate.

Unlocks:

- non-aggression, guarantee, faction, and access demands
- security arrangements with Favored and Respected countries
- sanctions against oath-breakers

Tradeoff:

- Africa becomes responsible for protecting countries that enter formal arrangements

## Protection branch

This branch gives compliance a strategic return.

### Working focus group: Shelter the Faithful

Unlocks:

- protection target selection
- equipment and fuel aid
- demand leniency for genuine hardship

### Working focus group: Relief Beyond the Continent

Unlocks:

- Event 013 disaster mitigation or reconstruction support
- famine and migration aid through owner APIs
- convoy and transport relief

Requirements:

- valid partner history and real African capacity

### Working focus group: The African Expedition

Unlocks:

- expeditionary force or volunteer assistance for highly reliable partners
- military advisers or training support where valid

Tradeoff:

- consumes African units, equipment, and supply

### Working focus group: A Place at the Final Council

Payoff:

- improves Favored and Respected final settlement packages
- connects to World Is One participation

## Judgment branch

The branch governs punishment and must remain subordinate to Strength.

### Working focus group: Record Every Refusal

Unlocks:

- improved offense memory
- broken-agreement proof
- priority-offender selection
- grouped reports

It does not raise punishment tier by itself.

### Working focus group: Reach Beyond the Coast

Unlocks:

- stronger covert, diplomatic, and logistical punishment families
- broader target reach when African capability supports it

### Working focus group: Let the World Answer

Unlocks:

- sanctions
- coalition pressure
- support for enemies of a defiant participant
- diplomatic punishment that can work without supernatural force

### Working focus group: The Hand of Judgment

Evolution II or III gate depending severity.

Unlocks:

- coordinated punishment evaluation
- higher punishment-family variety
- one-time credible-power presentation threshold

Hard rule:

- the focus cannot raise a punishment above the current Strength ceiling

### Working focus group: No Sanctuary for Occupiers

Evolution III gate.

Unlocks:

- stronger action against countries holding African core territory
- intervention preparation
- war goals or liberation support where Event 012 permits

### Working focus group: The Last Sentence

Rare high-Chaos route.

Unlocks:

- Tier V emergency ultimatum sequence only

Requirements:

- extreme Strength
- very high Wrath target
- high Chaos
- serious offense history
- Event 012 Evolution III

The focus does not trigger the sequence immediately and does not bypass target cooldowns.

## Revelation policy

A secondary pair of mutually exclusive focuses may define how the state handles ambiguity.

### Sacred State

Direction:

- leadership publicly and internally treats the Gods as literal sovereign powers

Effects:

- stronger high-Chaos manifestation weights
- stronger domestic identity effects
- higher risk from internal fracture if punishments repeatedly fail
- weaker foreign negotiation with secular powers

### Veiled Instrument

Direction:

- leadership preserves the public claim while keeping state institutions and methods deliberately ambiguous

Effects:

- stronger bluff, intelligence, and diplomatic families
- better attribution control
- lower risk from failed manifestations
- weaker access to the strangest high-Chaos consequences

This secondary fork is optional. Add it only if Event 012's existing political routes can support it without clutter.

## Evolution II expansion

When Event 012 reaches Evolution II, reveal or unlock:

- heavy equipment and aircraft demands
- continental reserve
- railway and port commitments
- reconstruction contracts
- larger protection packages
- improved emergency requests
- stronger Tier III and Tier IV access when Strength qualifies

The branch should visibly expand at this milestone through new nodes or changed availability. It should not reveal every later focus from the start.

## Evolution III expansion

When Event 012 reaches Evolution III, reveal or unlock:

- alliance and faction concessions
- foreign-base removal
- sanctions
- intervention against occupiers
- reconciliation route
- Tier IV and Tier V policy gates
- World Is One relationship integration

## Continental settlement capstone

### Working focus: Africa Speaks for Itself

Narrative role:

- Africa has secured the continent and can replace recurring tribute with permanent relations

Requirements:

- authoritative Event 012 secured-continent proof
- Gods system active or historically activated

Effects:

- stops new demands
- commits final evaluation
- issues Favored, Respected, Distrusted, and Enemy settlements
- converts selected temporary systems into lasting diplomacy
- connects eligible countries to World Is One

The focus can also be an auto-completing Event 012 milestone if the existing tree already owns the final settlement elsewhere. Do not duplicate the victory endpoint.

## Branch interaction

### With Event 012 liberation

- liberation victories increase Strength through continent control
- foreign occupiers become priority offenders
- territory-return demands can resolve some conflicts without war

### With Event 012 industry

- continental infrastructure creates demand use and protection capacity
- tribute feeds named projects
- industrial collapse lowers Strength

### With Event 012 military

- army, air, navy, and supply capability affect Strength
- expeditionary aid needs actual units and stockpiles
- punitive war routes require military readiness

### With Event 012 diplomacy

- Charter and congress outcomes alter doctrine behavior
- guarantees and recognition become demand and reward families
- rival blocs reduce Strength or change target selection

### With Event 012 foreign bases

- bases create Wrath and withdrawal demands
- voluntary withdrawal creates strong hidden positive history
- refusal can unlock intervention

## Focus reward diversity

The branch should use:

- decision unlocks
- mission families
- demand-family access
- dynamic modifier changes
- named infrastructure projects
- equipment and reserve mechanics
- diplomatic actions
- protection actions
- AI strategy changes
- final settlement access
- one or two staged ideas with a clear lifecycle

Avoid:

- repeated political power grants
- repeated small stability changes
- a new national spirit from every focus
- direct free Strength increases as the main reward
- equipment reward dumps without a lasting system change

## Idea lifecycle

| Idea | Start or unlock | Role | Upgrade route | Failure route | Final state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Office of the Voices | institutional setup | manages demands and reports | improved need matching and diplomacy | credibility loss after repeated failed manifestations | absorbed into final continental institution |
| Reciprocal Covenant | doctrine fork | cooperative tribute and protection | stronger alliance and relief tools | weakened by repeated betrayal | final friendship institution |
| Sovereign Exaction | doctrine fork | extraction and retaliation | sanctions and offender routes | isolation and mass defiance | coercive continental order |
| Continental Reserve | Provision branch | stores tribute for emergencies | larger protection and war response | hoarding or distribution crisis | strategic reserve or dissolved into final settlement |

Final idea names and exact modifiers require implementation review.

## AI route plans

### Cooperative continental government

Priorities:

- Reciprocal Covenant
- Provision
- Protection
- Oaths
- restrained Judgment

### Liberation government under foreign pressure

Priorities:

- Sovereign Exaction
- Return of African Soil
- Continental Arsenals
- No Sanctuary for Occupiers

### Africa losing a major war

Priorities:

- Provision emergency nodes
- Continental Reserve
- emergency aid requests
- selective leniency toward useful partners

### High-Chaos dominant Africa

Priorities:

- Judgment and global leverage
- protection of proven allies
- Tier V policy only against severe offenders

### AI blockers

AI should not choose:

- punishment nodes when Strength is permanently low and no capability route exists
- expeditionary aid when Africa cannot spare units
- heavy-equipment demand expansion without valid equipment access
- foreign-base retaliation when no hostile base exists
- final settlement before Event 012 victory proof

Every weighted route choice requires a probability audit.

## Layout standard

The implementation should:

- reserve one compact lane for Gods content
- keep doctrine fork symmetrical
- keep Provision, Oaths, Protection, and Judgment visually distinct
- reveal Evolution II and III nodes only when relevant
- use short prerequisite lines
- avoid crossing Event 012's main liberation and industry connectors
- add a Focus Navigation entry only if the branch is spatially separate enough to need one
- give each focus accurate search filters

A focus inlay window is unnecessary under the default presentation. The two public values belong in the decision layer.

## Focus audit requirements

Before completion:

- inspect and render the full Event 012 tree
- identify the Gods branch from normal zoom without source annotations
- confirm doctrine fork and functional branches are readable
- confirm no connector overlap
- test hidden and revealed states
- confirm all decision unlocks and route changes
- run route-specific AI probability review
- compare required route groups with implemented focus groups
