## Authority and accepted design additions

The current user brief fixes event identity, all-country occupation coverage, the 25-point baseline, the three evolution amounts and thresholds, future-only evolution effects, repetition, and the named owner connections. Those requirements override older catalog text and generic scaling suggestions.

The following details were selected by the parent within the user's authorization to expand the idea thoroughly. They are explicit design decisions, not quotations from the user and not claims about existing runtime behavior.

| Decision | Accepted result | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evolution persistence after falling Chaos | Granted stages persist, highest enabled granted stage is active | Gives a clear campaign evolution contract without reversing prior history |
| Exact threshold entry | Immediate stage grant or pre-fire catchup, with no MTTH wait and no wave on grant | Fits this intensity-only event and preserves the user's stated future amounts |
| Saturated state history | Zero actual addition creates no new support or refresh | Prevents capped repeated firings from inventing stronger armies |
| Local memory | Three strongest remaining unspent receipts at admission, each fading over 365 days | Makes later 095 firings meaningful with fixed storage and prevents a tiny top-up from replacing stronger support |
| Candidate support | Current-population weighted over all canonically assigned valid occupied homeland states, with unusable history scored zero | Preserves locality and prevents expiry from raising an average by shrinking its denominator |
| Additional uprisings | Alternative 2, 4, 6, and 8-movement bands at support 0.10, 0.35, 0.60, and 0.85 | Lets a world-scale wave yield many distinct local outcomes while keeping a clear ceiling |
| Local force uplift | Up to 50% of each affected state's ordinary 095 contribution | Strengthens the places actually affected without multiplying an entire country's army from one state |
| Chaos | Actual-addition bands of +2, +3, and +5, a 90-day gate, and +30 gross lifetime ceiling | Rewards real new instability without a repeatable farm |
| Containment | Same-state weighted 75% intersection at days 180 and 270, finite records, up to half the credited gain returned, -10 lifetime ceiling | Gives actual recovery a measurable counterpart with no player-managed currency |
| Achievements | Existing human occupier containment and existing human homeland recovery | Provides two difficult routes without relying on an unverified switch into a newly created tag |
| Art | One generated fictional documentary report image and two achievement triplets | Fits the actual report and achievement consumers |
| 095 outcome proof | Freeze validated local provenance before outcome mutations and publish it after physical success | Its own restoration callbacks must not erase the achievement link or reattach consumption to a new episode |
| Same-date observations | Due proof checks and settlement precede stage resolution and a new wave at that exact boundary | Produces one result independent of callback arrival order, with no later reconstruction of a missing checkpoint |
| Homeland terminal failure | Loss of independence or the eligible human route permanently closes the campaign attempt | Returning independence or player control cannot reuse old evidence through a subject shortcut |
| Lifetime credit identities | Allow up to 30 positive integer credits, separately from the four pending containment records | Does not assume every positive shared-meter request is accepted in full |

These secondary calibration values can be tuned through later evidence. The user's fixed wave values and thresholds are not tuning substitutions.

## Review and verification disposition

The initial architect proposal was promoted with a 365-day memory, the strongest-three admission rule, exact local force formula, fixed canonical allocation, and the corrected candidate denominator. Its original two-extra-movement cap was superseded by the reviewed 2/4/6/8 band design.

The initial improvement proposal was promoted with the documented Chaos, finite containment, and two achievement rules. The homeland challenge was amended to require two own hostile recaptures so a minimum three-state cohort can include one genuine 095 restoration without encouraging deliberate loss and recapture. All initial proposal items have a disposition before the final improvement pass.

The completion auditor's 48-scenario preparation was folded into Part 6 and expanded for the selected formulas, cohorts, and corrected identity routing. The separate Event Logs check resolved the text consumer and removed an unsupported conditional icon requirement.

The mandatory final improvement pass selected the same-date observation order and terminal homeland-failure rule. The final architect review selected the frozen pre-mutation 095 proof and conservative lifetime credit count. These four closure amendments are explicit in the canonical mechanics, prompts, and acceptance scenarios. They add no new player-facing system or asset.
