# 106 Resistance specification package

Event 106 Resistance is a Minor Repeatable event at Chaos level 1, in Domestic Unrest with Low severity. One firing raises current resistance in every valid occupied state under every country. It adds 25 percentage points at baseline, then 50, 75, or 100 through the user's three evolutions, always capped at 100%.

This package contains the complete expanded design and implementation handoffs. The game catalog status remains **To Be Reworked**. The package does not claim that gameplay, art, localisation, achievements, or catalogs have been implemented.

## What the expansion adds

Resistance remains an immediate native occupation shock. Its main depth comes from local consequences and later events. A bounded state record carries genuine recent positive waves into 095. The 095 rework can then support additional distinct movements through four bands, up to eight extras in one firing, and can increase each affected state's own force contribution by up to 50%.

The candidate calculation includes zero-support territory in its fixed geographical denominator. Aging, consumption, and failed validation cannot make an occupation look more ready merely by removing weak inputs. Every additional physical release remains limited to its reserved usable affected states.

Real new resistance supplies +2, +3, or +5 Chaos according to actual additions, with a 90-day gate and +30 gross lifetime ceiling. A finite credited footprint can return a limited amount after recovery at two stated checkpoints. Two achievements reward severe occupation recovery and the existing country's active recovery of an occupied homeland after a genuine local 095 result.

The native occupation screen remains the player's management surface. Final art comprises one report texture and six achievement texture states. Finished wording remains an implementation task, with full direction in Part 5.

## Reading and implementation limits

The parent and the three provided specialist roles fully processed all supplied sources before design: 90 distinct text versions, 132 supplied copies including archive contents, and 2,594,283 original distinct-text bytes. Older versions were fully accounted for through exact verified equal spans and complete reading of changed spans. No supplied source remained unread, and no source-content truncation remained unresolved.

Repository research was targeted and pinned. It found legacy 095 behavior and several missing 106 shared mappings. The exact zero-resistance adapter, full-map same-tick coverage, native uprising behavior, movement resolution, continuity hooks, 095 force adapter, and final runtime assets still require implementation evidence.

No HOI4 MCP, installed-game, save, or multiplayer test was run in this planning task. The numerical fixtures verify the specified mathematics, and the reviews verify the planning package. They do not replace those later proofs.
