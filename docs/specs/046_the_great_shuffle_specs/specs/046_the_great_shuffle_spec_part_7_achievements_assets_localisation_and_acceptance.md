# Event 046: Achievements, assets, localisation, and acceptance

## Achievement role

The Great Shuffle supports achievements because its repeatability creates rare recovery stories and world states that ordinary one-time objectives cannot produce.

Achievements must track adaptation, reversal, and survival.

They must not unlock merely because Event 46 fired.

All tracking state is protected from the Shuffle.

Force-trigger, debug, validation, and manual test routes disqualify achievement progress when the project has a detectable test marker.

Normal automatic cluster firing remains eligible because it is ordinary campaign play.

The working IDs below are implementation identifiers.

Player-facing titles remain direction only until final localisation.

## Achievement 1: repeated survivor

- Working ID: `chaosx_046_shuffle_veteran`
- Visibility: visible
- Difficulty: high
- Eligible scope: a human-controlled ordinary country present for every counted firing
- Core condition: survive five distinct successful Great Shuffles on the same persistent country scope
- Completion condition: remain independent, existing, and uncapitulated for 365 days after the fifth firing
- Disqualifiers: Event 46 force mode, Event 46 debug setup, country tag switching used to change the controlled country, or loss of the tracked country identity
- Why it is not trivial: ordinary repeatable cap decay makes five natural firings rare, while later firings can erase earlier gains
- Title direction: endurance through repeated revisions of reality
- Description direction: emphasize one government surviving several incompatible versions of its own country
- Icon direction: a battered globe or national seal assembled from five misaligned layers, readable at 64x64

Cosmetic tag changes and ordinary government changes can remain eligible when the underlying country scope persists.

## Achievement 2: recovery from empty stores

- Working ID: `chaosx_046_from_empty_depots`
- Visibility: visible
- Difficulty: very high
- Eligible capability: Evolution I or later
- Opening proof: one natural firing reduces the player's normalized ordinary equipment-stockpile value by at least 60 percent, reserve manpower by at least 50 percent, and fuel by at least 70 percent
- Recovery window: 730 days
- Completion condition: restore fuel to at least 75 percent of current capacity, reach at least 95 percent equipment fulfillment across 24 deployed ordinary divisions, and win a war after the qualifying firing
- Disqualifiers: deleting and recreating the tracked army solely to evade fulfillment, force-triggered Event 46, or changing the player country
- Why it is not trivial: the player must rebuild several different military resources after one combined shock and prove the recovery in war
- Title direction: depots rebuilt from near-total scarcity
- Description direction: focus on practical military recovery and omit the raw percentages
- Icon direction: an empty warehouse opening onto newly stacked crates, fuel drums, and a restored rail line

The normalized stockpile value uses a documented equipment-family score so a large pile of cheap tokens cannot replace every important loss.

## Achievement 3: impossible geography

- Working ID: `chaosx_046_impossible_geography`
- Visibility: visible
- Difficulty: high
- Eligible capability: Evolution II or later
- Opening proof: one natural firing gives the player one controlled state in the top world industry decile and bottom population quartile, plus another controlled state in the top population decile and bottom industry quartile
- Completion condition: keep both states owned or controlled, connected to supply, and outside enemy occupation for 365 days
- Disqualifiers: losing either state, changing the player country, or using an Event 46 test route
- Why it is not trivial: the exact crossed ranking is rare and the player must sustain two strategically mismatched regions
- Title direction: a country holding two states whose people and factories no longer belong together
- Description direction: describe the contrast through the lived map and omit statistical terms
- Icon direction: a crowded city silhouette paired with an empty industrial skyline, divided by a broken survey line

Ranking is calculated once from the committed transaction and stored as protected achievement proof.

Later ordinary development does not erase the opening qualification.

## Achievement 4: government rebuilt

- Working ID: `chaosx_046_government_rebuilt`
- Visibility: visible
- Difficulty: very high
- Eligible capability: Evolution III or later
- Opening proof: after one natural firing, Stability is at or below 10 percent, War Support is at or below 10 percent, ruling-party support is below 20 percent, and at least two ordinary law categories changed
- Recovery window: 540 days
- Completion condition: reach at least 70 percent Stability, 70 percent War Support, and 50 percent ruling-party support while remaining the same country and avoiding capitulation
- Disqualifiers: force-triggered Event 46, changing player country, or owner systems that make the ordinary politics proof invalid
- Why it is not trivial: the player must rebuild several independent political systems from a severe combined result
- Title direction: institutions reconstructed after political memory failed
- Description direction: emphasize governing a country whose laws and public loyalties changed together
- Icon direction: a cracked parliament or cabinet chamber restored with mismatched symbols and a firm central light

## Achievement 5: maximum transaction

- Working ID: `chaosx_046_world_redealt`
- Visibility: hidden until Evolution V becomes available
- Difficulty: extreme
- Eligible capability: Evolution V
- Opening proof: one natural firing commits at least one family from every available capability band and at least 90 percent of the complete eligible safe family pool
- Completion condition: remain an existing independent country for 180 days after the transaction
- Disqualifiers: any force or debug path, an aborted transaction, or a transaction that hid a missing core family instead of reporting it
- Why it is not trivial: it requires World Collapse reachability, broad registry health, rare event selection, and survival after the largest possible rewrite
- Title direction: the whole playable world dealt again
- Description direction: focus on living through the maximum proven Shuffle and avoid claiming every protected system changed
- Icon direction: a globe, factory, soldier, and government seal caught in one forceful circular rearrangement

## Achievement 6: three reversals

- Working ID: `chaosx_046_three_reversals`
- Visibility: hidden
- Difficulty: extreme
- Eligible scope: a human country with three consecutive natural Great Shuffle reports
- Tracked value: one approved national value selected at the first qualifying firing
- Sequence: top world quintile after the first firing, bottom world quintile after the second, and top world quintile after the third
- Completion condition: win a war after the third firing while remaining the same country
- Disqualifiers: another Great Shuffle interrupting the exact three-firing sequence, changing player country, force or debug routes, or selecting a protected or owner-hidden value
- Why it is not trivial: it requires three natural firings, extreme ranking reversals, and a final military success
- Title direction: the same country raised, ruined, and raised again
- Description direction: emphasize reversal without describing the random algorithm
- Icon direction: one national silhouette moving through high, low, and high positions across three shuffled layers

The tracked family is selected from Stability, War Support, fuel share, reserve manpower, one military experience store, or normalized ordinary equipment value when all three firings produced comparable legal results.

## Achievement tracking rules

Achievement tracking uses separate protected flags and variables.

It never feeds family selection, ranges, reports, or Chaos gain.

A qualifying proof is created only after a transaction closes successfully.

A recovery achievement reads ordinary gameplay state through bounded owner-country checks and avoids a whole-world daily scan.

The implementation must prefer existing achievement hooks and sparse country processing.

A completed achievement remains permanent and cannot be shuffled.

## Report event image

Event 46 needs one report-event image.

The source mode is generated because the event is fictional, global, and physically impossible.

The asset worker must inspect the canonical report-event references and the live Event 46 consumer before fixing the final dimensions.

The expected report family is the ordinary HOI4 report-event canvas, but installed vanilla and current project wiring remain authoritative.

### Image direction

The scene should look like a period documentary photograph from the late 1930s or early 1940s.

It should show a familiar urban or industrial setting where people, military supplies, machinery, transport, and civic space have been visibly reordered into an impossible but coherent scene.

Examples of useful visual ingredients include a small square crowded by factory machinery, civilian crowds beside oversized military stores, rail equipment where streets should be, and buildings whose functions visibly conflict.

The image should have one strong subject and realistic period photographic limits.

It should avoid readable generated text, modern uniforms, modern vehicles, digital imagery, a clean strategy map, a staff table, a flag collage, a card-game illustration, and a literal user interface.

The final art remains static.

## Achievement icons

Each planned achievement requires a dedicated completed icon and the additional grey or not-eligible variants required by the current achievement system.

The asset worker must inspect the canonical achievement triplet references before production.

Every icon is designed for the achievement surface and cannot be a resized crop of the report image.

The six icon directions are listed with the achievement designs.

Generated icons use native transparency only when the inspected achievement reference family uses transparent unused canvas.

The current achievement consumer decides the background and variant treatment.

## Event text direction

### Event title

The accepted event name is **The Great Shuffle**.

Final title presentation should remain direct and easy to identify in Event Log and Event Details.

### Opening and report description

The player-facing text should describe concrete facts that no longer match memory.

It can refer to armies finding different reserves, officials receiving incompatible totals, factories appearing in unexpected regions, or populations no longer matching recent records.

The tone should be serious with restrained absurdity.

The text should not explain random variables, probability, scripting, save files, or registry logic.

It should not build drama through official denial contrasted with witnesses.

It should describe the changed condition directly.

### Acknowledgement option

The option should express dry disbelief, administrative restraint, or resigned acceptance.

It should not promise reversal, invite a player choice that does not exist, or use a cheap joke about reloading the game.

Any cultural reference requires source research before final wording.

### Evolution text

The five accepted design labels are:

- The National Stores
- The Map Is Rewritten
- Governments and Armies Forget Themselves
- The Mechanics Lose Their Numbers
- Everything That Can Be Randomized

Implementation can refine their final display wording only when Event Details, Event Log, localisation, and workbook stay aligned.

Descriptions should explain the newly exposed domain and preserve the distinction between capability unlock and ordinary stage progression.

### Report rows

Rows use concise labels, before and after values, dynamic country or scope names, and clear grouping.

They do not show implementation IDs.

Large numeric values use the project's normal formatting.

Percentages use appropriate precision.

Categorical values show player-facing names.

### Achievement text

Achievement titles and descriptions follow each working direction.

They should communicate the campaign feat without exposing hidden percentile formulas, debug disqualifiers, or raw tracking flags.

Tooltips can explain visible requirements precisely.

## Event documentation

Implementation creates or updates `docs/events/046_the_great_shuffle.md` as the canonical event document.

It should explain the event, family domains, transaction behavior, protected state, owner adapter boundary, report, Chaos result, AI behavior, multiplayer behavior, evolutions, cluster membership, assets, achievements, and known blocked conditional families.

The event doc must distinguish implemented core families from accepted but blocked conditional families.

It must not claim a structural family exists merely because Evolution V permits it in principle.

## Expected implementation surfaces

The final file map is determined by repository inspection.

The implementation is expected to need Event 46 event script, event-owned scripted effects and triggers, script constants or another centralized tuning source, event-owned report localisation, event log and Event Details integration, evolution registration, Randomizations cluster registration, Chaos history integration, achievement tracking, asset wiring, event documentation, and workbook alignment.

Owner adapters remain in owner-controlled files with Event 46 declarations or callbacks.

No periodic whole-world on-action is accepted.

## Probability and audit requirements

Family weights, coverage, compatibility, quotas, evolution pacing, and any achievement percentile selection require named scenario evidence.

The implementation must begin with `hoi4.probability_inspect` for every weighted surface.

It then uses evaluation, sweep, simulation, sequence, comparison, or rendering according to the complete scenario matrix.

The read-only probability auditor does not choose the target balance.

The Event 46 owner applies changes and the auditor compares the same scenarios afterward.

## Completion standard

Event 46 is complete only when:

- every core family in the accepted capability has a legal implementation or a clearly reported blocker that prevents a completion claim
- every enabled conditional family has its full contract and evidence
- the protected-state audit finds no write path from Event 46
- repeated firing has no result memory
- one transaction creates one history entry and one direct Chaos entry
- generic sources are not double counted
- population rewrite does not create Deaths or migration
- save and load recovery does not reroll
- multiplayer uses one shared result
- reports are concise and accurate
- evolution toggles work
- Evolution V is reachable under the live World Collapse order
- Randomizations cannot duplicate a one-member firing
- Domestic Unrest and accepted cluster changes are aligned without destroying multi-membership
- achievements, assets, localisation, docs, Event Details, and workbook agree
- probability comparison and completion audits pass
- no unapproved fallback, simplification, placeholder, missing asset, or stale catalog identity is hidden

Implementation status should move to **Needs Testing** after source completion and required non-live validation.

A **Playable** status still requires explicit user approval.
