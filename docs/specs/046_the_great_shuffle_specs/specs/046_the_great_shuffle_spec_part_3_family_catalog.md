# Event 046: Shuffle family catalog

## Family status language

Every gameplay surface belongs to one of four design classes.

**Core safe** means the surface is expected to ship with Event 46 once the implementation confirms the current engine setter and legal range.

**Conditional safe** means the idea is accepted, but the family remains unavailable until repository, vanilla, and engine inspection proves its complete plan, commit, reconciliation, reporting, and save behavior.

**Owner adapter only** means Event 46 cannot touch the value directly.

The owning mechanic must register a complete adapter under Evolution IV or V.

**Protected** means the surface is outside the event's allowlist.

A high Chaos tier does not weaken a protected classification.

## Basic country values

### Stability

Stability is a Baseline core family.

Every valid ordinary country receives a new legal percentage when the family is selected.

The old percentage affects only the report.

Baseline uses the `broad_center` profile.

Later capabilities add stronger low and high tails, with Evolution V allowed to reach the full legal band frequently enough to be visible.

Countries whose owner system replaces ordinary stability with another authority model can veto the family or expose an adapter.

### War Support

War Support follows the same Baseline contract as Stability.

It is rolled independently.

High Stability does not imply high War Support.

A country can receive an internally contradictory political condition because the contradiction is playable and engine-valid.

### Political Power

Political Power is a Baseline core family.

The result is an absolute amount inside the engine-valid and project-approved band.

The band uses the current legal cap and Event 46's capability profile.

The country does not preserve its old share of the world total.

A legal negative balance should remain excluded unless current engine inspection proves that a negative amount is a supported persistent state and the design explicitly adopts it.

### Command Power

Command Power is a Baseline core family.

The result uses the country's frozen legal maximum and does not depend on the old reserve.

Temporary cap modifiers remain ordinary gameplay state.

Event 46 changes the stored reserve, not the source modifiers that determine the cap.

### Army, Navy, and Air Experience

The three military experience stores are separate Baseline families.

Each uses its own legal cap and independent result.

A country with no navy or air force can still receive the matching experience when the engine accepts the value, because the resource can shape later play.

A nonhuman or special actor can be excluded when its owner does not use the ordinary experience system.

### Reserve manpower

Reserve manpower is a Baseline core family with a stricter legality rule.

Its result is drawn from dynamic world and population bands and ignores the country's old reserve.

The range can use frozen era, total valid world population, valid-country count, and the country's current civilian population as legal or plausibility ceilings.

It must retain wide absolute tails so small countries can receive unexpectedly large reserves.

The family does not directly change state population.

It does not record births, deaths, famine, or migration.

When state population is also selected at Evolution II or later, both results are planned from the same frozen pre-transaction snapshot and committed through a declared bundle.

### Fuel reserve

Fuel is a Baseline core family.

The result lies between zero and the country's frozen legal fuel capacity.

Fuel capacity itself remains unchanged unless another separately proven family modifies the buildings or modifiers that create it.

A zero-capacity country receives zero and is counted as legally processed, not as a failed scope.

## Evolution I national stores

### Equipment stockpiles

Equipment stockpiles are an Evolution I family group.

Each compatible equipment family is a separate selectable family or a declared bundle when the owner needs several tokens to remain coordinated.

The legal token set is frozen before results are generated.

A token can enter the set when it exists in the loaded game, is a concrete stockpile object, is permitted by its owner, and can be added or removed through the current engine without creating an invalid reference.

The family can include ordinary infantry equipment, support equipment, artillery families, motorized families, armored families, aircraft, and other ordinary stores after direct inspection.

Special event equipment, biological or chemical payloads, nonhuman equipment, hidden project objects, one-off quest items, and owner-managed tokens require explicit owner registration.

Quantity bands are based on era, equipment family scale, and frozen global production anchors.

They are not scaled down merely because the receiving country is small.

A country can receive equipment it has not researched only when the current engine allows that concrete stockpile token to exist and the family contract confirms that doing so does not unlock, reveal, or corrupt protected technology state.

Variant identity is protected by default.

Where safe variant balancing is supported, the adapter may choose only from already valid concrete variants.

The event never fabricates a variant name.

### Convoys

Convoys are an Evolution I core family.

The result uses a dynamic absolute band and can create a convoy-rich or convoy-poor world.

The country's coastline, navy, old convoy amount, and industrial rank do not determine its entitlement.

The range retains a project performance ceiling so one result cannot create an unusable number of individual objects.

### Trains

Trains use the same Evolution I design as convoys.

A country without an active rail supply need can still receive trains.

The family changes the national store only.

It does not build railways, hubs, or routes.

### Other national inventories

Nuclear stockpiles, missiles, chemical payloads, biological payloads, project resources, unique devices, and event-created inventory tokens are not generic Evolution I families.

They are owner adapter candidates.

An owner must decide whether the object is a normal expendable store, a progression proof, a unique identity, or a protected capability.

Unique and historical objects remain protected.

## Evolution II state population

State population is an Evolution II core family for ordinary populated states.

Each state receives a new absolute population.

The result is independent of the state's old population and independent of the industry result.

The frozen world mean population per valid state is the main scale anchor.

The family uses several broad result bands, including near-empty, sparse, ordinary, dense, and megastate outcomes.

The exact probabilities change by capability and the selected distribution profile.

No band is derived from the state's old rank.

State category, impassable status, active wasteland state, special scenario ownership, and a protected population floor can alter legality.

A normal state can become nearly empty.

A result of literal zero remains conditional on engine and owner review because many systems assume a surviving civilian floor.

Population growth or loss caused by Event 46 is not history.

It must not add to Deaths, famine mortality, migration, civilian birth records, or Chaos from tracked deaths.

The transaction must reconcile any recruitable-manpower credit or debit that the engine creates as a side effect.

The separate reserve-manpower family remains the only Event 46 source of a newly shuffled national reserve.

## Evolution II industry and construction

### Shared factory bundle

Civilian factories, military factories, and dockyards belong to one capacity-aware dependency bundle.

Their individual results remain independent, but the bundle plans a legal shared capacity before assigning the three counts.

The state can receive a large industrial result even when its population result is tiny.

When the design permits extra shared building slots, the capacity change is part of the same prevalidated bundle.

The event does not silently delete an overflow after assigning impossible counts.

Dockyards require a coastal and consumer-valid state.

An inland state receives zero dockyards without making the entire family fail.

### Infrastructure

Infrastructure is an Evolution II core family.

It uses the full legal level range and does not depend on the state's old development.

The family changes the building level only.

It does not promise that the new industrial geography has good supply.

### Airbase, anti-air, radar, and forts

Airbases, state anti-air, radar stations, land forts, and coastal forts are separate state-building families.

Each uses its current legal level range.

Coastal forts require a coastal state and a valid coastal consumer.

DLC or feature-gated buildings are eligible only when loaded and usable.

The event can create militarily strange states without needing to keep their building levels proportional.

### Naval bases

Naval bases are conditional safe.

They require a valid coastal province placement contract. A coastal state check alone is insufficient.

The family remains unavailable until the implementation can select a legal province, preserve existing province-building identity, avoid duplicate invalid bases, and reconcile naval supply and pathing.

### Supply hubs and railways

Supply hubs and railways are conditional safe.

They are graph and province systems, not simple state numbers.

The family needs a full route graph contract, legal province placement, adjacency validation, duplicate prevention, and supply recalculation.

Evolution II does not require these families to ship if the proof is incomplete.

Their absence must be reported as a blocked conditional family, not filled by random infrastructure.

### State resources

Ordinary strategic resources are Evolution II conditional-core families.

Every resource type is registered separately with a legal token and resource-specific range.

A result can be zero.

A state can become a major resource center independently of its population or industry.

Scripted deposits, resource discoveries, owner-maintained deposits, unique map resources, and resources whose amount is historical proof remain protected or owner adapter only.

The family must refresh trade and extraction consumers after commit without creating a second reward or compensation.

### Building damage and temporary construction state

Building damage, repair queues, construction queues, temporary project progress, and province-level construction objects remain protected by default.

They describe ongoing actions and object identity, not stable final levels.

An owner can later expose a narrow numeric surface when safe.

## Evolution III politics

### Party popularity

Party popularity is an Evolution III core family for countries using the normal ideology system.

The full set of active ideology shares is planned together and normalized to a legal total.

Each ideology can receive a very low or very high share.

The current ruling ideology receives no automatic majority.

Countries with owner-defined ideology systems, no normal civilian politics, or special fixed-purpose governments are excluded unless adapted.

### Ruling ideology

Ruling ideology is conditional safe.

When implemented, it chooses from the country's loaded and owner-approved ideology set.

It can select an ideology with low popularity, because that political contradiction is valid gameplay.

The family must reconcile election state, ruling-party display, country-specific government restrictions, AI strategy refresh, and generic Chaos-source suppression.

It must not replace leaders, party names, characters, flags, or focus trees unless those owners already react to government changes through normal play.

### Laws

Each law category is a separate Evolution III compatibility family.

The candidate set contains loaded law tokens that belong to the correct category and can exist for the country under the current DLC and owner rules.

Ordinary purchase prerequisites do not need to preserve the old political path when the shuffle directly rewrites the law.

Tokens that require an absent system, invalid ideology object, missing country feature, or owner lifecycle proof are excluded.

The event cannot assign two laws from one mutually exclusive category.

Country-specific and event-specific laws require owner approval.

### Balance of power and internal authority

Generic balance-of-power values are not direct law families.

They are owner adapter only because the owning system defines sides, ranges, modifiers, decisions, thresholds, and lifecycle.

Stable side identities and decision history remain protected.

## Evolution III research and doctrine

### Active research progress

Progress on a currently active research project is an Evolution III conditional-core family.

The active project token and research slot identity remain protected.

The family changes only legal progress toward that project.

It cannot complete a technology during planning, remove a completed technology, select a different project, change the number of slots, or rewrite the technology graph.

The implementation must verify how the current engine stores and mutates progress before enabling the family.

A country with no active project in a slot simply has no scope for that slot.

### Doctrine progress

Doctrine progress is conditional safe.

The preferred contract changes progress toward an already selected valid doctrine or another numeric doctrine surface without granting or removing completed doctrine nodes.

Completed doctrine branches, mutual exclusions, prerequisites, and mastery history remain protected.

A broader completed-doctrine randomizer is outside the accepted core family until technology graph inspection proves a compatibility-preserving plan and the user accepts that added scope.

### Research bonuses and technology grants

Stored research bonuses, completed technology, research slots, special projects, facilities, prototype identity, breakthrough history, and technology ownership remain protected by default.

They can contain one-way history, dependency graphs, or owner-managed progression.

## Evolution III production

### Production-line efficiency and progress

Numeric production-line state is an Evolution III conditional-core family.

The production line, produced item, variant, military industrial organization, assigned factory object, and order identity remain protected.

Eligible numeric surfaces can include current efficiency, efficiency progress, and stored production progress when the engine supports safe setters.

Every result is clamped to the line's current legal cap.

The family does not create or delete production lines.

It does not change what the country is producing.

### Assigned factories and queue order

Assigned factory counts, production queue order, resource allocation, and military industrial organization assignment are conditional safe.

They require a complete factory availability and queue reconciliation contract.

They are not necessary for Evolution III to function.

### Construction and ship queues

Construction queue objects, ship hull identity, ship names, and build-history objects remain protected.

Numeric progress can become a later owner adapter only when the object survives the rewrite unchanged.

## Evolution III units and commanders

### Unit experience

Unit experience is an Evolution III core family for valid deployed units.

The unit's identity, owner, template, battalions, equipment definition, name, army assignment, and location remain unchanged.

The result uses the legal experience band for that unit type.

Special units can veto through their owner.

### Planning and readiness

Land-unit planning is conditional-core when a direct safe numeric setter exists.

Current organization, strength, entrenchment, supply status, equipment fill, and manpower fill remain conditional because they are strongly derived and can create destructive side effects.

The design permits these surfaces only after the implementation proves that changing one does not corrupt reinforcement, combat, deployment, or casualty accounting.

### Commander experience

Commander experience is an Evolution III core or conditional-core family after direct engine inspection.

Commander identity, level, traits, trait progress, assignments, portraits, and history remain protected.

The family changes only the approved numeric experience store.

Trait progress can become a separate conditional family when every trait token and cap is legal.

### Air wings and ships

Air-wing experience and ship experience are conditional safe.

Their objects, designs, names, bases, fleets, task forces, and equipment remain protected.

The implementation must prove the current scope and setter for each domain before enabling it.

## Evolution IV owner-registered mechanics

A registered mechanic can expose one or more mutable current-state values.

Typical candidates include legitimacy, authority, influence, cohesion, panic, readiness, preparedness, local crisis severity, reserves, pressure, contamination-like local state, or bounded progression.

The owner must distinguish a mutable current value from history.

The owner cannot expose a ledger of deaths, first discovery, lifetime contamination, permanent betrayal, fired events, completed stages, achievement proof, or other one-way accounting.

The owner decides whether all valid instances enter the family or whether only active instances are valid.

The owner also decides whether zero means inactive, empty, resolved, or invalid.

Event 46 never guesses.

## Evolution V structural candidates

### Claims

Territorial claims are the preferred first structural candidate.

Claims are one-way relationships and do not normally change current ownership.

A safe adapter can add, remove, or regenerate selected claims across valid country and state pairs while protecting cores, active owner proof, and historical claims that another system treats as permanent memory.

The adapter needs pair deduplication, invalid-country cleanup, and report limits.

### Cores

Cores are protected by default.

They affect country identity, resistance, compliance, manpower, focus logic, release logic, and historical claims.

A general core randomizer is outside the accepted Event 46 scope unless a future dedicated design proves every downstream contract.

### State ownership and control

Selected ownership relationships are conditional structural adapters.

A safe ownership bundle must preserve a valid owner and controller, handle capitals, prevent or deliberately resolve zero-state countries, refresh supply and occupation, protect lifecycle carriers, reconcile subjects and wars, and define what happens to units and buildings.

No ownership family is mandatory merely because Evolution V is active.

Control without ownership is a separate relationship and needs its own war and occupation contract.

### Capital relocation

Capital relocation is a conditional structural family.

The result must select an owned and valid state and preserve any owner-specific capital restrictions.

It cannot point to an unowned, impassable, terminal, or reserved state.

### Diplomatic relationships

Opinion values and owner-declared opinion modifiers are the safest diplomatic candidates.

Guarantees, access, docking rights, non-aggression pacts, faction membership, subjects, autonomy, truces, lend-lease, volunteers, attachés, and wars require separate pair or group contracts.

War state, faction identity, subject identity, peace-conference state, and treaty history remain protected by default.

The event must not create an unplanned world war as a side effect of a generic relationship family.

### Unit locations

Land-unit locations are conditional structural families.

A safe adapter must choose a legal owned or controlled province, preserve unit identity and assignment, avoid encircled invalid positions, respect transport and deployment state, and refresh pathing.

Navies need valid owned or allied ports and fleet contracts.

Air wings need valid airbases with capacity.

Each domain is separate.

### Country identity and map definitions

Country tags, cosmetic tags, colors, leaders, characters, flags, party names, country names, state IDs, province IDs, strategic regions, state definitions, supply node identity, building definitions, equipment definitions, technology definitions, and stable map geometry are protected.

The Great Shuffle changes gameplay state inside the save.

It does not rewrite the mod's data model.
