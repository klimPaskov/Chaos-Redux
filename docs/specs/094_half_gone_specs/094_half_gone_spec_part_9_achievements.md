# Achievements

## Recovery and consequence rather than a lucky disaster

Four achievement concepts are proposed. Their titles below are working labels. Final titles and descriptions require the normal localization review. An achievement never unlocks simply because the event fired or because many people disappeared.

All four require a natural, committed Event 094 incident in an otherwise eligible campaign. Debug calls, forced test incidents, edited achievement flags, and manual scenario shortcuts do not qualify. The tracked country and incident remain fixed, so switching controlled countries cannot combine unrelated accomplishments.

A completed achievement is a record of an accomplishment, not a future-loss baseline. Any stored pre-incident quantities are used only to verify its recovery conditions. Subsequent Half gone firings continue to read actual current values.

## Rebuilding under pressure

**Proposed identity:** `094_half_gone_rebuilding_under_pressure`.

This difficult, visible achievement recognizes sustained recovery after a genuine multi-category loss. The qualifying incident must be a natural Evolution IV result which removes equipment and at least three additional categories with positive actual effects. At least two of those additional categories must be independently measurable scalar reserves or national gauges.

Within 365 days of that incident, the same surviving country must restore at least two of those selected scalar values to their recorded pre-incident levels and restore its stored equipment value to at least seventy-five percent of its pre-incident value. It must also complete at least two different relevant Event 094 response programs. The tests must be true together at evaluation, rather than being assembled from values recovered and spent at unrelated earlier dates.

Equipment value uses a consistent production-cost basis frozen for the comparison. It must not treat one modern tank as equal to one rifle or allow a price-definition change to produce recovery without equipment. Implementation must prove this measure across supported variants and origins before enabling this achievement.

Normal production, real trade, and real allied transfers can qualify. An Abundance grant, Devil restoration, or exceptional temporal restoration applied to a tracked quantity during the attempt disqualifies this recovery attempt. Those extraordinary recoveries have a separate achievement concept.

The icon direction is an arsenal being rebuilt through visible work, with the absent portion suggested by unfinished shelving or an interrupted inventory. Its difficulty comes from recovering several usable systems while managing a still-active campaign, not from arranging a deliberately tiny loss.

## Supporting those who remain

**Proposed identity:** `094_half_gone_supporting_those_who_remain`.

This difficult, visible achievement follows a natural population disappearance of at least one hundred thousand people and at least forty percent of the incident's eligible population. The country must complete a household-support response and remain a live country for 180 days after the incident. At the end of that period it must control its current capital and have at least fifty percent stability.

It does not require the missing population to be replaced. It does not award progress for deaths, new disappearances, forced migration, or a government declaring people dead. Its focus is the continued functioning of the country and an actual completed effort for survivors.

Annexation before completion ends the attempt. A tag change which represents an owner-supported continuation must carry the same attempt without creating a second one. Debug population grants and forced completion of the response do not qualify.

The icon direction is a communal light, open doorway, or helping hands associated with a remaining household. It must avoid skulls, body counts, trophies, and triumphant imagery about the disappearance itself.

## A new command generation

**Proposed identity:** `094_half_gone_a_new_command_generation`.

This hard, visible achievement requires a natural loss of at least three distinct generals, field marshals, or admirals. Within 730 days, the same country must legitimately recruit at least three new military-leader identities which were not in its military roster before the incident. At least three qualifying new leaders must each gain a full skill level through normal service after recruitment.

Promotion or reassignment of a pre-existing identity is not recruitment of a new person. A genuinely new general who is later promoted can qualify. A restored missing commander, copied unique character, or debug-created high-skill leader does not count as a new generation.

The attempt stores identity and initial legitimate skill for the relevant recruits. It does not infer progress from a changed portrait, a different displayed name, or the total number of leader slots. The command-reorganization program can assist but cannot itself grant the skill level required for completion.

The icon direction is a new officer's insignia beside an unoccupied older place, with emphasis on earned responsibility rather than a claim that the missing officers have been found.

## From absence to excess

**Proposed identity:** `094_half_gone_from_absence_to_excess`.

This rare, initially hidden achievement connects the two phenomena. After a natural Evolution IV incident with equipment and at least three positive additional category losses, the country must receive genuine Abundance outcomes which restore at least two distinct selected reserve or national-value categories to their recorded pre-incident values within 365 days.

The Abundance owner must supply successful result evidence for the exact semantic values. Merely seeing a relevant card, selecting a failed provider, receiving unrelated abundance, or improving the same value through ordinary accumulation is insufficient. A pair or triple card can qualify for several categories only when each distinct atomic result actually applied.

The values must still meet the recovery condition together at completion. Population and unique-character restoration do not qualify through a generic scalar shortcut. Devil bargains and temporal interventions do not substitute for the Abundance receipts.

The icon direction contrasts a visibly emptied vessel with the same type of vessel filled to excess. It should communicate two different outcomes while refusing to imply that one event explains the cause of the other.

## Tracking and presentation

Each achievement needs a finite attempt record, eligibility, progress evidence, disqualifiers, expiry where applicable, and cleanup after completion or permanent failure. Tracking occurs through relevant event and owner results and bounded active-attempt evaluation. It must not create a permanent all-country daily sweep.

The same incident may seed more than one compatible attempt, but a single achievement can unlock only once. A repeated incident can begin a fresh eligible attempt after the previous attempt ends, without combining two countries or claiming a single restoration against two losses.

All four concepts require original completed icons and their grey and not-eligible states. Their exact asset stems and registry declarations must be checked against the existing achievement system before implementation. No proposed identifier in this document is evidence that a native registration already exists.
