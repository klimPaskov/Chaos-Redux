# Presentation and assets

## A clear report with an unresolved cause

The normal country report carries the event. Its headline uses the supplied event identity. Its main prose describes the kind of absence which actually occurred. Its result summary names each selected category, the measured loss, and the amount or roster remaining where that can be shown safely.

Equipment-heavy reports can group small entries beneath meaningful equipment families. A full underlying inventory must still exist for reliable calculation and verification. The interface cannot claim that a summary's largest three lines are the only stocks affected.

Human reports name publicly known characters where the owner's visibility rules permit it. Secret operatives and unrevealed unique roles remain protected. A hidden character identifier must not appear in the text because the ordinary name resolver failed.

The dominant report picture follows population, named people, broader national values, then reserve equipment in that order. A combined Evolution IV report lists its other losses in the result summary rather than stacking several modal reports for the same transaction.

## Writing directions

Material prose should make the contradiction concrete. A depot has space where familiar stacks stood. Records still describe goods which cannot be found. Officials can agree on the proportions while disagreeing about the circumstances. Dry humor belongs to bureaucratic confidence, failed explanations, or the practical demand to begin counting again.

Research and government prose should describe lost unfinished work, reduced political capacity, or institutions discovering that their current position has diminished. The text must match the actual affected quantity. Lost progress is not a lost research slot. Reduced stability is not automatically a new ruling party.

Human prose should attend to interrupted activity and the remaining people. It should not reuse a joke about missing rifles as a joke about missing households. It also should not describe confirmed funerals, bodies, an established afterlife, or a solved mechanism.

All labels for response programs and achievements in this package are working design labels. Final event descriptions, options, news text, achievement titles, super-event wording, quotations, and button text belong to the later localization and research pass.

## Event Details and history

The base Event Details description states the premise and reserve-equipment behavior. Separate evolution previews explain the expanding category reach. It should not expose implementation registries, raw probability weights, internal receipts, or a long list of exceptions intended for developers.

One incident history row identifies Event 094, its date, selected country, delivery source, and effective tier. The target must be established before generic history recording can attribute the event to a system carrier or unrelated caller.

Evolution history records genuine first-use behavior through the shared system, with the correct actor and enabled state. Normal response starts, support completion, recruitment, and a later aid offer are not extra evolutions. They can use their existing owner records or a compact country summary.

## News without constant interruption

Routine material losses remain primarily country-facing. A public national or human loss can warrant a news item when its magnitude and visibility make it internationally meaningful. Secret operative losses must not become global headlines by default.

A single incident produces at most one ordinary news package. A population loss takes precedence over a lost public commander, which takes precedence over a broader institutional result. Other selected losses can be included in that same report where publicly known.

If a super-event is used for the same milestone, its accompanying news must complement the presentation rather than repeating every sentence in another popup. Later incidents still receive accurate local reports even when a global presentation cooldown suppresses repetition.

## A major population milestone

One super-event is proposed for the first campaign incident which removes at least five million eligible civilians and at least forty percent of the selected country's pre-incident eligible population. These are presentation thresholds, not protection from loss. Smaller countries can still lose half their population and receive an appropriate country report and news.

The milestone can occur from Evolution III or IV when those settings are enabled. It is one campaign-wide presentation milestone, not one repeatable cinematic reward per country. Later qualifying losses remain mechanically complete and may receive news without replaying the super-event.

The image should show a recognizably functioning country with disturbing absences, such as a tram stop, public street, or communal interior with ordinary objects left in place. The scene must leave room for the viewer's uncertainty. No portal, fading bodies, divine hand, alien beam, identified perpetrator, or recognizable franchise reference should certify an explanation.

The text researcher must develop the final title, description, quotation, and reaction after source checks. A quotation about absence, uncertainty, or the duties of those who remain is preferable to one which calls the missing population dead. The final attribution and any cultural reference require a source-backed check. No final quotation or lyric is selected in this plan.

The audio direction is a structured piece with an identifiable musical line, development, and a controlled ending. It should begin with ordinary continuity, develop a restrained sense of absence, and leave the situation unresolved. A long silent file, a single sound effect, an ambient drone, or a short cinematic impact stretched to the slot is not an adequate music package. The proposed edited duration is 75 to 100 seconds, within the normal 120-second ceiling.

No final recording is selected. The research pass must check the existing track list, avoid unintended duplication, establish rights for both the composition and recording or generation source, and retain the edit and license evidence.

## Asset boundaries

The event adds no new likeness for Wu, Kruger, or an ordinary missing commander. Existing approved character assets remain the relevant identity. It also adds no 3D unit model, flag family, or animated portrait merely to represent absence.

Each required asset needs its intended consumer, source mode, final dimensions, crop or alpha handling, reference set, runtime destination, and acceptance evidence. Working source material belongs in the event's asset workspace. Final game assets are promoted only after review.

Achievement icons need the complete colored, grey, and not-eligible triplets. The not-eligible template must remain intact, and the three states must preserve the same underlying icon identity. One icon processed three unrelated ways is not three independently invented compositions.

The visual and audio inventories in this specification are production requirements. No finished image, audio file, native sprite registration, or in-game visual approval is claimed by this planning package.
