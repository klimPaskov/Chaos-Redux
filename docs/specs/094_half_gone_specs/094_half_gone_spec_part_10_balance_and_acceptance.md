# Balance, repeat behavior, and acceptance

## The loss stays proportional

The main balance anchor is the user's fifty-percent rule. Balance work can change eligible selection weights, response costs, presentation thresholds, or frequency through the existing shared event system. It must not quietly change fifty percent to a fixed grant, protect all high-value equipment, or use a smaller fraction for AI countries.

A country with a larger reserve loses more equipment because it owns more equipment. A depleted country loses less. The event does not equalize those countries by referring to a historical original amount or by subtracting a minimum stock package after the half has already been taken.

Whole-count rounding is allowed only to resolve indivisibility. It must not become a second balance system. The expected half of an odd small roster remains a half, and a saved result remains the same result.

## Repeated incidents

The existing Minor Repeatable weight recovery, repeat cap, and shared timer remain authoritative. Event 094 does not add a second private random timer or a guaranteed revenge firing after recovery.

Each natural firing obtains a new incident identity and a new current-state snapshot. Genuine later firings may hit the same country. Duplicate delivery of the same incident may not. A clustered delivery and a direct delivery which are actually two separate dispatches remain separate incidents, while repeated callbacks from one dispatch remain one.

An aftermath program never stores an amount for future removal. A benchmark retained for an achievement or restoration offer is explicitly separate from the next loss. This distinction must survive save loading, country continuation, and owner cleanup.

## Chaos impact

Chaos level 1 remains the catalog classification. It is distinct from the amount of actual campaign Chaos generated by a consequence.

The proposed Event 094 contribution uses a per-country severity high-water mark. A first positive equipment or reserve disappearance establishes a five-point severity mark. A first broader national-value disappearance establishes ten. A first named-character-group disappearance establishes fifteen. A first population disappearance establishes twenty.

Only an increase above that country's prior mark adds the difference to Chaos. A country moving from equipment loss to population loss therefore contributes the additional fifteen points, not another full twenty on top of the previous five. Several categories in the same incident use the highest actual positive consequence, not the sum of every category label.

This is an anti-farming proposal for the event-specific unexplained shock. Repeated destruction is still mechanically real even when it does not award another identical shock contribution. Ordinary separately measured consequences remain owned by their normal systems. Evolution unlocks, previews, target selection, report clicks, and a zero-effect rounded result add nothing.

Population disappearances do not pass through the confirmed Deaths-to-Chaos route. Chemical disappearance does not pass through a contamination route unless a separate real release occurs. The super-event is presentation and adds no second reward. Recovery has no automatic generic Chaos refund.

The high-water mark follows a legitimate country continuation and cannot be cleared through ordinary tag changes. Its implementation must use the shared Chaos ownership rules and avoid duplicating another already registered source for the same consequence.

## Response tuning

The starting programs deliberately improve future work rather than returning the full missing half. Ninety days of increased output can matter without guaranteeing that an entire arsenal is replaced. Command and research continuity address lost expertise and unfinished work while preserving normal recruitment and research requirements.

The household-support alternatives provide a meaningful response even when an evolved incident also removed resources. Their small civic benefit must be clear and finite. Neither creates population or a safe immunity period.

Balance testing should compare the benefit to the actual opportunity cost for a small player-controlled country, an industrial major, a country fighting for survival, and a country with few surviving institutions. Proposed numeric anchors remain subject to this testing. The fifty-percent disappearance and the four user thresholds do not.

## Acceptance examples

A baseline country with modern domestic equipment, captured foreign variants, obsolete models, and a special Chaos Redux stock must lose half of each compatible stored entry. Its active units must retain their issued equipment. A familiar equipment-only approximation fails this example.

A country with eighty percent stability must reach approximately forty percent when stability is selected. A country with twenty percent war support must reach approximately ten percent. Neither should lose fifty percentage points. Negative and empty resource balances must not become beneficial half-losses.

A population incident must reduce the eligible current state demographic total by approximately half, spread the result through those states, leave active forces outside the direct disappearance, and produce no confirmed Deaths increment. Refugee and cohort accounting must agree with that one physical loss.

A multi-role scientist selected for disappearance must leave every role requiring that person. A second selected roster cannot remove the same identity again. The loss of an admiral must not delete the fleet. The loss of a unique character must enter an owner-supported absence outcome rather than an arbitrary death branch.

At high Chaos with Evolution III disabled, Evolution IV must not select population or characters. At lower current Chaos after an earlier high-tier incident, the new firing must respect the lower current eligibility. A preview or a history row must not reapply a loss.

A save made after a committed incident must preserve its target, selected categories, odd rounding, missing identities, actual results, and owner claim state. Reloading that save or acknowledging its report must not remove another half or reroll the vanished person.

A funded response must charge exactly what it displayed and release its occupied capacity exactly once. A canceled response must not pay its completion reward. A later disappearance must not stack a second free copy of an existing recovery bonus.

An Abundance card must provide actual matching value recovery before it contributes to the connection achievement. A Devil restoration must be bounded by its qualified deficit and consumed claim. Neither may reveal the universal cause or make an old incident the basis of a new disappearance.

## Review standard

The finished event should feel sudden, comprehensible, and severe where the current country makes it severe. Its depth comes from dynamic coverage, the distinction between people and institutions, choices about recovery, and the interaction of existing systems.

A complete release requires the relevant native reference checks, owner integrations, probability audit, localization and asset review, save and multiplayer checks, and parent acceptance. A design document, arithmetic model, or list of intended adapters does not itself establish that those checks passed.
