# Connections and extraordinary recovery

## Connections start from an actual loss

A related event can respond only to a committed disappearance and the affected country's current circumstances. A failed target roll, an unlocked evolution, or a report which removed nothing is not a severe national crisis. The connection receives the affected category, actual result, date, and relevant owner identities. It does not need to copy the entire event into its own system.

The source incident retains its identity even after several recovery actions. A later event may change the country's current state, but it does not rewrite the original quantities, erase the mystery, or mark Event 094 as fired again. Each owner remains responsible for its own outcomes and history.

## Abundance can fill the gap

Abundance is especially valuable when one of its current choices concerns a quantity which Half gone reduced. A country which lost fuel may suddenly have a chance to fill its fuel reserve. A country whose stability fell can discover an extraordinary opportunity to restore confidence. A special national currency can become important in exactly the same way when both events support its owner.

The connection uses the semantic identity of the value, not the spelling of a tooltip or the raw storage variable. If the same fuel is described differently by two screens, it is still the same reserve. If two resources share a display name but belong to different institutions, they are not interchangeable.

Half gone does not force an Abundance card to appear or select a card for the player. The ordinary four-choice Abundance wave continues to generate the country's real current possibilities. A matching card can receive a short contextual explanation of why it would matter after the disappearance. It retains the Abundance owner's normal outcome, cost boundaries, persistence, and danger classification.

Abundance may exceed the old pre-loss amount. Its purpose is abundance, so an extraordinary result should not be capped merely because Event 094 once recorded a smaller reserve. Conversely, a partial or capped result should not be called complete restoration when the measured value does not support that claim.

An existing persistent abundance effect may refill a reserve after Half gone reduces it. The disappearance still happens to the current reserve at its own instant. Event 094 does not cancel the abundance state to guarantee suffering, and it does not skip the loss because the reserve may refill tomorrow. The two owners remain independent and their order is visible through actual state changes.

## Similar machinery, different candidate rules

The Abundance specification already describes owner-managed value providers rather than unrestricted inspection of arbitrary variables. Half gone should reuse compatible semantic identities and owner operations where genuine shared callers justify it. It must not clone the entire provider system or assume every abundance operation has a reversible half-loss operation.

Abundance can intentionally increase harmful values such as panic. Half gone's resource pool does not automatically become a convenient way to halve every harmful pressure. Its premise is the loss of things the country possesses. A harmful quantity requires an explicit, meaningful owner design before it could qualify as a separate extension. The baseline, four user-defined evolution pools, and confirmed global ledgers are not broadened by taking an indiscriminate numeric inverse of Abundance.

A value may be supported by Abundance but blocked for Half gone because no safe loss operation exists. That is a real compatibility result. A zero-effect dummy provider is not support.

## Doctor Wu's surviving work

When Wu himself disappears, the connection in Part 5 separates his absence from the medical institution. Surviving researchers, treatment stocks, documented methods, and legitimate foreign agreements remain real resources. A country which preserved transferable knowledge should be less dependent on one missing person than a country which left every decision in his hands.

The proposed institutional response distinguishes three conditions. An independent service can continue established work while seeking new leadership. A mixed institution can continue documented work but pause work requiring Wu personally. A highly dependent institution must choose between a genuine outside partnership and suspending unsupported work. These are owner-state distinctions to reconcile with the actual Wu design, not three new Event 094 meters.

When population disappears but Wu remains, his role is practical assistance to the survivors and responsible investigation of the circumstances. The event cannot assume he has a cure for absence. He may protect continuity of treatment, coordinate available expertise, or reject an unsupported promise of universal return. His established owner abilities determine any concrete benefit.

A later medical success improves the lives of the people who remain. It does not subtract from the disappearance count as if treating an illness had located the missing population. An extraordinary recovery of a specific person must be explicitly supported by the owner and still leave the event's universal cause unresolved.

## Kruger's independence test

A Kruger host which loses reserve equipment can use its real industrial and scientific capabilities to rebuild. Completed designs, production facilities, and surviving staff remain available. Missing pathogen equipment does not become a free native biological raid, and lost robotic stock does not remove deployed autonomous formations.

If Kruger remains while other scientists disappear, the owner may offer an established continuity intervention. It should carry the same political and institutional tradeoffs which the owner uses elsewhere. A government may regain short-term capability while becoming more dependent on him. Event 094 does not invent a cost-free global research reward merely because his name appears in the country.

If Kruger disappears, the earlier investment in independent expertise matters. Completed work survives, personal services stop, and actual owner-supported continuity forms are considered. A machine or clone continuity state is not automatically erased or created. The owner determines the current identity and its vulnerabilities.

A temporal intervention, when legitimately available, targets one bounded loss and pays the existing temporal costs. It may restore a named component, a valid leader state, or another supported target. It cannot reset all categories from several firings, refund its own cost, or repeatedly restore the same loss. Synchronization capacity, temporal debt, stabilization, and other owner obligations remain operative.

Even a temporal restoration does not establish the destination or cause of the original disappearance. The result demonstrates what the intervention achieved. It does not authorize an explanatory reveal which Event 094 explicitly forbids.

## Deal with the Devil

A severe incident can create an exceptionally tempting reason to accept an existing Devil offer. The eligible crisis must be real and recent. Suitable contexts include a population disappearance, the loss of a vital unique character, or an Evolution IV incident with several positive categories lost. A trivial rounded stock change should not produce the same offer as a national catastrophe.

The Devil owner decides whether it is active, enabled, allowed to approach this country, and permitted to offer another bargain. Half gone provides the crisis context. It does not initialize the Devil's influence, skip an introduction, compel acceptance, or bypass a pact-breaking route.

The proposed offer distinguishes replacement, replenishment, and personal restoration. Material replacement can grant goods matching a bounded recorded deficit. Replenishment can provide a newly supported quantity without pretending that the same objects have been found. A promise to restore a particular person or population requires the Devil owner's explicit identity and population operation.

Terms must be concrete before acceptance. The player needs to know the relevant present benefit, the initial price, and the kind of continuing obligation being accepted. Future narrative details can remain secret, but the bargain cannot be presented as free help and then acquire an undisclosed compulsory payment solely to punish the choice.

The offer is compelling because the damage matters, not because the interface prevents refusal. Refusing leaves ordinary recovery and other available assistance intact. Acceptance follows the Devil owner's existing influence and consequence system. Event 094 must not introduce a parallel demonic meter.

## A restoration cannot be claimed twice

A material restoration identifies the particular incident and quantity it addresses. Its maximum eligible grant is bounded by that incident's actual loss and the still-existing gap below its recorded pre-loss amount at acceptance. The smaller bound applies. A country which has already recovered the whole gap does not receive a full duplicate grant labeled as restoration.

This bound belongs to the bargain only. It does not cap a later ordinary Abundance result or alter future Half gone calculations. Spending goods before acceptance can create a new shortage, but it cannot increase the maximum grant above what the qualifying incident actually removed.

A completed bargain consumes the relevant claim once. Splitting the country, transferring the person, switching the controlled tag, or reloading after commitment cannot reopen it. A new Half gone incident supplies a new possible crisis, but the Devil owner still controls whether another offer is allowed.

For people, material arithmetic is not a sufficient contract. A unique identity must not be duplicated. Replenishing population must not subtract confirmed deaths which were never logged, create a second arrival record, or retroactively restore unrelated military losses. The owner must establish exactly what has returned or been added and how its ledgers remain consistent.

## The Devil does not become the answer

The Devil may boast, imply responsibility, or claim that the country's other advisers know nothing. These statements remain attributed to that character. Delivering a replacement or fulfilling a restoration proves the result of that bargain, not that the same actor caused every disappearance.

The report, super-event, achievement text, and hidden resolution must preserve that distinction. The player may form a theory. The event does not settle it.

## Aid promised, aid delivered and later disappearance

The [Tordesillas aid contract](../093_treaty_of_tordesillas_restoration_specs/093_treaty_of_tordesillas_restoration_spec_part_03_spanish_civil_war.md) and [revolt shipments](../095_occupation_revolt_specs/095_occupation_revolt_spec_part_07_foreign_support_and_settlements.md) distinguish donor stock, transit and recipient stock.
Half gone removes compatible reserve actually held by the selected country at that instant.
A promise is not another arsenal; goods already removed into a delivery transaction cannot be removed again through the donor's stale promise.
Transit enters only through a compatible owner-supported reserve operation with one physical result.

Donor loss before dispatch makes the aid owner recheck the remaining bill and stocks.
Existing pause, revision and inability-to-deliver terms apply, with no free replacement or automatic deadline extension.
Recipient loss after credited arrival leaves the donation genuinely fulfilled, including an earned Galicia bargain.
The donor does not owe it twice, while outstanding installments remain outstanding.

AI donors protect current defense and honor actual affordable terms; recipients may request normal new aid without duplicating a fulfilled claim.
A future acceptance case compares Portugal losing rifles before dispatch with Spain losing them after credited arrival.
Only the former changes Portugal's ability to pay its remaining bill.
The same boundary preserves revolt-shipment arrival and Cohesion accounting.
If either aid owner is absent, normal recovery continues.

## Property and national continuity

[Greenland Sale](../092_greenland_sale_specs/092_greenland_sale_spec_part_03_dynamic_value.md) sells state assets, not national reserve equipment.
Equipment-only loss changes military circumstances and bidding willingness without deleting the lot or adjusting funded principal.
Population loss in a listed state changes its current assessment; the auction owner applies its existing deterioration or capability rule.
Cancellation neither restores those people nor adds deaths.

[Occupation Revolt's force rules](../095_occupation_revolt_specs/095_occupation_revolt_spec_part_04_forces_and_opening.md) use surviving recruits and equipment before opening.
A loss cannot create another cache or become a suppression operation.
After opening, fielded formations remain protected by the existing incident scope.
A returning country preserves active character absence rather than recreating a missing commander from historical setup, with valid command and owner-supported replacement still available.

A future acceptance case restores a country after its commander disappears: valid government returns, that identity stays absent and no spent cache is replayed.
Population loss changes future recruit potential without retroactively halving an issued army.
The auction buyer, occupier and treaty partner gain no perpetrator attribution from the connection.

## Shared records after recovery

Equipment supplied by Abundance or another aid event becomes ordinary current reserve and can disappear on a later firing.
Its completed grant receipt remains history rather than a perpetual protection or repayment claim.
Research-sharing membership survives unless its owner changes it, while a participant's personal absence follows the character owner.

Population loss reconciles existing migration, famine and reception representations with one physical result.
It does not feed zombie bodies or confirmed-death rewards, and chemical or biological stock loss creates no release merely because a container is missing.
Condemnation, retaliation and world threat require their owners' actual evidence of a later action; the unexplained disappearance supplies no confirmed perpetrator.
