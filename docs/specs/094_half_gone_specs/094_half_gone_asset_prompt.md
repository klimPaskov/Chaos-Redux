## Scene constraints

Depict absence through remaining material, people, and ordinary surroundings. No proven portal, fading body, alien weapon, divine hand, corpse scene, identified culprit, or franchise homage may explain the phenomenon. Material reports may use restrained administrative absurdity. Human loss uses a sober civic or personal register. The super-event needs a distinct wide composition, not an enlarged report crop.
