# Equipment and reserves

## Every current stockpile matters

The equipment loss covers actual positive quantities held in reserve at the instant of the incident. Coverage includes base-game equipment, active DLC equipment, country-specific equipment, captured and imported equipment, obsolete models, designer variants, and compatible Chaos Redux equipment. A country's inability to manufacture an item does not protect an item it already owns.

The loss is proportional within each distinguishable stockpile entry. Ten thousand rifles and twenty obsolete tanks do not form one combined pool from which the event can remove only rifles. Both reserves lose half. Different variants and origins remain represented accurately, rather than replacing a captured vehicle with a domestic approximation or removing the newest model to stand in for several older ones.

This makes the event sensitive to a country's real history. A modern army with a thin reserve of advanced equipment loses that reserve. A country living from captured stocks loses captured stocks. An eccentric Chaos Redux arsenal loses the equipment it actually stores, not a standard package chosen because it is easy to enumerate.

The discovery of an unsupported equipment family during implementation is a coverage defect. It must not be concealed by reducing a few familiar types and describing the result as half of all equipment.

## Half of an indivisible thing

For values with adequate fractional precision, the target remaining amount is exactly one half of the pre-incident value. Percentage values use their real current percentage, not a fixed subtraction of fifty percentage points.

Whole equipment and individual people require a rounding rule. The proposed rule is unbiased rounding at the smallest independently counted inventory or roster boundary. For an odd count, either neighboring whole-number loss is equally likely. A stockpile of five loses two or three. A group of one can lose its only member or lose nobody. The choice is made once for that incident and cannot be rerolled by reopening the report or reloading a save after the result was committed.

This avoids permanently protecting every last rifle or every sole commander. It also avoids always rounding the loss upward and turning repeated halving into automatic extinction of every tiny stock. Actual measured losses are reported, even when a small category happens to lose nothing after rounding.

Population uses coordinated state rounding, described in Part 4, so a country with many small states does not suffer a systematic extra loss. Tuning conventions such as prices in multiples of five never override the actual half calculation. A reserve of ninety-five does not lose fifty merely to produce a round-looking tooltip.

## Stored, committed, and delivered goods

Free reserves are exposed. Already fielded equipment is excluded. Equipment in a production queue is not finished reserve equipment. A future lend-lease promise is not a physical stockpile, while equipment already received and stored by the recipient belongs to that recipient's reserve.

Transfers need a single ownership boundary. Goods are either still held by the sender, already held by the recipient, or committed to a transport or operation owner. The event must not find two accounting records and remove the same goods from both countries. The country selected for Half gone remains the only direct target.

Existing operations may reserve equipment before execution. Such equipment follows the established owner contract for that reservation. If it has actually left the free stockpile, the baseline cannot remove it a second time. If it remains free stock with an advisory allocation only, it remains vulnerable. The operation owner must then decide whether its preparation can continue with the remaining equipment.

No automatic refund recreates equipment which genuinely vanished from free reserve. No disappearance cancels an unrelated operation merely to get access to already committed equipment. The distinction is resolved from real ownership and tested behavior, rather than a special exemption invented for every integration.

## Trains and convoys

The user's reserve examples include trains and convoys. These names must be supported where the country genuinely has independently stored or accumulated quantities. They do not authorize a second loss from a stock already included in the baseline equipment transaction.

When train reserve equipment was halved with the other stored equipment, that same reserve cannot be selected again as an Evolution I extra. If a compatible owner exposes a separate free convoy reserve which the baseline does not touch, it can be a reserve candidate. Assigned transports and active logistics equipment remain outside the baseline unless they are genuinely represented as free stored stock.

The player should see ordinary names such as train reserves or free convoys. They should not need to understand a hidden accounting distinction. The distinction exists to make the visible claim accurate and prevent a supposed half-loss from becoming a quarter remaining.

## Unusual military stores

Actual chemical, biological, nuclear-related, robotic, creature, or other special equipment stocks remain equipment where their owning systems define them that way. Their disappearance does not automatically constitute their use. A missing chemical container is not a confirmed atmospheric release. A missing pathogen stock is not an outbreak. Missing nuclear-related equipment is not a detonation.

Existing owners retain responsibility for any genuinely independent aftermath. A hazardous system may react to a verified accounting loss if its established rules provide such a reaction. Half gone does not manufacture an accident merely because the equipment was dangerous.

Living formations and characters cannot be counted once as reserve equipment and again as population or named people. An owner which represents one entity on several surfaces must declare which representation is authoritative. Where that cannot yet be established, the integration remains blocked rather than being approximated by killing or spawning another object.

## Accumulated national reserves

Evolution I adds one reserve category where a positive compatible reserve exists. The standard examples are fuel, Political Power, Command Power, Army Experience, Navy Experience, and Air Experience. A later owner may expose additional accumulated national resources with the same clear meaning.

Each named experience pool is its own possible category. Losing Army Experience does not silently reduce trained divisions or remove purchased doctrines. Losing Political Power does not dismiss the government or undo laws. Losing fuel does not change fuel capacity or destroy refineries. The event takes the current reserve and leaves its sources of future generation intact.

An owner-defined resource joins the pool only after the country actually possesses that mechanic. Half gone does not open a secret project, create a currency, activate a crisis, or set another event's fired state in order to find something to remove.

Positive, spendable or otherwise useful quantities are the default reserve space. A negative Political Power balance is a debt, not a positive reserve that should improve when halved. Internal dates, sequence numbers, debug counters, proof flags, and arbitrary numeric variables are not national resources. The event must never treat a global Deaths total or world Chaos value as something this country owns.

## Recovery does not rewrite the loss

Factories continue producing. Fuel continues arriving. Political and military resources continue accumulating through their normal systems. This ordinary recovery is part of the strategic experience, because the loss changes what the country can safely spend or deploy next.

A receipt of the incident can retain the before and after amounts for explanation, owner responses, and achievements. It does not reserve a permanent entitlement to replacement goods. A second incident reads the new stockpile even when the previous aftermath is still visible.

For example, 100 units become 50. Production raises the stock to 80. A later incident leaves 40. Without production, a later incident would leave 25. The event never removes another 50 because the country once started with 100.
