# Super-Event Scope Decision

## Decision

No super-event is included in the Event 19 source design.

## Reason

The event is Minor Repeatable. Its evolutions deepen a recurring global military anomaly, and its triggerable scenario creates immediate wars, but none of the normal thresholds necessarily represents a singular campaign-defining reveal, world-order transformation, global defeat, or terminal state.

A super-event would add a unique image, quote, cultural remark, licensed audio, sound definitions, slot wiring, documentation, and spreadsheet work without a sufficiently specific campaign threshold.

## Future reconsideration gate

A separate improvement proposal can reconsider a super-event only when a derivative country:

- becomes a genuine global power
- controls a defined large share of the world or multiple continents
- creates a new world order or shared world-threat state
- reaches a unique final identity not shared by ordinary derivative actors

That future super-event must belong to the actual global threshold, not fire merely because Evolution IV unlocked or one derivative state appeared.

## Current package consequence

- no super-event prompt file
- no quote research
- no cultural remark research
- no audio research or licensed track
- no super-event image
- no super-event slot or audio ID requirement

Normal report and news event assets remain required.
