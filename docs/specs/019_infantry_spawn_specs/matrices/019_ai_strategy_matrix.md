# Event 19 AI Strategy Matrix

## Ordinary country policy profiles

| Profile | Selection factors | Formation requests | Lot disposition | Claimant policy | Anomalous policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Professionalizer | stable, industrial, adequate equipment, high control | few and purpose-specific | standardize and integrate | co-opt or counter-command | one controlled family at most |
| Territorialist | long border, low supply, defensive posture | territorial requests | garrison and local roles | limited district command | cantonment and restricted deployment |
| Wartime Opportunist | losing war, capital threat, force deficit | aggressive until congestion critical | emergency integration | temporary concession | accepts risk if survival requires it |
| Gambler | high chaos, unstable government, strong risk tolerance | Ask for Anything and anomalous draws | preserves strange lots | empowers useful claimant | high saturation tolerance |
| Panic Demobilizer | peace, low equipment, high congestion | rarely requests | dissolves weak and advanced burdens | refuses or arrests | purges or isolates |
| Claimant Appeaser | weak government, high claimant influence | follows claimant requests | assigns loyal lots | grants command and office | claimant-controlled families likely |
| Counter-Command State | strong civilian or rival military institutions | moderate | integrates only after audit | reassigns units and secures depots | denies claimant access |
| Anomalous Exploiter | high chaos, compatible family, military need | family-biased | preserves family lots | may appoint family claimant | trains or spawns within aggressive limits |
| Anomalous Containment State | stable, high awareness, low immediate threat | no anomalous requests | cantonment or dissolution | blocks family command | lowers saturation and seals breach |

## Profile transition triggers

| Campaign change | Likely transition |
| --- | --- |
| Capital becomes threatened | Professionalizer or Territorialist to Wartime Opportunist |
| Congestion reaches critical | Opportunist or Gambler to Panic Demobilizer or containment |
| Claimant wins major battles | Professionalizer to appeasement or counter-command |
| Claimant loses badly | Appeasement to discrediting or arrest |
| Peace after emergency mobilization | Opportunist to professionalizer or demobilizer |
| Saturation reaches dominant band | Exploiter to containment unless high-chaos or desperate |
| Former parent of derivative collapses | Derivative AI shifts from survival to regional expansion |

## Request willingness factors

| Factor | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Enemy division superiority | raises request willingness |
| Capital threat | strongly raises willingness |
| Low supply | lowers heavy and mobile requests |
| Low fuel | lowers mobile and armor requests |
| High equipment stockpile | raises specialist and standardization willingness |
| High congestion | lowers all requests except desperate emergency |
| Prior requests this generation | lowers willingness through escalating cost |
| High Muster Control | raises disciplined request willingness |

| High saturation | lowers anomalous requests except Exploiter or desperate AI |

## Claimant AI reactions

| State | Preferred action | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Moderate influence, wartime need | formal bounded command | unconditional national power |

## AI blockers

The AI must receive weight zero or hidden actions when:

- target country or state no longer exists
- selected lot is invalid or already resolved
- claimant is dead, removed, annexed, or no longer relevant
- family is not registered or locally eligible
- a spawn-only family is presented as trainable
- no valid dynamic-country territory exists
- country has one state and the action assumes partition
- route requires an unavailable supply corridor or impossible naval access
- parent-event isolation cannot be guaranteed
- event or evolution is disabled
- a terminal world state makes the action incompatible
