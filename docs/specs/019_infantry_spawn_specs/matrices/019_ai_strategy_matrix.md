| Profile | Selection factors | Formation requests | Lot disposition | Claimant policy | Anomalous policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Professionalizer | stable, industrial, adequate equipment, high control | few and purpose-specific | standardize and integrate | co-opt or counter-command | one controlled family at most |

| Wartime Opportunist | losing war, capital threat, force deficit | aggressive until congestion critical | emergency integration | temporary concession | accepts risk if survival requires it |

## Profile transition triggers

| Campaign change | Likely transition |
| --- | --- |
| Capital becomes threatened | Professionalizer or Territorialist to Wartime Opportunist |
| Congestion reaches critical | Opportunist or Gambler to Panic Demobilizer or containment |
| Claimant wins major battles | Professionalizer to appeasement or counter-command |
| Claimant loses badly | Appeasement to discrediting or arrest |
| Peace after emergency mobilization | Opportunist to professionalizer or demobilizer |
| Saturation reaches dominant band | Exploiter to containment unless high-chaos or desperate |
| Former parent of derivative collapses | Derivative AI shifts from survival to regional expansion |

## Evolution II prototype disposition

AI prototype handling evaluates every active unmanaged advanced lot through the
same exact-ledger gates used by the selected-lot player actions.

| Condition | Preservation weight | Cannibalization weight | Guard |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Major country or adequate military/civilian industry | high | low | full preservation payment and exact live cohort must exist |
| Strongest sustainable prototype, adequate fuel, manageable congestion | high | low | only one maintenance trial may run |
| Severe Army Congestion | low | high | no substitution if the selected lot fails proof |
| Fuel below one quarter | zero | high | preservation cannot consume unaffordable fuel |
| Severe equipment debt | low | high | only settled, unit-backed obligations become salvage |
| Capital-adjacent wartime threat | situational | zero | AI does not dismantle an advanced formation during immediate capital defense |
| Preserved prototype later becomes unsustainable | none | medium to high | preserved disposition remains cannibalizable through the same exact teardown |

Successful preservation adds a restricted-reinforcement national burden and one
finite profile-specific research insight. Cannibalization never counts as
supervised demobilization and never receives its achievement credit.

shared governance cooldown. Their AI weights read stability and war state; the
resulting flags activate self-removing AI strategy profiles.

players must prove a consolidated hierarchy, completed family transformation,

centralized recorded-war-victory threshold.

## AI blockers

The AI must receive weight zero or hidden actions when:

- target country or state no longer exists
- selected lot is invalid or already resolved
- claimant is dead, removed, annexed, or no longer relevant
- family is not registered or locally eligible
- a spawn-only family is presented as trainable
- no valid dynamic-country territory exists
- country has one state and the action assumes partition
- route requires an unavailable supply corridor or impossible naval access
- parent-event isolation cannot be guaranteed
- event or evolution is disabled
- a terminal world state makes the action incompatible
