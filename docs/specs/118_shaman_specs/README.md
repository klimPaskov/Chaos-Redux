## The playable promise

A culturally specific regional visitor approaches the major that directly controls their home.
The government can welcome them, peacefully refuse, or persecute them at the risk of local reaction.
Welcome opens one paid rite.
Land aid can address food shortage, civilian disease, active disaster relief or damaged industry.
Military guidance serves an actual nearby front through temporary local supply, movement, attrition and defensive preparation.

One public Local Cooperation value records the relationship.
Hidden capability remains uncertain and uses the same distribution across all six regional profiles.
The ordinary workable rite mixture is 14.5 percent exceptional, 46.5 percent effective, 26.5 percent inconclusive and 12.5 percent adverse.
Urgency saves work time while moving five percentage points from effective to adverse.
These are proposed design calculations, not measured campaign balance.

At Chaos 400+, a welcomed contact can develop True Sight.
It offers up to three state-specific visions, with one active warning and 120-day cooldowns.
Natural visions observe existing Event 013 jobs, add a concrete consequence hypothesis beyond the official forecast and permit limited or full preparation.
Military visions are conditional frontier warnings.
Evidence, payment, prevention, false alarms and unobservable outcomes have distinct rules.

The design is finite: one global opening, one original host, one home identity, one base rite, one persistent public value and one evolution.
Its depth comes from allocating aid, judging uncertainty, paying for preparation and managing real local consequences.
