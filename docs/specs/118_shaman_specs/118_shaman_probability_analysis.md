## Protection arithmetic

S3 specifies credit C = min(rD, max(0, D − 0.25B)), with r equal to 0.10 or 0.25.
Assuming the owner supplies nonnegative B and D, C is nonnegative and never exceeds D.
The final request D − C never exceeds D.
When D is at least 0.25B, the final request is at least 0.25B.
When the owner has already reduced D below that floor, Shaman adds zero and preserves the stronger owner result.

| Existing residual D/B | Limited final request/B | Full final request/B | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
