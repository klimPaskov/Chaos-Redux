## Durable record and invariants

One encounter record owns original host ID and incarnation, home state, regional profile, generation, authority epoch, current phase, Local Cooperation, backing context, hidden capability, rite entitlement and issued-vision count.
One active action owns action generation, target set, mode, pace, quote version, transport mode, exact stock debits, dispatched and held balances, industrial-burden identity, actual work days, original due dates, cached outcome and terminal disposition.

An active vision additionally owns source family, exact source tuple, component hypothesis, meaningful threshold, issue date, window, source revision, inquiry receipt, the limited/full quote pair, upgrade state, owner credit and final adjudication.
Event targets can carry synchronous scope.
They are not the sole identity for delayed work.

All callers initialize consumed temporary outputs before calling helpers.
Use private nested-loop variables.
Retain original actor and state identity until scoped removal succeeds or is explicitly recorded unresolved.
Never clear the pointers first and lose the ability to retire the old modifier.

The invariants are one campaign opening, one rite commitment, one active vision, at most three issued visions, one source credit per physical job, no hidden reroll, no double payment or refund, no transferred bargain, and no extension of absolute expiry through a callback.
The bounded daily military presence check exists only while its exact paid objective is active.
