# Shaman, Part 2: The one rite

All action and outcome labels are working labels, not final localisation.
Numeric values are proposed initial tuning.
Source-backed primitives and unverified engine bindings are distinguished in the implementation handoff.

## Choosing and paying for a rite

The government chooses one family, one target and one pace.
A land rite affects one eligible state.
Military guidance affects one focus state and up to two eligible adjacent states in the home region.
A successful commitment consumes the single rite entitlement.
Outcome reports, control changes, save reloads and later cooperation changes cannot restore that entitlement or reroll its result.

Normal preparation takes 21 days.
Urgent preparation takes 7 days and moves five outcome-weight points from effective to adverse after the cooperation adjustment.
The urgent path exists for an imminent local threat.
It is offered only when a real regional danger falls within 21 days, or the chosen military state borders an enemy during an active war.
A fictional deadline cannot unlock it.

These are short paid work periods tied to a visiting practitioner and an immediate local problem.
They are explicit exceptions to the ordinary 90-day mission guidance.
The 90-day selection window remains separate.
The player sees both the last day to commit and the rite's scheduled completion date.

### Material commitment

Define project scale S at confirmation.

| Rite | S |
| --- | --- |
| Land blessing | Ceiling of current civilian population divided by 2 million, minimum 1 and maximum 3 |
| Warrior guidance | Number of selected states, from 1 through 3 |

The ordinary quote is 100 × S support equipment, 25 × S trucks, and a +5 percent × S consumer-goods-factor burden for the preparation period.
The same ordinary material amounts apply to normal and urgent work.
The shorter urgent production commitment is part of its speed advantage and is offset by its additional outcome risk.
Shared price reductions affect eligible paid stockpiles exactly once.
The production burden follows Event 013's observed timed consumer-goods-factor route.
It is a factor change, not a claim that exactly 5 percent of all factories or a fixed number of factories is reserved.
Show its estimated current industrial consequence separately from the authoritative factor and duration.

A verified local transport mode determines whether trucks are appropriate.
Motor transport requires the profile's reviewed road-access condition and at least two infrastructure levels in the target.
Otherwise replace each quoted truck with two additional support-equipment units, with the same work duration and production burden.
That package represents portable supplies, shelter and locally carried equipment.
It does not create a new animal, boat or porter stockpile.
Freeze this mode before payment.
A remote delta, taiga or upland settlement is never assigned a truck requirement solely for cost uniformity.

The entire event family uses four spendable cost types: political power, support equipment, trucks and civilian production commitment.
Troops standing in a state are an operational commitment, not a manpower payment.
Cost rows show at most three inline icon/value entries.
Availability, AI affordability, the confirmation and the debit use the same saved quote.

At confirmation, revalidate the actor, target, generation, rite entitlement, selected pace, stockpiles and the current production-commitment gate.
Freeze the context, exact quote, hidden outcome and due date.
Then publish the payment receipt and make the transaction.
Failure before commitment charges nothing.
Partial debit failure rolls back measured debits from this transaction and clears its production burden.
It cannot roll back a previous transaction.

The full quoted equipment is removed from the available stockpile once at commitment.
Half of each measured paid amount is classified as consumed at dispatch, rounded up.
The remainder is recorded in refundable escrow for completion.
Held equipment is already debited and cannot be spent elsewhere.
It is not a second removal at completion.
A player cancellation or authority loss before completion returns the held half exactly once and removes its remaining production burden.
The dispatched half and elapsed production commitment are spent.
Completion consumes the held half.
A resolved inconclusive or adverse rite is a completed attempt and does not refund its materials.

If the selected crisis disappears before completion, the land rite follows its stated conversion rule below.
It never changes state or family automatically.
A target lost to another controller cancels its undelivered work.
Losing the military focus state cancels the entire guidance package.
Losing only a supporting state cancels that state's work while the original focus and any remaining valid supporting state can complete.
At commitment, split the measured dispatched balance and the measured held balance of each equipment type separately and equally across the selected states.
Assign integer remainders by ascending state ID and freeze the allocation.
Cancellation refunds only the exact held allocation belonging to canceled work.
The original outcome and expiry remain unchanged.

Refunds belong to the original paying country.
If that country has ceased to exist before a refund can be applied, its held balance is forfeited and the receipt closes.
A conqueror, released country or later reuse of the same tag does not inherit the refund.
A still-existing payer that loses the target can receive its eligible held refund once.

## Hidden capability and outcome weights

On welcome, make one hidden encounter draw: 20 percent deceptive claimant, 50 percent capable local practitioner, and 30 percent extraordinary practitioner.
These are internal interpretations used to generate uncertainty.
They are not public biographies or assertions about real traditions.
Every regional profile uses the same distribution.

The government never sees an identity classification, a guaranteed authenticity test, or a culture-specific supernatural probability.
A fraud can accidentally help.
Practical knowledge can produce an exceptional result.
A genuine supernatural practitioner can fail.

The base outcome pool has 100 weight points.

| Hidden interpretation | Exceptional | Effective | Inconclusive | Adverse |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Deceptive claimant | 0 | 15 | 55 | 30 |
| Capable local practitioner | 5 | 60 | 25 | 10 |
| Extraordinary practitioner | 40 | 45 | 10 | 5 |

Strong Local Cooperation moves five points from inconclusive to effective.
Strained cooperation moves ten points from effective to adverse.
Workable cooperation makes no change.
Then urgent preparation moves five more points from effective to adverse.
Every final row still sums to 100 and remains nonnegative.

With workable cooperation and normal pace, the mixed encounter pool is 14.5 percent exceptional, 46.5 percent effective, 26.5 percent inconclusive and 12.5 percent adverse.
With strong cooperation, it becomes 14.5, 51.5, 21.5 and 12.5.
With strained cooperation it becomes 14.5, 36.5, 26.5 and 22.5.
Urgency moves another five points from effective to adverse in each mixture.
These are exact calculations for this proposed pool, not measured game balance.

The final outcome is drawn once at commitment and stored.
The player sees qualitative risk direction and the actual material commitment.
Detailed hidden identity weights are developer data.
Current conditions can determine whether an already selected effect can still be delivered, but cannot replace its outcome with a new random draw.

## Bless the land

The player selects a state and one eligible need.
Where several crises overlap, the panel makes the choice explicit.
Food, civilian disease, active disaster relief and physical recovery are separate modes.
One rite does not automatically resolve every overlapping crisis.
The default recommendation follows actual severity and actionable owner data.
Calm-state presentation is valid only when the selected state has no eligible active civilian crisis.
A government may still choose a different calm state while another regional state needs aid, as the allocation tradeoff in Part 1 describes.
A state with an active crisis is never labeled calm merely because the player chooses food reserves instead of addressing that crisis.

The ordinary resource work is real and its cost is visible.
An inconclusive supernatural result can still leave a small useful delivery.
Exceptional results are substantially stronger.
An adverse result can spoil supplies, interrupt recovery or worsen a bounded local problem, without erasing unrelated aid or resurrecting earlier casualties.

### Food shortage and seasonal harvest

Use the famine owner's current daily food need and thousand-person-day reserve units.
The selected state's need at commitment fixes the total budget.
Population changes do not enlarge that budget after payment.
A new owner-held credit releases food only into actual reserve headroom and records the amount added.

| Outcome | Total credit | Release window | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Exceptional | 35 days of the frozen daily need | 120 days | A major local harvest or recovery of food supply |
| Effective | 21 days of the frozen daily need | 90 days | A substantial local food reserve |
| Inconclusive | 3 days of the frozen daily need | 30 days | The conventional supplies arrive, with no clear extraordinary effect |
| Adverse | No positive credit, plus bounded spoilage | One measured transaction | The attempt damages the usable harvest or relief stores |

Immediate additions clamp to the actual famine reserve capacity.
Unused positive credit remains with the same state and source receipt until there is headroom, using the famine owner's existing bounded processing.
It expires at the stated date.
It cannot be sold, transferred to another state, converted into population, or added a second time after save loading.
The reserve-credit wrapper is required new owner integration, not an already existing API.

The adverse result removes up to seven days of current need from actual existing reserves, capped at 25 percent of reserves immediately before the loss.
It records the measured amount.
It does not request a negative pressure value, declare a famine stage by hand, or deduct population.
Later scarcity and any measured mortality remain the famine owner's responsibility.
A state with no reserves cannot lose food it did not have.
Its adverse draw becomes neutral wasted work, with no harmful modifier, cooperation change or Chaos award.
No unspecified harvest penalty is added to manufacture a consequence.

This food mode also serves calm states.
The player sees their current reserve headroom and the future release window.
A full store receives no immediate increase.
The saved seasonal credit can refill it as supplies are consumed.
If projected need and capacity would make the entire package unusable, the target is not recommended and the confirmation explains the expected unused share.
A wholly unusable zero-need state is ineligible.

The benefit acts through the famine model.
Twenty-one added reserve days can materially improve food security, subject to logistics and the model's caps.
It does not guarantee an immediate stage cure.
Existing 14 to 30 day recovery clocks continue to run.
A blockade fact is cleared only by the owner of a proven opened route.
A blessing alone does not manufacture transport access.

### Civilian disease

This mode requires a real, active, state-addressable civilian outbreak with an owner that can accept an exact assistance receipt.
The initial required owner integration is Event 020 Black Plague where its state records are valid.
A queued Event 013 disease follow-up can instead be addressed through its own prevention objective.
Illness in military divisions is outside this civilian mode's target selector.

The result is finite protection of future disease consequences.

| Outcome | Requested owner effect |
| --- | --- |
| Exceptional | Reduce the selected outbreak's otherwise applicable civilian mortality by 40 percent for 60 days, and add one owner-approved treatment contribution toward recovery |
| Effective | Reduce that outbreak's otherwise applicable civilian mortality by 25 percent for 45 days |
| Inconclusive | Reduce that outbreak's otherwise applicable civilian mortality by 10 percent for 14 days through conventional support |
| Adverse | Treatment is disrupted for 14 days, increasing the selected outbreak's otherwise applicable mortality by 10 percent, within the owner's total caps |

Percentages apply to the outbreak owner's calculated future consequence, not to total state population.
The mitigation cannot affect unrelated disease sources, make mortality negative, remove historic deaths, or override a source's absolute immunity or eligibility rules.
The adverse multiplier cannot create an outbreak or a death where the owner computes none.

Other assistance combines through the disease owner's cap.
Event 118 does not remove an idea and claim a cure.
The owner reports positive accepted mitigation and modelled consequence reduction or actual treatment progress before an achievement can use it.
The arithmetic reduction in a modelled loss request is distinct from the measured population debit.
The owner calculates both in the same transaction without rerunning a random draw.
Reports cannot call the arithmetic difference an observed count of lives saved.

If the outbreak ends naturally before the rite completes, an exceptional, effective or inconclusive draw becomes a 30-day medical readiness credit on the same state for the same registered civilian disease family, preserving its original protection percentage.
An adverse draw becomes a wasted medical dispatch with no readiness, no new illness and no added mortality.
The readiness window ends 30 days after rite completion.
A qualifying recurrence consumes the readiness credit and receives the saved mitigation percentage only for the remaining part of that same 30-day window.
It does not start a fresh 45-day or 60-day protection period.
Otherwise the readiness expires unused.
It does not open a new outbreak.
A missing disease adapter must disable this mode with a specific reason during implementation.
The specification requires this integration to be completed for full feature acceptance, rather than silently treating famine relief as disease treatment.

### Active disaster relief

A live Event 013 card in the selected state can receive work toward its current rescue, stabilization or reconstruction phase.
Effective relief contributes two owner work units, exceptional relief three, and inconclusive relief one, clamped to the remaining meaningful work.
Existing phase thresholds are three for rescue, four for stabilization and five for reconstruction.
These source values make the contribution substantial, while final progression remains with the disaster owner.

An adverse result adds no work and causes 14 days of event-owned local relief disruption at -10 percent civilian repair speed.
It does not delete another action's progress, reopen a completed card or fabricate disaster casualties.
The regular disaster system continues to adjudicate any missed deadline.

Bind the card's incident identity at commitment.
At completion the owner may contribute to that same card's current phase, if it remains open and has a meaningful shortfall.
A changed phase within the same card is valid and is reported.
A merged replacement sequence, closed card or unrelated new disaster is not a replacement target.
A no-longer-needed card follows the same three-day food conversion or held-equipment refund as the physical-recovery mode.

No credited work sets a named search, clean-water, evacuation, chain-prevention or reconstruction-action flag unless that action actually occurred.
A contribution can improve the owner's reassessment without satisfying an unperformed physical objective.
Aftercare for an adverse or inconclusive disaster-relief result contributes one work unit to the same still-open card and removes only its remaining event-owned disruption.

### Physical recovery

This mode targets actual damaged civilian industry in the selected state.
If the installed engine verifies distinct state-local repair fields for infrastructure or transport, the owner may include those exact supported categories in the same selector.
The confirmation lists the supported damaged buildings and never advertises a generic reconstruction pool.

The effective result grants 60 service-days at +50 percent local civilian-factory repair speed.
The exceptional result grants 120 service-days at +75 percent.
An inconclusive result grants 14 service-days at +25 percent.
These are timed acceleration windows.
They do not certify a quantity of completed repairs or allocate factories by themselves.
The player must continue ordinary reconstruction, and competing construction priorities still matter.

The adverse result produces a 30-day, 25 percent reduction in the same local civilian repair-speed field.
It does not destroy a newly built factory or undo repair paid for by another system.
Aftercare can remove this event-owned disruption.
The final field, holder and beneficiary must pass installed documentation and state-bound execution checks before this mode is enabled.

The service receipt preserves its original end date.
Each day's presence consumes time whether the government uses the repair opportunity or not.
Expiry cannot create new building levels.
If all eligible damage is repaired early, end the service without moving its remaining days elsewhere.
Physical repairs remain completed.
A report can describe observed damage reduction, but cannot attribute every repaired point to Shaman when other construction also contributed.

Where an Event 013 recovery card owns the damage, the ordinary reconstruction owner continues to determine real progress.
A repair-speed modifier never automatically completes a rescue, stabilization or reconstruction mission.
That distinction also governs achievements.

If damage is gone before the rite completes, convert an exceptional, effective or inconclusive draw to the food mode's inconclusive three-day reserve credit on the same state, provided it has valid daily food need.
Otherwise report that the work is no longer needed and return the still-held equipment half.
An adverse draw becomes wasted work and grants no positive reserve conversion or new repair penalty on an undamaged site.
Do not retarget or roll again.

## Guide the warriors

The government must be at war and the selected focus state must border a state controlled by a current land enemy, or have an actual ongoing land battle recognized by an approved source.
At least two host-controlled land divisions with at least 50 percent strength must be present in the selected set at commitment.
Expeditionary or allied forces do not satisfy that payment-free commitment unless they are actually under the host's operational control and the installed scope query can prove it.

The selected set is one focus state plus up to two adjacent states in the same reviewed region.
The government can choose fewer states to reduce the material quote.
The troops are not teleported or given scripted battle orders.
The player moves them using normal controls.
AI uses normal front assignment strategies where the installed engine supports the exact target.

### Temporary benefit

The following state-local targets are the preferred tuning envelope.
Supply is a flat local-supply amount, not a percentage.

| Outcome | Local supply | Movement for the controller | Attrition for the controller | Defensive preparation | Duration |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Exceptional | +4 | +20 percent | -25 percent | +4 maximum entrenchment after scope validation | 120 days |
| Effective | +2 | +10 percent | -15 percent | +2 maximum entrenchment after scope validation | 90 days |
| Inconclusive | +1 | No change | No change | No extra preparation | 30 days |
| Adverse | No supply change | -10 percent | +10 percent | Existing entrenchment is not deleted | 30 days |

The repository already uses state `local_supplies`, `army_speed_factor_for_controller` and `attrition_for_controller` fields.
Its offline modifier documentation marks maximum entrenchment as state applicable.
Exact installed behavior, beneficiary binding and movement across the state border remain verification gates.
This package does not present a country bonus as a proven local implementation.

If state-scoped maximum entrenchment fails the exact installed check, use the preselected defensive-preparation implementation: +40 percent state land-fort construction speed for effective guidance or +60 percent for exceptional guidance, for the same duration.
The government still pays and performs real fort construction.
Any finished fort remains ordinary physical infrastructure after the temporary preparation window ends.
This alternative preserves the intended local preparation role and must be visible in the final effect text.
It cannot be substituted silently after release.

The controller-specific movement and attrition fields, one exact original-actor binding, and current direct-control validation must all agree.
Remove the event modifier immediately when that actor loses the state.
A new controller receives no inherited magical guidance.
Local supply can also assist allied forces using the state's normal supply network.
That practical coalition benefit is disclosed.
It does not grant the host a global supply reduction.

The complete set begins at rite completion and shares one immutable expiry.
Entering a supported state gains the applicable local effects.
Leaving loses them.
No permanent unit bonus, hidden country-wide combat modifier, technology grant or division cloning can implement this contract.
The coding acceptance matrix must test inside, outside, adjacent, allied and newly captured states separately.

### Dangerous guidance

An adverse result represents misleading routes, exposure and poor preparation.
It applies the bounded state-local movement and attrition penalties above.
It does not force an army into an enemy province, invent a battle, delete divisions, select the player's battle plans, or deduct military casualties directly.
Ordinary movement, combat and attrition remain the actual sources of losses.

The player can end the dangerous-guidance modifier immediately by abandoning the active guidance package.
This releases no spent material and closes its remaining benefits.
A paid 14-day aftercare option is available if the government wants to repair local cooperation while recovering from the failure.
It is not necessary to keep an adverse modifier running to obtain that opportunity.

If war ends before the rite completes, the military result becomes local logistical readiness on the already selected states: the same supply component for 30 days and no movement, attrition or entrenchment component.
An adverse result becomes a wasted deployment, with no new peacetime attrition penalty.
Report the conversion openly.
No reroll or second rite follows.

## Aftercare

An adverse or inconclusive rite opens one optional follow-up for 30 days.
The government can leave the matter alone or fund practical aftercare.
The latter costs 50 political power and 50 × S support equipment, with a 14-day work period.
At strong cooperation the political-power quote is 40.
At strained cooperation it is 60.
Shared cost rules then apply.

Aftercare is conventional work.
It cannot repeat the rite or improve its hidden identity.
On completion it adds 10 Local Cooperation once.
It releases a three-day food reserve credit after a food failure, adds seven days of 10 percent mortality mitigation to the same still-active disease outbreak, supplies one owner work unit after an active-disaster relief failure, supplies 14 service-days at +25 percent local civilian-factory repair speed after a physical-recovery failure, or removes any remaining event-owned adverse-guidance modifier after a military failure.
It can also halve remaining event-owned local disruption from the original rite.
These are mode-specific alternatives, not cumulative rewards.

Aftercare equipment is consumed on commitment.
Its political power is nonrefundable once the work starts.
A stale or invalid request charges nothing.
Authority loss cancels it without success, removes any event-owned production burden and leaves physical work already performed intact.
A missing target produces an explicit closed result.
A government cannot start aftercare twice by reopening a result report.

## Observed outcome and disclosure

Every resolution distinguishes the selected outcome from the actual delivered effect.
The report names the state, the paid work, the measured food or repair delivered, the active temporary effect and its expiry, and any unused credit.
Disease reports distinguish future mitigation from historic casualties.
Military reports identify the exact states and the circumstances under which benefits apply.

Narrative direction follows evidence.
Exceptional prose may suggest an impossible coincidence or a result that officials cannot explain.
Practical success can credit knowledge and cooperation.
Inconclusive prose acknowledges useful ordinary deliveries where they occurred.
Adverse prose names the harm without assigning a moral defect to a whole community.
The account never exposes internal identity flags as established truth.

## Converted and undeliverable outcomes

The stored draw remains an internal historical fact.
Cooperation, Chaos, achievements and the result description use the delivered classification.
A reduced three-day food conversion uses the inconclusive delivered class.
A positive food escrow or medical readiness credit remains pending until useful delivery or final expiry.
Each rite has one cooperation-settlement receipt.
Settle it at the first actual useful owner delivery using the applicable delivered class, or settle neutrally at final expiry if none occurs.
Do not apply an initial inconclusive cooperation penalty and later add a second exceptional reward to the same rite.
A canceled or unusable result is neutral and produces no cooperation or Chaos change.
An adverse draw that finds no applicable harm becomes wasted work, with no cooperation reward and no adverse Chaos award.
It cannot create a positive exceptional result.

An idle or expired credit that never delivers earns no manifestation award.
A partially delivered credit records only its measured delivery and uses the original outcome class for its actual positive effect.
A canceled attempted rite never reopens the consumed one-rite entitlement.
