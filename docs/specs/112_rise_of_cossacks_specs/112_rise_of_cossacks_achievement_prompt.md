# Rise of Cossacks achievement implementation and asset prompt

## Scope, ownership and presentation

Implement exactly eight Event 112 achievements through the current native Chaos Redux achievement system.
Read the current achievement-owning event instructions, AGENTS.md, the accepted thirteen-part specification and the asset prompt before implementation. Use archived source-authority records only for prior acceptance/provenance; do not repeat the completed supplied-source gate.
This file owns their precise design predicates and tracking requirements.
The keys and labels below are working labels, not final titles or final localisation.
Allocate their final identifiers only after inspecting the existing registry and naming rules.

The achievements cover a complete small-host state, a voluntary union under strain, military modernization, the Russian national project, the Golden Horde claim, a productive Ural settlement, successful refuge after loss and the evolved response system.
They do not award the initial event firing or a single ordinary click.
All eight are required for complete implementation.
If production is sequenced, A01, A02, A07 and A08 provide the earliest system-acceptance value, while A03 to A06 remain required route content rather than optional omissions.

Eligible play follows the Event 112 campaign lineage through an authorized human switch into the newly created host, later legitimate country continuity and an accepted restoration or receiving-country continuation.
The initial player may have started as the authority that loses the state or another country.
The native achievement framework decides its usual campaign and multiplayer eligibility, without this package inventing an Ironman or DLC rule.
A debug-only CXT application, test shortcut, synthetic receipt or direct console completion cannot count as a legitimate Event 112 achievement result.
Observe and record the framework's actual policy for cheats and save eligibility rather than assuming an undocumented engine flag.

A player joining an existing host receives its legitimate current institutions, but retrospective counters require actual historical receipts and the native eligibility policy.
Do not manufacture earlier actions from a country's final name or current stockpile.
Each achievement uses a durable once-only completion receipt.
A tag change, capital move, annexation, succession, restored state, renamed focus or changed cosmetic cannot repeat an award.
Disqualifiers are local to the achievement unless the existing framework makes them campaign-wide.

## A01. Winter watch and a functioning regional country

**Working key:** `112_rise_of_cossacks_winter_watch`.

**Title direction:** A restrained image of a host remaining on watch through a full year while its country becomes sustainable.
**Description direction:** State the institutional, military and civilian requirements clearly rather than promising an award for waiting 365 days.
**Visibility:** Visible after a human enters a legitimate Event 112 lineage.
**Difficulty:** Moderate.

**Eligible countries:** The principal host, a later legitimate Event 112 host or its preserved same-lineage successor.

**Unlock conditions:** At least 365 days have passed since that lineage's actual successful rising, with a controlled viable home administration and a ratified basic charter for at least the last 180 consecutive days.
The finite A01 provisional settlement or a fully documented equivalent original settlement must be complete.
The host must hold the full GP08 durable regional or service-autonomy settlement, Cohesion at least 60 and Legitimacy at least 60, and have completed the corresponding first-peace review.
It must retain at least two real owned land formations at 75 percent native equipment and 50 percent organization, one assigned to viable home defense.
Its sustainable arms source must have operated for 90 consecutive days, evidenced either by the actual functioning native arms-production condition or by fulfilled real deliveries under a still-valid contract.
No unresolved material charter breach or unsupported civilian protection promise may remain.

**Disqualifiers and reset rules:** A broken required 180-day constitutional interval restarts that interval, while elapsed lineage age remains historical.
A destroyed arms source suspends the 90-day supply interval.
Capital movement inside a continuously viable government does not reset the year.
Duplicate cached resources or unverified formation creation cannot satisfy the material predicates.

**Why it is substantial:** The player must finance institutions, defend a real force, sustain arms and resolve the transition to peace.
A peaceful negotiated survival is valid if the same actual work is completed.

**Tracking:** Rising date, human eligibility, charter validity intervals, A01 and GP08 receipts, peace-review receipt, arms continuity and a current native roster check.
Use the existing bounded host review plus relevant changes, without another daily world scan.

**Icon:** A sheltered winter watch fire, small tent and restrained mounted-service cue, asset A01 in part 12.

## A02. A union that survives disagreement

**Working key:** `112_rise_of_cossacks_durable_union`.

**Title direction:** Separate banners choosing to remain together after a meaningful test.
**Description direction:** Explain that the union must honor a partner's lawful refusal and subsequently fulfill common obligations.
**Visibility:** Visible when a human-led host first opens GP01.
**Difficulty:** Hard.

**Eligible countries:** A human-led Event 112 host that coordinates or lawfully succeeds to the accepted GP01 union.

**Unlock conditions:** Complete full GP01 with at least three distinct genuine hosts across at least two geographic cohorts and all required ratified charters.
At least one member must then refuse a specific unrelated offensive or demand outside its signed mandate.
The principal must honor that refusal without forced participation, unlawful annexation, fabricated war or a penalty for lawful exit.
After that recorded refusal, the union must fulfill one actual agreed defense, training, transport or measured material obligation involving at least two members and remain in compliance for another 180 days.
Complete its first-peace constitutional review with at least three still-willing qualifying members and no unresolved material federal breach.

**Disqualifiers:** Renamed sub-units of one host do not count as separate members.
A puppet compelled by an incompatible subject rule does not count as a voluntarily ratifying sovereign member.
A deliberately impossible request manufactured solely to obtain a scripted refusal does not qualify.
The request must arise from an actual declared strategic action with real means and a recorded mandate question.
A breach blocks completion until lawfully repaired and a new qualifying compliance interval is fulfilled.

**Tracking:** Stable member IDs, distinct constituency and cohort proofs, actual refusal context, principal response, later obligation receipt and compliance period.
Use one shared breach owner across GP01 and the associated regional program.

**Why it is substantial:** It tests restraint and workable common government rather than three invitations.

**Icon:** Three separate host standards around a council and charter, asset A02.

## A03. Modern forces with a living tradition

**Working key:** `112_rise_of_cossacks_modern_tradition`.

**Title direction:** Modernization that preserves useful inherited methods.
**Description direction:** Name the actual trained force types and the required retained traditional capacity.
**Visibility:** Visible after M01 registration and the first accepted tradition exchange.
**Difficulty:** Hard.

**Eligible countries:** A human-led Event 112 host or its legitimate successor with the same military-program receipts.

**Unlock conditions:** Complete M01 cadres, M02 plastun school and M05 motorization through their actual paid and objective-based programs.
Retain the founding tradition and at least one legitimately adopted second tradition within the two-adoption cap.
Own three distinct field formations at the same time: one native cavalry host formation, one native infantry plastun formation and one native motorized formation using the accepted templates or their audited functional successors.
Each must have at least 85 percent equipment and 60 percent organization, with required motor fuel and a viable home defender remaining available.
The current free-manpower readiness ratio must meet the shared 20 percent requirement.
Maintain Cohesion and Legitimacy at least 60 and complete one 30-day joint exercise or equivalent funded common-standards objective with those actual formations.

**Disqualifiers:** A name alone cannot turn infantry into motorized troops or a cavalry model into a new battalion type.
One formation cannot satisfy two of the three roster roles.
Transferred or generated units with missing equipment, fictitious experience or no proper receipt do not qualify.
A fourth stacked spirit or a third adopted tradition is not required and cannot supply a shortcut.

**Tracking:** Program receipts, adopted tradition IDs, current native templates, actual equipment, organization, manpower, fuel and exercise participation.
A unit's later destruction does not revoke an already valid award.
It prevents a pending award until another legitimately trained unit qualifies.

**Why it is substantial:** It needs technology, production, fuel, manpower, training and real combined arms while retaining a useful mounted tradition.

**Icon:** A saddle and separate rifle with a restrained gear motif, asset A03.

## A04. Cossack Russia after the march

**Working key:** `112_rise_of_cossacks_cossack_russia`.

**Title direction:** A national government accepting the obligations that follow its frontier origins.
**Description direction:** Make the postwar civilian and constitutional test as prominent as the formation.
**Visibility:** Visible when GP02 is discovered through its actual entry route.
**Difficulty:** Very hard.

**Eligible countries:** The legitimate human-led Event 112 Russian successor, including an audited continuity transaction with an existing country.

**Unlock conditions:** Commit the full GP02 formation with its required directly owned and controlled Moscow and civil anchors, integrated local settlements, supplied army and selected civic, military or popular-service-crown constitution.
Complete the first national peace review, including its actual civilian reconstruction or veteran obligation and ratified emergency-command or succession procedure.
Maintain the required national institutions and capital access for 180 consecutive days after that review.
At least one protected local or regional mandate from the pre-formation host must remain honored or have been consensually amended.
The current state must satisfy the chosen GP02 constitution's ordinary meter requirements with no unpaid breach repair or national integration transaction pending.

**Disqualifiers:** A cosmetic change, temporary occupation of Moscow, externally granted duplicate Russia or a title without its citizen and institutional mandate cannot qualify.
The crown version requires the accepted authentic institution or verified living person, never an invented lineage.
National formation and Chaos receipts must not have been replayed through a later constitutional change.

**Tracking:** GP02 formation variant and source lineage, exact geographic proof, civil settlement receipts, preserved local guarantee, peace-review outcome and post-review continuity.

**Why it is substantial:** It rewards both national victory and government after victory across the supported constitutions.

**Icon:** Ataman staff and a researched Moscow architectural cue, asset A04.

## A05. A credible new steppe order

**Working key:** `112_rise_of_cossacks_golden_horde`.

**Title direction:** A revived historical claim that has acquired functioning modern institutions.
**Description direction:** Emphasize distinct real mandates, the Lower Volga base and a first succession or constitutional review.
**Visibility:** Hidden until GP03 is legitimately revealed, then its remaining conditions are inspectable.
**Difficulty:** Very hard and rare.

**Eligible countries:** The legitimate human-led Event 112 GP03 successor.

**Unlock conditions:** Commit the full GP03 formation through the commonwealth or imperial-regency variant, with all required Lower Volga and middle-river evidence, two distinct steppe relationships and three genuine mandates.
At least one mandate must come from the required non Cossack sovereign or substantial independent authority, and no two required mandates may be aliases of the same constituency.
Complete the first-peace revenue, transit, representation and service review, including the agreed succession procedure or renewed executive mandate.
Fulfill one real post-formation material or defense obligation to a non Cossack signatory and maintain the accepted settlement for 180 consecutive days after that receipt.
A rival's continued political disagreement is permissible when the formation's actual sovereignty and required agreements remain valid.

**Disqualifiers:** A limited Volga outcome, fabricated khan, invented dynastic descent, coerced mandate mislabeled voluntary, free medieval-map cores or a cosmetic title alone cannot qualify.
An unresolved breach against a required signatory blocks the compliance interval.
Different cultural labels on one paid cohort cannot count as separate mandates.

**Tracking:** Formation method, authority and cohort IDs, independent-mandate proof, actual geography, postwar agreement and delivered obligation, succession review and compliance dates.

**Why it is substantial:** The route must support its historical claim through government, distinct partners and a sustainable political bargain.

**Icon:** An approved Lower Volga revival banner and congress seal, asset A05.

## A06. The river supports its defenders

**Working key:** `112_rise_of_cossacks_ural_commons`.

**Title direction:** Productive communal rights sustaining a disciplined service force.
**Description direction:** Identify the full commons settlement, maintained passage and respected seasonal rotation.
**Visibility:** Visible when P09 is available.
**Difficulty:** Hard.

**Eligible countries:** A human-led Event 112 host that owns the P09 full-settlement receipt and its relevant local authority.

**Unlock conditions:** Complete full P09 with inclusive resident and affected-neighbor terms, an actual transport or maintenance objective and the trained seasonal patrol.
Maintain an operating accepted outlet, protected productive base and agreed service rotation for 180 consecutive days after full completion.
Complete the first-peace household and industrial-use review.
During that 180-day interval, do not conduct a peacetime military-stores raid, confiscate protected communal rights or divert the same registered personnel into an incompatible permanent deployment.
One genuine counterpart controlling relevant passage must retain its accepted right to refuse a larger unrelated demand.
The host must remain supplied, with Cohesion and Legitimacy at least the full P09 thresholds.

**Disqualifiers:** A fictional fish resource, renamed stockpile, duplicated workforce or forced passage mislabeled consent cannot qualify.
A physical blockade pauses operation and restarts the required continuous interval after lawful restoration, without inventing a political breach by the victim.

**Tracking:** P09 full receipt, exact outlet and counterpart, actual completed work, service roster commitments, review receipt and continuity.

**Why it is substantial:** It rewards a military economy maintained without violating the very rights that support it.

**Icon:** River landing, commons gavel and productive-work emblem, asset A06.

## A07. A country lost and people protected

**Working key:** `112_rise_of_cossacks_safe_sanctuary`.

**Title direction:** Successful protection of a surviving community after the loss of its home.
**Description direction:** State that actual civilians must reach an accepted sanctuary after a real capital or home-loss episode.
**Visibility:** Hidden until a qualifying home-loss or imminent evacuation crisis reveals the route.
**Difficulty:** Hard and uncommon.

**Eligible countries:** The original human-led host, its accepted same-lineage restored government or the explicitly chosen receiving-country continuation under GP08.

**Unlock conditions:** Experience an actual hostile loss of the lineage's viable home capital or an independently verified evacuation emergency before that loss.
Evacuate at least 250 actual civilians from one or more identified source cohorts to a consenting receiver through the shared measured transfer contract.
All civilians counted for this achievement must arrive alive, with reconciled source debit, surviving custody, receiver credit and state and country reception ledgers.
Complete an accepted P11, P12 or GP08 sanctuary settlement, its real housing or livelihood objective and its first-peace protection review.
Maintain safe admission, current resident protections and a viable departure option or accepted permanent citizenship for 180 days after the counted arrival.
Do not draw a second founding cache or recreate the lost army when changing receiver or restoring a state.

**Disqualifiers:** Volunteers alone do not satisfy the civilian threshold.
A fictional population pool, an unmeasured transfer, double-counted arrivals, deliberate friendly state transfer staged as hostile loss or an unsupported landless game state cannot qualify.
Losses in a separate cohort do not rewrite the counted cohort, but the game must still fulfill every surviving protection obligation before award.

**Tracking:** Host-loss cause, authority and lineage, immutable transfer IDs, actual people and deaths, receiver acceptance, safe-arrival and settlement receipts, review and continuity.
The same family cannot count again after onward migration.

**Why it is substantial:** The player must recover from defeat through actual logistics, consent and civilian administration instead of receiving a replacement country for free.

**Icon:** Protected lantern, small boat and refuge arch, asset A07.

## A08. The wider hosts truly answer

**Working key:** `112_rise_of_cossacks_hosts_answer`.

**Title direction:** Distinct communities responding in different ways to one wider resurgence.
**Description direction:** Explain that three different committed response types, regions and actual source authorities are required.
**Visibility:** Visible when the legitimate The Hosts Answer evolution activates for the player lineage.
**Difficulty:** Very hard and rare.

**Eligible countries:** The human-led principal host or its legitimate coordinator successor in the same evolved campaign lineage.

**Unlock conditions:** The evolution must activate through the current tier-two Chaos and instability contract.
Complete three different response types from new independent rising, state defection, measured volunteer contribution, exact formation defection and existing-host compact.
The three counted responses must involve three distinct geographic cohorts and at least two distinct actual source countries or independent authorities.
At least one must be a territorial result and at least one a material or independently ratified institutional result.
Every counted response must have a complete distinct receipt, with resources, state ownership and counterpart authority correctly reconciled.
Maintain a viable principal or lawful coordinator and at least three willing participating host identities for 180 days after the third counted response, within the shared actor and created-host caps.
No unresolved material breach against a counted participant may remain.

**Disqualifiers:** An announced but uncompleted answer, repeated volunteers from the same receipt, a renamed host, a bootstrap counted again as a later response, an over-cap spawned country or a failed roll cannot qualify.
Exact formation defection is the fifth canonical response type after the reviewed dispatch correction in part 07.
It needs its own actual unit and exact native preservation receipt, and a personnel-only fallback cannot count as that type.
The total distinct response requirement is three of the five canonical types.

**Tracking:** Evolution activation receipt, source instability episode, outcome type, authority, cohort, resource or territorial completion receipt, stable participant identity and post-third-response compliance.
Use the actual sequence and cadence rules rather than inferring outcomes from current borders.

**Why it is substantial:** It needs a successful high-instability campaign, varied response mechanics and durable relationships without breaking bounded propagation.

**Icon:** Three distinct signal stations or host standards linked across separate ground, asset A08.

## Native tracking and acceptance

Inspect existing achievement registration, eligibility, once-only completion, save persistence and localisation patterns before allocating any IDs.
Use current domain inspection tools where available and keep these event-owned records separate from shared achievement framework behavior.
Current-state conditions and historical receipt conditions must be explicit in the implementation table.
A minimum time condition refers to a defined start event and a stated uninterrupted or cumulative interval.
A blocked or paused interval must not keep accruing invisibly.

Each achievement needs a positive fixture, a near-miss fixture and a reload or country-transition fixture.
The positive fixture uses actual ordinary Event 112 actions and receipts, not a direct unlock command.
The near-miss changes one decisive requirement, such as a missing real partner, one duplicated cohort, an invalid capital, an unfulfilled service promise or one unconsented receiver.
The transition fixture verifies preserved legitimate history without creating another reward.
Testing registrations in CXT must remain inert until the user deliberately applies them under the existing test-country rules.
A test application does not award the player's real achievement.
