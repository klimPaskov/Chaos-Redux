# Part 10. National focus architecture and progression

## Tree purpose

The focus tree gives the host a readable strategic direction.
Decisions and missions perform the targeted work that direction requires.
The country does not need to finish a focus before the initial rising occurs.
It begins with a functioning government and army, and the tree helps it become a durable state.

The tree has common survival, military, and economic foundations.
A ratified charter selects the country's institutional direction.
Regional programs depend on real traditions and geography.
Practical return and service modules can modify several political routes.
The optional great projects provide larger national or federal campaigns.
Postwar and succession content keeps those achievements playable.

This is a complete group-level architecture.
It deliberately does not prescribe a node-by-node layout or final focus localisation.
The future focus owner expands these accepted groups into a coherent tree after the exact country loading and native engine rules are verified.
Expansion must preserve every specified program, consequence, and endpoint.

## Country loading

A fresh generic host loads this tree immediately on formation.
The selected founding tradition determines its relevant regional branch and contextual presentation.
It does not remove the common survival, economy, or military routes.
Distant opportunities remain in a discoverable index and appear as active content after actual contact.

DHC and KHC require the shared country admission adapter described in part 09.
The focus owner must inspect their meaningful existing content.
A dormant entry can compose or migrate the tree under an accepted owner contract.
An already living partner keeps its own meaningful tree.
An invitation or federation must not replace that tree silently.

Country identity changes normally preserve focus completion and ongoing valid work.
A national formation reveals or enables the corresponding administration group.
It does not reload a fresh tree and repeat all opening rewards.
A full sovereign merger needs a dedicated continuity map for both source trees and their completed effects.

## Layout and pacing

The top of the tree should show the immediate host problem and the common foundation.
The institutional fork appears after the first settlement work.
Military and economic groups remain reachable beside it.
Regional and great-project branches extend below the relevant institutions, with a clear shared postwar group.

The design should not place every possible host and empire in one permanently visible horizontal row.
It should use native branch visibility, available-path treatment, and ordinary scrollable tree structure supported by the actual engine.
A player must be able to find why a route is unavailable.
Hidden future surprises remain absent from the public event premise.

The target for a single ordinary campaign is approximately 35 to 50 meaningful reachable focus steps across its selected path and shared development.
The complete authored tree may contain more because several institutional and national paths are mutually exclusive.
These are pacing targets, not a requirement to inflate the tree with filler.
The focus auditor may consolidate redundant steps while preserving the distinct work and choices.

Common administrative and organizational steps normally take 35 days.
Large institution, doctrine, and national planning steps normally take 70 days.
An emergency focus can be shorter when its exact function justifies it.
Every duration is specified in campaign days and must be bound to the current engine's focus-cost units correctly.

A focus should either make a strategic commitment, unlock a useful program, or resolve a real institutional result.
It should not exist only to award a small quantity of Political Power between two identical decisions.
A required mission can run alongside ordinary focus progress where the game supports that relationship.
The tree should not force the player to idle for an entire training or diplomatic deadline.

## The five-charter fork

The charter menu is civilian council, confederated council, professional ataman, socialist service, and sponsor service autonomy.
The specific entry costs, terms, outcomes, and reforms are in part 04.
The tree reflects the ratified result rather than granting a different constitution through a parallel focus click.

The professional charter can later submit to a federal congress through an explicit reform.
A civilian charter can grant a limited emergency command without permanently becoming a military dictatorship.
A socialist or sponsor-service charter can renegotiate its relationship and become independent.
Each reform must settle the existing obligations.

The popular service crown is a later GP02 constitutional culmination.
It is not a sixth cheap opening charter.
The Horde regency and Ukrainian hetman alternatives likewise arise from their national projects.
They cannot be selected at the start to bypass the founding political bargain.

Exclusive institutions use genuine mutual exclusions.
Compatible programs use shared prerequisites or alternative entry conditions.
The tree must not accidentally turn an intended OR into an AND requirement that forces a host to adopt contradictory governments.
The focus audit must inspect those actual source structures.

## Rewards and receipts

A focus that opens a military school grants access to that school.
It does not also complete training, deliver the required equipment, and create a division.
A focus that opens a congress grants the diplomatic process.
It does not also ratify every partner's charter.

A focus that recognizes a completed mission checks the mission's actual receipt.
It may supply the next capability or presentation.
It cannot issue the mission reward a second time.
If the player completed the legitimate objective before selecting the focus, the focus uses a proper bypass or alternate completion path.
It must not require the player to undo and repeat success.

The three event-owned spirit slots remain Institutional, Military, and Economic.
Focus rewards upgrade or replace the relevant slot.
State-specific work uses state effects where appropriate.
Treaties and promises use their discrete commitment records.
The tree never creates a permanent national spirit for each small administrative task.

A research reward is specific and finite.
A factory reward is bounded and placed in an actual state with a valid slot.
A territorial reward follows the separate claim or integration policy.
A national name or flag change occurs only after the great project's actual formation gate.

## Failed or invalidated focuses

A lost target state, unavailable historical candidate, destroyed partner, or changed treaty can invalidate a targeted program.
The tree must offer a truthful alternate path, suspension, or failure.
It cannot keep a focus apparently available while its completion silently does nothing.

The absence of a named leader does not close an entire constitutional route.
The authentic institutional office remains a functional alternative.
The loss of a port can suspend the Pontic branch while the country continues through civilian, military, or regional recovery.
The failure of a march on Moscow leaves the established host's common tree and regional settlement available.

A completed permanent institutional result remains recorded.
A conditional benefit can suspend when its condition fails.
The engine must not remove every past focus because a temporary transit route closes.
The focus and decision owners need one agreed distinction between completed history and currently valid benefit.

## AI focus behavior

The AI chooses among legal focuses according to the country's actual phase, threats, commitments, industry, equipment, and reachable objectives.
It prioritizes a viable home, the finite A01 civilian settlement, a working arsenal, and a charter before a distant empire.
The founding focus can expose or acknowledge A01, but cannot duplicate its payments or checkpoint rewards.
An AI with a reliable civilian economy can prefer representation and a durable regional peace.
A threatened AI can prefer a professional or sponsor-service arrangement if the contract is feasible.

Focus AI behavior is not assumed to be a normalized weighted lottery.
The installed engine's independent score and race semantics must be inspected.
The probability auditor records whether the relevant result is a score, ranking, bounded probability, sampled result, or unresolved behavior.
It must not report that a focus with weight 20 has a 20 percent chance without the actual adapter supporting that interpretation.

Every weighted change uses immutable before-source bytes and the same saved scenario set in the required comparison.
The focus owner chooses the intended balance targets.
The read-only auditor evaluates those targets and reports mismatches.
A source inspection or attractive tree render does not establish AI behavior.

## Focus acceptance

The focus package must pass country-loading, route-reachability, mutual-exclusion, bypass, completion-receipt, reward, icon, localisation, and AI review.
It must be rendered in fresh host, mature regional host, active great project, formed successor, invalidated target, and preserved living-carrier states.
Long names, all supported UI scales, and the widest reachable branch combination need review.

Every P01 through P14 program and every GP01 through GP08 project must have a real route into and out of the tree.
The audit must show how a player who declines the evolution or every great project still reaches a complete regional campaign.
The result should feel like a country learning how to govern and fight, with its traditions shaping the choices it faces.


## Focus continuation after a completed network

A completed GP releases active preparation while its treaties and history persist, so a compatible next project remains reachable.
The focus graph must allow the completed Pontic network to support Union work and the completed Volga-Ural coalition to support a later steppe or national project.
The ordinary unfinished-project abandonment fee does not apply to a completed compatible result.
A new incompatible constitution still requires its actual transition and cannot repeat earlier rewards.
