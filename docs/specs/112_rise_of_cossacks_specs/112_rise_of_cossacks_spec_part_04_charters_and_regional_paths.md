# Part 04. Charters and regional paths

## A host becomes a country through its obligations

The first ataman can organize a rising before anyone agrees on the country that will follow it.
These programs turn that emergency command into institutions that can recruit, pay, negotiate, modernize, and survive a peace.
Their differences concern who has political rights, who supplies the army, who controls a transport route, and which promises remain binding after victory.
The military and economic work is part of that political choice.

The parent accepted the P01 to P14 route proposals within the user's requested full expansion, as recorded in the [historical disposition record](../../plans/112_rise_of_cossacks_plans/imported_review/112_rise_of_cossacks_improvement_and_closure.md).
They form connected constitutional and regional programs and practical modules within a living country.
They do not create fourteen independent full focus trees.
Every charter, route, action, institution, and outcome label introduced here is a working design label, not final localisation.
The numerical gates and payoffs are accepted initial balance targets for implementation and testing.
Historical references identify inspirations.
The twentieth century programs, negotiations, costs, and outcomes are alternate history design.

Part 01 owns the immediate rising and the two public values.
Part 02 supplies host traditions and the exact geographic cohort definitions.
Part 03 owns readiness, native costs, material conservation, and common service actions.
Part 05 owns the Union of Hosts, Cossack Russia, Golden Horde, Ukrainian national settlement, Pontic network, Volga and Ural coalition, eastern host chain, and durable regional or refuge culmination.
This part supplies the institutions and practical achievements those projects can use.
Completing a program here does not grant a great project's national identity, sovereignty, capital, claims, cores, army, or formation reward.

## Five basic charters

The host has one basic charter at a time.
Regional agreements can preserve local powers within it, but they cannot secretly establish a contradictory second constitution.
A popular service crown is available only at the appropriate large-project culmination in Part 05.
It is not a sixth opening charter.

The first constitutional assembly becomes available after the initial survival objective succeeds, through the surviving-host recovery path in [Part 03 §5.2](112_rise_of_cossacks_spec_part_03_founding_and_shared_systems.md) after that mission times out, or after an accepted restoration settlement.
Its ordinary earliest point is day 90, and it still requires Host Cohesion at least 40, Settlement Legitimacy at least 35, a controlled home capital, and a living military and civilian delegation.
Post-timeout admission does not replay the survival reward or renew any opening grant.
The first civilian settlement and the paid meeting requirements remain in force.
Convening it costs 60 Political Power and opens a 120-day mission.
The mission requires the first accepted civilian settlement, a stated service rule, and an identified authority for the military budget.
The assembly can work during a defensive war.
Ratification requires the stronger gates in the table and a supplied capital.
Those conditions must hold for 30 consecutive days before automatic completion.

The first assembly payment also covers charter drafting.
A program that supplies the necessary delegates or service agreement counts that actual work toward the assembly.
It does not charge a second assembly fee for the same meeting.
When the first regional stage also supplies the initial charter, combine those objectives into one mission whose window is the longer of their stated windows.
Its ordinary Political Power charge is the higher of 60 and that stage's listed Political Power amount, before one shared quote.
Distinct listed resource components remain payable once.
The charter and program stage complete separately when their own objectives pass, using that one paid meeting.
A previously completed assembly can fulfill a program's first stage without another payment only when all that stage's specific mandates and objectives are already satisfied.
Otherwise the listed program cost funds the new regional negotiation, while the completed constitution remains intact.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Two institutions in the confederated charter must represent different constituencies with distinct decision authority.
Renaming one staff into two councils does not qualify.
This local charter is not the three-host Union of Hosts formation.
The temporary compact reward in Part 03 applies once to the immediate accepted provisional response, before charter ratification.
The founding Provisional Civil Settlement supplies the first accepted civilian settlement and a finite recovery path without an existing charter, additional conquest, sponsor or offensive readiness.
Its 35 Political Power base fee funds the three once-only administrative checkpoints in Part 03 and receives the current shared quote.
A higher modified price can require ordinary Political Power accrual.
The program remains available with its original unfinished-stage receipts after an interrupted or expired founding mission.
The host may wait to convene the first assembly until the intended charter is attainable.
An unopened assembly has no running timer.
Ratification does not repeat the compact or civilian checkpoint rewards.
Selecting a charter does not also award an additional stack of Cohesion, Legitimacy, or national spirits here.

A later voluntary charter change costs 75 Political Power and uses a 120-day constitutional mission, with a 180-day cooldown after successful change.
The current institutions continue until the replacement is ratified.
Their property and service guarantees must be honored, explicitly amended with consent, or openly breached.
An imposed emergency change remains possible when the relevant route permits it, but it receives the shared charter-breach consequences and does not bypass the new charter's civil settlement.
Defeat, displacement, or loss of a sponsor can suspend a dependent charter without charging the elective change fee.
The host then retains a provisional administration and access to recovery.

## Shared gates, costs, and finite rewards

### Readiness and geographic proof

Voluntary expansion uses Part 03's common readiness baseline.
The host has a controlled supplied capital and at least one field formation with at least 75 percent native equipment fulfillment and 50 percent organization.
Actual current host free manpower divided by current host fielded land-army manpower plus that same current free manpower must be at least 0.20 when committing to voluntary expansion.
This includes all later native-trained units, counts each person once, and does not assign a mixed national pool to an Event 112 cohort or create an additional manpower entitlement.
The proportion must remain valid after the quoted outgoing transfer.
Equipment and organization are read from the actual formations committed to the action, and the capital defender must remain viable afterward.
The exact inspected native weighted readings remain authoritative, with no empty formation counted as proof.
Defense, negotiation, civilian protection, and recovery remain available below these thresholds.
No route can make a defeated host recover only by first satisfying an offensive readiness gate.

Each geographic program selects its actual participating states, authorities, and route before payment.
The G01 to G16 cohorts have the definitions in Part 02.
A local program can begin from its stated anchor without satisfying the entire later national formation cohort.
Partner territory counts only where the program explicitly permits a signed local charter, transit, or receiving agreement.
An allied state does not become owned territory, and occupying a state does not prove its inhabitants accepted the settlement.
Existing countries, local institutions, and non Cossack partners retain their own identities.

Program access through an adopted tradition still uses Part 02's paid adoption and two-additional-tradition limit.
A program does not grant a free third adopted tradition.
A new frontier host can use the general civilian, confederated, or professional charter without pretending to have inherited a historic host's land.
Regional content opens when actual contact and the relevant geographic or institutional relationship exist.

### Prices, objectives, and opportunity costs

Every cost below is a base cost before the current accepted universal quote.
The displayed quote, inclusive affordability check, actual debit, and stored refund use Part 03's shared rules.
A delayed next step receives a fresh quote when that step starts.
The player does not lose a retroactive payment because a price modifier changed during an earlier mission.
The quote identifies refundable reservations and the point at which each component is consumed or settled.
Administrative and training fees settle when the funded meeting or training actually begins.
A later refusal does not refund that completed work.
Undispatched material remains governed by its actual reservation and refund record, while delivered or consumed material is not recreated on cancellation.
Preflight failure before work begins returns only the actual debit under the shared reconciliation rules.
No action charges more than four spendable resource types or more than 60 Command Power.
The AI preserves 20 Political Power and 10 Command Power except when a verified action prevents immediate collapse.

The listed special-action costs are exhaustive.
Native recruitment, equipment production, purchases, and construction keep their ordinary costs and displays.
They are not hidden extra decision payments.
Equipment named as a transfer is reserved and delivered once to its actual recipient.
It is not also removed as an abstract fee and then charged again to equip the same formation.

The stated duration is the mission's maximum ordinary window.
Actual objectives complete automatically once fulfilled, including any expressly required period of compliance.
An elapsed timer does not repair a railway, create a council, produce arms, or move a family.
If every relevant physical construction target is already intact and at its native capacity, the existing selected facility can satisfy that part of the objective through 60 days of verified operation, valid access, and completion of the program's actual service obligation.
The political, training, delivery, and receiving-capacity requirements still apply.
No player must demolish an adequate facility to make a repair mission available, and no second building reward accompanies recognition of existing capacity.
An eligible ordinary civil or regional-program mission may receive one extension of up to 60 days for 20 Political Power through A27 before failure.
External loss of a required route can instead suspend that mission once for up to 90 days without the elective charge.
Both alternatives consume the same one-extension receipt, so a mission cannot stack a paid extension with a free route-loss suspension.
Native raid preparation, counterpart response windows and the separate crisis-specific evacuation or physical-recovery windows keep their own exact owners and cannot be lengthened through A27.
An unfulfilled project then closes with its partial outcome and a 180-day restart cooldown.
Completed physical work persists, and a restart funds only unfinished work and renewed negotiation.

### Political and material payoff limits

Route completion changes are finite.
The tables distinguish full and limited outcomes so the lower reward has a concrete cause.
Those changes are the route's political completion budget, not a second award for every council, service settlement, and treaty already rewarded through the same action.
The common outcome owner credits already applied rewards from that same settlement before awarding the remainder.
Shared per-state and per-period council limits remain in force.
Selecting another label, repeating a completed mission, or changing the country presentation does not reset the reward.

A limited outcome can be upgraded once by completing its named missing requirements and settling any outstanding breach.
Opening the upgrade costs 25 Political Power and allows 180 days.
Any entirely unperformed funded stage still uses its listed cost when started, while completed paid work is credited without another charge.
The upgrade awards only the difference between the full completion budget and the already used limited budget and replaces the 3 percent benefit with the 5 percent version.
It does not restart the first-peace test, replace lost people or equipment, or repeat the earlier completion reward.

There are three working focus-created spirit slots: S01 Institutions and service settlement, S02 Army organization, and S03 Civil economy and reconstruction.
Institutional, Military, and Economic are the corresponding short working labels.
A listed institutional or economic improvement replaces a compatible version in its slot.
A military reform replaces the selected military version.
The same statistic cannot accumulate through several route labels or through a great project's replacement of the same institution.
The strongest eligible applicable effect is used where a meter band and a route would otherwise improve the same statistic.
Treaty access, actual buildings, ordinary trained formations, and retained rights can coexist with that limit.
The two public meters remain Host Cohesion and Settlement Legitimacy.

A route can offer a 3 percent limited or 5 percent full modifier only on its named relevant surface.
These are initial tuning bounds, not an invitation to add 5 percent to every national statistic.
Where a proposed local modifier has no suitable inspected native scope, use the same value for a single named program's administrative quote reduction in the appropriate existing slot.
Do not expand a local benefit into a nationwide combat bonus to avoid a scope constraint.
This binding must be recorded in the implementation acceptance evidence.

### Partners, refusal, and the first peace

An invitation opens a 30-day response window.
A human counterpart chooses its response.
An incompatible current war between the offering and receiving parties, a contradictory patron contract, an unresolved serious breach against that counterpart, or a physically impossible delivery makes the offered terms invalid.
A war against a third party can provide a valid reason for a defense or service offer.
A specific ceasefire negotiation during a bilateral war is permitted only through its explicit owner, without pretending that a normal invitation has already ended that war.
The host may submit revised lawful terms, but no payment purchases another country's consent automatically.
Only one counteroffer is resolved within a funded negotiation.
A refused invitation has a 180-day same-offer cooldown, unless a materially changed war, border, or government creates a genuinely new negotiation.

The initial AI response targets are 70 percent accept, 20 percent counteroffer, and 10 percent refuse for a supplied mutually protective offer with compatible institutions.
For a feasible neutral offer they are 40, 40, and 20 percent.
For a feasible offer opposed by current claims or an adverse institutional preference they are 15, 35, and 50 percent.
Invalid terms receive no acceptance roll.
These are named scenario probability targets for later verification, not already measured runtime probabilities.
The route-specific refusals below determine which scenario applies.

Each completed program receives one first-peace stress test.
It becomes due after 60 consecutive days of peace following that program's completion and after the home administration is secure.
If the program completes during peace, the 60-day count starts at completion.
A renewed war suspends a due civilian review until the capital is safe.
It does not erase the obligation.
The next free mission place admits the review, with emergency evacuation and immediate defense ahead of it.
Queued reviews do not expire invisibly or stack simultaneous penalties before their choices appear.

One actual broken promise produces one shared charter-breach result, normally Cohesion minus 10 and Legitimacy minus 8 under Part 03.
The route and a great project must not punish the same breach twice.
If their first-peace tests concern that same promise, one combined review fulfills both records after its real objectives are met.
Recovery restores no more than the loss caused by that review and respects the shared recovery caps.
It awards no repeatable Political Power, manpower, equipment, or constitutional completion reward.

## P01. Don civic settlement

### Entry and the bargain

The [Don regional reference](https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pagesD%5CO%5CDonregion) supports the distinction between military host institutions and wider urban and rural constituencies.
The following civic settlement is a modern alternate history program.

Entry requires 120 days since the rising, Cohesion 50, Legitimacy 45, and either a G02 home base or an accepted Don institutional relationship in a viable receiving region.
The selected region must contain a real rural recruiting constituency and an urban or workshop constituency with separate representatives.
A civilian or confederated charter must be ratified before the second step completes.

| Step | Base cost and window | Required work and opportunity cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Convene the territorial settlement | 45 Political Power, 120 days | Obtain military, rural civilian, and urban mandates and settle the franchise. An existing first assembly counts toward this work and is not paid twice |
| Fund the common service budget | 60 Political Power, 150 days | Complete one civilian workshop, infrastructure, or railway repair or improvement in the selected region through native construction, then maintain an agreed civilian and military budget for 30 days |
| Train the accountable field reserve | 20 Army Experience and 15 Command Power, 120 days | Exercise one existing mounted or infantry formation for 30 days, retain a separate home defense formation, and obtain council approval for its defined deployment obligation |

The first choice preserves exclusive service privileges temporarily or opens equal territorial representation.
The temporary compromise causes a one-off Cohesion loss of 2 and supplies a limited charter with a dated franchise review.
Immediate equal representation causes a one-off Cohesion loss of 4 while the veteran settlement is negotiated.
It requires a real service protection such as rotation, pension administration, or protection of an agreed household holding.
Neither choice creates recruits.
Training and recruitment continue through the native army.

Taking the last home formation for the exercise is invalid.
An emergency mass levy remains an ordinary military option, but taking the promised workshop workers or violating the agreed rotation opens the relevant budget dispute.
Modernization develops mounted screening and reserves alongside infantry, artillery, communications, and repair.
The economic objective supplies the means to reinforce that force.

### Partners and completion

A neighbor or host can accept a defensive or transit compact without joining a new country.
A Ukrainian or Russian counterpart can refuse a sovereignty claim while accepting trade or a bounded defense arrangement.
Military households can counteroffer protected service rights without retaining exclusive votes over everyone else's taxes.
If urban delegates refuse, the host can reduce the levy and reconvene once, or finish the limited military and municipal compact.
Refusal does not create a rebel country automatically.

| Outcome | Gate and finite payoff |
| --- | --- |
| Full civic settlement | Cohesion 55 and Legitimacy 60, equal representation, the completed economic objective, and 60 days of the budget honored. Completion budget is Cohesion plus 4 and Legitimacy plus 8. Institutional improvement is a 5 percent reduction in the administrative Political Power component of the ordinary civilian settlement program |
| Limited municipal compact | Cohesion 50 and Legitimacy 50 with accepted temporary service terms and one completed economic objective. Completion budget is Cohesion plus 2 and Legitimacy plus 4. The corresponding institutional improvement is 3 percent and applies only to the selected region's eligible settlement work |

The trained reserve and completed construction are retained because they were actually produced.
There is no extra division or factory at ratification.
A national service republic remains a later Part 05 project.

### First peace, failure, and recovery

The first peace brings a 150-day dispute over demobilization, municipal repairs, and veteran claims.
The council can spend 45 Political Power and complete one actual veteran housing, civilian repair, or service-rotation obligation.
It must return at least one formation from an expired external service assignment or obtain a renewed voluntary mandate.
This closes the review and keeps the full benefit.

Ignoring the review suspends the institutional improvement and costs Legitimacy minus 6 once.
Keeping a compulsory expired mandate is instead a charter breach with the shared consequences.
Recovery costs 50 Political Power and takes up to 120 days to settle the affected claim, end or ratify the deployment, and hold the repaired agreement for 30 days.
It restores up to 4 lost Legitimacy, with service-dispute Cohesion recovery handled once by Part 03.
A military turn remains possible through the real charter-change process.
The AI favors the civic path with a secure city and rural base, and postpones a distant war while the budget is disputed.

## P02. Campaign ataman and professional host

### Entry and the command mandate

This program turns the host's military service tradition into a professional command under a stated mandate.
The [Kuban host reference](https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pages%5CK%5CU%5CKubanCossackHost) is one historical foundation for service institutions, not evidence for this invented constitution.

Entry requires 90 days since the rising, Cohesion 50, Legitimacy 40, and either a current defensive war or a specific accepted emergency defense mandate.
The host must retain at least two formations, one available for training and one for home defense.
It needs an actual arsenal, functioning arms production, or a delivered arms agreement before standardization.
Professional ataman ratification still requires Cohesion 60 and Legitimacy 45.
A council can adopt the military work while keeping civilian control.

| Step | Base cost and window | Required work and opportunity cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Define the campaign mandate | 40 Political Power, 90 days | Name the protected territory, campaign aim, home reserve, budget authority, and the review after the emergency |
| Build the common staff | 25 Army Experience and 40 Political Power, 120 days | Train a real cadre, run a 30-day exercise, and select merit promotion or protected existing commissions |
| Standardize the field group | 20 Army Experience and 20 Command Power, 150 days | Bring one or two existing formations to 85 percent equipment and 60 percent organization, with a supplied home formation and an arms replacement source remaining available |

Merit promotion causes a one-off Cohesion loss of 4 while established officers are reconciled through actual assignments and service terms.
Protecting all existing commissions avoids that immediate loss but limits the program to one standardized formation until a later paid professional review.
The military choice concerns mounted reconnaissance, dismounted fighting, engineers, artillery, and transport.
Motorization requires the ordinary technology, trucks, fuel, and trained manpower.
The exercise consumes native supply and equipment and ties the assigned formation to training rather than another voluntary expedition.

### Partners and completion

An arsenal or staff partner can demand limited military access, purchase payment, or a defined service contribution.
It can refuse access to technology or equipment that it cannot actually provide.
Another host can accept a joint exercise while rejecting permanent command subordination.
The ataman can accept that limited relationship without treating it as disloyalty.

| Outcome | Gate and finite payoff |
| --- | --- |
| Accountable professional staff | Cohesion 60, Legitimacy 50, merit training, a live mandate, and two adequately equipped formations. Completion budget is Cohesion plus 6 and Legitimacy plus 2. The Military slot can provide 5 percent lower training time for the selected ordinary host standardization program |
| Restricted officer compact | Cohesion 60, Legitimacy 45, one standardized field formation, and protected commissions. Completion budget is Cohesion plus 4 and Legitimacy plus 1. The same selected military improvement is 3 percent |

One institution receives that training benefit.
It does not also increase attack, defense, organization, research, and manpower.
Professional capacity can support the larger military republic or a common host staff in Part 05.

### First peace, failure, and recovery

The first peace opens a 120-day mandate review.
Renewing professional government costs 60 Political Power and requires a civilian budget hearing, a defined successor or succession procedure, and one formation returned to its agreed home duty.
A council transition uses the charter-change process and counts the same hearing once.

Refusing review suspends the staff benefit and creates a charter breach.
If readiness fails without a political breach, the field group becomes temporarily unavailable for new voluntary expedition contracts until equipment returns to 75 percent and the home formation is supplied.
No army disappears and no replacement army appears.
Recovery from political refusal costs 60 Political Power and allows 150 days for a ratified mandate and 30 days of compliance.
It restores at most Cohesion plus 6 and Legitimacy plus 4 of the review's losses, respecting the common service recovery ceiling.
The AI favors this program during a supportable defensive emergency and accepts peace when its home army cannot sustain wider war.

## P03. Kuban federal settlement

### Entry and the territorial question

The [Kuban republic reference](https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pages%5CK%5CU%5CKubanPeoplesRepublic.htm) documents councils and contested constitutional directions.
Its reported Ukrainian federation proposal remains qualified and cannot establish unanimous consent.
The program also retains plastun infantry alongside the host's mounted formations.

Entry requires 150 days since the rising, Cohesion 50, Legitimacy 45, a G03 agricultural and administrative base, and an actual Black Sea exit or inland supply alternative.
A participating Kuban partner can retain ownership under an explicit regional agreement.
In that case the partner owns its local constitutional and construction results.
The leading host receives only the agreed common training and diplomatic benefits.

| Step | Base cost and window | Required work and opportunity cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assemble the regional councils | 55 Political Power, 150 days | Receive separate military-tradition, civilian, and affected local-community mandates, with a written land-arbitration procedure |
| Join farm, workshop, and exit | 50 Political Power and 100 support equipment, 180 days | Deliver the equipment once to the actual engineering or repair work and complete one native transport improvement linking the selected economic functions |
| Establish the mixed territorial army | 20 Army Experience and 15 Command Power, 120 days | Exercise one plastun infantry formation and one mounted or mobile formation, each at 75 percent equipment and 50 percent organization, under an agreed common staff |

The council can preserve regional powers under a shared civilian franchise or keep a narrower temporary military compact.
Settling affected civilian and Circassian claims requires an actual choice among compensation, shared administration, or a protected district.
Simply excluding the affected constituency blocks the full federal outcome.
Agricultural recruitment competes with the work needed to restore the transport objective.
The infantry and cavalry schools share scarce instructors, so they cannot conduct two parallel free standardization programs from the same cadre.

### External offers and completion

A Ukrainian or Russian government receives a concrete offer identifying regional legislation, army command, revenue, and limits on deployment.
Its response can be recognition, limited federation talks, a more centralized counteroffer, or refusal.
Accepting the centralized counteroffer requires a visible amendment with the affected Kuban representatives.
Until a Part 05 formation or continuity transaction is complete, the offer does not annex the host or the partner.

| Outcome | Gate and finite payoff |
| --- | --- |
| Inclusive regional federation | Cohesion 60, Legitimacy 60, accepted civilian and affected-community terms, a repaired route, and both exercised formations. Completion budget is Cohesion plus 5 and Legitimacy plus 7. The Military slot can reduce the selected mixed-force standardization program's training time by 5 percent |
| Independent limited compact | Cohesion 55, Legitimacy 50, a viable inland or coastal exit and accepted temporary local guarantees. Completion budget is Cohesion plus 3 and Legitimacy plus 4. Its corresponding training improvement is 3 percent |

The larger Ukrainian or Russian offer can fail while a competent Kuban government survives.
Independent regional consolidation remains a full strategic choice through Part 05.

### First peace, failure, and recovery

The first peace opens a 180-day land and export-budget review.
The host spends 50 Political Power, completes one actual civilian repair or compensation objective, and maintains the agreed regional budget and local powers for 60 days.
A patron may use the review to request greater control, which the region can refuse under its current charter.

Ignoring the review suspends the regional training benefit and costs Legitimacy minus 6.
Unilaterally removing a protected district or regional legislature is a charter breach instead.
Recovery costs 60 Political Power and permits 150 days to restore or consensually amend the affected term and complete the outstanding civilian work.
It restores up to 4 lost Legitimacy and the benefit after 30 days of compliance.
An officer capture of the council can lead to P02 through a real constitutional change while preserving actual infantry training and construction.
The AI compares security and accepted local powers, rather than choosing a federation solely by ideology.

## P04. Black Sea return network

### Entry and a real corridor

The [Black Sea Cossacks reference](https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pages%5CB%5CL%5CBlackSeaCossacks.htm) supplies the migration and institutional-memory anchor.
Modern return, citizenship, and property arrangements below require new consent.

Entry requires 180 days since the rising, Cohesion 50, Legitimacy 50, an accepted Black Sea relationship, and an actual G01, Azov, or G15 receiving destination.
The route must connect an identified source to that destination by controlled land, accepted transit, or usable shipping.
An urgent evacuation can use the common recovery system earlier, but it does not receive this module's completion reward automatically.

| Step | Base cost and window | Required work and opportunity cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Negotiate the receiving settlement | 45 Political Power, 120 days | Identify the receiving authority, current residents' rights, settlement scale, citizenship, service terms, and the return or evacuation route |
| Prepare the corridor | 35 Political Power and 15 Command Power, 150 days | Repair one relevant port, crossing, or inland transport link, retain an escort or home-defense formation, and demonstrate 30 days of valid passage |
| Deliver the first return group | 30 Political Power and 10 Command Power, 120 days | Complete one measured civilian or volunteer transfer within verified source, receiving, and transport capacity, with an actual housing or livelihood objective at the destination |

The first civilian group is capped at 1,000 people and the receiving capacity approved in the transfer plan, whichever is lower.
It can include fewer people without invalidating the module.
A volunteer alternative uses Part 03's existing measured volunteer limit and does not also receive the civilian group.
No source means no transfer.
The module still permits a limited institutional and transport agreement without moving population.
The same escort and transport cannot simultaneously satisfy an unsupported offensive expedition and civilian passage.

### Refusal, sovereignty, and payoff

The receiving authority can admit families, accept volunteers, offer shared defense, or reject a claim of direct host rule.
The source community can choose continued diaspora ties instead of moving.
Current residents can demand compensation, shared local representation, or a smaller first group.
The host can accept those terms, make one counteroffer, or close the invitation without an invented hostility penalty.
Direct sovereignty remains a separate territorial settlement and, where appropriate, a Part 05 project.

| Outcome | Gate and finite payoff |
| --- | --- |
