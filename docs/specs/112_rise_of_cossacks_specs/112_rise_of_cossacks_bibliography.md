# Rise of Cossacks historical research bibliography

Research accessed on 10 October 2026.
The historical sources below support the distinctions used in parts 02, 04, 05, 06 and 09 of this package.
The gameplay routes are explicitly alternate history proposals and are not presented as historical events.
Each entry distinguishes inspected evidence from a research lead.
The first twenty-four entries preserve the regional researcher's access record.
Additional entries record the parent's research on national projects, living candidates and Sloboda.
A full project-source reading claim does not mean every external book, article, archive image or recording was read or inspected in full.

## H01. Don regional institutions and society

**Source:** Internet Encyclopedia of Ukraine, Don region, Canadian Institute of Ukrainian Studies.

**URL:** [Don region](https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pagesD%5CO%5CDonregion).

**Used for:** The distinction between host military institutions, non Cossack inhabitants, lower Don industry, the historical capital, and competing post 1917 political projects.
Relevant social and historical passages were inspected.
The article's 1937 service restriction date conflicts with a directly dated 1936 resolution and must not be used.
Its ethnographic and political framing is not an automatic territorial legitimacy rule.

## H11. Ural communal fisheries as a contemporary description

## Interpretation and production limits

The bibliography supplies historical foundations and access records, not final source clearance for art, portraits, text quotations or audio.
A museum catalog object, scholarly abstract or finding aid can identify a promising research direction without providing the underlying image, full book or recording.
No quoted lyric, cultural remark, final super-event title or selected recording is supplied here.
Final source-dependent presentation requires the separate research and rights workflow in the super-event and asset prompts.
The numerical values in the specification are authored game-balance targets.
They are not demographic estimates or historical troop strengths drawn from these sources.
