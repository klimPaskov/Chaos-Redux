# Rise of Cossacks research and design rationale

## What the research changes

The central design is a military community trying to become a durable country.
Its strongest historical foundations are varied institutions, land and service bargains, distinct local constituencies, transport economies and relationships with larger powers.
That supports a much wider event than a cavalry revolt with a sequence of renamed territorial claims.
The first host appears immediately, but its later development asks who can govern, who supplies the army, which promises survive victory and what other communities gain by joining.

The specification treats every modern route, event outcome, price, meter value and formation threshold as alternate history design.
Historical sources inform plausible choices and conflicts.
They do not establish that one twentieth century Cossack nation had an uncontested program, that all hosts wanted the same country or that an old service estate supplied exclusive ownership of a modern territory.
All route and asset labels are working labels, not final localisation.

The [historical bibliography](112_rise_of_cossacks_bibliography.md) records thirty-four numbered entries and their actual access limits.
The [source authority record](../../plans/112_rise_of_cossacks_plans/imported_review/source_reading_and_authority.md) separately records supplied project-source authority and the historical team reading gate.
The distinction matters because every required supplied project text was read in full, while some external books, articles, collections and images were available only in part or as metadata.

### Why military modernization preserves multiple traditions

Kuban host history includes plastun infantry alongside mounted service.
That makes an infantry reconnaissance and territorial-defense branch a grounded companion to cavalry.
The specification uses native cavalry and infantry battalions, plus ordinary support arms, motorization and later conventional armor cooperation.
It does not need a new combat archetype to give those institutions a recognizable character.
See [Kuban Cossack Host](https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pages%5CK%5CU%5CKubanCossackHost).

Military traditions become training access, template identities and one selected emphasis within a bounded spirit slot.
A modern host must obtain rifles, support equipment, trucks, fuel, instructors and replacements through actual game systems.
The mounted branch stays useful for reconnaissance, screening and operating within its supply limits, while ordinary combined arms determines whether the country can sustain a national war.
These are authored gameplay decisions.
They make survival and modernization different stages instead of letting an opening cavalry bonus replace an economy.

### Why service to a larger power remains a substantial route

The formal Soviet change used here is the resolution of 20 April 1936 removing general restrictions on Cossack service in the Red Army, with its stated court-related exception.
The [archival catalog record](https://docs.historyrussia.org/ru/nodes/92024-o-snyatii-s-kazachestva-ogranicheniy-po-sluzhbe-v-rkka-post-tsik-sssr-ot-20-aprelya-1936-g) and [short transcription](https://istmat.org/node/44903) establish that date.
A Don secondary entry's 1937 date is not used.
The resolution did not itself grant independent host sovereignty or the autonomy proposed in P06.

P06 therefore offers an invented negotiation over actual service, appointments, property, local institutions and material support.
A Soviet or compatible socialist counterpart can accept, counteroffer or refuse.
P08 develops a distinct sponsor frontier arrangement with home-defense and outside-deployment limits.
These paths are useful because an undersupplied small country may value enforceable autonomy and arms more than an unsupported national restoration war.
Their first-peace reviews test whether temporary controls end and whether both sides keep their guarantees.

## Cossack Russia as a national project

The Russian route asks whether a frontier military polity can obtain a national civilian mandate.
Moscow, a supporting central district and another civil anchor matter because the proposed country must govern a society larger than the original service community.
The formation therefore needs actual sovereign territorial control, civilian institutions, an accepted citizenship rule and durable supply.
It grants no automatic ownership of an existing Russian or Soviet country, no free nationwide cores and no duplicate founding army.

Three constitutional outcomes share the real national task.
The civic service republic places military service under wider citizenship.
The military republic keeps a professional executive with a recorded national mandate and review procedure.
The popular service crown or regency is a later constitutional choice supported by real institutions.
A verified living candidate may be researched, while an authentic institutional regency prevents the route from depending on an invented heir.

The translated [Pugachev manifesto](https://home.uncg.edu/~jwjones/russia/377readings/pugachev.html) offers a limited historical inspiration for connecting a monarchic claim with promises about land and service.
Its coercive language and contested setting are part of that source's character.
The design does not treat it as a modern constitution or revive its author.
The national route's citizenship, budget, capital and postwar requirements are original design.

The decisive later conflict is whether the host can turn a military victory into legitimate government.
Veterans, municipalities, officers and civilian taxpayers have different claims on the same budget.
A successful formation must then review emergency command and preserve actual regional guarantees.
A failed national campaign can still leave a functioning regional state with its trained forces and paid construction intact.
The full gates, costs, partial result and first-peace consequences are in GP02 in part 05.

## Golden Horde as a modern claim that must earn substance

The historical Golden Horde belonged to a Jochid political world.
Lower Volga centers, changing religious institutions, government and trade belong in its account alongside military force.
The [Iranica Golden Horde article](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/golden-horde/), [Iranica Joci article](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/joci/) and [Oxford research overview](https://nomadicempires.site.ox.ac.uk/golden-horde) support those distinctions.
They do not establish a Cossack hereditary right to a twentieth century restoration.

The accepted GP03 route is explicitly a new appropriation of that legacy.
Its first useful result is a viable Lower Volga political and economic settlement.
The larger project adds a middle-river relationship, separately negotiated steppe partners and a congress with real mandates.
At least one qualifying mandate comes from a non Cossack sovereign or substantial independent authority.
That requirement gives the project political substance beyond renaming the original host.

A plural steppe commonwealth and an imperial regency create different authority problems.
The commonwealth needs accepted common functions and protected partner authority.
The regency needs an actual mandate, viable direct administration and a succession process.
A secular, plural or state-faith settlement must disclose its real rights and obligations.
No version automatically converts populations, merges distinct traditions or receives claims and cores across an imagined medieval maximum map.

Potential Crimean, Nogay, Volga Tatar, Bashkir, Kazakh and other partners are not interchangeable tokens.
The actual installed countries and local authorities determine who can negotiate.
Their acceptance is conditional on their interests and their power to fulfill the agreement.
An existing Mongol or Horde-related event remains a separate owner and potential rival rather than a source of free legitimacy.
The Event 073 integration requirement is recorded in part 13.

The [Sadri Maksudi Arsal biography](https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/arsal-sadri-maksudi) identifies a contemporary constitutional and scholarly research direction.
It does not make him a khan, a mandatory recruit or a supported portrait row.
No verified living Jochid claimant was established in this research.
The regency therefore has an institutional default and a strict later identity audit for any proposed person.

The project's first peace tests revenue, transit, military contribution, civilian representation and succession.
A treaty member that fulfilled a limited obligation can refuse a larger demand without the script branding lawful refusal a betrayal.
A smaller Volga settlement remains a meaningful limited result.
The full route and national formation costs appear in GP03 in part 05.

## Ukrainian, regional, eastern and refuge outcomes

The Kuban republic source supports competing constitutional directions and carefully qualifies the reported Ukrainian federation proposal.
It says that proposal was not enacted.
The design therefore makes Ukrainian or Russian federation a new offer requiring actual acceptance, rather than inheriting unanimous historical consent.
See [Kuban People's Republic](https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pages%5CK%5CU%5CKubanPeoplesRepublic.htm).

The Ukrainian project can become a civilian council commonwealth or a modern hetmanate with a ratified mandate.
An existing Ukrainian country retains its identity and meaningful content through any negotiated continuity transaction.
Pavlo Skoropadsky is an optional documented invitee, not a compulsory replacement for a living government or a timeless character.
His [biography](https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pages%5CS%5CK%5CSkoropadskyPavlo) supports the historical office and exile context, while current life, ownership, consent and role fit remain campaign checks.

The Union of Hosts answers a different question.
It can coordinate separately governed hosts without abolishing them.
Common defense, contributions and command limits must survive a real disagreement.
The strongest union achievement therefore requires a lawful refusal to be respected and a later obligation fulfilled, not merely three signatures.
A sovereign federal variant is a separate constitutional transaction.

The Pontic network, Volga and Ural coalition and eastern host chain each have a different practical foundation.
One depends on ports, passage and civilian protection.
One depends on river access, local powers and a sustainable common budget.
One depends on defended station links and the cost of distance.
Treaty networks preserve member flags and ownership until a real sovereign identity change occurs.
A network can be useful without annexing its partners.

Refuge is also a complete strategic outcome.
The Danubian and Nekrasov sources show why migration, patronage and service are not identical questions.
The game design records actual departure, losses if any, surviving custody and reception instead of granting another population pool at every arrival.
Families can be admitted without producing an army or an independent tag.
A landless continuation is offered only through capabilities the installed engine actually supports.
The alternative is an explicitly accepted receiving country's playable institutions and a retained lineage for a later bounded restoration.

## The Hosts Answer and why it is bounded

The evolution's regional spread follows the user's core idea, while its exact activation and probability model are original design.
It activates at the current tier-two Chaos threshold and requires the appropriate surviving-host and repeated-instability observations, with a defined pre-fire path.
A single rebellion then becomes a source of distinct responses rather than an automatic chain of identical rebel countries.

The response pool contains a new host rising, a state defection, a measured volunteer contribution, exact formation defection and an existing host compact.
The exact formation outcome activates only after native preservation is demonstrated and an actual unique unit is available.
The specification limits created landed hosts, active actors, custody records, per-source response cadence and simultaneous commitments.
No source can supply the same people or equipment twice.
A state must still have an actual authority that can lose or transfer it.

The evolution uses the twelve historical content families as reasons for different institutions, contacts and responses.
They are not twelve compulsory tags.
Don, Kuban, Dnipro and Sloboda are the strongest initial anchors, while more distant traditions are earned through contact, migration, service and geography.
That keeps the initial event within the user's East Slavic premise while allowing a later wider resurgence.

## Characters and historical responsibility

The four named candidate rows are Skoropadsky, Bych, Getmanov and Krasnov.
Their accepted roles are narrow and supported by identified biographies or archive descriptions.
The default government and succession use an authentic institutional acting-ataman representation when no person qualifies.
No randomized officer name, undocumented hidden heir, revived pre-start figure or generated substitute face supplies missing historical evidence.

Krasnov's later Nazi collaboration remains part of his documented historical context.
It does not make every Cossack community politically identical, and a useful exile contact is not presented as an endorsement.
Getmanov's later wartime context also remains in the research record.
The character specification keeps the country functional when an invitation is refused or a particular person is unavailable.
See bibliography R05 to R08 and part 09 for the exact ownership, role and temporal gates.

## Why the numerical detail is still provisional

The formulas, caps, prices, mission windows and AI targets make the plan implementable and reviewable.
They are not observed balance results.
Seven founding examples and the recorded arithmetic invariants have been calculated outside the game.
That verifies arithmetic relationships, not native script semantics, division creation, combat survival or AI behavior.

The future implementation must preserve a source baseline and evaluate named scenarios through the actual domain tools.
It must test the one-state opening, all constitutional paths, regional limited outcomes, refusal, interrupted delivery, occupation, partner exit, succession and defeat.
A changed value needs an explicit reason and renewed checks of every dependent threshold, cost and timer.
The full acceptance matrix is in parts 11 and 13, with domain usage maps and dedicated implementation prompts.
