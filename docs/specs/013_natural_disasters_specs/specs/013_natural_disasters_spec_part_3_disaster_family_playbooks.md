# Event 013 Natural Disasters, Part 3, disaster family playbooks

These playbooks are design handoffs, not final localisation. Working labels are not final in-game names. Every family must remain individually triggerable through the reusable Event 013 call contract.

## Availability ladder

Baseline starts with a limited but meaningful pool. Evolution I widens variety. Evolution II makes regional chains and whole-world pulse behavior possible. Evolution III opens abnormal high-chaos families and super-event moments.

| Stage | Family access direction |
| --- | --- |
| Baseline | Earthquake, flood, thunderstorm, hailstorm, wildfire, local extreme wind, local cold, local heat, small landslide. |
| Evolution I | Adds tropical cyclone, blizzard, drought, sandstorm, wet and dry mass movement, rare volcanic signals, stronger heat and cold, storm surge. |
| Evolution II | All normal families can hit regionally, chain aftermath, and affect neighboring states with supply and recovery modifiers. Tsunami, ashfall, lahar, regional flood, and serious cyclone behavior become routine enough to need recovery categories. |
| Evolution III | Meteor shower, major meteor impact, whole-earth rupture wave, massive eruption crisis, delayed global tsunami chains, moving tornado or storm corridor, and other abnormal disaster chains. |

## Family uniqueness rule

No family may be implemented as the same generic event with a swapped state name. Each family needs its own visible cause, target pool, warning flavor, damage pattern, recovery tasks, chain risks, and remark direction. If two families produce the same gameplay, merge them or make one a follow-up state instead of a separate family.
