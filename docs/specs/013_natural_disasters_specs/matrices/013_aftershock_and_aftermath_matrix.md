# Event 013 aftermath and chain matrix

| Disaster family | Immediate aftermath | Delayed chain | Prevention or mitigation | UI card value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Earthquake | collapsed buildings, broken rail, rescue zones | aftershock, landslide, tsunami if offshore | structure inspection, rescue teams, coastal warning | aftershock risk and trapped population |
| Flood | inundated districts, blocked roads, drowned depots | disease, famine, refugee pressure | clean water, evacuation, food corridor | water level and sanitation risk |
| Cyclone | port damage, airfield disruption, storm surge | flood, disease, supply collapse | port closure, aircraft dispersal, medical corridor | coast status and port closure state |
| Wildfire | scorched infrastructure, smoke, evacuation | spread, refugee pressure, crop loss | firebreaks, fuel and truck response, evacuation | fire line and shelter load |
| Drought | water shortage, production strain | famine, wildfire, migration | water trains, rationing, crop protection | water reserve and crop pressure |
| Heat | hospital overload, rail strain | drought, disease, unrest | cooling shelters, water distribution, medical teams | heat exposure and hospital load |
| Cold | frozen housing, fuel shortage | disease, supply collapse | fuel corridor, winter shelters, rail clearing | fuel reserve and exposure risk |

| Dust storm | grounded aircraft, convoy blindness | supply collapse, disease, drought worsening | airfield closure, convoy reroute, shelters | visibility and convoy status |
| Volcano | ash, lava or blast damage, evacuation | ashfall, lahar, famine, disease, tsunami | observatory watch, evacuation, ash cleanup | ash load and valley risk |
| Tsunami | coastal destruction, port loss | disease, refugee pressure, naval disruption | coast evacuation, port closure, medical response | wave follow-up and coast status |
