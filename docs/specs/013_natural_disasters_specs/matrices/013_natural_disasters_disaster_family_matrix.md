# Natural Disasters Disaster Family Matrix

Working values and labels are design guidance. Final numeric values belong in script constants.

| Family | Hazard group | Primary targets | Primary damage | Death profile | Follow-ups | Recovery focus | Evolution access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Earthquake | Geophysical | Fault zones, mountain belts, dense urban states | Infrastructure, factories, rail, hubs, forts | High in dense states | Aftershock, tsunami, landslide | Rescue, rail clearance, city stabilization | Baseline, stronger in II, global rupture in III |
| Flood | Hydrological | Rivers, deltas, lowlands, cyclone paths | Infrastructure, rail, hubs, factories | Medium to high | Disease, famine, refugee pressure, landslide | Levees, pumps, shelters, food stores | Baseline, broad in II |
| Tropical cyclone | Meteorological | Tropical coasts and islands | Ports, naval bases, dockyards, rail, airbases | High near landfall | Flood, landslide, refugee pressure | Port reopening, evacuation, route clearing | Baseline rare, common in I and II |
| Thunderstorm | Meteorological | Warm plains, humid regions, storm belts | Airbases, radar, infrastructure, rail | Low to medium | Hail, flash flood, wind, wildfire | Repair communications and local routes | Baseline and I |
| Hailstorm | Meteorological | Plains, agriculture, airbase states | Airbases, crops, light infrastructure | Low | Food pressure, thunderstorm, flood | Crop relief and airfield repair | Baseline and I |
| Extreme wind | Meteorological | Plains, urban states, storm belts | Infrastructure, factories, rail, airbases | Medium | Flood, wildfire, storm corridor | Clear roads, shelters, bridges | Baseline, abnormal in III |
| Wildfire | Climatological | Forest, drought, heat, dry lightning states | Infrastructure, resources, factories, rail | Medium | Flood after burn, refugee pressure, drought | Firebreaks, evacuation, slope stabilization | Baseline, strong in II |
| Drought | Climatological | Drylands, agricultural regions, repeated heat zones | Supply, agriculture, stability, resources | Low immediate, rising delayed | Wildfire, heat, famine, dust | Food imports, water rationing, irrigation | Baseline, broad in II |
| Sandstorm and dust storm | Meteorological | Deserts, arid zones, drought states | Supply, air, recon, infrastructure | Low to medium | Drought, heat, famine pressure | Clear routes, shelter, airfield protection | Baseline region-gated, broad in II |
| Blizzard | Meteorological | Cold regions, mountains, high latitudes | Rail, hubs, airbases, ports, supply | Medium | Cold wave, avalanche, thaw flood | Heating shelters and pass clearing | Baseline region-gated, I and II |
| Extreme heat wave | Climatological | Hot regions, urban states, drought zones | Output, manpower, supply, stability | Medium to high delayed | Drought, wildfire, storm break | Cooling shelters, water convoys, labor protection | Baseline local, II regional, no stack with Event 051 |
| Extreme cold wave | Climatological | Cold regions, high latitudes, winter fronts | Output, supply, rail, fuel strain | Medium to high delayed | Blizzard, thaw flood | Heating shelters, rail thaw, food distribution | Baseline local, II regional |
| Dry mass movement | Geophysical | Mountains, rail passes, quake zones | Rail, infrastructure, hubs, forts | Medium | Flood from blocked river | Pass clearance and evacuation | Baseline and I |
| Wet mass movement | Hydrological | Wet mountains, cyclone paths, volcanic slopes | Rail, infrastructure, hubs | Medium to high | Flood, lahar, disease | Slope stabilization and road rebuild | Baseline and II |
| Volcanic eruption | Geophysical | Volcanic regions and island arcs | Infrastructure, airbases, rail, factories, ash | High near volcano | Lahar, tsunami, ash crop shock | Evacuation, ash clearing, water repair | Baseline rare, massive in III |
| Tsunami | Geophysical or hydrological | Coasts after quake, volcano, landslide, meteor | Ports, naval bases, dockyards, coastal industry | High in dense coasts | Flood, disease, refugees | Evacuation, port reopening, debris clearance | Baseline follow-up, severe in II and III |
| Avalanche | Geophysical | Snowy mountains and passes | Rail, infrastructure, passes, forts | Medium | Isolation, blizzard | Pass clearing and rescue | Baseline as winter profile |
| Glacial lake outburst flood | Hydrological | Glaciated mountains and downstream valleys | Flood, rail, infrastructure | Medium to high | Landslide, refugees | Drain lakes and reinforce bridges | Evolution II optional |
| Sinkhole or ground collapse | Geophysical | Karst, mining, dense urban states | Local infrastructure and rail | Low to medium | Inspection mission | Local repair and evacuation | Baseline rare |
| Limnic eruption | Geophysical | Volcanic lake regions | Population and panic | High local | Refugees | Evacuation and containment | Rare region-gated |
| Meteor shower | Extra-terrestrial | Random impact clusters, coastal or ocean profiles | Crater, fire, infrastructure, airbases | Medium to extreme | Wildfire, tsunami, skyfall | Impact response and fire suppression | Evolution III abnormal |
| Global rupture | Geophysical | World rupture bands and epicenters | Multi-state buildings, supply, aftershocks | High aggregate | Tsunami, aftershocks, landslides | Global rescue and repair | Evolution III abnormal |
| Massive eruption | Geophysical | Volcanic region plus ash field | Epicenter devastation, ash, air closure | High and delayed | Tsunami, lahar, ash crop shock | Evacuation and ash recovery | Evolution III abnormal |
| Storm corridor | Meteorological abnormal | Moving state path | Severe path damage and adjacent warnings | High in path | Flood, hail, wildfire | Evacuation, shelters, bridge reinforcement | Evolution III abnormal with GUI |
