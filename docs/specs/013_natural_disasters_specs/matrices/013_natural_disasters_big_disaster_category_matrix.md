# Event 013 Natural Disasters big disaster category matrix

All labels are working labels and are not final localisation.

| Disaster | Big category required | Opens when | Must include mission | Must include non political cost | Can cause million scale deaths |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flood | Flood Relief Authority | Serious flood, basin flood, capital or dense state hit, regional flood aftermath. | Yes | Yes, trains, trucks, pumps, support equipment, fuel. | Yes, in dense basins or unresolved famine chains. |
| Tropical cyclone | Cyclone Emergency Command | Landfall in port, island, capital coast, dense coast, or multi state storm. | Yes | Yes, convoys, trains, dockyards, fuel, air or navy XP. | Yes, through surge, flood, island famine, dense coast. |
| Thunderstorm outbreak | Severe Storm Response Board | Serious storm line, airbase damage, flash flood risk, crop shock. | Yes if serious | Yes, support equipment, air XP, trucks. | Rare, mostly through follow up flood or famine. |
| Hailstorm | Hail Damage Board | Regional crop or airfield damage. | Optional unless regional | Yes, support equipment and repair material. | Only through delayed famine chain. |
| Extreme wind | Wind Damage Control | Serious airbase, rail, industry, or dense urban swath. | Yes | Yes, trucks, support equipment, air XP. | Possible in dense urban swath, more likely in Evolution III corridor. |
| Tornado corridor | Storm Corridor Command | Evolution III moving storm path. | Yes | Yes, shelters, rail, trucks, fuel, support equipment. | Yes, especially dense industrial belts. |
| Earthquake | Seismic Emergency Authority | Serious quake, capital quake, dense state quake, aftershock chain. | Yes | Yes, support equipment, army XP, manpower. | Yes, dense seismic regions. |
| Great rupture wave | Great Rupture Command | Evolution III seismic chain. | Yes | Yes, national engineer costs, trains, fuel, support equipment. | Yes, required possibility. |
| Tsunami | Tsunami Coastal Command | Tsunami warning or impact. | Yes | Yes, convoys, trains, navy XP, support equipment. | Yes, dense coasts and islands. |
| Volcanic eruption | Volcanic Crisis Board | Eruption, lahar risk, ashfall on important state. | Yes | Yes, air XP, trucks, support equipment, manpower. | Yes in dense volcanic regions or ash famine. |
| Massive volcano | Massive Eruption Command | Evolution III eruption pulses or volcanic ring. | Yes | Yes, large ash clearing and food costs. | Yes, required possibility. |
| Wildfire | Firefront Command | Serious firefront or spread risk. | Yes | Yes, fuel, trucks, infantry equipment, support equipment. | Possible in dense urban interface and firestorm chains. |
| Drought | Drought and Famine Board | Water emergency, crop failure, regional drought. | Yes | Yes, trains, trucks, fuel, grain import, convoys. | Yes through famine in dense regions. |
| Extreme heat | Heat Emergency Board | Dense urban heat, water emergency, regional heat dome. | Yes | Yes, water, shelters, trucks, fuel, support equipment. | Yes in dense heat domes and failed water systems. |
| Extreme cold or blizzard | Winter Emergency Directorate | Fuel shortage, frozen rail, regional cold, dense exposure risk. | Yes | Yes, fuel, trains, support equipment, manpower. | Yes in dense fuel shortage and refugee exposure. |
| Sandstorm | Dust Emergency Board | Serious dust belt, airbase closure, desert supply cut. | Yes if serious | Yes, air XP, filters, wells, trucks, fuel. | Rare, through water and refugee collapse. |
| Wet mass movement | Landslide Rescue Board | Landslide, mudslide, isolated valley, blocked rail. | Yes | Yes, engineers, trucks, support equipment. | Possible in dense valleys and dammed river follow up. |
| Dry mass movement | Slope Collapse Response | Rockfall, pass closure, mine collapse. | Yes if serious | Yes, engineers, trucks, army XP. | Rare, mostly delayed through isolation. |
| Meteor shower | Skyfall Emergency Bureau | Evolution III or manual meteor impacts. | Yes | Yes, shelters, skywatch, crater rail, fires. | Yes, required possibility. |
| Meteor storm barrage | Meteor Storm Command | Maximum manual or severe Evolution III. | Yes | Yes, national shelter, rail, air closure, relief. | Yes, required possibility. |
| Famine and displacement | Famine and Displacement Commission | Follow up pressure from any major disaster. | Yes | Yes, food, trains, convoys, shelters, medicine. | Yes, required possibility. |

No row in this matrix allows a fixed death amount. All death outputs come from per state population times dynamic loss rate.
