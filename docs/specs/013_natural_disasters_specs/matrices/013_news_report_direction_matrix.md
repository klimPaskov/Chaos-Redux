# Event 013 news and report direction matrix

This matrix gives direction only. It is not final localisation.

| Family | News threshold | Report focus | Remark direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Earthquake | first major quake, dense state, capital, aftershock chain | rescue, rails, collapsed buildings | clocks, rails, walls, bridges, and dust in streets |
| Flood | severe flood, cross-border river, capital, disease risk | water, depots, clean water, evacuation | waterlines, floating stores, cut roads, relief boats |
| Cyclone | port, island, naval base, storm surge | harbor, aircraft, coastal evacuation | broken cranes, grounded planes, wrecked quays |
| Hailstorm | aircraft loss, crop ruin, capital hit | airfield and crop damage | shattered glass, dented aircraft, ruined fields |
| Blizzard | supply crisis, capital cut, army trapped | blocked passes, fuel, exposure | frozen rail points, snowbound convoys, stranded villages |
| Heat | dense urban hit, hospital overload, drought chain | water, hospitals, rail heat | empty wells, overheated trains, night shelters |
| Drought | famine risk, wildfire risk, regional spread | food, water, livestock, rail shipments | dry reservoirs, ration lines, cracked fields |
| Dust storm | air operations hit, desert supply route hit | visibility, convoys, aircraft | dust-darkened roads, closed airfields, lost columns |
| Wildfire | dense evacuation, industry or resource loss | fire line, refugees, smoke | red night skies, roads under smoke, ash on factories |
| Volcano | eruption, ashfall, lahar, tsunami risk | ash, airfields, valleys, evacuation | dark noon, ash on runways, hot mud in valleys |
| Tsunami | any populated coast or major port | coast withdrawal, wave, port loss | silent harbor, empty foreshore, inland debris |
| Meteor | major impact or shower | impact site, fires, craters | night sky, blast windows, hot fragments |
| Rupture wave | first Evolution III global or continental wave | region-wide fracture, aftershock, tsunami | cracked rails, displaced coastlines, impossible tremors |
| Moving storm | path crosses several states | moving corridor, next regions | storm wall, rail belt, dark horizon |

Reports should always identify the disaster and place. News should stay varied and throttled.
