# Natural Disasters Recovery Decision and Mission Matrix

All names are working labels and not final localisation.

| Family or situation | Emergency decision | Stabilization mission | Reconstruction project | Non-political costs and requirements | Success outcome | Failure or neglect outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Earthquake | Deploy rescue and engineers | Clear collapsed rail corridor | Rebuild damaged city districts | Support equipment, manpower, trucks, rail or hub control | Lower delayed deaths, restore supply faster | Aftershock deaths rise, supply collapse lasts longer |
| Flood | Pump floodwater and open shelters | Hold river crossings and supply depots | Rebuild levees and bridges | Trucks, fuel, support equipment, supplied divisions | Reduced deaths and famine pressure | Disease, famine, refugee pressure |
| Tropical cyclone | Evacuate coast and secure ports | Keep port open during recovery | Rebuild naval base and dockyards | Convoys, trains, fuel, civilian factory burden | Port returns earlier, fewer deaths | Port closure, displaced population, industry loss |
| Thunderstorm and hail | Repair communications and airfields | Restore local supply route | Rural recovery program | Support equipment, air XP or fuel for airfield work | Reduces air disruption and food shock | Local stability loss, crop pressure |
| Extreme wind | Reinforce shelters | Clear roads and bridges | Harden emergency infrastructure | Infantry equipment, support equipment, command power | Lower casualties and faster movement recovery | Additional bridge collapse and supply strain |
| Wildfire | Firebreak mobilization | Hold evacuation corridors | Reforest and stabilize burned slopes | Manpower, trucks, fuel, support equipment | Reduces spread and future flood risk | More deaths, later floods, refugee pressure |
| Drought and heat | Water and cooling shelters | Keep food distribution stable | Irrigation and reserve program | Convoys, trucks, civilian factories, stability tradeoff | Reduces delayed deaths and famine | Famine pressure, wildfires, migration |
| Sandstorm and dust | Shelter and respirator distribution | Clear rail and airfields | Route hardening in arid regions | Support equipment, trucks, fuel, rail control | Restores supply and air operations faster | Prolonged supply and air penalties |
| Blizzard and cold | Emergency heating shelters | Clear mountain passes | Winterization of rail and depots | Fuel, trains, support equipment, supplied divisions | Lower deaths and attrition | Isolation, attrition, rail closure |
| Mass movement | Rescue isolated valleys | Reopen pass or tunnel | Slope stabilization works | Engineers abstraction, support equipment, construction capacity | Supply resumes, future slide risk lower | State remains isolated, more deaths |
| Volcanic eruption | Evacuate slopes | Clear ash from airfields and rail | Ash recovery and water restoration | Trucks, masks via support equipment, civilian factories | Reduces ash deaths and air closure | Lahar, delayed deaths, crop shock |
| Tsunami | Coastal evacuation | Reopen port and water supply | Coastal rebuilding program | Convoys, trucks, construction, port control | Large death reduction if before wave | Port disabled, high displaced population |
| Meteor shower | Fire suppression and evacuation | Secure impact zone | Rebuild cratered infrastructure | Support equipment, manpower, trucks, supply | Lower fires and panic | Fires spread, refugees, industry remains damaged |
| Storm corridor | Evacuate predicted path | Hold next-state supply corridor | Harden corridor route | Trains, trucks, fuel, support equipment, divisions in threatened states | Reduces next step damage | Corridor shift causes heavy casualties |
| Global rupture | National rescue coordination | Repair critical supply network | Regional rebuilding grants | Scaled by industry, support equipment, trains, manpower | Prevents long supply collapse | Long national recovery and stability shock |
