# Event 013 Natural Disasters, second-pass public research notes

These notes record external sources used as design grounding for the second pass. They are not final localisation and do not select super-event quotes, remarks, or audio.

| Source | Used for | URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EM-DAT Disaster Classification System | Hazard grouping and distinction between geophysical, hydrological, meteorological, climatological, biological, and extra-terrestrial hazard groups. | https://doc.emdat.be/docs/data-structure-and-content/disaster-classification-system/ |
| UNDRR Sendai Framework overview | Recovery framing around risk, vulnerability, capacity, preparedness, and recovery. | https://www.undrr.org/implementing-sendai-framework/what-sendai-framework |
| UNDRR Build Back Better terminology | Late reconstruction direction and resilience-focused recovery design. | https://www.undrr.org/terminology/build-back-better |
| USGS earthquake effects | Earthquake damage identity, including ground shaking, faulting, ground failure, and tsunami links. | https://www.usgs.gov/programs/earthquake-hazards/what-are-effects-earthquakes |
| NOAA tropical cyclone hazards | Cyclone hazard identity using wind, storm surge, currents, and inland flooding. | https://www.noaa.gov/jetstream/tc-hazards |
| NOAA tornado resources | Tornado damage and safety framing for path-based warning and shelter design. | https://www.noaa.gov/education/resource-collections/weather-atmosphere/tornadoes |
| NOAA flood hazards | Flash flood and flood behavior for warning and chain design. | https://www.weather.gov/safety/flood-hazards |
| USGS Volcano Hazards Program | Volcano preparedness, hazard mapping, and volcanic hazard framing. | https://www.usgs.gov/programs/VHP |
| World Bank GFDRR Resilient Recovery | Recovery framework direction and damage, loss, needs, and planning framing. | https://www.gfdrr.org/en/resilient-recovery |
| WMO Early Warnings for All | Multi-hazard warning direction and the idea that warning systems must be hazard-specific and actionable. | https://wmo.int/activities/early-warnings-all/wmo-and-early-warnings-all-initiative |

## Design use boundaries

- These sources support hazard grouping, warning logic, recovery phases, and family identity.
- They do not authorize final super-event quotes, final cultural remarks, final titles, final audio choices, or player-facing localisation.
- Any source-dependent super-event wording still requires the super-event text research workflow.
