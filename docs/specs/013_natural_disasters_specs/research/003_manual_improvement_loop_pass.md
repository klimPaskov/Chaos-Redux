## Depth checks passed in this package

- The event is not a single popup. It is a reusable disaster engine with delayed seasons, external calls, aftermath, chains, UI, and scenario support.
- The design avoids generic news spam by making news family-specific early and throttled later.
- The aftermath system asks the player to spend concrete resources and complete recovery work instead of buying flat political power modifiers.
- Evolution II adds regional systems, chained deaths, and recovery pressure rather than only stronger numbers.
- Evolution III adds abnormal disasters, scripted GUI map needs, animations, and super-event roles without turning the event into a terminal branch.
- Old Earth Earthquake and Sandstorm logic are not preserved.
- Achievements reward prevention, recovery, and rare abnormal survival rather than event firing.
