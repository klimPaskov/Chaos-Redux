# Achievement prompt for Event 013 Natural Disasters

Use the Event 013 specification package as source design. Do not write final achievement localisation without review. Working labels below are not final titles.

## Achievement set

Implement or plan achievements that reward preparation, response, recovery, rare abnormal survival, and manual scenario mastery. Do not unlock an achievement merely because Event 013 fired.

| Working id | Difficulty | Unlock direction | Disqualifier direction | Icon direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `013_after_the_sirens` | medium | Fully recover from a severe disaster season as the affected country with all aftermath cards closed. | unresolved card after deadline | shelter light under storm cloud |
| `013_no_second_wave` | hard | Prevent a delayed tsunami chain after an offshore quake, volcanic collapse, or ocean impact. | second wave creates major death spike | broken wave and coast marker |
| `013_every_bridge_counts` | medium | Clear transport recovery after regional flood, cyclone, earthquake, or blizzard without capital supply failure. | capital supply route fails | rail bridge under repair |
| `013_ashes_without_famine` | hard | Survive major ashfall and prevent famine chain in all affected states. | ash famine matures | mask and grain sack |

## Implementation tracking needs

Some achievements need tracking flags or variables because the final state alone will not prove the route. Track tsunami prevention, maximum barrage launch, family group recovery, refugee death thresholds, and rupture follow-up prevention in the Event 013 ledgers.

## Asset needs

Each achievement needs a completed 64x64 icon direction and later grey plus not-eligible variants following the achievement icon workflow.
