# Super-Event Prompt for Event 013 Natural Disasters

Use `chaos-redux-super-events` for quote, cultural remark, audio, image direction, and full super-event research documentation. Use `chaosx_super_event_text_researcher` for quote and cultural remark research and `chaosx_super_event_audio_researcher` for licensed audio research. Use `chaos-redux-event-assets` and the Event 013 asset prompt for image production.

Do not use unresearched working labels as final localisation. Super-event titles, button text, quotes, cultural remarks, slogans, lyric fragments, allusions, and audio choices are research gates until verified and documented.

## Super-event candidates

### Meteor shower threshold

Role: abnormal high-chaos escalation.

Trigger direction: Evolution III meteor shower with multiple impact states, a capital or major city impact, or Disaster Barrage maximum skyfall behavior.

Tone: awe, fear, shattered normality, and practical horror from many places seeing the same sky event.

Image direction: generated 457x328 super-event image with period-authentic observers, city or countryside silhouettes, multiple fire trails, and impact glow. No readable text.

Quote direction: research public-domain or historical text about falling stars, judgment, celestial disorder, or omens. Avoid invented prophecy.

Cultural remark direction: short grim or ironic reaction about the sky becoming unsafe. Research required.

Audio direction: licensed or public domain track with mass awe and dread, preferably orchestral, choral, or solemn, not a sound effect or oscillator cue.

### Global rupture threshold

Role: abnormal global escalation that integrates the old Earth Earthquake concept.

Trigger direction: first Evolution III global rupture family occurrence.

Tone: worldwide physical instability, broken roads, shattered cities, uncertain aftershocks, and delayed tsunami fear.

Image direction: generated super-event image showing cracked urban ground, damaged rail or port, and people amid a broad rupture scene. Keep period visual language.

Quote direction: research public-domain or historical text about the earth shaking, foundations failing, or the ground as a false certainty.

Cultural remark direction: short bleak reaction about the ground no longer being reliable. Research required.

Audio direction: solemn dramatic music with low pressure and rising threat. Must be a real licensed track.

### Massive eruption threshold

Role: regional devastation and atmospheric shock.

Trigger direction: Evolution III massive eruption with regional ash cloud, high casualties, or tsunami follow-up.

Tone: ash, choking light, evacuation, buried transport, and fear of delayed harvest or air closure.

Image direction: generated super-event image with volcanic ash plume, darkened sky, evacuation or ruined settlement, and strong central composition.

Quote direction: research public-domain text about fire, ash, mountains, or buried cities. Avoid overused generic apocalypse wording.

Cultural remark direction: short remark connected to ash, buried roads, or darkened day. Research required.

Audio direction: ominous and heavy, with slow musical structure.

### Moving storm corridor threshold

Role: high-chaos moving hazard reveal.

Trigger direction: first Evolution III moving storm corridor becoming visible on the dynamic disaster map.

Tone: a storm that behaves like a moving actor, with fear created by path prediction and uncertainty.

Image direction: generated super-event image of an enormous storm column or tornado corridor crossing landscape, with period observers, rail, fields, or city edge for scale.

Quote direction: research public-domain text about wind, wrath, storms, or being unable to command nature.

Cultural remark direction: short reaction about the path of wind or the road of the storm. Research required.

Audio direction: tense, kinetic, and musical. Do not use pure storm ambience.
