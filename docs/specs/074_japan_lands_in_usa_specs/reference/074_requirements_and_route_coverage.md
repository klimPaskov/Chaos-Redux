## Design choices made during expansion

The proposed baseline is 30 immediate divisions, increasing to 60, 100 and 150 at the three tiers.
The small frozen campaign factor adjusts for very large American armies without making a weak Japan ineligible.
Both the numbers and the support taper are untested tuning anchors.

Active evolutions follow the supplied project rule for delayed progression.
They enlarge the existing expedition through retained access and never repeat the surprise territorial seizure.
American emergency formations are purchased from real resources.
There is no scripted peace, new country, focus-tree replacement, extra hidden evolution, or separate strategic currency.

The interface is a native decision-category treatment because its actions can be understood through the normal map and supply views.
Static assets are sufficient.
A single researched tier III super-event recognizes a realized multi-region invasion.
These choices keep the expanded package centered on the user's mainland-war concept.
