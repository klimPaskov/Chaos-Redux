## American actions

Provide a compact generated program, family emphasis, independent job inspection, pre-commitment cancellation where actually supported, and any necessary choice between valid earned research benefits. The shared event picker creates waves. Decisions cannot spawn unlimited extra random waves or reroll target countries by reopening a menu.

Bind every action to its real job, slot generation, actor, target and current stage. Revalidate on execution. The displayed date and selected family must come from that job. Several simultaneous tests cannot share a mutable selected-state variable.

Use the supplied preparation and analysis schedules. Cancellation forfeits development and does not refund already consumed resources. A committed weapon cannot have a fictitious recall option when its provider cannot abort. AI USA executes valid actions without a hidden random stop chance.

## Victim actions and real objectives

Implement the single Domestic Hostility track and the small set of meaningful recovery and diplomatic actions. Access restoration requires real repair of test-damaged routes and supply access. Productive-district restoration requires actual repair or rebuilding of the specified damaged or removed levels. Foreign relief spends real resources and supports a useful valid objective. Protection uses existing diplomatic agreements and real guarantee rules.

Recovery normally runs for at least 90 days, using the specified longer 180 to 365 day missions for these map objectives. The five-day ultimatum is a separate emergency response. A timer reaching zero is not proof that a railway or factory was repaired. Failure can apply its specified once-only hostility change, but must not invent population deaths because a mission expired.

New tests add genuine new damage and preserve previous repair history. Do not reset every deadline to create an easy extension. Repairs reduce selected hostility through verified completion and never restore dead people, erase condemnation records or cancel a refusal war.

Containment and disease recovery remain actions of the existing owner. Link to them and consume verified outcomes. Do not implement a separate biological spread or cure system in this decision category.

## Cost and interface rules

Keep normally three to five primary actions, at most six, and one to three meaningful missions visible in the applicable context. Use no more than four spendable resource kinds in one action, normally two or three. Inline costs show at most three values with correct text icons. The tooltip must disclose every real debit, reservation, capacity requirement and restriction.

Use inclusive affordability so resources exactly equal to a cost enable the action. Derive button state, tooltip and actual debit from the same current calculation. Execute one exact debit. A required convoy route is not automatically a consumed convoy cost. Do not charge a cost merely to open a read-only result or inspect a job.

Prototype permissions, state validity and family research are requirements unless a real owner consumes something. Recovery supply costs follow the proposed support-equipment and truck scaling only after verifying actual resource ownership and useful objectives. Do not create a fallback civilian-factory allocation that the engine or current owner cannot represent.

Native categories, decision icons and mission text must follow the actual layout. A category picture is conditional on a compatible simple surface. Do not add a large scripted GUI solely to show the job list. Write final localisation from the direction in the specs, with correct dynamic country, state, family, dates and recorded losses.
