# 01 · The testing program

## The campaign it creates

The United States turns foreign minor countries into temporary test ranges. Its armed services want different kinds of results, so each firing allocates several experiments across suitable countries. A government receives a named test, a threatened war, and a schedule. Acceptance starts the experiment. Refusal makes the United States the attacking country in a real war.

For the United States, the attraction is sustained military development across its actual arsenal. A tank-gun trial helps armor development. A bombing trial helps aircraft, bombs or the relevant doctrine. A prototype nuclear trial moves an immature program forward without granting an unrestricted atomic arsenal. Rewards arrive after the destruction and analysis, and remain tied to the experiment that produced them.

For a targeted minor, the immediate decision is severe and simple. It must choose between the announced destruction and war with the United States. The longer campaign concerns restoring usable transport and industry, preserving independence, securing real foreign help, and deciding whether another demand can be endured. Repeated acceptance does not create immunity.

The Low cluster assignment remains fixed even when later experiments become catastrophic. Cluster membership, event type and actual physical severity are separate parts of the design. A testing wave counts as one Event 076 firing. Each experiment, progress notice, analysis report and aftermath update belongs to that wave and does not become another random-event draw.

## Ordinary progression

A valid firing establishes a wave and a set of independent test records. AI USA commissions every valid available assignment. Player USA chooses an emphasis from generated eligible programs or declines the wave. Once the program is committed, targets receive their individual ultimatums. Accepted experiments move through preparation, impact and analysis. Refused experiments terminate as tests and create wars. Completed experiments leave permanent loss records, real damage, domestic consequences and delayed American benefits.

Several waves can overlap. One country's repair work may continue while another experiment is being prepared elsewhere. The United States should not need to click through an arrival notice for every observer or engineer. Those intermediate changes belong in the selected test's status text unless they introduce a new decision or a material change.

The program persists as an institution between random firings, but it does not manufacture extra waves on its own. Every new wave still comes through the shared Event 076 selection route. Existing analysis and contamination continue without rolling another event.

## Three perspectives

The American side chooses useful tests, commits equipment or research capacity where the relevant owner requires it, and receives delayed reports. Its choices influence family selection, delivery development and follow-up benefits. It cannot turn cancellation into a development reward.

The victim side receives the exact two-choice ultimatum. After accepting, it can move its own units through ordinary game controls, arrange outside assistance, and prepare reconstruction. These actions do not replace or cancel the accepted physical strike. After impact, the country must work with the damaged map and surviving population.

Other governments can react through existing diplomacy, guarantees, sanctions and useful relief. Their participation should depend on relationships, capacity and actual stakes. A global news report does not force every country to join a new faction or start a war.

## The tone

Victim reports describe concrete places and consequences: a damaged rail junction, a ruined factory district, hospitals without access roads, or another envelope arriving at a ministry that is still counting the previous dead. Avoid interchangeable tragedy text that could describe any disaster.

American reports describe the tested system and the result obtained. Their detached language should expose the government's priorities without pretending that the losses are imaginary. Irony belongs in the difference between what the two countries regard as the important result. Do not make victims the target of the joke or attach comic sound effects to civilian deaths.

Country names, political titles and references to current leadership must follow the actual game state. The program does not assume that every United States campaign is democratic, that every victim is European, or that a particular historical leader remains in office.

## Persistent consequences with limited clutter

Use one new public mechanic value, Domestic Hostility, on affected countries. Use existing XP, technology, project progress, Condemnation, population and building interfaces for the rest. Active-test counts, dates and recorded losses are facts shown in reports, not additional currencies.

At most three Event 076 national spirits may coexist in one country. The intended package uses one American development institution and one victim backlash institution, with reconstruction support primarily state-scoped. Multiple experiments update these institutions and their records instead of creating a spirit for every test.
