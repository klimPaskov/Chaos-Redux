# 12 · International consequences

## A public program with public responsibility

Every completed test creates an international incident attributed to the United States. An acceptance extracted under threat of war does not reduce the incident to voluntary cooperation. The target's signature is part of the coercion record. Refusal creates a separate war incident through the existing war and diplomatic owners.

An ultimatum can already cause opinion loss and concern before the impact. Separate that preliminary record from the later completed-use record. A canceled proposal must not be recorded as a nuclear detonation, chemical attack or disease outbreak. Conversely, an actual impact must not escape condemnation because the American analysis is still unfinished.

The program does not acquire a secret mode. Unexplained illness can be described from the victim's limited medical knowledge, while the American test authorization remains attributable. A disease owner's discovery rules may reveal additional consequences later. They must not erase the known coercive program or invent knowledge about every secondary outbreak before discovery.

## Use the shared Condemnation system

The supplied mechanics documentation places shared standing thresholds at 25, 50, 100, 175, 300 and 500. Those thresholds belong to that system. Event 076 contributes incidents and evidence, not a second international reputation meter with competing thresholds.

The incident input should include the weapon family, conventional or unconventional status, actual civilian and military losses, measured building damage, removed building levels, coercive acceptance, previous completed tests, previous tests in this victim, confirmed contamination, cross-border spread, and current convention status. Freeze the original impact facts. Add later confirmed harm as distinct supplements with unique receipt identities.

Proposed conventional tuning starts with 5 points for a completed ordinary trial and 10 for an explicitly heavy trial. Add 5 for documented coercion and a repeat-program supplement of 5 per previous completed test in that country, capped at an additional 20. Actual death and destruction bands can contribute further shared severity, with their exact thresholds owned by the existing incident adapter. These are proposed Event 076 inputs, not verified names or current constants in the shared system.

Nuclear, chemical and biological incident values come from their owning use routines. Event 076 supplies the coercion and repeat-testing context where the owner permits it. It must not apply a second full nuclear-use penalty after the nuclear owner has already recorded one. Continuing outbreak consequences likewise use unique additional harm receipts, not a fresh charge for the entire accumulated total each month.

If the current Condemnation owner cannot represent a public conventional testing atrocity, extend its generic incident contract. Do not route every ordinary artillery test through a falsely named chemical or nuclear incident merely to obtain a penalty.

## Reactions must have actual effects

Early reactions consist of worsened opinion, reduced willingness to cooperate, and public censure. Higher shared tiers can cause the already supported sanctions, restrictions, recalled support and embargoes of participating countries. The supplied mechanics explicitly distinguishes new arrangements from existing agreements that may remain in place. Event 076 does not claim that all lend-lease, licences or research-sharing vanish automatically.

The supplied mechanics also makes trade embargoes dependent on By Blood Alone. Without that expansion, apply the other supported diplomatic and economic responses and show the actual result. A text-only declaration that an unsupported trade embargo is active is not an acceptable substitute.

Powerful countries may guarantee threatened minors or respond to requests for protection. A guarantee must be a real diplomatic relationship produced by a legal, owner-supported action. It is not a free event-local promise that automatically turns into a faction war. Existing guarantee, subject and faction rules determine the actual response to an American declaration.

A guarantee obtained after an ultimatum does not automatically cancel the test. It can make refusing more credible. If an actual new diplomatic relationship makes the intended declaration invalid, the pending ultimatum must be invalidated before it can present a false war consequence.

## Political pressure and international meetings

Domestic anti-American pressure in victims is represented by the single Domestic Hostility track described in [10](10_victims_and_recovery.md). American domestic pressure uses shared Condemnation consequences and the limited institutional idea only where supported. Do not add a national spirit for every angry foreign government.

A substantial series of incidents can invite a conference through an existing international diplomacy owner. Its proposed agenda is inspection access, guarantees, relief, sanctions and restrictions on future testing. Participants have distinct interests. A country rebuilding after a test has different priorities from a major power seeking to contain American development.

A conference does not automatically force AI USA to stop. The brief requires AI USA to continue valid testing. A resulting real diplomatic restriction may remove particular legal targets or weapon permissions, and therefore change which tests are valid. It must not create an event-local hidden refusal chance for otherwise valid American tests.

The meeting must either produce actual agreements and supported effects or remain a news and opinion response. Do not build an additional generic conference management system solely for Event 076, and do not present narrative proposals as enacted sanctions.

## Relief and accountability

Foreign aid can deliver real equipment or finance actual reconstruction through an established owner. The recipient must have a useful eligible objective, and the donor must pay the displayed resources once. Supplies arriving after an objective has been completed must be redirected through a valid new agreement or canceled before debit.

A joint effort that restores Event 076-damaged access in three distinct affected countries may earn the once-only direct Chaos reduction of 2 proposed in [08](../integration/08_damage_and_deaths.md). This cannot remove deaths from history or refund the nuclear-use ladder. Each country needs a separate verified repair objective and contribution receipt.

Survivors can continue to blame the USA after relations improve. Repairing a railway does not invalidate previous casualties, repeated-target memory, treaty violations or achievement evidence.

## Connections and limits of ownership

| Connection | What Event 076 supplies | What remains with the other owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deaths | Test, actor, state and exact loss attribution | Physical population transaction, shared totals, military aggregation, Deaths-to-Chaos |
| Condemnation | Public coercion, repeat program and actual damage context | Incident scoring, tier penalties, sanction participation and UI |
| Chemical and Biological Weapons Convention | Test intention and actual use, applicable treaty status | Prohibitions, inspections, membership, violations and diplomatic consequences |
| Missiles | A valid experimental job and impact context | Available delivery systems, test articles, technologies, launch effects and native costs |
| Nuclear development | A narrowly scoped prototype or mature test request | Project permissions, nuclear impact, fallout, nuclear-use accounting and research APIs |
| Chemical and biological systems | Responsible actor, accepted state and causal experiment identity | Agent eligibility, contamination, outbreaks, spread, containment and later mortality |
| Existing relief and diplomacy | Real damaged objectives and requests | Agreements, transport, guarantees, war participation and resource transfer |

Do not borrow a country-private scientist, Soviet nuclear or other event capability simply because its effects resemble a desired reward. It must have an explicitly reusable public interface. Shared mechanics can expose such an interface, but Event 076 must not trigger another random event to obtain it.

Fire, displacement, economic failure or unrest may connect where an existing owner accepts this cause and can return its consequences. These are conditional integrations. The physical test and its mandatory Deaths accounting must not depend on inventing several unrelated crisis systems first.

The supplied catalog and the newer convention references disagree about Event 036. The brief's named Convention connection is preserved. Its current owner and ID must be verified in the working repository before any numeric connection is wired.
