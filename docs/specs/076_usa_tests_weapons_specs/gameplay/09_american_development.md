# 09 · American development

## Useful results after analysis

Every successful completed experiment gives the USA a meaningful benefit associated with its actual weapon family. The reward is normally paid after the analysis delay. The impact cannot grant the reward early and the report cannot grant it again.

Record the development package at commissioning, including a preferred research or project target and permitted same-family alternatives. At payout, revalidate the current USA, the target and its remaining headroom. A canceled, refused, physically unexecuted or unverified experiment has no successful-test package.

Reward size follows the selected program's ambition, novelty and verified execution. It does not increase with civilian deaths. A more destructive outcome than expected can increase condemnation without increasing Army Experience. Deliberately choosing a populated state to maximize a casualty counter must not become a second reward multiplier.

## Initial reward scale

Use the following as central calibration anchors, subject to specialist and campaign testing. These are design values, not claims about current implementation.

| Tested family | Baseline successful analysis | Evolved development direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artillery and heavy artillery | 25 Army XP plus one 50 percent artillery research bonus | 50 Army XP plus a stronger relevant bonus or supported artillery production benefit |
| Tactical, strategic and incendiary air | 25 Air XP plus one 50 percent matching aircraft, bomb or bombing-development bonus | 50 Air XP plus a relevant project, doctrine or 100 percent research bonus |
| Armor and anti-armor | 25 Army XP plus one 50 percent matching armor or anti-tank research bonus | 50 Army XP plus supported design, reliability or production development |
| Aircraft weapons | 25 Air XP plus one matching weapon or aircraft-development bonus | Improved weapon-specific research or production support |
| Conventional rockets and missiles | 25 relevant XP where supported plus one 50 percent rocket or delivery bonus | 50 relevant XP or 10 to 20 percent of a valid project's remaining development budget |
| Naval guns and torpedoes | 25 Navy XP plus one 50 percent matching naval-weapon bonus | 50 Navy XP plus naval doctrine or relevant production development |
| Nuclear prototype | Major relevant nuclear research assistance, initially two 50 percent bonuses or supported project progress | Advances a still-incomplete nuclear development path without issuing a mature stockpile |
| Mature nuclear trial | Valid later nuclear or delivery progress | A supported 10 to 25 percent temporary production improvement for an existing program |
| Chemical or biological trial | Owner-approved research or development benefit for the tested family | Supported production, deployment or project benefit within that owner's rules |

A successful ordinary test should generally provide one experience component and one development component. The alternatives in a row are not instructions to award every listed benefit simultaneously. A provider without a meaningful XP channel can deliver a correspondingly substantial relevant development result instead.

## Research, projects and doctrine

Research bonuses must reference the current installed category or technology and remain available to the recipient. They should not be consumed by an unrelated infantry technology merely because it shares a broad catch-all category. Resolve ahead-of-time effects separately and do not grant arbitrary instant technologies to work around a missing category.

Special-project progress must be issued through the actual project owner. The table's percentage anchors are planning magnitudes. The implementation must translate them into the owner's supported progress unit and avoid exceeding the real remaining work. Optional DLC absence can use a documented technology-based alternative in the same field.

Doctrine rewards follow the project's current doctrine system. If it uses mastery choices or steps, use that owner interface. Do not assume obsolete doctrine-research technologies still exist or convert every test into a generic doctrine technology unlock.

## Production and reliability

Temporary production development belongs in one consolidated American institution, with at most two different family benefits active at once and a combined bonus cap defined by their actual modifiers. Start individual benefits at 10 percent for 90 days, with advanced profiles reaching 25 percent for 120 days. These figures must be validated against native modifier scope.

A benefit affects its real weapon category. It cannot silently become global factory output because a family-specific modifier is harder to wire. If the installed game cannot represent the desired narrow production or reliability effect, choose the documented same-family research or project alternative and report that substitution in the provider inventory.

Repeating a family refreshes or improves its bounded benefit according to the stored receipt. It does not stack unlimited permanent modifiers. A second institution slot is earned through useful completed families, not created for every report.

## Headroom and reward claims

Check reward headroom before committing a test and reserve its intended benefit where practical. Other jobs must not all promise the same final project progress. If the preferred research finishes during analysis, resolve a valid same-family alternative.

When every permitted alternative is temporarily capped, retain a pending typed claim. Do not replace it with Political Power, silently discard it, or exceed an engine XP cap. The job remains in the reward-ready state and occupies capacity until the claim is delivered. This gives a USA player a reason to spend experience or direct further research before commissioning more of the same work.

The claim is persistent and does not expire just before the player opens the report. If the country disappears, suspend its unclaimed reward under the original program identity. A released successor does not automatically inherit rewards unless the shared country-identity policy explicitly recognizes it. Never pay them to the victim or to the country currently executing a stale callback.

## Partial results and failed experiments

A delivery owner can report a genuine partial experiment with useful data. It may receive a clearly smaller, same-family development result, initially 25 percent of the normal non-XP benefit, only when the owner supplies evidence that a real test phase occurred. It is recorded as partial, does not trigger successful-impact achievements and does not qualify for the nuclear super-event.

The default is no partial reward for a canceled proposal, refusal, invalid state or missing capability. A technical failure cannot become a safe way to farm full research without performing the promised experiment.

## Player and AI research direction

Player USA chooses a generated emphasis, reviews expected typed benefits and can inspect individual pending results. It does not need to choose a reward for every small report unless the owner requires a genuine doctrine or project selection.

AI USA favors active and underdeveloped military research, uses valid headroom, and spreads work across families. It always proceeds with valid testing even when international condemnation is high. Its decision model must distinguish a missing capability from an unattractive diplomatic consequence.
