# 11 · Decisions and missions

## Two ordinary decision contexts

Use an American testing-program category and an affected-country response category. A country can see both if it legitimately has both roles, such as American territory suffering owner-supported spillover. Use normal decision controls, native timed entries and existing state selection where supported. No additional management window is required by this design.

Display the selected experiment's state, family, stage and next date, with its important warning or reward below. Keep at most five primary gameplay actions visible and at most three active objectives. Test records are informational entries, not dozens of duplicate action buttons. A selected-record filter can focus details without changing simulation ownership.

The categories should not expose raw internal IDs, provider revisions, receipt hashes or numbered slot fields. Their tooltips must explain a blocked action in terms of the actual game situation.

## American actions

| Working action | When it appears | Cost or commitment | Result and AI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commission generated program | A valid wave awaits a USA choice | Only the real provider commitments disclosed for its assignments | Starts demands once. AI chooses a valid research-directed program and always proceeds |
| Select research emphasis | Several valid program directions exist | No spendable cost | Changes family preference before commission, with the actual generated alternatives shown |
| Inspect selected experiment | There is an active or recent record | No spendable cost | Opens its ordinary details or report, with no gameplay reward |
| Cancel uncommitted experiment | Player USA, before impact commitment | Forfeits its future reward and retains already spent work | Closes that job and releases only genuine reservations |
| Resolve available development claim | An owner requires a real research or doctrine choice | No additional purchase of the earned reward | Chooses within its valid typed reward pool and consumes the claim once |

Selecting emphasis cannot reroll targets endlessly. Generate the candidate set once per wave. Allow comparison between its valid program options without issuing fresh random draws. A decline closes the wave proposal and does not reopen it for free.

Conventional commission has no mandatory generic Political Power fee. Use actual provider capability and any genuine consumed test article or resource commitment. Optional physical weapons such as missiles or mature nuclear devices must be debited by their owner. Never destroy reusable delivery platforms as an invented commissioning price.

## Victim actions

The ultimatum uses its two event responses and does not add a third safe bargain to this category. The category manages the consequences and preparation around that choice.

| Working action | World requirement | Initial spendable costs | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Organize district reconstruction | A recorded damaged district remains under friendly control | Support equipment and trucks, scaled to damage | Starts a 60 to 90-day state repair-support allocation and the actual reconstruction objective |
| Request a specific foreign relief package | A willing valid donor and usable route exist | Up to 25 Political Power for the diplomatic effort | Sends a real offer to the donor, without granting supplies before acceptance and delivery |
| Send a relief package | A selected valid victim, route and actual transferable stock exist | At most three disclosed equipment or transport types | Transfers the chosen real package once and records delivery |
| Seek protection | A selected potential guarantor can legally provide protection | Up to 25 Political Power for the request | Requests a real guarantee, with no guarantee of acceptance |
| Open owner containment response | A live owner contamination or outbreak exists | Costs remain entirely with the owning action | Exposes the real response and its current requirements |

Do not keep all five visible when they are irrelevant. A country that cannot donate should not see a donor action. A victim with only conventional damage should not see a biological containment placeholder. The selected donor, recipient or district is revalidated on execution.

## Dynamic cost scale

For district reconstruction, start with 50 support equipment and 25 trucks for a limited incident. Scale in steps of 25 with recorded damaged or destroyed capacity, required access work and the intensity profile, up to 250 support equipment and 150 trucks for the initial allocation. These are provisional game-balance anchors.

A country without trucks may use an explicitly disclosed owner-supported local-work alternative, initially support equipment plus a temporary civilian-factory allocation. The factory allocation must be a verified native or shared-owner reservation, not a guessed effect. If it cannot be supported, do not offer that alternative as implemented. Ordinary construction and external aid still remain possible.

Aid packages use the donor's real available equipment and the recipient's needs. A convoy route is normally a non-consumed capacity requirement, not an automatic sacrifice of ships. Landlocked cases require a real valid land-access route. The action cannot silently switch to instant overseas delivery because the intended route is difficult.

No action may charge more than four distinct spendable cost types. Normally use two or three. Inline cost text shows at most three values with the correct resource icons, and full tooltips show every spendable charge, reservation, non-consumed requirement and refund condition. Use one inclusive affordability predicate for availability, display and final debit.

## Missions with actual objectives

The access mission requires a selected damaged route or installation to be restored, the region to remain controlled, and the relief destination to be reachable. Its standard deadline is 180 days, with a disclosed 240-day severe profile. On success, remove that access pressure and record a verified recovery milestone. On failure, keep the real damage and worsen backlash by 5 once. Do not manufacture extra deaths solely because a UI timer expired.

The district mission requires the restored productive capacity described in [10](10_victims_and_recovery.md). Its 240 or 365-day deadline reflects the actual severity. Success reduces Hostility and ends the event-owned reconstruction pressure. Failure leaves the ruined area and any owner crises in place. A new attempt must use the remaining real damage and cannot farm another success from already restored buildings.

A containment mission mirrors an existing owner's objective and deadline. It has no separate cure timer. The owner determines success or failure. Event 076 records the result and updates its incident history once.

## Concurrency and interface integrity

One victim may have multiple past tests but should see a consolidated recovery view by affected district. Keep the most urgent unresolved objective and allow record selection for the others. Hiding an old entry for readability does not pause its simulation or remove its costs.

AI evaluates every valid candidate district, recipient or response. The player's selected UI filter must never restrict the AI to one random visible row. AI cannot use a cheaper hidden path that skips the same resource, route and ownership checks.

Native event and decision timers must agree with displayed dates. Timed preparation entries are not success missions, and analysis reports do not require an empty completion click to grant an already earned automatic benefit.

## Completion evidence

Before accepting the implementation, inspect and render the actual ordinary category surfaces through the available HOI4 tools. Check empty, crowded, ongoing-war, no-route, capped-reward, canceled-job, repeated-victim and long-country-name cases. Source-only inspection does not establish visual readability. Any future custom scripted GUI requires a separately justified design and its event-owned UI worker.
