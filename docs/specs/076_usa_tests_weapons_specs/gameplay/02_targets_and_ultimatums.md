# 02 · Targets and ultimatums

## A target must be a real minor with a credible refusal war

Build the candidate pool from the current world. A country must exist, be foreign to the United States, be classified as a minor under the project's current country rules, and control a valid inhabited state for at least one available weapon provider. The normal route uses an owned and controlled state so the government receiving the demand can meaningfully answer for it. Occupied or split-control cases require an owner-approved contract that identifies the actual recipient and affected population.

The United States must exist as the acting country and be able to issue the intended declaration against that candidate. A currently allied country, a direct American subject or another diplomatic case in which the required war cannot legally be created is excluded until a supported declaration route exists. This is a legal-effect gate, not protection for friendly or weak minors. A neutral minor with a powerful guarantor remains eligible when the United States can actually declare war on it.

Countries already at war with the United States are outside this particular ultimatum pool. Refusal cannot start the promised new war in that situation. They may re-enter after peace and the normal repeat delay. The military campaign itself is not treated as consent to a weapons test.

Do not exclude a country merely because it is small, poor, irrelevant to major-power strategy, recently defeated, or likely to suffer heavy losses. Use `uses_normal_civilian_systems` where the physical population contract requires it. Do not treat every special Chaos country as nonhuman. An actual nonhuman target may be eligible for a compatible military-only provider, but may not receive fabricated human civilian losses or a human disease outbreak.

## Select countries and experiments together

A country is eligible only when at least one matching family can produce real effects and a meaningful typed reward. Select a country, choose its compatible family and state, and remove that country from the remainder of the current wave. The normal wave has one experiment per country. A later wave can select the same country again.

Give a valid player-controlled minor a strong weight advantage, initially a fivefold country weight relative to an otherwise identical AI minor. This is a design calibration, not a guaranteed engine probability. Different player minors remain separate candidates. The United States player cannot manually replace generated victims with an unrelated protected favorite or turn every test into a farm on one cooperative multiplayer partner.

Geographic variety is a soft preference. After the first selection, halve the weight of candidates in a continent already represented when an equally valid unrepresented continent exists. Do not exclude a candidate solely to fill a geographic quota. Within the selected country, favor states that match the family and have not recently been struck. If only one country or one suitable state exists, use it after its cooldown. Do not invent another target.

## Repeat targeting

Start the country repeat delay at 60 days after its latest completed impact or resolved refusal. Evolution I reduces this to 45 days. Evolutions II and III permit 30 days. Start the same-state delay at 120 days, reduced to 90 days with Evolution I. These are minimum scheduling gaps and must be kept in central tuning.

The delay is measured from an actual resolved incident, not from opening and canceling a selection window. A canceled uncommitted proposal creates no lifetime protection. A canceled ultimatum can impose a short administrative retry delay of 15 days to prevent immediate identical reissue.

Previous victims regain eligibility. Their state suitability can outweigh the remaining soft novelty preference once the hard delay ends. Evolution I adds a modest preference for proven testing terrain, but the same-country exclusion within a wave remains. Different country identities created by civil war do not erase the physical state's history.

## Weapon-specific geography

Air and strategic-bombing providers favor actual industrial, transport, airfield or defensive capacity. Artillery favors valid land test areas and does not require a peacetime American land border. Armor trials favor a usable land area and relevant targets. Missile providers choose valid impact states through their delivery owner, with large target areas and important installations affecting preference. Naval providers require an appropriate coast, port or owner-supported sea interaction. Nuclear and unconventional providers use their own target and exposure rules.

No provider can claim that a generic state loop has inspected all rail links, coastal fleets or deployed units. Those are separate engine capabilities. Their absence is an implementation gate for the affected variant.

## The two responses

**Acceptance** confirms the named family, target state, disclosed severity band, expected timing, physical risk and American beneficiary. It authorizes the full announced test. It does not silently buy evacuation immunity, reduce the strike through a Political Power payment, or compensate the victim with free industry. Preparation begins only after acceptance is recorded.

**Refusal** atomically closes the test and has the United States declare war on the refusing country. The target does not become the attacker. The declaration is not a war goal that may or may not be used later. No strike or American test reward follows from the refused job. Allied intervention then follows the real diplomatic situation and normal war rules.

A player receives five days to respond. The visible deadline must agree with the actual timeout. Acceptance is the default when the deadline expires and the test remains valid. The interface must disclose this. Do not rely on an undocumented event-window default to enforce a different timer.

## Changes while the demand is open

Revalidate the country, state and declaration contract immediately before either response is committed. If a new faction relationship, country disappearance or other change makes the promised refusal war impossible, withdraw the demand. Do not secretly replace refusal with a penalty or switch the test to a different sovereign country. This withdrawal is recorded as invalidation, grants no test reward and cannot be counted as an achievement refusal.

A country becoming a major before resolution invalidates an unanswered demand. Once it has accepted while valid, later major status alone does not erase the experiment. A change of state owner or controller before impact invalidates the location unless the same legal authority still holds the accepted rights through a verified owner contract. The safe default is cancellation of that location with no reward, followed by a fresh demand in a future wave.

The victim can seek a guarantee while a demand is open. A guarantee changes the real war risk and its AI's decision, but it is not an automatic cancellation button. Joining the American faction only invalidates the threat when that relationship actually prevents the declaration.

### Scripted war capability

Check whether the actual scripted declaration can create the required USA-led war. Do not use the ordinary diplomatic button's justification timer, ideology restrictions or world-tension threshold as a blanket gate when the existing event-war owner can legally bypass those requirements. Otherwise an early democratic USA could incorrectly lose the entire event. Same-faction, subject, missing-country and other genuine declaration conflicts still require current engine verification.
