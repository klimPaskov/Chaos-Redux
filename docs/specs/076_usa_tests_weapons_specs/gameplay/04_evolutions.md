# 04 · Evolutions

## Opening profile and later activation

A first firing at high Chaos may open with every enabled qualifying evolution already active. At 450 Chaos, for example, the first wave can use The Great Testing Program and Nuclear Proving Grounds. At 650 Chaos it can also use Living Laboratories, provided its required weapon providers are valid. The opening announcement must show the families that are actually available, not promise an absent owner implementation.

After the program has begun, reaching a threshold starts an eligible evolution's delayed activation process. The initial design uses a 90-day MTTH setting. HOI4's MTTH semantics and the exact active-event implementation must be verified through the repository's MTTH skill and probability tools. Do not replace that process with a guaranteed day-90 transition or an immediate threshold check.

Use a USA-owned, bounded activation check while the program exists. Each enabled stage may activate once. Process newly eligible stages in ascending order. A disabled lower stage is skipped and does not make an independently enabled higher stage impossible. A higher stage does not silently re-enable disabled lower-stage content.

Pending activation requires the relevant Chaos threshold to remain satisfied when the activation is evaluated. Once activated, a stage is remembered. A later fall in Chaos does not by itself remove the learned testing capability. Disabling that evolution in settings stops new assignments from its content. It does not erase a completed impact, cancel ongoing owner contamination or rewrite an already accepted experiment.

Only new assignments use an activated stage's extra content. Existing jobs keep their accepted profile and their original reward scale. An evolution report records the actual actor and activation once, with no direct Chaos award merely for unlocking a stage.

## Baseline

The baseline program commissions conventional and eligible experimental weapons. It does not require every field to have a mature stockpile, but a prototype must have a real provider that explains how the test article exists. Artillery, aircraft weapons, bombs, armor and anti-armor, explosives, coastal ordnance and supported rockets are suitable starting families.

Baseline experiments must leave a meaningful loss. A strike on a populated industrial state can kill thousands and damage several useful buildings. A small country does not receive a harmless version solely because it is small. Its real population and infrastructure still bound what can actually be lost.

The ordinary wave begins with two assignments before research-breadth expansion. Country and state novelty are favored, and the American institution initially converts results into basic experience and focused research assistance.

## Evolution I · The Great Testing Program

**Chaos requirement: 200+.** American testing becomes an established multi-service program. Larger raids, longer bombardments, advanced missiles, experimental armor and aircraft weapons become more common when their providers exist. The normal wave baseline rises to three assignments, the same-country repeat delay falls, and useful prior ranges become more attractive.

Conventional profiles gain a default 50 percent intensity increase, with an upper 100 percent increase reserved for explicitly selected heavy variants. Apply the family profile to actual physical mutations. Do not multiply a casualty estimate again after a provider has already applied the heavier variant.

Heavy variants must change the nature of the damage as well as its quantity. A prolonged artillery trial can leave multiple defensive and transport installations damaged. A heavy incendiary trial leaves an owner-approved fire or recovery burden when that capability exists. An armored development trial can produce a useful specialized improvement without becoming an air-bombardment reskin.

The USA receives stronger typed development packages after analysis. Its reward advantage comes from a more ambitious tested program. It does not come from calculating experience per civilian killed.

## Evolution II · Nuclear Proving Grounds

**Chaos requirement: 400+.** A testing-only nuclear prototype route becomes eligible. The wave baseline rises to four assignments. The United States can order a prototype test before mature nuclear weapons are researched, but the prototype must be authorized through a nuclear development owner and must produce real nuclear-scale destruction.

The new permission applies to the specific test article and accepted target. It does not grant a reusable nuclear stockpile, a free reactor network, unrestricted missile warheads, or general first-use permission. After the experiment, the American benefit advances the actual nuclear research or project that is still relevant to its campaign.

Once mature weapons exist, the provider changes its development objective. It can improve delivery, later bomb projects or the existing production program. Repeated tests remain possible in the same minor across different waves. The system remembers that repeated nuclear testing is especially damaging politically, even after ordinary repairs have restored some buildings.

The first actual successful foreign nuclear impact qualifies for the separate presentation in [17](../presentation/17_nuclear_super_event.md). A threshold, proposal, canceled experiment or failed owner call does not qualify.

## Evolution III · Living Laboratories

**Chaos requirement: 600+.** Chemical, biological, disease and contamination providers can enter the catalogue. The wave baseline rises to five assignments. They require the relevant owner's current technology, weapon availability, deployment or prototype authority, valid target rules and effect contract.

Chemical agents, nerve-agent categories, biological weapons and weaponized diseases remain abstract game families. Smallpox is an explicitly requested example and may appear only through the existing owner-approved disease pathway. Event 076 does not invent its own epidemiology, manufacture instructions, exposure recipes or additional disease engine.

An accepted chemical test can leave a contaminated state whose later losses continue through the chemical owner. A biological test can open an outbreak with consequences beyond the first state where the owning disease system supports spread. The American analysis can finish while the victim is still fighting that outbreak. Every later recorded loss retains its causal connection to the test where the owner supports attribution.

The event's diplomatic burden increases through actual unconventional use, treaty status, confirmed losses and spread. A successful technical report does not mean the experiment was safe or that the aftermath has ended.

## Interaction between stages

A later stage does not replace the entire conventional catalogue. Artillery, aircraft, armor, naval and missile development remain useful alongside nuclear and biological options. Diversity and research relevance continue to influence selection at high Chaos.

Disabling Living Laboratories removes new unconventional allocations while leaving nuclear and conventional providers subject to their own settings. Disabling Nuclear Proving Grounds removes new Event 076 prototype permission. It does not delete weapons legitimately held through another owner or remove already applied fallout.

If an owning system is absent, list the affected variant as unavailable with a specific reason. Preserve the design requirement and implementation blocker. Do not label an ordinary bomb strike as a nuclear test, rename a stability penalty as an outbreak, or pretend that an unsupported military-damage route has harmed units.
