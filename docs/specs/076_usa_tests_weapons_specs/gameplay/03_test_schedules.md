# 03 · Independent test schedules

## Size and capacity

Use a wave baseline of two experiments at baseline, three with Evolution I, four with Evolution II and five with Evolution III. Add up to two experiments when multiple distinct American research families are active, one extra per two qualifying active families. Add one further experiment when at least eight eligible provider families exist. The final wave is bounded by eight distinct countries, actual valid country-family pairs and free job capacity.

The opening target should normally contain several countries. When only one valid minor exists, a one-country wave is allowed and explicitly shown as a reduced wave. Zero valid assignments makes the event unavailable. Never fill the wave with empty or impossible tests.

Start with 24 simultaneous jobs from ultimatum through reward settlement. This is a capacity limit on schedules, not a limit on remembered victims or continuing disease systems. Owner-controlled contamination can continue after its experiment is analyzed. Compact closed records preserve achievements, responsibility and cumulative totals without keeping a full active job forever.

A full job queue makes additional wave entry temporarily unavailable. Existing jobs continue. Do not discard an older experiment to make room for a new one, reset the shared random-event timer for each job, or create extra global daily scanning.

## Life of a test

| Stage | Action and player information | Exit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proposed | USA receives generated valid programs and their expected benefits | Commission or decline |
| Demand issued | Victim sees the target, family, severity and five-day choice | Accept, refuse, or invalidation |
| Preparing | Range personnel and delivery preparation appear in the record | Preparation completes or circumstances invalidate the test |
| Committed | Required owner resources are consumed once and the final impact context is locked | Physical execution |
| Impact applied | Buildings, population, units and owner contamination change | Receipt verified |
| Analyzing | USA receives a dated estimate for its results | Analysis finishes |
| Reward ready | The typed reward is granted or held for valid headroom | Settlement |
| Closed | Losses and diplomatic consequences remain in compact history | Any continuing owner tails operate independently |

A refusal closes the job before preparation and starts the promised war. A canceled job has no physical impact and no successful-test benefit. A failed verification is not a completed experiment.

## Timing that creates a sequence

The first demand can be issued on the wave's opening day, with additional demands staggered over the next one to five days. Initial preparation bands are 10 to 20 days for artillery and armor, 10 to 25 for air and naval trials, 15 to 30 for rockets and missiles, and 20 to 40 for nuclear or unconventional trials. The provider may narrow those bands when its existing mechanics require a particular schedule.

Stagger same-day impact candidates through deterministic offsets. Experiments should not all resolve on one tick just because the wave was created in a single effect. Different countries can have overlapping preparation, impacts and analysis.

Analysis takes 5 to 15 days for ordinary land trials, 10 to 20 for aircraft and naval trials, 15 to 30 for missiles, and 20 to 40 for nuclear or unconventional work. Biological analysis does not wait for every later outbreak to end. It produces its research receipt while the disease owner continues handling actual consequences.

Ordinary arrival and preparation stages are status changes. They do not become separate missions requiring the player to press a completion button. An arrival becomes a popup only when it changes a decision, cancels the announced experiment, or reveals a consequence the government needs to act on.

## What is frozen

At acceptance, freeze the country, state, family, provider revision, announced severity profile and permitted delivery route. Freeze the maximum declared expenditure and intended typed reward package when the American side commits it. Store the evolution profile that authorized this assignment.

Use current surviving population, actual buildings and units physically present at impact when calculating the real result. Freezing the announced profile must not let a later callback remove people who have already migrated or died. It also must not let a newly unlocked evolution replace an accepted artillery trial with a nuclear or biological trial.

At impact, freeze the applied casualty and damage receipts used by reports. Later reports can add clearly dated owner-tail losses but cannot rewrite the original impact total. The American analysis report should distinguish its experiment's immediate results from continuing contamination or disease.

## A mainland emergency does not undo an experiment

If Japan's Event 074 landing is active, the testing program retains its ordinary foreign target rules and independent schedules.
An actual invasion changes America's resource situation, not the minor's accepted test or the USA's responsibility for it.
Provider affordability must exclude resources already reserved for a western defense formation or transport project; defense likewise excludes provider reservations.
When several generated assignments require the same finite article or capacity, commission them against one reconciled available pool rather than quoting that article to every job.
A commitment accepted by one owner is unavailable to the other until its own cancellation or completion contract releases it.

Player USA can decline the generated wave, cancel an uncommitted test, or let the foreign program continue while paying for mainland defense from the remainder.
Cancellation releases only the test's real unspent reservation and earns no development result.
A committed impact and subsequent analysis continue unless their existing provider conditions become invalid; a lost American port does not erase accepted rights in an unrelated foreign state.
Any completed weapon-family result enters the normal research or production consumer and can help future defense without becoming another emergency division grant.

AI USA still proceeds with every valid assignment.
A provider genuinely unable to reserve its required article or capacity is invalid; condemnation, the landing's existence, or a desire to spare resources is not a hidden refusal branch.
Future acceptance cases pair one competing reservation with [Event 074's U01 or U03 action](../../074_japan_lands_in_usa_specs/design/074_japan_lands_in_usa_spec_part_4_decisions_and_campaign.md), then cover player cancellation before commitment and an impact already committed when coastal recovery occurs.
With Event 074 absent, these remain the ordinary provider-resource and cancellation rules.

## American cancellation

A player-controlled United States can decline the generated wave or cancel an uncommitted experiment. A declined wave uses the shared event's ordinary firing history and does not refund the random draw. Cancellation sacrifices the reward, releases only resources that were actually reserved, and returns no already consumed equipment or spent industrial work.

Once the physical effect has been committed, the cancel action disappears. Analysis cannot be canceled to undo casualties or remove condemnation. AI USA does not cancel a valid experiment because its target is weak, its Condemnation is high, or the likely harm is severe.

## A worked sequence

A baseline wave selects three minors because American research breadth grants an extra slot. One receives an artillery demand, one an air trial, and one a coastal ordnance demand. The second country refuses. That job starts a real American war and grants no bombing reward. The artillery and coastal experiments continue on their own schedules. Their damage is applied on separate days, their reports use separate states and casualty receipts, and their Army and Navy benefits arrive after different analysis delays.

A later wave may choose the first victim again after its repeat delay. Its first test remains in history even if its capital changes, and an unfinished reconstruction objective continues without being restarted. New damage can make reconstruction harder, but the original mission's deadline and recorded goals cannot be silently reset by the second wave.
