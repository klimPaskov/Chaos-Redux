# 07 · Nuclear and unconventional trials

## A test-specific nuclear prototype

At Nuclear Proving Grounds, request a one-use experimental article from the nuclear development owner. This request must explicitly identify the USA, the development field, one accepted test record and one target state. Its permission cannot be used by ordinary war decisions, stockpile screens, another country's missile system or a second experiment.

An immature program may need a research allocation or temporary test article that the owner supplies. The brief requires access before mature nuclear weapons are fully unlocked, so requiring completion of the mature bomb technology would fail the design. Conversely, setting a broad nuclear-unlocked flag or issuing reusable warheads would exceed it.

The prototype is consumed exactly once at impact commitment. The owner performs a nuclear-class impact and returns real population, building, military, fallout and shared-accounting results as supported by its interface. Event 076 must not run that owner and then apply a second nuclear damage package of its own.

For planning, use a population-loss calibration envelope of 500 to 1,500 basis points of the target state's current civilian population for a prototype and 1,000 to 2,500 for a mature proving-ground profile. These are fictional game-balance targets. The nuclear owner remains responsible for its valid internal profile, actual footprint and technical limits. If its established profiles already determine exact loss, tune or select an appropriate owner profile. Do not multiply the same loss again.

Structural consequences should be substantially greater than a conventional trial. A populated industrial state can lose multiple actual building levels and suffer major damage across its surviving installations. Unit and equipment harm must use verified local targeting. A nuclear animation alone does not satisfy the requirement.

## Mature nuclear development

A USA with mature nuclear capability can use a real stockpile article or an owner-approved dedicated research article. The provider identifies the source before the ultimatum is sent and consumes it once. A bomb already reserved for another test or launch cannot be promised twice.

Its reward resolves toward relevant later development: a further nuclear project, improved delivery, strategic-bombing development or supported nuclear production. It must not grant the same completed first-bomb technology repeatedly. A nuclear power with no usable reward headroom does not receive an empty development report.

Repeated nuclear tests can strike the same minor across later waves. There is no permanent nuclear-victim immunity. Real remaining population and installations naturally limit later damage. Their earlier destruction remains in history and increases the political significance of the next request.

## Chemical trials

At Living Laboratories, the chemical owner can expose supported agent families, including nerve-agent categories where they actually exist. Eligibility depends on the owner's technology, article availability, protection rules, contamination behavior and testing permission. Event 076 does not invent agent recipes, release methods or chemical-performance figures.

The owner applies the immediate exposure and contaminated-state state. Its existing protections and physical rules remain authoritative. Acceptance means the full selected owner profile is applied. It does not bypass real protection already built into the game, and it does not create a new purchase that makes acceptance harmless.

Long-term loss, air contamination and clearance remain with the chemical system. The test's country and state records consume actual owner receipts. Any later reduction in contamination comes from the owner's response systems and does not refund already registered deaths.

## Biological and disease trials

Biological providers identify an existing owner-controlled disease or weapon system. The rough brief explicitly includes weaponized diseases and smallpox. Those categories must be considered where the current owner supports them. Their names do not authorize an invented epidemiological model.

The experiment requests a bounded, abstract outbreak initiation through the owner. It passes the USA as the responsible actor and the test's causal identifier where supported. The disease owner handles exposure, incubation, outbreaks, spread, protection, mortality and recovery. Event 076 handles the demand, schedule, research reward and political consequences.

An outbreak can spread beyond the accepted state. Those later infections are a continuing consequence, not another American testing wave. They can harm allies or the USA itself if the owning system permits that spread. The event must not grant the American public or its armed forces automatic immunity.

American analysis is tied to the completed experimental phase, not to the death of every infected person. A result can be useful to the military program while the outbreak continues. Additional deaths do not generate an endless stream of experience or repeated research rewards.

## Aftermath and ownership

A fallout, contamination or outbreak instance may outlive the test job, its victim government, a civil war or the United States itself. Keep physical aftermath on the relevant state or owner instance. Keep responsibility in the causal record. Do not clear a plague because an event-local timer expires or because the original tag was annexed.

Reports distinguish immediate deaths, subsequent confirmed deaths and ongoing danger. The local situation can worsen after the American success report. A later summary updates cumulative totals from verified owner receipts and does not subtract the same deaths again.

The integration must support idempotent receipts and responsibility metadata. If an owner cannot expose them, the required extension belongs at its neutral shared boundary and must be documented and validated there. Duplicating its mechanics inside Event 076 is not an acceptable solution.

## Convention and international response

The Chemical and Biological Weapons Convention influences the existing norms and response to unconventional use. Its implementation identity must be resolved by content, because the supplied catalog still labels Event 036 differently. A planning document's existence does not prove the convention has been implemented.

Treaty status, prohibited use, contamination and disease spread affect Condemnation through the relevant owners. Coerced acceptance does not make the action diplomatically harmless. The technical success of a biological test does not cancel treaty consequences.

If a convention mechanic requires an explicit withdrawal or breach declaration before a test can legally execute in game, that must be handled as a real owner operation. AI USA chooses the available proceeding route when a valid test remains possible. The event cannot bypass an owner lock silently or cancel every valid test solely to avoid condemnation.

## Required implementation gates

Nuclear prototype permission, nuclear-scale damage, accurate military-loss attribution, owner-tagged continuing deaths and disease initiation are required capabilities. Their exact runtime interfaces were not all available for inspection in this planning session. Keep them visible as implementation gates and do not present generic bomb damage, a population modifier or a flavor-only outbreak as completion.
