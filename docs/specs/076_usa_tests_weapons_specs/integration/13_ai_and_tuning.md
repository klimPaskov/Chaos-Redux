# 13 · AI and tuning

## American behavior

AI USA commissions every valid wave selected by the shared event engine and proceeds with every valid assigned test. Its proceed branch has the entire valid probability mass. There is no concealed moral refusal, random cancellation or condemnation-based stop chance. Invalid jobs can still cancel through the same validation applied to a player.

A player USA receives a generated program and meaningful family emphasis. Research priorities influence the generated set, while the player can select between valid alternatives without freely rerolling target countries. Cancellation forfeits development and does not refund consumed test resources. A noncommittal player cannot keep the same proposal open forever while its targets or rewards change.

AI USA selects an appropriate reward for the completed family with available headroom. A capped experience pool must lead to another supported same-family benefit or an unresolved earned claim, not a silent zero reward presented as a successful payout.

## Build complete pools before rolling

Selection proceeds through eligible countries, compatible families, concrete providers, and valid states. Do not roll an attractive family first and then silently discard every country that cannot host it. The candidate construction must guarantee that each selected country has at least one physically valid test and a credible refusal-war path.

Every weight is evaluated only within its complete current pool. The figures below are proposed relative weights. They are not observed implementation probabilities. Normalize the final pool, including every active modifier and every candidate, in the mandatory AI probability audit.

| Selection level | Proposed weighting |
| --- | --- |
| Country base | 100 for every eligible country |
| Valid human-controlled minor | Multiply by 5 |
| Continent already represented in this wave | Multiply by 0.50 while an unrepresented eligible continent remains |
| Useful previously tested country with Evolution I enabled and active | Multiply by 1.50 after its repeat delay, without removing human priority |
| Family base | 100 for every family with a usable provider |
| Family matching an active American development priority | Multiply by 2 |
| Family not yet successfully tested in the last 180 days | Multiply by 1.50 |
| Family already selected in this wave | Multiply by 0.25 while another compatible family is available |
| State with a strong family-relevant target | Multiply by 1.50 |
| State used recently | Excluded during its state cooldown, then normal eligibility |

Do not multiply family weight by the number of equipment variants in that family. Choose the family first and then a compatible current or prototype provider. This prevents a large collection of aircraft variants from making artillery and naval research disappear from the distribution.

If a partial or failed trial produced no useful data, it does not reset the full-success novelty memory. However, a short attempted-family memory may reduce immediate repeated failure against the same technical blocker. That memory cannot turn an invalid provider into an eligible one.

## Minor-country behavior

AI refusal must remain unusual but credible. Use a base relative pair of 100 for acceptance and 5 for refusal. A country with a verified credible major-faction or guarantee response uses an acceptance weight of 75 and refusal weight of 25. A militarily competitive minor already strongly hostile to the USA may use 50 and 50. These are proposed behavior bands, selected by explicit non-overlapping conditions.

Domestic Hostility can raise refusal weight by 5 at each of its 25-point bands, up to an additional 20. Do not use a nonexistent army ratio, imagined guarantee response or unsupported diplomatic estimate. A strength comparison must include the actual owner-supported measures and foreseeable allies. Merely having negative opinion is not proof that the minor can survive the war.

Neither choice becomes safe through AI evaluation. If refusal wins the roll, USA declares the real war. If acceptance wins, the full frozen test resolves after preparation. There is no hidden damage discount for AI countries.

Validate the weights for ordinary weak minors, protected minors, hostile but undefended minors, strong minors, human targets and countries whose diplomatic status changes while a demand is open. A branch with no legal war consequence is invalid before the choice, not a refusal outcome with a substituted opinion penalty.

## Tuning anchors

The wave formula and timings in [03](../gameplay/03_test_schedules.md), profiles in [06](../weapons/06_conventional_trials.md) and [07](../weapons/07_unconventional_trials.md), and rewards in [09](../gameplay/09_american_development.md) are the starting calibration. Their explicit values give implementation and playtesting a measurable starting point.

Use the enabled and activated profile for all new assignments. Country repeat delays are 60 days at baseline, 45 at Evolution I and 30 at Evolution II or III. State repeat delays are 120 days at baseline and 90 after Evolution I. The delay starts from an actual impact, or from refusal for the country record. A refused country is also ineligible while the resulting USA war exists. A canceled issued demand uses its separate 15-day retry delay.

The live program has 24 job slots. A wave uses at most 8, subject to free slots and distinct eligible countries. A job whose impact occurred but whose valid earned reward is still unresolved keeps its slot. This bounds load and makes missing research headroom visible. It must not silently discard the reward in order to free capacity.

Continuing disease and contamination belong to owner instances after analysis. They retain attribution but do not occupy an entire Event 076 scheduling slot indefinitely. The owner sends uniquely identified tail receipts, and the event keeps compact history needed for totals and achievements.

Only two family-specific production benefits can coexist within the American institutional package. Their individual proposed upper strength is 25 percent, their maximum proposed duration is 120 days, and the same family cannot add another parallel copy of itself. Refreshing or replacing an existing benefit must show the actual result. These caps do not authorize a new global output bonus, and overlapping native bonuses need a stacking audit.

Domestic Hostility's stability effects are losses in percentage points, represented through the chosen valid idea modifier. A displayed 5-point reduction must not be implemented as multiplying the country's current stability by 0.95. The top political-power penalty is a 10 percent generation modifier. State loss, mission failure and incident flags must not repeatedly reapply additive stability loss when the single idea updates.

## Damage and reward checks

Danger should remain visible at baseline. A populated industrial state must lose real people and useful buildings, while a small state can lose only what exists. The same test in a nearly empty state can be strategically less destructive without receiving an artificial minimum casualty count that exceeds available population.

Research benefit is based on the tested family and verified technical completion. It does not scale upward with deaths, civilian density, indiscriminate targeting or a later epidemic's death toll. A nuclear prototype should materially advance a real program but cannot grant a mature stockpile or finish an unlimited series of project stages through duplicate callbacks.

An analysis reward can use a family-appropriate secondary route if the original technology was completed naturally during the delay. It cannot switch from naval testing to unrelated political power because that is easy to grant. If no valid useful route exists, record the earned unresolved claim and explain it to the player.

## Evolution timing

After the initial high-Chaos opening, the proposed activation median is 90 days while the stage's eligibility remains true. This is not a guarantee that all games activate on day 90. The missing dedicated MTTH skill, installed semantics and required probability tools must be used before choosing the actual scripted scheduling formula.

A bounded event-owned implementation must preserve the intended timing distribution or document a measured deviation for correction. Do not use a new all-country daily loop to approximate it. Save and reload must not reroll a hidden countdown in the player's favor.

## Balance fixtures

| Fixture | Required observation |
| --- | --- |
| Early USA, two ordinary targets | Distinct countries, dangerous conventional damage, useful typed delayed rewards |
| Human minor among many AI minors | Strong measurable priority without making the human the only possible target |
| One eligible minor | A legal one-country wave, not fabricated additional countries |
| No eligible minors or no USA | Shared eligibility reports unavailable and no empty wave is counted |
| Several targets on one continent | Geographic novelty matters when valid alternatives exist |
| Evolution II before mature nuclear technology | A real restricted prototype works and advances a real program |
| Conventional and nuclear providers together | Nuclear testing does not crowd every other family out |
| Evolution III with absent disease provider | Missing capability is explicit, no fake outbreak or false completion claim |
| Repeated industrial victim | Actual cumulative damage and hostility survive between waves |
| All useful XP pools capped | Supported research or production remains useful, otherwise the claim stays unresolved |
| Twenty-four unresolved jobs | No overwrite, no extra free wave, no disappearance of existing claims |
| Major guarantee gained during preparation | Actual legal state is rechecked without silently retargeting the accepted test |
| Existing owner applies nuclear deaths | One population reduction and one shared death record |
| Long-lived outbreak after analysis | Later losses remain attributable without holding all scheduler slots |
| Multiplayer USA and two minor players | Same authoritative targets, clocks, impacts and rewards for every peer |
