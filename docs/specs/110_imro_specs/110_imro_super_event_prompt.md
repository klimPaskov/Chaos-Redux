## Research gates

Spawn the configured `chaosx_super_event_text_researcher` and `chaosx_super_event_audio_researcher` with separate bounded tasks and `fork_turns="none"`. Provide the complete slot context, current skill references, exact output paths and uncertainties. The text worker owns evidence for wording and attribution. The audio worker owns evidence for composition, performance, recording and permitted editing. The parent reviews their results and owns final framework integration.

Follow the repository's web research workflow. Prefer original publications, archival records, reliable scholarly editions, official collections and authoritative recording catalogs. Search snippets, quotation websites without provenance and unattributed reuploads cannot establish exact wording or usable rights. Preserve the original-language wording, edition or timestamp, translation provenance, author, date and precise source location for any quotation. Distinguish a faithful translation from a newly authored summary.

Research a meaningful cultural remark or allusion only where it improves the specific scene. Check copyright limits for lyrics, film dialogue, books and other protected material. Do not invent a proverb or attribute new text to a historical figure. A real IMRO-associated person must not be made to endorse the fictional extermination doctrine through misleading selection or attribution.

Unresearched titles, button text, quotes, cultural remarks, slogans, lyric fragments, allusions and audio choices are blockers. Do not convert these research directions, working labels, achievement concepts, asset names or the prompt's descriptions into final super-event localisation. Record uncertain attribution or licensing as unresolved. Produce the final text package only after source checks.

## Audio and image requirements

Choose public-domain or clearly licensed recordings with documented redistribution and editing rights. A public-domain composition does not make a modern recording free to use. Record composer, performer, recording date, source URL, licence, permission terms where relevant, selected excerpt, edit boundaries and final credit requirements.

Use distinct sonic identities for liberation, terminal transformation, costly survival and extinction. Normal excerpts run 60 to 120 seconds and do not exceed 120 seconds without a recorded justified exception. Produce the exact required WAV format and sound wrapper after inspecting the current framework. Verify duration, channels, sample rate, loudness, clipping, fades and the final ending. Synthesis, generated audio and an unlicensed temporary track are not final.

Coordinate each 457 by 328 image with the asset owner. Preserve appropriate documentary styling and native readability. The radical image is explicitly alternate-history art. The extinction image must not contain a living human figure. Source historical design elements with their actual attribution and rights rather than borrowing a modern party emblem as unqualified period evidence.
