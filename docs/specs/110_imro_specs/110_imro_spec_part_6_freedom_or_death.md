# Freedom or Death

## A simultaneous break with subject rule

An independent IMRO-led Macedonia that survives and demonstrates a credible commitment to liberation can inspire every country currently under subject rule to break away at once. The global news event announces the completed worldwide declaration. Each former subject is independent, each is at war with its direct former master unless that hostility already existed, and every one belongs to the National Liberation Front.

The declaration includes human-controlled subjects and unusual country types. It does not consult ordinary AI ideology preferences before granting independence. Already independent countries keep their sovereignty and can choose to join or support the Front. Former masters may recognise their subjects' independence and leave the relevant restoration wars, or fight to restore control.

The proposed evolution threshold is Chaos 400. Independent Macedonia must have survived for 180 days, have Mandate at least 55, complete an outward liberation-policy anchor and have a completed local independence receipt. A peaceful, consent-based regional association qualifies as credible outward policy. Large territorial conquest by itself does not. There must be at least one existing subject at the final snapshot. A world with no subjects receives no fictional universal uprising, no launch reward and no false receipt for the terminal prerequisite.

## Who participates

The cohort contains every instantiated country whose actual subject relation exists at the opening boundary. It is frozen before any relation changes. The cohort is not a list of releasable historical countries and does not create countries that do not yet exist. Fully annexed territories may have separate independence movements, but they are not existing subjects and therefore are not magically released by this particular transaction.

| Case | Required treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary subject | Becomes independent, declares the required direct-master war once, joins the Front |
| Human-controlled subject | Same mandatory opening, with country, units, politics, focus progress and player control preserved |
| Subject with its own subjects | Every direct relationship is frozen. Each subject becomes independent and fights its own direct snapshot master |
| Subject already at war with its master | Keep that real hostility and record it as preexisting. No duplicate declaration |
| Subject inside an existing faction | Independence and Front membership still occur. Operational faction migration uses a proven safe leadership and membership transaction |
| Subject that is itself a faction leader | Preserve noncohort members and select a valid live successor where necessary. Do not dismantle an unrelated faction to simplify the event |
| Special Chaos country or actual nonhuman subject | Included in the universal sovereignty transaction if it is an existing subject. Its native biological classification remains unchanged |
| Independent country freed by 006 or 063 earlier | Excluded from compulsory release, eligible for voluntary participation and aid |
| Country freed during reconciliation | Preserve the real release and original provenance, satisfy the row from observed independence, issue only still-missing obligations |
| Subject created after the snapshot | Not retroactively inserted. It can receive later liberation assistance through an explicit new local action |
| Country no longer existing at commit | Revalidate before opening. A stale identity blocks the planned opening until reconciled rather than silently dropping a participant |

The event performs one world snapshot and one originating transaction. Sequential engine effects implement logical simultaneity. There is no week-long rolling release in which early countries fight while later human subjects wait for an event choice. Reports follow the observed result.

## One Front, with operational military arrangements

The **National Liberation Front is an authoritative global coalition organisation**. Every frozen former subject becomes a member. Its membership, charter, aid, command mandates, disputes and regional settlements exist independently of the engine's one-faction-per-country restrictions.

A real game faction bearing the NLF identity coordinates mutually compatible participants. Countries whose existing wars or nested former-master relationships make that faction membership invalid remain full members of the Front organisation and receive its substantive aid and diplomacy. Their operational arrangement is shown clearly. The interface must never show a country as a member of the game faction when it is only a Front organisation member.

This distinction resolves the nested-subject case without weakening the declaration. If A controlled B and B controlled C, B and C both gain independence and Front membership. B fights A, and C fights B. B then chooses whether to recognise C or continue that internal restoration conflict. C is not redirected to attack A, and B is not forced to accept C. Once the internal conflict is settled, compatible operational faction membership may be consolidated.

The Front supplies real coordination outside its operational faction: equipment deliveries, training, diplomatic recognition, supply-access agreements, refuge, mediation and compatible war participation. These use each country's explicit commitments. Ordinary ideology-based admission restrictions cannot remove the compulsory initial Front membership, while later command delegation and retention remain meaningful political choices.

## Declaration order and guarantees

1. Freeze every subject, direct master, human-control state, autonomy, relevant existing wars and faction leadership before mutation.
2. Validate the complete cohort and its native release and war operations. Handle nested relations deepest first while preserving the original direct edges.
3. End each actual subject relationship and verify independence. No country changes hands merely to make a tag easier to script.
4. Register every former subject in the Front and establish safe operational arrangements.
5. Issue each missing direct-master war declaration once and verify hostility. An already hostile pair keeps its existing war provenance.
6. Publish the completed worldwide receipt, global news and member reports. Send each former master one coherent response covering its snapshot children.

The ordering of operational faction changes and declarations must be validated against the current engine so that no required war is blocked by faction membership. The result, rather than an assumed sequence from memory, governs acceptance.

No popup, AI refusal or player choice can veto the compulsory opening. There is no option that leaves a player-controlled subject under its master while freeing everyone else. Player choice begins with strategy after independence, master recognition, voluntary deeper commitments and the Front's future.

### Irreversible partial-result recovery

Persist exact intent before each native release, faction migration and declaration, then persist its observed result. The engine operations are not assumed atomic or generically reversible. A row can be prepared, issued, observed complete, preexisting complete or unresolved. Issued-but-unobserved effects are quarantined and inspected rather than blindly reissued after loading.

If an unexpected engine failure occurs after some subjects have become independent, keep those countries independent. Do not restore subject status as rollback, recreate a country, reset the frozen cohort or hide the failed participants. The episode remains explicitly partial and unresolved, with no completed worldwide receipt, success Chaos, achievement or celebratory presentation. Safely resume only a still-missing operation whose current postcondition proves it has not already occurred and whose original identity and authorisation remain valid. Preserve all actual wars, deliveries and sovereignty results while reconciling the remaining rows.

A preflight failure before mutation changes no autonomy, territory, faction, war or player control. It releases only uncommitted reservations. A recovered partial episode remains exceptional diagnostic evidence that the implementation must fix before release, not the intended normal rolling-declaration behaviour. The normal accepted result remains the full frozen cohort in one originating transaction.

## Former-master responses

Each former master receives the same two fundamental choices. The message directions must explain its actual former subjects, current wars and the difference between recognition and restoration. A master that has itself just become independent receives both its liberation report and its obligations toward its own former subjects without contradictory state changes.

| Response | Immediate political result | Required war result | Continuing play |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accept independence | Recognise every direct snapshot subject's sovereignty and renounce this episode's restoration claim | Exit only the relevant liberation or restoration conflicts, after verified selective settlement | Trade, negotiated access, compensation talks and later voluntary cooperation are possible |
| Fight to restore control | Retain restoration policy toward the snapshot subjects | Continue the actual wars and receive appropriate restoration objectives | Separate regional victories, defeats and negotiated settlements remain possible |

There is no hidden subject-type exemption. The initial response applies to all direct snapshot subjects of that master. Later negotiated regional peace can differ by country because wars can develop differently, but the opening acceptance option cannot quietly free only convenient subjects.

AI masters assess military reach, distance, current wars, economic dependence, ideology, domestic stability and the Front's credible strength. A weak overstretched master often accepts. A capable expansionist master may fight. These are weighted preferences among legal choices, not deterministic outcomes or calibrated probabilities until the required probability analysis is performed.

A human master's silence is not acceptance. The response remains available with a time-sensitive strategic reminder. AI responses are bounded and resolved promptly after the opening so that unattended AI masters do not stay in a permanent undefined policy state.

## Peace in overlapping wars

Acceptance must preserve unrelated wars, occupation, control and diplomatic state. A new liberation war can merge with an older conflict, or a former subject may already be at war with its master for another reason. The implementation must distinguish recognition of sovereignty, removal of this episode's restoration objective and actual cessation of the relevant hostility.

The pinned repository and offline engine reference do not establish a safe selective merged-war peace primitive. Country-targeted white peace is insufficient evidence by itself. The coding handoff makes a proven selective-settlement adapter a release gate. It must inspect the actual war participant boundary, perform only the authorised change, and verify the resulting peace and preserved conflicts before recording success.

An implementation may not end the entire world war, recreate countries, or restart unrelated wars to simulate preservation. If the same pair has a preexisting independent reason for war, accepting this episode's independence renounces restoration but does not falsely claim that unrelated war ended. The UI must explain the surviving conflict accurately. The event cannot be marked complete while the required master-exit behaviour is unproven.

## Regional liberation play

Every member receives immediate sovereignty and a practical opening plan based on its geography and capacity. These are strategic archetypes applied to current countries, not new tags or hardcoded historical owners.

| Archetype | Immediate pressure | Useful Front support | Main settlement question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Large territorial subject | Long fronts, inherited production and administrative transition | Staff training, coordinated supply and recognition | Can it defend its own borders and keep civilian government functional? |
| Small exposed land subject | Strong adjacent master and limited depth | Equipment, defensive access, coalition intervention where compatible | Can the master accept independence before a rapid defeat? |
| Maritime or island subject | Blockade, transport and imported supply | Convoys where supported, access agreements, port repair and maritime escort commitments | Which routes and bases remain open after independence? |
| Resource-dependent subject | Loss of former imperial trade and industrial imbalance | Replacement trade, complementary production and negotiated access | Can independence coexist with a workable commercial settlement? |
| Subject of a newly liberated subject | Internal Front restoration conflict | Mediation, independent aid allocation and explicit sovereignty guarantees | Will the intermediate former master accept its own former subjects? |
| Already active independence war | Existing frontline, prior allies and distinct war purpose | Reinforcement and recognition without duplicate declaration | How does the liberation settlement interact with its older war? |
| Country with a fragile existing government | Constitutional transition and military pressure at once | Administrative assistance, refuge and route-compatible institutions | Can the new government retain consent without replacing its politics automatically? |

The former subjects keep their existing leaders, armies, production and focus content. A newly independent country without useful national content receives a bounded liberation decision module for survival, diplomacy, supply and settlement. It does not receive a cloned full Macedonian tree or an invented national identity. Existing Event 006 country packages retain their deeper content and share only compatible coalition actions.

## Front actions and constitutional choices

Members can request aid, offer aid, negotiate access, support a compatible war, mediate a dispute, propose the charter, delegate a specific common command, reject coercive demands or leave according to their commitments after the immediate declaration state is established. The original compulsory membership records the world movement's founding, while lasting cooperation depends on conduct.

IMRO can propose a **sovereignty charter**, **delegated operational coordination** or **central revolutionary command**. These are different institutional paths. The charter provides reliable rights and higher retention but limits coercion. Delegated coordination creates renewable mandates for particular theatres and projects. Central command can produce high Front Authority, rapid concentration and powerful combined projects, but members demand protection and may withdraw if IMRO uses them as subjects in substance.

Each command mandate has a sender, recipient, purpose, duration, revocation condition and actual contribution. One mandate for a Balkan defence project is not authority over every army the partner owns. A command delegation does not transfer human player control of the country. Operational military control uses only supported mechanics and the player's explicit commitment.

## Front Authority

Front Authority combines real command relationships and delivered capability. It is the only persistent public numerical measure of IMRO's leadership of the Front. The contributing inputs are hidden calculations with qualitative condition explanations, not separate bars or currencies.

Let the current valid Front members be the set F, excluding extinct or invalid identities. Let each member's weight be 50 percent of its share of Front civilian population, 30 percent of its share of usable military industry and 20 percent of its share of active effective land force. Each component normalises over valid members, with zero-denominator components redistributed among nonzero components. If every component is zero, Authority is zero and terminal qualification fails.

For each member, the command coefficient is 0 without a valid mandate, 0.35 for a narrow active defensive mandate, 0.65 for a renewable theatre command with actual contributions, and 1 for accepted central command with functioning logistics. IMRO's own contribution uses coefficient 1 only while its relevant government and command actually function. The weighted command total contributes at most 70 Authority points. The remaining 30 points come from verified common logistics and fulfilled coalition obligations, each with a maximum of 15. Nominal promises contribute zero.

The logistics component reaches its cap only when functioning routes support at least three independent regional theatres. The fulfilled-obligations component reaches its cap through completed support and protection commitments over the preceding 180 days. Broken guarantees, deliberate attacks on members and failed protection reduce or invalidate the relevant proof. The tuning part specifies bounded achievement units so repeated tiny shipments cannot farm this component.

Authority is recomputed at relevant mandate, membership, territory, contribution and lifecycle boundaries, plus an event-owned bounded refresh of active members. It is not a new daily whole-world scan. The terminal launch uses a fresh authoritative calculation after defections and all additional absolute power safeguards. A small remnant Front can never become world-dominant by having a smaller denominator.

## Regional endings and the Front's future

| Regional result | Member outcome | Front consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Master accepts | Independent country at peace in the relevant liberation conflict | Recognition and recovery, with optional continued membership |
| Front member wins militarily | Independence defended through an actual peace result | Greater credibility and a possible fulfilled-protection receipt |
| Restoration succeeds | Subject or annexed status follows the real peace outcome | The historical liberation remains recorded, surviving partners can support a later new independence effort |
| Negotiated compromise | Defined sovereignty, access, reparations or limited territorial settlement | Treaty monitoring and a region-specific closure |
| Prolonged conflict | No false winner | Continued aid and fatigue-driven diplomacy |
| IMRO loses leadership | Coalition appoints a willing successor or decentralises | Existing sovereignty and active local wars remain intact |

The Front does not dissolve automatically when IMRO is defeated. A charter-based coalition can continue under another coordinator. If members no longer want common institutions after their wars end, a negotiated decentralisation closes shared obligations while preserving independence.

## The path toward radical corruption

A strong worldwide liberation order makes IMRO influential, but its members have their own reasons to resist domination. The 800 Chaos evolution tests the founder's institutions and the coalition's commitments. High Authority can make a radical takeover globally dangerous. The same high Authority can also allow a legitimate government to protect the Front, defeat the radicals and keep the liberation order intact.

When the death doctrine emerges, every Front member receives a meaningful doctrine response. Founding membership is never treated as prior consent to extermination. Members that reject it can turn against IMRO, leave its operational faction safely and join resistance coordination. Their earlier independence remains valid. The Freedom AND Death part defines the contest, conquest and separate terminal lock.
