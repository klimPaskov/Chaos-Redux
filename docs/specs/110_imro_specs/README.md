## The designed experience

IMRO begins as a local Macedonian independence organisation whose opportunities depend on real resistance, war, repression, local institutions and delivered support. Its controller can contain it, negotiate autonomy or face a rising. A successful independent Macedonia has a substantial country tree with civic, anarchist, socialist and central revolutionary alternatives, competing patron relationships and concrete industrial, military and territorial choices.

A surviving network can support distinct Balkan movements. An independent successful Macedonia can then inspire Freedom or Death, a simultaneous breakaway by every existing subject, including human-controlled and nested subjects. All join the National Liberation Front organisation and fight their direct former masters. Masters can accept their direct subjects' independence or fight restoration, with different regional outcomes.

At Chaos 800, the later radical evolution opens an institutional struggle and a possible conquest route. The world-end lock is separate and requires Chaos strictly above 1000 plus sustained, substantial actual IMRO control over the global Front. Its fictional doctrine targets all humans, including Macedonians and IMRO itself. Losses are progressive and physically accounted for, opponents can stop the regime, and final success requires exact verified human extinction. Defeat preserves earlier independence.
