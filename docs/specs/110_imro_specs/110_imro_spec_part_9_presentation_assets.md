# Presentation and asset direction

## What the player should understand

The presentation follows the change in scale. Early reports concern a specific place, a vulnerable movement and its relationship with residents. Independent-country content shows public institutions, reconstruction and actual military organisation. The network and Front make different countries visible as political actors. The terminal transformation breaks the liberation imagery and makes the threat to all humans clear, including IMRO's own population.

All descriptions in this part are art and writing directions. They are not final localisation, historical quotations, final titles, selected recordings or finished asset prompts. Asset generation and production are implementation work. This planning package creates no final portraits, flags, icons, models, audio or engine GUI.

## Writing direction by surface

| Surface | Required content and tone | Information that must remain clear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery report | Concrete account of cells, affected territory, grievances and uncertainty | Actual controller, known strength band, response window and choices |
| Autonomy negotiation | Political terms in plain language, with restrained tension | Who can grant the terms, what each side promises, what counts as a breach |
| Uprising report | Territorial and military stakes, with civilian agency | Real participants, current war and optional player-country transfer |
| Country route text | Institutions, people, practical choices and consequences | Why the route changes command, production, rights or diplomacy |
| Patron dispute | Credible conflicting interests, without a cartoon all-powerful sponsor | Delivered aid, outstanding obligations and the cost of refusal |
| Regional network report | A specific partner's goal and its relationship to others | Its independence, consent and distinct outcome |
| Freedom or Death news | A factual worldwide announcement of the simultaneous subject breakaway | All existing subjects, the Front, actual master responses and regional variation |
| Front decision text | Direct description of mutual obligations and limits | Organisation membership versus operational faction, duration and revocation of command |
| Radical contest | Clear fictional doctrine and the threatened institutions, without theatrical vagueness | How to resist, how to commit, deadline and failure conditions |
| Terminal super-event | Grave account of the slogan's corruption and universal target | Active progressive campaign, inclusion of Macedonians and followers, opportunity for resistance |
| Defeat and recovery | Relief, political accountability and actual loss | Campaign stopped, earlier sovereignty retained, survivors and unresolved wars |
| Extinction ending | Finality without implying hidden human survivors | Verified zero living humans, including the regime itself |
| Achievements | Specific challenge and result, with earned irony where fitting | Actual eligibility, difficult conditions and hidden status |

Humour belongs in limited political irony, administrative absurdity or a patron's failed expectations when the context supports it. Do not use jokes to obscure autonomy terms or civilian losses. Cultural remarks and quotations require a verified source, exact attribution and rights review before final wording. An invented quotation presented as historical is unacceptable.

| Territorial settlement | Exact state-piece puzzle and a text qualification list | Several disputed regions and conditional integration need a precise map. Show grey and green with non-colour markers |

A reference image is required before implementing each rich GUI. It must be grounded in inspected installed vanilla and project references and translated into a native-element plan. Generated panel art supplies texture and composition. Buttons, text, tooltips, lists, map pieces and state changes remain native controls. Do not bake changing values or fake controls into an image.

## Coalition board composition

The main board has a restrained title area with the current coalition identity, a member list, a selected-member detail area and a compact actions area. A regional filter narrows the list without hiding mandatory members from the full roster. The detail area shows sovereignty, the actual former-master conflict, operational faction arrangement, current mandate and real aid obligations. Organisation, Mandate and Front Authority appear only where they belong and retain the same names and meanings across the system.

Nested subjects need an explicit internal-conflict state. A player viewing C must see that its former master B is also a Front member, that the C-B war remains active, and that B can recognise C. An accepted settlement updates the relevant conflict and may enable operational faction consolidation. The board never changes a country into a subject to make its display simpler.

At the doctrine split, the roster displays rejection, withdrawal, acceptance or unresolved domestic crisis as qualitative states. After rejection, the country's actual command contribution disappears. The terminal view marks only territory with verified active reach. A surviving independent state outside that reach cannot appear emptied or already doomed.

The board has no mandatory moving background. Keyboard and mouse hit areas, readable text, scroll position, empty states and disabled controls matter more than ornament. Test the longest plausible country names and translated strings, small display scales and dense coalition rosters. Default hidden reference-capture bindings must not leak into engine-loaded normal play.

## Exact territorial puzzle

The required puzzle covers the verified homeland and selected Pirin and Aegean claim pieces. It shows actual installed state boundaries, current owners, which treaty or integration condition is missing, and whether the chosen end-state is reunification or a limited-border settlement. Use a small inset for compact or fragmented state shapes. A numbered or named list gives the same qualifications without relying on colour.

The source registry, map-piece geometry and action eligibility must share one state set. A decorative historical map may be used in a report, but cannot be used as the interactive border source. Capture and compare unmet, partly met, fully met, occupied-but-unsettled, disputed-consent and changed-owner scenarios. Preserve the source geometry and final map source file for later map changes.

## Historical flags and institutional references

Research supports distinct regional and detachment banners for the 1903 uprising rather than a single uncontested early IMRO flag. A Kumanovo or Ohrid banner must keep its local attribution. The Tsonchev museum banner belongs to a specific Supreme Committee context and must not be labelled a universal internal-organisation flag. See the source record for [local banners](110_imro_research_reading_record.md#h03-local-banners), [heraldic context](110_imro_research_reading_record.md#h04-heraldic-context-and-distinct-civic-proposals), [the museum object](110_imro_research_reading_record.md#h05-a-specific-museum-banner) and [organisational distinctions](110_imro_research_reading_record.md#h06-different-organisations-and-interpretations). The familiar red-and-black interwar attribution needs stronger period-object or scholarly design evidence before it becomes a historical final. Modern political-party drawings do not establish interwar geometry.

For an existing Macedonia, preserve its already legitimate flag unless the route deliberately adopts a new identity. For event-created government variants, choose a verified historical design or an explicitly alternate-history adoption from sourced institutional material. A flag adopted from a particular regional banner should be described as this fictional government's adoption, not as a claim that every historical wing used it. The radical emblem is explicitly fictional and must be visually distinct from historical attribution.

Every final flag goes through flat ImageGen construction from its chosen reference and then manual geometry, symbol, glyph and size review. A generated flag with invented lettering or incorrect heraldry fails. Do not apply realistic fabric lighting, perspective, shadows or a waving mockup to a flag texture. The rights to an old design, a museum photograph and a modern redraw are recorded separately.

## Portrait and model production boundaries

Grounded historical people are sourced. Preserve originals, crops, biographies and rights. A source placeholder is an honest intermediate state and cannot be reported as a final styled portrait. The user alone performs the defined grounded styling workflow. Fictional collective government art can be generated where the route itself is fictional and contains no disguised imitation of a real person.

The custom subunit requires a complete 3D production handoff. Inspect the actual installed vanilla entity, skeleton, meshes, scale and axes first. Generate an empty-handed body with the current Meshy 7 workflow and a separate geometry task for each held item. Use one clean model reference image per task. For eligible human bodies, attempt the supported provider rig and each required provider action once, preserving receipts. Unsupported or failed stages continue through real live Blender work under the owner rules, with no repeated paid recovery or fabricated success.

In live Blender, fit separate items, correct hand contacts, author or correct required motion and review the assembled unit. Do not generate bones, weights or keyframes with a script as a substitute for the required live authoring route. Idle, movement, attack and death need actual relevant skeletal motion. Required animation cannot be replaced by a static pose. Reimport and verify the exported asset against the engine consumer, not just a pretty viewport still.

Model diffuse textures use the required BC1 profile, normal and specular maps use BC3, full mip chains and the standard 512-scale profile unless the inspected consumer requires another documented size. The parent owns final entity, mesh, animation and audio wiring. No provider or Blender service was available during this planning pass, so no production or animation evidence is claimed.

Selection voices are tag-wide through the actual `TAG_infantry_idle` convention and the Voices category. A subunit name does not imply a separate selectable voice bank. Use the actual active tag, preserve valid base cues and provide only sourced licensed recordings. No generated voice, synthetic sound or silent placeholder is a finished unit sound package.

## Deliberate animation

Two event-owned animations are justified. The coalition's radical-transformation emblem can change through genuinely different drawn states at the doctrine reveal. A restrained interrupted communications indicator can show the loss and restoration of a selected active logistics link. Neither runs as an endless attention-grabbing background or stands in for an actual game state.

Each animation requires distinct source frames, a horizontal DDS sheet, frame count, frame rate, loop or one-shot rule, native-size preview, GIF preview, contact sheet and static fallback. The transformation uses a one-shot sequence and a stable final state. The communications indicator uses a short low-frequency loop only while the corresponding real operation is unresolved. A transform-only movement of an unchanged image is not the required frame animation.

Animation is not required for every portrait, icon or panel. Add another animated asset only when it communicates a specific changing state better than a static one, and record the justification. Source art and final runtime states must remain reviewable.

## Super-event candidates

| Slot | Trigger and scale | Image and emotional direction | Text and audio research requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SE-A Worldwide liberation | Freedom or Death completed and materially affects at least three macroregions. The global news still occurs for every nonempty cohort | Several distinct liberation contexts and the scale of the political break, without claiming all outcomes are already victorious | Verify any chosen historical remark about national freedom. Source a distinct recording with a measured, rising collective character |
| SE-B Terminal transformation | Every terminal lock condition passed and the campaign committed | Corrupted liberation symbolism and abandoned civic promise, showing the regime's universal target without graphic gore | Verify title, description, button and any quotation. The fictional doctrine must be clearly separate from historical quotation. Source an appropriate distinct recording |
| SE-C Humanity stops the regime | A locked campaign actually affected multiple regions and is verifiably defeated | Survivors, ruined institutions and sober relief, with no restoration of the dead | Research an attributed reflection on survival or responsibility if used. Avoid triumphal comedy and reuse of the transformation track |
| SE-D Verified extinction | Complete fresh zero-human proof, including the regime, and one final receipt | Human absence and finality. No living figure, radio operator or implied human observer in the final scene | Any remark must fit a genuinely empty human world. Source a distinct final recording and finish cleanly rather than implying a new human narrator |

Each slot needs the current super-event framework's full title, description, button, quote, image, sound wrapper, getter and settings hooks, including `.t`, `.d`, `.a` and `.q` where the framework requires them. Exact identifiers are reserved through the shared registry audit. The planner does not assign apparently unused numerical IDs.

Audio is sourced, not generated. Verify composition rights and recording rights separately. A public-domain composition does not make a modern performance free to redistribute. Normal excerpts should last 60 to 120 seconds and remain within 120 seconds unless a justified exception is authorised. Record source, performer, date, licence, edit points, loudness and final WAV properties. Text researchers and audio researchers have separate bounded roles and deliver evidence before final localisation and sound wiring.

## Production and visual acceptance

Use the current canonical `gfx_references` and visual-component catalogs, not the superseded `vanilla_reference` path. Inspect the exact icon, counter, portrait, panel and event families that will be consumed. The canonical installed reference root was unavailable in this environment. Its inspection remains a production gate rather than an invented completed check.

Final 2D DDS conversion follows the project's strict BGRA profile and required 128-byte header. Preserve native alpha from generated source art and inspect small-size readability. Do not treat successful file conversion as visual approval. Validate dimensions, alpha, channel order, mip policy where relevant, sprite reference and all required runtime states.

Rich GUI, focus and event changes need matched native inspector and render evidence before and after implementation. Compare the same scenarios and preserve original source and image artifacts. A clean linter with clipped text, missing art or wrong state display is not a visual pass. Source fixtures and static mockups cannot substitute for actual native rendering evidence.

Temporary asset work belongs under the event's asset working folder during implementation. Promote durable source attribution, rights, original portrait sources, manifests and proof to their required lasting locations before any cleanup. The final package must not depend on deleting the only provenance copy to appear tidy.
