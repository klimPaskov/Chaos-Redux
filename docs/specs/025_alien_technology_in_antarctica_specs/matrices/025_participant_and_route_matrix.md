# Participant and route matrix

## Eligibility

| Actor | Offered entry | Normal route access | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human-controlled major | Yes | Any valid route | Always offered |
| Human-controlled minor | Yes | Any valid route | Always offered despite weak economy |
| Human-controlled landlocked country | Yes | Sponsored or chartered | Higher burden and access dependence |
| Human-controlled special chaos or actual nonhuman country | Yes | Any route it can materially support | Classification alone never removes the player offer. Use actor-appropriate expedition, personnel, and casualty text |
| AI major | Weighted candidate | Any valid route | Selected into bounded roster |
| AI minor | No under ordinary rules | Not applicable | Southern access alone does not bypass major gate |
| AI-controlled Kruger State | Conditional | Any valid route | Explicit scientific exception when valid |
| Other AI-controlled special chaos or actual nonhuman country | No | Not applicable | Requires a later accepted dedicated AI route |
| Observer after decline | Public reports | None | Cannot act on board |
| Withdrawn participant | No re-entry | Evacuation only | Retains verified fragments according to outcome |

## Route families

| Route | Typical user | Initial burden | Travel time | Maintenance | Main risk | Main advantage |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Direct southern route | Southern Hemisphere or gateway power | Low to medium | Short | Medium | Weather and sea ice | Fast first outpost |
| Long maritime route | Distant coastal major | High | Long | High | Convoy loss and fuel | Independent access |
| Sponsored gateway route | Ally or partner of a gateway holder | Medium | Medium | Medium | Access dependence | Lower material burden |
| Chartered route | Landlocked or small human entrant | High | Long | High | Diplomatic cancellation and cost | Universal human access |
| Improvised emergency route | Country whose original route failed | Very high | Variable | Very high | Breakdown and exposure | Prevents immediate elimination |

## Gateway groups

| Working group | Geographic role | Access examples | Design effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| South Atlantic | Routes through the southern Atlantic | Southern South America, South Atlantic ports | Strong access to Antarctic Peninsula sectors |
| Southern Indian Ocean | Routes south of Africa and the Indian Ocean | Southern African and Indian Ocean ports | Balanced access, longer eastern sectors |
| Australasian | Routes from Australia and nearby ports | Hobart and other southern ports | Strong East Antarctic logistics |
| New Zealand and Pacific | Pacific gateway routes | Christchurch and New Zealand ports | Strong Ross Sea access |
| Chartered neutral gateway | Negotiated foreign staging | Any willing valid gateway country | Political dependence and access risk |

These are gameplay groups. Final state, port, and access checks require live map and country inspection.

| Distant coastline | Higher | Higher | Higher | Medium |
| Landlocked charter | Higher | Higher | Higher | Higher access risk |

| Fuel shortage | Higher effective cost | Higher | Higher | Higher |

| Evolution III blockade | Neutral at start | Higher | Higher | Severe |
| Evolution I signal route | Neutral | Medium | Lower search time | Higher Exposure |

## Suggested route proof

A selected route is valid only when:

- a gateway or charter agreement exists
- initial material debit succeeds
- minimum post-spend reserves pass for AI
- no hostile owner blocks the exact route
- participant country remains valid

Human players may accept riskier post-spend reserves. They still need the real resources.

## Route invalidation

| Cause | Immediate result | Recovery route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gateway owner becomes hostile | Route interrupted | Negotiate another gateway or charter emergency access |
| Convoy reserve collapses | Readiness loss | Resupply or withdraw |
| Fuel reserve collapses | Progress stops | Emergency fuel commitment |
| Port lost | Route interrupted | Alternate gateway |
| Blockade | Maintenance rises, travel slows | Escort, negotiation, reroute, or counter-blockade under Evolution III |
| Country annexed | Participant invalid | Cleanup and fragment disposition |

## Human fairness requirements

- No hard major-status gate.
- No coastline gate.
- No hidden AI slot competition.
- Costs communicate the disadvantage before confirmation.
- A weak country may pursue fragments or cooperation even when winning is unlikely.
- Route difficulty does not become an automatic random failure.
