# Phase action matrix

## Phase 1: Mobilization

| Action | Who sees it | Main costs | Main result | Main risk | AI use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commit the Expedition | Entered participant | Convoys, fuel, support equipment, civilian industry | Opens expedition state | National opportunity cost | Required |
| Choose Southern Gateway | Participant without locked route | Access requirements, possible convoys or industry | Selects route burden | Foreign dependence | Required |
| Assemble Scientific Team | Participant | Support equipment, civilian industry, personnel or operator requirement | Survey and incident bonuses | Personnel exposure | High scientific AI |
| Overprovision the Voyage | Participant with reserves | Convoys, fuel, support equipment | Higher readiness and lower route risk | Larger opportunity cost | Naval and cautious AI |
| Conceal Departure | Secret entrant | Industry, intelligence exposure risk | Lower public profile | Harder rescue and access | Secretive AI |

## Phase 2: Crossing and Outpost

| Action | Main costs | Main result | Main risk | Visibility rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reinforce the Supply Route | Convoys, fuel, support equipment | Restores readiness | Home stockpile strain | Route active and below prepared band |

| Build Redundant Radio Links | Support equipment, industry | Reduces communication incidents | Signal exposure under Evolution I | Outpost planned or active |
| Prepare an Alternate Landing | Convoys, fuel, industry | Emergency route fallback | Slower ordinary progress | Route vulnerable |
| Escort the Supply Convoys | Fuel, convoys, military requirement | Defends route | Public militarisation | Evolution II or III, or hostile incident |

## Phase 3: Survey and Triangulation

| Action | Main costs | Main result | Main risk | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Survey Selected Sector | Fuel, support equipment, industry | Survey certainty and progress | Weather and crew exposure | Sector |
| Expand Aerial Reconnaissance | Fuel, aircraft requirement, industry | Faster broad survey | Aircraft loss | Sector group |
| Analyze Recovered Fragment | Industry, scientist or technical-operator requirement | Broad field clue and research | Exposure | Fragment |
| Exchange Weather Data | Small industry or access requirement | Mutual readiness and certainty | Rival gains information | Rival |
| Steal Survey Coordinates | Intelligence exposure, support equipment | Copies certainty | Attribution and retaliation | Rival |

Only three to five of these appear at once according to route, target, and evolution state.

| --- | --- | --- |

## Evolution II actions

| Action | Result | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Avoid Contact | Lower survivor risk | Slower access to some sectors |
| Track the Survivor | Reveals profile and location | Personnel danger |
| Attempt Communication | May create cooperation or safe route | Misinterpretation |
| Capture the Survivor | Strong reward potential | Severe losses and hostility |
| Add Military Escort | Protection | Higher militarisation and cost |

## Clutter rules

- Hide invalid target actions.
- Replace early versions once advanced versions appear.
- Show one selected rival at a time to humans.
- Keep active missions between one and three.
- Use emergency actions only while the emergency exists.
- Remove all race actions after resolution.
