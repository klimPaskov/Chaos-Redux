# AI strategy and probability

## AI design purpose

AI countries should create credible competition without receiving hidden completion shortcuts.

The AI must understand whether it can afford participation, which route fits its position, when to invest, when to protect its expedition, when to cooperate, when to interfere, when to launch final recovery, and when to withdraw.

Every weighted surface requires a named probability audit before source changes are accepted.

## Participant roster size

The roster is bounded.

Recommended target bands:

| Human entrants | AI participants | Total intended competition |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 4 to 6 | 4 to 6 |
| 1 | 4 to 6 | 5 to 7 |
| 2 to 3 | 3 to 5 | 5 to 8 |
| 4 to 6 | 2 to 4 | 6 to 10 |
| 7 or more | 1 to 3 | Bounded by GUI and performance budget |

These are design bands. The implementation should centralize exact caps.

Human entrants always take priority over AI slots.

## AI participation eligibility

An AI country must pass all hard gates:

- exists and is valid
- is a current major, or the narrow valid Kruger State exception
- is not a special chaos or actual nonhuman country unless the accepted AI route explicitly supports it
- is not in an incompatible global end-state
- can assemble a route through coastline or gateway access
- can meet minimum convoy, fuel, support equipment, and industry requirements
- is not already collapsing under an incompatible national crisis

Passing the gate makes the country a candidate. It does not guarantee selection. These AI-only gates never remove the entry choice from a human-controlled country.

## Participation score

The score should consider:

Positive factors:

- large convoy reserve
- high fuel reserve and fuel income
- strong civilian industry
- nearby gateway or Southern Ocean access
- strong navy or long-range air capability
- high research capacity
- missing Event 016 custom technologies
- missing Event 036 alien-aircraft reward
- high strategic interest in aviation, electronics, propulsion, or special projects
- existing scientific route or Kruger institutions
- rival participation by an ideological or strategic competitor

Negative factors:

- total or near-total war with dangerous fronts
- convoy deficit
- fuel crisis
- low stability
- capital or core territory under immediate threat
- extreme supply burden
- every custom technology and upgrade already owned
- recent expedition withdrawal or incompatible disaster
- no plausible route

The score must not treat major status alone as sufficient.

## AI archetypes

The implementation derives one temporary expedition posture from world state. It does not need a permanent personality system.

### Scientific competitor

Priorities:

- survey and signal study
- safe outpost
- fragment analysis
- data exchange when useful
- low hostile action

### Strategic monopolist

Priorities:

- secrecy
- counterintelligence
- data theft
- route interference
- early final recovery

### Naval expeditionary power

Priorities:

- direct route
- strong resupply
- escorts
- outpost defense
- blockade only under Evolution III

### Resource-constrained opportunist

Priorities:

- cheaper chartered route
- cooperation
- fragment recovery
- withdrawal when the main race becomes unaffordable

### Desperate wartime gambler

Priorities:

- high-risk actions
- rapid progress
- limited safety spending
- early final attempt

This posture appears only when winning could materially change a losing war and the expedition remains feasible.

### Kruger research state

Priorities:

- broad survey
- signal interpretation
- technology compatibility
- low tolerance for sharing unique results
- higher willingness to study Evolution V systems

It does not receive automatic progress or guaranteed technology selection.

## Route selection

The AI chooses among direct southern, long maritime, sponsored, and improvised routes.

Selection factors:

- nearest valid gateway
- route burden
- convoy and fuel capacity
- diplomatic access
- current wars
- naval safety
- likely rival interference
- time to first outpost

The AI should not select a cheaper route that it cannot sustain through the first season.

## Phase behavior

### Mobilization

AI goals:

- raise Logistics Readiness above the safe departure threshold
- secure one valid gateway
- choose one route
- avoid spending every convoy or all available fuel

It should delay or decline when post-spend reserves fall below central floors.

### Crossing and outpost

AI goals:

- protect route continuity
- complete the outpost mission
- repair setbacks before buying extra progress
- use emergency resupply only when it prevents collapse

### Survey

AI goals:

- maintain a balanced mix of survey and logistics
- pursue signal data when Evolution I is active and risk is tolerable
- use intelligence when a rival is close to confirmation
- collect fragments when the main race becomes unlikely

### Final recovery

AI launches the mission only when:

- final sector is confirmed
- Logistics Readiness meets the safe threshold or a desperate posture explicitly accepts risk
- outpost and route are valid
- no active crisis blocks recovery
- expected success is competitive with waiting

### Aftermath

AI chooses containment, transfer, concealment, destruction, or integration according to:

- current war state
- stability
- ideology and secrecy preference
- scientific capacity
- Dependence level
- foreign pressure
- Event 016 institutions
- whether the country can manage accidents

## Spending floors

The AI must preserve post-spend reserves.

Required reserve concepts:

- convoys for ordinary trade and military supply
- fuel for active war and naval operations
- support equipment for the army
- civilian industry for repairs and national construction
- manpower or personnel for active fronts

Exact floors should scale by country and war state.

A player may deliberately overcommit. AI overcommitment requires the desperate wartime posture and should remain rare.

## Progress strategy

AI should not maximize Expedition Progress while ignoring Logistics Readiness.

Suggested priority logic:

1. prevent critical logistics collapse
2. complete current phase gate
3. respond to urgent incident
4. protect against a leading rival
5. invest in survey or progress
6. collect optional fragments

At high Exposure Risk, safe AI postures favor containment and repair. Aggressive postures may continue when the reward is close.

## Rival selection

AI hostile and intelligence actions target valid active rivals.

Target factors:

- estimated lead
- proximity to final recovery
- fragment value
- poor defenses
- strategic rivalry
- ideology and relations
- previous hostility
- suspected sabotage
- ability to retaliate

Targeting should avoid repeatedly bullying the weakest participant when that target is irrelevant to the race.

## Cooperation target selection

AI cooperation favors:

- countries with good relations
- faction members
- countries that rescued its expedition
- countries sharing a gateway
- rivals whose cooperation prevents mutual collapse
- countries offering data without demanding artifact custody

Cooperation is not limited to ideological allies when the expedition situation makes practical coordination useful.

## Hostile action limits

AI uses target-specific and family cooldowns.

It should not:

- repeat the same sabotage against one rival every pulse
- launch an outpost seizure without Evolution III
- attack a country that already withdrew
- spend more resources on sabotage than on its own expedition
- risk an armed clash when its home war situation makes escalation irrational
- target a human because of player status

## Withdrawal logic

AI considers withdrawal when:

- route has collapsed twice
- Logistics Readiness remains critical
- it cannot meet the next phase cost
- home territory faces immediate defeat
- no competitive main-wreck path remains and fragment value is low
- another event creates an incompatible terminal state

It remains when:

- it holds major fragments
- it is near final recovery
- a rescue or sponsored route makes recovery affordable
- the technology could materially alter a losing war

## Evolution behavior

### Active Signal

Scientific AI studies remotely first. Aggressive AI follows the signal directly. Risk-averse AI invests in shielding.

### Something Survived

Scientific AI attempts communication or observation. Military AI adds escorts. Desperate AI attempts capture. Weak AI avoids contact.

### Militarised Antarctica

Naval powers reinforce routes. Risk-averse governments seek demilitarisation. Aggressive powers use blockade and seizure only against high-value rivals.

### Wreck Breaking Apart

A leading AI prioritizes the core. A trailing AI prioritizes fragments. A high-capacity AI may stabilize and attempt both.

### Technology Changes Its Users

Stable scientific governments favor containment. Secretive or authoritarian governments favor concealment. Desperate governments favor integration. Cooperative governments may transfer custody to a stronger ally.

These are tendencies, not fixed ideology scripts.

## Named probability audit scenarios

The probability auditor must inspect every weighted surface before tuning.

### P01: Peaceful maritime major

State:

- peace
- strong convoys and fuel
- direct southern route
- missing most custom technologies

Expectation:

- high participation score
- safe route preference
- scientific or naval posture
- low withdrawal chance

### P02: Continental major at total war

State:

- several dangerous fronts
- fuel and convoy pressure
- distant route

Expectation:

- participation below P01
- may decline unless technology need is very high
- withdrawal becomes plausible after setbacks

### P03: Weak human minor

State:

- human-controlled
- no coastline
- small industry

Expectation:

- always offered participation
- chartered or sponsored route available
- high cost and longer travel
- no hidden selection denial

This is a validity scenario, not an AI selection probability.

### P04: AI minor with excellent southern access

State:

- not a major
- strong local access

Expectation:

- not selected under ordinary AI rules
- confirms that access does not bypass major eligibility

### P05: Kruger State

State:

- valid KRG country
- stable scientific route
- adequate resources

Expectation:

- eligible through the explicit exception
- high signal-study preference
- no guaranteed selection or victory

### P06: Full technology owner

State:

- owns every base Event 016 operational family
- lacks several upgrades

Expectation:

- participation still possible
- reward value remains high through upgrade fallback
- no starvation caused by an empty base pool

### P07: Full external pool owner

State:

- owns all bases and compatible upgrades

Expectation:

- participation score lower than a country missing technologies but remains nonzero when strategic interest is high
- winner receives Alien Systems Integration

### P08: Rival near victory

State:

- another participant estimated one phase ahead
- actor has strong intelligence capacity

Expectation:

- defensive or intelligence action becomes more likely
- irrelevant weak rivals do not dominate target selection

### P09: Repeated failed sabotage

State:

- target-specific cooldown active
- attribution risk high

Expectation:

- same action weight becomes zero
- AI shifts to defense, survey, or another valid target

### P10: Critical logistics

State:

- actor has high progress but critical readiness

Expectation:

- resupply and repair dominate
- final recovery weight is zero unless desperate posture and minimum validity pass

### P11: Evolution IV trailing expedition

State:

- main core unlikely
- several fragment sites available

Expectation:

- fragment actions dominate over reckless core attempt

### P12: Evolution V stable winner

State:

- peace
- high research capacity
- moderate Dependence

Expectation:

- containment or controlled integration dominates
- destruction and transfer remain possible but lower

### P13: Evolution V collapsing winner

State:

- losing war
- low stability
- high Dependence

Expectation:

- emergency containment, transfer, or risky integration dominates according to available capacity
- concealment is not chosen when public exposure is already complete

### P14: Many human entrants

State:

- seven human participants

Expectation:

- AI roster shrinks to the configured minimum
- no human is removed
- GUI roster cap remains valid

### P15: Same-day final completion

State:

- two countries finish final recovery on the same pulse

Expectation:

- deterministic tie resolver selects one winner
- rewards apply once
- loser receives the correct fragment tier

## Timing audit scenarios

### T01: Prepared nearby power

Expected median completion should fall near the lower design band without becoming an instant race.

### T02: Distant ordinary major

Expected completion is longer because of route burden and higher maintenance.

### T03: Sabotage-heavy race

Expected duration increases, but the event should still normally resolve before multi-year stagnation.

### T04: All evolutions disabled

Baseline completion distribution remains valid.

### T05: Evolution IV active

Fragment activity increases, while at least one prepared country can still secure the main core.

The auditor should distinguish exact, bounded, sampled, and unresolved evidence. It cannot claim exact timing from an incomplete custom pool.

## Balance targets

The design target is a race with visible swings and no single dominant action.

Required ordering:

- preparation beats reckless spending in ordinary conditions
- direct access is useful but not decisive
- a distant rich power can compete by paying more
- a nearby weak power can compete through efficient logistics but cannot ignore industry
- sabotage can delay or expose a rival but cannot replace expedition progress
- cooperation is useful enough to appear in AI play
- the winner reward remains valuable for late-game scientific powers
- losing fragment rewards do not erase the value of winning

## Mandatory audit cycle

For each probability-bearing implementation change:

1. run `hoi4.probability_inspect`
2. define complete named scenarios
3. evaluate or sweep the baseline
4. let the owning implementation agent apply the bounded patch
5. run `hoi4.probability_compare` on the same scenarios
6. render timing or sensitivity evidence when it improves review

The probability auditor remains read-only and does not choose the intended balance target.
