## Core idea

> Gandhi inspires a worldwide movement demanding independence through nonviolence, noncooperation, truth, and honesty.

Every country holding non-core territory, including AI minors and player countries, must decide whether to grant independence to its occupied peoples.

## The ultimatum

- **Grant independence:** Release the affected countries or territories as independent states.
- **Refuse:** Non-fascist occupiers face mass noncooperation. Resistance and compliance fall to **0%**, occupied territory stops supplying useful factories, resources, and manpower, and the occupier receives a temporary **Loss of Morale** spirit harming war support and military effectiveness.

The campaign eventually loses momentum if the occupier keeps ignoring it, and its penalties fade. Independence ends the local campaign sooner.

**Fascist occupiers are the exception.** Noncooperation fails against them and can strengthen their occupation instead, reducing unrest and improving control.

# Evolutions

## Evolution I: The Peace League

- Chaos requirement: `400+`.

If Gandhi's movements achieve substantial independence or peace victories, liberated countries and other sympathetic governments can join a **Gandhi-led Peace League**. New supporters may adopt Gandhian political movements or governments, while existing members retain their national identities.

The League can jointly protect newly independent countries and pressure belligerents into ceasefires. Members may withdraw if Gandhi's international commitments threaten their own security or independence.

## Evolution II: Nuclear Pacifism

- Chaos requirement: `800+`.

A powerful Peace League embraces the belief that only overwhelming nuclear deterrence can make war impossible. Members pursue a shared nuclear program and demand disarmament or peace from countries still fighting.

The League may force concessions through nuclear threats, but some supporters reject the new doctrine, split away, or turn against Gandhi. Nuclear weapons must be acquired or developed through real capabilities rather than appearing for free.

# World-end scenario: The Ultimate Society of Peace and Nukes

At **Chaos above 1,000**, the world-end route can begin if Gandhi's League has already achieved major international peace or liberation successes, commands a powerful coalition across several regions, and possesses a credible nuclear arsenal.

Gandhi becomes the supreme leader of an **Ultimate Society of Peace and Nukes**. It offers the remaining countries permanent peace under its authority, enforced disarmament, and admission to its world order. Governments that refuse face military coercion and potentially nuclear attack.

The final conflict is playable. Other countries can form an opposition, members can defect, and Gandhi's nuclear peace project can be defeated. If his side prevails, the world is unified under a compulsory peace enforced by nuclear power.
