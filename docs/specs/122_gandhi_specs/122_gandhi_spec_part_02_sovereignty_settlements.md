# 122 Gandhi, Part 2: Independence that changes the map

A concession succeeds when people receive actual sovereignty and the promised land changes hands. A temporary autonomy modifier, a renamed puppet, a League invitation or a friendly report cannot satisfy the demand. Every agreement has a known beneficiary, an exact state footprint, a responsible owner and controller, and a result that can be checked after execution.

## Build territorial cases before offering a result

The opening collects holders and affected states once. It then groups states into coherent territorial cases, using existing countries, attested release packages, core geography and real administrative relationships. Countries with many possessions receive a summary and a paged case selector. Their remote territories remain in the underlying registry even when only three target cards are visible.

A case records the original owner, controller, relevant cores, claimant identities, population and useful economic footprint. It also records whether a living beneficiary exists and whether a war, subject relationship or competing release plan prevents immediate delivery. These facts are evidence for the current agreement. They do not grant claims or rewrite the map by themselves.

During the active campaign, scoped ownership and control callbacks add newly relevant cases. They do not restart the global deadline. A case becoming invalid is closed with its real reason, such as a genuine integration or a different completed peace settlement. A stale event target never redirects an agreement to another country.

## Beneficiary order

| Candidate | Admission rule | Content treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Living sovereign homeland | It accepts the defined return or accession, and the current owners and relevant claimants can lawfully deliver it | Keep its creator origin, ruler, research, army and meaningful focus tree |
| Living dependent homeland | Its required overlord and national authorities accept genuine independence and the territorial terms | Apply actual sovereign status, then preserve its existing identity |
| Dormant installed native tag | Its homeland and release footprint are valid and no other active plan owns it | Use its real country content with a bounded Gandhi reconstruction extension |
| Attested Event 006 identity | The existing package matches the territory and its identity is available | Reuse approved identity data through a Gandhi adapter, with a Gandhi creator receipt for a new birth |
| Existing Event 005 or 006 country | It is already living and consents to a transfer | Never overwrite its immutable origin or award a second birth |
| Territorial civil council | No suitable installed identity exists after the full identity audit, and the proposed region is coherent and governable | Use the explicit human civil-administration package in Part 6 |
| Unsupported or disputed identity | Required identity or consent is missing | Keep the case unresolved. Do not claim independence or create a blank nation |

The installed tag audit includes vanilla, the active mod, relevant Workshop content and the project's attested package registries. The coding agent records the real chosen identity. This plan deliberately assigns no speculative dynamic tags.

A civil council is a territorial administration whose name describes the place it governs. It is not a fabricated ethnic nation. A fictional temporary administrator must be identified as fictional in source documentation. Real leaders and communities require their own evidence. Existing core claims remain visible until a legitimate treaty resolves them.

## Overlapping claims

Two countries claiming the same state cannot both receive it. A treaty must publish one allocation. A living claimant materially affected by that allocation receives an informed choice. Its core is a reason to participate, not an automatic right to absorb every other claimant.

Preferred settlements follow coherent borders and avoid creating isolated ungovernable fragments merely to maximize the number of releases. An agreed shared guarantee, demilitarized border or protected transit arrangement may accompany the allocation, but the engine still needs one actual state owner. Do not imply that a picture or GUI can divide a state into new provinces.

If all participants accept only the undisputed portion, that portion may settle as a complete subcase. Disputed land keeps its refusal treatment and its original expiry. The interface states that the wider case is incomplete. It does not award a full homeland success for one token border transfer.

## Ordinary release transaction

The shared liberation coordinator already provides a frozen allocation, host protection, live validation, execution and finalization boundary. Gandhi adds a distinct owner and creator-origin adapter. It does not impersonate Event 005 or Event 006.

The intended transaction is:

1. Collect consent without holding the global release lock open during a human response window.
2. Construct a complete provisional allocation for the connected group of competing claims and affected hosts.
3. Reserve candidate identities, anchors, exact states and the ordinary protected host state.
4. Revalidate owner, controller, cores, wars, autonomy, capitals, living tags and all competing reservations immediately before lock and execution.
5. Perform the ownership and control changes through the appropriate documented release or transfer mode.
6. Verify the exact result, then finalize the new country's coherent package and immutable origin.
7. Commit success, remove local campaign effects and publish truthful reports.

Independent, unrelated claim groups can resolve separately. Within a connected group, one release cannot take a state promised to another. A candidate that has become stale is trimmed or rejected before mutation according to the coordinator's own contract. The event does not silently substitute a different beneficiary.

Current fixed-tag release support is evidence for dormant-country creation. A living-beneficiary transfer and Gandhi's whole-country succession require distinct audited extensions. The plan does not assert that those Gandhi modes already exist.

### Failed and competing transactions

If Event 005, Event 006 or another accepted participant owns the coordinator, Gandhi's case waits without seizing its lock. A country-local retry rechecks a still-valid case after the owning transaction finishes. Use at most two automatic retry attempts inside the thirty-day mandate. A continuing conflict then reports an unresolved case and returns to the ordinary settlement decision. No unbounded retry or periodic world scan is introduced.

A failure before the point of no return uses the coordinator's compensation contract and proves the restored owner, controller, cores, country existence and capital. A failure after finalization has begun preserves its diagnostic ledger. It does not claim a clean rollback or a successful independence report. Setup effects must be preflighted so that such a failure is exceptional and diagnosable.

The holder does not gain a success receipt from a failed transaction. Local denial is removed only from states whose actual settlement committed. An already granted and paid unrelated case remains valid.

## The last state and voluntary succession

An ordinary release preserves a surviving state for each host. That protection prevents a grant button from unexpectedly eliminating a country. Gandhi also needs to handle a country whose entire holding is non-core. Leaving its last occupied state permanently exempt would contradict the worldwide demand.

For that case, the event offers an explicit succession agreement. It lists every state to be transferred and makes the source country's disappearance clear. A human source selects the successor country it will continue playing before any mutation. If several sovereign successors are created, the choice is among those exact successors. All other participants consent to their own terms.

This is a separately audited zero-host mode, informed by the existing Event 006 Join the Independence Wave precedent. It must prove exact coverage of every owned state, a valid successor package, peace or a complete lawful settlement of the relevant wars, matching plan identity and an accepted continuation choice. A human tag change occurs only after the transaction commits. It cannot run after an uncertain finalization failure.

No response, a declined continuation or a lost prerequisite keeps the source country alive. Its still-held states receive the appropriate refusal treatment. The AI accepts succession only when its actual survival and territorial position make the agreed successor a rational continuation. It does not dissolve itself solely because a hidden random roll was unlucky.

Succession transfers only assets and relationships that the accepted package and native APIs can safely carry. It does not copy every diplomatic relationship, inherit unrelated wars, clone ships or move nuclear stocks without proof. Part 6 defines the national continuity obligations.

## Wartime occupations

The controller of a foreign-owned state can cease exploiting or occupying it, but it cannot always create the promised sovereign owner alone. The event therefore separates three outcomes.

| Outcome | Required proof | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Withdrawal or restored control | Actual control changes and the holder no longer controls the relevant non-core land | Ends that holder's local denial, but does not itself count as independence |
| Return to an existing sovereign homeland | Valid owner, controller and claimant settlement delivers the exact land and sovereignty | Sovereignty credit after durability observation |
| New independent state within a wider peace | All necessary war leaders and parties accept a complete supported settlement | Sovereignty and possibly mediation receipts with separate causal ownership |

The event cannot end only one visual front while leaving the same two countries at war in another native war, then report lasting peace. A mediation adapter must inspect all relevant war relationships, participants and war leaders. It must not call a broad white-peace helper against unrelated wars.

The existing Event 009 preparation and application contract owns its own selected pairs and durable results. Gandhi needs an explicit mediated-settlement adapter or a receipt from a genuine joint operation. An ordinary random White Peace that happens nearby is not evidence that Gandhi negotiated it.

If the owner refuses a reasonable sovereignty agreement while the controller accepts withdrawal, the controller can complete its own withdrawal. The owner receives its own demand if it now holds non-core land. Responsibility follows actual control and ownership rather than the first country's expired report.

## Costs and property

Sovereignty costs the former holder the land's future taxes, factories, resources, recruitment and strategic position. Those real losses are the principal cost. A release does not also require an arbitrary large political-power payment just to make the button expensive.

Optional reconstruction and security provisions add concrete negotiated costs. A host may transfer a measured stock of infantry equipment, finance repair through a temporary civilian-industrial commitment, provide safe convoy access, recognize a demilitarized border or support a limited defensive force. Each commitment names the payer, recipient, asset and completion condition. Paid assets are not duplicated by the country startup package.

The recipient can accept a lean independence agreement without waiting for an impossibly generous aid package. Missing aid changes relations, credibility and subsequent reconstruction needs. It does not reverse already committed sovereignty unless a separate lawful settlement does so.

Air and naval assets require explicit valid transfer support. Otherwise they remain with their existing owner and the new state builds its own capacity. Nuclear devices always follow the stricter owner and custody rules in Part 4.

## Durable success receipts

A release creates an immediate factual result. A political gate credit matures only after sixty observed days of genuine sovereignty and possession of the agreed footprint. The beneficiary must remain independent and must not have voluntarily returned the land as part of a prearranged farming loop.

A substantive sovereignty case normally resolves the beneficiary's agreed homeland demand, rather than a token state chosen only for credit. Its significance is supported by one of three facts: a substantial share of the documented homeland is delivered, at least 250,000 people gain sovereignty, or the agreement completes the independence of a historically coherent smaller polity. The last route preserves meaningful island and microstate independence without treating repeated transfers of a tiny friendly possession as unlimited progress.

The receipt identity includes the global Gandhi incident, beneficiary generation, territorial agreement and causal settlement. Count that beneficiary and episode once. One large multi-country treaty may produce several factual records but at most two gate credits. A later unrelated genuine treaty can earn new credit. Annexing and rereleasing the same target, changing a tag label or moving a capital cannot reset the receipt.

At sixty days, the observer checks the facts it can actually verify. It does not reconstruct an unobserved peaceful interval from a current snapshot. Missing evidence leaves the gate credit pending or invalid. Historical records distinguish committed independence from durability success.

## Regional differences

Overseas territories make naval access, convoy capacity and the loss of distant bases important. Continental borderlands make competing cores, rail access and demilitarized corridors more important. A colonial federation may need several independent beneficiaries and a shared transport agreement. A small island can be fully sovereign while remaining dependent on external supplies. An industrial homeland may inherit useful factories but lack raw materials. A devastated war zone may gain sovereignty while needing years of ordinary rebuilding.

These differences change agreements, country starts and League priorities. They do not create ideological or geographic exemptions from the initial demand. Every regional package uses actual local assets and constraints.

## After independence

The local noncooperation ends immediately on committed sovereignty. The new or enlarged country remains free to reject Gandhi, remain neutral, join a different faction or oppose later nuclear coercion. Independence does not enroll it in the Peace League.

Former holders can become constructive partners after fulfilling an agreement. Beneficiaries can remember incomplete promises, unfair borders or later aggression. Those relationships feed bounded AI choices and specific missions. They do not require a third public numerical value.
