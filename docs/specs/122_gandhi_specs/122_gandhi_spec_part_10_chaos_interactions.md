# 122 Gandhi, Part 10: Chaos, shared events and durable consequences

Gandhi can reduce disorder by securing real independence and peace. His movement can also interrupt a large occupation economy, expose the weakness of international promises and divide a coalition over nuclear coercion. Chaos changes follow those actual consequences. They do not reward reaching a threshold or clicking an evolution button.

Movement Momentum measures organizing credibility. League Cohesion measures cooperation inside the active institution. Chaos measures the wider world's condition through the shared mod system. A change in one is not automatically a change in the others.

## Causal ownership before arithmetic

Every material outcome has one primary cause receipt and one accounting owner. A release, native war, faction change, nuclear strike, civilian death or contamination event keeps its existing shared owner. Gandhi can record that outcome and use it as a political condition without paying its generic Chaos effect again.

An additional Gandhi Chaos effect is permitted only for a distinct political or institutional consequence that the shared owner does not already count. Its evidence must explain the difference. For example, a native war declaration is owned by the war system, while a later verified collapse of a previously functioning voluntary security network can be a separate political shock.

If an existing shared owner already accounts for the whole consequence, Gandhi's supplemental amount is zero. A lack of an inspected API is not permission to apply both amounts and hope they are small.

## Scale without rewarding microstate farms

Economic scale uses the useful output the holder actually received before the event and the output actually denied afterward. It does not use nominal factory buildings that were already unusable. Population and distinct-country factors are bounded. Creating many tiny countries cannot multiply one episode into unlimited Chaos, Momentum or gate credits.

When several effects share the same event, maintain an attribution ledger with the cause, owner, amount accepted by the shared system and any later reversal. Clamp only the event-owned component. Do not overwrite the global Chaos value or restore a previous world snapshot.

A rejected or impossible effect is not booked as accepted. If the shared system caps an amount, history records the actual accepted result. Save and reload do not change the causal receipt.

## Event 006 Independence Wave

Countries produced by Event 006 can support the movement, join the League, remain neutral or oppose Gandhi. Their origin, generation, focus framework, existing package mechanics and rights remain owned by Event 006.

Gandhi does not count their birth as his own success. A later genuine Gandhi-mediated treaty or paid project can earn its own receipt. A shared release incident must use the common reservation and allocation boundary, with a distinct Gandhi contribution and no duplicate state or tag claims.

An absent attested Event 006 identity can inform a new Gandhi package through an explicit adapter. The creator is still Gandhi for that birth. Borrowing a package's geographic identity does not grant its unrelated evolution mechanics or Soviet-collapse authority.

Whole-country succession follows the narrow shared precedent described in Part 2. It needs its own Gandhi plan identity and zero-host authorization. It cannot borrow the Event 006 Join Wave receipt.

## Event 009 White Peace

The League can propose mediation and seek an actual settlement. Event 009 keeps its own pair selection, exact war application, result counts and Chaos ownership. Its temporary outputs and event targets follow the current call contract.

A random global white peace is useful world context. It is not automatically a Gandhi success. A Gandhi credit requires an explicit mediated request, accepted parties and terms, a successful settlement receipt and sixty days of observed peace. The two systems can share the factual settlement without both paying the same Chaos effect.

Failure to settle records the real reason and removes stale offers. It does not call a broader peace effect simply to make the mission succeed. Native wars and faction obligations must remain coherent.

## Event 106 Resistance

Active non-fascist noncooperation states are excluded from the wave's resistance write. The wave still visits and truthfully counts its eligible occupations and protected cases as appropriate to its own reporting contract. Gandhi protection cannot depend on resistance already being positive.

Suppressed additions are consumed for that wave and not deferred. The next wave after local campaign expiry applies normally. A state that changed controller between selection and write is revalidated in the current state scope. Gandhi's old controller cannot keep its immunity or denial attached to the new controller by accident.

The current Resistance specification remains a planning source. Its folder is not proof that the runtime integration already exists. Both implementation owners must agree the protection predicate and exact result receipt before claiming the connection complete.

## Other occupation and civic systems

An existing revolt, civil war, famine, migration or reconstruction effect retains its own ownership. Gandhi's peaceful suppression does not cancel an already independent uprising country, resurrect casualties or claim every refugee project.

Country and civilian classifiers must recognize ordinary human Gandhi participants as human. Existing civilian consequences remain applicable. An unusual political tag or fictional civil council is not a nonhuman exemption from population rules.

Where the campaign overlaps the planned Peace Movement or other pacifist content, the two event identities stay distinct. A later adapter may share an exact material contribution or peace receipt with one causal owner. Gandhi does not silently adopt the other event's institutions, tags or unfinished implementation.

## Geography and institutional counts

The proposed macroregions are Europe, Asia, Africa, the Americas and Oceania. Antarctica and uninhabited special-map areas do not supply a political voice. Implementation maps these design groups onto the actual installed geography and existing shared region data.

A country receives a stable home-region identity from its coherent homeland or core-population footprint at admission. Overseas possessions and a moved capital cannot manufacture additional voices. A legitimate new successor receives its own documented homeland mapping. The exact state crosswalk is an implementation artifact requiring the map and country owners' review.

One global Cohesion value follows the active Gandhi-led organization or its lawful institutional successor. Member countries and opposition groups do not receive their own copies. Native faction strength and treaty status can still be displayed as ordinary facts.

## Shared world threat and terminal state

The compulsory mandate contributes a Gandhi-specific active threat source only while its real criteria hold. It asks the shared world-threat owner to refresh the aggregate. Removing Gandhi's source cannot clear another event's threat.

Nuclear escalation and the Society emergence are playable states. The terminal `world_end` flag belongs only to an actually resolved ending. Gandhi's final victory uses the shared terminal ownership check immediately before commit. A previously resolved Fallout or other ending wins that race and receives its own presentation.

## Public explanation

The event log should say which actual outcome changed Chaos, who owned the effect and whether the reported amount was capped or shared. It should distinguish an evolution becoming available from a real liberation, a member's threat from a confirmed launch, and a promise from completed disarmament.

Players need the reason and practical consequence. Internal transaction IDs, helper names, arrays and audit language remain in development evidence and debug surfaces rather than ordinary product flow.
