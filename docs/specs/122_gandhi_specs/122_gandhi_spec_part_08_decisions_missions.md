# Part 08. Decisions, missions, payments, and bounded target selection

This part turns the event's political choices into a complete native decision and mission design. It does not prescribe final script, final localisation, or finished player-facing names. Action identifiers and headings are working labels for review. The opening and occupation rules in Part 01, sovereignty transaction in Part 02, League charter in Part 03, nuclear rules in Part 04, and world-authority rules in Part 05 remain authoritative for their outcomes.

The interface must make the central choice legible. An occupier can keep its territory and endure the finite response, or agree to an actual sovereign settlement. A small country can participate in a useful civic institution without acquiring weapons. A nuclear owner can cooperate while retaining its own arsenal and veto. The late personal regime can be opposed, negotiated down, or defeated through actual play.

## 1. Two values and a small native interface

Show Movement Momentum and, after the League exists, League Cohesion. Their ranges are 0 to 100. Native factories, equipment, research, manpower, deployed forces, and intelligence-permitted bomb counts remain native facts. Mission days are clocks. Contributions, votes, peace episodes, sovereignty receipts, membership, and arsenal authorization are records of actual events. None becomes another spendable counter.

Use static category pictures, a compact native text table, ordinary country or state targeting, native decisions, mission timers, and bounded confirmation events. A scripted GUI is not required by this design. The decision pane may contain at most eight strategic decisions, three target cards, and three active mission widgets for the actor. These are whole-pane ceilings across all Gandhi categories.

| Strategic slot | Purpose | Bounded behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Choose the current workspace | One native selection event opens territory, Gandhi, civic work, League, nuclear, or world-order work. Show only relevant destinations |
| 2 | Review urgent obligations | Opens the nearest unresolved reply or payment obligation. The report identifies the next deadlines and gives access to the complete inbox |
| 3 to 6 | Current workspace actions | At most four applicable strategic actions. An ordinary event page exposes additional actions without permanent decision-row growth |
| 7 | Previous page | Present only when a preceding page exists |
| 8 | Next page | Present only when a following page exists |
| Target cards 1 to 3 | Selected countries or exact state bundles | Each opens a bounded action menu for that exact target and proposal generation |
| Mission widgets 1 to 3 | The actor's actual committed work | Show objective, real deadline, committed resources, completion condition, and cancellation consequence |

Selection pages use short groups with a return option. Do not put the world into a single event containing dozens of choices. The current workspace and page are navigation state, not a new political resource. A target card is a handle for a real country or settlement bundle. It is not a new country, hidden proxy owner, or promise of eligibility.

### Complete coverage with bounded display

Build the opening candidate inventory from the shared incident snapshot, including every living non-core owner or controller. Later relevant occupation, release, succession, membership, policy, war, and country-lifecycle callbacks update affected entries. Use the project's shared country and inhabited-region mapping. Do not poll the whole world every day or restrict candidates to majors, humans, selected ideologies, or capitals on one continent.

Within a workspace, show pending deadlines first, the actor's selected targets second, and untouched eligible targets next. Sorting uses actual urgency and a stable country or bundle order. Cycling a page advances through every eligible entry before returning to the start. The report states the eligible total, current range, unresolved count, and reason a previously selected entry is no longer actionable. Native filters by region or relationship narrow the view but can always return to the complete list. A hostile microstate must remain reachable through this process.

AI uses the same complete eligible inventory. Three visible cards do not limit an AI's evaluation to three preferred great powers. It evaluates a bounded slice on its scheduled reconsideration or relevant callback, preserves its cursor, and eventually reaches the whole inventory. Urgent expiry and active war participants can interrupt that order, but cannot permanently starve other countries.

Reply deadlines belong to exact proposals. Incoming invitations do not consume a paid-work slot or create a fourth mission widget. They appear in the urgent inbox with their true dates. At most three owned undertakings are active. A major congress or mediation case can contain several counterpart replies while remaining one owned undertaking, provided every reply retains its exact party, terms, and expiry. An actor cannot start a fourth undertaking by disguising it as a passive status.

## 2. Shared action contract

Every action below inherits this contract. Its row adds the actor, visibility, legal gates, payment, timer, outcome, failure or cancellation, and AI rule. The mission table supplies its success and cleanup details. Free consent, refusal, voting, veto, and exit are intentional political choices. Paid execution and actual material sacrifices carry the burden.

### Before commitment

The actor must exist and possess the relevant authority. The target, state allocation, war scope, counterpart, terms, and proposal generation must still match. Check the current shared terminal owner, action-specific lifecycle permission, real access, all required consents, mission capacity for a new owned undertaking, native affordability, and applicable policy before charging anything. Refusal, veto, withdrawal, cancellation, and a free response cannot be blocked by full mission slots or inability to finance another project. Country and state scopes remain distinct. A character's custodian, national ruler, institution chair, and weapons owner may be different parties.

Show one authoritative quote before commitment. The same quote must supply the tooltip, eligibility test, debit, reservation, refund limit, and AI cost assessment. Exact equality is affordable. Do not recompute a start payment at completion. Never create a second debit because a mission and its opening decision both claim the same factory or equipment.

Each payable action uses at most four spendable resource types. Command-power charges never exceed 60. The inline row shows at most three amounts and icons, with a complete tooltip for any remaining obligation. Contributions use actual existing resources and supported transfer or reservation owners. Political power cannot stand in for all industrial, military, and delivery costs.

Irreversible territorial, constitutional, disarmament, or military choices require a concrete confirmation of exact terms. Its first option is an always-available cancellation or return. The standard ephemeral confirmation has the native 13-day timeout and defaults safely to that first option. This confirmation does not extend the underlying 14, 30, 45, 120, 180, or campaign deadline. A stale event cannot overwrite a newer proposal, reverse an already committed settlement, or debit a later stockpile.

### Commitment and resolution

Commitment records the actor, target, owner, family, generation, accepted terms, deadline, actual payment, and actual custodial responsibility before starting work. The record is accounting, not another resource. A completed political vote is not completed aid. A signed ceasefire is not a durable peace receipt. A reactor is not a bomb. A selected nuclear order is not a confirmed strike.

Automatic completion occurs when the exact objective has actually been met. Record the observed result before cleanup and before reporting success. A timer ending alone cannot create territory, equipment, research, a nuclear device, a successful rescue, or terminal victory. Mature sovereignty and peace receipts require the full 60 days defined by the core contract.

Cancellation releases only the still-owned unused reservation or still-recoverable paid asset, once. Delivered aid and elapsed factory time are spent. Lost equipment is not refunded as new equipment. Unspent public administration does not imply reversing completed research. Loss of authority is neutral invalidation, without ordinary success or a punishment intended for deliberate breach. An honest failed negotiation differs from fraud, unilateral violence, or a knowingly broken promise.

Cleanup removes only this action's active handles, temporary target markers, owned reservations, queued confirmations, and temporary institution or burden variant when its function has ended. Preserve actual countries, research, native stock, completed deliveries, historical receipts, and other systems' wars or missions. A terminal outcome stops new incompatible work and settles existing accounting through its owner. It does not grant an unconditional refund.

## 3. Payment and timing profiles

These are authored balance anchors, not measured prices or exact script recipes. A quoted amount may scale within the stated bound for the affected country's current usable capacity. The purpose is to produce a real choice without pricing a small country's sovereignty out of existence. A third party can sponsor a project only through its own explicit payment and delivery receipt.

| Profile | Actual burden | Timer and result boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0, political choice | No resource debit for receiving information, accepting or refusing terms, voting, vetoing, or leaving. Any accepted property or policy obligation is shown explicitly | Uses the proposal's existing deadline. Consent alone gives no material or success reward |
| P1, sovereign settlement | The exact agreed land, autonomy, access, bases, property, or equipment actually changes hands. Direct eligible release has no extra PP toll | Immediate commitment when ready. Wartime or disputed mandate lasts at most 30 days. Durability is observed for 60 days |
| P2, administration | 15 PP and one usable civilian factory reserved for 30 days. A large host may quote up to three factories for the same period, with no overlapping reservation | Funds a genuine mediation, inspection, or constitutional administration episode. It does not buy the result |
| P3, material aid | A recipient-approved native bundle, initially 100 infantry equipment and 10 support equipment, or an explicitly quoted equivalent supported by the existing aid owner | Up to 60 days to deliver. Only received equipment counts. The actual owner handles route loss and any recoverable remainder |
| P4, civic works | 20 PP plus actual native construction or repair capacity assigned to the named work. The opening quote states the committed capacity and expected national opportunity cost | A 60-day modest undertaking or 90-day major undertaking reviews actual work. Unfinished construction can continue normally but yields no fabricated completion or automatic factory |
| P5, protective operation | 10 CP plus actual national forces, supply, transport, fuel, and access required by the operation. These ordinary native costs remain with their existing owner | Up to 30 days for a rescue or protected movement undertaking. No scripted equipment or unit grant supplies missing capability |
| P6, research cooperation | 20 PP plus a real national research or special-project commitment and a quoted one to three civilian factories for 90 days of coordination or facilities support | Cooperation lasts 90 days. Existing research continues at native elapsed speed with only an accepted bounded sharing benefit. Completion requires actual technology or project results |
| P7, verified reduction | 15 PP and one civilian factory for the quoted handling or observation term, plus the exact owner's real device or relevant military capacity removed under the accepted contract | Thirty days for inspection, or a 60-day small-batch dismantlement anchor scaled to actual device quantity and facility capacity. Only measured owner-confirmed removal counts |
| P8, sustained diplomacy | 25 PP and one to three civilian factories for 60 days, renewed only through a new affordable quote and explicit acceptance | Supports an international congress, major settlement, or monitored compliance case. It cannot pay for a war, weapon, or annexation |

P2 and P8 are alternatives for the same administrative burden, not cumulative charges for one meeting. Native construction capacity in P4 is the real work allocation, not an additional duplicate reserve against those same factories. P6 coordination and native reactor construction must use separately available capacity if both are paid concurrently. An unavailable transfer, factory reservation, or device-removal adapter is a visible implementation dependency. Do not silently replace it with PP or an invented resource.

Several paid undertakings can share the third spirit family only through one aggregate current burden derived from their actual active reservations. Replacing its presentation variant must not release an earlier project's payment or hide its opportunity cost. No factory can fund two simultaneous commitments.

A P8 administrative term lasts 60 days even when its congress or campaign lasts longer. Renewal needs a new affordable quote for the next term and explicit acceptance, but retains the original political deadline. If renewal fails, stop the newly unfunded administrative work and offer orderly cancellation or a real sponsor. Do not grant completion, extend the mandate, charge negative resources, or erase already committed settlements. A funded umbrella program cannot accept new work beyond its stated budget and scope. Administration alone is not a material contribution credit.

For a poor beneficiary, direct sovereign acceptance remains available. It may postpone optional construction, request a real sponsor, or select a smaller supported material undertaking. Sponsorship creates a paid contribution for the actual payer only after receipt. It never creates two contribution receipts for one delivery.

The 60 or 90, 30, and 90-day project anchors concern modest or major civic work, inspection, and research cooperation. They do not overwrite the initial response, occupation endurance, League exit, congress, world-demand, settlement, or final-compliance clocks. The world campaign lasts 720 days from mandate ratification after the separate 120-day congress. Only when every substantive settlement is complete by day 720 may its single final 90-day stabilization observation finish, with an absolute day 810 ceiling. Part 05 owns that rule. No negotiation, host change, or renewed mandate resets it.
