# Gandhi 122 historical research handoff

## Finding and use

The historical baseline supports organised noncooperation, constructive local work, reconciliation, voluntary public service, and opposition to atomic weapons. The accepted Nuclear Pacifism and Ultimate Society routes deliberately depart from that baseline. The automatic fascist exception and a one-time Chaos return after Gandhi's historical death are also authored fiction. This research makes those boundaries explicit so later implementation and writing can preserve political choices without attributing invented beliefs to a real person.

Research began after the combined project-source reading gate passed. Seven primary-text documents were located through public research and their relevant text was read as recorded below. Primary-text reprints establish what an author said in a particular context. They do not by themselves establish the practical effectiveness of every proposed policy, the intentions of every follower, or an entire community's experience.

This is a historical handoff for the accepted design. It contains no final player-facing quotation, achievement name, localisation, music selection, portrait, flag asset, or historical claim supporting a numerical gameplay threshold. All numbers, country allocations, timers, eligibility rules, and engine behavior remain governed by the design and implementation contracts elsewhere in the package.

## Historical findings and design implications

### H01. Noncooperation requires organisation and an attainable relationship

Gandhi describes noncooperation as active collective conduct requiring discipline and organisation. He rejects malice and injury as its purposes and leaves room for later cooperation with the opponent. He acknowledges failure when participants are not prepared for mass nonviolence. [H01](https://www.mkgandhi.org/momgandhi/chap35.php)

**Accepted design connection.** Finite local campaigns, clear settlement demands, and later peaceful relations are compatible with this historical framing. The event's exact zero-output rule and its deadlines remain authored abstractions. They are not measurements taken from Gandhi's writing.

**Optional implementation proposal.** Local reports can identify the concrete unresolved demand and remaining organisation burden. Any additional constructive action should reuse an existing accepted mechanic and supply an actual beneficiary rather than add another persistent public value.

### H02. Constructive work gives the peaceful route substance

Gandhi connects independence to constructive effort before a transfer of political power. His programme addresses interreligious relations, untouchability, livelihoods, sanitation, education, and economic equality. It treats khadi adoption as voluntary. Civil disobedience should address a definite demand the opponent can meet and requires preparation. [H02](https://www.mkgandhi.org/ebks/construct.pdf)

**Accepted design connection.** The League's paid reconstruction and civic commitments can produce useful outcomes after liberation. Their usefulness should be visible through actual repaired capacity, received aid, access, or protected civilians. A completed focus or signed promise alone is insufficient evidence.

**Optional regional proposal.** A rural Indian beneficiary might receive a locally appropriate livelihood or sanitation project, while a different region uses its own institutions and needs. This is design synthesis. It does not claim Gandhi prescribed identical administrative arrangements for every society.

### H03. Nuclear coercion is an explicit break with the historical baseline

Gandhi's 1946 writings condemn atomic destruction and reject counter-bombs as an escape from violence. The chapter disputes the idea that fear of atomic destruction necessarily makes nations nonviolent. It distinguishes possible humanitarian uses of atomic energy from atomic weapons. [H03](https://www.mkgandhi.org/momgandhi/chap94.php)

**Accepted design connection.** Nuclear Pacifism is a fictional reversal. A League member who opposes threats or strikes has a coherent principled position within the setting. Research cooperation, actual possession, national custody, permission to launch, delivery, and retaliation remain distinct in the accepted nuclear contract.

**Optional presentation proposal.** Later researched text can show a member objecting to the doctrinal reversal without portraying that member as secretly hostile to peace. Any quotation used for that scene requires a separate exact wording, context, translation, and attribution check.

### H04. The fascist exception is a game rule

In his early July 1940 appeal, Gandhi urges Britain to resist Nazism without armed war. He applies nonviolent noncooperation to a hypothetical occupation by Hitler and Mussolini. The text therefore does not supply a doctrinal exemption for fascist governments. It also does not prove that the proposed resistance would succeed. [H04](https://www.mkgandhi.org/selectedletters/80every_briton.php)

**Accepted design connection.** Fascist refusal retains output, avoids the ordinary morale loss, and receives the specified one-time occupation treatment because the user requested that asymmetry. It must not be justified with an invented Gandhi quotation, a claim of historical inevitability, or a claim that a population is naturally immune to civic action. Voluntary fascist release and lawful League participation remain possible under the accepted common charter rules.

### H05. Public service and personal sovereignty are different political models

The late Congress proposal describes Lok Sevak Sangh as a service organization built from local groups. Its duties include livelihoods, health, education, equal civic standing, and voter registration. It argues for authority earned through service and warns against competition for power. This was a proposal, not an implemented national constitution. [H05](https://www.mkgandhi.org/indiadreams/chap70.php)

**Accepted design connection.** Gandhi begins as a civilian convenor. Existing governments keep their leaders, national identities, and focus trees. A consenting command host and explicit domestic constitutional mandate are separate fictional prerequisites for the personal supreme route. League membership never itself makes Gandhi ruler of a member state.

**Optional institutional proposal.** Civic succession after Gandhi's death can emphasize continued public obligations and consultation. It should not be portrayed as the automatic implementation of the historical Congress proposal or as a legal requirement Gandhi imposed on all later governments.

### H06. Communal peace can coexist with divergent security policy

Nehru's January 1948 speech connects Gandhi's fast to communal conflict, reconstruction, and refugee rehabilitation. It condemns forcible seizure of Muslim homes while affirming the state's military and security responsibilities. These are Nehru's account and political positions. [H06](https://nehruarchive.in/documents/mahatma-gandhis-fast-16-january-1948-7yexg7)

**Accepted design connection.** Governments can support mediation and civilian protection while retaining conventional defence, existing ideology, and independent national leadership. This supports a League containing meaningful disagreement.

**Optional regional proposal.** Where the existing civilian systems can verify the outcome, South Asian peace work can include protected homes, actual access, refuge, or safe voluntary return. No ethnicity or religious identity should be inferred from a generic state modifier. A broad stability bonus cannot substitute for evidence that the specified civilians received protection.

### H07. Historical death and later fictional continuity must be explicit

Nehru's 30 January 1948 broadcast announces Gandhi's death, urges the public to avoid retaliatory violence, and calls for continuation of his public cause. [H07](https://nehruarchive.in/documents/the-light-has-gone-out-30-january-1948-onxyq1)

**Accepted design connection.** If Gandhi is already historically dead when the incident begins, the package allows one explicitly fictional Chaos return with its own history receipt. This is not presented as historical survival. There is only one Gandhi identity globally and no recurring resurrection. Death after his appearance prevents his personal supreme-leader route and leaves institutional succession or League breakup playable. These rules are authored continuity decisions, not historical claims.

## Source cautions and leads not promoted to evidence

The exact issue date of To Every Briton differs among accessible editions. H04 gives 3 July 1940. An indexed Reuters item in the [Malaya Tribune of 3 July 1940](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/newspapers/digitised/issue/maltribune19400703-1) is datelined New Delhi, 2 July. Use early July 1940 unless a later quotation task verifies the original publication. This newspaper lead was not promoted into the seven-source register as a fully read independent historical source.

The separately inspected [To Adolf Hitler reprint](https://www.mkgandhi.org/mynonviolence/chap60.php) labels the letter Christmas week 1941, while other reprints give December 1940. Its content was inspected, but the dating conflict remains unresolved here. H04 already answers the relevant doctrinal question. The text is not used to add a precise dated claim.

The drafting date of the late Congress proposal also varies among reprint introductions. H05 gives 29 January 1948. Avoid claiming a drafting hour or treating the editorial phrase Last Will and Testament as evidence of a legal will. H03 includes a source-line date that may contain a transcription error. This handoff relies on its broader 1946 and 1947 attribution and the clearly identified 7 July 1946 passage rather than promoting the uncertain line.

A Gandhi Smriti chronology page timed out. It is not used as a read source. H07 supplies the necessary primary death announcement. An inaccessible page is a recorded research limit, not grounds to infer its contents.

## Handoff to implementation and later writing

Use H01 through H07 as stable research labels and cite the exact URL beside any historical claim. The source register records what was actually read. Later authors should reopen a source before selecting exact wording, extending a claim beyond these excerpts, or resolving a date conflict. A historical quotation needs its original publication, context, wording, speaker, translation status, and intended use checked together.

The accepted package decides the fictional continuity, mandatory fascist rule, voluntary political institutions, distributed national arsenals, and explicit coercive transition. Optional ideas in this file do not add obligations, meters, decisions, assets, or achievements without parent disposition. If an optional regional action cannot be supported by existing state or civilian evidence, defer it rather than pretending a generic modifier proves its outcome.

No source here establishes production modifiers, resistance or compliance values, success counts, timers, Chaos gates, membership thresholds, national stock APIs, country tags, state numbers, catalog identity, or achievement backend behavior. Those remain design choices and future integration evidence. This handoff supports portrayal and political distinctions while preserving that boundary.
