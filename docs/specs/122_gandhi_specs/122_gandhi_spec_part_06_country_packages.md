# Part 06. Countries, people, forces, and succession

Gandhi's campaign changes the choices available to existing countries before it creates any new country. The Peace League is a voluntary civic network. Its members retain national identity, domestic politics, meaningful national trees, and control of their actual resources. A separate defensive compact can become a real faction only after the participating countries legally consent. The eventual coercive state transforms the consenting command host through its own political route. It does not begin as a second India.

The occupation, release, League, nuclear, and world-authority parts own their detailed gates and transactions. This part defines the country packages that those systems must leave playable. All new headings and identifiers are design labels. They are not final country names, leader titles, party names, or localisation.

## 2. One Gandhi identity

### Initial appearance and lawful location

Inventory the current character and history sources before adding a person. Reuse the existing live Gandhi identity when it is available and compatible with the incident. He begins as the civilian convenor of the movement, not an automatic national ruler or military commander. Prefer a consenting independent Indian host with lawful access. If none exists, offer a consenting eligible host with actual access. An occupied or dependent India does not gain independence merely to provide a convenient headquarters.

Hosting, holding the character, chairing civic consultations, commanding a faction, and governing a nation are separate roles. A country can provide sanctuary without accepting personal rule. A member can support the movement while retaining a different ideology and leader. Gandhi's movement office must not replace an existing country leader without an explicit domestic constitutional choice.

If the historical death has already occurred before the incident, the one-time appearance is explicitly the event's fictional Chaos return and receives a distinct history receipt. It must not be presented as ordinary historical survival. Nehru's contemporary broadcast announces Gandhi's death on 30 January 1948, which provides the historical boundary for this distinction. The one-time return is an authored expansion of this fictional event and is not a claim from that source. [H07](https://nehruarchive.in/documents/the-light-has-gone-out-30-january-1948-onxyq1)

### Custody, capture, and succession

Record one current custodian and the exact lawful offices held by that same person. A civic convenor who later becomes the consenting host's national ruler remains one Gandhi with two compatible roles. Before moving a role, settle the old incompatible appointment and prevent a second active character. Preserve the lawful host leader or successor so a later change can be reversed through the appropriate constitutional settlement. The implementation must verify the exact supported character-role transfer. A duplicate character is not an acceptable workaround for a missing transfer operation.

Capture makes Gandhi unavailable for gates that require him alive and free. Rescue, negotiated release, or lawful refuge can restore eligibility. These actions depend on an actual location and counterpart. They do not teleport him through an enemy front or erase an existing war. A lost host can seek a consenting refuge with real access while its other institutions follow the host-loss rules.

Death after the incident is final. Do not repeat the initial return. Death blocks the personal supreme-leader route and starts institutional succession or League breakup. Civic members may preserve mediation and reconstruction under a collective office or another lawful national representative. Nuclear owners keep national custody and vetoes. The movement's continued existence does not resurrect Gandhi or create a fictional lookalike.

Removal from the supreme office ends his personal mandate unless the world-authority rules provide a lawful, still viable recovery. Opponents continue playing. Defeat does not force an automatic nuclear revenge strike. A reformed League may survive the failed personal regime when surviving members actually consent.

### Portrait and public role

Use a sourced real portrait or an approved existing real portrait with verified identity and provenance. The historical Gandhi remains a grounded real person even when his actions become the event's fiction. A generated substitute face, invented military portrait, or unexplained second version is not acceptable. The same real portrait can appear through the proper existing character consumers as offices change. New symbolic scene art must not masquerade as a historical photograph of his fictional conduct.

The asset part defines the original, lossless crop, metadata, attribution, styling, and runtime requirements. Only the user operates the project's RunPod route. An approved source placeholder remains explicitly pending until the styled final is supplied and accepted.

## 3. Country identity and capital contract

### Existing identities first

Resolve a proposed beneficiary against living countries, dormant vanilla identities, attested Event 006 identities, Chaos Redux definitions, every installed Workshop mod, and other local mods. Reuse an existing suitable identity instead of inventing a duplicate. Preserve immutable Event 006 creator origin for a country already created by that event. A new Gandhi-created country receives Gandhi origin through its distinct adapter and does not inherit Event 006 setup indiscriminately.

A claim or core alone is not permission to annex a disputed state. Use the exact accepted allocation and the consent rules in the settlement part. A living country at war retains unrelated wars. Giving it territory cannot silently force peace with all opponents or import the occupier's entire diplomatic situation.

No new tag or cosmetic tag is assigned in this specification. The required audit must record the exact proposed identity, inspected tag roots, collisions, successful reuse, and unresolved matches before implementation chooses an identifier. Missing installed sources remain a real implementation prerequisite.

## 4. Starting technology, laws, stock, and forces

### Preserve real resources

Living beneficiaries retain their existing research, laws, leaders, stockpiles, ships, aircraft, divisions, and national production. Add only the resources and institutions that the accepted transaction actually changes. Do not reload a starting order of battle or country history over a living country. A settlement cannot award a second copy of its existing army.

A dormant native identity uses an inspected release baseline appropriate to the current game and DLC configuration. Preserve its national historical distinctions where they exist. Separate activating necessary country definitions from adding material equipment. A technology or starting-order definition that would create duplicate forces must be reconciled against the actual release process. Record which normal native setup runs and which event-owned additions are required.

A newly activated country uses the concrete baseline below to administer itself and begin ordinary production or training. It does not inherit every technology known to the former occupier. Research beyond the inspected release baseline comes through normal elapsed research, actual licenses where supported, or paid and balanced cooperation. Nuclear research, reactors, devices, advanced delivery systems, and special projects are never part of a generic liberation package.

Use the installed national release laws where they are appropriate. An uninitialized council starts with the civilian economy, volunteer recruitment, and export-oriented trade baseline below. Escalation to emergency mobilization requires a real threat and its ordinary cost. A peaceful release does not begin under maximum mobilization merely to make its army look stronger. All exact law definitions and non-DLC equivalents require installed-game verification.

Material independence uses the shared settlement's recorded transfer contract. Transfer agreed equipment only when the source loses the same equipment and the recipient can actually receive it. A contribution unavailable at commitment cannot become a phantom receipt. Paid procurement, delayed aid, access agreements, and reconstruction missions offer a viable alternative when direct transfer is unavailable. No constant free equipment bundle should appear every time a tag is released.

Transferred territory supplies the assets it actually contains. Damage remains damage until repaired. Ports and rail do not appear because a new country needs them. The country must have a usable path to access, repair, construction, imports, or a negotiated alternative when its economy is isolated. The reconstruction burden uses the third spirit family by replacement, with a finite lifecycle.

### Concrete dynamic startup baseline

These numerical anchors are proposed tuning for implementation and balancing. They define a bounded starting package rather than an additional public resource or a guarantee of military strength. The completed country card records every departure from them and its country-specific reason.

| Component | Dormant existing identity | Uninitialized fallback council |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research slots | Preserve its inspected installed national release count and eligible national progression | Two ordinary research slots at founding. Later expansion uses actual domestic development or a paid focus commitment |
| Initial knowledge | Preserve the appropriate installed native release technology package without copying the former occupier's research | Basic infantry-equipment, industrial production, and civilian construction concepts available by 1936. Resolve these concepts to the installed technology definitions before coding |
| Laws | Preserve appropriate installed release laws and the accepted political settlement | Civilian economy, volunteer-only recruitment, and export-focused trade, using the supported installed equivalents |
| Productive estate | Actual allocated industry, resources, infrastructure, airfields, ports, and damage | The same actual territorial estate, with zero free factories, reactors, ports, or other buildings |
| Land force | Existing or agreed transferred formations retain their real equipment and manpower. Use the native national templates where appropriate | An ordinary six-infantry-battalion training template. At most one engineer support company may be added after its technology is researched and its equipment is actually supplied |
| Fleet and air force | Preserve or transfer specific real ships, aircraft, bases, and equipment through the settlement | No free ships, aircraft, air wings, or overseas order of battle. Procure or build them through ordinary national systems if the geography supports them |

The council's knowledge floor enables basic administration, production, and infantry training. It grants no researched doctrine chain, support company, advanced weapon, strategic delivery platform, or research ahead of that baseline. A dormant country's meaningful native distinctions remain intact. Neither case reruns a starting order of battle to manufacture replacement formations or equipment after a release.

For an otherwise unprovided local defense force, use a desired training target of one six-battalion infantry division per roughly one million people in the accepted homeland, capped at six divisions. For tuning, round down the population ratio and keep a minimum desired target of one. A sub-million territory therefore has a first training objective, not an automatic division. This target is an internal planning bound, not a new progress meter. Actual recruitable manpower, available equipment, supply, training time, and affordable upkeep limit what can enter service. The plan may start with zero fielded divisions and a smaller training queue. A poor country can remain below its desired target indefinitely without acquiring free equipment or soldiers.

Count every soldier and item once. Recruits come from the country's actual native recruitable pool under its current laws. A transferred formation carries its actual personnel and equipment and leaves its source's control. A transfer from stock debits the exact source amount before the recipient receives it. Paid aid or procurement counts only on real delivery. Existing national forces remain untouched when no transfer is agreed. No initial training plan, repeated release, tag revival, or sponsor change can refill a missing stockpile or regenerate spent manpower.

Coastal and industrial variants change the feasible procurement and defense choices. An existing ship, aircraft, port, airfield, or factory is useful only when the agreed settlement actually delivers it and the recipient can operate it. Inland councils prioritize supply access and infantry defense. Islands prioritize a real convoy route, coastal protection, and negotiated basing or guarantees. Industrial releases can invest in their actual production and air defense. None receives a free matching order of battle.

A weak release must have a concrete survival route in its country card. Record its controlled administrative center, actual supply situation, an attainable access or import agreement where needed, and a feasible protection or reconstruction offer from a real consenting counterpart. The beneficiary can decline that offer and retain sovereignty. A guarantee does not silently create faction membership, and a sponsor does not acquire command or property without consent. Do not delay a ready lawful release merely to wait for its training target. Use the accepted settlement and paid follow-up work to address its actual exposure.

### Conventional force profiles

| Profile | Opening force and technology treatment | Active military choices | Constraint that remains meaningful |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small inland agrarian country | Retain or lawfully receive a modest coherent infantry force and basic support capability. Use available equipment and actual training | Secure a supply corridor, train reserves, obtain engineers or transport, and seek a credible protection agreement | Limited industry, supply, and stock prevent instant mass mobilization |
| Regional land country | Use its existing or inspected national army package, actual artillery, support, and available transport | Choose economical defense, a mobile reserve, or a stronger conventional deterrent within its production capacity | Fuel, equipment, and border exposure shape the build plan |
| Coastal or island country | Retain actual ports and naval assets. A newly released port does not create warships | Convoy access, escorts, coastal protection, air cover, and negotiated basing | Shipping and naval reach depend on real vessels, ports, fuel, and access |
| Industrial or air-capable country | Preserve its actual production, aircraft, airfields, and research | Protect production, provide air defense, contribute transport or aircraft, and fund reconstruction | Capacity committed abroad is unavailable for domestic priorities |
| League host | Use its own national profile and existing military institutions | Coordinate access, contributions, defense plans, and paid support instead of spawning a separate League army | Domestic support and real resources limit how many promises it can honor |
| Nuclear owner | Preserve devices, research, production, delivery platforms, bases, and vetoes in national ownership | Consent to inspection, reciprocal reduction, restraint, or a separately ratified command commitment | Possession, delivery, authorization, and willingness remain different facts |
| Society host or signatory | Use the real forces and resources still under its lawful authority | Coerce, defend, negotiate, fight actual wars, or sustain disarmament monitoring | Defection and loss of bases, industry, devices, or allies weaken actual capability |

All forces in this event are ordinary people using national military systems. Native infantry, engineers, logistics, transports, air, and navy express the intended role. Their distinctiveness comes from rules of engagement, national custody, consent, supply, and strategy. Their existing models, counters, equipment art, and sounds therefore remain appropriate to their military identity.

Do not delete already trained national formations or unrelated stock when occupation denial begins. That denial stops new useful flows from affected territory. Nor should release add a second reserve to compensate for equipment the occupier already used. Any agreed transfer of a formation must identify its actual owner, equipment, commander, and destination and settle it once.

## 5. Last-resort civil councils

A council is permitted only after the complete identity and homeland audit establishes that no suitable existing identity can administer the accepted territory. It is a territorial administration with a practical civic mandate. It must not invent an ethnicity, overwrite real communities, or resolve historical core disputes merely by choosing a new flag.

Use a coherent administrative area with a viable capital and a named geographic identity direction drawn from the actual allocated territory. The map label should describe the territory and form of administration. Final wording requires the locality and source review. Its flag is explicitly fictional and must not falsely claim to be a historical national standard.

The initial institution is a provisional civilian administration. Its leader can be an explicitly fictional ordinary administrator or a clearly collective council representation. Record the fictional classification in the portrait request and source notes. Do not apply this exception to Gandhi or to the real leader of an existing national identity. When a real claimant later becomes a lawful successor, transfer authority only through the accepted constitutional or territorial settlement.

The council begins with actual local industry, supply, population, and accepted assets. Its conventional technology floor and civilian laws follow the bounded setup rules above. It gets no free strategic weapons, overseas navy, imperial claims, or instant cores over disputed neighbors. Its military style is local defense and practical logistics. External protection, equipment purchases, and limited partnerships remain real choices.

At the committed founding settlement, give the council lawful cores only on the exact constitutive homeland accepted in its country card and allocation. These cores establish a functioning home administration and prevent its founding territory from immediately becoming another instance of the non-core occupation campaign. Preserve other countries' legitimate existing cores and unresolved claims. Do not add cores to unallocated neighboring land, later conquest, temporary military control, or property obtained outside the accepted sovereign settlement. The allocation owner records this founding operation once. Releasing, merging, or reviving the council cannot repeat a success receipt or expand its core footprint for free.

It requires a substantive but proportionate administration tree. Opening groups establish authority and supply. Politics offers representative civilian consolidation or a bounded emergency executive with a reform route. Economy offers local reconstruction, commercial access, or a negotiated external sponsor. Defense creates a viable national force. Diplomacy secures recognition. Settlement deals with actual borders and disputed claims. A late constitutional endpoint, lawful union, or continued independence completes its identity. Those routes adapt to the real locality rather than copy the host's entire Gandhi tree.

## 6. Host, members, opposition, and transformed identity

### Civic membership and hosting

Ordinary civic membership is compatible with existing factions. It does not move armies, declare wars, transfer nuclear authority, or replace a national ruler. Members receive consultations, eligible mediation, and paid reconstruction. A host has additional administrative burdens, constitutional choices, and responsibility for promises. Hosting must offer enough domestic and international benefit to be a real choice while retaining a viable way to relinquish the office.

Annual or crisis leadership selection follows the accepted civic voting rules. Moving the host requires consent and real access. Unfinished aid and diplomatic cases retain their actual parties and ownership. A changed meeting place cannot reset a mission deadline, erase debt, grant a new success receipt, or relocate national nuclear stores.

### The command host

The Supreme Society route uses the actual host after Gandhi is alive, free, and legitimately made its ruler through the explicit domestic choice. Its transformed identity needs a changed public name direction, flag, leadership role, institution, AI behavior, and late focus content. Preserve the underlying country's existence and history. Do not allocate a duplicate India tag or replace all allied national governments at activation.

The civic institution spirit becomes the supreme mandate stage through replacement. Material burdens remain in the third family. The occupation response family retains its separate actual function where relevant. A new government does not gain a fourth idea stack or a free strategic reserve.

Member signatories retain national arsenals and their consent until they separately accept a valid arrangement. Coercive military organization uses legal faction membership. Final compulsory peace follows the world-authority victory conditions and verified settlements. It is not an unexplained map-wide annexation. Any actual ownership, autonomy, disarmament, or institutional change must arise from the relevant ratification or surrender.

### Opposition and recovery

Ordinary withdrawal suspends new obligations immediately and completes the notice and property settlement. Emergency exit on coercion, unlawful nuclear use, or attack preserves the member's right to act immediately. National forces and arsenals remain with their actual owner. Paid property and basing disputes are settled explicitly rather than confiscated as punishment for choosing the opposition route.

Opposition content must work for a small state seeking protection, a former host restoring constitutional government, a nuclear owner retaining a veto, and a conventional great power resisting the Society. Each uses its actual strategic strengths. Defeat, negotiated reversal, and reconstruction preserve normal native continuation without an added scripted instant loss or silent player-tag change. Ordinary native war and surrender outcomes still apply. Full-territory consensual succession uses the separate explicit continuation choice.

## 7. Last-state succession

Ordinary release protects the holder's last state. The explicit exception supports a willing host surrendering all its non-core territory through a fully audited succession. This is an extension to be implemented for Gandhi, not an already verified Gandhi feature.

Before mutation, resolve the complete all-owned-state allocation, accepted successor countries, host and beneficiary consent, and the required peace or full relevant-war settlement. A human chooses the specific successor country they will continue as before any ownership change. Several possible successors require a real choice. Silence, refusal, or an invalidated choice leaves the original country and player intact and follows the refusal treatment for still-held states.

After successful commitment, use the verified Event 006 Join Wave continuation precedent while preserving Gandhi's distinct owner and origin. Revalidate the chosen successor and complete the appropriate player continuation only after actual commitment. Do not give a success report early, copy Event 006 identity flags, or assume that deleting a country safely transfers the player.
