## Historical interpretation

The research supports organized noncooperation, constructive civic work and continued cooperation after a dispute. It also records Gandhi's explicit opposition to atomic killing and counter-bombs. The requested Nuclear Pacifism and supreme-authority branches are therefore deliberate fictional reversals with principled opposition, not a description of his historical programme.

The automatic fascist exception is retained because the brief requires it. The cited 1940 appeal applies nonviolent resistance even to a hypothetical fascist occupation. That source neither supplies the game's exception nor proves whether the proposed historical response would have succeeded.

The package makes Gandhi's personal continuity explicit. His historical death is acknowledged. A later appearance, if required by the campaign, is a one-time fictional Chaos return. The game does not silently turn a historical date into an undocumented survival claim.

The historical research file states exactly which sections of the Constructive Programme PDF were processed. It does not claim that its whole 39-page edition was read. Reprint date discrepancies, a timed-out chronology and a blocked portrait lead remain labeled. No final super-event quotation, musical recording or portrait source was selected or cleared.

## Deliberate design choices added by this package

The following are authored expansions, not claims copied from history or measured from the game.

- Two public values, Movement Momentum and League Cohesion, and three replacing spirit families.
- A fourteen-day response, finite local campaigns and an absolute 314-day original-incident ceiling.
- A coherent sovereignty selector, exact competing-claim settlements and explicit last-state succession.
- A voluntary civic network that can coexist with existing factions, plus a separately consented military compact.
- Durable success receipts observed for sixty days, with causal and beneficiary anti-farming rules.
- Concrete civic work, mediation, leadership, lawful exit, nuclear dissent and peaceful completion.
- Distributed national arsenals that separate stock, custody, law, consent and delivery.
- Strong-League and supreme-route thresholds based on real members, regions, results, industry and weapons.
- A 120-day supreme congress, 720-day campaign, one thirty-day recovery window and conditional final observation ending no later than day 810.
- A one-time fictional return if Gandhi is already historically dead, followed by meaningful capture and death.
- Complete ordinary human country packages, additive focus architecture, bounded native decisions, difficult achievements and a full asset consumer plan.

These values are tuning anchors for future analysis. Their consistency was reviewed as design. They have not been empirically balanced in HOI4.
