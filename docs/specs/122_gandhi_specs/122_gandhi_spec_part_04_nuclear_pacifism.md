# 122 Gandhi, Part 4: Nuclear Pacifism

The League's nuclear turn begins with a strategic argument. Its peaceful settlements remain vulnerable to states willing to use overwhelming force. Some members want a deterrent, some want scientific cooperation without weapons, and others believe the threat itself destroys the movement's purpose. Gandhi can choose the fictional reversal requested in the brief, but the people who joined a voluntary peace organization can oppose him.

This evolution supplies coercive diplomacy and difficult security choices. It supplies no free bomb, reactor, technology, delivery aircraft or national launch permission.

## Programme eligibility and evolution activation

At Chaos 800 or above, a strong League meeting Part 3's political, material and success conditions may open a nuclear congress. The programme can begin while members still lack a usable weapon. That programme remains part of the Peace League until a real capability exists.

Nuclear Pacifism activates only when a consenting national owner has completed the required native nuclear research, possesses at least one usable device, has a credible delivery route, and accepts the League's chosen nuclear doctrine. Gandhi must remain alive and free, the evolution must be enabled, and the strong-League conditions must still pass at the congress's conclusion.

Possession acquired through an existing lawful, proven owner service can qualify. A captured depot, an ally's vague promise, a queued production project or a reported foreign stock cannot. If no lawful acquisition adapter exists, a member builds its own capacity through normal research and production.

The activation records the institution and opens decisions. It grants no Chaos and consumes no warhead. An owner can later withdraw consent. The historical evolution remains recorded, while actions that need the withdrawn capability close.

## National programme, shared assistance

The League can coordinate native technology sharing, scientific access and industrial aid. It uses existing research and project systems rather than creating a parallel technology tree merely for this event.

A ninety-day cooperation agreement commits real research administration, industrial capacity, access and equipment. It can provide a bounded research bonus or category-limited sharing advantage supported by the installed system. It cannot set nuclear technology as researched or complete a project on political approval alone. The receiving country preserves its existing research progress and uses its own normal slots, facilities and production.

The intended programme routes are:

| Route | Useful effect | Real limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scientific cooperation | Improve eligible native research through a paid, limited sharing agreement | No automatic technology completion, device or strike capability |
| Reactor and industrial investment | Fund an actual national construction or supported production commitment | The recipient needs sites, time, materials and usable industry |
| Delivery preparation | Develop real aircraft, crews, airbases, fuel and access for a supported route | Ownership of a bomb does not prove reach or air control |
| Existing national arsenal joins consultations | A verified owner contributes an agreed deterrent commitment | Inventory and launch authority remain national |
| Lawful acquired device | Use an existing proven owner transfer and integration service if one is available | No fictional donor debit, duplicated recipient stock or inferred technical knowledge |
| Captured nuclear site | Secure and inspect a real site through its existing event owner | Custody alone is insufficient. Operational access needs separate proof |

Aid cannot be paid twice through a focus and a decision that describe the same contribution. A completed focus may unlock a new agreement, improve its efficiency or provide a limited research bonus. The actual resources and native device production remain observable.

## Six different nuclear facts

The system tracks these facts separately without turning them into six new public meters.

1. **Knowledge.** The owner has the required completed research and operating expertise.
2. **Inventory.** A native usable device actually exists in that owner's stock.
3. **Custody.** The physical site or device is held by a known authority through an attested owner ledger where such a ledger exists.
4. **National permission.** The current nuclear law allows the contemplated action against that exact target.
5. **Political consent.** The owner accepted this specific League commitment and has not revoked it.
6. **Delivery.** The selected operational surface can genuinely reach and act against the target under its real constraints.

A country's consent to research is not consent to a threat. Consent to a threat is not consent to an actual strike. A League vote cannot create a retaliation right against an enemy that has never committed the relevant confirmed use.

National no-use, retaliation and unrestricted policies keep their own meaning. Retaliation uses the shared exact-offender evidence. It is not automatically inherited from an ally, faction partner or state owner whose territory another controller held when the weapon struck.

Readiness can exist during peace. A capability gate checks real inventory, facilities, delivery assets, reachable relevant geography and the owner's declared lawful commitment. It does not start a war or require a combat launch to be currently legal. A retaliatory commitment is described as conditional retaliation, never as permission for an immediate first strike. Every actual use separately rechecks war, exact target, current law, owner consent and the selected delivery route. This distinction lets a stable peace retain a real deterrent without inventing a legal target.

## Distributed arsenals

The core design leaves weapons in their national inventories. A programme summary can show an owner's consent, usable capacity and delivery status when those facts are known. It does not add them to a fungible League stockpile that can spend any member's bomb.

The world-authority route later requires several real owners. Each owner keeps an explicit command arrangement and a right to revoke uncommitted use. A mandate changes the political relationship only through that owner's own accepted procedure. The event never changes national nuclear law silently.

Foreign basing does not make a host the weapon's owner. A member leaving with its national arsenal does not receive new devices and does not lose existing ones merely for departure. Capturing a base does not immediately give the captor the old owner's native stock.

The inspected Event 032 custody proposal remains unresolved. Its proposed service names are not accepted current APIs. Borrowed devices, pooled escrow, cross-owner launch and exact captured-stock operationalization are conditional extensions. They are excluded from the minimum implementation path until a measured conservation service proves ownership, reservation, delivery, loss and refund. Peaceful League play and nationally owned deterrence do not depend on that extension.

## Information and credible threats

Members may disclose a verified inventory range, demonstrate real delivery readiness, permit an inspection or withhold classified details. The UI distinguishes verified facts, the owner's claim and the opponent's estimate. It never reveals every country's hidden native inventory merely because Gandhi opened a diplomatic screen.

Credibility depends on actual usable devices, legal and political authority, a viable route and the relationship between the target's risk and its ability to retaliate. One bomb in an inaccessible country is a weak threat against a distant great power. A large nominal stock under a no-use law cannot be treated as unrestricted first-strike capability.

A bluff can be selected only as a knowingly unverified claim. It creates no capability receipt and cannot satisfy an evolution or world-authority arsenal gate. Inspection, public refusal by the supposed owner or an obvious delivery failure can expose it. Exposure damages Momentum, Cohesion and future willingness to accept claims.

AI opponents assess what they can plausibly know. They do not use hidden native counts as omniscient information to call every bluff. The legal and payment code still uses exact facts to prevent an impossible launch.

## Demands and reciprocal offers

A nuclear demand targets a specific conflict or arsenal owner. It names a fulfilment condition and an actual response deadline. Ordinary nuclear diplomatic demands allow thirty days. The later supreme campaign uses its separate forty-five-day world-demand window.

The recipient can accept an inspection, negotiate reciprocal reduction, accept a verifiable ceasefire, reject the demand, seek outside protection or propose a conventional security guarantee. A poor recipient can always refuse. It does not need political power or equipment to retain its sovereignty.

| Demand or offer | What completion means | What does not count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inspection | Consenting owner permits a thirty-day bounded visit with real access and a truthful capability report | Selecting a policy or revealing an invented exact stock |
| Reciprocal reduction | Each participating owner completes its own measured device removal and the agreed security concession | A promise to remove devices later |
| Complete national disarmament | Verified usable stock reaches the agreed value, production obligations and permitted facilities are settled | A renamed no-use law while stock remains available |
| Ceasefire under deterrent pressure | A supported peace transaction resolves the relevant native wars and is observed for sixty days | A pause in combat or an unrelated Event 009 firing |
| Nonaggression and guaranteed sovereignty | The real agreement persists and no prohibited aggression occurs during its observation | A hidden permanent immunity against all wars |
| Refusal | The target retains its agency and faces only the publicly stated legal consequences | An automatic nuclear strike or forced annexation |

An inspection can be inconclusive because access was lost or the capability cannot be measured. Inconclusive is distinct from verified compliance and from proven deception. It offers renegotiation, a new lawful inspection route or an honest unresolved result.

## Verified dismantlement

Dismantlement is a national owner transaction. It begins with a defined device quantity or owner cohort, a valid payment and custody record where relevant, a secure site, and an observer agreement. It uses a sixty-day anchor for a small batch, scaled by actual devices and facilities. The exact owner service defines what can safely be reserved during the work.

Completion must prove the native stock or appropriate owner cohort actually decreased by the agreed amount. Allocation-only bookkeeping is not enough when the native bomb remains usable. Conversely, a native debit without the correct event-owned cohort update can corrupt another system's stock history.

The shared owner decides cancellation, capture, loss and refund. Gandhi never restores a nominal promised amount after a site was seized or a device was already consumed. A disputed stock result blocks the claimed success and preserves evidence. It does not spawn a replacement weapon to make the ledger look balanced.

Reciprocal disarmament can lower Momentum temporarily if members fear abandonment, then raise it when the agreement proves durable. The proposed default in Part 3 rewards only completed, verified removal and the actual reciprocal concession. Native device loss is not paid again as a fake research refund.

## Escalation and launch

Every threat has a de-escalation choice before actual use. An ultimatum's expiry can lead to renewed negotiations, conventional sanctions or protection, loss of face, a member vote, or a real war decision. It cannot automatically launch a weapon because the recipient failed to click a button.

Actual nuclear use must use an accepted delivery owner. The current source distinguishes native raids that reserve one device and then detonate without a second debit from a scripted native detonation route that consumes a device and waits for a matching callback. The coding agent must choose the applicable surface and preserve its payment contract. Combining both debits is a defect.

A scripted detonation helper is not evidence that a wing flew a native raid, had actual range or won air superiority. The event must not advertise those checks unless the installed route genuinely performs them. Core credible delivery therefore needs a verified preflight appropriate to the chosen surface and an honest description of any approximation.

Confirmation belongs to the native callback and stock owner. Record one launch request, one expected stock change, the exact actor and target, the result and its causal receipt. A function returning, a missile icon disappearing or a cinematic playing is not proof of detonation. Save and reload while a request is pending must not launch or refund it again.

Nuclear deaths, contamination, condemnation, retaliation and generic Chaos are accounted by their existing shared owners. Gandhi records the distinct political breach or settlement. It does not repeat the physical consequences.

## Dissent and fracture

Members can support scientific research while rejecting threats. A defence signatory can accept retaliation while rejecting first use. A threatened small country can demand stronger protection while opposing global authority. These are distinct coherent positions.

The nuclear congress publishes the proposed doctrine and allows thirty days for debate. It can choose a no-use scientific programme, national deterrence with vetoes, an inspection and disarmament initiative, or preparation for centralized coercion. Each actual owner must separately consent to operational commitments.

An unconsulted threat damages Cohesion. Proven deception damages both public values. Confirmed unlawful or civilian-directed nuclear use causes the large political losses in Part 3 and immediate emergency exit rights. It can form an opposition coalition from former members. The split does not transform all dissenters into aggressors or give them copies of the League arsenal.

Only one global Cohesion value exists. It follows the active Gandhi-led organization, or its lawful institutional successor after his removal. Splinter groups use native diplomatic relations and qualitative commitments. They do not create parallel hidden public currencies.

## Endings within the nuclear stage

The League can stabilize around national vetoes and decline further escalation. It can conclude a verified reciprocal disarmament treaty. It can suffer a political split that leaves several smaller ordinary alliances. It can abandon the programme while retaining useful scientific and civilian infrastructure. It can also move toward the supreme mandate in Part 5 if every earned gate is met.

Losing the last verified device closes credible-threat actions until actual capacity returns. It does not erase completed research or invent a new bomb. Losing Gandhi closes his personal supreme route while leaving a possible institutional League. A shared catastrophic nuclear ending can independently supersede the entire chain.

## Core play without optional DLC

Sovereignty, noncooperation, civic membership, mediation, constructive work, dissent and opposition must be available without optional DLC. The basic nuclear path uses a verified native atomic research, production and delivery route available to the installed base configuration.

DLC projects, missile launches, submarine launches, aircraft designers and thermonuclear delivery are enhancements only where their actual feature gates pass. The core route does not require a missile model or thermonuclear device. No unsupported typed stock syntax may be invented to distinguish bombs that the native query cannot distinguish.

If a particular DLC-specific delivery route is absent, its decision is replaced by the supported atomic route with appropriate requirements and wording. It is not replaced by a free political detonation. The full installed matrix and its evidence belong to Part 13's implementation acceptance.
