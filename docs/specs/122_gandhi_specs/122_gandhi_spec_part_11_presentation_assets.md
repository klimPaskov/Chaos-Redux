# Part 11. Presentation, art, and asset coverage

Presentation should let a player understand the current dispute, the real parties, the available commitment, and the consequence of choosing it. The ordinary campaign uses native decisions, targeted entries, mission timers, event reports, news, focuses, and the existing event-detail framework. Three later super-event packages mark the Society's emergence, its actual terminal victory, and a qualifying global defeat aftermath.

All labels, paths, and asset references below are design identifiers. They are not final localisation or claims that a file exists. The asset and super-event prompts turn this inventory into bounded production work after implementation has confirmed the actual consumers. Final quotation and musical selection remain research tasks.

## 1. Native information architecture

### One campaign category

Use a phase-aware Gandhi decision category with a short current-state description, a basic formatted value table, ordinary actions, and native mission deadlines. Its static category picture is eligible because the category contains no rich custom control, meter, animated panel, or custom map. The target cards mentioned in the decision design are ordinary native targeted decision entries, not a separate graphical card system.

At most eight strategic decisions and three context target entries are visible at once. At most three actor commitments are active concurrently. Native paged selection and aggregation must retain every eligible target. The display cap never becomes a hidden exclusion of minor countries, distant countries, human players, or a fourth unresolved target.

Movement Momentum and League Cohesion are the only new persistent public numerical values. Cohesion is absent before League constitution. After a split there is still one global Cohesion value, representing the Gandhi-led active League coalition or its lawful institutional successor after his death or removal. Departing opposition and splinter groups use native relationships and qualitative commitments. They do not acquire their own copies of the meter.

Native equipment stock, research, factories, relations, current wars, and finite mission time can be shown as native facts where the observer is entitled to know them. Device counts require the permitted intelligence and owner evidence. A hidden receipt total, mandate score, inspection score, or nuclear influence currency must not become another public gauge.

### Each action explains its actual case

An action's visible explanation identifies the acting country, affected owner and controller, beneficiary or counterpart, exact public demand, actual cost or burden, known deadline, visible baseline consequence, and relevant reason it is unavailable. A refusal or delay remains a meaningful choice. An uncommitted promise is described as pending, with no success language before its transaction completes.

Scope the displayed facts to the actual player context. A nuclear owner sees its own inventory and permission. A mediator sees the case and information the parties disclosed. An outside country sees public policy and observed capability, not a hidden device list or another player's private vote. Tooltips must distinguish a research programme, a credible device, delivery reach, national custody, and launch permission.

When the campaign expires, remove obsolete response pressure and show the true remaining institution or unresolved obligation. Completed history can remain in the shared log. An empty campaign category must not keep pretending that states are under active denial. A post-crisis compact category can retain membership, succession, and unfinished obligations without generating endless new crises.

## 3. Visual identity

The early visual language uses period cloth, public assembly, local productive work, open agreements, and distinct national participants. Reconstruction has grounded materials, repaired structures, transport, and practical work. Defense uses ordinary national equipment and clear logistical imagery. Nuclear stewardship introduces guarded infrastructure, inspection, and divided authority. The coercive stage develops centralized institutional symbolism and imposed order through an explicit fictional transformation.

Use distinct palette and composition families for politics, reconstruction, defense, diplomacy, settlement, nuclear policy, crisis, and aftermath. A repeated icon with a hue shift does not communicate route depth. Keep symbols readable at native size and avoid ornate small details that vanish in the game.

Gandhi's real likeness remains a sourced portrait. Anonymous period people and symbolic alternate-history scenes can be generated through the approved art route. A generated image of the real Gandhi committing a fictional action must not be presented as archival photography. The cleanest super-event compositions can express institutions, weapons, delegates, or consequences without inventing such a photograph.

Real national flags retain their proper identity. Generic League or council symbols are explicitly fictional and need a source and cultural-symbol review to avoid accidental attribution to an unrelated real institution or community. No single Indian symbol should be imposed on every independent beneficiary merely to unify the art palette.

## 11. Production roles and acceptance

The source researcher finds and documents real material. The generated event-art worker makes accepted fictional scenes and category pictures. The icon artist owns actual icon families and achievement subjects. The portrait specialist owns grounded and accepted fictional leader portraits. The super-event text and audio researchers handle their separate evidence tasks. The narrow portrait wiring exception remains as defined by the portrait skill.

Before generating, reconcile current accepted requirements, existing source packages, registered aliases, actual consumed runtime paths, and file and pixel hashes. A historical receipt or raw file count does not prove coverage. Existing valid art can satisfy a requirement only when its actual meaning, family, state, provenance, and consumer agree.

Every final row records logical requirement, current consumer, source mode, source or prompt, reference family, exact dimensions, background mode, source and processed paths, runtime path, sprite or engine lookup, conditional states, lineage, hashes, rights, reviewer, and status. Keep file inventory, registered aliases, actual consumption, provenance completeness, and accepted final coverage separate.

For 2D art, use the current repository conversion workflow and required legacy BGRA DDS format. Validate header, dimensions, channel masks, alpha, expected length, native-size readability, and decoded pixels. Transparent families request genuine alpha at generation. Full-canvas scenes and portraits keep the background their consumer requires. Report images receive the deterministic report treatment after source art. A GIF or contact sheet is evidence rather than runtime art.

Active evidence belongs under `docs/assets/122_gandhi/`. Portrait originals and their crop contracts remain in the separate durable portrait archive. Before full event implementation is declared complete, promote durable provenance, accepted coverage, licensing, and wiring facts into permanent documentation, verify that no runtime consumer points into the temporary workspace, and remove only that completed event workspace.
