# Event 063: Subjects Break Free

## Part 5: Decisions, missions, ideas, and AI

## Decision presentation

Event 063 uses one ordinary decision category with a static category picture. The category changes its visible content by country role and event phase.

The category should not become a permanent list of every released country. It shows the few current actions that matter to the country viewing it.

### Visibility target

- three to five visible decisions in an ordinary phase
- no more than six visible decisions at once
- one to three active missions
- target rows hidden when the target ceases to exist, becomes invalid, or resolves its dispute
- stronger later actions replace obsolete opening actions
- emergency actions appear only during a contested settlement or independence war

Before the Liberation Pact forms, the category shows no persistent custom counter. After formation, it shows Liberation Cohesion and its current band.

## Decision cost model

Costs should reflect the action being taken. Political power can support diplomacy, but it must not become the default price for every decision.

Available cost families include:

- infantry equipment
- support equipment
- artillery
- trucks and trains
- fuel
- convoys
- temporary civilian-factory use
- command power where actual command attention matters
- army, navy, or air experience where advisers or planning are involved
- stability or war support for politically divisive choices
- temporary division commitment
- diplomatic access or relations
- Liberation Cohesion
- time and opportunity cost through active mission slots

One decision may use at most four cost types. Costs scale with the target's size, distance, war state, and need. A minor state should not pay a major-power package to recognize a neighbor.

## Phase 1: Independence settlement

This phase begins when a country is released or when a former overlord loses one or more subjects.

## Phase 2: Liberation network

This phase appears to active liberated states after another country is freed.

| Working label | Target | Purpose | Cost | Result | AI use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recognize a Fellow Breakaway | New active liberated state | Establish a diplomatic link | Small diplomatic effort | Improves trust and opens later support | Common when compatible and regionally relevant |
| Send an Emergency Aid Package | Threatened liberated state | Transfer real equipment or logistics support | Donor stockpile, convoys, fuel, or temporary civilian factories | Delivers a bounded target-scaled package and records fulfillment | Used when donor has a surplus and access |
| Dispatch Military Advisers | Prepared threatened state | Improve planning and command without direct war | Army experience, command power, equipment, and adviser commitment | Temporary training and planning support | Used by experienced countries with manageable war load |
| Guarantee a Threatened State | Recognized liberated state | Deter restoration | Diplomatic risk and possible future war obligation | Creates a normal guarantee | Used only when donor can reach and defend the target |
| Mediate a Former-Overlord Dispute | Contested pair | Seek recognition or narrow terms | Relations with both sides and time | Can resolve the dispute or reduce escalation pressure | Favored by stable neutral liberated states |
| Support a Remaining Subject | Valid subject with network ties | Raise autonomy pressure and prepare recognition | Equipment, civilian effort, relations risk, or network opportunity cost | Raises future Event 063 weight and creates an aid pledge. It does not release the subject | Used against weak or hostile overlords, limited by cooldown |

The category should rank targets and show only the most relevant few. It should not create one permanent decision for every liberated country.

## Phase 3: Independence war

This phase appears to the breakaway war leader, members of the breakaway cohort, and eligible supporters.

### Breakaway-side decisions

| Working label | Purpose | Cost | Result | AI use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Request Liberation Support | Ask relevant network states for aid | Diplomatic effort and a public obligation | Sends bounded requests using the support ladder | High early in a difficult war |
| Open a Supply Corridor | Establish land, sea, or air delivery path | Route-specific transport using trains or convoys, fuel, and temporary civilian factories. A valid access route is required | Improves delivery and front sustainment where geography permits | High when supply is the main constraint |
| Coordinate the Independence Front | Align cohort plans under the war leader | Command power, army experience, and temporary planning delay | Improves cooperation and reduces duplicate support requests | High for multi-country cohorts |
| Seek Mediation | Offer a settlement that preserves independence | War support, political concession, and time | Opens peace talks when the war is stalled or costly | High during stalemate, low during imminent victory |

### Supporter decisions

| Working label | Purpose | Cost | Result | AI use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fulfill the Aid Pledge | Deliver promised equipment and logistics | Real stockpile and transport | Completes a pledge and improves trust or cohesion | High when already promised and feasible |
| Send Volunteers | Commit forces without full entry | Divisions, equipment, and command resources. Valid access is required | Normal volunteer support under safe rules | Moderate for capable compatible states |
| Guarantee the Independence Front | Raise deterrence and prepare future entry | Diplomatic risk | Guarantee or equivalent defensive commitment | Used when access and strength are credible |
| Enter the Independence War | Join directly | Full war burden | Direct intervention, subject to theater cap and topology | Rare, favored at high cohesion and severe threat |
| Press for a Ceasefire | Use recognition and relations to end a stalemate | Diplomatic capital and concessions | Increases mediation chance | Used by states that cannot or will not intervene militarily |

Direct-entry decisions should disappear when the theater has reached its Event 063 intervener cap. Material aid remains available when another package would still matter.

## Phase 4: Liberation Pact

The Pact phase is described in Part 3. It should use the same category and replace informal network actions with charter versions where appropriate.

### Charter and membership actions

- call a founding congress
- invite one full member
- offer partner status
- admit or advance an observer
- resolve a membership objection
- transfer leadership when needed

### Defense and support actions

- coordinate defense of a threatened member
- pool a bounded equipment package
- open a supply corridor
- prepare volunteers
- issue a collective guarantee
- mediate a member dispute

### Independence-support actions

- recognize a new breakaway
- sponsor autonomy support in one valid subject
- prepare a conditional aid pledge
- pressure a former overlord to recognize independence

Only the strongest relevant targets should appear. The category should not list every subject and every liberated state simultaneously.

## Missions

## Mission 1: Secure Recognition

### Trigger

A released state remains unrecognized or contested after the opening settlement.

### Duration

About `180` days, adjusted by war state, existing guarantees, and former-overlord pressure.

### Success

- former overlord recognizes independence
- a sufficient group of relevant states recognizes the country
- a credible guarantee or Pact admission makes restoration pressure untenable

### Failure

The contested condition hardens. The former overlord can gain a stronger ultimatum route, and the released country receives a narrower emergency-defense set. Failure does not restore subject status automatically.

### Player tools

Recognition requests, mediation, trade or access settlement, guarantees, and network support.

## Mission 2: Keep the Separation Peaceful

### Trigger

A negotiated or contested separation has an active deadline and no war has begun.

### Duration

About `120` days.

### Success

The countries avoid war and either recognize the settlement or agree on a bounded unresolved status with no active restoration ultimatum.

### Failure

The dispute can escalate to an ultimatum, border incident, sanctions, or an independence war when Evolution II and topology permit.

The mission should not fail because either side is fighting an unrelated war.

## Mission 3: Prepare for Reconquest

### Trigger

A released state faces a restoration ultimatum or strong former-overlord pressure.

### Duration

About `90` days.

### Success

The country secures command, supply, allies, or a guarantee. It enters any later war with a prepared opening and a better chance of network aid.

### Failure

The state enters the crisis without those preparations. It does not lose independence without a war result.

## Mission 4: Hold the Independence Front

### Trigger

A compound independence war begins.

### Duration

Runs for the relevant phase of the war and checks concrete front conditions.

### Success

The breakaway side survives the opening period, keeps at least one supply path, and prevents immediate capitulation of the war leader.

### Failure

Support urgency rises, some external countries may withdraw, and the former overlord gains negotiation leverage. No free capitulation or annexation occurs from the mission alone.

## Mission 5: Draft the Pact Charter

### Trigger

Evolution III is active, a founder begins congress preparation, and the minimum invitation pool exists.

### Duration

About `120` days.

### Success

Enough full members accept, the Pact forms, and Liberation Cohesion is initialized from trust, threat, and fulfilled prior commitments.

### Failure

The network remains informal. The founder receives a congress cooldown and can rebuild support before another attempt.

## Mission concurrency

A country should normally have no more than:

- one settlement mission
- one active war or defense mission
- one Pact or network mission

An independence war replaces the peaceful-settlement mission. A successful recognition removes preparation missions that no longer apply. A Pact congress does not block an emergency defense mission when the founder is threatened.

## Idea and modifier lifecycle

## Contested Sovereignty

A temporary national spirit or dynamic modifier for a released state whose independence is disputed.

### Starting role

- raises diplomatic pressure
- affects recognition and guarantee behavior
- reflects unsettled access, command, and border arrangements
- can modestly affect mobilization or foreign trade where the dispute actually matters

### Resolution paths

- removed by recognition
- replaced by a short recognized-settlement condition after successful negotiation
- replaced by independence-war mobilization when war begins
- suspended if the country becomes a subject again
- removed if the country ceases to exist

The idea should not become a broad permanent combat bonus.

## Former Authority Disputed

A temporary former-overlord condition used only when several subjects leave or a contested separation damages domestic authority.

It can affect willingness to recognize, domestic stability pressure, and the cost of further coercion. Its strength should scale with the number and importance of releases. One microstate and six major subjects must not create the same penalty.

The condition fades after recognition, successful restoration, or a long settlement period. Repeated firings should update one staged condition. They must not stack many copies.

## Independence War Mobilization

A temporary wartime condition for breakaway participants.

It can improve mobilization speed, local defense preparation, or aid absorption while creating costs in production, stability, or civilian capacity. It ends after peace and should not become a permanent reward.

## Liberation Pact Charter

A staged Pact national spirit with four visual states aligned to Liberation Cohesion:

- Fractured
- Consultative
- Coordinated
- United

The spirit changes how efficiently the country can participate in Pact aid, guarantees, volunteers, and defense. It should support the decisions. Passive modifiers must not replace the playable actions.

The spirit is removed when a country leaves, is expelled, becomes a subject, or when the Pact dissolves.

## AI selection principles

### Candidate selection

- autonomy and overlord weakness should materially matter
- a high-autonomy subject under a weak wartime overlord should have at least about two and a half times the selection weight of a loyal integrated subject under a stable overlord in the same pool
- no valid candidate should have zero weight solely because it is loyal
- recency and owner-system conflicts can reduce or block weight
- Evolution I should increase same-overlord cohort selection without making the largest empire win every firing

### Settlement

- peaceful outcomes should dominate at low Chaos when relations are good
- contested outcomes should dominate when serious claims, low autonomy, and strategic value combine
- baseline immediate war should remain uncommon
- Evolution II should make war meaningful for viable refused cohorts without making it the default for every firing
- common-war topology must outrank aggression weight

### Support

- recognition and material aid should be more common than direct intervention
- countries should not donate critical equipment while their own army is severely short
- distant countries need transport and access
- direct intervention should require capacity, political compatibility, and manageable war load
- Pact cohesion should increase commitment, but it should not override impossibility

### Pact

- invitation value should come from trust, threat, and usefulness
- factioned states choose partner status
- a candidate with a major core conflict against a member should be rejected or held as an observer
- the founder should prioritize cohesion over member count
- AI should call a congress only when formation has a real chance to reach the minimum

## Probability audit requirements

The implementation must route all weighted surfaces through the AI probability auditor.

Required sequence:

1. inspect candidate-selection weights and every settlement `ai_chance` or weighted list
2. evaluate the named scenarios in `quality/063_subjects_break_free_probability_scenarios.md`
3. sweep autonomy, relative strength, overlord weakness, ideology distance, claim severity, network support, and current war load
4. compare implementation against the expected ordering and dominance limits
5. simulate only uncertain mixed pools declared in the scenario file
6. render the candidate ranking, settlement matrix, evolution timing, and intervention ladder when that improves review

Exact probabilities are not acceptance criteria when the candidate pool is campaign-dependent. Ordering, starvation limits, dominance limits, and timing bands are acceptance criteria.

## Exploit and abuse controls

### Re-release farming

A country cannot cycle through liberation and resubjugation rapidly to produce repeated event benefits. The target cooldown, actual status-transition check, and origin generation record block this.

### Equipment farming

Network and Pact aid transfers real equipment from donors. A recipient cannot repeatedly request the same package during one crisis. Package size is bounded by need, donor surplus, and cooldown.

### Cohesion farming

The same aid shipment, guarantee, war, or recognition cannot grant cohesion through several hooks. Repeated low-value aid to one target has diminishing value.

### Free-war avoidance

Restore-subject goals are temporary and target-specific. They do not become permanent general conquest tools.

### Claim farming

The event does not create broad claims. Temporary restoration claims are cleaned after recognition or peace.

### Faction theft

Pact invitations cannot pull a country out of another faction. Such a country receives partner or observer status.

### Decision clutter

Only relevant targets are loaded. Resolved, dead, allied, hostile, impossible, or cooldown targets disappear.

### Transaction duplication

Each selected country has one reservation, one release completion marker, one latest-origin write, and one report row per transaction generation.

## AI and decision acceptance

- every important actor follows a mapped strategy
- weighted selection passes the named ordering scenarios
- player choices cannot cancel independence
- AI recognizes common-war and access constraints
- decision costs use real resources where relevant
- no more than six decisions appear at once
- no more than three missions remain active
- temporary ideas have clear exits
- aid transfers real donor capacity
- the Pact has no free member theft, equipment creation, or cohesion farm
- invalid targets are cleaned without whole-world daily scans
