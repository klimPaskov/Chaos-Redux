# Event 044 AI strategy matrix

## United States and American successor host

| Situation | Preferred approach | Avoid | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High stability, strong intelligence, peace | Infiltration and targeted financial disruption | Mass arrest | The AI should seek a low-risk contained outcome |
| Depression, high unemployment, low stability | Material concessions plus intelligence work | Purely cosmetic reform | Relief must address the hidden grievance inputs |
| Democratic government with strong reform capacity | Negotiation, rival institutions, housing and employment measures | Indiscriminate repression | The legal movement outcome is acceptable |
| Authoritarian government with high coercive capacity | Surveillance followed by selective suppression | Immediate nationwide crackdown when influence is low | Repression becomes harsher as influence rises |
| Country at major war | Secure arsenals, rail hubs, and military loyalty | Expensive long reform programs that cannot finish | War losses can raise Influence |
| Civil war or severe fragmentation | Prevent territorial consolidation | Low-priority media campaigns | The AI may accept a negotiated compact if it cannot hold the region |
| Player-backed Yakubite route | Permit organization, protect institutions, negotiate territory | Actions that accidentally dismantle the chosen route | Player intent should remain usable |
| Yakub already dead or missing | Target institutions and succession networks | Founder-only actions | The crisis must continue through institutional leadership |

## Foreign host governments

| Government type or condition | Preferred approach |
| --- | --- |
| Colonial power with high resistance | Divide Yakubite organizers from local anti-colonial rivals, offer bounded reforms, and secure transport nodes |
| Stable democracy | Legal oversight, anti-discrimination reform, public debate, and targeted intelligence |
| Authoritarian regime | Suppression, censorship, chapter infiltration, and controlled concessions when repression backfires |
| Independent Black-led government | Assert local sovereignty, judge doctrine independently, and resist automatic absorption |
| Mainstream Muslim government | Reject theological claims, then choose tactical cooperation, neutrality, or opposition from geopolitical interests |
| Existing anti-colonial movement | Borrow resources selectively, preserve local leadership, and resist foreign command |
| Special Chaos or actual nonhuman country | Invalid target |

## Yakubite state route choice

| Conditions | Route tendency |
| --- | --- |
| Yakub alive, high personal loyalty, weak institutions | Personal Revelation route |
| Strong schools, businesses, municipal councils, and negotiated settlement | Federal New Nation route |
| Strong labor cells, war radicalization, and secular cadres | Black Republic route |
| High repression memory, hardline faction dominance, low foreign recognition | Original Supremacy route |
| Several foreign chapters, high diplomatic reach, and broad anti-colonial support | Diaspora Congress route |
| Yakub discredited | Avoid personalist and literalist routes |
| Yakub dead with no named successor | Council or convention route |
| Low equipment and active invasion | Prioritize defense and state survival before ideological expansion |

## International action AI

| International state | Preferred actions |
| --- | --- |
| Low Cohesion | Congress, mediation, relief aid, and member guarantees |
| High Cohesion, low military threat | Schools, media, recognition, and economic aid |
| High Cohesion, members at war | Equipment, volunteers, intelligence, and corridor support |
| Supremacist anchor with many pragmatic members | Coercion risks schism, so the AI weighs member loss against central control |
| Broad congress anchor | Coalition growth and autonomy guarantees |
| Few convoys or no route | Avoid overseas aid actions |
| High Condemnation | Reduce exposed violent action unless the route deliberately accepts pariah status |
| Terminal readiness near completion | Preserve required members and avoid wasteful side wars |

## AI refusal rules

AI should refuse actions with dead leaders, invalid countries, missing territories, blocked routes, insufficient equipment, impossible convoy access, incompatible ideology, disabled evolution, concluded movement, active terminal conflict, or stale selected targets.
