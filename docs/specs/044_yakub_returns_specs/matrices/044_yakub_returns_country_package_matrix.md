# Event 044 country package matrix

All country and route names below are working labels unless the user already supplied the name. The implementation must complete a full vanilla, Chaos Redux, Workshop, and sibling local-mod collision audit before reserving a tag. Existing releasable identities should be reused where they fit. Dynamic carrier tags should come from the established country registry.

## Territorial formation packages

### Great Lakes industrial compact

- Preferred opening package.
- Detroit is the first capital.
- The valid territory pool centers on Michigan and nearby contiguous industrial states where Event 44 institutions reached entrenched or dual-power status.
- The package emphasizes factories, railways, arsenals, urban defense, and Great Lakes transport.
- The state begins with stronger industry and a larger risk of immediate federal counterattack.
- Expansion into nearby states requires local chapter status or wartime control.

### Atlantic urban compact

- Available when the strongest institutions formed in the northeastern urban corridor.
- The capital is selected from Philadelphia, Newark, Baltimore, or another valid institutional center.
- The package emphasizes ports, press networks, dense cities, finance, and international contact.
- It begins with stronger diplomatic reach and weaker territorial depth.
- Naval inheritance remains conditional on actual port control and local assets.

### Southern congress

- Available when the movement built durable institutions across a contiguous southern region and federal authority collapsed or a negotiated settlement recognizes the congress.
- The capital is selected from Atlanta, Birmingham, New Orleans, or another valid center.
- The package emphasizes rural supply, local defense, churches and civic organizations as rivals or partners, and contested transport.
- The design must avoid treating a historical population map as automatic political control.
- State inclusion depends on event-built institutions, local authority, and actual control.

### Negotiated district

- A peaceful settlement can create a one-state or compact autonomous country.
- The state receives guarantees, demilitarized terms, reparations, trade access, or shared infrastructure according to the agreement.
- The state begins smaller and more stable.
- The focus tree emphasizes recognition, institution building, and gradual federation.
- The settlement must remain playable and must not produce a trapped enclave without supply.

## Starting force bands

| Formation scale | Starting land package |
| --- | --- |
| One-state negotiated district | Three to five militia or community-defense divisions, one trained guard if equipment exists |
| Small compact | Five to eight divisions, including one mobile or regular formation when local industry supports it |
| Regional breakaway | Eight to fourteen divisions, mixed militia and defectors, with artillery or motorized elements only when inherited |
| Large wartime secession | Dynamic share of valid federal units stationed in participating states plus bounded local militia |

Defection should depend on local Influence, stationed units, officer loyalty, route, federal stability, and whether the settlement is peaceful. It should never copy the whole American army.

## Reinforcement pathways

- Convert local defense groups into trained divisions.
- Absorb federal defectors through a loyalty and equipment mission.
- Recruit veterans and unemployed workers through route-sensitive decisions.
- Secure depots, rail hubs, and arsenals.
- Accept foreign volunteers through the International.
- Import equipment through convoys or land corridors.
- Build domestic arms production.
- Integrate member-state formations under federal or congress command.
- Demobilize excess militias after peace to reduce supply and authority strain.

## Navy and air package

A landlocked state receives no token navy. A state without aircraft receives no decorative air wing.

Port control can unlock a small inherited patrol force, transport capacity, and later naval decisions. Airbase control can unlock reconnaissance, transport, and fighter units when equipment exists. The military branches should support these forces only when the formation package makes them relevant.

## Regional foreign state packages

Evolution II can create foreign Yakubite or aligned states. The implementation should reuse existing releasables and national identities where possible.

Each foreign package receives:

- a local leader or institutional council
- local route names and political language
- one shared Yakubite state-building core
- one regional module
- local starting territory and capital
- dynamic units and equipment
- a local relationship with Yakub
- an independent choice to adopt, adapt, reject, or tactically use the doctrine
- local AI and diplomatic goals
- route-specific flags and portraits
- integration or federation terms with the International

A foreign state must retain local identity. It should never read as an American branch with a different flag.
