## Anti-farming rules

- Influence ticks never add or remove Chaos directly.
- Evolution eligibility and activation never change Chaos.
- State and regional milestones use permanent one-shot memory.
- Foreign spread uses a bounded approved region registry.
- A civil war, annexation, liberation, ideology change, death, famine, migration harm, nuclear strike, or faction action remains owned by its shared system.
- Repression adds event-owned Chaos only when a unique Event 44 consequence occurs, such as a one-time martyr crisis. Routine police activity gives no Chaos.
- Reversal rewards require a genuine prior escalation state.
- Terminal preparation cannot lower Chaos to avoid its own gate.
