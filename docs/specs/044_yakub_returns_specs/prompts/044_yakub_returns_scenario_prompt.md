## Launch contract

The scenario bypasses:

- normal event timer
- Event 44 Chaos level
- date conditions
- prior event history
- ordinary evolution thresholds
- normal movement buildup
- normal territorial and International readiness where the selected type needs to replace them

The scenario does not bypass:

- map validity
- required country or carrier availability
- tag collision rules
- active incompatible world-end state
- safe actor creation
- duplicate-launch protection

Use narrow scenario-only flags or variables and clear them after setup.

## Type 1: Movement

Movement creates a playable Event 44 campaign and never sets the shared world-end state during setup.

### Low

Create:

- Yakub in the United States
- Yakubite Influence from 35 to 45
- several chapters
- one or two institution families
- no foreign chapter requirement
- no territorial state

The United States must have time to contain, legalize, negotiate with, or support the movement.

### Medium

Create:

- Influence from 55 to 65
- several institutions
- one state near Parallel Authority
- foreign chapters in two regions
- Evolution I and II behavior available to the created state
- no International

The United States begins close to constitutional settlement or secession.

### High

Create:

- a valid American Yakubite state
- United States relations set to war, armed truce, or negotiated separation through a bounded setup branch
- several foreign movements
- at least one foreign movement capable of state formation
- Evolution I and II active
- International close to formation
- Yakub in a valid founder state

The state package, forces, economy, focus tree, flags, leaders, and decisions must be complete.

### Maximum

Create:

- strong American anchor state
- several foreign Yakubite or aligned states
- chapters in several regions
- formed Yakubite International
- International Cohesion from 55 to 75
- Evolution III active
- terminal readiness incomplete or normal world-end commitment still pending

Maximum remains a campaign with several possible outcomes. It must not force The Yakubite World.

## Type 2: World Order

World Order creates the valid terminal actor and begins the final conflict directly.

Setup must:

- create or select a viable anchor state
- create a functioning International
- assign a valid leader or successor
- select and store a route variant
- raise Chaos into World Collapse
- set the shared world-end state once
- activate The Yakubite World
- assign supporting, opposing, neutral, and contested states
- establish bounded terminal military capacity
- fire the terminal super-event through the normal presentation helper
- stop ordinary event pacing through the shared world-end contract

This type bypasses normal territorial, evolution, and geopolitical readiness because direct terminal launch is its purpose.

### Low

One strong anchor, small International, limited supporters, many neutral states, and a strong opposing coalition.

### Medium

Several members across two or three regions, functional military coordination, balanced neutral and opposing groups, and limited regional uprisings.

### High

Several strong members, supporters in multiple regions, integrated command, pre-positioned chapters, and immediate crises in several opposing states.

### Maximum

Large multi-region bloc, high Cohesion, several aligned states, widespread chapters, the largest safe bounded starting force package, and meaningful opponents that can still win.

No intensity may grant automatic victory, unlimited units, universal member support, or total global control.
