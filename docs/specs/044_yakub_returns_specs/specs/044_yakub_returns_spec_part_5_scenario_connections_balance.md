# Event 044 specification part 5: manual scenario, connections, achievements, AI, and balance

## Triggerable scenario

Scenario name: Yakubite Uprising

Proposed scenario ID: `SCN-015`

The current scenario export reaches `SCN-014`. The missing `SCN-004` identity should remain reserved. Implementation should confirm the authoritative workbook before assigning `SCN-015`.

The scenario launches Event 44 without the normal timer, event Chaos level, evolution thresholds, date, prior movement history, or ordinary source-event prerequisites.

It keeps impossible-state and active-world-end gates.

The scenario has two types:

- Movement
- World Order

The four shared intensity stops remain:

- Low
- Medium
- High
- Maximum

## Scenario type: Movement

Movement creates a playable Event 44 campaign.

It never sets the shared world-end state during setup.

It can pre-create later evolutions, countries, chapters, and the International according to intensity.

Normal Event 44 logic resumes after setup.

### Low intensity

Opening state:

- Yakub appears in the United States
- Yakubite Influence begins in the organized or lower entrenched range
- several American chapters exist
- one or two institutions are active
- the United States has time to respond
- no territorial state exists
- no foreign chapter is required

Suggested Influence band: 35 to 45

Playable question:

Can the movement be contained, legalized, supported, or driven toward statehood?

### Medium intensity

Opening state:

- American Influence begins in the upper entrenched range
- several institutions exist
- one state approaches Parallel Authority
- several foreign chapters appear in two regions
- Evolution I and II behavior is available for the created state
- the United States begins close to a constitutional or secession crisis
- no International exists yet

Suggested Influence band: 55 to 65

Playable question:

Can the United States prevent state formation while foreign governments manage their own chapters?

### High intensity

Opening state:

- a viable Yakubite state already exists in the United States
- the United States and new state begin in war, armed truce, or negotiated separation according to the selected setup branch
- several foreign movements exist
- at least one foreign movement can become a state
- Evolution I and II are active
- the International is close to formation
- Yakub's status is selected from a safe valid set

Suggested active-host Influence band before state formation: 75 to 85

Playable question:

Can the new state survive and build an International, or can opposing governments contain the network?

### Maximum intensity

Opening state:

- a strong American Yakubite state exists
- several foreign Yakubite or aligned states exist
- chapters operate in several regions
- the Yakubite International exists
- International Cohesion begins in a functional or integrated band
- Evolution III is active
- terminal readiness conditions are incomplete or the world remains below the automatic commitment state
- normal campaigning remains possible

Suggested Cohesion band: 55 to 75

Playable question:

Can the International build a viable world-order campaign, fracture, reform, or be defeated before terminal activation?

## Movement type setup choices

Where the scenario interface supports a bounded setup branch, the confirmation can choose or randomize:

- United States response history
- Yakub alive, detained, missing, or dead
- negotiated settlement or civil war origin
- political route tendency
- regional spread emphasis

The main type control remains Movement.

The scenario should not add a second complex setup window.

## Scenario type: World Order

World Order begins the terminal conflict directly.

It:

- creates a valid terminal actor
- creates or selects a viable Yakubite state
- creates a functioning International
- assigns leadership and succession
- raises Chaos into World Collapse
- activates The Yakubite World
- sets supporter, opponent, and neutral groups
- fires the terminal super-event
- freezes normal event pacing through the world-end contract

This type bypasses normal territorial, evolution, and geopolitical readiness.

It does not bypass basic map validity or an existing incompatible world end.

### Low intensity

- one strong anchor state
- a small International
- limited supporters
- many neutral countries
- strong opposing coalition
- terminal war begins from a difficult position

### Medium intensity

- several member states
- supporters in two or three regions
- a functional military system
- balanced neutral and opposing groups
- regional uprisings available

### High intensity

- several strong member states
- supporters in multiple regions
- integrated International command
- more pre-positioned chapters
- several opponent governments face immediate internal crises

### Maximum intensity

- large multi-region bloc
- strong Cohesion
- several aligned states and subjects
- widespread chapter networks
- immediate global revolutionary crisis
- the largest safe bounded terminal force package
- meaningful opponents remain

Maximum should still produce a campaign.

It should not hand the terminal actor an automatic victory.

## World Order route selection

The terminal setup uses the selected or weighted political variant:

- Personal Revelation
- Federal New Nation
- Black Republic
- Original Supremacy
- Diaspora Congress

When the scenario UI can support one additional cycle control cleanly, the type-specific route selector can be exposed.

When that would clutter the shared window, the setup chooses a route from the scenario's current type and intensity, with the selected route shown in confirmation.

The player should know which world order will begin.

## Scenario cleanup

Scenario-only bypass state clears after setup.

The setup must clear:

- normal-event prerequisite bypass
- evolution threshold bypass
- territorial-readiness bypass
- temporary host-selection flags
- temporary intensity variables that are no longer needed
- temporary type routing
- temporary forced-success state

Persistent campaign state remains.

Repeated launch is blocked unless the existing scenario framework explicitly supports a safe reset.

## Scenario history

The scenario records itself through the scenario system.

It should not fabricate a normal random-event firing date or pretend that every skipped evolution occurred naturally.

Created movement, state, evolution, International, and world-end state should remain visible through their real current surfaces.

## Cross-event connection: Great Depression 2.0

Great Depression 2.0 increases the hidden grievance pressure behind Yakubite Influence.

Relevant effects include:

- unemployment
- industrial closure
- housing pressure
- low stability
- municipal failure
- political radicalization

Event 44 relief and employment decisions become more valuable during the depression.

A successful recovery can lower recruitment pressure.

The connection should use the actual depression state and evolution level.

It should not create a duplicate depression modifier.

## Cross-event connection: Murder Mystery

Murder Mystery can target:

- Yakub
- a designated successor
- a movement financier
- a local organizer
- a federal investigator
- a hostile official
- an International secretary
- a rival movement leader
- a military commander

The murder changes Event 44 leadership, Influence, Cohesion, investigation, and succession.

A murdered Yakub can create a martyr crisis.

A murdered hardliner can reduce or strengthen the supremacist faction depending on evidence and public response.

The event should preserve Murder Mystery ownership of the investigation.

Event 44 supplies valid targets and consequence hooks.

## Cross-event connection: Lawrence of Arabia

Lawrence of Arabia can alter Event 44 politics in North Africa and the Middle East through:

- British influence
- protectorate networks
- anti-colonial politics
- intelligence competition
- military aid
- religious diplomacy

Yakubite movements in the region should remain rare and context-driven.

Mainstream Muslim rejection of Yakubite theology remains distinct from British political influence.

A local government can oppose Britain and Yakub at the same time.

## Cross-event connection: Malta Crusaders

Malta Crusaders can intensify religious and colonial conflict in the Mediterranean and North Africa.

Possible Event 44 effects include:

- refugee and migration pressure
- Christian and Muslim reaction events
- anti-colonial recruitment
- competition for local allies
- International aid
- rejection of racial theology
- tactical military cooperation

The connection should avoid turning the region into a simple Christianity versus Yakubism conflict.

## Cross-event connection: Africa Is One

Africa Is One is the strongest future connection.

The two systems can:

- cooperate in anti-colonial campaigns
- compete for continental leadership
- negotiate dual membership
- dispute sovereignty
- exchange volunteers
- coordinate liberation
- split over Yakubite doctrine
- form rival federations
- merge into a Congress terminal route under strict conditions

Africa Is One should retain its own identity, leadership, goals, and world-order logic.

Neither event automatically absorbs the other.

A merged route requires member consent, compatible politics, and a dedicated high-chaos agreement.

## Cross-event connection: Famine

Event 44 relief institutions can:

- distribute reserves
- open local relief
- protect routes
- request International aid
- support evacuation
- expose government neglect
- fail and lose public confidence

Famine remains the owner of food security, reserves, relief access, and deaths.

Event 44 changes political trust and movement institutions.

A Yakubite state uses the normal Famine system.

## Cross-event connection: Migration

Migration can move civilians from:

- civil war
- suppression
- supremacist policies
- colonial conflict
- famine
- terminal war

Movement institutions can receive, reject, organize, exploit, or protect displaced cohorts.

Migration remains the owner of movement, reception, settlement, and forced-displacement deaths.

A route that uses forced return or violent border policy receives the normal consequences.

## Cross-event connection: Camp and Repression

The supremacist route and hostile governments can interact with the shared Camp and Repression system.

Event 44 can provide:

- route context
- political actors
- exposed records
- reform choices
- resistance
- Condemnation consequences

The shared system owns sites, evidence, population harm, discovery, reform, and Condemnation.

Event 44 does not add a race selector.

## Cross-event connection: Condemnation

Condemnation can rise from:

- public atrocities
- camps
- cover-ups
- violent repression
- chemical, biological, or nuclear use
- terminal occupation policies

Ordinary doctrine and separatist politics do not create Condemnation by themselves.

The supremacist route can reach high Condemnation through its actual policies.

## Cross-event connection: Deaths

Deaths occur through actual war, repression, camps, famine, migration harm, bombing, weapons, and occupation.

Yakubite Influence never kills population directly.

The event does not convert ideological growth into a death tick.

## Cross-event connection: Secret Alliance and faction systems

A Yakubite International can discover, oppose, infiltrate, or cooperate with a secret coalition created by another event.

The shared faction system continues to own normal faction joining and leaving.

Event 44 adds International membership states and congress politics.

## Cross-event connection: liberation and decolonization

Future liberation and decolonization events should be able to read:

- local Event 44 Influence
- movement identity
- International membership
- foreign aid
- route
- state recognition
- supremacist opposition
- prior colonial repression

A liberated state can choose Yakubite alignment, local adaptation, tactical alliance, or rejection.

## Cross-event connection: economic events

Industrial Boom can strengthen movement businesses, state revenue, and institutional expansion.

A collapse into Great Depression 2.0 can reverse those gains and raise recruitment pressure.

Riches Found can create corruption, patronage, or foreign-aid competition inside a Yakubite state.

Connections should use existing event results and avoid duplicating their mechanics.

## Event cluster role

Event 44 remains outside a cluster.

Its normal firing already opens a multi-stage domestic crisis.

Its later spread and terminal route do not need cluster-wide simultaneous firing.

High

## Pacing

The normal event is Minor Fire-Once and begins as a domestic crisis.

Influence growth should give the player several meaningful response cycles before secession.

Suggested pacing:

- first phase: several months
- institution phase: several months to several years
- evolution MTTH: around the normal 90-day design anchor after prerequisites, adjusted by state
- foreign spread: bounded incidents rather than daily world scans
- International formation: requires real buildup
- terminal preparation: a deliberate late-game commitment

The event should remain capable of sudden escalation when war, depression, repression, assassination, or another event changes conditions.

## Decision clutter limits

Each active category phase should normally show:

- three to five primary actions
- one to three active missions
- one clear value
- one current stage
- one current leader status

Targeted foreign support uses a selected-target flow when many countries are valid.

Obsolete actions disappear.

The event uses action-appropriate costs.

## Exploit prevention

### Influence farming

- thresholds use one-shot memory
- repeated decisions have cooldowns
- the same state cannot be repeatedly secured for identical rewards
- government suppression cannot be alternated with release to farm Influence or Chaos

### Unit farming

- militia decisions consume equipment and manpower
- state and institution proofs limit recruitment
- defectors transfer from valid existing units or a bounded pool
- volunteer units depend on sender capacity
- repeated spawning has cooldowns and caps

### Recognition farming

- each country grants recognition once
- recognition can be withdrawn for real reasons
- repeated opinion cycles do not generate unlimited rewards

### International aid farming

- aid consumes real stockpiles or capacity
- the recipient cannot return the same grant for repeated benefit
- member contribution scales with actual economy
- transport access matters

### Federation abuse

- membership does not grant instant cores
- subjects and autonomous members remain distinct
- integration requires time and political proof
- leaving and rejoining cannot reset rewards

### Terminal uprising farming

- local chapter proof and stored equipment are required
- state cooldowns prevent repeated uprising stacks
- failed uprisings reduce local capability
- no chapter means no unit spawn

## Multiplayer

The crisis remains global in state and shared in its world consequences.

A player controlling the American host receives the main response category.

When a New Nation forms, the host player can switch.

Another player already controlling the new tag keeps control according to the shared country-switch rules.

Foreign player countries receive full local choices when Evolution II reaches them.

International decisions should avoid simultaneous conflicting clicks through clear owner and congress rules.

The terminal route uses one central actor and member decisions.

## DLC compatibility

The design should remain functional without optional DLC.

Intelligence actions use the strongest available intelligence surface.

When an advanced agency feature is unavailable, the event uses decision and variable equivalents.

Native embargo behavior can use the DLC feature where available.

The shared Condemnation fallback continues without it.

Focus, decision, country, event, and faction content must not disappear because an optional DLC is absent.

## AI balance goals

The AI should:

- understand the phase
- protect key states
- avoid premature arrest
- choose a coherent government approach
- select routes from actual history
- support viable foreign targets
- stop funding invalid targets
- use transport limits
- preserve Cohesion
- avoid terminal commitment from a hopeless position
- defend members under treaty
- accept peaceful settlements when war is unwinnable
- reject supremacist control when local politics oppose it

The probability matrix defines named audit scenarios.

## Acceptance balance cases

### Stable democratic containment

Expected result:

- Influence can be reduced through intelligence and material reform
- no war is required
- legal movement or quiet containment remains possible

### Repression spiral

Expected result:

- early indiscriminate suppression can produce underground growth and martyrdom
- the government still has a recovery path
- escalation is not automatic

### Player-supported statehood

Expected result:

- the player can build institutions and negotiate or force statehood
- the state is viable
- the United States remains playable
- the route does not require exploiting random outcomes

### Small New Nation survival

Expected result:

- a one-state compact can defend itself through guarantees, supply, diplomacy, and focused army growth
- it does not receive a major-power army for free

### Foreign doctrinal rejection

Expected result:

- a local movement can reject Yakub and remain relevant
- the International can accept an associate or lose the country
- local politics do not disappear

### Supremacist isolation

Expected result:

- the route gains real short-term power
- resistance, Condemnation, member exit, and sanctions create real pressure
- the route remains playable and dangerous
- it does not become the automatic strongest route

### International schism

Expected result:

- low Cohesion and forced doctrine can split the bloc
- both sides remain valid actors
- the campaign continues

### Terminal readiness failure

Expected result:

- 1000 Chaos and a living Yakub do not activate the world end without a state, International, and footprint

### Terminal maximum scenario

Expected result:

- Maximum World Order begins a major global campaign
- opponents remain capable of winning
- no side receives unlimited forces
