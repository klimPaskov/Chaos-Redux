# Event 044 specification part 4: Original Supremacy, The Yakubite World, and succession

## The Original Supremacy route

Working route name: Original Supremacy

This route openly embraces Black supremacy as state doctrine.

Its leaders claim that Black people are inherently superior, Yakub's creation narrative is literal history, and the old racial hierarchy must be inverted.

The route is intentionally authoritarian, extremist, and unstable.

Objective narration identifies these claims as ideology, propaganda, and doctrine.

The route never receives biological racial bonuses, race-specific technology, scientific validation, or a protected-class target mechanic.

## Entry conditions

The route becomes available through the founding convention when several of these conditions exist:

- the hardline faction holds substantial influence
- government repression created martyrs
- the movement built doctrinal schools and security groups
- Yakub remains credible to the hardliners
- compromise attempts failed
- foreign recognition is weak
- the state formed through violent secession
- civic institutions remain weak
- a public enemy or war strengthens siege politics

A player can force the route from a weaker base.

Doing so creates immediate opposition and a difficult consolidation phase.

Yakub's discredit makes the route harder and can require a hardline successor who reinterprets the doctrine.

## State identity

The supremacist government declares a racial hierarchy and a state mission to replace existing political power with an Originalist order.

The regime can be led by:

- Yakub
- a Designated Messenger
- an Originalist Directorate
- a military-religious council
- a hardline successor during terminal succession

The state may keep Yakub as symbolic founder when another body governs.

## Constitutional order

The route creates an exclusionary constitution.

Possible public policies include:

- restricted citizenship
- racial separation
- ideological education
- loyalty oaths
- party monopoly
- political exclusion
- controlled residence
- confiscation under state law
- suppression of rival movements
- security vetting
- unequal public office access

The implementation should use generic political categories and event history.

It should not create a player selector for real protected classes.

The route can target political opponents, hostile institutions, occupied territories, resistance, or event-defined factions.

## Supremacist state mechanic

The route does not add another permanent meter.

Its condition appears through qualitative regime stages:

- Hardline Coalition
- Originalist Constitution
- Consolidated Supremacy
- International Schism
- Pariah Mobilization
- Terminal Directorate

Stage changes depend on focuses, decisions, opposition, Condemnation, war, and International relations.

## Main decision families

### Consolidate the Directorate

Centralizes party, security, courts, and appointments.

Costs:

- political freedom
- local autonomy
- administrative capacity
- risk of faction purges

Benefits:

- stronger central control
- faster mobilization
- better suppression of coups

### Enforce ideological education

Transforms schools into instruments of doctrine.

Benefits:

- short-term loyalty
- stronger hardline recruitment

Costs:

- civic opposition
- intellectual flight
- foreign backlash
- long-term institutional rigidity

### Loyalty review

Screens officers and officials.

Benefits:

- reduced coup risk

Costs:

- command disruption
- experienced personnel loss
- factional manipulation

### Restrict hostile organizations

Targets event-defined political and institutional opponents.

Benefits:

- less open opposition

Costs:

- underground resistance
- Condemnation when repression is exposed
- International member distrust

### Mobilize the Original Nation

Creates a temporary war and production surge.

Costs:

- consumer pressure
- workforce strain
- equipment wear
- severe aftermath
- resistance growth

The surge must have a clear duration and post-window penalty.

### Purge the International

Demands that members accept the supremacist constitution or leave.

Benefits:

- higher central obedience among remaining members

Costs:

- member exits
- rival coalition
- Cohesion loss
- possible war

### Offer subordinate autonomy

Allows selected members to remain under unequal terms.

This can preserve a larger bloc and generates resentment, resistance, and later revolt.

## Opposition inside the supremacist state

The route creates active opposition.

### Civic separatists

They support a Black-led state and reject racial hierarchy.

They can organize constitutional resistance, local governments, exile networks, and negotiated reform.

### Religious dissenters

They accept a religious state or Yakub's symbolic role and reject the supremacist interpretation.

They can form a Council of Temples or support a new messenger.

### Secular revolutionaries

They oppose the regime through labor, military, and republican networks.

### Internationalists

They fear that supremacy destroys the anti-colonial coalition.

They can move the International Secretariat abroad.

### Mainstream Muslim critics

They reject the theology and can support religious dissent.

### Military opposition

Officers can resist purges, failed wars, or political interference.

Opposition uses events, resistance, coups, exile states, and civil war where justified.

It should not become a passive stability modifier.

## Atrocities and repression

The route can commit serious abuses.

The design should route them through existing systems:

- Camp and Repression
- Deaths
- Condemnation
- Migration
- Famine
- occupation
- resistance
- evidence and discovery

Event 44 does not duplicate those systems.

The route never gains a race-specific atrocity button.

Actions with mass harm must carry severe costs, evidence risk, resistance, diplomatic consequences, and population loss.

The route can refuse atrocities and remain an extremist authoritarian state.

## Mechanical strength

The route's power comes from:

- centralized command
- rapid mobilization
- disciplined security
- ideological officer control
- temporary production surges
- willingness to accept diplomatic isolation
- forceful International leadership

The route's strength should feel immediate.

It should not rely on tiny modifiers.

## Mechanical weakness

The route suffers from:

- resistance
- population flight
- economic isolation
- scientific and professional loss
- International schism
- diplomatic sanctions
- Condemnation
- officer purges
- succession risk
- poor member autonomy
- internal revolt
- dependence on coercion

A long war can turn early mobilization into economic exhaustion.

## Foreign reactions

Foreign governments and movements distinguish the regime from other Event 44 routes.

Likely reactions include:

- sanctions
- refusal of recognition
- asylum for dissidents
- support for rival Black-led states
- doctrinal condemnation
- defensive alliances
- tactical cooperation against a common colonial enemy
- covert destabilization
- member withdrawal
- armed conflict

Black-led governments and anti-colonial movements do not support the regime automatically.

## Supremacist International

A supremacist anchor can control the Yakubite International only when enough members accept, submit, or remain dependent.

The bloc can use:

- loyalty congresses
- subordinate member status
- military missions
- political officers
- controlled aid
- sanctions against defectors
- forced leadership changes
- propaganda
- local hardline factions

These actions raise control and reduce broad Cohesion.

A large supremacist bloc can reach the terminal route through conquest and subordination.

It faces the highest risk of a rival International.

## The Yakubite World

The user-supplied terminal name is final: The Yakubite World

The terminal route begins only at 1000 or more Chaos.

It requires a real geopolitical system.

Yakub's existence alone is insufficient.

## Public world-end registration

The Yakubite World is one public Event 44 world-end branch.

It receives:

- its own Event Details row
- a persistent enable or disable toggle
- route-sensitive status
- premise and terminal-state details
- a dedicated super-event
- a unique audio package
- one stable registry identity

The row remains one public branch because the route variants share one terminal concept.

The active route changes the terminal government, supporters, military tools, and final order.

## Readiness requirements

Normal automatic readiness requires:

- 1000 or more Chaos
- Event 44 active or completed into a surviving movement
- Evolution III active
- one viable Yakubite or aligned anchor state
- a functioning Yakubite International or equivalent federation
- recognized leadership
- a substantial geopolitical footprint
- no other active world end
- the Event 44 terminal branch enabled

A substantial footprint can be proven through several paths.

### Multi-state international path

- several Yakubite or aligned states
- members in at least three world regions
- one strong anchor
- high or stable Cohesion
- military and economic capacity

### Federal domination path

- one large federation controlling several connected regions
- allied movements or states in other regions
- a working International Secretariat

### Revolutionary wave path

- several colonial or discriminatory governments replaced
- independent local governments join a common command
- the International controls meaningful armed and political capacity

### Conquest path

- a militarized Yakubite state subordinates several governments
- regional commands exist
- the International functions as an imperial system

### Congress path

- a broad anti-colonial coalition places Event 44 institutions at its center
- member states accept a terminal campaign
- the anchor has enough military and diplomatic power to enforce collective decisions

The implementation should centralize the final threshold values.

## Terminal commitment

Readiness does not activate the world end instantly.

The anchor receives a final commitment path.

The commitment requires:

- leadership decision
- member congress or coercive declaration
- war council preparation
- supporter classification
- opponent classification
- succession check
- terminal asset and super-event preparation

A player can delay or refuse the route.

An AI chooses it only when its route, strength, and strategic situation support global conflict.

## Terminal super-event role

The super-event marks the transformation of the Yakubite International into a world-order project.

The image should show a global congress, aligned armies, movement banners, communications, and the founder or successor as a political center.

The scene should avoid racial caricature, pseudo-scientific diagrams, and a simple map.

The text should identify the route's declared world order, the International's transformation, and the division of states into supporters and opponents.

The quote, cultural remark, title treatment, and music require dedicated research.

## Terminal actor

The terminal actor can be:

- the existing anchor state
- a route-specific federal transformation
- an International central authority
- a designated terminal carrier created from the anchor

The transformation must preserve armies, members, leadership, technology, wars, and route history.

It should not create a disconnected empty tag.

## Supporting states

Support is based on political history.

Factors include:

- International membership
- local Yakubite Influence
- prior aid
- anti-colonial alignment
- route compatibility
- government ideology
- colonial status
- relations with the anchor
- coercion
- subject status
- fear of rivals
- public Condemnation
- member autonomy guarantees
- previous opposition to supremacy

Support forms include:

- full union
- federation member
- faction ally
- subject
- autonomous member
- military partner
- neutral associate

No government joins because of a racial population rule.

## Opposing states

Opponents include governments that:

- reject the terminal order
- fear subversion
- oppose the route's ideology
- retain colonial systems targeted by the bloc
- support rival Black-led or anti-colonial movements
- oppose supremacist rule
- reject foreign domination
- lead another world-order system

Opposition can begin through sanctions, intelligence conflict, uprisings, regional wars, or direct world war.

## Neutral and contested states

Some governments remain neutral at the start.

They can receive:

- competing aid
- political pressure
- local movement events
- recognition demands
- coup attempts
- refugee pressure
- border incidents
- guarantees
- ultimata
- mediation

Neutrality gives the terminal campaign strategic depth.

## Terminal route variants

### Supremacist world order

The Original Supremacy route reorganizes the bloc into an authoritarian racial hierarchy.

Main features:

- strong central command
- coerced members
- rapid mobilization
- loyalty governments
- high resistance
- intense Condemnation
- frequent revolt
- pariah diplomacy
- severe postwar instability

The final objective is global submission to the Originalist order.

The game frames the regime as oppressive extremist rule.

### Federal separatist world order

The Federal New Nation route creates a federation of Black-led states, autonomous regions, and aligned governments.

Main features:

- member consent
- integration missions
- regional autonomy
- common defense
- negotiated accessions
- slower military centralization
- stronger stability
- disputes over borders and minority protection

The final objective is a federal international order centered on self-government and separation.

### Revolutionary anti-colonial world order

The Black Republic route leads a revolutionary campaign against colonial governments and hostile political systems.

Main features:

- uprisings
- volunteers
- revolutionary governments
- land and labor politics
- ideological diversity
- strong military aid
- factional competition
- postwar constitutional struggles

The final objective is a network of revolutionary Black-led and anti-colonial governments.

### Congress world order

The Diaspora Congress route builds the broadest coalition.

Main features:

- member autonomy
- negotiated entry
- congress votes
- relief and reconstruction
- shared defense
- tactical alliances
- weak direct command
- risk of member defection

The final objective is a global anti-colonial congress order where Yakub can remain a symbol without universal theological adoption.

### Religious commonwealth world order

The Personal Revelation route creates a doctrinal commonwealth.

Main features:

- Temple administration
- founder or messenger leadership
- ideological standards
- strong religious cohesion
- narrower membership
- succession pressure
- conflict with mainstream Muslim governments and civic movements

The final objective is a global commonwealth under Yakubite religious authority.

## Terminal military system

The terminal system gives the central bloc powerful coordination.

Tools include:

- shared mobilization
- equipment contributions
- volunteer coordination
- intelligence sharing
- local uprising support
- regional commands
- transport and convoy support
- member defense guarantees
- strategic planning
- emergency production
- resistance support in hostile states

Power comes from existing members and institutions.

The terminal system should not create unlimited armies without equipment, manpower, transport, and local support.

## Revolutionary units

Local uprising units can form when a hostile country has:

- a durable chapter
- stored equipment
- local institutions
- sufficient Influence
- International support
- a valid state target

The unit package scales with local conditions.

A state with no chapter cannot spawn a Yakubite army.

Repeated spawning uses cooldowns and stockpile limits.

## Terminal politics

The terminal actor manages:

- member loyalty
- regional war plans
- succession
- ideological conflict
- recognition
- neutral states
- occupation and integration
- reconstruction
- atrocities and Condemnation
- rival coalitions
- internal reform

The terminal campaign remains politically divided.

## World-end victory

Victory requires more than taking one capital.

A route reaches victory when:

- all principal opposing major powers capitulate or accept the order
- no rival global coalition can continue the conflict
- the terminal actor retains a valid government
- member collapse has not destroyed the International
- the route completes its final political settlement

The settlement differs by route.

The supremacist variant imposes hierarchy.

The federal variant negotiates or enforces federation.

The revolutionary variant establishes aligned governments.

The Congress variant forms a global congress system.

The religious variant establishes a doctrinal commonwealth.

## World-end defeat

The terminal order can fail through:

- anchor capitulation
- International collapse
- member revolt
- succession crisis
- defeat of regional commands
- economic exhaustion
- mass defection
- rival coalition victory
- internal overthrow

Defeat produces a full aftermath.

Possible outcomes:

- dissolution treaty
- member independence
- trials
- exile
- amnesty
- de-radicalization
- retained local states
- restoration of previous governments
- federal reform
- rival anti-colonial congress
- continued underground movement

A global defeat receives a dedicated defeat aftermath super-event.

A small pre-terminal defeat uses normal events.

## Terminal succession

The world-end route remains possible when Yakub dies or disappears.

Succession must be resolved before terminal commitment.

## Succession principles

- prior appointments matter
- institutions matter
- route matters
- military control matters
- International recognition matters
- Yakub's public status matters
- a dead founder cannot return through ordinary succession
- a missing founder can create uncertainty
- a discredited founder weakens literalist heirs
- no real historical person is automatically assigned extremist leadership

## Succession outcomes

### Yakub remains supreme leader

Available when alive, free, and politically active.

He leads directly or serves above the government.

### Designated Messenger

Available when Yakub appointed a successor.

The Messenger inherits personalist and religious authority.

The succession is stronger when institutions and International members recognized the appointment.

### Council of Temples

Religious institutions replace personal rule.

The council can preserve doctrine and reduce one-person fragility.

It can split over literalism, supremacy, and foreign membership.

### Federal Council

The constitutional route transfers authority through law.

Yakub becomes founder or symbolic figure.

The council has strong recognition and weaker personal mobilization.

### Revolutionary Committee

The secular route rejects personal succession.

Leadership passes through party, labor, military, or congress institutions.

### International Secretariat

Foreign members elevate the transnational institution over the American state.

This outcome supports the Congress route and can move the anchor abroad.

### Defense Council

War or state collapse gives the military emergency authority.

The council can restore civilian rule, become a dictatorship, or support a hardline successor.

### Originalist Directorate

The supremacist faction seizes succession.

It treats doctrine as more important than the founder's person.

It can continue after Yakub's discredit.

### Contested succession

No claimant has enough support.

The movement splits into rival states, councils, or Internationals.

The world-end route remains blocked until one side wins or a settlement restores leadership.

## Martyr succession

A murdered Yakub creates a temporary surge.

The surge can help a designated successor.

It also raises the risk of rival claims, purges, and uncontrolled violence.

The martyr effect expires and cannot become a permanent free bonus.

## Missing-founder succession

A missing Yakub creates competing interpretation.

The government can:

- declare him alive
- declare a regency
- appoint a successor
- form a council
- wait
- reject personal rule

A rare unexplained return can overturn the settlement.

The return creates a political crisis and does not automatically restore leadership.

## Discredited-founder succession

A discredited Yakub cannot easily transfer literal authority.

Civic, republican, and Congress routes gain strength.

Hardliners can claim betrayal, reinterpretation, or a corrupted public record.

The personal route may survive only through a strong institutional base.

## Succession and International Cohesion

A clear succession can preserve Cohesion.

An imposed successor, disputed appointment, assassination, or doctrinal reversal lowers Cohesion.

Members can recognize different claimants.

A succession war can create rival Internationals.

## Route restoration and reform

The player can reform a failing route.

Examples:

- Personal Revelation to Council of Temples
- Original Supremacy to Federal Council after overthrow
- Black Republic to Congress coalition
- Congress to emergency Defense Council during invasion
- Federal New Nation to stronger central federation
- Supremacist International to independent member coalition

Route change should require a crisis, convention, coup, or settlement.

It should not be a cheap policy switch.

## Post-terminal stability

Winning the world-end conflict does not remove internal politics.

The final order receives a short consolidation phase.

The phase addresses:

- demobilization
- member sovereignty
- occupation
- reconstruction
- refugees
- food supply
- former opponents
- succession
- constitutional settlement
- remaining resistance
- route promises

The campaign can end after the final settlement or continue in a stabilized terminal state.
