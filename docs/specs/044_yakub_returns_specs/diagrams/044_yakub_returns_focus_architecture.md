Industry, military, diplomacy, intelligence, and integration branches run beside the political lanes and connect through a small number of deliberate convergence focuses. They should remain usable across several political routes.

## Opening group

### Founding Emergency

Purpose:

- establish the crisis country
- secure a capital and supply
- identify the founding settlement or war origin
- open the three starting ideas
- reveal the first decision families
- keep the player from facing five ideological routes on the first day

The opening should use a small set of short focuses. Each focus must create immediate action, such as state security, relief, a decision unlock, or a political convention.

### Secure the Capital

- starts capital defense and local command
- opens depot and rail objectives
- interacts with the actual capital chosen by the country package
- helps a small negotiated district survive without giving free nationwide forts

### Feed the Cities

- converts movement relief networks into emergency supply
- opens food, transport, and municipal service decisions
- links to Famine and Migration if those systems are active
- begins the Parallel Institutions lifecycle

### Define Provisional Rule

- sets the first provisional authority
- determines whether Yakub rules directly, delegates, remains symbolic, or stays detained
- prepares the state-building convention
- changes route availability when Yakub is dead, missing, or discredited

## Political route family

### Personal Revelation route

Narrative role:

Yakub or a designated messenger turns the movement into a centralized religious state.

Mechanical role:

- rapid central authority
- strong mobilization and doctrinal unity
- public ceremonies and Temple administration
- succession pressure
- limited coalition breadth
- access to a strict but non-supremacist religious route

Route groups:

1. Establish the founder's authority.
2. Convert chapters into Temples and administrative districts.
3. Define religious courts and civil law boundaries.
4. Create a trained national guard.
5. Appoint or refuse a successor.
6. Decide whether the International demands doctrinal conformity.
7. Prepare the terminal order through a religious commonwealth.

Tradeoff:

Fast cohesion comes with diplomatic suspicion, weak succession, and resistance from civic and secular members.

Failure state:

Yakub's disappearance or discredit can trigger a council struggle, regional secession, or a transition into the Council of Temples.

### Original Supremacy route

Narrative role:

Hardliners turn Yakub's racial doctrine into an explicit Black-supremacist state ideology.

Mechanical role:

- authoritarian centralization
- loyalty tests and ideological education
- exclusionary citizenship
- coercive mobilization
- strong short-term command
- severe resistance, Condemnation, diplomatic isolation, and member schism

Route groups:

1. Declare the literal racial doctrine as state truth.
2. Purge civic and theological dissent.
3. Centralize schools, media, and security.
4. Create an Originalist officer corps.
5. Impose the first exclusionary constitutional order.
6. Force International members to choose compliance, autonomy, or departure.
7. Prepare the supremacist terminal variant.

Tradeoff:

The route gains coercive power and loses coalition breadth, legitimacy, population stability, and foreign access.

Failure state:

Resistance, defections, economic isolation, and International schism can overthrow the Directorate or split the state.

The route cannot create race-specific combat modifiers or scientific bonuses. Its racial claims remain propaganda and doctrine.

### Federal New Nation route

Narrative role:

Local institutions become a constitutional federation of Black-led states and municipalities.

Mechanical role:

- autonomy and negotiated integration
- high stability
- gradual core acquisition
- member-state councils
- recognition and treaties
- strong postwar settlement
- slower central mobilization

Route groups:

1. Convene a constitutional congress.
2. Protect local councils and municipal autonomy.
3. Convert parallel institutions into federal services.
4. Establish shared revenue and rail authority.
5. Negotiate recognition with the United States and foreign governments.
6. Admit new territories through support and integration missions.
7. Build a federation-centered International.

Tradeoff:

The state gains legitimacy and gives local governments vetoes, slower war mobilization, and limits on direct central rule.

Failure state:

Low federal confidence can create member exits, fiscal paralysis, or a return to competing authorities.

### Black Republic route

Narrative role:

Secular revolutionaries detach the state from literal Yakubite theology and build a militant Black republic.

Mechanical role:

- labor and military mobilization
- republican legitimacy
- anti-colonial alliances
- political education and land or workplace reform
- strong volunteer routes
- internal conflict with religious hardliners

Route groups:

1. Assert civil government over revelation.
2. Form labor, veterans, and municipal councils.
3. Build a republican constitution.
4. Nationalize or regulate strategic industry according to the chosen economic path.
5. Create a people's defense force.
6. Support anti-colonial revolutions without requiring religious adoption.
7. Prepare a revolutionary International.

Tradeoff:

The route gains broad secular allies and risks factional conflict, economic disruption, and a break with Yakub loyalists.

Failure state:

An army faction, hardline religious faction, or foreign patron can seize the republic if authority collapses.

### Diaspora Congress route

Narrative role:

The movement becomes a broad international congress where Yakub serves as founder, symbol, disputed prophet, or retired ideological source.

Mechanical role:

- maximum coalition breadth
- high diplomatic reach
- member autonomy
- cross-regional aid
- cultural and political pluralism
- weaker direct command
- frequent congress disputes

Route groups:

1. Invite independent movements and states.
2. Guarantee the right to reject literal doctrine.
3. Create a permanent secretariat.
4. Establish relief, education, press, intelligence, and volunteer offices.
5. Balance member sovereignty with shared strategy.
6. Form a congress-based International.
7. Prepare a coalition terminal variant.

Tradeoff:

Broad membership reduces central authority and can dilute the American movement's original doctrine.

Failure state:

A failed congress can split into regional blocs, leave the anchor isolated, or empower the supremacist faction.

## Community economy and institutions

This branch changes the map and state capacity.

Major focus groups:

- integrate schools and public education
- formalize food distribution and public health
- convert businesses into cooperatives, regulated private firms, or state enterprises
- restore railways and urban supply
- secure housing and local construction
- build a revenue system
- create regional development decisions
- settle refugee and migration pressure
- support regional member states through investment

Economic choices:

1. Cooperative commonwealth.
2. State-directed industrialization.
3. Regulated movement enterprise.

The choices may converge on shared infrastructure and supply. They should not produce identical modifiers.

## National defense

Major focus groups:

- regularize community defense
- screen and integrate federal defectors
- create officer training
- secure arsenals and depots
- improve urban and industrial defense
- build mobile reserve forces
- organize foreign volunteers
- create International joint command
- demobilize or professionalize after victory

Route effects:

- Personal Revelation creates a Temple Guard.
- Federal New Nation creates a federal guard with state contingents.
- Black Republic creates a people's defense force.
- Original Supremacy creates an Originalist army with loyalty costs.
- Diaspora Congress creates a volunteer command and member coordination system.

No custom unit family is required. The military identity comes from templates, training, command, equipment access, and route mechanics.

## Diplomacy and recognition

Major focus groups:

- negotiate the founding settlement
- seek recognition from neighboring states
- define relations with the United States
- approach Ethiopia and Liberia
- open Caribbean, Brazilian, British, French, West African, and South African networks
- create recognition missions
- balance sponsors
- reject dependency
- form the International
- define member accession and exit

Diplomatic routes should distinguish recognition, tactical cooperation, alliance, subject status, federation, and ideological adoption.

## Intelligence and internal security

Major focus groups:

- preserve the chapter network
- detect federal infiltration
- screen defectors
- protect foreign organizers
- build counterintelligence
- expose hostile covert action
- choose legal oversight or political police
- coordinate International intelligence

Route consequences:

- Federal and Congress routes use legal oversight and member consent.
- Personalist and Supremacist routes can centralize security at a legitimacy and Condemnation cost.
- Revolutionary route uses party and labor networks with a factional capture risk.

## Territorial integration and formables

The tree should not hand out automatic cores across the United States or the world.

Integration requires:

- control of the target state
- prior chapter or local institution proof
- a settlement, referendum, constitutional accession, compliance, or postwar integration mission
- supply connection or a valid autonomous member arrangement
- time and material cost
- route compatibility

Working formation stages:

1. Founding compact.
2. North American New Nation federation.
3. Multi-state Diaspora Federation.
4. The Yakubite World terminal reorganization.

A route may stop at any stable stage.

## International and terminal preparation

This branch stays hidden until the corresponding evolution and world state exist.

Major focus groups:

- establish the International Secretariat
- define member autonomy
- create shared aid offices
- coordinate volunteers and intelligence
- hold ideological congresses
- resolve leadership succession
- establish regional commands
- meet terminal readiness
- choose the route-dependent world-order program

The terminal focus cannot bypass the 1000 Chaos gate, Event 44 world-end toggle, valid state, functioning International, or geopolitical footprint.

## Focus filters and navigation

Suggested filter families:

- Event 44 opening and survival
- political and government
- economy and institutions
- army and defense
- diplomacy and International
- intelligence and security
- territorial integration
- high-chaos and terminal

Focus Navigation should provide entries for the political convention, economy, defense, diplomacy, and International regions when the final tree is large enough to require them.

Hidden supremacist and terminal content should stay unavailable in navigation until revealed.

## Layout direction

- Center the opening and state-building convention.
- Place the three first political families in clear lanes.
- Split the religious lane into Personal Revelation and Original Supremacy.
- Split the civic lane into Federal New Nation and Black Republic.
- Keep the Congress lane separate and later reconnect it to diplomacy and International preparation.
- Place economy and defense as parallel support branches below or beside the opening.
- Use a small number of route-specific convergence focuses.
- Avoid long connector lines, crossed lines, decorative ladders, and mixed-purpose islands.
- Reserve terminal content at the far end of the International branch.
