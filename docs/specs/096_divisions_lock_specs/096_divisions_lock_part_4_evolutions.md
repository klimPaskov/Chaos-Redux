# Event 096: evolutions and inspection waves

Proposed design: [README](README.md#authority-and-current-workflow) records current authority, owner workflows and historical receipt limits.

## 1. Shared rules

The four supplied evolution names and Chaos requirements are retained.
They modify one coherent system.
Every regime uses the same structural force, live capacity, optional decisions, release predicate, lock ownership, and safe transaction.

Chaos thresholds make an active evolution eligible.
They are not deadlines or automatic active-event upgrades.
A pre-fire evolved opening and an evolution during an ongoing incident are separate entry paths.

Ordinary lifecycle transitions, including the end of the minimum week, becoming oversized, demobilizing, unlocking, and passing an inspection, are not evolution stages.

| Regime | Chaos eligibility | Allowance multiplier | Reviews granted by one firing | Days between reviews | Relock when |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Base | Base event eligibility | 1.00 | 0 | none | A new genuine firing is needed |
| I: Stricter Limits | 200+ | 0.85 | 0 | none | Same as base |
| II: Military Reinspection | 400+ | 0.80 | 1 | 30 | F greater than 1.25A |
| III: Rolling Restrictions | 600+ | 0.70 | 3 | 20 | F greater than 1.15A |
| IV: Global Demobilization | 800+ | 0.55 | 5 | 10 | F greater than 1.05A |

Each stage tightens three things the player can read at a glance: a smaller allowance, more reviews, and a narrower relock margin.
The review count and interval are fixed per regime so the next-review time is predictable; severity comes from the country's own gap, not from a hidden peak-excess formula.

These are bounded inspection budgets, not fixed lock durations.
A country may remain in its first lock much longer than the entire review program because its force remains above allowance.
No review deletes a unit automatically.

## 2. Both entry paths

### Pre-fire evolved opening

At a genuine firing, determine the highest enabled regime whose threshold is satisfied by current Chaos.
Enter that regime directly.
Historical activation from an already closed grant does not bypass a current threshold.
An 800+ opening therefore applies the Evolution IV allowance and future-review contract from the start.
It does not wait through four long upgrade timers before delivering its high-Chaos version.

If a prior grant is still active, preserve any stricter already activated and still-enabled regime when the repeat coalesces that obligation.
The repeat uses the higher of that retained regime and the current-Chaos opening regime.
This is continuity of an existing active restriction, not permanent cross-incident evolution memory.
The shared registry may retain historical records without changing this mechanical selection rule.

The opening still affects every current country, starts a seven-day floor, and preserves the fielded army.
Deliver one coherent opening report describing the current regime.
Register inherited lower-stage progression according to the shared evolution registry, without multiplying the allowance reductions or producing four redundant country popups.

### Active-event evolution

While at least one valid participant is locked or in an evolved watch, an enabled next stage can become eligible at its Chaos threshold.
Use the project's active evolution MTTH route with a 90-day tuning anchor.
The final installed-version adapter must establish the actual timing distribution.
Do not present ninety days as a guaranteed wait or a measured native mean.

Advance at most one new active stage in a single evolution completion.
A sudden Chaos jump can make later stages eligible but does not execute an unreviewed cascade in one callback.
The direct pre-fire opening already supplies the high-tier immediate route.

Initial dynamic MTTH factors are:

| Condition | Factor on the 90-day anchor |
| --- | ---: |
| At least one major country is currently in an oversized hold | 0.75 |
| At least one active participant currently has e at least 1 | 0.75 |
| Current Chaos is at least 200 above the next stage's threshold | 0.80 |
| No participant remains oversized, but a valid evolved watch remains | 1.50 |

Combine applicable factors and bound the final factor between 0.25 and 2.
These are candidate MTTH factors for native audit.
The current held or oversized status comes from participant refreshes.

If the threshold ceases to be satisfied, stop eligibility for an unactivated stage.
Activated regimes do not regress merely because Chaos drops.
An explicit settings change can disable their effects under Section 7.

Activation tightens the allowance for currently held participants on their next guarded immediate or local refresh.
It invalidates requests based on the old regime.
A previously released cohort participant is not instantly relocked by the activation callback.
A newly added reinspection capability first opens a genuine watch window with a fresh baseline and delay.

If every participant has finished and the director has closed, an old queued evolution cannot resurrect the incident.
A later firing can use a new evolved opening.

## 3. Evolution I: Stricter Limits

The first evolution reduces the generosity of the capacity calculation to 85 percent.
The floor remains seven days.
A country that was near compliance can now have a larger gap to close, while a powerful country still receives a much larger allowance than a minor.

For an active country, refresh the allowance immediately through the existing country route.
If its minimum week has ended and it is still compliant, release it.
Do not impose a new week solely because the coefficient changed.
If it is oversized, retain the current hold and show the changed allowance.

For a pre-fire opening, apply the stricter allowance from the first comparison.
This evolution has no post-release watch.
Its harsher duration follows the larger live capacity gap.
It does not add an arbitrary extension timer.

Narrative direction: a revision of inspection standards that makes previously tolerated establishments harder to justify.
The evolution log should communicate the standards change.
Its activation or log has zero direct Chaos effect.

## 4. Evolution II: Military Reinspection

The second evolution reduces the multiplier to 80 percent and grants one review 30 days after release.
It targets a country that rebuilds far beyond its capacity as soon as its templates are free.

At the review, refresh F and A and relock only if F is greater than 1.25A.
The wide margin means a country slightly above allowance, or one that lost a little capacity, keeps its freedom.
A watch opened by activating this capability after a country had already finished its initial hold starts on the activation day.

The distinction is intentional.
A currently held country can release only at F at most A.
An already released country has a separate, higher reacquisition threshold.
The latter is not permission to release a held country at 1.25A.

Whether it passes or fails, the review consumes its single credit.
A failure starts a new seven-day hold, then releases through the ordinary compliance predicate.
The same credit cannot schedule another review after that release.

Narrative direction: provisional restoration of design access followed by a check for rapid rebuilding.
The player should understand the difference between being unlocked now and the watch still being active.

## 5. Evolution III: Rolling Restrictions

The third evolution reduces the multiplier to 70 percent and permits three reviews, 20 days apart, for the current firing.

Relock when F is greater than 1.15A, whether the deterioration came from new formations or from lost land, population, or factories.
A country can pass some reviews, fail another after a defeat, and later recover through growth or voluntary demobilization.

A passed review consumes a credit and begins the next watch if credits remain.
A failed review consumes a credit and returns the country to a minimum-week hold.
Remaining reviews wait until it unlocks again.
An oversized locked country does not burn through its review budget while waiting for compliance.

Narrative direction: recurring inspections that different countries pass or fail according to their own circumstances.
The evolution does not create synchronized global unlock dates or synchronized relock waves.

## 6. Evolution IV: Global Demobilization

The final supplied evolution reduces the multiplier to 55 percent and permits five reviews, 10 days apart.
It uses a narrow five-percent relock margin.

The event can now keep a country's establishment frozen through long stretches of war and rebuilding.
It still does not compel administrative disband, destroy a surviving army, force a peace treaty, or levy an unrelated resource tax.
A country that can materially support its force can still satisfy the current calculation.

Narrative direction: relentless international or anomalous military scrutiny, with the contrast between frozen establishment documents and continuing operations.
Preserve the event's administrative identity and restrained presentation rather than introducing a separate catastrophe mechanic.

## 7. Watch timing, credits, and settings

At each watch start, the due day is the watch-start day plus the regime's interval from Section 1.
Do not recompute it as today plus delay on each refresh, which would push the appointment forward forever.
A later active regime may bring an existing review forward according to its original watch start, but never earlier than the next game day.
A newly opened watch always receives its full fresh delay.

Consume one credit before resolving each actual review.
Keep a per-firing count of spent reviews.
When a higher regime raises that firing's review count, remaining credits become the greater of the old remaining credits and the new count minus already spent credits, bounded below by zero.
A relapse does not reset the budget.

A genuine repeat firing gives a new grant and a new current-state target.
Its pending future review count becomes the greater of the old remaining count and the new target, not their sum.
The repeat starts or renews the country lock and pauses watches.
Old callbacks cannot spend or restore credits belonging to the new grant.
Historical spent-review records used by achievements and anti-farm guards remain distinct from the new operational counter.

Disabling evolutions removes effects of disabled stages and cancels reviews that no enabled active regime supports.
Recalculate A under the highest still-enabled applicable regime, or base if none remains.
Keep a running seven-day floor.
If the country is then compliant and its floor has elapsed, release it.

Re-enabling a stage during the same still-active director uses preserved spent-credit receipts, not a fresh free grant.
Re-enabling after full closure waits for a new firing.
An explicit clear-active-effects operation retires all Event 096 watches and holds without compliance rewards.

### Political succession, splits, and mergers

A verified successor inherits the original firing, regime, spent-credit history, and remaining obligation.
A split may carry the outstanding local obligation into each legitimate continuing branch, including a surviving predecessor, but every branch starts with the predecessor's already-spent history.
It does not receive a new grant or a reset review budget.
Copy consumed receipt identities so delivery of an old callback cannot repeat an already resolved review.

An inherited watch keeps its original start and absolute due day.
An inherited hold receives Part 1's first-application floor.
Once it legitimately releases, any remaining review starts a normal new watch.

For a merger, keep the strictest still-applicable active regime and the maximum remaining count rather than summing counts.
If any merged obligation is held, hold the resulting actor, keep the latest unexpired inherited floor, and also apply a fresh seven-day floor if this is that actor's first hold.
Otherwise retain the earliest pending watch appointment.
A held actor pauses any retained watch appointment until release, after which one normal watch begins.
Keep one logical evaluator and one future watch chain.

Consumed-credit receipts remain distinct even when multiple grants merge into one operational maximum; do not reconstruct them from a guessed sum of spent counters.
None of these transfers is a new Chaos manifestation or a new achievement attempt.

The actual political or provider transaction must identify the relationship and provide a complete transfer snapshot.
No generic successor getter or native transfer hook has been proved here.
If that adapter cannot meet these rules, the affected portion of G3 remains blocked.
No speculative transfer may authorize removal, attribution, or successful restoration.

## 8. Consequences and connections

Military Madness can improve combat performance while the establishment remains frozen.
It does not inflate the allowance through a combat-strength variable.
If it changes real template structure or grants units, F follows that change.

The Offensive can make the AI want to retain its army and pursue growth.
Event 096 does not override another event's strategic identity by forcing it to demobilize.
Its war and security veto in Part 5 keeps the AI from random army liquidation during a survival conflict.

Famine, migration, camps, condemnation and fallout reach Event 096 only through the live inputs they already change, such as population lost to famine or flight lowering P, or destroyed infrastructure lowering T.
Event 096 adds no hook to those systems: demobilized soldiers return to the manpower pool, not to a civilian ledger, and an arms embargo restricts imports rather than establishment.
CBRN support companies count as ordinary support attachments (Part 2).

Territorial, population, industrial, and army-grant events need no hardcoded pairwise allowance bonus.
They change the same live inputs.
Provider callbacks are useful for prompt refresh, but a bounded local reconciliation must also catch changes that lack a specific callback.

Each actual renewed hold can contribute to the bounded Chaos consequence policy in Part 5.
Eligibility, stage activation, logging, credit allocation, a passed inspection, and simply staying above the limit give no direct Chaos by themselves.
