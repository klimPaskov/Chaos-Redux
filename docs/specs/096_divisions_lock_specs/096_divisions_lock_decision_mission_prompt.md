## Action and display contract

Use proposed IDs `division_inspection_disband_one` and `division_inspection_disband_batch`, after checking actual repository collisions. The normal action requests one eligible owned fielded formation. The severe action requires raw e at least 0.75 and uses Part 3's live formula with N_owned meaning owned fielded land formation records, not battalions. Its maximum bound is ten and its displayed request is limited by the eligible population.

Division count is allowed only as the batch-sizing input. The army measure and allowance always come from Part 2's structural calculation. Keep all special units positive in that measure even when provider protection makes them ineligible for removal.

Use no political-power fee, no hidden resource charge, no per-click reward, and no mission. The event has a minimum and a compliance condition, so a mission timeout must not become a forced disband or automatic release.

Show the two current point values, one contextual minimum or review time, and the confirmation's authorized up-to count. The maximum is four across the mechanic. Do not add an eligible-unit count, excess meter, fixed release forecast, or individual target-selection UI. A category picture or custom window is not part of this assignment.

Frame-driven visibility and availability use complete current-context published snapshots and cheap guards. Missing or stale snapshots withhold availability and request the existing local refresh. No state, formation, or world scan belongs in those per-frame predicates. Opening a request and committing it still require fresh complete calculations. Profile the category held open during refresh.

## Confirmation and mutation

The first click only creates one immutable country request and opens the confirmation. Freeze the request identity, exact country and authority, inspection grant and episode, regime revision, action, and bound. Do not choose or reveal a random target before confirmation. Cancel is the first and default option, and every timeout or invalid authority path is non-destructive.

Revalidate on click, not only when the popup opens. A repeat, release, relevant evolution, authority change, disappearance, or invalid request must block mutation. Keep invalidated receipt fields until the old native popup terminates. Do not overwrite a slot that a stale option could read as a new authorization.

At commit, refresh complete F and A, verify the hold and excess, prove current action availability, and reject a batch whose fresh allowable bound is below the presented bound. Mark the receipt executing before selection. Choose uniformly among exact current eligible owned formations, remove one through the proved administrative operation, refresh actual F/A, and repeat only while the authorized bound and all stop conditions permit it. Stop immediately at compliance, even during the minimum week.

The native country lock remains active throughout. Never open it briefly to use ordinary UI disband. Never substitute broad template deletion or an empty `delete_unit` block for one selected formation. Exact returns of surviving manpower and equipment, including variants and zero-human units, must be proved. The scalar `destroy_unit` reference does not establish those returns.

Human quiet-front units and a final oversized formation may be deliberately risked under the current explicit confirmation and pool rules. Active combat and invalid removal authority remain excluded. The AI additionally has war, threat, capitulation, other-owner, growth, and survival vetoes. Do not invent a generic critical-front predicate or silently widen protected-provider eligibility.

The final human confirmation must explicitly disclose when the authorized one-formation action or batch can leave the country with no owned fielded land formation. Freeze whether that consequence was disclosed and authorized. Newly introduced final-formation risk requires a fresh confirmation, with invalidation before the first removal or a safe stop before the final removal in a partial batch. Keep the same cancel path and up-to bound, without another public counter or a target preview. The AI prohibition remains absolute.

Complete or invalidate each receipt once. Report actual removal count. Apply the shared one-successful-transaction-per-day guard only when at least one real removal occurred. A partial batch keeps its actual result and does not respawn approximate replacements to simulate rollback.
