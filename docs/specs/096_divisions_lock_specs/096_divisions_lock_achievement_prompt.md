## Mechanical integration

Use durable country records keyed by the actual qualifying episode or firing grant.
A new firing cannot merge old progress into a new easy attempt.
Record exact Event 096 transaction outcomes before and after actual removal, and distinguish scheduled review consumption from a skipped or cancelled appointment.

Bind every unfinished objective to one exact grant and valid actor episode.
A genuine repeat retires the old unfinished attempt, then may start a fresh independently qualifying attempt with new entry values and zero observation, reduction, and review progress.
Duplicate dispatch delivery does not reset it.
A same-grant active evolution does not reset it by itself.
A different successor actor receives no unfinished achievement qualification, and an already awarded shared achievement remains awarded.
Exercise this boundary separately for all three IDs.

The industrial achievement's force continuity is defined at completed authoritative daily observations, with exact Event 096 removal receipts in addition.
Do not invent continuous tracking for every intra-day state change.
A missing observation interval is not assumed compliant.
The industrial-only counterfactual uses the preserved entry population and territorial inputs with current industry and the actual release regime.

The demobilization achievement requires a meaningful verified contribution, not a button press.
An invalid request, partial failure without enough contribution, or a final force below the specified broad band cannot qualify.
Ordinary battle deaths must not be attributed to an Event 096 transaction.

The rolling achievement uses the stricter F at most A test at every qualifying review, regardless of the higher relock hysteresis.
It must complete the actual current grant and its review budget.
Do not award it from elapsed time, a disabled regime, an annexed actor, or a repeat that replaced the grant.

Use existing project eligibility for human campaigns, debug/test conditions, and registry settings.
Add no mechanical reward or new resource currency.
Keep progress in the existing achievement interface and preserve the event's four-slot public information budget.
