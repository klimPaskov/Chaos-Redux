## 6. Additional parent consistency decisions

The parent explicitly closed two related ambiguities while integrating the reviews.

First, a closed historical grant does not permanently bypass a later firing's current Chaos threshold. A new opening selects its highest enabled current-Chaos regime. A repeat that coalesces an existing active obligation preserves a stricter already activated and enabled regime. Historical evolution records remain historical unless they represent a still-active obligation.

Second, a genuine repeat retires an unfinished prior grant's restoration eligibility without paying a negative Chaos credit. It preserves already paid consequences and the family budget. A new grant may qualify only through its own actual manifestation and full legitimate completion. This prevents coalesced operational work from later paying several completion rewards for the same restoration.

The growth estimator, regime-memory selection, and restoration retirement are explicit design clarifications. They are not claimed to be existing repository behavior or user-provided numerical rules.
