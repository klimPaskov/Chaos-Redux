# Sandstorm: responses and missions

## Response structure

Five primary action families cover the recurring decisions during the storm.
The selected location determines their precise targets and availability.
The ordinary panel shows the most relevant three to five rows and never expands into a list for every state in the world.
Each row shows the expected benefit, sacrifice, preparation time, affected scope and all payable components.
Every stated protection or operating duration begins when preparation completes successfully and the response becomes effective.
Preparation time is additional, with its own completion date, and never consumes the promised effective-duration clock.
Earlier clearance, invalid authority or another explicit interruption can end a response as described below.
Shared mask distribution, migration, air orders and native repair views are contextual links to their own systems.
Those links do not charge a second Sandstorm fee for the same shared action.

All action names in this file are working labels.
Final names and descriptions should identify the practical activity and the geographic target in direct period-appropriate language.
Descriptions explain why the player may choose the action, what they give up and what happens if the front changes course.
No response grants a permanent blanket immunity to dust.

## Action overview

| Family | Primary purpose | Main sacrifice | Eligible authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 Household retreat | Reduce exposure from outdoor activity and travel | Temporary additional industrial and transport interruption | Current civilian authority in the selected state |
| D2 Protect machinery | Preserve real factories, infrastructure and airfield equipment | Support equipment, Political Power and civilian-factory commitment | Controller of the relevant facilities |
| D3 Essential corridor | Preserve part of a useful route's throughput | Support equipment, trucks, fuel, Political Power and continued outdoor exposure | Authority over the covered route, subject to route-owner validity |
| D4 Field command precautions | Preserve part of a force's coordination and organization | Command Power, Army Experience and slower operations | Country commanding its own eligible formations |
| D5 Airfield preservation | Reduce exposure-related accidents and base damage | Support equipment, Political Power and lower local sortie output | Controller of the airfield and relevant own operations |

## D1 Household retreat

The action becomes visible for a state in the committed or likely approach corridor and for a currently affected state with residents.
It is available without a stockpile cost.
The controller chooses to suspend exposed civilian activity, discourage nonessential travel and prepare existing buildings for shelter.
The response takes six hours to become effective and then follows the state through the current passage.
It cannot claim an instantaneous benefit for an interval already committed before the order.

Its risk multipliers are defined in Part 04.
While both the response and actual exposure are active, productive capacity is multiplied by a further 0.65, construction by 0.50, extraction by 0.80 and local supply throughput by 0.90 after the weather contribution.
For example, severe weather leaves 40 percent of comparable productive capacity before this order, and household retreat reduces that to 26 percent.
This is a substantial civilian-protection choice during a war or supply emergency.

The policy has a twenty-four-hour minimum commitment to prevent hourly toggling around exposure commits.
It automatically ends its weather-related sacrifices when the state clears, even if that minimum would otherwise run longer.
The player may cancel after the commitment, accepting the remaining risk.
A forecast diversion before exposure cancels the prepared order without an invented population loss or completion reward.
The preparation itself does not stop unexposed industry.

A control change stops the former government's future policy authority.
Household behavior already in effect fades over a short six-hour handover unless the new authority adopts it.
The new controller receives the actual current condition and can decide immediately.
The policy does not erase shared masks, shelter projects or unrelated protection.

AI normally uses this action for severe or extreme exposure over a populated state.
It may keep a uniquely important corridor or industrial district operating when an immediate military or supply consequence dominates, but the decision must use the country's known context and a credible civilian cost.
It should not refuse the free protective option simply because it lacks equipment for more expensive responses.

## D2 Protect machinery

The action targets one threatened state with actual industry, infrastructure or an airfield that can still benefit.
Preparation lasts forty-eight hours.
Two available civilian factories are committed during preparation, with no creation of additional factories or hidden double use of assigned capacity.
The proposal charges 15 Political Power and Support Equipment equal to the ceiling of eight times the state's currently working factory count plus five times its infrastructure level plus five times its airbase level, clamped from 20 to 200.
These are the complete consumed resource types for this action.

Completed protection lasts fourteen days or until the current passage clears, whichever occurs first.
It reduces qualifying physical industry and airfield damage by 60 percent and infrastructure damage by 40 percent.
It also recovers 20 percent of the weather-attributable productive-capacity loss while the protected facilities remain operational.
Under a severe 60 percent weather loss, this recovers 12 percentage points before any separate household-retreat sacrifice.
It does not restore already damaged capacity or resource deposits.

Preparation may continue inside the storm if resources and authority remain valid.
Only subsequent exposure receives protection after completion.
If the wall passes before completion, the task ends as an interrupted preparation and any consumed physical materials remain spent.
An execution failure before successful payment refunds actual debits through the transaction owner and applies no benefit.
That is different from a paid project whose strategic value changed after it started.

A forecast diversion before resources are committed cancels freely.
Once the project commits, equipment is consumed and factory time is spent as elapsed.
An unfinished project releases unused future factory commitment when canceled or when authority is lost.
Delivered completed physical protection remains attached to the facilities for its remaining duration after capture.
It is not duplicated in the former controller's territory or refunded to either side.

Renewal during one unusually long continuous passage is allowed only when the prior fourteen-day protection expires and there is remaining useful exposure to address.
A fresh quote pays for actual remaining protectable facilities.
The same receipt cannot be completed twice or renewed early to farm events, achievements or Chaos reductions.

After clearance, this action's location link selects actual repair work rather than offering a free restoration button.
Any later acceleration of native repairs must have its own existing owner cost and capacity proof.

## D3 Essential corridor

The player selects a real route segment through currently controlled or otherwise valid cooperating territory.
It must connect two meaningful endpoints and carry an identified supply, civilian movement or emergency-access function.
The selected protected section is at most 1,000 kilometers and is drawn from the actual route network.
An isolated map highlight with no connected usable path is ineligible.
Enemy control, native damage, reception capacity and the migration owner's safety rules can still block the route.

For a section of length L kilometers, the seven-day package consumes the following proposed quantities.

| Resource | Proposed cost |
| --- | --- |
| Political Power | 10 |
| Support Equipment | Ceiling of 20 plus L divided by 10, capped at 120 |
| Trucks | Ceiling of 10 plus L divided by 20, capped at 60 |
| Fuel | Ceiling of 500 plus 2.5 times L, capped at 3,000 |

There are four consumed cost types.
The row may show at most three compact icon amounts, while its complete payment tooltip must show all four and explain the route length used in the quote.
An affordability check and final execution recheck use the same complete current quote.
The project's actual debits and any failed-payment refund belong to the universal cost owner.

The response takes twelve hours to establish markers, convoy discipline and maintenance support.
While valid, it recovers 30 percent of the weather-attributable throughput loss on the selected section.
Severe weather's 60 percent loss becomes a 42 percent loss for that protected route contribution.
It cannot restore infrastructure already physically damaged below capacity or grant access through hostile territory.

Maintaining traffic multiplies the movement-and-transport component of direct resident risk in the covered operating area by 1.5.
The operating area is the canonical physical route cells clipped to the actual current storm footprint, projected to resident exposure with the same declared geographic approximation as Part 04.
The traffic-risk contribution applies only during verified useful traffic on that section.
A paid but idle, blocked or unused corridor provides neither an invented service benefit nor continuing traffic-related deaths.
With the proposed 20 percent baseline channel, that raises unprotected total direct resident risk by 10 percent where the operating-area approximation applies.
This increase does not apply to the entire country.
Migration cohorts use the migration owner's route-risk accounting after they depart and are excluded from the resident calculation.
The route view must disclose the remaining danger and any coexisting severe heat or Acid Rain.

The player can close the supported route after a twenty-four-hour commitment.
Closing removes its additional traffic risk and future throughput benefit, with consumed supplies remaining spent.
If authority, continuity or a valid endpoint disappears, the route immediately stops offering the throughput benefit and the active mission records interruption.
An unsafe route is never represented as safe merely because the fee was paid.

The same action supports a new seven-day package only after the existing package ends.
Renewal rechecks the real route, current length, resources and need.
Only one Sandstorm corridor package can apply to the same physical section at once, including overlapping packages from cooperating countries.
Donor or allied participation uses the route owner's explicit payment and authority seam.

## D4 Field command precautions

The action targets the country's own formations in one selected affected or soon-threatened operating area.
Eligible units must be physically present or have a verified committed approach into that area.
It cannot target a distant enemy state to obtain private unit information.
The proposed price is 20 Command Power and 10 Army Experience.
Preparation takes twelve hours and the response lasts seven days while the same operating-area context remains valid.

The benefit reduces the Sandstorm coordination penalty by 25 percent, the organization-recovery penalty by 20 percent and the direct weather organization drain by 25 percent.
It recovers 10 percent of the weather-attributable attack-output loss.
These are fractions of the penalty, not flat percentage-point bonuses added to all army statistics.
Severe coordination loss of 70 percent therefore becomes 52.5 percent for eligible prepared forces.

The sacrifice multiplies remaining movement speed by a further 0.85 and planning acquisition by a further 0.85 while the precautions apply under exposure.
It represents slower formations, tighter spacing, repeated position checks and conservative orders.
It cannot restore visual reconnaissance through an extreme wall.
The actual unit-local implementation must preserve player command and avoid silently rewriting army fronts or unit orders.

Only the purchasing country's eligible formations benefit.
Other armies continue to receive ordinary local weather effects.
A unit leaving the operating area loses both the response contribution and its movement sacrifice once it is clear of the local context.
A commander does not carry the benefit to every division under their command elsewhere.
Canceled, invalid or expired contexts do not award experience or refund a completed drill.
Only one live preparation or effective D4 package may exist for the same commanding country and canonical operating area.
Manual and automated requests share that identity, so a duplicate request links to the existing package or is rejected without payment.
The package cannot stack or refresh early, and renewal requires expiry, a fresh quote and remaining useful exposure.
If permitted operating areas overlap, one formation still receives at most one D4 response contribution, using the strongest valid applicable package without additive stacking.

AI considers this response when an exposed force is fighting, retreating under pressure or defending an essential position.
It should conserve scarce Command Power when the affected force is small, already withdrawn or able to wait safely.
That choice must not depend on hidden enemy strength unavailable to the AI's ordinary intelligence model.

## D5 Airfield preservation

The action targets a threatened controlled airfield with actual usable capacity and own relevant operations.
Preparation takes twenty-four hours.
The proposed seven-day package consumes 15 Political Power and Support Equipment equal to the ceiling of 20 plus ten times the usable airbase level, capped at 120.
The completed response protects the selected field through reduced exposed operations, covers and maintenance.

While the field is affected, own local sortie output associated with the protected operating policy is multiplied by a further 0.50 after weather.
The Sandstorm-attributable increment in accident and ingestion risk is reduced by 60 percent for the operations the protection actually covers.
It also reduces qualifying physical airfield damage by 50 percent, composed once with D2 using the stronger applicable protection rather than adding two full reductions.
Planes operating elsewhere retain their normal context.

The local airfield, wing and mission relationship must be demonstrated by the chosen native consumer.
A country-wide efficiency modifier cannot represent this choice.
If implementation requires actual reduced or suspended sorties through existing air controls, the interface must link the correct operations and observe that state before granting the protection.
Paying for covers alone cannot make aircraft flying continuously through extreme dust safe.

The action ends its weather-related operational sacrifice when the field clears.
Consumed supplies remain spent.
Changing controller stops the old operator's policy, preserves whatever physical covered-base protection actually remains and requires the new operator to establish its own operating discipline.
The AI favors it when losing exposed aircraft would be worse than temporarily giving up the remaining small mission output.
Only one live preparation or effective D5 package may exist for the same airfield controller and physical airfield.
Manual and automated purchases share that identity and cannot pay twice, stack protection or refresh the effective clock early.
Renewal requires expiry, a fresh quote, valid operating-policy proof and remaining useful exposure.

## Mission structure

At most three mission cards are active for a country, one per mission family.
They summarize meaningful deadlines and outcomes across selected targets instead of spawning a mission for every affected state.
A mission never creates a second payment for an action already purchased.

| Mission | Start and target | Success or partial success | Failure and closure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M1 Prepare the threatened district | A valid warning identifies a populated priority district and its first committed arrival | Household retreat or completed relevant protection becomes effective before the first severe interval, with actual timing retained | Late preparation is partial success and still helps later exposure. A diverted forecast closes neutrally. It does not kill civilians or award a reward for timeout alone |
| M2 Keep an essential corridor functioning | A successfully paid D3 package identifies the actual section, preparation and seven-day effective interval starting at completion | The corridor retains real positive verified throughput during at least five exposed days, or throughout its shorter actual exposure | Breaks, lost authority or unsafe endpoints yield partial or failed service based on actual valid intervals. The card cannot declare success solely because seven days elapsed |
| M3 Restore the damaged district | A cleared district contains actual Sandstorm-attributed damage and a valid native repair plan | Within fourteen days, at least half of its recorded repairable disruption is removed by actual repair while essential access is restored | Remaining damage gives a factual partial outcome. A returning storm suspends the recovery assessment and preserves the remaining work rather than charging again |

M2 requires at least one complete six-hour interval of actual qualifying exposure, positive observed useful throughput and an applied D3 benefit.
Its shorter-exposure success clause means service throughout that real exposed interval, not an empty interval after a diverted forecast.
Zero exposure or a complete diversion closes neutrally.
Missing required service observations leave the result unverified rather than successful.

Mission M3 does not automatically debit a new resource package.
It requires positive measured attributable repairable damage and an actual essential facility or route, so an empty district cannot qualify.
The country assigns existing repair capacity through the native owner and its actual costs.
Its receipt freezes the actual eligible damage cohort present at mission start and a fourteen-day clear-weather assessment budget.
Only actual recurrence of the storm footprint over the mission district pauses that budget, and the same receipt resumes with the unused time after complete clearance.
Control loss interrupts the mission rather than resetting it for another owner.
New returning-passage damage remains a separate cohort and does not enlarge, shrink or restart the original mission denominator.
The mission's result describes restoration of its original tracked cohort while acknowledging any newer damage still present.
Its receipt measures actual allocated repair and route restoration, excluding new construction, already repaired levels and unresolved competing changes.
It grants no free factories, population or repeatable Chaos reward.
Achievement progress can use the real mission evidence subject to the stricter multi-condition requirements in Part 09.
The absolute fourteen-day and twenty-one-day achievement windows in that part do not inherit the mission's pause rule.

## Automation and target selection

The player can enable or disable automatic use of the already defined action families for threatened own targets.
Automation evaluates the bounded warning and affected-state registries rather than every state in the world.
It orders candidates by forecast certainty, intensity, estimated exposed residents, essential route importance and the country's existing military needs.
An affordable useful state should not starve forever because the first state is unaffordable.
Evaluation therefore proceeds through a bounded ranked queue and records the reason a candidate was skipped.

The default automatic policy preserves at least 25 Political Power, 20 Command Power and ten percent of each relevant equipment stock after a purchase.
It also preserves the country's existing verified fuel reserve policy and does not consume factory capacity already committed to another owner.
When no verified fuel reserve policy is available, automated D3 requires seven days of observed current fuel use to remain after the quote.
Manual actions may use resources below those policy reserves if they still satisfy actual affordability and protected commitments.
The interface uses existing resource amounts and qualitative policy states without adding a new preparedness meter.

There is no payment merely for turning automation on.
Each actual purchase has the same quote, final validity check, receipt and result as a manual action.
No hidden daily replenishment creates stock for the AI or the player.
War, capture, population movement and forecast changes immediately affect the next bounded evaluation.

## Payment, timing and cancellation acceptance

Every paid action must behave correctly just below, exactly at and just above the total current price.
All required components are preflighted before a benefit is granted, including real factory commitments and protected resource reservations.
Actual successful debit occurs once.
Failed execution records what was actually paid and refunds only those components once through the owner.
Repeated clicks, save and load, expired targets and duplicate completion callbacks cannot duplicate benefit, payment or refund.

Costs passed through a shared discount source use its current quote and its proper rounding.
The displayed amount, availability and amount charged must agree.
Four consumed resource types is the maximum for an action in this design, and D3 is the only family using all four.
The implementation may not hide a fifth cost in a mission or completion effect.
