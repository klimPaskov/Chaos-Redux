# Sandstorm: achievements and campaign acceptance

## Achievement principles

Eight achievements reward preparation, difficult operation, repeated recovery and rare evolved conditions.
Their keys and labels are working identifiers, not finished titles.
Each needs an original completed 64×64 icon direction and the shared achievement system's required variants.
Achievements use actual paid responses, authoritative front history and measured outcomes.
None unlocks merely because the event fired, a stage flag exists or the player clicked an obvious button.

Continuous country eligibility follows the actual player-controlled country through the qualifying interval.
Changing tags, loading an already completed foreign record or taking control only after the hard part cannot transfer the achievement.
Debug grants and testing contexts follow the shared achievement system's normal disqualification policy.
Where an exact required observation is unavailable, the achievement remains blocked until its tracker is implemented honestly.
It cannot substitute a timer for an unobserved outcome.

## ACH099_01: protected population across a broad front

**Eligible countries:** Any country controlling the qualifying populated district throughout both preparation and passage.
**Visibility and difficulty:** Visible, difficult.

Qualify in at least three controlled states whose combined resident population at the first severe exposure is at least three million.
Each state must accumulate at least five fully covered equivalent days of severe-or-extreme exposure during one continuous passage.
Household retreat must have become effective before its first severe interval, and the shared mask owner must prove a respiratory input R of at least 0.40 during at least eighty percent of the qualifying exposure.
Combined actual direct Sandstorm deaths and attributed route deaths among evacuated residents from that district must remain at or below 0.20 percent of the snapshot population.
Any evacuated cohort counted toward the outcome must reach a valid safe settlement rather than disappear from the accounting.

The tracker retains the qualifying population snapshot, continuous authority, exposure integrals, actual response timing, valid mask facts, actual losses and cohort receipts.
Later population loss cannot shrink the denominator.
Unrelated territories cannot be added after impact to dilute the measured district.
The challenge is sustained preparation over a genuinely large threatened population, with limited stocks and economic costs.
The icon direction is a protected doorway and fitted mask in front of a vast dust wall, with a strong small-scale silhouette.
Title and description direction should emphasize organized protection across a broad district without claiming that nobody died.

## ACH099_02: keep a wartime corridor functioning

**Eligible countries:** A country at war controlling or validly operating the qualifying corridor.
**Visibility and difficulty:** Visible, difficult.

Maintain a real corridor at least three hundred kilometers long through five consecutive days of severe-or-extreme exposure.
The route must have been genuinely functional for at least three days before the committed warning and must support at least ten of the country's own deployed divisions during the qualifying interval.
A valid D3 package must be active, and actual useful throughput must remain at least half of the verified pre-warning comparable level without an interval of complete route failure.
The country must still be at war when the exposed interval ends.

Store the route's endpoints, physical section, pre-warning measurement, supplied formations, paid receipt, control validity and observed throughput.
Artificially idling a route to create a near-zero baseline does not qualify.
The route cannot be replaced by a different clear route while retaining the old exposed identity.
The challenge is keeping an essential system operating despite the cost and civilian transport risk.
The icon direction is a marked lamp and rail or road line disappearing into dust, with a small convoy silhouette.
Writing direction should convey reliable service under disorienting conditions.

## ACH099_03: recover and endure a genuine return

**Eligible countries:** Any country continuously controlling the qualifying district.
**Visibility and difficulty:** Visible after The Returning Storm is known, very difficult.

A district with at least one million residents at its first impact must endure two related severe-or-extreme passages, each lasting at least three fully covered equivalent days.
They must be separated by at least seven days completely clear of every Sandstorm front.
Before the second passage, the country must actually repair at least half of the first passage's recorded physical disruption and restore an essential route or facility.
Each passage must produce a positive measured attributable damage cohort involving actual essential capacity, and each repair percentage uses that passage's immutable cohort.
Within fourteen days after the second clearance, it must repair at least eighty percent of the second passage's recorded repairable disruption.
Combined direct and attributed evacuation-route deaths across both passages must remain below 0.80 percent of the original district population.

Track lineage, actual clear interval, repair receipts, original population and both loss receipts.
One continuous overlap, a renamed merged front or a state briefly removed from the display cannot count as a return.
The challenge is committing recovery resources under a recurring threat while preserving lives and capacity.
The icon direction is a repaired rail arch or workshop confronting a second wall, with two distinct weather arcs.
Writing direction should recognize repeated recovery without triumphal treatment of the losses.

## ACH099_04: evacuate before a distant ocean landfall

**Eligible countries:** The controller of the threatened coastal origin district.
**Visibility and difficulty:** Hidden until a qualifying distant approach is known, very difficult.

The approaching lineage must have traveled at least 1,500 kilometers through continuous sea geography before making landfall on the threatened district.
Use the shared migration system to move at least 50,000 actual residents from that district to a valid destination before its first severe interval.
The source district must then actually suffer at least forty-eight hours of severe-or-extreme weather, proving the evacuation addressed a real threat.
At least 99 percent of the departed cohort must arrive alive, and the destination must remain validly safe and supplied for twenty-one days after settlement.

The tracker uses the true sea path, committed warning, source population debit, route mortality, destination credit and food-safety receipts.
Teleporting residents, moving already absent people or selecting an untracked destination cannot qualify.
The challenge is using warning time, route capacity and receiving-state support before an unpredictable long-distance wall arrives.
The icon direction is a period vessel or marked evacuation convoy leaving a dust-covered coast, with a clear distant destination.
Writing direction should focus on foresight and actual safe arrival.

## ACH099_05: restore a country after the World of Dust

**Eligible countries:** A country present throughout the global milestone and its own qualifying impacts.
**Visibility and difficulty:** Visible after Evolution IV is known, exceptional.

The actual global World of Dust milestone must occur.
At the country's first committed severe-warning receipt, or its first severe impact if no prior warning exists, freeze its then-controlled state set and each state's current population as the country eligibility snapshot.
Qualifying exposed states and their original residents are drawn from that fixed set, so later capture, selective removal or population decline cannot inflate or dilute the snapshot.
The country must have at least six controlled states and at least three million original residents within its qualifying district, with five fully covered equivalent severe days across each state during that season.
It must complete a valid essential-corridor mission and an actual recovery mission, and use at least two additional response families under conditions where their benefit truly applied.
Within twenty-one days after global season closure, it must restore at least eighty percent of its recorded Sandstorm-damaged essential industrial and transport capacity without losing continuing country eligibility.
The restoration denominator is the positive actual Sandstorm damage cohort within the fixed eligible state set, frozen at global season closure, with actual essential capacity required.
It includes cumulative actual new attributable damage through closure, and verified allocated repairs completed before closure remain credited as restoration progress.
Its receipts exclude unexplained changes and use the proportional verified-repair allocation in Part 04.
An empty damage denominator does not qualify.

The tracker uses the actual milestone, district snapshot, exposed-day integrals, response receipts and measured repair outcomes.
Every state already destroyed before the season is excluded from newly damaged capacity.
The challenge spans the whole severe campaign rather than rewarding the presentation itself.
The icon direction is a functioning industrial and transport skyline emerging from several retreating dust arcs.
Writing direction should emphasize persistence and restoration at global scale.

## ACH099_06: protect a district under three atmospheric hazards

**Eligible countries:** The controller of the qualifying district.
**Visibility and difficulty:** Hidden until a genuine triple overlap is observed, exceptional.

A district with at least 500,000 residents at overlap start must face at least seventy-two continuous hours of severe-or-extreme Sandstorm together with owner-proven severe Heat Wave and active Acid Rain.
Household retreat must be effective before the triple-overlap interval begins, valid mask input R must remain at least 0.55, and machinery protection must apply to real working facilities.
Actual direct Sandstorm and bounded Sandstorm interaction deaths during the qualifying overlap must stay below 0.30 percent of the district snapshot population.
At least half of the district's previously working industrial capacity must remain physically undamaged at the overlap's end.

The tracker requires all three current geographic receipts and does not infer overlap from country flags.
Companion hazards retain their own ordinary deaths and history.
The achievement tests the Sandstorm protection outcome and must disclose that scope in final description direction.
The challenge is preparation and resources during a rare, locally compounded emergency.
The icon direction combines a sealed doorway, sun and corrosive rain marks behind a heavy dust curtain, with the protected subject clearly dominant.
Writing direction should remain factual about the simultaneous conditions.

## ACH099_07: sustain verified foreign protection

**Eligible countries:** A real donor using an accepted shared protection or relief route.
**Visibility and difficulty:** Visible, very difficult.

Deliver actual protective-equipment crates from the donor's stock to two distinct foreign-controlled threatened districts in different countries.
The receiving districts must each contain at least 500,000 residents at delivery and later undergo at least three fully covered equivalent severe days.
The donor's deliveries must provide at least 250,000 newly effective civilian coverages in each district, measured as the shared owner's positive delivered coverage increment rather than nominal crates alone.
The receiving response must remain valid through the qualifying exposure, and both districts must later complete actual recovery of an essential route or facility.

Use external-supplier, real stock debit, positive usable-delivery and recipient-state receipts from the shared owner.
An existing issue by another country cannot be relabeled as this donor's contribution.
The achievement remains blocked if the existing owner lacks an accessible valid cooperation flow and requires an accepted narrow integration to expose that flow.
It does not authorize an invented free foreign-aid system.
The challenge is material cooperation and continued useful protection across borders.
The icon direction is two distinct protected communities linked by real supply crates across a dust-filled gap.
Writing direction should attribute the actual help without implying political ownership of the recipients.

## ACH099_08: bring formations through the wall

**Eligible countries:** A country commanding the qualifying deployed formations throughout the interval.
**Visibility and difficulty:** Visible, difficult.

At least ten existing deployed divisions must each experience seventy-two hours of severe-or-extreme weather while the country is at war.
The units must use D4 precautions during at least half their exposure, leave the affected footprint through ordinary movement, and survive as continuing formation identities.
Within seven days of their own final clearance, each must recover to at least eighty percent of its current legitimate maximum organization and receive useful supply.
None may gain organization directly from the Sandstorm exposure or cleanup operation.

Track unit identities, qualifying exposure, actual response application, real exit, survival and post-clear organization.
Disbanding and recreating units, merging away a failed tracked identity or awarding the achievement from an army-wide flag cannot qualify.
The challenge is preserving operational cohesion through a dangerous local movement and recovery problem.
The icon direction is a compass and intact formation emerging from the trailing dust.
Writing direction should recognize disciplined withdrawal or passage rather than requiring an artificial offensive.

## Campaign acceptance scenes

The implementation must demonstrate the complete identity in a range of campaign situations.
The following scenes are design acceptance criteria and future native validation targets.
The separate balance and execution files expand their inputs and evidence requirements.

| Scene | Required experience |
| --- | --- |
| Sparse desert opening | A huge connected front with relatively small population losses and visible outgoing movement |
| Dense industrial passage | Far greater exposed population and serious temporary output, supply and physical damage proportional to duration |
| Symmetric battle | Attackers and defenders visibly lose useful capability, including occupied territory and attackers outside the affected state |
| Ocean crossing | Continuous sea geometry and air disruption followed by a real coastal or island warning and landfall |
| Weak splinter and dominant branch | Material-conserving split with different survival and independent directions |
| Returning wall | A real clear interval, actual recovery and later renewed exposure through a curved path |
| Desert reseed | A crossed emitting area creates a new finite front while sharing the seasonal source allowance |
| Multi-source merge | At least three simultaneous source families, independent fronts and a genuine stable geographic merge |
| Triple overlap | Exact companion footprints, one bounded interaction, valid protection and owned cleanup |
| Final attenuation | Every front retires by the original deadline, while actual deaths and unrepaired damage remain |
| Late-season aftermath | A qualifying recovery or evacuation near closure retains its original bounded assessment window and receipts after the wall ends, with no renewed weather damage or repeated award |

## Definition of design coverage

All seventy-eight requirements from the user's brief must map to concrete design sections and future evidence.
All four evolutions need both active and pre-fire entry behavior.
Every primary action needs authority, cost, timing, scope, cancellation, failure and AI treatment.
Every achievement needs actual nontrivial proof and its asset consumer.
The two public values must remain consistent across all surfaces.
The source and evidence files distinguish this complete planning coverage from implementation readiness.
