# Sandstorm: shared systems, Chaos and AI

## Natural Disasters membership

The design registers Event 099 as a distinct High member of the existing Natural Disasters cluster **5**.
The [shared identifier registry](../shared_identifier_registry.md) and [2026-10-08 import evidence](../../plans/099_sandstorm_plans/099_import_evidence_2026-10-08.json) distinguish that target from the unchanged current workbook and runtime bridge.
It receives the tier-1 eligibility and Minor Fire-Once behavior supplied by the user.
The current cluster's Event 013 slots, Acid Rain and Heat Wave remain separately identified members.
Sandstorm is added as its own optional High entry with aligned registration metadata and a single global fire-once guard.
Cluster selection and direct Event 099 entry must claim the same receipt.
Several country-scoped entry attempts cannot create several independent seasons.

Event 013 retains its ordinary local dust family, independent disaster sequences and aftermath ownership.
The new Event 099 lifecycle owns the traveling global front.
It can reuse proven neutral primitives and shared population, cost and recovery owners without copying their private queues or generating an Event 013 aftermath card for every daily storm exposure.
The existing 099-to-013 bridge requires an explicit legacy migration described in the implementation contracts.

Root registration, actor context, fired history, Event Details and cluster metadata must agree on Event 099.
The first reporting country's role is observer or affected authority.
It is not presented as the country that caused the weather.
Ordinary country crisis limits may govern response surfaces, but they cannot make a worldwide physical front skip a country already managing three other problems.

## Shared owner boundaries

| Owner | Sandstorm supplies | Owner remains responsible for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Population and Deaths | One measured state loss, direct disaster cause, exact current attribution and receipt | One population mutation, actual-loss logging, recruitable-manpower reconciliation and shared Chaos conversion |
| Civilian protection | Valid local dust exposure and an accepted interpretation of actual issued coverage and quality | Mask stock, real automatic issue, fitting, filter condition, persistence and transfer |
| Migration | Bounded time-stamped route hazard, threatening footprint and valid receiving-state safety facts | Cohorts, departure, route deaths, destination credit, reception capacity and settlement |
| Famine | Proven temporary extraction, access, logistics or food-pressure facts through accepted adapters | Food stages, duration, relief access, famine deaths and food-safety projection |
| Air Cleanliness | A bounded accepted particulate emission request and cessation of future dust emissions | Shared aerosol pressure, accepted atmospheric change, decay, recovery, source history and its Chaos conversion |
| Universal costs | Current target-specific cost components, valid commitment requirements and intended payment | Shared quote, discount composition, actual debit receipt, refund and settlement |
| Native repairs and supply | Actual exposed building or segment damage and completed response facts | Real remaining capacity, repairs, network propagation and existing construction allocation |

The implementation must inspect the actual current owner seams before treating these design relationships as callable APIs.
An unverified adapter is an implementation dependency.
Its absence does not authorize a parallel event-owned famine, migration, protection or pollution meter.

## Particulate contribution

The inspected Air owner has one shared capped aerosol reservoir and six stable source categories.
It does not document a separate withdrawable Sandstorm account.
The intended integration is a narrow accepted addition to the existing aerosol admission path, with an Event 099 emission receipt that prevents repeated deposits from the same qualifying exposure.
It must not impersonate an Event 013 sequence merely to satisfy a private gate.

The proposed event contribution is deliberately modest relative to the acute local disaster.
Use a season maximum of two basis points of additional monthly reservoir pressure, subject to the existing four-basis-point shared natural reservoir cap and actual accepted room.
Admissions depend on substantial severe land exposure, duration and remaining event allowance.
The precise cadence and amount must be calibrated with the shared owner so six-hour movement updates cannot multiply a daily or monthly source.
Current owner's one hundred basis points equal one percentage point.

When local dust clears, the storm stops submitting future emissions from that footprint.
It does not subtract a guessed original amount from the shared reservoir or global atmosphere.
Natural pressure decay and accepted global recovery remain the Air owner's work.
No separate dust meter or new visible source category is added by this proposal.

## Concrete Chaos impact map

Storm evolution flags and menu actions give no Chaos.
The following event-owned changes represent concrete geographic disruption beyond the shared accounting of deaths, pollution and ordinary war.
They use simple tuning anchors and actual receipts.
Each positive receipt is awarded at most once, and its corresponding reversal can occur at most once and can never exceed the amount actually awarded.

| Receipt | Concrete positive outcome | Proposed change | Reversal | Guard and shared overlap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 Broad regional disruption | The initial connected wall meets the broad opening area requirement and maintains actual normal-or-worse disruption for twenty-four hours | +5 | Up to −5 when the season fully closes | One episode receipt. The entry event, flags and opening report add zero separately |
| C2 Distant continental disruption | A front makes continuous geographic landfall on a different major landmass and sustains severe disruption to an actual international or interregional transport connection for forty-eight hours | +5 per qualifying landmass, at most two | Up to −5 for each awarded landmass after its affected corridor is clear and usable for seven days | At most +10 total. No award per border, state or casualty. A continuous ocean trajectory and actual route impairment are required |
| C3 Divergent regional disruption | Two related independent fronts sustain severe disruption in distinct operating regions for forty-eight concurrent hours | +5 | Up to −5 after that multi-front disruption has ended for seven days | One season receipt. A split or new child identity alone adds zero |
| C4 Renewed regional disruption | A meaningful recovered district with an actual restored essential facility or route suffers a severe returning passage after at least forty-eight clear hours | +5 | Up to −5 at final season closure | One season receipt. Continuous coverage and duplicate interval processing cannot qualify |
| C5 Global disruption | The actual World of Dust footprint satisfies the global presentation milestone in Part 06 | +10 | Up to −10 after concurrent coverage stays below half of those land and population thresholds for fourteen days, or at final closure | One season receipt shared with the super-event. Presentation adds zero extra |

The maximum positive event-owned total is 35 Chaos for a season that achieves every concrete milestone.
The allowed negative total is bounded by actual awarded receipts and the shared Chaos floor.
Reappearing after a receipt's reversal does not award the same receipt again.
This prevents a split, clear and repeat loop from creating farmable Chaos transactions.

Shared Deaths continues to convert actual civilian and native military deaths according to its existing rules.
Air Cleanliness converts only actual accepted atmospheric change through its existing remainder buffer.
War, annexation or other unrelated shared sources remain independently owned.
The event does not add a second death or pollution bonus under a geographic label.
Reversing a temporary storm-disruption receipt never reverses historical deaths or erases accepted contamination.

Responses contribute to recovery by producing the actual clear, usable or repaired conditions required by the receipt.
Clicking an action, finishing an empty mission or lowering a hidden intensity variable directly cannot claim a reversal.
Current Chaos influences eligibility and physical severity, but it does not multiply every +5 award into a runaway feedback loop.

## Heat and Acid Rain

The bounded direct-risk interaction is defined in Part 04.
The two companion owners supply exact current footprints and severity facts.
An expired effect, a country-level label or a state that was affected last month cannot count as current overlap.
The response panel explains compounded danger where it actually exists and preserves the two-value public budget.
It does not duplicate either companion's management interface.

Acid Rain may make exposed machines and outdoor operations more hazardous, and severe heat can make long exposure more dangerous to people and armies.
The implementation can express those relationships through the proven local channel and protection adapters.
It must not invent a universal combined chemistry system or silently add uncapped damage for every coexisting disaster.

## AI strategy

The AI reads the same current footprint and forecast certainty available to its country.
Its response priorities are expected civilian exposure, intensity and duration, irreplaceable route or industrial capacity, and the immediate needs of its own forces.
It evaluates all affordable candidates in a bounded queue rather than repeatedly attempting an impossible first choice.

| Situation | Intended response ordering | Important restraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poor peaceful country, dense threatened district | Household retreat first, then useful machinery protection if affordable | Never require paid equipment for the basic survival response |
| Major industrial state with strong warning | Machinery preparation and household retreat, with the economic sacrifice made explicit | Do not purchase protection for already absent or fully damaged facilities |
| Essential wartime supply bottleneck | Verify and support the actual corridor, then prepare exposed formations | Preserve fuel and equipment commitments needed for the country's existing operations |
| Small isolated field force with safe withdrawal | Ordinary withdrawal or waiting, with field precautions only if useful | Do not spend scarce Command Power for negligible exposure |
| Exposed concentrated air force | Airfield preservation and reduced operations | Do not damage distant unexposed missions through a global policy substitute |
| Returning branch after recent recovery | Reuse persistent shared protection facts and reprioritize current population and remaining facilities | Do not assume that the original population, stock or repair status is still present |
| Simultaneous heat or Acid Rain | Raise civilian and route caution using exact overlap evidence | Do not pay for the same shared protective issue twice |

Current action costs, forecast validity, authority and remaining useful duration affect each score.
Thresholds and weights belong in a central tunable model and receive scenario inspection.
A proposed policy may not always select the strongest protective action because its opportunity cost can be strategically decisive.
The country should nevertheless consistently react to an approaching extreme wall over millions of residents.

The AI should avoid starting a discretionary local offensive under severe dust when a short wait preserves its objective.
It should retain the ability to retreat, defend an essential corridor or respond to an immediate threat.
Any strategy adapter must affect the relevant geography and preserve ordinary military agency.
The weather is not a tool for forcing every AI country to stop fighting globally.

## Alerts, history and political behavior

Issue a country warning on a genuinely new approach, a material escalation, a distinct returning threat or an important forecast revision.
Deduplicate ordinary motion updates.
A sensible starting alert cooldown is seventy-two hours per country and warning family, with an override for a newly threatened major population area or a newly extreme committed impact.
The override requires a new geographic fact, not another front ID over the same footprint.

The global event log records the root event and actual evolutions through the shared framework.
Operational warnings and exposure updates do not spend fresh event pacing or become hundreds of fired-history rows.
Event Details describes the current form, affected geography, two public values, active choices and actual eligibility.
Historical summaries use the shared Deaths and source history where appropriate rather than introducing an additional cumulative Sandstorm meter.

Natural occurrence does not generate Condemnation against an arbitrary desert owner.
Ordinary diplomacy and military conflict continue under their existing rules.
Relief, donated protection and migration support are attributed to the actual provider through the shared owner, without inventing free foreign stock or guaranteed acceptance.
