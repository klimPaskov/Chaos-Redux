## Accepted current brief

The accepted identity and catalog requirements are U099-01 through U099-07.
The desert origin is dynamically selected from broad suitable regions, including the Sahara, Arabian, Australian, Gobi, Central Asian, and other major deserts.
The initial front is a broad regional phenomenon.
Its visible moving path crosses countries, land, and coasts, carries dust beyond the origin, and can widen, narrow, slow, or accelerate.
Local intensity varies within that footprint.

Both armies suffer disorientation in recognizing friendly and enemy formations, roads and landmarks, defensive lines, attack directions, and artillery targets.
Air operations are disrupted.
Civilian deaths depend on actual population exposure and include suffocation, exposure, accidents, collapsed structures, transport disasters, and people trapped outdoors.
Intensity and exposure duration govern temporary industry disruption and permanent building and railway damage.
The industry description includes factories, engines, railways, workshops, warehouses, machinery, extraction, construction, and supply.
The front and its effects clear finitely.
Shared Natural Disasters behavior and overlaps with Heat Wave and Acid Rain are accepted requirements.

| II, Splinter Storms | 400+ | The main front splits into separate fronts moving in different directions. Some branches weaken quickly and others become major storms. A country may escape the original front and encounter another branch elsewhere. |
