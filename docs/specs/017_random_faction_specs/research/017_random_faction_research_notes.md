# Event 17 research and inspiration notes

These notes provide design inspiration for the planning package. They are not final localisation, and they should not be pasted into the game.

## Historical and thematic anchors

Small-state alliance politics in the interwar period often mixed fear, revisionism, guarantees, and regional pacts. The event draws inspiration from that pattern rather than modeling any one treaty.

- The Little Entente linked Czechoslovakia, Romania, and Yugoslavia after World War I and was aimed against Hungarian revisionism and Habsburg restoration. Source direction: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Entente
- The Balkan Entente or Balkan Pact was signed in 1934 by Greece, Romania, Turkey, and Yugoslavia to maintain the postwar regional status quo. Source direction: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balkan_Pact
- League of Nations collective security is a useful inspiration for faction rhetoric, especially the idea that an aggressor against one member can become a concern to all. Source direction: https://www.ungeneva.org/en/about/league-of-nations/overview
- Nordic neutrality and Swedish neutrality are useful inspirations for countries that try to keep neutrality through policy, trade, military readiness, and caution rather than through indifference. Source direction: https://nordics.info/show/artikel/neutrality

## Design use

The design should use those anchors in broad form:

- local blocs forming because small states fear revisionist neighbors
- governments using guarantees and staff missions as political pressure
- neutrality becoming expensive and active, not passive
- faction leaders treating minor states as corridors, buffers, and symbolic victories
- rival factions responding to one minor's alignment by courting nearby neutrals

## Source caution

If implementation adds exact references, final names, quotes, or real historical claims into localisation, the relevant wording should be researched and cited through the project research workflow before it becomes final text.
