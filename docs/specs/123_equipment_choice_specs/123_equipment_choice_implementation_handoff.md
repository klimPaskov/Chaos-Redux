## Transaction requirements that deserve direct review

The global firing identity, recipient identity and generation, offer identity, selected format, per-format manifest, selected groups, actual delivered row amounts, and origin marker must remain separable.
Before each nested helper, initialize the caller-owned outputs and preserve any index or actor that the helper could overwrite.
Explicitly reset foreach break state and retain unvisited queue records under bounded processing.

The final native confirmation owns an immutable quote until its after-block closes.
Cancel is the first consequence-free option.
An expiry can request automatic resolution but cannot overwrite that visible receipt or execute a competing grant.
Confirmation revalidates legal rows and the binding handling quote.
Its immutable receipt captures the human controller, control mode, and selection authority, with an exact supported native recheck at confirmation.
A changed controller or lost authority supplies no consent. Close the old popup without granting, then retain the country entitlement for the current valid actor or normal AI resolver after closure.
Already committed country-owned cargo retains its original contract.
A changed material quote causes a clean return for a fresh confirmation, preserving frozen cargo.
The AI captures and validates the same quote synchronously.

Freeze the unusual setback result at commitment, with a key containing the firing, recipient generation, and family.
Apply it after the actual unusual consignment completes, including when it is the second staged consignment.
Use each arrival's confirmed maximum ordinary strain and permit a lower actual burden.
Keep premium budget charge separate from unmultiplied physical value.

Partial delivery records actual successful rows before the next row.
Retry only the missing remainder.
Load projection counts actual positive portions at their actual timestamps and only still-undelivered committed quantities at planned timestamps, with each exact row quantity represented once.
No broad stockpile reversal, second full package, successful achievement, or completed-delivery Chaos can conceal a failed grant.
Before any delivery, loss of every distinct second category permits a return to format selection with the same frozen opportunity.
After delivery begins, preserve the original same-group remainder contract.

Apply Part 4's current-use preference filter before AI ranking and the top-two format comparison.
Reserve-only unusual cargo remains legal for human selection, while the AI defers it whenever a currently useful conventional candidate remains.
The score formula and unusual-family draw weights remain unchanged.
