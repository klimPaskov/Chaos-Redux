# Equipment Choice
## Part 3. Overflow, impossible cargo, and consequences

## Evolution entry

Overflowing Arsenals requires at least 200 Chaos.
Impossible Arsenals requires at least 600 Chaos and an enabled, recorded Overflowing Arsenals stage.
They form one ordered evolution track with one mutation at each of the two specified Chaos tiers.
Crossing 400 Chaos does not create an additional evolution.

Once the event has fired, eligible evolutions use the shared MTTH framework.
Use 90 days as the initial MTTH anchor, reduced by ten days per full 100 Chaos above the threshold and by fifteen days when a real Event 123 delivery occurred within the previous 120 days.
Clamp the result to 30 through 90 days.
The recent-delivery test uses the latest completed qualifying Event 123 physical delivery globally.
It includes real forced deliveries and excludes explicit CXT test fixtures.
Use the interval strictly after now minus 120 days and through now, so a delivery exactly 120 days earlier is outside it.
These are timing inputs for the verified shared evolution adapter.
Do not reinterpret them as a guaranteed deadline or publish a cumulative probability without the adapter's timing semantics.
Only the next unrecorded enabled stage can advance.

When the event has never fired, its first opening may immediately resolve the highest fully enabled eligible stage.
At 600+ Chaos with both evolutions enabled, record the two ordered evolution entries once and create the first offer with Impossible Arsenals active.
The first offer then uses the evolved formats and the recipient's eligible unusual-family pool.
At 200 through 599, the opening uses Overflowing Arsenals.

Activation grants no material and changes Chaos by zero.
Only future firings use the changed offer rules.
Every already published offer retains its frozen stage, format options, quantities, and allowed family.
Already delivered equipment remains genuine stock.
Disabling an evolution changes subsequent firings to the highest enabled recorded prefix.
The record of an earlier activation remains history, and re-enabling it does not award activation again.
A disabled first stage prevents the second stage from affecting newly created offers.

## Absorbing the larger deliveries

The absorption allowance A is `6,000 + 300 M + 80 C + 120 K`, using the recipient's uncapped controlled factory and dockyard capacities at the offer snapshot.
This is an internal estimate of national handling capacity.
It is distinct from the capped material allowance, so very large industrial countries can absorb a shipment more easily.

At final confirmation, project load R separately for each scheduled arrival.
Include actual qualifying Event 123 deliveries in its preceding 30-day window, already committed deliveries scheduled within that window, and the current offer's own arrivals, then divide by A.
Count each physical quantity once by its exact consignment and row receipt.
Positive delivered portions enter the actual ledger at their actual timestamps, including portions of an incomplete consignment.
The committed schedule contributes only the still-undelivered remainder at its planned timestamps.
As a portion arrives, replace its scheduled contribution with its actual contribution. Never count the same units in both.
This fixes the maximum ordinary severity and duration accepted for each consignment.
At arrival, a lower actual delivered load may reduce its penalty.
A later offer or an unrelated change cannot make this consignment exceed its confirmed severity or duration.
Each later offer receives its own quote that accounts for the already committed schedule.
Ordinary baseline cargo contributes its full delivered value.
Concentrated and combined immediate cargo also contribute full value.
Combined staged cargo contributes 75% of its real value to this load only.
The 30-day window includes timestamps strictly after arrival time minus 30 days and through the arrival time.
A delivery exactly 30 days earlier is outside it.
Use frozen actual timestamps and one consistent unit across publication, projection, and callbacks.
Older contribution records expire individually from that window.
Gifts from unrelated events do not enter this private load ledger.
Their real shared effects continue normally.

| Load at an evolved arrival | Ordinary handling consequence | Initial duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R at most 1.5 | No ordinary strain | None |
| R above 1.5 and at most 3 | Training time increases 15%, division organisation recovery decreases 10% | 30 days |
| R above 3 | Training time increases 25%, division organisation recovery decreases 15% | 45 days |

At offer creation, a combat group has a new-training flag when none of its compatible primary-family consumers is already fielded by the recipient.
For a combined offer, add five days only when both selected groups are combat groups and both have that flag.
Subtract five days when either selected group is a logistics profile.
A concentrated offer cannot receive the two-group addition.
Clamp the resulting ordinary strain duration to 20 through 60 days.
Show the exact projected severity, effects, and duration with the final confirmation.
For staged delivery, show both arrival quotes and use the greater projected load for its single unusual-setback chance.
All coefficient names and modifier keys need exact installed-reference confirmation during implementation.
The percentages here are gameplay magnitudes.

The load window makes unusually frequent firings matter without introducing another public meter.
A country receiving only one normal baseline shipment does not acquire an automatic penalty.
The ordinary overload check begins with evolved offers.
A new valid arrival may refresh the relevant condition, with its own bounded duration.
Repeated UI clicks cannot refresh it.

Construct a candidate's concentrated, combined immediate, and combined staged manifests from their respective allowances.
One eligible family is selected for the current unusual group, retaining those three frozen manifests.
Changing format never draws a different family.
The selection is frozen before the player views the offer.
The same source pool and opportunity rules apply to humans and AI.
The pool is evaluated separately for each recipient because practical fielding and existing rights can differ.

The six families are the adopted design roster.
Their current source definitions and existing fielding receipts are documented in the source evidence file.
Current model-owner repair and acceptance work remains a separate readiness limitation.
Implementation must not equate a registry entry with a fully accepted visual package.

A received unusual family may use the existing battalion-only hidden receipt and editable neutral template seam.
It must enable only the accepted consumer and create the missing template once.
An existing template or receipt from Equipment from Heavens remains valid and is not duplicated.
Manpower, training time, fuel, supply, and deployment remain ordinary recipient responsibilities.

The grant must preserve production locks, project completion, originating-event flags, native strategic permissions, and existing owner suspension rules.
Do not call the Event 042 aggregate special grant or an Event 016 operational-technology grant.
Those existing orchestration paths have consequences outside a finite Equipment Choice delivery.
The implementation handoff identifies the precise reusable seam and the side effects that must remain isolated.

Clone cohorts are outside the adopted roster because their stored equipment can create recurring reserve manpower.
Repeated worldwide gifts would require a separate long-run population design.
Alien laser weapons require a contact and cohort contract beyond this finite stock-and-consumer design.

These are equipment-pool decisions, with the actual six-family unusual opportunity preserved.

A family shown in either of the recipient's previous two natural offers has its weight multiplied by 0.25.

Apply legal eligibility first, then those factors.

## Temporary unusual setbacks

A strange cargo has one disclosed risk check keyed by firing, recipient generation, and selected unusual family.
Freeze its result when the complete delivery quote is committed and apply it only when the unusual consignment itself has physically completed.
This remains true when unusual cargo is the second staged category.
A preceding conventional consignment cannot trigger or consume the check.
The initial chance is 25%, with ten percentage points added for combined immediate delivery and ten added when the projected ordinary load exceeds 1.5.
Cap it at 45%.
Combined staged delivery uses the 25% base and adds five points when its discounted load exceeds 1.5, capped at 30%.
A callback for the same unusual consignment cannot roll the family's setback again.
The final confirmed quote fixes the chance and maximum duration, including the greater of the two staged arrival projections.
Partial physical delivery retains the frozen result without applying a completed-cargo setback early.
Freeze the random result in the committed receipt.

## One handling condition with precise expiry

Ordinary strain and an unusual setback share one visible event-owned handling condition.
Its tooltip lists the active components and their individual expiry dates.
For the same effect, apply the strongest active magnitude, with training-time increase capped at 30%, organisation-recovery reduction at 20%, supply-consumption increase at 10%, planning-speed reduction at 20%, and the repair-speed reduction at 15%.
Do not add identical penalties from repeated receipts.
Each component retains its own expiry, so a short penalty cannot persist merely because a different condition lasts longer.

Only an actual new delivery may add or refresh a component.
Expiry removes the owned component and refreshes the remaining combined modifier immediately.
When the last component ends, remove the condition and hide the empty category unless another offer or staged arrival remains.
Political change and evolution toggles do not reset the timer.
Recipient death removes future callbacks and transient condition state for that exact country generation.
