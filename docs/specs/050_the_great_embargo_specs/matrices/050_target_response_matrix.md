# Event 050 target response matrix

All names are working labels.

| Response | Availability | Primary costs | Duration | Pressure effect | Economic effect | Failure risk | AI profile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Self-Sufficiency | Opening onward when a useful project exists | Civilian capacity, construction, trains or support equipment, possible fuel | 120 to 180 days | Modest fall when leverage is removed | Durable mitigation in one exposure family | Delay, lost state, opportunity cost | Import-dependent states with construction room |
| Smuggling Networks | Opening onward with a valid route | Convoys, fuel, equipment, civilian burden, exposure | 90 to 150 days | Fall on clean success, rise on exposure | Fast material relief | Secondary sanctions and host defection | Targets with routes and risk tolerance |
| Neutral Intermediaries | Opening onward with a useful neutral | Commercial concession, civilian burden, access, convoys | Negotiated, then route establishment | Fall when route weakens enforcement | Reliable relief at a lasting price | Refusal, disclosure, route closure | Weak or import-dependent targets |
| Diplomatic Concessions | Opening onward with a receptive core participant | Policy, access, guarantee, compensation, withdrawal | Negotiation and settlement window | Medium or large fall on acceptance | Can end the crisis | Hardliner rejection and loss of leverage | Weak targets or limited-cost settlement |
| Defy the World | Opening onward with minimum political viability | Civilian welfare, construction, future exhaustion, political cost | Strategic commitment | Usually stable or higher at first | Temporary mitigation and mobilization | Long-term exhaustion and coalition unity | Strong ideological or self-sufficient targets |
| Resource Seizure | Severe Pressure or critical shortage with valid target | Troops, equipment, fuel, supply, diplomatic escalation | 120 to 180 day preparation | Rises when public | Potential access through coercion or war | Suicidal conflict or coalition intervention | Strong aggressive targets with reachable resources |

## Opening mission matrix

| Outcome | Proof | Immediate result | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full success | Credible route before severe Pressure | Pressure reduction and mitigation | Stronger version of successful response family |
| Partial success | Route opens late or covers one shortage only | Limited relief | Crisis remains severe and follow-up action appears |
| Failure | No viable route by deadline | Pressure rise and focused economic penalty | Emergency project, concession, defiance, or seizure becomes urgent |

## Phase visibility

| Phase | Visible primary actions | Active mission cap | Replacement behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shock | Three to five opening responses | 1 | Selected-target views prevent action overflow |
| Adaptation | Current projects and useful response families | 1 | Completed setup actions become status or upgrade actions |
| Severe isolation | Emergency project, active route, settlement, defiance, possible seizure | 1 | Resource Seizure replaces a weak or invalid opening action |
| Coalition fracture | Coalition-splitting and settlement actions | 1 | Failed neutral targets disappear |
| Resolution | Only obligations and closure actions | 0 to 1 | All ordinary setup decisions hide |

## Cost quality gate

| Rule | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Cost type cap | No more than four spendable cost types on one action |
| Political power | Used only when the action is genuinely political or administrative |
| Equipment and logistics | Match the action and current route |
| Custom values | Embargo Pressure is a consequence or threshold, not a spendable currency |
| Requirements | Named states, routes, ports, resources, and targets appear in clear tooltips |
| AI | Preserves minimum survival stockpiles before paying |
