## High-level route map

| Step | Target experience | Coalition experience | Neutral experience | State change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Selection | One valid player country or major is selected | Convenor and core enforcers are chosen | Useful neutral routes are identified | Fresh crisis ledger begins |
| Opening | Trade access and foreign support contract | Members choose commitment level | Important neutrals see opportunity and risk | Opening Pressure and duration are set |
| First response | Target selects adaptation, evasion, settlement, or defiance | Coalition prepares first review | Selected neutral can accept intermediary talks | Replacement-supply mission begins |
| Adaptation | Projects and routes start producing results | Enforcement costs become visible | Traders profit, refuse, or expose offers | Pressure moves in meaningful steps |
| Consolidation or fracture | Severe isolation or coalition weakness becomes clear | Hardliners and commercial members diverge | Neutrals can become decisive | Stage and duration update |
| Resolution | Target survives, concedes, escalates, or becomes invalid | Coalition dissolves or records settlement | Temporary routes close or become contracts | Crisis-owned state clears |

## Target strategy map

| Strategy | Primary response | Speed | Reliability | Main cost | Main risk | Typical ending |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Adaptation | Self-Sufficiency | Slow | High | Construction and industrial capacity | Project delay and opportunity cost | Endurance or coalition collapse |
| Evasion | Smuggling Networks | Fast | Variable | Convoys, equipment, fuel, exposure | Public discovery and secondary sanctions | Porous embargo or coalition fracture |
| Commercial diplomacy | Neutral Intermediaries | Medium | Medium to high | Contracts, access, civilian burden | Intermediary defection or pressure | Replacement route and negotiated exit |
| Political settlement | Diplomatic Concessions | Medium | High when coalition is receptive | Lasting policy or strategic concession | Hardliner rejection and loss of leverage | Negotiated settlement |
| Mobilization | Defy the World | Fast | Depends on regime and resources | Civilian welfare and future exhaustion | Coalition consolidation | Defiant endurance or escalation |
| Expansion | Resource Seizure | Slow preparation | High variance | Military stockpile, fuel, troops, diplomacy | Major war | Wartime blockade or resource access |

## Event surface map

| Surface | Role | Frequency | Primary audience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entry event | Builds a valid crisis transaction | Once per firing | Hidden or limited presentation |
| Target opening report | Explains isolation and opens decisions | Once per target | Target |
| Core participant choice | Sets commitment | Opening and major reviews only | Important participants |
| Neutral intermediary event | Resolves a selected offer | Per selected target | Neutral or target |
| Threshold report | Marks first arrival at a major stage | Once per stage per crisis | Target |
| Coalition review report | Announces material renewal, fracture, or settlement | Only when a review changes the crisis | Relevant actors |
| Evolution report | Activates evolved behavior after pacing | Once per evolution | Relevant target or world context |
| Resolution report | Explains the outcome and aftermath | Once per crisis | Target and selected outside actors |
| Event history | Records the firing and primary target | Once per firing | Event Logs |
| Evolution history | Records each evolution once | Once per evolution | Evolutions and related history |
