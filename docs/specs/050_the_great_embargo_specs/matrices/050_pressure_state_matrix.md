# Event 050 Pressure state matrix

Embargo Pressure is the only persistent event-specific value shown to the player.

| Range | Working stage label | Coalition condition | Low-dependence target | High-dependence target | Primary player response | Review behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `0` | Embargo broken | Enforcement has failed | Restrictions close | Restrictions close | Resolve aftermath | Begin cleanup |
| `1-19` | Collapsing coalition | Too few effective enforcers or widespread defection | Residual friction | Limited shortages can remain | Secure final defection or settlement | Require two low reviews unless a decisive action ends it |
| `20-39` | Porous embargo | Important leakage and neutral routes exist | Mild loss of flexibility | Noticeable resource and shipping friction | Expand routes and split members | Fatigue grows quickly |
| `40-59` | Coordinated restrictions | Coalition functions | Moderate output and support loss | Serious production choices | Establish replacement supply | Normal review cadence |
| `60-79` | Severe isolation | Key suppliers and routes comply | Broad inefficiency and diplomatic loss | Major fuel and material crisis | Emergency adaptation, concessions, or defiance | Hardliner and neutral actions gain weight |
| `80-100` | Near-total isolation | Most important routes are closed or punished | Strong but survivable isolation | Critical operational emergency | Endure, settle, or prepare Resource Seizure | Maximum enforcement and fatigue pressure |

## Opening Pressure factors

| Factor | Direction | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Major convenor | Up | Greater diplomatic reach |
| Principal resource supplier joins | Strongly up | Removes a meaningful replacement source |
| Nearby corridor state joins | Up | Closes practical land access |
| Major shipping state joins | Up | Increases maritime enforcement |
| Strong target faction refuses | Down | Preserves support and routes |
| Public Condemnation | Up | Broadens willingness to enforce |
| Weak public justification | Down | Increases conditional participation |
| Convenor losing a major war | Down | Reduces attention and credibility |
| Evolution I active | Up | Neutral pressure begins earlier |
| Several profitable neutrals remain outside | Down | Creates immediate leakage |

## Pressure rise events

| Outcome | Expected magnitude | Repeat rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core enforcer joins | Large | Once per member entry |
| Major supplier closes access | Large | Once while the route remains closed |
| Smuggling route exposed | Medium to large | Per unique route |
| Neutral accepts secondary sanctions | Medium | Per important neutral |
| Target refuses supported settlement | Medium | Once per distinct settlement package |
| Enforcement renewed | Small to medium | Bounded by review and current stage |
| Resource-seizure preparation exposed | Medium | Once per crisis |

## Pressure fall events

| Outcome | Expected magnitude | Repeat rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core enforcer leaves | Large | Once per member exit |
| Important intermediary route stabilizes | Medium | Once per route establishment |
| Self-sufficiency removes a critical leverage point | Small to medium | Once per completed project stage |
| Major concession accepted | Medium to large | Per distinct obligation |
| Coalition review fractures | Large | Once per fracture outcome |
| Convenor becomes invalid and replacement fails | Large | Once |
| Fatigue cascade | Medium to large | Bounded review outcome |

## Hidden dependence profiles

| Profile | Typical evidence | Damage expectation at Pressure 60 | Preferred response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Low | Domestic resources, strong land access, deep stockpiles | Strong diplomatic and efficiency pressure, limited critical shortages | Defiance, diplomacy, selective projects |
| Moderate | Several deficits with substitutes or allies | Material production friction and fuel planning | Mixed adaptation and intermediary play |
| High | Several important deficits, convoy dependence | Serious production and operational losses | Intermediaries, smuggling, projects, concessions |
| Critical | Essential deficit, low reserves, weak routes | Immediate military and economic emergency | Fast route, settlement, emergency project, possible seizure |

## Duration bands

| Coalition type | Expected duration | Typical exit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fragile | 180 to 300 days | Early fracture or limited settlement |
| Normal | 270 to 540 days | Mixed adaptation and fatigue |
| Highly unified | 450 to 600 days | Major concessions, endurance, or escalation |
| Hard cap | Around 720 days | Mandatory final review and resolution |
