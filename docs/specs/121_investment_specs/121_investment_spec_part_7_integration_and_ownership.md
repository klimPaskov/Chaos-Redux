## 7.2 Identity and owned state

A charter belongs to its original paying country. Its durable key includes actor identity and a never-reused charter generation. A site obligation adds the state, phase, amendment generation and accepted payment receipt. Province work retains the actual province, and a railway obligation retains its verified path.

Keep these records distinct within bounded owner storage:

| Record | Required content |
|---|---|
| Charter | Original payer, charter generation, sector, evolution state, accepted scope, initial usable-capacity snapshot, terminal state |
| Site | State and any province or path, original payer, current holder, site generation, phase, paid work, remaining work, delivery legality |
| Authorization | Accepted quote including legitimate zero political cost, integer hold, exact phase or service purpose, actual debits, accounting dates, active proof |
| Delivery | Specific promised output, in-progress claim, observed output delta, durable success, source authorization |
| Incident | Immutable checkpoint ID, roll and truth, disclosure, response deadline, audit consumption, resolution |
| Exposure | Source identity, whether already applied, actual applied and remaining residual, highest accepted policy risk, critical-warning elapsed days |
| License | Method, beneficiary, original expiry, one extension receipt, service proof, contribution identity, warning and cleanup state |
| Distress recovery | Original surviving payer, consequence and distress receipt, original expiry, one attempt, actual political authorization and recovery hold |
| Terminal record | Outcome, own release, earned outputs, salvage use, history and achievement attribution, external acknowledgment |

A callback authenticates the full applicable key. A menu selection, event target or state match cannot distinguish cancelled work from a later project.

Amendment invalidates superseded actions while retaining outputs, paid work, checkpoints and audit quotas. Fresh authorization resumes remaining work without restoring consumed proof or stale callbacks.

Retain compact terminal proof, reuse bounded storage through generations and leave narrative history with the shared owner.

Exposure is one public sum backed by bounded internal source residuals. Every addition records only room actually available beneath raw 100 and marks its source consumed even if clipped to zero. Inspection consumes up to 15 existing procurement points in order: oldest unresolved ordinary incident, context, policy, then Fever. It cannot bank a reduction or consume future, method, ambient, malfunction, renewal or hazardous-continuation risk. Correction removes only that source's remaining applied residual. Full-risk operation imposes a display floor of 50 without changing the underlying residuals. Validation retires its remaining method uncertainty and floor, then uses the smaller contribution only while the license remains live. Fault repair retires only its own remaining residual. Never subtract nominal 20 or 10 when less was applied.

Context eligibility is remembered once per charter. A new amendment can introduce a previously absent context, while policy risk increases only above the highest previously accepted policy level. Scope removal, recapture and safer future policy do not automatically subtract these sources. Preserve the 90-day critical financing warning's qualifying elapsed time across pauses. On failure, settle actual losses and evaluate the preserved warning and material threshold before retiring risk or cleaning the case.

## 7.3 Transaction and output contract

Payment uses one pure preflight, accepted quote and transaction path. Immediately before mutation, repeat inclusive affordability, site control and delivery checks.

Authorization reserves capacity. Eligible time under that actual hold produces paid work. Quotes, missions and flags alone earn nothing. Allocate and round prospective phase work under Part 4, then freeze it before acceptance.

Accepted quotes retain Part 2's exact site-context factors and duration envelope. Non-core status, infrastructure below half the native maximum and payer war each contribute 10%, summed up to 30% and applied once after sector and policy factors. Later callbacks cannot reprice accepted work or add an undeclared supply multiplier.

A completion follows this order:

1. Authenticate actor, charter, site, phase, generation, accepted branch and actual paid work.
2. Establish an in-progress delivery claim for each undelivered output.
3. Recheck current delivery legality and execute the specific legal grant.
4. Observe the actual output or resource change and store durable success for that delivery.
5. Consume its active proof and settle the corresponding reservation and phase state.

The claim prevents reentry without proving success. Observe the promised delta and record each bundle component separately, so retry resolves only the undelivered remainder.

Completion checks paid work and present delivery legality. It must not reapply the original purchase balance or require the site's original empty-slot count after another paid component has already been delivered. A slot consumed by unrelated construction requires the bounded relocation or smaller legal settlement in Part 4. Never record success for an illegal or missing facility.

Freeze the exclusive native or alternative facility branch at acceptance. Deliver only its declared buildings and bonuses, without implied project completion, equipment or scientists.

Materials Fever owns one, two or three legal refineries, two principal allocations each, remaining civilian support and quoted infrastructure. Engineering owns the corresponding facilities or two-factory alternatives under the same allocation budget. A self-assembly promise uses frozen ordinary U or mega B, independently of its native or no-DLC representation. Its extras are civilian for civilian and special routes, military for military, and dockyards for naval. Freeze legal placements within existing charter sites. No extra facility, refinery, research coupon or support work can be inferred.

The whole currently agreed conventional scope supporting the method and prototype validation must be paid and commissioned before licensed activation. A reduced scope lowers its unearned U/B basis. At conventional commissioning, the first-service window is the smaller of 90 calendar days and the flagship site's retained unused portion of its cumulative 180-day dormancy allowance. Waiting consumes that same flagship allowance. Other commissioned sites supply no additional grace. Zero remainder retires the unstarted method immediately. Actual funded activation inside the window starts the 180-day license. Expiry of the pending window retires an unstarted method without an operating premium or coupon.

Only actually debited, separately refundable material may be returned, once, to its valid original payer. Spent civilian-factory-days are not refundable. Brownfield reuse requires the original payer, the same site, corresponding verified reusable work and a once-consumed receipt. Its maximum contribution is 25% of the later corresponding work. Inherited credit cannot create new credit.

Every wrapper preinitializes outputs, including nested boundaries. Validate persistent scope identity. A permissive existence check or callee-created temporary cannot supply that proof.

## 7.4 Independent capacity reservation

Event 34's `industrial_boom_start_reserve` freezes its quote, starts Boom-owned state and changes Overheating. `industrial_boom_finish_reserve` clears that reserve and can grant a Boom benefit. They are not generic capacity services. Likewise, its project and protection helpers own their own flags, state commitments and lifecycle. [Event 34 action effects](https://github.com/klimPaskov/Chaos-Redux/blob/41bc4e5045a7b5c131be69dda2f08079d478634c/common/scripted_effects/034_industrial_boom_action_effects.txt) and [region effects](https://github.com/klimPaskov/Chaos-Redux/blob/41bc4e5045a7b5c131be69dda2f08079d478634c/common/scripted_effects/034_industrial_boom_region_effects.txt).

Investment therefore owns a recomputed aggregate of authenticated construction and service holds. Expose that aggregate through its own native `civilian_factory_use` modifier. Remove or refresh only this modifier. Zero aggregate removes it. A terminal site clears only the exact contribution that belongs to that site and generation.

Availability must use a verified read of genuinely usable civilians after consumer goods and existing reservations. Do not subtract another owner's hold twice if the native read already excludes it. Replacing an Investment hold must account for its existing contribution exactly once. Affordability accepts equality. A positive commitment flag cannot conceal zero actual held capacity.

Recompute the aggregate after acceptance, phase completion, amendment, capture, warning, service renewal and terminal release. Accrual uses the authorized actual allocation. If capacity falls below it, pause that allocation until a restored or revised hold is authorized. Do not reconstruct solvency by adding a nominal hold to a clamped free-capacity value.

Service and distress recovery are distinct paid purposes. Neither can use a construction receipt as its authorization. Construction, service and the single residual recovery job share the Investment reservation family through separately authenticated contributions. Recompute that aggregate when recovery starts, pauses or ends, even after the charter has closed.

The inspected Boom reserve decision does not supply the required explicit free-capacity preflight. Source acceptance must establish Investment's own adapter instead of treating the precedent as proof of affordability. Native acceptance then checks the displayed usable capacity and actual construction opportunity cost.

## 7.5 Dates, bounded work and cleanup

Store canonical absolute dates for proposal expiry, phase windows, incidents, license expiry, warning deadlines and aftermath expiry. Native mission durations and `days =` values remain day counts.

The current shared date helpers are `chaosx_convert_days_to_date_offset` and `chaosx_convert_date_offset_to_days`. For the first, set `chaosx_date_days` and preinitialize output `chaosx_date_offset`. For the second, set the date difference in `chaosx_date_offset` and preinitialize output `chaosx_date_days`. The documented encoding is 0.00001 per hour and 0.00024 per day. The first rounds fractional days up to an hour. The reverse rounds future differences up to whole days and floors past dates at zero. Never add raw days to `global.date` or convert an already encoded date again. [Current shared-helper source](121_investment_sources_and_review.md#source-register).

Use one bounded daily owner worker over at most four registered sites. It accounts only for eligible funded intervals since the stored accounting point. Duplicate wakeups cannot add work twice. Capture and expiry boundaries clip the eligible interval before completion is evaluated.

A delayed callback is a wake hint. The dispatcher must authenticate the stored job and its generation before performing a mutation. It must not infer authority from whichever project happens to occupy the site when the callback returns. Serialize worker scheduling so a duplicate wake cannot create another recurring chain.

Dormant time is cumulative per site, capped at 180 inactive days. Shortage, occupation and unresolved incident time overlap rather than add twice. Funding and incident response windows of 90 days plus the allowed 30-day extension remain inside that cap. Resumption preserves the accumulated value.

Operating reviews count 30 and 90 qualifying funded days, with a 180-day review only under a paid extension. Calendar expiry remains absolute at 180 or 360 days and settles before a coincident day-180 hazard. Paid validation, repair and containment do not advance the operating-day count.

A warning pauses anomalous output and service. Its 30-calendar-day deadline governs response acceptance and payment. Authenticated containment or validation retires that deadline and creates a 30-funded-day job with a 60-calendar-day hard deadline. Output remains off. Cleanup can finish after license expiry without extending operation, and validation completed after expiry cannot restart it. Failure defaults to emergency closure. A valid same-case containment or validation political authorization already covers its fee, including a legitimate zero quote. Otherwise charge the smaller of the exact current ordinary-cost quote from base 25 and nonnegative available PP.

Malfunction repair similarly needs 30 funded service days within 60 calendar days, with output off and no PP fee. Retire its remaining applied fault residual only after observed repair. Quote additional unfinished anomalous rework separately. Validation may include that correction once. A day-30 self-assembly fault retains its coupon and check until paid correction. Disappearance, cascade or license expiry retires the pending coupon. Late cleanup cannot grant it or reroll the check.

End daily work when construction, licensed service and due owner work are all finished. Finite dividends or distress may use native expiry plus a bounded exact-date cleanup callback. They do not justify an endless daily loop or repeated world scan. Terminal cleanup invalidates queued work, releases own holds and preserves real buildings, durable receipts, earned history and legitimate finite aftermath.

## 7.6 Capture, transfer and foreign scope

| Change | Required settlement |
|---|---|
| Temporary loss of control | Pause the affected unfinished site, release its allocation, retain paid work and dates |
| Recapture before retirement | Revalidate the site and obtain fresh phase authorization for remaining work, with no new subsidy or reset grace |
| Permanent ownership transfer | Retire the original payer's unearned obligations there and retain already real buildings |
| Annexation of payer | Retire its charter and tombstone callbacks through owner and registered-state records |
| Permanent transfer of licensed site | Remove former beneficiary effects, then allow a legal new owner-controller to assume remaining service with fresh proof |

Other eligible sites in a multi-state charter continue. A lost site does not authorize cancelling unrelated sites or another country's charter. Ordinary dividends require continuing control of the qualifying site and retain their original expiry on recapture.

Fever has one program dividend receipt. Early sites grant physical assets alone. Full mandatory-network commissioning grants the route amplitude for 180 days. Agreed reduced terminal commissioning with a usable principal site grants it for 90 days. Failure or abandonment without that settlement grants none. Freeze the retained qualifying site set at terminal commissioning. Every retained site must remain controlled for the dividend to operate, with no restarted expiry after recapture.

Engineering throughput contributes licensed research speed: inspected +10% ordinary or +15% mega, full-risk +20% ordinary or +30% mega, in both capability modes. The existing dividend family applies only the strongest currently valid same-type contribution, including the ordinary +5% Engineering dividend. Custody, real service, pause and expiry govern the licensed contribution separately. Two extra one-use 50% industry research bonuses keep a distinct once-only anomaly-commissioning receipt and never replace or renew the ongoing benefit.

Service assumption requires no conflicting active Investment charter or anomaly service and uses the new beneficiary's charter slot. It inherits no pending building coupon, one-use research reward or purchase. Original construction, operating-day, incident and reward receipts cannot qualify successor achievements. Assumption never restarts expiry, restores the extension allowance or recovers the old payer's costs.

Keep the source's cumulative review history separate from beneficiary achievement proof. Part 5's stewardship requires 180 consecutive actual operating days and resets on any pause. That reset never restores consumed reviews or extends the original 360-day ceiling.

Callbacks must retain explicit original payer and recipient identities. In the documented annexation context, ROOT is the winner and FROM is the annexed country. In state-control change context, ROOT is the new controller, FROM is the old controller and FROM.FROM identifies the state. Confirm the exact callback binding in the current on-action owner during implementation. Never debit, refund or clean ROOT merely because it is present.

Freeze required scope identities into recipient-owned storage before nested effects. The gateway's own `great_depression_capture_call_scope_inputs` demonstrates this requirement because regular event targets can be overwritten by nested chains. The same rule applies to Investment helpers and aftermath consumers.

### Bounded settlement

A temporary local distress bridge lasts at most 90 days from the original failure and stops if Event 35 accepts ownership of the same effects.
