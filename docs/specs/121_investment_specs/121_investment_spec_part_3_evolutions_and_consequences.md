# Investment specification, part 3: Evolutions and consequences

## 1. Escalation expands an agreement

The two evolutions offer larger commitments and unfamiliar returns. They do not turn the ordinary paid phases into additional evolutions or silently replace a safe agreement with a dangerous one. A government can finish the scope it accepted.

Every name in this part is a working design label. Mechanical thresholds, numeric ranges and effect packages are authored proposals. They require the source and native checks described in part 6. The historical research grounds conventional investment, while the impossible methods are fictional.

| Current Chaos | Available investment direction |
|---|---|
| 0–199 | Ordinary four-sector charters |
| 200–399 | Megaproject Fever offers, subject to real capacity and multi-state geography |
| 400–599 | The same Fever evolution, with greater willingness to propose large linked programs |
| 600–799 | Impossible Industry methods, including inspected prototypes and full-risk operation |
| 800–999 | The same impossible evolution, with +10 ambient Exposure for anomalous methods |
| 1000+ | The same impossible evolution, with +20 ambient Exposure instead of +10 |

The higher bands do not invent extra evolution stages. Their ambient Exposure affects an anomalous agreement, not a conventional factory project merely because the world has become chaotic. The current six Chaos bands and the zero-Chaos rule for unlocking an evolution come from the supplied mechanics and event integration sources. [Source register](121_investment_sources_and_review.md#source-register).

## 2. Active evolution and pre-fire evolved opening

### Active charters

After the country reaches the relevant enabled threshold, an active charter may receive one offer for that evolution. A 90-day MTTH is the starting timing anchor. Relevant spare capacity and suitable linked sites can shorten it by a 0.75 factor. A later eligible Chaos band can apply another 0.75 factor. War disruption or a nearly finished charter can lengthen it by 1.25 or 1.5. The final MTTH behavior must be inspected in the installed engine before reporting a cumulative probability.

The offer is an amendment to unpaid future work. A Fever amendment can add linked sites or a second construction sequence. An Impossible amendment can add a prototype to the flagship site. Accepted existing phases, already consumed issue checkpoints, actual payments, site custody and completed buildings remain valid.

If too little ordinary work remains for a sensible amendment, the offer can be retained as a paid follow-on stage after ordinary commissioning. The country must accept that extension while the original charter is still live. It remains the same independent firing and carries its history forward. Declining allows normal closure and cooldown. The offer itself does not delay delivery or hold civilian factories.

An amendment preview distinguishes completed original scope, paid unfinished scope and proposed new scope. Only the last category receives the new purchase quote. A government must not repay its completed factory because it accepted a new research wing.

### Pre-fire evolved openings

When a country is selected at a higher eligible tier, the opening already knows the highest enabled available evolution. It offers the relevant scale or method alongside ordinary construction. A one-state minor at Chaos 700 can still choose its Small ordinary investment even when it cannot host a two-state megaproject.

The shared history records the actual evolved opening once with the selected actor and correct starting stage. Access to a lower stage is not proof that a separate lower-stage activation happened. Follow the shared recorder's supported starting-stage contract and do not fabricate a second activation to fill a sequence.

Check the evolution-enabled setting before writing unlock, offer or recorded flags. When evolution is disabled before an offer, only the baseline is armed. If settings change after a funded extension exists, honor the existing paid obligations and expose all safe completion and closure controls. Suppress new extensions and new renewals where the setting forbids them. Disabling a feature must not strand a paid project or delete its buildings.

## 3. Megaproject Fever at Chaos 200+

### 3.1 Larger promises and linked sites

Fever turns one investment into a coordinated program. The country can build an industrial district across neighboring states, connect factories through a transport corridor, expand a coastal production complex or establish a research and materials network. The larger returns depend on multiple useful sites and coherent delivery.

| Envelope | Normal held civilians | Principal allocation ceiling | Sites | Nominal funded days |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Regional | 3 | 4 | 2 | 480 |
| National | 6 | 8 | 3 | 600 |
| Grand | 10 | 12 | 4 | 720 |

Policy and site factors modify these anchors. Special networks apply the 1.5 work multiplier. Quote each phase with the rounding rules in part 2. A typical program should remain within 360–1,440 expected days after scaling and complexity. A proposed schedule outside that envelope needs a smaller offered scope or a documented exceptional balance decision before purchase.

The principal ceiling is a finite output budget, B equal to 4, 8 or 12. It includes every civilian, military, dockyard and refinery output attributed to the conventional program. A refinery, special facility or exclusive engineering alternative bundle consumes two allocations, with the remaining allocations available for supporting factories. The no-DLC alternative's two factories therefore count toward the conventional building ceiling. Infrastructure and verified transport works have separately quoted work shares and bounded counts.

The player chooses sequential or parallel delivery. Sequential delivery concentrates the available hold on the first site, commissions it, then proceeds through the ordered sites. It reduces simultaneous unfinished exposure and provides earlier usable assets. Parallel delivery distributes a larger hold among up to four legal sites and aims for a shorter final opening. It risks leaving several unfinished sites when the government loses capacity or the lead contractor fails.

The summed actual hold is always the displayed country commitment. Each site receives a positive integer allocation only while funded. No fractional factory is assigned to each of four sites when the country has only one usable civilian factory. The Regional envelope's three-factory hold can fund two sites through a 2+1 allocation, with the work plan reflecting different completion dates.

### 3.2 Sector forms

| Form | Concrete conventional output | Distinct strategic value |
|---|---|---|
| Civilian industrial district | Up to the envelope ceiling in civilian factories, distributed across the agreed sites, plus one infrastructure level per site | A large durable increase in ordinary industrial capacity |
| Transport and fabrication corridor | Three-quarters of the ceiling in civilian factories, rounded down, one infrastructure level per site and up to six verified existing railway edges improved by one level | Sacrifices some factory count for a real connected route between production and supply |
| Military production chain | About three-quarters of principal allocations in military factories and the remainder in civilian supplier factories, with the exact integer split frozen | Expands armament capacity while supporting its industrial base |
| Naval construction complex | At least three-quarters of principal allocations in dockyards, with remaining allocations in civilian supplier factories, plus one port level at each quoted coastal site | Combines coastal yards and supporting industry without requiring every program state to be coastal |
| Strategic materials network | R equal to one, two or three refineries for Regional, National or Grand, using two allocations each, plus B−2R civilian support factories and one infrastructure level per quoted site | Provides real refinery capacity and the industrial support needed to use it |
| Experimental engineering network | F equal to one, two or three native facilities or exclusive engineering alternatives for Regional, National or Grand, using two allocations each, plus B−2F civilian support factories and one infrastructure level per quoted site | Builds a limited network of specialized sites with real support and appropriate native or alternative capability |

The transport corridor uses actual existing supply endpoints and railway geometry. Six edges is a ceiling, not a promise to upgrade an arbitrary path of any length. The offer shows the verified route. When geography cannot support that route, offer the industrial district or a smaller quoted corridor. A decorative line on a map does not satisfy the output.

A naval complex needs at least one legal coastal yard state. An inland supplier state can host civilian support factories. Dockyards and port levels remain in legal coastal locations. Capacity must fit those states before purchase. A program cannot distribute twelve dockyards among inland states to satisfy a numerical ceiling.

A research network's facility types follow the selected branch and the installed game's one-facility-group-per-state restrictions. Three research facilities require three legal states. The network awards the base two industrial research bonuses once for the whole program, not twice per facility. Its native and alternative outputs remain mutually exclusive per accepted capability mode.

Materials networks use actual refinery construction permissions and available capacity. Their promised refinery and support output is therefore 1 refinery plus 2 civilian factories, 2 plus 4, or 3 plus 6, before the separately specified infrastructure. Both special networks use the 1.5 work multiplier. No refinery is treated as a special-project facility, and a facility award cannot satisfy a promised refinery.

### 3.3 Coordination and partial success

Each site retains its own three phase receipts and earned outputs. The public Work Complete value aggregates their accepted work. Site rows show qualitative status, such as funded, ready for commissioning, commissioned or suspended. The interface does not add four independent progress gauges.

The program has three single-use incident checkpoints at 25%, 50% and 75% of total accepted work. They belong to the charter, not to each site. Adding a site cannot reset or duplicate them. A program amended from an ordinary charter preserves consumed ordinary checkpoints and receives only enough unconsumed additional checks to reach the three-check ceiling.

Before parallel funding, the proposal explains how much capacity is exposed at once and which sites could open independently. Each early site provides its physical assets without starting a separate dividend. One program-level receipt grants the selected Part 2 route's dividend amplitude for 180 days when all mandatory sites and links finish. An explicitly agreed reduced terminal commissioning with at least one usable principal site grants the same amplitude for 90 days. Abandonment without such commissioning grants no dividend. Freeze the qualifying retained site set at that terminal result. The contribution requires control of all those sites, pauses on loss and never restarts its expiry. Partial work cannot claim a full-program achievement.

## 4. Ordinary trouble and the path to economic failure

### 4.0 Exact Exposure transitions

Exposure is one public value backed by bounded source records. Initial policy contributions are 10, 25 and 40, with Fever adding 15 once. The following contextual additions apply when first included in accepted scope, with a combined contextual cap of 30.

| Accepted context | Once-per-charter addition |
|---|---:|
| At least one selected site is non-core | +10 |
| At least one selected land site borders hostile-controlled land | +10 |
| Peak accepted total Investment hold is at least 50% of usable civilian capacity frozen at initial charter acceptance | +10 |

A later accepted amendment can introduce a previously absent context once. Removing and reselecting a site, recapture, resumption and callbacks cannot repeat an addition or automatically subtract it. A policy upgrade adds only the difference above the highest policy risk previously accepted. A safer future policy does not erase the risk already accepted.

Each ordinary incident also has one unresolved contribution: delay +5, overrun +10 and a corruption case +20. The corruption-case amount is the same while its hidden truth remains unknown. Freeze and apply that addition once with the case. Its prescribed corrective work must be paid and verified before removing the residual contribution. An investigation plus an actually funded viable settlement may also resolve it. Merely accepting a quote does not prove correction. Failure evaluates its warning and actual material loss before retiring the case.

Store each contribution's remaining applied points. Each addition is limited by the room below raw Exposure 100, with only the amount actually applied recorded. An inspection removes up to 15 current procurement points in this fixed order: oldest unresolved ordinary incident, contextual contributions, policy contribution, then Fever contribution. It cannot bank unused reduction. Later resolution removes only a source's remaining residual, so an incident already reduced by inspection cannot be deducted a second time. Removed or clipped amounts never reappear through replay.

Procurement inspection does not remove method uncertainty, ambient anomaly risk, a malfunction's separate contribution, renewal or hazardous-continuation additions. Their stated validation, repair or closure owns those changes. Public Exposure is the sum of live residuals, bounded 0–100, with the separate full-risk floor of 50 while that operation remains uncertified. That floor can mask an otherwise valid procurement reduction. No additional public risk meters are introduced.

First reaching unresolved Exposure 75 starts a disclosed 90-calendar-day critical financing warning. Count only days at 75 or more with an unresolved material obligation. A temporary reduction pauses that count without erasing elapsed critical days. A genuine settlement ends that case. The financial-shock conjunction still requires the actual material loss below. Its warning shares the ordinary obligation presentation and never permits a fourth simultaneous mission.

### 4.1 Checkpoint probabilities

| Exposure when the checkpoint resolves | Chance of an incident at that checkpoint |
|---|---:|
| 0–24 | 5% |
| 25–49 | 15% |
| 50–74 | 30% |
| 75–100 | 45% |

The initial conditional incident mix is 40 parts delay, 35 parts genuine overrun and 25 parts corruption. Real conditions may reweight that mix through documented factors, while the checkpoint's overall issue probability stays explicit. A war-related supply interruption favors delays. A poorly supervised rapid expansion favors overruns and corruption. Legality is enforced separately from weights.

All randomness is resolved once against the charter and checkpoint identity. If another incident is already live, a newly due checkpoint queues behind it and is not rerolled while waiting. Work that becomes due in a single delayed callback must not create several simultaneous response panels.

### 4.2 Bounded incident effects

| Issue | Initial consequence | Full-completion response | Investigation or reduction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery delay | 15–45 additional funded days for affected remaining work | Pay the amended schedule at a valid hold | Inspect the bottleneck, choose a supported substitute or remove the undelivered optional component |
| Genuine overrun | 15–30% additional work on the affected unfinished scope | Approve that exact remaining-work surcharge | Verify the cause, then accept a smaller design or a different contractor where meaningful |
| Corruption | 20–40% rework of the affected unfinished work, with a frozen case truth | Finish through an expensive replacement agreement | Pay for evidence and contractor replacement, removing unjustified prospective charges and preserving verifiable reusable work |

The cost cannot exceed the affected remaining scope merely because the country has become richer since acceptance. Inspection can save future work and recover physically accounted reusable material. It does not refund consumed civilian-factory-days or create a stockpile the project never owned.

A real incident that needs a new quote pauses affected work and releases its old hold. The response has 90 days, plus one justified 30-day shortage extension within the site's cumulative 180 dormant days. No response results in an orderly wind-down of that unfinished scope. The default does not keep charging the country indefinitely.

### 4.3 National failure thresholds

A megaproject becomes a candidate for a national economic shock only after material committed value has been lost. At least one of the following must be true.

1. Two or more linked sites have failed, and written-off paid work is at least the equivalent of 120 days of the country's usable civilian capacity frozen at initial charter acceptance.
2. At least half of the program's promised principal scope has failed, the peak accepted project hold was at least 35% of that same frozen usable capacity, and verified written-off paid construction work is at least 25% of the program's frozen accepted conventional construction-work budget.

Freeze the second threshold's work-budget denominator when the Fever scope is accepted. Increase it only for genuinely added and accepted conventional construction scope. Scope reduction, failed-scope settlement, wind-down and cleanup cannot lower it. Exclude anomalous work and inspection, operating or recovery service from both that conventional budget and its paid-loss numerator. Unpaid promised work contributes no actual loss. This guard leaves the first threshold unchanged and prevents a briefly funded large promise from satisfying the material-value requirement.

A failed site is one whose principal delivery has been terminated after genuine paid work, or whose completed principal capacity is rendered unusable by the authored catastrophe. A voluntarily cancelled unpaid site does not count. A partial compromise does not automatically count as losing every promised building. Compare the frozen material scope and observed lost value, with source receipts for each included loss.

For an ordinary financial collapse, the country must also have unresolved critical Exposure of at least 75 for 90 days after a warning and a real rescue or wind-down opportunity. Count critical elapsed time only while the unresolved material obligation remains. Rapidly toggling a policy cannot erase the accumulated warning, and a genuine paid settlement ends it. An explicit decision to continue an impossible operation after a critical warning can cause a qualifying catastrophe sooner.

For a concrete reachable path, take a National Fever program with 20 usable civilians at initial acceptance, an accelerated hold of 9, base Exposure 55 and no contextual additions. The hold is below half that capacity. A genuine corruption case adds 20, reaching 75. Suppose the verified site receipts include at least 2,400 paid factory-days that remain uncommissioned and later become actual write-off across at least two linked failed sites. After 90 unresolved critical days, the same deadline's wind-down can satisfy both the warning and material-loss conditions. Settle observed loss and evaluate the preserved warning before case cleanup. Earlier completed correction, sufficient inspection or earlier wind-down prevents the conjunction when its timing or loss conditions no longer hold. These are design-case inputs, not a simulated native result.

These conjunctions prevent a routine delay in a low-cost small project from producing a depression. They still allow an overextended government to waste an enormous investment and suffer a national consequence. Early reduction can preserve a viable first site. Late abandonment can release future capacity while leaving enough actual loss to trigger the shock.

### 4.4 Great Depression handoff

The pinned Event 35 gateway has six caller types and no production Investment caller. Implementation must add a distinct owner-reviewed Investment source context. It cannot pass a fabricated Event 34 collapse snapshot or the debug caller. The extension must define source event and charter, affected states, frozen loss and capacity base, source receipt, shock profile, pacing and acknowledgment. [Pinned gateway evidence](121_investment_sources_and_review.md#source-register).

Event 35 owns receipt allocation and its accepted-source ledger. Fresh success is exactly result 1, 2 or 3. A duplicate or a positive rejection code is not new success. Preserve the original consequence receipt and Investment's durable acknowledgment across retries.

The initial attempt occurs when the material shock is settled. A genuinely transient rejection can be retried at day 7 and day 30, for at most three total attempts. A permanent rejection closes immediately to the explicit Investment aftermath. Never allocate a new receipt to evade an earlier rejection. This fallback is not permission to ship an unimplemented Investment caller.

Release all failed-project construction holds before settling the consequence. Where no accepted depression already owns the same national penalty, apply an Investment distress bridge of −15% construction speed and −10% factory and dockyard output, lasting no more than 90 days from the original failure. On accepted Event 35 handoff, remove the bridge as the crisis owner takes over. If an existing depression is already active, deepen or merge it through the owner and avoid layering the bridge over that crisis.

If a supported runtime context permanently prevents the external handoff, the same finite Investment distress continues as the complete fallback. Total duration is 180 days from the original loss, or 360 days when at least half of the program's paid work was written off. This duration includes the bridge time and never restarts on retries. It is a recorded economic failure, without an acknowledged Event 35 success.

### 4.5 Paid aftermath recovery

The surviving original payer may make one recovery attempt against an active Investment-owned distress receipt while Event 35 does not own that consequence. The exact ordinary-cost quote comes from a 25 political-power base. Reserve one actually usable civilian for an ordinary aftermath or three for a mega aftermath, for 30 funded recovery days, with a 60-calendar-day hard deadline. This work verifies accounts and settles contractor obligations nationally. It does not require recapturing a lost site or grant construction credit, buildings or coupons.

Verified completion shortens that same distress receipt by the lesser of 90 days and its then-remaining duration, once. Cancellation, failure, original distress expiry, payer annexation or Event 35 accepting that consequence releases only the recovery hold and grants no reduction. Capacity loss pauses the job within its original 60-day limit. No outcome restarts the distress, original cooldown or charter. The residual distress record owns this job, with no new independent firing or charter slot. Use the existing category, investigation icon and obligation mission. Part 6 defines its legal AI preference.

## 5. Impossible Industry at Chaos 600+

### 5.1 One flagship method

An Impossible charter attaches one anomalous method to one flagship site. Its ordinary sector and other paid sites remain recognizable. The government receives a conventional completion option, an inspected prototype or a full-risk contract. It can choose extraordinary returns without granting every site an unrelated supernatural system.

The method contributes up to 20 Exposure when accepted, plus the higher-Chaos ambient amount where applicable, recording only each amount actually applied beneath the cap. An inspected prototype adds 20% to remaining relevant work for validation. When that paid validation finishes, it removes the method's remaining recorded uncertainty contribution. Other unresolved Exposure remains. Full-risk operation preserves an effective Exposure floor of 50 while the uncertified process is active, even after ordinary procurement inspections.

| Method | Inspected prototype | Full-risk operation | What can be lost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-assembling production | Extra principal units equal to one-third of conventional principal units, rounded up, capped at 1 ordinary or 2 mega, plus +15% construction speed during operation | Extra units equal to half the conventional principal units, rounded up, capped at 3 ordinary or 6 mega, plus +25% construction speed | Undelivered anomalous units, the temporary operating contribution and paid unfinished anomalous work |
| Impossible extraction | +20 selected resource units at the flagship state, or +40 for mega | +40 selected resource units, or +80 for mega | The exact source-owned resource contribution, with separate damage only if the authored accident causes it |
| Impossible throughput | +20% relevant construction, factory or dockyard performance, or +30% for mega | +30% relevant performance, or +50% for mega | The licensed output contribution and operation time |

The selected resource is one supported ordinary resource type, chosen before purchase from the sector's relevant needs. The method can produce a new source where the geology would not normally justify it. Its fictional nature is explicit. The removable contribution must have exact source custody so closure cannot subtract an unrelated mine or another mod's resource grant.

Experimental engineering uses a continuing research-speed benefit for throughput. Inspected operation grants +10% ordinary or +15% mega. Full-risk operation grants +20% ordinary or +30% mega. The strongest valid research-speed contribution applies in the existing benefit family, without adding the ordinary +5% dividend. Actual service, custody, pause, expiry and closure govern this licensed contribution in both native and no-DLC branches. Two additional one-use 50% industry research bonuses remain a separate verified anomaly-commissioning reward. Renewal, recapture and mode changes cannot issue them again. Neither effect completes a special project or grants a locked technology.

Self-assembly counts the frozen conventional principal-allocation basis, ordinary U or mega B, equally for native and no-DLC representations. A reduced agreement decreases this unearned basis before its revised quote is accepted. Extra buildings are civilian factories for civilian and special routes, military factories for military routes, and dockyards for naval routes. Self-assembly never copies a refinery, native facility, research coupon or support improvement. Freeze each extra unit's legal placement among existing charter sites before purchase, retaining the same site-count ceiling and actual slot/coastal restrictions. The stated ratios and caps apply to that one frozen basis.

Self-assembly's extra physical buildings require enough legal capacity in the accepted scope. Conventional project units arrive through ordinary construction receipts. The anomalous extra units arrive only after the first 30 fully funded operating days, a valid commissioning record and a successful day-30 safety check. A malfunction keeps that same one-time delivery coupon pending. Successful paid correction of the original case can satisfy its safety proof without another hazard roll while the original license remains unexpired. Expiry, disappearance or cascade retires the pending coupon. Safety cleanup that finishes after expiry cannot produce a late extra-building grant. Extra units arrive once, with no recurring factory grant.

Extra units that have already become conventional commissioned buildings remain ordinary physical assets. A later disappearing plant withdraws the anomalous annex and its operating contribution. It does not identify unrelated factories as reclaimable property. A separately authored industrial accident can damage real buildings in its disclosed affected area.

### 5.2 Finite licensed operation

Operation begins only after the whole currently agreed conventional scope supporting the method and its required prototype validation are paid and commissioned. This prevents an unfunded network from supplying the self-assembly count or licensing an unbuilt complex. The first-service authorization window is the smaller of 90 calendar days and the flagship site's retained unused portion of its cumulative 180-day dormant allowance, measured at conventional commissioning. Waiting consumes that same flagship allowance. A zero remainder retires the unstarted method immediately. Otherwise authorize the first actual service hold before the earlier deadline or retire the method with no operating Chaos premium, operating coupon or indefinite pending charter. The license starts at actual funded activation.

The first license lasts 180 calendar days from activation and reserves one civilian factory for service, or three for a mega flagship. Operation needs a valid positive service commitment and actual funded time. Benefits pause when service or legal custody fails. The absolute expiry keeps advancing during a pause.

The country may buy one extension before expiry, up to 360 calendar days from original activation. The extension has a new service receipt and adds 10 Exposure. It cannot restart the clock, repeat commissioning rewards or reopen consumed checkpoints. Show the remaining licensed time and commitment in the ordinary category, without adding another public meter.

An anomaly service record keeps the charter active after construction has finished. Cooldown begins at final service or containment closure. A country cannot accumulate multiple active anomalous licenses by declaring each construction phase complete.

A country can move from full-risk to inspected operation through one paid validation action, using the exact ordinary-cost quote from a 25 political-power base and 30 funded service days with the anomalous output paused. The job has a 60-calendar-day hard deadline from acceptance and consumes its existing calendar license. Successful validation retires the method's remaining uncertainty contribution and the full-risk Exposure floor. If it finishes before license expiry, the smaller prototype operating benefit applies for the remaining term. Validation that finishes after expiry cannot restart operation. Already earned physical buildings remain, but unearned full-risk extra units are reduced to the inspected promise. No change can reclaim a bonus that was already spent as a research reward.

At ordinary license expiry, automatic safe retirement removes the source-owned operating contribution and releases service capacity. Retirement work is included in the license commitment. The safe-expiry receipt records actual funded operating days and any unresolved hazard. A clean 180-day stewardship achievement requires the complete qualifying history, not an active modifier one day after expiry.

### 5.3 Control and succession

Control loss immediately disables the old beneficiary's anomaly contribution and service hold. A temporary occupier does not inherit the charter. If the original owner recovers control within the remaining license and grace, it may fund the remaining service under the same identity, with no restarted dates or checkpoints.

After a permanent ownership transfer, a new owner-controller can elect to assume the remaining service only if it has the real capacity and no conflicting active Investment charter or anomaly service. That election creates its own paid inherited-service record tied to the same site anomaly and original expiry. It uses the one-charter slot but does not create an independent random-event firing.

The successor receives only the future operating contribution it actually funds. Pending extra-building coupons and one-time research rewards from the original construction do not transfer. Original project achievements remain with their actual beneficiary. If assumption is unavailable or declined, the anomaly safely retires through its owner cleanup. Physical conventional buildings follow the state normally.

## 6. Anomalous incidents and safe closure

### 6.1 Review cadence

Anomaly reviews occur at 30 and 90 funded operating days. The 180-day checkpoint applies only when a paid extension is actually in force. If the license expires without renewal, safe expiry settles before any day-180 hazard roll. A paused license cannot earn operating days or extra buildings, and its fixed calendar expiry prevents indefinite storage of a nearly finished safety test.

The checkpoint incident probabilities use the same four Exposure bands as the construction checkpoints. The normal conditional mix is 50 parts malfunction, 30 parts disappearance and 20 parts critical warning. At Exposure 75+, the mix becomes 40, 25 and 35. These are authored single-use checkpoint probabilities. Part 6 distinguishes their analytical arithmetic from native-engine evidence.

### 6.2 Malfunction

A malfunction suspends the abnormal benefit, adds up to 10 Exposure beneath the cap and requires a revised service or repair decision. Repair costs 30 funded days at the one-civilian or three-civilian service hold, with no additional political-power charge, and has a 60-calendar-day hard deadline from acceptance. Its verified success removes only that incident's remaining actual Exposure contribution while preserving any full-risk floor. It may damage up to 10% of eligible industrial building levels at the flagship, capped at two full building-level equivalents. It does not cause automatic civilian deaths. If extra self-assembly units are still unfinished, a genuine fault can add up to 15% rework to their remaining anomalous work, through a separate explicit work amendment.

The government can repair and retain the method, convert to inspected operation, perform paid containment or use emergency closure. A validated conversion using the exact quote from a 25-political-power base may include fault correction in its 30 funded service days only when the quote explicitly buys both outcomes. The same paid work is not charged twice or credited again to construction. The fault does not start a second anomaly or create a new license term.

A malfunction awaiting a response remains bounded by absolute license expiry. It has no additional automatic 30-day emergency-close timer. Part 5's containment achievement separately requires a qualifying response within 30 calendar days of the first actionable qualifying fault, and the tooltip discloses that eligibility deadline. It does not alter the ordinary malfunction lifecycle.

### 6.3 Disappearance

The abnormal annex ceases to exist as an operating source. Remove its exact resource or production contribution, retire pending extra units, release its service hold and retain the conventional assets already commissioned. The country loses the outstanding abnormal promise and remaining usable license. The outcome is recorded as anomalous failure rather than full completion.

This route should feel unsettling through the physical absence and contradictory records, with clear gameplay accounting. It cannot remove random ordinary factories merely to imitate disappearance. Any additional building damage belongs to a separate observed consequence with its own receipt.

### 6.4 Critical warning and response

A critical warning identifies a dangerous mechanism and gives a 30-day emergency response window. This shorter mission is justified by a current industrial hazard. The abnormal output and ordinary service hold pause while a new response is selected. The government can choose paid containment, undertake a validated lower-risk restart, explicitly continue hazardous operation, or trigger emergency closure.

Paid containment uses the exact ordinary-cost quote from a 25 political-power base and 30 funded days at the service hold. Lawful discounts, including a legitimate zero political quote, still require an authenticated authorization and actual industrial service. The warning's 30-day deadline governs accepting and paying for a response. A valid paid response retires that warning mission and starts its own job, with a 60-calendar-day hard deadline from acceptance. Output stays off throughout. Containment can finish authorized cleanup after the operating license expires, but cannot extend or restart the license. It removes the abnormal source safely, preserves ordinary commissioned assets and can repay the recorded reversible operating-anomaly Chaos premium. Its success depends on the actual paid containment receipt. Losing funding cannot leave the job open forever. At its hard deadline, incomplete cleanup defaults to emergency closure.

Emergency closure is always executable. It charges the smaller of the exact current ordinary-cost quote from a 25 political-power base and the country's nonnegative available political power. If the same case already has a valid paid containment or validation political-authorization receipt, including a lawful zero quote, that fee is covered and is not charged a second time. It immediately isolates the source, withdraws anomalous output and may damage up to 5% of eligible flagship industrial levels, capped at two. It grants no paid-containment achievement. The country cannot be trapped because it lacks political power or construction capacity.

No response by the emergency deadline defaults to emergency closure. The script must not keep an unaffordable service hold or silently choose continued dangerous operation for the player.

Explicit hazardous continuation resumes the service commitment, preserves a warning receipt and adds 15 Exposure. At the warning's due resolution, the cascade probability is 30% below Exposure 75 and 60% at 75 or above. Surviving that resolution adds 10 unresolved Exposure and consumes the warning. It does not reset other operating checkpoints. The tooltip discloses the risk and the possible physical and economic loss before authorization.

### 6.5 Serious industrial cascade

An ordinary flagship cascade damages 30% of eligible industrial building levels at the affected site, capped at four full building-level equivalents. A mega cascade damages 40%, capped at eight equivalents across the flagship and at most two genuinely linked project sites. Select only valid observed buildings and record actual damage. These are disaster effects, not ownership-based removal of the event's past grants.

The catastrophe removes its anomaly, ends further abnormal delivery and applies −15% construction speed and −10% factory and dockyard output through the distress family. The normal period is 180 days, with 360 days for a material mega loss. A qualifying Event 35 handoff owns the national consequence instead, avoiding duplicate penalties.

Where the chosen incident explicitly affects civilians, use a local loss anchor of 0.1% of the affected eligible population, capped at 5,000, or 0.25% capped at 20,000 for the entire mega incident. These are fictional game consequence magnitudes. Respect the shared protected-population floor, apply the physical loss once and record the observed result through the shared Deaths owner. Never add a second death debit to match a displayed estimate.

A serious cascade can meet the material megaproject shock thresholds. If it does, use the same single consequence receipt and Event 35 integration. It does not become a second independent Investment or Depression draw.

| Ordinary delay, fraud, overrun or voluntary wind-down | 0 | A bounded investment loss | No bonus Chaos for clicking a risky financial choice |
| Megaproject financial shock | 0 from Investment | Event 35 or the finite economic aftermath owns the actual consequence | Do not invent a second crisis premium for the same loss |
| First verified operation of an industrial anomaly | +3 ordinary, +5 mega | A real abnormal process is operating, once per charter anomaly source | This is the reversible operating premium eligible for containment repayment |
| Material disappearance after verified abnormal operation | +3 | A distinct observed industrial disappearance, once per source, with real active contribution lost | Do not award if a never-operational proposal is cancelled |
| Serious local cascade | +5 | Distinct destabilizing industrial anomaly, once per cascade receipt | Shared casualties and physical world effects are accounted by their owners |
| Material national mega cascade | +10 instead of the local +5 | A genuinely systemic linked failure meeting the national material threshold | Never award both local and national premiums for the same cascade |
| Verified containment or safe retirement of the anomalous source | Up to the outstanding +3 or +5 operating premium, downward | Removes the remaining actual abnormal operation, once | Cannot erase disappearance/cascade premiums, casualties or unrelated global Chaos |

The total Investment-specific positive Chaos from one charter is capped at 20. This cap does not suppress legitimate independent shared Deaths or other system consequences. It prevents repeatedly presenting the same anomaly as fresh industrial destabilization.

The reversible balance is source-owned. A controller change, a reload callback, a second closure attempt or a country with no remaining premium cannot generate a reduction. Safe retirement can repay only the outstanding operating premium after the actual source is removed. Permanent consequences remain in world history. The project cannot create net-negative Chaos by repeatedly opening and closing ordinary or never-operational facilities.

## 8. Outcomes the implementation must distinguish

| Outcome | Physical assets | Remaining obligations | History and achievement result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full conventional completion | All agreed conventional outputs | None after dividend bookkeeping | Full scope, eligible for relevant achievements |
| Full network completion | All essential sites and links | None after dividend bookkeeping | Complete Fever program, with original scope proof |
| Reduced completion | Explicit renegotiated outputs only | Closed after agreed delivery | Reduced result, not original full-scope credit |
| Partial wind-down | Earned assets and valid reusable work | Unfunded promises retired, own holds released | Partial result with actual lost work |
| Safe anomaly expiry | Conventional and earned extra buildings | Source retired, service closed | Stewardship credit only with full qualifying funded history |
| Paid emergency containment | Conventional assets preserved | Paid containment settles and source closes | Containment credit only for a real warning and paid success |
| Emergency trip | Conventional assets with disclosed bounded damage | Immediate retirement | Safe termination, no paid-containment credit |
| Disappearance | Commissioned conventional assets | Pending abnormal promises retired | Anomalous failure |
| Cascade with national shock | Observed surviving and damaged assets | One crisis owner and finite pending settlement | Catastrophe, with no duplicate successful completion |

The distinctions must survive cleanup. A terminal flag cannot erase the receipts that explain what was paid, what was delivered, which anomaly operated and why a crisis or achievement was recorded.
