# 075 · Decisions and missions

## A compact control surface

The decision category contains the event's operational controls.
It does not reproduce every focus as a purchase button.
Aid campaigns are standing priorities, not a separate mission for every donor.
The player manages one network and can inspect particular countries when needed.

The main category shows no more than five primary actions in any phase.
Contextual donor offers and target rows live inside their relevant selection flow.
They are not an unbounded list of fifty equivalent global buttons.
Every disabled action states the missing condition before the player spends anything.

## Accumulation actions

| Action family | Player choice and result | Cost or sacrifice | Timing and conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manage foreign appeals | Choose current campaign priorities, donor emphasis, and bounded automatic dispatch | New requests create real diplomatic commitments and same-donor cooldowns, not a click fee | Standing policies can be reviewed, while pending offers remain unique and retain their terms |
| Approach a government | Select one genuinely eligible donor and a supported useful package | Uses that donor's request opportunity and any stated diplomatic commitment | At most one pending offer per donor, normally subject to the tier's cooldown |
| Fund the industrial program | Choose a valid civilian, arms, transport, or dense-district project from available paid backing | Commits construction effort and the specific material or sponsor support required by the project | A funded project takes time and is vulnerable to loss of its site or route |
| Prepare the reserve army | Select a supported force mix and commit recruits and equipment to training | Actual manpower and equipment enter exclusive paid custody, plus the required training opportunity | Training completes over time and cannot also spend the same stock in a native queue |
| Review and commit the reveal | Inspect current targets and activate the early or prepared commitment when legal | Ends protective ties and future aid, exposes the army, and starts all opening border wars | Irreversible, once, blocked when political or ownership-safe mobilization is not ready |

Seeking or changing protection is handled through the foreign-appeals flow and the opening faction event.
It does not add a permanent sixth button.
A genuine emergency defense can replace the normal reserve action with an action to deploy paid reserves now.
That deployment is not the reveal and is recorded against the same reserve backing.

## Donor response

An actual offer states the resource type, exact current quoted amount, delivery conditions, and donor commitment.
The response choices are a full affordable package, a stated smaller affordable package, a useful supported substitute, and refusal.
An unavailable alternative is not offered as a button with an impossible payment.
The donor can inspect how the package affects its own resources.
It is not shown a fictional worldwide total for TAN.

A standing human donor policy may authorize small recurring support within a visible ceiling and reserve protection.
It can be stopped at any time and never authorizes a larger future category silently.
An audited or suspended program does not continue through an old automatic-acceptance setting.

An offer expires after a visible response window.
The proposed baseline window is 15 days, extended by any project-wide paused multiplayer handling that genuinely exists.
Expiry makes no transfer and is not treated as acceptance.
A new later offer must be independently affordable.

## Expansion actions

| Action family | Player choice and result | Cost or sacrifice | Timing and conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prioritize the fronts | Select operational emphasis among actual opponents | Commits existing forces and logistics, with no free army | Cannot exempt a valid non-allied neighbor from the political war rule |
| Reorganize reserves and replacements | Activate remaining paid cohorts or assign available stock to replacement | Actual equipment, manpower, training capacity, and available support | Rechecks supply and stock before each activation |
| Restore and develop occupied industry | Repair a real damaged complex or complete an established funded project | Industrial effort and stated transport or material costs | No instant repair of every conquered state |
| Settle conquered territory | Choose eligible direct integration or real subordination | Time, local control, administrative effort, and any declared stability sacrifice | Requires held territory and real political conditions |
| Review continental objectives | Inspect the leading continent and its holding requirement, then begin the global campaign when valid | No payment for viewing progress, a one-way strategic commitment for starting the scenario | Requires current certified control, 1000 Chaos, and shared scenario permission |

A former-donor objective is selected through front priorities.
Its flavour and bounded benefit attach to one qualifying campaign and do not add a parallel donor currency.

## Global-campaign actions

| Action family | Player choice and result | Cost or sacrifice | Timing and conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Select the next theater | Prioritize real independent opponents and a reachable route | Commits existing command and strategic attention | The policy eventually reaches every remaining organized opponent |
| Mobilize continental industry | Assign the earned program to shipyards, aircraft, ground reinforcements, or logistics | Consumes the program's one-time funded allocation and available continental resources | The initial program cannot repeat on recertification or a second continent |
| Prepare the overseas operation | Choose an actual port and supported invasion or reinforcement plan | Convoys, fuel, trained forces, and transport or naval preparation | Must have a route that can be supplied and an appropriate destination |
| Demand submission or negotiate administration | Give an eligible independent government a genuine submission decision, or organize conquered territory | Political commitment and the consequences of acceptance or refusal | Refusal does not remove the country from the global objective |
| Review the final settlement | Check remaining opposition and complete the final campaign when none remains independent | No payment to falsify victory | Requires the actual worldwide sovereignty and control check |

## Outside-TAN actions

A donor can review its own program, suspend further assistance, or initiate a justified audit before the reveal.
After the reveal, an affected country can coordinate defense with genuinely eligible partners and examine its own completed aid record.
These controls are visible only where relevant.
They do not give an uninvolved minor a second full Tuvan management category.

## Timed missions

At most three event-created countdown missions are active for a country at once.
Use them for a funded works program, a paid training or support operation, and one current operational holding objective.
Several small deliveries may belong to a single works program.
The player should not receive a separate countdown for each donor's paperwork.

A standing aid campaign is a priority setting and consumes no countdown-mission slot.
Routine native construction, production, research, and training retain their existing queues.
A special event mission is justified only when it has an event-specific success or failure outcome beyond those queues.

| Mission family | Success | Interruption or failure |
|---|---|---|
| Funded industrial works | The paid project completes at its valid site, and its actual output begins | Loss of the site pauses or ends the remaining work under its contract, without duplicating refunded goods |
| Paid reserve preparation | The assigned recruits and equipment become a trained sovereign cohort | Missing support delays activation, while losses and cancelled work are settled from actual custody |
| Continental certification | Both continental control tests remain valid for 30 days | Losing either threshold resets the consecutive holding period |
| Durable overseas foothold | A real port and overseas supply route remain usable for 30 days | Loss of the port or sustainable connection interrupts the operation |
| Continental containment | A formerly certified Tuvan continent remains below the required dominance for 30 days | Tuvan recovery interrupts the claimed reversal |

Only the relevant operational holding mission is exposed at a time.
An observer's containment mission and TAN's mission are owned by their respective countries and cannot award the same global reversal repeatedly.
Multiple observing coalition members cannot each charge a separate global relief payment.

## Cost language

Decisions use the resources that actually support their outcome.
A military cohort spends recruits and equipment, an industrial project commits productive effort, and an overseas operation needs transport and fuel.
Political power can support a genuine administrative or diplomatic action, but is not the sole payment for every program.

A player should normally see one to three spendable cost types, with a hard maximum of four for an action.
An equipment manifest can be opened for detail rather than squeezing five families into the button label.
At most three values belong in an inline cost display.
Use valid native resource text icons, not new currency symbols.
Availability requirements and non-spendable conditions are stated separately from prices.

A cancelled program states which unused quantities return and which committed work is already spent.
No decision can temporarily send a donor negative and repair the amount with an unrelated grant afterward.
