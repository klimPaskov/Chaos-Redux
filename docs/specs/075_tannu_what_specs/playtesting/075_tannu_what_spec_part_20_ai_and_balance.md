# 075 · AI behavior and balance intent

## Patient preparation, persistent expansion

TAN's AI should secure lawful protection where possible, ask widely for affordable aid, diversify material needs, and prepare a supplied sovereign army before it reveals.
It is allowed to remain small when the world provides little help.
Patience does not mean deliberately hoarding an army that has already met its selected objective while all useful opportunities expire.

After the reveal, the AI obeys the recurring neighbor-war policy and uses operational priorities to allocate its real forces.
It should preserve functioning supply, replace losses, repair valuable captured industry, and avoid putting its entire army in one province.
The global AI must build an actual route overseas and keep pursuing remaining independent opponents.

## Strategic matrix

| Campaign situation | Preferred response | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| God's Plan exists under a valid Holy Realm protector | Attempt that protective route first, then evaluate alternatives if rejected | Forced religious or ideology conversion |
| Already in a useful strong faction | Retain its protection while developing wider direct aid contacts | Leaving merely to repeat an entry reward |
| No faction will accept | Continue lawful useful aid and domestic preparation with lower security | Free faction membership or event invulnerability |
| Strong patrons but narrow material mix | Request missing manpower, training, transport, industry, or fuel | More rifles when rifles are not the limiting component |
| Broad but weak donor network | Favor a varied sequence of small affordable offers and domestic compounding | Treating a small donor's stock as equal to a major's |
| One exceptionally useful patron | Consider the concentrated route while preparing for the loss of future support at reveal | Counting that patron's borrowed army as TAN's future invasion force |
| Limited physical building room | Complete valid dense industrial development and supporting transport | Fake physical factories or percentage growth across every future conquered factory |
| Real defensive war before reveal | Use paid reserves for survival and seek actually useful emergency aid | Duplicate emergency army at later reveal |
| Preparation below objective, donors refusing | Revise priorities, finish funded work, and wait or remain a minor | A calendar-based free major-power package |
| Preparation objective met and opening viable | Use the prepared reveal route without unnecessary extra buildup | Waiting forever for a daily moving global benchmark |
| Opening wars exceed local supply | Field the supported prepared army and retain excess paid reserves | Stacking every reserve formation in the capital |
| Multiple post-reveal fronts | Obey declarations while concentrating offensive strength on supported priorities | An operational priority becoming a permanent political exemption |
| Former major donor is a practical opponent | Use the donor-specific campaign route when genuinely useful | Chasing a distant inaccessible donor while the home front collapses |
| First useful coastline acquired | Repair and connect the port before planning naval reach | Assuming coastline ownership alone supplies an invasion |

| Former ally remains independent globally | Offer real submission when eligible, otherwise pursue it as an opponent | Counting equal alliance as world conquest |

## Choosing a preparation objective

The proposed baseline ambition begins around 30 standard formation equivalents and can rise with a contemporary major-power comparison.
Evolution I begins around 60, Evolution II around 100, and Evolution III around 150.
These are untested design seeds for the AI's desired preparation, not direct division rewards.
A standard equivalent has a fixed documented material and manpower meaning, so template splitting cannot increase strength.

The ambition also requires a working replacement base, reserves beyond the initial deployment, and usable opening supply.
A tank-heavy objective needs a different backing manifest from infantry-heavy preparation.
Force quantity alone cannot satisfy a program with no fuel or training.

The selected program holds its objective steady for a meaningful preparation period, proposed at 90 days.
At a review point, the AI can choose a revised objective based on the current world and active evolution.
Already paid assets remain intact.
The early-reveal player route has a lower practical floor, proposed at ten complete supported equivalents, and plainly warns about the gap from the normal ambition.

The baseline military-industrial target is proposed at ten effective military production equivalents, rising to 20, 40, and 60 across the evolutions.
Dense development contributes only its verified absolute additional capacity.
Foreign contracts scheduled to cease at the reveal do not count as permanent replacement output.
These numbers require real calibration in the target game version.

## Donor generosity

The donor favors a modest affordable request from a much weaker apparent defensive partner.
Friendly relations, shared faction membership, an actual relevant rival, a credible reported emergency, and limited requested sacrifice can increase acceptance.
A donor's own ongoing war, depleted reserve, prior payments, known inconsistent claims, and lack of a valid delivery route can reduce it or remove an option.

The donor evaluates its own offer and legitimate observations.
It does not read TAN's aggregate private reserve, total worldwide requests, or eventual reveal score as a secret universal refusal trigger.
It can notice ordinary visible changes that the game genuinely exposes.
The design aims for generous behavior, not magical ignorance of every observation.

Full aid, smaller aid, a supported substitute, and refusal need separate weights among the options that are actually valid.
Invalid options have no participation in the probability denominator.
A stated AI weight is not a probability and cannot be reported as a measured acceptance percentage without the requested tool evaluation.

## Focus preferences

The AI chooses broad sponsorship when many useful donors are available and no single dependency dominates.
It favors concentrated patronage when a particularly useful protector offers material depth and access.
Industrial independence becomes more attractive when TAN has reliable development backing, time, and a weak sustainable replacement base.
The chosen route changes later focus emphasis.
It does not make the common converging administration focus require mutually exclusive predecessors.

Within the common branches, the AI prioritizes its real shortage.
It develops transport before unsupported mass motorization, airfields before air packages that cannot be used, and industrial sustainment before a reveal that would consume the entire stock in one campaign.
Post-reveal focus choices respond to actual fronts, captured infrastructure, local control, and the availability of a first coast.
Global focus choices favor the next workable operational route.

## Balance acceptance

A generous world should produce a materially stronger TAN than a stingy world from the same starting position.
A large rifle contribution should not compensate numerically for zero recruits.
The best industrial route should compound through actual output without multiplying forever across captured factories.
A human donor should be able to refuse and preserve its resources.
A human TAN should be able to reveal early, suffer defeat, and understand why its preparation failed.

A successful late-evolution reveal should be capable of threatening major neighbors, but geography, enemy strength, and supply must still matter.
The global campaign must be able to reach the final opponent through a real playable route.
All quantitative tuning remains provisional until the required tool results and user-owned live playtest evidence support it.
