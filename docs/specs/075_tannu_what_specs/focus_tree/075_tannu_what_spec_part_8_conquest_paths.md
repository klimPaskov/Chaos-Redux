# 075 · Conquest and the Tuvan state

## They Actually Believed Us

This is the conversion from protected client to independent war administration.
It closes the defensive appeal system, turns completed donor projects into their permanent domestic role, and carries paid reserves into the active army.
The focus recognizes what TAN accumulated.
It does not recalculate the ledger as a second set of gifts.

The branch then deals with foreign dependence.
The player can retain a mixed arsenal for rapid deployment or invest in standardization and domestic production for a longer war.
The first choice favors immediate strength and uses what is on hand.
The second consumes production and time but makes replacement less fragile after foreign contracts end.

## Return Their Generosity

The donor branch identifies countries that made substantial delivered contributions.
A donor that only agreed to an undelivered promise does not receive the same treatment as a country whose factories and recruits materially built the army.
The branch uses equipment, manpower, training, and industry histories separately when deciding what kind of connection exists.

Its first stage unlocks accurate donor-specific reaction flavor and campaign dossiers.
Its second stage offers a bounded military advantage against recorded major donors when TAN genuinely learned from their equipment or instruction.
Its final stage turns captured donor infrastructure into useful repair and production capacity through real control and investment.

The targeted advantage is temporary or tied to a defined first campaign.
It does not stack once for every shipment.
A donor's disappearance or the end of the relevant war closes the active effect.
The branch does not remotely destroy donor stockpiles or award TAN weapons merely because their serial numbers once appeared in an aid file.

## Every Border Is a Front

The common expansion rule is already active after the reveal.
This branch makes the army more capable of handling it.
It improves command allocation, replacement priority, frontier supply, and the organization of operational groups.

The player chooses a strategic emphasis within that rule.
A concentrated offensive keeps stronger reserves for one decisive front while other fronts hold.
A broad offensive distributes strength and pursues several weak neighbors.
Neither choice allows an eligible non-allied neighbor to become permanently immune merely because the player did not select its button.

The mature branch helps prepare newly acquired frontiers before committing the next offensive group.
The wars themselves remain governed by current borders and valid diplomacy.
There is no fixed list of Soviet, Mongolian, or Chinese targets.

## The Tuvan War Machine

The industrial conquest branch repairs what the army captures and adapts it to sustained war.
The player first chooses between rapid repair of essential transport and immediate arms conversion.
A later program establishes standardized supply and equipment replacement across the growing territory.

The funded home-industry contribution remains bounded by its own projects.
Captured factories create real additional output through their normal ownership and occupation conditions.
They do not each inherit the full pre-reveal industrial-depth multiplier.

The endpoint provides an operating war economy with repair, production, and manpower organization that can survive the end of foreign assistance.
It must still suffer meaningful losses when key territory, transport, or industry is destroyed.
No focus grants unlimited resources or makes the army independent of logistics.

## From Tuva to the Sea

This branch identifies a strategically useful coastline based on the current map and ongoing wars.
Its opening chooses a reachable corridor, not a random coastal state on the other side of the world.
The middle stage connects that corridor to TAN's working supply system.
The endpoint requires an actually controlled, functioning port and makes limited naval preparation possible.

Reaching the coast does not unlock the world-end tree by itself.
It provides a useful base for ordinary play and a foundation for later global operations.
A destroyed or lost port removes the practical benefit until repaired or replaced.
A focus cannot count military access through a foreign port as ownership of the first Tuvan coastline.

## Nobody Asked Tannu Tuva

Conquered territory creates a choice of government.
The player can prioritize direct administration or subordinate states.
The choice governs occupation and settlement behavior without creating an unrelated new ideology tree.

| Route | Early program | Mature program | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct administration | Repair essential administration and pay garrison needs | Integrate selected stable regions through sustained control and investment | Greater direct access to industry, with greater occupation and manpower costs |
| Subordinate governments | Establish valid subjects through real settlements | Coordinate subject defense, production, and transit | Less direct control, with dependence on subject loyalty and actual contribution |

Neither route creates instant cores over every conquered state.
Integration requires stable control, functioning administration, supplied security, and a substantial commitment.
A state that changes hands or returns to serious resistance pauses or loses the relevant progress.
The exact regional selection follows current geography and control.

Subjects count toward continental and global subordination only while TAN is their real sovereign overlord and their territory is not controlled by an enemy.
An equal faction member does not count.
A puppet cannot be released and reabsorbed repeatedly to farm rewards or Chaos reductions.

## Tannu Tuva, Great Power

This anchor recognizes a real international position.
It requires a functioning major-scale military economy and sustained control appropriate to the current campaign.
It cannot make TAN a great power merely by changing a cosmetic name.

Its branches finish the current administration path and prepare the continental proof.
The player may retain Tannu Tuva's familiar identity.
A later cosmetic world-imperial presentation is available only at the actual global outcome and must remain visibly fictional.
No settlement names, leader portraits, or historical flags are replaced without a reason.

## Defeat and stalled conquest

The tree remains useful when the first war goes badly.
Repair, defensive concentration, replacement, and a difficult peace remain possible through normal play.
The event does not automatically restore lost armies or retake territory.

A final defeat that removes TAN's independent campaign closes its special conquest machinery.
A surviving subordinate TAN retains ordinary country life without reviving the same fire-once deception.
The world-end route cannot be reopened by reloading a focus tree or changing a cosmetic tag.
