# 075 · The defensive military program

## Just for Self-Defense

The common military opening creates a practical training program and identifies the first complete equipment package that TAN can support.
It establishes the core infantry reserve and a replacement allowance.
The tree then develops force composition through useful branches.
A country with donated rifles and recruits can progress without first acquiring tanks or aircraft.

## Broad ground force

The broad-force route starts with a dependable infantry organization and a domestic instructor base.
Its next stage improves the preparation of several cohorts at once, but each cohort still needs full material backing.
The mature route emphasizes front coverage, replacement, and the ability to defend industry while an offensive group advances.

Its reward is affordable, repeatable mobilization from future deliveries and production.
It does not award several fully equipped divisions solely for completing a focus.
A force using many foreign models also needs a standardization program to avoid maintaining too many incompatible replacement demands.

## Artillery and support

The support route begins when TAN has or can obtain useful specialist equipment.
It prepares engineers, support crews, artillery, and supply staff in a mix that matches the actual campaign.
A mountain approach, an open steppe front, and an industrial river crossing should not all receive the same support template by default.

The middle program integrates donated specialist equipment into a limited number of prepared formations.
The final program makes that organization repeatable through training and domestic replacement.
This provides a quality option for a country whose manpower is more limited than its equipment.

A large artillery donation cannot replace the recruits, rifles, transport, and supply needed by the complete formation.
The player's readiness explanation identifies whichever requirement still prevents deployment.

## Mobility and armor

The mobile branch starts with reconnaissance, transport, and the conditions for sustained movement.
The player can emphasize motorized exploitation or progress toward a smaller armored core when the appropriate stock, training, and fuel exist.
The two forms can later operate together.
Their first large investment should remain a real choice.

| Path stage | Military result | Material and strategic cost |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile support | Transport and staff suitable for a faster operational group | Trucks and fuel cannot simultaneously support every supply route |
| Motorized exploitation | Paid, trained mobile infantry for favorable terrain | More fuel and replacement demand than the broad infantry route |
| Armored instruction | Crews, maintenance, and compatible armored templates | Time, actual tanks, and specialist training |
| Combined-arms core | A limited armored force with the support needed to fight | Fewer total formations for the same available resources |
| Standardized replacement | A sustainable equipment mix for later campaigns | Surplus incompatible models may remain in reserve or be phased out |

The branch does not grant modern armor regardless of date.
The active game's equipment and research surface determines what can exist.
An advanced donated model can be fielded where supported without granting its entire production tree.

## Air force

The air family opens only when there is a useful aviation route.
The first stage establishes an operating base, support staff, and a viable aircraft package.
The next stage chooses between protecting the buildup and supporting the future offensive.
The mature stage expands maintenance, replacement, and range within actual basing limits.

An early defensive fighter group can reduce the risk of losing the home industrial complex.
An offensive support group can strengthen the first land campaign.
Neither counts as operational while all its aircraft sit in stock because TAN lacks a usable airfield or fuel.

Air gifts, pilot preparation, and foreign advice contribute to the reveal army's quality.
They are not prerequisites for an infantry-focused early reveal.
At the highest evolution, a large supported air force is a viable outcome, not an automatic decoration attached to a land-army grant.

## Staff, doctrine, and training

Foreign doctrine instruction offers choices compatible with TAN's existing doctrine position.
A staff mission can provide a limited eligible bonus or mastery opportunity through the installed system.
It does not complete all known doctrine branches or undo mutually exclusive choices.

The command route improves coordination, planning, training, and management of several fronts.
Foreign-trained officers retain learned competence after the reveal.
The loss of active advisers removes future access and ongoing instruction.
The distinction must be reflected in both effects and text.

Routine officer preparation uses existing TAN commanders and supported generic military organization.
The plan does not invent a list of real historical officers with unverified biographies or traits.
A later character expansion must use its own research and portrait handoff.

## Revealing the army

The military branches converge at an actual mobilization review.
The review checks paid reserve strength, supply, replacement, and the country's ability to open the required wars.
A mature force can unlock the normal **Tannu What?** commitment.
The player retains an early-reveal route at a lower, explicit fieldable minimum.

A short final focus is the normal commitment path.
Its completion uses the same reveal outcome as the early decision.
It grants no separate copy of the army and cannot repeat the first super-event.
The player can cancel an unfinished focus under the normal rules, but that cancellation does not refund already delivered aid or completed training.

## Retained military play

The tree does not become a dead end after the initial army appears.
Training and replacement programs remain useful throughout the conquest phase.
New equipment from domestic production or ordinary capture can fund further cohorts, with the same ownership and training rules.

A specialist branch cannot repeatedly award its original equipment package through focus replacement, tree reload, or country restoration.
Its lasting reward is the ability to organize and support that kind of force.
Actual forces continue to consume manpower, equipment, fuel, and supply.
