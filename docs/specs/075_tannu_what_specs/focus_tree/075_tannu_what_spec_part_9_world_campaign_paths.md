# 075 · The final global focus family

## Entry

The global family opens only after TAN holds the required continental position, global Chaos is at least 1000, the world-end setting permits it, and no incompatible world-end owner blocks the transition.
Its opening super-event announces the global campaign.
It does not grant worldwide territory or skip the fleet and supply problems below.

The branch uses the continent TAN actually dominates.
A non-Asian victory receives the same mechanical recognition, with presentation adapted to that continent and the location of its available ports.

## A Navy, Apparently

The first naval program surveys captured and domestic maritime capacity, usable coasts, available ships, current technology, and the resources for fleet construction.
It chooses an operating coast and a first overseas theater.
A state with no useful port must obtain and repair one before it can complete operational naval deployment.

A major initial mobilization package is allowed in this world-end phase.
It is explicitly a scenario mobilization reward based on the conquered continental economy.
It is not retroactively described as secret foreign aid.
Its ships, manpower, equipment, and industry are granted or converted once under a declared budget.
The ordinary aid-phase conservation rule is not used to disguise this separate world-end reinforcement.

## Tuvan Shipyards

The industrial maritime branch commits working continental resources to dockyards, naval bases, repair yards, convoy production, and a sustainable fleet program.
Its first stage prioritizes basic transport and escorts.
Its second stage chooses a fleet suited to the first theater.
Its mature stage supports sustained ship replacement and repair.

An escort and invasion-support fleet is useful for a nearby crossing.
A longer ocean campaign requires greater range, basing, air cover, and a stronger fleet.
The player should not have to build an irrelevant prestige fleet before making a practical first landing.

The branch can grant targeted research assistance or an explicitly budgeted compatible technology unlock.
It cannot import all naval technologies, ignore contradictory research choices, or grant a ship design that the installed game cannot construct.

## Across Every Ocean

This path prepares transport capacity, invasion planning, convoy protection, staging bases, and the first supplied bridgehead.
The opening selects a reachable theater and its staging port.
The middle stage creates a feasible operation with actual transport and cover.
The endpoint is a supplied overseas foothold that can receive reinforcements.

The focus reward is the means to conduct the operation.
It does not transfer the target port to TAN or guarantee naval superiority.
The enemy can disrupt convoys, defend the landing, damage the staging area, or isolate the bridgehead.
A failed invasion consumes real forces and equipment.

## The Tuvan Marines

The amphibious branch trains actual marine formations using the installed unit and technology families.
Its first stage develops the required doctrine and instruction.
Its next stage prepares a paid amphibious group.
Its mature stage supports replacements, port repair, and sustained operations beyond the landing.

Marines are not an excuse for a new custom subunit.
They use existing compatible equipment, models, counters, and voice behavior.
Additional specialized content would require a separate accepted design and a complete custom-unit asset package.

## No Border Required

This anchor expands the event's target policy beyond adjacent countries.
It identifies independent opponents anywhere in the world and organizes them into practical theaters.
A distant target can be politically eligible while its operation remains blocked by range, basing, or transport.
The interface explains that distinction.

The AI chooses a useful reachable theater, builds the necessary support, and then advances.
It does not send its entire army toward an unreachable island while an active land front collapses.
The player's strategic priorities change the order and allocation of the global campaign without exempting independent opposition forever.

## Every Country Is Our Neighbor Now

This path replaces the earlier neighbor-only pursuit with a global submission policy.
Existing equal allies receive a clear choice between genuine subordination and opposition when the campaign reaches them.
A human government can refuse.
Faction membership alone never completes the world-conquest condition.

Subjects that already belong to TAN remain part of its empire when their actual relationship and territorial control are valid.
An equal ally cannot obtain permanent exemption by joining TAN immediately before the global transition.
The policy must end incompatible faction protection before a war is opened against a refusing former ally.

Submission is an alternative to a war when the recipient accepts valid subject terms.
It is not an unconditional annexation button.
The military branch remains available against refusal or a later subject revolt.

## The Last "Tannu What?"

This final anchor organizes the remaining global opposition into the last campaign.
It requires real overseas reach and a functioning global war economy, or a world already unified through valid subordination.
It unlocks the final settlement check and its outcome presentation.

Completion is determined by the map and actual political relationships.
Every remaining organized sovereign opposition must be defeated or subordinated.
An independent friendly country, an unoccupied hostile island, or an effective rival government with territory prevents victory.
The focus cannot replace that check with a flag saying the world is conquered.

## The endpoint

The final state is a Tuvan world empire.
The post-victory record can connect that empire to the particular aid categories and donors that helped start it.
The ending should use the campaign's real history rather than claiming every government contributed.
A player who won with limited aid receives a different emphasis from one funded by most of the world.

The ending closes repeated target acquisition, submission requests, and reinforcement rewards.
Existing armies and countries remain in their resolved state.
No additional super-event is required beyond the two specified major moments unless a later accepted presentation pass adds one with its own purpose and licensed audio.
