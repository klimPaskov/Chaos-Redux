# 075 · Focus-tree architecture

## A complete Tuvan campaign

The dedicated tree opens when the event establishes TAN as its host.
Its public opening concerns protection, development, and self-defense.
The conquest families are concealed until TAN's own preparation makes them available.
The global families require actual continental dominance and the world-end transition.

The tree preserves the current ruler, ruling ideology, characters, and ordinary technology identity.
It does not offer four unrelated ideology trees merely to fill the screen.
Political choices concern how the existing government organizes foreign aid, manages its sponsors, trains its army, and governs its conquests.

The full tree should contain a substantial opening and buildup, several interacting military and industrial paths, a distinct conquest government, and a complete overseas endgame.
The path stages in Parts 6 through 9 are design units for meaningful focuses.
They are not permission to turn each sentence into another small bonus node.
Exact node count and coordinates belong to the final tree layout.

## Architecture map

```text
    +-- Concentrated patronage ---+-- Coordinated aid administration
    +-- Industrial independence --+
    |
    +-- We Are Surrounded ------ Defensive appeals and regional access
    +-- A Few Factories Would Help -- Civilian base / arms / logistics
    +-- Just for Self-Defense -- Infantry / specialists / mobile forces / air
    +-- No One Is Watching ----- Cover / reserve organization / staff planning
                                      |
                                 Tannu What?
                                      |
    +-- They Actually Believed Us -- Convert the network into a war economy
    +-- Return Their Generosity --- Former-donor campaigns
    +-- Every Border Is a Front --- Simultaneous-front command
    +-- The Tuvan War Machine ----- Repair, replacement, and standardization
    +-- From Tuva to the Sea ------ A supplied first coastline
    +-- Nobody Asked Tannu Tuva --- Direct administration / subordinate states
    +-- Tannu Tuva, Great Power --- Earned continental position
                                      |
                          Continental control + 1000 Chaos
                                      |
    +-- A Navy, Apparently / Tuvan Shipyards
    +-- Across Every Ocean / The Tuvan Marines
    +-- No Border Required / Every Country Is Our Neighbor Now
    +-- The Last "Tannu What?"
```

The diagram shows major relationships, not exact connector syntax.
The three sponsorship approaches are mutually exclusive institutional commitments.
Their common aid-administration endpoint is reachable from any one approach.
It must not accidentally require completion of all three.

The industrial, military, access, and staff families are complementary.
Each has internal choices, but none should make the basic reveal impossible through an unintended exclusion.
The player can build infantry and later add armor or aviation when the material basis exists.
Research and industrial branches should have useful endpoints before the reveal and retained value afterward.

## What is hidden

The foreign-facing opening does not display a long row of global conquest claims.
Conquest focus names, previews, filters, and navigation shortcuts must not reveal the hidden family to an uninvolved observer through the event's own presentation.
TAN's player knows the event's purpose and can see the requirements for committing to it.
Normal espionage and game interfaces retain their ordinary limits.

The global naval route does not appear as a completed or selectable route merely because Chaos reaches 1000.
It opens only with the earned continental condition and a valid world-end start.
Disabling evolutions does not remove the baseline conquest tree.

## Pacing

The first useful aid route should open within the opening focus period.
There must not be a year of compulsory administrative focuses before the event can request equipment.
Use roughly 35-day focuses for focused institutional choices, roughly 70 days for major programs, and a short final commitment for the reveal.
These are pacing anchors, not a requirement that every node share one duration.

The first political choice changes a real capability.
The following paths offer different combinations of time, material investment, dependency, and operational readiness.
Longer routes earn a better-supported army or a stronger economy.
They do not simply repeat the same national modifier in five small increments.

## Reward families

The tree uses concrete permissions and outcomes: wider request access, better package selection, training capacity, paid development projects, valid production licenses, research assistance, template access, supply preparation, occupation choices, naval preparation, and campaign authority.
Political power and experience support those activities where appropriate.
They do not replace the activities themselves.

Rewards tied to material grants show their backing.
A completed focus may activate the use of an accumulated reserve or finish a paid project.
It does not independently award the same donated equipment a second time.
A bypass removes an obsolete step without granting its unpaid material reward.

## National-spirit lifecycle

At most three focus-tree-created Tuvan national spirits coexist.
One represents the current diplomatic or strategic posture, one summarizes funded industrial development, and one summarizes the military organization.
Most opening states need only the posture carrier.

| Carrier | Aid phase | Reveal and conquest | Global phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic posture | Defensive credibility and the chosen sponsor administration | Public mobilization and limited former-donor campaign effects | Continental war administration |
| Industrial development | Completed funded capacity and its current limitations | Repair and production organization | Naval-industrial conversion within the same carrier |
| Military organization | Reserve training and logistics preparation | Multi-front organization and replacement | Amphibious and overseas coordination |

A focus upgrades or replaces the appropriate carrier.
It does not add a fourth permanent spirit for a small branch reward.
State-bound projects keep their geographic conditions even when one national summary describes them.
Existing unrelated vanilla spirits are not deleted to make this budget appear satisfied.

## Navigation and layout

Use native focus navigation and meaningful filter families for diplomacy, industry, army, air, conquest, and the overseas campaign.
Hidden navigation anchors follow the same visibility rules as their branches.
Avoid giant empty spaces reserved for content the player cannot yet reach.

A separate focus inlay is not required.
Readiness already has a home in the decision category and a concise linked focus requirement.
Duplicating it in another custom window would add maintenance and visual clutter without a new decision.
The final tree still requires inspected and rendered layout evidence at its supported scales.
