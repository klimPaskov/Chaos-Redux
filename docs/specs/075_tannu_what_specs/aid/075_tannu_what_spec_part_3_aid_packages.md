# 075 · Aid packages and ownership

## A request is a real offer

Every request identifies a donor, recipient, resource family, amount, useful destination, and payment or commitment.
The amount is based on the donor's usable surplus after its own needs and previous obligations.
The quoted amount is fixed for that offer.
Acceptance rechecks availability before anything is transferred.

A partially affordable offer can become the clearly stated smaller option.
It must not silently charge a different amount from the one the player approved.
A donor that can no longer provide even the minimum useful package can refuse or let the offer expire.
The event does not create the missing balance.

A transfer reaches only one destination.
Paid quantities are either still in declared custody, received, consumed by their documented purpose, returned once, or lost through a documented interruption.
The same rifles cannot be in the donor stockpile, TAN's stockpile, and a newly raised division simultaneously.

## Equipment

Rifles, support equipment, artillery, trucks, trains, tanks, and aircraft use actual compatible stock.
Equipment model and variant identity should survive the transfer when the game exposes that information.
The system chooses from what the donor owns, with older surplus often more likely than the equipment its own formations urgently need.
A donor is not restricted to one hard-coded historical equipment model.

A recipient can keep equipment that is not yet useful.
TAN may need a template, pilots, a suitable airbase, maintenance, fuel, or a relevant technology before it can employ that donation well.
The offer should explain the immediate shortage without automatically granting every missing prerequisite.
A model that the installed game cannot transfer or use is omitted from that donor's offer pool and remains an explicit compatibility gap.

The basic request is a small share of real exportable surplus, with a family-specific maximum.
Higher evolutions increase this ceiling and favor heavier packages when TAN can support them.
A request for tanks does not become large merely because the donor owns thousands of rifles.
Stock and demand are evaluated in the requested family.

| Family | What TAN receives | What the donor gives up | Important limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Infantry and support equipment | The actual delivered stock | The same equipment quantity | Keep the donor's reinforcement and replacement reserve |
| Artillery and anti-air equipment | Compatible pieces with their model identity | The matching stock | A formation still needs its other equipment |
| Trucks and trains | Transport stock for supply and mobile forces | The corresponding vehicles | Do not count transport already committed elsewhere |
| Tanks | Compatible armored equipment | Actual available variants | Training, fuel, and production access are separate |
| Aircraft | Aircraft stock or a supported transfer package | The aircraft actually removed | No free pilots, airfields, or unlimited range |
| Fuel | A real fuel delivery up to available storage | The same fuel quantity | Overflow is rejected or reduced before payment |

## People and military support

Permanent recruitment support is different from native volunteers and expeditionary forces.
The donor explicitly transfers an affordable number of available recruits for Tuvan service.
Those people leave the donor's available manpower and enter TAN's manpower or a paid training cohort once.
This is not a population increase and must not create a second recruitable population base.

A civilian refugee appeal does not authorize compulsory recruitment.
Where a project uses the existing migration system, arrival and recruitment remain separate outcomes under that system's rules.
The event should not invent demographic numbers for flavor and then convert those numbers into military strength.

Native volunteer and expeditionary formations remain under their actual ownership rules.
They may assist a genuine defensive war when their diplomatic prerequisites are met.
They can train alongside TAN or support a front, but the reveal does not convert them into free Tuvan divisions.
Foreign units belonging to a newly hostile donor must follow a verified recall or return process before the opening war is committed.
Their removal is excluded from TAN's reveal-readiness calculation.

Military advisers create limited training and organizational benefits while their agreement is active.
Experience genuinely learned remains after they leave.
Continuing access, future training bonuses, and foreign-owned assets can end with the agreement.
A donor's officer is not copied into TAN's commander roster without a separately researched, lawful character-transfer route.

## Industry and production

A factory gift is a completed, funded construction project in a valid Tuvan location.
The donor commits real industrial effort, construction capacity, or another supported material contribution.
TAN supplies a usable site and any required transport or supporting materials.
The completed factory belongs to TAN.
Its sponsor does not retain a remote destruction switch.

Production assistance can instead reserve a donor's productive capacity for deliveries over time.
This remains a foreign contract until each installment arrives.
It cannot be counted as permanent domestic industry or as a future source of supply after TAN attacks its sponsor.

When physical building slots are exhausted, further aid may fund improvements to existing industrial districts through the industrial-depth system in Part 4.
This represents funded productive capacity within a developed complex.
It must have a real cost, completion time, location, loss condition, and bounded output effect.
It cannot be recorded as several physical factories that the state does not contain.

## Research and licenses

Advisers and research partners help in fields where the donor has useful expertise and TAN has a genuine gap.
The normal outcome is a limited bonus, an eligible research-sharing benefit, or a supported license.
A major grant may provide one clearly identified compatible technology when the accepted package and installed game support that route.
A donor's whole technology tree is never imported through a routine aid request.

License arrangements preserve their normal duration, access, and production restrictions.
Owning donated equipment does not by itself prove that TAN can manufacture it.
Doctrines retain the installed doctrine system's own prerequisites, branch exclusions, and mastery behavior.
The plan adds no separate technology folder and no parallel doctrine tree.

## Political and economic support

Diplomatic sponsorship can improve relations, support a faction application, establish valid access, or create a supported guarantee.
A donor may spend political power to make that commitment.
Political power is not described as money and is not automatically copied to TAN.
A direct political-power transfer is only eligible if the implementation verifies a meaningful bilateral mechanism and presents both sides accurately.

Economic assistance uses a real project, licensed production, supported international-market funding, or another established economic system.
There is no generic currency invented merely to make every donor capable of paying.
Optional market funding is unavailable without its working feature and transfer adapter.
The request selector then offers another genuinely supported family.

## Delivery and landlocked access

Worldwide contact does not guarantee worldwide physical delivery.
For each equipment or recruit package, use a verified overland route, a supported native transfer route, or a contracted transit arrangement.
A port in a friendly country is useful only when the remaining corridor to TAN is also valid.
TAN's opening faction diplomacy and transport projects help establish those routes.

Remote donors with no physical corridor can provide research cooperation, diplomatic support, or a funded project only when its own delivery model remains valid.
Physical goods can wait in donor custody under a pending agreement.
They do not increase TAN's strength before arrival.
The event must not silently teleport every type of cargo because TAN is landlocked.

A transit loss cannot create both a casualty reward and a returned full shipment.
Human losses use the existing casualty or population system where applicable, with separate ownership of those totals.
The event does not award Chaos merely for an administrative delay.

## Compatibility principle

The aid pool expands through verified capabilities.
Ordinary supported equipment, recruits, industry, fuel, training, and diplomacy remain the core experience.
Licenses, market funds, special equipment, native volunteers, and expeditionaries appear only when their real conditions can be satisfied.
Unsupported paths are not described as functioning and are not replaced by mislabeled free equipment.
The technical review must identify every blocked resource family before claiming implementation completeness.
