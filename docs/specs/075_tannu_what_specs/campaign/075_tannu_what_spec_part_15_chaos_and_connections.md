# 075 · Chaos consequences and cross-event connections

## One ordinary occurrence

The Diplomacy cluster selects Event 075 as one Low member with Chaos level 1.
That describes its opening diplomatic premise.
The event's later consequences can become much larger without reclassifying every aid request as another ordinary event occurrence.

The original root starts the episode once and receives the ordinary scheduler treatment once.
Aid letters, project stages, focus completions, mobilization, donor reactions, new-border checks, and the world-end continuation do not reset the random-event timer or charge another cluster occurrence.
The accumulated risks come from the campaign's actions and milestones.

## Shared consequences stay shared

Ordinary war declarations, peace settlements, annexations, subject creation, faction changes, military growth, factory growth, deaths, and other shared consequences remain the responsibility of the existing global mechanics.
Event 075 does not add another identical Chaos payment for the same war or annexation.
TAN's human population and conventional army do not receive a special exemption.

The following event-specific values are proposed balance seeds.
They require review against the actual shared implementation and save-persistent ownership rules before implementation.
The campaign's own additional contribution is deliberately bounded.

| Event 075 outcome | Proposed event-specific Chaos | Bound and rationale | Shared consequence kept separate |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Root fires and the aid office opens | 0 | No blanket root payment | Ordinary scheduler and event weighting only |
| Request, refusal, audit, delivery, or project bookkeeping | 0 | No farming through diplomatic paperwork | Any genuinely affected shared economic measure still behaves normally |
| Evolution activates | 0 | Eligibility is not a catastrophe | Existing global evolution handling |
| First military reveal commits successfully | +10 | Once for the public collapse of the defensive deception | Opening wars and faction departures use their own shared consequences |
| A new neighbor becomes a target | 0 | No repeated border-scan reward | A real new war can have its ordinary shared effect |
| First former-major-donor campaign reaches a durable victory | +5 | Once in the episode, not once per donor or shipment | Annexation, peace, and subordination consequences remain shared |
| First continent is certified under the two control tests | +15 | Once in the episode, not on recertification or each continent | Territorial and economic growth have already had normal effects |
| Tuvan world-end commitment | +10 | Once, and only after the pre-existing 1000 threshold is met | Never raises Chaos merely to satisfy its own eligibility |
| First durable overseas foothold in the global campaign | +5 | Once, after an actually supplied port survives the operational holding period | War and territorial control consequences remain shared |
| TAN fails its first offensive but remains active | 0 | Tactical failure is not a reusable global relief reward | Casualties, peace, and territorial losses follow normal systems |
| Organized containment durably reverses continental dominance | -10 | Once, after a certified continent is denied for 30 days | Ordinary peace and liberation effects remain shared |
| Dominant TAN is defeated before continental certification | -5 | Once, when the episode truly ends | Ordinary victory and peace effects remain shared |
| Dominant TAN is defeated after continental certification or world-end entry | -15 | Replaces the lesser defeat relief, once | No second -5 award and no double credit for one defeat |
| Final Tuvan global victory | 0 | The political endpoint supplies no arbitrary extra catastrophe score | Shared outcome and world-end manager own their normal aftermath |

The maximum additional positive Chaos from this table is 45.
The maximum additional relief is 25 when both a genuine durable continental reversal and the later large-scale defeat occur.
A smaller early defeat permits only the relevant 5-point relief.
Repeated loss and recovery of the same state, continent, or donor capital cannot repeat these payments.

The former-major-donor milestone requires that donor's real defeat or actual submission in a campaign against TAN.
A temporary occupation of an empty border province does not qualify.
The overseas foothold requires a controlled usable port, a real overseas supply route, and a 30-day holding period.
The same conquest outcome cannot be claimed through two alternative paths to the same named milestone.

## World Threat and world-end ownership

After the first reveal, the Tuvan campaign can become a registered source in the existing World Threat system when the shared system's accepted criteria are met.
It must use the common source registration and refresh behavior.
There is no separate private World Threat meter.
Defeat removes the active source through the same shared owner, while the historical episode record remains.

The continental-to-global transition uses the shared world-end manager.
If that manager stops ordinary event scheduling, TAN's already-active campaign and its required operational tasks must not depend on another ordinary random event being drawn.
The global campaign remains a continuation of 075.
It is not a second Fire-Once catalog entry.

## The Holy Realm and God's Plan

The real Holy Realm country leading the real God's Plan faction receives the strongest opening-protector preference.
A namesake faction or a different country with a similar cosmetic title does not qualify through its text alone.
Refusal remains possible.
TAN can then seek another valid protector and continue contacting donors.

The relationship provides protection and useful contacts.
It does not grant divine authority, copy the Realm's focus tree, or make TAN exempt from ordinary human-country systems.
If the Realm is an opening land neighbor, the reveal breaks protection and includes it in the opening wars like every other bordering country.

## State fragmentation and regional powers

Soviet collapse, genuine independence waves, and comparable actual map changes alter donors, protectors, transit, and the set of neighbors.
The episode follows the resulting countries rather than a fixed Soviet-Mongolian-Chinese target list.
A new state can become a useful donor before the reveal or a target after a new border appears.

The Mongol event is a meaningful regional interaction when it has actually produced a neighboring power.
The outcome depends on current territory and relationships.
This package does not assume that an unreworked catalog entry already supplies a particular tag, army, focus, or script helper.

## Peace, global wars, and military preparation

White Peace can interrupt an existing Tuvan war through its real shared effect.
The event records the changed diplomatic state and later obeys the same legal restrictions as other wars.
It does not undo the peace in the middle of the same operation or repeatedly farm declaration effects.

A broad global-war event can create actual defensive need before the reveal.
TAN may activate paid reserves for survival without acquiring another army at its later reveal.
Shared doctrine or mastery systems can improve applicable military preparation only through their established unlocks and conditions.
This event does not grant every doctrine branch because several sponsors have different doctrines.

## Other aid and technology systems

Ordinary external equipment gifts that genuinely enter TAN's inventory can help supply its army.
They remain attributable to their own source when the source is known.
They are not recorded as completed Event 075 requests that never occurred.
This preserves both the real military stock and accurate donor reactions.

Technology assistance must remain compatible with the project's technology-union and assistance systems.
A broad helper that grants all compatible missing technology is not a safe substitute for a narrow adviser or research package.
Unreworked or internally inconsistent catalog rows do not establish an implemented integration contract.
Any such connection needs direct inspection before it becomes part of the production plan.
