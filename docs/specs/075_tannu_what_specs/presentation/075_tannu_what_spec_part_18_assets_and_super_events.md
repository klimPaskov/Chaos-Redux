# 075 · Visual assets and the two super-events

## Shared art direction

The aid phase uses official correspondence, modest offices, transport paperwork, military instruction, and unfinished industrial work.
The reveal uses the same material world at a much larger military scale.
The global campaign adds shipyards, convoys, ports, and continental command planning.
The visual link should come from repeated objects and institutions, not an unrelated fantasy style.

Use the existing HOI4 report, focus, decision, and news conventions.
Historical Tuva is a research reference for appearance and institutions.
The conquest empire is fictional.
Do not reuse an unrelated Central Asian flag, portrait, uniform, throat-singing performance, or religious symbol as though it were verified Tuvan material.

## Source and generation modes

Existing repository assets can be reused only after inspection confirms the correct subject, quality, dimensions, rights, and consumer.
Historical flags and real-person portraits follow the sourced identity-preserving workflows.
The existing TAN country supplies its ordinary identity and portraits here.
No new real-person portrait or 3D unit production is required by the accepted design.

New generic scene art and symbolic icons can use the approved generated-art workflow.
Icon generation must produce native transparent alpha where the consumer needs it.
Do not create a flat background and pretend that automatic color removal is equivalent to the required alpha workflow.
A 32-pixel decision icon and a 94 × 86 focus icon are separate designs, not arbitrary reductions of one crowded source.

Use the project converter for ordinary DDS consumers and validate its final dimensions, format, alpha, and actual file readback.
Achievements use their dedicated template-aware three-state processing tool.
Final production manifests include consumer, source mode, source or generation provenance, license where relevant, crop and size, alpha treatment, final path, and verification state.
No asset produced later may be called approved solely because its filename matches the plan.

## First super-event · Tannu What?

The user-provided title is retained as the concept anchor.
The final typographic treatment can use **TANNU WHAT?**.
The political moment is the sudden opening of all current border wars with a paid, prepared army.
The illustration should emphasize grounded scale: organized troops, supply columns, varied foreign equipment, and a visible industrial or logistical foundation.
It should not portray an already conquered world.

Description direction connects the apparently modest diplomatic appeals to the military that now exists.
The quote or cultural remark must be independently researched before selection.
A verified modern allusion can be used deliberately as an out-of-period reference, but cannot be passed off as a 1930s Tuvan official's words.
The button needs a researched and tone-checked direction appropriate to this first surprise.
No final attributed quote, lyric fragment, or sourced slogan is selected in this package.

Audio direction is an opening with enough space to establish the unexpected subject, followed by a controlled military sense of scale.
Tuvan vocal or instrumental material is a research possibility, not a license to use any recording labeled “throat singing.”
The recording's actual origin, performers, date, composition rights, recording rights, and permitted distribution all require evidence.
No recording has been selected or cleared.

## Second super-event · Continental power goes global

This is a distinct major event after actual continental certification and valid 1000-Chaos world-end entry.
Its final subtitle or second title wording remains a research task.
The user-specified scenario name remains **Tannu What?**.
The image emphasizes shipyards, transport routes, naval preparation, and the strategic reach of an actual continental state.

The text recognizes the specific continent and the decision to challenge the remaining independent world.
A second quote or cultural remark must have its own documented purpose.
It must not repeat the first event's surprise as though observers had never heard of the Tuvan military.
A separate audio selection should establish the broader campaign without recycling the first recording under a new filename.

## Audio production gate

Both super-events need traceable, distributable recordings.
The preferred edited duration is about one to two minutes, with two minutes the normal upper bound unless the accepted scene requires a justified exception.
Research must identify exact cut points, the opening, transition, ending or fade, loudness treatment, and whether editing is permitted.
Composition and recording rights are checked separately.

No synthetic placeholder tones, unlicensed commercial clip, invented public-domain status, or generated imitation recording can satisfy this requirement.
A rights-unresolved candidate stays a candidate.
The production handoff must retain working source links and item-level rights evidence alongside the final audio manifest.

## Animation and portraits

No animation is necessary for the core category or first release of this event.
Any later proposal needs a real communicative purpose, a complete frame plan, supported sprite consumer, static counterpart, and inspection at the intended scale.
The reveal should work through its scene and timing without forcing moving portraits or an animated flag.

Existing real-person portraits are retained unless a separately accepted character change requires a researched replacement.
The portrait worker owns that task when it exists.
No RunPod session, historical face reconstruction, or speculative leader selection is implied by this source package.
