# 075 · Achievement design

All six labels below are working title directions, not final localisation.
IDs are proposed stable event-scoped IDs and require collision inspection against the actual achievement registry before use.
The conditions refer to recorded gameplay, not art completion, a debug command, or a free event-opening reward.
None unlocks merely for receiving Event 075 or pressing the reveal button.

## 1 · A Very Small Request

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_075_many_small_requests`  
**Eligible country:** the TAN episode host.  
**Visibility:** visible once the aid operation begins.  
**Difficulty:** medium.

Before the reveal, receive completed permanent aid from at least 20 distinct independent donor governments, covering at least five aid families.
At least one qualifying gift must be manpower or training support, one must be material equipment, and one must be completed industrial assistance.
A donor counts once regardless of repeated gifts, cosmetic names, or a later change of faction.
A family counts only after it has a useful completed contribution.

Disqualify promises, refused or expired requests, returned support, purely borrowed divisions, transfers from TAN's own subject, and aid created through a debug or test path.
Ordinary accepted gifts from a country before it later becomes TAN's subject retain their genuine historical status.

This requires a varied functioning network, not twenty cheap repeat clicks.
The icon direction is a restrained group of stamped envelopes with distinct resource marks.
Track distinct donor identity, completed family presence, timing before reveal, and the exclusion of simulated setup gifts.

## 2 · Return Their Generosity

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_075_return_generosity`  
**Eligible country:** the TAN episode host.  
**Visibility:** hidden until the first reveal.  
**Difficulty:** hard.

After the reveal, actually defeat or subordinate a former major donor that contributed at least 10% of the aid-backed military or industrial preparation used at the reveal.
The donor must have been an independent recognized major power when its qualifying aid was delivered.
TAN must still be the surviving independent host when the result is recorded.

Temporary capital occupation, equal faction membership, a cosmetic puppet label, and a donor that provided only a revocable promise do not qualify.
Use a documented category-sensitive contribution assessment so that a few trucks are not valued as an entire industrial complex.
The exact assessment must be fixed before the campaign and preserved at reveal, not changed to manufacture an achievement afterward.

The icon direction is a supplied equipment crate whose destination has reversed.
Track donor-major status at delivery, completed contribution history, reveal snapshot, and the donor's actual defeat or subject relationship.

## 3 · Just for Self-Defense

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_075_self_defense_reserve`  
**Eligible country:** the TAN episode host.  
**Visibility:** hidden until the first reveal.  
**Difficulty:** hard.

Complete a reveal with at least 50 fully backed standard formation equivalents, with at least 50% of their manpower and equipment backing originating in completed foreign assistance.
The preparation must also include an actual domestic replacement base and enough valid support for the active opening force.
The army can include already deployed paid defensive formations, counted once.

Do not count foreign-owned volunteers, unpaid template shells, an arbitrary division split, incompatible equipment, or reserves copied by a reload.
Formation equivalents use a fixed documented baseline manpower-and-equipment requirement, so splitting one formation into five does not improve the result.
Only traced foreign quantities actually allocated to the formations count toward the foreign-backing percentage.
Unattributable native stock mixing is not presumed to be foreign, and the same donated rifle cannot back two cohorts.

The icon direction is a small official shield in front of organized military stores.
Track formation-equivalent backing, donor-origin consumption, domestic replacement capacity, opening support, and successful one-time reveal commitment.

## 4 · From Tuva to the Sea

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_075_continent_and_coast`  
**Eligible country:** the TAN episode host.  
**Visibility:** visible after the first reveal.  
**Difficulty:** very hard.

Certify a continent through the two 90% control tests and maintain a usable supplied coastal port under TAN or a loyal actual subject for 30 days.
The port must connect to the military economy through a valid route.
It need not be in Asia and the achievement does not require the 1000-Chaos world-end setting.

A foreign-access port, enemy-occupied coast, equal ally's port, one-day landing, or empty continental definition does not qualify.
The icon direction is a map-based route from an inland command point to a working port.
Track continent identity, fixed denominators, holding duration, port ownership and control, and actual supply connection.

## 5 · Every Country Is Our Neighbor Now

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_075_world_empire`  
**Eligible country:** the TAN episode host.  
**Visibility:** hidden until valid world-end entry.  
**Difficulty:** exceptional.

Win the valid Tuvan world-end scenario after continental certification and a legitimate 1000-Chaos start, with no independent organized opposition remaining.
At least one successful sustained overseas campaign must have been required and completed through an actual port and supply route.
The first global mobilization reward must not have repeated.

Disqualify a small map setup with no meaningful overseas opponent, debug victory, equal-alliance membership counted as subordination, and victory after the episode's defeat.
Ordinary loyal subjects under TAN can contribute to the real global settlement.

The icon direction is an inland state emblem extended through restrained sea routes across a globe.
Track valid world-end entry, earned continental condition, overseas holding milestone, reward integrity, and the full sovereignty check.

## 6 · The Last Donation

**Proposed ID:** `chaosx_075_containment_victory`  
**Eligible country:** a surviving independent country other than TAN that actually donated and later fought it.  
**Visibility:** hidden until that donor receives a reveal reaction.  
**Difficulty:** very hard.

After TAN has certified a continent, materially participate in the war that ends the dominant Tuvan episode while keeping the donor country independent.
The donor must have made a meaningful completed aid contribution before the reveal and remain an actual belligerent in the decisive defeat.
Material participation must be proved by a documented accepted military contribution measure, such as actual controlled liberation of a qualifying objective or verified war participation.

Joining at the last moment without meaningful action, watching an unrelated power win, receiving a scripted automatic victory, or defeating only a civil-war copy does not qualify.
If the installed game cannot prove the selected participation measure, this achievement remains blocked until a supported condition is chosen and documented.
It does not fall back to mere war membership.

The icon direction is a donor's opened aid ledger beside a recovered military standard.
Track actual pre-reveal contribution, TAN's continental certification, the qualifying war, material participation, independence, and the true episode-defeat result.

## Shared achievement integrity

Progress belongs to the actual episode and country identity.
Cosmetic changes and save/load preserve it.
Tag switching, observer or debug manipulation, duplicate reserve grants, and controlled test scenarios must follow the project's existing achievement-disqualification rules.
This package does not invent a separate global achievement mode.

All achievement checks must use real completed outcomes and stable provenance.
An implementation that cannot prove one of these conditions must report that blocker instead of weakening it silently.
Icons need the project's normal, unavailable, and achieved state production through the dedicated achievement templates and tool.
