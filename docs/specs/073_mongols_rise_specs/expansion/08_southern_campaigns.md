# Southern campaigns

## Shared access rule

Southern campaigns open from actual connected frontiers. They are not a reward that grants war goals against every country south of Mongolia at once. Each direction has a different military problem and a different settlement payoff.

A completed northern Chinese, Central Asian, or Persian settlement can open the relevant approach. Political route choice does not force an order among the southern campaigns.

## The rest of China

The deeper Chinese route follows a northern settlement. The player can support the Yuan-style government's regional consolidation, negotiate with remaining governments, or expand direct imperial administration.

The main problem is holding a large population and industrial area while cavalry remains weak in dense urban and defended terrain. The route offers support allocation, administrative agreements, and limited military objectives. It does not remove those terrain problems.

Its capstone is a coherent Chinese settlement with a dependable contribution to the empire. Full control of every Chinese state is a larger optional ambition, not a prerequisite for obtaining any value from the route. A regional government that completes its own campaign should gain actual standing in the empire and succession.

## Korea

The Korean direction opens from a real approach through northeastern China or through a later valid maritime plan. Its first choice concerns a limited settlement with the current sovereign or a military campaign against the actual controlling power.

A regional association can preserve Korean government and provide a defined contribution. Direct occupation offers more control and a greater political burden. The route does not presume that one Chinese settlement gives Mongolia the right to bind Korea.

The capstone is a viable peninsula settlement with a secure approach. Follow-up concerns coastal supply, regional autonomy, and relations with nearby maritime powers. A fleet remains an actual production and naval problem.

## India

The Indian direction opens through a viable Central Asian or Persian approach. Mountain barriers and long supply lines are the first problem. The route begins with a frontier objective that can lead to a larger campaign when transport and support are ready.

The player can seek a limited tribute relationship, support a regional settlement using an existing country, or pursue direct conquest. Current colonial, independent, or fragmented ownership determines diplomacy. A demand does not skip the actual sovereign power.

The payoff is access to a substantial regional economy and a new imperial frontier. Its capstone requires a settled foothold and a functioning route, not automatic mastery of the whole subcontinent. Further expansion brings difficult occupation and a choice between direct control and regional government.

## Southeast Asia

The Southeast Asian direction develops from a viable Chinese or other southern frontier. Jungle, difficult terrain, rain, and fragmented transport are deliberate counters to the cavalry empire. The player needs ports or connected land transport where the actual route requires them.

The route favors limited campaigns, transit agreements, and tribute from reachable countries. Its military anchors improve planning and support without pretending mounted movement is equally effective in every environment.

The capstone is a sustainable regional agreement or foothold. A failed campaign can end with withdrawal and a limited settlement. It does not require the empire to fight indefinitely for a scripted total annexation.

## Continuing play and AI

Each southern settlement can contribute trade, equipment, access, or regional military support according to its actual resources and terms. No country pays all possible rewards because the route is late in the tree.

AI should select southern opportunities that match its current approach and support capacity. It should not prefer India or Southeast Asia merely because those branches are geographically large. A blocked mountain or jungle offensive is a valid failure that can reduce Momentum and expose the empire's wider commitments.
