# Opening and sovereignty

## Selecting Mongolia

The event uses the existing Mongolian country as its actor. Reduced territory, an unpopular government, weakness, or subject status do not by themselves disqualify it. There must be an owned and controlled reception location with a valid capital arrangement. A country that exists only as a tag with no territory waits until it can actually receive a country event and army. A restoration equivalent to this empire makes another opening unavailable.

The restoration does not erase an unrelated civil war, debt, shortage, or population crisis. Its military muster is exceptional. Its civilian country remains part of the ordinary world.

## The first offer

The country-facing opening presents acceptance of the restoration as the intended campaign. A human can refuse the proclamation. Refusal ends this one-time attempt without spawning a punishment civil war, removing the player's government, or granting the horde anyway. AI normally accepts when it has a valid reception site and a plausible path to survival. Its decision must account for occupation and overwhelming opposition, not a universal 90 percent chance.

Acceptance commits the actor, grants the tier-appropriate muster, opens the dedicated focus tree, and creates the new political mandate. Its proclamation can challenge an existing government without pretending that every ideology has become hereditary monarchism overnight. The route selected in the tree determines the lasting institutions.

## Military arrival

The initial field force appears immediately in safe reception areas. Its equipment is supplied with the force. A separate reserve pool supplies reinforcement and later formations. The full tier entitlement is visible, including troops waiting for a safe reception location. Deferred formations are not silently discarded.

The player is warned when congestion or a lost reception site prevents deployment. A prepared muster reserve can be called forward without paying for the same soldiers twice. It cannot appear inside enemy-controlled land or at a future khanate that does not yet exist.

Opening commanders are drawn from valid Mongolian characters and the later vetted character package. The design needs an offensive field commander, a logistics specialist, and a commander suitable for the personal guard. It does not require inventing an unresearched descendant of Genghis Khan. Institutional command holds the office while any required character research remains blocked during development.

## Independence confrontation

An independent Mongolia can proceed to early expansion after the two opening focus anchors. Existing alliances are handled deliberately. Proclamation alone does not delete faction relationships or immediately create a second faction.

A subject Mongolia sends a sovereignty demand to its actual overlord. The overlord can recognize independence with a temporary nonaggression settlement, negotiate continued association without direct control, or reject the demand. The human overlord sees the consequences before responding. A refusal opens an independence-war route using the actual diplomatic relationship and the required legal transition.

The horde does not start an unrelated foreign war while still prevented from doing so by subject rules. A delayed or blocked independence war is surfaced as the current campaign problem. It does not strand the focus tree behind an invisible restriction. The war need not end with annexation of the overlord. Recognition of Mongolian sovereignty is a sufficient limited objective where the existing peace systems permit it.

## Early focus spine

**Restoration proclaimed** is the first short anchor. It establishes the mandate and gives access to the army explanation, reception status, and regional opportunities.

**Authority of the Great Khan** is the second short anchor. It opens expansion demands and the three political routes. The first campaign does not require a completed constitution, a rebuilt Karakorum, or new industrial construction.

The normal independent-country timing is two seven-day anchors. These are intentional emergency exceptions to normal focus lengths. A target still receives its response interval. The timing promises access to a demand, not guaranteed victory or an unconditional declaration that ignores diplomacy.

## Target choice

The opening displays a small set of currently reachable campaigns. Each shows the territory involved, current owner, likely alliance exposure, principal reward, and reason it is available. A larger neighbor remains a valid risk. It is not advertised as weak merely because it owns land on the historical route.

The player can first unite adjacent Mongol territory, approach northern China, demand limited tribute from a vulnerable neighbor, or prepare a Central Asian or Siberian route. A route without a legal reachable target remains visible as a future ambition but does not occupy a primary action slot.

## Continuity

The opening does not replay after a save reload, a change of ruler, a civil war, loss of the capital, or an Evolution unlock. A successful successor can recover imperial standing later through the recovery route. It cannot claim the original muster again.
