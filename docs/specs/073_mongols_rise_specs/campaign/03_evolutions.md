# Evolutions

## One campaign, progressively stronger institutions

The three Evolutions deepen the existing campaign. They do not start a new event, change its fire-once status, or replace the player's political route. The currently active Evolution determines the opening entitlement when Mongolia accepts the restoration.

An Evolution unlocked during the campaign adds its unclaimed entitlement and opens its content. Previously received soldiers, equipment, and one-time rewards remain received even if they were lost. A defeated player does not get a new opening army merely because the world crosses another Chaos threshold.

## The Yassa Restored

Eligibility begins at Chaos 200. This tier gives the horde a larger muster and stronger offensive organization. Its distinctive content is the binding imperial settlement behind the army.

The law branch gains decisions about military discipline, the obligations of tumen commanders, the treatment of tributaries, and a succession arrangement. Appointments become politically important. A capable commander who accepts the settlement helps the next campaign. A powerful commander denied a promised office becomes a named political dispute.

The Heir emphasizes a personal law proclaimed by the Great Khan. The New Great Khan uses written service obligations and administrative accountability. The Council negotiates which obligations belong to the center and which remain regional. These are different paths through a shared problem, not identical focuses with different icons.

Military gains improve attack, breakthrough, reconnaissance, and the ability to maintain an advance. Defensive weakness remains. Law does not make cavalry suitable for holding a fortified industrial front indefinitely.

## The Khanates Return

Eligibility begins at Chaos 400. The opening entitlement becomes a major regional army. Established khanates gain stronger regional armies, deeper tribute agreements, and more independent campaign responsibilities.

This tier expands the choices after a large conquest. Direct government keeps more immediate control but increases administrative strain. A khanate takes responsibility for a region and can campaign there, but its ruler now matters in the next succession. Several strong khanates can support several fronts or challenge a weak center.

Golden Horde, Chagatai, Ilkhanate, and Yuan-style settlements receive their full regional identities. The tier does not instantly release all four across land that Mongolia does not own. A khanate must have an actual territorial foundation, a valid country arrangement, and a negotiated relationship.

The new political content includes joint campaigns, disputes over overlapping ambitions, a regional refusal to provide troops, and mediation of route rights. Human-controlled khanates receive the relevant choices themselves.

## Universal Khan

Eligibility begins at Chaos 600. The opening entitlement becomes enormous and supports several simultaneous campaigns when the empire has the supply and command structure to field it. Reinforcement opportunities are larger, but they still consume actual recruitment or received obligations.

The universal claim is a late political milestone. It requires a functioning imperial center, substantial achievements in several different regions, and a credible Authority position. The Evolution provides access to the claim. Reaching Chaos 600 does not automatically proclaim universal rule.

After proclamation, the empire can issue broader submission demands, pursue faster follow-up campaigns, and coordinate stronger khanates. Remote countries still need a meaningful diplomatic or military connection before a demand becomes more than rhetoric. Refusal does not teleport armies to a distant coast.

Universal rule also raises expectations. Losing a regional capital, failing to defend a submitted ruler, or allowing tribute disputes to spread damages its credibility. The final offensive form retains the same basic counters: cities, forts, modern weapons, exposed supply, and sustained defensive warfare.

## Progression behavior

The existing Evolution scheduler, enable settings, and appropriate notifications govern when an eligible tier actually activates. Threshold eligibility is not a promise of instantaneous activation. A higher unlocked tier includes lower-tier content without repeating lower-tier grants.

Changing a global Evolution setting does not remove a completed focus, delete units, or refund spent equipment. Development must define the existing project's setting behavior before implementation. The minimum invariant is that already claimed opening entitlement cannot become claimable again.

## Outcome diversity

A baseline restoration can become a successful regional power without receiving every late tier. An evolved empire can still fail in its first major war. Evolution increases scale and possibilities. Player choices, opposition, supply, and political cohesion decide whether that scale becomes a lasting empire.
