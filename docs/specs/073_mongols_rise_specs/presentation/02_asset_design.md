# Visual and audio asset design

## Common visual direction

The package needs readable Hearts of Iron IV assets with a consistent mounted imperial identity. The visual language should distinguish military command, law, regional government, trade routes, and political crisis. Dense detail that disappears at native size is not useful.

Flags are flat graphic designs, not fabric photographs. New flags are produced through the required image-generation workflow after symbols and identity are researched. Existing national flags remain intact unless a specific cosmetic form intentionally changes them.

Icons use separate compositions for focus, idea, decision, and achievement sizes. A large focus picture shrunk into a tiny decision icon is not a finished asset.

## Leadership and institutions

Every required real-person portrait needs an attributed source and character verification. No arbitrary face becomes a documented descendant of Genghis Khan. An institutional interim government uses a suitable people-free institutional image and a clear institutional identity.

Portrait work belongs only to the portrait-creator role. The source archive and durable portrait manifest survive temporary-folder cleanup. Styling that requires the user's RunPod workflow remains a user-controlled handoff. Other agents do not run that workflow or substitute a generated final face without approval.

## Main presentation families

The opening report emphasizes mounted troops assembled for a new campaign. Command and law images emphasize the governing settlement. Regional images distinguish Chinese industry, Central Asian transport, western campaigning, Persian administration, and a southern logistical challenge. Collapse images show political separation and surviving institutions.

Karakorum receives a report image, a major news treatment, and imagery suitable for its focus route and possible super-event. A custom on-map building is not required by this design. Adding one later requires a separate accepted asset and map-integration brief.

## Interface and animation

The two-meter inlay and territorial puzzle use native controls and validated state geometry. Decorative art must not obscure live values or imitate controls that do not exist.

Major presentation assets can have real animated variants where the skill and final design require them. Each has a static fallback, actual frame sources, a properly built sprite sheet, and a review preview. A pulsing still image is not a substitute for a required generated or sourced animation. No animation backend is claimed unless it was actually used.

## The mounted model

A dedicated mounted unit needs a coherent horse-and-rider body, separate required weapon geometry, appropriate textures, a functioning rig, and the required game animation states. The firearm workflow follows the owning 3D skill: fresh Meshy 7 weapon-free body geometry and a separate geometry task for each firearm, then direct Blender assembly, rigging, animation, attachments, and export.

The source for model geometry is eligible modern professional game or tabletop concept art with usable rights and a period-appropriate identity. Archival photographs, museum images, and historical plates can support research and two-dimensional presentation but are not silently substituted as this model's concept-art input. One image is used per geometry task.

A horse-and-rider assembly cannot assume that a humanoid automatic rig will work. Horse gait, rider contact, reins, weapon grip, recoil, and ground contact require direct review. The exported unit must have correct scale, materials, animation, locators, and sound at runtime.

## Sound

Selection, movement, and weapon sounds need suitable real licensed sources. Generated audio and self-recorded replacements are not allowed by the supplied production rules. A country-wide infantry selection voice must not be overwritten merely because a custom cavalry unit cannot select its own voice consumer.

Super-event music has its own source and rights review. The visual team coordinates image timing with the audio and text teams after the final presentation is approved.

## Dimensions and delivery

The asset manifest in the planning folder records the required native sizes, consumer families, source modes, ownership, and proof. Every final item must be inspected at actual display size. Repository conversion tools produce the final DDS files. Temporary PNGs or previews alone do not satisfy an in-game asset requirement.

No final art, portraits, models, animation, audio, or GUI renders were produced as part of this planning package. Their briefs are complete enough to assign work, while their production and source checks remain open.
