# Achievement design registry

A01 through A16 are planning IDs and working directions. They are not final titles, descriptions, runtime registry numbers, or final localisation. Allocate unique runtime IDs through the existing achievement registry after inspection. All entries require the existing campaign eligibility rules and exclude developer-only force and auto-completion unless the framework explicitly says otherwise.

## A01: First sustained mounted campaign

**Title direction:** First sustained mounted campaign.

**Description direction:** Explain this exact sustained achievement and its key restriction in ordinary player language. Do not reuse this planning paragraph as final localisation.

**Eligible countries:** MON restoration.

**Unlock:** Complete a first substantial campaign and retain its lawful settlement through the hold period.

**Disqualifiers:** Forced test entry, duplicated recapture reward, noncavalry normal field army.

**Difficulty and visibility:** Medium. Visible.

**Why it is not trivial:** Requires war and durable settlement, not merely accepting the event.

**Icon direction:** Mounted standard above a secured frontier.

**Tracking:** Restoration occurrence, army eligibility, objective and hold record.

## Shared asset and proof requirements

Each achievement needs the exact three-state asset treatment from the existing immutable templates, a unique registered consumer, valid localisation, and positive and negative tests. Icons belong in the existing central achievement asset structure. Do not create an event-local parallel achievement engine. Continuous cavalry eligibility must be tracked over the relevant campaign interval, not sampled only on the final day.
