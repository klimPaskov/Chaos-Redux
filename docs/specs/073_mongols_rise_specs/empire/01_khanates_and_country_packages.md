# Revived khanates and country packages

## A khanate is a country

A revived khanate needs a coherent territory, an owned and controlled capital, a viable economy, a sustainable military, a valid leadership arrangement, and a clear relationship with the imperial center. It remains meaningful if the empire later collapses.

Use an appropriate existing country before adding a new tag. A country already created by another Chaos Redux event must not be duplicated. Existing human-controlled countries retain their choices. A new focus tree is appropriate for a genuinely new package or an explicitly accepted replacement. An existing developed tree receives compatible regional content instead of being silently discarded.

The designation Golden Horde, Chagatai, Ilkhanate, or Yuan-style administration identifies a regional settlement. It does not authorize the transfer of territory that Mongolia neither owns nor has the legal right to assign.

## Golden Horde package

**Country purpose.** A western military partner with a strong mounted identity, responsibility for its frontier, and leverage in imperial succession.

**Opening.** Receive only the assigned western territory, a validated regional capital, a share of actual available equipment and manpower, and a limited mounted field force. A country's inherited forces remain separate from its new grant. Its command structure must be viable without depending on an immortal Great Khan in Mongolia.

**Political path.** Choose a campaigning khan with wider military initiative or a tribute-centered governor with narrower obligations. Both recognize a defined imperial relationship. The campaigning route gains more regional war responsibility and makes stronger claims in a future kurultai.

**Military path.** Develop standard and raider cavalry, then a limited elite western force. Its field army follows the restoration's cavalry identity. Supporting equipment and transport determine how much aid it can actually send.

**Economic path.** Secure transport anchors, rebuild usable production, and decide how much of its reserve belongs to local defense or common campaigns. It cannot send an army it does not possess.

**Regional and late play.** Support a Great Western Campaign, settle a frontier, demand a larger settlement share, or seek independence after the center fails to protect the compact. Independent survival retains regional content and the mounted army.

## Chagatai package

**Country purpose.** A Central Asian route partner responsible for transit, supply continuity, and the political management of a narrow but valuable connection.

**Opening.** Receive a connected territorial foundation and a usable capital, limited mounted security forces, and the specific routes it is expected to maintain. Disconnected exclaves do not count as a finished package.

**Political path.** Choose broad local responsibility under an agreed compact or closer central supervision of key anchors. Autonomy improves local management and the khan's bargaining position. Supervision makes demands more predictable and disputes more likely when the center exceeds its rights.

**Military path.** Develop affordable corridor cavalry and a smaller campaign contingent. Its value comes from defending access and supporting operations along that access. It does not need a massive duplicate opening horde.

**Economic path.** Repair a selected route, develop existing usable resources, and manage equipment obligations. Reopening a genuine broken link can help the whole empire. Deliberately breaking and reopening a friendly route is not a repeatable reward source.

**Regional and late play.** Support Persia, the Rus, or a southern campaign. Mediate or contest transit with neighboring khanates. During collapse, preserve a viable corridor state, join a rival compact, or negotiate renewed association.

## Ilkhanate package

**Country purpose.** A Persian and adjacent regional administration connecting usable institutions, industry, and southern or Middle Eastern campaigns.

**Opening.** Reuse a valid regional country where possible, including identities changed by other formable events. Preserve legitimate existing technology and industry. Add only the agreed territory and a sustainable mounted security force.

**Political path.** Preserve more local administrative practice for dependable cooperation or accept stronger imperial direction for a larger immediate common effort. Religious and personal narratives require specific research before final writing.

**Military path.** Prepare mounted forces with support suitable for difficult approaches. Develop anti-air and anti-tank support as technology and production allow. The route does not give cavalry immunity to mountains.

**Economic path.** Rebuild workshops, allocate equipment to the center or local defense, and protect agreed transport links. Resources use actual trade or transfer mechanisms. An oil-field icon alone cannot produce a synthetic recurring stockpile transfer.

**Regional and late play.** Support a Middle Eastern objective, negotiate with a neighboring khanate, demand revised obligations, or preserve a regional successor state after a weak imperial succession.

## Yuan-style administration package

**Country purpose.** A Chinese regional government capable of supplying industry and administering a large population without turning every occupied state into a Mongolian core.

**Opening.** Use the actual Chinese territorial settlement. Preserve existing ownership outside it. Establish a viable capital and functioning industry. The package must account for civil wars, foreign occupation, and other Chinese governments still in play.

**Political path.** Choose closer central production direction or broader regional administrative authority. The former supports an immediate campaign allocation. The latter creates a more dependable long-term partner and a stronger regional voice.

**Military path.** The restoration's new imperial field formations are cavalry. Existing inherited forces require the same safe conversion decision as the center. Local security and supporting forces cannot become a loophole for a hidden conventional imperial army.

**Economic path.** Restore damaged industry, select equipment obligations, and develop a transport connection to Mongolia. Production rewards depend on actual usable facilities and cannot duplicate conquest capture rewards.

**Regional and late play.** Consolidate a Chinese settlement, support Korea or a deeper Chinese campaign, seek equal recognition in a council, or become a successor with its own regional ambitions.

## Common succession and survival

Khanates participate according to their actual agreements. They can endorse a candidate, negotiate, withhold an obligation, or reject the result through a visible political choice. The center cannot receive troops from a country that has become hostile or no longer exists.

Independence clears incompatible imperial obligations and shared benefits while preserving owned territory, surviving forces, legitimate research, and regional government. Reassociation is possible through a new compact. It does not repeat the country's initial equipment grant or erase the fact that it once received an imperial package.
