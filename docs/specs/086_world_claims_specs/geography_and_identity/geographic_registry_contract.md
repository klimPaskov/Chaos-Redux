# Geographic registry and dynamic-territory contract

The runtime resolver uses current holdings, while the geographic vocabulary and installed-map geometry are prepared data. No exact state IDs or geometry hashes are invented in this planning package. The required active installed map was not available here. A missing registry is an implementation blocker, not a reason to replace the design with six hardcoded country routes.

## Two distinct registries

The canonical state geometry registry owns state identity, province membership, outlines, wrap rules, and map provenance. World Claims' geographic metadata owns named regions, components, anchors, adjacency policies, descriptor families, and justified relationships between those regions. Geographic metadata references canonical state IDs and never becomes a second geometry source.

Country ownership, controller, claims, and selected ambitions are live inputs. They do not belong in the immutable geometry record. A peace conference changes those live inputs without changing the state outlines.

## Minimum metadata record

A record contains a stable region key, family, supported scales, canonical candidate state IDs, child/parent relationships, administrative cost group, display-page membership, name descriptors, prepared flag-family references, required anchors or anchor candidates, explicit land/maritime relationships, research references, and review status.

Membership may be static for a known region or computed from the current connected component. For computed territory, the candidate universe remains finite and installed-map-backed. The chosen envelope and anchor set are stored at adoption with their exact thresholds and territorial signature.

Every named anchor must be a genuine held or objective state with geographic significance under the record's rule. Anchor selection is deterministic: prefer the actual connected administrative center, then meaningful registered approaches, then stable state ID. A capital relocation does not silently replace the adopted anchor.

## Connected-component resolver

Build the country's owned-and-controlled connected components. Evaluate large controlled components as potential emergence centers, but ownership requirements remain necessary for proclamation. Take each meaningful component's related nearby geography and a bounded objective shell to form a candidate envelope. The shell cannot include an unrelated remote region because it gives an attractive name.

A component candidate must have at least five states at regional scale unless a reviewed smaller geographic exception exists. It must include some genuine extension or integration challenge rather than instantly completing a project identical to the starting country every firing. Suppress candidates whose territorial signature is already completed and whose successor adds no new geography.

Candidate discovery uses controlled share to recognize military success, but final Q and I use the precise ownership, holding and integration policy. A country can see what its occupation might become without receiving the identity before its settlement is complete.

## Stable selection and frozen objectives

Score candidates by native-provider fit, proportion and significance of actual holdings, connected center, reachable missing territory, geographic coherence, and continuity with the chosen project. Use stable deterministic tie-breaking. Native eligibility gates precede any provider preference score.

At adoption freeze the exact envelope, anchors, scale, formation floors, and parent project. Ownership losses do not redraw it. A missing state after an installed-map migration requires a versioned mapping or a suspended project, not deletion from the denominator. Runtime ownership changes alone do not invoke this migration.

## Maritime ambition without maritime claim leakage

A maritime relationship can qualify a set of held islands or explain a future objective. It cannot become a land adjacency used by the automatic claim wave. Countries without a foreign land neighbor receive no territorial claims in that wave, even if their selected ambition includes other shores.

A real acquired coastal foothold can produce ordinary neighboring land claims at later firings. A native provider may issue its own validated overseas claims through its actual route. Those claims remain provider-owned and do not create World Claims provenance until independently reached by the World Claims rules.

## Research boundary

The United Nations M49 system is a possible source of neutral region vocabulary and expressly distinguishes statistical grouping from political recognition. It is not evidence for 1936 state membership, historic cultural entitlement, or a native formable's requirements. Historical and physical-family records need their own primary references.

Reference consulted: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/ (targeted methodology passages only). The exact installed-state mapping and historical family research remain required work. Do not claim this package completed them.

## Completeness before runtime promotion

All active installed terrestrial states need a geometry-backed display page and a connected-component fallback path. Every proposed identity needs prepared localisation and flag coverage. Every named geographic record needs reviewed membership and research appropriate to its claim. Map changes require registry provenance validation and regenerated consumers before runtime promotion.
