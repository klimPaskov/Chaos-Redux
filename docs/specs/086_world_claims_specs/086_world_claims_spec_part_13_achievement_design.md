# World Claims: achievements and memorable campaign results

The following are achievement concepts and internal working identifiers. Final titles, descriptions, and artwork are produced during the writing and asset passes. Each completion must be based on committed campaign evidence, not a preview, current cosmetic name, console-triggered display, or approximate cached count.

## A frontier that moved: WC-A01

Demonstrate the basic expansion loop. The country receives a World Claims target in one firing, acquires ownership and control of it, and receives a new second frontier target in a later firing because of that acquired state.

The proof records the two distinct wave receipts and the owned connecting state. A depth-two grant from one evolved firing does not satisfy the two-wave achievement. The achievement can be earned by a minor without a large geographic project.

## A country nobody planned: WC-A02

Complete a dynamically assembled connected-territory ambition that did not select a native provider. The shape must include at least five states and meet the same formation rules as any other regional project.

This recognizes the campaign's unscripted geography. It does not require a deliberately ugly border or reward an invalid name. Selecting a generic identity for a native-shaped project is insufficient when the territory resolver actually used the native provider path.

## The former minor: WC-A03

A country that was not a major when it first participated completes a continental-or-larger World Claims ambition and retains its required anchors for the consolidation mission.

The original classification is recorded once for the claimant epoch. Switching country tags, releasing a new country, or changing the displayed name cannot rewrite that history. A legitimate successor adapter transfers the one original classification with the rest of the continuing claimant record.

## Integration without the shortcut: WC-A04

Earn World Claims cores in at least ten distinct targets under Evolution III's ordinary standard, with no connection-project preparation credit in those targets.

The receipt records which profile and requirements applied at the actual core award. Cores supplied by a native formable, console command, or unrelated event do not qualify. Raising the Evolution later does not remove the achievement.

## A contested heartland: WC-A05

Complete and consolidate a regional-or-larger ambition while at least one other living government retains an overlapping selected public ambition covering at least five states in the completed envelope.

The rival must be a real distinct claimant, not a dormant tag or the same continuing country under another cosmetic name. The achievement recognizes competing futures without requiring scripted war or population harm.

## A border settled: WC-A06

Complete an assurance that contains a real prior World Claims public-project crisis and reverses its eligible contribution without either party breaking the agreement during its full term.

Both parties may receive their own achievement credit from the same successful agreement, but the Chaos reversal occurs once. Empty offers between peaceful countries with no qualifying crisis do not satisfy it.

## Two centers, one country: WC-A07

Complete a transcontinental ambition with the required qualifying centers on at least two registered continents and then finish its consolidation mission.

The same state's geometry cannot count on both sides through a registry overlap. A distant naval occupation without legal ownership cannot supply the possession requirement. The test uses the adopted ambition's fixed continent groups and anchors.

## Beyond the proclamation: WC-A08

Complete the distinct finished-world milestone after a global ambition. Own and control every valid owned terrestrial state in the registered campaign map and satisfy the global integration threshold.

A global cosmetic identity by itself is insufficient. Dormant unowned state slots are handled by the explicit valid-state predicate, while foreign survivors remain part of the requirement. The achievement does not change the map or remove countries to make its own predicate true.

## Registration and presentation

Use the project's achievement system and its actual state conventions. Each concept needs one unique identifier, completion predicate, claimant persistence rule, reset policy, and full required icon family. The concepts above do not reserve unverified numeric achievement IDs in the shared catalog.

The artwork should distinguish movement, improvised geography, minor-to-great-power ascent, sustained integration, overlapping borders, diplomatic settlement, multiple centers, and final territorial completion. A recolored globe for every concept would fail to communicate their differences.

Achievement completion is idempotent. Multiplayer recipients are resolved per country using the established achievement ownership policy. A save migration must not fabricate the historical evidence required for achievements whose earlier milestones were never recorded.
