# World Claims: seeing the frontier and the future

## Opening and repeat reports

The first public report presents a worldwide change in territorial ambition. Its mechanics summary is specific to the receiving country: new targets, claim depth, and the applicable starting-Compliance entitlement. The mechanical effect commits independently of the acknowledgement so a delayed click cannot change the result.

Repeat reports emphasize what changed. A country with no new targets should not receive a dramatic list of old claims presented as discoveries. It can receive a compact no-change notice through the established event-reporting surface. High-stage reports reveal the actual enabled profile without reciting every historical Evolution.

The report's visual direction is diplomatic and geographic: competing annotated maps, crowded planning tables, border commissions, or public proclamations. It should suggest national certainty and conflicting claims rather than a literal glowing border painting the entire globe. Report imagery is not the exact territorial interface.

## One selected ambition

The main category presents one selected project. Two headline measures are sufficient: qualifying possession and integrated territory. Anchors, held-time requirements, and missing conditions appear in the state hover or the relevant decision explanation. They are conditions, not additional spendable meters.

Up to three candidate suggestions give the player a useful next choice. The larger candidate browser must stay separate from the primary paid actions. Repetition must not create dozens of identical country-specific decisions.

The selected envelope, adopted name proposal, scale, and active projects remain stable until a real transition changes them. The country should not see its suggested identity flicker because a neighboring battle changed one candidate's score.

## Exact-state territorial puzzle

The ambition's geographic display is an exact-state puzzle built from the active installed map's canonical geometry registry. Every relevant state appears in its real relative position. A state qualifies according to the same live helper that governs the formation decision and the AI.

Unresolved pieces are grey with a hatch or equivalent texture. Qualifying pieces are green with a non-color confirmation cue. Both retain an outline. The display must remain readable without relying on color alone.

Hover text names the state, current owner, current controller, claimant-specific World Claims status, actual core status, and the condition that prevents qualification. Missing countries or obsolete pointers produce a safe, truthful unresolved state rather than a stale country name.

The pieces are informational icons. They are not fake buttons and do not change territorial control. There is no state-piece animation. Candidate selection and page navigation use separate, genuine supported controls.

A category that exposes formation or integration in this family must embed its corresponding puzzle. A static picture or a detached status window is not a substitute. The category attachment audit must cover every such category explicitly.

## Regional pages for large ambitions

A continent or global project cannot remain legible as hundreds of tiny pieces in one narrow category panel. It uses precompiled regional pages with consistent framing and a separate regional overview. The overview summarizes regions without pretending to be a state-level control surface.

The runtime chooses among prepared pages and shows the states relevant to the current ambition. It does not create new textures, new sprite definitions, or new positioned GUI elements during play. The full installed-map candidate coverage and the page partition are build-time requirements.

A dynamically assembled connected territory can cross several pages. Its aggregate formation helper evaluates the complete selected envelope. Page selection changes only presentation and cannot change the qualifying denominator or make off-page states disappear from the requirement.

The geometry producer and consumer toolchain must be approved for the current registry revision. Archived producers are not silently reinstated as routine commands. A new or changed map requires a reviewed rebuild of the whole relevant artifact chain.

## Integration and project feedback

A target's hover distinguishes a consumed starting grant from an available higher entitlement. It shows whether the current control run is long enough and whether the high-Compliance interval is still accumulating. An existing core is shown as a core rather than a fictitious 100-percent occupation value.

Project tooltips show the frozen cost, target group, anchors, duration, success effect, and failure condition. Conditions are described in terms the player can inspect on the map. A hidden accounting variable or internal epoch identifier never appears as player text.

Normal core awards are aggregated into a restrained report or existing log entry. A major proclamation receives its own visible event and appropriate news treatment. Neither uses repeated popup chains for each individual state.

## Visual assets

The asset package includes the opening and relevant stage reports, significant proclamation or rivalry news, compact decision-family icons, one staged confidence-effect icon, and the prepared dynamic identity flags. Native provider assets are reused where valid.

The ordinary report image uses the project's 210 by 176 report contract. News uses 397 by 153. Decision icons are composed for 32 by 32 readability. The confidence icon is composed for 64 by 64. Achievement artwork uses the required 64 by 64 state family. Native flags use their large, medium, and small TGA sizes.

World Claims does not add a decorative category picture behind the exact-state puzzle. Map pieces come from installed geometry, never generated illustration. Their source PNGs and runtime DDS files require native-size round-trip validation.

Each illustration brief states the focal subject, framing, contrast, period vocabulary, and distinct role. Similar reports should not be near-duplicate crops of one map. Final production must record provenance or generation evidence and native-size visual review.

## Writing direction

Public text can express the absurd confidence of governments discovering that all their neighbors belong in a greater destiny. The humor belongs in the political certainty and selective historical memory. Mechanical tooltips remain precise and readable.

A successful minor's proclamation should sound specific to its new geography. Rival reports should acknowledge incompatible claims rather than speak as if one claimant's preferred history is objectively established. Cultural or historical labels must be supported by the geographic registry's research.

The package supplies narrative briefs and localisation contracts rather than final paste-ready event prose. The writing pass must match the actual committed mechanics and avoid exposing hidden future outcomes or implementation notes.

## Accessibility and truthful status

Every important result has a textual equivalent. Long dynamic names must fit supported resolutions or wrap predictably. A missing localisation, invalid provider, unavailable identity resource, or unsupported map page remains a visible validation failure during development.

The finished interface must never advertise an identity or core as ready because a cached count says so. Live territorial qualification is authoritative at preview, hover, decision availability, and final commit.
