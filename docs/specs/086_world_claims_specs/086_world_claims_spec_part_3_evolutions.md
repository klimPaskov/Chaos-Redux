# World Claims: the growth of national ambition

## Evolution availability and persistence

The Chaos thresholds are 200, 400, 600, 800, and 1000. Reaching a threshold makes its Evolution eligible. The event's normal Evolution mechanism controls activation for an already active campaign. A newly firing event may begin with the highest eligible enabled opening profile, following the shared project's evolved-opening contract.

Each Evolution is a complete benefit profile. Disabling an earlier stage does not silently prevent a later enabled stage from functioning. A later stage's own description must state the claims, Compliance, ambition scale, and core behavior it contains. The effective profile is the highest activated stage that remains enabled, or baseline when none is enabled.

Falling Chaos does not erase an activated stage. Disabling that stage prevents its further benefits while leaving already granted claims, actual Compliance, identities, and cores intact. Re-enabling it does not reset any reward receipt or reissue spent benefits. Activation, eligibility, history recording, and a higher-stage opening do not award Chaos by themselves.

For an active event, use a target Evolution interval of 90 days while a higher stage is eligible, shortened to 60 days at 800 Chaos and 30 days at 1000 Chaos. These are planning timing inputs for the verified shared MTTH adapter, not claims about a fixed deadline or a measured probability. An unresolved timing adapter remains a validation gate. No periodic whole-world scan is introduced to drive the interval.

An activation upgrades qualifying integration entitlements for existing targets and exposes newly available ambition features. Claim depth changes apply at the next actual World Claims firing. An Evolution activation is not an extra free claim wave.

## Baseline: The Neighboring Lands

Every country claims every immediately neighboring foreign land state. A World Claims target first controlled by its claimant receives a starting-Compliance entitlement of 25 percent. Higher existing Compliance is preserved.

The baseline creates a practical reason to look outward, especially for small countries with several neighbors. It does not create a formation race, force an ideology, or demand public commitment. The initial effect should be understandable from the changed diplomatic map and the claimant's occupation information.

Repeated baseline firings extend the frontier only after ownership changes. Successful integration continues in previously marked targets between firings.

## Evolution I: Greater Ambitions, 200+

The immediate ring remains complete. A limited connected second ring becomes available. Starting Compliance rises to 50 percent for qualifying World Claims occupation relationships.

Dynamic ambitions become active. The system recognizes a coherent group of actual holdings and offers a project rooted in their geography. A country can choose a regional identity, work toward its anchors, and integrate the frontier instead of receiving a generic greater-country button after an arbitrary number of wars.

A completed regional ambition can provide a new name and flag, connected claims on its remainder, a temporary period of national confidence, and access to a successor objective. The identity is optional for a human country. A country can complete the territorial project while retaining its familiar name and flag.

This stage favors compact stories: a connected valley, neighboring countries joined under one government, a peninsula's northern approaches, or an island group already substantially held. It does not immediately encourage every successful country to rename an entire continent.

## Evolution II: Continental Ambitions, 400+

Second-ring allowances become broader and selected third-ring claims become available. Starting Compliance becomes 75 percent.

Ambitions can encompass a major geographic region, peninsula, island chain, subcontinent, or connected geographic sphere. A completed regional project can reveal a larger successor. The smaller project remains an achieved historical milestone even when the country chooses to continue outward.

The successor must extend the actual center of power. It cannot merely select an unrelated large region because that region has more states. A country centered on a mountain corridor can pursue its neighboring plateau and lowlands. A country that secures a peninsula can look to its approaches. A country whose center has moved overseas can develop a new regional line around those holdings.

A large ambition gives the player choices about sequencing, corridors, and integration. It should not require collecting every province in a disconnected modern boundary before any intermediate success is recognized.

## Evolution III: Lands of Our People, 600+

The expanded claim system and 75 percent starting Compliance continue. Successfully integrated World Claims territory can now become a core.

The initial standard is 360 continuous days of control, at least 80 percent Compliance at completion, and 60 continuous days above that Compliance threshold. The country must control the state when the core commits. An ordinary integration project can provide bounded preparation credit, but cannot eliminate the holding period or the high-Compliance test.

Control before this Evolution can count when it was recorded after the country acquired World Claims provenance. A country that has already held and integrated a target for a year need not repeat the entire year. A newly conquered state never becomes a core simply because the Evolution activated that day.

World Claims cores do not remove another country's cores. They do not transfer legal ownership, close an ongoing war, or rewrite peace-conference results. Their political meaning is deliberately contested.

## Evolution IV: Greater Nation, 800+

Every immediate and second-ring target becomes available. Connected selections can reach as far as five land-state layers from owned territory. Starting Compliance becomes 100 percent.

The core standard becomes 120 continuous days of control, 90 percent Compliance, and 30 continuous days above that threshold. Starting at 100 percent therefore helps greatly, but time and continued control still matter. Losing control interrupts the process.

Regional minors can become new great powers. An appropriate completed ambition can install a greater-state name and flag, consolidate an integrated heartland, and reveal continental objectives. The country's original military, government, technologies, and country content remain intact unless another valid native route changes them.

A greater-state identity is earned through holdings and integration, not the original country's major-power classification. The formerly small country's success should be visible and specific. Its flag and name should reflect the country it has become rather than a generic administrative office.

## Evolution V: The World Is Ours, 1000+

The national project can now outgrow regional geography. Continental, transcontinental, hemisphere-spanning, and global ambitions become possible. An established connected claim network can grow outward by one additional layer at each firing, within the outer-frontier budget.

Starting Compliance remains 100 percent. The core standard becomes 30 continuous days of control, 95 percent Compliance, and 10 continuous days above that threshold. Even the most extreme profile retains a genuine integration interval.

The late game can produce several incompatible projects at once. An ideological union may face a continental empire with overlapping targets. A regional survivor may begin its own expansion from an overlooked frontier. One claimant reaching global scale does not delete every other country's ambition or reserve the world for the first player to qualify.

The stage never grants all states through an unconditional world scan. Where all remaining foreign states genuinely lie inside a country's valid immediate frontier, the final wave can naturally reach all of them. That is the conclusion of geographic expansion, not an exception that hands the whole world to a small country.

## Repeat firings at advanced stages

A repeat refreshes the actual map, fills eligible unclaimed outer targets, and discovers new ambition possibilities. It does not reset control clocks, refill consumed starting Compliance, regrant a proclamation reward, or replay every old formation report.

A country that chooses consolidation remains playable. High-Chaos ambition expands the range of possible futures. It does not require constant identity changes or a new timed mission every time the global event repeats.
