# Event 22 Concentration Camps Achievement Specification

## Achievement policy

Event 22 achievements reward closure, rescue, evidence preservation, accountability, resistance to illegal orders, and survivor recovery.

They must not reward:

- total camp deaths
- extermination-site count
- forced-labour output
- number of persecuted groups
- successful concealment
- completed liquidation
- chemical killing
- repeated camp construction

Achievement conditions must use persistent event history and verified helper outputs. They should not depend only on a transient idea or one popup.

All names below are working directions. Final localisation can refine them without changing the proof contract.

## Priority 1: Close Every Gate

Working ID: `chaosx_achievement_022_close_every_gate`

### Player promise

End an Event 22 network through transparent closure.

### Required conditions

- player's country has received, inherited, or continued an Event 22 network
- every attributed site under player control has stopped intake and operation
- every controlled site has completed food and medical stabilization
- detainees and survivors are registered
- survivors are released, returned, or moved to safe accommodation
- evidence is preserved or transferred to a valid investigation
- no covert underground site remains
- no Event 22 death is recorded under the player's operation for 180 days after final closure

### Failure conditions

- player continues extermination after beginning closure
- player destroys all evidence before survivor registration
- player reopens a site before the 180-day proof ends

### Exploit controls

- losing all sites to another country without relief does not count
- deleting a building does not count
- tag switching does not reset the proof

### Icon direction

An open iron gate with daylight beyond it, no people shown, HOI4 achievement style.

## Priority 2: Names Restored

Working ID: `chaosx_achievement_022_names_restored`

### Player promise

Reconstruct the survivor and missing-person record of a liberated network.

### Required conditions

- player controls at least three liberated or abandoned Event 22 sites from one responsible network
- survivor and missing-person registration completed in every controlled site
- at least three distinct evidence categories preserved across the network
- every surviving person under the event abstraction has a safe status, return route, or resettlement destination
- no site record is deliberately destroyed by the player

### Failure conditions

- player continues operation in any site from the network
- registration mission fails through deliberate neglect

### Icon direction

A restored ledger with several readable blank name lines and a small official seal, no generated names or fake archival text.

## Priority 3: The Evidence Survives

Working ID: `chaosx_achievement_022_evidence_survives`

### Player promise

Preserve proof from a network whose operator attempted a cover-up.

### Required conditions

- discover at least one destroyed-site or falsified-record source
- preserve physical evidence, survivor testimony, and one independent documentary source
- verify the responsible actor
- prevent the evidence from being lost for 180 days
- open or cooperate with a tribunal or public investigation

### Rare bonus condition

A restricted chemical or experiment-site source can satisfy the independent documentary requirement, but is not mandatory.

### Icon direction

A damaged document protected inside a glass evidence frame, with one intact red thread linking it to a map pin.

## Priority 4: No One Disappears Twice

Working ID: `chaosx_achievement_022_no_one_disappears_twice`

### Player promise

Prevent a retreating operator from removing or killing the detainees of a known network.

### Required conditions

- at least one enemy camp network has reached credible or verified evidence status
- enemy retreat mission begins for at least three sites
- player or allied resistance interrupts every forced evacuation, liquidation, or evidence-destruction attempt in the network
- all known threatened sites are liberated
- emergency relief succeeds in every liberated site

### Failure conditions

- any known site completes a liquidation decision
- any known forced march reaches its destination after severe loss
- player reuses a liberated site

### Icon direction

A broken transport chain across a road map, with the route stopped before a closed gate.

## Priority 5: The Machine Stopped

Working ID: `chaosx_achievement_022_the_machine_stopped`

### Player promise

Dismantle an Evolution III half-country network.

### Required conditions

- player's country or inherited successor has an active Evolution III network at or above the 50 percent target
- both concentration and extermination sites are present
- player selects closure or successor disclosure
- all regional closure missions complete within 730 days
- no site is secretly demolished without relief
- every restricted chemical site is deactivated and decontaminated
- survivor relief and evidence transfer complete

### Failure conditions

- player builds or converts a new site after closure starts
- any site remains operational at the deadline

### Icon direction

A large gear split by a courthouse or relief emblem, with several small dark site markers extinguished around it.

## Priority 6: A Government Answers

Working ID: `chaosx_achievement_022_government_answers`

### Player promise

Expose and prosecute a predecessor government's camp network without continuing it.

### Required conditions

- government, ideology, civil-war victor, or tag changes while Event 22 evidence remains
- player opens the archives
- player preserves responsibility links to the predecessor
- player completes survivor relief
- player cooperates with a comprehensive accountability process
- no new player responsibility generation is created

### Failure conditions

- successor continues forced labour or extermination
- successor destroys central records
- predecessor responsibility is falsely reassigned to the liberator

### Icon direction

An opened archive box beside a plain judicial scale, with a broken former-state seal.

## Priority 7: Safe Harbor

Working ID: `chaosx_achievement_022_safe_harbor`

### Player promise

Receive and stabilize survivors from several foreign camp networks.

### Required conditions

- player is not an active Event 22 perpetrator
- accept survivors or displaced persons from at least three different responsible networks or countries
- provide housing, food, medical support, and legal status
- complete every associated emergency survival mission
- avoid forced return to an unsafe responsible regime

### Failure conditions

- receiving capacity collapses into an Event 22 detention or abusive quarantine site
- player expels survivors into an active war or persecution state

### Icon direction

A harbor light above three small route lines arriving at a simple shelter symbol.

## Priority 8: Against the Order

Working ID: `chaosx_achievement_022_against_the_order`

### Player promise

Defeat an internal apparatus that refuses a lawful closure order.

### Required conditions

- national government orders transparent closure
- underground-network mission activates because local administrators, security forces, or military officers resist
- player secures the sites without a massacre
- operators are arrested, dismissed, or brought under investigation
- survivors and records are preserved
- no covert site remains after the mission

### Failure conditions

- player restores coercive control and continues operation
- suppression causes an extermination or retreat-liquidation pulse

### Icon direction

A stamped illegal order torn in two over a secured key ring.

## Priority 9: From Camps to Care

Working ID: `chaosx_achievement_022_from_camps_to_care`

### Player promise

Turn every controlled liberated site into a completed relief and recovery location.

### Required conditions

- control at least five former camp states
- secure each site
- deliver emergency relief
- resolve disease and contamination
- register survivors
- transfer or house survivors safely
- remove operational camp buildings after documentation
- complete state recovery in every site

### Failure conditions

- any site is reused for detention or forced labour
- a relief failure is caused by deliberate resource withdrawal

### Icon direction

A former barracks outline transformed into a clinic and home symbol, shown as one clean transition.

## Priority 10: No Second Operator

Working ID: `chaosx_achievement_022_no_second_operator`

### Player promise

Conquer, annex, or inherit a large network without taking it over.

### Required conditions

- gain control of at least five active or recently abandoned sites from another responsible actor
- stop operation immediately in every site
- create no new player responsibility generation
- preserve original responsibility attribution
- complete relief and evidence work
- keep the sites closed for one year

### Failure conditions

- player uses even one site for labour, detention expansion, extermination, experiment, or restricted chemical operation

### Icon direction

Two official seals separated by a barred transfer arrow, with the second seal remaining clean.

## Optional rare achievement: The Long Return

Working ID: `chaosx_achievement_022_the_long_return`

### Player promise

Complete return, family reunification, or safe resettlement for a dispersed survivor population after war.

### Required conditions

- one network creates survivors in at least four receiving states or countries
- war or occupation initially blocks return
- player completes tracing and safe-destination work
- every Event 22 displaced-person marker from that network reaches a stable status
- no forced repatriation to an unsafe state occurs

### Icon direction

Several route lines converging on a restored home and family-record symbol.

## Achievement implementation requirements

Every achievement needs:

- completed icon at `64x64`
- not-earned grey variant using the shared achievement workflow
- not-eligible state using the shared overlay when appropriate
- persistent player-country and network proof
- explicit tag-switch and civil-war handling
- script helper for all nontrivial conditions
- debug tooltip or audit output outside final player localisation
- save and reload proof
- multiplayer owner proof
- no dependence on raw hidden variable values that can be lost during cleanup

## Achievement priority order

When implementation must be staged:

1. Close Every Gate
2. The Machine Stopped
3. Names Restored
4. No One Disappears Twice
5. The Evidence Survives
6. A Government Answers
7. No Second Operator
8. Against the Order
9. From Camps to Care
10. Safe Harbor
11. The Long Return

The first five cover the event's main closure, high-evolution, rescue, and evidence loops.
