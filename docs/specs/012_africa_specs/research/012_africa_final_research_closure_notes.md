## Research standards to preserve

- Use UNESCO General History of Africa as broad historical background.
- Use UNESCO World Heritage pages for Aksum, Great Zimbabwe, Kilwa, and Meroe site anchors.
- Use African Union Agenda 2063 and the OAU Charter for modern unity and anti-colonial institutional language.
- Use National Archives material for Marcus Garvey and the Black Star Line.
- Use Met Heilbrunn Timeline pages for Asante, Benin, Kongo, Luba, Lunda, and regional art motifs.
- Use Wikimedia Commons only after reading each file page license and recording composition, performance, and recording rights when audio is involved.
- Do not use quote websites as final source unless a primary or reliable text cannot be found and the uncertainty is marked.
