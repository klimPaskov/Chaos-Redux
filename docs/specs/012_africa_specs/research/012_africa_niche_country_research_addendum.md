# Event 012 Africa — Niche African Polities Research Addendum

This addendum expands the research base for Event 012 with additional historical African polities, royal houses, riverine states, island kingdoms, trade cities, and high-chaos fantasy inspirations. It is not final localisation. It is a design source for country packages, route branches, decisions, asset prompts, and AI behaviour.

## Source discipline

- Real historical polities are used as inspiration for alternate-history country packages. The mod must not claim that a fictional tag is the direct legal continuation of any living kingdom, community, religion, or political institution unless the implementation has performed a dedicated source review.
- Real flags, sacred objects, royal insignia, leader portraits, and attested symbols must be handled by `chaosx_asset_source_researcher`; invented route flags and supernatural variants can be generated.
- The higher-tier absurd routes are intentionally separated from human African cultures. They should unlock through high chaos, mythic route flags, or nonhuman classification. They must never imply that living or historical African peoples are animals, monsters, or caricatures.
- Some names below are working design labels only. Implementation should localise them with sensitivity and may use more neutral names if research finds a better term.

## Research-to-gameplay takeaways

1. The “native African subjects” layer should not be only ten generic regions. It should support a second layer of optional **Chartered Old States**: smaller, historically anchored authorities released from or nested inside larger regional authorities.
2. Each old-state authority should have a local action loop: hold a capital or sacred/administrative seat, restore archives/courts/markets, raise distinct local units, and negotiate its charter status.
3. The player should decide whether old-state packages are preserved as federal members, folded into regional authorities, used as crown legitimacy, militarised into liberation commands, or radicalised into high-chaos mythic forms.
4. High-chaos absurdity should be mechanically meaningful. It should add new rules, costs, AI behaviour, assets, risks, and failure states, not only a funny country name.
5. The design must include clutter controls: at most a limited number of old-state authorities should be active at once unless the player opens a dedicated board or intentionally enables “Many Names” mode.
