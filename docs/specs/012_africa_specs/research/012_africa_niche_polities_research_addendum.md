# Event 012 Africa — Niche Polities and High-Chaos Research Addendum

This addendum expands the Africa package with a much deeper set of niche historical-polity inspirations and absurd high-chaos country packages. It is design research, not a claim that every polity should appear as a full independent country at once. The implementation should use these entries as a controlled restoration-dossier system: a country, authority, compact, guardian office, or nonhuman delegation becomes active only when the unifier has unlocked the correct regional archive, controls or protects the relevant area, and has enough mechanic pressure to justify the extra surface.

## Source confidence model

| Confidence | Meaning for the spec | Implementation handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | The polity/site is supported by a major reference source used below. | Can be a visible dossier, restoration subject, focus branch, formable fragment, or asset research target. |
| Medium | The polity is historically plausible but needs a narrower source check before final flags, leaders, or quotations. | Use as a dossier or local route; mark final symbols and portraits as source blockers. |
| Speculative / mythic | The entry is supernatural, nonhuman, folklore-derived, or absurd. | Treat as fictional generated content. Never present it as an ordinary human ethnic caricature. Register actual nonhuman tags in shared nonhuman classifiers if implemented. |

## Expanded research target map

The following lists are implementation prompts for deeper source review. Each entry should either become a dossier package or be folded into a neighboring regional authority. Final flags, symbols, real leader portraits, quote choices, and exact local names require sourced asset/text work.

### North Africa, Sahara, and Nile corridor

- **Kush / Napata / Meroe**: pyramid courts, ironworks, cataract guards, river tolls, royal cemetery protection, Nile-dam bargaining.
- **Aksum and Zagwe memory**: stelae, inscriptions, Red Sea routes, mountain logistics, monastic legitimacy. Aksum can connect to both Horn and Nile branches.
- **Carthage / Punic Ledger**: trade republic route, naval accountants, harbor works, elephant corps ancestry. The spec should avoid treating Carthage as simply “Rome’s enemy”; make it a customs-house and naval-finance restoration.
- **Numidia / Mauretania**: cavalry, desert diplomacy, Roman-border memory, anti-imperial riders. Good for North African unifiers and anti-colonial cavalry units.
- **Garamantes / Fezzan Wells**: foggara-style water networks, desert roads, mirage logistics, subterranean supply missions. Medium-source; needs source review before final assets.
- **Tuareg / Aïr / Kel Ahaggar networks**: caravan law, desert scouts, salt roads, confederation diplomacy. Sensitive and living-community-adjacent; use as regional network/dossier, not caricature.
- **Senussi zawiya network**: religious, desert, and anti-colonial organizing inspiration. Needs careful sourcing and route-specific tone.

### Western Sudan, Senegambia, and Upper Niger

- **Ghana / Wagadu memory**: gold-road claims, old capital survey, caravan protection, griot legitimacy.
- **Mali / Manden charters**: river diplomacy, gold and book caravans, griot offices, Sankore scholars if Timbuktu is relevant.
- **Songhai / Gao-Timbuktu-Jenne**: Niger flotillas, scholar militias, mud-brick urban repair, desert cavalry.
- **Takrur and Futa Toro**: Senegal River legitimacy, Islamic reform, riverine levies.
- **Jolof, Waalo, Cayor, Sine, Saloum**: Senegambian nested authority system with rival rulers, peanut-route disruption, coastal diplomacy, and small cavalry/river guards.
- **Futa Jallon**: mountain-imamate route, pastoral logistics, refuge corridors.
- **Massina / Hamdullahi**: inland delta governance, cattle protection, rice-marsh control, jurist missions.
- **Gajaaga / Galam**: gold, river tolls, small treaty states that can become useful buffer subjects.
- **Mossi kingdoms**: cavalry-heavy inland fortitude, anti-raid missions, strong resistance to absorption.

### Gulf of Guinea and forest-savanna border

- **Asante / Fante / Akwamu / Denkyira**: gold and stool legitimacy, coastal forts, forest roads, rival confederacies.
- **Oyo / Ife / Ijebu / Owo / Benin**: palace roads, cavalry from the north, bronze/ivory court assets, forest-edge diplomacy. Ife/Owo/Benin should focus on ritual legitimacy and craft/court offices more than raw conquest.
- **Dahomey / Allada / Porto-Novo**: palace guards, coastal diplomacy, sacred regalia, anti-slave-fort tribunal routes. High care required because historical slave-trade links are sensitive; route should allow abolitionist reckoning, not glamorization.
- **Aro Confederacy / Bonny / Opobo / coastal city states**: trade networks, oracle/diplomatic influence, river commerce, anti-company or company-capture paths.
- **Igala / Nupe / Kwararafa**: Niger-Benue crossing politics and cavalry/river hybrid warfare.
- **Bamum / Tikar / Grassfields chiefdoms**: palace art, script/archives, highland federations, court emissaries.

### Central Africa, Kongo basin, and Angola corridor

- **Kongo / Loango / Kakongo / Ngoyo**: coastal diplomacy, church/court syncretic institutions, Portuguese and Belgian pressure, river mouth customs.
- **Ndongo / Matamba / Kasanje**: guerrilla corridors, queen-court legitimacy, fort-line wars, diplomatic deception.
- **Luba / Lunda / Chokwe**: dynastic court offices, copper routes, masks, ivory roads, long-distance integration missions.
- **Kuba**: court art, raffia administration, memory boards, masked court ritual. Avoid making masks into horror by default; high-chaos corruption is a separate route.
- **Yeke / Garanganze**: copper belt trade and strongman diplomacy; good for resource route tension.
- **Lozi / Barotseland**: floodplain administration, canoe logistics, seasonal capital route.
- **Azande / Mangbetu / Mbuti forest pacts**: use as forest knowledge, scout networks, and autonomy concerns. Living communities require careful handling and no caricature.

### Horn, Red Sea, and Somali coast

- **Ifat / Adal / Harar**: highland-border missions, Red Sea ports, reformist court versus merchant city split.
- **Ajuran**: hydraulic/well systems, fortified wells, Somali coast trade, anti-Portuguese/Oromo-pressure memory. Needs source verification before final named content.
- **Mogadishu / Merca / Barawa**: city-state merchants, coinage, convoy and port duties.
- **Aussa / Afar sultanates**: desert gatekeeping, salt routes, Red Sea access.
- **Beja / Medri Bahri / Dahlak**: coast-highland link, island tolls, Red Sea diplomacy.
- **Oromo Gadaa-inspired civic path**: if used, write as civic institution inspiration and source carefully; avoid flattening Oromo societies into one fantasy tag.

### East African coast, Great Lakes, and Indian Ocean

- **Kilwa / Mombasa / Malindi / Pate / Lamu / Zanzibar**: Swahili coastal republics, port leagues, coral-stone assets, customs houses, convoy windows.
- **Comoros / Anjouan / Moheli / Grande Comore**: small island diplomacy, naval basing, spice/convoy route, external patron pressure.
- **Buganda / Bunyoro / Toro / Ankole / Busoga / Karagwe / Buhaya**: lake fleets, court roads, hill levies, cattle legitimacy, royal court modernization.
- **Rwanda / Burundi**: court centralization, hill administration, border tensions. Handle with care because of later history; avoid high-chaos jokes or ethnic reduction.
- **Zanzibar / Shirazi memory**: can be a coastal focus, but source and phrase carefully.
- **Merina / Sakalava / Betsimisaraka / Antemoro**: Madagascar route families with highland bureaucracy, coastal confederacies, scribal networks, and island fleets.

### Southern Africa and Zambezi corridor

- **Great Zimbabwe / Mutapa / Rozwi / Butua**: stone cities, gold routes, fortress ruins, old-enclosure restoration.
- **Maravi / Chewa confederacies**: lake corridor, iron, spiritual offices, central-southern diplomacy.
- **Lozi / Barotseland**: listed above, but also belongs to Zambezi floodplain settlement.
- **Zulu / Swazi / Sotho / Tswana / Ndebele**: strong military and political memories, cattle/fortress/hill-country defence, but must be routed through existing HOI4/RSA sensitivity. Do not flatten them into identical “warrior tribes.”
- **Khoekhoe / San restoration offices**: land restitution, frontier memory, small autonomous protectorates, scout networks. High care and sourcing required.

## Absurd content research boundary

Absurd paths are allowed and encouraged at higher chaos tiers, but they must be structurally separated from human African societies.

- **Nonhuman actors** are not “niche tribes.” They are supernatural, animal, ecological, legal-fiction, or impossible entities created by Chaos Redux escalation.
- **Animal polities** use institutional leaders such as `Council of the Silverbacks`, `The Termite Survey`, `The Baobab Senate`, or `Crocodile Rivers`, not joke-human names. The new Absurd / Obscene Name Mode applies to human joke-route courts, counterfeit claimants, and ceremonial aliases; nonhuman actors may react to those names but do not adopt them as their own identity.
- **Gods and spirits** should be written as mythic pressure, divine court claims, or possession of institutions. They should not replace living religious communities one-to-one.
- **No caricature rule**: Never describe a human African country as “gorilla,” “chimp,” “savage,” “ape,” or similar. Those labels only apply to explicit nonhuman tags and must be mechanically excluded from ordinary diplomacy where appropriate.
- **Research gate**: final super-event titles, quotes, button remarks, songs, chants, and cultural references remain blocked until the super-event research workflow verifies sources.
