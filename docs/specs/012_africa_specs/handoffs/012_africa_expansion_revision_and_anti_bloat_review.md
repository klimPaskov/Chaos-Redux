# Event 12 Africa expansion revision and anti-bloat review

## Revision purpose

The first edition established the continental system, constitutional route families, polity pool, decision structure, presentation plan, and world-order escalation. Its strongest material was continental. Its thinner material was the experience of choosing a particular host, the internal operation of each constitution, and the continuing play of selected member or restoration countries.

The second edition deepens those three areas. It does not add a parallel continental mechanic or replace the shared tree with dozens of country trees.

## New source design

### Host-country playbooks

`specs/012_africa_spec_part_7_host_country_playbooks.md` adds:

- 22 full host playbooks
- 29 compact host signatures
- a first proof mission for every full host
- host-specific domestic weaknesses, economic leverage, military constraints, regional coalition logic, restoration pressure, rival risks, and route mutations
- a promotion rule for compact hosts that gain a durable campaign role
- a host-overlay model that changes the opening and selected branches without duplicating the entire continental tree

The matching 51-row host matrix gives implementation a compact disposition ledger.

### Constitutional route depth

`specs/012_africa_spec_part_8_focus_route_deepening.md` converts the seven route labels into governing systems. Each route now defines:

- representation and franchise
- executive selection and removal
- fiscal and resource settlement
- military command and emergency authority
- member withdrawal and clause enforcement
- constitutional crisis families
- failure and recovery paths
- post-unification government
- mutations of the shared economic, military, diplomatic, diaspora, restoration, and expansion lanes

The matching 78-row payoff matrix maps route openers, commitments, dilemmas, branch interactions, crises, capstones, and postwar roles.

### Priority member and restoration packages

`specs/012_africa_spec_part_9_priority_member_country_packages.md` provides full design direction for 16 selected countries:

- Asante
- Oyo
- Sokoto
- Kanem-Bornu
- Manden
- Kongo
- Buganda
- Aksum
- Harar
- Kilwa

Each package has a bounded map, starting problem, distinct mechanic, focus modules, force identity, League role, refusal path, route preferences, and post-settlement play. These packages show the depth standard for promoted polity entries without requiring all 215 candidates to become full tags.

## Existing ledgers expanded

- decision and mission matrix: 92 to 102 rows
- AI route matrix: 42 to 64 profiles
- asset and animation matrix: 219 to 239 work items
- architecture diagrams: 3 to 5
- research notes: 2 to 3

The 44-achievement matrix and 215-entry polity catalog remain stable. Their breadth was already sufficient. The achievement implementation prompt now accounts for host origin, constitutional crisis history, and priority-member promotion without creating extra achievements for every country.

## Historical grounding added

The host-country research addendum strengthens the design basis for Ethiopia, Egypt and Sudan, the divided Somali territories, Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Libya, Liberia, Nigeria, the Gold Coast, the Congo, Angola, Uganda and Buganda, Kenya, South Africa, Southern Rhodesia, Madagascar, and the Indian Ocean islands.

The research is used to identify playable problems, institutions, and regional tensions. It does not convert broad country studies into final claims, borders, flags, or leader selections. Those remain specialist implementation gates.

## Anti-bloat decisions

### One common continental backbone

All hosts use the Charter League, regional councils, selected-target flow, congresses, integration ladder, and shared support lanes. Host content changes the opening, values, targets, proofs, branch emphasis, and risk. It does not create a duplicate League or full tree.

### Two host depths

Full playbooks are reserved for starts with a clearly different strategic problem. Compact signatures provide enough identity for smaller or closely related starts. A compact host is promoted only when campaign conditions create a durable new role.

### Selected priority packages

Sixteen countries demonstrate the full member-package standard. The larger polity catalog stays a controlled pool. A candidate is promoted when it can support distinct mechanics and continuing play, not because a historical name appears in the catalog.

### Route depth instead of route length

The constitutional expansion adds dilemmas, crisis handling, branch mutations, and postwar government. It does not prescribe a much larger raw focus count. Implementation should combine weak rewards into fewer stronger focuses and use decisions for recurring administration.

### Shared action families

The ten added action concepts solve constitutional deadlocks, host proof failure, postwar review, and member promotion. Country flavour should parameterise these actions rather than create dozens of nearly identical decisions.

### Bounded presentation

Twenty asset-family rows cover the new visual requirements. The package does not demand separate full icon sets for every host. Super-event roles remain four. Host and route identity should appear through dynamic text, emblems, and a small justified image-variant set.

## What should not be added before implementation

- a bespoke full focus tree for every eligible host
- a full country package for every polity catalog row
- a separate influence or legitimacy system beside the Charter values
- one achievement for every host or restoration
- one super-event for every constitution
- host-exclusive duplicates of ordinary League actions
- additional high-chaos actors without a distinct campaign role
- complete separate specifications for all other continent unifiers inside Event 12

## Remaining design and research gates

- final state IDs and region groups
- specialist review of territorial overlap and restoration maps
- final historical leaders, parties, symbols, flags, and portraits
- final starting force balance for implemented hosts and promoted members
- native-speaker review of source-language ruler flavour
- final super-event title, quote, remark, image, audio, license, and slot research
- final cluster registry identity and player-facing catalog wording
- implementation evidence for AI, cleanup, exploit control, and interface performance

## Tooling status

The project-specific `chaosx_improvement_loop_planner` contract was fully read, but the subagent was not exposed as an invokable tool in this environment. The same playable-promise, depth, connection, and anti-bloat questions were applied directly. This document records that review without claiming an external subagent ran.

## Revision conclusion

This expansion is useful because it changes how campaigns begin, how constitutional choices govern, and how selected countries continue to play. Further broad expansion before implementation would mainly increase content obligations. The next design pass should respond to implementation evidence, audit findings, or a clearly new gameplay problem.
