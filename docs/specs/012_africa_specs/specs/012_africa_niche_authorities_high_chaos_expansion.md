# Event 012 Africa — Niche Authority Atlas and High-Chaos Absurd Path Expansion

This file is accepted source design for the updated Event 012 Africa package. It expands the prior regional-authority design with a larger pool of niche African polities, micro-authorities, specialist military schools, heritage offices, restoration subjects, and explicit nonhuman/supernatural high-chaos actors.

The goal is to make “Africa is one” feel like a continent full of negotiated identities rather than a single instant tag paint. The unifier can still proclaim a continental destiny, but the player must decide which local authorities become federation members, which become protectorates, which become permanent allied subjects, which are integrated by law, and which are too strange to annex at all.

## Design boundaries

1. Baseline play remains grounded. Regional authorities are human political subjects or faction members, not cartoon kingdoms.
2. Historical polities are not automatically restored as exact replicas. They usually appear as heritage offices, councils, academies, charter authorities, or restoration movements.
3. Higher chaos can turn research anchors into absurd actors, but the absurdity must be opt-in, tier-gated, and mechanically costly.
4. Nonhuman actors are explicitly nonhuman/ecological/supernatural and must be registered as such during implementation.
5. Real religions, sacred symbols, historical flags, and real leaders are research-gated. The spec may define direction, but final asset/text/source use requires the correct subagent workflow.
6. Source-language joke names belong to event-created court/ruler display masks, not to real African peoples, historical polity names, or human-community labels.

## The Authority Atlas mechanic

The unifier gains an **Authority Atlas** after the first regional authority is created or invited. This is a decision/GUI layer that tracks smaller subject packages and heritage offices inside broad regions.

Values:

| Value | Meaning | Increases from | Decreases from | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Local Mandate | Local support for an authority or restoration office. | successful survey missions, voluntary defence, low-resistance integration, cultural offices | forced annexation, failed defence, extractive industry | peaceful integration, specialist units, local advisors |
| Archive Integrity | Whether heritage offices are protected and believable. | Guard the Artefact Roads, source-protection decisions, avoiding looting | foreign occupation, heavy bombardment, failed guard missions | safe restoration, legitimacy, construct route safety |
| Restoration Pressure | How strongly a local office wants autonomy or revival. | crown route, high local mandate, regional pride, weak unifier | federal concessions, successful congress votes, shared defence | restoration subjects, faction votes, possible revolt |
| Habitat Trust | Trust from nonhuman/sanctuary actors. | habitat compacts, reduced extraction, protected forests, anti-poaching/anti-looting missions | mine expansion, road spam through sanctuary states, forced integration | sanctuary pacts, forest units, nonhuman observer states |
| Mythic Volatility | How likely impossible outcomes become unstable. | high chaos, disaster ultimatums, construct units, living monuments | containment offices, archive integrity, careful research | absurd route power, disaster backfire, hidden super-event roles |

Presentation:

- The normal decision category can show the current top three authority offices.
- A custom Congress Board/Authority Atlas tab should show selected authority cards by region.
- Cards have grounded, restored, and absurd states.
- Each authority card has visible status: `Surveyed`, `Charter Office`, `Restoration Subject`, `Protectorate`, `Federal Member`, `Integrated`, `Hostile`, `Sanctuary`, or `Impossible Actor`.
- The player can show/hide regional cards to avoid a decision wall.

## Authority classes

### Class 1 — Heritage Office

A Heritage Office is the safest baseline version. It is not a tag by default. It gives flavour, decisions, advisors, unit schools, legitimacy, and local integration modifiers.

Examples: Asante Stool Confederacy Office, Benin / Edo Registry, Kongo Mbanza Council, Aksumite-Red Sea Stelae Office, Great Zimbabwe Stone Congress.

### Class 2 — Restoration Subject

A Restoration Subject is a real subject/faction member with a tag/cosmetic identity. It appears when the unifier controls the required region, has enough Local Mandate, and chooses restoration/autonomy over direct annexation.

Examples: Kanem-Bornu Lake Secretariat as a subject, Merina Highland Board as an island authority, Lozi/Barotse Floodplain Authority, Kilwa Coral Admiralty, Kuba-Luba-Lunda Court Federation.

### Class 3 — Specialist School

A Specialist School may be attached to the unifier or a subject. It creates templates, commanders, military decisions, and terrain-specific forces.

Examples: Mossi cavalry, Oyo cavalry, Dahomey Guard, highland schools, desert columns, river guards, elephant logistics.

### Class 4 — Archeology-to-Chaos Chain

A cultural archive, monument, or old capital begins grounded but can become impossible under high chaos. These routes must be staged:

1. Survey and protect the site.
2. Build a heritage office or restoration subject.
3. Open a high-chaos investigation or ritual/engineering branch.
4. Choose safe containment, propaganda exploitation, military exploitation, or full absurd pact.

Examples: Sao Terracotta Host, Benin Bronze Automata, Aksum Stelae Broadcast, Kush-Meroe Iron Pyramid Batteries, Great Zimbabwe Walking Walls.

### Class 5 — Nonhuman Sanctuary Actor

These actors are not normal countries. They may be represented as tags only when the engine needs a subject or diplomacy surface, but design-wise they are pacts, sanctuaries, ecological congresses, or impossible nonhuman councils.

Examples: Congo Basin Primate Confederacy, Gorilla Highland Court, Chimpanzee Telegraph League, Bonobo Peace Congress, Okapi Courier Kingdom, Lemur Parliament.

Rules:

- No normal ideology names.
- No human leader-name pools.
- No ordinary conscription.
- No direct annexation.
- No claims on human capitals.
- They may grant support units, decisions, intelligence, terrain effects, or world-end delegation status.

## Expanded authority packages

### Garamantian Foggara Authority

**Region:** Fezzan and Sahara corridors.  
**Opening:** appears after surveying Saharan oases or liberating Fezzan/Libyan interior states.  
**Role:** desert supply and hidden water-grid logistics.

Baseline content:

- Oasis depot missions.
- Desert convoy decisions using trucks, trains, fuel, and infantry equipment.
- Foggara repair projects that reduce attrition and increase desert Local Mandate.
- A desert scout template with low supply use.

Restoration/autonomy route:

- Can become a `Desert Water Commission` subject.
- Strong autonomy; weak manpower; valuable supply modifiers.
- Resists forced integration if the unifier ignores desert supply missions.

High-chaos route — **The Buried Canal State:**

- Unlocks mirage decoy forts, sudden oasis supply bursts, and enemy-route misdirection.
- Costs Mythic Volatility and fuel/truck/supply investment.
- Backfire: drought, false-route panic, divisions losing planning, or local trust collapse.

### Kanem-Bornu Lake Secretariat

**Region:** Lake Chad basin.  
**Opening:** survey Lake Chad / Chad-Nigeria-Cameroon-Niger state group.  
**Role:** lake/cavalry/caravan authority.

Baseline content:

- Caravan Guard missions.
- Lake Chad supply hub projects.
- Camel/cavalry scout detachments.
- Jurist and merchant councils that increase Local Mandate if treated as partners.

Restoration/autonomy route:

- Can become a powerful Sahel subject with mounted units.
- If strong enough, may refuse annexation and demand federation guarantees.

High-chaos route — **The Black Lake Arsenal:**

- Predicts enemy route timing around the lake.
- Can “withdraw water” as a sabotage fantasy: represented by temporary infrastructure/supply penalties, not literal map deletion.
- Backfire causes disease/famine pressure and Congress Cohesion loss.

### Sao Terracotta Cities

**Region:** Chari-Logone / Lake Chad edge.  
**Opening:** follows Kanem-Bornu or Lake Chad survey.  
**Role:** river-city garrison and archeology-to-chaos chain.

Baseline content:

- Terracotta archive protection.
- Bronze/terracotta craft school icon family.
- Defensive garrison missions around river cities.

High-chaos route — **Terracotta Host:**

- Clay constructs become static defence militia.
- They do not use manpower.
- They require support equipment, artillery, and Archive Integrity.
- If Archive Integrity is low, units crumble or become hostile monument events.

### Mossi Cavalry Estates

**Region:** Upper Volta / Sahel-forest transition.  
**Role:** fast mounted response and border defence.

Baseline content:

- Savanna cavalry school.
- Mobile patrol missions.
- Anti-raider and anti-colonial border response.
- Good for military route; acceptable for federal route.

High-chaos route — **Dry Thunder Riders:**

- Storm-intimidation cavalry that can disrupt enemy planning before a battle.
- Requires army XP, horses abstraction via cavalry equipment, trucks/fuel if motorized, and high Local Mandate.
- Bad in jungle/city/low-supply overuse.

### Asante Stool Confederacy Office

**Region:** Akan/Ghana interior.  
**Role:** federal legitimacy and congress unity.

Baseline content:

- Congress vote legitimacy decisions.
- Gold-route security and archive missions.
- Federation ritual flavour without final sacred-symbol claims unless sourced.

Route interactions:

- Federal path: strongest Local Mandate gain.
- Crown path: can call “small throne” conferences.
- Military path: risks insulting local autonomy and raising Restoration Pressure.

High-chaos route — **Sky-Stool Clause:**

- Congress claims a literal mandate has appeared in the chamber.
- Huge Legitimacy boost, foreign alarm, and Restoration Pressure spike.
- If abused, powerful authorities demand equal standing or leave.

### Benin / Edo Registry

**Region:** Edo / southern Nigeria.  
**Role:** art, court diplomacy, urban defence, restitution propaganda.

Baseline content:

- Artifact-road missions and archive protection.
- Bronze foundry projects that increase industry and legitimacy.
- Urban guard detachments.

High-chaos route — **Bronze Palace Guard:**

- Bronze constructs guard capitals and recovered archives.
- Requires metal/resource projects and high Archive Integrity.
- Cannot be converted into normal divisions.
- Backfire: foundries consume factories and cause foreign panic.

### Nri Ritual Arbitration Office

**Region:** Igbo interior.  
**Role:** peaceful integration, arbitration, anti-force route.

Baseline content:

- Dispute arbitration missions.
- Resistance reduction in federation routes.
- Blocks or penalizes forced integration if selected as a protected office.

High-chaos route — **The Unarmed Kingdom That Cannot Be Occupied:**

- Occupying powers suffer administrative failures, cancelled collaboration, and wrong-map events until they negotiate.
- Very low military power; very high political disruption.
- Military unifier can suppress it but loses major legitimacy.

### Aro Oracle and Trade Network

**Region:** southeastern Nigeria.  
**Role:** intelligence, trade, and counter-infiltration.

Baseline content:

- Telegraph and trade-route influence decisions.
- Detect colonial infiltration in member states.
- Increase Ananse Web power if Green Covenant route is active.

High-chaos route — **Long Juju Telegraph:**

- Impossible signals intercept or scramble colonial orders.
- Must be written as rumours and signal anomalies, not a definitive religious claim.
- Backfire causes false orders in the Charter League.

### Oyo Cavalry Secretariat

**Region:** Yoruba north/savanna edge.  
**Role:** cavalry academy and savanna operations.

Baseline content:

- Mounted school and officer cadres.

High-chaos route — **Thunder Palace Branch:**

### Dahomey Guard Office

High-chaos route — **Red Oath Battalion:**

- Strength scales with Covenant Pressure and command obedience.
- Too much use triggers loyalty crises and stability damage.
- Spec must not romanticize slavery or captive-taking.

## Niche authority focus-tree integration

Add an **Authority Atlas lane** to the existing Africa focus tree. It should not become a separate linear checklist. It should branch out of `Native Authorities Registry`, `Regional Delegates`, or equivalent trunk focuses and interact with politics, industry, military, diplomacy, and Green Covenant.

### Anchor focus groups

| Focus group | Unlocks | Interacts with | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Survey the Forgotten Capitals | first wave of Heritage Offices | industry, legitimacy, regional integration | Gives survey decisions, not instant rewards. |
| The Small Thrones Question | restoration subject path | crown/federal/military political routes | Determines whether strong offices can become autonomous subjects. |
| Archive Roads and Guarded Museums | Archive Integrity value | industry, military, anti-colonial war | Uses guard missions, trains, support equipment, state control. |
| Specialist Schools of the Continent | military schools | military branch, regional authorities | Unlocks Mossi/Oyo/Dahomey/desert/highland/river schools based on states. |
| The Authority Atlas Board | custom UI/decision layer | all routes | Shows cards and status for selected authorities. |
| Pacts with the Impossible | nonhuman/supernatural actors | Green Covenant, Evolution III/IV | Opens only after high chaos and relevant regional events. |
| Monuments That Refuse Silence | archeology-to-chaos chain | Green Covenant, industry, world-end | Living monument/construct branches. |

### Political route reactions

- **Federal Congress:** treats authorities as federation members; highest Local Mandate; slowest direct annexation; best long-term stability.
- **People’s Liberation Front:** turns offices into revolutionary committees and anti-colonial schools; gains units and legitimacy among anti-colonial members but risks suppressing crown/restoration offices.
- **Continental General Staff:** converts offices into military districts; strong specialist schools; high Restoration Pressure and revolt risk.
- **Crown Congress:** revives restoration subjects and ceremonial councils; strong Local Mandate where accepted; can become imperial if abused.
- **Green Covenant:** opens nonhuman/supernatural and living-landscape branches; strongest absurd content; hardest diplomatic recognition.

### Industry route reactions

Industry cannot simply mine every authority region. It must choose development type:

- **Protective development:** lower extraction, higher trust, unlocks nonhuman pacts and safe heritage routes.
- **Sovereign extraction:** medium trust, high resources, possible labour/rail missions.
- **Forced extraction:** high short-term industry, low trust, higher hostile high-chaos actor risk.

### Military route reactions

Specialist schools require geography. Implementation should not unlock every school for every host instantly.

- Desert schools require Sahara/Sahel states.
- Cavalry schools require savanna/West African authority or equivalent.
- Highland schools require Ethiopian/Horn/Great Lakes highland states.
- River guards require Congo/Nile/Niger/Zambezi or Great Lakes state groups.
- Port guards require coast/island states.
- Elephant/forest guardian units require Green Covenant or Evolution II+ plus relevant terrain.

## Decision families

### Survey Forgotten Capitals

**Who:** unifier, sometimes regional authority.  
**Targets:** Mbanza Kongo, Great Zimbabwe, Kilwa coast, Aksum/Adulis, Kasubi/Kampala, Lake Chad, Fezzan oases, Harar/Zeila, Barotse floodplain, Merina highlands, Benin City area, Asante/Kumasi area, Nile/Meroë area.  
**Costs:** convoys/trains/support equipment/civilian factory burden/local trust/time.  
**Results:** heritage office unlock; Local Mandate; Archive Integrity; possible foreign reaction.

### Convene the Small Thrones

**Who:** federal/crown routes.  
**Requirements:** at least three Heritage Offices with high Local Mandate; no ongoing forced integration mission in those regions.  
**Costs:** stability, direct authority, autonomy concessions.  
**Results:** restoration subjects or federation members; strong legitimacy; slower annexation.

### Guard the Artefact Roads

**Who:** all routes.  
**Requirement:** place supplied divisions in named rail/port/river states for 120–180 days.  
**Costs:** divisions, trains, support equipment, opportunity cost.  
**Success:** Archive Integrity, safer high-chaos route.  
**Failure:** theft/looting/foreign intervention event; future construct route more dangerous.

### Restore Specialist School

**Who:** military and route-compatible authorities.  
**Costs:** army XP, infantry equipment, support equipment, cavalry/truck/fuel depending school.  
**Success:** template unlock, one-time cadre, decisions for training more units.  
**Failure/abuse:** equipment drain and local distrust.

### Habitat Compact

**Who:** Green Covenant/federal routes with forest/island authorities.  
**Costs:** lost extraction, construction capacity, local trust, alternative routes.  
**Success:** nonhuman pact eligibility, habitat trust, lower hostile forest events.  
**Failure:** hostile nonhuman actor or sanctuary flight.

### Treaty with the Impossible Actor

**Who:** high-chaos route after nonhuman/supernatural actor appears.  
**Costs:** legitimacy, covenant pressure, habitat trust, route restrictions, sometimes rejecting exploitation decisions.  
**Success:** sanctuary subject/observer, special support, achievement hooks.  
**Failure:** actor becomes hostile, leaves, or causes regional disaster.

## Acceptance additions

Implementation is incomplete unless:

- The Authority Atlas or equivalent system can represent at least 20 niche offices/authorities/actors without dumping every decision into one category at once.
- At least 12 of the researched historical anchors have concrete decisions, focus hooks, assets, AI preferences, and integration/failure rules.
- At least 5 high-chaos absurd branches are fully designed and implementation-ready, including nonhuman or construct classification where relevant.
- The Congress/faction system can keep authority actors as allies, subjects, protectorates, federation members, or sanctuaries rather than only annexing them.
- Nonhuman actors cannot be annexed, conscripted, or treated as ordinary African human countries.
- Historical/source-sensitive flags, symbols, real leaders, sacred references, and final super-event text/audio remain research-gated until their proper workflows complete.
