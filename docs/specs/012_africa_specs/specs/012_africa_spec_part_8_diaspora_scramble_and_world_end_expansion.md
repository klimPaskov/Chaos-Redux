# 012 Africa spec part 8, diaspora, Scramble reaction, and world-end expansion

## Diaspora route expansion

The diaspora route should be one of the most distinctive non-conquest routes. It ties Africa to Afro-American, Caribbean, and wider Black diaspora networks through shipping, propaganda, technical skill, and return migration.

### Route values

| Value | Meaning | Raised by | Lowered by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Returnee confidence | diaspora belief that the route is safe | safe ports, housing, victories, stable laws | lost ports, overcrowding, civil war, spy scares |
| Settlement capacity | ability to absorb returnees | housing projects, factories, health programs | bombing, resistance, food shortage |
| Maritime link | safety and reach of shipping lanes | convoys, escorts, port control, naval aid | blockades, raids, colonial panic |
| Diaspora prestige | global symbolic power of the route | successful congresses, radio, Black Star shipping | failed convoys, repression, route hypocrisy |

### Mission families

- secure a return port
- build returnee housing
- open a Black Star style shipping link
- invite engineer cadres
- invite medical and school missions
- recruit diaspora volunteers under strict route limits
- protect convoys against outside power pressure
- settle local representation disputes
- publish anti-colonial radio and press campaigns

### Failure states

- overcrowded ports lower stability and local support
- outside intelligence infiltration creates spy or sabotage events
- returnee disillusionment lowers prestige
- colonial blockade can force a naval or diplomatic crisis
- local communities can resist settlement if housing and autonomy are ignored

## Scramble for Africa reaction expansion

The Scramble reaction is a late crisis after the unifier secures Africa. It should invert the old colonial scramble. Outside powers are no longer dividing a passive continent. They are reacting to a united power that can punish them.

### Outside reaction lanes

| Lane | Actor type | Tools | Counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sanctions | powers with trade exposure | embargoes, resource pressure, diplomatic isolation | resource sovereignty, alternate trade, diaspora commerce |
| Expedition | powers with ports and fleets | naval staging, expeditionary planning, coastal raids | port shields, coastal forts, air patrols, League reserves |
| Rival sponsorship | powers with African contacts | arm rival blocs, fund autonomy movements | counter-intelligence, autonomy reform, member confidence |
| Ultimatum | powers with remaining claims | demand port access, debt, concessions, or demilitarization | reject, bargain, mobilize, or split the demand |
| Colonial restoration fantasy | revanchist powers | attempt to recover old holdings | final anti-colonial war, propaganda victory |
| Diplomatic recognition | pragmatic powers | recognize Africa for trade or security | negotiated concessions or neutrality route |

The crisis should be able to end without world war if Africa is strong and diplomatic. It can also become a major war if panic and expedition pressure rise too far.

## Super-event thresholds

### Africa is one milestone

Use when Africa has achieved a meaningful continent-scale state. Possible threshold combinations:

- direct control or federal control of all African state groups
- major restored polities are federated or integrated
- League cohesion above a major threshold
- no rival bloc controls an African capital
- colonial panic exists but has not yet resolved

### Scramble for Africa reaction

Use after the unification milestone when at least one outside power has holdings, claims, expedition capacity, or high panic. It should not fire if outside powers are already defeated and no meaningful crisis exists.

### The World is One terminal path

Use only when the continent war system exists and other continent unifiers have their own identities. The super-event must be terminal and must set the world-end state.

## Continent unifier sponsorship

Africa can sponsor other continent unifiers after Evolution III. This should be a post-unification route, not a baseline distraction.

Possible sponsorship actions:

- send envoys to a continent movement
- fund an anti-colonial or anti-imperial congress
- arm a continent unifier under route conditions
- invite a continent unifier into a federation or rivalry structure
- demand union negotiations after both sides unify
- refuse union and prepare continent war

Each other continent movement needs its own package. Africa can trigger or support them, but this spec does not replace their unique trees.

## Dynamic union names

When Africa unites with or conquers another continent-scale power, public names should remain readable.

Valid style directions:

- Afro-Asian Union
- Afro-Eurasia
- Afro-Middle Eastern Federation
- Afro-Oceanic Union
- Atlantic Africa if tied to diaspora and Americas route
- The World for terminal outcome only

Avoid administrative names as country names. Institutional names can be used inside the state.

## World-end campaign shape

The terminal path should have three stages.

1. Continental alignment stage: all continent unifiers exist and choose union, rivalry, or war posture.
2. Continent war stage: continent-scale powers fight through major theaters, alliances, and betrayal.
3. Terminal settlement stage: last standing power becomes The World and the campaign enters end-state.

The route should be hard because the player must first unite Africa, survive Scramble pressure, cause or support other continent unifiers, then win against them.

## World-end AI restrictions

AI should not trigger this path just because conditions are technically met. It should require extreme chaos, route aggression, high strength, and a configuration or AI personality that allows terminal choices. A federal Africa should prefer union or federation. A command or high-chaos Africa can choose war more often. A weak Africa should not start continent wars.
