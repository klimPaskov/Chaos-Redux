# Event 012 Africa — Niche Polity Expansion and Absurd High-Chaos Paths

This expansion upgrades the regional authority layer from broad administrative subjects into a living atlas of local authorities, historical-revival packages, high-chaos legacy routes, and ecological/nonhuman pact actors. It should be folded into the country-package, focus-tree, decision, asset, AI, and achievement surfaces during implementation.

## Playable promise

The original event promise is “Africa is one.” The expanded promise is more interesting: Africa can become one **without becoming flat**. The unifier has to decide how much local memory, regional autonomy, old institutions, royal courts, imamate networks, coastal city-states, mountain refuges, forest pacts, and supernatural mandates can survive inside the continental project.

A good campaign should feel different when the selected host builds Africa through:

- federal congresses and broad regional authorities;
- military command and coerced protectorates;
- old thrones and ceremonial legitimacy;
- revolutionary fronts and workers' councils;
- diaspora return, port politics, and global Black networks;
- high-chaos mythic compacts;
- ecological/nonhuman forest actors.

The player should see fewer generic “Africa puppet” subjects and more meaningful local cards that argue, contribute, resist, remember, and sometimes become absurdly powerful.

## The Living Atlas system

The Living Atlas is a decision-category layer and optional scripted GUI tab that appears once the unifier completes the early focus group around regional authorities. It prevents clutter by showing one selected region and a limited set of candidate authorities.

### Values displayed on every authority card

| Value | Meaning | Raised by | Lowered by | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Local Trust | Whether the authority believes the Congress protects its people. | Rescue wars, relief, local missions, respecting autonomy. | Forced annexation, ignored raids, exploitative extraction. | Volunteers, peaceful integration, local focus branch. |
| Historical Claim | Strength of the authority's old institutional memory or public claim. | Source-backed restoration focus, relic/symbol event, local recognition. | Rival authority recognition, failed public congress, route contradiction. | Revival State decision, ceremonial legitimacy rewards. |
| Integration Cost | How difficult peaceful integration will be. | Rival claims, high autonomy demands, regional distance. | Federal constitution, local offices, trust missions. | Core upgrades and federation status. |
| Foreign Sensitivity | How likely colonial powers or neighbours react. | Ports, mines, sacred symbols, anti-colonial militancy. | Treaties, recognition, foreign mediation. | Scramble escalation, crisis news, guarantees. |
| Mythic Drift | Whether the authority is beginning to behave as a high-chaos force. | Chaos tier, Mythic Mandate focuses, strange event chains. | Grounded reform route, sceptical investigation, low chaos. | Mythic Court branch, animated UI, supernatural units. |
| Abuse Risk | Whether forced integration can backfire. | Forced labour, military governors, ignored atrocities, exploitation. | Reparations, local law, court settlements. | Revolts, refusal, forest retaliation, achievements. |

### Selector flow

1. Choose a region from the atlas.
2. The UI shows broad regional authority plus up to three niche authority candidates.
3. The player can recognize one candidate, table a second candidate for later, or ask the regional authority to mediate.
4. Recognition starts a 90–180 day mission with map, supply, local trust, and resource requirements.
5. Success spawns or upgrades the authority; partial success grants a weak council with higher integration cost; failure raises Colonial Alarm and local resistance.
6. Later focuses can transform recognized authorities into federal partners, protectorates, integrated regions, rebellious rivals, or high-chaos courts.

The AI should use the same helper effects without relying on human GUI clicks. AI should prefer authorities in controlled regions, avoid high Foreign Sensitivity while weak, and never pick nonhuman/ecological actors unless high-chaos route gates are met.

## New focus-tree content

Add a side lane branching from the regional authority / integration lane:

1. **Open the Living Atlas** — unlocks authority selector, regional cards, and first candidate research.
2. **No Region Is a Blank Map** — regional authority subjects gain local names and first trust missions.
3. **Invite the Old Courts** — reveals low-chaos legacy authorities in controlled regions.
4. **The Congress of Many Thrones** — federal route can seat multiple legacy authorities without forcing integration.
5. **Registers, Drums, Stools, Stones** — unlocks source-sensitive identity decisions and asset gates.
6. **The Archive Chooses Sides** — mutually exclusive split:
   - **Archive Under Law:** grounded integration, lower Mythic Drift, stronger Legitimacy.
   - **Archive Under Command:** forced protectorates, stronger immediate military aid, higher Abuse Risk.
   - **Archive Under Thunder:** high-chaos mythic route, raises Mythic Drift and unlocks Mythic Courts.
7. **Authorities of River and Road** — Wadai, Lozi, Kilwa, Ajuran, Kongo, Luba-Lunda supply route upgrades.
8. **Authorities of Throne and Court** — Asante, Oyo, Mossi, Buganda/Bunyoro, Merina, Aksum legitimacy route.
9. **Authorities of Script and Memory** — Bamum, Nri, Aro, Kuba, Luba-Lunda, Nubian archive route.
10. **Authorities of Mountain and Refuge** — Basotho, Rif, Futa, Kalahari, Herero-Nama protective route.
11. **The Forest Must Sign Separately** — high-chaos ecological branch; recognizes nonhuman/ecological pacts.
12. **Africa Is One, Not One Voice** — late federal finisher if at least eight recognized authorities remain loyal and no forced-annexation revolt is active.
13. **The Continent Speaks in Too Many Tongues** — high-chaos finisher; unlocks absurd authority interactions and a super-event role label, research required.

## New decision and mission families

### Candidate research decisions

- **Survey Local Archives:** costs civilian factory time, local stability, and convoys when offshore or island-based. Reveals source-dependent authority candidates.
- **Host the Regional Hearing:** timed mission requiring control of named capitals/ports/rail hubs and at least one supplied division in the region. Success raises Local Trust.
- **Recognize a Legacy Authority:** grants a subject/council with autonomy terms. Costs Legitimacy and raises Foreign Sensitivity.
- **Offer Charter Autonomy:** reduces Integration Cost but increases the subject's ability to resist later forced annexation.
- **Demand Command Subordination:** lowers immediate military delays but raises Abuse Risk and possible resistance.
- **Invite the Mythic Witnesses:** high-chaos only; raises Mythic Drift, unlocks route events, and risks losing grounded support.

### Authority-specific missions

Each recognized authority should get one small mission chain. Examples:

- Asante: hold Gold Coast inland roads and port link for 120 days.
- Oyo: field or lend cavalry/motorized/scout units and keep them supplied for 100 days.
- Bamum: run a literacy/coded-order program using civilian factories and command staff.
- Kongo: rebuild a river-administration line between capital, port, and Congo basin state group.
- Luba-Lunda/Kazembe: secure copper/lake/river states and local trust.
- Kilwa: keep convoy route open to at least two Indian Ocean ports.
- Lozi: complete a seasonal floodplain movement mission without losing the capital.
- Basotho: keep mountain passes guarded during an enemy offensive or colonial alarm spike.
- Great Forest Congress: protect designated forest states from resource extraction and enemy occupation.

### Clutter control

- Only one region selected at a time.
- Maximum three candidate authority cards shown to humans at once.
- Maximum active authority missions should scale with Congress Cohesion and route.
- Obsolete cards hide when the region is integrated, lost, or under revolt.
- AI uses weighted invisible helper decisions instead of GUI card spam.

## Absurd path design rules

High-chaos absurdity should feel earned and mechanically sharp.

1. A grounded authority must exist or be researched before its mythic form appears.
2. Mythic routes require high chaos, high Mythic Drift, and either a Mythic Mandate focus or repeated strange incidents.
3. Mythic rewards are strong and weird, but not free: they consume Legitimacy, Regional Trust, supply, stability, faction cohesion, or time.
4. Each mythic route has a failure state.
5. Nonhuman pact routes cannot be integrated as normal states or recruited as normal humans.
6. The implementation report must list which absurd routes are implemented, which are queued, and which are blocked for source or asset review.

## Implementation acceptance for this expansion

The implementation does not need every listed candidate to be fully playable in the first pass if scope must be staged, but it must not call the expansion complete unless the implemented package includes at least:

- 24 researched candidate authority entries in docs/specs and localisation planning;
- 12 implemented low/mid-chaos legacy authorities across at least five regions;
- 6 implemented high-chaos/mythic routes;
- 3 implemented explicit nonhuman/ecological pact actors with shared nonhuman classification;
- the Living Atlas selector or an equivalent clutter-controlled decision category;
- dynamic costs and missions for each implemented authority;
- route-specific AI choices and invalid-route blockers;
- asset source-mode notes for every visible authority identity;
- acceptance/audit report listing implemented, queued, blocked, and rejected authorities.
