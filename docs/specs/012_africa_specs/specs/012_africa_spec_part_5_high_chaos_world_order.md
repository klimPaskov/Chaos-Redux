# Event 12 Africa specification, part 5

## High-chaos actors, Scramble response, continent unifiers, and The World

Public country, route, actor, super-event, focus, and mechanic names in this file use direct readable names in the current package. Technical slugs remain separate from player-facing localisation. Historical African identities remain grounded. Impossible actors are explicitly fictional, nonhuman, elemental, or supernatural.

## Escalation boundary

The baseline and first two evolution layers should remain human and politically grounded. Strange units may appear as experiments or symbolic formations at Evolution II, but full nonhuman countries, living landscapes, impossible diseases, and elemental warfare belong to Evolution III or the post-unification phase.

High-chaos content must never imply that an African ethnic group is animal-like, primitive, supernatural by nature, or less human. Nonhuman actors must be visibly nonhuman. Human nature cults, oracle movements, and restoration movements should be described as political or religious organisations that interact with impossible forces, not as caricatures of a real people.

## High-chaos entry logic

A hidden branch becomes possible only when all basic conditions are met:

- Event 12 is active
- Evolution III has fired or an equivalent forced tier 6 opening exists
- the relevant high-chaos content is enabled
- the world has extreme chaos or a connected supernatural event is active
- the host has completed at least one regional congress or controls a valid high-chaos origin region
- no incompatible terminal state is active

Each actor also needs a specific discovery condition. The player first sees symptoms, rumours, missing patrols, impossible animal behaviour, moving monuments, altered rivers, or accurate disaster predictions. The first report must not explain the hidden mechanic directly.

## High-chaos actor families

### Pan

Nature:

A clearly nonhuman chimpanzee polity with organised intelligence, tool use, territorial memory, and its own political factions. The public country name can be `Pan`, with direct state forms such as `Kingdom of Pan`, `Pan Republic`, or `Pan Commune` according to route.

Possible origin:

- dense forest region
- repeated research or animal-intelligence incidents
- a failed military experiment
- a supernatural forest event

Playable identity:

Pan is not a joke-only tag. It has a compact country package built around forest movement, engineering, scavenged equipment, human diplomacy, and internal debate over coexistence or expansion.

Routes:

- forest coexistence
- toolmaker republic
- war troop hierarchy
- high-chaos oracle troop

Units:

- Pan sappers
- forest raiders
- scavenger support companies
- canopy reconnaissance

League relationship:

Pan can become a special Charter member, autonomous forest subject, rival nonhuman state, or high-chaos regional anchor. Ordinary members may demand safeguards.

### Gorilla Kingdom

Nature:

A clearly nonhuman gorilla monarchy or council state. The name is direct and intentionally absurd. It should have a serious internal political package beneath the comedy.

Possible origin:

- mountain or forest sanctuary
- repeated protection of a remote region
- supernatural leader reveal
- high-chaos animal mobilisation

Routes:

- guardian kingdom
- silverback council
- isolationist sanctuary
- continental conquest

Units:

- gorilla heavy infantry
- forest shock guards
- porter and engineer formations

Balance:

Gorilla units have high strength and breakthrough, low equipment compatibility, poor strategic mobility outside suitable terrain, and severe supply or population limits.

### The Green

Nature:

A forest superorganism, collective intelligence, or supernatural ecological state. It is not a human tribe. The direct public name can be `The Green` if final localisation accepts the article, or `Green` with route forms.

Mechanic:

- forest spread
- ecological demands
- protection of core biomes
- retaliation through weather, growth, and supply disruption

Units:

- rootbound defenders
- moving thickets
- spore scouts
- human covenant auxiliaries

League relationship:

The Green can sign a covenant rather than an ordinary treaty. The host must protect selected regions and limit extraction. Breaking the terms raises Ecological Wrath.

### Living rivers

Nature:

One major river system becomes an actor with a spirit, collective, or impossible hydrological will. The public name uses the river directly, such as `Nile`, `Congo`, `Niger`, or `Zambezi`, according to the origin.

Mechanic:

- flood and drought bargaining
- river transport
- control of crossings and ports
- seasonal demands
- waterborne defence

Units:

- riverborn infantry
- amphibious hosts
- flood engineers
- current riders as a clearly supernatural formation

League relationship:

A living river may become a protected natural member, demand sovereignty over banks, or fight states that dam, poison, or exploit it.

### Stoneborn

Nature:

Living statues and animated stone armies. The design can use fictional monuments, generated art, or a carefully researched high-chaos transformation of an archaeological region. It must not claim that a real ancient culture literally created living weapons.

Possible origin:

- Great Zimbabwe legacy route
- Kush or Meroe high-chaos route
- an unknown ruin or meteor event

Routes:

- guardians of the ruins
- wandering stone host
- crown of stone
- silent world conquest

Units:

- stone cohorts
- colossus support
- siege statues
- immobile monument garrisons

Balance:

Very high hardness and defence, extremely slow reinforcement, severe supply or special-resource costs, vulnerability to heavy weapons and air attack.

### The Rain

Nature:

An oracle state, weather consciousness, or human political order led by impossible predictors. The public name can be `Rain` or a region-specific direct name after research.

Mechanic:

- forecast accuracy
- disaster prediction
- rain bargains
- crop and drought control
- false prophecy risk

Units:

- oracle reconnaissance
- storm signal corps
- weather wardens

League relationship:

The Rain can serve as a high-chaos adviser to the League, demand tribute, or create a rival state.

### The Drought

Nature:

An elemental antagonist rather than a human country. It represents moving desiccation, dust, famine pressure, and a hostile environmental will.

Mechanic:

- state degradation
- supply collapse
- forced migration
- water seizure
- disaster pressure

The Drought should usually be an enemy or temporary disaster state. Making it playable requires a dedicated destruction route and clear world-threat classification.

### Ancient hosts

Nature:

Armies connected to a restored identity, legendary court, or supernatural memory. They can be spectral, reincarnated, stone, or otherwise impossible. They must be presented as a high-chaos mutation, not factual history.

Mechanic:

- relic sites
- command by ritual or memory
- limited recruitment
- powerful route-specific units
- conflict with modern institutions

## Ecological Wrath mechanic

Ecological Wrath is the main high-chaos pressure value. It is shown only after the covenant route becomes public.

Wrath rises when the host or members:

- violate a natural covenant
- extract protected resources
- destroy a sacred or supernatural site
- use biological or chemical weapons in protected regions
- ignore a disaster demand
- attack a nonhuman member
- break a river or forest agreement

Wrath falls when the host:

- completes restoration or relief projects
- protects a biome
- returns territory or access
- accepts a costly covenant demand
- defeats a destructive high-chaos faction
- contains contamination

Thresholds unlock:

- accurate warnings
- local weather effects
- disaster-family pressure against enemies
- uncontrolled regional disaster
- monthly enemy calamity during war
- continent-scale backlash

The player should see the cause of Wrath changes. Hidden future effects may remain uncertain.

## Disaster prediction and retaliation

The high-chaos route can connect to the Natural Disasters system through shared pressure and family selection.

### Prediction

Oracle actors can warn of an eligible disaster family before it occurs. Prediction quality depends on:

- oracle power
- recent success
- local Reach
- intelligence
- chaos tier
- whether the host paid the requested cost

A true prediction allows evacuation, stockpiling, fortification, or diplomatic leverage. A false prediction wastes resources and damages Authority.

### Retaliation

When at war, a covenant actor can direct environmental pressure toward an enemy region. The action should:

- require high Wrath or stored ritual capacity
- choose an eligible disaster family based on geography
- use a substantial cooldown
- create foreign condemnation and world fear
- risk backfire or uncontrolled spread
- interact with existing disaster recovery

Player-facing text describes repeated storms, floods, drought, wildfire, earth movement, or impossible weather. It should not state that Event 13 was fired.

### Monthly calamity state

At the highest route stage, an enemy can receive a recurring monthly disaster roll while fighting the covenant. This is a rare capstone. It needs:

- clear visual warning
- counterplay through destroying ritual sites, making peace, meeting demands, or reducing the covenant’s control
- strict target validity
- no unbounded daily or world iteration
- cleanup when war ends

## High-chaos disease system

The design uses fictional or supernatural diseases and the existing biological warfare, contamination, deaths, and condemnation systems. It does not provide real pathogen development instructions.

### Disease families

- forest sleep
- river fever
- stone lung
- red rain sickness
- memory plague
- animal-host mutation
- oracle wasting

Names are working labels. Final disease names require localisation review and should not copy real diseases casually.

### Research and containment

The host can:

- study symptoms
- build field hospitals
- quarantine states
- create medical corridors
- invite foreign or diaspora specialists
- develop a cure or prophylaxis
- destroy contaminated stockpiles
- hide, exploit, or weaponise the disease

### Weaponisation

A high-chaos route can convert a fictional disease into an abstract biowarfare payload or strike. It uses existing stockpiles, raids, condemnation, contamination, and outbreak logic. It must not specify laboratory methods, culture conditions, dissemination engineering, or real-world biological procedures.

### Backfire

Stockpiles, rituals, and strikes can cause domestic outbreaks. Safety investment, medical capacity, and route choices change the risk. The strongest weapon route can become a world threat.

## Scramble for Africa response crisis

The Scramble response occurs when Africa is fully unified or reaches the accepted confederal equivalent. It represents foreign powers attempting to contain, divide, punish, negotiate with, or destroy the new continental order.

The term is a deliberate reversal of the historical partition. It does not mean that the same colonial conference repeats unchanged.

## Crisis participants

Potential foreign participants include:

- countries still holding African or adjacent territory
- former colonial powers
- great powers with bases or concessions
- faction leaders that lost an Allied member through the RSA branch
- ideological rivals
- states threatened by resource nationalisation
- states that fear other continent unifiers

Participation depends on current interests. A defeated or friendly former colonial power should not join only because of its historical tag.

## Foreign response tracks

### Recognition

A foreign power accepts the new state, negotiates assets, and seeks trade. It can gain access without joining the crisis.

### Conditional recognition

The power demands base rights, debt settlement, minority or subject guarantees, compensation, or limits on external liberation.

### Sanctions

The power restricts trade, finance, equipment, convoys, or resource access. Sanctions create real economic missions.

### Ultimatum

The power demands withdrawal from selected bases, release of a member, return of property, or abandonment of a world-order route.

### Expedition planning

The power prepares landings, proxy support, air operations, and naval blockade. The League can detect and disrupt the plan.

### Colonial panic

Remaining colonial administrations militarise, suppress movements, or seek immediate evacuation. This can connect to Independence Wave.

### Coalition war

Several powers form a containment coalition and attack. The coalition needs a clear goal, such as restoring access, breaking the League, or seizing strategic bases. It should not be a generic total-annexation war.

## Scramble crisis phases

### Phase 1: diplomatic shock

Foreign states choose response tracks. The player sees ultimatums, sanctions, recognition offers, and naval concentration.

### Phase 2: coalition formation

Hostile powers negotiate a common plan. Africa can divide them, offer settlements, mobilise members, and secure neutral trade.

### Phase 3: intervention or settlement

The crisis becomes war, a recognised treaty, a prolonged siege economy, or a divided diplomatic result.

### Phase 4: aftermath

The continental constitution addresses emergency powers, member sacrifice, destroyed infrastructure, foreign bases, and new world status.

## Scramble super-event treatment

The crisis qualifies for super-event treatment when at least one of these is true:

- a multi-major intervention coalition forms
- the ultimatum expires and war begins
- Africa defeats the principal coalition
- a treaty forces broad recognition of the unified continent

Final title, button text, quote, cultural reference, image, and audio records are installed through the separate super-event research workflow. The spec remains the role and tone authority, while final playback, localisation, and event-completion review remain open.

## Other continent unifiers

Evolution III and the post-unification phase can sponsor six other continent-scale projects. Each must have a unique identity and focus architecture. They are not color swaps of Africa.

The Africa package supplies the trigger, diplomacy, sponsorship, and interaction interface. Each project still requires a dedicated implementation package before it can be considered complete.

### Core mechanic

**Crossroads Balance** tracks Arab unity, Persian influence, Anatolian power, minority guarantees, holy-site legitimacy, water security, and foreign oil control.

### Focus families

- survival and end of foreign mandates
- Arab federal route

- restored imperial or monarchic route
- socialist republics route
- oil and pipeline sovereignty
- water and food security
- holy cities and religious settlement
- desert and mountain armies
- Africa and Asia diplomacy
- high-chaos desert, djinn, or prophetic route with source and sensitivity review

### Identity package

Direct names should reflect the successful route, such as a regional union, federation, empire, republic, or commune. Avoid a single ethnic label when the state includes several major peoples unless the route is explicitly exclusionary and carries consequences.

## Dynamic union rules

A two-continent union needs:

- both actors’ consent or a military victory route
- a constitutional settlement
- public name and adjective
- flag and emblem
- capital or distributed government plan
- subject and member treatment
- focus tree continuation
- AI
- breakup rules

Names remain short and geographic. Three or more continent unions can use a larger readable identity such as Afro-Eurasia only when the map and constitution support it.

## The World is One path

The World path is a terminal, rare, campaign-ending system.

### Required conditions

- global chaos above 1000
- `world_end` is not already set
- Africa is one has fired
- Africa has completed a post-unification world-order route
- every required continent has a valid continent-scale actor or a documented absence resolution
- at least two other continent actors have completed their own world-order path
- no ordinary diplomatic settlement can satisfy the chosen route
- the player or AI actor has chosen continental war preparation

The Middle East can exist as a separate actor when its package is active. It may join Asia, Africa, or Europe through prior union, but the state must be resolved before the final war network.

### War structure

The path begins with a congress, ultimatum chain, or failed world settlement. It then creates continent-scale wars according to alliances and rivalries. It should not instantly declare every actor on every other actor if existing unions or treaties create a more coherent conflict.

Possible structures:

- all-against-all continental wars
- two or three grand coalitions
- sequential challenge wars
- a hegemon against a defensive world coalition
- high-chaos elemental powers against ordinary continent states

### Elimination and succession

A continent actor is eliminated when it no longer controls its required heartland and has no valid successor or exile path. Conquered continent identities can become subjects, federated partners, annexed regions, or absorbed union components according to the victor’s route.

### Last standing power

The last valid continent-scale power receives:

- `world_end`
- a scenario-specific global flag
- The World identity
- final map name, adjective, flag, leader, portrait or governing body
- final focus or decision closure
- super-event visibility
- unique final audio ID and track
- shutdown or gating of incompatible future event systems
- campaign-ending documentation and achievement checks

### Peaceful world route

A peaceful world union can be attempted only if every continent actor accepts and has a compatible constitution. It should be harder diplomatically than winning one ordinary alliance. Rejection may start the continental wars.

### The World super-event

The final presentation has an installed researched package for title, button, quote, image, and audio. It should feel terminal and should not reuse the Scramble or Africa is one presentation. Final playback, localisation, and event-completion review remain open.

## Anti-bloat limits

The high-chaos and world-order package should remain deep without swallowing every other event.

Do not add:

- a separate full disaster system when Event 13 already exists
- real-world biological procedures
- dozens of nonhuman tags with identical mechanics
- a full unique continent package without its own later source spec and implementation pass
- instant continent unions from one focus
- extra super-events for every regional congress
- animal or supernatural versions of real ethnic groups
- blanket claims outside Africa before the post-unification phase

## Completion conditions for this part

The high-chaos and world-order implementation is incomplete unless:

- nonhuman actors are explicitly nonhuman
- each major high-chaos actor has politics, units, AI, assets, and League behavior
- Ecological Wrath has visible causes and consequences
- disaster retaliation has counterplay and cleanup
- disease content remains abstract and uses existing systems
- special units have limits and counters
- the Scramble response has diplomacy, sanctions, expedition, war, and aftermath paths
- every continent unifier has a unique mechanic and focus architecture
- sponsorship creates obligations and possible rivalry
- dynamic unions have real constitutional and country-package work
- The World path is guarded, rare, terminal, and fully presented
