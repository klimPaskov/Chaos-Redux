# Event 012 — Evolutions, Super-Event Roles, Triggerable Scenario, and World-End Path

## Baseline versus evolutions

Baseline progression is the ordinary lifecycle of the Africa event: proclamation, first charter, liberation wars, regional authority creation, integration, Africa Is One, Second Scramble, and post-unification ambitions. These ordinary stages are not logged as evolutions.

Evolutions are mutation tracks that change how the event opens or how active actors behave once chaos increases. Event 012 can have up to four evolutions from the user brief. Each evolution must define both:

- **Active-event evolution:** what changes for an already active Africa unifier.
- **Pre-fire evolved opening:** what changes if Event 012 has not fired yet and the world is already chaotic when it first fires.

## Baseline — The Continental Claim

### Fire-once baseline

A valid African-capital country becomes the Africa unifier, receives the Africa package, and starts with a grounded but dramatic political/military/faction system.

Immediate content:

- Cosmetic tag change.
- African paper cores on all African states.
- Continental Congress decision category.
- Shared/overlay focus tree.
- Early news.
- First faction diplomacy tools.
- Regional authority system.
- RSA civil-war branch if RSA is selected and in Allies.

### Baseline stage flow

| Stage | Player-facing state | Main mechanics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proclamation | The world learns of a continental claim. | Cosmetic tag, cores, paper burden, values, first news. |
| Charter offers | African countries receive aid/protection/faction offers. | Observer/protection/member decisions, aid missions. |
| Liberation wars | Colonizers become primary early enemy. | Colonial ultimatums, support wars, liberation momentum. |
| Regional authorities | Local African subject/faction authorities emerge. | Release/federate/protectorate/integration mechanics. |
| Integration pressure | Members and authorities face federation/puppet/annex pressure. | Influence, trust, cohesion, resistance. |
| Africa Is One | Africa is controlled/integrated/loyal. | Super-event role label, final continental identity. |
| Second Scramble | Outside powers react to unified Africa. | Diplomatic crisis, sanctions, intervention, war pressure. |
| Continental pole | Africa begins acting beyond Africa. | Anti-colonial global pressure and continent-unifier sponsorship. |

## Evolution I — New African Power

### Design role

Evolution I makes the unifier’s early expansion clearer and better communicated. It adds consolidation of nearby African states, stronger public flavour, and early news so other countries understand why wars and interventions start.

### Availability

- World has reached at least the first meaningful chaos escalation tier after baseline selection, or Event 012 fires in an already unstable world.
- The Africa unifier exists and is not defeated.
- Evolution is enabled in Event Logs.

### Active-event evolution effects

For an existing unifier:

- Unlocks `Nearby Congresses` decision family: adjacent African states can be invited, protected, pressured, or targeted through clearer route-specific actions.
- Adds early regional consolidation missions for the home region and one adjacent region.
- Adds early news event: “a new African unifier has declared a continental charter,” with route-specific observer text.
- Improves default AI priority for adjacent-region integration before distant wars.
- Adds first “member resistance” incident if an African state is pressured too quickly.

### Pre-fire evolved opening

If Event 012 has not fired yet:

- The selected unifier starts with a stronger opening package.
- It can immediately identify a home region and adjacent region.
- It receives one additional regional authority or observer candidate.
- First news is more explicit and fires for major powers, African states, and colonial holders.
- Colonial Alarm starts higher, because the world already recognizes the threat.

### Player-facing additions

- Early report event for the unifier.
- News event for observers.
- Decision icons for `Nearby Congresses`, `Protection Offer`, and `Home Region Survey`.
- Event log evolution entry: role label “New African Power” or similar, final localisation researched/implemented later.

## Evolution II — Continental Instruments

### Design role

Evolution II gives the unifier stronger tools as it controls more of Africa. It makes cosmetic tag, leader/title flavour, units, and special mechanics more dramatic. It introduces weirder but still contained content such as elephant units and high-chaos route reveals.

### Availability

- Africa unifier exists.
- It controls or protects a meaningful share of African regions, or the world chaos tier is high enough for an evolved opening.
- At least one regional authority exists or one colonial holder has been defeated in Africa.

### Active-event evolution effects

For an existing unifier:

- Unlocks stronger integration actions with dynamic costs.
- Unlocks `Continental Instruments` focus cluster and decisions.
- Adds leader/title/cosmetic escalation options once the unifier controls a large part of Africa.
- Unlocks Elephant Logistics Corps planning path.
- Unlocks the first high-chaos myth/nature rumours if chaos is high.
- Improves paper-core cleanup for controlled regions.
- Adds stronger but costly temporary buffs during liberation wars.

### Pre-fire evolved opening

If the event fires for the first time at this evolution:

- The unifier starts with a more dramatic public identity.
- Initial forces include stronger irregulars and possibly a veteran cadre.
- The first focus tree route lock can be closer; the unifier is not just starting from a congress.
- First regional authority may spawn if enough African states are already colonized or chaotic.
- Elephant units remain locked behind focus/decision, not immediate free units.

### New systems

| System | Gameplay |
| --- | --- |
| Elephant Logistics Corps | Special high-cost support/battalion route; stronger in rough terrain, high supply burden. |
| Dramatic title changes | Route-specific leader/title/cosmetic stage once enough Africa is held. |
| Continental war preparation | Temporary buffs tied to legitimacy, equipment, and selected region. |
| Paper-core cleanup | Better integration tools, but still requires missions. |
| High-chaos rumours | Unlocks Green Covenant branch previews. |

### Player-facing additions

- Focus icons for elephant corps, continental instruments, and cosmetic escalation.
- Idea icons for upgraded government and paper-core mitigation.
- Optional generated animated leader/council frame for dramatic route reveals.

## Evolution III — Continental Export

### Design role

Evolution III turns unified or near-unified Africa into a global chaos actor. It can sponsor other continent unifiers, pressure colonial-adjacent interests outside Africa, and start conflicts that help other continents unify.

### Availability

- Africa unifier controls/protects a large share of Africa or has fired Africa Is One.
- The Second Scramble is active, resolved, or close.
- World chaos is high enough for global contagion of formable movements.

### Active-event evolution effects

For an existing unifier:

- Unlocks `Continental Export Office` focus group.
- Unlocks decisions to sponsor continent unifiers: Middle East, Asia, Europe, South America, and other available continent systems.
- Unlocks anti-colonial war goals against countries holding African-adjacent interests, overseas bases, or ex-colonial intervention rights.
- Adds diplomatic crisis events where other major powers debate whether Africa is a liberator, empire, or global threat.
- Adds route-specific sponsor styles:
  - Federal: congresses and recognition.
  - Revolutionary: insurgencies and liberation fronts.
  - Military: general staffs and war plans.
  - Crown: ceremonial protectorates and dynastic congresses.
  - Green Covenant: disasters, prophecy, and nonhuman/nature pacts.

### Pre-fire evolved opening

If Event 012 first fires at Evolution III:

- The unifier begins as a severe world actor, not a local anomaly.
- It starts with a stronger faction seed and one outside-continent contact.
- Colonial Alarm starts high.
- Major powers receive a stronger news event.
- The tree begins with some early branches bypassed or shortened, but still requires integration work.

### Sponsor mechanics

| Target continent movement | Africa can do | Costs | Risks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Middle East / West Asia | Fund, arm, recognize, or pressure a regional unifier. | Convoys, equipment, legitimacy, diplomacy. | Regional identity tension, great-power intervention. |
| Asia | Support anti-colonial congresses or continental claimants. | High equipment/convoy/industry costs. | Huge alarm, possible wars with majors. |
| Europe | Sponsor radical unification or anti-colonial collapse in Europe. | Very high legitimacy and post-Scramble strength. | Major war, ideological backlash. |
| South America | Use Atlantic/diaspora networks to sponsor continental congress. | Convoys, diplomacy, recognition. | Naval pressure and foreign intervention. |
| Other continents | Hook into future event systems with shared helper. | Dynamic. | Dynamic. |

### Player-facing additions

- Global map cards in UI post-unification tab.
- Decision category for continent sponsorship.
- News/super-event role labels for successful continent unions if they join Africa later.
- AI matrix for when Africa should sponsor or avoid each target.

## Evolution IV — Africa as World-Chaos Pole

### Design role

Evolution IV is the fully escalated version. Africa becomes one of the major world-chaos poles and can dynamically integrate other successful continent movements into combined formables such as African-Middle Eastern Union, Afroasian Union, Afroeurasian Union, Afro-Atlantic Union, or eventual world union.

### Availability

- Africa Is One has fired or is nearly complete.
- World chaos is Totalen Chaos or World Collapse level.
- One or more other continent unifiers exist or can be created by sponsor route.
- The Second Scramble has either been defeated, contained, or converted into a global confrontation.

### Active-event evolution effects

For an existing unifier:

- Unlocks dynamic continent-union decisions.
- Adds super-event role labels for major unions when the campaign threshold is large enough.
- Adds `Congress of Continents` focus branch.
- Adds final cosmetic identities and route-specific flags.
- Allows Africa to annex or federate successful continent unifiers if both have completed post-unification paths.
- If all prerequisites align, unlocks the World Is One path.

### Pre-fire evolved opening

If Event 012 somehow first fires at Evolution IV:

- The opening is absurdly severe.
- The selected unifier starts with a major continental claim, stronger forces, higher alarm, and immediate world attention.
- It may begin with a regional authority network and a pre-existing charter faction.
- It still must unify Africa before world-end content.

### Dynamic union rules

| Union state | Formation condition | Main result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| African-Middle Eastern Union | Africa Is One + Middle East unifier success and acceptance/defeat. | Cosmetic identity, new claims/integration path, route tension between African and West Asian identities. |
| Afroasian Union | Africa + Asia unifier; Middle East included under Asia if applicable. | Stronger global identity and huge alarm. |
| Afroeurasian Union | Africa + Europe, or Africa + Europe + Asia. | World-chaos pole; likely super-event role label. |
| Afro-Atlantic Union | Africa + South America or Atlantic/diaspora route. | Naval/diaspora/global south route. |
| Congress of Continents | Africa leads multiple continent unifiers without full annexation. | Federation route alternative; lower alarm than military annexation but slower. |
| The World Is One | All required continent unifiers and world-end prerequisites. | Terminal scenario. |

## Triggerable scenario

Event 012 has a triggerable scenario entry because it is a large formable sandbox.

Implemented scenario ID: `SCN-008`.

Scenario name: `Africa Is One`.

Scenario types:

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Africa Is One | Selects or creates a valid African-capital unifier, immediately forms a strong Africa, opens the Charter League, regional authority, Authority Atlas, sponsor, proof-ledger, and post-unification systems, and starts unification wars. |
| World Is One | Starts from the strong Africa opening, guarantees supported external continent-unifier actors for Middle East, Asia, Europe, and South Atlantic if no live candidate exists, verifies the proof/certification chain, applies the Congress of Continents identity, and triggers the terminal World Is One scenario. |

Intensity slider:

| Intensity | Setup |
| --- | --- |
| Low | Strong Africa, regional authority seeding, continental unification wars, low scenario alarm, and limited external-continent actor stockpiles and guard divisions for World Is One. |
| Medium | Strong Africa with larger logistics and medium external-continent actor stockpiles and guard divisions for World Is One. |
| High | Stronger Africa forces, sharper colonial reaction, regional authority seeding, and high external-continent actor stockpiles and guard divisions for World Is One. |
| Maximum | Maximum Africa logistics, severe opening pressure, Totalen Chaos readiness, multiple regional authorities, and the largest external-continent actor stockpile and guard package for World Is One. |

Scenario gates should block only impossible launches, not normal chaos/evolution prerequisites. The scenario directly creates instant chaos and setup according to controls.

Implementation note: the shared scenario window allows SCN-008 even if Event 012 has already fired or a world-end branch is active. The selector uses only `Africa Is One` and `World Is One`; the RSA scenario type is not part of this manual scenario. Both types use the weighted unifier selector and create the West African Congress host only if no valid African-capital country exists. `Africa Is One` starts continental wars immediately. `World Is One` seats the external continent-unifier readiness/proof/certification chain, creates a default external region actor where a region has no live candidate, gives supported actors intensity-scaled stockpiles and Continental Unifier Guard divisions, and starts the terminal branch through a scenario-only terminal helper.

## World-end scenario — The World Is One

### Unlock requirements

The world-end path can unlock only if all are true:

- Africa fully unifies and `Africa is one` has fired.
- World is already in extreme chaos / World Collapse state.
- Other continental unifiers exist.
- Other continental unifiers have pursued their post-unification focus paths.
- Other continental unifiers have also unlocked or accepted their own world-end path.
- Event 012 is not defeated or fractured.
- No incompatible world-end scenario already set `world_end`.

### Terminal premise

The African unifier, now a continental pole, declares that continental unification was only the first draft. The last borders are administrative errors, rival congresses must become one congress, and every continent that learned to speak as one must now answer whether the world itself can be made one.

The route should vary by political path:

| Africa route | World-end interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Federal Congress | A world federation built from continent congresses; slow, legalistic, fragile. |
| People’s Front | A world liberation front that abolishes empires and states through revolution. |
| General Staff | A unified world command, imposed by ultimatum and war. |
| Crown Congress | A ceremonial world crown/council of old seats, part ritual and part imperial order. |
| Green Covenant | A world compact between humanity, nature, rivers, forests, animals, and supernatural forces. |

### World-end gameplay steps

1. **Congress of Continents.** Invite or pressure continent unifiers.
2. **Unification Accession.** Integrate each unifier by federation, revolution, command, crown, or covenant.
3. **External proof verification.** Verify Middle East, Asia, Europe, and South Atlantic unifier proofs through the cross-continent sponsor ledger after the matching continent systems expose their own world-end-ready mandates.
4. **Refusal crisis.** Remaining major powers refuse and receive ultimatum or war.
5. **Last Borders mission.** Timed objective to hold major continental capitals, sea gates, and supply routes.
6. **The World Is One.** Terminal event fires; set `world_end`; stop incompatible event systems.

### Failure states

- A continent unifier refuses and forms an anti-world congress.
- Africa collapses under paper-core remnants and overextension before final focus.
- Green Covenant route loses control and creates nature/nonhuman world threat instead of unity.
- Military route triggers a final coalition war.
- Federal route fails if cohesion between continent unifiers collapses.

### End-state effects direction

This branch should be a terminal scenario, not a mere strong modifier. It should:

- Set `world_end` and a scenario-specific global flag.
- Fire final super-event role label `se_world_is_one`.
- Gate automatic random events as world-end scenarios do.
- Change map/order dramatically through annexation, federation, or world-congress mechanics depending on route.
- Document end-state in event docs and spreadsheet.



## Revision 2 evolution additions: old offices to impossible congress

The evolution track now has a clearer content ladder for subject expansion.

- **Evolution I:** Old Names Return. Historical/legacy authorities appear as observer offices and selected Charter subject candidates. No nonhuman countries.
- **Evolution II:** Totem Logistics. Elephant columns, omen-themed military logistics, old-office special units, and stronger authority missions unlock. Weirdness remains mostly symbolic, institutional, or experimental.
- **Evolution III:** Covenant Actors. Explicit nonhuman/supernatural actors can appear if route, region, Wild Mandate, Covenant Pressure, and Omen Reliability allow it. They use special classification and bespoke AI.
- **Evolution IV:** World Root. Nonhuman, supernatural, historical authority, continent sponsor, and cross-continent union systems can merge into the World Root Mandate and later The World Is One branch.

The new high-chaos actor list and disaster-warning mechanics are defined in `012_africa_high_chaos_absurd_paths.md`.
