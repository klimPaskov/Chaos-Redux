# 012 Africa spec part 4, evolutions, high-chaos routes, super-events, and world-end

## Baseline versus evolutions

Baseline progression is the normal growth of the Africa package. Evolutions are separate mutation tracks that make the event more severe, strange, or globally important. Do not log every integration stage as an evolution.

## Evolution I, regional consolidation

Evolution I begins when the unifier has active League relationships, controls nearby targets, or wins early anti-colonial interventions. It opens more expansion and intervention tools.

New content:

- regional consolidation focus groups
- early intervention news and report events
- stronger war preparation missions
- first rival bloc checks
- colonial panic stage one and two
- early restored-polity decisions
- League member confidence events

The player should understand that the event is no longer a local movement. Other countries start treating the unifier as the center of a continental chain reaction.

## Evolution II, continental tools

Evolution II begins once the unifier controls or strongly influences a large part of Africa. It gives stronger temporary buffs, claims, integration tools, and route-specific identity upgrades.

New content:

- advanced integration decisions
- stronger subject pressure and federation votes
- resource and industry acceleration projects
- visible leader title changes and portrait direction
- strange but still mostly human special units
- elephant logistics and shock route gates
- broader coastal and desert operations
- higher colonial panic

This evolution can make the cosmetic identity more explicit. The state can feel like Africa in public symbolism, while still needing staged integration to make that claim true.

## Evolution III, world-chaos actor

Evolution III begins when Africa is unified or near-unified and colonial power has been heavily broken. Africa becomes a global actor.

New content:

- pressure on countries holding African-adjacent interests
- support for other continent unifiers
- continent-to-continent diplomacy
- global anti-colonial war goals
- post-unification economic and manpower tools
- super-event eligibility for Scramble for Africa
- dynamic interaction with Middle East, Europe, Asia, North America, South America, and Oceania unifiers

Every other continent unifier should receive its own unique focus tree and identity package. Africa can sponsor, pressure, direct, federate with, or fight them. The Africa spec should not design all those trees fully, but it should require their own later packages.

## Evolution IV, continental pole and world-end gates

Evolution IV is the fully escalated state. Africa becomes one of the main world-chaos poles.

New content:

- dynamic union names such as Afro-Asian or Afro-Eurasian when relevant
- continent unions, rivalry, federation, annexation, and war logic
- high-chaos branches for oracle states, nature cults, animal actors, ancient hosts, and supernatural powers
- final gates for The World is One route
- ability to start terminal continent wars only when all requirements are satisfied

The naming must remain readable. Do not make public country names bureaucratic.

## High-chaos human and supernatural routes

High-chaos content should escalate from strange human politics into impossible forces. It should never write nonhuman or supernatural actors as caricatures of African peoples. Nonhuman countries should be clearly nonhuman, animal, spiritual, ancient, or supernatural.

### Nature covenant route

This route bargains with forests, rivers, droughts, flood prophets, or oracle councils. It can warn enemies that disasters will happen if demands are refused. In gameplay, it can trigger abstract disaster pressure against enemies, raise colonial panic, and create internal instability if the unifier overuses it.

Rules:

- disaster pressure should be abstract and game-level
- avoid naming a specific external event in player-facing text
- enemies at war can suffer recurring weather, flood, drought, disease, or supply crises
- refusal of demands can raise disaster pressure
- allies can fear the unifier and lose cohesion if nature powers are used recklessly

### Great ape and animal actors

Possible high-chaos countries include gorilla and chimpanzee nations. They should be nonhuman and strange. Direct names can use habitats or animal identities, such as Virunga Gorillas or Mahale Chimpanzees, but final names should be checked for tone and readability.

Gameplay roles:

- forest defensive powers
- impossible terrain bonuses
- nonhuman leader portraits
- special units that are strong in jungle or forest
- diplomacy limited to high-chaos pacts and demands
- classification as actual nonhuman countries in implementation

### Oracle and living-statue routes

Oracle states and living-statue armies should feel mythic rather than bureaucratic. They can predict disasters, demand offerings, punish colonisers, and unlock ancient host units.

Gameplay roles:

- prediction missions that reduce disaster risk or redirect it
- living-statue battalions with low speed and high defense
- ancient host levies tied to old capitals and monuments
- route-specific leader portraits and animated frames

### Disease and biowarfare route

High-chaos nature or oracle branches can interact with the existing biological warfare system only at the mod-mechanic level. The spec should never include real biological details. Diseases are fictional gameplay pressures.

Uses:

- fictional disease outbreaks as enemy debuffs
- abstract state contamination or outbreak pressure
- high condemnation if used by ordinary states
- special nonhuman or supernatural exemptions only if the broader chaos system supports them
- internal blowback if the player uses disease too often

## Scramble for Africa reaction

When Africa secures the whole continent, trigger a Scramble for Africa moment. This does not mean old colonial powers get a second historical scramble. It means outside powers panic over a unified Africa and attempt diplomatic crisis, sanctions, ultimatums, expeditionary planning, rival sponsorship, or a final attempt to split the continent.

This moment is strong enough for super-event treatment if the implementation package supports full image, quote, audio, localisation, docs, and spreadsheet alignment. The final title, quote, button text, and audio choice are research-gated.

## Africa is one super-event direction

This can fire when Africa is fully unified or when the campaign reaches a major public recognition threshold. It should feel like continent-scale unity, not a normal annexation popup. The image should show people, banners, old and new symbols, or a continent-scale congress with military and civilian presence. Avoid a plain map-first image.

Research gates:

- title research required
- main quote research required
- cultural remark research required
- audio research required
- generated or sourced image decision required

## Scramble for Africa super-event direction

This can fire when outside powers enter the final reaction crisis after African unification. It should feel like foreign panic, not a repetition of 1884. The image should show ports, convoys, colonial offices, African crowds, or expeditionary anxiety, but not center the emotional weight on a staff table.

Research gates remain required.

## The World is One world-end path

The world-end path requires all of the following design conditions:

- Africa fully unified
- chaos above world-end threshold
- Africa is one public milestone has fired
- other continent unifiers exist and have pursued their own post-unification paths
- no other world-end is active
- the player or AI reaches the final continent-war branch

The route begins continental wars between continent-scale powers. Europe, Asia, North America, South America, Oceania, Africa, and a separate Middle East unifier if present can fight. The last standing continent-scale power becomes `The World`.

`The World` is the terminal identity. It should be rare, hard, and campaign-ending. It should not be reachable just by conquering Africa quickly.

World-end super-event research is required. The final title, quote, button, image, and audio must not be invented in this package.
