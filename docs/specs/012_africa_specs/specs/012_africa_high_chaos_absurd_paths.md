# Event 012 Africa — High-chaos absurd path book

This file expands the absurd, supernatural, animal, and impossible-history routes for Event 012 Africa. These routes are locked behind higher evolutions and must remain separate from the grounded subject layer.

The rule is simple: human African societies are not monsters. Absurd animal states, spirit courts, plant senates, living ruins, and weather powers are explicitly nonhuman, supernatural, symbolic, or impossible actors. They should use nonhuman/supernatural classification, special localisation, and separate generated assets. They must not be presented as ethnic caricatures or as ordinary releasable human countries.

## Unlock ladder

| Layer | Chaos/evolution gate | What can appear | Design limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Baseline** | Event fires | Human Charter League, regional authorities, paper cores, anti-colonial rescue wars. | No nonhuman countries. Elephant units can be planned but not supernatural. |
| **Evolution I — Old Names Return** | Early evolved escalation | More historical observer offices and legacy subjects. | Old courts are political/cultural, not magical. |
| **Evolution II — Totem Logistics** | Stronger evolved tools | Elephant logistics, omen-themed icons, unusual unit templates, intensified authority missions. | Still mostly human/institutional; “weird” content is framed as propaganda, symbolism, or experimental logistics. |
| **Evolution III — Covenant Actors** | Totalen Chaos-like threshold or major Covenant route | Gorilla Nation, Chimpanzee Assembly, Great Herd Compact, Orisha Court, Ananse Web, Mami Wata Tidemark, Baobab Senate as possible nonhuman/supernatural actors. | Must use nonhuman/supernatural classification. Human states react with awe, fear, bargaining, or denial. |
| **Evolution IV — World Root** | World Collapse or near-world-end | Nature courts can merge with continental unification and continent-sponsor paths. | Becomes a world-chaos pole; avoid infinite tag spam; focus on a few dramatic actors. |

## Green Covenant master route

The Green Covenant is the main path that unites the absurd systems. It begins as an environmental/anti-colonial stewardship path and can become a supernatural/nonhuman world-order route.

### Values

| Value | Meaning | Rises from | Falls from | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Covenant Pressure** | How strongly nonhuman/supernatural forces are entering politics. | colonial devastation, forest exploitation, high chaos, Green Covenant focuses, ignored disaster warnings | federal compromise, conservation settlements, scientific containment, peaceful integration | nonhuman actors, disaster ultimatums, animated UI states |
| **Human Legitimacy** | Whether normal African states still trust the unifier. | fair arbitration, liberation, protecting subjects, respecting autonomy | sacrificing subjects to nature actors, forced integration, occult threats | safe integration, federal subject loyalty |
| **Wild Mandate** | Strength of nonhuman/nature actors. | forest control, animal sanctuaries, spiritual court decisions, successful disaster predictions | habitat destruction, defeated animal actors, scientific suppression | Great Herd, Gorilla Nation, Baobab Senate, Nature Congress |
| **Omen Reliability** | Whether prophecies/disaster predictions are believed. | correct warnings, completed Ananse/Mami Wata/Oshun/Shango missions | false warnings, overuse, weather failures, ignored costs | stronger disaster-warning decisions and super-event eligibility |

### Player dilemma

The player should not simply take all supernatural power. Each Covenant decision trades power for political danger:

- Threatening colonizers with floods or lightning can avoid a war, but raises Colonial Alarm and scares human League members.
- Accepting Gorilla or Chimpanzee subjects protects forests and adds absurd units, but can make normal governments doubt the unifier’s sanity.
- Binding Baobab or Termite actors can create huge fort/supply power, but slows offensive wars and increases Paper-Core Burden if normal administrators are ignored.
- Calling the Orisha Court can give devastating event-driven powers, but if Human Legitimacy falls too low, historical authorities revolt against the Covenant.

## Nonhuman and supernatural country packages

### Gorilla Nation

**Type:** actual nonhuman country / high-chaos forest polity.

**Home trigger:** central/equatorial forest regions, especially Congo basin or Great Lakes mountain-gorilla-adjacent regions, when Covenant Pressure and Wild Mandate are high.

**Opening event direction:** forest patrols report disciplined gorilla formations dismantling poacher camps, tearing down colonial survey posts, and returning captured rifles stacked neatly beside broken logging machines. Human witnesses disagree over whether this is a coordinated animal uprising, a supernatural command, or an ecological hallucination.

**Gameplay role:**

- Defensive forest power.
- High organization in jungle/forest, low attack outside suitable terrain.
- Cannot use normal diplomacy except through Africa unifier or Green Covenant envoys.
- Refuses offensive wars outside forest unless Evolution IV unlocks world-root path.
- Requires actual nonhuman classification and exclusions from ordinary migration, ideology, and human panic systems.

**Focus/route families:**

- **The First Knuckle Congress:** establishes nonhuman council, unlocks leader portrait as generated fictional gorilla council.
- **Break the Survey Chains:** sabotage colonial extraction, remove foreign resource decisions, raise Colonial Alarm.
- **Forest Drill Without Words:** special forest infantry template, high defense, poor artillery/tank integration.
- **The Canopy Courts:** grants jungle supply and hidden movement, increases Omen Reliability.
- **Do Not Cross the Tree Line:** defensive ultimatum; enemies entering forest states face attrition/disruption events.
- **World-Root Ambassadors:** Evolution IV only; can join world-unification congress and treat forests on other continents as diplomatic nodes.

**Decisions:**

- **Guard the Basin:** place/maintain divisions or nonhuman guardian units in named forest states; success lowers exploitation pressure.
- **Dismantle the Poacher Rail:** targeted sabotage mission against colonial-controlled rail/resource state.
- **Return the Rifles:** converts captured equipment into legitimacy instead of stockpile gain.
- **The Tree Line Ultimatum:** warns a colonial power to leave a forest state; refusal triggers forest disaster/ambush events.

**Weaknesses:**

- Severe penalties in desert, arctic, open plains, and naval operations.
- Cannot be integrated through normal annexation. Must be bound through Covenant treaty or remain autonomous.
- Human Legitimacy penalty if the unifier gives Gorilla Nation authority over human historical authorities.

**Absurd route payoff:** **The Forest Votes No.** If a colonial power tries a new Scramble for Africa and enters protected forest, the Gorilla Nation can veto the offensive through ambush events, railway destruction, and a super-event-like regional news chain.

### Chimpanzee Assembly

**Type:** actual nonhuman country / high-chaos intelligence and tool-use actor.

**Home trigger:** Congo basin and central/western equatorial forests at Evolution III, especially if Ananse Web or Gorilla Nation exists.

**Gameplay role:**

- Sabotage, tool workshops, signal interception, stolen-radio networks.
- Lighter and more aggressive than Gorilla Nation.
- Can turn unstable if overarmed.

**Route families:**

- **The Tool Piles:** primitive-to-modern tool and equipment conversion decisions.
- **Stolen Radios in the Canopy:** intelligence network, enemy planning disruption, false orders.
- **Assembly Screams Become Signals:** weird communication mechanic; raises Omen Reliability if successful, lowers it if false alarms fire.
- **The Arsenal Problem:** player chooses whether to limit arms, train cadres, or unleash chaotic tool-war units.
- **No Human King Here:** refuses human-style monarchy or ideological conversion; must be treated as nonhuman assembly.

**Decisions:**

- **Raid the Depot, Leave the Crates Open:** steals support equipment but risks losing control of the Assembly.
- **Canopy Wiretaps:** reduces enemy entrenchment/planning in nearby forest fronts.
- **Signal Stone Workshops:** spends support equipment/army XP to create special irregular units.
- **The Screaming Vote:** emergency Covenant vote that can save a forest region or trigger panic among human members.

**Failure state:** if overarmed and neglected, becomes a rogue nonhuman warband that attacks both colonizers and careless League authorities.

### Bonobo Kinship Congress

**Type:** actual nonhuman diplomatic/peacekeeping actor.

**Home trigger:** central Congo region with high Wild Mandate but lower militarization than Gorilla/Chimpanzee routes.

**Gameplay role:**

- De-escalation, League Cohesion, prisoner exchanges, refugee recovery, and human legitimacy repair.
- Weak direct military, strong diplomacy.
- Can prevent a nonhuman-human war if introduced before Gorilla or Chimpanzee routes radicalize.

**Decisions:**

- **Kinship Envoys:** lowers tension between human subject and nonhuman actor.
- **Shelter the Displaced:** spends supplies/support equipment, lowers deaths, raises Human Legitimacy.
- **The Gentle Veto:** blocks one extreme Covenant decision at the cost of Wild Mandate.

**Absurd payoff:** can force a peace conference between Gorilla Nation, Chimpanzee Assembly, Baobab Senate, and human Charter League members. If successful, unlocks the **Forest Parliament** formable/nonhuman federation.

### Great Herd Compact

**Type:** nonhuman or semi-symbolic elephant logistics actor depending route.

**Baseline/Evolution II:** elephant units and logistics are not a country. They are special units, mascots, or heavy-transport formations raised through high-cost decisions.

**Evolution III:** if Wild Mandate is high, the Great Herd becomes a semi-autonomous nonhuman actor.

**Gameplay role:**

- Supply, breakthrough, heavy transport, panic shock, river crossing support.
- Excellent in forest/savanna, bad in harsh desert/mountain/snow.
- Costs massive supply/food/manpower-equivalent support and risks local damage if overused.

**Route families:**

- **Memory Roads:** old paths become logistics corridors; improves supply and rail repair.
- **Ivory Cannot Be Currency:** anti-poaching, anti-colonial resource route; using ivory as money is forbidden and should lower legitimacy if exploited.
- **Thunder Feet:** special shock units and enemy morale events.
- **The Herd Chooses Its War:** Great Herd refuses unjust offensive wars unless the target is colonial/extractive or Covenant route is extreme.

**Decisions:**

- **Open the Memory Road:** spend trucks/trains/support equipment to create supply in a target region.
- **Raise the Elephant Columns:** one-time or limited special units with supply burden.
- **Break the Colonial Fence:** target fort/infrastructure sabotage against colonial state.
- **The Herd Refuses:** event if player tries to use them in low-legitimacy conquest.

### Crocodile Rivers

**Type:** supernatural/nonhuman river actor.

**Home trigger:** Nile, Congo, Niger, Zambezi, Okavango, or other major river/delta regions at Evolution III.

**Gameplay role:**

- River crossings, ambushes, convoy/boat disruption, port intimidation.
- Cannot project far inland without river states.

**Route families:**

- **Eyes Above the Water:** river scouting and enemy movement warnings.
- **The Low Ferry Vanishes:** sabotage river crossings and enemy supply.
- **Treaty of Teeth:** bargain with human authorities; grants defense but lowers normal legitimacy if overused.
- **Delta Court:** coastal/river hybrid path with Mami Wata.

**Decisions:**

- **Close the Ferry:** temporarily blocks enemy movement/supply in river-adjacent state.
- **Guard the Crossing:** defensive modifier if the player holds both river banks.
- **The River Takes Evidence:** grim cover-up option; should be dangerous and morally dark, not a joke.

### Hyena Radio Dominion

**Type:** absurd propaganda/intelligence actor, possibly nonhuman or supernatural.

**Home trigger:** high chaos, radio tech or propaganda branch, savanna/desert edges.

**Gameplay role:**

- Enemy morale damage, false broadcasts, resistance manipulation, creepy/funny event options.
- Should be tonally absurd but not a stand-in for human groups.

**Route families:**

- **Laughing Static:** radio disruption and morale events.
- **Carrion News Service:** fake casualty reports; can backfire and lower credibility.
- **The Night Announcers:** night attack and intelligence bonuses.

**Failure state:** fake news spirals out of control, making human subjects distrust official communications.

### Termite Citadel Engineers

**Type:** nonhuman infrastructure/fortification actor.

**Home trigger:** Evolution III/IV, high Covenant Pressure, savanna/forest or arid regions.

**Gameplay role:**

- Fort construction, underground works, supply hubs, hidden depots.
- Very strong defensive/logistics support but poor mobility.

**Route families:**

- **Mounds Like Cities:** rapid low-cost forts/infrastructure with high civilian disruption.
- **Underground Charter Offices:** resistance reduction but human administrators become unnerved.
- **The Great Chewing:** sabotage enemy forts/rails, but can damage friendly industry if uncontrolled.
- **Citadel Treaty:** binds termites into human planning and prevents runaway damage.

**Decisions:**

- **Raise a Citadel Mound:** build fort/supply bonus in a state with terrain restrictions.
- **Chew the Rail Spurs:** sabotage enemy railway; risk spread.
- **Seal the Depot Underground:** protect supply hub from bombing at cost of construction speed.

### Baobab Senate

## Disaster and prophecy mechanics

High-chaos nature/spirit actors can predict or cause disasters, but the system must be interactive and risky.

### Disaster warning loop

1. Actor issues a warning: flood, drought, lightning, locust, river crossing collapse, port storm, forest ambush, rail subsidence.
2. Target country receives a response decision or event.
3. If target complies, it pays cost, loses tempo, but avoids worst damage.
4. If target ignores it and Omen Reliability is high, disaster event fires.
5. If target ignores it and Omen Reliability is low, warning may fail and Africa loses credibility.
6. If Africa overuses warnings, Human Legitimacy and Omen Reliability drop.

Warnings should be strongest against colonial/extractive powers holding African states. Using them casually against African League members should create severe legitimacy loss and possible revolt.

### Disaster families

| Disaster | Linked actor | Effects | Counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Flooded Depot Belt** | Oshun/Mami Wata/Barotse/Crocodile | Infrastructure/rail/supply damage, river crossing disruption | evacuate depot, spend trains/equipment, accept African demand |
| **Lightning Court** | Shango/Orisha | temporary airbase/AA/factory damage, command disruption | make concession, ground aircraft, spend command/engineers |
| **Forest Refusal** | Gorilla/Chimpanzee/Baobab | attrition, rail sabotage, ambush events in forest states | withdraw, negotiate sanctuary, anti-poaching concession |
| **Locust Customs** | Locust Customhouse | supply/food/factory penalties, famine pressure | grain reserves mission, local relief, pay customhouse, risk legitimacy |
| **Termite Subsidence** | Termite Citadels | fort/rail sabotage or friendly underground depot | engineers, construction burden, treaty with termites |
| **Omen Panic** | Bird/Ananse/Giraffe/Okapi | reveals hidden target or causes false alarm | build Omen Reliability with successful warnings |

## Absurd subject focus branch additions

The main Africa focus tree should gain a high-chaos **Green Covenant / Impossible Congress** branch. It should not appear in ordinary play unless route flags/evolutions permit.

Branch architecture:

- **The Trees File a Petition** — reveal Covenant Pressure and Wild Mandate.
- **The First Nonhuman Envoys** — allows one nonhuman actor to appear as observer.
- **No Seats for Caricature** — safeguards human legitimacy; clarifies that nonhuman actors are separate legal categories.
- **The Forest Parliament** — creates a joint Gorilla/Chimp/Bonobo/Great Herd council if conditions met.
- **Treaty of Teeth and Roots** — binds Crocodile, Termite, Baobab actors into defensive network.
- **The Court of Thunder and Tides** — unlocks Orisha/Mami Wata disaster-warning route.
- **The Spider at the Signature Table** — unlocks Ananse trick diplomacy.
- **The World Root Mandate** — Evolution IV; connects to continent-unifier sponsorship and world-end path.

Political route interactions:

- Federal Congress can legalize nonhuman observers but limits their war powers.
- People’s Liberation can treat them as revolutionary allies, raising normal legitimacy among radicals but scaring conservatives.
- General Staff can weaponize them, creating the strongest military effects and the worst revolt risk.
- Crown Congress struggles: some old courts see spirits as proof of sacred legitimacy; others reject nonhuman participation.
- Green Covenant embraces them fully but risks losing human politics.

## High-chaos achievements added

- **The Forest Votes No:** As Africa or a Charter subject, defeat a colonial Scramble offensive after Gorilla Nation has issued the Tree Line Ultimatum, without losing any protected forest capital.
- **Who Gave Them a Microphone?:** Use Hyena Radio Dominion to misdirect three different colonial offensives, then survive the fake-news backlash without losing League Cohesion below the danger threshold.
- **The Gentle Veto:** Use Bonobo Kinship Congress to prevent a war between a human historical authority and a nonhuman Covenant actor.
- **The Bird Was Right:** Have the Bird of the Walls correctly predict a major colonial offensive or naval invasion and defeat it within the warning window.
- **No Ivory Treasury:** Complete the Great Herd route without using any exploitative ivory/resource decision and defeat a colonial power holding African forest states.
- **A Treaty With Teeth:** Bind Crocodile Rivers and Mami Wata Tidemark into the same coastal/river defense network and force a colonial power to abandon a port through warning decisions.
- **The World Has Roots:** Complete the World Root Mandate after Africa is unified and at least two other continent unifiers have completed post-unification paths.

## Cleanup and classification requirements

- All nonhuman actors must be included in shared special/actual-nonhuman country classification if implemented as tags.
- They should be excluded from ordinary human migration, ideology drift, panic, country-release, and cultural assimilation systems where inappropriate.
- If a nonhuman actor is removed, its disaster warnings, decisions, event targets, and scripted-GUI cards must clean up.
- Human historical authorities should never use nonhuman classification unless they have truly transformed into a supernatural actor through a clearly locked high-chaos route.
- Nonhuman countries should have bespoke AI. They should not use generic country AI that tries normal diplomacy, ideology conversion, or naval invasions.


## High-chaos ruler/court display-name flavour

High-chaos actors can be ridiculous. Their public ruler, court, or council display names can draw from the source-language joke pool in `specs/012_africa_country_packages_and_subjects.md`, while their country names, institutional names, and nonhuman/supernatural classifications remain clear. The names stay untranslated and never appear as raw internal ids.

This is flavour around farcical courts, puppet diplomas, translation bureaus, and foreign-legation confusion. It should not become the centre of the route or replace the actual Bestiary, Green Covenant, Archive, or World Is One mechanics.
