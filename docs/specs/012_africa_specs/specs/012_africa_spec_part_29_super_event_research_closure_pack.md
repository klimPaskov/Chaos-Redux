The earlier package blocked final titles, quotes, cultural remarks, and audio until research. This final pass does not paste final in-game localisation into the spec. The implementation agent may select one only after the super-event workflow writes the normal research note, confirms copyright and license status, wires audio, and updates docs.

## Super-event candidate packages

### Continental reveal super-event

Working key: `se_012_africa_continental_reveal`

Role: reveal or major escalation when the unifier has formed a credible continent project, secured a major share of Africa, and the League is no longer a local faction.

Trigger direction:

- Africa unifier exists and is stable enough to be recognised as a continental actor.
- At least one major regional block is integrated, federated, or protected.
- League cohesion or command authority is above the reveal threshold.
- The event has reached Evolution II or a stronger opening if pre-fire evolved.

Title direction:

- Research direction should use a short Pan-African or continental unity frame.
- The strongest researched phrase is Garvey-linked but can be politically route-sensitive.
- Avoid generic apocalypse words and avoid making the title sound like a modern institution.

Quote candidates:

| Candidate | Source | Fit | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Africa for the Africans` | Marcus Garvey slogan in sourced Garvey material | direct, iconic, concise | politically loaded and route-sensitive |
| `To promote the unity and solidarity of the African States` | OAU Charter Article II | official continental unity language | formal and less dramatic |
| `the dream a possibility within another generation` | Marcus Garvey, Philosophy and Opinions | diaspora and long struggle | use only as a short excerpt with source note |

Button or cultural remark direction:

- Serious route: short affirmation of continental unity.
- Revolutionary route: anti-colonial defiance.
- Federal route: sober recognition of a new continental order.
- Command route: military acknowledgement.
- Black Star route: shipping or return motif.
- No modern meme or cheap joke.

Image direction:

- Generated period documentary super-event image.
- Strong central composition with a continental congress, port, rail, flags, and soldiers or delegates.
- Do not make the main image a map table.

Audio direction:

- `Nkosi Sikelel iAfrika` White House 1994 recording is a viable researched candidate if the implementation accepts the South African association and documents the public domain status.
- Use only with care for non-RSA route if the route wants broad Pan-African tone.
- If used, trim only after preserving source and documenting edit.

### Black Star return super-event

Working key: `se_012_africa_black_star_return`

Role: rare route super-event for a major diaspora return and settlement success, not for the first returnee decision.

Trigger direction:

- Black Star route selected.
- At least three lanes completed or one Atlantic lane and one Indian Ocean lane operating.
- Settlement backlash under control.
- At least one industrial cadre project completed.
- No coercive settlement or land seizure failure if the achievement path is active.

Quote candidates:

| Candidate | Source | Fit | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Africa for the Africans` | Garvey source tradition | concise and thematically direct | overlaps with main reveal |
| `those at home and those abroad` | Garvey quote tradition, verify from source before final | fits diaspora route | exact wording needs source workflow confirmation |
| OAU Charter unity phrase | OAU Charter | safer official tone | less specific to diaspora |

Image direction:

- Period documentary port scene with ship arrival, workshops, dock cranes, returnee families, local reception committees, and no readable generated text.
- The image should show people and work, not a propaganda poster.

Audio direction:

- Search for a 1 to 2 minute public domain or CC-licensed hymn, choral piece, march, or maritime song with African or diaspora fit.
- The Djembe CC0 file is too short as final super-event music, but it can become a short transition sound if the audio workflow approves it.

### Scramble reaction super-event

Working key: `se_012_africa_scramble_reaction`

Role: outside powers react after Africa is effectively unified or old colonial claims are made impossible.

Trigger direction:

- Unifier controls, federates, or protects every required African region.
- Colonial holdings are gone, converted, or under final siege.
- Outside powers have enough interest, colonies lost, or guarantees broken to react.
- The moment should fire only when global politics notice the continent-scale change.

Title direction:

- Research direction should evoke a reversal of the old scramble without declaring a simple repeat.
- Avoid a final title until the super-event workflow selects a sourced remark or short title.
- The title can be ironic, legalistic, or stark.

Quote candidates:

| Candidate | Source | Fit | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `The trade of all nations shall enjoy complete freedom` | General Act of Berlin Article 1 | bitter legal irony | must frame as colonial treaty language, not endorsement |
| `to eradicate all forms of colonialism from Africa` | OAU Charter Article II | direct anti-colonial answer | formal treaty tone |
| `Africa for the Africans` | Garvey | direct answer to old scramble | may duplicate another super-event |

Image direction:

- Generated or sourced depending on final role.
- Strong option: period diplomatic hall with torn colonial maps as props, but the people and tension must be the subject.
- Do not use a literal map-only image.

Audio direction:

- Use separate track from continental reveal.
- Suitable mood: tense orchestral, choral, or military music with public domain or clear license.
- Do not reuse `Nkosi Sikelel iAfrika` unless user explicitly approves reuse.

### Deep Green high-chaos super-event

Working key: `se_012_africa_deep_green_reveal`

Role: hidden high-chaos reveal when nonhuman or supernatural nature actors become visible and disaster pressure changes diplomacy.

Trigger direction:

- Deep Green route selected.
- High chaos gate open.
- Disaster pressure or nonhuman actor visibility crosses threshold.
- Normal human Sacred Soil route has already split away.

Quote direction:

- Use public domain religious, mythic, or literary quote about earth, rain, forest, plague, or judgment.
- Do not use a quote from a living poet, modern song, or unsourced internet proverb.
- Do not use any quote that frames a human African ethnic group as animal or monstrous.

Image direction:

- Generated high-chaos scene with impossible forest weather, nonhuman silhouettes, river or coastal supernatural pressure, and terrified armies or colonisers.
- Nonhuman actors must be visibly nonhuman or supernatural.
- No caricatured human tribal imagery.

Audio direction:

- Search for public domain choral, ritual, or orchestral track.
- Natural sound effects alone are not enough for final super-event music.

### The World is One terminal super-event

Working key: `se_012_africa_world_is_one`

Role: world-end gate when continent-scale powers have fought and the last standing continent identity becomes The World.

Trigger direction:

- World chaos value over terminal threshold.
- World-end flag not yet set.
- Africa has fired its continent unity milestone.
- Other continent unifiers exist and have pursued their own world-end paths.
- Continental wars have resolved or entered final selection.
- Last standing continent-scale power becomes the terminal world identity.

Quote candidates:

| Candidate | Source | Fit | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `The world is everything that is the case` | Wittgenstein, Tractatus, Project Gutenberg | cold terminal finality | philosophical and not African-specific |
| `The world is the totality of facts, not of things` | Wittgenstein, Tractatus | fits terminal abstraction | may feel too cerebral |
| OAU and AU unity language | OAU Charter or Agenda 2063 | ties back to Africa | too bureaucratic for terminal state |

Image direction:

- Generated world-end image with continent emblems, a world seal, broken treaties, and armies or flags subordinate to one identity.
- It should feel campaign-ending, not like a normal victory parade.

Audio direction:

- Unique final track required.
- Mood should be final and severe.
- Do not use a default track or a reused reveal track.

## Final super-event acceptance

A super-event remains incomplete until the final implementation has:

- selected one title, one description, one button remark, one quote, one image, and one unique audio track
- documented quote source and confidence
- documented button source if it is a cultural reference
- downloaded audio from a legitimate source
- converted audio to game-ready format
- wired image, text, audio id, visibility flag, and settings-aware playback
- updated docs and music track table
- verified no placeholder audio, no generated test tones, and no reused track without approval
