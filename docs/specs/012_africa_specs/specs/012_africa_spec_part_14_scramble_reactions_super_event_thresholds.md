# 012 Africa spec part 14, Scramble reaction and super-event thresholds

This file expands the outside-power reaction tree and super-event thresholds. It does not set final super-event title, quote, button, cultural remark, or audio. Those remain research-gated.

## Scramble reaction principle

The Scramble reaction is not a repeat of nineteenth-century partition. It is a panic response to a unified or near-unified Africa. Outside powers should react through diplomacy, sanctions, naval pressure, colonial evacuation, expedition planning, sabotage, rival African support, and recognition fights.

The reaction should become super-event eligible only when the campaign has clearly crossed a global threshold.

## Trigger thresholds

| Threshold | Condition direction | Gameplay result | Super-event gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Continental alarm | Unifier controls or leads a large share of African regions | Outside powers begin warnings, sanctions, and colonial emergency decisions | No super-event by default |
| Anti-colonial cascade | Multiple colonial owners lose African territory or accept evacuation | Foreign reaction trees intensify | Possible news or report, no final super-event unless global |
| Africa is one | All African regions are controlled, federated, or securely joined through recognized member route | Major milestone, unlocks post-unification content | Super-event candidate, research required |
| Scramble response | Outside powers form a significant anti-Africa reaction after Africa is one | Ultimatums, sanctions, expeditionary planning, rival bloc support | Super-event candidate, research required |
| Continental war | Outside reaction escalates to large war against unified Africa | War economy and defense branches open | Can be news or super-event if global enough |
| World is one gate | Africa is unified, chaos is extreme, and other continent unifiers exist | Terminal world-end route becomes eligible | World-end super-event, research required |

Africa is one should require more than raw state control. It should count:
- owned and controlled African states
- federal members that accepted permanent accession or durable member status
- associated states that are stable and not in rival bloc
- restored polities that are bound through accepted route
- island regions only after convoy and port settlement
- no active colonial owner controlling a required African region
- no active rival African bloc holding major capitals

## Outside-power reaction model

Outside powers receive reaction intensity based on interests.

| Interest source | Reaction increase |
| --- | --- |
| Controls African colony | Very high |
| Has African cores or claims through vanilla or event logic | High |
| Has faction member threatened by unifier | Medium to high |
| Lost colony to Africa | Very high |
| Lost prestige war in Africa | High |
| Major naval power with African port access | Medium |
| Ideological enemy of unifier | Medium |
| Trade or resource dependence | Medium |
| Already fighting world war | Lower diplomacy capacity but higher emergency paranoia |
| Friendly to anti-colonial ideology | Lower hostile reaction, higher recognition option |

Reaction intensity bands:
- watch
- pressure
- sanctions
- containment
- expedition planning
- open war

## Reaction trees by unifier route

| Unifier route | Outside perception | Common reaction |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Crisis events and decisions

Outside reactions should produce concrete gameplay:
- sanctions that increase convoy and trade costs
- naval blockade missions
- ultimatum decisions
- colonial evacuation offers
- expeditionary plans
- rival bloc sponsorship
- arms shipments to African rivals
- intelligence exposure
- recognition conferences
- emergency trade deals
- League cohesion tests
- diaspora lane disruptions

These should not all be war goals. Diplomacy, logistics, sanctions, and proxy support should carry much of the reaction.

## Super-event research gates

### Africa is one milestone

Role:
- major reveal or confirmation of continental unification
- should feel like a world-chaos moment
- should not use final title, quote, button, cultural remark, or audio until researched

Trigger direction:
- all African regions are integrated, federated, or permanently secured
- no colonial owner controls required African states
- major rival African bloc is reconciled, defeated, or reduced below threshold
- no world-end flag already set

Presentation direction:
- image direction should show a continent-scale public moment through people, banners, ports, rail, or restored capitals, not a flat map
- quote direction should research Pan-Africanism, unity, liberation, self-determination, or continental destiny
- audio mood should be researched through licensed or public domain music
- button tone should fit the chosen unifier route

### Scramble response milestone

Role:
- outside powers panic, bargain, threaten, or coordinate against unified Africa
- should be considered a super-event only if several great powers join the crisis or a major war threat forms

Trigger direction:
- Africa is one has fired
- at least two major outside powers reach high reaction intensity
- or one major outside power forms a coalition with several minors
- or outside ultimatum threatens war over African unity

Presentation direction:
- image should focus on colonial panic, ports, ships, diplomats, crowds, or mobilized armies
- quote direction should research anti-colonial reversal, empire, panic, or sovereignty
- no final wording before research

### The World is One terminal path

Role:
- world-end scenario
- campaign-ending continental war and final unifier outcome

Trigger direction:
- chaos above world-end threshold
- Africa fully unified
- Africa is one fired
- other continent unifiers exist and have pursued post-unification world-end routes
- terminal branch not already active
- continent-scale wars begin or final union sequence starts

Presentation direction:
- image should show terminal world-order transformation, not ordinary conquest
- quote direction should research finality, unity, world order, destiny, or collapse
- audio must be unique, licensed, and researched
- final title, quote, button, and audio remain blockers until the super-event workflow completes

## Outside power AI

Outside AI should not always declare war. It should choose based on:
- colony ownership
- faction commitments
- naval reach
- ideology
- current wars
- home stability
- Africa route
- Africa military strength
- League cohesion
- own losses in Africa
- chance to sponsor a rival bloc
- fear of world-end branch
- player involvement

AI should prefer pressure before war if it lacks reach. AI should escalate faster if it still controls threatened colonies or if the unifier is Continental Command or Revolutionary Congress.

## De-escalation

The reaction tree needs off-ramps:
- recognition conference
- colonial evacuation treaty
- member autonomy guarantee
- port access treaty
- diaspora lane safety agreement
- anti-piracy or Red Sea security pact
- African pledge not to attack non-African homelands unless attacked
- prisoner and civilian evacuation
- postwar colonial compensation, if route allows

De-escalation should reduce outside reaction but may anger hardline routes.
