# 012 Africa spec part 6, focus route expansion

This file expands the focus tree at route-group level. It is not final focus localisation and does not lock exact coordinates.

## Opening structure

The opening should be wide enough to show that the country has more than one way to claim Africa.

Opening groups:

| Group | Purpose | Unlocks | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Continental proclamation | gives the player the public claim and starts the event identity | Africa cosmetic identity, first legitimacy value, event details | should mention selected country dynamically in final text |
| Secure the capital | stabilizes the selected country after the proclamation | first guards, temporary stability, League category setup | RSA civil war uses a different opening sequence |
| Survey the continent | maps African state groups and possible members | region selectors and first target list | should not expose all secret high-chaos branches |
| First congress | chooses early charter tone | Federal, Revolutionary, Crown, Command, Diaspora, Sacred route openers | creates first route lock pressure |
| Emergency rail and port audit | reveals logistics weakness | rail and port project decisions | industry branch starts here |
| League envoys | first invitations to nearby states | invitation and protection decisions | target AI can refuse |

The opening should include a mixed starting idea representing overextended continental ambition. The idea should be mitigated by early logistics, League, army, and legitimacy focuses rather than removed by one click.

## Federal Charter route

Playable promise: build a voluntary Africa that survives because members trust it.

Focus groups:

- draft the federal charter
- create regional chambers
- guarantee member autonomy
- standardize member defense rights
- create federation courts or arbitration councils
- protect minority and local autonomy claims
- establish shared rail and customs rules
- hold the first continental vote
- create federation member status
- unlock peaceful integration missions

Rewards should increase cohesion, legitimacy, target trust, and peaceful integration chance. They should slow direct annexation and make coercive actions more expensive. This route should have the best chance to survive rival blocs without war.

Failure states:

- low cohesion causes members to demand emergency reforms
- failed federation votes create temporary autonomy protections
- colonial powers can exploit slow decision-making

## Revolutionary Congress route

Playable promise: use continental unity as an anti-colonial revolutionary engine.

Focus groups:

- form worker and farmer congresses
- create liberation schools
- send revolutionary instructors
- arm anti-colonial cells
- connect ports, mines, and rail workers
- declare anti-puppet principles
- build a people's army route
- create revolutionary member councils
- pressure conservative monarchs or chiefs
- post-liberation land and labor settlement

Rewards should unlock stronger anti-colonial decisions, volunteers, political warfare, and socialist advisors. This route should be good at breaking colonial powers but risky with monarchist and conservative African states.

Failure states:

- revolutionary pressure can split religious, monarchist, or conservative members
- outside powers sponsor anti-communist blocs
- coercive revolutionary integration can create resistance

## Crown of the Continent route

Playable promise: unity through crowns, old courts, restored legitimacy, and theatrical continental kingship.

Focus groups:

- summon old courts
- restore regional crowns
- define a royal federation
- create regnal name pools and court flavor
- recognize sacred stools, drums, palaces, and dynastic symbols where appropriate
- form royal guard and court escort units
- negotiate with old polities as privileged members
- hold a continental coronation or oath ceremony after enough legitimacy
- create royal arbitration over rival claims
- unlock ancient high-chaos host gate if later evolutions allow it

Rewards should improve old-polity restoration, local legitimacy, royal guard units, and peaceful integration of monarchist subjects. It should anger republican and revolutionary routes unless compromises are taken.

Failure states:

- restored polities can become too autonomous
- republican members can leave
- succession or regnal disputes can trigger crisis events

## Continental Command route

Playable promise: unity by command, rail control, and military pressure.

Focus groups:

- establish continental command staff
- create rail guard districts
- centralize arms procurement
- train command integration cadres
- impose emergency laws
- build border war plans
- create League strategic reserves
- force protectorate treaties
- prepare continental campaigns
- create command-integrated subject armies

Rewards should improve war readiness, unit spawning, command power efficiency, faster integration, and war goal preparation. The route must damage trust if overused.

Failure states:

- members leave or form rival blocs
- local resistance rises after forced integration
- colonial powers use coercion as propaganda

## Sacred Soil route

Playable promise: unity through land, shrine, prophecy, and spiritual geography.

Focus groups:

- identify sacred capitals and old burial sites
- protect pilgrimage roads
- form shrine and scholar networks
- create spiritual legitimacy missions
- arbitrate old claims through ritual or religious settlement
- open oracle route gates at higher chaos
- create disaster prophecy decisions at high chaos
- negotiate with nature covenant actors when Evolution IV is active

The baseline can remain human and religious. High-chaos content unlocks only after conditions. Do not make ordinary religious route text reveal supernatural mechanics too early.

Failure states:

- rival religious claims split members
- failed pilgrim protection lowers legitimacy
- high-chaos bargains can frighten ordinary League members

## Black Star Return route

Playable promise: diaspora return, shipping, industry, medicine, and propaganda reshape Africa.

Focus groups:

- open diaspora liaison offices
- charter return shipping
- secure ports and housing
- invite engineers, doctors, teachers, organizers, and veterans
- create returnee settlement missions
- build Black Star style commerce routes
- connect Caribbean and Afro-American networks
- create anti-colonial radio and press routes
- turn returnee success into legitimacy
- integrate returnee communities into army and industry

Rewards should be gradual. Returnees should build capacity over time. Ports, convoys, housing, stability, and local acceptance should matter.

Failure states:

- overcrowded ports create unrest
- foreign intelligence infiltration scares members
- colonial powers attack shipping or spread propaganda

## Deep Green Covenant route

Playable promise: high-chaos Africa bargains with forests, rivers, droughts, storms, animals, and impossible councils.

Unlocks only through high chaos and Sacred Soil or related route gates. This route can appear as an evolution extension rather than a normal baseline route.

Focus groups:

- listen to the forests
- mark forbidden river crossings
- bargain with drought and flood prophets
- call animal actors as nonhuman allies
- awaken living statues at old sites
- create oracle councils that predict disaster pressure
- punish colonisers through abstract disaster mechanics
- accept blowback if the League fears the covenant

Rewards can be powerful and strange. They should carry diplomatic, stability, cohesion, and blowback risks.

## Branch convergence

The tree should have convergence points after major route identity is set.

Convergence groups:

- continental rail and port integration
- anti-colonial war council
- regional integration assemblies
- postwar settlement conference
- resource sovereignty program
- Africa is one milestone
- Scramble for Africa response
- continent-unifier diplomacy

Convergence should not erase route identity. A federal Africa solves member disputes differently from a command Africa or a nature-covenant Africa.
