# 012 Africa spec part 22, high-chaos bio, disaster, and nonhuman safety atlas

This file expands high-chaos mechanics while preserving the boundary that baseline Africa remains grounded and researched. Fictional disease mechanics are abstract game mechanics only. The package must not describe real pathogen engineering, real disease weaponization, or operational biological harm.

## Disaster pressure loop

Disaster pressure should be a visible high-chaos value. It rises from ignored demands, war escalation, special focus nodes, failed oracle predictions, and high chaos. It falls through relief missions, tribute or concession missions, weather preparation, regional infrastructure, and successful negotiations. At thresholds it can fire abstract state effects, damage supply, lower stability, hurt industry, or disrupt enemies. Every strong use must risk blowback.

## AI and safety restrictions

- Ordinary African countries and baseline unifier AI should not choose high-chaos routes by accident.
- The player can reach high-chaos routes only through explicit gates, chaos tier, route commitments, and visible warnings through mechanics rather than final warning prose.
- Nonhuman and supernatural actors must be registered as nonhuman or special chaos actors in implementation.
- Fictional diseases must use invented names or abstract labels after source review. They must never use real pathogen instructions or real-world harm procedures.
- High-chaos power can be absurd and overpowered when earned, but it needs route cost, blowback, AI limits, and cleanup.
