## Research gate rules

- The text researcher must compare real quote candidates and document attribution confidence before final `.q` wording exists.
- The cultural remark or button researcher must keep modern copyrighted references short, sourced, and tone-fit.
- The audio researcher must find a real licensed or public domain musical track, not generated tones, drones, or placeholder audio.
- The image asset worker must choose generated or sourced mode based on whether the scene is fictional, symbolic, archival, or real.
- The implementation agent must not paste working labels into localisation.
