# 012 Africa spec part 3, focus tree and country packages

## Main focus tree architecture

The Africa unifier needs a large non-linear tree. The tree should change according to ideology, route choices, evolutions, League state, and how much of Africa is controlled or integrated. It should not be a straight conquest ladder.

### Opening lane, continental claim

Purpose: establish the event identity, solve the immediate shock, and open the League.

Focus groups:

- proclamation and first congress
- capital security and continental command
- public claim to Africa
- first envoys and League charter
- emergency army and rail survey
- old-capital symbolic choices when the selected country has a major historical anchor

Opening rewards should create the mechanic, not hand out a full continent. It should add claims or visible ambitions, unlock decisions, add early national spirits, and start legitimacy values.

### Political route family

The political routes define what unity means.

| Working route label | Role | Typical effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Federal Charter | pluralist, legal, slower integration | high cohesion, federation votes, lower panic, slower cores |
| Revolutionary Congress | socialist or anti-imperial worker route | worker aid, liberation cells, stronger colonial war support |
| Crown of the Continent | monarchist or restored imperial route | old polities, kings, regnal flavor, royal federation, legitimacy swings |
| Continental Command | military government route | faster war prep, coercive integration, stronger army, lower trust |
| Sacred Soil | religious or spiritual unity route | pilgrimage, shrine networks, oracle events, high-chaos gates |
| Black Star Return | diaspora-led or diaspora-supported route | returnee convoys, Afro-American and Caribbean links, industry and manpower growth |
| Deep Green Covenant | high-chaos nature route | disaster bargaining, forest actors, nonhuman or supernatural gates |

The tree should not force all ideologies into identical text or rewards. Each route needs different advisors, party names, names, flags or symbols where appropriate, and AI behavior.

### League and diplomacy branch

This branch grows the Charter League. It should unlock member votes, defensive guarantees, shared reserves, anti-puppet clauses, member aid, expulsion logic, and regional councils. Later focuses can split between federation, protectorate network, or command hierarchy.

### Liberation and expansion branch

This branch handles wars against colonial powers and hostile rival blocs. It should unlock state claims, war preparation missions, intervention decisions, ultimatums, and postwar settlement. It should avoid instant wars on all native African countries.

Sub-branches:

- defend existing African countries
- break colonial ports and garrisons
- seize rail arteries
- shield the Sahel and Sahara
- contest the Nile and Red Sea
- Indian Ocean route and island security
- southern crisis route, including RSA civil war outcomes

### Integration branch

This branch opens staged coring and federation tools. It should require legitimacy, cohesion, state control, and completed regional projects. Some focuses should improve subject integration speed, but they should not bypass local work entirely.

### Industry and resources branch

Africa should gain resource expansion and industry through named regional projects. The branch should build railways, supply hubs, ports, mines, oil surveys, rubber routes, steel and chromium works, and local factories.

Project examples:

- Sahel rail and caravan motorization
- Congo Basin river depots
- Nile irrigation and textile route
- Guinea Coast ports
- Copperbelt and southern minerals
- Swahili and Indian Ocean docks
- Ethiopian highland roads
- Madagascar and island supply network

### Military branch

The unifier needs a credible army. Military branches should include local defense forces, League volunteers, colonial-defector units, rail guards, desert columns, mountain detachments, river flotillas, air patrols, and late high-chaos special units.

Route-specific options:

- League volunteer cadres for federal routes
- command integration forces for military routes
- revolutionary columns for socialist routes
- royal guard or old-polity levies for monarchist routes
- elephant logistics and shock formations at higher chaos
- supernatural or nonhuman formations only after high-chaos gates

### Diaspora route

The diaspora route should include Afro-American, Caribbean, and wider African diaspora return mechanics. It should be inspired by Pan-African and Back-to-Africa currents, without turning them into a simple manpower faucet.

Design pillars:

- returnee convoys need ports, convoys, housing, and public safety
- diaspora engineers and doctors can improve industry and health systems
- diaspora propaganda can raise legitimacy abroad and anger colonial powers
- Black Star style shipping can connect Africa, the Caribbean, and the Americas
- failures can create overcrowding, local resentment, spy scares, or convoy losses

### Old polities and regional subjects branch

The tree should let the unifier revive or recognize many African polities as regional subjects or League members. These countries provide local troops, local legitimacy, and integration paths. They are not only flavor tags.

The branch can unlock restored-state decisions by region. A federal route can keep them as federated members. A monarchy route can use crowned restorations. A military route can use strategic protectorates. A high-chaos route can awaken ancient hosts or impossible councils.

### Post-unification branch

Once Africa is fully unified or near-unified, new content opens.

Post-unification choices:

- settle member autonomy
- centralize or preserve regional polities
- nationalize old colonial resource concessions
- confront outside powers through Scramble for Africa reactions
- sponsor other continent unifiers
- create dynamic union names after continental unions
- prepare for the rare world-end route

## Country package principles

Every restored country should have a direct name, local role, likely territory, leader model, flag source mode, starting forces, and integration path. Not every polity needs a full massive focus tree. Important restored countries and rival blocs should have small or shared trees adapted by region.

Country names should remain readable. `Kongo`, `Asante`, `Oyo`, `Aksum`, `Nubia`, `Kilwa`, `Buganda`, `Merina`, `Mutapa`, and similar names work. Ideology-specific names can add Kingdom, Republic, Commune, Sultanate, Federation, or Empire where appropriate.

## Starting forces and reinforcement

Newly restored countries that fight need starting units and growth paths. They should not appear as empty flags.

Unit families:

- capital guards and congress guards for political centers
- rail guards for logistics corridors
- desert columns for Sahara and Sahel states
- river flotillas and port guards for Congo, Nile, and Swahili routes
- mountain detachments for Ethiopia, Rwanda, Burundi, Lesotho, and highland states
- local militia for newly liberated regions
- royal guard for monarchist restorations
- League volunteer cadre for federal routes
- ancient host or living statue units only through high-chaos routes

Scaling factors:

- population and industry in restored territory
- chaos tier and evolution stage
- local support and legitimacy
- whether the state was liberated or conquered
- foreign aid and League cohesion
- captured depots, ports, rail hubs, and supply state
- unifier ideology and focus route

## Country identity changes

The Africa unifier and regional states can change names, flags, leaders, party names, and portraits through routes. Real historical flags and symbols should be sourced where attested. Fictional flags and high-chaos variants can be generated. Real portraits need source images. Fictional leaders, councils, strange kings, animal leaders, oracle councils, and symbolic bodies can use generated portraits.

## Focus route asset needs

Each focus branch needs an icon family. Do not derive all icons from one master image. Focus icons, idea icons, and decision icons need separate target-size art. The asset prompt maps icon families rather than final per-focus filenames, since the next planning pass should expand the exact focus node count.
