## Super-event packages to research

### 1. Full continental unification

Role label: `africa_is_one_unification`

Trigger moment: the selected unifier has secured and integrated all of Africa through the staged system; “Africa is one” is publicly recognised.

Tone: awe, danger, anti-colonial triumph, uneasy world recognition. The public sees a continent-scale power rather than a border change.

Description direction: explain that the congress, wars, regional authorities, and integration missions have produced a single African political order. Avoid listing mechanical cores or modifiers.

Quote direction: Pan-African unity, anti-colonial liberation, self-determination, continent-scale destiny, or old political writing. Consider public-domain speeches/writings where usable, but verify exact wording and source.

Button/cultural remark direction: short, memorable, possibly ironic or solemn. Avoid generic “OK.” Do not use an unverified line.

Image direction: generated super-event image, 457x328. A central continental congress/banner/crowd with regional standards; not a flat map.

Audio mood: triumphant but tense; choral, ceremonial, or march-like; not generic apocalypse music.

### 2. Scramble for Africa reaction

Role label: `africa_scramble_reaction`

Trigger moment: unified or nearly unified Africa triggers outside colonial/imperial reaction, diplomatic crisis, sanctions, ultimatums, or war pressure.

Tone: dread, panic, hypocrisy, old empires discovering that their old maps no longer obey them.

Description direction: foreign chancelleries, naval offices, companies, and colonial ministries react; African diplomats read their threats aloud; League/Union prepares a response.

Quote direction: colonialism, imperial panic, anti-imperial reversal, or cautionary historical/political writing. Verify source.

Button/cultural remark direction: dry irony about “scramble” returning in reverse; research-gated.

Image direction: generated documentary/symbolic super-event image: foreign delegations, cables, ports, troops, African flags; avoid map-only visual.

Audio mood: tense, martial, diplomatic crisis; 1–2 minute licensed/public-domain track.

### 3. RSA continental victory and Allied peace

Role label: `rsa_continental_victory_peace`

Trigger moment: if RSA was selected while in the Allies, the Continental Proclamation side wins the civil war and the Allies are forced into peace with Africa.

Tone: civil rupture, humiliation for old alliance assumptions, fragile victory.

Description direction: mines, railways, ports, Allied envoys, continental delegates, war-worn South African cities. Explain why the Allied war stops without mechanical details.

Quote direction: civil war, divided loyalty, freedom, or political rupture. Avoid cheap joke. Verify.

Button/cultural remark direction: grim administrative irony or sober line; research-gated.

Image direction: sourced if using real leaders/Smuts; otherwise generated alternate-history documentary scene.

Audio mood: reflective military aftermath, not triumphalist.

### 4. Continental sponsor reveal

Role label: `africa_continent_sponsor`

Trigger moment: Evolution III/post-unification Africa begins sponsoring another continental unifier or directly creates a continent-unification movement outside Africa.

Tone: global escalation, ideological export, uneasy solidarity or domination depending route.

Description direction: Africa is no longer only defending itself; it is teaching other continents how to unify, arm, or revolt.

Quote direction: revolution export, solidarity, ambition, warnings against empire, or world-order writing. Verify.

Button/cultural remark direction: short, possibly ironic: Africa learned the rules and is teaching them. Research-gated.

Image direction: generated diplomatic/intelligence image: radio rooms, shipping crates, continental seals, envoys.

Audio mood: covert, rising, transcontinental.

### 5. Dynamic cross-continental union formation

Role label: `africa_cross_continental_union`

Trigger moment: Africa and another continent unifier have secured their respective continents and the dynamic union branch completes.

Dynamic naming guidance:

- Africa + Middle East: `African-Middle Eastern Union` or route-specific equivalent.
- Africa + Asia: `Afro-Asian Union`; if Middle East is also present, still usually `Afro-Asian Union` because Middle East is treated as part of Asia in the user direction.
- Africa + Europe + Asia: `Afro-Eurasian Union`.
- Other combinations need route-specific dynamic naming.

Tone: grand, unsettling, bureaucratically impossible becoming real.

Description direction: focus on the form of union, whether federation, protectorate, command, congress, covenant, or empire; not just annexation.

Quote direction: world unity, federation, empire, solidarity, political ambition, or warning against universal domination. Verify.

Button/cultural remark direction: avoid generic; research-gated.

Image direction: generated symbolic super-event image with continent standards and delegates, not a plain map.

Audio mood: enormous, ceremonial, slightly ominous.

### 6. Terminal World Is One path

Role label: `africa_world_is_one_terminal`

Trigger moment: Africa is fully unified, extreme chaos/world-end conditions are active, “Africa is one” has fired, all other continental unifiers exist and have completed post-unification/world-end unlock paths, and the Africa route pursues The World Is One ambition.

Tone: terminal, finality, not ordinary victory. The world recognises that the continent-unifier logic has become a campaign-ending force.

Description direction: all continents’ unifiers converge or are swallowed into a final world-order proclamation. Avoid revealing it early in earlier super-events.

Quote direction: final judgment, world unity, universal empire/federation, apocalypse of borders, or philosophical warning. Verify; public-domain sources preferred.

Button/cultural remark direction: short and final. Research-gated.

Image direction: generated high-chaos symbolic super-event image. Continent banners, world congress, impossible horizon. No readable text.

Audio mood: final, grave, world-end scale. Unique final track; no placeholder.

## Quote research suggestions

The researcher should compare multiple candidates from:

- Pan-African political writing and speeches where source/copyright can be verified;
- OAU/AU founding language only if usage is safe and exact wording is sourced;
- public-domain anti-colonial or political writing;
- religious/philosophical texts for terminal branch;
- historical speeches about unity, freedom, imperialism, world federation, or self-rule.

Do not use unsourced quote-site material. Do not invent quotes. Mark uncertain attributions.

## Audio research constraints

Before outside search, inspect existing approved Chaos Redux super-event music docs and track list for reusable documented tracks, but every completed Africa super-event should have a unique final track unless exact reuse is explicitly approved.

Reject unclear licensing, YouTube-only uploads without terms, modern commercial tracks, generated tone/noise music, drones, or texture beds. Music should usually be 1–2 minutes or be trimmed with the original preserved.
