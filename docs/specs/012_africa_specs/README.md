## Country naming style

Country and cosmetic names should be direct names, not administrative labels. Use names such as `Asante`, `Kongo`, `Aksum`, `Nubia`, `Buganda`, `Merina Kingdom`, `Kilwa Sultanate`, or `Funj Sultanate of Sennar`. Do not name countries after generic offices, compacts, boards, bureaus, military missions, protectorate councils, registries, or similar attachments. Those labels can still describe decision categories, mechanics, advisors, missions, or internal institutions.

Ideology can change the country name when it adds flavour: a monarchist route may use `Kingdom` or `Sultanate`, a republican route may use `Republic`, a socialist route may use `Commune`, `Union`, or another direct state form, and a military route may use a direct martial state name without turning the country into “Military Office.”

## Leader display-name flavour

Event-created or Event-recast ruler/council display names can use the untranslated source-language joke pool:

- `qaama saalaa koo xuuxaa`
- `haadha kee waliin wal qunnamtii saalaa raawwadhe`

Use neutral script ids, filenames, tags, variables, sprites, and achievement ids. Do not translate or gloss the meanings in player-facing English. Serious country, office, polity, institutional, and historical asset names remain researched; nonhuman/supernatural actors remain explicitly nonhuman or supernatural.

## Important constraints

- Final super-event titles, quotes, cultural remarks, and audio remain research-gated.
- No instant annexation or instant full-core fallback should be used for the main event.
- RSA in the Allies must take the civil-war path.
- Human historical polities must not be animalised or turned into caricatures. Nonhuman actors are separate fictional/high-chaos entities.
