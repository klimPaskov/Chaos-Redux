# Event 12 Africa planning package, expanded second edition

## Purpose

This is the source planning package for the Chaos Redux Event 12 Africa rework.

It designs a protection-first continent-unification campaign built around the Charter League, regional congresses, member agency, staged integration, restored polities, infrastructure, resource sovereignty, diaspora return, military defence, rival blocs, high-chaos actors, the Scramble response, continent-scale world order, and the rare terminal identity The World.

The second edition deepens the parts that most affected replay value. It adds host-country openings, constitutional route dilemmas, and a selected group of fully playable member and restoration packages. It does not add a second continental mechanic, duplicate decision stores, or a full bespoke tree for every eligible African start.

This package contains planning and handoff material. It does not contain gameplay implementation or finished assets.

## What the second edition changes

### Host countries

The original host is now a campaign-defining input rather than a flag and regional modifier. Twenty-two strategically distinct starts receive full playbooks with an opening dilemma, first proof mission, economic leverage, military constraints, regional coalition, restoration pressure, rival-bloc risk, and route mutations. Twenty-nine smaller or less distinct starts receive compact signatures that reuse the same framework without pretending to be full bespoke trees.

### Constitutional routes

The six grounded constitutions and the hidden Covenant route now define representation, executive power, resource settlement, command structure, withdrawal rights, crisis failures, recovery paths, and post-unification government. Shared economy, military, diplomacy, diaspora, restoration, and expansion branches change their meaning according to the chosen constitution.

### Priority members and restorations

Sixteen selected countries receive full package direction with safe territorial scope, internal politics, a distinct mechanic, force identity, League role, refusal behaviour, and post-settlement gameplay. The remaining polity catalog stays a candidate pool. It is promoted only when a route, host, or regional settlement gives the country a durable role.

### Anti-bloat rule

New depth reuses the Charter League, selected-target flow, congresses, integration ladder, regional overlays, and existing decision families. The package rejects host-exclusive duplicate stores, one achievement per country, one super-event per route, and a full standalone tree for every eligible start.

## Key design decisions

- The unifier begins by protecting African states rather than instantly conquering them.
- The selected host changes the opening campaign problem, proof of leadership, and regional risk.
- League members retain agency and can reject integration, leave, form rivals, or fight.
- Integration is staged through confidence, clauses, institutions, local support, state control, and manageable burden.
- Constitutional routes remain distinct after unification through government, crisis, and postwar play.
- Historical restorations use package tiers, promotion rules, and overlap settlements.
- Public country names remain direct and readable.
- South Africa in the Allies enters the continental route through civil war.
- Diaspora return is voluntary and rights-based.
- High-chaos actors are visibly nonhuman or supernatural and never stand in for human African identities.
- Event 12 begins at tier 4, so requested Evolution IV content is preserved as a post-unification escalation layer under the current evolution framework.
- Other continent unifiers require unique packages before world-order use.
- Super-event titles, quotes, remarks, and audio remain research-gated.

- full repository audits after gameplay exists

## Improvement-loop status

The project-specific improvement-loop subagent was not exposed as an invokable tool during package creation. The required source files and subagent contract were read in full. A direct expansion and anti-bloat review is recorded in `handoffs/012_africa_expansion_revision_and_anti_bloat_review.md`, and the current stop decision is recorded in `handoffs/012_africa_improvement_loop_closure_review.md`. A fresh external pass remains required against actual implementation.

## Completion standard

The Event 12 implementation is not complete until the source specs, accepted matrices, assets, AI, localisation, event logs, cluster data, docs, super-events, achievements, and event catalog agree. Every merge, omission, queue, rejection, blocker, and simplification must be reported.
