# Event 012 Africa — AI Strategy Matrix

This matrix describes intended AI behaviour for Event 012. It is a design handoff, not final script. The implementation should translate these priorities into route weights, focus AI, decision AI, strategic plans, target scoring, and validity blockers.

## Universal AI rules

- The unifier should never instantly annex every African country by AI action. It should first build the Charter League, fight external occupiers, and integrate members through staged pressure.
- AI should avoid suicidal wars against every major colonising power at once unless the world is already in high chaos, the League is strong, or an evolution explicitly unlocks reckless escalation.
- AI should never pursue a route whose required states, subjects, faction members, or targets do not exist.
- AI should prefer helping an African country at war with an outside coloniser before pressuring that country for integration.
- AI should pause integration pressure when League cohesion is near collapse unless it has chosen the military or high-chaos route.
- AI should not use high-chaos supernatural routes below their unlock conditions.
- AI should treat nonhuman actors as special chaos countries and avoid ordinary diplomatic logic unless a route explicitly permits bargaining with them.

## War and intervention AI

| Situation | AI action | Target priority | Escalation limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| African member attacked by coloniser | Intervene or send volunteers/aid | Adjacent coloniser holdings, member capital, ports | Avoid declaring on all colonisers unless prepared. |
| Coloniser holds isolated enclave | Ultimatum, blockade, local uprising | Ports, capitals, rail hubs | Prefer limited war goal if possible. |
| Coloniser is a major power/faction leader | Build coalition first | Colonies closest to League members | Delay if unifier has low equipment or supply. |
| African state refuses integration | Pressure, autonomy offer, border conference | Only target after external wars stabilise | Strong states can leave and declare war; avoid repeat spam. |
| Scramble for Africa crisis active | Rally League, emergency command, defensive missions | External powers with African holdings or guarantees | Can become broad war only in high chaos/unified Africa. |

## AI validation expectations

The implementation completion report should include:

- route coverage table comparing AI routes against implemented focus routes;
- decision AI table for each decision family;
- no invalid-route AI warnings for dead tags, absent continents, unavailable focuses, missing faction, or impossible wars;
- no repeated war-goal spam against the same target;
- no repeated free unit or free core loops;
- RSA civil-war test where Allies peace correctly after Continental victory;
- high-chaos test where nonhuman actors are excluded from normal country systems.
