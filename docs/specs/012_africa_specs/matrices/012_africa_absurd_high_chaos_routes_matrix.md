## Nonhuman route safeguards

- Register every implemented nonhuman actor in shared special-chaos and actual-nonhuman classifiers.
- Do not give them human ideology parties, human leader-name pools, normal conscription, or normal annexation integration.
- Their “leaders” should be institutional names, symbolic councils, or generated nonhuman portraits.
- Their assets must be generated fictional/nonhuman; no real wildlife photograph is required unless an asset researcher chooses sourced material for reference, not direct portrait identity.
- Their decisions should be terrain/ecology/pact based, not ordinary human diplomacy.
