# Event 012 Africa focus-route payoff matrix notes

This matrix is the compact route-level reference for spec part 8. It contains 78 focus groups, dilemmas, support-lane mutations, and post-unification reviews.

## What a row represents

A row is an implementation role, not a mandatory single focus. The implementation may use one focus, a short focus group, a decision family, or an event-backed crisis when that better fits the tree. The row remains an acceptance item because its choice, mechanic change, payoff, and tradeoff must exist somewhere visible.

## Route structure

The matrix covers:

- five shared opening groups
- Charter federalism
- continental republic
- council of crowns
- people's union
- military continentalism
- confederation of African states
- the hidden Covenant route
- shared support lanes
- post-unification constitutional review and world-order sponsorship

The six grounded constitutions share economy, defence, diaspora, intelligence, restoration, and Scramble lanes. Each lane changes meaning according to the constitution. The hidden route is gated and cannot replace the grounded opening.

## Anti-bloat rule

Do not create one focus for every clause in every row. Combine closely related decisions into coherent focus groups. Preserve all meaningful dilemmas, route locks, consequences, and payoffs while avoiding decorative nodes and repeated small modifiers.

## Audit use

A route is incomplete when:

- it lacks a governing model beyond a route title
- it has no internal dilemma or failure state
- it does not change shared support branches
- its capstone grants only statistics
- the AI has no policy for the route
- the route leaves no post-unification constitutional problem
- its visible asset states cannot communicate its key choices and crises

The full narrative and branch logic remain in spec part 8.
