# 012 Africa regional integration staged requirements matrix

| Region | Stage 1 protection | Stage 2 administration | Stage 3 missions | Federal route | Coercive risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maghreb coast | protect ports and borders | coastal customs and desert transit | repair ports, secure rail, mediate cities | high autonomy for strong states | naval reaction, desert insurgency |
| Nile valley | secure crossings and capitals | river administration and heritage protection | rail, irrigation, Nubian settlement | Kush, Nubia, Makuria partners | river sabotage, Nile League |
| Horn and Red Sea | defend highlands and ports | mountain and port mediation | roads, convoys, Aksum restoration | highland federal member | mountain resistance, naval crisis |
| Sahel caravan belt | open caravan corridors | wells and desert supply | roads, wells, Kanem-Bornu and Songhai work | autonomy-heavy federation | Sahel Pact, supply collapse |
| Gulf of Guinea | protect ports and labour centers | customs and forest-road coordination | ports, labour mediation, restored polities | strong if polities respected | strikes, forest resistance |
| Congo basin | protect river and forests | river authority and forest safeguards | river traffic, Kongo, Luba, Lunda work | strong with cultural restoration | extraction revolt, nonhuman reveal |
| Great Lakes | protect lake ports | lake customs and highland roads | lake guards, Buganda restoration | high autonomy federal path | Lakes Union, highland revolt |
| Swahili coast | secure ports | maritime customs | convoys, Kilwa and city-state work | maritime city-state model | port revolt, naval reaction |
| Zambezi and Zimbabwe plateau | secure rail and mines | plateau roads and resources | Great Zimbabwe, Mutapa, Zambezi crossings | resource sharing and heritage | rail sabotage, stone host |
| Southern Cape and plateau | resolve RSA and secure mines | southern rail and port settlement | civil-war cleanup, Zulu route | possible after Allied peace | loyalist insurgency, mine sabotage |
| Indian Ocean islands | secure convoys | island autonomy | Merina, harbor repair, shipping defense | associated island members | blockade, island revolt |
| Atlantic islands and diaspora ports | open ports | housing and customs | lane escort, settlement, labour mediation | port member or autonomy route | foreign surveillance, dock strike |
