# 012 Africa decision and mission matrix

| Family | Action type | Visible target | Costs and requirements | Success | Failure or risk | AI behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Charter founding | clickable decision plus timed setup | unifier capital or symbolic congress city | political capital, stability, security units, time | opens League and legitimacy | weak opening lowers cohesion | AI always starts if valid |
| Invite neighbor | targeted decision | nearby African country | envoy cost, relation, legitimacy | protected or member state | refusal raises rival pressure | AI invites weak threatened countries first |
| Arms corridor | targeted decision | African country at war with coloniser | infantry equipment, support equipment, convoys, route access | target trust and defense | convoy loss, colonial panic | AI uses when affordable and target can survive |
| Officer cadres | targeted mission | League member | army XP, command power, equipment | target units improve, cohesion rises | dependence or command friction | military AI likes, federal AI uses sparingly |
| Rail project | timed mission | named corridor region | civilian factory burden, trains, state control | logistics and integration support | cost overruns, local resentment | industry AI prioritizes connected regions |
| Port shield | timed objective | coastal member or island | naval access, convoys, coastal defense units | prevents colonial landing pressure | blockade risk | AI uses for threatened ports |
| Federation vote | targeted decision | trusted League member | legitimacy, cohesion, target trust, stability | federated member or integration stage | failed vote lowers trust | AI checks high success chance |
| Coercive integration | targeted decision | dependent or occupied member | command power, garrisons, equipment, low autonomy | puppet or annexation progress | resistance and panic rise | authoritarian AI only, high threshold |
| Restore polity | targeted regional decision | controlled or liberated region | state control, local support, legitimacy, leader or council asset | regional subject appears | rival claim or weak subject | AI uses if occupation burden high |
| Suppress rival bloc | mission or war prep | rival African bloc | military readiness, border forces, war support | bloc contained or war goal opens | wider African war | AI avoids if weak |
| Anti-colonial ultimatum | crisis decision | colonial power holding African land | war support, readiness, legitimacy, allied support | withdrawal, war goal, or crisis | colonial panic jumps | AI uses if strong or backed |
| Diaspora convoy | timed mission | return port and settlement region | convoys, port control, housing project, stability | returnee spirit and manpower over time | overcrowding or spy scare | AI uses if ports safe |
| Resource nationalization | post-unification decision | resource concession region | legitimacy, garrisons, industry, diplomatic cost | resources and industry | sanctions and panic | AI uses after strong control |
| Nature demand | high-chaos decision | enemy or hostile outside power | high-chaos gate, oracle support, stability risk | disaster pressure or compliance | League fear and blowback | rare AI only in high chaos |
| Disease pressure | high-chaos abstract action | enemy state group | fictional bio route gate, condemnation risk, containment risk | outbreak pressure as game mechanic | blowback and condemnation | AI only for extreme route |
| Scramble response | crisis mission family | outside powers | readiness, cohesion, ports, allies | survive outside pressure | expeditionary crisis | AI route based on strength |
