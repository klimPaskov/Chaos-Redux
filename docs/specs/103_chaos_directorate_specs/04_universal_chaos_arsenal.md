# 04. The entire living arsenal

Universal access is a release requirement. Every current and future Chaos Redux unit, technology, equipment type, special project, facility, doctrine and relevant operational system must have a working Directorate disposition. A list of familiar families, a large vanilla army, a technology-only unlock and a debug refill button each leave the central promise unfinished.

The institution draws together the mod's actual forces. Zombies, their mutated and wendigo variants, robots, clones, cannibals, African formations, revolutionary units, ghosts, cave broods, rat forces, assassins, golems and experimental scientific forces illustrate its character. This paragraph is an illustration, not an authoritative roster or a selector. Future additions participate without edits to an Event 103 family list.

## Availability, materialization and use

| State | Meaning | What proves it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entitled | The country may acquire or operate the capability regardless of ordinary source-country, ideology or narrative gate | Valid owner authorization and a real supported consumer |
| Ready for the current operation | Equipment, physical site, basing, compatible mode and target requirements are met | Current owner preflight with exact inputs |
| Materialized | The actual unit, equipment, building or operational asset exists | Concrete creation or delivery result, not a research flag |
| Used | The capability performs its real game role | Owner-certified combat, transport, support, deployment or operation outcome |

Every capability must reach the first state. The opening includes a coherent minimum field roster from every deployable combat family and immediate usable special operations. The other states depend on real physical circumstances and paid sustainment. A landlocked country can have full naval entitlement before it has a legal port. A support company can be available without being illegally placed beside every mutually exclusive support variant.

The country may not be described as having universal operational coverage if an actual family or system is missing its adapter. A registered test-readiness flag is not proof of a consumer. Geography pending is a legitimate operational state only when the capability exists and the design supplies an attainable route to its needed site.

## Discover everything through its owner

The current CXT documentation supports a native runtime technology array. It explicitly says equivalent global arrays are unavailable for several other surfaces. Do not invent an engine reflection call that enumerates all unit or project definitions.

Use two complementary paths. Technology discovery reads every real entry in the documented technology array. Non-enumerable content is published by its owning package into the shared capability manifest, reusing existing family and CXT registration identities where they are useful. The manifest describes capabilities and their consumers. It does not duplicate Event 19's family list or rewrite its ordinary rules.

An independent build-time inventory compares all active Chaos Redux definitions, including loaded overrides and DLC variants, against those registrations. This closes the gap between an opt-in bus and the word every. If a future author adds a definition but forgets its registration, the completeness gate fails that change. The solution is to register and implement the missing capability, not to make the final boss quietly ignore it.

### Required coverage

| Surface | Discovery and disposition | Operational requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technologies | Every real installed technology entry, with reserved/default objects excluded by the verified engine contract | Native completion where compatible, otherwise truthful entitlement and owner-supported active-mode handling |
| Combat subunits | Every active concrete combat definition mapped to its real family and valid formation builder | At least one legal materialization pathway, required equipment and manpower, replacement or owner-spawn support, actual visuals |
| Support subunits | Every active support definition with an appropriate combat anchor and support-exclusion metadata | A valid combined formation and substantive support use, never a support-only phantom division |
| Equipment | Every concrete equipment model, variant and required dependency | Real stockpile, production, variant creation, consumption and salvage paths |
| Air and naval content | Every active custom airframe, ship, related formation and delivery definition | Legal basing, production or materialization, transport and active theater use |
| Special projects | Every active project owned by the mod or required by its equipment and systems | Authorized gameplay outputs and genuine operational access without replaying original event history |
| Facilities and strategic sites | Every active facility or site type used by a capability | Legal owned-state construction or adoption, real capacity, loss and repair behavior |
| Doctrines and institutional systems | Every active doctrine track, mastery and institutional capability | Real bonuses and operational consumers, compatible active modes, no mastery in name only |
| General operational systems | Every package-owned country capability, delivery system or reusable strategic operation | Valid authorization, preparation, cost, target, execution, AI and cleanup |
| Framework-only entries | Explicitly identified nonplayable or purely diagnostic infrastructure | A documented reason it has no independent country-use surface. This classification cannot conceal an actual missing weapon or system |

The conventional adapter also covers the installed vanilla dependencies needed to make the army, navy, air force, transport and industry function. Definition overrides are resolved using the real game's load order before the inventory is accepted. A stale source file or an unloaded DLC token cannot be counted as a working active capability.

## Neutral authorization

Existing providers may refuse ordinary countries, require an original tag, depend on a named scientist or assume a particular parent event stage. The Directorate receives a separate owner-approved consumer context that removes those narrative barriers while preserving the real content.

For example, the distinct refined-clone provider must retain its actual refined unit and equipment. Its Directorate adapter cannot substitute the ordinary clone family or set flags claiming the country is Germany, that a particular source program happened, or that its exclusive scientist moved. The same rule applies to all identity-gated forces.

Owner authorization allows the capability. It does not turn Event 103 into the parent event. Do not create unrelated parent countries, advance their evolutions, fire their super-events, request their endings, acquire their unique leader tokens or award achievements based on histories that never happened. If a project bundles capability and narrative effects, separate those effects in the owner package so both ordinary play and the Directorate have truthful execution paths.

Ordinary users of a provider keep their established gates. The new exception is scoped to a verified live Directorate episode and its allowed operation context. A random CXT tag, copied flag, invalid receipt or a test fixture cannot claim that authority.

## Compatible active modes

Every technology and capability remains available even when native mechanics prohibit simultaneous effects. Prefer native completion of the full inventory where the current engine supports it. Where simultaneous technologies, doctrine tracks, equipment variants or supports conflict, use explicit owner-supported modes or legal parallel formations.

The country retains durable entitlement to each mode and may switch through a visible valid operation. Switching cancels or settles incompatible pending work, updates actual production and effects, and preserves existing legitimate equipment and knowledge. It does not erase a family from the registry or permanently lock the alternative behind a focus route.

A technology card or provider flag cannot falsely claim native completion merely to satisfy a checklist. Conversely, copying the ordinary compatible-technology-union helper without review may silently omit an entire branch. The Directorate-specific adapter must prove both truthful engine state and access to every branch's actual capability.

Support exclusions are resolved with multiple valid formations, not a single impossible template. Terrain and equipment distinctions remain meaningful. Human manpower is never assigned to a robot battalion merely to fit an ordinary infantry accounting shortcut.

## Special projects, sites and personnel

The opening country has all project and facility entitlements and the essential operational outputs that its initial military package needs. It does not illegally stack all special-facility types in one state. Existing CXT logic that acquires foreign campus states is forbidden in Event 103 activation.

Prepare relevant facilities on legal owned and controlled land, preserve captured functioning sites, and conquer needed coast or additional campus states. Native project outputs that can be granted without a physical facility still need the owner's neutral completion contract. Outputs that inherently require a real site remain tied to that site. Completed-project history is not a substitute for a missile base, laboratory, delivery platform or portal that must physically exist.

Scientific staffing uses ordinary nonexclusive staff or explicitly designed institutional support. Exclusive named scientists remain with their proper owner unless a genuine independent transfer occurs through that system's rules. The boss can reproduce or adopt a capability without fabricating the original person's participation.

Facility occupation, damage, loss, repair and mode changes follow the owner ledger. If the country loses the only site for a capability, it retains entitlement but must rebuild a real operating site. That temporary physical interruption gives resistance an understandable target.

## Active special warfare

The Directorate adopts the unrestricted in-game CBRN policy, completes the relevant command and doctrine setup, stocks the actual protective and delivery equipment, and uses biological, chemical and other special systems in its campaigns. It has no moral, ideology or political-condemnation gate that prevents use.

Operations still use their real native or package-owned preparation, range, target, payload and delivery rules. Native raids are actually dispatched and their results processed. Project availability, an aircraft sitting idle, a support-company icon or a manufactured attack-history flag is not an operation.

Protection and logistics support frequent use and the survival of its own army. Risks and friendly effects remain actual game consequences, not a reason to suppress all operations or to grant invulnerability. The AI selects targets that help conquest, including supply, staging, industrial and force concentrations where the owner system permits them. It is allowed to create consequences that contribute to another world end, and must not hide those outcomes to keep its own branch eligible.

## Growth, replacement and future updates

The initial grant is finite and tied to the frozen opening budget. From then on, native production, captured capacity, manufactured-body systems and owner-defined operating flows sustain the army. There are no recurring debug refills.

When a new package or version registers after arrival, the manifest becomes dirty and wakes the active boss's bounded consumer. The consumer verifies compatibility, adds missing entitlement, grants only the new version's explicitly required initial capability setup, and puts it in the service queue. It never reruns the original force snapshot, restocks every old model or redeploys the entire roster.

Registration and application are separate. The existing CXT daily fallback may advertise a missing token and mark pending work, but cannot dispatch debug `_apply` grants. The Directorate consumes neutral callbacks in its own event-owned lifecycle. Old and new queue records retain their cursor and receipts across save/load, controller changes and the native iteration ceiling.

If a package is renamed or removed, preserve its stable identity through a documented migration. Existing assets and pending operations receive the owner's correct settlement. A mod update cannot silently shrink the achievement set, orphan an army or replay a removed capability's reward.

## Completion proof

The final coverage report is generated from active definitions, not from whichever adapters happen to exist. For every row it records definition identity and source, owner, capability class, native or alternative route, authorization, materialization, sustained operation, AI use, visual consumer, version receipt, cleanup and supported DLC configurations.

A normal runtime view may show a capability as waiting for a coast or material production. A release report may not classify a missing adapter, absent family, unverified project output or a default test flag as complete. Missing coverage is repaired before the feature is called universal.

The implementation architecture appendix supplies the exact proposed helper and migration map. Those interfaces remain proposals until their real source consumers and installed engine behavior are validated.
