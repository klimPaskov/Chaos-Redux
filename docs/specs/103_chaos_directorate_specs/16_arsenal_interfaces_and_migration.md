## 1. Universal capability access

The Directorate has entitlement to every installed unit, technology, concrete equipment, special project, facility, doctrine and operational system from committed arrival.
Entitlement includes every future addition after registration and version consumption.
It is independent of intensity, a focus choice, historical continental completion, source-event activation, or the terminal branch.
The immediate force is finite and deliberately overwhelming under the force plan in Part 03.
Universal entitlement does not require putting every support into one illegal template, simultaneously selecting mutually exclusive doctrines, or constructing six mutually exclusive facilities in the first state.

Every definition needs a concrete disposition with a way to obtain, sustain, and use it.
Allowed dispositions describe operations, including recruitable formation, owner-created formation, support attachment, production or equipment variant, special operation, project output, native physical installation, compatible active mode, or non-operational presentation definition with its real consumer.
No disposition may mean that the Directorate is permanently denied a family or technology.
An unavailable owner adapter is an implementation blocker to repair before release.
It is never a design waiver or a generic infantry substitute.

An asset pilot such as the documented non-buildable `anomaly_signal_beacon_pilot` still receives a disposition naming its actual map-asset consumer.
It is not reclassified into a military research facility or silently deleted from the audit.
Where a definition is genuinely non-operational, preserve its installed behavior and document why there is no executable military action.

Part 03 supplies the force formula and human reserve policy.
This specification consumes that plan without inventing a normal-country power ceiling.
Minimum legal family coverage takes precedence over a numerical division quota.
Every combat family gets its valid opening formation at natural intensity when its owner adapter is implemented and can legally materialize it.
Support-only definitions are covered by legal provider-owned attachments or operation consumers.
Additional formations, aircraft, ships, reserves and replacements follow battlefield roles, real tokens and the committed quantity plan.

### Availability and compatibility

Maintain separate records for entitlement, installed readiness, current active mode, current physical operation legality, and fresh use.
Entitlement is permanent for a committed episode while the actor exists.
Operation legality can change with stockpiles, a lost site, blocked transport, active compatibility mode or current target authority.
These states explain the actual blocked action without suggesting that the capability vanished from the arsenal.

All technologies remain discoverable and selectable.
Mutually exclusive technologies and doctrine tracks need an owner-certified compatible active set rather than an indiscriminate native grant.
The current `union_compatible_researched_technologies_from_donor` explicitly preserves flexible versus streamlined production and concentrated versus dispersed industry exclusions.
That interface does not establish that all exclusive technology effects can coexist.
The Directorate receives both branches as available modes, with their unlocks and equipment preserved where supported, while only a legal modifier or doctrine combination operates at one time.
Switching uses owner-defined transition and reconciliation, without reacquiring a technology reward, wiping equipped armies or repeating project completion.
An exact native representation for reversible technology modes remains unresolved until installed references and source bodies are inspected.
If the native representation cannot safely switch, the owner must expose equivalent capability outputs and lawful separate operational modes, with the changed representation recorded and reviewed.
Permanent omission of the opposing branch fails the requirement.

Compatible support exclusions remain real.
The documented 18 chemical-tank and seven Livens support variants use separate valid formations where `cbrn_offensive_delivery` prevents coexistence.
Spawn-only ghosts, golems, mutated zombies, elephant, cave and rat bodies remain usable through their owner creation paths.
They are not forced into trainable templates that violate their source design.
Mixed armies retain native unit terrain, equipment and manpower behavior.

### Proposed neutral package manifest

Each package owner publishes one stable carrier identity plus a versioned manifest describing its complete current installed surface.
Prefer attaching this manifest to the existing CXT carrier identity with a neutral query callback.
The consumer must never infer that `_apply` means safe or authorised.
A separate inert registration callback can publish metadata when the current carrier bus cannot query without application.
This is an extension to the owner bus, not a second Event 19 family registry.

| Manifest field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Stable package and capability IDs | Owner-assigned identity, with duplicate and conflict rejection |
| Contract and content version | Supported schema and exact version whose grants may be consumed once |
| Definition bindings | Every concrete technology, project, equipment, sub-unit, facility, doctrine or system owned by this row |
| Provider binding | Existing family/provider pair for units, where applicable |
| Consumer authorisation | Explicit support for neutral live-country operation, separate from debug or original parent use |
| Disposition and role | Real recruit, spawn, attachment, production, mode, installation or operation path |
| Compatibility and physical predicates | Owner-defined exclusions, actual site or basing conditions and DLC configuration |
| Callback binding | Owner query, authorise, activate, construct, quote, pay, operate, reconcile and cleanup entry points |
| Resource and presentation publication | Concrete tokens, variants, actual obligations and localisation-key tokens |
| Provenance and side-effect policy | Which receipts are capability entitlement, real paid work, parent-source history or achievements |

Aligned runtime rows must be versioned and validated together.
Repeat registration is idempotent only when ownership and every immutable identity field match.
Conflicting ownership or unknown contract versions set an invariant failure and block the affected integration.
No positional row index is a durable identity.
No registration effect grants resources, creates actors, starts parent incidents, fires world ends, or changes the human country.

The existing Event 19 provider registry stays the source for family ownership and provider identity.
Event 103 can read its aligned rows and request neutral capability authorisation from those owners.
It must not call Event 19 derivative setup to obtain a unit, install another public country package, or borrow a derivative's nonhuman-country proof.
Equipment, system and project registrations join through their owning package.
Support attachments retain the same provider or parent transaction rather than a fake standalone support division.

### Required build-time gate

Before release, enumerate effective installed definitions after same-path masking and configured DLC, then compare them against manifest bindings and technology discovery.
The census covers `common/units/`, including support definitions, `common/units/equipment/`, technology and doctrine definitions, native project definitions, special facilities and relevant building definitions, equipment modules or variants where their owner uses them, and registered operational systems.
General systems are also cross-checked against the owner API index, integration surfaces and CXT carrier census because they may not have one native database folder.
An owner must declare each system it exposes.
The audit cannot infer completeness from a successful CXT application alone.

Every definition resolves to exactly one reviewed owner and at least one valid disposition.
Verify concrete equipment tokens, variants, provider pairs, support compatibility, localisation payloads, physical predicates, content versions, dynamic dispatch targets, CXT coverage and neutral capability callbacks.
Check missing IDs, duplicate IDs, stale callbacks, malformed aligned rows and unregistered definitions.
Reject grants that call a parent setup, world end, exclusive character transfer, false provenance helper, achievement fixture or debug refill.
Resolve actual nested bodies rather than accepting a literal proof flag.

A missing definition or malformed registration blocks completion of the universal arsenal.
The audit is a mechanical inventory gate with a human owner review.
It generates drift evidence and never bulk-rewrites gameplay or invents a consumer-maintained family switch.
Package authors add their own registration in the same change as new content.
The current 87 sub-unit, 71 equipment and 83 project snapshots are evidence of a documented baseline, not an authoritative permanent inventory.

## 13. Required implementation closure

The architecture retains every installed capability and every future registered addition. It does not authorize shipping with a missing adapter, a debug substitute, an illegal opening force or an unproven universal inventory.

The highest-risk implementation gates are the dormant-history migration, neutral authorization for identity-gated providers, safe project-output separation, native compatible technology representation, effective-definition completeness and provider census, one-state military readiness under real supply rules, and dead-actor callback and destruction semantics. Parts 12 and 13 assign the integration sequence and acceptance cases. Part 15 records the final design review disposition.

No gameplay implementation, asset generation or live-game completion is claimed. Commit and validation work belongs in the actual Chaos Redux repository when this specification is implemented.
