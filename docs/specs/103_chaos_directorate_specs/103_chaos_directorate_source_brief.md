# Chaos Directorate

## Catalog entry

- Event ID: `103`
- Event name: Chaos Directorate
- Replaces: Conscription
- Type: Major
- Status: To Be Reworked
- Chaos level: 6
- Cluster: None

## Core idea

> The world has survived at maximum Chaos for too long without reaching a world-end scenario. Chaos produces its own final answer.

The **Chaos Directorate** appears as the final boss country of Chaos Redux.

It uses the existing Chaos Redux test-country carrier internally, but its entire visible identity changes when this event occurs.

Nothing shown to the player should indicate that it is a test country.

Its public identity is simply:

**Chaos Directorate**

The country exists for one purpose:

**conquer the world.**

## Automatic unlock

Event 103 is unavailable during ordinary play.

The world must first enter World Collapse with Chaos above `1000`.

A one-year grace period then begins.

If any normal world-end scenario triggers during that year, the Chaos Directorate never needs to appear.

If the world remains above `1000` Chaos for a full year without reaching a world-end scenario, Event 103 becomes available in the normal major-event pool.

It does not fire immediately.

The world has already received plenty of chances to end through the normal Chaos Redux routes.

The Directorate becomes the fallback.

## Growing major-event weight

When Event 103 first becomes available, it receives a relatively low major-event weight.

From that point onward, its weight continually increases for as long as:

- the world remains above `1000` Chaos
- no world-end scenario occurs
- the Chaos Directorate has not already appeared

The longer the world somehow survives without producing an ending, the more likely the final boss becomes.

There is no fixed point where it must fire.

Its probability simply keeps becoming harder to avoid.

## The arrival

When Event 103 fires, one random valid **non-island state** is selected.

The Chaos Directorate takes the state and establishes its first foothold.

Its appearance should feel abrupt.

There is no long preparation period and no diplomatic introduction.

The country already exists as a functioning military state when it appears.

The previous controller of the state immediately becomes an enemy.

Every country bordering the Directorate also becomes an enemy.

The final boss begins expanding at once.

## The Chaos Directorate

The Directorate should feel like the accumulated result of Chaos Redux itself.

It should be vastly stronger than an ordinary one-state country.

Its opening strength is calculated dynamically from the world it has appeared in.

Important factors can include:

- current Chaos
- time spent beyond the one-year grace period
- global military strength
- global industry
- world population
- current technology level
- number and strength of surviving major powers
- how advanced the campaign has become

A 1930s-sized army would not be an appropriate final boss in a heavily developed late-game world.

Its starting power grows with the world it must defeat.

## The Chaos arsenal

The Directorate should make broad use of Chaos Redux content.

- advanced conventional equipment
- unusual Chaos Redux weapons
- special unit families
- experimental technologies
- special projects
- chemical and biological capabilities
- strange military technologies
- other powerful systems created by the mod

So literally every unit, tech, etc in the mod must be available to the country and it must all be dynamic. So when in future when new techs and units are added, the chaos directorate would also have access to them. So you could have a mix of all zombies, wendigos, robots, clones, cannibals, african units, lenin's revolutionaries, etc. And the country would actively use biological warfare and other special chaos technologies, without any regard with no restrictions. Its goal is to cause as much chaos as possible.

The idea is that the final boss should feel like **Chaos Redux fighting back with its own toys**, rather than a generic country with large vanilla divisions.

## Conquest behavior

The Directorate begins by attacking every country bordering its territory.

As it conquers land and gains new borders, it attacks the new neighboring countries as well.

It does not negotiate lasting peace.

It does not stop because its original enemies have been defeated.

Every expansion creates the next set of targets.

Its wars continue until either:

- the Directorate is destroyed
- the Directorate conquers the world

## Growing through conquest

Every conquest makes the Directorate stronger.

It absorbs the useful parts of conquered territory, including things such as:

- population
- industry
- infrastructure
- military stockpiles
- strategic positions
- research capacity
- useful Chaos Redux capabilities already present in the conquered country

A Directorate that has consumed several powerful countries should be much stronger than the original one-state invasion.

Its expansion therefore creates a natural snowball.

Stopping it early is easier than stopping it later (but still extremely difficult, since it starts with a large army)

## The first continent

The first major objective is total control of the continent where the Directorate appeared.

It expands through neighboring countries until that continent has been consumed.

If geography leaves countries on the continent that cannot be reached through ordinary land expansion, the Directorate should still pursue them as part of the same continental campaign. It receives the naval, airborne, transport, or other established capabilities needed to reach those remaining territories before completing the continent. The later terminal escalation strengthens its worldwide reach further.

Consuming the entire continent satisfies the territorial requirement for the terminal stage. The ending begins when the remaining world-end conditions are also met.

# World-end scenario: Chaos Always Wins

When the Chaos Directorate completely conquers its first continent, **Chaos Always Wins** becomes eligible. The ending begins only while Chaos is above **1000**, its own ending is enabled, and no world-end scenario has already begun. The Directorate must still hold the conquered continent when those conditions are met.

The meaning is simple:

The world reached maximum Chaos.

Every other ending failed to happen.

The final boss appeared.

The world failed to contain it before an entire continent disappeared.

Chaos has won.

The Directorate becomes an even stronger terminal threat and continues its conquest beyond the first continent.

From this point onward, every remaining sovereign country becomes part of its global war.

The Directorate gains the means necessary to cross oceans and continue the campaign on other continents.

Its final objective remains complete world conquest.

## After Chaos Always Wins

The Directorate should continue absorbing the remaining world.

Countries can still fight it, but the world-end scenario marks the point where the campaign has entered the final Chaos Directorate order.

The boss should increasingly use the resources, technologies, armies, and strange capabilities accumulated from conquered continents.

A successful Directorate eventually leaves no independent country outside its control.

## Defeating the final boss

The Chaos Directorate can be defeated before it consumes a continent.

If every Directorate state is conquered and the country is destroyed, the final-boss attempt has failed.

**Chaos Always Wins** does not trigger.

The world has survived the fallback ending.

This should be extremely difficult and actually nearly impossible, especially if the Directorate has already absorbed several powerful countries.

# Chaos Directorate focus tree

The Directorate receives its own dedicated focus tree.

It does not need ordinary democratic, fascist, communist, or non-aligned political routes.

Its entire identity is built around final-boss conquest.

The tree should broadly support:

- establishing the first foothold
- rapidly building the war machine
- absorbing conquered countries
- exploiting Chaos Redux weapons and technologies
- destroying continental resistance
- preparing intercontinental conquest
- strengthening after major victories
- completing the world conquest

Its exact branches belong to the full planning package.

## Triggerable scenario: The Final Boss

Event 103 also receives a triggerable scenario named:

**The Final Boss**

This scenario directly creates the Chaos Directorate without waiting for the normal:

- `1000+` Chaos requirement
- one-year grace period
- major-event weight roll

The Directorate still appears from a random valid non-island state and follows the same conquest identity. This manual setup bypasses the arrival prerequisites only. **Chaos Always Wins** still requires the first continent and its normal world-end conditions, including Chaos above **1000**.

Scenario intensity changes how powerful the Directorate is when it arrives.

Higher intensity can provide stronger opening forces, industry, military capacity, and access to more of its final-boss arsenal.

The scenario should preserve the same core experience:

> Spawn the final boss and see whether the world can stop it.

## Design identity

Event 103 is the fallback ending for a world that somehow refuses to end.

The progression should remain simple:

1. Chaos rises above `1000`.
2. The world survives for one full year without a world-end scenario.
3. Chaos Directorate enters the major-event pool.
4. Its weight keeps increasing as the world continues surviving.
5. The Directorate appears in a random non-island state.
6. It attacks its neighbors and grows through conquest.
7. Every conquest makes it stronger.
8. If it holds the entire first continent and meets the enabled branch's remaining world-end conditions, **Chaos Always Wins** begins.
9. The Directorate continues until the rest of the world is consumed or manages to destroy it.

The Chaos Directorate should feel like the final examination of everything the campaign has created.
