# 07. Orders and resistance

## Shared action and mission rules

### Payment quotes

Each action has one fresh authoritative quote. Display, blocked text, availability, payment, refunds, and AI use that same quote and payer. A custom payment uses the inclusive shared affordability predicate and one debit. Native payment is not debited again by the effect.

The CP and XP values below are proposed action-local anchors for implementation tuning. All are round values and CP never exceeds 60. They do not define global power, Chaos, industrial, or capacity scaling. Equipment, fuel, population transport, construction, and convoy quantities come from the selected real task, current country, valid route, and Part 03 tuning envelope. They must be large enough to matter at that campaign's scale.

At most four distinct spendable cost types are charged. At most three appear in the inline custom cost row. Any fourth remains explicit in the full cost tooltip with its correct texticon. A bundle label must not conceal several concrete equipment debits and pretend they are one cost. If a quote cannot fit the contract, the owner must redesign the payment before implementation rather than hide extra costs.

Non-consumed requirements name the state, route, force, or facility. A unit that must remain in a place is a work requirement. If the action actually reserves or removes a resource as payment, it counts in the cost budget.

### Lifecycle and active work

Each actor may have one primary Event 103 mission and up to two supporting Event 103 missions active, with three as the maximum proposed cap. Other available work remains queued or offered when relevant. AI can evaluate valid targets without using the human selector.

Strategic mission deadlines normally use 90, 120, or 180 days, modified by real distance and work. Battlefield emergency variants may use 30 or 45 days when an active attack or immediately threatened withdrawal provides the reason. The mission records which variant it is using. An ordinary project cannot adopt an emergency deadline merely for faster rewards.

A mission defines a target, required work, deadline, success, partial success where valid, failure, and cancellation. Objectives auto-complete when their actual work is finished. Paying or accepting a mission is not success. A mission that begins with the public condition already true must still require the specified action or sustained defense rather than auto-award a receipt.

At acceptance, freeze and disclose the exact target, work or continuous-hold duration, absolute deadline and quantified owner-defined work requirement. Evacuation, aid, reconnaissance, staging, reconstruction and other noncombat projects freeze their actual transfer, project, route or operation requirements. They acquire a force or escort requirement only where their real owner operation needs it.

Campaign concentration, counterattack, defense objectives and equivalent combat variants also freeze a minimum serviceable-force requirement and delivered-supply requirement. Derive that force requirement from the selected objective's verified native frontage and real legal formations, with one fully serviceable formation as the absolute minimum. The starting force anchor is enough serviceable equivalents to cover one complete verified objective frontage. Use Part 03's normalized accounting without forcing every family into a twenty-width template. The initial supply anchor is at least 75 percent delivered coverage of that frozen combat-work requirement. The exact native frontage and serviceability measurements must be bound and tested, not replaced by a literal proof flag. Continuous defense is available for acceptance only when its initial required force and supply are already present at the target.

The accepted numeric requirements and their formation or resource basis cannot shrink when units are disbanded, equipment is removed or the actor changes priority. Legal reinforcement and rotation can satisfy the same frozen combat requirement. An empty template, a unit counter with no usable force, an unsupplied token detachment or control already true at acceptance cannot award combat-work success.

Campaign concentration and counterattack require a genuine hostile capture after acceptance followed by 30 consecutive calendar days of control and the frozen work coverage, all inside the original 120-day deadline. Control loss or a break in the required coverage resets the hold only. It never extends the original deadline or creates another capture or absorption premium. A longer remote variant must quote its real revised deadline before acceptance. A defense objective performs its disclosed continuous defense work for the entire 90-day period, or the justified 30-day emergency period, from acceptance. Existing control starts that work but does not complete it. A broken defense interval fails that attempt, with any new offered task subject to the original action's cooldown and fresh payment.

Stored deadlines and native timers remain synchronized. Temporary mission modifiers have the correct removal duration. Setup receipts guard activation, cancellation, and successful resolution. Queued callbacks recheck the episode, actor, target, active record, and lifecycle phase.

Rejection before any work refunds all debited costs and publishes no success. Once physical work has occurred, record its actual result and refund only documented unspent escrow. Do not duplicate refunds or restore resources already transformed into buildings, supplies, transfers, or operations. Authority-loss cancellation cleans the contract without pretending that a normal victory or failure occurred.

Every durable result uses an actor, target, episode, work type, and transaction identity. Cooldowns and receipts block farming. Capturing a target again cannot create a new absorption reward for the same resource. Repairing genuinely new damage may be a new paid repair, but is not another conquest windfall.

## Directorate action families

All baseline action roles are available at arrival when their physical conditions exist. Focus progress strengthens their scope, coordination, or efficiency. The main category shows the current relevant actions and selected target, with no more than six primary actions. A special-operation browser is navigation into an existing owner surface, not another newly invented operation system.

The Directorate is run by AI unless ordinary player control is verified, and arrival never switches the human player. These families exist so that the AI and any supported player act through one set of quotes, costs and lifecycles. Observers see their consequences as wars, news and resistance responses, not as a menu. Terminal redeployment replaces campaign concentration when Part 09 has opened the global war. It uses the same action contract with a remote theater and a longer strategic objective where needed. A final pocket sweep replaces reconstruction or ordinary campaign work when remaining independent territory makes it the useful next task. Neither is an extra permanent button or a force-annex action.

### Operational restrictions are physical, not moral or narrative locks

The Directorate does not inherit another country's reluctance, humanitarian policy choice, source-event route requirement, or exclusive-character requirement as an accidental ban on a capability. The owner must give it explicit authorization without impersonating the original source country or leader. Real costs, launch safety, payload existence, valid geography, incompatible active modes, and native engine requirements remain meaningful.

The source review identified provider 522 as needing a new owner authorization while preserving its distinct unit token. Implementation must not solve that gap by setting German or Mengele-related provenance flags. The same rule applies to every provider with source-specific identity gates.

## Resistance decision and mission families

Resistance actors keep their existing country identities, focus trees, politics, military, and technologies. They receive additive Event 103 responses. They are not forced into a new faction or a shared replacement tree. Cooperation can help, but no AI conference result controls whether the Directorate can reach terminal eligibility.

The response category follows the selected theater or target. A threatened front offers defense, denial, reconnaissance, counterattack, and protection. A rear or transfer corridor offers industrial relocation and aid convoys. State-based replacement keeps the list usable without warehousing many weak actions in tabs. The same action can have several targets, but a human examines one target context at a time.

Every resistance success must change a physical state of the war (control, supply, production, preserved forces or assets) or extend what another action can target. No row grants a flat modifier, a standalone currency or a score. A row that cannot name its physical effect is removed rather than tuned.

Civilian evacuation is not an Event 103 action. Migration already owns Evacuate Civilians and famine already owns Evacuate, Escort Relief and Airlift Relief. Directorate captures produce the trapped obligations and front flight through the migration system's existing capture rules, and resistance countries answer them with those actions. The classifier audit in Part 16 section 8 must confirm that a Directorate capture sends front flight from the former owner's neighbouring states exactly as a capture by another Chaos power does. Event 103 adds no refugee, evacuation or population-transfer mechanic of its own.

The native action owner must verify the exact resource types and quote, especially where an existing operation already pays a cost. The table does not authorize a second payment on top of that native cost. Existing owner operations retain their supported native cadence rather than being slowed artificially to fit an Event 103 mission template.

### Work proof and anti-farming

Defense and counterattack tasks use current ownership, valid unit presence, supply, and a documented sustained-work requirement. The exact available native triggers need later verification. A source-only test cannot prove that a division-location or supply check executes correctly in the game.

Aid convoys credit only measured recipient delivery and account for convoy losses once. Any people carried on a route belong to the migration and custody owners, whose transfer, release-proof and reception rules a military decision cannot bypass.

Industrial relocation needs equivalent conservation for physical assets and any stores actually moved. If the native engine lacks an exact supported move operation, the implementation must establish and validate the bounded remove-and-rebuild transaction. It must not quietly replace the requirement with free destination factories.

Denial affects only assets the actor is allowed to dismantle or destroy, or a separate native sabotage operation with its own valid target rules. It cannot arbitrarily delete another country's buildings from a map click. It never rewinds a previous accepted Directorate absorption receipt or removes unrelated acquired power.

Reconnaissance and protection produce bounded operational benefits. They do not expose a secret global shutdown condition. The institutional authority intentionally has no single person whose removal defeats the state.

### Counterplay

The levers that defeat or delay the Directorate, and the surfaces that make them readable, are specified in Part 09's counterplay table. This part supplies the actions behind them. Before the ending, keeping or recovering one required continental state prevents live territorial completion. After Chaos Always Wins, the same operations remain meaningful for protecting armies, keeping transport open, interrupting replacement, and recovering territory. They cannot clear the terminal history. A later military destruction of CXT is recorded as a distinct outcome as specified in Part 11.

## Multiplayer, player control, and ordinary countries

Arrival never switches the human player. The Final Boss remains a scenario for the current country to face the Directorate. Event 103 adds no takeover button and no player-only boss initialization path.

If an explicitly supported existing country-selection method permits someone to control CXT, that player receives the same paid actions, native production, targets, and capability availability. They do not receive the old debug category or infinite manual refills. Achievement eligibility for that mode follows Part 11.

Per-country selected targets, pending costs, mission records, and information must be separate. One player's selector cannot replace another player's target or authorize their payment. Shared targets use real scope and transaction identity. AI sees valid candidate targets independently of each human selection.

A resistance participant receives credit only for its own accepted work or a clearly defined accepted shared operation. A promise, canceled project, duplicated capture, stale information pointer, or another country's completed task cannot become its success. Part 11 defines achievement attribution and scenario eligibility.

## Cleanup and continuing behavior

Owner action records are bound to the global episode and their actor and target. On target loss, annexation, route invalidation, actor destruction, operation cancellation, or episode defeat, update or retire the exact active record. Remove native missions even when an old private flag is stale. Preserve refunds due, accepted shipments, measured civilian outcomes, actual finished construction, durable history, and achievement receipts.

Changing a focus priority replaces only the relevant priority and temporary effects. It does not revoke basic capability access, destroy valid equipment, or erase a registered family. New compatible technology does not reset a paid project. A callback from an old production or mission context must not affect the new one.

The country's ongoing behavior continues independently of the ordinary random-event scheduler. Existing war maintenance, valid operational adapters, production, missions, and focus behavior still operate after a different world end or the Directorate ending. Exact bounded lifecycle seams are Part 12 implementation bindings. This specification does not authorize a new whole-world daily, weekly, or monthly scan.
