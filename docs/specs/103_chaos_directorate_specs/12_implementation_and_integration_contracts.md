## Clean carrier and save compatibility

Inventory dormant CXT history before changing it. The repository document says it pre-completes technology and projects, can emit condemnation and achievement effects, and contains a prior activation-freeze investigation. That makes a superficial cosmetic rename insufficient.

Separate four categories of state: neutral technology or definition access, test-only fixture records, genuine campaign records, and Event 103 episode state. New games should avoid creating test-only external effects in dormant history. Existing saves require a receipt-aware migration that removes only provably test-only local state and reconciles queued work without undoing genuine global history. A tainted save that cannot be made safe must remain unable to activate the boss through that carrier until the defect is resolved. Do not hide contamination under an achievement-disable flag and call the country clean.

Do not overwrite a live test session, another human's CXT control or a committed Directorate. The original debug command remains a stable interface for valid test use outside active Directorate mode. The new event mode does not grant access to its free buttons.

## Event-owned lifecycle and scopes

The active owner is one episode with an immutable actor incarnation and origin state. Global state holds the current episode identity and generation, arrival provenance, dates and origin continent, alongside retained historical terminal and defeat records and the independent automatic fire-once history. The country holds military plans and private owner receipts. States and operations hold their own durable transaction and due-date records.

A fresh manual episode is allowed only through Part 09 after confirmed destruction and completed actor-mutating cleanup. Freeze a new generation and isolate all pending work before reusing CXT. Old callbacks, shipments, reports, grants, payments, refunds and owner results cannot resolve the new tag incarnation as their actor. Preserve finalized provenance and completed achievements. Any remaining post-defeat score check uses its old frozen episode and destruction receipt and cannot draw evidence from the replacement boss. Automatic grace and history remain unchanged, and separate attempts cannot combine challenge progress.

Use an actor-root entry for initialization and later country work. Entering a CXT scope does not by itself rebind ROOT in all helpers. State callbacks, RAID_INSTANCE outcomes, participant loops and nested effects must bind actor, target, source and episode explicitly. Every temporary output is initialized in its caller, including nested helpers. A validator's tombstone is not the consuming call's fresh success receipt.

Pending irreversible actions freeze identity and quoted work. The current invocation clears its own consumed receipt, performs exactly one mutation, sets the fresh result there, and passes immutable outputs to the next stage. Duplicate requests cannot overwrite an unresolved receipt. Cancel-first confirmations revalidate at click time. All exits clear private scratch state while preserving legitimate unresolved refunds or completed outcomes.

Prefer state-control changes, country creation and destruction, war outcomes, capability registration, operation results and existing shared Chaos changes. The active boss can own a bounded dated recovery pulse. It cannot depend on the normal random-event scheduler after an ending, and it must not add a recurring all-country scan to find its own actor.

Bounded loops preserve their cursor, unvisited suffix and unresolved receipt across the documented native iteration limit. A batch of more than 1000 technologies, registrations, state records or targets must continue safely instead of dropping the tail. Never turn a loop limit into an undisclosed arsenal or map cap.

Manpower calculations use the supported thousands-based accessors where necessary. Population, encoded dates, fixed-point arithmetic and integer receipts require exact installed documentation. A Python result using unlimited integers is not proof that the corresponding game arithmetic is safe.

## Territory, war and physical asset seams

Freeze pre-transfer ownership, control and real asset measurements. A normal military conquest uses the game world's resulting state, not a second scripted reward for the same native transfer. Stockpile absorption requires a proved actual debit and credit for concrete equipment. Industrial relocation requires exact source removal, legal destination construction and conservation of the accepted recovered quantity. Civilian evacuation uses one physical-transfer owner and symmetric ledgers.

Native raids have country-scoped preflight but outcome blocks begin in RAID_INSTANCE according to the supplied contract. Bind the actual actor, victim and state before any result, cost or achievement hook. Native equipment and command-power costs are not debited again by an Event 103 wrapper. Cancelled or intercepted operations preserve real losses and only return supported refunds.

Country destruction must be demonstrated under the installed ownership and control semantics. A zero-state GUI row, capitulation flag or missing cached pointer is not enough. Retire active work only after the real final settlement, then preserve durable aftermath and actor-owned delayed achievement checks.
