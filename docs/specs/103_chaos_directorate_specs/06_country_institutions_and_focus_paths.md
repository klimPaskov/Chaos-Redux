# 06. Country institutions and focus paths

These are proposed country-setup anchors, applied once after the clean-carrier preflight. The broader military quantities follow Part 03 and the selected scenario intensity.

| Surface | Required setup and purpose |

| Sovereignty | Independent country, no overlord or faction, no inherited guarantees or treaty constraints that block conquest |
| Government | One fictional institutional authority, no elections, no alternate public ideology path |

The five ordinary research slots are an administrative baseline. Additional slots are not a focus reward in this tree because universal technology access would make that reward hollow. Conquest of research centers instead improves meaningful project operation, scientific staffing, construction and production capacity through the relevant owner contracts.

## Complete country identity and package

### Authority and public identity

Reuse CXT. Do not register another tag. The active event mode supplies the Chaos Directorate country name and adjective, institutional authority name, flags, loading presentation, focus tree, and every relevant country-specific string. No active event surface may show Test Directorate, test country, console harness, debug, fixture, refill, setup trace, or any equivalent development wording.

The authority is a fictional institutional collective. It has no historical head of state, inherited foreign leader, exclusive event character, personal random-name pool, election route, restoration claimant, or alternate ideology program. Its public purpose is conquest. The portrait is an invented institutional composition owned by `chaosx_portrait_creator`. A people-free composition can represent the collective without inventing a portrait family or a named individual. The portrait worker must provide the required source mode, processing, runtime wiring, and final handoff.

Use a valid existing native ruling-ideology slot as the internal administrative binding. The exact token must be inspected rather than guessed from the public identity. Do not create a fifth ideology or expose four cosmetic political alternatives. All country-name contexts that the native engine can display during the active episode must resolve to the accepted identity. Ordinary election and voluntary reform logic must not change the conquest institution accidentally.

Do not add a bespoke advisor roster merely to fill slots. Route-specific institutional benefits belong in the three staged spirit slots below. Necessary army and fleet command capacity uses valid CXT-owned or ordinary nonexclusive native commanders, with source and portrait-owner review. It cannot steal another event's exclusive leaders or commanders. A named individual roster is not required for this institutional identity.

### Arrival package contract

Country setup is a participant in the specified validated one-state arrival transaction. It does not select another state, annex the requester, select the human player, or perform a separate territorial grant. It sets the capital to the selected state only after the shared episode owner has established valid ownership and control.

The country must begin with:

- The Part 03 army, valid division templates, reinforcement resources, fuel, supply support, and command capacity needed for immediate war.
- Every installed technology and the Part 04 authorized project, equipment, doctrine, facility, and operational dispositions.
- Recruitable valid formations covering registered combat and support content, with group compatibility and support exclusions preserved.
- Native conventional naval, air, convoy, transport, and airborne capability appropriate to valid deployment geography, plus every registered custom naval or air surface when present.
- An active production and reinforcement plan that can sustain the initial force and react to losses.
- A valid war economy, ordinary country administration, government metadata, AI package, focus tree, national spirits, localisation, and flag set.
- Event-owned action and mission access that also works if a player later controls CXT through an explicitly supported existing country-selection method.

Exact starting forces, equipment quantities, industrial output, manpower, fuel, technology completion semantics, and operating bonuses come from Parts 03 and 04. Use those quantities consistently across country history, units and production.

Do not call `chaosx_test`, its queued console receiver, or the ordinary test initialization path. The cached document states that those paths transfer a capital, annex the former country, switch the player, acquire an occupation fixture, and can acquire extra facility states. Those behaviors contradict the shared episode owner arrival contract.

The event mode also excludes repeatable zero-cost refills, debug formation batches, false chemical-attack history, fake respirator reserves, test research-choice provenance, source-event evolution completion, and achievement credit generated by test project outputs. Implementation must audit each inherited history or package receipt before event activation. Reusing a technology or equipment definition does not authorize reusing its test fixture or source-event narrative.

The active event receipt and the test initialization receipt must remain distinct. Debug decision visibility and debug grants must explicitly reject active Directorate mode. Package registration may continue to advertise content, but an event-owned non-debug consumer decides how it becomes available or enters production. Registration is never an automatic unlimited stockpile grant.

Do not clear CXT indiscriminately with a broad erase-all operation. Implementation needs an explicit inventory of harness-only state, reusable neutral state, genuine game state, and source-event receipts. Cleanup must preserve the meaning of any state it retains. A conflicting already-active carrier is an arrival-preflight failure, not permission to erase another play session.

### Territory, facilities, and population

Exactly one state is held at arrival. Facilities fit actual native placement rules and existing controlled geography. Their types are all available, but mutually incompatible facilities are not stacked illegally in the foothold. No facility allocator may take a second foreign state. An inland arrival uses its available force to reach suitable staging geography while retaining access to air transport and every other valid route supplied by the shared episode owner.

Territorial population remains physical state population. Conquest changes ownership and the use of real resources. Neither a focus completion nor a registration event may add a second copy of the captured population, stockpile, buildings, or research capacity. Any later civilian movement must use the shared measured transfer contract.

Ordinary human administration is a compatibility requirement, not a mandate to copy the harness's automatic camp fixtures. Applicable operations become available through their owner adapters and act on real valid sites and records. They must not fabricate custody, attacks, victims, release proof, or historical campaign activity to make an interface visible.

### Diplomacy and defeat

The Directorate seeks no lasting peace, voluntary subordination, defensive alliance, guarantee bargain, or political reconciliation. Native AI configuration and event-owned war maintenance must agree with the specified aggression contract. The country cannot escape its identity by selecting a civilian reform route.

Country defeat, capitulation, destruction, and ownership transitions follow the specified exact lifecycle contract. A lost capital is a military setback. Capitulation alone does not authorize deleting the episode. No focus or mission callback can re-create the country after confirmed destruction. A post-terminal defeat does not remove the recorded world end.

## Three staged spirit slots

Every focus-owned starting and earned spirit belongs to one of these three slots. Replacement upgrades the slot instead of adding a fourth. Arrival modifiers should be consolidated with these institutions when they would otherwise create duplicate visible spirit systems. Modifier-free registration carriers are not visible national spirits.

| Working slot | Starting role | Later development | Real tradeoff and failure expression |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Command institution | Coordinates immediate multi-front war and an already functioning mixed arsenal | Better force allocation, replacement command capacity, and concentration across theaters | Concentration improves the chosen offensive while leaving less support elsewhere. Lost command and supply locations reduce the effectiveness of that concentration through real field conditions |
| War economy institution | Supports the shared episode owner-scaled starting force with an active production plan | Expands sustained output, repair, transport, and advanced production according to focus investment | Short production surges consume existing reserves or delay durable construction. A broken supply network and sustained losses affect actual operational capacity |
| Absorption institution | Organizes useful captured resources without duplicating them | Improves recovery, repair, integration of production, and use of authorized captured capabilities | Rapid exploitation produces earlier usable output with slower local recovery. Deliberate reconstruction takes longer and commits industry while fronts continue |

Each stage names the one consumer it changes, such as delivered supply throughput, replacement rate or Reconstruction time, and its tooltip says so. A stage that would only raise an unnamed efficiency is dropped. Part 03 sets numeric effects within the common economy and force envelope. Spirit stages may reflect arrival, established operations, and terminal escalation, but those are baseline lifecycle stages rather than optional evolutions. Never use an evolution-enable setting as the activation gate.

A territory loss may replace a stage with its appropriate damaged or constrained form. It must not erase durable acquisition receipts and then award them again after recapture. Important temporary effects must expire or reconcile without leaving another permanent spirit behind.

## Path-level focus architecture

### Timing and opening

The country is combat-ready before its first focus. One short opening group establishes command priorities rather than granting the army or unlocking all systems. A narrow 7-day handoff focus is appropriate here because the focus skill explicitly describes that role. Record it as an exception to ordinary round-five tuning. Initial meaningful allocation choices use approximately 35 days. Sustained strategic work uses approximately 70 or 140 days when the selected work and payoff justify the commitment. These are proposed duration roles, not a final per-node list.

Do not divide one reward into several short focuses. Do not force the country through every support branch before it can attack, use a special operation, cross water, or continue after continental completion. Obsolete preparation must bypass cleanly without duplicating its reward.

### Eight branch families

| Working family | Main choice and active work | Material payoff | Tradeoff, failure, and AI response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Command establishment | Choose initial concentration on breakout, protection of the foothold, or preparation of a second operational axis. All available capabilities remain usable | A coherent native army and production priority, campaign objectives, and improved coordination actions | Concentrated forces leave other borders with fewer reserves. AI protects the capital and supply first, then chooses the feasible route that most improves expansion |
| War economy | Invest first in an immediate output window or durable repair and construction. Later investment can cover both | Real production lines, repair and construction projects, transport output, and the capacity to replace losses | An immediate surge consumes reserves and delays durable work. ### Branch depth and interconnection

Each family contains several focus groups with an opener, at least one meaningful investment choice, integration with native actions or missions, and a durable payoff. The table is not permission for one generic focus per label.

Command sets priorities. War economy and arsenal support the corresponding replacement and transport demand. Absorption restores captured logistics and production. Continental work supplies real victory receipts for adaptation. Logistics serves disconnected origin-continent targets before terminal and later overseas campaigns. Global conquest benefits from all branches without requiring an optional support chain.

Use a small number of deliberate prerequisites or shared work receipts rather than crossing every branch with long connectors. Command and absorption can form a clear central structure, with war economy and arsenal grouped together and campaign and logistics grouped together. Victory adaptation and global conquest provide later convergence. Implementation owns exact geometry.

Focus filters and navigation must agree with these roles. Use current verified native filter tokens and branch anchors, not guessed keys. Separate large regions need useful Focus Navigation entries. Hidden terminal presentation must not reveal an ending before its public state, but it must not hide or disable ordinary worldwide conquest tools.

### No access locks or terminal dependency

Branches compete through time, production priorities, and compatible active postures. Completing one branch does not permanently exclude another. A one-at-a-time command or production posture is acceptable only when it represents an actual allocation and can be changed through a clear paid or timed action. It cannot deny technologies, units, or required transport.

No focus fires Chaos Always Wins. No focus completion substitutes for live continental control. No focus vote or required capstone can delay the ending after the specified conditions become true. A disabled or superseded world-end branch must not strand the focus tree or stop the active conquest episode.

The global conquest family becomes useful when a real remote campaign exists under the specified targeting contract. It does not require the terminal flag to provide baseline operations. Terminal activation increases power and broadens wars through the shared episode owner lifecycle. It does not unlock naval transport for the first time.

## Required focus anchor groups

The following groups give each path concrete depth. Their labels remain working labels. Implementation chooses final node count, titles and coordinates while preserving every choice and payoff. A group may contain several focuses when separate real work warrants them, but splitting a single small modifier into filler is not acceptable.

Every percentage below is subject to Part 03's single capacity ledger. Role allocations redistribute its current envelope. Earned improvements to the same output dimension combine once as approved additive values, with terminal amplification applied once to the resulting target. Preparation, repair, reinforcement coordination and transport effects keep their actual distinct consumers. None may also multiply the fixed opening allowance or grant a second equipment shipment. The spirit stage renders the current result rather than accumulating its previous stages.

### Command establishment

The seven-day opening group organizes the already active command. It records the first focus-directed priority theater and highlights the already available concentration and logistics actions. A thirty-five-day allocation group then emphasizes either a concentrated breakout or distributed front reserves. Breakout shortens Campaign concentration preparation on the selected objective (starting target 20 percent) and lets a second concentration run as a supporting mission. Distributed reserves raise reinforcement and recovery on every threatened front (starting target 10 percent) and give up the second concentration. These are switchable allocations with real unit commitments, not permanent mutually exclusive research paths.

A later command-continuity group prepares alternative command sites and adequate nonexclusive commanders for separate theaters. It reduces the disruption of losing a capital without preventing its capture. The institutional payoff upgrades the command spirit within its existing slot. AI chooses breakout only when it can retain an adequate capital defense, then invests in continuity once two viable fronts require it.

### War economy

The first group turns the opening industrial allowance into an explicit production plan using real equipment deficits. Its first investment choice is an output surge or durable industrial servicing. The surge directs approximately 15 percent additional effective output toward the selected active requirement for a bounded campaign, while delaying comparable civilian repair effort. Servicing improves sustained repair and efficiency, with a 10 to 15 percent throughput target after a seventy-day commitment. The two can be completed in sequence, but their temporary full-intensity allocations cannot be stacked without paying the lost construction opportunity.

The next group expands transport and replacement supply, then develops advanced production at facilities actually controlled. Its final payoff moves the war-economy spirit from exceptional foothold support to a mature, territorially supported war machine using Part 03's common output envelope. It must not multiply the opening virtual allowance by every acquired factory. AI rushes short output under direct danger and adopts servicing when a supplied front gives the investment time to matter.

### Absorption and reconstruction

The opener creates a prioritized list of genuinely captured supply and productive sites. A salvage group processes measured recoverable stores and establishes the exact one-time transfer receipt. A restoration group repairs industrial and transport capacity through the reconstruction action. The initial choice is early usable materiel or earlier restoration of productive infrastructure. It is an order-of-work choice, so the unfinished physical work remains available later.

A deeper integration group shortens Reconstruction (starting target 15 percent) so more of the controlled capacity becomes usable sooner. A final knowledge-and-facilities group incorporates real laboratories and owner-authorized operational installations. Its reward is meaningful project and operational throughput, not a redundant technology flag or a duplicate named scientist. Losing the selected territory ends unfinished local work and retains only already transferred reserves and knowledge.

### Combined arsenal coordination

The opener certifies compatible combined formations from the complete dynamic registry. The role emphasis (sustained assault, concentrated striking force, or protective and logistical support) is the Production priority decision in Part 07 and is not repeated here. The role-coordination group raises the number of concurrent emphases from one to two, so the Directorate can sustain an assault emphasis and a protective one at once. All families remain available in every emphasis.

A joint-operations group coordinates native air, land, sea and special operations around real campaigns. An interoperability group handles compatible equipment, support exclusions and switching among mutually exclusive native modes without losing durable capabilities. The mature payoff improves concurrent operation capacity and replacement coordination across all registered owners. It never adds blanket combat bonuses in place of missing family consumers. AI can change emphasis when terrain, fuel or enemy armor makes its current choice ineffective.

### Continental campaign

The initial group establishes a named decisive axis toward useful continental supply or industry. A breakthrough group improves concentration for that target, while a containment-of-pockets group keeps bypassed hostile territory from becoming a permanent rear threat. The player chooses whether to pursue a large industrial breakthrough or close a dangerous pocket first, paying in time and force concentration.

A late group highlights the remaining exact continental states, including remote possessions, and coordinates the appropriate campaign actions. Its payoff is stronger preparation and sustained operations against those objectives. It cannot mark them conquered, reduce the required denominator or create an island exemption. AI prioritizes objectives that both reduce the remaining set and support the next route. Completion at low Chaos remains useful and does not wait for an ending.

### Logistics and ocean reach

The opening logistics group connects the growing front to real hubs, rail and transport. A staging group improves an owned port or airfield and its practical route. The main choice is concentrating maritime lift and escort, concentrating airborne transport and air support, or funding a slower balanced network. Each route improves existing baseline access. The other route remains available when geography requires it.

A sustained-expedition group adds redundancy, repairs captured bases and supports more than one landing. Its target is approximately 20 percent more usable transport throughput or shorter owner-verified preparation at the same resource commitment, subject to the actual delivery system. A global logistics group later supports several continents. It may operate before Chaos Always Wins whenever a remote campaign exists. If a site is lost, the remaining network keeps its real capacity, while that site's contribution is removed.

### Victory adaptation

This path requires genuine victory evidence. Its opener reviews a real captured major base, defeated major army or sustained new theater. The first choice uses the opportunity for consolidation or immediate redeployment. Consolidation directs productive effort toward replacement and repair. Redeployment uses the same finite capacity to prepare the next reachable target, leaving less depth behind.

A technical adaptation group uses captured installations and operational knowledge through the universal owner contract. Its mature payoff shortens the owner-verified preparation of an operation from a capability class that the victory actually demonstrated, or opens a better coordinated theater plan. It does not create extra unit entitlement. Each qualifying victory identity can pay the intended one-time material outcome once, and a recapture cannot make the same reward available again.

### Global conquest

This path becomes visible and useful when a real external theater is present, with terminal-themed presentation only after the ending. The opener prioritizes remaining sovereign production and staging. A theater-concentration group assigns an expeditionary force and its sustainment. A parallel-front group expands the number of viable campaigns without stripping all defenders from the origin continent.

The final-sweep group identifies actual remaining sovereigns and land states, allocates appropriate transport, and closes real pockets through combat. Its payoff is a stronger application of the existing force and logistics network. Completion is not a world-annex effect. If the branch ending is disabled or another ending has begun, the practical worldwide campaign groups remain usable.

## DLC absence and technological maturity

Availability means every installed capability receives a truthful disposition. An absent DLC may remove native definitions, facilities, designers, operations, or supporting interfaces. Do not call absent objects, fabricate completion, or mark an unavailable native route as working.

Where an owner already supports a documented non-DLC representation, the Directorate uses that supported representation with the same identity and purpose. If a required mod capability has no legal non-DLC consumer, the owner must provide and validate one or report the exact implementation blocker. A missing family is not silently removed from the promise.

Conventional reconnaissance must remain available to resistance countries without a native agency system. It uses actual military observation, air activity, or another verified existing operation. It does not pretend that a missing DLC agency has agents or completed intelligence operations. Similarly, naval and airborne access use the installed engine's valid transport and deployment mechanisms rather than guessed DLC features.

Facility-dependent operations respect legal sites, coast, capacity, and the installed project system. All facility types are entitled from baseline, but a one-state inland country cannot host every physically incompatible installation. Conquest and paid construction provide sites. The focus tree does not grant extra arrival territory to solve that constraint.

Technological maturity changes how much the shared episode owner initially fields, the amount needed to sustain it, production emphasis, and the importance of different delivery systems. It does not deny future-era technology to the final boss. An early campaign can face unusual advanced forces because that is part of the premise. A late campaign must face a force appropriate to its developed world rather than the cached test roster or a fixed historical army.

Because current technology is already universally available, generic research-bonus focuses are weak rewards. Strategic science work should improve application, production, valid active doctrine use, operation preparation, and replacement capacity. Newly registered content becomes available through the Part 04 consumer without adding a new focus gate or editing a curated list.
