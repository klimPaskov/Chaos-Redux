# Parliament of Fear
## Part 11. Localisation and narrative direction

### General voice

The writing uses the affected country's political institution and current situation. It should sound like official reports, political arguments, and administrative decisions within Hearts of Iron IV. The text is clear about what the government knows, what it alleges, and what remains uncertain.

Routine bureaucratic incidents may use restrained sarcasm or official hypocrisy. Serious removal, detention, disappearance, death, coups, and civil war use direct or carefully judged official language. Humor must not trivialize real suffering or turn a historical person into a joke without support from the intended context.

The writer distinguishes allegations from findings. A report cannot call a person a proven traitor when the hidden case is unresolved and the player has only an anonymous accusation. An official propaganda claim can make that accusation, but the narrative must identify it as the government's claim.

### Dynamic entities

The appropriate fields include the country, governing institution, current administration, selected bloc, available named figure, actual relevant office, affected command or institution, investigation source, agreement, and removal result.

A named figure is used only when the character link is valid. Otherwise, the text refers to the institution or an abstract office. The writing does not invent a name to fill an empty substitution. It does not call every institution a parliament.

### Choices and tooltips

Options explain the political commitment made by the player. A short option label can carry the government's attitude, but the tooltip supplies the concrete consequences. It must distinguish a guaranteed removal from a chance of arrest failure, a temporary guarantee from full vindication, and a promised support agreement from permanent loyalty.

Known mechanical costs are accurate. Uncertain case outcomes remain uncertain. If the game shows a percentage, it must be generated from the same validated probability used by the resolution path. A qualitative risk label cannot conceal that an action certainly kills a named figure.

### Institutional and constitutional wording

The writer uses the verified profile. Legislative confidence, a presidential removal process, a royal appointment, and a military command transfer receive different language. A constitutional monarchy may require parliamentary wording. A one-party state's internal disagreement is not automatically described as a multiparty election dispute.

The generic fallback uses governing establishment, council, administration, and political bloc only where those terms are appropriate to the chosen profile. It does not introduce unsupported historical institutions, royal relatives, revolutionary movements, or party names.

### Evidence wording

Case text distinguishes a document's existence from its authenticity, a meeting's occurrence from its purpose, an accusation from corroboration, and an organization from an operational plot. A confession obtained through a compromised process is marked accordingly in the evidence direction.

When a later finding exposes a fabrication, the earlier case record remains legible. The writer can show that the government acted on a false report. The system must not quietly replace the earlier text so that the mistake disappears.

### Reuse without repetition

Incident families can share a structure while varying the actual source, institution, target relationship, and political reaction. A general's command dispute and a research administrator's funding dispute should not receive the same prose with only a name changed.

Recurring actions use concise text. Major state changes receive fuller reports. The design does not require a new popup for every five-seat appointment or every small weekly pressure adjustment.

### Research-sensitive material

Real character biographies, party names, formal constitutional powers, historical institutional titles, quotations, and cultural references require verified sources. Source uncertainty is recorded in the handoff. Unverified claims are not turned into finished localisation merely to fill a required field.

The planned text surfaces are complete as direction. Final prose, option wording, achievement titles, super-event quotation, and audio selection are implementation and production deliverables.
