# Implement and audit Event 077 decisions and missions

Read the complete source package in `docs/specs/077_parliament_of_fear_specs/`, especially Parts 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, and 13. Read the full decisions/missions, events, scripted-GUI, universal-cost, subagent, and applicable offline reference material before editing.

Retain four primary action families. Investigate a Bloc contains standard inquiry, independent review, and slow preliminary review. Purge the Accused contains targeted removal, a contextual broad purge, and an urgent arrest of a prepared threat. Protect the Accused contains provisional guarantee, public vindication, and negotiated protection/support. Pack the Chamber contains loyal appointments, qualified replacement, and institutional restoration.

These are modes inside a compact interface. Do not create a permanent separate button for every mode. There is one investigation commission, one appointment queue, three ordinary case files, two ordinary objectives, and a limited emergency-warning exception.

Every action needs exact visibility, availability, target, legal profile, cost quote, duration, cooldown or capacity, uncertainty, resolution, cancellation, cleanup, and AI conditions. Use one authoritative transaction path for GUI, decisions, missions, event options, and AI. Inclusive affordability and exactly one debit are mandatory. A stale quote or unavailable person must never silently select a new victim.

Use political power only where it represents political effort. Preserve the other actual sacrifices: time, command or office loss, competence, political support, and concrete concessions. Do not add arbitrary equipment or manpower payments to routine inquiries. No more than four spendable cost types belong to one action, with at most three values in a compact price line.

Protect against recovery deadlocks. A full but minority chamber can create a confidence-settlement case and negotiate support without inventing vacancies or a personal accusation. A country with no political power can use slow preliminary review and institutional restoration. These routes sacrifice time, capacity, or political control. They are not free instant majority buttons.

Opposition alone is not conspiracy. Elected seats cannot be removed through an ordinary executive personnel action. Protection does not guarantee innocence, and investigating does not reroll truth. A broad purge shows actual people and offices at risk, loses governing support, and creates vacancies without lowering the majority threshold.

Implement actual objectives: restore working support, rebuild an underfilled chamber, resolve a disputed essential case, respond to an already prepared threat, and sustain a valid recovery interval. Close completed objectives immediately. Ordinary restoration and case missions normally use the 120 to 180 day design windows. The shorter prepared-threat warning is justified by a plot already at the operational stage.

Stable closure needs at most 20 Paranoia, at least 55 dependable seats, at least 80 functioning seats, no essential unresolved threat or purge wave, and 21 consecutive safe days. Minor unsubstantiated leads can be closed appropriately and must not make the event permanent.

Use `chaosx_decision_mission_auditor` for the bounded audit and `chaosx_ai_probability_auditor` for the real probability surfaces. Test resource-poor recovery, minority-with-no-vacancies, current-price changes, double clicks, concurrent AI requests, guarantees, target disappearance, and callbacks from an earlier crisis. Return exact edited paths and actual evidence, not a source-only claim of engine correctness.
