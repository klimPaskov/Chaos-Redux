# Country and institutional outcome handoff

This matrix is a production contract. It does not claim that a country package or transition has been implemented.

| Outcome | Country identity | Government and people | Territory and forces | Political content | Required proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary settlement | Existing country continues | Surviving leader and available characters remain, valid agreements persist | No territorial or military transfer | Existing meaningful focus content remains available | Cleanup preserves permanent losses and valid aftermath |
| Peaceful parliamentary successor | Existing country continues | Select an actual valid successor through the country profile | No free force creation or state transfer | Preserve compatible national politics and existing focus commitments | Confidence process and candidate availability are verified |
| Presidential constitutional removal | Existing country continues | Use the actual valid succession or removal process | Existing country retains assets | Do not replace constitutional content with a generic junta | Exact country and date-specific process is sourced |
| Council leadership replacement | Existing country continues | Use valid existing or properly sourced leadership | Command changes follow actual role ownership | Preserve relevant state content | No invented person or duplicate character |
| Successful coup | Existing country normally continues | Coup leader and administration derive from real participants | Transfer control of existing state assets without duplication | Resolve compatible ideology and focus transitions using established rules | Plot backing, candidate, and transfer are committed once |
| Failed coup | Existing country continues | Participants are removed or remain according to the committed result | Forces are not copied into a second country unless an actual split follows | Existing country content continues with damage | Failed attempt does not become an automatic civil-war spawn |
| Civil-war split | A valid native dynamic or audited successor identity | Distinct leader and party identity drawn from the actual organization | Viable states, supply, divisions, equipment, navy and air allocation with conservation | Inherit meaningful compatible content, avoid an empty default shell | One split owner, asset conservation, state viability, tag and character audit |
| Annexation or disappearance during crisis | Existing country becomes invalid or loses relevant control | Preserve records only where a valid successor contract exists | External outcome owns the territorial change | Do not resurrect the country to finish an inquiry | Pending callbacks become inert and the crisis slot is released |

## Civil-war package checklist

The successor needs coherent country and ideology names, a ruling party grounded in the participating organization, a valid leader, appropriate flags in all required sizes, a playable state and capital, supply access, and an actual military force. Its available technology and political content must match what it inherited.

Use native identities and flag resources where they already represent the participating side. Before creating a custom tag, audit vanilla, Chaos Redux, Workshop, and local-mod tag sources. Before adding a portrait, establish an actual figure and inspect existing assets. Do not use a generic blank country as the final product.

A starting force is scaled from the parent country's actual available assets and the organization's backing. An explicit extra emergency force must draw from stated manpower and equipment or another established recruitment source. It cannot copy the parent's best divisions and stockpiles for free.

The country's focus content must remain meaningful. Preserve or inherit a compatible existing tree where valid. Any later proposal for a custom branch requires an actual political purpose and a separate design decision. A token short tree added merely to make a package look complete is not accepted.

## Scope of technology verification

Inspect actual inheritance and helper behavior. Use the project's technology graph tools when technology grants or inheritance changes are in scope. A broad claim that the civil-war effect probably copies everything does not satisfy the handoff.

## Country-role constraints

A nonhuman Chaos country is not given a conventional human parliament solely to keep the event targetable. A special human country with normal civilian institutions can participate. The documented classifiers assist the decision only after their actual live contracts are read.
