# Parliament of Fear
## Part 12. Achievements

### Achievement principles

Achievements recognize difficult political outcomes that require several decisions and a meaningful crisis. They do not unlock from receiving the event, clicking a single action, or allowing a large number of people to disappear.

Each achievement uses the existing Chaos Redux eligibility and persistence rules. Debug-forced outcomes, invalid state manipulation, or disabled achievement conditions must not grant an unlock. Normal supported save and reload behavior must preserve legitimate progress. An AI country cannot grant an unrelated player's achievement.

The labels below are design directions and IDs, not final achievement titles. The title writer develops finished names after the mechanical conditions and sources are settled.

### 077.01. Evidence-led recovery

**ID:** `chaosx_077_evidence_recovery`

**Title and description direction:** Emphasize proving innocence and preserving a functioning state through difficult accusations. The final description must state the combined case, pressure, and removal conditions.

**Eligibility:** Any player-controlled country with an Event 077 crisis that reaches at least 60 Paranoia through ordinary valid play and lasts at least 60 days.

**Unlock:** Resolve at least three distinct accused targets through credible findings of innocence or exposed fabrication, preserve or lawfully restore the relevant living figures, and complete stable closure without an irreversible Event 077 character loss or a broad purge.

**Disqualifiers:** A false charge generated repeatedly from the same source does not count as several cases. A self-created duplicate target, debug result, broad purge, or irreversible Event 077 character loss during the qualifying crisis disqualifies it.

**Difficulty and visibility:** Hard, visible. It requires handling several serious allegations while pressure is high.

**Icon direction:** A verified case record beside an intact chamber seat, with one clear visual subject.

**Tracking:** Peak Paranoia, minimum elapsed crisis time, unique cleared case IDs, figure survival or explicit rehabilitation, broad-removal history, and stable closure.

### 077.02. Negotiated governing support

**ID:** `chaosx_077_negotiated_majority`

**Title and description direction:** Emphasize a durable agreement among competing political groups. Avoid implying that they become ideological allies.

**Eligibility:** A country with at least three distinct politically relevant blocs able to enter valid support agreements, after support falls below 51.

**Unlock:** Establish agreements involving three distinct blocs, restore at least 55 dependable seats, honor every relevant concession for 45 consecutive days, and reach stable closure without an extra-constitutional removal or Great Purge.

**Disqualifiers:** Splitting one bloc into cosmetic duplicates, expiring or breached agreements during the counted interval, or purchasing already loyal seats does not qualify.

**Difficulty and visibility:** Hard, visible. The government must survive a minority position and keep several obligations.

**Icon direction:** Three distinct institutional seals or folders joined by one formal agreement, readable at native size.

**Tracking:** Unique bloc identities, agreement terms, support continuity, breach history, lawful-action history, and final closure.

### 077.03. Prepared plot contained

**ID:** `chaosx_077_targeted_containment`

**Title and description direction:** Emphasize disabling a real prepared threat through limited action.

**Eligibility:** A player country facing a genuine Event 077 organization that has reached preparation with practical backing.

**Unlock:** Establish credible evidence, remove or neutralize its operational backing, resolve the threat without a coup or civil-war split, remove no more than 10 influence seats in handling that organization, and complete stable closure.

**Disqualifiers:** Fabricated plots, mere grievance networks, a plot canceled by an unrelated country's disappearance, and broad automatic purge removal of the organization do not qualify.

**Difficulty and visibility:** Hard, visible. It requires accurate investigation and restraint under a real threat.

**Icon direction:** A small coordinated threat marked on a document, isolated without destroying the surrounding record.

**Tracking:** Truth category, preparation state, evidence quality, organization-specific removed seats, backing loss, attempted takeover outcomes, and closure.

### 077.04. Command preserved under accusation

**ID:** `chaosx_077_command_preserved`

**Title and description direction:** Emphasize protecting a functioning command while resolving serious wartime allegations.

**Eligibility:** Evolution I is active, the country is in a real war for at least 60 days during the crisis, and two distinct available senior military figures face eligible cases.

**Unlock:** Preserve both figures' relevant service through provisional protection and credible case resolution, survive a pressure peak of at least 60, and complete stable closure without Event 077 removing an essential active command figure.

**Disqualifiers:** A figure with no active relevant role, duplicated character handles, protected genuine plotters whose cases remain unresolved, or instant debug wars do not count.

**Difficulty and visibility:** Very hard, visible when the relevant module is available. Countries without the required figures do not receive a misleading eligible indicator.

**Icon direction:** An intact command insignia over a closed investigation folder, without national symbols tied to one country.

**Tracking:** War duration, actual character roles, case identities, protection and findings, command removals, peak Paranoia, and closure.

### 077.05. Recovery after the Great Purge

**ID:** `chaosx_077_institutional_recovery`

**Title and description direction:** Emphasize rebuilding after substantial institutional loss, without praising the purge itself.

**Eligibility:** A player country has suffered a material Great Purge with at least 20 seats made vacant or nonfunctional and two impaired institutions.

**Unlock:** Stop remaining purge waves, restore at least 80 filled seats and 55 dependable seats, reach stable closure, and maintain restored function for 60 further days while beginning qualified institutional recovery.

**Disqualifiers:** Reload rollback of committed losses, debug repair, an armed split that transfers the entire problem away, or counting a new unrelated country as the recovered original does not qualify.

**Difficulty and visibility:** Very hard, visible after a qualifying purge. The challenge includes a genuine aftermath period.

**Icon direction:** A repaired but visibly marked council chair or institutional seal, with clear evidence of restoration.

**Tracking:** Committed purge losses, unique impaired institutions, wave cancellation, closure, successor continuity where lawful, and post-closure function. The aftermath tracker must survive ordinary crisis cleanup.

### 077.06. Fabrication network exposed

**ID:** `chaosx_077_fabrication_exposed`

**Title and description direction:** Emphasize tracing several false accusations to one source and accepting the finding.

**Eligibility:** A valid fabrication network links at least three materially distinct cases in one crisis.

**Unlock:** Obtain an independent review, publish the finding, clear all linked false cases, preserve or explicitly rehabilitate their living targets, and complete stable closure without concealing the report.

**Disqualifiers:** Three copies of one allegation, fabricated evidence invented by a debug action, or removing the source without resolving the victims' records does not qualify.

**Difficulty and visibility:** Rare and hard, hidden until the shared-source clue is discovered.

**Icon direction:** Several accusation sheets traced to one visibly falsified source, simplified to a legible central motif.

**Tracking:** Shared-source identity, unique cases, independent review result, publication, target outcomes, concealment choice, and closure.

### 077.07. The self-consuming inquiry stopped

**ID:** `chaosx_077_inquiry_restored`

**Title and description direction:** Emphasize ending a cycle in which investigators and replacements become targets.

**Eligibility:** Evolution III is active, with a valid case against a previous investigator and a separate case against an Event 077 replacement.

**Unlock:** Resolve both cases credibly, preserve a functioning investigation body, stop any pending automatic removal through a valid response, and complete stable closure without a Great Purge wave during the qualifying crisis.

**Disqualifiers:** A cosmetic accusation with no case, an investigator adjudicating their own allegation, or deleting the warning state without meeting its conditions does not qualify.

**Difficulty and visibility:** Very hard, hidden until both relevant case types have occurred.

**Icon direction:** A circular chain of accusation records interrupted by a sound finding, without a dense literal diagram.

**Tracking:** Investigator and replacement roles, separate case identities, credible findings, institutional function, warning resolution, and purge-wave history.

### 077.08. Lessons carried into a later crisis

**ID:** `chaosx_077_continuity_preserved`

**Title and description direction:** Emphasize preserving institutional memory across two serious crises.

**Eligibility:** The same continuing player country experiences two separate valid Event 077 instances, each with at least three material cases and a pressure peak of 60 or more.

**Unlock:** Complete stable closure in both crises, preserve at least one actual eligible figure involved in a credible cleared case across both, avoid reaccepting an already disproved source as proof, and finish the second with no more than one impaired institution.

**Disqualifiers:** Treating a reload as a second crisis, using a resurrected dead character, duplicate case farming, or erasing earlier findings does not qualify.

**Difficulty and visibility:** Long and hard, visible after the first qualifying closure.

**Icon direction:** Two dated case volumes with one intact institutional symbol linking them, with no readable generated text.

**Tracking:** Two crisis identities, continuity of country and figure, prior evidence record, case counts, peak pressure, closure records, and remaining institutional impairment.

### Asset and localisation delivery

Every achievement receives final title and description localisation from these directions, a normal 64 by 64 icon, and the standard grey and not-eligible variants. Runtime filenames follow the existing flat `gfx/achievements/` pattern with the exact achievement ID. The asset worker must inspect the existing templates and converter workflow before producing variants.
