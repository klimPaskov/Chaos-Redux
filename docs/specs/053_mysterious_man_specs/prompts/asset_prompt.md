## Identity anchor

Create one fictional ordinary-looking adult man and preserve his face, age range, hair, build, posture, and reserved expression across all five scenes. He should wear a plain late-1930s or early-1940s suit or overcoat. Modest scene-based clothing changes are allowed, but he must remain recognizable.

He is not a known historical person. Do not resemble a famous leader, actor, criminal, fictional villain, or public figure.

## Visual direction

Create period-authentic documentary photographs with the contrast, grain, crop, border treatment, and monochrome or restrained sepia look established by the inspected report-event references.

The man is calm and physically present inside a room that should be inaccessible. Officials, guards, cabinets, maps, secure doors, telephones, files, and period equipment can establish the setting. The secure room must remain readable at final size.

The images should make the access impossible without showing how it happened.
