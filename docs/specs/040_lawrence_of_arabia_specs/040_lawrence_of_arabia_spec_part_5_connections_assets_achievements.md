# Event 40: Connections, Presentation, Assets, and Achievements

## Event Log and Event Details

### History behavior

The initial Event 40 firing creates one normal History entry. It records:

- Event 40
- firing date
- Britain as sponsor context when valid
- the first target as the primary actor context where the shared logger can present it cleanly
- Minor Fire-Once classification

Later country-to-country interventions are internal baseline stages. They can create ordinary follow-up events and regional reports, but they do not create additional random-event History rows or apply extra event pacing.

A compact regional campaign summary can remain in Event Details or the active decision category. It should show resolved countries by qualitative settlement, not a debug ledger of every hidden value.

### Evolution behavior

Only these milestones enter the Evolutions log:

1. The Arab Revolt Network
2. The British Arabian System
3. Lawrence's Arabia

The evolution row must show the correct event ID, stage, tier, actor where relevant, and date. Normal intervention stages do not appear as evolutions.

### Event Details

Event Details should explain:

- Britain has recalled a man believed dead
- one Arabian government becomes the first target of an influence campaign
- later interventions can build a regional network
- the target can cooperate, bargain, resist, dismantle the network, or redirect it toward an independent project
- Lawrence's status can change the campaign

Event Details should not list hidden defection conditions, exact AI formulas, federation readiness, or secret outcome gates.

The evolution preview should describe the public premise of each evolution without claiming that activation itself changes Chaos.

## Regional reports and reaction events

Nearby Arabian governments receive bounded reaction events when the campaign changes the regional situation.

Possible reaction families:

- invite Lawrence in exchange for arms or protection
- prepare counterintelligence before becoming a target
- support a resistant neighbor
- request British guarantees
- demand proof of Britain's promises
- close borders to British agents
- allow transit or deny it
- compete to become Britain's favored partner
- join an anti-intervention conference
- seek observer status in a federation congress

Reaction events should be prioritized by proximity, existing relations, route access, and current Event 40 settlement. Do not notify every country about every small incident.

## Cross-event connections

### Malta Crusaders

Malta Crusaders can become a direct rival for influence and access in the eastern Mediterranean and Levant.

Relevant Malta actions include:

- conquering Jerusalem, Jordan, Levantine states, or strategic islands
- blocking British sea or air routes
- recruiting local allies
- offering a religious or military patronage system that competes with Britain
- attacking a British client or counter-bloc member

Event 40 responses include:

- Britain reroutes aid or reinforces clients
- Arabian governments play Britain and Malta against each other
- anti-British states accept Maltese help at the cost of a different dependency
- a federation congress excludes, negotiates with, or fights Malta

Malta is a special Chaos country and is never an ordinary Lawrence target.

### Independence Wave

A country released through Independence Wave can enter Event 40 when its stable capital and core territory belong to the Arabian registry.

The country should inherit relevant regional preparedness and British-route facts. Event 40 must not duplicate an Independence Wave carrier or create a second version of the same country.

A release that occurs during an active intervention can create:

- a new eligible target after stabilization
- a route break
- a new anti-British participant
- a successor to the active target when the old government disappears

### Secret Alliance and faction systems

A hidden alliance or revealed coalition can support or obstruct the British Arabian System.

Event 40 should read existing faction and alliance relationships before creating new ones. It should prefer extending a valid British faction or regional compact over creating overlapping faction structures.

### Great Embargo

A Great Embargo against Britain weakens Event 40 through:

- reduced equipment supply
- route costs
- convoy and fuel pressure
- lower promise credibility
- delayed infrastructure projects

A target under embargo can become more dependent on British aid or less willing to accept a relationship that brings sanctions.

### Intel Leaked

Intel Leaked can expose Event 40 contacts, routes, promises, or client records.

Exposure should create evidence and diplomatic consequences, not automatically dismantle every network. The value of the leak depends on which records exist and whether the target can act on them.

### Murder Mystery and leadership violence

Murder Mystery can affect Event 40 only through an explicit bridge.

Possible bridges include:

- assassination of a client leader
- disappearance of a key witness
- suspicion that a British or anti-British network arranged a killing
- a succession crisis that changes target eligibility or AI profile

Event 40 must not claim responsibility for an unrelated murder without evidence from the bridge.

### Famine and Migration

Event 40 can affect famine or migration only when its actions create real food, transport, siege, displacement, or border conditions.

Examples:

- railway sabotage obstructs relief
- a revolt closes a port
- civil conflict displaces civilians
- a federation opens a relief corridor
- a client blocks refugees at a border

Population loss remains owned by the exact Famine, Migration, Deaths, or combat transaction. Event 40 must not debit the same civilians again.

### Oil, embargo, colonial collapse, and regional war events

Later events can read Event 40 settlement identities:

- British client
- independent ally
- sovereign partner
- anti-British coalition member
- British Arabian System node
- federation member
- Lawrence-led government

Oil agreements, embargoes, independence crises, colonial collapse, faction wars, and regional settlement events should use these identities where relevant.

## UI presentation

### Decision category picture

Event 40 needs one static category picture for the intervention campaign.

The image should show a period intelligence and liaison network with a strong human subject. Suitable directions include Lawrence meeting Arab officers and political leaders, a guarded desert conference, or arms and papers arriving under local supervision.

The image should not become a map diagram, fake interface, lone glamour portrait, or film composition.

### Category header

The header shows:

- Lawrence's Influence
- qualitative band
- trend
- next threshold
- current access or crisis state in one short line

Use an icon, meter, threshold markers, and concise tooltips. Do not use a plain telemetry list.

### Federation presentation

Federal Authority can use an ordinary decision category header and national-spirit tooltip. A custom mechanic window is not required.

A focus inlay is also not required unless implementation evidence shows that Federal Authority must remain visible during focus selection. The default plan avoids one.

## Asset inventory

### Grounded character portrait

#### T. E. Lawrence

- Asset type: country leader, adviser, commander, and operative source identity as required by one canonical character
- Identity classification: grounded source only
- Source mode: attributed archival photograph
- Final runtime portrait: source-placeholder mode unless the user later supplies an approved styled final
- Required ownership audit: exact and variant name search across vanilla and Chaos Redux
- Forbidden sources: actor stills, reenactors, statues, illustrations as identity masters, generated faces, or reconstructed likenesses

One portrait identity can serve the canonical character consumers when the engine and role framing permit it. Do not create several inconsistent faces for different roles.

### Event and report images

Suggested report-image family:

1. The concealed recall, sourced or period-authentic documentary scene without inventing Lawrence's face
2. Lawrence with Faisal or a wider group of Arab and Allied officers, sourced archival image
3. Railway or route sabotage, sourced archival image when the event shows that exact context
4. Arab congress or regional settlement, generated only when it depicts a fictional alternate-history assembly without fabricating a real person's likeness

Historical images need source, archive, date, rights, and fit documentation.

### Flags and emblems

Final flags are required only for federation outcomes that implementation actually supports.

- British Arabia needs a distinct British-aligned federal design.
- Independent Arab Federation needs a sovereign flat design grounded in researched Arab-federation symbols without copying a modern state flag uncritically.
- Lawrence's Kingdom needs a route-specific flat design that reflects the accepted political structure and avoids fake text or invented pseudo-Arabic lettering.
- A federation-led faction or league needs an emblem when the runtime uses one.

Historical symbols require cited research before ImageGen creates the final clean flag.

### Animation and 3D boundary

Baseline Event 40 does not require animated portraits, animated category pictures, or custom 3D units.

Evolution III super-events can use static images. Animation should be proposed only if later implementation shows a clear state or identity that motion would explain.

Existing HOI4 unit models are sufficient for British officers, local forces, irregulars, and federation armies.

## Super-event packages

Evolution III creates three possible regional-order super-events. Each needs a unique slot, image, text direction, quote research, button-reaction research, audio, sound ID, settings-aware playback, documentation, and catalog alignment.

### British Arabia

Role: regional order formation.

Image direction:

- federal delegates and military officers under visible British sponsorship
- ports, aircraft, maps, or route symbols can appear as supporting context
- local political actors remain visible

Tone:

- formal, controlled, uneasy
- promises of stability alongside visible dependence

Quote direction:

- verified writing about empire, promises, tutelage, or political obligation
- no invented quote

Audio direction:

- licensed or public-domain period march, orchestral piece, or ceremonial music
- unique to this super-event

### Independent Arab Federation

Role: sovereign regional settlement.

Image direction:

- a congress, public charter, or federal proclamation led by local governments
- no lone Lawrence-centered composition

Tone:

- constitutional, determined, wary of foreign promises

Quote direction:

- verified Arab political, anti-imperial, constitutional, or independence source
- research translation and attribution carefully

Audio direction:

- licensed or public-domain regional or ceremonial music with a defensible source and recording license

### Lawrence's Kingdom

Role: rare personal political transformation.

Image direction:

- Lawrence elevated among local rulers, officers, and institutions
- the scene must show the strangeness and political tension of the settlement
- a sourced real-person identity must be preserved if Lawrence is visible

Tone:

- unusual, contested, and personal
- avoid parody and film imitation

Quote direction:

- verified Lawrence writing or another source on ambition, leadership, promises, or burden
- do not assume a famous line is authentic

Audio direction:

- unique licensed or public-domain structured music
- no film soundtrack or imitation

These super-events are not terminal world ends.

## Achievement set

All names are working labels. The implementation must create stable IDs, triggers, tracking, disqualifiers, localisation, and achievement icon triplets.

### `chaos_redux_040_uncrowned_kingdom`

Working name: The Uncrowned Kingdom

Goal: form Lawrence's Kingdom while Lawrence remains alive and no British subject relationship controls the new state.

Difficulty: very high.

Disqualifiers:

- force-trigger shortcuts when achievements exclude them
- Lawrence dies before formation
- federation formed through British Arabia and later renamed without completing the personal route

Icon direction: Lawrence's headcloth and a restrained crown over a federal charter.

### `chaos_redux_040_promises_kept`

Working name: Promises Kept

Goal: as Britain, establish the British Arabian System, create at least three durable partners, and complete the campaign without breaking a registered constitutional or autonomy guarantee.

Difficulty: high.

Icon direction: two sealed documents held over a route map, with no readable generated text.

### `chaos_redux_040_better_bargain`

Working name: A Better Bargain

Goal: as an Arabian target, accept a major British aid package, prevent dominant Influence, and finish as a sovereign armed partner or independent ally.

Difficulty: medium.

Icon direction: a rifle crate under a national lock and key.

### `chaos_redux_040_every_ledger_has_a_name`

Working name: Every Ledger Has a Name

Goal: expose the British network, turn a contact, and dismantle the active network without a civil war.

Difficulty: medium to high.

Icon direction: an open ledger, marked payments, and a magnifying lens.

### `chaos_redux_040_gold_without_chains`

Working name: Gold Without Chains

Goal: receive British financial or military support in at least two stages, then join or form the Independent Arab Federation without becoming a British subject.

Difficulty: high.

Icon direction: broken chain around a gold purse and federal seal.

### `chaos_redux_040_desert_conference`

Working name: The Desert Conference

Goal: found a counter-bloc or Arab congress with at least three independent governments and end the regional campaign through a negotiated settlement.

Difficulty: high.

Icon direction: three tents or podiums around a charter seal.

### `chaos_redux_040_rails_ports_promises`

Working name: Rails, Ports, and Promises

Goal: as Britain or British Arabia, maintain a connected intervention route through at least one port, one rail connection, and one air route while completing a later intervention.

Difficulty: medium.

Icon direction: locomotive, ship, and aircraft arranged around one route marker.
