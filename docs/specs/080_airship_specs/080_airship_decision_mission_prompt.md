# Event 080: Decision and mission prompt

Implement and audit Airship's operations using `chaos-redux-decisions-missions` and the complete seven-part source spec. Read the implementation handoff, validation matrix, and current owner contracts. Preserve the event's three headline values: Condition, progress, and people aboard.

## Phase map

Underway, expose standing orders, next-stop assistance, and a currently eligible experiment. At a stop, expose repair, the local programme, passenger handling, eligible experimentation or refit, and departure. An emergency replaces routine choices with landing, a valid diversion, or a warned continuation. Missing contact shows the bounded search work. After a crash, flight choices vanish and the rescue controller receives actual rescue work. Shared fire recovery remains in its owner interface.

The host receives only its relevant reception, service, inspection, crowd-control, or rescue choices. The United States controls expedition policy and its own payments. Other observers may view the public map without acquiring controls. Keep three to five main actions per phase, a hard maximum of six, and one to three relevant missions. Use variants and filters instead of an always-visible long decision list.

## Required services and commitments

Implement basic scheduled service once per actual visit and the complete patch repair, major repair, inspection, replacement supplies, pre-crash emergency evacuation, extended public programme, and poor-field variants from Part 2. Apply the baseline, Grand Tour, and Flying City material scaling exactly once. Show real cost and capped Condition benefit before payment.

Implement Grand Tour refit, experimental-suite installation, Flying City refit, and all six trial-family commitments from Part 5. A physical refit needs a capable actual stop and its Condition prerequisite. Trial progress uses actual completed flight steps. Only one trial is active, at most four are committed, and each family can earn its successful reward once.

Implement post-crash rescue as the separate people-based work order in Part 4. It costs 25 motorized equipment and 25 support equipment for each started group of 600 living people requiring rescue. Do not apply the pre-crash physical-form service multiplier again to this charge. Smaller assistance must expose its exact covered population and result.

## Timing and ownership

Departure is position 0. Every uninterrupted route transition takes two days, with success at 121 after 242 days. Scheduled routine stop activity fits its ordinary window. Only a declared extension adds time. Preserve partially consumed travel time through repair, inspection, search, and other holds. A mission cannot accidentally create a second route scheduler or advance the map while repair is underway.

Implement the repair, missing visitor, contact restoration, ship search, ground rescue, passenger release, and five-flight-step trial missions from Part 2. Use their real durations, response defaults, outcomes, and terminal cancellation rules. The one-day urgent response window is explicit because the ongoing flight step is only two days. Expiry must execute the displayed valid default once in multiplayer.

Transfer undelivered ground work to a new controller only through a valid consent and ownership transaction. Preserve delivered help and actual rescue credit. The original impact controller remains the war target even if the rescue controller changes. A recent host's missing-visitor work can continue after the airship departs, without restoring its flight controls.

## Costs and affordability

Use real stockpile, fuel, Political Power, and Air Experience costs where specified. Keep each action at four or fewer spendable resource types. Inline costs show at most three text-and-icon entries, with any remaining cost in the proper hover treatment. Do not insert literal resource names into icon-only cost rendering.

The available and custom cost triggers must share inclusive affordability. Exactly the displayed resources are enough. One payer is debited once, inputs are reset correctly for each shared helper, and benefits occur only after the work is actually delivered. Expose a useful AI Political Power cost hint where the framework requires it, without substituting PP for the physical price.

Cancellation, interrupted control, failed delivery, and terminal events must follow one declared refund policy. Do not permit repair benefits before a refund, repeated embarkation transfers, repeated stop rewards, or trial rerolls by reopening an action. A disabled button is not proof that the hidden effect has a valid guard.

Have `chaosx_ai_probability_auditor` inspect the actual surfaces and evaluate complete contextual pools. `ai_will_do` scores are preference evidence, not literal click probabilities. Use the full sequence matrix to test loops, expiry, recurring visits, changing hosts, and terminal cleanup.
