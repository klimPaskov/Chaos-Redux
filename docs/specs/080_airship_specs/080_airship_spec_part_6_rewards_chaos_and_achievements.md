# Event 080: Airship
## Part 6: Rewards, Chaos, and achievements

## Successful completion

A complete voyage reaches the final homecoming after all 121 progression transitions and survives arrival resolution. Delays affect the arrival date, not the right to finish. No speed-run reward encourages the player to evade the passenger and repair systems.

The proposed baseline American reward is 150 Political Power, 5 percentage points of Stability, 50 Air Experience, and one 50% single-use aviation research bonus. The technology family must be relevant and valid in the installed technology graph. It gives no ahead-of-time exemption. The research work from Experimental Flight can add at most two more single-use bonuses, for a total maximum of three research bonuses across the entire voyage.

The completed expedition also gives a 180-day American aviation-prestige benefit. Its intended effect is 10% better air-training experience gain, subject to verification of an appropriate native modifier. It is one bounded national spirit, not a stack of separate spirits for every stop and experiment. Research completion or a missing valid technology target never creates a guessed technology grant.

Grand Tour adds 50 Political Power and stronger temporary bilateral goodwill with successful participating hosts when its exposure conditions in Part 5 are met. Flying City adds 100 Political Power and 25 Air Experience when its own operating and service conditions are met. A late cosmetic upgrade receives recognition in the report but does not earn these exposure-based rewards.

Experiments retain earned knowledge if the voyage later fails, provided the result had actually been completed and transmitted or otherwise recovered before the loss. Unfinished or unrecovered data produces no reward. A crash cannot trigger the homecoming package.

## Host benefits

A successful stop means that the promised essential service or programme was delivered, the relevant people were accounted for, and the ship departed without an unresolved locally caused emergency. A flyover alone is not a successful stop for reward purposes.

At its first qualified successful stop, a host receives 10 Political Power and a two-year mutual opinion modifier with the United States, refreshed rather than stacked by later successful stops. Goodwill is the meaningful part: it is the relationship a host carries into later diplomacy, trade, and faction decisions with the USA. At voyage completion, a host that delivered at least two successful services or one major repair receives 15 additional Political Power and recognition in the final account, once. Host rewards are capped per country, not per state or popup.

A country that deliberately attacks the ship, conceals a proven destructive act, or causes the final crash through its own recorded deliberate action cannot claim the clean-host completion reward. An innocent host of a mechanical crash can still receive rescue recognition. This does not cancel the user's automatic war rule.

## Failure consequences

A domestic American crash gives a proposed immediate loss of 50 Political Power and 5 percentage points of Stability, with a 90-day mourning consequence. A foreign crash produces the same American political loss and a 10-point War Support increase associated with the declared retaliation. The impact host receives consequences from actual damage and a public response, rather than an unconditional additional penalty for being the victim of a false accusation.

The mourning consequence replaces the event's aviation-prestige spirit if needed. No country should carry more than two Airship-owned national spirits at once. State fire aftermath remains under its shared owner and does not become a third Airship spirit.

A safe abort loses 25 Political Power for the United States but preserves earned transmitted research and living people. Intact impoundment loses 50 Political Power, and the detained people go home when the voyage closes. An ocean loss gives no completion reward and reports the people presumed lost as such, not as confirmed casualties of a known cause.

## Chaos principles

The opening is unusual, but launching a civilian voyage is not itself a world crisis. There is no flat Chaos award for launch, movement, a routine stop, ordinary passenger life, eligibility, or setting an evolution flag.

Concrete coercion, public destabilisation, destruction, and failed containment can add direct Chaos. Their values are separate from the shared Deaths, war, disaster, and pollution contributions. A single crash may produce several different real consequences, but the same deaths or building damage must not be counted under several owners.

The shared mechanics document assigns Chaos to recorded deaths and wars. Airship submits the underlying facts through those systems. It does not reproduce their generic Chaos increments with an extra event-local effect.

Small local disputes and crowd accidents carry no Chaos of their own; their deaths, if any, count through the shared Deaths owner. A later genuine separate attack can be a separate cause, and the causal record must explain the distinction.

There is no special-country growth, territorial spread, world-end path, or repeatable expansion mechanic in Event 80. Those categories therefore receive no invented Chaos milestones. A fire spreading under Event 013 remains a shared effect even when the original crash was a Flying City catastrophe.

| Shared event pool and history | One Minor Fire-Once launch and its continuing voyage | No extra normal event-pool firings for steps, flavour, experiments, or rescue |

If [The Peace Movement](../084_the_peace_movement_specs/084_the_peace_movement_spec_part_12_shared_systems_and_chaos.md) governs the United States or the impact controller, the foreign-crash rule in Part 4 still declares the exact required war before any report choice. Event 84 subsequently reads that real war for its service and defense choices; it does not cancel retaliation, force a white peace, or open a second war. American rescue support still requires a valid route and accepted delivery, and the current host can rescue civilians while at war. Its AI evaluates those existing rescue choices from resources and access, without treating pacifist affiliation as humanitarian access. War-derived Chaos belongs to the shared war owner once; neither event charges a second amount for this connection. Without an active movement, ordinary crash and rescue resolution is unchanged.

Human civilians under unusual governments remain eligible for appropriate civilian interactions through the shared classifier. A genuinely nonhuman controller gets a suitable security or contact encounter instead of a normal tourist ceremony. The physical route still passes over its actual territory.
