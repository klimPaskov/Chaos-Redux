# Event 080: Airship
## Part 3: Passengers and hosts

## People aboard

Passengers give the voyage continuity. A story begun at one stop can matter at a later one. A journalist remembers a concealed accident, a family decides to leave after a frightening approach, and an engineer's earlier repair can make a later emergency more manageable. These connections use a small number of named story threads. They do not require a personality simulation for thousands of individuals.

The headline manifest separates passengers and crew in its expanded view while showing one total aboard. Proposed capacity is 500 passengers and 100 crew at baseline, 1,500 passengers and 250 crew for Grand Tour, and 9,000 passengers and 1,000 crew for Flying City. Experimental Flight uses the current physical ship's capacity until a later physical refit changes it.

Not all berths must remain filled. An emergency disembarkation reduces later crash exposure. Filling an enlarged ship requires boarding real people at a valid stop. Reaching an evolution threshold does not instantly create passengers while the ship is over the ocean.

At ordinary visits, up to 50 passengers at baseline, 150 during Grand Tour, or 500 in Flying City can exchange places with new guests, within capacity and the actual available local population. Crew are not routinely replaced by the sightseeing exchange. A specific medical, safety, or staffing incident can require a crew replacement.

## Population conservation

Every group aboard has an origin state, a recorded responsible country, a count, and a current custody status. The relevant statuses are aboard, temporarily ashore, missing but unconfirmed, rescued, settled, returned, and confirmed dead. A person belongs to one status at a time.

The required accounting model is population-backed travel custody. Boarding removes the actual group from its origin state's resident population into a shared in-transit population balance. This is movement, not death. The shared world population accounting must include people in that balance. Surviving disembarkation adds the people to an accepted destination and closes the corresponding travel balance. Death removes people from the balance and registers the actual loss once. Ground residents killed by a crash are removed from the impact state separately.

This prevents an American passenger from being charged against a foreign town's population and prevents a passenger from being removed twice after having already left a resident population. It also allows a ship to carry survivors whose home state has changed controller or suffered a disaster during the voyage.

The supplied shared-effect documentation proves exact state population loss and Deaths registration, but it does not prove a complete reusable travel-custody contract. The implementation handoff therefore requires an owner-approved shared population or migration adapter before the manifest is considered implemented. This package does not present a newly invented helper name as an existing public API.

Births add one actual person to the appropriate travelling household and the conserved population total. A birth is not migration and is never counted as a death. A stowaway must come from an actual boarding location. Uncovering a stowaway changes the manifest's known status, without creating an extra person a second time.

Illness and injury can create a bounded group requiring care. They do not create a new transmissible disease system. A missing person is not a confirmed fatality. Rescue claims use the recorded living pool. Reported casualties always match the actual population transaction, including any valid cap.

## Choosing who travels

The player can accept ordinary travellers, a diplomatic delegation, scientific guests, or a commercial party at an appropriate stop. The choice changes the next eligible stories and the possible visit or research result. It does not replace actual national leaders or advisers with invented passenger versions. A reference to a real officeholder must verify that the person exists and holds that role in the current campaign.

A large guest programme can displace ordinary berths. The tooltip shows how many places it uses. Essential crew capacity remains protected. Crowding is a temporary condition with a specific cause and resolution, such as returning an overbooked party ashore. It is not a permanent fourth management meter.

After a serious accident, passengers can request to leave. Respecting the request reduces the number exposed to later danger and can preserve trust in future reports. Refusal keeps the manifest larger but can trigger protest, hostile press, or a subsequent refusal to board. There is no generic reward for forcing civilians to continue.

## Flavour cadence

Passenger and host stories are the largest content pool. The proposal makes an eligible routine story draw on completed route steps, with a 30% base chance and at least four days between routine story notifications for the voyage. A host normally receives at most one routine popup in an eight-day window. Other small observations can appear in the voyage notes without requiring a click.

Passenger and ground-social stories should account for at least half of selected routine content over a peaceful complete voyage. Serious emergencies, evolution milestones, and a terminal outcome are outside the routine cooldown, but a related thread resolves through its own message rather than generating several duplicate popups on the same day.

A thread cannot restart until its earlier instance has closed. Most distinctive stories are once per voyage. Generic crowd, press, and sightseeing themes can repeat with a new actual host after a twenty-day family cooldown. Repeating a theme never repeats its one-time reward.

## Connecting stories without flooding the event feed

Use at most three open social threads at once. One can concern a household or missing person, one a public or diplomatic issue, and one an experiment or specialist guest. A new unrelated thread waits until a slot is free. Essential rescue records are not discarded to meet this storytelling limit.

An earlier safe medical transfer can produce a brief later report that the passenger recovered. A journalist given access to a repair can report accurately after a subsequent emergency. A host that settled a customs dispute can receive credit in the successful voyage account. These are factual consequences of recorded choices, not arbitrary cross-event bonuses.

Stories with no state-level consequence can remain personal. They do not all need Political Power, Stability, or Chaos changes. National effects are reserved for a genuinely public diplomatic success, a substantial scandal, or a major loss. Humour can appear in final options through dry bureaucracy and passenger behaviour, while death and rescue reporting remains clear.
