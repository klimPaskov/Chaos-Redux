# Event 080: Super-event research and production prompt

Use `chaos-redux-super-events`, `chaos-redux-event-assets`, the Airship source spec, and the current shared super-event framework to research and produce one rare Flying City crash presentation. Read Part 4, Part 6, Part 7, the source report, and the specialist handoffs before proposing final text or audio.

This prompt supplies a functional role only: confirmed large Flying City crash. It does not supply a final title, button, quotation, cultural remark, slogan, lyric, or recording. Unresearched final text and unverified audio rights are blockers. Do not convert the working role, an asset filename, or an achievement phrase into final localisation by convenience.

## Trigger and factual context

The physical Flying City must have been operating before the crash. The crash must cause extensive immediate state destruction and at least 5,000 confirmed direct impact and rescue deaths. Use the actual impact and immediate four-day rescue record. Capacity, unconfirmed missing people, and later unrelated fire losses do not satisfy the death threshold.

The presentation fires once for that voyage after the required facts exist. It uses the existing shared framework and its normal world distribution. It does not create a new world end or independently stop normal automatic event firing. The crash, immediate American retaliation, and subsequent shared fire remain owned by their actual mechanics.

The actual impact state, physical site type, controller at impact, present rescue controller, cause certainty, and casualty categories must be available to the writing and image work. A foreign controller may have been attacked by the United States despite an unproven accusation. Do not state that sabotage is established when the recorded cause is mechanical or weather-related.

## Text research

Spawn the configured `chaosx_super_event_text_researcher` using the actual no-history handoff workflow with a full context bundle. Research an appropriate final title direction, description, button text, and a sourced quotation or cultural remark where the framework expects one. Prefer primary or strong institutional sources and verify original wording, attribution, date, language, and the context of any quotation.

Research can consider period writing about civilian aviation, technological ambition, public spectacle, responsibility for passengers, and the consequences of an accident. It must not invent a historical Flying City, copy an unrelated dramatic quotation because it sounds suitable, or falsely attribute fictional wording to a real person.

Provide candidates with source excerpts kept within lawful quotation limits, accurate attribution, and a clear explanation of fit. Distinguish public-domain source text from a protected translation. If no supported quotation fits, use a framework-permitted original descriptive alternative after review. Never fabricate a source or disguise original writing as a quotation.

## Audio research

Spawn `chaosx_super_event_audio_researcher` with the same actual context. Research a suitable one-to-three-minute presentation within the current skill and framework limits. Verify the composition rights and the particular recording rights separately. A work's age does not prove that a modern recording is free to redistribute.

Assess period orchestral, documentary, or archival material only after checking provenance and reuse permissions. Archive footage or a museum page is a research lead, not automatic audio clearance. Do not select a specific track from memory, generate a synthetic substitute, fabricate archive provenance, or include copyrighted lyrics without an appropriate permitted use.

Deliver the source record, rights basis, acquired source file where authorised, edit plan, final timing, format, volume treatment, and reproducible conversion evidence. Coordinate the audio with the image and text so the presentation has a clear start and finish without obscuring gameplay controls.

## Image and implementation

Use the event asset specialist for a 457 by 328 image in the correct shared style. The image depicts a fictional civilian flying settlement destroyed at the actual type of location. It must not look like a nuclear attack, reuse an ordinary healthy airship scene, or place unsupported famous landmarks in the impact state.

Keep fictional imagery clearly separate from historical source references. Final art can be generated under the reviewed direction. Use the actual canonical super-event references and required runtime conversion. Supply processed pixels, final DDS, sprite binding, native-size comparison, and an image provenance or generation record.

Integrate through the existing framework without redesigning it. Coordinate ordinary crash news with the super-event so the same facts are not repeated through several simultaneous global popups. A subsequent casualty update should normally update the event record and involved-country reports unless it establishes a genuinely separate major discovery.

## Delivery and acceptance

Deliver the final researched text package, source and attribution notes, audio rights and production manifest, final image and conversion evidence, exact framework bindings, trigger fixture, once-only test, and matched in-game or tool-rendered presentation evidence. Mark every unresolved source, licence, missing tool, or untested integration explicitly.

No final quote, title, audio, or artwork was produced in the planning session. Treat this as a complete research and production assignment, not as an approved finished presentation.
