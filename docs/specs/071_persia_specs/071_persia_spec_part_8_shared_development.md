# 071 Persia: shared development branches

## Common development principles

All three routes inherit these branches. Their political institutions can alter project terms, but no route loses access to basic industrial, army, air, naval, or supply development. The opening force creates urgency for these investments because losses need replacement.

Projects improve real states, routes, production, and formations where supported. A focus that opens a project should not also secretly pay its full construction result. A completed existing asset can satisfy a requirement, but cannot produce a duplicate grant. Construction amounts scale to the country's actual base and available sites through verified project helpers.

## Home industry and repair

The first industrial branch surveys shortages in the opening force and existing army. It offers repair workshops, distributed arsenals, and a concentrated production option. The survey is information and access to choices, not a new persistent numerical mechanic.

A repair program uses support equipment, trucks, trained personnel, and factory capacity. It creates a useful maintenance and replacement base. A concentrated arsenal provides a stronger selected production path but is more vulnerable to losing its state. Distributed workshops are easier to retain after a military setback.

Later branches support heavy industry and military production. The country chooses which equipment family receives the first major investment. Infantry equipment and artillery are always practical. Armor, mechanization, and aircraft depend on the technology and industrial situation. A player can eventually develop more families, but cannot pay once and receive every production line.

The reconstruction branch becomes useful after damage. It prioritizes essential transport and production, then civil recovery. It must use the actual damage and local control state. A peaceful country cannot farm reconstruction rewards by repeatedly selecting an undamaged region.

## Rail, roads, and water

The transport branch connects the administrative center with the main industrial base, frontier supply, and maritime outlet. A Trans-Iranian corridor project can build on or complete an existing railway. The historical railway was built during 1927 to 1938, so the game must inspect the actual campaign map and date instead of always presenting it as nonexistent. [H05](071_persia_research_notes.md#h05-the-trans-iranian-railway)

A desert support branch combines transport, local water access, and maintenance. Qanat references support an irrigation and settlement theme, not a claim that underground water channels are a substitute for military railways. [H04](071_persia_research_notes.md#h04-qanats) The actual reward can improve appropriate local infrastructure or support a reconstruction project after the relevant state data is checked.

A regional corridor is completed only when its important segments work together. A supply hub without a connection does not satisfy the project. An allied route needs continuing access. A lost segment suspends the network benefit until repaired or replaced.

## Staff, training, and regular army

The army branch establishes a staff college, standard training, logistics officers, and formation repair. It uses military experience and real equipment where appropriate. It does not grant dozens of commanders with invented biographies.

A terrain fork develops either mountain approaches or desert operations first. Both remain available later. The mountain path supports engineers, mountain training, and northern logistics. The desert path supports motor maintenance, water provision, and sustained movement across long routes. Neither should apply a global bonus to terrain that was never part of the program.

A conventional army modernization branch improves production and research for infantry, artillery, anti-air, and support units. It must preserve the country's existing research choices and mutually exclusive technology paths. A research bonus is useful when the relevant technology is still available. Already completed research needs a bounded equivalent research allocation, not a duplicate technology grant.

## Air force

The opening aircraft require airfields and maintenance. The first air branch surveys operating bases, builds a practical training structure, and supports a fighter and battlefield-support force. Newly granted aircraft remain in reserve until a valid base can accept them, with the reserve and operating counts shown separately.

The player can prioritize air defense of the heartland or close support of regional campaigns. Later branches add long-range operational planning and naval support where technology and bases permit. A naval air program needs coastal access. It cannot create carrier aviation without a valid carrier program.

The air force's growth uses ordinary production and research. The event does not grant an unlimited stockpile of modern aircraft whenever the calendar advances. The starting package is the only extraordinary one-time aircraft grant.

## Navy and Gulf reach

The naval branch begins with surveying a usable port, training crews, expanding dockyard capacity, and protecting coastal traffic. It then offers escort and patrol development or a more ambitious regional fleet program.

A regional fleet requires production time, fuel, ports, and maintenance. A focus can support a bounded construction program and research, but cannot instantly create an unresearched carrier fleet. A ship design must match supported equipment and the campaign's technology.

Control of shipping is demonstrated through protected routes, naval presence, and access. Owning a coast alone does not prove dominance of the Gulf. The exact sea regions and strait behavior must be inspected in the installed game.

## Intelligence and diplomatic preparation

A small shared branch supports military observation, foreign missions, regional language expertise, and diplomatic channels. It uses existing intelligence mechanics when present. It does not invent complete enemy information without an intelligence route.

Before a major demand, the player can fund a regional assessment. The result identifies known political commitments, access obstacles, and visible military information. Uncertain information remains uncertain. An AI planning score is not presented to the player as an exact chance of victory.

Diplomatic missions can prepare a treaty, improve the terms of a charter, or secure transit. Their costs and effects should be concrete and bounded. They cannot stack unlimited relations or compel a foreign player to accept.

## Research and doctrine

The main tree uses normal research and doctrine systems. The strongest special content is the guard subunit family and its command development. Any added unlock must have a meaningful prerequisite and an intended place in the relevant technology graph.

Doctrine support should allow the player's existing approach to continue. The route can favor concentrated armor, flexible infantry, or mobile combined arms without automatically replacing every doctrine choice. A grant must respect incompatible paths.

The technology graph audit is mandatory because the opening package and later guards depend on valid equipment families. The implementation must inspect, render, and compare every affected branch and document the actual consumer of each unlock.

## Ending the opening mobilization

The temporary opening support lasts 180 days. Before it expires, the player chooses a funded replacement program, a smaller professional settlement, or an emergency extension during a serious war. The default expiry does not remove the granted divisions or reclaim their equipment.

A funded replacement program transitions the mobilization spirit into a sustainable production institution. A smaller professional settlement reduces future recruitment ambition but eases the material burden. An emergency extension lasts 90 days, requires real resources, and can be used once. It does not indefinitely suspend the costs of maintaining the army.

Failure to complete a replacement program leaves the army intact under normal supply, reinforcement, and production rules. It withholds the program's benefits. It must not instantly destroy an otherwise functioning army because a mission expired.

## Acceptance

Every development branch has a useful early choice, a paid or constructed result, and a later expansion. Projects do not exceed building limits, place assets in inaccessible states without explanation, or require a DLC-only system without a verified route. The three-spirit budget remains intact throughout development and crisis.
