# 071 Persia: achievement design

## Shared eligibility and tracking

These achievements belong to the restored Iranian country that received Event 071. Subjects cannot unlock Persian achievements for their overlord. Multiplayer eligibility follows the project's existing achievement system, with each eligible human country evaluated independently.

All listed labels are working labels. The localisation worker must write final names and descriptions from the direction below. Each achievement needs completed, grey, and not-eligible icon states under the supplied deterministic template workflow.

Use current ownership, control, valid charters, route commitments, and recorded project completion. An occupation does not count as a settled province. A friendly partner does not count as a satrapy. A force in a training queue does not count as a deployed guard. Save and reload must preserve all duration checks and first-completion records.

Debug or forced setup must follow the project's existing disqualification rules. This plan does not invent a separate global anti-cheat system. Test hooks must not accidentally unlock achievements in normal play. Once an achievement is earned, later defeat does not revoke it unless the shared framework explicitly uses a different rule.

## 071_persia_chartered_empire

Working label: Chartered Empire. Visible. Difficulty: hard.

Eligible country: Event 071 Persia on the Achaemenid route.

Unlock after completing the chartered imperial network, retaining the full homeland, and maintaining at least five valid regional satrapies for 180 continuous days. The network must include a western settlement and an eastern or northern settlement, with valid connections. Legitimacy must remain at least 60 during the final duration.

Disqualifiers include an invalid route, a subject counted without its charter, an active secession in one of the required satrapies, and loss of the required homeland or connection during the duration. The duration restarts when the condition genuinely fails.

The achievement is difficult because it requires durable administration and several functioning relationships, not simply releasing five tags. Track distinct regions so five tiny subjects carved from the same region cannot satisfy it.

Icon direction: a central imperial seal connected to five restrained regional symbols, readable at native size. Avoid a miniature political map full of unreadable borders.

## 071_persia_two_frontiers

Working label: Two Frontiers. Visible. Difficulty: hard.

Eligible country: Event 071 Persia on the Sasanian route.

Unlock after completing the command-system conclusion, a western Mesopotamian settlement, and both a northern and an eastern frontier program. All three must have working supply and settled administration for 180 continuous days. At least one qualifying defensive or regional campaign must have ended in a recognized settlement during the restoration.

Disqualifiers include an unresolved command split, an unconnected command, a temporary occupation counted as settlement, and bypassed projects that never met their actual requirements. A command can be direct or a valid frontier satrapy according to the route.

The achievement requires development on several difficult fronts and a functioning military institution. A single conquest of Baghdad cannot unlock it.

Icon direction: a central command emblem between two clearly different frontier forms, using a mountain and a fortified western approach.

## 071_persia_industrial_order

Working label: Industrial Order. Visible. Difficulty: hard.

Eligible country: Event 071 Persia on the modern route.

Unlock after completing the industrial bloc conclusion, finishing investment projects with at least three distinct partners, and maintaining a functioning maritime outlet and the major transport program for 180 days. The domestic replacement program for the principal army equipment families must be completed. At least one partner must be under a valid defense agreement.

Disqualifiers include unpaid or incomplete projects, temporary docking counted as permanent access, and clients that have exited during the duration. One project transferred between several owners counts once, not as several partner investments.

The achievement tests whether Persia built an actual productive bloc. Signing three empty diplomatic offers is insufficient.

Icon direction: an industrial wheel and port connection around a simple imperial emblem, with no tiny contract text.

## 071_persia_persepolis_restored

Working label: Persepolis Restored. Visible. Difficulty: medium-hard.

Eligible country: Event 071 Persia on any route.

Unlock after completing all Persepolis stages, the route-specific institution, and the public recognition stage, while holding the full homeland and maintaining legitimacy of at least 60 for 180 days after recognition.

Disqualifiers include site loss, skipped paid or constructed stages without factual completion, and a ceremony fired by a debug-only path. A previously completed stage can remain valid after repair if its actual institution and site are restored.

The achievement adds an enduring political requirement to a substantial site project. It is not awarded for owning the site's state when the event begins.

Icon direction: a small group of restored monumental columns with a clearly modern conservation or administrative element, avoiding a fictional complete ancient city.

## 071_persia_guard_of_the_empire

Working label: Guard of the Empire. Visible. Difficulty: hard.

Eligible country: Event 071 Persia at Evolution II or higher.

Unlock after completing an advanced guard institution, fielding at least 35,000 authorized guard manpower in valid deployed formations, and sustaining their equipment at the project's verified readiness threshold for 180 days. At least one advanced guard formation must have participated in a qualifying completed regional campaign after its creation or conversion.

The proposed readiness threshold is 90 percent of required equipment, evaluated through a supported actual unit query. The exact query is a verification gate. It cannot be replaced by a decorative national spirit claiming readiness.

Disqualifiers include over-cap recruitment, empty templates, training queues counted as deployed, duplicated personnel, and formations transferred away before the duration ends. Counting uses manpower and actual family membership, not names or division count.

The achievement requires training, production, a campaign, and sustained reinforcement. The root's free guard allocation alone does not satisfy the institution and campaign conditions.

Icon direction: one modern guard figure or emblem with a restrained command motif, distinct from a focus icon.

## 071_persia_gulf_power

Working label: Gulf Power. Visible. Difficulty: hard.

Eligible country: Event 071 Persia on any route.

Unlock after completing the Gulf political settlement, coastal readiness, and the regional fleet program. Retain the essential outlet, durable regional access, and a supplied operational fleet meeting the verified program's composition for 180 days. At least one protected transport or regional naval campaign objective must have been completed.

Disqualifiers include a landlocked state with only reserve convoys, temporary occupation of a port, a fleet that cannot legally use its base, or an unsupported abstract naval dominance flag. The final fleet composition is resolved from legal designs during implementation and documented with the naval program.

The achievement tests the complete naval development sequence. Owning one coastal state is insufficient.

Icon direction: a regional escort force and a Gulf port, simplified to a strong silhouette.

## 071_persia_imperial_guarantor

Working label: Imperial Guarantor. Visible. Difficulty: very hard.

Eligible country: Event 071 Persia at Evolution III with a completed route conclusion.

Unlock after honoring an imperial guarantee issued before the qualifying attack, achieving a settlement that preserves the protected state's agreed independence or charter, and retaining at least five distinct valid subordinate or protected states for 180 days afterward. Legitimacy must remain at least 80 during that final period.

Disqualifiers include guarantees issued after the war began, a war deliberately created through an unsupported test path, a protected state annexed by Persia in the settlement, and duplicate counting of one state under several agreements.

This requires an actual defense commitment and a durable regional order. A submission offer accepted by a weak neighbor alone cannot unlock it.

Icon direction: a central imperial shield protecting several smaller distinct emblems, without suggesting a historical flag design.

## 071_persia_restore_after_ruin

Working label: Restore After Ruin. Hidden until its genuine crisis entry. Difficulty: very hard.

Eligible country: the surviving Event 071 Iranian state after an actual fragmented-center outcome.

Unlock after recovering the full homeland, rebuilding the chosen route's central institution, completing the outstanding command or charter settlement, and maintaining legitimacy of at least 60 for 180 days. The root grant record must still show exactly one opening delivery.

Disqualifiers include a cosmetic crisis without real territorial or institutional loss, debug-only fragmentation, a second opening army grant, and a different released country claiming the original receiver's history without a supported succession contract.

The achievement recognizes recovery from a consequential defeat. It must not reward a player for briefly lowering legitimacy and paying one cheap decision.

Icon direction: a repaired imperial emblem with visible but restrained reconstruction, separate from the collapse news art.

## Evidence and icon acceptance

Each achievement receives a positive scenario, a near-miss scenario, a save and reload scenario, and a forced invalidation test. Duration boundaries must be tested immediately before and after completion. Current ownership and control are rechecked at unlock time.

The icon artist generates one original transparent subject per new achievement, then derives the grey and not-eligible states through the required deterministic workflow. Preserve the supplied completed and grey templates and red-cross overlay exactly. Final runtime triplets go directly under `gfx/achievements/` with the registered ID, `_grey`, and `_not_eligible` filenames. Do not place them in an event subfolder.
