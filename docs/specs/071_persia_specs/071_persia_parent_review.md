## Issues found and corrected during drafting

Guard recruitment initially risked hiding several equipment types in one cost label. The final design separates equipment preparation into bounded batches and personnel training into a second stage. Army experience is charged once. Capacity is reserved when preparation begins and incomplete preparation has an expiry policy.

Guard capacity was initially described beside the claim that legitimacy was the only public numerical mechanic. The final interface budget explicitly counts guard manpower capacity as the second custom reading. It remains distinct from the single main legitimacy meter.

Later evolution capacity increases could have been read as automatic free growth. Part 9 now states that floor and share upgrades require the paid guard institution and do not grant people. The opening tier remains recorded separately.

The mountain component also needs native special-forces capacity, independently of the custom guard ceiling. Part 9 now requires a bounded legal opening entitlement and normal later recruitment, without an unlimited exemption or another national spirit.

The one-time opening grant separates deployed manpower and material from additional reserves. Aircraft in operating wings and aircraft in reserve are distinguished. Fuel and capped values report actual delivered quantities rather than pretending overflow was retained.

The existing Iranian DLC restoration concern is explicit. The design does not assume a blank generic Iran or permit a duplicate completed empire. The exact installed-tree transition remains an inspection gate.

The final goal prompt is within the required 3,500 to 4,000 character range. Planning labels, diagrams, role handoffs, and source receipts are not represented as final localisation, actual agent reports, or game validation.
