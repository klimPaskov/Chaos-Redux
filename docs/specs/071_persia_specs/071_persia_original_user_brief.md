# Persia

## Catalog entry

- Event ID: `71`
- Event name: Persia
- Type: Minor Fire-Once
- Status: To Be Reworked
- Chaos level: 1
- Cluster: Formables
- Cluster role: Medium member

## Core idea

> Iran declares the restoration of the Persian Empire and begins rebuilding an imperial state stretching far beyond modern Iranian borders.

Event 71 turns Iran into a major imperial formable with a large dedicated focus tree, historical territorial ambitions, unique military content, and several different interpretations of what a restored Persia should become.

The event creates an immediate geopolitical problem rather than only changing Iran's name and giving it claims.

## Availability

The event requires a valid Iranian state to exist.

Iran must control at least part of its normal homeland and must not already have completed an equivalent Persian imperial restoration.

If Iran has been reduced to a minor remnant, the event can still fire. The restoration begins from whatever Iranian territory remains.

## Immediate restoration

When the event fires:

- Iran becomes the **Persian Empire**
- the country receives a new imperial identity and flag set
- a large Persian focus tree becomes available
- historical Persian territorial ambitions are activated
- surrounding countries receive news and diplomatic reactions
- Persia immediately receives a **strong army package**

The current Iranian government does not automatically disappear. The focus tree determines what kind of restored empire ultimately emerges.

## Immediate imperial army

The Persian Empire must receive a strong military force immediately when Event 71 fires.

This is not a small spirit or temporary combat bonus. Persia receives actual military assets so the restored empire can begin acting as a serious regional power immediately.

The package can include:

- newly raised divisions
- infantry equipment
- support equipment
- artillery
- trucks
- tanks where appropriate
- aircraft
- fuel
- trains and convoys
- experienced officers
- command power and military experience
- a limited number of elite formations

The package should respect the current campaign and avoid creating invalid equipment types, but it is intentionally generous.

The army scales with the active Event 71 Evolution when the event fires.

### Baseline army

At baseline, Persia receives a strong conventional army large enough to threaten nearby regional powers and immediately pursue the restoration of the Persian heartland.

### Evolution I army

At Evolution I, the opening force is substantially larger and better supplied.

It can include more divisions, stronger artillery and motorization, larger stockpiles, more aircraft, and additional elite formations.

### Evolution II army

At Evolution II, Persia begins with a major-power-quality regional army.

The opening package can include stronger armor, mechanization, air support, larger reserves, and more experienced commanders.

### Evolution III army

At Evolution III, Persia begins with an enormous imperial mobilization.

It should be capable of opening several regional campaigns without first spending years building a basic military.

The force can include large elite guard formations, strong armored and mechanized elements, major air support, deep equipment stockpiles, and enough logistics to sustain early imperial expansion.

The immediate army does not replace the normal Persian military tree. It gives the empire a powerful opening, while focuses and later conquests determine whether it can sustain that strength.

## Persian territorial system

The event uses a broad historical Persian territorial map rather than one static set of generic war goals.

Territories should be divided into several historical importance bands.

### Persian heartland

The Iranian plateau and the immediate historical Persian homeland.

These are the territories the empire considers indispensable.

### Imperial heartlands

Regions repeatedly controlled by major Persian empires, including parts of:

- Mesopotamia
- the Caucasus
- Afghanistan
- Central Asia
- the Persian Gulf
- eastern Anatolia
- the Levant

### Greater imperial claims

The widest historical ambitions associated especially with the Achaemenid Empire.

These can extend toward:

- Egypt
- western Anatolia
- the eastern Mediterranean
- Central Asia
- the Indus region
- other territories historically incorporated into the largest Persian imperial formations

Not every route needs to pursue the maximum historical borders.

## Immediate wars

The restored empire receives immediate hostility toward countries holding the most important Persian restoration territories.

Countries controlling Persian heartland territory can be attacked immediately.

Other territorial wars are opened dynamically through the focus tree rather than declaring war on half the world on the first day.

If one country controls several required regions, Persia should deal with that country as one strategic enemy rather than generating redundant war content.

## Imperial legitimacy

The focus tree should track Persia's success in rebuilding a recognizable empire.

Important sources include:

- recovering Iranian territory
- controlling Mesopotamia
- reaching the Caucasus
- securing the Persian Gulf
- controlling important historical capitals
- defeating major regional rivals
- establishing satrapies or subjects
- completing imperial restoration focuses

A successful Persia gradually stops looking like an ambitious regional state and begins functioning as an actual imperial power.

## Main focus-tree routes

The tree should be large and highly replayable.

### The Achaemenid Legacy

The widest territorial route.

Persia openly invokes Cyrus, Darius, Xerxes, and the ancient empire.

This route emphasizes:

- enormous historical territorial ambition
- restoration of ancient imperial capitals
- a multinational empire
- satrapies
- tolerance of subordinate peoples
- roads and imperial infrastructure
- control of the eastern Mediterranean
- expansion toward Egypt and the Indus
- revival of the title **King of Kings**

This is the route most capable of rebuilding the greatest historical territorial extent of Persia.

### The Sasanian Restoration

A more militarized imperial route.

It focuses heavily on:

- Mesopotamia
- Armenia and the Caucasus
- the Persian Gulf
- Central Asia
- eastern frontier warfare
- elite armies
- heavy cavalry traditions
- fortified imperial frontiers
- conflict with major powers controlling Persia's western approaches

The restored Sasanian state should feel more centralized and militarized than the Achaemenid route.

### The New Persian Empire

A modern imperial route that uses historical legitimacy without trying to recreate one specific ancient dynasty exactly.

It focuses on:

- modern industry
- centralized administration
- mechanized armed forces
- control of strategic oil regions
- regional client states
- Persian economic dominance
- building a modern imperial bloc

This route can be used by governments that do not fit an ancient monarchist restoration.

## Satrapies

Persia can organize conquered territory into **satrapies** rather than annexing everything directly.

Satrapies are imperial subordinate states with their own governments but strong obligations to Persia.

They can provide:

- manpower
- resources
- equipment
- military access
- tribute
- divisions
- regional administration

The empire can choose between direct integration and satrapy rule depending on the territory.

A heavily decentralized Persian Empire can become enormous without directly administering every conquered state.

## Imperial capitals

The focus tree should make historical Persian centers strategically important.

Possible imperial centers include:

- Tehran
- Isfahan
- Persepolis
- Ctesiphon where controlled
- other important historical Persian seats

Restoring major sites can grant legitimacy, development, decisions, and unique national bonuses.

**Persepolis** should receive especially strong restoration content.

## The Immortals

Persia can restore **The Immortals** as a unique elite military formation.

They should be substantially stronger than ordinary infantry and limited enough to remain distinctive.

Different imperial routes can reinterpret them:

- ceremonial royal guard
- elite mechanized formation
- enormous imperial guard
- special mountain and desert force

The player should be able to expand and modernize the Immortals through the focus tree.

## Imperial army

Persia should receive extensive military content around the terrain surrounding Iran.

Important specializations can include:

- mountain warfare
- desert warfare
- mechanized operations
- cavalry traditions
- heavy armor
- elite guards
- long-distance logistics
- fighting across the Caucasus and Central Asia

The military tree should support the enormous geographical range of the restored empire.

## Persian Gulf

Control of the Persian Gulf becomes a central strategic objective.

Persia can pursue:

- Gulf naval bases
- oil infrastructure
- control of important ports
- naval expansion
- pressure on foreign-controlled Gulf states
- domination of strategic shipping

A strong Persian Empire should eventually be capable of becoming a serious naval power rather than remaining permanently land-focused.

## Evolution I: The Satraps Return

- Chaos requirement: `200+`.

The satrapy system becomes substantially deeper.

Persia can establish specialized imperial subjects with different roles, including:

- military satrapies
- resource satrapies
- frontier satrapies
- autonomous historical kingdoms
- heavily controlled imperial provinces

Satraps can become powerful political figures.

Loyal satraps strengthen the empire. Ambitious satraps can create court intrigue, refuse demands, compete with one another, or become dangerous during periods of Persian weakness.

The immediate army granted when Event 71 fires at this Evolution is substantially stronger than the baseline force.

## Evolution II: The Immortals

- Chaos requirement: `400+`.

The revived Immortals become much more than an elite ceremonial formation.

Persia unlocks several powerful Imperial Guard formations and dedicated military development.

The emperor can increasingly build the army around elite formations whose loyalty is tied directly to the imperial state.

The strongest versions of the Immortals should become some of the best conventional units available to an ordinary human country.

The immediate Event 71 army at this Evolution becomes a major-power-quality regional force.

## Evolution III: King of Kings

- Chaos requirement: `600+`.

Persia's imperial ideology expands beyond recovering historical territory.

A sufficiently successful Persian Empire can claim the right to dominate the entire surrounding international order.

The late focus tree can unlock:

- forced submission of neighboring kingdoms and governments
- broader satrapy creation
- demands for tribute
- intervention in wars involving Persian subjects
- imperial guarantees
- establishment of a Persian-led international bloc
- claims to universal regional supremacy

A restored Persia can eventually build an imperial system extending well beyond territories it intends to annex directly.

If Event 71 first fires at this Evolution, the opening Persian military package is enormous and should immediately establish Persia as one of the strongest military actors in its region.

## Losing the empire

The restored Persian state should remain vulnerable to collapse.

Major military defeat, loss of the Iranian heartland, or destruction of imperial legitimacy can cause:

- satrapies to break away
- conquered peoples to revolt
- rival claimants to emerge
- the empire to fragment
- restoration routes to fail

A huge Persian Empire should therefore be powerful but capable of collapsing spectacularly if its central state is destroyed.

## AI direction

AI Persia should choose its imperial route based partly on its government and situation.

It should:

- prioritize reunifying the Iranian heartland
- avoid suicidal simultaneous wars when possible
- use satrapies for distant territory
- pursue historical regions dynamically
- build logistics before extremely distant campaigns
- recognize opportunities created by weak regional powers
- become increasingly aggressive as imperial legitimacy rises

## Presentation

The opening should receive a major news event announcing the restoration of Persia.

Later news can cover:

- restoration of Persepolis
- creation of the first satrapy
- revival of the Immortals
- conquest of Mesopotamia
- restoration of major imperial borders
- proclamation of the King of Kings
- establishment of a Persian imperial bloc
- catastrophic imperial collapse if the restoration fails

The visual identity should draw heavily from Persian imperial imagery while adapting it to whichever route the country follows.
