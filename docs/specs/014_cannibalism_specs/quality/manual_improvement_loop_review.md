# Manual Improvement-Loop Review

## Status

Historical pre-implementation design review. Its depth boundaries remain accepted, but its implementation-status language is superseded by `package_status.md`, the two accepted closure addenda, and the final audit handoffs. The implemented closure tranche used the project improvement-loop workflow and promoted accepted behavior into the source specifications.

## Playable promise review

The event now supports four complete campaign scales:

- one-country wartime breakdown
- regional ritual-cell crisis
- territorial war against islands, communes, and warlords
- global Hannibal or Wendigo Hannibal endgame

Each scale changes play. The design does not rely on more popups as a substitute for mechanics.

## Depth findings and resolutions

### Baseline risk: too many response buttons

Finding:

A large containment system could become a static decision store.

Resolution:

The category uses phases, selected targets, one major mission per family, active caps, and replacement of obsolete actions. Logistics, formation control, investigation, legal response, and humane containment have different costs and outcomes.

### Evolution risk: ordinary stages mislabeled as evolutions

Finding:

The event contains many escalation stages.

Resolution:

Only ritualization, organized network, and global reveal are true evolutions. Field evidence, territorial fracture, convergence, unification, and transformation countdown remain baseline or route stages.

### Warlord risk: shared tree becomes generic

Finding:

Reusable tag slots and one base tree could erase country identity.

Resolution:

Four origin archetypes receive distinct starting problems, units, decisions, AI, idea lifecycles, and visible overlay branches. Hierarchy, Larder, network, and expansion routes interact with origin.

### Hannibal risk: reveal driven only by chaos

Finding:

A chaos threshold alone would make the reveal feel arbitrary.

Resolution:

Reveal readiness requires multiple independent actors, territory, consumed population, network routes, a viable host, and high chaos. A convergence mission gives final counterplay.

### Mystery risk: hidden identity leaks through supporting surfaces

Finding:

Achievements, scenario labels, UI, portraits, super-event defaults, and event-log previews could expose the final leader.

Resolution:

The package includes a surface-by-surface secrecy matrix, hidden achievement rules, neutral scenario types, pre-reveal asset bans, and a dedicated completion-audit test.

### Final-route risk: ordinary balance weakens the concept

Finding:

Conservative modifiers would fail the user's special-chaos-country direction.

Resolution:

Completed Hannibal routes gain structural military, recruitment, supply, population, cell, naval, and enemy-collapse power. Wendigo Hannibal keeps every ordinary bonus, every existing Wendigo system, new recruitment, anchor progression, and an effectively undefeatable terminal form.

### World-end risk: no final counterplay

Finding:

An instant terminal switch would remove agency.

Resolution:

Ordinary Hannibal has pre-terminal Larder, capital, loyalty, and network targets. Wendigo Hannibal has visible anchor states and a transformation countdown. Counterplay ends only after terminal lock.

### Population risk: Event 10 duplication

Finding:

Cannibal islands could duplicate Death's wasteland behavior.

Resolution:

Event 14 uses gradual actor-driven population loss, finite Larder yield, reclaimable states, and long recovery. Death wastelands provide no Larder.

### Presentation risk: gore without mechanic clarity

Finding:

A large horror asset package could become spectacle detached from play.

Resolution:

Every major animated or static asset maps to a phase, value, route, state, leader, unit, or super-event. Gore remains mandatory, while real atrocity photography is excluded from final fictional art.

### Cultural risk: primitive warlords and Wendigo imagery

Finding:

The concept could reproduce racialized cannibal stereotypes or detach Wendigo imagery further from living traditions.

Resolution:

Any ordinary country can host the event. Warlord clothing is invented from hides, raw cloth, bone, and scavenged military gear rather than copied regalia. The alternate branch reuses only existing Chaos Redux Wendigo language and makes no authenticity claim.

## Anti-bloat decisions

The review recommends against adding:

- a fourth or fifth evolution
- cluster membership
- more than eight reusable warlord slots before implementation proves a need
- a separate global anti-cannibal faction by default
- a second hidden supreme leader
- a parallel food currency beyond Larder Stores
- a separate world-end for every hierarchy route
- super-events for ordinary local containment
- a unique full focus tree for every reusable warlord slot
- more scenario types that only duplicate intensity settings

These additions would increase maintenance without creating a distinct player decision.

## Final disposition

The design proceeded to implementation without the broad expansions rejected above. Repository exploration confirmed the original-ZZZ Wendigo identity, reusable tags, GUI precedents, and state-selection helpers.

Implementation, asset production, super-event research, catalog alignment, and the accepted focus-closure remediation are represented in the current package. Final repository completion status belongs to `package_status.md` and `event014_final_completion_audit_2026-07-13.md` rather than this historical review.
