## Route lock rules

- AI cannot choose hidden-leader branch before Evolution II seed strength exists and cannot reveal it before Evolution III network strength allows it.
- AI cannot exploit cult terror unless ideology, desperation, chaos, or route flags permit it.
- AI cannot launch island missions without naval access unless a land route exists.
- AI cannot form Last Table unless it has real territory and active Evolution III conditions.
- AI cannot keep obsolete decisions after containment or defeat cleanup.
- AI must stop cannibal country focus pursuit if annexed, puppeted in an invalid way, or tag origin becomes invalid.

## Power behavior

Cannibal AI must not be tuned like a timid minor country. Early cannibal actors should raid, survive, and punish exposed neighbors. Mature actors should snowball through ports, hunting grounds, absorbed cult nodes, and strong focus rewards. The Wendigo fusion branch should be treated as terminal and functionally unbeatable.
