# Event 014 Cannibalism, hidden unifier and terminal fusion

## Evolution II sealed-leader seeding

Evolution II creates the hidden supreme leader inside Event 014. The player should see the network becoming coordinated through indirect signs. Orders appear in separate garrisons with matching phrasing. Island communes begin using the same symbols without visible contact. Captured cult couriers carry carved marks, body-part tallies, and scraps of repeated doctrine. Remote packs start refusing local warlord names and describe a single hunger beyond the sea or beyond the front.

The true name must not appear before the final reveal. Pre-reveal text may mention an unseen patron, a high voice, an old commander, the first hunger, the eater behind the camps, or another indirect in-world label. These are writing directions, not final localisation.

Evolution II should still support lesser pack chiefs before unification. These are generic cannibal warlords, visually framed as bloody bald men wearing tribal, scavenged, or improvised ritual clothing. They can lead islands, communes, prisons, or occupied corridors. They should feel dangerous but temporary. They are not the final unifier.

## Evolution III reveal and unification

Evolution III becomes the reveal stage only when the network is strong enough. The reveal should require a serious threshold, such as several infected states, multiple countries with active outbreaks, one or more cannibal island or commune actors, repeated failed containment, high chaos, and enough network power from courier, ritual, prison, and island systems.

When the gate opens, the hidden leader reveals himself and unites the cannibal actors into one country or dominant terminal identity. Existing cannibal countries, islands, communes, pack chiefs, and infiltration nodes should be absorbed, subordinated, annexed, or violently purged. The leader becomes the ruler of the unified country. This is a real country transformation, not only a trait or event flag.

The reveal should have a super-event role, generated gore-heavy image direction, final leader portrait, animated portrait or overlay, unified flag, and a shift in AI behavior. After reveal, the leader name may appear in player-facing text because the secret has become public.

## Wendigo fusion alternate world-end branch

If the Wendigo country exists when the reveal or terminal unification threshold is reached, the cannibal network and the Wendigo country should fuse into one terminal actor. The player should not see the true name before this branch fires. The implementation-only reveal identity is kept in the reveal identity note.

The fusion branch must do all of the following:

- change the leader portrait to a new supernatural fusion portrait
- use a static fallback plus an animated portrait or overlay built from real source frames
- inherit the strongest cannibal country buffs and route effects already earned
- gain access to Wendigo units
- unlock training decisions or focus rewards for Wendigo units
- absorb or subordinate cannibal tags and Wendigo territory safely
- fire a distinct alternate world-end super-event
- set terminal world-end state and world-threat state
- make the final country functionally impossible to defeat after the lock

This branch is not balanced for ordinary play. It is a late terminal payoff. Counterplay belongs before the branch locks, through early containment, disruption of island networks, destruction of courier chains, killing pack chiefs, preventing the reveal gate, or defeating the Wendigo country before fusion becomes possible.

## Strength and tuning floor

The unified cannibal country and the fusion branch must not receive conservative values. Weak values would contradict the event identity and the planning standard for special chaos countries. Ordinary countries can fight and contain early outbreaks. Once the sealed unifier reaches the final reveal, the route should become a severe world threat. Once the fusion branch locks, the terminal country is destined to take over the world.

The cannibal focus tree should use strong rewards with real consequences. It should unlock elite units, severe state modifiers, powerful recruitment loops, terrifying army bonuses, hunting-ground economy tools, population-consumption systems, real expansion decisions, extreme AI aggression, and world-end progress. Small modifiers can support those systems, but they must not be the main payoff.

Suggested strength direction for final route planning:

- final unified country army bonuses should be major and visible, not tiny percentage adjustments
- terminal recruitment should convert captured population and destroyed garrisons into forces or buffs
- state consumption should feed deaths, chaos, and local collapse
- conquered states should become hunting grounds with severe local penalties and strong cannibal sustainment
- focus payoffs should unlock decisions, units, modifiers, claims, absorption effects, and world-end progress
- fusion branch should add Wendigo unit access, supernatural movement, extreme attrition pressure, severe morale damage to enemies, and harsh local depopulation mechanics

## Asset and text safety

The package needs the following visible assets:

- final reveal leader portrait, generated fictional portrait, gore required
- final reveal animated portrait or overlay, with real source frames, static fallback, frame sheet, and DDS handoff
- fusion portrait, generated supernatural portrait, gore required
- fusion animated portrait or overlay, with real source frames, static fallback, frame sheet, and DDS handoff
- unified country flag and cosmetic identity assets
- fusion country flag and cosmetic identity assets
- final reveal super-event image and fusion world-end super-event image, generated fictional gore-heavy scenes
- icons for elite cadres, cannibal unification, Wendigo unit training, fusion hunger doctrine, and terminal world-end progress

Pre-reveal assets, sprite names, file names, achievement ids, focus ids, and visible labels should use sealed wording. The true name may appear only in implementation-only internal comments or post-reveal localisation gated behind the reveal, and in the implementation-only reveal identity note.

## Acceptance rules

- Event 014 creates the hidden leader during Evolution II.
- No player-facing pre-reveal text displays the true name from the implementation-only reveal identity note.
- Evolution III reveals the leader only when the network is strong enough.
- The reveal unites cannibal actors into one country or dominant terminal identity.
- Generic cannibal warlords can exist before unification but cannot replace the final reveal.
- If the Wendigo country exists at reveal or terminal selection, the fusion branch takes priority.
- The fusion branch changes portrait, unlocks Wendigo units, allows Wendigo unit training, inherits cannibal buffs, and adds terminal supernatural buffs.
- Final cannibal and fusion route values are severe, visible, and intentionally overpowered after their gates.
