# Event 014 Cannibalism, Part 5: Focus Tree Architecture

## Tree strategy

Event 14 needs three focus surfaces:

1. A shared local warlord tree with strong origin overlays and route variation.
2. A full unified Hannibal tree after the reveal.
3. A Wendigo Hannibal overlay or transformed tree after the alternate merge.

The implemented node counts are exact:

- local warlord tree: 72 focuses
- unified Hannibal tree: 108 focuses
- Wendigo Hannibal overlay: 28 focuses

Every focus needs its own focus-specific icon direction. Idea and decision icons must not be resized focus icons.

## Local warlord tree

### Opening trunk: survive the first encirclement

Purpose:

- establish capital and supply
- turn the first commune into a country
- appoint the warlord and first lieutenants
- secure the first Larder
- identify the origin archetype
- open recruitment and emergency repair decisions

Key gameplay:

- starting units depend on origin and local captured equipment
- opening choices solve immediate starvation or military collapse
- the trunk reveals the public country identity without exposing Hannibal

The trunk should branch early into hierarchy, origin, and Larder development rather than remaining a long vertical chain.

### Hierarchy route family

#### Personal tyranny

Narrative role:

The warlord kills or subordinates every rival captain and turns the country into his personal hunting ground.

Mechanical role:

- elite guard
- leader trait upgrades
- faster purges and decisions
- centralized Larder
- lower commander count
- high succession risk

Anchor groups:

- seize the knives
- break the captains
- build the personal guard
- claim the first share
- name provincial servants
- final personal dominion

Payoff:

A very strong warlord and Bone Guard, with decisions that execute rival officers for Larder and temporary Frenzy. The route should become vulnerable if the leader is captured or killed.

AI:

Chosen by suspicious, hoarding, or militarily dominant warlords.

#### Feast council

Narrative role:

Local captains formalize shares, districts, recruitment, and disputes.

Mechanical role:

- more commanders
- better administration
- state integration
- council vote decisions
- slower emergency action
- faction conflict

Anchor groups:

- divide the first table
- recognize the captains
- assign feeding districts
- settle the challenge rites
- council of hosts

Payoff:

A stable regional state that can absorb communes and field several specialized armies.

AI:

Chosen by large multi-state countries, Prison Hosts with many factions, and warlords lacking personal dominance.

#### Pack-captain confederacy

Narrative role:

Independent warbands cooperate through raids and shared prey.

Mechanical role:

- mobile warfare
- decentralized recruitment
- resistance to leader decapitation
- weak industry and planning
- strong anti-absorption branch

Anchor groups:

- free the captains
- mark the hunting grounds
- share captured roads
- common raid season
- no single mouth

Payoff:

A fast, dispersed military state that is difficult to destroy but difficult to unify.

AI:

Chosen by March Hosts and defiant personalities.

### Larder economy branch

This branch exists for every hierarchy route.

Early groups:

- inventory captured stores
- secure prisoners and livestock
- restore workshops
- create battlefield recovery teams
- establish Larder accounting

Middle groups:

- route-specific Larder systems
- equipment stripping
- state consumption controls
- captured industry conversion
- feeding transport

Late forks:

#### Rapid consumption

- immediate Larder and recruitment
- severe population loss
- rapid state exhaustion
- high Frenzy
- short campaign horizon

#### Managed herds

- slower population consumption
- stronger occupation control
- better long-term Larder
- greater resistance and revolt risk

#### Mobile Larder

- trains, trucks, ships, and prisoner columns
- high transport and escort burden
- supports expansion outside core territory

Payoff:

The branch changes how the country wages war and how quickly controlled states die. It must not be a set of small production modifiers.

### Military branch

#### Warband discipline

- convert irregulars into Feast Cohorts
- officer training through captains
- reduce uncontrolled consumption
- improve planning and coordination

#### Origin specialists

- Island Reavers
- Siege Eaters
- Carrion Riders or mobile columns
- Prison Columns

The origin overlay determines the available specialist and related decisions.

#### Bone Guard

- limited elite formation
- expensive Larder and equipment
- leader protection
- strong urban and breakthrough performance

#### Battlefield harvest

- gain capped Larder and equipment from major victories
- anti-loop limits prevent repeated farming from the same losses

#### Final war doctrine

Route-specific capstone:

- tyrant route creates a disciplined personal army
- council route creates coordinated host armies
- confederacy route creates distributed raiding columns

### Origin overlay branches

Each origin gets a visible side branch with several focus groups and a clear payoff.

#### Island Host overlay

- repair ports
- seize local boats
- convoy ambushes
- naval reconnaissance
- landing cadres
- hidden anchorage network
- final archipelago hunt

Payoff:

Sustained naval raiding, island integration, and strong amphibious operations.

#### Siege Commune overlay

- fortify feeding districts
- tunnel networks
- captured municipal workshops
- hospital and prison control
- night sorties
- break the siege or turn the city into a fortress

Payoff:

Extreme urban defense and assault, with a decision family for relief-column ambushes.

#### March Host overlay

- seize trucks and horses
- mobile kitchens
- depot raids
- rail sabotage
- pursuit doctrine
- abandon exhausted territory
- final moving front

Payoff:

Fast expansion and the ability to relocate the capital and primary Larder under pressure.

#### Prison Host overlay

- unite guards and prisoners
- arm the cells
- transfer-train infiltration
- internal purge or reconciliation
- penal assault cadres
- prison network expansion

Payoff:

Powerful infiltration and mass recruitment, balanced by internal faction risk.

### Expansion branch

Early expansion:

- raid neighboring states
- mark supply hubs
- seize prisons and depots
- create temporary claims from network intelligence

Middle expansion:

- target weakened neighboring countries
- support foreign cells
- create new communes
- establish corridor between warlord states

Late expansion:

- regional Host identity
- subjugate or absorb nearby warlords
- prepare for network convergence

Postwar handling:

- controlled states enter consumption stages
- new territories begin as claims or occupation zones
- integration decisions depend on Larder, cell strength, and local resistance
- cores are earned only in territory fully reorganized by the cult system

### Terror and infiltration branch

- protect foreign cells
- train Network Cadres
- prisoner-route intelligence
- false surrender operations
- fear campaigns
- enemy officer corruption
- synchronized sabotage

Payoff:

The country can seed cells and weaken fronts before invasion. The branch becomes stronger during Evolution II.

### Network branch

Hidden before Evolution II.

#### Alignment path

- accept common symbols
- standardize ranks
- open courier routes
- share Larder
- coordinate offensives
- prepare submission

Payoff:

Strong network reinforcement and favorable treatment during Hannibal's reveal.

#### Manipulation path

- copy shared doctrine
- hide local strength
- divert courier routes
- steal recruits and Larder
- bargain with rival warlords

Payoff:

Can become unification host or preserve autonomy through leverage.

#### Defiance path

- reject common orders
- execute couriers
- destroy alignment cells
- fortify against other warlords
- claim independent supremacy

Payoff:

Strong anti-absorption tools and a war against Hannibal's aligned network after reveal.

### Local late-game payoffs

A local tree should answer what the player does after regional survival.

Possible end states:

- become strongest unification host
- remain independent and fight the network
- dominate an island chain
- create a siege kingdom around a major city
- build a mobile continental Host
- control a prison and infiltration empire

No local route should end with a small modifier and nothing else.

## Unified Hannibal tree

### Opening convergence branch

Purpose:

- establish the revealed leader
- choose the capital and host identity
- resolve immediate warlord submissions
- centralize or federate network territory
- unlock the full global mechanic

Key groups:

- reveal the command
- summon the warlords
- settle the host question
- absorb communes and islands
- establish the first continental command

The opening should quickly connect to warlord disposition, state economy, military integration, and global expansion.

### Warlord disposition route family

#### Keep the lieutenants

- retain warlords as commanders and governors
- use regional origin bonuses
- risk faction rivalry
- unlock a council or hierarchy mechanic

Payoff:

Broad commander roster, regional recruitment, and strong occupation administration.

#### Break the warlords

- purge or sacrifice local leaders
- centralize all Larder
- create loyal Bone Guard officers
- short-term instability and rebellion wars

Payoff:

Highest central authority and strongest direct Hannibal bonuses.

#### Chain the rivals

- keep warlords as disposable servants and hostage governors
- use loyalty, fear, and tribute decisions
- strong foreign campaign flexibility
- high internal management burden

Payoff:

Powerful subject-like regional system and frequent loyalty crises.

### Supreme hierarchy branch

#### One command

A centralized military state with standardized ranks, training, and Larder quotas.

#### Many jaws

A confederated global Host where regional warlords retain armies under Hannibal's strategic command.

#### Ritual administration

A formal cult state that turns feeding, recruitment, and punishment into institutions.

These internal structures can use institutional names. The public country name remains a country name.

### Continental Larder branch

Early:

- central accounting
- state classification
- transport and storage
- captured industry boards

Middle forks:

- rapid depopulation and military surge
- managed long-term exploitation
- mobile global prisoner routes
- battlefield conversion

Late:

- continents become supply regions
- conquered capitals become network anchors
- population consumption directly supports army reinforcement and special-unit recruitment
- final route eliminates most ordinary supply constraints

The branch should include strong map and production effects. Ideas can support those effects.

### Army branch

#### Integrate the warbands

- convert local templates
- standardize equipment
- preserve origin specialists

#### Cannibal legions

- high-organization main infantry
- strong recovery and reinforcement
- expensive Larder and equipment

#### Bone Guard command

- elite formations
- leader protection
- capital assault

#### Enemy collapse doctrine

- fear and surrender pressure
- organization damage
- retreat disruption
- prisoner capture

#### Terminal army capstone

The completed army must be visibly overpowered:

- major supply-use reduction
- exceptional organization recovery
- strong breakthrough and attack
- rapid reinforcement from Larder
- enemy penalties in active consumption states
- special tactics and commander traits

### Navy branch

The unified country inherits Island Host knowledge.

- captured shipyards
- raider flotillas
- prison hulks
- convoy hunting
- amphibious feeding columns
- silent anchorage network
- global island integration

Payoff:

A credible ocean-crossing threat that can move Larder and units. It should not be blocked permanently by weak starting naval technology.

### Air branch

- capture and repair aircraft
- terror reconnaissance
- supply interdiction
- airborne cell insertion where engine support allows
- battlefield recovery marking
- fear bombing and transport attacks

Payoff:

Air power supports network spread and front collapse rather than becoming a generic air-stat branch.

### Intelligence and cell branch

- global courier network
- foreign formation infiltration
- prison and port cells
- officer corruption
- false surrender operations
- synchronized uprising missions
- hide cells during retreat

Payoff:

Hannibal can weaken major countries before invasion and reactivate locally cured countries through real external operations.

### Continental expansion branch

Regional campaign groups should be selected dynamically from borders, population, supply routes, and world powers.

Tools:

- terror ultimata
- cell-backed border incidents
- claims on network anchor regions
- global war goals after terminal progression
- absorption of cannibal allies
- destruction of anti-cannibal coalition hubs

Postwar:

- states enter consumption and integration stages
- ordinary cores are not granted instantly across huge foreign regions
- terminal world-end can replace ordinary integration limits with direct consumption rule

### World-threat counterwar branch

Hannibal reacts to the world's coalition:

- break relief corridors
- assassinate or corrupt coalition officers
- strike memorial and command capitals
- sever naval blockades
- convert captured anti-cannibal units
- exploit countries that previously used terror policies

### Ordinary world-end branch

Hidden until:

- chaos exceeds the world-end threshold
- unified country exists
- sufficient territory and population consumption
- Network Reach and Larder conditions
- no existing world-end

The branch includes:

- final global mobilization
- dismantling ordinary state limits
- universal enemy designation
- terminal population-consumption mechanics
- ordinary Hannibal world-end super-event

The capstone should make global conquest the clear campaign state.

## Wendigo Hannibal overlay

The overlay appears only after the existing Wendigo country merges with the revealed network.

### Merge trunk

- preserve Wendigo units and technology
- transform Hannibal portrait and identity
- reconcile command systems
- convert ordinary Larder into supernatural hunger resources
- establish transformation anchors

### Winter hunger branch

- blizzard and cold bonuses
- enemy attrition
- frozen supply routes
- movement through harsh terrain
- winter offensives

### Wendigo recruitment branch

- retain all existing Wendigo units
- train additional units
- improve supernatural templates
- convert consumed population and enemy deaths into recruitment
- strict pre-lock requirements prevent instant terminal power

### Cannibal legacy branch

- keep ordinary cannibal country bonuses
- preserve warlord commanders
- retain Larder, cells, islands, and population mechanics
- improve origin specialists with supernatural variants

### Transformation countdown branch

- establish anchor states
- begin irreversible transformation mission
- protect anchors from coalition assault
- respond to sabotage and liberation
- accelerate through consumption and winter victories

### Alternate terminal branch

After the final lock:

- set the Event 14 Wendigo world-end
- apply actual-nonhuman classification
- grant the strongest military, recruitment, supply, and enemy-penalty package
- make defeat effectively impossible
- direct AI toward complete world conquest and consumption

The branch is stronger than ordinary Hannibal and must feel categorically different.

## Focus AI requirements

Every route has campaign-aware AI.

AI considers:

- origin archetype
- warlord personality
- controlled state count
- Larder
- Frenzy
- Network Alignment
- current enemies
- naval access
- encirclement
- player proximity
- Hannibal reveal status
- Wendigo existence
- world-end eligibility

Invalid routes are hidden or weighted to zero. An Island Host without a port cannot pursue the completed naval route. A warlord cannot choose alignment when Evolution II is disabled. Hannibal cannot enter the Wendigo overlay when no valid Wendigo country exists.

## Focus reward standard

Focus rewards should use:

- units and templates
- commanders and warlord characters
- decisions and mission families
- state modifiers and map work
- factories, ports, railways, supply hubs, airbases, forts, and captured-resource conversion
- Larder and Frenzy mechanic changes
- war goals, claims, and integration systems
- ideology and leader changes
- network and cell actions
- super-event and world-end eligibility
- strong staged idea upgrades

Small modifiers can support these rewards. They cannot be the main point of important focuses.

## Accepted closure integration

The focus-closure addendum did not add, remove, move, or rename a focus. It deepened the existing 28-focus Wendigo overlay through live operations and structural upgrades:

- `Open the Winter Hunt` opens the scored pre-lock target profile.
- `Count the Winter Victories` opens non-retroactive bounded enemy-death receipts.
- `Drill the Original Pack`, `Expand the Hunting Packs`, and `Army of the Frozen Larder` add recon, engineer, and logistics support to the existing locked 16-battalion Pack exactly once.
- `Open the Pack Musters` opens the one-Pack receipt muster alongside the ordinary two-Pack paid trainer.
- `Retain the Warlord Captains` and `All Inheritances Intact` apply the two inherited-commander stages.
- `Keep the Foreign Cells` opens the paid inherited winter-cell operation.
- `All Inheritances Intact` also upgrades only the four inherited origin templates that actually exist.
- `Hunt Every Remaining Capital` opens the four terminal-hunt surfaces.
- `The World Beneath Winter` keeps the route and scored priorities open but does not set the terminal lock.

The final country and focus audits confirm that normal queue recruitment for the Pack closes immediately during the in-place ZZZ transformation, before the first overlay focus. Both scripted Pack musters validate the complete requested batch against capacity before population consumption.

All 208 focuses retain unique IDs, AI blocks, reward helpers, localisation, and distinct registered DDS icons. The accepted tuning normalization uses five-point increments for authored percentage and meter ladders, with documented exceptions only for semantic counters, engine building levels, Pack batch multiples, research-use counts, and encoded AI factors.
