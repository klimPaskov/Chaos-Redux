# Event 014 Cannibalism, Part 2: Baseline and Containment

## Opening incident

The first popup is a military report directed at the selected host country. The visible information should be concrete and limited:

- a named formation or garrison has missing personnel
- burial or recovery details have failed to return
- ration records no longer match delivered supplies
- bodies show deliberate cutting or removal
- frightened soldiers refuse transfers into one position
- evidence points to soldiers rather than civilians or an outside enemy

The event must not begin with cult robes, supernatural language, a master symbol, ritual proclamations, or a named ideology. The first incident should be believable as wartime collapse.

Immediate hidden effects establish the country crisis, select the initial state and formation context, add the baseline Cannibalism national spirit, initialize Field Hunger and Command Integrity, and open the containment category. The visible option can summarize the immediate consequence through a custom tooltip without reapplying it.

## Opening response families

These are working response roles, not final option localisation.

### Open emergency response

The government acknowledges the military emergency and commits supply, investigators, and replacement officers.

Immediate direction:

- lower Field Hunger
- raise Command Integrity
- reveal the first target state and compromised formation
- begin an emergency logistics mission
- increase short-term equipment, convoy, train, manpower, and civilian-factory burden

Long-term direction:

- strongest clean-containment route
- lower chance of cell survival
- higher chance that witnesses cooperate
- foreign allies are more willing to share intelligence

AI direction:

- preferred by democracies, high-integrity governments, countries with adequate resources, and countries facing no immediate capital threat

### Conceal and isolate

The government treats the incident as a disciplinary embarrassment and attempts to suppress information while quietly moving the unit.

Immediate direction:

- modest short-term stability protection
- lower Command Integrity
- delay public panic
- increase hidden cell survival and foreign spread risk
- make later investigations more expensive

Long-term direction:

- can still return to clean containment if the state has not been exploited and evidence remains recoverable
- creates blackmail, missing records, and officer-complicity events

AI direction:

- preferred by authoritarian governments with moderate resources and strong fear of political fallout

### Exploit the terror

The government authorizes controlled use of compromised soldiers against enemies or prisoners.

Immediate direction:

- gains a temporary battlefield terror tool
- raises Cult Cohesion when Evolution I is available
- lowers Command Integrity sharply
- creates condemnation and evidence exposure risks
- disqualifies the cleanest achievement route

Long-term direction:

- unlocks terror battalion and penal-feeding decisions
- makes warlord emergence more likely
- gives the cult institutional access to prisons, occupied territory, and transport
- can produce powerful short-term combat effects but should be one of the main causes of global escalation

AI direction:

- rare for ordinary governments
- more likely for desperate authoritarian or extremist countries with low stability, heavy casualties, and immediate military collapse risk

## Baseline national spirit lifecycle

### Initial form: Cannibalism in the Ranks

Role:

- serious military and social crisis
- weekly manpower loss from disappearances and desertion
- stability pressure
- supply and organization disruption in compromised areas
- visible Field Hunger and Command Integrity breakdown

The spirit must matter. It should create enough pressure that the player cannot ignore the category for a year while continuing ordinary play.

### Containment form: Emergency Ration and Discipline Command

Unlocked by open emergency response.

Role:

- reduces uncontrolled losses
- increases the cost of military administration and supply
- improves investigation and rotation success
- exposes the current high-risk state group

### Concealment form: Sealed Military Scandal

Unlocked by concealment.

Role:

- reduces public stability damage in the short term
- worsens cell detection
- increases foreign spread chance through transfers
- increases the severity of later exposure events

### Exploitation form: Terror Feeding Program

Unlocked by exploitation.

Role:

- strong temporary enemy morale and surrender penalties around designated units
- escalating Cult Cohesion and condemnation risk
- access to compromised recruitment and prisoner decisions
- cannot become a stable positive final spirit

### Clean final form: Discipline Restored

Unlocked after local victory and stabilization.

Role:

- temporary recovery and veteran vigilance
- protection from passive relapse
- improved detection of foreign seeding
- expires or becomes a small historical-memory spirit after recovery

### Corrupted final form: The Line Was Crossed

Unlocked if the country ends the immediate crisis while preserving exploitation structures.

Role:

- strong terror and occupation tools
- persistent instability, cell protection, and future defection risk
- route into Evolution I even if Field Hunger is later low

## Baseline decision category

The category should open immediately and remain readable. It should show:

- Field Hunger
- Command Integrity
- current compromised state or theater
- active emergency mission
- number of suspected cells
- whether foreign spread is currently possible
- current policy stance

The early category should use normal decisions with a compact attached scripted GUI header. A large separate window becomes justified only after Evolution II creates multiple states and foreign nodes.

### Logistics action family

#### Emergency ration audit

Purpose:

- identify stolen, substituted, or falsified supplies
- reduce Field Hunger
- reveal whether officers are complicit

Costs and requirements:

- civilian factory burden
- trains or trucks when the theater is inland
- convoys when the theater is overseas
- administrative time

Failure:

- evidence disappears
- Command Integrity falls
- a false accusation can damage a loyal unit

#### Restore the supply corridor

Timed objective:

- repair or control named rail links, ports, supply hubs, or convoy routes serving the selected theater
- place supplied divisions in the relevant states
- keep the route open for a dynamic duration

Success:

- major Field Hunger reduction
- compromised units become eligible for rotation
- lower chance of survival cannibalism recurring

Failure:

- initial cell strength rises
- missing personnel event
- possible spread to another unit sharing the region

#### Emergency overseas convoy

Use when the selected theater lacks a land connection to the capital.

Requirements:

- convoys
- fuel
- escort or naval access
- a valid port

Partial success:

- supplies arrive, but one ship or detachment disappears
- unlocks a later island investigation if circumstances support it

#### Evacuate the isolated formation

Use when an encircled or remote unit cannot be sustainably supplied.

Requirements:

- transport capacity
- controlled exit route or port
- manpower and equipment loss accepted during evacuation

Tradeoff:

- gives up local military position
- strongly protects Command Integrity and reduces Field Hunger

### Military discipline action family

#### Rotate compromised formations

Timed objective:

- move identified divisions out of the active theater
- keep them away from frontline combat during screening
- replace them with supplied formations

Costs:

- temporary unit lock or combat penalty
- manpower replacement
- infantry and support equipment
- command attention

Success:

- reduces cell strength
- reveals organizers
- raises Command Integrity

Failure:

- infected personnel desert during transit
- spreads risk to a rail hub, port, or new state

#### Replace the officer chain

Purpose:

- remove officers who falsified rolls, shared spoils, or protected cells

Requirements:

- army experience
- available commanders or staff capacity
- evidence threshold

Risk:

- acting too early with weak evidence reduces Command Integrity
- acting too late creates a cult officer cadre

#### Protect burial and medical details

Purpose:

- ensure bodies are recovered, identified, and guarded
- protect wounded personnel from predation

Requirements:

- manpower
- support equipment
- medical or military police capacity

Success:

- lowers Field Hunger and cell opportunity
- creates reliable casualty records

#### Establish military police cordons

Purpose:

- restrict movement in the target state
- inspect kitchens, camps, prisons, and transport

Costs:

- tied-down divisions or garrison manpower
- support equipment
- stability or local resistance tradeoff in occupied territory

Failure:

- indiscriminate cordon creates civilian panic and strengthens recruitment

### Investigation action family

#### Forensic recovery teams

Purpose:

- confirm the nature and scale of killings
- separate rumor from evidence

Requirements:

- secure access to the state
- support equipment
- Command Integrity above a minimum useful band

Success:

- reveals true cell strength
- enables targeted prosecution

#### Witness protection

Purpose:

- move frightened soldiers, prisoners, civilians, medics, and quartermasters out of cult reach

Costs:

- manpower
- transport
- political exposure

Success:

- improves later infiltration and trial outcomes

#### Follow the ration ledger

Purpose:

- identify black markets, diverted livestock, field kitchens, prison transfers, and suspicious unit supply patterns

Outcome variants:

- survival theft ring with no organized cell
- officer-protected predation
- civilian black market
- early cult recruitment network

#### Search the missing burial party

A state-targeted mission with a meaningful chance of partial success, ambush, or discovery of a larger site.

### Legal and social response family

#### Public court-martial

Available with sufficient evidence.

Benefits:

- Command Integrity increase
- lower denial and rumor pressure
- foreign trust

Risks:

- panic
- martyr creation if organizers are tried without breaking the cell
- exposure of exploitation policy

#### Quiet disbandment

Benefits:

- faster removal of a compromised unit
- lower immediate political shock

Risks:

- personnel scatter into other formations
- cell organizers survive
- future spread becomes harder to trace

#### Conditional amnesty

Purpose:

- separate frightened participants and coerced followers from organizers

Requirements:

- strong investigation
- no active terror exploitation
- protected surrender route

Success:

- sharp cell reduction
- testimony against leaders

Failure:

- organizers use the surrender process to relocate

#### Family notification and burial program

Purpose:

- restore public trust after evidence becomes undeniable
- begin long recovery

Costs:

- civilian capacity
- stability shock in the short term

Benefits:

- lower rumor and recruitment pressure
- state recovery progress

### Hard suppression family

#### Targeted purge of organizers

Available when the cell is mapped.

Requirements:

- high evidence quality
- military police
- sufficient Command Integrity

Success:

- destroys cell leadership

Failure with low integrity:

- kills witnesses and ordinary soldiers
- deepens secrecy
- raises future Cult Cohesion

#### Sweep prisons and detention sites

Purpose:

- prevent prisoners becoming victims, recruits, or supply

Costs:

- guards
- transport
- support equipment
- temporary prison disruption

#### Seal the occupied district

Use in occupied territory.

Tradeoff:

- reduces movement and predation
- increases resistance if relief and food are not provided at the same time

## Active mission capacity

The category should not show every possible action at once.

Recommended ordinary capacity:

- one major logistics mission
- one formation mission
- one investigation mission
- one emergency response

The player can queue later actions, but only the highest-priority target states and units remain visible. Evolution I and II expand the capacity through better institutions, while also increasing the number of threats.

## Baseline event family

### Field evidence events

Purpose:

- provide concrete signs
- update suspected states and units
- create investigation targets

Variants:

- burial detail disappears
- medical tent incident
- ration ledger mismatch
- prisoner transfer arrives short
- kitchen fire destroys evidence
- a junior officer refuses orders
- a survivor identifies an organizer

### Supply and isolation events

Purpose:

- make Field Hunger respond to campaign state

Variants:

- rail line cut
- convoy lost
- garrison abandoned
- relief arrives
- local population shares food
- stockpile theft uncovered

### Command events

Purpose:

- test Command Integrity

Variants:

- commander demands collective punishment
- quartermaster implicated
- military police protect a witness
- officers destroy records
- loyal unit resists transfer screening

### Public reaction events

Purpose:

- manage rumor, fear, grief, and political pressure

Variants:

- families demand bodies
- civilians avoid soldiers
- local workers refuse military transport
- foreign press obtains photographs
- religious or community leaders organize burial relief

### Exploitation events

Purpose:

- make the dark route powerful and dangerous

Variants:

- terror attack succeeds
- prisoners disappear
- enemy propaganda exposes the program
- compromised troops refuse normal orders
- officers create their own feeding hierarchy

## State-level baseline modifiers

### Suspected Predation

Light state modifier used when evidence is incomplete.

Effects direction:

- local fear
- reduced movement and recruitment efficiency
- investigation target

### Compromised Garrison

Used when military units or prison guards are directly implicated.

Effects direction:

- supply loss
- manpower drain
- sabotage risk
- cell growth

### Empty Burial Grounds

Used when bodies and records disappear.

Effects direction:

- stability and local trust damage
- forensic mission target

### Recovery and Identification

Applied after successful suppression.

Effects direction:

- temporary civilian capacity cost
- population recovery cannot restore dead population, but it can end continuing losses
- improves local compliance and trust over time

## Clean-containment victory sequence

The clean route should require several kinds of success rather than one button:

1. Reduce Field Hunger below the active crisis band.
2. Restore a real supply route or evacuate the isolated formation.
3. Rotate or screen compromised units.
4. Identify and remove organizers.
5. Protect prisoners, wounded personnel, and burial details.
6. Keep all country cells suppressed for a stabilization period.
7. Complete a final local recovery mission in every affected state.

A clean victory closes the category gradually. Recovery decisions remain until state trauma has been addressed.

## Concealment victory sequence

A concealed government can end immediate incidents without achieving a clean outcome.

Requirements:

- disband or relocate compromised units
- keep evidence below public exposure threshold
- reduce active predation

Consequences:

- hidden survivors remain possible
- foreign spread risk stays elevated
- later exposure can reopen the crisis
- local victory memory is weaker

This route should never be as reliable as open containment.

## Exploitation sequence

The exploitation route grants strong battlefield tools:

- temporary enemy organization damage
- surrender and retreat penalties
- occupation terror
- intelligence from coerced prisoners

The price is structural:

- Cult Cohesion rises
- cells gain access to prisons and transport
- officers become independent organizers
- condemnation and foreign exposure rise
- warlord emergence can occur even after military victory

This route exists because the player may choose it, not because it is an efficient default. It should feel tempting in a losing war and disastrous when treated as free power.

## Baseline AI behavior

### Resource-secure governments

- prioritize logistics and rotation
- use public investigation when stability can survive it
- avoid exploitation
- accept territorial retreat to save isolated forces

### Resource-poor governments

- use conditional amnesty and targeted screening
- request foreign supply aid
- evacuate overseas garrisons when convoys cannot be protected
- may conceal when public exposure threatens collapse

### Authoritarian governments

- prefer quiet disbandment and targeted purges
- use public trials when evidence can reinforce authority
- risk indiscriminate suppression if Command Integrity is low

### Desperate extremist governments

- may exploit terror when capital defense or imminent capitulation conditions apply
- should understand that exploitation raises future internal danger
- must not click exploitation merely because the option exists

## Baseline defeat conditions

The ordinary country loses control of the crisis when one or more of these occur:

- multiple state cells reach mature strength
- a compromised formation refuses rotation and controls territory
- a military prison or island garrison becomes autonomous
- Cult Cohesion reaches an organized threshold after Evolution I
- a state remains cut off through repeated relief failure
- the government institutionalizes feeding and then loses control of the units

Defeat does not always create a country immediately. It can first create a commune, silent island, or strong local modifier. Country formation belongs to later baseline stages and Evolution II conditions.
