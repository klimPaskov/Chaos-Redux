## Super-event roles

Research packages are needed for these roles:

1. Cannibal islands reveal
2. hidden-leader network reveal, concealed until Event 014 reveal state
3. World-end terminal scenario
4. Wendigo fusion world-end, only if Wendigo exists and fusion branch fires
5. Defeat aftermath, only if the threat reached global scale and was defeated

## Research rules

- Find real quote candidates.
- Verify wording and attribution.
- Use public-domain, historical, religious, philosophical, military, or literary sources where possible.
- Keep any modern copyrighted button remark very short.
- Reject unsourced quote-site material.
- Do not invent quotes.
- Do not select audio with unclear license.
- Check composition rights and recording rights separately.
- Use real music, not drones, oscillator tones, beeps, or generated test-tone audio.
- Every final track must have title, creator or composer, source URL, license, duration, usage terms, and fit notes.

## Role directions

### Cannibal islands reveal

Role: escalation reveal.

Tone: naval reports, missing ships, remote islands, uncertain public evidence, military panic.

Quote direction: hunger, taboo, isolation, discovery of forbidden acts, public-domain or historical source.

Button remark direction: grim maritime or bureaucratic understatement.

Audio mood: cold, slow, dread-driven, musical, preferably 1 to 2 minutes after trim.

Image handoff: generated island horror, ruined dock, explicit fictional gore.

### hidden-leader network reveal

Role: hidden commander or ideological unifier reveal.

Status: concealed until Evolution III reveal state. Pre-reveal materials must not use the true leader name or direct identity.

Tone: scattered outbreaks share discipline, symbols, or orders.

Quote direction: command, appetite, empire, moral corruption, public-domain source preferred.

Button remark direction: short, cold, no cheap comedy.

Audio mood: controlled and imperial, not chaotic.

Image handoff: generated cult command scene for reveal and generated final leader portrait direction. Do not spoil the true identity in filenames or pre-reveal text.

### World-end terminal scenario

Role: world-end scenario.

Tone: the world is reorganized as hunting ground and larder, terminal and global.

Quote direction: judgment, devouring, hunger, human collapse, scripture or ancient literature acceptable after verification.

Button remark direction: short, fatalistic, research required.

Audio mood: final, severe, unique licensed music.

Image handoff: generated global threat composition with explicit fictional gore.


### Wendigo fusion world-end

Role: alternate terminal world-end.

Tone: hunger and winter predation become one force. The branch is final and should not sound like a normal escalation.

Quote direction: hunger, winter, pursuit, final doom, mythic predation, and collapse. Use a verified source.

Button remark direction: short, fatalistic, research required.

Audio mood: severe ritual finality, cold and musical, unique licensed track.

Image handoff: generated fused leader or transformed silhouette, Wendigo unit shapes, frozen hunting ground, explicit fictional gore.

### Defeat aftermath

Role: post-threat reflection.

Tone: grim recovery, trials, missing-person lists, archive sealing, fear of recurrence.

Quote direction: memory, responsibility, survival, vigilance.

Button remark direction: quiet and severe.

Audio mood: reflective, mournful, not triumphant.

Image handoff: generated tribunal, island memorial, or archive recovery with aftermath gore.
