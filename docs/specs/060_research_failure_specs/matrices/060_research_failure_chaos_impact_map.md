# Research Failure Chaos impact map

## Ownership rule

Event 60 owns only Chaos changes caused by the collapse and reconstruction of a national scientific system.
Generic war, annexation, death, contamination, nuclear use, ideology, and faction changes remain owned by their existing systems.

Evolution activation and evolution logging always give zero Chaos.
A higher evolution can add Chaos only when it applies new concrete damage.

## Scaling inputs

The opening amount can use a bounded score from:

- Research slots actually suppressed.
- Safe technology nodes actually regressed.
- Generations actually lost.
- Active projects actually cancelled.
- Owner-approved research advantages actually damaged.
- Whether the target is a major.

The score selects the intended severity band and never adds one Chaos point per node.
A shallow target receives a smaller amount than a full advanced-major collapse.
The result is recorded before the player can recover a technology through a gift.

## Non-sources

The following actions produce no Event 60-owned Chaos by themselves:

- Evolution becoming eligible.
- Evolution being enabled or disabled.
- Starting or completing an ordinary reconstruction decision.
- Spending equipment, factories, political power, or stability.
- Researching one lost technology again.
- Receiving one lost technology through Gift from Scientists.
- Selecting an institutional settlement.
- Foreign aid that does not cause a separate political outcome.
- Routine project delay.
- Archive Recovery changing without durable new loss.
- Forced cleanup after annexation.
- Debug or manual completion.

## Anti-farming acceptance

- Reloading cannot repeat an opening, evolution, setback, or reconstruction receipt.
- A new incident sequence is required for a new opening source.
- Target immunity and active-incident exclusion prevent rapid repeat farming.
- Full lost-ledger closure gives no second reconstruction reduction.
- Gift and Brilliant Scientist grants give no direct negative Chaos.
- A project cannot be cancelled and repeated to produce Chaos changes.
- Civil-war transfer preserves receipts.
- Annexation cleanup gives no reconstruction reward.

## History direction

Every Event 60 Chaos entry should use a concise source label tied to the public outcome and target country.
The History view should distinguish the national collapse, named evolved damage, severe setback, and institutional reconstruction.
It should not expose internal node counts, sequence variables, or debug reasons.
