# Research Failure decision and mission map

## Category contract

- Working category identity: Research Reconstruction
- Presentation layer: Ordinary decision category with one static category picture and a concise attached status summary
- Public values: Scientific Capacity and Archive Recovery
- Supporting visible state: Operational research slots compared with the incident ceiling, current major project, current priority domain
- Normal visible primary actions per phase: `3-5`
- Hard maximum visible primary actions per phase: `6`
- Normal active missions: `1`
- Hard maximum active missions: `2`
- Major institution projects active at once: `1`, with a costly route-specific overextension exception if implementation proves it useful
- Spendable cost types per action: Maximum `4`

## Phase map

| Phase | Entry condition | Exit condition | Main pressure | Normal visible content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening response | Entry event completed and rollback transaction applied | One response selected | Public admission, secrecy, or Kruger authority | Use event options for this one-time choice |
| Emergency triage | Incident active and Capacity below Reopened Institutes band | Stabilization objective resolved and Capacity reaches the first institute threshold | Prevent another loss and identify what survived | Four actions and one mission |
| National reconstruction | Emergency triage resolved | Capacity reaches 100, slots restored, settlement complete | Rebuild institutions and choose priorities | Four or five actions and one project mission |
| Institutional settlement | Capacity reaches the route-choice threshold | One settlement route completed | Decide how scientific authority will be governed | Three mutually exclusive route actions, plus Kruger route when already mandated |
| Residual lost knowledge | Institutional crisis resolved and lost ledger nonempty | Every ledger entry recovered, obsolete, or validly abandoned | Relearn lost technologies | Two or three actions |
| Closed | No outstanding institutional or lost-knowledge state | Final state | None | Category hidden |

## Emergency mission

### Stabilize the scientific network

- Working type: Timed objective mission
- Duration direction: `150-240` days, scaled by severity and war
- Success basis: Reach the required early Scientific Capacity and Archive Recovery through active decisions, retain the necessary territory or exile support, and avoid an unresolved second loss
- Success: Unlock first institute project, prevent the strongest cascade incidents, improve public confidence, and mark emergency triage complete
- Failure: Apply one stored setback such as archive loss, project delay, specialist flight, or worsening crisis state, then move to a harder recovery state without making completion impossible
- AI behavior: Highest priority while a realistic completion path remains
- Exploit control: Progress and hidden outcome are stored when the mission begins or when material actions occur, not rerolled on completion reload

## Institution project mission pattern

Every major project uses one clear timed mission or progress-bearing decision. No second payment is required after the work is complete.

| Project family | Duration direction | Success | Partial success | Failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Research institute | `180-360` days | One owed slot restored, Capacity gain | Institution opens but slot waits for specialists or standards | Delay, partial Capacity, cost overrun, or site relocation |
| University network | `240-450` days | Capacity gain, education pressure resolved, cohort unlocked | One region reopens, slower national gain | Staff dispersal or political interference |
| Standards network | `180-360` days | Verification pressure reduced, both values rise | Limited fields certified | False certification or repeated testing cost |
| Central archive | `180-420` days | Large Archive Recovery and verified series | Records indexed but not fully verified | False archive, missing index, or theft |
| Replacement cohort | `360-720` days | Large durable Capacity and resilience | Smaller cohort, one domain supported | Dropout, conscription, emigration, or funding collapse |
| Foreign commission | `120-300` days | Chosen project accelerated | Aid arrives with stronger obligation | Partner withdraws, route loss, or exposure |
