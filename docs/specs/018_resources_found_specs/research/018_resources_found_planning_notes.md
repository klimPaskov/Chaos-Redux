## Design constraints carried into the package

- Baseline remains straightforward: one random valid state gains around 100 of one random resource.
- The owning country receives the first popup and a resource exploitation decision surface.
- Diplomacy and trade reactions matter from the first firing.
- Border and contested-region discoveries can create diplomatic crisis and border war.
- Evolutions must distinguish pre-fire evolved openings from active-event evolution.
- Evolution I increases resource richness, international attention, and demilitarized pressure.
- Evolution II introduces sickness, corrosion, deaths, and cave incidents.
- Evolution III turns the danger public through monster attacks and evacuation decisions.
- Evolution IV creates the Cave Host, a nonhuman cave monster country with automatic resource-based divisions.
- The Cave Host declares war on neighbouring countries and grows from captured resources.
- The world-end scenario triggers when the Cave Host owns enough of a continent and chaos is over 1000.
- The event belongs in the economy positive cluster and should be medium severity there.
- Player-facing wording must be direction-only here. Final text belongs to implementation.
- Super-event titles, quote text, button text, cultural references, and audio are research-gated.
- No fallback or simplification is allowed without explicit reporting.
