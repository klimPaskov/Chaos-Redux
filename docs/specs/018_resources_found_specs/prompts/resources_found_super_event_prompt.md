# Super-Event Prompt for Event 018 Resources Found

Use `chaos-redux-super-events`. Use `chaosx_super_event_text_researcher` for quotes, button remarks, slogans, and cultural allusions. Use `chaosx_super_event_audio_researcher` for music research, license verification, download, conversion, and audio notes. Use `chaosx_generated_event_art` for fictional super-event images.

All titles, button remarks, quotes, cultural references, and audio choices below are research-gated. Do not treat any working label as final localisation.

## Super-event roles

### Cave Host reveal

Trigger: Evolution IV creates the Cave Host from the unclosed resource field.

Role: first public recognition that a resource discovery has become a nonhuman country and a military threat.

Tone direction: severe, physical, and uncanny. The world can understand that something has emerged and seized territory, but not why resource seams became bodies.

Image direction: generated fictional image, 457x328, nonhuman cave host emerging from an excavated resource field, period military observers or workers at the edge if composition allows, no readable text.

Quote direction: research a real quote about digging too deep, hidden powers under the earth, greed, forbidden discovery, or the consequences of extraction. Prefer public domain literature, mythology, scripture, philosophy, or a traceable historical source.

Button remark direction: research a short grim cultural remark or allusion about mines, depths, hunger, or earth opening. Keep modern copyrighted fragments very short or use title-like reference.

Audio mood: dark, heavy, slow, musical, not a drone or generated tone. Public domain or clearly licensed. Prefer 1 to 2 minutes or documented trim.

### Cave Host world-end

Trigger: Cave Host controls enough of a continent and chaos is over 1000, then starts appearing on other continents.

Role: terminal world-end scenario. The discovery is no longer local. Resource seams across the world are connected.

Tone direction: finality, planetary collapse, and realization that economic dependence on resources has become an entry path for the Host.

Image direction: generated fictional image, 457x328, multiple cave mouths or glowing seams opening across continents or industrial regions, strong central composition, no text.

Quote direction: research a real quote about the end of the world, earth, judgement, hunger, or human greed awakening ruin. Prefer public domain, scripture, mythology, classical literature, or traceable historical text.

Button remark direction: research a short fatalistic or grimly ironic line. Avoid generic apocalypse wording.

Audio mood: terminal and massive, but still music. Public domain or clearly licensed. Unique track, documented source, 44.1 kHz final `.ogg`.

### Cave Host defeat aftermath

Trigger: Cave Host defeated after it became a broad world threat, held many states, or approached world-end threshold.

Role: costly relief and memory. Use only if implementation confirms the threat lasted long enough and caused broad damage.

Tone direction: reflective, not clean triumph. The ground is sealed but not trusted.

Image direction: generated fictional image, 457x328, soldiers and survivors around sealed depths or ruined mining works, no readable text.

Quote direction: research a real quote about survival, memory, sealing evil, or the cost of knowledge.

Button remark direction: short cultural remark or sober statement, researched before use.

Audio mood: reflective, grave, and musical. Public domain or clearly licensed.

## Required outputs

For every super-event produce:

- selected title only after research, or mark blocked
- selected quote with exact wording, source, author, work, year or period, URL, confidence, and copyright note
- selected button remark with source or cultural note
- selected audio with title, creator or composer, performer if relevant, source URL, license, duration, downloaded source path, final `.ogg` path, and sound definition id suggestion
- image handoff to asset prompt
- implementation notes for slot, visibility flag, audio id, settings-aware playback, localisation keys, docs, and spreadsheet alignment

Do not use placeholder audio. Do not use generated tones, drones, beeps, or oscillator music. If licensing is unclear, mark the audio blocked.
