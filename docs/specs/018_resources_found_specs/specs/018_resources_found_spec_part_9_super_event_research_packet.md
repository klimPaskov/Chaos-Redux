# Event 018 Resources Found, Part 9 Super-Event Research Handoff

This file is a research handoff, not final localisation and not final audio selection. It does not approve any final title, button remark, quote, cultural allusion, slogan, lyric fragment, or music track. The future super-event text and audio subagents must verify sources, wording, rights, licensing, duration, download paths, conversion, and implementation fit before anything is wired.

## Text research direction

The text researcher should compare several real quote candidates for each role. Prefer public-domain scripture, public-domain literature, public-domain classical or ancient writing, medieval hymn text, geological or mining writing, and traceable historical sources. Avoid unsourced quote sites, invented quotes, modern song lyrics, modern film dialogue, and meme references.

| Role | Quote direction | Cultural remark direction | Research warnings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cave Host emergence | Mining, violated earth, mountains trembling, hidden things under the ground, or human greed below the surface. | A short public-domain mountain, mine, cave, or underworld reference may fit if it does not make the reveal funny. | Do not use a familiar line only because it mentions a mountain. The tone must stay frightening. |
| Continental maw world-end | Judgment, people hiding in rocks or dens, the earth breaking open, final ruin, or an underworld swallowing surface order. | A short liturgical, mythic, or older literary reference may fit if sourced. | Do not choose a title-like line without verifying source and rights. |
| Defeat aftermath | Sealing, memory, survival, restraint, scars, or the cost of knowledge dug from the ground. | Restrained survivor register, public-domain literature, or plain in-world response. | Avoid triumphant or comedic references. |

## Audio research direction

The audio researcher should find legally usable music, not sound effects or generated tones. Public-domain compositions still need recording-rights checks. Preferred length is 1 to 2 minutes after trimming. The final track must be downloaded from a legitimate source, preserved as a source file, converted to 44.1 kHz OGG, documented, and wired through the settings-aware super-event audio system.

| Role | Audio mood | Candidate source families to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cave Host emergence | Escalating, subterranean, rhythmic, uncanny, and not campy. | Public-domain orchestral recordings with cave, mountain, underworld, or mining fit. |
| Continental maw world-end | Terminal, heavy, ritual, geological, and global. | Public-domain requiem, chant, dramatic orchestral public-domain recordings, or other licensed music with finality. |
| Defeat aftermath | Grave, slow, reflective, and exhausted. | Public-domain organ, chamber, choral, or restrained orchestral recordings with clear rights. |

## Blockers before implementation

- Final title text remains blocked until final writing and research review.
- Final button remark remains blocked if it references scripture, literature, music, film, song, myth, or another source.
- Final quote remains blocked until exact wording, attribution, source, copyright status, and excerpt length are verified.
- Final audio remains blocked until legitimate download, source preservation, license note, conversion, sound id, and music documentation are complete.
- Super-event image assets remain blocked until generated or sourced assets have source files, processed PNGs, final DDS files, manifests, and GFX handoff.
