## Shared proof requirements

Achievement evaluation consumes recorded outcomes. It does not infer success from a popup being opened, an option being offered, the original's current country flag or the absence of a pending window. Episode, actor, attempt, country, contribution and permanent-disposition identity must survive save/load and country-control changes.

A human government receives credit for actions committed while that country is controlled by that player. Ordinary AI progress is legitimate game progress, but it cannot retroactively become four human deductions after a hot-join. A player can earn cooperative credit through an accepted fact and their own substantive reasoning even when an AI partner conducts the final operation.

Use the project's actual debug/forced-activation provenance. Artificially completed cases, debug-set proof flags, duplicated receipts and forced achievement calls are ineligible. A test or forced setup must not silently leak into ordinary achievement eligibility. A normal campaign reaching the required Chaos through gameplay remains eligible.

Do not require the player to cause additional deaths, intentionally fail, keep a proven killer free or enable a disabled source. Achievements can depend on a real opportunity arising naturally. Their ordinary event descriptions should not become an achievement checklist.

## Achievement definitions

### A1. Complete original inquiry

- Candidate ID: `serial_killer_complete_original_inquiry`.
- Title direction: precise reasoning that closes a dangerous case.
- Description direction: a full correct original-killer attempt, completed through the player's own deductions and a valid permanent operation.
- Eligible country: any human-controlled affected country with an actual local inquiry against the original. Major status is not required.
- Unlock: the player commits all four supported deductions in one current attempt, with no wrong deduction in that attempt, then commits or conducts its valid permanent stopping operation. Prior failed attempts do not invalidate this clean later attempt. A stale location refresh preserves earned correct deductions and is not a mistake.
- Disqualifiers: any of the four deductions in the counted attempt was made by AI or debug completion, a wrong deduction in that attempt, a copycat mistaken for the original, a mere departure conclusion, or an uncommitted/duplicate operation.
- Difficulty and purpose: medium. Four connected deductions and an actual capture provide the challenge. It is not awarded for the opening report or the first obvious response.
- Visibility: ordinary visible achievement, with no hidden archetype named in its locked wording.
- Tracking: original actor identity, case attempt, four committed human deduction receipts, wrong-answer state for that attempt, refreshed-location continuity and unique permanent outcome.
- 64 by 64 completed icon direction: an original evidence-folder composition with four coherent document elements and a restrained closure seal. It should remain readable without tiny digits or labels.

### A2. Cooperative regional capture

- Candidate ID: `serial_killer_cooperative_regional_capture`.
- Title direction: two jurisdictions establishing one real trail.
- Description direction: materially help stop the original through authenticated evidence from countries on its actual cross-border route.
- Eligible country: a human-controlled actual investigator or material contributor on that route.
- Unlock: the same original has real incidents or verified pursuit evidence in at least two countries connected by a feasible regional journey. At least two different countries supply distinct authenticated facts used in the successful proof. The credited player makes at least one correct local identity, chronology or route deduction and either conducts the final valid operation or supplies a fact actually consumed by it. The original is permanently stopped.
- Disqualifiers: rumor forwarding, an unanswered offer, receiving news, two copies of the same fact, an impossible journey, a copied motif treated as identity, or an operation against an independent killer instead.
- Difficulty and purpose: hard. The challenge is useful cooperation and supported cross-border reasoning, with credit available to the substantive sender as well as the arresting country.
- Visibility: visible with general cooperation wording.
- Tracking: one original identity, real route, distinct source countries and fact receipts, actual deduction and final-operation contribution links, and once-only award by eligible player country.
- 64 by 64 completed icon direction: two differently oriented evidence pages joined by a precise route motif. Compose this independently from the decision icon and avoid national flags.

### A3. Multinational cell dismantled

- Candidate ID: `serial_killer_multinational_cell_dismantled`.
- Title direction: exposing the support that kept an organization alive.
- Description direction: complete the investigation and containment of a real cell whose operations or shelter relationships affected more than one country.
- Eligible country: a human-controlled investigator with a substantive operation against the actual cell.
- Unlock: the cell had verified active dependencies in at least two countries. Its leadership, actual support and remaining dangerous membership are identified and contained through the required organizational proof. The credited player commits at least two correct organizational deductions and conducts a material operation used in the cell's final closure. All actual dangerous members belonging to that cell are accounted for as permanently contained, withdrawn into verified safe nonparticipation or already stopped through their own cases.
- Disqualifiers: one empty building raided without membership proof, a speaker arrested while an active coordinator survives, a departed cell, an unused reservation, a renamed cell, or a supposed closure that leaves an untracked splinter.
- Difficulty and purpose: hard. It rewards a complete organizational inquiry and actual loss of multinational support, rather than one conspicuous arrest.
- Visibility: visible after worldwide organization has been publicly established. Earlier locked presentation should remain general.
- Tracking: stable cell and membership history, active country dependencies before disruption, two correct human deduction receipts, operation result, all participant dispositions, reserved-fragment handling and final closure identity.
- 64 by 64 completed icon direction: a small refuge-and-ledger composition with its operational links visibly broken. Avoid a generic skull or the original killer's portrait.

### A4. Distinct causes established

- Candidate ID: `serial_killer_distinct_causes_established`.
- Title direction: separating a real source crisis from a criminal misuse of it.
- Description direction: use a valid connected inquiry to reject an unsupported attribution, stop the responsible Event 102 activity and produce a real source-approved consequence.
- Eligible country: a human-controlled affected investigator in a genuine Evolution III connection.
- Unlock: an active eligible source participates in a supported connection. The player correctly distinguishes the source's actual incident or identity from a separate Event 102 action, completes the relevant culprit or cell operation, and receives an accepted meaningful source consequence or current evidence result tied to that exact source participant. The credited proof contains the real contradictory observation, without requiring the player to choose the false theory first.
- Disqualifiers: a rumor-only connection, initializing an absent source, invoking a disabled source, a rejected or stale source operation narrated as success, hidden identity exposed before its source gate, duplicated victims, or a cosmetic event-name reference with no applied consequence.
- Difficulty and purpose: hard and situational. It rewards source-aware reasoning and a completed connected case while avoiding deliberate-failure incentives.
- Visibility: hidden until a real relevant connection is discovered. Locked and early revealed wording must not name Hannibal, Wendigo or another concealed identity before its source permits it. The accomplishment can be earned through any of the supported named source families, not only hidden content.
- Tracking: source availability and knowledge proof at the relevant times, source actor/object identity, current case, the player's valid attribution deduction, Event 102 outcome, accepted source result and separate incident ownership.
- 64 by 64 completed icon direction: two clearly distinct evidence trails resolved by a comparison instrument or divided dossier. Keep the shared icon neutral so it cannot reveal which hidden source exists.

### A5. Worldwide crisis concluded

- Candidate ID: `serial_killer_worldwide_crisis_concluded`.
- Title direction: a worldwide investigation reaching a genuine ending.
- Description direction: make a material contribution to the fully contained global crisis, including its independent offenders and organizations.
- Eligible country: a human-controlled country with a recorded substantive stopping or cell-containment contribution during that episode.
- Unlock: the episode reached its real worldwide manifestation with the required countries, continents, distinct offenders and functioning cell. The original, every established independent killer and all dangerous surviving cell components then receive genuine permanent containment or resolution. The credited player completed at least one full individual or organizational inquiry and a material operation during the global phase. Other countries may complete their own cases through human or AI investigation.
- Disqualifiers: original-only capture while independent threats survive, a quiet but active cell, disabled or suspended work treated as closure, disappearance of a country pointer, terminal interruption, unused recruitment reservations counted as enemies, or a player who only read the global news.
- Difficulty and purpose: very hard. The challenge is contributing to a real worldwide conclusion without demanding that one player personally answer every foreign case.
- Visibility: visible after the worldwide pattern is confirmed. It does not reveal a future supernatural branch.
- Tracking: manifested global proof, episode-wide real-threat lifecycle, each actor and cell disposition, the player's complete case and operation contribution, and a once-only true closure receipt.
- 64 by 64 completed icon direction: a closed international case dossier with a simple globe form and preserved remembrance detail. Compose for native size and keep it distinct from the global news image.
