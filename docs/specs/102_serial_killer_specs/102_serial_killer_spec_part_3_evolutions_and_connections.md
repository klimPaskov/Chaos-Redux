# 102 Serial Killer: evolutions and connected investigations

All new route and action labels are working labels, not final localisation. This part specifies the three evolutions and connections attributed to the user brief, with acceptance governed by [Part 1](102_serial_killer_spec_part_1_core.md#design-authority-and-reading-order). Numbers that describe pacing or capacity are proposed tuning anchors. Historical repository observations and current integration evidence are distinguished in the coding prompt.

## How an evolution becomes real

Chaos establishes eligibility. A living crisis and a concrete new development establish manifestation. Passing 200, 400 or 600 Chaos does not finish a case, create a culprit after closure or skip an inquiry. Each evolution records one distinct entry through normal Event Logs when its actual mutation begins.

Use variable timing centered on the project's ordinary evolution pacing, approximately a 90-day eligible waiting period before case-specific factors. The relevant history changes that wait. Repeated supported failures, continued free activity, actual travel opportunities, a demonstrated audience for copycats and a genuine source-event connection can bring a development forward. Effective local protection, isolation and successful disruption can delay or prevent its required manifestation.

The waiting period is an opportunity for play. It is not a universal expiry date for the killer. Reaching a threshold while a country is completing its final correct inquiry must not make capture fail or spawn a successor before the concluding action can commit.

In the default configuration the mutations develop in order. A campaign already above 600 when Event 102 starts still begins with a comprehensible domestic inquiry and the original actor. It receives observable progression before a global network or a crossover can exist. Use meaningful time and activity between manifestations, not three immediate threshold popups.

Each evolution respects its own current enable state. A disabled earlier mutation must not be fabricated as a prerequisite for another enabled one. Later enabled content can mature from the surviving crisis using the equivalent activity and pacing requirements, while the disabled mutation's effects and log record remain absent. Thus a global network can develop without secretly granting the disabled regional frequency bonus, and a compatible original can acquire a source connection without inventing a disabled cult stage. Re-enabling a switch does not award backdated manifestations.

If a mutation loses its required participant before manifestation, the waiting opportunity returns to the active crisis. If the last real threat has been resolved, it is cancelled permanently with the episode. Once a mutation has occurred, history remains. Current effects continue only for the actors, places and enabled mechanics that still justify them.

Evolution activation, the log record and unlocking its content each give zero direct Chaos. The actual consequences use the impact map in Part 4.

## Evolution I: Escalating Murders

### A more dangerous original

At 200 or more Chaos, continued free activity can lead to Escalating Murders. The original's opportunities become much more frequent and more severe within the physical limits in Part 1. New scenes show larger civilian groups, intimidating preparation or a more deliberate pattern. Communities change how they work and travel. Authorities face several pieces of local evidence while trying to prevent the next opportunity.

The increase is attached to the existing original actor. It does not multiply that person, seed a copycat or establish a cult. An apparent matching foreign crime must first fit that person's possible journey. A later global copycat may imitate this public pattern, but it cannot have existed secretly as part of this first mutation.

Pressure and clues develop together. The Gourmet's access to respectable institutions exposes custody records. The Cipher Killer's greater volume of correspondence creates publication and delivery comparisons. The Hunter's more frequent travel reveals a repeatable route. The Ritualist's larger sequence leaves more independent witnesses and preparatory traces. Higher danger therefore creates more opportunities for reasoning, not just more damage.

### Real neighboring travel

The first foreign opportunities favor a country sharing a real land border with the original's current country or another country already on the same trail. Evaluate the actual current geography. Countries in the same region name or alliance are not automatically neighbors. Countries can be victims regardless of their initial major or player eligibility.

The original needs a feasible travel leg between its last verified location and its next opportunity. A usable border crossing, a supplied rail or road connection, and a plausible interval establish a land journey. War can make a crossing easier to conceal but harder to traverse. A closed or impassable crossing can redirect the trail to another real neighbor or keep the killer domestic.

An isolated island can experience the full intensified domestic series without an immediate foreign scene. Regional maritime travel requires a genuine nearby port connection and an evidential journey. Without one, the killer stays in accessible domestic territory until another feasible route or the global travel mutation becomes available. A decorative line across a sea is not proof of movement.

The next jurisdiction receives a scene or an authenticated request tied to its own country. It is not summoned to answer another country's clue in an unrelated popup. Both sides can investigate the same person from different evidence. Each local scene retains its occurrence date, so two delayed reports cannot imply that the original killed on opposite sides of an impossible journey at once.

### Three regional pursuit patterns

| Working route | Evidence structure | Cooperation changes | Local conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Border chronology | The sending country knows the last authentic scene and departure window. The receiving country knows a border arrival and a later scene. A public rumor has the wrong order. | Combining both authenticated records establishes which direction the same person traveled and which crossing is relevant. | A country can stop the traveler locally or prove departure and transmit the supported trail. |
| Split custody | An object or message is recorded before dispatch in one country and after receipt in another. The two records distinguish the carrier from a person who merely handled the outer package. | The recipient supplies the missing custody interval. A local copy of the shipping evidence remains obtainable if cooperation is refused. | The local handler is cleared or implicated on actual conduct. Clearing that handler does not claim the original has been captured abroad. |
| Interrupted route | A transport closure changes which actual neighboring route is possible. A stale report still points to the old crossing. | Timely local confirmation avoids protecting a route that no longer operates and supports a current interception. | Protection of the real crossing can deny the specific opportunity. The actor remains at its actual alternative location until a full case stops it. |

Each pattern uses the individual casebook's full deduction chain. Shared evidence can provide one of those deductions with its facts visible. It cannot replace all four with a diplomatic agreement. If the original's identity is already proven abroad, the receiving country still establishes its current local presence and the valid stopping plan.

### What refusal and delay mean

Refusing a request leaves a particular fact unavailable through that channel and can preserve a real escape opportunity. It does not create a fixed punishment murder or transfer control of the refusing country's investigation to a foreign player. The affected government can obtain an equivalent decisive fact locally after additional work, or follow the next real scene when the trail moves.

A late packet can still be useful history. It must be presented as late when its travel prediction has expired. Repeating the same request cannot roll a different truth, stack cooperation bonuses or make a country share information that it refused to disclose.

The original can move back across a frontier. Return is a real journey with a new local opportunity, not an excuse for permanent fear modifiers in every country ever visited. Each country tracks whether the particular threat is present, departed, stopped or unknown on the available evidence.

## Evolution II: A Worldwide Cult of Killers

### Manifestation at a real global scale

At 400 or more Chaos, a continuing unresolved crisis can inspire independent offenders and functioning cult organizations. The global mutation needs actual reach beyond the initial continent. A chain of neighboring countries on the same continent is still regional, regardless of how many borders it crosses.

The first global development establishes a supported distant connection, an independent offender in a valid country and a real organized cell with leadership and support. The original remains at its actual location and can later travel through documented distant connections. It is not copied into the new country's first scene.

The worldwide news is published only when investigators have confirmed a pattern across at least three valid countries on at least two continents, with at least two distinct offenders and one functioning organized cell. Some of those countries can come from the preceding regional trail. The distant component must be real. Evidence of shared correspondence, imitation or support explains why the cases form a global phenomenon.

If current geography offers fewer than three valid countries, defer the international manifestation until a genuine qualifying global pattern can exist. Do not mislabel one country's remaining domestic cells as worldwide. Existing domestic play and earlier enabled mutations remain fully solvable.

The original does not need to be caught before independent offenders appear. It must still be free and part of an active crisis when the first independent global development is committed. If the final original case resolves first and no independent threat has yet been established, the episode closes. An uncommitted recruitment idea is not a survivor.

### Distinct threats

The global crisis has three kinds of active threat with different stopping conditions.

| Threat | What exists in the world | What it can do | What stops it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original killer | The same actor selected at the first incident | Travel, commit personally attributed crimes and influence followers | A complete correct inquiry against that actor |
| Independent killer | A newly established person with its own identity, archetype and case | Copy a visible method, invent a different pattern, cooperate with a cell or operate alone | Its own complete correct inquiry and permanent disposition |
| Cult cell | Actual leadership, available members and specific support locations or contacts | Recruit, conceal people, fund preparation, coordinate a bounded operation and obstruct an inquiry | Leadership and operational support neutralized, with dangerous surviving members actually contained or separately tracked |

A copycat has its own origin date and first actual incident. Similarity is not identity. Its private case cannot inherit the original's biography, confession, individual evidence or arrest. It may share a public method, but its final attribution must rest on its own conduct.

Independent archetypes are varied deliberately. A Gourmet can inspire a Hunter who follows the public travel pattern or a Ritualist who turns publicity into a sequence. Such an offender is not a supernatural conversion of the original. The game should not force every global campaign into seven indistinguishable versions of the initial family.

A cell is more than a modifier attached to a killer. It has a currently responsible leader, an actual support purpose and available members. Members can include a named internal killer record and a small finite cohort of other participants. Those participants have recorded functions such as courier, recruiter or safehouse keeper. A cohort's size can change through documented recruitment, arrest, withdrawal or loss. It cannot regenerate from an empty record.

A new cell ordinarily starts with three to six actual participants. Its proposed maximum is twelve, including any affiliated independently tracked killer. A person has one primary membership and one participation schedule. References from other cells describe contacts or shared evidence, not another copy of that person. A transfer ends the old membership before the new one becomes active. These small finite cohorts justify the operation scale and make complete containment a real accounting of surviving people.

### Growth with a cause

Recruitment needs a living recruiter or leader, a supported local audience and an opportunity. Publicity about unresolved murders, an existing radical circle, frightened displaced people or a proven link to another event can influence who becomes reachable. The game does not imply that ordinary religious, cultural or ethnic communities are criminal cults. The organization is fictional and identified through its actual conduct.

The default recruitment opportunity is one operation per cell every twenty-one to forty-two days, with no more than one successful addition of one to three actual new participants during that interval. Recruitment cannot exceed remaining membership capacity. A cell without a living recruiter, usable support or an actual reachable audience cannot refill itself. Disruption changes the next genuine opportunity, rather than running repeated recruitment rolls until one succeeds.

Growth occurs in steps that leave evidence. A new courier route can reveal a repeated recipient. A new safehouse requires a place and a keeper. A funded operation leaves a transfer or procurement connection. A new killer separates from the support group only after the game establishes a distinct person and case.

Successful investigation cuts these concrete links. Removing a recruiter stops recruitment by that person. Securing a safehouse removes that refuge and can expose its actual visitors. Breaking a funding link reduces the connected cell's preparation capacity. Arresting a leader disrupts coordination and can expose successor candidates. Each benefit applies to the connected countries and operations, not the entire worldwide crisis by default.

Failed infiltration can alert a current cell and cause it to move a real safehouse or change a real contact. A wrong murder suspect can buy that same cell time to recruit. These effects require the surviving participants and an available destination. Failure cannot create a second network record from the same people or restore a cell whose last members have been contained.

### Operational capacity and player attention

The proposed active-world envelope is eight independently mobile killer records including the original and four active cult cell records. New recruitment and travel normally admit no more than two locally operating violent threats into one country. A cell's participating cohort is finite and belongs to that cell. The cell and an affiliated killer cannot both schedule an independent attack using the same person's same opportunity.

These are performance and attention ceilings, not recruitment rewards or player-visible meters. A proposed downward tuning change requires recorded acceptance if it reduces the requested capacity. It must preserve multiple independent offenders, organized cells, international breadth and independent closure.

At a ceiling, prospective recruitment remains unrealized and creates no murder worker or hidden active enemy. A cell can improve an existing support relationship within its real capacity instead. The game must not create an unlimited queue of already-existing attackers behind the visible record limit.

Reserve the required free cell capacity before a planned fracture becomes an independent faction in the world or is presented as one in evidence. The N3 route needs capacity for both actual branches at that point. A reservation is capacity only. It has no member, murder opportunity, pressure or closure status and is released if the proposed fracture becomes invalid. If capacity is unavailable, the existing cell can change leadership, lose support or remain cohesive, but the game does not establish an untracked independent fragment. Every already established fragment retains its real identity until resolved.

The local two-threat limit governs prospective admission. It does not delete people when annexation or controller changes bring existing threats under one administration. Such a consolidation can temporarily leave more than two real local threats. Preserve all of them within the unchanged global ceilings, suspend new local admissions and use the shared national pressure ceiling and bounded case service in Part 4. A full destination discovered before travel commits leaves the original at its actual previous location or on its still-valid existing travel leg while another permitted destination is considered. It never erases or duplicates the traveler.

Countries enter in staggered cohorts, normally one or two newly affected countries over an eligible spread interval of several weeks. Actual geography, transport, surviving organizations, local capacity and previous successful containment influence the destination. An eligible country with no current Event 102 burden generally has priority over a country already handling two threats. Human-controlled countries remain legitimate victims, without becoming the default dumping ground for every new case.

Each affected country gets one foreground deduction chain. Other existing local cases receive ordinary evidence-based investigation on their own bounded work dates, including possible full resolution. The opening handling choice explains this local-investigator role. A human can take over at the next unresolved step. Secondary cases do not produce simultaneous streams of compulsory deductions. Part 4 defines service dates, delegation, summaries and deferral so that solving the mystery remains practical in multiplayer.

### The international news event

This is a major international news event with a wide, monochrome documentary image. Its subject is the confirmed worldwide pattern and the existence of distinct offenders and organized support. It distinguishes a traveling original from independent copycats when investigators have established that distinction.

The report gives verified countries or regions, the reason authorities now connect the cases, and the practical implication that cooperation and local investigations both matter. It avoids names, methods or source-event identities that the reader has not earned through the relevant knowledge gates. The original's hidden archetype is not globally exposed merely because one government has an unconfirmed theory.

The news fires once for the first proven global manifestation. Additional international summaries require a materially new development, such as an exposed coordinating organization or the final collapse of the worldwide crisis. Ordinary local murders and every arrested courier do not become global broadcasts.

The Event Logs evolution record and the news have different timing responsibilities. The mutation is recorded when its real global structure begins. The news waits for the specified public proof. A report delayed by investigation must not display a fictional earlier public revelation.

## Investigating the organization

An organization inquiry uses the same event interface as a murder inquiry. It asks the player to infer who leads, which support relationship matters and what operation can actually contain the remaining threat. There is no separate management window or public cult meter.

The following three families add organizational reasoning to the individual casebook. Each uses four decisive deductions, a supported containment operation and a genuine changed-fact retry. They are required gameplay families, not optional narrative examples.

### N1. Custody of a refuge

**First deduction.** Two suspect addresses appear in intercepted correspondence. At the first, a tenant receives public letters and leaves them sealed for collection. At the second, a caretaker witnesses the same known cell member admitting fugitives and storing their belongings. The player follows the second address as the supported refuge. A shared postal address by itself does not prove a safehouse.

**Second deduction.** A dated accommodation ledger records who authorizes stays. One organizer repeatedly approves new occupants and can refuse entry. A wealthy donor pays a fixed subscription but appears in none of the admissions. The supported inquiry follows the person controlling admissions, distinguishing leadership over this refuge from wealth or publicity.

**Third deduction.** The players see which connected cases used the refuge. Two current offenders stayed after their attributed incidents. A third person's entry was cancelled before arrival and confirmed elsewhere. Only the first two are operational dependencies of this refuge. The inquiry must not claim that every copied name in the ledger is an active member.

**Fourth deduction.** Current observation establishes that the admitting organizer and the remaining participants will be present for a documented transfer, while both resident offenders are either already contained or separately identified for coordinated containment. Protecting the civilian caretaker and securing those actual participants removes the refuge. Merely arresting the donor leaves it functional.

**Result.** The cell loses this support location. Its connected operations are denied that refuge. The organizer and actual present participants receive recorded dispositions. Any independently active offender who was absent remains a separate case. Full cell closure occurs only if this operation accounts for the cell's remaining dangerous membership and support.

**Failed lead.** Pursuing the subscription donor leaves admission authority intact. The later disproof is a new witnessed admission signed by the same organizer after the donor's access was removed. The original admissions record already showed the relevant distinction.

**Changed-fact retry.** A new refuge has a public caretaker who cannot admit anyone without a remote authorization. D1 follows the new refuge's verified custody observations to establish the actual support location. At D2, the displayed ledger and refusal show that the remote organizer now controls admission. The correct leadership inference changes from the former on-site admissions-controller theory. The caretaker remains a protected witness. The retry follows the authenticated authorizer and their documented local intermediary.

D3 uses current records from the new refuge to establish which offenders actually reside there, which entry was cancelled and which absent person is independently confirmed elsewhere. It preserves the original distinction between current operational dependencies and names merely copied into a ledger. Old-site residence or presence cannot stand in for these new-site facts.

D4 uses current observation of the local intermediary and the actual present participants at the documented transfer. The operation protects the caretaker, secures those identified present participants and removes only the proved local support. The remote organizer is not physically present and remains an active tracked participant requiring a separate verified resolution, along with any absent dangerous member. Local support loss does not establish full cell closure while those real participants remain unresolved.

### N2. A courier with several customers

**First deduction.** A courier carries sealed ordinary parcels and a separate set of cell instructions. Delivery observations and matching dispatch records distinguish the two streams. The inquiry follows the instruction stream, without treating every customer as a member.

**Second deduction.** Three recipients appear in the instructions. One forwards an unchanged envelope. One changes the requested meeting location and the receiving participants obey that change. One receives the instruction after the meeting. The person whose authenticated changes control the meeting is the supported coordinator lead.

**Third deduction.** A recovered route list contains two current meeting locations and one former location crossed out before dispatch. Current observations confirm the two active sites. The correct response separates current operational support from historical evidence. Raiding only the crossed-out site would find a real past connection but leave the current operation intact.

**Fourth deduction.** A fresh observed dispatch establishes where the coordinator and remaining current participants will convene. The affected countries can combine their current records to contain those people and close the active route. If cooperation is unavailable, the local branch follows a supplied duplicate instruction and resolves its own current site.

**Result.** The real courier relationship and the specified local or linked operation are disabled. An innocent parcel customer remains uninvolved. An independent killer not present on this route retains its case.

**Failed lead.** Arresting the simple forwarder appears promising until a new instruction still changes the operation through the actual coordinator. The earlier ability to alter accepted instructions was the decisive fact.

**Changed-fact retry.** The old coordinator is absent or already contained. New evidence shows that the apparent forwarder is now issuing authenticated alterations that participants follow, while the other recipient merely relays them. The leadership inference changes because authority has actually changed. A surviving member can take over. A dead or contained former leader does not return.

### N3. Fracture or coordination

**First deduction.** Two local groups use the same public symbol. One follows identical fresh instructions with shared delivery marks. The other explicitly changes the sequence and its observed actions follow the changed order. The inquiry distinguishes the coordinated branch from the independent faction. Shared symbols alone do not justify a single operation against both.

**Second deduction.** The coordinated branch shares a refuge keeper and an instruction source. The independent faction has moved to a different site and stopped using both. The supported disruption targets the still-shared support relationship. It does not claim that the independent faction will collapse through a link it no longer uses.

**Third deduction.** Witnessed attendance establishes which dangerous participants remain in the coordinated branch after earlier arrests. The leader's recorded successor has already withdrawn under verified protection. A different active recruiter is directing the remaining meeting. The inquiry follows the actual active authority, not a stale succession list.

**Fourth deduction.** The present authority and remaining dangerous participants are secured through the current meeting evidence. The independent faction's separate location is transmitted to its own case. The country can obtain a genuine coordinated-branch conclusion without pretending that the detached faction has vanished.

**Result.** The shared branch is dismantled and its support benefits end in the connected countries. The independent faction survives only if its actual members, location and record were already established under the reserved capacity. It retains that separate cell identity or an already evidenced affiliation with an existing compatible cell. It is not created retroactively by the success popup, and reaching the cap cannot silently remove it.

**Failed lead.** Treating the common symbol as proof of continued unity leaves the detached faction active. A later independently authenticated instruction and observed new sequence expose the mistake. The earlier changed order already provided the required warning.

**Changed-fact retry.** Fresh evidence shows the formerly detached faction accepting the same coordinator's amendments and using the shared refuge again. The correct inference is now renewed coordination. Containment can affect both branches only after their present membership and shared dependency are proved. Visual similarity remains insufficient.

## Evolution III: Entangled with Chaos

### A connection needs both sides

At 600 or more Chaos, a currently active Event 102 threat can form a consequential link to another event that genuinely exists in the campaign. The connection can provide concealment, evidence, shelter, recruitment or a dangerous opportunity. It changes an investigation and at least one real participating threat. It is not satisfied by inserting another event's name into a report.

The source confirms its current availability, participant identity, relevant route, knowledge gate and permitted consequence. Historical flags, a familiar country tag or a public rumor are insufficient. A disabled or already resolved source cannot be initialized to make an Event 102 branch work.

This mutation is recorded on its first actual accepted connection. Later different connections are branches of the same third evolution. They do not introduce a fourth evolution. The original can remain fully human throughout, including a Gourmet at very high Chaos. A cult connected to a supernatural crisis can coexist with an ordinary human original who has never encountered it.

Each connection has a complete local investigative alternative. If the source becomes unavailable after the branch begins, the game preserves the facts already learned and supplies a grounded route to stop the surviving Event 102 actor. A source disappearance cannot trap the player at a question whose only valid answer has ceased to exist.

### Cannibalism and Hannibal

An active Gourmet or a compatible cell can discover a shelter, courier or procurement relationship with a real Cannibalism network participant. The connection uses the source's actual actor and current node. It does not make a second Hannibal organization under Event 102.

The first case facts can be anonymous. Two recovered packing records may share an individually authenticated destination with a current source node. A medical order may connect the Event 102 suspect to a real sheltering participant. The evidence establishes that relationship before the branch offers a joint operation or a shelter benefit.

Before the source's own Hannibal reveal, reports refer to the documented contact or network relationship without identifying Hannibal, displaying his face or implying that a single concealed mastermind has already been publicly established. The source's existing reveal gate remains authoritative. This follows the inspected Event 014 overview's concealed-coordinator rules, recorded in the coding prompt's pinned sources.

The investigation compares three explanations. The suspect might be using a real refuge, merely copying a network-associated symbol, or handling legitimate material that happened to pass through the same address. Individually authenticated travel, custody and current node evidence distinguish them. A cuisine, a profession or a public symbol cannot establish membership.

A correct infiltration removes the Event 102 shelter relationship and offers a bounded disruption to that exact source-owned node. The Cannibalism owner accepts and applies the permitted consequence. The player sees the applied result, including a source rejection if the node ceased to exist. Event 102 does not write the source's strength, consumption or command values directly.

If the inquiry fails, an actual surviving contact can shelter the killer or help move a current safehouse. That movement leaves a new route and remains within real membership, geography and capacity. The failure does not create a new Cannibalism actor or issue another consumption operation.

An existing Cannibalism crime can supply a clue. Its victims are evidence only in Event 102. Independently caused murders by the Serial Killer remain separate physical incidents. Neither event counts the same people twice.

### Murder Mystery

An active political inquiry and an active civilian inquiry can discover a shared courier, correspondence source, building or route. The link can expose a real assassination operation while also advancing the serial case. It does not imply that the two principal culprits are the same person.

A useful branch compares message provenance. One document belongs to the current political case and carries its actual case identity. Another concerns the civilian series. A courier delivers both. The player must distinguish the courier's access, the originators' separate instructions and the person responsible for each crime. A shared delivery service cannot become proof of a universal mastermind.

The Serial Killer inquiry can offer a verified fact that improves the current Murder Mystery case through a source-approved evidence or route consequence. The political inquiry can return an authenticated observation relevant to the civilian suspect's movement or correspondence. Each packet is bound to the current source case or cell generation.

The receiving inquiry still owns its decisive reasoning and final operation. A successful Event 102 arrest does not automatically complete a political inquiry. A successful political operation does not stop a surviving civilian killer unless the separate case has actually identified and contained that same proven person through its own authorized result.

Political deaths, named characters and political resolutions remain with Event 039. An Event 102 clue cannot invoke its character-removal callback as a generic civilian casualty effect. If the political case retires, splits or enters a transaction before the packet resolves, the branch keeps the historical fact and requests a current admissible fact or a local alternative.

### Death or a genuinely active supernatural crisis

A Ritualist-led cell can exploit an existing phenomenon without having caused it. A route already affected by Death might be used to explain away ordinary disappearances. A cult may circulate false instructions that direct people toward an existing danger or conceal its own crime among real warnings. The source incident must exist in the affected country's relevant experience.

The inquiry distinguishes source-caused losses from the cell's independently caused murders. Actual occurrence dates, safe surviving witnesses and the source's confirmed affected places establish the difference. A cell's claim to control Death is not taken as a fact. The player can expose a human deception while the source phenomenon remains real.

A concrete success can remove the cell's misleading guidance, close its current support route and offer a valid source observation to the country's existing investigation. A concrete failure can let an actual cult participant maintain that interference or exploit a current route opportunity. Any change to Death's maritime evidence or source pressure is accepted and applied by the Death owner.

The source's presence, active crisis status, local evidence and public revelation are separate checks. A historical wasteland or the continued existence of DTH does not establish all of them. The inspected owner surfaces make those distinctions material. Event 102 does not call a consumption or setup helper to obtain an apparently harmless crossover consequence.

The same structure can support another already active supernatural source after its own contract is inspected. Its unique rules must be specified before implementation. A generic supernatural flag is not an interchangeable actor or permission to summon a phenomenon.

### Active outbreaks and mass disasters

The crisis must be present in the relevant place and time. An evacuation, closed institution, damaged route or disrupted record system can provide a real opportunity for an existing cell. The investigation then separates ordinary source losses from an independently caused disappearance.

One route compares departure records with arrival records. The source crisis explains why people traveled. Verified arrivals show that a particular group survived that leg. A later witnessed diversion links the cell to a different place. The correct inquiry follows the diversion rather than recording the group as source casualties a second time.

A second route examines an abandoned facility. The source event explains its closure, but current supplies, recent admission records and observed visitors establish that the cell is actively using it. An old ruin by itself does not prove a living network.

A third route follows recruitment among displaced people. Evidence identifies a real recruiter, a specific fraudulent promise and the resulting support relationship. Protecting those civilians and removing that recruiter reduces the cell's actual available participation. It does not stigmatize all refugees or all recipients of relief.

Success closes the Event 102 concealment or recruitment opportunity and can offer a bounded source-approved relief or evidence result. The source retains its mortality, spread, disaster damage and recovery. Failure does not independently amplify a plague or trigger another disaster without a separate accepted source operation.

The outbreak and disaster adapter must be inspected with its own owner before implementation. This package specifies the required gameplay and ownership boundary. The targeted repository pass did not establish a universal external adapter for those owners.

### Hidden Wendigo connection

This branch is available only when the separate source requirements for an actual Wendigo actor or an already valid connected source structure are satisfied. A Gourmet, a compatible cell, high Chaos or a story about cannibalism cannot satisfy those requirements on its own.

The initial evidence remains appropriately hidden. A recovered route can point to a current source-owned place. A consistent source observation can distinguish an actual eligible connection from the killer's theatrical imitation. Public previews, generic artwork and early report labels must not reveal the hidden identity before the relevant investigation and source gates allow it.

If a genuine connection is confirmed before a source terminal lock, a complete Event 102 inquiry can contribute a specific lead against a current source-owned cell or anchor. The source verifies its actor origin, generation, current ownership, route and permitted counterplay. Any operation, cost or reduction remains under the source's rules.

The original killer is not transformed merely to supply this branch. The actual source actor is not duplicated or replaced. An inherited anchor still requires its current source provenance. A dormant country shell, a stale anchor or an old map label is not a living participant.

The source's independent terminal prerequisites remain binding. Event 102's 600-Chaos evolution, an exposed connection or a failed investigation cannot set a final lock, force a merge, award a terminal resource or bypass the source's own controls. A high-Chaos campaign can finish an entirely human Gourmet case while eligible hidden content exists elsewhere.

## Cross-event cooperation as a playable choice

The player first proves the relationship, then chooses an operation supported by that relationship. A source offer contains the actual observation, the local benefit and the possible consequence for the connected system. It does not expose internal identifiers or API terminology in the event prose.

The source owner can accept a current result, reject an ineligible one, report that the target has changed or apply no change because the relevant threat is already gone. Event 102 then branches honestly. A rejected disruption cannot be narrated as a successful raid. A source already resolved elsewhere can supply closure information without granting another reward.

A local independent path remains available. Refusing to share evidence can cost time or leave a useful route unexamined, but a player need not activate, fund or endorse another crisis to obtain the sole clue needed to catch their own killer. The core murder inquiry remains solvable in every supported source configuration.

## Ending a worldwide crisis

A national ending identifies what changed locally. It can stop an independent killer, remove a functioning refuge, dismantle a cell, protect a current threatened place or prove that the original has departed. The country's active pressure is recalculated from its remaining real threats. Success abroad can help, but it is not required for every form of national relief.

A cell survives leadership loss only through actual surviving members with an operational relationship. If its command is broken and its remaining participants are all contained, the cell closes. If one still-active organizer can reassemble a real cohort, a later fracture or regeneration is possible and leaves fresh evidence. The original killer's fame alone cannot repopulate an empty cell.

Global closure requires the original to be permanently stopped and every established independent killer and dangerous cell component to be stopped or genuinely contained. It cancels future unrealized recruitment opportunities and travel offers. It preserves confirmed history and sends one appropriately scoped conclusion, with credit for countries that made material contributions.

A quiet world map is not sufficient proof. A cell temporarily deprived of a safehouse can still have dangerous members. Conversely, a historical rumor or a prospective recruitment roll is not an active enemy that can hold closure hostage. Only established real threats determine whether the episode remains alive.
