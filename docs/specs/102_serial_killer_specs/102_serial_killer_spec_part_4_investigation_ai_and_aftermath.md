# 102 Serial Killer: investigation delivery, AI and aftermath

New action, outcome and achievement labels in this part are working labels, not final localisation. The design follows the current brief's event-chain interface, independent AI inquiries, civilian accounting and meaningful national outcomes. Tuning figures are proposed design targets rather than measured game results.

## Presenting an inquiry through events

The player experiences one foreground local case at a time. Its event sequence supplies a scene, a comparison, a supported decision and the resulting development. A small temporary native decision can reopen the current pending inquiry. It appears only while that country's actual case needs attention and opens the same facts and choice state.

Every decisive event recalls the facts necessary for that step. The recap identifies what is verified, what is only reported and what has already been disproved. It includes relevant dates, locations, movement direction and document versions. It excludes administrative transaction details. A player returning after a save or a long multiplayer session can reason from the event currently in front of them.

The normal choice set contains two or three semantic interpretations plus a neutral deferral. Use the meaningful alternatives actually supported by that casebook step. Do not pad a two-way evidential distinction with an invented third interpretation. The interpretations are materially different readings of the displayed evidence. Their order varies within the non-default positions. The first option is the safe deferral when an automatic native timeout could otherwise commit an irreversible choice. Deferral preserves the same case and does not reveal, reroll or answer the question.

Clue selection is not a resource purchase. The player obtains every decisive fact through the ordinary investigation. Optional protection, expedited comparison or cooperation changes time, opportunity and consequences. It never sells the sole fact needed to solve the case.

### Presentation structure

| Surface | Information it supplies | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| First local report | Real place, current victims, reliable initial observations and the government's immediate duty | Starts the local case and offers its initial handling choice |
| Deduction event | Relevant recap, new observation if any, clear inferential question and competing intentions | Commits one current case deduction or defers it |
| False-lead report | The accepted theory, the observation that contradicts it and the consequence of the lost opportunity | Clears the mistaken local attribution and prepares a genuinely changed route |
| Protection or assistance event | Exact target, expected benefit, timing and any current cost | Changes a real opportunity or supplies authenticated evidence |
| Final operation | Proven actor, current intervention point, protected civilians and explicit expected result | Permanently stops the identified actor after a fresh valid confirmation |
| Local conclusion | What has ended locally, who contributed and what real threat remains elsewhere | Removes only the pressure and work no longer justified |
| International news | A verified worldwide development and its practical significance | Informs relevant readers without assigning foreign clue choices |
| Normal Event Details and history | Established status and historical outcomes, with appropriate spoiler gates | Supports review and navigation through the existing shared surfaces |

Every factual field is generated from the current case and the recorded incident. A late report states that it concerns a past crime. A prevented incident reports prevention, not positive deaths. A partial population loss reports the measured count. The visual image supports mood and recognition, while the text carries all decisive evidence.

### Readability and spoilers

Use plain text suitable for the light event and news surfaces. Do not encode a clue in color or require the player to distinguish nearly identical icons. Dates use an unambiguous format. A coordinate puzzle explicitly defines rows, columns and its worked checks. Reflected arrangements describe the relevant asymmetric object in words as well as any illustration.

The original's true archetype remains hidden until evidence supports it. The casebook's route names and internal answer labels never appear as ready-made hints. Event Details may summarize the established pattern, but not the next correct choice. Unrelated countries receive only information their report or shared knowledge permits.

The later Hannibal and Wendigo connections obey their source reveal gates on every surface, including news images, tooltips, achievements, event previews and history captions. A file called a hidden connection internally is not a public clue. The ordinary Gourmet's art must remain compatible with an entirely human case.

## The opening and the evidence that already exists

The first report can reveal a small linked series rather than just a single known death. If a route needs several scenes at its first deduction, each murder scene must correspond to a real Event 102 incident with a feasible occurrence order and a population receipt. The aggregate opening series stays within the ordinary small-incident scale and the original's real movement capacity. It cannot add unrecorded historical victims to make a puzzle look established.

A prepared opening operation can leave several scene locations within one country's usable territory. Apply each physical loss to its actual state once, and do not report a victim before that incident has committed. When a route requires more history than is available, preliminary local work continues until real incidents or genuinely prevented preparations provide the stated observations. It does not create bonus murders merely to fill four clue slots.

H1 explicitly needs separate dated service days, and H2 needs the relevant changes of work group across different weeks. Their ordinary default is to start handling and protection at the first incident, then offer D1 only when compatible real dated comparisons exist. A single prepared opening operation cannot stand in for those different days or weeks. R3's supplied planned and actual date sets must likewise fit the current actor's opportunity band. A compact casebook date is a fictional evidence value to instantiate consistently, never permission to force an extra early murder.

Case facts can also come from nonfatal preparation, a recovered message or an averted attempt where the route permits that interpretation. The localization and evidence must identify those as such. A prevented scene cannot be called a completed murder to preserve an older paragraph's wording.

This chronology rule applies equally to AI cases and retries. The actor's current location, previous movements and all already-known facts constrain the next compatible family. A route that would require an impossible second profession, simultaneous travel or a forgotten exoneration is not eligible for that actor's next attempt.

## Time and attention

An ordinary deduction arrives after investigators could plausibly perform the required comparison, typically a few days to a week. A cross-border fact can take longer. Several facts can be summarized in one report when they arrive together. The offender's real opportunity schedule runs independently and does not advance when the player clicks through reports.

The expected foreground cadence is usually one or two meaningful decisions in an in-game week during active inquiry. A serious escalated case can briefly demand more, but it must not become a daily click tax. A murder that adds no new decision-relevant fact can join the next local summary while its actual civilian loss still occurs at the correct time.

One case is foregrounded per country. Other existing local cases receive ordinary evidence-based investigation and temporary protection through the country's police. The first handling event explains that this work continues while the player leads the foreground case. It can progress through all four deductions and a valid permanent result. When the player solves, delegates or parks the first inquiry, the next ready case can take its place. A globally active actor does not gain unlimited opportunities because another popup is waiting.

Each active local case has its own next work date, normally four to seven in-game days after its last completed step. On that date a secondary or explicitly delegated case performs at most one evidence-based AI deduction if the necessary facts are available. A necessary comparison can extend the date by one stated report interval. Once a secondary deduction is answerable, it must receive its next investigator action within ten days. An unanswered foreground popup cannot postpone that date. Newly consolidated cases after a controller change receive the same bounded service under the fixed global actor envelope.

That ten-day service means an actual attempted semantic deduction. Deferral, an optional protection action or a summary cannot satisfy it. An evidenced imminent opportunity can bring the next ready deduction forward, usually to one to three days, so the ordinary work interval does not make a correctly predicted interception impossible by construction. Every accelerated step still requires its supplied evidence and the same reasoning. If the facts cannot legitimately arrive before that opportunity, preserve the proof for the next actual compatible operation instead of inventing an earlier report or an extra killing.

Combine ordinary secondary updates into a summary at most once every fourteen days. A material capture, proven departure or urgent evidenced-place warning can be reported when it occurs. Human takeover preserves the exact next unanswered step, accepted facts and work date. The temporary re-entry flow can move to the next current local case without spawning duplicate inquiries. These service intervals are proposed tuning bounds for later probability and multiplayer review, not case-expiry timers.

The same government can delegate a case to ordinary investigators. Delegation runs the evidence-based AI described below for that actual case and reports material outcomes. A player can resume at the next unresolved step. Delegation neither changes the true offender nor grants an automatic arrest. Human governments can therefore manage attention without abandoning their country's real participation.

Deferral is narrower than delegation. It simply leaves the current question unanswered. It does not wipe out local police activity, create duplicate pending reports or cancel ordinary protection. A deferred case can be reopened through the temporary decision. Real time can still create another murder opportunity, so indefinite deferral has a clear cost without becoming an artificial answer timeout.

### Current operation conflicts

If the target moves or a state changes controller while a choice is open, the final click rechecks the operation's actual context. A stale final operation is cancelled without a false success, duplicate cost or arbitrary death. The case then supplies the current continuation supported by retained evidence.

Deduction correctness is evaluated against the facts actually displayed for that dated question. A later closure or movement cannot retroactively make its supported inference wrong. Retain the four-step proof when the uncommitted stopping location becomes stale, and refresh only the current operational continuation. Do not restart the full case or count a new investigative failure because the world changed after the correct answer was presented.

The original cannot use this validation as an unannounced escape after four correct deductions. Once the supported final operation has been validly committed against the current actor, its result is permanent. Movement and final containment use one ordered resolution, so two countries cannot both resolve the same person independently.

If two players have valid operations against the same actor, the first valid permanent result ends that actor's activity. The other receives the actual outcome and contribution credit where its authenticated evidence mattered. It does not execute another loss, consume a second operation cost or manufacture a replacement culprit.

## Supporting actions

These are compact event choices and one temporary re-entry decision. They support the deduction chain. They do not form a separate persistent management system.

### Review the current inquiry

The temporary native decision is available only to the country with an unresolved local case. It shows which case will open and whether the country is currently delegating it. It costs no PP, CP, manpower or equipment. Opening it does not restart a timer or receive a new answer order that changes the evidence.

If a current dialog already exists, the decision returns to that same inquiry without duplicating it. On local closure the entry disappears. Historical facts remain available through the normal log and details surfaces.

### Protect an evidenced place

The ordinary event response can protect the particular civilian place supported by the current evidence. The basic version is available to a weak or insolvent government. It redirects existing local work for one report interval, which can delay the next comparison. Its benefit is local, such as reducing the loss if the next actual opportunity targets that place or allowing a threatened civilian to remain safe.

A more concentrated response can add a modest dynamically quoted PP cost and a short local work disruption. The proposed PP range is roughly 5 to 15, scaling with the actual task and national capacity rather than the player's current stockpile alone. The event states the affected place, duration and complete cost before commitment. It does not require CP above the project's limit or an intelligence-agency DLC.

A correct place prediction can deny that specific opportunity entirely. A wrong place prediction still protects those civilians, but it does not move the murderer there or cause a punishment death there. It leaves the actor's actual opportunity elsewhere subject to the normal schedule.

Protection cannot be stacked indefinitely to purchase permanent safety everywhere. An existing covered place is extended only within the stated action's limit, and a country chooses between its real current demands. Identifying and stopping the actor remains the durable solution.

### Compare or transmit a specific record

Ordinary comparison and evidence transmission are administrative and available without a paid requirement. They take the time needed for the current channel. The packet contains a fact, its origin and its reliability. It does not contain a hidden answer key.

An expedited response can spend a small current quote to shorten a real delivery delay. If no relevant delay or authenticated packet exists, the paid option is unavailable. A rejected or stale receipt does not spend resources. The sender cannot pay to force another human government to cooperate or expose information it has not authorized in the event flow.

### Reassess a failed inquiry

After repeated failed attempts, the country can assign an independent review of the contradictions already established. This costs a report interval, not money required for access. The review emphasizes the decisive comparison and preserves previous exonerations. It makes the next evidence presentation clearer and improves the AI's use of known reliability, without naming the correct option or changing the offender's truth.

AI governments adopt this review after sustained failure. A weak country therefore has a plausible route to improved performance instead of an indefinitely worsening competence penalty. The review is not a timer that closes the case.

## Cooperation between actual investigators

A government can share a specific authenticated observation, propose a joint comparison or request confirmation of a local fact. Every exchange names the real countries, the relevant case and what the recipient is being asked to do. A country without that fact can say it is unavailable. It does not manufacture a helpful clue for diplomatic convenience.

The receiving player makes choices only for their own affected government. An unrelated human does not answer the AI major's inquiry. A third country may receive an informational news report, but it receives a cooperation choice only when it actually holds a relevant record or can perform an evidenced local task.

Different political relationships affect willingness and speed. A friendly partner is more likely to cooperate promptly. A hostile government can restrict an observation or decline. The case remains solvable through a local equivalent, though it can take longer or permit another real opportunity. A necessary clue is never permanently locked behind another player's goodwill.

Shared evidence can shorten a later inquiry when it proves the same actual relationship. An authenticated cell admissions ledger can spare a recipient from reconstructing that admissions system. It cannot identify a new copycat merely because the copycat likes the original's symbols. Every permanent stopping result still needs a complete supported chain for its actor.

Shared credit follows contributions. The government conducting the final valid operation gets the stopping record. Countries whose accepted facts were used receive cooperation credit. A country that merely received news, forwarded an unverified rumor or opened a request gets no equivalent achievement progress.

## AI that reasons imperfectly

An AI government uses the same scene facts, candidate interpretations, reliability distinctions and current operations as a human government. It does not select an option by reading the original's hidden archetype or a variable that directly marks the correct answer.

For each available interpretation, the AI evaluates observable support and contradiction. Verified chronology, independently confirmed custody and current movement evidence carry more weight than reputation, visual resemblance or an unsupported claim. Capacity and pressure affect how consistently the AI makes those comparisons. They do not change which answer is actually supported.

The implementation can represent observable facts as explicit features of the current case. Those features must correspond to information in the human-facing evidence. A debug audit should be able to show that the same fact supported both the human deduction and the AI's weighting. The hidden validator that decides whether a deduction is correct remains separate from the inputs used to choose an answer.

All plausible available responses retain a bounded chance of selection, including mistakes. Repeatedly contradicted interpretations lose weight when the contradiction is already part of known history. An AI cannot repeatedly forget a cleared suspect merely because a new case attempt began. When the facts reverse, its weights respond to the new observation instead of hard-coding the previously successful answer.

### Factors that matter

| Factor | Effect on investigation | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Case difficulty | More comparisons and competing plausible explanations increase error | Every decisive fact remains available |
| Civil administration and stability | More reliable comparison and record handling improve inference | Low capacity never makes all correct responses impossible |
| War and occupied territory | Interrupted access, staff pressure and stale reports make current evidence harder to combine | Actual closures and routes can also make an incorrect theory visibly impossible |
| Previous leads | Verified contradictions improve later weighting and prevent repeated unsupported attribution | A new changed fact can still require a new answer |
| Cooperation | An accepted authenticated packet supplies useful evidence or saves time | Mere diplomatic friendliness is not evidence |
| Archetype and current mutation | More complex correspondence, travel or cell relationships change the inquiry burden | The AI does not read the hidden archetype as an answer key |
| Delegation or independent review | A fresh review emphasizes available contradictions and reliable observations | It improves reasoning without automatic resolution |

The proposed initial benchmarks are roughly 75 to 90 percent correct per decisive step in a well-supported, stable case, 60 to 80 percent in ordinary mixed conditions, and 45 to 65 percent under severe disruption. Repeated review and retained evidence should improve a failing country's effective performance into a viable range rather than compound failure indefinitely. The later source-based probability audit determines the actual distribution and duration.

These are per-step targets. They are not claims that the full four-step chain succeeds that often. Even an illustrative independent 80 percent at each of four steps gives about 41 percent for a clean chain. Real steps share facts and history, so the implementation requires a complete sequence analysis, including retries, rather than reporting a single option share as overall success.

### AI protection, cooperation and growth

The AI protects a place when the evidence supports a near opportunity and the local benefit justifies the quoted cost. It uses the basic response when resources are scarce. It does not buy a benefit against a stale scene, pay for the same cover twice or retain a protection choice that prevents completing its only inquiry.

An AI shares relevant reliable evidence when the expected local or mutual benefit outweighs its relationship-based reluctance. Hostility can delay help, but a country pursuing the same traveling killer should recognize the value of a current border record. An AI does not transmit hidden truth that its own inquiry has never established.

Cell growth uses actual surviving recruiters, members and support. It competes with the same finite opportunity schedule and capacity envelope as violence. An organization disrupted repeatedly should have less ability to prepare fresh operations even if global Chaos remains high.

Where the project's current crisis rules apply stronger pressure to player countries, apply it as a modest transparent opportunity or case-complexity adjustment within the same physical caps. Keep the uniform initial country pool and evidence fairness. Do not add the older optional player-difficulty switch or secretly reduce a player's chance after a correct final plan.

## Chaos caused by actual outcomes

Direct Chaos represents the abnormal public significance of this crisis. Shared civilian Deaths remains responsible for its own generic conversion. Killing the same number of people under a new report title does not earn a second death-based contribution.

The impact map uses restrained context-dependent anchors. The event is a criminal crisis and cannot farm large Chaos grants from every ordinary clue or repeated border crossing. Magnitudes scale within their stated bands according to the actual new reach, affected public and organizational consequence. They do not multiply directly by current Chaos.

|---|---|---|---|

| First evidenced foreign spillover by the original | +2 to +3 | A domestic pursuit becomes a real cross-border threat | Once for the first foreign manifestation. Repeated crossings of the same trail earn zero. Regional containment can reverse its active significance. |

The regional direct contribution is capped around +5 overall. |
| A consequential failed containment | 0 to +1 | A publicly credible operation fails and an actual offender or support relationship gains a new opening | At most one increment for the same material opening, with a small local failure ceiling. An ordinary wrong answer without a new consequence gives zero. |
| First proven global coordination | +5 to +8 | Actual independent offenders, multiple continents and functional organized support become established | Once for the episode's first proven global structure. The evolution flag and later publication of the same news add zero. |
| New operational cell capability | +1 to +2, within a bounded global growth allowance | A real cell gains a new support relationship or capacity with an actual new consequence | Once per distinct achieved capability. Reversals share one diminishing record of active event-specific significance. A capture, cell closure and global ending cannot each refund the same earlier contribution. Prevented murders are successful protection, not a negative Deaths transaction. Past victims stay in history and population is not restored.

The lifetime positive direct-contribution envelope is at most +2 for the opening, +5 for regional spread including the first foreign spillover, +3 for consequential failed containment, +8 for the first global coordination, +8 for later distinct cell capabilities and +4 for source-connected worsening. The combined ceiling is therefore +30 for the episode. The first global cell is included in the global manifestation contribution. Later capability charges require a genuinely new achieved relationship or capacity.

Lifetime positive-budget usage and remaining active significance are separate internal histories. A reversal reduces only the latter and never refills the positive budget. Replacing a refuge, cycling an organizer, returning to a country or renaming a cell cannot bypass either guard. Any unretired significance left after the bounded final reversal becomes historical at closure and cannot fund later repeated rewards. These are private accounting facts, not additional public meters.

The later coding handoff must implement the complete map, including real spread and meaningful failed containment, rather than limiting Chaos to the opening popup. The shared Deaths contribution remains outside this direct envelope and is never duplicated by it.

## Endings that state exactly what happened

### Original stopped before global independence

The full original case is correctly completed. The actor is permanently contained or dead through the supported operation. Its personal opportunities end in every country. With no independent threats established, the active episode closes. Local disruption recedes and a conclusion records the solver and any real contributors.

### Original stopped during a global crisis

The original receives the same permanent ending, but the report identifies any independently active threats as separate. Those threats retain their own cases, membership and opportunities. The arrest can provide authentic historical evidence or weaken a cell that depended on that person. It does not disband unrelated organizations.

### A locally concluded traveling case

The country proves that the person has left through a current departure record and a feasible onward route. It transmits the authenticated trail if cooperation is available. Pressure caused only by that person's local presence ends. Historical victims remain attributed correctly. The conclusion acknowledges that a later real return can reopen local danger.

### An independent offender stopped

That person's case and personal opportunities close. The report distinguishes them from the original. An affiliation record can expose a cell, but catching a subordinate does not establish the disposition of its leader or other members.

### An organization dismantled

The operation has identified and disabled the actual leadership and support relationship, and all dangerous surviving participation is contained or explicitly transferred to separate existing cases. Connected local opportunities end or weaken according to their real dependency. Countries that used the same support node receive the applied result.

### A source relationship ended

The Serial Killer contact, shelter or interference ends through a completed local case. The source reports its own accepted consequence. An ongoing source event can remain active. A source's unrelated success can also end the connection without solving the surviving Event 102 culprit, who then continues through the grounded local branch.

### Worldwide closure

Every established independent actor and dangerous cell component is resolved, along with the original. No future recruitment or travel offer can revive the closed episode. The final international summary records the proven scale and material contributions without recharging casualties, replaying rewards or disclosing still-hidden unrelated source content.

## Country and campaign transitions

When a scene changes controller, the physical incident and the offender remain the same. Current investigation authority transfers only when the new authority can actually access the scene or hold the relevant records. A lawful owner can retain historical evidence. It does not keep administering crimes in territory it no longer controls.

When a country disappears, its active evidence is preserved with the case and transferred to a valid current authority where a real location exists. If the actor is traveling, its travel leg is re-evaluated against current geography. The game does not spawn a new country to carry a lost inquiry.

When a human joins an AI country, the next unresolved step opens with the accumulated evidence and acknowledged AI choices. Leaving or switching control hands the same case back to AI. Neither transition rerolls the archetype, resets failure history or duplicates the opening series.

When an event control suspends active Event 102 work, it preserves actors, evidence and history. Suspension is visibly distinct from a solved case. It creates no new autonomous murders, recruitment or movement during the suspension. Resuming uses current valid participants and a fresh future opportunity, rather than applying a backlog of missed killings. The owner integration must respect the actual shared control semantics and provide this active-work distinction where the control supports suspension.

A setting that affects only future random selection must not be misrepresented as an active-crisis suspension. The coding handoff requires inspection of that setting's owner contract before wiring it. In either case, no toggle erases historical victims, marks an uncontained actor as captured or creates a new fire-once episode.

When a source event is disabled or enters cleanup, new crossovers with it are unavailable. A pending source-dependent action is revalidated and either receives the owner's valid current result or branches to the local alternative. Event 102 preserves its independent victims and threats. It cannot reactivate the source merely to finish an integration.

When a terminal campaign transformation makes a local inquiry impossible, record an interrupted or superseded case according to the actual source outcome. Do not claim that the player solved it or grant success achievements. The transition owner remains responsible for its real casualties and terminal result.

## Compatibility and presentation scale

The full murder investigation, protection, cooperation, cell inquiry, evidence exchange and permanent stopping loop must work without optional DLC. It uses event choices, ordinary country and state facts, standard modifiers and the project's existing systems. An intelligence agency can supply optional contextual flavor or a bounded capacity advantage when supported. It cannot unlock the only laboratory, courier comparison or final arrest path.

The design adds no persistent public numerical tracker. Facts, historical casualties and case status are presented through their appropriate existing surfaces. There is no permanent investigation meter or large investigation window, as required by the brief. The small re-entry decision disappears when no current inquiry exists.

Asset requirements follow actual consumers. The opener, archetype scenes, failure, travel, final operations, cell investigations, global news and approved source connections each have a clear visual purpose. The imagery never substitutes for text evidence. The asset prompt identifies reusable scene families and separate native icon families without creating unnecessary portrait or model production.

## Required achievement experiences

The separate achievement prompt specifies exact candidate identifiers, conditions, disqualifiers and icons. The intended accomplishments are a clean complete original inquiry, a materially cooperative cross-border capture, dismantling an actual cell serving more than one country, exposing a consequential source-linked deception while keeping victim ownership correct, and contributing to a genuinely completed worldwide crisis.

Achievements follow verified actions and historical receipts. Merely firing Event 102, reading the worldwide news, selecting a default option or receiving another country's success report is insufficient. They should reward inference, effective cooperation and durable containment. They should not encourage deliberate civilian losses or repeated failure farming.

## Experience-level completion tests

A reader can follow a complete path from the first real incident to a permanent original ending without needing another event, a paid-only clue, a human-controlled major or a new interface. Every archetype has three distinct case families with genuine changed-fact retries. Every false lead has an understandable contradiction.

A regional example demonstrates one physically possible traveling person, a real neighboring AI minor, useful cooperation and a local departure conclusion. A global example demonstrates a distant independent offender, a functioning organization, staggered country involvement and national success while foreign threats remain. A source-connected example demonstrates a real accepted consequence and a grounded alternative when the source disappears.

An AI example demonstrates both failure and recovery using visible evidence. A human example demonstrates that answering all four deductions correctly leads to a permanent valid result. A multiplayer example demonstrates that two governments cannot capture, charge for or count the same actor's final incident twice.

The exact implementation, asset, source and engine checks live in the coding prompt. These experience tests describe what the finished mechanic must allow a person to observe and understand.
