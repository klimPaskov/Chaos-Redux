# 070 Cookie Click: super-events and writing

## Writing responsibility

The user's supplied names, core labels and unit-family names are preserved as working design labels.
The coding and localization workers write final event titles, options, descriptions, focus names, decision text, achievements and reports.
The descriptive path labels and operation IDs in these specifications must not be copied into localization as finished prose.

The tone begins with an absurd but useful government problem.
Officials attempt to administer a creature that is clearly enjoying their attention and resources.
As the creature develops, its demands become more deliberate.
The Monster's later hostility should remain connected to that recognizable personality.

Humor comes from the concrete situation, administrative language and the costs the player actually paid.
Avoid unrelated jokes, invented historical quotations and long repeated descriptions of invisible variables.
Options can have dry irony, but their tooltips still explain actual consequences.
Hunger flavor names what was really consumed.
A train-stockpile bite does not produce a report about a demolished factory.

## Event and flavor coverage

| Beat | Trigger | Writing purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | First living instance | Explain clicking, rewards, appetite and the early removal opportunity without revealing every future threshold |
| First full day | First completed daily target | Establish the creature's response and the real payout |
| Major appearance band | First entry into each visual band | Show increasing value, size and awareness |
| First small self-feeding | Actual first soft-value debit | Make the loss understandable and leave a practical route to resume care |
| Material theft | Actual new material family debit | Name the real stockpile and amount through supported dynamic text |
| Weak death | Completed weak-starvation route | Confirm permanent removal and the absence of further feeding or rewards |
| Maturity | First permanent survival threshold | Make loss of the safe starvation route clear |
| Watchful behavior | Evolution II and a valid interaction | Give the cookie a memory of a real action or resource |
| Severe warning | Armed five-cycle interval | State the current threat and how timely feeding can still stop it |
| Recovery | Warning canceled by genuine recovery | Confirm the danger has receded without refunding actual losses |
| Uprising | Successful country commit | Establish the Monster's country and the permanent end of pet rewards |
| First sustained region | Real settled expansion milestone | Show a distinct government method, not a generic conquest report |
| Subject administration | First functioning Cookie subject | Explain the new obligation and the chosen method |
| Empire defeat | Actual resolved defeat | Confirm the threat is ended and show enduring damage |
| Final Bite | World-end commitment | Declare the global campaign with precise current consequences |
| Terminal Cookie world | Actual terminal confirmation | Present the remaining world and its continuing consumption |

Routine hunger text is nonmodal where supported and shares a per-family cooldown.
Important warnings and irreversible transitions can bypass that cooldown once.
Do not repeat a popup on every consumed equipment batch or on each daily check.

## Super-event roles

Three super-events are required.
Their exact runtime IDs and final text are assigned only after registry and research checks.

| Role | Fire condition | Image direction | Audio direction | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SE-01, the uprising | First successful normal or explicit manual Empire commit | The familiar Cookie identity now dominates a real government and bakery-industrial setting | A short comic-to-hostile transition with readable rhythmic drive | Once per instance, observer-aware, after game-state commit |
| SE-02, The Final Bite begins | Irreversible world-end activation | A global administrative or industrial scene being consumed by the established Empire | A more forceful and sustained cue, with clear opening and closing edits | Once per world-end activation, never for eligibility alone |
| SE-03, the Cookie-world result | Confirmed terminal scenario result | A ruled, processed and depleted world with surviving Cookie institutions | A deliberately resolved ending cue matching the actual outcome | Once after terminal confirmation |

A weak-cookie death uses its report and animation.
It does not need a fourth large super-event.
An Empire defeat uses appropriate world news, with a separate super-event only if the shared campaign framework requires a general world-end defeat presentation.
Do not duplicate an existing shared defeat presentation.

Titles, button text, quotations and music remain separate slots in the research ledger.
An available quotation does not automatically determine all four.
Do not copy a path label into an unresearched final quote field.

## Verified literary directions

A verified source text is the Project Gutenberg edition of Grimms' Fairy Tales, attributed there to Jacob and Wilhelm Grimm and based on translations by Edgar Taylor and Marian Edwardes.
The relevant passages are in Hansel and Gretel and Cat and Mouse in Partnership.
The exact source and passage locations are recorded in `070_cookie_click_research_notes.md`.

Two short quotation candidates are verified against that edition:

> “Nibble, nibble, gnaw, Who is nibbling at my little house?”

This is suitable as an ironic literary direction for the uprising, where a previously appealing edible object becomes a territorial threat.
It is not dialogue originally spoken by the Cookie Monster.
Attribution must name the tale and edition's authorship or translation context.

> “When everything is eaten up one has some peace”

This is suitable as an ironic direction for the terminal Cookie-world result.
The source speaker is the cat in Cat and Mouse in Partnership.
Do not attribute the line to Hansel and Gretel or invent a historical author for it.

These are researched candidates, not locked final localization.
The publisher's general public-domain notice is jurisdiction-sensitive.
The production researcher must confirm the intended redistribution context and preserve the attribution record.
No copyrighted song lyrics are proposed.

SE-02 still needs a separately researched text direction or a deliberate source-approved use of the verified material that fits its distinct role.
Do not invent an impressive quotation to close the slot.
Original final title and button wording remain the localization worker's responsibility under the project's research requirements.

## Music research direction

The official Incompetech catalog identifies a track called The Builder.
Its search-visible metadata gives a 1:58 duration, 123 BPM, and a comic, dark, bouncy scoring description.
The site identifies its music as by Kevin MacLeod.
This makes it a candidate for an early absurd-to-hostile presentation.
It has not been auditioned or approved for a cut.

The official licensing page offers a credit-required Creative Commons route and a separate standard-license route.
The specific track's generated license text, exact downloadable recording, permitted edits and attribution still need capture.
Do not claim an exact Creative Commons version or a final cleared recording from the general licensing page alone.

The earlier attempted The Dread lookup did not produce a verified track record.
It is not a selected music candidate.
SE-02 and SE-03 require further source-backed music selection.
All three roles require actual listening, duration and loop or fade decisions, file provenance and licensing confirmation before runtime inclusion.

## Audio cut and playback

The audio researcher supplies one primary and one fallback candidate per role with a verified recording source.
The production plan should favor a short coherent excerpt over a long track cut at an arbitrary point.
The opening accent must align with the actual super-event reveal.
The final fade must not leave a clipped percussion or vocal fragment.

Use the current super-event framework's actual audio duration and blocking behavior.
The event's game-state mutation must not wait for the player's local audio playback.
No recorded music is embedded in this specification package.

## Localization coverage

The localization audit checks root event, internal warnings, bite families, recovery, daily rewards, level rewards, GUI labels, every focus, decision, mission, technology, equipment family, unit, country identity, advisor, commander, scenario control and achievement.
It also checks event-detail entries and spreadsheet-facing wording after the canonical catalog is updated.

Every dynamic sentence uses a real supported variable or scripted localization value.
A missing target should produce a valid alternate sentence, not an exposed runtime identifier.
Saved reward previews and actual payout messages agree on quantities.
The UI does not show hidden scaling components as extra permanent meters.

The Monster's later voice becomes direct and demanding.
Low-danger text stays playful.
Avoid claiming the country is already destroyed before the actual operation completes.
The final global result states what the map and campaign actually support.
