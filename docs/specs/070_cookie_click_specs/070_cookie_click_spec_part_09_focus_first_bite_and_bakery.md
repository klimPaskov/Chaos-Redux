# 070 Cookie Click: The First Bite and The Endless Bakery

## Tree structure

The seven major branches retain the brief's working labels.
The following group IDs describe required playable paths, not final focus names or a fixed node count.
The implementation chooses the exact focus count and grid while preserving every group, its choice, unlock, consequence and aftermath.

The First Bite sits near the center.
The Endless Bakery grows to its left.
Legions of Dough grows to its right.
A Hungry State develops below the starting political center.
Eat the Neighbours and The Sweet Empire occupy adjacent outer lanes because conquest and administration interact.
The Final Bite is a late lower section with separate clear prerequisites.

Do not require victory over the former host before the economy or army becomes usable.
Do not make one foreign AI response the only way to reach a major branch.
Support branches coexist unless a genuine method or doctrine choice makes them incompatible.

A 7-day emergency focus is permitted when it activates a survival response.
Ordinary openings and tactical choices use roughly 35 days.
Major institutions and strategic commitments use roughly 70 days.
Long construction or conversion is usually a decision mission unlocked by a focus, not an arbitrary 140-day wait for a small modifier.

## The First Bite

### FB-01: establish command

This root is available immediately after the uprising.
It establishes the Monster's command, opens the Empire interface and allows the first reserve policy.
The new country already has a working army and valid capital before this focus.
The focus must not repair a deliberately incomplete spawn.

Its early continuation chooses whether the first reserve supports the field army or protects the bakery region.
The army choice gives a finite reinforcement and recovery package.
The bakery choice gives a defensive construction and local supply package.
Both spend from the same startup reserve entitlement.
The player cannot receive both by changing a choice after loading.

This group should complete quickly enough to matter in the first war.
The AI chooses bakery protection when its capital region is threatened and field support when it has a safe rear and a promising front.
The icon direction combines the Monster's crown with an actual command object.

### FB-02: secure the first production site

Require FB-01, but not a victorious war.
Choose one real controlled bakery anchor and start a 90-day objective to keep it supplied and restore basic industry.
Success improves the bakery-network spirit and unlocks the first equipment-processing recipe.
Partial success grants only the facilities actually completed.
Failure leaves the production problem in place and opens a costly emergency alternative.

The objective uses the selected state's actual factories, damage and supply.
A focus cannot claim that the bakery is secure while the state is occupied.
If the state is lost, the mission closes and allows a new site after a 30-day recovery period.
The initial industrial grant does not repeat with the new site.

This group connects to EB-01 and EB-02.
It also gives the former host a visible operational target.
The icon should show a defended oven or converted industrial workshop.

### FB-03: decide the former host's fate

This group begins after command is established and the host conflict has a meaningful outcome.
Defeating the host is one route.
Holding the birth region and surviving 180 days while the host was destroyed by another power is an alternate continuation.
An absent host must not permanently lock the Empire.

The result depends on the chosen governing method.
Rapid consumption takes immediate assets.
Long-term farming preserves a useful administration or productive core.
Population conversion opens the first large conversion program.
Direct destruction opens the first irreversible capital-region harvest.

The player receives a postwar operation with costs, an actual target and a duration.
It is not a one-time reward dump that ignores the map.
The previous host's national focus tree is not overwritten while that country remains independently playable.

### FB-04: make the uprising a state

Require a functioning bakery site and a stable capital region.
The group replaces the worst improvised-government penalty, enables the three institutional advisors and opens HS-01.
It uses 70-day state-building programs with a clear opportunity cost in political and economic capacity.

The institution unlocks do not create three additional spirits.
Their benefits connect to recipes, missions and production choices.
A badly supplied Empire may delay the programs and fight with its rough startup structure, at the cost of weaker long-term administration.

Its capstone establishes the country as a persistent actor after the host war ends.
It preserves all consumed-asset and territorial history.
It does not reset the Empire to a fresh, fully stocked uprising.

### FB-05: survive the first counterattack

This optional survival group becomes important when a strong host or neighbor attacks the birth region.
It unlocks a reserve redeployment decision and a timed hold objective for a real supply hub, capital approach or port.
The player chooses one defensible position.
The system does not generate an objective in territory already impossible to reach.

Success improves the field army's recovery for 30 days and protects the chosen bakery operation.
Failure means the real military loss and a temporary disruption to that operation.
The objective does not also add an unrelated Stability punishment simply to make failure look larger.

The group remains optional for a successful offensive Empire.
A route bypass records that survival preparation was unnecessary and gives no free defensive reward.
It feeds the achievement for surviving a much stronger opening opponent.

## The Endless Bakery

### EB-01: a regular body-production line

This branch root requires FB-01 and opens a real Cookie-equipment production path.
It supplies the basic shaping technology and enough initial production support to use it.
The player chooses a preferred line emphasis between cheap mass bodies and durable standard bodies.
That preference informs production and AI, but does not permanently lock the later military doctrine.

The initial focus reward is a production setup.
The following program develops efficiency through actual production and a 90-day objective.
Its capstone improves the bakery-network spirit and raises conversion efficiency by 25 percentage points within the global cap.

A country without spare slots receives a valid targeted construction program.
Do not grant a fictional factory outside the map.
The art direction is industrial baking equipment with clear HOI4 focus-icon composition.

### EB-02: feed the supply network

This group requires a real production site.
It identifies the supply route from the capital to the main Cookie army.
The player chooses railway repair, hub capacity or port support according to geography.
These are alternatives for the current bottleneck, not a permanent ban on building the others later.

The first program consumes trains or convoys and construction resources.
Its mission succeeds when the selected route exists and the relevant supply infrastructure is usable.
A front moving away from the route closes the old target cleanly and permits one replacement.
The project cannot keep paying for the same repaired railway after every ownership change.

Its late payoff reduces the heavy-unit supply penalty and unlocks a supply-corridor mission family.
Landlocked and island Empires receive different concrete targets.
This group is a prerequisite for the strongest golem and oven programs.

### EB-03: sort captured equipment

This group opens the inspection and conversion of captured stocks.
Its early focus distinguishes useful conventional equipment from obsolete or incompatible surplus.
Its middle choice specializes the workshops in food conversion or military replacement.

The food branch improves surplus-to-food efficiency and reduces conversion time.
The replacement branch produces better body and support equipment but leaves less food from the same stock.
Both retain the no-double-use input ledger.
The player can later add a costly secondary workshop, but cannot receive both specialized maximum efficiencies without paying for a late common capstone.

A 120-day throughput objective measures actual equipment converted.
The player must also preserve a minimum field reserve.
This avoids rewarding the pointless destruction of the army.
Successful completion unlocks larger batch sizes, not free stockpiles.

### EB-04: industrial concentration or distributed ovens

The central industrial choice is between a concentrated bakery region and several smaller sites.
It is independent of governing method.
A concentrated region has strong efficiency and lower construction cost.
Losing it causes a large disruption.
Distributed sites have higher setup cost and a lower peak bonus, but keep part of the economy operating after a local defeat.

The player selects actual eligible states.
The focus unlocks construction programs and the relevant state modifiers.
Each site receives a finite industrial package with slot checks.
The route does not repeatedly grant factories every time a new state becomes eligible.

The late payoff is a production network with observable map consequences.
Concentrated sites are valuable targets for enemy bombing and occupation.
Distributed sites require a larger supply network and more convoy or railway investment.

### EB-05: harvest without planning for tomorrow

This group is enhanced by rapid consumption and direct destruction.
Other methods can access a limited emergency version.
It opens large equipment and building harvests, then develops faster processing and field-side conversion.

Rapid consumption preserves a minimum useful industrial base and turns the rest into immediate reserves.
Direct destruction can remove more levels and gains greater siege support, but leaves a smaller future production base.
The decision preview shows those permanent losses.

Its capstone supports one sustained offensive from a large actual harvest.
The reward is capped by the material consumed and the army that can use it.
There is no flat infinite reinforcement flag.

### EB-06: productive feeding territories

The full route requires the long-term farming method.
An initial survey identifies eligible states and their population, infrastructure, supply and famine condition.
The next programs prepare farms, improve transport and formalize their recurring input commitments.

The branch's major choice is direct administration versus delegated feeding territories.
Direct administration produces more predictable food but increases the Empire's state burden.
Delegation supports the subject route, gives up direct industry and relies on tribute compliance.

A 180-day objective requires several complete production cycles without destructive harvesting in the selected region.
Success improves the network spirit and unlocks a higher sustainable yield tier.
Failure does not reset surviving farms, but delays the efficiency reward.
This route gives a real alternative to eating the world immediately.

### EB-07: restore useful territory

Every method can repair a selected state, but long-term farming gains the most value.
This group creates postwar restoration programs for factories, infrastructure, hubs and ports.
It uses actual damage and current control.
Population previously killed remains absent.

The player chooses an industrial, logistics or civilian-restoration priority.
Each program consumes two or three relevant resources and takes 120 to 180 days.
The program pays its improvement as work completes and does not double-grant on mission completion.

The capstone reduces future disruption in a connected productive region.
It does not core every conquered state.
It connects to Sweet Empire integration and the achievement for rebuilding a previously devastated region.

### EB-08: the mature bakery state

Require a developed production line, a functioning supply program, and one completed method-compatible economic route.
This late branch consolidates the chosen economy.
Rapid consumption improves mobile processing.
Farming improves reserve reliability.
Conversion improves body production and reduces failed batches.
Destruction improves immediate extraction while making its permanent cost clear.

The capstone replaces the bakery-network spirit with its final ordinary stage.
It unlocks a further research slot under the four-slot ceiling and the largest ordinary industrial program.
The player must complete a 180-day operating objective with adequate food and supply.
A starving army cannot collect the mature-economy reward merely by waiting for a focus timer.

This capstone supports The Final Bite but is not the only route into it.
An aggressively destructive Empire can qualify through its actual conquest and consumption route instead.
