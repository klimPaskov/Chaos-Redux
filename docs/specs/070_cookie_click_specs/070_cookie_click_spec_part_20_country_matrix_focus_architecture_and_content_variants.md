The compatible starting technology subset is derived from the actual chosen equipment and infrastructure package.
Add only technologies that the package needs and that the donor genuinely has, plus the event's own basic Cookie technologies.
Respect mutual exclusions, doctrines, DLC and current technology graph rules.
For a late host, this avoids an uprising carrying modern equipment that its production cannot replace.
For an early host, it avoids free advanced research.

If naval assets are required for an island setup, the naval program receives the compatible transport and escort foundations the host actually possesses.
When no suitable donor technology exists, use the supported earliest valid baseline and a real research program.
Do not guarantee immediate ocean invasion technology through an unverified setup effect.

## Method identities

Rapid consumption uses mobile processing institutions, military procurement priorities and a state identity centered on forward movement.
Its route-specific advisor emphasis is logistics and conversion throughput.
It retains the Monster and the common army.
Its weakness is a depleted rear and repeated need for new material.

Long-term farming uses controlled human administrations, functioning infrastructure and reliable tribute.
Its state identity preserves selected local naming and administration.
Its advisor emphasis is bakery administration and stable output.
Its weakness is protecting a wide productive rear.

Population conversion uses shaping institutions, demographic transformation and controlled specialist recruitment.
It changes selected settled-region names and administrative identity after actual conversion.
Its advisor emphasis is military shaping.
Its weakness is occupation pressure, population limits and expensive production.

Direct destruction uses demolition institutions and immediate processing of captured capacity.
It changes the regime's flag and institutional tone without inventing a second supreme leader.
Its advisor emphasis is industrial dismantling and heavy forces.
Its weakness is severe loss of future production and supply.

No method is a peaceful diplomatic alternative that removes the written external-neighbor aggression.
Each changes the means and consequences of that aggression.
The choice is visible in laws where supported, cosmetic identity, advisor emphasis, operations, production, army composition and settlement.

## Focus architecture

The tree has seven primary branches.

```text
                          The First Bite
                         FB-01 to FB-05
                           /     |     \
                          /      |      \
          The Endless Bakery   A Hungry State   Legions of Dough
             EB-01 to EB-08     HS-01 to HS-08    LD-01 to LD-10
                    \           /    |    \          /
                     \         / four methods     /
                      \       /        |         /
                      Eat the Neighbours   The Sweet Empire
                         EN-01 to EN-06      SE-01 to SE-06
                               \              /
                                \            /
                                  The Final Bite
                                   FF-01 to FF-06
```

This is a dependency overview, not a final coordinate grid.
The detailed group specifications in parts 09 through 12 govern exact relationships.
Each path group can contain its named preparation focus, major choice, optional follow-up and capstone where those have distinct mechanics.
Do not force one focus per group or inflate every group into the same number of nodes.

The command root opens immediate military and bakery survival programs in parallel.
Winning the host war is a major payoff, but cannot block all development while the host survives.
The political method shapes industry, military priorities, subject policy and settlement.
The conquest route's postwar objectives connect back into production and administration.
The final branch requires actual campaign proof and a viable army.

## Mutual exclusions

The four government methods are mutually exclusive after the central commitment.
The three principal military doctrines are mutually exclusive at their doctrine choice.
Within a route, the specifically declared concentration, settlement or specialist choices have their own exclusions.

Basic logistics, ordinary support, repair, aviation and naval development can coexist when valid.
Do not turn every pair of useful support programs into an exclusion merely to make the tree look branched.
A selected governing method must leave a complete path to military development and The Final Bite.

Mutual exclusions affect both focus validity and the operations they unlock.
A saved program from a rejected method cannot remain active to harvest both routes.
Completed actual infrastructure and legitimately acquired equipment are not deleted merely because a later choice closes a future path.

## Idea lifecycle audit

The appetite lifecycle starts unstable, gains its chosen governing-method stage and upgrades once for The Final Bite.
Only one appetite stage exists at a time.
Low-Fullness penalties are part of that same lifecycle.

The bakery lifecycle starts improvised, changes through logistics and industrial programs, and ends at a method-appropriate mature stage.
It does not add a fresh permanent national spirit for every factory focus.
State-specific effects use actual state targets where their effect belongs.

The military lifecycle starts after doctrine commitment and changes through its matching specialist and combined-arms programs.
Military equipment, unit unlocks, production, training institutions and technology provide the rest of the branch's rewards.
They are not all represented by additional national spirits.

Focus rewards include actual infrastructure, production, templates, research, operations, institutions, settlement, supply and territorial objectives.
Pure Political Power, Stability or War Support grants are supporting rewards.
They are not the main payoff of most nodes.

## Content variants without extra systems

Daily reward variation comes from the registered reward families, saved quantities, milestone order and the full-day bonus.
This already supplies a large set of valid outcomes.
Do not add several unrelated pet species, a separate affection meter or an inventory of collectible toppings.

Three rare full-feeding outcome treatments are allowed within the existing 10 percent bonus result.
They are cosmetic or use the already budgeted valid bonus.

| Variant | Eligibility | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unusually tidy crumbs | Any full day with a valid small bonus | A clean, surprised facial reaction and a small supported material or government packet |
| An improvised decoration | A newly reached visual band with a valid bonus | A temporary visible decoration incorporated into that band's approved assets, with no new permanent meter |
| An unsettling gift | Evolution II or III, mature cookie and valid advanced bonus | A deliberate offering tied to a real reward family and a more aware facial response |

These are alternative presentations of the same saved bonus.
They do not run three additional random rolls or increase the budget three times.
The art manifest includes a variant only when the actual implemented outcome uses it.
Final names and prose remain localization work.

## Formable assessment

The Cookie Empire is itself the event-created polity.
Its four methods are administrative routes within that polity.
The Sweet Empire creates a real Cookie political domain, and The Final Bite is its larger world-end identity.

The brief does not require a separate geographically fixed formable such as a named continent with a fixed state list.
The package therefore does not invent additional regional tags, duplicate focus trees or exact-state puzzle graphics.
The dynamic expansion proof uses actual world-state shares, supplied territories and settled regions.
A later fixed-territory formable addition would need its own explicit map requirements and exact-state visual consumer.

## Final route coverage contract

The implementation handoff must enumerate every actual focus node under FB, EB, LD, HS, EN, SE and FF.
For each node, record entry conditions, prerequisites, exclusion group, duration, final text, icon, effect, operation unlock, AI preference and completion evidence.
Every group described in parts 09 through 12 must map to at least one meaningful implemented node or an explicitly approved shared mechanism.

The audit verifies early, middle and late pacing for all four methods.
It checks that a no-DLC game, a weak uprising and an island uprising can progress.
It checks that state loss, host survival, third-party capitulation and enemy fragmentation cannot leave the entire tree permanently impossible.
A blocked optional naval branch inland is acceptable.
A permanently blocked country campaign is not.
