## Important design resolutions

The normal event is a singleton human-owned Cookie system.
The opening requires a host with enough valid territory for a viable uprising, normally at least two owned controlled states.
The country-creation method does not pretend it can divide ownership within a single state.
Manual presets bypass progression requirements but still require valid engine and map structure.

The daily target begins at 300, grows through Level, lifetime clicks, feeding streak, rewards and evolution, and has an explicit 20,000-click safety ceiling.
Rewards stop when the daily target is full.
The two public custom values remain Cookie Fullness and Cookie Level in both the pet and Empire phases.

Maturity is permanent.
Self-feeding does not prevent weak-starvation death.
Genuine 75-percent recovery can cancel a warning, while a token click cannot prevent danger forever.
The Empire uses actual food conversion and production after the revolt.
It does not collect old pet rewards.

Four governing methods change how the Empire uses territory.
Cookie subjects are explicitly exempt from external-neighbor aggression while they remain members of its domain.
This interpretation permits the requested subject route without changing its aggression toward other neighbors.
