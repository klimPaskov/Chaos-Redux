# 070 Cookie Click: Eat the Neighbours and The Sweet Empire

## Eat the Neighbours

Border aggression already exists when the Empire appears.
This branch must not sell redundant war goals against countries with which the Empire is already at war.
It makes expansion more effective, develops new operational reach and decides what happens to each conquered region.

### EN-01: choose the first front

The root follows FB-01.
It identifies the host front and up to two other immediately relevant external fronts.
The player chooses one primary operation.
All required border wars still exist, but the Empire concentrates its scarce forces.

The operation has a real target: a capital approach, supply hub, industrial state or port.
Its 90 to 120-day mission rewards capture and maintained control, not a momentary province touch.
Failure closes the operation without repeatedly adding identical war goals.

The AI prioritizes the host when that front threatens its capital.
It may prioritize a dangerous neighbor instead when the host is already collapsing.
The branch should expose a military choice, not force suicidal historical-looking order.

### EN-02: secure the next food region

This group follows a first successful operation or a survival continuation after an unsuccessful one.
It selects a connected region with usable stocks, industry or productive population.
The player chooses whether the campaign seeks immediate reserves or a sustainable rear area.

The chosen objective changes the postwar operation.
Immediate reserves open a bounded harvest.
A sustainable rear opens a farm, restoration or subject program.
The same conquered factories cannot supply both rewards at full value.

Its late stage creates a regional supply corridor.
The target expands with actual borders.
It does not use a hardcoded list of European states when the Empire began elsewhere.

### EN-03: overcome the first major opponent

This group appears when a real major or comparably strong regional opponent joins the war.
The player prepares air support, a heavy assault force or a wider encirclement route.
Each option has an actual production, supply or territorial prerequisite.

The 180-day campaign objective allows several forms of success.
Taking the selected industrial center, breaking the relevant supply corridor or forcing the opponent's capitulation can resolve the mission.
The conditions must be declared before it begins.
Do not add an easier victory condition after the player has already failed.

The payoff opens a major postwar choice in The Sweet Empire.
It does not simply add another 10 percent attack forever.
A failed campaign leaves the Empire with a costly army and a food requirement, making the economic method matter.

### EN-04: cross difficult borders

This group develops mountain, river, fortified and coastal operations according to the current map.
Use a small rotating set of relevant objectives.
Do not show every terrain campaign at once.

The player chooses an operation that changes a real obstacle.
Engineering opens a crossing.
Oven artillery supports a fortified attack.
Riders exploit a completed breakthrough.
Naval development opens an overseas landing.
Each operation requires the unit or technology that makes its promise credible.

The late payoff improves the relevant military doctrine stage or unlocks a repeatable mission family.
It does not grant permanent immunity to terrain penalties.

### EN-05: widen the war

The group opens after a substantial regional conquest.
It increases operational reach and prepares new fronts against actual non-cookie countries.
An inland Empire needs a coast before an overseas campaign.
A distant enemy needs a viable route.

The branch coordinates automatic border hostility with deliberate campaign selection.
New external neighbors become war targets through the border system.
The focus route decides which fronts receive preparation and resources.
There is no recurring focus that declares the same war every month.

Its capstone creates a continent-scale operating plan based on the Empire's real location.
The plan must cover supply, naval or air reach where relevant, and the rear-area policy.
A geographic label without an actual target set is insufficient.

### EN-06: settle the regional conquest

The final ordinary conquest group requires a meaningful defeated region and a governing method.
It resolves claims, occupation, subjects, restoration or destruction through actual postwar actions.
The player sees what territory is kept directly and what obligations survive.

Success requires maintained control for 180 days and completion of the chosen regional program.
It opens a late achievement and helps qualify the Empire for The Final Bite.
Failure leaves the conquered territory's real instability and damage, with no duplicate settlement reward.

No focus automatically cores an entire continent.
Core grants require a separately justified integration contract.
Ordinary Cookie occupation, subjects and state modifiers are sufficient for most of this event.

## The Sweet Empire

This branch gives the Empire a way to use conquered societies over time.
Its subjects and administrations are part of the Cookie domain.
They do not become neutral friendly exceptions outside it.

The branch must preserve meaningful existing-country content.
A released historical country uses its real tag and relevant identity.
Its new subordination or converted administration may use a suitable cosmetic identity and institutional government.
Do not overwrite every subject with the parent's full Cookie focus tree.

### SE-01: decide what to preserve

Require a governing method and a real conquered territory with surviving assets.
The first choice preserves an administration, incorporates a region directly, or begins a method-compatible extraction program.
The available choices depend on actual control and a valid country or state identity.

This choice is not a free release-and-annex loop.
A saved settlement ID tracks the territory and outcome.
A region already rewarded for initial settlement cannot pay again after a tag change.

The initial objective takes 120 days.
It establishes a government, supply connection and a real resource obligation.
The reward is a functioning settlement, not a flat lump of Political Power.

### SE-02: human-managed feeding grounds

This route is strongest under long-term farming.
It creates or subordinates a valid administration whose population and industry remain substantially intact.
The subject receives a defined minimum reserve, a 30-day tribute contract and a narrow set of responsibilities.

The middle choice trades heavier immediate tribute against slower but more reliable development.
Heavy tribute gives more food now and a greater risk of missed obligations and resistance.
Development preserves more domestic capacity and improves later deliveries.

The late payoff is a productive subject network.
Its success objective requires three complete tribute cycles and a defended supply link.
A subject with no route to the Empire cannot pay abstract food through a nonexistent corridor.

### SE-03: converted administrations

This route requires the population-conversion method and a completed conversion program.
It creates a Cookie-aligned administration in a valid country or region.
The institution is visibly different from a human-managed subject and uses the appropriate classification.

The early program establishes recruitment and local control.
The middle choice favors a military production administration or a direct feeding administration.
The former supplies bodies and equipment.
The latter supplies food.
The same tribute input cannot pay both at full value.

The capstone gives a reliable specialist contribution from the subject.
It does not grant the parent's entire unit roster for free.
Technologies, equipment and provider registrations must support the chosen contribution.

### SE-04: directly administered regions

This route is useful to every method and especially to a concentrated industrial Empire.
It integrates supply, production and occupation in a connected conquered region.
Its first program repairs a real corridor.
Its second develops the selected industrial or feeding role.
Its third addresses actual resistance and administrative cost.

The payoff is better productivity and lower disruption in that region.
It does not require universal core grants.
If an integration core is proposed for a narrowly defined fully converted territory, it needs explicit population, duration and governance conditions and separate review.

Direct administration increases the number of states in the Empire's appetite.
This is a real cost of retaining more direct industry.

### SE-05: enforce tribute or absorb the subject

This group becomes relevant after a subject genuinely misses its obligations or loses control.
The player can reduce the demand, impose a corrective mission, or prepare direct absorption.
The chosen action has a clear effect on future food and political control.

A corrective mission requires a real achievable action such as repairing the supply route or restoring production.
Failure can remove privileges or open a war or annexation route under the verified diplomatic contract.
It cannot instantly seize territory from an unrelated overlord or end an arbitrary coalition war.

Absorption takes actual control and respects the subject's remaining military situation.
It does not refund the resources used to establish the subject.
A repeated release cannot reset its consumed-population or settlement history.

### SE-06: a Cookie political domain

Require at least one functioning subject and a completed regional settlement.
The branch creates a Cookie-domain faction or equivalent verified subject coordination.
Its obligations are military support, supply access and registered tribute.
Its members are exempt from the external-neighbor war rule only while they remain genuine members of the Cookie domain.

A member that leaves loses that exemption.
If it then borders the Empire, the normal border-hostility rule applies.
No peaceful exit can erase prior real losses or refund tribute.

The late objective requires a working multi-country supply system and sustained obligations over 180 days.
Its reward improves subject reliability and opens a global domination outcome.
It does not add a cohesion meter.
The relevant status is shown through actual subject relationships, deliveries and mission states.

## Convergence with The Final Bite

Each governing method can reach the endgame through a different proof.

Rapid consumption qualifies through sustained conquest and actual material processed.
Long-term farming qualifies through a stable productive domain and sufficient territorial reach.
Population conversion qualifies through completed programs and a developed Cookie army.
Direct destruction qualifies through actual irreversible harvesting and continued military control.

These proofs converge on the same world-end threat at 1,000 or more global Chaos.
They do not create four duplicate public world-end scenario rows.
Their operational choices and aftermath remain distinct inside the single Final Bite branch.

## Focus layout and coverage

Keep method commitments in one readable political section.
Place their related economy and administration branches nearby.
Use short convergence connections and avoid crossing all seven lanes with long lines.

No decorative focus inlay is required.
The Cookie window already carries Fullness and Level.
A duplicate inlay would add work and repeated information without a new decision.

The implementation must produce a route coverage table for FB, EB, LD, HS, EN, SE and the Final Bite groups.
For every group, record the final focus IDs, actions, missions, technology, spirit stage, icon and AI route.
A renamed or merged group is acceptable only when its gameplay requirement remains explicit.
A missing group is not hidden by a larger final focus count.
