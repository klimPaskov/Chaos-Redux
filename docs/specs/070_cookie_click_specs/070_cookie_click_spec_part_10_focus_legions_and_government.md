# 070 Cookie Click: Legions of Dough and A Hungry State

## Legions of Dough

### LD-01: turn a crowd into formations

The root follows FB-01.
It establishes the standard Cookie Infantry template, a usable training path and the basic military-shaping technology.
Its first improvement gives logistics or reconnaissance according to the country's most immediate need.

A 90-day objective asks the Empire to equip and maintain a small balanced army, not simply accumulate manpower.
Success opens the doctrine choice.
Failure leaves ordinary production available and offers a cheaper defensive template.
No route requires winning a war before the army can function.

### LD-02: choose the military emphasis

The central commitment chooses mass, disciplined or heavy doctrine.
Mass improves Crumb screening and cheap body replacement.
Disciplined doctrine improves line cohesion and Chocolate Guard integration.
Heavy doctrine improves golem and oven assault.
The choice changes the military-shaping spirit and AI production plan.

Only the central maximum doctrine stages are mutually exclusive.
Every branch can still build its basic specialist roles.
Common logistics, air and naval development remain available.
Do not draw dozens of false mutual-exclusion connectors across support branches.

The final doctrine stage requires practical use of its army.
Mass must sustain a front and replace real losses.
Disciplined forces must complete a supplied offensive with limited unit destruction.
Heavy forces must capture a defended objective while keeping the supply route open.
These are 120 to 180-day mission families with target validation.

### LD-03: the Guard and the ovens

Two neighboring subpaths develop Chocolate Guard and Oven Artillery.
Both are available, but each requires its own technology, production line and equipment commitment.
The Guard route creates expensive assault and capital-defense formations.
The oven route creates strong fire support and a later fort-pressure action.

The middle Guard choice favors a small professional reserve or wider integration into the line.
The reserve is more effective but has a lower cap.
Wider integration raises capacity with a smaller individual bonus and higher appetite.
The oven choice favors mobile field ovens or slow heavy siege ovens.
Their equipment and supply costs differ.

Each subpath ends with a real template, production recipe and mission unlock.
A focus that provides only a generic attack modifier does not satisfy either unit family.

### LD-04: shaped shock bodies

The golem group requires EB-02 and the durable-body technology.
The first program proves a body that can survive movement and sustained combat.
The next choice favors armored protection or more mobile articulated forms.
Do not give both maximum protection and maximum speed without a later major tradeoff.

The route's objective requires a supplied assault on a selected city, fort line or difficult crossing.
Its reward improves the heavy doctrine stage or unlocks a new equipment tier.
A failed assault consumes its real equipment and delays further experimentation.
It must not magically remove every surviving golem.

This route gives the Empire a reason to invest in railways and hubs.
The strongest golems remain expensive to feed and replace.

### LD-05: fast wafer formations

The rider group requires the mobile-frame technology and a viable fuel or movement-support contract.
Its early program opens Wafer Riders and a reconnaissance template.
Its middle choice favors deep exploitation or defensive interception.

Exploitation unlocks a mission to cut a real supply route after another army has opened a gap.
Interception improves reinforcement and recovery when defending a threatened bakery region.
Neither route grants an unsupported teleport or province movement effect.

The capstone combines riders with a balanced army.
A mission must demonstrate that the Empire can hold the territory taken.
Rapidly touching a province and retreating cannot pay a conquest reward.

### LD-06: common support services

This common branch develops engineers, anti-air, transport and field repair or equivalent support.
Use compatible native support companies where appropriate.
A unique Cookie support unit is added only when its mechanic genuinely needs one and its full asset package is authorized.

The branch reduces avoidable supply and attrition costs.
It does not eliminate the Fullness mechanic.
Its late reserve-management program protects useful equipment from emergency food conversion.
A starving Empire may override that protection through an explicit costly emergency action.

This is a bridge between military and bakery branches.
It should be visually close to both and require short, readable prerequisite connections.

### LD-07: air power over the bakery

An initial 35-day program secures a real airfield, repair route and fuel supply.
The next paths emphasize interception or army support.
The player may develop both over time, but the first commitment determines early research and production priority.

The branch opens actual aircraft production and compatible technologies.
Its 120-day operational objective requires usable aircraft, a valid air zone and support for an active front.
A landlocked country remains eligible.
A country without an airfield receives the construction objective first.

The late payoff improves coordination with Cookie formations and protects industrial sites.
It does not grant every aircraft technology or invent a new aircraft roster without models and equipment art.

### LD-08: reach the next shore

The naval root appears when a viable port is controlled.
Its first program creates convoy and repair capacity.
Its middle paths favor protected transport, raiding, or a small surface-force core.
An island Empire gives this branch high priority.

A naval invasion program requires the actual technology, transport capacity, base and operational route.
The objective chooses a reachable coast relevant to a real enemy.
A landlocked Empire cannot receive ships in a nonexistent port.
An enemy beyond operational range requires more preparation, not a fake instant invasion.

The late route supports worldwide conquest.
It also improves subject tribute and overseas food transport.
This gives naval development value before the final world-end war.

### LD-09: an army that can sustain an offensive

Require the doctrine commitment, common support services and two specialist programs.
The route tests combined operations in a 180-day campaign objective.
The player chooses a real defended region.
The objective needs capture, supply continuity and adequate Fullness.

Success unlocks the final ordinary military-shaping spirit and a larger reserve policy.
Failure leaves the unit roster intact but withholds the capstone.
The mission may be attempted again after a 90-day recovery period against a valid new target.
Its one-time reward cannot be farmed.

Successful first completion also grants one permanent research slot, up to the ordinary four-slot ceiling.
This is the military program's single slot entitlement.
EB-08 owns the other additional slot.
Their order does not matter and neither can pay twice.

### LD-10: forms beyond ordinary warfare

At Level 30, a mature doctrine and a developed bakery network open elite research.
The ordinary stage improves existing specialist bodies and prepares the two late monster formations.
Actual deployment of the colossal oven and monster guard remains tied to the world-end tier.

The branch includes the necessary equipment and supply costs.
It does not unlock elite units through a picture-only focus.
The Final Bite can accelerate this research, but cannot excuse missing models, counters or animations.

## A Hungry State

### HS-01: institutions under the Monster

Require FB-04 or its valid survival continuation.
The root establishes the bakery administration, logistics command and shaping office.
Their functions are separate.
The state remains a fixed-purpose country under the Monster.

Early programs define which assets may be requisitioned, how reserves are protected and how conquered civilians are administered.
They open actual policy decisions and change the appetite spirit's initial penalties.
They do not expose three new institutional-power meters.

### HS-02: the governing commitment

The four methods are the branch's central choice.
The player must have experienced at least one food shortage or completed one actual feeding operation before choosing.
This requirement teaches the system through play without imposing a long arbitrary delay.

The choice is a 70-day commitment with an announced opportunity cost.
It changes the appetite-spirit route, opens method-specific economy and administration groups and locks the incompatible maximum method stages.
Ordinary emergency measures remain available across routes.

The AI chooses from actual geography, resources and military pressure.
A tiny surrounded uprising should not blindly choose slow farming.
A large productive Empire with secure territory should not always destroy its own industrial base.

### HS-03: rapid consumption

This method develops mobile processing, fast requisition and an army-led administration.
Its first program increases immediate food returns from captured stocks.
Its middle choice either preserves selected strategic industry or accepts deeper destruction for a larger reserve.

The late payoff is a strong finite offensive window after a real regional harvest.
Its cost is a continuing need for new material.
When the front stalls, the Empire faces a food problem that cannot be solved by another free focus reward.

The method's postwar objective secures a second supply region before the first region is exhausted.
Failure leaves a visible shortage and strengthens the need for a new offensive.
It does not spawn a second unrelated civil war.

### HS-04: long-term farming

This method develops productive feeding territories, regular tribute and predictable reserve management.
Its early penalty is slower extraction and the need to commit resources before receiving a return.
Its middle choice favors directly administered productive regions or subject-managed feeding territories.

The late payoff is a stable five-day reserve and a less fragile economy.
The route needs successful farming and logistics missions.
A country that has eaten most of its own population cannot claim the same result without rebuilding a viable productive region.

The method's diplomacy remains coercive and expansionist.
It does not make external neighbors exempt from the Empire's war behavior.
It changes what happens after conquest.

### HS-05: population conversion

This method creates controlled shaping programs and a new Cookie recruitment base.
Its early programs establish the safe accounting and production capacity needed for conversion.
Its middle choice favors rapid mass conversion or slower specialist conversion.

Mass conversion supplies more ordinary bodies with lower efficiency and greater resistance.
Specialist conversion supplies fewer but more capable bodies and requires a stronger industrial base.
Both remove actual population and respect their conversion-capacity limit.

The late payoff is a developed nonhuman state with a wider army base.
Its cost is permanent demographic change and an occupation burden.
It cannot be presented as a harmless manpower gift or counted again as an eating-death total.

### HS-06: direct destruction

This method organizes the removal of buildings, stores and population as a war program.
Its early benefit is the strongest immediate food and siege return.
Its middle choice determines whether destruction follows the advancing front or is concentrated in selected rear regions.

Front-side destruction makes captured territory harder for enemies to recover but weakens supply.
Rear-region destruction protects the current front while hollowing out the Empire's own economy.
Both leave persistent state evidence.

The late payoff is a major destructive offensive and a direct route toward The Final Bite.
The method has the weakest long-term recovery.
An ordinary victory leaves a damaged world that continues to constrain the Empire.

### HS-07: enforce the reserve policy

This common group formalizes the military-reserve, balanced and emergency feeding policies.
It unlocks a finite audit of one resource family and reduces waste in conversion.
The player can protect critical trains, convoys or specialist equipment while allowing other stocks to be eaten.

The advanced stage permits one emergency override during a genuine shortage.
The override has a cooldown and an explicit reserve cost.
It cannot be toggled back and forth to create food, clear hunger penalties or change a saved reward.

### HS-08: rule after the first conquests

Require a governing method and a real administered region.
The branch creates method-specific occupation decisions and connects to The Sweet Empire.
Its capstone requires a 180-day objective showing that the chosen method can maintain control.

Rapid consumption must keep a supplied front and obtain new material.
Farming must complete productive cycles.
Conversion must maintain order through a completed program.
Destruction must hold a devastated region without losing the army to its own supply damage.

The final appetite-spirit stage is earned through that objective.
Its label, visual identity and consequences differ by method.
The Monster remains the same ruler, while the state it commands has developed a distinct form.
