# 070 Cookie Click: evolutions and personality

## Evolution and ordinary growth

Cookie Level describes this individual cookie.
Evolution describes the new kinds of behavior that global Chaos has made possible.
A larger Level number alone cannot consume factories before Evolution II or civilians before Evolution III.
An evolution can unlock those capabilities without granting free levels, lifetime clicks or rewards.

The requested thresholds remain 200, 400 and 600 Chaos.
An already active cookie uses the project's evolution eligibility and MTTH route.
Use a nominal 90-day evolution timing baseline after the relevant threshold is met, with scenario-tested modifiers for sustained starvation and high Level.
Do not implement the threshold as an automatic same-day activation.
The exact MTTH probability depends on the verified game-version adapter.

An opening selected in an already evolved world may initialize at the highest eligible evolution allowed by the framework.
It has no invented past feeding history.
Its initial target uses that evolution's multiplier, and its early-removal status follows that evolution.
The 300-click promise describes the unevolved baseline.

An active instance evolves in order.
Only one evolution transition is resolved in a cycle.
An already active evolution is permanent for that instance even if global Chaos later drops.
The framework's disabled-evolution settings prevent a new activation, without pretending that past consumption never occurred.

Activation changes future reward and consumption pools, appearance overlays, personality and maturity.
Today's target and saved reward track stay fixed.
New destructive families become eligible at the next cycle, and the player receives their warning before the first bite from that family.
Evolution activation alone does not add Chaos.

## Evolution I: A Bigger Appetite

The first evolution makes the cookie visibly heavier and more interested in material objects.
Metallic crumbs and occasional equipment-shaped silhouettes join its expressions.
It can now eat stored fuel, manpower, trains, convoys and equipment.
These losses are large enough to affect mobilisation and logistics without deleting deployed armies.

The daily target multiplier becomes 1.5.
Daily and development reward budgets use the same 1.5 evolution multiplier.
More valuable equipment and logistics packets become valid.
The player may receive a complete missing logistics component instead of another small abstract reward.

Maturity begins at Level 5.
A still-weak specimen can be killed after 5 entirely unfed cycles, but an already mature one survives.
The transition warning identifies this loss of the safe-removal option.

This stage introduces a preference memory.
The cookie remembers the last successfully eaten family and has an additional 25 relative weight to choose that family during its next hungry bite.
The preference lasts for 5 cycles.
It cannot make an empty family valid.
Repeated consumption therefore has a recognizable personality without becoming an always-identical drain.

A host can begin protecting one exposed stockpile family through a preparation decision.
Protection reduces that family's chance of selection and preserves a minimum reserve.
It does not stop other bites, and it has a finite duration and a real equipment or administrative cost.
Only one protected family exists at a time.

## Evolution II: The Cookie Is Watching

The second evolution changes the relationship from a hungry pet to an actor that understands the country around it.
The cookie looks toward the next reward icon, notices production shortages, remembers broken feeding streaks and makes demands tied to real assets.
It does not claim knowledge of private multiplayer messages or actions outside the game.

The daily target and reward multipliers become 2.0.
Industry, research and supported national-mechanic packets enter the reward pool.
A mature cookie can now take finite construction or production capacity, verified research progress, factory machinery, building levels and valid national values supplied by other systems.

The first productive-asset bite identifies the state or national project that was actually affected.
This exposes a bakery anchor.
The anchor is a real eligible state with industry, supply access or a strong reward history.
There are at most three candidate anchors.
They are not instantly converted into free buildings.

At high Level, the cookie begins preparing to govern.
It leaves recognizable demands, makes its crown more prominent, and changes the surrounding frame.
The host can identify a threatened region, evacuate civilians or prepare a resistance depot.
These actions become useful before the revolt occurs.

The revolt route requires the thresholds in Part 03.
Threat flavour is attached to warning milestones, not randomly spammed every few hours.
The cookie can still recover fully before the uprising commit.

## Evolution III: Feed Me

The third evolution allows targets in the thousands and rewards that can reshape the recipient's military and economy.
The cookie can now eat major assets, including actual civilian population, infrastructure, stockpiles and military reserves.
Consumption still requires a valid registered operation and exact accounting.

The daily target and reward multipliers become 3.0.
Advanced equipment, major construction and compatible technology outcomes become possible.
The strongest prizes remain tied to full feeding and development thresholds.
Clicking the first quarter of a day should not deliver a rare technology.

The lower revolt threshold makes a moderately developed cookie dangerous.
The warning remains visible, and a real recovery can still stop it before commitment.
The cookie's final demands are attached to the actual threatened region and existing government.

This evolution adds a second consumption choice when hungry.
First choose a valid family by the weighted pool.
Then compare two valid assets inside that family, preferring the one that better sustains the future uprising.
The comparison may favor a useful industrial state, a large reserve, a critical railway stockpile or a known bakery anchor.
It is bounded to the selected family's cached candidates.
It is not a daily exhaustive optimization across the whole world.

## Personality memory

Keep four internal memories: last consumed family, last major reward family, number of completed full cycles, and whether the owner has recovered from a genuine revolt warning.
Their values feed expressions and flavour selection.
Do not expose them as relationship points.

A cookie that has mostly supplied military equipment should refer to military objects more often.
A cookie that has repeatedly consumed political resources should treat official documents as food.
A cookie rescued from a late warning temporarily shows grudging compliance rather than resetting to the introductory smile.

Personality never changes a numerical consequence without a matching gameplay rule.
Flavour cannot falsely promise a reward, claim a loss or add an unsupported threat.

## Event beats

| Beat | Trigger and cadence | Player-facing direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Normal opening only | An attractive opportunity with a clear care requirement |
| First full day | Once after actual completion | Confirm the practical reward and visible growth |
| First Level band | Once per appearance band | Show what physically changed and the next day's greater appetite |
| First hunger | Once for the instance | Establish that neglect has consequences |
| First successful bite | Once per newly eaten family | Name the actual loss and source |
| First protected reserve breached | Once per protection episode | Explain the limit of the preparation |
| First bakery anchor exposed | Evolution II or later | Connect a local state to the larger threat |
| Maturity | Once | Make the loss of safe starvation removal clear |
| Threat begins | Once per genuine warning episode | Identify the developing uprising and recovery route |
| Warning nears deadline | Once at the final two warning cycles | Make the remaining response unambiguous |
| Recovery | After a genuine warning is cancelled | Acknowledge that losses remain and the relationship has changed |
| Weak death | Once | Dryness and collapse, without a celebratory reward |
| Revolt | Once per instance | Permanent transition to the country crisis |
| Empire defeat | Once per instance | Explain the end of the military threat and lasting damage |

Ordinary bite flavour has a 5-cycle cooldown per family.
A new high-severity loss can bypass that cooldown once.
Use compact non-modal notices for routine appetite reactions.
Reserve modal events for decisions, major transitions and information the player needs to act on.

## Surprise and fairness

The complete unit roster and final world-end route can remain unrevealed early.
The ability to lose resources, the permanent maturity threshold, the next feeding target and an active revolt warning cannot be hidden.

Do not add arbitrary instant failure when a player reaches a round Level.
Feeding is always mechanically useful before the revolt commits.
The danger comes from maintaining a growing obligation and later neglecting it, not from secretly punishing a successful click.
