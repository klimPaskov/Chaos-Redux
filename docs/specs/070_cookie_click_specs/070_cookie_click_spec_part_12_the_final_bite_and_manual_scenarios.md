# 070 Cookie Click: The Final Bite and manual scenarios

## The final campaign

The Final Bite is a world-end campaign owned by Event 070.
Reaching 1,000 Chaos makes its ordinary entry eligible.
It does not instantly finish the game, annex every country or convert the entire map.
The Cookie Empire must already exist, be independent, possess a viable army and complete the expansion and development requirements below.

The scenario has a stable registry identity, a default-enabled scenario toggle and an owner reference to Event 070.
The event-picker toggle and this scenario toggle remain independent.
Disabling the ordinary Cookie opening does not silently disable a manually selected uprising.
Disabling The Final Bite prevents its ordinary activation and automatic selection by a world-end picker.
An explicit manual setup selection can launch this named scenario directly, with its bypass shown in setup.
Disabling a setting after an active campaign has started does not delete that campaign or refund its damage.

## Qualification

Ordinary qualification requires 1,000 or more current Chaos, Cookie Level 30 or higher, a functioning capital, the combined-arms program, an established governing method, and one completed regional settlement program.
The settlement proof can come from direct rule, managed feeding territories, conversion or destruction.
No method must secretly finish the farming route to qualify.

Expansion is measured against the world snapshot taken when the Empire formed.
The Empire must directly control or govern through Cookie subjects at least 10 percent of that snapshot's eligible inhabited land states, with a minimum of five states.
An unusually small custom map uses its actual eligible total and an attainable minimum.
Do not change the denominator every time a country fragments.
Do not count uninhabited map artifacts, inaccessible states or a subject's territory twice.
Territories merely occupied by a non-Cookie ally do not count.

At least half of the qualifying territory must be supplied from the Cookie capital or from a functioning Cookie port network.
This makes the route an established empire's escalation.
A mass of disconnected occupation flags cannot substitute for the required campaign.

## Final-branch architecture

| Path group | Entry | Choice and work | Payoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FF-01, prepare the global campaign | All ordinary qualification requirements | A 70-day focus commits industry and reserves, then a 90-day preparation mission requires working supply, equipped frontline forces and a maintained food reserve | Opens the irreversible commitment |
| FF-02, cross the final threshold | FF-01 completed and the scenario remains enabled | A separate 35-day commitment with an explicit warning and a cancel opportunity before completion | Activates The Final Bite, the transformation presentation and the global-war campaign |
| FF-03, great ovens | FF-02 | Build a real supplied production center, equip an enormous oven formation and preserve its fuel or equivalent operating supply | Unlocks the mobile oven formation and its reinforcement line |
| FF-04, the Monster's household | FF-02 and the elite preparation program | Choose a small strong guard establishment or a broader weaker elite establishment within the shared elite cap | Unlocks the final Monster Guard formation |
| FF-05, consume the world | FF-02 and one late-unit program | Choose method-specific world-end administration, with real state operations and a final opposition objective | Opens the final settlement sequence |
| FF-06, the last organized resistance | FF-05 and a real surviving opposition | Defeat remaining independent enemies and eliminate durable organized opposition through war and control | Confirms the terminal Cookie-world result |

The focus tree shows the final branch in an appropriate late-game filter.
Before the Empire can know its requirements, a concise high-danger description can conceal exact late rewards without hiding present costs.
Once the commitment is available, the player sees the irreversible consequences.
The ordinary pet interface never offers this branch.

FF-01 requires a food reserve of at least 75 Fullness, 100 percent equipment in its designated assault formations, and two functioning supply anchors.
Its payment is 25 percent of current Cookie-body reserves above the published domestic floor, 50 Army Experience, and 5 percent of the empire's current fuel stockpile when a fuel-using army, air force or navy is present.
The body-equipment payment is capped at a country-scaled package equivalent to equipping five standard Cookie Infantry formations.
The resource combination is previewed before commitment.
A country without fuel-using forces receives a real logistics-equipment requirement instead of a fictional fuel debit.
The plan does not demand more than four distinct spendable resources.

The 90-day preparation mission requires the reserve and designated supply conditions for 30 consecutive days.
A temporary interruption restarts that maintained-condition interval, not the entire focus.
Failure delays the commitment and keeps spent preparation resources spent.
A second attempt takes 90 days and cannot repay the original reward.

## The irreversible launch

At FF-02 completion, take one world-end activation snapshot and set the active campaign state before any presentation.
Give a reinforcement package equivalent to 25 percent of the Cookie army's currently equipped battalion strength.
Limit its immediate portion to formations that can deploy into supplied controlled states.
Queue the remainder through a visible mobilization mission.
This is one package, not a daily free-division effect.

Grant 30 days of its corresponding Cookie equipment and operating-food support through the same saved package budget.
A force still waiting for deployment does not also receive equipment as an unrelated national grant.
Previously consumed assets remain part of history and are not charged again.

Upgrade the existing appetite spirit to its world-end stage.
At high Fullness it adds 25 percent breakthrough and 25 percent organization recovery as the total world-end appetite contribution.
It does not stack every earlier appetite tier.
At low Fullness the severe hunger penalties remain.
The scenario gives substantial power while keeping the army dependent on logistics.

Remove the ordinary protected population floor only for explicitly selected world-end consumption operations.
The player must choose destructive actions and see their actual demographic consequences.
Activation does not automatically kill people or delete buildings in every owned state.

## Global aggression

At activation, build a saved queue of all existing non-Cookie countries.
Declare wars in small bounded batches, with a target of five new valid declarations per daily campaign update.
The player-facing announcement is a global war policy.
The implementation must not fire an unbounded recursive war cascade in one callback.

Continue adding newly created non-Cookie countries through country-creation and border-change hooks.
Recheck queued targets before declaration.
Do not redeclare a war already in progress.
Countries inside the Cookie political domain remain exempt until they leave that domain.

Separate islands, distant capitals and hostile fleets still matter.
The Empire requires ports, transport technology, naval access and actual movement to conquer them.
The Final Bite does not teleport armies across the sea.
Its naval program receives a one-time convoy and escort-production acceleration when it has valid ports and ship technology, paid from the world-end reserve package.
Do not grant a battleship fleet to a landlocked country.

## World-end units

The mobile oven is a slow heavy support formation.
Its role is sustained siege and conversion of delivered material into reinforcement near a supplied front.
It cannot consume population in neighboring enemy states from a hidden radius.
The player or AI must capture and control the target first.
Its supply use is 150 percent of the equivalent ordinary heavy Cookie formation and its soft-attack target is 175 percent of that formation before shared country modifiers.
Its distinctive weakness is movement and supply interruption.

The Monster Guard is an elite assault formation with strong organization, defense and breakthrough.
Its base stat target is 150 percent of the comparable Chocolate Guard in its chosen role, with an equipment cost and food burden of at least 175 percent.
The broader-establishment choice lowers its stat target to 125 percent and raises the formation allowance within the same total elite-battalion budget.
Both versions share one visually coherent family and clearly different equipment requirements.

All world-end elite formations together remain capped at 10 percent of deployed Cookie land battalions.
The cap is an authored gameplay rule enforced before recruitment or scripted rewards.
A minimum of one small elite formation is permitted so a smaller qualifying Empire can use the branch.
That floor cannot be multiplied by selecting several elite families.
A unit above the cap after casualties is retained but prevents further elite recruitment until the ratio recovers.

## World-end administration

Rapid consumption gains a 25 percent increase to actual material-to-food conversion and faster destructive operations.
It still needs new territory when reserves run out.
Efficient farming gains 25 percent greater output from functioning prepared feeding territories and stronger protection for their supply.
It does not receive food from destroyed farms.
Population conversion gains a 25 percent improvement to the Cookie manpower return, with outputs rounded down to complete supported units.
Direct destruction gains a 50 percent food return from actual destroyed building levels and captured major stockpiles.
Its reward is finite and its ruined rear remains a liability.

These changes replace earlier method stages where they affect the same modifier.
They do not add a fourth national spirit.
The applicable bonus operates once on the actual transaction.
Moving a resource through a subject and back cannot apply it twice.

## Organized opposition

The surviving countries receive a readable world threat and practical defensive responses.
They can evacuate threatened civilians, deny selected stockpiles, protect ports, coordinate supply access and recover consumed territory.
Use existing diplomatic systems where supported.
Do not force every democracy, dictatorship and rival faction into a new faction by an unverified automatic effect.

An emergency anti-Cookie league is optional and requires at least two independent participants, a shared war against the Empire and valid diplomatic participation.
Existing factions can cooperate through military access, aid and coordinated objectives without being overwritten.
The league has a concrete goal of destroying the Cookie Empire or keeping a functioning supplied resistance alive.
Membership ends through withdrawal, defeat or success.
Its decisions use real member resources and do not create an unlimited reinforcement fund.

Resistance remains organized while a non-Cookie actor controls an inhabited state and can sustain an army, government or supplied organized resistance there.
Exiled governments without controlled territory do not by themselves prevent the map result forever.
A functioning overseas state does.
Evaluate this distinction through verified game data and document unsupported custom-map cases.

## Completion, defeat and aftermath

The terminal result requires all eligible inhabited states to be controlled by the Cookie domain, or their remaining non-Cookie inhabitants and organized opposition to have been removed through actual resolved operations.
Do not treat an empty event variable as evidence that every remaining enemy has been defeated.
Use a bounded maintained set of surviving actors and confirm the result once.

At completion, stop unnecessary global target scans, show the final presentation once, and retain a playable aftermath.
The remaining world continues to support Cookie rule and its appetite.
Already consumed population, industry and resources remain gone.
Do not instantly restore the map as a reward.

If the Empire is destroyed before completion, end The Final Bite, release only event-owned emergency locks, and leave ordinary postwar settlement to the surviving countries.
Its subjects do not all vanish.
They can retain their country identities while their Cookie-domain status is removed through the defeat settlement.
The world-end campaign cannot reactivate from an old saved focus completion.

## Manual scenario setup

The manual Cookie Monster Uprising starts the revolt immediately.
It does not require prior clicks, Level, hunger, Chaos, evolutions, elapsed days, event-picker availability, a prior pet or a completed focus.
The selected host, valid uprising territory and unused country identity remain structural requirements.
Explain an impossible map setup instead of pretending those requirements are progression gates.

A selected host with an existing living Cookie instance can be promoted directly into the chosen manual uprising.
A different active Cookie Empire creates a structural identity conflict.
The setup must offer a different host within the same active campaign where that makes sense, or reject the conflicting creation explicitly.
It must not silently create a second country using the same tag.
Ending a previous Cookie instance does not permanently prohibit future explicit manual scenarios.

These are direct setup profiles, so they intentionally replace the ordinary uprising percentage formula.
Small hosts still use the five-light-formation floor and structural territory rules.
The final profile can exceed the ordinary 30 percent territory cap because the setup explicitly selects that intensity.
It must not remove the last viable host state.

Manual history is synthetic scaling information.
It does not add fictional past deaths, previously eaten factories, historical reward payouts, achievement progress or Chaos to the world.
Use a separate scenario-strength field where necessary.
Do not fabricate lifetime-click statistics to satisfy ordinary achievements.
Current setup changes are real and are recorded once.

The first three presets start active wars with the host and current external neighbors.
The world-end preset additionally queues all remaining non-Cookie countries immediately.
Default player control remains with the selected host.
A separate explicit setup choice permits playing the Empire without changing the preset's strength.

A custom intensity slider interpolates only documented quantities such as initial strength, territory and reserves.
It cannot bypass country identity, capital, ownership, equipment availability or map validity.
Show the resulting preview before committing.

## Scenario replay

Each manual launch receives a new instance ID.
Retired instance histories remain sufficient to prevent old callbacks from paying rewards into the new campaign.
Scenario setup records whether normal achievements are eligible.
Default manual launches are ineligible for normal campaign achievements.
A separate scenario-specific achievement set can use its own explicit eligibility rule.
