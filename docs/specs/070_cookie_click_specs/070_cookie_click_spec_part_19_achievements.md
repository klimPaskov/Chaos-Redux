# 070 Cookie Click: achievements

## Achievement rules

The achievement identifiers and descriptions below are design records.
Final titles and player-facing text are localization work.
Do not use these role labels as finished names.

Normal achievements require a normal campaign instance with an actual human owner.
Manual scenario initialization, debug grants, direct state edits and unauthorized control changes cannot fabricate normal achievement progress.
The optional disconnected-owner suspension disqualifies achievements that require continuous care.
Historical fake clicks from a scenario are never eligible clicks.

Every achievement has earned, unearned and disallowed icon states.
Use 64 × 64 outputs through the actual project achievement pipeline.
Track relevant milestones event-wise.
Do not run fourteen full-world scans daily.

## Contribution proof

Achievement A-05 requires the host to contribute real combat or territorial recovery.
Use the game's supported war contribution and occupation evidence where available.
Do not invent an exact contribution statistic that the current game cannot expose.
A supported fallback is control of the original bakery capital plus participation in the war that eliminates the Empire.
Document the chosen implementation before enabling the achievement.

A-10 uses a dynamically selected genuinely strong enemy at the campaign start.
Save its qualified status and industrial baseline.
Do not let the AI or player choose a one-state released subject and label it a major after its industry falls.
The proof requires an actual meaningful share of that baseline to have been captured and consumed by this instance.

A-11 checks each required family in a real combat-capable formation within the regional campaign.
A formation has to remain supplied and participate in the selected operation.
A battalion parked at home for a single frame does not count.
Use supported combat and control evidence, with an explicit fallback test contract when the engine cannot report every desired detail.

## Hidden and visible information

The early-removal and continuous-care achievements can be visible from the pet phase.
Revolt, method and world-end achievements can reveal with their corresponding gameplay.
An achievement must not reveal every hidden late unit or exact danger draw before the event exposes that route.

The visible achievement description explains what the player can actually do.
The internal tracking note preserves strict anti-farming conditions without turning the achievement screen into an implementation report.
Disallowed icons and tooltips explain manual or debug ineligibility.

## Scenario badges

The four manual presets can record separate scenario completion badges in the scenario framework when that framework supports them.
They do not impersonate normal campaign achievements.
Their condition is the chosen host surviving or the explicitly selected Empire completing its scenario objective at the selected intensity.
This is optional integration and does not reduce the fourteen required achievement designs.
