# Event 011 Secret Alliance Research Notes

These notes are design inspiration only. They do not provide final localisation, final super-event text, or final historical claims for in-game text.

## Historical Inspiration Anchors

Use these patterns to ground the fictional pact:

- Interwar defensive pacts: the Little Entente and Balkan Entente support the idea of small states forming consultation structures and defensive compacts around a named threat.
- Ideological pacts: the Anti-Comintern Pact supports a public justification that differs from the actual anti-player target.
- Covert influence networks: Polish Prometheism and wartime special operations support press work, emigre contacts, courier routes, sabotage preparation, and intelligence cells.
- Manufactured provocation: false-flag incidents and staged border crises support the border-war and provocation design, but should be handled seriously and never as a gag.
- Secret-war logistics: railway, port, safehouse, and industrial security problems give the player concrete defensive targets.

## How To Use The Research

The event is fictional and campaign-dynamic. Do not imply that a real historical alliance was secretly directed at the player's country unless a later country-specific implementation researches that claim.

Use history for:

- plausible council and consultation language
- covert network mechanics
- sabotage and counter-sabotage surfaces
- neutral observer and public dossier decisions
- border provocation pacing
- faction reveal tone

Avoid:

- naming real-world atrocities casually
- turning false-flag references into jokes
- implying every small-state pact was secretly aggressive
- using real photographs of a historical meeting as if it were the fictional pact
