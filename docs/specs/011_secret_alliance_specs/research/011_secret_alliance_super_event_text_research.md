# Event 011 Secret Alliance super-event text research

Status: historical candidate research. The title is no longer blocked. The implemented package uses `THE PACT UNMASKED`, the Sun Tzu quotation, the `Look about you.` button fragment, five route descriptions, slot `73`, and audio ID `43`. See `docs/super_events/011_secret_alliance_super_event_research.md`.

## Super-event role

The super-event appears the first time the concealed pact becomes a public faction. It is a reveal and faction-formation moment. It is not a world-end presentation and should not sound like an inevitable apocalypse.

The player should feel that many earlier incidents have acquired a single meaning. The coalition has stopped hiding, yet its unity and military competence still depend on prior play.

## Title research direction

At the time of this candidate pass, final title wording remained open. That planning state is superseded by the implemented title `THE PACT UNMASKED`.

Useful concepts:

- the conference becomes public
- the ring closes
- the pact declares itself
- hidden coordination acquires a name
- separate governments speak through one coalition

The title should not use the target country's dynamic adjective unless the UI can guarantee clean grammar in every language.

## Main quote candidates

### Selected recommendation

> “All warfare is based on deception.”
>
> Sun Tzu, *The Art of War*, Lionel Giles translation

**Source verification:** Project Gutenberg reproduces the line in Chapter I, passage 18.

**Attribution confidence:** High for the Giles translation hosted by Project Gutenberg.

**Rights note:** The Giles translation is public domain in the United States. Local distribution rights should still be checked by the implementation researcher for the mod's intended distribution regions.

**Fit:** The quote covers the full event arc. It applies to secret coordination, target counterintelligence, false plans, and the moment when deception gives way to open war. It is short enough for the super-event UI.

### Backup candidate A

> “There is but one mind in all these men, and it is bent against Caesar.”
>
> Artemidorus in William Shakespeare's *Julius Caesar*, Act II, Scene III

**Source verification:** Project Gutenberg text of *Julius Caesar*.

**Attribution confidence:** High.

**Rights note:** Public-domain play.

**Fit:** Strong for a player-targeted coalition and an investigation that gradually identifies members. It is more personal and dramatic than the selected recommendation. It may feel too specific because it names Caesar.

### Backup candidate B

>
> Psalm 83:5, King James Version

**Source verification:** The King James text is reproduced by several public-domain repositories, including Project Gutenberg and Wikisource.

**Attribution confidence:** High for the translation wording.

**Rights note:** King James Version text is public domain in the United States. Crown rights considerations can differ in the United Kingdom and should be checked before final distribution.

**Fit:** Very direct fit for a confederation against one target. It carries a religious register that may not fit every campaign or target ideology.

## Description direction

The description should state that several governments have publicly entered one faction against the target after a period of coordinated incidents. It should mention delegations, military liaison, public commitments, and the immediate strategic consequence. It should not list every hidden operation or explain the Evidence and Preparedness formulas.

Dynamic content may mention:

- the faction's dynamic public name
- the faction leader
- the number of public members
- whether one or two major sponsors are present
- whether the coalition emerged through war, public proclamation, or target exposure

The prose should vary by reveal route:

- **Hostile war reveal:** emphasize rapid alignment as one member's war activates prearranged commitments.
- **Pact-controlled reveal:** emphasize a public conference and declared common purpose.
- **Player-forced reveal:** emphasize hurried admissions, denials, and members choosing whether to stand together.
- **Fractured reveal:** emphasize incomplete attendance and visible uncertainty without making the coalition harmless.

The description should avoid contrast formulas built around officials denying what witnesses saw. It should describe the public commitment directly.

## Audio direction

Final audio selection is blocked until the audio researcher verifies composition and recording rights.

Desired shape:

- 75 to 115 seconds after editing
- real music with a controlled, tense opening and a firm martial or ceremonial middle
- a sense of formal alignment rather than supernatural horror
- suitable for a reveal that can be dangerous without being terminal
- no modern trailer percussion
- no pure drone, stinger, test tone, generated oscillator, or abstract sound bed
- enough restraint to fit a coalition formed by diplomats and officers

Potential source families for the audio researcher:

- public-domain chamber or orchestral recordings with a measured march or tightening motif
- clearly licensed period-style ensemble music
- government or institutional archive recordings with verified recording rights
- Creative Commons music that permits redistribution and derivative editing

The audio researcher must compare several candidates, preserve source files, document composition and recording rights separately, convert the selected track to 44.1 kHz OGG, and provide unique audio IDs and paths for this super-event.

## Image direction

Use generated alternate-history documentary art because the coalition is procedural and cannot be represented by a real archival meeting.

The image should show several period delegations and military representatives in a formal hall at the moment of common commitment. The central composition should be the human coalition, not a map. A broken seal, folded map, or shared emblem can appear as a secondary symbol. No readable generated text or fixed national flags should limit the image to one participant set.
