## Research limits

- Historical examples are structural references, not faction templates.
- No source is used to claim that all secret alliances follow one pattern.
- No real-world operational sabotage instructions are reproduced in the design.
- The selected quote and remark remain subject to final localisation and distribution review.
- Audio remains unselected until a dedicated licensing and recording-rights pass is completed.
