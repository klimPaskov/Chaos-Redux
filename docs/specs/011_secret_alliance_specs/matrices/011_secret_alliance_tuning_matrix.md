# Event 011 Tuning Matrix

This is a design tuning matrix. Final values should live in script constants or documented tuning files.

| Tuning area | Low pressure direction | Medium pressure direction | High pressure direction | Dynamic factors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening member count | 3 minors | 4 to 5 minors in E1 opening | Major founder plus minors in E2 opening | Chaos tier, valid candidates, target strength |
| Operation pacing | Slow, around 45 to 90 days | Moderate, around 35 to 70 days | Fast, around 25 to 55 days | Secrecy, hostility, readiness, player counter-network |
| Evolution I pacing | Long delay after baseline | Shorter if pact has successful operations | Faster if chaos and target threat high | Chaos, target wars, pact cohesion |
| Evolution II pacing | Requires meaningful pressure or chaos | Major patron joins after enough recruitment | Pre-fire opening starts with major founder | Chaos, valid major patron, target isolation |
| Evolution III pacing | Requires E2 pressure and readiness | Public crisis after repeated sabotage or failed counterplay | Fast if player ignored pact and chaos high | Preparedness, evidence, pact readiness |
| Evidence gain | Small clue | Member exposure clue | Patron or full pact clue | Operation failure, decision success, agency, allies |
| Sabotage damage | Minor repair burden | Named state damage or temporary idea | Major industrial hit, rare and counterable | Industry size, security missions, pact hostility |
| Defection odds | Low | Moderate if exposed and weak | High if evidence strong and patron absent | Commitment, cohesion, target diplomacy, ideology |
| War countdown | Long delay | Moderate delay | Short delay | Readiness, cohesion, player preparedness, public exposure |
| Member join after reveal | Hesitation checks common | Most committed members join | All valid core members join on war-trigger reveal | Reveal type, evidence, commitment, cohesion |
