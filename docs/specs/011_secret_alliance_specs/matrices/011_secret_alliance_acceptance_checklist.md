# Event 011 Secret Alliance Acceptance Checklist

Use this as a completion proof checklist after implementation.

- Founder selection requires three valid live countries and returns unavailable when it cannot satisfy the rule.
- Founder roles are assigned and affect behavior.
- Member invitations are dynamic, capped, and cleaned up.
- Baseline incidents are subtle and do not reveal member identities.
- Evolution I, Evolution II, and Evolution III are logged as evolutions, not ordinary baseline stages.
- Active-event and pre-fire evolution paths are implemented.
- Major patron logic exists and is safe.
- Player decision category opens at Evolution II or equivalent suspicion state.
- Decisions use varied resource costs and real objectives.
- Border missions require real state, border, or route conditions.
- Public exposure can weaken or reveal the pact and open public crisis decisions without forcing instant war, faction formation, or the reveal super-event.
- Any war between target and member triggers formal reveal.
- Formal reveal through target-member war or final counter-ultimatum pressure forms or reuses the Anti-[target country] Pact path and joins live members to war.
- Super-event package is final, researched, sourced, wired, and documented.
- Static and animated assets have final DDS outputs and manifests.
- Event log, event detail, docs, and spreadsheet handoff align.
- Achievements have conditions, disqualifiers, icons, and tracking.
- AI has valid behavior for pact members, target, patrons, and observers.
- Localisation uses final in-world wording and does not paste planning text.
- Required audits have been run and all simplifications are reported.
