# 08. Decisions, missions, faction play and AI

## Decisions turn the campaign into concrete operations

Every action changes a real military, political, logistical or institutional situation. The six-family and one-to-three-mission presentation budgets below are design limits, not native engine row limits. Rendering or consumer evidence is needed for any clipping or capacity claim. The player chooses a target, commits resources, protects a process, and receives the result actually achieved. Opening a menu, repeatedly buying a small modifier, or allowing a timer to expire without a relevant objective is not a complete operation.

The ordinary decision category is sufficient for the accepted design. It has the required animated picture, current phase, Momentum, Influence when relevant, a concise objective summary, and native actions. No dedicated scripted management window is assumed. Each role and phase exposes at most six principal action families and normally one to three active objective missions. Target variants remain part of a meaningful family. Moving dozens of weak buttons into extra categories does not satisfy this budget.

## Shared action contract and dynamic costs

Every action records its initiating country, exact target, event episode, operation identity, quoted resources, reserved resources, due date, success predicate, interruption predicate, consumed result and AI intent. The same cost owner supplies the displayed amount, affordability test and payment. A delayed callback revalidates those facts. It cannot quietly change the target to another country or consume a new leader's authority.

The four spendable cost types are political power, command power, actual personnel and actual equipment. Time and a held location are requirements. Influence, Momentum, Chaos, Deaths, and hidden preparation markers are not extra currencies. Equipment is a typed vector of real existing items, not a fictitious universal stock. A country's inability to afford a preparation never hides its free final independence action.

The table amounts below are initial tuning anchors. For domestic institutional work let `s = clamp(sqrt(max(1, eligible country states) / 10), 0.5, 2)`, using the country's actual eligible owned or controlled pool at the accepted quote. Political-power cost is the listed base multiplied by s and rounded upward to the next five. Command-power cost is the listed base multiplied by `0.75 + 0.25s`, rounded upward to the next five and capped at sixty. Zero stays zero. These scale a small state differently from a large state without making tiny countries pay a great power's bill.

For target operations, use the actual target set in that scale calculation. For personnel and equipment, quote the exact native template, actual intended cohort, current equipment deficit, route and transport requirements. Do not multiply a nominal infantry package and pretend that it supplies a tank formation. The country sees the actual total before acceptance. The formula is a planning proposal requiring installed-engine arithmetic and affordability scenarios.

Unless a row specifies a fixed political deadline, ordinary preparation takes its listed base duration, plus thirty days when a required depot or institution is disrupted, minus fifteen days for the directly relevant completed focus, bounded between forty-five and one hundred eighty days. Continuous occupation or supply proofs reset when their stated condition breaks. This does not reset an already accepted political ultimatum. The thirteen-day response, 180-day critical struggle, 120-day ordinary subject countdown and ninety-day terminal countdown retain their explicit fairness contracts.

Reserved resources remain traceable. Cancellation refunds only the action's undelivered reservations. Already delivered aid, consumed equipment, moved people, real casualties, completed political concessions and used transport remain real. A failed operation does not grant its success reward or let the AI buy it again on the same day.

## Trotsky's autonomous operation set

Trotsky's protected country shares joint-offensive, council, research and targeted-cell operations, with real costs and contributions. Its accepted status is autonomous cofounder for the entire crisis, including after a leadership change. It has no Influence schedule, compulsory foreign command, disguised transformation threshold, submission choice or annex timer.

An independent-councils project uses the party-security cost and duration anchors but establishes a contributing voluntary institution rather than reducing a nonexistent meter. Opposing forced absorption takes a real diplomatic stand, protects a named partner or blocks participation in that specific operation. This may close military cooperation or produce a political break, but it cannot silently annex Trotsky's country.

The permanent-revolution campaign can complete two materially contributed operations on distinct continents and witness Lenin's actual rule exceed half the frozen inhabited world. That milestone is available without forcing the Red World terminal scenario. At terminal entry, the protected country chooses an autonomous concord or a political break. Refusal of concord ends the partnership and creates the specified war, while continued concord preserves its separate state and prevents a false claim of universal absorption.

## Faction identity and diplomatic rules

The working international is a supernatural successor to the Communist International, with a final name selected through historical and live-name collision review. Its practical goals are coordinated war against declared enemies, defense of accepted revolutionary bases, material and research cooperation, expansion through real local networks, and the political settlement chosen by the World Revolution. Those goals change with the collective, central or distributed route rather than being a decorative faction label.

Membership is limited to the World Revolution, actual accepting ordinary communist governments, and rebels whose real political choice qualifies. Trotsky receives the protected cofounder relationship. A joining country leaves an incompatible old faction through a valid native transition, retains unrelated wars and obligations where the engine permits, and receives a clear explanation where a real obligation prevents a proposed operation. A subject cannot independently create an impossible faction transition against its current overlord. Its response must use a verified subject-liberation or overlord-alignment transaction, with the limitation disclosed before implementation is accepted.

Joining does not imply immediate puppeting. Ordinary members retain their state, leaders, focus tree and local assets subject to the origin exception and later explicit institutions. Foreign troops, research, aid and command arrangements require the actual membership or access needed by their native mechanics. Faction goals and war plans do not teleport units or grant technology that no contributor knows.

Members can decline a specific project, negotiate narrower access, defend autonomy or leave through the free independence contract. Lenin's central route may retaliate politically or militarily but cannot retroactively label an ordinary aid acceptance as annexation consent. A collective route that later adopts terminal forced integration must make that political rupture visible and apply the final response contract. The faction is re-evaluated after Lenin's defeat, with actual surviving members choosing dissolution, a human international or another valid settlement. It does not leave a dead leader as an invisible permanent faction owner.

## AI model: willingness, discrete choices and targets

The numeric models below are initial implementation proposals. They define scenarios for the required probability review, not measured probabilities from the installed game. A discrete event option can be evaluated as a mutually exclusive weighted choice. Focus priorities and decision willingness must be inspected through their actual native adapters. A decision value of one hundred is not a guarantee of purchase or a percent chance.

All optional choices first apply validity. An impossible target, unaffordable action, absent actual actor, completed receipt, invalid subject transition or expired authority receives zero. Hard user rules override every weight: current Stalin in the Soviet Union refuses, current Trotsky in the Soviet Union joins autonomously, and ordinary noncommunist governments enter war. Human players receive the actual applicable choice and are not silently routed by AI preference.

### Ordinary communist join or refuse

Use `join weight = max(1, 50 + 40D + 20P + 20M + 15H - 35S - 30T - 20F)` and `refuse weight = max(1, 50 + 40S + 30T + 20F + 20A - 30D - 15H)`. Inputs are zero through one. D is immediate military danger measured as the greater of surrender progress and the fraction of eligible states actually lost in the opening. P is communist popularity. M is Momentum divided by one hundred. H is one when a verified current ideological or patron relationship already favors Lenin's international, otherwise zero. S is a sovereignty preference of one for a government that was an independent major at the opening and one half for other governments, including subjects once a valid response transition exists. A separate explicit voluntary-submission policy sets S to zero, while automatic binding does not. T is verified current hostility toward Lenin or allegiance to the Stalin refusal coalition. F is one for an incompatible existing faction obligation, otherwise zero. A is an accepted offer of material support from a real refusal partner, otherwise zero.

Normalize those two positive weights only within the valid two-option choice. These are disclosed design factors for native evaluation. Do not infer a character's preferences from an unverified surname or use the old blanket Soviet zero-join modifier. Existing rebel governments use the same danger and sovereignty factors for join/refuse, with voluntary merge and subordination offered only where valid. Join and refuse retain those exact formulas. The other valid weights are `subordinate = max(1, 25 + 20D - 20S)` and `merge = max(1, 15 + 20D - 20S)`. The political choice affects their real assets rather than adding an extra popularity roll.

### Focus routing

At the early strategic fork, the single-base domestic connection remains valid with L equal to one, while remote protection is zero without a real remote-base problem. Otherwise connection weight is `40 + 60L`, where L is the fraction of selected bases reachable through a short land corridor requiring at most two hostile intervening states. Remote protection weight is `40 + 60R`, where R is the fraction of selected bases that are isolated from such a corridor but have a real potential port, air or local-stock solution. Ineligible routes are zero. If both are valid, a native focus-choice scenario must test this exact competing set.

At the exclusive political fork, collective weight is `40 + 40C + 20I`, central weight is `40 + 40D + 20G`, and distributed weight is `40 + 40R + 20M`. C is the fraction of actual revolutionary institutional weight supplied by independent cooperating partners. I is one when a real voluntary international congress is functioning, otherwise zero. D is immediate military danger as above. G is the fraction of actual field strength under Lenin's direct command. R is the fraction of active bases isolated from the sovereign origin, and M is Momentum divided by one hundred. These values prefer routes suited to the map without making a route mandatory because of a single ally.

Subsequent focus priorities use an initial base of twenty for valid support anchors, plus eighty for resolving a current existential supply or origin threat, plus sixty for an active mission objective, plus forty for the chosen political route, and plus twenty for an affordable operation available immediately on completion. A focus requiring a nonexistent country, continent, port, donor or institution receives zero or its explicitly equivalent domestic alternative. A terminal focus is zero unless its actual terminal gates pass. A spent milestone cannot regain high priority merely because Momentum later crosses its old threshold.

### Decision willingness and military priorities

For valid discretionary operations use `will_do = min(300, 20 + 80U + 60Q + 40R + 30B - 40S)`. U is one for an existential origin, capital, body or annexation threat, otherwise zero. Q is one when the operation directly satisfies an active objective, otherwise zero. R is one when it advances the chosen political or military route, otherwise zero. B is the smallest post-payment to pre-payment reserve ratio across positively quoted resource types, with each ratio clamped to zero through one. Evaluate each equipment type separately, never add unlike resources together, and use B equal to one for a zero-cost action. S is supply strain, defined as the fraction of involved actual divisions currently failing the native supported supply criterion. If that exact native criterion cannot be inspected, the input remains unresolved until an accepted equivalent is verified. Clamp a valid discretionary weight to at least one. The named maximum is a willingness tuning scale and must not be converted to a probability by hand.

Maintain enough actual reserves for current front needs before starting optional conversions, distant cells or research exchanges. Defense of the origin or frozen homeland capital, preventing imminent absorption, protecting a paid reconstruction, and keeping essential supply open outrank prestige targets. When two paid operations need the same reserved cohort or stock, accept only one reservation. Do not let independent AI actions spend the same equipment quote twice.

Ordinary joined AI prefers party and command preparations at Influence sixty or above, and treats an active eighty-plus mission as urgent. If annexation is within thirty days, preparations cannot finish in time, and it has not made a separate explicit voluntary submission choice, it uses free independence even with poor survival odds. Terminal timeout never counts as choosing stay. At the terminal explicit choice, an ordinary unbound AI compares stay weight `30 + 60D + 20M` with break weight `60 + 40S + 30A`, using the same inputs above. An existing subject uses the same comparison and can still break. Trotsky compares autonomous concord base sixty plus forty for functioning joint cooperation against political break base forty plus sixty for actual forced-absorption conflict. Neither option enters Influence.

For military or cell targets, first exclude unreachable, invalid, already consumed and incompatible special-actor targets. Then use `target weight = 1 + 4S + 3L + 2P + 2W + 2N`, with each factor zero through one. S is strategic objective value normalized over the current target pool, L is one for a currently usable supplied route and zero otherwise, with an unreachable target already excluded. P is one for completed local preparation, one half for a real surviving disrupted preparation, and zero otherwise. W is the actual defending country's surrender progress, or zero when it has none. N is one when the target adjoins an accepted current operation or is connected by a real consenting transport route, and zero otherwise. Use stable target identities, inspect the actual pool, and save the accepted target. Strategic objective value assigns five to the actual enemy capital or active body/anchor objective and one to a named regional industry or supply objective. A target's actual current defense and logistics require native evidence. They are not guessed from flag color.

Stalin AI prioritizes its frozen capital, human command, industrial defense and compatible refusal aid. Trotsky AI prioritizes real joint operations and voluntary councils while protecting its permanent autonomy. Communist refusers seek human partners and targeted command destruction. Noncommunist AI retains actual other wars and faction obligations while confronting the universal Lenin war. Lenin AI first keeps the origin and host supplied, then connects fronts, forms viable commands and invests in a coherent route. All roles can pursue a reachable campaign ending without requiring the player to rescue their basic logic.

Those role descriptions map entirely to the stated validity gates and U, Q and R factors, with no extra hidden multiplier. Use the following exact intent mapping. U is set by an actual endangered origin, frozen homeland capital or body, or an annexation or critical binding deadline within thirty days. Q is set only by an active recorded mission that the action directly advances. R is set by the following role policy or the accepted political-route objective.

| Role | Actions that receive the role-policy R factor |
| --- | --- |
| Lenin before a sustainable first front | Secure origin, actual host supply, and the chosen connection or remote-base operation |
| Lenin after first-front success | The chosen philosophy's real institutions, a supplied command campaign, and currently affordable army standardization |
| Ordinary joiner below sixty Influence | The accepted cooperation policy's joint operation, actual aid or research project |
| Ordinary joiner at sixty Influence or above | Secure party and independent command, then removal of an actual access exposure. At eighty the active sovereignty mission additionally supplies Q |
| Stalin's Soviet refusal | Frozen-capital defense, human command, the industrial corridor and a real refusal-partner delivery |
| Other communist refuser | Human command, a real human-coalition project and a reachable command-liberation operation |
| Trotsky | Material joint operations, voluntary councils, protected research and an actual political defense of autonomy |
| Noncommunist defender | Frozen-homeland defense, essential supply, a reachable command-liberation operation and restoration of recorded losses |

For competing reservations over the same people, stock or target, resolve the greater willingness first, then the earlier accepted active-mission deadline, then the older unserved valid operation request, then stable operation identity. This reservation arbitration is deterministic and does not claim to replace the native decision scheduler's willingness semantics. Equal valid unreserved decisions retain equal willingness. The native auditor must report the scheduler's actual ordering and unsupported external behavior separately.

Post-opening dilemmas are state-driven reports with explicit eligible situations. If several become eligible at once, urgent sovereignty, body and terminal requests take priority over narrative flavor. Do not build a daily, weekly or monthly scan over every state or country.
