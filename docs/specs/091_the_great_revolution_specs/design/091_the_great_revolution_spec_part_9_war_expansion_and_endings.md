# 09. War, expansion, Red World, and defeat

## The central war

World Revolution fights every noncommunist ordinary country from the opening and every communist government that refuses. Countries with no selected states are still enemies. The war establishes Lenin's worldwide claim while preserving the strategic consequences of prior wars, alliances, occupations, and supply arrangements.

The central war cannot be dismissed by an ordinary random Peace event. The Peace owner must recognize the 091 war contract and exclude generic white peace against the active supernatural regime. Local settlements remain possible when explicitly defined, such as a humanitarian corridor, a rebel council's withdrawal, a prisoner transfer, or a restored border after the network has been defeated. These agreements do not silently end the central war for the entire world.

The event does not create one mandatory anti-Lenin faction. Existing factions can coordinate through intelligence sharing, access, deliveries, guarantees, and bounded joint operations. A communist refusal coalition can remain politically distinct while cooperating with noncommunist defenders. An existing war between two intended Lenin allies must be reconciled through a valid diplomatic transition before incompatible faction or war effects commit. The implementation must investigate the exact native conflict, not discard those wars or grant a fictional alliance in a tooltip.

## Expansion after the single opening

Later revolutionary growth comes from targeted 001 cells, propaganda, party infiltration, local councils, actual military victories, forced government change under valid war outcomes, accepted allied access, captured industry, and functioning regional commands. Each target operation names the state, country, or region it affects. A higher Momentum stage or a new focus does not repeat the full worldwide popularity allocation.

| Expansion method | Preparation | Payoff | Failure and counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Develop an 001 cell | Existing organizing access, time, local conditions, actual support | Raise a real local state level or prepare an uprising | Containment reduces or removes that cell through 001's owner |
| Support a local council | Protect local institutions and deliver useful resources | Stable administration, recruitment, and political access | Broken protection or coercion can move the council toward human refusal |
| Coordinate an uprising | Several prepared cells, a regional command or equivalent operation, finite sources | A bounded revolt that opens a strategically useful front | Defenders disrupt preparation or defeat the actual rebel force |
| Military conquest | Supplied formations, operational planning, control of key routes | Real territory and captured assets | Occupation, garrisons, resistance, and reconstruction remain necessary |
| Establish a subject | Valid conquered or voluntarily bound government and actual diplomatic authority | Subordinate administration and qualifying ruled territory | Sovereignty resistance, outside support, independence, and lost control |
| Institutional absorption | Ordinary supporter reaches complete binding and survives its explicit resistance window | Real annexation of country assets and governance | Independence before commit cancels the transaction and creates war |

## Occupation and councils

Directly occupied territory retains resistance, damage, supply needs, civilian systems, and legitimate claims. Lenin can restore a human council, build a mixed administration, impose transformed institutions, or establish a subordinate communist government. Each choice has a different production, manpower, legitimacy, and military consequence.

A human council can stabilize an industrial region and provide reliable repair while resisting later coerced conversion. A mixed administration can coordinate transformed labor and human officials but needs a genuine settlement over authority. A central conversion institution can provide strong supernatural output and recruits at the cost of resistance and the risk of actual harm. A subject government reduces direct administrative burden but creates a country with a real independence route.

No route grants free stable cores across the world. Integrating a major region requires actual occupation, collaboration, institutions, reconstruction, or transformation under the appropriate contract. A national focus can unlock that work or fund a significant part of it. It cannot turn every claimed state into a fully compliant core as an incidental reward.

## Six regional campaigns

Every command requires a valid regional base, a functioning institution, and a completed objective that demonstrates its logistical and political purpose. The focus chapter develops the command lanes in detail. These are different campaigns, not six identical percentage bonuses.

| Region | Strategic identity | First functioning-command proof | Continuing operation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Europe | Dense industrial corridors, symbolic revolutionary centers, strong organized opposition | Connect an industrial base to a viable rail and supply spine while securing its council or command | Coordinated factory and rail operations, heavy assault preparation, institutional competition |
| Asia | Long distances, population centers, mixed infrastructure, and large regional armies | Sustain a long regional corridor between a base and a meaningful operational population center | Distributed musters, protected transport, regional cooperation and force standardization |
| Africa | Colonial organizing, resource and port links, uneven infrastructure, local political legitimacy | Establish a locally supported inland-to-port or equivalent viable supply arrangement | Support councils and anti-colonial cells, protect extraction and civilian supply, avoid an imported generic European government |
| North America | Strong industry, large distances, major transport and production networks | Secure a durable industrial base and actual access to its distribution network | Disrupt or capture high-value production and logistics, assemble mechanized strength against a powerful defender |
| South America | Terrain barriers, coastal links, rail chokepoints, and regional political networks | Sustain a mountain, inland, or coastal connection appropriate to the actual base | Regional cavalry or infiltration operations, cross-border organizing, targeted supply protection |
| Oceania | Maritime separation, islands, ports, convoy vulnerability, and limited local mass | Operate a real protected port and transport route between viable bases or a base and allied support | Maritime uprisings, convoy defense, port repair, evacuation, and selective amphibious support |

At most one registered survival anchor belongs to each regional-command slot. A command can use a transformed leader or site as its custodian, but that does not add a new anchor for every ruler or every Level 3 state. A lost region has a real strategic consequence. Its identity cannot be recreated endlessly in another location to avoid defeat or farm Chaos.

## The inhabited-world rule measure

At the opening, freeze a baseline population weight for every valid inhabited world state. Sum those weights once to form the denominator. Include inhabited territory outside the ordinary opening pool, including territory held by other special actors, so excluding a difficult enemy does not make world rule easier.

At later checks, each state contributes its fixed weight once if its actual current controller is the World Revolution, an actual Lenin subject, or a fully bound government whose completed sovereignty transaction proves Lenin's rule. Legal ownership without current control gives no credit. Independent voluntary allies, Trotsky's autonomous country, mere faction membership, and a numeric Influence value without a completed binding transaction give no credit.

This is a population-weighted territorial measure based on the opening world. It is not a live census of surviving people. Death, migration, depopulation, and conversion cannot reduce the denominator to manufacture a majority. The technical owner defines the exact current-control updates and verifies that split ownership and overlapping pools cannot double count a state.

## Red World readiness

The Red World becomes ready when all four user-defined conditions hold: Chaos is strictly above 1000, Lenin and the actual World Revolution country still exist, strictly more than half of the valid inhabited-world measure is under Lenin's rule, and no other world-end scenario is active. Shared event and scenario settings also remain authoritative.

At exactly 50 percent rule or exactly 1000 Chaos, it is not ready. The 50 percent Chaos milestone can legitimately push Chaos above 1000, but cannot substitute for strictly more than half of the world. The capstone focus and decision presentation explain readiness and provide the event's terminal commitment. They do not add an undisclosed requirement to own every continent or complete an arbitrary technology list.

The final check occurs at the commitment boundary, after any intervening occupation, independence, leader change, or other terminal outcome. If it fails, no terminal flag, special cue, forced binding, or irreversible world shutdown occurs. The next valid opportunity remains visible according to the shared selection and focus contract.

## Terminal transition

Red World registers one dedicated world-end scenario and sets the shared world-end state. Ordinary random event firing stops. Existing wars, active 091 operations, supported cross-event lifecycles, and the final resistance continue under their owners. Terminal history is not rewritten by a later popup or a country changing its name.

Lenin receives the strongest supernatural coordination and Momentum enters its terminal stage. This is an explicit terminal-state override, not a claim that the pre-terminal contribution formula always produced 100. Finite reinforcement budgets remain finite. New powers strengthen existing regional and military systems without recreating the opening or granting unlimited bodies and equipment.

Every unannexed ordinary supporter receives a final explicit choice. Remaining with Lenin accepts real puppeting and later annexation and provides the strongest available military support for the final war. Independence leaves the faction, terminates Influence, cancels annexation, and begins war while preserving past consequences. Silence follows the non-consenting path. A supporter does not become willing because a popup timed out.

Terminal pressure accelerates integration through the final explicit choice. An ordinary supporter that chooses to remain accepts an immediate real subject transition and then a 90-day annex window. This explicit submission can resolve its unfinished critical struggle, but it does not waive the remaining independence opportunity. An existing subject resumes with the smaller of its previously remaining annex interval and 90 days after explicitly choosing to remain. A longer interval is shortened only through that explained choice. No retroactive expiry occurs on the activation day.

The final native confirmation follows the project's default-cancel and 13-day timeout rules. Snapshot and pause any annex deadline during this one final response window so an old timeout cannot absorb a country before its final choice. Explicit refusal or the non-consenting timeout executes independence and war. Explicit acceptance resumes the recorded remaining interval under the rule above. This one-time response pause cannot be renewed by reopening a menu or changing leaders. Longer integration missions use stored due dates and do not rely on the popup remaining open.

## Trotsky's terminal choice

Trotsky remains outside the ordinary Influence, puppeting, and annexation sequence. His final route offers continued autonomous partnership under a negotiated division between Lenin's supernatural center and the human international, or a separate political break and war against the terminal order.

No voluntary-surrender option is added by this package. It is unnecessary to fulfill the requested autonomous partnership and risks undermining its central distinction. Continued partnership does not add Trotsky's territory to Lenin's rule numerator. A terminal settlement that retains this autonomous partner must describe the surviving exception honestly. It is different from complete absorption of all governments into one state.

If the partnership fails, the cause must be an actual political choice over the final order, forced conversion, or the fate of independent councils. Normal military conflict then follows its own rules. High Momentum, a changed portrait, a leader replacement, or a generic world-end adapter cannot silently force that failure.

## Final outcomes

Full absorption is reached when the remaining qualifying ordinary governments have been annexed or reduced to genuine subordinate administrations and the remaining inhabited opposition has been resolved under the terminal campaign. Its report describes one supernatural revolutionary order and the actual surviving populations. It does not execute an automatic worldwide kill or fabricate complete nonhuman replacement.

A continued autonomous Trotsky settlement is a distinct political ending. The revolution may dominate the world strategically while its protected human international retains sovereignty. The game should report that arrangement rather than falsely call it complete absorption.

Final resistance can defeat the active World Revolution even after terminal entry if the full body and network defeat conditions are met. Such a counter-victory closes the 091 threat and opens its aftermath. The shared terminal history and ordinary-event shutdown remain under the terminal framework. Defeating Lenin does not silently reboot the normal random-event world after a declared world end.

## Finite survival

The network consists of the origin anchor and at most six regional anchors. Distributed survival becomes available at Momentum 60. It fails below 40 after a declared severance interval when its actual support has been removed. This specific survival hysteresis is separate from the ordinary public power bands.

Capturing one disconnected capital is not full victory while valid supernatural commands remain. Defenders must capture or destroy the central body, neutralize the origin, dismantle major transformation sites and command links, liberate territory, support real breakaways, and reduce Momentum. The exact sufficient set is the finite anchor inventory and continuity contract, not an unbounded search for every portrait with an eye.

With the body and origin secured, 90 continuous days below 40 Momentum can prove the remaining registered network severed when no qualifying anchor maintains its active connection. Permanently destroyed anchors remain destroyed. Temporarily disrupted ones can be restored only through their same identity and a real operation before final severance. A mission identifies the concrete surviving sites or links that still prevent victory.

One campaign-wide body reconstitution is permitted from a pre-existing valid surviving anchor after 180 days of actual preparation and a finite cohort commitment. It consumes its one reconstruction receipt and a real source under the military and population contracts. It cannot use Trotsky or an independent ally's territory automatically. It cannot occur from a newly invented anchor after capture. If the native country has no territory, restoration of sovereignty is a separate contested and verified operation, not an assumption that a stateless country is still alive.

## Defeat and aftermath

Full defeat closes every active supernatural command and survival receipt, stops new manifestations, cancels pending Influence and annexation, resolves subject independence through actual diplomacy, and clears only 091's world-threat source. Human remnants and ordinary communism remain. Dead people remain dead. Living transformed people remain eligible for the appropriate recovery or political treatment.

| Aftermath route | Continuing objective | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liberation and restoration | Return actual states and institutions under legitimate claims and settlements | Rebuilt governments and a documented restoration outcome, with no duplicate state grants |
| Reconstruction | Repair factories, railways, ports, and civilian supply over 180 to 360 days | Productive regions and reduced hardship through real repair and shared relief systems |
| Trials or amnesty | Identify responsible officials and distinguish coercion from collaboration | Political accountability, reconciliation, or renewed unrest tied to actual choices |
| Treatment and recovery | Support qualified cure or suppression programs for surviving transformed people | Bounded recovery, persistent hard cases, no instant national cure |
| Human communist international | Protect surviving independent communist governments and councils | A political alternative that can coexist with noncommunist victors |
| Body and relic custody | Destroy, imprison, research, transfer, or conceal the captured object through a single custody owner | Bounded later incidents and research, never a second 091 Major firing |

The earned defeat super-event celebrates the dismantling of the extraordinary system and acknowledges the scale of repair. It is a major aftermath package because the crisis was global. It is not a generic capital-capture notification or an excuse to erase every trace of the movement.
