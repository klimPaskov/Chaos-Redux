# 04. Country identity, political roles, and playable campaign routes

This chapter defines the accepted planning direction for Event 091, The Great Revolution.
The current brief fixes Lenin, the World Revolution, the connected Event 001 crisis, the simultaneous opening, the Stalin and Trotsky rules, unrestricted sovereignty resistance, and The Red World.
Detailed institutions, route labels, force roles, timers, and reward packages below are proposed design within that brief.
Every name other than the user's fixed names is a working label, not final localisation.
The design is accepted for implementation planning. Its numeric tuning and native behavior still require the specific verification gates in the technical handoffs.

## A playable country assembled from a real revolutionary network

The World Revolution begins with a complete fighting country whose immediate problem is maintaining scattered bases during a worldwide war.
Its initial superiority comes from preparedness, defecting equipment, transformed workers, and coordinated insurgency.
Its weaknesses come from isolated commands, incompatible captured equipment, exposed transport, contested civilian administration, and the price of transforming people.
A player who switches to Lenin should receive useful forces, a capital, production, stockpiles, and action access immediately.
The opening never waits for a focus before creating the sovereign country, wars, initial formations, public Momentum, or communist alignment choices.

| Country surface | Proposed initial treatment | Later play and counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity and tag | One central World Revolution country after the full installed-tag collision audit. Preserve the user's country name and use a deep red and black map color | Political routes change the flag, party presentation, institutions, and commander roster without manufacturing several unrelated Lenin countries |
| Population classification | Special Chaos actor immediately, with ordinary civilians, human defectors, and nonhuman workers living under one state | Actual nonhuman classification requires the later institution transformation and proof that ordinary civilian society has been replaced. Momentum alone does not prove this |
| Capital | Select the sovereign origin through the connected Event 001 priority chain and then a valid controlled city in that state | Loss or isolation starts a command evacuation action with a valid surviving city. Moving the administrative capital does not recreate or move the finite origin anchor |
| Political leadership | Lenin leads a communist revolutionary government with a third-eye identity that preserves the real person's likeness | Collective, personal-apotheosis, and distributed-party institutions produce different command relationships and diplomatic consequences |
| Research | Three starting research slots, with technologies inherited coherently from the origin and proven equipment donors | A fourth and fifth slot are earned through the protected exchange and the late foundry network defined in Part 6 |
| Economy | Inherit the actual transferred industrial and resource map, relevant captured production, and documented defecting stocks | Repair, standardization, regional foundries, allied contracts, salvage, and transport work build a durable economy |
| Laws | Emergency revolutionary mobilization and trade rules suited to the actual manpower, factory, resource, and occupation position | Collective institutions can preserve civilian autonomy. Apotheosis can impose compulsory conversion. Distributed institutions can delegate local production while becoming vulnerable to anchor disruption |
| Land army | Preserve existing Event 001 forces and add dynamically funded transformed and defecting formations | Recruitment draws from local populations, converted formations, captured pools, available equipment, or finite supernatural milestones |
| Navy and air | Transfer or create forces only when ports, airfields, defectors, fuel, and equipment substantiate them | Maritime and aviation decision families become available when their operational prerequisites exist, with meaningful transport and reconnaissance roles before large fleets or air forces |
| Diplomacy | Dedicated revolutionary alignment with ordinary communist governments and valid revolutionary republics | Members have defined sovereignty terms, aid obligations, war participation, refusal, departure, and absorption rules |
| Playable access | Offer the origin player an optional switch after the country exists and its setup is complete | Only the origin player receives the direct multiplayer switch offer under the user's rule. Other players retain their countries and alignment decisions |

Starting technology inheritance must follow a coherent donor package rather than taking the best technology in every field from every country affected by the opening.
Captured tanks and aircraft can exist as stocks before the World Revolution can reproduce them.
Production licenses, donor workshops, captured technical personnel, and foundry standardization explain later domestic manufacture.
The exact technology list, laws, equipment tokens, designer compatibility, and vanilla ship or airwing transfer precedents remain implementation verification work.

The World Revolution uses no more than three simultaneous focus-created national spirits.
The proposed three lifecycle families are Scattered Commands, Captured Industrial Network, and Revolutionary Government.
These working institution families consolidate logistical weakness, industrial conversion, and political identity.
Their effects should be substantial enough to change early priorities, such as a 20 percent logistical or production penalty on the affected activity, and their mitigations should require actual actions.
The implementation must choose native modifiers that express those constraints after reference inspection.
A failed region can worsen the existing command institution rather than adding another spirit.
Political route forms replace Revolutionary Government rather than adding a fourth badge.
Short-lived local mission modifiers may exist without becoming a permanent loophole around the three-spirit ceiling.

## First choices that establish the country

The first ordinary focus lasts 35 days and chooses a real opening method.
A player can instead begin the immediately available base-link, repair, invitation, or conversion actions while choosing a focus.
The opening fork gives two compatible strategic answers and then exposes the three mutually exclusive political philosophies.

| Opening commitment | Immediate action | Opportunity cost | Why the choice remains useful |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Connect the nearest strongholds | Prioritize one feasible land, port, or allied transit corridor and unlock a supplied-command mission | Other distant cells receive fewer initial deliveries | Consolidated fronts retain captured industry and create a reliable base for later offensives |
| Keep the world signal active | Protect the most endangered remote bases through local reinforcement and synchronized diversions | Main-front consolidation is slower and more transport is committed | Surviving cells create later uprisings, access, and regional commands instead of disappearing during the first month |

These opening commitments do not silently remove other continents, country choices, or unit families.
They choose the first supported operations and the early deployment of finite resources.
After that choice, support branches remain available and the country can rebalance through meaningful actions.

## Three mutually exclusive political philosophies

The three philosophies are chosen through early 35-day commitments after the opening fork.
Their identities must affect institutional decisions, ally behavior, recruitment, production, expansion, and defeat counterplay.
The country remains recognizably the World Revolution on every route.

| Philosophy, working label | Governing principle | Main institutions | Military and economic distinction | Diplomatic distinction | Distinct vulnerability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The First Among Workers | Councils and human communist institutions participate in the supernatural movement | Council congress, local worker administration, negotiated military liaison | Slower compulsory conversion, stronger loyal local reserves, donor standardization, protected civilian production | Autonomous aid compacts, more attractive invitations, less institutional exposure when partners decline binding access | Councils can withhold contributions after coercion, failed protection, or attempts to absorb a useful ally |
| The First Coming | Lenin claims personal supernatural authority and directs state transformation | Central conversion institutions, personal commissariat, sacred industrial command | Faster conversion, concentrated Hammer Guard and Foundry commitments, powerful timed output surges | Strong aid tied to lasting institutional access, binding directives for eligible ordinary supporters, direct absorption | Dependence on high Momentum, stronger human communist opposition, exposed transformation sites, severe civilian resistance |
| The Eye in Every Party | Lenin's presence operates through selected transformed institutions | Regional party nodes, linked commissars, distributed command | Better coordinated multi-region actions, dispersed production, resilience to loss of the central body | Influence through accepted party and command access, shared target planning | Finite visible anchors and transformed institutions create several military targets. Destroying them disrupts the network |

The collective philosophy can still pursue The Red World because it can eventually choose to extinguish sovereignty in the terminal order.
That commitment must be visible as a later political rupture.
Before that commitment, voluntary councils and genuinely independent allies do not count as territory under Lenin's rule.
The collective player therefore faces a real choice between maintaining autonomous partners and acquiring enough actual ruled territory for the terminal gate.
The final collective route cannot satisfy the gate by relabeling alliance membership as rule.

Apotheosis is baseline progression tied to Momentum.
The First Coming political route changes how Lenin uses that power and how rapidly it develops institutional consequences.
It does not turn apotheosis into a new evolution or grant terminal powers during the opening.
The distributed route can unlock finite survival infrastructure at Momentum 60. Below Momentum 40, enemy custody of the body and origin can start the ninety-day severance objective, but every remaining anchor must still be destroyed or individually proven severed.
Other powers use the actual public 20, 40, 60, and 80 bands without general hysteresis.
Recovering a band restores eligible effects but never repeats finite manifestation, equipment, seed, or Chaos rewards.

## Human and nonhuman institutions

Institution choices govern actual behavior without adding a third public event meter.
Momentum remains the public global value.
An ordinary joined country actively manages only its own Lenin's Influence in addition to the global Momentum summary.
Local populations, conversion records, council allegiance, supply conditions, institutional access, and anchor state remain internal or qualitative.

| Institution choice | Public action and resource commitment | Immediate benefit | Lasting consequence | Defender response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Preserve a human council | Protect a named industrial district and spend support equipment and construction time on local administration | Reliable repair, local supply, human recruitment, and a legitimate invitation precedent | Council representatives can object to coercion and demand protection during regional failures | Refusers can negotiate evacuation, political protection, or peaceful disengagement when the council has resisted transformation |
| Accept a mixed council | Commit both human cadres and transformed workers to one region with retained civilian institutions | Strong labor and military coordination with a meaningful conversion limit | Influence rises in an allied host only when it accepts the actual liaison access. Conversion remains population-backed | Liberation can restore the civilian administration while separating commissars and transformed formations |
| Establish a conversion institution | Commit a named factory or hospital district, equipment, manpower or documented population, and a 70-day institution-building focus, followed by the separately funded conversion operation | Awakened recruitment and continuous industrial shifts | Failed conversion, coercion, and destruction can create real deaths and occupation resistance through their owners | Destroy or capture the named site, interrupt transport, evacuate eligible people, and lower Momentum through the actual loss |
| Open a third-eye command | Station an eligible transformed command family at a supply-connected site and dedicate officer training | Strong coordination, regional operation access, and potentially one finite anchor | Creates a clear enemy objective and makes allied command dependence real | Isolate the command, capture its node, remove the local commissars, and break the corresponding anchor |
| Restore civilian independence | Remove a conversion or party-control institution and hold the protected district during recovery | Reduces ordinary supporter Influence and provides a human reconstruction route | Costs production time, support equipment, military cover, and potentially the strongest aid | Lenin can pressure the route through ordinary war and permitted directives, while the independence action remains available |

No human-preservation action generates an unlimited free manpower pool.
No nonhuman institution creates deaths solely because people have changed species.
Doctor Wu or scientific assistance modifies recovery or protection actions through separately owned interfaces and never supplies a button that deletes the entire revolution.
Political institutions should receive sourced real figures or authentic people-free institutional presentation when appropriate.
Wholly fictional high-chaos command characters may be proposed, with original portrait production governed by the portrait workflow.

## The eight army families as a coherent war economy

These roles complement the parent army and equipment chapter.
They define route use and action connections without replacing its manpower, equipment, model, sound, counter, or reinforcement contracts.
Every family receives real battlefield identity and a production or recruitment pathway.

| Family | Recruitment and inheritance | Strength and limit | Political route interaction | Action and command use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awakened Workers | Transform eligible local volunteers, convert an existing formation, or use a finite registered supernatural milestone | Durable line holding and cohesion, with real equipment, manpower, supply, and reinforcement demand | Collective route prefers controlled consent. Personal route accelerates compulsory conversion. Distributed route attaches local command links | Hold the base, protect a foundry, secure a corridor, and sustain the continental fronts |
| Hammer Guard | Level 3 preparations, heavy tool and armor stocks, and a trained assault conversion | Strong urban and fort assault with slower movement and heavy supply demand | Personal authority can concentrate a short offensive window. Councils can demand local protection before committing them | Breach an industrial city, destroy a defended anchor, or secure a command capstone |
| Sickle Host | Specialized volunteers or converted assault cadres with blade, support, and infiltration equipment | Night and rough-ground penetration with weaker prolonged open combat | Distributed route coordinates several bounded incursions. Collective route uses them to protect independent local cells | Sabotage one rail objective, open a corridor, disrupt a depot, and exploit encirclement support |
| Red Motor Columns | Documented captured or produced trucks, tractors, fuel, and trained drivers | Rapid reinforcement and transport over supported routes, vulnerable to fuel and road loss | Foundry and donor choices determine equipment standardization | Deliver aid, relieve an enclave, exploit a linked front, and supply mobile command reserves |
| Revolutionary Cavalry | Real available horse stocks and regionally recruited riders, including human and transformed variants | Useful where roads and fuel are weak, with limitations against modern concentrated fire | Collective councils retain local traditions. Personal conversion changes riders without creating horses from nothing | Patrol a long corridor, support interior defense, and operate across suitable terrain |
| Foundry Brigades | Captured armor or mechanized stocks followed by a working heavy-industry program | Heavy breakthrough and protection with expensive replacement, fuel, and repair needs | Personal route favors concentrated shock. Distributed route can place repair nodes across regions | Capture an industrial center, guard a conversion program, or support a regional offensive capstone |
| Third-Eye Commissars | Trained support cadres and accepted institutional access | Strong cohesion and coordination with political dependence and exposed personnel | Ordinary allies accept or remove them through Influence actions. Trotsky can accept a strictly voluntary joint liaison without any binding access | Establish a command, coordinate an uprising, or contest sovereignty institutions |
| Red Engineers | Documented specialist manpower, support tools, trains, repair stocks, and construction commitments | Supply repair, siege support, and prepared defense, with lower value when isolated or poorly equipped | Collective route preserves local repair boards. Personal route drives dangerous surge programs. Distributed route links workshops | Repair rails, maintain ports, fortify an enclave, restore a liberated foundry, and sustain regional command operations |

Regional unit names should follow recruitment origin and local military or industrial tradition.
The World Revolution's shared symbols can coexist with names associated with a mine, port, factory, rail district, agricultural community, or local council.
The same generic Russian list cannot name every continent's formations.
Regional names are research directions until linguistic and cultural review confirms them.

Conditional naval development begins with convoy security, small port detachments, dockyard repair, and inherited ships.
A coastal command can progress to real fleet coordination only after it has fuel, repair capacity, a deployment port, and documented vessels.
Air development begins with captured airfield protection, inherited pilot and aircraft packages, and sustainable reconnaissance or support.
Neither branch requires new fictional ship or aircraft archetypes solely to fill space.
If later custom air or naval identities become part of the accepted army plan, they need full unit, model, sound, production, and AI coverage.

## Six commands with different campaigns

Commands are created from a valid supplied revolutionary base or locally administered allied base in the corresponding region.
They are operational institutions with named objectives and follow-up actions.
They never grant ownership of an entire continent.
Every command can coordinate local names and stocks, but its first campaign is different.
At most one distributed-survival anchor can attach to each command slot.
The finite origin plus six command slots prevents an unlimited hidden survival network.

| Command, working label | Starting problem | First operation | Development choice | Capstone and continuing use | Failure and opposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Europe | Dense fronts, defended industrial corridors, rival communist leadership, and severe strategic exposure | Connect two useful factory or rail districts without abandoning the origin | Urban foundry warfare or coalition command with human communist partners | Protected industrial corridor supporting repeated repair, standardized equipment, and a justified confrontation with Stalin when he exists | Losing the linked corridor breaks delivery and foundry benefits. Refusers can defend institutions and isolate a command rather than needing to defeat every European state |
| Asia | Long distances, uneven industry, occupied territories, and political movements with local ambitions | Secure a supply spine joining a populated recruiting region to a rail hub, river crossing, or port | Interior cavalry and local councils or port and industrial expansion | Regional recruitment and depot routes that sustain inland armies and protect local autonomy where the route promises it | Overextended lines and coerced councils can produce transport failure and breakaways. The command does not claim an entire Asian identity |
| Africa | Sparse transport, colonial control, vulnerable ports, and different local liberation claims | Protect a port-to-interior corridor or a feasible interior command when maritime access is absent | Coastal delivery network or negotiated interior council federation | Local supply and liberation settlement actions connecting human recruitment, resources, and resistance to colonial exploitation | Losing the route isolates donors. Occupation pressure and rejected local terms can deprive Lenin of councils without creating a generic continent-wide buff |
| North America | Large industrial prizes, strong potential defenders, and long transcontinental transport | Maintain one industrial uprising while securing its transport or evacuation route | Deep industrial uprising with selective conversion or a viable maritime bridge | Heavy equipment and research contributions from actually secured industry, plus repeated coastal or railway operations | A failed industrial seizure gives no tank windfall and can cost valuable cadres. Defenders gain real foundry, rail, and port objectives |
| South America | Mountain barriers, inland resource districts, coast-dependent exports, and fragmented foreign relations | Link an interior resource or recruiting district to a working port or defensible local hub | Mountain and cavalry council network or port and foundry contracts | Resource, regional mobility, and aid routes with named infrastructure targets | Losing mountain passes or ports breaks the route. Local communist governments can support the revolution while refusing absorption |
| Oceania | Separated islands, small populations, maritime exposure, and limited heavy industry | Keep one island base supplied and secure a staging port or airfield | Convoy and repair specialization or air reconnaissance and island protection | Sea-lane relay, aircraft recovery, evacuation, and small sustainable landing operations | Naval isolation stops expansion and consumes convoys. The command cannot manufacture a huge fleet or workforce from a small island |

All six commands have independent existence and operational usefulness when their region is available.
A normal victory, defeat route, or Red World activation does not require all six commands.
The Workers of Every World achievement requires a functioning command on every inhabited continent.
The focus tree can open a command's planning lane before the region is secured, but its operating effects require the real base and objectives.

## Defenders preserve their own wars and countries

The noncommunist defender role begins immediately at war with Lenin.
It has a distinct playable sequence that works for a major or a small country without imposing one new global faction.
Existing focus trees remain meaningful.
Additive crisis content and the shared response category provide emergency tools, while optional country-specific branches connect them to existing politics and institutions.

| Phase | Player priority | Actions and sacrifices | Successful result | Failure that creates more play |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening shock | Protect the capital and a useful supply line | Redeploy supplied divisions, commit repair equipment, evacuate an exposed district, and select the nearest feasible containment target | Government survives and one frontier becomes defensible | A lost district can create an evacuation or relocation mission instead of an automatic generic debuff |
| Local containment | Isolate revolutionary bases and destroy transformation institutions | Hold named rail hubs, ports, or industrial centers while keeping enough forces on existing wars | Momentum falls from actual liberated territory and lost commands | A failed containment mission strengthens the existing local enemy and opens a harder recovery objective |
| Coalition operations | Share intelligence, equipment, corridors, or bounded joint plans | Pay actual deliveries and agree precise operational objectives with real partners | Joint operations become feasible without rewriting unrelated faction commitments | Broken access or a defeated partner cancels only the affected operation and returns unspent commitments where applicable |
| Network defeat | Break the finite origin and command anchors | Capture a named anchor, protect liberated councils, and disrupt transformed institutions | Distributed survival loses the actual source supporting it | Capturing the body early can open guarded captivity while remaining commands continue fighting |
| Restoration | Restore government and civilian institutions without deleting ordinary communist politics | Reconstruction, public accountability, conversion recovery, and collaborator choices | A durable postwar settlement proportional to the crisis | Purges, relic misuse, or abandoned reconstruction can produce bounded aftermath incidents owned by the proper systems |

A defender that loses every eligible state follows normal valid exile conditions.
If exile is unavailable, its collapse is real.
The planning package must still make the opening survivable in plausible low and medium uprising cases, with dynamically funded forces and meaningful selection counterplay.
It must not grant immunity merely because a human player declined the Lenin switch.

## Communist refusal is an independent political role

Refusers defend human communist institutions while fighting the supernatural movement.
Their actions include protecting party leadership, evacuating councils, exposing third-eye access, helping supporters regain sovereignty, and competing for undecided communist governments.
They can cooperate with ideological enemies through bounded operations or emergency compacts without abandoning communism.
A new human communist international needs real members, mutual commitments, and an agreed purpose.
It cannot replace the Soviet, Allied, Axis, or other existing factions by default.

Stalin-led Soviet refusal is fixed.
Its additive confrontation content prioritizes the Soviet capital region, Leningrad when valid, rail-connected defense, counter-infiltration, support for anti-Lenin communist governments, and the fate of Lenin's body.
If Stalin is absent, the current Soviet leader follows the ordinary applicable alignment rules. Refusal content remains available when that government actually refuses, while current Trotsky receives the mandatory autonomous partnership.
The Stalin-specific achievement remains tied to the real character identity and required capital retention.
Stalin outside the Soviet Union receives a strong proposed refusal preference subject to actual character ownership.

Other refusers can specialize in external rescue or domestic defense.
Small states can secure one indispensable institution and participate through deliveries or political refuge rather than paying a major's costs.
Refusal is dangerous because selected states transfer and global war begins.
It must nevertheless offer a complete long-term path instead of acting as a punishment for declining the strongest option.

## Ordinary communist supporters

An ordinary supporter keeps its identity and selected states under local administration, apart from Lenin's sovereign origin.
Its immediate aid scales to what the country lacks and can sustain.
Military aid can save an exposed front, industrial conversion can restore production, and intelligence liaison can reveal a priority enemy.
Each large benefit asks for a real choice about institutional access.

The Influence stages, concessions, resistance actions, the 180-day critical mission, binding and the 120-day annexation window are specified once, in [Part 5](091_the_great_revolution_spec_part_5_influence_momentum_and_powers.md) and the sovereignty actions of [Part 8](091_the_great_revolution_spec_part_8_decisions_missions_and_ai.md).
Two commitments shape this role and are not repeated elsewhere in this chapter.
Every supporter and subject can declare independence at any stage with no political-power cost, no Influence limit and no prerequisite mission, and an unprepared country can still choose it.
Compromised institutions, transformed units and economic dependence are consequences of earlier choices and never become permission checks on that declaration.

## Trotsky as autonomous cofounder

An active Trotsky-led Soviet Union joins Lenin and remains autonomous.
A valid Trotsky-led country elsewhere can receive the same partner package when its actual character ownership and political context support it.
This partner has no Influence meter, no automatic control pressure, no binding directive adapter, no forced puppet path, and no substitute absorption system.
A voluntary joint command is an agreement between governments rather than a concealed annexation counter.

| Trotsky route group, working label | Choice | Continuing gameplay | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shared international command | Commit a real command contribution on one continent and agree one bounded operation | Repeat coordinated uprisings, deliveries, and regional operations when the agreement remains valid | Lenin receives military help, while Trotsky retains appointments, laws, territory, and diplomacy |
| Human revolutionary institutions | Protect a council or worker population and refuse compulsory conversion there | Rescue or support ordinary allies, improve independence preparation, and offer human replacement cadres | Personal-apotheosis Lenin can object politically, but the disagreement does not activate Influence |
| Permanent international campaign | Complete two actual operations on two inhabited continents and support a major ruled-world milestone | Expand the human international, share research, and create regional cooperation | The Permanent Revolution achievement uses the 50 percent inhabited-rule milestone and those operations, with no Red World requirement |
| Break the partnership | Freely leave the agreement and declare war through a distinct political choice | Join communist refusers or lead an independent coalition | Existing military and economic commitments resolve under their receipts, with no hidden forced absorption |
| Red World decision | Preserve a separately agreed autonomous relationship or reject the final order through a political break | The final partner choice follows Trotsky's own route | Automatic supporter puppetization and annexation never apply. Independent Trotsky territory gives no ruled-world credit |

The terminal Trotsky choice offers continued autonomous concord with explicit limits or a political break and war.
The protected route has no surrender option and cannot enter the ordinary Influence or absorption pipeline.
A leader change, timeout or high Momentum cannot revoke the accepted crisis-specific protection.

## Terminal transformation and defeat

The Red World gate, the frozen inhabited-world measure, the final supporter choice and the finite-network defeat contract are specified once, in [Part 9](091_the_great_revolution_spec_part_9_war_expansion_and_endings.md).
For the roles in this chapter the consequences are these.
The terminal order strengthens earned supernatural powers and accelerates remaining integration, while each unannexed supporter keeps a final sovereign choice and silence is never acceptance.
Independent allies, aid recipients, critical Influence without a completed binding transaction and autonomous Trotsky give no ruled-world credit.
At low Momentum, destruction of the body and sovereign country can end the crisis when no valid survival capability remains.
At high Momentum, the surviving origin and formed command anchors must be removed or deprived of the conditions that sustain them.
An early body capture creates a captivity branch and meaningful prisoner choices while active commands remain.
Defeat clears only this event's world-threat source and wartime institutions, preserving earned history, normal communist politics, real liberation and other events' threats.

Aftermath plays differently for each role.
Defenders reconstruct and negotiate restoration.
Refusers rebuild an independent communist international and dispute collective punishment.
Former supporters remove compromised institutions and recover transformed people.
An autonomous Trotsky country survives as a politically accountable partner or opponent.
A fully transformed World Revolution route can be eliminated without automatically killing or erasing every transformed person.
Captured Lenin research or custody belongs to bounded aftermath and does not create a second ordinary major-event firing.
