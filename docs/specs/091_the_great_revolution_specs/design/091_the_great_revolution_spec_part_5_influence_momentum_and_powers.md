# 05. Influence, Momentum, and the Third Eye

## Two values with different purposes

Revolutionary Momentum measures the World Revolution's current strategic power. Lenin's Influence measures the institutional dependence of an ordinary joined communist country. They answer different player questions. Momentum explains what the movement can do next. Influence explains how much independent control a joining government still retains.

They are the only persistent public event values. Regional preparation, transaction receipts, force sources, political history, and survival-anchor status remain internal. Native map facts, objective lists, costs, and deadlines communicate the practical choices without turning each subsystem into another currency.

## Why an ordinary country joins

Joining gives immediate and continuing benefits scaled to need, contribution, actual resources, and the relationship chosen. A desperate government should receive useful aid for its current war. A strong government should gain meaningful cooperation without being handed an identical emergency package it does not need.

| Benefit | Delivery and usefulness | Political relationship |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equipment support | Real infantry, support, transport, or captured equipment appropriate to the recipient's shortages | Larger or repeated emergency reliance can invite more institutional access |
| Custom volunteers or expeditionary support | A finite actual force with valid native ownership and return behavior | Commissars and altered formations have disclosed Influence consequences |
| Coordinated offensives | Strong organization and attack support for a named operation | Requires joint planning and a real objective, not a permanent universal combat multiplier |
| Mobilization and recruitment | Access to willing recruits, cadres, and bounded regional muster | Preserving human command is slower but protects institutions |
| Industry and supply | Repair, production cooperation, emergency stock movement, rail or port support | Receiving an adviser is a political choice, not a hidden side effect of every trade |
| Intelligence | Third-Eye observation and cell information for specific threats | Continued access to domestic institutions creates a disclosed dependence route |
| Research | Compatible technology sharing and cooperative research programs | Adds actual useful knowledge through the shared owner |
| Revolt protection | The local 001 network recognizes the agreement and stops competing hostile actions within the agreed jurisdiction | Does not erase past damage, refusal factions, or another event's rebels |
| Surrender resistance | Continued command and emergency support in a real crisis | Does not provide immortal government or an invalid exile |
| Extraordinary powers | Access to a limited synchronized operation or protected corridor | Explicit costs, preparation, and sovereignty consequences |

Benefits follow the country's accepted role. They do not duplicate stock from the World Revolution and a regional command, nor consume an ally's equipment without a verified delivery contract. Aid can fail when the sender lacks supply or the route is blocked. The request should explain the actual shortage and the partial result.

## Influence stages

Use a 0 to 100 range. Twenty-point stages provide a clear shared tuning model. The current leader receives the appropriate stage presentation without losing their identity.

| Influence | Institutional state | Portrait stage | Main player opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to below 20 | Independent ally with limited agreed cooperation | Ordinary portrait | Choose useful aid and establish protected local commands |
| 20 to below 40 | Advisers and foreign access become embedded | Faint forehead mark | Audit access, limit advisers, or accept a deeper program |
| 40 to below 60 | Party procedure and appointments respond to Lenin's instructions | Closed third-eye line | Secure the party and military chain, expose compromised officials |
| 60 to below 80 | Orders bypass normal institutions and conversion pressure becomes public | Partially open eye | Prepare a breakaway or a major sovereignty operation while aid remains powerful |
| 80 to below 100 | Critical struggle for sovereignty | Bright open eye | Resolve the 180-day sovereignty mission, choose open independence, or explicitly submit |
| 100 | Complete personal and institutional binding pressure | Full transformation | A real binding transaction is still required, followed by a separate annexation window |

Influence at 100 is not itself a country transfer. Subject status, command authority, and annexation are real political transactions. A public number cannot silently substitute for them. Likewise, reducing a subject's number does not automatically restore independence without the appropriate sovereignty transaction.

## Growth and resistance

Ordinary automatic pressure is evaluated through the actor-owned schedule and is capped at 5 Influence per rolling 30 days. The initial model is `automatic pressure = min(5, 1 + floor(Momentum / 40) + min(2, active exposure count))`. An active exposure is an actually granted foreign commissar office, binding party directive, industrial or intelligence access agreement, or embedded foreign command arrangement. Count each of those four families once while its real institution remains active. Aid alone and an ordinary joint battle do not create a permanent exposure. The first evaluation is thirty days after joining, with one saved due date per country. Retries cannot repeat it, missed callbacks cannot exceed the rolling cap, and no unused allowance is banked. Trotsky's protected country and countries that have completed independence receive zero and never enter this schedule.

This produces one automatic point at low Momentum without special access, up to five at high Momentum with substantial institutional dependence. These are initial balance values that require native scenario evaluation. Defeat and compromised leadership affect the real exposure and institutional situation rather than applying an additional unspecified automatic increase. Explicit accepted programs use the following disclosed amounts and their own consumed receipts.

| Accepted concession | Influence on actual completion | Receipt that must really exist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Establish a foreign commissar office | +10 | One per actual office agreement |
| Accept a binding party directive | +15 | One per distinct accepted directive |
| Accept emergency material aid with the disclosed dependence condition | +10 | Only on actual delivery. Ordinary unconditional allied transfers are outside this action |
| Complete a foreign-supervised formation conversion | +15 | One per real personnel cohort |
| Grant industrial or intelligence access | +10 | One shared access-family receipt |
| Install an embedded foreign command | +10 | Only on real placement and command agreement |

One pacing rule governs all six rows instead of six separate cooldowns. A country completes at most one concession in any 30 days, so the most Influence it can take on in 30 days is 15 from a concession plus the automatic 5. Reaching the critical stage from zero therefore takes at least four months, and the warning stages are always seen. The same office, access or command family can be granted again only 180 days after its earlier agreement ended. Removing and immediately restoring an agreement cannot create another benefit, payment or Influence receipt. A normal concession reaching 100 still does not count as explicit early submission. Resistance reductions and their actual costs are specified with the corresponding actions in Part 8, so the implementation must not add a second discretionary reduction in a popup or focus reward.

No same-day cascade can skip the critical warning and mandatory resistance opportunity through several duplicate callbacks. A player may explicitly choose voluntary submission earlier, but silence, a canceled popup, or accepting unrelated aid is not consent to immediate annexation.

| Raises Influence | Why it matters | Reduces or contains Influence | Real sacrifice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Appointing commissars in domestic command | Gives Lenin personnel authority | Dismiss or restrict foreign advisers | Loss of their coordination and intelligence support |
| Accepting binding party directives | Gives Lenin institutional vetoes | Secure the party and expose compromised officials | Time, political effort, and temporary disruption |
| Repeated emergency aid | Creates material dependence | Win and supply a major operation independently | More local equipment, manpower, and logistical risk |
| Converting formations under foreign supervision | Transfers knowledge and command of the host | Refuse conversion or preserve human formations | Slower access to some supernatural strengths |
| Granting industrial or intelligence access | Opens domestic systems to the network | Protect laboratories, factories, and intelligence | Reduced cooperation and an expensive security program |
| Foreign units embedded in key regions | Makes force and administration inseparable | Build and supply an independent command | Real local army investment |
| Defeats and high revolutionary Momentum | Make outside intervention more attractive and coercive | Receive bounded anti-Lenin support and prove sovereignty | Diplomatic exposure and possible retaliation |
| A captured or compromised leadership change | Weakens institutional resistance | A legitimate restored leadership with secured institutions | A real political outcome, not a free portrait swap |

These are weighted pressures and interventions, not eight permanent new values. High communist popularity can make Lenin's political appeal stronger without making every communist a supernatural subject. A government can remain communist while resisting Influence or going to war.

## The critical mission

Crossing 80 starts a 180-day sovereignty mission once for the current crisis episode. The deadline is stored independently of category visibility and native popup state. Closing the menu, changing the leader, losing and regaining a state, or reloading cannot refresh it.

The mission presents two practical objectives alongside the Influence requirement: protect an independent military command and secure the governing party or its equivalent sovereign institution. A country can complete these through its own programs, useful outside support, or actual victories. Successful defense of sovereignty lowers Influence below the high-dependence state and closes the current coercion episode with the real institutional changes preserved.

The initial success target is Influence below 60 plus both institutional objectives. This makes a sovereignty victory more substantial than temporarily slipping from 80 to 79. Completing a real recovery below 60 with both institutions secured closes the episode and keeps every action cooldown. A new episode needs a new rise to 80 after that recovery.

This table is the single home for the exact expiry results. Part 8 and the prompts refer to it.

| State when the 180 days end | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Below 60 with both institutions secured | Success, already closed above |
| 100 and the real binding gate valid | Lenin can complete puppeting, with no fresh 180-day clock |
| 80 to 99, or 100 with the binding gate invalid | Institutional setback. The party's recognized independent authority is compromised first, or the independent command's authority when no valid party proof exists. No divisions are destroyed, no casualties occur, no territory is annexed and no unlisted Influence is added |
| 60 to 79, or below 60 with either institution unsecured | An unresolved coercion episode with no new institutional penalty and no puppet transaction |

The original deadline stays expired in every unsuccessful case. A later rise to 80 cannot mint another grace period, and if Influence later reaches 100 with the binding gate valid, binding can proceed without a fresh clock. Expiry never leaps straight to annexation, and free independence stays available throughout.

Influence can reach 100 before the 180-day deadline. That produces full personal transformation and binding pressure while the country retains the remaining sovereignty struggle. Automatic binding waits for the critical deadline. Only a separate explicit voluntary-submission choice can bind earlier. Accepting an ordinary concession that raises the value to 100 is not itself consent to early submission. The interface shows the remaining deadline and current political status alongside the value.

## Subject stage and annexation

After an actual subject or equivalent fully bound-government transaction, begin a separate 120-day annexation mission. The display explains that the country still exists and has a final independence opportunity. The annexation operation consumes only the exact active request, current subject relationship, and current deadline.

Resistance can delay or prevent the final political absorption through a defined operation, but cannot endlessly refresh the timer by canceling and reopening it. A legitimate independence transaction cancels it outright. If Lenin is defeated or the binding relationship is invalidated, the countdown closes through the shared lifecycle owner rather than firing against a stale country pointer.

Annexation transfers real territory, units, equipment, leaders where legitimate, industrial assets, and liabilities. It preserves population and past casualties correctly. The extraordinary Chaos premium is separate from the generic annexation outcome. A government that has already been absorbed cannot issue another annex receipt after a later release.

## Independence is always accessible

The decisive independence action has no extra political power, command power, equipment, Influence, or stability payment gate. Preparation can be expensive. The actual decision to break with Lenin remains possible even at zero resources and complete Influence before the annexation transaction commits.

Independence leaves the faction, ends the binding relationship where required, cancels the annex countdown, stops future Influence growth, and creates the real war through one guarded operation. It does not conjure an army, restore already lost land, refund every past sacrifice, revive dead people, or instantly cure all transformed personnel. Those consequences create the difficult but playable breakaway campaign.

The player sees the likely military danger and retains agency. AI ordinarily prepares first, but a critical imminent absorption can justify a desperate break. The event must not interpret poor survival odds as permission to hide the action.

## Trotsky's separate contract

Trotsky as the current Soviet leader joins as an autonomous cofounder. An independently justified non-Soviet Trotsky route uses the same protected contract only after character and current role validation.

This contract has no Influence variable, no transformation pipeline that silently reimplements Influence, no compulsory puppet directive, and no forced annex timer. It remains protected from Red World's accelerated absorption. Replacing the leader later does not silently void the accepted autonomy contract. An explicit political story can change the partnership, but it must not masquerade as a delayed Influence consequence.

Trotsky's country can support joint commands, defend independent communist governments, cooperate in research, sponsor uprisings, and oppose forced conversion. It does not count as Lenin's ruled territory while autonomous. Its own ordinary wars and explicit political choices remain real.

## Revolutionary Momentum

Momentum ranges from 0 to 100 and uses broad stages at 20, 40, 60, and 80. It reflects current inhabited rule, actual victories and losses, effective allied or bound institutions, functioning regional commands, real host development, and completed campaign objectives. The technical handoff defines the bounded contribution model and anti-farming receipts.

Territorial credit uses one current physical controller per state and the frozen inhabited-world weights. A state selected by two countries does not give double Momentum. An independent ally can contribute through actual cooperation, but its territory is not relabeled as Lenin's direct rule. A captured command, liberated region, breakaway, lost ritual site, destroyed elite force, or collapse of organizing can reduce power.

Momentum is a thermometer that no action raises directly. It earns its place because both sides read their next move from it: the World Revolution sees which power or manifestation the next stage opens, and defenders see which of their operations is pulling it down. The category and tooltip show the current stage, the next threshold and one plain sentence naming the component that is holding the value back, for example "Territory: under a quarter of the world is under Lenin's rule". They never list the six components as numbers. The component sentence is the only breakdown the player sees, and it exists so that the value points at a decision.

The ordinary stage thresholds have no hidden hysteresis. The displayed value must not round upward across a threshold the engine has not reached. One-time manifestations, Chaos premiums, and achievements have separate consumed receipts, so losing and regaining a stage changes current capability without farming another reward.

| Momentum | Strategic stage | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Below 20 | Fragile international outbreak | The host exists and fights, but distant coordination and institutional reach are weak |
| 20 to below 40 | Organized revolutionary war | Reliable regional planning, useful aid, stronger local operations |
| 40 to below 60 | Expanding international movement | Better synchronized operations, sustainable commands, stronger industrial and political programs |
| 60 to below 80 | Distributed revolutionary authority | Registered survival anchors can support the body, broad coordination and deeper Influence pressure |
| 80 to 100 | Red apotheosis | Highest baseline Third-Eye powers, strongest finite operations, greater transformation and terminal pressure |

The collective route can reach high Momentum through successful institutions, alliances, and operations. It is not forced into mass civilian conversion to complete its campaign. The central route retains the contribution of actually integrated institutions after annexing a government, rather than losing all progress because an allied tag disappears.

## Six power families

| Power | Operation and source | Counterplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Third-Eye perception | Identifies a limited operational target through cells, intelligence, and a committed observation program | Counterintelligence, dismantled cells, protected command locations, false leads with explicit outcomes |
| Synchronized revolution | Coordinates a bounded group of prepared 001 cells into strikes, sabotage, mutinies, or uprisings | Disrupt preparation, protect supply and key institutions, defeat the actual local revolt |
| The common voice | Strengthens a named joint operation or applies disclosed political pressure through accepted access | Independent command and party security, refusal of the directive, loss of the transmission site |
| Transformation | Converts actual eligible people or formations through a finite operation | Refusal, protected human formations, early treatment, failed conversion consequences, real supply costs |
| Industrial awakening | Intensifies a defined foundry, railway, depot, or production program | Capture the site, interrupt inputs, sabotage the machinery, expose destructive labor practices |
| Distributed survival and apotheosis | Uses a finite origin and regional-anchor network to keep the movement viable at high Momentum | Destroy or sever registered anchors, liberate regions, support breakaways, hold the body and origin while the network fails |

Powers strengthen the campaign systems already on the map. They do not create arbitrary new tech evolutions, unlimited spawning, universal mind control, or invulnerability. Each has a valid target, real preparation, meaningful cost or opportunity cost, cooldown, completion evidence, interruption behavior, and AI intent.

## Recovery and cure

Doctor Wu and other specifically supported scientific routes can restore early transformations through bounded treatment. Later transformations are harder and can leave lasting changes. Suppressants can reduce symptoms or interrupt network access without being a complete cure. A country's ruler being cured does not instantly reverse every institution, unit, and population classification.

Successful recovery reclassifies living people and restores appropriate institutions. It never counts the same living person as a resurrection, a new recruit, and an avoided death. The shared population, military, disease, and Deaths owners supply the actual physical accounting.
