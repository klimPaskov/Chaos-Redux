# 07. Focus tree architecture and complete path design

## The tree develops an existing playable country

The World Revolution already has its origin, government, Lenin, first nonhuman host, global wars, public Momentum and political responses when this tree becomes available. A focus never substitutes for those opening obligations. The first focuses choose how to use that country, and later focuses create institutions, operations, equipment pathways and political consequences that remain useful after completion.

There are eight family lanes: the opening, the Third Eye, worldwide cells, the awakened army, red foundries, the Red International, continental commands, and the Red World. Three early political philosophies form the identity spine through these lanes. Most support branches coexist. Mutual exclusion is reserved for political settlement and concrete incompatible operating methods, with an explicit cost to changing an institution later.

All labels and anchor identifiers below are planning references. They establish complete path logic and asset requirements, and are not final localisation or reserved native focus IDs. The implementation owner selects collision-free identifiers and writes final copy from these purposes. A focus reward that opens an operation does not automatically complete that operation, transfer a state, invent equipment, or award an unearned Momentum objective.

The layout uses an opening band, a three-column identity choice, and separated support lanes beneath it. Continental commands occupy a coherent regional group with their own navigation anchor, rather than six long disconnected trees. The terminal lane sits below the shared late milestones and remains visually distinct from ordinary military development. No focus inlay window is needed for the accepted design. Use native tree navigation, filters, meaningful icon families and normal-zoom readability.

## Reading the path tables

An entry reading “opening” requires either O1 or O2. “Route choice” means the appropriate one of P1, P2 or P3. Conditions such as a supplied port, real partner, current Stalin, active command or Momentum threshold are continuing actual-world gates and must be revalidated where they matter. A focus can expose a plan before its target is secured, but its operational reward remains tied to real completion.

Thirty-five days is the standard early choice or bounded preparation. Seventy days is a substantial institution or mature campaign commitment. A seven-day focus exists only for the explicitly urgent body or final-resistance crisis, with no free resources or repeatable milestone reward. Focus duration is a political commitment, while the funded mission can continue separately afterward.

## Six regional command paths

Each regional path begins with a thirty-five-day bridgehead planning anchor, offers two seventy-day operational methods, and converges at a seventy-day command capstone. The two methods are mutually exclusive as the command's initial specialization, while shared army, industry and political branches remain compatible. The capstone also requires the real regional mission and sustained supply from Parts 4 and 8. Completing a focus alone does not make an anchor or command operational.

The region codes expand into explicit bridgehead, method-A, method-B and capstone anchors, four per region. Their presence in the path specification is required even when the current campaign cannot activate that region. Each gets individual icons and region-specific final copy. The tree exposes a meaningful unavailable reason or an appropriate conditional reveal rather than showing a broken empty focus.

One completed command campaign fills the finite Momentum objective slot. Current command-component Momentum reflects the actual functioning regional slots. All six are required only by Workers of Every World, and that achievement additionally requires actual Red World entry. No normal terminal or defeat condition gains an all-six requirement.

## Rewards, AI and visual acceptance

The three simultaneous focus-created spirit families defined in Part 4 are the whole permanent ceiling. Focus rewards advance their stages. The tree does not accumulate extra permanent spirits for each command, unit, eye stage or council, and local institutions and time-limited operational effects use the appropriate owners without circumventing the ceiling.

Focus filters cover opening and government, Third-Eye powers, cells and liberation, army, industry and research, diplomacy, regional commands, and endings. Commonly searched unit families, regions and role branches receive useful search labels. Review filters and search wiring against the native consumer, with targeted tool evidence when available. Record unavailable observation separately from missing implementation. Icons follow the family grammar in Part 12, with clearly distinct politics, tools, transport, foundries, councils, bindings and terminal imagery.

Part 8 supplies the exact initial AI fork and support-priority model. Validate the full available candidate set for each role and scenario, including a single base, an isolated archipelago, no foreign partners, no Stalin or Trotsky, a landlocked origin, scarce modern equipment, low Momentum and a threatened body. A missing conditional branch must not block unrelated support or an ending. An AI priority is not a percent chance, and native focus inspection does not replace probability inspection where weighted selection matters.

The implementation route-coverage table must contain every anchor and regional expansion, its prerequisites, exclusions, conditional reveals, action unlock, actual reward, AI, icon, final localisation, institution owner, failure behavior and evidence. Render the whole tree and each conditional view at normal zoom, inspect every connector and convergence, and verify that the eight lanes remain navigable without overlap or clipped controls. A screenshot of the opening roots alone is not full-tree acceptance.
