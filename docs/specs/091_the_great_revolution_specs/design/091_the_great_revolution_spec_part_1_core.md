# 01. Vision and player experience

## The signature event

The Great Revolution is Event 091, a fire-once Major event at Chaos level 5 with no cluster. Its catalog status remains To Be Reworked throughout this planning package. World Revolution is the connected country and crisis name. The catalog title remains The Great Revolution even when the opening super-event is called the January Revolution, the October Revolution, or another current-month title.

Lenin returns as a physical political actor with an organic third eye. Nonhuman workers assemble around him carrying literal hammers and sickles, while real human organizers attempt to turn the simultaneous uprisings into governments, supply systems, and a worldwide war. One image should make those three facts recognizable: Lenin, altered labor, and a revolution that crosses borders at the same moment.

This is an alternate-history supernatural scenario. The historical research supports political arguments, recognizable symbols, and the texture of institutions. It does not claim that historical communism, Lenin, workers' councils, or any nationality possessed supernatural properties. Human communists can be allies, opponents, victims, rescuers, or independent rivals of the returned Lenin.

The fixed names are The Great Revolution, World Revolution, Whispers of the World Revolution, The Red World, Lenin, and The First Coming. Other route, action, mission, focus, unit, achievement, faction, and incident labels in this package are working design labels. They communicate function and tone for later writing.

## What a campaign should feel like

Before the opening, Event 001 makes industrial districts and political organizing matter. The player recognizes the same factories, rebels, state levels, and unresolved containment failures when the global revolution begins. Those prior choices determine where the enemy is prepared. They do not merely add a number to a new random map.

At the opening, the map changes once across the ordinary world. The event makes its consequences immediately visible and establishes actual wars. A country can lose no territory and still face Lenin as an enemy. A country can lose all its eligible territory and truly collapse. A joining communist government can keep its selected states as allied revolutionary bases, while the sovereign origin still becomes the center of the new country. Existing rebel armies remain real formations with histories and assets.

The first military problem is connection. The World Revolution can begin with impressive local armies and very poor strategic continuity. Rail junctions, ports, depots, fronts, civilian support, and allied access determine whether those armies can cooperate. Defenders have the chance to isolate enclaves before they become continental systems. Lenin has the chance to turn isolated labor movements into a viable international war effort.

The middle campaign changes the meaning of alliance. Ordinary communist joiners receive enough equipment, coordination, recruitment, research, and emergency relief to make joining a serious choice. They then decide how much institutional access to exchange for that help. Refusers can build a human communist alternative. Trotsky's Soviet route offers an autonomous partnership with its own political arguments and military responsibilities. Stalin's Soviet route is a fixed refusal and a major ideological war.

The late campaign is earned through control and organization. Lenin's powers become distributed through commands and transformed institutions, with finite, discoverable vulnerabilities. The Red World requires Chaos strictly above 1000 and rule over strictly more than half of the inhabited world measure. It starts a terminal campaign phase with final resistance and political decisions. It does not perform an instant global annexation.

## Playable perspectives

| Perspective | Main ambition | Recurring tension | Evidence of progress |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| World Revolution | Build a sustainable revolutionary order and decide what kind of Lenin rules it | Rapid expansion against supply, local legitimacy, and the cost of transformation | Connected bases, functional commands, active focus systems, Momentum stages |
| Noncommunist defender | Preserve a viable state, recover territory, and dismantle the supernatural command system | Fight the external host while avoiding domestic measures that strengthen its organizing story | Open supply routes, liberated state receipts, broken commands, protected civilians |
| Human communist refuser | Defeat Lenin without surrendering communist independence | Cooperate with ideological enemies while protecting autonomous parties and councils | Independent partners, rescued institutions, public alternatives to apotheosis |
| Ordinary communist joiner | Win with powerful aid while deciding how much sovereignty to preserve | Immediate survival against long-term institutional dependence | Aid delivered, Influence changes, completed sovereignty objectives, prepared independence |
| Trotsky-led Soviet Union | Coordinate international revolution as an autonomous cofounder | Shared war against forced absorption and dehumanization | Joint operations and independent governments, with no Influence meter or annex countdown |
| Stalin-led Soviet Union | Hold the Soviet state and defeat a rival claiming Lenin's authority | Strategic survival and the political credibility of refusal | Capital defense, a human war coalition, defeat of Lenin's body and commands |

The origin country's human player receives an explicit opportunity to play the World Revolution. The switch is never forced. In multiplayer, only the origin-country player receives the default switch offer. Other players retain their countries and their own diplomatic choices. The switch does not create extra resources, erase the former country, or reset the opening snapshot.

## Five design commitments

1. Prior play remains legible. State levels, rebel entities, real units, stocks, leaders, containment, and whisper history feed the opening through a documented handoff.
2. Political choices change systems. The three Lenin philosophies alter governing arrangements, force generation, command survival, and relationships with allies. Joining and refusal have continuing gameplay.
3. Strength has sources. Armies use existing formations, measured manpower, finite conversion, captured equipment, production, aid, and explicitly bounded supernatural milestones.
4. Counterplay remains concrete. Supply interdiction, institutional resistance, liberated states, exposed anchors, cures, and independent command structures reduce Lenin's power.
5. Presentation carries state without becoming another management application. The map, one World Revolution category, native decisions, focus trees, spirits, reports, and changing portraits explain the crisis.

## Public information contract

Revolutionary Momentum is the single event-owned global public value. Lenin's Influence is the single event-owned country public value for an ordinary joiner. The player sees their current stage, the next important threshold, and the actions that can change the situation. Trotsky's autonomous route has no Influence value.

Territorial facts, unit counts, mission objectives, dates, costs, and a one-time opening summary remain ordinary information. They must not grow into additional permanent event currencies or public dashboards. The inhabited-world share appears as a specific capstone condition and milestone report, not a competing always-visible management meter. Hidden accounting exists to make actions reliable, not to ask the player to manage implementation fields.

Event 001 can retain its existing crisis presentation where its owner still operates. The 091 category does not duplicate 001's threat dashboard or create a second control-level economy. Map markers and native state tooltips communicate inherited levels and subsequent local operations.

## Country identity

World Revolution is a special Chaos country with an initially mixed society. Its field host can be nonhuman while most inhabitants, allied officials, supply workers, and local councils remain human. It continues to use normal civilian systems until a separate, evidenced societal-replacement condition has been met. The existence of one altered division or one transformed leader is insufficient to change a country's entire species classification.

Its flag uses deep red and black, a readable worker and peasant tool motif, and a distinct third-eye element. Route variants should alter the political emphasis of the same identity. They should not make the map look like three unrelated countries. The final faction name requires historical and collision review. The recommended working direction is a successor international claiming continuity with the Comintern, with clear separation between voluntary international cooperation and Lenin's personal authority.

The current remote repository already registers REV and REV_vladimir_lenin. The new playable package should first investigate migrating that dormant identity. A new tag is selected only after the installed vanilla, mod, and workshop collision audit demonstrates the correct route. The design requirement is one complete World Revolution country and one authoritative Lenin identity. A second registered Lenin must not be created merely because the old country package is inadequate.

## What completion means

The complete implementation will include the connected 001 changes, opening transaction, full country setup, all role routes, all eight army families, focus tree, decisions and missions, AI, influence and transformations, powers, regional commands, occupation and expansion, Red World, earned defeat and aftermath, shared-system adapters, achievements, original visual families, sourced audio, catalogs, and validation evidence.

This package is a full design and implementation handoff. It does not claim that the game scripts, art, audio, models, animation, installed-DLC compatibility, or runtime behavior already exist. Feasibility questions remain explicit deliverable requirements with named owners and proof expectations. They are not permission to quietly remove part of the idea.

### Source basis

The user brief is the authority for event behavior and fixed names. Supplied Event Planning, Events, Focus Trees, Decisions and Missions, Scripted GUI, Mechanics, and Project Source Protocol govern the planning structure. Repository findings are recorded in the research and technical handoffs with their pinned revision.
