# 06. The revolutionary host and its economy

## An army with recognizable work behind it

The host should read as altered workers becoming an army. Heavy tools, work clothing, repeated movements, factory organization, captured military equipment, and unnatural coordination provide its identity. It is not a set of ordinary infantry models with glowing eyes. Its formations still need weapons, replacement bodies, fuel, transport, supply, and officers who can turn the Third Eye's commands into operations.

Human formations inherited from 001 remain visible in the order of battle. A surviving rebel rifle division does not become a nonhuman Hammer Guard because its country joins. Human councils and allied governments can deliberately preserve those units. Conversion is a separate, finite action with a real source, political consequences, and an observable result.

Every family below is required. The family design is independent of the final engine representation. A native battalion, support company, equipment type, template, technology, or entity may implement part of it after reference and compatibility review. An implementation must preserve each family's battlefield purpose and visual identity rather than substituting eight names for one generic formation.

## Eight core families

| Family | Battlefield role and distinguishing behavior | Material and human source | Counterplay and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awakened Workers | The broad infantry base. Hold factory districts, absorb pressure, and turn local organizing into a front. Their tools and captured rifles coexist visibly. | Finite converted workers, inherited cells, basic infantry equipment, workshop output | Vulnerable in open ground without support, poor motor mobility, quality depends on preparation and organization |
| Hammer Guard | Slow, heavy breakthrough and close assault against urban positions and forts. Large hammers remain readable in silhouettes and attacks. | Stronghold cadres, deliberate heavy conversion, forged tool equipment, support weapons | High supply and equipment burden, poor pursuit, exposed to armor and air power without combined arms |
| Sickle Host | Infiltration and assault through broken terrain, forests, hills, and darkness. Hooked blades and disciplined local routes distinguish it from fast generic infantry. | Prepared local cells, lighter equipment, reconnaissance and guide networks | Weak sustained frontal combat, limited anti-armor ability, loses advantages when isolated from local knowledge |
| Red Motor Columns | Operational movement using captured trucks, altered drivers, tractors, and workshop maintenance. Link enclaves and exploit gaps. | Actual trucks, fuel, mechanics, roads, and converted or defecting personnel | Fuel starvation, vehicle attrition, poor mountains and deep mud, cannot substitute tractors for limitless motor equipment |
| Revolutionary Cavalry | Regional mobile forces that retain the local history of horse warfare and rural organizing. Useful where road networks are weak. | Existing cavalry, real horses represented by the appropriate equipment and manpower contract, regional recruits | Limited armor protection and breakthrough, vulnerable to sustained fire, not universally available in equal quality |
| Foundry Brigades | Armor and mechanized forces built around captured industrial knowledge and repaired vehicles. Later variants support siege or mobile warfare. | Captured vehicles, actual tank production, salvage, compatible technologies, fuel, trained crews | Long production lead time, expensive losses, poor supply tolerance, no free modern tanks from a focus alone |
| Third-Eye Commissars | A support and command family that coordinates assaults and creates institutional pressure when accepted into allied forces. | Selected cadres, training, communication equipment, an explicit host-government access agreement | Targetable political dependence, limited support slots, does not grant blanket mind control or free national conversion |
| Red Engineers | Siege, rail repair, bridging, supply reconstruction, fortification, and industrial recovery. Their tools serve both civilian and military roles. | Engineers and skilled workers, support equipment, construction capacity, captured plans | Requires time and protected access, cannot repair an encircled economy instantly, loses people and equipment under attack |

## Family progression and meaningful tuning

The first production pass should tune capabilities in strong, comprehensible steps. These are design targets to evaluate against the installed combat model, not claims that final unit statistics have been validated.

| Family | Entry package | Specialized choice | Late payoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awakened Workers | A supplied line formation with modest training and a clear replenishment route | Preserve human rifle cadres for flexibility or standardize altered mass infantry for stronger coordinated assaults | Reliable regional replacements drawn from a real reserve and supported local industry |
| Hammer Guard | A limited breach formation at prepared Level 3 bases or a completed heavy-conversion program | Urban breach equipment or fortress assault equipment, with one active specialization per formation family | A decisive prepared offensive, balanced by recovery time and replacement cost |
| Sickle Host | Reconnaissance and infiltration formations from suitable cells | Forest and hill infiltration or urban night networks | Targeted opening of a front or supply disruption that a conventional army can exploit |
| Red Motor Columns | A small mobile group only where trucks and fuel exist | Fast exploitation or protected convoy and corridor work | A connected operational reserve that can reinforce threatened enclaves |
| Revolutionary Cavalry | Regional inherited cavalry and locally justified recruits | Rural security and supply screening or raiding and pursuit | Useful mobility without requiring every command to develop armor |
| Foundry Brigades | Repair and field captured armor before inventing a full domestic tank industry | Heavy siege vehicles or mobile combined-arms formations | Sustained indigenous armor production, with compatible designs and a real industrial base |
| Third-Eye Commissars | Limited coordination teams under an explicit appointment | Voluntary liaison cadres or coercive political command, governed by the political route | Strong coordinated operations with correspondingly different allied sovereignty costs |
| Red Engineers | Repair detachments and support companies | Rail and port restoration or assault engineering | Reduced strategic isolation and the ability to prepare a costly major offensive |

Ordinary family improvements should use a small number of impactful modifiers, commonly 10 or 15 percent when the engine supports the intended effect. A doctrine benefit should not become a stack of unrelated 2 percent bonuses. Large strengths require a visible tradeoff such as supply, movement, replacement burden, preparation time, political resistance, or a scarce equipment source.

No family has universal immunity to attrition, encirclement, air attack, contamination, or supply loss. The Third Eye can coordinate a disconnected enclave. It cannot transport fuel through a hostile sea without a valid logistics mechanism.

## Opening forces: one regional accounting pass

Opening state selection determines the map. A separate regional army allocator determines how much of that map can be defended. It reads the frozen opening evidence and produces a finite initial army plan for each connected group of revolutionary states. Overseas fragments and allied local bases can form separate supply regions when the engine cannot support one contiguous network.

The allocator considers inherited formations, state levels, population, industrial capacity, military stock, host manpower and division strength, local communist support, current wars, supply and ports, ritual preparation, whispers, and the current Chaos tier. These inputs alter family mix, organization, readiness, and the proportion of immediately deployable versus reserve forces. They do not each grant an additional independent stack of units.

Use the following sequence:

1. Register every inherited unit and stock once. Decide whether its country remains allied, becomes subordinate, joins a human refusal route, or is absorbed under its actual political choice.
2. Group the selected physical states into sustainable regional muster areas. Never repeat a full unit-generation program for each country's overlapping claim on the same state.
3. Compute real personnel and equipment available to the new host. Separate defecting formations, stored equipment, converted existing units, newly raised personnel, and the bounded First Coming manifestation.
4. Estimate the local defense and offensive burden against the actual neighboring opposition. Use host strength and local front conditions to avoid isolated token armies, while retaining equipment and supply as hard constraints.
5. Allocate immediate field formations, reserve personnel, and future production orders. A shortage produces understrength or delayed formations with clear repair paths. It does not create unsupported modern equipment.
6. Create or transfer each formation through a consumed receipt. Capture the resulting units and remaining reserve for subsequent maintenance and AI.

The First Coming provides one explicitly finite supernatural seed host at the sovereign origin so the promised nonhuman army exists immediately even in a poor origin. The initial tuning is a campaign-wide opening seed budget of 10,000 person-equivalents, distributed into a small usable package after native template validation. It is a supernatural source recorded separately from local population. It is not awarded once per country, state, continent, or retry. Its equipment is a finite tool-and-basic-weapon package, with no tanks, aircraft, fleet, or fuel conjured by default.

Additional opening units must have real sources. A country-strength estimate may authorize a larger attempted defection, but only formations or equipment actually debited from the source count as delivered. A failed or partial stock transfer yields a corresponding partial force. The opening does not add invented manpower while also transferring the same existing division.

## What the inherited levels provide

| Inherited state | Opening quality | Important restraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 Agitation Zone | Local guides, caches, weak worker formations, low initial organization, and scattered intelligence | A low-preparation state cannot independently field heavy nonhuman armor |
| Level 2 Insurgent Stronghold | Organized infantry, local command, useful support equipment, prepared sabotage, and conditional motor or cavalry elements | Mobile elements still require suitable sources and regional logistics |
| Level 3 Communist Lockdown | Strong nonhuman cadres, heavy tool units, commissars, depots, command potential, and conditional armor | Remove the demilitarization imposed by 001 only through its ownership receipt, preserve unrelated demilitarization rules |
| Active 001 rebel territory | The real government, army, leaders, stocks, and documented preparation already present | Political disposition controls transfer, and unknown legacy levels remain unknown |
| Newly selected ordinary state | An uprising shaped by popularity, strike history, infrastructure, supply, and local weakness | It does not inherit an imaginary Level 3 history because Lenin selected it |

The union of preparations should create stronger coherent bases while allowing vulnerable isolated outbreaks. Industry and population guide additional selection, and the military plan gives that selection operational meaning. Impassable wasteland is not a substitute for a real base.

## Reinforcement after the opening

Later musters are regional operations with a source, preparation time, and cooldown. The ordinary early cycle is 90 days. A developed command can run a stronger 120-day regional program. A high-Momentum emergency can shorten one clearly described operation to 45 days, but it spends or commits the same finite sources and cannot be refreshed by canceling and reopening it.

Human recruitment, voluntary conversion, coerced conversion, captured-unit processing, and supernatural milestone reinforcement are separate source types. The public interface exposes the relevant action and consequence, not five new currencies. Every result consumes the source receipt and records delivered strength.

A conversion of an existing unit changes its personnel classification and allowed equipment or template behavior while preserving its actual strength, losses, experience treatment, and equipment balance. If native conversion cannot preserve an important property, the technical owner must implement and test a measured replacement transaction. The old unit is not deleted until its transfer contract can commit. The replacement cannot contain more people or equipment than the approved source and explicitly paid additions.

Conversion does not automatically mean death. Living residents who become nonhuman remain living residents. Recruitment and movement use the shared personnel and civilian-transfer owners. Failed conversions, executions, purges, industrial accidents, and combat deaths produce actual losses and are sent once to the shared Deaths system. The event must not count the same person as a transformed civilian, a recruitable human, a newly spawned soldier, and a death.

At high Momentum, supernatural reinforcements come from a finite milestone schedule. The initial allowances are three additional 10,000-person-equivalent manifestations at the first earned crossings of forty, sixty and eighty Momentum. Together with the opening seed, the entire campaign can manifest at most 40,000 person-equivalents. The terminal power override does not award a skipped milestone grant. The schedule is campaign-wide, has consumed receipts, and cannot be rebuilt by losing and regaining a threshold. Exact unit strength is a balance target to validate, not a promised free division count. A force that survives its milestone does not disappear merely because Momentum later falls, but its extraordinary coordination can weaken.

## Production and repair

The economic spine is captured industry connected by repair and transport. Factories initially retain damage, local disruption, production compatibility, and occupation obligations. Red Engineers can repair priority sites. Councils can restore cooperation. Central coercion can extract short-term output at the cost of resistance, population harm when it actually occurs, and allied opposition.

| Economic system | Player decision | Result and constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tool and rifle production | Standardize simple equipment or keep flexible captured lines | Broad infantry supply against lower efficiency and compatibility burdens |
| Ammunition and support stores | Supply an immediate offensive or rebuild regional reserves | Faster pressure against readiness for the next defensive crisis |
| Rail and supply restoration | Repair a trunk corridor, local hub, or port | A real map connection and reduced attrition, with time and construction opportunity cost |
| Salvage | Recover damaged vehicles near secured fronts | Limited equipment returns based on actual recoverable sources |
| Night-shift mobilization | Use voluntary industrial mobilization or supernatural coercion | Stronger production with different fatigue, political, and failure consequences |
| Foundry specialization | Support siege armor or mobile combined arms | Distinct equipment requirements and strategic use |
| Research cooperation | Exchange compatible knowledge with a willing ally | Adds genuinely missing compatible technologies, with no blanket technology reset or free research slots |
| Emergency supply | Prioritize a threatened command | Redirects real stocks and transport, potentially weakening another front |

The new country does not inherit the legacy fixed 1,000 convoys, five research slots, maximum stability, maximum war support, and arbitrary naval setup as a default opening reward. Its starting research capacity is proposed at three slots, with a fourth and fifth earned through a functioning industrial and international research program. This is a design tuning choice. Actual inherited country and donor technology must be reconciled through the supplied compatible-technology union helper.

Starting laws reflect immediate war and mobilization, while civilian administration reflects the origin and political route. The three focus-created spirit families, Scattered Commands, Captured Industrial Network and Revolutionary Government, are defined in Part 4 and are the only permanent spirit slots. Their stages here carry the logistical and production consequences of the inherited army and economy.

## Conditional air and naval development

Air capability starts only from actual aircraft, usable airfields, fuel, ground support, and compatible research. An air branch first secures a maintenance and training base, then recovers or produces aircraft, then develops a doctrine for interdiction, defense, or support. An inland revolution with no aircraft can pursue its main campaign without waiting on an irrelevant air prerequisite.

Naval capability starts only from valid ports, ships, shipyard capacity, crews, convoys, fuel, and access. A port uprising can bring a documented defection or captured vessel. A faction partner can provide access and actual aid. Neither event ownership nor communist ideology creates a free fleet. Naval focuses make overseas connection and convoy survival meaningful, especially for Oceania and separated continental bases.

DLC branches must preserve equivalent gameplay intent with native equipment and doctrine structures that actually exist in the installed configuration. The full design remains required, but the precise technology and variant wiring follows verified native capabilities.

## Naming, models, counters, and sound

Regional naming pools follow documented language and military traditions. Existing 001 pools provide continuity for inherited units. New labels require native-speaker or primary-language verification before release. The fallback is a neutral localized family name with a regional or state designation, never an invented translation presented as authentic.

Every family receives bespoke map geometry, a legible counter, a division emblem, appropriate equipment or technology art, and sourced sound roles. Human and nonhuman formations must remain distinguishable at ordinary map scale. Hammer and sickle silhouettes should survive small counters and the actual model zoom. The asset handoff specifies measured vanilla scale, separate held-item geometry, real skeletal actions, and runtime routing checks.

Selection and command voices must not accidentally change every human unit in a mixed country. If native tag-level audio routing cannot distinguish the requested families, record that exact capability limit and investigate a verified supported representation. A generic supernatural voice replacement for the whole country is not an accepted silent substitute.

### Acceptance cases

The army package must be reviewed in a tiny industrial origin, a large Soviet origin, a colonial port origin, isolated inland enclaves, a mostly communist allied world, and a late war with depleted stockpiles. In every case the opening shows a nonhuman host, inherited armies survive correctly, the force has an equipment and personnel explanation, and logistics create meaningful play.
