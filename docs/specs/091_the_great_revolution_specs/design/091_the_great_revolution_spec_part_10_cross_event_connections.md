# 10. Connections and Shared Crises

## One connected crisis with distinct owners

The Great Revolution grows from Communist Insurgency and then intersects with other crises through concrete people, institutions, territory, and operations. Each connection should change a choice already facing the player. A scientist needs a specimen and a protected laboratory. A sanctuary needs a real route and reception capacity. A demonic bargain needs a surviving government capable of accepting its price. The presence of another event never supplies a universal cancel button.

Connection content appears only when the relevant actor, project, target, or historical receipt exists. An unavailable partner leaves the ordinary campaign complete. These are contextual routes with full costs, outcomes, interruption, and AI treatment when enabled. They are not mandatory prerequisites for beating Lenin or reaching The Red World.

## Communist Insurgency remains the local organizing owner

Event 001 supplies the opening's state history, rituals, current fronts, rebel countries, inherited preparations, and accumulated whisper history. Event 091 converts the accepted bases into a fighting network. The conversion preserves real people, equipment, leaders, and territorial provenance before retiring incompatible local containment effects.

Later expansion follows the same relationship. Lenin sponsors an actual local organizing operation. Existing communist pressure, worker institutions, supply, counterintelligence, and prior repression influence the result. Successful preparation can become an armed base through the Event 091 military transaction. A failed or interrupted operation can leave ordinary political agitation without creating a country or free army.

A liberated former Level 3 state remembers its history. It can receive reconstruction, political autonomy, renewed legal organizing, or repression. It does not silently restart at Level 3 merely because an old callback arrived. Ordinary communism survives the defeat of Lenin, and countries can remain communist without retaining supernatural institutions.

## Brilliant Scientist

The scientist can examine six distinct subjects: the third eye, altered worker anatomy, synchronized communication, awakened industry, distributed survival, and unusual tools or armor. Each subject needs a relevant captured object, consenting or lawfully held living subject under the chosen political route, laboratory access, equipment, and a protected research interval. A general encounter with one worker does not unlock every subject.

| Project direction | Proposed operation | Useful success | Cost, uncertainty, and opposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Study the eye | Secure a third-eye specimen, observation record, or captured body under guarded custody for 120 days | An anti-eye device enables a bounded protected command or exposes one valid anchor connection | Equipment and laboratory commitment, with theft, compromised staff, and incomplete understanding as explicit outcomes |
| Decode the common voice | Preserve recordings and a captured transmission institution for a 90-day comparison program | Better counterintelligence for one regional operation and a warning before its next synchronized attack | Protect multiple observations and maintain independent command, while Lenin can relocate the operational target |
| Analyze awakened machinery | Hold a real foundry and restore enough of its plant to study it for 120 days | A usable repair or industrial adaptation that still consumes ordinary inputs | Reconstruction competes with immediate production, and unsafe exploitation can create a measured local incident |
| Study altered armor and tools | Deliver captured material and complete a 90-day engineering program | A compatible equipment or countermeasure direction tied to the material actually recovered | No automatic highest-level tank or universal damage bonus, with manufacture depending on real industry |
| Offer unlimited research | Lenin invites the scientist to a protected revolutionary laboratory | A genuine scientist-side political branch and a named research contribution | The scientist can demand autonomy, refuse conversion, be compromised, or defect through the scientist owner's established rules |

Successful research unlocks operations and knowledge. It does not delete Lenin, revoke every anchor, or make all nonhuman troops ineffective. A captured laboratory becomes a defendable map objective. Research lost to occupation retains completed knowledge and actual losses according to its owning system, while uncompleted benefits remain unavailable.

## Doctor Wu and transformation medicine

Wu's route distinguishes early symptoms, established transformation, fully altered living workers, and network dependence. Treating a symptom, severing an external command link, and restoring a human body are different medical outcomes. Fully transformed people are harder to restore and can remain living altered survivors even after successful protection from Lenin.

| Treatment direction | Proposed operation and duration | Successful outcome | Persistent limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early worker restoration | Protect a clinic, supply medical equipment, and treat a finite identified cohort over 90 days | Some actual living early cases return to their prior classification and eligible civilian or military roles | No population creation and no automatic equipment or manpower duplication |
| Leader protection | An invited 90-day medical and security program with independent access to the current leadership | Reduces a disclosed Influence increment and protects one accepted institution for a bounded period | Does not cure unrelated officials or cancel a real subject relationship |
| Failed-conversion care | Evacuate actual survivors to a valid reception site and complete a 120-day recovery mission | Saves or stabilizes the observed cohort within the medical owner's actual outcomes | Real route deaths and treatment deaths are recorded once, while survivors remain survivors |
| Resistance drugs | Produce and deliver a finite course to one operation over 90 days | Temporarily weakens third-eye interference or protects a limited force | Requires repeat material production for another use and carries disclosed medical uncertainty |
| Soldier enhancement | A voluntary or coercive political choice followed by a 120-day program | A finite improved or stabilized formation using actual people and equipment | Changes the government's legitimacy and may create failed-conversion harm or later recovery needs |
| Late restoration study | A protected 180-day research and care program with a real late-stage cohort | Partial restoration, stable nonhuman independence, or a useful failed experiment record | No guaranteed total cure and no reversal of conquest merely because a person changes |

An ordinary supporter can use Wu's program as one method of securing institutions during the 180-day sovereignty struggle. This assistance does not become a permission requirement for independence. Trotsky can sponsor voluntary treatment without gaining Influence or losing protected autonomy. Lenin can accept care that preserves useful workers, reject independent access, or attempt to seize a clinic through a real political or military choice.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deal with the Devil | A threatened country receives a survival bargain for a protected corridor, exceptional information, or a finite defensive intervention | The government accepts the Devil owner's real deferred price, while Lenin treats the bargain as rival control over people and institutions | Separate actors and threat sources remain. Neither absorbs the other merely because both are supernatural |
| The Holy Realm | Establish a sanctuary, protect refugees, or organize a 120-day defense of a significant religious and civilian site against an identified conversion operation | Real reception, supply, and military commitments, with political disputes over sanctuary and treatment of communist civilians | Resistance changes the local operation. One religious decision cannot defeat the entire World Revolution |
| Ten Plagues of Egypt | Defend a supply or civilian refuge corridor while plague pressure and revolutionary operations overlap | Divert scarce forces and relief supplies, choose which exposed site can actually be protected, and accept the consequences elsewhere | Each owner applies its own observed damage. Lenin's condemnation of another supernatural claimant is ideological and strategic content. It does not establish that claim as true history. Refusers can reject both claimants, and communist civilians do not automatically become hostile to a sanctuary because of their politics.

## Time Traveler and a single Lenin identity

The existing-Lenin case is an identity problem before it is an art problem. When a valid historical Lenin already exists, the preferred route transforms that actual individual and preserves the legitimate character identity, ownership history, and political consequences. A temporal visitor can recognize the historical figure while disputing the third eye, claimed continuity, or authority of the new movement.

An explicit duplicate-confrontation story is available only if a separately owned temporal event already proves two distinct bodies. It must identify their provenance, public names, character ownership, and which body can sustain Event 091. It cannot be used as a shortcut around an unsupported native character transfer. Until the relevant owner proves that contract, the fallback is a recorded implementation blocker with the ordinary dormant-Lenin path still defined, not an invented second character.

Past or future Stalin and Trotsky appearances follow current active leader ownership at the relevant choice. Their mere existence in a roster does not force the Soviet response. A previously accepted autonomous Trotsky partnership retains its protection after later legitimate leadership change.

## Disease, zombies, and nonhuman classification

The World Revolution begins as a special Chaos actor with a mixed population. Human civilians, human defectors, altered workers, and visiting allies remain distinguishable through their actual owners. An eye overlay, high Momentum, or the presence of a zombie does not classify every resident as the same organism.

Zombie Outbreak and Black Plague can receive bounded study, containment, and cross-infection incidents when their own eligibility allows it. A 120-day quarantine mission can protect a named port, district, or medical site while the normal disease owner processes the actual outbreak. A worker formation may have a specific supported resistance profile after research and balance review. It receives no blanket immunity to every disease, chemical agent, nuclear effect, or supply crisis.

Zombie, rat, and other special actors retain their country, unit, and threat providers. Lenin can fight them, negotiate a specifically supported temporary objective, or exploit a local distraction. He cannot silently annex them through communist alignment or convert their military assets using the ordinary worker muster path.

## Other military and political events

Infantry Spawn can supply a human or nonhuman formation only through that family's provider. Event 091 asks the provider whether the formation can join, oppose, be transferred, or remain independent. The actual manpower, equipment, reinforcement, presentation, and cleanup behavior follow the accepted provider result.

The Offensive can intensify existing operations through its own AI behavior. Lenin still selects viable corridors, defends the origin, maintains supply, protects active missions, and reacts to sovereignty losses without that event. Defenders still select useful anchor and rescue objectives. No campaign route depends on a global aggression event being active.

Ordinary white peace cannot settle the central existential war while Lenin's active claims and threat contract require it to continue. A local ceasefire is a bounded agreement about one region, route, evacuation, or autonomous institution. It identifies participants, territory, duration, and breach conditions, and cannot terminate another faction's unrelated war. An independence arrangement completes its actual sovereignty transaction before any ceasefire can rely on it.

Independence Wave and subject revolts use their own release and ownership transactions. A valid Lenin subject can become a target. Successful release closes that subject's Event 091 annexation generation, ends future Influence pressure, and creates the appropriate current diplomatic relationship once. Occupied regions can seek local liberation without receiving duplicate states, armies, or population from two simultaneous events.

## Food, movement, death, and unconventional warfare

Hunger and displacement reach the shared owners through the owners' own evidence, never through a 091 hunger or refugee rule.
When the revolution's war cuts an ordinary country's rail, port or farmland, or captures its states, the famine and migration owners read the local-war and hostile-capture facts they already read.
The World Revolution is a special Chaos country and sits outside those owners' country validity, so the same exclusion applies to its own administered states.
091 contributes military objectives whose completion changes the facts those owners read, and each one makes an existing owner decision possible or effective.
It adds no relief currency and no second set of buttons.

| 091 objective | Existing owner decision it supports | Who plays it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reopen a rail hub or port that feeds a starving state | Restore Relief, Escort Relief, Airlift Relief | Defenders, refusers and joiners |
| Hold a threatened district until its evacuation completes | Evacuate Civilians, Request Passage, Open Emergency Reception | Defenders, with Wu clinics as an optional destination |
| Keep a safe region open for arrivals | Open Emergency Reception, Distribute Arrivals | Sanctuary partners such as the Holy Realm |
| Return families to a liberated state | Help Families Return, Local Integration | Aftermath governments |

The point of each row is that a front-line choice, such as which hub to retake first, becomes a food or refugee choice with a visible downstream result.
A country that never sits in the owners' registries sees none of these rows, and the ordinary campaign stays complete without them.

Living transformation is not death. Battle casualties, repression, failed conversion, famine, route deaths, and attacks are counted through the owner that actually applies the loss. A survivor cannot become a casualty again because an aftermath report describes the same incident. A liberated or cured person does not create a new population grant.

Chemical, biological, and nuclear operations keep their contamination, outbreak, Air Cleanliness, Condemnation, attribution, and action records. Revolutionary ideology and supernatural appearance do not remove responsibility. A third-eye laboratory exposed to contamination must satisfy the same relevant operational conditions as any other site unless the owning system documents a specific exception.

## Several world threats can remain active

Lenin's activation registers the World Revolution as its own existential threat. Defeating him removes that source and refreshes the aggregate. Zombies, plague, Death, ZIN colonies, Mephistopheles, and other surviving threats remain active according to their own state.

The Red World requires the shared terminal gate and cannot compete through an accidental second scenario commit. If another terminal scenario is already active, ordinary regional interactions can continue only where the shared world-end rules permit them. The terminal registry and settings determine availability, while each event retains the history of its actual actions.

## Connection completion standard

Every enabled connection names its provider, eligible actor, target, practical cost, duration, real success, real failure, interruption, AI preference, and report. Technical handoffs identify shared APIs and missing engine evidence. A connection that lacks an installed provider is recorded as unavailable in that campaign rather than exposed as a broken button. Implementation must finish all accepted available connections or document a concrete blocker for parent review, with no silent deletion of the requested route.
