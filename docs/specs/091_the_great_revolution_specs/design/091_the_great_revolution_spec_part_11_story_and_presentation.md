# 11. Story structure and presentation

## Narrative ownership

Event 001 earns the uncertainty. Event 091 states the impossible fact. Before Whispers, reports describe strikes, missing people, changed tools, industrial spaces, and conflicting testimony. After the opening, reports can state plainly that Lenin is physically present, the third eye is visible, and the revolutionary host is nonhuman.

Lenin is intelligent, politically informed, and capable of choosing. He makes arguments about war, labor, state power, international organization, and the failures of earlier revolutions. His supernatural knowledge does not make every prediction true or every government his willing subject. The player determines whether he presents himself as a first among workers, the center of a godlike state, or an intelligence distributed through foreign parties.

The writing must distinguish followers' claims from observable facts. A council can believe that Lenin has returned to complete emancipation and still refuse to surrender its institutions. A frightened government can accept commissars to survive a war without accepting total transformation. A defender can oppose Lenin while protecting ordinary communists. This gives the political routes human stakes beyond a simple ideology test.

## The opening sequence

The opening consists of one globally committed state change followed by presentations appropriate to each participant. It is not a succession of country popups that each reroll a different map. The exact date and Gregorian month are frozen at actual successful firing. A rejected proposal does not reserve the eventual title month. If later presentation crosses a month boundary, every player still sees the same committed firing-month title.

| Presentation beat | Audience | Purpose | Mechanical relationship |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Month-named super-event | Global audience under the shared super-event display policy | Establish Lenin, the host, and simultaneous revolution in one clear image and cue | Only after a valid opening commit, with one display receipt |
| Origin report and switch offer | Former owner and controller, with the eligible human controller receiving the default switch choice | Explain the local loss and offer the new playable country | Explicit switch only, current authority revalidated |
| Territorial shock report | Every affected ordinary country | Show its selected states, inherited bases, and immediate role | Reads the frozen snapshot and committed disposition |
| Enemy report | Every noncommunist ordinary government, including zero-loss cases | Explain the actual global war and the first defensive priorities | Never gates war creation behind an option click |
| Communist alignment report | Communist governments | Present powerful joining benefits and real refusal consequences | Native reply flow, immediate forced historical routes, default refusal on timeout |
| Rebel continuity report | Existing 001 rebels where meaningful | Recognize the prior movement and its choice of relationship to Lenin | Preserves actual assets and political history |
| Global news | World audience | Summarize the changed strategic reality and first major responses | Does not repeat every national popup or grant duplicate outcomes |

Joining and refusal text varies by current authority. Stalin as current Soviet leader always refuses, even for a player. Trotsky as current Soviet leader joins as an autonomous cofounder. Elsewhere, those characters require valid current role checks and an explicitly justified route. A surname in history or a dead character definition is not sufficient.

Ordinary communist players receive an honest decision. The interface explains that joining preserves their ordinary country and selected local bases except for the sovereign origin, gives aid, and opens the Influence struggle. Refusal creates war and transfers selected revolutionary territory according to the opening contract. Silence is not consent to join. A late popup must revalidate the country, role, and current request before changing anything.

## A campaign of recurring dilemmas

The following are complete incident directions for later event writing. They are not final localisation strings or a required popup quota. Use the state and country changes that actually occurred to choose incidents. A report should either announce a meaningful consequence, offer a decision with distinct outcomes, or reveal evidence that supports a future action.

| Incident family | Situation | Choice and consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first council congress | The origin is secure enough for government | Recognize local delegates, centralize their powers, or place liaison eyes in their institutions, establishing the political fork |
| A human division asks to remain human | An inherited or allied formation is selected for conversion | Preserve its identity and conventional growth, accept voluntary transformation, or impose conversion with real resistance and loss risks |
| The foundry and the village | Heavy production competes with civilian needs | Protect civilian supply, redirect production for a military emergency, or attempt a supernatural night shift with bounded output and documented failures |
| The isolated port | A distant revolutionary base lacks a secure sea route | Spend real convoys and escort support, request allied access, or withdraw and preserve personnel through the migration owner |
| The council refuses an eye | A useful allied institution rejects commissar access | Respect the refusal, negotiate a narrower military liaison, or escalate dependence through an explicit political confrontation |
| A leader speaks in another voice | An ordinary joiner enters a new Influence stage | Report altered behavior and open relevant sovereignty actions, without replacing the person's identity invisibly |
| A command claims independence of the body | A high-Momentum region becomes a survival anchor | Choose the political meaning of distributed authority and reveal corresponding vulnerabilities to opponents |
| The rival human international | Communist refusers establish a credible coalition | Offer human institutional cooperation, a temporary anti-Lenin military arrangement, or a competing revolutionary program |
| The returned Lenin meets another Lenin | Another supported timeline or mod already has the person alive | Resolve one authoritative body through the identity contract, with a witnessed transformation or explicit alternate-timeline narrative |
| A cured witness returns | Wu or another validated treatment restores an early victim | Gain practical evidence and a bounded treatment method, without curing whole nations instantly |
| The tools fall silent | A command is permanently broken | Show liberated workers and disrupted coordination, apply one real command-loss receipt, and open reconstruction choices |

The collective route's tension is that a stronger coalition can constrain Lenin. The central route's tension is that military and industrial strength can destroy the institutions claiming to represent workers. The distributed route's tension is that preserving the movement through other leaders makes sovereignty and identity difficult to recover. Those consequences should appear in operations, alliances, and occupation, not only in prose.

## Stalin and Trotsky

Stalin's Soviet refusal is an immediate political rupture. The Soviet route defends the state's authority, capital, industry, army, and claim to the revolution's inheritance. Its serious gameplay includes preserving human command, securing factories and rails, discrediting the Third Eye, supporting compatible refusers, and deciding how much to cooperate with noncommunist powers. The historical debate informs the language. It does not prove that the fictional Stalin must behave this way in every possible timeline.

Trotsky's Soviet partnership begins as an autonomous agreement. It uses joint command, international organizing, independent councils, and disputes over forced absorption. It never contains a hidden Influence variable, renamed dependence meter, automatic puppet route, or forced annex exception disguised as a world-end effect. If the story allows the partnership to fail, it requires an independent and explicit political choice. Disagreement can change cooperation without making Trotsky secretly obey the ordinary joiner pipeline.

## Three super-events

| Role | Trigger and emotional purpose | Image direction | Audio and text requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Successful global opening, once | Recognizable sourced Lenin identity with an organic third eye, tools in foreground, distinct distant revolutions | A unique structured cue of mass movement, industry, and unease. Verified historical quote candidates, separate cultural remark |
| The Red World | Terminal scenario successfully enters after all conditions pass | One revolutionary order spanning inhabited regions, visibly transformed institutions, remaining human resistance | A separate escalating terminal cue. Text conveys the end of ordinary sovereign competition and the beginning of final resistance |
| Earned defeat | Central body and all relevant survival systems are resolved, with meaningful campaign participation | A broken command site, silent tools, surviving people, and reconstruction rather than a generic victory parade | A separate reflective cue. Text distinguishes liberation from erasing communism or declaring that every transformed person has died |

The opening and terminal art are different compositions. The defeat event does not fire after every lost capital or failed enclave. The actual shared victory criteria determine whether the achievement has been earned. A minor containment success before 091 is not a full Lenin-defeat super-event.

Text research is recorded separately from the main design. Each selected direct quotation needs exact wording, author, work, date, primary source, translation note, and rights note. Each cultural remark has its own source and fit explanation. A familiar line is not acceptable just because it sounds revolutionary. Main localisation is produced later from the accepted research and the actual mechanics.

## Portrait transformation

Five stages communicate the ordinary joiner's institutional change: a faint mark, a closed line, a partly open eye, a bright open eye, and full transformation. The current leader remains recognizable. The overlay follows the actual current leader at the relevant presentation surface, with stage-specific adaptation and a valid static fallback.

The event cannot solve dynamic leader transformation with a short hardcoded roster of famous figures. Politics and diplomacy views both require consumer inspection. The intended implementation uses independent noninteractive overlay containers where verified. If the consumer cannot support the placement, that is an unresolved implementation requirement with specific evidence needed. The feature is not considered complete by omitting diplomacy or replacing every portrait with the same Lenin picture.

## Information hierarchy

The World Revolution category opens with the animated category picture, a compact explanation of the current phase, Momentum, and Influence for an ordinary joiner. The native action list carries real decisions. The picture contains no painted button, simulated meter, or fake control.

Each phase normally exposes at most six primary action families and one to three active objective missions. Targeted states and countries can be chosen through native targeting and map highlights. A player should understand the next strategic problem without opening a custom global management window.

Reports distinguish the historical fixed name, dynamic opening title, current country name, and working political route. Tooltips explain costs, deadlines, consequences, and reasons for unavailable actions. Internal array indices, raw helper names, asset-production steps, and debugging terminology do not belong in player-facing text.

## Aftermath and body custody

After defeat, different countries can prosecute crimes, offer amnesty, protect human communist organizations, reclaim property, study the returned body, support cures, or hide a relic. These choices operate on real surviving governments, populations, and evidence. The same actor cannot receive a prisoner or body twice.

The body's disposition can be destruction, imprisonment, transfer to a qualified scientist or Doctor Wu, return to a legitimate communist authority, or secret custody. Each is a guarded custody transaction. Later incidents can reveal theft, a failed experiment, public pilgrimage, or an attempted restoration. None reopens Event 091's Major selection or silently restarts its world war.

### Writing acceptance

Review a full campaign sequence for each role rather than judging isolated event paragraphs. Verify that major choices have distinct consequences, ordinary communists retain moral and political variety, Lenin remains a capable actor, unfamiliar working labels are localized clearly, and the next action is understandable without reading the specification.
