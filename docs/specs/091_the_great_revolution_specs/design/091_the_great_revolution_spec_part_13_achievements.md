# 13. Achievement design

## Achievement policy

Eight substantial achievements cover distinct campaigns and hard choices. Their names are working labels. Each uses durable facts from actual gameplay, a clear qualifying player identity, and a one-time completion receipt. Their conditions must remain understandable from ordinary play and do not require a console, impossible political setup, fabricated cross-campaign persistence, or an unannounced hidden metric.

The shared achievement owner remains authoritative for registration, eligibility, progress, notification, and art states. Event 091 supplies concrete evidence such as opening losses, voluntary alignment, critical Influence reached, independence, command destruction, autonomous cooperation, and final defeat. It does not create a parallel achievement database.

## The Revolution Devours Its Children

Start as an ordinary communist country, explicitly join Lenin, reach the critical Influence stage at least once, declare independence before full annexation, and participate materially in defeating the World Revolution while remaining communist from joining through the qualifying defeat.

The intended play is accepting useful help, recognizing the loss of sovereignty, preparing a break, and winning a difficult human communist war. The achievement must not force the player to wait helplessly at complete transformation. Independence remains available at every Influence stage.

Record the player's country identity at the join choice, the critical-stage receipt, the independence transaction, continuous relevant ideology history, and a meaningful defeat contribution. A contribution is the permanent destruction of a command, recovery of an opening base followed by thirty continuous days of control, or participation in the operation that resolves the body. Merely being at war on the final day is insufficient. A scripted tag switch to the World Revolution cannot satisfy this ordinary-country route.

## Socialism in One Country

As the Soviet Union with Stalin as the current leader at the opening, take the required refusal, retain continuous control of the frozen Soviet capital region, and materially help defeat Lenin's body and survival system.

Freeze the actual Soviet capital state at the opening rather than hardcoding Moscow in every alternate timeline. Continuous control is the achievement condition. Moving the capital later does not move the protected target. The country can lose other states and still qualify. A pre-opening capital move is part of the actual campaign snapshot and should be visible in the achievement description.

This is a defensive and political achievement. It rewards preserving the human Soviet state under extreme pressure, not changing ideology to evade the communist choice.

## Permanent Revolution

As the Soviet Union with Trotsky as current leader at the opening, join as the autonomous cofounder, remain independent, and help the World Revolution reach the 50 percent inhabited-world milestone. Red World activation is not required, because its Chaos threshold and terminal decision should remain separate from this achievement.

The assistance requirement is two completed joint operations on two distinct inhabited continents, each with a real military or organizing outcome. A joint operation can liberate a designated strategic region for the coalition, establish a functioning continental command through actual support, or carry a successful uprising through its first defense period. A repeat mission against the same target does not count twice.

Trotsky's own independent country is excluded from Lenin's inhabited-rule numerator. Its contribution can still help other controlled or bound territories reach the milestone. There is no requirement to accept Influence, puppeting, annexation, or dehumanization. If Trotsky later leaves through an explicit story choice before completion, qualification stops, with earned historical receipts retained for explanation.

## Workers of Every World

Play as the World Revolution, establish a valid command on all six inhabited continents, and successfully activate The Red World. A command must have its required regional base, institution, logistical access, and completed establishment objective. Clicking a regional focus or briefly occupying a port is insufficient.

The six regional functions are Europe, Asia, Africa, North America, South America, and Oceania. The achievement requires all six valid commands at the activation moment. Antarctica is not a seventh ordinary inhabited command. If a supported total-conversion map changes the continent registry, the compatibility owner must provide a documented equivalent without counting empty map geometry.

## The Eye Closes

Defeat Lenin after red apotheosis has genuinely occurred and with at least three distinct foreign leaders transformed when the decisive defeat operation is accepted. The three-leader condition is proposed concrete tuning for the brief's word several.

The achievement recognizes a difficult late victory. It should not demand that the player deliberately kill transformed leaders or block cures throughout the campaign. Freeze the qualifying set at the final operation's accepted start. If a successful liberation or cure resolves one of those leaders during that operation, the player's constructive action does not invalidate the achievement.

The final victory still requires all relevant body, origin, command, influence-link, and survival-anchor conditions. A country cannot obtain the achievement from a cosmetic apotheosis portrait or a temporary capital occupation.

## Human Comrades

Lead a communist refusal coalition, defeat Lenin, and preserve at least three independent communist governments including the player at the final settlement. These governments must have explicitly refused or broken away, retain actual sovereign institutions, and survive as valid countries.

A coalition can be a valid faction where native diplomacy permits it or a documented set of coordinated wartime agreements. The achievement should not force every defender into one global faction. Material leadership requires organizing the coalition and completing a joint defense, rescue, or command-destruction objective. Counted partner governments require at least ninety days of actual independent cooperation as initial tuning. Tiny countries released on the last day without that real protected history do not count. A legitimate continuing government preserves actual recorded history, while a fresh release cannot inherit a nonexistent cooperation interval.

The intended contrast in play is between a human international and Lenin's supernatural control. The achievement does not require hostility toward every noncommunist ally or the erasure of all other communist movements.

## No State Is an Island

As an ordinary country, lose strictly more than half of its frozen eligible owned states through the opening, survive as a continuing government or a valid native exile, and later recover every original state in that recorded loss set from the World Revolution's control or binding rule.

Count original owned states once. Occupied foreign states are recorded separately and do not inflate the country's homeland-loss condition. A state selected by multiple country pools remains one lost state. The loss must actually commit. A reserved state retained by a joining government is not a loss.

Recovery requires legitimate ownership or the approved restoration settlement and current friendly control, not merely a temporary allied occupation that leaves the state bound to Lenin. The surviving government must materially participate in its restoration. A full opening collapse remains a real possibility, and an exile qualifies only when the native framework creates a valid continuing state.

## First Among None

Defeat every instantiated major continental command permanently before capturing or destroying Lenin's central body. At least three distinct continental commands must have been established during the campaign for this achievement to be eligible. This explicit minimum prevents a trivial award when no distributed network ever existed.

The command inventory is snapshotted when the final body operation is accepted, then every actually instantiated command is rechecked at the decisive body resolution. The acceptance snapshot remains an audit record and cannot exclude a command formed afterward. A later command must also be permanently destroyed before the body is captured or destroyed. Commands already permanently destroyed remain satisfied. A command cannot be temporarily disabled, marked as destroyed, and then return without affecting the achievement. An earlier capture or destruction of the central body before the qualifying complete command destruction disqualifies this ordered achievement even if Lenin later returns. Body destruction through an unrelated event or an unguarded script invalidates the intended order unless the same registered resolution contract records the player's qualifying operation.

## Twelve Possible Revolutions: explicit disposition

The across-campaign version is parked as unsuitable for the normal campaign achievement layer until a verified cross-campaign persistence provider exists. Event 091 fires once per campaign, so the ordinary save cannot honestly record all twelve openings across separate campaigns. A twelve-part hidden family would create unnecessary achievement clutter and still would not provide a single reliable meta-completion condition.

The dynamic month system remains fully required and tested for all twelve months. The archive includes a future acceptance case for cross-campaign tracking if the project later adds a supported provider. This disposition changes only the proposed achievement form. It does not remove the month-title feature or falsely report the meta-achievement as implemented.
