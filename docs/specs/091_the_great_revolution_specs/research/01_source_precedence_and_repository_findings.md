## Irrecoverable legacy information

In the inspected 001 rebel creation paths, temporary original-country and rebel targets exist, but the exact state level and some state-control history can be cleared during creation. A later implementation cannot honestly reconstruct those erased values from the current country flag alone.

The accepted migration keeps the actual country, territory, units, leaders, and stocks. It marks exact unknown history as unknown. A valid original tag or other surviving provenance can narrow identity but must not manufacture a precise earlier level or episode. Future creations gain durable lineage before mutation. The old save's missing history is a documented limit, not an excuse to discard the rebel.
