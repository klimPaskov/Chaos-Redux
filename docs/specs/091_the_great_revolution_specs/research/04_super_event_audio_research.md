## License handling

The [CC0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en) provides a waiver and fallback permission for rights the affirmer controls. It cannot clear an undisclosed third party's recording or sample. Record the specific affirmer, source file, and page at acquisition. Courtesy attribution remains useful even where the recorded CC0 basis does not require it.

The [CC BY-SA 3.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/legalcode.en) requires attribution and the license information for the licensed work, with its conditions applying to distributed adaptations. Preserve the Damskier credit, source link, license link, and exact change notice. Treat an edited audio derivative under the applicable ShareAlike terms. Have the distribution owner verify the relationship between that audio adaptation and the game package, rather than assuming the license changes the status of unrelated game code.

A public-domain marker is a status statement, not a new grant from every possible rights holder. Composition, words, arrangement, performance, recording, scan or transfer, and redistribution basis are separate evidence fields. Unresolved layers remain unresolved in the production manifest.
