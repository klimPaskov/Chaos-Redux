# Unit audio sources and role coverage

## Status and selection standard

The canonical 3D pipeline agent researched fourteen source-page leads and completed one targeted follow-up for voice and tracked movement. The parent reviewed the handoff and independently reopened the source records. No audio was downloaded, auditioned, edited, converted, hashed, generated, or wired. All usable-looking entries remain candidates, with audible fit and actual rights-chain acceptance pending.

Page-level CC0 statements do not verify third-party source ownership. The [CC0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) identifies the limits of that statement. Preserve the actual file and source page before production acceptance. Unidentified video extracts, unnamed composite components, and unsuitable equipment remain unresolved or rejected even when an uploader marks the page CC0.

## Native routing and synchronization

Inspect the exact installed entity, animation, soundeffect, sound, and voice consumer before assigning a file. The project workflow warns that infantry selection voices can be tag-level. A mixed army's ordinary human troops must not acquire all of the custom host's voices through a broad override. Per-subunit selection is a capability to verify, not an assumption.

| Role | Required synchronization proof |
| --- | --- |
| Selection or acknowledgement | Exact native selection event, correct family or tag scope, no substitution with a spawn or idle event |
| Foot and hoof movement | Measured contacts in each actual gait, distinct surface behavior when supported, no invented frame numbers |
| Idle and engine | State-specific start and stop, verified clean loops, separate moving and stationary behavior where available |
| Ranged attack | Exact discharge frame and actual muzzle or effect locator, with matching weapon recording |
| Tool or blade attack | Swing separated from contact, impact at the real strike point, grip and recovery aligned with the sound |
| Special action | A source appropriate to the actual repair, command, charge, or industrial action, stopped on interruption |
| Death and destruction | Human body, horse, engine, armor, and debris treated as distinct outcomes, synchronized to collapse and contact |

Use the final real skeletal actions to choose phases and frames. Do not derive timing from concept art, name one still pose as an animation, or bind one noise to every state. The map-scale mix should remain readable during several simultaneous engagements.

## Required production evidence

The 3D owner keeps immutable originals under the deterministic unit job evidence root, with exact source and download URL, title, creator and performers, source provenance, license version, permitted transformations, acquisition date, source checksum, original duration and format, audible suitability, and remaining uncertainty. No hash can be supplied before the file exists.

Only permitted mechanical trimming, fading, silence removal, normalization, channel conversion, resampling, and codec conversion may derive the final audio. Do not generate, synthesize, record, or manually compose a new final sound. Respect creator requests relevant to the task. No model training is involved in this package.

The expected voice target is signed PCM16 at 44.1 kHz mono under the supplied workflow. Inspect the actual consumer before final conversion and apply its documented requirements to other sound roles. Preserve a recipe and derivative checksum. Validate channel handling, loudness, clipping, loop boundaries, and synchronization against the final animation.

The parent owns final sound definitions, wrappers, entity and animation binding, provider cleanup, and runtime integration. The handoff names proposed stable identifiers only after the actual unit and consumer identifiers are settled. Installed reference, native routing, final source approval, actual file preparation, animation timing, and live-game acceptance remain open production gates. A missing role stays blocked until resolved through its owner.
