# Super-event text research and selections

## Status and role

These are researched candidates for the three required super-event roles. They are not a completed localisation file. The exact excerpt appears only once in the table below, and the recommendation sections refer to its candidate ID to avoid repeated quotation. All surrounding interpretation is original planning analysis.

Attribution is verified against the cited source text. Translation and release rights remain separately qualified. A source page's rights statement is recorded as its claim, not presented as a worldwide legal determination.

## Opening recommendation

Select Q1. It describes revolution as already spreading, which matches the actual committed worldwide opening and Event 001 continuity. Lenin speaks at the founding congress of an international organization, so the line supports the formation of a country and coalition as well as the visual reveal.

Q2 is the main backup. It communicates a new historical scale but is less specific about the spread of revolution. Q3 is a second backup whose certainty may fit the returned Lenin's public claim, though its confidence is stronger than the opening's actual strategic position.

The surrounding event copy should say that the uprisings are real and simultaneous. It should not treat the quote as proof that the World Revolution is guaranteed to win. The dynamic title uses the frozen current month, without inserting an extra word or changing the user's January Revolution through December Revolution mapping.

## Red World recommendation

Select Q4 as an intentionally unsettling use of an international revolutionary thesis at a fictional terminal threshold. Trotsky's historical argument concerns global development. It is not an endorsement of this scenario's coerced annexation or dehumanization.

That distinction is especially important because the autonomous Trotsky route can oppose forced incorporation. The quote belongs to the event's theme of world-scale revolution. The surrounding copy must not make Trotsky celebrate every possible Lenin route or imply that his country is now subject to Influence.

Q3 is the more triumphal backup. Q5 was considered for ideological contrast but is not recommended for the global terminal role, because it concerns construction within one country and the worker and peasant alliance. It belongs more naturally in Stalin-route research.

At activation, the World Revolution rules strictly more than half of the frozen inhabited-world measure through the specified qualifying forms of rule. Independent voluntary allies are excluded. The event does not claim that every country or person is already absorbed.

## Earned defeat recommendation

Select Q6 for a reflective aftermath. Its source concerns the careful repair of a deficient state apparatus. Using it after the supernatural command network falls creates an apt reversal and supports reconstruction rather than another triumphal march.

Q7 is the backup when the desired emphasis is the danger of centralized personal command. It is an early organizational polemic, not a prophecy of the later Lenin cult or of this fictional event. It carries more translation uncertainty and a more argumentative tone.

The final presentation should show survivors and unresolved political choices. It should not say that communism has been erased, every converted person has died, or every nation has been restored automatically.

## Production handoff

The localisation owner verifies the final chosen wording against the exact source, checks translation permissions and any required credit, and writes complete role-specific text using the implemented facts. The super-event owner pairs that text with its own image and unique audio cue. A provisional quotation does not make audio, portrait rights, or registration complete.

The parent review corrected a researcher attribution error for Q3 by checking the source's actual section, corrected the research draft's state-count interpretation of Red World to the accepted inhabited-world measure, and restored the exact current-month title mapping. These corrections are included here so they cannot re-enter implementation through an older research draft.
