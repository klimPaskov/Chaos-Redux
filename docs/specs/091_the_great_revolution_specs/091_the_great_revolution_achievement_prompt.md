## The Revolution Devours Its Children

Eligible country: an ordinary communist government that explicitly joined Lenin, excluding the World Revolution and permanently protected Trotsky route. Description direction: accept help, endure critical dependence, reclaim sovereignty and materially help defeat the host while remaining communist.

Unlock requires the actual join receipt, at least one crossing into critical Influence, free independence before completed annexation, continuous communist identity from joining through the qualifying defeat, and a material defeat contribution. A qualifying contribution is permanent command destruction, recovery and sustained control of an opening base, or participation in the operation resolving the body. Use the common recorded contribution owner, with thirty continuous days for the recovered-base proof as initial tuning.

Disqualify completed annexation before the break, an unauthorized tag switch used to substitute the World Revolution, loss of required continuous ideology or lack of actual contribution. Do not require waiting at 100 Influence or surrendering before independence. The difficulty comes from a real dependence-and-breakaway campaign. Track player-country lineage, join, critical episode, independence, ideology transitions and defeat participation. Icon direction: a party document breaking from an organic eye.

## Socialism in One Country

Eligible country: the Soviet Union with Stalin as the actual current leader at the committed opening. Description direction: preserve the human Soviet state's command and capital against Lenin's returned movement.

Unlock requires mandatory refusal, continuous control of the actual frozen Soviet capital state and material contribution to defeating the body and survival network. A later capital move does not change the target. Losing other states is permitted. Disqualify loss of control of that target, a different opening leader or nonparticipation in defeat. Stalin need not be falsely kept alive forever if normal later character history changes, but the opening identity and continuous capital proof remain binding.

Track actual Stalin identity, current leadership at commit, frozen capital, every relevant control transition, refusal and contribution. This is not a trivial ideology switch. Icon direction: a defended Soviet capital with the Third Eye's shadow broken, grounded in the campaign's actual capital context.

## Permanent Revolution

Eligible country: the Soviet Union with Trotsky as the actual current leader at opening and its accepted autonomous cofounder receipt. Description direction: sustain independent international cooperation across continents while Lenin's actual ruled world expands.

Unlock requires continued autonomy and partnership, two completed materially contributed joint operations on two distinct inhabited continents, and Lenin's actual rule reaching the fifty-percent inhabited-world milestone. Use the same frozen population-weighted denominator and actual ruling attribution as the specification. Red World activation and its Chaos threshold are not required. Trotsky's own autonomous territory contributes zero to the rule numerator.

Qualifying operations have real military or organizing results, such as a supplied command establishment, liberation of an actual strategic region for the coalition, or an uprising surviving its first defense objective. Disqualify a political break before completion, duplicated target missions or purely symbolic participation. No Influence, puppeting, forced conversion or surrender requirement is allowed. Track opening leader, protected relationship, operation identities, continent identities, contributions and the milestone receipt. Icon direction: separate revolutionary banners cooperating without being absorbed.

## Workers of Every World

Eligible country: the actual World Revolution player. Description direction: build a functioning revolutionary command on every inhabited continental region and carry that network into the terminal order.

Unlock requires all six commands functioning at actual Red World activation: Europe, Asia, Africa, North America, South America and Oceania. Each needs its real site, regional mission, institution and logistics, with no permanently destroyed slot. A focus click, brief port capture or nominal commander is insufficient. Disqualify an absent or nonfunctional command at terminal commit or a player not controlling the World Revolution.

The challenge is six different geographic campaigns and a real terminal gate. All six remain an achievement condition only, never a general world-end requirement. A total-conversion map needs an approved documented continental equivalent, with empty geometry excluded. Track player identity, all actual command generations and operational predicates, and the terminal receipt. Icon direction: six inhabited command regions around the central eye.

## The Eye Closes

Eligible country: a real opposing participant that materially helps defeat Lenin. Description direction: overcome the mature transformed international and preserve survivors.

Unlock requires actual apotheosis history, at least three distinct transformed foreign leaders when the decisive defeat operation is accepted, and full valid defeat of the body and network. Freeze those distinct leader and country identities at the accepted operation. A cure or liberation achieved during that operation does not invalidate their prior qualifying transformation.

Disqualify cosmetic-only apotheosis, temporary capital occupation, a stale or aborted operation used for proof, fewer than three valid leaders at acceptance, or no material player participation. Do not reward killing leaders or obstructing cures. Track power history, accepted operation, valid transformed leader set, contribution and defeat. Icon direction: an organic eye closing over surviving human faces.

## Human Comrades

Eligible country: a communist refuser or breakaway that actually leads a human communist coalition. Description direction: defeat Lenin while sustaining an independent human international.

Unlock requires real coalition leadership, a completed joint defense, rescue or command-destruction objective, full defeat, and at least three independent communist governments including the player at the final settlement. Members must have refused or broken away, retain actual sovereign institutions and have a real protected wartime history. A valid faction or a recorded coordinated agreement can qualify without forcing all defenders into one faction.

Disqualify dependent puppet shells, merely symbolic agreements, no material leadership, loss of player communism or last-day releases manufactured for the count. Use at least ninety days of actual independent cooperation for counted partner history as initial tuning, allowing genuine native continuations rather than a fresh release to inherit a nonexistent record. Track member identity and lineage, independence, ideology, cooperation interval, leadership, objective and settlement. Icon direction: distinct councils standing together after the host falls.

## No State Is an Island

Eligible country: an ordinary opening participant with a real continuing government or valid native exile. Description direction: recover from losing most of the original homeland during the opening and restore the complete recorded loss set.

Unlock requires actual committed loss of strictly more than half the country's frozen eligible owned states, survival through a legitimate native continuation, and later recovery of every state in that original loss set with legitimate ownership or an approved restoration settlement and friendly control outside Lenin's binding rule. Occupied foreign states do not inflate the homeland denominator. Retained allied bases and merely reserved transfers are not losses.

Disqualify duplicate states, a false exile without native existence, a temporary allied occupation that leaves Lenin's binding claim intact, an incomplete recovery set or no material player restoration role. The challenge is real military and institutional recovery, not release-and-reannex bookkeeping. Track original owned set, committed losses, continuing player lineage, every actual restoration and participation. Icon direction: isolated homeland regions reconnected by a surviving national outline.

## First Among None

Eligible country: a material opposing participant in the final campaign. Description direction: dismantle the distributed network before taking its central body.

Unlock requires at least three distinct continental commands ever formed and every actually instantiated command permanently destroyed before Lenin's central body is first captured or destroyed during the event episode. Snapshot the inventory at acceptance, then recheck the complete actual formed inventory at body resolution. A command formed later cannot disappear from the requirement.

Disqualify premature body capture, including an earlier capture followed by a return, temporary disabled commands, a reappearing supposedly destroyed command, an unqualified external body-destruction shortcut or no material player participation. Track formation and permanent-destruction receipts, command generations, acceptance snapshot, later formations and exact body-resolution order. Icon direction: broken command nodes surrounding an unresolved center. A campaign with no distributed network cannot earn it.
