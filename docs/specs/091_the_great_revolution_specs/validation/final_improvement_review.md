# Event 091 fresh near-completion improvement review

## Closure recommendation

Stop broad design expansion.
The assembled specification has enough playable depth, connected routes, consequences, counterplay, country detail, AI intent, asset direction, endings and aftermath to proceed to package delivery.
Another mechanic, public value, political philosophy, unit family, command family, management window or super-event would add maintenance and cognitive burden without resolving a demonstrated gap.
No expansion addendum is recommended.

This closes the design-depth loop for the planning goal only.
It does not certify gameplay implementation, native feasibility, numerical balance, art, audio, models, animation or live-game acceptance.
The package must retain its explicit implementation gates and To Be Reworked catalog status.

## Review scope

This was the mandatory fresh near-completion review, separate from the closed initial planner pass.
It covered all fourteen design chapters, the technical handoffs and the research handoffs, and confirmed that the earlier country and role proposal was incorporated with its stale rules reconciled.
No earlier accepted planner addendum remains unresolved.

## Gates to carry into implementation

The actual tool inventory exposes no HOI4 tools.
This review has no native focus, event, probability, map, GUI, technology or runtime evidence.
Tool exposure, connected service health and standalone Technology Tree Viewer availability remain separate future questions.
The Linux planning environment does not establish whether that viewer exists in the user's Windows installation.

The existing feasibility handoff remains authoritative for the material open gates: split owner and controller rights, sovereign activation, single-Lenin continuity, installed tag and DLC compatibility, conservative military and population transactions, native clocks and receipts, complete AI scenarios, focus and technology rendering, current-leader overlays, actual model and animation production, source acceptance and audition, and live-game review.
Candidate recordings and portrait records remain research candidates, not completed assets.

During implementation, preserve the existing distinction between a regional campaign's real logistical and political proof, its command-establishment operation, and its focus capstone.
Do not accidentally require an already active command to begin the same command's establishment path.
Likewise, a single-base origin, absent foreign partner, landlocked origin or missing historical leader must keep the explicitly defined domestic and conditional routes available.
These are checks against the current accepted design, not requests for extra mechanics.
