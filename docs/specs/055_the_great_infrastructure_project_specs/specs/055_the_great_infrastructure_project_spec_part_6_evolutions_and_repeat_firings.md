# Event 55 Specification, Part 6

## Evolutions and Repeat Firings

## Evolution principle

The event's evolutions expand what the national works program can attempt. They should not replace the baseline system with unrelated bonuses.

Each evolution changes candidate generation, project capacity, construction methods, route difficulty, and permanent project value.

Evolution activation itself gives no Chaos. The threshold makes the evolution eligible, then normal evolution pacing determines when it becomes active.

The working base pacing target is about `90` days after the evolution becomes eligible. The actual timing can respond to successful projects, engineering experience, current active work, and the number of countries with Event 55 programs.

A disabled evolution must not set its recorded flag or unlock its content. Baseline project progression must continue without it.

## Evolution I: Infrastructure Mania

### Requirement

- Chaos: `200+`

### Promise

Governments stop treating the first infrastructure miracle as a unique national achievement. Rail authorities, road ministries, contractors, and military planners begin treating permanent expansion as an ordinary instrument of policy.

Existing national networks are upgraded more aggressively. Countries can supervise larger programs and attempt routes through terrain that baseline planners would avoid.

### System changes

Evolution I should:

- reduce survey refresh cooldown
- reduce normal survey duration
- lower the National Works Capacity threshold for ordinary railways and highways
- reduce normal railway and highway construction duration
- reduce selected equipment costs where institutional experience would realistically help
- allow a second active project for a country in the highest capacity stage
- unlock difficult terrain variants for national railways and highways
- heavily upgrade selected existing railway trunks when a new railway project extends them
- improve maintenance and repair speed for completed baseline projects
- add strategic radar support at a small number of important project nodes when useful

Radar should appear only at major ports, bridge approaches, tunnel approaches, frontier junctions, or high-value logistics nodes. It should not spread to every project state.

### Candidate changes

New candidate roles include:

- mountain and desert railways
- polar or severe-winter trunks
- long frontier highways
- national port and rail integration projects
- large resource corridors through difficult interiors
- allied cross-border routes with two or three participants

### Risk changes

Evolution I makes ambition easier but can create overcommitment.

A country with two active projects receives a larger capacity penalty and higher overrun risk when its economy cannot sustain both.

The player should be able to use the new slot carefully. Evolution I should not make two projects automatically optimal.

### Repeat firing interaction

A repeat Event 55 firing after Evolution I can:

- upgrade one active railway or highway milestone
- provide an emergency national survey
- repair one damaged route stage
- temporarily reduce the burden of a second project

It should not duplicate the opening maximum infrastructure grant.

## Evolution II: Engineering Without Limits

### Requirement

- Chaos: `400+`

### Promise

Projects that had remained studies, sketches, or political fantasies become practical national options. Governments accept severe terrain, longer construction, and specialized engineering as normal parts of strategic planning.

### System changes

Evolution II should:

- unlock the curated fixed crossing registry
- unlock the largest great bridges and underwater tunnels
- unlock extreme mountain, desert, jungle, marsh, and arctic alignments
- allow two active projects for a capable recipient
- reduce penalties from difficult terrain without removing them
- improve survey accuracy
- add stronger radar and defensive logistics at selected major nodes
- unlock conservative safety and long-term maintenance bonuses for extreme works
- unlock repair and reinforcement projects for fixed links
- allow grand ports to become major multinational terminals

### Candidate changes

New candidate roles include:

- underwater fixed links
- hybrid bridge and tunnel systems
- very long mountain railways
- desert trunk railways
- major strait bridges
- island-mainland connections
- long international resource corridors
- multinational grand ports

Every fixed link remains dependent on a validated map registry. Evolution II unlocks eligible entries. It does not make every narrow-looking sea gap valid.

### Costs and risks

Evolution II projects can be much more expensive and take several years.

The evolution should not make them universally cheaper than baseline projects. It makes them possible, improves planning, and reduces some technical failure risk.

Extreme works can require scheduled overhaul after completion. A conservative method can reduce that burden. An accelerated method can produce an early Strained opening state.

### Radar support

Important fixed-link approaches, grand ports, and border nodes can gain radar when it serves navigation, logistics, air defense, or route security.

Radar placement remains bounded and tied to real nodes.

### Repeat firing interaction

A repeat Event 55 firing after Evolution II can:

- unlock one extreme candidate immediately
- fund a major survey on a registered crossing
- rescue a suspended extreme project
- reduce one scheduled overhaul burden
- strengthen one major port or crossing node

The repeat should follow current need. It should not give a random extreme project to a country that lacks valid geography.

## Evolution III: The World Connected

### Requirement

- Chaos: `600+`

### Promise

The project system expands from national prestige works into continental transport planning. Several countries can join their railways, highways, ports, bridges, and tunnels into one operating network.

### System changes

Evolution III should:

- unlock continental network proposals
- allow projects with three or more sovereign participants
- allow linked chains of national projects to become one network
- allow sequential segment opening
- allow a third active project for an experienced country in the highest capacity stage
- reduce survey refresh cooldown again
- improve partner negotiation for countries already linked by completed projects
- create a multinational operating authority for major networks
- unlock network-level repair, rerouting, and emergency priority decisions
- allow linked port networks and strait chains
- strengthen famine relief, migration transport, and allied logistics through operational networks

### Candidate changes

New candidate roles include:

- continent-spanning railway systems
- continent-spanning highway systems
- international freight corridors
- chains of bridges and tunnels across several straits
- major port networks connected by rail
- routes linking several allied economies
- multi-country resource and industrial systems
- continental relief arteries

### Network entry requirements

A normal Evolution III network should require:

- at least three meaningful nodes
- at least three countries, or one exceptionally large country with several completed Event 55 trunks
- a continuous or clearly multimodal route
- a qualified host
- high National Works Capacity
- no active war among core participants
- partner contributions
- a completed national project or equivalent route base in the host

A network should not appear to a weak isolated country merely because global Chaos reached `600`.

### Segment construction

Continental networks can open in stages.

A completed segment gives local benefits and remains part of the active project. The network does not count as fully complete until every required core segment and operating agreement is active.

A failed outer segment can lead to reduced completion when the remaining route still connects several major nodes.

### Network vulnerability

A network can be stronger than individual routes because traffic can reroute. It can also create shared dependence.

A central bridge, tunnel, port, or border node can become a single point of failure. The network should identify critical nodes and allow the host to invest in alternate routes.

### Global report

The first fully operational network linking at least three sovereign participants should receive a global news event.

The report should focus on trains, freight, workers, ports, and borders opening along the route. It should not use world-ending language or claim that every country is connected.

Later networks need only national or participant reports unless they achieve a distinct rare variant.

### Repeat firing interaction

A repeat Event 55 firing after Evolution III can:

- generate one continental network proposal
- add a missing partner or segment to an existing network
- fund emergency repair of a critical node
- open a network rerouting survey
- strengthen the multinational authority after a dispute

A country without a valid network can still receive a national assistance outcome.

## Evolution timing factors

Evolution timing should use named scenarios and later probability audit evidence.

Possible accelerators include:

- several completed Event 55 projects worldwide
- the current recipient has completed a project
- the current recipient has an active project at a high milestone
- a relevant high-difficulty project exists
- several friendly countries already operate linked corridors

Possible delays include:

- no country has completed a baseline project
- most Event 55 programs are suspended
- the relevant evolution is disabled
- the world lacks any valid candidate family for the evolution

The activation should still be a global event evolution, not a separate evolution per country.

## High-Chaos first firing

When Event 55 first targets a country after one or more evolutions are already active, the country receives:

- the normal one-time maximum infrastructure grant
- the baseline national works program
- candidate access appropriate to active evolutions
- capacity and slot rules appropriate to active evolutions

It does not need to replay obsolete lower-tier evolution popups.

The global Event 55 evolution log should already contain the active milestones. The country simply enters the current version of the system.

## Repeatable event purpose

Repeatability serves three goals:

1. Spread the national works program to countries that have not received it.
2. Give a useful intervention to a country that already has the program.
3. Create new project opportunities when the world, geography, or evolution state has changed.

It should not repeat the opening grant for the same country.

## Repeat target weighting

Suggested relative target priorities for later implementation and probability audit:

| Target state | Relative design priority |
| --- | --- |
| Valid country that never received Event 55 | Very high |
| Existing recipient with damaged or suspended major project | Medium |
| Existing recipient with no active project and stale proposal set | Low to medium |
| Existing recipient with active project and no urgent problem | Low |
| Country selected within the recent-target cooldown | Zero |
| Invalid special or nonhuman country | Zero |
| Fully capitulated country | Zero |

A repeat firing should normally find an unserved country while many remain.

## Repeat outcome selection

When the selected country already has the program, the outcome should match its current state.

### Damaged project assistance

Priority when a completed or active project is Severed, Partially Disrupted, or suspended by physical damage.

The firing can create an emergency repair grant, reduce the repair timer, or cover one major equipment requirement.

### Construction breakthrough

Priority when a normal active project is progressing and no emergency exists.

The firing can advance one milestone, shorten the remaining duration within a cap, or remove one overrun state.

### New survey campaign

Priority when the country has no active project or only stale proposals.

The firing creates a fresh diverse candidate set and a temporary National Works Capacity increase.

### International opening

Priority when a later evolution is active and the country has valid partners but no multinational route.

The firing creates one strong international proposal and improves initial negotiation willingness for a limited period.

### Maintenance renewal

Priority when several completed projects are Strained.

The firing can start a national maintenance campaign that improves repair and operating status without creating free new construction.

## Repeat reward limits

A repeat firing should respect these limits:

- no second maximum infrastructure grant for the same country
- no automatic project completion from an early stage
- no duplicate project slot
- no free fixed crossing
- no project assistance when the selected project or route is invalid
- no salvage or equipment grant larger than the project's remaining need
- no bypass of partner consent
- no reset of unrelated project failure consequences

## Evolution and Event Log direction

Each evolution needs one Event Logs evolution entry with:

- Event 55 identity
- evolution type and stage
- Chaos tier presentation
- activation date
- no actor unless the milestone belongs to a specific country

The Event Details evolution preview should describe the expanded project possibilities and world behavior. It should not display fake dates or history indexes.

Repeat firings remain ordinary Event 55 history entries with the selected actor. Their detail can distinguish first program establishment, repair assistance, survey renewal, construction breakthrough, or international opening.

## Evolution acceptance criteria

The evolution system is acceptable only when:

- the thresholds are `200`, `400`, and `600`
- activation uses evolution pacing instead of an immediate threshold switch unless Event 55 has not fired yet and the established framework supports pre-firing escalation
- activation gives zero Chaos
- disabled evolutions set no recorded or unlock flags
- Evolution I expands national capacity and difficult routes
- Evolution II unlocks only validated extreme engineering and fixed crossings
- Evolution III creates real multi-country network objects
- high-Chaos first recipients enter the current system cleanly
- repeat firings help current project state without duplicating the opening grant
- every evolution updates candidates, AI, assets, Event Details, logs, and documentation
