# Doctor Wu: National Medical Ascendancy

## When the institute becomes a national service

A hospital outside the institute can now begin a recognized Wu treatment without waiting for his personal visit. Its physicians know the applicable method, its stores contain the right material, and its appointment gives them authority to act. Another hospital has the same instructions but lacks a qualified team. A military service has reserved the next shipment. These differences determine how much of the country benefits from National Medical Ascendancy.

The first evolution makes Wu's work part of national medical administration. Hospitals, teaching institutions, treatment works, field medicine, and emergency services acquire standing relationships with the institute. The government decides who appoints their medical leaders, which commitments take priority, and how much of the network can function when Wu cannot answer. The practical advantage is faster use of completed medicine across a larger, prepared network. Its corresponding risk is that a shared dependency or compromised instruction can interrupt many participating services together.

The evolution changes the organization around the portfolio in Parts 1–11. Cure development, enhancement, replication, designed life, and modified capabilities retain their existing prerequisites and costs. A country with only basic medicine gains national organization for basic medicine. A country with an advanced portfolio has more difficult services to distribute and protect.

All new numerical anchors, action labels, timing factors, and institutional procedures in this part are proposed design. The working labels identify content for implementation and are not finished localisation.

## The national turn

### Active-chain eligibility

National Medical Ascendancy becomes eligible at **200 Chaos** while the host has a working institute and a living, available Wu who can exercise its medical role. A qualified continuity copy may fill that role under Part 7. A captive person who has not accepted an appointment cannot supply it.

One of three developments must also have occurred. A major cure has produced an accepted medical result, a funded institute expansion has entered actual service, or separate national services have repeatedly relied on Wu. Announcing a project, moving a portrait, copying an archive, ordering stock, or displaying a large budget is insufficient.

The proposed major-cure test accepts either a disease-owner-confirmed end to a substantial domestic episode or confirmed treatment of at least **1,000,000 living people** or **25 percent of the host's eligible living population**, whichever population test is easier to reach. The percentage uses a recorded population basis from the episode and does not become easier through unrelated deaths or the loss of populated territory. An owner-confirmed major episode can qualify without meeting either population test. The small contained arrival demonstration does not qualify by itself.

The expansion test accepts a completed clinical, teaching, or production extension that has taken on a real service commitment beyond the initial demonstration. It does not require a large country or a second state. A one-state host can add a second medical function in its existing location, provided the staff and industrial capacity are distinct. A renamed room or the same team reassigned between two descriptions is not an expansion.

Repeated reliance requires **two distinct completed service episodes** with national importance. Two ministries can rely on the same institute, but splitting one delivery, renewing one course, or reporting the same patients twice does not create two episodes. Each qualifying use identifies the service, the reason Wu was needed, and the accepted result. Repeated treatment of a genuinely new outbreak can qualify. Routine automatic maintenance cannot.

A low-Authority public physician can reach this evolution. Authority changes the settlement that follows, not access to the evolution or any research family. A classified institute can also qualify through documented internal use without publishing its military work. Its first national arrangement may be a compartmentalized medical service whose existence is public and whose sensitive departments remain restricted.

### Contextual MTTH

The proposed active-chain timing anchor is **90 days of MTTH** after eligibility is established. The value is a pacing parameter, not a guaranteed 90-day appointment and not proof of the expected waiting time in a particular game version.

Apply each relevant factor once to the anchor, then round the nominal result upward to a multiple of five and clamp it to **30–240 days**. Where two entries describe the same cause, use the stronger entry for that cause. Do not count the same episode twice.

| Current cause | Proposed factor | Required basis |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| A completed major cure has prompted actual requests to extend its use | 0.75 | Accepted cure result and a current requesting service. |
| A funded regional or second-function expansion is already serving recipients | 0.80 | Completed expansion and a live commitment. |
| An active outbreak is demanding coordinated work from several medical functions | 0.65 | Current disease-owner context and actual participating functions. |
| Repeated national reliance has left a continuing coordination problem | 0.85 | Distinct reliance episodes and an unresolved allocation or appointment need. |
| Wartime casualty services are competing with civilian care | 0.85 | Recorded demand from both services, not war status alone. |
| The host is conducting a genuine review of the proposed national powers | 1.50 | A live institutional review with a defined outcome. |
| A necessary connecting site or route is interrupted | 1.25 | Current interruption affecting the prospective service. |
| Wu's only national sponsor is politically unable to conclude the settlement | 1.25 | An unresolved sponsor or appointment dispute. |

A major cure with a functioning expansion gives a nominal value of **55 days** after rounding. A contested settlement with an interrupted route gives **170 days**. Neither calculation claims an observed in-game frequency. A fully lost institute suspends eligibility instead of producing an extreme timing factor.

The timing calculation responds to changed conditions. A completed review removes its delaying factor. An ended outbreak removes its urgency factor. Falling below 200 Chaos suspends an undelivered evolution. Earlier elapsed time does not become a payable research reward, and repeated threshold crossings do not create additional scheduled copies of the incident.

Only the event's current responsible host can own the pending delivery. A transfer, capture, death, or new appointment revalidates that responsibility. The arriving successor inherits the actual outstanding situation, not a second independent roll for each Wu. The exact game-version timing adapter and its treatment of changing eligibility require probability inspection and comparison before implementation acceptance.

### What happens at the first delivery

The incident concerns existing requests and the people already delivering care. A hospital has adopted a known procedure, the army seeks a standing allocation, a provincial service wants recognition, or a treatment works wants a national contract. The government must settle how these commitments will relate to the institute.

Activation records National Medical Ascendancy once and changes its institutional context. It grants **zero Chaos**, no treatment stock, no completed research, no civilian population, no manpower, and no extra research lane. Any earlier medical result retains its own consequence history. The evolution does not pay for that result a second time.

The immediate service map contains only proven existing facilities, teams, stocks, appointments, and contracts. A prepared provider may begin using a standing authorization as soon as the relevant settlement is valid. An unprepared provider receives an admission or expansion task. Its promised coverage remains visibly unfulfilled until the work is done.

## The first national settlement

The government chooses an allocation authority and the scope of its powers. These are national arrangements within the existing mandate, not replacement mandates or technology branches. Public medicine can use centralized procurement. A military mandate can protect civilian hospitals. A classified directorate can let regional doctors reproduce harmless treatments without opening its restricted departments.

Four responses define the first settlement. Each preserves current care and national institutional rights while resolving who may make the next decisions.

### A publicly accountable national service

The health administration coordinates participating hospitals, treatment works, universities, and military liaison. Wu becomes its principal medical adviser or director under the host's existing appointment. He can define the medical requirements of his methods, but public officials control admission, procurement, and the distribution of non-reserved supplies within the agreed charter.

This arrangement gives the government the clearest route to a service that can outlast him. Known medicine can move between qualified hospitals through common admission rules. Provincial physicians can reject an incompatible treatment without surrendering their entire appointment. A local failure can be investigated by another qualified service.

The cost is the time and staff needed to train those services and preserve their records. The government cannot announce independent medicine and immediately remove every dependence on Wu. Methods still known only to him remain personal until the existing replication, teaching, or verification work makes them reproducible.

The opening response approves the national charter and identifies its first participating providers. It does not finish their qualification. Where the same public administration already owns a valid mandate, the charter recognizes that authority without granting it again. Existing emergency powers still need their own expiry or reversal.

### A personal national directorate

Wu receives final medical appointment authority for the participating network and the right to redirect its uncommitted medical resources within the charter. He can settle an ordinary clinical disagreement without a second ministry review and can send an established method through the network quickly.

The concession is concrete. Hospitals accept appointments made through his office, central works take production priorities from it, and emergency services rely on his interpretation of disputed cases. These powers create an actual national bottleneck. A government order to stop a programme may require cooperation from administrators whose current positions depend on him.

The proposed Authority consequence is **+10** when the government grants a new national appointment concession and **+10** when it separately grants a new national allocation concession. A charter that contains both can apply both, but only to rights not already held. No increase occurs merely because the evolution is recorded. These are the same kinds of rights considered in Part 2, so overlapping concessions are reconciled instead of stacked.

Wu's faster decisions do not increase physical output or make him present at several hospitals simultaneously. He can delegate routine application to trained staff. Unique diagnosis and unresolved experimental decisions still compete for his attention or that of a separately qualified continuity copy.

### A federation of recognized institutes

Regional hospitals and universities keep their own appointments, stocks, and qualified methods. They recognize a shared medical standard and exchange patients, records, and assistance through agreements with the national institute. Wu coordinates exceptional cases and may chair a joint clinical review, but he does not become the employer of every participating physician.

A disruption at the capital need not close a distant regional service. A qualified regional works can continue producing its existing standard while the central institute repairs an archive or settles a leadership dispute. The federation also gives the government more than one institution capable of checking a proposed change.

Its disadvantages are separate qualification work, more public knowledge of medical practice, and less freedom to divert another region's committed supplies. A province can have a sound reason to refuse a request because its own recipients still need treatment. A ministry must negotiate that refusal or fund another provider. It cannot declare the disagreement cured by raising a coordination score.

Choosing the federation does not establish new independent institutes. It recognizes those that already qualify and offers a funded route for the others. Personal Authority falls only as actual appointments, reproducible methods, or allocations cease to depend on Wu. Each completed transfer of an existing national concession removes the amount attached to that concession, with the clamped application history preserved.

### Keep national expansion under case-by-case approval

The government accepts that Wu's methods now affect national decisions but refuses a standing power to enroll hospitals or redirect supplies. Every large extension remains an explicit commission. Existing public and military medicine continues. Requests for national action can be approved individually through the baseline service and research routes.

This response preserves control without destroying the institute. It is viable for a small host, a government recovering from a failed protocol, or a country whose medical network is still too thin for a national charter. Its cost is that simultaneous requests compete for individual administrative decisions and may miss opportunities that a prepared standing service could have met.

The response settles the evolution's initial political question. It does not create repeated demands every month. A new national review needs a materially new cause, such as another functioning provider, a serious unmet request, or a changed appointment. The player can later adopt a standing arrangement through the ordinary paid reorganization procedure without replaying the evolution.

## The institutions that participate

A national medical service is made from identifiable participants. A participating hospital has a location, a qualified service, a responsible appointment, and a budget or supply commitment. A university has teaching staff and a defined curriculum. A treatment works has its actual production standard and output. An army medical service has recipients, assigned personnel, and continuing obligations.

Existing institutions retain their owners. A university nationalized through an earlier route is not turned into Wu's private property by a medical agreement. A Kruger facility does not become an unoccupied Wu laboratory. A foreign-funded hospital retains the terms of its funding, and a captured site cannot be admitted solely because it has a laboratory name.

### Hospitals and receiving services

A hospital can join for one or several specific services. Examples are a particular cure, advanced field medicine, rehabilitation, care for enhanced veterans, or a supported nonhuman medical service. Admission to one service is not qualification for the others.

The first participating hospital establishes the immediate working standard. Later hospitals can be admitted through a paid transfer of the relevant instructions, training, and inspection. Their staff must demonstrate the capability that the service requires. They do not need a second discovery of a cure already documented, but they do need the ability to use it safely and support its patients.

A hospital with a valid method but no delivery stock is prepared and unsupplied. A hospital with stock but no valid clinical team is stocked and unqualified. Neither is counted as providing the completed treatment. The national board should make the missing condition clear enough for the player to choose between supply, training, a visiting team, or another destination.

A receiving hospital also shares real space, food, and transport with other arrivals. Its medical accreditation does not reserve unlimited housing. Survivors, migrants, displaced existing citizens, and people awaiting first settlement use the same proven receiving places where their owners require them.

### Universities and teaching hospitals

The national service needs physicians who can explain a method without invoking Wu's personal authority. A university appointment therefore names the material being taught and the level the institution can reproduce. A general lecture does not confer control over engineered formations or the ability to improvise a new cure.

The teaching route extends the existing baseline training and Part 11 visiting-scientist arrangements. It can train another local service, establish a second examiner for a known standard, or qualify a successor for a defined clinical role. Time assigned to teaching is unavailable for simultaneous full-time field service or research.

A chair can be publicly appointed, selected by participating regional institutions, or nominated by Wu within the chosen national charter. Those appointment methods change who can replace the chair and who can challenge the curriculum. They do not create different versions of the same scientist or free national research bonuses.

A dissenting faculty can preserve a validated older method when it rejects an unproven replacement. The government can retain that faculty, commission a bounded comparison, or replace its appointment. Replacing staff does not settle the medical question. The replacement team still needs qualification, and an unresolved incompatibility remains unresolved.

### Military medicine

The armed forces enter through the services they actually possess. Their claim may concern wounded soldiers, infection prevention, advanced field medicine, enhancement support, or the care and control of engineered formations. Each claim retains its own recipient and support requirements.

A military medical appointment decides who assesses fitness and provides treatment. It does not transfer operational command of a division to a hospital or to Wu. The high-Authority directorate can acquire influence through dependence, but an actual command transfer needs the separate authority and formation rules already established in Parts 6 and 10.

The army can offer transport, staff, protected facilities, or industrial time to secure additional service. Those are real commitments and may help the civilian network as part of an agreement. It cannot obtain a priority allocation by counting the same hospital convoy as both an army contribution and a national contribution.

### Treatment works and pharmaceutical contracts

A works is admitted for the standards it can actually manufacture. Its national contract identifies output, existing reservations, delivery destinations, maintenance, and who may order a future change. A production licence is distinct from the right to sell the material or appoint its users.

The national service can coordinate several works without pooling their stock into an additional inventory. A central warehouse is a physical destination with its own actual contents. A national list of supplies is a view of those holdings, not a second source from which another shipment can be drawn.

Ordinary cures, replacement clinical material, military support supplies, control treatments, and modified-capability stock remain distinct. The proposed limit of **two active modified production standards** applies across the whole country, including all regional works, Wu branches, and qualified copies. A federation does not receive two standards per province. Ordinary cure production and necessary care for earlier recipients remain outside that modified-standard ceiling.

## National medical reach

### Reach is specific to a service

The national service needs to distinguish where it is organized from where it is actually treating people. A charter can name an entire country while its first completed service reaches only a few hospitals. The government may promise broader access, but the promise is not a disease modifier.

For a selected disease and treatment version, actual reach is the unique living population receiving a valid, supplied service during the current service interval. The upper bound is the smallest of eligible recipients, available matching material, qualified provider capacity, accepted delivery capacity, and any receiving limit that applies. A geographic label cannot increase that bound.

Different purposes are not added into one medical total. Vaccination, active treatment, rehabilitation, and control support may concern the same person. National presentation may show their separate service status, but it must not count that person four times as evidence of population growth or four independently protected residents.

If two hospitals cover overlapping recipients with the same purpose and interval, the national view takes the union of those recipients. Where the owner can expose only an aggregate total without proof of overlap, the service retains an unresolved overlap. It cannot claim an exact union. The implementation must obtain the missing owner contract before promising precise national coverage.

### A worked coverage boundary

Consider a proposed allocation of **2.50 million** eligible people for one current disease. Hospital A can support **1.00 million**, hospital B **0.75 million**, and a military medical service **0.50 million**. A and B share **0.25 million** recipients already counted in the same treatment purpose. The military service's recipients are disjoint in this example.

Their combined capacity is therefore **2.00 million**, not 2.25 million. If matching delivered stock can cover only **1.75 million**, completed treatment reach cannot exceed 1.75 million. If a route closure then prevents 0.50 million of that delivery, the current supplied reach falls to **1.25 million** until the route or another valid source restores it.

None of these changes creates or removes a person. The disease owner still determines actual recovery, mortality, and the duration of protection. Previously recorded recoveries remain historical facts when current coverage expires. A national shortfall does not turn an already completed treatment into a second untreated person.

### Uneven territory

Connected industrial regions can share a dispatch service and stores. A remote island, occupied district, isolated enclave, or damaged inland area needs its own workable delivery and reception arrangement. The national service does not infer access from ownership, a port icon, or a faction relationship.

An occupied state's physicians may be medically qualified without accepting the occupying government's appointment. Clinical access and political control are recorded separately. A voluntary medical agreement can provide treatment under an independent institution. Coercion and the seizure of its staff or supplies retain their political and evidence consequences.

A one-state country remains a complete supported case. Its hospitals, military service, and teaching function can compete within the same state. The design must not create a fictional second province to meet a regional-network requirement. Conversely, a large country must not gain national reach merely because its capital has one capable hospital.

## Standing medical orders

The most important practical change is that a qualified service can act on an already authorized, completed method without requesting a fresh personal commission for each ordinary case. The standing order specifies the disease or medical purpose, permitted recipients, current standard, available material, responsible provider, and the limit of the delegated power.

A standing order can cover recognition of a supported outbreak, release of a pre-positioned treatment allocation, dispatch of an already assigned response team, or a routine continuation of known care. It cannot authorize a new experimental method, change the properties of stock, conscript a patient into a study, or cancel another provider's commitment.

### From recognition to treatment

A prepared hospital recognizes a case through its own supported detection service. Its standing authority then allows the existing treatment owner to validate and begin the matching response. Diagnostic uncertainty still takes time. Preparation, transport, treatment duration, and follow-up keep their existing clocks.

The proposed coordination benefit reduces only a separately identified **administrative mobilization interval by 25 percent**, while the competent dispatch office and its records remain available. A **20-day** administrative interval becomes **15 days**. A **30-day manufacturing interval remains 30 days**, and a 90-day clinical qualification remains 90 days. Where the underlying owner exposes no distinct administrative interval, do not shorten its whole timer and call the difference coordination. That specific numerical benefit remains unavailable until the owner supplies a supported seam.

Standing authorization still has value where no separate timing field exists. It replaces repeated approval with a paid, bounded authorization under the same owner checks. A trained hospital can respond while Wu is occupied elsewhere. Its actual capacity and material are still required for every response.

Coordination savings use the strongest applicable administrative reduction for that interval. They do not multiply with the same staff's teaching bonus, a copied protocol, a regional charter, and a second Wu. Existing research and clinical rate limits remain unchanged. This benefit does not shorten the N01–N12 institution-building and review durations.

### What local physicians may decide

Under a public or regional charter, physicians may apply a validated method, refuse a known incompatible case, request a different provider, and report a suspected failure. Their ordinary clinical decisions do not raise Wu Authority. The institutional gain comes from having trained people capable of making those decisions.

Under a personal charter, local physicians apply the same validated methods but defer disputed interpretation and senior appointments to Wu's office. Routine work can be fast, while unusual cases queue behind the personal service. The player can fund independent verification to reduce that queue without discovering the treatment again.

At high Authority, a hospital may accept a directive from Wu despite a conflicting ministry order when its appointment, supply, and records genuinely place it under his control. The effect is limited to that institution and the authority already conceded. One high value does not convert unrelated hospitals, all military units, or every university into his followers.

### When standing authority ends

Authority can expire, be withdrawn, or become invalid when a provider loses its qualification or a protocol is questioned. New admissions then stop or return to explicit approval. Patients already receiving necessary care remain obligations. Withdrawal is scheduled against their actual supplies and the availability of a qualified handover.

The national service may pay to keep a temporary clinical authorization during the transition. It may also withdraw a formation, transfer patients through a safe route, or end an optional programme. An expired charter cannot be used to manufacture continued care, but a political revocation cannot erase the people who still need it.

## Appointments that change the service

A national appointment grants a particular responsibility. It can put a physician in charge of a hospital's admitted service, give a university authority to verify a curriculum, place a military liaison at the allocation board, or give a production director control over future orders. The appointment does not grant the entire appointee's institution to the person who selected them.

### The incumbent hospital physician

An established physician may support Wu's methods while resisting replacement by one of his assistants. The government can retain the incumbent with verified training, appoint a qualified Wu nominee, or divide clinical leadership from procurement authority. The first route preserves local continuity. The second concentrates responsibility. The third creates two offices with separate duties and requires enough actual staff to fill them.

An incumbent who already has the relevant qualification does not need to repeat the same course solely because the hospital joins a national service. A nominee with only a prestigious title does not bypass it. Where a named character exists, the appointment uses that identity. Where the source provides only a medical team, the office remains an institutional role without an invented historical person or additional advisor reward.

Replacement can interrupt care if the outgoing physician held knowledge that no other available team possesses. A safe handover identifies the outstanding recipients, current protocol, remaining material, and replacement provider. Removal can be immediate as a political act, but its medical consequences are evaluated from those facts. The game must not pretend that an unqualified replacement preserves the old service.

### A national chair and regional examiners

A national chair can organize teaching across several sites and nominate examiners for a completed method. The chair's value is a reproducible qualification pathway. It does not create an additional national research slot or a third Wu project lane.

The proposed national teaching recognition work takes **120 days**, with one teaching team, one civilian factory, **150 support equipment**, and **50 Political Power**. It concerns a selected documented curriculum and an existing teaching institution. Its output is the authority and ability to qualify the corresponding service under that curriculum. Producing a new curriculum or treating another disease still requires the earlier research or teaching route.

Regional examiners can review a proposed extension independently. Their qualification is specific. An examiner for routine treatment cannot certify the care of an engineered formation. Several examiners can use the same documented medical knowledge, but their actual appointments and teaching time remain separate.

A chair selected by Wu can remain scientifically sound. A public chair can make mistakes. The appointment method determines accountability, access, and replacement rights, while the validity of medical results remains evidence-dependent. The design must not give one political appointment method automatic scientific truth.

### The army's medical representative

The army may appoint a qualified representative to argue for its patients and operational needs. This person can explain a genuine casualty surge, an upcoming loss of support for enhanced units, or the need to preserve a reserve for a supplied defensive operation. The representative cannot classify every future recruit as a current emergency.

A civilian medical representative can show the consequences of diverting the same supply from hospitals. Their dispute is settled through actual allocations and follow-up obligations. The outcome may protect a military timetable at the cost of delaying a civilian expansion, or preserve civilian treatment while an optional enhancement programme waits.

No appointment changes the citizenship, loyalty, or person count of the recipients. Enhanced veterans remain people with their existing care requirements. A newly appointed military director does not turn their treatment dependence into ownership of them.

### Dismissal, dissent, and replacement

Protecting a qualified dissenting service preserves a potential independent check and may slow the adoption of a contested new standard. Dismissing it can remove that check and create a real staffing gap. Neither action automatically creates sabotage, a foreign agent, an atrocity, or a medical failure.

If an institution refuses a directive, record what it refused and why. A physician can reject a mismatched treatment while continuing ordinary care. A provincial authority can reject a stock transfer while honoring existing clinical agreements. These are narrower events than a nationwide strike.

A government can investigate an apparent refusal, negotiate a division of duties, or replace the responsible office. Evidence of misconduct belongs to the actual actor and action. An inconclusive review leaves the uncertainty visible and cannot be converted into a clean bill of health or proof of betrayal.

## Centralized and distributed production

### A central dispatch office

A central office coordinates supplies held at admitted sites and receives requests from participating services. It can assign uncommitted stock to an accepted request, arrange a delivery through an available route, and reserve later output from a named works. Its officers can resolve a shortage across several hospitals without requiring a separate personal negotiation with Wu for each consignment.

The proposed dispatch-office programme takes **120 days**, commits a coordination team, **two civilian factories**, **200 support equipment**, and **1,500 fuel**. The selected location must already support the necessary medical and transport functions. The programme creates the coordination service, not free factories, warehouses, roads, railways, or treatment stocks.

After preparation, the office retains the assigned coordination team and **one civilian factory** while operating. The second factory is released. Physical shipment and medical upkeep continue through their existing owners. Closing the office releases its own remaining reservation after live consignments have a valid custodian. It does not refund consumed equipment or recreate fuel.

A country unable to spare that continuing burden can keep explicit commissions or a regional agreement. National scale is not a compulsory fixed bill for every small host. The central office is worthwhile when it can serve several concurrent requests, support a larger preparedness programme, or remove a real personal allocation bottleneck.

### Central works

Centralized production can concentrate scarce qualified staff and limited industrial capacity at a strong site. It can prepare large batches of one completed standard and use the central dispatch office to assign their actual output. Its advantage comes from sustained production and less fragmented qualification, not a free output multiplier.

The government must preserve replacement supply for patients using earlier treatments. Retooling a works does not cancel their needs. If the only works changes standard, the country may need to finish a reserve, qualify a regional producer, or purchase a valid delivery before the old service ends.

A central works also makes its actual downstream services more exposed to the loss of that location. Bombing, occupation, missing staff, a recalled lot, or an interrupted route can stop its output. The national effect follows the recipients that depend on it. Hospitals with qualified independent stocks remain in service.

The political choice concerns who may order a change and whose contracts it may displace. A ministry can retain the power to protect civilian allocations. A personal directorate can let Wu favor a programme he considers more important. An army-led procurement office can prioritize military care inside its permitted budget. Each remains limited by existing reservations and care obligations.

### Regional works

A distributed network can place production near its recipients and reduce dependence on one route. Each regional works still needs the appropriate design, qualified manufacture, real factory time, and materials. Admission is not an instant conversion of ordinary civilian industry into a functioning medical works.

Regional contracts can share a common standard without sharing all research. A public method may be taught widely while a difficult diagnostic interpretation still requires Wu. Conversely, a region can retain the rights to a method independently verified before its national admission. The national charter must describe which knowledge is common and which service remains personal.

A region may refuse to surrender its protected reserve while its existing patients need it. The national government can negotiate a later delivery, compensate actual supply, or use a separately authorized compulsory transfer. A compulsory transfer creates the recorded shortage where material was removed. It does not manufacture an equivalent reserve in the donor region.

Distribution makes secrecy harder only through actual dissemination. A closed clinical standard sent to five institutions has more custodians than one held at the central institute. This can expose a limited curriculum, staff relationship, or production contract. It does not reveal every restricted experiment or automatically provide a foreign actor with the access needed for theft.

### One physical item throughout the network

A production standard, a shipment, a warehouse holding, and a treatment allocation remain separate things. A national list can show all of them, but the same item may occupy only its current physical or reserved state.

For example, a works holds **120 units** of standard A. A native operation has reserved **40**, a hospital delivery has committed **30**, and **50** remain available. Moving **20** of the available units to a regional warehouse leaves **30** available at the works and **20** at the warehouse. The hospital's 30 and native operation's 40 remain reserved under their own transactions.

If the country switches its future production to standard B, all 120 historical A units retain their A identity and disposition. The national director cannot call the regional warehouse a new institute and gain another two modified standards. Nor can a regional purchase make an old A reservation satisfy a B requirement.

The shared native owners determine cancellation and consumption for their reservations. The national medical service cannot return reserved material on the assumption that a diplomatic or appointment change made it unused. It can request an owner-supported cancellation and act only on the result that owner supplies.

## Civilian and military claims on medicine

### Protected care comes first in the accounting

Existing treatment, withdrawal, continuing support, and accepted delivery obligations are identified before discretionary allocations. This protects the truth of the ledger. It does not remove the government's ability to make a harmful choice, but a harmful choice must be explicit and retain the people and shortages it creates.

An optional new military enhancement course should not quietly consume the withdrawal reserve of soldiers already enrolled. A civilian publicity campaign should not count stock promised to a foreign client as uncommitted domestic medicine. A hospital should not accept a new group on the assumption that its current patients will disappear at the end of an administrative timer.

The national service can divide available material between claims, delay a new programme, purchase supply, expand production, or request a qualified substitute. The correct option depends on the remaining time and the exact standard. A generic medical reserve does not necessarily meet the requirement for a specific control treatment.

### Three allocation policies

**Clinical urgency** directs uncommitted compatible material toward the most time-sensitive living patients and continuing-care obligations. Military patients can have the highest urgency. The policy does not equate civilian status with priority or exclude soldiers from public medicine.

**Strategic reserve** protects a disclosed amount for a named military or national emergency purpose. That amount remains real reserved stock and is removed from the available pool. The reserve can be released deliberately, but doing so changes the future support that the army or emergency service can rely on.

**Regional guarantees** protect agreed local allocations and leave a smaller discretionary pool for national requests. This can keep isolated communities supplied and make regional cooperation more credible. It also means that the centre cannot solve every new shortage by emptying a distant hospital.

These policies are compatible with different charters, but one policy governs a particular allocation round. It is not possible to count all three as cumulative national bonuses. The policy can change when a new cause justifies review. Existing clinical and shipment commitments survive the change unless a separate authorized action alters them.

### Emergency override

A severe outbreak or care failure can justify a bounded emergency allocation power. The proposed emergency authority lasts **60 days** and names its medical purpose and participating services. Its administrative preparation takes **15 days** where no already valid emergency delegation exists. Immediate baseline treatment remains available during that preparation.

The host can retain the ordinary government as allocating authority or temporarily delegate the specific power to Wu. A genuinely new personal emergency concession applies the existing **+10 Authority** direction once. Extending the same concession does not apply another increase. Its expiry or successful return to public control reconciles the actual concession history. Repeated subtraction of a fixed amount is not permitted.

Emergency authority may reprioritize uncommitted supplies and approved transport. It cannot secretly drain an incompatible military reserve, rewrite native raids, convert human research permissions, or grant permanent control over hospitals. A more extensive compulsory action requires the separate authority, visible consequence, and shared-owner transaction appropriate to it.

The emergency can end with ordinary government control restored, a negotiated limited extension, a funded independent service taking over, or an unresolved shortage. A shortage is not itself proof that Wu must receive permanent authority. The government can choose to bear the cost of slower expansion or seek another provider.

### The national shortage example

Assume the country can make **ten compatible treatment allocations** during the current interval. Four are already committed to ongoing civilian care, two to the continuing medical support of selected formations, and one to an accepted foreign delivery. Only **three** are discretionary.

A new civilian request needs three and a new military programme needs two. The government cannot fulfill both from those three. Under clinical urgency, the civilian request can receive all three if its time-sensitive need is proven. Under strategic reserve, the army can receive two and the civilian request one, with the remaining two civilian allocations recorded as unmet. Under regional guarantees, the result follows the guarantees actually adopted, not a fictional extra reserve.

The foreign delivery cannot be erased by renaming the national policy. Renegotiating it may create one additional available allocation only after the contract owner accepts the change. A material failure or deliberate breach retains the Part 11 payment, responsibility, and restitution rules.

## Independent regional institutions

The federation and public-service routes become stronger when local medicine can continue without an instruction from Wu. Independence is specific to a method and service. It requires the knowledge, qualification, physical supply, and appointment needed to carry out that work.

### The independent examiner

A regional teaching institution can become an independent examiner for a selected completed standard. The proposed programme takes **120 days**, using a qualified verification team, one civilian factory, **150 support equipment**, and **50 Political Power**. It compares the actual documented method with its supported applications and establishes the institution's right to certify corresponding local service.

This is distinct from discovering a cure or copying records. A hospital can possess a validated method and treat patients without becoming a national examiner. Existing Part 2 distribution and Part 11 teaching remain sufficient for the roles they already grant. The examiner programme adds a cross-institutional verification responsibility and must not recharge work already completed under an equivalent contract.

An independent examiner can confirm that a proposed hospital is qualified, identify a missing requirement, preserve a sound earlier version, or report that available evidence is insufficient. It cannot declare a new cure complete or overrule a disease owner's compatibility boundary.

### Mutual assistance between regions

Two or more qualified services can agree to lend a response team, purchase one another's actual production, or receive a defined patient group. The agreement specifies which continuing commitments remain protected and which authority may request assistance. It creates access to a real response path, not a second national stockpile.

A region can supply another only while its contribution exists. A factory strike, lost route, occupied hospital, or exhausted reserve can invalidate the next shipment. The recipient keeps its claims and patients, while the donor keeps the consequences of any earlier accepted commitment. A national agreement cannot make missing supply harmless.

The national government can encourage mutual assistance without assigning every regional worker to the capital. Local teams can remain available for their ordinary cases. A team temporarily assigned elsewhere must leave its original capacity unavailable for that interval unless a separate qualified replacement takes over.

### Personal dependence and its removal

A service is personally dependent when its continued operation needs Wu's unique knowledge, personal appointment power, exclusive allocation decision, or a support standard that no other qualified provider can sustain. A documented cure used by independently trained hospitals can remain effective after his departure even if his portrait is no longer present.

The national board communicates dependence qualitatively through the services at risk and their remaining support. It does not add a permanent Dependence meter beside Wu Authority or a third rivalry value. The player should see which hospital, formation, or programme needs a replacement and what would make it independent.

Successful medicine can make departure more consequential because more people rely on the resulting service. That does not mean every successful course increases personal Authority. An independently delivered cure can extend national reach while reducing the practical need for Wu's personal intervention. A successful personal monopoly can extend reach while leaving more recipients exposed to his departure.

The government can reduce dependence through real teaching, independent verification, diversified qualified production, and appointment reform. Repeated audits, repeated office renaming, and toggling a charter do not reduce it. A cure's permanent knowledge remains in the country regardless of whether its last service is currently operating.

## Compromised standards across the network

The national system creates a larger consequence only when several actual consumers share the compromised dependency. A fault at one hospital is local until evidence identifies a common cause. A nationally circulated instruction can affect all of its actual adopters, including later adopters, while services using another verified instruction remain separate.

### Four different failures

**A defective physical lot** affects the actual items from that lot and recipients whose care used them. Other production under the same design is not automatically defective. Isolation or recall follows the stock owner and preserves reserved or already consumed dispositions.

**An incompatible clinical instruction** affects the application of a method to a recipient or disease version it does not support. The treatment's physical identity does not change. The service may need corrected instructions, a current adaptation, or a different treatment. Existing patients retain their actual outcomes.

**An unqualified appointment** leaves a service unable to make a required judgment or maintain a supported procedure. Supplying more material does not replace the missing qualification. The government can use a visiting qualified team, train a replacement, narrow the service, or close new admissions.

**A false national report** makes the government's view unreliable. A hospital may be counted as prepared despite missing staff or stock, or an allocation may have been claimed without delivery. The correction changes the administrative record to match the actual state. It does not retroactively manufacture a shipment or apply a death total that no owner recorded.

These failures can coexist, but each needs its own supporting facts. A suspicious pattern is enough to open a review. It is not enough to identify an attacker, invent an outbreak, or declare every national item contaminated.

### Common-standard review

A review begins with the known failing service, exact instruction or lot, and actual adopter set. The government can pause new adoption of that instruction, continue a separately verified older method, invite an independent examiner, or maintain a restricted temporary service when the medical owner permits it.

The proposed ordinary national review takes **90 days**, commits one verification team and one civilian factory, and spends **150 support equipment** and **40 Political Power**. It does not occupy a discovery lane merely to inspect an already documented service. New medical work uncovered by the review still uses its normal lane and prerequisites.

A time-sensitive service may not be able to wait 90 days. The response then uses a currently qualified alternative, the existing owner-specific emergency care action, or withdrawal. The review continues to determine responsibility and future eligibility. It is not shortened automatically until it happens to fit the deadline.

Clean confirmation restores only the affected service or instruction. A partial finding can isolate one lot or one institution while other verified consumers continue. An inconclusive result keeps uncertain claims restricted. A confirmed systemic flaw sends the relevant applications back to their proper adaptation or qualification route without erasing the underlying knowledge.

### Correlated harm without a second disease system

When a compromised instruction was adopted at several hospitals, the medical owner evaluates the actual exposed or underserved recipients at each site. National aggregation may present the combined result, but it cannot apply a second mortality calculation on top of those local outcomes.

A faulty instruction does not automatically become a new biological strain. A laboratory accident, outbreak, or deliberate use requires the underlying owner to establish that event. Disease identity, containment, spread, contamination, responsibility, and recovery remain with that owner.

A discovered defect can also identify a threat that has not yet harmed anyone. The response may successfully withdraw unused material or replace an instruction before recipients are affected. That is a prevented failure, not a fictional death-and-revival cycle or a repeated source of negative Chaos.

### Responsibility and disclosure

A government can publish the confirmed scope, commission an independent explanation, restrict further use, or conceal evidence. A publication is tied to the actual finding. It does not expose every hidden programme merely because Wu's name appears on the institution.

Evidence of deliberate concealment or harmful experimentation passes through the existing responsible system. Ordinary possession, a disputed theory, or a hospital's refusal of an unqualified instruction is not proof of biological warfare. Curing the affected patients cannot erase confirmed use, responsibility, recorded deaths, or previous evidence.

A public or regional network can discover a fault earlier because more institutions can examine the same method. That advantage needs actual qualified review and accessible records. It is not a blanket reduction in all hidden evidence or a permanent guarantee against errors.

## Foreign access to a national service

National reach makes Wu's completed medicine easier to identify and request. Foreign governments may approach a participating hospital, university, ministry, or treatment works. The person who receives the approach is not automatically entitled to sell the requested material or disclose another institution's work.

A regional institution can offer a public curriculum it owns, a paid teaching visit, or actual uncommitted production under its contract. Restricted methods, patient custody, military control protocols, and export permissions remain separate. A foreign appointment cannot silently convert a domestic physician's patients into the foreign employer's property.

### National procurement and foreign clients

A central office can negotiate a single agreement covering several admitted domestic producers. Each promised contribution remains attached to the producer that must supply it. If one fails, another may substitute only compatible material it can actually spare and is authorized to transfer.

A country can reserve a limited share of future ordinary cure output for foreign medical partners. That is a real production commitment with the Part 4 and Part 11 payment and delivery rules. It creates an opportunity cost for domestic expansion. It does not grant automatic faction membership, military access, or a foreign monopoly over all future Wu research.

Regional foreign contracts can protect a service from a national political dispute by retaining a separate lawful source of medicine. They can also make the country dependent on a foreign route or employer. The national charter determines who can negotiate such contracts and when the government can intervene, while the material and diplomatic owners determine what can actually be transferred.

### Sanctions and inspection

A national medical charter does not change native embargo ownership or create a shipment exemption. Humanitarian arrangements retain their exact purpose and route limits. An existing sanctions carve-out can reduce practical pressure without proving that a blocked physical consignment may proceed.

Medical accreditation is also distinct from a CBRN inspection. A hospital can be qualified to treat patients while an investigation into another activity remains open. The national service cannot certify the entire country as free of hidden evidence or cancel source-owned sanctions after one successful cure.

Part 11's ordinary medical coalitions can support a national network with actual personnel, stock, or teaching. Coalition membership does not place a second copy of those resources in the national account. Foreign institutions remain independent unless a separate lawful and supported transfer changes their ownership.

### Intelligence exposure

Only actual dissemination changes access. A published curriculum may reveal a treatment method. A foreign teaching visitor may learn the material they were admitted to study. A compromised employee may expose the records they can reach. A national-service title alone does not reveal the locations or contents of every laboratory.

Suspicious staff changes, missing consignments, and conflicting standards can create the corresponding Part 11 cases. The foreign actor still needs a valid target, access, resources, and the appropriate action. National Medical Ascendancy does not initialize Kruger's private international contest or activate Wu's later International Biological Contest early.

## Wu, Kruger, and several qualified Wus

The six domestic relationships in Part 10 continue to govern shared work. A national medical charter allocates health responsibilities within those settlements. It does not automatically settle ownership of a joint institute or transfer Kruger's conventional projects to Wu.

Under an ordered division, the national service can use Wu's clinical work and a separately commissioned Kruger contribution. Under competitive grants, successful methods can enter service under their recorded credit and ownership. A joint institute can become one participating provider with its own charter. Wu supremacy and Kruger supremacy retain their actual jurisdictional concessions. Laboratory conflict can interrupt a shared service but cannot delete the independent hospitals that use a reproduced method.

The national appointment process must respect Kruger's current assignment and transfer rules. It never creates a second canonical Kruger, changes his personal history, or replays his original research reward. A university programme that already belongs to his Directorate is not an unoccupied pool of staff for the Wu service.

### Continuity copies in public office

Several qualified Wus can hold different medical responsibilities when their appointments and capacity permit it. One may lead a clinical service and another teach or maintain an independent branch. Their identities, qualifications, consent, physical locations, and care remain separate.

A copy does not inherit a national office merely by sharing Wu's appearance or archive lineage. The government can recognize a prepared successor, appoint an already qualified alternative, or conduct the existing continuity qualification. An incomplete archive cannot confer a skill that its copy has not acquired.

The national service still has the inherited maximum of **two primary Wu research lanes**. The country-wide maximum of **two active modified production standards** also remains unchanged. Additional people can fill real clinical and administrative posts, but do not multiply the country's research-slot, production-standard, or national modifier entitlements.

Competing Wus may support different charters. One can favor regional medicine and another personal centralization. Their disagreement is handled through the appointments, contracts, and actual institutions they control. It is not evidence that one is unreal, and it cannot be settled by deleting the less favored copy from population or death records.

## Removing Wu from a national service

Removal now affects a network of existing responsibilities. The consequence is determined service by service. The country retains permanent knowledge, physical investments, valid national rights, and staff who remain in its service. It loses Wu's personal improvisation, appointments, or control only where those depended on the person who has left.

### Planned retirement or negotiated departure

A planned departure begins with the current appointments and outstanding obligations. The government can retain a qualified public successor, distribute selected roles among existing regional services, employ an available qualified continuity copy, or arrange limited foreign support. Each successor receives only the duties they can actually perform.

The departure plan names which work can continue, which work must pause, and which recipients need transfer or withdrawal. It does not make every hospital requalify from the beginning. A service that already operates independently keeps its proven qualification. A personal method that has never been independently reproduced remains a real limitation.

Wu can remain available as a narrowly contracted consultant while relinquishing national appointment power. This creates a continuing service agreement, not his permanent presence in two countries or two simultaneous full-time roles. The agreement specifies the actual person, location, availability, and purpose. His later capture or refusal can interrupt it.

### Sudden loss

Death, disappearance, abduction, or the loss of the central institute can open the existing succession and care emergencies. Part 7's proposed **60-day institutional succession** window does not extend Part 6's **45-day control-service** emergency. A service with only 20 days of remaining qualified support has 20 days, even if the national hearing lasts longer.

The national government can preserve routine known care immediately through qualified existing providers. It must separately secure the records, supplies, and appointments needed for services that depended on Wu. The discovery of an available copy is not enough until that copy's qualification, consent, custody, and appointment are established.

The country must not receive a replacement scientist automatically when no qualified successor exists. It can fund the existing continuity work, seek a genuine foreign appointment, continue a limited documented service, or close new admissions while preserving the patients it already has.

### A personal network resists reassignment

Hospitals that rely on Wu's appointment power or exclusive records can resist a proposed replacement. The government can negotiate their participation, protect an independent team, secure records through an authorized action, or withdraw the personal institution's role. The effect follows the actual staff and services involved.

The first evolution does not create a coup or a breakaway country. A dispute can leave a hospital closed to new admissions, a works without an authorized director, or a medical team unwilling to serve under a new employer. Those outcomes create real problems without invoking later sovereignty mechanics.

A high-Authority institute can have more leverage because it controls more indispensable services. The government can still prepare independent provision and defeat that leverage through actual work. A low-Authority public network can also face a difficult loss if Wu alone understood its newest treatment. Authority and technical replaceability are related but separate facts.

### Foreign relocation

A departed Wu carries only his own knowledge, permitted archive copies, and staff who actually leave under the Part 11 rules. National hospital appointments, buildings, public records, and local treatment reserves stay with their lawful owners. The new host receives no copy of the old country's national service.

The global evolution history remains attached to Event 089. A new host may negotiate a national arrangement suited to its own institutions, but it must fund and qualify those institutions. It does not receive a second evolution reward or replay the first host's grants. Existing foreign clients retain the counterpart and contract that actually owes them service.

An old host with strong independent institutions may remain a medical power after Wu leaves. Its ordinary production, teaching, and accepted care continue. Its ability to answer a genuinely new disease without his special role remains governed by the surviving baseline and shared medical systems.

## The evolved first opening

At 200 or more Chaos, the first appearance can begin with several ministries and regional hospitals already involved in the medical emergency around Wu. The scene is a temporary coordination problem. National context does not enlarge the first proof into an uncosted nationwide cure.

A prospective evolved opening is revalidated when a resident appointment and national settlement can actually commit. A country that merely refused him is not recorded as the successful national-service actor. A later recipient is assessed from its own institutions and current Chaos conditions. Where the event later opens at a higher evolution tier, this first-evolution component contributes only its valid national institutional situation. ## National cases and objectives

Cases arise from actual disagreements, failures, and new commitments. A resolved case needs a new material cause before it can recur. Display at most three current objectives, with clinical emergencies taking precedence. ### NM01: One shipment, two waiting services

A civilian receiving hospital and a military service request the same uncommitted compatible supply. The objective is to deliver the selected allocation or establish a qualified substitute within the real demand window. Existing ongoing care is identified before either new request.

The government can apply clinical urgency, retain a strategic reserve, honor a regional guarantee, or commission additional actual supply. Success means the accepted recipient receives valid treatment support. Partial success leaves the remaining request open without duplicating recipients. Failure delays or cancels the relevant new programme, or invokes the actual clinical shortfall if an existing obligation was deliberately broken.

An ordinary expansion dispute receives a proposed **120-day** planning window. An urgent clinical or control-service request uses its existing shorter deadline, clearly explained by the patient's or formation's remaining support.

### NM02: A university will not certify the new instruction

A qualified examiner finds that the proposed extension lacks an established compatibility result or a required record. The institute may preserve the older method, fund the missing baseline work, request a second qualified examination, or override the institution politically.

The objective is to establish a valid supported service before enrolling the disputed recipients. Replacing the examiner changes the office, not the unresolved medical fact. An unsupported override can create a real failure only through actual use and the medical owner's result. The case never guarantees that dissent proves sabotage or that Wu must be wrong.

### NM03: The central works loses its route

A real route interruption leaves hospitals dependent on the central producer. Their stocks and existing deliveries determine how long they can continue. The national office can use a supported alternative route, purchase compatible stock, turn to an already qualified regional works, or reduce new admissions.

The objective concerns actual delivery, not ownership of a nominal port or a political payment. A regional works that is still being qualified cannot immediately replace production. Success can preserve the current service while delaying expansion. An unresolved shortage affects only the services whose supplies fail.

### NM04: Several hospitals copied the same mistake

Two or more actual adopters of the same instruction report a compatible failure pattern. The government can halt further adoption, preserve a verified older service, and run N10. The medical owner evaluates actual harm at each site.

A finding may identify a common instruction, one defective lot, an unqualified team, or insufficient evidence. The national report distinguishes those outcomes. It does not convert the whole country's inventory into a new strain or apply another country-wide mortality charge after local losses were recorded.

### NM05: A regional institute signs a foreign agreement

An independent regional institution accepts an approach within or beyond its recorded rights. The government can recognize the valid contract, negotiate its scope, provide a domestic substitute, or dispute an unauthorized commitment through Part 11.

The objective is to settle the named access and delivery relationship. A public curriculum can be shared without surrendering restricted material. A foreign contract may keep a hospital supplied, but its promise does not bypass sanctions or a closed route. Existing patients remain obligations during the dispute.

### NM06: A senior physician leaves a personal network

The departing physician holds a clinical or administrative role needed by one or more participating services. The government can retain the physician under a different appointment, use a prepared deputy, qualify a replacement, or narrow the service until a valid handover exists.

The objective uses the actual support window. A proposed **90-day** ordinary appointment-resolution mission gives time for a qualified candidate and handover when no immediate clinical shortage exists. It cannot extend a 45-day control emergency. Departure does not create a replacement team, erase the physician's identity, or confiscate their patients as transferable property.

### NM07: Army allocations continue after the emergency

A temporary strategic reserve remains allocated after its stated emergency has ended. The government can return the uncommitted portion to national use, document a new genuine need, or retain a deliberate long-term military priority through an explicit settlement.

The objective is to resolve the actual reserve and its live obligations. It never takes stock already consumed or reserved by another owner. Ending a temporary personal concession reverses only its recorded applied change. The same stock and concession cannot generate repeated Authority reductions through successive emergency labels.

### NM08: A clinic is accredited only on paper

The national register shows a functioning service, but the owner lacks a current staff, material, or qualification proof. The government can inspect the discrepancy, send an already qualified team, fund missing supply, or correct the register and stop new admissions.

The objective is to restore a real service or publish its actual limited status. Correcting a false record can lower reported reach without creating a new disease outbreak. If there were real untreated patients, their condition is resolved through the medical owner. The record alone is not a mortality event.

### NM09: A joint laboratory receives two incompatible orders

Wu's national office and a Kruger institution issue conflicting instructions concerning a shared medical service. The government uses the active Part 10 relationship to identify who owns clinical judgment, production, staff, and command. A bounded emergency agreement can protect current recipients while the dispute is settled.

The objective is a qualified, funded service with one valid operating instruction. The case does not advance a Kruger project, open his sovereignty board, or initialize his foreign contest. If the joint institution closes, independent participating hospitals retain the methods and supplies they already own.

### NM10: The physician disappears during a national programme

Wu becomes unavailable while the country has active patients, a national appointment dispute, and planned future expansion. Prepared successors can continue their qualified work. Personal improvisation pauses, and the existing succession and control-service clocks apply independently.

The objective is to assign valid continuing providers and settle the most urgent obligations. Some work can continue, some can wait, and some may need withdrawal. A successful recovery of Wu or the appointment of a qualified copy changes future availability without undoing earlier deaths, failures, or lost material.

## AI national-service policy

AI countries must solve the same real tasks as the player. They cannot treat a charter, national office, high Authority, or a large factory total as proof of medical coverage. They should first identify current care obligations, then the available material and staff, then the useful institutional change.

### Strategy profiles

A **public civilian host at peace** favors a public charter, teaching, limited standing orders, and independent verification when those investments serve actual expansion. It does not build every optional office simply because it can afford one. If only one small service exists, case-by-case approval can remain preferable.

A **centralized administrative host** can prefer concentrated procurement and national public appointments without giving Wu personal ownership. It uses central dispatch when the expected request load justifies the continuing factory and team reservation. It should build a regional alternative when a single route or producer endangers many actual recipients.

A **military host under casualty pressure** favors reliable field care, protected support for existing formations, and a disclosed strategic reserve. It can delay optional civilian expansion, but should not begin a new enhancement course by silently consuming its own withdrawal reserve. Losing a war increases willingness to accept a bounded emergency concession, not permission to ignore impossible support.

A **classified high-Authority host** favors a personal or compartmentalized arrangement where existing rights support it. It weighs exposure from regional teaching against the risks of a single-person bottleneck. It can still choose independent verification during a serious common-standard failure. Secrecy does not make an unqualified service valid.

A **weak or isolated host** limits expensive national coordination, protects one viable service, seeks a lawful foreign supply or teaching arrangement, and uses explicit commissions when a standing central office would consume too much industry. It does not fabricate a regional network to satisfy a large-country pattern.

A **host with several Wus or a Kruger partnership** values existing qualifications and ownership over character names. It assigns distinct duties, avoids double-booking, protects jointly required care, and does not use a new copy or national charter to multiply research lanes or active standards.

### Decision ordering

Existing urgent care and expiring support outrank optional national prestige, new offices, and speculative expansion. A corrective action that can actually finish inside the remaining window outranks a more ambitious replacement that cannot. Where no timely action exists, the AI must consider withdrawal, limited continuation, or another qualified provider instead of repeatedly purchasing an impossible solution.

Independent verification becomes more valuable when a service has many actual adopters or one person controls a critical judgment. Central dispatch becomes more valuable when several compatible requests can share an actual route and producer. Regional production becomes more valuable when supply interruption is geographically concentrated and local qualification is achievable.

These are intended orderings, not measured probabilities. Exact weights, random pools, MTTH survival curves, and game-version scheduling remain for the named probability workflow. Missing resource, route, recipient, or qualification inputs make a candidate invalid. A low weight must not be used to disguise an invalid action.

## Presentation of the national service

The existing Wu decision category remains the main surface. Its national-service phase shows **Wu Authority** as the primary value, the selected service's **actual reach** as one supporting value, and a concise **continuity condition** as the other. The current mandate and charter appear as contextual labels. Selecting a research task replaces the national detail view with the relevant research details.

The continuity condition identifies a practical situation such as independently supported service, a named personal bottleneck, or an expiring qualified handover. It does not expose another numeric meter. The reach label names its disease or medical purpose and interval so that a cumulative number of past patients cannot masquerade as present coverage.

Show three to five useful actions in the current phase and never more than six. During a shortage, the allocation, supply, and withdrawal choices take precedence over teaching and optional offices. During a charter settlement, the national responses and the selected participants take precedence. A target browser may list actual records, but it must not become a hidden store of dozens of simultaneous generic purchases.

### National-spirit lifecycle

The existing institute spirit can acquire a national-service form that reflects the active charter and supported service. It replaces the earlier representation. Regional appointments, reserves, and reviews remain inside that institution. The event retains its maximum of three simultaneous national spirits.

A working public or regional service shows its genuine collective provision. A personal service shows the actual dependence of its supported programmes. A compromised or interrupted network uses the same institution's impaired form where a meaningful modifier is justified. Recovery restores only the service that has actually recovered.

After Wu leaves, the institution's representation changes to the surviving national or regional service. Permanent knowledge is not hidden inside a removable personal spirit. Losing a personal bonus does not revoke the learned method, and keeping the institution does not preserve Wu's absent unique contribution.

### Text and image direction

The first evolution should show medical practice spreading through real workplaces. Avoid making a map, a conference table, or a stack of administrative forms the emotional centre.

Public-service responses use practical confidence with attention to remaining shortages. Personal centralization can carry restrained pride and impatience with competing authority. ## Three national-service campaigns

### A public network that survives its founder

A public host completes a substantial cure and a second clinical function while Chaos exceeds 200. After the contextual interval, it accepts a public national charter and pays the actual coordination work. Two existing qualified services receive standing authority for the known method. A third hospital is admitted after its own training and supply are ready.

The player puts a later teaching investment ahead of an optional enhancement programme. That choice gives the country another qualified source of routine medicine, while the enhancement remains available through its baseline prerequisites. The regional examiner later questions an adaptation intended for a new disease version. The older verified treatment remains in use only where it is still compatible.

Wu eventually departs. The national service loses his unique ability to improvise a new cure, but its trained hospitals continue the methods they know. The next unfamiliar outbreak is difficult because it needs new work, not because the game erased every previous medical achievement.

### A fast personal service with a costly interruption

A military host gives Wu national appointment and allocation power. Those rights are real new concessions, so their actual Authority changes are recorded. It concentrates production and keeps enough support for its existing selected formations before opening another course.

A supply interruption then affects the central works. The standing office can make decisions quickly, but it cannot move material through a broken route. The player can protect the remaining control-service reserve, delay the new enhancement course, and purchase a qualified foreign contribution. Alternatively, diverting protected supply creates the actual medical and command emergency specified by its owner.

A qualified regional institution offers an independent service with a long preparation time. The player may fund it for the next campaign, but cannot count it as the solution to a shorter current deadline. Centralization has produced useful speed and a real dependency, with neither outcome reducing to an arbitrary national bonus or unavoidable takeover.

### A federation under external pressure

Several regions retain their own medical institutions and adopt compatible shared standards. Their methods and staff are easier for legitimate foreign visitors to encounter, but restricted programmes remain separate. One region signs an authorized ordinary-cure contract with a foreign partner.

Sanctions and a route closure interrupt that agreement. The national government cannot invoke humanitarian language to create physical access. It instead negotiates a supported route, allocates actual compatible domestic stock, or reduces new admissions. Existing payments and unmet delivery obligations remain with the original parties.

A copied instruction later proves unreliable for one current recipient group. The regional examiner limits the finding to that application. Other compatible services continue. The federation's value is that it has qualified institutions able to distinguish those cases, while its cost is the industrial time and political work needed to build them.

## What national medicine places on the international stage

National Medical Ascendancy leaves the country with a real pattern of medical power. It may have a public service that can reproduce known methods, a fast personal network with a narrow point of failure, a federation of independent institutions, or a careful set of explicit national commissions. Those outcomes depend on the appointments, material, staff, and rights established during play.

Foreign governments can already use the baseline agreements and competition in Part 11. The later International Biological Contest will change how those governments pursue the existing network and its valuable results. It inherits the actual institutions, standards, commitments, and unresolved cases described here. It does not retroactively build them, grant their research, or clear their obligations.
