# Doctor Wu: Sovereign Biology

## When medical authority contests government authority

A ministry orders a treatment works to change its allocation. The works follows Wu's instructions instead. An independent hospital continues to obey the ministry, while an enhanced formation asks which institution will provide its next course. Sovereign Biology turns those existing relationships into a dispute over who can give binding orders.

The fourth evolution becomes eligible at 800 Chaos. It can lead to a supervised constitutional settlement, limited institutional autonomy, removal, exile, a divided scientific administration, or an armed confrontation. A country appears only when an actual territorial and political separation occurs. The evolution does not create an automatic coup or provide Wu with an army.

Every new value, timing anchor, action label, and settlement mechanism in this part is proposed design. The four evolution thresholds and their zero activation Chaos are inherited requirements. All research remains baseline. The institutional and biological fiction supplies no real methods for coercive treatment, weapons, abduction, or attacks.

## Eligibility built from the campaign

An active-chain entry requires a viable qualified Wu institution, Chaos at least 800, and a live dispute about an order the government is trying to enforce. There must also be a real basis for the dispute: dependence on a unique medical service, independent security, a personally controlled works, a loyal formation, several durable sites, a continuity branch with its own following, or an unresolved high-Authority refusal.

The government's institutional strength is evaluated at the same time. Independent cure production, qualified successors, enforceable archive access, trusted local appointments, and reliable forces can make the state stronger than Wu. Those facts do not prevent the constitutional question from appearing. They make a government victory practical.

A doctor with no independent following can refuse an order as a person, but cannot be awarded control of the national hospital system. A high Authority number without the corresponding surviving rights does not supply a power base. A low-Authority institute can still create a serious constitutional dispute if a large public service depends on one unique contribution, but the government retains its valid means of resolving it.

### Contextual MTTH

The proposed nominal active anchor is 180 days, rounded upward to a multiple of five and clamped to 60–540 days. A documented refusal of a binding order applies factor 0.65. A unique critical service with no qualified substitute applies 0.75. Existing independent armed security applies 0.80. A qualified continuity branch that has publicly claimed separate authority applies 0.85. A foreign sponsor that has actually committed support applies 0.85. Completed independent replication applies 1.50. A valid negotiated review already progressing applies 1.25.

Each factor needs a distinct current cause. A single guard unit cannot count as private security, a loyal field army, and a foreign intervention. A foreign promise that has not been accepted or funded does not shorten the clock. A refusal plus a unique critical service gives a nominal 90 days after rounding. Completed independent replication and an active review give 340 days. These are design arithmetic, not game timing evidence.

The pending delivery belongs to one responsible institutional confrontation. Several Wus cannot each schedule a duplicate fourth evolution. A change of host, actual capture, disappearance, or institutional collapse revalidates the incident. A transfer does not travel backward in chronology or award a new evolved opening reward.

Activation records Sovereign Biology once and gives zero activation Chaos. The starting confrontation contains the exact powers, demands, institutions, and obligations already established by the campaign.

## The power base that can answer Wu

The government sees a qualitative account of actual support. It can inspect the institutions and formations relevant to the current demand. The event does not introduce another permanent public sovereignty score or a universal loyalty meter.

| Existing source of leverage | What it can support | What it cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public medical confidence | Support for an appointment, an inquiry, or a constitutional medical charter | Control of territory, soldiers, or every treated person's politics. |
| Personally controlled treatment works | Refusal of future uncommitted output and leverage over its clients | Seizure of stock held by other countries or reversal of delivered medicine. |
| Unique qualified care service | A real deadline for dependent recipients | Permission to erase recipients or create a cure on demand. |
| Independent institutional security | Resistance at the site it actually guards | A national army or possession of every state containing a clinic. |
| Enhanced or clone formations | Support from formations with actual allegiance and command access | Free duplication, automatic loyalty, or complete transfer of the host's army. |
| Dangerous retained material | A credible restricted-material dispute when custody and use capability exist | An automatic outbreak, uncontrolled global contamination, or scripted native refund. |
| Qualified continuity branches | Surviving expertise and more than one possible claimant | Shared omniscience or several copies of national resources. |
| Foreign support | Accepted recognition, protection, supplies, or refuge | Instant intervention, automatic military access, or a guaranteed naval route. |
| Joint Kruger institution | Shared capacity and contested jurisdiction over joint contributions | Ownership of Kruger's unrelated projects or a second canonical Kruger. |

Material, people, institutions, and legal rights remain separate. A hospital can support Wu's clinical leadership while rejecting his political claim. A military commander can demand continued medicine while refusing to defect. A university can preserve a discovery and oppose its creator's private authority.

## The opening demand

The demand concerns one central power and identifies the institutions affected. Four working demand families are sufficient to express the different portfolios without creating a generic escalation ladder.

**Medical jurisdiction** asks for control of hospital appointments, clinical standards, or emergency allocation. A public route can face this demand when popular confidence has concentrated on Wu. The government can preserve clinical autonomy while retaining legal and budgetary control.

**Institutional independence** asks for protected facilities, a defined budget, archive custody, and freedom from ordinary suspension. A multi-site institute can use this to secure a legal space that persists beyond one cabinet. The state can offer a limited charter or insist on independent access and spending control.

**Command jurisdiction** asks for authority over specific enhanced formations, handlers, or private security. Military medicine gives this demand force only when named units really depend on the institute and their command relationships support it. A cure contract alone grants no command.

**Continuity and succession** asks the state to recognize a particular Wu, a council of qualified copies, or an institutional succession rule. The problem is who can hold offices and accept obligations. Claims about being the original are historical questions, not an automatic test of political legitimacy or professional competence.

A demand has a proposed 180-day settlement horizon. This is a political review period. A shorter care deadline remains shorter. Early lawful resolution closes the demand without requiring the player to wait out the whole horizon. A 60-day emergency phase can replace that horizon only after an actual seizure, armed refusal, or immediate loss of life-support access makes the normal period impossible.

## Keeping patients outside the bargaining

The government can establish a care continuity arrangement before deciding the political outcome. It identifies current recipients, compatible treatment, physical stock, qualified teams, remaining service intervals, and a lawful temporary authority. That arrangement preserves actual care and cannot award new research or duplicate a reserve.

Wu may agree, refuse, or lack the capacity to fulfill it. Refusal of future discretionary medicine differs from diversion of already committed patient stock. The latter is a specific breach with responsible actors, affected obligations, and evidence. Independent providers can take over only when their qualification and delivery interval meet the real deadline.

A patient protection agreement can include foreign assistance, but requires a valid route, reception proof, and actual contribution. Missing food-safety evidence blocks admission. People who cannot move remain in their real place and custody with continuing demand. Political urgency does not change the finite first-settlement rule for new people.

Protection is not conditional on endorsing a scientist. A government that conditions essential care on loyalty creates its own coercion record. An institution that continues to treat both sides does not automatically join either army or receive immunity from unrelated investigations.

## The government's strategic choices

### Retain public authority through a clinical constitution

The government offers a charter with enforceable rights for clinical independence and enforceable limits on budgets, custody, military command, and appointments. It must support the arrangement with qualified reviewers and alternative services. A paper charter without access or staff cannot remove a practical dependency.

Wu can accept a senior professional role, negotiate a narrow reserved domain, refuse and face removal, or leave under agreed terms. Independent institutions retain the knowledge and roles they already hold. The state wins when binding orders can be carried out without abandoning care or conceding the demanded sovereign powers.

### Grant limited institutional autonomy

The government gives Wu a defined legal domain inside the existing country. The charter specifies sites, budget, permitted programmes, civilian protection, audit rights, and the rules for renewal and removal. The host remains the sovereign country and owns its ordinary diplomacy, armed forces, and territory.

This path can preserve valuable cooperation at the cost of harder oversight. Autonomy is not a free technology reward or a new country disguised as an idea. If Wu later violates the charter, the case concerns the violated powers and current institutions. Earlier settled concessions are not charged again.

### Divide authority between independent institutions

The state transfers specified administration to qualified hospitals, universities, military medicine, and regional works. Wu may keep research leadership or a limited appointment. The transition needs real archives, staff, and compatible care, and cannot instantly make every institution independent.

A successful division removes a particular personal bottleneck. It can also reveal incompatible standards or insufficient teaching capacity. The government can phase the transfer by service, leaving urgent medicine under a temporary arrangement until a successor is ready.

### Remove, arrest, or exile Wu

Removal from office, custody, and expulsion are separate outcomes developed in Part 16. Removing a title does not seize a guarded laboratory. Arrest requires actual access to the person and a capable operation. Exile needs a destination, safe movement, and terms for existing patients and portable material.

The state can fail to secure a person without losing the entire country. It can also capture the person while losing access to a hidden archive. The outcome follows the actual target and conditions, with uncertain findings left uncertain.

### Recognize a separate political entity

Recognition requires an actual territorial or jurisdictional settlement. It can be negotiated or follow a conflict. A clinic network is not automatically territory. Part 17 defines the complete country package when a real state is created.

The former host can negotiate transit, medical services, property claims, demobilization, and civilian rights. Recognition does not erase prior deaths, coercion, or foreign obligations. It also does not grant all treated territory as cores or all former patients as citizens of Wu's new state.

## Institutional defiance and armed confrontation

A refusal begins with a concrete order that a named institution will not carry out. The government can negotiate, protect staff, secure another service, enforce a lawful transfer, or escalate. A refusal at one works does not mean every hospital has joined a revolt.

Armed conflict requires actual armed resistance and a territorial or command situation that the war owner can represent. The event can transfer a consenting or defiant formation only through a validated owner transaction. If precise transfer is not supported, it remains an implementation blocker. The coding agent may not delete the formation and grant a fresh full-strength replacement as a silent approximation.

Supply and care remain relevant during fighting. A well-armed breakaway can still fail if its treatment works is lost. A government can win the main laboratory and inherit patients it cannot immediately support. A neutral clinic can be evacuated or remain under a protected local agreement. These are playable consequences, not automatic rewards for occupying a state.

A blockade does not automatically kill a fixed population. Food, route, care, and disease owners evaluate their actual conditions. Attacks use existing military or native-operation owners. The event does not attach an automatic infection effect to ordinary movement or combat by Carrier formations.

## Several Wus and one Kruger

Each qualified Wu can support a settlement, claim an independent office, remain neutral, or defect. Their affiliations follow their appointments and choices. They cannot each exercise the same exclusive national mandate at the same time. A shared council requires a charter that assigns responsibility and prevents duplicate commands.

A copy can preserve clinical continuity after the original is removed while rejecting the original's political programme. A copy that lacks the necessary archive cannot become a universal substitute by winning a political vote. The government must distinguish political recognition from professional qualification.

When Kruger is co-resident, the six relationships in Part 10 determine the starting jurisdiction. Ordered division protects separate contributions. Competitive grants leave project-specific rights. A joint institute can negotiate a dual charter or divide contributions. Wu or Kruger supremacy gives the stronger institution practical leverage but does not erase the other's knowledge or historical ownership.

Kruger's sovereignty board and Wu's confrontation remain separate owner systems. Shared sites, staff, and stock require one common custody fact, not two incompatible settlements. A joint outcome must be explicitly accepted by both owners and recorded once for each actual contribution. It must not create a second KRG character or invoke Kruger's terminal routes as a shortcut.

Cross-border scientists use Part 13's international agreements. Foreign recognition can support Wu politically, but cannot force Kruger's host to release him or give Wu access to unrelated technology.

## The evolved first opening at 800 Chaos

This opening presents Wu as the visible head of a transnational ordinary medical network. The opening dossier must identify real participating hospitals, donors, staff, and foreign commitments. Those institutions use ordinary medicine and existing resources. Their association with Wu does not mean they possess advanced cloning, engineered organisms, or every universal cure.

The initial owner establishes the temporary network from compatible existing providers and explicitly funded opening commitments. Each site's employer, stock, staff, and permissions remain identifiable. Donors contribute actual resources through a valid transaction. An unfulfilled promise stays an unfulfilled promise. The normal initial medical proof remains bounded.

The host can absorb the network into supervised national medicine, recognize a limited compact, appoint Wu under military terms, or refuse him and manage the existing foreign and domestic commitments separately. The choice changes who may direct the network. It does not create a free advanced portfolio or transfer another government's hospitals.

The higher-tier context is eligible only when the opening can honestly establish that network and political claim. Reaching 800 Chaos alone does not manufacture it. When those facts are absent, the ordinary valid arrival can still occur and the fourth evolution remains subject to its active-chain requirements. This follows the brief's relevant-condition rule rather than treating a Chaos threshold as an automatic content grant.

Refusal leaves the real participants and copied emergency knowledge with their owners. Wu can seek another valid appointment or go underground. A donor cannot reclaim consumed medicine by cancelling its support, and the refused host does not retain Wu as a captive unless an actual custody action occurs.

## Disabled continuity

Before delivery, disabling the fourth evolution prevents its demand, evolved political AI, and new sovereign claim. Baseline appointments, dismissal, voluntary transfer, and ordinary foreign protection remain possible.

With a pending confrontation, disabling stops an uncommitted evolved escalation and returns remaining institutional choices to their baseline or already accepted legal authority. It does not remove people, undo accepted shipments, erase evidence, or cancel native reservations through Wu code.

After a country, war, custody change, or treaty really exists, the toggle cannot reverse those facts. It stops new evolution-specific demands and claimant recruitment. Existing actors continue through ordinary country, war, care, and settlement owners. A safe transition can end the special confrontation while preserving the actual political outcome.

Re-enabling an already delivered fourth evolution reconciles surviving institutions and offices without another activation or free country. A resolved constitutional settlement remains resolved unless a new material breach occurs. Earlier disabled positions retain the proposed N-D11 disposition and its separate acceptance requirement.

## AI and campaign outcomes

A strong public host should usually prefer a clinical constitution or independent administration when its real services can support them. A dependent wartime host may accept limited autonomy to preserve an urgent military service. A weak host can negotiate exile or a protected compact instead of attempting an arrest it cannot carry out. A government that has funded private armed institutions may choose confrontation, but the event must make the force and care consequences visible.

Wu's response follows actual survival, autonomy, research access, followers, and care responsibilities. A supervised doctor without guards should not prefer a hopeless armed breakaway. A well-supported sovereign claimant can negotiate a viable compact instead of always fighting. Qualified copies can make different choices because they hold different offices and obligations.
