# Doctor Wu: Forbidden and Autonomous Biology

## When the approved project no longer describes the work

A hospital is still receiving the treatment it ordered, but an additional group now appears in the institute's follow-up records. A research team has spent the materials assigned to a cancelled project. A formation receives an unfamiliar control instruction from a laboratory its ministry never approved. These discrepancies give the government specific objects to investigate.

At 600 Chaos, this evolution allows existing programmes to develop unauthorized extensions. The institute may preserve material, divert staff, produce a different standard, create unreported people, or demand approval after work has already begun. The government can expose and stop the extension, regularize a bounded part, protect independent care, or deliberately let Wu acquire greater autonomy.

The change concerns permission, disclosure, institutional control, and the consequences of real work. It grants no technology and no resources. Every compatible research family remains baseline and reachable in Calm World. The names and numerical anchors below are proposed design, and all biological mechanisms remain fictional game rules without real experimental procedures.

## Active-chain admission and pacing

Eligibility requires Chaos of at least 600, a functioning Wu institution, a qualified active Wu or continuity branch, and one supported cause of autonomous work. Causes include a dangerous project with a documented unresolved oversight failure, repeated approval of extreme methods under weak review, an actual unauthorized project finding, or a delegated domain that Wu has begun using beyond its agreed limits. High Authority can increase opportunity, but a number alone is not proof of an experiment.

A fully supervised public route does not automatically receive a national covert programme when the threshold is crossed. A limited incident can still concern one real staff member or one existing project. Independent review then gives the host a strong practical response. A harmless completed cure with no deviation, unexplained missing supplies, or relevant access does not qualify solely because the campaign is chaotic.

The proposed nominal MTTH is 150 days, rounded upward to a multiple of five and clamped to 45–450 days. An unresolved unauthorized extension multiplies it by 0.65. A genuinely exclusive personal facility multiplies it by 0.80. Repeated accepted extreme methods multiply it by 0.80. A public emergency that has already weakened supervision multiplies it by 0.75. An active independent review multiplies it by 1.50, while a completed enforceable division of custody multiplies it by 1.25. Each distinct causal fact applies once. The same incident cannot be counted as four different causes.

An exclusive facility and an unresolved extension yield a nominal 80 days. An active independent review without other factors yields 225 days. These calculations are tuning examples, not verified game probabilities. Losing the only usable institute or available qualified scientist suspends an undelivered evolution.

The active entry exposes a bounded discrepancy involving work that already exists. It records the evolution with zero activation Chaos. If harmful work subsequently occurs, its actual owner records the resources, patients, results, evidence, and consequences. The milestone cannot retroactively create those acts.

## A hidden project still occupies the institute

The hidden-work record has a real institution, responsible person, source family, approved brief, deviation, material assignment, staff commitment, current stage, and affected recipients or custody object where relevant. Hidden means concealed from a government or foreign actor. It never means absent from the game's authoritative accounting.

A private team consumes the same staff and project capacity that ordinary work uses. A concealed production order consumes actual input and produces physical stock with a fixed identity. A living person created outside the public register still has finite cohort accounting, food demand, custody, and a unique first-settlement entitlement. A government may be unaware of those facts, but the simulation cannot ignore them.

If Wu lacks the prerequisites for the proposed extension, he can demand them, conceal a preliminary study, negotiate with another institution, or fail to proceed. He cannot use this evolution to complete cloning, manufacture a modified agent, or create an engineered formation before the baseline family permits it. An incomplete attempt can leave evidence and costs without a completed capability.

A project can be concealed from one actor and known to another. An assistant may know its staffing. A military purchaser may know the delivered standard. A foreign sponsor may know only the promised result. Discovering one aspect does not automatically reveal every country, subject, or archive connected to the institution.

## Autonomous extensions by research family

### Preserved samples and withheld results

A claim that samples were destroyed can be false when the destruction never physically occurred. The game may record that an official report asserted destruction while an actual quantity remained in custody. It must not resurrect stock that a valid destruction transaction really removed. An investigation can therefore discover an existing hidden reserve, not a new reserve produced by changing a flag.

Wu can retain unpublished knowledge from a completed project. That changes who can reproduce or audit it, but does not reverse the completion, delete lawful copies, or supply another laboratory with the result. The government can seek a licensed copy, protect the staff who know it, preserve a backup, or close access to further public resources.

A failed organism may remain in a real containment place instead of being destroyed. Its continuing husbandry and security costs remain payable. If those costs fail, the organism's owner determines the actual incident. The choice cannot both retain the organism for study and receive the safety result of verified destruction.

### A second version of a cure

An unauthorized version is a new fixed design with its own qualification and production history. It may be a useful adaptation, an incomplete response to a changed disease, or an unsafe attempt to extend a known treatment. It does not rewrite existing medicine or the outcome of patients already treated.

The government can isolate unused stock, assess current recipients, commission independent validation, authorize a restricted continuation, or withdraw the version. A useful result can become ordinary approved knowledge after review. Acceptance is limited to the result actually proved and does not endorse every act used to obtain it.

A cure that suppresses symptoms without stopping spread is represented as two separate owner outputs. The clinical benefit remains real. Disease transmission follows the underlying disease's supported result. The player must see enough clinical uncertainty to choose follow-up and containment, without being shown an omniscient answer about every hidden defect.

### Intrusive treatment conditions

The institute can attach unauthorized surveillance, dependence, obedience, or fertility-related conditions to a fictional treatment programme. These remain distinct game-level deviations with different counterplay. Surveillance can be unauthorized follow-up and access to records. Dependence creates a continuing care obligation tied to actual recipients. Obedience affects an established command relationship only where that family supports it. Fertility effects require a specific fictional medical outcome and cannot retroactively change population already counted.

No biological property is assigned by ethnicity, religion, nationality, or political belief. Political conformity is not a disease that Wu can cure. An official claim about a population's obedience is not proof that its individuals have lost agency. If a family cannot support the requested fictional effect, the extension remains an attempted claim or an explicit implementation blocker, never a generic ideology modifier.

Exposure creates options to restore consent, separate clinical care from intelligence access, replace the protocol, provide independent treatment, and investigate responsibility. Patients do not lose necessary care as an automatic punishment for having received a concealed treatment.

### Long-term subject groups

Treated patients can be enrolled in unauthorized long-term follow-up. The record names the existing people, the institution responsible for contacting or confining them, and the scope of interference. Creating this record does not debit the population or create a second cohort for the same people.

Soldiers, prisoners, refugees, occupied civilians, and institute staff retain their original identity and custody. Access to one group does not imply permission to experiment on it. Coercion, confinement, and injury generate their proper evidence and care consequences. A public route can expose and close such a programme while preserving valid medicine developed separately.

The event does not provide a menu for real harmful testing methods. The playable problem is discovering an unauthorized act, protecting affected people, preserving evidence, and deciding what the institution may do next.

### Enhancement beyond the agreed tour

A formation may receive a stronger fictional course than its military brief approved, or remain on a temporary course beyond its funded tour. Any new physical effect begins only after a real application to identified recipients. It cannot be applied to every national division because the institute has reached an evolution.

A commander may conceal dependence to keep the formation in service. Withdrawal can reduce operational performance and require care. The government can rotate the formation, fund an independent provider, regularize a specific course, or order an early retirement. Existing lifelong care from permanent alteration does not disappear when a military programme is closed.

Enhanced veterans can support Wu politically because he supplies their care, oppose him after injury, or seek independent treatment. A veteran's biological status alone does not determine allegiance. Loyalty requires recorded institutional or personal relations and a valid political choice.

### Unreported clone batches and continuity bodies

A concealed batch consumes finite production inputs and occupies real maturation and training capacity. Its people are counted by Part 7 even when the government has not discovered them. Hiding the batch never enables recurring legacy reserve manpower or a second civilian credit.

On discovery, the government can protect the people, continue necessary maturation and care, investigate the responsible institution, and decide their legal reception. It cannot delete living people as a routine inventory cleanup. A failed or dead batch is settled by the proper medical and Deaths owners with one physical loss.

An undisclosed Wu continuity body must be produced, receive the appropriate archive, mature, qualify, and accept an appointment. A body alone is not an active scientific successor. Several qualified Wus can disagree about the unauthorized programme. Their individual knowledge, consent, offices, and loyalties decide which one can continue or oppose it.

A government that discovers a hidden qualified copy can offer supervised employment or independent refuge. Discovery does not grant automatic obedience, remove the original Wu, or make the copy legally responsible for acts it did not perform.

### Changed control of engineered organisms

A deployed organism can be affected by an unauthorized control-method change only through a real conversion or service action. The old command record remains until that action succeeds. Already reserved treatment stays attached to its original standard and recipients.

The five lineages remain distinct. Command organisms depend heavily on reliable handlers and orders. Assault organisms impose the greatest safe transport and support burden. Hunters can become isolated from their control service. Carriers retain the separate native-operation boundary. Guardians rely on their prepared sites. A single secret instruction cannot turn all five into one free army.

An attempted control takeover can fail, be detected, or be accepted by a qualified independent operator. Counterplay includes isolating the affected order channel, providing the matching service, rotating handlers, recalling the specific group, or using an already qualified shutdown treatment. Shutdown does not restore dead people and does not delete an independent outbreak actor.

### Designed life and living machinery

An unauthorized filter colony, reconnaissance organism, agricultural treatment, or living-machine interface retains its actual role and support bill. A private extension might broaden surveillance, preserve a damaged organism, or divert output from public work. The resulting benefit and failure remain specific to that service.

A filter cannot remove fallout because Wu concealed its design. Agricultural medicine cannot replace food reserves or transport. A new population-restoration batch still needs receiving places and current food proof. A living machine requires both its biological service and mechanical owner. The evolution supplies no blanket multiplier that makes all designed life stronger.

Joint Wu–Kruger extensions require actual access to both contributions. One scientist can conceal a result from the other, but cannot acquire the other's private project ledger or equipment through a narrative accusation. Part 10's relationship determines which authority can investigate each contribution.

## How the government learns about the deviation

Evidence can come from missing assigned materials, staff testimony, contradictory recipient records, an unreported custody group, an escaped organism, a foreign leak, or a specific accusation by Kruger. Each source identifies what it can actually establish. Missing stores establish a discrepancy, not a confirmed weapon. A patient's report establishes an experienced condition, not the identity of a foreign sponsor.

The government has three increasing levels of actionable knowledge: a discrepancy worth checking, an authenticated deviation, and an attributed harmful act. These are case states, not another permanent public meter. A later finding may resolve one part of the case while leaving another uncertain.

Independent archives improve the ability to compare instructions. Qualified staff make clinical findings more reliable. Access to the actual site allows custody and stock checks. Protection of witnesses can preserve evidence but must not be confused with suppression or detention. An unqualified security raid can damage records without solving the medical issue.

A malicious accusation is possible, particularly during laboratory conflict or international competition. Disproving it changes the credibility of the accuser and closes that allegation. It does not certify every unrelated project as safe. Investigations should not reward repeated baseless accusations with free Authority reductions or generic intelligence gains.

## Four government responses

### Isolate the deviation and preserve medicine

The government suspends the specific unauthorized work, preserves records, protects affected people, and appoints a qualified review. Unaffected cure production and current care continue. This is the strongest response for a low-Authority public institute with independent staff and adequate replacement services.

The response costs review capacity and can delay the selected project. It reduces Wu's control only over rights actually removed. It does not remove permanent knowledge or grant full independent competence to the reviewer. A successful review can return an approved corrected version to service.

### Negotiate a bounded extension

The government acknowledges the useful part of the result and writes an explicit permission for a defined scope, duration, recipient group, and independent review. Wu obtains no general immunity. Existing evidence of harm remains discoverable, and prior victims retain care and compensation claims.

This path can keep urgently needed medicine available when a complete shutdown would cause immediate harm. Its danger is renewal under pressure. A new extension needs another actual concession or new resources, not an automatic Authority increase every time patients receive their scheduled care.

### Delegate further private work

The government deliberately permits Wu to control more funding, staff, or custody. The new autonomy must correspond to specific rights. A public signature does not supply additional factories, samples, or people. The current case becomes an accepted political responsibility, while its hidden earlier acts keep their evidence.

This route supports rapid personal decision-making and stronger institutional leverage. It also makes independent inspection and future removal harder. The player sees the scope of the delegation and the continuing burden before accepting it. An attractive result never conceals that the state is giving up a real power.

### Close the responsible programme

The government withdraws the programme's permission, stops new admissions, secures actual material, and carries out a qualified care and custody plan. Closure may preserve harmless knowledge, transfer a valid clinical service, or destroy dangerous stock through its owner.

The state can close one programme without abolishing every Wu institution. A wider closure is a separate political outcome under Parts 15–16. A programme with living recipients cannot be made safe by clearing its active flag. Its care and food obligations remain until real discharge, transfer, settlement, or death is recorded.

## Wu's response and the limits of resistance

Wu can comply, negotiate, offer a narrower disclosure, shift permitted work to another qualified branch, or resist through staff and institutions that actually support him. Resistance uses real delegated control, protected sites, dependent services, or political allies. The evolution does not guarantee that every audit fails.

Low Authority, independent production, and distributed archives make isolation of a deviation more effective. High Authority and personal control of care give Wu more bargaining power. Military reliance can make commanders reluctant to suspend treatment. Foreign sponsorship can offer an exit route. Several continuity copies can divide those resources and weaken any one claimant.

Concealment cannot teleport material, duplicate a staff team, erase a completed seizure, or move a living person through a closed route. If the government actually controls the site and has a qualified custody service, a transfer requires the corresponding physical transaction. Political resistance and physical capacity are both needed for a successful evasion.

The host remains able to win through preparation. A supervised settlement stops new autonomous extensions in the controlled domain, retains this evolution as history, and returns current programmes to their baseline permissions. A later breach requires a materially new cause. Replaying the same discrepancy cannot reopen the entire crisis indefinitely.

## Evolved first opening

The 600-tier opening shows that Wu has treated the contained emergency using an unapproved method. The original clinical proof remains real and bounded. The government discovers a withheld sample, incomplete record, or second set of people already present in the opening's medical context.

Those people must have an explicit origin and existing population or custody status. The opening cannot invent a hidden clone batch before cloning exists. It can establish ordinary patients whose treatment was not properly authorized. The doctor's success does not make the method generally qualified or grant an advanced research project.

After choosing a mandate, the host can require disclosure, preserve independent oversight, permit a defined emergency extension, or refuse the appointment and follow the normal referral route. Surviving patients retain their care. Refusal does not erase the unauthorized opening act or transfer its responsibility to the next host.

A combined higher-tier opening presents one coherent medical and political situation with phased decisions. International interest and national coordination remain based on ordinary institutions and actual contacts. The player is not forced to approve four complete institutions in succession before any normal play begins.

## Disabled continuity and later projects

Disabling before delivery prevents the evolution and new autonomous extensions. Baseline classified projects, intentional permission, existing risks, and ordinary misconduct already supported by other owners remain available. They are not relabeled as this disabled milestone.

Disabling during a pending extension prevents the uncommitted evolution-owned deviation from progressing. Actual materials, existing people, consumed costs, injuries, evidence, and accepted care remain real. Hidden physical objects are reconciled to their owners rather than destroyed by a settings toggle. A pending native operation retains native cancellation ownership.

Disabling after delivery stops new evolution-specific autonomous project creation and the corresponding AI. Current cases settle through their existing care, custody, and investigation routes. Restoring the setting reconciles those surviving cases without replaying discoveries or producing new hidden stock. N-D11 remains subject to explicit shared-settings acceptance for earlier never-delivered bypassed stages.

A later baseline project inherits autonomous risk only when the active institutional conditions justify it. Completing a new cure under enforced independent review does not automatically create a secret second cure. A continuity copy with a supervised appointment does not inherit the original's personal right to conceal work. Institutional permissions are assessed separately from the person's scientific knowledge.

## Case resolution and aftermath

A case closes when the identified unauthorized work is stopped, explicitly regularized, transferred to a valid owner, or becomes part of an active sovereignty confrontation. Closure records which people still need care, which material remains, what knowledge is retained, and what evidence is unresolved. A political settlement can close the dispute over authority without closing an injury claim.

A successful public investigation can strengthen independent medical institutions by leaving them qualified, funded, and legally protected. It does not award another free research slot or a new spirit for every review. A private settlement can leave Wu in control of one restricted department while ordinary hospitals continue independent treatment. An outright failure can feed the next evolution only through a real loss of control or surviving personal power base.

Exposure of an atrocity uses the shared evidence and Condemnation owner once. Treatment of victims does not erase responsibility. If a controlled group becomes an independent actor, medical care cannot delete it. If an organism escapes, its owner determines whether there is an outbreak, an independent formation, or a recoverable escaped unit. The institute's failure is not a licence to create every possible catastrophe at once.

## Campaign examples and AI

A well-supervised public host has an independent archive and two qualified clinical services. It discovers an unapproved follow-up group. It can preserve testimony, replace the responsible appointment, and continue the successful cure. The episode leaves a contained institutional correction without forcing a coup.

A military host has useful enhanced formations and only one compatible care provider. An unauthorized stronger course is discovered during a war. Immediate closure would strand existing recipients. The host can fund interim treatment and reduce new enrolment while qualifying an alternative. Continuing the stronger course is a deliberate political choice with recorded obligations.

A classified multi-site host has funded hidden continuity work. Discovery reveals two living qualified Wus with different loyalties and an unsettled batch of ordinary people. The government must choose custody, reception, employment, and programme authority separately. It cannot resolve the situation by selecting which portrait looks original.

AI prioritizes actual care deadlines, containment, and preservation of living people before scientific credit. A supervised host favors targeted review and continuity. A coercive host can accept a wider private mandate when it has real resources and support, but inability to contain failure remains a hard constraint. A weak host seeks outside qualified assistance or programme closure rather than expanding every risky family.

## What autonomous work leaves for sovereignty

The evolution leaves a specific distribution of knowledge, authority, custody, loyal staff, dependent services, and exposed responsibility. Sovereign Biology reads that distribution. A government that made care reproducible and maintained access has practical leverage. A government that repeatedly accepted secrecy and personal command has ceded identifiable powers. The next confrontation grows from those choices and from the institutions that actually survived.
