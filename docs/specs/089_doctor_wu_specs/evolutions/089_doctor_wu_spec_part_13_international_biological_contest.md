# Doctor Wu: The International Biological Contest

## When medical success becomes an international contest

A foreign government that previously bought one emergency delivery now offers to maintain the entire treatment works. Another government offers Wu a protected appointment. A third has obtained an incomplete copy of the same treatment and is asking its hospitals to use it. These countries are pursuing different objects and accepting different risks. Their interaction makes this evolution an international contest.

The second evolution occurs at 400 Chaos. It connects the baseline foreign actions in Part 11 into project-specific campaigns with competing sponsors, shared information, limited production, and persistent responses to success or failure. The host can build a cooperative medical network, retain a valuable monopoly, separate civilian medicine from military secrecy, or become the target of a coordinated containment effort. None of those strategies creates knowledge or material that the underlying portfolio lacks.

All new labels, durations, formulas, limits, and institutional arrangements in this part are proposed design. Labels identify design content and are not final localisation. The biological content is entirely fictional game behavior. Harmful experiments and operations are represented by abstract authorization, evidence, custody, and outcome records without real methods.

## Eligibility and contextual timing

### The active-chain entry

The evolution becomes eligible when Chaos is at least 400, the event has a functioning institute and an available qualified Wu, and the existing portfolio has generated a meaningful international object of interest. At least one current foreign country must have a supported reason to care about that object. A major cure with a real foreign request, a revealed military application, a verified foreign research contact, or a public dispute over a significant result qualifies. A generic increase in world tension does not reveal a secret project.

Earlier evolution positions follow the ordered settings contract in Part 12. A disabled earlier stage can be bypassed without being recorded as having occurred. N-D11 remains an unapproved proposal about later re-enabling of a bypassed position. This part does not silently resolve that shared-settings decision.

The proposed nominal active MTTH is 120 days. Apply each distinct cause once, round upward to the next multiple of five, and clamp to 30–360 days. These are nominal pacing calculations, not measured means or guaranteed event dates.

Urgent need and two genuine competing offers produce a nominal 70 days after rounding. A route interruption and pending review produce 225 days. A disappearing foreign actor or destroyed institute can invalidate eligibility. A diplomatic delay cannot generate a second pending evolution.

Activation changes the relationship context and records the milestone once. It grants zero activation Chaos, zero research, zero material, zero appointments, and zero population. The first case uses the real project and foreign interest that made the evolution eligible.

## The unit of competition

Each contest concerns a named object: a treatment version and its next available output, a specimen held at an identified site, a teaching programme, an individual appointment, a specific design, or a defined security obligation. A broad national attitude determines priorities but cannot substitute for the object.

A country can be a patient, buyer, producer, transport provider, sponsor, rival laboratory, protector, or hostile actor. Roles can overlap. An ally can buy medicine and also recruit a researcher. A rival can support a humanitarian shipment without gaining military access. The host does not gain an automatic friend or enemy classification from one transaction.

Public facts, private knowledge, and physical access remain separate. A government may know that a cure exists without knowing its production standard. It may possess the standard without qualified production. It may receive medicine without access to the archive. The affected actor sees only the facts its observation, contract, intelligence, or clinical experience establishes.

The visible case has one main objective and usually three to five meaningful actions. The existing Part 11 limits remain in force: one discretionary covert Wu operation per actor, normally two incoming hostile Wu operations per host, and no diversion of clinical capacity through hostile operation slots. A contest groups those operations and diplomatic actions. It does not grant additional covert attempts or reset their histories.

The event keeps a bounded list of active cases and reveals only the most important current case in the category. Proposed presentation capacity is three active international case objectives per participating country. Care emergencies take precedence over commercial bids. Old offers remain searchable as records without remaining active objectives.

## Competing appointments for Wu and senior staff

### Offers that can actually be accepted

An appointment offer states its employer, place of work, legal protection, permitted research, working facilities, funding commitment, current care obligations, intended start, and exit terms. The receiving government must be able to supply the ordinary institution it promises. A pledge to create a laboratory is a construction commitment, not a completed laboratory.

Wu considers freedom to work, safety, access to relevant cases, treatment continuity, recognition, and the restrictions already imposed on him. Senior staff weigh their actual role. A regional clinical director values a functioning service and a trained replacement at the old hospital. An ambitious researcher may value access to a specimen that their current employer refuses to release. A disaffected assistant can accept an independent university appointment without joining Wu's political faction.

The host can make a retention offer, permit a visiting appointment, negotiate a secondment, prepare a permanent handover, or refuse institutional permission while allowing the person to decide whether to resign. Coercive confinement is a different action with different evidence and diplomatic consequences. A rejected offer does not automatically create a defection.

The same person cannot accept two full-time appointments covering the same interval. Acceptance reserves the person's future service only after existing commitments are reconciled. A visiting position has a start and end, and its host receives the stated teaching or clinical work rather than the scientist's entire capability package. A qualified continuity copy is a separate person, with the knowledge, legal identity, and assignments recorded in Part 7. Offering that copy a job does not transfer the original.

### A finite contest window

The proposed appointment window lasts 45 days from the first valid competing offer. A material counteroffer can extend consideration once by 15 days. Repeated trivial increases cannot hold a person in permanent negotiation. An active threat can shorten the travel decision only when a safe refuge already exists. It cannot shorten professional qualification.

At settlement, the person selects one valid offer or remains with the current employer. The result may be a deferred departure with a funded successor, an immediate safe departure without employment, a visiting term, or an unsuccessful negotiation. The departure transaction carries only the person, authorized portable records, and actual material that reaches the destination. National institutions and settled rights remain with their owners.

A foreign country that fails to fund its accepted appointment loses its reservation on the person's service. It retains responsibility for any harm or commitments already caused. The person can return, take refuge, or negotiate elsewhere according to actual access. The failed sponsor does not get the appointment reward and cannot claim that the person disappeared.

## Cure bidding and production competition

### Define the lot before bidding

A cure contest can sell access to a completed standard, a qualified production arrangement, a fixed amount of unreserved medicine, a future output reservation, or an emergency clinical team. Each lot has one object type. A bid for knowledge cannot silently include material or staff.

The seller publishes or privately shares the known treatment match, actual quantity or service capacity, available delivery interval, intended recipient episode, accepted payment types, and any access conditions. Existing patient reservations and accepted deliveries are excluded from the lot. A promised factory expansion is presented as future capacity with construction risk.

The ordinary cure-sharing policies from Part 4 remain available. Freely released knowledge has no exclusive licence to auction. A seller can still sell transport, physical medicine, or qualified services. A monopoly on a particular design does not prove control over every other valid cure for the disease.

### Compare offers without inventing a currency

The auction uses a declared contract objective. An emergency distribution objective ranks feasible delivery and clinical need first. A commercial output sale ranks the agreed same-type payment. An industrial partnership compares the promised working capacity and permitted access against explicit minimum conditions. Unlike resources are not converted into an invented universal money balance.

Each bid contains at most four spendable or committed cost types. The selected payment is reserved from the bidder's real resources. The bid can name one authorized alternative package, but both packages cannot be charged. Political concessions and access promises are explicit treaty terms with their own consequences.

The proposed offer window is 30 days with one 15-day extension for a materially improved feasible offer. Severe medical cases can use a 15-day window when delay would defeat the clinical purpose. Immediate baseline emergency aid remains available throughout and never waits for the auction to finish. Non-emergency commercial preference cannot overwrite an accepted patient reservation.

The seller can accept, reject all, split an explicitly divisible lot, or issue a counteroffer. An exclusive lot has one winner. Divisible medicine has a total award quantity that cannot exceed available stock. Each accepted slice acquires its own recipient and delivery commitment without creating another copy of the lot.

### Settlement and partial delivery

The baseline payment and delivery owners settle accepted transfers. Losing bidders recover only their unspent reservations, not consumed preparation. The seller receives value only through the agreed transfer. Future production remains subject to actual factory operation and qualification. A failed delivery creates a breach or partial settlement, not a scripted refund from nowhere.

For example, a producer has 100 allocations. Forty are already committed to domestic patients and twenty to an earlier foreign order. Only forty can be offered. Two accepted awards of twenty-five and fifteen use that remaining amount exactly. If the first buyer later accepts ten of its twenty-five, the fifteen undelivered allocations remain with their proven holder and contract status. The second buyer's fifteen are unaffected. An auction result cannot relabel domestic stock as an additional donation.

## Withheld specimens and withheld information

A specimen agreement distinguishes a physical sample, clinical access to living patients, anonymized observations, a treatment-match report, and production knowledge. Governments may lawfully restrict some of these while continuing other cooperation. The case identifies what is missing and which project actually needs it.

The host can negotiate narrower access, invite an independent observer, offer reciprocal data, pay for a controlled custody transfer, seek another valid source, or continue a slower clinical route using the cases it already has. A foreign refusal cannot make a biological disease permanently incurable. It can delay a particular source route and increase the cost of obtaining enough evidence through an alternative.

Deliberately false information is separate from silence. A fabricated strain label can cause a matching review to fail or an unqualified order to be stopped. It cannot automatically infect patients. Actual treatment harm requires a real application and the disease or medical owner's result. Verified deception changes trust in the named supplier, not all institutions of the same ideology.

Patients are never treated as transferable specimens without their existing custody and population context. A refugee with a useful medical history remains a person requiring reception and food. A government cannot create a research subject by adding a sample variable. Withholding a clinical archive does not remove copies already lawfully held elsewhere.

## Rival laboratories

### Three credible competitors

A rival laboratory can be a licensed independent producer, a conventional institute researching a narrow answer, or a holder of incomplete stolen work. These routes have different strengths. The licensed producer can compete in reliable scale. The conventional institute can discover a bounded treatment within its own system. The thief may know an unusual design but lack the matching staff, source material, or quality record.

No rival receives universal Wu competence because the contest opened. A laboratory begins from actual facilities, qualified staff, copied or researched knowledge, and a funded project. An announcement creates public expectations only. A rival's successful claim must be tied to a completed object and an accepted result.

The host can recognize equivalence, cooperate on production, offer a controlled comparison, challenge a specific unsupported claim, recruit the team, or restrict access to proprietary new work. A valid independent success survives a political refusal to recognize it. Recognition affects access and credibility, not whether the medicine physically works.

### Competition that changes play

A producer with higher verified capacity can take future orders away from Wu's home works. An institute that trains independent clinical teams can reduce personal dependence. A rival with a stronger military patron may attract staff but lose civilian cooperation after an exposed abuse. A weak laboratory can remain useful as a local provider for one common disease without entering every exotic project family.

Rival labs have their own care obligations and failure records. A country can close a failed manufacturing line while preserving valid clinical work. An old unsafe design does not contaminate every later design from the same institution. Conversely, renaming the institute does not clear the failed lot or erase the responsibility of its sponsors.

Independent labs interact through existing baseline research and foreign service costs. Evolution II adds coordinated offers, comparisons, and responses to the results. It does not create a second global research tree or a new public scientific prestige meter.

## Counterfeit, degraded, and incompatible medicine

A quality case begins with an identifiable mismatch: a forged maker claim, damaged transported stock, an incomplete manufacturing process, an old treatment used against a changed disease, or a deliberately altered consignment. These causes must remain distinguishable. A legitimate supplier is not automatically guilty because a treatment failed.

The first response is to identify actual distributed lots and affected services. Unused suspect material can be quarantined. Existing recipients receive qualified assessment and replacement where it is available. Unaffected stock and unrelated medicine continue operating. A nationwide suspension is a deliberate political response with an exposed service cost, not the default result of uncertainty.

The host can send an inspection team, exchange retained records, withdraw the specific standard, publish a limited defect notice, compensate a proven breach, or challenge a false accusation. These actions alter access and confidence. Only actual replacement deliveries or treatment change the recipients' condition.

A counterfeit certificate does not grant a technology or a valid quality record. Degraded stock retains its physical identity and measured remaining condition. Repair or conversion needs an owner-supported process. Destroying it consumes the actual quantity and preserves its provenance and any earlier harm.

A commercial rival may exploit a genuine defect by offering a qualified replacement. An intelligence service may spread an unsupported allegation. The public record distinguishes confirmed findings, unresolved allegations, and disproved claims. Investigations cannot produce convenient perfect attribution merely because one actor is hostile.

## Stolen incomplete designs

A theft result states exactly which portion was obtained. Possible objects include theoretical notes, a dated clinical record, a manufacturing drawing, a control-treatment description, a qualified archive copy, or an actual item. The thief receives no missing prerequisites and no automatically functioning unit.

An incomplete design can support a recovery project. The receiving institute must obtain missing qualification, reproduce the allowed game-level result, and establish a safe service route. It can abandon the work, purchase the missing rights, cooperate with a third laboratory, or fund its own narrow validation. The new capability remains subject to the original family owner and baseline costs.

A poor copy of a continuity archive does not recreate Wu. A stolen clone material item does not create both reserve manpower and civilian people. A copied control treatment does not give command over existing formations. A captured biological item cannot be used as a generic research token and remain in the original holder's stock at the same time.

Hidden harmful testing of a stolen treatment is a possible fictional incident for an actor already capable of coercion and actual access. Its record names the responsible institution, affected living group, missing consent, and eventual medical and evidence consequences. The game exposes opportunities to protect subjects, stop the project, preserve testimony, and provide care. This design specifies no real experimental procedure, exposure method, or operational advantage from victim selection.

## Protection, custody, and hostile pressure

A protective compact grants specified protection and safe access, not ownership of Wu. He can retain a clinical appointment, enter provisional refuge, or leave a sponsor that breaks its terms. The protecting country commits its actual personnel or military resources and can be called on to protect a named site or route. Diplomatic protection without reach remains a promise with a visible limitation.

A hostile operation continues to use Part 11's prerequisites, exact target, costs, and outcome separation. Evolution II lets several governments exchange permitted evidence, choose different objectives, and react to one another. It does not combine their operation slots into a free simultaneous strike. Each acting country must qualify and pay for its own action.

A coalition investigation can make an exposed military programme a shared concern. A separate country can still negotiate medicine while opposing that programme. Abduction, assassination, sabotage, and attacks require real hostile intent and access. Refusing an auction bid alone does not automatically authorize an assassination preference.

A successful abduction changes custody only. Employment, archive access, and cooperation remain separate. An assassination outcome follows the actual targeted person. Another living Wu may continue work, but cannot erase that person's recorded death. Kruger's continuity has its own owner contract and must not be rewritten by this event.

## Medical blocs and sanctions

### A production and access bloc

A medical bloc can coordinate qualified producers, common quality review, reciprocal teaching, reserve delivery commitments, and protection of agreed routes. Its founding charter lists contributions and exceptions. It is not automatically a military faction and has no duplicate common stockpile.

Three proposed charters deepen Part 11's coalition forms. A public access charter prioritizes need and published harmless standards. A strategic mutual-service charter protects members' military and civilian commitments while permitting narrower disclosure. A proprietary production compact accepts exclusive commercial terms and has the strongest bargaining leverage but the greatest dependence on a limited provider.

Members vote only on powers actually delegated to the bloc. One member cannot promise another member's stock, physicians, or intelligence. Common decisions can reserve proposed contributions, but each contributing owner accepts and commits its share. An incomplete coalition response remains partial instead of generating the missing contribution.

A member can withdraw from future participation while fulfilling existing care and delivery obligations. Emergency suspension can bar new access after a proven breach. It cannot retroactively revoke copied knowledge or confiscate an unrelated national laboratory. A medical compact may later support a military alliance through normal diplomacy, with an independent decision and the proper faction owner.

### Pressure for withheld cures

A government can face demands, public criticism, commercial exclusion, or diplomatic pressure for withholding a cure during a real emergency. The case must establish that a matching capability and feasible assistance existed, what was requested, and what the host refused. A country unable to manufacture or transport medicine cannot be treated as if it destroyed an available shipment.

Formal Condemnation and native embargoes remain shared-owner actions. A refusal alone is not relabeled as biological weapon use. Proven coercive withholding, treaty breach, an exposed experiment, or deliberate harm can supply the appropriate evidence context when the owner supports it. Mere patent disagreement can still produce bilateral consequences without fabricating an atrocity bucket.

A humanitarian carve-out reduces the practical burden specified by the sanctions owner. It does not establish a cargo exemption from a native embargo or bypass a material-transfer prohibition. Bloc membership also supplies no such exemption. Valid relief routes must be proved separately.

Medical sanctions can refuse future scientific access or new procurement. They cannot erase retained standards, revoke every existing licence through an unsupported helper, or seize reserved native material. Existing care is identified for protected handover or an explicit breach with real consequences.

## Cross-border Wu and Kruger

When the scientists serve different governments, their rivalry concerns actual overlapping work. A shared specimen can lead to competing access agreements. A biological-machine project can require both sides. A stolen archive can lead to a scientific-credit dispute. A military demonstration can prompt defensive cooperation among their clients.

The six domestic relationships in Part 10 are not applied to two different national governments. Neither scientist can grant jurisdiction over the other's country. Cross-border outcomes are contracts, joint projects, sponsored independent institutions, protected visits, hostile operations, or a negotiated move that passes the character owner's guards.

Kruger's private international contest remains gated by his own Evolution II. Wu's contest can observe and react to a verified Kruger outcome without initializing that contest or writing its operation records. If Kruger's contest is inactive, baseline diplomacy and authorized public projects remain possible. There is no replacement backdoor into his locked operation category.

Joint research uses separate contribution ownership. Wu can provide medicine, living-system compatibility, or qualified biological staff. Kruger can provide a machine, facility, or other accepted contribution. Completion grants only the agreed result. It does not merge their whole portfolios or award the same prototype twice.

A scientist's transfer can bring the two into one country. At that point Part 10 settles the new domestic overlap from the actual institutions. Cross-border contracts, foreign copies, and settled national rights do not disappear. If they later separate again, the former domestic arrangement leaves its real rights and obligations with the proper owners.

## The evolved first opening

At the 400-tier opening, several foreign governments know about Wu's arrival from the bounded medical proof or prior public clinical contacts. Their awareness is limited to what the opening establishes. A donor may offer ordinary hospital space, a threatened country may request treatment, and a rival may want an observer at the first appointment.

The host receives the normal mandate decision and foundation. Foreign offers appear after that choice has a valid recipient, with at most the normal current action budget. An immediate refuge offer can protect a person, but a promised institute still needs facilities, qualification, and funding. Covert teams have opportunity and intent only when their normal access and costs are satisfied.

Refusal follows the original referral route. Foreign awareness of the refused appointment remains a fact, but the failed host receives no medical institute or ownership of Wu. A later accepted host becomes the active counterpart after revalidation. Existing foreign copies of the public proof stay with their holders.

A higher opening can combine this foreign context with later institutional pressure. It cannot present a cascade of four reward popups, seed advanced projects, or invent past cure deliveries. Enabled evolution history records the actual opening date and actor according to the shared contract.

## Disabled continuity and later research

Disabling before delivery prevents this milestone, its coordinated-case selection, and its evolved AI. Baseline emergency aid, purchase, recruitment, theft, protective custody, and coalition actions remain as written in Part 11.

Disabling with a pending case stops an uncommitted evolved escalation. Real accepted bids, shipments, treatment, custody, and research continue under their own baseline owners. A covert operation settles through its actual owner without a new refund. Removing a case from the active display does not delete its records.

Disabling after delivery keeps the historical milestone and all real outcomes. New contest coordination stops. Existing institutions become ordinary counterparties. Re-enabling a delivered stage reconciles those surviving objects without replaying awards or restarting old auctions. N-D11 continues to govern the proposed treatment of a never-delivered stage bypassed by a later milestone, subject to its explicit acceptance gate.

Later baseline projects can enter the contest when they actually produce relevant knowledge, output, demand, or exposure. A completion does not broadcast secret details to every country. A second standard can create a competing order but does not enlarge the country-wide ceiling of two active modified standards. A new qualified Wu does not create another national covert-operation allowance.

## Cases that make the contest different

**The buyer with the fastest route.** A smaller country bids less for available medicine but can deliver it to the affected population sooner. Under emergency allocation it can win on feasible treatment need. Under an explicitly commercial sale it may lose. The host sees the resulting unmet need and can fund aid separately without pretending that the commercial auction already supplied it.

**The indispensable assistant.** A foreign university recruits the only qualified teacher for a control service. The old host can improve the appointment, qualify a successor, or retire the affected formations safely. Detaining the assistant avoids none of those actual competence and care problems and creates its own political consequences.

**The false cure.** A rival has copied one outdated treatment record. Its hospitals report failure against a later version. The investigation identifies an incompatibility, not proof that every Wu cure is fraudulent. The rival can buy a current standard, develop its own update, or continue an unsafe political claim with recorded consequences.

**The shared military secret.** A Wu country provides living-interface medicine while a Kruger country supplies a device. Both retain their own national rights. When war breaks out, further cooperation can stop, but patients already using the device still need care and the existing device does not vanish.

**The protected captive.** A sponsor calls confinement protection and refuses a requested departure. The person's actual consent and custody decide the record. Other countries can request access, provide refuge, expose the breach, or pursue an authorized operation. None automatically receives the captive as an employee.

**The donor accused of withholding.** A producer has matching knowledge but no unreserved output or safe route. The valid response is a supply expansion or route agreement. An accusation of deliberate withholding requires evidence beyond the existence of the cure design.

## AI behavior and review cases

Emergency clinical need takes priority over prestige. AI first checks whether an already qualified alternative can meet the deadline. It then compares trade, local production, temporary staff, and longer research. A weak country should not establish a rival institute when purchasing a short clinical service is the only affordable timely response.

A public host favors reciprocal teaching and qualified open production when those reduce real outbreak demand. A proprietary host values exclusive output and funded partnerships. A military host protects active service commitments and sensitive details while still seeking civilian containment where it reduces danger. Coercive governments may tolerate harmful methods, but capacity and source access remain hard validity conditions.

An exposed armed threat can raise defensive intelligence and coalition priorities among normal rivals. Ordinary commercial disappointment does not dominate those choices. Isolation, unsafe reception, missing treatment match, or absent stock makes a route invalid, not merely unattractive. Every AI action uses the same quotes and target checks as the human action.

## What the contest carries into autonomous biology

The contest leaves real foreign copies, accepted contracts, denied requests, protected people, disputed quality, and obligations that may outlast Wu's original appointment. A secret side project in the next evolution can exploit these relationships only where the institute actually has access, staff, material, and freedom to act. International pressure creates motives and opportunities. It does not manufacture a hidden laboratory or make the government lose control by itself.
