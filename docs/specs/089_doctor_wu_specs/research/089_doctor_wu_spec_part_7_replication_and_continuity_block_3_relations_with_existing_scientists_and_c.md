## Relations with existing scientists and clone systems

Wu can treat living legacy clone soldiers, study their care needs, or help an existing provider improve a documented service. Those soldiers already exist under their own owner. Treatment creates no new finite birth record, no fresh manpower entitlement, and no conversion of old recurring-income equipment into a cashable population reserve.

Legacy access, Kruger refinement, ordinary Mengele refinement, and the strict Aryan refinement keep their existing meanings. A Wu medical project must not select one merely to reach the equipment it wants. It must not remove a legitimate existing refinement, revoke a permanent external grant, or grant a restricted provider's country identity. Any hybrid that changes those rules requires an explicit owner amendment and its own accepted design.

A genuine Kruger-Wu replication collaboration can contribute a completed biomedical method, compatible equipment knowledge, a supervised facility assignment, or an authenticated non-person technical archive. Each contribution keeps its actual owner and provenance. Joint work can reduce duplicated preparation for the same demonstrated capability, but it cannot manufacture a native Kruger project stage, count a borrowed site twice, or award both a shared technology package and its already installed bonus again.

Kruger remains one canonical character under his own continuity contract. Wu's ability to produce several separate copies does not authorise cloning Kruger's token or changing his origin conclusion. The full rivalry remains a later part. Here, conflict is limited to the specific reference, facility, teaching post, or person that both institutions actually need.

Existing neutral CBRN clone discovery remains an independent legacy consumer. Its completed knowledge is preserved, but its ordinary clone-package output does not qualify as a finite Wu production order. A finite medical discovery must use the reviewed finite mode without calling that legacy completion result as a shortcut.

Independent Wu discovery never launches Army of Clones or marks its source as fired. A later legitimate launch retains its own opening, units, scaling, and history. The shared clone owner reconciles compatible learned capabilities without granting another copy of a completed Wu project or erasing an existing cohort. The finite extension does not add an eighth candidate to Kruger's seven-family random custom-technology helper.

## Disease, mortality, and difficult populations

Replicated people are living humans. They remain eligible for ordinary civilian treatment and population systems after settlement. A country does not become nonhuman because it employs a copied surgeon, commands a clone formation, or appoints a continuity copy. Shared country classification changes only if a later owner-defined transformation actually establishes a different kind of population or institution.

An infected living copy can receive the same compatible medical care as another living person. Biological treatment does not grant a replacement copy for someone who dies. Reanimation belongs to its actual owner and never restores the original finite entitlement. A dead clone's earlier first settlement and death record remain consumed history even if another event later uses its reanimated body.

A common clinical flaw is not automatically a contagious disease. Only a disease owner that genuinely recognises a biological condition can add the corresponding outbreak effects. A training failure, memory disagreement, missing archive, or disobedient person cannot be relabelled as infection so that a medical action can remove them.

Unsettled cohorts need food and shelter before they enter ordinary state population. Their receiving sites must expose that additional demand to the appropriate famine and reception owners without also inserting those people into a second population total. After civilian settlement, the temporary cohort demand ends and ordinary population demand takes over. After military settlement, the army's supply and medical services take responsibility. A demand transition cannot leave both providers charging the same people indefinitely or neither provider supporting them.

A receiving state damaged by plague can be an appropriate destination only when the actual housing, food, protection, and medical conditions support reception. The programme cannot erase plague deaths by adding newcomers, restore destroyed industry through their headcount, or declare a Rat Nation cured. Population restoration at national scale remains a later designed-life programme with its own reconstruction requirements.

Native biological operation ownership remains unchanged. A continuity copy can understand a documented medical or controlled-biology standard, but it does not acquire a second payload stockpile, attack queue, reservation, or refund path. Carrier remains a separately authorised native operation consumer. A change of institute lead cannot replay a settled attack or erase its evidence, contamination, Condemnation, or deaths.

## Four useful campaign paths

### A hospital network that outlives its founder

A public host develops R01, R02, R03, R04, R07, and R12. It creates a small number of consenting specialist copies, completes their reception, and places them in supplied hospitals. Independent teams hold the treatment standards and take over known care if Wu leaves.

The main choice is how much scarce teaching and production capacity should go to new specialists instead of direct treatment. An active epidemic can make immediate clinical supplies more valuable than a future surgeon. During peace, the same host can build a durable distributed service. The route ends with ordinary people and functioning hospitals, not an unavoidable private army.

### A finite wartime replacement programme

An industrial host develops R05 after basic replication. It commits factories to a cohort while using existing medical work to preserve current soldiers. The new class completes maturation and training, then enters native manpower once. The next class requires another full paid order.

This route works when the country can wait for people but has a long-term recruitment problem. It is a poor emergency purchase when its only nursery is about to fall. Its useful endpoint is a reliable finite intake that does not need permanent batch-specific unit management after settlement.

### A specialised cohort corps

A host with trained personnel and a stable support network develops R06 and R07, retains cohort identity, and commissions a small number of formations. The choice between pure-cohort replacements, mixed personnel, and optional enhancement changes the force the country can sustain.

A common flaw or captured depot can affect several formations at once. The player responds through actual rotation, medical recall, and supply. The corps can survive Wu's departure through independent service archives, but it cannot survive indefinitely without people, equipment, and care. Its later political importance depends on who actually controls those services and commands its officers.

### Several qualified Wus with separate obligations

A host develops R09 and R10 while the original is alive, then uses R11 to place a qualified copy at another supplied institute. The government gains continuity through a real second person. It also gains another participant in decisions about research, clinical duty, and the ownership of records.

The copies can cooperate for the rest of the campaign. They can also diverge because their experiences and employers differ. A later sovereignty route must build on those living people, institutions, and obligations. It cannot invent a network of copies only when the political ending needs one.

## Decisions the player should actually see

The institute keeps its existing compact decision-led presentation. The current phase normally offers three to five primary actions, with six as the ceiling. A selected cohort record exposes its next useful decision: finish care, assign training, settle into a receiving service, transfer custody, or respond to a demonstrated problem. Completed and incompatible actions do not remain as an accumulating list.

The main event value remains Wu Authority. On a selected replication record, show the relevant remaining people, the care-covered-until date, and one maturity or qualification status. Keep the four-value ceiling for the combined current surface. Production history, individual provenance, past settlements, and already resolved disputes remain available as records without turning them into extra public currencies.

Selecting a person shows their actual host, current appointment, reference date, qualified field, and any unresolved restriction. The interface must distinguish a living original, a qualified copy, an unqualified candidate, a captured person, a missing person, and a recorded death. An empty host pointer must not make a dead person look available for appointment.

Only one to three active objectives should occupy the same phase. A medical recall and an urgent succession can appear together when both genuinely require action. Elective proposal clutter should recede until the immediate obligations are resolved. The player can still inspect ongoing production and known records without receiving a separate crisis popup for every member of a large cohort.

Costs use the existing texticon and affordability rules. The full tooltip states the committed people, physical goods, factories, and duration, with individual shortages marked correctly. The compact row must not hide a fifth cost or describe a promised refund as already returned. Stock, remaining entitlement, and currently ready people are distinct quantities and must not be presented as synonyms.

## AI behaviour and named review cases

The AI first protects already living people and existing dependent services. It then considers projects that answer a demonstrated shortage. It must not start a large production order merely because its nominal manpower output is high. The protected site, full industrial commitment, clinical reserve, receiving plan, and time before the likely need all matter.

A public host favours clinical material, specialist continuity, and independent care. Military demand increases the value of R05 and R06 when equipment and long-term supply are adequate. Classified authority can increase willingness to conceal or monopolise records, but it never substitutes for a paid order or an actual qualified copy. Every governance choice remains available to the human player under its real requirements.

## Shared owners and the new finite interface

The new finite interface must cover birth registration, physical stock creation, readiness, exact first settlement, care responsibility, custody transfer, cancellation, and reconciliation of an interrupted result. Its arithmetic authority stays with the shared clone owner. It does not create another global all-country pulse or a second copy of native military and population accounting.

Each production and handover has a stable identity that survives changes of tag, office, or file version. Old pending callbacks cannot settle a newer order merely because both used the same factory or person. Successful settlement retires authority before replayable reports or dependent callbacks run. Cancellation returns only recorded unconsumed material and genuinely returned people. It never restores elapsed time, already consumed clinical supplies, or a person who has been settled elsewhere.

Military providers must declare their exact Event 019 disposition. Finite cohort infantry and its enhanced consumer need owner-side registration and the full provider contract, including payment, refund, formation identity, care, derivative isolation, and cleanup. A missing adapter cannot fall back to legacy provider 504 or strict provider 522.

The existing Event 019 fees need particular review. Its general management costs cannot simply be added to this part's four-cost commissioning profile. The proposed ordinary finite-provider transaction uses the same people, rifles, care, and Command Power profile as direct commissioning, with no additional Political Power or Army Experience charge. The enhanced consumer instead uses the four commitments in its own commissioning profile: people, rifles, Alteration Preparation Packs, and Military Care Packs. It adds no Command Power, Political Power, or Army Experience fee. Each profile requires an explicitly accepted owner-side arrangement for its consumer. It does not authorise changing the fees of other families. Until that arrangement exists, the Event 019 consumer remains blocked instead of charging hidden extra costs.

Clinical Material Lots are parent-owned medical supply. They have no standalone combat lot. Unissued finite people are shared-owner custody stock, not an independently spawnable battalion. Specialist and continuity appointments use the character or agency owner appropriate to the role and do not masquerade as land-unit providers.

Every new concrete equipment type, project capability, and land subunit also needs CXT registration through the existing package-owned extension contract. Registration exposes the test surface without producing an ordinary campaign cohort, firing Event 089, or granting recurring manpower. Test grants remain explicit debug actions with separate provenance and must never become legitimate natural-progression evidence.

New finite operations must preserve D01's legacy Event 089 queue and history meaning, D02's separate Scientific Research proposal, D03's unapproved shared-owner extension, and D04's native biological ownership. None of those migrations is performed by writing this specification.

## Visual and writing direction

Replication presentation should make the people and their work legible. Early scenes belong in hospitals, classrooms, workshops, and receiving institutions. A normal copied specialist should look like a person learning a job, with period clothing and tools appropriate to that role. Avoid transparent tanks as the default image for every stage, interchangeable masked technicians, specimen-number identities as finished names, or an automatic descent into monster imagery.

Personal continuity needs a recognisable relationship to the established fictional Wu portrait. Each visible body retains its own age, health, and service history. A newly qualified copy can resemble him closely without sharing every expression or sign of age. No real person's face or existing historical character is generated or duplicated as a convenient reference participant. Any later request involving an actual historical person requires the separate source and ownership review.

## What replication leaves for designed life

At the end of this progression, the institute can manufacture useful replacement material, educate new specialists, deliver finite military replacements, support recorded clone formations, and preserve Wu's exceptional medical work through separately qualified living people. Its reach depends on the sites, staff, records, and commitments that the country actually maintained.

The next research family can build on that foundation without treating a new person as a reusable production token. Designed organisms, biological filters, living reconnaissance, chimeric formations, broader population restoration, and joint biological machinery will need their own source material, purposes, control, supply, ecological limits, and owner connections. Those projects remain baseline possibilities, and their full design belongs in the next part.

The four evolutions remain separate future behaviour changes at 200, 400, 600, and 800 Chaos. They can affect the scale of competition, institutional independence, divided loyalties, and the political use of completed replication. They cannot create free cohorts, invent hidden copies without a prior history, substitute for a missing archive, or become the only route to qualified continuity. Replication leaves living people and accountable institutions for those later parts to develop.
