# L07: living reconnaissance (block 2)

## L07: living reconnaissance

L07 creates a recoverable living observation service. It is useful when a formation needs fresh local information and can protect the handlers and receiving point. The organisms are fictional instruments with limited attention, range, endurance, and interpretation. The project never supplies real methods for biological surveillance, infiltration, or dissemination.

Research qualifies one observation role at a time. Ground observation supports a known front or a named nearby operational district. Route observation supports an already established movement or relief route. A source organism suited to one role does not automatically acquire every other role. A country can qualify another design later without losing its first standard.

The practical loop is assignment, observation, return, interpretation, and rest. A team that fails to return cannot deliver a perfect report simply because the timer expires. The government must decide whether to wait, send a recovery team, accept an incomplete observation, or abandon the attempt. Information belongs to the actual returned observations and expires as the situation changes.

A reference assignment commits twenty qualified reconnaissance animals, twenty-five trained handlers, fifty Support Equipment, and ten Husbandry Lots for 30 days. The handlers remain unavailable for another assignment during that period. A supplied receiving site and a supported target relationship are requirements. The target must be an actual eligible military or route surface. The player does not select an arbitrary distant capital and receive its complete military state.

Successful ground observation grants a proposed 25 percent reconnaissance contribution to one recorded formation for up to 30 days after interpretation. The contribution addresses the supported local reconnaissance calculation, not the entire country's intelligence ledger. Successful route observation instead supplies a dated finding to that route's owner: confirmed usable, confirmed interrupted, or unresolved. It does not open the route, establish naval superiority, or move a population.

The reconnaissance contribution applies only while the formation remains in the relevant operational setting. Moving to a different front ends its relevance. A route finding becomes stale after the same 30-day reference interval or a known material change, whichever comes first. The new service does not reveal exact enemy division locations or hidden country variables without an independently supported engine consumer.

Incomplete returns reduce the contribution in proportion to the qualified observers and useful coverage actually recovered. Twenty animals are the reference team, not a guarantee of complete information. If only half return with usable observations, the maximum contribution is half the reference result. A duplicate report cannot restore the missing half. Rest and husbandry remain necessary before survivors take another assignment.

A damaged interpretation station can preserve the returned observations until a trained team becomes available, but it cannot freeze their operational relevance. Waiting for interpretation consumes their remaining useful window. This makes staff and communications matter without granting another research lane or permanent intelligence bonus.

The player can prioritise military use or humanitarian route verification. Both require real assignments. A classified mandate can restrict who sees the findings, while a public mandate can share a route report with a partner. Knowledge sharing transmits the report and its date, not the organisms, handlers, current physical access, or a new intelligence operation.

Failure should be shown through the service itself: a partial return, a fatigued handling team, or observations that no longer match a changed front. A recovery uses the existing named team and location. It must not create a random escaped swarm across unrelated states. Ordinary combat and route hazards remain capable of destroying the service.

## L08: biological camouflage

L08 provides a reversible living covering or protective surface for a selected formation. It does not permanently alter the soldiers and does not give every division a national concealment modifier. The living material has to remain compatible with its recipients, serviced, and appropriate to its approved operating conditions.

The reference production batch supplies ten camouflage allocations. Each allocation covers up to five hundred actual recipients. A five-thousand-person formation therefore requires ten allocations, while a smaller formation uses a proportional whole-unit quantity. Excess coverage cannot be reassigned while the original unit still claims it. A split or merge preserves the total covered people and the remaining service period under Part 6's formation-identity rule.

The reference application to five thousand people commits ten allocations, ten Clinical Material Lots, twenty-five Support Equipment, and fifteen Command Power. Preparation lasts 30 days and the operational course lasts 60 days. The recipient remains an ordinary available army formation during fitting and receives no camouflage benefit until preparation is complete. Its identity is a target, not an extra formation payment. The quote includes removal and routine recovery. It cannot conceal a fifth factory or manpower charge in a follow-up confirmation.

The government chooses a movement-protection posture or a prepared-position posture for the course. Movement protection proposes a 20 percent reduction in the enemy's applicable local reconnaissance contribution against the covered formation. It does not remove the formation from the map or prevent ordinary detection, combat, air attack, or supply consumption. Prepared-position use instead proposes 25 percent faster planning preparation for that formation while it remains at its assigned prepared position. The two postures cannot apply simultaneously to the same allocation.

These are intended outcomes that require exact formation-scoped implementation evidence. A country-wide modifier is not an acceptable substitute. If the engine cannot express the proposed reconnaissance-denial consumer directly, the implementation must return the exact limitation for a reviewed redesign. It must not silently replace the effect with a generic national defence bonus.

A posture change requires the formation to return to a supported service point and complete a new 15-day fitting interval. It keeps the original course's expiry and consumes five Clinical Material Lots per full reference formation. It does not restart the sixty days or create another allocation. A formation cannot alternate postures daily to obtain both results or reset its maintenance.

Heavy damage, incompatible conditions, or an interrupted clinical supply can require early removal. The player can withdraw the formation, accept loss of the special benefit while maintaining ordinary care, or continue an explicit emergency use with a risk to the actual recipients. A consequence such as illness or impaired readiness is recorded on that formation. It cannot be removed by renaming the template.

The material does not confer resistance to all diseases or grant a new biological weapon. Infected recipients still use their actual medical case. Camouflage that becomes unsuitable after mutation needs adaptation. A clean completion removes the temporary covering and leaves learned production knowledge, the formation's authentic history, and any real medical consequences intact.

The presentation should show the difference between a fitted living surface and permanent enhancement. It must not imply invisibility or a change of species. A leader portrait is not needed for a formation-level service. Any visible unit alteration must have a separately accepted material and model variant rather than an unreviewed recolour of another unit's asset.

## L09: chimeric support animals

L09 creates a deliberately unusual working animal for difficult transport and casualty retrieval. Its body is built around carrying a useful load, maintaining stable footing, and tolerating the handling needed to move wounded personnel. Its appearance can be impossible, but its service remains understandable: it carries things and living patients through a place where ordinary wheeled transport performs poorly.

The institute can reach this design through its own qualified living-organism work. Access to a paleogenetic specimen can improve the source basis, but a completed Kruger army is not mandatory. A xenobiological source requires that family's separate compatibility and control permission. Mixed source material does not merge those owners' technologies, histories, or military entitlements.

The reference support attachment contains fifty qualified animals, one hundred and fifty human handlers, fifty Support Equipment, and fifteen Husbandry Lots for the initial thirty-day operational allocation. Those are the four commissioning cost types. Commissioning lasts 90 days and uses the normal training commitment of the proposed attachment. There is no additional command-power purchase, political fee, or factory reservation on the same action.

Its human handlers use real manpower. The animals use their separate physical stock and never generate manpower. The attachment is unarmed as a firing unit. It relies on its supported combat formation for protection and does not receive invented rifle effects or gunfire audio. Its support-equipment requirement covers the actual carried and handling equipment that the model must depict where visible.

The intended military advantage is a 25 percent reduction in applicable rough-terrain equipment attrition for the formation's supported movement and casualty-retrieval work. Its additional medical role is to make a bounded local casualty group reachable by an existing field-medical service where the transport owner accepts that connection. It does not apply another universal casualty-trickleback bonus on top of the same treatment. The medical owner still decides which living casualties can be saved.

The proposed tradeoff is a 10 percent speed penalty during ordinary road movement with the attachment, plus its husbandry and protection burden. It is useful for a difficult approach or an evacuation from broken ground. It is a poor default attachment for every fast armoured formation. It cannot carry supplies across a sea, reopen a destroyed bridge by itself, or restore a cut strategic supply network.

The player chooses between a transport task and a casualty-retrieval task. The same animal capacity cannot carry a full military load and a full patient load simultaneously. A mixed task divides the actual capacity. Patient movement never creates new manpower. The wounded remain in the appropriate military or civilian owner's custody until that owner's treatment and movement transaction resolves.

The attachment needs a compatible combat anchor. It cannot enter Event 019 as a support-only division. If it is registered as an attachment to a new chimeric combat family, that provider owns its template, payment, equipment publication, sustainment, and cleanup. A native army can also train the attachment through its exact access and equipment contract once the implementation proves the supported unit definition.

Loss of the host formation does not automatically kill every animal or free every handler. The actual unit owner determines surviving manpower and recoverable stock. An identified surviving animal group can enter a field recovery or care-only state. Capturing that group does not immediately qualify a new country's handlers. The receiving country needs the retained handling standard or a paid requalification.

The post-service choices matter because the animals remain alive. A healthy reserve group uses the resting husbandry rate. A civilian transport service requires a supported assignment with the same physical capacity limits. Discharging animals into an unsupported district is abandonment and can create a local problem. It is not a free retirement command that erases their food demand.

## L10: chimeric assault organisms

L10 creates a living assault family intended for a prepared, difficult attack. It should have a recognisable physical role and should not become a better version of every existing creature. The proposed identity is a heavy, low-speed organism whose body can force a passage during a supported assault, with handlers controlling its deployment and recovery.

Paleogenetic creatures retain their terrestrial reserve, hatchery, and mobile shock identity. Xenobiological organisms retain their separate adaptive and control architecture. L10 may use a genuinely supplied source from either family, but its new body, training, and medical standard need their own qualified design. Research does not grant either existing operational package, and an external operational grant does not automatically grant L10.

The reference combat element contains twenty-five qualified organisms and two hundred human handlers. Commissioning commits those organisms, those handlers, one hundred Support Equipment, and twenty Husbandry Lots, with a 120-day training period. It has four spendable cost types. Handlers are human manpower and can be lost. The organism stock is a finite nonhuman military resource and cannot substitute for those people.

The intended strength is concentrated. Compared with the appropriate unenhanced creature profile chosen during implementation, the proposed defining changes are 30 percent greater breakthrough and 35 percent more physical staying power, with 25 percent lower speed. The additional 40 percent fort-assault advantage belongs only to the prepared opportunity described below. The package must remain vulnerable to concentrated fire, air attack, disrupted approach routes, and loss of handlers. It receives no blanket armour, piercing, disease immunity, or organisation superiority.

These comparison values are balance targets, not fabricated native modifiers. The implementation must choose the exact verified unit statistics and category consumers that express the role, document the comparison, and keep unrelated units unchanged. A benefit that the engine cannot isolate to the intended family remains an explicit design decision rather than a reason to grant a national bonus.

The formation's distinctive action is a prepared assault assignment. The player commits the actual formation to a named reachable fortified front, provides twenty Husbandry Lots, one hundred Support Equipment, and twenty Command Power, and maintains the assigned approach and supply for a proposed 60-day preparation. The formation commitment is the fourth cost type. The action does not also charge fuel, experience, political power, or another generic fee.

Completion establishes a bounded prepared-assault opportunity for that formation against that front. Its special fort-assault advantage applies during the opportunity, while ordinary combat still decides the result. The preparation does not destroy a fort through a script effect, transfer a state, eliminate defenders, or grant victory. Changing the target requires another real preparation and cannot carry the old target's opportunity to a different continent.

The player can interrupt preparation to save the formation, use it conventionally with its speed and husbandry limitations, or commit to the prepared attack. The special preparation expires after a proposed 30-day opportunity if unused. Supplies spent on preparation remain spent. The formation and surviving organisms do not disappear because the opportunity expires.

A control failure is tied to the actual handling and service state. Exhausted handlers, an unmaintained cohort, or a captured control archive can lead to loss of readiness, refusal to advance, or a local dangerous incident. The response can be recovery, a controlled stand-down, retraining, or an explicitly authorised destructive action against the affected organisms. Routine losses never create a fresh full-strength cohort through a respawn.

A routine stand-down requires a secure site and the actual recorded formation. A proposed 90-day recovery commits the formation, one civilian factory, twenty Clinical Material Lots, and fifty Support Equipment. Husbandry continues under its existing standing obligation and is displayed separately as an ongoing animal-care obligation, not a new hidden admission fee. The same period is not billed again through an invented second upkeep package.

The public mandate can authorise this family through a visible military exception with independent veterinary and medical review. The classified mandate can concentrate records and handling staff under Wu, increasing later dependence through the actual custody concession. Neither mandate receives a technology solely because Authority rose. The dangerous political outcome grows from who controls the formations and their care.

### Shared military identity and inheritance

Both chimeric designs retain a birth or production generation, trained handling standard, real stock, and current custodian. Ordinary training scales through equipment and manpower rather than a lifetime unit cap. A hard limit is appropriate for a free event-created opening force only when its specific owner authorises that grant. This part creates no such free opening.

A new standalone chimeric combat family requires an owner-side Event 019 registration and all thirteen runtime callbacks. Its support animals are an explicit attachment, with a compatible combat anchor and separate equipment needs inside the same manifest. Existing provider IDs are not reused. No provider ID is reserved in this specification.

Event 019 fees create an integration issue already recorded for Part 7. Native chimeric commissioning uses four cost types. The shared consumer must not append unrelated generic fees as a fifth or sixth type. A provider-specific cost disposition needs acceptance by the Event 019 owner before implementation. That disposition may not change the established prices or eligibility of other providers.

An Event 019 derivative receives its own exact unit and public-package identity. It does not receive Wu, the institute, Authority, cure research, evolution history, or a right to create human copies. Its leadership and classification must follow the shared provider proof. This is particularly important when the visible authority is a human handling corps around nonhuman organisms. The presence of animals alone cannot be used to classify an ordinary human government as nonhuman.

Transfer of an exact damaged formation remains subject to Part 7's unresolved engine boundary. The existing Event 019 natural-release replacement exception does not authorise a general full-strength recreation of Wu units. Captured or transferred physical organisms retain only their actual surviving number and known care history. A recipient does not inherit completed research, equipment production, or private command rights from a photograph of the formation.

## L11: joint biological machinery

L11 begins when a real Wu team and a real Kruger team agree to build a particular service machine. Wu supplies the qualified biological process and its clinical limits. Kruger's institution supplies the relevant machinery knowledge and engineering staff. A completed operational technology can establish part of the technical basis, but an old contact flag does not prove that either team has agreed to collaborate.

The first agreement names one output, one site, the staff who can maintain it, and the knowledge each participant may retain. The government can choose shared technical custody or a more restricted split. Shared custody allows trained national staff to keep the completed machine working. Restricted custody can make the same useful installation depend on two separate private teams. That dependency follows real maintenance rights and staff access rather than an automatic Authority increase from research.

Three bounded machine roles are proposed. Each is a separate physical installation under the same research family. Completing one does not build the other two or grant every conventional and custom technology in the broader Kruger portfolio.

| Installation | Required technical basis | Proposed construction commitment | Continuing use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Service-tender works | A qualified L02, L03, or L05 design and operational Robotics | 2 civilian factories, 50 compatible robot equipment, 2,000 fuel, 25 Clinical Material Lots for 180 days | Tends a larger declared set of the same installed living service. |
| Biological observation works | L07 and operational Electronics with a trained interpretation team | 2 civilian factories, 50 compatible CBRN instrument equipment, 1,000 fuel, 20 Clinical Material Lots for 150 days | Interprets returned observations and distributes the resulting dated findings. |
| Recovery-cradle works | M09 and M10 with operational Biomedical and a qualified engineering team | 3 civilian factories, 300 Support Equipment, 2,000 fuel, 50 Clinical Material Lots for 210 days | Supports five actual exceptional living patients through an approved preservation service. |

These are four-cost construction profiles. Robot equipment used in service-tender works becomes part of that installation. It cannot also appear in a combat robot division. Instrument stock is quoted through the existing supported equipment family and its actual models. A generic label must not permit an unsupported model or a fabricated substitute payment.

Service-tender works add two maintained Living Service Unit places to that site's reference five-place capacity. They do not create the two units or supply their upkeep. Their value comes from operating a larger useful installation with the same trained supervisory team, provided the site has the required clinical and mechanical service. The extra places close when the machinery is inoperative. The affected living units enter supported care or are reassigned without disappearing.

Biological observation works improve the interpretation of returned material. The proposed staffed working rate is one third higher, equivalent to reducing the remaining eligible interpretation time by one quarter only while the machine and staff actually work. It does not add completed work on activation. A qualified Part 7 specialist and the machine use the strongest applicable rate for the same task, not multiplied rates. Neither can recover information that never returned or make a stale report current again.

Recovery-cradle works provide five physical exceptional-patient places. A place requires the living patient's actual admission and the M10 care contract. Constructing five places does not create five preserved leaders, summon missing patients, or award a national research bonus. Existing preservation costs remain with the care owner, which must adopt the construction-supplied reserve only when it represents the same purpose. The site must not charge the same fifty clinical lots again through a duplicate admission package.

All three installations remain at their actual sites. Capturing a building can give the captor physical machinery and damaged living stock. It does not teleport its former operators, transfer either scientist, or grant an entire project history. A recipient with the retained maintenance standard can resume supported operation after revalidation and repair. A recipient without it can preserve patients or organisms through emergency care while negotiating or reconstructing the missing knowledge.

Separation of the two institutions does not automatically destroy the installations. Learned public methods stay learned. A proprietary component can leave a genuine maintenance dependency, and the government's response can be licensing, independent reconstruction from authentic records, a narrowed service, or an orderly closure. The machine's physical and medical failures remain distinct. A mechanical repair does not cure an incompatible organism, and a medical correction does not replace a broken power or control component.

The joint team must not manipulate the existing xenobiological control exclusivity. A machine-assisted medical service is not a second control doctrine. If a xenobiological patient requires machine control, its existing owner must authorise that architecture through its normal contract. L11 cannot call a private runtime helper to force the grant or remove an already chosen channel.

The collaboration supplies concrete evidence for the later rivalry part: who delivered the agreed component, who retained the records, who paid for repair, and whether either scientist obstructed continuing care. It does not choose the entire Kruger-Wu relationship from one construction result. A successful collaboration can coexist with disputes elsewhere in the portfolio.

## L12: reconstruction districts

L12 creates a real receiving district in damaged territory. The programme brings together medical access, native repair work, shelter, food safety, and reception capacity. Its purpose is to make a selected place usable for surviving residents, returning migrants, and later new citizens. It cannot restore a country's pre-disaster population by changing a number after a cure succeeds.

A candidate district must have a real damage or reception problem and a viable path to recovery. Eligible causes include disease devastation, destructive occupation, a liberated feeding state, and another owner-recognised disaster. The original owner keeps its cause, losses, phase, memorial obligations, and long-term aftermath. A country can begin reconstruction while a larger crisis continues, provided this particular district can be protected and supplied.

The initial proposal names the state, the specific usable receiving location, its access route, and the work needed there. The player chooses a medical-return district or a broader civilian receiving district. The first prioritises existing patients and displaced residents with a connection to the place. The second can receive a larger mix when its actual housing and food capacity support them. Neither choice grants claims, cores, automatic compliance, or ownership of an occupied population.

The reference programme commits two civilian factories, three hundred Support Equipment, 2,000 fuel, and fifty Political Power over 210 days. These are the four cost types. The factory commitment must be the actual repair and establishment work budget. It must not also be counted as a separate idle reservation while another hidden factory bill pays for the same repairs.

The programme's first objective is to establish a supplied care and shelter endpoint. Its second is to restore the selected existing infrastructure through the native construction or repair owner. Its third is to obtain valid food-safety and reception evidence for the people it proposes to receive. These objectives can progress together. The timer alone never certifies success while the road is cut or food access remains unproven.

The proposed completed district can support up to five thousand additional reception places. That figure is a planning ceiling. The actual capacity is the least of usable shelter, safe access, food support, clinical service, and the migration owner's accepted capacity. A damaged district may complete only two thousand places and continue work later. The missing three thousand are not hidden people and do not receive any population credit.

Physical repairs remain real work. A destroyed factory, railway, or other structure cannot be recreated from a medical completion flag. The exact native reservation and repair consumer must be verified during implementation. Where the engine cannot connect the same committed factories to that work as intended, the construction contract remains unresolved. It is not replaced with free buildings or a generic national construction modifier.

Independent engineers can improve the rate of work they actually supervise under Part 7. They do not create new factories or reception places at appointment. A district's completed work persists when the engineer leaves. Remaining work loses the service-dependent rate if no qualified replacement exists. A reused team cannot accelerate several active sites simultaneously unless the existing assignment contract genuinely supplies that capacity.

### Recovery with existing owners

In Black Plague territory, the district must preserve the disease owner's Recovery or Cured state, actual remaining burden, relapse conditions, and measurable devastation. The existing Population Recovery Programme, vigilance work, inspection compact, and memorial charter remain their owner's programmes. Wu contributes separately identified clinical service or physical receiving work. The same reduction of devastation cannot be awarded once by each programme under different names.

In former zombie territory, reconstruction preserves the real history of occupation and the postwar survivor settlement. A clean district does not count as final global zombie defeat while another outbreak polity survives. The medical network can service a region before the shared final-defeat condition is met, but it cannot fire the defeat super-event or recreate a dismantled league to claim its rewards.

In a liberated cannibalism district, emergency relief, identification, institutional reconstruction, and trauma remain real stages. Medical treatment does not discharge legal obligations or delete witness records. A new settlement must not turn the people killed there into a numerical resource for breeding replacements. The district's new inhabitants are not evidence that the original loss has been undone.

Famine and migration remain independent. The food owner publishes actual food safety, including whether its projection is valid. An untracked state is not automatically safe. Migration owns the real movement, reception, integration, and return of people who already exist in a population system. A successful medical inspection cannot override a closed border, unavailable destination, or insufficient food.

### Scarcity and interruption

A completed reception place can be reserved for only one actual incoming obligation at a time. Survivors, migrants, patients, and new replicated people compete for the same physical capacity. The government can reserve a portion for a planned cohort, but the reserve is visible and can be released when a larger immediate need arises. That release does not delete people already born or create room that does not exist.

Losing a route pauses new admissions. People already living in the district remain where they actually are until a valid evacuation moves them. Food and medical demand do not disappear with the programme's decision category. The government can protect the district, distribute people among reachable places, or arrange an owner-approved evacuation. It cannot empty a threatened district through a refund.

Captured districts retain buildings, current people, damage, and real custody. The new controller does not inherit authority to alter the former sponsor's research or erase the disaster history. Humanitarian service can continue through a new agreement when safe access and staff permit it. A change of flag cannot restart the same construction award or duplicate the reception ceiling.

The lasting payoff is a district with usable physical services and an accepted place in the normal civilian systems. Wu may remain its leading physician, or local institutions may take over. An independently sustained district no longer needs a special permanent Wu modifier for every completed repair. Its real buildings and people continue to exist.

## L13: population restoration programme

L13 uses the existing finite replication mode to create new civilian lives for a viable receiving district. It does not replace R03's production, R07's care, common adult orientation, or the first-settlement contract. It supplies the missing national programme: where new people can live, how they are counted, what happens while they wait, and how they enter an ordinary society.

The first decision defines the programme's purpose and receiving capacity. A public charter can prioritise rebuilding a damaged community, establishing civilian independence, and preventing compulsory military or specialist service. A more restrictive government can seek binding service obligations, creating refusal, staff, legitimacy, and later political consequences. A charter never grants the government automatic ownership of a new person's memories or loyalty.

Research is available through actual replication and reconstruction prerequisites, even in Calm World. It does not require a catastrophic death threshold. A recorded disaster can make the project urgent and change its presentation, but the dead are never an input stock or a quota that may be restored. A country with a smaller real receiving need can operate a smaller programme.

Production uses Part 7's existing normal or accelerated cell. A normal cell has the same maximum of 250 people, the same 270-day production and protected-maturation schedule, and the same four cost types. The new programme adds no second factory reservation or clinical charge to that cell. Common adult orientation remains a separate 90-day requirement before independent reception and service. The accelerated route keeps its earlier limitations and follow-up burdens.

A programme may run several paid cells when the host has actual industrial, staffing, care, and receiving capacity. It cannot use a cloned director to exceed the institute's research-lane ceiling. Production is separate from research, but it still consumes its real factories and supplies. Every cell has its own output count, living milestone, destination reservation, and care responsibility.

### Before the first population settlement

A planned person is not a living person. Before the living milestone, the programme can cancel an unstarted or unfinished allocation under the original production contract. After that milestone, actual surviving people need care and food even while their first settlement remains pending.

Those unsettled people have not yet been added to the state's civilian population. Their real needs must nevertheless reach the food and care owners through an explicit external-demand contract. That contract identifies the nursery or actual custody location, living count, already provisioned care, current custodian, generation, revision, and the finite production owner. It is a proposed extension, not an existing helper assumed to be present.

The receiving food owner must not count the same people through both the external-demand projection and ordinary state population after settlement. The transition retires the matching unsettled projection when the population transaction commits. If settlement fails, the projection and care obligation remain with the actual people. A failed callback cannot make them disappear from demand or add them twice.

Movement before first settlement also needs a distinct contract. The cohort owner supplies the exact unsettled people and transport authority. A journey can move that custody and record actual losses. It must not debit a state's civilian population that never contained them. Only the surviving people admitted at the destination receive the one first civilian-population settlement. This is not permission to call an ordinary migration transfer with a fictitious origin population.

After first settlement, the normal population and migration owners take over. Later movement reduces the real origin population and adds survivors to the destination through the existing exact transfer. The finite replication record remains historical and cannot be redeemed again. Demobilisation, specialist recruitment, and a later Wu appointment similarly do not add another person.

### Shared receiving capacity

A five-thousand-place district might reserve three thousand places for returning residents, one thousand for other accepted migrants, and one thousand for four new replication cells. These allocations use one shared capacity, not three independent five-thousand-place pools. Unborn allocations can be reduced before the corresponding cells begin. Already living people retain their care obligation when a reservation is changed.

If two thousand five hundred returning residents arrive and one thousand migrants are accepted, the district has one thousand five hundred unoccupied places before the new cells settle. Receiving one thousand new people leaves five hundred places. None of those transfers adds the original displaced residents to the replication birth count. None of the births reduces the recorded deaths from the earlier disaster.

If only nine hundred of the one thousand new people survive to settlement, the district receives nine hundred. The other one hundred enter the appropriate real loss record once if their deaths occurred after the living milestone. They do not also reduce a destination population they never entered. Unused housing remains available according to the receiving owner rather than being awarded as a cash refund.

A 250-person cell still obeys Part 7's identity equation: new living people equal unsettled people plus historical first settlements plus deaths before settlement. After settlement, a later death does not lower the historical first-settlement term and reopen entitlement. Population restoration is a new production and reception effort, not a reversible expenditure against a death total.

### Civilian independence and work

Common orientation prepares people for ordinary life, language, communication, and work. It does not automatically make them surgeons, pilots, researchers, soldiers, or loyal political followers. Those roles retain their own training and appointment rules. A person who refuses a proposed profession remains a living civilian, not a defective unit that can be deleted.

The programme can help new residents enter existing work, but it does not grant a factory, resource deposit, research slot, or instant veteran workforce for each batch. Actual industrial and agricultural capacity continues to determine employment and supply. L12 can rebuild physical services while L13 creates new residents, with separate paid work and no doubled completion reward.

New citizens remain ordinary living humans for shared civilian classification. A replicated mayor, a continuity copy of Wu, or a clone-derived workforce does not make the whole state nonhuman. The government cannot switch classification to escape famine, migration, or medical obligations. Any genuinely different later political or biological transformation belongs to its own accepted route and proof.

The public programme can record family and community ties that develop after arrival without pretending that imperfect copied memories establish old legal relationships. A copied specialist may remember a place that no longer exists. The charter must allow a new legal identity and independent future. Neither inheritance, marriage, office, military rank, nor property passes automatically from the original person.

A coercive settlement may create resistance, refusal, emigration pressure, or exposure through the existing owners. It cannot receive automatic cores or compliance from manufacturing a population. Medical capability does not establish sovereignty over the state or erase the rights of surviving inhabitants. The later political parts may develop those conflicts, but their outcomes cannot be pre-awarded here.

### Completion and closure

The programme's successful endpoint is a group of actual new residents who have completed reception and whose ordinary care and food systems can support them. The institution retains knowledge and authentic programme history. Its active replication service can close while those people continue ordinary life.

A failed programme can stop future orders, narrow its intake, seek another receiving district, or transfer unfinished living cohorts through an accepted custody contract. It cannot cancel lives as though cancelling an unbuilt factory. Closure must account for every living cohort, patient, reserved place, and actual outstanding supply allocation.

Population growth after ordinary settlement belongs to the ordinary population owner. There is no permanent Wu manpower dividend for the same people, no civilian-plus-military double credit, and no legacy clone-stock reward. D03 remains the required proposed shared-owner extension. Until its exact birth, custody, food-demand, and settlement boundaries are implemented and accepted, this programme is a designed feature with an explicit dependency.

## L14: functional longevity

The anti-aging proposal concerns the continued function of an existing living person. It extends M09 and M10 into repeated assessment and sustained care. It does not create a replacement body, copy memories, revive someone already dead, or reset the historical age and identity of a character.

A qualifying case needs an actual biological decline that the character or specialist owner exposes as treatable. A famous name, old portrait, or approaching scripted event is not enough. The owner's political, military, and narrative rules remain separate. Wu cannot cancel an execution, a battle death, an unrelated assassination, or an owner-mandated departure by declaring every cause of death medical.

The project can be completed from a supported clinical reference in Calm World. A player need not wait for the leader to become incapacitated before developing the capability. Actual treatment nevertheless needs a living patient, a real clinical need, consent or appropriate care authority, and a supplied institution. The government must decide whether the person's continued work justifies the commitment.

The reference treatment commits the actual patient, two civilian factories, sixty Clinical Material Lots, and one hundred Support Equipment.
