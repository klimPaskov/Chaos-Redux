# Doctor Wu: replication and continuity
## From replacement tissue to another living person

The institute already knows how to supply replacement blood, restore damaged tissue, support failing organs, and keep an injured person alive while treatment continues. Replication begins when Wu proposes using that medical foundation to produce a separate living person. The hospital patient keeps their identity and history. The new person begins a different life, even when the institute has used the patient's likeness and recorded skills as its starting reference.

The government must decide what it is asking the institute to preserve. A ward needs reliable replacement material. A surgical service needs another qualified surgeon. An army needs trained people who can receive orders, use equipment, and survive deployment. Wu wants someone who can continue his work after he is gone. These demands share facilities and knowledge, but they do not have interchangeable results. A warehouse of medical material contains no recruitable people, and a completed human body does not arrive with a military career.

This part develops those demands as connected baseline programmes. M07 and M08 remain the existing blood, tissue, and organ foundations. M09 supplies regenerative knowledge, M10 supplies preservation experience, and the earlier independent teams, rehabilitation, and service archives remain useful. Their rewards are not granted again. None of the replication projects requires soldier enhancement, an engineered-zombie programme, high Wu Authority, or an evolution unless a particular optional combination actually uses that earlier work.

All new project codes, route names, product names, quantities, costs, durations, and numerical effects below are working design proposals. They are not final localisation or approved balance. The growth and memory mechanisms are fictional devices expressed through game time, institutions, and resources. They supply no real biological methods.

## What the player is trying to build

The early objective is a dependable medical service that survives the loss of one exceptional doctor. Replacement material should make an operating hospital less vulnerable to a single exhausted stock or missing specialist. The next objective is a small group of capable new people who can work outside Wu's immediate supervision. Military production becomes attractive when the country has equipment and industry but cannot replace its losses through ordinary recruitment. Personal continuity becomes attractive when the institute's remaining work depends on knowledge that its ordinary staff still cannot reproduce.

Each objective creates a different commitment. Medical material needs continuous manufacture and delivery. Specialists need teaching, supervised work, and a vacancy that makes their training useful. Replacement personnel need an actual destination and a service establishment. Clone formations need a continuing replacement source for their recorded cohorts. A continuity copy needs a body, a dated archive, clinical qualification, and a recognised place inside an existing institution.

A public institute can complete the entire sequence with independent review. It can also stop after tissue supply or specialist replication and remain a strong medical route. The decision to create people does not hand them to Wu as private followers. Institutional control grows only when the government actually concedes appointment, custody, distribution, or command powers. A copy can be grateful to Wu and still choose a different employer or refuse the office prepared for it.

## R01: replacement material that stays medical

R01 turns the results of M07 and M08 into a traceable service product, using the working label Clinical Material Lot. A lot represents an organised supply assignment for compatible replacement material, storage, and clinical handling. It is not a dose, an organ count, a recipe, or a person. The earlier treatment owner still determines which living patients can benefit and what their recovery means.

A proposed production order makes 40 lots over 30 days, committing one civilian factory and spending 100 Support Equipment and 500 fuel. Production requires a functioning medical fabrication site. The civilian factory remains occupied for the full order. A lost site interrupts the order and preserves its paid and consumed portions under the production record. Completion adds only the finished lots that the order actually produced.

Hospitals assign these lots to existing organ replacement, regeneration, preservation, or recovery work. A lot supplements an appropriate treatment allocation. It cannot replace the disease-specific cure, the hospital, the attending staff, or the route to the patient. The same material cannot simultaneously satisfy a hospital order and a replication nursery's care reservation.

The new capability is useful before whole-person replication exists. The government can hold a modest reserve against disruption, support a foreign hospital with copied clinical knowledge, or place material production under an independent university network. A damaged lot is withdrawn from the affected service, and the hospital can turn to an alternative manufacturer if the relevant standard has been shared. Wu's disappearance does not invalidate material that was already certified.

Material failure produces a supply or treatment problem, not an automatic new disease. Patients already receiving the affected material need reassessment through their own treatment programme. Unused material can be discarded without recording human deaths. The clinical record must distinguish a spoiled supply, a living patient's injury, and a death that the medical owner actually applied.

## R02: a reference for a person

A personal pattern archive contains two distinct records. One describes the intended biological likeness in the event's fictional terms. The other records demonstrated habits, professional experience, language, and remembered methods. The second record is dated and incomplete. A later copy cannot know an event that happened after the reference was recorded unless someone teaches it or supplies a later authenticated record.

The public route uses a consenting participant and an independent witness. A surgeon can agree to preserve medical competence without permitting military assignment, public impersonation, or unrestricted production of their likeness. A pilot can permit a training reference while retaining their current commission and family identity. Permission is recorded by use, not as one permanent consent to everything the institute might later propose.

A reference participant remains the original person. Making an archive does not dismiss them, transfer their character, copy their advisor bonus, remove their nationality, or create another office. A completed archive grants a capability to prepare future people. It has no manpower or population output.

The government chooses institutional custody, personal custody, or shared custody of the completed record. Institutional custody allows a supervised successor team to continue authorised work if Wu leaves. Personal custody makes future access depend on the participant and any lawful successor agreement. Shared custody gives the institute an operational reference and an independent custodian a verification copy. Copying information in this way does not copy the person's body or multiply a pending production order.

A secret programme can falsify permission or conceal the archive. Its advantage is the ability to attempt work that the public settlement would refuse. It leaves specific evidence of the unauthorised use and a later dispute over the resulting people. Concealment does not make those people obedient, remove their need for care, or erase the original participant's ownership and history. These are fictional institutional consequences, without practical methods for coercion or impersonation.

The first archive report should show the participant recognising some recorded habits and disputing others. The player sees which field is represented, the recording date, and the authorised use. Exact later disagreements remain undisclosed. Final writing should avoid treating a transcript as a perfect mind or turning the scene into an inventory of technical settings.

## R03: the first separate life

R03 establishes the possibility of producing a living person through the event's fictional replication process. Completing the research does not create a cohort. The government must place a production order, fund its whole declared schedule, identify the receiving service, and protect the site.

A production order begins as non-person material and work in progress. Its first living-person milestone is a distinct recorded outcome. From that milestone onward, the people exist, need care, and remain in the finite cohort record even if they are immature, medically unstable, unqualified, or no longer useful to the sponsoring institution. Cancelling the programme after this milestone cannot turn them back into refunded material.

The first successful case should be small. The government sees a separate person who resembles the participant but has no lived history in the country. The copy may recognise a room from the archive without having visited it. It may reproduce a practiced movement while failing to understand the situation in which that movement is appropriate. These observations establish the difference between a useful reference and a finished professional.

The host can continue under the public charter, restrict further production while completing the existing person's care, or stop the expansion and admit the person to ordinary life. A refusal to expand does not kill the first copy or remove the knowledge already learned. A classified host can hide the case, creating a custody dispute and a future discovery path, but concealment supplies no extra research lane or free production.

An existing living patient is never consumed as the raw input for a new person. A repaired organ, amputated tissue, a stored archive, and a reanimated body also do not supply a second claim on the same person's population or military manpower. The finite birth record begins only when this production programme creates a genuinely new living individual in the fiction.

## Production orders, sites, and receiving capacity

There are two production scales. Personal orders create one or a few individually referenced people. Cohort orders create a larger group around a compatible standard, with individual identity records retained inside the batch. They share the finite owner contract but have different fixed preparation costs and cannot be freely relabelled halfway through production.

The proposed normal cohort cell produces up to 250 people. A standard order uses one, four, or ten cells for a planned maximum of 250, 1,000, or 2,500 people. Every cell commits one civilian factory for 270 days and spends 150 Support Equipment, 1,000 fuel, and 10 Clinical Material Lots. The first 180 days develop the production order. The final 90 days are protected maturation after the living-person milestone. These are fictional campaign durations, not biological claims.

| Normal cohort order | Maximum new people | Factories committed for 270 days | Support Equipment | Fuel | Clinical Material Lots |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Small receiving class | 250 | 1 | 150 | 1,000 | 10 |
| Replacement cohort | 1,000 | 4 | 600 | 4,000 | 40 |
| Large institutional intake | 2,500 | 10 | 1,500 | 10,000 | 100 |

Personal orders use a separate preparation profile. One factory is committed for the same 270-day normal schedule. The proposed direct cost is 100 Support Equipment, 1,000 fuel, and 10 Clinical Material Lots for the order, plus 10 Support Equipment, 100 fuel, and 5 lots for each planned person. The first-order sizes are one or five people. A one-person order therefore costs 110 Support Equipment, 1,100 fuel, and 15 lots. A five-person order costs 150 Support Equipment, 1,500 fuel, and 35 lots. The higher personal cost pays for individual reference and supervision in game terms.

An order can produce fewer viable people than its planned maximum. The actual living count is recorded once. A failed material stage has no living casualties. A loss after the living milestone removes actual people and uses the appropriate Deaths owner. Neither outcome awards a consolation manpower payment. Completing research or repeating the result report cannot repeat the production output.

For personal and cohort production, Support Equipment and fuel are consumed at admission as the order's setup and running allocation. Cancellation does not refund them or elapsed factory time. Clinical Material Lots leave freely available stock at admission but remain a dedicated care reservation. Before any living person exists, cancellation can return the proven unused reservation once. After the living milestone, that reservation remains with the surviving people and their receiving service. Cancelling the production order releases its factory commitment without cancelling those people's care. A failed output report cannot return supplies that another completed receipt has already assigned or consumed. The same rule applies to the accelerated profile.

The site must have a receiving plan before work starts. The plan names the hospital or maturation institute, the destination for adult reception, the supply route, and the authority responsible for existing people if the sponsor cancels. These are actual places and institutions. They are not additional resource currencies. The committed factories express production capacity, while the institute's ordinary staffing and clinical requirements express whether the cell can operate at all.

A country can expand through additional paid sites and orders. There is no lifetime quota on people produced. Every additional cell occupies its own real industrial commitment for the full schedule. A secondary laboratory does not automatically become a nursery, and a new copy of Wu does not multiply the factory output of an existing one. Concurrent orders compete for the same available factories, clinical material, fuel, and receiving capacity.

The ordinary research lanes remain research commitments. Production does not consume a third research lane after the relevant programme is learned. Conversely, completing a production order does not free a lane occupied by an unrelated research project. This distinction keeps an established institute capable of manufacturing and researching at the same time without turning either activity into an unrecorded capacity grant.

## Maturation and adult reception

The maturation period develops a living person's ability to function independently. A physically complete body can still be unready for ordinary life or professional service. The programme must establish adult physical readiness, orientation, communication, and basic decision-making before any military training or deployment. An unfinished candidate cannot be used to fill a manpower shortage by skipping reception.

At the living-person milestone the order's actual cohort is fixed. A supervised maturation class then continues for the proposed 90 days. A damaged nursery can delay completion. It cannot erase elapsed time or send the people back to an earlier non-person state. Evacuated people retain their age, condition, and unfinished work. A receiving institute resumes the remaining schedule after it can supply them.

The initial Clinical Material reservation covers normal maturation and the first 90 days of adult reception at the recorded site. The minimum cell reservation is paid per admitted cell and does not become cheaper by splitting the batch after admission. Unused reserved care can follow those people to a legitimate receiving site. It cannot be refunded as unrestricted money or used to count a second group as supplied.

Reception begins when the participants are ready to leave protected maturation. All classes first complete common adult orientation. Civilian reception teaches ordinary work, local language where necessary, and life outside the institute. Military and specialist classes begin their separate professional periods only after that orientation. A continuity candidate remains a person undergoing assessment, with no automatic right to Wu's office.

The sponsor chooses a destination, but the public charter must include a refusal route. Someone who refuses military or specialist service enters civilian reception when a viable destination exists. Refusal wastes the sponsor's intended training opportunity and can delay a deployment. It does not make the copy a failed batch or justify deleting it from the living count. The classified route can produce a dispute over withheld choice, with service refusal, exposure, and loss of staff support as concrete consequences.

No production receipt changes the death count of the reference participant. A copy created after that participant's death is a new person with an inherited reference. The source person's death, funeral, awards, and earlier office remain part of the campaign's history.

## A finite cohort has one remaining entitlement

The shared clone owner needs a distinct medical-replacement mode for these people. The existing `clone_equipment` economy remains unchanged. It continues to serve its existing Kruger, Mengele, and other approved consumers. Wu's finite mode must not place its people into that archetype, call a legacy access reward as a substitute, or add a second recurring manpower modifier.

The proposed finite mode has one physical unissued-person archetype and a matching owner-managed cohort record. One whole physical item corresponds to one living person still awaiting a first population or military settlement. Fractional engine stock is never rounded upward into another person. This item is an accounting representation for the game, not a consumable body part or a market licence to sell people. It is not ordinary industrial equipment that a country can manufacture directly from a technology unlock.

The owner creates physical stock only against the actual living output of an identified production order. The record identifies the source order, reference standard, birth milestone, current custodian, current location, maturity, care coverage, and unspent count. The stockpile is the physical quantity check. The cohort record supplies provenance and settlement authority. Neither can independently mint people when the other is absent or inconsistent.

Settlement can use only the lower of verified physical stock and the matching unspent entitlement. A country with unexplained extra stock receives no extra people from reconciliation. A country with a surviving cohort record but missing physical stock cannot manufacture the difference by calling a repair helper. The mismatch opens a custody investigation. Care remains due for known living people, and the unresolved amount cannot be spent elsewhere.

This mode belongs inside the shared clone owner. Wu owns his research, permissions, institutions, and personal continuity choices. He does not own a parallel national manpower bank. The extension is proposed design and remains unapproved and unimplemented. Until the owner contract and its engine-facing settlement paths are accepted and proven, implementation must leave those new paths blocked with their exact dependency. It must not replace them with legacy stock, a national manpower bonus, or a free division.

### First settlement destinations

A cohort has one first settlement for each person. That settlement moves responsibility from the unissued cohort into the destination that now represents the person. Historical membership remains visible after settlement, but it is no longer spendable entitlement.

| Destination | What is committed once | What remains after settlement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civilian reception | The exact surviving count enters a named state through an approved population transaction. | Ordinary population, food, shelter, migration, and medical systems own the people. Cohort history identifies origin without remaining redeemable. |
| Ordinary military replacement | The exact trained surviving count enters national military manpower once. | The native military pool and its consumers own subsequent recruitment, loss, and demobilisation. The finite cohort cannot be redeemed again. |
| Cohort formation | The exact trained count fills the selected new formation through one reviewed formation transaction. | Native unit manpower represents the soldiers. Cohort records track service and care but do not provide a second population or manpower grant. |
| Named specialist or continuity service | The person first completes civilian reception, then receives the qualified role. | The character or specialist record is an identity and assignment overlay on that already counted person. It grants no second headcount. |

The civilian transaction must not also award a free national manpower payment. Any native side effect that would create such an extra event-funded credit must be measured and neutralised through an approved population-owner contract. A guessed inverse of the existing population-loss helper is not sufficient evidence. Later ordinary conscription belongs to the population and recruitment owners. It must not reactivate the consumed replication entitlement.

The ordinary military path deliberately ends exact cohort control over each future posting. Once people join a fungible national manpower pool, the institute cannot reliably identify which later ordinary division received each person. That route therefore grants no batch-specific combat bonus and cannot later claim a matching population refund. It remains valuable because it supplies a finite, trained replacement contribution that the army can use normally.

The cohort-formation path preserves a narrower service identity and needs separate proof for exact admission, reinforcement, demobilisation, and transfer. It cannot use ordinary pool bookkeeping as evidence that every later replacement is a clone from the correct batch. Unsupported identity-preserving operations remain unresolved implementation requirements. A country-wide enhancement or a refreshed replacement division is not an accepted substitute.

### Conservation and historical records

For a produced cohort, the first-settlement record obeys this accounting identity:

`actual new living people = still-unsettled people + people first-settled once + deaths before first settlement`

Still-unsettled people include maturation, reception, reserved training, transfer, captivity, and unresolved custody. A person occupies only one of those locations at a time. A transfer changes location and custodian. It does not create another birth or another entitlement.

The first-settled total is historical. It includes people who later die in the ordinary population or military systems. Their later death does not reduce that historical total and reopen room for another settlement. Current living totals and historical first-settlement totals must never be added as if they were separate populations.

For example, a cohort produces 1,000 living people. After maturation, 600 enter ordinary military manpower, 250 enter a receiving state's population, and 150 remain in the institute. Five of the civilian arrivals later qualify as specialists. Their appointments add no people. If ten of the remaining institute residents die, the birth identity becomes 1,000 = 140 unsettled + 850 first-settled + 10 deaths before settlement. A later military loss among the 600 is recorded by the military owner without changing that identity or creating a replacement entitlement.

A shortage in an aggregate equipment counter is not proof of ten deaths. The people may have been transferred, captured, committed, or stranded by an interrupted transaction. The owner must establish the actual outcome before recording a loss. Unknown custody blocks further redemption of the disputed amount without declaring the people dead or assigning them to an unrelated country.

Material discarded before the living milestone creates no Deaths entry. Deaths among unsettled living people debit the actual cohort, retire the corresponding physical entitlement, and enter the shared Deaths system once. They do not also remove people from a state population that never contained them. After settlement, the ordinary civilian or military owner applies the real loss. Wu records the medical or institutional consequence without repeating the death transaction.

### Splits, mergers, and changed custody

A split assigns disjoint parts of a cohort to separate destinations. Their counts sum to the original unspent amount, and every part retains the same production and reference history. A merger groups records for presentation or a shared service order. It does not erase individual ages, flaws, spent entitlements, or care deadlines.

Transferring 200 people from a cohort of 1,000 never transfers the original 1,000-person entitlement. The sender loses authority over those 200 before the recipient can settle them. The recipient receives their remaining state, not a fresh version of the original production reward. Recapture, a change of country name, annexation, and later release cannot restart the same handover.

An unresolved delivery has one owner record and one pending outcome. Departure, transit, arrival, cancellation, and capture settle against that record. Restoring the sender after a failed delivery is permitted only when the people actually remain with or return to that sender. A refund cannot coexist with a delivered group. Physical stock that arrives through an untracked native transfer stays non-redeemable until its original entitlement is identified and the previous custodian's authority is closed.

Ordinary lend-lease and market routes must not be assumed to preserve personal provenance. The intended finite mode uses reviewed custody transfers, with the equipment transfer gates closed against unsupported routes. A captured holding remains an actual custody problem, with emergency care available even when its production history cannot yet be authenticated. The capture mechanics must be tested against the exact stockpile and site behaviour of the installed game.

## R04: copied skill needs apprenticeship

A replicated specialist enters a particular service, not a nationwide bonus pool. The host must name the vacancy, reference field, mentor, training site, and eventual employer. A completed body is eligible for assessment. It is not automatically a surgeon, scientist, ace, chief engineer, or operative.

The proposed apprenticeship has a 90-day common orientation, which can overlap the already funded adult reception when the destination is suitable. It then requires the field-specific service period below. The candidate must remain at a supplied training site with a qualified mentor and the relevant working institution. Losing the mentor pauses the uncompleted professional period. It does not erase skills already demonstrated.

An apprenticeship action commits the named candidate, one civilian factory, Clinical Material Lots, and Political Power or the appropriate service experience. Candidate commitment is counted as a spendable commitment. No fifth staff or manpower debit is hidden in the action. Existing mentors remain part of the institution's staffing requirement, and their occupied post cannot simultaneously supervise another incompatible assignment.

| Specialist field | Professional period after orientation | Clinical Material Lots per candidate | Fourth cost per class of up to five | Assignment unlocked |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Surgeon | 150 days | 10 | 35 Political Power | One supplied hospital's independent advanced-care service. |
| Researcher | 180 days | 10 | 50 Political Power | One existing research lane or archive-restoration assignment. |
| Pilot | 180 days | 10 | 35 Air Experience | One qualified training or operational air-service assignment through its owner. |
| Engineer | 120 days | 5 | 35 Political Power | One named facility, repair programme, or production-standard assignment. |
| Intelligence specialist | 210 days | 10 | 50 Political Power | One agency-owned training or analytical assignment with the appropriate access. |

The class limit is a supervision limit for one apprenticeship order, not a permanent cap on specialists. Additional classes need their own facilities and committed teaching time. Their cost is quoted for the whole class, and cancelled or transferred candidates cannot repeatedly claim its completion.

Every specialist timing benefit is a quoted reduction in the time needed to complete one identified assignment while qualified service continues. Appointment awards no completed work. The assignment retains its underlying remaining work, with the strongest applicable specialist contribution changing the working rate only while that person actually serves. A 20 percent time reduction therefore corresponds to completing the same remaining work in 80 percent of the ordinary time. Reassignment, dismissal, a replacement instructor, or reopening the same job cannot compound that rate or grant another block of completed work. Losing qualified service preserves only work actually completed, then pauses the dependent work or resumes it at the ordinary rate. This service-based interpretation governs all specialist duration benefits below.

### Surgeons

A qualified surgical copy can keep a named hospital's learned advanced-care standard operating when Wu is elsewhere. It does not invent a cure, staff an empty building, or create an additional treatment allocation. The hospital gains a capable local practitioner whose service can continue across a political change if its supplies and permissions survive.

The proposed payoff is a 25 percent reduction in the remaining time of eligible local organ-replacement or rehabilitation assignments, with one qualified surgical lead applying per hospital programme. This replaces any weaker copy-apprentice assistance on that assignment. It does not multiply with another copy of the same surgical reference. Patient eligibility, consumed treatment, and actual survivor accounting remain with the medical project.

A surgeon who has copied a narrow wartime specialty may perform reliably in that field and struggle with an unfamiliar patient group. The first difficult case tests judgement through a supervised choice. The government can retain the specialist within the qualified scope, pay for additional cross-training, or support independent practice through R12. A bad case creates an actual patient-care response, not a universal loss of all cloned surgeons.

### Researchers

A research copy works in a selected existing lane. Its main value is continuity of learned methods and specialised attention. It can complete eligible documented work when the original reference scientist is absent, repair a damaged archive, or support a project in a field that the reference actually understood.

The proposed specialist contribution reduces an eligible lane's projected remaining research duration by 20 percent while the qualified copy remains assigned. It cannot retroactively complete work, add a national research slot, or add a third primary institute lane. Several copies in the same lane provide redundancy and a paid teaching capacity. They cannot compound reductions of the same clock. Only the strongest applicable specialist contribution controls the current working rate.

A research copy does not inherit Kruger's personal project history, his six-field role, or the unique powers of his appointment. A compatible externally granted technology remains a learned capability with its actual provenance. The copy's successful work records its own contribution. An archive copied from a famous scientist is not authority to reproduce that scientist's engine character or exclusive event rewards.

### Pilots

A copied flight reference can shorten the distance between a new adult and useful training, but it cannot supply an aircraft, a combat record, or instinct for a mission the reference never flew. The service period requires an operating air school and compatible equipment. Current operational demands may change the assignment, with incomplete qualification retained rather than fabricated as success.

The first payoff is an experienced instructor for one air training establishment or a qualified replacement pilot contribution to the relevant air owner. A named operational pilot requires a supported native role and its own observed skill. No ace status, victory count, medals, or national air bonus is copied from the source person. The exact owner interface for pilot personnel must be verified before that operational route is implemented.

The narrative should allow a copy to recognise an obsolete cockpit and need retraining for the aircraft now in service. A captured archive can therefore be valuable without being an instant answer to an air-force manpower shortage. R12 can broaden judgement after actual service, but it does not make every aircraft type interchangeable.

### Engineers

An engineering copy is attached to a real facility or repair assignment. It can preserve a production standard through the loss of the original specialist, lead restoration of a damaged medical fabrication site, or teach another team to maintain equipment that the country already owns.

The proposed benefit is a 25 percent reduction in the remaining time of one eligible repair or standardisation assignment. It grants no free factory, growth cell, power source, railway, or resource deposit. Several engineers can cover different sites when the host funds them, but one project cannot repeatedly restart its clock to collect the same reduction.

An engineer's useful knowledge can outlive the office that trained them. On foreign transfer, the former institution keeps its own manuals and completed repairs. The receiving institution gets the person, their qualified field, and only the records actually transferred. It does not receive a copy of the former host's buildings or an automatic right to use every proprietary method.

### Intelligence specialists

An intelligence copy can serve as an analyst, instructor, or eventually a field specialist through the agency owner's real appointment route. Its reference may contain remembered languages and analytical methods. It does not contain current enemy plans, passwords, networks, contacts, or diplomatic secrets that were never recorded.

The initial assignment strengthens one evidence-analysis or archive-authentication task. A proposed 25 percent reduction in that task's projected remaining review time applies while the qualified analyst serves and the agency and relevant evidence remain available. It cannot increase the number of operative slots, create a free field operative, grant an enemy network, or supply current intelligence from an old memory.

A field deployment needs separate agency eligibility and its normal equipment, training, and operation costs. The character and operative systems are distinct in the consulted documentation, so a scientist-style token is not an accepted substitute for an operative. A copy discovered by a foreign service may become a witness or an asylum applicant. Its value depends on what it actually knows and is willing to disclose.

## Memory, testimony, and professional independence

Every specialist begins with a reference date, a qualified field, and a level of demonstrated independence. These are record attributes, not three new public meters. The player sees a short status such as supervised practice, qualified practice, or review required, with the relevant missing qualification stated nearby.

Memory problems should be specific. A copy may confidently apply an outdated method, confuse a remembered relationship with a current colleague, omit an unrecorded stage of a procedure, or rely on an instructor's authority in an unfamiliar situation. These incidents affect the associated assignment and open a meaningful response. They do not randomly remove all skills from every copy in the country.

Repeated copies of the same reference share some blind spots. Placing them together can preserve a scarce method quickly, but it can also leave a whole service without independent judgement. Mixing a copied specialist with an independently trained colleague costs teaching time and reduces that shared weakness. It does not create a new research family or add an unrelated national modifier.

R12 develops independent professional judgement after real supervised service. Completion allows an assessed copy to lead the same work without the original reference mentor, train later classes within its field, and evaluate a new case through ordinary study. It does not reconstruct missing personal memories or prove that the copy is the original person.

A failed independence assessment produces a specific remedial assignment lasting a proposed 60 days at the same type of working institution. It consumes five Clinical Material Lots per candidate and one civilian factory for the class. The candidate remains committed, making three cost types. The remedial result resolves the identified gap when the required work is actually completed. The route must not use an endless series of unchanged failure rolls that makes independent practice unattainable.

Copies can acquire new experience that the original lacks. A surgeon who remained in a besieged city may later be the best reference for emergency care. An engineer who rebuilt a captured laboratory may know its local faults better than Wu. Their later teaching records must name their own service history. A new archive made from them is a new paid and dated reference, not an automatic synchronization of every related mind.

## R05: replacement soldiers for an existing army

R05 creates a service establishment for people who have completed normal adult maturation. The army accepts a cohort for training, provides a real training site, and declares whether the graduates will enter ordinary replacement manpower or remain together for later cohort formation. The country cannot count an immature class as soldiers merely because an offensive is about to begin.

The proposed service course lasts 120 days after the 90-day common orientation. For every 1,000 trainees, it commits those 1,000 people, 90 Infantry Equipment for training, 20 Clinical Material Lots, and 25 Army Experience. Smaller courses scale people, rifles, and care to the actual group, with a minimum of five Army Experience per course. The full quote is calculated once for the admitted class. Splitting the class afterward does not refund experience or create another completion. Physical training and care commitments round upward to whole stock units for the complete class. Army Experience scales with class size and rounds upward to a multiple of five, with the five-point minimum. Person counts remain exact whole people. These cost-rounding rules never round a fractional physical person entitlement upward.

Training rifles are committed assets. Intact rifles return to the equipment stockpile when the course closes, and actual losses remain losses. The manpower route supplies trained people only. It does not also award an armed division or a second set of rifles. The clinical material is consumed as the training and care occur. A cancelled course can return unused material and intact committed rifles through its exact receipt, while spent teaching time and experience remain spent.

At graduation, the ordinary replacement route offers a one-time handover of the actual ready survivors. Those people are removed from finite unissued stock and credited to native military manpower once. An interrupted handover cannot leave both the original cohort and the new manpower available. The same commitment also closes any pending civilian admission for those people.

The army receives no automatic elite trait from this handover. Its advantage is the availability of a trained replacement intake backed by a long industrial programme. Existing divisions still need their equipment, supply, and ordinary reinforcement process. The country must choose whether scarce factories should make more rifles and trucks or sustain the next replication order.

A government that loses its war before graduation can complete civilian reception, retain a small service reserve, or negotiate a transfer to an ally that has a genuine vacancy and accepts the care obligation. The end of war does not delete the trainees. A country that can no longer house them must use the existing migration and reception owners, preserving the exact living count.

The military report should identify the receiving army's need and the actual class size. A class that is too small to fill a division should remain visibly too small. A disappointed staff can demand acceleration or accept a later intake, but its demand does not change the people already created.

## R06: a formation with a recorded generation

Cohort formations preserve the military identity of related replication classes. They can receive group-specific care, share a trained doctrine, and support a recorded service history. Their defining feature is controlled composition and continuity of support. They do not receive the legacy stockpile's recurring manpower effect.

The proposed standard battalion has 1,000 actual living personnel, including its ordinary officers and embedded medical staff. It uses 90 Infantry Equipment, two combat width, 70 organisation, and 20 strength as the initial comparison profile inherited from the inspected shared clone battalion. These inherited values are reference values for the proposed finite battalion, not a claim that it already exists or is balanced. Its defining new burden is the finite personnel supply and continuing generation care.

A proposed first formation contains five such battalions. It needs 5,000 trained ready people, 450 Infantry Equipment, 75 Clinical Material Lots for its first 90 days of service, and 20 Command Power. Formation organisation takes 90 days at an actual supplied military site. The people, equipment, care, and command allocation are the four cost types. There is no additional national manpower charge for those same 5,000 people and no separate manpower grant before creation.

The normal paid commissioning result is a new formation at its earned training state. It does not inherit the reference person's decorations, a donor formation's current experience, or the strength of a unit it replaces. Full-equipment and full-manpower creation can occur only when the exact physical contributions have been reserved and consumed in the same reviewed transaction. A failed creation restores only those contributions, with no second birth or first-settlement receipt.

The proposed steady care cost is five Clinical Material Lots per 1,000 living personnel for each 30-day service interval. A five-battalion formation therefore needs 25 lots per interval while fully manned. Its ordinary supply and rifle replacement remain native army requirements. Clinical care is paid against actual registered service strength, not the template's paper maximum. Exact measurement of that strength is an implementation requirement.

Formation strength can fall during combat. A batch cannot repair casualties by renaming the division, rebuilding its template, transferring its command, or deleting and recreating it. Replacement personnel must come from another trained finite cohort or an explicitly approved mixed-personnel policy. A mixed formation is no longer presented as a fully replicated generation, and any composition-dependent benefits must adjust to its actual recorded status.

### Reinforcement without another population grant

The intended pure-cohort reinforcement path reserves a specified number of ready people against one eligible understrength formation. It debits the remaining finite entitlement once and fills only the proved vacancy. Surplus people remain in their original reserve. The transaction cannot heal organisation, replace rifles, raise experience, or fill a second formation unless those outputs are separately supplied by their actual owners.

Native automatic reinforcement from an ordinary manpower pool must not silently turn a pure-cohort formation into a mixed one while the UI continues to claim complete generation control. The owner must prove either a supported restriction with exact cohort reinforcement or an explicit mixed-personnel state that accurately describes what occurred. The preferred pure-cohort path remains a required feature. An unavailable engine operation is a recorded blocker, not permission to reduce the design to a national modifier or pretend that all replacements came from the same source.

The new finite battalion therefore needs its own shared-owner unit contract. Reusing the existing `clone_infantry` token unchanged would retain its legacy equipment requirement and its provider assumptions. The intended new identity is a generic finite medical-cohort infantry consumer, with final identifiers reserved only after collision review. It is not a new Kruger random-technology family and is not an alias for the strictly gated Mengele refinement.

### Training and obedience

A standard cohort learns ordinary military discipline. Its order-following comes from training, officers, supply, and the institution that holds its commission. Wu does not gain personal command because the soldiers share his medical system. A formation can remain loyal to the state while demanding treatment from the institute.

The government can choose integrated command, a specialist corps, or an institute service contract. Integrated command places formations in the ordinary army with independent medical inspection. A specialist corps keeps related cohorts under trained officers and concentrates the care burden in a small number of depots. An institute contract gives Wu greater control of postings, treatment, and internal appointments. Only that actual concession increases his command leverage.

These arrangements produce different failures. Integrated command can overuse a formation whose medical restrictions are unfamiliar to ordinary staff. A specialist corps can lose much of its support when one depot falls. An institute contract can leave the army unable to order a deployment without bargaining over treatment and authority. Each has recovery through better command practice, distributed support, or a changed contract. None is a hidden permanent technology lock.

## Enhancement follows people and service

R06 can use the temporary endurance and combat courses from Part 6 after the relevant research and explicit admission. A formation receives the applicable course once, with the same tour, withdrawal, recovery, and supply rules. It does not receive a fresh course whenever a cohort is renamed or its command changes.

Permanent enhancement requires H05 and its real preparation. It is an optional branch. It never becomes a prerequisite for ordinary replacement soldiers, specialists, or continuity. The proposed enhanced-cohort commissioning profile uses actual ready people, Infantry Equipment, Alteration Preparation Packs, and Military Care Packs as its four cost types. It omits the standard commissioning Command Power payment and clinical-care reservation, which are replaced within this profile rather than charged as hidden fifth and sixth costs.

For five battalions, the initial proposal is 5,000 people, 450 Infantry Equipment, 25 Alteration Preparation Packs, and 75 Military Care Packs, with the Part 6 permanent-alteration training period of 120 days. The people settle directly into the final military formation once. They are not first credited to national manpower and then also counted as newly created enhanced bodies.

The enhanced cohort uses the H05 performance package and its lifetime-care consequences. The ordinary finite battalion's reference organisation and strength are not an extra bonus layered on top. A dedicated compatibility comparison must establish the final combined unit values before implementation. Repeated grants of research, a change of provider, or several copied instructors cannot stack the same enhancement again.

The Part 6 permanent service bill covers the enhanced cohort's ordinary continuing clinical care. The same monthly medical purpose must not also charge the standard five-lot cohort bill. A genuinely separate recall, reconstruction, or specialist treatment can have its own declared action, but it must identify the different work and its costs.

A permanent alteration can affect only a subset of a generation. That subset retains its own service history, care level, and altered status. Merging it with unaltered people cannot spread the enhanced benefits to the whole formation. Separating it afterward cannot create more altered people than were admitted. If exact mixed-composition effects are unsupported, that conversion remains blocked until the owner has an accepted solution.

## R07: generation care and recall

Generation care makes the people less dependent on one physician's private knowledge. It establishes a shared clinical record for related cohorts, teaches independent teams to recognise known weaknesses, and creates a practical recall route. It does not guarantee that all members are identical or that one treatment fits every later generation.

A normal settled civilian cohort uses ordinary hospitals after reception. During its initial follow-up, it retains a generation-care appointment and an identified provider. The proposed follow-up lasts 180 days and costs five Clinical Material Lots per 1,000 people per 30-day interval, rounded up only at the whole service-order level. Where the initial production reservation still covers the same interval, that reservation pays first. The country does not pay two providers for the same promised care.

After a clean completed follow-up, ordinary civilian care becomes sufficient unless a specific continuing condition exists. The public route can therefore produce people who no longer need Wu's exclusive treatment network. Military cohorts keep their service-related bill while deployed. Enhanced people keep the distinct Part 6 obligation, and a continuity copy can retain a specific medical condition if its actual development created one.

### Batch flaws

A flaw belongs to a defined source standard, production order, or training reference. It may affect several related groups, but it cannot automatically jump to every clone, every patient treated by Wu, or every person of the donor's nationality. The discovery identifies the suspected scope. A review then establishes which cohorts require action.

The useful flaw families are incomplete physical endurance, unstable recovery after prolonged service, shared training errors, a missing qualification, accelerated decline after a compressed schedule, and a disputed or corrupted reference. These are fictional gameplay conditions. They do not specify real biological targets or laboratory mechanisms.

An endurance flaw reduces the affected formation's sustained organisation recovery until rest and treatment are completed. The proposed penalty is 20 percent to organisation recovery, with a 60-day supervised recovery assignment. A training flaw causes poor response to a particular operational demand and requires a 90-day retraining period. A qualification flaw suspends one specialist role until the missing assessment is completed. None removes the person's existence or grants replacement manpower for their dismissal.

A compressed-schedule cohort can develop a continuing care requirement after prolonged service. The response can be medical stabilisation, a less demanding posting, retirement, or an honestly recorded decision to continue under strain. Continuing service cannot be disguised as a harmless pause while still retaining every combat benefit. The exact increased care bill and restricted capabilities are shown before that choice.

A corrupted reference can leave useful skills intact while undermining testimony or professional confidence. The host can authenticate the relevant archive, pair the affected specialists with independent reviewers, or remove them from the disputed assignment while preserving their civilian status. Destroying an archive does not erase the evidence of its misuse or remove the people created from it.

### Recall as an actual operation

A recall names the affected service groups and a receiving hospital or training site. The player must bring field formations to the selected safe location, maintain supply, and complete the treatment or retraining interval. A selected army must not continue receiving full deployed benefits while being counted as present in an unrelated hospital.

The proposed ordinary recall action commits the affected people or formations, one civilian factory, and 10 Clinical Material Lots per 1,000 people for a 60-day treatment. Those are three cost types. A military order that requires a command allocation can add 15 Command Power as the fourth. Existing equipment remains with the unit unless a separate legitimate transfer occurs.

A divided generation can be recalled in stages when the medical sites can support only part of it. The treated portion receives the resolved-condition record. The untreated portion remains affected. The resolution does not automatically repair another group overseas or certify a production standard that has not been reviewed.

Military refusal to release an affected formation creates a command incident. The government can enforce the medical withdrawal, accept a shorter and narrower deployment under stated penalties, or concede posting authority to the institute. Concession can increase Wu Authority because it changes actual control. Successful treatment under an independent military doctor can reduce that leverage. Neither result grants another research reward.

## R08: a faster schedule with later obligations

Accelerated maturation is an optional production standard, available after the institute has completed a normal cohort and observed its reception. It cannot be obtained by choosing high Authority, activating an evolution, or declaring an emergency. Research alone does not shorten existing paid orders without a recorded conversion and its real costs.

The proposed accelerated cohort cell keeps the same maximum of 250 people and the same one-factory commitment, but shortens the full production and protected maturation schedule from 270 to 210 days. The living milestone occurs after 150 days, followed by 60 days of protected maturation. Common adult orientation and professional or military training remain separate and are not shortened.

Each accelerated cell spends 250 Support Equipment, 2,000 fuel, and 20 Clinical Material Lots. The order therefore still has four cost types including the factory commitment. The larger care reservation includes the accelerated schedule's first reception period. A four-cell intake costs 1,000 Support Equipment, 8,000 fuel, and 80 lots, with four factories committed for 210 days.

Accelerated people must meet the same adult-readiness requirements. A failed readiness assessment extends protected care until the identified gap is resolved. It does not produce an underage military replacement or allow the government to declare the person mature by spending Command Power.

The first 180 days after qualification use twice the normal generation-care bill. A military formation from this standard also begins with a proposed 15 percent organisation-recovery penalty until a completed 90-day field-acclimatisation period. It must receive its full care during that period. The penalty can be removed through a successful recorded assignment, not by merging the formation into another template.

The strategic choice is earlier availability at greater industrial and clinical cost, followed by a restricted initial service period. A country facing immediate defeat may still be better served by medical aid or ordinary recruitment, since accelerated replication remains a long programme. The AI should not begin it merely because a surrender value is high when the country cannot protect the production site long enough to finish.

No claim is made that acceleration improves memory. Personal continuity and specialist qualification keep their own requirements. A rushed body does not reconstruct an absent archive, shorten a pilot's missing training, or restore the original Wu's unrecorded experience.

## R09: Wu records what a successor could know

Wu's personal continuity archive is a deliberate project. It is not an automatic result of ordinary cloning, organ replacement, or a government order issued after his death. The institute must have developed its reference methods, trained real specialists, and preserved a usable account of Wu's medical work while he can still participate.

The archive separates medical method, institutional knowledge, personal memory, and authority claims. Medical method includes the treatments and reasoning that the institute has actually recorded. Institutional knowledge identifies the services, staff, and projects that Wu knew at that time. Personal memory remains incomplete and can conflict with other records. Authority claims state what Wu wanted a successor to inherit. The government is not obliged to treat those claims as appointments.

The first archive can be held by an independent medical board, by Wu personally, or under a divided-custody agreement. Independent custody makes succession easier to verify and reduces the risk that one person can conceal every usable record. Personal custody gives Wu a stronger negotiating position and makes the loss of his location more dangerous. Divided custody lets the state preserve service continuity while Wu retains a personal account whose private content cannot be used as a universal government credential.

A copied archive has the same date and known gaps as its source. Duplication protects against physical loss but creates no new knowledge. Its location and custody are recorded separately from its content. Two archives stored in the same threatened laboratory are not geographically independent backups.

A proposed archive update occupies one existing research lane and one civilian factory for 60 days, consuming 100 Support Equipment and 40 Political Power. The update incorporates work and experience that its participating Wu actually acquired. It cannot read the private experience of another copy, an enemy laboratory, or a missing original. If several copies contribute, the record identifies the participating people and preserves conflicting accounts instead of pretending that their memories have become identical.

The update does not alter living copies without their participation. A qualified copy may study the new material, reject part of its interpretation, or need further clinical assessment. An old archive remains valid historical evidence even after a newer one is made. The institute cannot erase responsibility for an earlier programme by replacing its current reference file.

## R10: a copy becomes capable of continuing Wu's work

R10 establishes a qualification programme. It does not create the candidate and does not itself replace the original Wu. The host must already have produced a living adult copy through a paid personal order, preserved an authenticated continuity archive, and supplied the people who will conduct the assessment.

The candidate has its own identity from the living milestone. It completes civilian reception before appointment, with no parallel military manpower reward. Until qualification, it is a continuity candidate with limited demonstrated skills. It cannot supply the full national medical package, grant advanced research, control every engineered formation, or complete a universal cure simply because its face matches Wu's portrait.

The proposed individual qualification assignment lasts 180 days. It commits the candidate and one civilian factory, consumes 20 Clinical Material Lots, and costs 50 Political Power. Those are the four cost types. It is a clinical and institutional assessment, so it does not also reserve a primary research lane. The R10 research project has already established the programme. A candidate in assessment cannot simultaneously occupy another professional or command assignment.

Three connected assessments fill that period. A 60-day clinical assignment demonstrates recorded treatment in a working hospital. A 45-day service assignment shows that the candidate can understand current care and control obligations without assuming that the archive describes the whole present world. A 75-day independent case assessment tests the ability to reason through a medically valid problem that the candidate has not merely memorised. These periods describe gameplay work, not medical procedures.

A current outbreak can supply a real case. A country in Calm World can use an appropriate contained clinical case or a reviewed diagnostic exercise rooted in existing medical practice. The programme never requires the government to create an epidemic, attack another country, or wait for high Chaos. It cannot manufacture a national disaster as a test fixture.

Successful qualification establishes that this copy can carry Wu's exceptional medical capability under the same real conditions as the original: a functioning institute, available staff, valid disease access, production, and delivery. It can begin future universal adaptations and eligible new research. It does not inherit treatments, specimens, or projects that were absent from its reference and subsequent education. The ability to solve a future case is distinct from already knowing its answer.

The national medical package is applied once through the existing institution. A country with the original and a qualified copy does not receive two copies of the founding protection multipliers. The second doctor provides continuity, can lead a different existing service, and can preserve work during a local absence. It does not add a free research lane or another founding stockpile.

### Incomplete qualification

A candidate can demonstrate strong medical memory but fail independent judgement, understand clinical work but misunderstand current control obligations, or present a reference whose authorship is disputed. The result names the missing qualification. It does not erase the person's successful work or reduce them to generic failed material.

The host can continue supervised service, complete a remedial assessment, or admit the person to civilian life without appointing them as Wu. The proposed remedial assignment lasts 90 days, commits the candidate and one civilian factory, and consumes 10 Clinical Material Lots. Completion resolves the named deficiency when the required work and evidence exist. The design does not permit an endless unchanged failure roll to block an otherwise valid successor.

An archive missing essential medical content needs reconstruction before qualification can continue. Reconstruction uses authenticated institutional records, surviving qualified staff, and any reliable contribution from the candidate. It cannot create personal knowledge from a photograph, a body, or a rumour that a backup once existed. The proposed reconstruction project uses one lane, two civilian factories, 250 Support Equipment, and 60 Political Power over 180 days. It restores only the supported reference scope and records what remains unknown.

If Wu is dead and no qualified copy or usable continuity archive exists, a biological lookalike cannot revive his unique role by name alone. The institute keeps its learned medicine and ordinary successor routes. New exceptional continuity remains unavailable until a genuine surviving reference or qualified person is found. This is a consequence of lost material and knowledge, not a Chaos or Authority gate.

## Preparing succession while the original is alive

A functioning continuity route should allow preparation before disaster. The original Wu can teach a qualified copy, the government can identify a preferred medical successor, and independent staff can rehearse the transfer of service responsibilities. None of this requires killing, retiring, or capturing the original.

The government chooses a single recognised institute lead, a medical collegium with one accountable lead for current decisions, or separate institutional assignments. These are working governance labels. They determine who can authorise current clinical work, which archive supports the appointment, and how disagreements are resolved. They do not merge the people into one character.

Under a designated successor, the copy knows which responsibilities it may assume and which powers remain subject to a new government decision. Under a collegium, several qualified Wus can cover different hospitals or existing research lanes, with shared clinical records and independent witnesses. Under separate assignments, one copy can concentrate on public medicine and another on an authorised specialist programme. Each assignment still needs its actual staff, site, supplies, and permission.

Preparation can reduce an emergency interruption without making succession automatic in every circumstance. A designated copy that is captured, unqualified for a newly changed system, or cut off from the institute cannot take office through a stale flag. A board can temporarily keep documented care running while it locates a valid lead.

The office transfer preserves the institution's existing mandates, contracts, Authority, project progress, and obligations. Selecting a younger or more cooperative copy does not reset Authority to the public founding value or cancel promises made to dependent formations. The new lead can renegotiate those commitments through actual choices, audits, and service transfers. It cannot clear them by changing the portrait.

The original's medical absence and death remain different conditions. A captured Wu is alive and can return. A preserved but critically injured Wu remains the same living person under M10. A dead Wu remains dead in the historical record even when a qualified copy takes the office. Appointment of a successor never subtracts the original death from the shared totals.

## A succession crisis after loss

Loss of the recognised lead first asks whether another qualified person is already available and whether the institution can still operate. Existing independent hospitals continue their certified treatments. Active universal adaptations keep their actual progress, but work requiring exceptional judgement pauses until a qualified lead takes responsibility.

The proposed succession response has a normal 60-day institutional window. An already qualified, supplied successor can take the medical office through a 15-day handover. An unfinished candidate continues its actual remaining qualification time. The window does not accelerate a body or award a completed archive. Its purpose is to settle current authority and emergency service priorities while long work continues separately.

The host's practical choices are to appoint a qualified prepared successor, maintain an independent interim board, negotiate the return of a living qualified Wu, or acknowledge the permanent loss of exceptional leadership and preserve the ordinary institute. The player should see the actual available candidates and the reason each unavailable route cannot proceed.

An interim board can preserve completed cures, current material manufacture, and standard-specific control archives. It cannot improvise a universal cure or take personal knowledge from another living copy without access. It prioritises existing patients and dependent formations over starting elective replication orders. New production can be paused while already living cohorts remain supplied.

A failed handover can leave one service interrupted while another continues. The host might keep hospital care functioning but lose access to a private control archive. It might retain the army's medical service while a foreign copy holds the latest cure adaptation. These outcomes should follow the actual division of knowledge and custody. They should not all collapse into one country-wide penalty.

The Part 6 engineered-formation archive emergency remains distinct. Its existing 45-day control-archive response is not extended or restarted by the new 60-day succession window. A qualified copy can resolve the relevant part of that emergency only if it has the needed standard, access, and service capacity. The country cannot buy an unlimited extension by repeatedly appointing different Wus.

## R11: distributed continuity has real locations

R11 lets the institute maintain continuity at more than one functioning site. It requires two supplied institutions and an agreed division of custody. The research creates the organisational capability to distribute records and qualified responsibility. It does not create a second body, clone another archive's missing content, or duplicate the main laboratory's buildings.

A proposed secondary continuity placement commits the named qualified copy, two civilian factories, 150 Support Equipment, and 60 Political Power for 90 days. A viable receiving institute and a real transfer route are requirements. This placement has four cost types. The copy's existing paid care follows it where the relevant service can be delivered, and any uncovered future care is quoted as its own continuing service obligation.

The second site receives the person and the agreed records. A material convoy is separate from a knowledge transfer, and a copy that travels without the newest archive may arrive with an older medical scope. The former site retains its staff, copied knowledge, equipment, and patients. Neither site can claim that the other one's physical facilities exist locally.

Within one country, the established two primary research lanes remain the normal maximum. Distributed continuity improves survival and can keep one existing lane operating when the other site is threatened. It does not produce a third lane by placing a new Wu in an empty building. Any later expansion of that ceiling would require a separate explicit design decision.

Across countries, a qualified copy can negotiate an actual independent appointment after a paid transfer and a viable institutional settlement. This is the replication branch's proposed extension to the original one-person lifecycle. It does not widen the initial player-or-major arrival draw, replay the first demonstration, or reopen Event 089's fire-once selection. The new host must support ordinary civilian medicine and a functioning institute. The paid foundation creates one working lane, with a second available only through the existing funded expansion route.

A foreign appointment grants the medical institution only when its qualified lead and real services exist. It does not inherit the former host's Authority value, factories, treatment reserves, specimen stores, foreign contracts, or completed national investments. Each country records its own actual concessions and responsibilities. The event keeps one origin history and a bounded registry of its living copies and current institutions.

A host that later loses major status does not lose its doctor on that basis. A host that loses all viable medical sites needs evacuation, aid, or a different appointment. The distinction is material capacity, not a reroll of the original arrival pool.

## Several Wus in the same world

Several living copies are separate people. Each has a source reference, a birth history, a personal learning history, a current custodian or employer, and a clinical qualification state. The original remains separately identifiable by the event's established arrival identity, even when the fiction never proves his earlier age or origin.

A single engine character must not be recruited into several countries and presented as several bodies. Conversely, several legitimate copies must not be collapsed into one movable token that silently kills or teleports the others. The implementation needs distinct supported carriers with stable person identities and guarded role ownership. A visible office or portrait can change without changing the underlying person's identity.

The country presents one Wu Authority value for its institutional relationship. Internal disagreements appear through assignments, custody disputes, and reports. They do not add a permanent rivalry meter or a separate public Authority meter for every copy. Where several institutions exist, the visible value reflects actual conceded powers and cannot be lowered by moving an obligation onto a less visible copy.

### Cooperative continuity

Cooperating copies divide actual work. One may lead a hospital, another maintain the control archive, and another teach specialist classes. Each uses an existing assignment and its real capacity. Their cooperation makes the institution harder to disable with one absence and can preserve a complete medical service through a local crisis.

Cooperation also requires reconciliation of records. Copies who have worked in different countries may disagree about which treatment assumptions remain valid. A paid archive review can preserve both accounts, identify the case to which each applies, and teach the current staff. It does not grant every participating country every private treatment or specimen by default.

### Disputed identity and office

Two qualified Wus can both claim to remember receiving the same appointment. The government can recognise the actual current officeholder, establish a collegium with divided responsibilities, or negotiate separate employment. The result changes authority and work allocation. It does not determine that one person is biologically unreal.

The dispute should centre on specific rights and work. One copy may claim access to an institute founded after its archive date. Another may insist that a programme has already been prohibited. The host can use witnesses, contracts, and observed service to decide the institutional issue. It cannot repair a contradiction by erasing the older copy's birth or inventing memories for the younger one.

### Different professional commitments

A copy can choose a different research direction after its own experience. A public physician may refuse a classified programme that the original once supported. A military clinician may prioritise dependent soldiers over an elective continuity project. A copy living abroad may value its new host's hospitals more than an order to return.

These disagreements require an actual cause such as divergent service, a disputed contract, mistreatment, an exposed experiment, or a blocked clinical duty. High Chaos can increase the pressure surrounding them through the later evolutions, but it does not create a free copy or force every clone to revolt. Low-Authority public institutions retain workable cooperation routes.

### Hidden continuity

A concealed copy needs an actual prior order, an actual site, supplies, a living milestone, and a recorded but undisclosed custody history. A later revelation exposes that hidden history. It does not retroactively invent production because the story would benefit from another Wu.

Unauthorized production follows the institution's actual independent resources and the applicable autonomous-work permissions or evolved behaviour. Before such behaviour exists, high Authority alone cannot mint a hidden order outside all paid capacity. A secret site competes for real supplies and creates exposure through the work it performs.

Discovery can lead to recognition, independent assessment, asylum, a custody hearing, or separation of the institute. The discovered person still needs care. Its existence can make Wu's removal more difficult, but it does not automatically transfer government power or create a sovereign country. Those larger outcomes retain their later full-depth design.

## Capture, rescue, and transfer

Capturing a laboratory can place several different things under foreign control: nonliving material, unfinished production, unsettled living people, already settled residents, professional archives, and a particular Wu. Each has its own outcome. Control of the state is not proof that every person or record remained there.

An occupier first receives information about what it actually found. Unfinished material can be secured or abandoned without becoming free adult personnel. Living unsettled cohorts require immediate care and a decision about custody. Settled residents remain part of the real population and cannot be counted as a fresh replication reward. An archive can be copied without creating its associated person.

A captured qualified Wu remains the same person. Capture suspends duties that require freedom and access, but it does not record death. The old host can continue through another qualified copy, a standard-specific independent archive, or ordinary learned medicine. Rescue restores access to the rescued individual without dismissing a legitimate successor or undoing the successor's work.

If an original Wu returns after a copy has taken office, the government settles the current institutional arrangement. It can restore the original to the lead, retain the successor and offer the original another assignment, or form a collegium. Existing patients, contracts, Authority, and research do not restart. The displaced lead remains alive and retains its own knowledge unless a separate actual event changes that condition.

Transfer preserves the exact person, reference date, qualification, injuries, service history, and unsettled or settled accounting status. A change of nationality or role must remove the previous active appointment before activating the new one. A pending clinical or research assignment must be safely paused or completed first. An invalid recipient must leave the person and every unrelated obligation with the original host.

For living cohorts, evacuation carries the surviving count and actual remaining maturation or training. For deployed formations, exact ownership transfer remains a separate engine problem. The inspected Event 019 recreate-and-delete exception does not prove that Wu can preserve arbitrary live strength, equipment variants, experience, decorations, or orders. That exception is not inherited as blanket permission. Wu's formation transfer must have its own accepted, evidence-backed disposition before it can run.

Knowledge transfer is permanent where the receiving country actually learns a completed method. Ending a treaty can stop future access, supplies, or updates. It cannot remove an already learned neutral technology or rewrite who supplied it. A captured archive cannot grant the original source event's characters, countries, opening rewards, project history, or scenario launch.

## Closing a replication programme

Stopping new orders is the simplest closure. Work before the living milestone can be cancelled under its recorded material contract. Living cohorts continue into care and reception. Qualified people keep their personal existence and learned skills, and the host decides whether their services remain employed.

The proposed closure action chooses a receiving institution for each unsettled group, a custodian for surviving records, and a funded care period. The main task is to settle actual responsibilities. It must not become a Political Power button that deletes people, converts them to manpower, or removes every trace of a failed programme.

A civilian closure can distribute healthy graduates among viable receiving states through the population and migration owners. Military trainees can enter ordinary civilian reception before any military settlement or remain in a finite reserve while another employer is negotiated. Deployed soldiers use the native military demobilisation contract, with no additional replication population refund. Enhanced veterans keep the Part 6 medical settlement.

An independent medical foundation can retain tissue production, copied professional knowledge, and supervised specialist employment after Wu's personal office ends. This is a complete positive endpoint for a public route. The state has preserved useful medicine and created new people without granting the institute private command over them.

A classified closure can leave unresolved custody, missing archives, disputed staff testimony, or a hidden paid site. Those consequences require actual surviving records or actors. They do not appear automatically to punish every attempt at supervision. A successfully audited and completed closure remains a real result.

Wu can treat living legacy clone soldiers, study their care needs, or help an existing provider improve a documented service. Those soldiers already exist under their own owner. Treatment creates no new finite birth record, no fresh manpower entitlement, and no conversion of old recurring-income equipment into a cashable population reserve.

Legacy access, Kruger refinement, ordinary Mengele refinement, and the strict Aryan refinement keep their existing meanings. A Wu medical project must not select one merely to reach the equipment it wants. It must not remove a legitimate existing refinement, revoke a permanent external grant, or grant a restricted provider's country identity. A genuine Kruger-Wu replication collaboration can contribute a completed biomedical method, compatible equipment knowledge, a supervised facility assignment, or an authenticated non-person technical archive. Each contribution keeps its actual owner and provenance. Joint work can reduce duplicated preparation for the same demonstrated capability, but it cannot manufacture a native Kruger project stage, count a borrowed site twice, or award both a shared technology package and its already installed bonus again.

Existing neutral CBRN clone discovery remains an independent legacy consumer. The shared clone owner reconciles compatible learned capabilities without granting another copy of a completed Wu project or erasing an existing cohort. ## Disease, mortality, and difficult populations

Replicated people are living humans. They remain eligible for ordinary civilian treatment and population systems after settlement. A country does not become nonhuman because it employs a copied surgeon, commands a clone formation, or appoints a continuity copy. Shared country classification changes only if a later owner-defined transformation actually establishes a different kind of population or institution.

An infected living copy can receive the same compatible medical care as another living person. Biological treatment does not grant a replacement copy for someone who dies. Reanimation belongs to its actual owner and never restores the original finite entitlement. A dead clone's earlier first settlement and death record remain consumed history even if another event later uses its reanimated body.

A common clinical flaw is not automatically a contagious disease. Only a disease owner that genuinely recognises a biological condition can add the corresponding outbreak effects. A training failure, memory disagreement, missing archive, or disobedient person cannot be relabelled as infection so that a medical action can remove them.

Unsettled cohorts need food and shelter before they enter ordinary state population. Their receiving sites must expose that additional demand to the appropriate famine and reception owners without also inserting those people into a second population total. After civilian settlement, the temporary cohort demand ends and ordinary population demand takes over. After military settlement, the army's supply and medical services take responsibility. A demand transition cannot leave both providers charging the same people indefinitely or neither provider supporting them.

A receiving state damaged by plague can be an appropriate destination only when the actual housing, food, protection, and medical conditions support reception. The programme cannot erase plague deaths by adding newcomers, restore destroyed industry through their headcount, or declare a Rat Nation cured. Population restoration at national scale remains a later designed-life programme with its own reconstruction requirements.

Native biological operation ownership remains unchanged. A continuity copy can understand a documented medical or controlled-biology standard, but it does not acquire a second payload stockpile, attack queue, reservation, or refund path. Carrier remains a separately authorised native operation consumer. A change of institute lead cannot replay a settled attack or erase its evidence, contamination, Condemnation, or deaths.

## Four useful campaign paths

### A hospital network that outlives its founder

A public host develops R01, R02, R03, R04, R07, and R12. It creates a small number of consenting specialist copies, completes their reception, and places them in supplied hospitals. Independent teams hold the treatment standards and take over known care if Wu leaves.

The main choice is how much scarce teaching and production capacity should go to new specialists instead of direct treatment. An active epidemic can make immediate clinical supplies more valuable than a future surgeon. During peace, the same host can build a durable distributed service. The route ends with ordinary people and functioning hospitals, not an unavoidable private army.

### A finite wartime replacement programme

An industrial host develops R05 after basic replication. It commits factories to a cohort while using existing medical work to preserve current soldiers. The new class completes maturation and training, then enters native manpower once. The next class requires another full paid order.

This route works when the country can wait for people but has a long-term recruitment problem. It is a poor emergency purchase when its only nursery is about to fall. Its useful endpoint is a reliable finite intake that does not need permanent batch-specific unit management after settlement.

### A specialised cohort corps

A host with trained personnel and a stable support network develops R06 and R07, retains cohort identity, and commissions a small number of formations. The choice between pure-cohort replacements, mixed personnel, and optional enhancement changes the force the country can sustain.

A common flaw or captured depot can affect several formations at once. The player responds through actual rotation, medical recall, and supply. The corps can survive Wu's departure through independent service archives, but it cannot survive indefinitely without people, equipment, and care. Its later political importance depends on who actually controls those services and commands its officers.

### Several qualified Wus with separate obligations

A host develops R09 and R10 while the original is alive, then uses R11 to place a qualified copy at another supplied institute. The government gains continuity through a real second person. It also gains another participant in decisions about research, clinical duty, and the ownership of records.

The copies can cooperate for the rest of the campaign. They can also diverge because their experiences and employers differ. A later sovereignty route must build on those living people, institutions, and obligations. It cannot invent a network of copies only when the political ending needs one.

The institute keeps its existing compact decision-led presentation. The current phase normally offers three to five primary actions, with six as the ceiling. A selected cohort record exposes its next useful decision: finish care, assign training, settle into a receiving service, transfer custody, or respond to a demonstrated problem. Completed and incompatible actions do not remain as an accumulating list.

Keep the four-value ceiling for the combined current surface. The interface must distinguish a living original, a qualified copy, an unqualified candidate, a captured person, a missing person, and a recorded death. An empty host pointer must not make a dead person look available for appointment.

Only one to three active objectives should occupy the same phase. A medical recall and an urgent succession can appear together when both genuinely require action. Elective proposal clutter should recede until the immediate obligations are resolved. A public host favours clinical material, specialist continuity, and independent care. Military demand increases the value of R05 and R06 when equipment and long-term supply are adequate. ## What replication leaves for designed life

At the end of this progression, the institute can manufacture useful replacement material, educate new specialists, deliver finite military replacements, support recorded clone formations, and preserve Wu's exceptional medical work through separately qualified living people. Its reach depends on the sites, staff, records, and commitments that the country actually maintained.

The next research family can build on that foundation without treating a new person as a reusable production token. Designed organisms, biological filters, living reconnaissance, chimeric formations, broader population restoration, and joint biological machinery will need their own source material, purposes, control, supply, ecological limits, and owner connections. Those projects remain baseline possibilities, and their full design belongs in the next part.

The four evolutions remain separate future behaviour changes at 200, 400, 600, and 800 Chaos. They can affect the scale of competition, institutional independence, divided loyalties, and the political use of completed replication. They cannot create free cohorts, invent hidden copies without a prior history, substitute for a missing archive, or become the only route to qualified continuity. Replication leaves living people and accountable institutions for those later parts to develop.
