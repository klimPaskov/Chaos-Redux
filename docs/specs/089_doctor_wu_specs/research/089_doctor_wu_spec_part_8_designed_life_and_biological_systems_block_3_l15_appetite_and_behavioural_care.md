## L15: appetite and behavioural care

L15 treats an owner-identified biological appetite or aggression problem in a specific living group. It builds on rehabilitation and the relevant ordinary or nonhuman medical foundation. It is distinct from military obedience, political ideology, command authority, hunger caused by a lack of food, and the deliberate conduct of a criminal organisation.

A human formation can qualify when a previous enhancement or medical incident has produced a treatable condition. A compatible nonhuman group can qualify through its own clinical owner. An engineered zombie lineage can receive its existing maintenance or shutdown treatment when that owner authorises it, but the project does not turn dead troops into living patients. Each route retains its actual source and limitations.

The reference clinical course commits the affected group, one civilian factory, twenty Clinical Material Lots, and one hundred Support Equipment over 90 days. The bounded group and its scaling are supplied by the owner. A full formation is not assumed equivalent to twenty patients. The complete quote retains four cost types, and the patient commitment prevents the group from claiming full normal service during treatment.

At full supported coverage, the proposed course can reduce up to half of the owner's diagnosed medical aggression or appetite-pressure component. It does not halve the entire event's crisis meter. A condition with no exposed medical component has no such reduction. The remaining pressure can still require food, rest, command changes, legal accountability, or another event-owned response.

For Event 014, Field Hunger is a material supply problem, and organised predation includes command, political, and network causes. L15 cannot create Larder Stores, replace ordinary rations, erase Cult Cohesion, dismantle a network, or absolve perpetrators. The medical team can treat compatible harm in living patients and support a real rotation or rehabilitation. The Event 014 owner continues to decide whether local or global containment has actually been achieved.

A patient who regains judgment may refuse a previous duty. That refusal is not proof that treatment failed. The government can return the patient to an appropriate role, arrange civilian rehabilitation, or pursue an independently grounded legal process. It cannot receive a loyalty bonus for every treated person or use recovery as a covert programme of automatic political conversion.

The classified route can attempt to turn care into coercion. Its consequences must follow the concrete institutional choice: withheld discharge, disputed orders, staff refusal, exposure, or a patient group that leaves when custody breaks. No real coercive treatment procedure is described. The gameplay does not award a guaranteed obedient population from a medical button.

Continuing care is justified only by the actual condition. A recovered ordinary patient can leave the specialist programme and remain in normal medicine. Permanently altered veterans retain their earlier care needs. Ending an appetite course does not remove Part 6's permanent alteration or supply dependency. A separate clinical purpose may coexist, but it cannot bill the same care twice.

The report should identify the affected people, the improvement they experience, and any continuing limitation. Serious harm and atrocities receive a serious tone. Humour can address the institution's self-serving language, never the suffering of patients or victims. The project's useful endpoint is a safer living group with a viable future, not a magically loyal army.

## Deployment under changing conditions

### Healthy periods and early access

The institute remains useful when no national epidemic exists. It can qualify a fixed filter, train a handling team, conduct a contained agricultural case, prepare a service animal design, or build a receiving district around a real reconstruction need. These activities preserve their actual scale. They do not create a national disease, a rat country, a zombie outbreak, a clone scenario, or an event-history entry for a source that has not fired.

A source-specific design still needs its actual source. A healthy host cannot study a Rat King's unique biology merely because Black Plague exists in the catalogue. A compatible ordinary plague-vector case can support ordinary vector defence. The Rat King's later independent case needs its owner-defined evidence. This gives earlier research a useful role without making it an omniscient unlock for every future organism.

The player can select an eligible project directly through the existing proposal surface. Context changes proposal priority, not the existence of a lawful research path. A public government can approve a military exception. A classified government can publish a safe maintenance standard. Neither needs to raise Chaos or surrender a fixed amount of Authority to make the project technically possible.

### Regional service choices

An industrial port can support fixed filters and reception medicine but still depend on a contested sea route. An inland farming region can benefit from crop treatment and lack the staff to operate a complex living observation station. A mountain front can justify support animals despite their poor road speed. A ruined urban basin can need mobile cleanup and reconstruction before any incoming population is safe.

These differences come from current territory, facilities, supply, food, damage, and people. They are not national stereotypes or fixed bonuses assigned to a country's tag. A technologically advanced state can still fail an isolated deployment. A small public institute can succeed with a narrow local service and a genuine partner.

No single site should offer every service at maximum strength by default. A works site can change its active standard, but it must finish or withdraw existing obligations before committing the same staff and equipment elsewhere. The new work is not an excuse to multiply factories, clinical stock, or handler teams through a renamed facility.

### Knowledge, patents, and service agreements

A partner can receive a finished product, a qualified service, a maintenance licence, or the full reproducible design. Those are separate transfers. Part 4 owns the general commercial and humanitarian terms. This part adds the physical and clinical facts that a designed-life agreement must carry.

A product shipment transfers only the verified physical stock that arrived. A maintenance licence supplies the documented operating knowledge. A full design transfer permits future reproduction only when the receiving institution has the actual production and care prerequisites. None of these transactions transfers Wu, a clone, Kruger, trained staff, or a complete military package automatically.

A publisher can preserve its own learned standard after sharing it. A patent dispute can restrict future legal access without erasing knowledge already authentically learned. A government that withdraws a licence does not remotely kill foreign animals or delete a partner's filter. A real service dependency can interrupt future supplies, which produces a medical or maintenance problem through the existing local contract.

Emergency aid should remain usable without forcing a recipient into a faction or a new event-owned country. A coercive commercial demand can create political consequences through Part 4 and the later foreign part. The service cannot use the ordinary disease owner to award unrelated diplomatic concessions.

### Capture and conflicting custody

Capturing a site can yield equipment, intact living stock, damaged assemblies, patients, and incomplete records. Each category has its own consequence. Intact equipment does not prove learned science. A living patient does not become property. A missing animal is not a confirmed death. Records can be evidence of earlier conduct even when the machine that produced them no longer works.

The original owner loses only what actually changed custody. The receiving owner gains only the verified remaining stock or people. A transfer closes or moves the matching assignment before another deployment becomes eligible. The old site cannot continue claiming protection from units that now serve the captor.

A competing custody claim opens an account of the actual disputed stock and service. The institute may preserve care while the dispute is resolved. It cannot solve the dispute by duplicating the organisms or by assigning two simultaneous legal owners to the same finite person. Unknown provenance remains non-redeemable until the relevant owner can establish it.

Captured or abandoned human cohorts retain D03's first-settlement history. Captured animals retain their own production and loss history. Neither becomes legacy clone stock. A later recovery does not recreate resources already consumed by the enemy or reset an existing population loss.

### Failure, containment, and repair

The ordinary failure families are loss of service, care shortage, incompatibility, handling failure, and unauthorised use. They should arise from the actual design and current assignment. A filter failure primarily exposes its served endpoint. A missed reconnaissance return primarily loses information and risks its team. A feeding failure threatens the real animal group and the surrounding district. The entire world does not receive an identical disaster from every failed device.

The player receives an actionable account of what remains controllable: the supplied site, the missing team, the affected generation, or the route that has become unsafe. Useful responses include narrowing coverage, moving intact stock, restoring a route, funding a supported repair, withdrawing the assignment, and preserving living recipients. A response should not be a repeated political-power purchase that simply lowers a hidden danger number.

Rare escalation requires concrete state. An escaped organism needs an actual source and escape. A covert programme needs a real earlier order and custody. A hostile autonomous group needs an owner-supported transition and the people or organisms that actually compose it. High Chaos can increase the relevance or danger of these situations through the later evolutions, but it cannot invent the missing material history.

A successful containment response retires only the affected live service or escaped group. It preserves research, the original incident, recorded deaths, public evidence, and the correct responsibility. A repair cannot remove contamination from a different source. If Air Cleanliness changes because an accepted biological footprint genuinely shrinks, the shared owner records that actual change once.

### Departure and independent operation

A functioning public standard can survive Wu's departure through the staff and records that actually exist. A novel adaptation still needs a qualified Wu or another properly developed capability. A continuity copy must meet Part 7's qualification rules before supplying the unique medical role. Possessing the copy's body or portrait does not qualify it.

A departing Wu cannot automatically take every animal, machine, hospital, patient, and publicly owned record. Physical property, people, institutional knowledge, and private methods follow their actual ownership and transfer rules. Several qualified Wus can operate separate real institutions, but they do not multiply one national medical package or a single site's service capacity.

Closing a programme stops future orders first. Existing living commitments enter care, discharge, transfer, or a supported retirement. The closure can succeed while long-term human care continues through ordinary institutions. It cannot be declared complete by hiding the decision category and leaving unaccounted living groups behind.

## Player presentation and artificial intelligence

### Current choices, not a second management game

Designed life uses the existing institute project and service presentation. It does not add a permanent separate ecology board. The current surface normally shows Authority, the selected service's supported count or coverage, and its next meaningful deadline. A fourth visible value is justified only when it governs a separate immediate choice. Famine and migration retain their own three-value surfaces.

A research phase should show three to five primary actions: choose an eligible proposal, examine its supported use and prerequisites, commit work, change the research direction when permitted, or review the current project. An active service should show its relevant maintenance, narrowing, transfer, withdrawal, or repair choices. It must not expose all fifteen projects and every possible response as simultaneous buttons.

At most six primary actions and normally one to three active objectives share the current phase. The count of records in a permitted service list is not an excuse to give each record a row of duplicate controls. Obsolete actions disappear. A completed service that has no further decision remains history rather than a permanently highlighted crisis.

Costs use the real texticons and the exact payment quote. Inline cost presentation stays within the existing three-value rule, while the full tooltip shows all four cost types where relevant. The tooltip distinguishes consumed material, committed people or units, reserved care, and non-consumed requirements. It does not hide an extra fee behind a short status label.

Descriptions identify the actual institution, recipients, site, service, and current problem. They retain uncertainty about unproved source properties and hidden conduct. They do not reveal later sovereignty or rivalry outcomes. Working project names in this specification are organisational labels, not ready-to-paste game text.

The AI uses the same prerequisites, custody, affordability, food, service-capacity, and owner-result rules as the player. It first protects existing living commitments, then selects new work that answers a current need. A high Authority score cannot make an impossible deployment valid. Ideology can influence ownership and publication after practical validity has been established.

The first qualification, fixed filter, cleanup operation, predator return, agricultural recovery, nonhuman clinic, living observation, chimeric deployment, joint works, new district, and reception of new people each need a scene direction when they become an important report. These are proposed asset requirements, not produced images or accepted source candidates.

Report images should show the service being performed in the 1936–1945 setting with its explicitly intended fictional biological elements. Staff, vehicles, tools, clothing, and architecture remain period-fitting. News strips retain their separate monochrome format. A generic laboratory table or map is not a substitute for every scene. Final composition and source selection belong to the appropriate asset worker.

L01–L15 need distinct research identities at the actual project consumer size. Physical filter, cleanup, agricultural, camouflage, husbandry, and animal stocks need readable inventory identities that cannot be confused with human cohorts or attack payloads. Custom spendable values need proper texticons before appearing as costs. A new icon does not itself authorise another permanent national spirit.

For each model, the future worker must inspect the actual closest installed vanilla mesh and entity, measure the scale relationship, define the required custom nonhumanoid rig, and prove ground contact, load contact, turning, movement, attack where applicable, retreat, and death through genuine skeletal actions. The support attachment's actual model-display consumer must be established rather than assumed. Animations should communicate a real operating state, such as living filter activity, a service being withdrawn, or machinery entering recovery. All asset requirements remain proposals subject to the accepted consumer crosswalk. No final production prompt, image, model, sound, counter, or licence clearance is produced by this part. ## What designed life leaves for the biological contest

The institute can now operate useful living systems, care for compatible unusual patients, field a small number of purpose-built formations, and receive new civilian lives through a real reconstruction programme. Its strength depends on actual stock, staff, sites, knowledge, care, and agreements. Those same dependencies give other institutions meaningful choices about cooperation, independent maintenance, and refusal.

The completed research families do not predetermine the next political direction. A public medical network can retain independent knowledge and support advanced work. A private directorate can accumulate control over supplies and specialists. Existing patients, new citizens, military handlers, partner institutions, and independent actors retain their own existence and obligations whichever route develops.

The next part must develop the remaining modified-capability and strain-ownership design at the same level, using only fictional game properties and actual shared-owner boundaries. Detailed Kruger rivalry, foreign competition, the four evolutions, and sovereignty remain separate later parts. Nothing in this ordinary research progression adds an evolution, activates one for free, changes its threshold, or supplies activation Chaos.
