# Doctor Wu: modified capabilities and strain ownership
## Designs that retain their source and consequences

Wu can keep a treatment, a dangerous specimen, and a proposed alteration of that specimen in the same institute. Each has a different purpose. The treatment can be sent to patients. The specimen can support further study. The alteration can become a manufactured capability only after the government authorizes that work and the institute proves that it can recognize, support, and retire the resulting standard.

A country that has developed a cure therefore faces a continuing choice. It can destroy the dangerous material and preserve its medical findings, keep a supervised research collection, or finance a separate programme that changes the source's game behaviour. The last choice competes with hospital work for staff and facilities. It does not supply an arsenal on completion. Production, custody, permitted use, and the consequences of an actual operation remain separate commitments.

This family gives Wu a way to change compatible biological capabilities without making every source interchangeable. An ordinary disease retains its disease identity. Black Plague retains its own progression and Rat connections. A controlled zombie retains its lineage, handlers, and shutdown obligations. A human clone remains a person whose body and first settlement have already been counted. An engineered service animal remains an animal with an existing handler and food bill.

The labels S01–S12 and the profile names in this part are working design labels. All new quantities, durations, limits, and effect anchors are proposals. They describe fictional campaign rules and do not establish biological mechanisms, real production methods, or tested engine behaviour.

## The source remains recognizable

Every alteration starts from a particular, authorized source. The institute records the owning disease or unit family, the source's existing version, where the material came from, and which part of the source can actually change. Source possession, research knowledge, manufacturing authority, and permission to use a finished capability are independent facts.

A donated clinical case can qualify the institute to study an illness and prepare a countermeasure. It does not automatically authorize an offensive programme. A captured shipment proves that some physical material exists. It does not prove that the label is correct, that the institute knows its design, or that the captor can manufacture more. A foreign licence can supply methods while leaving the physical collection in another country. The government must obtain the appropriate access through the existing medical, production, and foreign-service routes.

A registered design has a stable source family, a fixed profile, a defined target class, an identified countermeasure relationship, and a production identity. Repeating the same qualification in a second institute establishes that institute's competence. It does not create a new strain merely to replay a discovery reward. Changing an allowed game property creates a different design identity. Renaming the old design does not.

The source family supplies the permitted changes. A missing compatibility entry closes that proposed alteration. It does not cause Wu to use the nearest disease or an ordinary infantry substitute. Compatible future diseases can join through their owners, including diseases that have never appeared naturally in the campaign. The original event remains available for its own later firing.

Source access must survive long enough to complete the work that needs it. A finished archive can preserve learned methods after material is destroyed. New manufacture still requires whatever valid physical source, facility, and production entitlement the owner demands. A past sample receipt cannot operate a laboratory whose collection has been lost.

### A design is not a command to existing stock

The design used to manufacture an item remains attached to that item. A new institute setting cannot update an old shipment, alter an operation already being prepared, or turn a captured ordinary stockpile into Wu's latest standard.

This rule also applies to defective material. A correctly identified failed generation keeps its actual defect. A correction can qualify future production and may open a paid recovery programme for the affected stock or people. The correction does not rewrite the old generation's history. Material that cannot be safely recovered can be withdrawn or destroyed through its owner.

The institute can retain any number of learned, distinct designs that the installed owner supports. Its active manufacturing capacity is deliberately smaller. The proposed Wu programme begins with one active modified standard and can support a second after a separate manufacturing annex is commissioned. Two is the country-wide Wu programme ceiling, including regional institutes and qualified continuity branches in that country.

An active standard is a production entitlement, not a numerical balance of weapons. The normal physical stockpile remains the quantity source. An inactive design can still have usable, correctly identified stock, current patients, or formations requiring care. Those obligations remain visible until they are resolved.

## Permission at four different moments

The public institute can study every compatible family through explicit permission and ordinary prerequisites. Public oversight changes the way it reaches a result. It does not require Wu Authority to rise before advanced work becomes possible.

The first permission concerns custody. The government can authorize clinical study, a defensive collection, or a separately recorded military collection. The collection has an actual location and responsible staff. A ministry cannot spend material still held by a foreign donor or move a collection by changing its nominal owner.

The second permission concerns a particular project. The approval names the source, the proposed profile, the intended recipients, and the institution responsible for a failure. A project authorized to improve protection does not acquire permission to produce an offensive stockpile when a promising result appears.

The third permission concerns manufacture. It identifies the qualified standard and its producing site. A government can retain the research and decline to commission a production line. A country can also stop further manufacture while continuing to care for recipients of older products.

The fourth permission concerns deployment. Ordinary medical delivery follows Parts 3 and 4. Formation treatment follows the relevant family in Parts 6–8. Offensive use follows the relevant native biological operation and its existing use policy. A scientific approval, a military appointment, or high Wu Authority cannot supply missing war, target, delivery, or policy authority.

An ordinary baseline refusal stops the requested work. Actual evasion belongs to separately enabled evolved behaviour or another explicitly established hostile action. The baseline does not secretly start an unapproved second project whenever a government says no.

### Changing a mandate

A government moving from classified work to public medicine can halt new offensive manufacture, secure existing stock, and publish medical findings. It cannot promise immediate disappearance of every dangerous item. The transition includes a stock disposition and a care settlement for every dependent formation.

A government moving in the opposite direction must identify the source and pay for the new project. It does not convert treatment reserves into offensive material by changing the institute's mandate. Medical production remains available while the government considers the proposal.

The institution's two research lanes remain the same lanes used elsewhere. A new modified-capability project can displace another planned project, but it cannot silently suspend an active cure or consume clinical supplies already assigned to patients. The player must see the competing commitment.

S03 qualifies a deliberately ordinary modified standard before the specialized branches. Its payoff is reliable manufacture of a source-identified result with a matched response. It does not grant a superior effect by itself. S04–S09 then offer different ways to change what that result does in the campaign.

S10 does not require an offensive S03 programme when it revises an already established controlled formation. S11 likewise remains reachable through the defensive branch without commissioning offensive manufacture. Both still require their own actual source, qualification, and physical production.

Familiar institutional work can benefit from the earlier independent-team and specialist preparation rules. The combined preparation saving is capped at the existing 20 percent and is applied once. Having a surgeon, a copied researcher, an independent archive, and a second Wu does not multiply the same saving. Research still occupies a real lane for its adjusted duration.

## S01: source and variant charter

The first project begins when Wu has enough knowledge to describe what he proposes to change. The source can be a cured outbreak, a documented captured capability, or compatible material acquired independently. The proposal should appear beside the current medical or research context, not as an empty menu of every disease in the mod.

The government chooses the purpose of the collection. A clinical collection supports recognition and treatment. A defensive research collection also supports protective alterations and counterstrain work. A military collection can support offensive modification after a separate approval. Those choices do not change the identity or ownership of material already elsewhere.

The project ends with a usable distinction between a reference source and a manufactured design. Staff can compare a proposed alteration with the reference and report whether its behaviour belongs to the expected family. The hospital receives the medical findings that it has actually learned. No country receives a weapon, a production licence, a new patient, or a source-event reward from this administrative and clinical qualification.

A source may be incomplete. An intercepted shipment can have reliable physical custody and unreliable provenance. A recovered clinical history can have reliable treatment findings and no surviving material. S01 accepts the known facts and marks the missing relationship. It does not turn partial evidence into a complete manufacturing authorization.

The player can pause the project to obtain a better source, finish a narrower defensive dossier, or terminate the dangerous collection while retaining the clinical archive. Finishing the narrower dossier satisfies later medical work only to the extent that the known material supports it. A deliberately incomplete offensive proposal remains unavailable.

The principal failure is misidentification discovered during comparison. The institute loses the remaining planned research time and must repeat the missing comparison under a new paid order. It does not automatically create a new infection. An actual incident requires the responsible owner to confirm exposure or injury. Staff testimony, faulty labels, and a donor's refusal to confirm the source can make the error politically important without inventing a casualty.

The completion report should follow the physicians and collection staff who must decide which material they can trust. A public institute treats an admitted gap as a reason to limit use. A classified directorate may press for a narrower disclosure. The choice concerns truthful access and custody, with no finished localisation prescribed here.

## S02: matched safeguards

A qualified alteration must have a practical answer to its own foreseeable failure. S02 identifies who can recognize the result, which existing protection remains useful, which treatment version matches it, and which institution can carry out a withdrawal or recall.

The project does not require a country to possess an unlimited reserve. It requires a funded response sized to the first planned use. A production programme can begin small. Expanding its intended recipients increases the required reserve and service capacity. The game should show the unsupported commitment before the player authorizes it.

The safeguard package distinguishes four destinations. Stored nonliving material needs secure custody and owner-defined disposal. Living patients need treatment and follow-up. Controlled formations need handlers, care, and a shutdown or stand-down plan appropriate to their actual bodies. A registered outbreak needs its disease owner's response route. These destinations cannot be settled by one universal destruction button.

The host can keep the recovery archive under Wu's personal control or establish an independent service team using the previously developed independence routes. Personal control makes the first review easier to staff, but leaves future support dependent on the current qualified practitioner and archive. Independent custody occupies the appropriate existing staff and site resources. It does not invent another research slot.

A safeguard can be incomplete without making the disease permanently incurable. The institute may know how to recognize a variant but still need time to manufacture a matched treatment. During that period the government can authorize further study, preserve its material, and finance treatment production. It cannot count an unfinished treatment as delivered protection.

The most important failure is a mismatch discovered after a shipment has left. The affected recipient's service remains active, but its claimed protection is reduced to the protection that actually matches. The institute must notify the recipient and supply the correct response. Hiding the mismatch retains the historical evidence and can create a separate responsibility record when the relevant owner supports that consequence.

A new outbreak during this work does not erase the original project. The emergency competes for staff and production. The player can give the emergency priority, keep the project running with separately available capacity, or seek a partner's medical help. The earlier cure system remains available throughout.

## S03: qualifying a production standard

S03 turns a learned design into a standard that a named works can reproduce. Qualification consumes staff time and industrial commitment. Completion opens the appropriate physical production route. It supplies no operational stock.

The first standard carries the source's unchanged underlying game behaviour and the new source, support, and manufacturing identity. It is useful because it can be tracked, matched to treatment, supplied to an authorized consumer, and recalled. More powerful profiles require their own subsequent research. This prevents a general manufacturing project from becoming an unpriced package of every desirable alteration.

Commissioning records which standard occupies the first active slot. The selected works must remain owned and controlled, have the required ordinary production access, and maintain its support commitments. Losing the works stops further authorized production from that site. It does not erase the design or change finished stock held elsewhere.

The host can decline manufacture after completing qualification. The archive remains available for defensive study and later commissioning. The country can also keep a standard active while setting its ordinary production allocation to zero. That holds the qualification in place but creates no material.

Qualification failures have two different outcomes. A reproducibility failure leaves an unqualified design and an intact clinical record. A correctly identified defective lot leaves real defective material under the stock owner. Neither outcome should silently supply a weaker ordinary weapon as compensation. Recovery can correct the standard and restart manufacture after the faulty material has an explicit disposition.

The second manufacturing annex is an optional institution action after S03. The proposal commits 2 civilian factories, 250 Support Equipment, 2,000 fuel, and 50 Political Power for 180 days at a second secure site. It creates a second qualified slot, with a country-wide maximum of two. It does not add a third research lane, another Wu, free equipment, or a second national defensive bonus.

The annex remains an industrial choice even in Calm World. A small country may prefer to retain one active standard and buy or receive medical services from elsewhere. A large country may accept the second commitment to keep a defensive standard and a military standard available together.

## S04: episode tempo

Tempo work changes the timing of an owner-defined episode. Its fictional profile uses the disease owner's existing stages and scheduling vocabulary. It never creates another global outbreak clock or allows the institute to decide when unrelated disease events fire.

The source must support both a recognizable opening and a later active period. A capability that exists only as a controlled formation can use its formation programme instead. It cannot be forced into an incubation model just because S04 has completed elsewhere.

The government selects one compatible working profile for the qualification. The profile becomes part of the manufactured identity. Another selection requires another qualified design.

### Compressed episode

The compressed profile brings the source's first active gameplay window forward by 25 percent, subject to the owner's minimum interval. The subsequent active window is also 25 percent shorter. These are changes to fictional campaign scheduling, not biological incubation claims.

The shorter timetable can make an operation matter during the present campaign instead of a later one. It also gives the host and affected country less time to place the correct response. The producer must maintain current emergency cover before manufacture is commissioned. The profile does not reduce evidence, remove civilian consequences, or make an operation more likely to reach its target.

A source whose owner cannot shorten its opening without breaking established stage order cannot accept the compressed profile. It remains eligible for another compatible S04 profile or another research branch. The invalid option must not be implemented by skipping an obligatory recognition event or starting directly at a late crisis phase.

A premature activation incident uses the responsible owner's existing accident path if an actual release is confirmed. Without that confirmation, the failure is a spoiled consignment and a stopped order. The institute cannot report an outbreak merely because its expected timetable was wrong.

### Extended episode

The extended profile lengthens the source's opening and active windows by 25 percent. An interrupted war can therefore leave the consequences of an earlier authorized action unresolved. Ending hostilities stops new offensive authorization, but it does not clear an existing episode.

This profile changes the burden of supervision and recovery. It requires longer response coverage and keeps the producing country responsible for the resulting material after the military no longer needs it. A public institute can study it for long-term countermeasure work without authorizing its offensive use.

The owner retains the normal chance of detection, natural decline, and successful treatment unless a separately qualified recognition profile changes the permitted part. An extended opening cannot promise invisibility. Hospitals may recognize a real case before the planned window ends.

The useful counterplay is earlier surveillance, a matching treatment, sustained medical access, and the owner's ordinary containment actions. A defender that completes the response need not wait for an arbitrary minimum suffering period. Recovery remains responsive to actual treatment.

### Contained tempo

The contained profile preserves the source's normal opening interval and halves the additional spread opportunity contributed by this modified episode. It also reduces this profile's active pressure contribution by 25 percent. It supports a host that accepts a narrower, less powerful capability and wants a more manageable aftermath.

The reduction applies only to the modified contribution. Existing infection, other attackers' episodes, Rat occupation, and unmodified stock remain unchanged. A state with several causes cannot be declared safe by subtracting a blanket percentage from its entire disease burden.

This profile is a substantive alternative to the two timing profiles. It gives up part of the capability's game effect in exchange for a smaller continuing response burden. It does not waive political consequences or make deliberate harmful use humanitarian.

### Spread behaviour within the source's contract

A compatible source may offer a dispersive tempo variant instead of one of the timing profiles. Its game effect adds one bounded spread opportunity to the owner's next eligible spread cycle, followed by a reduced active-pressure contribution of 25 percent for the remaining episode. It cannot invent a route, ignore an owner's geographical condition, or cascade repeatedly through newly affected states in the same cycle.

Only one S04 profile can belong to a standard. Compressed, extended, contained, and dispersive behaviour are alternatives. The player cannot select every timing advantage and omit the associated loss.

All exposure, including effects on the producer and its allies, remains owned by the source. Friendly status cannot make a state biologically absent. The same safeguards used against an enemy-origin episode apply to a returning episode from the host's own design.

## S05: burden and persistence

S05 separates immediate pressure from the duration of cleanup. The source must expose a bounded active burden and a meaningful recovery condition. The project offers an acute-load profile and a persistent-footprint profile. They cannot coexist in one standard.

The acute-load profile raises this design's initial abstract medical or operational pressure contribution by 50 percent. It does not receive an independent mortality multiplier. Any resulting population loss is calculated by the ordinary source owner against the actual living population and its protected floor.

The price is a shorter useful episode and a heavier production commitment. The modified active window is 25 percent shorter, and the new physical item has twice the reference production cost in industrial-capacity terms. Its required physical resource types remain those established by the approved equipment owner. The higher IC cost must not be implemented as an additional scripted payment when the production line completes an item.

The producer needs matched response cover for the acute profile. A cure that matches the reference source may retain some protection, but it does not automatically satisfy the full response requirement for a design whose pressure profile differs. The institute can finish S05 and keep it archived while it builds enough medical cover.

The persistent-footprint profile raises the cleanup workload attributable to the modified contribution by 50 percent and doubles the duration of that contribution's environmental service obligation. Its initial active pressure is 25 percent lower. It is a continuing responsibility, with real costs after a battle or war ends.

Persistence does not mean immunity to medicine. A correctly delivered matching response can still reduce or end the active biological component through the owning system. The profile cannot erase a cure result, repeatedly reinfect a cured state from no remaining source, or keep a cleanup mission alive after its real work has ended.

### A returning district

A state that has passed into recovery can retain a persistent contribution requiring further service. The local government may have enough doctors to treat surviving patients and still lack the transport, staff, or protected access needed to finish environmental work.

The player chooses between continuing service in place, transferring responsibility to a qualified local institution, or withdrawing personnel while acknowledging that the affected source remains active. A withdrawal removes only the departing service's contribution. It does not reset recovery, erase casualties, or award a second crisis resolution when the same staff return.

The producer's political responsibility follows the actual use record. A new occupier may inherit the task of protecting people in the state without inheriting the original attacker's responsibility. Capture of the district does not rewrite authorship.

A failed cleanup can leave the state in its current owner-defined phase or worsen a verified active component. It cannot automatically create Rat sovereignty, a zombie country, or a world-end branch. Those actors and outcomes still need their own event conditions.

### Limits on severity

An altered capability remains within the source's accepted severity contract. The profile can change a permitted component without reclassifying a source into another agent's reserved severity tier or inheriting another source's special terminal result.

Where an existing source reserves an effect for a particular native programme, S05 does not bypass that restriction. The proposed modification adapter must reject the unsupported result before production is offered. An attractive label is not evidence that a source can legally produce the selected game effect.

## S06: countermeasure divergence

A diverging design has a different treatment-match relationship from its reference. The project explores that difference through fictional clinical records and qualified comparisons. It provides no recipe for creating treatment resistance.

The institute must retain a reachable matching cure route before the design can enter manufacture. It can know that an earlier treatment is incomplete while still using that treatment's valid protection. It cannot declare every ordinary medical service ineffective or require an enemy to recruit Wu personally before any response becomes possible.

The proposed profile reduces the protection supplied by an unmatched, reference-only treatment to half of that treatment's otherwise applicable contribution. A completed matching treatment restores the normal contribution. General hospitals, population protection, source-independent medical services, and containment retain the effects their owners allow.

The producer faces the same mismatch. Its own reference-only medicine is not secretly exempt. A government that intends to retain this design must fund the matching medical standard and its deployment. The new medicine is manufactured through the earlier treatment system, with real delivery and recipient capacity.

Countermeasure divergence occupies the standard's principal alteration position. It cannot be paired with delayed recognition, dispersive tempo, or acute load in this part. This restriction prevents one design from combining the branch's strongest offensive pressures while leaving only a nominal supply penalty.

### Adaptation remains achievable

A country obtains a valid clinical case, recovered treatment record, or authorized matching dossier and begins the ordinary adaptation route from Part 3. The producer does not own an absolute veto over another country's cure. A completed independent match cannot be invalidated merely because the original institute keeps its archive classified.

The adaptation report names the affected design or its best-known source identity. It distinguishes a confirmed match from partial protection. Medical cooperation can share the learned match, while actual treatment delivery remains limited by manufacture, transport, and clinical coverage.

An incomplete foreign copy can be a distinct clinical version when its owner can prove that its behaviour differs. It must not create a new research hurdle every time the country renames the product. A copied design with unchanged behaviour uses the existing match.

The branch should produce a contest over records, samples, independent capacity, and access to medicine. It should not become an endless series of random mutations that invalidate the cure whenever the player makes progress. Once the actual version is understood, its matching treatment remains valid for that version.

### When the matching reserve fails

A compromised treatment reserve is a physical and clinical incident. The institute identifies which shipments and recipients used the affected reserve, withdraws unsupported claims, and opens a recovery plan. It does not revoke all treatment knowledge in the country.

The host can distribute the correct match from another site, accept a partner's equivalent medicine, or fund local replacement production. Existing patients continue to receive whatever effective care remains. Destruction of a bad batch is not permission to abandon living recipients who depend on treatment.

Concealment can create a separate evidence consequence, but it does not alter the disease owner's existing use record. Publication can expose a mistake and improve future response without canceling earlier responsibility.

## S07: bounded recipient compatibility

Recipient compatibility applies to a game-owned biological body class or an explicitly registered treatment cohort. It does not use nationality, ethnicity, ancestry, religion, sex, or political allegiance as a susceptibility selector.

A country is not one body class. A human country can host unusual formations, foreign volunteers, refugees, prisoners, civilian residents, and replicated people. A nonhuman army can retain human handlers. The profile must identify the actual supported recipient class and leave every unclassified group unresolved.

A class-bound profile reduces this design's contribution against an explicitly incompatible body class by 50 percent. It retains the normal contribution against its qualified class. It does not increase the effect against an unclassified population or infer that everyone living under a nonhuman controller belongs to the army's body type.

This creates a bounded design choice. A specialist capability can perform its defined role while becoming less useful outside that role. Mixed or unknown recipients undermine the promise of selectivity. The operation cannot advertise civilian immunity unless the source owner can prove that exact result.

The project never allows a hospital to select an independent actor for deletion. A medically treatable condition can be reduced in a Rat, zombie-related, alien, or other nonhuman body only through the appropriate owner. The country, army, identity, and political loyalties remain separate.

### Protected service for friendly formations

Friendly protection is a service relationship. A formation receives the matching medicine, qualified medical staff, and whatever existing protective equipment its source requires. A promise of future delivery does not count.

The proposed protected-service profile reduces the new modified contribution against a fully covered registered formation by 50 percent. Partial coverage reduces only the covered share. The same medicine can protect a qualifying foreign formation that has actually received it. Faction membership alone provides nothing.

The effect follows the recipient's real service identity and remaining coverage. It cannot be copied by renaming a division, changing its commander, or splitting a formation. Replacements require new treatment and cannot inherit coverage from soldiers who have left or died.

A protected formation can still suffer from another source, an incompatible version, ordinary combat, hunger, or an interrupted medical route. The profile should show its matched protection clearly without claiming universal immunity.

If the army refuses to release a formation for treatment, the government must decide whether to keep it in service with reduced protection or withdraw it for care. Wu's presence does not automatically resolve that command dispute. The decision connects to Part 6's existing treatment and rotation obligations.

### Captured and transferred recipients

A captured treated soldier remains one living person. A captor may obtain clinical information and retain the person's existing care needs. It does not obtain a new clone entitlement, a fresh military manpower grant, or automatic access to the producer's technology.

A transferred formation carries only the protection that its actual recipients still have. The receiving country must secure continuing service if the effect requires it. A stock transfer and a formation transfer are different owner transactions, with separate proof and separate limits.

Public medical practice can continue across political hostility when the relevant access agreement exists. That care does not make the patient obedient to the provider. A hostile government cannot be cured into loyalty.

## S08: operating conditions and recognition

A design can qualify for one particular operating context only when the source owner exposes that context as a current game fact. This includes a verified terrain or infrastructure condition, a named type of prepared site, or a body family with an existing environmental requirement.

The project uses one selected context per qualification. It does not assemble a design that receives every advantage in heat, cold, cities, ports, and damaged infrastructure. A setting that the current owner cannot observe remains an unavailable specialization.

The proposed context-bound profile preserves the reference contribution inside the qualified condition and reduces that contribution by 25 percent outside it. In return, its physical production cost is 25 percent lower than an otherwise equivalent general modified standard, with a floor at the reference item's own production cost. It is a specialized production choice, not a free improvement over the unmodified source.

A verified environmental-service variant can instead reduce the qualified formation's additional medical upkeep by 25 percent in its prepared context, rounded up to whole required supplies. Outside that context the normal upkeep remains. This option belongs to the formation or service owner and does not alter a disease's spread formula.

For either variant, missing context proof supplies no bonus. The system does not guess weather from latitude, a country's name, a map colour, or the season. Moving between states can change which current condition applies, while the item's design and history remain fixed.

### Recognition profile

A separate recognition qualification changes only the first unconfirmed presentation and response window of a compatible fictional episode. It cannot remove evidence, cancel an investigation, hide a confirmed diagnosis, or alter a stored responsibility record.

The proposed delayed-recognition profile allows one additional owner response interval before ordinary recognition, subject to the owner's maximum unconfirmed duration. Existing clinical surveillance, an exact matching dossier, or a confirmed case can end that interval earlier. Once recognition is established, the profile cannot re-hide the episode.

The tradeoff is institutional. The producer must maintain a matching observation service and fund a longer follow-up period for its own exposed personnel. An institute that cannot recognize its own design cannot commission this profile. The delay applies to the producer's mistakes as well as to a foreign episode.

The government can instead choose a traceable profile. It receives earlier source identification when the appropriate observers obtain a valid case, and the institute can open the correct response without another general identification project. The source's physical effect remains unchanged. This is useful for a public programme that values accountable use, prompt treatment, and reliable recall.

Traceable and delayed-recognition profiles are mutually exclusive. Changing between them changes future production only. A box of older delayed-recognition stock does not become traceable when the government publishes a policy statement.

### A wrong environment report

A faulty context report affects the claims made about the design. The institute can suspend use, obtain a valid observation, or continue under the conservative general result. It cannot choose a more favourable condition retrospectively after an operation resolves.

If the effect needs a fact that the native operation does not expose, the specialized operation remains blocked. The design can stay learned. A later verified owner extension can make it usable without awarding the research again.

The report should show the actual mismatch through service experience, failed equipment compatibility, or differing clinical observations. It should not substitute an invented weather report or a guaranteed explanation for uncertain evidence.

## S09: a formation-linked burden

The military application of this family changes the medical burden borne by a formation that the source owner can identify. It does not give the institute an unrestricted country-wide attack modifier. The source must already have a supported way to connect its incident to actual formations, and the proposed change must remain inside that connection.

The working profile is a recovery burden. An affected, identified formation takes 50 percent longer to complete its source-owned medical recovery assignment. Its ordinary weapons, manpower, nationality, commander, and orders remain its own. The source's existing combat effects continue without another Wu attack or defence penalty being applied on top. The result matters because a formation remains unavailable for its next funded tour, replacement assignment, or special treatment for longer.

The interval starts from an actual accepted medical case. Moving through a state, sharing a border, or being assigned to an army is not sufficient evidence that the formation received the condition. Where the source only models state-level disease, this particular profile remains unavailable until that owner supplies a formation connection. The institute can still research other compatible profiles.

The defender can answer with an appropriate medical assignment, rotation, replacement personnel, or a matching course. A funded matching course removes the extra recovery burden and leaves the source's normal recovery requirement. It does not heal unrelated combat damage or turn new replacements into veterans. A partial delivery supports only the recipients actually covered.

The producer pays for the operational specialization through a 25 percent lower contribution to the source's other active pressure. That reduction applies to the same modified episode. The profile cannot retain full general pressure and add a free formation-disruption effect. An owner that cannot separate those contributions cannot expose this profile yet.

### The army's choice

A receiving army can keep an affected formation together during treatment, rotate its surviving personnel through an existing medical service, or retire it from a specialist role. Keeping it together preserves organizational continuity but occupies a medical place for longer. Rotation releases the front-line assignment and requires replacements through the army's ordinary accounting. Retirement ends the specialist deployment without erasing the people or their care needs.

The institute cannot make those choices for a foreign army. A medical offer provides the treatment and its conditions. The receiving government chooses the assignment. A hostile government's refusal leaves the original condition active under its source owner, without granting Wu command of the formation.

The most important failure is a mismatch between the named formation and the actual recipients. A renamed template, a split unit, or a transferred commander cannot stand in for the original patients. The response must preserve the surviving recipient record or suspend the specialized benefit until the family owner can identify it again. It cannot treat the replacement formation and the original recipients as two fully paid groups.

### A bounded campaign role

This profile gives military medicine a visible reason to matter during an operation. It can delay a force's return to a prepared assignment, yet the defender retains a concrete recovery route. It never guarantees encirclement, surrender, or destruction. The final effect is gated by a real source-owned medical case and a formation identity that remains valid through recovery.

Report direction should emphasize the practical interruption to an identified unit's service and the command decision about care. The affected soldiers should remain people with a military history. Do not describe the army as a single biological target or imply that every unit in the country received the same condition.

## S10: revising control without replacing the recipient

S10 qualifies a change to an existing controlled organism or supported treatment relationship. It starts from the actual lineage and its current control archive. Command, Assault, Hunter, Carrier, and Guardian retain the roles, costs, and failures already defined in Part 6. A control revision does not create a sixth lineage.

The project can qualify an institutional handover profile. A formation that has completed the paid handover can continue using its existing control method when a recognized government service replaces Wu's personal service. This removes a single-person administrative dependency. It does not remove control supplies, handlers, continuing medicine, or the lineage's physical limitations.

A second, exclusive profile is a service-bound command arrangement. It can sustain the lineage's existing advanced tasks through a named provider's qualified service, while accepting a 25 percent increase in that lineage's control-supply requirement. The additional commitment is rounded up to whole control packs. It grants no general attack increase. Its payoff is continuity of the already qualified task during an authorized provider change, with the old and new provider jointly supervising the handover.

These profiles cannot be combined on the same recipients. One emphasizes independent institutional succession. The other retains a closer continuing service relationship. Neither profile silently changes political allegiance. A later dispute over command must use the actual command and loyalty rules of the organism's owner.

### The handover assignment

A handover uses one named formation, the existing archive, a qualified receiving team, and a prepared service location. The proposed assignment takes 90 days and commits one civilian factory, 150 Support Equipment, 1,000 fuel, and the exact lineage-specific control packs for that interval. Handler availability and the formation's presence are non-consumed requirements. No additional manpower payment is hidden in the assignment.

The control packs are an actual payment drawn from the existing care product. They are not a new control currency. Starting the assignment cannot spend packs already reserved for another tour or for emergency shutdown. The player sees the full commitment before accepting the order.

The formation cannot conduct its specialist operational assignment while undergoing the handover. Its recorded people, organisms, equipment, and prior damage remain unchanged. Completion changes the recognized service relationship once. A failed assignment leaves the old relationship in place when it remains viable, or moves the formation into the already defined restricted-care state when it does not.

The assignment provides no free unit replacement. If the engine cannot modify the intended relationship without recreating a formation and losing essential state, that particular implementation remains blocked. The owner must prove any proposed substitute and obtain the required design disposition. The specification does not authorize using a new full-strength unit as a convenient stand-in.

### Carrier and territorial custody

Carrier remains a controlled formation whose offensive action is separate from ordinary combat. A new control standard can improve the continuity of its authorized care relationship. It cannot turn movement into an infection action, remove the requirement for a native operation, or convert the formation's upkeep into an offensive stockpile.

Guardian remains tied to its prepared site. Institutional handover can move responsibility for that site's care and command between qualified services. It does not carry the site's defensive advantages to an unrelated front. Hunter and Assault retain their original limitations during isolation and transport. A control revision does not erase them.

Part 6's approved design proposal for limited lineage combinations remains the only existing combination list for those formations. S10 does not widen it. In particular, Carrier cannot enter an ordinary combat-lineage combination through a differently named control project.

### Human recipients

Human enhancement, clone medicine, and memory care use their own consent, identity, qualification, and continuing-care rules. S10 can support a documented change of medical provider or treatment standard for those people. It cannot turn human beings into an equipment-controlled extension of Wu.

An obedience proposal concerning human recipients must remain an explicitly coercive political and medical choice where the later event design permits it. It cannot be disguised as routine aftercare. The existing person's first settlement, service history, freedom to disagree, and legal status remain recorded. No treatment automatically grants ownership of that person to the institute.

The public route supports informed care and independent service. A classified government can propose a restrictive service agreement, but the agreement does not create a technical guarantee of loyalty. Refusal, withdrawal, testimony, and care obligations remain meaningful outcomes. Actual unauthorized coercion belongs to its separately established event route and evidence owner.

## S11: a defensive counterstrain

S11 produces a fictional defensive capability matched to a particular owner-defined condition. Its role is to suppress an incoming or active compatible contribution through an authorized medical service. It does not turn a defence project into permission for an offensive release.

The institute needs an identified source, matched safeguards, and a real receiving service. An enclosed living service also needs the relevant Part 8 designed-life qualification. A nonliving medical product uses the ordinary production and delivery route from Part 4. These alternatives keep the physical product honest and avoid treating every defensive concept as a new organism family.

The first working profile is an interception service. It halves the matched incoming contribution for the recipients or site actually covered, subject to the source owner's protection floor. It does not remove an established episode. The second is a suppression service. It reduces the matched active contribution by 40 percent while the paid service operates. It does not prevent a new unmatched contribution. One manufactured counterstrain standard selects one of these roles.

The percentages are fictional response anchors. They are not claims about actual biological competition or a method for creating a counteracting organism. The owner must accept the matched contribution and return the effect actually applied. Unknown, non-biological, or unsupported conditions receive no fabricated response.

### Relationship with existing medicine

Parts 3–5 still own cure development and ordinary treatment. Part 8's enclosed filters and cleanup colonies still own their established service roles. S11 adds exact matching and a distinct service choice. It does not award another copy of their whole benefit.

For the same incoming contribution, interception and an existing filter use the strongest applicable protection for that purpose. For the same active contribution, suppression and an existing cleanup service use the strongest applicable reduction. Different services can coexist when they serve different patients, different contributions, or genuinely different purposes. Their supplies and staff remain separately committed.

For example, a receiving district can operate a filter against new exposure while a hospital treats already affected residents. That does not entitle the district to multiply every protective percentage together and declare the entire state immune. The source owner performs the final combination, including ordinary protection and any applicable cap.

A defensive standard occupies one of the modified-production slots while its special material is being manufactured. Ordinary cure production, existing control medicine, and aftercare remain outside those slots. Closing a special standard does not terminate medical care for earlier recipients.

### Failure and withdrawal

A mismatched counterstrain can fail to provide its promised reduction. The ordinary disease continues under its own owner. The default mismatch does not create a second disease. An actual harmful incident requires a source-supported accident with its own proven material and affected recipients.

Living counterstrain services retain their physical custody and food requirements from Part 8. The institute cannot release them into the environment and treat that as maintenance-free protection. Loss of a service site closes its protective contribution and creates a recovery obligation for the surviving service stock. It does not prove that the entire stock died or escaped.

The player can continue ordinary treatment, bring the correct standard, withdraw the service, or preserve an isolated specimen for a new qualification. Public notification can help other recipients identify the same mismatch. Destroying the faulty material does not destroy the clinical findings or remove responsibility for previous harm.

### An independent defensive route

A supervised institute can complete S01, S02, and S11 without commissioning offensive manufacture. S11 includes the manufacturing qualification for its own defensive product, so this route does not require S03. It opens the same limited modified-production allowance for that product and grants no physical stock. Its payoff is a qualified defensive product and an exact receiving service. It can share the resulting medical design through Part 4's access agreements while retaining responsibility for the material it actually supplies.

An existing source owner's defensive conversion remains defensive. Black Plague's conversion state cannot be cleared to make the same project supply offensive material. A later offensive proposal must meet the owner's own separate authority and compatibility rules. It cannot interpret a defensive completion receipt as a general weapons entitlement.

## S12: independent standards and bounded combinations

S12 allows a qualified institution to maintain a learned modified standard after Wu leaves and to qualify a small set of useful combinations. These are separate results of the same institutional work. Independent maintenance does not require the government to authorize a combination.

The institute needs two qualified teams, the relevant retained design records, funded safeguards, and a working manufacturing or medical site. A team that has only copied a title cannot maintain the standard. A continuity copy of Wu contributes through its actual qualification and service assignment, without adding another country-wide production allowance.

Once independent maintenance is established, known standards remain usable by the trained institution under the existing production and care conditions. New universal adaptations and wholly new research still require Wu, a qualified continuity successor, or another explicitly developed successor route. S12 does not make every scientist another Wu.

### Combination rules

A combined standard has one principal profile and one supporting profile. It is a distinct qualified design with its own physical identity. The constituent designs remain known, and existing stock of either constituent is not converted by the research result.

The proposed combination list is deliberately short:

| Principal profile | Supporting profile | Playable purpose and retained cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contained tempo | Traceable recognition | A limited, identifiable standard with a smaller continuing burden and prompt matching response |
| Extended episode | Traceable recognition | A longer supervised obligation whose origin can be recognized without a new identification project |
| Context-bound service | Protected-service recipient cover | A prepared medical or formation service that loses its special economy outside its qualified setting |
| Formation-linked recovery burden | Traceable recognition | A military medical condition that retains a clear response route and pays its reduced general-pressure contribution |
| Institutional handover | Traceable recognition | A controlled-organism service that can pass between qualified institutions without concealing the standard in use |
| Defensive interception | Context-bound service | A matched defensive installation with a specific site requirement and continuing physical support |

No other combination is authorized by this part. In particular, countermeasure divergence, delayed recognition, dispersive tempo, and acute-load behaviour cannot be combined with one another. Persistent-footprint behaviour remains a single-profile standard. These restrictions prevent a designer from assembling every adverse property into one universally preferable item.

A combination takes the longer of its two constituent qualification durations and adds 60 days of verification. It uses S12's four research commitments for that qualification. It does not charge another simultaneous research lane for each constituent, because those constituent designs must already be learned.

The combined physical product costs 50 percent more IC than the more expensive constituent product. It retains both constituents' actual support obligations, with duplicate obligations for the same service replaced by the larger single requirement. Distinct services remain distinct. This avoids charging twice for one handler team while still requiring both a treatment supply and a genuinely separate prepared site.

No combined profile may change the source family's identity, native severity classification, geographical permission, actual recipient class, or political-use authority. Where the relevant owners cannot compose two effects safely, the combination remains unavailable. The institute retains each separately qualified constituent.

### A divided institute

If two qualified institutions disagree over a combination, each can maintain the designs it actually possesses. Neither can invalidate the other's retained knowledge by claiming authorship. The government chooses which standard occupies its limited producing capacity and which service receives the available supplies.

A split therefore creates a material and political problem. It does not produce extra research lanes, duplicate stock, or a new scientist. Later rivalry and sovereignty parts can use that dispute as a real conflict over facilities and authority, with the portfolio facts supplied here remaining intact.

## Manufacturing a known design

A qualified standard supplies an entitlement to manufacture a defined physical item. The production line still consumes its normal industrial allocation and the resource requirements accepted by the equipment owner. Completing research, passing an inspection, or appointing another director grants no stock.

The general modified-item production anchor is 150 percent of the comparable source item's IC cost. Acute-load items use 200 percent. A combined item uses the combination rule above. Context specialization can reduce the equivalent modified cost by 25 percent, with a floor at the source item's unchanged cost. These are fictional industrial balance anchors. They do not describe material recipes or real production yields.

The equipment owner retains its established resource types. This specification does not invent chemical inputs, laboratory reagents, quantities of organisms, or manufacturing procedures. A special source without an equipment-backed production route needs an owner-approved physical service route instead. It cannot be represented by an arbitrary national variable that produces deployable material on request.

### What occupies a production slot

A slot belongs to the country's Wu modified-material programme. It names one immutable standard and one qualified producing works. The country begins with one slot and can earn the second annex. A slot can be active, paused, retooling, or unavailable because its works cannot operate.

A paused slot retains its standard but produces nothing. Retooling reserves that slot for the change and cannot briefly open both the departing and arriving standards. An occupied slot does not become empty when the country changes its mandate, renames its institute, or appoints a continuity copy.

The slot limit covers modified physical products commissioned through this family. It does not cap ordinary cures, military care packs, the original control supplies, existing Part 8 service products, or another event's independent production programme. Those retain their own capacities and obligations. A newly modified version of one of those products can occupy a slot without replacing the original product's entitlement.

Aftercare for already treated people remains available when the special manufacturing slot closes. The institute can continue the necessary original or matching care product through its established medical owner. That continuation cannot manufacture new offensive stock or admit another formation to the closed modification programme. The distinction prevents a safety obligation from becoming a third unrestricted production line.

The two active standards are a limit on simultaneous manufacture, not on the number of supported historical generations. An army can still contain older formations, and the stockpile can still hold older consignments. Their care, identification, and retirement obligations continue. The player must account for that burden when deciding whether another standard is worth commissioning.

### Switching the works

A normal switch requires the incoming standard to be qualified, its source access to remain valid, and its matched safeguards to be funded. The proposed retooling action takes 60 days and commits one civilian factory, 150 Support Equipment, 1,000 fuel, and 35 Political Power. It uses no additional research lane because the research is already complete.

During the interval, the selected modified line cannot manufacture either standard. Other valid production remains unaffected. At completion, the slot points to the new standard once. Any old stock remains old stock, with its original permissions and response relationship.

The government can cancel a switch. Consumed materials and elapsed work are not refunded by the Wu action. The factory reservation ends. The former standard can resume only if its works, permissions, source access, and safety conditions are still valid. Otherwise the slot remains paused. Cancellation cannot grant the incoming entitlement or duplicate a reservation release.

An emergency suspension closes new manufacture immediately and requires no Political Power payment. Recovery may still require a funded repair or requalification. The player should never have to buy permission to stop an unsafe production line.

A damaged works can be repaired through the actual construction or facility owner. A national retooling report does not restore destroyed buildings. Staff and service assignments must be available again before the slot becomes operational.

### Production identity and old equipment

The existing native biological operations reserve named equipment archetypes. Adding a modified item beneath an old archetype can therefore let an old operation consume it without knowing its profile. That is an identity failure, even when the stockpile total decreases by the expected amount.

The proposed extension requires a profile-distinguishable physical equipment identity and a matching native consumer. Unmodified operations must not draw modified stock as a silent substitute. Modified operations must not accept ordinary stock merely because both products belong to the same broad source family.

This is an owner-interface requirement, not a claim that a generic variant API already exists. The installed equipment and native reservation rules must prove the exact distinction. A branch that cannot make that distinction remains blocked for deployment. It can retain its research and medical analysis without receiving a fabricated operational fallback.

Stock behaviour must be defined by installed, stable profile identities. The institute does not invent an unlimited set of new engine equipment definitions during a campaign. Future content adds a declared owner profile and its physical identity through the owner's normal content update. Reusing a profile identity for different behaviour while older stock or operations survive is forbidden.

Campaign records may distinguish two institutions that independently learned the same installed profile. That provenance does not require two mechanically different equipment types. Conversely, two genuinely different profiles cannot share an indistinguishable physical balance and rely on whichever institute setting happens to be current at use.

### A standard change with material already committed

Consider a fictional source with 120 physical items of standard A. A native operation reserves 40 of them, leaving 80 in the available stockpile. The institute then retools its line to standard B and produces 30 B items.

The outstanding operation remains an A operation. It does not gain B's properties. The available balances are 80 A and 30 B. The 40 reserved A items are already inside the native operation's own accounting and cannot be spent again through a Wu decision.

If that completed operation fails, the inspected native outcome contract still accounts for its collected reservation. Wu adds no refund and no replacement shipment. A separate native cancellation follows the engine's proven cancellation rules, which require their own evidence. The specification does not infer those rules from the failure outcome.

If the new line is closed before any B item is produced, the country has learned B without owning B stock. The archive cannot satisfy an equipment requirement. This distinction should remain visible in the production and operation views.

## Custody, capture, and sharing

Custody has a physical meaning only where the owning system can establish it. An institute site can contain a specific registered collection or an assigned service. A national equipment balance does not prove that every item is stored in that site's basement.

When a site falls, its proven collection, staff, and assigned services receive the appropriate capture, evacuation, or destruction outcome. National equipment changes only when the native or shared stock owner reports an actual transfer or loss. Wu cannot grant the occupier an invented share of the national arsenal based on the site's importance.

The same rule protects the former holder. It cannot claim that stock was evacuated solely because Wu escaped. A character transfer, a copied archive, and a physical shipment are distinct transactions. Each needs its own existing owner evidence.

### Captured stock with incomplete knowledge

Captured items retain their physical identity when the owner can establish it. The captor does not automatically receive the matching design, production entitlement, or treatment. An identified sealed consignment can be held for examination, returned under a valid agreement, or destroyed through the stock owner.

Unidentified or contradictory material cannot satisfy a modified operation's readiness requirement. The conservative result is restricted custody, not a guessed source profile. The institute can use the source-identification project when a valid clinical or owner-supported examination is available. Successful identification reveals the existing identity. It does not rewrite the material into the captor's preferred standard.

A captured manufacturing works also requires requalification of its staff, source access, and support. Its physical survival may shorten reconstruction through the actual facility owner. It does not grant free stock or revive a destroyed archive.

### Sharing the design

A design-sharing agreement transfers retained knowledge and an explicit description of the recipient's rights. It does not transfer a quantity of material. Public medical findings can be shared through the established cure and access routes, with offensive manufacturing permission kept separate.

A recipient can possess a complete design and still lack a compatible production works or source. It may request treatment from another country, build the required institution, or keep the knowledge for future use. The donor cannot revoke knowledge already copied simply by ending a licence.

Licence termination can close a continuing service obligation or future authorized production where the agreement and owner support that result. Existing patients remain entitled to the recorded care settlement. Existing dangerous stock retains its identity and must receive a lawful disposition. The political dispute belongs to the later foreign-competition part.

### Sharing physical stock

The inspected ordinary biological equipment definitions prohibit ordinary licensing and lend-lease. A Wu agreement therefore cannot assume that those native channels already carry modified physical stock.

A future physical-transfer route requires an explicit stock-owner extension. It must identify the exact equipment, source holder, destination holder, amount, reservation state, and accepted transfer result. No quantity can appear at the recipient unless that same quantity was actually removed from the donor or supplied by another proven native source.

The transfer extension must keep restricted, reserved, and unidentified stock out of eligible cargo. It must preserve the profile after capture and transfer. A missing native transfer result leaves the transfer unresolved without crediting the recipient. The proposal does not add a Wu stock balance that promises eventual delivery while leaving the original items spendable.

The recipient receives no manufacture or deployment authority merely from possession. A foreign military can capture material it is unable or unwilling to use. The matching countermeasure can be supplied separately as medical aid, and that aid does not erase the capture or the material's earlier use history.

## One operation, one physical reservation

A modified offensive capability must enter a compatible native operation. The operation itself names or otherwise proves the fixed profile that matches its physical reservation. The final choice cannot be made by reading a country-wide current-strain setting when the operation resolves.

The native owner retains the target, route, preparation clock, unit requirements, policy, stock collection, cancellation, result, and history. Wu supplies a qualified profile and the medical relationship needed to understand its consequences. The institute does not add a second launch button, a separate staging readiness value, or an intermediate arsenal currency.

Current Event 016 native biological outcomes do not debit or refund equipment a second time. The inspected strategic-raid resolver also accounts for the full collected reservation on completed outcomes. Earlier strategic-raid guide language about unused-payload refunds and a selected staging complex is not a basis for extending the current runtime.

This part preserves the current distinction between the actual native target and an internal handling location used for a source-supported accident. Neither is an inferred launch airbase. It introduces no weather estimate or ordinary-air-mission infection hook.

### Binding the operation to its profile

The proposed native extension needs an immutable relationship among the operation identity, source family, installed profile, reserved equipment identity, actor, and selected target. It must remain valid when another operation is created, a standard is changed, an institute closes, or a different Wu assumes office.

A fixed native raid type per supported profile is one possible implementation shape. It remains a proposal until its equipment selection and callback identity are verified. A shared adapter is also acceptable only if the native instance can preserve the exact profile throughout preparation and resolution. A mutable country variable is not an acceptable substitute.

An operation can lose current permission while preparing. The native cancellation or invalid-context path then resolves that operation. Wu cannot invent a release to justify already collected cargo, send the material to a different target, or refund it through a second accounting path.

The same rule applies to a failed consequence dispatch. A paid native result that cannot establish its target or profile records the owner's unresolved or failed transaction. It does not fabricate infection, deaths, evidence, or a substitute country. This is distinct from an actual failed delivery whose own owner legitimately records an investigative lead.

### Existing source-specific differences

Ordinary biological episodes, weaponized zombie actors, Black Plague exposure, and a controlled Carrier formation are different source contracts. The institute cannot send them all through one ordinary-disease callback.

A zombie operation that creates an actor must preserve the zombie owner's creator profile and actor initialization. The existing single resolved creator profile does not establish support for an arbitrary archive of simultaneous Wu variants. A profile-aware owner extension is required before an older consignment can safely be used after the creator's current standard changes.

Black Plague's established operation can use physical stock shared with an ordinary plague route while retaining its own project and consequence history. Wu must preserve that accepted distinction. It cannot infer Black Plague identity from the physical stock token alone or apply the same extra exposure twice through two wrappers.

Carrier's standing supply remains formation upkeep. An offensive operation needs its own actual native material and permission. The same control packs cannot count as continued care, offensive cargo, and emergency shutdown stock at once.

### Defenders and independent actors

A modified result remains answerable by the source's ordinary medical, containment, military, and political responses. Exact matching can improve the response. General care still matters when a match is not yet known.

A cure cannot delete an existing Rat country, zombie polity, clone state, or other independent actor. A control revision cannot transfer a country's allegiance through a medical flag. The source owner decides what changed biological support means for recruitment, spread, or ongoing service. Defeat, negotiation, succession, and territorial transfer remain their own events.

Recorded deaths remain recorded. A source's reduction in active disease does not create negative deaths or restore population. A separately produced person is counted through the finite first-settlement route, and a treated existing person is not counted as a newly produced person.

## Responses that remain attached to the case

A response belongs to a named case, episode, recipient group, or service assignment. It must identify the version it can treat and the actual delivery that supports it. Changing the institute's preferred response cannot update medicine already delivered abroad.

The ordinary medical owner already distinguishes pending orders and completed services by state, provider, and episode. A modified-capability extension must preserve that specificity. A late callback for an older episode cannot clear a newer hospital reservation or grant a fresh treatment benefit to unrelated patients.

### More than one contribution in a state

A state can receive an unmodified source, a modified source, and an unrelated biological condition at the same time. The owner must distinguish the contributions that need different treatment and responsibility. It may present one readable summary to the player while keeping the relevant constituent records internally.

A matching cure reduces only its accepted contribution. Shared hospitals and broad protection are evaluated once by the responsible owner. The same living patient cannot be counted as a separate person in every constituent record, and the total applied population loss cannot exceed the available living population after the protected floor.

The proposed constituent extension belongs inside the disease owner. It must preserve each material's source and contribution without creating a second Wu disease simulator. Existing single-agent episode records are not evidence that this finer distinction is already supported.

When contributions cannot be separated reliably, a specific modified result must remain unavailable until the owner contract is extended. Treating the entire state as the newest strain would erase older responsibility and misapply treatment. Choosing the strongest variant for every purpose would make mixed episodes stronger than their actual material supports.

### Reachable adaptation

A newly recognized mismatch can start an adaptation project using an actual case, retained design, shared finding, or captured record. Wu is especially capable of completing that work. Other qualified medical institutions must still have a reachable source-approved response route.

The creator's absence cannot make its own material permanently untreatable. A classified archive can delay access to exact knowledge, while clinical observation and the source owner's ordinary countermeasure progression remain available. Permanent immunity to treatment is not a valid profile.

Adaptation updates the newly produced treatment and the services that actually receive it. Existing supplies retain their version. The receiving country can replace outdated stock, continue a still-useful older course, or divide scarce matching treatment among the highest-priority cases.

The gameplay question is who receives an adequate service now. It should not become a repetitive requirement to click through the same identification project for every factory batch of an unchanged design. A new qualification is justified by a meaningful difference, a damaged record, or a real service mismatch.

## Failure creates a specific recovery problem

Failures should follow the work that was actually authorized and the material that actually exists. Research uncertainty, physical defects, a broken service relationship, and an uncontrolled source episode need different responses. A generic accident roll that always starts a national outbreak would erase those differences.

| Failure | What the government can establish | Immediate consequence | Recovery direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wrong source identity | The case or consignment does not match its claimed source | Suspend the affected qualification and restrict unidentified stock | Obtain a valid identity, preserve useful clinical findings, or destroy the proven material |
| Failed standard qualification | A producing works cannot reproduce the qualified result | No new production entitlement | Repair the works or repeat the failed verification with actual resources |
| Defective manufactured stock | The stock owner identifies a fixed defective profile | That profile becomes unavailable for new operations | Withdraw, recover through an approved conversion, or destroy only the affected stock |
| Mismatched medical cover | Delivered care does not match the actual recipients or version | Remove the unsupported special protection | Continue general medicine and deliver a matching service |
| Lost works | The producing site's ownership or usable facility is lost | Stop that site's modified production | Evacuate proven material, negotiate custody, or reconstruct another works |
| Broken control handover | The receiving team cannot maintain the existing lineage | Keep the old valid service or use restricted care | Restore a qualified team, return to the former arrangement, or retire the assignment |
| Unaccounted consignment | Physical identity or reservation cannot be proved | Do not authorize its use | Reconcile with the actual stock owner without creating a replacement balance |
| Confirmed harmful incident | The source owner establishes an actual affected case | Start that owner's response and consequence path | Containment, treatment, evacuation, and evidence preservation through the proper owners |
| Conflicting copies of the design | Two archives describe incompatible profiles under one label | Reject the ambiguous production identity | Preserve both records and qualify separate fixed identities where supported |
| Publicly exposed unauthorized work | Existing evidence proves a breach of permission | Suspend the offending project and open the appropriate oversight response | Investigation, institutional reform, care settlement, or later political confrontation |

### A funded recall

A recall first stops new authorized use of the exact affected profile. The stop itself is immediate and free. The later work identifies available stock, pending native operations, issued medical products, and assigned formations without merging their owners.

The proposed domestic recall assignment takes 120 days and commits two civilian factories, 200 Support Equipment, 1,500 fuel, and 40 Political Power. It requires the actual producing or receiving sites to remain accessible. The goal is to account for the affected consignments and place every known recipient under an appropriate service or withdrawal plan.

Success means that every in-scope item or recipient has a recorded disposition. It does not require the government to destroy useful research. The country can retain a sealed collection, replace faulty medicine, keep an unaffected consignment, or retire a formation through its existing owner.

Partial success preserves the recovered material and protected recipients while leaving the unresolved remainder under restriction. The government cannot claim a complete recall because it recovered most of the expected stock. A foreign-held consignment can remain unresolved without blocking the domestic work already completed, provided the report clearly separates those scopes.

Failure leaves the restriction in place and creates a renewed recovery decision based on what remains. It does not regenerate the original full stock for another attempt. A missed deadline can damage public trust or institutional authority when the case supports that consequence, but it does not automatically kill every person associated with the programme.

### Destruction and material conversion

Destruction is a stock-owner action against an exact amount of identified, unreserved physical material. The proposed safety work consumes that material, Support Equipment, fuel, and at most 25 Command Power. These are four payment types. Its resource amount scales with the actual material accepted by the owner, and no charge is collected for material that cannot be identified or removed.

A destruction order cannot consume native-reserved cargo. The native operation must first reach its own cancellation or completion outcome. The resulting available stock, if any, can then enter a separate safety order. Wu never treats the same reserved item as destroyed at home and used abroad.

Conversion is permitted only when the existing physical owner provides an exact input and output contract. A design correction alone does not establish that old stock can be recovered. The converter must debit the correct input once and return only the accepted output once, with any loss kept as material loss. It cannot produce a living person, a free ordinary weapon, or refunded research costs.

Where no conversion contract exists, the options are continued restricted custody, owner-supported return, or destruction. A promised future conversion is not a quantity credit. The retained design remains available for new manufacture after proper qualification.

### People during a recall

Patients, handlers, enhanced veterans, specialists, and continuity copies cannot be destroyed or refunded as defective equipment. A harmful treatment creates a medical duty to the people who received it. Their existing nationality, identity, population settlement, service record, and evidence remain intact.

A recalled formation can lose permission for a specialist task while retaining its people and ordinary military obligations. It needs the care and retirement routes already developed in Parts 6 and 7. Removing an idea or closing the institute cannot settle that obligation by itself.

The same principle applies to pre-settlement living cohorts. They still occupy real custody and food-demand records even before their first civilian or military settlement. A recall cannot invent a population origin, erase their food requirement, or admit them into a district whose food-safety proof is missing.

### Responsibility and disclosure

The government can acknowledge a defect, suppress a report, or contest an uncertain allegation through the later oversight and foreign-response systems. Those choices affect what becomes public and who controls the institution. They cannot change an actual release into a harmless test or erase evidence already held by another country.

The source owner records actual deaths, outbreak change, and contamination. The evidence owner records attribution and the consequences of concealment. Wu can record a research failure or a breach of permission without writing another copy of those losses.

Routine manufacture, standard changes, recalls, and destruction do not repeatedly award Chaos reductions. A genuine reversal of an active crisis uses the existing one-time or outcome-specific relief rule. Creating a dangerous new capability can use the eventual event-wide milestone policy, but this part does not add a reward for every factory batch or relabelled design.

## The proposed owner agreement

Future compatibility should be added by the disease, formation, service, or equipment owner that understands the source. The institute needs a small set of explicit answers. It does not need access to every private variable in the source system.

| Owner answer | Information supplied | What remains with that owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source and modification eligibility | Stable family, installed version, supported fictional profiles, valid source evidence, compatible recipient classes | Source-event firing, unrelated research, hidden story state, and source lifecycle |
| Design qualification | Fixed profile identity, countermeasure relationship, qualification result, conflicts and prerequisites | Definition of the source's actual behaviour and permitted combinations |
| Physical production | Exact equipment or service identity, producing-site requirements, quantity owner, normal production route | Material creation, stock, transfers, consumption, and any native industrial cost |
| Operation binding | Native operation identity, actor, target, fixed profile, and actual reserved material | Preparation, policy, selection, payment, cancellation, result, and operation history |

| Response and retirement | Matching version, delivered care, surviving obligation, available safety or conversion route | Medical reservations, physical disposition, cleanup, and continuing evidence |

These are proposed interfaces. Their names in this table are design roles, not callable helper names. The inspected current input contracts do not establish a general arbitrary-strain modification API. Each required extension needs its own explicit acceptance and implementation evidence.

### Version and ownership rules

An owner response must distinguish a valid zero contribution from an absent or malformed answer. Missing facts close the dependent action. A rejected source cannot be replaced by another source selected at random.

A qualified design records the owner contract version under which it was accepted. A compatible owner update can continue to recognize that design. A materially changed contract must migrate or retire its use explicitly while preserving older stock, recipients, and historical consequences. It cannot reinterpret old records merely because the current schema uses the same numeric field.

Concurrent operations need independent identities. A later project or operation cannot overwrite the inputs of an earlier one. Repeated reconciliation can repair the current presentation and availability but cannot repeat a production reward, a source-event firing, a population settlement, or a death transaction.

The receiving owner returns the result it actually accepted. The institute uses that returned result to update its own project or service status. It cannot assume full success because a callback was invoked.

### Scope of the source families

Ordinary disease owners can expose compatible fictional episode profiles while retaining their existing baseline identities and severity rules. Their current fixed agent slots are not free positions for a new unrelated disease list. A profile extension must preserve their established callers.

Black Plague can expose its supported medical and modified-capability results through its own programme. Its defensive conversion, distinct project history, Rat actors, and accepted compound consequence path remain Event 020-owned. No source alias converts ordinary plague knowledge into those histories.

The zombie owner retains its distinction between living infected patients, reanimated forces, controlled lineages, and independent outbreak countries. A modified cure can treat an eligible living case. A control project can maintain an eligible force. Neither operation recreates dead people.

The shared clone owner retains finite first-settlement accounting for the proposed Wu extension and its separately established legacy stock economy. A new control or medicine profile cannot turn a finite person into recurring manpower-producing equipment. Holding a legacy clone item does not prove that a named finite person was produced.

Designed-life services use their existing physical units, food requirements, handlers, and service sites. The current chapter can qualify a matching response or a narrow control change. It cannot grant another colony or animal through a research receipt.

Alien and nonhuman medicine requires an actual biological component exposed by the owning family. Temporal medicine requires a condition its owner makes medically treatable. Wu receives no machine, temporal, or alien technology merely because a source belongs to a broader Kruger project.

A future source joins by supplying its own compatibility and physical-consumer contract. That keeps discovery additive without turning Event 089 into a manually maintained replacement list for the rest of the mod.

## Three campaigns through the branch

### A supervised institute during peace

A public institute has treated a real disease and preserved a lawful clinical collection. It completes source identification and matched safeguards, then chooses a defensive interception product. It does not commission an offensive programme.

The host places its limited production behind a receiving hospital district whose food, shelter, staffing, and route requirements are already proven. Its medical service reduces only the matched incoming contribution. The district still maintains its ordinary hospitals and receives no general immunity.

A foreign partner requests the findings. Sharing the design helps that partner prepare its own service without transferring a physical shipment or pretending that the partner has built the necessary works. The host can later add an independent team so that known production continues after Wu departs.

This is a complete baseline use of the modified-capability branch in Calm World. Its outcome is a durable medical institution with an exact product and a manageable responsibility. It does not require an evolution or an increase in Wu Authority.

### A military programme changes standards

A military host has one qualified standard and a force already depending on its medical support. A second project promises a different operational role, but the producing works cannot run both standards simultaneously.

The government can build the second annex, retool the existing works, or retain the present standard. Retooling gives up current production for the switch interval. Building the annex occupies real industry and a second secure site. Keeping the current standard avoids those costs but leaves the new design in the archive.

The old force keeps its original treatment and service relationship. Its next tour cannot consume the newly produced material until the relevant owner accepts a paid conversion or treatment assignment. An operation already being prepared keeps its original profile even if the government prefers the new design.

When peace arrives, the native use policy and target conditions close further offensive operations. The government still owes care and a disposition for the existing stock. The resulting political question concerns how much of the institution to retain, not whether the war's end can erase its material consequences.

### A captured archive and two incompatible consignments

An occupier captures a works containing a verified archive and a separately identified collection. Its national stockpile also receives material through an actual native seizure. The archive describes two standards, but the captured material does not have a proven split between them.

The occupier can preserve the archive, restrict the unclassified material, and fund identification. It cannot assign its preferred profile to the whole captured quantity. A foreign physician can provide a matching medical dossier without granting production rights or identifying an unrelated shipment.

The first successful identification can make one consignment usable for an authorized purpose. The other remains restricted. A failed identification creates no replacement stock. The captor's patient and stock records stay separate, and the original producer remains associated with any earlier proven use.

A later diplomatic or Kruger dispute can focus on who owns the works, who can publish the findings, and who must supply the ongoing care. The material facts do not change to suit the winning claimant.

## AI choices under named conditions

AI hosts use the same qualification, capacity, physical-stock, policy, and recipient checks as players. A positive preference never creates a missing source, a free shipment, a valid target, or another production slot.

Medical emergencies take precedence over optional modified-capability research when they compete for the same lane or staff. A country can continue an already funded independent programme if its resources do not conflict with the emergency. The AI should not automatically cancel every long project whenever a small clinical case appears.

The main preference order comes from actual need, matched care, sustainable production, and the risk of leaving existing recipients unsupported. Ideology changes preferences after those facts are valid. It cannot make an unavailable action selectable.

### Choosing among valid alternatives

When several valid projects compete, the AI compares the actual case served, expected service continuity, use of scarce lanes, production interruption, continuing care, diplomatic exposure, and the availability of a lower-burden response. It should avoid researching a new profile solely because it has not yet collected every project receipt.

Standard switching needs hysteresis through its real retooling cost and minimum useful production plan. A minor change in the front should not cause repeated cancellation and retooling. The AI can revise its plan after a lost works, a meaningful new disease version, a completed medical partner agreement, or the end of the relevant war.

The later probability review must compare these cases on the actual candidate pool and source version. A normalized list of project preferences is not an exact native decision probability. Required native inspection and comparison remain separate from the symbolic conservation checks supplied with this design.

## Presentation and material identity

The ordinary Wu category remains the entry point. The current phase should show the selected project or standard, its meaningful constraint, and the next useful action. The player does not need a thirteen-slider biological designer or a permanent table of every internal contribution.

Three normal contexts cover the branch. Research shows the source and proposal, its qualification state, and the committed project. Manufacture shows the active standard, physically available compatible stock, and matched response coverage. An incident shows the affected standard or recipients, the unresolved obligation, and the recovery deadline.

The existing Authority display counts toward the same visible-value budget. With it visible, the branch may expose at most three other live mechanic values. Normal phases should use fewer. Historical design records can appear as an ordinary authorized list without turning every record into another permanent meter.

Each phase offers three to five primary actions, with six as the maximum. An incident can temporarily replace an ordinary commissioning action with recall or care. Active objectives remain limited to the existing mission capacity, normally one to three visible objectives. A list of every completed design must not create another wall of permanent action buttons.

### Costs and disabled states

Research rows use the exact four commitments shown in the project table. An occupied research lane counts as a committed capacity type. The passage of the research interval is not an extra resource payment. Source access, permission, valid sites, and existing funded safeguards are requirements, not undisclosed debits.

A required care programme must already exist as a real service with its own disclosed budget. It cannot be charged silently when a research project starts. When a player needs to fund that service first, the interface should show the missing service plainly and keep the dependent project unavailable until it is established.

Inline costs show no more than three values with their correct texticons, without repeating a native cost display. The full tooltip discloses every payment and commitment. An unregistered custom icon is a presentation blocker for that cost, not permission to show a misleading ordinary resource symbol.

The disabled explanation should distinguish unknown design, missing source, unavailable works, occupied slot, inadequate matched care, unrecognized stock, and prohibited native use. A country that knows a design but lacks stock should not be told that it lacks the research.

### Text direction

The physician's viewpoint emphasizes what can be treated and what support is required. The military viewpoint emphasizes the chosen formation and the interruption to service. A public institution explains the result it can substantiate. A classified institution may use guarded language, but the mechanical consequence must remain readable.

Options concerning a failed harmless qualification can use dry professional disagreement or restrained institutional irony. Options involving harmed patients, coercion, or civilian loss require severity and accountability. Humour must not make the affected people the joke.

No final localisation is supplied here. Working labels should be replaced during implementation with concise wording appropriate to the actual actor, mandate, source, and campaign state. Evidence, uncertainty, and public knowledge must be kept distinct so that a player is not shown a hidden foreign archive as a confirmed fact.

### Asset roles

The branch needs a coherent source-and-standard icon family, a matched-response icon, a retooling icon, and recall or restricted-custody states where the existing assets do not already represent the action. New physical equipment identities need their own authorized equipment presentation and a clear distinction from the source's ordinary stock.

Useful report art depicts a specific clinical service, a visibly interrupted production works, or the care of an affected formation. It should not rely on an invented technical diagram, readable laboratory instructions, or a generic archive desk to communicate every incident.

A profile change alone does not create a new creature model requirement. Existing formations retain their existing bodies unless the accepted design specifies a visible change. A genuinely new battlefield identity needs its own approved model, counter, and sourced sound package through the established owner. Renaming an existing zombie portrait cannot prove that a new lineage is visually complete.

Animation is reserved for a meaningful current state, such as a supported recall or active medical service whose motion helps recognition. It requires real source frames and a static fallback. No art, animation, model, audio, or native interface has been produced in this specification stage.

## What the portfolio leaves for the rivalry

The institute now has a portfolio whose value can survive its founder. It can retain several designs, manufacture a limited set, support older recipients, and share or withhold the knowledge needed to maintain them. Every claim of scientific ownership is tied to a real source, works, archive, service, or completed project.

These facts give the coming Wu–Kruger rivalry concrete stakes. A dispute can concern a shared facility, an exclusive producing standard, a qualified team, a captured collection, or the right to publish a matching treatment. The scientists cannot win that dispute by duplicating stock, rewriting another project's history, or treating people as unspent material.

Later foreign competition and evolved behaviour can change who controls the institution and how honestly it operates. They inherit the portfolio that the campaign actually built. They do not supply missing research or excuse an unsupported native transaction.
