# Doctor Wu: designed life and biological systems

## Living systems beyond human replication

Wu's next proposals concern the places where medicine has to keep working. A treatment can save patients inside a hospital and still leave the surrounding district exposed. A reclaimed town can have clean wards, ruined houses, hungry livestock, and a road that closes whenever fighting returns. The institute begins producing living systems that work alongside its physicians: enclosed filters, maintained cleanup organisms, agricultural treatments, unusual working animals, and living instruments that need an operator and a home.

The government chooses which problems deserve these systems. It can build a network of reliable medical works, support a small number of unusual formations, rebuild damaged communities, or combine several of these uses. Each commitment competes with the institute's existing treatment and production obligations. A government that has distributed Wu's methods can operate an advanced programme through public institutions. A government that has concentrated custody, maintenance, and training under Wu can become dependent on his staff even when it owns the buildings.

The working labels and numerical profiles in this part are proposed design. They are not final localisation or approved balance. Research remains ordinary baseline progression. Calm World, low Authority, and disabled evolutions do not close these families. Actual material, compatible knowledge, trained staff, facilities, and explicit permission decide access. Some campaigns never provide a particular source organism, and their institute must develop another eligible family until such material becomes available.

### What changes in play

A fixed filter protects a known receiving point for as long as it is maintained. A mobile cleanup colony works through an already affected district and must be collected when its assignment ends. Agricultural medicine protects the productive work that remains. A living reconnaissance team can obtain a short-lived observation, but it needs to return. A chimeric formation brings a distinct capability with a real food and care bill. A population programme creates new lives only through Part 7's paid production and first-settlement rules.

The institute therefore has three recurring choices. It chooses where to commit its living stock, which maintenance obligations to renew, and what to do when a useful design no longer fits the local conditions. Research and operation are separate. A completed project adds a reproducible design and its approved uses. It does not fill a warehouse, cure an entire country, recruit an army, or populate a ruined state.

### Research commitments

The existing research lanes remain the only institute research capacity. L01, L02, L04, L05, L07, L08, L12, and L15 use the substantial profile: one lane, two civilian factories, 150 Support Equipment, and 50 Political Power over a proposed 120 days. L03, L06, L09, L10, L13, and L14 use the exceptional profile: one lane, three civilian factories, 300 Support Equipment, and 75 Political Power over 180 days. L11 uses the same four cost types as the exceptional profile over 210 days because its joint qualification tests a working interface between two institutions.

A lane and a factory reservation are real commitments. They count among the four cost types. No project adds another sample debit, experience payment, or hidden personnel charge to these profiles. Source material, a qualified team, and appropriate sites are admission requirements. A project that needs to acquire material uses an independently meaningful acquisition or exchange with its own disclosed purpose. Possessing the specimen never stands in for paying the research commitment.

Projects that adapt an existing standard retain the work already demonstrated. L02 adds enclosed operation to M14. L06 adds a continuing service to M16. L13 applies existing replication to a real reconstruction programme. They do not repeat the original cure, tissue, or clone reward. A qualified specialist can improve the rate of the actual work under Part 7. Reassignment never awards a second completed interval.

## Living stock and working assignments

### Designs, service units, and individual animals

The institute records a design separately from its physical output. A filter design can be retained after every installed filter has failed. A foreign hospital can possess a filter without possessing the knowledge to reproduce it. A captured working animal can remain alive without accepting a new handler. These states must not collapse into a single technology flag.

Small colonies and enclosed assemblies use a Living Service Unit as an accounting scale. A unit contains one qualified living assembly and the habitat needed for its intended role. It is not a human cohort and cannot be exchanged for manpower, civilian population, clone equipment, or another organism design. Filter units, cleanup units, repellent stations, and agricultural units retain their own design identity even when the interface groups them under the same production family.

Large animals use actual whole-animal counts. Their record distinguishes available, assigned, resting, under treatment, missing, captured, and confirmed lost animals. Human handlers are accounted for separately. Losing an animal does not add a human death. Losing a handler or another actual person enters the appropriate shared Deaths path once. A discarded colony or damaged habitat does not create a fictional population loss.

The programme does not breed free replacement units through a passive stockpile bonus. Reproduction for new usable stock requires another paid order, a suitable site, care, and a recorded qualification. An escaped population can become a problem, but it cannot be harvested repeatedly as a free industrial output. Any recovery returns only the surviving organisms that the owner can actually identify.

### Husbandry and food demand

Husbandry Lots cover the provisioned feeding, housing, ordinary animal care, and handling supplies for large living organisms. They are distinct from human Clinical Material Lots and from any biological weapon payload. The reference production order prepares 40 Husbandry Lots over 30 days using one civilian factory, 100 Support Equipment, 500 fuel, and a food allocation quoted by the famine owner. Those are four cost types.

The food allocation must be expressed in the famine owner's actual supported units and debited or committed once through that owner. No conversion from an unspecified food-security score into tonnes is assumed. The amount is deliberately left to a reviewed crosswalk because the inspected famine documentation does not supply a universal food-to-animal conversion. Until that contract exists, husbandry production is an explicit implementation dependency. It cannot quietly become free food manufactured from Support Equipment.

An animal's ration remains part of the receiving area's food demand while it lives there. The husbandry receipt tells the food owner which part has already been provisioned, so the same feed is not also removed through another projected demand. Unprovisioned demand remains visible to the food owner. A relocation changes the actual demand location once. Ending military use does not end the animal's need for care.

Healthy reserve animals use half the full operational husbandry quantity. Full operational reference quantities per 30 days are five lots for twenty reconnaissance animals, fifteen lots for fifty support animals, and twenty lots for twenty-five assault organisms. Quantities scale to actual surviving animals and round upward to whole lots for the complete assignment. Dividing an assignment cannot reduce its combined bill. A reserve cannot claim operational benefits while paying the resting rate.

### Coverage and service capacity

One reference Living Service Unit can support up to 100,000 eligible living people in one serviced state. This is a coverage ceiling, not a claim that every person in the state has been reached. Actual coverage is the least of the contract, the supplied service capacity, the eligible living population, and any smaller limit imposed by the disease or receiving owner.

The institution can service five units from a qualified works site under the reference profile. Further sites need actual staff and physical capacity. A country cannot move the same five units between reports and claim five units at every endpoint. A service unit has one current physical assignment. A larger district needs additional units or must accept partial coverage.

A selected service shows its covered population or organism count, its next maintenance deadline, and the important operating condition. Those contextual values replace unrelated details in the current view. They do not add a permanent national ecology meter beside Authority, treatment, research, and all the famine and migration values.

The most effective applicable Wu contribution governs each overlapping purpose. A hospital filter and a second filter do not repeatedly halve the same exposure. A completed cleanup and an ongoing cure can address different owner-defined components, but the owner must distinguish those components. The producer reports supported coverage and performed work. The disease owner decides the actual accepted change.

### Maintenance, withdrawal, and retirement

A standard installation is funded for a 90-day service period, including ordinary maintenance and removal. The final 30 days reserve enough service capacity for an orderly withdrawal. A renewal replaces the future service obligation. It does not renew the research reward, undo earlier damage, or restore a lost unit.

A shortage first interrupts the benefit that depends on the missing input. Staff then attempt the recorded withdrawal or care-only state. A dangerous failure requires a genuine neglected organism, incompatible conditions, or damaged containment. Missing a payment does not automatically create a nationwide epidemic. The local consequences follow the organisms that actually remain.

A recovered reusable assembly returns to available stock only after inspection establishes that it is still viable. Used clinical material, food, fuel, elapsed factory time, and completed field work remain spent. A retired animal stays in the living record until transferred, discharged into a supported non-military setting, or actually lost. The government cannot obtain a manpower refund by calling it equipment.

## L01: living production qualification

L01 establishes the first independently reproducible living design. The player selects a source with a defined useful property and a service the institute can explain in gameplay terms. A medical institute might begin with an enclosed assembly that tolerates a hospital's conditions. A military institute might begin with a working animal that can follow a handler and return to shelter. Neither proposal needs to begin with a pathogen.

The first choice concerns the work the organism will perform. Public-service qualification prioritises stable operation, independent maintenance, and an exit from institute custody. Restricted military qualification tests handling, field supply, recall, and coexistence with ordinary troops. The same institute can qualify both uses through separate relevant work. It does not receive a permanent technological exclusion for choosing the public use first.

Qualification must show a useful output under the declared conditions, a recognisable loss of performance outside those conditions, and a practical retirement or recovery path. A filter that works only while its housing remains intact can qualify as a fixed installation. It cannot simultaneously qualify as a mobile cleanup organism. A strong animal that has never completed a handling return cannot qualify for field deployment.

The first independent result gives the host a reproducible standard and permits its matching production order. Independent staff can maintain that standard after Wu departs. Wu retains his unusual ability to adapt it to a genuinely new medical problem when he is available. The public institution therefore gains lasting competence without receiving every later design for free.

A flawed result can reveal narrow usefulness. The government may keep a hospital-only version, fund a corrected field qualification, or retire it and recover reusable habitat equipment. The correction reuses authenticated work and does not restart the original project from zero. The reference corrective qualification takes 60 days, one research lane, one civilian factory, 75 Support Equipment, and 25 Political Power. It consumes four cost types and resolves the identified limitation rather than rerolling unrelated rewards.

Unexplained behaviour creates an observation case tied to the affected design and generation. The player chooses between continued controlled observation, restricted use, and retirement. The institute must not announce sentience because one animal disobeys. If later evidence supports an independent person or organised actor, the appropriate owner must recognise that transition before political consequences use it. Research cannot classify a living human or a clone as nonhuman merely to escape civilian obligations.

Presentation should centre on the first useful interaction between people and the new organism. Show the trained animal completing a difficult return, the maintained hospital installation, or the recovery of a failed assembly. Working diagrams belong in the research record, while the report image should depict the actual service. Option tone can distinguish practical confidence, guarded approval, and Wu's impatience without drafting final text.

## L02: enclosed biological filters

L02 places a maintained living assembly inside a defined service installation. Its first useful role is protecting the passage between an affected district and a functioning institution. Hospitals, receiving centres, ports, and depots become different deployment settings because they have different access problems. The installation protects the biological exposure that its approved standard can address. It does not remove chemical residues, radiation, volcanic ash, or a supernatural effect.

The government chooses a hospital-centred assignment or a transport-centred assignment. A hospital assignment protects eligible patients and staff at the supplied medical endpoint. A transport assignment protects an owner-recognised receiving or distribution point. The latter helps an existing route remain medically usable, but it cannot establish a sea corridor, repair a railway, open a border, or grant permission to enter another country.

The reference installation of five units commits those five units, fifteen Clinical Material Lots, fifty Support Equipment, and 500 fuel. Smaller assignments scale the three field allocations to the units actually installed. Preparation takes 30 days, followed by the funded 90-day service period. Existing trained staff and a suitable building are requirements. They must have capacity for the assignment, but this action does not add a fifth personnel or factory payment.

At complete coverage, the proposed filter contribution halves compatible new biological exposure through that endpoint. Partial coverage scales the contribution to the living people actually served. This contribution is evaluated once alongside the earlier Wu protection standard for the same purpose. It does not halve the already reduced result again. Existing infection continues to require treatment, and outgoing exposure through unserviced endpoints remains possible.

One unit assigned to a 100,000-person receiving district can provide its full local contribution. One unit promised to a million-person state supplies only one tenth of that coverage. A port installation cannot claim the inland hospitals that have no working delivery connection. The player sees the practical choice between protecting a smaller critical point well and spreading available service across a larger area.

Damage can interrupt the housing, the living assembly, or the supply route separately. Intact living units can enter care-only storage while workers repair the housing. Destroyed units cannot return through a maintenance renewal. A loss of medical access cancels the claimed protection for the affected people without reversing treatment they already received. The owner retains the original exposure, contamination, and investigation history.

The local recovery choices are repair in place, transfer an intact unit to a reachable service point, or retire the damaged assembly. A standard repair uses one civilian factory, fifty Support Equipment, 500 fuel, and five Clinical Material Lots over 45 days. It restores only the recorded damaged installation. A successful repair does not create a second one. In a crowded city, the more important choice can be which hospital remains protected during that interruption.

Independent maintenance can keep these filters working after Wu is absent. A changed disease reference still needs adaptation through the cure and qualification systems. Possessing a filter is not permanent immunity to every future disease. The visible installation should change between operating, partially supplied, withdrawn, and damaged states, with a clear non-colour cue for each condition.

## L03: disease-eating service colonies

L03 develops a living cleanup assembly for an already identified biological burden. The organisms remain fictional and are described through their work, care, limits, and visible behaviour. The project supplies no recipe or real mechanism for producing or releasing an organism.

The distinction from L02 is practical. Filters protect a supplied endpoint against additional exposure. L03 commits a maintained team to reducing an existing, compatible local burden. The cleanup team can work in a district that is already damaged, but it must have access to the assigned ground and a way to recover its living stock. It cannot cleanse an entire front from a distant laboratory.

Research requires the current strain or biological reference, M14, and an approved response from the owning disease system. A country may complete the design using a contained case while healthy. Operational cleanup remains unavailable until an actual burden exists. The institute cannot create an outbreak to satisfy its own research reward or count a simulated case as a national disease incident.

The reference five-unit assignment commits five cleanup units, thirty Clinical Material Lots, one hundred Support Equipment, and 1,000 fuel. Preparation takes 30 days. The following 90-day operation has a named target and maintained access. The service staff, removal capacity, and current compatibility record must remain valid throughout. The government can recall the team early when military control becomes uncertain.

The proposed full operation offers work equivalent to removing 40 percent of the compatible local biological burden attributable to the covered portion of the selected state. It cannot remove more than the owner still recognises at settlement. The initial burden is a work ceiling, not a frozen promise that survives reinfection or reclassification. Continued disease activity can add new burden while the team works. The owning system decides whether the remaining conditions meet its normal containment, recovery, or cure thresholds.

For example, an owner can expose one hundred units of eligible local burden when the assignment begins. A fully supplied operation can offer forty units of cleanup work. If another valid response has already reduced that burden to twenty, this assignment cannot remove forty more or transfer the unused twenty to a different state. If twenty new units appear during the operation, the owner records those new units through their real source. The cleanup does not erase their history or manufacture an earlier cure date.

The strongest overlapping Wu cleanup contribution is used for the same work interval and component. M14 provides the prior medical competence. L03 changes how that competence is delivered and sustained. An independent hospital cure can continue treating living patients while the service colony works on a separate local biological burden, provided the disease owner supports that distinction. No second mortality reduction is assumed from two names for the same response.

Incomplete work can still matter. An orderly withdrawal retains accepted work already performed and returns only surviving, inspected units. An access failure strands the actual remaining team or forces it into protected withdrawal. An incompatible reference stops effective cleanup and opens an adaptation project. Neither failure automatically creates a stronger pathogen. A harmful side effect needs a real local incident with a known affected population or organism group.

The important choices are whether to finish a limited safe assignment, replace it with an updated design, or withdraw from a district the army can no longer protect. A government may be tempted to leave the colony behind because it still appears useful. Doing so ends the supervised service contract. Any later uncontrolled activity belongs to the exact escaped colony and the owner-approved local consequence, not an immediate global epidemic or a free permanent cleaning modifier.

Recovery can include collecting identifiable units, treating harmed staff through existing medicine, and closing the damaged service point. A correction of compatibility uses the current cure research path. A physical recovery operation commits the recovery team, fifty Support Equipment, 500 fuel, and ten Clinical Material Lots over a proposed 60 days. Its objective is to regain access and account for the recorded units. It does not refill the stock to its original maximum.

## L04: rat-vector defence

L04 addresses plague support carried by eligible local vectors. It begins with the real case exposed by Black Plague or another compatible disease owner. Ordinary nuisance animals, a hostile country's identity, or a rumour about the Rat King do not establish a treatable vector population.

The government selects one of two operating approaches for an assignment. Repellent stations protect a prepared receiving point through a maintained fictional living assembly. Predator units work through a bounded accessible district under handlers. Their purpose, physical stock, food demand, and failure conditions differ. Completing the project does not make the two approaches interchangeable or create an unlimited predator population.

Repellent stations use the standard Living Service Unit production profile and the L02 installation profile. Their proposed full-coverage contribution reduces the compatible vector-entry component by 50 percent at the protected endpoint. They do not clear established plague, reduce a Rat Nation's divisions, or remove a Royal Node. A maintained station can help keep a hospital or food depot useful after nearby areas become unsafe.

Predator units use twenty qualified fictional ward animals per operational team. Their appearance should be unfamiliar and coherent, with no identification as a real species selected for a real biological-control programme. Each team is manufactured and trained through the reconnaissance-animal production profile, with a distinct ward-animal design identity. Reconnaissance animals cannot be renamed into ward animals after production.

The reference field assignment commits twenty ward animals, twenty-five trained handlers, ten Husbandry Lots, and fifty Support Equipment for 60 days. Human handlers are committed from an existing available group and remain separate from animal stock. The assignment aims to reduce the owner-proven local vector burden by up to 35 percent at full supported coverage. It is strongest in a small accessible district and weak where the owner cannot establish access to the affected ground.

The food bill creates a meaningful difference from repellent stations. A hungry district may be able to maintain a fixed station and lack the food to support predators. The government can fund food and safe housing through their actual owners, choose the fixed method, or withdraw the animals. It cannot feed a growing predator force by subtracting an unverified number of rats from a military country.

The player can protect a grain depot, support a refugee receiving point, or work along a recaptured urban district. These are different strategic priorities. Protecting one location does not count as serving the other two. A wider programme uses additional qualified teams, supplied routes, and separate food allocations. Assignment size scales within the actual staff and husbandry capacity rather than a universal campaign cap.

An organised Rat Nation remains an independent actor. The medical programme can weaken the biological contribution the rat owner exposes. It cannot issue annexation, remove the Rat King, consume Brood Mass as if it were field vermin, or decide that a surviving brood has been defeated. Rat military action can destroy or capture the installations in ordinary play. Any specific response to the new ward animals must be an owner-approved reaction, not an invented automatic rat evolution.

Failures include animals refusing a return, an abandoned feeding site, or a station that displaces the problem beyond the protected point. A displaced vector burden is not silently deleted. The disease owner must accept its destination and amount before any movement is applied. Without that evidence, the result remains a failed local exclusion or unresolved movement, not a guessed neighbouring infection.

An orderly withdrawal returns surviving animals to a supported reserve and preserves the completed local work. A missing animal becomes a tracked recovery problem. Human casualties from recovery are recorded only when they actually occur. The report should focus on changed activity at the protected site, the handlers' experience, and the practical limits observed. It should not announce the extermination of a political actor because a medical operation succeeded.

## L05: agricultural survival medicine

L05 applies the institute's biological knowledge to existing food production. The project is offered when a crop, herd, or working-animal problem is medically compatible and materially affects an owner-recognised food shortage. The institute can also qualify a preventive standard from a contained agricultural case during a healthy period. Operational benefits still require actual cultivated ground or surviving animals.

The crop and livestock assignments share the project foundation but keep distinct targets. A crop assignment protects a diagnosed cultivated area through the next relevant production interval. A livestock assignment treats an existing herd, reduces its diagnosed biological losses, and supports continued care. Neither creates food, replacement animals, workers, or agricultural land at research completion.

The agricultural Living Service Unit profile produces five units of the selected standard. Installation uses the L02 four-cost profile and a 30-day preparation. The reference service period is 90 days, extended only through a funded renewal when the food owner's production interval requires it. The local agricultural staff, safe access, and compatible production remain requirements. A district with abandoned fields and no workforce cannot receive a full harvest effect merely because its installation is supplied.

At full coverage, the proposed benefit prevents up to half of the disease-attributed production loss the famine owner has accepted. The reference is the biological part of the current shortfall. Suppose an eighty-unit production shortfall contains forty units explicitly attributed to a compatible crop disease. The maximum proposed improvement is twenty units, leaving a sixty-unit shortfall before the owner evaluates weather, extraction, transport, and other conditions. It does not halve the complete famine score or turn the whole eighty units into food.

The famine owner applies the resulting production condition at the appropriate interval. It continues to calculate food need, reserves, access, mortality, and recovery. A successful treatment can still leave a district hungry because the harvest is being removed, a railway remains cut, or too many people have arrived. The institute's medical report should identify that limit without presenting a failed political or transport system as medical resistance.

Livestock service preserves actual surviving animals. Replacing lost animals needs a distinct paid production and husbandry commitment under the appropriate owner. L05 cannot use human replication stock, a disease-death total, or a biological payload as its replacement resource. A herd brought into a different district carries its physical and food obligations rather than appearing in both locations.

The government chooses between protecting domestic production, maintaining food for a relief district, and servicing a foreign partner that permits the work. A foreign assignment carries no automatic extraction rights. Selling treatment can create a commercial dependence through Part 4, but the food itself remains with its actual producer and owner unless a separate lawful or coercive transaction moves it.

A failed assignment can leave a working standard with a limited season, an incompatible herd, or a treatment that requires more follow-up than the district can support. The response is adaptation, reduced coverage, or withdrawal. A new failure does not reset the harvest clock. Destroyed crops and slaughtered animals remain lost. Evidence of a deliberate cover-up or reckless use persists after the biological problem is corrected.

This family should make food security more responsive to medical choices without making famine a disease that Wu can cure. Its strongest successful outcome is a district that can keep producing and supplying its existing people. Later recovery then comes from the food, transport, migration, and reconstruction work that the government actually completes.

## L06: nonhuman clinical services

M16 established that some nonhuman biological conditions can be treated. L06 turns a qualified case into a continuing clinical service for the same family. The programme can serve a state's own unusual formations, an independent nonhuman society that accepts care, or living people held inside a nonhuman-controlled region when the population and custody owners expose a valid medical case.

An independent nonhuman recipient can receive a qualified foreign service without becoming an eligible resident host for Wu's arrival event. Its existing country and population classifiers remain unchanged.

A service is qualified per family and problem. A treatment that works for a paleogenetic creature does not automatically work for a xenobiological organism. A rat population's existing plague immunity does not prove immunity to every condition or permit the institute to invent a treatable illness. Mechanical damage in a robot belongs to mechanical maintenance. A supernatural being has a medical case only when its owner identifies an actual biological component.

The first clinical commitment selects the recipients, the accessible care site, and the authority that can consent or provide lawful custody. For a dependent animal, its owner and qualified handlers supply the care authority. For an independent sentient group, the agreement must include its representative or the relevant owner-defined consent. Defeating a military unit does not grant Wu ownership of every member of its species.

The reference clinical assignment commits the selected patients or cohort, one civilian factory, ten Clinical Material Lots, and fifty Support Equipment over 60 days. That profile is for a bounded ordinary case. A large organism group uses an owner-quoted scale based on actual patients and needs. The quote changes the quantities, not the four cost types. Urgent injury, a difficult family, or a long follow-up can change the supported duration before the order is accepted.

The service has three useful outcomes. Treatment can remove the compatible illness, stabilise patients until another service is available, or establish a safe maintenance standard that prevents recurrence under the same conditions. None of these outcomes grants recruitment, changes a country's species classification, installs a control doctrine, or makes its recipients loyal to Wu. Military fitness is returned only for surviving individuals whose treatment and ordinary readiness support it.

Paleogenetic treatment remains separate from the reserve, hatchery, transport, and feeding owners. Xenobiological treatment preserves the existing exclusive control architecture. The institute can cooperate with the current control operator without selecting a second chemical, neural, machine, or researched doctrine. A medical apparatus is not permission to bypass that exclusivity. Treatment may need to wait until the legitimate control owner can provide a safe operating state.

A living infected civilian in rat-held territory remains a living patient. A fully dead reanimated soldier does not become a living citizen through L06. The owner can expose a maintenance or shutdown response for an engineered undead unit, but that belongs to its existing identity and cannot restore the person's spent first-settlement entitlement. A successful medical assignment never deletes an independent country from the map.

Discharge requires a viable destination. Returning a recovered organism to a collapsing facility can recreate the same problem. The institute can negotiate continued care, a different qualified custodian, or a supported return. Human population movement goes through migration. Nonhuman animals move through their actual cohort or unit owner. Neither route uses a fictional population refund to make the transfer convenient.

The political tension comes from access, trust, confidentiality, and continuing need. A recipient may accept medicine and reject military inspection. Wu may demand research access as a price, or public physicians may insist on separating care from that demand. These choices feed the later foreign and rivalry systems through real agreements and evidence. This part supplies the medical transaction and its limits, without declaring those later systems complete.

The clinical scenes should preserve the dignity and distinct physical needs of the recipients. Show the handling equipment, the adapted care space, and the difficulty of communication where relevant. Avoid treating an entire people as a specimen shelf. No extra portrait family follows automatically from adding a clinical agreement.

## L07: living reconnaissance

L07 creates a recoverable living observation service. It is useful when a formation needs fresh local information and can protect the handlers and receiving point. The organisms are fictional instruments with limited attention, range, endurance, and interpretation. The project never supplies real methods for biological surveillance, infiltration, or dissemination.

Research qualifies one observation role at a time. Ground observation supports a known front or a named nearby operational district. Route observation supports an already established movement or relief route. A source organism suited to one role does not automatically acquire every other role. A country can qualify another design later without losing its first standard.

The practical loop is assignment, observation, return, interpretation, and rest. A team that fails to return cannot deliver a perfect report simply because the timer expires. The government must decide whether to wait, send a recovery team, accept an incomplete observation, or abandon the attempt. Information belongs to the actual returned observations and expires as the situation changes.

A reference assignment commits twenty qualified reconnaissance animals, twenty-five trained handlers, fifty Support Equipment, and ten Husbandry Lots for 30 days. The handlers remain unavailable for another assignment during that period. A supplied receiving site and a supported target relationship are requirements. The target must be an actual eligible military or route surface. The player does not select an arbitrary distant capital and receive its complete military state.

Successful ground observation grants a proposed 25 percent reconnaissance contribution to one recorded formation for up to 30 days after interpretation. The contribution addresses the supported local reconnaissance calculation, not the entire country's intelligence ledger. Successful route observation instead supplies a dated finding to that route's owner: confirmed usable, confirmed interrupted, or unresolved. It does not open the route, establish naval superiority, or move a population.

The reconnaissance contribution applies only while the formation remains in the relevant operational setting. Moving to a different front ends its relevance. A route finding becomes stale after the same 30-day reference interval or a known material change, whichever comes first. The new service does not reveal exact enemy division locations or hidden country variables without an independently supported engine consumer.

Incomplete returns reduce the contribution in proportion to the qualified observers and useful coverage actually recovered. Twenty animals are the reference team, not a guarantee of complete information. If only half return with usable observations, the maximum contribution is half the reference result. A duplicate report cannot restore the missing half. Rest and husbandry remain necessary before survivors take another assignment.

A damaged interpretation station can preserve the returned observations until a trained team becomes available, but it cannot freeze their operational relevance. Waiting for interpretation consumes their remaining useful window. This makes staff and communications matter without granting another research lane or permanent intelligence bonus.

The player can prioritise military use or humanitarian route verification. Both require real assignments. A classified mandate can restrict who sees the findings, while a public mandate can share a route report with a partner. Knowledge sharing transmits the report and its date, not the organisms, handlers, current physical access, or a new intelligence operation.

Failure should be shown through the service itself: a partial return, a fatigued handling team, or observations that no longer match a changed front. A recovery uses the existing named team and location. It must not create a random escaped swarm across unrelated states. Ordinary combat and route hazards remain capable of destroying the service.

## L08: biological camouflage

L08 provides a reversible living covering or protective surface for a selected formation. It does not permanently alter the soldiers and does not give every division a national concealment modifier. The living material has to remain compatible with its recipients, serviced, and appropriate to its approved operating conditions.

The reference production batch supplies ten camouflage allocations. Each allocation covers up to five hundred actual recipients. A five-thousand-person formation therefore requires ten allocations, while a smaller formation uses a proportional whole-unit quantity. Excess coverage cannot be reassigned while the original unit still claims it. A split or merge preserves the total covered people and the remaining service period under Part 6's formation-identity rule.

The reference application to five thousand people commits ten allocations, ten Clinical Material Lots, twenty-five Support Equipment, and fifteen Command Power. Preparation lasts 30 days and the operational course lasts 60 days. The recipient remains an ordinary available army formation during fitting and receives no camouflage benefit until preparation is complete. Its identity is a target, not an extra formation payment. The quote includes removal and routine recovery. It cannot conceal a fifth factory or manpower charge in a follow-up confirmation.

The government chooses a movement-protection posture or a prepared-position posture for the course. Movement protection proposes a 20 percent reduction in the enemy's applicable local reconnaissance contribution against the covered formation. It does not remove the formation from the map or prevent ordinary detection, combat, air attack, or supply consumption. Prepared-position use instead proposes 25 percent faster planning preparation for that formation while it remains at its assigned prepared position. The two postures cannot apply simultaneously to the same allocation.

These are intended outcomes that require exact formation-scoped implementation evidence. A country-wide modifier is not an acceptable substitute. If the engine cannot express the proposed reconnaissance-denial consumer directly, the implementation must return the exact limitation for a reviewed redesign. It must not silently replace the effect with a generic national defence bonus.

A posture change requires the formation to return to a supported service point and complete a new 15-day fitting interval. It keeps the original course's expiry and consumes five Clinical Material Lots per full reference formation. It does not restart the sixty days or create another allocation. A formation cannot alternate postures daily to obtain both results or reset its maintenance.

Heavy damage, incompatible conditions, or an interrupted clinical supply can require early removal. The player can withdraw the formation, accept loss of the special benefit while maintaining ordinary care, or continue an explicit emergency use with a risk to the actual recipients. A consequence such as illness or impaired readiness is recorded on that formation. It cannot be removed by renaming the template.

The material does not confer resistance to all diseases or grant a new biological weapon. Infected recipients still use their actual medical case. Camouflage that becomes unsuitable after mutation needs adaptation. A clean completion removes the temporary covering and leaves learned production knowledge, the formation's authentic history, and any real medical consequences intact.

The presentation should show the difference between a fitted living surface and permanent enhancement. It must not imply invisibility or a change of species. A leader portrait is not needed for a formation-level service. Any visible unit alteration must have a separately accepted material and model variant rather than an unreviewed recolour of another unit's asset.

## L09: chimeric support animals

L09 creates a deliberately unusual working animal for difficult transport and casualty retrieval. Its body is built around carrying a useful load, maintaining stable footing, and tolerating the handling needed to move wounded personnel. Its appearance can be impossible, but its service remains understandable: it carries things and living patients through a place where ordinary wheeled transport performs poorly.

The institute can reach this design through its own qualified living-organism work. Access to a paleogenetic specimen can improve the source basis, but a completed Kruger army is not mandatory. A xenobiological source requires that family's separate compatibility and control permission. Mixed source material does not merge those owners' technologies, histories, or military entitlements.

The reference support attachment contains fifty qualified animals, one hundred and fifty human handlers, fifty Support Equipment, and fifteen Husbandry Lots for the initial thirty-day operational allocation. Those are the four commissioning cost types. Commissioning lasts 90 days and uses the normal training commitment of the proposed attachment. There is no additional command-power purchase, political fee, or factory reservation on the same action.

Its human handlers use real manpower. The animals use their separate physical stock and never generate manpower. The attachment is unarmed as a firing unit. It relies on its supported combat formation for protection and does not receive invented rifle effects or gunfire audio. Its support-equipment requirement covers the actual carried and handling equipment that the model must depict where visible.

The intended military advantage is a 25 percent reduction in applicable rough-terrain equipment attrition for the formation's supported movement and casualty-retrieval work. Its additional medical role is to make a bounded local casualty group reachable by an existing field-medical service where the transport owner accepts that connection. It does not apply another universal casualty-trickleback bonus on top of the same treatment. The medical owner still decides which living casualties can be saved.

The proposed tradeoff is a 10 percent speed penalty during ordinary road movement with the attachment, plus its husbandry and protection burden. It is useful for a difficult approach or an evacuation from broken ground. It is a poor default attachment for every fast armoured formation. It cannot carry supplies across a sea, reopen a destroyed bridge by itself, or restore a cut strategic supply network.

The player chooses between a transport task and a casualty-retrieval task. The same animal capacity cannot carry a full military load and a full patient load simultaneously. A mixed task divides the actual capacity. Patient movement never creates new manpower. The wounded remain in the appropriate military or civilian owner's custody until that owner's treatment and movement transaction resolves.

The attachment needs a compatible combat anchor. It cannot enter Event 019 as a support-only division. If it is registered as an attachment to a new chimeric combat family, that provider owns its template, payment, equipment publication, sustainment, and cleanup. A native army can also train the attachment through its exact access and equipment contract once the implementation proves the supported unit definition.

Loss of the host formation does not automatically kill every animal or free every handler. The actual unit owner determines surviving manpower and recoverable stock. An identified surviving animal group can enter a field recovery or care-only state. Capturing that group does not immediately qualify a new country's handlers. The receiving country needs the retained handling standard or a paid requalification.

The post-service choices matter because the animals remain alive. A healthy reserve group uses the resting husbandry rate. A civilian transport service requires a supported assignment with the same physical capacity limits. Discharging animals into an unsupported district is abandonment and can create a local problem. It is not a free retirement command that erases their food demand.

## L10: chimeric assault organisms

L10 creates a living assault family intended for a prepared, difficult attack. It should have a recognisable physical role and should not become a better version of every existing creature. The proposed identity is a heavy, low-speed organism whose body can force a passage during a supported assault, with handlers controlling its deployment and recovery.

Paleogenetic creatures retain their terrestrial reserve, hatchery, and mobile shock identity. Xenobiological organisms retain their separate adaptive and control architecture. L10 may use a genuinely supplied source from either family, but its new body, training, and medical standard need their own qualified design. Research does not grant either existing operational package, and an external operational grant does not automatically grant L10.

The reference combat element contains twenty-five qualified organisms and two hundred human handlers. Commissioning commits those organisms, those handlers, one hundred Support Equipment, and twenty Husbandry Lots, with a 120-day training period. It has four spendable cost types. Handlers are human manpower and can be lost. The organism stock is a finite nonhuman military resource and cannot substitute for those people.

The intended strength is concentrated. Compared with the appropriate unenhanced creature profile chosen during implementation, the proposed defining changes are 30 percent greater breakthrough and 35 percent more physical staying power, with 25 percent lower speed. The additional 40 percent fort-assault advantage belongs only to the prepared opportunity described below. The package must remain vulnerable to concentrated fire, air attack, disrupted approach routes, and loss of handlers. It receives no blanket armour, piercing, disease immunity, or organisation superiority.

These comparison values are balance targets, not fabricated native modifiers. The implementation must choose the exact verified unit statistics and category consumers that express the role, document the comparison, and keep unrelated units unchanged. A benefit that the engine cannot isolate to the intended family remains an explicit design decision rather than a reason to grant a national bonus.

The formation's distinctive action is a prepared assault assignment. The player commits the actual formation to a named reachable fortified front, provides twenty Husbandry Lots, one hundred Support Equipment, and twenty Command Power, and maintains the assigned approach and supply for a proposed 60-day preparation. The formation commitment is the fourth cost type. The action does not also charge fuel, experience, political power, or another generic fee.

Completion establishes a bounded prepared-assault opportunity for that formation against that front. Its special fort-assault advantage applies during the opportunity, while ordinary combat still decides the result. The preparation does not destroy a fort through a script effect, transfer a state, eliminate defenders, or grant victory. Changing the target requires another real preparation and cannot carry the old target's opportunity to a different continent.

The player can interrupt preparation to save the formation, use it conventionally with its speed and husbandry limitations, or commit to the prepared attack. The special preparation expires after a proposed 30-day opportunity if unused. Supplies spent on preparation remain spent. The formation and surviving organisms do not disappear because the opportunity expires.

A control failure is tied to the actual handling and service state. Exhausted handlers, an unmaintained cohort, or a captured control archive can lead to loss of readiness, refusal to advance, or a local dangerous incident. The response can be recovery, a controlled stand-down, retraining, or an explicitly authorised destructive action against the affected organisms. Routine losses never create a fresh full-strength cohort through a respawn.

A routine stand-down requires a secure site and the actual recorded formation. A proposed 90-day recovery commits the formation, one civilian factory, twenty Clinical Material Lots, and fifty Support Equipment. Husbandry continues under its existing standing obligation and is displayed separately as an ongoing animal-care obligation, not a new hidden admission fee. The same period is not billed again through an invented second upkeep package.

The public mandate can authorise this family through a visible military exception with independent veterinary and medical review. The classified mandate can concentrate records and handling staff under Wu, increasing later dependence through the actual custody concession. Neither mandate receives a technology solely because Authority rose. The dangerous political outcome grows from who controls the formations and their care.

### Shared military identity and inheritance

Both chimeric designs retain a birth or production generation, trained handling standard, real stock, and current custodian. Ordinary training scales through equipment and manpower rather than a lifetime unit cap. A hard limit is appropriate for a free event-created opening force only when its specific owner authorises that grant. This part creates no such free opening.

A new standalone chimeric combat family requires an owner-side Event 019 registration and all thirteen runtime callbacks. Its support animals are an explicit attachment, with a compatible combat anchor and separate equipment needs inside the same manifest. Existing provider IDs are not reused. No provider ID is reserved in this specification.

Event 019 fees create an integration issue already recorded for Part 7. Native chimeric commissioning uses four cost types. The shared consumer must not append unrelated generic fees as a fifth or sixth type. A provider-specific cost disposition needs acceptance by the Event 019 owner before implementation. That disposition may not change the established prices or eligibility of other providers.

An Event 019 derivative receives its own exact unit and public-package identity. It does not receive Wu, the institute, Authority, cure research, evolution history, or a right to create human copies. Its leadership and classification must follow the shared provider proof. This is particularly important when the visible authority is a human handling corps around nonhuman organisms. The presence of animals alone cannot be used to classify an ordinary human government as nonhuman.

Transfer of an exact damaged formation remains subject to Part 7's unresolved engine boundary. The existing Event 019 natural-release replacement exception does not authorise a general full-strength recreation of Wu units. Captured or transferred physical organisms retain only their actual surviving number and known care history. A recipient does not inherit completed research, equipment production, or private command rights from a photograph of the formation.

## L11: joint biological machinery

L11 begins when a real Wu team and a real Kruger team agree to build a particular service machine. Wu supplies the qualified biological process and its clinical limits. Kruger's institution supplies the relevant machinery knowledge and engineering staff. A completed operational technology can establish part of the technical basis, but an old contact flag does not prove that either team has agreed to collaborate.

The first agreement names one output, one site, the staff who can maintain it, and the knowledge each participant may retain. The government can choose shared technical custody or a more restricted split. Shared custody allows trained national staff to keep the completed machine working. Restricted custody can make the same useful installation depend on two separate private teams. That dependency follows real maintenance rights and staff access rather than an automatic Authority increase from research.

Three bounded machine roles are proposed. Each is a separate physical installation under the same research family. Completing one does not build the other two or grant every conventional and custom technology in the broader Kruger portfolio.

| Installation | Required technical basis | Proposed construction commitment | Continuing use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Service-tender works | A qualified L02, L03, or L05 design and operational Robotics | 2 civilian factories, 50 compatible robot equipment, 2,000 fuel, 25 Clinical Material Lots for 180 days | Tends a larger declared set of the same installed living service. |
| Biological observation works | L07 and operational Electronics with a trained interpretation team | 2 civilian factories, 50 compatible CBRN instrument equipment, 1,000 fuel, 20 Clinical Material Lots for 150 days | Interprets returned observations and distributes the resulting dated findings. |

These are four-cost construction profiles. Robot equipment used in service-tender works becomes part of that installation. It cannot also appear in a combat robot division. Instrument stock is quoted through the existing supported equipment family and its actual models. A generic label must not permit an unsupported model or a fabricated substitute payment.

Service-tender works add two maintained Living Service Unit places to that site's reference five-place capacity. They do not create the two units or supply their upkeep. Their value comes from operating a larger useful installation with the same trained supervisory team, provided the site has the required clinical and mechanical service. The extra places close when the machinery is inoperative. The affected living units enter supported care or are reassigned without disappearing.

Biological observation works improve the interpretation of returned material. The proposed staffed working rate is one third higher, equivalent to reducing the remaining eligible interpretation time by one quarter only while the machine and staff actually work. It does not add completed work on activation. A qualified Part 7 specialist and the machine use the strongest applicable rate for the same task, not multiplied rates. Neither can recover information that never returned or make a stale report current again.

Recovery-cradle works provide five physical exceptional-patient places. A place requires the living patient's actual admission and the M10 care contract. Constructing five places does not create five preserved leaders, summon missing patients, or award a national research bonus. Existing preservation costs remain with the care owner, which must adopt the construction-supplied reserve only when it represents the same purpose. The site must not charge the same fifty clinical lots again through a duplicate admission package.

All three installations remain at their actual sites. Capturing a building can give the captor physical machinery and damaged living stock. It does not teleport its former operators, transfer either scientist, or grant an entire project history. A recipient with the retained maintenance standard can resume supported operation after revalidation and repair. A recipient without it can preserve patients or organisms through emergency care while negotiating or reconstructing the missing knowledge.

Separation of the two institutions does not automatically destroy the installations. Learned public methods stay learned. A proprietary component can leave a genuine maintenance dependency, and the government's response can be licensing, independent reconstruction from authentic records, a narrowed service, or an orderly closure. The machine's physical and medical failures remain distinct. A mechanical repair does not cure an incompatible organism, and a medical correction does not replace a broken power or control component.

The joint team must not manipulate the existing xenobiological control exclusivity. A machine-assisted medical service is not a second control doctrine. If a xenobiological patient requires machine control, its existing owner must authorise that architecture through its normal contract. L11 cannot call a private runtime helper to force the grant or remove an already chosen channel.

The collaboration supplies concrete evidence for the later rivalry part: who delivered the agreed component, who retained the records, who paid for repair, and whether either scientist obstructed continuing care. It does not choose the entire Kruger-Wu relationship from one construction result. A successful collaboration can coexist with disputes elsewhere in the portfolio.

## L12: reconstruction districts

L12 creates a real receiving district in damaged territory. The programme brings together medical access, native repair work, shelter, food safety, and reception capacity. Its purpose is to make a selected place usable for surviving residents, returning migrants, and later new citizens. It cannot restore a country's pre-disaster population by changing a number after a cure succeeds.

A candidate district must have a real damage or reception problem and a viable path to recovery. Eligible causes include disease devastation, destructive occupation, a liberated feeding state, and another owner-recognised disaster. The original owner keeps its cause, losses, phase, memorial obligations, and long-term aftermath. A country can begin reconstruction while a larger crisis continues, provided this particular district can be protected and supplied.

The initial proposal names the state, the specific usable receiving location, its access route, and the work needed there. The player chooses a medical-return district or a broader civilian receiving district. The first prioritises existing patients and displaced residents with a connection to the place. The second can receive a larger mix when its actual housing and food capacity support them. Neither choice grants claims, cores, automatic compliance, or ownership of an occupied population.

The reference programme commits two civilian factories, three hundred Support Equipment, 2,000 fuel, and fifty Political Power over 210 days. These are the four cost types. The factory commitment must be the actual repair and establishment work budget. It must not also be counted as a separate idle reservation while another hidden factory bill pays for the same repairs.

The programme's first objective is to establish a supplied care and shelter endpoint. Its second is to restore the selected existing infrastructure through the native construction or repair owner. Its third is to obtain valid food-safety and reception evidence for the people it proposes to receive. These objectives can progress together. The timer alone never certifies success while the road is cut or food access remains unproven.

The proposed completed district can support up to five thousand additional reception places. That figure is a planning ceiling. The actual capacity is the least of usable shelter, safe access, food support, clinical service, and the migration owner's accepted capacity. A damaged district may complete only two thousand places and continue work later. The missing three thousand are not hidden people and do not receive any population credit.

Physical repairs remain real work. A destroyed factory, railway, or other structure cannot be recreated from a medical completion flag. The exact native reservation and repair consumer must be verified during implementation. Where the engine cannot connect the same committed factories to that work as intended, the construction contract remains unresolved. It is not replaced with free buildings or a generic national construction modifier.

Independent engineers can improve the rate of work they actually supervise under Part 7. They do not create new factories or reception places at appointment. A district's completed work persists when the engineer leaves. Remaining work loses the service-dependent rate if no qualified replacement exists. A reused team cannot accelerate several active sites simultaneously unless the existing assignment contract genuinely supplies that capacity.

### Recovery with existing owners

In Black Plague territory, the district must preserve the disease owner's Recovery or Cured state, actual remaining burden, relapse conditions, and measurable devastation. The existing Population Recovery Programme, vigilance work, inspection compact, and memorial charter remain their owner's programmes. Wu contributes separately identified clinical service or physical receiving work. The same reduction of devastation cannot be awarded once by each programme under different names.

In former zombie territory, reconstruction preserves the real history of occupation and the postwar survivor settlement. A clean district does not count as final global zombie defeat while another outbreak polity survives. The medical network can service a region before the shared final-defeat condition is met, but it cannot fire the defeat super-event or recreate a dismantled league to claim its rewards.

In a liberated cannibalism district, emergency relief, identification, institutional reconstruction, and trauma remain real stages. Medical treatment does not discharge legal obligations or delete witness records. A new settlement must not turn the people killed there into a numerical resource for breeding replacements. The district's new inhabitants are not evidence that the original loss has been undone.

Famine and migration remain independent. The food owner publishes actual food safety, including whether its projection is valid. An untracked state is not automatically safe. Migration owns the real movement, reception, integration, and return of people who already exist in a population system. A successful medical inspection cannot override a closed border, unavailable destination, or insufficient food.

### Scarcity and interruption

A completed reception place can be reserved for only one actual incoming obligation at a time. Survivors, migrants, patients, and new replicated people compete for the same physical capacity. The government can reserve a portion for a planned cohort, but the reserve is visible and can be released when a larger immediate need arises. That release does not delete people already born or create room that does not exist.

Losing a route pauses new admissions. People already living in the district remain where they actually are until a valid evacuation moves them. Food and medical demand do not disappear with the programme's decision category. The government can protect the district, distribute people among reachable places, or arrange an owner-approved evacuation. It cannot empty a threatened district through a refund.

Captured districts retain buildings, current people, damage, and real custody. The new controller does not inherit authority to alter the former sponsor's research or erase the disaster history. Humanitarian service can continue through a new agreement when safe access and staff permit it. A change of flag cannot restart the same construction award or duplicate the reception ceiling.

The lasting payoff is a district with usable physical services and an accepted place in the normal civilian systems. Wu may remain its leading physician, or local institutions may take over. An independently sustained district no longer needs a special permanent Wu modifier for every completed repair. Its real buildings and people continue to exist.

## L13: population restoration programme

L13 uses the existing finite replication mode to create new civilian lives for a viable receiving district. It does not replace R03's production, R07's care, common adult orientation, or the first-settlement contract. It supplies the missing national programme: where new people can live, how they are counted, what happens while they wait, and how they enter an ordinary society.

The first decision defines the programme's purpose and receiving capacity. A public charter can prioritise rebuilding a damaged community, establishing civilian independence, and preventing compulsory military or specialist service. A more restrictive government can seek binding service obligations, creating refusal, staff, legitimacy, and later political consequences. A charter never grants the government automatic ownership of a new person's memories or loyalty.

Research is available through actual replication and reconstruction prerequisites, even in Calm World. It does not require a catastrophic death threshold. A recorded disaster can make the project urgent and change its presentation, but the dead are never an input stock or a quota that may be restored. A country with a smaller real receiving need can operate a smaller programme.

Production uses Part 7's existing normal or accelerated cell. A normal cell has the same maximum of 250 people, the same 270-day production and protected-maturation schedule, and the same four cost types. The new programme adds no second factory reservation or clinical charge to that cell. Common adult orientation remains a separate 90-day requirement before independent reception and service. The accelerated route keeps its earlier limitations and follow-up burdens.

A programme may run several paid cells when the host has actual industrial, staffing, care, and receiving capacity. It cannot use a cloned director to exceed the institute's research-lane ceiling. Production is separate from research, but it still consumes its real factories and supplies. Every cell has its own output count, living milestone, destination reservation, and care responsibility.

### Before the first population settlement

A planned person is not a living person. Before the living milestone, the programme can cancel an unstarted or unfinished allocation under the original production contract. After that milestone, actual surviving people need care and food even while their first settlement remains pending.

Those unsettled people have not yet been added to the state's civilian population. Their real needs must nevertheless reach the food and care owners through an explicit external-demand contract. That contract identifies the nursery or actual custody location, living count, already provisioned care, current custodian, generation, revision, and the finite production owner. It is a proposed extension, not an existing helper assumed to be present.

The receiving food owner must not count the same people through both the external-demand projection and ordinary state population after settlement. The transition retires the matching unsettled projection when the population transaction commits. If settlement fails, the projection and care obligation remain with the actual people. A failed callback cannot make them disappear from demand or add them twice.

Movement before first settlement also needs a distinct contract. The cohort owner supplies the exact unsettled people and transport authority. A journey can move that custody and record actual losses. It must not debit a state's civilian population that never contained them. Only the surviving people admitted at the destination receive the one first civilian-population settlement. This is not permission to call an ordinary migration transfer with a fictitious origin population.

After first settlement, the normal population and migration owners take over. Later movement reduces the real origin population and adds survivors to the destination through the existing exact transfer. The finite replication record remains historical and cannot be redeemed again. Demobilisation, specialist recruitment, and a later Wu appointment similarly do not add another person.

### Shared receiving capacity

A five-thousand-place district might reserve three thousand places for returning residents, one thousand for other accepted migrants, and one thousand for four new replication cells. These allocations use one shared capacity, not three independent five-thousand-place pools. Unborn allocations can be reduced before the corresponding cells begin. Already living people retain their care obligation when a reservation is changed.

If two thousand five hundred returning residents arrive and one thousand migrants are accepted, the district has one thousand five hundred unoccupied places before the new cells settle. Receiving one thousand new people leaves five hundred places. None of those transfers adds the original displaced residents to the replication birth count. None of the births reduces the recorded deaths from the earlier disaster.

If only nine hundred of the one thousand new people survive to settlement, the district receives nine hundred. The other one hundred enter the appropriate real loss record once if their deaths occurred after the living milestone. They do not also reduce a destination population they never entered. Unused housing remains available according to the receiving owner rather than being awarded as a cash refund.

A 250-person cell still obeys Part 7's identity equation: new living people equal unsettled people plus historical first settlements plus deaths before settlement. After settlement, a later death does not lower the historical first-settlement term and reopen entitlement. Population restoration is a new production and reception effort, not a reversible expenditure against a death total.

### Civilian independence and work

Common orientation prepares people for ordinary life, language, communication, and work. It does not automatically make them surgeons, pilots, researchers, soldiers, or loyal political followers. Those roles retain their own training and appointment rules. A person who refuses a proposed profession remains a living civilian, not a defective unit that can be deleted.

The programme can help new residents enter existing work, but it does not grant a factory, resource deposit, research slot, or instant veteran workforce for each batch. Actual industrial and agricultural capacity continues to determine employment and supply. L12 can rebuild physical services while L13 creates new residents, with separate paid work and no doubled completion reward.

New citizens remain ordinary living humans for shared civilian classification. A replicated mayor, a continuity copy of Wu, or a clone-derived workforce does not make the whole state nonhuman. The government cannot switch classification to escape famine, migration, or medical obligations. Any genuinely different later political or biological transformation belongs to its own accepted route and proof.

The public programme can record family and community ties that develop after arrival without pretending that imperfect copied memories establish old legal relationships. A copied specialist may remember a place that no longer exists. The charter must allow a new legal identity and independent future. Neither inheritance, marriage, office, military rank, nor property passes automatically from the original person.

A coercive settlement may create resistance, refusal, emigration pressure, or exposure through the existing owners. It cannot receive automatic cores or compliance from manufacturing a population. Medical capability does not establish sovereignty over the state or erase the rights of surviving inhabitants. The later political parts may develop those conflicts, but their outcomes cannot be pre-awarded here.

### Completion and closure

The programme's successful endpoint is a group of actual new residents who have completed reception and whose ordinary care and food systems can support them. The institution retains knowledge and authentic programme history. Its active replication service can close while those people continue ordinary life.

A failed programme can stop future orders, narrow its intake, seek another receiving district, or transfer unfinished living cohorts through an accepted custody contract. It cannot cancel lives as though cancelling an unbuilt factory. Closure must account for every living cohort, patient, reserved place, and actual outstanding supply allocation.

Population growth after ordinary settlement belongs to the ordinary population owner. There is no permanent Wu manpower dividend for the same people, no civilian-plus-military double credit, and no legacy clone-stock reward. D03 remains the required proposed shared-owner extension. Until its exact birth, custody, food-demand, and settlement boundaries are implemented and accepted, this programme is a designed feature with an explicit dependency.

## L14: functional longevity

The anti-aging proposal concerns the continued function of an existing living person. It extends M09 and M10 into repeated assessment and sustained care. It does not create a replacement body, copy memories, revive someone already dead, or reset the historical age and identity of a character.

A qualifying case needs an actual biological decline that the character or specialist owner exposes as treatable. A famous name, old portrait, or approaching scripted event is not enough. The owner's political, military, and narrative rules remain separate. Wu cannot cancel an execution, a battle death, an unrelated assassination, or an owner-mandated departure by declaring every cause of death medical.

The project can be completed from a supported clinical reference in Calm World. A player need not wait for the leader to become incapacitated before developing the capability. Actual treatment nevertheless needs a living patient, a real clinical need, consent or appropriate care authority, and a supplied institution. The government must decide whether the person's continued work justifies the commitment.

The reference treatment commits the actual patient, two civilian factories, sixty Clinical Material Lots, and one hundred Support Equipment. It takes 120 days and includes a 365-day follow-up allocation. These are four cost types. The clinical lots are reserved for that patient and consumed over the actual course and follow-up. They cannot also be spent on another copy, hospital, or preservation programme.

The patient cannot perform duties that their treatment state genuinely prevents. A country's own leadership system handles any temporary acting authority. A researcher loses their service contribution while unavailable. The institute does not create a duplicate officeholder to cover the absence. A second trained specialist can take over an assignment through the normal role system, with their own identity and current capacity.

A successful course restores or preserves the supported functional condition and gives the owner a dated period of continuing medical support. It does not promise immortality or remove every future health check. Each later continuation needs a current assessment and the remaining care conditions. Completing a fresh course does not stack several future lifetimes or rewrite earlier medical history.

Partial success can maintain life while requiring reduced duties or more specialised care. A failed course can leave the patient at their previous condition with consumed resources and a need for another supported treatment. An actual harmful outcome is attributed to the real case and recorded once. The system must never invent a fatal result simply to balance a successful longevity programme elsewhere.

A public institute may prioritise irreplaceable medical and technical skill while declining a ruler's demand for exclusive access. A classified institute may reserve the treatment for senior officials, creating dependence through actual patient custody and supply. A government can publish the standard when it has reliable independent replication of the medical result. Publication does not create global treatment coverage.

Wu can undergo the same service when he has a supported living condition. His original identity remains distinct from any continuity copy. A healthy original does not make the qualified copies disappear, and a treated copy does not become the original. Changes to authority, succession, office, and personal claims continue through their own records.

Presentation should show retained ability, changed daily work, and the burden of continuing care. It should not use a youthful replacement face as proof that time has been reversed. Any authorised portrait update preserves the actual character identity and source rules. No new advisor-card family is inferred from adding this treatment.

## L15: appetite and behavioural care

L15 treats an owner-identified biological appetite or aggression problem in a specific living group. It builds on rehabilitation and the relevant ordinary or nonhuman medical foundation. It is distinct from military obedience, political ideology, command authority, hunger caused by a lack of food, and the deliberate conduct of a criminal organisation.

A human formation can qualify when a previous enhancement or medical incident has produced a treatable condition. A compatible nonhuman group can qualify through its own clinical owner. An engineered zombie lineage can receive its existing maintenance or shutdown treatment when that owner authorises it, but the project does not turn dead troops into living patients. Each route retains its actual source and limitations.

The reference clinical course commits the affected group, one civilian factory, twenty Clinical Material Lots, and one hundred Support Equipment over 90 days. The bounded group and its scaling are supplied by the owner. A full formation is not assumed equivalent to twenty patients. The complete quote retains four cost types, and the patient commitment prevents the group from claiming full normal service during treatment.

At full supported coverage, the proposed course can reduce up to half of the owner's diagnosed medical aggression or appetite-pressure component. It does not halve the entire event's crisis meter. A condition with no exposed medical component has no such reduction. The remaining pressure can still require food, rest, command changes, legal accountability, or another event-owned response.

For Event 014, Field Hunger is a material supply problem, and organised predation includes command, political, and network causes. L15 cannot create Larder Stores, replace ordinary rations, erase Cult Cohesion, dismantle a network, or absolve perpetrators. The medical team can treat compatible harm in living patients and support a real rotation or rehabilitation. The Event 014 owner continues to decide whether local or global containment has actually been achieved.

A patient who regains judgment may refuse a previous duty. That refusal is not proof that treatment failed. The government can return the patient to an appropriate role, arrange civilian rehabilitation, or pursue an independently grounded legal process. It cannot receive a loyalty bonus for every treated person or use recovery as a covert programme of automatic political conversion.

The classified route can attempt to turn care into coercion. Its consequences must follow the concrete institutional choice: withheld discharge, disputed orders, staff refusal, exposure, or a patient group that leaves when custody breaks. No real coercive treatment procedure is described. The gameplay does not award a guaranteed obedient population from a medical button.

Continuing care is justified only by the actual condition. A recovered ordinary patient can leave the specialist programme and remain in normal medicine. Permanently altered veterans retain their earlier care needs. Ending an appetite course does not remove Part 6's permanent alteration or supply dependency. A separate clinical purpose may coexist, but it cannot bill the same care twice.

The report should identify the affected people, the improvement they experience, and any continuing limitation. Serious harm and atrocities receive a serious tone. Humour can address the institution's self-serving language, never the suffering of patients or victims. The project's useful endpoint is a safer living group with a viable future, not a magically loyal army.

## Deployment under changing conditions

### Healthy periods and early access

The institute remains useful when no national epidemic exists. It can qualify a fixed filter, train a handling team, conduct a contained agricultural case, prepare a service animal design, or build a receiving district around a real reconstruction need. These activities preserve their actual scale. They do not create a national disease, a rat country, a zombie outbreak, a clone scenario, or an event-history entry for a source that has not fired.

A source-specific design still needs its actual source. A healthy host cannot study a Rat King's unique biology merely because Black Plague exists in the catalogue. A compatible ordinary plague-vector case can support ordinary vector defence. The Rat King's later independent case needs its owner-defined evidence. This gives earlier research a useful role without making it an omniscient unlock for every future organism.

The player can select an eligible project directly through the existing proposal surface. Context changes proposal priority, not the existence of a lawful research path. A public government can approve a military exception. A classified government can publish a safe maintenance standard. Neither needs to raise Chaos or surrender a fixed amount of Authority to make the project technically possible.

### Regional service choices

An industrial port can support fixed filters and reception medicine but still depend on a contested sea route. An inland farming region can benefit from crop treatment and lack the staff to operate a complex living observation station. A mountain front can justify support animals despite their poor road speed. A ruined urban basin can need mobile cleanup and reconstruction before any incoming population is safe.

These differences come from current territory, facilities, supply, food, damage, and people. They are not national stereotypes or fixed bonuses assigned to a country's tag. A technologically advanced state can still fail an isolated deployment. A small public institute can succeed with a narrow local service and a genuine partner.

No single site should offer every service at maximum strength by default. A works site can change its active standard, but it must finish or withdraw existing obligations before committing the same staff and equipment elsewhere. The new work is not an excuse to multiply factories, clinical stock, or handler teams through a renamed facility.

### Knowledge, patents, and service agreements

A partner can receive a finished product, a qualified service, a maintenance licence, or the full reproducible design. Those are separate transfers. Part 4 owns the general commercial and humanitarian terms. This part adds the physical and clinical facts that a designed-life agreement must carry.

A product shipment transfers only the verified physical stock that arrived. A maintenance licence supplies the documented operating knowledge. A full design transfer permits future reproduction only when the receiving institution has the actual production and care prerequisites. None of these transactions transfers Wu, a clone, Kruger, trained staff, or a complete military package automatically.

A publisher can preserve its own learned standard after sharing it. A patent dispute can restrict future legal access without erasing knowledge already authentically learned. A government that withdraws a licence does not remotely kill foreign animals or delete a partner's filter. A real service dependency can interrupt future supplies, which produces a medical or maintenance problem through the existing local contract.

Emergency aid should remain usable without forcing a recipient into a faction or a new event-owned country. A coercive commercial demand can create political consequences through Part 4 and the later foreign part. The service cannot use the ordinary disease owner to award unrelated diplomatic concessions.

### Capture and conflicting custody

Capturing a site can yield equipment, intact living stock, damaged assemblies, patients, and incomplete records. Each category has its own consequence. Intact equipment does not prove learned science. A living patient does not become property. A missing animal is not a confirmed death. Records can be evidence of earlier conduct even when the machine that produced them no longer works.

The original owner loses only what actually changed custody. The receiving owner gains only the verified remaining stock or people. A transfer closes or moves the matching assignment before another deployment becomes eligible. The old site cannot continue claiming protection from units that now serve the captor.

A competing custody claim opens an account of the actual disputed stock and service. The institute may preserve care while the dispute is resolved. It cannot solve the dispute by duplicating the organisms or by assigning two simultaneous legal owners to the same finite person. Unknown provenance remains non-redeemable until the relevant owner can establish it.

Captured or abandoned human cohorts retain D03's first-settlement history. Captured animals retain their own production and loss history. Neither becomes legacy clone stock. A later recovery does not recreate resources already consumed by the enemy or reset an existing population loss.

### Failure, containment, and repair

The ordinary failure families are loss of service, care shortage, incompatibility, handling failure, and unauthorised use. They should arise from the actual design and current assignment. A filter failure primarily exposes its served endpoint. A missed reconnaissance return primarily loses information and risks its team. A feeding failure threatens the real animal group and the surrounding district. The entire world does not receive an identical disaster from every failed device.

The player receives an actionable account of what remains controllable: the supplied site, the missing team, the affected generation, or the route that has become unsafe. Useful responses include narrowing coverage, moving intact stock, restoring a route, funding a supported repair, withdrawing the assignment, and preserving living recipients. A response should not be a repeated political-power purchase that simply lowers a hidden danger number.

Rare escalation requires concrete state. An escaped organism needs an actual source and escape. A covert programme needs a real earlier order and custody. A hostile autonomous group needs an owner-supported transition and the people or organisms that actually compose it. High Chaos can increase the relevance or danger of these situations through the later evolutions, but it cannot invent the missing material history.

A successful containment response retires only the affected live service or escaped group. It preserves research, the original incident, recorded deaths, public evidence, and the correct responsibility. A repair cannot remove contamination from a different source. If Air Cleanliness changes because an accepted biological footprint genuinely shrinks, the shared owner records that actual change once.

### Departure and independent operation

A functioning public standard can survive Wu's departure through the staff and records that actually exist. A novel adaptation still needs a qualified Wu or another properly developed capability. A continuity copy must meet Part 7's qualification rules before supplying the unique medical role. Possessing the copy's body or portrait does not qualify it.

A departing Wu cannot automatically take every animal, machine, hospital, patient, and publicly owned record. Physical property, people, institutional knowledge, and private methods follow their actual ownership and transfer rules. Several qualified Wus can operate separate real institutions, but they do not multiply one national medical package or a single site's service capacity.

Closing a programme stops future orders first. Existing living commitments enter care, discharge, transfer, or a supported retirement. The closure can succeed while long-term human care continues through ordinary institutions. It cannot be declared complete by hiding the decision category and leaving unaccounted living groups behind.

## Player presentation and artificial intelligence

### Current choices, not a second management game
