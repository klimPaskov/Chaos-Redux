# Doctor Wu: military medicine and controlled biology

## From protective medicine to selected formations

The army can keep Wu's work within ordinary field medicine. Hospitals become better at saving wounded people, treating infection, and returning surviving personnel to duty through the medical services established in Part 5. That is a complete military use of the institute. The government does not have to alter its soldiers or maintain engineered creatures to receive useful wartime medicine.

The next choice concerns a smaller force. Wu offers to keep selected troops moving longer, help them recover between engagements, and eventually give them physical abilities that ordinary training cannot produce. Those formations require a continuing service behind them. Their advantage has to remain visible in the army the player fields, the production capacity they reserve, the units they rotate out of action, and the care owed to people who cannot safely leave the programme immediately.

Controlled reanimated organisms form a separate branch. Their development needs a valid source of reanimated material and a functioning containment service. A cure for a living infected patient supplies clinical knowledge, but it does not turn that patient into a recruitable zombie. The government must separately authorize an engineered-organism programme and establish who commands its formations, who maintains their control standard, and who can order their withdrawal.

All the projects in this part belong to baseline research. A supervised public institute can complete them in Calm World when it has the necessary knowledge, material, facilities, and explicit permissions. Wu Authority changes his discretion and the government's ability to enforce a restriction. It never substitutes for a scientific prerequisite. The four later evolutions can change the scale, conduct, and political consequences of these programmes without creating the technologies themselves.

The labels below are design working labels. All new costs, durations, strengths, and numerical thresholds are proposed tuning. The projects and formations have not been implemented or tested. The medical, institutional, and production systems in Parts 1–5 retain their existing draft values unless an explicit rule here identifies a new military application.

## A programme follows its people

### Enrolment and formation identity

Endurance and temporary combat enhancement apply to selected, identifiable human land formations. The country chooses an actual formation, its responsible medical service, and its recovery location. Completion of a research project does not enrol the whole army, modify every infantry category, or apply an invisible national attack bonus.

The normal first candidates are human infantry formations whose personnel and support can be identified. Ordinary motorized transport remains possible, but treatment cannot increase a vehicle's mechanical speed, fuel supply, armor, or carrying capacity. Mixed formations need a verified way to restrict the effect to eligible personnel. Until that exists, the selection interface must explain why the mixed formation cannot enter that particular course. This does not remove its ordinary medical support.

Use the formation's full authorized eligible strength to reserve care capacity. Understrength units do not purchase a cheap course and then reinforce to full strength under the same small obligation. Actual delivery and outcomes still follow the living people present. A proposed accounting block represents up to 1,000 authorized human personnel. A final partial block reserves a complete block of service, with the exact displayed total calculated before commitment. This is a game service unit, not a drug quantity or a claim about medical practice.

Record the formation identity, enrolled strength ceiling, course standard, care payer, medical custodian, depot, recovery location, and current phase together. Renaming a division changes its label, not its entitlement. Splitting or merging formations divides or joins the existing obligation without duplicating a course. A larger merged formation needs enough additional coverage before the enlarged force receives the full result. Repeatedly editing a template must not renew the tour, remove accumulated strain, or refill its equipment.

A replacement entering an enrolled formation consumes an unused place in its authorized programme. It does not inherit completed rehabilitation or an extended tour allowance merely because the division name is familiar. When losses reduce the actual force, the country can close surplus future capacity at the next renewal. It cannot retrospectively refund care already delivered.

### Three distinct relationships

Operational command belongs to the army authority that issues lawful orders. Medical custody belongs to the service responsible for treatment and recovery. Research ownership describes who retains the method and can produce a new standard. These relationships may be held by different institutions.

A public institute can train army medical teams and distribute known standards while the ordinary military chain of command remains intact. A military institute can keep methods classified without giving Wu personal command of the troops. An institute-run force requires a separate political concession that actually assigns command posts, staff loyalty, or exclusive control of essential services to Wu.

Medical dependence does not prove political loyalty. A formation can obey the government and still become medically unfit when its supplies fail. It can be fully supplied and refuse an unlawful order because its officers reject the programme's command settlement. The presentation should identify the actual cause instead of treating every interruption as a rebellion.

### Care status without another national meter

The selected formation displays one qualitative care state: Supported, Stretched, Interrupted, or In Clinical Withdrawal. It also shows the course end date, the next funded service interval, and the named care location where those facts change the player's next action. These are records attached to a selected formation, not additional permanent national pressure meters.

Supported means that the current service interval has been paid, the required staff and records are available, and the formation can receive the service. Stretched means that an identifiable requirement has become unreliable, such as a route losing access or a replacement team not arriving. Interrupted means the service cannot reach the formation. Clinical Withdrawal means the country is actively ending a temporary course through funded recovery.

A nominally full national stockpile does not make an isolated formation Supported. Conversely, a temporary loss of contact does not erase supplies already delivered to it. The remaining funded interval gives the player time to reconnect the route, rotate the unit, or arrange a genuine local service. The same physical pack cannot count simultaneously as stock at the national depot and as delivered cover at the front.

## The enhancement research path

Advanced Field Medicine, M05, remains the entry foundation. The new work does not award M05 again. M01 provides independent clinical teams, M06 provides rehabilitation, and M07–M09 provide the existing tissue, organ, and regeneration foundations.

The path has a reversible service branch and a permanent alteration branch:

```text
M05 + M01 -> H01 Endurance course
H01 + M06 -> H02 Rotation and withdrawal service
H01 + H02 + M07 -> H03 Combat enhancement course
H03 + M08 -> H04 Long-service care
H03 + H04 + M08 + M09 -> H05 Permanent alteration
H05 + M06 -> H06 Enhanced veteran settlement
(H02 OR C02) + M01 -> H07 Independent service archive
```

H06 is the institutional settlement for a completed permanent programme. It is not another scientific barrier placed in front of the first patient's treatment. A country must already fund continuing care before H05 begins. H06 gives that obligation a stable peacetime home.

Every research action below occupies one existing research lane. The lane, civilian-factory commitment, support equipment, and the stated political power or army experience are its four spendable cost types. Existing facilities, qualified teams, documented source material, and an approved participant group are requirements. They are not also removed as undeclared payments. A military mandate can change preparation time through the existing institute rules, but it does not receive a second completion reward.

### H01. Endurance course

The first proposal concerns an army that is losing useful service time to exhaustion and slow recovery. It may appear during a sustained war, a difficult retreat, or a peacetime training programme that has a real military sponsor. An active war is not required. Wu can develop the method under a limited, supervised training mandate.

The government approves a named pilot cohort and a recovery site before work starts. Research produces a known course standard and the ability to manufacture military care packs. It does not place packs in storage or enrol a division for free. A government without a working supply arrangement can finish the research and retain it until an appropriate formation is ready.

An enrolled formation receives a substantial sustained-movement and recovery advantage while Supported. The proposed full-service target is 20 percent better eligible foot movement and 25 percent faster organization recovery, accompanied by 10 percent greater supply demand. The result contains no attack bonus and provides no automatic protection against every disease. Any disease protection comes from a compatible treatment already included in its medical service.

H01 includes the conservative completion and withdrawal method for its own first course under qualified institute care. H02 later makes repeated rotation and independent withdrawal service more resilient. The first enrolment cannot proceed without the H01 recovery method and its funded cover.

The ordinary field tour lasts 90 days. The country then rotates the formation into 30 days of recovery before beginning another ordinary tour. The formation remains an army formation throughout. Its people, equipment, losses, and experience are not recreated at either boundary.

The first use should show the formation completing a demanding task with fewer exhausted personnel, followed by the medical staff's request for the promised rest period. The player chooses whether to keep the original rotation, replace the formation with an ordinary unit, or authorize one emergency extension. There is no automatic increase to Wu Authority for using a supervised treatment as agreed.

### H02. Rotation and withdrawal service

H02 gives temporary enhancement a dependable exit. It establishes a recovery schedule, trained replacement teams, and a way to end a course without leaving the patient group dependent on Wu's personal presence. It is especially valuable to a public institute and to a military host fighting far from the main laboratory.

A completed service can receive an enrolled formation at a valid recovery location, remove the temporary enhancement over its recorded recovery interval, and return surviving personnel to ordinary duty. Removing the enhancement is not a new casualty transaction. Any genuine illness or injury discovered during recovery belongs to the military or medical owner that already tracks the affected living personnel.

The country can use a central recovery service or train distributed army teams. The central arrangement uses existing facilities more efficiently but gives the force a single destination to protect. The distributed arrangement requires H07 or the equivalent approved archive work before it can independently maintain every standard. It makes ordinary rotation possible closer to a remote front and reduces reliance on Wu's personal permission.

Missing a scheduled rotation first creates Stretched care and a clearly dated decision. It does not immediately turn a loyal unit hostile. A funded recovery programme returns the unit to ordinary service. Continuing to deploy it beyond its safe interval creates a specific medical case and an investigation obligation. The player cannot clear that obligation by renaming the unit or changing research direction.

### H03. Combat enhancement course

H03 is a separate authorization to improve combat performance. It draws on the earlier endurance and recovery work, but its effects and risks are sufficiently different that an endurance mandate cannot silently authorize it.

A completed project offers a powerful temporary package to a selected force. The proposed Supported target is 25 percent greater soft attack, 20 percent greater breakthrough, 15 percent better eligible foot movement, and 20 percent faster organization recovery. Supply demand rises by 20 percent. These values replace the endurance package for that formation. They do not stack with a second endurance course.

The country must fund a 60-day field tour and a 45-day recovery interval when it enrols the formation. It can employ the force in an offensive or hold it as a reserve for a threatened front. It cannot keep renewing a cheap flag each month to avoid the recovery commitment.

The principal failures concern impaired judgment, aggression, withdrawal, and serious medical harm. They are fictional programme outcomes, not descriptions of real substances. Early problems should produce observable consequences in the affected formation, such as a commander requesting a rotation, a team reporting unfit personnel, or an order becoming disputed. The government can withdraw the formation, replace its medical team, or continue under an explicit emergency concession.

A supported, reviewed course should normally deliver its promised advantage. The ordinary result must not be a disguised random punishment for selecting military research. Severe outcomes become more likely through a documented combination of overextension, failed care, concealed adverse results, or incompatible additions. The exact weighted model remains an implementation and probability-review task.

### H04. Long-service care

H04 responds to the people who cannot safely return to ordinary service at the end of a temporary tour. It builds a continuing military-clinical service and informs the permanent alteration proposal. It does not itself alter anyone permanently.

The government chooses whether the continuing service belongs to the ordinary army medical system, the public institute, or an exclusive institute command. This choice assigns an actual custodian. The ordinary army and public routes preserve government oversight. Exclusive institute custody increases Wu's authority only when he receives real control over budgets, records, appointments, or access to patients.

The completed service can stabilize a living affected cohort, fund a longer rehabilitation case, or support a medically cleared return to limited service. Return to duty follows the surviving personnel actually recorded by the owner. It never converts an estimated number of avoided deaths into recruitable manpower.

The programme creates a meaningful allocation conflict during a major outbreak. The government can reserve enough service for existing military patients, borrow unused hospital capacity through the established medical network, or pause new combat courses. It cannot resolve the shortage by ending all care as part of an interface cleanup.

### H05. Permanent alteration

Permanent alteration requires the long-service service, successful reconstructive work, and an explicit government decision about the people entering it. The ordinary supervised route uses an identified volunteer or otherwise owner-approved participant group, independent review, and continuing care. Prisoners, refugees, and patients receiving emergency aid do not become available participants through possession of a state, a camp, or a medical corridor.

A coercive government can seek a coercive policy through the event's later political and misconduct systems. That is a distinct action with recorded responsibility and consequences. It is not the cheapest default research input and is not required to reach the scientific result in Calm World.

Research completion creates the permanent enhanced-human family and its production and training contract. It does not convert every enrolled temporary-course soldier or produce a free starting army. The government can begin a small formation, improve the support for an existing one, or retain the capability without fielding it.

The proposed defining advantages over an ordinary comparable human infantry body are 35 percent greater soft attack, 30 percent greater breakthrough, 20 percent greater organization, 25 percent faster organization recovery, and 15 percent better eligible foot movement. Supply demand rises by 25 percent. The family has no automatic armor value, immunity to conventional weapons, freedom from food, or ability to recover recorded dead personnel.

Permanent alteration replaces temporary combat and endurance packages on the same people. It does not combine all three tables. Additional ordinary medical care remains useful when it treats an actual distinct problem. A later compatible clone refinement or designed-life trait requires its own explicit compatibility result.

The altered people retain their history and legal identity. They can remain loyal soldiers, become organized veterans, demand better care, or support Wu after a genuine political struggle. The act of alteration does not make the host a nonhuman country and does not create an unavoidable private army.

### H06. Enhanced veteran settlement

The first permanent formation creates a future civilian question. The country must decide where its surviving members receive care when they retire, how ordinary institutions recognize them, and which authority can represent their needs.

H06 offers three continuing settlements. A public veteran service disperses care through accountable clinics. A military reserve service retains specialist units with a reduced operational obligation and a path back to duty. An institute settlement concentrates housing, care, and employment around Wu's facilities. Each settlement uses actual supported locations and retains an ongoing service bill.

The public route reduces exclusive dependence when records and staff really move into independent institutions. The military route preserves a useful reserve but keeps personnel and care capacity committed. The institute route can provide the quickest continuity when the institute already holds the necessary facilities, while concentrating the people and services that may later support Wu's political power.

The first settlement should resolve an actual retiring cohort. Completing H06 without any veterans creates the capability, not a population, a settlement state, a national spirit stack, or a free manpower reserve. A later government can reform the settlement through a paid transfer of care. It cannot erase the residents by closing the programme.

### H07. Independent service archive

H07 separates a known medical or control standard from Wu's continued personal availability. It uses trained teams, usable records, and an independently held production standard. A pile of stolen papers does not satisfy all three conditions.

The project can begin after H02 for human military care or after C02 for engineered-organism control. Each completed application covers the standards actually reviewed. It is not a universal entitlement to every future Wu method. A new standard needs a bounded adaptation before the independent teams can maintain it.

The host can establish one independent archive at home or a treaty-backed archive held by a real partner. The foreign arrangement gives the host redundancy and creates a diplomatic relationship with an actual service obligation. It does not grant the partner Wu, his unresearched projects, the host's army, or a new source-event firing.

Wu's death, capture, or departure leaves an independently maintained known standard usable while its staff, supplies, and facilities survive. New research and unfamiliar failures still lose his unique contribution. This gives a supervised government a practical reason to distribute expertise without making Wu's presence irrelevant.

## Paying for a force that stays useful

### Three physical military service products

Part 4 continues to own ordinary civilian treatment production and delivery. Military service uses three distinct product roles so that a civilian cure allocation is not silently spent as combat enhancement or as control supplies.

A Military Care Pack supports a known temporary or permanent human programme. A Control Service Pack supports the registered command standard of a reanimated lineage. An Alteration Preparation Pack is consumed by the paid admission process for a permanently enhanced human cohort. These are fictional equipment and service abstractions. They specify no substance, composition, laboratory process, or real medical quantity.

Each product must resolve to a real owner-managed stock item or verified equivalent physical transaction. Its identity includes the applicable standard when incompatible standards must not be interchangeable. A second numerical stockpile beside the actual equipment is forbidden. The final concrete tokens and supported variant handling remain implementation work.

Proposed production orders use the institute's existing production network:

| Order | Completed physical output | Work interval | Civilian factories occupied | Support equipment spent |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Military service order | 100 Military Care Packs | 30 days | 1 | 100 |
| Control service order | 100 Control Service Packs | 45 days | 2 | 150 |
| Alteration preparation order | 10 Alteration Preparation Packs | 90 days | 2 | 250 |

These orders use two spendable cost types, committed factories and spent support equipment. Access to a functioning, authorized production site is a requirement. There is no additional research-lane payment for manufacturing a known standard. The orders represent alternative uses of real production capacity and cannot occupy the same factory twice.

The output is delivered only on completed work. Cancellation releases the actual reservation and returns only unspent material according to the chosen production contract. It does not award the completed packs, refund factories as new buildings, or convert unfinished work into manpower. An approved continuing order can repeat while its real costs remain affordable. The player sees and can stop that recurring commitment.

### Service demand

Demand follows authorized force size. The following table is the proposed bill for one 30-day service interval. Human rows use one care block of up to 1,000 authorized personnel. Reanimated rows use one full lineage battalion with the body and handler composition defined later.

| Temporary combat enhancement | 10 Military Care Packs | Intensive course and observation |

A service renewal consumes the applicable packs. Temporary-course recipients continue their existing external service assignment, while permanent and reanimated formations use their already paid embedded teams. It is not a free national modifier. Packs leave available stock when allocated into the existing physical delivery transaction. An initial commitment funds the complete tour and recovery, but it does not prove that every future interval has already been delivered. Undelivered allocations remain at their recorded shipment or service location and can be delayed or lost. Only delivery to the applicable service creates Supported cover for its dated interval, without a second payment. The same packs cannot remain spendable stock and also fund a shipment. Renaming, trading, or retagging a formation cannot give delivered cover back to the sender.

Endurance enrolment requires the first 90-day tour plus its 30-day recovery cover to be available. Under the proposed demand table this is 20 Military Care Packs per care block. Combat enrolment requires a 60-day tour at its ordinary rate plus 45 days of recovery at 5 packs per 30 days, rounded upward to a multiple of five once for the complete enrolled cohort. For ten care blocks, the resulting commitment is 275 Military Care Packs. The recovery part is a funded obligation, not a later surprise charge.

The player may authorize recurring renewal for established permanent units and known control standards. The service first protects existing patients and live formations. New enrolment cannot spend already committed recovery stock. When supply is inadequate, the country must reduce new commitments, relocate a service, arrange a real partner contribution, or accept an explicit emergency allocation. It does not quietly deprive civilian hospitals of their separately committed treatment.

### Capacity and routes

A staffed external military service team can support a proposed five temporary-course care blocks. These teams are formed through the existing institute staffing system and assigned to one service location at a time. They are not spawned each time an ordinary division requests treatment. Permanent enhanced and reanimated formations instead include their own embedded medical or handler teams in the admission manpower contract. They do not also reserve an external team's slots as a fifth admission cost. A functioning archive and production service are non-consumed requirements for those embedded teams. The production site's standing civilian staff remain within its existing institutional commitment.

A country can expand by funding more real teams and sites, not by raising Wu Authority. Independent services and foreign partners can provide the same ordinary capacity once their standards are compatible. The government may keep a small reliable force or accept the industrial burden of a larger one.

Domestic land access, overseas transport, occupation permission, and local service readiness follow the established delivery owner. A remote island depot is a useful reserve only when packs have actually reached it. Capturing a laboratory can interrupt production without destroying packs already delivered elsewhere. A blockade can prevent new deliveries without making the archive forget the method.

Native unit supply remains separate from specialist service. A fully Supported formation with no food, fuel, ammunition, or conventional supply still suffers the appropriate ordinary penalties. Specialist care never creates native supply, transports a formation, or grants military access.

### The commitment decision

Before enrolment, the player sees the selected force, the full initial service bill, the care location, the ordinary tour and recovery dates, and the visible consequence of interruption. The initial action consumes the packs and commits one available service assignment. It has no added political-power, experience, fuel, convoy, or manpower debit. Conventional deployment and transport continue through their actual owners.

A government may pay a separate, genuinely political action to change who controls the service. That action changes the institution and remains meaningful without an immediate enrolment. It must not be inserted as a mandatory extra payment merely to split a five-cost admission into several buttons.

Funding an existing force's care is distinct from purchasing a new formation. The interface separates the two choices. A player should never discover that a care renewal silently raised another division or that a recruitment request left its first recovery interval unfunded.

## Tours, interruptions, and permanent service

### Temporary performance is earned and temporary

A Supported endurance formation receives its course advantage for the recorded tour. If its next delivery becomes doubtful, the player can continue on supplies already delivered, move the formation toward a supported depot, or begin an early withdrawal. Early withdrawal uses the recovery stock already reserved for that cohort. It does not require buying the same recovery twice.

A Supported combat-enhancement formation follows the same principle, with its shorter tour and longer recovery obligation. It cannot change to the endurance label to extend the tour. The more demanding course remains recorded until its recovery has finished.

An emergency extension is available only when the formation is involved in a genuine threatened operation and still has usable care. The proposed extension is 30 days, once per tour. It consumes another full interval of the current course's packs and commits the service team for that interval. It postpones the existing recovery date without cancelling recovery. A second extension is unavailable until the formation has completed the required recovery.

The extension adds a medical review when the formation returns. Its possible results are ordinary recovery, extended rehabilitation, or a persistent care case. The result depends on the recorded tour, support interruptions, and actual adverse incidents. The review does not roll an unrelated massacre, automatic defection, or new disease merely to make the choice appear dangerous.

### A broken service has a readable progression

A missed delivery changes the formation to Stretched when its remaining cover cannot support the planned next interval. The enhanced advantage applies only to the service still delivered. The exact engine representation of partial cover must be proved during implementation, especially where a single formation contains eligible and ineligible personnel.

Once delivered cover ends, the temporary advantage stops. The formation enters Interrupted care and receives the programme-specific impairment for the living enrolled force. Proposed interruption anchors are a 15 percent organization-recovery penalty for endurance and a 25 percent organization-recovery plus 15 percent attack penalty for combat enhancement. These are temporary medical states, not new base statistics for every unit of that type.

Re-establishing delivery begins stabilization. It does not reset the tour and immediately award a fresh full course. The country must bring the affected unit through its remaining recovery before another ordinary tour. The proposed minimum supported stabilization interval is 15 days for endurance and 30 days for combat enhancement, followed by any recovery still owed. These intervals are fictional gameplay pacing.

If a force is already in combat, the player can order its normal retreat or move another formation into its place. The medical interface cannot teleport it away, remove hostile control of a route, or pretend that an encirclement has ended. An interrupted formation may remain militarily useful, but the country has a visible reason to relieve it.

Serious medical harm is a separate outcome supported by an actual incident record. It can remove living personnel from service, create a patient group, or record deaths when the owner confirms deaths. A generic support penalty cannot also debit a guessed share of the country's population. Existing engine casualties and event-attributed medical deaths must never be charged twice.

### Admission to the permanent family

Permanent enhanced infantry enters through an owner-controlled admission and training process. One proposed battalion contains 1,000 living personnel, including its embedded medical staff, and requires 90 infantry equipment, 5 Alteration Preparation Packs, and the first 30 days of active care at 15 Military Care Packs. Manpower and the three equipment roles are the four spendable cost types. The first care interval consumes its packs into the service obligation. It is not also listed as unused national stock.

A five-battalion formation therefore needs 5,000 manpower, 450 infantry equipment, 25 Alteration Preparation Packs, and 75 Military Care Packs before admission can commit. Its embedded medical staff provide the formation service and are already included in the 5,000 manpower. A functioning archive, production access, a valid training location, and a government-approved command settlement are non-consumed requirements. No separate service-slot reservation is added as a fifth admission cost. The proposed initial training interval is 120 days.

The base admission produces a new formation from the manpower actually supplied. Its personnel are not additionally added to state population. An optional conversion of an existing volunteer formation must conserve its living personnel, surviving equipment, losses, and experience through a verified conversion method. Where exact conversion cannot be proved, that conversion action remains unavailable and specifically blocked. The implementation must not secretly replace an experienced damaged division with a fresh one. The paid new-recruit route remains a separate valid design route.

The permanent family is initially admission-controlled. Its subunit cannot be inserted into an ordinary template to bypass preparation, care, or training. Additional admissions remain possible whenever the country can sustain them. There is no lifetime cap on enhanced people and no increase in admission capacity from a high Authority score. A later native recruitment integration is acceptable only when it preserves the full admission and continuing-care contract.

### Service after the institute closes

Closing a laboratory ends its new admissions and research. It does not reverse the physical alteration of people already living in the country. The host must retain a public or military service, transfer patients with real consent and reception capacity, or accept an unresolved care crisis.

An active permanent formation with interrupted care loses the part of its advantage that depends on current support and becomes medically unready. Some defining physical difference remains. The proposed unsupported comparison retains a 15 percent soft-attack advantage but imposes 25 percent lower organization recovery and 20 percent greater supply demand. It receives no automatic renewal, free organs, or self-sustaining population growth. Exact modifier composition must avoid stacking the Supported and unsupported packages.

The government can place the unit in supported reserve, provide active-duty care again, or demobilize surviving personnel into the veteran settlement. Demobilization returns only living eligible manpower according to the military owner. It does not refund consumed preparation packs or delivered care. The veteran care obligation survives outside the division roster.

A state containing veterans remains an ordinary civilian state unless some later event actually changes its population or political identity. Medical need alone does not justify a nonhuman classifier, a new country tag, or compulsory institute ownership.

## A controlled-organism programme needs its own foundation

### Knowledge, specimens, and fieldable bodies

The programme may begin after Zombie Outbreak has fired, after a valid specimen has been captured, or after another owner supplies an authenticated source of reanimated material. The source must distinguish clinical knowledge, a research specimen, a usable body cohort, and an already independent force.

A specimen can establish research access without supplying enough material to field a battalion. An old medical report can support an adaptation without proving that the institute possesses a living or reanimated subject. The global Deaths total is never a material inventory. People who died in the campaign do not become an unlimited pool that Wu can spend.

The first deployable bodies come from a finite, owner-confirmed cohort or another explicitly authorized production result. Captured reanimated bodies retain their origin and the fact that the people are already dead. Rebuilding their military role neither restores those people to life nor records their deaths a second time. Living infected patients remain in the cure system until their owner establishes a different irreversible state.

Later replication or designed-life projects may produce compatible new cohorts through paid production. Those future outputs need a shared-owner contract of their own. Completing a control project in this part does not create such production automatically. This preserves a meaningful distinction between knowing how to command a lineage and having enough bodies to deploy it.

A human host can field a controlled lineage without becoming a zombie country. The unit's medical classification, the country's civilian classification, and any independent escaped actor are separate facts. Ordinary human hospitals, migration, famine, and government systems continue to apply to the host.

### C01. Characterization and custody

C01 requires M03, a real compatible source, and an approved containment location. Its proposed work is 120 days, one research lane, two civilian factories, 200 support equipment, and 25 political power. The material remains under its source-owner restrictions. Research does not grant permission to infect living patients or turn a foreign medical sample into an unrestricted military stock.

The result distinguishes which parts of the source can be treated, which can be controlled, and which remain outside Wu's current method. The player can preserve only the knowledge, retain the permitted research material, or authorize a military control proposal. Destroying the retained material closes material-dependent starts until another valid source exists. It does not erase completed research or public evidence.

The first report should focus on the difference between a responsive patient, an unresponsive reanimated organism, and a specimen whose non-biological features remain unexplained. A demonic or otherwise supernatural source cannot be made wholly controllable by copying its biological label. Its owner must identify a valid component before it can enter the ordinary lineage branch.

### C02. Control service and emergency shutdown

C02 requires C01, trained handlers, a compatible medical countermeasure, and the political permission to maintain controlled reanimated organisms. The proposed work is 180 days, one research lane, three civilian factories, 300 support equipment, and 35 army experience.

Completion produces a control standard, trained-service requirements, a reviewable archive, and an emergency shutdown capability for that standard. It does not produce a battalion. Before an operational cohort is admitted, the institute must demonstrate that its own emergency response can retire or immobilize that cohort under controlled conditions. A prototype that cannot be safely retired remains a research case.

The shutdown is not a resurrection treatment. On fully dead organisms it ends or interrupts their controlled military activity. A recoverable shutdown may preserve an inert cohort for later supported requalification. Destructive retirement removes the surviving body material from military use. Neither action returns the dead people to civilian population or recruitable manpower.

The government chooses custody through the ordinary command settlement. An army-owned handler service can receive a known protocol without recognizing Wu as its commander. A Wu-owned service creates political dependence only when the government actually assigns him exclusive records, appointments, or removal authority.

An independent archive under H07 can preserve a known control standard after Wu disappears. A retained archive does not cure every unfamiliar mutation or grant ownership of unrelated zombies. New failures still require a valid medical response and the relevant owner.

### Researching a lineage

C03 through C07 each develop one of the five lineages. They require C02, a compatible source, an approved role, and the facilities needed to sustain that role. The player selects the role intentionally. There is no random draw that permanently withholds Guardian or grants Carrier as an unwanted result.

## Control incidents as local, recoverable problems

### Distinguish the failure before choosing the response

A control incident identifies an affected cohort, its current owner, the service standard it depends on, the actual site or formation, and the known cause. The first public cause can remain uncertain when the evidence is uncertain. A hidden rival operation must not be revealed merely because the failure handler knows its source internally.

Five cases need different responses. A supply interruption can be repaired through delivery or withdrawal. A handler loss needs trained replacements and continuity of orders. A damaged archive needs a surviving known standard or an independent custodian. A command dispute needs a political or military settlement. A novel biological failure needs Wu or another compatible owner-approved medical response.

Only the fifth case can make an otherwise intact control treatment inadequate. Routine lack of trucks cannot silently rewrite the strain. A government change cannot make every physical pack evaporate. A laboratory capture can deprive the host of a production site without revoking knowledge already held by a separate archive.

### A bounded incident sequence

The first incident report names what has actually become unusable and offers the relevant immediate actions. The normal sequence is diagnosis, a supported response, and a settlement. The player should have one to three active objectives across the current military-service surface, not a separate mission for every battalion with the same interruption.

A route interruption might ask the player to restore access to the named depot or bring the affected formation to a supported receiving site. A damaged control archive might ask for the surviving records to be secured at a controlled institution and reviewed by an existing qualified team. A command dispute might require a real replacement commander, a negotiated custody agreement, or a government decision to leave the formation under the existing authority.

Normal recovery operations take 90 to 180 days when they involve rebuilding an institution or qualifying a replacement service. An urgent clinical or control stabilization can use a shorter 30- to 60-day interval when existing delivered cover will soon expire. The urgency must come from that actual interval and affected cohort. It must not be a generic ten-day mission that expects an army to cross a continent.

Success preserves the surviving force, resolves only the affected incident, and restores the service that has genuinely been rebuilt. Partial success can recover handlers and some body material while leaving a smaller formation in custody. Failure can leave a medically unfit force, an escaped finite cohort, a local outbreak, or a genuine command break. The outcome must follow the failed objective and its owner contract.

A recovery does not restore original unit strength, undo combat losses, manufacture replacement equipment, or erase responsibility. The country must replace actual losses through its normal military or approved cohort-production paths.

### Shutdown, retirement, and a surviving independent actor

A routine supported shutdown uses the C02 capability and the appropriate current standard. It consumes a proposed extra 5 Control Service Packs per affected battalion and removes that cohort from operational service. A recoverable shutdown leaves only the surviving inert material. Returning it to duty requires requalification and a newly funded ordinary service interval.

Destructive retirement permanently removes the recorded surviving body material. Living handlers are evacuated or returned through the normal owner. Already dead organisms are not registered as new human deaths, while any confirmed living casualties remain genuine losses. The operation creates no manpower payout for the bodies.

If a control failure has already produced an independent country or army, routine shutdown cannot delete that actor. A compatible medical operation can weaken its biological support or retire an exact reachable cohort under the actor owner's rules. Territory, diplomacy, remaining forces, and surrender still require the relevant political or military resolution.

Wu-controlled units held inside an ordinary country are not automatically counted as the main Zombie Horde, a dynamic outbreak country, or a Rat Nation. An escaped force needs explicit provenance and a valid owner-created identity before it enters those actor-specific systems. The same event cannot both declare its victims cured and replace them with a free independent army.

### Medical responsibility after a failure

Care for living handlers and nearby civilians begins through the existing medical network. Their eligibility does not depend on whether the government chooses to disclose its military programme. A concealed incident can preserve secrecy for a time, but it leaves its actual evidence and responsibility available to later investigation.

An audit can establish a misuse of the programme, a contractor failure, a concealed adverse result, or an external attack. Attribution follows evidence. A successful treatment does not erase the incident, reduce already recorded deaths, or clear the original attacker's Condemnation.
