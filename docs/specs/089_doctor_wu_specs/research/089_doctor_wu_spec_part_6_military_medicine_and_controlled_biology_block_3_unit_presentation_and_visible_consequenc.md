## Unit presentation and visible consequences

### Six new combat identities

The permanent enhanced-human family and the five reanimated lineages each need their own authorized unit presentation. Temporary endurance and combat courses continue to use the enrolled ordinary formations. They do not require a new creature model merely to show a treatment status.

| Family | Model role | Required distinctive motion | Counter and sound identity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Permanent enhanced infantry | Period human infantry with the intended fictional physical alteration | Coherent armed movement, handling, firing, recovery, retreat, and death | Enhanced human service, distinguishable from clones and ordinary infantry |
| Command | Coordinated firearm-bearing reanimated infantry | Deliberate orders, armed movement, firing, disciplined holding, and control loss where supported | Controlled army integration |
| Assault | Heavy non-firing creature formation | Weight-bearing movement, close assault, recovery, defense, retreat, and ground contact at death | Heavy handling and impact |
| Hunter | Fast non-firing creature formation | Distinct pursuit gait, turns, observation, attack, retreat, and death | Mobile pursuit and return |
| Carrier | Non-firing restricted-custody formation | Guarded movement, holding, withdrawal, shutdown, and destruction | Controlled custody, without a real dispersal-device depiction |
| Guardian | Prepared-site firearm-bearing guard | Steady defense, weapon handling, firing, relocation, retreat, and shutdown | Site protection with a distinct guard motif |

An existing owner-approved visual variant can satisfy a family only after it is reviewed against the actual design and all its consumers. Renaming ordinary zombie assets or changing their color is not evidence of a completed stronger lineage. The existing Rat model package remains outside this asset assignment.

### Production ownership and evidence

Each model begins with an eligible, attributed modern designed-artwork reference that fits its declared period and non-anime identity. Source rights, derivative permission, and any restrictions on generative use must be checked before reference preparation. This part selects no actual source image and authorizes no claim that a source has been researched or licensed.

For the firearm-bearing permanent, Command, and Guardian units, the production plan requires one approved weapon-free body input to Meshy 7 and a separate firearm geometry task for each required firearm. Body rigging, attachment, weights, and genuine actions then follow the direct Blender route. The final assembled unit must preserve the intended firearm identity and prove grip, support contact, aim, discharge, recoil, recovery, and effect and sound synchronization.

Non-firing Assault, Hunter, and Carrier models follow their applicable humanoid or nonhumanoid profile. A nonhumanoid profile needs a measured scale crosswalk and a dedicated rig plan before generation. Failed or unsupported provider rig or action stages follow the skill's bounded manual Blender route. A static pose, whole-object transform, or renamed action cannot satisfy a missing role.

Scale must be measured against an actual installed vanilla mesh and its runtime entity. Record source dimensions, entity scale, and effective runtime dimensions separately so the scale is applied once. This specification does not invent a reference mesh path, a measured height, an export result, or a successful reimport.

Every used counter surface needs bespoke art with the sampled palette and frame behavior of the correct installed-vanilla reference family. Final sound roles need sourced, licensed audio, original evidence, and an exact consumer. A generic country-wide infantry voice override is unacceptable for one Wu unit. If unit-specific selection audio is unsupported, that role remains an explicit consumer blocker until an authorized solution is established.

The parent owns runtime wiring and the final review. Model and icon workers own their bounded production outputs. In-game validation remains with the user. No art, model, audio, native render, or animation has been produced by writing this part.

### Reports and institutional tone

Military success reports should show the force carrying out its assigned role and the people maintaining it. Failure reports should show the actual disruption, lost access, missing cohort, unfit personnel, or contested order. The text should name relevant formations, sites, custodians, and dates when those facts are known.

A public institute's responses can use clinical confidence and restrained criticism of a premature deployment. Military command can use practical concern about rotation and readiness. A classified directorate can conceal responsibility through institutional language, with later evidence exposing what it did. Those are writing directions, not final strings.

The people receiving care are not a source of cheap humor. Ironic options may target a government's attempt to demand exceptional service without funding it, or its assumption that exclusive records will always remain in friendly hands. Do not use invented quotations, cultural references, or ready-to-paste option lines.

## The exact shared-system boundary

The future Wu owner supplies its own new unit-family registration and complete provider callbacks. Event 019 remains a consumer. It must not gain a second central family list, a second registry file, or special hardcoded Wu selection branches. Existing provider identifiers remain reserved for their owners. New identifiers are assigned only after a fresh collision check.

Endurance and temporary combat courses are support services on an existing human force, not separate Event 019 combat families. Permanent enhanced infantry and each genuinely standalone reanimated lineage need their own explicit provider disposition. An externally received family retains its actual source, equipment, handler, service, command, and cleanup obligations. Receiving that family through Event 019 does not create Wu or fire his arrival.

A provider can expose an externally maintainable known standard when the receiving country has the actual archive, material, and service capability. It cannot assume that every consumer has Wu. If that recipient cannot support the standard, the provider must return an honest unavailable result or preserve a previously issued exact reception under the registry's own settlement rules. It cannot substitute an ordinary zombie formation.

CXT coverage uses the current registration-only extension contract. Startup and tag-scoped fallback registration announce the package and mark pending work. They do not periodically grant new equipment, spawn forces, complete research, or fire Event 089. Initial harness setup and later explicit player actions apply registered content according to CXT ownership.

All disease effects remain with the disease owner. Black Plague phases, Rat actors, Zombie Outbreak continuity, confirmed use, contamination, and exact Deaths accounting are not copied into a Wu ledger. Wu's clinical and control stocks have different purposes from native attack payloads. They cannot become a second balance for the same weapons.

Exact selected-formation effects, service persistence across template edits, prepared-zone bonuses, force conversion, and operational ownership changes require installed-documentation and engine evidence. The implementation must not replace an unproved selected-force effect with a country-wide modifier. These are specific feasibility gates, not reasons to remove the requested design from the specification.

## What the institute carries into replication

A successful military programme leaves documented methods, trained custodians, real production, surviving cohorts, and continuing obligations. A supervised institute can retain all of those without surrendering the army to Wu. An exclusive institute can accumulate the same resources in a form that later supports political power, but it still needs a real chain of concessions and conflicts before that power becomes sovereignty.

The next part begins with medical replacement material and moves into replicated specialists, replacement soldiers, clone formations, and Wu continuity. It must distinguish a new living person from a repaired existing person, an already dead reanimated body, and a legacy clone stock item. No result in this part grants free clone equipment, starts the Army of Clones scenario, copies Wu, or resolves the shared clone economy by adding a second manpower stream.

The shared clone owner currently preserves recurring manpower from legacy stock. Wu's finite replacement-person route needs its own explicitly reviewed shared-owner mode with conserved material and one-time person accounting. That integration decision is recorded in the current disposition document. It must be completed before the next part can promise an operational clone-production result.
