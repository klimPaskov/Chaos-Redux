# Command lineage (block 2)

## Command lineage

### Its military purpose

Command is the most reliable line for use inside a conventional army. It holds a position, follows a sequence of orders, and can operate near protected friendly populations while its service is maintained. Its value is predictable cooperation with ordinary forces. It does not spread infection merely by sharing their province.

The proposed comparison against an ordinary controlled zombie body is 25 percent greater organization, 15 percent greater defense, and 20 percent faster organization recovery. Its 90 infantry equipment per battalion supports its explicit firearm-bearing role. It does not gain the Assault lineage's heavy breakthrough or the Hunter lineage's pursuit speed.

The source comparison must be calibrated against the actual owner unit. These are design targets, not a claim about existing zombie statistics or a universal improvement applied to every zombie in the world.

### Qualification and use

The first formation qualifies with an ordinary army command and a dedicated handler service at a supplied location. The government demonstrates that it can maintain the standard without placing Wu in the army chain of command. Its first useful assignment is holding a threatened sector, protecting a route used by conventional troops, or relieving an exhausted ordinary formation.

Its orders remain ordinary military orders. It receives no special map teleport, supply override, or automatic core on occupied land. The service must move with it or arrange a compatible receiving depot.

A five-battalion Command formation consumes five body cohorts, 500 living manpower, 450 infantry equipment, and 75 Control Service Packs for its first service interval. Its 500 paid handlers provide its embedded five-battalion service. No additional external team reservation is charged. Follow-on care uses 75 packs per 30 days while all five battalions remain authorized.

### Failure and recovery

Loss of handlers first reduces its ability to carry out complex orders. Its fictional command state becomes Restricted, with a proposed 25 percent organization penalty and no qualification for new independent assignments. A loss of supply creates the care interruption described by its dated service record. Neither event automatically grants its ownership to Wu.

The host can replace the handler team, move the formation into a supported holding area, or use the validated shutdown response. A rival laboratory can redirect it only after a real operation obtains the necessary access and defeats the current command settlement. Capturing an unrelated hospital or merely possessing another zombie technology is insufficient.

If the government has previously given Wu exclusive command appointments and the archive, an order to seize his institute may become disputed. The force can refuse that specific order, remain in place while command is resolved, or follow an actual breakaway authority if the later political chain reaches that outcome. Its loyalty follows the recorded appointments and conflict. It is not generated by a universal Authority threshold.

### Presentation

The model should show deliberate coordinated movement and period equipment, with living handler support legible through the unit package. The counter must distinguish controlled military service from the ordinary horde. Selection, acknowledgement, and movement audio need sourced material appropriate to the unit's command behavior without replacing the whole country's infantry voices.

Its first report should show ordinary soldiers working beside a force that follows instructions consistently. A later failure report should identify the lost handler, disputed order, or interrupted service. It should not reveal an undiscovered foreign operation as established fact.

## Assault lineage

### Its military purpose

Assault is designed for a concentrated attack against a defended position. It trades movement and inexpensive support for a stronger body and a short, demanding combat commitment. A country should build it because it has a difficult position to overcome and can maintain the force afterward.

The proposed comparison against an ordinary controlled zombie body is 50 percent greater soft attack, 40 percent greater breakthrough, and 35 percent greater hit points, accompanied by 25 percent lower movement speed. The family has no automatic tank armor, freedom from artillery losses, or immunity to encirclement. Its defining strength belongs to close combat and formation endurance.

Assault is explicitly non-firing. Its model, actions, and equipment cannot quietly add a firearm, projectile, or ranged attack effect to compensate for a weak animation package.

### Qualification and use

The first cohort requires a prepared handling location and an actual transport plan for leaving it. The country can commit the force to a nearby operation or retain it as a heavy reserve. Moving it overseas requires the normal military transport system and an agreed receiving service. A medical dispatch cannot bypass naval access or a blockade.

A five-battalion Assault formation consumes five body cohorts, 750 living handler manpower, and 150 Control Service Packs for the first interval. Its recurring care bill remains 150 packs per 30 days at full authorized strength. Production, staff, and native supply make maintaining several such formations a substantial national commitment.

After a demanding deployment, the formation needs a supported holding period before the next specialized assignment. A proposed 30-day requalification checks its surviving bodies, handlers, and current control standard. Requalification uses the ordinary service already funded for those days. It does not charge the same packs again or repair all battlefield damage for free.

### Failure and recovery

Assault deteriorates through loss of handling capacity and physical containment. Stretched service first prevents a new specialized assignment. Interrupted care imposes a proposed 35 percent organization penalty and a further 20 percent movement penalty. These penalties make withdrawal difficult enough to plan ahead without automatically deleting the force.

The host can establish a nearby supported holding site, bring the force back to a prepared depot, or retire the affected cohort through its proven shutdown. If it continues to move a failing force through populated territory, a later containment incident can harm nearby living people. That consequence needs a named affected state and a confirmed event result. Routine transport does not roll civilian deaths as a hidden tax.

An escaped Assault cohort remains a finite military problem tied to its surviving material. It cannot generate a replacement army out of its own dead bodies. An independent hostile actor, when genuinely created by an authorized later chain, owns only the transferred force and territory that the transaction proves.

### Presentation

The silhouette needs to communicate mass and slow, forceful movement without becoming an oversized ordinary zombie with a renamed counter. Its attack, recovery, retreat, and death actions require genuine articulated motion and readable ground contact. Sourced impact and movement audio should fit that body rather than use gunfire for a non-firing unit.

The first field report should focus on the surviving force and the strain on its handlers after the operation. A supply-failure report should make its location and recovery choices clear. It must not present a generic laboratory explosion as the only possible failure.

## Hunter lineage

### Its military purpose

Hunter is a mobile force for pursuit, reconnaissance, and exploiting an enemy's disordered withdrawal. It is valuable when an army can keep a moving support route behind it. It is poor at prolonged defensive fighting and becomes unreliable when repeatedly sent beyond its supporting service.

The proposed comparison against an ordinary controlled zombie body is 50 percent greater movement speed and 25 percent greater reconnaissance performance, with 30 percent lower defense and 25 percent lower hit points. The reconnaissance result must use a supported combat or reconnaissance consumer. It does not reveal every hidden enemy unit or grant intelligence capabilities that the engine does not provide.

Hunter is explicitly non-firing. Its speed does not create a special crossing, paradrop, amphibious movement, or escape from an encirclement. Terrain, access, and ordinary supply continue to matter.

### Qualification and use

The government must designate both a service base and a place where the formation can return. The base can move through a paid relocation of existing service. It cannot appear in the captured destination at the same moment the Hunters arrive.

A five-battalion Hunter formation consumes five body cohorts, 625 living handler manpower, and 100 Control Service Packs for its first interval. Its ongoing bill is 100 packs per 30 days. A mobile receiving service makes the force more useful, while adding the real logistical commitment already required by that service.

The player can pursue a broken enemy, scout a threatened approach through ordinary military tools, or hold the formation back until support catches up. The programme creates a useful reason to stop an advance temporarily. It must not force a separate decision click for every province crossed.

### Failure and recovery

The first Hunter problem is a lost connection to its handler service. The selected formation displays the actual last supported location and the expiring service interval. A force that returns while cover remains can requalify without a new political crisis.

Interrupted Hunters lose their special reconnaissance and pursuit advantage and take a proposed 30 percent organization penalty. Prolonged isolation creates a finite loss-of-control case. The government may restore contact, accept a supported withdrawal, or deploy a shutdown response where access is real.

A missing Hunter cohort can become an escaped local threat only through a recorded failure. It does not create an invisible world network or infect arbitrary states because it once had a reconnaissance role. Recovery of part of the cohort returns only the bodies and surviving handlers actually recovered.

### Presentation

The model needs a distinct gait, low mass, rapid changes of direction, and a readable retreat. A stretched version should remain the same physical identity. An animation swap must not masquerade as a new strain or a free upgrade.

The first report should show a unit that arrives ahead of ordinary troops and must wait for its handlers to catch up. Failure text should retain uncertainty about a missing group until a genuine recovery, sighting, or incident establishes its fate.

## Carrier lineage

### Its military purpose

Carrier is the lineage whose intended offensive role is creating a fictional outbreak through a separately authorized biological operation. It is deliberately poor at ordinary combat and expensive to keep under control. Selecting the project means choosing that role and accepting its custody and political consequences.

The proposed ordinary-combat comparison is 50 percent lower soft attack and 35 percent lower defense than an ordinary controlled zombie body. The family receives no infection effect from standing in a province, moving with an army, attacking in a normal battle, being defeated, or losing equipment. A controlled military formation and a deliberate biological operation are different actions.

The specification defines no real agent properties, laboratory process, preparation method, practical dissemination mechanism, or operational optimization. Carrier's disease role remains a fictional game outcome resolved by the owning biological and zombie systems.

### Qualification and custody

C06 can be researched under explicit supervision, including in Calm World. The government can study the capability and keep it in restricted custody without approving a release. A public institute's permission to characterize a Carrier does not automatically become an offensive order.

Field admission requires C02, a lineage-specific shutdown result, compatible source material, a supported custody location, and a declared release authority. A five-battalion formation consumes five body cohorts, 750 living handler manpower, and 150 Control Service Packs for its first interval. Its standing control bill is 150 packs per 30 days.

That bill pays for custody and control. It contains no abstract stock of attack payloads and does not credit an extra biological weapon inventory. A release, when authorized, uses the native owner's real eligible resources and transaction. If the native owner cannot distinguish custody from release or cannot support a Carrier-specific consumer, the offensive action remains a named implementation blocker. The unit must not gain a substitute passive contamination aura.

### Authorization and outcome ownership

The player must choose the deliberate operation through the existing biological action owner. The Wu category may explain the Carrier's current eligibility and link to that surface. It cannot create a competing attack queue, selected-agent ledger, reservation balance, or scripted cancellation refund.

The native transaction owns the operation's reservation and settlement. The disease and zombie owners own the resulting exposure, outbreak progression, attribution, contamination, and confirmed use. Wu receives a factual record of the relevant result for his institute and foreign competition. He does not debit the same equipment again, count the same deaths again, or create a second copy of the outbreak.

The operation must explicitly determine the fate of any committed Carrier cohort. If the owner consumes or loses it, the corresponding formation material cannot remain fieldable. If it survives under the owner's result, only those survivors return to their service. A completed operation cannot both refund its original cohort and create a new one from the resulting outbreak.

Existing source-event firing remains separate. A Carrier incident cannot mark the natural Zombie Outbreak opening as fired unless that source event's own accepted contract explicitly does so. It cannot set Wu's evolution stage simply because an operation occurred.

### Failure and recovery

A failed control service creates a custody emergency, not an automatically successful attack. The host first isolates the affected cohort through the validated shutdown and supports living handlers and nearby patients through the actual cure system. A confirmed escape can create a local biological consequence through the proper owner. The source, location, responsibility, and surviving material remain recorded.

Carrier has the strongest reason for an independent shutdown archive and a reserved response capability. Reserving that response is a real requirement. A player who knowingly abandons it may face a more difficult incident, but the event must show the loss of cover before a normal supply interruption becomes a major crisis.

Publishing evidence, concealing the incident, or refusing access changes the later political and foreign-operation response. Clinical success does not remove responsibility for an earlier release. Destroying the surviving stock does not clear Condemnation or erase victims from the Deaths system.

### Presentation

Carrier's visual identity should communicate guarded, restricted custody. It is non-firing and must not use a model or animation that illustrates a real dispersal device. The counter and unit description must distinguish an ordinary military formation from the separately authorized operation it may support.

Reports should identify observed illness, loss of contact with a contained group, or the confirmed result of an investigation. They should never provide real biological instructions. The tone remains serious when people are harmed, with any institutional euphemism exposing the speaker's choices rather than making victims the subject of a joke.

## Guardian lineage

### Its military purpose

Guardian protects a prepared laboratory, depot, hospital district, or other specifically designated strategic site. It is reliable inside the service zone that supports it and weak as a long-range expeditionary force.

The proposed comparison inside a valid prepared zone is 40 percent greater defense and 30 percent greater entrenchment capability than an ordinary controlled zombie body. These benefits require the formation to be assigned to that zone and supported there. They do not turn every friendly province into a protected zone or increase the defense of unrelated units.

Outside its prepared zone, Guardian loses the zone-specific bonuses and has a proposed 20 percent movement penalty. It can be relocated through ordinary military movement, but the receiving site must be prepared before the full defensive package returns. It cannot create a zone simply by arriving.

### Preparing a zone

The host selects one controlled site with a real service function and enough local access to maintain it. A proposed preparation takes 90 days, occupies one civilian factory, and spends 150 support equipment. Site control and a valid service standard are requirements. An admitted Guardian formation supplies its own already paid handlers. The operation creates a maintained assignment, not a free supply hub, fort, or building level.

The zone may protect civilian medical services, military storage, or an institute campus. The government chooses which authority controls its guard orders. A hospital receiving treatment does not become Wu's territory merely because Guardians protect it.

A five-battalion Guardian formation consumes five body cohorts, 500 living manpower, 450 infantry equipment, and 50 Control Service Packs for its first interval. Its continuing bill is 50 packs per 30 days while the prepared site and its service remain valid.

Guardian is firearm-bearing. Its weapons and ammunition remain part of the conventional equipment contract. It does not receive attack advantages simply because its defensive zone is active.

### Failure and recovery

Loss of a zone's medical access removes the Supported defensive package when its actual cover expires. Loss of site control removes the zone benefit immediately for forces that can no longer use that site, while preserving whatever portable service they actually retain.

A conflict over the institute can leave a Guardian force holding its assigned position and rejecting a change in orders. That result requires the recorded command relationship. Government-command Guardians can protect a public hospital during an attempt to arrest Wu. Institute-command Guardians may obstruct access to his campus. Both outcomes follow earlier choices and actual geography.

The government can negotiate a command handover, replace the service team, prepare a receiving zone, or retire the force. A new custodian must prove access to the actual handlers and standard. Taking possession of an empty building does not automatically transfer the surrounding army.

### Presentation

The model should be recognizable as a prepared defensive guard with period firearms and a steady stance. Its defend, patrol-like idle, retreat, and orderly shutdown actions must remain visually distinct from Command's mobile army role. The counter can use a site-guard motif without adopting a real protected medical emblem as fictional military branding.

The first report should show the site functioning with a reliable guard. A later command dispute should focus on the people unable to enter, leave, supply, or command that specific place. Generic statements that the whole country has fallen under Wu's control would misrepresent a local dispute.

## Compatible additions without an all-purpose lineage

### C08. Restricted combined-role research

C08 requires two completed lineage projects, C02, and a maintained shutdown standard for both. Its proposed work is 180 days, one research lane, two civilian factories, 250 support equipment, and 35 army experience. The government authorizes one named combined role. It does not unlock a collection of unrestricted trait sliders.

The first permitted combinations are Command with a prepared-site Guardian role, Command with a limited Hunter reconnaissance role, and Assault with a fixed-site Guardian role. Each retains a primary lineage. The addition cannot deliver the full defining advantages of both parent tables.

Command with Guardian gains a proposed 20 percent defensive bonus inside its prepared assignment while retaining Command's ordinary service outside it. Command with Hunter gains a proposed 15 percent reconnaissance advantage and 15 percent eligible movement advantage, without receiving Hunter's full pursuit profile. Assault with Guardian gains a proposed 20 percent defensive bonus inside a prepared site, while retaining Assault's slow movement and heavy handling burden.

One addition increases the primary lineage's Control Service Pack demand by 25 percent, rounded upward to a multiple of five for the complete funded formation. It requires both standards to remain supported. A five-battalion Command-Guardian formation therefore requires 95 packs per 30 days under the proposed table, compared with 75 for ordinary Command. It receives no refund of the base interval when an addition is suspended.

A cohort can have one combined-role addition at a time. Switching roles requires a supported requalification interval and preserves the underlying body, losses, and command history. It does not create another cohort, restore destroyed material, or clear the evidence of a failed earlier configuration.

Carrier is excluded from this combined combat-role project. Mixing its release role into an ordinary combat lineage would blur custody, command, and deliberate-use accounting. A future compatible Carrier derivative would require a separately designed owner-approved transaction, not an unchecked trait combination. This restriction does not block Carrier research itself.

### What an unstable combination does

A combined-role failure first removes the added role. The primary control standard remains usable when its own service is intact. This makes rollback a meaningful action and prevents every small experiment from becoming an unavoidable national outbreak.

If the failure damages the primary control relationship, the affected cohort enters the same control incident process as its parent lineage. The event retains the tested combination, responsible authorization, and surviving material. Repeating the same failed combination without revising its service or oversight cannot be presented as a first harmless experiment.

The report should describe incompatible behavior in the actual assigned role. A Command-Guardian cohort might refuse to leave the protected site when the army asks it to move. A Command-Hunter cohort might outrun the service needed for complex orders. These are fictional behavioral consequences that create a military choice, not descriptions of biological modification methods.

## Control incidents as local, recoverable problems

### Distinguish the failure before choosing the response

A control incident identifies an affected cohort, its current owner, the service standard it depends on, the actual site or formation, and the known cause. The first public cause can remain uncertain when the evidence is uncertain. A hidden rival operation must not be revealed merely because the failure handler knows its source internally.

Five cases need different responses. A supply interruption can be repaired through delivery or withdrawal. A handler loss needs trained replacements and continuity of orders. A damaged archive needs a surviving known standard or an independent custodian. A command dispute needs a political or military settlement. A novel biological failure needs Wu or another compatible owner-approved medical response.

Only the fifth case can make an otherwise intact control treatment inadequate. Routine lack of trucks cannot silently rewrite the strain. A government change cannot make every physical pack evaporate. A laboratory capture can deprive the host of a production site without revoking knowledge already held by a separate archive.

### A bounded incident sequence

The first incident report names what has actually become unusable and offers the relevant immediate actions. The normal sequence is diagnosis, a supported response, and a settlement. The player should have one to three active objectives across the current military-service surface, not a separate mission for every battalion with the same interruption.

A route interruption might ask the player to restore access to the named depot or bring the affected formation to a supported receiving site. A damaged control archive might ask for the surviving records to be secured at a controlled institution and reviewed by an existing qualified team. A command dispute might require a real replacement commander, a negotiated custody agreement, or a government decision to leave the formation under the existing authority.

Normal recovery operations take 90 to 180 days when they involve rebuilding an institution or qualifying a replacement service. An urgent clinical or control stabilization can use a shorter 30- to 60-day interval when existing delivered cover will soon expire. The urgency must come from that actual interval and affected cohort. It must not be a generic ten-day mission that expects an army to cross a continent.

Success preserves the surviving force, resolves only the affected incident, and restores the service that has genuinely been rebuilt. Partial success can recover handlers and some body material while leaving a smaller formation in custody. Failure can leave a medically unfit force, an escaped finite cohort, a local outbreak, or a genuine command break. The outcome must follow the failed objective and its owner contract.

A recovery does not restore original unit strength, undo combat losses, manufacture replacement equipment, or erase responsibility. The country must replace actual losses through its normal military or approved cohort-production paths.

### Shutdown, retirement, and a surviving independent actor

A routine supported shutdown uses the C02 capability and the appropriate current standard. It consumes a proposed extra 5 Control Service Packs per affected battalion and removes that cohort from operational service. A recoverable shutdown leaves only the surviving inert material. Returning it to duty requires requalification and a newly funded ordinary service interval.

Destructive retirement permanently removes the recorded surviving body material. Living handlers are evacuated or returned through the normal owner. Already dead organisms are not registered as new human deaths, while any confirmed living casualties remain genuine losses. The operation creates no manpower payout for the bodies.

If a control failure has already produced an independent country or army, routine shutdown cannot delete that actor. A compatible medical operation can weaken its biological support or retire an exact reachable cohort under the actor owner's rules. Territory, diplomacy, remaining forces, and surrender still require the relevant political or military resolution.

Wu-controlled units held inside an ordinary country are not automatically counted as the main Zombie Horde, a dynamic outbreak country, or a Rat Nation. An escaped force needs explicit provenance and a valid owner-created identity before it enters those actor-specific systems. The same event cannot both declare its victims cured and replace them with a free independent army.

### Medical responsibility after a failure

Care for living handlers and nearby civilians begins through the existing medical network. Their eligibility does not depend on whether the government chooses to disclose its military programme. A concealed incident can preserve secrecy for a time, but it leaves its actual evidence and responsibility available to later investigation.

An audit can establish a misuse of the programme, a contractor failure, a concealed adverse result, or an external attack. Attribution follows evidence. A successful treatment does not erase the incident, reduce already recorded deaths, or clear the original attacker's Condemnation.

Event-owned Chaos changes require a distinct consequence. A real escaped force becoming a new continuing threat may justify a separate registered consequence when the shared war, death, and contamination sources do not already represent it. Starting research, raising a control flag, changing care status, or activating an evolution supplies zero Chaos. Repeated shutdown and reactivation cannot farm reductions.

## Who controls the service when Wu is unavailable

### The first continuity response

Wu's death, capture, disappearance, or forced departure creates a review of active military obligations. It does not instantly destroy all known standards or make every engineered formation disobey. The review reads each actual custodian, remaining service interval, archive, and trained team.

A standard already maintained under H07 continues through that independent service. A standard retained only inside Wu's institute can be secured by a surviving qualified staff group at a real controlled site. An unfamiliar failure still requires a competent research response. A future continuity copy may fill Wu's role only after the replication specification establishes that copy's identity and capability.

The proposed archive emergency lasts 45 days where the host has a recoverable archive and remaining service cover. The government must secure the named site or transfer its records and qualified team to a valid receiving institution. It cannot complete the objective by paying political power while every relevant facility is lost.

At the deadline, each standard is settled separately. Known care with a functioning successor continues. A recovered incomplete standard permits limited holding and withdrawal while research resumes. An inaccessible standard leaves its dependent cohorts in their actual interrupted state. A single successful archive recovery does not recover unrelated sites or grant every new standard completed abroad.

### A trained successor

A successor can maintain documented known programmes and lead ordinary clinical work that the retained institution actually supports. The successor does not inherit Wu's universal improvisational ability, all his uncompleted research, or a second copy of his unique character role.

The player can appoint an army medical service, a public clinical board, or an institute staff authority as the custodian. This is institutional succession. A named person's portrait requires its own authorized identity and asset process. It must not be invented merely to fill a vacant advisor slot in this part.

The successor's immediate job is to protect existing patients and forces. New admissions can resume after the relevant standard and production line have been requalified. An institution that can maintain a Guardian cohort is not automatically qualified to run temporary combat courses, Carrier operations, or permanent alteration.

### Capture and hostile custody

Capturing an archive can provide permanent knowledge when the owner validates what has actually been learned. Capturing stock provides only the physical material captured. Capturing a live formation does not grant the captor Wu, its trained handlers, or automatic allegiance.

A captor may preserve the cohort in supported custody, seek a compatible service, retire it, or pursue a later recruitment and command settlement. Its own political choices and obligations remain distinct from those of the former owner. There is no automatic upgrade to the captor's whole army.

A transfer of operational formation ownership requires an exact supported transaction. The existing Event 019 recreate-and-delete method has documented limits on live formation details. Its acceptance cannot be assumed to authorize an equivalent Wu transfer. Wu's implementation must either prove the required identity and conservation behavior or keep that specific transfer route blocked with the precise limitation. The design does not accept fresh full-strength replacements as a silent substitute.

## Joint work and foreign service

### A bounded connection to Kruger

A co-hosted Kruger can contribute compatible knowledge, a documented project result, or a real facility service. Wu can contribute military care, a control standard, or a medical response. The contribution belongs to the actual participant and can be used only within its accepted permission.

A joint assignment must name a concrete problem. Examples are keeping an altered human formation medically fit, providing a compatible care service for a known clone cohort, or comparing an engineered-organism control failure with a documented xenobiological control system. The contribution does not grant unrelated Portal, temporal, robot, or alien technology.

The Kruger owner retains his character, project stages, Directorate values, external knowledge receipts, and source history. Wu retains the care and control obligation he actually owns. A completed joint review cannot report both a new Kruger project reward and a new Wu military formation when the work only improved an existing service.

Known shared technologies and their source records remain learned after a later dispute. Physical access, paid service, and future cooperation can end. The six full rivalry routes remain for their own specification part. This narrow military connection must not reduce them to one cooperation modifier or one inevitable betrayal.

### Serving an allied formation

A partner can request a temporary course, a continued care service, or a qualified control standard. Its government retains operational command unless a separate supported military arrangement transfers that command. Purchasing medicine does not hand its army to Wu's host.

An agreement records the recipients, standard, care payer, manufacturing party, destination, service interval, and recovery obligation. Payment creates a real order or supplies real stock through the existing diplomacy and production framework. The recipient's force receives the effect only after delivery and support become valid.

War, a broken agreement, or a blockade can end future cooperation. The parties still retain their learned knowledge and the care already delivered. A responsible termination includes the recovery already promised to living patients. Refusing to provide that recovery can create evidence and diplomatic consequences, but it cannot make an unverified foreign death estimate appear in the global ledger.

### Enhanced veterans abroad

A veteran receiving foreign care remains a living person under the appropriate population or military owner. Permanent relocation uses the migration or character owner, with real origin, destination, and reception. The medical service cannot add the same people to two populations.

The host of the care service can offer employment, military service, or permanent residence only through the relevant later political and demographic design. A medical contract alone creates none of those commitments. This preserves a useful source of foreign influence without turning every clinic into a hidden recruitment button.

## Decisions the player actually manages

The military-service phase remains inside ordinary event-owned decision categories and their compact status presentation. It does not add a new full-screen Wu army manager. Selection must identify the actual formation or supported cohort that the action will affect. A state-only selector is insufficient when several different forces occupy the same state.

The existing institution remains the national presentation owner. Course states and lineage differences do not add one national spirit per formation or per project, and the event remains within the three simultaneous spirit limit. The main institute surface continues to show Wu Authority. In the military-service state, the supporting summary may show current service demand and available cover for the selected programme. Detailed dates, recipients, and custody records belong to the selected record. Do not expose every internal risk calculation as another meter.

## AI that can preserve its own force

The AI first protects current medical obligations and the basic civilian treatments it has already promised. It then considers new courses and cohorts according to war pressure, manpower needs, production capacity, service coverage, and the role it can actually use. A country should not complete an expensive project and immediately abandon its first formation because it spent the recovery stock elsewhere.

A public institute normally builds independent teams, rotation, and archive continuity before expanding the force. A military command can prioritize a first endurance or combat course when it has a real front and enough care for recovery. A classified directorate is more willing to fund controlled biology, but it still needs source material, a supported custody site, and a specific authorized role. None has an Authority or Chaos threshold for research access.

Lineage selection follows the army's problem. Command suits conventional integration, Assault a difficult defended position, Hunter an operation with a maintainable mobile service, and Guardian a threatened prepared site. Carrier research and custody require a distinct authorized purpose. The AI must not select it as a generic substitute when it cannot afford ordinary infantry.
