## Living systems beyond human replication

Wu's next proposals concern the places where medicine has to keep working. A treatment can save patients inside a hospital and still leave the surrounding district exposed. A reclaimed town can have clean wards, ruined houses, hungry livestock, and a road that closes whenever fighting returns. The institute begins producing living systems that work alongside its physicians: enclosed filters, maintained cleanup organisms, agricultural treatments, unusual working animals, and living instruments that need an operator and a home.

The government chooses which problems deserve these systems. It can build a network of reliable medical works, support a small number of unusual formations, rebuild damaged communities, or combine several of these uses. Each commitment competes with the institute's existing treatment and production obligations. A government that has distributed Wu's methods can operate an advanced programme through public institutions. A government that has concentrated custody, maintenance, and training under Wu can become dependent on his staff even when it owns the buildings.

The working labels and numerical profiles in this part are proposed design. They are not final localisation or approved balance. Research remains ordinary baseline progression. Calm World, low Authority, and disabled evolutions do not close these families. Actual material, compatible knowledge, trained staff, facilities, and explicit permission decide access. Some campaigns never provide a particular source organism, and their institute must develop another eligible family until such material becomes available.

### What changes in play

A fixed filter protects a known receiving point for as long as it is maintained. A mobile cleanup colony works through an already affected district and must be collected when its assignment ends. Agricultural medicine protects the productive work that remains. A living reconnaissance team can obtain a short-lived observation, but it needs to return. A chimeric formation brings a distinct capability with a real food and care bill. A population programme creates new lives only through Part 7's paid production and first-settlement rules.

The institute therefore has three recurring choices. It chooses where to commit its living stock, which maintenance obligations to renew, and what to do when a useful design no longer fits the local conditions. Research and operation are separate. A completed project adds a reproducible design and its approved uses. It does not fill a warehouse, cure an entire country, recruit an army, or populate a ruined state.

The project map uses fifteen working project identities. Several are new applications of earlier medical knowledge. Their prerequisites retain that earlier work instead of charging for the same discovery again.

These distinctions keep public medical work reachable without forcing a country through unrelated military or replication projects.

### Research commitments

The existing research lanes remain the only institute research capacity. L01, L02, L04, L05, L07, L08, L12, and L15 use the substantial profile: one lane, two civilian factories, 150 Support Equipment, and 50 Political Power over a proposed 120 days. L03, L06, L09, L10, L13, and L14 use the exceptional profile: one lane, three civilian factories, 300 Support Equipment, and 75 Political Power over 180 days. L11 uses the same four cost types as the exceptional profile over 210 days because its joint qualification tests a working interface between two institutions.

A lane and a factory reservation are real commitments. They count among the four cost types. No project adds another sample debit, experience payment, or hidden personnel charge to these profiles. Source material, a qualified team, and appropriate sites are admission requirements. A project that needs to acquire material uses an independently meaningful acquisition or exchange with its own disclosed purpose. Possessing the specimen never stands in for paying the research commitment.

Projects that adapt an existing standard retain the work already demonstrated. L02 adds enclosed operation to M14. L06 adds a continuing service to M16. L13 applies existing replication to a real reconstruction programme. They do not repeat the original cure, tissue, or clone reward. A qualified specialist can improve the rate of the actual work under Part 7. Reassignment never awards a second completed interval.

## Living stock and working assignments

### Designs, service units, and individual animals

The institute records a design separately from its physical output. A filter design can be retained after every installed filter has failed. A foreign hospital can possess a filter without possessing the knowledge to reproduce it. A captured working animal can remain alive without accepting a new handler. These states must not collapse into a single technology flag.

Small colonies and enclosed assemblies use a Living Service Unit as an accounting scale. A unit contains one qualified living assembly and the habitat needed for its intended role. It is not a human cohort and cannot be exchanged for manpower, civilian population, clone equipment, or another organism design. Filter units, cleanup units, repellent stations, and agricultural units retain their own design identity even when the interface groups them under the same production family.

Large animals use actual whole-animal counts. Their record distinguishes available, assigned, resting, under treatment, missing, captured, and confirmed lost animals. Human handlers are accounted for separately. Losing an animal does not add a human death. Losing a handler or another actual person enters the appropriate shared Deaths path once. A discarded colony or damaged habitat does not create a fictional population loss.

The programme does not breed free replacement units through a passive stockpile bonus. Reproduction for new usable stock requires another paid order, a suitable site, care, and a recorded qualification. An escaped population can become a problem, but it cannot be harvested repeatedly as a free industrial output. Any recovery returns only the surviving organisms that the owner can actually identify.

The animal profiles include a final handling period. A new animal cannot skip that period because its body is mature. The paid order identifies the intended role and actual living milestone. Proposed living milestones occur at day 90 for reconnaissance animals, day 120 for support animals, and day 150 for assault organisms. The remaining interval is protected development and qualification, including a final 60-day handling period. These dates are fictional pacing choices.

Support Equipment and fuel are spent on the order's setup and operation. The assigned clinical or husbandry reserve leaves freely available stock when the order starts. Before living output exists, only a proven unused reserve can return on cancellation. After the living milestone, the reserve follows the surviving organisms into care. Closing the order releases the factory commitment without deleting its living output or returning supplies already used.

A failed qualification can leave fewer usable organisms, a care-only group, or an unfinished handling group. It cannot pay the promised maximum and then report that the same animals died during qualification. Usable output, living care obligations, and actual losses must reconcile to the same order.

### Husbandry and food demand

Husbandry Lots cover the provisioned feeding, housing, ordinary animal care, and handling supplies for large living organisms. They are distinct from human Clinical Material Lots and from any biological weapon payload. The reference production order prepares 40 Husbandry Lots over 30 days using one civilian factory, 100 Support Equipment, 500 fuel, and a food allocation quoted by the famine owner. Those are four cost types.

The food allocation must be expressed in the famine owner's actual supported units and debited or committed once through that owner. No conversion from an unspecified food-security score into tonnes is assumed. The amount is deliberately left to a reviewed crosswalk because the inspected famine documentation does not supply a universal food-to-animal conversion. Until that contract exists, husbandry production is an explicit implementation dependency. It cannot quietly become free food manufactured from Support Equipment.

An animal's ration remains part of the receiving area's food demand while it lives there. The husbandry receipt tells the food owner which part has already been provisioned, so the same feed is not also removed through another projected demand. Unprovisioned demand remains visible to the food owner. A relocation changes the actual demand location once. Ending military use does not end the animal's need for care.

Healthy reserve animals use half the full operational husbandry quantity. Full operational reference quantities per 30 days are five lots for twenty reconnaissance animals, fifteen lots for fifty support animals, and twenty lots for twenty-five assault organisms. Quantities scale to actual surviving animals and round upward to whole lots for the complete assignment. Dividing an assignment cannot reduce its combined bill. A reserve cannot claim operational benefits while paying the resting rate.

### Coverage and service capacity

One reference Living Service Unit can support up to 100,000 eligible living people in one serviced state. This is a coverage ceiling, not a claim that every person in the state has been reached. Actual coverage is the least of the contract, the supplied service capacity, the eligible living population, and any smaller limit imposed by the disease or receiving owner.

The institution can service five units from a qualified works site under the reference profile. Further sites need actual staff and physical capacity. A country cannot move the same five units between reports and claim five units at every endpoint. A service unit has one current physical assignment. A larger district needs additional units or must accept partial coverage.

A selected service shows its covered population or organism count, its next maintenance deadline, and the important operating condition. Those contextual values replace unrelated details in the current view. They do not add a permanent national ecology meter beside Authority, treatment, research, and all the famine and migration values.

The most effective applicable Wu contribution governs each overlapping purpose. A hospital filter and a second filter do not repeatedly halve the same exposure. A completed cleanup and an ongoing cure can address different owner-defined components, but the owner must distinguish those components. The producer reports supported coverage and performed work. The disease owner decides the actual accepted change.

### Maintenance, withdrawal, and retirement

A standard installation is funded for a 90-day service period, including ordinary maintenance and removal. The final 30 days reserve enough service capacity for an orderly withdrawal. A renewal replaces the future service obligation. It does not renew the research reward, undo earlier damage, or restore a lost unit.

A shortage first interrupts the benefit that depends on the missing input. Staff then attempt the recorded withdrawal or care-only state. A dangerous failure requires a genuine neglected organism, incompatible conditions, or damaged containment. Missing a payment does not automatically create a nationwide epidemic. The local consequences follow the organisms that actually remain.

A recovered reusable assembly returns to available stock only after inspection establishes that it is still viable. Used clinical material, food, fuel, elapsed factory time, and completed field work remain spent. A retired animal stays in the living record until transferred, discharged into a supported non-military setting, or actually lost. The government cannot obtain a manpower refund by calling it equipment.

## L01: living production qualification

L01 establishes the first independently reproducible living design. The player selects a source with a defined useful property and a service the institute can explain in gameplay terms. A medical institute might begin with an enclosed assembly that tolerates a hospital's conditions. A military institute might begin with a working animal that can follow a handler and return to shelter. Neither proposal needs to begin with a pathogen.

The first choice concerns the work the organism will perform. Public-service qualification prioritises stable operation, independent maintenance, and an exit from institute custody. Restricted military qualification tests handling, field supply, recall, and coexistence with ordinary troops. The same institute can qualify both uses through separate relevant work. It does not receive a permanent technological exclusion for choosing the public use first.

Qualification must show a useful output under the declared conditions, a recognisable loss of performance outside those conditions, and a practical retirement or recovery path. A filter that works only while its housing remains intact can qualify as a fixed installation. It cannot simultaneously qualify as a mobile cleanup organism. A strong animal that has never completed a handling return cannot qualify for field deployment.

The first independent result gives the host a reproducible standard and permits its matching production order. Independent staff can maintain that standard after Wu departs. Wu retains his unusual ability to adapt it to a genuinely new medical problem when he is available. The public institution therefore gains lasting competence without receiving every later design for free.

A flawed result can reveal narrow usefulness. The government may keep a hospital-only version, fund a corrected field qualification, or retire it and recover reusable habitat equipment. The correction reuses authenticated work and does not restart the original project from zero. The reference corrective qualification takes 60 days, one research lane, one civilian factory, 75 Support Equipment, and 25 Political Power. It consumes four cost types and resolves the identified limitation rather than rerolling unrelated rewards.

Unexplained behaviour creates an observation case tied to the affected design and generation. The player chooses between continued controlled observation, restricted use, and retirement. The institute must not announce sentience because one animal disobeys. If later evidence supports an independent person or organised actor, the appropriate owner must recognise that transition before political consequences use it. Research cannot classify a living human or a clone as nonhuman merely to escape civilian obligations.

Presentation should centre on the first useful interaction between people and the new organism. Show the trained animal completing a difficult return, the maintained hospital installation, or the recovery of a failed assembly. Working diagrams belong in the research record, while the report image should depict the actual service. Option tone can distinguish practical confidence, guarded approval, and Wu's impatience without drafting final text.

## L02: enclosed biological filters

L02 places a maintained living assembly inside a defined service installation. Its first useful role is protecting the passage between an affected district and a functioning institution. Hospitals, receiving centres, ports, and depots become different deployment settings because they have different access problems. The installation protects the biological exposure that its approved standard can address. It does not remove chemical residues, radiation, volcanic ash, or a supernatural effect.

The government chooses a hospital-centred assignment or a transport-centred assignment. A hospital assignment protects eligible patients and staff at the supplied medical endpoint. A transport assignment protects an owner-recognised receiving or distribution point. The latter helps an existing route remain medically usable, but it cannot establish a sea corridor, repair a railway, open a border, or grant permission to enter another country.

The reference installation of five units commits those five units, fifteen Clinical Material Lots, fifty Support Equipment, and 500 fuel. Smaller assignments scale the three field allocations to the units actually installed. Preparation takes 30 days, followed by the funded 90-day service period. Existing trained staff and a suitable building are requirements. They must have capacity for the assignment, but this action does not add a fifth personnel or factory payment.

At complete coverage, the proposed filter contribution halves compatible new biological exposure through that endpoint. Partial coverage scales the contribution to the living people actually served. This contribution is evaluated once alongside the earlier Wu protection standard for the same purpose. It does not halve the already reduced result again. Existing infection continues to require treatment, and outgoing exposure through unserviced endpoints remains possible.

One unit assigned to a 100,000-person receiving district can provide its full local contribution. One unit promised to a million-person state supplies only one tenth of that coverage. A port installation cannot claim the inland hospitals that have no working delivery connection. The player sees the practical choice between protecting a smaller critical point well and spreading available service across a larger area.

Damage can interrupt the housing, the living assembly, or the supply route separately. Intact living units can enter care-only storage while workers repair the housing. Destroyed units cannot return through a maintenance renewal. A loss of medical access cancels the claimed protection for the affected people without reversing treatment they already received. The owner retains the original exposure, contamination, and investigation history.

The local recovery choices are repair in place, transfer an intact unit to a reachable service point, or retire the damaged assembly. A standard repair uses one civilian factory, fifty Support Equipment, 500 fuel, and five Clinical Material Lots over 45 days. It restores only the recorded damaged installation. A successful repair does not create a second one. In a crowded city, the more important choice can be which hospital remains protected during that interruption.

Independent maintenance can keep these filters working after Wu is absent. A changed disease reference still needs adaptation through the cure and qualification systems. Possessing a filter is not permanent immunity to every future disease. The visible installation should change between operating, partially supplied, withdrawn, and damaged states, with a clear non-colour cue for each condition.

## L03: disease-eating service colonies

L03 develops a living cleanup assembly for an already identified biological burden. The organisms remain fictional and are described through their work, care, limits, and visible behaviour. The project supplies no recipe or real mechanism for producing or releasing an organism.

The distinction from L02 is practical. Filters protect a supplied endpoint against additional exposure. L03 commits a maintained team to reducing an existing, compatible local burden. The cleanup team can work in a district that is already damaged, but it must have access to the assigned ground and a way to recover its living stock. It cannot cleanse an entire front from a distant laboratory.

Research requires the current strain or biological reference, M14, and an approved response from the owning disease system. A country may complete the design using a contained case while healthy. Operational cleanup remains unavailable until an actual burden exists. The institute cannot create an outbreak to satisfy its own research reward or count a simulated case as a national disease incident.

The reference five-unit assignment commits five cleanup units, thirty Clinical Material Lots, one hundred Support Equipment, and 1,000 fuel. Preparation takes 30 days. The following 90-day operation has a named target and maintained access. The service staff, removal capacity, and current compatibility record must remain valid throughout. The government can recall the team early when military control becomes uncertain.

The proposed full operation offers work equivalent to removing 40 percent of the compatible local biological burden attributable to the covered portion of the selected state. It cannot remove more than the owner still recognises at settlement. The initial burden is a work ceiling, not a frozen promise that survives reinfection or reclassification. Continued disease activity can add new burden while the team works. The owning system decides whether the remaining conditions meet its normal containment, recovery, or cure thresholds.

For example, an owner can expose one hundred units of eligible local burden when the assignment begins. A fully supplied operation can offer forty units of cleanup work. If another valid response has already reduced that burden to twenty, this assignment cannot remove forty more or transfer the unused twenty to a different state. If twenty new units appear during the operation, the owner records those new units through their real source. The cleanup does not erase their history or manufacture an earlier cure date.

The strongest overlapping Wu cleanup contribution is used for the same work interval and component. M14 provides the prior medical competence. L03 changes how that competence is delivered and sustained. An independent hospital cure can continue treating living patients while the service colony works on a separate local biological burden, provided the disease owner supports that distinction. No second mortality reduction is assumed from two names for the same response.

Incomplete work can still matter. An orderly withdrawal retains accepted work already performed and returns only surviving, inspected units. An access failure strands the actual remaining team or forces it into protected withdrawal. An incompatible reference stops effective cleanup and opens an adaptation project. Neither failure automatically creates a stronger pathogen. A harmful side effect needs a real local incident with a known affected population or organism group.

The important choices are whether to finish a limited safe assignment, replace it with an updated design, or withdraw from a district the army can no longer protect. A government may be tempted to leave the colony behind because it still appears useful. Doing so ends the supervised service contract. Any later uncontrolled activity belongs to the exact escaped colony and the owner-approved local consequence, not an immediate global epidemic or a free permanent cleaning modifier.

Recovery can include collecting identifiable units, treating harmed staff through existing medicine, and closing the damaged service point. A correction of compatibility uses the current cure research path. A physical recovery operation commits the recovery team, fifty Support Equipment, 500 fuel, and ten Clinical Material Lots over a proposed 60 days. Its objective is to regain access and account for the recorded units. It does not refill the stock to its original maximum.

## L04: rat-vector defence

L04 addresses plague support carried by eligible local vectors. It begins with the real case exposed by Black Plague or another compatible disease owner. Ordinary nuisance animals, a hostile country's identity, or a rumour about the Rat King do not establish a treatable vector population.

The government selects one of two operating approaches for an assignment. Repellent stations protect a prepared receiving point through a maintained fictional living assembly. Predator units work through a bounded accessible district under handlers. Their purpose, physical stock, food demand, and failure conditions differ. Completing the project does not make the two approaches interchangeable or create an unlimited predator population.

Repellent stations use the standard Living Service Unit production profile and the L02 installation profile. Their proposed full-coverage contribution reduces the compatible vector-entry component by 50 percent at the protected endpoint. They do not clear established plague, reduce a Rat Nation's divisions, or remove a Royal Node. A maintained station can help keep a hospital or food depot useful after nearby areas become unsafe.

Predator units use twenty qualified fictional ward animals per operational team. Their appearance should be unfamiliar and coherent, with no identification as a real species selected for a real biological-control programme. Each team is manufactured and trained through the reconnaissance-animal production profile, with a distinct ward-animal design identity. Reconnaissance animals cannot be renamed into ward animals after production.

The reference field assignment commits twenty ward animals, twenty-five trained handlers, ten Husbandry Lots, and fifty Support Equipment for 60 days. Human handlers are committed from an existing available group and remain separate from animal stock. The assignment aims to reduce the owner-proven local vector burden by up to 35 percent at full supported coverage. It is strongest in a small accessible district and weak where the owner cannot establish access to the affected ground.

The food bill creates a meaningful difference from repellent stations. A hungry district may be able to maintain a fixed station and lack the food to support predators. The government can fund food and safe housing through their actual owners, choose the fixed method, or withdraw the animals. It cannot feed a growing predator force by subtracting an unverified number of rats from a military country.

The player can protect a grain depot, support a refugee receiving point, or work along a recaptured urban district. These are different strategic priorities. Protecting one location does not count as serving the other two. A wider programme uses additional qualified teams, supplied routes, and separate food allocations. Assignment size scales within the actual staff and husbandry capacity rather than a universal campaign cap.

An organised Rat Nation remains an independent actor. The medical programme can weaken the biological contribution the rat owner exposes. It cannot issue annexation, remove the Rat King, consume Brood Mass as if it were field vermin, or decide that a surviving brood has been defeated. Rat military action can destroy or capture the installations in ordinary play. Any specific response to the new ward animals must be an owner-approved reaction, not an invented automatic rat evolution.

Failures include animals refusing a return, an abandoned feeding site, or a station that displaces the problem beyond the protected point. A displaced vector burden is not silently deleted. The disease owner must accept its destination and amount before any movement is applied. Without that evidence, the result remains a failed local exclusion or unresolved movement, not a guessed neighbouring infection.

An orderly withdrawal returns surviving animals to a supported reserve and preserves the completed local work. A missing animal becomes a tracked recovery problem. Human casualties from recovery are recorded only when they actually occur. The report should focus on changed activity at the protected site, the handlers' experience, and the practical limits observed. It should not announce the extermination of a political actor because a medical operation succeeded.

## L05: agricultural survival medicine

L05 applies the institute's biological knowledge to existing food production. The project is offered when a crop, herd, or working-animal problem is medically compatible and materially affects an owner-recognised food shortage. The institute can also qualify a preventive standard from a contained agricultural case during a healthy period. Operational benefits still require actual cultivated ground or surviving animals.

The crop and livestock assignments share the project foundation but keep distinct targets. A crop assignment protects a diagnosed cultivated area through the next relevant production interval. A livestock assignment treats an existing herd, reduces its diagnosed biological losses, and supports continued care. Neither creates food, replacement animals, workers, or agricultural land at research completion.

The agricultural Living Service Unit profile produces five units of the selected standard. Installation uses the L02 four-cost profile and a 30-day preparation. The reference service period is 90 days, extended only through a funded renewal when the food owner's production interval requires it. The local agricultural staff, safe access, and compatible production remain requirements. A district with abandoned fields and no workforce cannot receive a full harvest effect merely because its installation is supplied.

At full coverage, the proposed benefit prevents up to half of the disease-attributed production loss the famine owner has accepted. The reference is the biological part of the current shortfall. Suppose an eighty-unit production shortfall contains forty units explicitly attributed to a compatible crop disease. The maximum proposed improvement is twenty units, leaving a sixty-unit shortfall before the owner evaluates weather, extraction, transport, and other conditions. It does not halve the complete famine score or turn the whole eighty units into food.

The famine owner applies the resulting production condition at the appropriate interval. It continues to calculate food need, reserves, access, mortality, and recovery. A successful treatment can still leave a district hungry because the harvest is being removed, a railway remains cut, or too many people have arrived. The institute's medical report should identify that limit without presenting a failed political or transport system as medical resistance.

Livestock service preserves actual surviving animals. Replacing lost animals needs a distinct paid production and husbandry commitment under the appropriate owner. L05 cannot use human replication stock, a disease-death total, or a biological payload as its replacement resource. A herd brought into a different district carries its physical and food obligations rather than appearing in both locations.

The government chooses between protecting domestic production, maintaining food for a relief district, and servicing a foreign partner that permits the work. A foreign assignment carries no automatic extraction rights. Selling treatment can create a commercial dependence through Part 4, but the food itself remains with its actual producer and owner unless a separate lawful or coercive transaction moves it.

A failed assignment can leave a working standard with a limited season, an incompatible herd, or a treatment that requires more follow-up than the district can support. The response is adaptation, reduced coverage, or withdrawal. A new failure does not reset the harvest clock. Destroyed crops and slaughtered animals remain lost. Evidence of a deliberate cover-up or reckless use persists after the biological problem is corrected.

This family should make food security more responsive to medical choices without making famine a disease that Wu can cure. Its strongest successful outcome is a district that can keep producing and supplying its existing people. Later recovery then comes from the food, transport, migration, and reconstruction work that the government actually completes.

## L06: nonhuman clinical services

M16 established that some nonhuman biological conditions can be treated. L06 turns a qualified case into a continuing clinical service for the same family. The programme can serve a state's own unusual formations, an independent nonhuman society that accepts care, or living people held inside a nonhuman-controlled region when the population and custody owners expose a valid medical case.

An independent nonhuman recipient can receive a qualified foreign service without becoming an eligible resident host for Wu's arrival event. Its existing country and population classifiers remain unchanged.

A service is qualified per family and problem. A treatment that works for a paleogenetic creature does not automatically work for a xenobiological organism. A rat population's existing plague immunity does not prove immunity to every condition or permit the institute to invent a treatable illness. Mechanical damage in a robot belongs to mechanical maintenance. A supernatural being has a medical case only when its owner identifies an actual biological component.

The first clinical commitment selects the recipients, the accessible care site, and the authority that can consent or provide lawful custody. For a dependent animal, its owner and qualified handlers supply the care authority. For an independent sentient group, the agreement must include its representative or the relevant owner-defined consent. Defeating a military unit does not grant Wu ownership of every member of its species.

The reference clinical assignment commits the selected patients or cohort, one civilian factory, ten Clinical Material Lots, and fifty Support Equipment over 60 days. That profile is for a bounded ordinary case. A large organism group uses an owner-quoted scale based on actual patients and needs. The quote changes the quantities, not the four cost types. Urgent injury, a difficult family, or a long follow-up can change the supported duration before the order is accepted.

The service has three useful outcomes. Treatment can remove the compatible illness, stabilise patients until another service is available, or establish a safe maintenance standard that prevents recurrence under the same conditions. None of these outcomes grants recruitment, changes a country's species classification, installs a control doctrine, or makes its recipients loyal to Wu. Military fitness is returned only for surviving individuals whose treatment and ordinary readiness support it.

Paleogenetic treatment remains separate from the reserve, hatchery, transport, and feeding owners. Xenobiological treatment preserves the existing exclusive control architecture. The institute can cooperate with the current control operator without selecting a second chemical, neural, machine, or researched doctrine. A medical apparatus is not permission to bypass that exclusivity. Treatment may need to wait until the legitimate control owner can provide a safe operating state.

A living infected civilian in rat-held territory remains a living patient. A fully dead reanimated soldier does not become a living citizen through L06. The owner can expose a maintenance or shutdown response for an engineered undead unit, but that belongs to its existing identity and cannot restore the person's spent first-settlement entitlement. A successful medical assignment never deletes an independent country from the map.

Discharge requires a viable destination. Returning a recovered organism to a collapsing facility can recreate the same problem. The institute can negotiate continued care, a different qualified custodian, or a supported return. Human population movement goes through migration. Nonhuman animals move through their actual cohort or unit owner. Neither route uses a fictional population refund to make the transfer convenient.

The political tension comes from access, trust, confidentiality, and continuing need. A recipient may accept medicine and reject military inspection. Wu may demand research access as a price, or public physicians may insist on separating care from that demand. These choices feed the later foreign and rivalry systems through real agreements and evidence. This part supplies the medical transaction and its limits, without declaring those later systems complete.

The clinical scenes should preserve the dignity and distinct physical needs of the recipients. Show the handling equipment, the adapted care space, and the difficulty of communication where relevant. Avoid treating an entire people as a specimen shelf. No extra portrait family follows automatically from adding a clinical agreement.
