# From replacement tissue to another living person (block 1)

## From replacement tissue to another living person

The institute already knows how to supply replacement blood, restore damaged tissue, support failing organs, and keep an injured person alive while treatment continues. Replication begins when Wu proposes using that medical foundation to produce a separate living person. The hospital patient keeps their identity and history. The new person begins a different life, even when the institute has used the patient's likeness and recorded skills as its starting reference.

The government must decide what it is asking the institute to preserve. A ward needs reliable replacement material. A surgical service needs another qualified surgeon. An army needs trained people who can receive orders, use equipment, and survive deployment. Wu wants someone who can continue his work after he is gone. These demands share facilities and knowledge, but they do not have interchangeable results. A warehouse of medical material contains no recruitable people, and a completed human body does not arrive with a military career.

This part develops those demands as connected baseline programmes. M07 and M08 remain the existing blood, tissue, and organ foundations. M09 supplies regenerative knowledge, M10 supplies preservation experience, and the earlier independent teams, rehabilitation, and service archives remain useful. Their rewards are not granted again. None of the replication projects requires soldier enhancement, an engineered-zombie programme, high Wu Authority, or an evolution unless a particular optional combination actually uses that earlier work.

All new project codes, route names, product names, quantities, costs, durations, and numerical effects below are working design proposals. They are not final localisation or approved balance. The growth and memory mechanisms are fictional devices expressed through game time, institutions, and resources. They supply no real biological methods.

## What the player is trying to build

The early objective is a dependable medical service that survives the loss of one exceptional doctor. Replacement material should make an operating hospital less vulnerable to a single exhausted stock or missing specialist. The next objective is a small group of capable new people who can work outside Wu's immediate supervision. Military production becomes attractive when the country has equipment and industry but cannot replace its losses through ordinary recruitment. Personal continuity becomes attractive when the institute's remaining work depends on knowledge that its ordinary staff still cannot reproduce.

Each objective creates a different commitment. Medical material needs continuous manufacture and delivery. Specialists need teaching, supervised work, and a vacancy that makes their training useful. Replacement personnel need an actual destination and a service establishment. Clone formations need a continuing replacement source for their recorded cohorts. A continuity copy needs a body, a dated archive, clinical qualification, and a recognised place inside an existing institution.

A public institute can complete the entire sequence with independent review. It can also stop after tissue supply or specialist replication and remain a strong medical route. The decision to create people does not hand them to Wu as private followers. Institutional control grows only when the government actually concedes appointment, custody, distribution, or command powers. A copy can be grateful to Wu and still choose a different employer or refuse the office prepared for it.

## R01: replacement material that stays medical

R01 turns the results of M07 and M08 into a traceable service product, using the working label Clinical Material Lot. A lot represents an organised supply assignment for compatible replacement material, storage, and clinical handling. It is not a dose, an organ count, a recipe, or a person. The earlier treatment owner still determines which living patients can benefit and what their recovery means.

A proposed production order makes 40 lots over 30 days, committing one civilian factory and spending 100 Support Equipment and 500 fuel. Production requires a functioning medical fabrication site. The civilian factory remains occupied for the full order. A lost site interrupts the order and preserves its paid and consumed portions under the production record. Completion adds only the finished lots that the order actually produced.

Hospitals assign these lots to existing organ replacement, regeneration, preservation, or recovery work. A lot supplements an appropriate treatment allocation. It cannot replace the disease-specific cure, the hospital, the attending staff, or the route to the patient. The same material cannot simultaneously satisfy a hospital order and a replication nursery's care reservation.

The new capability is useful before whole-person replication exists. The government can hold a modest reserve against disruption, support a foreign hospital with copied clinical knowledge, or place material production under an independent university network. A damaged lot is withdrawn from the affected service, and the hospital can turn to an alternative manufacturer if the relevant standard has been shared. Wu's disappearance does not invalidate material that was already certified.

Material failure produces a supply or treatment problem, not an automatic new disease. Patients already receiving the affected material need reassessment through their own treatment programme. Unused material can be discarded without recording human deaths. The clinical record must distinguish a spoiled supply, a living patient's injury, and a death that the medical owner actually applied.

## R02: a reference for a person

A personal pattern archive contains two distinct records. One describes the intended biological likeness in the event's fictional terms. The other records demonstrated habits, professional experience, language, and remembered methods. The second record is dated and incomplete. A later copy cannot know an event that happened after the reference was recorded unless someone teaches it or supplies a later authenticated record.

The public route uses a consenting participant and an independent witness. A surgeon can agree to preserve medical competence without permitting military assignment, public impersonation, or unrestricted production of their likeness. A pilot can permit a training reference while retaining their current commission and family identity. Permission is recorded by use, not as one permanent consent to everything the institute might later propose.

A reference participant remains the original person. Making an archive does not dismiss them, transfer their character, copy their advisor bonus, remove their nationality, or create another office. A completed archive grants a capability to prepare future people. It has no manpower or population output.

The government chooses institutional custody, personal custody, or shared custody of the completed record. Institutional custody allows a supervised successor team to continue authorised work if Wu leaves. Personal custody makes future access depend on the participant and any lawful successor agreement. Shared custody gives the institute an operational reference and an independent custodian a verification copy. Copying information in this way does not copy the person's body or multiply a pending production order.

A secret programme can falsify permission or conceal the archive. Its advantage is the ability to attempt work that the public settlement would refuse. It leaves specific evidence of the unauthorised use and a later dispute over the resulting people. Concealment does not make those people obedient, remove their need for care, or erase the original participant's ownership and history. These are fictional institutional consequences, without practical methods for coercion or impersonation.

The first archive report should show the participant recognising some recorded habits and disputing others. The player sees which field is represented, the recording date, and the authorised use. Exact later disagreements remain undisclosed. Final writing should avoid treating a transcript as a perfect mind or turning the scene into an inventory of technical settings.

## R03: the first separate life

R03 establishes the possibility of producing a living person through the event's fictional replication process. Completing the research does not create a cohort. The government must place a production order, fund its whole declared schedule, identify the receiving service, and protect the site.

A production order begins as non-person material and work in progress. Its first living-person milestone is a distinct recorded outcome. From that milestone onward, the people exist, need care, and remain in the finite cohort record even if they are immature, medically unstable, unqualified, or no longer useful to the sponsoring institution. Cancelling the programme after this milestone cannot turn them back into refunded material.

The first successful case should be small. The government sees a separate person who resembles the participant but has no lived history in the country. The copy may recognise a room from the archive without having visited it. It may reproduce a practiced movement while failing to understand the situation in which that movement is appropriate. These observations establish the difference between a useful reference and a finished professional.

The host can continue under the public charter, restrict further production while completing the existing person's care, or stop the expansion and admit the person to ordinary life. A refusal to expand does not kill the first copy or remove the knowledge already learned. A classified host can hide the case, creating a custody dispute and a future discovery path, but concealment supplies no extra research lane or free production.

An existing living patient is never consumed as the raw input for a new person. A repaired organ, amputated tissue, a stored archive, and a reanimated body also do not supply a second claim on the same person's population or military manpower. The finite birth record begins only when this production programme creates a genuinely new living individual in the fiction.

## Production orders, sites, and receiving capacity

There are two production scales. Personal orders create one or a few individually referenced people. Cohort orders create a larger group around a compatible standard, with individual identity records retained inside the batch. They share the finite owner contract but have different fixed preparation costs and cannot be freely relabelled halfway through production.

The proposed normal cohort cell produces up to 250 people. A standard order uses one, four, or ten cells for a planned maximum of 250, 1,000, or 2,500 people. Every cell commits one civilian factory for 270 days and spends 150 Support Equipment, 1,000 fuel, and 10 Clinical Material Lots. The first 180 days develop the production order. The final 90 days are protected maturation after the living-person milestone. These are fictional campaign durations, not biological claims.

| Normal cohort order | Maximum new people | Factories committed for 270 days | Support Equipment | Fuel | Clinical Material Lots |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Small receiving class | 250 | 1 | 150 | 1,000 | 10 |
| Replacement cohort | 1,000 | 4 | 600 | 4,000 | 40 |
| Large institutional intake | 2,500 | 10 | 1,500 | 10,000 | 100 |

Personal orders use a separate preparation profile. One factory is committed for the same 270-day normal schedule. The proposed direct cost is 100 Support Equipment, 1,000 fuel, and 10 Clinical Material Lots for the order, plus 10 Support Equipment, 100 fuel, and 5 lots for each planned person. The first-order sizes are one or five people. A one-person order therefore costs 110 Support Equipment, 1,100 fuel, and 15 lots. A five-person order costs 150 Support Equipment, 1,500 fuel, and 35 lots. The higher personal cost pays for individual reference and supervision in game terms.

An order can produce fewer viable people than its planned maximum. The actual living count is recorded once. A failed material stage has no living casualties. A loss after the living milestone removes actual people and uses the appropriate Deaths owner. Neither outcome awards a consolation manpower payment. Completing research or repeating the result report cannot repeat the production output.

For personal and cohort production, Support Equipment and fuel are consumed at admission as the order's setup and running allocation. Cancellation does not refund them or elapsed factory time. Clinical Material Lots leave freely available stock at admission but remain a dedicated care reservation. Before any living person exists, cancellation can return the proven unused reservation once. After the living milestone, that reservation remains with the surviving people and their receiving service. Cancelling the production order releases its factory commitment without cancelling those people's care. A failed output report cannot return supplies that another completed receipt has already assigned or consumed. The same rule applies to the accelerated profile.

The site must have a receiving plan before work starts. The plan names the hospital or maturation institute, the destination for adult reception, the supply route, and the authority responsible for existing people if the sponsor cancels. These are actual places and institutions. They are not additional resource currencies. The committed factories express production capacity, while the institute's ordinary staffing and clinical requirements express whether the cell can operate at all.

A country can expand through additional paid sites and orders. There is no lifetime quota on people produced. Every additional cell occupies its own real industrial commitment for the full schedule. A secondary laboratory does not automatically become a nursery, and a new copy of Wu does not multiply the factory output of an existing one. Concurrent orders compete for the same available factories, clinical material, fuel, and receiving capacity.

The ordinary research lanes remain research commitments. Production does not consume a third research lane after the relevant programme is learned. Conversely, completing a production order does not free a lane occupied by an unrelated research project. This distinction keeps an established institute capable of manufacturing and researching at the same time without turning either activity into an unrecorded capacity grant.

## Maturation and adult reception

The maturation period develops a living person's ability to function independently. A physically complete body can still be unready for ordinary life or professional service. The programme must establish adult physical readiness, orientation, communication, and basic decision-making before any military training or deployment. An unfinished candidate cannot be used to fill a manpower shortage by skipping reception.

At the living-person milestone the order's actual cohort is fixed. A supervised maturation class then continues for the proposed 90 days. A damaged nursery can delay completion. It cannot erase elapsed time or send the people back to an earlier non-person state. Evacuated people retain their age, condition, and unfinished work. A receiving institute resumes the remaining schedule after it can supply them.

The initial Clinical Material reservation covers normal maturation and the first 90 days of adult reception at the recorded site. The minimum cell reservation is paid per admitted cell and does not become cheaper by splitting the batch after admission. Unused reserved care can follow those people to a legitimate receiving site. It cannot be refunded as unrestricted money or used to count a second group as supplied.

Reception begins when the participants are ready to leave protected maturation. All classes first complete common adult orientation. Civilian reception teaches ordinary work, local language where necessary, and life outside the institute. Military and specialist classes begin their separate professional periods only after that orientation. A continuity candidate remains a person undergoing assessment, with no automatic right to Wu's office.

The sponsor chooses a destination, but the public charter must include a refusal route. Someone who refuses military or specialist service enters civilian reception when a viable destination exists. Refusal wastes the sponsor's intended training opportunity and can delay a deployment. It does not make the copy a failed batch or justify deleting it from the living count. The classified route can produce a dispute over withheld choice, with service refusal, exposure, and loss of staff support as concrete consequences.

No production receipt changes the death count of the reference participant. A copy created after that participant's death is a new person with an inherited reference. The source person's death, funeral, awards, and earlier office remain part of the campaign's history.

## A finite cohort has one remaining entitlement

The shared clone owner needs a distinct medical-replacement mode for these people. The existing `clone_equipment` economy remains unchanged. It continues to serve its existing Kruger, Mengele, and other approved consumers. Wu's finite mode must not place its people into that archetype, call a legacy access reward as a substitute, or add a second recurring manpower modifier.

The proposed finite mode has one physical unissued-person archetype and a matching owner-managed cohort record. One whole physical item corresponds to one living person still awaiting a first population or military settlement. Fractional engine stock is never rounded upward into another person. This item is an accounting representation for the game, not a consumable body part or a market licence to sell people. It is not ordinary industrial equipment that a country can manufacture directly from a technology unlock.

The owner creates physical stock only against the actual living output of an identified production order. The record identifies the source order, reference standard, birth milestone, current custodian, current location, maturity, care coverage, and unspent count. The stockpile is the physical quantity check. The cohort record supplies provenance and settlement authority. Neither can independently mint people when the other is absent or inconsistent.

Settlement can use only the lower of verified physical stock and the matching unspent entitlement. A country with unexplained extra stock receives no extra people from reconciliation. A country with a surviving cohort record but missing physical stock cannot manufacture the difference by calling a repair helper. The mismatch opens a custody investigation. Care remains due for known living people, and the unresolved amount cannot be spent elsewhere.

This mode belongs inside the shared clone owner. Wu owns his research, permissions, institutions, and personal continuity choices. He does not own a parallel national manpower bank. The extension is proposed design and remains unapproved and unimplemented. Until the owner contract and its engine-facing settlement paths are accepted and proven, implementation must leave those new paths blocked with their exact dependency. It must not replace them with legacy stock, a national manpower bonus, or a free division.

### First settlement destinations

A cohort has one first settlement for each person. That settlement moves responsibility from the unissued cohort into the destination that now represents the person. Historical membership remains visible after settlement, but it is no longer spendable entitlement.

| Destination | What is committed once | What remains after settlement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civilian reception | The exact surviving count enters a named state through an approved population transaction. | Ordinary population, food, shelter, migration, and medical systems own the people. Cohort history identifies origin without remaining redeemable. |
| Ordinary military replacement | The exact trained surviving count enters national military manpower once. | The native military pool and its consumers own subsequent recruitment, loss, and demobilisation. The finite cohort cannot be redeemed again. |
| Cohort formation | The exact trained count fills the selected new formation through one reviewed formation transaction. | Native unit manpower represents the soldiers. Cohort records track service and care but do not provide a second population or manpower grant. |
| Named specialist or continuity service | The person first completes civilian reception, then receives the qualified role. | The character or specialist record is an identity and assignment overlay on that already counted person. It grants no second headcount. |

The civilian transaction must not also award a free national manpower payment. Any native side effect that would create such an extra event-funded credit must be measured and neutralised through an approved population-owner contract. A guessed inverse of the existing population-loss helper is not sufficient evidence. Later ordinary conscription belongs to the population and recruitment owners. It must not reactivate the consumed replication entitlement.

The ordinary military path deliberately ends exact cohort control over each future posting. Once people join a fungible national manpower pool, the institute cannot reliably identify which later ordinary division received each person. That route therefore grants no batch-specific combat bonus and cannot later claim a matching population refund. It remains valuable because it supplies a finite, trained replacement contribution that the army can use normally.

The cohort-formation path preserves a narrower service identity and needs separate proof for exact admission, reinforcement, demobilisation, and transfer. It cannot use ordinary pool bookkeeping as evidence that every later replacement is a clone from the correct batch. Unsupported identity-preserving operations remain unresolved implementation requirements. A country-wide enhancement or a refreshed replacement division is not an accepted substitute.

### Conservation and historical records

For a produced cohort, the first-settlement record obeys this accounting identity:

`actual new living people = still-unsettled people + people first-settled once + deaths before first settlement`

Still-unsettled people include maturation, reception, reserved training, transfer, captivity, and unresolved custody. A person occupies only one of those locations at a time. A transfer changes location and custodian. It does not create another birth or another entitlement.

The first-settled total is historical. It includes people who later die in the ordinary population or military systems. Their later death does not reduce that historical total and reopen room for another settlement. Current living totals and historical first-settlement totals must never be added as if they were separate populations.

For example, a cohort produces 1,000 living people. After maturation, 600 enter ordinary military manpower, 250 enter a receiving state's population, and 150 remain in the institute. Five of the civilian arrivals later qualify as specialists. Their appointments add no people. If ten of the remaining institute residents die, the birth identity becomes 1,000 = 140 unsettled + 850 first-settled + 10 deaths before settlement. A later military loss among the 600 is recorded by the military owner without changing that identity or creating a replacement entitlement.

A shortage in an aggregate equipment counter is not proof of ten deaths. The people may have been transferred, captured, committed, or stranded by an interrupted transaction. The owner must establish the actual outcome before recording a loss. Unknown custody blocks further redemption of the disputed amount without declaring the people dead or assigning them to an unrelated country.

Material discarded before the living milestone creates no Deaths entry. Deaths among unsettled living people debit the actual cohort, retire the corresponding physical entitlement, and enter the shared Deaths system once. They do not also remove people from a state population that never contained them. After settlement, the ordinary civilian or military owner applies the real loss. Wu records the medical or institutional consequence without repeating the death transaction.

### Splits, mergers, and changed custody

A split assigns disjoint parts of a cohort to separate destinations. Their counts sum to the original unspent amount, and every part retains the same production and reference history. A merger groups records for presentation or a shared service order. It does not erase individual ages, flaws, spent entitlements, or care deadlines.

Transferring 200 people from a cohort of 1,000 never transfers the original 1,000-person entitlement. The sender loses authority over those 200 before the recipient can settle them. The recipient receives their remaining state, not a fresh version of the original production reward. Recapture, a change of country name, annexation, and later release cannot restart the same handover.

An unresolved delivery has one owner record and one pending outcome. Departure, transit, arrival, cancellation, and capture settle against that record. Restoring the sender after a failed delivery is permitted only when the people actually remain with or return to that sender. A refund cannot coexist with a delivered group. Physical stock that arrives through an untracked native transfer stays non-redeemable until its original entitlement is identified and the previous custodian's authority is closed.

Ordinary lend-lease and market routes must not be assumed to preserve personal provenance. The intended finite mode uses reviewed custody transfers, with the equipment transfer gates closed against unsupported routes. A captured holding remains an actual custody problem, with emergency care available even when its production history cannot yet be authenticated. The capture mechanics must be tested against the exact stockpile and site behaviour of the installed game.

## R04: copied skill needs apprenticeship

A replicated specialist enters a particular service, not a nationwide bonus pool. The host must name the vacancy, reference field, mentor, training site, and eventual employer. A completed body is eligible for assessment. It is not automatically a surgeon, scientist, ace, chief engineer, or operative.

The proposed apprenticeship has a 90-day common orientation, which can overlap the already funded adult reception when the destination is suitable. It then requires the field-specific service period below. The candidate must remain at a supplied training site with a qualified mentor and the relevant working institution. Losing the mentor pauses the uncompleted professional period. It does not erase skills already demonstrated.

An apprenticeship action commits the named candidate, one civilian factory, Clinical Material Lots, and Political Power or the appropriate service experience. Candidate commitment is counted as a spendable commitment. No fifth staff or manpower debit is hidden in the action. Existing mentors remain part of the institution's staffing requirement, and their occupied post cannot simultaneously supervise another incompatible assignment.

| Specialist field | Professional period after orientation | Clinical Material Lots per candidate | Fourth cost per class of up to five | Assignment unlocked |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Surgeon | 150 days | 10 | 35 Political Power | One supplied hospital's independent advanced-care service. |
| Researcher | 180 days | 10 | 50 Political Power | One existing research lane or archive-restoration assignment. |
| Pilot | 180 days | 10 | 35 Air Experience | One qualified training or operational air-service assignment through its owner. |
| Engineer | 120 days | 5 | 35 Political Power | One named facility, repair programme, or production-standard assignment. |
| Intelligence specialist | 210 days | 10 | 50 Political Power | One agency-owned training or analytical assignment with the appropriate access. |

The class limit is a supervision limit for one apprenticeship order, not a permanent cap on specialists. Additional classes need their own facilities and committed teaching time. Their cost is quoted for the whole class, and cancelled or transferred candidates cannot repeatedly claim its completion.

Every specialist timing benefit is a quoted reduction in the time needed to complete one identified assignment while qualified service continues. Appointment awards no completed work. The assignment retains its underlying remaining work, with the strongest applicable specialist contribution changing the working rate only while that person actually serves. A 20 percent time reduction therefore corresponds to completing the same remaining work in 80 percent of the ordinary time. Reassignment, dismissal, a replacement instructor, or reopening the same job cannot compound that rate or grant another block of completed work. Losing qualified service preserves only work actually completed, then pauses the dependent work or resumes it at the ordinary rate. This service-based interpretation governs all specialist duration benefits below.

### Surgeons

A qualified surgical copy can keep a named hospital's learned advanced-care standard operating when Wu is elsewhere. It does not invent a cure, staff an empty building, or create an additional treatment allocation. The hospital gains a capable local practitioner whose service can continue across a political change if its supplies and permissions survive.

The proposed payoff is a 25 percent reduction in the remaining time of eligible local organ-replacement or rehabilitation assignments, with one qualified surgical lead applying per hospital programme. This replaces any weaker copy-apprentice assistance on that assignment. It does not multiply with another copy of the same surgical reference. Patient eligibility, consumed treatment, and actual survivor accounting remain with the medical project.

A surgeon who has copied a narrow wartime specialty may perform reliably in that field and struggle with an unfamiliar patient group. The first difficult case tests judgement through a supervised choice. The government can retain the specialist within the qualified scope, pay for additional cross-training, or support independent practice through R12. A bad case creates an actual patient-care response, not a universal loss of all cloned surgeons.

### Researchers

A research copy works in a selected existing lane. Its main value is continuity of learned methods and specialised attention. It can complete eligible documented work when the original reference scientist is absent, repair a damaged archive, or support a project in a field that the reference actually understood.

The proposed specialist contribution reduces an eligible lane's projected remaining research duration by 20 percent while the qualified copy remains assigned. It cannot retroactively complete work, add a national research slot, or add a third primary institute lane. Several copies in the same lane provide redundancy and a paid teaching capacity. They cannot compound reductions of the same clock. Only the strongest applicable specialist contribution controls the current working rate.

A research copy does not inherit Kruger's personal project history, his six-field role, or the unique powers of his appointment. A compatible externally granted technology remains a learned capability with its actual provenance. The copy's successful work records its own contribution. An archive copied from a famous scientist is not authority to reproduce that scientist's engine character or exclusive event rewards.

### Pilots

A copied flight reference can shorten the distance between a new adult and useful training, but it cannot supply an aircraft, a combat record, or instinct for a mission the reference never flew. The service period requires an operating air school and compatible equipment. Current operational demands may change the assignment, with incomplete qualification retained rather than fabricated as success.

The first payoff is an experienced instructor for one air training establishment or a qualified replacement pilot contribution to the relevant air owner. A named operational pilot requires a supported native role and its own observed skill. No ace status, victory count, medals, or national air bonus is copied from the source person. The exact owner interface for pilot personnel must be verified before that operational route is implemented.

The narrative should allow a copy to recognise an obsolete cockpit and need retraining for the aircraft now in service. A captured archive can therefore be valuable without being an instant answer to an air-force manpower shortage. R12 can broaden judgement after actual service, but it does not make every aircraft type interchangeable.

### Engineers

An engineering copy is attached to a real facility or repair assignment. It can preserve a production standard through the loss of the original specialist, lead restoration of a damaged medical fabrication site, or teach another team to maintain equipment that the country already owns.

The proposed benefit is a 25 percent reduction in the remaining time of one eligible repair or standardisation assignment. It grants no free factory, growth cell, power source, railway, or resource deposit. Several engineers can cover different sites when the host funds them, but one project cannot repeatedly restart its clock to collect the same reduction.

An engineer's useful knowledge can outlive the office that trained them. On foreign transfer, the former institution keeps its own manuals and completed repairs. The receiving institution gets the person, their qualified field, and only the records actually transferred. It does not receive a copy of the former host's buildings or an automatic right to use every proprietary method.

### Intelligence specialists

An intelligence copy can serve as an analyst, instructor, or eventually a field specialist through the agency owner's real appointment route. Its reference may contain remembered languages and analytical methods. It does not contain current enemy plans, passwords, networks, contacts, or diplomatic secrets that were never recorded.

The initial assignment strengthens one evidence-analysis or archive-authentication task. A proposed 25 percent reduction in that task's projected remaining review time applies while the qualified analyst serves and the agency and relevant evidence remain available. It cannot increase the number of operative slots, create a free field operative, grant an enemy network, or supply current intelligence from an old memory.

A field deployment needs separate agency eligibility and its normal equipment, training, and operation costs. The character and operative systems are distinct in the consulted documentation, so a scientist-style token is not an accepted substitute for an operative. A copy discovered by a foreign service may become a witness or an asylum applicant. Its value depends on what it actually knows and is willing to disclose.

## Memory, testimony, and professional independence

Every specialist begins with a reference date, a qualified field, and a level of demonstrated independence. These are record attributes, not three new public meters. The player sees a short status such as supervised practice, qualified practice, or review required, with the relevant missing qualification stated nearby.

Memory problems should be specific. A copy may confidently apply an outdated method, confuse a remembered relationship with a current colleague, omit an unrecorded stage of a procedure, or rely on an instructor's authority in an unfamiliar situation. These incidents affect the associated assignment and open a meaningful response. They do not randomly remove all skills from every copy in the country.

Repeated copies of the same reference share some blind spots. Placing them together can preserve a scarce method quickly, but it can also leave a whole service without independent judgement. Mixing a copied specialist with an independently trained colleague costs teaching time and reduces that shared weakness. It does not create a new research family or add an unrelated national modifier.

R12 develops independent professional judgement after real supervised service. Completion allows an assessed copy to lead the same work without the original reference mentor, train later classes within its field, and evaluate a new case through ordinary study. It does not reconstruct missing personal memories or prove that the copy is the original person.

A failed independence assessment produces a specific remedial assignment lasting a proposed 60 days at the same type of working institution. It consumes five Clinical Material Lots per candidate and one civilian factory for the class. The candidate remains committed, making three cost types.
