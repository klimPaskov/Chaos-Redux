# R05: replacement soldiers for an existing army (block 2)

Verbatim part of [089_doctor_wu_spec_part_7_replication_and_continuity.md](089_doctor_wu_spec_part_7_replication_and_continuity.md), which indexes this specification.

## R05: replacement soldiers for an existing army

R05 creates a service establishment for people who have completed normal adult maturation. The army accepts a cohort for training, provides a real training site, and declares whether the graduates will enter ordinary replacement manpower or remain together for later cohort formation. The country cannot count an immature class as soldiers merely because an offensive is about to begin.

The proposed service course lasts 120 days after the 90-day common orientation. For every 1,000 trainees, it commits those 1,000 people, 90 Infantry Equipment for training, 20 Clinical Material Lots, and 25 Army Experience. Smaller courses scale people, rifles, and care to the actual group, with a minimum of five Army Experience per course. The full quote is calculated once for the admitted class. Splitting the class afterward does not refund experience or create another completion. Physical training and care commitments round upward to whole stock units for the complete class. Army Experience scales with class size and rounds upward to a multiple of five, with the five-point minimum. Person counts remain exact whole people. These cost-rounding rules never round a fractional physical person entitlement upward.

Training rifles are committed assets. Intact rifles return to the equipment stockpile when the course closes, and actual losses remain losses. The manpower route supplies trained people only. It does not also award an armed division or a second set of rifles. The clinical material is consumed as the training and care occur. A cancelled course can return unused material and intact committed rifles through its exact receipt, while spent teaching time and experience remain spent.

At graduation, the ordinary replacement route offers a one-time handover of the actual ready survivors. Those people are removed from finite unissued stock and credited to native military manpower once. An interrupted handover cannot leave both the original cohort and the new manpower available. The same commitment also closes any pending civilian admission for those people.

The army receives no automatic elite trait from this handover. Its advantage is the availability of a trained replacement intake backed by a long industrial programme. Existing divisions still need their equipment, supply, and ordinary reinforcement process. The country must choose whether scarce factories should make more rifles and trucks or sustain the next replication order.

A government that loses its war before graduation can complete civilian reception, retain a small service reserve, or negotiate a transfer to an ally that has a genuine vacancy and accepts the care obligation. The end of war does not delete the trainees. A country that can no longer house them must use the existing migration and reception owners, preserving the exact living count.

The military report should identify the receiving army's need and the actual class size. A class that is too small to fill a division should remain visibly too small. A disappointed staff can demand acceleration or accept a later intake, but its demand does not change the people already created.

## R06: a formation with a recorded generation

Cohort formations preserve the military identity of related replication classes. They can receive group-specific care, share a trained doctrine, and support a recorded service history. Their defining feature is controlled composition and continuity of support. They do not receive the legacy stockpile's recurring manpower effect.

The proposed standard battalion has 1,000 actual living personnel, including its ordinary officers and embedded medical staff. It uses 90 Infantry Equipment, two combat width, 70 organisation, and 20 strength as the initial comparison profile inherited from the inspected shared clone battalion. These inherited values are reference values for the proposed finite battalion, not a claim that it already exists or is balanced. Its defining new burden is the finite personnel supply and continuing generation care.

A proposed first formation contains five such battalions. It needs 5,000 trained ready people, 450 Infantry Equipment, 75 Clinical Material Lots for its first 90 days of service, and 20 Command Power. Formation organisation takes 90 days at an actual supplied military site. The people, equipment, care, and command allocation are the four cost types. There is no additional national manpower charge for those same 5,000 people and no separate manpower grant before creation.

The normal paid commissioning result is a new formation at its earned training state. It does not inherit the reference person's decorations, a donor formation's current experience, or the strength of a unit it replaces. Full-equipment and full-manpower creation can occur only when the exact physical contributions have been reserved and consumed in the same reviewed transaction. A failed creation restores only those contributions, with no second birth or first-settlement receipt.

The proposed steady care cost is five Clinical Material Lots per 1,000 living personnel for each 30-day service interval. A five-battalion formation therefore needs 25 lots per interval while fully manned. Its ordinary supply and rifle replacement remain native army requirements. Clinical care is paid against actual registered service strength, not the template's paper maximum. Exact measurement of that strength is an implementation requirement.

Formation strength can fall during combat. A batch cannot repair casualties by renaming the division, rebuilding its template, transferring its command, or deleting and recreating it. Replacement personnel must come from another trained finite cohort or an explicitly approved mixed-personnel policy. A mixed formation is no longer presented as a fully replicated generation, and any composition-dependent benefits must adjust to its actual recorded status.

### Reinforcement without another population grant

The intended pure-cohort reinforcement path reserves a specified number of ready people against one eligible understrength formation. It debits the remaining finite entitlement once and fills only the proved vacancy. Surplus people remain in their original reserve. The transaction cannot heal organisation, replace rifles, raise experience, or fill a second formation unless those outputs are separately supplied by their actual owners.

Native automatic reinforcement from an ordinary manpower pool must not silently turn a pure-cohort formation into a mixed one while the UI continues to claim complete generation control. The owner must prove either a supported restriction with exact cohort reinforcement or an explicit mixed-personnel state that accurately describes what occurred. The preferred pure-cohort path remains a required feature. An unavailable engine operation is a recorded blocker, not permission to reduce the design to a national modifier or pretend that all replacements came from the same source.

The new finite battalion therefore needs its own shared-owner unit contract. Reusing the existing `clone_infantry` token unchanged would retain its legacy equipment requirement and its provider assumptions. The intended new identity is a generic finite medical-cohort infantry consumer, with final identifiers reserved only after collision review. It is not a new Kruger random-technology family and is not an alias for the strictly gated Mengele refinement.

### Training and obedience

A standard cohort learns ordinary military discipline. Its order-following comes from training, officers, supply, and the institution that holds its commission. Wu does not gain personal command because the soldiers share his medical system. A formation can remain loyal to the state while demanding treatment from the institute.

The government can choose integrated command, a specialist corps, or an institute service contract. Integrated command places formations in the ordinary army with independent medical inspection. A specialist corps keeps related cohorts under trained officers and concentrates the care burden in a small number of depots. An institute contract gives Wu greater control of postings, treatment, and internal appointments. Only that actual concession increases his command leverage.

These arrangements produce different failures. Integrated command can overuse a formation whose medical restrictions are unfamiliar to ordinary staff. A specialist corps can lose much of its support when one depot falls. An institute contract can leave the army unable to order a deployment without bargaining over treatment and authority. Each has recovery through better command practice, distributed support, or a changed contract. None is a hidden permanent technology lock.

## Enhancement follows people and service

R06 can use the temporary endurance and combat courses from Part 6 after the relevant research and explicit admission. A formation receives the applicable course once, with the same tour, withdrawal, recovery, and supply rules. It does not receive a fresh course whenever a cohort is renamed or its command changes.

Permanent enhancement requires H05 and its real preparation. It is an optional branch. It never becomes a prerequisite for ordinary replacement soldiers, specialists, or continuity. The proposed enhanced-cohort commissioning profile uses actual ready people, Infantry Equipment, Alteration Preparation Packs, and Military Care Packs as its four cost types. It omits the standard commissioning Command Power payment and clinical-care reservation, which are replaced within this profile rather than charged as hidden fifth and sixth costs.

For five battalions, the initial proposal is 5,000 people, 450 Infantry Equipment, 25 Alteration Preparation Packs, and 75 Military Care Packs, with the Part 6 permanent-alteration training period of 120 days. The people settle directly into the final military formation once. They are not first credited to national manpower and then also counted as newly created enhanced bodies.

The enhanced cohort uses the H05 performance package and its lifetime-care consequences. The ordinary finite battalion's reference organisation and strength are not an extra bonus layered on top. A dedicated compatibility comparison must establish the final combined unit values before implementation. Repeated grants of research, a change of provider, or several copied instructors cannot stack the same enhancement again.

The Part 6 permanent service bill covers the enhanced cohort's ordinary continuing clinical care. The same monthly medical purpose must not also charge the standard five-lot cohort bill. A genuinely separate recall, reconstruction, or specialist treatment can have its own declared action, but it must identify the different work and its costs.

A permanent alteration can affect only a subset of a generation. That subset retains its own service history, care level, and altered status. Merging it with unaltered people cannot spread the enhanced benefits to the whole formation. Separating it afterward cannot create more altered people than were admitted. If exact mixed-composition effects are unsupported, that conversion remains blocked until the owner has an accepted solution.

## R07: generation care and recall

Generation care makes the people less dependent on one physician's private knowledge. It establishes a shared clinical record for related cohorts, teaches independent teams to recognise known weaknesses, and creates a practical recall route. It does not guarantee that all members are identical or that one treatment fits every later generation.

A normal settled civilian cohort uses ordinary hospitals after reception. During its initial follow-up, it retains a generation-care appointment and an identified provider. The proposed follow-up lasts 180 days and costs five Clinical Material Lots per 1,000 people per 30-day interval, rounded up only at the whole service-order level. Where the initial production reservation still covers the same interval, that reservation pays first. The country does not pay two providers for the same promised care.

After a clean completed follow-up, ordinary civilian care becomes sufficient unless a specific continuing condition exists. The public route can therefore produce people who no longer need Wu's exclusive treatment network. Military cohorts keep their service-related bill while deployed. Enhanced people keep the distinct Part 6 obligation, and a continuity copy can retain a specific medical condition if its actual development created one.

### Batch flaws

A flaw belongs to a defined source standard, production order, or training reference. It may affect several related groups, but it cannot automatically jump to every clone, every patient treated by Wu, or every person of the donor's nationality. The discovery identifies the suspected scope. A review then establishes which cohorts require action.

The useful flaw families are incomplete physical endurance, unstable recovery after prolonged service, shared training errors, a missing qualification, accelerated decline after a compressed schedule, and a disputed or corrupted reference. These are fictional gameplay conditions. They do not specify real biological targets or laboratory mechanisms.

An endurance flaw reduces the affected formation's sustained organisation recovery until rest and treatment are completed. The proposed penalty is 20 percent to organisation recovery, with a 60-day supervised recovery assignment. A training flaw causes poor response to a particular operational demand and requires a 90-day retraining period. A qualification flaw suspends one specialist role until the missing assessment is completed. None removes the person's existence or grants replacement manpower for their dismissal.

A compressed-schedule cohort can develop a continuing care requirement after prolonged service. The response can be medical stabilisation, a less demanding posting, retirement, or an honestly recorded decision to continue under strain. Continuing service cannot be disguised as a harmless pause while still retaining every combat benefit. The exact increased care bill and restricted capabilities are shown before that choice.

A corrupted reference can leave useful skills intact while undermining testimony or professional confidence. The host can authenticate the relevant archive, pair the affected specialists with independent reviewers, or remove them from the disputed assignment while preserving their civilian status. Destroying an archive does not erase the evidence of its misuse or remove the people created from it.

### Recall as an actual operation

A recall names the affected service groups and a receiving hospital or training site. The player must bring field formations to the selected safe location, maintain supply, and complete the treatment or retraining interval. A selected army must not continue receiving full deployed benefits while being counted as present in an unrelated hospital.

The proposed ordinary recall action commits the affected people or formations, one civilian factory, and 10 Clinical Material Lots per 1,000 people for a 60-day treatment. Those are three cost types. A military order that requires a command allocation can add 15 Command Power as the fourth. Existing equipment remains with the unit unless a separate legitimate transfer occurs.

A divided generation can be recalled in stages when the medical sites can support only part of it. The treated portion receives the resolved-condition record. The untreated portion remains affected. The resolution does not automatically repair another group overseas or certify a production standard that has not been reviewed.

Military refusal to release an affected formation creates a command incident. The government can enforce the medical withdrawal, accept a shorter and narrower deployment under stated penalties, or concede posting authority to the institute. Concession can increase Wu Authority because it changes actual control. Successful treatment under an independent military doctor can reduce that leverage. Neither result grants another research reward.

## R08: a faster schedule with later obligations

Accelerated maturation is an optional production standard, available after the institute has completed a normal cohort and observed its reception. It cannot be obtained by choosing high Authority, activating an evolution, or declaring an emergency. Research alone does not shorten existing paid orders without a recorded conversion and its real costs.

The proposed accelerated cohort cell keeps the same maximum of 250 people and the same one-factory commitment, but shortens the full production and protected maturation schedule from 270 to 210 days. The living milestone occurs after 150 days, followed by 60 days of protected maturation. Common adult orientation and professional or military training remain separate and are not shortened.

Each accelerated cell spends 250 Support Equipment, 2,000 fuel, and 20 Clinical Material Lots. The order therefore still has four cost types including the factory commitment. The larger care reservation includes the accelerated schedule's first reception period. A four-cell intake costs 1,000 Support Equipment, 8,000 fuel, and 80 lots, with four factories committed for 210 days.

Accelerated people must meet the same adult-readiness requirements. A failed readiness assessment extends protected care until the identified gap is resolved. It does not produce an underage military replacement or allow the government to declare the person mature by spending Command Power.

The first 180 days after qualification use twice the normal generation-care bill. A military formation from this standard also begins with a proposed 15 percent organisation-recovery penalty until a completed 90-day field-acclimatisation period. It must receive its full care during that period. The penalty can be removed through a successful recorded assignment, not by merging the formation into another template.

The strategic choice is earlier availability at greater industrial and clinical cost, followed by a restricted initial service period. A country facing immediate defeat may still be better served by medical aid or ordinary recruitment, since accelerated replication remains a long programme. The AI should not begin it merely because a surrender value is high when the country cannot protect the production site long enough to finish.

No claim is made that acceleration improves memory. Personal continuity and specialist qualification keep their own requirements. A rushed body does not reconstruct an absent archive, shorten a pilot's missing training, or restore the original Wu's unrecorded experience.

## R09: Wu records what a successor could know

Wu's personal continuity archive is a deliberate project. It is not an automatic result of ordinary cloning, organ replacement, or a government order issued after his death. The institute must have developed its reference methods, trained real specialists, and preserved a usable account of Wu's medical work while he can still participate.

The archive separates medical method, institutional knowledge, personal memory, and authority claims. Medical method includes the treatments and reasoning that the institute has actually recorded. Institutional knowledge identifies the services, staff, and projects that Wu knew at that time. Personal memory remains incomplete and can conflict with other records. Authority claims state what Wu wanted a successor to inherit. The government is not obliged to treat those claims as appointments.

The first archive can be held by an independent medical board, by Wu personally, or under a divided-custody agreement. Independent custody makes succession easier to verify and reduces the risk that one person can conceal every usable record. Personal custody gives Wu a stronger negotiating position and makes the loss of his location more dangerous. Divided custody lets the state preserve service continuity while Wu retains a personal account whose private content cannot be used as a universal government credential.

A copied archive has the same date and known gaps as its source. Duplication protects against physical loss but creates no new knowledge. Its location and custody are recorded separately from its content. Two archives stored in the same threatened laboratory are not geographically independent backups.

A proposed archive update occupies one existing research lane and one civilian factory for 60 days, consuming 100 Support Equipment and 40 Political Power. The update incorporates work and experience that its participating Wu actually acquired. It cannot read the private experience of another copy, an enemy laboratory, or a missing original. If several copies contribute, the record identifies the participating people and preserves conflicting accounts instead of pretending that their memories have become identical.

The update does not alter living copies without their participation. A qualified copy may study the new material, reject part of its interpretation, or need further clinical assessment. An old archive remains valid historical evidence even after a newer one is made. The institute cannot erase responsibility for an earlier programme by replacing its current reference file.

## R10: a copy becomes capable of continuing Wu's work

R10 establishes a qualification programme. It does not create the candidate and does not itself replace the original Wu. The host must already have produced a living adult copy through a paid personal order, preserved an authenticated continuity archive, and supplied the people who will conduct the assessment.

The candidate has its own identity from the living milestone. It completes civilian reception before appointment, with no parallel military manpower reward. Until qualification, it is a continuity candidate with limited demonstrated skills. It cannot supply the full national medical package, grant advanced research, control every engineered formation, or complete a universal cure simply because its face matches Wu's portrait.

The proposed individual qualification assignment lasts 180 days. It commits the candidate and one civilian factory, consumes 20 Clinical Material Lots, and costs 50 Political Power. Those are the four cost types. It is a clinical and institutional assessment, so it does not also reserve a primary research lane. The R10 research project has already established the programme. A candidate in assessment cannot simultaneously occupy another professional or command assignment.

Three connected assessments fill that period. A 60-day clinical assignment demonstrates recorded treatment in a working hospital. A 45-day service assignment shows that the candidate can understand current care and control obligations without assuming that the archive describes the whole present world. A 75-day independent case assessment tests the ability to reason through a medically valid problem that the candidate has not merely memorised. These periods describe gameplay work, not medical procedures.

A current outbreak can supply a real case. A country in Calm World can use an appropriate contained clinical case or a reviewed diagnostic exercise rooted in existing medical practice. The programme never requires the government to create an epidemic, attack another country, or wait for high Chaos. It cannot manufacture a national disaster as a test fixture.

Successful qualification establishes that this copy can carry Wu's exceptional medical capability under the same real conditions as the original: a functioning institute, available staff, valid disease access, production, and delivery. It can begin future universal adaptations and eligible new research. It does not inherit treatments, specimens, or projects that were absent from its reference and subsequent education. The ability to solve a future case is distinct from already knowing its answer.

The national medical package is applied once through the existing institution. A country with the original and a qualified copy does not receive two copies of the founding protection multipliers. The second doctor provides continuity, can lead a different existing service, and can preserve work during a local absence. It does not add a free research lane or another founding stockpile.

### Incomplete qualification

A candidate can demonstrate strong medical memory but fail independent judgement, understand clinical work but misunderstand current control obligations, or present a reference whose authorship is disputed. The result names the missing qualification. It does not erase the person's successful work or reduce them to generic failed material.

The host can continue supervised service, complete a remedial assessment, or admit the person to civilian life without appointing them as Wu. The proposed remedial assignment lasts 90 days, commits the candidate and one civilian factory, and consumes 10 Clinical Material Lots. Completion resolves the named deficiency when the required work and evidence exist. The design does not permit an endless unchanged failure roll to block an otherwise valid successor.

An archive missing essential medical content needs reconstruction before qualification can continue. Reconstruction uses authenticated institutional records, surviving qualified staff, and any reliable contribution from the candidate. It cannot create personal knowledge from a photograph, a body, or a rumour that a backup once existed. The proposed reconstruction project uses one lane, two civilian factories, 250 Support Equipment, and 60 Political Power over 180 days. It restores only the supported reference scope and records what remains unknown.

If Wu is dead and no qualified copy or usable continuity archive exists, a biological lookalike cannot revive his unique role by name alone. The institute keeps its learned medicine and ordinary successor routes. New exceptional continuity remains unavailable until a genuine surviving reference or qualified person is found. This is a consequence of lost material and knowledge, not a Chaos or Authority gate.

## Preparing succession while the original is alive

A functioning continuity route should allow preparation before disaster. The original Wu can teach a qualified copy, the government can identify a preferred medical successor, and independent staff can rehearse the transfer of service responsibilities. None of this requires killing, retiring, or capturing the original.

The government chooses a single recognised institute lead, a medical collegium with one accountable lead for current decisions, or separate institutional assignments. These are working governance labels. They determine who can authorise current clinical work, which archive supports the appointment, and how disagreements are resolved. They do not merge the people into one character.

Under a designated successor, the copy knows which responsibilities it may assume and which powers remain subject to a new government decision. Under a collegium, several qualified Wus can cover different hospitals or existing research lanes, with shared clinical records and independent witnesses. Under separate assignments, one copy can concentrate on public medicine and another on an authorised specialist programme. Each assignment still needs its actual staff, site, supplies, and permission.

Preparation can reduce an emergency interruption without making succession automatic in every circumstance. A designated copy that is captured, unqualified for a newly changed system, or cut off from the institute cannot take office through a stale flag. A board can temporarily keep documented care running while it locates a valid lead.

The office transfer preserves the institution's existing mandates, contracts, Authority, project progress, and obligations. Selecting a younger or more cooperative copy does not reset Authority to the public founding value or cancel promises made to dependent formations. The new lead can renegotiate those commitments through actual choices, audits, and service transfers. It cannot clear them by changing the portrait.

The original's medical absence and death remain different conditions. A captured Wu is alive and can return. A preserved but critically injured Wu remains the same living person under M10. A dead Wu remains dead in the historical record even when a qualified copy takes the office. Appointment of a successor never subtracts the original death from the shared totals.

## A succession crisis after loss

Loss of the recognised lead first asks whether another qualified person is already available and whether the institution can still operate. Existing independent hospitals continue their certified treatments. Active universal adaptations keep their actual progress, but work requiring exceptional judgement pauses until a qualified lead takes responsibility.

The proposed succession response has a normal 60-day institutional window. An already qualified, supplied successor can take the medical office through a 15-day handover. An unfinished candidate continues its actual remaining qualification time. The window does not accelerate a body or award a completed archive. Its purpose is to settle current authority and emergency service priorities while long work continues separately.

The host's practical choices are to appoint a qualified prepared successor, maintain an independent interim board, negotiate the return of a living qualified Wu, or acknowledge the permanent loss of exceptional leadership and preserve the ordinary institute. The player should see the actual available candidates and the reason each unavailable route cannot proceed.

An interim board can preserve completed cures, current material manufacture, and standard-specific control archives. It cannot improvise a universal cure or take personal knowledge from another living copy without access. It prioritises existing patients and dependent formations over starting elective replication orders. New production can be paused while already living cohorts remain supplied.

A failed handover can leave one service interrupted while another continues. The host might keep hospital care functioning but lose access to a private control archive. It might retain the army's medical service while a foreign copy holds the latest cure adaptation. These outcomes should follow the actual division of knowledge and custody. They should not all collapse into one country-wide penalty.

The Part 6 engineered-formation archive emergency remains distinct. Its existing 45-day control-archive response is not extended or restarted by the new 60-day succession window. A qualified copy can resolve the relevant part of that emergency only if it has the needed standard, access, and service capacity. The country cannot buy an unlimited extension by repeatedly appointing different Wus.

## R11: distributed continuity has real locations

R11 lets the institute maintain continuity at more than one functioning site. It requires two supplied institutions and an agreed division of custody. The research creates the organisational capability to distribute records and qualified responsibility. It does not create a second body, clone another archive's missing content, or duplicate the main laboratory's buildings.

A proposed secondary continuity placement commits the named qualified copy, two civilian factories, 150 Support Equipment, and 60 Political Power for 90 days. A viable receiving institute and a real transfer route are requirements. This placement has four cost types. The copy's existing paid care follows it where the relevant service can be delivered, and any uncovered future care is quoted as its own continuing service obligation.

The second site receives the person and the agreed records. A material convoy is separate from a knowledge transfer, and a copy that travels without the newest archive may arrive with an older medical scope. The former site retains its staff, copied knowledge, equipment, and patients. Neither site can claim that the other one's physical facilities exist locally.

Within one country, the established two primary research lanes remain the normal maximum. Distributed continuity improves survival and can keep one existing lane operating when the other site is threatened. It does not produce a third lane by placing a new Wu in an empty building. Any later expansion of that ceiling would require a separate explicit design decision.

Across countries, a qualified copy can negotiate an actual independent appointment after a paid transfer and a viable institutional settlement. This is the replication branch's proposed extension to the original one-person lifecycle. It does not widen the initial player-or-major arrival draw, replay the first demonstration, or reopen Event 089's fire-once selection. The new host must support ordinary civilian medicine and a functioning institute. The paid foundation creates one working lane, with a second available only through the existing funded expansion route.

A foreign appointment grants the medical institution only when its qualified lead and real services exist. It does not inherit the former host's Authority value, factories, treatment reserves, specimen stores, foreign contracts, or completed national investments. Each country records its own actual concessions and responsibilities. The event keeps one origin history and a bounded registry of its living copies and current institutions.

A host that later loses major status does not lose its doctor on that basis. A host that loses all viable medical sites needs evacuation, aid, or a different appointment. The distinction is material capacity, not a reroll of the original arrival pool.

## Several Wus in the same world

Several living copies are separate people. Each has a source reference, a birth history, a personal learning history, a current custodian or employer, and a clinical qualification state. The original remains separately identifiable by the event's established arrival identity, even when the fiction never proves his earlier age or origin.

A single engine character must not be recruited into several countries and presented as several bodies. Conversely, several legitimate copies must not be collapsed into one movable token that silently kills or teleports the others. The implementation needs distinct supported carriers with stable person identities and guarded role ownership. A visible office or portrait can change without changing the underlying person's identity.

The country presents one Wu Authority value for its institutional relationship. Internal disagreements appear through assignments, custody disputes, and reports. They do not add a permanent rivalry meter or a separate public Authority meter for every copy. Where several institutions exist, the visible value reflects actual conceded powers and cannot be lowered by moving an obligation onto a less visible copy.

### Cooperative continuity

Cooperating copies divide actual work. One may lead a hospital, another maintain the control archive, and another teach specialist classes. Each uses an existing assignment and its real capacity. Their cooperation makes the institution harder to disable with one absence and can preserve a complete medical service through a local crisis.

Cooperation also requires reconciliation of records. Copies who have worked in different countries may disagree about which treatment assumptions remain valid. A paid archive review can preserve both accounts, identify the case to which each applies, and teach the current staff. It does not grant every participating country every private treatment or specimen by default.

### Disputed identity and office

Two qualified Wus can both claim to remember receiving the same appointment. The government can recognise the actual current officeholder, establish a collegium with divided responsibilities, or negotiate separate employment. The result changes authority and work allocation. It does not determine that one person is biologically unreal.

The dispute should centre on specific rights and work. One copy may claim access to an institute founded after its archive date. Another may insist that a programme has already been prohibited. The host can use witnesses, contracts, and observed service to decide the institutional issue. It cannot repair a contradiction by erasing the older copy's birth or inventing memories for the younger one.

### Different professional commitments

A copy can choose a different research direction after its own experience. A public physician may refuse a classified programme that the original once supported. A military clinician may prioritise dependent soldiers over an elective continuity project. A copy living abroad may value its new host's hospitals more than an order to return.

These disagreements require an actual cause such as divergent service, a disputed contract, mistreatment, an exposed experiment, or a blocked clinical duty. High Chaos can increase the pressure surrounding them through the later evolutions, but it does not create a free copy or force every clone to revolt. Low-Authority public institutions retain workable cooperation routes.

### Hidden continuity

A concealed copy needs an actual prior order, an actual site, supplies, a living milestone, and a recorded but undisclosed custody history. A later revelation exposes that hidden history. It does not retroactively invent production because the story would benefit from another Wu.

Unauthorized production follows the institution's actual independent resources and the applicable autonomous-work permissions or evolved behaviour. Before such behaviour exists, high Authority alone cannot mint a hidden order outside all paid capacity. A secret site competes for real supplies and creates exposure through the work it performs.

Discovery can lead to recognition, independent assessment, asylum, a custody hearing, or separation of the institute. The discovered person still needs care. Its existence can make Wu's removal more difficult, but it does not automatically transfer government power or create a sovereign country. Those larger outcomes retain their later full-depth design.

## Capture, rescue, and transfer

Capturing a laboratory can place several different things under foreign control: nonliving material, unfinished production, unsettled living people, already settled residents, professional archives, and a particular Wu. Each has its own outcome. Control of the state is not proof that every person or record remained there.

An occupier first receives information about what it actually found. Unfinished material can be secured or abandoned without becoming free adult personnel. Living unsettled cohorts require immediate care and a decision about custody. Settled residents remain part of the real population and cannot be counted as a fresh replication reward. An archive can be copied without creating its associated person.

A captured qualified Wu remains the same person. Capture suspends duties that require freedom and access, but it does not record death. The old host can continue through another qualified copy, a standard-specific independent archive, or ordinary learned medicine. Rescue restores access to the rescued individual without dismissing a legitimate successor or undoing the successor's work.

If an original Wu returns after a copy has taken office, the government settles the current institutional arrangement. It can restore the original to the lead, retain the successor and offer the original another assignment, or form a collegium. Existing patients, contracts, Authority, and research do not restart. The displaced lead remains alive and retains its own knowledge unless a separate actual event changes that condition.

Transfer preserves the exact person, reference date, qualification, injuries, service history, and unsettled or settled accounting status. A change of nationality or role must remove the previous active appointment before activating the new one. A pending clinical or research assignment must be safely paused or completed first. An invalid recipient must leave the person and every unrelated obligation with the original host.

For living cohorts, evacuation carries the surviving count and actual remaining maturation or training. For deployed formations, exact ownership transfer remains a separate engine problem. The inspected Event 019 recreate-and-delete exception does not prove that Wu can preserve arbitrary live strength, equipment variants, experience, decorations, or orders. That exception is not inherited as blanket permission. Wu's formation transfer must have its own accepted, evidence-backed disposition before it can run.

Knowledge transfer is permanent where the receiving country actually learns a completed method. Ending a treaty can stop future access, supplies, or updates. It cannot remove an already learned neutral technology or rewrite who supplied it. A captured archive cannot grant the original source event's characters, countries, opening rewards, project history, or scenario launch.

## Closing a replication programme

Stopping new orders is the simplest closure. Work before the living milestone can be cancelled under its recorded material contract. Living cohorts continue into care and reception. Qualified people keep their personal existence and learned skills, and the host decides whether their services remain employed.

The proposed closure action chooses a receiving institution for each unsettled group, a custodian for surviving records, and a funded care period. The main task is to settle actual responsibilities. It must not become a Political Power button that deletes people, converts them to manpower, or removes every trace of a failed programme.

A civilian closure can distribute healthy graduates among viable receiving states through the population and migration owners. Military trainees can enter ordinary civilian reception before any military settlement or remain in a finite reserve while another employer is negotiated. Deployed soldiers use the native military demobilisation contract, with no additional replication population refund. Enhanced veterans keep the Part 6 medical settlement.
