# Doctor Wu: baseline research and the medical portfolio

## Research without an evolution gate

Every Wu research family belongs to ordinary baseline development. A campaign in Calm World can reach any compatible family through clinical knowledge, real source material, facilities, production, and political permission. Disabling every Wu evolution leaves those routes available.

Universal cure development begins with the founding institute and a valid medical lead. The elective portfolio below strengthens that capability and creates other useful medical roles. It is not a preliminary technology ladder that a desperate host must finish before Wu is allowed to treat its first outbreak.

Research maturity is connected rather than strictly linear. Independent clinical teams improve deployment and knowledge survival. Adaptive treatment work informs prevention and later controlled biology. Battlefield medicine can lead toward selected military enhancement. Tissue and organ work leads toward replication. Compatible nonhuman medicine and biological remediation can provide leads for designed life. A connection requires the relevant earlier result, not completion of every unrelated project in the same band.

None of these connections gives the host a free advanced result. A completed organ programme supplies a foundation for later replication research. It does not create a clone formation, a continuity copy, or the Army of Clones scenario. A successfully treated zombie patient does not automatically become a specimen archive or an obedient engineered unit.

## How proposals appear

The institute normally presents three relevant proposals. An active outbreak, a threatened medical network, or a severe wartime need takes priority. When those needs are absent, Wu proposes preventive work, a useful extension of completed research, or a project supported by newly acquired material.

Proposal ranking does not determine whether a family exists. The player can select an eligible family through a compact research browser and retain one preferred proposal for later. Rejecting a proposal removes its immediate prominence without permanently deleting a valid research opportunity. A random offer is never the sole way to reach a required baseline capability.

The player sees the project's practical result, required previous work, material lead, cost class, risk category, and important political permission. Exact rare failures can remain uncertain. The government still knows whether it is approving ordinary care, exceptional treatment, military application, or a project involving a vulnerable population.

A new source changes what can be proposed. A foreign physician can bring a valid clinical record. A captured site can provide a particular archive. A compatible scientist gift can supply staff or an approved project lead. The institute receives the thing the source actually provided. It does not automatically acquire every technology associated with the donor event.

## Medical prerequisite map

The project labels below are design references. They are not reserved engine identifiers. A functioning founding institute is the common starting requirement. Equivalent facilities or clinical capabilities supplied by another owner can satisfy a named requirement when that owner explicitly recognizes the equivalence.

| Project | Required knowledge or clinical condition | Physical or political requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M01 | Appointment completed | Founding staff and a selected accessible clinical region |
| M02 | M01 or an equivalent functioning clinical network | Communication with the region's actual medical service |
| M03 | One validated disease-specific cure | A working archive and an explicit records-custody policy |
| M04 | One validated cure and M01 or an equivalent clinical network | A region that can support a surge service |
| M05 | A functioning military medical service | Access to supported formations and military approval |
| M06 | M05 or a completed major civilian treatment programme | An actual group of living survivors and recovery capacity |
| M07 | M04, M05, or two validated cures | A working research centre and approved clinical care |
| M08 | M07 | Sustained production, care, and an approved patient group |
| M09 | M07 and successful care of a severe living injury case | An exceptional medical research commitment |
| M10 | M04 or M05, plus a compatible cure or regenerative lead | An owner-recognized living critical patient |
| M11 | M10 | A supported preservation site and an approved living group |
| M12 | An actual migration-related medical need or retained relevant clinical experience | Access to an existing route or reception programme |
| M13 | An actual famine-related medical need or retained relevant clinical experience | Accessible living patients and ordinary clinical support |
| M14 | A validated cure and a compatible remediation lead | A registered biological footprint and a supported deployment site |
| M15 | A completed compatible treatment or clinical record | An actual living survivor group and clinical permission |
| M16 | A valid nonhuman biological case | A compatible provider, medical access, and the required research capacity |

The medical profession supplies ordinary valid clinical cases without requiring the state to create a national disaster. Unusual diseases, nonhuman biology, and event-specific material still require their genuine owner-recognized source. The map contains no Chaos threshold, evolution receipt, minimum Authority score, or requirement to launch another event.

## Common project costs

The following proposed cost classes establish readable differences between a routine extension, a substantial medical programme, and exceptional research. They are tuning proposals for this design, not claims about implemented costs.

| Class | Normal research time | Committed civilian factories | Support equipment consumed | Political power |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Routine | 60 days | 1 | 50 | 25 |
| Substantial | 120 days | 2 | 150 | 50 |
| Exceptional | 180 days | 3 | 300 | 75 |

A project occupies one primary research lane. There are three spendable cost types in the table. Trained staff, an accessible site, approved clinical access, and earlier research are requirements rather than hidden additional payments. A military project can replace the political payment with an army-experience payment when the result principally concerns doctrine and training. It should not append several new currencies to an already crowded action.

Target scale affects deployment and production separately. Completing a substantial hospital research programme does not immediately build hospitals in every state. A project with a longer or shorter research duration identifies that difference in its own entry. Mandate advantages apply to the relevant part of the work and do not eliminate the minimum physical commitment.

Support from a compatible earlier project or a legitimate foreign partner can reduce repeated preparation. The proposed normal reduction is 20 percent of the affected preparation requirement. Several descriptions of the same supporting knowledge do not stack into free research. An unrelated high research-speed bonus does not automatically complete Wu's clinical, production, or political work.

## Project outcomes and recovery

Routine projects usually complete cleanly when their requirements remain satisfied. Their ordinary setback is delay, an incomplete local rollout, or a need to replace missing staff or supplies. They should not routinely generate a new epidemic as punishment for investing in basic public medicine.

Substantial projects can produce a limited initial result, a production burden, a regional shortfall, or a staff dispute. The result identifies what works and what remains to be completed. A follow-up can preserve useful progress, narrow the target, or fund a safer completion.

Exceptional projects introduce more serious medical and institutional risks. They still need a credible benefit and a disclosed danger category. A failure may require continuing care, suspension, independent review, or destruction of a specific dangerous result. A permitted experiment that fails and an unauthorized experiment concealed from the government remain distinct events.

Project completion records the actual discovery once. Repeating production, retraining staff, or changing the deployment region does not award the same technology, Chaos change, or breakthrough consequence again. A later project with a genuinely new result can have its own discovery without pretending that routine manufacture was another scientific revolution.

## Medical project catalogue

### M01. Independent Clinical Teams

**Entry and work.** This routine project is available after appointment. Wu trains a selected group of civilian or military physicians to use the institute's documented emergency methods. It requires a functioning founding site and staff the host can actually assign. The project does not delay the universal cure process, which remains available from the beginning.

**Result.** One selected clinical region gains reliable local support that can continue through a temporary interruption in Wu's personal availability. Completed treatment standards used there become documented rather than dependent on a visiting specialist. This is the first useful investment for a host that wants a durable public institution.

**Choice and recovery.** The host chooses public professional appointments or a centrally appointed institute team. Public appointments improve continuity and can reduce a genuine exclusive staffing dependency. A central team is easier to redirect to another approved programme. If staff are displaced by war, their training survives with the personnel that were actually evacuated, while the abandoned region loses its local support.

**AI and presentation.** A weak host and a public institute give this project high priority. A military host prefers a region supporting its main casualty network. The project uses a clinical-training icon and a concise distinction between independent local care and institute-dependent care. It does not require another national spirit.

### M02. Early Outbreak Recognition

**Entry and work.** This routine project improves communication between hospitals, the institute, and the disease systems already operating in the host. It requires one functioning clinical network or an equivalent owner-supported medical service. It uses existing cases and reporting, not a deliberately created outbreak.

**Result.** The host receives earlier, more useful notice of compatible biological incidents. A warning identifies an affected region and an appropriate medical response before the disease reaches its more severe owner-defined state. Wu's strong passive protection becomes easier for the player to use because the next action arrives with enough context.

**Choice and recovery.** The public route reports clinical uncertainty openly. A classified route can restrict the report to the cabinet, trading public cooperation for secrecy. An uncertain report opens investigation rather than automatically imposing nationwide quarantine. A failure of communication delays notice in the affected region and can be repaired through a targeted staffing or transport response.

**AI and presentation.** Outbreak-prone countries and hosts managing migration routes prioritize the project. The visible benefit is an earlier actionable medical notice, not an unverified percentage increase in a hidden detection statistic. The icon should communicate observation and communication rather than a weapon or a generic research flask.

### M03. Adaptive Treatment Archive

**Entry and work.** This substantial project becomes available after one complete disease-specific cure. Wu organizes verified treatment versions, surviving clinical records, and production instructions into an archive that ordinary institute staff can use. Dangerous viable material is a separate custody decision and is not required for every archive entry.

**Result.** Later work on a genuinely related treatment version can reuse valid knowledge and reach the adaptation stage sooner. The archive also allows documented older treatments to continue after Wu leaves. It does not grant a broad cure for unrelated illnesses or make an obsolete standard fully compatible without review.

**Choice and recovery.** The government can distribute clinical records among independent institutions or keep a single protected archive. Distribution improves survival after capture and personnel loss. Centralization protects secrecy and gives Wu or the cabinet a stronger role in access. A damaged archive loses the records actually destroyed, while distributed copies remain available where they were held.

**AI and presentation.** Hosts that have already faced mutation or expect repeated outbreaks prioritize it. A public AI normally distributes the records. A classified AI prefers a secure archive but should copy essential treatment instructions when losing its main site is plausible. The project uses an archive-specific icon and names the standards currently benefiting from its work.

### M04. Hospital Surge Organization

**Entry and work.** This substantial project requires a documented treatment and at least one clinical network. It develops the organization needed to expand ordinary care rapidly when an outbreak begins. Research prepares the method, while actual surge deployment still commits staff, medical allocations, and local capacity.

**Result.** The country can open one bounded emergency surge programme for a selected region. The programme increases the share of eligible living patients who can actually receive available treatment. It does not manufacture that treatment or raise the population's food and housing capacity.

**Choice and recovery.** The player can use a civilian surge or divert military medical resources into the region. Civilian surge preserves military support but may need more preparation. Military diversion can act sooner while reducing support for the formations that supplied the staff and equipment. Ending the emergency returns the surviving committed resources and closes the surge rather than leaving a permanent free capacity bonus.

**AI and presentation.** Every AI gives the deployment high weight during a severe supported outbreak. It avoids diverting the last medical support from an equally endangered front without a clear benefit. The presentation names the source of the surge resources and the affected region. A temporary clinical status replaces overlapping institute presentation instead of adding several spirits.

### M05. Advanced Field Medicine

**Entry and work.** This substantial project is available to every mandate. It requires functioning military hospitals or a compatible field-medical provider, access to the formations being supported, and the political approval to integrate Wu's methods. The military mandate receives its preparation advantage here. The political-power payment can be replaced with 25 army experience.

**Result.** The programme improves the survival and recovery of personnel receiving genuine medical support. A proposed starting benefit is 15 percent greater effectiveness in the supported casualty-recovery channel, subject to the military owner's actual accounting. The result applies where that support is present rather than producing free recovered manpower throughout an unsupported army.

**Choice and recovery.** The player can integrate the methods into ordinary medical support or concentrate a specialist team on a selected front. Broad integration is more resilient and slower to establish. Concentration produces a stronger local medical service while depending on a route and assigned personnel. Lost support removes the added service without converting recovered soldiers back into casualties.

**AI and presentation.** Countries in sustained war prioritize the programme after urgent outbreak research. The status names supported formations or the relevant medical support family. The clinical icon and report should emphasize evacuation and survival. Later enhancement research remains a separate decision with different risks.

### M06. Recovery and Rehabilitation Service

**Entry and work.** This substantial project follows advanced field medicine or a major civilian treatment programme. It organizes recovery for living patients who have survived severe disease or injury but cannot immediately return to normal activity. It requires a supported recovery location and a valid patient group supplied by the owning system.

**Result.** Eligible survivors recover more reliably and can return to civilian activity or military service through the owner that already tracks them. The project cannot subtract recorded deaths or create a new recruitable pool beside a recovery already counted elsewhere.

**Choice and recovery.** The player selects civilian rehabilitation, military return to duty, or care for a specifically displaced population. Each use needs an actual group and capacity. Forcing an early return can provide a short-term service benefit with a risk of renewed medical incapacity. A conservative programme takes longer and reduces that risk. The choice concerns a living group, not a generalized claim that all battlefield losses are reversible.

**AI and presentation.** A manpower-strained host values military recovery, while a public institute gives more weight to the largest unmet clinical need. The visible result identifies the group restored and any continuing care requirement. The project does not need a casualty counter based on invented estimates of people who might have died.

### M07. Synthetic Blood and Tissue Replacement

**Entry and work.** This substantial project requires advanced clinical care and a functioning research site. It is an explicitly fictional extension of Wu's medical ability. It is available under public supervision, military command, or a classified mandate with the same core prerequisites.

**Result.** The institute gains a medical replacement-material capability that supports surgery and severe injury care. It provides a foundation for later organ and replication projects. Research completion does not create a population, a trained specialist, or a clone battalion.

**Choice and recovery.** The government can establish an accountable hospital supply programme or retain production within institute sites. A hospital programme improves access and institutional independence. Exclusive production concentrates expertise and makes continuity more dependent on Wu's staff. A failed early batch creates a medical supply withdrawal and replacement obligation, with its affected clinical result determined by the patient owner. It does not automatically become a contagious new illness.

**AI and presentation.** Hosts with heavy casualty needs or a mature public service prioritize the work. A weak institute without production capacity is more likely to seek a partner before committing. The asset family should make the distinction between replacement material and whole-person replication immediately legible.

### M08. Organ Replacement and Compatibility

**Entry and work.** This exceptional project follows synthetic blood and tissue work. It requires a viable production partnership, sustained clinical care, and approval for the intended patient group. It does not require cloning to have been discovered elsewhere or any Wu evolution to be active.

**Result.** Wu can provide compatible replacement organs and more extensive reconstructive care through a dedicated medical programme. This expands the range of living patients who can be saved and establishes another prerequisite for replication science. The result still depends on available production and continued treatment.

**Choice and recovery.** The host can prioritize civilian hospitals, military casualties, or a small number of critical specialists. Scarcity makes this choice meaningful. A successful result for one supported group does not imply a free organ programme for the entire nation. Complications require continued care, a revised production standard, or a narrower eligible group. The institute retains the parts of the work that remain reliable.

**AI and presentation.** A public host usually prioritizes clinical need. A desperate military host can prioritize trained personnel it cannot replace quickly. The project uses a distinct medical-production icon and a report centred on the living consequences of access, rather than treating patients as a new equipment archetype in the narrative.

### M09. Regenerative Treatment

**Entry and work.** This exceptional project requires established tissue replacement and successful treatment of severe injury. Its research duration is proposed at 240 days because it extends beyond ordinary reconstruction. A supervised host can approve it without granting Wu independent control over patients.

**Result.** A limited supported programme repairs injuries that existing field medicine cannot adequately address. It improves the survival and restoration of eligible living patients supplied by the appropriate owner. The new capability can later support exceptional military or nonhuman medicine, while those uses retain their own prerequisites.

**Choice and recovery.** The government chooses a narrow clinical trial with continuing oversight or a larger emergency programme during a qualifying crisis. Emergency scale requires more production and care. An incomplete result can preserve life while leaving a dependence on continuing treatment. The recovery path funds that care, narrows the programme, or seeks a safer completion. It does not silently transform every recipient into an enhanced soldier.

**AI and presentation.** The AI considers the work when ordinary treatment leaves a significant unresolved need and the host can sustain long-term care. The presentation identifies what the treatment restores and what remains uncertain. A successful project should feel extraordinary without promising resurrection.

### M10. Preservation of Critical Patients

**Entry and work.** This substantial project follows advanced clinical care and an appropriate treatment or regenerative lead. It creates a framework for keeping a critically ill living leader, specialist, or other exceptional patient alive until compatible care becomes available. The relevant character or event owner must expose a medically treatable case.

**Result.** The institute can preserve the patient through an explicit ongoing care commitment. The patient remains unavailable for activities they cannot plausibly perform in that state. The government gains time to complete a treatment, arrange transfer, or seek a compatible foreign contribution.

**Choice and recovery.** Preserving a political leader can create a temporary leadership settlement owned by that country's systems. Preserving a rare specialist can delay the loss of an important research contribution. The project does not override an unrelated scripted death, recreate a dead character, or invent a duplicate officeholder. If the host cannot maintain care, it receives a specific medical and political choice before support ends whenever the surrounding crisis permits warning.

**AI and presentation.** The AI prioritizes patients whose continued survival has an actual supported consequence. It does not preserve an unlimited list of unnamed specialists for free research bonuses. The portrait consumer belongs to the real patient identity, while Wu's project icon remains a separate medical asset.

### M11. Controlled Hibernation

**Entry and work.** This exceptional project follows advanced preservation work and a viable exceptional-medical site. It is fictional medicine for keeping a selected living group stable during evacuation, disaster, or a prolonged treatment delay. It requires informed institutional approval appropriate to the mandate.

**Result.** A supported group can enter a temporary inactive medical state with a clearly stated sustainment and awakening requirement. Civilians remain in their actual population location or recorded transport. Military personnel are unavailable for ordinary combat while in that state. Hibernation cannot make active divisions fight normally while avoiding all supply and casualty consequences.

**Choice and recovery.** The player chooses the group, destination or preservation site, and the condition for ending the programme. A disrupted site creates an evacuation or emergency-awakening problem. The surviving group's outcome belongs to its population or unit owner. A partial failure can require rehabilitation rather than deleting the whole programme and refunding its costs.

**AI and presentation.** The AI uses the result for genuine disasters or treatment delays, not as a default economic trick for hiding the entire national population. The status must clearly distinguish sleeping patients from dead or missing people. The project needs its own visual language so it cannot be mistaken for cloning.

### M12. Medical Corridors for Displaced People

**Entry and work.** This substantial project requires a migration or refugee situation that exposes a medical need, plus an accessible clinical network. It prepares treatment and screening services for a particular existing movement or reception programme. The host can conduct the research before a crisis when it has compatible prior experience, but deployment still needs a real route and population.

**Result.** The programme reduces biological pressure associated with that supported route or reception area. It can make the existing migration programme safer and reduce disease-related interruptions. It does not move people, increase housing, create food, or replace reception-capacity rules.

**Choice and recovery.** The player can concentrate care at the departure region, along an accessible corridor, or at reception. Each choice addresses a different part of the actual movement. A route closure can move the medical service to another approved route, while people remain where the migration owner records them. A medical success does not erase earlier abuse or turn a coercive population transfer into a humanitarian one.

**AI and presentation.** The AI prioritizes the route with the greatest actual disease burden and viable access. The decision shows the named movement or reception region and the medical problem it addresses. It uses a medical-corridor icon rather than a generic diplomacy emblem.

### M13. Famine Medical Support

**Entry and work.** This substantial project requires a valid famine-related medical need or a prior compatible clinical lead. It prepares treatment for disease, deficiency-related medical complications, and weakened civilian populations where the famine owner exposes a medical component.

**Result.** The programme reduces the biological burden that worsens the crisis and supports living patients while food relief is organized. Its effect is deliberately separate from food reserves, harvest, transport, and population movement. Medical success can keep people alive long enough to benefit from relief without claiming that medicine itself supplies their calories.

**Choice and recovery.** The player coordinates with an existing relief programme or supports the most threatened accessible region independently. Independent care remains limited when food and transport fail. The institute can preserve the medical result and redeploy staff when a region becomes inaccessible. It cannot report the famine as solved merely because its own treatment objective is complete.

**AI and presentation.** The AI values the programme when a medical complication is actually contributing to the crisis. It still funds food and transport through their owners. The report describes the remaining need honestly and does not use a successful medical project to erase the consequences of an avoidable famine.

### M14. Protective Biological Remediation

**Entry and work.** This exceptional project requires a completed treatment, a registered biological outbreak footprint, and a compatible remediation lead. It develops a fictional filter or controlled protective system capable of suppressing the relevant biological burden. The current medical part defines its protective use. Later designed-life research can supply distinct organisms through its own project permissions.

**Result.** The host can support a selected contaminated region, medical site, or transport corridor through a paid remediation programme. The disease owner reports the actual reduction of its biological footprint. Shared Air Cleanliness accounting then reflects that change through its normal ownership.

**Choice and recovery.** The programme can protect a small critical site or support a broader region at greater cost. It does not remove unrelated chemical contamination or radioactive fallout. A failed installation can become ineffective or require replacement without automatically spreading a new disease. A genuinely dangerous organism-based variant requires the separate project and its own disclosed failure category.

**AI and presentation.** The AI prioritizes regions where biological burden is preventing important medical or relief work. It does not spend resources on this project merely because total Air Cleanliness is poor for unrelated reasons. A footprint-specific icon and region name communicate what is being treated.

### M15. Survivor-Based Protective Medicine

**Entry and work.** This substantial project requires a genuine group of living survivors and a compatible completed treatment or clinical record. It develops Wu's fictional capacity to derive protective medical insight from survival of an exceptional outbreak. The group must exist through the disease owner, with the necessary clinical permission.

**Result.** The institute gains a specific protective lead or a more durable supported treatment for the relevant disease family. It can also provide a medical prerequisite for later exceptional work. It does not copy universal immunity from one survivor or automatically protect against unrelated conditions.

**Choice and recovery.** A public programme uses voluntary clinical cooperation and distributes the resulting medical knowledge according to policy. A restricted programme can keep the work secret without silently authorizing coercion. An unethical use requires its own explicit approval or an applicable discoverable misconduct branch. A limited initial result preserves the valid lead and identifies the remaining treatment work.

**AI and presentation.** The AI considers the project after a major outbreak has produced an accessible survivor group. It never deliberately creates an outbreak solely to satisfy this prerequisite. The presentation centres surviving people and clinical cooperation, with a separate icon from the original cure.

### M16. Compatible Nonhuman Medicine

**Entry and work.** This exceptional project requires an actual nonhuman or alien biological case supplied by a compatible event or unit provider. The provider identifies a living group, its medical need, and which treatment consequences it allows. A rumour or an unrelated alien technology receipt is insufficient.

**Result.** Wu can develop and deliver medicine for that supported group, improving its survival or correcting a treatable instability. The formation or population remains owned by its original provider. The result does not grant recruitment access, create a derivative unit, or make the host medically compatible with every nonhuman species.

**Choice and recovery.** The government chooses a limited care agreement, a shared research partnership, or a restricted institute programme. The patients or their governing actor can have their own consent and diplomatic response where supported. A failed early result leads to adaptation, stabilization, or a safe suspension. It does not erase the actor from the map as a convenient cleanup.

**AI and presentation.** The AI values the project when an existing allied, hosted, or controlled population has a real medical need. It does not spend on an unsupported species merely because a project name is dramatic. The report uses the actual provider's identity and a distinct medical project icon, while any new battlefield appearance remains a separate asset obligation.

## Medical production extensions

The research catalogue does not duplicate the clinical-network expansion, research-site expansion, standing treatment reserves, and production licences already defined in the institution and access specifications. Those actions consume the relevant knowledge and create capacity or output. They do not each award a second copy of the underlying medical discovery.

A completed project can create a recurring service, but that service has a real deployment target and sustaining commitment. Hospital surge, organ replacement, preservation, hibernation, and remediation do not become five permanent national bonuses applied without cost after the research completes.

The host can discontinue an elective service, preserve its knowledge, and resume it later when resources allow. Continuing care for a dependent living group requires a transition decision. That obligation is part of the project's result rather than a surprise penalty introduced only after the player tries to cancel it.

## Political permissions for medical work

Ordinary clinical training and preventive care require the appointment's normal permission. Exceptional projects require an explicit patient group, supervision arrangement, and care commitment. Military use requires a military programme agreement. Coercive use is a distinct severe political choice, never a prerequisite for safe public research.

A low-Authority institute can conduct every compatible medical project above. The cabinet provides the required approvals and resources while preserving independent review. High Authority may make some decisions faster and can create greater dependency. It does not improve the doctor's basic scientific ability or replace missing source material.

The research direction changes which proposals are emphasized. A public institute can select nonhuman medicine or hibernation when a genuine humanitarian need exists. A military institute can fund rehabilitation and medical corridors. A classified institute can share a completed cure when national survival or diplomacy makes that policy attractive.

## Early access and shared research

Wu can independently reach compatible biological knowledge before another event would normally reveal it. The early result grants only the relevant capability and preserves the original source event's normal appearance. It does not grant the source event's country, character, narrative rewards, or unrelated technology.

When the normal source later appears, the two routes recognize the existing capability. The owner can provide a genuinely new refinement, a compatible project stage, or a contextual reaction. It must not grant the same discovery twice or remove Wu's valid earlier work.

The existing clone system is especially important for later replication. Its documented economy already assigns reserve-manpower consequences to stockpiled clone cohorts. Wu must not add another manpower reward for the same stock. A new medical or military refinement needs an explicit shared-owner settlement, and the user's prohibition on an unlimited free-manpower loop remains a design requirement. The detailed replication specification must resolve that relationship rather than assuming a second Wu-specific economy.

## Research during a national emergency

The host can pause an elective project to free a lane for an urgent cure. Verified knowledge remains, future commitments are released, and used consumables are not refunded. The paused project remains a selectable continuation when the emergency is under control.

The institute can sustain ordinary documented care while Wu concentrates on the new illness. A programme requiring his exceptional continuous intervention must identify that dependence before the player assigns him elsewhere. The game should not assume that one doctor can personally supervise every exceptional patient and two unrelated research projects without a staff or time consequence.

The AI ranks an active lethal biological emergency above elective enhancement or prestige medicine. A threatened institute values preserving treatment records and staff over finishing a marginal project in a lost state. Desperation can justify a provisional treatment, but it should also make reliable cooperation and established medicine more attractive.

## Medical project presentation

Project labels in this catalogue are working design labels. Final text should describe the patient group, practical medical result, current limitation, and the government's choice in a voice appropriate to the host and mandate. It should not copy these headings into repetitive final button prose.

Each medical family needs an icon composed for its actual decision or project consumer. A report image is justified for an important first clinical result, exceptional treatment, or a meaningful failure. Routine production does not need another dramatic illustration every cycle.

The selected project can show progress and the immediate resource barrier, while Wu Authority remains the principal persistent public value. A healthy institute displays a useful preventive proposal. A mature institute presents new opportunities through existing research and actual world conditions, keeping ordinary medicine relevant throughout the campaign.

## A complete supervised medical route

A small public host can appoint Wu, complete independent clinical teams, treat a first outbreak, organize an adaptive archive, and license production to a larger partner. It can then invest in tissue replacement, organ care, and an exceptional treatment while maintaining low Authority through distributed knowledge and explicit review.

Every step has a usable result before the next one completes. The first cure saves eligible living people. The archive preserves that knowledge. The production agreement reaches more patients. The later medical research expands what the institute can do. None requires an evolution or an unrestricted directorate.

The route remains powerful after Wu leaves because trained institutions retain documented medicine. It also remains uniquely valuable while he stays because he can adapt to the next valid biological crisis. That distinction is the foundation for the later military, replication, rivalry, and sovereignty decisions.
