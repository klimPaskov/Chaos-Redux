# Doctor Wu: settlements and aftermath

## What a settlement must leave working

A settlement determines who holds authority over the institute and what happens to the people, medicine, records, and formations already dependent on it. It can be a government victory, a negotiated compromise, a scientist's departure, a territorial separation, or the end of an armed conflict. The result must preserve a playable medical situation after the political decision.

The settlement record identifies the affected institutions and offices, the parties, the actual objects transferred, the commitments that continue, the obligations breached, and the remaining unresolved claims. A settlement can succeed politically and still require years of clinical care. It can preserve an archive without preserving the scientist who understands every unfinished note.

This part supplies proposed consequences for the fourth evolution and baseline removal or capture where the same physical issue exists. It does not make ordinary resignation, care, or custody dependent on an evolution. Names and numerical anchors are working design labels rather than finished localisation or implemented behavior.

## Constitutional settlement

### A public clinical constitution

The government keeps sovereign authority over budgets, public appointments, custody, military command, and foreign commitments. It recognizes a protected clinical domain in which qualified physicians can treat patients according to accepted methods and can report a harmful order without losing their livelihood automatically.

Wu can remain a research director, become a senior clinical adviser, or retire from public command while continuing a specifically licensed project. The selected appointment determines his active personal contribution. Keeping his portrait in the country without a working assignment supplies no universal cure service.

The constitution needs an independent archive, a qualified review body, access to actual facilities, and a funded care handover for services whose leadership changes. The government can reach these conditions through Parts 2, 7, 11, and 12. The final agreement recognizes completed work rather than charging the same qualification again.

Its practical payoff is enforceable suspension and replacement of an unsafe programme without collapsing every hospital. A new project still needs a qualified Wu or permitted successor when the project requires unique expertise. Ordinary institutions can continue the known medicine they have actually learned and can support.

A breach can occur when the government removes clinical protection, Wu hides another programme, a commander refuses a valid care withdrawal, or a regional authority seizes reserved stock. The breach names the responsible party and power. It reopens only the affected settlement terms unless a wider breakdown is proved.

### A divided institutional charter

Separate universities, hospitals, military services, and treatment works receive defined responsibilities. Wu retains a narrower scientific role. The arrangement lowers personal dependence through actual alternative competence and supply. It does not lower every Authority consequence by a fixed amount merely because the charter is signed.

Public and military work can coexist. Sensitive biological material stays with its legal custodian. Harmless treatment knowledge can be shared without releasing offensive designs. Regional services can choose a qualified independent provider while continuing a national emergency agreement.

The payoff is resilience. Losing one office or person interrupts only the services still dependent on it. The cost is duplicated administrative work, separate quality checks, and slower agreement on shared resources. Those costs belong to actual institutions and assignments rather than a permanent generic research penalty.

## Limited autonomy inside the host

The host can recognize an autonomous medical institute without creating a country. The charter names its sites, funding source, permitted programmes, appointments, audit rights, security limits, dispute procedure, and maximum term. Proposed review terms are 180 or 365 days depending on the institution's scale and the existing political commitment.

Wu can select staff within the charter, issue permitted clinical instructions, and manage a defined budget. The government retains the powers not granted. A charter for medicine does not confer offensive release authority, authority over refugees, ownership of military formations, or a right to annex the surrounding state.

Renewal requires evidence of actual operation and a current affordable commitment. It cannot repeatedly grant the founding Authority increase. A modified charter records only the new right, its actual clamped effect, and its expiry or reversal conditions.

The host can end the charter through its agreed process, negotiate an extension, or violate it through force. An orderly ending preserves care and lawful copies. A violation can create resistance, foreign claims, or loss of cooperation. The physical outcome remains separate from the legal argument.

## Removal from office

Removing Wu from an office ends the office's powers and future assignments. It does not delete him, erase learned methods, destroy stock, or move him out of the country. If he is free, he can accept another lawful appointment, negotiate departure, or refuse to cooperate further. If he is confined, custody is recorded separately.

The government first identifies which duties rely on Wu personally. Known production with qualified staff can continue. A cure under a reproducible protocol can finish. An unfinished universal adaptation that depends on his judgment may pause until he returns or a qualified successor can take over. A political successor who lacks that competence cannot finish it by signing the same papers.

Office removal can be complete even when a clinical transition remains active. The outgoing person can perform a limited supervised handover without retaining the removed political mandate. The handover has a defined scope and end. It cannot become an invisible permanent second appointment.

A dismissal of one continuity copy affects that person's office. Other copies retain their own legal positions and histories. If all qualified Wus leave, retained institutional medicine continues at the actual level of independent competence. The event's active universal improvisation ends until a valid qualified successor exists.

## Arrest and custody

An arrest requires an identified living target, a valid responsible actor, actual access, and a capable operation. A laboratory occupation is not proof that Wu was inside. An empty room, an incomplete body, a false identity, or a departed person produces the appropriate unresolved or failed result.

A successful arrest creates custody and removes incompatible active assignments. It does not grant cooperation, reveal every archive, or transfer a foreign institution. The captor can offer a protected scientific appointment, allow independent clinical contact, negotiate exchange or exile, investigate the case, or keep the person confined under the game's political rules.

Medical cooperation while confined must be explicit. It has a real service scope, qualification, supervision, and treatment supply. The captor cannot advertise voluntary employment while preventing the person from refusing or leaving. Coercive work creates its own evidence and institutional consequences.

Injury, illness, and death follow actual events. A dead original remains dead even if a qualified copy survives. An unqualified stored body remains a body requiring the Part 7 process. Suspected death remains uncertain until its owner can establish a result. There is no automatic total knowledge loss from one assassination and no automatic perfect continuity from a stored archive.

## Exile, transfer, and refuge

A negotiated departure separates the person, portable personal knowledge, authorized archive copies, physical samples, patients, and national rights. Each travels only through a valid route and owner transaction. A hospital cannot be packed into a scientist's inventory.

The destination can offer temporary refuge without employment. It can offer a visiting post, a supervised appointment, or a funded institute. A new institute has ordinary setup and operation costs. The arriving scientist's past projects do not become the recipient country's free completed portfolio.

The old host retains lawful copied knowledge and incomplete projects, with their original prerequisites and current progress. It loses services that depend on the departed person unless a qualified replacement exists. Destroyed or removed unique material remains unavailable even if the abstract research record survives.

A failed departure can leave the person at the origin, at an intermediate refuge, captured, or missing according to the actual route result. No country receives a second copy as compensation. A dangerous sample lost in transit is not automatically released into the atmosphere. Its material and incident owners determine the physical result and evidence.

The destination's food and reception proof is mandatory for ordinary people and living unsettled cohorts. Already settled people move through migration accounting. New unsettled people retain their finite origin and food obligation until first settlement. Neither route creates a fictional population debit from a country that never counted them.

## Dismantling the institute

Dismantling is phased by function because a medical institute contains different kinds of obligations. The government stops new admissions to the affected programme, secures the actual material, preserves authorized records, assigns care to qualified providers, and ends or transfers the responsible offices.

The player chooses the disposition of useful knowledge. Public archives can preserve harmless treatment and trained practice. Sensitive military knowledge can remain restricted. A dangerous physical sample can be destroyed while its historical study remains known. Erasing research records for political reasons can weaken independent medicine and create a cover-up finding when it conceals harm.

Buildings remain where they are. A laboratory closed as an institution can become an ordinary hospital, university facility, warehouse, or unused site after its proper conversion. The conversion has actual construction and qualification work. Clearing a laboratory flag does not remove factories or grant a new factory elsewhere.

Living experimental recipients require continuing care, retirement, reception, or supported transfer. An engineered organism needs containment or a qualified shutdown disposition. A clone formation needs normal personnel and unit settlement. Deaths are never implied by closure or silently deducted as a disposal cost.

The programme is fully dismantled when its offices are ended, remaining material has a known custodian or verified destruction, outstanding recipients have a valid continuing arrangement, and no active production or hidden assignment still uses the closed authority. Historical evidence, lawful knowledge, and genuine unresolved claims remain.

## A negotiated separation

A separate political entity requires exact territory or a recognized jurisdiction the country system can represent. The initial settlement lists the states, current owners and controllers, institutions, population, existing cores and claims, forces, equipment, public property, and outstanding obligations.

A state containing a hospital is not automatically eligible for transfer. A territorial claim needs actual administrative support or accepted political terms. A negotiated host can cede an exact controlled state. An armed claimant must establish real control through the appropriate war or country owner. Distant hospitals remain institutions unless their territory is separately and legitimately settled.

The former host can keep a transit right, receive continued medical service, retain a share of jointly owned records, or accept a demilitarized boundary. These are specific terms with costs and enforcement. A promise of medicine cannot become permanent unpaid tribute from a provider that has no production.

The new country receives the complete origin-specific package in Part 17. It is classified as an ordinary human society when its population and institutions remain human. Clones are not automatically nonhuman. Independent nonhuman groups retain their own classification and owner.

The separation creates no automatic global core map. Existing political identity, local acceptance, civilian governance, and subsequent integration determine lawful core or claim outcomes. The player can build a medical commonwealth through treaties without converting every patient state into national territory.

## Armed settlement and defeat

### Government victory

The government can retake the main site, restore lawful command, secure the archive, and remove the claimant's independent armed power. It does not have to destroy every hospital to win. Its objective set names the actual contested sites, units, and offices.

Victory can return local institutions to public administration, preserve a supervised research branch, arrest or exile an actual claimant, and demobilize or reassign supported formations. The government must still restore compatible care and fund repair. Captured stock retains its design and condition. A victorious country does not gain unresearched knowledge merely because it owns the site.

### Wu victory

Wu can establish a viable recognized state, force limited autonomy, or secure a negotiated withdrawal of the government's claim. His victory depends on territory, support, supply, and actual command. It does not automatically annex the entire former host or alter every soldier's loyalty.

A victorious institute faces the same medical commitments that helped it survive. It must feed its civilian population, care for dependent formations, pay for production, and choose a political order. Private power can be consolidated, divided among qualified institutions, or constrained by a civic settlement.

### A divided result

Different services can settle differently. The capital government may regain political control while a distant institute receives autonomy. A qualified Wu may enter exile while another stays as a supervised physician. A military formation may stand down after a care guarantee while its commander remains under investigation.

A divided result is one reconciled outcome with explicit object ownership. It cannot assign the same laboratory or person to both sides. Where precise territorial or unit transfer cannot be implemented safely, that transaction is blocked pending owner support. It is not replaced with fresh units, arbitrary border transfers, or an invented population loss.

## Several claimants and succession

A continuity dispute asks who can hold an office and deliver a service. It does not decide which person is metaphysically real. Each claimant's dated memory, qualification, current appointment, staff support, and obligations are assessed separately.

Three settlement forms cover the useful outcomes. A sole qualified successor takes a specific office. A college of qualified Wus shares defined duties under a single charter. Separate appointments place different copies in different institutions or countries. Each form preserves one full-time assignment per person and the country-wide modified-production ceiling.

A college is not a free research multiplier. Each member supplies only the capabilities and time actually available. Disagreement can suspend a shared command or send one member into an independent appointment, but cannot duplicate the college's stock and staff. The same treatment result is credited once even when several copies contributed.

A copy that supported coercive work retains responsibility for its own acts. It does not inherit an original's guilt purely by genetic identity. The original does not become innocent because another copy performed later clinical repair. Evidence stays attached to the responsible actor and act.

## Joint and divided sovereignty with Kruger

A joint Directorate can exist when both scientists and their actual institutions accept a shared political charter. The charter assigns clinical authority, mechanical or other scientific authority, public oversight, security command, budget, and succession. It does not merge every private helper or technology record.

Kruger remains the single canonical character owned by Event 016. A move into a new country must pass his guarded transfer and origin rules. Wu copies retain Part 7's identities. If one scientist cannot legally transfer, the joint arrangement can remain a cross-border compact or fail to form. The package never creates a duplicate to make the story convenient.

A negotiated division can give Wu medicine and biological custody, Kruger non-biological research, and the state public administration. Shared devices require continuing cooperation or a safe discontinuation plan. Neither side can seize a completed joint result twice by declaring that its contribution was indispensable.

A scientific civil conflict requires real parties and resources. It can divide institutions or territories through their owners. The conflict is not automatically a global war and does not launch either scientist's unrelated ending. Foreign governments can support a particular legal settlement or clinical service without automatically joining a faction.

## Reconstruction, responsibility, and memory

Reconstruction identifies damaged sites, interrupted care, displaced people, missing materials, and unresolved command. Repair work changes actual buildings and services. Care can reverse a living person's disease or injury where the owner supports it. It cannot restore recorded dead population.

The host can open independent archives, compensate a proven breach, support affected veterans, receive displaced staff, investigate a hidden programme, or create a protected public successor institution. These are genuine post-crisis choices. A blanket positive spirit does not replace them.

Evidence can be public, hidden, unresolved, or disproved. Responsibility remains with the owner of the act. A national name change, new cabinet, qualified copy, or transferred laboratory cannot reset Condemnation, history, or liability. Existing sanction withdrawal uses its shared owner and releases only the correct source claim.

The final active event state can be supervised medicine, a limited autonomous institute, dispersed independent services, a foreign-hosted Wu, a sovereign medical country, an active unresolved confrontation, or retained medicine without a qualified Wu. These are distinct ongoing conditions with appropriate current actions. None needs a new event ID or a repeated arrival reward.

## AI settlement priorities

AI evaluates whether it can preserve care, enforce the selected outcome, and survive the resulting political and military position. It must not choose arrest because it has enough Political Power when it cannot reach Wu. It must not choose exile when no destination accepts the party. It must not choose territorial separation as a free way to remove a costly population or erase responsibility.

A state with independent services prefers constitutional resolution. A dependent state may choose a phased limited-autonomy settlement. An armed claimant without food or treatment should seek a viable compromise or safe departure. A medically successful Wu can accept a professional role without sovereign rule, particularly when independent institutions protect his work.
