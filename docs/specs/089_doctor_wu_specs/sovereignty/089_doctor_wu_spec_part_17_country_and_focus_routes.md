# Doctor Wu: the sovereign medical country

## A country that must support the people who depend on it

A sovereign Wu country begins with a medical institution, a contested political settlement, and an economy that must keep its promises. Its laboratories may be unusually capable, but its food, equipment, transport, civilian administration, and military command remain real constraints. The player must decide whether medical leadership becomes a public service, a personal scientific state, or a negotiated federation of institutes.

This country package is used only when Parts 15–16 produce an actual country. The ordinary host keeps its meaningful national focus tree and manages Wu through decisions. An autonomous institute inside a host does not load a replacement country tree.

All country identities, route labels, flags, focus groups, and values below are proposals for a wholly fictional institution. They do not claim historical existence. The carrier's actual three-character tag must be resolved through current country-registry and collision evidence before implementation. A working package identifier is not a reserved tag.

## Origins and starting territory

Three origins share the package. A negotiated separation receives exactly the territory and institutions accepted in the settlement. A breakaway receives territory actually controlled and transferred through the relevant conflict owner. A recognized existing host can adopt a Wu political administration through a real internal political transition without creating a duplicate country.

Each origin records the former host, the sovereign settlement, the principal qualified Wu or governing institution, the founding sites, the exact states, the participating formations, and the obligations retained. Origin controls which opening focuses, diplomacy, and claims make sense. An exiled institute without sovereign territory remains a foreign institution and cannot use a country carrier as a substitute for refuge.

The capital must be an actual controlled administrative site that the package can govern and supply. A laboratory anchor by itself is insufficient if the state cannot support civilian administration. The founding set must respect current map boundaries, existing ownership, native country viability, and the former host's accepted survival terms. No historical state ID is guessed from a place name.

Additional institute sites outside the founding set remain foreign property or contractual access. Their medicine, staff, and cooperation can support the new country only through actual agreements. Receiving medical care does not give another state's population automatic citizenship or make that state a core.

The package uses a compatible existing carrier or a newly reserved verified carrier only after checking active ownership, reservations, regional identity, and current vanilla and installed-mod collisions. It must not invoke an Event 006 country setup or Soviet Collapse identity merely because it reuses a carrier listed in their collections. The new origin owns its own loading and cleanup.

## The founding resource settlement

State ownership changes do not move population physically. Existing residents remain in the same states and are not credited again through migration. Actual moving refugees and staff use their movement owner. Unsettled finite people retain their first-settlement status and food demand even if their custodian becomes a new country.

Buildings remain in the states where they exist, with their real damage. National stock must be divided by an explicit accepted settlement or validated conflict transaction. Local depots can transfer their proven contents. An unproved share of the former host's entire stockpile cannot be invented from the new country's territory ratio.

Material retains its fixed design, model, condition, source, and reservation. A sovereign transition cannot reset Part 9's standards or convert legacy clone stock into D03 finite people. The country-wide ceiling is two active modified production standards across all branches and qualified Wus in that country. A physical works transferred from another country needs its continuing qualification and cannot manufacture a newly selected standard automatically.

Staff and formations transfer with their actual identity and assignments. Native casualties, experience, equipment, and service obligations must be preserved. When the engine or owner cannot prove that transfer, the implementation must close that gap explicitly before claiming the route works. It must not generate an undamaged replacement as a hidden shortcut.

The founding transaction cannot replay arrival, cure, research, cloning, or project-completion rewards. The new administration acquires custody and control over the objects actually transferred. Its access to knowledge depends on the records and qualified people that arrived.

## Political identity and leadership

The first public identity follows the founding settlement. A negotiated civic institution can use a medical commonwealth working identity. A personal claimant can use a Wu Directorate working identity. A federation of autonomous institutes can use an institute union working identity. These are alternative visible political states of the same package, not a requirement to create several tags.

The leader can be the active qualified Wu, a specifically recognized continuity copy, or an institutional council created by the civic or federal route. A council uses an institutional portrait. It is not represented by an invented historical official. No grounded foreign scientist is duplicated as a free advisor.

The ruling party and ideology follow the actual founding political settlement, then change only through a route that changes governance. A civic charter can establish accountable public government. A personal Directorate can consolidate non-aligned scientific rule. A centralized public-service alternative can preserve the host's existing political tradition where the founding constituency supports it. The tree does not add four shallow ideology lanes to satisfy a quota.

High command and military leadership come from actual participating commanders or a funded officer programme. Ordinary named fictional officer generation follows the owning country's rules and does not grant an exceptional commander as a founding reward. Any new visible portrait needs its own explicit asset row and identity basis.

## Starting forces and sustainable reinforcement

The initial forces are the formations that supported or were assigned to the settlement, plus any paid and completed local muster. A country expecting armed resistance must have a valid founding defense package before separation commits. A negotiated demilitarized country instead needs an actual accepted protection arrangement and a playable route to its own defense.

A local muster uses the chosen template's real manpower and equipment cost. Its quantity is the minimum supported by available recruits, available equipment, training places, command staff, supply, and the founding force ceiling. None of these inputs is inferred from Wu Authority. The template owner supplies the real per-unit cost vector, so the specification does not invent native battalion costs.

A small negotiated enclave begins with the minimum supported guard and medical transport structure. An armed breakaway can field its actual transferred enhanced or clone units, but must immediately secure their care supply. An established host adopting the government keeps its existing forces and meaningful templates, with no duplicate founding army.

Reinforcement has three routes. Ordinary recruitment trains conventional defense forces through real barracks and equipment. Medical specialization enrolls selected existing formations through Part 6. Finite replacement personnel use Part 7's shared-owner extension after its acceptance and implementation. Captured legacy clones retain their existing recurring economy and never substitute for the finite route.

Foreign volunteers, equipment, and officers require valid diplomatic agreements and their owner's transactions. A promise of assistance does not count as a starting unit. Naval and air forces are not created merely because the country has a coast or airbase. It can retain actual transferred assets and develop later affordable capabilities.

## Three national spirits with real lifecycles

The country uses no more than three package-owned national spirits at once. Existing external crisis modifiers are not duplicated under Wu names. Where an inherited Wu institution spirit already represents a role below, the route replaces or evolves that spirit instead of adding another.

The numerical effects are calculated from the actual supported service state and accepted tuning. They cannot award blanket free medicine, equipment, or staff. A negative spirit must have visible actions that can mitigate it. It must not remain a permanent arbitrary tax after its underlying problem is solved.

## Focus-tree architecture

The sovereign tree has an opening survival group and six distinct branch families. Each family has a clear root, an internal choice, an action unlock, and a lasting payoff. Focus roles are specified below. The implementation owns final focus names, count, coordinates, and exact connectors while preserving the route architecture.

| Lane | Opening group and development | Principal fork or convergence | Play after the branch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founding settlement | Confirm the actual administration, maintain care, reconcile founding forces | Negotiated, armed, or existing-host origin | A viable country with explicit obligations and a legal political choice. |
| Politics and institutions | Define medical office, public oversight, rights, and succession | Civic service, personal Directorate, or institute federation | Different appointment, budget, autonomy, and foreign-access powers. |
| Industry and logistics | Stabilize working sites, repair routes, choose production geography | Central works or distributed regional capacity | Real manufacture, transport, maintenance, and shortage response. |
| Defense and military medicine | Secure founding forces, restore training, qualify care | Conventional defense or selected specialist formations | Sustainable force growth and genuine operational choices. |
| Foreign medicine and security | Obtain recognition, fulfill old agreements, negotiate new services | Neutral access network or strategic protection compact | Trade, teaching, protective obligations, and evidence-based competition. |
| Regional settlement | Resolve former-host claims, link legitimate sites, settle borders | Negotiated association or prepared limited conflict | Territory or treaty rights with integration and postwar administration. |
| Scientific responsibility | Preserve archives, continue baseline work, resolve hazardous programmes | Independent qualification or personal control | A late institutional outcome tied to the actual portfolio. |

The opening has a 7-day administrative confirmation followed by a small set of 35-day strategic choices. A 70-day institutional or construction focus is used only where it creates substantial work. Longer projects belong in visible decision or mission systems when actual physical objectives matter. No focus exists solely to divide one reward into more clicks.

Each spatially separate lane needs a Focus Navigation anchor and accurate search filters. Important convergence points connect politics to funding, defense to care, and regional settlement to logistics. The final layout must avoid intersecting lines and deceptive offset ladders. Render-based first-glance branch review is required during implementation.

## The founding branch

The first choice is how to keep current obligations alive. The country can prioritize the main clinical service, secure the founding supply route, or reconcile military command. The right choice depends on the real origin and current shortage. A peaceful enclave should not begin with a compulsory war plan. An armed breakaway cannot spend months on ceremonial recognition while its only care supply expires.

The next group establishes provisional civil administration and names the offices responsible for stock, patients, and security. It does not grant more people or factories. Completion unlocks the ordinary target-specific actions that repair sites, train replacements, and negotiate property claims.

The branch ends when the founding obligations have owners and the country can operate without relying on a duplicated or missing transaction. Its payoff is removal of the relevant starting administrative weakness and access to the main political fork. It does not erase unpaid commitments or confirmed misconduct.

## Civic medical service

This political route places public administration above the personal institute. Its first group protects clinical reporting and establishes independent appointment and budget review. The second group qualifies regional services and preserves accessible harmless knowledge. The third group settles succession and civilian rights for enhanced people and finite new citizens.

Its major unlock is a public service compact that lets qualified institutions replace one another without requiring Wu's personal political approval. The replacement still needs real qualification and material. The route favors open treatment standards, foreign teaching, and need-based medicine contracts.

The route's military choice favors conventional forces with strong ordinary medical support, while allowing selected enhancement through explicit lawful permission. It rejects using treatment as a loyalty test. Its regional policy emphasizes association and negotiated transit, with defensive conflict available when actual threats require it.

The capstone is a medical commonwealth working identity after the public charter and service obligations are functioning. Wu can remain a qualified research leader or surrender political office to the institutional council. The flag and portrait state change only at that real transition. The route does not promise automatic world peace or cure every disease without resources.

## Personal Directorate

This route gives Wu direct appointment, priority, and security powers. Its early choices consolidate the actual institutions that already follow him. Later groups can bind military medicine, personal research direction, and continuity succession into a single administrative command.

Its advantage is rapid direction within the delegated scope and the ability to pursue a coherent specialized programme. Its costs are dependence, political isolation, and correlated failure when the central service is disrupted. It receives no free research and no automatic extra production standard.

A controlled branch retains public clinical guarantees and a qualified emergency successor. An unrestricted branch can accept coercive methods, concealed programmes, and stronger personal command when those actions meet their actual prerequisites. The latter creates evidence and opposition. It cannot remove the shared constraints on physical material or use an evolution flag as scientific access.

The capstone confirms a personal scientific government with a named succession rule. It may recognize one Wu or a controlled continuity college. The state remains vulnerable if its required care and material cannot be maintained. A constitutional reform route can later transfer power to public institutions through real transition work, without replaying the founding package.

## Institute federation

This route gives regional institutions authority over local appointments and preserves a limited common clinical and diplomatic compact. Its early groups define the membership charter and divide existing resources. Its middle groups build reciprocal qualification, dispute resolution, and shared emergency contributions. Its later group permits association of willing foreign institutions through treaty rather than automatic territorial absorption.

The route's advantage is resilience and access to several sources of knowledge and care. Its cost is slower common agreement and the possibility that a member declines a proposed contribution. The common authority cannot reserve a member's stock twice or promise its staff without acceptance.

Several qualified Wus can work in different member institutions under distinct appointments. A council can coordinate shared standards without becoming a single immortal scientist. Disagreement can lead to an institutional split, a new contract, or withdrawal from future commitments. Existing patient care must still be settled.

Its capstone is a functioning federation with reliable mutual service and an agreed external representation. Political integration of territory remains a separate regional settlement branch. Membership of a medical network does not itself grant a core, claim, or annexation option.

## Industry, food, and transport

The economic branch begins with existing damaged works and routes. It unlocks target-specific repairs, storage, transport maintenance, and training capacity. A focus can authorize a construction programme, but the programme builds or repairs the actual site with its ordinary costs and time.

Central works concentrate qualified production and simplify dispatch. Distributed works need more qualification and logistics but reduce dependence on one site. Both obey actual factory limits and the country-wide modified-standard ceiling. Harmless cure production and continuing control treatment remain separate from the two modified standards.

Food security is a national constraint. Agricultural medicine can reduce a disease component through Part 8, while food production, reserves, imports, and relief remain with the famine owner. New people are admitted only to real receiving capacity with current food proof. A successful population-restoration focus does not create a free civilian population grant.

A coastal state can develop commercial ports, convoys, repair, and escorted medical transport. A landlocked state focuses on rail, roads, supply hubs, and negotiated transit. The route grants useful logistics actions appropriate to geography. It does not add a token naval column to a country with no maritime role.

The economic capstone is sustained production and delivery of the country's accepted commitments. The player can then choose to expand foreign contracts, reduce emergency burden, or fund additional baseline research. Its payoff is a functioning economy and changed options, not an automatic stockpile refill.

## Defense, air, and naval support

Conventional defense builds training, command, ordinary equipment production, and strong medical support. It can remain the preferred route for a small country. Selected specialist formations enroll only the units and care capacity the country can support. Enhanced troops, engineered lineages, and clone formations retain their original provider and support rules.

The military branch splits further only when the geography and actual force base justify it. An air branch supports reconnaissance, transport, evacuation, and protection of working sites using actual aircraft and trained crews. A maritime branch supports commercial routes, escorts, ports, and limited coastal defense using actual naval capacity. Their rewards are useful to the wider medical economy.

The intelligence group secures archives, investigates specific threats, and manages foreign access. It does not grant automatic knowledge of every hostile operation. An intelligence focus that reveals a case must be tied to an actual observation or operation owner.

The military capstone chooses a sustainable doctrine of protected national defense or limited specialist expeditionary service. Expeditionary commitments require the relevant foreign agreement, route, and care. They are not a mechanism for giving every ally a free enhanced army.

## Regional settlement and expansion

The regional branch begins with the founding dispute. It can negotiate recognition, restore property, establish transit, agree a border, or prepare a limited conflict where a real claim and capable force exist. Treatment demand alone never creates a war goal.

A medical enclave can negotiate association with nearby willing authorities, receiving administrative rights or an exact territorial settlement. A larger Directorate can pursue control of a strategic laboratory or route only through a genuine political or military conflict and the proper declaration owner. A federation can seek voluntary membership, with territorial integration requiring a separate accepted settlement.

Postwar work is mandatory. The country must restore civil administration, resolve local opposition, maintain care, repair transport, and decide between autonomy, return, association, or direct integration. Cores are earned only where the political and population integration contract supports them. A destroyed city does not become integrated because its living population is smaller.

The branch's late payoff is a secure regional compact, recognized political union, or a bounded strategically viable state. Its success must not depend on one arbitrary foreign AI acceptance. Alternative sponsors, negotiated narrower terms, domestic development, and legitimate defensive preparation provide different routes without guaranteeing expansion.

## Baseline science inside the country

The sovereign government inherits access only to the projects, records, material, and qualified people actually transferred. Its focus tree can prioritize or fund the existing baseline portfolio, establish oversight, or open a valid contribution agreement. It cannot replace baseline prerequisites with focus completion.

Every compatible family remains reachable at Calm World through real requirements. Disabling an evolution does not remove the country's learned technologies, people, or normal research. It stops the extra behaviors of that evolution while the country continues through its own ordinary political and economic life.

The scientific branch offers a public independent qualification path, a controlled military-service path, and a personal research-priority path compatible with the chosen government. Its capstone determines who may approve the next project and how the country handles failure. It does not add a fifth evolution named Biology Without Borders.

## Decisions unlocked by the tree

The tree reuses the established research, production, care, and diplomacy actions. New country-specific decision families cover provisional administration, founding property settlement, paid local muster, route repair, foreign recognition, institutional association, and postwar integration.

Each family has dynamic named targets, at most four spendable cost types, phased visibility, and AI using the same validity as the player. A founding muster commits manpower and actual equipment through the template owner. A repair action commits construction capacity and necessary material at the named site. An association action negotiates terms and then asks each member to commit actual contributions.

Proposed ordinary objective horizons are 90 days for an administrative transfer, 120–180 days for recognition or route security, and 180–365 days for reconstruction and integration. Care emergencies retain their actual shorter clocks. Physical objectives auto-complete when achieved and do not require a second payment click.

## AI route plans

A small negotiated country prioritizes food, current care, recognition, and conventional defense. It usually chooses civic or federal administration when its institutions are independently qualified. A dependent armed breakaway prioritizes treatment supply and command, then chooses personal consolidation or a negotiated constitutional settlement according to actual supporters.

A public successful network favors treaties and teaching over territorial seizure. A threatened Directorate can accept strategic protection and selected military specialization. A multi-site federation favors route redundancy and reciprocal qualifications. All routes refuse unsupported expansion, unsafe reception, unaffordable force growth, and projects lacking actual source material.

AI focus selection must respond to shortages and active objectives. A focus that cannot produce its intended result because its site is lost or its required person departed should be blocked or replaced through the supported route, without granting a generic compensation bonus.
