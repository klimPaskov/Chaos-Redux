## Cost contract

Every gameplay-changing action has at most four distinct spendable or committed cost types, including staff, formations and factory capacity when used as payment. Requirements that are not spent remain separate. Command Power never exceeds 60 for one decision. Do not hide a fifth payment in a tooltip, nested effect, confirmation or second panel.

Use one frozen quote where bidding, transfer or accepted service commitments require it. Current affordability, the displayed quote and actual debit must agree. Shared inclusive affordability must be called by both availability and custom cost validation. Debit once at the correct transaction boundary. Preserve reserved amounts and record refunds only when the owning transaction proves that exact refund is due.

Inline rows are compact, use matching texticons and follow the skill's three-value display limit. Full tooltips still disclose every payment and reservation. Do not repeat native PP display or replace missing texticons with long literal cost labels. Scope and target names must be current and understandable.

The Part 12 quarantine delegation has a known unresolved quote issue because the inspected shared helper references six possible payment paths. Inspect actual nonzero values and callers before admitting that shared action to automatic national service. Do not simply declare those payments free or move them into hidden effects.

## Missions, deadlines and shared obligations

Goal-style objectives auto-complete after the player does the work. Do not require another paid click after holding the named site or delivering the actual agreed lot. Normal objectives usually need at least 90 days, with longer horizons for larger tasks. A genuine emergency may be shorter, with the reason visible and a feasible response.

Care clocks are owned by the patients, courses or formations they protect. A political review or 90-day qualification does not reset a 45-day control deadline. Use qualified interim service, another feasible provider or timely withdrawal. New offices cannot spend material already reserved for domestic or foreign care.

Use actual places and routes. Dynamic localisation must name the selected states, institutions, recipients or country. Preserve current food and reception proof and avoid duplicate receiving capacity. A movement is not a death, and a custody change is not a second first settlement.

Define success, partial success, failure, cancellation and invalidation separately. Successful stabilizing work cannot accidentally increase the same pressure it claims to reduce. Failure follows the actual project and leaves the specified recovery choices. Native raid cancellation is not a scripted medicine-contract cancellation and cannot inherit its refund rule.

## Visibility and action integrity

The default is one ordinary Wu decision category with an attached Authority and context display. Phase relevant actions by selected object and campaign state. Normally show three to five primary actions, with six as the hard maximum, and one to three active objectives. The main value is Authority, normally with at most two relevant supporting values. Do not evade these limits by distributing duplicate weak controls over tabs.

Public, military and classified routes have different permissions but retain a valid path into every compatible baseline family. All four evolutions can be disabled without locking research. Evolution-specific competition, unauthorized behavior and sovereignty powers stop according to their own continuation rules, while actual care and committed transactions remain valid.

Every delayed callback must match the original object, generation, actor, host and action type. Reopening a popup, changing the current target, moving Wu, annexing a country or releasing it again cannot mutate an unrelated later action. A double click must not spend twice, reserve two copies, restore a consumed lot or create a second person.

Foreign hostile slots are separate from clinical request handling. Merging repeated requests must not erase legitimate different recipients or obligations. Kruger's private contest and sanction participant automation retain their own owners. Do not enable two AI paths for the same sanction pair action.
