# Doctor Wu: achievements and campaign validation

## What a completed campaign should demonstrate

The achievement set rewards the difficult parts of managing Wu: supplying actual treatment, building independent institutions, preserving people through a handover, supporting unusual formations, and resolving the political consequences of scientific dependence. Hiring the doctor, purchasing a first project, or reaching a Chaos threshold does not by itself earn an achievement.

All labels and identifiers below are proposed design identifiers. They are not final localisation. Quantities and durations are proposed tuning. The final implementation should retain the intended challenge when current owner contracts require a different measurement, and record that change before altering a requirement. An unavailable measurement is a validation blocker, not permission to award the achievement from a nearby flag.

Achievement tracking consumes the authoritative outcome that already changed the world. It never repeats treatment, transfers people, grants equipment, launches another source event, or calls the same consequence again to obtain a record. A qualifying event can update several compatible achievements, but each unlock occurs once.

## Common tracking rules

Use the existing achievement framework and preserve its eligibility settings. A forced or debug setup cannot prove a naturally earned research, institutional, population, or crisis outcome. CXT setup is specifically useful for testing guards, but it cannot earn campaign achievements. Separate a test result from a legitimate campaign unlock in both documentation and persistent data.

Track the responsible country, campaign generation, actual recipient or project identity, and completion date where the condition requires them. A country losing territory or changing its name does not obtain another copy of an old achievement. A released country begins with the history actually transferred to it, not every achievement recorded by its former host. A scientist taking a foreign job cannot carry national care achievements as personal rewards.

For duration challenges, count only days for which the responsible owner confirms the qualifying condition. A short documented suspension can pause a measure only where the specific achievement says so. Otherwise a broken condition restarts the continuous interval. Never treat an unloaded or untracked state as a successful day. Completed historical medical results remain historical after patients later migrate, age, or die from another cause.

Person-count requirements use unique first-settlement or treatment identities at the relevant owner's supported resolution. A repeated course is not another person. Formation renaming, splitting, merging, strategic redeployment, captured stock, and country transfer cannot duplicate credit. Where precise individual identities are not available, require a disjoint owner-certified cohort partition and record that limitation.

The icons have three framework states: completed, grey or not yet completed, and ineligible. Use the supplied achievement templates and unchanged ineligible overlay through the current asset workflow. Do not bake achievement guidance into ordinary event options or reveal hidden routes through the public Event Details premise.

## Achievement roster

### ACH01: Independent medicine

Working purpose: prove that the institution can survive the loss of its founder. The country must have three independently qualified service providers in different controlled states, each able to deliver an already learned treatment without Wu's personal improvisation. After Wu leaves the relevant national appointment, maintain all three providers for 180 consecutive days and complete at least one actual treatment delivery from each.

A continuity copy personally directing any of those three services prevents that service from counting as independent. A qualified ordinary successor is permitted. The country must meet every existing care obligation associated with the providers during the interval. Copying a curriculum without staffing the service is insufficient.

Track departure, provider independence, live qualifications, supply continuity, and accepted deliveries. Losing one provider resets the interval, while historical qualifications remain. Difficulty is hard. The icon direction is three separate clinical lamps sharing a medical archive, without Wu's portrait.

### ACH02: Calm research portfolio

Working purpose: demonstrate that research genuinely remains baseline. During one continuous period with Chaos below 200 and all four Wu evolutions disabled, complete at least one qualified project from each of the medical, enhancement, controlled-organism, replication, designed-life, and modified-capability families. Each must meet its normal source-material and permission requirements.

The achievement does not demand every project in the mod or force unrelated source events to fire. It records the six family proofs produced during the qualifying period. Previously completed work may satisfy prerequisites but does not count as a new family completion. Crossing 200 ends the attempt and clears only this challenge's accumulated qualifying set, never the research itself.

Forced technology grants and debug material disqualify the attempt. Difficulty is very hard because the player must obtain compatible material without relying on Chaos as an unlock. The icon direction is six distinct research instruments around an unbroken clinical seal.

### ACH03: Treat the living

Working purpose: complete owner-confirmed treatment reversals for both a living-population zombie episode and a Black Plague human episode. Each result must concern an actual active disease, not a manufactured demonstration or deliberate farming episode.

An existing Zombie or Rat actor remains under its own rules throughout the cure. The achievement does not require keeping a hostile actor alive indefinitely. It requires proof that the medical result did not delete that actor or restore recorded dead population. A later military victory is compatible.

Track disease-specific episode identity, accepted treatment result, and conservation assertions. Difficulty is hard. The icon direction is two distinct treatment records protecting living silhouettes, with no reanimated person shown becoming alive again.

### ACH04: A supplied international compact

Working purpose: support a medical compact spanning at least three sovereign countries and two independently qualified production providers. Deliver the correct treatment to an active outbreak in each participating country and keep the agreed continuation service operating for 180 days after the last of those deliveries.

A nominal member with no accepted contribution does not qualify. The compact must use actual national stores, valid routes, and the same unique recipients used by the medical owner. No coalition stock balance may duplicate those stores. Losing one of the minimum three participants before the interval ends resets the duration.

Track agreements, provider independence, deliveries, and continuing duties. Difficulty is hard. The icon direction is three linked medical seals with separate supplies beneath each.

### ACH05: Finite people, lasting records

Working purpose: settle 10,000 distinct people through the approved finite replication mode, with at least 2,500 entering civilian life and at least 2,500 entering ordinary manpower or a specific formation. All first settlements must conserve the shared cohort ledger.

The number is a campaign total of new people, not recurring income from stock. Specialist appointments drawn from civilian recipients do not add credit. A later transfer or death does not allow first settlement again. Legacy clone cohorts cannot substitute for the finite mode.

D03 must be accepted and implemented before this achievement can ship. Track unique first settlements and the full conservation equation. Difficulty is very hard. The icon direction is one cohort ledger branching into civilian and military lives without duplicated entries.

### ACH06: Five supported lineages

Working purpose: maintain one qualified formation or owner-supported service group from each engineered lineage, Command, Assault, Hunter, Carrier, and Guardian, through a complete 180-day supported service period.

Each must perform its actual permitted role. Guardian remains in a prepared site, Hunter completes reconnaissance or pursuit service, and the other families use their defined service obligations. Carrier can satisfy the challenge through secure handling and authorized defensive study. Offensive infection is not required or rewarded.

A substitute generic zombie formation does not count. Any unresolved control failure pauses the campaign attempt until every affected group returns to safe service, after which the 180-day interval restarts. Difficulty is very hard. The icon direction is five distinct formation marks linked to one visible support train.

### ACH07: Veterans remain patients

Working purpose: return a permanently altered formation from operational service and provide 365 days of continuous qualified veteran care to its surviving recorded cohort. Complete the supported civilian or continuing-service settlement selected for that cohort.

Do not require every original member to survive unrelated combat. The denominator is the surviving cohort at retirement, with later medically recorded deaths and transfers handled by the care owner. Simply disbanding a formation cannot clear the duty or award the achievement.

Track retirement, recipients, care, and settlement. A missed care obligation restarts the interval and remains part of the medical record. Difficulty is hard. The icon direction is a military service badge beside a continuing clinical appointment.

### ACH08: The archive survived the journey

Working purpose: evacuate Wu or a qualified successor, one genuine treatment archive, and a threatened patient cohort from a site that is actually endangered, then resume the displaced service at a prepared destination.

The patient movement must use a proven route and receiving capacity. Only surviving arrivals count. Material and people cannot be teleported by the archive transfer. The destination must complete one real follow-up course using the moved or legitimately copied knowledge.

Track source threat, separate people and material transfers, destination admission, and service restart. Self-created threat solely to farm the achievement does not qualify. Difficulty is medium to hard. The icon direction is an intact case of clinical records beside a correctly received patient transport.

### ACH09: Cure access under restrictions

Working purpose: deliver a necessary cure to a country under an active sanction relationship through a route that the relevant material, diplomacy, and sanction owners affirmatively permit. Maintain the recipient's care through the next required follow-up.

A humanitarian carve-out alone is insufficient. The active sanction claim must remain correctly represented, including other systems' embargo claims. The challenge is meeting a difficult legal and logistical constraint, not clearing sanctions temporarily to bypass the condition.

Track the exact pair, route permission, material transfer, accepted delivery, and follow-up. Difficulty is hard. The icon direction is a medical consignment with a valid limited-access seal, without implying a universal exemption.

### ACH10: A counterfeit exposed

Working purpose: trace a failed or counterfeit foreign treatment to the correct supplied lot, identify the unsupported claim, withdraw the affected stock, and provide a qualified replacement to its surviving recipients.

Accusation without evidence is insufficient. A genuine treatment damaged in transport must not be falsely classified as forgery. The underlying case must establish either intentional false identity or a documented incompatible substitution. The player may expose a friendly supplier when evidence supports that conclusion.

Track lot identity, finding, actual withdrawal, replacement, and truthful attribution. Difficulty is medium. The icon direction is two visibly different seals under a clinical magnifier.

### ACH11: Several Wus, separate offices

Working purpose: have three independently produced and qualified Wu continuities serving different recorded appointments for 180 days. Their qualifications may overlap, but no individual, laboratory slot, reserved material, or ongoing personal service can be counted twice.

Stored bodies and unqualified copies do not count. The copies must have completed their own orientation and office-specific requirements. Their agreement or disagreement is not itself a condition, so the challenge does not force a scripted conflict.

Track finite creation, qualification, appointment identity, and resource separation. D03 and character-carrier support must be proven before shipping. Difficulty is very hard. The icon direction is three related portraits with distinct office symbols and dated archive marks.

### ACH12: A joint service that works

Working purpose: complete one of Part 10's joint Wu–Kruger application families and operate the resulting service for 180 days with both biological and technical responsibilities supplied by qualified staff.

An ordinary research bonus or a shared institute flag is insufficient. The service must have a concrete recipient, physical equipment or material, and a completed task. Kruger remains one canonical character, and either scientist may later be replaced by a properly qualified service owner where the project permits it.

Track joint admission, both owner completions, commissioning, and support. Difficulty is hard. The icon direction is a biological interface joined to a mechanical component with two separate approval marks.

### ACH13: Evidence without abandonment

Working purpose: expose and close an actual unauthorized programme under Evolution III, preserve the relevant evidence, and maintain necessary treatment for all surviving recipients of that programme until qualified discharge or transfer.

Publicity alone does not count as closure. Destroying every record or stopping essential care invalidates the challenge. A lawful new programme may continue under a new consent and permission basis, but it must not erase the original abuse.

Track an authenticated deviation, attributed responsibility, programme closure, care outcomes, and evidence retention. Difficulty is hard. The icon direction is an open investigation file beside an uninterrupted clinical service.

### ACH14: A constitutional settlement

Working purpose: resolve Sovereign Biology through a binding public settlement that the institute actually obeys, then maintain independent review and supported national services for 365 days.

A high or low Authority number alone cannot award it. The country needs proof that contested orders, custody rights, appointment powers, and care responsibilities were settled. No active private command may continue the same prohibited programme under another name.

Track the settlement obligations and their observable compliance. A new material breach resets the interval and remains historical evidence. Difficulty is very hard. The icon direction is a medical charter held by a civilian council, without a personal ruler's crown.

### ACH15: A state with patients

Working purpose: form a viable Wu-led country through a genuine settlement or conflict, preserve the transferred people and material exactly, and sustain its civilian medical obligations for 365 days.

The state must have an actual capital, territorial administration, supplied defense or a binding protection arrangement, and a working care network. It must also complete one post-formation diplomatic or regional settlement objective from its focus route.

An empty tag, a free army, duplicated stocks, or missing food proof fails eligibility. Track country origin, transfer conservation, survival, care, and the route payoff. Difficulty is very hard. The icon direction is a medical civic seal over an inhabited settlement.

### ACH16: A federation of institutes

Working purpose: maintain a federation with three genuinely autonomous institutes in distinct states, settle one real dispute through its agreed procedure, and have each institute complete a different accepted service contribution.

The institutes must retain independent appointment or review rights under the federation charter. Three branches controlled by the same personal office do not satisfy the challenge. A dispute manufactured by repeatedly toggling the same settled policy does not count.

Track charter rights, distinct contributions, dispute, and settlement compliance. Difficulty is hard. The icon direction is three different institutional emblems around an open shared charter.

### ACH17: Medicine during retreat

Working purpose: during a major losing war, move one active military treatment service to a secure location, complete its planned withdrawal or rotation, and keep the surviving selected formation under qualified care for the following 180 days.

The war owner must establish genuine strategic danger. Deliberately deleting friendly formations or creating a debug war cannot establish that proof. The achievement does not require a biological attack, reckless enhancement, or a counteroffensive.

Track wartime threat, exact formation identity, service relocation, withdrawal completion, and continuing care. Difficulty is hard. The icon direction is a medical transport moving with a worn formation standard.

### ACH18: An unfamiliar patient

Working purpose: complete adapted medicine for three distinct compatible nonhuman or alien recipient families, each admitted through its own provider and supplied with an actual course of treatment.

A cure must leave the recipient's political and military identity under its owner. A family that has no biological treatment surface does not count and cannot be made treatable merely for this achievement. Repeated variants of one family count once.

Track provider-family identity, compatibility, recipient, and accepted outcome. Difficulty is very hard and depends on campaign material. The icon direction is three different anatomical silhouettes around a common clinical instrument, without implying identical anatomy.

### ACH19: A dated future

Working purpose: diagnose and treat an owner-proven temporal biological condition while preserving the actual date and provenance of the information used. The treatment must be adapted by current work and manufactured for the actual recipients.

This is a hidden achievement until the relevant source makes the condition discoverable. A future archive does not grant every missing medical technology or create the source event. Contradictory records must remain distinguishable.

Track temporal-source receipt, present qualification, correct treatment, and final outcome. Difficulty is rare and very hard. The icon direction is a dated clinical record with two distinct chronological impressions.

### ACH20: A place to return to

Working purpose: restore one genuinely damaged receiving district and settle 5,000 distinct surviving displaced people there through the migration owner. Maintain positive food-safety proof, sufficient receiving capacity, and an operating disease-care service for 180 days after settlement.

Buildings must actually be repaired or constructed. A reconstruction flag without physical work is insufficient. New finite people may use the district, but cannot replace the 5,000 surviving displaced people required for this challenge. The two groups compete for the same real places.

Track repair, living-origin movement, unique survivor credit, food, reception, and medical service. Difficulty is hard. The icon direction is an occupied clinic beside repaired homes and a returning civilian group.

## Achievement visibility and reward handling

ACH19 is hidden until its source is known. Other achievements may be visible in the achievement browser, with clear conditions that do not leak unrevealed personal identities or hidden case details. Sovereignty achievements may state their broad political aim without disclosing every secret requirement of a particular confrontation.

These achievements grant the framework's normal achievement recognition, not additional factories, manpower, research, Authority, treatment stock, or negative Chaos. They must never turn clinical work into a repeatable reward economy. Portraits and national flags do not change merely because a challenge unlocked.

## Campaign validation by surface

### Baseline research and clinical outcomes

Run a healthy opening, a current ordinary outbreak, a zombie living-patient episode, and a Black Plague episode. The healthy opening demonstrates treatment without creating a national epidemic. The other openings target the actual disease and retain its source identity. All four mandates remain valid choices according to their real costs and conditions.

With every evolution disabled and Chaos held below 200 through a recorded test fixture, demonstrate that all 70 planned project roles have a valid prerequisite path where their source material exists. This fixture proves accessibility and must not award ACH02. Test missing source, missing permission, missing qualification, occupied site, unavailable stock, and interrupted care separately. A project can be unavailable because a source is absent, but cannot require a Wu evolution merely to exist.

Test cure success, incomplete success, outdated treatment, failed deployment, and recovery. Check that living population and recorded deaths remain distinct. Existing political actors must survive a medical result unless their own independent resolution actually changes them. Inspect the downstream disease and contamination owners, rather than only the Wu report.

### People, formations, and continuity

Exercise production, maturation, first settlement, specialist qualification, recruitment, deployment, retirement, death, capture, movement, annexation, and re-release. Use at least one mixed cohort whose civilian and military destinations differ. The same person must never appear in both first-settlement destinations. A later specialist appointment uses an already counted person.

Exercise every engineered lineage, finite infantry, enhanced infantry, support organism, and assault creature. Required equipment and medical service must remain distinct from formation manpower. Renaming, splitting, merging, template conversion, transfer, and disbandment must not erase control or care duties. A captured formation must not receive a fresh fully supported replacement simply because exact preservation is difficult.

Test Wu's death with no continuity, one unqualified stored body, one qualified successor, and several qualified copies with different archives. An older archive must not know a later discovery automatically. Personal death, retained knowledge, institutional activity, and another copy's appointment need separate evidence.

### All four evolutions

At 199, 200, 399, 400, 599, 600, 799, and 800 Chaos, compare eligibility before and after relevant institutional conditions change. The threshold alone does not cause immediate active-chain delivery. Evaluate declared MTTH with the verified installed-game adapter, distinguishing a computed interval from a measured firing probability.

For each milestone, test active-chain entry, evolved opening, pending disable, post-delivery disable, re-enable, host transfer, multiple qualified copies, and later baseline project completion. Check zero activation Chaos, a single valid chronology entry, correct actor, and no repeat appointment grant. N-D11 remains a shared-settings acceptance question. A fixture must not quietly select one policy and label that policy approved.

Part 13 adds strategic competition around objects that exist. Part 14 adds unauthorized deviation from actual work. Part 15 adds a political confrontation based on real support. None creates a free advanced project, a fifth evolution, or compulsory personal control.

### Foreign and domestic competition

Run the named IAI, AAI, SAI, SET, and CNT scenarios together with the inherited baseline and NAI scenarios. A foreign partner must be able to request medicine during a hostile-operation capacity limit. A forged lot must not become genuine through a diplomatic agreement. A protected person must not become an employee without consent and qualification.

When Wu and Kruger serve different countries, prove that only their respective owners can create their private operations. When they share a country, test all six domestic relationships and at least one joint application. A relationship change cannot transfer a settled national institution simply by moving the scientist.

Test competing bids, tied valid offers, withdrawn routes, partial deliveries, capped deposits, unavailable counterpart stock, third-party sanctions, and two concurrent raids with different fixed material standards. Old native reservations must remain unchanged. Native cancellation and refund behavior require installed-engine evidence and cannot be inferred from the completed-outcome resolver.

### Sovereignty and country play

Test supervised constitutional victory, limited autonomy, institutional division, voluntary exile, arrest, personal death, negotiated country formation, armed breakaway, government victory, institute victory, and a divided settlement. Include an affected foreign care contract and an unsettled living cohort in at least one test of each transfer family.

For each country origin, demonstrate actual territory, capital, food and supply, roster identity, starting forces or protection, and a viable early action. Review all major focus branches at normal zoom. Political, industrial, military, diplomatic, regional settlement, and scientific responsibility routes must be recognizable without a source file. A peaceful ordinary host retains its own meaningful national tree.

### Presentation and integration

Inspect the ordinary decision surface at minimum and crowded states. There must be one clear primary value, no hidden fifth payment, no duplicated action masquerading as another tab, and no expired objective occupying a current slot. Dynamic place and actor names must refer to the selected current object.

Review every required image at its actual consumer size. Review portraits, flags, counters, focus icons, project icons, achievement triplets, real animation frames, model materials, skeletal actions, and sourced audio against the relevant references. A successful file conversion is not visual acceptance. Super-events require aligned trigger, image, text, quote, unique musical recording, settings-aware playback, and documentation.

Save and reload at every irreversible transaction boundary. Test simultaneous requests in multiplayer fixtures and delayed callbacks after ownership changes. Repeated callbacks must preserve the first committed result. Terminal shared cleanup must close only the affected owner records, retain actual history, and leave necessary care to the permitted continuation policy.

## Completion boundary

The planning set ends here. Parts 1–20, the asset inventory, the six execution prompts, and the final coverage and ownership reports define the complete proposed event scope. There is no further mandatory specification-writing stage in this delivery.

Design approval remains distinct from coverage. D03, N-D11, new owner interfaces, numerical tuning, and presentation choices retain their recorded proposal status. Implementation must resolve those decisions, construct the mapped content, produce the actual assets, and run the named owner and MCP validations. A source-only planning check cannot substitute for those results.

The implementation improvement loop should now concentrate on demonstrated gaps, contradictory behavior, and weak route payoffs. Broad expansion for its own sake is not recommended. A new addendum should identify a specific missing player choice or integration failure, preserve this package's obligations, and receive an explicit disposition before another addendum is started.
