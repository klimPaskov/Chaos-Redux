Presentation rules:

- Full screen black overlay.
- Ordinary UI blocked during the sequence.
- One centered text beat visible at a time.
- Text beats fade in and out slowly.
- Final text must be written during implementation. This prompt gives only direction.
- No final title, quote, button text, lyric, slogan, or cultural reference is provided here.
- No super-event slot, quote, button, or super-event audio wiring.

Beat directions:

1. The old world has ended.
2. Mass death has already happened.
3. Survivors are sealed away or scattered.
4. Governments have lost control.
5. The sky is now a border.
6. Some survivors are altered.
7. The player will continue in a successor world.
8. The new map returns.

The final wording must follow Chaos Redux writing rules. Avoid em dashes, semicolons, staccato filler, generic apocalypse wording, direct tutorial phrasing, and implementation-history text.
