# Fallout matrix research notes

Canonical status: retained baseline research. The newer living-world research notes add climate, shelter, EMP, and fiction-boundary guidance.

This continuation pass used the existing package research notes and added a small grounding pass for matrix design.

## Food and trade collapse

The successor matrix treats food states, ports, and southern agricultural buffers as strategic centers because nuclear winter research links soot injection to crop and fisheries decline, while recent food-system work highlights trade disruption as a key survival factor. The design turns that into food compacts, lifeboat states, grain shields, port federations, and convoy politics.

Sources to consult during implementation research:

- Nature Food, Global food insecurity and famine from reduced crop, marine fishery and livestock production due to climate disruption from nuclear war, 2022.
- Earth System Dynamics, Food trade disruption after global catastrophes, 2025.
- Environmental Research Letters and related 2025 reporting on crop adaptation under nuclear winter conditions.

## Shelter and early survival

Bunker authority, quarantine republics, and shelter-life decisions are grounded in emergency guidance that prioritizes getting inside, staying in protective locations during the highest fallout danger window, and using decontamination procedures. The mod design expands this into shelter capacity, filter stores, sealed districts, surface permits, and early blackout processing.

Sources to consult during implementation research:

- CDC Radiation Emergencies, Get Inside guidance, 2024.
- Ready.gov Radiation Emergencies guidance, 2025.
- ICRP public protection advice for nuclear attack.

## EMP and infrastructure

Machine protocol, radio chains, listening posts, and relay states use EMP and communications disruption as infrastructure inspiration. EMP should affect communications, power, logistics, and command reliability. It must not cause direct population mutation.

Sources to consult during implementation research:

- REMM Electromagnetic Pulse following a nuclear detonation.
- DHS EMP mitigation best practices.
- FEMA nuclear detonation response guidance.

## Mutant country boundary

Mutant countries remain fictional Chaos Redux content. They should be tied to high-chaos cause memory, biological and chemical contamination, reactor failure, occult terminal events, and player-visible warnings. They must not be presented as ordinary radiation science.
