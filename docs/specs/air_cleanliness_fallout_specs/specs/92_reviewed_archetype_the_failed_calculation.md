# Reviewed archetype chain: The Failed Calculation

## Contract

The Failed Calculation is a dormant ordinary Fallout chain for the Manchurian Reactor Keeps Technate. It follows a closed Grid Vote district and covers the first technical failure that can damage a hospital relay, cooling circuit, or regional repair roster. The consequence is not a super-event, does not request Fallout, and does not register Fallout as a normal Event Log evolution.

The candidate producer selects the lowest eligible current-generation East Asian industrial or arms state owned and controlled by the Technate. The state must carry the closed Grid Vote memory, produced Air Winter values, surviving population, native infrastructure, Supply Access, a foreign neighboring state, bounded Disease pressure, and enough technical buildings to make a calculation failure meaningful. The country must retain the Manchurian Reactor Keeps memory, Technate government, durable survival resources, calculation legitimacy, inquiry pressure, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, and one affordable branch.

The candidate uses id `831`, transaction `710090`, route `7212`, event ids `831` through `837`, and Event Log history `9196`. The result resolves after `49` days. The technical audit callback resolves after `300` days. Branch modifiers last `240` days and callback modifiers last `390` days.

## Branches

| Branch | Cost | Identity | Delayed result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open the ledger to a public inquiry | Medicine 2, Scrap 1, Recognition 2 | Exposes the failed assumptions to repair councils. | Raises accountability and named evidence, but can lose time to damaged equipment. |
| Seal the calculation and protect the archive | Power 2, Scrap 2, Recognition 1 | Keeps the error inside the reactor archive. | Preserves continuity if the repair succeeds, but leaves neighboring councils dependent on private assurances. |
| Blame the operator and preserve the plan | Cohesion 2, Medicine 2, Recognition 1 | Removes the named operator while preserving the current model. | Can protect the roster through personal accountability, or leave the crew unwilling to sign a dangerous table. |
| Rebuild the model before another shift | Fuel 2, Power 2, Scrap 2 | Closes the damaged circuit and recalculates from surviving equipment. | Can establish a safer technical standard, or consume reserves while the line remains dark. |

## State and country ledgers

The chain initializes and clamps calculation legitimacy, inquiry pressure, operator trust, safety margin, recovery capacity, research confidence, and technical memory. It freezes those ledgers with the target state owner, controller, generation, shelter, Supply Access, Adaptation, Exposure, Disease, and the lowest authenticated foreign neighbor before payment. Result grading combines the frozen state values, Medicine, Recognition, calculation legitimacy, inquiry pressure relief, operator trust, safety margin, recovery capacity, research confidence, and Technate identity. Branch thresholds remain distinct and deterministic.

Successful and partial results change Air Winter, Supply Access, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, and the technical ledgers. Failure damages native infrastructure and requests bounded Deaths against the authenticated state. The callback changes the same state values, writes the durable technical memory, and can apply a second bounded Deaths request. Bilateral opinion records the selected policy and calculation failure against the frozen neighbor.

## AI and cleanup

The hidden AI event scores all four authored branches with the same affordability and grade paths as the human event. Delayed result, callback, cleanup, generation, owner, controller, neighbor, target, and transaction checks are revalidated before each step. Cleanup is idempotent. It releases the state and neighbor reservations, refunds an opening cost only when the chain never committed, clears transient ledgers and receipts, and records cancellation only when the authenticated row is stale.
