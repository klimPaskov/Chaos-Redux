# Reviewed Archetype Chain: Doctor's Coup

## Admission

Doctor's Coup is a dormant Quarantine State chain that follows a completed Permit Market memory. The producer selects the lowest current native owned state with current Air Winter identity, Shelter Capacity, Supply Access, Adaptation, bounded Exposure and Disease Pressure, surviving population, and a current owner and controller. The state must carry `fallout_event_698_memory_closed`. The country must remain a Quarantine State with public-health and grievance ledgers, enough Medicine, Cohesion, and Recognition, and one affordable branch.

The candidate is `705`, transaction `710072`, route `7176`, and survivor Event Log history `9178`. The event suffixes are `705` through `711`. The scheduler activation flags remain unset.

## Opening choice

The opening takes place at the Ash Ward Hospital after its medical board rejects the civil council's quarantine timetable. The four authored branches are:

1. Accept Medical Rule spends Food, Medicine, and Recognition to place the medical board above the civil council. A successful result changes the government archetype to Technate and records medical rule.
2. Build a Medical Coalition spends Medicine, Fuel, and Recognition to give doctors, ward delegates, and civil officers a shared emergency cabinet.
3. Arrest the Medical Leadership spends Food, Scrap, and Recognition to seize the clinic board and restore the civil chain of command.
4. Call a Public Vote spends Medicine, Fuel, and Power to hold a witnessed ward vote under winter lighting.

The hidden AI evaluates every affordable branch with distinct priorities and schedules the same branch-specific delayed result contract.

## Result and callback

The result arrives after 30 days. It grades frozen state Supply Access, Shelter Capacity, Adaptation, Exposure, Disease Pressure, country Medicine, Recognition, medical authority, civil legitimacy, medical fatigue, coup risk, and Quarantine government alignment. Success, partial, and failure outcomes have distinct Air Winter, Supply Access, public-health, grievance, medical-authority, civil-legitimacy, fatigue, coup-risk, cause-memory, stability, and dynamic-modifier effects. Result failure routes a bounded state population request through Deaths and damages one repairable infrastructure level.

The callback arrives after 240 days. It grades the durable hospital settlement against current Supply Access, Reclamation, Disease Pressure, medical authority, civil legitimacy, medical fatigue, coup risk, cause memory, and Recognition. Success, partial, and failure update the same ledgers with different outcomes. Callback failure uses a second bounded Deaths request. Callback cleanup releases the delayed receipt only after the result cleanup is released.

## Memory and ownership

The chain writes survivor-country Event Log history `9178` for the selected branch, result, callback, and cancellation. It does not register the Fallout consequence as an ordinary event, evolution, or ordinary super-event. It creates no tag, state, successor, population transfer, or recurring scheduler. Every opening, result, callback, and cleanup lane revalidates generation, owner, controller, target state, and delayed transaction receipts.
