# Reviewed archetype contract: The Quarantine Ultimatum

The Quarantine Ultimatum is reviewed candidate `929` for the Congo Green Basin mutant polity. It is a dormant ordinary Fallout chain, not the Fallout consequence and not a super-event. The chain opens after The Cohort's First Ballot closes on a current lower-river or hill-road gate with a foreign neighbor.

## Regional setting and selection

The producer is Fallout-owned and requires the Congo Green Basin country memory and mutant-polity government archetype. It selects the lowest owner-controlled state with current Air Winter receipts, durable survival identity and resource rows, living population, shelter, Supply Access, adaptation, disease, exposure, a foreign neighbor, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, and an affordable branch. The selected state represents a lower river gate or hill-road inspection site. The candidate stores one idempotent target, the owner, controller, neighboring country, Fallout generation, phase, cooldown family, and the source pressure before any opening event is issued.

The foreign demand is concrete. A neighboring government has placed red quarantine cloth across the bridge after ash rain and will allow medicine through only under a named inspection, a shared fever register, and a return seal. The mutant polity remains fictional. The chain does not treat ordinary radiation biology as an established explanation.

## Branches and delayed mechanics

1. Accept the named controls. Food 2, Medicine 1, and Recognition 1 fund foreign inspection access at the river clinic. Disease and exposure fall while local cohesion pays for the loss of exclusive control.
2. Negotiate a joint inspection. Scrap 1, Power 1, and Recognition 2 put Basin healers beside the foreign team. The joint ledger costs medicine and time while improving recognition and inspection legitimacy.
3. Defy the red line. Fuel 2, Recognition 1, and Cohesion 2 keep the bridge open under Basin guards. Movement and local authority improve only if the guards can sustain the gate, otherwise disease, exposure, and fuel pressure grow.
4. Seek allies along the hill road. Food 1, Medicine 2, Fuel 1, and Recognition 2 send a courier for neutral clinic witnesses. The branch can create a three-party relief corridor, but the lower gate remains exposed while the courier travels.

The 56-day result grades each branch from frozen Air Winter values, Supply Access, shelter, adaptation, disease, exposure, Medicine, Recognition, border legitimacy, inspection pressure, movement trust, health confidence, road grievance, and guard fatigue. Success, partial, and failure outcomes change those values through bounded scripted effects. Failure requests a bounded Deaths-system outcome and can damage one building without deleting the country, moving population between states, or activating the Fallout consequence.

The 330-day wet-season review uses the same immutable receipts and branch memory. It changes Air Winter, Supply Access, shelter, disease, exposure, adaptation, reclamation, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, and the gate ledgers. The chain writes a cause memory, bilateral opinion, Event Log payload, and a result modifier. Authenticated cleanup releases the gate and witness reservations and closes the memory exactly once. Hidden AI uses the same branch grading and cleanup lanes without a player-facing event.
