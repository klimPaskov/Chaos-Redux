# Reviewed Archetype: The Adaptation Rite

The Adaptation Rite is a fictional Congo Green Basin mutant-polity chain about whether a survival practice born during cold rain, ash, dead vegetation, and frozen channels becomes civic memory. Its altered society is fictional and its customs do not prove ordinary radiation biology. Fallout remains a consequence rather than an ordinary event, evolution, scenario log entry, or super-event.

The dormant runtime package uses human and hidden-AI opening events `chaosx.fallout.1030` and `chaosx.fallout.1031`, delayed result events `chaosx.fallout.1032` and `chaosx.fallout.1033`, callback events `chaosx.fallout.1034` and `chaosx.fallout.1035`, and cleanup event `chaosx.fallout.1036`. Its scheduler candidate id is `908`, transaction key `710101`, route `7234`, and Event Log history id `9207`.

## Candidate boundary

The row is eligible from campaign day `730` through day `12000` after the Old Doctor's Trial state memory closes. The country must retain the Congo Green Basin memory and the `mutant_polity` government archetype, with current registry and survival-resource rows, Medicine at least `14`, Cohesion at least `32`, Recognition at least `18`, rite legitimacy at least `50`, tradition pressure at least `5`, and at least one affordable branch. An open, committed, pending, or closed Adaptation Rite chain blocks a duplicate request.

The scheduler selects the lowest owned state with living population, current state identity and survival-resource rows, current Supply Access, Shelter at least `22`, Supply Access at least `16`, Adaptation at least `18`, Disease Pressure from `20` through `89`, Exposure from `10` through `89`, and Air Winter phase one through six. The state must have a current foreign neighboring state. Controlled evacuation flags block selection. The closed predecessor memory is `fallout_event_901_memory_closed`.

The opening candidate freezes the owner, controller, transition generation, selected ward, Air Winter values, Supply Access, rite ledgers, and the lowest eligible foreign witness state. Each result, callback, and cleanup lane revalidates those receipts. A changed owner, controller, state, witness, generation, or durable row cancels the chain and authenticated cleanup releases reservations.

## Branches

| Branch | Cost | Civic direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hold a civic rite | Food `2`, Medicine `2`, Recognition `2` | River and hill wards write the practice into a public shelter charter. |
| Entrust it to the river shrine | Scrap `1`, Power `1`, Recognition `2` | Shrine keepers hold the practice as a river custom that can cross ward roads. |
| Certify it through the field school | Fuel `1`, Recognition `2`, Cohesion `2` | Teachers issue a shared certificate after checking shelter, food, and work records. |
| Reject the rite and keep practical rules | Food `1`, Medicine `1`, Fuel `1`, Recognition `1` | The Basin rejects ceremonial authority while preserving dated household rules. |

The opening choice is paid once through a country flag and is refunded only on a failed queue transaction. Branch triggers expose only choices whose exact survival resources and Cohesion requirement are affordable.

## Grading and delayed consequences

The deterministic result grade weights frozen Supply Access, Shelter, Adaptation, Disease Pressure relief, Exposure relief, Medicine, Recognition, rite legitimacy, tradition-pressure relief, adaptation trust, and outsider-fear relief. A small mutant-polity bonus is explicit. Each branch has separate success and partial thresholds, with failure as the remaining outcome.

Result effects change state disease, shelter, exposure, adaptation, reclamation, supply, and survival resources. They also change Cohesion, Recognition, rite legitimacy, tradition pressure, adaptation trust, witness confidence, outsider fear, elder fatigue, and rite cause memory. Success, partial, and failure write separate branch memories. Failure uses the established bounded Deaths request contract with a minimum remaining population. Bilateral opinion modifiers distinguish civic, religious, scientific, rejected, and failed practice outcomes.

The result transaction resolves after exactly `63` days. It schedules a callback after exactly `360` days. The callback grades rite legitimacy, tradition pressure, adaptation trust, witness confidence, outsider fear, elder fatigue, rite cause memory, Cohesion, Recognition, and current state Air Winter values. Callback outcomes alter Air Winter, Supply Access, survival resources, civic stability, and the same country ledgers. Callback failure uses a separate bounded Deaths request. The state memory closes only after both delayed transactions and their cleanup receipts settle.

Human and hidden-AI lanes share branch legality, costs, deterministic grading, result timing, callback timing, bilateral memory, and cleanup. AI branch priorities are authored for civic, religious, scientific, and rejected practice according to the dedicated constants. No lane creates a country, changes government archetype, requests Fallout, transfers population between states, or adds a recurring scheduler.

## Characters and fictional boundaries

The elder ward keeper, field-school teacher, shrine keeper, and foreign witness are authored event roles and durable country and state memory flags. No real person or grounded historical leader is invented. The chain deliberately describes fictional altered society through institutions and practices instead of ordinary radiation science.
