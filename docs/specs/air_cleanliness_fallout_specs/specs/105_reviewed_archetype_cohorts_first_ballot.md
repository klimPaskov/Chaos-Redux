# Reviewed archetype contract: The Cohort's First Ballot

The Cohort's First Ballot is reviewed candidate `922` for the Congo Green Basin mutant polity. It is a dormant ordinary Fallout chain, not the Fallout consequence and not a super-event. The chain opens only after the Children of Two Settlements memory closes on a current ward with a foreign witness.

## Selection and receipts

The candidate producer remains Fallout-owned and uses the mutant-polity and Congo Green Basin memories. It selects the lowest owned ward that has current Air Winter receipts, durable survival identity and resource rows, living population, shelter, Supply Access, adaptation, disease, exposure, a foreign neighbor, country Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, ballot legitimacy, service pressure, and one affordable branch. The candidate row stores the owner, ward, foreign witness, Fallout generation, phase, cooldown family, source pressure, mechanic pressure, state value, and route before any opening event is issued.

Every human and hidden AI lane rechecks the owner, ward, witness, generation, ordinary-event eligibility, and dispatch receipt. Result and callback lanes remain dormant until the Fallout scheduler owns their request. The chain has no recurring scheduler and does not create a country or move population between states.

## Branches and delayed mechanics

The opening choices are:

1. Seat a youth council. Food, Medicine, and Recognition fund a witnessed ballot under the ferry canopy.
2. Reserve guardian seats. Scrap, Power, and Recognition preserve ward records while giving the cohort a partial voice.
3. Constitute a mixed ward assembly. Fuel, Recognition, and Cohesion fund a table for cohort delegates, teachers, guardians, and the foreign witness.
4. Require a service year before voting. Food, Medicine, Fuel, and Recognition fund ferry, clinic, and shelter service before the first ballot.

The 77-day result grades each branch from current Air Winter, Supply Access, shelter, disease, exposure, adaptation, Medicine, Recognition, ballot legitimacy, service pressure, council trust, civic confidence, ward pressure, and council fatigue. Success, partial, and failure lanes change those values through bounded scripted effects. Failure requests a bounded Deaths-system outcome and can damage one building without deleting the country or changing the Fallout consequence.

The 450-day election review uses the same immutable receipts. It changes Air Winter, Supply Access, shelter, disease, exposure, adaptation, reclamation, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, and the civic ledgers. It writes a delayed cause memory, bilateral opinion, Event Log payload, and a result modifier. Authenticated cleanup releases the ward and witness reservations and closes the memory exactly once.

The altered society remains fictional and the text does not treat ordinary radiation biology as established fact.
