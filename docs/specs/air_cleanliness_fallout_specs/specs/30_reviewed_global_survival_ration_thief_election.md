# The Ration Thief Election

## Accepted identity

This is reviewed global-survival candidate 394. It belongs to the successor-memory cooldown family and the global survival and society primary family. The row remains dormant until Fallout activation authority is proven.

## Opening

The selected native state must have a current Fallout state row, a produced Air Winter snapshot, surviving population, exposure between 20 and 82, and an accepted scarred, ash-zone, remote-refuge, or dead-city grade. The owning country must have a current registry row, food, medicine, recognition, and cohesion reserves, plus ration trust, government legitimacy, and faction-pressure ledgers. The lowest qualifying native state id is selected so a replay cannot move the accusation between states.

## Branches

1. Public election lets the wards choose a keeper. It spends food, recognition, and cohesion, and is available to democratic or neutral governments.
2. Tribunal brings clerks, guards, and families before a repeatable hearing. It spends food, medicine, and recognition, and is available to communist or neutral governments.
3. Purge strips the office and gives the keys to armed stewards. It spends food, recognition, and cohesion, and is available to fascist or communist governments.
4. Private settlement binds the storehouse to millers and official signatories. It spends food, medicine, and recognition, and remains available when the country can pay the price.

## Delayed results and callback

The result arrives after 28 days and grades food, medicine, cohesion, recognition, and ration trust from frozen ledgers. Success, partial success, and failure alter food, medicine, recognition, cohesion, stability, war support, reclamation, supply access, exposure, ration trust, government legitimacy, faction pressure, building integrity, and population through the Deaths system. Failure damages infrastructure before an industrial building and never writes population loss directly.

The callback arrives after 240 days. It tests whether the selected precedent can survive a second ration count, updates the three government ledgers, leaves a branch-specific state memory and dynamic modifier, records a second Event Log entry, and releases both cleanup receipts only after the result and callback transactions are consumed.

## AI, government, and cleanup

AI selects a branch from government identity, cohesion, recognition, faction pressure, and food thresholds. Human and AI receipts carry separate event tokens and visible-budget modes. Every registry variable is generation-bound and owner-bound. Cleanup clears frozen inputs, tickets, registry pointers, branch state, and temporary flags without erasing durable state memories or Event Log history.
