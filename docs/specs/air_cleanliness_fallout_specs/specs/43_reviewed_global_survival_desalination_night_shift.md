# Reviewed global-survival chain: The Desalination Night Shift

## Scope

The Desalination Night Shift is a dormant Fallout-owned regional chain for a Middle Eastern coastal state that still has a native naval-base port proxy, a produced Air Winter water-security snapshot, and enough surviving population for one night of work. It is an ordinary country event chain inside the Fallout event family. It is not the Fallout blackout and it is not an ordinary super-event.

The chain is defined by events `chaosx.fallout.485` through `.491` under `add_namespace = chaosx.fallout`. Candidate `485` uses transaction `710043` and route `7143`. Event Log history `9148` carries twelve branch outcomes and three callback outcomes.

## Entry proof

The producer chooses the lowest valid owned state id. The state must be controlled by the requesting country and current in the Fallout identity and durable resource ledgers. It must be on the Middle East continent, be coastal, contain a native naval base above zero, have a town, large-town, city, or large-city category, and retain surviving state population. Its produced Air Winter snapshot must match the current transition generation and carry adaptation, exposure, reclamation, supply, and water-security values in the reviewed band. The row refuses already committed state memory.

The country row requires campaign day `300` through `2599`, current durable survival resources, Water at least `12`, Power at least `15`, Medicine at least `5`, Cohesion at least `22`, a positive water-security pressure record, and no committed chain or closed memory flag. The plant is represented by the existing native naval base and industrial state surface. No invented building type is introduced.

## Choice surface

The opening exposes four government-aware responses with separate Water, Power, Medicine, and Cohesion costs.

1. Run the plant at full strain fills public tanks quickly and is available to democratic and neutral governments.
2. Call the desert technicians creates a written neighboring technical credit and leaves the clinic outside foreign command.
3. Reserve power for the clinic protects patients while the plant works slowly and is available to democratic, neutral, and communist governments.
4. Lock the gates and ration the shore protects the intake crew through a guarded shoreline line and is available to fascist and communist governments.

The human and hidden-AI lanes use the same frozen ledgers, deterministic branch chooser, delayed transaction, outcome thresholds, and cleanup receipts. AI selection checks the branch resources and the government-independent water, adaptation, medicine, power, and cohesion pressures before falling through to an affordable policy.

## Numerical and gameplay contract

The chain freezes Water, Power, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, Adaptation, Reclamation, operation memory, and the state target before the result. Viability weighs Water, Power, Adaptation, Reclamation, operation memory, Cohesion, and Recognition. Each branch has separate success and partial thresholds.

The result arrives after `42` days. It updates Water, Power, Medicine, Cohesion, stability, war support, operation memory, water reserve, technical credit, Air Winter adaptation, exposure, reclamation, and supply. A successful or partial result adds a state modifier that records the selected water policy. Failure damages infrastructure, then the native naval base, then an industrial complex if earlier buildings are unavailable. Failure also requests population loss through the Deaths system.

The result schedules a second-shift callback after `300` days. The callback applies another branch-independent water, power, medicine, cohesion, stability, war-support, Air Winter, supply, reserve, and technical-credit update. It can request another Deaths-backed loss, adds a callback memory, and closes only after the authenticated cleanup receipt is consumed.

## Text and asset contract

Player-facing text names the Middle Eastern coast, intake pipes, ash in the filters, the clinic lamps, the port road, the harbor quarter, ceramic membranes, tankers, and the second-shift ledger. It does not describe implementation history or tuning mechanics. The dedicated report image is `GFX_report_event_fallout_desalination_night_shift` and is documented under `docs/assets/air_cleanliness_fallout/fallout_desalination_night_shift/`.

## Deliberate boundary

This chain remains dormant. Scheduler activation, host authority, save recovery, multiplayer input blocking, full-screen blackout delivery, and runtime Event Log display remain unproven engine surfaces. The bilateral technical credit is a country ledger rather than a native relationship object. The chain does not create a character, a successor tag, a new state, or native plant topology. The exact engine-native all-valid-province thermonuclear sweep for the manual scenario remains separately blocked.
