
# Regional Event Bible

Working labels in this file are not final localisation.

## Regional rule

Regional events must use concrete geography, infrastructure, climate, food systems, migration patterns, political memory, and transport. The broad regions organize content production. They do not erase local cultures or national histories.

Country-memory overlays and final implementation research must add local names, institutions, leaders, languages, and historical connections.

The complete event matrix is in `matrices/fallout_regional_event_matrix.md`.

## North America

Core themes:

- old federal and provincial legitimacy
- state guards and local government
- highways
- aqueducts
- Great Lakes transport
- prairie grain
- hydro power
- oil and water conflict
- island and northern refuges
- indigenous and local continuity institutions
- remote strategic sites
- long-distance communications

Regional events should make transport infrastructure politically important. A road, lock, aqueduct, dam, grain elevator, or cable can become a government institution.

Historical and indigenous content requires dedicated regional research. The final text must not reduce living communities to folklore or generic survival knowledge.

## Europe

Core themes:

- dense urban ruins
- metro and tunnel populations
- old borders
- river transport
- ports and islands
- religious relief
- Alpine and mountain shelters
- lowland pumping
- reactor and industrial belts
- old parliaments and capitals
- cross-border families

European restoration politics should create real disputes. Old borders, capitals, parliaments, crowns, republics, and resistance memories compete with new survivor institutions.

## Former Soviet Space and Central Asia

Core themes:

- very long rail corridors
- command archives
- mine towns
- reactor keeps
- arctic stations
- steppe wells
- regional republic identity
- depot and convoy politics
- military districts
- old insurgent memories
- mixed religious relief
- new interregional trade routes

The rail network should function as geography, government, food system, military system, and diplomatic network.

Country-specific historical material requires research. Old movements must not be used as empty aesthetic labels.

## East Asia

High population density should create strong shelter, food, public health, and citizenship pressure.

## South Asia and Middle East

## Regional overlay rules

A regional event should adapt to the active country.

Examples:

- A continuity government treats the Great Lakes lock as interstate law.
- A warlord treats it as a toll fortress.
- A food compact treats it as a grain route.
- A technate treats it as a power and engineering system.
- A maritime remnant treats it as a naval corridor.
- A mutant polity may treat it as a contested recognition border.

The regional event supplies the place and material problem. The archetype supplies the governing logic.

## Local research gate

Before final implementation of a regional chain, confirm:

- historical institutions
- names and languages
- real infrastructure
- relevant political and military traditions
- religious and cultural context
- state and province fit
- historical flags and symbols
- whether a real person is involved
- whether the event could misrepresent a living community

Uncertain material must be marked or replaced.

## Regional pool acceptance

- Every broad region has at least twelve anchor families.
- Every selected successor receives several region-matched incidents.
- Climate presentation differs by biome.
- Regional events change mechanics.
- Trade and diplomacy follow real map connections.
- Regional content has AI outcomes.
- Historical content is researched before final text.
- No region is represented only through generic famine and ruins.
