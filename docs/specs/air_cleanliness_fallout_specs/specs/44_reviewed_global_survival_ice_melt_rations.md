# Reviewed global-survival chain: Ice Melt Rations

## Scope

Ice Melt Rations is a dormant Fallout-owned first-winter chain for a boreal or highland state with a produced Air Winter frozen-water surface, a damaged urban category, surviving people, and a current water-security receipt. It is an ordinary country event chain inside the Fallout event family. It is not the Fallout blackout and it is not an ordinary super-event.

The chain is defined by events `chaosx.fallout.492` through `.498` under `add_namespace = chaosx.fallout`. Candidate `492` uses transaction `710044` and route `7144`. Event Log history `9149` carries twelve branch outcomes and three callback outcomes.

## Entry proof

The producer initializes a durable station filter reserve when the country first enters the candidate review. It chooses the lowest valid owned state id. The state must be controlled by the requesting country and current in the Fallout identity and durable resource ledgers. It must pass the existing regional Air Winter frozen-water eligibility trigger, retain a town, large-town, city, or large-city category, contain surviving state population, and have current adaptation, exposure, reclamation, supply, and water-security values. The produced Air Winter snapshot must match the current transition generation. The row refuses already committed state memory.

The country row requires campaign day `150` through `2199`, current durable survival resources, Water at least `10`, Fuel at least `12`, Medicine at least `5`, Filters at least `8`, a station filter reserve at least `8`, Cohesion at least `22`, a positive water-security pressure record, and no committed chain or closed memory flag. Fuel and Filters remain separate ledgers. National Filters pay the public stock cost while the station filter reserve pays the physical ice-melt operation cost.

## Choice surface

The opening exposes four concrete responses with separate Water, Fuel, Medicine, Filters, station-filter, and Cohesion costs.

1. Boil and filter the ice runs a controlled boiler through the frost and is available to democratic, neutral, and communist governments.
2. Use the first untreated meltwater keeps the wards supplied before the filter room is ready and leaves a public risk record.
3. Send a clean-ice expedition sends a crew to a shaded ravine and is available to democratic, neutral, and communist governments.
4. Ration fuel for a guarded boil protects the boiler crew and preserves a smaller reserve under fascist or communist authority.

The human and hidden-AI lanes use the same frozen ledgers, deterministic branch chooser, delayed transaction, outcome thresholds, and cleanup receipts. AI selection checks the separate public and station filter costs before falling through to an affordable policy.

## Numerical and gameplay contract

The chain freezes Water, Fuel, Filters, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, Adaptation, Reclamation, operation memory, and the state target before the result. Viability weighs Water, Fuel, Filters, Adaptation, Reclamation, operation memory, Cohesion, and Recognition. Each branch has separate success and partial thresholds.

The result arrives after `42` days. It updates Water, Fuel, Filters, Medicine, Cohesion, stability, war support, operation memory, water reserve, station filter reserve, expedition credit, Air Winter adaptation, exposure, reclamation, and supply. A successful or partial result adds a state modifier that records the selected frozen-water policy. Failure damages infrastructure, then a naval base if present, then an industrial complex if earlier buildings are unavailable. Failure also requests population loss through the Deaths system.

The result schedules an ice-ledger callback after `300` days. The callback applies another water, fuel, filter, medicine, cohesion, stability, war-support, Air Winter, supply, reserve, station-filter, and expedition-credit update. It can request another Deaths-backed loss, adds a callback memory, and closes only after the authenticated cleanup receipt is consumed.

## Deliberate boundary

This chain remains dormant. Scheduler activation, host authority, save recovery, multiplayer input blocking, full-screen blackout delivery, and runtime Event Log display remain unproven engine surfaces. The station filter reserve is a Fallout country ledger rather than a native equipment store. The chain does not create a character, a successor tag, a new state, or native frozen-water topology. The exact engine-native all-valid-province thermonuclear sweep for the manual scenario remains separately blocked.

## Future extension

A later reviewed tranche can connect the station ledger to a named water chemist, a migration route from the clean-ice ravine, and a regional disease follow-up. Those additions should retain the current generation receipts and should not replace the separate Fuel and Filters costs.
