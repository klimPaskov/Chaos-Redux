# The Counterfeit Antibiotics

## Accepted identity

This is the reviewed global-survival candidate 387. It belongs to the medicine cooldown family and the global survival and society primary family. It is a dormant scheduler row until Fallout activation authority is proven.

## Opening

The selected native state must have a current Fallout state row, a produced Air Winter snapshot, surviving population, exposure between 24 and 86, and an accepted scarred, ash-zone, remote-refuge, or dead-city grade. The owning country must carry a current registry row, medicine below 34, food and recognition reserves for at least one branch, and cohesion of at least 18.

## Branches

1. Public trial puts the first batch on a table in front of the fever ward. It spends medicine, food, and cohesion, and rewards transparent testing when the frozen ledger is strong.
2. Quiet purchase keeps a river-road supplier unnamed. It spends medicine and recognition, and can preserve treatment while weakening public trust.
3. Guild regulation gives apothecaries a charter. It spends medicine, scrap, and recognition, and can create a durable testing institution.
4. Military seizure assigns the shipment to a garrison quartermaster. It spends medicine, scrap, and cohesion, and can move stock quickly at the cost of clinic autonomy.

## Delayed results and callback

The result arrives after 42 days and grades medicine, food, cohesion, recognition, and the current antibiotic trust ledger. Success, partial success, and failure alter medicine, food, recognition, cohesion, stability, war support, state reclamation, supply access, exposure, adulteration, supplier influence, building integrity, and population through the Deaths system. Failure damages infrastructure before an industrial building and never writes population loss directly.

The callback arrives after 330 days. It reviews the named supplier, updates the three medicine-market ledgers, leaves a branch-specific state memory and dynamic modifier, records a second Event Log entry, and releases the result and callback cleanup receipts only after both have been consumed.

## AI and cleanup

AI chooses a branch through authored recognition, medicine, scrap, and cohesion thresholds. Human and AI receipts carry separate event tokens and visible-budget modes. Every registry variable is generation-bound and owner-bound. Cleanup clears frozen inputs, tickets, registry pointers, branch state, and temporary flags without erasing the durable state memories or Event Log history.
