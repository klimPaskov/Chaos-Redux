# Reviewed Archetype: The Repair Charter

## Identity

The Repair Charter is a dormant Fallout Technate chain that follows a closed First Regional Standard district in the Manchurian Reactor Keeps. It turns a shared regional table into a practical charter for repair crews, parts seals, route duty, and training.

The chain uses `chaosx.fallout.859` through `chaosx.fallout.865`, candidate id `859`, transaction key `710094`, route `7220`, and Event Log history `9200`. It is an ordinary Fallout-owned consequence chain and never registers the Fallout consequence itself as an ordinary event, evolution, or scenario log entry.

## Candidate boundary

The candidate admits a current-generation East Asian country with Manchurian Reactor Keeps memory, a Technate or Continuity Government archetype, the closed First Regional Standard state memory, a foreign neighbor, surviving population, native infrastructure, current Air Winter values, durable Supply Access, and the required Food, Medicine, Power, Scrap, Cohesion, Recognition, open crew access, and licensed repair pressure ledgers. The candidate chooses the lowest eligible owned state so the result is deterministic and reviewable.

The candidate is dormant until the Fallout scheduler activation audit opens the row. It writes one state target and does not create a country, release a tag, move population between states, or start a recurring scheduler.

## Opening branches

The human and hidden AI lanes choose among four distinct branch contracts.

- Issue an open repair charter. Spend Food 2, Scrap 1, and Recognition 2. The branch expands crew access and route reach while increasing the risk of uninspected service.
- Commission licensed repair houses. Spend Power 2, Scrap 2, and Recognition 1. The branch protects quality through licenses while increasing bargaining pressure on settlements.
- Bind crews to a council charter. Spend Fuel 1, Medicine 2, and Recognition 2. The branch shares training and duty rosters while making urgent changes depend on council trust.
- Reserve the charter for perimeter corps. Spend Medicine 1, Fuel 2, and Recognition 2. The branch protects generators, clinics, and radio corridors while civilian roads lose priority.

Every option has a trigger tooltip, a visible cost, and an authored effect tooltip. Hidden AI uses the same affordability checks and selects the highest deterministic priority among affordable branches.

## Delayed results and audit

The selected result arrives after exactly 77 days. It freezes the country, target state, owner, controller, generation, neighbor, Air Winter values, Supply Access, and repair ledgers before scheduling.

The result grades Shelter, Supply Access, Adaptation, Disease Pressure, Exposure, Medicine, Recognition, open crew access, licensed repair pressure, standards council trust, crew training capacity, parts traceability, and route safety. Success, partial, and failure paths have separate Air Winter deltas, resource deltas, dynamic modifiers, bilateral opinion, durable branch memory, and bounded infrastructure or Deaths effects.

The compatibility audit arrives after exactly 450 days. Its score uses the frozen branch ledgers, current cohesion, Recognition, charter memory, Supply Access, Reclamation, and Disease Pressure. The audit writes a settled, contested, or failed memory and releases both state and neighboring receipts through authenticated cleanup.

## Deaths and cleanup

Result failure requests a bounded population loss through the Deaths system with Fallout aftermath as the reason and a minimum remaining population. Audit failure uses a smaller bounded request. Neither path deletes a country, transfers population, or bypasses the Deaths accounting contract.

Scheduling failures refund branch costs and clear reserved state receipts. Result, audit, and cleanup handlers revalidate generation, ownership, controller, target type, branch, and issued ticket. Cleanup is idempotent and releases the state and neighbor reservations only after the delayed tickets are authenticated.
