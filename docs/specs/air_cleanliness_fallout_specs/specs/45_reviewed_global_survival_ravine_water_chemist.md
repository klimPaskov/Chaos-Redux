# Reviewed global-survival chain: The Ravine Water Chemist

## Scope

The Ravine Water Chemist is a dormant Fallout-owned follow-up to a successful or partial Ice Melt Rations memory. It names Dr. Mara Vey, binds a clinic method to a current Air Winter state, and creates one deterministic migration route from another owned state. It remains an ordinary Fallout country-event chain. It is not the Fallout blackout and it is not a super-event.

The chain uses events `chaosx.fallout.499` through `.505` under `add_namespace = chaosx.fallout`. Candidate `499` uses transaction `710045`, route `7145`, and Event Log history `9150`.

## Entry proof

The candidate producer initializes four durable country ledgers when absent, selects the lowest current owned state with the Ice Melt success or partial memory, produced Air Winter identity, supported urban category, surviving population, adaptation at least `25`, exposure from `8` through `79`, reclamation below `95`, disease pressure at least `10`, refugee pressure at least `4`, water security at least the Ice Melt minimum, and supply access below `94` when supply is present. A second lowest-id owned state with surviving population is frozen as the migration origin. The origin remains a native state id rather than an invented province or tag.

The country gate requires campaign day `450` through `2799`, Food at least `12`, Water at least `12`, Medicine at least `8`, Filters at least `10`, Recognition at least `12`, Cohesion at least `28`, a positive ravine pressure sum, current durable country rows, and no committed or closed chain memory. The producer writes the origin id beside the candidate until the chain closes, so the result and callback use the same source state.

## Choice surface

The opening has four government-aware responses with separate costs.

1. Give Dr. Vey the water bench establishes a named clinic service under democratic or neutral authority.
2. Escort the clean-ice families moves a recorded cohort from the origin state through the Deaths-backed population-loss contract and adds manpower to the target state.
3. Quarantine the ravine ward closes the fever line under communist or fascist authority and creates a durable disease protocol memory.
4. Share the filter method with districts sends the charcoal and cloth method through regional pumps and preserves a shared-method memory.

Human and hidden-AI openings use the same candidate row, frozen ledgers, delayed transaction, deterministic outcome, and cleanup receipts. The AI prefers a funded chemist, then a high-disease quarantine, then a high-refugee escort, and finally a funded district method.

## Numerical and gameplay contract

The chain freezes Food, Water, Medicine, Filters, Recognition, Cohesion, Adaptation, Reclamation, Disease Pressure, Refugee Pressure, chemist service, disease memory, and migration ledgers. Viability weighs Food, Water, Medicine, Filters, Cohesion, Recognition, Adaptation, Disease Pressure, and Refugee Pressure. Every branch has separate success and partial thresholds.

The result arrives after `49` days. Success and partial outcomes update survival resources, cohesion, stability, war support, Air Winter disease pressure, refugee pressure, exposure, adaptation, reclamation, supply, chemist service, and disease memory. Failure damages infrastructure or an industrial complex and requests a Deaths-system loss of `1.2%` of the target state's surviving population. A selected escort also applies a deterministic origin-state migration loss through the same population-loss effect with the log-deaths field disabled, then records arrivals and departures on both states.

The callback arrives after `210` days. It applies a second resource, Air Winter, disease, migration, service, and memory update. Callback failure damages a building and requests a Deaths-system loss of `0.8%` of the target state's surviving population. The callback closes the named chain memory only after the authenticated result and callback cleanup receipts are consumed.

Player-facing text names Dr. Mara Vey, the ash-dusted ravine, charcoal layers, cloth filters, clinic wards, pump houses, clean-ice families, district governors, school shelters, and the frozen bridge. It does not describe scheduler internals or tuning history. The dedicated report image is `GFX_report_event_fallout_ravine_water_chemist`.

## Deliberate boundary

This chain remains dormant because scheduler release receipts, reviewed producers, and activation setters are absent. The migration route records native state population and does not create a new tag. The full-screen blackout and exact engine-native all-valid-province thermonuclear sweep belong to the completed consequence core.

## Future extension

A later reviewed tranche can give Dr. Vey a named character object and connect the shared method to a bilateral water compact. That extension must preserve the current-generation receipts, source-state ledger, and separate Food, Water, Medicine, Filters, Recognition, and Cohesion costs.
