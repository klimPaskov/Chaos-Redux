# Reviewed Archetype: Power as Citizenship

Power as Citizenship is a dormant ordinary Fallout consequence chain for a Technate or Continuity Government successor in the Manchurian Reactor Keeps. It follows a closed Repair Charter memory and turns accumulated repair practice into a mature answer to who may request, schedule, and overrule the eastern current.

The chain uses `chaosx.fallout.866` through `chaosx.fallout.872`, candidate id `866`, transaction key `710095`, route `7222`, and Event Log history `9201`. It is owned by the Fallout scheduler and does not register Fallout itself as an ordinary event, evolution, or scenario log entry.

## Candidate boundary

The candidate is eligible from campaign day `900` through day `9600` in East Asia. It requires the Manchurian Reactor Keeps country memory, a Technate or Continuity Government archetype, a closed Repair Charter state memory, a controlled living state, native infrastructure, one foreign neighbor, durable Air Winter ledgers, and affordable resources. It chooses the lowest eligible state id so the review is deterministic.

The candidate freezes the country, state, owner, controller, neighbor, transition generation, Air Winter values, Supply Access, and its power-citizenship ledgers. A changed owner, controller, neighbor, generation, state identity, or Fallout phase cancels the chain and refunds its paid branch cost through authenticated cleanup.

## Choices and consequences

The public service republic branch spends Food 2, Medicine 1, and Recognition 2. It opens a published power register, increases public access, and makes shortage disclosure part of citizenship.

The engineer state branch spends Power 2, Scrap 2, and Recognition 1. It places certified engineers over substations and production quotas, trading local veto power for a stable central schedule.

The grid empire branch spends Fuel 1, Medicine 2, and Recognition 2. It binds eastern settlements into a protected grid compact with priced current, repair duty, and a seat at review.

The machine transition branch spends Medicine 1, Fuel 2, and Recognition 2. It permits a planning machine to schedule current while a human board can overrule unsafe orders and must publish exceptions. A successful machine transition moves the government archetype to `machine_protocol`. A successful grid empire moves it to `continuity_government`. The public service and engineer-state branches retain `technate`.

The result resolves after `77` days. Each branch applies deterministic Air Winter, Supply Access, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, building, and bounded Deaths effects. It writes public service access, engineer authority, grid integration, civic education, machine trust, citizenship legitimacy, identity memory, government, and bilateral memories. A later citizenship audit resolves after `450` days with success, partial, or failure grading and a second bounded Deaths path.

## AI and cleanup

The hidden AI opening, result, and callback use the same affordability, branch scoring, frozen receipts, outcome grading, memory writes, and cleanup as the human lane. The Event Log stores the selected branch, result, callback, and cancellation payloads under history `9201`.

The chain does not request Fallout, create countries, transfer population across states, add a recurring scheduler, or alter the blackout GUI. It remains dormant and outside the release-floor count until the reviewed Fallout scheduler opens it. Exact native all-valid-province Fallout delivery, host authority, save recovery, multiplayer behavior, and live consumer presentation remain outside this source-package proof.
