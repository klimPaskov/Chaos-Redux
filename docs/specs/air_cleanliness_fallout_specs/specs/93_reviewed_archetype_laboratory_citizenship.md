# Reviewed archetype chain: Laboratory Citizenship

## Contract

Laboratory Citizenship is a dormant ordinary Fallout chain for the Manchurian Reactor Keeps Technate. It follows a closed Failed Calculation district and asks who may enter the laboratories, clinics, schools, and civic registers that keep the East Asian winter grid alive. The consequence is not a super-event, does not request Fallout, and does not register Fallout as a normal Event Log evolution.

The candidate producer selects the lowest eligible current-generation East Asian industrial or arms state owned and controlled by the Technate. The state must carry the closed Failed Calculation memory, produced Air Winter values, surviving population, native infrastructure, Supply Access, a foreign neighboring state, bounded Disease pressure, and enough technical buildings to make a citizenship settlement meaningful. The country must retain the Manchurian Reactor Keeps memory, Technate government, durable survival resources, citizenship legitimacy, class pressure, Medicine, Power, Scrap, Cohesion, Recognition, and one affordable branch.

The candidate uses id `838`, transaction `710091`, route `7214`, event ids `838` through `844`, and Event Log history `9197`. The result resolves after `56` days. The citizenship review callback resolves after `360` days. Branch modifiers last `270` days and callback modifiers last `420` days.

## Branches

| Branch | Cost | Identity | Delayed result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open citizenship to every repair household | Medicine 2, Scrap 1, Recognition 2 | Gives households with repair, clinic, or shelter records full civic standing. | Can rebuild eastern trust and education, or expose incompatible household lists. |
| Tie citizenship to service tiers | Power 2, Scrap 2, Recognition 1 | Attaches civic rights to visible reactor service and a route upward. | Can keep the grid staffed, or turn citizenship into a disputed queue. |
| Build an apprentice citizenship path | Cohesion 2, Medicine 2, Recognition 1 | Trains younger crews into civic membership beside the reactor school. | Can create a durable succession, or leave trainees outside the next intake after a shelter failure. |
| Keep civic rights under the senior engineers | Fuel 2, Power 2, Scrap 2 | Preserves a strict technical register under the senior order. | Can protect immediate output, or hide missing repair hands and lose outside recognition. |

## State and country ledgers

The chain initializes and clamps citizenship legitimacy, class pressure, service trust, education capacity, apprentice capacity, civic confidence, and citizenship memory. It freezes those ledgers with the target state owner, controller, generation, shelter, Supply Access, Adaptation, Exposure, Disease, and the lowest authenticated foreign neighbor before payment. Result grading combines the frozen state values, Medicine, Recognition, citizenship legitimacy, class-pressure relief, service trust, apprentice capacity relief, civic confidence, and Technate identity. Branch thresholds remain distinct and deterministic.

Successful and partial results change Air Winter, Supply Access, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, and the citizenship ledgers. Failure damages native infrastructure and requests bounded Deaths against the authenticated state. The callback changes the same state values, writes the durable citizenship memory, and can apply a second bounded Deaths request. Bilateral opinion records the selected policy or citizenship failure against the frozen neighbor.

## AI and cleanup

The hidden AI event scores all four authored branches with the same affordability and grade paths as the human event. Delayed result, callback, cleanup, generation, owner, controller, neighbor, target, and transaction checks are revalidated before each step. Cleanup is idempotent. It releases the state and neighbor reservations, refunds an opening cost only when the chain never committed, clears transient ledgers and receipts, and records cancellation only when the authenticated row is stale.
