
# Ash Wolves

## Event identity

Ash Wolves is a reviewed Fallout ordinary-event chain in `chaosx.fallout`. It belongs to the cause-memory and altered-fiction family and is a dormant scheduler candidate until the activation audit opens it.

The candidate requires a produced Air Winter snapshot, a state with the explicit `altered_biosphere` subtype or its state receipt, a populated state, usable reclamation, and a current Fallout owner. The trigger refuses an ordinary rural state that lacks the altered-biosphere receipt.

## Opening conflict

A wolf-like pack follows an ash-covered rail cut into a settlement. Rangers, water crews, and field biologists disagree about whether the corridor should be treated as a threat, a boundary, or a protected altered habitat. Player text states that the animals are fictional Fallout content and does not present radiation as a real source of new species.

## Policy branches

- Hunt the pack. A ranger cordon spends food and medicine, reduces pack pressure, and returns manpower when it works.
- Fence the corridor. A guarded boundary protects the clinic and road, but consumes building materials and slows local movement.
- Study the tracks. Field biologists trade medicine and recognition for research, route knowledge, and a possible altered-ecology archive.
- Leave the corridor. The council marks the route and protects the habitat, accepting a continuing safety burden and a different settlement identity.

## Delayed result and callback

The result arrives after 60 days. It grades a deterministic viability value from pack pressure, safety, research, food, recognition, and cohesion, with branch-specific success and partial thresholds. Failure applies a Deaths-system population loss request, damages a state building, increases exposure, and records a corridor breach.

The 480-day callback tests whether the selected corridor policy survived a season of frost and cold rain. It updates the same ledgers, state adaptation, reclamation, supply access, exposure, food, medicine, recognition, cohesion, stability, war support, and manpower. Callback failure uses the Deaths system and a building loss rather than a harmless narrative result.

## AI and memory

AI governments choose a study lane when research capacity is available, a hunt lane when a security government faces low safety, and a protected corridor when a neutral government faces high altered pressure. The chain uses a country-owned registry, generation and owner checks, delayed-result tickets, callback tickets, history flags, cleanup, and deterministic lowest-state selection. Cleanup clears ledgers and the state registry receipt only after both delayed transactions are released.

## Fiction boundary

The pack, its altered territory, and the field archive are fictional high-chaos content. The chain does not create a mutant country, infer a biological inheritance mechanism, or reuse the zombie system. Its effects describe food, safety, research, movement, supply, and memory consequences rather than real radiation science.
