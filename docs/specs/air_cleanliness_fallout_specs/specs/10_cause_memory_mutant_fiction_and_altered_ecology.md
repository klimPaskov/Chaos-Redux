
# Cause Memory, Mutant Fiction, and Altered Ecology Events

Working labels in this file are not final localisation.

## Shared aftermath, different memory

Fallout uses one world-rewrite framework. The source catastrophe changes:

- state grading weights
- opening orientation
- visual direction
- health pressure
- successor selection weights
- government legitimacy
- regional blame
- military relics
- treaty remnants
- mutant-fiction eligibility
- late world-order questions

The detailed cause matrix is in `matrices/fallout_cause_memory_event_matrix.md`.

## Cause memories

### Thermonuclear exchange

Emphasis:

- firestorms
- blast ruins
- EMP
- military bunkers
- command responsibility
- launch sites
- weapons remnants
- disarmament and new arms race

### Gradual air collapse

Emphasis:

- years of visible decline
- evacuation ledgers
- failed or successful relief
- treaty remnants
- filter culture
- industrial and military blame
- state abandonment
- continuity and public inquiry

### Final Silence aftermath

Emphasis:

- missing time and broken signals
- survivor interpretation
- religious and skeptical conflict
- impossible-looking places
- fictional altered communities
- memory of the source institution
- ambiguous phenomena

Final Silence memory does not move Fallout into another event namespace. It remains an input to `chaosx.fallout.*` content.

### Chemical saturation

Emphasis:

- mask inheritance
- filters
- poisoned water
- canister sites
- breathing class
- release-command responsibility
- long cleanup

### Biological follow-through

Emphasis:

- quarantine founders
- medical records
- emptied districts
- carrier accusations
- lab archives
- health charters
- abuse and public trust

### Mixed catastrophe

Emphasis:

- uncertain public cause
- layered exclusion zones
- overlapping protective cultures
- blame inherited by successors
- adaptation congresses
- fear of a second collapse

## Fiction boundary

Mutant polities and altered ecology are deliberate high-chaos fiction.

The package must not state or imply that real radiation predictably creates the people, societies, abilities, organisms, or inheritance patterns described in the fictional content.

Final documentation should distinguish:

- real radiation injury and cancer risk
- real contamination and decontamination problems
- real climate and food disruption
- fictional altered people
- fictional altered animals and plants
- metaphysical Final Silence content
- machine and supernatural routes

## Mutant societies

Mutant countries should have varied social models.

Possible models:

- plural civic republic
- medical protectorate
- bunker-altered federation
- mixed human-altered settlement
- isolated habitat polity
- religious altered refuge
- military altered state
- scientific adaptation council
- nomadic altered community
- high-chaos transformation movement

Do not write every mutant country as an aggressive monster state.

## Mutant event subjects

Required subjects:

- self-chosen identity
- medical consent
- citizenship
- family
- mixed settlements
- education
- fertility policy
- adoption
- old experimentation
- public office
- military service
- external recognition
- quarantine
- land claims
- internal factions
- late world-order status

The full matrix is in `matrices/fallout_mutant_fiction_event_matrix.md`.

## Fictional ecology

Altered ecology events should have regional and cause gates.

Possible content:

- unusual fungi and moss
- changed predator ranges
- changed insects
- altered fish behavior
- strange protected crops
- contaminated wetlands
- high-chaos forests
- Final Silence signal phenomena

The player should be able to:

- destroy
- study
- regulate
- protect
- cultivate
- trade
- quarantine
- use as military asset at severe cost

Every choice changes mechanics.

## Avoided patterns

Do not use:

- generic hordes as the only mutant threat
- real-world ethnic or disability coding
- claims that visible difference proves disease
- automatic evil based on biology
- medical abuse presented as neutral progress
- all mutant events as body horror
- identical mutant flags and portraits
- identical recognition choices
- unexplained powers with no route or cost
- fictional science written as fact

## Internal mutant politics

Required faction directions:

- integration
- autonomy
- medical oversight
- civic equality
- isolation
- transformation
- religious interpretation
- militant defense
- scientific adaptation
- return to old national identity

These factions should affect leaders, focus branches, decisions, and diplomacy.

## Human-mutant diplomacy

Diplomacy must include:

- first envoy
- medical cooperation
- health inspection
- mixed settlement
- border movement
- trade
- military service
- compact entry
- public office
- rights charter
- exclusion bloc
- war and settlement

Countries remember prior treatment.

## Machine and mutant intersection

Rare content can explore:

- machine medical records
- automated exclusion
- altered technicians
- mixed machine-human-altered settlement
- protocol categories that do not fit people
- shared adaptation science
- machine exploitation
- machine protection

These remain route-specific.

## Asset direction

Mutant assets must be generated as fictional art.

Required care:

- varied appearances and institutions
- regional clothing and material culture
- clear person or council identity
- no generic green-skin convention
- no reused zombie visual language
- no zombie event images
- no zombie sound
- no zombie flags
- no zombie portrait treatment

## AI

AI mutant policy depends on:

- government archetype
- medicine
- cause memory
- prior contact
- domestic altered population
- quarantine pressure
- ideology
- military threat
- compact rules

Mutant polities also have their own AI routes and should not behave as one faction.

## Acceptance

- Every cause memory has several event chains.
- Cause memory changes opening and late events.
- Mutant fiction is clearly labeled.
- Mutant societies have political variety.
- Human-mutant relationships have bilateral memory.
- Altered ecology has regional gates and real effects.
- Final Silence content remains ambiguous.
- No mutant asset or event reuses zombie content.
- AI supports integration, exclusion, trade, isolation, and conflict.
