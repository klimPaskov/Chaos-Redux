# Reviewed Archetype: The Name We Choose

The Name We Choose is a dormant ordinary Fallout consequence chain for the fictional Congo Green Basin mutant polity. During the first ash week, a river council must establish the public identity that will appear on water ledgers, school registers, border papers, and service seals. The chain treats altered society as fictional high-chaos content and does not present mutation as ordinary radiation science or as a medical diagnosis.

The chain uses `chaosx.fallout.873` through `chaosx.fallout.879`, candidate id `873`, transaction key `710096`, route `7224`, and Event Log history `9202`. It belongs to the Fallout-owned scheduler and does not register Fallout itself as a normal event, evolution, scenario log entry, or super-event.

## Candidate boundary

The candidate is eligible from campaign day `84` through day `7200` for the Congo Green Basin country memory and mutant-polity government archetype. It requires a controlled living state with current survival identity and resource rows, valid Air Winter and Supply Access values, surviving population, Shelter, Adaptation, Exposure, bounded Disease Pressure, a foreign neighbor, civic legitimacy, outside pressure, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, and one affordable branch. The lowest eligible state id is selected so the row is deterministic.

The candidate initializes and clamps its identity ledgers, records one ordinary request through the shared scheduler coordinator, and freezes the country, state, owner, controller, neighboring state, transition generation, Air Winter values, Supply Access, and identity ledgers. Any changed receipt, owner, controller, neighbor, state identity, generation, or Fallout phase cancels the delayed lane and returns paid resources through authenticated cleanup.

## Choices and consequences

The local identity branch spends Food 3, Medicine 1, and Recognition 3. It names the polity from its own river settlements, increases identity coherence, and gives schools and water crews one public term.

The old nationality branch spends Scrap 2, Power 3, and Recognition 3. It preserves a pre-Fallout nationality in records while negotiating inherited debts, conscription claims, and foreign recognition.

The biological label rejected branch spends Fuel 4 and Recognition 2, then accepts a Cohesion cost. It rejects an outside body-based label and defines citizenship through conduct, service, and consent. The branch never claims a real biological mechanism.

The religious identity branch spends Food 2, Medicine 3, Fuel 2, and Recognition 2. It ties the public name to river service, burial duty, and shared stores while allowing the chain to produce a contested boundary if the rite becomes exclusive.

The result resolves after `42` days. Each branch applies deterministic Air Winter, Supply Access, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, Stability, War Support, bounded Deaths, bilateral opinion, and identity-memory effects. The delayed generation review resolves after `300` days and grades success, partial, or failure from the frozen receipts and identity ledgers.

## AI, Event Log, and cleanup

The hidden AI opening, result, and callback use the same affordability, branch priorities, result grading, frozen receipts, and cleanup as the human lane. Event Log payloads record all four choices, twelve branch outcomes, three generation-review outcomes, and cancellation under history `9202`.

The chain does not request Fallout, create a country, transfer population between states, add a recurring scheduler, alter the blackout GUI, or consume a zombie asset. It remains dormant and outside release-floor credit until the reviewed Fallout scheduler opens it. Exact native all-valid-province Fallout delivery, host authority, save recovery, multiplayer behavior, and live consumer presentation remain outside this source-package proof.
