# Spec 56: The Nile Pump Congress

Status: accepted implementation tranche for the dormant Fallout scheduler.

This ordinary sub-Saharan African regional chain is a seven-block water-governance story about a surviving pumping station, competing authorities, and the first river agreement after the Fallout rewrite.

The chain is not a super-event, decision category, mission, focus route, bilateral partner, country creation, recurring on-action, scripted GUI, achievement, formable, or map rewrite.

## Admission and target selection

The country must have a current Fallout identity, durable country resources, current generation, the sub-Saharan Africa region, a campaign day from `730` through `5999`, sufficient food, clean water, Cohesion, and Recognition, and at least one affordable branch.

The target must be a current-generation native owned and controlled rural state with a produced Air Winter snapshot, surviving population, Food reserve, Supply Access, adaptation, reclamation, infrastructure, exposure, and disease values inside the named thresholds.

The candidate producer scans owned states and stores the lowest eligible native state id.

Only that state is frozen and reserved. The chain does not invent a partner state or perform a multi-state write.

## Four authored branches

The opening offers Public Pump Board, Upstream Command, River Cooperatives, and Emergency Hold.

Public Pump Board spends Food, Scrap, Recognition, and Clean Water to improve water access and civic trust.

Upstream Command spends Fuel, Power, and Command Power to protect the flow while placing a branch-specific cost on cohesion and military control.

River Cooperatives spend Medicine, Food, Recognition, and Clean Water to reduce disease pressure and strengthen reclamation.

Emergency Hold spends Power, Medicine, and Clean Water to keep a narrow flow alive while risking infrastructure and local deaths when the state cannot sustain the reserve.

Human branches disclose their costs, local risk, and 42-day result timing.

Unaffordable branches are unavailable to the player and receive no hidden AI selection.

## Frozen inputs and deterministic result

The opening freezes the country resource row, Cohesion, War Support, Recognition, generation, owner, controller, target state identity, state Food, Supply Access, adaptation, reclamation, exposure, disease, population, and infrastructure values consumed by the grade.

The branch cost is paid once after the exact ordinary receipt, current target, and branch affordability checks pass. It is refunded when the delayed-result transaction is rejected before commitment.

The result is scheduled exactly 42 days after a valid choice.

The result snapshots the target and country ledger before calculating a deterministic viability grade.

Each branch resolves to success, partial success, or failure through named threshold constants without a random list or MTTH grade.

Result effects update country Food, Clean Water, Medicine, Scrap, Recognition, Cohesion, Stability, War Support, Air Winter reclamation, exposure, disease, state Supply Access, and local infrastructure where failure requires damage.

Failures route bounded civilian loss through `apply_exact_state_civilian_population_loss` with the Fallout aftermath cause and the minimum remaining population contract.

Branch ledgers track public trust, upstream discipline, cooperative trust, or emergency discipline.

Public or cooperative success records a river compact, partial results record a contested river agreement, and failure records a basin war memory.

## Callback and cleanup

The callback is scheduled exactly 240 days after result settlement.

The callback outcome is calculated once when a human callback is delivered and is locked until the player closes the callback. Hidden AI callbacks calculate once immediately before resolution. Both paths use the frozen baseline and current target-state Supply Access, Food, reclamation, and exposure values.

Callback effects apply a second bounded country and state survival pass, branch-specific ledgers, Air Winter disease refresh, and Fallout Deaths for a callback failure.

Result, callback, and cleanup reauthenticate the country generation, candidate identity, target metadata, branch, transaction stage, and cleanup ticket.

If the target loses ownership or control, the result or callback follows the metadata-only invalidation path, records the cancelled payload, cancels the matching delayed row, and proceeds to authenticated cleanup without applying stored state effects. If the opening receipt becomes stale before a choice is accepted, the exact ordinary receipt is cancelled and the same cancellation memory is recorded.

Cleanup releases the result and callback rows exactly once, closes the state reservation, preserves durable river memory, and clears transient chain variables.
