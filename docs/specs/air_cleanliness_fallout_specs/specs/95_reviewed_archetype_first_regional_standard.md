# The First Regional Standard

The First Regional Standard is a dormant ordinary Fallout chain for the Manchurian Reactor Keeps Technate. It follows a closed Machine Suggests district and asks whether the surviving East Asian workshops will share one language for voltage, parts, measures, and radio intervals. Fallout remains a consequence rather than an ordinary event or Event Log evolution.

The candidate producer selects the lowest eligible current-generation East Asian industrial or arms state owned and controlled by the Technate. The state must carry the closed Machine Suggests memory, produced Air Winter values, surviving population, native infrastructure, Supply Access, a foreign neighboring state, bounded Disease pressure, and enough technical buildings to make a regional standard meaningful. The country must retain the Manchurian Reactor Keeps memory, Technate government, durable survival resources, open access, proprietary pressure, Medicine, Power, Scrap, Cohesion, Recognition, and one affordable branch.

## Branch contract

- Publish one open standard spends Food `2`, Scrap `1`, and Recognition `2`. It gives every neighboring workshop the same tables and raises access while exposing the Keeps to copied errors.
- Protect a proprietary standard spends Power `2`, Scrap `2`, and Recognition `1`. It creates a licensed repair market and increases bargaining pressure on settlements outside the register.
- Bind it to a regional compact spends Fuel `1`, Power `2`, and Recognition `2`. It shares tables through a council and improves compact trust while accepting slower revisions.
- Keep the standard under military secrecy spends Medicine `1`, Fuel `2`, and Recognition `2`. It protects perimeter generators and radios while reducing civilian compatibility.

The result resolves after exactly `70` days. The compatibility review resolves after exactly `420` days. Every branch freezes the country, state, owner, controller, transition generation, neighbor, Air Winter values, Supply Access, survival resources, and standard ledgers before payment. The delayed lanes recheck all of those receipts before applying effects.

## Deterministic effects

The grade combines current shelter, Supply Access, Adaptation, Disease relief, Exposure relief, Medicine, Recognition, open access, proprietary pressure relief, compact trust, radio compatibility, and the Technate bonus. The result and callback never use a random list or MTTH. Result and callback effects change Air Winter, Supply Access, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, trade confidence, measure compatibility, radio compatibility, and the named standard memories. Failure requests bounded population loss through the existing Deaths system and damages native infrastructure once.

Successful compact settlement may move the government archetype to Continuity Government. All other successful branches preserve the Technate. Bilateral opinion records whether the neighboring settlement received an open table, a license, a compact seat, military secrecy, or a failed standard. The country writes one branch memory and one durable standard memory. Cleanup releases the state and neighbor reservations, clears delayed receipts, closes the state memory, and records cancellation only for a stale authenticated row.
