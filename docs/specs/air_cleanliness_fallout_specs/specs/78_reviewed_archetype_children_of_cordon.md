# Reviewed Quarantine archetype chain: Children of the Cordon

Children of the Cordon is a dormant Fallout-owned generation-change chain for a country that has closed a Border Inspection Crisis in at least one owned state. It owns candidate `733`, transaction `710076`, route `7184`, history `9182`, and event ids `733` through `739` under `chaosx.fallout`.

## Opening contract

The candidate producer requires a current country registry row, a durable survival identity and resource row, a completed `fallout_event_726_memory_closed` state memory, at least one generation-change count, the reviewed campaign-day window, minimum Cohesion, Food, Shelter, and one affordable branch. The candidate is country-scoped and stores no invented state, province, character, or foreign target.

The opening freezes the current survival Cohesion, Food, Shelter, Recognition, generation count, youth trust, and movement-law ledgers. It authenticates the current country and scheduler generation before reserving the delayed result. Both human and hidden AI lanes use the same country receipt, branch token, result transaction, callback transaction, and cleanup token.

## Branches and delayed results

The player can rewrite the movement law, open service travel, continue the strict cordon, or give wards their own crossing boards. Each branch has a distinct Food, Power, Scrap, Medicine, or Recognition cost and a distinct AI priority. The deterministic grade weights Cohesion, Food, Shelter, generation count, and movement law. Branch thresholds then test youth trust, movement law, or Cohesion so the generation ledger changes what each choice can achieve.

The result applies branch-specific Food, Medicine, Power, Recognition, Cohesion, Stability, War Support, future-leader pool, youth trust, and movement-law deltas. It writes the selected government route and branch memory, applies a dedicated dynamic modifier, and routes failure through the exact Fallout aftermath Deaths contract across every owned state. The result schedules a delayed callback instead of closing the memory immediately.

## Generation callback and cleanup

The callback applies its own branch outcome deltas, future-leader pool, youth trust, and movement law. A failed callback uses the callback Deaths contract. The callback adds a maintained or failed dynamic modifier, sets the durable generation memory, records a second Event Log payload, and releases the result and callback receipts through the existing authenticated cleanup helper. Cleanup clears only transient registry, delayed-result, and scheduling flags while preserving the durable youth trust, movement-law, future-leader, government-route, branch-memory, and Event Log state.

## Content and asset ownership

The chain has authored quarantine-generation text for reform, service travel, strict continuation, and local autonomy. Event Log history `9182` routes through the Fallout-owned detail localizer and covers all branch outcomes plus the callback. It uses the dedicated `GFX_report_event_fallout_children_of_cordon` sprite and the dedicated report DDS under `gfx/event_pictures/fallout_children_of_cordon/`.

The chain is not Fallout itself, an ordinary super-event, or a second scheduler. It remains dormant until the Fallout-owned candidate coordinator selects it.
