# Ashline Firebreak

## Acceptance role

Ashline Firebreak is a dormant Fallout regional and biome chain for an owned state with an open wildfire, ashfall, volcanic eruption, or massive-eruption aftermath card. It is an ordinary Fallout chain, not a super-event. The natural-disaster system remains the sole owner of the low capped Air Contamination contribution. The chain interprets settled ash through Air Winter, Deaths, buildings, supply, disease, military operations, and state memory.

## Admission and target proof

The candidate producer selects the lowest owned state that still satisfies the native aftermath card, owner, controller, Fallout state identity, durable resource row, Air Winter pre-transition snapshot, state population, and supply access guards. The state must have dry or volcanic hazard history, exposure at or above the reviewed entry band, reclamation below the recovery band, and no prior Ashline state memory. The candidate stores state subject type, state id, candidate `554`, transaction `710052`, and route `7152`. No province, neighbor, character, country tag, or invented target is created.

The candidate pressure combines the existing Air Winter exposure with the native natural-disaster severity. This is a scheduler score input only. It does not write another contamination source and does not bypass the capped natural-disaster reservoir.

## Three choices and deterministic delayed lanes

1. Cut a firebreak through the ash grass spends Scrap and Fuel. It favours adaptation, reclamation, food movement, and road access.
2. Seal the roofs before the next fall spends Shelter Capacity and Scrap. It favours indoor clinics, shelter, and disease control.
3. Put the military on the ash road spends Power and Fuel. It favours guarded operations, Command Power, and route supply while consuming civilian stores.

The hidden AI lane chooses the cordon when supply and Power support it, the roof seal when adaptation and shelter support it, and the firebreak otherwise. The same delayed receipt and outcome calculation is used for human and AI countries.

The result freezes Air Winter adaptation, reclamation, exposure, state Supply Access, disease pressure, natural-disaster family, and natural-disaster severity. Viability weights adaptation, reclamation, supply, and inverse exposure. Each branch has authored success, partial, and failure thresholds and distinct effects. Failure damages infrastructure or an industrial fallback, degrades the state category through the existing Air Winter category route, applies a Deaths-system population loss with an explicit minimum remaining population, and writes persistent branch memory. The callback arrives after 210 days, repairs or worsens infrastructure and supply, refreshes Air Winter disease modifiers, and applies a second Deaths-system loss on failure.

Result and callback modifiers are state-scoped. The result and callback histories use Event Log history `9157` with the state as a secondary actor. Cleanup releases both delayed receipts, clears frozen ledgers, and preserves branch and state memory for later regional, successor, and recovery chains.

## Engine-sensitive boundary

Static review proves the target is selected through the current Fallout country candidate loop and revalidated by owner-bound state triggers. It proves that the existing `air_contamination_register_natural_disaster_aftermath_source` helper remains the only aftermath source writer and that all population loss requests use the Deaths contract. No HOI4 runtime was launched. Live scheduler issuance, state-scoped delayed delivery, Event Log secondary actor rendering, AI choice order, and host presentation remain unobserved.
