
# Successor Country-Memory Event System

Working labels in this file are not final localisation.

## Purpose

The successor matrix contains 99 candidate countries. Shared archetype and regional content cannot make those candidates feel unique by itself. Every selected successor needs event memory that grows from its surviving states, starting institutions, resource profile, military package, leadership, old-country memory, and late ambition.

The full matrix is in `matrices/fallout_successor_event_overlay_matrix.md`.

The manually bounded Ash-week pilot registry is in `matrices/fallout_orientation_pilot_memory_candidate_registry.md`. It maps exact country-memory ids to opening overlays and candidate institutions or fictional characters. It does not select or materialize a live successor.

## Required arc package per selected successor

### Opening chain

Three to five event blocks.

The chain must establish:

- what physical asset made the country possible
- what resource can sustain it
- what resource is failing first
- who currently speaks for the government
- what military force exists
- what population or citizenship dispute begins immediately
- what nearby ruin, corridor, port, river, mine, shelter, or field will dominate early play

The final opening event sets a country-specific route memory.

### Domestic identity chain

Four to six event blocks.

The chain must turn the country's starting ideas and leader direction into a real conflict.

Possible subjects:

- council versus commander
- refugees versus early occupants
- farmers versus ration board
- engineers versus civil authority
- guilds versus state
- regional autonomy versus old national claim
- religious relief versus secular law
- medical authority versus public representation
- fleet law versus port citizens
- mobile families versus settlement faction
- human liaison versus machine protocol
- altered community versus external medical control

At least one outcome changes a leader, advisor, commander, idea lifecycle, government route, or decision family.

### External relationship chain

Three to five event blocks.

The chain selects a relevant neighbor or contact based on:

- food
- water
- power
- ports
- rails
- fuel
- shelter
- salvage
- medical capacity
- radio range
- old claims
- shared population
- cause memory

The relationship can become:

- recognition
- trade
- aid debt
- client dependence
- compact membership
- border commission
- shared authority
- ideological rivalry
- tribute
- smuggling link
- refugee corridor
- war preparation

Both sides store memory.

### Late memory chain

Four to seven event blocks.

The chain opens in Years 4 to 8 and asks what the country has become.

It should connect to:

- the unique focus overlay
- the old capital or rejected old capital
- country name and flag direction
- the leadership institution
- a regional ambition
- a first-generation grievance
- a second-generation claim
- a major recovery project
- a formable, federation, league, or restoration route where justified

The capstone changes late focus access or recurring events.

## Minimum unique event count

Each selected successor requires at least:

- two fully unique multi-event chains
- two tailored overlay chains that may reuse structure but not text, actors, states, or effects
- three local routine incidents with country-specific actors or institutions
- one Year 5+ callback
- one failure callback
- one AI-specific hidden resolution path for each important choice

A full campaign does not spawn every candidate. The complete mod still needs design and implementation coverage for all candidates that can be selected by the rewrite.

## Country memory inputs

Required inputs:

- source tag and original country
- surviving or dead old capital
- main state classes
- regional climate
- cause memory
- starting ideas
- resource strengths and weaknesses
- military package
- leader direction
- archetype
- selected focus overlay
- treaty and evacuation memory
- old government conduct before collapse
- refugee policy
- altered-population policy
- first war and first treaty
- first recovered city or power node

## Country-specific art and characters

Each selected successor should receive:

- one orientation report image where practical
- one domestic identity report image
- one late memory image
- at least one unique leader or institutional portrait direction
- country-specific flag and cosmetic identity coverage
- event icon motifs tied to its main assets

A reused generic archetype image cannot carry the whole country package.

## Shared structure limits

Shared event structure is allowed for production efficiency.

The final content fails if:

- country names are the only difference
- every successor receives the same ration choice
- every continuity state has the same cabinet
- every warlord has the same succession
- every technate has the same reactor
- every food state has the same seed vault
- every mutant state has the same medical dispute
- regional geography does not affect effects
- late ambitions are copied between unrelated countries

## AI

Country-specific AI behavior in the accepted successor matrix must influence event choices.

Examples:

- a lake authority values locks and water trade
- an oil fortress tolerates food dependency and seeks water clients
- a tunnel compact avoids open plains
- an island lifeboat state calculates shelter and food before admission
- a rail polity prioritizes line continuity
- a reactor keep accepts risk only with engineer capacity
- a grain state avoids wars during harvest
- a warlord uses tribute before annexation if cohesion is weak

## Acceptance

- All 99 candidates have event overlay obligations.
- Every spawned country gets unique opening memory.
- Every spawned country gets a late identity arc.
- At least one country-specific chain changes leadership or government.
- At least one country-specific chain changes a neighbor relationship.
- At least one country-specific chain changes a focus or decision route.
- Small countries receive internal depth without being forced into expansion.
- Shared content remains adapted and manually reviewed.
