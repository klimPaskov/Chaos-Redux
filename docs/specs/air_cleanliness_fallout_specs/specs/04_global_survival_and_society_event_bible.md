
# Global Survival and Society Event Bible

Working labels in this file are not final localisation.

## Purpose

The global pool provides the common human problems that every region can face. Regional, archetype, country-memory, cause-memory, and character overlays must alter these events. A bunker state and a maritime remnant can both face medicine scarcity, but the actors, costs, risks, and political consequences should differ.

The full global matrix is in `matrices/fallout_global_event_family_matrix.md`.

## Chain construction rule

An anchor family should normally become three to six event blocks:

1. discovery or first pressure
2. immediate choice
3. mission or delayed consequence
4. success, partial success, or failure
5. institutional memory
6. late callback where useful

A one-popup chain is acceptable for a compact routine incident. It is not acceptable for a major institution such as ration law, grid restoration, seed custody, refugee citizenship, dead-city salvage, or military professionalization.

## Food and water chains

Food chains must model reserves, production, distribution, labor, seed, livestock, trade, and political legitimacy.

Core principles:

- stored food delays crisis but cannot solve several bad years
- food-exporting regions gain power and refugee pressure
- high-latitude crop failure is often more severe
- coastal fisheries help but do not replace collapsed agriculture
- crop conversion, animal-feed use, greenhouse work, fungi, algae, and protected agriculture require capacity and time
- food choices alter class, regional, refugee, and military relations

Water chains must model source, pumping power, filtration, contamination, access law, and upstream control.

A country with high water but weak power should play differently from a fuel state with damaged aqueducts.

## Shelter and medicine chains

Shelter is a political institution.

Events should address:

- admission law
- air and water rationing
- crowded levels
- private and public space
- surface work
- child care
- disease
- funerals
- citizenship
- labor
- conflict between early occupants and later refugees
- conflict between surface survivors and bunker survivors

Medicine events should use actual medicine capacity, support equipment, doctors, clinics, power, transport, and quarantine. Triage choices create long memory.

## Power and infrastructure chains

Infrastructure events should create named institutions and strategic objectives.

Examples:

- a rail crew can become a respected guild, military labor corps, coerced workforce, or dead martyr group
- a dam can become a shared authority, fortified state, flood threat, or diplomatic asset
- a grain elevator can become a granary, military depot, market, or refugee center
- a power grid can be centralized, cooperative, military, or foreign-backed

Restoration broadcasts should be rare and tied to visible map changes.

## Salvage and technical chains

Salvage must remain dangerous after the player acquires basic equipment.

Risks include:

- unstable structures
- radiation and toxic material
- unexploded weapons
- rival claimants
- corrupt permits
- false maps
- old security systems
- machine protocols
- trapped survivors
- historical evidence
- strategic technology

Salvage law should change who benefits.

Technical events should not treat every old machine as a miracle. Many systems are incomplete, unsafe, dependent on missing parts, or politically contested.

## Law, market, and culture chains

The scenario needs ordinary institutions.

Required subjects:

- market law
- ration theft
- courts
- prisons
- inheritance
- marriage
- adoption
- education
- work duty
- language
- public memory
- sport
- music and broadcast
- mourning
- calendar
- maps and borders
- property in abandoned land
- veteran status
- citizenship

Cultural events must have effects through cohesion, ideology, character creation, education, diplomacy, military spirit, or old-world memory.

## Security and military chains

Military events should explain where units come from and why they remain loyal.

Required pressures:

- militia autonomy
- ammunition
- commanders
- guard duty
- weapons accidents
- patrol disappearance
- prison recruitment
- checkpoint tribute
- deserters
- veterans
- officer training
- civil rescue duties
- succession
- war spoils
- integration of foreign volunteers

A fighting country should gain forces through institutions, resources, local support, and missions. It should not receive repeated free divisions from unrelated popups.

## Refugee chains

Refugees can add population, labor, soldiers, doctors, engineers, teachers, political factions, and future leaders.

They can also strain food, medicine, shelter, language, and legitimacy.

Refugee events must remember:

- origin country or region
- whether entry was voluntary or forced
- quarantine treatment
- family separation
- citizenship
- employment
- military service
- property
- return policy
- political organization
- children raised in the receiving state

Refugees should later appear as voters, rivals, advisors, commanders, settlers, or founders.

## Environment and recovery chains

The environment changes through cold, soot, radiation, altered ecosystems, thaw, flood, ultraviolet exposure, and human land use.

Recovery events should create:

- new crops
- new pests
- moving animal ranges
- water changes
- forest and grassland change
- contaminated floodplains
- restored fields
- protected corridors
- conflict over reopening land

Fictional altered ecology must remain clearly fictional. The design can be strange without claiming that real radiation would produce the described societies or creatures.

## AI and hidden resolution

AI uses the same outcome logic.

The AI decision score should consider:

- government archetype
- survival-resource values
- state and population value
- war state
- ideology route
- recurring character influence
- neighbor relations
- previous memory
- risk tolerance
- climate phase
- cause memory

When a choice would produce a bilateral consequence, the target country receives memory even if the initiating country is AI.

## Event art batching

Global event art can use thematic families:

- shelters and filters
- food and agriculture
- water and pumping
- medicine and triage
- power and rail
- ports and ships
- ruins and salvage
- markets and law
- schools and family
- patrols and militia
- climate recovery

Each image family needs several regional variants. Reusing one generic ruin image across all global events is insufficient.

## Global pool acceptance

- Global events change mechanics.
- Choices have different long-term consequences.
- Major chains create institutions.
- Positive and mixed events exist.
- Character and relationship memory persists.
- Regional and archetype overlays modify actors and stakes.
- AI resolves the same systems.
- The global pool does not replace unique successor content.
