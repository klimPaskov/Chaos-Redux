# Reviewed archetype: The Patent After the End

Status: accepted bounded implementation tranche. The chain is dormant until the Fallout scheduler activation contract opens. It does not register Fallout as an ordinary event, create a successor country, or add a world iterator.

The Patent After the End is a Technate chain for a Manchurian Reactor Keeps district after a Reactor Shift state memory closes. The district has repair drawings, filter plates, pump measurements, radio circuits, and workshop knowledge that can no longer be treated as ownerless salvage. The player chooses a public design register, inventor rights, state custody, or a regional license compact.

## Admission

The country must have a current Fallout identity and resource row, the Technate government archetype, the Manchurian Reactor Keeps memory, the East Asia region, the closed Reactor Shift predecessor, current Medicine, Power, Cohesion, Recognition, patent legitimacy, and market pressure ledgers, and at least one affordable branch. The host state must be owned and controlled, current-generation, supplied, sheltered, adapted, populated, and have a surviving industrial or arms factory plus an external neighbour.

The chain begins after the first two years of the Fallout campaign and remains eligible through the rival-orders window. It is not produced when a conflicting state receipt or the current Patent After the End memory exists.

## Branches and effects

| Branch | Direction | Main identity effects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Publish the designs | Public domain register | Improves research capacity, lowers market pressure, opens trade, and records public design ownership. |
| Protect inventor rights | Workshop claims and apprenticeships | Improves production capacity and inventor trust while raising the risk of narrow access. |
| Keep the archive secret | State patent custody | Improves production security and legitimacy while increasing trade and espionage pressure. |
| License the region | Time-limited external licenses | Improves trade, regional recognition, and research circulation while exposing the archive to copying disputes. |

All four branches have distinct Food, Medicine, Scrap, Fuel, Power, Recognition, or Cohesion costs. The delayed result resolves after 35 days through deterministic grading. Success, partial success, and failure each change the patent, market, inventor, research, production, licensing, and memory ledgers. State Air Winter Supply Access, Shelter Capacity, Exposure, Adaptation, Reclamation, Disease Pressure, and bounded Deaths outcomes are updated through existing helper contracts. A failed result can damage one host infrastructure level.

The callback resolves after 270 days. It reauthenticates generation, owner, controller, host state, neighbour, branch, and delayed receipt before applying the second-year review. The callback leaves a durable patent memory and releases both result and callback cleanup tickets idempotently.

## Human and AI parity

The hidden AI lane shares affordability, branch payment, frozen ledgers, grading, delayed timing, callback, Event Log payload, and cleanup with the visible lane. The authored ordering favours inventor rights when production capacity is strong, state custody when legitimacy and security are strong, regional licensing when Fuel and Recognition are strong, and public domain when research capacity and Cohesion are strong. Invalid or unaffordable branches receive zero effective weight.

## Presentation and exclusions

The chain uses the dedicated fictional report card `GFX_report_event_fallout_patent_after_end`. It has no audio, portrait, focus, decision, mission, map rewrite, successor tag, or scripted GUI surface. Fallout remains a consequence system and is never added to the ordinary Event Log as a scenario event.
