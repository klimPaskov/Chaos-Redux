
# Government Archetype Event Bible

Working labels in this file are not final localisation.

## Purpose

Government archetypes define how a successor interprets survival. They must produce different event choices, institutions, AI behavior, and mature political outcomes.

The complete matrix is in `matrices/fallout_archetype_event_matrix.md`.

The Ash-week orientation coverage for all twelve archetypes across the nine live regions is in `matrices/fallout_orientation_region_archetype_coverage_matrix.md`. Its rows are working design directions. They do not replace country-memory research or final localisation.

## Composition rule

A country event is built from three layers:

1. government archetype
2. regional overlay
3. country-memory overlay

The archetype supplies the political problem. The region supplies the material setting. Country memory supplies the unique history, leaders, symbols, rivals, and state assets.

## Continuity government

Core conflict:

- legal continuity
- emergency necessity
- regional autonomy
- military survival
- old claims
- public representation
- rival continuity claimants

Mature outcomes can include restored constitution, revised federal order, survivor republic, or permanent emergency government.

The event pool must test whether old law still has practical legitimacy.

## Bunker authority

Core conflict:

- life-support control
- shelter class
- surface and bunker citizenship
- technical labor
- security command
- controlled information
- reopening the surface

Mature outcomes can include civic bunker republic, technical shelter state, authoritarian protocol state, or surface-bunker federation.

## Warlord command

## Archetype change

A country can change archetype through major events, focus routes, civil conflict, or institutional transformation.

Examples:

- bunker authority opens into continuity republic
- warlord command becomes military republic
- food compact becomes regional federation
- technate becomes machine protocol
- quarantine state becomes medical republic
- maritime remnant settles into coastal state
- nomad convoy establishes permanent territory
- religious refuge becomes elected relief league

Archetype change must:

- finish or transform active arcs
- update AI behavior
- update decision families
- update focus branches
- preserve relevant character memory
- change future event weights
- change visible country identity where appropriate

## Archetype AI

Each archetype needs an event-choice profile.

AI must consider:

- current resource crisis
- route
- leader traits
- war state
- cohesion
- legitimacy
- neighboring archetypes
- climate
- cause memory
- previous successful and failed choices

High-chaos AI may choose extreme routes. Ordinary AI should not repeatedly select suicidal options.

## Acceptance

- Every archetype has ten or more anchor chains.
- At least one chain changes mature government direction.
- At least one chain changes character or leader structure.
- At least one chain affects military or security.
- At least one chain affects citizenship or social order.
- At least one chain affects diplomacy.
- AI resolves the same choices.
- Archetype change cleans up obsolete events and decisions.
- Mutant and machine content remains clearly fictional where needed.
