# Reviewed Archetype: Children of Two Settlements

Children of Two Settlements is a fictional Congo Green Basin mutant-polity chain about the first generation raised between the river and hill wards. It treats schooling, guardianship, local authority, and movement as civic institutions. The altered society is fictional and its records do not prove ordinary radiation biology. Fallout remains a consequence rather than an ordinary event, evolution, scenario log entry, or super-event.

The dormant runtime package uses human and hidden-AI opening events `chaosx.fallout.1037` and `chaosx.fallout.1038`, delayed result events `chaosx.fallout.1039` and `chaosx.fallout.1040`, callback events `chaosx.fallout.1041` and `chaosx.fallout.1042`, and cleanup event `chaosx.fallout.1043`. Its scheduler candidate id is `915`, transaction key `710102`, route `7236`, and Event Log history id `9208`.

## Candidate boundary

The row is eligible from campaign day `1460` through day `12000` after the Adaptation Rite state memory closes. The country must retain the Congo Green Basin memory and the `mutant_polity` government archetype, with current registry and survival-resource rows, Medicine at least `14`, Cohesion at least `32`, Recognition at least `18`, youth legitimacy at least `50`, schooling pressure at least `5`, and at least one affordable branch. An open, committed, pending, or closed Children of Two Settlements chain blocks a duplicate request.

The scheduler selects the lowest owned state with living population, current state identity and survival-resource rows, current Supply Access, Shelter at least `22`, Supply Access at least `16`, Adaptation at least `18`, Disease Pressure from `20` through `89`, Exposure from `10` through `89`, and Air Winter phase one through six. The state must have a current foreign neighboring state. Controlled evacuation flags block selection. The closed predecessor memory is `fallout_event_908_memory_closed`.

The opening candidate freezes the owner, controller, transition generation, selected ward, Air Winter values, Supply Access, youth ledgers, and the lowest eligible foreign witness state. Each result, callback, and cleanup lane revalidates those receipts. A changed owner, controller, state, witness, generation, or durable row cancels the chain and authenticated cleanup releases reservations.

## Branches

| Branch | Cost | Civic direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open one public school | Food `2`, Medicine `2`, Recognition `2` | River and hill wards share lessons, food records, and pupil stewards. |
| Keep separate institutions | Scrap `1`, Power `1`, Recognition `2` | River and hill schools retain local practice while sharing one exchange book. |
| Give each settlement local autonomy | Fuel `1`, Recognition `2`, Cohesion `2` | Each settlement governs lessons and youth council while exchanging teachers. |
| Write a migration compact | Food `1`, Medicine `1`, Fuel `1`, Recognition `1` | Children move between wards under named guardians and dated return records. |

The opening choice is paid once through a country flag and is refunded only on a failed queue transaction. Branch triggers expose only choices whose exact survival resources and Cohesion requirement are affordable.

## Grading and delayed consequences

The deterministic result grade weights frozen Supply Access, Shelter, Adaptation, Disease Pressure relief, Exposure relief, Medicine, Recognition, youth legitimacy, schooling-pressure relief, school trust, and migration-pressure relief. A small mutant-polity bonus is explicit. Each branch has separate success and partial thresholds, with failure as the remaining outcome.

Result effects change state disease, shelter, exposure, adaptation, reclamation, supply, and survival resources. They also change Cohesion, Recognition, youth legitimacy, schooling pressure, school trust, generation confidence, migration pressure, cohort fatigue, and generation cause memory. Success, partial, and failure write separate branch memories. Failure uses the established bounded Deaths request contract with a minimum remaining population. Bilateral opinion modifiers distinguish public school, separate institutions, local autonomy, migration compact, and failed record outcomes.

The result transaction resolves after exactly `70` days. It schedules a callback after exactly `420` days. The callback grades youth legitimacy, schooling pressure, school trust, generation confidence, migration pressure, cohort fatigue, generation cause memory, Cohesion, Recognition, and current state Air Winter values. Callback outcomes alter Air Winter, Supply Access, survival resources, civic stability, and the same country ledgers. Callback failure uses a separate bounded Deaths request. The state memory closes only after both delayed transactions and their cleanup receipts settle.

Human and hidden-AI lanes share branch legality, costs, deterministic grading, result timing, callback timing, bilateral memory, and cleanup. AI branch priorities are authored for shared school, separate institutions, local autonomy, and migration compact according to the dedicated constants. No lane creates a country, changes government archetype, requests Fallout, transfers population between states, or adds a recurring scheduler.

## Characters and fictional boundaries

The river child, hill child, ward teachers, named guardian, youth councils, and foreign witness are authored event roles and durable country and state memory flags. No real person or grounded historical leader is invented. The chain describes fictional altered society through schools, movement records, and local authority instead of ordinary radiation science.
