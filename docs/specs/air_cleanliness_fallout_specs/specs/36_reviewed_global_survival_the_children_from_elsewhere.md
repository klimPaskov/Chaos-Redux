# The Children From Elsewhere

## Purpose

The Children From Elsewhere is a second-generation identity chain for refugee children who have lived through the first post-rewrite year.

The chain is a country-level candidate that follows the closed Skilled List memory and selects one deterministic native state with shelter or industrial capacity.

Its conflict is concrete. Children can enter the public ledger, be fostered through local homes, keep a school quarter, or preserve a legal return claim for families beyond the border.

## Opening and result

The human opening is `chaosx.fallout.436` and the hidden AI opening is `chaosx.fallout.437`.

The four policies have distinct government-aware eligibility, Food, Medicine, Recognition, and Cohesion costs, and player-facing tooltips.

The delayed result is `chaosx.fallout.438` for human control and `chaosx.fallout.439` for hidden AI.

The result freezes survival resources and citizenship, foster, school, and return ledgers, then updates Food, Medicine, Recognition, Cohesion, Stability, War Support, Adaptation, Reclamation, Supply Access, Exposure, and cohort memory.

Failure damages infrastructure or industrial capacity and routes population loss through the Deaths system contract.

## Generation review

The 365-day review is `chaosx.fallout.440` for human control and `chaosx.fallout.441` for hidden AI.

The review tests whether the cohort has a durable home, lessons, and public memory before the `chaosx.fallout.442` cleanup receipt closes the chain.

## Engine boundaries

The chain records population and identity ledgers only.

It does not create native characters, dynamic tags, or a fabricated return country.

Those surfaces remain explicit follow-up work until their engine scope and conflict handling are proven.
