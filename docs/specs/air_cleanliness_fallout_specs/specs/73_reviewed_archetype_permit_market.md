# Reviewed Archetype Chain: Permit Market

## Admission

Permit Market is a dormant Quarantine State chain that follows a completed False Positive memory. The producer selects the lowest current native owned state with current Air Winter identity, Shelter Capacity, Supply Access, Adaptation, bounded Exposure and Disease Pressure, surviving population, and a current owner and controller. The state must carry `fallout_event_691_memory_closed` and the country must retain the Quarantine government, public-health memory, grievance memory, and one affordable branch.

The candidate is `698`, transaction `710071`, route `7174`, and survivor Event Log history `9177`. The event suffixes are `698` through `704`. The scheduler activation flags remain unset.

## Opening choice

The human opening presents a winter movement-permit market at an ash gate. The four authored branches are:

1. Issue Local Passes spends Food, Medicine, and Recognition to issue free ward passes.
2. Audit Every Register spends Medicine, Fuel, and Recognition to reconcile clinic seals with ward ledgers.
3. Delegate Permits to the Wards spends Food, Scrap, and Recognition to let local councils issue and appeal movement rights.
4. Sell Temporary Permits spends Medicine, Fuel, and Power to run a bounded paid registry with household exemptions.

The hidden AI opening evaluates all affordable branches with distinct priorities and schedules the same branch-specific delayed result contract.

## Result and callback

The result arrives after 18 days. It grades frozen state Supply Access, Shelter Capacity, Adaptation, Exposure, Disease Pressure, country Medicine, Recognition, registry reliability, inspection fatigue, appeal backlog, and Quarantine government alignment. Success, partial, and failure outcomes have distinct Air Winter, Supply Access, public-health, grievance, reliability, compensation, fatigue, backlog, cause-memory, stability, and dynamic-modifier effects. Result failure routes a bounded state population request through Deaths and damages one repairable infrastructure level.

The callback arrives after 210 days. It grades the durable movement record against current Supply Access, Reclamation, Disease Pressure, registry reliability, compensation, fatigue, backlog, and cause memory. Success, partial, and failure update the same ledgers with different outcomes. Callback failure uses a second bounded Deaths request. Callback cleanup releases the delayed receipt only after the result cleanup is released.
