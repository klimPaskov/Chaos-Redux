# Reviewed Quarantine archetype chain: The Clean Certificate

## Identity

The Clean Certificate is a dormant Quarantine-state chain after the Returning Disease memory. It owns candidate `719`, transaction `710074`, route `7180`, history `9180`, and event ids `719` through `725` under `chaosx.fallout`.

It is a post-consequence survivor chain. It does not register Fallout itself as an ordinary event, evolution, or super-event.

## Admission

The candidate selects the lowest current native owned state with a current Air Winter state receipt, Shelter Capacity at least `20`, Supply Access at least `15`, Adaptation at least `15`, bounded Disease Pressure and Exposure, surviving population, and the completed state memory `fallout_event_712_memory_closed`.

The owner must be a current Quarantine State with public health at least `42`, grievance at least `2`, Medicine at least `12`, Cohesion at least `30`, Recognition at least `15`, and at least one complete branch cost. The row opens only from campaign day `1200` through `5999` and remains behind the dormant Fallout scheduler activation gates.

## Branches and timing

The opening presents four authored policies.

- Public service spends Food `2`, Medicine `2`, and Recognition `2` to staff the North Gate desk for every ward.
- Paid inspection spends Scrap `2`, Power `1`, and Recognition `3` to fund a verified route.
- Political leverage spends Fuel `2`, Recognition `2`, and Cohesion `2` to use the seal against rival wards.
- Regional standard spends Food `1`, Medicine `3`, Fuel `1`, and Recognition `2` to publish one eastern-road standard.

The result resolves after `30` days. The certification review resolves after `180` more days. Both human and hidden-AI lanes use the same branch reservation, deterministic grade, delayed receipts, and cleanup ticket contract.

## Mechanical depth

The chain freezes Air Winter, Supply Access, public health, grievance, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, inspection capacity, public trust, route dependence, appeal backlog, and cause memory. Result grading varies by branch and records success, partial, or failure without random selection. A failed result or review routes only bounded state civilian loss through the Deaths contract and preserves a minimum population.

Result effects change Air Winter disease, exposure, shelter, adaptation, reclamation, Supply Access, Medicine, Cohesion, Recognition, public health, grievance, inspection capacity, public trust, route dependence, appeal backlog, and cause memory. Branch memories record public service, paid route, political seal, regional standard, or a failed register. The Quarantine government archetype is preserved.

The callback reads the surviving ledgers and state receipt again before writing its settlement memory. Cleanup releases the delayed receipts, clears reservations and transient flags, and closes the state memory idempotently.
