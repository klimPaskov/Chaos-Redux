# Spec 70: The Hungry Battalion

The Hungry Battalion is the second first-year Food Compact chain. It follows a completed Work for Rations state memory and tests whether the compact's army remains a protected caste, accepts the civilian ration rule, takes food by force, or releases rear personnel back to the harvest.

The working matrix label is not final localisation. Final text should center the selected harvest state, its granary gate, the battalion ration list, and the households whose allotments are being measured against the army issue. The player should see a civil and military food conflict grounded in named institutions and physical stores. Avoid generic collapse language, abstract map summaries, and cheap humour.

## Player conflict

The selected state still carries one of the four Work for Rations policy memories. A battalion quartermaster presents a protected ration request after field kitchens begin issuing thinner meals. The Food Compact government must decide how much military readiness the harvest can support without destroying the civilian rule that made the first crop possible.

The four policy directions are:

1. A protected military ration reserves a fixed share for the battalion and ties every issue to a quartermaster ledger. It improves immediate organization and battalion loyalty. It raises civilian hunger and weakens civil trust when the reserve is already narrow.
2. One public ration measure places soldiers and households under the same scale. It protects cohesion and legitimacy. It reduces short-term military readiness when exposure or disease has already weakened the state.
3. Field requisition lets patrols collect grain and livestock along their route. It can restore food and military readiness quickly. It risks damaged farms, rising exposure, civilian deaths, and a durable coercion memory.
4. A rear-echelon stand-down releases depot staff, labor companies, and medically limited personnel to the harvest. It improves labor availability and food recovery while imposing a substantial temporary penalty to army organization, attack, and war support. It must not create a repeatable manpower or equipment refund loop.

Every option needs a concrete mix of Food Compact resources and national military capacity. Political power is not a store for this chain. Food, Fuel, Medicine, Recognition, Command Power, Army Experience, War Support, and the existing survival ledgers carry the tradeoffs.

## Target and timing

The candidate selects the lowest current native state that remains owned and controlled by the Food Compact country, has a durable Fallout identity and resource row, has a produced Air Winter snapshot, and carries a Work for Rations branch memory from the current Fallout generation. The country must have a meaningful army manpower commitment and at least one complete affordable branch.

The result arrives after 28 days. The military ration review arrives after 210 days. This separates the chain from the 35-day and 270-day Work for Rations cadence while keeping it inside the first-year pressure band.

The result freezes Food, Supply Access, Cohesion, War Support, Army Experience, Exposure, Adaptation, Reclamation, Disease Pressure, population, ration discipline, battalion loyalty, civilian hunger, and civil-military trust. The grade is deterministic. It rewards strong supply, cohesion, adaptation, reclamation, disciplined issue, and trust. It penalizes exposure, disease, hunger, and coercive pressure.

## Consequences and memory

Each branch has distinct success, partial, and failure outcomes. Results alter Food, Fuel, Medicine, Recognition, Cohesion, Stability, War Support, Command Power, Army Experience, state Supply Access, Air Winter Exposure, Adaptation, Reclamation, Disease Pressure, ration discipline, battalion loyalty, civilian hunger, civil-military trust, and the Work for Rations labor memory.

Failure can damage repairable infrastructure or a civilian factory in the selected state. Civilian loss must use the Deaths system with the Fallout aftermath cause. Requisition failure receives the largest bounded loss because violence reaches farm households. The stand-down route never deletes a division or refunds equipment. Its military sacrifice is a strong timed modifier and a bounded personnel release into the national manpower pool, with a durable receipt that prevents repeated harvesting.

The callback measures whether the army issue rule survived another season. Success creates a stable civil-military food compact. Partial success preserves the rule with a continuing grievance. Failure records a ration mutiny or coercive breakdown, applies a second smaller Deaths-system loss, and leaves a durable military-food crisis memory.

The chain writes ordinary survivor-memory Event Log history for its choices and outcomes. It does not register the Fallout consequence, blackout, strike sweep, population deletion, or permanent 99 percent Air Contamination state as an Event Log event or evolution.

## Hidden AI

Human and hidden-AI openings use the same target, branch costs, deterministic grade, result effects, callback effects, and cleanup.

The AI should prefer:

- protected military ration when battalion loyalty is low, War Support is high, and civilian hunger is still contained
- equal measure when Cohesion and Recognition are fragile or democratic government memory is strong
- field requisition only under severe food pressure, high military authority, and low civil-military trust
- rear-echelon stand-down when Adaptation and reclamation are strong enough to absorb labor, the country is at peace, and Army Experience can pay for the temporary military contraction

Invalid or unaffordable branches receive no AI score. Fixed branch order resolves ties.

## Continuation

The chosen policy creates the Food Compact civil-military ration memory used by later military citizenship, land ownership, and constitutional chains. Requisition can feed a future town grievance or officer trial. Equal measure can feed a citizen-soldier route. Protected issue can feed a quartermaster institution or coup-risk chain. Stand-down can feed a reserve army and labor-rights settlement.
