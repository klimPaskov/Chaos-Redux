# Fallout Run Event Budget Matrix

The library is large. One campaign uses a context-selected portion.

## Visible event budget

| Campaign band | Country scale | Ordinary visible target | Arc target | Cooldown | Priority rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ash week | Any human country | Orientation only | 5 mandatory orientation components, combined into 2 to 4 popups | No ordinary event before orientation closes | No broad diplomacy or unrelated routine events |
| Days 8 to 30 | Small | 0 to 2 ordinary, 1 active arc | 1 crisis may interrupt | 14-day ordinary cooldown | Prioritize immediate survival |
| Days 8 to 30 | Medium or large | 1 to 3 ordinary, 1 active arc | 1 crisis may interrupt | 12-day ordinary cooldown | Prioritize capital and highest-population state |
| Days 31 to 90 | Small | 2 to 4 ordinary, 1 to 2 arcs | 1 bilateral contact | 14-day ordinary cooldown | No repeated family |
| Days 31 to 90 | Medium | 3 to 6 ordinary, 2 arcs | 1 to 2 bilateral contacts | 12-day ordinary cooldown | Use different states and families |
| Days 31 to 90 | Large | 4 to 8 ordinary, 2 to 3 arcs | Up to 2 bilateral contacts | 10-day ordinary cooldown | Capital does not consume every event |
| Months 4 to 12 | Small | 10 to 18 visible events | 1 to 2 major arc advances per quarter | 14-day ordinary cooldown | First-winter milestone guaranteed |
| Months 4 to 12 | Medium | 14 to 24 visible events | 2 to 3 major arc advances per quarter | 12-day ordinary cooldown | First-winter and year-end state event |
| Months 4 to 12 | Large | 18 to 30 visible events | 2 to 4 major arc advances per quarter | 10-day ordinary cooldown | Spread across states and institutions |
| Year 2 | Small | 8 to 16 visible events | 2 major arcs, 1 external arc | 14-day ordinary cooldown | Constitution or permanent rule begins |
| Year 2 | Medium | 12 to 22 visible events | 2 to 3 major arcs, 1 to 2 external arcs | 12-day ordinary cooldown | Trade and recognition grow |
| Year 2 | Large | 16 to 28 visible events | 3 major arcs, 2 external arcs | 10-day ordinary cooldown | Regional politics begin |
| Years 3 to 4 | Any | 10 to 24 per year depending activity | Up to 3 major arcs | 12 to 18-day ordinary cooldown | Wars and compacts can replace routine events |
| Years 5 to 6 | Any | 10 to 22 per year | Up to 3 major arcs plus generation arc | 14-day ordinary cooldown | Second-generation politics enters |
| Years 7 to 8 | Any | 10 to 22 per year | Climate recovery arc can reserve slots | 14-day ordinary cooldown | Thaw and ultraviolet events gain priority |
| Years 9 to 10 | Any | 10 to 24 per year | World-order and late identity arcs | 14-day ordinary cooldown | Interregional events require contact |
| Year 10 onward | Any | 8 to 20 per year | Seasonal, succession, war, and late ambition | 14 to 21-day ordinary cooldown | No artificial event exhaustion |
