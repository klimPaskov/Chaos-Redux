# Fallout Run Event Budget Matrix

The library is large. One campaign uses a context-selected portion.

## Visible event budget

Country scale is frozen from committed successor assignment. Small countries have 1 through 3 assigned states. Medium countries have 4 through 9. Large countries have at least 10. Later conquest and fragmentation do not change this identity.

| Campaign band | Elapsed days | Small base cooldown | Medium base cooldown | Large base cooldown | Main event layer |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Ash week | 0 through 7 | none | none | none | Orientation only |
| First season | 8 through 90 | 24 | 18 | 14 | Immediate survival and local authority |
| First winter year | 91 through 365 | 28 | 24 | 20 | Heating, food, migration, first settlement |
| Consolidation | 366 through 730 | 32 | 28 | 24 | Permanent government, trade, militia reform |
| Rival orders | 731 through 1460 | 34 | 30 | 26 | Compacts, borders, wars, constitutions |
| New states | 1461 through 2190 | 36 | 32 | 28 | Second generation, larger states, federations |
| Soot retreat | 2191 through 2920 | 38 | 34 | 30 | Thaw, ultraviolet exposure, recovered land |
| Second world | 2921 through 3650 | 40 | 36 | 32 | Interregional blocs and durable order |
| Open continuation | 3651 onward | 46 | 42 | 38 | Succession, late ambition, continuing recovery |

An opening reserves this base cooldown multiplied by an immutable visible-budget cost from 1 through 4. Hidden AI results and mechanical cleanup cost zero visible beats. Later visible results may extend a reservation and cannot shorten it.

| Frozen size | Ordinary visible beats through day 3650 | Planned visible Ash-week beats | Ten-year baseline |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Small | 103.9 | 11 | 114.9 |
| Medium | 118.0 | 11 | 129.0 |
| Large | 136.2 | 11 | 147.2 |

These baselines assume continuous eligible content and exact use of each reserved visible share. Candidate droughts, invalid targets, route locks, wars, and player choices can reduce them. A pressure-80 crisis may exceed ordinary cadence only after a seven-day popup gap and outside a 180-day crisis-break lock. Its reservation is 42 days for each visible beat. Broadcasts keep a thirty-day global minimum.

Transaction limits are three active major arcs, eight delayed or callback rows, six bilateral rows, one independent arc per recurring character or institution, one fixed ordinary opening receipt, and one newly selected envelope per country and engine date. Cleanup-pending and issued rows retain their slot until exact release.

The coordinator reviews every current human successor once per engine date. Its nonhuman batch is 1 country for a registry of 1 through 30, 2 for 31 through 60, 3 for 61 through 90, and 4 for at least 91. Due results and cleanup take priority over new selection.
