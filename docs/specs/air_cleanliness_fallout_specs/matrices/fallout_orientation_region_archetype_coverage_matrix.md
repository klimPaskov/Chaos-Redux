# Fallout Ash Week Region and Archetype Coverage Matrix

Working directions in this file are not final localisation. They define the regional and governmental substance that the Ash-week orientation package must express after the shared opening.

## Runtime identity rule

The matrix follows the live `fallout_region` and `fallout_government_archetype` constants. The nine runtime regions are North America, Europe, Eurasian Interior, East Asia, South Asia, Middle East and North Africa, Sub-Saharan Africa, Latin America and Caribbean, and Oceania and Remote Islands.

Polar and remote-station material is a route overlay. It inherits the runtime region of the station or its controlling country. It is not a tenth runtime region.

Every keyed region and archetype cell supplies seven required directions. The first five appear in the regional content tables. The AI and availability tables below complete the same keyed cells.

1. language through which national authority presents itself
2. the condition of the capital or governing seat that receives emphasis
3. a concrete local resource response
4. the institution or rival that can contest the first settlement
5. a candidate character class or institution class for later researched implementation
6. one meaningful AI preference tied to the cell's material and political problem
7. one branch that becomes unavailable when its required world-state evidence is absent

The country-memory layer must replace broad offices with researched local institutions, people, infrastructure, and political memory. A completed cell does not authorize an approval setter by itself.
