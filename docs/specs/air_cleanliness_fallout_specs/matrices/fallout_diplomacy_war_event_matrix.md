# Fallout Diplomacy, Trade, War, and Settlement Matrix

Working labels are not final localisation. Bilateral and multilateral events must reserve valid actors, give both sides memory, and resolve through the same mechanics for humans and AI.

| Working label | Family | Trigger | Choice directions | Actual effects | Continuation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The First Reply | Contact | Two countries within radio or route range have no formal contact. | Open identity exchange, cautious technical exchange, demand proof, remain silent. | Creates contact level, trust, intelligence, and future event access. | Can lead to recognition, trade, espionage, or missed-contact memory. |
| Recognition by Signal | Recognition | Contact is stable and both governments have identifiable authority. | Full recognition, practical relations without recognition, observer exchange, refusal. | Changes recognition, legitimacy, diplomacy, and compact eligibility. | Creates embassy or liaison chain. |
| The Envoy's Route | Recognition | Countries agree to meet but route is dangerous. | Escort jointly, meet at border, remote negotiation, cancel. | Changes manpower, supplies, trust, and named envoy status. | Can create ambush, successful summit, or blame. |
| Bread for Fuel | Trade | Food state and fuel state have compatible routes. | Equal exchange, long contract, client bargain, seize cargo. | Moves food and fuel, changes dependency, trust, and war pressure. | Creates trade axis or betrayal memory. |
| Water for Power | Trade | Water system needs pumping power or hydro state needs water access. | Joint utility, fixed-price contract, political union, military pressure. | Changes water, power, dependency, and compact cohesion. | Creates utility authority or conflict. |
| Medicine Corridor | Aid | One state has medicine and another has outbreak or exposure. | Humanitarian grant, paid mission, political conditions, refuse. | Moves medicine, changes recognition, dependency, deaths, and medical trust. | Creates health compact or resentment. |
