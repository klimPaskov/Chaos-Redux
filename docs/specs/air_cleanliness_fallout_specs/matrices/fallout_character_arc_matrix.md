# Fallout Character Arc Matrix

Working role names are not final localisation. Every one-person portrait must use an actual regional name and gender-compatible portrait and metadata. Councils and collective bodies use institutional names.

| Role | Introduction | Core pressure | Possible outcomes | Mechanical reach | Late callback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quartermaster | Opening food or equipment inventory. | Accused of favoritism, forced to choose civilian or military allocation, tempted by black market. | Honest administrator, military patron, corrupt broker, scapegoat, elected ration official. | Changes food distribution, equipment readiness, corruption, advisor roster, and ration decisions. | Can become finance or logistics leader, face trial, or be remembered as first-year institution. |
| Grid engineer | First transformer, dam, reactor, or grid repair. | Safety versus speed, labor control, foreign aid, technical monopoly. | Public engineer, technate founder, exhausted expert, saboteur accusation, regional grid envoy. | Changes power, repair speed, technate influence, character health, and foreign dependency. | Can lead black-start project or die during major failure. |
| Doctor or medical board | Triage or outbreak event. | Care priorities, quarantine authority, coercive research, medicine theft. | Public-health reformer, medical ruler, disgraced doctor, martyr, foreign medical envoy. | Changes medicine, deaths, quarantine policy, legitimacy, and mutant relations. | Can trigger trial, coup, health compact, or school. |
| Radio operator | First long-range signal. | Public broadcast versus military secrecy, false signal, foreign patron, personal voice becoming famous. | Civic broadcaster, intelligence officer, cult voice, foreign agent, lost operator. | Changes contact range, intelligence, cohesion, diplomacy, and propaganda. | Can become national symbol or vanish in signal mystery. |
| Militia commander | First security crisis. | Loot discipline, civilian control, officer rivalry, integration into regular army. | Professional commander, warlord rival, elected protector, deserter, coup leader. | Changes units, command obedience, leader roster, and government route. | Can lead first war, be removed, or become national founder. |

| Scavenger captain | Dead-city or sealed-warehouse expedition. | Risk tolerance, salvage division, smuggling, rescue obligation. | Guild leader, state explorer, criminal boss, radiation casualty, foreign contractor. | Changes salvage, corruption, expedition success, and character network. | Can discover landmark asset or start guild war. |
