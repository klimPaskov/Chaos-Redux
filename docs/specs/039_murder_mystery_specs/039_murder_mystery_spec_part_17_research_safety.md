# Murder Mystery Specification Part 17: Research Basis, Terminology, and Cultural Safety

## Research purpose

The outside research for Event 39 supports structure, investigation, clandestine organization, sabotage, and presentation. It does not assign the fictional movement to a real religion, ethnicity, historical group, or modern political community.

## Nizari Ismaili and assassin-legend boundary

The Institute of Ismaili Studies documents that the European label `Assassin` was applied to the Nizari Ismailis through hostile medieval terminology and later legends. Its scholarship states that the drug and hashish stories lack evidence and grew from abuse, rumor, and later European elaboration.

Event 39 must therefore avoid:

- calling the movement Nizari, Ismaili, Hashashin, or an order of Alamut
- using the Old Man of the Mountain as the fictional leader
- using hashish or drug control as recruitment lore
- using a secret garden or paradise promise
- using Islamic, Persian, Syrian, or Crusader imagery as shorthand for murder
- presenting a real living religious community as the origin of the event

The word assassin is used only in its modern common meaning and in the event's fictional state label.

## Revolutionary clandestine-network research

Narodnaya Volya, also known as the People's Will, provides a useful historical design anchor for an executive committee, specialized underground groups, propaganda, local circles, anti-infiltration work, printing, and assassination planning. Its experience also shows that killing a ruler did not automatically create the expected mass uprising and that repression, arrests, executions, and political polarization followed.

Event 39 uses these observations to support:

- separate operational roles inside one network
- a distinction between the central organizer and local sympathizers
- incomplete control over imitators
- government infiltration and counter-infiltration
- the possibility that a major assassination fails to produce the intended political result
- a movement that can survive in decentralized form after leadership loss

Event 39 does not copy Narodnaya Volya's ideology, names, Russian identity, or historical actors.

## Civil-military conspiracy research

The Black Hand provides a design anchor for clandestine networks that cross formal military and civilian boundaries, support cells across borders, and create uncertainty about state knowledge or control. The 1914 assassination network also supports the idea that a small cell can create geopolitical consequences far beyond its own size.

Event 39 uses this only for:

- cross-border routes
- military insider risk
- state and nonstate ambiguity
- high-value assassination as an international crisis source
- later conflict between secret networks and formal government

It does not assign Serbian, Balkan, nationalist, or First World War identity to the fictional movement.

## Sabotage and resistance research

The Special Operations Executive provides a design anchor for sabotage, subversion, support to resistance, clandestine communications, and work inside enemy-held territory. This supports the Assassin State's military and intelligence mechanics after it becomes a country.

Event 39 uses this for operational categories such as:

- secure communications
- resistance support
- route interdiction
- sabotage teams
- local cells
- liaison and supply
- intelligence preparation for military action

It does not equate wartime resistance organizations with the murder cult or use real SOE personnel as fictional Assassin characters.

## Anarchism boundary

The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy describes anarchism as a broad political philosophy with disputes over law, authority, direct action, violence, and the relation between means and ends. Violence is not a universal definition of anarchism.

`World of Anarchy` is the Assassin movement's self-description and a public scenario title. The event must not claim that anarchism means murdering every leader or replacing states with violent cult administrations.

Player-facing text should make the movement's contradiction visible. It attacks hierarchy while building command, subject relations, intelligence systems, and armed administration. The route is a fictional high-chaos ideology, not a summary of anarchist thought.

## Fictional identity rules

The original killer remains fictional. The event should preserve three kinds of uncertainty:

- personal origin is not fully confirmed
- early copycat crimes may not be directly ordered
- later public doctrine may emerge as a post hoc justification after the original crimes

The event should not resolve uncertainty by revealing a secret foreign ethnic group, religion, intelligence service, or real historical society as the mastermind.

## Violence presentation

The design deals with political murder and war. Presentation should avoid graphic bodies, fetishized blades, ritualized gore, and victim portraits used as spectacle. The player should see consequences through offices, command disruption, succession, evidence, protection, public order, and institutional loss.

The event should not reward indiscriminate killing as an efficient generic action. Assassin power depends on target selection, intelligence, route access, organization, and strategic tradeoffs. Civilian harm remains a serious consequence through shared deaths, migration, famine, condemnation, resistance, and occupation systems.

## Language rules

Avoid:

- exoticized terms borrowed from real cultures
- language about drugged obedience
- claims that all cells share one faith or ethnicity
- generic `madman` explanations
- repeated words such as shadow, darkness, whisper, ancient order, and blood oath when they add no information
- calling every act terrorism in player-facing text when assassination, sabotage, insurrection, or war is more precise

Use:

- murderer, cell, network, safe house, courier, evidence, protector, investigator, command, derivative, administration, and campaign council
- route-aware terms that describe what the movement is doing
- institutional language for government response
- uncertainty grounded in evidence and free of mystical vagueness

## Quote research

Three primary public-domain candidates have been verified for further super-event research:

- William Shakespeare, *Henry IV, Part 2*, for the vulnerability of leadership
- Joseph Conrad, *The Secret Agent*, for the relation between coercive law and political violence
- Francis Bacon, `Of Revenge`, for postwar suppression and wild justice

The final super-event researcher must compare alternatives, verify exact wording and attribution, and document the selected edition. These candidates are not automatic final choices.

## Research gaps for implementation

Before final text and art, narrow research should confirm:

- period government protection and investigation visual references for the selected host contexts
- intelligence office, communications, transport, safe-house, and evidence-room material culture
- period special-operations clothing and equipment for 3D source art
- country-specific succession and institutional replacement for every opening host row
- licensed music and unit audio sources
- cultural meaning of any final symbol that could resemble a real movement

A missing research result remains blocked. Do not fill it with a stereotype or generic modern image.

## Safety acceptance

The event passes the cultural-safety gate only when its movement is clearly fictional, historical examples remain structural references, the Nizari assassin legends are not repeated, anarchism is not reduced to political murder, images avoid borrowed real symbols and ethnic shorthand, final text does not assign guilt to a real community, and all source-dependent references are documented.
