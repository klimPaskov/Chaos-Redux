# Murder Mystery Specification Part 13: AI and Probability Contract

## AI design goal

AI countries must understand Event 39 as a changing strategic system. Governments should protect leadership, investigate, share evidence, and prevent revolt according to their capacity. Assassin countries should survive, build a mixed army, support a bounded network, choose a political route, and pursue World of Anarchy only when they can sustain it.

No AI action should use an invalid target, unavailable country carrier, protected character, impossible state split, missing route, insufficient equipment, or disabled evolution.

## Mandatory probability workflow

Every complex or balance-sensitive weighted surface must be routed through the AI probability auditor. The auditor first uses `hoi4.probability_inspect`, then selects the tool that matches the question.

- use evaluation for one complete current state
- use sweeps for threshold and sensitivity behavior
- use simulation for repeated random selection where exact calculation is impractical
- use comparison for before and after logic
- use timing tools for MTTH and scheduled readiness
- use sequence analysis only when the full pool, cadence, cooldown, recovery, cap, removal, reset, and terminal state are declared
- render timing, matrix, sensitivity, or unresolved views when they improve audit clarity

Exact probability claims are forbidden when the pool or external state is incomplete.

## Weighted surfaces

The audit covers:

- automatic Event 39 host selection
- incident office-group selection
- safe named target selection inside a complete role pool
- attempted attack outcome where chance remains after public preparation
- investigation method result variation
- mission pool selection
- evolution pacing
- foreign cell target selection
- cell spread route selection
- foreign revolt candidate selection
- original and foreign state anchor selection among validated candidates
- Assassin State political route choice
- focus AI priorities
- decision and mission AI weights
- unit template and production priorities
- movement inheritance candidate selection
- World of Anarchy sector selection
- terminal government administration outcome for AI

## General AI safety order

Before any weight is evaluated, the AI path applies hard validity gates. The order should be:

1. system and evolution enabled
2. actor exists and owns the correct package
3. target exists and is eligible
4. character, country, map, and carrier safety pass
5. required resources and capabilities exist
6. action does not violate mission, unit, derivative, or sector caps
7. action fits current route and war state
8. only then evaluate dynamic weight

A zero or unresolved weight cannot substitute for a missing hard gate.

## Government AI priorities

Government AI should normally prioritize:

1. verified succession and capital continuity
2. evidence preservation
3. protection of an exposed office group
4. one suitable active mission
5. investigation method diversity
6. final capture when public readiness is sufficient
7. foreign evidence sharing after domestic survival is stable

It should reduce foreign assistance when its own capital, supply, or government is collapsing.

## Response philosophy selection

### Rule-bound investigation dominance

Expected when:

- high stability and government legitimacy
- strong intelligence agency
- adequate civilian industry
- allied evidence-sharing access
- no imminent attack
- low or moderate Network Reach

### Integrated security dominance

Expected when:

- moderate stability
- active war or serious cult pressure
- usable agency and military capacity
- need to protect several offices
- evidence remains recoverable

### Emergency repression dominance

Expected when:

- low stability or near revolt
- weak agency
- recent successful murders
- imminent escape or attack
- authoritarian route or emergency law context
- limited time for slower evidence work

Repression should not dominate merely because it has a high base weight. Its long-term costs must matter in the AI score.

## Final capture timing

AI should wait for the safe public threshold when time permits. It may act early when:

- the murderer is preparing to flee
- a national leader or command group faces imminent attack
- Evolution II spread is near
- the government is close to collapse
- evidence is decaying beyond recovery

An early attempt should select a route that fits current assets. A weak agency with strong military security may choose coordinated raids. A strong agency with good witnesses may choose silent arrest.

## Foreign-government AI

Secondary countries begin with better method knowledge. AI should:

- join evidence sharing when relations and security allow
- protect the office group currently threatened
- prioritize local dismantling over distant assistance
- exploit inherited evidence
- accept help when it lacks capacity
- prevent revolt when stage 4 pressure appears
- preserve recent immunity and avoid paying for redundant actions

It should not overreact to a stage 1 rumor with maximal repression.

## Assassin State route choice

### Hidden Hand dominance

Expected when:

- original leader survives
- Cohesion is high
- territory is compact
- central industry and supply are adequate
- subjects need control
- military victory depends on synchronized action
- foreign network is smaller and higher quality

### Cells Without Masters dominance

Expected when:

- territory is dispersed or capital threatened
- foreign cells are numerous
- central industry is weak
- local derivatives are strong
- the movement has lost or distrusted its leader
- resistance and uprising strategy offers more value than conventional war

### Necessary Mask dominance

Expected when:

- transferred industry and trade access are meaningful
- the war will be long
- conventional enemy strength is high
- diplomatic aid or recognition is available
- radical recruitment is low
- mechanized or combined-arms development is practical

The audit should prove that no route dominates every plausible scenario.

## Assassin operational priorities

The central AI should normally prioritize:

1. capital and supply survival
2. reinforce existing units and produce operations kits
3. maintain Cohesion above route danger threshold
4. complete one selected foreign operation
5. support threatened derivatives
6. prepare a new revolt only when tag, resource, and map capacity exist
7. expand the network after domestic stability is adequate
8. pursue terminal preparation only after Evolution V and sufficient readiness

It should not create more cells merely because Network Reach is below maximum.

## Unit and production AI

AI production should reserve enough operations kits for reinforcement before foreign operations. It should maintain ordinary infantry equipment, support equipment, transport, trains, fuel, anti-air, and artillery according to route and enemy.

Template priorities:

- ordinary infantry and Assassin Cadres hold most of the line
- Shadow Companies attack selected objectives
- Silent Guard protects capital and critical nodes
- Master Assassins serve narrow objectives
- Mechanized Assassins appear only with fuel, equipment, and supply

The AI should cancel or delay an elite expansion when reinforcement falls below the safe band.

## Foreign cell target selection

Target score should include:

- live route and access
- local agency weakness
- local stability and war disruption
- existing cell maturity
- valid target and character safety
- future revolt map viability
- strategic value to the movement
- distance and support cost
- current cooperation defenses
- immunity and recent failure
- derivative capacity

The score should favor a few plausible strategic targets. It should not always select the largest country or the weakest country.

## Revolt decision

AI prepares a territorial revolt only when the cell has a realistic chance to survive. It considers:

- validated state cluster
- parent-government front and reserve strength
- local population and industry
- central support
- subject and faction capacity
- terrain and supply
- friendly borders or ports
- likely foreign intervention
- current movement mission load

A revolt with no viable capital, no supply, or no forces has zero eligibility.

## Movement inheritance

Candidate scoring considers territorial survival, industry, population, active cells, Cohesion, central route compatibility, subject relationships, supply, and leader package. Eligibility remains strict and inheritance happens only within its one-use limit.

## World of Anarchy AI

AI activation requires:

- Evolution V active
- terminal branch enabled
- no incompatible world end
- sufficient Cohesion
- viable central country and subjects
- healthy reinforcement and supply
- enough active cells or military position to open at least one sector
- completed command settlement

AI should avoid activation while facing imminent capitulation or severe internal fracture. It can activate under pressure only when delay would clearly destroy the movement and one terminal sector offers a credible escape.

## Sector selection

Sector score should include borders, ports, active cells, derivatives, enemy strength, coalition structure, supply distance, strategic capitals, existing wars, and ability to complete a campaign mandate.

The AI should not open several distant sectors while current sectors remain unresolved. It should reinforce a threatened derivative or finish a near-complete sector before adding another front.

## Named audit scenarios

### Scenario A: Stable major with strong agency

State:

- high stability
- developed agency
- adequate civilian industry
- no major war
- low Network Reach

Expected ordering:

- evidence preservation and rule-bound investigation dominate
- target protection precedes repression
- final capture waits for strong readiness
- Event 39 has a high chance of ending before Evolution II

### Scenario B: War-torn major with weak agency

State:

- low stability
- active multi-front war
- weak or absent agency
- recent successful murder
- rising Network Reach

Expected ordering:

- integrated security or repression dominates
- military cordon and protected-office decisions rise
- early capture risk becomes acceptable
- cult survival and Evolution I readiness are materially higher than Scenario A

### Scenario C: Small eligible player country

State:

- player controlled
- enough states for a safe future split
- modest industry and agency

Expected ordering:

- host-selection weight remains meaningful
- dynamic costs scale down
- investigation remains solvable
- state split preserves two viable countries

### Scenario D: Secondary country with strong shared evidence

State:

- active stage 2 cell
- high inherited evidence
- strong agency
- recent partner capture

Expected ordering:

- local dismantling and evidence sharing dominate
- named murder risk remains low
- revolt readiness should fall or remain blocked

### Scenario E: Secondary country near revolt

State:

- stage 4 cell
- weak government
- valid revolt cluster
- central Assassin support

Expected ordering:

- counterinsurgency and route interdiction dominate government AI
- revolt preparation dominates movement AI only when support and derivative capacity exist
- both sides have reachable success paths

### Scenario F: Assassin State under immediate threat

State:

- capital threatened
- low stockpile
- several foreign cells
- moderate Cohesion

Expected ordering:

- survival, supply, reinforcement, and command settlement dominate
- new foreign revolts and elite expansion fall sharply
- AI does not activate a second distant front

### Scenario G: Strong decentralized movement

State:

- many mature cells
- weak central industry
- several autonomous derivatives
- original leader lost

Expected ordering:

- Cells Without Masters route dominates
- local support and resistance actions rise
- centralized subject demands fall
- movement can survive central capital loss without free military strength

### Scenario H: Pragmatic industrial movement

State:

- strong transferred industry
- trade and fuel access
- long conventional war
- low radical recruitment

Expected ordering:

- Necessary Mask route dominates
- conventional adaptation and mechanized production rise
- terminal route remains delayed until an explicit late recommitment

### Scenario I: World of Anarchy ready

State:

- 1000 or more Chaos
- Evolution V
- high Cohesion
- healthy army and subjects
- mature cells in a connected sector

Expected ordering:

- terminal activation becomes plausible
- the best connected sector dominates first selection
- opening all sectors remains invalid

### Scenario J: World of Anarchy nominally unlocked but movement collapsing

State:

- 1000 or more Chaos
- Evolution V
- low Cohesion
- capital near capitulation
- poor reinforcement

Expected ordering:

- Cohesion repair, defense, or negotiated survival dominates
- terminal activation is near zero or invalid
- AI cannot use terminal setup as a free rescue

## Pacing expectations

A capable stable government should usually have time to act before the second serious incident. A weak government can lose officials and reach Evolution I, but the chain should not become inevitable from one bad roll.

Foreign spread should create several active cases, not universal coverage. Revolts should remain uncommon before Evolution IV and should require mature local conditions. World of Anarchy should occur only in campaigns that reach World Collapse and sustain an advanced movement.

## Anti-exploit and starvation tests

Audit for:

- farming Case Progress through repeatable cheap actions
- farming Chaos through repeated cell creation and dismantling
- reseeding one country after immunity
- infinite movement inheritance
- free unit or operations-kit loops
- foreign operations that refund more than they cost
- target selectors that preserve dead countries
- AI mission starvation from one high-weight action
- one political route dominating every state
- derivative creation consuming no central resources
- terminal sector expansion with no front or supply limits

## Acceptance evidence

The AI and probability surface is accepted only when the complete candidate pools are inspected, named scenarios have measurable expected ordering, thresholds do not create discontinuities that break play, invalid actions are gated before weighting, simulation or exact evidence supports repeated selection, and unresolved external state is reported instead of guessed.
