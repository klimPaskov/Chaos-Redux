# Super-Event Production Prompt: Event 039 Murder Mystery

Produce and integrate the complete Event 39 super-event package. Read `AGENTS.md`, `chaos-redux-super-events`, `chaos-redux-event-assets`, `chaos-redux-frame-animation`, the Event 39 source package, especially Parts 14, 15, 17, and 18, and the live super-event registry and local precedent.

## Accepted roles

Create three complete packages:

1. Assassin State public reveal after Evolution III split and war setup succeed
2. World of Anarchy after terminal activation succeeds
3. Brotherhood defeat aftermath only when the movement reached the scale gate in Part 15

Do not create a super-event for the opening murder, Murder Cult, ordinary foreign cell, normal capture, minor derivative, or early local defeat.

## Slot and registry preflight

Audit the live super-event catalogue, GUI slots, sprites, scripted localisation, audio IDs, sound definitions, and existing docs before reserving identifiers. Each role needs one unique slot and one unique audio ID. Do not reuse unrelated content or overwrite a sibling slot.

The defeat role requires a conditional trigger and should remain unused in small local defeats. A distinct terminal-victory super-event is outside the required set unless the shared world-end framework already owns a supported resolution slot and the parent accepts the addition.

## Text research

Route text research through `chaosx_super_event_text_researcher` when available. Produce final title, description, button reaction, quote, attribution, source, confidence, and note on why the set fits the exact role.

Direction:

- reveal text identifies the murderer-led movement becoming a territorial state and preserves uncertainty about origin
- terminal text identifies a coordinated campaign against ordinary government leadership and exposes the movement's command contradiction
- defeat text records the scale of the movement, the cost of defeating it, surviving records or cells, and unstable restoration

Do not use generic end-of-order wording, fake quotations, unverified slogans, lyric fragments, or modern copyrighted passages beyond a short compliant reference.

Research candidates already verified for comparison:

- Shakespeare, *Henry IV, Part 2*, for vulnerable leadership
- Conrad, *The Secret Agent*, for the shared logic of coercive law and political violence
- Bacon, `Of Revenge`, for postwar suppression and wild justice

Compare each candidate with at least two role-appropriate public-domain or historical alternatives. Verify exact wording, attribution, edition, rights, and final excerpt length. Do not treat the candidate as selected until the research note explains the choice.

## Image production

Route final source art through `chaosx_generated_event_art`. Each image must be a distinct full scene in the correct super-event aspect and crop.

- reveal image shows a real territorial seizure, organized cadres, new state symbol, and open war
- terminal image shows worldwide government command disruption and a concealed coordinating network without a flat title card or world-map diagram
- defeat image shows captured records, dismantled movement structures, guarded reconstruction, and no clean triumph

The movement is fictional. Avoid Nizari, Islamic, ethnic, national, real anarchist, real extremist, and ninja stereotypes. Avoid graphic bodies, generated text, identifiable real leaders, and copied symbols.

Preserve source PNG, processed preview, final DDS, prompt evidence, reference shelf, and manifest.

## Audio research

Route each role through `chaosx_super_event_audio_researcher` after correcting or overriding its stale OGG instruction. The current super-event skill requires final WAV and is authoritative.

For each role:

- find one unique legally usable musical recording
- verify composition and recording rights separately
- document title, composer or creator, performer or source, source URL, license, usage terms, attribution, duration, and confidence
- preserve the legitimate original download
- edit only as allowed by the license and repository workflow
- convert to event-scoped game-ready WAV
- propose and register one unique sound definition and settings-volume wrappers
- set the unique current audio ID and use the settings-aware playback helper
- add the track to the canonical HTML music catalogue

The track should normally be one to three minutes before or after role-specific trimming. It must have musical structure and fit reveal, terminal activation, or unstable aftermath. Generated tones, noise beds, beeps, generic ambience, placeholder music, and unclear-license recordings are forbidden.

## Integration

Wire title, description, button, quote, image, audio, slot, trigger, settings, and catalogue as one package. The visible content and sound must all refer to the same role.

Fire the Evolution III reveal only after the country split commits. Fire World of Anarchy only after terminal setup commits. Fire the defeat package only after its scale gate and movement defeat transaction commit.

Update Event Details, super-event catalogue, event docs, asset docs, audio docs, and catalog workbook fields. Regenerate CSV exports from the authoritative workbook when required.

## Validation

Inspect and render every slot. Test settings-aware playback, image crop, quote and button fit, no raw keys, unique audio, correct trigger, no duplicate fire, and save and reload. Confirm no other slot changed.

Do not mark a package complete when any text, quote, image, audio, slot, licence note, catalogue entry, or runtime consumer remains placeholder or unverified.
