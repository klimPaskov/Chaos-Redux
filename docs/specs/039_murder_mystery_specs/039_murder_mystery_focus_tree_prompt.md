# Focus Tree Implementation Prompt: Event 039 Murder Mystery

Implement the Assassin State focus tree from Part 7 and its integrations across the Event 39 specification package.

## Required reading

Read `AGENTS.md`, `chaos-redux-focus-trees`, `chaos-redux-decisions-missions`, `chaos-redux-events`, the country, unit, AI, asset, terminal, and acceptance parts of this package, relevant offline wiki pages, vanilla focus files and documentation, and the closest Chaos Redux country-tree precedent.

Use `chaosx_focus_tree_auditor` after the first complete implementation. Route complex focus and decision weights through the probability auditor.

## Country and origin gate

The tree loads only for the reserved Event 39 central Assassin State carrier and valid inherited central movement origin. Foreign derivatives use their reduced shared or overlay content, not the full tree. Ordinary countries and Event 19 derivatives must never receive this tree.

## Architecture

Build a compact tree with founding and survival above a central political fork. Keep industry and logistics to the left, military and custom units to the right, intelligence and foreign cells lower left, diplomacy and Veiled Compact lower right, and Evolution V convergence at the bottom center.

The political routes are:

- The Hidden Hand, centralized command and elite coordination
- Cells Without Masters, autonomous cells and resistance
- The Necessary Mask, temporary conventional statehood and industry

Use clean short connectors, no overlapping nodes, no long crossing lines, no fake depth, and no filler focus whose only reward is a tiny modifier.

## Opening play

The first focuses must solve capital survival, supply, captured inventory, first Assassin Cadres, intelligence ownership, and the initial command dispute. Give the player meaningful early choices between front survival, industrial conversion, foreign contacts, and covert operations.

Opening rewards must call real decisions, missions, buildings, stockpiles, units, agency setup, or spirit transformation. Do not give an empty country several passive focuses before it can act.

## Branch requirements

### Political

Each route changes Brotherhood Cohesion, command, succession, subject relations, foreign cells, army use, and terminal preparation. Route choice must not be cosmetic.

### Industry and logistics

Implement clandestine workshops, seized state industry, and pragmatic contracts. Focuses should build, repair, or unlock decisions for factories, railways, supply hubs, infrastructure, anti-air, airbases, ports, resources, transport, and operations-kit production in named valid states.

### Military

Implement Assassin Cadre progression, Saboteur Cells, Shadow Companies, Silent Guard, conventional adaptation, Master Assassins, and Mechanized Assassins. Preserve equipment, technology, cap, Cohesion, and route gates. Do not turn custom units into universal infantry replacements.

### Intelligence and foreign cells

Unlock secure communications, cell contact, selected-target support, foreign operations, revolt preparation, cooperation countermeasures, and network protection. Every foreign action uses an eligible target and bounded cost.

### Diplomacy and subjects

Create the Veiled Compact only at the correct evolution and milestone. Implement subject support, autonomy, campaign sectors, central demands, defection risk, and route-specific faction goals.

### Original host and postwar

Support host-capital objectives, archive seizure, defections, surrender, provisional administration, and staged integration. Do not annex or core the original host through free focus effects.

### Evolution V

Keep the final route hidden before World Collapse and Evolution V. Require a command settlement, then unlock elite systems, leadership dossiers, uprising coordination, administration policy, logistics, and the deliberate World of Anarchy activation action.

The Necessary Mask and other routes may stop without activating the terminal branch. World Collapse creates access, not obligation.

## Spirit lifecycle

Transform the three starting spirits instead of stacking stages:

- Underground Becomes Government
- Cells Under Arms
- Leadership Is a Crime

Each route needs a mature replacement that changes decisions and country play.

## Decisions, characters, and assets

Every major branch must unlock or replace decision families. Route characters and advisors must be explicitly authorized, fictional, compact, and produced through the portrait worker. Every final focus needs a correct focus icon produced for that surface.

Add accurate focus filters and Focus Navigation for founding, politics, industry, military, intelligence, diplomacy, and terminal content. Hidden navigation remains hidden until reveal.

## AI

Implement route plans and tactical priorities from Part 13. AI handles survival and supply before network expansion. It does not train units it cannot reinforce or support revolts it cannot sustain.

## MCP audit loop

Use focus inspection for semantics and references, render the full tree, inspect first-glance readability, rewrite layout when needed, render again, and compare. Repeat until branch families, route locks, mutual exclusions, bypasses, hidden paths, filters, navigation, AI, and payoffs are correct.

A parser-clean but shallow, disconnected, or unreadable tree is not complete.
