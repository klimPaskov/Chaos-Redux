# Murder Mystery Specification Part 4: Evolution Tracks

## Evolution model

Event 39 has five sequential evolutions. Baseline investigation phases are ordinary event progression and must not be recorded as evolutions. Each evolution changes what the movement can do, what the player must manage, and what later content can become available.

Evolution state itself changes no Chaos. Each evolution uses dynamic pacing and its own availability gate. The default base pacing should be close to 90 days after all conditions become valid, with meaningful variation from active murders, investigation success, Network Reach, host weakness, cell maturity, Assassin State performance, and campaign state.

An evolution that is disabled in Event Details cannot set its recorded flag, unlock its content, or block the baseline resolution path. A disabled evolution creates a clean ceiling. The active event continues at the highest enabled state and remains resolvable.

## Prefire evolution handling

Event 39 can be selected after one or more Chaos thresholds are already met. The opening should remain readable and should not fire five evolution popups on the same day.

The initial setup resolves the highest enabled opening intensity that the current world can support, then stages the public reveal:

- at 0 to 199 Chaos, start the baseline investigation
- at 200 to 399 Chaos, start with an early Murder Cult forming after the opening shock
- at 400 to 599 Chaos, start with a mature national cult and seed the first foreign cells after a short evidence-sharing interval
- at 600 to 799 Chaos, start with a compressed investigation and a prepared territorial split that fires only after the player receives the opening and public movement reveal
- at 800 to 999 Chaos, start with the Assassin State and a bounded initial foreign network, then reveal the international movement through normal follow-up events
- at 1000 or more Chaos, start at Evolution IV readiness, preserve the Assassin State war and foreign network, and make Evolution V available through dynamic pacing. Do not activate World of Anarchy automatically

Every compressed opening still records each true evolution once when its behavior becomes active. It does not fabricate dates or repeat the same actor record.

## Evolution I: The Murder Cult

### Availability

- global Chaos at least 200
- Event 39 active in baseline investigation
- original murderer or core support circle not fully captured
- Evolution I enabled
- Network Reach or concrete incident history supports organized followers
- no incompatible terminal state

### Playable change

The crisis gains a national cult layer. Followers provide safe houses, imitate attacks, intimidate witnesses, infiltrate offices, destroy evidence, and help the murderer change methods. The investigation remains national. A complete resolution is still possible.

### New systems

- cell maturity in the original country
- witness intimidation incidents
- evidence destruction missions
- institutional infiltration states
- cult recruitment and mythology pressure
- copycat distinction, so not every murder is directly ordered by the original attacker
- investigator protection as a separate target priority

### Government response

The original host gains actions to map sympathizers, isolate compromised offices, protect investigators, seize records, break recruitment routes, and separate copycats from the central network. A strong agency can still dismantle the cult. Emergency repression may reduce immediate murders while increasing recruitment, evidence loss, and foreign distrust.

### Incident pacing

The murder interval shortens according to Network Reach and cell maturity. The system preserves a country incident cooldown and role cooldowns. Evolution I should feel more dangerous without creating several named deaths in a few days.

### Full resolution

If the original murderer, core cult leadership, and every mature national cell are eliminated before international spread, Event 39 ends permanently. Copycat remnants may produce one aftermath report, but they cannot silently restore the chain.

### Evolution event direction

The public event should communicate that the murderer has gained followers and institutional protection. It should not claim a confirmed ideology, religion, country sponsor, or historical order.

## Evolution II: Cults Across Borders

### Availability

- global Chaos at least 400
- Evolution I active or compressed opening has established equivalent national maturity
- original movement survives
- Evolution II enabled
- Network Reach at the organized-network threshold or a concrete escape and foreign-route incident occurred

### Playable change

The movement becomes international. Foreign cells appear in a bounded set of eligible ordinary countries. Each affected government receives a smaller local case, a protection problem, and access to intelligence sharing. Secondary countries begin with better public knowledge than the original host.

### Cell seeding

Initial foreign cells should be selected from weighted eligible countries. Weight increases with weak counterintelligence, low stability, shared borders or transport routes, diplomatic or commercial access, active conflict, existing unrest, prior cult contact, and proximity to an exposed network route. Weight falls with strong agencies, recent cell immunity, high evidence sharing, and hardened target protection.

Special Chaos countries, actual nonhuman countries, invalid governments, countries with no safe office-casualty path, and countries already carrying an incompatible Event 39 derivative are excluded.

### Secondary investigation

Each foreign cell has Local Case Progress, local maturity, exposure, and a current office threat. Secondary countries receive partial starting progress from shared methods and captured evidence. A country can dismantle its own cell without ending the global chain.

### International cooperation

Countries can share evidence, captured documents, operative access, border routes, and target-protection doctrine. Cooperation improves discovery and reduces reseeding. It can also expose intelligence assets or create political friction if one government used repression, concealed a murder, or manipulated evidence.

### Major assassination news

The death of a national leader, prominent commander, or other major public figure in a secondary country can produce an international news event. Generic office casualties and failed attempts normally use reports or local events.

### Movement survival

Capturing the original murderer after Evolution II lowers Network Reach, damages every cell, and creates an inheritance test. The chain ends only if no mature independent cell can inherit the network. If one or more qualify, one becomes the movement heir after a delay and a public leadership crisis. This inheritance can happen once before Evolution III and once through a country succession after Evolution III. It cannot create an endless chain of new hidden leaders.

## Evolution III: The Assassin Reveals Himself

### Availability

- global Chaos at least 600
- Evolution II or equivalent compressed opening active
- original movement or valid heir survives
- Evolution III enabled
- Network Reach at least 70 or the original host has suffered a mapped sequence of murders, failed raids, and institutional compromise
- original host exists or a valid successor host has inherited the core movement
- dynamic territorial split preflight passes

### Split viability gate

The event delays Evolution III while no valid split exists. It does not create an empty tag, disconnected enclave, one-state remnant with no capital, or movement country without population, supply, and industry. The host selection model strongly favors countries that can support this evolution, but later wars can still invalidate the map.

A delayed split gives the government a final chance to crush the armed underground. Network Reach can continue changing, but Evolution III does not record until state transfer and war creation succeed as one transaction.

### Public reveal

The original murderer or movement heir adopts a public political identity and openly commands an armed movement. The precise personal origin remains unknown. The reveal confirms a leader, organization, objectives, and territorial claim without explaining every earlier murder.

### Country creation

The event creates the Assassin State through the country package in Part 6. A validated connected state cluster transfers from the host. Both countries receive viable capitals, forces, supply, and economic support. War begins through an explicit event-owned war setup after the transfer transaction.

### Investigation transformation

The original host's investigation category becomes a combined counterintelligence and counterinsurgency system. Case Progress is retained as operational intelligence against the movement. Capture of the leader now weakens Cohesion and can alter the war, but it does not delete the country.

Foreign cell investigations continue. Evidence from the new state's territory and battlefield captures can reduce cell maturity or reveal foreign revolt plans.

### Super-event

The successful public creation of a viable Assassin State and the opening war justify a super-event. The reveal should fire after the split is complete and the new country is visible, not before the state transfer.

### Player choice

If the human controls the original host, the player remains with that country by default and receives an explicit option to switch to the Assassin State after setup. If the human controls another country, no forced switch occurs. If the original host is AI and the Assassin State is intended as a playable campaign, the switch option can be offered to the initiating player only through the project multiplayer-safe pattern.

## Evolution IV: The Brotherhood Spreads

### Availability

- global Chaos at least 800
- Assassin State exists and is not defeated
- Evolution IV enabled
- Network Reach at least 80 or the movement has achieved a concrete military and foreign-cell milestone
- at least one foreign cell is active or the state has completed a foreign-network route

### Playable change

Foreign cells can cross from covert crisis into territorial revolt. The Assassin State becomes the center of a faction and subject network. It must balance local military survival, foreign uprising support, subject control, and Brotherhood Cohesion.

### Foreign revolt eligibility

A foreign cell may prepare revolt only when:

- local cell maturity is high
- local government weakness or war disruption is substantial
- local investigation has not dismantled the cell
- a valid connected revolt cluster exists
- one bounded derivative country slot is available
- the target is an ordinary human government
- no incompatible civil war, terminal event, or special country transaction owns the same states
- the parent Assassin State can provide a minimum support package or the cell has local captured capacity

### Subject relationship

A successful foreign revolt creates a reduced Assassin country package, joins the Veiled Compact, and becomes a subject of the central Assassin State. The subject receives starting Assassin units, local recruitment, a compact overlay or shared focus route, and revolt survival decisions. It must not receive the full original tree or every terminal capability.

Subjects can break away if central Cohesion collapses, the original Assassin State is defeated, or a route explicitly grants decentralized autonomy. The movement inheritance rules choose a new central actor only once and only from a viable mature derivative.

### Global response

Ordinary governments gain broader intelligence cooperation, anti-cell support, recognition policy, interdiction, exile handling, and assistance to threatened states. A coalition may form through normal diplomacy if enough countries are affected. Event 39 should connect to existing faction and coalition systems without creating an automatic universal anti-Assassin faction in every campaign.

## Evolution V: A World Without Leaders

### Availability

- global Chaos at least 1000
- Assassin State or valid central movement heir exists
- Evolution V enabled
- movement not in terminal defeat
- World of Anarchy branch enabled in Event Details for automatic access
- no incompatible active world-end state

### Playable change

The central country unlocks the final focus route, advanced unit tiers, synchronized uprising support, expanded infiltration, resistance assistance, subject coordination, and terminal war preparation. Evolution V makes World of Anarchy available. It does not activate it automatically.

### Internal contradiction

The movement's doctrine attacks organized leadership, yet its state, army, intelligence network, and subject system require command. Evolution V turns that contradiction into the central risk. High Brotherhood Cohesion enables global action while raising internal accusations of hierarchy. Low Cohesion satisfies decentralized factions but makes coordinated war and supply fail.

The final route should offer different answers:

- preserve a secret central command and accept ideological hypocrisy
- distribute command to autonomous cells and accept military fragmentation
- create temporary campaign councils whose authority expires after each conquest

All routes can reach the terminal war, but they create different subject behavior, unit access, stability, and victory aftermath.

### Elite capabilities

Evolution V unlocks Master Assassins, larger Shadow Company caps, Silent Guard formations, improved sabotage support, and Mechanized Assassin units. These unlocks use technology, focus, equipment, and Cohesion gates. They do not simply replace the entire army.

## Evolution pacing factors

Factors that shorten evolution pacing include successful named murders, captured government offices, escaped final operations, foreign cell creation, original host instability, high Network Reach, Assassin military victories, subject creation, and failed coalition action.

Factors that lengthen or reverse readiness include captured cell leadership, secured evidence, protected targets, strong intelligence cooperation, low Network Reach, Assassin battlefield defeats, subject defections, low Cohesion, and original host recovery.

Evolution pacing should never become deterministic from the Chaos threshold alone. The threshold makes the evolution possible. The movement must still survive and achieve concrete readiness.

Generic deaths, wars, annexations, puppeting, faction changes, and contamination continue through their shared systems. Event 39 adds Chaos only for the distinct movement consequence that those systems do not already represent.

## Evolution cleanup

Every evolution registers only the content it owns. Disabling, defeat, capture, terminal resolution, or invalidation removes its decisions, missions, flags, targets, country links, subject links, faction links, and scheduled incidents. Lower-stage history remains visible. Cleanup does not erase the fact that an evolution occurred.
