# Event 039 Murder Mystery Research Bibliography

## Research scope

Research was used to improve organizational design, investigation mechanics, cultural safety, terminology, and super-event quote candidates. Historical organizations are design references only. Event 39 remains fictional and does not claim that any real movement, religion, ethnicity, nation, or political philosophy caused its events.

All sources below were accessed on 2026-08-27.

## Cultural and historical safety

### Farhad Daftary, `Assassin`

- Publisher: The Institute of Ismaili Studies
- URL: https://www.iis.ac.uk/scholarly-contributions/assassin/
- Source type: scholarly reference article
- Use in the design: establishes that the European label arose through hostile medieval usage and later became a general word for murderer. It also rejects the popular hashish story as unsupported.
- Specification result: the fictional movement does not use Nizari, Ismaili, Alamut, fida'i, Old Man of the Mountain, hashish, paradise-garden, or related legend imagery as its identity.
- Reliability note: the author is a specialist in Ismaili studies and the page identifies the article's earlier encyclopedic publication.

### Farhad Daftary, `Introduction to The Assassin Legends`

- Publisher: The Institute of Ismaili Studies
- URL: https://www.iis.ac.uk/scholarly-contributions/introduction-to-the-assassin-legends/
- Source type: scholarly historical essay
- Use in the design: explains how polemic, secrecy, European storytelling, and later repetition turned hostile legends into popular history.
- Specification result: mystery is created through fictional evidence, copycats, witnesses, cells, and incomplete investigations. The event does not reuse defamatory legends as factual lore.
- Reliability note: this is a detailed specialist treatment published by an institution focused on Ismaili studies.

### Andrew Fiala, `Anarchism`

- Publisher: Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Fall 2024 archive
- URL: https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2024/entries/anarchism/
- Source type: scholarly philosophy reference
- Use in the design: confirms that anarchism contains varied traditions and extensive internal debate about authority, violence, pacifism, and political action.
- Specification result: World of Anarchy is the fictional movement's chosen title. The event never defines anarchism as leader murder, indiscriminate violence, or the Assassin State's doctrine.
- Reliability note: the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy is an academic reference with expert-authored and reviewed entries.

## Clandestine organization and network design

### `People's Will, the`

- Publisher: Encyclopedia.com reference entry
- URL: https://www.encyclopedia.com/history/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/peoples-will
- Source type: historical encyclopedia entry
- Use in the design: provides a reference for a clandestine revolutionary organization that used specialized functions, underground operations, political murder, and organizational survival after repression.
- Specification result: Event 39 separates support, intelligence, protection, propaganda, operational cells, leadership, and inheritance. It does not copy the historical organization's ideology or targets.
- Reliability note: useful as a secondary reference. Claims that matter to final historical localisation would need a stronger specialist source before use.

### Andrej Mitrovic, `Black Hand`

- Publisher: 1914-1918 Online, International Encyclopedia of the First World War
- URL: https://encyclopedia.1914-1918-online.net/article/black-hand/
- Source type: scholarly historical encyclopedia entry
- Use in the design: provides a reference for covert membership, cross-border support, state penetration, military links, and assassination planning.
- Specification result: foreign cells can benefit from routes, institutional access, unrest, war, and weak counterintelligence. The movement remains fictional and is not mapped onto Serbia or Balkan nationalism.
- Reliability note: the encyclopedia is an academic First World War reference with named authorship and editorial oversight.

### `Records of Special Operations Executive`

- Publisher: The National Archives of the United Kingdom
- URL: https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/details/r/C153
- Source type: official archival catalogue
- Use in the design: establishes a period reference for organized sabotage, subversion, trained agents, support to resistance, and compartmented clandestine work.
- Specification result: the investigation uses agencies, operatives, supply routes, sabotage evidence, raids, witness protection, and counter-network work. The Assassin State's covert systems remain fictional.
- Reliability note: this is the official catalogue for the archival record group.

### `Intelligence and security services`

- Publisher: The National Archives of the United Kingdom
- URL: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/intelligence-and-security-services/
- Source type: official research guide
- Use in the design: corroborates the SOE role and provides a broader map of British intelligence and security records.
- Specification result: intelligence capacity is treated as institutions, operations, agents, and records. It is not reduced to one abstract political-power purchase.
- Reliability note: official research guidance from the archive that holds the records.

## Research limits

The research did not select final audio, final archival images, grounded portrait subjects, historical flags, or final quotations. Those choices require dedicated production research, rights review, source preservation, and manifest evidence.

No source authorizes the project to present a real group as the Event 39 movement. Historical structures were converted into general design questions about cells, secrecy, intelligence, succession, sabotage, infiltration, repression, and organizational survival.
