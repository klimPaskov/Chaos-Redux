Read `AGENTS.md`, `chaos-redux-event-assets` with its portrait-production reference, `chaos-redux-frame-animation`, the full Event 39 spec package, and the exact matching `assets/vanilla_reference/` categories and contact sheets before production. ## Ownership split

Use narrow project workers when available:

- `chaosx_asset_source_researcher` for period non-portrait references and any archival source

## Identity and safety

The movement is fictional. Do not use Nizari Ismaili, Alamut, hashish, Old Man of the Mountain, Islamic, Persian, Syrian, Crusader, real anarchist, real extremist, or generic ninja imagery. Do not depict an identifiable real political leader. Use invented motifs such as an empty chair, cut command knot, broken seal with a hidden thread, absent center, or divided cell network.

Use 1930s to 1940s material culture, clothing, offices, communications, transport, military equipment, and photographic language. No modern tactical gear, fantasy blades, glowing effects, generated text, graphic gore, or borrowed insignia.

Create distinct source art for the narrative image family in Part 14, including opening assassination, emergency succession, failed attempt, evidence preservation, witness loss, cell exposure, capture success, capture failure, Murder Cult, foreign cell, major foreign assassination, Assassin State split, foreign revolt, movement inheritance, central defeat, and remnant cleanup.

Produce the imminent-incident warning family only after confirming a supported native consumer. Transform-only motion, filter-only motion, glow-only frames, one-still repackaging, and runtime GIF are forbidden. If the ordinary category cannot consume the animation, deliver the accepted static state variants and record the blocker. Do not create a custom UI only for this asset.

## Icon families

Create asset-type-specific art for every implemented consumer. At minimum cover:

- one distinct icon per final focus across all branch families

- all visible decision and mission actions

Use the current reference size for each family. Focus icons are normally 94 by 86, ideas 64 by 64, and decisions 32 by 32 when the live references confirm those dimensions. Never crop or resize a focus icon to satisfy an idea, decision, technology, intelligence, or achievement consumer.

For alpha-backed assets, request native transparent ImageGen output, preserve the source alpha, validate over solid and checker backgrounds, and reject white matte, square fill, fake checker pixels, chroma fringe, or transparent holes.

Use native ImageGen through the portrait worker. Lock runtime basename, dimensions, country role, and consumer before generation. Produce PNG and DDS variants, portrait-specific GFX, manifest, and handoff. Avoid real-person resemblance, modern clothing, and generic mask treatment.

## Validation

Review every asset at native in-game size. Verify correct crop, silhouette, alpha, frame order, distinctness, no missing paths, no generated text, no cultural stereotype, and no reuse across unrelated roles. Confirm every final runtime consumer has one final asset and every asset has a live consumer.

Do not deliver placeholders, generic substitutes, locally drawn primitive icons, unverified archival downloads, or assets with no consumer. Mark blocked items directly and explain the missing source, reference, identity, tag, or engine evidence.
