# Occupation revolt: national development after the opening

## Where this architecture applies

A country with a meaningful existing focus tree keeps it and receives the essential restoration campaign through decisions and missions.
A newly activated claimant with an empty or generic tree receives a country-adapted restoration tree built from this architecture.
Safe additive integration into an existing tree is welcome only after its native behavior and layout are verified.
Every essential payoff below must also have a complete decision-and-mission route for countries whose existing tree is preserved.
A missing additive-tree capability cannot remove gameplay from a restored country.

This is a path-level architecture.
Working labels describe focus roles rather than finished focus names.
Final coordinates, icon sprites and localisation belong to implementation after native inspection.
The tree should open with one national-restoration root and three visible priorities: establish command, sustain the population and define the provisional settlement.
Later paths appear when the country has the territory, relationships and resources they need.

Normal substantial focuses use 70 days.
Narrow coordination steps and meaningful route commitments use 35 days.
A 7-day focus is permitted only for an immediate, already-resolved administrative activation that does not hide a major reward behind a token wait.
Do not split a complete institutional reform into several short filler nodes.
Program outcomes should be real unlocks, conversions, repairs, commitments and country changes rather than long rows of small modifiers.

## Command and the first base

The opening command path unifies the provisional seat and the actual initial formations.
Its first 35-day step establishes a common operational authority and the concrete survival mission.
It does not grant another opening army.
Its first choice distinguishes a concentrated defensible base from support for several already viable centers.
The second choice exists only with Evolution II or a genuine already divided surviving country.

Concentration prioritizes the seat, supply hub and immediate approaches.
Its payoff is a supported logistics and command program that removes the initial coordination burden after the survival mission succeeds.
It can complete with a small homeland base rather than requiring every national core.
The distributed path builds the command agreement between actual centers and makes their connection missions available.
Its payoff is coordination of real forces and supply, with no duplicated resources.

The follow-on path regularizes local defense formations when the country has the equipment and officers to do so.
Prepared Underground can accelerate its organization component, but cannot bypass the material bill.
A country that already has a functioning regular army uses this path to integrate new local formations rather than receiving redundant templates and doctrine bonuses.

## Legal continuity

This political route is available when a credible national government or valid legal successor survives.
Its first 35-day commitment recognizes that government's authority in the restored homeland.
The route then restores the actual administrative institutions needed for taxation, appointments and civilian services over a 70-day program.
It must distinguish missing institutions from institutions that survived abroad.

Its principal fork concerns local resistance authority.
The government can integrate local commands through a defined national settlement or preserve a stricter central chain while accepting a continuing obligation to resolve local representation.
Integration requires a 70-day political and military agreement and allows eligible local officers and formations to enter the supported national structure.
Centralization can complete the command phase sooner, but cannot reach the highest Cohesion benefits while the local settlement remains unresolved.

The capstone is a functioning national administration with a fulfilled homecoming agreement.
It removes the emergency-administration burden and preserves the country's existing political identity.
Its follow-on development deals with reconstruction, existing constitutional institutions and the remaining occupied homeland.
It must not replay completed pre-annexation political focuses or restore a regime the player had already replaced.

## The resistance compact

This route is available to a local movement or a government willing to share provisional authority with its genuine domestic partners.
The opening 35-day commitment establishes the participants and their actual institutional roles.
The next 70-day program settles national command and the distribution of administrative responsibility.
The choice is grounded in the existing political culture and cannot be a universal election event for every ideology.

Its principal fork concerns the relationship between regional initiative and national standardization.
A locally anchored compact preserves meaningful regional participation and makes prepared-region integration easier once its conditions are fulfilled.
A standardized national compact invests more time in a unified administration and army, reducing future coordination disputes.
Neither route grants universal free cores or removes all political disagreement.

The capstone is a ratified national agreement with its agreed representation actually established.
It removes the emergency burden and opens the postwar development paths.
The continuing obligation is to honor that agreement during appointments, aid negotiations and demobilization.
A breach creates the specific Cohesion consequence defined in Part 05.
The tree does not manufacture a rival country for each dissatisfied participant.

## Emergency military authority

This route requires a genuine immediate military threat or a documented inability to coordinate the opening through ordinary institutions.
Its initial 35-day commitment establishes the emergency command.
It then concentrates the current army and administration on the survival objective.
Its practical benefit is the 35-day reduction to the initial command-coordination program specified in Part 05.
It does not increase every division's combat power indefinitely.

After the survival objective, the route branches into a return to legal continuity or a negotiated resistance compact.
The player has 180 days to complete or materially commit the review settlement.
A 70-day transition program honors the accepted emergency mandate and converts its institutions into the chosen government.
Keeping emergency authority beyond that deadline is an explicit political choice with the recorded Cohesion loss and unresolved mandate issue.

The capstone is either a fulfilled transfer of authority or a deliberately prolonged emergency government facing its consequences.
The latter is not an automatic fascist route and does not spawn a generic civil war.
Country-specific existing political systems determine what further contest can develop.
No unique ideology must be added solely to make the branch look different.

## Civilian administration and rebuilding

This path begins with a 35-day assessment of the recovered territory's actual administrative and material needs.
The assessment selects concrete recovery targets and does not itself repair buildings or distribute free relief.
A country with intact local services can bypass already satisfied repair requirements without collecting their rewards.

The principal fork is between concentrated restoration of the main national center and a dispersed basic-services program across the current base.
The concentrated program restores a real damaged administrative, industrial or supply center through 70–140 days of work and native construction progress.
The dispersed program focuses on usable transport and basic access in several poorer states, with progress tied to actual state improvements and relief receipts.
The country cannot receive both complete program rewards by repeatedly switching the target.

The capstone removes the justified emergency recovery burden after the selected essential conditions are achieved.
The follow-on path rehabilitates national production and civilian return where recorded damage and displacement exist.
There is no mandatory artificial famine, mass migration or destruction event to make these focuses useful.
When there is no such damage, the country instead develops ordinary national services and productive capacity through supported construction and technology programs.

## Industry and procurement

A restored industrial country should rebuild and reorganize what exists.
A poor rural claimant should gain credible access to procurement, workshops and future investment.
Neither receives a fixed number of factories per state.
The opening path identifies usable military industry, available civilian construction capacity and external procurement possibilities.

The domestic production fork commits to repairing or expanding a small number of genuinely useful sites.
Its 70-day preparatory focus unlocks a specific construction program, whose completion requires actual building progress and any supported material commitments.
Its payoff is the completed facility and a viable equipment-production path.
A pre-existing undamaged factory does not count as a newly built reward.

The external procurement fork establishes a documented agreement with a real supplier.
Its payoff is access to supported deliveries, licenses or limited technology assistance with concrete terms.
It cannot buy unlimited equipment through a flat political-power decision.
The capstone is a sustainable national supply arrangement, whether domestic, imported or mixed.
It supports the army-development and reconstruction paths without requiring the country to become economically self-sufficient immediately.

## The national army

The military path has three substantial programs: integrating surviving formations, training new national units and developing geographically appropriate specialist capabilities.
Integrating survivors preserves actual experience and equipment.
Training expands capability only as recruits, officers and material become available.
Specialist development depends on real terrain and military need.

A mountainous homeland can develop the supported mountain-defense profile.
A large mobile front can develop transport and cavalry or motorized support where equipment exists.
A coastal country can develop port defense and the means to support maritime supply.
These are conditional route families rather than mandatory new battalion types.
The exact supported equipment and technology definitions must be read before authoring their game implementation.

The principal army fork distinguishes a compact professional core with integrated local reserves from a broader mobilized army that needs more equipment and administrative capacity.
The professional route gives a manageable training and command program and leaves more native manpower uncommitted.
The mobilized route can field more ordinary formations when real resources permit, but increases the government's demobilization and supply obligations.
The payoff is a real force structure, not a permanent abstract attack bonus that ignores equipment.

The capstone recognizes a functioning national military establishment after its concrete force, equipment and command objectives are met.
It removes obsolete emergency force restrictions and hands routine expansion back to native recruitment and production.
Temporary units are integrated or demobilized according to the chosen settlement.

## Maritime and air access

This path is conditional on relevant geography and surviving capability.
An island or coastal claimant first secures a real port and a supported transport arrangement.
The principal choice is between relying on protected external shipping and developing a limited independent maritime capability from actual vessels, industry and technology.
The former has diplomatic obligations.
The latter has a longer material program.

A surviving national air force can return through real access and basing arrangements.
A new air arm requires actual aircraft, trained personnel and a usable airfield.
A 70-day coordination focus can unlock the relevant procurement and return programs, but does not create aircraft from an airfield.
The capstone is a sustained route or functioning service appropriate to the country's scale.
An inland country without a relevant aviation base does not receive an empty naval or air branch merely to complete a template.

## Foreign relations and the national frontier

The diplomacy path starts with identifying the governments that recognize the claimant and the actual terms of its support.
Its principal fork is between a strong security alignment and greater freedom of action through several limited agreements.
Alignment can improve access to reliable material and defense commitments while accepting the explicit obligations of the alliance.
The diversified path preserves room to negotiate but requires more work to keep supply and guarantees credible.

The restoration-frontier branch concerns remaining authentic home states.
It can organize remaining regions, negotiate restoration, coordinate with allies or pursue supported war goals when diplomacy fails.
A war goal is not a promise of a safely isolated war.
The implementation must account for guarantees, factions, subjects and current wars before presenting the consequences.
The tree does not automatically escalate national restoration into annexing the occupier's entire homeland.

Its capstone is a settled national frontier or an explicitly chosen continuing claim.
A peaceful partial settlement can be a successful route.
A country that recovers every home state through ordinary war must not be forced to declare another war merely to satisfy a focus prerequisite.
Already achieved territorial requirements bypass the acquisition task without repeating its reward.

## Postwar institutions and veterans

This final route connects the governing settlement, army structure, support terms and reconstruction program.
A 70-day program establishes the ordinary national administration after the emergency.
A second substantial program resolves the actual temporary military arrangements and fulfilled foreign obligations.
These should be integrated branches with a few meaningful junctions, not a separate mandatory focus for every checklist item.

The principal choice concerns the permanent place of the resistance in national life.
One route preserves it through supported veteran and reserve institutions.
Another prioritizes full integration into the ordinary military and civil administration.
Both can honor the people involved without adding invented real characters or automatic demographic rewards.
The capstone is a stable ordinary country whose event-owned crisis obligations have genuinely been settled.

## Shared rules for implementation

Keep at most three tree-created national spirits active, consolidating or replacing stages rather than stacking them.
Branch payoffs must have one-shot receipts so reconquest, tag restoration, bypass or focus reload cannot repeat factories, equipment or political rewards.
Use accurate focus filters and native navigation for a large tree.
Reveal conditional branches from actual geography and state, not from undisclosed arbitrary progress meters.

Every country profile needs a route-coverage review that proves politics, industry, military capability, diplomacy and postwar continuation are playable.
Existing-tree countries need the corresponding decision-and-mission coverage rather than a claim that their old tree already covers the new event.
Native focus inspection and rendering are required before layout or AI behavior is reported as validated.
No such implementation or render is claimed by this planning package.
