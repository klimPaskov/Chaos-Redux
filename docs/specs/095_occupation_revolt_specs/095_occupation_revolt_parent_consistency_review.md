## Findings resolved in this package

### Dormant progression lacked a concrete completion path

The first draft described underground activity and an opening threshold but left a nonexistent country's means of reaching it too implicit.
Part 03 now specifies three finite 35-day local milestones, their actual prerequisites, one-shot organization/Readiness changes and a bounded disrupted-network recovery option.
Ordinary successful values reach local organization 50 and Readiness 50 after 105 days within the preparation window.
The material milestone authorizes the same finite opening cache rather than creating two separate stocks.
An occupier can interrupt actual requirements, and failure does not renew the window automatically.

### A bounded Readiness decrease could have produced an excessive refund

The first Evolution II draft removed a nominal 10 points and restored 10 after the new center's connection objective.
At very low Readiness, the lower bound could have made the actual removal smaller.
Part 08 now records the actual bounded decrease and restores only that amount once.
This closes the arithmetic mismatch without weakening the connection objective.
The Evolution I pre-opening cap is also explicit that a bonus cannot lower an already higher evidence-based Readiness value.

### National Readiness and local organization were conflated in one AI sentence

The candidate-scoring description incorrectly described a population-weighted local network condition as Readiness 60.
Part 12 now explicitly uses population-weighted local network organization reaching 60.
It does not add another public meter or imply that candidate scores are probabilities.

### Focus and decision access needed an explicit payment-parity distinction

An early sentence could have been read as demanding identical native focus opportunity costs and decision prices.
Part 10 and the national-program registry now distinguish interface opportunity cost from the same material bill and one-shot result.
The same explicit setup fee cannot be charged twice when the player changes interfaces.
Native focus spending never implies that an equipment or construction requirement has been paid.

### Chaos scaling needed a boundary when evolutions are disabled

Part 04 now states that current Chaos affects military opportunity through enabled earned capabilities and the actual military situation.
It is not an uncapped independent multiplier generating new resources while every evolution is disabled.
This preserves the baseline uprising and all 16 enabled/disabled combinations without granting disabled benefits through another formula.

### Anti-farming needed observable conditions

The initial repeat-rule wording referred too loosely to player-arranged transitions.
Part 14 now relies on observable release, dependency, diplomatic annexation, actual independent hostile war, sovereignty and debug receipts.
It does not pretend the script can infer player intent.
A genuinely independent later conquest remains eligible when its actual conditions justify a new incident.
