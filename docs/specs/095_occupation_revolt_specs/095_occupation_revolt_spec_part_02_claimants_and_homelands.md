# Occupation revolt: claimants and the homeland

## The national identity record

Each candidate has one canonical country identity, one authentic homeland profile and a record of its current sovereign position.
Use existing country identities and the established country-origin registries before adding an event-specific profile.
An Event 006-created country keeps its recorded origin rather than being rediscovered as a different nation.
An ordinary historical tag remains itself after annexation.
A cosmetic name or flag change does not create a second claimant.

The homeland profile records accepted home states, a national center, alternate seats, and any supported regional distinction.
Native cores are the ordinary territorial evidence.
Existing country-origin records may clarify which cores belong to a created national identity.
A core granted by an unrelated worldwide expansion effect is not sufficient evidence that a distant conquest became this country's original lost homeland.
Claims without cores require an explicit existing national-origin justification and cannot silently acquire the force of a core.
The profile must record the reason for each nonstandard inclusion.

Do not use present population to infer ethnicity.
Population measures how many people and resources are at stake.
Support is evaluated separately.
Overlapping cores establish a dispute, not unanimity for either claimant.
A native owner's core and a dormant claimant's core can coexist without automatically making all residents of the state insurgents.

## Primary candidates

The primary pool has three routes.
An active government in exile qualifies when an authentic home state is held by a hostile foreign controller and a viable opening can be assembled.
A fully annexed country qualifies when its identity remains valid and its homeland has a meaningful opening opportunity.
A surviving country outside its homeland qualifies when it has suffered a national displacement rather than a minor border loss.

For the third route, use the following draft calibration.
The claimant must have lost at least 75% of the population in its authentic homeland, or have lost its recorded national center together with at least 50% of that homeland's population.
The current capital is not automatically the national center, because a displaced government may already have moved its capital abroad.
These thresholds are design choices for this rework, not claims about existing engine rules.
A primary candidate must still pass geographic viability, identity, conflict and resource checks.

A country with a minor occupied border region remains eligible for ordinary resistance and its existing war goals, but is not treated as a nation returning from national displacement.
A returning country that has already secured its national center and a majority of its homeland does not repeatedly qualify merely because one distant home state remains occupied.
A new qualifying displacement can reopen eligibility after the previous incident closes.

## Dormant candidates

The fallback pool is considered only after the complete suitable primary pool is empty.
It does not require the world to be at peace.
A dormant claimant must already exist as an authentic country identity with a supported homeland inside another country.
The local evidence must support a nationalist movement of that identity, rather than an arbitrary ideological civil war.

The dormant route begins with underground activity.
No map territory, conventional army or completed national government is granted at selection.
Its preparation record can live with the incident registry until the country is activated.
The occupier and any genuine external sponsor can interact with their own relevant decisions.
A nonexistent claimant cannot be expected to click national decisions or pay political power that it does not possess.

An integrated, peaceful region can still be selected as a dormant political movement when the identity is authentic, but must build organization through the slower preparation path.
Absence of native resistance in an owner's core state is recorded as unavailable occupation evidence.
It is not replaced with invented 50% resistance.
No compatible claimant means no firing rather than a fabricated nation.

## Controller, owner and enemy

Every candidate state records its current owner and current controller separately.
Military occupation and annexation are different legal situations.
A claimant-owned state held by its enemy normally needs restoration of control rather than a new sovereign ownership award.
An annexed home state needs the correct restoration of both sovereignty and territorial control.
The exact engine path for each case must be verified before implementation.

Friendly allied control does not automatically constitute hostile occupation.
An allied army liberating the claimant's territory should use the ordinary return process and cannot be attacked merely to satisfy the event.
If a friendly power refuses restoration under a genuine conflicting political arrangement, that becomes a diplomatic dispute with explicit state evidence.
It is not inferred from controller inequality alone.

Where several occupiers divide a homeland, record each actual opposition relationship.
The baseline opens against the occupier of the selected base.
It may support cells under the others without secretly placing the claimant at war with every controller at once.
Evolution II may coordinate multiple openings when the political and military consequences are committed explicitly.
A guarantor, faction member, subject or overlord enters only through a verified relationship or an explicitly accepted intervention.

## Local assessment

For each authentic home state, assess identity strength, underground organization, native resistance where applicable, compliance where applicable, local administrative weakness, command and supply access, military geography, population and industry.
Major cities can provide political legitimacy and industrial capacity while also being heavily defended.
Mountains or forests can support survival without supplying the weapons of an industrial city.
A port is valuable only when a usable external route exists or can plausibly be contested.

Use qualitative bands in the player view: unsupported, organizing, prepared and ready to join.
The implementation may retain normalized internal scores, but must show the concrete reasons behind an assessment.
An unobserved input remains unknown.
It must not be silently filled with a favorable value.
A large current garrison or nearby combat army reduces the chance of a bloodless territorial breakaway without erasing the national claim.

## Selecting the first territorial base

Choose the strongest viable seed among the claimant's eligible home states, allowing bounded weighted variation between comparably suitable choices.
Build outward through land adjacency within the eligible home-state graph.
Prefer a defensible connection to another national region, a supply center, a port with a real route, or an allied front.
Population, organization and military resources determine how much territory can join at once.
There is no rule that the initial number of states must be one, five, half the homeland, or all cores.

The draft opening population-share target is `10% + 40% × Readiness/100`, before local readiness and administrative-control constraints.
This provides a 10–50% target rather than a guaranteed transfer.
Select a connected set whose local organizations can support the opening and whose armed force can plausibly contest it.
State granularity may exceed the target when the smallest viable homeland component consists of one large state.
That exception must not generate extra manpower or equipment.

A strong, broadly prepared homeland facing an administrative collapse may exceed the normal target.
This is a documented collapse opening, permitted when at least 75% of eligible home population is locally ready and the relevant occupier is capitulated or cannot maintain effective control of those states.
It may reach the full viable homeland without a fixed state cap.
An intact hostile field army still requires military contest.
The exception does not transfer peaceful third-party holdings.

Do not make a land corridor through unrelated states to satisfy contiguity.
A maritime link can support two parts of a country without pretending they are land-adjacent.
At baseline, select the most viable component when disconnected regions cannot coordinate.
Evolution II may activate additional components through their own local preparation and share of the national military budget.

## Capitals and inherited territory

The first seat is the safest usable political and supply center in the opened territory.
A historic capital is preferred when it is available and defensible.
A provisional seat remains legitimate when the historic capital is still occupied.
Changing the seat cannot create factories, victory points, supply nodes or population.
The national restoration objective can still refer to the historic center without forcing a suicidal early attack.

Existing overseas possessions remain attached to the surviving claimant under ordinary game rules.
They are not part of the uprising's opening-state list and receive no local-volunteer allocation from it.
Their real surviving stocks, industry and military units can support the war through normal transfer and supply routes.
Their civilian population is not reclassified as part of the reclaimed homeland merely to inflate revolt strength.

## Eligibility exclusions and revalidation

Exclude placeholder, invalid or unsupported identities and identities already represented by a live overlapping Event 095 incident.
Use the shared actual-nonhuman classifier for civilian eligibility rather than excluding all unusual human countries.
A human national movement under a special occupier remains human.
A nonhuman conqueror does not justify a generic human claimant or automatically permit seizure of unrelated territory.

Immediately before the opening, revalidate identity, owner, controller, conflict, capital placement, territory and resources.
If the map changed, rebuild the opening from the remaining valid home states.
If an ally already restored the homeland, close the preparation as overtaken by events without spawning another army.
If the seed became invalid and no coherent alternative exists, preserve any legitimate surviving underground record and release the event slot.
Do not report an armed revolt that did not occur.
