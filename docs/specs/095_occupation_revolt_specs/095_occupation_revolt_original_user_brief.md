# Occupation revolt

## Catalog entry

- Event ID: `095`
- Event name: Occupation revolt
- Type: Minor Repeatable
- Status: To Be Reworked
- Chaos level: 1
- Cluster: Liberations
- Cluster role: Medium member

## Core idea

> A country that has lost its homeland suddenly rises again from inside occupied or annexed territory.

Each firing searches for a valid displaced country whose homeland is currently controlled by another country.

This can be an active government in exile, a country that still exists elsewhere, or a country that was completely annexed but still has a valid national identity and homeland.

The selected country launches an armed revolt inside its own territory and returns to the map.

If no suitable occupation exists, the event can instead use a dormant claimant whose homeland lies inside another country and begin a new nationalist uprising there.

## Baseline revolt

The revolt uses the selected country's actual homeland rather than a fixed rebel region.

The opening territory is selected dynamically from states where the claimant has the strongest basis.

Important influences can include:

- cores
- population
- resistance
- compliance
- major cities
- industry
- terrain
- local organization
- weakness of the occupying country

The revolt should normally create a coherent territorial base when the geography allows it.

The number of states joining the revolt is dynamic. A strong underground movement can begin across a large part of the homeland, while a weak one can begin from a much smaller regional base.

## Rebel strength

The new or restored country receives forces based on the actual uprising.

Strength can scale with:

- population of the revolting territory
- local industry
- resistance strength
- existing exiled military organization
- foreign support
- occupier strength
- current war situation
- current Chaos

Possible forces can range from irregular militias to organized regular formations when the claimant has enough support and resources.

There is no universal rebel army size.

## Government in exile

If the country still has a government in exile or surviving national leadership, that government returns with the uprising.

Its previous political identity and surviving leaders remain relevant.

A completely annexed country can be restored from its existing country identity rather than being replaced by a generic rebel state.

## Dormant claimant route

If no suitable occupied or annexed country exists, Event 095 can select a valid dormant claimant with a real territorial basis inside another country.

The claimant begins through underground nationalist activity and can then break into open revolt.

Possible signs before the uprising include:

- nationalist demonstrations
- illegal flags
- underground newspapers
- missing weapons
- desertions
- attacks on local authorities

The event should not invent a completely new country identity solely because no better candidate exists.

## Revolt spread

A successful uprising can spread into more of the claimant's homeland.

States become more likely to join when the rebels are nearby, resistance is strong, occupation control is weak, or the uprising is clearly succeeding.

A revolt that is being crushed should have much less ability to trigger further breakaways.

## Foreign support

Other countries can support the uprising when the current diplomatic situation gives them a reason to do so.

Possible supporters include allies of the displaced country, enemies of the occupier, neighboring states, ideological partners, and regional rivals.

Support can strengthen the revolt through equipment, volunteers, recognition, or other useful assistance.

## Defeat and later revolts

If the revolt is defeated, the national identity can survive underground or in exile.

Because Event 095 is repeatable, the same country can rise again later if conditions once again favor an uprising.

A previous failed revolt can influence how prepared the next attempt is.

# Evolutions

## Evolution I: Prepared Underground

- Chaos requirement: `200+`.

Resistance organizations become much better prepared before the uprising begins.

Revolts can start with more territory, stronger local organization, better weapons, and more organized officers.

## Evolution II: Uprising on Every Front

- Chaos requirement: `400+`.

A single national revolt can begin from several separate parts of the homeland at the same time.

This is especially important for large countries, islands, separated regions, and homelands divided by occupation.

Each uprising center is still based on the actual geography and local strength of the claimant.

## Evolution III: The Army Breaks

- Chaos requirement: `600+`.

Parts of the occupation structure can defect when the revolt begins.

Locally recruited troops, security forces, equipment, officers, or other occupation assets can join the restored country when support for the claimant is strong enough.

A weakly controlled occupation can therefore collapse much faster than a stable one.

## Evolution IV: Liberation Cascade

- Chaos requirement: `800+`.

A major successful uprising can inspire other displaced countries under the same occupier or in nearby regions.

Additional valid national movements can begin preparing their own revolts.

Each new claimant still uses its own homeland, support, and military situation rather than receiving a copy of the original uprising.

A large empire holding many annexed countries can therefore face a chain of restoration wars.

## Connections

**Independence Wave** can create countries that later become valid Occupation Revolt claimants if they are conquered or annexed again.

**Subjects Break Free** can create similar liberation pressure, but Occupation Revolt focuses on countries or national identities that have already lost control of their homeland.

Wars, annexations, occupations, and other Chaos Redux territorial events naturally change which countries can become valid revolt candidates later.
