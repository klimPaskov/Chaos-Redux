# Occupation revolt: the underground before the front

## A movement with a local shape

The underground consists of regional networks that belong to the selected national claimant.
A network records its home state, local contacts, organization, material stores, exposure and relationship to the claimant's leadership.
These records describe the preparation of a specific revolt.
They are not an additional global resistance system competing with native resistance or Event 006.
Existing local resistance, a previous uprising, an exile organization and proven foreign assistance can provide starting evidence.

Readiness summarizes the ability of the active movement to coordinate an opening.
Its initial calibration is 25 for a newly activated dormant movement, 40 for a displaced claimant with scattered surviving networks, 60 for an organized surviving national movement, and 75 for a movement with strong local organization and a functioning external liaison.
These values are starting bands, not guaranteed assignments by country status.
Evidence can move a claimant down a band when its institutions exist on paper but its local links are broken.
An annexed country is not automatically less prepared than a nominal government in exile.

Native resistance is useful evidence of hostility to occupation, but does not by itself prove weapons, a national command structure or agreement on a government.
Compliance can reduce willingness to join immediately without deleting the national identity.
A political movement in a core state with no native occupation interface uses its own documented local preparation and does not invent resistance statistics.

## The dormant preparation interval

A dormant claimant receives a preparation window with a nominal target of 150 days.
The actual window is selected in the 120–180 day band according to local organization, communication difficulty and occupier control.
A short extension of up to 60 days is possible only when a concrete remaining operation is already underway, such as a registered supply delivery or the completion of a regional liaison network.
Repeated extensions cannot keep an inactive claimant occupying a crisis slot indefinitely.

There are two material prerequisites for an armed opening: a viable prepared base and enough verified weapons and recruits to form a defensible initial force.
A third prerequisite is political: at least one viable national command arrangement must exist.
The movement can use a provisional local command without a complete postwar constitution.
The preparation mission succeeds when these prerequisites are met, rather than merely when its timer expires.

If the prerequisites are absent at expiry, the movement remains a dormant national organization and the active preparation closes.
Spent equipment remains spent or cached according to its actual receipt.
Arrested or lost personnel remain lost to the organization under their actual disposition.
There is no automatic civil war at a fixed day and no refund for a political gamble that genuinely failed.
A charge for an operation that became impossible before any delivery or work occurred is handled separately through the exact transaction refund rules.

## The dormant movement's actual progress

The dormant route progresses through finite local milestones because there is no living claimant to operate national decisions.
At selection, its initial local organization and national Readiness normally begin at 25, with the evidence adjustments described above.
The first 35-day milestone establishes local contacts and an initial network.
Successful completion adds 15 local organization and 10 Readiness once.
The second 35-day milestone establishes the provisional command agreement and adds 10 local organization and 5 Readiness once.
It also records the agreed governing participants and adds 10 Cohesion once when the agreement is actually settled.
The third 35-day milestone completes the finite initial material-preparation assessment and adds 10 Readiness once if a viable cache authorization or real delivered inventory exists.
At the ordinary undisturbed values this produces local organization 50 and Readiness 50 after 105 days, leaving time within the normal window for a real opening or a limited response to disruption.

These milestones are requirements with possible failure, not automatic rewards for waiting.
A milestone cannot complete while its local network is destroyed, its command agreement is unresolved, or its required material source is absent.
The material milestone authorizes the same finite cache counted by the opening transaction.
It does not create a second pre-opening stockpile grant and then repeat that grant after the country activates.
Actual foreign deliveries can replace missing material capacity but retain their own donor and arrival receipts.

A surviving disrupted network can undertake one 35-day local rebuilding milestone within the current preparation window.
It restores at most 15 of the organization actually lost during this preparation and adds at most 5 Readiness toward the pre-disruption level.
This consumes the movement's finite remaining preparation effort and does not renew the overall deadline or generate new equipment.
Further reconstruction needs a new material contribution or a later legitimately qualified incident.
The occupier therefore has a real opportunity to interrupt the progression, while an unopposed authentic dormant movement has a complete route to the map.

## Warning and intelligence

Early signs appear as a small chain of concrete local developments.
Underground newspapers and prohibited national symbols indicate political organization.
Missing military stores or verified desertions indicate a more immediate armed risk.
Attacks on local authorities require an actual prepared cell and affect that locality, rather than assigning arbitrary nationwide casualties.
The presentation names the affected place and the type of evidence.
It does not reveal every covert state or print the claimant's exact hidden values to its enemy.

The ordinary dormant opening must give the occupier at least one actionable warning while there is still time to choose a response.
The draft warning threshold is Readiness 40 or the first confirmed arms operation, whichever produces credible evidence first.
Warnings are based on what was detected.
A completely covert action does not produce omniscient intelligence.

A displaced government can launch a sudden baseline revolt when the opening is already prepared.
It is not forced into a long new warning phase simply because Event 095 selected it today.
For fairness, the opening report immediately identifies the actual lost region and available responses.
The surprise comes from pre-existing preparation and occupation weakness, not from silently deleting the occupier's frontline army.

## Organizing a base

The claimant can concentrate on one eligible local base at a time during the initial phase.
The Organize Region action from Part 10 requires an authentic local network and a realistic communication link.
For an existing country, the draft political cost is 25 political power and the work takes 35 days.
Successful completion raises the target network's organization by 15 and national Readiness by 5, subject to the local organization ceiling.
The operation may be repeated only while it adds real missing organization, and cannot farm Cohesion or permanent national rewards.

A dormant country does not pay this political cost from a nonexistent treasury.
Its first network develops through the finite initial preparation allocation and local political activity.
An existing foreign sponsor may fund an equivalent operation from its own resources after establishing a liaison.
The sponsor does not become owner of the national movement or its country tag.

Choosing a second region before the first base is viable divides available organizers and stores.
At baseline, it improves later spread rather than opening an additional disconnected front on demand.
Evolution II removes that coordination restriction once each selected center has its own viable share of the national force.

## Stores, donors and exposure

The movement's material record distinguishes accumulated local caches, equipment supplied by the surviving claimant, delivered foreign equipment and captured or defecting occupation assets.
Every source has a finite amount.
Equipment already counted in a cache cannot also remain in the donor's active stockpile.
An exposed cache may be seized, moved or lost only once.

Local stockpiling is represented as accumulated underground preparation over the occupation period.
The initial abstract cache is a bounded opening allocation described in Part 04.
After that allocation, new stores come from real national production, actual donations or a specifically completed local procurement operation with a finite capacity and period of work.
A second firing cannot repeat the original accumulation grant as though the previous cache had never existed.

External delivery is a strategic game transaction, not a real-world tactical procedure.
Its route is evaluated through supported land, sea or air access.
An intercepted shipment is removed from transit and resolved into verified delivered, captured, destroyed or returned amounts.
It cannot simultaneously equip the claimant and reward the occupier.
Detection raises the local qualitative exposure assessment and can reveal the receiving region.
It does not automatically identify every other network.

## Preserving a network under pressure

When the occupier exposes a prepared network, the claimant chooses whether to disperse it, relocate its surviving organizers, or accelerate the opening.
Dispersal lowers local organization by 15 and national Readiness by 5, but preserves the surviving network and eligible unseized stores.
Relocation requires an actual valid home region and a supported route.
Only surviving people and movable equipment arrive.
Accelerating the opening commits the force available now and accepts the smaller base or poorer equipment that follows from early action.

The occupier can reduce local pressure through lawful administration, negotiated political concessions and reliable relief where those actions are actually delivered.
A military security operation can disrupt an exposed network when adequate forces are present.
Neither route deletes the claimant's cores or national identity.
A cheap universal suppression button cannot set every home state to zero readiness.

Political concessions can close the active dormant preparation without war when the agreement genuinely resolves the current demand and has sufficient local acceptance.
The settlement records its terms and the resulting status of the claimant.
A symbolic promise with no territorial, institutional or administrative effect cannot count as a permanent settlement.
Breaking an agreement can become evidence for a later preparation, but does not instantly summon a new army.

## Leadership and disagreement before liberation

An exiled government can claim legal continuity while local organizers claim authority earned through resistance.
The event treats these as interests within the same national identity unless an existing separate civil-war system explicitly owns a split.
It does not create a second copy of the country to represent every disagreement.

A claimant starts with Cohesion 60 when a credible surviving government and local command have an agreed relationship.
Use 45 when the local movement recognizes the national cause but disputes who should govern.
Use 30 when an unresolved sponsor-backed or internal leadership conflict is already documented.
These are draft design calibrations and require contextual evidence.
Dormant claimants begin with a provisional local agreement at Cohesion 50 unless their country profile supplies a genuine conflict.

An early leadership conference costs time and political attention but can establish the command settlement before open war.
For a living claimant, it costs 25 political power and takes 35 days.
It grants 10 Cohesion after the agreed representation is actually established.
For a dormant claimant, the equivalent agreement is a preparation milestone rather than a paid decision.
The same conference cannot be replayed for repeated Cohesion gains.
Later changes of government replace the agreement and carry their own consequences.

## What survives a failed preparation

The national identity survives unless another owner system genuinely transforms or removes that identity.
The event records which local networks survived, which stores were retained, which leaders remain available and why the attempt ended.
It stores surviving capacity rather than a permanent positive modifier for having failed.
A quiet, integrated region can lose active revolutionary momentum while retaining a historical claimant.

A new attempt normally requires 180 days after closure and a material change in conditions.
Examples include renewed foreign delivery, a new occupation war, a real military setback of the occupier, or rebuilt local organization after losses.
A previous survivor's experience can improve initial Readiness by at most 10 over the equivalent current evidence band.
This benefit does not stack per defeat.
Actual surviving stores and formations matter more than the number of failed attempts.
