# Occupation revolt: who governs the restored country

## Continuity before replacement

The restored country uses its existing national identity.
A living government returns as that government, including its current ideology, valid leader, surviving institutions and actual diplomatic relationships.
The event must not reload an old historical setup over later player choices.
An annexed country resumes valid persistent state where available and receives only the missing elements needed for a functioning country.
A dormant claimant uses the existing country package associated with its identity and homeland.

A previous country name, leader portrait or flag is not evidence that every former law, party relationship and foreign guarantee still applies.
The restoration record distinguishes surviving facts from expired arrangements.
Dead, removed or reassigned characters remain unavailable.
Country-specific naming, language, symbols and historical institutions should come from the established profile rather than a universal fictional restoration cabinet.

## The emergency government

At the armed opening, the country must have a valid seat, a functioning command authority and usable national systems.
It does not need to settle every constitutional question before defending itself.
The emergency arrangement has three possible origins: returning national institutions, a locally organized provisional authority, or a joint agreement between the two.
The origin changes who initially expects influence over appointments, military command and the political settlement.
It does not automatically change the country's ideology.

The emergency administration uses at most one consolidated event-owned national spirit.
Its design purpose is to represent the burden of governing a contested homeland.
It can apply a temporary 10% stability penalty and a 10% construction-speed penalty while basic administration is being restored.
The penalties are draft tuning values and must be tested against weak countries before shipping.
They are not applied to every overseas state of a surviving country when the actual burden is confined to newly recovered territory.
Prefer a state-scoped recovery burden in that case.
No penalty may make the essential survival and negotiation actions unavailable.

Successful basic administration removes the emergency burden.
It does not leave a permanent national disadvantage merely to keep the event visible.
A renewed occupation may create a new real burden, but cannot stack several copies of the same emergency spirit.
Across the event's focus and decision package, no more than three event-owned national spirits may be active at once.

## Cohesion as an agreement, not universal popularity

Cohesion represents working agreement among the participants needed for national restoration.
It does not claim that the government is democratic, morally legitimate or universally popular.
An authoritarian country can have a cohesive command structure.
A broadly representative movement can still have serious strategic disagreements.
Native stability and party popularity retain their own meanings.

At Cohesion below 25, unresolved authority prevents additional politically coordinated breakaways and delays the final government settlement until mediation or a clear emergency command agreement is reached.
Ordinary combat, reinforcement, relief and escape remain available.
At Cohesion 25–49, the country can conduct restoration but can sustain only one major institutional program at a time.
At 50–74, the normal parallel political and military program is available.
At 75+, a supported local command can integrate into the national army without an additional political waiting period once its equipment and training conditions are fulfilled.
This does not waive its material costs or create formations.

Important fulfilled national agreements grant 10 Cohesion.
A major agreement broken by the government removes 15.
A documented conflict over command or foreign commitments removes 10 until resolved.
A recaptured state does not repeatedly award Cohesion every time it changes hands.
The same agreement has one success receipt and one breach disposition.
A fresh political settlement can replace a failed one after a real change in government or circumstances.

## Three governing settlements

Legal continuity restores the surviving government's institutions and established military hierarchy.
It is strongest when the old government has real leaders, diplomatic access and a surviving army.
It can restore administrative capacity quickly and make external recognition easier.
Its cost is an explicit obligation to incorporate or settle the demands of local resistance commands.
Ignoring that obligation keeps the local command dispute unresolved and prevents the highest Cohesion benefits.
The route cannot revive abolished institutions that the player previously chose to remove unless a new political decision explicitly re-establishes them.

The national resistance compact gives participating local commands and civil organizations a formal role in the provisional settlement.
It is strongest when the homeland movement did most of the fighting or when no credible national government survived.
It improves integration between the territorial base and returning institutions.
Its cost is a slower initial settlement and a binding commitment to the agreed representation.
The compact does not turn every country democratic or communist.
The country's real political profile determines the institutions through which the agreement operates.

Emergency military authority concentrates command while the homeland is in immediate danger.
It is strongest during a genuinely threatened opening or a fragmented multi-front revolt.
It shortens the initial command-coordination program by 35 days and permits a coherent emergency defense before the final political settlement.
It carries a 180-day review obligation after the immediate survival mission succeeds.
Honoring the review can transition to either civilian settlement without a free ideology switch.
Refusing it produces a sustained political dispute and a 15-point Cohesion loss, rather than an automatic generic civil war.
A wider civil conflict belongs to the established civil-war owner if its actual conditions develop.

## Complete activation requirements

The country must have a valid capital and legal territory, compatible name and flag references, surviving or appropriate leadership, ordinary party and ideology data, valid laws, a working research setup, compatible technologies, military templates, equipment handling, AI priorities and a playable political-development route.
Each missing element is repaired only to the minimum supported national profile.
Do not copy a major patron's complete technology tree or industry into a small restored country.

The country's factories are the actual usable factories in its territory plus any legitimate surviving overseas economy.
Destroyed buildings remain damaged.
A dormant claimant with little industry receives access to real procurement and recovery programs, not free factories in every home state.
Research slots follow the supported country package or preserved country state.
The event must not reset a mature country's research capacity to a generic starter package.
A newly activated country without usable basic infantry technology needs the established compatible baseline required to operate its initial formation.
Advanced technology assistance is negotiated and limited to actual supported transfers.

National laws should fit the emergency and the country's current resources.
Do not automatically apply Total Mobilization or a maximum conscription law.
The army, navy and air force have separate real starting-resource checks.
An inland claimant does not need a fictional naval tradition to fill a template.
An island claimant does need a practical plan for transport, ports and maritime supply.

## Existing focus trees

Preserve a meaningful existing national focus tree, completed focuses and committed mutually exclusive choices.
The event's essential survival, political settlement and recovery functions must remain available through decisions and missions.
A supported additive branch can supply restoration depth where native and repository precedents permit it.
If safe additive integration has not been verified, do not replace the tree and call that an acceptable shortcut.
Implement the complete required gameplay through the decision route while the optional tree integration remains a separate, explicitly recorded enhancement.

A newly activated country with only a generic or empty tree receives the restoration-tree architecture in Part 09.
The tree uses a shared structure with country-specific identity and geographically relevant branches.
It is not a universal override of historical trees.
The country-created flag and activation state must exist before any tree-loading path that depends on them.
A retry after load failure must not issue the country package twice.

## Leadership, portraits and symbols

Reuse valid existing character portraits and national flags wherever they already serve the restored identity.
New real-person portraits require grounded source research and the project's portrait-production workflow.
The user-produced grounded styled final is distinct from a researched source placeholder.
A placeholder is never marked as a completed portrait.
A symbolic provisional institution can use a people-free composition without inventing a historical officeholder.

Flag changes occur only when the real political settlement or supported country profile warrants them.
The base flag path and required sizes must remain valid for the restored tag.
An emergency military government is not a reason to invent an unrelated fascist symbol.
A constitutional settlement can change institutions without requiring an entirely new flag family.

## Postwar authority

Once a settlement secures the country's sovereignty or agreed autonomous status, the government must fulfill the obligations that made restoration possible.
These may include integrating the national command, returning civilian administration, honoring aid terms, demobilizing temporary formations or reopening access to separated home regions.
Completion converts the emergency government into an ordinary functioning country.
The player keeps the consequences of its political choice, but loses the temporary crisis burden and obsolete emergency decisions.

A patron-backed victory cannot silently erase national sovereignty.
Any subject relationship must be an explicit agreement or a real consequence of native diplomacy and war.
A restored country that becomes a subject still exists as its real identity and can later interact with the proper subject-liberation event.
Its Event 095 incident closes according to the settlement actually achieved rather than awarding an independence result it did not obtain.
