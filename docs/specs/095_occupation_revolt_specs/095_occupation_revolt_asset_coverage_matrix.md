## Production and evidence requirements

The JSON register retains source and verification requirements for every row.
Use the asset prompt and the separate achievement and super-event prompts as the production contracts.
A source researcher must establish an exact identity and suitable rights before a real historical image is used.
A staged fictional scene must not masquerade as archival evidence.
A final runtime manifest must record source, source hash, generation or acquisition method, processing, preview, output path, consumer, sprite and verification result.
Never invent model metadata that the generating tool did not return.

Generic scenes should avoid identifiable national flags or people when one sprite serves many countries.
An identity-specific image requires a matching selector, and unresolved country art remains an explicit blocker for that identity rather than a generic replacement.
Existing valid national art may be reused, but existence and correct identity must be checked before the row is marked satisfied.
The final template emblem is conditional, whereas ordinary report, news, decision, mission and authored focus consumers require complete coverage.

## Deliberately absent surfaces

The accepted design has no new custom GUI, new skeletal model, required animated portrait or native raid type.
Their skills were read and their applicability considered.
Do not create a meaningless new gameplay surface merely to exercise a production skill.
A later authorized change introducing one must reopen its source, production and specialist gates.

## Runtime placement

Keep runtime art in the actual repository event-family convention confirmed by the asset owner.
Achievement icons stay directly under `gfx/achievements/` and country flags under their native roots.
Portrait wiring belongs to the portrait owner, while other shared integration belongs to the main implementation owner.
The source snapshot in this planning ZIP is never a substitute for installed visual references or immutable production templates.
