# Occupation revolt: the opening army and the first campaign

## One national force budget

The uprising receives one national opening budget, even when several states or centers join.
Allocate that budget among the actual opened states.
Do not run a complete force-generation routine separately for each state, each occupier, each report recipient or each evolution callback.
A surviving country retains its existing army and stocks, but those existing resources are not counted as newly created revolt strength.

The local volunteer ceiling begins with the population of the opening territory.
The proposed design coefficient is 5 additional potential volunteers per 1,000 residents for each Readiness band, beginning with 5 at Readiness below 25 and reaching 25 at Readiness 100.
The band formula is `5 + 5 × floor(Readiness/25)` volunteers per 1,000 residents.
These coefficients are game-design calibration, not demographic facts.
The final local manpower commitment is also bounded by actual native recruitable manpower available after lawful country and state activation.
The event does not create manpower by reducing a civilian-death ledger or by granting the country's entire nominal core population.

For example, an opening containing two million people at Readiness 50 yields a volunteer-potential ceiling of 30,000 people before the native manpower, equipment, command and supply limits apply.
If only 10,000 native recruits and equipment for 6,000 soldiers are available, the opening cannot field 30,000 soldiers.
It may commit a smaller equipped force and leave the rest as uncommitted potential.
Potential volunteers are not a resource that can later be cashed out repeatedly.

## The finite opening cache

A country restored after annexation needs a credible baseline source of weapons even when no donor exists.
The first occupation episode can therefore receive one explicitly recorded abstract underground cache representing accumulated local concealment, repair and small-scale procurement.
The cache is restricted to basic compatible infantry equipment.
It contains no free tanks, aircraft, artillery train, fleet or advanced support package.

First calculate the infantry-equipment requirement of the local volunteer ceiling using the actual approved baseline formation's equipment bill.
The opening-cache share is `25% + 50% × Readiness/100` of that bill.
An authentic home armory or usable military-industry base can add 10 percentage points, with a final cap of 75%.
The grant is reduced by already recorded accumulated cache equipment so the same underground history is not represented twice.
Any existing usable claimant stores and actual donations are counted separately as real stock, not subtracted from the donor after a second copy has been created.

The cache is a declared game abstraction.
It does not pretend to be a verified theft from the occupier's national stockpile.
Its amount, equipment family, occupation episode and prior consumption are recorded.
After the first issue, later attempts receive only retained stores plus new capacity accumulated through an actual period of renewed preparation.
Quiet-time accumulation uses the same annualized ceiling prorated by elapsed eligible preparation time, with no accrual while an armed incident is active and no accrual from repeatedly releasing and annexing the claimant.
A later event can never reset the occupation episode solely to obtain another full grant.

Current Chaos affects military opportunity through enabled earned evolution capabilities and the current military situation.
It does not apply an uncapped independent multiplier that creates resources when all evolutions are disabled.

## Force quality

Use three functional formation profiles.
The local defense profile is a small infantry formation suited to holding a narrow base and uses the cheapest supported native infantry structure.
The organized national profile is a coherent infantry formation with command and support appropriate to the claimant's available equipment and surviving military organization.
The returning veteran profile refers to actual surviving national units or an exact transfer of a donor-owned national exile formation.
It is not a template that generates veterans from nothing.

Exact battalion counts and engine tokens must be verified against installed definitions before implementation.
The design requires a useful small formation rather than an expensive mixed infantry, irregular and cavalry template imposed on every country.
A baseline native-infantry substitute for an unavailable DLC irregular type must preserve the same military role and resource bill.
This is a compatibility requirement, not permission to reduce the revolt to one generic division per state.

Readiness below 40 favors lightly organized local formations.
Readiness 40–70 permits a mixture of local and organized national formations when equipment and officers support them.
Readiness above 70 favors an organized opening, but still cannot supply missing equipment or trained specialists.
Initial training, organization and reinforcement status must reflect the source of the soldiers.
Do not apply a universal permanent combat bonus that lets militia match any army.

## Allocation across the map

First protect the provisional seat and its immediate supply position.
Then allocate strength to exposed approaches, important crossings and any prepared link to a friendly front.
A state with no useful military position does not receive a division merely because it joined politically.
A state that cannot support a viable independent force may join the territorial base while sharing defense with adjacent states.

Multiple centers share the same local-volunteer, cache, foreign-delivery and command budgets.
Each disconnected center must have enough local resources to survive long enough to pursue a concrete connection or supply objective.
No center can borrow the same rifles from another center before a real transfer route exists.
The system can retain an underprepared region as an underground network rather than opening a doomed enclave only to satisfy Evolution II.

Existing hostile formations are not deleted by a state handover.
The implementation must establish how the supported engine method handles contested provinces, retreat, control and unit placement.
A strongly defended state may remain a contested spread objective instead of being transferred at the opening.
The narrative claim that an occupation office collapsed cannot erase an intact armored army standing there.

## Returning national forces

Surviving national formations remain attached to their actual owner and location until a valid return is possible.
A surviving claimant overseas may use its ordinary transports and military access.
An exile host may return or transfer national formations only through a supported native path with exact donor and recipient accounting.
A formation currently fighting on a distant allied front does not teleport into the provisional capital.
The claimant can request release and transport, but cannot bypass blockade, access or war obligations through a free event spawn.

Returning command staff can improve the ability to organize local recruits when their identity and availability are verified.
A commander cannot be assigned to two countries at once.
A commander killed or invalidated by another event stays unavailable.
Where no real commander survives, use the country's existing valid leadership generation or a people-free institutional command presentation until a supported character exists.

## Heavy weapons and captured assets

Captured field equipment enters through native combat capture or a discrete verified seizure.
A local depot operation requires an actual eligible depot record and a finite inventory source.
National enemy stocks are not automatically local to every occupied state.
A successful depot seizure removes or reallocates the same equipment that reaches the claimant.
The operation cannot simultaneously count as an abstract cache, a foreign shipment and a defection reward.

Evolution III may transfer local security assets whose national recruitment or administrative association is known.
It does not grant a percentage of the occupier's entire army.
Airfields and naval bases can change hands through territorial control, while aircraft and ships require their own real ownership and transfer evidence.
A captured port is not a generated navy.

## The first campaign objective

Each opening receives a survival mission selected from its actual geography.
The default is to hold a defensible national base and establish a usable supply route.
A coastal movement can secure its port and a delivery route.
An inland movement can connect to an allied front or preserve a supplied local center.
A separated island movement can maintain a viable port or local supply base without being required to capture a distant continental capital immediately.

The ordinary mission lasts 100 days.
A large, divided or distant theater can use 150–180 days.
A genuinely immediate encirclement or evacuation mission can use 30–60 days because the military emergency is already present, with that reason stated in the mission brief.
Mission success is checked from control, supply and the agreed political seat, not a decorative timer.
The benefit is a real transition into sustained restoration and an end to the initial emergency burden, not a free second army.

## Reinforcement and loss

After the opening, the country uses ordinary recruitment, production, reinforcement and supplied equipment.
Event operations can coordinate regional volunteers or delivered aid, but must respect the same available resources.
Readiness can recover by organizing forces and securing command links.
It cannot heal destroyed divisions or replace all lost equipment by itself.

If the base is lost, remaining forces continue under ordinary war rules where they have legal territory or access.
Eligible units can evacuate through a real route or return to an existing exile host.
Only the surviving portion enters the later-revolt record.
Dispersing a formation into the underground must remove that formation and place only its verified surviving manpower and recoverable equipment into the appropriate records.
If a supported exact conversion path does not exist, do not fake a division transfer with an unbalanced clone and deletion.
That capability is an implementation blocker requiring a verified solution.
