# Event 095 national-program and payoff registry

This register makes the development architecture in Part 09 available through the operation families in Part 10.
Its working labels are not final focus or decision localisation.
Rows are substantial program variants, not 23 simultaneously visible buttons or a mandated 23-node focus tree.
The implementation owner chooses a lean native layout while preserving every essential route and payoff.

## Shared transaction rule

A preserved existing national tree receives complete decision-and-mission counterparts through the one national-program selector.
A generic or empty tree can use the country-adapted architecture.
Both access paths call one material requirement and outcome contract, with one incident or country-program completion record.
The same setup fee is charged once, and completing a native focus never implies payment of an outstanding equipment or construction bill.
Focus time and native opportunity cost may differ from decision access, but neither access path may create a duplicate physical or institutional reward.
The standard 25/50 political-power figures below are explicit organization costs only where the operation actually performs that work.
A program wholly paid and executed through native construction or procurement must not add an arbitrary political charge for the same work.

The normal substantial program uses 70 days.
An explicit 35-day commitment is a meaningful choice, not a substitute for the following substantial program.
The emergency-authority transition is the same legal-continuity or compact settlement selected after survival, not a second new permanent ideology.
Only the selected political settlement is active at a time.

## Capacity, cancellation and equivalence

Observe the phase budgets in Part 10 at all times.
Cohesion below 50 allows one major national program and Cohesion at least 50 allows two, subject to the existing overall mission cap.
The first survival mission and urgent escape needs take priority over optional expansion.
A branch not relevant to the country's real geography stays hidden without blocking essential government restoration.

The selected program records its exact location, participants, materials, operation generation, start and durable deadline.
Changing interfaces, loading a save, switching a selected state or losing a diplomatic partner cannot renew the deadline or reset the paid status.
When a target disappears, stop impossible work, preserve actual completed world changes and settle only the genuinely unconsumed reserved resources.
A completed building, already delivered shipment or already integrated unit is not refunded as if nothing happened.
An intact institution bypasses its repair requirement without creating its construction, manpower or equipment reward.

Every implementation must prove essential payoff parity for at least one preserved national tree, one generic-tree restoration and one country with a surviving overseas base.
The exact focus IDs, native costs, technology IDs, construction callbacks and layout remain native-inspection work.
This registry is a complete gameplay access contract, not tested engine syntax or a source-only replacement for the focus and decision auditors.
