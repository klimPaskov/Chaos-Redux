# Occupation revolt: AI behavior and balance

## AI must play the same restoration

AI claimants use the same identity, geography, resource, political and diplomatic conditions as human claimants.
They do not receive an invisible full-strength army or automatically know every hostile network.
AI occupiers need information that their occupation administration could actually possess.
Sponsors need real resources and routes.
All actors must be able to choose a viable modest objective instead of repeatedly attempting an unaffordable grand restoration.

The following rules are proposed design priorities.
They are not measured probabilities, tested engine AI or reports from an executed probability-auditor subagent.
The exact adapter, source context and evaluation method must be established before converting a priority into a probability claim.
A weight of 100 is a relative input, not a 100% chance.
A nominal MTTH of 90 days is not a promise that the event fires on day 90.

## Candidate selection

Construct the suitable primary candidate pool before considering dormant identities.
Invalid identities, duplicate active claimants, impossible openings and unavailable crisis slots have zero selection eligibility.
Do not multiply a positive weight by a late hidden validity check and then log a failed revolt as a real firing.
Only genuine candidate records enter the selection routine.

Use a base selection weight of 100 for each suitable primary claimant.
A verified surviving national command adds 50.
A usable external route or an adjacent friendly front adds 25.
A materially overstretched occupier adds 25.
A homeland whose population-weighted local network organization reaches 60 adds 25.
A recently defeated movement that has only just requalified subtracts 50.
Clamp the resulting proposed weight to 50–250 after eligibility.
Country size and major status are not independent requirements or automatic bonus multipliers.

These are initial tuning inputs for probability inspection.
The final verified design must retain meaningful chances for smaller authentic claimants without favoring a nation that cannot field a viable opening.
Dormant selection uses the same geographical and identity validity but favors genuine local organization and political space rather than inventing native resistance values where none exist.

## Claimant strategy

| Situation | Preferred plan | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Small isolated base with poor equipment | Defend a viable seat, seek a real supply route, concentrate the opening | Opening every possible region |
| Surviving government with a trained army abroad | Secure access and return real forces, reconcile local command | Generating replacement veterans |
| Strong local movement with disputed returning authority | Settle command early, then coordinate expansion | Spending every resource on military spread while Cohesion collapses |
| Several prepared islands or separated regions | Open only centers with finite resources and a viable supply plan | Copying a complete national army into each center |
| Weak occupier and favorable local control | Expand through prepared opportunities and actual combat | Waiting indefinitely for a perfect nationwide opening |
| Overwhelming enemy with no workable supply | Seek partial settlement, dispersal or real evacuation | Repeatedly buying units that cannot be supplied |
| Stable partial restoration | Rebuild and negotiate remaining claims | Requalifying itself for repeated opening grants |

The draft priority scale is 100 for a useful ordinary action, 200 for resolving an immediate survival bottleneck and 300 for avoiding an imminent irreversible loss where a viable alternative exists.
An unaffordable action, invalid target or unsupported diplomatic route has priority zero.
A large nominal reward cannot override the resource or route preflight.
The AI should maintain enough political and material capacity for its essential next obligation rather than spending everything on optional secondary actions.

## Government preferences

A surviving government with recognized institutions and a real exile army favors legal continuity.
A primarily local movement with weak surviving central institutions favors the resistance compact.
A claimant facing an immediate divided-command military emergency can favor emergency authority.
These are contextual tendencies rather than ideology quotas.

The AI avoids a military-emergency route when it cannot plausibly satisfy or deliberately manage the review obligation.
It does not prolong an emergency merely for a small temporary benefit.
An existing player's political route is not automatically reversed when the AI later controls the country.
A sponsor's ideology can influence an offered agreement without deterministically selecting the claimant's government.

## Occupier strategy

The occupier values the actual military and economic importance of the threatened home region.
It compares the cost of holding it with its other fronts, supply situation, force commitments and available settlement terms.
A major country can accept a limited restoration when maintaining the occupation is strategically wasteful.
A minor can fight firmly for a critical defended position when it has the means.

Containment is favored when real local forces and supply can isolate the claimant without unacceptable diversion.
Political settlement is favored when the claimant holds a viable national base, continued occupation consumes disproportionate resources, or the occupier faces a more dangerous war elsewhere.
Targeted network disruption is useful only against known exposed networks.
Civilian-service restoration is favored when a proven local grievance can actually be addressed.

The AI must not enter a costly security operation while also withdrawing the forces needed to complete it.
It must not offer contradictory territories to two claimants or agree to a sovereignty transfer it cannot legally perform.
If several nations rise under one occupier, it prioritizes a coherent regional strategy rather than issuing the same expensive response independently for every claimant.
Shared crisis limits and operation reservations prevent overcommitment.

## Sponsor strategy

A sponsor weighs diplomatic motive, practical access, claimant viability, equipment surplus and the consequences of the proposed agreement.
An enemy of the occupier can rationally aid a nonaligned claimant.
A former ally can prioritize returning its national forces over issuing another equipment shipment.
A nearby neutral can prefer a political settlement or humanitarian corridor over open intervention.

The AI does not send its last usable equipment lot while facing an immediate shortage of its own.
The proposed shipment cap of 10% of uncommitted compatible stock helps prevent this, but the broader native AI economy must also be inspected.
The sponsor can stop new deliveries when the route is closed or the agreement is breached.
It does not confiscate completed deliveries by deleting the claimant's equipment or divisions.

## Evolution behavior

Prepared Underground favors a coherent opening and meaningful reorganization, not repeated political-power farming.
Uprising on Every Front selects centers that solve a military problem or exploit a real weakness.
The Army Breaks assesses only the verified local defection pool and the claimant's ability to honor integration terms.
Liberation Cascade prepares suitable additional national movements only after an actual initiating success.

When a setting is disabled, AI cannot access its operations through a focus, delayed event or debug-only path.
Mixed evolution configurations retain ordinary baseline behavior.
The AI can complete an already committed lawful transfer after a setting changes only where the design's committed-transaction rule permits it.
Uncommitted evolution grants are stopped before their effects or logs.

## Balance targets

The baseline should be a credible disruption with several possible outcomes.
A weak movement may lose, especially without supply or outside support.
A well-prepared movement should threaten an overstretched occupier without needing a predetermined scripted victory.
A highly evolved revolt can collapse a weak occupation through several interacting national fronts, while a well-supported and coherent occupier can still contain or settle it.

The starter cache should make the opening playable without making deliberate annexation a profitable equipment-production method.
An ordinary restored country should remain economically smaller than a major patron unless its actual homeland and surviving economy justify the difference.
Political choices should change command, agreements and development paths rather than provide one obviously superior pile of modifiers.
Victory should leave a functioning ordinary country, not an indefinite crisis dashboard.

## Required probability scenarios

The mandatory evidence set covers no primary candidates, one weak primary candidate, several mixed-size primary candidates, primary-versus-dormant precedence, no dormant identity, low and high compliance, unknown resistance, surviving exile forces, no external route, partial aid delivery, multiple occupiers, separated centers, local defection pools, recent failed revolts and cascade saturation.
Test all 16 enabled/disabled combinations of the four evolutions, then targeted boundary fixtures just below and at Chaos 200, 400, 600 and 800.
Test the active-evolution route separately from the pre-fire route.

Inspect the actual event pool, country selector, local opportunity selector, evolution timing, AI action, focus route, donor choice, agreement response and any uncertain defection outcome.
Use the appropriate probability evaluation, sweep, simulation and sequence tools supported by the installed MCP surface.
Retain immutable baseline and candidate revisions with matching scenario inputs.
Unsupported adapters or partial graphs remain unresolved evidence, not a pass derived from manually normalizing weights.

## Tuning discipline

Keep the design constants centralized and named by their actual purpose.
Prefer round visible values and substantial effects.
Derived population totals, map counts, equipment bills and native identifiers are not rounded into incorrect facts merely to fit a visual convention.
Change one connected balance concern at a time and re-evaluate its downstream effects on resources, survival and repeat exploits.
Do not solve poor AI with free armies or solve overwhelming revolts by deleting the user's core restoration mechanics.
