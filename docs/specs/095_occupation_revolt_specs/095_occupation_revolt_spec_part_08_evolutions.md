# Occupation revolt: the four evolutions

## Shared evolution rules

Each evolution has a Chaos eligibility threshold, an enabled-setting check, a meaningful capability, a pre-fire application and an active-incident application.
Reaching a threshold makes the evolution eligible.
It does not alone execute its effect or guarantee a revolt.
The normal active-evolution timing uses the project's dynamic MTTH approach with an initial 90-day anchor, modified by the actual opportunity relevant to that evolution.
The timing value is not a published probability until the required probability tools have evaluated its actual adapter.

The enabled check occurs before flags, logs, allocations and delayed work are committed.
A queued evolution whose setting becomes disabled cannot apply new benefits when its callback arrives.
Already completed real state changes remain real.
Disabling an evolution does not destroy troops that defected legally, remove equipment that was already paid and delivered, or reverse a restored state's sovereignty.
It stops new evolution-specific operations and closes uncommitted work according to its refund and cleanup rules.

A later drop below the Chaos threshold does not remove a capability already earned by an active incident.
It prevents a new incident from newly acquiring that capability until eligibility returns.
Future openings use the enabled and eligible pre-fire state at their actual commitment time, together with any persistent project-wide evolution state required by the shared framework.
The implementation must record whether the framework stores a global unlock or an incident unlock and reconcile that with this capability rule before coding.
No unverified global flag name is prescribed here.

Disabling one evolution does not mechanically disable the others.
Evolution II can coordinate several independently prepared centers without Prepared Underground.
Evolution III can recruit valid local defections without a multi-front opening.
Evolution IV can inspire other movements without requiring a defection in the initiating country.
Ordinary survival, combat, local spread, political settlement and repeat eligibility work with all evolutions disabled.

## Evolution I: Prepared Underground, Chaos 200+

### The change in play

The movement is better able to coordinate stores, local commands and national officers before exposing itself.
It can begin with a more coherent base and use the equipment it possesses more effectively.
It still needs actual recruits, weapons and a viable home region.
The evolution does not substitute an automatic modern army for the military budget.

### Applied before a new opening

Raise evidence-based initial Readiness by 15, capped at 90 before any subsequent completed preparation operation.
This bonus never reduces evidence-based initial Readiness that is already above 90.
Improve the local organization of each actually supported opening network by 15.
Use the resulting Readiness in the normal territorial and material-budget calculations exactly once.
Do not first issue a baseline army and then run the evolved opening again.
The first equipment cache may use the best basic infantry-equipment model the restored national identity can validly operate, subject to the normal finite cache ceiling and actual supported definitions.
There is no free technology union with a patron.

A country with a genuine surviving officer network can begin with more organized national formations instead of the same number of weak militia formations.
This is a conversion of the finite allocated manpower and equipment bill.
It is not an additional layer of divisions.
A country with no surviving officers can still use the organizational improvement, while developing national command through the existing program.

### Applied during an active revolt

Unlock a single coordinated reorganization program for the current incident.
The program takes 70 days, requires a functioning national command and real equipment for the intended formation conversion, and can be completed in stages for separate actual cohorts.
The draft organization cost is 25 political power, with material costs supplied by the exact conversion quote.
It raises Readiness by 15 once for the incident and brings the supported local formations into the organized national profile.
It cannot replace destroyed formations or grant unearned equipment.

When the country is still underground, the program strengthens its current preparation rather than starting an independent second preparation clock.
When the country has already settled the war, it can finish an already committed reorganization, but the evolution does not reopen a closed incident merely to award its benefit.

### Counterplay and limits

Exposure of a prepared network can still force dispersal or premature revolt.
A better-organized uprising has more to lose when it concentrates its stores in an unsafe place.
The occupier can reduce the local opportunity through actual security and political results.
Prepared Underground does not confer permanent immunity from detection or a universal resistance increase across the world.

## Evolution II: Uprising on Every Front, Chaos 400+

### The change in play

Several actual parts of one homeland can enter the revolt together.
This matters for islands, divided national regions, distant enclaves and homelands held by multiple occupiers.
Each center retains its own local preparation, supply needs and initial military allocation.
There is still one national claimant and one national force budget.

### Applied before a new opening

Evaluate each connected component of the authentic eligible homeland.
A component qualifies when it has a ready local network, a viable seat or command link, an allocated armed force and a concrete supply or survival plan.
Select all components that meet these conditions within the national resources and supported political commitments.
The number of centers is dynamic.
There is no fixed grant of two, three or five full rebel armies.

The claimant sees which centers are ready, which are weak and which must remain underground.
Opening under a second occupier requires an explicit conflict commitment for that occupier and any native diplomatic consequences.
The event cannot silently turn a single-front liberation into an unannounced war against several factions.

### Applied during an active revolt

A living claimant can organize an additional center through a coordinated opening operation.
It takes 70 days under ordinary conditions and requires that center's own verified local resources or completed delivery.
The existing army's equipment can support it only through an actual transfer.
A candidate center that is already liberated becomes a command-integration objective instead of receiving an uprising reward.
A candidate whose route collapses stays underground or closes its operation with the actual expenditure recorded.

Opening another center reduces national Readiness by up to 10 until its command link and initial supply objective are secured.
Record the actual decrease after the 0–100 bounds are applied.
Completing that objective restores only that recorded actual decrease once, never a nominal 10 points when fewer were removed.
This is the temporary coordination burden of that opening, not a farmable permanent Readiness gain.
Several concurrent centers cannot recover the same recorded reduction more than once.

### Counterplay and limits

An occupier can isolate centers, retain ports and disrupt their real command and supply links.
Loss of one center does not automatically dissolve every other center.
It removes that center's real military and administrative contribution and can damage Cohesion when an explicit national promise was broken.
A single-state homeland does not split into invented substates to showcase this evolution.
Its multi-front capability remains unused until a genuine additional home region becomes relevant.

## Evolution III: The Army Breaks, Chaos 600+

### The change in play

Parts of the occupation's local military and administrative structure can join the national claimant.
The strongest opportunities occur where local recruitment, exhausted occupation control and a credible restored command align.
This can accelerate an already unstable occupation's collapse.
It does not apply a random transfer percentage to the occupier's worldwide armed forces.

### Eligible sources

A source must be tied to the claimant's homeland and have a documented local or national basis.
Possible sources include locally recruited security formations with supported provenance, a compatible officer serving in the local occupation structure, a finite garrison-equipment allocation, or a documented local store whose custodians switch sides.
A division's current location alone does not prove its soldiers belong to the claimant's national movement.
A foreign expeditionary army, an unrelated armored corps and the occupier's strategic reserve are excluded without specific valid provenance.

### Applied before a new opening

Evaluate the verified local source pool once before finalizing the opening force allocation.
The draft defection share is 15% of eligible capacity under strained occupation control, 25% under weak control, and 40% during an actual local administrative collapse.
These shares apply only to the proven eligible pool.
A country with no eligible local sources receives no defections even at Chaos 1,000.
Transferred troops and equipment enter the same national budget and reduce the donor by the actual corresponding amounts.
No normal opening calculation is rerun after the transfer.

### Applied during an active revolt

The claimant can offer a specific local command an integration agreement after demonstrating a credible base and adequate Cohesion.
The offer is not a repeatable lottery against every hostile formation.
It has a finite target pool and a 35-day decision period.
The local commander assesses survival, national affiliation, treatment under the agreement and the credibility of the claimant's government.
An accepted defection transfers the verified source once.
A refusal can close that target until a material local change, such as loss of the occupier's command center or a new national settlement.

The same pool may be revisited only for its untransferred remainder after a material change.
Repeated offers cannot progressively siphon the entire pool merely through repeated timer completion.
The per-occupation cumulative ceiling is the applicable highest defection share already justified for that pool.
Moving a unit between states or changing a local target identifier cannot reset that ceiling.

### Exactness and consequences

A real unit transfer must preserve or explicitly account for unit identity, manpower, equipment, experience, organization, location and commanders.
A verified inventory transfer needs exact before-and-after amounts and variant preservation.
If the native engine cannot support a promised unit transition, implementation must resolve that capability gap before marking this evolution complete.
An unbalanced cloned replacement is not an accepted fallback.
The design may represent a source as finite personnel and equipment only when the source is genuinely such an allocation, not when a real division was promised to transfer.

The occupier can retain local forces through reliable supply, a defensible command position and an accepted political arrangement.
A decision cannot purchase perfect loyalty forever.
The claimant must honor the integration terms.
Breaking them creates a concrete Cohesion and command issue, not a free repeat defection reward.

## Evolution IV: Liberation Cascade, Chaos 800+

### The change in play

A successful national restoration demonstrates that the occupation order can be broken.
Other displaced identities under the same occupier, or in nearby directly connected regions, may begin their own preparations.
Each new movement keeps its own homeland, political leadership, local resources and military situation.
The initiating claimant does not receive ownership of the other countries.

### The initiating success

An armed opening alone is insufficient.
The initiating claimant must complete its survival objective and either recover its national center with at least 50% of authentic homeland population, or force an actual independence or restoration settlement that substantially removes the occupation.
It must retain a viable government and supply position at the trigger check.
A staged release, agreed farming cycle or duplicate receipt is excluded from cascade achievement and reward logic.

### Applied before a new opening

An eligible evolved incident is marked as capable of inspiring a cascade when it later achieves the initiating success.
No secondary countries spawn at the moment the first event opens.
The evolution does not assume that an untested revolt has already demonstrated a regional collapse.

### Applied during an active revolt

An already active revolt that meets the initiating success can assemble a secondary-candidate pool.
The pool includes genuine displaced claimants under the same occupier and genuine displaced claimants in directly adjacent or operationally connected neighboring home regions.
Dormant identities are secondary fallback candidates only when no suitable displaced identity exists in the relevant secondary pool.
An unrelated distant nationalist movement is not included merely because it shares an ideology.

Each selected movement begins preparation using its own evidence.
The draft inspiration benefit is 10 initial Readiness and a political opportunity to establish liaison.
It receives no copy of the initiating country's army, cache, occupied-state list or national government.
Its own armed opening still needs the normal military and political prerequisites.
Its preparation duration normally falls within 90–150 days according to actual readiness.

### A chain without an infinite dispatcher

A cascade has one origin record and a finite candidate set.
Secondary movements do not recursively launch another full scan when their own opening occurs.
Their later success can be registered in the existing cascade and can make an already recorded nearby candidate more credible.
A new independent Event 095 firing may create a later cascade after the previous origin closes and a new material situation develops.
This permits a chain of restoration wars without an unbounded recursive event storm.

Respect the shared three-crisis country limit.
When an occupier or claimant has no capacity, keep the candidate as a time-limited political opportunity rather than an already active uprising hidden in a queue.
The opportunity expires after 90 days unless an actual committed preparation is proceeding under an available slot.
Slot release revalidates the candidate rather than spawning it from stale facts.
The same empire can face successive restorations as earlier incidents settle or collapse.

### The imperial outcome

A large occupation system can fracture into several national restoration wars.
The initiator may help its neighbors through real liaison, supply and diplomacy.
The occupier can negotiate some restorations while fighting others.
The outcome depends on individual movements rather than a universal empire-dissolution command.
One major super-event is justified only after several consequential restorations have actually occurred, under the separate trigger in Part 13.
