# Occupation revolt: connections to the wider mod

## Independence Wave and national origins

Independence Wave can create countries that later lose their homeland.
Those countries become legitimate Event 095 claimants through their recorded identity and origin.
Use the established origin registry and country package rather than trying to recreate them from a generic list of possible cores.
A dynamic country's cosmetic changes, leadership decisions and completed political development remain relevant after annexation.
Event 095 does not repeat the original independence event's resources, focuses or institutional grants.

An Event 006-created state can be a small, unusual or nonhistorical national arrangement while still having an established in-game identity.
That supported provenance distinguishes it from an invented fallback country created solely to keep Event 095 firing.
The restoration package uses the identity already established by the other event.
It does not retrospectively claim that the country's borders or institutions are historical facts.

## Subject liberation and the existing liberation network

[Event 063, Subjects Break Free](../063_subjects_break_free_specs/specs/063_subjects_break_free_spec_part_2_settlements.md) owns independence of existing subject countries.
Event 095 concerns identities displaced from their homeland.
A subject controlling its homeland normally belongs to Event 063; a displaced subject can qualify here through the ordinary identity and displacement tests.
If a protected subject release changes status during preparation without returning the homeland, the claimant may still need its restoration campaign.
An actual external restoration instead uses the overtaken-by-restoration closure without another opening grant.

A completed independent restoration can offer its existing origin and outcome record to the [liberation network](../063_subjects_break_free_specs/specs/063_subjects_break_free_spec_part_4_evolutions_clusters_and_connections.md) through a reviewed, owner-approved adapter.
The claimant must actually exist independently.
Preserve earlier Independence Wave or Soviet-origin identity; Event 063 owns network eligibility, recognition, congress and Pact decisions.
Event 095 grants no automatic membership, guarantee, Liberation Cohesion or duplicate independence reward.

A restored subject closes with that honest outcome and may later pursue Event 063.
AI follows current status and diplomatic conditions: an independent claimant can seek compatible support, while a subject cannot claim independent-network credit.
When Event 063 or its adapter is absent or disabled, normal restoration and diplomacy remain playable.

A future acceptance case compares independent and subject restorations sharing an earlier Event 006 origin.
Only the independent country can be offered as an active independent network participant.
Neither reruns Event 006 setup, and a later subject release does not spawn another revolt army.

## Civil wars and ideological revolutions

A disagreement within the restored government is not automatically a new national claimant.
Use the established civil-war owner when a real ideological or sovereignty split develops.
Event 095 must not spawn a duplicate country and then ask another event to reconcile the tags.
Its Cohesion system provides political friction, command disputes and negotiated settlements without requiring a civil war every time the value falls.

The Great Revolution and other ideological events may change the claimant's government, occupier, leadership or support environment.
Read current facts when they change.
Do not restore an obsolete ideology because it existed at the first occupation.
A legitimate surviving national identity can continue its restoration under a different government.
Its foreign agreements may need renegotiation when their actual terms or eligibility change.

## Sales, partitions and the claimant's real homeland

[Greenland Sale](../092_greenland_sale_specs/092_greenland_sale_spec_part_04_territory_and_sovereignty.md) and [Tordesillas partition](../093_treaty_of_tordesillas_restoration_specs/093_treaty_of_tordesillas_restoration_spec_part_06_geographic_partition.md) change territorial authority, rather than national origin.
A sale preserves an authentic displaced claimant's homeland record.
A treaty claim or imperial integration core alone cannot make a distant acquisition that crown's original lost homeland.
Part 02's provenance test still distinguishes genuine overlapping identities from invented expansion rights.

An ownership or control change invalidates the tracked state's cached opening or spread assessment.
At the next existing operation boundary, recheck the current counterpart, local forces, access and political terms.
The buyer does not inherit the seller's personal sponsor contract or earlier civilian responsibility without the relevant owner's explicit succession rule.
A saved occupier target cannot declare war on the former owner after a sale.

An accepted restoration settlement or committed opening exposes its exact state disposition to the other territorial owners before an incompatible cession can be promised.
A dormant network or foreign core alone is not that commitment and does not expose covert cells through a public listing.
[The auction owner](../092_greenland_sale_specs/092_greenland_sale_spec_part_11_connections_and_consequences.md) handles its cancellation and refund when actual liberation removes an advertised holding; completed purchases carry no insurance against later revolt.
If a lawful sale finishes first, preparation revalidates against the buyer.

Liberation of a treaty region makes Event 093 refresh its real Consolidation numerators against the frozen denominator.
Neither event removes the other's legitimate core, and independent faction association does not preserve absorbed-territory credit.
Claimant AI evaluates the new opponent and support route rather than treating payment as national consent or an automatic war.

A future acceptance case sells a prepared claimant's home port before opening: the movement must use the current buyer, actual route and finite material budget.
A second liberates a treaty-integrated state: authentic identity survives and imperial credit falls only where real authority was lost.
Ordinary territorial changes use the same revalidation when either connection is absent.

## Disappearance during a restoration campaign

[Half gone](../094_half_gone_specs/094_half_gone_spec_part_7_connections.md) owns an unexplained loss, not an occupier's suppression action.
A living claimant or donor can lose its reserve; a dormant unactivated identity is not a live country target.
Dispatched stores, underground caches and issued equipment retain Parts 03 and 04's ownership distinctions, with no new cache-loss provider or second debit through stale national stock.

Before opening, use surviving recruits and equipment in the existing national budget.
A population disappearance in a tracked home state limits future volunteer potential, but is neither lost sovereignty nor confirmed deaths.
A smaller viable opening may proceed; an unviable one follows ordinary preparation or closure.
Readiness cannot replace stores or replay that episode's finite cache.
After opening, demographic loss does not halve the army already fielded.

Country activation consults the character owner's active absence state.
Historical setup repair cannot recreate a missing commander or unique person; use valid leadership and legitimate owner-supported absence or replacement.
A future acceptance case restores a country with a missing commander and depleted reinforcement stocks: the identity stays absent, reinforcement spends only available equipment, and the cache is not issued again.
Without Event 094, ordinary country and force continuity remain authoritative.

## Famine, relief and displacement

Changing territory is not evidence that people moved.
The repository's historical Event 095 adapter handoff explicitly distinguishes its old transfer loop from a civilian transaction.
The new event preserves that boundary.
Local food insecurity, disrupted relief, forced movement and protected administration can influence a network only when their owners supply exact current state and causal facts.
The event name and an abstract occupation status are insufficient.

A destroyed supply route can matter to the military campaign without automatically causing civilian famine.
A real food-route loss, inadequate local supply and a verified affected population can enter the famine owner's process.
A real civilian departure needs the migration owner's origin, cohort, route, destination, responsible actor and reception evidence.
The physical population debit belongs to that owner once.
Route deaths are recorded separately from the survivors arriving.
Native manpower recruitment is not a civilian death or a migration cohort.

A claimant can cooperate with an enemy on civilian evacuation while continuing to fight over sovereignty.
The two transactions remain separately recorded.

### Grievance states

A home state is a grievance state while any of these owner facts holds: its famine Food Security is famine or catastrophic famine; the controlling occupier took Requisition Food from it, chose Continue Requisitions there or concealed hunger there within the last 180 days; it holds an active camp run by the occupier; or migration records violent border enforcement or forced repatriation by the occupier from that state within the last 180 days.
A grievance state counts one display band higher for breakaway and opening assessment (organizing reads as prepared), and only one band however many facts apply.
The band returns to its evidence value on the next assessment after the facts lapse, for example when the occupier's relief brings Food Security back to acute shortage or better, or the camp closes.
Event 095 reads these facts through the owners' published state triggers and never writes their ledgers.

This gives the occupier a real choice with felt costs: spend food, transport and political power through the famine actions to keep a region quiet, or squeeze it and accept that the region will be the first to rise.
It is the only humanitarian input to revolt pressure; there is no separate Event 095 relief or grievance meter.

### Liberation, flight and return

When the claimant captures one of its own authentic home states from the occupier, Event 095 asks the migration owner, through an owner-approved predicate, to treat the capture as a liberation by the owner: no war flight is requested.
The occupier's recapture of a revolt state is an ordinary hostile capture and produces ordinary war flight through the shared hostile-capture adapter.
People fleeing the fighting therefore leave when the occupier returns, not when their own country does.

A restoration settlement's term for the return of displaced civilians is carried out through migration's Help Families Return for cohorts whose origin is a restored state, with the restored country choosing its own Border Policy.
Neither the claimant nor the settlement moves people directly.

## Deaths, responsibility and condemnation

Native combat losses continue under the ordinary military and Deaths systems.
A state transfer does not add a scripted casualty count.
An exposed network or failed political operation does not require fabricated executions to explain its failure.
When another system records actual deaths, custody, abuse or forced movement, keep its responsible actor, state, amount and public-evidence status.
Do not charge the liberator for an occupier's earlier actions merely because the liberator now owns the state.

Liberation may uncover evidence relevant to Condemnation.
Only the existing evidence and discovery processes decide what becomes public; liberating a camp state uses the camp owner's liberation and discovery path.
A condemned occupier is easier to oppose: countries enforcing the condemnation system's sanctions against it treat supporting the claimant as a natural motive (Part 12), while the occupier's own Tier 3 arms embargo already limits the equipment it can import to contain the revolt.
There is no automatic worldwide condemnation bonus or penalty attached to every revolt.
Supporters are not cleared of their own conduct because they assisted a national liberation.
Local civilian protection is an actual policy and delivery outcome, not a decorative moral alignment.

## Special occupiers and nonhuman systems

A genuine human claimant remains subject to normal civilian systems even when its occupier is an unusual Chaos country.
Use the shared actual-nonhuman classification for the relevant actor rather than treating every special tag as nonhuman.
An actual nonhuman occupier may lack meaningful diplomatic negotiation or local nationally recruited security forces.
Those operations remain unavailable when their premises are absent.
The claimant can still fight, organize a homeland, accept human foreign support and restore its government.

A revolt does not automatically turn into an existential world-end event because a special occupier is involved.
Existing zombie, plague, contamination or other system owners retain their actual hazards and cleanup rules.
A reclaimed state does not become safe simply because its ownership changed.
Nor does every reclaimed state receive an invented outbreak merely to connect Event 095 to another feature.

## Shared Chaos and event pacing

Event 095 remains a Minor Repeatable event with the user's baseline Chaos level 1.
Use the existing event-selection and repeat-weight framework rather than a new global timer.
The supplied mechanics describe a default repeatable minor weight of 1,000, recovery of 20 per month and a halving repeat ceiling.
The current implementation must be checked before editing these shared rules.
This rework does not alter them independently.

Actual wars, annexations, liberation, exile, restoration and independence can already affect shared Chaos through their existing owners.
The Event 095 root must not add a second bundle of diplomacy-based Chaos changes for the same transition.
Evolution unlocks, report displays, logs, failed preflight and duplicate callbacks add no outcome Chaos.
Where the shared engine distinguishes the root's catalog contribution from native diplomatic consequences, preserve that distinction and document the exact once-only owner.

## What this event does not add

Event 095 does not add a new world-end scenario, a universal separatism engine, a second famine system, a new migration ledger, a generic ethnic map, a global patron-influence meter, or a new supernatural army.
It needs no bespoke 3D unit, native raid, large custom GUI or animated portrait; those skills govern any later justified use of their surfaces.
