# Occupation revolt: connections to the wider mod

## Independence Wave and national origins

Independence Wave can create countries that later lose their homeland.
Those countries become legitimate Event 095 claimants through their recorded identity and origin.
Use the established origin registry and country package rather than trying to recreate them from a generic list of possible cores.
A dynamic country's cosmetic changes, leadership decisions and completed political development remain relevant after annexation.
Event 095 does not repeat the original independence event's resources, focuses or institutional grants.

An Event 006-created state can be a small, unusual or nonhistorical national arrangement while still having an established in-game identity.
That supported provenance distinguishes it from an invented fallback country created solely to keep Event 095 firing.
The restoration package uses the identity already established by the other event.
It does not retrospectively claim that the country's borders or institutions are historical facts.

## Subject liberation

The user's connection name is Subjects Break Free.
The supplied catalog contains Event 063 under the name End Subject Status.
Treat the relationship between those labels as a repository alias to verify, not permission to rename the other event or assume its exact current root.

Subject liberation concerns countries that exist under another country's political authority.
Occupation revolt concerns an identity displaced from control of its homeland.
A subject that controls its home territory ordinarily belongs to the subject-liberation system.
A subject or former subject that has actually lost its homeland can qualify for Event 095 through the same displacement tests as other countries.
A claimant restored as a subject has not earned a full-independence result.
Its later pursuit of freedom can transfer naturally to the subject event.

## Civil wars and ideological revolutions

A disagreement within the restored government is not automatically a new national claimant.
Use the established civil-war owner when a real ideological or sovereignty split develops.
Event 095 must not spawn a duplicate country and then ask another event to reconcile the tags.
Its Cohesion system provides political friction, command disputes and negotiated settlements without requiring a civil war every time the value falls.

The Great Revolution and other ideological events may change the claimant's government, occupier, leadership or support environment.
Read current facts when they change.
Do not restore an obsolete ideology because it existed at the first occupation.
A legitimate surviving national identity can continue its restoration under a different government.
Its foreign agreements may need renegotiation when their actual terms or eligibility change.

## Territory-changing events

Annexations, partitions, state transfers and wars change which home states are eligible.
Event 095 revalidates its tracked homeland and active target list at the relevant ownership and control changes.
It does not build a new global territorial monitoring system for every event in the mod.
Recorded agreements and source responsibility survive a change of controller where their owning systems require it.

A state transferred by Treaty of Tordesillas Restoration, another partition or a sale is not automatically part of a claimant's homeland merely because its owner changed.
A genuine claimant displaced by such a transfer can become eligible through the ordinary identity and control checks.
Overlapping liberation events cannot each grant the same state, army and political reward.
The first actual transaction is authoritative and later operations revalidate against it.

## Famine, relief and displacement

Changing territory is not evidence that people moved.
The repository's historical Event 095 adapter handoff explicitly distinguishes its old transfer loop from a civilian transaction.
The new event preserves that boundary.
Local food insecurity, disrupted relief, forced movement and protected administration can influence a network only when their owners supply exact current state and causal facts.
The event name and an abstract occupation status are insufficient.

A destroyed supply route can matter to the military campaign without automatically causing civilian famine.
A real food-route loss, inadequate local supply and a verified affected population can enter the famine owner's process.
A real civilian departure needs the migration owner's origin, cohort, route, destination, responsible actor and reception evidence.
The physical population debit belongs to that owner once.
Route deaths are recorded separately from the survivors arriving.
Native manpower recruitment is not a civilian death or a migration cohort.

Humanitarian access can reduce a proven grievance contribution when the assistance actually reaches the relevant state.
It does not make national independence demands disappear automatically.
A claimant can cooperate with an enemy on civilian evacuation while continuing to fight over sovereignty.
The two transactions remain separately recorded.

## Deaths, responsibility and condemnation

Native combat losses continue under the ordinary military and Deaths systems.
A state transfer does not add a scripted casualty count.
An exposed network or failed political operation does not require fabricated executions to explain its failure.
When another system records actual deaths, custody, abuse or forced movement, keep its responsible actor, state, amount and public-evidence status.
Do not charge the liberator for an occupier's earlier actions merely because the liberator now owns the state.

Liberation may uncover evidence relevant to Condemnation.
Only the existing evidence and discovery processes decide what becomes public.
There is no automatic worldwide condemnation bonus or penalty attached to every revolt.
Supporters are not cleared of their own conduct because they assisted a national liberation.
Local civilian protection is an actual policy and delivery outcome, not a decorative moral alignment.

## Special occupiers and nonhuman systems

A genuine human claimant remains subject to normal civilian systems even when its occupier is an unusual Chaos country.
Use the shared actual-nonhuman classification for the relevant actor rather than treating every special tag as nonhuman.
An actual nonhuman occupier may lack meaningful diplomatic negotiation or local nationally recruited security forces.
Those operations remain unavailable when their premises are absent.
The claimant can still fight, organize a homeland, accept human foreign support and restore its government.

A revolt does not automatically turn into an existential world-end event because a special occupier is involved.
Existing zombie, plague, contamination or other system owners retain their actual hazards and cleanup rules.
A reclaimed state does not become safe simply because its ownership changed.
Nor does every reclaimed state receive an invented outbreak merely to connect Event 095 to another feature.

## Shared Chaos and event pacing

Event 095 remains a Minor Repeatable event with the user's baseline Chaos level 1.
Use the existing event-selection and repeat-weight framework rather than a new global timer.
The supplied mechanics describe a default repeatable minor weight of 1,000, recovery of 20 per month and a halving repeat ceiling.
The current implementation must be checked before editing these shared rules.
This rework does not alter them independently.

Actual wars, annexations, liberation, exile, restoration and independence can already affect shared Chaos through their existing owners.
The Event 095 root must not add a second bundle of diplomacy-based Chaos changes for the same transition.
Evolution unlocks, report displays, logs, failed preflight and duplicate callbacks add no outcome Chaos.
Where the shared engine distinguishes the root's catalog contribution from native diplomatic consequences, preserve that distinction and document the exact once-only owner.

## What this event does not add

Event 095 does not add a new world-end scenario, a universal separatism engine, a second famine system, a new migration ledger, a generic ethnic map, a global patron-influence meter, or a new supernatural army.
It does not need a bespoke 3D unit, a native raid, a large custom GUI or an animated portrait merely to demonstrate that the corresponding supplied skill was read.
Those skills govern any later justified use of their surfaces.
Their conditional applicability is recorded separately from missing mandatory review or tool execution.
