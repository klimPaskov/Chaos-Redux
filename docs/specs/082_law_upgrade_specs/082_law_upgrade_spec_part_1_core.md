## The event

Every country takes another step toward complete wartime mobilization.

Factories receive military priorities, recruitment exemptions disappear, and governments acquire a public mandate to sustain the change. At first, the event accelerates familiar preparations. Its later evolutions extend the same pressure through compatible wartime institutions and eventually push the economy and recruitment laws beyond their normal limits.

The word “upgrade” describes movement toward war. It does not guarantee that the resulting law is better for the country. A peaceful trading state can lose a productive economic arrangement, an exhausted army can gain more recruits than its factories can equip, and a victorious government can remain trapped in an expensive emergency settlement.

The main strategic question is how long a country should accept the new setting. The initial advance is compulsory. Afterward, normal law changes remain possible, and the two extreme laws have explicit paid reversals.

## The complete firing rule

A firing uses one evolution profile for the whole world. Every existing country receives that profile's War Support increase and one forward step in each participating law category. Countries can begin at different positions and therefore finish at different positions.

| Active profile | War Support gain | Categories advanced |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Baseline | +10 percentage points | Economy and conscription |
| Evolution I, Emergency State | +25 percentage points | Baseline categories, trade, and registered military, economic, production, manpower, and wartime-policy systems |
| Evolution II, Total War State | +50 percentage points | Every compatible registered wartime law or policy category |
| Evolution III, Totalen Krieg!!! | +50 percentage points | Evolution II categories, with the two extreme final steps unlocked |

The War Support figures replace one another. Evolution III grants 50 points, not 10 + 25 + 50 + 50. Law coverage is cumulative. A category reached through several descriptions still advances only once.

War Support is applied before the new extreme-law penalties are evaluated. The gain stops at the normal War Support ceiling. Unused points are discarded and do not become Political Power, manpower, Stability, or another reward.

## Compulsory advance, voluntary response

Every valid recipient is affected regardless of ideology, size, faction, subject status, player control, peace, or involvement in another crisis. A country does not escape the event by leaving its notification open. A human and an AI country follow the same advancement rules.

Ordinary purchase costs and the ordinary political conditions for buying a law do not obstruct this forced advance. An actual structural prerequisite remains binding. For example, the event cannot advance a policy inside an institution that the country does not possess or manufacture a DLC system that is not loaded.

The player receives an explanation of what already changed. The acknowledgment is not a second opportunity to accept or refuse the worldwide order.

After the firing, the country can keep the laws, reverse ordinary laws through their normal controls, or pay for an extreme-law reversal. The event does not add a global demobilization prohibition. Another firing can undo a previous reversal by advancing the country again.

## What the player does between firings

Early in the campaign, the player decides whether compulsory mobilization creates an opening worth exploiting. Additional recruitable population can support reinforcement and expansion, while a more mobilized economy can accelerate armament.

Later, the player must compare the next legal step with actual needs. A country with ample manpower but severe equipment shortages should fear further recruitment. A resource-poor country must account for the worldwide effects of restricted exports. A country already at the ordinary maximums knows that an Evolution III firing can place it under one or both extreme laws.

At the extreme, the meaningful choices are asymmetric. Keeping Totalen Krieg!!! while leaving Totalen Menschen!!! preserves a war-industrial economy with a less damaged workforce. Keeping Totalen Menschen!!! while leaving Totalen Krieg!!! preserves exceptional recruitment while surrendering the industrial emergency's bonuses. Leaving both is expensive and offers the clearest recovery. Remaining under both can be justified by a desperate defensive war, but even maximum War Support leaves severe economic damage.

## Worldwide participation

The recipient set contains countries that actually exist at the start of the firing. It includes subjects and governments that remain in existence despite losing their territory. Dormant tags are not created to receive laws. Countries created after the firing join the next one rather than receiving a retroactive grant.

Special countries are not excluded merely because they are unusual. Human states created by other Chaos Redux events remain normal participants. An actual nonhuman country receives the global War Support change and advances whatever compatible law systems it genuinely uses. A human civilian-law family does not get installed into a nonhuman system solely to manufacture an apparent result.

A missing or incompatible category does not exempt a country from its other categories. It also does not produce compensation.

## The two endpoints

Totalen Krieg!!! follows Total Mobilization in the economy family. It represents a 99% economic mobilization policy.

Totalen Menschen!!! follows Scraping the Barrel in the conscription family. It establishes a 99% legal recruitment ceiling over the population eligible for that country's recruitment system.

Neither law is an extra spirit added on top of the previous law. Each replaces the law in its existing category. The two laws remain independent, so a country can hold either one or both.

## Boundaries that preserve the idea

There is no extra mobilization currency or separate public meter. The player reads the existing law positions and War Support.

The event advances laws. It does not issue free equipment, create divisions, grant technologies, change population totals, or declare wars as a substitute for a legal step.

The event remains a Minor Repeatable, Chaos level 1, Low member of Military Preparation. Late evolutions can be extremely dangerous without changing its catalog identity. Its worldwide reach does not consume one event selection for every country.

## Basis of the specification

The catalog, worldwide scope, law directions, War Support amounts, evolution thresholds, extreme-law names, 99% values, single-step rule, and doubled reversals come directly from the supplied brief. The detailed penalty tables, response presentation, compatibility contract, AI priorities, achievements, and test conditions are author-developed expansions within the request to create full specifications. Their numerical settings are proposed starting balance, not claimed playtest results.
