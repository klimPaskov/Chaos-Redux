# Law Upgrade
## Part 4. Totalen Krieg!!!

## Place and purpose

Totalen Krieg!!! is the final economy law, immediately after Total Mobilization. It replaces the previous economy law rather than adding a second economy-law payload.

Its strategic purpose is exceptional war production purchased by consuming the country's civilian capacity and future development. It should be attractive to a country that must produce equipment immediately and frightening to a country trying to rebuild, conduct research, or sustain a civilian society.

The law is independent of Totalen Menschen!!!. A country may run an extreme war economy without also subjecting almost its entire population to recruitment.

## The 99% mobilization commitment

The law establishes a 99% economic-mobilization policy, leaving a 1% civilian-consumption allocation in its own basic legal setting. This is a deliberately absurd emergency target.

The 99% is not a claim that 99% of every factory, every resource, or national GDP becomes military output. Factory types, integer allocation, infrastructure, trade, other national effects, and the game's economic limits continue to matter. The interface must distinguish the law's allocation from the country's actual resulting consumer-goods requirement.

The intended law-level consumer-goods baseline is 1%. A country-specific implementation must be verified against the installed game's economic calculation. It must not lower a global minimum for every country or silently describe an unsupported cosmetic label as an implemented 99% policy.

The military-industrial bonuses below are separate from that allocation. A low legal allowance for civilian consumption is not itself an output multiplier.

## Proposed wartime benefits

These are the complete positive contributions of this law while its holder is at war. They are proposed starting balance, not measured live-game outcomes.

| Area | Law contribution |
| --- | ---: |
| Military factory output | +100% |
| Dockyard output | +75% |
| Military factory construction speed | +100% |
| Dockyard construction speed | +75% |
| Production efficiency cap | +20 percentage points |

The law does not grant factory buildings, equipment, military technology, stockpiled resources, fuel, trained recruits, or free production efficiency. A larger efficiency cap still has to be reached by operating production lines.

Resource shortages, bombing, supply problems, and foreign economic commitments remain relevant. The law does not waive them.

## Proposed civilian penalties

The endpoints below are losses relative to the capacity before this law's own contribution. Intermediate War Support uses the relationship in Part 6.

| Area | At 0% War Support | At 100% War Support |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Civilian factory construction speed | -95% | -75% |
| Infrastructure construction speed | -85% | -50% |
| Factory and infrastructure repair speed | -70% | -40% |
| Research speed | -60% | -25% |
| Population growth | -95% | -75% |
| Political Power gain | -25% | -10% |
| Stability | -40 percentage points | -20 percentage points |

The civilian penalties persist for as long as the law is held. They are not repeated instant deductions each day or each event firing.

Population growth is reduced, not turned into a recurring mass-death effect. Existing population does not disappear. Any separate demographic catastrophe remains the responsibility of the event or system that actually causes it.

The Stability contribution is an ongoing law penalty. Leaving the law removes that contribution. It does not refund unrelated Stability lost to war, other laws, or other events.

## War Support does not solve the civilian problem

A country at maximum War Support still loses three quarters of the law's affected civilian-construction capacity and population growth, before other effects. Support makes the emergency more coherent and less destructive, but it does not restore normal life.

The government can therefore have widespread support for the war and still be sacrificing its long-term economy. The player's choice should remain substantive at 100% War Support.

## Peacetime operation

When the country is not at war, the five wartime benefits in the first table are inactive. The civilian penalties and the exceptional economic commitment remain until the country leaves the law.

A peace settlement does not automatically undo an extreme legal system. This makes the reversal action important and prevents a country from using the law as a routine peacetime industrial optimization.

The change between war and peace does not add a law step, grant War Support, charge a fee, or reset an achievement. It changes only the law's war-dependent effects.

## Interaction with ordinary Total Mobilization

The new law's complete payload replaces Total Mobilization's payload. The implementation must not retain an old recruitable-population reduction or another former-law modifier invisibly beneath the new law.

Country-specific conditions that mean “at least Total Mobilization” should recognize this law through their owner's compatible progression. Conditions that intentionally require the exact historical law are not broadened without examining their purpose.

A later reversal restores the actual Total Mobilization law, including its normal effects. It does not restore a stale snapshot of every national modifier that existed before entry.

## Strategic situations

A country with substantial industry, equipment shortages, and a war approaching its decisive stage can gain an enormous production window. A country with little industry cannot conjure an arsenal from the bonus alone.

A heavily bombed country faces a real conflict between extra military output and poorer repair capacity. A country relying on imports remains exposed when simultaneous law upgrades push exporters toward Closed Economy.

Keeping this economy law while reversing Totalen Menschen!!! is an intended recovery route. It exchanges extraordinary recruitment for a functioning workforce while retaining the emergency war-industrial commitment.

## Exit

The manual reversal returns the country to Total Mobilization and costs twice the current ordinary adjacent law-change price. Its full transaction and availability rules are in Part 6.

The economy law does not add a permanent reconstruction debt or a second recovery minigame after exit. Existing damage, equipment losses, disrupted construction, lost research time, and missed population growth already provide durable consequences.
