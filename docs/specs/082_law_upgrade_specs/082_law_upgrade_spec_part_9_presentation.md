# Law Upgrade
## Part 9. Presentation, writing direction, and assets

## Presentation budget

The event uses the existing country report, existing law interface, existing War Support display, existing event information and history surfaces, and one small native decision category containing at most two reversal actions.

It does not add a dedicated scripted window, map overlay, new meter, focus tree, portrait, unit model, leader animation, or super-event. The extreme law names provide enough spectacle without creating a second presentation system.

No new national spirit is necessary for storytelling. Mechanical effect carriers must not produce a row of redundant visible spirits that merely repeat the two laws.

## The worldwide report

A human recipient receives one report for the completed firing. Its artwork and narrative direction reflect the applied profile. The report describes changes that have already occurred.

The report's mechanical information shows the War Support grant and actual result, followed by the important law changes. Economy, conscription, and trade come first. Other participating categories are grouped into a bounded summary with readable details.

Each displayed category distinguishes “advanced”, “already at maximum”, and “unable to advance”. Unavailable systems do not appear as a long list of irrelevant missing DLC.

A country reaching an extreme law receives a prominent warning about its severe current penalties and doubled exit price. A country that remained several steps below the cap must not see wording claiming it has already enacted total economic or population mobilization.

There is one acknowledgment option. It does not apply mechanics and cannot refuse, delay, or multiply the firing.

## Law information

The two law entries show their fixed names exactly as supplied.

Totalen Krieg!!! explains its 99% economic-mobilization commitment, distinguishes its allocation from actual consumer-goods results, identifies war-only benefits, and lists the current civilian penalties.

Totalen Menschen!!! explains the 99% legal recruitment setting, ongoing mobilization rather than an instant grant, and the current workforce and training penalties.

Both explain that current War Support reduces penalties while leaving them severe. A tooltip must not imply that 100% support eliminates the cost.

The law panel also identifies the applicable reversal action and its required immediate predecessor. The reversal action itself is the authoritative source of the current confirmed price.

## Reversal presentation

The native decision category appears only while the country holds at least one extreme law. It contains the relevant action or both actions.

Each action shows the current law, the law restored by the action, the current doubled Political Power price, the fact that already-deployed forces are not automatically disbanded, and the principal effects that would remain from the other law.

A price change before confirmation must be visible rather than silently charging an old tooltip's different amount. Insufficient funds explain the actual missing price, not a generic unavailable label.

No action displays a fake duration or cooldown. A successful reversal is immediate after the valid transaction.

## Event Details and historical records

The event's general Details text explains the premise and escalating mobilization direction. It stays concise and nonnumeric where required by the shared Details convention.

Evolution descriptions identify how the scope widens. Actual outcome records carry the numbers and observed law transitions.

The shared history records one world firing and its profile, with country outcomes accessible through the existing appropriate surface. It does not generate a separate world-firing count for every recipient.

Evolution activation and actual law entry remain distinct historical events. The first activation of Evolution III does not claim that every country immediately enacted either extreme law.

## Narrative direction

The tone moves from administrative confidence to desperate legal absurdity.

The baseline report treats mobilization as a supposedly prudent preparation that every government has independently found impossible to avoid. Emergency State broadens the same rationale into institutions that were previously civilian. Total War State portrays society being organized around an indefinite emergency. Totalen Krieg!!! presents a government that has stopped recognizing a meaningful boundary between the country and its war effort.

Humor belongs in bureaucratic reasoning and an occasional dry acknowledgment. It must not trivialize real civilian suffering or suggest that catastrophic workforce collapse is a harmless upgrade.

This package provides direction and information requirements, not final event prose, translated strings, option jokes, or historical quotations.

## Artwork plan

The four report images should share a 1936–1945 visual language while becoming more extreme. Use distinct scenes, not the same image with different color overlays.

| Image | Composition and story |
| --- | --- |
| Baseline | A mobilization office processing industrial and recruitment paperwork, with an operating factory or assembly floor visible beyond it |
| Emergency State | Civilian transport, industrial scheduling, and recruitment brought under an emergency administrative command |
| Total War State | A city or industrial district reorganized around uninterrupted war production, with visibly diminished ordinary civilian activity |
| Totalen Krieg!!! | An enormous mobilization apparatus consuming the remaining civilian workplace, with queues and deserted workstations conveying the contradiction |

The images depict fictional composite situations. They are not falsely presented as documentary photographs of a particular real incident.

No readable text, precise numbers, nationality-dependent flag, identifiable historical leader, or critical tooltip information should be baked into the artwork.

## Icon subjects

The economy-law icon should combine an industrial engine or factory with an unmistakable emergency-war commitment. The conscription-law icon should depict a recruitment apparatus drawing people away from civilian work.

The two reversal icons need distinct silhouettes: one restores a civilian-industrial function, the other releases the recruitment apparatus. They are not simply recolored copies of the law icons.

The decision category can reuse a suitable established generic category icon after verifying its source and active consumer. A new category illustration or background is outside this event's asset budget.

## Formats and production ownership

Report images use the established 210×176 report-image pipeline and its tilted-card processing. Law icons use the verified 64×64 idea consumer. Reversal icons use the verified 32×32 decision consumer.

Three achievement subjects each require the three established visual states, producing nine 64×64 achievement outputs from three approved source designs.

The planned new final asset budget is 17 images: four reports, two law icons, two reversal icons, and nine achievement-state outputs. Reused category artwork is not counted as a new asset.

All sizes remain subject to checking the actual consumer and canonical processor before production. No final assets were generated in this planning task.

The generated-event-art role owns fictional report imagery. The icon-artist role owns law, decision, and achievement subjects. The source-research role verifies any reused reference or archival input. The parent implementation owner integrates the approved assets.
