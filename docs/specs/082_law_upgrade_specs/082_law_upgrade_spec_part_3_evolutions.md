# Law Upgrade
## Part 3. Evolutions and escalation

## Eligibility, activation, and application

The Chaos thresholds make an evolution eligible. For an already-running Event 82 lifecycle, evolution activation follows the project's shared dynamic evolution timing. A threshold crossing is not itself a law-change event.

The three operations remain distinct.

1. Eligibility permits the evolution to develop.
2. Activation expands the profile available to a later Event 82 firing.
3. A firing applies that profile and advances laws.

Activation never grants War Support or advances a law by itself. A law reaches its next step only through an actual firing or an allowed manual law change.

The first worldwide firing may open at the highest eligible enabled evolution. This preserves evolved openings in campaigns that already have high Chaos. The opening still applies only one law step.

## Evolution I: Emergency State

**Chaos requirement: 200+.**

The War Support grant becomes 25 percentage points. Economy and conscription continue their ordinary progression. Trade begins moving toward Closed Economy.

Compatible military, economic, production, manpower, and wartime-policy categories also begin advancing. These categories should be institutions that already exist and already have a meaningful wartime direction. The event does not add a new policy system simply to fill out the list.

The player should feel that an exceptional mobilization order has become a general governing method. More institutions now move in the same direction, and avoiding an unwanted economy law alone may no longer preserve a peacetime balance.

### Starting timing proposal

Use a nominal dynamic evolution MTTH value of 90 days after eligibility. Multiply that value by 0.75 when at least half of existing normal-civilian countries are at war. Multiply it by 0.50 when Chaos is at least 400. Combine eligible modifiers and keep the nominal value within 20 to 180 days.

These are proposed MTTH inputs, not a promised deadline or an asserted cumulative probability. The installed shared adapter determines how they translate into timing.

## Evolution II: Total War State

**Chaos requirement: 600+.**

The War Support grant becomes 50 percentage points. All categories from Evolution I continue to move one step. Every other compatible registered wartime law or policy category can now participate.

This wider scope covers a policy only when its owner has explicitly defined an ordered wartime branch. A set of mutually exclusive administrative styles or military specializations does not become a ladder merely because it appears in a menu.

Evolution II is valuable even when a particular installation has no additional categories beyond Evolution I. Its larger War Support grant still matters. It must not manufacture nominal policies to create an artificial difference.

### Starting timing proposal

Use a nominal base of 90 days after the prior evolution is active and the Chaos requirement is met. Apply a factor of 0.75 when at least half of normal-civilian countries are at war. Apply another factor of 0.75 when at least half of those countries are already at or above both War Economy and Extensive Conscription. Keep the nominal value within 20 to 180 days.

## Evolution III: Totalen Krieg!!!

**Chaos requirement: 800+.**

The War Support grant remains 50 percentage points. All compatible categories continue their ordinary one-step advance. The economy and conscription paths gain their two extreme final steps.

Totalen Krieg!!! follows Total Mobilization. Totalen Menschen!!! follows Scraping the Barrel.

The evolution makes those endpoints available. It does not put every country into them immediately. A weakly mobilized state still needs several firings to reach either endpoint.

### Starting timing proposal

Use a nominal base of 90 days after Evolution II is active and Chaos is at least 800. Apply a factor of 0.50 when at least half of normal-civilian countries already hold Total Mobilization or a stronger registered economy law. Apply a factor of 0.75 when at least half of all relevant normal-civilian countries hold both Total Mobilization-or-higher and Scraping the Barrel-or-higher. Keep the nominal value within 20 to 180 days.

The denominators use the relevant existing normal-civilian countries. A denominator of zero makes that modifier inapplicable. No nonhuman actor with an unrelated population system should artificially accelerate a human legal transformation.

## Falling Chaos

An activated evolution remains part of Event 82's history when Chaos later falls. The event does not automatically reverse current laws or revoke already-established legal positions.

Before activation, falling below the Chaos threshold suspends further progress toward that evolution. It does not grant a partial evolution or a reduced law step.

This separates the conditions that produced an emergency from the difficulty of reversing it. A country that already holds an extreme law must still use its reversal route even after the world becomes calmer.

## Evolution settings

The profiles form a cumulative chain. Evolution II depends on Evolution I, and Evolution III depends on Evolution II. When a lower evolution is disabled, higher profiles are unavailable for future applications until that dependency is enabled again.

Turning off an evolution does not remove an already-held extreme law. Reversal remains available to prevent a settings change from trapping a country. Disabled profiles cannot create new extreme-law entries or silently record an activation.

Re-enabling a previously activated profile restores its application without repeating its historical activation rewards or logs. There are no activation rewards to replay.

## Voluntary entry into an extreme law

While Evolution III and its dependencies are active and enabled, a country at the immediate predecessor can voluntarily buy the extreme law through the law system. The purchase uses the ordinary current one-step law cost.

For voluntary entry, the proposed political gate is war plus at least 80% War Support. These conditions apply to both extreme laws. They do not limit the compulsory Event 82 advance.

The country cannot buy an extreme law directly from a lower position. It must first reach Total Mobilization or Scraping the Barrel. Voluntary entry produces the same law effects, ongoing War Support sensitivity, and doubled reversal cost as forced entry.

No menu label or AI preference should suggest that voluntary entry is a routine peacetime improvement.
