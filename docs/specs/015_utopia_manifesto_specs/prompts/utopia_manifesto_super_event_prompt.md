# Super-event prompt for Event 015 Utopia Manifesto

Research and prepare super-event presentation packages for the two late Event 015 Utopia Manifesto candidates. Read the full spec pack under `docs/specs/015_utopia_manifesto_specs/` first, especially part 5.

Do not treat working labels as final localisation. Final title text, button remarks, quotes, cultural allusions, and audio choices are research-gated.

Super-event candidate 1:

- working label: commonwealth reveal super-event
- role: ideological transformation and regional order announcement
- trigger direction: Utopian Commonwealth formation or Eutopian League leadership with several willing members, high Eutopia, high Common Stores, and low recent coercion
- tone: hopeful, formal, strange, civic, public, and not generic apocalypse wording
- image direction: public halls, common stores, island or inland island map, small-country delegates, and period documentary or symbolic civic scene
- quote direction: public domain or historical text about commonwealth, public good, shared need, law, or the limits of perfect societies
- audio mood: ceremonial, restrained, civic, maybe early music or public domain orchestral, one to two minutes if practical

Super-event candidate 2:

- working label: no-place mandate super-event
- role: coercive world-order announcement
- trigger direction: hidden branch or Good Place Mandate finisher after high Fracture and successful needful wars or coercive wards
- tone: alarming, bureaucratic, righteous in its own voice, not a monster reveal
- image direction: measuring instruments, occupied ward ledgers, geometric island seal, public shelves turned into administrative power, no generic fire or skull imagery
- quote direction: public domain or historical text about measure, law, necessity, tyranny of ideals, or false common good
- audio mood: formal, uneasy, processional, licensed or public domain, not drones or test tones

Use `chaosx_super_event_text_researcher` for quotes, exact wording, attribution, button remarks, and cultural references. Use `chaosx_super_event_audio_researcher` for audio search, license verification, download, conversion, and notes. Coordinate images with the asset prompt.

Required output:

- research note at `docs/super_events/015_utopia_manifesto_super_event_research.md`
- quote candidates and selected quote for each super-event
- selected button remark or cultural allusion for each super-event
- title direction transformed into final title only after research and source review
- audio candidates and selected track with source, creator, license, duration, and final paths
- image handoff to asset workflow
- implementation notes for localisation keys, audio ids, image ids, and settings-aware playback

Reject unclear audio licenses. Do not invent quotes. Do not use default audio as final.
