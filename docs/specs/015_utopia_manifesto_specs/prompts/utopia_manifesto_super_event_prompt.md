# Super-event research prompt for Event 015, `utopia_manifesto`

Use this prompt with `chaos-redux-super-events` plus the narrow research subagents.

Read the event specs and asset prompt first. The initial event does not use a super-event. These super-events are late route outcomes.

## Research gates

Do not use any unresearched final title, button text, quote, slogan, cultural allusion, lyric fragment, or audio track. Treat all title, quote, remark, and audio wording here as direction only. The super-event researcher must verify wording, attribution, copyright status, audio license, and source links.

Use:

- `chaosx_super_event_text_researcher` for quotes, title-style references, button remarks, slogans, and cultural allusions
- `chaosx_super_event_audio_researcher` for public domain or clearly licensed music
- `chaosx_generated_event_art` for generated super-event images from the asset prompt

## Super-event A, New Utopia proclamation

### Role

Ideological transformation and regional order announcement. This should fire only when a small country has built a durable Utopian model rather than merely accepting the manifesto.

### Trigger direction

- accepted manifesto
- late proclamation focus or decision complete
- high Consent
- high Surplus
- stable Vocation Balance
- low Overreach
- geography condition complete, such as island or coastal network, inland ring, port charter, or federated port partner
- at least one meaningful external recognition, Friend, League member, or integrated district if the country has grown

### Title direction

Research required. The title should be short, specific, and tied to public recognition of a new commonwealth. Avoid generic apocalypse or generic utopia wording.

### Description direction

Describe common stores, household councils, public work, and foreign observers treating the experiment as a real state model. Keep uncertainty about durability. Do not list mechanics.

### Main quote direction

Research required. Prefer public domain or early modern sources about commonwealth, justice, need, public good, or humanist reform. Thomas More can be considered, but do not force a More quote if a better public-domain source fits.

### Button remark direction

Research required. It can be a restrained literary or civic allusion. Keep it short and not comedic.

### Audio mood

Solemn civic procession, early modern choral mood, quiet hope, and unease. Prefer 1 to 2 minutes. Must be real licensed or public domain music, not generated tones or placeholder audio.

### Image direction

Use generated super-event image from asset prompt: period civic square or harbor with common stores and manifesto symbolism. No readable text.

## Super-event B, Marked Bounds doctrine

### Role

Dangerous ideological reveal and regional escalation. This route shows the manifesto being used to justify coercive land claims.

### Trigger direction

- Marked Bounds route complete
- Overreach very high
- multiple forced integrations, marked districts, or contested claims
- at least one foreign reaction exists, such as guarantee, conference, sanctions, or rival aid

### Title direction

Research required. Short, specific, and centered on boundary doctrine becoming public. Avoid jokey wording.

### Description direction

Describe surveyors, boundary posts, guarded settlement columns, and neighboring states preparing for the consequences. Do not call it a warning. Show the situation through visible actions.

### Main quote direction

Research required. Prefer public-domain sources about justice, false necessity, land, conquest, or the corruption of public good. Do not use unsourced quote-site material.

### Button remark direction

Research required. Grim, terse, and non-comedic.

### Audio mood

Austere march, tense strings, or dark civic hymn. Must have clear source and license. Keep final audio unique unless reuse is explicitly approved.

### Image direction

Use generated super-event image from asset prompt: boundary posts, old manuscript symbolism, surveyors, troops, and wary neighbors. No modern props or readable text.
