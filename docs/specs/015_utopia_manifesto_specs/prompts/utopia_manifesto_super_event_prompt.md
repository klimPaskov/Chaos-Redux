# Super-Event Research Prompt: Event 15 Utopia Manifesto

Use the Chaos Redux super-events skill and the dedicated text, audio, and image subagents. This prompt defines one route-sensitive super-event package. Do not write final localisation from the working labels in the source specs.

## Event identity

- Event ID: 15
- Event slug: `utopia_manifesto`
- Super-event role: ideological and regional-order proclamation
- Working internal key: `utopia_manifesto_regional_proclamation`

## Trigger meaning

The super-event fires only when:

- the Event 15 actor has completed a route-specific final identity
- the island or capital-ring project is complete
- at least three meaningful external partners, associates, league members, or recognized agreements exist
- one regional commonwealth objective has succeeded
- the actor is not a trivial puppet
- the super-event has not already fired

Formation alone is not enough. The moment should represent a real regional order.

## Route variants

### Voluntary commonwealth

Emotional role:

- a small country has made local consent, common provision, and voluntary association into a regional model

Visible world state:

- public assemblies
- open stores
- linked municipalities
- small states entering a compact without immediate annexation

Text tone:

- civic, hopeful, cautious, grounded
- avoid universal salvation claims

### Council union

Emotional role:

- worker and civic councils have built shared production, reserves, and defense across borders

Visible world state:

- delegates
- common workshops
- reserve shipments
- cross-border councils

Text tone:

- collective and material
- avoid generic revolutionary slogans

### Planned Utopia

Emotional role:

- a network of planned cities, transport, housing, and measured provision has become a regional standard

Visible world state:

- completed settlements
- rail and harbor connections
- public works
- expert delegations

Text tone:

- precise, civic, impressive, slightly uneasy when Assignment is high

### Closed island-state

Emotional role:

- a fortified society of service, reserves, assignment, and dependent outposts has declared itself complete

Visible world state:

- sealed boundary
- mass service
- guarded stores
- disciplined districts
- foreign fear and fascination

Text tone:

- severe, ordered, self-certain
- do not glamorize forced labor or assigned colonies without showing the visible cost

### Practical commonwealth

Emotional role:

- the country has used the manifesto as a critical instrument, keeping plural institutions and social guarantees while rejecting textual infallibility

Visible world state:

- public debate
- local institutions
- aid and reconstruction
- a loose plural compact

Text tone:

- reflective, civic, modest
- avoid making it the only morally intelligent route

## Required text research

Spawn `chaosx_super_event_text_researcher` with a bounded brief covering all five variants.

### Title

- final title requires research and writing
- keep it short and specific
- connect the imagined place becoming a real regional order
- avoid generic titles about a new dawn, perfect world, final society, or history changing

### Main quote

Research several verified candidates.

Preferred source directions:

- Thomas More's public-domain text
- public-domain humanist or political writing
- a verified statement on common provision, work, freedom, law, place, or political imagination

The quote should fit the exact variant or a shared package. It should not falsely present *Utopia* as an uncomplicated endorsement.

For every candidate, document:

- exact text
- author or speaker
- work or document
- date or period
- source URL
- public-domain or copyright note
- attribution confidence
- fit by route

### Button remark or cultural reference

Research a short, meaningful reaction.

Possible direction:

- brief public-domain literary echo
- short title-like reference
- restrained irony about no place becoming a place
- civic or political phrase

Modern copyrighted material must remain very short and should be avoided when a public-domain option fits.

### Description

Write final description after route and trigger context are known.

It should:

- explain what the world can see
- identify the regional order
- distinguish route institutions
- preserve unresolved tension where relevant
- avoid mechanics and reward lists

## Required audio research

Spawn `chaosx_super_event_audio_researcher`.

Find one unique final track for the Event 15 super-event package, or separate route tracks only if the implementation and licensing burden is justified and accepted before wiring.

The track must be real music. It must not be a generated tone, drone, test waveform, abstract ambience, or placeholder.

Preferred mood:

- civic and reflective
- hopeful without simple triumph
- capable of carrying unease for the planner or closed variant
- period-compatible or timeless enough for the setting

Preferred rights:

- public domain recording
- Creative Commons recording with compatible terms
- official archive recording with clear terms

Document:

- title
- composer or creator
- performer or recording source
- source URL
- license
- composition rights
- recording rights
- duration
- attribution
- original source path
- final WAV path
- editing and conversion
- suggested audio ID
- route fit
- uncertainty

Final WAV requirements:

- 44.1 kHz
- event-scoped music folder
- two minutes or less unless an exception is approved and documented
- unique audio ID and settings-aware playback wiring
