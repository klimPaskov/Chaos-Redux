## Lane guidance

### Center trunk

Keep the recovery, survey, first store, interim charter, and interpretive congress visually central. The player should see that every route begins from the same material problem.

### Political lanes

- Consent occupies the left civic lane.
- Common Table occupies the left-center collective lane.
- Guardians occupies the center planning lane.
- Closed Island occupies the right authoritarian lane.
- The hidden humanist route should appear between Consent and Guardians or as a concealed lower bridge, depending on line clarity.

### Support lanes

Callings, stores, settlements, and defense should run beneath and between political lanes. Route connectors should alter them without making every support focus mutually exclusive.

### Expansion and diplomacy

Foreign Commonwealth and Necessary Ground should be separate lower branches. They converge through stewardship, associates, and final proof.

### Crisis lane

The crisis lane should be hidden until triggered. It should reconnect to a valid route and should not become a permanent second tree.

### Formation lane

Formation should appear low in the tree and require both domestic and external proof. Post-formation content should remain visible after the cosmetic identity changes.
