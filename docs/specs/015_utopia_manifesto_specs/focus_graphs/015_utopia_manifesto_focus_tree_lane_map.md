## Visual layout notes

- Opening trunk should be centered and compact.
- Normal interpretation routes should spread horizontally with enough space for branch icons and mutual exclusions.
- Common Stores and Vocation branches should sit near each other because their decisions interact.
- Needful Land should be visually separate from Just War, but connected enough to show that claims require just-war review.
- Geography adaptation can sit under the middle of the tree and use mutually exclusive conditional focus availability instead of separate visible trees for every country type.
- Marked Bounds should be hidden or visually isolated until revealed. Do not clutter the ordinary route view with it from the start.
- Late proclamation should converge at the bottom and show different finishers for peaceful, League, necessary, and hardline paths.
