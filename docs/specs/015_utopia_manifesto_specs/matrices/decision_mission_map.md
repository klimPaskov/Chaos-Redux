# Utopia Manifesto expanded decision and mission map

## Main categories

| Category | Phase | Purpose | Clutter control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Need Ledger | all accepted phases | show stores, work, land, wards, subjects, and Fracture | one header and selected target panels |
| Common Stores | reading through ultimate branch | build and spend public stores | show only urgent state projects and selected routes |
| Vocational Rolls | counting through late game | manage chosen work and public occasion work | route-specific actions only |
| Need Dossiers | first dossier onward | identify valid land requests and foreign targets | capped active targets and selected target display |
| Wards and Charters | settlement onward | integrate, release, charter, or manage wards | active ward mission cap |
| Common Table | diplomacy route | manage League members and shared stores | visible after route unlock |
| World Household Enforcement | late tree | export, impose, or renounce Utopia abroad | selected target and doctrine filters |
| Daughter Commonwealth Office | late subject branch | manage utopian puppets and local values | selected subject cards or compact header |
| Outopia | hidden route | manage high Fracture precinct actions | hidden until reveal |

## Core cost palette

Use civilian factory burden, support equipment, infantry equipment, trucks, trains, convoys, fuel, manpower, local support, stability, war support, resistance, compliance, supply, rail control, port control, tied-down divisions, legitimacy, and timed objectives. Political power and command power can appear when the action is bureaucratic or command-based, but they should not dominate.

## Timed mission families

| Mission family | Objective style | Success | Failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trial Storehouse | build or maintain stores in named state | raises Stores and unlocks projects | Empty Stores pressure |
| Trial Trade Roll | complete production or training goal | raises work acceptance | strike or Fracture |
| Guard the Storehouses | place supplied divisions in state or subject | lowers unrest and protects stores | riot or sabotage |
| Keep the Route Open | hold port, rail, or convoy route | store convoy succeeds | convoy loss and subject shortage |
| Stabilize the Ward | lower resistance and build local stores before deadline | ward can become charter or subject | revolt or foreign appeal |
| Charter Congress | meet local Consent and observer conditions | peaceful subject or League member | relations loss and Fracture |
| Necessary Ward Order | hold target and impose stores before deadline | direct ward or integration path | harsh backlash |
| Ring of Full Shelves | maintain several stable subjects or League members | ultimate proof and achievement hook | delays final capstone |
| Renounce False Dossier | remove unjust claims and compensate target | low Fracture route reward | domestic hardliner backlash |
| World Household Congress | hold final late goal with stable network | ultimate route payoff | congress fails and weakens diplomacy |

## Mission quality targets

Every timed mission should require action such as holding a state, keeping a route open, supplying divisions, building a state project, completing a survey, escorting convoys, stabilizing a subject, or controlling rail. Do not implement passive stockpile checks as the main mission design.

## Cleanup rules

Clear selected targets when states change owner, when the host loses the route, when Land Need is solved, when a ward becomes a core or autonomous charter, when a target country dies, when a subject changes final fate, or when the event actor is annexed. Claims generated by Need Dossiers should decay or become renounceable when they no longer match the ledger.
