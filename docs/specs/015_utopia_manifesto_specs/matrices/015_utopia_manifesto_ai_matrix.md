## Targeting safety

AI cannot target:

- majors for ordinary Need claims
- countries with much stronger army or industry unless target is collapsing and a defensive route exists
- dead countries
- allies, faction members, or League members unless a special settlement route exists
- states without control path or state group support
- overseas states without naval access and convoys
- states already marked by another active Utopian process
