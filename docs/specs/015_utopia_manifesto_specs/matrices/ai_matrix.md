# Utopia Manifesto AI matrix

| Situation | Focus priority | Decision priority | Expansion behavior | Safety blocker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| peaceful democratic minor | consent, stores, league | readings, stores, trade adoption | leases and charters | no war against stronger neighbors |
| unstable democratic minor | council, stores, guard | stabilize capital and stores | delay expansion | avoid forced assignments unless crisis |
| communist minor | stores, trade, council or surveyor | worker training and public stores | charters with ideological friends | avoid hidden route before high chaos |
| neutral monarchy | surveyor, council, guard | surveys and household guard | leases or managed wards | avoid full mandate without chaos |
| fascist or militarist minor | mandate, arsenal, guard | forced assignments and need dossiers | coercive demands against weak targets | no suicidal major demand |
| island minor | island, ports, convoy stores | port leases, convoy protection | overseas charters if safe | avoid landlocked branch |
| landlocked minor | inland island, rail, border guard | rail guard and neighbor leases | adjacent claims only with need | avoid naval branch |
| at war | household guard, stores, arsenal | guard stores, raise units, secure supply | no new demands unless defensive | avoid extra fronts |
| high Fracture and high chaos | hidden or mandate route | coercive ledger actions | stronger but riskier expansion | only if not facing certain defeat |
| player neighbor is much stronger | council or defensive route | stores and diplomacy | non-aggressive unless player hostile | no unjustified demand |

AI should always accept the opening event if selected as host. It should then use this matrix, not flat route weights.
