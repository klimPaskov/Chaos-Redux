# Event 15 Utopia Manifesto, Part 5: Country Identity, League, and Formation

All names in this specification are working labels, not final localisation.

## Country-package principle

Event 15 transforms an existing country. It does not normally create a new tag. The original tag, map ownership, army, technology, and existing named people remain the starting material. The event replaces the approved focus tree, creates new institutions, adds route-specific advisors and leadership options, and can apply a route-specific cosmetic identity after the country proves that the manifesto has become a functioning state.

The package must feel specific to the recipient. Dynamic localisation should use the country's original name, capital, demonym, states, leader, coastline, and regional context. The same focus tree architecture should produce different visible projects and foreign cases in an island minor, a landlocked republic, a colonial subject, or a small coastal monarchy.

## Identity continuity

### Original tag

The original tag remains active by default. This avoids unnecessary map disruption and preserves country history, units, claims, technology, and compatibility.

### Original leader

The existing leader remains in office during the opening unless the recipient's country package makes that impossible. The interpretive congress can later retain, replace, subordinate, or transform the office.

### Original flag

The no-suffix base flag is preserved. Event 15 uses route-specific cosmetic tags or route flags only after the corresponding political change. Ideology variants must be distinct and intentional. They must not be simple recolors of one Utopia emblem.

### Original parties

Existing parties remain visible during the founding debate. Route commitment can rename or replace the ruling political organization, but opposition and prior traditions should not vanish instantly.

Route:

Leadership presentation:

### Board of Measure

Route:

Leadership presentation:

## Leader and portrait rules

- Real existing leaders keep sourced portraits already present in the country package.
- New fictional personal leaders use generated portraits and plausible regional personal names.
- Female-presenting portraits use female name pools and supported female metadata.
- Male-presenting portraits use male name pools and no female metadata.
- Institutional councils use institutional names and council portraits.
- The event should not generate a false portrait for a real historical person.
- A dramatic route can use an animated institutional seal or category emblem. Animated leader portraits are not required by default because the event can communicate transformation through the Ledger and final seal without replacing every recipient's leader art.

## Existing military package

The event does not erase the recipient's existing forces. It adds a reinforcement and reform path suited to a weak country.

### Opening army

- existing divisions remain
- existing templates remain
- no free division package on acceptance
- severe supply or equipment deficits are reflected in Need

### Military transformation routes

#### Citizen Watch

- local militia and civil defense
- equipment and manpower cost
- capped by population and district network

#### Worker Defense

- Common Table route
- workplace and commune units
- council authorization

#### Engineer Corps

- Guardians and shared defense route
- support equipment, trucks, army experience
- repair, fortification, and supply roles

#### Service Formations

- Closed Island route
- compulsory service
- stronger manpower access
- Choice and Concord cost

#### Auxiliary Contracts

- foreign mercenary or volunteer support
- expensive and risky
- temporary or contract-bound

### Reinforcement principle

The country grows forces through real institutions.

- district watches
- railway guards
- reserve depots
- engineer schools
- foreign volunteer agreements
- league defense council
- compulsory service registry
- professional officer reform

The tree should not repeatedly spawn generic infantry divisions.

## Technology and research direction

The event should not grant advanced technology because a book was found. It should redirect research and bonuses toward the country's chosen institutions.

Shared priorities:

- agriculture and industry
- construction
- logistics
- engineering
- medicine
- education and research speed
- militia or support companies
- radio and administrative communication

Route priorities:

- Consent favors dispersed industry, civil defense, and education.
- Common Table favors mass education, worker production, and reserve pooling.
- Guardians favors planning, construction, logistics, radar, and technical administration.
- Closed Island favors fortification, autarky, military engineering, and controlled mobilization.
- Hidden humanist favors adaptable research, civilian industry, and institutional resilience.

## The League of Small Places

### Purpose

The league is the external political proof that the manifesto has become more than a domestic experiment. It should help weak states cooperate without instantly becoming subjects of the Event 15 actor.

### Entry conditions

A candidate member should normally have:

- small or medium power status
- no active hostile Need case with the founder
- basic sovereignty
- sufficient relation or shared threat
- willingness to accept reserve and autonomy rules

A major can become a guarantor, observer, or sponsor, but should not normally join and dominate the league.

### Membership rules

Members commit to a selected subset of obligations.

- emergency reserve contribution
- mutual reconstruction
- migration and housing coordination
- technical education
- arbitration
- defensive consultation
- respect for local autonomy

The founder cannot freely annex members without breaking the league's legitimacy.

### Cohesion

League cohesion should be visible or represented through the founder's Ledger and decisions.

Cohesion rises from:

- delivered aid
- respected autonomy
- successful defense
- fair arbitration
- shared reconstruction

Cohesion falls from:

- forced integration
- unmet reserve promises
- unjustified war
- founder dominance
- member ideological conflict
- sponsor interference

#### Plural Compact

### Faction transition

The league becomes a formal defense faction only when:

- minimum member count is met
- at least one shared reserve or reconstruction mission succeeded
- a real external threat exists or members vote for defense integration
- the founder has not broken core autonomy rules, unless the route is the Island Hierarchy

The faction should have shared decisions and goals. It should not exist only as a faction name.

## Associate municipalities

Associates are territories or small states linked to the commonwealth without immediate annexation.

Possible forms:

- autonomous municipality
- cooperative republic
- planned city partner
- leased island community
- reconstruction protectorate
- assigned colony

Each form should define:

- sovereignty
- contribution
- reserve access
- defense obligation
- local government
- exit or status review
- integration path

The same target can move through several forms. A stewardship can become an associate, an associate can integrate by consent, or an assigned colony can revolt and become independent.

## Final formable identity

### Formation fantasy

The recipient proves that the manifesto can organize a real state. It builds common provision, a functioning calling system, a capital or island project, and an external network. The final act proclaims a route-specific Utopian identity.

### Tag handling

Default:

- original tag with cosmetic identity

Use a new tag only if repository review finds a compelling engine or compatibility reason. The event concept does not require one.

### Required proof

Formation should require:

- completed route capstone
- completed island or capital-ring project
- stable or improving Plenty
- route-appropriate Concord
- Choice or Assignment position consistent with route
- completed first external case with a recorded method
- at least one associate, league member, or recognized compact
- no active founding crisis
- no unresolved stewardship failure

### Route-specific formation conditions

#### Voluntary commonwealth

- high Concord
- high Choice
- no penal labor
- no unjustified enforced case
- at least one consensual associate or league member

#### Council union

- functioning calling councils
- strong common stores
- council autonomy not fully abolished
- at least one external commune or league member

#### Planned Utopia

- high Plenty
- completed city network
- Assignment within route-defined stable range
- no active data scandal or district revolt

#### Closed island-state

- completed separation project
- high Assignment
- strong reserves and defense
- can form with low Concord
- receives stronger resistance and diplomatic consequences

#### Practical commonwealth

- hidden humanist route
- high Concord and Choice
- mixed property settlement
- abandoned or peacefully resolved false Need cases
- broad external recognition rather than a large hierarchy

### Formation effects direction

Formation can change:

- cosmetic country name
- adjective
- flag
- ruling party name
- leader title or institutional leader
- route institution idea
- decision category state
- league name and emblem
- final focus lane
- super-event eligibility
- achievements

Formation should not automatically:

- annex league members
- grant all claims as cores
- remove resistance
- refill reserves
- finish every district
- erase route costs

## Post-formation integration

### Capital and district integration

The player completes unfinished district charters and transport links.

### Associate status review

Each associate receives a route-compatible status event or decision.

Possible outcomes:

- remain autonomous
- deepen federation
- integrate by consent
- leave peacefully
- revolt against assigned rule

### Founding generation succession

A mature route should decide how the founding leader, congress, board, or steward is replaced.

- election
- council rotation
- technical appointment
- hereditary or appointed stewardship
- constitutional cabinet succession

This prevents the country package from depending forever on one founding person.

### Second-stage ambitions

Voluntary route:

- regional welfare and arbitration order

Council route:

- transnational worker and reserve network

Guardians route:

- planned city system and technical bloc

Closed Island route:

- enforced network of outposts and strategic corridors

Humanist route:

- plural reform league and cultural influence

## Super-event threshold

Formation alone is not enough for the super-event. The event should become a global presentation moment when the new identity visibly alters regional order.

Suggested threshold:

- formation complete
- at least three meaningful external partners, associates, or recognized agreements
- one regional commonwealth objective complete
- the country still exists and is not a trivial puppet

The super-event variant should reflect the formation method. A voluntary league, council union, measured city network, closed island hierarchy, and practical commonwealth should not use identical description direction or image emphasis.

## Country-package failure states

### Government outlives the institutions

The leader proclaims Utopia while Plenty, Concord, or project completion is inadequate.

Result direction:

- formation blocked
- legitimacy crisis
- route correction or coercive shortcut

### League becomes a subject network

The founder uses integration and pressure to erase member sovereignty.

Result direction:

- cohesion collapse
- foreign opposition
- voluntary formation disqualifier
- Closed Island convergence possible

### Planner succession crisis

The Board of Measure cannot agree on a successor or data standard.

Result direction:

- project delays
- local autonomy demand
- technical coup or constitutional correction

### Council deadlock

Calling councils refuse national targets.

Result direction:

- shortage mission
- central plan referendum
- hidden humanist compromise possible

### Closed Island reserve crisis

Autarky and militarization exhaust the stores.

Result direction:

- severe Need
- repression or reopening choice
- associate rebellion risk

### Humanist diffusion

The hidden route becomes so flexible that it loses a distinct state project.

Result direction:

- the player must codify a social floor and public institutions
- failure produces an ordinary mixed state with weakened final payoff, not an automatic collapse
