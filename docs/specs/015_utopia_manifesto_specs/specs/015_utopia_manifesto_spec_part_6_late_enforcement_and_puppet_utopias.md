# Event 015 Utopia Manifesto, part 6 late enforcement and puppet utopias

This part expands the late game systems that should appear once the focus tree has moved beyond domestic reform. The event should not end when the host builds its own stores and laws. The stronger version asks what happens when a country that believes it has built Utopia tries to apply that model to other countries.

All names are working labels, not final localisation.

## Design purpose

The late game should create a new play loop:

1. Prove domestic Utopia through stores, chosen work, housing, law, defense, and land use.
2. Select a foreign target through the Need Ledger or Common Table.
3. Choose invitation, survey, wardship, or enforcement.
4. Create a charter state, protectorate, ward, League member, or hidden precinct.
5. Keep the new utopian country functioning through stores, local consent, work acceptance, and autonomy.
6. Decide whether the subject becomes a member, remains a puppet, integrates, rebels, or corrupts the whole project.

This loop should be demanding. A utopian puppet is not only a flag. It is a test of whether the overlord can keep its promises outside its original borders.

## Late enforcement doctrine

The focus tree should unlock one or more enforcement doctrines. These are not separate ideologies by themselves. They are late external policies shaped by the political route.

| Doctrine | Main route basis | What it does | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Invitation of the Table | Free Household and Morean | invites minors, breakaways, and occupied regions into charters or League membership | slow growth and dependency on high consent |
| Technical Wardship | Surveyor | builds protectorates through surveys, housing, stores, and training | dependence and bureaucratic backlash |
| Mandate Enforcement | Good Place Mandate | forces states into wardship through ultimata, wars, and postwar settlement | revolt, coalition pressure, high Fracture |
| No-Place Projection | Outopia | converts foreign territory into precincts of the no-place ledger | severe instability and diplomatic fear |
| Renunciation Diplomacy | any low-Fracture route | renounces false claims and builds influence through restraint | weaker expansion and slower achievements |

The player should not be able to use all doctrines at full strength. A route can borrow limited tools from another doctrine, but the strongest version should require the matching political route or convergence ending.

## Target selection

Late external action should use selected targets and active caps. The player should not see every country and every state as a visible button at once.

### Target tiers

| Target type | Eligible direction | Usual actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| willing minor | small or weak country with good relations, high instability, or ideological affinity | invitation, store aid, charter treaty, League membership |
| protected neighbor | neighbor under threat, weak subject, civil conflict remnant, or released state | technical wardship, guarantee, local ledger mission |
| occupied ward | state or country taken during needful war | ward constitution, local stores, trade rolls, integration or release |
| strategic need target | state with port, rail, food, resources, or security relevance | lease, concession, ultimatum, temporary claim |
| major under pressure | major that is losing a war, fragmented, or under high chaos | rare congress pressure or settlement after major defeat |
| hidden no-place target | target selected by high Fracture logic | precinct conversion and severe reaction |

Majors should usually resist direct utopian enforcement unless they are defeated, fragmented, dependent, or under a special high-chaos condition. The system should target minors and war-torn regions by default.

## Puppet utopia forms

The system should support several subject forms. The implementation can use subject types, cosmetic tags, national spirits, decision flags, or a combination. The design requirement is that each form plays differently.

| Subject form | Source doctrine | Public identity | Mechanic profile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Charter Commonwealth | Invitation of the Table | high autonomy utopian subject or future League member | high Local Consent, moderate Stores, low Dependence |
| Surveyor Protectorate | Technical Wardship | measured protectorate with local ledger | high Dependence, high project speed, moderate Consent |
| Necessary Ward | Mandate Enforcement | coercive ward under subsistence mandate | low autonomy, strong output, high Fracture import |
| Daughter Commonwealth | convergence branch | route-colored utopian subject | balanced subject with overlord store link |
| No-Place Precinct | Outopia | hidden or uncanny puppet form | extreme Dependence, unstable Consent, high rupture risk |
| Renounced Partner | Renunciation Diplomacy | non-subject friendly state | no puppet control, strong diplomacy and achievement hooks |

A subject should not be forced into one final fate. It should be possible to move from ward to charter, charter to League member, protectorate to daughter commonwealth, or ward to rebellion depending on choices and values.

## Local subject values

Every utopian puppet should track a small set of local values. These can be country variables, national spirits, scripted localisation, or a compact decision header.

| Value | Meaning | Raised by | Lowered by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Local Stores | whether the subject can feed and supply its own households | store convoys, local warehouses, agriculture projects, rail links | blockade, empty stores, harsh extraction, war damage |
| Local Consent | whether local households accept the charter or ward | plebiscites, local law guarantees, elections, released autonomy | forced labor, annexation pressure, failed promises |
| Vocational Acceptance | whether trade rolls are seen as chosen work or imposed work | trade schools, apprenticeship aid, local workshops | command trades, quotas, labor seizures |
| Ledger Dependence | how much the subject relies on the overlord's surveyors and stores | technical aid, protectorate boards, store convoys | local elections, autonomous stores, local magistrates |
| Ward Autonomy | current political space of the subject | charter elections, settlement congress, League membership | mandate orders, emergency wardship, no-place conversion |
| Fracture Import | how much overlord contradiction leaks into the subject | harsh enforcement, false dossiers, broken charters | audits, renunciations, fulfilled stores, released autonomy |

The subject system should stay readable. A compact header or scripted localisation breakdown is enough if a custom window would be too heavy. If a custom window is used, it should show selected subject cards and the six values above.

## Overlord decisions

Late decisions should feel like state actions, not purchases.

| Decision family | Costs and requirements | Effect direction | Failure risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Send Store Convoy | convoys, trains, trucks, fuel, civilian burden, route access | raises Local Stores and Dependence | convoy loss, domestic Empty Stores pressure |
| Dispatch Trade Teachers | support equipment, civilian burden, education branch progress | raises Vocational Acceptance | backlash if Consent is low |
| Fund Local Warehouses | civilian factories, building slots, local state control | raises Stores and autonomy potential | corruption or sabotage if Fracture is high |
| Appoint Ward Magistrates | political authority, local compliance, security presence | lowers resistance and raises Dependence | lowers Consent and raises Fracture Import |
| Hold Charter Plebiscite | local stability, low resistance, observers, time | raises Consent and autonomy | failure can trigger protests |
| Train Household Guard | infantry equipment, support equipment, manpower, army XP | creates subject defense and lowers revolt risk | armed subject can demand autonomy later |
| Run Local Ledger | surveyor route, time, local access | reveals subject needs and project priorities | false data increases Fracture |
| Correct False Dossier | admission cost, relations, stability | lowers Fracture and target hostility | weakens future claims |
| Settlement Congress | mature subject values, peace, time | chooses final subject fate | failure freezes subject status and raises instability |
| Emergency Ward Order | Mandate or crisis condition, command resources, troops nearby | fast stabilization or extraction | strong revolt and coalition risk |
| Convert to Precinct | Outopia only, high Fracture, hidden route | strong control and strange bonuses | severe subject rupture risk |

Political power can be part of some actions, but the main costs should be stores, equipment, convoys, factories, rail access, divisions, local support, compliance, and time.

## Subject country decisions and goals

Puppet utopia countries should not be inert. Even if the player does not control them, their internal values should move.

| Subject-side action | Who uses it | Effect direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request Store Convoy | AI or player subject | asks overlord for aid, raises Dependence if accepted |
| Petition for Charter Elections | subject with high Consent | pushes toward autonomy or League membership |
| Organize Local Trade Schools | subject with enough equipment and support | raises Vocational Acceptance |
| Resist Forced Assignments | low Consent subject | raises autonomy pressure, can trigger unrest |
| Appeal to the Common Table | charter subject or protectorate | asks League or overlord for arbitration |
| Guard the Storehouses | subject at war or unrest | uses equipment to protect Local Stores |
| Hide the Empty Shelves | unstable subject | delays crisis but raises Fracture Import when exposed |
| Demand Settlement Congress | mature subject | asks for final status |

AI subjects should prefer stability first, then autonomy if Consent and Stores are high. Harsh subjects should comply when weak but resist when the overlord is at war, stores are empty, or local guard strength is high.

## Subject instability and rupture

Subjects need failure states. A failed puppet should not only sit at low autonomy forever.

| Rupture type | Trigger direction | Outcome direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empty Stores Riot | Local Stores low and support failed | unrest, resistance, Fracture spike, emergency aid mission |
| Trade Roll Strike | Vocational Acceptance low and forced work high | production penalty and local mission |
| Charter Breach | promised autonomy delayed or denied | Consent collapse and appeal event |
| Surveyor Scandal | false data, corrupt magistrates, or bad local ledger | protectorate legitimacy loss |
| Ward Revolt | low Consent, high Fracture Import, armed locals | rebellion or autonomy crisis |
| Precinct Break | Outopia subject with severe instability | hidden rupture event and possible hostile tag |
| Foreign Rescue Claim | neighbor or major backs subject unrest | diplomatic crisis or proxy war |

The player should be able to correct some failures. A subject should not revolt instantly unless the player ignores repeated pressure or abuses the system.

## Final subject fates

A mature utopian subject should have several possible endings.

| Fate | Requirements | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| League Member | high Consent, high Stores, stable autonomy, peaceful doctrine | subject leaves puppet status and joins Eutopian League or faction as equal |
| Daughter Commonwealth | high Stores, stable Dependence, accepted charter | remains subject with special positive mechanics |
| Integrated Ward | compliance, low resistance, accepted local law, route permits integration | gradual cores or state integration, not instant blanket cores |
| Autonomous Charter | moderate Consent and stable Stores | high autonomy subject with low revolt risk |
| Direct Ward | Mandate route and compliant values | low autonomy subject with output and resistance tradeoff |
| No-Place Precinct | Outopia route and hidden conditions | strong control and severe instability risk |
| Renounced Partner | player releases or refuses control after fulfilling need | strong diplomacy, low Fracture, achievement hook |
| Failed Ward | values collapse or revolt succeeds | independence, hostile country, foreign intervention, or civil conflict |

The best peaceful endings should support the Ultimate Utopia branch. The harsh endings should be powerful, but they should make the final capstone darker and less stable.

## Focus integration for subject mechanics

The focus tree should not unlock subject mechanics all at once. It should stage them.

| Focus stage | Subject mechanics unlocked |
| --- | --- |
| Need Dossier branch | selected target and temporary claims |
| Consent Charter branch | peaceful invitation, plebiscites, high autonomy charters |
| Wardship branch | occupied ward stabilization and local stores |
| Surveyor route middle | local ledger and protectorate boards |
| Mandate route middle | emergency ward orders and forced provision |
| Military enforcement branch | subject guard training and coercive settlement |
| Common Stores late branch | store convoys abroad |
| Convergence branch | settlement congress and final subject fates |
| Ultimate Utopia | network bonuses based on stable subjects or League members |
| Outopia reveal | precinct conversion and hidden rupture risks |

## World goals tied to puppet utopias

The late tree should include difficult goals that reward mastery.

| Goal working label | Requirements | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ring of Full Shelves | maintain several subjects or League members with positive Local Stores | proves the overlord can support expansion |
| No Ward Without Voice | convert several wards into charters or League members without coercive failures | peaceful mastery path |
| Measured Without Breaking | run several protectorates with low Fracture Import | Surveyor mastery path |
| The Mandate Holds | keep several Necessary Wards compliant through war and shortage | coercive mastery path |
| The False Claim Renounced | abandon multiple invalid dossiers while keeping domestic Stores high | restraint achievement path |
| Every Island Has a Table | apply the system across coastal or island targets | geography route path |
| Inland Island Completed | stabilize a landlocked ring through rails and wards | landlocked route path |
| The No-Place Network | convert several precincts and survive rupture pressure | hidden high-chaos path |

These goals should not unlock automatically. They should require timers, selected targets, subject values, and actual campaign state.

## Country package notes for puppet utopias

When a utopian puppet appears as a newly released country, it needs enough package support to function.

| Surface | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| tag or cosmetic handling | use existing releasable tags when possible, new tags only when required |
| flags | subject cosmetic flags for Charter, Surveyor, Mandate, and Outopia variants when visible |
| leader | current local leader if suitable, otherwise council or magistrate with route-specific portrait direction |
| parties | local party names should reflect charter, protectorate, ward, or precinct status |
| ideas | local stores, local trade rolls, ward autonomy, overlord dependence, and fracture import |
| focus access | small subject overlay branch or shared subject mini tree if implementation supports it |
| units | local household guard or ward militia scaling by population, stores, and overlord support |
| AI | stabilize stores first, then autonomy or compliance based on subject form |
| docs | subject mechanics must be documented with fates and failure states |

If implementation cannot support full subject focus trees, the minimum acceptable version is a compact decision and idea package for each subject form. It must still have visible values and failure states.

## Balance and abuse controls

Late enforcement can become too strong if it becomes free subject spam. The design should prevent that.

| Abuse risk | Control |
| --- | --- |
| free puppet snowball | subject creation requires dossier, war settlement, invitation, or state control |
| instant cores | integration requires Local Consent, Stores, compliance, resistance control, and time |
| endless claims | claims expire and depend on current Land Need |
| free subject armies | guard training consumes equipment, manpower, and subject values |
| overlord ignores subjects | failed subjects raise Fracture and can revolt or appeal abroad |
| majors converted too easily | majors require defeat, fragmentation, dependency, or special high-chaos state |
| peaceful route uses coercion without cost | severe actions raise Fracture and can close peaceful ultimate endings |
| Mandate avoids consequences | coercive subjects raise coalition pressure and revolt risk |
| Outopia becomes pure reward | precincts are powerful but unstable and diplomatically alarming |

The late game should let a successful player become very strong. It should not let the player click cheap subject buttons without building the domestic and foreign systems first.

## Asset and UI needs

Puppet utopias need clear visual states.

| Asset family | Use |
| --- | --- |
| daughter commonwealth seal | subject branch, decision category, settlement congress |
| local ledger icon | subject value and Surveyor protectorate decisions |
| store convoy icon | aid missions and Common Stores abroad |
| charter ballot icon | plebiscite and autonomy decisions |
| ward magistrate icon | coercive administration and compliance decisions |
| household guard icon | subject defense and local militia decisions |
| broken shelf warning | subject store failure and riot missions |
| no-place precinct seal | hidden subject form and rupture events |
| subject card UI states | selected, stable, empty stores, unrest, settlement ready |
| animated convergence seal | Ultimate Utopia branch when all pillars are satisfied |

A custom scripted GUI is optional but recommended if implementation can support it. A selected subject card would be useful because the system has multiple target values. Static decision headers are acceptable only if they show the current selected subject and value breakdown clearly.

## Completion standard for late enforcement

The late enforcement and puppet systems are complete only when:

| Requirement | Completion meaning |
| --- | --- |
| late focus unlocks | enforcement and subject mechanics unlock through late focuses, not from the first event |
| target selection | the player manages selected targets or capped target pools |
| subject forms | at least Charter, Surveyor, Mandate, and hidden Outopia variants are represented |
| local values | subjects track stores, consent, work acceptance, dependence, autonomy, and Fracture import or a compact equivalent |
| subject actions | overlord and subject both have decisions or scripted behavior |
| failure states | subjects can riot, appeal, revolt, rupture, or demand settlement |
| final fates | subjects can become League members, daughter commonwealths, integrated wards, direct wards, precincts, partners, or failed wards |
| AI | AI can use support, settlement, and restraint behavior without player-only GUI dependence |
| assets | subject branch and value icons are planned and wired |
| convergence | stable subjects count toward Ultimate Utopia, failed subjects delay or corrupt it |
