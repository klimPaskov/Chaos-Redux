# Event 15 Utopia Manifesto, Part 4: Decisions and Missions

All names in this specification are working labels, not final localisation.

## Decision-system purpose

The decision layer is where the player operates the manifesto between focuses. It should convert national promises into resource commitments, deadlines, map work, local choices, and foreign cases.

The system must not become a list of political-power purchases. Most important actions should consume or commit the resources that the country is actually using, such as equipment, manpower, trains, convoys, civilian capacity, factory output, experience, local support, reserves, time, and territorial presence.

## Category architecture

The system uses one main category and several phased subfamilies.

### Main category: Commonwealth Ledger

Always visible after acceptance while the country exists and the system remains active.

Header content:

- Need
- Plenty
- Concord
- Choice versus Assignment
- current route
- active mission count
- selected foreign target
- current reserve band
- current settlement project

The main category should present only urgent and current actions. Targeted foreign decisions should appear in a selected-target subcategory or filtered view.

### Phase visibility

#### Founding phase

Visible families:

- survey
- first store
- calling shortage
- interim charter
- public edition

#### Institutional phase

Visible families:

- calling management
- reserves
- district projects
- route councils
- workday and learning

#### External phase

Visible families:

- Necessary Ground cases
- leases and treaties
- stewardship
- associate municipalities
- league actions

#### Mature phase

Visible families:

- two-year reserve
- formation proof
- regional commonwealth
- post-formation integration
- high-chaos adaptations

Obsolete founding decisions should close when their purpose is complete.

## Decision family 1: The National Survey

### Purpose

Establish and periodically update the state's material and social picture.

### Founding mission: Count Houses and Hands

Owner:

- Event 15 actor

Duration direction:

- roughly one season for a small peaceful country
- longer when the country is at war, has many states, or lacks infrastructure
- shorter with education, administrative capacity, and high Concord

Requirements:

- control of the capital
- no active collapse state
- enough civilian administration to run the survey

Costs:

- temporary civilian factory burden
- small train or truck commitment for larger countries
- temporary reduction in construction speed or administrative output

Success:

- initializes the four values
- identifies urgent calling shortage
- identifies district candidates
- opens the Ledger

Failure:

- noisy or incomplete data
- higher project risk
- an event about disputed figures
- the player can rerun a narrower survey

### Recurring decision: Recount the Country

Used after major war damage, annexation, release, migration, or route change.

Dynamic cost factors:

- number of controlled states
- occupation burden
- transport network
- Concord
- planner route bonuses

The action should have a cooldown and should not be spammed to fish for favorable values.

### Decision: Publish the Accounts

Effect direction:

- improves Concord
- makes false case manipulation harder
- exposes shortages and elite privilege

Cost direction:

- political embarrassment
- temporary stability or ruling-party cost when the numbers are bad

Route behavior:

- Consent and hidden humanist AI prefer publication.
- Common Table prefers council publication.
- Guardians publishes technical summaries unless hard-planning pressure is high.
- Closed Island usually refuses unless propaganda value is strong.

## Decision family 2: Common Stores

### Mission: Establish the Capital Store

Objective:

- construct or allocate a reserve facility in the capital region
- supply it with minimum tools and transport

Costs:

- civilian construction
- trains or trucks
- support equipment or infantry equipment proxy for tools where needed
- temporary consumer burden

Duration:

- dynamic by infrastructure and industry

Success:

- first reserve band
- Plenty gain
- Concord gain if completed on time

Failure:

- Found Manifesto credibility loss
- higher Need
- choice between scaling down, foreign purchase, or emergency assignment

### Decision: Fill the Seasonal Reserve

Objective:

- reach a defined reserve band

Costs:

- consumer output
- convoys for import-dependent countries
- civilian factory burden

Success:

- seasonal reserve
- reduced shortage volatility

Failure:

- reserve shortfall event
- no free refund

### Decision: Rotate Old Stores

Purpose:

- prevent reserve decay and diversion

Costs:

- transport and administrative effort

Benefits:

- maintains Plenty
- creates civilian distribution
- can expose corruption

### Decision: Release Emergency Stores

Availability:

- high Need
- war damage
- famine-like shortage
- refugee crisis

Effect:

- immediate Need reduction
- reserve band loss
- Concord gain when distribution is fair

Route variation:

- Closed Island can prioritize loyal districts, gaining control and losing Concord.

### Mission: Two Years Against Hunger

Objective:

- maintain a high reserve band for a long period
- avoid severe ration crisis
- keep transport connected

Duration:

- long enough that war, blockade, and economic planning matter

Success:

- permanent reserve institution upgrade
- final commonwealth proof
- achievement tracking

Failure:

- partial reserve remains
- public debate on whether the target was wasteful or necessary

### Decision: Send Surplus Abroad

Requirements:

- high reserve band
- positive Plenty trend
- valid recipient with shortage or reconstruction need

Costs:

- reserves
- convoys or trains
- equipment or consumer support

Benefits:

- foreign influence without puppeting
- league credibility
- Concord on voluntary routes

Risks:

- domestic backlash during hidden shortage
- convoy loss
- recipient dependency

## Decision family 3: Callings

### Selected calling model

The player selects one calling family at a time in the GUI. The category shows its shortage, current method, training pipeline, and regional pressure.

### Decision: Issue an Open Call

Requirements:

- education capacity
- no active ban on voluntary transfers

Costs:

- consumer support
- training time
- possible wage or housing guarantee

Success:

- reduces shortage
- increases Choice
- increases Concord when the calling was filled voluntarily

Partial success:

- shortage reduced but not eliminated
- regional imbalance remains

Failure:

- no adequate volunteers
- player can improve terms, build training capacity, or use guaranteed placement

### Decision: Guarantee Placement

Requirements:

- housing or relocation capacity
- training institution

Costs:

- trains or convoys
- civilian construction
- support equipment or trucks
- temporary consumer burden

Success:

- reliable shortage reduction
- modest Choice gain
- migration support

Failure:

- housing strain
- local backlash
- wasted training capacity

### Decision: Set an Assignment Quota

Requirements:

- route allows assignment
- shortage or emergency justification

Costs:

- Concord
- enforcement burden
- temporary productivity disruption

Success:

- fast shortage correction
- Assignment shift

Failure:

- evasion
- unrest
- black-market labor
- larger Concord loss

### Timed mission: Fill the Unpopular Calling

Objective:

- meet a specific national shortage through a route-compatible method before the deadline

Possible callings:

- sanitation and care
- agricultural labor
- railway repair
- mine work
- convoy crews
- defense engineers

Success:

- route-specific institutional upgrade

Failure:

- rising Need
- emergency measure becomes available

### Decision: Learn a Second Trade

Purpose:

- expand multi-skilled reserve labor

Costs:

- education capacity
- temporary output

Benefits:

- resilience to mobilization and war damage
- lower future calling conversion cost

### Decision: Suspend the Short Day

Availability:

- universal short day active
- severe emergency

Effect:

- temporary output gain
- Choice and Concord cost

Repeated use:

- weakens the permanent reform
- can move the country toward Assignment

### Mission: Secure the Island Site

Objectives vary by geography.

- control and supply the island or coastal state
- negotiate a lease
- secure rail and water infrastructure for the Inland Island
- place defensive forces

### Decision: Build the Common Harbor

Costs:

- construction
- convoys
- naval or civilian industrial capacity

Landlocked alternative:

- central rail terminal and waterworks

### Decision: Complete the Provision Ring

## Decision family 7: Doctrine of Necessary Ground

### Selected target flow

The human player selects one eligible country or state target. The system then shows only decisions relevant to that target and the active case. A close action clears the selection. AI evaluates all valid targets through separate logic.

### Decision: Survey Domestic Alternatives

Mandatory on peaceful routes before an external case.

Costs:

- time
- civilian capacity
- research or planning attention

Outcomes:

- domestic solution found
- partial solution
- no viable solution

Finding a domestic solution lowers Need and can close the case. Ignoring it damages case integrity.

### Decision: Draft a Need Case

Requirements:

- valid deficit
- relevant target
- active-case slot

Costs:

- administrative effort
- intelligence exposure
- route-specific political cost

Output:

- case type
- target
- initial integrity
- peaceful offer list

### Decision: Offer Purchase or Long Supply Contract

Costs:

- civilian factories or trade commitment
- diplomatic effort

Target AI factors:

- relations
- resource surplus
- war state
- fear of annexation
- major-power pressure

### Decision: Request a Lease

Possible objects:

- port
- island
- rail corridor
- defense strip
- extraction zone

Costs:

- payment
- guarantee
- limited duration or reciprocal access

### Decision: Propose Joint Administration

Requirements:

- adequate Concord
- reconstruction or settlement plan
- target willing to retain sovereignty

Costs:

- reserves
- advisors
- construction

### Decision: Invite an Associate Municipality

Requirements:

- local support or target consent
- functioning common stores
- route compatibility

Costs:

- reserve commitment
- defense guarantee
- autonomy obligation

### Mission: Wait for the Answer

Duration:

- dynamic by relations, ideology, threat, and target politics

Possible outcomes:

- acceptance
- counteroffer
- refusal
- foreign intervention
- local appeal

The player should not immediately spam the next step.

### Decision: Revise the Offer

Purpose:

- respond to counteroffer
- trade sovereignty, access, autonomy, payment, or defense terms

### Decision: Issue an Ultimatum

Requirements:

- route permission
- severe Need or repeated refusal
- military readiness

Costs:

- Concord
- stability or war support
- international opinion
- mobilization

### Decision: Enforce the Case

Purpose:

- create war goal or conflict path

Requirements:

- ultimatum refusal or direct threat
- valid target

Costs:

- case integrity
- foreign reaction
- route-specific constitutional review

### Decision: Renounce the Case

Benefits:

- Concord
- foreign trust
- closure of obsolete claim

Costs:

- political embarrassment

### Decision: Convert the Case

Possible conversions:

- claim to lease

- stewardship to autonomy

## Mission capacity and clutter

The system should limit simultaneous active missions.

Suggested structure:

- one national founding or constitutional mission
- one active calling mission
- one active district project
- one active external Need case
- one active stewardship or integration mission per selected target, with a low global cap
- one league objective
- one emergency mission

The GUI should show queued or blocked projects without placing every possible state-target decision in the main category.

## Dynamic duration factors

Mission duration should respond to:

- country size
- infrastructure
- war state
- Plenty
- Concord
- route
- calling balance
- transport equipment
- local support
- weather or terrain where relevant
- foreign cooperation
- previous failure

Easy domestic missions should generally allow at least a season. Medium construction or integration missions should run several months. Major district, reserve, island, and formation objectives should take long enough to define strategy.

## Success, partial success, and failure

Important missions should rarely be binary.

Examples:

- a district can open physically but fail to secure local self-government
- a calling shortage can improve but remain regionally severe
- a lease can be granted for a shorter period than requested
- stewardship can restore transport but fail to win local consent
- a reserve target can be met by imports while domestic production remains weak
- a league can survive a crisis but lose one member

Partial outcomes should move the mechanic and open follow-up choices rather than simply refunding costs.

## AI equivalents

Every meaningful GUI action needs an AI path through decisions, scripted effects, or timed evaluation.

AI should:

- prioritize urgent shortages
- avoid starting more projects than it can fund
- prefer route-compatible methods
- keep a reserve before sending aid
- avoid external cases against overwhelmingly stronger targets unless desperate or high-chaos
- use peaceful offers before war on voluntary routes
- close invalid cases
- integrate only when supply and control are plausible
- avoid mercenary loops
- protect the capital and common stores during war
- form a league only when it has credible partners

## Exploit controls

The implementation must prevent:

- creating artificial Need by canceling trade for one day
- stacking multiple identical calling decisions
- cycling emergency assignment to farm output
- releasing and refilling reserves for repeated rewards
- starting endless district projects for construction bonuses
- selecting dead or unreachable foreign targets
- gaining claims unrelated to the case
- retaining claims after the need is solved without consequence
- repeating plebiscites until a favorable result appears
- farming associate integration for cores
- infinite auxiliary recruitment
- entering a route, collecting rewards, then switching without institutional cost
- using formation to reset integration or case penalties
