# Event 15 Utopia Manifesto, Part 7: AI, Balance, Multiplayer, and Compatibility

All names in this specification are working labels, not final localisation.

## AI promise

An AI recipient always accepts the manifesto. Acceptance does not mean that every AI follows the same route or performs the system perfectly. The AI should build a coherent interpretation based on ideology, government, war state, industry, geography, social stability, and chaos.

AI countries must use the same material logic as players. They should not receive hidden free reserves, free districts, free integration, or free military power merely because the scripted GUI is human-facing.

## Recipient selection AI

The event's target score should favor countries that can survive the transformation and have a strategic reason to use it.

### Strong positive target factors

- generic or approved replaceable focus tree
- low total industry
- low research capacity
- small state count
- high economic need without imminent defeat
- stable control of capital
- coastal, island, or viable Inland Island geography
- sufficient peace or defensive breathing room to complete the founding survey
- no recent full country-transformation event

### Moderate positive target factors

- migration or housing pressure
- weak trade access
- poor infrastructure
- high unemployment proxy
- no faction protection
- high local reform pressure

### Strong negative target factors

- active capitulation risk
- unique or protected focus tree
- extensive completed political route
- major status
- large subject network
- active civil war
- special Chaos country
- nonhuman country
- world-end actor
- large occupied empire
- current full-scale offensive war

### Human priority

Eligible human players receive priority over AI minors, but the weighting should not select a player whose current campaign would be destroyed by tree replacement. Replacement safety remains a hard gate.

## Route selection AI

The AI evaluates a route only after the first survey and opening institutional tasks. Route weights should respond to actual country state.

### Consent of Households

Ordinary preferences:

- democratic government
- high stability
- high or rising Concord
- modest Need
- several small neighbors suitable for peaceful partnership
- low immediate war pressure
- country with elections or municipal traditions

Avoidance factors:

- severe total war
- very low state capacity
- repeated failed Open Calls
- authoritarian ideology without reform pressure

High-chaos exception:

- a democratic AI can still choose this route as a refuge model when world threat is high and its domestic position is stable

### Common Table

Ordinary preferences:

- communist or socialist government
- strong labor movement proxy
- high inequality or property-conflict pressure
- industrial workforce
- neighboring socialist or post-colonial states

Avoidance factors:

- tiny administrative base with no council support
- strong anti-communist government
- isolation from possible partners

High-chaos exception:

- a desperate non-communist AI can choose the route after a founding crisis if council support is strong and the old order has failed

### Guardians of Measure

Ordinary preferences:

- neutral or nonaligned government
- low Plenty
- weak infrastructure
- high education or technical capacity
- landlocked geography
- strong need for district and transport construction
- moderate Concord

Avoidance factors:

- very high local autonomy resistance
- strong democratic opposition to assignment
- weak administrative capacity with no path to improve it

High-chaos exception:

- more attractive under severe shortages and infrastructure collapse

### Closed Island

Ordinary preferences:

- fascist or authoritarian government
- war or encirclement
- severe Need
- low Concord
- high military threat
- island or defensible compact geography
- ruler willing to use compulsory service

Avoidance factors:

- strong democratic institutions
- high Choice and Concord
- deep dependence on open trade
- no defensible project variant

High-chaos exception:

- can become attractive to several ideologies when chaos is extreme, but peaceful democratic AI should still require severe desperation

### The Joke Understood

Ordinary preferences:

- democratic or reformist government
- high education
- high Choice and Concord
- preserved open debate
- failure of a literal program
- no penal labor or unjustified case

Avoidance factors:

- censorship
- high Assignment
- active colonial war
- low public debate

AI reveal:

- the route should be rare but possible
- an AI that meets the conditions should sometimes take it, especially when its initial route is failing without a severe security emergency

## Route commitment and switching

The AI should not switch routes casually after collecting rewards.

Route switching requires:

- constitutional crisis
- route failure
- explicit convergence focus
- leadership change
- high-chaos evolution

When a switch occurs, the AI should:

- close obsolete decisions
- replace incompatible ideas
- change target-selection behavior
- revise active external cases
- update advisor and leader preferences

## Ledger AI priorities

### Need response

At low Need:

- build reserves
- support education
- send limited aid
- avoid unnecessary external cases

At moderate Need:

- use domestic substitution
- balance callings
- repair infrastructure
- prepare trade or lease offers

At high Need:

- prioritize emergency store release
- fill critical callings
- suspend lower-priority district projects
- open one external case if domestic alternatives fail

At extreme Need:

- use emergency assignment if route allows
- seek foreign aid
- accept a less favorable lease
- use coercive escalation only when route and military situation support it

### Plenty response

At low Plenty:

- do not send major surplus aid
- do not start multiple districts
- do not integrate distant territory
- protect stores
- reduce military overcommitment when possible

At high Plenty:

- expand district network
- prepare shorter workday
- support associates
- pursue formation proof

### Concord response

At low Concord:

- voluntary AI should reduce quotas and publish accounts
- council AI should convene delegates
- planner AI should correct data or add appeals
- Closed Island AI can increase repression, but should understand revolt risk

At high Concord:

- use ambitious constitutional or integration actions
- form league
- hold status votes

### Choice versus Assignment response

The AI should maintain a route-appropriate band rather than always maximizing one side.

- Consent targets high Choice.
- Common Table targets a flexible middle leaning toward Choice.
- Humane Guardians targets a measured middle leaning toward Assignment.
- Hard Guardians targets high Assignment without immediate Concord collapse.
- Closed Island targets very high Assignment.
- Hidden humanist targets high Choice with limited emergency tools.

## Calling AI

The AI identifies the most severe calling shortage and chooses a method.

### Open Call preference

Use when:

- Concord is healthy
- shortage is not immediately fatal
- education capacity exists
- route favors Choice

### Guaranteed Placement preference

Use when:

- shortage is important
- housing and transport exist
- route allows mixed planning
- Open Call recently failed

### Assignment Quota preference

Use when:

- shortage is severe
- war or emergency exists
- route favors Assignment
- Concord can absorb the cost or route accepts repression

### Emergency Levy preference

Use only when:

- severe Need
- active war, famine-like shortage, infrastructure collapse, or stewardship emergency
- no ordinary method can resolve the shortage in time

The AI should not keep the levy active after the emergency ends.

## Reserve AI

The AI should maintain route-appropriate reserve goals.

- Peaceful voluntary routes aim for steady seasonal or one-year reserves before major aid.
- Common Table aims for shared reserve capacity and member support.
- Guardians aim for forecast-based reserve bands.
- Closed Island aims for high internal reserves and low foreign aid.
- High-chaos AI values reserve security more strongly.

The AI should release stores during genuine emergency. It should not hoard while Concord collapses unless the Closed Island route deliberately prioritizes military survival.

## District AI

The AI should choose district roles from actual state needs.

Priority order:

- housing crisis
- capital overcrowding
- broken rail or port network
- severe agricultural shortage
- industrial housing need
- refugee settlement
- island project
- research town

The AI should limit simultaneous construction according to civilian capacity. It should not start a project in a state likely to be lost immediately.

## Necessary Ground target AI

### Target relevance

A target must solve the declared need.

Examples:

- port case selects a reachable port or coastal state
- resource case selects a state or agreement with the missing resource
- corridor case connects relevant territory
- settlement case selects a plausible neighboring or partner area
- island case selects a nearby or strategically connected island when possible

### Strength safety

Ordinary AI should not issue an ultimatum to a much stronger country without:

- major ally support
- severe strategic emergency
- high chaos
- direct threat
- route-specific recklessness

### Peaceful offer preference

Consent, Common Table, hidden humanist, and humane Guardians AI should normally attempt:

1. domestic alternative
2. trade or purchase
3. lease
4. joint administration
5. associate arrangement

Closed Island AI can skip steps, but should still prefer a cheap accepted concession over a disastrous war.

### Case closure

The AI should renounce or convert a case when:

- Need is solved
- target disappears
- target joins the league
- war makes the target unreachable
- another state provides the same remedy
- military risk becomes unacceptable

### Claim integrity

AI route behavior should recognize integrity.

- voluntary routes avoid low-integrity escalation
- Common Table can rationalize ideological integration but should respond to council dissent
- Guardians can manipulate data only under hard-planning pressure
- Closed Island accepts low integrity more often
- hidden humanist AI closes false cases quickly

## Stewardship and integration AI

The AI should not annex territory and forget it.

Priority sequence:

1. emergency provision
2. transport restoration
3. local administration
4. route-compatible status
5. long integration or autonomy

The AI should prefer association over integration when:

- territory is large
- culture or politics differ greatly
- local support is low
- supply is weak
- league value is higher than direct ownership

The AI should use Assigned Colony only when its route permits and it can sustain garrison and supply.

## League AI

### Founder behavior

The founder should:

- invite plausible small partners
- keep reserves before promising aid
- avoid demanding annexation from members
- respond to member emergencies
- refuse a member that would dominate the league or immediately drag it into an impossible war

### Candidate behavior

A candidate evaluates:

- threat
- shortage
- founder credibility
- autonomy guarantees
- ideological compatibility
- existing faction
- distance
- founder's claim history
- reserve benefits
- sponsor pressure

### Member behavior

Members can:

- request aid
- resist centralization
- leave after betrayal
- support formation
- push their route model inside the league

### Major-power behavior

A major can:

- guarantee the league
- offer technical aid
- seek bases or influence
- oppose territorial cases
- sponsor a rival member

A major should not join as an ordinary member unless a later repository design explicitly supports that rare case.

## Military AI

### Defensive route behavior

- protect capital and stores
- build engineers and militia
- avoid distant offensive war
- support members under direct threat

### Council route behavior

- use volunteers and shared reserves
- mobilize workplace defense
- avoid mercenary dependency

### Planner route behavior

- fortify supply network
- prioritize engineers
- maintain reserve formations

### Closed Island behavior

- fortify project area
- use compulsory service
- hire auxiliaries when equipment and payment exist
- prepare coercive external cases

### Auxiliary safety

The AI should not hire auxiliaries when:

- unable to pay
- no equipment
- no valid source
- domestic army already exceeds supply
- contract risk is extreme

## Focus AI priorities

Each route should use a plan that interleaves branches.

### Opening

1. public edition
2. survey
3. common store
4. agriculture and callings
5. interpretive congress

### Early route

- establish political institution
- solve one urgent shortage
- begin transport or housing project
- secure capital defense

### Middle route

- develop stores and districts
- open diplomacy
- build island variant
- complete first Necessary Ground case when needed

### Late route

- form league or associate network
- complete route capstone
- meet formation proof
- continue post-formation content

The AI should not rush the political capstone while Plenty and core institutions remain broken.

## AI personality modifiers

The implementation can use broad personality or strategy proxies.

### Cautious provider

- reserves
- peaceful cases
- high Concord
- slow expansion

### Cooperative organizer

- council or household route
- league and aid
- local autonomy

### Civic planner

- districts
- logistics
- guaranteed placement
- technical diplomacy

### Fortress steward

- Closed Island
- high reserves
- assignment
- defense and coercive cases

### Humanist reformer

- open debate
- mixed economy

- high route flexibility

These personalities should emerge from country state and route rather than random labels alone.

## Balance goals

### Weak-country viability

Acceptance should give a weak country a strategic path, not instant major power status.

The event should improve:

- internal coordination
- resilience
- district development
- reserve security
- diplomacy among small states

The event should not immediately grant:

- several free factories
- advanced technology
- a large army
- free cores
- a large faction
- automatic resource independence

### Route power curves

#### Consent of Households

Early:

- slow
- stable
- administratively demanding

Middle:

- strong Concord and local integration

Late:

- powerful league and voluntary network
- lower raw military output

#### Common Table

Early:

- disruptive property transition

Middle:

- strong reserve and production coordination

Late:

- powerful cross-border network
- council deadlock risk

#### Guardians of Measure

Early:

- strong survey and project setup

Middle:

- high construction and shortage control

Late:

- highest Plenty potential
- Assignment and legitimacy risk

#### Closed Island

Early:

- fast mobilization
- lower Concord

Middle:

- strong defense and coercive expansion

Late:

- high output and isolation
- resistance, supply, and diplomatic burden

#### The Joke Understood

Early:

- delayed reveal
- lower immediate specialization

Middle:

- flexible and resilient

Late:

- strong diplomacy and civil stability
- lower maximum output and territorial reach

## Dynamic scaling

Important values should scale with:

- factory count
- state count
- population and manpower
- infrastructure
- war state
- reserve band
- chaos tier
- route
- local support
- target distance
- existing commitments

A small country should pay less absolute equipment and construction cost than a larger eligible country, but costs should remain meaningful relative to capacity.

## Industrial cap and strength gate

The event should use a tuning table rather than one universal number.

Suggested design bands for implementation review:

- preferred recipient: very small to small industry
- eligible recipient: modest industry with no major status
- soft exclusion: high regional industry or extensive dockyards
- hard exclusion: major-level industry or protected bespoke country

The exact thresholds must be checked against current Chaos Redux balance and vanilla industrial distribution. The planning package does not claim final numbers.

## Ledger thresholds

Use public bands rather than demanding exact numbers from the player in every tooltip.

### Need bands

- resolved
- manageable
- pressing
- severe
- existential

### Plenty bands

- scarcity
- fragile provision
- stable provision
- abundance
- surplus commonwealth

### Concord bands

- fractured
- doubtful
- cooperative
- trusted
- common purpose

### Choice versus Assignment bands

- free callings
- guided choice
- guaranteed placement
- planned assignment
- compulsory service

Thresholds should have route-specific effects. The same Assignment band is more stable under Guardians than under Consent.

## Failure balance

Failure should hurt and create new play.

- failed store mission creates credibility and shortage pressure
- failed district creates debt and local anger
- failed lease can escalate or force domestic substitution
- failed stewardship creates resistance and international cost
- failed reserve target blocks formation and can trigger crisis
- failed council or planner governance opens route correction

Failure should not delete the tree, annex the country automatically, or make recovery impossible.

## Exploit and abuse review

### Tree replacement abuse

Prevent:

- using Event 15 to escape an existing route lock after heavy focus investment
- resetting national focus progress for free benefits
- replacing a protected event tree

### Reserve abuse

Prevent:

- cycling release and refill
- converting cheap equipment into large permanent bonuses
- sending aid that is immediately returned for repeated influence

### Calling abuse

Prevent:

- stacking identical methods
- ending quotas before cost applies
- farming Choice or Assignment through rapid toggles

### District abuse

Prevent:

- repeated construction rewards in one state
- canceling projects after receiving partial map effects
- completing a district without housing, transport, and calling requirements

### Necessary Ground abuse

Prevent:

- one shortage producing claims on many unrelated states
- keeping permanent claims after Need resolves without cost
- selecting inaccessible targets
- repeated ultimata without cooldown
- war-goal spam

### Integration abuse

Prevent:

- instant cores
- repeated status votes
- farming associate creation and annexation
- integration after deliberate depopulation or severe repression without route cost

### League abuse

Prevent:

- annexing members while keeping voluntary bonuses
- infinite aid loops
- recruiting major members for effortless protection
- using observer status as a free faction substitute

### Military abuse

Prevent:

- infinite militia
- free auxiliary divisions
- contract cancellation without payment
- template conversion that creates equipment

## Multiplayer behavior

### Selection

- scan eligible human players first
- choose among them by the same weighted score
- do not always target the host
- log the selected actor clearly

### Player choice

Only the selected player's country receives the accept or reject choice. Other players may receive later news or diplomatic reactions.

### Pause and GUI

The scripted GUI should be local to the actor. Other players should not be able to click the actor's Ledger controls.

### League

Human-controlled candidates receive real invitations and can refuse. AI fallback logic should not override a human decision.

### Targeted cases

A human target should receive clear negotiation options. The Utopia actor should not gain a war goal without the human target seeing the offer, refusal, counteroffer, or ultimatum stage.

### Formation and super-event

The global presentation fires once and uses the actor's actual route and identity.

## Compatibility with country focus trees

### Replaceable tree registry

Implementation should create or reuse a repository-level test for whether a country's current tree can be replaced by Event 15.

The test should recognize:

- generic tree
- explicitly approved country trees
- Event 15 tree already loaded
- protected bespoke trees
- other event-created trees

### Completion depth

Even a normally replaceable tree should become protected after substantial political or identity progress. The exact focus-count and key-focus tests require repository review.

### Existing country with meaningful unique tree

The event should not target it. Event 15 is designed around comprehensive replacement, so a shallow additive branch would be a silent fallback and is not approved by this specification.

### Subject countries

Subject eligibility requires implementation review.

Possible approved design:

- low-autonomy subject can accept only through a route that begins with internal reform and later autonomy
- overlord receives reaction and pressure
- focus tree remains usable without independent diplomacy until autonomy grows

If the repository cannot support this cleanly, subjects should be excluded. That is a target-gate decision, not a reduced event implementation.

## Compatibility with world threat and world-end systems

The event should read shared world threat when it affects:

- route AI
- league defense
- reserve targets
- high-chaos island projects
- migration pressure

Event 15 should not create a parallel generic world-threat flag.

When a terminal world-end state occurs:

- close new external cases unless the scenario explicitly supports them
- preserve or freeze essential local provision decisions as appropriate
- stop formation and super-event duplication
- clean selected targets and temporary missions

## Implemented AI and balance record

The current package has 12 Event 15 AI strategy plans. All 124 focuses contain AI selection logic. All 121 decisions contain `ai_will_do`. AI reads the same Ledger, calling, reserve, case-integrity, local-support, target-survival, district-suitability, and obligation gates as the player.

The living Ledger and calling refresh is actor-scoped and idempotent. AI selects the highest uncovered calling pressure after recomputation. It avoids launching a second method for a family already under an active method or cooldown. Necessary Ground remains unavailable without a live relevant deficit, and state-transfer methods remain unavailable when they would erase the selected target.

District AI chooses among suitable state roles, considers whether it can meet the three project obligations, and rejects likely ownership loss. Closed Island weighs garrison, resistance, supply, reserve, and foreign reaction before Penal Works. Penal Works is attractive only when construction pressure and district need justify its material and social cost.

Evolution AI pays the same action cost and inherits the same shared obligation as the player. Route preference cannot bypass obligation setup, case cleanup, district proof, stewardship disposition, or conduct history.

No Event 15 AI or maintenance behavior uses a daily, weekly, or monthly iteration over every country. The accepted cadence is the actor pulse plus narrow affected-country hooks and direct decision or mission terminals.

The current focus-tree atomic re-audit passes with 124 reachable focuses, 174 valid prerequisite references, zero node-through connector cases in the conservative static check, and no open P0 through P3 finding. All 34 paid focuses, split into 26 institutional and 8 military, cancel if their live gate becomes invalid, refresh and recheck the current price, and guard the full reward tail behind payment success. ROOT and FROM dynamic-cost refreshes remain outside the Fallout-only part of the state-control callback. The current decision and country re-audits also pass. Player and AI case choices use the same target-survival, exact-state, cleanup, affordability, and disposition contracts.
