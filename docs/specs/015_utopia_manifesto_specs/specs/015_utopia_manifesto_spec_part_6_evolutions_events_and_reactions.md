# Event 15 Utopia Manifesto, Part 6: Evolutions, Event Families, and World Reactions

All names in this specification are working labels, not final localisation.

## Baseline stages and evolutions

The ordinary campaign phases described in the core specification are baseline progression. They are not evolutions.

The event has five evolution tracks, one available at each rising chaos tier. Each evolution can enter in two ways.

- Active-event evolution changes a country that already accepted the manifesto.
- Pre-fire evolved opening changes the starting package when Event 15 has not yet fired and the relevant world state already exists.

An evolution should normally use dynamic mean-time-to-happen pacing after its conditions become valid. It should not fire instantly unless it is modifying the first opening itself.

Disabled evolutions must not block the baseline tree. Every gated focus or decision needs a clean ordinary alternative.

## Founding event family

### Public edition dispute

Trigger direction:

- shortly after acceptance

Actors:

- government
- labor or household groups
- religious or educational institutions
- property interests
- military or planning advocates

Choice direction:

- publish openly
- circulate through approved institutions
- restrict early interpretation

Consequences:

- Concord
- hidden humanist reveal potential
- route delegate strength
- future censorship pressure

Text direction:

- show people arguing over practical passages and forming reading groups
- avoid treating the book as supernatural
- do not explain hidden route mechanics

### Survey credibility

Trigger direction:

- first survey completes or fails

Choice direction:

- publish bad news
- revise the figures
- hide shortages
- let councils verify the results

Consequences:

- Concord
- Need integrity
- planner legitimacy
- first contradiction record

### Property petitions

Trigger direction:

- property transition begins

Variants:

- smallholders fear expropriation
- industrial owners demand compensation
- tenants demand public land
- religious or communal land claims conflict

Consequences:

- route property path
- local support
- district cost

### The first store opens

Trigger direction:

- capital store mission succeeds

Tone direction:

- concrete public scene of distribution, storage, and expectation
- avoid reward-list prose

Variant outcomes:

- orderly opening
- crowd pressure
- elite boycott
- missing stock
- improvised success

### The first store fails

Trigger direction:

- mission expires or corruption is discovered

Choice direction:

- scale down the promise
- prosecute diversion
- use emergency assignment
- request foreign support

Consequences:

- crisis branch pressure
- Concord
- Plenty
- route movement

## Evolution I: Glosses in the Margin

Chaos tier:

- Gathering Storm

### Concept

Copies of the manifesto begin to circulate with annotations, rival translations, and political commentaries. The text becomes a field of organized interpretation rather than one recovered document.

### Active-event entry

Conditions:

- manifesto accepted
- interpretive congress active or complete
- evolution enabled

Changes:

- creates interpretation schools or factions
- adds route advisors and debate decisions
- increases route event variety
- makes hidden humanist path easier to reveal when debate remains open
- can intensify dogmatism when censorship is used

### Pre-fire evolved opening

Changes:

- the recovered copy already contains several generations of notes
- opening congress occurs faster
- starting Concord is more contested
- route delegates have stronger starting positions
- one early route event fires during the founding survey

### Evolution log direction

The log title should describe the manifesto acquiring rival margins and public schools of interpretation. It should not reveal which route will win.

## Evolution II: Necessary Shores

Chaos tier:

- Rising Chaos

### Concept

Migration, blockade, resource shortage, and displaced populations make the doctrine of necessary land immediately relevant.

### Active-event entry

Changes:

- opens expanded lease, settlement, and port-access cases
- adds refugee and housing factors to Need
- improves peaceful case options on voluntary routes
- increases coercive temptation under severe Need
- adds foreign reaction events

### Pre-fire evolved opening

Changes:

- recipient begins with an active material or housing pressure
- first external case can appear earlier
- the opening survey identifies a coast, port, corridor, resource, or Inland Island need
- one peaceful domestic alternative remains available so the route is not forced into expansion

### Evolution log direction

The log should present the manifesto moving from domestic reconstruction toward the question of where a commonwealth can live.

## Evolution III: Cities of One Measure

Chaos tier:

- Chaos Tier

### Concept

The settlement model spreads. Planned districts, cooperative municipalities, and associated cities begin to form a network.

### Active-event entry

Changes:

- unlocks advanced Garden City projects
- adds associate municipality roles
- strengthens district network mechanics
- enables one foreign sympathetic circle or municipality when campaign conditions support it
- creates planner, council, or local-autonomy conflicts

### Pre-fire evolved opening

Changes:

- one domestic planning circle or foreign municipal movement already studies the text
- the recipient begins with a district candidate and a diplomatic contact
- Plenty starts slightly higher when the candidate has infrastructure, or Need starts higher when the project is an emergency settlement

### Evolution log direction

The log should emphasize repeated settlement forms and public attempts to make the design visible in streets, homes, and transport.

## Evolution IV: Nowhere Made Law

Chaos tier:

- Totalen Chaos

### Concept

The manifesto becomes a regional political identity. Governments, movements, and small states must decide whether it is a refuge, a rival order, or a mask for expansion.

### Active-event entry

Changes:

- unlocks final formation proof
- expands league and associate content
- opens super-event eligibility after regional threshold
- adds major-power sponsorship and opposition
- adds route-specific foreign propaganda
- strengthens final cosmetic identity

### Pre-fire evolved opening

Changes:

- the recovered manuscript arrives in a world already searching for alternative orders
- public circulation is faster
- foreign observers appear early
- one route can receive outside material support, with dependency risk
- the country begins with greater ideological opportunity and stronger foreign scrutiny

### Evolution log direction

The log should describe an imagined place becoming a public legal claim and a regional reference point.

## Evolution V: The Perfect Island

Chaos tier:

- World Collapse

### Concept

Under extreme chaos, the idea of a complete and separated society becomes more literal. The country can attempt radical self-sufficiency, engineered separation, or a final closed order. Voluntary routes can instead turn the island idea into a refuge network.

### Active-event entry

Changes:

- unlocks high-chaos island project variants
- intensifies reserve, migration, fortification, and autonomy pressure
- opens Perfect Island route content for Closed Island
- opens refuge commonwealth content for voluntary routes
- increases risk that external Need cases become predatory
- adds severe league cohesion tests

### Pre-fire evolved opening

Changes:

- the recipient begins in a world of displacement and institutional collapse
- starting Need is severe
- the first common store and island project become urgent
- AI route weighting allows extreme interpretations more often
- the opening receives stronger starting tools and stronger failure risk

### Evolution log direction

The log should present a country trying to make a bounded place survive while ordinary regional order is breaking down. It should not declare a terminal world-end state.

## Evolution interactions

- Evolutions can be enabled independently.
- Each evolution must add content without replacing the baseline route.
- Active countries receive new content without waiting for Event 15 to fire again.
- Pre-fire evolution changes the opening only when Event 15 remains unfired.
- Evolution route changes should respect selected interpretation and geography.
- An evolution should not automatically move the country toward Closed Island.
- Evolution incidents should use dynamic pacing based on chaos, route stability, Need, Plenty, Concord, and current war.

## Foreign reaction matrix

### Nearby minor democracies

Likely reaction:

- curiosity
- municipal partnerships
- concern about claims
- potential league membership

### Nearby socialist states

Likely reaction:

- interest in Common Table
- rivalry over ideological leadership
- worker missions
- suspicion of mixed property routes

### Authoritarian states

Likely reaction:

- interest in planning and assignment
- fear of household or council contagion
- willingness to support Closed Island as a client

### Colonial powers

Likely reaction:

- strong concern about associate municipalities and migration settlements
- possible support when the doctrine weakens rivals
- opposition when necessity claims threaten colonies or strategic ports

### Great powers

Likely reaction:

- sponsorship
- infiltration
- guarantees
- pressure for basing rights
- attempt to turn the league into a bloc

A major should not automatically invade a tiny Utopia state. Reaction should depend on strategic location, claims, ideology, faction position, and regional influence.

### Existing cooperative, autonomous, or reformist states

Likely reaction:

- faster technical partnership
- higher chance of joining reserve compact
- route-specific ideological debate

## Recipient-specific reaction variants

The event should use country context to vary foreign reactions.

- An island recipient attracts naval and trade interest.
- A landlocked recipient attracts corridor and rail politics.
- A subject recipient creates an autonomy crisis.
- A monarchy creates a crown-versus-charter debate.
- A democracy creates party competition over the manifesto.
- A communist state creates council-versus-party debate.
- A fascist state creates a unity and service interpretation.
- A country at war makes the first store and calling shortages urgent.

## News and report presentation

Use ordinary country events for internal constitutional and labor choices.

Use report-style events for:

- first public manuscript display
- first common store
- first Garden City district
- first associate municipality
- major island project milestone

Use news events only when the moment is internationally visible.

- league formation
- first coercive Necessary Ground war
- final commonwealth proclamation
- assigned-colony revolt with regional consequences

News descriptions should focus on visible institutions, movement of people, construction, public meetings, fortifications, or conflict. Avoid making the map change itself the emotional center.

## Final proclamation super-event

### Role

- ideological and regional-order transformation

### Trigger

- route-specific formation completed
- at least three meaningful external partners, associates, or recognized agreements
- one regional commonwealth objective completed
- country is not a trivial puppet
- super-event has not already fired

### Presentation variants

#### Voluntary commonwealth

Emphasis:

- public assemblies
- open stores
- linked municipalities
- small states entering a voluntary regional order

#### Council union

Emphasis:

- worker and civic delegates
- common production and reserve institutions
- cross-border councils

#### Planned Utopia

Emphasis:

- completed city network
- transport, housing, and public works
- measured order becoming a regional model

#### Closed island-state

Emphasis:

- fortified separation
- mass service
- rigid stores and assigned districts
- foreign fear and fascination

#### Practical commonwealth

Emphasis:

- plural institutions
- reform without a single sacred model
- regional influence through aid and example

### Text research gates

- final title: research required
- final button remark: research required
- final main quote: research required
- final cultural reference: research required
- final audio track: research and license verification required

### Image direction

Use generated alternate-history art because the final state is fictional and varies by recipient.

Visual requirements:

- 1930s or 1940s documentary or painted-news realism
- central public institution such as a common store, civic square, planned district, harbor, or assembly
- route-specific people and architecture
- no readable generated text
- no map as the main subject
- enough contrast for super-event UI

### Audio direction

The audio should be a real licensed or public-domain musical recording with a civic, reflective, hopeful, regimented, or uneasy character according to route. It should not be a generic triumph cue or synthesized placeholder.

## Defeat and aftermath events

Event 15 is not a global enemy that needs a universal defeat super-event. It can still have local aftermath when the country collapses, abandons the manifesto, or loses a coercive colonial war.

### Commonwealth dissolved

Trigger direction:

- annexation, civil collapse, or constitutional abandonment

Outcomes:

- associates review status
- league may survive without founder
- route ideas removed or inherited selectively
- event history records collapse

### The stores remain

Trigger direction:

- regime changes but practical institutions survive

Purpose:

- preserve district, reserve, education, or cooperative benefits without preserving the old political route automatically

### The colonies leave

Trigger direction:

- Closed Island defeated or low-control associates revolt

Outcomes:

- independence
- claims review
- reconstruction need
- former founder Concord crisis

### The book returns to debate

Trigger direction:

- literal route fails but education and public debate remain

Purpose:

- hidden humanist recovery or modest institutional legacy

## Event log and details direction

### Event name direction

The event name should identify the revived manifesto and its political experiment without presenting final localisation from this planning package.

### Event Details direction

The detail summary should describe:

- a weak country revives a famous or old utopian program
- common provision, chosen work, planned settlements, and necessary-land claims become public questions
- the recipient may build several different forms of commonwealth

It must not list modifiers, hidden routes, evolution thresholds, final formables, or achievement conditions.

### Evolution detail direction

Each evolution detail should explain the new public form the experiment takes, such as annotated interpretations, territorial pressure, settlement networks, regional law, or high-chaos separation. It should not describe exact unlocks or numerical effects.

### Actor direction

The Event 15 actor is the selected recipient. History and evolution rows should show that country's flag when available.
