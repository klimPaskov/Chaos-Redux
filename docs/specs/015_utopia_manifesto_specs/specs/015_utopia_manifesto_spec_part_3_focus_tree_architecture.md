# Event 15 Utopia Manifesto, Part 3: Focus Tree Architecture

All names in this specification are working labels, not final localisation.

## Tree role

The replacement focus tree is the playable identity of the recipient country. It must solve the country's starting weakness, establish the manifesto's institutions, create a political interpretation, connect that interpretation to labor, industry, defense, diplomacy, territorial policy, and integration, then continue into a mature commonwealth or closed island.

The tree should be broad enough for a full campaign. It should not be a temporary event branch attached to the generic tree. The implementation agent chooses exact focus count, duration, coordinates, and final names, but the branch architecture and route promises in this file are required.

## Visual architecture

The tree should read from top to bottom in six bands.

1. Recovery and survey.
2. First institutions and interpretive congress.
3. Political route commitment.
4. Interlocking economy, callings, defense, diplomacy, and settlement branches.
5. External Need cases, associate municipalities, and regional order.
6. Final identity, proclamation, and post-formation ambitions.

The five political routes should occupy distinct lanes. Support branches should remain available to several routes, but their rewards and decisions should change according to the chosen interpretation. Connector lines should show that political choices alter the support branches. The final layout should avoid five isolated vertical columns.

## Opening trunk

### Recover the Manuscript

Purpose:

- mark acceptance
- replace the temporary discovery state with an active national project
- begin the public edition and household survey

Gameplay direction:

- starts the Found Manifesto idea lifecycle
- opens the Ledger category
- records the country as the Event 15 actor
- reveals the first common-store and survey decisions

### A Public Edition

Purpose:

- translate the manifesto into the country's political and social language
- make debate public enough that the route is not chosen by a hidden clique

Gameplay direction:

- increases Concord when press and debate remain open
- enables route-specific public circles
- unlocks the first event about competing interpretations

### Count Houses and Hands

Purpose:

- establish initial Need, Plenty, callings, housing, transport, and reserve baselines

Gameplay direction:

- performs the country survey
- creates the first visible Ledger breakdown
- identifies one urgent shortage and one regional strength
- opens map-grounded district projects

### The First Common Store

Purpose:

- turn the manifesto into a visible provisioning institution

Gameplay direction:

- creates a reserve objective in the capital region
- uses civilian capacity, transport, and tools rather than only political power
- failure or delay creates a public credibility event

### Agriculture for All

Purpose:

- establish universal basic agricultural and civil-response training

Gameplay direction:

- improves resilience and reserve recovery
- opens Provisioning and Agriculture calling decisions
- does not convert the country into an agrarian economy

### Convene the Interpretive Congress

Purpose:

- open the political route family
- establish the Choice versus Assignment balance as an explicit national dispute

Gameplay direction:

- unlocks route delegates, advisors, and debate events
- requires the first survey and common store
- can be delayed during war, but delay raises route pressure

### An Interim Charter

Purpose:

- prevent the transition from becoming legal chaos

Gameplay direction:

- protects the existing state until a route is chosen
- establishes temporary rules for property, rationing, local government, and emergency assignment
- creates a time-limited constitutional mission

### The Country as a Question

Purpose:

- final opening convergence
- lets the player commit to an interpretation

Gameplay direction:

- mutually exclusive political openers appear
- support branches remain visible but route-sensitive
- the hidden humanist route remains concealed until its conditions are met

## Political route A: Consent of Households

### Narrative role

This route treats the manifesto as a mandate for social provision and local liberty. The household, municipality, cooperative, and voluntary association become the foundations of the commonwealth. The state guarantees housing, education, health, and access to work while preserving lawful exit, religious plurality, and local votes.

### Mechanical identity

- high Choice
- high Concord
- slower emergency correction
- strong cooperative growth
- peaceful Necessary Ground ladder
- the best plebiscite and associate integration tools
- strong diplomacy among small states
- limited access to forced labor and offensive territorial escalation

### Route opener: The Household Gives Consent

Purpose:

- establish that state reforms require local and household consent

Unlocks:

- local charter decisions
- voluntary calling preference
- municipal vote events
- cooperative land-trust path

Tradeoff:

- major reorganization takes longer
- local refusal can delay projects

### Focus group: Free Callings

Themes:

- open recruitment
- paid training
- right to change work
- protection against hereditary occupational assignment
- public lectures

Mechanical rewards:

- cheaper Open Call actions
- higher training success
- faster Choice gain
- reduced shortage penalty after education investments

Failure state:

- if the player refuses to fund training, the route can produce persistent labor gaps and pressure for guaranteed placement

### Focus group: Municipal Charters

Themes:

- elected local councils
- local control of common stores
- transparent accounts
- minority and religious protections

Mechanical rewards:

- higher Concord
- better local compliance
- district projects with local contribution
- Open Municipality integration route

Tradeoff:

- lower direct central control
- slower military requisition

### Focus group: Cooperative Land Trusts

Themes:

- public or community stewardship of land
- protection of personal property and small enterprise
- capture of public land value for services

Mechanical rewards:

- housing projects
- district construction
- lower cost for common stores and local infrastructure
- cooperative enterprise decisions

### Focus group: A Constitution of Provision

Themes:

- social guarantees
- legal rationing standards
- public right to minimum provision
- independent review of Need cases

Mechanical rewards:

- high-Concord crisis recovery
- stronger public legitimacy
- case-integrity bonus
- blocks the most abusive Assigned Colony tools

### Diplomacy interaction

The route can create a Voluntary Commonwealth League. Membership requires local government, public reserve commitments, and a vote or equivalent approval. Members retain sovereignty.

Shared actions include:

- reserve pooling
- reconstruction aid
- voluntary migration corridors
- arbitration
- mutual defense
- technical education

### External Need interaction

The route must normally try purchase, lease, migration treaty, and joint administration before ultimatum. War can become available only after repeated refusal, severe Need, or direct threat, and even then the player faces a constitutional review mission.

### Route capstone: Commonwealth by Consent

Payoff:

- mature voluntary identity
- strongest Concord and Choice thresholds
- high-quality associate municipalities
- eligibility for the peaceful final proclamation
- shorter workday and public learning package

Late-game play:

- expand the league
- mediate disputes
- integrate willing associates
- build regional reserve security
- resist domination by majors

## Political route B: Common Table

### Narrative role

This route reads the manifesto through common ownership, worker governance, and the abolition of class privilege. Production, land, and distribution become a collective system. The route can remain democratic through councils or drift toward centralized assignment when emergency planning dominates.

### Mechanical identity

- common ownership
- worker and civic councils
- strong Plenty growth after transition
- high capacity for reserve pooling
- mixed Choice and Assignment struggle inside the route
- international commune network
- stronger class conflict during early transition

### Route opener: Nothing Private in Necessity

Purpose:

- socialize major land, infrastructure, and strategic production

Gameplay direction:

- begins a property transition mission
- creates temporary production and stability costs
- unlocks council ownership rather than instant factory rewards

### Focus group: Councils of Callings

Themes:

- each calling elects delegates
- national shortages are negotiated among councils
- workers share information and training resources

Mechanical rewards:

- calling conversion through council action
- Choice gains when councils meet quotas voluntarily
- Assignment gains when central targets override council votes
- council composition events

### Focus group: The Common Table

Themes:

- public dining
- universal provision
- common stores
- equality in visible consumption

Mechanical rewards:

- reserve efficiency
- lower crisis Need
- stronger relief and aid actions
- elite backlash and black-market risks during transition

### Focus group: Social Workshops

Themes:

- worker-owned factories
- local repair shops
- production federations
- shared tools and designs

Mechanical rewards:

- production and repair decisions
- equipment exchange within the league
- route-specific industrial districts
- reduced dependency on private elite cooperation

### Focus group: Congress of Communes

Themes:

- national council constitution
- recall and rotation
- balance between local and central planning

Mechanical rewards:

- route balance decision between council autonomy and central plan
- leader or council identity change
- council diplomacy

Failure state:

- a deadlocked congress can create shortages and emergency assignment
- a central plan that repeatedly ignores councils can convert the route toward technocratic or coercive behavior

### Diplomacy interaction

The route can establish a Union of Common Tables or similar direct public country and faction identity. Membership favors socialist, cooperative, post-colonial, and small threatened states. The league should not require ideology purity when material cooperation is strong, but it should reject openly exploitative protectorates.

Shared actions include:

- equipment pools
- reconstruction brigades
- worker education missions
- anti-blockade transport
- collective guarantees
- support for voluntary local communes

### External Need interaction

The route can negotiate joint ownership, shared extraction, and cross-border communes. It can also rationalize annexation as ending private control, which creates a route-specific danger. Low Concord or high Assignment can turn a nominally internationalist case into imposed centralization.

### Route capstone: The Union of Tables

Payoff:

- strong collective provision
- regional reserve network
- council-based final identity
- mature worker-governance decisions
- eligibility for a socialist commonwealth proclamation

Late-game play:

- resolve council versus plan tension
- build cross-border production networks
- integrate associates without erasing local councils
- confront capitalist blockade or authoritarian imitation

## Political route C: Guardians of Measure

### Narrative role

This route treats the manifesto as a problem of accurate information and competent administration. Surveyors, planners, engineers, physicians, statisticians, and educators become guardians of a measured society. The route can produce impressive provision and orderly settlements. It can also reduce people to categories and quotas.

### Mechanical identity

- high Plenty potential
- high Assignment pressure
- strong construction and logistics
- fast shortage correction
- powerful Inland Island and Garden City projects
- lower political volatility
- risk of technocratic paternalism and concealed inefficiency

### Route opener: The Country Measured

Purpose:

- give expert boards authority over the first national plan

Gameplay direction:

- strengthens survey accuracy
- unlocks shortage forecasting
- moves balance toward Assignment
- creates a legitimacy requirement for planners

### Focus group: Standard Houses

Themes:

- modular housing
- public utilities
- planned district layouts
- transport-linked settlement

Mechanical rewards:

- faster housing projects
- capital relief
- district network bonuses
- risk of monotony and local resistance when designs ignore custom

### Focus group: Tables of Need

Themes:

- national input-output survey
- reserve forecasts
- occupational quotas
- dynamic emergency plans

Mechanical rewards:

- stronger Need calculation
- cheaper guaranteed placement
- improved emergency levy
- forecast events that can still be wrong under chaos or bad data

### Focus group: The Board of Measure

Themes:

- institutional planner leadership
- technical appointments
- standards for evidence and performance

Mechanical rewards:

- institutional leader or council
- route-specific advisors
- lower project failure
- higher cost when local Concord is ignored

### Focus group: Cities in Series

Themes:

- linked Garden City districts
- specialized towns
- rail and power integration
- planned population distribution

Mechanical rewards:

- strongest district construction
- distributed industry
- rapid Inland Island progress
- route-specific city network interface states

### Focus group: Useful Freedom

This is the internal reform side branch.

Themes:

- protected personal life
- professional independence
- transparent appeals
- flexible quota systems

Mechanical rewards:

- limits Concord loss from Assignment
- keeps hidden humanist convergence available
- reduces raw planning efficiency compared with strict measure

### Focus group: Exact Obedience

This is the hard-planning side branch.

Themes:

- compulsory occupational placement
- standardized service
- centralized district authority
- performance courts

Mechanical rewards:

- rapid shortage correction
- military production capacity
- strong emergency response
- significant Concord and Choice cost
- possible convergence with Closed Island

### Diplomacy interaction

The route exports technical missions, planning standards, and district construction. It can form a Planned Cities Network that begins as a technical compact and can become a faction after enough members share reserve and defense rules.

### External Need interaction

The route produces detailed cases with strong apparent integrity. This makes it more capable of exploiting the doctrine. An accurate survey can justify a real lease or joint project. A manipulated survey can make expansion look scientific. Route events should test whether the Board corrects bad evidence or protects its authority.

### Route capstone: The Perfect Measure

Payoff:

- highest stable Plenty potential
- advanced district network
- strong planning state identity
- choice between humane technocracy and rigid assignment
- eligibility for planned commonwealth proclamation

Late-game play:

- maintain accurate information
- prevent planner capture
- manage a network of associated cities
- decide whether statistical success can excuse low consent

## Political route D: Closed Island

### Narrative role

This route turns the manifesto into a doctrine of sealed unity, compulsory usefulness, and expansion justified by necessity. The country promises abundance to insiders and imposes the cost on assigned workers, colonies, penal laborers, mercenaries, and subject populations.

The route should be tempting. It solves shortages quickly, protects citizens from some military costs, and builds a disciplined island project. Its strength must create visible resistance, isolation, and long-term burden.

### Mechanical identity

- high Assignment
- rapid mobilization
- strong fortification and autarky
- early access to coercive Necessary Ground escalation
- Assigned Colonies
- penal labor
- auxiliary mercenaries
- low Concord and high foreign hostility
- possible high-chaos Perfect Island transformation

### Route opener: One Island, One Measure

Purpose:

- declare unity and obedience as the condition of provision

Gameplay direction:

- moves balance sharply toward Assignment
- locks out the most voluntary constitutional route
- unlocks service registration and loyalty rules

### Focus group: Households of Service

Themes:

- hereditary or assigned occupational groups
- compulsory agricultural and military training
- relocation orders
- service-based access to housing

Mechanical rewards:

- rapid calling correction
- manpower and construction mobilization
- severe Choice loss
- route events about evasion and privilege

### Focus group: The Closed Store

Themes:

- ration cards
- national reserve priority
- exclusion of outsiders
- emergency autarky

Mechanical rewards:

- strong reserve defense
- lower import dependence
- high control during blockade
- reduced aid and foreign trust

### Focus group: Penal Works

Themes:

- criminal labor
- political labor battalions
- colonial construction gangs
- punishment through work

Mechanical rewards:

- fast fortification, resource, and infrastructure projects
- garrison and repression tools
- condemnation and deaths integration
- persistent revolt risk

### Focus group: Zapolet Contracts

Themes:

- foreign auxiliaries
- mercenary recruitment
- externalized casualties
- disposable border forces

Mechanical rewards:

- hired formations or temporary support
- lower citizen casualties in selected operations
- equipment, convoy, and foreign leverage cost
- betrayal, scandal, and dependency events

The final localisation should not use the source name automatically unless research and setting fit justify it. The structural inspiration is required, but the in-game group can have a period-appropriate fictional identity.

### Focus group: The Natural Right of Need

Themes:

- strong interpretation of underused land
- settler corridors
- coerced joint administration
- ultimata

Mechanical rewards:

- faster case escalation
- wider target options
- Assigned Colony integration
- strong foreign backlash

### Focus group: Cut the Channel

Themes:

- extreme separation project
- fortification, waterworks, controlled transport, and a politically manufactured island

Geographic variants:

- true channel or canal project where supported
- fortified island network for archipelagic countries
- port and coastal barrier for coastal countries
- Inland Island ring for landlocked countries

The focus must never promise actual province geometry changes.

### Route capstone: The Perfect Island

Payoff:

- closed high-output regime
- strong defense and assignment
- severe diplomatic isolation
- unique high-chaos decisions
- possible route-specific proclamation variant

Late-game play:

- suppress colonial resistance
- sustain autarky
- manage mercenary loyalty
- prevent reserve collapse
- decide whether to maintain isolation or impose the model abroad

## Hidden political route E: The Joke Understood

### Narrative role

This route becomes available when the country preserves public debate, education, local consent, and interpretive uncertainty. It treats the manifesto as a mirror held to bad institutions. The state adopts practical reforms without declaring that every feature of the fictional island must become law.

### Reveal conditions

The route should require a combination such as:

- high Choice
- healthy Concord
- completion of public lecture and open debate content
- no penal labor
- no unjustified external case
- limited emergency assignment
- a route event in which the player permits criticism of the text

The route can reveal before final political commitment or as a mid-game constitutional correction from Consent of Households, Common Table, or humane Guardians of Measure.

### Mechanical identity

- mixed property order
- strong civil liberty
- adaptive decisions
- moderate Plenty growth
- highest route flexibility
- reduced access to extreme capstones
- strong recovery from failed literal experiments

### Route opener: Read the Island as a Mirror

Purpose:

- reject textual infallibility
- preserve useful institutions
- establish constitutional revision

Gameplay direction:

- removes literalist route pressure
- protects debate
- opens mixed ownership and adaptive planning

### Focus group: Institutions That Can Be Left

Themes:

- lawful exit
- voluntary association
- municipal experimentation
- sunset clauses
- independent review

Mechanical rewards:

- easier cleanup of failed policies
- lower Concord loss from reform reversal
- stronger protection against route lock abuse

### Focus group: A Mixed Commonwealth

Themes:

- public land trusts
- cooperatives
- regulated private enterprise
- social guarantees
- public options

Mechanical rewards:

- balanced Plenty and Choice
- fewer transition shocks
- lower maximum planning output

### Focus group: Satire in Government

Themes:

- institutional criticism
- public audit
- protection for opposition
- humility in policy

Mechanical rewards:

- contradiction events resolve more constructively
- false Need cases are easier to abandon
- lower propaganda and coercion capability

### Focus group: Reform Without Paradise

Themes:

- practical completion
- limited claims
- social floor
- plural constitutional identity

Mechanical rewards:

- resilient final commonwealth
- strong diplomacy
- lower snowball potential
- achievement and peaceful proclamation eligibility

### Route capstone: A Good Place That Admits Its Limits

Payoff:

- adaptive mixed commonwealth
- strong Concord and Choice
- stable medium Plenty
- best failure recovery
- limited territorial ambition

Late-game play:

- spread institutions through example and aid
- protect pluralism during war
- mediate other routes inside the league
- maintain reform without a sacred blueprint

## Crisis and failure branch

The tree should reveal a temporary crisis branch when Plenty and Concord collapse or when the interim charter expires without a viable settlement.

### Crisis entry states

- empty stores
- failed first district
- repeated calling shortages
- severe labor evasion
- local revolt
- planner coup attempt
- property conflict
- external case scandal

### Crisis options

#### Restore Consent

- emergency local votes
- release from quotas
- foreign or cooperative aid

#### Empower the Councils

- route toward Common Table
- worker control of stores

#### Give the Surveyors Authority

- route toward Guardians of Measure
- emergency plan

#### Seal the Island

- route toward Closed Island
- rationing and compulsory service

#### Admit the Book Was a Question

- hidden humanist recovery
- requires preserved debate and education

The crisis branch should be temporary and should rejoin a stable route. It must not create an endless alternate tree beside the main content.

## Final identity and formation lane

### Proof of the commonwealth

The player must demonstrate more than focus completion.

Required proof should combine:

- route capstone
- adequate Plenty
- route-appropriate Concord
- stable Choice or Assignment position
- functioning capital or island project
- resolved first external Need case
- at least one associate, league partner, or recognized foreign compact for a regional proclamation
- no active constitutional crisis

### Formation decision

The final identity should normally use a cosmetic tag or dynamic route identity while preserving the original tag.

Route identity directions:

- voluntary commonwealth
- council union
- planned Utopia
- closed island-state
- practical humanist commonwealth

The public map name must remain a direct country name. Institutional bodies can have longer names inside the mechanic.

### Post-formation focus groups

#### Integrate the Ring

- stabilize districts and associates
- route-specific integration

#### The Second Generation

- education, leadership succession, and removal of temporary founding ideas

#### A Rule for Need

- codify claim limits or expansion doctrine

#### Beyond the Founder's Island

- decide whether to export institutions, build a league, remain local, or impose the model

#### The Commonwealth at War

- late route response to major conflict
- protects mechanic viability under total mobilization

#### Plenty in an Age of Chaos

- high-chaos resilience
- connects evolutions and final regional role

## Focus reward standards

The final tree should use varied rewards.

Expected reward families include:

- decision and mission unlocks
- staged idea upgrades
- map construction
- rail, port, supply, housing, and district work
- advisors and institutional leaders
- calling methods
- reserve capacity
- militia and engineer templates
- diplomatic actions
- associate and league rules
- claims and case preparation
- local charters and integration missions
- route events
- cosmetic identity
- achievements
- super-event eligibility

Flat political power, stability, war support, small percentages, and equipment grants may support a larger focus package. They should not be the main reward pattern.

## Tree pacing

- Opening focuses should usually be shorter and establish the mechanic quickly.
- Constitutional route openers should require meaningful early commitment.
- Support branches should develop across early and middle play.
- Necessary Ground and league formation should require institutional proof.
- The final identity should normally be a middle-to-late campaign achievement.
- High-chaos evolution branches should become available through event state, not through a simple date lock.

## Route validity

The implementation must hide, bypass, or disable content when required geography or actors do not exist.

Examples:

- island lease content needs a valid target
- foreign league content needs eligible partners
- port-access cases need a relevant coastal target
- associate integration needs a living target or controlled state
- mercenary contracts need a valid foreign source or a fictional contract system approved by implementation review
- high-chaos separation projects need a geographic variant

Invalid content must not trap the focus tree. Every critical path needs a safe alternative that preserves the route's identity without becoming a silent fallback.
