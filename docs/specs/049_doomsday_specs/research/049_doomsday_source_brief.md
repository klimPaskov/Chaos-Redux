# Event 049 Accepted Source Brief

## Catalog identity

- Event ID: `49`
- Event name: Doomsday
- Replaces: Mass Panic
- Event type: Major
- Status: To Be Reworked
- Chaos level: 4
- Cluster: None

## Accepted premise

Unrelated prophecies, religious expectations, folk traditions, dreams, numerological systems, occult movements, astronomical interpretations, and political subcultures begin converging on the belief that the world will end within only a few years.

No public authority can prove a cause. The shared crisis comes from convergence. Groups that had no prior contact increasingly identify roughly the same period, and later the same date, as the end of ordinary history.

The first event has immediate global effects. War support, stability, recruitment, research, education, construction, investment, and long-term institutional planning weaken. Thousands of Doomsday Societies form around pacifism, judgment, survival, communal preparation, ascetic withdrawal, hedonism, and political surrender.

## Public mechanic values

The event exposes only two persistent player-facing values:

1. **Doomsday Conviction**, which represents how strongly the world accepts the prediction.
2. **Time Until the End**, which presents the predicted date and the remaining time.

Country belief, society organization, current composition, government legitimacy, suppression backlash, institutional continuity, war pressure, and terminal readiness remain hidden or appear through qualitative status text.

## Predicted date

The event creates a dynamic date around two to four years after firing. The leading date is publicly visible from the opening. Early groups still disagree about exact calculation. Evolution I makes the date socially dominant and names it **The Last Day**.

## Government responses

Countries can discredit the claims, reassure the public, cooperate with religious and civic authorities, suppress societies, build shelters and reserves, accept the possibility privately, negotiate with pacifists, or redirect state purpose toward the remaining years.

The response loop must remain functional without DLC-only politics or intelligence systems.

## Peace movement

Doomsday belief creates real pressure to end costly wars. It must not white-peace every conflict automatically. Defensive wars, wars of survival, ideological wars, civil wars, colonial wars, and wars of conquest produce different public reactions.

Some governments demobilize. Some continue through coercion. Some become more aggressive because their leaders believe that there is no future cost to bear.

## Evolution I

**The Last Calendar** becomes eligible at 800 or more Chaos.

Predictions converge on an exact date. Conviction accelerates. Pacifism, refusal to serve, institutional withdrawal, and cancellation of long projects intensify. Countries gain Last Days policies around demobilization, prisoners, borders, debt, reserves, shelters, religious observance, and representation for the societies.

If this evolution is active before the event fires, the opening begins with higher Conviction and a broadly credible Last Day.

## Evolution II

**Nothing After Tomorrow** becomes eligible at 1000 or more Chaos.

Doomsday Societies begin operating as parallel governments. Municipal authorities, religious administrations, communal networks, soldiers, workers, and local councils transfer practical power away from ordinary states. Countries with severe institutional collapse can install Doomsday administrations peacefully or through revolution.

The evolution unlocks the terminal route.

## World-end route

**The Final Vigil** is a public world-end route at 1000 or more Chaos.

Earth is not physically destroyed. Conventional political life ends because enough of humanity no longer believes that territory, wealth, ideology, empire, and long-term victory have meaning.

Doomsday administrations replace ordinary governments. Wars face severe ceasefire pressure. Armies demobilize or fragment. Military production and long-term construction collapse. The Final Assembly coordinates food, shelter, peace, and basic order until the predicted end.

The route can emerge through voluntary government replacement, overwhelming global Conviction, major-power demobilization, organized pacifist transfer of military authority, or cascading collapse after repeated violent suppression.

## Arrival of the predicted date

If The Final Vigil has not activated, the date arrives and nothing clearly supernatural occurs.

Movements collapse, reinterpret the prediction, move the date, claim that mercy prevented destruction, or radicalize. Governments that preserved institutions recover faster and gain legitimacy. Countries that dismantled armies, research systems, education, industry, and long-term development must rebuild them.

The failed date leaves lasting social and institutional consequences. It must not become an endless loop of revised dates.

## Presentation

The event should focus on crowds, vigils, calendars, abandoned workplaces, empty classrooms, pacifist marches, communal settlements, stockpiles, shelters, desertion, and public fatalism.

The event should not be framed around a single scientist, laboratory, or expert warning.
