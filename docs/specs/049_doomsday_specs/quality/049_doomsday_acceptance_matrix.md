## Hard failure conditions

The implementation remains incomplete if any of these conditions is true:

- A third persistent public event value is introduced.
- The date rerolls or leaves the accepted range without an explicit accepted change.
- Doomsday administrations are replaced by generic new tags.
- The event automatically white-peaces every war.
- Evolution activation changes Chaos directly.
- The Final Vigil fires below 1000 Chaos or without Evolution II and readiness.
- The failed date is reduced to one popup.
- Famine, Migration, Deaths, Condemnation, or Air Cleanliness receives duplicate ledgers or transactions.
- The focus branch is a shallow reward ladder or reads identically in every country.
- AI weighted behavior lacks probability evidence.
- Required art or audio remains placeholder, copied, unlicensed, or unwired.
- Event Details, docs, localisation, and workbook disagree.
- The improvement-loop requirement remains unresolved at implementation completion.
