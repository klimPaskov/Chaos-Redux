# Event 049 Doomsday Specification

## Part 7: Doomsday Administration Focus Content

## Focus-content role

Evolution II can replace an ordinary government with a Doomsday administration. That political transformation needs playable national direction for the remaining time.

The event should not create a full replacement tree for every country. It should add a shared emergency focus branch or event-owned focus layer that adapts to the country, takeover route, movement currents, war state, institutions, and countdown.

The branch should contain roughly 18 to 24 focuses in a normal administration path. Rare revolutionary content can add a small hidden extension.

The focus labels in this part are working labels, not final localisation.

## Loading and identity rules

- The branch becomes available only after a valid national Doomsday administration takes power.
- Existing national focus content remains available where it still fits the new government and remaining time.
- Offensive expansion, long-horizon prestige, and incompatible political content can be blocked, bypassed, or made irrelevant only when the administration has explicitly abandoned those purposes.
- The branch must not erase completed national history.
- The branch must adapt text, targets, rewards, and AI behavior to the country.
- A shared branch cannot read identically in every country.
- No focus inlay window is planned. The two public event values remain in the decision category.

## Duration rules

- Use a 7-day opener for the immediate transfer of authority.
- Use 35-day focuses for policy choices, institution transfers, and urgent programs.
- Use 70-day focuses for major administrative restructuring, broad demobilization, or international commitments.
- Do not create several 35-day focuses that divide one reward without a new choice or action.
- The branch must remain meaningful even when less than one year remains.
- A country that enters during the final month should receive accelerated emergency alternatives or automatic bypasses for content that cannot finish in time.

## Branch architecture

The branch has a compact central trunk and five visible families:

1. Peace and Demobilization
2. Bread, Shelter, and Records
3. Observance and Social Peace
4. The internal government fork between Communal Devolution and Custodial Administration
5. Final Assembly Diplomacy

A hidden Avenging Witnesses extension can appear after severe suppression history and revolutionary takeover.

## Opening trunk

### Working focus: Government Until the Last Day

Role:

A 7-day route opener that establishes the administration, confirms the surviving chain of authority, and opens the event-owned branch.

Effects direction:

- Applies the correct administration identity package.
- Converts active ordinary response decisions into administration decisions.
- Preserves or replaces the existing leader according to takeover route.
- Opens an immediate choice between preserving centralized custody and devolving practical authority.
- Starts a national inventory of food, shelter, records, transport, military units, and public institutions.

### Working focus group: Count What Remains

Role:

A short trunk group that assesses surviving institutions and sets country-specific priorities.

It should not provide free resources. It reveals or unlocks the real missions needed to protect them.

Possible focus roles:

- Inventory the reserves.
- Register functioning hospitals and schools.
- Record available trains, convoys, depots, and shelters.
- Establish the administration’s public mandate.

### Working convergence: The Custodial Mandate

Role:

Defines the government’s limited purpose around food, shelter, peace, records, and public order.

This focus opens the five branch families and creates the administration’s main staged national spirit.

## Branch 1: Peace and Demobilization

### Narrative role

This branch removes military authority from offensive war while deciding what defensive and civil-protection capacity remains.

### Mechanical role

- Raises armistice and truce capacity.
- Converts units and production toward territorial defense, transport, medical support, and relief.
- Preserves command records for a possible failed-date recovery.
- Changes alliance behavior and Final Assembly eligibility.

### Internal focus groups

#### Working group: The Last Muster

The administration records units, weapons, depots, officers, and service claims before demobilization.

It unlocks a mission to keep military records and depots intact.

#### Working fork: Lay Down the Offensive or Guard the Living

One focus accelerates broad demobilization and strengthens pacifist legitimacy.

The other retains defensive-only units for cities, borders, relief, and public safety.

The choice should be mutually exclusive because it defines the military role of the administration.

#### Working group: Prisoners and Objectors

The administration releases or reviews conscientious objectors, deserters, political prisoners, and military detainees.

The content adapts to the country’s laws and suppression history.

#### Working capstone: No War Beyond the Date

The country renounces new offensive war and qualifies for stronger Final Assembly peace diplomacy.

A country under invasion can retain immediate territorial defense. The focus does not require suicidal surrender.

### Decisions and missions unlocked

- Defensive-only service.
- Depot preservation.
- Prisoner release and review.
- Armistice proposals.
- Arms-line conversion.
- Relief escort and military transport missions.

### AI direction

Losing countries, pacifist administrations, military-refusal takeovers, and countries with high desertion should favor this branch.

Countries under immediate invasion should select defensive retention before full demobilization.

## Branch 2: Bread, Shelter, and Records

### Narrative role

This branch keeps people alive and preserves the material capacity to continue after the date.

### Mechanical role

- Improves famine and migration resilience through bounded adapters.
- Builds or converts shelters, storage, hospitals, and continuity sites.
- Protects archives, seed, medicine, and transport.
- Raises institutional continuity.

### Internal focus groups

#### Working group: The Common Storehouse

The administration inventories and distributes reserves through state, civic, religious, or communal networks.

The focus opens targeted distribution decisions and anti-hoarding enforcement.

#### Working group: Shelter for Every District

The country selects priority states and commits construction, tools, transport, and support equipment.

The focus should unlock projects instead of granting shelters instantly.

#### Working group: Copy the Records

The administration duplicates civil, military, scientific, medical, and financial records across protected sites.

#### Working group: Keep the Roads Open

This group protects rail hubs, ports, roads, and communications through named missions.

#### Working capstone: A Country That Can Wake Again

The administration creates a reopening plan, preserves selected institutions, and improves failed-date recovery.

### Decisions and missions unlocked

- Regional shelter projects.
- Reserve distribution.
- Archive duplication.
- Hospital staffing.
- Rail and port continuity.
- Settlement reception and family reunion.
- Conversion of shelters after the date.

### AI direction

National Preparation and custodial administrations should favor this branch. Countries facing famine, bombing, disaster, contamination, or migration pressure prioritize it strongly.

## Branch 3: Observance and Social Peace

### Narrative role

This branch manages mass religious, secular, civic, and cultural preparation without allowing observance to destroy relief and transport.

### Mechanical role

- Reduces violent disorder.
- Channels devotional and prophetic organization into public service.
- Shares legitimacy with trusted institutions.
- Creates final-month vigil and reconciliation options.

### Internal focus groups

#### Working group: A Place for Every Vigil

The administration identifies safe public spaces, transport rules, medical support, and crowd limits.

#### Working group: Reconciliation Before the Date

The country opens prisoner, family, religious, ethnic, regional, or political reconciliation processes that fit its history.

The content must avoid generic forgiveness that erases ongoing atrocity or occupation.

#### Working fork: One National Observance or Many Traditions

A centralized observance improves national coordination and risks exclusion.

A plural observance protects local traditions and makes coordination harder.

#### Working capstone: The Bells and the Sirens

The administration establishes the final-day public schedule, emergency signals, hospital staffing, and communications plan.

The final localisation needs country-specific cultural research. The working label must not become the universal final title.

### Decisions and missions unlocked

- Protected vigils.
- Pilgrimage transport.
- Public reconciliation.
- Joint religious and civic councils.
- Final-day communications.
- Crowd and relief-route management.

### AI direction

Concordat administrations, devotional currents, plural societies, and countries with high movement organization should favor this branch.

## Branch 4A: Communal Devolution

### Narrative role

The administration transfers practical authority to municipalities, villages, cooperatives, congregations, labor councils, and local relief boards.

### Mechanical role

- Improves local relief and resilience.
- Weakens central taxation, command, and uniform policy.
- Strengthens Communal Stewards in the Final Assembly.
- Creates local autonomy that can survive after a failed date.

### Route lock

This branch is mutually exclusive with Custodial Administration after the initial shared trunk.

### Internal focus groups

#### Working group: The Districts Feed Themselves

Local councils gain authority over food, shelter, and repair.

#### Working group: Local Peace Committees

Municipal and community institutions negotiate local ceasefires, prisoner exchanges, and public order.

#### Working group: Open the Empty Buildings

The administration transfers abandoned offices, estates, schools, and factories into shelter and communal use.

#### Working capstone: A Federation of Final Communities

The country formalizes broad local autonomy and sends communal delegates to the Final Assembly.

### Tradeoffs

- Stronger local survival and movement legitimacy.
- Weaker central mobilization, tax collection, and uniform distribution.
- Higher risk that wealthy or armed regions exclude outsiders.
- More difficult post-date recentralization.

### AI direction

Municipal cascade takeovers, communal currents, fragmented states, and countries with strong local institutions favor this route.

## Branch 4B: Custodial Administration

### Narrative role

The central government keeps a disciplined national chain of custody for reserves, archives, transport, and public safety.

### Mechanical role

- Preserves central administration and post-date restoration.
- Coordinates national distribution and defensive service.
- Strengthens Reserve Custodians in the Final Assembly.
- Risks elite capture, exclusion, and coercive continuity.

### Route lock

This branch is mutually exclusive with Communal Devolution after the initial shared trunk.

### Internal focus groups

#### Working group: One Ledger, One Reserve

The administration centralizes inventory and distribution.

#### Working group: The Continuity Service

Civil servants, engineers, medical workers, and transport staff receive protected status and obligations.

#### Working group: Guard the Stores

Defensive units and public workers protect reserves, archives, hospitals, and communications.

#### Working capstone: The State in Trust

The country defines itself as a temporary custodian with a legal reopening or succession plan.

### Tradeoffs

- Stronger national coordination and restoration.
- Higher political burden and risk of unequal access.
- Lower communal autonomy.
- Greater danger that Emergency Order methods survive inside the administration.

### AI direction

Voluntary custodial transfers, countries with strong civil services, major powers, and states facing cross-regional distribution needs favor this route.

## Branch 5: Final Assembly Diplomacy

### Narrative role

This branch turns national preparation into an international order centered on food, shelter, peace, and public continuity.

### Mechanical role

- Sends observers and delegates.
- Negotiates common demobilization standards.
- Pools transport and relief.
- Creates membership and voting influence.
- Shapes the dominant Final Assembly current.

### Internal focus groups

#### Working group: Send the Delegation

The country enters the preparatory congress and chooses the kind of delegation it sends.

#### Working group: The Common Relief Routes

Members open transport, shelter, prisoner, and medical arrangements.

#### Working group: The Demobilization Compact

Countries coordinate arms reduction and defensive obligations.

#### Working group: Speak for the Remaining World

The administration campaigns for its dominant current and seeks recognition from skeptical or holdout states.

#### Working capstone: Petition the Final Assembly

The country completes the political and practical requirements for full membership.

### AI direction

AI weight depends on takeover route, movement current, military danger, transport access, and whether other major powers have joined.

A country under immediate invasion should not disarm fully only to qualify.

## Hidden extension: Avenging Witnesses

### Reveal conditions

The hidden extension appears only when:

- The country entered through revolutionary collapse or a related violent takeover.
- Suppression backlash is severe.
- Real deaths, disappearances, destroyed records, or exposed atrocity exist.
- The Avenging Witnesses current is materially present.

### Narrative role

The route argues that the old order must answer for its crimes before the Last Day.

### Mechanical role

- Investigates prisons, police, camps, elite shelters, and destroyed archives.
- Seizes or publishes records.
- Removes perpetrators or former officials through trials, exile, or revolutionary punishment.
- Strengthens revolutionary influence in the Final Assembly.
- Risks revenge cycles, executions, archive destruction, and post-date counterrevolution.

### Internal focus groups

#### Working group: Open Every Cell

The administration releases prisoners and investigates detention sites.

#### Working group: Name the Missing

The country reconstructs evidence and records real deaths through the owning systems where applicable.

#### Working fork: Public Tribunal or Revolutionary Settlement

A tribunal preserves more legitimacy and records.

A revolutionary settlement acts faster and creates higher violence and counterrevolution risk.

#### Working capstone: Judgment Before the Dawn

The administration commits its final political identity to punishment and memory.

The final wording requires careful historical and cultural research. Cheap revenge language is forbidden.

## Shared idea lifecycle

The branch should use a small number of staged ideas.

| Working idea | Start or unlock | Starting role | Improvement path | Failure path | Final forms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Doomsday Administration | Applied at takeover | Defines the limited emergency government and its initial weakness. | Strengthened by a clear mandate and functioning services. | Worsens through factional conflict, empty reserves, or illegitimate seizure. | Communal Federation, State in Trust, Peace Administration, or Revolutionary Vigil. |
| Hollowed Institutions | Inherited when continuity is weak | Represents lost staff, records, command, and services. | Reduced through Bread, Shelter, and Records. | Worsens through abandonment and final-war mobilization. | Removed, reduced, or retained as a long aftermath burden. |
| The Last Muster | Unlocked by military route | Tracks orderly demobilization and preserved command. | Improved by depot and defensive-service missions. | Collapses through mutiny, seizure, or lost records. | Civil Protection Corps, Demobilized Republic, or Scattered Arms. |
| Common Relief Network | Unlocked by relief route | Connects councils, transport, hospitals, and shelters. | Improved through route missions and Assembly cooperation. | Corrupted by hoarding, exclusion, or route failure. | National Continuity Service or Federation of Relief Councils. |
| Suppression Reckoning | Hidden after severe repression | Carries prisoner, evidence, and revenge pressure. | Resolved through tribunals, disclosure, or reconciliation. | Escalates through summary punishment or destroyed evidence. | Public Record, Unsettled Graves, or Revolutionary Justice legacy. |

The implementation can rename or merge these ideas. It should not create a separate national spirit for every focus.

## Country adaptation

The branch must adapt at least these surfaces:

- Government title and council composition.
- Religious, civic, labor, municipal, military, and regional institutions.
- Named states, ports, railways, capitals, archives, and shelter regions.
- War and occupation status.
- Existing national leaders and laws.
- Dominant society current.
- Takeover route.
- Existing famine, migration, contamination, disaster, and military pressure.
- Final Assembly current preference.

A continental major, island country, colonial empire, occupied state, small neutral, and civil-war government cannot receive the same transport, demobilization, and local-government content.

## Focus rewards

Rewards should emphasize:

- Decision and mission unlocks.
- Changed military service and unit roles.
- Shelter, hospital, storage, rail, port, and archive projects.
- Relief and migration adapters.
- Leader, council, party, and government identity changes.
- Final Assembly membership and current influence.
- Institution preservation and failed-date recovery.
- Armistice, prisoner, and border policy.

Flat stability, War Support, political power, and small modifiers can support these effects. They cannot be the main reward of important focuses.

## Layout direction

The tree should be compact and symmetrical at normal zoom.

Suggested lane order from left to right:

1. Peace and Demobilization
2. Communal Devolution
3. Central trunk
4. Custodial Administration
5. Bread, Shelter, and Records
6. Observance and Social Peace
7. Final Assembly Diplomacy as a lower convergence line

The hidden Avenging Witnesses extension should appear below the internal-government area after reveal.

Major branches need clear roots and capstones. Connectors should be short and direct. Do not create a spiderweb between every branch.

## Focus filters and navigation

Every focus needs accurate search filters for political, military, industry, infrastructure, diplomacy, social policy, or special-mechanic content.

The branch is moderate in size. Focus Navigation is useful if the implementation places major families far enough apart that normal panning becomes inconvenient. At minimum, navigation candidates are:

- Peace and defense
- Relief and continuity
- Government form
- Final Assembly

Hidden content must not receive a visible navigation shortcut before reveal.

## AI route plans

### Custodial stabilizer

Chooses central custody, relief, records, defensive service, and Assembly membership.

Preferred by stable administrations, major powers, and strong civil services.

### Communal federation

Chooses local councils, open buildings, local peace, relief networks, and communal Assembly leadership.

Preferred by municipal cascade takeovers and countries with strong local organization.

### Pacifist demobilizer

Prioritizes prisoners, armistice, arms conversion, and full demobilization.

Preferred by losing wars and military-refusal takeovers.

### Devotional reconciler

Prioritizes observance, religious and civic councils, reconciliation, hospitals, and social peace.

Preferred by Concordat histories and devotional currents.

### Revolutionary reckoning

Prioritizes prisoner release, evidence, tribunals or punishment, communal control, and Avenging Witnesses influence.

Preferred only after severe repression and revolutionary takeover.

AI plans must remain valid when the country is invaded, landlocked, isolated, occupied, or missing named institutions.

## Focus-branch end states

A branch completion should leave a visible political order.

Possible end states include:

- A demobilized peace administration.
- A defensive civil-protection state.
- A federation of autonomous final communities.
- A centralized state held in trust.
- A devotional and civic reconciliation government.
- A relief-centered Final Assembly member.
- A revolutionary administration pursuing judgment.

If the date fails, these end states shape whether the emergency government dissolves, reforms the constitution, survives as a permanent regime, or faces counterrevolution.
