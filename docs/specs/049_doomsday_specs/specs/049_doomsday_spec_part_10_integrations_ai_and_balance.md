# Event 049 Doomsday Specification

## Part 10: Integrations, AI, Chaos, and Balance

## Integration principle

Event 049 should react to the living Chaos Redux world without taking ownership of systems that already track war, peace, deaths, contamination, famine, migration, natural disaster, and terminal state.

The event can submit bounded inputs, receive proven state, and create its own belief and institutional consequences. It cannot create duplicate ledgers or count the same harm twice.

## Shared system ownership matrix

| System | Owner | Event 049 use | Forbidden duplication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chaos Meter | Shared Chaos system | Adds or removes Chaos for distinct Event 049 outcomes. | Recounting wars, peace, deaths, annexation, nuclear use, or contamination. |
| Deaths | Shared Deaths system | Records real deaths from event-owned riots, suppression, abandonment, or revolutionary violence. | Flat scripted death totals without population loss, or counting the same deaths through two events. |
| Condemnation | Shared Condemnation system | Receives proven violent repression, cover-up, atrocity, or blocked inspection. | A generic condemnation penalty for every legal restriction. |
| Famine | Famine system | Receives bounded labor, hoarding, transport, governance, relief, and reserve effects. | New Food Security, Food Reserves, Relief Access, mortality, or famine decision values. |
| Migration | Migration system | Executes real movement to settlements, family reunion, pilgrimage, sanctuary, and return. | Separate population subtraction, survivor credit, trapped-person ledger, or route deaths. |
| Air Cleanliness | Air Cleanliness system | High contamination and Air Winter can act as confirmation shocks. | Direct Event 049 contamination or duplicate atmospheric changes. |
| World end | Shared world-end contract plus event owner | The Final Vigil commits one public terminal route. | Generic random selection or double commitment with another owner. |
| Special-country classification | Shared dynamic triggers | Uses `uses_normal_civilian_systems` for ordinary eligibility. | Adding Doomsday administrations to nonhuman or special-country classifiers. |
| Natural disaster | Event 013 owner | Disaster outcomes can confirm the belief and use shelters or relief capacity. | Reimplementing disaster damage or event sequence. |
| Event log and Event Details | Shared event systems | Records Event 049, evolutions, and the public world-end route. | Separate event-history framework. |

## Cross-event interaction map

### Event 002 Zombie Outbreak

A visible outbreak or major spread can produce a one-time confirmation shock.

Doomsday shelters, hospitals, transport, and continuity sites can improve response where the outbreak system accepts support.

Actual zombie or nonhuman countries do not receive ordinary Doomsday civilian decisions.

Event 049 does not own outbreak deaths, spread, cure, units, or terminal state.

### Event 003 The Holy Realm

The Holy Realm can interpret the date through its own doctrine and political state.

Possible interactions include endorsement, rejection, competing prophecy, pilgrimage, or an attempt to dominate devotional societies.

The Holy Realm should not become the universal source of the prediction. Its owner retains its own event and world-end logic.

### Event 009 White Peace

White Peace can provide an existing precedent or helper for negotiated war resolution where compatible.

Event 049 does not call global white peace automatically. It uses contextual peace pressure and bilateral or faction settlement.

### Event 010 Death

A visible Death crisis can strongly increase Conviction once per relevant milestone.

Doomsday movements can interpret Death as proof, judgment, or a reason for final resistance.

Actual special actors follow their owner classifications and do not receive ordinary civilian treatment.

### Event 013 Natural Disasters

Severe disasters can raise Conviction through bounded confirmation events.

Shelters, hospitals, reserves, rail repair, and civic relief built under Event 049 can provide real preparation benefits.

Event 013 remains the owner of disaster selection, impact, aftermath, and damage.

### Event 015 Utopia Manifesto

Utopian movements can cooperate with, compete with, or reject Doomsday Societies.

A society that promises a future beyond the date can reduce Conviction among its supporters. A final-community route can strengthen communal organization.

The two events should not merge their public values.

### Event 016 Brilliant Scientist

Scientists and research institutions can support independent calculation, public evidence, shelter science, continuity archives, or emergency research.

A compromised or extremist scientist can create false confirmation or exploit the crisis.

Event 049 does not grant custom technologies through ordinary reassurance.

### Event 020 The Black Plague

A severe plague outbreak can produce a bounded confirmation shock.

Hospitals, quarantine-capable shelters, relief councils, and transport choices can change response through existing plague and humanitarian systems.

Event 049 does not own plague mortality or nonhuman rat actors.

### Event 028 Asteroid incoming

An asteroid warning is one of the strongest possible public confirmation shocks.

If an impact occurs, its owner controls destruction, population loss, wasteland, minerals, presentation, and any terminal result.

A miss can produce a disconfirmation opportunity or a movement claim that the Last Day was delayed.

### Event 033 Acid Rain

Global or regional acid-rain coverage can increase Conviction once per major severity milestone.

Shelters and public-health continuity can provide bounded protection where the Acid Rain design permits it.

Air Cleanliness changes remain owned by the contamination system and Event 033.

### Event 044 Space race

Space observation can create credible data, disputed images, public reassurance, or new astronomical fears.

The event should use actual campaign achievements and avoid inventing a modern global satellite network in an early campaign.

### Other major wars and world threats

Fury, nuclear exchange, cannibalism, caves, strategic singularity, and other existential systems can influence belief through one-shot public milestones.

Event 049 should use a maintained interaction registry or owner callbacks where possible. It should not scan every event flag daily.

## Confirmation-source rules

- Each event family has a bounded confirmation or disconfirmation allowance.
- A threshold milestone can fire once.
- Repeated minor incidents from the same source do not farm Conviction.
- Generic Chaos from the underlying event remains separate.
- Deaths and contamination are not re-added as Event 049 Chaos.
- A source can affect local conviction more strongly in directly affected countries than in the whole world.
- A public false alarm can reduce Conviction after exposure.

## Event 049 Chaos impact map

Evolution state itself always gives zero Chaos.

| Outcome | Event-owned Chaos direction | Guard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First global manifestation | Moderate one-time increase | Once per campaign. |
| First major Conviction threshold | Small one-time increase | Once per threshold family, with global cap. |
| Country movement gains practical national authority | Small increase | Once per country and only for real authority transfer. |
| Major power adopts a Doomsday administration | Larger one-time increase | Once per major country. |
| Major power demobilizes under the crisis | Moderate increase | Once per country and distinct from generic peace. |
| Violent suppression radicalizes a national movement | Small or moderate increase | Only for the separate radicalization consequence, without recounting deaths or condemnation. |
| Revolutionary takeover | Moderate increase | Once per country. |
| Founding of the preparatory international congress | Small increase | Once globally. |
| The Final Vigil commits | Terminal event-owned increase or terminal accounting according to shared rules | Once globally. |
| Prediction fails without overlapping catastrophe | Material one-time decrease | Once globally. |
| Peaceful return of authority | Small decrease | Once per country with cap. |
| Major institutional recovery | Small decrease | One milestone per country with global cap. |
| Exposure of major fraud or false confirmation | Small decrease | Once per source family. |

Generic peace, war, death, contamination, annexation, puppeting, liberation, and nuclear effects remain in their shared systems.

## AI strategy families

### Institutional skeptic

Typical profile:

- High stability.
- Strong education and civil service.
- Low local conviction.
- Defensive or peaceful campaign position.

Preferred posture:

Civic Continuity.

Preferred actions:

- Independent calculation.
- Open archives.
- School and research missions.
- Archive protection.
- Targeted fraud exposure.

Avoids:

- Broad repression without evidence.
- Early demobilization.
- Full government transfer.

### Cooperative guardian

Typical profile:

- Strong trusted religious, civic, labor, or municipal institutions.
- Moderate or high conviction.
- Relief needs or public gathering pressure.

Preferred posture:

Concordat of Vigil.

Preferred actions:

- Joint relief councils.
- Hospitals and shelters.
- Protected observance.
- Movement representation.
- Voluntary custodial transfer if ordinary authority collapses.

### Coercive stabilizer

Typical profile:

- Authoritarian ideology.
- Strong police and army.
- Fear of immediate political capture.
- Moderate institutional continuity.

Preferred posture:

Emergency Order.

Preferred actions:

- Protect strategic institutions.
- Restrict organization.
- Arrest proven violent cells.
- Compel service.

Risks:

- Underestimates hidden backlash.
- Escalates after visible failure.
- Can produce revolutionary takeover.

### National preparer

Typical profile:

- Strong industry.
- Exposure to bombing, disaster, famine, contamination, or isolation.
- Strong survivalist or custodial current.

Preferred posture:

National Preparation.

Preferred actions:

- Shelters.
- Reserves.
- Continuity sites.
- Archive duplication.
- Rail and port missions.

### Pacifist exit government

Typical profile:

- Losing or costly war.
- Low War Support.
- High desertion.
- Strong pacifist movement.
- Enemy has a plausible settlement path.

Preferred posture:

Peace Before the End.

Preferred actions:

- Armistice contact.
- Prisoner release.
- Defensive-only service.
- Arms conversion.
- Final Assembly diplomacy after takeover.

### Final-war regime

Typical profile:

- Highly militarized government.
- Strong state-loyalist current.
- Existential defeat risk.
- Low willingness to surrender.

Preferred posture:

Nothing Left to Lose.

Preferred actions:

- One defined final objective.
- Compelled service.
- Strategic factory protection.
- Final mobilization.

Avoids:

- Early Assembly membership.
- Broad demobilization.

### Communal transition

Typical profile:

- Weak central government.
- Strong municipal and communal organization.
- High local conviction.
- Parallel relief systems.

Preferred outcome:

Municipal cascade and Communal Devolution focus route.

### Revolutionary avenger

Typical profile:

- Severe suppression backlash.
- Real atrocity or disappearance evidence.
- High revolutionary organization.
- Collapsing government and military refusal.

Preferred outcome:

Revolutionary takeover and hidden Avenging Witnesses route.

This AI family must remain rare without the required campaign history.

## AI posture stability

The AI should use posture commitment and emergency-change rules.

A posture change needs a major reason, such as:

- Conviction threshold crossing.
- War reversal.
- Evolution activation.
- Collapse of key institutions.
- Major public confirmation or disconfirmation.
- Government takeover.

AI weights must include strong persistence so countries do not switch every evaluation cycle.

## Balance targets

### Conviction

- The event should normally remain active long enough for the player to experience more than the opening and final day.
- Baseline worlds should not reach the highest Conviction stage automatically.
- High-Chaos worlds with major wars and disasters can reach it without every country converting.
- Strong coordinated continuity can produce a falling global trend.

### Date distribution

- The middle of the two-to-four-year range should be common.
- Dates near either edge should remain possible.
- Pre-fire Evolution II should not generate a date so distant that the advanced crisis loses urgency.

### Repression

- Repression should reduce open organization quickly.
- Repression should not be the dominant long-term strategy in most scenarios.
- Violent repression should create enough backlash, Condemnation, death risk, and post-date cost to matter.
- Targeted lawful action against proven violence or fraud can remain useful.

### Cooperation

- Cooperation should preserve relief and order.
- It should raise movement legitimacy and make peaceful transfer more likely.
- It should not guarantee a harmless outcome.

### Preparation

- Preparation should create real shelter, reserve, transport, archive, and health benefits.
- Its cost should delay other national priorities.
- Corruption and unequal access should be possible.

### Peace

- Losing and exhausted countries should have a credible peace route.
- Aggressive enemies should be able to reject or exploit the offer.
- Defensive survival wars should retain higher public support.

### Militarist route

- The temporary window should be strong enough to change a war.
- It must have a defined objective and serious failure cost.
- It must not become a permanent way to ignore the event.

### Terminal route

- The Final Vigil should be rare in ordinary games because it requires 1000 or more Chaos and broad political proof.
- It should be reachable through several different world histories.
- Releasing many microstates must not make it easy.

## Exploit checks

### Microstate release farming

Released or split countries should not provide full country weight immediately. Terminal and Conviction weighting should cap or discount tiny and newly created states.

### Posture switching

The player cannot switch postures repeatedly to collect opening effects or erase penalties.

### Suppression cycling

Relaxing and reapplying restrictions cannot repeatedly reduce organization or create new Chaos milestones.

### Armistice spam

Failed negotiation attempts need cooldowns and diplomatic consequences. Repeating an offer cannot farm peace effects.

### Shelter and reserve loops

Projects require real capacity, have bounded state coverage, and cannot grant infinite relief resources.

### Arms-conversion loops

Conversion cannot create more equipment or industrial value than the committed lines and resources justify.

### Government-transfer reversal

The player cannot install and remove a Doomsday administration repeatedly for Chaos, focus, idea, or membership rewards.

### Assembly membership cycling

Membership, observer status, expulsion, and holdout states need persistence and reentry rules.

### Revised-date repetition

The hardliner aftermath branch can occur only once and cannot restart the global countdown.

### Branch toggle abuse

Disabling The Final Vigil before readiness and enabling it after readiness should require the normal readiness evaluation and cannot fire two terminal paths.

## Edge cases

### Event fires with no major wars

The event focuses on education, investment, preparation, movement politics, and future abandonment. Peace decisions remain hidden.

### Event fires during global war

Peace pressure, mobilization, casualties, rail allocation, and major-power choices receive higher priority.

### Predicted date is near because of pre-fire evolution

The opening uses accelerated phases and bypasses missions that cannot finish in time.

### A country is annexed

Its local state is removed or transferred only where an accepted successor rule exists. Its previous contribution cannot remain as a ghost weight.

### A country becomes nonhuman

Ordinary civilian decisions close. Any interaction moves to the owner event’s rules.

### A country is released after Event 49 fires

It receives initialized local state based on territory, institutions, war, parent country history, and global Conviction. It should not default to zero or maximum belief.

### Civil war begins during the event

Each side receives valid local state derived from the parent, ideology, territory, movement networks, and military split. The original country does not retain double weight.

### A government in exile exists

The exile and occupied homeland can have different organization and conviction. The system should avoid two full country contributions for the same population.

### The player switches country

The global date remains fixed. The decision category rebuilds using the new country’s state.

### Another world end commits

The Final Vigil becomes unavailable. Event 049 can stop, freeze, or become flavor under the terminal owner’s rules.

### Evolution is disabled during an active campaign

Already applied evolution state should follow the project’s evolution-toggle contract. The implementation must define whether disabling prevents future progression or also closes current gated content. It cannot leave broken missions or hidden terminal readiness.

### Final Assembly has very few eligible countries

The terminal route should require stronger major-power or global Conviction proof. It cannot form from one or two isolated states.

### All major powers are gone

The route can use broad country share and geographic spread without an impossible major-power requirement.

## Performance direction

- Avoid new daily whole-world iteration.
- Use bounded registered country sets and owner-local processing.
- Use event-owned scheduled pulses for global aggregation.
- Use callbacks or maintained source flags for cross-event confirmation.
- Avoid rebuilding all war pressure for every war every day.
- Clean invalid country, war, state, and movement targets promptly.

## Multiplayer direction

- The date and global Conviction are shared.
- Each player manages the posture and decisions of the current country.
- Player countries should not be silently forced into a government-transfer choice through AI logic.
- Global super-events and evolution logs fire once.
- Final Assembly membership remains country-specific until terminal commitment rules resolve the world.
- Tag switching must not duplicate decisions or contributions.

## Completion evidence

Implementation needs evidence for:

- Conviction aggregation and caps.
- Date distribution.
- Evolution timing.
- AI posture stability.
- Peace acceptance ordering.
- Repression short-term and long-term outcomes.
- Government takeover validity.
- Final Vigil path readiness.
- No duplicate Chaos, Deaths, contamination, famine, or migration transactions.
- Save and reload persistence.
- Invalid target cleanup.
