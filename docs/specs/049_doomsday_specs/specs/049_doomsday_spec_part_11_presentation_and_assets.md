# Event 049 Doomsday Specification

## Part 11: Presentation, Writing, and Asset Direction

## Presentation purpose

The event should show a world reorganizing daily life around an expected ending.

The strongest images are crowds, calendars, empty schools, abandoned yards, full stations, relief kitchens, shelters, public vigils, prisoners, soldiers refusing deployment, families spending savings, and local councils taking over practical work.

The event should not center on one mad scientist, one laboratory, one telescope, or one government briefing.

## Player-facing writing direction

Final localisation should be written during implementation from the directions in this package.

The planning package does not provide paste-ready event descriptions, option text, focus text, achievement text, quotes, cultural remarks, or audio selections.

### Viewpoint

Use the viewpoint that best fits each surface:

- Global super-events should describe public convergence and world-scale behavior.
- Country events should focus on named institutions, states, cities, military units, communities, and government choices.
- Report events should show one concrete incident.
- News events should show international milestones such as the Last Calendar or Final Assembly congress.
- Decisions and missions should state the public action, real requirement, and visible consequence.
- Focus text should explain the administration’s chosen purpose and institutional change.

### Information that remains uncertain

- Whether the world will actually end.
- The true cause of the convergence.
- Whether independent prophecies are connected.
- Whether a real campaign disaster is part of the prediction or coincidence.
- Whether a failed date means error, mercy, prevention, or symbolic fulfillment.

### Information that must be clear

- The predicted date.
- Current Doomsday Conviction.
- The country’s posture.
- The public purpose of each decision and mission.
- The immediate tradeoff of demobilization, repression, cooperation, preparation, or final war.
- The political meaning of a Doomsday administration.
- The Final Assembly’s public purpose.

### Tone

The tone should be serious, socially observant, and concrete.

Humor can appear in minor fraud, household spending, bureaucratic confusion, commercial remedies, or public absurdity. It should not turn mass persecution, war deaths, famine, imprisonment, or social collapse into a joke.

Irony should come from behavior and consequence. It should not rely on generic dramatic formulas.

### Language to avoid

Avoid:

- A single expert explaining the whole event.
- Claims that every religion agrees on one doctrine.
- Generic lines about darkness, madness, or humanity losing hope.
- Repeated contrast formulas between public reports and official denial.
- Text that labels an incident as a warning instead of showing what happened.
- Technical references to hidden variables, caps, event weights, or implementation history.
- Final localised names copied from working labels without review.

## Option and reaction direction

### Opening reaction

The opening option should express the government’s need to respond to a public fact without claiming certainty.

The tone can vary by country and ideology, including controlled skepticism, frightened restraint, official piety, grim bureaucracy, military impatience, or political opportunism.

### Reassurance options

These should sound credible only when the country retains trusted institutions. An unstable regime should not use the same confident wording as a strong civil service.

### Suppression options

The wording should reveal the state’s public justification and the scope of action. It should not celebrate persecution or hide visible harm from the player.

### Pacifist options

The tone should distinguish moral refusal, exhaustion, family pressure, strategic withdrawal, religious objection, and organized nonviolent resistance.

### Final-war options

The tone should be state-loyalist, militarized, and aware of the remaining time. The text should make the public cost visible.

### Failed-date options

The tone should vary among relief, anger, shame, vindication, institutional duty, reinterpretation, and refusal to restore the old order.

## Event family

### Global entry event

Purpose:

- Introduce the converging predictions.
- Establish the date.
- Apply the opening world shock.
- Launch the immediate super-event.
- Open country response events.

Image direction:

A mass urban or regional gathering around public calendars, printed sheets, prayer, or political assembly. The composition should include multiple social types and no readable generated text.

### First country response event

Purpose:

- Present the country’s starting local pressure.
- Explain the two public values.
- Offer posture choices.

Image direction:

A government and public institution under visible social pressure, such as a crowded ministry entrance, recruitment office, station, or public hall.

### Movement emergence reports

Purpose:

Show the first dominant and secondary currents in concrete action.

Recommended report images:

- Pacifist march at a recruitment office.
- Communal kitchen and shelter.
- Religious and civic vigil.
- Remote survival settlement.
- University departure.
- Shipyard or factory abandonment.
- Police raid and seized meeting place.
- Soldiers refusing an offensive order.

### The Last Calendar news event

Purpose:

Announce that independent calculations have converged on the exact date.

Image direction:

Newspapers, public wall calendars, station boards, church or civic notices, and crowds. Generated text must remain unreadable or abstract.

### Evolution II reports

Purpose:

Show parallel government through food lines, municipal councils, military refusal, factory conversion, prisoner release, and national authority transfer.

### Final-month reports

Purpose:

Show public vigils, final trains, staffed hospitals, locked archives, prisoners, shelters, and military standing orders.

### Failed-date global event

Purpose:

Show ordinary dawn, exhausted crowds, reopened doors, and uncertainty about what comes next.

This should be a global news or major aftermath event, not a super-event by default.

### Reconstruction reports

Purpose:

Show schools reopening, workers returning, soldiers registering, archives being restored, shelters converted, and settlement negotiations.

## Super-event package

Event 049 needs two super-events.

### Super-event A: Initial Major event reveal

Role:

First global revelation of the converging date and mass social reaction.

Title direction:

Short and specific to the prediction becoming a world fact. The final title requires research and review.

Description direction:

Explain that unrelated systems point toward one period, public behavior has changed immediately, and no one can prove what will happen.

Image direction:

A broad mass gathering or several layers of public preparation. Avoid a scientist at a desk, a single prophet, a visible comet as confirmed cause, modern objects, and readable text.

Quote direction:

Research historical, religious, literary, or philosophical material about prophecy, time, final judgment, or the social power of expectation. Do not invent a quote.

Audio direction:

Research a licensed structured musical recording with a restrained, expectant, public, or ritual character. Pure drones and sound effects are not acceptable.

### Super-event B: The Final Vigil

Role:

Public world-end commitment after conventional politics yields to Doomsday administrations and the Final Assembly.

Title direction:

The accepted route name is The Final Vigil. The implementation should confirm whether the super-event title uses this exact name or a sourced variant that remains aligned with Event Details.

Description direction:

Describe mass demobilization, transfer of government purpose, and the Final Assembly’s focus on food, shelter, peace, and order. Make clear through content that Earth has not visibly been destroyed.

Image direction:

A huge public assembly, surrendered weapons, extinguished military activity, relief distribution, and a shared vigil. Avoid an exploding planet, supernatural beam, or generic map.

Quote direction:

Research public-domain or reliably sourced material about watchfulness, final judgment, peace, time, or the end of worldly ambition.

Audio direction:

Research a unique licensed musical recording with finality, public ritual, and human scale. The track must be separate from the opening super-event unless the user explicitly approves reuse.

## Category picture variants

The decision category uses stage-specific static pictures.

### Variant 1: Convergence

Scene:

A public hall, street, market, or station where people compare calendars and notices.

### Variant 2: Organized societies

Scene:

Relief kitchens, marches, settlements, and abandoned workplaces visible in one coherent composition.

### Variant 3: The Last Calendar

Scene:

A city visibly organized around the countdown, with mass public notices and altered schedules. Text remains unreadable.

### Variant 4: Parallel government or final month

Scene:

Local councils, shelters, prisoners, soldiers, and public vigils sharing the same civic space.

### Variant 5: Failed-date dawn

Scene:

Crowds dispersing, doors reopening, workers and officials returning, and unused preparations remaining.

The asset family should use the canonical decision-category picture reference set and its native consumer size after inspection, with no painted fake buttons, meters, or controls.

The Assembly needs a flat emblem for Event Details, decisions, focus content, and related UI where supported.

## Institutional council portraits

A small people-free portrait family is planned for revolutionary or institutional administrations that cannot retain an existing leader and do not have an approved grounded individual.

Working portrait roles:

- Civic relief council.
- Religious and civic synod.
- Communal delegates’ council.
- Defensive service committee.
- Revolutionary witness assembly.

These are fictional institutional compositions, not substitutes for a real person. They should use institutional names and people-free or clearly collective framing.

If implementation chooses a specific grounded individual for a country, the portrait must use attributed source material through the portrait workflow.

## Flags and country identity art

The event does not require a new national flag set for every Doomsday administration.

Existing national flags should remain unless a specific focus or administration route creates an accepted cosmetic identity that clearly needs a flag. Any such later flag requires its own researched or generated flat-design workflow.

The Final Assembly emblem is separate from national flags.

## Animation decision

No required animation is planned for the category picture, icons, portraits, or super-events.

Static stage variants communicate the mechanic more clearly and reduce production overhead.

An implementation asset review may propose one bounded animated category picture or emblem only when it adds clear state communication. Such a proposal must include a static fallback and follow the frame-animation skill, while the accepted design remains complete with static variants.

## 3D decision

The event introduces no custom unit, building entity, vehicle, aircraft, ship, creature, or other 3D surface.

No Meshy or Blender package is required for the accepted specification.

## Source modes

- Fictional period scenes use generated documentary-style art.
- Real historical photographs or symbols require sourced archival material and provenance.
- Grounded individual portraits require attributed real source material.
- Fictional institutional council portraits can use native ImageGen through the portrait workflow.
- Icons and emblems use native ImageGen with transparency where the consumer requires it.
- Flags, if later authorized, use flat ImageGen designs after source research where historically grounded.

## Period and quality rules

Generated scenes should reflect 1936 to 1945 photographic technology, clothing, transport, architecture, public institutions, military equipment, and print culture unless the campaign’s actual technology or alternate history clearly supports another element.

Avoid:

- Modern tactical equipment.
- Modern digital displays.
- Readable generated text.
- Cinematic science-fiction lighting.
- Generic laboratory scenes.
- One prophet shown as the sole cause.
- Waving flag scenes for flat emblems.
- Primitive local placeholder icons.
- Opaque square backgrounds where transparency is required.

## Dynamic localisation needs

Final text should be able to mention:

- The predicted date.
- Remaining time.
- Current Conviction stage and trend.
- Country name and government posture.
- Named states, cities, ports, rail hubs, archives, schools, and industrial sites.
- Current war and enemy where relevant.
- Dominant society current through public qualitative language.
- Final Assembly status and dominant current.
- Takeover route.
- Failed-date recovery state.

The implementation should avoid raw variable names and generic text that could leak another country’s institutions.

## Event Details direction

The Event Details entry should explain the global premise and the two public values.

Evolution rows should describe The Last Calendar and Nothing After Tomorrow without revealing hidden trigger weights.

The Final Vigil row should explain the political terminal state without listing raw readiness conditions.

The catalog and Event Details wording should match final in-game language after implementation.

## Audio and quote research boundary

No final quote, lyric, cultural remark, or audio track is accepted by this planning package.

The super-event prompt requires source research, attribution, licensing, separate composition and recording rights review, final WAV conversion, unique sound IDs, settings-aware playback, and documentation.

Unresearched final super-event text or audio is a blocker.
