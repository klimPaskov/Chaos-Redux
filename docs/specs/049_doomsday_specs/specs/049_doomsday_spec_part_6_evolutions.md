# Event 049 Doomsday Specification

## Part 6: Evolutions

## Evolution structure

Event 049 has two evolutions.

- Evolution I: The Last Calendar
- Evolution II: Nothing After Tomorrow

The ordinary movement from convergence to organization, final-year pressure, final month, and failed-date aftermath is baseline progression. It must not be logged as a chain of evolutions.

Evolution activation gives zero Chaos. Chaos changes come from concrete outcomes caused by the evolved behavior.

## Shared pacing rule

When the event has already fired, an eligible evolution should normally occur through paced event logic around a base of roughly 90 days, adjusted by campaign conditions.

The evolution should not activate the instant the global Chaos threshold is crossed.

Faster factors can include:

- High global Conviction.
- A short remaining countdown.
- Many countries adopting Last Days policies.
- Several major confirmation shocks.
- Major-power demobilization.
- Widespread government capture.
- Strong international movement coordination.

Slower factors can include:

- Falling Conviction.
- Strong institutional continuity across major powers.
- Successful public disconfirmation.
- Disabled evolution state.
- A recent major movement scandal.
- The predicted date being very close, when a new evolution would not have enough time to function cleanly.

When an evolution is eligible before Event 49 fires, it changes the opening package immediately. This is a pre-fire evolved opening, not an evolution that waits 90 days after the first popup.

## Evolution enable and disable behavior

Each evolution needs its own persistent Event Details enable state.

If Evolution I is disabled:

- The baseline event still progresses to the date.
- The date remains the leading predicted date, but it does not receive the full Last Calendar convergence package.
- Last Days policies tied specifically to Evolution I remain unavailable.
- Baseline preparation, peace, suppression, cooperation, and continuity actions remain playable.

If Evolution II is disabled:

- Doomsday Societies can remain powerful pressure groups.
- Baseline local government crises and political incidents can occur.
- National Doomsday administration replacement, the shared emergency focus branch, and The Final Vigil terminal route remain unavailable.
- The event still reaches the failed-date aftermath.

Disabled evolutions must not set recorded flags, unlock later content, or count toward terminal readiness.

## Evolution I: The Last Calendar

### Chaos requirement

The Last Calendar becomes eligible at 800 or more Chaos.

### Evolution promise

The world stops arguing about the general period and begins organizing around one exact date.

The date becomes a social fact. Calendars, schedules, contracts, school terms, military plans, religious observances, debt maturities, construction programs, and political campaigns are reorganized around it.

The event should show convergence through independent public behavior. It should not reveal one hidden organization coordinating every society.

### Pre-fire evolved opening

If The Last Calendar is active or selected by the pre-fire opening logic before Event 49 fires:

- Global opening Conviction begins around 45 to 60, adjusted by world state.
- The predicted date is presented as the accepted Last Day from the first super-event.
- Society organization begins higher.
- The first country response includes Last Days policy access.
- Pacifist, devotional, communal, and prophetic currents begin with stronger international links.
- Long-term construction and education pressure begins at the stronger evolution level.
- The first month includes a convergence news event and country-specific calendar consequences.

The opening still gives the player time to respond. It must not jump directly into national government capture.

### Trigger direction after firing

The Last Calendar becomes more likely when:

- Global Conviction is at least in the Mass Conviction range.
- Several independent regions have high local conviction.
- A major power publicly accepts the date.
- Religious, pacifist, scientific, and folk interpretations all produce similar calculations.
- The countdown remains long enough for the evolution to shape play.
- Recent confirmation shocks have not already been spent as evolution proof.

A low-Conviction world should not evolve only because Chaos reached 800 through unrelated mechanics.

### Immediate evolution consequences

- Global Conviction rises by a bounded one-time amount because public uncertainty narrows.
- Local conviction gains stronger global cross-feed.
- Society organization rises in countries where calendar-centered or devotional networks are already present.
- The exact date becomes central in category text, news, reports, and decision timing.
- Recruitment, investment, research, and long-term construction penalties scale more strongly with remaining time.
- Pacifist peace pressure grows faster.
- Last Days policies become available.
- The evolution is recorded once through the shared evolution log.

The one-time Conviction change affects belief and adds no Chaos. The evolution state itself also adds no Chaos.

### Last Days policy families

#### Wind down military programs

Countries can cancel or suspend long military projects, release personnel, and convert production.

The player chooses priorities. The event should not delete projects or equipment indiscriminately.

#### Cancel long projects

Governments can preserve immediate infrastructure and abandon prestige or distant projects.

#### Release prisoners

Countries can release conscientious objectors, political prisoners, debt prisoners, selected civilian prisoners, or broad categories according to laws and posture.

Release improves peace and movement relations. It can create public-order and political backlash if executed without preparation.

#### Open borders

Countries can permit pilgrimage, family reunion, sanctuary, or settlement movement.

The Migration system owns population movement and reception.

#### Forgive or suspend debts

Debt policy becomes a central political choice. Relief can prevent social collapse while damaging finance and post-date recovery.

#### Distribute reserves

Countries can use stored resources early, preserve them for the final month, or delegate distribution to trusted councils.

#### Prepare shelters and continuity sites

Preparation becomes nationally legitimate and more expensive as demand rises.

#### Organize final observances

Governments can support public vigils, religious services, secular assemblies, remembrance, or national days of reconciliation.

#### Recognize movement representation

Society delegates can enter advisory councils, parliaments, municipal boards, military committees, or relief administrations.

This is the main peaceful bridge to Evolution II government transfer.

### Social effects

The Last Calendar changes ordinary behavior.

- Schools rewrite terms around the final date.
- Companies stop issuing long contracts.
- Couples, families, and communities accelerate life decisions.
- Courts face debt, inheritance, and prisoner petitions.
- Armies plan final rotations and service limits.
- Religious calendars add vigils and pilgrimages.
- Newspapers publish daily countdowns.
- Markets price delivery before the date differently from delivery after it.
- Political parties lose members to Last Days organizations.
- Governments face demands to disclose every emergency plan.

### Incident families

Recommended Last Calendar incidents include:

- Several independent institutions publish nearly identical calculations.
- A national newspaper prints the countdown on every edition.
- A university closes programs that would graduate after the date.
- A military class refuses commissions beyond the Last Day.
- A city council replaces its budget with a final-years plan.
- A mass pilgrimage overwhelms rail capacity.
- Banks refuse mortgages that mature after the date.
- A movement exposes false shelters or elite escape plans.
- A religious and civic council offers to keep hospitals open.
- A state bans public calendars and creates a larger underground market.

### Government posture changes

The evolution strengthens each posture differently.

Civic Continuity gains stronger public evidence and continuity missions, with higher penalties for failure.

Concordat of Vigil gains formal councils, observance, and joint relief.

Emergency Order gains calendar censorship, protected-industry missions, and stronger backlash.

National Preparation gains larger shelter, reserve, archive, and continuity-site programs.

Peace Before the End gains prisoner, truce, defensive-service, and arms-conversion actions.

Nothing Left to Lose gains final-war planning and state-loyalist organization.

### AI direction

The AI should use the evolution as a posture stress test.

- Stable governments can remain in Civic Continuity if their missions are succeeding.
- High-conviction states with strong trusted institutions should prefer Concordat or National Preparation.
- Losing belligerents should consider Peace Before the End.
- Highly militarized regimes should resist demobilization and may enter Nothing Left to Lose.
- Weak authoritarian governments may choose Emergency Order and later face backlash.

### Evolution I failure and containment

The Last Calendar does not guarantee rising Conviction forever.

A coordinated public review can expose contradictory methods. Movement scandals can split the calendar coalition. Strong institutions can keep long projects alive. A war can end and remove a major confirmation source.

Evolution I remains recorded even if Conviction later falls. Its policy and social consequences can weaken or close according to current conditions.

## Evolution II: Nothing After Tomorrow

### Chaos requirement

Nothing After Tomorrow becomes eligible at 1000 or more Chaos.

### Evolution promise

Doomsday Societies stop asking governments to prepare and begin replacing the functions of government.

Food distribution, shelters, local justice, military refusal, debt settlement, public order, records, and transport move into parallel institutions. Ordinary parties and ministries lose people, information, and practical authority.

### Pre-fire evolved opening

If Evolution II is active or selected by the pre-fire opening logic before Event 49 fires:

- Global opening Conviction begins around 60 to 75, adjusted by world state.
- The Last Calendar is treated as active if its evolution is enabled.
- Society organization begins high in several regions.
- Selected countries begin with local parallel administrations.
- National government-capture paths are available after a short opening stabilization period.
- The Final Vigil remains locked until its separate readiness path succeeds.
- The opening super-event presents an advanced global movement without declaring that ordinary geopolitics has already ended.

### Trigger direction after firing

Evolution II becomes more likely when:

- Global Conviction is high or rising sharply.
- The final year has begun.
- Several countries have recognized movement representation.
- Local councils control food, shelters, or public order.
- Military refusal has become coordinated.
- Ordinary parties, schools, factories, or ministries are losing staff.
- Suppression backlash is producing revolutionary organization.
- At least one major power faces government-transfer pressure.

Chaos at 1000 is necessary but not sufficient for an immediate evolution. The social system must show that movements can govern.

### Immediate evolution consequences

- Parallel administration incidents begin in countries with enough organization.
- Government-capture decisions and missions unlock.
- The shared Doomsday administration focus branch becomes available after national takeover.
- Final Assembly diplomacy begins.
- Terminal readiness paths start accumulating proof.
- The evolution is recorded once through the shared evolution log.

Evolution activation adds no Chaos.

### Parallel administration sectors

#### Food and shelter

Societies run kitchens, storage, shelters, farms, clinics, and distribution.

#### Local authority

Municipal councils, village committees, congregational boards, labor councils, and relief committees issue orders and settle disputes.

#### Military refusal

Soldiers, officers, transport workers, and families coordinate refusal of offensive deployment.

#### Records and debt

Movement councils seize, preserve, forgive, or destroy records of debt, property, prisoners, and service.

#### Public order

Society patrols, volunteer wardens, communal guards, religious stewards, or revolutionary groups replace absent police.

#### International coordination

Delegates travel to common congresses and prepare the Final Assembly.

## National takeover routes

### Voluntary custodial transfer

The existing government invites society delegates into a narrow administration for food, shelter, peace, records, and order.

This route preserves the most institutional continuity.

### Constitutional transfer

A parliament, monarch, council, or constitutional body replaces the ordinary cabinet with a Last Days administration.

This route preserves legal continuity and can survive the failed date as a reformed political system.

### Municipal cascade

Local governments transfer enough practical authority that the national government follows or becomes irrelevant.

This route strengthens Communal Stewards.

### Military refusal transfer

The armed forces withdraw support from offensive command and recognize a civil-protection administration.

This route preserves some defensive capacity and creates a difficult post-date command settlement.

### Revolutionary collapse

Repeated suppression, hunger, defeat, elite flight, and high organization produce a national uprising.

This route strengthens Avenging Witnesses and creates the highest risk of violence, counterrevolution, and archive destruction.

## Existing-government survival route

A country can resist takeover through functioning institutions, credible public authority, successful missions, a defensive war, or a strong skeptical continuity network.

Survival should require active proof. Emergency Order alone cannot guarantee success if the state has hollowed out.

A surviving government can still join limited Final Assembly relief arrangements without surrendering full authority, but it cannot count as a Doomsday administration for terminal readiness unless it makes the political transfer.

## Shared focus branch unlock

After a national takeover, the country gains access to the shared Doomsday administration emergency focus branch defined in Part 7.

The branch does not replace the entire national tree. It provides a visible political and administrative route for the remaining time.

## Final Assembly preparation

Evolution II creates an international preparatory congress.

Countries and movement networks can:

- Send observers.
- Seek recognition.
- Pool relief and transport.
- Negotiate demobilization standards.
- Establish common prisoner and border policies.
- Debate the Assembly’s dominant current.
- Apply for full membership after national authority transfer.

The preparatory congress can fail, fragment, or remain a limited relief organization until The Final Vigil activates and establishes the terminal Final Assembly.

## Evolution II incidents

Recommended incidents include:

- A city recognizes a Last Days council over the national ministry.
- Soldiers establish a defensive-only command.
- Parliament loses its quorum to society delegates.
- A religious and civic relief board takes over food distribution.
- Factory councils refuse military orders and convert production.
- A government destroys records before evacuation.
- A suppression raid triggers a national funeral and strike.
- A major power announces phased demobilization.
- A neutral country offers to host the preparatory congress.
- A revolutionary council seizes prisons and archives.
- A survivalist administration refuses outside refugees.
- A communal network opens borders and overwhelms reception.

## AI direction

AI takeover willingness should consider:

- Local conviction and organization.
- Institutional continuity.
- Stability and government legitimacy.
- War direction and military refusal.
- Dominant movement current.
- Posture history.
- Suppression backlash.
- Major-power and neighbor examples.
- Remaining time.
- Whether the evolution and terminal branch are enabled.

A strong functioning state should not surrender authority only because Conviction is high. A hollowed-out state with organized relief councils should be much more vulnerable.

## Evolution II and the Last Day

If the date arrives before The Final Vigil activates, every Doomsday administration enters the failed-date aftermath.

Some administrations dissolve and restore the previous constitution. Some survive as reformed communal, religious, pacifist, or custodial governments. Revolutionary administrations may face counterrevolution or consolidate a permanent regime.

Evolution II does not protect its governments from disconfirmation.

## Evolution log presentation

The evolution log should show:

- Event 049 as the source event.
- The correct evolution name.
- The Chaos tier at activation.
- The activation date.
- The actor only when the evolution is represented through a country-specific milestone. The global activation itself can remain actorless.
- One record per evolution.

The Event Details evolution catalog should explain the premise and gameplay direction. It should not show fake log dates or sequence numbers.
