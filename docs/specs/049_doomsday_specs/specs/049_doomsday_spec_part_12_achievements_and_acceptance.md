# Event 049 Doomsday Specification

## Part 12: Achievements and Acceptance Criteria

## Achievement purpose

The achievement set should reward distinct ways of managing the countdown, not passive survival after Event 49 happens to fire.

Each achievement needs explicit eligibility, tracking, disqualifiers, difficulty, icon art, localisation, and documentation.

The titles below are working labels, not final localisation. Final wording should be reviewed for tone and fit.

## Achievement 1: A Future Worth Planning

### Route

Civic Continuity.

### Eligible country

Any ordinary player-controlled country affected by Event 049.

### Unlock conditions

- Choose Civic Continuity as the first posture or through the one valid emergency realignment.
- Complete major education, archive, transport, and public-evidence missions.
- Preserve high institutional continuity until the predicted date.
- Do not install a Doomsday administration.
- Reach the failed date without a severe national movement takeover.
- Resume at least one long construction or research program after the date.

### Disqualifiers

- Violent mass suppression.
- Full demobilization that destroys military continuity.
- Joining The Final Vigil.
- Failure of the required public promises.

### Difficulty

Hard.

### Why it is not trivial

The player must keep expensive long-horizon institutions active while global pressure grows.

### Icon direction

A lit drafting table, school or blueprint extending past a torn calendar, designed as a `64x64` achievement icon.

## Achievement 2: The Last Recruit

### Route

Military continuity without broad repression.

### Eligible country

Any player country at war or maintaining a substantial armed force.

### Unlock conditions

- Preserve the muster rolls and military depots through the final year.
- Maintain a valid defensive force until the date.
- Use conscientious or defensive service for at least one part of the military.
- Avoid the Nothing Left to Lose final-offensive route.
- Reconstitute the armed forces after the failed date without mass punishment of objectors or deserters.

### Disqualifiers

- Chaotic demobilization.
- Destruction of military records.
- Final-war mobilization.
- Event-owned atrocity during suppression.

### Difficulty

Medium to hard.

### Icon direction

A military registration book beside a grounded rifle and civil-defense armband, with no readable text.

## Achievement 3: No One Dies for Tomorrow

### Route

Pacifist settlement.

### Eligible country

A player-controlled belligerent.

### Unlock conditions

- Choose Peace Before the End or form a pacifist Doomsday administration.
- Secure an armistice or full peace in every active war before the final month.
- Release conscientious objectors or establish conscientious service.
- Avoid starting a new offensive war after Event 049 fires.
- Avoid member-to-member war if the country joins the Final Assembly.

### Disqualifiers

- Nothing Left to Lose posture.
- Breaking a Last Day truce.
- Starting a new offensive war.

### Difficulty

Hard in a major war.

### Why it is not trivial

The enemy must accept or be brought to a valid settlement, and the player must survive the military weakness created by demobilization.

### Icon direction

A line of stacked rifles under a public vigil light, with civilian hands distributing food nearby.

## Achievement 4: The Calendar Was Wrong

### Route

Failed-date recovery.

### Eligible country

Any player country that reaches the date without The Final Vigil.

### Unlock conditions

- Experience The Last Calendar.
- Reach the predicted date.
- Complete all required national recovery phases.
- Close the acute Doomsday category.
- End with functioning education, transport, finance, and government authority.
- Prevent the revised-date hardliner branch from gaining national power.

### Disqualifiers

- The Final Vigil commits.
- The country remains in unresolved government collapse.

### Difficulty

Medium to hard, depending on prior choices.

### Icon direction

A broken calendar page in morning light beside an open school or factory gate.

## Achievement 5: The Final Assembly

### Route

Terminal world-end leadership.

### Eligible country

A player-controlled Doomsday administration.

### Unlock conditions

- Help commit The Final Vigil through a valid readiness path.
- Become a full Final Assembly member.
- Complete the Assembly diplomacy branch.
- Provide at least one real relief, transport, demobilization, records, or shelter obligation.
- Hold meaningful influence in the dominant Assembly current.

### Disqualifiers

- Observer-only status.
- Joining after the terminal commitment without prior contribution.
- Exploitative microstate release contribution.

### Difficulty

Very hard because the world must reach 1000 or more Chaos and satisfy terminal proof.

### Icon direction

The Final Assembly emblem above a crowded hall with food, shelter, and surrendered weapons represented symbolically.

## Achievement 6: No Future, No Masters

### Route

Communal devolution.

### Eligible country

A player-controlled Doomsday administration formed through municipal cascade, voluntary transfer, or revolutionary collapse.

### Unlock conditions

- Complete the Communal Devolution focus route.
- Keep local relief functioning in all selected priority regions.
- Avoid creating a centralized custodial state.
- Prevent exclusionary hoarding or armed settlement domination.
- Reach the date or Final Vigil with stable communal institutions.

### Disqualifiers

- Custodial Administration route.
- Central military dictatorship.
- Severe famine caused by deliberate local exclusion.

### Difficulty

Hard.

### Icon direction

Several local hands holding keys, bread, and a small shelter beneath a shared horizon.

## Achievement 7: Prepare for Everything

### Route

National Preparation.

### Eligible country

Any ordinary player country.

### Unlock conditions

- Complete meaningful shelter, reserve, archive, hospital, and transport preparation.
- Use at least one preparation asset successfully against another real crisis, such as disaster, bombing, famine, outbreak, contamination, or displacement.
- Reach the failed date.
- Convert the preparation network into useful post-date institutions.

### Disqualifiers

- Shelters remain elite-only after exposure.
- National reserves collapse through corruption or hoarding.
- The country joins The Final Vigil before the cross-system protection proof is achieved.

### Difficulty

Hard because it depends on a real overlapping crisis and responsible preparation.

### Icon direction

A shelter cutaway with medicine, seed, records, and transport tools, designed as one readable symbol.

## Achievement 8: The State Outlived the Prophecy

### Route

Authoritarian or coercive survival with lawful restraint.

### Eligible country

A player country using Emergency Order for a substantial period.

### Unlock conditions

- Prevent national government capture.
- Preserve high institutional continuity.
- Reach the failed date.
- Avoid event-owned mass death, disappearance, destroyed records, and severe Condemnation.
- Conduct a post-date legal review and release or compensate wrongfully detained people.

### Disqualifiers

- Revolutionary takeover.
- Severe suppression backlash at the date.
- Concealed atrocity or destroyed evidence.

### Difficulty

Very hard because the player must use coercive tools without allowing the route to become indiscriminate repression.

### Icon direction

An intact government archive and courthouse beyond a discarded calendar, with restrained security imagery.

## Achievement 9: Still Waiting

### Status

Hidden.

### Route

Revised-date hardliner aftermath.

### Eligible country

A player country where the bounded revised-date branch appears.

### Unlock conditions

- Allow the revised-date hardliner movement to survive as a legal local minority.
- Prevent it from taking national power or restarting a global crisis.
- Reach a later defined observation date with stable institutions and no violent suppression.

### Disqualifiers

- National takeover by the revised-date movement.
- Mass repression.
- The branch is created through debug or forced repetition.

### Difficulty

Rare and medium in direct play.

### Icon direction

A small group beside an outdated calendar under a distant empty sky.

## Achievement 10: Every Bell at Midnight

### Route

Plural observance and public order.

### Eligible country

A player country with several significant religious, civic, regional, or cultural institutions.

### Unlock conditions

- Complete the plural observance route.
- Protect final vigils in all selected regions.
- Keep hospitals, communications, and relief routes open through the final night.
- Avoid lethal crowd suppression.
- Reach dawn with no major public-order collapse.

### Disqualifiers

- One exclusive national observance imposed through coercion.
- Relief routes closed for spectacle.
- Mass casualties during the final night.

### Difficulty

Hard.

### Icon direction

Several distinct bell, siren, lamp, or civic symbols arranged around one horizon, with no readable text and no appropriation of one sacred emblem as universal.

## Achievement tracking rules

- Every achievement needs a stable identifier and one-time unlock guard.
- Forced debug setup and Force Trigger Mode should disqualify achievements according to existing project rules where applicable.
- Conditions must use actual event state, mission results, government route, war results, and institutional outcomes.
- A focus completion alone should not unlock a route achievement when the promised outcome has not been achieved.
- Hidden achievements remain hidden until unlock or until the established achievement UI pattern reveals them.
- Tracking must persist through save and reload.
- Player tag switching should follow the existing achievement ownership rule and cannot duplicate credit.
- The achievement icons require completed, grey, and not-eligible states if the project achievement surface uses the full triplet.

## Event-level acceptance criteria

### Identity and registration

- Event 049 is registered as a Major event.
- Event 049 requires Chaos level 4 for normal automatic firing.
- The event replaces the Mass Panic identity across event name mapping, Event Details, docs, and the authoritative workbook.
- The event has no cluster membership.
- The current status remains To Be Reworked until implementation and audits are complete.

### Opening

- The entry event creates one persistent predicted date two to four years in the future.
- The opening applies a country-scaled global shock.
- The immediate Major super-event uses final researched text, art, quote, and unique audio.
- Every eligible ordinary human country receives a valid response surface.
- Actual nonhuman countries do not receive ordinary civilian response decisions.

### Public values

- Only Doomsday Conviction and Time Until the End are persistent public values.
- All other simulation state remains hidden or qualitative.
- The Conviction display shows stage, trend, next threshold, and actionable causes without a full component ledger.
- The countdown persists and displays correctly through save, reload, and tag switch.

### Country response

- Every eligible country can select a valid posture.
- Posture changes are bounded and costly.
- Decisions use varied real costs with no more than four spendable cost types.
- A normal phase shows no more than six primary actions and three active missions.
- Obsolete actions disappear or are replaced.
- All targeted decisions clean invalid country, war, and state targets.

### War

- Peace pressure is contextual and linked to each war and belligerent.
- No automatic global white peace occurs.
- Defensive survival wars retain stronger continued-war logic.
- Pacifist countries can negotiate real armistice and prisoner outcomes.
- Defensive-only service works as a distinct route.
- Nothing Left to Lose has a defined objective, duration, and aftermath.

### Economy and institutions

- Construction, research, education, recruitment, finance, and labor effects scale with Conviction, time, local state, and posture.
- Playability floors prevent unavoidable pre-terminal zero output.
- Shelters, reserves, records, hospitals, and transport can provide real cross-system benefits.
- Famine and Migration retain their own values and ledgers.
- Institutional continuity shapes government capture and failed-date recovery.

### Evolutions

- The Last Calendar requires 800 or more Chaos and uses paced activation after firing.
- Nothing After Tomorrow requires 1000 or more Chaos and uses paced activation after firing.
- Pre-fire evolved openings apply the correct stronger opening package.
- Disabled evolutions do not record, unlock, or gate baseline progression incorrectly.
- Evolution state itself gives zero Chaos.
- Evolution log rows show correct identity, date, tier, and actor behavior.

### Focus content

- Doomsday administrations gain a real 18 to 24 focus emergency branch or equivalent depth.
- Existing national trees remain where compatible.
- Branches cover peace, relief, observance, government form, Assembly diplomacy, and the hidden repression route where valid.
- The layout is compact, readable, and reviewed through the mandatory focus MCP workflow.
- Every focus has accurate filters.
- AI route plans reflect country state and movement current.
- No focus inlay window is added for the two-value event surface.

### Government transformation

- Doomsday administrations remain transformations of existing human countries.
- No mass set of new tags is created.
- Leadership, party, council, ideas, military role, focus content, and failed-date outcome match the takeover path.
- Grounded leaders use sourced portraits.
- Fictional institutional councils use approved people-free portrait packages.

### The Final Vigil

- The branch requires 1000 or more Chaos, Evolution II, its own enabled toggle, no existing world end, and valid terminal readiness.
- At least five readiness paths or valid mixed combinations are supported.
- Microstate release cannot satisfy global proof cheaply.
- The Final Assembly forms with a valid dominant current.
- Member, observer, and holdout states are valid.
- Member wars, holdout wars, civil wars, occupations, and colonies receive contextual handling.
- Normal event firing stops after terminal commitment.
- The Final Vigil super-event uses final researched text, art, quote, and unique audio.
- The date’s later failure does not undo the committed world end.

### Failed date

- The final week and final day use a scheduled bounded sequence.
- No automatic physical catastrophe occurs.
- Conviction falls according to evidence and overlapping campaign state.
- Movement responses include collapse, reinterpretation, mercy, institutional persistence, shame, and rage.
- Every posture and administration form has a meaningful aftermath.
- Reconstruction covers government, education, finance, production, transport, military, and movement settlement.
- The revised-date hardliner branch can occur only once and cannot restart the global countdown.

### Chaos and shared systems

- Event-owned Chaos changes occur only on distinct outcomes.
- Generic war, peace, death, contamination, annexation, famine, migration, and nuclear sources are not double counted.
- Real deaths use the shared population and Deaths transaction.
- Violent repression uses Condemnation only when proven by that system’s rules.
- Air Cleanliness remains owner-controlled.
- No Event 049 world-threat source flag is added.

### AI and probability

- Every weighted surface receives baseline inspection and named scenario evaluation.
- Any patched weights receive before-and-after comparison through `chaosx_ai_probability_auditor`.
- AI postures remain stable until a major reason changes them.
- AI cannot choose invalid wars, targets, institutions, routes, or Assembly states.
- Terminal readiness cannot be dominated by one country or released microstates.

### Presentation and assets

- Two complete super-event packages exist.
- Required report, news, category, focus, decision, idea, emblem, portrait, and achievement art is final and wired.
- No primitive placeholders or copied icon-family resizes remain.
- Static category picture variants match event stages.
- Final text follows the project writing rules and contains no hidden implementation language.
- No unresearched quote, cultural remark, or audio remains.

### Documentation and catalog

- Event docs describe the implemented behavior.
- Event Details, localisation, docs, and workbook wording agree.
- Only the XLSX is edited, then CSV exports are regenerated.
- All accepted plans are implemented, folded into specs, queued with a reason, or rejected with a reason.
- The required improvement-loop subagent pass is resolved before completion.

## Completion report requirements

The implementation completion report should list:

- Files changed.
- Event chain and public surfaces.
- Decision and mission coverage.
- Focus route coverage.
- Government transformation coverage.
- AI and probability evidence.
- Chaos source and reversal evidence.
- Shared-system transaction checks.
- Assets and super-event packages.
- Event Details and workbook alignment.
- Meaningful scenario tests.
- Remaining blockers, omissions, or simplifications.

If no simplifications remain, the report should state that directly and support the claim with audit and file evidence.

## Planning package status

This specification maps the complete event concept without deliberate design truncation.

The only process blocker in the planning environment is the unavailable `chaosx_improvement_loop_planner` execution route. The package records that blocker separately and does not claim that the manual review replaced it.
