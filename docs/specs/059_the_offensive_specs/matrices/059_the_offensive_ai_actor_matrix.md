# Event 059 AI actor matrix

## Actor behavior by layer

| Actor type | Baseline Offensive Posture | Relentless Offensives | Predatory Powers | Total Offensive | Protected limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Continental major | Several ranked land fronts, combined arms, air support, logistics, existing war goals | Sustains one decisive operation through follow-up phases | Uses valid claims, faction interests, and exposed rivals more readily | Can coordinate several axes and accept a thinner reserve | Core industry, capital, supply, fuel, replacement, owner route |
| Maritime major | Fleet-supported wars, convoy capacity, port targets, viable invasions | Reinforces bridgeheads and prepares follow-up supply | Intervenes where sea access and strategic interests make sense | Can plan larger or secondary landing axes | Home islands, route control, convoys, ports, air cover |
| Regional power | One main theater, limited combined arms, attainable objectives | Keeps pressure on one main axis and one support action | Can consider one additional valid opportunity when capacity is free | Can expand operation size or add a limited second axis | Reserve, replacement, fuel, stronger enemy coalition |
| Industrially weak minor | Concentrated infantry, artillery, support, trucks, one main front | Sustains short attainable offensives and reinforces local success | Usually avoids another war while a serious conflict is active | Can accept greater local risk, not a major-power force model | Rifles, support equipment, manpower, supply, home defense |
| Landlocked country | Land fronts, logistics, artillery, mobile reserve, feasible air support | Stronger follow-up on one land axis | Uses valid nearby claims and intervention paths | Larger land operation when capacity permits | No naval production or invasion layer |
| Island or coastal minor | Port defense, convoys, one feasible landing or coastal front | Reinforces a successful landing instead of scattering attempts | Uses nearby valid maritime opportunities | Can attempt a larger landing only with transport and follow-up | Home port defense, route control, convoy and supply capacity |
| Faction leader | Ranks fronts by faction survival and strategic value | Sustains operations that protect or finish a decisive faction theater | Answers useful calls, intervenes, and exploits valid rival weakness | Coordinates broader faction theaters when capacity remains | Expected coalition response and member survival |
| Ordinary faction member | Supports nearby useful fronts and valid calls | Maintains pressure where contribution is material | Joins strategically useful wars more readily | Accepts greater contribution and theater risk | Distance, supply, home defense, current wars |
| Subject | More active inside legal overlord wars | Reinforces valid nearby operations | No independent predatory diplomacy beyond autonomy rules | Greater operational commitment inside legal wars | Subject restrictions and overlord strategy |
| Government in exile | Valid air, naval, expeditionary, or liberation support | Sustains a feasible contribution | Limited intervention only through existing legal systems | Larger contribution only with real forces and access | Territory, supply base, legal authority |
| New ordinary AI country | Current baseline begins after creation or release | Current active layer inherited | Current active layer inherited | Current active layer inherited | Starting force, economy, supply, and route identity |
| Human-controlled country | Inactive | Inactive | Inactive | Inactive | Human control is decisive |
| Country returned to AI | Reassesses and activates | Restores if active | Restores if active | Restores if active | Must not resume obsolete plans blindly |
| Special Chaos actor | Compatible generic execution only | Compatible persistence only | Only owner-approved targets and diplomacy | Compatible scale under owner rules | Hard owner strategy and event gates always win |
| Capitulated or annexed country | No independent strategic layer | Inactive | Inactive | Inactive | Can return only after valid restoration |
