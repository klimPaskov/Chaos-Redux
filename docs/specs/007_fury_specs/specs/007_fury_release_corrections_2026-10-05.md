## Entry and message budget

Every successful ordinary or manual challenge preparation broadcasts one immediate opening World News, `chaosx.news.7008`.
The first new actor represents the batch, and a frozen count selects the single-country or aggregate text.
A later repeatable event firing is a separate batch and may have one opening news.
The first genuine conquest broadcasts `chaosx.news.7007` once across the campaign.
An invalid manual request reports `.3` to its original human requester and writes no false ordinary-event history.
The manual challenge announces its actual actor count, offers up to 2/5/9/16 eligible actors, and rejects an empty pool before changing existing challenge state.
Neither ordinary nor challenge setup selects a player or player-linked country as Fury.

## Army and paid actions

Paid depot deployment releases one finite reserve column in wartime after Depot Cadres.
Its ordinary price is 15 command power, 610 rifles, 40 support equipment, and 6,300 manpower.
Cadres and Depot Officers provide three and two finite reserves through the existing lifetime cap.
Their original direct recruitment waves remain one-time focus rewards.
Reserve sharing transfers the actual paid manpower/rifle/support vector; discounted purchases do not manufacture the difference.
Settlement failure refunds the payment and retracts any delivery.
Forced-march coring has four costs: command power, manpower, rifles, and stability.
Inline rows show at most three components, with every payment in the full hover and independently calculated affordability colours.
Border Survey and Recognition Denial are retired because their payments added no distinct action or readable gameplay consequence.
Unread Reach, Pact Cohesion, and numeric Compliance Drive counters are retired; real ideas, preparation, momentum, compliance, and supplies remain.

## Focus and route contracts

Paid cooperation/rivalry choices lock the opposite focus route, and their choice actions close during terminal Fury.
Other Fire Answers and Pact Command use the existing project-pact membership and sharing rules before a native faction is created.
Rival map claims add the existing centrally tuned score only for a legal third-country target also pursued by a non-allied rival.
Rival attacks wait for exhaustion of ordinary legal neighbours except in the explicit Hostile Fury challenge.
The final surviving rival can reach terminal preparation without completing the cooperation branch.
Frontier War Plans, Motorised Supply Columns, and Joint Campaign Plans promise their implemented preparation, command, and truck rewards.
They do not promise capital selection, rail sabotage, or partition of territory.
Beyond the Old Frontier fits either sustained territorial success or exhaustion of legal neighbours.
The stable source identifiers remain unchanged.

## Lifecycle and presentation

Evolution checkboxes govern manual content even when manual intensity bypasses the chaos-tier gate.
Actor-specific watch and war proof cannot qualify a different actor's defeat.
Capitulation and annexation clear the defeated actor, refresh the representative and Fury threat source, and preserve unrelated alliances.
Terminal seed and target warnings are once per human recipient rather than weekly repetitions.
Reports use the event-owned war-office card; opening news uses the preserved original mobilisation image, and conquest news uses the existing battle scene.
Hardened Fury adds five percentage points of recruitable population, reflecting forced mobilisation without the former twenty-five-point grant.
