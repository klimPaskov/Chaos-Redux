# 117 Autonomy: presentation, writing, and art

The presentation should make the political difference between points, readiness, and an actual status change immediately understandable.
The opening feels like a shared increase in self-government across varied relationships.
Later reports focus on the particular bargain or institutional setback that the player can influence.

## Information hierarchy

The root report explains the worldwide political development and identifies the player's relevant role.
Its mechanical tooltip reports the actual native point change, any cap behavior, and whether legal requirements still prevent a status change.
A master sees a concise account of affected subjects rather than a report claiming that every dependency became independent.

During an evolution, the decision category shows the current parties, phase, meaningful deadline, applicable opportunity cost, and the action that can change the situation.
The native autonomy bar remains authoritative.
No separate influence or institutional-strength meter competes with it.

Keep momentum and domestic condition legible through at most two main subject-facing national effects.
One conveys the finite growth window.
The other conveys the current program or merged disruption, with its cause and mitigation in the tooltip.
A country that also acts as a master may have an additional aggregate commitment effect for its distinct obligations.
Do not create a separate national-spirit badge for every assisted member, rejected demand, or repeated firing.

Do not expose receipt IDs, generation counters, validation terms, compatibility adapter names, or production notes in the player's political narrative.
Do not write that content was reworked, capped for implementation convenience, or added in response to a specification request.
Where a native score ceiling limits the award, explain the country's current political readiness and the actual retained change in the mechanical tooltip.

Reports use readable punctuation and a coherent narrative perspective.
Avoid ethnic caricature, triumphalist imperial assumptions, a universal depiction of subjects as helpless, and a claim that all cooperation is coercion.
The subject government and its opposition can disagree over security, timing, institutional capacity, and the terms of self-government.

Parchment report prose remains free of colour markup.
Use supported texticons and restrained emphasis in the dark mechanical tooltip surfaces where they improve comprehension.
Dynamic names and terms must come from the exact current or immutable shown participants, as appropriate.

## Response voice and reaction direction

The response voice belongs to the government currently making the decision.
A shared report may use an external political narrator, but an offer cannot speak for a recipient that has not accepted it.
Keep the gravity of an actual disruption separate from the lighter friction of routine negotiations.

| Response family | Speaker and reaction direction | Severity and humour direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subject accepts a compact or lawful improvement | A government weighing practical self-government against security, with confidence proportional to the actual concession | Measured and constructive. Restrained political irony can acknowledge paperwork or bargaining, without mocking dependence |
| Master offers or accepts a negotiated settlement | A government deliberately preserving cooperation through a changed arrangement | Pragmatic, with neither humiliation nor effortless benevolence assumed. Light bureaucratic irony is possible before a crisis |
| Either government retains the current arrangement | The actor explains why the existing relationship remains useful to it | Plain practical wording is intentional. Do not portray retention as cowardice or automatic betrayal |
| Subject maintains demands or master rejects terms | The actor makes a conscious political refusal and acknowledges what remains disputed | Firm and consequential. Avoid a comic dismissal where actual unrest or military pressure is present |
| Civil consolidation or targeted assistance | The responsible government commits capacity to a specific administrative problem | Serious, concrete, and service-focused. No joke should trivialize strikes, institutional breakdown, or loss of authority |
| Civilian withdrawal or stand-down | The actor ends its special pressure while preserving earned progress and ordinary rights | Deliberate de-escalation with a visible tradeoff. Do not imply surrender of independence rights or immediate erasure of unrest |
| Local ultimatum or confrontation request | The named government makes a limited political demand through the actual conflict owner | Sober and precise about participants and the remaining civilian exit. No triumphant language that promises an easy war |
| Harm after agreement or independence | The country's current government faces domestic consequences of a settlement it chose | Serious without declaring the settlement a mistake. Any former master appears only in a factual or currently authorized role |

Any later cultural reference requires its own source check and relevance to the speaker.
These are directions for writing, not draft options or final flavour lines.

## Historical grounding for the writing direction

The Statute of Westminster 1931 provides a useful example of greater legislative autonomy combined with continuing association and explicit consent requirements.
Its different adoption provisions also caution against presenting every subject relationship as the same constitutional ladder.
This supports the design's distinction between common political momentum and locally different legal settlements.
It does not justify copying one historical constitutional arrangement into every country.
[H-01]

A September 1943 United States memorandum concerning Philippine independence discusses economic reconstruction, constitutional continuity, and continued defense arrangements as related questions.
It provides a period example of the practical choices that can remain around independence.
Its position is that of the issuing government and should not be treated as a neutral verdict on what the Philippines ought to have chosen.
The design inference is that security and administrative preparation can matter alongside political freedom.
[H-02]

These references guide plausible themes.
They are not final flavour text, a quota for named historical people, or evidence for the game's numerical probabilities.
No quotation is required by this plan.

## Visual direction

Use institutional scenes and clear symbolic icons suited to the game's historical visual language.
The opening art should suggest authority moving toward local administration through documents, offices, and delegates, with enough visual ambiguity to serve many countries.
Avoid a generic broken-chain image for every stage, since the baseline does not itself declare independence.

Negotiation art should show a shared table with distinct delegations and separate documents, reflecting coordinated demands with individual settlements.
Disruption art should show a specific administrative or civic interruption, such as a contested office or stalled work, without inventing executions, massacres, or a battle.
The same accepted disruption scene can support institutionally related report variants when its depicted content remains truthful.
