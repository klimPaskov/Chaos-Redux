# 117 Autonomy: achievements and acceptance cases

The achievements reward difficult political outcomes rather than merely receiving the worldwide award or clicking an obvious response.
Each uses actual legal changes, completed work, and a sustained aftermath.
The labels below are working design labels and provide no final localisation.
All four achievements are visible in the achievement interface, with exact entry eligibility explained so a situational feat does not look available to every campaign.

## Shared achievement rules

Use the project's existing achievement eligibility rules and the single root achievement registry.
Check the actual player country and its living incarnation at entry and completion.
A debug-forced outcome, console setup, unsupported relationship adapter, duplicated callback, or reconstructed unproven history cannot satisfy a condition.
Existing general project eligibility restrictions remain authoritative and must be inspected before implementation.

Track actual native status changes and completed owner outcomes, not requested changes or points crossing a threshold.
Record the original relationship and actual old and new statuses.
Do not count an automatic tag replacement, temporary type switch, or reapplication of the same state as an advance.
Use legal upward steps recognized by the actual family owner, with independence counting as the final upward step only when it really occurs.

Periods of stability or peaceful cooperation require evidence of the stated duration.
Do not infer 180 consecutive days from two snapshots 180 days apart.
Use bounded tracking for active eligible candidates and actual outcome callbacks, with no new global periodic scan.
Once a tracking attempt fails, the specified later fresh attempt may start from real new conditions, without erasing historical facts.

## ACH-01: sustained self-government

**Difficulty:** Hard.

The subject must have at least two legal upward steps available in its actual relationship family at the first qualifying grant.
It must receive a positive retained Event 117 grant, then complete two distinct legal upward transitions within 365 days of that grant, using ordinary requirements.
At least one transition must occur during an active momentum window or a valid completed compact sequence.

After the second transition, the same country must remain alive, keep stability at or above 60 percent for 180 consecutive days, and avoid war with the master or former master involved in the tracked relationship.
Its choices may end in independence or in a more autonomous cooperative relationship.
The achievement does not require discarding a useful alliance.

An annexation or re-incarnation ends the attempt.
A downward transition, a forced type replacement, or a disputed transition with no owner proof cannot count as a qualifying step.
The two advances must attain two distinct higher statuses in the tracked legal progression.
Cycling down and then repeating the same lower advance cannot manufacture the second step.
The 365-day deadline applies only to completing the two qualifying transitions.
The subsequent 180-day aftermath may finish after that deadline.
Once the stated stability and peace conditions are restored, the aftermath may restart while the attempt otherwise remains valid, preserving the two qualifying transition receipts.
An unrelated external war does not fail the achievement unless it violates the stated stability or master-war conditions.

**Tracking:** qualifying grant, starting family, two exact upward-transition receipts, responsible master relationship, second-transition date, continuous stability interval, and master-war interruption.

**Icon brief:** an ascending civic structure joined to a document of local authority, with two readable stages and no national flag.
Use a distinct 64 × 64 achievement subject and the complete template-derived state triplet.

## ACH-02: a negotiated sphere

**Difficulty:** Hard.

The player must be the immediate master of at least three distinct living subjects that actually participate when its Common Demands petition opens.
Freeze those participating opening members as the original cohort.
Records excluded by the subject's own active stand-down are not participants and cannot be used to reach the three-member entry requirement.
All of those original members must resolve their demands through explicit agreement or a legal higher-status settlement before that petition's deadline.
At least one must complete a legal upward status change and at least one different member must complete a 90-day compact.

Compact acceptance must occur before the petition deadline, but its 90-day service may finish afterward.
For 180 days after all required settlements and compact service have completed, the original cohort must remain alive and at peace with the player.
At least two members must retain actual military or diplomatic cooperation through a shared faction or a verified accepted defense arrangement.
An Event 117 disruption can occur, but every such disruption in the tracked cohort must be resolved through completed civil work, delivered support, or an honored voluntary stand-down before completion.

A majority vote, unanswered expiry, forced war settlement, annexation of a member, or automatic treatment of silence as assent fails that attempt.
New members do not replace an original failed member.
A genuine later petition with a fresh eligible cohort can start a new attempt.
This prevents farming the condition by deleting inconvenient participants from the denominator.

**Tracking:** frozen original cohort, each member's explicit settlement, distinct transition and compact receipts, actual cooperation predicates, completed recovery receipts, and the full peaceful observation period.

**Icon brief:** three distinct civic documents around a common negotiating table, with separate seals retained.
Avoid a crown dominating passive satellites, since the feat is negotiated cooperation.

## ACH-03: recovery through agreement

**Difficulty:** Medium to Hard because it requires a genuine adverse outcome and an active response.

The player must begin an eligible evolution episode as a subject with stability below 50 percent.
It must experience a real Event 117 harmful outcome and complete an agreed compact with valid civil recovery work.
Explicit compact acceptance counts as undertaking its civil program.
When that program absorbs the harm, its existing start and completion receipts satisfy the civil-work requirement, including when harm occurs after acceptance.
No second consolidation action or recovery mission is required.
Normal automatic completion and its earned reward are valid, but an unearned or duplicated reward cannot satisfy the requirement.
It must then complete a legal upward status change within 180 days of compact completion and maintain stability at or above 60 percent for 90 consecutive days afterward.

The original points remain part of its opportunity, but the achievement requires the full recovery and political result.
Standing down without completing the compact does not satisfy this feat.
Triggering unrest through debug controls, supplying a fake program receipt, or changing master to discard an unfinished obligation fails the attempt.
Suppression followed by a fabricated second harmful episode cannot replace the original required outcome.

An actual military confrontation with the tracked master before the final observation period ends fails this attempt.
The country can attempt a later genuine recovery episode if it remains otherwise eligible.

**Tracking:** entry stability, actual harmful result, civil-work start and completion, compact reward receipt, upward transition, and peaceful stable aftermath.

**Icon brief:** a damaged civic ledger repaired and reopened beside a modest governing emblem.
It should communicate restored functioning, not a military victory.

## ACH-04: governing in both directions

**Difficulty:** Very Hard and situational.

At an eligible Common Demands firing, the player's country must be both a subject of one master and the immediate master of at least one other living subject.
It must actually participate in its own petition and have at least one actual participating opening member in its downstream petition.
Its own active stand-down cannot be ignored to establish the first role, and a nonparticipating downstream record cannot establish the second.
Freeze the participating downstream opening members as its original cohort.
It must settle its own petition through a completed compact or legal higher-status agreement, and it must explicitly settle every original member of its own subject petition through agreement.
At least one of these two levels must include an actual legal upward status change, and at least one must include completed compact service.

All required agreements must be honored through their service periods.
The player and the frozen downstream cohort must then remain alive and at peace with their tracked counterpart for 180 days after the last required service completes.
The player's own independence is allowed if achieved legally and if it preserves the downstream settlements.
It cannot manufacture the condition by placing countries in temporary subject relationships solely for an already consumed firing.

A downward forced status change, war with a tracked counterpart, or a missing original downstream member fails the attempt.
No grandmaster consent substitutes for the player's consent toward its own subject.
The tracked identities must survive every status update and settlement.

**Tracking:** simultaneous two-role entry proof, original downstream cohort, separate upward and downward-facing settlements, at least one legal advancement and one completed service, and the full aftermath interval.

**Icon brief:** a central civic seal connecting two separate levels of agreements, with the central country visibly responsible in both directions.
Keep the composition readable in the 64 × 64 template without tiny flag details.

## Player-experience acceptance

A new player should be able to answer five questions from the opening and its tooltip.
They should know what native progress they received, whether their status actually changed, what requirements still apply, whether an evolution is active, and where a meaningful response is available.

An affected subject should understand that agreeing, retaining the current arrangement, seeking a lawful higher status, and withdrawing are distinct outcomes.
A master should understand who has accepted, who remains unresolved, what effort it has actually committed, and which promises will expire.
Both should see that a bad outcome is recoverable and that a local confrontation is a deliberate owner-validated escalation.

## Acceptance status of this package

The design and handoff cover the baseline, both independent evolutions, harmful branches, recovery, partial and failed negotiations, local confrontation requirements, AI, Chaos, continuity, presentation, art, and achievements.
The quantitative anchors are proposed balance choices ready for implementation review.
They are not claimed playtest results.

Three material implementation requirements remain unresolved by the accessible evidence.
The project needs a verified full-family native autonomy contract, including a useful compatible treatment of consent-locked types.
It needs an accepted local-confrontation owner contract for the military branch.
It needs an explicit catalog identity migration that preserves the existing ID 117 record before the proposed new root and workbook entry are integrated.

These are named dependencies with acceptance criteria, not hidden omissions or claims that the missing systems already work.
No requested branch has been silently replaced by a weaker mechanic to produce a quicker package.
