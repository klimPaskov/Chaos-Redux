# 117 Autonomy: decisions, work, and recovery

The decision surface appears only when a country has something useful to do about an active evolution.
The unconditional award needs no button.
The later choices concern a finite political commitment, a particular relationship, and a consequence the player can understand before acting.

## One phased category

Use one ordinary event-owned category with normal decisions, a compact description, a category icon, and native mission rows.
There is no new spendable resource or custom progress meter.
Native autonomy remains the measure of autonomy.
Names, current role, unresolved-member counts, offer terms, and time remaining provide context without creating parallel legitimacy, influence, pressure, and cohesion systems.

Show the country's own subject relationship and its master role as distinct selectable contexts when both exist.
Selection is navigation and never supplies political consent.
The simplest supported native target selection should expose one current dossier and a readable group summary.
If ordinary native presentation cannot supply that selection, inspect the real target-list layout before choosing a bounded event-owned presentation extension.
Do not promise an unverified native dropdown or hide unvisited subjects behind a truncated loop.
The intended budget is at most six primary action families and normally three mission rows in the active context.

| Current context | Primary choices |
| --- | --- |
| Baseline only | No event-owned action |
| Momentum before a setback | Begin consolidation, request or accept offered assistance |
| Subject in an open petition | Seek practical terms, consider the current offer, retain the present arrangement, withdraw |
| Master with an open petition | Offer a compact or lawful status route, offer targeted assistance, maintain the present arrangement, end its negotiating effort |
| Accepted compact | Review the active commitment, withdraw from it, seek assistance if a setback occurs |
| Active domestic disruption | Consolidate, accept valid assistance, stand down, consider a legal local confrontation if it is truly available |
| Local ultimatum | The owning system's settlement responses and the subject's civilian stand-down |

A review or navigation control is not a second path to apply a reward.
The AI works directly with valid relationship records and does not depend on a player's selected target.

### The stand-down tail

D08 is available to an independent country for its own surviving domestic aftermath and optional work.
Quiet withdrawal before an agreement or actual public breakdown creates no new petition draw.
A draw already earned by agreement or real breakdown remains once-only, and its existing rejection or broken-compact disposition is not erased.
An otherwise valid momentum draw also remains, with its consequence subject to this finite tail.

Existing Event 117 harm ends at the earlier of its current expiry and 30 days after withdrawal.
Any result joining that still-active incident cannot extend the shortened tail.
If an assessment already committed before withdrawal first creates harm during the 90-day stand-down, that harm receives an automatic maximum 30-day tail from its actual onset.
No second withdrawal click or restarted stand-down is required.
The country-level 120-day continuous-harm ceiling still applies.
At a same-boundary expiry and new onset, preserve the original continuous-harm clock.

The full honored 90-day stand-down, rather than tail expiry alone, can qualify for removal of the incident's actual outstanding Event 117 Chaos contribution.
The confirmation shows any already committed assessment and the applicable tail, so the exit does not promise immunity from a real preceding agreement or breakdown.
Repeating D08 cannot postpone its end or renew its shortened-harm promise into a fresh active episode.
During its own subject-role stand-down, the country cannot accept a new Event 117 compact or acquire a fresh II demand from another firing in that exact relationship.
It remains entitled to baseline grants, ordinary legal status actions, domestic recovery, and the existing momentum rules.
No deferred demand appears automatically when the stand-down ends.
A master's stand-down does not impose that membership restriction on its subjects, and it may still offer voluntary concessions while refraining from its own special coercion and retention pressure.

There are nine action references because lawful status use and assistance deserve distinct contracts.
They are not nine simultaneous primary choices.
The final action grouping can reuse one implementation family where actor and phase differences are explicit, while preserving every listed behavior.

## Civil programs and their real price

The core opportunity cost is a finite reduction in an existing productive or political output.
No decision removes political power, command power, manpower, equipment, fuel, or civilian-factory slots up front.
The baseline, risk resolution, and emergency civilian exit have no purchase price.

For a preventive or compact program, the default burden is **5 percent construction speed for 90 days** where the country has a meaningful construction consumer.
Use a **5 percent political-power-gain burden** when that construction burden would be empty, with the applicable route stated before acceptance.
A country with zero current political power remains eligible because the cost concerns future output.
The native modifier spelling, allowed scope, zero-income behavior, and duration need verification before implementation.

For an existing disruption, consolidation immediately reduces the event's temporary stability burden by **5 percentage points**, to a minimum of zero.
It reduces the chosen 10 percent economic disruption to **5 percent** while the program is valid.
That smaller economic burden is the program's price for this recovery context.
Do not add a second construction penalty over a factory-output or administrative penalty to represent the same civil work.
For a political-only fracture, the 5 percent political-output program burden applies instead.

The 90-day commitment begins when the action starts.
A player starting late receives immediate relief but may keep the smaller program burden beyond the original harm deadline.
Show that tradeoff explicitly.
The player can stand down instead, surrender the episode's special pressure, and accept the shorter finite tail.
The AI should not begin a long program when a negligible remaining burden makes withdrawal or ordinary expiry the better choice.

If a later unrest result merges into a recovery already underway, the program remains the same work receipt.
It does not require a second payment or restart 90 days of service.
Its effect applies to the current merged Event 117 burden, within the continuous-harm limit in Part 3.

Accepting a compact during an existing preventive or recovery program keeps one civil program and one current burden.
The offer displays its revised service end, and the compact still requires 90 full days from bilateral acceptance.
Earlier work cannot be credited as service under a compact that did not yet exist.
The earlier work remains part of the country's history, and any current harm keeps its own bounded recovery deadline.

## Master effort and targeted support

A master maintaining a political compact or defending the current arrangement carries one aggregate political-effort burden for that petition.
The default is **5 percent political-power gain** for one or two active member commitments and **10 percent** for three or more.
The burden is recomputed from actual active commitments and never stacked once per callback.
A member's 90-day service obligation is measured from that member's acceptance.
The aggregate ends when the last actual member obligation ends, with no obligation extending beyond 90 days after the petition's last permitted acceptance.

Targeted administrative assistance is optional and stronger.
It costs **5 percent construction speed per supported member for 90 days**, or the disclosed political-output equivalent if construction has no meaningful consumer.
A master may maintain at most **three** such assistance commitments at once.
It cannot promise a fourth target free support by hiding the extra cost under a cap.
This creates a real decision about where an empire invests attention without multiplying flat stability losses across all its subjects.

Only separately accepted targeted assistance supplies the minus-ten-percentage-point adjustment to raw assessment risk.
Compact-wide political effort alone does not supply that adjustment.
The final chance remains subject to its floor and ceiling, so the offer displays the actual current change rather than promising ten fewer percentage points in every case.
For an already disrupted recipient, it applies the same 5-point stability and 5-point economic relief as consolidation.
If both already provide that relief, assistance does not remove the burden twice.
After 60 days of actual delivered help, assistance can end the recipient's remaining Event 117 disruption early.
The master's accepted 90-day effort continues unless the relationship ends or either party withdraws under the terms.

The master's compact-wide political effort and its separate targeted assistance serve different commitments.
Their combined effects must be shown together in its tooltip.
There is no invented transfer of money, factories, or equipment, and no refund of resources that were never debited.

## Confirmation, quotes, and silence

Consequential confirmations have an always-available, consequence-free Cancel as their first option.
The native popup timeout therefore closes the prompt safely.
The lasting decision remains available until its own disclosed deadline, so dismissing a popup does not discard the substantive opportunity.

The confirmation must identify the exact master, subject, offer, service duration, output burden, retained legal gates, and risk.
If any of those facts changed, the old confirmation cannot accept replacement terms.
It closes its own old prompt and invites a fresh review through the normal decision surface.

An actual native status purchase keeps its native payment contract.
If a future implementation adds another spendable resource, treat it as an explicit design change and apply the project's full exact-quote, inclusive-affordability, once-only debit, and actual-paid refund rules.
No action may exceed four spendable cost types, and a compact cost row uses at most three exact texticon values with complete detail in its tooltip.

## End reasons that must remain different

Completed work earns its specified result once.
Voluntary withdrawal ends future effort and applies its chosen political opportunity cost.
Unanswered expiry records an unresolved demand and the relevant risk, except for protected short-notice members.
Loss of valid authority cancels neutrally and cannot impose a voluntary-failure punishment.
An acknowledged external handoff retires only the replaced Event 117 authority.

Cleanup removes the actual native mission and the exact owned modifier even if a private activity flag is stale.
It preserves already earned score, historical outcomes, consumed reward receipts, and immutable shown proposals until their safe closure is complete.
It cannot remove a different country's program or the same tag's later incarnation.
