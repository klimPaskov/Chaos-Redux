## ACH-01: sustained self-government

**Title direction:** earned progress across two meaningful stages of self-government.
**Description direction:** explain both legal advances, the entry deadline, and the sustained stable aftermath.
**Difficulty:** Hard.
**Eligible country:** a current subject with at least two legal upward steps available in its actual family when it receives the qualifying Event 117 grant.

Require a positive retained native grant and two distinct legal upward transitions within 365 days of that grant.
At least one transition must occur during an active momentum window or a valid completed compact sequence.
The two steps must attain two distinct higher statuses in the tracked legal progression.
Independence can be the final step when it actually occurs through its legal owner.
Repeating the same lower advance after cycling down cannot manufacture the second required step.

After the second step, require the same living country to maintain stability of at least 60 percent for 180 consecutive days and remain out of war with the tracked master or former master.
The 365-day deadline applies to the transitions, not the end of the observation period.
If stability or the specified peace condition is interrupted, the aftermath may restart once conditions are restored while the attempt otherwise remains valid.
Keep the qualifying transition history.

Annexation or a new incarnation ends the attempt.
A forced type replacement, automatic reapplication, downward step, or transition without owner proof cannot count.
An unrelated war alone does not fail the feat unless its actual consequences violate the stated requirements.

**Tracking:** qualifying grant and retained amount, entry family, exact old/new statuses, each owner transition receipt, timing relative to I or compact service, master identity, second-step date, and continuous stability/peace evidence.
**Why difficult:** points alone are insufficient, the subject must complete two real political transitions and sustain its result.
**Icon:** two readable ascending civic stages connected to a governing document, no national flag, with a complete native achievement-state triplet.

## ACH-02: a negotiated sphere

**Title direction:** successful self-government settlements across a cooperating sphere.
**Description direction:** make individual agreement, different settlement routes, and the original cohort explicit.
**Difficulty:** Hard.
**Eligible country:** the immediate master of at least three distinct living subjects that actually participate when its Common Demands petition opens.

Freeze that original cohort.
Exclude records already nonparticipating under the subject's own honored stand-down before counting the three-member entry requirement.
Every original member must explicitly agree or complete a lawful higher-status settlement before the petition deadline.
At least one must complete a legal upward status change and a different member must complete a full 90-day compact.
Compact acceptance must precede the petition deadline, while its service may finish afterward.

Start the 180-day aftermath only after all required settlements and compact service complete.
Every original member must remain alive and at peace with the player.
At least two retain real military or diplomatic cooperation through a shared faction or a verified accepted defense arrangement.
Every Event 117 disruption in the tracked cohort must be resolved through completed civil work, delivered support, or a full honored stand-down before achievement completion.

Majority agreement, silence treated as assent, unanswered expiry, forced war settlement, or annexation of an original member fails the attempt.
New subjects cannot replace inconvenient original members.
A later genuine petition can begin a new attempt with its own actual eligible cohort.

**Tracking:** original cohort identities, explicit member dispositions and dates, distinct status-change and compact-completion receipts, actual cooperation predicates, all active/recovered domestic incidents, and continuous peace/survival.
**Why difficult:** the player must complete varied voluntary settlements across a fixed cohort and preserve useful cooperation afterward.
**Icon:** three distinct documents around a common table, retaining separate seals, with the complete state triplet.

## ACH-03: recovery through agreement

**Title direction:** recover governing capacity while achieving a negotiated political advance.
**Description direction:** identify genuine adversity, actual recovery work, completed compact, and the later legal improvement.
**Difficulty:** Medium to Hard.
**Eligible country:** a subject below 50 percent stability at the start of the eligible evolution episode.

Require a real Event 117 harmful outcome and completion of an agreed compact with valid civil recovery work.
Explicit compact acceptance counts as undertaking its program.
When that program absorbs the harm, its existing start and completion receipts satisfy the civil-work requirement, including when harm begins after acceptance.
Do not require a redundant consolidation button or separate recovery mission.
Normal automatic completion after valid service and its earned reward are eligible.
Unearned or duplicated rewards are not.

Require a legal upward status transition within 180 days after compact completion, followed by 90 consecutive days at stability of at least 60 percent and peace with the tracked master or former master.
A military confrontation with that master before the observation period finishes fails the attempt.
Standing down without compact completion does not qualify.
Changing master to discard unfinished duties, debug harm, fake work, or a manufactured second episode cannot substitute for the required path.
A genuinely later eligible recovery can start a fresh attempt.

**Tracking:** entry stability and subject identity, genuine harm receipt, civil program's valid start and completion, compact service and earned installment, legal transition date, and continuous stable peaceful aftermath.
**Why difficult:** the government must absorb a real setback, finish a meaningful bargain, and produce a lawful durable gain.
**Icon:** repaired civic ledger with a modest governing emblem, without military-victory imagery, in the complete state triplet.

## ACH-04: governing in both directions

**Title direction:** a government successfully manages autonomy obligations above and below itself.
**Description direction:** clearly distinguish the player's consent toward its master from each downstream subject's consent.
**Difficulty:** Very Hard and situational.
**Eligible country:** simultaneously a real subject and the immediate master of at least one living subject at the eligible Common Demands firing.
Require actual participation in the player's own petition and at least one actual participating opening member in the downstream petition.
An active own stand-down or a nonparticipating downstream record cannot supply either entry requirement.
Freeze the actual participating downstream opening members as the original cohort.

Settle the player's own petition through a completed compact or legal higher-status agreement.
Explicitly settle every original downstream member's demand through agreement.
Across these two levels require at least one real legal upward status change and at least one completed compact service.
Honor all agreements through their service periods.
Then require the player and frozen downstream cohort to remain alive and at peace with their tracked counterparts for 180 days after the last required service completes.

Lawful independence of the player is allowed if the downstream agreements remain intact.
A downward forced transition, war with a tracked counterpart, or a missing original downstream member fails the attempt.
Grandmaster agreement cannot replace the player's separate consent.
Temporary or retrospective subject relationships cannot be invented to qualify an already consumed firing.

**Tracking:** same-boundary proof of both roles, original identities and downstream cohort, separate agreements on each level, actual advance and service receipts, all applicable counterpart identities, and complete aftermath duration.
**Why difficult:** the player must succeed as both petitioner and negotiating master while preserving every tracked relationship's outcome.
**Icon:** a central civic seal linking two distinct levels of agreements, readable at native size, with the complete state triplet.
