# 117 Autonomy: political continuity and shared outcomes

Autonomy acts on the countries and relationships that already exist.
Their earlier history remains relevant, and their later independence or conflict belongs to the system that actually produces it.
This keeps a country recognizable as it moves from dependence toward a different political position.

## When IMRO's crisis begins

IMRO owns the global crisis boundary.
Autonomy does not use a high score, rejected petition, failed roll, or local war as a shortcut to that dispatcher.
When the shared crisis actually begins, it determines its complete current-subject cohort and their direct masters.
No Autonomy compact exempts a country that remains a subject from the user's specified worldwide crisis.

Pause conflicting Event 117 diplomatic escalation once the shared owner has accepted authority over the named country.
After actual independence is confirmed, close that country's old petition and pending status offers.
Retain history, earlier retained points, and any bounded local domestic recovery that can coexist with the shared owner's administration rules.
If the shared crisis absorbs the same domestic unrest, it becomes the single owner of that consequence and receives the existing disorder receipt.

A partially completed global transaction must not cause Event 117 to declare every participant free before that fact is true.
Respect each actual outcome and the shared owner's pending or failed states.

## Country effects and relationship effects

The momentum window and a domestic recovery episode belong to the recipient's living country incarnation.
Petitions, compacts, offers, master assistance, retention effort, and confrontation requests belong to an exact political relationship.
These ownership distinctions determine what survives a transfer or independence.

| Change in the world | Momentum and domestic recovery | Diplomacy, promises, and pending assessments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Higher native autonomy level, same subject relationship | Keep the original window's rate and expiry, and the current domestic recovery | Revalidate offers whose terms assumed the old tier. A still-valid compact keeps its frozen W |
| Independence | End subject-only growth and cancel an unused momentum assessment. Keep existing finite domestic recovery and the one already-earned negotiated-settlement domestic assessment | Close former-status petitions, cancel other unused relationship assessments and unearned subject installments, and remove former-master unilateral authority. Lawful termination is not a broken compact |
| Master transfer while the country remains a subject | Keep the country-owned growth rate, deadline, and used or pending single assessment. Use the actual new relationship's conditions at a future assessment | Cancel the old master's offers, aid, compact, pending petition assessment, and coercion authority. Do not transfer consent to the new master |
| Annexation | End active effects for that incarnation | Invalidate its old diplomatic records and retain enough history to reject delayed work |
| The same tag returns later | New incarnation starts without inherited active obligations | Old tag equality supplies no consent, reward, or authority |
| Civil war or country split | Do not clone the old window, receipts, or recovery reward by assumption | Let the actual civil-war owner determine political continuity. New subjects enter a later firing normally |
| Master succession with a valid owner continuity contract | Country effects remain finite | Continue only what the owner explicitly transfers with valid generation proof. Otherwise cancel neutrally |
| Loss or change of human control | No automatic change to committed passive effects | Pending consent is revalidated or safely reissued. Control loss is not agreement |

A transferred subject's old conflict can remain part of its factual history without being charged as misconduct by the new master.
The momentum risk's relationship-history factor concerns the relationship current at assessment.
General current instability remains visible through the country's actual stability and crisis conditions.

The surviving negotiated-independence assessment keeps its original due time and ten-percent agreement base.
Current domestic conditions and eligible pre-petition history may affect it, but the former group's unresolved count and relationship-bound assistance cannot.
The independent country retains its own consolidation and stand-down actions.
This aftermath supplies no political or military authority to the former master.
An external independence unrelated to an already-earned negotiated settlement does not create a new Event 117 draw.

The native growth route must be compatible with the new subject type before claiming uninterrupted delivery after transfer.
The whole-family acceptance requirement covers that transition as well as initial entry.
Do not disguise an unsupported transfer as a successful ongoing bonus.

## One orderly outcome at a shared deadline

Use a consistent ordering when several Event 117 changes are due at the same game boundary.
First reconcile actual external political changes.
Next retire effects whose elapsed interval has ended and finalize still-valid completed work.
Then resolve a due momentum assessment.
Publish its domestic consequence before resolving a same-boundary petition assessment.
Finally update presentation and available actions from the resulting state.

Durations represent elapsed intervals that include the start and exclude the end.
A 120-day window means 120 days of contribution, not 121 daily awards.
An assessment at a deadline sees the effects that are actually active after expiry processing.
Native timing and modifier refresh must be proven to implement this ordering, rather than inferred from source file order.
Expiry processing preserves the domestic first-onset timestamp when a new consequence starts at the same boundary, so the continuous-harm limit cannot be evaded through sequential labels.

Each genuine root firing is its own serialized immediate grant transaction.
A replay of that firing is not a new political event.
The timing rules cannot rerun the baseline or a completed compact because a queued report is delivered later.

## Repeated opportunities without duplicated work

The baseline receipt belongs to one firing and one living recipient.
Momentum has one active country window.
A master has one open common petition, and a relationship has one active compact.
A subject has one current domestic recovery episode, with merged causes and bounded severity.

A new firing uses the current world for its fresh award.
It continues any already active momentum without refresh.
It adds genuinely new members to an open petition without changing old terms or deadlines.
It recognizes an existing compact without promising another installment for the same service.
When those episodes have really ended, a later firing can create new opportunities.

Historical records remain useful for prior-conflict factors and achievements.
They are not permanent locks on political activity.
A stand-down restricts only the event's special pressure for its stated 90 days, with no effect on later unconditional grants or ordinary legal autonomy actions.
The subject's own honored stand-down makes it a withdrawn nonparticipant in fresh II petitions for that exact relationship, with no new aftermath or queued reentry at expiry.
Existing earned assessments keep their due times and shortened-harm terms.
The master's separate stand-down cannot supply that subject decision or cancel another country's claim.

## Existing-country continuity

Autonomy does not create a new country simply because it becomes independent.
Keep the actual tag, government, leader, ideology, owned and controlled territory, army, stockpiles, national spirits, and meaningful focus tree unless the true owner of a separate outcome changes them.
An event-created subject keeps its creator's origin and generation history.

An independent country can qualify for additive recognition or survival opportunities using its actual circumstances.
It cannot automatically inherit every Event 006 package, focus tree, achievement, or origin-specific privilege.
The user-requested connection is political continuity, not a second country initialization.

## Preserving wars and faction commitments

Points never end a war or alter faction membership.
A negotiated political settlement preserves military and diplomatic cooperation only to the extent the actual relationship and faction rules allow it.
Describe any change to those terms before consent.

If an owner uses a status-changing native operation, its war and civil-war effects need explicit verified intent.
The supplied installed-document description and offline wiki disagree about some defaults, so omission is not an acceptable way to preserve unrelated conflicts.
Any local peace must name the exact war relations it can settle and leave separate wars under their existing owners.
