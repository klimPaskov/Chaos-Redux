# Supplied Event 048 brief, normalized summary

Event 048 is **Old Great Bulgaria**, a Minor Fire-Once Formables event at Chaos level 1 with Medium member severity.

Its core promise is an immediate Bulgarian military, industrial, logistical, manpower, and political surge followed by a large dedicated focus tree. Bulgaria receives claims on historically relevant Balkan target regions, gains cores through integration, and can progress from Bulgaria to Greater Bulgaria, then to a Bulgarian Empire and finally Old Great Bulgaria as higher Chaos evolutions unlock.

The supplied branch families are National Awakening, The Bulgarian Army Reborn, Arsenal of the Balkans, The San Stefano Dream, Crown of Simeon, Kubrat's Legacy, Government of the Greater State, and Balkan Hegemony.

Evolution I, The San Stefano Dream, requires 200+ Chaos. Evolution II, Tsar of Bulgarians, requires 400+ Chaos. Evolution III, Kubrat's Legacy, requires 600+ Chaos.

The final route reaches north and east through the Black Sea sphere toward Crimea, Azov, Kuban, Taman, southern Ukraine, and historically relevant neighboring steppe territory. Exact HOI4 state binding is deferred to installed-map research.
