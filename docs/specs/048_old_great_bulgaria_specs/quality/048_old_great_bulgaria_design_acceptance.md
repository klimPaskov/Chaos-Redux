# Design acceptance

## Accepted event contract

- ID 048 remains Minor Fire-Once.
- Minimum Chaos level remains 1.
- Cluster is Formables.
- Intended member severity is Medium.
- The event fires on Bulgaria and gives a concrete immediate surge.
- Greater Bulgaria is baseline content.
- Evolution I begins at 200+ Chaos.
- Evolution II begins at 400+ Chaos and unlocks the Bulgarian Empire.
- Evolution III begins at 600+ Chaos and unlocks Old Great Bulgaria.
- Claims can be immediate. Large-scale core grants require integration or earned formation proof.
- The focus tree is large, branch-readable, and fast in its opening.
- The player tracks only National Momentum and Imperial Administration as persistent event-specific values.
- The final Old Great Bulgaria proclamation receives one dedicated super-event.

## Anti-bloat decisions

The specification does not add a separate scripted GUI, custom technology tree, 3D model, manual triggerable scenario, or additional persistent currencies because none is necessary to deliver the event promise.

## Production gates

Exact state IDs, focus layout, weighted AI tuning, final art, portrait ownership, quote selection, and audio licensing remain implementation evidence gates.

## Acceptance principle

A production implementation is not complete if one of the three formables, four political settlements, integration system, major tree families, AI route logic, final super-event, or final state binding is replaced with a smaller generic fallback.
