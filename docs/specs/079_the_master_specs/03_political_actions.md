# 03. Political campaign families

## Shared political-action rules

These three families occupy the political campaign slot: Political Propaganda, Support the Government, and Back the Opposition. A participant can switch between them when its active campaign finishes or is cancelled. Costs are paid on confirmation. The action retains its target, pledged ideology, political recipient, stage, and generation throughout its duration.

Political power is an appropriate main payment for ordinary public persuasion and party organization. The limiting sacrifices are elapsed time, the political campaign slot, family saturation, diplomatic exposure, and the lost chance to attack a rival. Do not attach arbitrary equipment costs to a speech simply to add another resource type.

Target popularity adjustments use the engine's normal ideology normalization. A promised +5 percentage points must not create a total above 100%. Read the actual resulting value for subsequent compatibility calculations. If a protected national system owns that political transition, use its adapter or offer a different valid political recipient.

## Political Propaganda

The sponsor funds a sustained public campaign for its pledged ideology and political program. It can be used from a weak position without needing the incumbent's consent.

| Variant | Stage | Cost | Duration | Influence anchor | Domestic result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public campaign | Baseline | 25 political power | 25 days | 10 | +5 percentage points to the pledged ideology. Target opinion of sponsor +5 for 60 days unless already hostile. |
| National campaign | Influence Campaigns | 50 political power | 20 days | 20 | +10 percentage points. Marks a visible campaign exposure for 60 days. |
| Coordinated media campaign | The Great Game | 75 political power | 15 days | 25 | +10 percentage points. Opens the campaign to targeted counter-propaganda. |

Use one visible action row with a scale selector. Earlier scales remain available when a sponsor cannot afford the stronger version. They belong to the same saturation family. The board shows the target ideology being promoted, so two countries promoting the same ideology can recognize that they are helping the same domestic political pool.

Popularity changes occur before the action's Influence calculation. A campaign that lifts the pledged ideology through 25% or 50% can therefore improve its own completion gain. This ordering is intentional and must be consistent across all implementations.

Propaganda never changes the ruler automatically. Existing elections may respond through their normal rules. Foreign-backed appointments and forced realignment use the separate institution operations in Part 06.

## Support the Government

The sponsor provides diplomatic backing, campaign organization, and support to the existing ruling party. Baseline availability requires the pledged ideology to match the current ruling group and the target's opinion of the sponsor to be at least 0. A verified country-specific friendly-government relationship can also qualify, but it cannot be invented from a generic faction membership check.

| Variant | Stage | Cost | Duration | Influence anchor | Domestic result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Support the incumbent | Baseline | 30 political power | 25 days | 15 | Ruling ideology +5 percentage points. Target opinion of sponsor +5 for 60 days. |
| Support a governing bloc | Influence Campaigns | 60 political power | 20 days | 25 | Ruling ideology +5. Eligible civilian institution becomes sponsor-backed for 60 days. |
| Underwrite a governing pact | The Great Game | 100 political power | 20 days | 30 | Ruling ideology +10. Eligible governing institution becomes sponsor-backed for 90 days. |

Support the Government is efficient when the incumbent remains friendly. Its weakness is dependence on that incumbent. If the government changes to an incompatible recipient before completion, the campaign cannot quietly pay the new opponent. Cancel the uncompleted political service with a 50% refund of actual political power paid, rounded down to the allowed quantum. Delivered previous results are untouched.

The last two variants produce a temporary institutional relationship, not a freely stackable country-wide modifier. Only one sponsor can hold a particular institutional alignment at a time. Replacing it requires a valid competing domestic action. The institution is defined by the country's actual political form.

## Back the Opposition

The sponsor funds a legal, tolerated, or underground movement associated with the pledged ideology. There must be a usable ideology group different from the current government or a registered same-group opposition bloc supplied by a country-specific system. Do not invent named parties or ministers for a generic country.

| Variant | Stage | Cost | Duration | Influence anchor | Domestic result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Organize opposition support | Baseline | 35 political power | 30 days | 10 | Pledged ideology +10 percentage points. Incumbent's opinion of sponsor -5 for 60 days. |
| Fund a national opposition movement | Influence Campaigns | 75 political power | 25 days | 20 | Pledged ideology +15. One eligible opposition institution becomes sponsor-backed for 60 days. |
| Consolidate an alternative government | The Great Game | 100 political power | 20 days | 25 | Pledged ideology +15. Creates a 60-day readiness window for a valid realignment operation. |

This family is the main outsider route. Its popularity change can make later propaganda, government support, or leadership pressure more efficient. It carries an explicit diplomatic cost. Repeating opposition actions cannot create automatic civil wars or erase the country's focus tree.

If the pledged movement enters government while the action is running, complete the delivered organizational work as opposition-family work, using the new compatibility. Subsequent campaigns can use Support the Government. The movement does not receive a duplicate gain for changing action families after completion.

If a country-specific system exposes political parties but not a legal same-group opposition, retain the ideology-level option. A named character route is an enhancement, not a requirement for every target to have a playable opposition campaign.

## Exposure and defensive response

Stronger campaigns leave an exposure record. That record is an opportunity for a rival to expose the operation through the interference family. The record does not create an automatic scandal by itself. Ordinary baseline persuasion can also be countered, but intelligence-based exposure requires an actual exposed operation or documented evidence.

The target's own government can demand that a sponsor change its political recipient during the domestic incidents in Part 06. No incident gives the target a hidden veto over a sponsor already legally completing the takeover threshold. Government reactions alter future actions and costs before that point.
