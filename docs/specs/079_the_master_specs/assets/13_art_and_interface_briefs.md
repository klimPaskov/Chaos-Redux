# 13. Art production briefs

These are production directions for later art workers. They are not image outputs or approved game screenshots. Source-dependent details require reference review before generation.

## Opening and diplomatic takeover images

The opening image should communicate foreign influence entering an ordinary national political setting. Use a period-appropriate exterior or institutional interior with anonymous diplomatic delegations, competing pamphlet distribution, industrial contracts, or military advisers. Choose one clear main scene. Avoid a tiny world map covered with pins, a generic staff table, puppet strings around a human neck, or a crowded collage of every action family.

The takeover image should show the domestic administration remaining in place while a foreign delegation takes the decisive role in a formal agreement. Keep people anonymous and secondary to the composition. It must work for any country without a baked-in national flag. Native UI flags will identify the actual actors.

The alignment image concerns a change inside government. Use an anonymous institutional scene that reads clearly at report size. It cannot imply execution, a coup battle, or a named leader unless that is the actual documented route. The same image can support a legal parliamentary realignment and an executive-government arrangement through contextual text direction.

The news image is a separate black-and-white composition with strong horizontal readability. It can show a formal public announcement and diplomatic presence. It must not be a squeezed copy of the report crop if that destroys legibility.

## Action icon identities

| Family | Main visual subject | Distinction to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Propaganda | A period loudspeaker or printing cylinder with a few visible paper forms | Public communication, not an intelligence dossier |
| Investment | A compact industrial structure with a construction element | Productive assets, not coins alone |
| Military assistance | A supply crate and a clearly secondary training symbol | Assistance rather than declaration of war |
| Government support | A formal institutional seal or desk emblem | Existing authority, not a crown for every country |
| Opposition support | An organized political meeting or raised ballot-paper motif simplified to icon scale | Domestic organization, not generic violent revolt |
| Interference | A marked dossier or crossed communication element | Disruption of another campaign, distinct from ordinary propaganda |

Use separate silhouettes and inspect the six icons together at 32×32. Do not reuse a single handshake image and recolor it six times. Fine lettering and many tiny objects will not survive the consumer size.

## Category and idea identity

The category emblem should represent a contested diplomatic relationship without choosing a permanent master in its art. The target's live flag and sponsor flags belong to the interface. The consolidated target idea can show several diplomatic seals surrounding one institution, with the central institution remaining visible.

Avoid visual metaphors that imply the minor has ceased to exist. Event 79 creates a subject country with its own armed forces, state administration, and later autonomy development.

## Interface reference brief

Create a reference for the real decision-category window after inspecting its native dimensions. The image should show one selected target, the player score and leading rival score, a small political-alignment row, six main action families, and two active commitments. A second state should show The Great Game's compact target selector and the expanded ranking.

Use native HOI4 typography, spacing, muted panels, flags, and restrained highlight states from the canonical visual references. All values and names in a reference are demonstration data and cannot be baked into the runtime asset. Record the exact intended viewport and scale alongside the reference.

The visual reference requires review before the interface worker implements the final board. Since no reference image has been generated in this package, this gate remains open.

## Production handoff

Each delivered family needs the source image, processing path, final DDS path, consumer size, proposed sprite ID, and its generation or reference provenance. The art worker can write event-owned images and its manifest. It cannot modify unrelated shared GUI windows or the achievement registry.

A real character portrait request must go to the portrait workflow with a sourced subject and explicit crop. This event's generic operations do not require newly invented historical faces.
