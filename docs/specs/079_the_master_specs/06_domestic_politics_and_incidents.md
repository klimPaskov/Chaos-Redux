# 06. Domestic political development

## Domestic institutions

The target remains a country with its own political structure. Event 79 uses three categorical institutional relationships: civilian administration, armed forces, and economic administration. These are displayed as short status lines in the expanded panel, not three new progress meters. A relationship records its current sponsor, political recipient, expiry, and the action that established it.

Institutional relationships do not add automatic daily Influence. They enable specific actions, provide evidence for military or economic cooperation, and help determine the ending's political description. Each institution can have one current sponsor. Replacing its sponsor does not erase the displaced country's score.

Use the target's existing political structures where an established owner provides them. A parliamentary country may express civilian support through a governing coalition. A royal state may use a royal council or court faction. A one-party state may use a party committee. A military government can express it through the governing command. These are context-specific presentation directions and cannot be used to invent historical names or additional institutions that the country does not have.

These operations are subroutes within Support the Government, Back the Opposition, or Military Assistance. They do not add extra permanent top-level decision families. All labels below are working labels, not final localisation.

The standard compatibility and saturation formula applies using each operation's family. Actual native popularity or political changes occur before the Influence gain, except where an action explicitly states otherwise. A leadership change is not a puppet relationship and cannot independently award mastery.

### Leadership preservation

A valid transition promotes an existing eligible political leader or a country-owner-provided recipient. It preserves protected characters, national flags, focus-tree ownership, and ongoing country systems. Do not create a random politician with a fabricated historical identity. If the engine cannot perform the requested appointment through the owning system, the named leadership operation is unavailable with a clear reason. The ordinary ideology-level opposition and propaganda routes remain playable.

If there is no leadership adapter, institution alignment still matters through the other actions. This does not authorize changing a leader portrait or silently replacing a national focus tree.

## Target reactions

Domestic reactions are chosen from the target's actual situation. They create political choices before the finish line, not an additional acceptance vote after a sponsor reaches 100. At most one target reaction can be unresolved at a time. Evaluate on completed action milestones with a minimum 45-day interval. Do not run an unbounded daily country scan.

These reactions are deterministic when their stated conditions first qualify after the interval. If several qualify together, select the oldest qualifying unmet condition. Random weighting can be added later only with an audited probability contract.

| Incident | Trigger and political meaning | Sponsor choices | Target response and consequences |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Funding disclosure | A sponsor completes a second exposed political operation within 60 days | Publish funding records for 25 political power, or leave the operation exposed | Publication clears that single exposure and raises target opinion +5 for 60 days. Declining retains the exposure for interference. Neither choice grants Influence. |
| Government procurement demand | The target has an actual equipment shortage and a sponsor-backed officer institution | Redirect the sponsor's next military action to a valid requested equipment category, or keep its current offer | Fulfilling the request establishes the normal advisory relationship for an extra 30 days. Refusal grants no penalty beyond missing the extension. No invented shortage or compulsory payment. |
| Investment dispute | An unfinished project loses its valid state slot or its state is no longer controlled | Relocate to a named valid state, pause up to 30 days, or terminate future funding | Already funded work remains attached to the contract. No extra Influence, free factory, or double refund. |
| Coalition confidence vote | Target has a parliamentary adapter, an opposition movement at 50%+, and a sponsor-backed civilian institution | Fund the valid confidence route for 50 political power, or keep organizing | Use the country's own parliamentary resolution. Success grants the existing readiness window for a later realignment operation. It does not immediately puppet or award extra Influence. |
| Officer objection | A sponsor attempts a civilian leadership change while another sponsor controls the officer institution | Spend 15 command power to preserve the current military chain, or accept a 15-day longer transition | Either valid choice can finish. The objection cannot create a permanent veto over political change. No purge is automatic. |
| Budget review | An industrial program would add at least 10% of the target's pre-program industry | Continue the agreed program, or publicly renounce institutional preference | Continuing grants the economic institution on delivery. Renouncing still grants the building and Influence but does not align that institution. |

A target AI does not secretly pick its future master. It reacts to the stated domestic conditions. The player sees the choice's effect before committing. A target cannot repeatedly demand free aid from a sponsor after every action.

## Competition within a shared ideology

Two sponsors can support the same domestic movement. Both can improve its popularity and thus each other's compatibility, but only the sponsor completing an action receives its own Influence. A governing institution can be aligned with only one of them. The other sponsor can compete through investments, military relationships, rival interference, or a later valid institutional bargain.

A foreign-backed government is not automatically loyal to every country sharing its ideology. This distinction is central to the contest and must appear in the expanded tooltips and ending direction.

## Shared political crisis owners

When Parliament of Fear or another national political crisis controls a chamber, purge, succession, or leadership lock, Event 79 must use its documented adapter. Without one, Event 79 can fund compatible ideology-level movements and material work but cannot remove the lock by direct character or party writes. Country-specific political systems retain authority over their own transitions.

Ordinary foreign influence does not create a separate condemnation, civilian-death, or military atrocity system. Any actual downstream war or political consequence is handled by its existing game owner.
