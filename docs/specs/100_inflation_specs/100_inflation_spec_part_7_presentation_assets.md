# Inflation: presentation and assets

## What the player sees

Use one ordinary economic decision category with an eligible static picture.
Its short description presents the signed pressure index, its named band and a qualitative trend.
The selected credit stance and the still-effective stance appear as named policy states.
Their signed fixed effects and the review sequence belong in tooltips.
The picture remains an economic scene without painted numbers, controls or a simulated meter.

The conservative budget is four public quantities: the signed index, one capacity-project timer, one fiscal or currency commitment timer, and one current recovery or political objective timer.
Each timer has a different actionable purpose.
Two support projects and the one current objective are the maximum.
During an ultimatum, its deadline occupies the objective position.
Preserve valid internal stability progress and reveal the recovery objective again after reprieve.
Do not add live extreme-exposure or reprieve counters.

Normally show three to five primary actions, never more than six in a phase.
Tighten and Ease remain ordinary decisions.
Relevant fiscal, supply and evolved actions fill the remaining space according to the actual country context and the existing commitment slots.
Changing from inflation to deflation reveals genuine expansion choices.

Each actionable tooltip explains eligibility, the quoted debit, output and time sacrifices, the next committed index effect, and the consequence of cancellation or expiry.
Use at most four spendable cost types, with the correct texticons.
Separate debited resources from temporary throughput or output losses.
Do not turn opportunity costs into invented treasury balances.
An unaffordable action names the actual missing requirement.

Report events cover the opening, a new material evolution incident, severe overcorrection, the political ultimatum, political succession and recovery.
Routine reviews use the category and existing notification behavior.
Text direction is concrete: prices, wages, shortages, damaged factories, current government choices and inherited obligations.
Working labels below are production identifiers and concept directions, not final strings.

Public works is the contextual productive use of Public Investment and shares its decision consumer.
Ordinary Spending Reductions and Emergency Budget Consolidation use separate native variant consumers with mutually exclusive offers.
The inventory contains fifteen decision art subjects.
No row is satisfied by resizing a spirit, report or achievement into another family.

The ten report compositions should remain distinct.
Use a shopkeeper's hands replacing price slips for opening inflation, a workshop agreement for wages, banknote bundles and a monetary seal for currency settlement, public economic demands for radicalization, a wholesale counter refusing old prices for runaway inflation, silent machinery for deflation, closed government doors for the ultimatum, an orderly exchange of official papers for transfer, a damaged commercial street for civil conflict, and active ordinary trade for recovery.
Keep institutional scenes people-free where they represent an abstract government body.
Anonymous contextual hands or workers must not become invented named leaders.

Report art follows the supplied 210 by 176 tilted documentary card treatment, with monochrome and sepia processing, transparent corner space and a restrained shadow.
Category art uses its own native composition.
If a report is changed to depict a particular real historical person or photographed event, its source classification changes to archival research.
Do not use a generated scene as historical evidence.

Only two national-spirit slots exist.
The crisis slot replaces its band form on transitions, including the stable-attempt form.
The adjustment slot combines current commitments and aftermath effects behind the dominant icon.
Inherited damage takes priority while its transferred aftermath remains unresolved.
Otherwise the icon follows the net direction of committed policy and programme effects at the coming review, with restrictive adjustment winning a tie when any restrictive obligation remains.
Several simultaneous policy obligations do not create extra spirits.

Existing countries retain their current identity, portraits, flags, unit graphics, equipment and voices.
Political transfer selects an eligible existing character where the native country package permits it.
A genuinely missing historical character follows the separate grounded portrait route using an attributed source and the correct existing country role.
The portrait remains separate from the generated economic scene and icon families.

## Presentation in important states

The opening view identifies the two or three current causes that matter most and the two credit directions.
A player should recognize whether the next review still carries an old restrictive stance before choosing another action.
During stabilization, the native objective explains that the band must be held continuously and that outstanding programmes remain active.
A known currency milestone belongs in that programme's tooltip with its fixed correction, conditional shock and remaining follow-through date.

Deflation changes the relevant action set and current crisis art while preserving the same economic category.
The player can see productive expansion, tax relief or easier credit as circumstances permit.
Rebound obligations remain described qualitatively, with the amount and date of an already scheduled payment shown as a consequence in the tooltip.

An ultimatum takes the objective position and clearly identifies the government at risk.
Its deadline is frozen when issued.
The reprieve condition is explained in words, without a second exposure or reprieve gauge beside the countdown.
After transfer, the existing country identity and actual new government frame the inherited economic situation.
The successor's protected period appears as a dated condition in the relevant tooltip while economic recovery stays actionable.

The current severity and adjustment icons must make simultaneous economic damage and policy sacrifice distinguishable.
All ordinary decisions remain native actions with their actual availability and cost behavior.
The mechanic's core presentation, government choices and economic management remain available with the base game.
Country-specific DLC content can refine a native political adapter without becoming a requirement for controlling prices.
