# Inflation: difficult achievements

## Common eligibility and tracking

These achievements provide recognition without factories, equipment, permanent modifiers or Chaos rewards.
The following are the achievement registration ids and working labels.
Qualifying episodes must originate from the ordinary repeatable event or ordinary cluster path, with a real human-controlled target, valid country eligibility and an intact episode lineage.
Manual setup, console/debug mutations, forced test firing and unauthorized direct index setters disqualify that attempt.

Freeze qualifying opening facts and control continuity when the episode begins.
Store only the receipts needed for the six objectives: qualifying opening, first relevant extreme, paid-policy provenance, political inheritance, war continuity and completed distinct episodes.
Use the existing index transition and episode ledger for all episode checks.
Save/load preserves receipts and exact deadlines.
A country split copies consumed proof, not a fresh achievement opportunity.

A cosmetic country change can preserve continuity.
Annex-and-release, switching to an unrelated country and copied civil-war instances cannot manufacture additional completed episodes.
Use campaign-level achievement ownership so only one award is possible.
An inherited side earns credit only under the explicit inheritance achievement unless it independently satisfies another achievement's original eligibility.

## 1. Measured Response

Achievement id: chaosx_100_measured_response.
Eligibility requires an ordinary evolved opening at +90 with Currency Crisis enabled and actual currency damage recorded.
The episode must therefore carry the 90-day stable requirement.
The player brings the index into the stable band within 60 days of firing, then completes the first 90-day stable attempt without interruption.

At no point may the index reach +100 or fall below -10.
No political ultimatum, emergency reversal, inherited country split or debug mutation is permitted.
A currency settlement is allowed if the player times the effective stance and supply support correctly.
Award only after the ordinary success transaction confirms the full attempt and its paid obligations.

Track opening eligibility, first stable entry date, uninterrupted attempt identity, minimum and maximum index, and disqualifying receipts.
A brief stable crossing followed by an excursion fails permanently for that episode.
The 64 by 64 motif is a finely balanced scale holding a price tag and a small gear.
Its challenge is accurate anticipation of lag and settlement, not merely reaching a low index.

## 2. Out of the Furnace

Achievement id: chaosx_100_out_of_the_furnace.
Eligibility requires active Hyperinflation and at least 20 cumulative days at +140 or above during a naturally worsening episode.
Before the first +100 crossing, the player must have paid for at least two different applicable containment families.
The crossing cannot be created by choosing positive credit or stimulus while already above +10.

Complete genuine recovery, including the 90-day stable attempt, within 180 days of the first +140 crossing.
No political rupture is allowed, and the subsequent minimum index must stay above -60.
Paid fiscal and supply programmes must complete or settle their obligations normally.
A lower-tier settings toggle cannot erase the qualifying damage or bypass the recovery requirement.

Track high-band exposure, the first +140 date, pre-extreme containment receipts, prohibited upward interventions, lowest later index and final success.
Repeating callbacks or sitting at a clamped +150 does not add new qualifying episodes.
The motif is a banknote bundle emerging intact beside a cooling industrial furnace.

## 3. Factories Reopen

Achievement id: chaosx_100_factories_reopen.
Eligibility requires a first crossing to -80 or below from a genuine inherited deflation shock or a settlement committed when the index was at least +60.
No additional voluntary tightening may have been selected after the episode first reached the stable band or deflation.
This excludes the obvious tactic of deliberately tightening an already stable economy.

Return to stability and finish the 90-day shock recovery requirement within 180 days of the qualifying extreme.
After that first extreme, the index may never reach +60.
Complete one real productive expansion or repair programme, and avoid political rupture.
A renamed policy and a canceled project do not count as completion.

Track the causal settlement or inheritance receipt, the date and stance at commitment, later tightening selections, first -80 date, subsequent peak and the productive-project completion.
The motif is an open factory gate with a repaired gear and a small price tag.

## 4. Same Books, New Government

Achievement id: chaosx_100_same_books_new_government.
Eligibility requires the player to control the genuine government inheriting an Event 100 political failure transaction.
Either a valid peaceful transfer or the eventual winning civil-war side can qualify.
The inherited index, damage classification, original lineage and unpaid obligations must survive the transfer without a reset.

Resolve the inherited economy within 270 days of the actual transfer and complete at least 90 continuous stable or subsequent calm days.
If the ordinary recovery requirement ends before day 90 of that interval, uninterrupted calm fills the remainder without reopening the crisis.
Then maintain 120 further calm days without another Inflation episode or forced political displacement.
A subsequent political failure during the attempt permanently disqualifies that attempt.
Any civil-war branches belonging to this rupture must have ended, and the player's government must control more than half the lineage's original core industrial capacity before award.

Track the transfer receipt, preserved economic snapshot, player continuity, surviving branch ownership, industrial baseline and calm deadline.
A tiny detached rebel country cannot earn the award while the main inherited economy remains unresolved.
The motif is an old economic ledger with a replacement government seal beside it.
The objective recognizes successful management of inherited damage.

## 5. Keep the Bread Moving

Achievement id: chaosx_100_keep_the_bread_moving.
Eligibility requires an ordinary opening of at least +70 during a real foreign war against a current major country.
The opponent cannot be a subject of the player or another branch of the same economic lineage.
There must be verified positive native war participation or combat contribution during the qualifying period.

Maintain that foreign war for 120 consecutive days from firing without capitulation.
Resolve the economy by day 180 while retaining control of at least half the opening core industrial capacity.
Do not use Reduced Military Procurement during the qualifying period, and do not suffer a political rupture.
Other fiscal sacrifices, tax changes, supply repair and carefully timed credit remain legitimate.

If economic recovery finishes before day 120, continue the achievement's war-continuity check until that date.
Track war identity, actual participation proof, core industrial baseline, procurement decisions, capitulation and economic completion.
The motif is a bread delivery wagon beside an operating wartime factory.
This demands recovery through civilian choices while the military commitment remains real.

## 6. Steady Hands

Achievement id: chaosx_100_steady_hands.
Eligibility requires three consecutive naturally selected, distinct Inflation episodes in the same continuously player-controlled country lineage.
At least two must open at +70 or +90, and the last must include enabled Economic Radicalization or Hyperinflation.
The first qualifying episode must begin with at least one genuine previous failure-memory step.

Resolve each within 180 days without a new political rupture.
Across the three episodes, neither +100 nor -80 may be reached.
Honor every actual re-entry cooldown and require at least 180 days between the preceding recovery and the next qualifying firing.
After the third recovery, complete 180 calm days and confirm that the original failure memory has been removed through the normal recovery-and-calm rule.

Track distinct episode ids, opening severities, relevant capabilities, each natural-origin receipt, extrema, resolution dates and the original memory's legitimate retirement.
An inherited copy cannot fill another slot, and changing countries resets the sequence.
The motif is three separately repaired price scales linked by one continuous ribbon.
The challenge is sustained repeated competence in an economy with a genuine history of failure.

## When an award becomes final

Each objective is evaluated from the committed episode and country history after the qualifying economic transaction completes.
A pending popup, an uncommitted programme result or a rejected cross-event request supplies no proof.
Inclusive stable thresholds apply exactly, while each achievement's stated extreme disqualifiers retain their own boundaries.
Successful reloads preserve the same dates, programme provenance and consumed awards.

The original core industrial baseline used by the inheritance and wartime objectives is frozen at the qualifying opening.
It measures the actual controlled capacity relevant to that country and cannot be reduced retroactively to excuse later losses.
If a required positive industrial or war-participation proof does not exist, that attempt does not qualify for the corresponding achievement.
Other achievements remain available under their own conditions.
