# Inflation: AI and balance scenarios

## The AI manages the same delayed economy

The AI uses the same index, policy lag, resource payments, programme slots, political clocks and inherited obligations as the player. Its advantage is consistent attention to known commitments. It receives no free correction, private refund, immunity to deflation or automatic rescue on a political deadline.

Its central objective is to establish a sustainable stable combination. A falling index is useful only while it is moving toward a range that can be held. An already committed contraction can make easing the right action while prices remain positive. Conversely, a stimulus programme approaching its stronger second phase can make further support unwise while prices are still negative.

Ordinary policy remains available in every phase. Human players retain the freedom to mismanage it. AI choices use expected economic results and real strategic sacrifice, with political survival becoming more urgent during extreme exposure and a warning.

## What the economic AI considers

The internal evaluation combines current index, effective stance, selected stance, current external causes and known programme milestones. It projects the next two unapplied reviews in their actual order. It also projects far enough to cover a known major-programme release and the ordinary stance steps, cooldowns and lag needed before that date.

The forecast assumes current external causes remain unchanged except for already verified material changes and scheduled commitments. It is an estimate with declared uncertainty. An unknown Event 034 input is not silently treated as zero. The player sees a qualitative trend and the relevant commitment explanation.

At opening and after each valid review, the AI refreshes its policy and project intent. A material immediate shock also refreshes that intent. Native decision selection remains subject to the engine's actual scheduling. A willingness score is not an exact chance to click or a promise that the action occurs on that day.

Economic urgency should normally order the goals as follows:

1. Avoid a known immediate severe opposite-side shock when an affordable correction can prevent it.
2. Escape extreme prices before an active political deadline or further extreme exposure makes failure unavoidable.
3. Correct the current direction while preserving enough construction and military output for the country's actual situation.
4. Repair a material cause that would otherwise require permanent emergency policy.
5. Maintain the stable attempt and prepare for programme release.

This ordering does not require the AI to buy every available measure. A country whose +5 baseline is already offset by moderate restriction should not repeatedly spend on another programme merely because its category is visible.

The AI can choose emergency consolidation when the immediate contraction is necessary and the country can endure its exceptional industrial profile. Currency settlement is preferable when damaged currency itself needs resolution and its slower milestone can be managed. Their relative value should vary with deadline, current output, repair state, policy lag and the cost of a future overcorrection.

A programme that becomes unaffordable or materially invalid cannot retain positive AI willingness through an old cached quote. The same final eligibility and payment contract used by the player decides whether it can be committed.

## Choosing the affected country

Each eligible country enters once through the union of current majors and players. A proposed event-owned risk score starts at 100 and adds 25 for each of up to four distinct material risk families, capped at 200. This is a relative ticket input, not a probability.

The verified shared load adjustment then multiplies that input by four, two, one or zero for current local-crisis loads zero, one, two or three and above. Shared player-crisis settings apply through their actual existing owner contract. They should not be recreated as an undocumented Event 100 player handicap.

This combination favors a plausible strained economy while still giving less burdened countries priority when risk is comparable. It does not hardcode a particular historical country as the permanent target. A country with three counted local crises is ineligible. A country with an active Event 100 episode is not admitted again even if a stale selector still holds its tag.

The complete pool must be known before anyone states an exact selection probability. A displayed percentage calculated from only some majors would misrepresent the event.

## Choosing actual opposition

The political selector begins with the complete credible current opposition pool from Part 5. Ordinary candidates need at least 5% support, with exceptional organization or country-route evidence permitting a lower-support movement. Invalid identities, unavailable leaders and impossible routes receive zero weight.

A useful initial relative rule squares native support and then applies bounded organization and practical-access factors. Those factors normally remain between 0.75 and 1.25. They can distinguish two substantial movements without allowing a negligible party to routinely overtake a large opposition on a tiny arbitrary baseline.

These are proposed score relationships. The final probability pass must inspect actual source, complete candidate pools and native support units. It must preserve the intended ordering across the 5% floor and across country-specific exceptions. A same-ideology faction is eligible only when the country actually represents it as a distinct movement with a supported transfer route.

The selected challenger is frozen at political failure before the concession or refusal report. Both branches use that identity. Neither an option click nor a repeated callback can reroll the opposing party.

## Concession, refusal and successor behavior

| Political situation | Intended preference |
| --- | --- |
| Functioning constitutional replacement with a strong eligible opposition | Peaceful transfer should dominate ordinary AI behavior. |
| Entrenched regime with viable command, organized challenger and a feasible territorial split | Refusal becomes substantially more plausible. |
| Dictatorship with overwhelming opposition and a viable negotiated route | Concession remains possible and should gain weight as resistance becomes untenable. |
| Nominal constitutional regime whose actual replacement route is blocked | The country-specific route and feasibility decide the result. The ideology label alone supplies no immunity. |
| Infeasible map split but valid selected opposition | Use the accepted same-country coercive or constitutional transfer, preserving the challenger. |
| No real eligible party or another owner holds a locked succession | Use the explicit bounded political-aftermath handling. |

Economic Radicalization increases the importance of early containment because actual political support changes while hardship remains severe. The AI should recognize those changes. A five-point popularity transfer is not simply another hidden failure score.

After a takeover, the successor's first economic evaluation includes the inherited effective stance, selected stance and every paid project. The ninety-day political reprieve does not justify neglect. At its end, fresh extreme exposure can start, so a successor that never repaired the economy remains vulnerable to a later genuine failure.

## Evolution timing

The four user thresholds remain 200, 400, 600 and 800 Chaos. Enabled qualifying capabilities can be present at firing. Active entry uses the actual shared evolution pacing and settings, with a ninety-day MTTH anchor. A severe relevant condition can justify about sixty days, while a successful stable attempt can justify about 120.

Those values describe the intended time distribution. They must be implemented through the verified MTTH adapter, not interpreted as an arbitrary percentage rolled every ten days. Multiple display surfaces cannot trigger multiple rolls for one evolution opportunity.

The probability review should compare just-below and exact-threshold states, enabled and disabled tiers, relevant severe hardship, stable recovery, a shrinking global Chaos total and a continuing inherited episode. A later enabled tier must retain a playable recovery route when an earlier tier is explicitly disabled.

## What must be demonstrated in balance review

The implementation handoff contains a named scenario manifest with separate country and policy comparisons. Its minimum comparisons cover the following outcomes:

- A mild opening can recover through ordinary policy without compulsory emergency sacrifice.
- Aggressive delayed restriction can overshoot into deflation even after a new easier stance is selected.
- Both the maintained currency route and the independent emergency-budget route can regain control from the upper bound when used early enough.
- Starting a slow settlement too late in an already issued short ultimatum does not guarantee political rescue.
- Severe deflation has an affordable route back upward, with excessive support capable of restarting inflation.
- Known major-programme expiry is anticipated across the complete ordinary stance ladder and its lag.
- The strongest plausible actual opposition usually leads the candidate ordering, and impossible candidates remain impossible.
- Native decision costs, project slots and displayed timers match the actual actions available to both AI and player.
- Recurrence, country splitting, reunification and settings changes cannot duplicate outcomes or reset historical obligations.

Inspect the source before evaluating probabilities. Evaluate the named states, sweep boundaries and rank reversals, then compare immutable before-and-after source with the same fixtures. Use uncertainty simulation only for explicitly declared uncertain inputs. Sequence analysis requires a complete declared event pool, cadence and transition manifest. Source arithmetic and example trajectories remain separate from those probability results.

The design is successful when the AI understands the same balancing problem as the player, with varied but explainable choices and meaningful consequences for prolonged neglect.
