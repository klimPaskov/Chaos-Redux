# Inflation: recovery, recurrence and continuity

## Continuous stabilization

The economy recovers by remaining between -10 and +10 inclusively. The clock begins at the actual time the index enters that band. It advances only while the index remains there. Every immediate decision, delayed correction, review and inherited episode change must respect that continuity.

| Worst realized history before the attempt | Required uninterrupted stable period |
| --- | ---: |
| Ordinary inflation without severe damage | 30 days |
| Severe inflation or severe deflation | 60 days |
| Hyperinflation, damaged currency, or a qualifying currency/deflation shock | 90 days |

The requirement is frozen when an attempt begins. An initially mild episode does not arbitrarily become a ninety-day task because global Chaos rises while its prices remain calm. If the country later suffers actual severe damage, it loses its current stable attempt and the next attempt uses the appropriate longer history.

A shock qualifies for the ninety-day history when the currency programme applies its conditional contraction shock or when a committed contraction reaches -80. Merely crossing from +5 to -5 is ordinary stable movement. It must not create a hidden severe-deflation record.

Any genuine excursion outside the stable band resets progress at that time. Moving from +10 to +15 and back to +10 later on the same day still interrupts continuous stability. Replaying the same callback does not create another interruption because it is the same already-consumed economic action.

The review movement and its scheduled programme milestones form one ordered economic settlement for that timestamp. Every actual applied index change can interrupt continuity and record realized severity, even when no time elapses before the next change. The final state governs the new interval. Intermediate arithmetic totals that are never applied are not economic states, and same-time changes add no fictitious days of exposure.

## What happens when the stable period finishes

Completion first settles any economic movement due at that exact instant. A scheduled correction that carries the index outside the band prevents success. A political reprieve that becomes true at the same instant is resolved before political deadline failure.

If the stable requirement remains satisfied, the crisis ends. It removes the current crisis spirit, stops its economic review ownership, releases its one local-crisis claim and records one successful recovery. The index becomes historical when the crisis closes.

Outstanding real commitments do not disappear as a reward. Their remaining industrial sacrifices finish through their accepted wind-down, with only the adjustment spirit retained. Unconsumed equipment can be returned only under its actual receipt. An already used repair benefit, currency correction or stability payment cannot be refunded.

Before success commits, all outstanding fixed index-release or correction obligations settle through the ordinary index-change gateway in their recorded due order, including installments originally due later. Their exact receipts are consumed. Every actual applied movement rechecks continuity. If any movement leaves the stable band, the attempt is interrupted at that moment and recovery is not awarded, even if another same-time movement returns prices to stability.

The programme and recovery tooltips disclose this closing treatment of fixed liabilities. It prevents a country from retiring the index just before an advertised wage rebound. Late callbacks for consumed installments do nothing. Future recurring policy effects retire with the index through orderly closure, while their original real economic costs remain paid. Unknown future external events are not invented or prepaid. The recovery report identifies the remaining temporary adjustment burden where one exists.

The event's own positive Chaos consequences can be partly reversed under the lineage-capped rule in Part 6. A mild episode that awarded no Chaos cannot become a source of repeated negative Chaos. Economic recovery gives no permanent output bonus, construction reward, free factory or generated equipment.

## Extreme exposure and political warning

Two regions accumulate actionable extreme exposure: +100 or above and -80 or below. Thirty cumulative days in either region create the economic-failure warning. The clock measures actual time, including a mid-review decision crossing a threshold. It is not simply three successful checks of a flag.

| Price situation | Effect on the extreme-exposure record |
| --- | --- |
| At or above +100, or at or below -80 | Accumulate actual elapsed extreme time |
| Severe but outside the extreme region | Retain accumulated exposure without advancing it |
| Between -60 and +60, exclusive | Stop advancing exposure and begin the clearing streak |
| Thirty continuous days outside both severe bands | Clear the current exposure record |

Alternating between +99 and +100 does not erase exposure. Crossing directly from hyperinflation into the deflation extreme also retains responsibility within the same episode. Both extremes contribute to the same exposure history.

The warning's deadline is chosen when issued and remains visible.

| Current political condition | Initial deadline |
| --- | ---: |
| Severe institutional weakness or repeated failure | 20 days |
| Ordinary case | 30 days |
| Functioning institutions with recent actual economic improvement | 40 days |

Institutional weakness is derived from current politics and capped failure memory, not ideology alone. A dictatorship with coherent command may be willing to resist, but that preference is a different question from how much time economic collapse has already consumed.

No index jump, evolution entry or inherited callback can create an already expired warning. The player receives the complete selected deadline. New governments do not inherit an executable old failure token.

## Earning political reprieve

Twenty continuous days strictly above -60 and strictly below +60 can stop the warning. Stable prices are sufficient, but full stabilization is not required for this political reprieve. A recovery from +130 to +40 can therefore save the government while the economic crisis continues.

The committed next movement must also avoid an immediate return to an extreme band. This condition uses known committed policy and scheduled corrections, not speculative random future shocks. Its warning belongs in the relevant policy tooltip. A government can remove a dangerous commitment through the existing economic choices.

Successful reprieve is checked before deadline failure at the same timestamp. A country that receives twenty days and achieves the displayed condition for those twenty days must not lose because the failure callback ran first.

One earned extension is available. At expiry, a country with at least ten continuous nonsevere days and a known next-review forecast remaining nonsevere receives only the time still needed to complete the twenty-day reprieve, up to ten additional days. The extension is granted once and its new deadline is shown. A broken streak during that extension does not grant a second extension.

After reprieve, retained exposure cannot issue a new warning on its own. A fresh entry into an extreme is required while old exposure remains uncleared. A new warning then receives a complete fresh deadline. Repeatedly oscillating around an extreme therefore remains dangerous, while an economy steadily recovering through +40 is not punished with another warning every review.

Political reprieve is distinct from full recovery. During a warning, the visible objective is the political deadline. Valid stable time is preserved internally. After reprieve, the recovery objective reappears with the remaining continuous requirement. The category never displays an extra live exposure or reprieve-progress meter.

## Political failure and the successor's chance

An expired ultimatum commits one political failure transaction. Part 5 defines actual-opposition selection, peaceful transfer, refusal, civil war and the explicit cases where a valid armed split is impossible. Its result never resets the economy to a mild opening.

A replacement government receives ninety days of political reprieve from the completed transfer. Fresh actionable extreme exposure begins only after those ninety days. A later failure therefore requires a new thirty extreme days and a new complete ultimatum. Historical damage and failure memory remain in place throughout.

An unresolved civil war prevents recursive Inflation-created civil-war splits within that conflict lineage. Further economic hardship continues to matter through current prices, production, popular support and the existing conflict. It cannot mint another set of rebel stockpiles.

Later genuine political failures are possible after a valid new exposure-and-warning sequence. The once-only rule belongs to each failure transaction. Shared economic Chaos and achievement allowances remain capped at the wider episode-lineage level, so repeated government changes cannot multiply the same economic reward.

## Recurrence after an episode ends

The normal re-entry anchor is 180 days after recovery, rising to 270 after a political rupture. Current severity, failure memory and shared crisis load can move the interval within 120 to 360 days. The chosen interval is set when the episode closes. It is not shortened every day by a temporary fluctuation.

| Completed history | Starting recurrence treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Mild, clean recovery | About 180 days, with the calmest cases eligible for a 120-day lower bound |
| Severe but politically contained recovery | About 180 to 240 days |
| Government turnover or civil conflict | About 270 days |
| Repeated failed episodes with unresolved weakness | Up to 360 days, paired with greater opening risk when selection becomes legal again |

Longer protection after failure prevents immediate repeated targeting while the country pays its aftermath. It does not erase the danger. The next legal firing reassesses current war, shortages, damage, stability and political opposition.

At most two hidden failure-memory steps remain. A genuine new political failure can add one step. A successful recovery followed by 180 continuous calm days removes one existing step. At most one step is removed per genuine recovery. Removing a second requires another real recovery-and-calm sequence.

Calm means the country has no active Inflation episode, no newly realized severe economic-collapse marker from the connected owner systems, and no new political rupture during that period. It is not a demand that the world remain at peace. A qualifying country at war can still rebuild its institutions.

The global repeatable-event owner retains global frequency and pacing. Country cooldown and selection weight do not create an additional independent random-firing loop. Event 100 becomes normally available again only through the actual shared event pool.

## Country continuity

| Change during the episode | Economic continuity |
| --- | --- |
| Cosmetic name or flag change | Preserve the same episode and actual country ownership |
| Peaceful or coercive opposition transfer | Preserve index, effective and selected stance, projects, damage history and actual payments |
| Civil-war split | Copy price intensity and shared history, partition material obligations by actual territory and capacity, and give each valid side an owned continuation |
| Reunification | Keep the victor's current price state, merge consumed lineage receipts once, and settle obligations attached to recovered territory without duplicating them |
| Annexation | End invalid active ownership as a non-success result. The annexer is not automatically granted a new Inflation episode. |
| Later release | A verified national-identity relationship can retain bounded failure memory. A new crisis still needs current normal eligibility. |
| Exile or loss of territorial authority | Suspend domestic project and political operations, release invalid counted ownership, and record non-success. A later valid return uses a single resumed continuation if still applicable. |
| Conversion to an economy outside normal civilian systems | End or suspend the ordinary economic owner according to the shared classifier, with explicit settlement of already paid obligations |

Civil-war sides may start with the same index because price pressure is an intensity. Their factories, equipment, state repair commitments and paid material benefits are extensive assets and must be split. The distinction prevents a false conservation rule for the index and a real duplication exploit for resources.

Every delayed economic operation belongs to a specific episode and current owner. A callback from a concluded episode cannot lower a later episode's index, replay a political failure or award another achievement. This is part of the game's continuity, particularly for saves made near a programme milestone or government transfer.

## Closure that can be understood

The final recovery report describes what was stabilized, how severe the episode became, which government now holds power and what temporary obligations remain. Failure reports distinguish a government transfer, an actual armed split and an exceptional unresolved political replacement. The event history records the country that was actually affected.

Repeated crises should feel like a country's changing economic history. The historical record contains real episodes and material consequences, while the active category contains the decisions that can still change the current one.
