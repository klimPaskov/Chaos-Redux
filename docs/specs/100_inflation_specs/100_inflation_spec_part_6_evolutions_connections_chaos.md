# Inflation: evolutions, connections and Chaos

## Capability entry and shared rules

Preserve the four names and gates exactly: Wage-Price Spiral at 200 Chaos, Currency Crisis at 400, Economic Radicalization at 600 and Hyperinflation at 800.
A naturally selected country receives each enabled, qualifying capability at firing, in ascending tier order.
The economic profile selects the opening index, including the permitted +90 evolved opening.
Capabilities do not create four additional simultaneous incidents or four mandatory opening popups.

During an active episode, a newly qualifying enabled tier enters through the shared evolution pacing contract.
Evolution MTTH anchors are 90 days normally, 60 days when that tier's relevant hardship is already severe, and 120 days during a successful stable attempt.
These describe intended timing, not per-review percentages.
Do not reroll multiple times because an evolution is displayed on several surfaces.
Activation and logging give zero Chaos.

An applied capability stays attached to the episode when global Chaos subsequently falls.
An explicitly disabled tier supplies no new effects, and a later enabled tier must remain playable without silently reactivating a disabled predecessor.
Already paid obligations retain their disclosed settlement and cancellation terms.
Toggling a capability cannot refund expenditure, erase an issued political deadline or delete a queued consequence that was accepted as part of an earlier action.

Use existing episode severity, exposure, paid-project and lineage records wherever possible.
The additional compact evolution history consists of a bounded wage rebound liability, a currency-damage state and a radicalization tranche receipt.
The last receipt records accumulated severe days and consumed awards.
Hyperinflation acceleration is calculated from the current index and needs no separate runaway meter.
Each record exists to settle an actual obligation or prove an outcome, with no confidence, wages or opposition gauge added to the category.

Every index mutation immediately checks band changes and continuous-stability interruption.
A review first applies the previously effective credit stance, then promotes the selected stance for the following review.
Thus a decision made between reviews waits through the next review and affects the one after it.
Tooltips distinguish that old commitment from the newly selected policy.
Neither evolution entry nor a settlement completion silently changes this ordering.

## Evolution I: Wage-Price Spiral

Wage-Price Spiral makes suppressing visible prices harder when goods remain scarce or military expenditure continues.
It adds consequences to actual price controls and their release when the stated economic conditions apply.

Use a wage rebound liability from 0 to 20 index points in steps of 5.
At a review under active price controls, add 5 only when verified supply strain remains unresolved or the war-spending family is still contributing.
Count these causes once for that review.
A genuine completed supply repair stops new liability from that repaired cause.
A new control order does not reset an older liability or postpone its original settlement date.

When controls expire, settle up to 10 liability points at each of the next two reviews.
Use the ordinary review transaction and record each consumed installment.
This is an explicit release adjustment, not a second copy of the same external shortage contribution.
The tooltip states qualitatively whether rebound remains and gives the fixed amount and date of the next scheduled release.
It does not display a changing numerical liability total.
The trend can explain that a release will raise prices.

The Wage-Price Agreement is the constructive response.
The agreement is a 30-day negotiated commitment with a 5% factory-output opportunity cost and no minimum factory or equipment bill.
It uses the fiscal support slot, with any replacement of price controls preserving the original release dates and remaining obligations.
While maintained, it prevents new wage rebound liability and retires 5 outstanding points at each review.
Its description explains that employers and workers accept temporary production constraints while supply catches up.
Actual material repair remains necessary to remove the underlying external cause.

One report presents the agreement when controls have accrued a liability or unresolved severe inflation makes negotiations relevant.
The government can accept the agreement, retain its current controls with the disclosed release obligation, or let controls lapse and absorb that obligation.
Refusing negotiations gives no free benefit and creates no invented party, union leader or separate political currency.

Cancellation preserves the remaining liability.
If the country falls into deflation before the release payment, the liability still represents a known upward commitment and belongs in the forecast.
It can help absorb contraction, but the agreement cannot generate a permanent reward when no liability exists.
A stable attempt fails immediately if a scheduled release carries the index above +10.

The AI favors an agreement when a real liability would interrupt recovery or when controls have become a repeated substitute for repair.
It favors supply repair when the actual bottleneck is affordable to fix.
It avoids paying for negotiations with zero liability, no relevant future strain and no protective purpose.
The agreement must remain useful during war, after repair and before a pending release, with costs reflecting its actual protective purpose.

## Evolution II: Currency Crisis

Currency Crisis introduces a persistent damaged-currency state when severe inflation undermines ordinary contracts.
Currency damage begins after two consecutive reviews at +60 or above.
An evolved +90 opening may begin in this state because its opening profile already represents severe current damage.
This state is an episode receipt, not a spendable reserve or a confidence score.

While the currency remains damaged and inflation is severe, apply a +5 contribution within the existing capped external-driver calculation.
It replaces any duplicated currency-confidence contribution from the same cause.
Record actual currency damage in the worst-severity classification so that the next stable attempt requires 90 continuous days.

Currency Settlement immediately starts a real 90-day hard-fiscal commitment, contributing -15 at each 10-day review while maintained.
Its first phase completes after 30 days, followed by 60 days of continued commitment under the same native project timer.
While fully maintained it also pauses Evolution IV runaway pressure.
The programme pays the fiscal allocation and opportunity cost defined in the decision specification.
The settlement requires a supported supply assessment.
An undamaged country can satisfy the supply assessment through verified adequate capacity.
A country with actual damage must complete the specified repair or accept the disclosed unsupported-restoration risk.
A large fixed reserve, factory or stockpile purchase must not make the route inaccessible to a tiny country.

On valid day-30 phase completion, apply a correction of -45.
Apply an additional -20 contraction shock if the previously effective stance is -10 or -20, or if the promised supply restoration is unsupported at completion.
These conditions are OR conditions and produce one -20 shock, never two.
The shock also causes one actual stability loss up to five points and ninety days of 10% construction and 10% factory-output loss from that milestone. It uses the existing capped adjustment spirit and retains its remaining duration after other programmes end. The extra downward price movement can be useful during hyperinflation, but it represents a costly hard landing. Unsupported restoration retains the damaged-currency cause until actual repair occurs.
Evaluate the effective stance that actually applied to this review, not the stance that will only take effect next time.
Both phase effects share one exact-once receipt.

The completion preview shows the base correction, the conditional shock, the next old-stance review and the subsequently selected stance.
An early decision to ease can therefore prevent an avoidable shock if it becomes effective in time.
An emergency reversal can cancel the old stance early at the real 90-day adjustment cost.
It does not erase the already paid fiscal programme, an applied correction or the record of currency damage.

A failure report distinguishes a funding interruption, an unmet repair commitment and a completed settlement that caused contraction.
Funding interruption produces no correction or refund and preserves the damaged state.
Unsupported restoration still completes only under the explicitly accepted risky branch, with its visible shock.
A successful supported first phase removes the continuing currency contribution, while the -15 follow-through can still cause deflation.
Aborting keeps actual paid costs, applies the disclosed release pressure and restores runaway pressure when the index qualifies.
Relapse cannot replay the day-30 correction or award another lineage rupture.

The AI starts a settlement when severe inflation and real support commitments make it preferable to continuing ordinary restraint.
It forecasts both old and queued stances, pauses additional tightening before completion, and pays for emergency reversal when its material cost is lower than the predicted extreme contraction.
Emergency Budget Consolidation provides the major fiscal recovery route when this evolution is disabled.

## Evolution III: Economic Radicalization

Economic Radicalization links prolonged severe hardship to the country's actual political alternatives.
Use native party popularity and the political selection rules in Part 5.
A movement needs a real supported party or an explicitly verified country-specific political route.
An ideology label alone does not establish that it is a plausible economic opposition.

Award one radicalization tranche for each 30 cumulative days in either severe band.
Unlike the extreme-failure clock, this includes +60 through +99 and -60 through -79.
Days outside both severe bands do not advance the tranche.
Thirty consecutive days outside both severe bands clear an unfinished tranche.
Completed awards remain real political consequences and are not reversed automatically by economic recovery.

Each completed tranche transfers up to 5 native popularity points away from the incumbent toward the strongest plausible economic opposition.
Cap Event 100's direct extra popularity transfer at 20 points per episode lineage.
Never drive a donor below zero or invent support outside the native total.

A report after the first award describes a concrete challenge, such as demands for a different budget, control of credit or broader relief.
Its working choice directions are to negotiate an available economic concession, keep the current programme, or rely on the existing country's lawful political response.
Any extra economic concession uses one of the accepted action families and its real cost.
Do not sell a free reduction of opposition through a generic political-power click.

The same real opposition pool supplies the final challenger if the ultimatum expires.
Popularity awarded to one movement is not subsequently reassigned to an irrelevant party because a different failure branch is easier to script.
Re-evaluate the actual pool at the defined political selection point, with country-specific exclusions and support-aware weights.
A dictatorship has a stronger refusal preference, while institutional willingness, current support and valid political routes still determine the branch.

A successor inherits consumed radicalization awards, damaged prices and committed obligations.
Its 90-day political reprieve prevents immediate renewed failure.
After that reprieve, a later genuine failure requires a fresh 30 days of extreme exposure and its own political ultimatum.
Each failure transaction can create only one political consequence.
Inherited copies cannot reset the 20-point allowance or replay a consumed failure transaction.
Severe hardship can still influence the successor's real politics, and a later government can fall if it fails again.

The AI should attach more urgency to a long severe episode when an eligible opponent is already strong.
It should compare the economic recovery route against the real cost of concessions.
Political behavior must remain coherent with one dominant opposition, several credible alternatives, no eligible challenger, a willing transfer or an existing civil war.

## Evolution IV: Hyperinflation

Hyperinflation accelerates the upper tail and makes weak restraint inadequate.
Apply an additional runaway term of +5 per review at +100 through +119, +10 at +120 through +139, and +15 at +140 through +150.
This term is separate from the bounded -10 through +20 external driver.
The external driver already includes the +5 baseline, which must not be added twice.
The index still clamps at +150, and discarded excess does not accumulate as hidden debt.

At +100 or above, ordinary credit restraint loses one strength step: -5 becomes 0 and -10 becomes -5.
The emergency -20 stance retains its full effect and full sacrifice.
Easing and stimulus keep their upward effects, including the danger of restarting runaway inflation.
Below +100, ordinary restraint immediately regains its normal effect on the next valid review.
This deterministic rule makes the deterioration explainable without random failed clicks.

Major paid fiscal action, genuine supply improvement and a completed currency settlement remain effective.
A settlement retains its -45 correction and conditional -20 shock.
When Currency Crisis is disabled, fiscal and supply commitments must still provide a paid recovery path.
Do not make an enabled upper evolution depend on a disabled lower capability.

The worst gross load is +35 per review: the capped +20 external total, including baseline, and +15 runaway.
Under the currency programme, the maintained 90-day programme pauses runaway and contributes -15 while the effective emergency stance contributes -20.
At +150 with external pressure +20, the index therefore reaches +135, +120 and +105 at the first three reviews.
The day-30 correction and one conditional shock then move it to +40.
Twenty continuous days outside both severe bands can stop an ultimatum by day 50, before an ordinary day-60 expiry.
The Part 2 review order resolves successful reprieve before a tied deadline.

This fixture assumes an already effective emergency stance and immediate valid programme funding.
Weaker or newly selected stances need separate lag scenarios.
The sixty-day follow-through continues suppressing runaway and maintains -15 until the final twenty-day taper in Part 3. A programme aligned to the review cadence applies -15 at day 70, -10 at day 80 and -5 at day 90 before expiry. Easing after the first phase and preparing for the later release are both important.
Aborting or expiry restores the ordinary forecast and discloses every due release.
The alternative major fiscal route below preserves recovery when Currency Crisis is disabled.

The incident report should show the material consequences of runaway repricing, such as wholesalers refusing yesterday's prices and wages arriving after basic goods have risen again.
Its choices point toward the available major fiscal programme, currency settlement when enabled, or the current emergency stance.
The report creates no free correction.
Once prices fall, the pending restrictive policy and settlement shock can still drive severe deflation.

The AI uses emergency restraint when ordinary action cannot overcome the current load, starts eligible major commitments early, and changes course before the combined correction overshoots.
It must not repeatedly choose a numerically weakened ordinary action merely because its political-power cost is lower.
A poor country has the same core route through output, stability and time sacrifices.

## Emergency Budget Consolidation

The severe variant of Spending Reductions provides a major route even when Currency Crisis is disabled.
It becomes available at an index of +80 or above, or when the known committed forecast reaches +100.
It occupies the same fiscal programme slot as Currency Settlement, so the two major programmes cannot stack.
Ordinary spending cuts remain a weaker, less costly option in their appropriate context.

Consolidation lasts 90 days, immediately reduces the index by 30 and contributes -15 at each ten-day review.
While fully maintained it pauses Evolution IV runaway pressure.
It costs 40% construction throughput, 30% factory output and an actual ten-point stability loss capped to the stability the country can pay.
These are the exceptional adjustment-slot ceilings defined in Part 2, including overlapping Event 100 commitments within that slot.
The current severity spirit remains separate.
The route is expensive even in a tiny country and requires no minimum factory block or stockpile purchase.

At +150 with emergency restriction already effective and external pressure at +20, the opening correction produces +120.
The following reviews produce +105, +90, +75, +60 and +45.
With fresh extreme exposure and prompt use, the route leaves the extreme band before thirty extreme days accrue.
A country that has already consumed much of its ultimatum still faces the consequences of that delay.

This programme has no currency-settlement milestone or extra -20 currency shock.
Its continued -15 effect can nevertheless cause deflation once prices fall.
The government must ease credit in anticipation of the remaining commitment.
Abandonment retains actual paid costs and applies the disclosed release terms.
Currency damage, if already present, survives austerity and continues to require the longer recovery period.
A fresh programme has a fresh paid receipt, while replaying an old programme cannot repeat its upfront correction.

## Connected economic events

Industrial Boom, Great Depression 2.0 and British Tax can change the conditions Inflation must manage.
Each connection reports a real present economic cause through its owning event.
Receiving a value does not transfer ownership of that event's private state, expiry, rewards or political outcomes.
A cause already represented in the external-pressure calculation cannot be charged again under a connection label.

### Industrial Boom, Event 034

Add +5 external pressure only while Event 034 confirms realized overheating in the same country.
An active boom flag, the presence of a temporary bonus or a future landing date is insufficient by itself.
The owner decides whether its current economy actually meets the overheating condition and withdraws the contribution when that condition ends.
The +5 belongs inside the capped external-pressure total.

The current Event 034 supplier adapters describe depressed-market relationships between distinct countries.
Their supplier restrictions and Overheating checks are not a general same-country macroeconomic query.
Inflation therefore requires a new narrow Event 034-owned same-country context export.
It asks whether the host is currently overheated and returns the bounded pressure contribution without changing Overheating or consuming a boom landing receipt.
This distinction follows the [inspected Event 034 adapter ownership](100_inflation_research_and_bibliography.md).

When a boom cools, remove its Inflation contribution on the next valid economic review.
Do not reset the index, refund an Inflation programme or claim that the country has recovered.
If the boom's own hard landing opens Event 035, Event 035 owns that receipt and opening consequence.
Inflation then reads the resulting real contraction through the demand family.
A later deflation request cannot charge the original hard landing again as a new independent loss.

### Great Depression 2.0, Event 035

Forty continuous days in severe deflation create the opportunity for a depression handoff.
At the actual request point, the current index must still be -60 or below.
An excursion above -60 immediately interrupts that forty-day interval, even between reviews.
Past hyperinflation does not excuse the new prolonged contraction.
The request records the actual Inflation source country, source episode, economic lineage and uninterrupted severe-deflation proof.

There is one immutable depression request per Inflation lineage and at most one accepted start or deepening from it.
Splitting the country, changing the government or crossing the threshold again cannot create a second request identity.
A request may wait for real capacity or admission to become available.
If a previously blocked request becomes admissible, revalidate the same immutable receipt.
If prices left severe deflation while it waited, a new forty-day uninterrupted qualifying interval is required before that same request can proceed.
Invalid source proof cannot be cured by relabeling the caller.

The current great_depression_start_or_deepen gateway does not admit Event 100 as a caller.
Its existing lanes are owner-specific and include Event 034 collapse and Event 035 contexts.
The connection requires an explicit Event 035-owned Inflation caller extension, with its own source-event and episode proofs, severity mapping and compatible pacing.
Inflation must not impersonate Event 034, Event 035 or a debug caller, and it must never start a depression by setting private Event 035 flags.
The [pinned gateway findings](100_inflation_research_and_bibliography.md#pinned-repository-evidence) record the source basis for this planned extension.
Revalidate the current owner contract before implementing it.

A new depression needs room under the shared crisis capacity and must pass Event 035's own active-country, receipt, economy and compatibility admission.
Inflation remains counted while those checks run.
An accepted continuation must leave shared accounting coherent for both active crises.
Deepening an existing depression uses the owner's compatible continuation rules and creates no duplicate depression instance.
Capacity rejection, incompatible active state or failed source admission leaves Inflation active with its existing index, commitments and objectives.
A failed handoff is never economic recovery.

Record successful transfer only when the owner returns an explicit accepted start, accepted deepening or accepted merge result.
An ignored duplicate, rejected request or exhausted registry is not a success.
The caller retains its immutable proof because the gateway clears public call inputs on return.
A successful request does not itself end Inflation.
The country still has to satisfy Inflation's continuous stable period while Event 035 manages its own recovery.

Event 035 can subsequently contribute verified demand contraction to Inflation's existing demand family.
It cannot automatically open another Inflation episode, renew the forty-day request, or turn receipt consumption into a fresh collapse.
Any deepening attributed to Inflation must represent prolonged deflation and its new material consequences. Exclude consequences already charged to a boom landing or depression opening.
This keeps the economic connection meaningful without a mutual escalation loop.

### British Tax, Event 081

The current legacy british_tax idea is a supported compatibility signal.
While that real burden remains, use a -5 demand contribution unless an Event 081-owned adapter establishes a distinct supply-cost effect instead.
The legacy choice reflects the actual contractionary allocation burden.
It can help offset inflation while worsening an existing deflation episode.
Its political and production sacrifices still matter.

A verified supply interpretation contributes +5 instead of -5.
Choose one causal interpretation for the actual levy, and count it once.
Do not apply both signs or charge the same input again under ordinary material shortages.
If the levy is already represented in a verified demand-contraction input, its compatibility signal adds no duplicate reduction.
All contributions remain inside the common external-pressure cap.

Event 081 owns the tax's acceptance costs, duration, diplomatic consequences and inherited presence.
Inflation reads the active burden and does not remove it during success, political transfer or civil-war setup.
A later native expiry or owner-approved tax settlement removes the relevant pressure at the next review.
The [pinned tax source and idea](100_inflation_research_and_bibliography.md#pinned-repository-evidence) record the source basis for this legacy route, while a later tax rework can replace it through its explicit owner adapter.

## Chaos consequences and gameplay acceptance

Use a small lineage-owned economic award ledger.
Generic war, civil-war, ideology and annexation systems remain owners of their own consequences.
The following anchors count material economic damage and its eventual containment.

| Actual outcome | Event 100 Chaos | Exact receipt and limit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| First sustained extreme economic rupture | +5 | Thirty cumulative extreme days with the corresponding real economic penalties applied, once per lineage across either polarity. |
| Genuine recovery of an awarded rupture | -5 | First full recovery of that lineage with outstanding obligations settled or in their paid wind-down. Never exceed Event 100's own previous positive award. |
| Evolution entry, report display, ultimatum issue, duplicate firing or inherited copy | 0 | These update capabilities, information or ownership without an additional material-loss award. |
| Political transfer, civil-war start or allied intervention | 0 | Shared political and war systems retain their own accounting. |
| Cross-event pressure input or gateway request | 0 | The realized consequence belongs to the verified source event or receiving gateway. |

Positive Event 100 awards are capped at +5 per lineage. Political failure adds no second economic award merely for changing the government. The conditional currency-shock aftermath also adds no separate Chaos award.
The containment reversal is capped at -5 once per lineage, so mild repeat farming cannot lower global Chaos.
A switch from hyperinflation to severe deflation does not open a second rupture allowance.
Country splitting, annexation, delayed callbacks and load replay must retain these consumed receipts.

Acceptance cases must cover pre-fire gates immediately below and at each threshold, active entry with settings changes, no immediate unannounced political expiry, control renewal with outstanding rebound, settlement completion under old and queued stances, and a shock that interrupts stability on the same day.
They must also prove a disabled earlier tier does not remove an upper-tier recovery route, a successor cannot replay awards, later genuine failures remain possible after reprieve and fresh exposure, and actual recovery consumes the reversal once.
