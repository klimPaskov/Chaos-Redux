# Super-Event Research Prompt

Create the research and production handoff for Event 051 Evolution III, The Scorched World.

Use separate context-complete assignments for `chaosx_super_event_text_researcher` and `chaosx_super_event_audio_researcher`.

Read:

- `AGENTS.md`
- `chaos-redux-super-events`
- `docs/specs/051_heat_wave_specs/18_evolution_iii_super_event.md`
- current super-event registry, localisation, sound definitions, image GFX, event docs, and canonical music catalogue

## Text research

Find at least five verified quote candidates about severe sun, drought, burning land, heat, or habitability loss.

Research directions include:

- Revelation 16:8-9 in a usable verified text
- Dante, *Inferno*, Canto XIV, in a public-domain translation
- public-domain scripture, poetry, travel writing, or military memoir

For every candidate record exact text, author or work, source, date, rights, attribution confidence, fit, and risks. Recommend one short quote. Do not invent or misattribute.

Research short button-reaction directions separately. Keep modern copyrighted references very short and use them only when tone fits.

## Audio research

Inspect existing tracks first and prevent accidental reuse.

Find several structured musical recordings with severe, restrained pacing. Check composition and recording rights separately. Prefer public domain or clear Creative Commons sources. Final edited length should normally be one to two minutes.

Reject drones, test tones, generated audio, sound-effect beds, unclear licensing, and copyrighted film or game music.

Download and preserve the selected source, prepare the final WAV, record hashes and editing steps, and write sound ID and wrapper handoff. Do not edit event or sound-definition files unless scope is explicitly expanded.

## Final handoff

Write or update:

`docs/super_events/051_heat_wave_super_event_research.md`

Include title direction, description direction, button direction, selected quote and source, image crosswalk, selected audio and rights, duration, paths, proposed IDs, and unresolved items.
