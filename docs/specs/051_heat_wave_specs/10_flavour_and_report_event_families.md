# Flavour and Report Event Families

## Purpose

Event 51 needs frequent evidence that the crisis is changing ordinary life. Reports should be selected from real local conditions, use compact cooldowns, and avoid becoming disconnected decorative popups.

A report event should normally do at least one of these:

- explain why a state changed band
- reveal a system under pressure
- show the result of a player priority
- present a tradeoff or local request
- mark a mission success, partial success, or failure
- show a world milestone
- establish recovery

## Selection rules

Each report candidate receives weight from:

- Local Heat Stress band and trend
- dominant local pressure
- state population and role
- current national priority
- active war and front status
- completed or failed decisions
- evolution stage
- incident cooldown
- whether the report has already appeared for this country or episode

The same family can fire in several countries, but each country should not receive repeated copies within a short period.

Reports aimed at the human player should be rate-limited. AI countries can process hidden or compact effects without every flavour popup appearing globally.

## World reaction reports

Global reports should mark real thresholds:

- first continent with several Extreme populated states
- first country to declare national water rationing
- first major cross-border relief route
- first heat-triggered Famine request
- first major displacement cohort
- first permanent environmental degradation
- first desertification
- first Scorched capital
- first cross-continental near-uninhabitable footprint
- full end of the global episode

These reports should use named countries and regions where safe. They should not fire for hidden internal point changes.

## Report options

Most report events should have one acknowledgement option and apply their condition-linked result automatically. Reports that present a real policy choice can have two or three options, but each option must change a priority, cost, mission, or outcome.

Do not create fake choices between one good option and one obviously reckless option unless the reckless choice supports a meaningful high-risk strategy.

## Tone

Baseline reports should focus on physical conditions, changed routine, maintenance, water, fatigue, and difficult choices.

Evolution I reports should be factual and restrained around death.

Evolution II reports should show lasting physical change through soil, vegetation, wells, migration, and repair failure.

Evolution III reports should show places becoming difficult to occupy without relying on generic end-of-world language.

## Cooldowns and repetition

- Per-state report cooldown should normally exceed 30 days.
- A country should receive no more than one ordinary Heat Wave flavour report within a short configurable interval.
- Severe mission failure and evolution milestones may bypass the ordinary flavour cooldown.
- A family that fired for a country should receive lower weight for the rest of the episode.
- Recovery reports should not repeat for the same restored system.
