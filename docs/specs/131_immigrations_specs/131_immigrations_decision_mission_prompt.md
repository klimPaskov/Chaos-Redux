## Payment, timing, and rejection

Preserve native law purchases and operational resource payments. Do not add an event-specific Political Power charge to physical migration actions. Resolve actual current prices from the owner, including route-dependent transport requirements. The price shown, the affordability check, the custom cost trigger, and actual debit must agree, with exact-cost affordability allowed.

Before an immediate mutation, revalidate the actor, target, current control, episode, cohort or request generation, route, capacity, and available resources. A delayed project retains its actual payer, action, state, generation, due date, and paid receipt. A later quote must not overwrite an earlier paid obligation.

Preparation must represent a real service improvement. An unchanged service does not consume another successful expansion payment. Rejected paid setup follows the matching receipt's refund policy. Refunds must not erase the cost of a transfer that already happened. Authority-loss cleanup neither grants the success reward nor consumes an unrelated receipt.

Host consent, a service purchase, and actual passage are separate operations. Pending, rejected, and expired requests preserve the exact unmoved shared obligation. A retained prospective contract that requires full headroom must not resize silently to fit a host. Preserve the neutral transfer primitive's residual-restoration behavior within the contracts that legitimately use it.

## Recovery and AI

Keep valid recovery actions usable during overload. Further admissions may need new headroom, while existing people still need distribution, integration, relief, resettlement, or return. A single global condition that disables every operation during a crisis fails this requirement.

Provide AI access through the same effect and trigger contracts. Use the existing decision path for native AI missions. Preference follows actual bottlenecks, current law, transport, and capacity. Ideology and relations can adjust preferences only after validity. Shared humanitarian hazard rules remain authoritative.

Preserve sparse pending-action processing. Use the existing active registry and relevant lifecycle hooks. Do not add recurring world-country or world-state scans for this event.
