# Event 41 Chaos impact map

## Design rule

Disease in Divisions contributes modest event-owned Chaos because it is a Low repeatable member. The event adds Chaos when the military epidemic proves materially destabilizing. Shared systems keep ownership of their ordinary sources.

Evolution activation gives zero Chaos. Event selection gives zero Chaos. Routine weekly pressure movement gives zero Chaos.

The implementation should cap event-owned positive Chaos from one episode generation so a repeatable event cannot farm the campaign through the same spread chain.

Recommended episode-generation cap:

- positive event-owned Chaos cap: 8
- negative event-owned Chaos cap: 3

The exact values remain centrally tunable. The relative order and repeat guards are mandatory.

## Anti-farming rules

- A primary outbreak gives first-manifestation Chaos once even when it creates several reports.
- Secondary national outbreaks do not each repeat first-manifestation Chaos. Their first international consequence is already covered by the cross-border milestone.
- Army-crisis Chaos requires sustained high pressure and cannot fire again after pressure falls and rises inside the same national episode.
- Country count milestones use episode generation and active full outbreaks.
- Civilian spillover counts only accepted adapter receipts.
- Strengthening the same civilian outbreak repeatedly does not repeat the first spillover Chaos.
- Resolution reductions require a sustained no-spread window.
- A containment reduction never exceeds event-owned positive Chaos already generated by the same episode.
- Repeat firings use a new episode generation and can earn new milestones, subject to the normal repeatable event weight decline and recent-survivor resistance.

## Shared-source boundary

Event 41 must not duplicate:

- Chaos from the underlying war
- Chaos from military or civilian deaths
- Chaos from Air Cleanliness changes
- Chaos from chemical or biological weapon use
- Chaos from state destruction, annexation, or puppeting
- Chaos from the Diseases cluster firing itself

The event-owned map covers the military epidemic's abnormal manifestation, military spread, civilian boundary crossing, international scope, and durable containment.

## History direction

Each event-owned Chaos change should enter the shared Chaos History path with a concise source direction that identifies:

- Disease in Divisions as the owning event
- the affected country or source episode where supported
- the milestone category
- the magnitude

The history entry should not expose internal generation IDs or raw pressure formulas.
