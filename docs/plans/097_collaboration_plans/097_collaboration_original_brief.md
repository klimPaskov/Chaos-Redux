# Collaboration

## Catalog entry

- Event ID: `097`
- Event name: Collaboration
- Type: Minor Repeatable
- Status: To Be Reworked
- Chaos level: 1
- Cluster: Intelligence
- Cluster role: Medium member

## Core idea

> Collaboration movements suddenly appear everywhere. Every country gains collaboration against every other country.

The effect is global and symmetrical.

Every valid country gains a prepared collaboration network inside every other valid country.

This makes wars across the entire world easier to finish.

Countries can capitulate earlier because their enemies already have collaborators inside them.

When enemy territory is occupied, the occupier receives much higher compliance because a local collaboration network is already prepared to cooperate.

## Baseline

Every country gains one layer of collaboration against every other country.

The collaboration uses the normal collaboration-government mechanics.

It should affect the normal benefits associated with collaboration, especially:

- lower enemy surrender thresholds
- higher compliance after occupation
- easier administration of conquered territory
- stronger foundations for later collaboration governments

The event does not care about ideology, alliances, relations, or historical plausibility.

Democracies can have collaborators prepared inside other democracies.

Faction members can have collaborators prepared inside each other.

Bitter enemies can each have collaboration networks inside the other.

The entire world suddenly becomes much easier to conquer.

## Repeat behavior

Event 097 is repeatable.

Each later firing deepens the existing collaboration networks.

A country that already has collaboration prepared against another country gains more.

This means repeated firings gradually push the world toward increasingly powerful collaboration networks.

The values stack only as far as the normal collaboration system meaningfully allows.

The event therefore becomes more dangerous naturally without needing a separate permanent mechanic.

# Evolutions

## Evolution I: Deep Networks

- Chaos requirement: `200+`.

The collaboration created by each firing becomes stronger.

Networks are no longer small groups of sympathizers.

They include larger sections of:

- local government
- administration
- industry
- police
- political organizations
- military bureaucracy

Countries become noticeably easier to occupy and hold after defeat.

## Evolution II: Administrations in Waiting

- Chaos requirement: `400+`.

Collaboration networks begin preparing complete replacement administrations before conquest happens.

When territory is captured, local collaborators can immediately begin running occupied areas for the invader.

Compliance becomes much stronger in territory where collaboration was deeply established.

A successful invasion increasingly feels as though part of the defeated country's state apparatus was already waiting for the new ruler.

## Evolution III: The Fifth Column

- Chaos requirement: `600+`.

Collaboration networks become openly active when their country begins losing a war.

As defeat approaches, collaborators can undermine resistance from inside.

Government officials can stop cooperating with the war effort.

Local authorities can begin preparing for the victor.

Military and political resistance to capitulation becomes weaker.

The effect should become strongest in countries already close to defeat.

## Evolution IV: Collaboration Governments

- Chaos requirement: `800+`.

The networks are now capable of forming actual governments.

When a country is defeated by an enemy with strong collaboration inside it, the victor can establish a collaboration government much more easily.

Instead of every conquest ending in direct occupation, large parts of the world can become controlled through collaborator regimes.

These governments remain tied to the power that helped install them.

Because Event 097 affects everyone, different powers can build competing networks of collaboration governments across the world.

## Connections

### Occupation revolt

Event `095 - Occupation revolt` creates the opposite pressure.

Collaboration makes occupied populations easier to govern.

Occupation revolt represents national resistance trying to overthrow foreign rule.

A collaboration government can therefore become the target of a later national restoration uprising if enough opposition survives.

### Treaty of Tordesillas Restoration

If Spain and Portugal are pursuing their restored world partition, Collaboration can make their conquests much easier to consolidate.

Their expanding spheres can increasingly rely on local collaborator governments instead of direct occupation everywhere.

## Design identity

The event should remain easy to understand:

> Everyone has collaborators everywhere.

The first firing makes conquest easier.

Repeated firings make collaboration stronger.

Later evolutions allow those networks to become increasingly organized until they can effectively govern conquered countries themselves.

There is no separate Collaboration currency or large event-specific management system.

The normal collaboration mechanic is the event.
