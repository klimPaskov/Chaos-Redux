## Findings and implementation

On the supplied fresh-USA run, Italy–Ethiopia was the only war and the major branch was locked, so the Settings preflight returned without a White Peace message.
The event-owned failure callback sends one `.6` explanation to the original human requester and suppresses duplicate generic feedback and false dispatch history.
Its reason is stored on that country before the report, so later global preparation cannot change the displayed explanation.
The base entry reuses the Settings-selected pair, revalidates it, settles before notification, and leaves all visible options as acknowledgements.

The parent accepted a safety restriction to isolated one-on-one wars: neither endpoint may have another enemy, and faction, subject, protected, special-crisis, capital, and surrender safeguards remain.
Several settlements are several separate safe wars rather than several pairs extracted from one crowded war.
Majors are available through enabled Stage II and its existing war-pressure conditions; the great-power restriction remains at baseline.
Disabled evolution checkboxes cannot be bypassed by stale unlock flags.
Circular requires three separate wars in one broad chain; the redundant five-pair criterion is retired.

Temporary stage, branch-weight, selection, array, and loop-success outputs are owned by their callers before helper return.
Both human endpoints of each successful pair join a transient notification queue; after the chain, every affected human gets one actual-count/actual-major aggregate report.
An AI-only chain sends one World News instead.
Entry and final dispatch clear the queue, and the per-invocation human marker is a flag rather than a numeric boolean.

No Winner uses a carried partner and continuous 180-day independence checks.
Capitulation, annexation, and subject/release transitions invalidate the watch through narrow callbacks.
The 181-day cooldown/watch lease prevents re-arming before the day-180 callback.

Changed gameplay surfaces are the owned events, effects, triggers, constants, on-actions, scripted localization, English localization, news GFX definition, and new news DDS.
