# Event 005 UWR And KMB Follow-Up Package Resolution

Date: 2026-08-09

## Unconventional Warfare Republic

UWR has a fourteen-focus specialist tree, a visible successor decision category, route-aware AI, exact-state prisoner intake, facility overclocking, native-release preparation, and ownership-sensitive aftermath policy.

The seven decision actions are:

- `uwr_overclock_blacksite_network`
- `uwr_expand_experiment_camp_registry`
- `uwr_authorize_field_release_raids`
- `uwr_intake_prisoner_transports`
- `uwr_dismantle_captured_blacksite`
- `uwr_secure_captured_blacksite`
- `uwr_exploit_captured_blacksite`

The field-release decision prepares payload, equipment, and doctrine but does not fabricate a biological release. Successful native strategic raid, battlefield, or sabotage dispatches call the Event 005 aftermath marker only when the exact native actor is UWR and the exact native release dispatch succeeded.

UWR-built facilities and experiment camps mark their exact state as blacksite aftermath sites. A non-UWR owner and controller can then choose one of three mutually exclusive state-targeted policies. Dismantlement removes Event 005 facilities and uses the shared camp dismantlement ledger. Secure containment grants controlled breakthrough progress and stability. Exploitation grants larger breakthrough and army-experience gains at a stability cost. The Event 005 marker clears after resolution without deleting an active native biological episode.

The final five-design UWR ideology flag family is installed at normal, medium, and small sizes with source, previews, hashes, contact sheets, and manifests.

## Kuznetsk Mining Board

KMB has a sixteen-focus resource-sovereignty tree, eleven decision actions, route-aware production and aggression AI, coal-golem production and field formations, foreign industrial competition, explicit concession targeting, and three mutually exclusive conquered-basin outcomes.

The completed follow-up actions are:

- `kmb_force_mining_concession` is a country-targeted decision whose `FROM` scope is the exact eligible neighbor chosen by the player or AI.
- `kmb_accept_machine_tool_bid` and `kmb_accept_armaments_bid` are mutually exclusive foreign tender outcomes with centralized convoy, political, factory, equipment, resource-rights, and crisis-pressure values.
- `kmb_integrate_conquered_basin` applies direct board rule to states 570, 571, 572, and 578.
- `kmb_establish_concession_authority` creates a KHA concession puppet in the conquered basin, returns state 569 to KMB, sets the KHA capital in state 570, applies the normal Event 005 breakaway setup, and grants KMB resource rights.
- `kmb_establish_resource_corridor` develops the conquered basin as a claimed, fortified extraction corridor without direct integration.

All three basin outcomes share one policy-chosen lock, so no country can combine their cores, puppet, and corridor rewards. Every cost and reward uses Event 005 script constants or existing dynamic helpers.
