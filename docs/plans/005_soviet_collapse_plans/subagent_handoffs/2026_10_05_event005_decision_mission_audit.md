## Priority findings

1. **Superseded localization snapshot: cost-hover wording.** During the original audit snapshot, three changed tooltips still quoted removed fees and eleven added hover keys omitted the payment quote; that snapshot evidence is retained under “Cost, requirement, and payment audit” below. The final localization handoff supersedes those defects: it covers the 12 changed cost surfaces and 11 added hover keys, with the Black Soil and Last Harvest hovers shared across both sets, for 21 unique keys total. The handoff reports full icon-first payment quotes followed by requirements or effects, with no more than three icons per quote (`docs/plans/005_soviet_collapse_plans/subagent_handoffs/2026_10_05_event005_report_news_prose_patch.md:476-491`). A current-source scan of the 21 unique tooltip definitions confirms each contains one to three icon-first spendable values, with any requirement or effect on a separate line. No cost-hover localization defect remains in this bounded audit.

2. **The mission pool permits eight simultaneous active objectives.** The Soviet category contains eight clickable crisis-action definitions, plus a nonclickable risk display: `restore_party_control` at decision line 51, `send_loyalist_officers` at 121, `cut_rebel_supply_routes` at 177, `broker_provisional_command_compact` at 239, `accept_emergency_corridor_board` at 288, `offer_interim_republic_statute` at 338, `emergency_mobilize_reserves` at 388, and `declare_union_territory_recovered` at 485. Several rows have phase, objective, or terminal gates, so source-only counts do not prove eight are simultaneously visible. The objective refill nevertheless uses `active_cap = 8` at `common/script_constants/005_soviet_collapse_constants.txt:891`, with alternate caps of 6, 6, and 7 at lines 892–894. This exceeds the three-mission cognitive-load guideline and has no source-side rationale or prioritization display.

3. **`soviet_collapse_soviet_mission_005_count_the_missing_trains` may complete without a logistical action.** The mission at `common/decisions/005_soviet_collapse_decisions.txt:656` becomes successful when the Soviet Union already has more than the train and fuel thresholds, controls one of the listed corridor hubs, and keeps depot vulnerability below its threshold. The trigger is at `common/scripted_triggers/005_soviet_collapse_triggers.txt:5815`; the localized requirement at `localisation/english/005_soviet_collapse_l_english.yml:9962` names the Moscow–Smolensk–Minsk corridor. The mission is nonselectable and auto-completes when `available` becomes true, so it may complete immediately on activation without the player repairing, dispatching, or guarding anything. Success lowers depot vulnerability and command-obedience loss; timeout increases the chance of “Depots Choose Flags”; cancellation when the selected rail project becomes invalid queues a refill. It has a 120-day timer. Its `partial_tt` key at localisation line 9964 has no source consumer in the repository.

4. **The release-risk display exposes numbers without a useful threshold or immediate response.** The disabled display decision is `soviet_collapse_review_republic_release_risk` at decision line 15882. Its description at localisation line 2812 shows release pressure, the count of republics under strain, accumulated pressure, and a four-state status. The states at localisation lines 2813–2816 are only “Open,” “Accumulated pressure ready,” “Waiting for release pressure,” and “Closed.” The description names pressure contributors but does not show how close a threshold is or which available action reduces the risk.

5. **Settings invalid-country feedback remains unverified in the decision scope.** The resolved-breakaway cleanup at `common/scripted_effects/005_soviet_collapse_effects.txt:5329` resets selected-target desks and removes the resolved country from the target array. The generic `chaosx_sovcollapse_decision_unavailable_target_invalid` string at localisation line 7728 has no source reference in the scoped 005 decisions, categories, effects, or triggers. Those files contain no Settings decision or setting-specific country-invalid message. If “Settings” means disabling successor countries, this bounded decision audit cannot prove that each entry retargets or explains its invalidation; the event/country owner should verify the associated setting entries.

## Mission quality and cleanup

All 118 SOV objective missions are nonselectable and effect-activated through the existing refill system. All 118 have timeout effects and distinct completion/timeout handling. There are 21 mission cancel triggers and 21 cancel effects; the corridor objectives cancel and refill if their live selected project becomes invalid. `soviet_collapse_prune_invalid_active_soviet_objectives` at effect line 27704 removes missions when their objective band is no longer eligible, and `soviet_collapse_queue_objective_refill` at effect line 29821 activates the next eligible mission. Individual missions have one-time `*_done` flags to prevent repeat completion farming.

For contrast, `soviet_collapse_soviet_mission_001_confirm_the_emergency_chain_of_command` at decision line 517 has a concrete unit-placement requirement across Moscow, Arkhangelsk, Leningrad, and Pskov, and the mission lasts 95 days. Its requirement helper starts at trigger line 5755. Success grants Army Experience/manpower and resolves command pressure; timeout applies failed-command pressure. The mission set has varied 95–365 day timers. Mission identifiers are unique, but a complete semantic duplicate comparison across all 118 objective definitions was not repeated for this release audit.

The mission objectives have no per-mission `ai_will_do`; they are activated through the shared Soviet refill effect. The 216 ordinary decision definitions retain their AI weights and route gates. The dated probability handoff at `docs/plans/005_soviet_collapse_plans/subagent_handoffs/2026_08_09_event005_probability_completion.md` records 211 decision candidates and 422 decision scenario-candidates with no unresolved inputs at that historical snapshot, plus five mission candidates and ten mission scenario-candidates. That evidence predates the current cost patch and does not compare current availability or AI rankings.
