## Result classification

The decision AI adapter reports raw willingness scores and eligibility, but no normalized selection probability or timing distribution. Every decision evaluation below is score-only. Do not describe a score or rank as the chance that the AI will click a decision.

Inspect reported poolComplete=false for every decision source. The supplied candidatePool included every currently indexed active candidate from each source inspect, but MCP still marks the wider selection pool incomplete. External factors and scripted conditions are also incomplete.

The scenario adapter accepts only id, actor, flat primitive state, flags, and scopes with id/state. It does not bind many Clausewitz variables, arrays, compound triggers, or typed campaign state. Results with unresolved inputs are diagnostic probes, not campaign balance proof.
