# Nine-state dated direct-control sensitivity

Disposition: implemented as a partial source-model sensitivity; realistic full-period controller and receipt acceptance remains unresolved.
Acceptance basis: the user requested historically grounded dated control scenarios, measured losses and a comparison with the wider Holocaust record without converting historical totals into casualty quotas.

`test_staged_direct_control_state_sensitivity` in [`test_historical_envelopes.py`](test_historical_envelopes.py) selects nine installed European starting states from the package's 21-origin inventory. It apportions each original country's existing 1933 ceiling over **all** of its initially owned European states by installed starting population, then applies the unchanged 2.5% monthly German campaign fraction only during an explicit staged direct-control window. Each state keeps its own origin and original German responsibility, reports one actual modeled physical receipt per active month, and closes without later deaths or population restoration. It introduces no gameplay rate, quota, transfer, killing-center attribution or shared-core change.

These month windows deliberately use full-month ticks, exclude the initial occupation month before the package's June 1941 radical-campaign gate, and stop before uncertain partial liberation months. Poland's 1945 liberation and Kraków's January 1945 change support ending both Polish rows in December 1944. [USHMM's 1945 key dates](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/1945-key-dates) and [Auschwitz chronology](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/auschwitz-key-dates) support that boundary. [USHMM's Netherlands account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/the-netherlands) documents May 1940 German occupation, and [Dutch liberation testimony](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/gallery/the-netherlands-stories) supports a May 1945 end in the Holland sensitivity. [USHMM's Belgium account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/belgium) dates Allied liberation to September 1944. [USHMM's 1944 key dates](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/1944-key-dates) date Paris's liberation to August 1944. [USHMM's eastern-front account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/the-soviet-union-and-the-eastern-front) places the Soviet summer offensive in June 1944; the Minsk row conservatively stops before that offensive rather than claiming a precisely dated state controller.

The Kaunas window stops a full month before the documented July 1944 liberation, the Riga window before its 13 October 1944 liberation, and the Central Macedonia window before the German autumn evacuation of Greece. [USHMM's Kovno account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/kovno), [Riga account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/riga), and [Greece account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/greece) establish those regional bounds. The installed `731-Central Macedonia.txt` identifies victory-point province 11818, localized as Thessaloniki in vanilla `victory_points_l_english.yml`. The result does not count all deaths documented for those cities, including deportation and murder at killing centers, as local campaign-cause deaths.

The original snapshot ran within a nine-test historical-envelope suite and stored its rows under `staged_nine_state_direct_control_sensitivity` in [`historical_envelope_results.json`](historical_envelope_results.json). Its nine rows yield 354 separate monthly campaign receipts; their 909,411 allocated people equal 589,297 measured modeled campaign losses plus 320,114 survivors. No custody, arrival, forced-labor, institutional or unobserved territorial death was imported into that occupation cause. The [approximately six-million Holocaust total](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/the-final-solution) includes many victims and methods outside these nine selected states and this single cause, so subtracting 589,297 from it is a coverage observation, not a target for increasing a script rate. This sensitivity is also **not additive** to the separate state-87 Łódź transfer envelope, which starts from part of the same Polish source.

The allocation itself has a material historical limitation. It spreads each 1933 country ceiling by general state population, which assigns 20,497 modeled Latvian source people to Riga even though the [USHMM Riga account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/riga) describes roughly 40,000 Jews in that city before the war and at least 25,000 murdered in the Rumbula shootings alone. The state-808 sensitivity records only 12,850 local campaign losses; its modeled source is already below that one documented massacre. Likewise, Central Macedonia receives 13,461 modeled Greek source people while the [USHMM Greece account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/greece) records about 43,000 Jews in Salonika, over 40,000 murdered, chiefly after deportation to Auschwitz-Birkenau. These city populations and deaths are not directly interchangeable with the mod's wider state rows or its single local occupation cause, but the direction and scale of the discrepancy show that raising a monthly mortality rate would conceal a geographic-allocation and omitted-movement problem. Kaunas's 68,470 modeled state source also cannot be equated to the 30,000 people [USHMM counts in the Kovno ghetto](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/kovno), because the state is wider and the dates and custody categories differ. A historically credible full matrix needs sourced state-origin allocation and documented destination movement before any rate calibration.

Remaining evidence: a dated controller for every package-seeded state, mandate and site-payment history, changing state population and outside territorial receipts, additional documented origin-preserving movements, later custody and institution receipts, and a combined origin/cause conservation ledger through aftermath. The current result is a staged partial direct-control scenario, not a live game run, a historical reconstruction, or completion of the wider-loss acceptance gate.

The later [supplementary control sensitivity](supplementary_direct_control_sensitivity_2026-09-27.md) adds four disjoint Norway/Czech/Serbia state windows while preserving this nine-state row result byte-for-byte. The current historical-envelope suite has eleven tests. It separately rejects a Denmark no-rescue calculation as implausible and does not use its modeled deaths in the staged subtotal.
