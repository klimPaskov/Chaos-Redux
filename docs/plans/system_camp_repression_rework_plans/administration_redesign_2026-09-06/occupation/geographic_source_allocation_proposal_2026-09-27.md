# Geographic source-allocation decision

Disposition: accepted limited two-state source-anchor refinement, implemented in source; dated movement and historical calibration remain incomplete.
Acceptance basis: on 2026-10-03 the parent selected the sourced refinement under the user's current instruction to take recommended choices without waiting, within the approved wider-campaign reconciliation.
This is a current parent decision and makes no claim that an earlier direct human reply exists.
The [USHMM Riga account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/riga) and [Greece account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/greece) were rechecked on that date and support the approximately 40,000 and 43,000 prewar city populations used as finite source anchors.
These populations are not casualty targets, and source-city and game-state boundaries remain an explicit approximation.
`camp_occ_seed_origin` now reserves an eligible anchor from the unchanged national ceiling before proportionally distributing the remainder, with one-shot inputs supplied only by the Latvian and Greek setup branches.
The earlier diagnostic below describes the pre-choice proportional snapshot and does not override this disposition.

Current `camp_occ_seed_origin` in `common/scripted_effects/camp_administration_occupation_effects.txt` lines 1185–1232 allocates each finite national 1933 ceiling across all initially owned European states by general state population. It preserves the ceiling and does not infer deaths from it. The unchanged 2.5% monthly campaign rate then applies only to registered controlled shares. This is a documented approximation, not a historical state-level source census.

The [USHMM Riga account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/riga) records roughly 40,000 Jews in prewar Riga and at least 25,000 murdered in the Rumbula shootings. The current state-808 allocation is 20,497 source people and yields 12,850 modeled local campaign losses in its staged window. The [USHMM Greece account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/greece) records around 43,000 Jews in Salonika, over 40,000 murdered, most after deportation to Auschwitz-Birkenau. The current state-731 allocation is 13,461 and yields 8,434 local campaign losses. These numbers are not interchangeable casualty targets: city and game-state borders differ, national ceiling dates differ, and deportation deaths belong at destinations. They do establish that general-population proportional seeding cannot represent these concentrated origins faithfully.

If the user accepts the refinement, keep the national Latvian 95,600 and Greek 73,000 source ceilings unchanged. Reserve up to 40,000 source people for owned European state 808 and up to 43,000 for owned European state 731 at one-time seed; clamp each to the available national remainder. Apportion only each country's remaining source among its other initially owned European states by their present general population. If the anchor state is not owned and European at seed time, reserve nothing and apply the existing proportional rule to all eligible states. No person is added beyond the national ceiling. No death, casualty quota, protected-group selector, transport or current-controller assumption is introduced by the anchor. The country-specific constants and state IDs would be documented in the occupation effect contract; all other origins continue unchanged.

The engine-facing implementation would require a two-pass bounded `every_owned_state` seed, one pair of shared script constants, and no recurring world hook. Validation would assert national conservation for both anchors, the no-anchor fallback when the state is unavailable, unchanged totals for the other 19 origins, no population or Deaths change at seed, historical source/date limits, and matched before/after dated scenario totals. The full source and MCP map checks would be rerun. The three new archival comparisons and the possible accepted-design deviation would be added to the package documentation and completion report. This still would not solve unlabelled outside losses, missing destination movement, all-European controller coverage or engine receipt proof.

The parent selected the limited sourced allocation on 2026-10-03 under the current user authorization.
The archived nine-state results remain evidence of the older proportional baseline and must not be relabelled as post-anchor calibration.

The later [Danish negative control](supplementary_direct_control_sensitivity_2026-09-27.md) exposes a different defect: an early generic occupation campaign without the documented 1943 rescue movement produces implausible Danish modeled losses. This Riga/Salonika source-allocation choice does not decide Danish policy timing or authorize a Denmark–Sweden movement adapter; those remain separate historical and exact-receipt work.
