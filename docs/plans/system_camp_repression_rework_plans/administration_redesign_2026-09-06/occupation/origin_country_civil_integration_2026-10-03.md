## Programme and final-grant contract

The state predicate `camp_admin_country_program_site_ready` checks operational authority, fresh reconciled custody plus co-located occupation provenance, original responsible country and actual living custody.
Only an identity-valid positive completed engineer-release receipt can substitute for now-zero living custody at the saved selected host.
The `PREV` country is retained through bounded site-array selection and saved-site scopes; global-pulse ROOT is not adopted as the responsible authority.
`camp_admin_country_has_proven_program_site` supplies the availability gate without imposing national provenance on an unrelated programme.

`camp_admin_country_start_input_valid` validates the supplied programme enum against that exact programme's historical and authority gate.
`camp_admin_country_start_program` rejects an occupied institution slot or pending engineer receipt, chooses a proven eligible site, validates fixed Auschwitz/Pingfang overrides and consumes the temporary input.
Invalid selection grants no programme and clears the transient saved site.
Japanese transport still requires its existing Chinese industrial host predicate; no geography or eligibility balance target was chosen.

`camp_admin_country_tick_institution` observes the saved host before payment or progress.
`camp_admin_country_finish_program` observes the saved host again before its dispatcher.
All nine `camp_admin_country_award_program_<program>` variants require their own exact programme enum plus `camp_admin_country_program_completion_ready` before any award.
That trigger repeats current selected-site authority/provenance, paid-work completion, local support, mandate, spending, category availability, active slot and annual limit.
Wrong-programme, incomplete, unsupported, captured or uncertain direct calls grant nothing.
Existing fixed bonus strength, single use, per-programme completion flags, category no-stacking and two-per-calendar-year guards remain in force.
Deaths cannot advance completion or supply award strength.

An unrelated uncertain occupation-only host blocks national reform but does not block an otherwise proven selected programme or its valid award.
Uncertainty at the selected host in either custody or occupation does block that programme.
`camp_admin_country_halt` remains independently reachable and preserves saved work; it does not assert survivor release.

## Engineer release receipt and cleanup

`camp_admin_country_award_program_sov_engineer_release` requests the finite saved engineering subset through the actual foundation `camp_admin_release_detainees` API before requesting a research award.
Only measured positive `camp_admin_release_actual_k` produces a pending receipt.
Zero/rejected release produces no research award, annual-count increment, completion flag or success flag.
This is an in-place membership release and creates neither state population nor military manpower.

Persistent receipt fields are `camp_admin_country_engineer_receipt_k`, `_state_id`, `_authority`, `_program`, `_progress` and `_target`, under `camp_admin_country_engineer_release_pending`.
`camp_admin_country_engineer_receipt_identity_valid` requires a positive quantity, original country, exact current programme, selected state, unchanged completed progress and target.
The historical `camp_admin_country_last_engineers_released_k` remains separately retained.
The receipt is a completed release receipt, not a labelled outside-loss restoration contract.
It never clears host ambiguity or changes cohort origins.

After release the award repeats identity and final readiness checks.
If category contention, the annual ceiling or capture appears at this boundary, the receipt remains pending.
Pending monthly retry attempts only the final award: there is no second release or payment and no substitute host selection.
The same site may have zero held custody after releasing every proved engineer; valid zero-held completion relies on its own completed receipt rather than inventing new detainees.
The accepted award clears the pending receipt and finite engineering subset while retaining historical release and ordinary programme bookkeeping.

`camp_admin_country_clear_engineer_pending_receipt` is a country effect with no inputs, no defaults and no external scope pointer.
It clears the pending flag and all six receipt fields, retaining historical release.
Current callers are the accepted engineer award, gated `camp_admin_country_reform` and intentional programme abandonment through `camp_admin_country_start_oversight` before it changes programme/state.
Ordinary halt deliberately preserves the receipt for valid same-site resumption.
Parent-owned terminal abandonment should call this helper when the programme is deliberately discarded; it must retain independent uncertain-origin rows, reasons and history.
No global event target or recovery selector is introduced.
