## ImageGen prompts and processing

The built-in ImageGen tool was used in native edit mode. No CLI or API fallback was used. Each first-pass prompt identified Image 1 as the vanilla Decisions style reference, Image 2 as the vanilla Occupied Territories style reference, and Image 3 as the sole authority for that route’s Chaos Redux text, values, hierarchy, and control count. Each asked for an opaque, flat, full-window HOI4 scripted-GUI design reference at 900×540, with dark charcoal and steel surfaces, restrained warm-metal trim, readable type, and no added gameplay claims, objects, prices, icons, map thumbnails, portraits, people, watermarks, or extra controls.

Administration prompt content was the current Administration crop’s title and tabs; four summary pairs 20% OUTPUT, 200 THOUSAND, 130 THOUSAND, and 1.24 THOUSAND; the Industrial Production, Intensified Coercion, and Controlled Development selectors; the Economic Development panel with Industrial contract work and Brandenburg; the Institutional Program panel with No institutional program authorized; and the two displayed actions. It explicitly preserved the synthetic-fixture caveat and red monthly-deaths value.

Locations prompt content was the current Locations crop’s title and tabs; All Locations; its six visible roster entries and role labels; the selected Brandenburg panel with every displayed role, contribution, custody, workforce, support, monthly and cumulative death, capacity, and maintenance-status value; and the four displayed actions. It explicitly prohibited a close button because the supplied crop has none, and prohibited map thumbnails and additional actions.

Records prompt content was the current Records crop’s title and tabs; the Human Consequences table’s three headings and six cause rows with their displayed values; Original Responsible Authority and Germany; the retention sentence; and exactly one Institutional Records item, Detention site established / 5/1943 / Kraków. It prohibited additional history rows, dates, losses, locations, values, and a close button.

Maintenance-shortage prompt content was the current shortage crop’s title and tabs; the same four summary pairs and three selectors visible in that crop; the gold heading SUPPLIES FALL SHORT OF EXISTING OBLIGATIONS; Country support ratio / 54%; New development / Deferred; the displayed maintenance-support sentence; and the two displayed actions. It explicitly prohibited costs, prices, extra counts, new actions, or an invented supply breakdown.

The first Administration and shortage renders introduced triangle glyphs and separate end compartments in the selector buttons, which the source crops do not contain. Each therefore received a focused built-in precise-object edit: remove only those three glyphs and narrow compartments, extend the existing button face through the space, and preserve all other text, values, controls, and layout. Final source PNGs are the resulting edited renders listed above.

Generated source images measured 1619×971 pixels, except the shortage source at 1620×971. Processing center-cropped the horizontal excess to 1618×971 where needed and resized with Pillow Lanczos to 900×540. Outputs remain opaque RGB PNGs.
