# Killing-role locked-state reference

Acceptance basis: the user's requirement for distinct detention, forced-labor and killing-center roles, contextual costs and blocked reasons, and separate functional native states.
The [current-source 1920×1080 baseline](../mcp/after/picker_current_source_1920.png) shows `Convert to Killing Center` highlighted under the fixture's `Intensified Coercion` mandate even though `camp_admin_can_change_selected_role` blocks that click until the separately paid radical mandate is enacted.
The intended corrected reference keeps the 900×540 picker layout, four role locations, labels and Back control exactly as shown, but displays that bottom-right role option with the same dark, non-clickable native treatment as other unavailable actions whenever its gameplay availability trigger is false.
The active option remains visibly selectable only when the same trigger that enables its click succeeds.

| Region | Native source elements | State contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bottom-right role option at picker-local x434–832, y219–267 | `camp_admin_option_role_killing` plus the mandate, site, existing-level, missing-level, funds and residual locked native variants | Exactly one is visible under eligible German or other authorized radical-route identity. Each locked variant says `Killing Center Unavailable`, has no click effect and owns a context-specific tooltip. |
| Blocked reason and quote | `camp_admin_option_role_killing_tt` and six `camp_admin_option_role_killing_*_tt` keys | Each visible native state names its current blocker or its valid conversion, the political-power quote where a conversion can proceed, and the loss of productive work and accountability consequences. |
| Other role options | Existing separate buttons | Their positions and behavior remain as in the baseline. |

This is a scoped correction to the generated role-picker visual state, not a new bitmap control, new icon, or gameplay authorization route. An initial scripted-localisation approach timed out the connected picker inspection; it was removed and replaced with direct native state-specific tooltip keys.
