# National administration redesign

Status: accepted and in implementation.
Acceptance basis: the user explicitly requested implementation of the complete Camps, Forced Labor and Repression plan on 2026-09-06.
This document records the task boundary and integration contract; current evidence and unresolved work belong in completion.md.

## Accepted behavior

- National economic priority, repression mandate and spending ceiling govern automatic administration.
- Automatic maintenance, eligible development and previously authorized institutional programs remain quiet.
- Economic and institutional projects occupy separate slots; both require continuing support and valid authority.
- Surviving allocated workforce, supported capacity and relevant opportunity determine output.
- Maximum developed contributions are 40 percent equivalent participating industrial output, capped at 25 percent nationally, 40 percent relevant state construction and 40 percent local extraction.
- Institutional projects award 50–100 percent limited-use research bonuses, at most twice per year, with no stacking exploit.
- All payments, commitments, reserves, quotes, bounds and durations use centralized subsystem tuning.
- Costs belong in tooltips; no permanent button-price grids or programmer-facing state labels.
- The interface has administration, locations and records views, native controls, reference images before implementation and ongoing MCP preview review.
- Finite affected populations determine real civilian losses through the existing shared accounting API.
- Record the actual accepted loss exactly once, separating monthly/cumulative values, location, cause and responsible authority.
- China totals represent combined occupation losses; experimentation is only one distinct cause.
- Historical Holocaust calibration covers the wider murder campaign rather than only camp deaths.
- Germany, Japan, USSR and Britain/Raj receive deep distinct institutions; existing USA, France/Vichy, Italy and Belgium/Congo kits remain distinct and are revised.
- Historical content spans 1936–1948 and aftermath; Mengele's Auschwitz branch is hidden before 1943-05-30 and requires the appropriate regime and controlled institution.
- Research abstraction and historical departures are documented here, never as player-facing fictionality notes or spoilers.
- No protected-group selector, casualty-score reward or technical experimental/weapon-development procedure is introduced.
- Reform releases survivors and replaces economic dependence without restoring deaths or deleting built infrastructure.
