# Soviet famine crisis cutover and terminal-state audit

Disposition: implemented narrow terminal-state and exact-quote corrections; administration-native famine redesign remains unresolved.
Acceptance basis: the user's accepted administration contract preserves distinct Soviet famine, paranoia, relief, evidence and lasting consequences while retiring duplicate routine missions and purchases. The read-only `chaosx_decision_mission_auditor` supplied the bounded source findings; the parent reviewed and applied the safety fixes.

## Current route

`sov_emergency_famine_relief` remains a paid decision after `camp_admin_initialized` whenever famine pressure is positive. It starts the distinct 180-day `sov_emergency_famine_relief_mission`; migration cancels the routine `sov_famine_pressure_cycle` but does not cancel this crisis mission. The bounded Soviet monthly bridge still updates famine pressure and can fire `soviet_gulag.1` warning and `.2` crisis events after cutover. `sov_admit_local_administrative_collapse` remains a paid state decision during the crisis or reform route. The repeatable map decision `sov_conceal_famine_mortality` remains pre-cutover only; `.1.b` and `.2.b` preserve one-time concealment choices in the threshold events. Restoring the repeatable decision after cutover would add another paid state entry without a separately accepted need, so this patch does not do that.

The audited events also retain unconditional alternatives: `.1.a` and `.2.a` relief and `.3.a` NKVD reinforcement. Adding a terminal flag to the other options therefore cannot leave those events without an option. The acute relief event's equipment consumption has no separate affordability trigger and is **not** proven to handle an empty stockpile safely; gating the only unconditional crisis option or inventing a weaker relief fallback needs a reviewed design. The relief decision's slot-2 cooldown flag has no mapped action ID, but its active mission and relief flag prevent overlapping starts; a failed or cancelled mission permits another paid attempt. These remain explicit limitations, not success claims.

## Applied source fix

- `common/decisions/camp_repression_major_country_decisions.txt`: conceal and admission target lists now require the same current responsible control as their final dispatcher. Per-frame `available` rechecks control and the target's own terminal flag, preventing a stale daily target list from offering a repeat paid action. Concealment remains hidden after administration initialization. Relief and concealment equipment/manpower/command-power requirements use `NOT (< quoted constant)`, so stock exactly equal to the displayed cost is affordable without unsupported `>=` or duplicated cost-minus-one values. `custom_cost_trigger` and `available` use the same requirement expression.
- `common/scripted_effects/camp_repression_action_dispatcher_effects.txt` and `common/scripted_effects/camp_repression_major_country_effects.txt`: the dispatch and each action-state, event-state, `FROM`, and current-state effect recheck `camp_rework_soviet_records_concealed` or `genocide_site_discovered` before debit, record mutation or country benefit. Repeating a processed target cannot take another non-PP resource debit or award its country effects through these helpers.
- `events/soviet_gulag.txt`: `.1.b` and `.2.b` hide concealment when that state was already concealed; `.2.c` and `.3.b` hide admission after discovery. The base crisis alternatives remain present.
- `localisation/english/camp_repression_requirement_flags_l_english.yml` defines the player-visible concealed flag requirement. `localisation/english/camp_repression_country_kits_l_english.yml` supplies specific consequence tooltips for the concealment and admission decisions. Concealment's public-report action leaves measured death records intact; admission documents the site and reduces famine/corruption while raising visibility and reform pressure. Both files retain UTF-8 BOM.

The affected route identifiers are `sov_emergency_famine_relief`, `sov_conceal_famine_mortality`, `sov_admit_local_administrative_collapse`, `sov_emergency_famine_relief_mission`, and `soviet_gulag.1/.2/.3`. No AI weight, scenario rate, population-loss helper, shared Chaos Meter source, event log, or spreadsheet fact changed. The workbook needs no update because the event choices and outcomes already existed; this patch changes stale availability and clarifies existing decision consequences.
