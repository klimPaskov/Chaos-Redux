## Historical evidence

The US Holocaust Memorial Museum's [Greece page](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/greece) describes the 1943 Salonika deportations and the later German deportations from mainland Greece; its [1943 key dates](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/1943-key-dates) gives 15 March as the first Salonika transport date. Yad Vashem's [Athens transport record](https://collections.yadvashem.org/en/deportations/10967781) documents the 24 March 1944 roundup deception and the transport's 2 April departure. The US Holocaust Memorial Museum's [Rome article](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/rome) gives the mid-October timing; its [French Rome article](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/fr/article/rome) dates the start to 16 October.
