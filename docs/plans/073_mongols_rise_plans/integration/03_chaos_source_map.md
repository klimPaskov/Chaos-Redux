# Chaos source map

## Baseline and distinction

The uploaded mechanics reference already assigns Chaos to war, peace, annexation, puppeting, liberation, faction changes, world tension, military growth, deaths, and other shared conditions. Event 073 must not manually reproduce those generic charges.

Event-owned additions represent the abnormal mounted restoration becoming a stronger political and military system. They use one-time achievements with bounded scaling. They do not multiply directly with current Chaos, pay per state every day, or fire merely because an Evolution becomes eligible.

Numbers below are untested tuning anchors. Round authored values to multiples of five. Use the smallest applicable footprint band that describes the distinct event-owned consequence. The design requires positive and negative lifecycle coverage, not these exact untested constants.

## Negative outcomes and timing

Fragmentation may increase political instability while later containment reduces the special imperial threat. These are different outcomes. Do not pay both from the same unqualified breakup action. Containment requires a sustained result after the breakup or a meaningful halted expansion. A normal peace treaty does not automatically qualify for an additional containment reward.

A peaceful succession without a real preceding crisis preserves stability but need not generate a negative Chaos transaction. C11 requires a genuine severe crisis and durable resolution.

## Transaction ownership

Each source owns a record for actor, occurrence, supporting milestone, footprint band, amount, and reason. A source emits through the existing shared Chaos logging interface after the concrete outcome commits. Multiple notifications do not multiply the amount.

C05 and C06 require a coalescing rule for the same settlement. C01 and an active Evolution addition require inspection of shared military-growth accounting. A tier increase itself remains zero. A new physical entitlement may qualify only through a distinct reviewed consequence, never by relabeling the unlock.

## Required proof

Test refused entry, accepted entry, forced test entry, first settlement, a repeated capture, first khanate delivery, a disconnected khanate, multi-region growth, a European settlement, universal proclamation, a failed small restoration, continental fragmentation, durable containment, succession recovery, save reload, and multiplayer duplicate delivery.

For every case, record generic sources separately from event-owned additions. The source coverage and overlap checks are release gates. A positive opening transaction alone does not satisfy lifecycle coverage.
