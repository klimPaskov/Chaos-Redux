## Parent-authored expansion

The two-meter implementation, staged warning and recovery phases, clean human refusal, narrow campaign capacity, four click-cost types, detailed tribute contracts, sixteen achievements, three super-event moments, and numerical test anchors are authored elaborations under the request to expand the idea. They were not independently approved in a later user message.

The main files incorporate these elaborations as the proposed design baseline. Further changes should update the owning section and this record instead of accumulating contradictory addenda.
