# Event 073 super-event research and production prompt

Follow the current chaos-redux-super-events skill. Read `docs/specs/073_mongols_rise_specs/presentation/01_events_and_super_events.md` and the Event 073 asset manifest. Assign the provided text and audio researcher roles within their limited reading contracts. Neither role was executed during preparation of this planning package.

Complete three presentations. SE-A marks a functioning restored imperial center after a governing settlement and substantial achievement. SE-B marks the completed Universal Khan political order, not merely Chaos 600 or an Evolution unlock. SE-C marks the breakup of a previously substantial continental empire into meaningful successors, not the failure of a tiny opening army.

For each presentation, research the final title, description, exact quotation, attribution, translation, meaningful cultural remark, audio source, license, cut, and image direction. Keep the twentieth-century alternate campaign distinct from documented medieval history. A ruler’s propaganda is not automatically objective narration.

Use verified real sources and preserve enough evidence to check wording and attribution. Do not invent a famous saying, copy an unverified quotation site, or turn an achievement label, asset name, route label or planning heading into final super-event text. Respect quotation and copyright limits. An exact source passage may support a short verified quote, but it does not authorize copying an entire protected work.

Research real recordings appropriate to each moment. Confirm the rights of the composition and the particular recording, redistribution, editing, attribution, and intended cut. Use the owning skill’s normal one-to-two-minute duration and do not exceed 120 seconds without an explicit approved exception. The shorter owning-skill rule resolves the supplied audio profile’s broader duration wording.

Coordinate the final image and any required animation with the asset roles after text and source review. Require a native static fallback, correct final dimensions, actual consumer wiring, one-time triggers, and runtime playback. SE-C should communicate political separation and successor survival without substituting graphic spectacle for the actual outcome.

Unresearched titles, buttons, quotes, cultural references, slogans, lyric fragments, allusions, uncertain audio rights, and missing final assets are blockers. Return source-verified final content only where the research supports it. Otherwise return a narrow unresolved item and the work required to close it. No usable quote or licensed track was preselected in this package.
