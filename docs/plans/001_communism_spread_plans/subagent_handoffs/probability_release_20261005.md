## Source values and weighted-logic findings

`random_list` is probability-proportional sampling after normalizing eligible nonnegative entry weights. Its entry weights do not have to sum to 100. Source constants and report-receipt flags are raw weights and eligibility inputs; they are not probabilities by themselves.

The spread roll at effect line 1414 has entries `var:communism_state_spread_chance` and `var:communism_state_no_spread_chance`. The source constructs the second as the complement of the first. The escalation roll at line 1552 has the same complementary pattern. The source formula includes state count, controlled-state share, support, stability, war, and non-AI pressure; the successful evaluator scenarios did not compute those preceding effects, and the attempted raw-weight scenarios did not produce results.

The sabotage helper at effect line 1624 runs only when its cooldown is inactive, an eligible communist-controlled state exists, and at least one of the industry/strike reports remains unreported. The source inner report selector sets an already-reported incident weight to zero, sets its receipt flag before queuing the report event, and does not call the selector when both report flags are set. This supports the one-report-per-type design at source level; probability mass and target validity were not analyzer-confirmed.

The surprise, ritual, and whisper variant helpers set their receipt flags before scheduling their report popups. Their local weights become zero after the matching event ID is reported. `has_unreported_communist_surprise_revolt_variant`, `has_unreported_communist_worker_ritual_variant`, and `has_unreported_communist_world_revolution_whisper_variant` are OR gates in `common/scripted_triggers/001_communism_spread_triggers.txt:74-98`; an exhausted family retains zero family chance in the parent selector. The parent no-incident entry is the complement of active family weights. This source structure avoids repeated reports and prevents an exhausted family from consuming selection probability, assuming the helper gates are called as shown. It does not prove runtime callback order or live event delivery.

The source comparison shows the Event `.2`, `.3`, and `.8` `ai_chance` blocks are unchanged between the frozen baseline and the audit-snapshot event file; no engine-level option-share comparison completed. The Event001 decision scores remain score values, not probability-proportional shares. `communism_national_counter_agitation` has base 1 plus two additive conditions; local intervention levels have bases 1, 2, and 3 with state-level additions for the selected target and war/stability factors; emergency has base 0 with conditional additions and war/stability factors. The inspected pre-release and current blocks retain those score values. The release changes the PP hints from national 35 to 65, removes fixed 50-point hints from the three local actions, and removes the emergency 100-point hint. The only current fixed PP hint is national 65. These hint changes affect resource-saving behavior, not the `ai_will_do` raw score formula, and their AI selection effect remains unmeasured.

Current emergency constants are CP base 25, +2 per counted state, +3 per Level 3 state, with a 60 CP cap; the PP, manpower, and infantry-equipment costs remain dynamic and have no equivalent MCP evaluation here. The helper caps counted states at 10; the Level 3 increment and the actual custom-cost price quote need to be included when building the exact candidate-validity scenario. In the pre-parent audit snapshot, Event `.2.b` spent the global CP10 price without an affordability trigger. Its AI weight depends on war and stability, not command power. The parent has added the inclusive CP10 trigger in the current tree; whether it removes `.b` only below the quote remains unverified until the post-change compare returns.
