## Change and rationale

Before the change, the panel told players to select a marked state even when the controlled-state total was zero. This was observable in the zero-count USA fixture; the decision category remains visible while the spread idea is present, even without controlled states. The display now swaps that prompt for `No states are currently marked for intervention.` at exactly zero and keeps the existing prompt when the total is positive. The row keeps the same bounds, font, tooltip binding, and composition.
