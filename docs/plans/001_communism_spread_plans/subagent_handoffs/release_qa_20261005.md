The duplicate failed-intervention support award is resolved: the old outcome added the configured 1.5 percentage points in its helper and again in `.7`. It now applies 1.5 points once per failure. Stability costs remain per outcome. Success outcomes range from 0.5 to 2 points by progress/control level; failures lose 1 point in the first two levels and 3 at Lockdown.

A country can receive at most one each of `.4`, `.5`, `.6`, `.7`, `.8`, `.9`, and `.10`, one `.2` industry incident, one `.34` strike incident, and one of each of the eleven rare variants `.14/.18/.19`, `.15/.27/.28/.29`, and `.16/.30/.31/.32` during a crisis. State spread/escalation and intervention consequences still apply whenever eligible. Once both sabotage/strike types have occurred, further sabotage/strike rolls stop. Each rare family selects among unseen variants until it is exhausted. The normal source-level ceiling is 25 distinct visible Event 001 IDs per country crisis, excluding forced shell `.3` and hidden timer events; this is a source count, not a live-campaign count. Event 001 defines no `news_event`. The root's `communism_spread` flag protects its one-time global fan-out; evolution stages are global one-time transitions.
