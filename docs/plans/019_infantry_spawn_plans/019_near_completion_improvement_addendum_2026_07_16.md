## Finding 1: Natural releases need separate claimant and family modes

### Live evidence

`infantry_spawn_resolve_due_claimant_crisis` in `common/scripted_effects/019_infantry_spawn_claimant_effects.txt` sends every non-microstate claimant crisis to `infantry_spawn_begin_selected_claimant_natural_revolt`.

That entry point prepares the natural derivative transaction in `common/scripted_effects/019_infantry_spawn_derivative_package_effects.txt`. Its preflight requires a valid selected claimant and calls `infantry_spawn_select_natural_derivative_revolt_candidate`.

The selector in `common/scripted_effects/019_infantry_spawn_pressure_effects.txt` iterates registered anomalous-family rows. Its base-safety trigger in `common/scripted_triggers/019_infantry_spawn_triggers.txt` requires Evolution IV.

This creates two contradictions with the accepted design:

1. An Evolution III claimant crisis can mature before Evolution IV, but every multi-state ordinary claimant revolt fails its release preflight until Evolution IV is active.
2. A registered family can satisfy the designed weak-central-control branch for a natural breach, but there is no claimant-independent caller that can start that family release. The implemented `weak control OR claimant influence` eligibility therefore behaves as though the claimant branch were mandatory.

An ordinary claimant with real loyal Event 019 formations must not depend on an unrelated zombie, ghost, or golem family being present. A family host with enough presence and pressure under weak central control must not need a fabricated claimant.

### Historical and narrative grounding

The claimant route should express divided command. Historical cases such as soldiers' committees, mutinous garrisons, revolutionary guards, emergency levies, and late-war territorial militias became politically dangerous when command loyalty separated from state loyalty. The Event 019 version should turn the claimant headquarters and the actual formations that recognize it into the breakaway's territorial core.

The family route is different. A zombie, ghost, or golem host is an institution and military ecology becoming territorially autonomous. Its release should grow from accumulated host presence, saturation, and loss of central control. It should not borrow a human claimant merely to satisfy an implementation precondition.

Keeping those causal stories separate makes the same exact-formation transaction support two readable political outcomes without duplicating derivative-country content.

### Required mechanic structure

Introduce a centralized release-mode value backed by Event 019 script constants. At minimum it needs these semantic modes:

- ordinary claimant release
- anomalous claimant release
- claimant-independent family breach

Store the selected mode in the transaction context and prove it before any source deletion. Do not encode a second family registry, a static family list, or a fixed derivative tag.

#### Ordinary claimant release

- Require Evolution III, a valid selected male claimant, a multi-state source country, and at least one exact active Event 019 formation that meets the existing claimant-loyalty rules.
- Permit `family_id = none`. Do not require Evolution IV, a selected registry row, or a provider callback.
- Select the origin and connected region from stable claimant evidence, including the claimant headquarters and exact loyal-unit origins. Do not choose a random unrelated state.
- Transfer exactly the selected claimant-loyal recorded formations through the approved recreate, prove, then delete transaction.
- Construct the dynamic actor as a human claimant breakaway. Preserve the fixed claimant identity and army-scene sprite mapping.
- If no exact eligible formation exists, do not manufacture a substitute formation. Resolve the crisis through the existing visible failed-coup outcome and clean or cool down the crisis state.

#### Anomalous claimant release

- Require Evolution IV, a valid claimant, and a complete eligible registry row.
- Preserve the intended union rules for exact claimant-loyal and selected-family formations.
- Use the registered provider profile, family classifier, family history, and derivative route without hardcoded family dispatch.
- Keep the claimant's influence as the political cause and the anomalous host as the military character of the new actor.

#### Claimant-independent family breach

- Require Evolution IV, an eligible registered family, sufficient exact family presence, the accepted pressure and saturation gates, and weak central control.
- Do not require or fabricate a claimant.
- Select territory from exact family formation origins and the connected-region rules already used by the release transaction.
- Transfer only the exact rows belonging to the selected family.
- Use the provider identity, council or family leadership profile, nonhuman classifier, and family derivative history.
- For a one-state source country, use the verified same-tag provider-takeover contract. Do not create a zero-state dynamic actor.

### Transaction and failure rules

- Generalize the existing preflight and commit helpers rather than creating a second lossy transfer path.
- Freeze the release mode, claimant UID when present, family registry index when present, source rows, target region, and transaction nonce before actor creation.
- Make proof mode-aware. An ordinary claimant proof must not demand a family row. A family-breach proof must not demand a claimant UID.
- Advance source and global ledgers only after target formation proof and source deletion both succeed.
- On a pre-commit failure, restore the source snapshot and expose a visible failed-coup, containment, or deferred-breach consequence that matches the selected mode.
- On an unprovable post-creation state, keep the existing lock and quarantine guarantees. Do not continue progression and do not silently accept a partial transfer.
- One country may own only one active Event 019 release transaction nonce at a time.

### AI, balance, exploits, isolation, and performance

- Player and AI must use the same release eligibility and exact-formation transaction.
- Claimant AI evaluation should use actual loyal formation value, claimant influence, territorial continuity, containment capacity, and war risk.
- Family-breach AI evaluation should use family presence, pressure, saturation, central-control strength, neighboring exposure, and the ability to contain the host.
- Keep all tuning in the existing Event 019 constant families or new subsystem script constants. Do not introduce numeric magic values in the caller chain.
- Use the existing country-local scheduled pulse or an action-triggered call after pressure recalculation. Do not add a daily, weekly, or monthly all-country on-action.
- Registry iteration must remain bounded by registered providers. Exact formation scanning may occur at transaction time, not continuously.
- Scenario SCN-013 must keep its direct scenario context and must not inherit natural Evolution III, Evolution IV, claimant, pressure, or cooldown prerequisites.
- Failed and deferred releases must clear or rate-limit their warning state so the same crisis cannot fire repeatedly in one tick or remain silently mature forever.
