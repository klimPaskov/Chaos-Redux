## Approval-blocked engine contracts

### Exact recorded-formation revolt transfer

The installed engine documentation exposes country-ratio or whole-army
transfer, not division-scoped ownership transfer. The only exact-ledger
substitute is recreate-then-delete. It can preserve recorded Event 19
composition, equipment liability, generation/lot/unit identity, family or
claimant identity, and loyalty, but cannot preserve live organisation,
veterancy, medals, officer history, army assignment, or orders. The natural
claimant revolt eligibility trigger is explicitly false until the owner
approves that fallback; retained warnings are cleared without revolt or
achievement credit, and no undefined helper remains in the live call graph.
One-state takeover/failed-coup handling does not require this substitute.

### Exact same-battle achievement proof

No documented callback atomically exposes an exact Event 19 division, victory,
24-hour duration, enemy-strength ratio, and casualties inflicted in the same
battle. The exact-battle recorder therefore remains dormant with no public
caller, and the four affected achievements remain deliberately unawarded. A
controlled Event 19 combat trial would be a substitute for native combat and
requires explicit owner approval.
