## Outcome

Updated the reusable event skill so every new land sub-unit or equipment-backed special family declares one consumer disposition before implementation: standalone provider family, support attachment under an existing provider, or intentionally parent-owned and not independently consumable.

The rule requires owner-side startup registration and the complete provider callback contract where a standalone family is exposed to shared consumers.
