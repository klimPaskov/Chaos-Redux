## Processing and DDS evidence

The full generated `1522x1033` canvas was resized to `1120x760` with Pillow `Image.Resampling.LANCZOS`. Source aspect is `1.4733785092`; target aspect is `1.4736842105`, a delta of `0.0003057013`.

The repository converter was run with:

`python -B .agents/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/tools/convert_to_dds.py --input docs/assets/019_infantry_spawn/gui_background_rebuild_2026_07/processed_png/infantry_spawn_muster_board_background_1120x760.png --output gfx/interface/019_infantry_spawn/infantry_spawn_muster_board_background.dds --width 1120 --height 760`

The final DDS has `DDS ` magic, header size `124`, declared dimensions `760x1120`, pixel format `(32,65,0,32,0x00FF0000,0x0000FF00,0x000000FF,0xFF000000)`, texture caps `0x1000`, exact length `128 + 1120 * 760 * 4 = 3404928`, and alpha range `255..255`.

## Visual QA

- Source and processed previews were inspected at high detail and in the contact sheet.
- The panel has one broad uninterrupted central paper field with generous text/control negative space.
- Header treatment is a single shallow, quiet charcoal band with restrained brass edging.
- Lower treatment is a single shallow action band without button-like compartments or implied click targets.

- No decorative slots, repeated wells, button-like boxes, portraits, humans, faces, vehicles, readable generated text, pseudo-text, seals, emblems, icons, grids, filing pockets, stacked documents, or tactical map-dominant diagram are present.
