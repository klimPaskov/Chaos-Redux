## Approval-blocked engine contracts

### Exact loyal-formation ownership transfer

Natural claimant and anomalous-family revolts must transfer only recorded loyal
Event 19 formations. Installed HOI4 1.19.2 documentation and local vanilla and
approved-mod precedents expose whole-army or country-ratio transfer, not an
exact division-scoped ownership-transfer effect.

The available fallback is a prove-before-delete recreation transaction. It can
preserve recorded composition, equipment and manpower liability, generation,
lot, unit, claimant or family, and loyalty identity. It cannot preserve live
organisation, veterancy, medals, officer history, army assignment, or orders.
Because fallbacks require explicit approval, it is not implemented. The natural
revolt capability trigger remains explicitly false, retained warnings clear
without revolt or achievement credit, and no undefined helper remains. One-state
takeover and failed-coup handling remains available.

### Exact same-battle proof

No documented callback atomically exposes an exact Event 19 division, battle
victory, at least 24 hours of duration, enemy-strength ratio, and casualties
inflicted in that same battle. A controlled Event 19 combat trial could prove
those facts, but it would substitute a bespoke trial for native combat and is a
fallback. It is not implemented without explicit approval, leaving the four
achievements above hidden.
