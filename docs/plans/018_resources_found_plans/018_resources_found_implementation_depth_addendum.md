# Event 018 — Resources Found: Implementation-Depth Addendum

## Disposition

**Result: bounded implementation addendum required. Broad design expansion is closed.**

Event 018 already has the intended playable spine: a persistent exact resource ledger, repeat enrichment, field management, trade and concession routes, commission and border escalation, Evolutions I–IV, reversible closure, the DHO cave country, captured-resource capacity, regional containment, terminal world end, super-event wiring, achievements, and shared event-log/detail integration. The package does not need a fifth evolution, a second economy, another cave tag, or a new route family.

Completion is nevertheless blocked by several concrete implementation defects and proof gaps. These items deepen or finish mechanics already required by the accepted specification; they do not change the event premise.

This addendum is a planning-era improvement-loop handoff. It is no longer an active queue; the closure disposition below is authoritative. The implementation findings and requirement language later in this file remain historical traceability and must not be used to recreate deleted temporary evidence workspaces or reopen closed items.

## Must-fix implementation tranche

### RF-018-01 — Recalibrate cave opening strength against the four required bands

**Finding.** `resources_found_calculate_cave_starting_strength` begins with the all-resource ledger, then adds six points per distinct resource and four per discovery before development, deaths, failed sealing, military exploitation, or long extraction are considered. Evolution III/IV injects at least 120 units of each of the six standard resources. At current constants, the mandatory package alone contributes at least 21.6 resource score plus 36 diversity score and four discovery score, while initial safety removes only 4.8. A lightly exploited breach therefore begins around the heavy band before its actual exploitation history is counted. The 6–10 cautious/early band is effectively unreachable for an Evolution IV breach, and the maximum-30 achievement risks becoming routine.

**Implementation surfaces.** Review and tune:

- `common/script_constants/018_resources_found_constants.txt`, especially `resources_found_strength` and `resources_found_strength_factor`;
- `common/scripted_effects/018_resources_found_effects.txt`, especially `resources_found_calculate_cave_starting_strength`;
- `common/scripted_effects/018_resources_found_achievement_effects.txt` and `common/scripted_triggers/018_resources_found_achievement_triggers.txt` only if recorded thresholds change;
- DHO emergence tooltips, event text, tuning documentation, and acceptance records if player-visible wording or thresholds change.

Keep all contributors dynamic and centralized. Reduce the mandatory package/diversity floor enough that safety, evacuation, sealing, suspension, and hunts materially alter the result; make depth, developed yield, extraction duration, deaths, failed sealing, and military exploitation determine the upper bands. Preserve the smooth integer 6–30 mapping and the hard cap.

**Player effect.** A cautious owner who reaches breach despite mitigation faces a credible but containable opening, while sustained greed and failed containment produce the 25–30-division disaster the player was warned about.

**Balance and AI implications.** Cautious and exploitative owner AIs must generate visibly different emergence strength. Recheck early regional containment, prepared-major hard-attack response, DHO survival, and the difficulty of “Thirty From Below.” Do not tune only the cap case.

**Research/design rationale.** Deposit tonnage is not the same as organized military capacity. Mining depth, connected galleries, exposed labor, logistics, accumulated deaths, and deliberate continued extraction are the credible bridge from a resource field to a mobilized underground army. This is also the causal promise made by the accepted exploitation design.

**Acceptance impact.** Blocks the starting-strength, cave-balance, AI-behavior, and achievement acceptance sections. Record at least these deterministic profiles: minimal qualifying breach 6–10; developed and dangerous 11–18; heavily exploited 19–24; extreme/repeated/military exploitation 25–30. Include boundary profiles showing that one small input change does not jump multiple bands.

### RF-018-02 — Make controlled resource denial change activation and captured capacity

**Finding.** `resources_found_prepare_cave_resource_denial` sets `resources_found_cave_denial_capacity_penalty` and the prepared-denial flags. Anchor activation consumes the prepared flag and delays the timer, but the capacity penalty is never read anywhere. The defined `resources_found_denied_resource_state` dynamic modifier is never applied. Localisation promises slower activation and damaged local output, and the decision matrix promises lower capture yield; two of those three effects are currently no-ops.

**Implementation surfaces.** Update:

- `common/scripted_effects/018_resources_found_decision_effects.txt` for preparation, one-shot consumption, and cleanup;
- `common/scripted_effects/018_resources_found_cave_effects.txt` and/or `common/scripted_effects/018_resources_found_effects.txt` where activation capacity is finalized;
- `common/script_constants/018_resources_found_constants.txt` for denial duration and capacity reduction;
- `common/dynamic_modifiers/018_resources_found_state_modifiers.txt` and the matching GFX/icon registration;
- relevant decision triggers, state cleanup/restoration effects, localisation, and mechanic documentation.

Apply the denial modifier when demolition completes. On successful anchor activation, subtract the prepared penalty from the calculated captured capacity and clamp at zero. Consume the capacity variable and activation-delay flag exactly once. Define whether the output scar lasts through liberation cleanup or post-cave restoration, then clear it through the chosen existing lifecycle. Do not mutate the Event 018 raw resource ledger in a way that breaks exact closure.

**Player effect.** A threatened owner gains real, costly pre-loss counterplay: local production is damaged now, but a captured rich state feeds fewer broods and takes longer to connect.

**Balance and AI implications.** Ordinary AI denial weights become meaningful. DHO target selection should devalue denied states only by the actual reduced capacity, not ignore them forever. Recheck zero-capacity, cap-10, recapture-before-activation, and repeated-loss cases.

**Research/design rationale.** Mine demolition, machinery evacuation, shaft collapse, and well denial historically traded immediate production for delayed enemy exploitation. The mechanic should model damaged usable infrastructure, not erase geological deposits.

**Acceptance impact.** Blocks anti-cave response, capacity, state modifier, tooltip, cleanup, and AI acceptance. Proof must show activation delay, visible state damage, reduced capacity, single consumption, correct clamp, and deterministic cleanup.

### RF-018-03 — Give the five zero-effect DHO focuses real route mechanics

**Finding.** The following focuses set a flag that is not consumed anywhere else and call `resources_found_cave_refresh_template_access`, which only sets another unused marker:

- `DHO_interlocking_carapaces`;
- `DHO_resist_the_great_guns`;
- `DHO_urban_cellar_networks`;
- `DHO_split_the_great_broods`;
- `DHO_lighter_plates`.

They therefore complete without changing a mechanic, template, decision, anchor, map objective, or AI behavior, contrary to the focus-tree source specification. Other focuses that immediately select a rich or transport target do satisfy the map-objective requirement and should not be expanded merely because their unlock flag is currently unused.

**Implementation surfaces.** Update:

- `common/national_focus/018_resources_found_cave_focus_tree.txt`;
- the relevant DHO unit/subunit, idea, dynamic modifier, scripted effect/trigger, and decision files;
- `common/script_constants/018_resources_found_constants.txt` for route tuning;
- DHO AI strategy/weights, focus localisation/tooltips, focus icons if the visual metaphor changes, and focus-tree documentation.

Use the existing three-doctrine structure rather than adding branches. Interlocking Carapaces and Resist the Great Guns should produce a Stone-route defensive/anti-piercing response with a real tradeoff. Urban Cellar Networks should improve an existing prepared urban or transport-burrow action. Split the Great Broods and Lighter Plates should change Scree formation mix, movement, spawn use, or capacity efficiency with an armor/defense cost. Prefer existing ideas/templates/decisions; add a new helper only when the behavior cannot be expressed cleanly through an existing one.

**Player effect.** Completing a 7–14 day doctrine focus changes how the cave player fights rather than only acknowledging that the focus finished.

**Balance and AI implications.** Stone must remain slow and vulnerable to concentrated prepared hard attack; Scree must gain mobility or coverage without becoming the best armored route. DHO AI should use the unlocked behavior and select targets appropriate to its doctrine. Check that template changes affect already spawned and future broods in the intended way.

**Research/design rationale.** Interlocked armor, anti-gun adaptation, cellar infiltration, formation splitting, and lighter protection imply distinct battlefield methods. Doctrine is credible and legible when it changes force employment and exposes a counter, not when it grants an inert flag.

**Acceptance impact.** Blocks focus reward, route identity, template/decision integration, AI usage, localisation accuracy, and the mandatory focus-tree audit.

### RF-018-04 — Record each evolution once in the shared chronology

**Finding.** Each `resources_found_record_evolution_*_from_field` helper calls `record_events_log_evolution_entry` without a global tier guard. That shared helper always prepends a new row. Repeatable fields and later breaches can therefore add multiple Evolution I–IV rows even though the chronology is defined as one global milestone per tier. Field-specific progression should remain in the field record/history payload.

**Implementation surfaces.** Update:

- `common/scripted_effects/018_resources_found_log_effects.txt`;
- shared log localisation or documentation only if the display contract changes;
- cleanup/reset logic only if the shared event framework explicitly resets evolution chronology between scenarios.

Add one global, event-specific recorded flag or equivalent stable ledger entry for each tier. The first qualifying field supplies the actor/state context; later fields update their own state history but do not append another global row.

**Player effect.** The Evolutions tab presents a clean four-stage Event 018 chronology rather than a list flooded by repeat fields.

**Balance and AI implications.** None mechanically. Avoid a guard that accidentally suppresses field evolution effects; gate only the shared log append.

**Research/design rationale.** The event catalog records global evolution milestones, while individual deposits are separate physical records. Keeping those histories distinct makes the system understandable and preserves chronology semantics.

**Acceptance impact.** Blocks event-log/evolution-log acceptance. Test first and later fields for every enabled tier and confirm exactly one shared row for I, II, III, and IV.

### RF-018-05 — Tighten global-defeat severity and repair the reconstruction lifecycle

**Finding.** `resources_found_cave_global_history` is set at the 75% origin-continent milestone. Defeat then becomes globally eligible if that flag, world end, or any world-end foothold exists. This allows a single-continent campaign to receive the global defeat super-event and world-order aftermath without the required global/costly/duration evidence. In addition, Event .97 grants `resources_found_global_reconstruction_eligible` to every human and immediately fires Event .99. Event .99 grants join/lead/refuse flags before cleanup is complete. The later “Join Reconstruction Compact” project sets membership and fires .99 again, so the choice can be duplicated or contradict a prior refusal.

**Implementation surfaces.** Update:

- `common/scripted_effects/018_resources_found_cave_effects.txt` for severity evidence and defeat classification;
- `common/script_constants/018_resources_found_constants.txt` for minimum duration/scale thresholds;
- `common/scripted_triggers/018_resources_found_decision_triggers.txt` and `common/decisions/018_resources_found_decisions.txt` for survivor eligibility and cleanup gates;
- `common/scripted_effects/018_resources_found_decision_effects.txt` for compact completion;
- `events/018_random_resource.txt`, especially .97–.99;
- global-defeat/reconstruction localisation, achievement evidence, aftermath documentation, and super-event acceptance records.

Use explicit evidence for global or near-global impact. A robust gate should combine world-end history or cross-continent footholds with a centralized minimum campaign duration and/or durable scale/loss evidence. A 75% origin-continent milestone may remain a cave progression milestone, but it must not alone authorize the global aftermath. Also require no incompatible terminal resolution.

Choose one coherent reconstruction sequence. The source event map expects .97 to open the aftermath, .98 to present the justified global defeat, and .99 to offer eligible survivors a join/lead/refuse choice after sufficient cooperation. Fire .99 once per eligible country, block mutually exclusive repeat choices, and make the cleanup/contribution project either establish eligibility or complete a previously chosen commitment—not both. Do not fire the same choice event again on project completion.

**Player effect.** A brief regional cave war ends regionally. A genuinely global, costly campaign unlocks the memorial, cleanup, and compact once, with a choice the player can understand and trust.

**Balance and AI implications.** AI eligibility should use the same severity and contribution evidence as players. Leader weights should favor majors with actual contribution, while refusal remains final unless the design explicitly provides a separately localized reconsideration route. Ensure the global-defeat achievement cannot be earned from the 75% proxy.

**Research/design rationale.** Multinational reconstruction standards, shared doctrine, and a durable international compact are plausible after prolonged cross-border mobilization and destruction, not after a momentary regional threshold. The accepted research and super-event brief explicitly require global severity and duration.

**Acceptance impact.** Blocks regional/global defeat distinction, global-defeat super-event, reconstruction, achievement, terminal compatibility, and AI acceptance. Test regional defeat, 75%-continent defeat, full-continent pre-world-end defeat, post-foothold/world-end defeat, incompatible world end, contributor/non-contributor, major/minor, join/lead/refuse, and duplicate-event prevention.

### RF-018-06 — Finish rendered decision and mission localisation by behavior family

**Finding.** The decision file contained widespread generated boilerplate that misidentified targets and promised costs or durations for instant controls. Examples included “Commit the selected field” for field cycling and clock refresh, selected-field language for threatened-state denial and liberated-anchor cleanup, and field-based cost claims for national intelligence/cooperation actions. This is a presentation defect, not a request for more decisions.

**Implementation surfaces.** `localisation/english/018_resources_found_decisions_l_english.yml`, plus Event 018 documentation/spreadsheet fields whose wording is required to match in-game text. Keep UTF-8 with BOM. No gameplay script should change solely to preserve inaccurate prose.

Rewrite all 118 rendered decision/mission names, descriptions, and tooltips family by family: field controls; development projects; safety and investigation; extraction and closure; contracts/diplomacy; commission/border conflict; DHO anchors/doctrine/warfare; anti-cave action; aftermath. Describe the actual actor, target, persistent state, dynamic cost basis, duration, completion result, cancellation condition, and principal risk when those are visible. The nine evolution clock/reschedule missions are permanently hidden by `allowed`, `activation`, `visible`, and `available = always no`; intentionally leave them unlocalized.

**Player effect.** Decisions communicate what will happen before political power, construction, equipment, or time is committed.

**Balance and AI implications.** No script balance change. Accurate copy makes existing tradeoffs legible and prevents players from treating instant UI controls as projects.

**Research/design rationale.** A deep decision system is playable only when choices expose their material commitment and world-state consequence. Boilerplate describing implementation categories defeats the historical-administrative voice and the decision-planning contract.

**Acceptance impact.** Blocks localisation, decision tooltip/effect-description, and documentation alignment. The event-chain worker owns this active repair; re-audit the rendered set after its handoff.

## Mandatory proof gates after the fixes

These are validation work, not optional polish. They do not authorize a fallback. If the engine contradicts an accepted design assumption, stop and ask the user how to proceed.

### PG-01 — Equipmentless brood engine behavior

Spawn representative 6-, 18-, and 30-brood openings and verify that zero-manpower, no-equipment, `force_allow_recruiting = no` templates:

- spawn at intended strength and organization;
- can move, fight, take losses, and be destroyed;
- cannot be trained or queued normally;
- do not obtain free normal manpower/equipment reinforcement;
- remain constrained by protected origin allocation plus active captured capacity;
- receive and clear Unfed Broods correctly after capacity loss.

There is no confirmed vanilla precedent for a fully equipmentless combat country. If any item fails, do not quietly add hidden manpower/equipment or replace the mechanic with normal recruitment.

### PG-02 — Capacity and denial matrix

Verify state totals 0, 9, 10, 19, 20, 48, 99, 100, and 150; origin exclusion at every value; continuous-control interruption; delayed activation; denial reduction and zero clamp; 21-day capacity-loss grace; one destroyed division freeing one slot; ordinary and world-end spawn intervals; mature-anchor cleanup; and exact ledger preservation.

### PG-03 — DHO combat and AI route behavior

Run Stone, Burrow, and Scree routes against unprepared infantry, support anti-tank, dedicated anti-tank, armor/piercing, low supply, mountains, and urban terrain. Confirm each of the five repaired focuses changes observable behavior, DHO selects and defends anchors, wars on every new land neighbor, reacts to hard attack, uses route-appropriate target decisions, and does not abandon the origin chamber through `front_control` strategy behavior.

### PG-04 — Baseline/evolution/closure matrix

Run baseline-only, Evo I first-fire/repeat, Evo II careful/exploitative, Evo III accelerated opening, permanent full seal, failed full seal, later-field evolution, ownership transfer, border-war settlement, and closure restoration. Confirm one evolution row per global tier and exact removal of every Event 018 resource addition on successful full closure.

### PG-05 — Terminal and aftermath matrix

Run regional defeat, 75%-continent defeat, continent-complete defeat before world end, verified world end with footholds, global defeat after footholds, incompatible terminal state, and reconstruction eligibility/choice variants. Confirm slot 84 and Event .99 occur only under the final justified gate, Event .99 occurs once, cleanup and restoration finish, threat flags clear, and no secret cave restart remains.

### PG-06 — UI, assets, and text

Inspect the selected-field scripted GUI at supported UI scales and state transitions: baseline, active development, unsafe, suspended, closed, and cave crisis. Verify real frame animation, static fallback, no missing texture, stable sprites, accurate visible decision text, all fifteen achievement triplets, and manifest/GFX parity.

## Optional future depth

No optional gameplay expansion is recommended before this addendum closes. The prior design review correctly rejected breadth that would dilute the existing event. In particular, do not add:

- Evolution V or a hidden cave retaliation/restart;
- a second cave country or alternate monster species;
- a global commodity exchange, separate resource currency, or replacement trade system;
- a normal cave manpower/equipment economy, navy, or air-force route;
- a fourth doctrine or another hierarchy branch;
- broad historical reenactment in place of the fictional random-event identity;
- transform-only animated assets presented as final frame animation.

After every must-fix and proof gate is resolved, a later release may consider small memorial/vigilance incidents or richer per-field history display only if player feedback shows that the existing aftermath or field record is hard to read. Those ideas remain unapproved and must not delay completion.
