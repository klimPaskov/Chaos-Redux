## Behavior and visual rationale

Before the correction, the active and closed panels each showed four core rows, then could show both Disturbance and Breach at once. Event 018 sets Disturbance during Evolution II at `events/018_random_resource.txt:1854,1899,1976,2009`; the later Evolution III effect can fire `chaosx.nr18.61` at `common/scripted_effects/018_resources_found_decision_effects.txt:3063-3082`, and that event sets Breach at `events/018_random_resource.txt:2245-2253` without clearing Disturbance. No `clr_state_flag` for Disturbance or Breach was found in Event 018 source. Both optional rows therefore could coexist with the four base rows, exposing six field-mechanic values.

The panels now keep Developed Yield, Excavation Depth, and Workforce Safety in the core value box. One fourth row shows Foreign Pressure in routine/unsafe states; Subsurface Disturbance replaces it after reveal; Breach Pressure replaces both at breach. Breach wins even when the Disturbance flag remains. A suspended field falls back to Foreign Pressure under the existing rule that hides Disturbance and Breach while suspended. The same selection priority uses `resources_found_last_closed_field` in history.

The content budget is three core mechanic values plus one contextual value. The separate `Added` and `Total` resource ledger summary remains in place because it reports the Event 018 resource ledger and full state total required by the accepted spec; those two totals are not field pressure metrics. The panel has two navigation arrows and three information/display controls: Show State, Animation, and History. None changes gameplay or has a gameplay cost, so the panel has zero gameplay-changing actions, zero spendable costs, and no cost texticons. The category's decisions remain parent-owned.

The visible text stack is the existing title and state header, one compact resource-summary line, three core rows, one contextual row, and the existing status band. Source-level bounds keep labels in the left slate and art in the right medallion. Actual glyph centering, wrapping, painted-bound clearance, disabled appearance, z-order, scaling, and click-region alignment remain unverified because no production MCP render was returned.
