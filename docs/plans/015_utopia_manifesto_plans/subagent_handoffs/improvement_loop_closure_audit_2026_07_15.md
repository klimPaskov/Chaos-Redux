## 1. Repaired calling entry gate retained

The regression gate remains intact in common/script_constants/015_utopia_manifesto_constants.txt and common/scripted_effects/015_utopia_manifesto_effects.txt:

- utopia_manifesto_calling_initial_pressure supplies the centralized base of 25 plus condition-specific pressure.
- utopia_manifesto_calling_limits uses a 40 shortage entry threshold and a 30 clear threshold.
- utopia_manifesto_initialize_callings and the actor-scoped structural rebuild seed all six families from the accepted material conditions.
- utopia_manifesto_prepare_dominant_case_family selects the highest live relevant family rather than privileging Provisioning by file order.
- Necessary Ground and leased-island eligibility continue to read a live relevant deficit.
- Acceptance and chaosx.nr15.150 retain actor-scoped initialization and maintenance. No daily, weekly, or monthly world iteration was introduced.

The entry profile can therefore produce Provisioning, Workshops and Arsenal, Civic Works and Transport, Learning and Care, Maritime and Settlement, or Defense and Watches pressure according to the actor's actual condition.

## 2. Living Ledger and calling lifecycle

The live implementation separates durable policy history from replaceable current-condition contributions:

- utopia_manifesto_recalculate_live_ledger_contributions resets and rebuilds live Ledger components.
- utopia_manifesto_rebuild_ledger_totals combines centralized bases, durable movements, and the new live layer.
- utopia_manifesto_refresh_ledger rebuilds rather than repeatedly adding current conditions, making an unchanged second refresh idempotent.
- Structural, temporary, and effective calling components are rebuilt before current and uncovered severities are derived.
- utopia_manifesto_apply_prepared_ledger_delta and utopia_manifesto_apply_prepared_calling_change preserve the durable policy layer and use centralized limits.
- The 40/30 shortage hysteresis prevents a family from oscillating at the display and availability boundary.
- chaosx.nr15.150, acceptance, the manual Ledger refresh, and existing Event 15 terminals provide bounded refresh entry points.

Static scenario tracing passed the addendum's material interlocks:

- War, occupation, reserve, infrastructure, transport, industry, research, migration, housing, trade, coastline/convoy, and defensive-stock conditions can add or remove only their live component.
- Durable focus, decision, case, district, evolution, and conduct movements remain after a live refresh.
- District calling relief is state-derived and is reversed once if the chartered state ceases to be valid.
- Necessary Ground reads the current uncovered family deficit and ceases to treat a cured shortage as grounds for a case.

## 4. District suitability, proof, and ownership lifecycle

The four ordinary district roles use verified state and country proxies in common/scripted_triggers/015_utopia_manifesto_triggers.txt:

- Market-Garden: population and infrastructure plus live Need, reserve, or Provisioning pressure.
- Industrial Housing: Homes Near Work, live unresolved housing pressure, population, infrastructure, existing industry, and a free civilian-factory slot.
- Rail Junction: population and bounded infrastructure plus a railway or supply-hub connection.
- Refugee Municipality: population, infrastructure, industrial room, live migration or housing pressure, and reserve capacity.

Survey results are stored on the selected state. Build start and terminal effects use housing/settlement, transport/supply, and role-plan obligations. Full completion alone grants role and network proof; partial, delayed, refused, failed, and lost-state paths do not grant the full terminal.

Route charter completion remains one shared lifecycle with five distinct obligations and modifiers for Consent, Common Table, Guardians, Closed Island, and hidden humanist review. Provision Ring remains the separate fifth achievement role rather than being duplicated as an ordinary district button.

### Ownership and control scenario proof

The final parent-owned repair was re-audited after every correction:

1. on_state_control_changed saves the exact old founder and checks membership in that founder's utopia_manifesto_district_states array before touching Event 15 district state.
2. The existing actor pulse snapshots only the actor's tracked district array and reconciles loss of ownership, control, or core status without scanning countries.
3. utopia_manifesto_clear_exact_lost_district_project_pointer clears a country project pointer only when it resolves to the exact lost state.
4. utopia_manifesto_reverse_lost_district_calling_relief runs before the state role and charter flags are cleared, so the exact primary and secondary relief is reversed once.
5. utopia_manifesto_clear_district_state_package removes survey, suitability, active role, obligation, build, charter, Penal Works, route-modifier, and related state-variable records while leaving already constructed physical buildings on the map.
6. The state is removed from the founder's district array, then utopia_manifesto_rebuild_district_network_proof recounts only owned, controlled core states and refreshes formation and achievement proof.
7. If the valid charter count falls below the accepted network threshold, the Garden District network idea is degraded through its existing lifecycle.
8. Current Provision Ring proof is invalidated by the island-project cleanup and is preserved by ordinary district recounts when the island ring remains valid.
9. Durable charter Ledger choices and country-wide history, appeals, observations, conduct, and achievement exclusions are not erased by the loss of one state. They clear only through the accepted total terminal cleanup.

This closes both active-project loss and previously missing completed-charter ownership loss. It also prevents one Event 15 founder from clearing another founder's district package.

## 5. Penal Works and exact Deaths registration

decision_utopia_attach_penal_works_to_district is a paid Closed Island-only method attached to the existing active district lifecycle. It consumes centralized manpower, infantry-equipment, support-equipment, reserve, and civilian-population costs. utopia_manifesto_penal_works_active_state supplies construction and local-supply output together with garrison and resistance penalties.

utopia_manifesto_start_penal_works_method requests 500 civilian population loss and utopia_manifesto_complete_penal_works_method requests 1000. The shared state-scope apply_exact_state_civilian_population_loss effect clamps the transaction against the minimum remaining state population, returns the exact applied loss, and calls chaos_meter_register_deaths with constant:chaos_meter_deaths_reason.gulag_repression. The reason is localized as “From camps and forced labor.”

The method records the durable penal-labor and coercive-conduct history used by achievements and route reactions. Completion, halt, project failure, revolt, ownership/control loss, route exit, and Event 15 terminal cleanup remove the active method flag, modifier, pointer, and state variables without erasing durable conduct early or granting full output after failure.

## Audit-time corrections rechecked

The audit identified the following defects while the parent still owned the live implementation. Each was repaired in source and re-audited before this PASS:

- Industrial Housing originally allowed its unlock flag to substitute for live housing pressure. The role trigger now requires utopia_manifesto_has_live_housing_pressure, and its description states the same unresolved-housing requirement.
- District ownership cleanup originally covered active projects but could leave completed charter roles, modifiers, calling relief, counters, and proof behind. The exact state-package cleanup and bounded proof rebuild now cover completed states.
- A lost state's current-project pointer could remain stale. Exact pointer comparison now clears only the matching record.
- The state-control callback could act on a state tracked by a different Event 15 founder. The callback now checks the changed state against the exact old founder's array.
- Ordinary district proof rebuilding briefly cleared valid Provision Ring proof. Ordinary recount now leaves that independent island proof intact, while island invalidation and total cleanup clear it explicitly.
- The focused lost-state pointer cleanup briefly cleared durable district history and conduct. It now preserves those country-wide records until accepted terminal cleanup.

No regression or replacement finding remains from those repairs.

## Balance and AI disposition

- Ledger and calling values are centralized, clamped, and separated into durable and live layers.
- Calling entry and clear thresholds provide hysteresis; family selection uses current severity.
- Evolution actions charge the same centralized costs to human and AI actors, use shared validity triggers, and install timed obligations rather than free permanent rewards.
- District role selection is constrained by current suitability and one-project capacity, with AI weights tied to the same live deficits and affordability surfaces.
- Charter relief is role-specific and is lost with the qualifying state; durable political consequences remain historical.
- Penal Works has a tempting construction/supply benefit but charges manpower, equipment, reserve, civilian lives, Concord/Need consequences, garrison, resistance, conduct, and foreign-reaction risk. AI access remains Closed Island-only and affordability/risk bounded.

The implemented routes retain their intended asymmetry; this audit found no convergence toward an identical Ledger outcome.
