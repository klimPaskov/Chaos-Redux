## Review verdict

Event 15 is not shallow as a whole. The live package has a 124-focus replacement tree, five island and capital variants, a seven-type Necessary Ground case family, durable lease and association lifecycles, a developed league system, route-specific identities, five evolution deliveries, 87 Event 15 events, broad visual assets, and substantial AI and cleanup infrastructure.

The correct next pass is connective depth, not structural breadth.

No new focus branch, public meter, decision category, evolution stage, super-event, country tag, league architecture, case type, island variant, advisor family, identity family, or broad event catalog is recommended. The focus tree should remain at its current node count unless a later audit proves that an accepted mechanic cannot be completed through existing decisions, missions, events, ideas, and scripted helpers.

Completion is still blocked by four connective gaps and one historically required route interlock:

1. The Ledger records opening conditions but does not recalculate changing country conditions.
2. Calling pressure has a repaired opening seed but does not yet have a durable state-responsive lifecycle.
3. Evolution choices mostly terminate in one-time deltas and inert flags.
4. District roles are selectable without enough proof that the chosen state has the named problem.
5. Penal Works records conduct but does not power a real, costly state project.

The optional-depth queue is intentionally empty until these items are implemented or formally rejected and the accepted specifications are amended. More breadth before that point would hide missing interlocks rather than deepen play.

## Finding 1: make the Ledger and calling pressures respond to the living country

### Diagnosis

Category: disconnected and low-impact over time.

The opening Ledger reads real conditions, but later refreshes do not rebuild those contributions. The system can therefore display the memory of the recipient at acceptance rather than its present war, occupation, infrastructure, industry, reserve, subject, or territorial condition.

The new calling seed has the same lifecycle boundary. It reads real conditions during initialization, after which calling methods directly lower or clear severity. A completed method can permanently erase the symptom even when the underlying equipment, transport, housing, reserve, war, or occupation deficit still exists. Conversely, a new war or loss of infrastructure does not necessarily create new pressure.

### Bounded implementation recommendation

Implement two layers and keep them separate.

1. Durable policy layer
   - Store lasting movement caused by focuses, decisions, mission outcomes, route conduct, league choices, cases, and evolutions in dedicated persistent components.
   - Route utopia_manifesto_apply_prepared_ledger_delta through those components.
   - Do not recompute or erase this layer during a live-state refresh.

2. Live condition layer
   - Add one actor-scoped helper that recalculates named contribution variables for current war, occupation, reserve, industry, infrastructure, housing or migration, coast or convoy access, territorial loss, and other already accepted inputs.
   - Rebuild the four displayed totals from base, durable policy components, and current live components.
   - Rebuild calling structural pressure from the same current inputs, then subtract durable relief and current method commitments.
   - Keep the six family calculations centralized in scripted effects and script constants.

Do not repeatedly add the current condition contribution to an existing total. Each recalculation must replace the prior live component, then rebuild the bounded total. That is the required idempotence rule.

### Trigger cadence

Use only bounded actor-scoped entry points:

- acceptance initialization
- completion of the National Survey
- chaosx.nr15.150
- Event 15 GUI refresh
- existing Event 15 decision and mission terminal effects
- country-scoped war, peace, capital, and state-control hooks where the existing on-action structure supplies the affected actor safely

Do not add on_daily, on_weekly, on_monthly, or another world iteration. Do not loop over every country. State inspection must remain limited to the Event 15 actor and its relevant controlled or core states.

### Interlocks and costs

- Calling relief must be durable enough to reward investment, but structural pressure must return when the material deficit remains.
- A method should record both its family and the kind of relief it produced.
- Open Call should provide lower durable relief at lower coercion.
- Guaranteed placement should provide stronger relief with administrative and Concord exposure.
- Quota should provide fast relief with Assignment and legitimacy costs.
- Emergency levy should provide temporary crisis relief, then expire or create backlash.
- Necessary Ground must continue reading the live relevant family rather than a generic high Need flag.
- District completion should relieve only the calling families the project actually addresses.
- Reserve release should affect provisioning pressure without permanently deleting unrelated shortage families.

### AI implications

- AI chooses the highest live pressure after recomputation.
- AI must not launch a second method for a family already under an active method or cooldown.
- AI should stop an emergency levy when crisis pressure falls below an exit threshold.
- AI should not open Necessary Ground if a domestic project can resolve the relevant family within an accepted time and cost window.
- Closed Island AI may tolerate lower Concord, but must still consider revolt, supply, and reserve risk.

### Cleanup and stability

- Add separate entry and exit thresholds or a cooldown so severities do not oscillate around 40.
- Clear temporary recalculation variables after every rebuild.
- Clear actor-only live components and method commitments in the existing terminal cleanup.
- Preserve durable historical conduct flags used by achievements and final variants until the accepted terminal cleanup says otherwise.
- A selected Necessary Ground case must close or convert when its specific family no longer has a live deficit.
- Ownership loss must remove state-derived contributions on the next bounded refresh.

### Acceptance scenarios

1. A recipient begins with weak infrastructure and insufficient transport equipment. Civic Works becomes live.
2. Completing a rail project and supplying transport equipment lowers that pressure.
3. Losing the rail state or exhausting trains causes Civic Works pressure to return without replaying acceptance.
4. A peacetime recipient entering war develops Defense and Watches pressure.
5. Ending the war removes only the war component while durable defense policy effects remain.
6. A calling method resolves its family but does not erase a separate family.
7. Repeated manual and pulse refreshes with unchanged state produce identical totals.
8. Necessary Ground is unavailable when the relevant deficit is gone and available when the deficit is real.

## Finding 4: give Penal Works one real, costly project lifecycle

### Diagnosis

The focus applies paid institutional growth, flags the conduct, and moves Ledger values. The assigned-colony state modifier supplies local supply and resistance-target variables. Neither surface by itself creates a player-managed penal-work project with state selection, material obligation, resistance, foreign reaction, and terminal cleanup.

### Accepted specification requirements

- Part 1 identifies penal labor as one of the manifesto's core dangers and requires the first use to be recorded.
- Part 3, Closed Island route, requires coercive interpretation to be materially strong and politically defining.
- Part 4, Garden Settlements and stewardship, requires state projects and route-specific administration.
- Part 6 requires penal-labor challenge events and foreign reactions where appropriate.
- Part 7 requires Closed Island AI to understand revolt, supply, reserve, and garrison risk.
- Part 8 requires route and conduct consequences to remain visible and localized.
- Achievement rules for The Joke Understood and No One in Chains explicitly exclude penal labor.

### Bounded implementation recommendation

Add one Closed Island labor-method selector to an existing active district, stewardship, or assigned-administration project. It must not become a parallel repeatable project family.

The method should:

- require utopia_manifesto_penal_works and a valid active state project
- attach a state-scoped penal-work flag and temporary modifier to that project
- accelerate or strengthen one clearly defined project output
- charge manpower, support goods, garrison capacity, supply, or reserves
- reduce Concord
- increase local resistance, local anger, or revolt pressure
- record the existing penal-labor conduct flags
- create a route incident or foreign reaction through the existing event family
- end at project completion, failure, ownership loss, revolt, route exit, or terminal cleanup

The benefit must be strong enough to tempt a desperate Closed Island actor. The full cost must be visible before selection. The method cannot grant free divisions, generic equipment, instant cores, or permanent output without maintenance.

### AI implications

Closed Island AI may use penal work only when:

- Need is severe or the project is strategically critical
- the state can be supplied
- garrison and resistance risk are within a centralized limit
- no active revolt or project invalidation makes completion implausible
- the actor can pay the visible material cost

The AI should avoid the method when a normal project can finish safely within the accepted time window. Non-Closed routes must never select it.

### Cleanup

- Remove the state method flag and temporary modifier on every terminal path.
- Preserve the durable historical conduct flag for achievements, route events, and final identity until accepted system cleanup.
- Ownership loss must end the project benefit before reconciliation evaluates resistance or revolt.
- Failure must not grant the full project output.

### Death-system integration blocker

Before implementation, the parent must do one of the following:

2. Obtain an explicit design decision that deaths integration is not applicable to this bounded project.

Do not silently substitute manpower removal for deaths integration. Do not add a new Event 15-only deaths system.
