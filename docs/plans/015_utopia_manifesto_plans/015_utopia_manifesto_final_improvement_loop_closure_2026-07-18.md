## Historical and regional connection review

The accepted design already turns its research into play rather than decorative lore:

- Thomas More's agriculture, trades, occupational preference, public need, six-hour day, and learning become the Calling and workday systems.
- Common stores, reserves, and the demotion of gold become material provision, reserve rotation, public accounts, aid, and route-specific property choices.
- Utopia's colonial claim to allegedly underused land becomes Necessary Ground, with domestic alternatives, purchase, lease, joint administration, local consent, expiry, coercive escalation, and conduct memory.
- Slavery and hard labor become the tempting but costly Penal Works and Assigned Colony paths, including exact civilian loss and shared Deaths registration.
- Mercenary warfare becomes the paid auxiliary-contract lifecycle with betrayal, dependency, demobilization, and conduct consequences.
- Incomplete religious toleration and the book's disputed satirical purpose become the Concord, censorship, criticism, and hidden humanist route.
- Utopus's made island becomes five geography-respecting island variants without pretending the engine changes province geometry.
- modern cooperative principles distinguish voluntary households from council ownership, while Garden City planning grounds housing, work, transport, land return, and linked districts.
- New Harmony supplies the failure lesson: governance conflict can redirect or reinterpret the experiment without deleting the campaign.

No new research theme is presently stranded without a mechanic. Further historical additions would be flavor accumulation unless a future implementation audit identifies a precise missing consequence.
