### Foreign partner score and validity

riches_found_prepare_scored_foreign_partner in common/scripted_effects/029_riches_found_incident_effects.txt:301–350 is a deterministic maximum-score selector. It clears the cached foreign target, offer-controller variable, and availability flag, then evaluates eligible countries and saves a candidate only when its score is strictly greater than the prior maximum. This is not probability-proportional sampling. Ties preserve the first encountered candidate, while enumeration order is not established here.

The candidate gate excludes a missing country, the current controller, excluded special/CXT actors, capitulated countries, and countries at war with the controller. A candidate then needs to be a neighbor, in the controller's faction, have opinion above 25, or have at least 20 convoys, a controlled coastal state with a naval base, and a mine marked with a valid port route, port route, or island-mine access. The current source has no major-power requirement on that maritime branch. The parent explicitly authorized the same concrete maritime access for a minor country. Major status adds 35 score points, so this eligibility change does not change authored numeric weights.

The maritime eligibility threshold uses greater-than-or-equal to 20 convoys, while the overseas-access score bonus uses a strict greater-than 20 convoy check. A candidate at exactly 20 can therefore pass the maritime gate without receiving that score addition. The threshold sensitivity is unmeasured.

The additive score terms are base 20, major 35, medium industry 10, large industry 25, neighbor 45, same faction 30, positive relations 20, hostile relations −20, wartime need 15, controller weakness 20, contract network 20, overseas access 10, and severe crisis −35. The score is clamped to minimum 1 and maximum 1,000. Thresholds are medium industry 30 factories, large industry 80, positive opinion above 25, hostile opinion below −25, weak controller below 20 factories, and the maritime selector threshold of at least 20 convoys.

The selector threshold admits exactly 20 convoys. The separate overseas-access score addition uses a strict greater-than-20 convoy condition, so exactly 20 qualifies the maritime route without that score bonus. The threshold sweep could not evaluate this boundary.

The constants riches_found_foreign_interest.rival_involvement = 15 and riches_found_foreign_interest.exclusive_conflict = −80 are defined in common/script_constants/029_riches_found_constants.txt:963–985, but a repository-wide common/ search found no source references to either token. The scorer does not apply those two declared terms in the current source. No numeric replacement is proposed.

Because every valid candidate is clamped to at least 1, a hostile-at-peace neighbor or faction member can remain selectable after receiving the hostile-relations penalty. War with the controller is excluded. If cooperative concessions must reject a hostile rival even at peace, that needs an eligibility rule or a separate hostile action. The source does not establish the resulting partner rank or frequency because the complete country pool and external diplomacy state are missing.

riches_found_foreign_target_is_valid in common/scripted_triggers/029_riches_found_triggers.txt:541–571 now checks that the cached offer exists, its stored offer controller equals the mine's current controller, the target differs from that controller, the actor remains ordinary and non-capitulated, and it is not at war with the recorded controller. It then rechecks current neighborhood, faction, opinion, or the concrete maritime route. The comparison to the current controller prevents a cached offer from surviving a controller change as a valid offer.

The scorer runs during mine initialization/discovery and from riches_found_refresh_selected_foreign_offer for the selected mine after deliberate actions.
