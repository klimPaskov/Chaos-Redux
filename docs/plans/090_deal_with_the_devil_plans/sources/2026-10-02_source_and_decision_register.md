## Direct user requirements preserved

Event 090 replaces Suicide Craft, remains Minor Fire-Once and unclustered, and owns the duplicate Devil concept rather than allowing a second parent. The stranger is Mephistopheles but is initially presented plausibly. Rewards are real and strong. Immediate visible terms are honest; hidden institutional consequences emerge later. There is no public corruption/contract dashboard or secret-phase custom window.

Five accepted main deals produce peaceful possession with the familiar leader and continued player control. Total refusal produces open manifestation. Partial acceptance creates a stronger institutional confrontation. The first clean refusal is unpunished. Side favors do not finish possession. Evidence, subtle-to-overt portrait transformation, sacrifice, discovered loopholes, and post-possession liberation are required.

One shared focus tree has three distinct origins and complete baseline political, military, economic/scientific, foreign, client, and liberation content. Custom demon families require full presentation. Evolutions alter danger and behavior rather than gate basic content. The terminal route needs Chaos above 1000 and a genuine global order. Existing specialists, diseases, research failures, and other actors retain ownership.

## Explicit new design proposals

D01: clean refusal bids seek the first arrangement rather than consuming acceptance slots; maximum five clean invitations before manifestation.

D02: a protected office, trusted appointments, delegated national authority, protected continuity, and final mandate structure the five institutional steps independently of reward category.

D03: concrete timing, reward magnitudes, AI starting probabilities, force envelopes, and Chaos increments are the values in the tuning and outcome maps, subject to current-source and balance review.

D04: one-arrangement separation can close the relationship peacefully after a real sacrifice; an unresolved closed relationship supplies no continuing new rewards and never drifts to five acceptances.

D05: public mask and concentration of possession are separate strategic axes in the shared tree, avoiding duplicated trees while preserving their distinct consequences.

D06: foreign clients use three explicit principal commitments, bounded diplomacy, real delivery costs, and distinct controlled-client versus open-possession states.

D07: the proposed Compact and Last Signature numeric qualification, durability, and disruption rules make the broad user requirements testable; they are not claimed source constants.

D08: the proposed one-state eligibility restriction is a recommendation awaiting approval, not an accepted change to the host pool. A same-tag substitute remains unapproved.

D09: exact unit material recipes, military-strength measure, portrait implementation and mixed audio routing remain verification work. Role envelopes are not legal engine syntax.

D10: proposed localisation and achievement titles are draft wording for review. No actual art, model, sound, achievement integration, or catalog change is delivered.
