# Parent design review and release gates

This is the drafting assistant's self-review, not an independent specialist audit or a gameplay acceptance report.

## Design corrections incorporated

The five-acceptance requirement is separated from the number of invitations, so early refusals do not make peaceful possession impossible or reduce the number of accepted main arrangements. Personal symptoms, local favors, and unsolicited gifts cannot bypass explicit acceptance. A failed delivery cannot acquire a completed principal concession.

The hidden-versus-visible split covers the Event Log, Event Details, achievements, focus visibility, family discovery, and foreign summaries, not only the acceptance tooltip. The final offer is blocked until concrete evidence and a counterplay interval exist.

Breakaway settles actual institutions and useful benefits. A successful cure remains successful, recoverable personnel are living people, and scientific restoration respects real prior losses. Diplomatic promises preserve the actual agency of other governments.

Three distinct origins converge on one tree without equalizing their opening resources. Public mask and distributed possession are separate strategic choices. The full baseline includes foreign bargains, clients, armies, and liberation without evolution gates.

Revolt planning preserves real geography, a viable host, existing wars, and accurate military material. The one-state conflict is explicitly unresolved rather than “solved” by a different route. The portrait and unit-audio presentation also retain real evidence gates instead of unsupported technical promises.

Event 019 families retain complete owner contracts and derivative isolation. Human population needs survive infernal political ownership. Terminal qualification cannot be manufactured by empty puppets, duplicate actors, or a raw division-count exploit.

## Not shortcuts

Do not resolve a missing treatment hook with a flat stability bonus while retaining a cure promise. Do not resolve one-state geography by stealing a neutral island. Do not resolve an overlay problem with unapproved portrait repainting. Do not resolve missing creature animation with renamed infantry. Do not resolve an unavailable terminal metric with raw division count. Do not resolve absent specialist dispatch by claiming role-based prose review was independent work.
