## Design strengths established by inspection

The same funded high-bid contract applies throughout the market.
The four evolutions change the seller's choice and the importance of the property, rather than replacing the core event with unrelated systems.
A losing bidder's money remains useful and a seller receives the same principal that the winner committed.
Divided Greenland, small countries, independent Greenland, current subjects, wars and territorial hazards have explicit boundaries.
Later acquisitions do not grant automatic cores or erase liberation and population history.
No ordinary bid or refund becomes a repeatable direct-Chaos farm.
The native category has a bounded set of meaningful values and no unnecessary standalone GUI.
