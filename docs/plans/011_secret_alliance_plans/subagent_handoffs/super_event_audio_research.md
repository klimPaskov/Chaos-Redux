## Scope and outcome

This handoff covers the unique music package for the Event 011 public-revelation and faction-formation super-event. It does not wire gameplay, localisation, music definitions, sound definitions, the music table, or spreadsheets.

**Selected recording:** `Revelation`, a 1901 march by William Paris Chambers, performed by the United States Marine Band under Col. John R. Bourgeois for the 1992 album *Sound Off!*.

**Proposed numeric audio ID:** `43`. The live Chaos Redux catalogue and helper definitions currently end at audio ID `42`; no existing audio ID `43` or Event 011 track was found.

**Final duration:** `86.101746` seconds.

**Rights confidence:** high. The composition and the exact recording have separate public-domain declarations on the Wikimedia Commons file page. The official U.S. Marine Band catalogue independently confirms the work, composer, performer, album, director, and recording year.

## Event fit

The cue serves a public revelation, not a world end. Its opening section moves from a brief restrained entrance into a formal full-band march, giving the scene ceremony, disciplined coordination, and military intent without supernatural horror or modern trailer percussion. The first large section ends at a natural break at about `00:87`, allowing a self-contained `86.10`-second derivative rather than an arbitrary mid-phrase cut.

The instrumental recording is also tag-neutral: it contains no anthem lyrics, national text, or participant-specific references that would falsely bind the procedural coalition to one country. The source title reinforces the reveal role, but the selection was made for the musical shape and rights clarity rather than the title alone.

Suitability rating: **5/5** for the specified public faction-formation role.

## Rights verification

### Composition rights

The Wikimedia Commons file page identifies William Paris Chambers as the composer, gives the composition year as 1901, and marks the composition public domain in its country of origin and in jurisdictions using copyright terms of the author's life plus 100 years or fewer. Chambers died in 1913. The page also applies the Creative Commons Public Domain Mark.

**Composition status:** public domain.

### Recording rights

The same file page identifies the United States Marine Band's 1992 performance and separately marks that performance public domain because it was made by United States Marines or federal employees as part of their official duties. The official `.mil` album page independently lists W. Paris Chambers's `Revelation` as track 3 on *Sound Off!* and identifies Col. John R. Bourgeois as director.

**Recording status:** public domain U.S. federal-government performance.

## Editing and conversion

The retained source interval is `00:00.940113` through `00:87.040113`. This removes the source's leading silence and ends inside the short natural break that begins at approximately `00:86.966`. The edit adds a `0.40`-second fade-in, a `2.00`-second fade-out, and targets `-18 LUFS` with a `-1.5 dBTP` ceiling. The final WAV measures approximately `-18.07 LUFS`, `-1.93 dBTP`, with `6.3 LU` loudness range.

Actual conversion command:

```powershell
ffmpeg -y -v warning `
  -i "docs/assets/011_secret_alliance/source_audio/revelation_us_marine_band_commons_source.ogg" `
  -filter_complex "[0:a]atrim=start=0.940113:duration=86.10,asetpts=N/SR/TB,afade=t=in:st=0:d=0.40,afade=t=out:st=84.10:d=2.00,loudnorm=I=-18:TP=-1.5:LRA=11:measured_I=-18.30:measured_TP=-2.13:measured_LRA=6.40:measured_thresh=-28.52:offset=-0.64:linear=true,aresample=44100,asplit=2[aogg][awav]" `
  -map "[aogg]" -c:a libvorbis -b:a 160k -ar 44100 -ac 2 `
  -metadata title="Revelation - Event 011 Public Reveal Excerpt" `
  -metadata artist="United States Marine Band" `
  -metadata composer="William Paris Chambers" `
  -metadata album="Sound Off!" `
  -metadata date="1992" `
  -metadata license="Public domain composition and U.S. federal government performance" `
  -metadata comment="Edited excerpt: source 00:00.940-00:87.040; 0.40 s fade-in; 2.00 s fade-out; loudness normalized to -18 LUFS target." `
  "sound/011_secret_alliance/super_event_43_public_reveal.wav" `
  -map "[awav]" -c:a pcm_s16le -ar 44100 -ac 2 `
  "sound/011_secret_alliance/super_event_43_public_reveal.wav"
```

The matching WAV uses 44.1 kHz stereo signed 16-bit PCM because that is the format used by the current repository super-event sound files and helper definitions.
