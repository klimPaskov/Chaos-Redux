The current report accident change repairs the source-level count mismatch: a successful accident replaces a middle visit rather than appending an eighth scheduled report. This is a parent-applied source change, not an MCP-verified balance result.

The report-day schedule uses a first-day range and 1-to-3-day increments with a day-14 clamp. Multiple late reports can therefore share the final date when the accumulated schedule reaches the clamp. No MCP timing evaluation established how often this occurs or whether same-day popups are acceptable.

The AI assessment uses a 365-day timed strategy window in the current source. Its production-factor changes are gated by the source's fuel, manpower, and base checks, but the strategy file does not encode a stockpile replacement line or a custom special-template score. The engine-level production rank and risk of reduced replacement are unresolved. Do not treat the numeric strategy modifiers as probabilities.
