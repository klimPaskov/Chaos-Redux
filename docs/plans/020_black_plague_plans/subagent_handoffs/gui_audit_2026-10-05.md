The audited surface belongs exclusively to Event 020 Black Plague. `black_plague_response_category` is registered in `common/decisions/categories/020_black_plague_response_categories.txt`, attaches `black_plague_response_category_scripted_gui` from `common/scripted_guis/020_black_plague_response_scripted_guis.txt`, and binds only `black_plague_response_category_window` in `interface/020_black_plague_response.gui`. The accepted layout reference is `docs/plans/020_black_plague_plans/subagent_handoffs/references/020_black_plague_gui_visibility_capacity_intended.png` and its SVG source. I inspected the PNG. The recorded parent authorization is the 2026-09-27 visibility-and-capacity direction, which hides the category until an established relevant outbreak or active national response, retains one category picture, distinguishes unorganized 0/0 capacity from exhausted positive-total capacity, and keeps the meter empty at 0%.

On 2026-10-05, the parent explicitly accepted the current supporting values, National Deaths and Response Capacity, because deaths communicate disease urgency and capacity determines which actions can proceed; Medical Reserve is already visible in the national decision and stockpile surface. This is the acceptance basis for the current view and does not retroactively change the 2026-08-21 specification.

The 2026-08-21 GUI correction and handoff specify Medical Reserve as a supporting live value, while the current GUI and later visibility reference show National Deaths in that position. Today's parent decision resolves the current design in favor of National Deaths plus Capacity. The earlier specification remains historical evidence and should be reconciled by the parent as a separate documentation update if needed.

The current reference retains the category-owned picture `GFX_decision_cat_picture_black_plague_response` in the native decision-category header. The attached panel has no picture element, so it does not paint a duplicate. This is the specifically accepted Event 020 composition despite the general simple-category picture gate.

## Native layout and content budget

The 520×282 board uses `GFX_tiled_window`, a 20px content margin, and 480px content width. The title is at (20,14), programme status at (20,53), progress label and value at y=98, meter at (20,131) with 480×27 bounds, summary panel at (20,167) with 480×70 bounds, and conditional capacity warning at (20,246) with 480×22 bounds. The meter uses `GFX_tiled_progress_bar`; the summary panel uses `GFX_tiled_window_transparent`. Sibling rows have a consistent 9px gap and 14px top and bottom padding.

The meter uses five 96×27 `GFX_black_plague_response_progress_fill` elements at x=0, 96, 192, 288, and 384 inside the 480px meter. The summary row places National Deaths in the left 220px cell and Response Capacity in the right 220px cell. The organized and unorganized capacity labels share the same bounds and have complementary visibility predicates. The unorganized label appears for absent or zero capacity; the exhausted warning requires positive total capacity and zero or absent remaining capacity.

The visible numeric budget is one primary value, Countermeasure Progress, plus two supporting values, National Deaths and Response Capacity. Programme status is qualitative. There are zero GUI actions, zero GUI costs, zero spendable cost types, and no button click regions. Costs and cost strings do not appear in this named window; the `.gfx` file's Event 020 texticons belong to the ordinary decision surface. Hover, selected, locked, and disabled button states do not apply. The applicable states are normal, active, warning, completed, minimum and maximum value, and long text.

The longest dashboard text includes the capacity warning and the unorganized-capacity explanatory tooltip. There are no GUI cost strings. The long-text render request also used synthetic maximum numeric values to stress the National Deaths and Capacity labels; these values are fixture assumptions, not claimed campaign state.
