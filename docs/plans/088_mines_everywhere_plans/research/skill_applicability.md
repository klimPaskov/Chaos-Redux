# Skill and subagent applicability

All supplied skills and all supplied profiles were fully read. Applicability is distinct from reading completion and from tool execution.

The planning, events, event-assets, subagents and improvement-loop skills directly shape this package. AGENTS, mechanics, catalogs and dynamic helper registries establish shared-system boundaries. Their applicable rules are carried into the core design, separate prompts, source ledger and implementation handoffs.

The decisions/missions and scripted-GUI skills informed the decision to keep native naval play and the existing report/details presentation rather than adding a redundant action panel. Their conditional production and rendering workflows were not executed. The debug-playtest skill was read without activating desktop or game-launch authority.

The focus-tree, super-event, frame-animation, ComfyUI and 3D pipeline texts were read completely. They do not justify adding unrelated branches, countries, animations, models or provider jobs to this fixed mine incident. Their conditional providers, paid calls, images, templates and runtime consumers were not invoked or inspected merely because their prose was read.

Relevant future custom profiles are the repository explorer, scripted-system architect, AI/probability auditor, localisation auditor, generated-event artist, icon artist, documentation curator, spreadsheet worker, completion auditor and mandatory improvement planner. Their self-contained briefs are included. No profiles were run in this session.

The event-only UI worker does not own the shared Event Logs, Event Details or settings frameworks. Country, focus, portrait, super-event source and 3D workers receive no job for this design. Skill-maintainer work would require a demonstrated reusable workflow change rather than a task-local blocker. No skill files were edited.
