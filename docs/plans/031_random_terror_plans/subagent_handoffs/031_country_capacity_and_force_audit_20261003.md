The normal spawn dispatcher in common/scripted_effects/031_random_terror_effects.txt, lines 1811-1956, chooses the first unused carrier and reserves it before state transfer. Its carrier checks use the package's random_terror_slot_*_used flags and do not test whether a chosen country already owns land.

The separate Global Jihad gate in common/scripted_triggers/031_random_terror_triggers.txt, lines 268-292 and 363-373, and the preflight in common/scripted_effects/031_random_terror_effects.txt, lines 1218-1239, count carriers only when each tag scope passes exists = yes and its used flag is clear.

Every carrier has a country history but no state ownership in the mod. The histories set only capital values, an empty shared OOB, focus loading, technologies, ideas, characters, and politics. The country-creation wiki says assigning states is the step that adds a country to the game, and the installed triggers documentation defines exists as an in-game country-existence test. Together those references make the pre-transfer exists = yes checks a likely false-negative for landless carriers. The live reference_context, reference_search, and source_lookup routes did not return usable evidence, so the exact engine result remains unresolved rather than engine-confirmed.

There is also a confirmed occupied-slot reuse path. In the current shared source hash, common/scripted_effects/031_random_terror_effects.txt, lines 4613-4734, the human-controlled cleanup branch retains the country's states and units, clears random_terror_actor_setup_complete through random_terror_clear_actor_runtime, and clears the carrier-used flag for the original tag. If another eligible crisis target keeps the pool open, the normal crisis path can later call random_terror_try_spawn_actor from lines 689-694 after another incident reaches lost-local-control stage. That dispatcher sees the clear used flag, transfers another state into the retained country, and runs the one-time setup again. The free-carrier preflight then also counts the retained country because exists = yes and the used flag is clear.

This can reuse an occupied player-controlled tag and add another set of starting divisions, manpower, and equipment to a country whose prior units and states were deliberately retained. Part5 requires a carrier to be cleared only after all actor state and references are cleaned, and the current slot release does not prove that condition. No parent units or parent stockpile are directly transferred by the spawn helper, but repeated Event31 setup can duplicate the actor's own starting package.

The recommended bounded owner repair is to keep a carrier reserved while its country, states, units, or package references remain, and to make both preflight and the normal spawner check an explicit carrier-availability contract. The empty-tag case and retained-player case need distinct states. Do not infer that exists = yes alone means free.

## Starting military, technology, industry, supply, and reinforcement

All eight country histories define the locked Infantry Division template with six infantry battalions and one recon support company. The shared OOB is empty, so the Event31 initializer is the only current division-creation path found in the scoped files. The template sets is_locked = yes and force_allow_recruiting = no. A scoped search found five create_unit calls, all in the profile branches of random_terror_initialize_actor. No recruitable template unlock or later actor create_unit/recruitment effect was found in the Event31 decision, focus, event, or setup files.

The initializer in common/scripted_effects/031_random_terror_effects.txt, lines 1535-1679, computes controlled_states times two, then applies profile floors of 4 local, 8 regional, 12 transnational, 15 jihadist, or 20 final divisions and an actor cap of 30. A one-state founding actor therefore receives its profile floor regardless of that state's population, captured unit count, or stockpile. The current shared effects diff changes the JMX profile path under parent-owned terminal work, so this audit does not assign a terminal profile to JMX or classify that route.

Each new actor receives fixed values from common/script_constants/031_random_terror_constants.txt, lines 311-313: 12,000 manpower, 1,200 infantry equipment, and 180 support equipment. The state-count floors are not capped by those values or by population.

The installed vanilla sub-unit definitions provide a concrete demand crosswalk. One infantry battalion requires 1,000 manpower and 100 infantry equipment, and the recon support unit requires 500 manpower, 40 infantry equipment, and 10 support equipment. The Event31 template's six infantry battalions plus one recon company therefore represents 6,500 manpower, 640 infantry equipment, and 10 support equipment per full-strength division.

At the local floor of four divisions, the template represents 26,000 manpower and, at the current 0.80 equipment factor, 2,048 infantry equipment and 32 support equipment inside the divisions. The country stockpile grant is only 12,000 manpower, 1,200 infantry equipment, and 180 support equipment. The current command requests start_manpower_factor = 1.00 and start_equipment_factor values from 0.80 to 0.90.

Installed effects documentation says create_unit supports any scope and describes owner, count, and province selection. The offline Effects wiki says country-scope create_unit does nothing and requires state scope, which conflicts with the installed documentation. The wiki calls the factors division stockpiles, while the installed documentation does not say whether those per-division stocks debit the national stockpile. The main Event31 route timed out, and no live engine execution was performed, so I cannot claim that the package either deducts or duplicates those materials. The source does not document a single authoritative supply ledger for the initial country stockpile and the per-division factors.

The actor resource actions do not grow the force with new divisions. random_terror_actor_raise_local_militia adds manpower and rifles to the country pool. Its decision debits those resources before and after the helper so the net debit remains real, but it does not create a division. random_terror_actor_integrate_defectors changes authority and control flags without transferring a captured unit. random_terror_actor_repair_captured_depot raises External Supply without drawing depot equipment. random_terror_actor_convert_militia changes control and flags without creating a converted unit. No additional create_unit call exists in these paths.

The foreign-fighter route is not a formation source either. In common/decisions/031_random_terror_decisions.txt, lines 3046-3056, the decision spends 220 infantry equipment, calls the commit helper, then subtracts the same 3,000 manpower the helper added. The helper returns 180 infantry equipment and 3,000 manpower in common/scripted_effects/031_random_terror_effects.txt, lines 3426-3435; the net is no manpower gain, a 40-equipment debit, and no division creation. This is grounded source arithmetic, not an evaluated probability result.

Two decision AI modifiers are unreachable under their own availability gates. random_terror_recruit_foreign_fighters is available only when External Supply is above the low threshold at decisions lines 3020 and 3033, while its urgent AI modifier requires that same value below low at line 3060. random_terror_prepare_state_offensive requires has_war = yes in its availability block at lines 2782-2808, while its AI low-factor modifier at lines 2851-2855 requires has_war = no. Actor absorption also lacks verified force reconciliation. The accept option in events/031_terrorist_attack.txt, lines 351-364, transfers all owned states, requests cleanup, and calls the accepting actor helper, but the reviewed acceptance body does not itself show a one-time unit/stockpile reconciliation. The absorption decision target at common/decisions/031_random_terror_decisions.txt, lines 2635-2658, checks actor/route/capitulation status but not connected territory or supportability. Cleanup's actual unit and stockpile outcome remains unresolved; Part5's merger rows require a valid transaction with forces and stockpiles reconciled without duplication (matrix lines 24, 57, 87).

The startup resource grant is not a transfer from the parent stockpile. No parent equipment or unit transfer effect appears in the carrier creation path.
