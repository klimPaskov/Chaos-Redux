## Preserved timer behavior and limits

Native evolution and revelation delays may be fractional.
The enqueue keeps its exact original `days =` variable.
A separate earliest-native receipt uses the whole-day lower bound solely to avoid rejecting a valid native callback under truncation, fractional-hour parsing or rounding.
Absent-host recovery waits for the unchanged canonical ceil-hour deadline.
Before the current earliest window, a stale callback cannot consume the replacement timer.
Once that window has arrived, an old callback on the same country is indistinguishable from a valid delivery, so no immutable event-instance generation guarantee is claimed.
Current-pending and exact-recipient checks still reject missing receipts, duplicates after consumption and callbacks on a replaced host.

Evolution and revelation processing clear their host/earliest-date receipts before successor scheduling.
Revelation invalidation and repeatable-cycle reset clear the revelation receipts.
Terminal finalization consumes its deadline inside its original active/pending gate, and withdrawal, commit and defeat clear that deadline.
Defeat also clears a pending terminal commit.
The final actor's loss does not force defeat while any existing unresolved-network condition remains true.
