## Current source findings before MCP

`random_terror_security_surge` costs 10 command power, 35 fuel, and 100 support equipment. Its shared availability/custom-cost affordability predicate uses inclusive `< cost` rejection. The regular branch requires the initial phase, no response cell, free slot 1, an active crisis state, and no existing surge target. Its emergency branch applies to a ROOT-controlled active hostage or uprising subject before terminal phase and can coexist with that emergency mission’s occupied slot. The `complete_effect` currently branches only on the emergency-subject receipt or a free slot-1 check. It then debits all three costs and dispatches the corresponding surge helper without rechecking affordability, `random_terror_can_government_response`, the exact current response target, current controller, world-end/terminal state, or the complete visible/available/target contract.

`random_terror_targeted_raid` costs 15 command power, 5 army experience, and 140 support equipment. Its current availability checks affordability, the government-response gate, ROOT control, a non-duplicate raid target, and either the response-cell/intelligence plus active-state path or the emergency-subject path. Its target trigger additionally requires `random_terror_state_is_response_target`. The `complete_effect` currently debits all three costs unconditionally, saves the selected state, sets the raid-target flag, and calls the raid and outcome helpers without click-time revalidation. Foreign ownership is not itself disqualifying when ROOT remains the controller; custody receipts are receiver-keyed with `@ROOT`.

The relevant transitive scripted predicates are in `common/scripted_triggers/031_random_terror_triggers.txt`: `random_terror_can_government_response`, `random_terror_state_is_emergency_subject`, `random_terror_state_is_response_target`, `random_terror_state_is_active_crisis`, `random_terror_can_afford_security_surge`, and `random_terror_can_afford_targeted_raid`. The decision effects, hostage mission lifecycle, raid resolver, and five outcome branches are in `common/scripted_effects/031_random_terror_effects.txt`. The raid’s army-experience debit also reads `constant:universal_cost_framework.negative_one`, hence the additional frozen shared-constant file.

The hostage mission’s current timeout is 45 days. Full progress auto-completes through its success resolver; timeout chooses the existing partial resolver when the receiver-keyed partial-freed state flag is present, otherwise failure; cancellation and all terminal paths run receiver-local cleanup. These are existing handlers, not proposed changes.

The decision AI recipes are score inputs, not click probabilities. Current source bases are 7 for surge, 3 for raid, and 12 for the hostage mission; decision factors are 0.5, 2, and 4, and mission factors are 2 and 4. Surge adds urgency for a capital-infiltration or hostage-crisis target, a high factor for a recent incident, and a low factor during war. Raid adds a high factor for intelligence breakthrough, urgency for capital infiltration, and low factors during war or after a failed raid. The hostage mission adds urgency while its hostage crisis is active and a high factor during war. Exact evaluated traces, eligibility and any ranking remain pending MCP.

The targeted-raid resolver has low, mid, and high five-branch `random_list` pools. The frozen constants define all five outcomes in each tier, and response capacity adds a clean-success ticket bonus capped at six effective points. The high-band base ticket values are 65/20/10/4/1, mid-band values 35/30/22/10/3, and low-band values 10/15/25/35/15. These are source values only; no probability has been calculated by hand or claimed as MCP evidence.
