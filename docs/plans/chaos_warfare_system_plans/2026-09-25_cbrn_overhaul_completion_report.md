## Balance and scenario evidence

The current legal source upper bounds for the CBRN package are +4.0375% army attack, +4.5807% organization, +14.7368% planning, +7.7496% coordination, and +2 percentage points reinforcement. Doctrine-only artillery soft attack is +9.7980% for line and +9.8246% for support artillery; including the strongest legal global attack source gives +14.2311% and +14.2588%. The highest sampled dedicated-unit static endpoints are projector defense +34.010% and Chaos Battalion organization +33.340%; the largest sampled hazard-conditional product is 1.495. These are conservative products of legal source modifiers, not a measured engine-effective division result. The commander `defense_skill = 1` conversion and all applicable equipment/module aggregation remain open.

The equal-IC comparison in `subagent_handoffs/2026-09-20_cbrn_completion_audit_handoff.md` covers clean combat, unprotected exposure, protected exposure, and a mask shortage. One protected nine-infantry formation costs 756.5 IC against 1.449 equal-cost plain formations. Under the current country-wide exposure dispatcher, both protected and plain formations in the same country receive the same national coverage score; the Protection Company adds native defense/breakthrough and a supply benefit, but no proven per-division hazard immunity. This is a meaningful specialization tradeoff and an unresolved consumer distinction, not a claim that the protected division wins every equal-cost battle.

Sponsor Decontamination's immediate first-model equipment cost is 327.5 IC after the cost-row simplification, compared with 1,262.5 IC for its former seven-commitment package; the two-factory, 45-day commitment remains. This source arithmetic identifies a large affordability change, not a measured AI or cleanup-balance verdict. ## Simplifications, omissions, and blockers

1. **Native raid lifecycle:** Installed `common/raids/_documentation.md` establishes `essential_equipment` collection at raid creation and outcome callbacks, but exposes no creation, cancellation, or refund callback sufficient to prove the accepted exactly-once reservation/refund contract. The native AI path also lacks a probability adapter. Cancellation, target loss, war termination, and stock-loss outcomes remain unverified.
2. **Event 026 Five Departments:** The achievement requires a sale-day paid receipt before the next daily tick, while Japan's native chemical and biological sale raids resolve after 5, 30, or 45 days. Source counts the later receipt only while the sale is active and clears the family state at expiry. Documented native hooks cannot reconcile these conditions without changing the accepted achievement or counting an uncompleted raid. No substitute was installed.
3. **Technology presentation:** Ten branch labels now identify the lanes, but the user's reported disconnected, clipped tree still lacks a reliable post-change visual acceptance result. Current MCP folder images have unresolved `@`-constant geometry, despite resolved sprite identifiers. This is an unresolved validation task, separate from technology mechanics.
4. **Exact HQ association:** The accepted prepared-army achievement receipt is country scoped because the documented native callback does not provide a selected-division-to-specific-HQ pointer. It cannot prove that the deployed HQ participated in that raid.
5. **Balance consumers:** Engine-effective stats, commander skill conversion, unit-specific protection behavior, MIO equipment effects, and equal-cost battle results were not established by source arithmetic. The four equal-cost cases are source scenarios only.
6. **Remaining visual and historical consumers:** The chemical facility's material repair is installed: all eight active material slots resolve the selected DDS files, and the [source-linked before/after render](../../assets/chaos_warfare_system/models_3d/cw_facility/evidence/material_repair_v1_before_after.png) shows a brighter diffuse result. Its actual PDX gloss response remains unverified because the Blender preview does not reproduce the engine shader. Per-character historical portrait provenance, actual 1936/1939 country-by-country mask inventory, other 1939 civilian issue sources, all native project cards, and full before/after technology/doctrine/event/GUI comparisons also lack complete evidence. The Event 020 offline GUI proof closes the specific zero-capacity render concern but not actual outbreak lifecycle behavior.
10. **1939 agent inventories and industry:** The seven major-country offensive agent-lot quantities remain identical at 1936 and 1939. A sourced U.S. 1937 phosgene process is represented as 1939 research knowledge, but the documented production tonnage has no accepted conversion to in-game agent lots. Other country-specific stock changes, 1939 production-line grants, and Fildes' precise prewar military role remain unresolved rather than assigned invented values.
7. **Weighted behavior:** Doctrine and policy evaluations were partial with unresolved eligibility, and no native-raid AI probability surface exists. No scenario-specific engine choice probabilities are claimed.
8. **Dormant decision-based occupation attack code:** `common/decisions/cbrn_occupation_decisions.txt` still defines timed Sarin and Soman nerve-suppression decisions with their own preparation, costs, cancellation and `cbrn_occupation_execute_nerve_suppression` adapter. `cbrn_occupation_current_version_condition_hook_verified` is `always = no`, so even CXT cannot prepare these actions; they are dead source, not a competing executable path. Existing occupation history and defensive administration must be preserved; simply calling the route implemented would be false.
9. **International decontamination cost-row evidence:** The four-type payment redesign is in source and documented by `../../specs/chaos_warfare_system_specs/corrections/2026-09-25_international_decontamination_cost.md`; native decision-list rendering remains unavailable for this ordinary decision row.
