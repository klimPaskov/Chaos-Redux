# CBRN industrial concern and MIO overlap audit

**Disposition: source differentiation confirmed; native appointment and AI choice remain unverified.** The accepted overhaul requests six matching historical industrial concerns without granting duplicate benefits from essentially identical appointments. This audit compares the current concern ideas in `common/ideas/cbrn_industrial_concerns.txt` with their six original-tag national organizations in `common/military_industrial_organization/organizations/cbrn_national_organizations.txt` and the three inherited MIO families. No gameplay, localisation, asset, or AI weight was changed.

Each concern is restricted to its own `original_tag`, so the repeated +8% synthetic-resource value on ICI, IG Farben, and Montecatini is not a stack of three choices for one country. The national MIOs are separate appointments that can coexist with a concern, but their inherited research categories are `gas_masks_tech`/`decontamination_tech`, `cbrn_instruments_tech`/`biowarfare_tech`, or `chemical_warfare_tech`, and their national overrides affect CBRN equipment production or reliability. The concern ideas instead use ordinary `synth_resources`, `industry`, and `electronics` category bonuses. A source search of the five current CBRN technology files found none of those three ordinary tags in their category blocks. This establishes distinct source targets, not the engine's final research-speed aggregation or the player's ability to appoint a particular pair at runtime.
