# Japan and Soviet 1939 CBRN start entitlements

**Disposition: This source audit is complete. Japan’s existing 1939 CBRN setup is defensible without extra stocks, civilian issue, or a factory line. The Soviet military and institutional setup is also defensible; an archival 1938 order for children’s respirators is a specific procurement fact, but it does not establish completed production or issue by 14 August 1939 and therefore does not support a numeric civilian-stock grant. No gameplay source was changed.**

The accepted basis is the user-approved 50% Japanese and 75% Soviet military mask issue bands at both bookmarks, each with a separate 25% replacement reserve, and zero non-British civilian mask coverage absent country-specific issue evidence. The evidence below distinguishes documented program activity from present script and from gameplay calibration.

## Japan (JAP)

**Current setup.** The startup grant in common/scripted_effects/chaosx_startup_history_effects.txt:1320-1378 is date-invariant across the January 1936 and 14 August 1939 starts. It grants basic gas-mask and monitoring technology, biological surveillance and pathogen-handling knowledge, Livens-projector technology, chlorine and mustard knowledge, abstract chlorine and mustard stock lots, and 1.0 gameplay biological-breakthrough progress. The stock constants are 480 chlorine lots and 420 mustard lots in common/script_constants/startup_history_constants.txt:18-40; they are gameplay units, not historical production or inventory counts. The shared protection profile uses a 50% active military issue target and separate 25% reserve (same file, lines 66-74). The grant adds no CBRN protective-equipment production line.

JAP’s conditional CBRN facility sites are province 1182 in state 282 (Japan), province 4179 in state 530 (Tokushima), and province 3900 in state 609 (China 8), all in the same startup block. These are existing scripted facilities, not a dated 1939 expansion. The installed vanilla files 282-Japan.txt:28, 530-Tokushima.txt:18, and 609-China 8.txt:33 confirm province membership in those states. This review makes no map claim about exact historical factory capacity.

The static CBRN roster has Shiro Ishii, Chikahiko Koizumi, and Ryoichi Naito in history/general/chaosx_startup_character_recruitment.txt:55-60. The dated Japan helper has no 1936–39 appointment; Masaji Kitano remains gated until after 1 January 1942 in common/scripted_effects/chaosx_startup_history_effects.txt:728-754. The 1939 roster therefore gains no unsupported person. Existing parent approval of the 67 portrait set is accepted; this audit does not reopen portrait sourcing.

Showa Denko becomes visible and available to JAP after 31 May 1939 in common/military_industrial_organization/organizations/cbrn_national_organizations.txt:101-114. The National Diet Library corporate-authority record dates its formation to June 1939 through the merger of Showa Fertilizers and Nihon Electrical Industries. That supports the identity and current date gate. The existing MIO’s high-output CBRN filling modifier is a gameplay abstraction: the company-history evidence does not establish CBRN-specific filling output, as already recorded in docs/plans/chaos_warfare_system_plans/2026-09-27_national_mio_source_audit.md:12.

**Historical evidence and limit.** The U.S. Army Center of Military History’s official history, The Chemical Warfare Service: From Laboratory to Field, chapter III, p. 115, dates Japanese biological-warfare studies to about 1936 and identifies a principal wartime research organization near Harbin. It cites Lt. Col. Arvo T. Thompson’s 31 May 1946 report, so this is an official retrospective based on postwar reporting, not a surviving 1939 production ledger. It supports an established program by the 1939 bookmark, not additional agent lots or a continuous factory line. The National Archives’ Japanese War Crimes collection describes its holdings as covering 1934–2006 and includes official records and postwar interviews, but its finding aid does not quantify 1939 stock.

Japan’s Anti-Aircraft Defense Law took effect on 1 October 1937 (JACAR document A03022074400). The Showakan Digital Archive catalogs a September 1937 public gas-defense handbook, 國民防毒讀本, with material on civilian masks and collective protection and links to its National Diet Library Digital Collections record. These are evidence of organized preparedness and public instruction; neither proves a nationwide civilian mask issue or the amount held by August 1939.

**Country disposition.** Keep the accepted 50%/25% military mask calibration, existing abstract agent lots, technologies, facilities, and 1939 Showa Denko date gate. The evidence does not support a further stock increase, civilian issue, mask factory, or agent-production factory. Preserve the distinction between Showa Denko’s sourced industrial identity and the MIO’s modeled CBRN bonus.

## Soviet Union (SOV)

**Current setup.** The startup grant in common/scripted_effects/chaosx_startup_history_effects.txt:1478-1524 is also date-invariant across both bookmarks. It grants basic mask and monitoring technology, pathogen-handling protocols, meteorological stations, Livens-projector technology, chlorine, phosgene, and mustard knowledge, abstract stocks of 720 chlorine, 480 phosgene, and 960 mustard lots, and 0.8 gameplay biological-breakthrough progress. The constants are in common/script_constants/startup_history_constants.txt:27-57. The profile remains at 75% active military issue and a separate 25% reserve (lines 66-74). These abstract stock and breakthrough values are not historical quantities.

SOV’s scripted CBRN facilities are province 326 in state 239 (Saratov), province 344 in state 247 (Tver), and province 12233 in state 823 (Karakalpakstan), conditional on ownership. The installed vanilla files 239-Saratov.txt:22, 247-Tver.txt:21, and 823 - Karakalpakstan.txt:26 confirm province membership. The startup grant creates no CBRN mask-production queue or ordinary civilian-factory grant.

The static scientist roster includes Georgy Gause, Nikolai Ginsburg, Sergei Muromtsev, Zinaida Yermolyeva, and Nikolai Zelinsky (history/general/chaosx_startup_character_recruitment.txt:62-70). The dated helper in common/scripted_effects/chaosx_startup_history_effects.txt:776-857 excludes Yakov Fishman after his documented 5 June 1937 arrest and Ivan Velikanov after his 8 April 1938 execution, while recruiting Beria after his 25 November 1938 appointment and Mairanovsky from the 1937 access floor. Mairanovsky’s toxicology-director/advisor availability begins only on the 1938 gameplay floor. These source-backed gates explain a different 1939 roster without claiming a general expansion or collapse of the Soviet research program.

**Historical evidence and limit.** A Soviet Defense Commissar memorandum to Molotov dated 9 January 1938 discusses the need to put a new army gas-mask model into service. This documents a military protective-equipment standardization effort; the memorandum alone does not prove a production total or the number issued by August 1939.

The Russian State Military Archive’s published document list identifies Defense Committee order 5940/KO, dated 22/23 October 1938, as an order for children’s gas-mask production in 1938–39. This is direct, country-specific evidence of a civilian-protection procurement program. The accessible catalog entry gives no completed production quantity, delivery record, or distribution total. It therefore does not establish how many masks were available or issued by the 14 August 1939 bookmark, and it does not support converting the order into a numeric share of civilian coverage. It is a concrete candidate for future child-specific modeling if an execution or issue record is found; it is not a blanket civilian-stock entitlement.

The archive’s historical summary describes the Soviet local air-defense system’s civilian protection, warning, shelter, medical, and anti-chemical responsibilities, and notes a 1939 organizational change. This supports an institutional civil-defense capacity, not universal personal-mask issue. The official Soviet archival memorandum reproduced in the historical-document library dates Mairanovsky’s entry into NKVD service to 1937 and a specialized toxicology laboratory to 1938. The current person and date gates reflect that narrow institutional fact; it should not be conflated with mask manufacture or general battlefield chemical-defense industry. An official Rosgvardia history dates Beria’s appointment as People’s Commissar for Internal Affairs to 25 November 1938, supporting the current 1939 availability gate but not independent scientific expertise. The Russian history-of-science register records Velikanov as head of the Red Army Biotechnical Institute and his execution on 8 April 1938; it supports his removal from the later start, not a claim that the whole Soviet program ceased.

**Country disposition.** Keep the current 75%/25% military mask profile, existing abstract stock and research values, facilities, and date-gated roster. Do not add a civilian coverage percentage, extra mask stock, or factory from the procurement order without evidence that production was completed or issue occurred before the bookmark. No numeric reduction to Soviet research progress follows from the documented personnel loss alone.
