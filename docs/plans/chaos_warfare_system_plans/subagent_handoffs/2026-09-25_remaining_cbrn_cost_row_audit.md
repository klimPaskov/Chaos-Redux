## Prioritized findings

1. **Critical — nerve suppression exceeds both payment limits.** `cbrn_nerve_suppression_sarin` and `cbrn_nerve_suppression_soman` share `custom_cost_text = cbrn_nerve_suppression_operation_cost` in [cbrn_occupation_decisions.txt](../../../../common/decisions/cbrn_occupation_decisions.txt:86) and [cbrn_occupation_decisions.txt](../../../../common/decisions/cbrn_occupation_decisions.txt:197). The current row at [cbrn_occupation_l_english.yml](../../../../localisation/english/cbrn_occupation_l_english.yml:19) lists seven consumed types in prose: 40 nerve-agent payload lots, 40 gas masks, 30 decontamination sets, 15 CBRN instruments, 40 support equipment, 20 trucks, and 30 Command Power. It has no texticons and exceeds the four-type hard cap and three-inline-value cap. The equipment and Command Power package is debited in `cbrn_occupation_effects.txt:197-245`; payload debit occurs through the operation flow after the final exact-state check. The blocked key is generic (`..._blocked`, line 20), and the cost tooltip at line 21 shows reserves for five equipment types but omits payload and Command Power. Safe bounded repair: reduce the paid package to no more than four types while preserving the operation’s accepted intent, show every retained payment as a matching texticon with no more than three inline values, and make blocked/hover text identify the unavailable retained charges without duplicating the row. No arithmetic mismatch was found between the current listed amounts and their linked constants/effects. Both decisions have `ai_will_do`; a row/localisation-only edit does not change weights, but changing cost triggers changes eligibility and therefore the scored choice pool. Parent-owned baseline and probability comparison are required for any such eligibility change.

2. **High — hospital protection hides two consumed charges outside the payment row.** `cbrn_protect_hospitals_and_utilities` is at [cbrn_protection_decisions.txt](../../../../common/decisions/cbrn_protection_decisions.txt:338), with native 10 PP and no custom payment row. Its effect computes and debits population-scaled respirator crates and Support Equipment (`cbrn_protection_decision_effects.txt:418-435`, debit at `491-505`); the ordinary tooltip spells out both quantities without cost icons at [cbrn_protection_l_english.yml](../../../../localisation/english/cbrn_protection_l_english.yml:61). Inventory is PP 10 plus two equipment types, all consumed when the protection action is applied. Safe bounded repair: expose the same calculated debit values with their correct texticons in a concise cost row or cost tooltip, keep PP native, and leave protection duration and effect text focused on the result. No cost arithmetic mismatch was found in the matching availability/debit path. AI scoring is not changed by a display-only edit; changing the affordability predicate would affect the candidate pool and needs parent-owned probability comparison.

3. **High — emergency distribution and the occupied-population program disclose stock drawdown only as an effect.** `cbrn_emergency_state_mask_distribution` ([decision](../../../../common/decisions/cbrn_protection_decisions.txt:257), [tooltip](../../../../localisation/english/cbrn_protection_l_english.yml:52)) costs 10 PP and consumes a computed amount of national respirator stock to approach the emergency coverage target; its state effect calls the shared exact distribution/debit helper. `cbrn_supply_occupied_population` ([decision](../../../../common/decisions/cbrn_protection_decisions.txt:286), [tooltips](../../../../localisation/english/cbrn_protection_l_english.yml:56)) costs 25 PP, reserves two civilian factories for its 30-day program, then distributes and consumes available mask stock across the selected occupied states at completion (`cbrn_protection_decision_effects.txt:232-257`). Neither decision has an iconized stock amount in its payment row; the occupied tooltip describes allocation but not a quantity. Treat the stock debit as a variable consumed consequence, not as stock merely required at program start; the factory use is a continuing reservation, not consumed payment. Safe bounded repair: expose a calculated current-state/target estimate or the exact “up to available stock” amount using the same distribution computation, with a respirator icon; retain PP and distinguish the occupied program’s factory reservation and delayed stock use. No fixed-cost mismatch was found because the quantity is coverage- and state-dependent. Both have AI scoring; display-only disclosure does not affect it, while any new affordability gate does.

4. **High — both CBRN protective-aid shipments omit mask stock from the payment row.** `cbrn_export_protective_equipment` ([decision](../../../../common/decisions/cbrn_protection_decisions.txt:135)) spends 15 PP, reserves one civilian factory during the decision, and transfers 500 respirator crates on completion (`cbrn_protection_decision_effects.txt:340-353`; quantity constant `cbrn_system_constants.txt:971-973`). Its tooltips state the 500-crate transfer, but the payment row only shows native PP. `cbrn_deploy_protective_aid` ([decision](../../../../common/decisions/cbrn_occupation_decisions.txt:308)) spends 25 PP, reserves two civilian factories for 21 days, and consumes a state-specific variable amount of donor respirator stock through the distribution helper; its localisation explains the variable amount but does not iconize it in the row (`cbrn_occupation_l_english.yml:29-32`). These are two consumed types each plus a non-consumed factory reservation, within the distinct-spendable-type limit. Safe bounded repair: show the fixed 500-crate transfer for export and a same-helper-derived quantity or explicit variable drawdown for occupied aid, both with respirator icons, and label factory reservation separately. Existing reserve checks exceed the 500-crate export amount, and no exact-cost mismatch was found in the inspected paths. AI weight edits are not proposed; a changed stock/eligibility trigger would change the scored pool and needs parent-owned probability comparison.

5. **Moderate — shelter evacuation has a missing cost tooltip and repeats the payment list; three valid procurement rows repeat affordability text.** `cbrn_move_civilians_to_shelters` uses an exact three-value, three-icon row for PP, Support Equipment, and trains at [cbrn_protection_l_english.yml](../../../../localisation/english/cbrn_protection_l_english.yml:66), with matching blocked display at line 67, but there is no `black_friday_cbrn_move_civilians_to_shelters_cost_tooltip` key. The basic respirator crates granted by the conversion and simplified-filter actions are outputs, not costs. No mismatch was found between the displayed procurement charges and debits. These display/localisation fixes do not affect AI scoring; changing affordability does.

## Cross-cutting audit notes

- **Payment inventory:** nerve suppression is the only reviewed action that exceeds the four-spendable-type cap; its prose row also exceeds the three-value inline cap. Other listed actions have at most two consumed types plus a factory reservation, or the shelter action’s three consumed types. Factory use, stock conditions, and continuing disruption are separated above from consumed payment.
- **Affordability and cost accuracy:** custom-cost rows reviewed use matching affordability/debit helpers. No exact numerical mismatch was found. The main clarity gap is variable stock consumption hidden in effect tooltips, not a proven debit calculation error. Export eligibility reserves more than the fixed shipment quantity.
- **Missing localization hooks:** among the reviewed custom payment rows, the shelter decision has a `_blocked` key but no matching `_tooltip` key. No other missing custom-row `_blocked` or `_tooltip` key was found in the remaining in-scope CBRN rows. Hospital, emergency distribution, occupied distribution, and the two aid decisions use native PP with non-PP payments hidden from their row rather than custom cost hooks.
- **AI and route locks:** all five findings include decisions with `ai_will_do` scoring. No score patch is recommended. Parent owns the mandatory probability baseline/compare if the implementation changes availability, affordability, AI weights, or target eligibility. No route-lock or target-validity audit was performed beyond the payment predicates referenced above.
- **Lifecycle, missions, and player-facing values:** the occupied distribution and protective-aid decisions have timed factory commitments; the occupied program consumes stock at completion, while emergency distribution and hospital protection debit stock when applied. This cost-focused pass did not review category action counts, all mission lifecycle/cancellation paths, or broader decision cognitive load. The variable stock quantities are currently described by their cause/target but are not always shown numerically as a player-facing payment.
- **Cleanup and exploit risk:** no exploit finding was established in the inspected payment paths. This was not a full exploit, category lifecycle, or mission-quality audit. The nerve suppression flow deliberately defers payment until final target validation; preserve that check when revising its payment package.
- **Validation limits:** source review only. No HOI4 launch, GUI inspection/render, live-game visual proof, or MCP probability baseline/compare was performed. Parent owns MCP AI analysis and final review. No separate plan was created.
