## Subunit modifier correction

The completion auditor found that the two projector technologies and the 1942 Chaos Battalion grant used combat-stat fractions as if they were flat points. The offline Technology Modding wiki at lines 105–116 describes these subunit combat fields as multiplicative; installed vanilla `common/technologies/artillery.txt` uses `soft_attack = 0.15` for a 15% technology bonus, and `infantry.txt` uses fractional breakthrough but flat `max_organisation` and `recon` technology rewards. The unit owner and completion auditor reviewed the category memberships and legally cumulative CBRN sources before this correction. No engine combat result is claimed.

The projector's legal CBRN doctrine category adds soft attack 0.20, defense 0.20, and breakthrough 0.15; the compatible CBRN-wide army attack sources currently add 0.015 and 0.025 to soft and hard attack only. The raw pre-correction source sums with both projector tiers and the earlier 0.02/0.03 army attack factors were soft attack 1.45, hard attack 1.25, defense 0.80, and breakthrough 1.65. The corrected current raw sums are 0.29, 0.30, 0.30, and 0.30 respectively. The engine's cross-layer combination formula was not established from installed documentation, so a conservative all-independent product bounds these as `1.05^4 × 1.025^2 × 1.015 × 1.025 − 1 = 32.860%` soft, `1.13^2 × 1.015 × 1.025 − 1 = 32.845%` hard, `1.05^6 − 1 = 34.010%` defense, and `1.05^3 × 1.075^2 − 1 = 33.778%` breakthrough. The fully independent product is a bounding scenario, not a claim about the engine formula or observed combat. Chaos Battalion's legal doctrine adds category breakthrough 0.10 and direct breakthrough 0.05, taking its raw dedicated sum from 2.85 to 0.30 and its all-independent product to 33.228%. The projector's base 12 soft/4 hard/4 defense/10 breakthrough values make one corrected technology worth 0.30/0.52/0.20/0.75 native points before equipment and other modifiers.
