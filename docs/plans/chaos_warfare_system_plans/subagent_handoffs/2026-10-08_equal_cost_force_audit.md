## Exposure and equipment-shortage conditions

The current protection snapshot counts mask equipment already in armies and the separate program-issued-mask ledger. Its military mask requirement is deployed army manpower multiplied by 10 crates per 1,000 manpower. Automatic issue calculates the uncovered remainder after those counted pools and debits only available stockpile masks. I found no source-proven second debit of the same physical mask for both unit needs and automatic national issue.

The clean case has no exposure damage. In the unprotected case, the shared country dispatcher applies organization loss D and strength loss L at zero protection to all relevant divisions; the Protection Company does not grant a separate unit-specific immunity. At full improved-mask protection, the current guide records respiratory score 75, choking multiplier 0.25, and disruption multiplier 0.4375, again shared country-wide. The current shortage illustration has 50 of 100 improved masks against a 9,320-man force, requiring 93.2 crates and yielding 53.65 coverage, 0.45 choking multiplier, and about 0.598 disruption multiplier.

Those four cases describe source protection ratios and missing equipment, not combat outcomes. Native division aggregation, combat frontage, terrain, tactics, reinforcements, equipment replacement, and combat results remain unmeasured.
