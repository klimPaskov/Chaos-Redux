# Historical-start source follow-up — 2026-09-25

**Disposition: implemented dated technology; unquantified stock differences remain unresolved.** The read-only country-package audit found that the seven major-country offensive agent-lot constants in `common/script_constants/startup_history_constants.txt` are unconditional at both bookmarks. They are gameplay calibration and cannot be presented as archival 1936 or 1939 inventory totals. The 1939 force-scaled mask and British civilian baseline were implemented separately in commit `9818a2f6b` under the user's explicit 2026-09-25 choice.

The [U.S. defence history](https://www.denix.osd.mil/rcwmprogram/history/) records Edgewood's 1937 upgrade and standardization of its phosgene plant, and 154 additional tons of mustard production. The former supports granting U.S. phosgene knowledge at the August 1939 bookmark while leaving the January 1936 bookmark without it. The latter proves a dated production change but supplies no conversion from tons to the mod's abstract `mustard_agent_lot_1`; no extra lots were granted. The phosgene source effect is in `chaosx_startup_grant_usa`, and no phosgene lot is granted. This preserves research and production as the route to usable stock.
