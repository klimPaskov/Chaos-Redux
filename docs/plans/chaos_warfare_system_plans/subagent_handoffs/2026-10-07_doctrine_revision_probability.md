## AI source values and validity

`chaos_warfare` has base AI weight `0.20`. Its source modifiers use factors `0` for actual nonhuman countries, `4.00` for a viable program, `2.00` for prepared-power or mass-civil-defence profiles, `2.50` for military-first, `1.75` for industrial reserve, `3.00` after enemy chemical use, `1.50` during war, `0.25` for democratic countries with no first-use route and no enemy chemical-use history, and `0.15` for limited or minimal profiles. These are literal source factors only; I did not multiply them by hand.

Each subdoctrine has base AI weight `1.00`, an unavailable factor of `0`, and a committed factor of `2.00`. All four also require `has_doctrine = chaos_warfare` in `available`. The committed trigger is mass civil defence or prepared power for infantry, military first or first-use route for armor, military first or industrial reserve for combat support, and prepared power, mass civil defence, or enemy chemical use for operations. The source supplies a direct doctrine gate, but the adapter did not prove its eligibility or rank as a complete track race.

The track AI weights are score inputs, not normalized click probabilities. The offline AI wiki describes `ai_will_do` as a highest-score race and distinguishes it from event `ai_chance` probability-proportional sampling; vanilla doctrine documentation records the `available`, `ai_will_do`, mastery, and reward structure. Reference paths, revisions, and helper source identities are retained in [baseline_evidence.md](../../../testing/2026-10-07_doctrine_revision_probability/baseline_evidence.md) and [manifest.json](../../../testing/2026-10-07_doctrine_revision_probability/manifest.json).

The source-level nonhuman finding is conditional: grand-doctrine AI weight is forced to zero for actual nonhuman countries, while each track source has no separate nonhuman factor and has a positive base if `chaos_warfare` is active. Whether a nonhuman AI can start with, inherit, or otherwise acquire that doctrine is unresolved. The adapter did not prove a valid or impossible campaign state for that case.
