## Accepted and implemented fixes

### Mengele — trait begins on the documented Auschwitz assignment

Before the patch, `GER_josef_mengele` had a static `scientist_trait_inhumane` and the minimum `specialization_biowarfare = 1` at `common/characters/cbrn_historical_scientists.txt:305-316`. The existing CSH transfer in `common/scripted_effects/chaosx_startup_history_effects.txt:566-575` opens on 1 January 1943, and `history/general/chaosx_startup_character_recruitment.txt:101` gives CSH its single history-time copy.

The 1 January 1943 scientist start is supportable: the US Holocaust Memorial Museum's biography says Mengele returned to Germany in January 1943 and resumed work with Verschuer before his Auschwitz assignment. The same source dates the SS assignment to Auschwitz to 30 May 1943 and describes his subsequent lethal prisoner experiments. The static `inhumane` trait therefore becomes available with the January scientist transfer, roughly five months before the historical role used to justify that trait. [USHMM, “Josef Mengele,” lines 171-188 and 209-233](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/josef-mengele).

Implemented abstraction: keep the identity, approved portrait, level-1 baseline, and January 1943 research availability; move only `scientist_trait_inhumane` into a one-time dated award on or after the documented 30 May 1943 Auschwitz assignment (`date > 1943.05.29`). The award is guarded by `has_character = GER_josef_mengele` and the one-time country flag `mengele_inhumane_trait_awarded`. Neither the scientist role nor the ethical trait grants offensive-use authorization.

**Confidence and validation:** high that the static trait predated the documented assignment; medium that assignment is the right onset for this interpretive trait. The parent accepted this mapping on 2026-10-09. The source test confirms Mengele remains a scientist after the January 1943 transfer, has no `inhumane` trait through 29 May 1943, and receives it once from 30 May 1943 onward. The nearby one-time awards in `common/scripted_effects/chaosx_startup_history_effects.txt:608-648` provide the existing pattern.

### Rascher — trait begins on the documented request date

Before the patch, `GER_sigmund_rascher` had a static `scientist_trait_inhumane` and `specialization_biowarfare = 1` at `common/characters/cbrn_historical_scientists.txt:292-303`. His transfer in `common/scripted_effects/chaosx_startup_history_effects.txt:552-563` opens on 1 January 1941. The National Archives finding aid dates a letter from Rascher to Himmler requesting human subjects for potentially fatal Luftwaffe experiments to 15 May 1941; it also lists a 22 May reply concerning prisoner availability. This is the earliest directly dated evidence located for the conduct represented by the trait. [U.S. National Archives, *United States of America v. Erhard Milch*, Special List 38, page 30, lines 931-939](https://www.archives.gov/files/publications/lists/special-list38.pdf).

Implemented abstraction: keep the 1941 scientist availability and the minimum-level scientist entry, but award `scientist_trait_inhumane` once on or after the dated 15 May 1941 request (`date > 1941.05.14`). The award is guarded by `has_character = GER_sigmund_rascher` and the one-time country flag `rascher_inhumane_trait_awarded`. The Federal German Research Foundation's historical record supports Rascher as a physician with earlier research grants and a later 1942 research assignment, but it does not establish a precise day when conduct began. [DFG GEPRIS Historisch, Sigmund Rascher](https://gepris-historisch.dfg.de/person/5109532).

**Confidence and validation:** medium. The archival record establishes the dated request, not the exact first experiment or first unethical act. The accepted date represents the documented request and does not claim it was the first experiment. The parent accepted this interpretation on 2026-10-09. The source test confirms scientist entry remains eligible from 1 January 1941, the trait is absent through 14 May 1941, and it is awarded once from 15 May onward. Rascher's portrait, roster membership, and offense policy are unchanged.
