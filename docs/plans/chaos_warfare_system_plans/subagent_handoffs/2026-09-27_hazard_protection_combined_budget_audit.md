# CBRN combined hazard protection budget audit

**Parent disposition after this read-only snapshot:** The three-file same-mask floor design below was promoted into the [accepted source correction](../../../specs/chaos_warfare_system_specs/corrections/2026-09-27_same_mask_hazard_budget.md). Disposition: unresolved, pending parent acceptance and a source patch. This is a read-only balance audit of the current chemical exposure path. No gameplay source was changed.

## Accepted limit and finding

The accepted [2026-09-20 overhaul amendment](../../../../docs/specs/chaos_warfare_system_specs/specs/13_2026_09_20_accepted_cbrn_overhaul.md) caps the **combined** conditional hazard benefit at 50%. The [doctrine balance handoff](2026-09-20_doctrine_balance_handoff.md) explicitly treats issued mask coverage as foundational equipment protection and subsequent treatment and protection as one additional 50% benefit budget. `cbrn_hazard_budget.residual_floor = 0.50` says the same in source. Therefore a mask casualty multiplier below 0.50 is not inherently illegal. The legal comparison is the full endpoint against the casualty result of the same actual mask issue with later protection and treatment removed.

The current path violates that comparison. At 89% effective issue of sealed masks, with 100 decontamination and 100 medical support, choking casualties fall from the mask-only 0.25 multiplier to 0.06, a **76% additional reduction**. Blister casualties fall from 0.60 to 0.15, a **75% additional reduction**. At full sealed issue, the advanced floors alone exceed the budget for blister (0.42 to 0.15, 64.29% additional reduction) and nerve (0.32 to 0.10, 68.75%). At full sealed issue the choking 0.06 floor is exactly half the mask-only 0.12 value, but later advisor, treatment, and designer factors can lower the final death result further. The final floor in `cbrn_exposure_effects.txt` is computed from the already improved protection-table multiplier, so it does not enforce the accepted combined ceiling against the mask-only baseline.

## Source path and legal scenarios

`cbrn_refresh_country_mask_snapshot` derives respiratory, skin, and warning scores from actually issued model crates, fielded demand, and filter condition. Decontamination requires `field_decontamination_kits` plus actual stock or army-issued kits. The state resolution passes those scores, `cbrn_medical_capacity`, and civilian equivalents to `cbrn_calculate_action_protection`. That helper clamps each layer to 0–100, sums six agent-class weights, takes the maximum of that sum and the respiratory score, chooses a discrete casualty band, sets disruption to `1 - 0.75 × effective_protection / 100`, and sets contamination to `max(0.50, 1 - 0.50 × decontamination / 100)`. The class weights sum to one. The casualty bands are `low=25`, `medium=50`, `high=75`, `very_high=90`, with `advanced_support=75` for both decontamination and medical capacity.

The following are source-reachable configurations, with full filter condition and ordinary issued crates. `R/S/W` are respiratory, skin, and warning scores; `D/M` are decontamination and medical scores. The table isolates the protection helper before attacker dose, target medical treatment, and endpoint caps. A zero score may still require the normal initialization path; it is not a claim that the target lacks a CBRN programme.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

The 89% choking weighted sum is `89×0.55 + 85×0.05 + 100×0.10 + 100×0.15 + 80×0.15 = 90.20`; the mask-only score is 89, so the branch jumps directly from `high=0.25` to `advanced_floor=0.06`. The corresponding blister sum is `89×0.10 + 85×0.35 + 100×0.25 + 100×0.20 + 80×0.10 = 91.65`, jumping from 0.60 to 0.15. The sealed model's quality points are `100/85/80` in `cbrn_mask_model_quality`. A country can produce sealed crates after the sealed protection technology; partial issue follows the stock-to-deployed-requirement ratio and is not quantized to the initial country-profile bands. Full decontamination follows the real-kit coverage calculation. Medical capacity can reach 100 through the repeatable `bio_expand_medical_capacity` investment, which adds 20 up to the 100 meter cap from an eligible start at or below 80. These are source-path reachability statements; native gameplay execution was not run.

The disruption output exceeds a 50% reduction against an unprotected country at full sealed issue, reaching 0.25, but the same sealed issue is the accepted foundational equipment baseline. The **extra** non-mask disruption change in the 89% choking and blister examples is only 2.71% and 5.98% respectively relative to their mask-only 0.3325 multiplier. No over-budget additional disruption contribution was found in the reviewed path. Decontamination's contamination reduction reaches exactly 50% at 100 points. The separate active cleanup output reaches `1.10 × 1.15 × 1.15 = 1.45475` of its base in the previously accepted worst legal source stack, below the 1.50 efficiency ceiling. Contamination points then multiply the civilian protection result alongside attacker class, route, doctrine, advisor, and MIO factors; those attacker factors do not enlarge the target's protection ratio.

## Downstream compound breach

`cbrn_calculate_chemical_action_outputs` saves `cbrn_action_*_casualty_before_treatment` **after** protection bands and attacker advisor factors. It then applies the target medical pioneer advisor to the casualty multiplier, followed by Dimercaprol and, where applicable, the respiratory-care or antidote designer. The military and civilian final death floors multiply that already improved `before_treatment` value by 0.50. The civilian shelter/project exposure budget is separately clamped to 0.50, but its death benefit also stacks with the advanced-table reduction before the final death floor. Medical saturation uses the same already improved casualty reference for its floor.

For full sealed issue plus full support, a legal medical pioneer (0.85), Dimercaprol generic treatment (0.85), and class-specific treatment yield these illustrative final casualty factors before dose, lethality, civilian shelter, and maximum death caps:

| Class | Mask-only casualty baseline | Advanced table value | Additional legal factors | Final factor | Additional reduction against mask-only |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Choking | 0.12 | 0.06 | medical pioneer 0.85 × generic treatment 0.85 × respiratory-care designer 0.85 | 0.0368475 | 69.29% |
| Blister | 0.42 | 0.15 | medical pioneer 0.85 × generic treatment 0.85 × blister treatment 0.80 | 0.0867 | 79.36% |
| Nerve | 0.32 | 0.10 | medical pioneer 0.85 × generic treatment 0.85 × antidote designer 0.85 | 0.0614125 | 80.81% |
| Incapacitating | 0.20 | 0.10 | medical pioneer 0.85 × generic treatment 0.85 | 0.07225 | 63.88% |

The listed products are all above the present floor of `advanced table value × 0.50`, so that floor does not correct them. They are relative multipliers, not absolute death fractions. The attacker toxicological director's 1.05 casualty factor can multiply both sides of a like-for-like comparison; the endpoint maximum cap may obscure a result in a particular raid, so validation should use a non-saturated dose and severity.

## Minimum source correction for parent review

A constants-only retune is insufficient. Setting the full-sealed blister and nerve advanced floors to 0.21 and 0.16 would fix those two isolated table ratios, but 89% sealed choking still needs at least 0.125 and blister at least 0.30. Further medical and shelter factors would still stack below those limits. A static advanced floor cannot preserve all legal mask-issue bands and enforce the full combined budget.

The narrow correction is to compute and carry one **same-mask casualty baseline per military and civilian target** inside `cbrn_calculate_action_protection`. Use the current model-adjusted **respiratory score**, after actual issued stock, deployed or civilian demand, filter condition, and fitting have resolved. This is the equipment-backed baseline already named by the source comment at lines 486–491. Do not use a theoretical fully issued model score or replace current coverage with the starting-profile target: zero stock must still produce baseline 1.0 and partial or worn stock must retain its weaker protection. Evaluate the existing class band helper with `cbrn_action_effective_protection` temporarily set to that respiratory score and both decontamination and medical locals temporarily zero, then save the casualty multiplier as `cbrn_action_mask_only_casualty_mult`. Zeroing those two locals for this call is necessary because the helper's advanced branch checks their values separately from the effective score. Restore them before the normal six-layer calculation. The actual model skin, antidote, and warning layers still participate in that six-layer result, but any increase beyond the respiratory equipment baseline consumes the same additional-benefit budget.

Keep the current six-layer score for presentation and disruption. Before exporting the casualty multiplier, clamp it to at least `mask_only_casualty_mult × cbrn_hazard_budget.residual_floor`. `cbrn_resolve_action_target_protection` must preserve distinct military and civilian temporary baseline outputs before its second call overwrites the generic working variables. In `cbrn_apply_historical_actor_chemical_advisor_modifiers`, multiply those two baselines by the same attacker toxicological-director casualty factor applied to the current casualty multipliers, so a like-for-like attacker's potency stays on both sides of the budget comparison. In `cbrn_calculate_chemical_action_outputs`, after that attacker helper and before the target medical-pioneer helper, freeze the actor-adjusted military and civilian baselines. Use the frozen military baseline for the military death floor; use the frozen civilian baseline for the civilian death and medical-saturation floors. Do not anchor any of these floors to `cbrn_action_*_casualty_before_treatment`, because those values already contain the improved six-layer table result. Civilian shelter exposure retains its existing separate 0.50 clamp; the final death floor must cover its combined effect with the protection table and treatment. The existing episode maximum caps are independent severity bounds; validate the mitigation ratio with non-saturated outputs, and compare capped baseline to capped final when testing a saturated receipt. This uses the existing `cbrn_hazard_budget.residual_floor` tuning constant and changes no accepted mask quality or decontamination/medical unlock rules.

Proposed helper map: extend `cbrn_calculate_action_protection` (country scope, six 0–100 layer inputs and agent class, output current four protection values plus `cbrn_action_mask_only_casualty_mult`, no persistent side effects); extend `cbrn_resolve_action_target_protection` in the same file (country scope, existing exact-state target, outputs separate military and civilian mask-only casualty baselines); mirror the attacker factor in `cbrn_apply_historical_actor_chemical_advisor_modifiers` in `cbrn_historical_advisor_effects.txt` (country scope, two baseline temporary values); and change the three floor anchors in `cbrn_calculate_chemical_action_outputs` in `cbrn_exposure_effects.txt` (country scope). No new event target is needed; the existing `cbrn_action_target_state` target remains chain-local, and all proposed outputs are temporary. No flag or persistent-variable cleanup changes are needed. Migrate only the casualty-table clamp and military-death, civilian-death, and medical-saturation floor call sites, leaving dose, disruption, contamination, condemnation, and cleanup calculations intact. Document the expanded helper inputs, outputs, defaults, and baseline contract in the matching protection-effect documentation if the parent implements it.

Task-specific validation after a patch should repeat the eight table configurations above through the actual source arithmetic, then test a non-saturated chemical action for each class at 89% and 100% sealed issue with the maximum legal target medical stack. Assert final military death, civilian death, and medical-saturation outputs stay at or above half their same-mask/no-extra baseline, and verify disruption, contamination, and cleanup outputs against their existing formulas. Inspect the relevant technology/doctrine path and compare source-linked artifacts when the MCP technology route is healthy. The user owns live-game confirmation.
