## Cross-person audit findings

The strongest defensive medical profiles are Florey and Fleming, and both already have an advisor trait that is ungated by Chaos Warfare. Their current `specialization_biowarfare` assignments need explicit category semantics: the evidence supports antimicrobial therapy and infectious-disease medicine, not BW-agent development. If biological research in this system includes countermeasure work, that should be made explicit in the accepted CBRN design and reflected in how their levels are calibrated.

The strongest documented offensive BW program specialists are Fildes, Ishii, Velikanov, and Baldwin; Schrader is the clear CW specialist. Their advisor-role Chaos Warfare gates are consistent with separating science from authorization. Their scientist roles have no per-person doctrine condition in this file, which allows scientific competence to exist independently of release policy.

Date issues that need parent disposition are Blome's 1942 gate versus a July 1943 assignment, Witaszek's September 1939 gate versus mid-1940 organization leadership, Mairanovsky's 1937 gate versus the 1938 secret lab, and Olson's 1943 advisor label versus his 1950 SOD and 1952-53 acting-chief service. Kitano's 1942 gate and Baldwin's 21 December 1942 gate are supported at year and day resolution respectively. Schrader's 1937 floor is reasonable from the 1936 discovery, but the exact day and source comment still need a dated primary citation. Velikanov lacks an end date despite his documented execution in 1938.

The current roster reuses `scientist_trait_brilliant_theorist scientist_trait_resourceful` for Florey, Fildes, and Velikanov (`:38`, `:64`, and `:319`); `scientist_trait_inhumane` appears on Ishii and Kitano (`:191`, `:222`); and `scientist_trait_gifted_engineer` appears on Olson and Baldwin (`:351`, `:383`). These repeated pairings do not prove a defect by themselves, but the sources support distinct profiles: penicillin/countermeasures for Florey, bacterial chemistry and offensive BW operations for Fildes, military vaccine-sera and BW program science for Velikanov, human experimentation leadership for Ishii/Kitano, aerobiology for Olson, and program engineering/scale-up for Baldwin. Avoid treating the repeated traits as biographical facts.
