### Stage timing that scenarios must preserve

Story-stage settlement and ordinary operational grants are different milestones.
Biomedical's ordinary route applies the existing Deployment package, whereas a fresh story completion records Prototype and preserves the owner's later Deployment stage.
Custom Kruger capabilities derive from authenticated Prototype history when its force runtime exists, as shown by `016_brilliant_scientist_project_force_effects.txt:443-478` and matching force-history predicates.
`brilliant_scientist_sync_project_force_runtime_after_ledger_change` at lines 553-565 rebuilds only sovereign or already-initialized force packages, preserving the existing pre-state host boundary.
Mengele's fresh custom callback can grant the neutral operational package through its authenticated native bridge.
Neither story path should be modeled as an unconditional neutral grant after every callback.

The durable story-output predicate is intentionally broader than successful Prototype advancement.
For example, a current host with Theory but insufficient Capacity can satisfy ordinary discovery and still choose the story adapter, whose stage advancement remains pending.
Likewise the Kruger Xenobiological Prototype waits for its existing control-doctrine readiness and native integration order.
The native-integration effects at `016_brilliant_scientist_project_effects.txt:147-211` preserve that transaction.
These existing lifecycle constraints are scenario prerequisites and timing limits, not a demonstrated absent reward guard.
This bounded review does not certify every decision/focus route into later story stages.
