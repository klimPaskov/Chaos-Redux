## Firm chronology versus unresolved proposals

The Koizumi and Hirt findings are firm at the historical-evidence level. Koizumi’s documented CW research began decades before the 1936 start, so his current BW field is contradicted by his recorded expertise. Hirt’s first documented Lost inmate experiment is 1942-11-25, while the current gate opens in 1941; the existing date therefore precedes the source-supported CBRN chemical-research role. The proposed CW 3 tier for Koizumi and proposed replacement date for Hirt remain gameplay recommendations for parent acceptance.

The proposed removal of Rascher and Mengele from the CBRN scientist roster is unresolved and must receive parent review; this report does not enact either removal. The other proposed dates for Ambros and Kuhn also remain unresolved pending acceptance. The static inhumane trait is unresolved for Rascher, Mengele, Hirt, Bickenbach, Rose, and Mrugowsky; the existing character fields do not date-condition that trait, and this report does not remove, replace, or split any character. Gutzeit’s year-only evidence and the suitability of other generic vanilla traits also retain source-confidence limits as noted above.
