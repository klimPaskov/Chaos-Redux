# CBRN commander service timing audit

**Disposition: source schedule verified; native leader-service and raid association remain unverified.** The accepted overhaul requires Chemical Operations Commander and the four related traits to reflect real command service, not repeated preparation or canceled operations. This bounded read-only audit checked the paid HQ order completion path in `events/cbrn_hq_events.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/cbrn_hq_effects.txt`, `common/scripted_effects/cbrn_commander_progression_effects.txt`, and `common/scripted_triggers/cbrn_commander_progression_triggers.txt`. It changed no gameplay source.

The intervals come from `cbrn_hq_force.weekly_tick_days = 7` and the five duration/tick pairs in `common/script_constants/cbrn_hq_constants.txt:65-96`. In `cbrn_hq.2`, each order requires an active matching trait, valid command, and the corresponding upkeep affordability before `cbrn_hq_debit_*_upkeep`, `cbrn_hq_complete_upkeep_tick`, and `cbrn_commander_record_completed_hq_operation` run in that order (`events/cbrn_hq_events.txt:148-238`). The tick helper decrements to zero without clearing the active trait or operation code (`common/scripted_effects/cbrn_hq_effects.txt:764-768`). The service trigger then requires an assigned army leader, a nonempty command, zero remaining ticks, the matching active trait, and no prior completion receipt (`common/scripted_triggers/cbrn_commander_progression_triggers.txt:47-84`). The completion effect sets that receipt before incrementing its bounded service counter. Failed upkeep and canceled or invalid orders do not reach the completion call; new preparation clears the receipt, so another **fully paid** order can count but a repeated preparation click alone cannot.

Every final paid tick precedes its active-trait expiry by at least two days under the source durations, avoiding a same-day cleanup race with `cbrn_hq.3`. The source sequence therefore supports genuine completed HQ service without credit from early cancellation. It does not prove actual engine event ordering, unit-leader assignment at tick time, stock deduction, or save/reload persistence. Native raid callbacks expose no documented participating commander pointer, so completed raids cannot yet be attributed to that leader's forces; the full user requirement remains partial rather than claimed satisfied by country-level outcomes.
