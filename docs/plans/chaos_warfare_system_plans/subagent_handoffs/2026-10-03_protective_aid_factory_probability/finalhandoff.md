## Interpretation, skipped routes, and owner recommendations

The source-level AI terms are identical across the ordinary and sale IDs, while the new quote and factory-gate helpers introduce unresolved analysis for the ordinary candidate and the new sale ID has no baseline candidate. Because the sale gates and same-target exclusivity are unresolved, the adapter does not prove that exactly one purchase ID is eligible at once. The exact-three comparison cannot determine the sale ID’s relative willingness or the sale PP hint’s effect.

No numerical tuning target is recommended. Keep score claims separate from eligibility, preserve the full five-model shipment when applying the sale PP discount, and use a resolved same-scenario pass to verify the intended rule that only one purchase variant is eligible per target at a time. Before interpreting AI ranks, provide authoritative campaign facts for the country/scope relations and a complete target pool, or record that the adapter cannot represent those source constructs. Do not seed the quote outputs with hand-calculated values.
