# CBRN MIO neighboring asset source reaudit

Disposition: implemented in source, native consumer unverified. This is a read-only audit of the three accepted generic MIO families and their neighboring national/advanced logos, badge aliases, trait icons, and narrow-list names. It does not replace the earlier MIO implementation handoff or establish in-game rendering.

The active organization files are `common/military_industrial_organization/organizations/cbrn_chemical_protection_organizations.txt`, `cbrn_biomedical_organizations.txt`, `cbrn_delivery_organizations.txt`, and `cbrn_national_organizations.txt`. The three generic IDs are `cbrn_chemical_protection_consortium`, `cbrn_biomedical_industry_directorate`, and `cbrn_delivery_engineering_bureau`; each generic file contains twelve `trait` nodes. The old six-family organization IDs, including `cbrn_chemical_munitions_combine` and `cbrn_biological_security_directorate`, have no active organization definitions. The biomedical generic retains the latter legacy icon sprite token, but its current player-facing organization name is **Biomedical Directorate**. The other generic names are **Chemical Protection Works** and **CBRN Delivery Bureau** in `localisation/english/cbrn_mio_redesign_l_english.yml`. Thus the clipped Biological Security Directorate title in the supplied screenshot describes an earlier source identity, not the current generic MIO name.

The three generic trees declare 21 distinct exact equipment archetypes. All 21 have `GFX_military_industrial_organization_<archetype>` badges registered in `interface/cbrn_mio_industry.gfx`; that file has 23 badge sprite entries because it additionally aliases two specific gas-mask models. Every registered badge DDS exists and decodes as 48×48, matching the installed vanilla `generic_mio_department_icon_infantry_offense.dds` reference. All eight unique generic trait icon tokens resolve in installed vanilla `interface/military_industrial_organization/industrial_organization_policies_and_traits_icons.gfx`. The three generic organization logos registered in `interface/cbrn_designers.gfx` exist at 64×64. The eight national/advanced logos registered in `interface/cbrn_mio_industry.gfx` exist at 60×68; at the installed native list icon position `(10,20)`, their source rectangles fit within the 520×100 `industrial_organisation_list_item`. The [badge contact sheet](../../assets/chaos_warfare_cbrn/icon_package/contact_sheets/mio_badges_processed.png) and [organization/concern contact sheet](../../assets/chaos_warfare_cbrn/icon_package/contact_sheets/mio_organizations_concerns_processed.png) were visually inspected as processed asset sheets; their thumbnails show distinct imagery and no obvious cut-off subject. They are not native MIO cards or DDS consumer renders.

Installed reference evidence: `common/military_industrial_organization/organizations/_documentation.md:20-24,92-112,202-235` documents organization, initial-trait, trait, include, and override sprite fields. Vanilla `interface/military_industrial_organization/industrial_organization_department_icons.gfx:6-21` states that the automatic badge key is `GFX_military_industrial_organization_` plus an equipment category or archetype, and `industrial_organization_list.gui:229-262,381-389` supplies the list name/icon and badge geometry. Installed `common/military_industrial_organization/organizations/00_generic_organization.txt:123-140` is the initial-trait and trait-icon precedent. The connected MCP does not expose a dedicated native MIO card inspector or renderer; its GUI routes have not produced a verified MIO list/detail consumer for these assets.

Remaining proof: render the three generic, six historical, and two advanced current MIO cards in their eligible and locked states; inspect the name glyph bounds, badge and trait icons, branch headings, logo alpha/scale, click regions, and combined native effects. The 48×48 badge and 60×68 logo dimensions plus source path resolution cannot prove those consumer outcomes. The older six-family account in `docs/systems/cbrn_warfare/cbrn_designers.md` is explicitly labeled superseded and retains historical context only.
