# Legacy famine/migration custom-cost affordability audit — 2026-10-03

Disposition: unresolved source finding. The parent accepts the strict-affordability and gate-parity findings as repair work under the original complete-response goal; no gameplay implementation or engine result is claimed here. The bounded owner patch remains pending.

Scope: read-only review of the 25 existing non-Open-Reception decisions with `custom_cost_text` in `common/decisions/famine_decisions.txt` (10) and `common/decisions/migration_decisions.txt` (15). The already repaired `migration_open_reception` is excluded. No source, localisation, AI, GUI, workbook, or asset files were changed, and no commit was made.

## Findings by severity

1. High — All 25 legacy `custom_cost_trigger` blocks use strict `>` at their nominal amounts, and every corresponding resource-colour selector in `humanitarian_cost_scripted_localisation.txt` uses the same strict comparison. Exactly nominal stock therefore selects the blocked colour and fails the custom-cost predicate; zero and one-below do too. The accepted Part 6 cost rule is one inclusive affordability predicate shared by availability, native cost selection, and payment, so the current independent checks do not meet parity. This is a source finding, not an observed native-click failure.
2. High — No decision has one complete, inclusive paid-bundle predicate shared across `available`, `custom_cost_trigger`, cost colours, and the final payment. Some `available` branches indirectly check part of a bundle through `famine_relief_has_*_candidate`; those checks are strict, actor-scoped through `ROOT`, and skipped when an already-valid route contract satisfies the alternative branch. The exact gaps are in the row inventory below.
3. High — Three Famine delivery actions (`famine_emergency_imports`, `famine_escorted_relief_convoy`, and `famine_emergency_airlift`) can create/select a route contract before a conditional resource-debit block; their later `remove_effect` still calls `famine_relief_deliver_contract`. If a stale activation reaches the completion block with an insufficient tuple, the debit branch skips but the valid contract remains, and the current delivery validator does not require a fee-paid receipt before calling the reserve-transfer owner. This is a possible unpaid-delivery bypass in source, not an observed native exploit; engine click-time revalidation is not established here. The fourth action, `famine_invite_relief`, also creates the land contract before its conditional fee, but its own `remove_effect` only checks the valid contract and grants stability. Delivery is deliberately left to a later `famine_emergency_imports` action, which has its own fee path; do not describe invitation alone as delivering food.
4. Medium — `migration_negotiate_corridor` has no `complete_effect` charge. It prepares the contract and submits the offer from `remove_effect`, then debits `ROOT` only after the offer-submission result is valid. It does not revalidate the fee immediately before submission/debit, so affordability at selection is not a safe proxy for affordability at the deferred payment point. The host’s reject path cleans up the offer; no fee credit appears in the corridor reject/cleanup source inspected.
5. Medium — Five movement decisions explicitly refund the full fixed tuple only when `civilian_transfer_actual_origin_debit` is not positive: `famine_evacuation`, `migration_evacuate_vulnerable`, `migration_evacuate_workers`, `migration_voluntary_return`, and `migration_forced_repatriation`. Their source does not refund merely because an outcome is partial or costly after a real origin debit. Preserve that distinction; do not broaden this parity repair into refunding all late failures or funded work.
6. Low/presentation follow-up — `famine_escorted_relief_convoy`, `famine_emergency_airlift`, and `migration_third_country_resettlement` each display four spendable values. They meet the user’s four-type ceiling, but the decisions skill’s compact inline display guidance is three values. Keep all fees and requirements; review a justified visible formatting remedy separately. No native render/clipping finding is claimed.

## Source and payer contract

The file-scoped nominal payment macros are defined at `famine_decisions.txt:4-33` and `migration_decisions.txt:4-29`. The decision blocks below use those amounts in `custom_cost_trigger` and the corresponding negative amount in the payment effect. The positive amounts shown in the table are the exact fixed fees, not a proposed retune.

The outer decision actor is the payer: unqualified `has_political_power`, `has_equipment`, `has_fuel`, `has_air_experience`, and the direct top-level resource effects use the acting country. `FROM` is the selected state. Famine’s fresh relief-candidate helpers explicitly switch to `ROOT` for payer-stock checks while called inside the selected `FROM` state; `migration_negotiate_corridor` makes the payer explicit with `ROOT = { ... debit ... }`. Do not move a payer-side gate or charge into the selected state or foreign donor `OWNER` scope.

All strict comparisons below use `>`; none accepts exact nominal stock. Existing availability checks that are routed through candidate helpers also use strict `>`.
