## Resolved findings

### F1: active crisis with missing numeric stage could acquire a stable receipt

At the first reviewed observer identity `B8CF3FA18CFF04C04FB3A69CB2C7A8AC86451B0DD1B13FFA8974CBA90277A4D9`, the durable-input guard ran only when `famine_food_stage` existed and was greater than stable.
A source negative control with `famine_food_security_active` plus `famine_food_stage_famine` or `famine_food_stage_catastrophic`, but missing numeric stage, could bypass that guard.
With valid ordinary state scope and no current externally collected cause, the initializer could fill stable stage and neutral missing inputs, yielding a quiet current assessment and proven stage-zero projection while the active crisis marker remained.
This contradicted the accepted incomplete-crisis fail-closed boundary.
It was a source counterexample, not a claim that the malformed state occurred in a live game.

The actual stage markers are produced at `common\scripted_effects\famine_core_effects.txt:1781-1784` and consumed by `famine_refresh_dynamic_modifiers` at `:2861-2882`.
Retirement clears active registration and scalar stage, so a retired historical flag alone was not treated as proof of a current crisis.

The parent corrected the observer before final review.
Final `common\scripted_effects\famine_adapter_effects.txt:259-383` identifies a current crisis from active nonstable markers or actual current famine modifiers, rejects their mismatch with the scalar enum, and requires the scalar stage and all existing durable inputs before defaults are allowed.
The new local temporary known-crisis marker is initialized at entry and reset at exit.
The final source SHA-256 is `09BF27C2F784BBDCBD1883DFA7192B796EF8E18CAD3D622820C131B18145FEA0`.
The missing-stage counterexample therefore rejects before initialization, while quiet retired markers remain eligible for fresh model observation.
No new balance threshold, stage, stock or mortality behavior was introduced by this correction.

### F2: caller-side temporary result declarations were missing at exact consumers

The parent explicitly extended this audit to reception and Camp caller outputs after the food work made the lifetime contract explicit.
Offline `paradox_wiki\Data structures - Hearts of Iron 4 Wiki.md:436-442`, especially `:440`, warns that a temporary first defined inside a scripted helper may not remain visible outside it and guarantees modification of a temporary defined before the call.
Installed `documentation\effects_documentation.md:7820-7839` supports the unscoped temporary assignment syntax but provides no stronger cross-helper lifetime guarantee.
This distinction establishes a missing safe declaration contract, not deterministic output loss on every engine call.
A bounded installed scripted-effects census found no exact comparable caller-declared return pattern, so no native precedent or execution result was invented.

`migration_controlled_medical_reception` read `migration_reception_medical_service_result` after the setter without declaring that output first.

`camp_rework_commit_exact_cohort_custody` read the wrapper result, duplicate marker and resolved amount without caller-side declarations.
