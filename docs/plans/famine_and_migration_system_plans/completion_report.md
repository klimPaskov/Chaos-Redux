## Famine formula and mortality

The state food-security score is:

`clamp(((1.15 * production) + (1.10 * transport) + (1.25 * extraction) + (1.00 * need) + (0.80 * environment) + (0.90 * vulnerability) + (0.95 * governance) - (1.20 * relief)) / 7.15, 0, 200)`.

Stage entry thresholds are 25, 50, 75, and 100 for supply strain, acute shortage, famine, and catastrophic famine. Recovery thresholds are 20, 40, 60, and 80 with longer confirmation windows. The reserve ledger stores amount, capacity, target, daily need, logistics factor, depletion, replenishment, relief consumption, imports, and exact reserve transfers; it is not a flat modifier fallback.

`famine_apply_mortality` reads live population as `state_population_k * constant:chaos_meter_deaths.people_per_k`. Acute shortage, famine, and catastrophic famine use population-scaled base pulse rates of 0.0004 per 14 days, 0.0015 per 7 days, and 0.0030 per 3 days after their minimum exposure. The request is multiplied by bounded exposure, vulnerability, access, extraction/policy, environment, governance, and relief factors, then clamped to the live population above a dynamic protected floor.

The physical debit is performed once through `apply_exact_state_civilian_population_loss`. The exact applied amount is written once to Chaos Meter under `constant:chaos_meter_deaths_reason.famine`, localized as `From famine`. No fixed historical death total is used.

Island blockade pressure requires war, island/isolation proof, maritime dependence, port or route disruption, convoy or escort shortage, inadequate local supply, and no proven relief corridor at the same time. An island or a war flag alone cannot satisfy it.

## Exact civilian transfer proof

`civilian_transfer_civilians_exact` validates the current origin host and route before population mutation, measures origin population before and after the one physical debit, clamps route deaths to that measured debit, and requests destination credit only for survivors.

The transaction enforces:

`actual origin debit = route deaths + exact survivor credit`.

If the destination credits fewer survivors than requested, the positive residual is restored to the origin before the transaction may validate. Route deaths and global conservation totals do not commit until reception, bind, and cohort finalization all return exact matching receipts; the Deaths call has population application disabled because the origin debit already contains them. A defensive partial-inverse branch quarantines both touched states and their owner countries so no later transfer can replay or compound an engine-level rollback failure. Movement without route deaths therefore changes no death total. Successful cohort bind, reception, history, reconciliation, reward, and cleanup paths are guarded by the finalized exact receipt.
