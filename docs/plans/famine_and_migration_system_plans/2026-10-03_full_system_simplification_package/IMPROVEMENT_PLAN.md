# A crisis the player can act on

Disposition: `unresolved` proposal.
Working labels below describe design roles and are not final localisation.
All numerical budgets and tuning anchors below are proposals, not measurements of current balance.

## The playable loop

Famine asks whether to restore food access in place or reduce need by moving people.
Migration asks whom the country can protect now and which durable future it will support.
Each decision should change transport allocation, access, reception, policy responsibility, or a terminal outcome.
Routine distribution follows the response already chosen while its resources and proof remain valid.

The normal sequence is: identify the affected place and cause, choose one response commitment, resolve the physical obstacle if one exists, observe arrivals or food delivery, and make a durable settlement choice.
One mixed 60-day crisis should normally need four to seven strategic interventions in total.
A simple supply interruption should require one commitment and at most one correction.
These are interaction targets; a new front collapse, route attack, or destination loss legitimately creates another decision.

Keep Food Security, Food Reserves, and Relief Access for famine.
Keep Displacement Load, Reception Capacity, and Border Policy for migration.
Present reserves as days of coverage rather than unexplained thousand-person-days; keep the exact units internal.
Present reception as occupied/usable places with a readable comparison rather than two unconnected totals.
Policy remains one displayed supporting state, while departure and admission retain their separate owner contracts underneath.
Show one dominant cause and at most one secondary cause beside each value; keep the full formula in documentation.
Operation terms, projected delivery, and one deadline are contextual explanations, not additional permanent meters.

## Clear phases, real intervention time

Famine uses four public response phases over the existing five physical stages: watch/supply strain, acute response, emergency, and recovery.
Migration uses preparation/departure, protection/reception, and durable settlement; different exact cases can occupy different phases at the same time.
Country-wide phase summaries guide the interface but must not lock a ready cohort because an unrelated state is still in crisis.

| Phase | What appears | What the player does | Exit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Famine watch | Cause, current stock coverage, route condition; at most three responses. | Commit domestic rationing or restore the failing route before mortality. | Pressure sustained into acute stage or stable recovery confirmed. |
| Famine acute response | One funded operation and the remaining alternative; a physical deadline if relevant. | Supply in place, accept externally supervised aid, or relocate a proven endangered group. | Positive delivery changes trajectory, or sustained failure enters emergency. |
| Famine emergency | The same options with emergency terms; no extra button tier. | Pay for a limited bridge such as airlift while restoring a durable route, or evacuate. | Mortality risk falls and recovery persistence begins. |
| Famine recovery | Active commitment and its expected end; no repeat relief shopping list. | Continue funded routine work or deliberately release the commitment. | Owner-local stable retirement after the existing hysteresis and remaining famine-owned demand. |
| Migration preparation/departure | Destination readiness, departure policy, one evacuation priority. | Prepare/open departure, choose priority, or seek a corridor. | A measured arrival or no-route trapped obligation. |
| Migration protection/reception | Load against usable capacity, border stance, funded reception/distribution. | Protect arrivals, choose controlled medical terms only for exposed cohorts, or fund onward movement. | Each exact hosted cohort becomes eligible for its durable plan. |
| Migration settlement | Return, local settlement, or third-country settlement; coercive return only where the existing harmful policy permits it. | Commit to a future and finance its term. | Exact terminal outcome, symmetric load release, independent registry retirement. |

Proposed famine score anchors are 25/50/75/90 for strain/acute/famine/catastrophic, retaining the existing lower recovery thresholds initially.
Catastrophic entry also requires sustained failure of effective access or deliberately denied relief and depleted stock, so severe weather alone does not create a political catastrophe.
Use a 21-day catastrophe candidate window initially; retain the existing ordinary stage windows until the comparative model proves a reason to change them.
The goal is a reachable severe state under a plausible combination of intense local harms, rather than requiring all seven components at 100.
Calibrate to the scenario bands in `IMPLEMENTATION_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md`; threshold 90 is the starting proposal, not an approved balance target.
An alternative exposure-based catastrophe trigger is an open choice in that document, not a second system to implement beside this one.

Reserve release provides useful relief within one registered processing step after commitment, without an artificial 21-day wait.
Rail repair success follows actual rail/control restoration; physical repair duration remains the game player's construction/logistics problem.
Transport mobilization takes a proposed 3–7 days by damage/access band; mortality immediately reflects food already delivered, while stage recovery remains deliberately slower.
Relief and reception use 28-day funded terms with four weekly slices.
Durable integration uses 60 days initially, and return/resettlement uses a 7-day mobilization plus 7–28 days of proven movement/readiness.
There is no second payment click after completing an objective.

## Finite commitments and automatic routine work

A commitment has one actor, one exact subject or bounded set of up to three registered states, one route mode, one quote, one term, and one terminal receipt.
The normal presentation shows one selected case's operation and up to three urgent case summaries; this is an interface budget, not a national operation lock.
Parallel exact cases remain actionable whenever actual uncommitted transport, fuel, factory time, food and destination service permit them.
National reservations sum across both systems through narrow resource-allocation receipts, so choosing to serve one crisis can reduce another operation's affordable scale without arbitrarily hiding it.
Each operation serves its own bounded set automatically, and the category shows the specific shortage blocking a proposed parallel operation.
Emergency bridging is a second transport mode within its exact famine case with explicit remaining budget; other cases may still use their separately funded routes.
Existing corridor and protected-cohort contracts retain exact identity; presentation groups them without merging physical rows.

The player authorizes the whole term upfront.
Store the exact paid quote, reserved reusable equipment, actual expenditure, remaining food/movement service budget, actor authority, and expiry on the owning operation.
Weekly slices revalidate routes, capacity, policy, hazard, cohort identity, and current ownership before doing work.
They use existing sparse jobs; they never scan the world or issue spontaneous international agreements.

Routine stock release, admitted-arrival distribution, and already selected durable-plan progress may run without another click while the term is funded and its proof holds.
Routine work never changes border policy, starts harmful coercion, selects a new foreign country, withdraws protection, or extends a spending ceiling.
Automatic integration is permitted only for the exact cohorts named by the accepted durable plan.
An unsafe return does not silently convert into forced return or integration.

Funding exhaustion pauses work and shows the remaining obligation.
Route loss pauses only affected slices and releases unused consumables according to the payment contract; it does not erase people, mark success, or replace the destination.
Policy change invalidates incompatible work before the next slice.
Controller or actor loss freezes the operation for owner review and refunds/releases only resources whose original payer still exists and is entitled to them.
Expired terms stop; one renewal action can authorize another complete term with a fresh quote.
The default is no automatic paid renewal.
One loss-of-route notice, one exhaustion notice, and one terminal report per generation suffice; repeated unchanged pauses do not create report spam.

Food is a real finite ledger resource, not free points minted by the operation.
For land/sea/air modes it counts toward the four-cost limit even if its stock is paid by a donor; the full cross-actor contract names each payer.
Do not add PP as a fifth item.
Concealment is a term of procurement/governance policy, revealed only for actors with current authority and an actual public disclosure seam.
If concealment merely adds pressure and a two-percent stability reward without affecting reporting or aid, remove its separate button and keep existing evidence ownership.
Requisition is the coercive term of domestic supply with a named donor and its minimum retained stock, not a separate generic emergency answer.

## Concrete migration choices

Use one evacuation operation family with an evacuation priority chosen at each exact operation's start.
The two possible priority terms are vulnerable-first and productive-continuity.
Vulnerable-first devotes more support/medical service per person and suppresses the existing route-hazard loss component where medical protection has proof.
Productive-continuity moves the proven worker cohort or, where occupational identity is absent, cannot claim a worker-specific benefit.
It may preserve an existing production-recovery consumer only after measured arrival; it never transfers a factory or creates instant recruitable manpower.
If the implementation has no authentic worker-cohort identity or durable production consumer, merge the two lanes into one evacuation action and label the missing distinction as an accepted simplification only after an explicit decision.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Policy-only permission uses a small political setup cost at most once per generation. | Preparation persists through its term and subsequent exact slices; open departure permits already valid spontaneous movement. Restrictions preserve the living trapped obligation. |
| Protect and distribute | Proven hosted or admitted cohort, safe registered receiving places, actor can fund services. | 28 days; support equipment and 1–3 civilian factories committed according to served people, plus transport/fuel only where redistribution physically occurs. | Shelter/medical services and automatic safe internal distribution up to the explicit budget. Pure reception has no transport payment; one service producer/consumer proof contract. |

Unsafe origin pauses the plan. |

No automatic country substitution after rejection. |
| Coercive removal | Existing authorized harmful policy, exact cohort, actual destination, responsibility and hazard receipt. | Explicit security/transport terms with at most four types and a meaningful political consequence. | The population cannot disappear; direct policy and route losses retain exact attribution and anti-farming history. Automation never chooses this. |

Overloaded hosts should be able to start safe local settlement or third-country negotiation for an eligible cohort while another cohort remains active.
One authoritative terminal claim prevents simultaneous integration and return/resettlement.
Batch selection means separate bounded exact transactions for each chosen cohort, preserving identity and history.
The normal list offers at most three durable alternatives for the current case; harmful removal replaces the relevant ordinary route where context permits it, not a fifth permanent button.

## Quote design and allocation

Proposed served-population bands are small at up to 10,000 people, medium at up to 50,000, and large above 50,000.
An ordinary operation initially targets at most `min(proven outstanding people, live origin above protected floor, destination headroom, proven route throughput × term)`.
Food service instead targets `min(actual donor surplus, recipient stock headroom, proven transport throughput × term, served population need)` in the existing reserve units.
These are maximum budgets, not proof that all those people will move or all food will arrive.
Quotes show actual accepted scope and expected delivery range before payment.

Initial transport reservation anchors are 2/5/10 trains for land and 5/10/20 convoys for sea across those bands, reduced if the operation serves a smaller proven amount.
Initial fuel envelopes are 500/1,500/4,000 for one term, multiplied by 1.0/1.5/2.0 for short/strained/severely damaged proven access.
Initial support issues are 25/75/150 for ordinary service, doubled for exposed/vulnerable protection where the operator actually supplies it.
For air bridging begin with 2/5/10 transport aircraft and two times the fuel envelope, while keeping the quarter-throughput cap.
Treat these as bounded calibration anchors to be tested across major/minor countries and route types; centralize them, including caps, in owner constants.
Distance bands require actual proven route data; adjacency alone does not justify an invented mileage multiplier.

Reusable trains, convoys, and aircraft are committed, not automatically destroyed.
If the engine-supported reservation pattern removes them from available stock temporarily, the persisted paid quote must return precisely the unused/released amount once.
Fuel and actually issued support/food are consumed only as their slice executes.
A click rejected before any useful service refunds everything; a partly executed term refunds unused consumables and unlost transport, retaining actual expenditure and proven loss.
A cancelled action cannot refund more than it paid, reward a second actor, or return a replacement equipment model different from the exact original quote.
If reusable reservations cannot be implemented cleanly, leave the proposed contract blocked rather than substituting unexplained equipment destruction.

Each action has at most four spendable types; aim for two or three.
Inline native cost rows show at most three amounts with correct texticons.
The tooltip names every consumed or reserved resource, duration, release condition, and the actual payer.
Verify a matching texticon for food stock and every commitment type; a missing custom cost icon is an explicit scoped asset need, not permission to use literal resource-name cost labels.
Use the same fresh quote and inclusive affordability predicate for availability, cost trigger, activation debit, refunds, AI, and tooltip values.
Compare exact-budget, one-below, one-above, stale quote, and unavailable-route cases.

## AI commitment rules

AI shares human validity and payment gates.
It chooses one strategy for the current case, funds the term, and uses the same automatic slices rather than repeatedly scoring administrative clicks.
Safety, actual delivery feasibility, capacity, and protected population take priority over bounded ideology.
A starving isolated state with available air bridge and no timely surface route may bridge while repairing; a functional cheap surface route suppresses airlift.
An overfull host distributes or seeks a durable plan before expanding admission without capacity.
Persecution excludes return even when ideology matches; controlled medical entry requires actual exposure.
Harmful procurement/return needs the existing policy, material motive, and explicit accepted consequence; automated routine upkeep never initiates it.
An unchanged invalid target is retried only on a relevant dirty callback or at term review, not on every country pulse.

Weighted preferences, custom pools, and timing changes require the probability specialist workflow and same-scenario before/after comparison.
Concrete acceptance orderings and deterministic invariants are separated in `IMPLEMENTATION_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md`.
No exact normalized AI probability is inferred from the current score-only or incomplete-pool evidence.
