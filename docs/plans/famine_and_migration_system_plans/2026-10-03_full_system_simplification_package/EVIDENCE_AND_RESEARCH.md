## Parent handoff

The design problem is a sophisticated simulation whose public choices and physical/payment promises can diverge.
Recommended redesign: immediate useful response, finite funded upkeep, case-scoped physical objectives, parallel cases constrained by real national resources, and genuinely timed durable outcomes.
Keep three public values per independent mechanic, at most six actions per current case, at most four spendable types per action, explicit adapters and atomic conservation.
Files are in this new package only; no existing source/spec/catalog/asset/index edit was made by the planner.
Prior integration, probability and mapmode execution obligations remain open; their current owner census must be refreshed.
The redesign should stay in plans until accepted, then be folded into the existing eight-part spec pack with acceptance basis and validation limits.
