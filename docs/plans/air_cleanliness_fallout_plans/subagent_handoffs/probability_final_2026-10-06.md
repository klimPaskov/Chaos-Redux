## Relief decision source trace and baseline comparison

At `9ec627597`, `air_cleanliness_treaty_call_relief_vote` used native `cost = constant:air_cleanliness_treaty_joint_action.relief_vote_political_power`, whose value is 25. The working-tree version sets `cost = 0`, adds a `custom_cost_trigger` requiring political power at least that same constant, and displays `fallout_treaty_relief_vote_cost`. The whole `ai_will_do` block was extracted from baseline and current source and compared after newline normalization; the blocks are byte-equivalent after that normalization.

The unchanged willingness formula is `base = constant:air_cleanliness_treaty_joint_action_ai.relief_vote_base` with conditional factors `relief_vote_own_state_factor` when `FROM` is `ROOT`, `relief_vote_democratic_factor` for democratic government, and `relief_vote_war_factor` at war. Baseline and current constants are base 2, own-state factor 2, democratic factor 1.3, and war factor 0.5. These are source components, not an evaluated score. The offline wiki describes decision `ai_will_do` as an MTTH-style score, and the broader AI modding page distinguishes score races from `ai_chance` probability-proportional sampling. No value here is a click probability.

The current `available` block and `air_cleanliness_treaty_can_call_relief_vote` jointly require a live member, open operation phase, no open relief vote, no circulating-report flag, at least 25 political power, no outstanding paid-quote variable, no caller cooldown, a valid live recipient and recipient state, peace with that recipient, and at least one live, peaceful, non-recipient member holding the required support equipment. The helper `air_cleanliness_treaty_open_relief_vote` rechecks that trigger before mutating state. Only inside its successful branch does it call `air_cleanliness_treaty_charge_relief_vote_quote`; the fee helper saves the 25-point quote, negates it, and applies the political-power debit. An already-present quote prevents a second debit. A failed stale admission follows the uncharged branch.

The resolution path sets global `air_cleanliness_treaty_relief_reports_circulating` for `constant:air_cleanliness_treaty_joint_action.relief_vote_report_gap_days`, which is 14 days, and the admission trigger blocks new relief calls while that flag exists. The flag is global, so it blocks every member from opening another relief vote during the report window, not only the caller or the recipient whose result was just reported. The source proves the guard and requested duration; it does not prove that the intended event-notice overwrite is eliminated in a running game or establish the precise expiry tick.

The custom-cost documentation states that `custom_cost_trigger` displays a cost and does not deduct it, so an effect must perform the debit. It also warns that custom costs do not combine with native `cost` and that the AI will not reserve political power for them unless an `ai_hint_pp_cost` is provided. The current decision has no `ai_hint_pp_cost`. Although the availability trigger prevents taking the decision below 25 political power, the AI may fail to budget for the future fee as it did for the former native cost. This is a source-backed risk, not an observed AI failure.

## Findings, recommendations, and limits

The relief decision's numeric AI score is unchanged, but eligibility and payment semantics are not. The source correctly prevents admission when PP is below 25, when a paid quote is already outstanding, when the target or phase is invalid, when a vote or report window is open, or when no prospective relief donor exists. The positive AI score is consequently attached to an availability-gated decision, not an impossible target when those guards execute as written.

The decision score is not normalized against all other available decision candidates or every targeted member entry. No score dominance, starvation rate, rank reversal, or campaign repetition frequency was established. The global 14-day report lock creates a structural delay for unrelated recipients; whether that tradeoff is acceptable depends on the intended separation of notices from new relief calls.

Recommended follow-up for `air_cleanliness_treaty_call_relief_vote` is to decide whether to add `ai_hint_pp_cost = 25` so the AI can budget for the same fee it previously saw as native `cost`. Preserve the current eligibility and debit semantics while comparing that choice against the baseline with identical, MCP-validated scenarios; do not interpret the current base-2 score as a selection probability.

Recommended follow-up for `air_cleanliness_treaty_relief_reports_circulating` is a scenario with a second unrelated eligible recipient immediately after any vote result. That scenario should establish whether a global 14-day pause is intended for all relief calls or whether only report delivery needs spacing. No timing target was selected by this audit.

For the advisor migration, no numeric AI tuning is recommended. If a future audit needs selection or willingness results, the service needs an advisor/character adapter or a documented supported mapping to another adapter, plus a complete candidate pool and declared AI-strategy state. The current generic source inspect did not supply that support.
