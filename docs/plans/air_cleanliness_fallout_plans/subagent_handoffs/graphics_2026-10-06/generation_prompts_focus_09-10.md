## F14 — rejected atlas 09: Africa, Latin America, Oceania, Federation

References: canonical focus style, existing Fallout style, and focus-piece cues `focus_piece_hands_shaking.png` and `focus_piece_book_open.png`. The source was retained but not used because top and bottom icons abutted the horizontal split.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent source atlas of four separate Hearts of Iron IV national-focus medallion illustrations for the Fallout generic survivor-era focus tree. Generate an original 2x2 atlas on genuinely transparent canvas. Fixed placements: top-left = African River Wards; top-right = Latin American Water Charters; bottom-left = Oceania Remote Stations; bottom-right = Federate Survivor Leagues. Exactly one isolated complete medallion per cell, with a clear transparent gutter, no touch or overlap between cells. No grid lines, labels, letters, numbers, logos, flags, or watermark. Images 1 and 2 are focus style anchors only; transfer compact painted HOI4 relief, dark iron and aged brass, detailed but readable shading, and tiny-icon contrast without copying their subjects. Images 3 and 4 are object-composition cues only, not artwork to trace.
- African River Wards: a stone well, a small river crossing and a ward watch hut among warm ochre riverbanks, bronze and muted green, local water protection.
- Latin American Water Charters: a highland reservoir channel feeding a coastal pump-house through a terraced stone aqueduct, turquoise water, sandstone and copper seal, no writing.
- Oceania Remote Stations: an isolated harbor beacon beside a small island runway and sheltered fuel depot, deep blue-gray sea and warm brass lamp, remote convoy network.
- Federate Survivor Leagues: four distinct small unmarked community seals joined by radial bridge links around one shared central seal, slate blue and bronze, a federation with local autonomy.
Each focus icon must have a different silhouette, full visible rim, no portraits/faces, and stay readable at 94x86. Outside the four separate icons must be real transparent empty canvas, not solid color or checkerboard.
```
