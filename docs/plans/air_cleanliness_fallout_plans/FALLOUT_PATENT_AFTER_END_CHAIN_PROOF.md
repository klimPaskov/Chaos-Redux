## Runtime contract

The candidate is produced only for a current Manchurian Reactor Keeps Technate country in East Asia with a host state carrying `fallout_event_810_memory_closed`. The selector chooses the lowest eligible native state. Four branches pay real survival resources and share one generation-bound state transaction. The result waits 35 days and the second-year callback waits 270 days after the result.

The result and callback consume the shared Deaths contract for bounded state losses, update Air Winter and Supply Access through existing helpers, write branch and outcome memories, and record choice, result, callback, or cancellation payloads under history `9194`. The cleanup releases result and callback tickets independently and leaves no reservation flag behind.

No Fallout request, ordinary scenario row, super-event registration, on-action sweep, variable-only province proxy, or Zombie asset path is added.
