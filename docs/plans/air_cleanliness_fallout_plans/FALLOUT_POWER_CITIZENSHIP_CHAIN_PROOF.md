## Deterministic boundary

The candidate follows a closed Repair Charter state memory in the Manchurian Reactor Keeps. It requires Technate or Continuity Government identity, East Asia, durable state rows, a living controlled state, native industry or arms production, a foreign neighbor, Air Winter values, durable survival resources, and an affordable branch. The lowest eligible state id wins.

The registry freezes the state and neighbor receipts, owner and controller, transition generation, Air Winter, Supply Access, and power-citizenship ledgers. Every delayed lane revalidates those receipts. A changed receipt cancels the chain and cleanup returns paid resources.

## Branch and delayed mechanics

The four branches are Make Power a Public Service, Constitute an Engineer State, Build a Grid Empire, and Authorize a Machine Transition. They use distinct resource costs and write distinct memories, government routes, ledgers, dynamic modifiers, opinion modifiers, Event Log payloads, and authored localisations. Successful public-service and engineer-state branches retain `technate`, grid empire sets `continuity_government`, and machine transition sets `machine_protocol`.

The result delay is `77` days. The citizenship audit delay is `450` days. Success, partial, and failure outcomes alter Air Winter and Supply Access and can apply bounded Deaths through the existing country and state request path. Callback grading uses the power, cohesion, recognition, grid, civic, identity, and state receipts.

Human and hidden AI lanes share the same helper effects. Cleanup clears reservations, pending flags, delayed tickets, temporary deltas, neighbor receipts, and branch costs only when the authenticated transaction matches.
