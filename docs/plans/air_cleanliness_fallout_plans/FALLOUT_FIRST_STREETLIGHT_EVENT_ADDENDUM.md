# Fallout First Streetlight event addendum

First Streetlight adds a country-level urban recovery family after Black Start.
It treats the first public lamp as a decision about civic time, medical access,
industrial work, or guarded roads. Four authored priorities have distinct
costs, thresholds, durable ledgers, prose, AI priorities, modifiers, and Event
Log payloads.

The implementation surfaces are:

- candidate `317`, transaction `710019`, route `7119`
- events `chaosx.fallout.317` through `.323`
- history `9124` with fifteen payload values
- Power, Scrap, Medicine, Fuel, Cohesion, Recognition, Reclamation, Black Start
  capacity and governance, plus city identity, civic, production, security,
  and exposure variables
- dedicated First Streetlight report art and a registered sprite
- human and hidden AI parity, delayed result, callback, authenticated cleanup,
  Deaths-backed failure, and dormant scheduler state

The branch result is delayed by 35 days. The public maintenance callback is
delayed by 240 days. The result modifier lasts 360 days and the callback
modifier lasts 270 days. Failure rates are 0.06 percent for the result and
0.03 percent for the callback, both routed through Deaths.

This tranche is intentionally dormant until a live activation coordinator is
approved. It does not claim the engine-native all-province thermonuclear sweep,
blackout authority, save recovery, or multiplayer proof.
