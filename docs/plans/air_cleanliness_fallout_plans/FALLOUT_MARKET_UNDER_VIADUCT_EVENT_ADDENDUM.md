# Fallout Market Under the Viaduct event addendum

## Accepted scope

The Market Under the Viaduct is the next manually reviewed global-survival candidate after The Old Weather Station.
It uses a native supply-node and railway state with the dormant Fallout scheduler contract.

## Implementation surfaces

- candidate `380`, transaction `710028`, route `7128`
- events `380` through `386`
- four authored policies with distinct food, salvage, medicine, recognition, and governance costs
- 35-day result and 270-day civic review callback
- price, merchant, and ration ledgers
- Deaths-backed state failure and supply-node-first damage ladder
- dedicated Event Log history `9133`
- dedicated report art and runtime DDS

## Review boundary

The candidate is dormant. It is not counted toward the 660-block release floor until scheduler activation, caller ownership, save recovery, multiplayer behavior, Event Log delivery, and final audits are proven.
