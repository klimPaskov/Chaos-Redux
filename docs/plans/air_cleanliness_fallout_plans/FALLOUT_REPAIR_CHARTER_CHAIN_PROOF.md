## Deterministic state

The producer admits a current East Asian Technate or Continuity Government carrying Manchurian Reactor Keeps memory and one of the closed First Regional Standard branch or failure memories. It chooses the lowest owned state with the closed First Regional Standard state flag, current Air Winter and Supply Access receipts, surviving population, native industry or arms capacity, and a foreign neighbor.

The opening snapshots the state, owner, controller, generation, neighbor, Air Winter values, Supply Access, and repair ledgers. Any changed receipt, owner, controller, generation, or target state cancels the delayed lane and refunds the paid branch cost.

## Branch and result contract

The four authored branches are open repair charter, licensed repair houses, council charter, and perimeter corps. Each branch has its own resource cost, AI priority, result thresholds, Air Winter effects, repair ledger deltas, bilateral opinion modifier, and durable memory.

The result resolves after exactly 77 days. Failure damages one infrastructure level through the bounded effect and requests a small Deaths loss through the Fallout aftermath reason. The human and hidden AI result lanes share the same transaction and effect path.

## Audit and cleanup

The charter audit resolves after exactly 450 days. Its success, partial, and failure paths update repair ledgers, Air Winter, Supply Access, dynamic modifiers, bilateral opinion, and bounded Deaths outcomes. Cleanup authenticates the issued delayed cleanup ticket, releases both reservations, writes the state memory, clears the chain flags, and leaves the country and tags intact.
