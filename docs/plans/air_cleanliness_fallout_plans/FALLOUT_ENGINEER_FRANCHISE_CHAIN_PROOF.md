## Mechanics proof

The candidate is restricted to the East Asia region, Manchurian Reactor Keeps country memory, the technate archetype, and a closed Reactor Without a Country memory. It selects the lowest eligible owned state with Air Winter shelter, Supply Access, Adaptation, Exposure, Disease Pressure, population, a power or industrial building, and an external neighbor.

The four branches use distinct survival costs and government-aware effects. The delayed result freezes the state and country receipts, resolves after `35` days, applies Air Winter, Supply Access, Medicine, Recognition, Cohesion, Stability, War Support, technical ledgers, bilateral opinion, a selected government route, and bounded Deaths failure. The callback resolves after `270` days and grades every technical ledger before authenticated cleanup.
