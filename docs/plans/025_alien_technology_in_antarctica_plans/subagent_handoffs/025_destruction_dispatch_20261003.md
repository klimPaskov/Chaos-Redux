## Implemented behavior

The existing `chaosx_nr25_dependence_destroy` decision dispatches through the common evolution executor and `chaosx_nr25_execute_destroy_material`.
The preflight requires enabled Evolution V, an actionable expedition/aftermath state, valid independent institutions, scientific capacity, consistent physical custody, and both real resource commitments.
The inclusive fees are 50 support equipment and 0.02 stability, stored in the event's custody-policy constants.
Support is removed through the existing `remove_support_equipment_from_stockpile` helper with its documented positive input.
Only a successful physical retirement receipt publishes costs, the action cooldown, 60 points of bounded Dependence relief, exposure consequences, and policy flags.
Rejected or stale dispatch leaves those outcomes unset and does not call the shared Chaos or presentation tail.

The transaction permanently retires only the caller's core and fragment slots, preserves learned technology and historical winner receipts, rebuilds country counts and aligned six-slot projections, and leaves another country's fragments alone.
Running containment is removed after clearing its active context, using native `remove_mission` without completion or timeout effects.
A specific containment-only exception permits this choice during the active race after its action cooldown, without bypassing unrelated phase missions.
Both Dependence streaks are reset.
The retired package cannot trigger material-driven Dependence growth or accidents and cannot support further integration, even through a stale direct integration dispatch.
New physical material re-arms those predicates without resurrecting destroyed slots.

Native affordability, complete-effect revalidation, and the four cost-display branches share the same support/stability predicates.
The native ready, blocked, and hover cost keys are defined, and requirement wording distinguishes no active project, running containment, and an unrelated phase project.
The cost row uses the existing support-equipment and vanilla stability texticons.
No image, new equipment, formation, technology grant, portrait, country, or world scan is introduced.
Existing AI willingness scores are unchanged.
