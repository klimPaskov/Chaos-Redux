# Event 025 pulse and analysis lifecycle, 2026-10-03

Disposition: implemented within the user-authorized Event 025 rework.
Acceptance basis: the Expedition Board and Evolution V must support verified losing fragment holders, preserve multiplayer country independence, use a bounded scheduled pulse, and track the stated continuous achievement intervals.

## Behavior

A participant has at most one outstanding chaosx.nr25.280 callback.
The callback clears its pending flag before processing and scheduling the next country event.
Registration, winning settlement, losing settlement, and the pulse tail use the same scheduler.

Registered losing fragment holders open their own Evolution V aftermath state and receive pulses.
Race weather, survivor crew loss, outpost seizure pressure, and wreck decay stop when the primary race ends.
Dependence incidents and achievement clocks remain country-owned.

Settlement clears the phase-action cooldown so analysis is usable even when no Evolution V aftermath clock is required.
A losing expedition archiving its record no longer gains the winner-only research idea.
Its verified survey and fragment rewards remain intact.

Sealing an archive closes only that country's board and aftermath.
The shared analysis state closes after a transaction-time check of the stored registry finds no remaining existing, unsealed participant.
No daily or weekly world scan is introduced.

The containment and integration counters represent continuous intervals.
Leaving the safe Dependence band resets containment time.
An accident or loss of the integration band resets stable integration time.
