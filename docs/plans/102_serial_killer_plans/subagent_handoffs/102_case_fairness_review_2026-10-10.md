## Focused findings

### F1. N1 retry refers to a former correct answer that its original inquiry never establishes

Evidence: Part 3 N1 first deduction, line 137, makes the caretaker a witness to a known cell member admitting fugitives.
Its second deduction, line 139, follows the organizer controlling admissions.
The retry, line 149, calls the earlier answer "caretaker-first".
That role reference is inconsistent with the written original.
This is a proved wording defect, not proof that the admissions puzzle is unsolvable.
Minimal clarification: refer to the former on-site admissions controller, while retaining the distinction between a witnessing caretaker and a person with admission authority.

### F2. G2 D1's painting alternative has a possible fact-timing ambiguity

Evidence: Part 2 G2 D1, lines 197 to 215, supplies the three exact sightline comparisons and calls the painting superficially similar.
The painting's concrete round-bowl and missing-fruit differences appear at D2, lines 217 to 223.
The worked later disproof repeats those differences at lines 251 to 256.
Tracing the exact alcove view is supported as a line of inquiry.
The evidence does not prove a fairness violation if "superficially similar" is visibly demonstrated and the alternative is merely an unsupported attribution to the owner.
If D1 instead asks the player to exclude copying from that painting, it needs its distinguishing comparison at D1.
Minimal clarification: make that limited inquiry framing explicit, or show the already specified painting mismatch at that point.
No new source fact or new deduction is needed.

### F3. N1's remote-authority retry needs its containment scope stated

Evidence: Part 3 line 133 requires four organizational deductions and a supported containment operation.
Original N1 D3/D4, lines 141 to 145, supplies actual residents, current presence and separate treatment of absent offenders.
The retry, line 149, supplies remote control and a local intermediary without stating whether the remote organizer is physically reachable or what current members the concluding operation contains.
The authority reversal is answerable.
A literal reuse of the original on-site-organizer transfer would be unsupported.
The current text does not demand that reuse, so it does not prove an impossible arrest.
Minimal clarification: identify which original D3/D4 evidence requirements carry into this new refuge, and state whether the result removes local support or also contains the remote organizer.
Preserve any absent dangerous participant as an unresolved real record under the existing N1 result rule.
N2/N3 likewise need their inherited current-site and membership facts in the delivered recap, but their changed authority and shared-dependency descriptions do not prove a contradictory operation.
