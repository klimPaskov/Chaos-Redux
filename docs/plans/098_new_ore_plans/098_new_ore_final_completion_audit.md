## Core strength and analytical checks

The armor contract is unusually strong and is consistently treated as an effective outcome. The baseline comparison includes unrelated armor bonuses and the independent Event 028 benefit. With a hypothetical measured asteroid-only armor value of 200, baseline New Ore requires at least 300, Deep Vein at least 350, and one Super-Alloy source at least 600. P5 marks these as examples rather than asserted native stacking behavior. P9 explains why an additive raw +50% can fail the required ratio when other positive modifiers exist.

The Super-Alloy expression simplifies to `300% − 100%/n`. For a positive direct source count, its first source gives +200%, its second +250%, and its twelfth approximately +291.6667%. The marginal gain between n and n+1 is `100% / (n × (n + 1))`. It is positive and decreases with source count. Analytical evaluation for counts 1–12 confirms strict increase, diminishing increments and a value below +300%. The one-source floor remains +200% when additional sources are lost. This is mathematical evidence about the declared curve, not native armor evidence. Sources: P2 and P9.

The additional-discovery formula is explicitly linear rather than compounded. The scarcity examples are consistent with the declared formula, including a two-state test map. Temporary controller invalidity does not shrink the recorded playable map size, and later compatibility changes cannot delete established deposits merely to match a smaller ceiling. Sources: P2 and P8.

The declared positive Event 098 Chaos maximum with twelve deposits is 52: initial manifestation 2, first foreign access 2, actual deeper grade 3, eleven later states totaling 22, first intercontinental spread 3, first attributable ore war 5, direct operational Super-Alloy 10, and first concentration gain 5. The one possible peace counterpart subtracts 3. Evolution activation alone grants zero. The +10 receipt also owns the single global reveal. Controller cycling, callbacks and renewal cannot replay these receipts. This accounting is analytically consistent and remains subject to real provenance and shared-source validation in A41. Sources: P5, P6 and P9.

## Agreement lifecycle and exploit review

The agreement flow has a single counteroffer, fresh final confirmation, payment only on successful activation, and explicit source/actor/cap revalidation. Three spendable components are the maximum used in the planned commercial/security actions. Exact debit and supplier credit must consume one normalized quote, including discounts. Rollback returns only the components actually paid and does so once. These rules address phantom access, overbooking, equipment creation and stale scope. Sources: P3, P9 and H1/H5.

Continuous renewal can prepay one future period and starts that period at the existing expiry. It does not discard the current purchased days or charge adaptation again. Buyback includes the unused fraction of the current actual equipment receipt and the entire actual future receipt before that future period starts. Reassignment preserves both receipts and dates and requires consent before any source loss. Supplier-caused or unattributed non-hostile relationship changes protect already purchased current and future periods. Renewing again still requires lawful current qualification. Sources: P3 and H5.

The friendly-transfer escape found during this audit is closed in the source design. Event-controlled voluntary peaceful source sales and cessions cannot commit until every affected unused paid period settles through real supplier stock. Event 092 gets the verified settlement result and must not pay it again. Other already effective non-hostile native transfers still change direct access immediately and terminate old source agreements. An unsettled prepaid delivery loss then blocks every AI country from purchasing any new prepaid commercial/security period from the former supplier for 720 days. The gate covers recipient requests, supplier offers, renewals and other sources. Reacquisition, better terms, repeated callbacks and an unrelated war cannot erase it. Humans see the history and can consciously accept risk. Already paid valid agreements continue. This records failed delivery without inventing intent or a new debt system. Genuine hostile capture and imposed wartime settlement remain disclosed no-refund risks. Sources: P3/P5/P8/P9 and H1/H5/H6.

The design also rejects permanent exported ore equipment, command-transfer laundering, foreign-licence stacking, repeated original discovery, claim-based direct access, subject-based source counting, revival of extinct-tag licences, copied civil-war spirits and sham defense rewards. Dates and receipt identity govern sustained conditions and once-only outcomes. Those are concrete prevention rules and future tests, not claims of empirical immunity. Sources: P1, P3, P7/P8 and A12–A26/A31/A42–A44.

The contract records are detailed enough to preserve a real bargain but remain bounded. There are three agreement families, two or three places per source, six distinct foreign customers per supplier, two agreements per recipient, at most three displayed pending negotiations, one counteroffer, and one prepaid future period. They operate through ordinary decisions, offers and native relationships. There is no new diplomacy currency, prestige ladder, extraction meter, automatic faction cartel, or independent global auction. The two public aggregate values remain armor advantage and direct source count. Sources: P1, P3/P4/P6 and H5.
