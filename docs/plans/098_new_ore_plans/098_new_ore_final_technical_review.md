## Armor composition and source aggregation

The effective ratio is defined against an otherwise identical ore-free result, including Event 028 and other independent contributions. For ore-free armor 100, the direct floors are 150, 175 and 300 at the three grades. An otherwise equivalent result of 200 with the separate asteroid material requires 300, 350 and 600. These examples express accepted composition. They do not establish the game's internal modifier order.

The additive-bucket failure is correctly retained in part 9. An unrelated +50% already raising 100 to 150 would become 200 after another raw +50% in the same bucket. That is only a 33.33% effective increase. The handoff therefore requires either a proven independent multiplier or a properly scoped compensating approach. A larger fixed coefficient cannot guarantee the floor for unbounded independent bonuses.

Coverage is not limited by equipment whose category happens to be named armor. The intrinsic elephant contribution and the coal-golem case remain explicit acceptance examples. A combined equipment and intrinsic subunit consumer receives one intended effective ratio across its resulting armor. Neither missing an intrinsic contribution nor double-applying another is acceptable. Armor-zero consumers remain zero. Aircraft defense and armor-themed module names are not evidence of an actual qualifying armor statistic.

For Super-Alloy, `D(n) = 200% + 100% × (1 − 1/n)` is monotonic, with decreasing additional gains and an asymptote below +300%. One, two and three sources give +200%, +250% and +266.666…%. Compatibility counts above the ordinary ceiling remain mathematically continuous. Before Super-Alloy, extra sources confer resilience, capacity and denial rather than unannounced additive armor.

Highest manifested grade controls delivery. Deep Vein after Super-Alloy still records the original vein and increases export places to three, but cannot downgrade the grade, reprice paid terms or receive a false +3 grade-gain Chaos award. Foreign sources use the strongest valid foreign result, and direct access takes precedence. Losing the last direct source can immediately expose an already valid foreign backup.

## Payment, renewal and transfer audit

The final agreement contract consistently distinguishes unpaid proposal, confirmation, current paid period, at most one future paid period, protection, reassignment and terminal invalidation. No proposal reserves a lasting place or grants access. Confirmation revalidates current controller, actors, diplomacy, capacity and the fresh normalized quote. Partial activation restores only actual successful payments once.

The dynamic price uses controlled military factories plus dockyards regardless of current queue assignment. That closes the temporary production-unassignment price exploit. Ordinary equipment quantities are rounded upward, then eligible shared discounts apply through the same quote used for inclusive affordability and debit. Supplier credit equals actual payer stock removed. Buyback and rollback consume actual receipts rather than reconstructed undiscounted anchors. The known shared helper documentation/code mismatch is explicitly a dependency to resolve, not presumed fixed.

Early renewal starts at the existing expiry and does not discard purchased days. It has its own equipment quote, duration and receipt, occupies the same place and adds no repeated adaptation XP for uninterrupted access. At most one future period prevents an unbounded prepaid queue. Buyback refunds the floor of each actual current-period component multiplied by the unused fraction, plus the entire actual future-period component before that period starts. It requires real supplier stock. XP after successful adaptation remains spent.

The smallest coherent security anti-abuse policy is now integrated. Supplier-caused non-hostile qualification loss preserves already paid commercial/security access through the purchased period or a real buyback. Unknown attribution uses the same conservative protection. This includes a future period confirmed while qualified. Proven recipient breach ends access, and another renewal needs currently valid qualification. The policy does not require undoing native diplomacy or inventing reliable cause attribution. Faction allocation remains conditional on faction membership and has no prepaid equipment income.

The final source-transfer policy closes the analogous territorial loophole:

- An event-controlled voluntary peaceful transfer by the supplying controller settles all affected actual unused current and future equipment periods before transfer. The transfer owner receives a verified result and cannot commit unpaid settlement or debit the receipt twice.
- A title-only transfer with unchanged actual control neither removes armor nor terminates valid contracts.
- An already effective external non-hostile control transfer remains immediate and ends the old source agreements. Unsettled prepaid delivery records a 720-day supplier-wide absolute AI eligibility block on all new prepaid commercial/security periods, including requests, supplier offers and renewals at other sources.
- Actual hostile capture and imposed wartime territorial peace remain the disclosed no-refund risks. A friendly side deal during an unrelated war does not acquire hostile provenance merely from the supplier's war status.

The block follows the supplier rather than the source, so reacquisition, another deposit, premium terms and a changed callback cannot erase it. Already paid valid periods remain honored. Human customers receive the history and can consciously accept risk. This bounds AI prepayment farming without delaying real control, fabricating compensation or asserting unproved malicious intent. Reassignment requires prior consent and cannot retroactively rescue a terminated source.

## Timing, political lifecycle and escalation

The physical deposit is distinct from country ideas, contracts and payments. No valid controller suspends the direct contribution and terminates source contracts while retaining geological history. A restored former controller or released tag does not revive terminated licenses. A subject's own control grants its own armor rather than automatic overlord access. Paid protection remains applicable to otherwise valid non-hostile commercial/security periods after puppet status changes, while new purchases need current legality.

Stored campaign dates and episode identity govern access, deadlines, achievements and evolution opportunities. Chaos loss or an unmanifested disabled toggle pauses earned progress. Manifested grade and geology persist. Repeated callbacks and save loading do not redraw full delays or extend confirmed periods. The MTTH reference intervals are honestly treated as calibration parameters whose actual distribution vocabulary must be established later.

The map-sized deposit ceiling and linear additional-source cadence are explicit rather than an unspecified repeated 20% multiplication. For a playable pool of 1,000 states the ceiling is eight, and for 500 it is four. Small maps cannot exceed their valid-state population. Pool exhaustion and the scarcity ceiling have truthful terminal or waiting handling.

Demand acceptance that cannot complete payment, capacity or qualification closes as uncompleted acceptance. It grants neither access nor refusal credit. Delivered attention remains spent. Demand failure, refusal, preparation and peace cleanup preserve unrelated native objectives. State-targeting AI evidence is required beyond generic country hostility. The defense mission requires a real continuing threat and force commitment, with neutral cancellation if the threat ends and no reward for merely activating while controlling a source.

The Navy XP entry condition is now explicit: one deployed positive-armor ship or a verified active positive-armor production/refit project suffices. Five deployed armored capital ships is the higher effort factor, not the minimum naval eligibility threshold. One selected eligible XP branch buys the same foreign armor coverage and does not create separate land and naval licenses.
