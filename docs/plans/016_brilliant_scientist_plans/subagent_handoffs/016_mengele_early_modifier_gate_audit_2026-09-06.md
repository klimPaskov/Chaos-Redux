## Cleanup and persistence

`brilliant_scientist_mengele_cleanup_provider_receipts` at private stage-effects line 1389 cancels active payments and clears availability flags while preserving durable completion flags and neutral entitlements.
It does not explicitly remove early-stage modifiers or restore them when a provider becomes valid again.
The modifier removal predicates therefore own early-stage invalidation after program closure or defeat.
A receipt can survive even after its modifier is removed.
Changing a gate does not reattach a modifier that has already been removed, and the private predecessor prevents replaying an already-completed stage solely to attach its bonus.
The parent subsequently authorized the bounded exact-receipt restoration helper documented in `016_mengele_early_modifier_restoration_2026-09-06.md`.
That implementation resolves the reattachment gap when the existing private reconciliation entry point runs, without a new scheduler or stage-reward replay.

The shared `brilliant_scientist_disable_project_stage_modifiers` at `016_brilliant_scientist_project_effects.txt:726` explicitly removes selected-family stage modifiers, then reconciles permanent conventional packages.
Its observed call sites are the Kruger inheritance loop, project dismantle helper, and host-transfer loop.
The private stage and bridge files do not invoke it.
The custom operational runtime clear/rebuild in `016_brilliant_scientist_project_force_effects.txt:51,428` does not remove these thirty modifier IDs.
This gate patch does not change explicit removal or transfer semantics outside the private lifecycle.
