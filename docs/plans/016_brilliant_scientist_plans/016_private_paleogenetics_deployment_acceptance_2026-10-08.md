## Construction transaction

The player freely selects a reserve and a distinct hatchery from owned and controlled states.
Both sites need existing infrastructure and room for their intended construction, and the hatchery needs an unlocked shared building slot.
Selection creates no building, payment or completion history and can be changed only while no paid deployment is pending.
The existing Deployment retains its 130 political power, 975 support equipment, 1,500 fuel, four native civilian factories and 480-day schedule.
The stage quote remains the authoritative direct-payment source.
Native availability checks the free factory commitment, and the complete-effect entry rechecks direct resources without charging the native reservation twice.
At payment, the selected actor and state pointers and the quantity of one infrastructure level at the reserve and one civilian factory at the hatchery are frozen.
The private paid proof is durable and actor bound.

Completion measures each site's native building level immediately before and after its own construction attempt.
Only a measured increment caused by that attempt is credited as delivery.
Competing construction neither satisfies the receipt nor changes the paid quantity, and full sites can wait for a slot without causing duplicate construction.
Successful components are never repeated on recovery.
An authenticated partial result retains its paid authority and offers free recovery of only the undelivered output.
Cancellation before any output refunds the three recorded direct costs once.
Cancellation after a partial output retains the paid receipt and physical output, without a full refund or demolition of other construction.
Deployment history is recorded only after both measured outputs exist.
Current site loss suspends private production without deleting learned technology or existing stores.
