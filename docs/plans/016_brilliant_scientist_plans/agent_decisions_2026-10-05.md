# Event 016 unit presentation decisions

Acceptance basis: the user assigned the complete WP4 repair tickets, including shared clone, portal, robot and alien unit consumers.
This record covers their presentation and recorded audio; project gameplay is outside this tranche.

## Alien idle

Select the ticket's explicit alternative to remove the one-shot vehicle hum from the looping idle state.
The archived cue plays once and then falls silent; treating it as a seamless suit or creature loop would assert source and edge evidence it lacks.
Its original file and definition remain intact while the idle event stops invoking it.
The idle posture, breathing, head scan and weapon recoil still require deliberate Blender authoring.
Defend is the ticket's pistol-up scan rather than an invented firing action; no discharge event is added without an authored shot.

## Clone firing

Attack and support attack lead into the existing defend action, an explicitly permitted successor in the ticket.
The delivered attack/support actions fail the seam gate; self-chaining them would repeat their discontinuity.
Defend has a passing loop gate and receives the measured rifle flash, report and bolt-cartridge events.
The inherited simulated report is replaced with a source-bound CC0 recording of a Lee-Enfield report.

## Portal and robot audio

The portal electrical loop is rebuilt from the exact archived CC BY 4.0 original and is started once per idle/guard entry.
The one-second loop passes the measured seam and loudness gates; it is limited to one audible source.
Robot attack/support use eleven edited recorded reports per gun, arranged at 0.25 second intervals; defend uses four reports at 0.1667 second intervals.
Each gun triggers its completed burst cue once at the existing first discharge, with volume 0.36 and maximum two audible sources.
The two-gun overlap is intentional; repeated reverberant report tails and repeated smoke emitters are removed.

Full source hashes, derivative measurements, remaining authoring and take-coverage requirements are recorded in `docs/plans/unit_fix_2026-10/wp4_gun_troopers_report.md` and its linked evidence.
