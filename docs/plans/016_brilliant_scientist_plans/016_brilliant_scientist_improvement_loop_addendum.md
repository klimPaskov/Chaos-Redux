## R1. International recognition becomes a major news threshold [rejected]

### Historical recommendation, rejected

Do not give international recognition a full super-event package.

Evolution II is an escalation of an active system, not an irreversible campaign transformation. Event 016 already reserves full super-events for sovereign state formation, an existential Kruger threat, Laboratory World, Strategic Singularity, and a qualifying global defeat. A recognition super-event would consume a unique track, quote package, button remark, 457x328 image, sound wiring, and documentation while overlapping the later formation and threat reveals.

### Replacement surface

Retain a conditional major news event with a dedicated 397x153 image and follow-up country reactions. It should require a verified global relevance threshold, such as a public strategic Prototype plus sustained foreign contest, an international demonstration, or a failed attack that exposes the program. Evolution II alone is insufficient.

The news event may update foreign interest, open inspection or invitation actions, and record that Kruger is internationally known. It must not reuse a generic placeholder image or pretend to be a super-event without its full package.

### AI

- Public-science AI can deliberately cross the recognition threshold for prestige and cooperation.
- Secret hosts should avoid it unless exposure, an accident, or a failed foreign operation forces disclosure.
- Foreign AI should react through existing invitation, theft, protection, challenge, and countermeasure actions.

### Asset effect

- Remove one 457x328 super-event image, one unique music package, one quote and remark research package, and one super-event slot.
- Add one dedicated 397x153 recognition news image for the international contest threshold.
- The super-event inventory becomes four required packages plus the conditional global-defeat package.

### Acceptance

- No recognition super-event can fire.
- The recognition news event cannot fire from Evolution II alone.
- Secret and public routes reach the news threshold differently.
- Country reactions use the existing foreign-interest system.
- No final super-event asset or audio manifest lists recognition.

## R2. Host takeover remains a rare institutional-capture route

### Decision

Keep host takeover because it represents a distinct campaign result. Kruger captures the existing state through institutions instead of creating a laboratory secession.

It must not be used merely because territory selection failed, the host is small, or a new tag would be inconvenient. Those are technical fallback conditions and are not valid route triggers.

### Exact route contract

Host takeover requires all of the following design categories:

- Evolution III or IV permits sovereign science.
- Institutional Dependence is in its extreme band.
- Government Control is visibly compromised or lost.
- Campaign history proves control across multiple independent national domains. Valid domains include military command, civil administration, communication and logistics, laboratory security, clone replacement, machine administration, or temporal authority.
- At least one state-wide control domain exists. A private guard at one laboratory is not enough.
- The host has received multiple visible incidents that communicate the takeover risk.
- The confrontation outcome is not being rerolled after a failed territorial split.

All thresholds and domain weights belong in documented script constants. Territorial invalidity may change the available non-takeover resolutions, but it never grants takeover eligibility by itself.

### Gameplay identity

- Preserve the host tag and territory where technically appropriate.
- Apply a Kruger political and cosmetic transformation.
- Load the same Kruger focus architecture with an `origin_takeover` state that bypasses secession survival work.
- Begin with institutional consolidation, loyal-command cleanup, resistance inside the old state, and project-history inheritance.
- Do not create a second takeover-only focus tree, project portfolio, or duplicate Kruger State route family.

### AI

- AI may choose takeover only when the route contract is already true and a sovereign Kruger strategy is active.
- AI must not choose it as the easiest way to avoid a difficult rebellion or invalid territory.
- Opposing AI receives resistance, government-restoration, and counter-Kruger actions based on actual surviving institutions.

### Acceptance

- A small host with no institutional capture cannot transform merely because a split would be awkward.
- A prepared machine, clone, temporal, or command-network route can transform the state without a duplicate tag.
- The takeover origin has distinct opening content and correct focus bypasses.
- No full-power Kruger remains duplicated in a former host roster.

## R3. Paleogenetics and xenobiological monsters receive separate identities

### Decision

Keep both project families. The source fantasy is already different, but the current production and focus descriptions overlap too heavily around breeding, handlers, feed, containment, and creature units.

### Paleogenetics contract

- Source is reconstruction of extinct terrestrial organisms from incomplete evidence.
- Core facilities are breeding reserves, hatcheries, transport pens, and handler schools.
- Main burdens are feed, land, handlers, transport, veterinary capacity, and long replacement time.
- Military roles are reconnaissance, rough-terrain transport, intimidation, and heavy dinosaur or megafauna shock.
- Civil routes can produce prestige, protected reserves, scientific tourism, and controlled ecological restoration.
- Failure is escape, breeding collapse, habitat damage, or ecological spread.
- Counters are feed denial, handler loss, air attack, anti-armor weapons, transport interdiction, and reserve capture.

### Xenobiological synthesis contract

- Source is deliberate design of new organisms from modular anatomy, artificial tissue, and optional exotic material.
- Core facilities are growth vats, medical fabrication laboratories, control centers, and sealed containment cells.
- Main burdens are medical capacity, reagents, power, control infrastructure, and containment integrity.
- Military roles are laboratory defense, tunneling, fortification breaking, sensing, adaptive support, and specialized assault.
- Every deployed line selects a control method, such as chemical, neural, machine-linked, or another researched interface. This method determines both benefit and countermeasure.
- Failure is mutation, control-channel loss, unauthorized reproduction, or an autonomous nest.
- Counters are identifying and breaking the control method, destroying growth laboratories, isolating command systems, and protecting human handlers.

### Focus and unit separation

- The two families may share a broad Bestiary lane for readability.
- They must split into separate openers, production decisions, unit templates, accident missions, counters, and capstones.
- They converge only through an accepted Synthesis route or explicit machine or clone synergy.
- A paleogenetic stage never grants a monster unit. A xenobiological stage never grants a restored dinosaur unit.

### AI

- AI chooses paleogenetics for terrain, transport, intimidation, food access, and a stable handler route.
- AI chooses xenobiological synthesis for fortified war, laboratory defense, medical capacity, and a viable control method.
- AI cannot build either force when its distinct supply system is missing.

### Asset effect

- Keep separate project icon progressions.
- Keep separate unit silhouettes, facility scenes, accident scenes, and countermeasure icons.
- A combined Bestiary category icon cannot replace family-specific stage and unit assets.

### Acceptance

- Each family has a unique production decision, operational role, failure mission, and countermeasure.
- Rebellion inheritance reads the exact family stage.
- Supply failure in one family does not use the other family's generic penalty.
- Focus and localisation never call designed monsters restored species or call dinosaurs synthetic organisms.

## R4. Temporal power gains a bounded counterplay contract

### Decision

Keep the existing temporal branch and add exact limits. Paradox pressure alone is not enough unless the player and AI can see how temporal actions create vulnerability and how opponents can exploit it.

`Temporal debt` is the implementation-facing form of the existing paradox-pressure concept. It replaces that loose pressure label as the tracked route burden. It is not a second parallel resource.

### Temporal action contract

- Every meaningful temporal action consumes synchronization capacity and adds temporal debt.
- The UI exposes named timeline-strain bands and causes, not the hidden arithmetic.
- Temporal debt does not passively disappear.
- A stabilization mission reduces debt while disabling temporal actions, occupying the relevant facility, and exposing a known window of weakness.
- Severe actions leave a persistent timeline scar that raises later costs or closes one repeated outcome.
- A temporal action can affect only a declared crisis window, named project component, named leader, or bounded unit package.
- No action rewrites an arbitrary completed battle, repeats an achievement condition, or restores unlimited equipment and divisions.
- Each eligible target records use so that the same loss cannot be recovered repeatedly.

### Opponent counterplay

- Counter-Kruger actors need temporal evidence before seeing temporal actions.
- Evidence unlocks record-authentication and observation work.
- Successful observation identifies a synchronization anchor or temporal ledger site.
- Capturing, isolating, or disabling the anchor blocks recovery actions tied to that network.
- A captured ledger unlocks false-order detection, paradox pressure, or a component-specific disarmament action.
- Bombing an unknown site does not safely cancel temporal work.

### Failure content

Use the existing paradox-crisis family for outcomes such as duplicate command, loss of one recorded project state, a hostile future Kruger, or a temporary collapse of authentication. Do not add another temporal resource beyond synchronization capacity, temporal debt, and the existing project history.

### AI

- AI spends temporal capacity only during a genuine capital, core facility, leader-continuity, or singularity crisis.
- AI begins stabilization when timeline strain crosses its route band.
- AI opponents prioritize identified anchors only after evidence exists.

### Acceptance

- Every temporal action has a target, capacity cost, debt increase, use record, AI rule, and opposing countermeasure.
- Stabilization has a meaningful duration and disables the action family while active.
- Loss of an anchor blocks the linked benefit.
- The temporal achievement requires a real crisis and successful paradox closure.

## R5. Alien origin uses campaign-specific conclusions

### Decision

Do not establish one universal Kruger origin. Use mutually exclusive campaign conclusions and allow uncertainty to persist when evidence remains insufficient.

The conclusion is selected from actual project and cross-event history, not a free final dialogue choice.

### Conclusion families

#### Extraterrestrial provenance

Requires independent exotic-material, biological, interface, or alien-event evidence. This conclusion can qualify for `016_brilliant_scientist_not_from_here` after a public proof event.

#### Temporal displacement

Requires a coherent temporal evidence chain that identifies Kruger as a human or post-human arrival from another time. This does not qualify as proof of alien origin.

#### Manufactured continuity

Requires clone, machine, or synthesis history showing that the observed nonhuman Kruger is a transformation or successor rather than proof about the original arrival. This does not qualify as proof of alien origin.

#### Unresolved origin

Remains valid when no evidence family reaches its proof threshold. Rumors and one anomalous test cannot force a conclusion.

### Consistency rules

- A campaign can lock at most one proof conclusion.
- Later text can challenge interpretation but cannot award a contradictory proof flag.
- Public localisation distinguishes origin from later transformation.
- The alien achievement requires extraterrestrial provenance, several independent evidence sources, and public proof.
- The xenobiological or alien severe portrait package serves the route actually proven. Do not add a sixth parallel animated portrait package solely for a second alien label.

### AI

- AI publicizes a conclusion only when evidence, ideology, diplomatic value, and exposure support it.
- Secret AI may keep proof classified for security and project access.
- AI never selects a conclusion without the required evidence history.

### Acceptance

- No campaign can hold contradictory proof conclusions.
- Early appointment and Evolution I text remain uncertain.
- Nonhuman transformation alone cannot unlock the alien-origin achievement.
- The unresolved state remains fully playable.

## R6. Merge the safe-route achievements and retain sixteen [rejected]

### Historical recommendation, rejected

Retire the working key `016_brilliant_scientist_clean_break` and merge its distinctive conditions into `016_brilliant_scientist_public_method`.

The two current achievements test the same safe institutional campaign. Both require reduced Dependence, independent knowledge, no violent rebellion, no biological catastrophe, and a peaceful Kruger resolution. Keeping both creates two icons and tracking paths for one story while the other fifteen achievements test distinct routes.

### Retained combined achievement

Keep `016_brilliant_scientist_public_method` with the working identity `The Method Belongs to Everyone`.

The combined challenge requires:

- Public appointment or a completed public-governance reform.
- Publication of the Kruger Method.
- The full independent-replication requirement from the source spec.
- Dependence in the accepted safe band.
- A functioning permanent institutional research legacy.
- Peaceful retirement, safe dismissal, or a binding public settlement that ends Kruger's exclusive control.
- No violent Kruger State formation.
- No assassination, assistant massacre, offensive biological weapon use, or fail-deadly release.
- No duplicate full-power Kruger left in another active appointment.

Do not make the merge an easier OR condition. It is one harder safe-route achievement.

### Inventory effect

- Final Event 016 achievement count is sixteen.
- Asset inventory remains sixteen completed icons plus grey and not-eligible variants.
- Remove `clean_break` from the achievement prompt, tracking plan, localisation plan, docs, and manifest.
- Keep the clean-break outcome as normal gameplay and event documentation.

### Acceptance

- Exactly sixteen Event 016 achievement keys are registered.
- The retained achievement proves both public replication and peaceful loss of exclusive control.
- No retired key or seventeenth icon remains in prompts, manifests, localisation, or completion checklists.

## R7. Separate the two terminal endings by commitment and play

### Decision

Keep Laboratory World and Strategic Singularity. Their eligibility is already different, but the route commitment needs to prevent both from feeling like interchangeable final buttons.

### Laboratory World identity

- It is a conquest, submission, administration, and integration ending.
- It requires overwhelming world control, defeated or submitted major opposition, a functioning global facility network, and the normal chaos threshold.
- The late campaign is about maintaining subject systems, integrating populations, protecting facilities, and proving that the world order can operate.
- It never fires the singularity.

### Strategic Singularity identity

- It is a vulnerable multi-year project race and denied-victory ending.
- It can mature before world conquest when the sovereign Kruger route, project prerequisites, facilities, delivery, and arming chain are complete.
- The late campaign is about component sites, secrecy, assistant loyalty, intelligence, raids, disarmament, surrender terms, and fail-deadly doctrine.
- On firing, it calculates and adds the chaos deficit above the shared terminal threshold before entering the Fallout pipeline.

### Commitment lock

- Selecting automatic fail-deadly or deliberate global-use doctrine blocks the Laboratory World terminal capstone while the device remains armed.
- Beginning final Laboratory World consolidation requires disarming the singularity and disabling automatic fail-deadly control.
- A deterrent-only device may exist during conquest, but it must be dismantled or placed under a verified nonterminal state before Laboratory World can fire.
- Singularity firing permanently prevents Laboratory World.
- Laboratory World firing permanently cancels singularity construction, arming, and activation.

### AI

- Expansion and integration AI prefers Laboratory World when it controls the required world share and its global administration is stable.
- Besieged, extreme, or project-race AI prefers Singularity when it has the component network but cannot secure orderly global control.
- AI cannot switch terminal commitments merely because one short-term score changes. A doctrine change requires the full disarmament or consolidation transition.

### Acceptance

- The two terminal branches cannot fire in one campaign.
- Laboratory World requires active governance and integration evidence, not only map ownership.
- Singularity remains preventable through a fast victory and component counterplay.
- Fallout threshold sequencing remains exactly as specified.
- Each ending retains a separate super-event, image, researched text, unique audio, documentation, and cleanup path.

## Other review findings

### No further expansion recommended

- The four evolutions already have active and pre-fire behavior.
- Host choices include public, secret, transfer, safe settlement, removal, charter, rebellion, and takeover states.
- The country package covers territory, capital, economy, supply, politics, diplomacy, classification, forces, AI, and cleanup at the design level.
- Project armies already have production and supply burdens. R3 makes the only materially overlapping force families clearer.
- The focus architecture has real government, economy, military, diplomacy, expansion, integration, crisis, and postwar content. Keep the existing 85 to 115 authored-focus target. Do not add another branch before implementation proves a missing route.
- Static fallbacks and real frame-animation packages are already mandatory. R5 resolves the only portrait-count ambiguity.
- State formation, global threat, both terminal endings, and qualifying defeat still justify super-event treatment.
- The defeat aftermath and survivor-rights achievement remain distinct from the former-host and singularity-disarmament achievements.

### Implementation risks that remain

- Vanilla proves that one scientist can hold more than one specialization, but the complete all-field Kruger definition and every custom specialization still need engine-level validation.
- Dynamic country formation must verify tag conflicts, valid capitals, ownership and control, host viability, supply, and focus loading for every formation type.
- Scripted GUI cannot rely on event targets in the same way as ordinary script. Selected targets and facility references need supported variable-scope patterns.
- Direct `complete_special_project` use can bypass facility and scientist effects when scope parameters are missing. Event 016 should normally advance its own staged portfolio and preserve the correct project context.
- The asset package remains large even after the recognition cut and achievement merge. Missing or unwired final assets remain blockers, not permission for placeholders.
- Terminal cleanup and the chaos-deficit calculation require direct integration with the existing world-end helpers.
