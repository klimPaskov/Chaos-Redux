## Gap and design boundary

Before this accepted tranche, Part 4 and Part 5 called for groups for the REG-03 matrix packages IW-023/TRA, IW-029/BOS, and IW-026/MAC, while source supplied only package-gated callbacks on common nodes. This addendum records that initial gap, the accepted implementation, and its remaining AI and MCP evidence limits. The later IW-023 callback audit confirms its carrier repair and settlement reward; the older regional coverage audit's claim that TRA still overwrites its carrier is superseded by that later source/audit, while its **partial REG-03** coverage conclusion remains valid.

Keep `independence_wave_focus_tree` as the sole Event 006 full framework. TRA and BOS preserve `austro_hungarian_releasable_focus` when it is the active reviewed carrier and take the full framework otherwise; MAC takes the full framework. The same focus IDs should be defined once as root-level `shared_focus` blocks in `common/national_focus/006_independence_wave_focus.txt` and imported into the full tree and the Austro-Hungarian carrier as appropriate. Do not add a national `focus_tree`, country transfer, claim, core, new admission, portrait, government leader, new formable, or new mission.

The three current local crises last **420 days** (`independence_wave_transylvania_duration.founding_crisis`, `independence_wave_bosnia_duration.founding_crisis`, and `independence_wave_macedonia_duration.founding_crisis`). The 210-day foundation mentioned elsewhere in Event 006 belongs to other package families and the broad planning horizon; it is not these three timers. Here the four selected focuses cost 5 + 5 + 7 + 7 focus units, nominally 168 days at normal speed, so the player can finish a chosen group by a 210-day checkpoint while still needing paid projects and capital control to resolve the actual 420-day crisis. Do not silently replace the three mission durations.

The three forks are deliberately **institutional tradeoffs**, not extra government locks: civilian representation improves one ledger while exposing a defensive weakness; frontier command does the reverse. This lets the existing five paid government routes and the shared host/League routes retain ownership. If a focus branch is taken after its companion paid project, award its single +5 companion bonus immediately from the paid-only receipt; if the project is still pending, award it in that project's successful effect. This is one bonus per route choice, never both, and never a copy of a common-node callback. Completed-focus history and the matching durable companion-awarded flag jointly prevent another award when additive package cleanup removes the paid receipt but preserves focus progress. A full-framework reload clears that flag with the focus history. A project cancellation or timeout gives no companion bonus. The user accepted these companion bonus rules as part of the bounded implementation design; they do not replace or waive any existing paid action.

## AI and fail-closed evidence gate

The intended focus order is opener → rail/depot step → one branch → docket, interleaved with survival and paid package projects. Route selection responds to the **lower current ledger**, war/host threat, and the paid project the AI can afford: civilian/peasant/federal governments favor the civic branch when safe; military guardians favor the frontier branch when threatened; patron or royal routes still use the same public proof and cannot skip costs. MAC's patron-rail government does not automatically select the veteran branch. After a founding failure, avoid prioritizing the docket over recovery; after settlement, do not starve host, League, or wider framework work. These are behavioral targets, **not numeric weight recommendations**.
