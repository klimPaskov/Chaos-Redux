Keep `independence_wave_focus_tree` as the sole Event 006 full framework. The same focus IDs should be defined once as root-level `shared_focus` blocks in `common/national_focus/006_independence_wave_focus.txt` and imported into the full tree and the Austro-Hungarian carrier as appropriate. Do not add a national `focus_tree`, country transfer, claim, core, new admission, portrait, government leader, new formable, or new mission.

Here the four selected focuses cost 5 + 5 + 7 + 7 focus units, nominally 168 days at normal speed, so the player can finish a chosen group by a 210-day checkpoint while still needing paid projects and capital control to resolve the actual 420-day crisis. Do not silently replace the three mission durations.

## AI and fail-closed evidence gate

MAC's patron-rail government does not automatically select the veteran branch. After a founding failure, avoid prioritizing the docket over recovery; after settlement, do not starve host, League, or wider framework work. These are behavioral targets, **not numeric weight recommendations**.
