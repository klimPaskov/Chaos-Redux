## Symbol and flag research

### Basque / Ikurriña

The historical Basque design is a red field, green saltire, and white cross. The historical summary at <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_the_Basque_Country> records the Arana brothers' 1894 design, EAJ-PNV adoption in 1895, the 1933 whole-country proposal, and Basque Government adoption in 1936. The Commons geometry reference <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_the_Basque_Country.svg> is a modern self-published CC BY-SA 2.5 reconstruction, not a period source or final runtime file.

The downloaded SVG reference is `docs/assets/006_independence_wave/iberian_portrait_source_research_2026_08_03/source_masters/flag_basque_country_reference.svg`, SHA-256 `f282e4ea7981c707c5db8a10094e5cd3094c3e9689b384d6882cdda89c91b255`. The compact 792 País Vasco package should use this Basque identity; 172 Navarra and 806 Pyrénées-Atlantiques are optional extensions. Navarre's red-and-gold-chain flag is regionally attested but is not a substitute for the compact Basque-country Ikurriña without an explicit route choice.

### Galicia

The official Xunta page <https://www.xunta.gal/a-bandeira> states the civil design as a 3:2 white flag with a light/cobalt blue diagonal band from the upper-left to the lower-right and distinguishes the institutional form with a centred coat of arms. The official shield page <https://www.xunta.gal/o-escudo> records the blue field, gold chalice, silver host, and seven silver crosses and notes the modern definitive model (1972) and symbols-law regulation (1984). The historical summary at <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Galicia> records public use of the white/blue diagonal design by 1891 and identifies the red-star `Estreleira` as a route-specific nationalist/left variant with modern popularisation after the war.

The downloaded flat reference is `docs/assets/006_independence_wave/iberian_portrait_source_research_2026_08_03/source_masters/flag_galicia_reference.svg`, SHA-256 `fbdaf8a27bd279ba167a8956ce94bccee4a06257bfa4bafedf2d1560c8ec8db5`. Commons marks the old SVG public domain but also tags it as an invalid Inkscape SVG; it is reference evidence only. For a grounded 1936 GLC baseline, use the plain civil white/blue design unless the parent explicitly selects a shielded institutional route, and exclude the red-star variant by default.
