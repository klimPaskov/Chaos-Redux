## Requested identity decision

The current Event 006 research resolution describes FER as a railway, port, municipal, customs, shipping, merchant, labor, and local-defense provisional institution and permits a historical route variant only when its source and released identity match. FER was an entity of 1920–1922, so any 1936 use requires an explicit revived, restored, or continuity interpretation rather than an assertion of uninterrupted government.

No source reviewed here supports a non-socialist neutral 1936 FER identity. The surviving flag and arms language uses a red field or shield, a dark-blue canton, a star, and industrial or agricultural tools in the context of the Far Eastern buffer republic. Those motifs may be historically accurate but cannot be presented as ideologically neutral without a separate parent decision.

## Promotion blockers

1. No directly attested neutral 1936 FER identity or flag exists in the reviewed sources.
2. H0 needs explicit parent acceptance of a 1936 revival/continuity interpretation, and the Commons reconstruction's CC BY-SA permission does not independently clear the underlying SHPL scan rights.
3. H1 and H2 are constitutional-text reconstructions marked public domain by Commons metadata, but they are not verified 1936 designs, do not solve the neutral-ideology requirement, and have no independent primary-scan rights instrument.
4. H3 and H4 have unspecified image rights and are not hydrated locally; H5 has no image source; R0 is a 2006 redraw without recorded historical provenance.
5. The proposed `FER_INDEPENDENCE_WAVE_PROVISIONALX` tag and three unsuffixed target paths are only dated plan identifiers. They are not registered and no assets exist.
6. The exact installed FER runtime family is four ideology suffixes across the 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7 ladders, while the addendum lists three unsuffixed files. This family mismatch requires parent/asset-owner resolution before production.
7. The separate identity, institutional-roster, portrait, route-leadership, typed-probability, central-attestation, and current-engine evidence gates remain open and are outside this symbol tranche.
