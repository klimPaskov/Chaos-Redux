# IW-030 Montenegro male-leader source research tranche (2026-08-03)

Date: 2026-08-03 (Europe/Kyiv).

Scope: bounded source research for grounded male Montenegrin leaders and institutional officeholders suitable for the Event 006 release era (1936 onward). This tranche does not alter gameplay, characters, history, localisation, `.gfx`, DDS, flags, advisors, attestation, or runtime admission.

## Promotion recommendation

`SAFE_PACKAGE_PROMOTION = NO` and `IW-030 = HOLD / fail-closed`.

The strongest new lead is Jovan Simonov Plamenac (1873–1944), who was alive at the 1936 release start and has an unchanged archival portrait, exact crop proof, and a deterministic source-placeholder preview. The source record's `PD-old` assertion is explicit, but the photographer, first-publication chain, and current `gov.me` source page are unresolved. This is a distinct candidate only; it is not a relabel for any vanilla MNT character.

The stronger Danilo Petrović-Njegoš lead remains in the existing v107 package. A source-placeholder preview was added there from its retained exact crop. Danilo has a named Carl Vandyk source and LOC corroboration with “No known restrictions on publication,” but still needs explicit identity admission and independent portrait review. Neither lead clears the four current rows or content attestation.

Marko Daković and Sekula Drljević were screened out because their Commons records point to live Digital Library of Slovenia records whose rights display is `InC` (rights reserved), despite Commons `PD-Slovenia`/`PD-1996` templates. No source files were retained for those rows.
