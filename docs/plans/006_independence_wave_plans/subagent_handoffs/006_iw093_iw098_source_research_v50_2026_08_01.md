CC0 from the uploader cannot by itself clear unknown underlying book/photograph rights. Treat as `needs_user_review`/blocked, not as an admitted source.
- If rights are independently resolved, the crop may enter the mandatory source-locked identity-preserving HOI4 repaint sequence. No repaint, 156x210 candidate, DDS, or runtime file was created in v50.

### SOK Muhammadu Dikko / Abdullahi Bayero — Walter Mittelholzer, 1930–31 (strong rights/date lead; identity-position blocker)

- Commons file: [ETH-BIB-Die beiden Emire von Kano und Kadsena nach ihrem ersten Fluge-Tschadseeflug 1930-31-LBS MH02-08-0732.tif](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ETH-BIB-Die_beiden_Emire_von_Kano_und_Kadsena_nach_ihrem_ersten_Fluge-Tschadseeflug_1930-31-LBS_MH02-08-0732.tif), accession `ETH-Bibliothek_LBS_MH02-08-0732`, DOI `10.3932/ethz-a-000257733`.
- Archive metadata: Walter Mittelholzer (1894–1937), exposure between 1930 and 1931; caption says Sarkin Kano Abdullahi Bayero, Sarkin Katsina Muhammadu Dikko, and Wazirin Kano Muhammadu Gidado at Kano airport; the image was published in *Chadseeflug* (1932).
- Rights: ETH-Bibliothek collection record marks the photograph Public Domain Mark / `PD-old-auto` because Mittelholzer died in 1937. Commons requests a US public-domain tag as an additional jurisdiction check; retain ETH-Bibliothek, Bildarchiv/Stiftung Luftbild Schweiz, photographer, accession, and DOI attribution if ever processed.
- A 3840x2800 JPEG review derivative was downloaded through the i0.wp.com proxy for visual inspection only: `docs/assets/006_independence_wave/iw093_iw098_source_research_v50_2026_08_01/source/SOK/SOK_bayero_dikko_mittelholzer_1930_31_review.jpg`, SHA-256 `30edcf2c2ed84e11f6de5a2b50907e5b0d4a903c8a8e57a78f19d94466332a4c`. The source is therefore `needs_user_review`/blocked for portrait processing despite its excellent era and rights fit.
