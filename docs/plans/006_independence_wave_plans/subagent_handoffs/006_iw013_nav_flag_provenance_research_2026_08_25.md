## Authoritative period and design evidence

### Government of the Basque Country technical page

Primary source: <https://www.euskadi.eus/web01-ejeduki/es/contenidos/informacion/himo_ikurrina/es_461/ikurrina_especi_tecnicas.html>.

Accessed `2026-08-25`; the page reports a last modification date of `2005-10-10`.

The official page states that the Ikurriña's institutional basis is the Presidency decree of the Provisional Government of Euskadi dated `1936-10-19`, published in the Diario Oficial del País Vasco on `1936-10-21`, and modified by a decree dated `1936-10-24`, published on `1936-10-28`.

The page reproduces the final Article 2 wording: a vermilion-red field, a vivid-green saltire reaching the flag's corners, and a superimposed white cross reaching the midpoints of the sides.

The reproduced period proportions are five metres horizontal by `2.80` metres vertical, with proportional reduction or enlargement and `0.43` metre bands for that reference size.

The page's later technical standard identifies red as Pantone `485 C` and green as Pantone `347 C`, but those Pantone values are a modern reproduction standard rather than evidence that a particular 1936 surviving flag used a digital colour value.

This is the strongest primary design and period-fit evidence found for a Basque state-792 baseline.

### Official Government of the Basque Country identity manual

Primary source: <https://www.euskadi.eus/contenidos/informacion/v2_simbolos/es_simboide/adjuntos/manual_id_corporativa.pdf>.

The manual is governed by Decreto `318/1999`, dated `1999-08-31`, and its Ikurriña section repeats the 1936 decree dates, publication dates, Article 2 geometry, proportions, and later Pantone references.

The retrieved PDF was `2,831,478` bytes with temporary retrieval SHA-256 `afefdc6bf3c978a3daaf4bd1b777fbea58264c0f0da9328a427aa8155fd3491` on `2026-08-25`; no binary copy was added to the repository because this pass did not select a runtime source asset.

The same manual describes the 1936 Government of Euzkadi heraldic shield as deriving from the Presidency decree of `1936-10-19` and retaining the historical territorial elements, but it does not turn the Navarrese carrier arms into a Basque neutral flag.

### Official site rights notice

Rights source: <https://www.euskadi.eus/informacion-legal/web01-s1ezaleh/es/>.

The Government of the Basque Country legal notice states that intellectual-property rights in the Euskadi website and its contained elements belong to the General Administration of the Autonomous Community of Euskadi and warns that unauthorised use may lead to legal action.

The official technical page and manual therefore provide historical and geometric evidence, not a blanket licence to redistribute their page artwork, scans, or rendered flag diagrams in a mod.

### Period-context archive lead

Context-only source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Amaiur_bataloia._1937._Euzko_Gudarostea._Euskal_Herria..jpg>.

The Commons record captions the scene as the Amaiur Battalion of the Basque Army in Biscay in `1937`, credits GureGipuzkoa, lists the original photographer as unknown, and marks the uploaded image `CC BY-SA 3.0`.

The file metadata reports `2007-01-09` rather than a capture date, and the monochrome scene does not provide a clean, colour-verified flat flag geometry or an unambiguous original rights chain.

It is useful only as contextual evidence that the Basque Army period is a valid historical visual domain and is not selected as a runtime source asset.

Modern Commons photographs titled `Ikurrina - Eusko Gudarostea ... - 1936-1937.jpg` are 2016 own-work recreations licensed `CC BY-SA 4.0`; they were rejected as modern reconstructions rather than period sources.

## Existing package provenance assessment

The existing package manifest classifies the source mode as `generated_alternate_history_flat_flag` and states that the generated masters were not copied from `NAV_democratic.tga` or from the Commons SVG.

The prompts keep the documented red, white, and green geometry strict, but the AGRARIANX wheat sheaf, SOCIALISTX star/lozenge/canton, and EMERGENCYX stripe/roundel are generated route marks without a period attestation.

The CIVICX design has the best period fit because it contains only the documented cross and saltire, but it is still a generated reconstruction and not evidence that an identical flat flag was the 1936 government issue.

The package's own `metadata/generation_evidence.json` records that parent attribution/ShareAlike acceptance is required before release or central attestation.

The package's existing TGA and DDS QA therefore establishes processing readiness only and must not be reported as historical-source clearance.
