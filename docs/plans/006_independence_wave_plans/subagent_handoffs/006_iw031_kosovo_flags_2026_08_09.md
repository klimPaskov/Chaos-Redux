Two accepted route-specific cosmetic families were generated with the official built-in ImageGen tool after inspecting the canonical flag reference family at `.agents/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/assets/vanilla_reference/flags/`.

| Family | Accepted identity | Source mode | Runtime ladder |

Normal, medium, and small sizes are 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7 respectively.

No ideology variants were generated because the accepted runtime cosmetic lookup does not require ideology suffixes.

## Design constraints verified

The Albanian civic family is a plain red 3:2 field with one centered black double-headed eagle and no map, stars, crown, fasces, text, border, gradients, lighting, fabric, pole, folds, scenery, or invented heraldry.

The Yugoslav municipal family is the exact blue-white-red horizontal equal-band 2:3 tricolour with no emblem or text.

Neither family is a universal historical Kosovo flag; the parent must preserve the route identity boundary in the cosmetic registration and player-facing text.

The carrier tag `KOS` base files were not overwritten.
