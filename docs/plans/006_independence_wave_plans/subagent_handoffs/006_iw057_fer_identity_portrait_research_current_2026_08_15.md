Status: HOLD / FAIL-CLOSED / RESEARCH-ONLY as of 2026-08-15.

This handoff covers only the grounded opening identity research for IW-057, including source attribution, historical role/date checks, rights metadata, crop/framing review, and vanilla/project portrait ownership. ## Scope and decision

Pyotr Mikhailovich Nikiforov and Alexander Mikhailovich Krasnoshchyokov are conditionally defensible research candidates because each has a directly archived attributed historical source and a separately verified FER role, but neither clears the final identity-and-rights gate. The package therefore remains fail-closed.

Nikiforov is the stronger research fit if the parent later chooses a male FER opening anchor because his third-chairman role and dates are precise and the source is a clean solo head-and-shoulders photograph. This is a recommendation for review only, not an acceptance.

Krasnoshchyokov remains a viable first-leader alternative, but his source is darker and grainier and the captured identity page has conflicting dates for his foreign-affairs service. The parent must resolve that wording before any identity decision.

The Far Eastern Republic ceased to exist as an active state in 1922 and entered the RSFSR on 15 November 1922. Both men were alive in 1936, but neither was an active FER officeholder at a historical 1936 start. Any later use would be a deliberately restored or revival-era 1936 leader token, not a claim of uninterrupted historical FER officeholding.

No source is cleared for a final grounded portrait. Commons public-domain metadata and its United States rationale are preserved as source claims, while independent rights review remains outstanding.
