# IW-057 FER symbol gate — interim status

The historical source gate is evidence-backed but not runtime-admissible yet.

- The Far Eastern Republic existed from April 1920 through 15 November 1922, so no FER flag or emblem is historically a 1936 symbol. Any 1936 release must be a continuity/revival use or a clearly marked alternate-history synthesis.
- The strongest historical flag candidate is the 11 November 1920 government design, codified again by Constitution Article 181 in 1921: red field, dark-blue hoist canton, and red D.V.R. letters arranged in a triangle. A Commons reconstruction is CC BY-SA 3.0 and cites an SHPL scan; the underlying scan rights are not independently stated.
- The strongest institutional emblem candidate is the Article 180 design: red shield, conifer wreath, sunrise, silver star, crossed anchor and pointed pickaxe through a wheat sheaf, and D/V/R letters on the wreath ribbons. Commons reconstructions are marked public domain, while Heraldicum corroborates the description and provides reconstruction/banknote/banner references whose image rights are not stated.
- The historical flag/emblem is suitable as a default continuity identity, but its red field and star carry socialist/communist-era semantics. It is not evidence for a non-socialist neutral flag.
- If “neutral baseline” means non-socialist visual language, no directly attested 1936 FER candidate was found. A civic or railway-port synthesis may use the documented anchor, pickaxe, wheat, conifer, and maritime motifs only as an explicitly alternate-history design after parent approval. It must not be presented as historical or silently replace the historical baseline.
- No candidate is complete or wired. This research tranche created only source copies, a contact sheet, and documentation under this handoff folder; it created no processed PNG, DDS, TGA, GFX entry, cosmetic tag, portrait, or gameplay change.
