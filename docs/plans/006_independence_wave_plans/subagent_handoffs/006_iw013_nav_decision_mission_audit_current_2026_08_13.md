Date: `2026-08-13`.

## Disposition

The NAV surface has real resource commitments, route locks, dynamic ledger effects, completion markers, host validity checks, and cleanup coverage. It is not a passive political-power checklist. The lifecycle gate is now closed by `is_independence_wave_nav_project_ready`, which is used in all eleven project visibility/availability paths and in the ten timed-project cancellation paths. Quantitative AI balance is separately blocked by the probability adapter limitations recorded in the companion receipt.

The appended re-audit below records the pre-fix duplicate-penalty observation. The current source additionally guards each timed project's `cancel_effect` with the same project-ready helper, so a founding-crisis failure cancels an active project without applying a second NAV failure penalty.

## Issues by severity

1. **Resolved in the lifecycle follow-up: terminal founding failure now disables NAV projects.** The original audit finding was that the founding mission could leave paid NAV projects visible or available after `independence_wave_nav_compact_crisis_failed`, and that pre-setup package identity could expose them. The follow-up adds `is_independence_wave_nav_project_ready` and applies it to all eleven project visibility/availability paths and the ten timed cancellation paths. Each timed `cancel_effect` also checks the helper before applying the non-idempotent failure penalty, so a founding-crisis failure cannot levy that penalty a second time. The original finding is retained below as dated evidence; the current source status is closed by the lifecycle patch handoff.
2. **Medium: emergency route timing can make the founding mission unreachable for AI.** NAV starts at `46` fueros legitimacy and `34` industrial capacity (`common/script_constants/006_independence_wave_iberian_constants.txt:67-85`). The emergency route adds `+15/-5`, while the three core projects needed to reach both stable thresholds add `+25/+30`; taking the route first therefore requires four 120-day projects, or 480 days, against the 420-day founding mission timeout. The route has urgent AI weight and doubles at war (`006_independence_wave_iberian_decisions.txt:143-156`). The older combined audit recorded this risk; current probability evidence cannot establish whether the AI actually selects this sequence.
3. **Medium/low: strict `>` gates do not match exact payment amounts or player-facing wording.** `common/scripted_triggers/006_independence_wave_decision_triggers.txt:227-273` requires more than the displayed command power, manpower, equipment, convoy/train, or spare-factory amount, while shared effects spend the exact constant (`common/scripted_effects/006_independence_wave_decision_effects.txt` payment helpers). The localised cost strings say `Requires` or show icons without explaining the strict reserve, and diplomatic strings show convoy/train alternatives as adjacent icons. Either align gates to exact affordability or state the reserve/“more than” rule in the tooltip.
4. **Low: the shared founding-mission concurrency helper omits the NAV crisis mission.** `has_independence_wave_active_founding_mission` in `common/scripted_triggers/006_independence_wave_decision_triggers.txt:41-50` lists the generic founding missions but not `independence_wave_nav_hold_fueros_together`. This may be intentional parallel pressure, but it should be documented or included if the package mission must serialize with the common founding layer.
5. **Low: four Iberian duration constants are unused.** `regional_project`, `route_project`, `host_project`, and `sovereignty_project` in `common/script_constants/006_independence_wave_iberian_constants.txt:88-99` are not consumed by the current NAV decisions; project durations use shared `independence_wave_decision_duration` constants instead. Remove them or wire them deliberately to avoid stale tuning.
6. **Low: raw availability conditions remain largely engine-generated.** The category has clear names, descriptions, cost text, and effect tooltips, including the project-failure tooltip, but capital control, active-project serialization, route availability, living-host, and failed-state requirements are not surfaced through dedicated custom trigger tooltips. This is a clarity gap rather than a syntax blocker.
