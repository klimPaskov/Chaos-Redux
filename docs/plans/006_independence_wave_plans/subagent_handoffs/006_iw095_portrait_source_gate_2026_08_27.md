## Why this candidate is 1936-capable

The captured [1936 Dahomeyan Administrative Council election record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1936_Dahomeyan_Administrative_Council_election) states that three Administrative Council members were elected from Abomey, Ouidah, and Porto-Novo and lists Casimir d'Almeida as the Porto-Novo winner.

The election record cites Patrick Manning, *Slavery, Colonialism and Economic Growth in Dahomey, 1640-1960*, Cambridge University Press, 2004, p. 273.

This supports an exact 1936 council representative role for a package-local provisional council or cabinet if the parent accepts that role boundary.

The election record is a secondary Internet source and no primary council minute or independent 1936 portrait was located during this gate, so the historical-role gate is `supported_needs_parent_review`, not unconditionally cleared.

The Albert-Kahn [delegation record](https://collections.albert-kahn.hauts-de-seine.fr/document/chefs-du-dahomey-sous-la-conduite-du-reverend-pere-aupiais/617aa7e0cf8b8968b338b280) dated 11 July 1931 identifies Casimir d'Almeida as a Dahomeyan delegation member and `homme politique` (politician).

The Albert-Kahn spelling varies between `Casimir d'Alméida`, the original caption `Casimir Dalméida`, and the election record's `Casimir d'Almeida`; the parent must explicitly accept these as the same candidate before identity wiring.
