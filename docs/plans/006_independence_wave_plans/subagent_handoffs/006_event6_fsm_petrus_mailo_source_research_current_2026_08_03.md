## Identity and role evidence

Petrus Mailo is a defensible named identity for the route. The English Wikipedia biography identifies him as a Micronesian chief and politician born on 27 December 1902, appointed secretary of Moen by Japanese authorities in 1932, made Chief of Nepukos in 1933, and serving as Leader of Section No. 2 of Moen from 1936 to 1938. The page is secondary evidence only, but it is consistent with the earlier route handoff and the cited *Micronesian Reporter* profile, “Petrus Mailo: A Micronesian Chief.” See [Petrus Mailo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrus_Mailo).

The earlier FSM source-gate handoff already records the same chronology and correctly treats Petrus as a role-fit lead rather than proof of portrait admission: [006_fsm_source_gate_fail_closed_2026_08_02.md](006_fsm_source_gate_fail_closed_2026_08_02.md).

## Portrait-source findings

### UHM Trust Territory archive: identity attribution, but postwar and rights-unclear

The University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa Digital Image Collection has an attributable record titled “Petrus Mailo. (N-001-304),” created by the Department of Public Affairs – Public Information Office in the Trust Territory Archives Photograph Collection, circa 1958–1975. The record and IIIF manifest identify the collection and attribution but provide no public-domain or open-reuse grant: [UHM item 22714](https://digital.library.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/show/22714), [IIIF manifest](https://digital.library.manoa.hawaii.edu/iiif/22714/manifest).

The related reel is explicitly 1965–1975 and labels image 9 “Chief Petrus Mailo of Truk (N-200)” and image 10 “Petrus Mailo examining document.” These are useful identity references but cannot depict the 1936 opening and are not era-compatible source portraits: [UHM reel 1921](https://libweb.hawaii.edu/digicoll/ttp/ttp_htms/1921.html), [N-200 JPEG](https://libweb.hawaii.edu/digicoll/ttp/ttp_jpg/192109.jpg), [N-201 JPEG](https://libweb.hawaii.edu/digicoll/ttp/ttp_jpg/192110.jpg).

UHM’s own copyright policy states that collections may be governed by U.S. copyright, that uses beyond fair use require consultation or consent, that an unlisted copyright holder should not be assumed absent, and that the user is responsible for determining rights. It also warns that some material may carry cultural or community restrictions: [UHM copyright policy](https://manoa.hawaii.edu/library/research/scholarly-communication/repositories/policies-guidelines/copyright-policy/). This is a rights hold, not a production licence.

### Wikipedia local file: identifiable image, explicitly non-free

The English Wikipedia file `File:Petrus Mailo.png` credits the UHM Digital Image Collection and points to the UHM item, but MediaWiki metadata marks it `Fair use`, `NonFree: true`, and `Copyrighted: true`. The local upload is only 280x353 pixels and has MediaWiki SHA-1 `68b39b1a4273714a58d472ed5f00c9fc4c618edc`. File metadata: [Wikipedia file page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Petrus_Mailo.png), [UHM credit target](https://digital.library.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/show/22714).

This file is an identity reference only. Its fair-use status and postwar UHM provenance disqualify it from the immutable source, repaint, and final portrait chain. No local source copy was retained in this pass.

### Micronesian Seminar catalogue: “Not Restricted” is not a sufficient reuse grant

The Micronesian Seminar search returns several Petrus Mailo photographs, including `CMS01022` (“An American with Petrus Mailo”), `CMS02064`, `LCM02053`, `LCM02063`, `PCP01013`, and Trust Territory Archive records from the 1950s–1970s: [Micronesian Seminar Petrus Mailo search](https://micsem.org/library/search-photos-results?subject=Petrus%20Mailo). Some catalogue rows say “Not Restricted,” but the site does not provide an explicit mod-reuse licence for the records, and the site footer retains an all-rights-reserved notice. The only directly retrievable `CMS01022` thumbnail is a small 120-ish-pixel catalogue thumbnail, not a full-resolution attributable source suitable for exact cropping or a 156x210 portrait.

These records remain `needs_user_review` only if a rights holder later supplies a written reuse grant and the full-resolution original. They are not admitted as SAFE sources.

## Period alternatives checked and rejected

Commons contains genuinely period-matching public-domain material from Japanese-era Micronesia, but the available files are unnamed groups or role-only scenes and therefore fail the named-identity gate:

* [Police officer at Chuuk, Micronesia in 1931](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Police_officer_at_Chuuk,_Micronesia_in_1931.png) is a 1931 NDL book scan marked public domain (`PD-1996`, `PD-Japan-oldphoto`), SHA-1 `41728adb2a197a2e7c90ade8fadfeac65d508880`, but the caption identifies only an assistant teacher and islander auxiliary police; it does not identify Petrus Mailo or another named civic leader.
* [Truk Islanders in full dress (circa 1930s)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Truk_Islanders_in_full_dress_(circa_1930s).jpg) is a CC BY-SA 4.0 scan, SHA-1 `44d1a01b364de5812aafd32a24357c3e7293610d`, but shows three unnamed men and is only approximately dated.
* [Native people in Truck (from a book published in 1932)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Native_people_in_Truck_(from_a_book_published_in_1932).png) is a public-domain NDL scan, SHA-1 `3b993e1acbcb31888da24234f1afaaf21a6d0e43`, but is an unnamed group.
* [Ponapean natives gathering](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ponapean_natives_gathering.jpg) is a public-domain 1935 Japanese album scan, SHA-1 `a11aa9f764337d669896c287ea58ce4ce67f5a5f`, and its caption mentions a “great chief,” but no person is named and the image is a group ritual scene.
* [Drinking Sakau, Pohnpei](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Drinking_Sakau,_Pohnpei_(from_a_book_published_in_1935).png) is a public-domain 1935 scan, SHA-1 `45b87da4cb34b18faea4f29751e273d2a3b7a474`, but has no named or attributable subject.

The unnamed period files may document regional visual context, but using any of them as Petrus Mailo or another grounded real leader would invent identity and fail the source gate. They are rejected, not fallback portraits.
