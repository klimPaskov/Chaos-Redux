## Rights, era, and role blockers

- Galimzhan Ibrahimov: Commons credits the Kazan State University library and marks the source public domain; the original KSU page is currently unreachable, so a separate reviewer must retain this provenance uncertainty.
- Shamil Usmanov: Commons credits the Tatarstan government archive and marks the source public domain, but 187x250 is too small for a dependable `156x210` leader-family identity-preserving repaint. No higher-resolution redistributable source was found; the row is blocked.
- Ahmet Zeki Velidi Togan: the Bashkortostan State Assembly/Kurultai museum source and Commons PD-old mark are promising, but the date is estimated and the photographer is unknown. Vanilla only has `TUR_zeki_velidi_togan` idea localisation, not a character or portrait owner. Keep `needs_user_review` pending rights/role approval.
- Mar Benyamin Shimun XXI: the LOC/Bain source is public domain and visually strong, but the subject died in 1918. Mar Eshai Shimun XXIII was the active 1936-era lead, yet the Mar Shimun Foundation photo archive provides no reusable license; Malik Ismail II family material is watermarked and rights-unclear. Keep the concordat row blocked.
- Naum Faiq: the Commons file is a standalone 1920s Assyrian portrait with a PD-Syria/PD-US rationale and a 1936 memorial-volume context, but Faiq died in 1930. Parent approval is required for legacy institutional continuity.
- Agha Petros: the 1920 H. G. Donatossian portrait is public-domain-marked and directly depicts an Assyrian military leader in uniform, but Petros died in 1932. Parent approval is required for legacy institutional continuity.

No generic, generated, modern, watermarked, group-only, female, invented, or re-encoded source substitute was accepted. No real-person repaint master was generated because the sourced visual subagent is prohibited from generating real portraits; the repaint prompts and processing contract are documented for a separately authorized producer.
