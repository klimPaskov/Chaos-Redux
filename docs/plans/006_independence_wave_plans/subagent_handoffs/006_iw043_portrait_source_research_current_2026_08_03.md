## New source candidate: Hadi Atlasi

**Subject and role.** Muhammed-Hadi Miftakhutdinovich Atlasi, known as Hadi Atlasi, was a Tatar public figure, historian-Turkologist, pedagogue, publicist, and 1907 deputy of the Second State Duma. The biography records 1876-1938 dates and therefore places him alive during the 1936-centered scenario. Those civic and political roles fit a Bolgar civic-presidium transfer as a fictional institutional appointment, not as a claim that Atlasi historically held that office.

**Primary source.** [Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hadi_Atlasi_mug_shots_(1937).jpg) and [Commons raw record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File:Hadi_Atlasi_mug_shots_(1937).jpg&action=raw) identify a 1937 two-view NKVD prison/mug-shot sheet. The raw record names the source as a Facebook repost, author `NKVD`, date `1937`, and `PD-old`.

[Wikidata Q13208970](https://www.wikidata.org/entity/Q13208970) independently labels him as a Tatar politician born in 1876 and deceased in 1938.
