## Identity, birth, and 1936 role evidence

- [La Nuova Sardegna, “Il generale Pinna Parpaglia, un militare al servizio dell'isola” (24 June 2011)](https://www.lanuovasardegna.it/regione/2011/06/24/news/il-generale-pinna-parpaglia-un-militare-al-servizio-dell-isola-1.3473189) names Pietro Pinna Parpaglia, born in Pozzomaggiore on 12 January 1891, and describes his military rise, 1936 promotion to air-fleet general, 1940 command of East African air forces, and 1944 appointment as High Commissioner for Sardinia.
- [Generals.dk biography](https://generals.dk/general/Pinna_Parpaglia/Pietro/Italy.html) records the service chronology: Deputy Chief of the Air Staff from 1933-10-15 to 1939-12-01 and Commanding Officer, Italian East Africa Air Command from 1936-09-21 to 1936-12-04. Its picture note attributes the reproduced portrait to the 1969 Ufficio Storico dell'Aeronautica Militare volume *Ordine Militare d'Italia 1911-1964*.
- [Italian Wikipedia article](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pietro_Pinna_Parpaglia) is a secondary cross-check for Pozzomaggiore, the 1936 Italian East Africa command, and the later Sardinian High Commissioner office; it gives the command transition as 15 August-4 December 1936. The differing August versus September start dates should not be silently normalised.
- Pozzomaggiore is in Sassari province, Sardinia. This establishes a genuine Sardinian-born identity, but not a dynastic or crown-office identity.
