# Event 006 ASSET-046 formable identity and emblem source audit

Date: 2026-09-02

Scope: bounded source, identity, rights, era-fit, consumer, and release-gate review for the 48 Event 006 formable emblem rows and the shared league/faction emblem row in ASSET-046.

Disposition: `blocked` overall, with the FORM-05 and FORM-48 emblem art packages retained as accepted existing outputs. No new image was downloaded, generated, cropped, processed, converted, or promoted in this tranche. No gameplay, country, decision, GUI, or GFX file was edited. No central admission flag was changed, and nothing was staged or committed.

## Executive conclusion

The current ASSET-046 audit is correct that the family is not complete. Only `independence_wave_formable_form_05.dds` and `independence_wave_formable_form_48.dds` exist in the runtime emblem directory, and only their matching sprites are registered in `interface/006_independence_wave_small_assets.gfx`. The Event 006 status GUI uses the separate generic eligibility-seal animation, not an ASSET-046 family emblem, and no `GFX_independence_wave_league_emblem` sprite or texture exists.

FORM-05 (`MIX`) has a complete project-generated emblem source, processed PNG, 128x128 DDS, contact sheet, and sprite registration. Its package manifest and current GFX registry do not prove a live decision or GUI consumer; the current decision file uses the FORM-05 decision sprites instead. FORM-48 (`PFX`) has the same complete art evidence and is consumed by `independence_wave_hbx_prepare_pacific_maritime_congress`; the parent still owns PFX identity wiring and the separate FSM/FORM-48 admission gate.

FORM-01, FORM-02, FORM-03, FORM-04, FORM-09, FORM-12, FORM-13, and FORM-18 have identity-specific flag packages but no formable emblem source or stable family emblem consumer. FORM-07 is explicitly identity-blocked. FORM-08 and FORM-16 reuse existing vanilla cosmetic identities and therefore have no authorized new ASSET-046 emblem. FORM-39 has a proposed MFX flag package that remains `needs_user_review` and has no emblem. The other 34 registry rows remain metadata-only in the current Event 006 readiness surface.

No existing flag, faction logo, state-puzzle piece, decision icon, or family emblem can be cropped, relabelled, or promoted as a substitute. The parent must lock each final public identity, gameplay or cosmetic tag, motif, route policy, palette, and exact UI consumer before any missing emblem is produced. The parent must also decide whether the reserved league emblem is universal or charter/route-specific.

## League/faction emblem disposition

The reserved shared asset is `gfx/interface/006_independence_wave/emblems/independence_wave_league_emblem.dds` with sprite `GFX_independence_wave_league_emblem`. Neither exists in the current runtime tree, and no GUI, decision, event-detail, or category consumer names it. The current generic status seal, the vanilla faction references, the FORM-05 charter emblem, and the FORM-48 Pacific emblem are not substitutes.

The FORM-05 emblem is deliberately tied to the Mediterranean charter identity, while the FORM-48 emblem is deliberately tied to the Pacific federation identity. Reusing either as a universal league mark would be a cross-family substitution and would misrepresent the identity contract. The canonical vanilla faction references (`allies.png`, `allies_miniature.png`, `faction_logo_axis.png`, `faction_logo_comintern.png`, and `faction_logo_eu.png`) establish transparent faction-logo treatment and separate main/miniature surfaces only; they are review references, not rights-cleared source art.

The parent must decide whether the league emblem is one universal Event 006 charter mark, a family-specific emblem, or route-specific variants before commissioning art. A historical or community-attested mark requires an attributed source, author or archive, date/era fit, redistribution licence, and ownership note. A fictional or alternate-history mark may use the generated route only after the parent locks the identity, motif, palette, target size, and live consumer. No universal motif or rights owner is currently established.
