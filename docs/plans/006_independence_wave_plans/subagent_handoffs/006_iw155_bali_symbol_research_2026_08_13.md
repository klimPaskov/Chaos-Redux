# IW-155 Bali/BLI symbol research handoff (2026-08-13)

## Disposition

**HOLD / fail-closed for a neutral 1936 BLI runtime flag or emblem.** The search found no single source that simultaneously proves a Bali-wide civic or royal design was in use in 1936 and clears a reusable design source. The only plausible Bali-wide motif is a nine-stripe red/white/blue reconstruction whose historical adoption is uncertain and likely post-1908. It may be used later only as an explicitly labelled generated civic synthesis, never as a directly attested 1936 state flag.

No source image was copied into the repository, no image was generated or processed, and no GFX or gameplay file was changed. This handoff is research evidence only.

The accepted row is `IW-155` / `Bali` / `BLI` in `docs/specs/006_independence_wave_specs/research/006_package_research_resolution.csv`. A restrained generated civic flag is allowed only when the design is clearly documented as a modern baseline synthesis because no attested state flag exists. The current compatibility audit correctly keeps IW-155 outside the Event 006 runtime until this evidence and the incumbent/institution gate are resolved.

## Historical-versus-modern boundary

- The 1881 record cited by Hubert is the strongest period evidence located, and it cuts against an unqualified 1936 flag claim: rulers reportedly said flags were not used in their kingdoms at that time.
- The nine-stripe red/white/blue design is therefore a plausible post-1908 regional motif, not a proven 1936 state standard. The rights-cleared Commons files prove only that the modern vector/PNG may be reused, not that the historical claim is true.
- Badung/Pemecutan/Kesiman, Karangasem, and Klungkung materials are palace- or regency-specific. They cannot be silently promoted to a neutral BLI identity without choosing and documenting the actual institution represented.
- Surya Majapahit, vajra, lotus, naga, Garuda, temple gates, and similar imagery are religious, archaeological, or later provincial symbolism. They are not interchangeable with a 1936 civic flag and should remain route-owned or research-only.
