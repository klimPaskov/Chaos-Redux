## Design decisions

### CCX

The generated heather branch was accepted because it contains three broad
bell-shaped blossoms rather than a generic five-petal flower. The generated
outer green and center ivory fields were cropped to the accepted 1:2:1 ratio.
The final palette is `#22543D`, `#F1E8CF`, and `#70456C`. The 10x7 export uses
nearest-neighbour reduction so all three generated purple heads, the green
stem, and straight panel geometry survive native-size aliasing.

### NUX

The generated broad white saltire and offset red Nordic cross were accepted.
The crop centers the generated vertical arm at one third width and the
horizontal arm at one half height while retaining the generated intersections.
The final palette is `#102E4A`, `#FFFFFF`, and `#B72F3B`. The red cross remains
above the saltire at every intersection.

### RLX

The generated equal vertical green, white, and red fields and continuous wavy
Rhine device were accepted. The final palette is `#16834A`, `#F2F0E6`,
`#C52D34`, and `#245B86`. The vertical arrangement deliberately avoids the
horizontal 1923/RHI democratic layout identified by the research.

The slight ImageGen tonal variation was mapped to the researched solid
colours. This cleanup retained the generated silhouettes and did not rebuild
any design from local primitive geometry, vector tracing, or a replacement
drawing.

## Validation and review

- The source, cleaned master, native-size, and enlarged nearest-neighbour
  contact sheets were inspected together. All three designs remained legible
  at 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7.
- CCX retains three distinct four-connected purple blossom heads at all three
  native sizes and retains central green stem pixels at 10x7.
- NUX retains a red vertical arm through every row, a red horizontal arm
  through every column, and white saltire pixels in every quadrant at all
  three native sizes.
- RLX retains cobalt-blue Rhine pixels through every row at all three native
  sizes, with green at the hoist and red at the fly.
- `metadata/flag_validation.json` records exact palettes, native dimensions,
  semantic checks, TGA headers, decoded-image comparisons, runtime hashes, and
  package/runtime equality.
- Every package and runtime TGA decodes pixel-identically to its processed PNG.
- Every package/runtime pair is byte-identical.
- `C:/Program Files/Git/usr/bin/file.exe` identifies all nine runtime files as
  RGBA Targa images at 82x52, 41x26, or 10x7 with 8-bit alpha. None reports the
  `- top` origin suffix. Header inspection records uncompressed type 2, 32-bit
  depth, 8 alpha bits, bottom-left origin, and exact uncompressed byte length.
