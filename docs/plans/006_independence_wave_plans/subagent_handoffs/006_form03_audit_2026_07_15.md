# FORM-03 Low Countries Audit Handoff

Date: 2026-07-15
Owner: `event6_form03_audit`
Scope: `FORM-03` only

## Result

`FORM-03` is the third row in the authoritative formable registry. Its accepted
public identity is **Confederation of the Low Countries**, carried as the
cosmetic identity `LCX` by exactly `AFX`/IW-006 or `AGX`/IW-007.

The bounded formation transaction, living-member settlement, origin gates,
identity mutation, founding-anchor integration, AI consent, and flag ladder are
wired. This audit corrected four local execution defects and completed the
missing player-facing identity/localisation and asset documentation. The family
still lacks the post-charter federal language and industrial-integration
progression required by the registry row and the Event 006 formable acceptance
standard; that is broader design/implementation work and was not invented in
this bounded patch.

## Audit findings by surface

### Eligibility, territory, control, and cores

- Carrier eligibility is fail-closed to active Event 006 countries with exact
  `original_tag`/package pairs `AFX`/IW-006 or `AGX`/IW-007.
- `AFX` must own and control state 34 with its capital there; `AGX` must own and
  control state 36 with its capital there.
- The living Belgian founding delegation must be `BEL` with `BEL_flanders` and
  must both own and control state 6. The missing control test was corrected.
- A second consenting founding member and a non-war border, faction, access,
  guarantee, or non-aggression connection are required.
- Only a consenting `AFX` state 34 or `AGX` state 36 may transfer and receive an
  immediate core. States 6, 7, 8, 35, 977, and 980 are never transferred or
  cored by FORM-03. Belgium, the Netherlands, and Luxembourg remain sovereign.

### Route, costs, AI, and progression

- The registry permits a constitutional carrier or a carrier participating in
  the formal league's Development Compact. That restriction was absent and is
  now enforced by `has_independence_wave_form03_compatible_route` at consent,
  invitation, category, and final commit gates.
- FORM-03 supports only the negotiated-federation method. It reuses the shared
  administration, strategic, diplomatic, and 360-day congress costs, all of
  which use real payment effects and affordability predicates.
- AI authorization now favors the constitutional route and the persisted
  Development Compact instead of the incompatible popular-council route.
- The generic focus branch exposes discovery, congress preparation, terms, and
  the integration commission; FORM-03 adds explicit authorize/withhold choices
  and sovereign post-charter association.

### Creation, identity, origin, and package integration

- `independence_wave_formable_identity_adapter_3` applies `LCX` atomically,
  preserves the carrier's original tag, leader, focus framework, package, and
  capital anchor, and rolls the cosmetic identity back if integration fails.
- `independence_wave_formable_integration_adapter_3` processes only frozen,
  consenting member rows. It ends only an absorbed AFX/AGX Event 006 origin and
  preserves living Belgian institutions.
- Founding invitation checks now exclude an already formed carrier, preventing
  the pre-charter Belgian decisions from reappearing after formation.
- The former runtime `LCX = { exists = no ... }` country scope was removed.
  `LCX` is intentionally an unregistered cosmetic identity, not a country tag;
  static collision evidence belongs to the X-identity attestation, while the
  global `independence_wave_form03_lcx_identity_in_use` flag provides runtime
  exclusivity.
- A current collision scan found no `LCX` country-tag registration in vanilla,
  Chaos Redux, Kaiserreich 1521695605, or the approved 2265420196 and
  1458561226 reference installs.

### Localisation and visual identity

- Added all five FORM-03 decision names/descriptions, the category, effect
  tooltips, and the route requirement tooltip.
- Replaced the internal family-3 player-facing mapping with the researched
  public name `Confederation of the Low Countries`.
- Added base plus democratic, communist, fascist, and neutral `LCX` name,
  definite-name, and adjective keys. All ideology groups intentionally share
  one constitutional identity; no unsupported route identity was invented.
- Verified the normal, medium, and small `LCX` flag triplet against the
  generated source/contact sheet and canonical vanilla flag ladder. Added the
  required asset manifest and retained regeneration prompt.

## Focused validation scenarios

1. `AFX` or `AGX` on a constitutional route, controlling its exact capital
   anchor, can expose the consent surface; the same carrier on an incompatible
   government/league route cannot.
2. A non-constitutional carrier qualifies through the alternative only while
   it is a league member and the persisted global league route is
   `development_compact`.
3. Occupied state 6 prevents `BEL_flanders` from entering or counting in the
   founding ledger even if Belgium still owns it.
4. Founding consent succeeds with one connected consenting AFX/AGX partner or
   eligible Belgium; disconnected or hostile consent does not satisfy final
   commit proof.
5. Formation transfers/cores only the consenting AFX/AGX anchor, retains state
   34 or 36 as capital, and gives Belgium only sovereign membership flags.
6. After `LCX` becomes active, Belgian founding choices close and the bounded
   BEL/HOL/LUX sovereign-association choice becomes the only FORM-03 member
   action.

## Simplifications, omissions, and blockers

- **Completion blocker:** no FORM-03-specific post-charter federal-language
  settlement or industrial-integration progression exists. The current carrier
  keeps its package gameplay and can accept sovereign members, but the registry
  row's named post-formation system and the Part 7 requirement for meaningful
  post-formation play are not satisfied. This requires an accepted design
  addendum and implementation tranche; it was not replaced with generic stat
  rewards or a copied fallback.
- The exact original `$imagegen` service request envelope for the retained flag
  source was not stored. The accepted source, deterministic build, validation,
  contact sheet, and a canonical regeneration prompt are present, and the
  provenance caveat is explicit in the manifest.
- No other formable, package, focus route, spreadsheet row, or gameplay family
  was changed. No fallback or unreported substitute was used, and no commit was
  created.
