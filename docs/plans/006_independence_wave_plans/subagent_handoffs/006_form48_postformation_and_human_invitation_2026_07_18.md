## Public values, ideas, and work cycles

The carrier owns five visible 0–100 ledgers, shown to all three governments:

- shipping coordination: 45 opening;
- industrial specialization: 35 opening;
- defense readiness: 40 opening;
- member autonomy: 60 opening; and
- compact strain: 0 opening.

HBX/PFX receives `independence_wave_form48_federal_coordination`; HAW/FSM
receive `independence_wave_form48_autonomous_compact`. Both reuse the existing
league-membership picture. No new art, `.gfx` registration, character,
adviser, small portrait, or leader portrait was added.

The work program is recurring and serialized:

1. **Convoy defense:** HBX pays 20 Command Power, 20 convoys, 1,000 fuel, two
   civilian factories, and 120 days. The 180-day mission succeeds only after
   HAW and FSM each pay 10 Command Power, five convoys, and 75 days.
2. **Shared procurement:** HBX pays 10 convoys, 500 infantry equipment, 100
   support equipment, three civilian factories, and 180 days. The 240-day
   mission succeeds only after each member pays 250 infantry equipment, 50
   support equipment, one civilian factory, and 75 days.
3. **Negotiated basing:** HBX pays 20 Command Power, 100 support equipment, two
   civilian factories, and 150 days. The 210-day mission succeeds only after
   each member separately pays 10 Command Power, 50 support equipment, and 75
   days to grant limited, revocable consent.

Each member may instead take its dedicated 45-day withholding choice. The
response-in-progress trigger prevents simultaneous fulfillment and
withholding. Withholding raises the autonomy ledger by five but lowers the
relevant centralized value by five, adds ten strain, worsens the member's
league contribution/confidence record, raises former-host pressure, and lowers
league cohesion/confidence.

Mission success improves its relevant public values, removes 15 strain,
advances the cycle, improves the three former-host ledgers, and improves league
cohesion/common cause/reserve/confidence. Procurement success additionally
reduces patron influence for all three governments and league patron capture.
Mission failure removes 15 from its relevant values, adds 25 strain, worsens
the three former-host ledgers, reduces league values/reserves/confidence, and
increases patron capture.

All ordinary choices have explicit AI weights. Carrier AI prioritizes convoy
defense during war or former-host danger and procurement under patron
dependency. Stable member AI normally fulfills; war, severe instability, or
patron dependency raises the appropriate withholding preference.

## Failure, dissolution, and cleanup

Compact strain is clamped to 100. At 70, or if one exact member departs, HBX
can take the timed dissolution decision. Reaching 100 calls dissolution
automatically, including immediately after a withholding action that crosses
the threshold.

A fully withheld failed cycle adds 45 strain; two such cycles produce 90, so a
third withholding crosses the automatic threshold. A deadline failure without
withholding adds 25. Successful cycles can recover 15 strain but never below
zero. This makes failures consequential without allowing one unopposed timeout
to erase the federation.

Dissolution applies carrier/member country penalties, former-host pressure,
and league penalties, then uses the shared formable cleanup in its safe order.
FORM-48 cleanup removes all three missions, all dedicated choices, both ideas,
the five public ledgers and stage/cycle variables, both autonomous bindings,
and only transaction-owned reciprocal access and guarantee relations before
dropping PFX and releasing its global collision guard. The three countries and
their Event 006 origins remain alive.
