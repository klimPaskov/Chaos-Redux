## Design and source boundary

All three are original fictional 1936 alternate-history designs made with the official built-in ImageGen workflow and then mechanically flattened/resized. HZX uses a Basotho mokorotlo-and-shield silhouette; EUX uses an Nguni shield, crossed assegai, and woven border; ELX uses a Swahili-coast dhow, coral arch, waves, and clove rosette. They are not claims of exact historical standards.

No ideology variants were requested or produced. The first HZX generation was rejected because its small export collapsed into a detailed blob and is kept under `source_png/rejected/` for provenance.
