## Slot 23: The League of New States

### Role and context

Slot `23` announces the first durable Event 006 league after minimum founding membership, a successful congress, charter adoption, leadership selection, and activation as a diplomatic or military institution.

The accepted broad package must remain compatible with defensive and legalist, hopeful and developmental, sovereign-equality and anti-patron, armed-liberation, and radical-revisionist charter variants.

The Wilson source is especially suitable because Point XIV proposes an association formed under covenants and does not force the super-event to choose one later charter ideology.

### Considered main-quote candidates

| Candidate | Source and exactness | Role fit and rights assessment | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `A general association of nations must be formed under specific covenants...` | Exact opening of Point XIV on both the Yale and National Archives pages. Speaker: Woodrow Wilson. Document: *Fourteen Points*, Point XIV. Date: 8 January 1918, address to Congress. | Names the institutional act and covenant structure at the moment of formation. The underlying 1918 historical speech is age-cleared public-domain material in the United States, and the displayed excerpt is short. | **Selected. Attribution confidence: high.** |
| `mutual guarantees of political independence and territorial integrity to great and small states alike` | Exact continuation of Point XIV on both corroborating pages. | Strong sovereign-equality language, but it omits the association being formed and therefore reads as a guarantee clause rather than the first institutional reveal. Same historical-source status as the selected excerpt. | Backup fragment only. |
| `The Members of the League undertake to respect and preserve...` | Short exact opening of Article 10 in the 1919 Covenant page at the United Nations Office at Geneva. | Strong legalist and defensive guarantee, but it presupposes an already constituted League and is less suitable for a first-act formation cue. Historical treaty text; no modern work is being selected. | Rejected for this slot; retain as a documented alternative. |

The selected quotation is not a translation or transliteration; it is the English wording shown by the cited archival pages.

The player-facing excerpt uses three ASCII periods as an editorial omission marker because the source sentence continues after `covenants` and the inherited static UI evidence records that the quote font lacks a Unicode ellipsis glyph.

## Slot 24: Every Border a Casus Belli

### Role and context

Slot `24` announces a global threat escalation in which radical or militarized Event 006 governments convert sudden sovereignty into coordinated claims, ultimatums, arms shipments, sponsorship of breakaways, and synchronized war.

The accepted route-neutral package covers the offensive-league, high-chaos wave, synchronized former-host war, hidden-aggressive-formable, and league-sponsored-cascade trigger families.

It does not fit a normal wave, ordinary league election, weak consultative congress, isolated regional formable, or routine border war.

Hosea 8:7 is a compact cause-to-amplified-consequence image: deliberate sowing produces a larger whirlwind, matching the escalation from coordinated claims and support arrangements into a wider war system.

## Rights and copyright decision

The Wilson excerpt is the strongest low-risk selection for slot `23`: it is a short fragment of a 1918 historical address, independently corroborated by Yale and the U.S. National Archives, and no modern copyright work is involved.

The KJV excerpt and button remain the strongest role fit for slot `24` under the accepted Event 006 design, but the research record must distinguish the Bible Gateway passage-page `Public Domain` label from the version page's United Kingdom Crown-rights notice.

If the intended distribution policy requires an explicitly source-declared worldwide public-domain translation, the exact WEB package below is the preferred replacement candidate, but it is not selected in this handoff because the parent prompt forbids silent substitution.

```yaml
chaosx_super_event.24.a: "They sow the wind."
chaosx_super_event.24.q: "\"For they sow the wind, and they will reap the whirlwind.\"\n §Y-Hosea 8:7, World English Bible-§!"
```

The WEB alternative is supported by [eBible's Hosea 8 page](https://ebible.org/engwebp/HOS08.htm) and [World English Bible FAQ](https://ebible.org/eng-web/webfaq.htm), which state public-domain and personal/commercial-use terms.

No legal opinion is offered for either translation; the rights statements above are source-based distribution-risk notes.
