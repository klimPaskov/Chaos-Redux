## Decision and current blocker

The accepted Jeremiah Clarke / London Brass Players recording of *A Trumpet Voluntary* remains blocked. Its 1948 recording, first released in 1949, has no verified United States or worldwide redistribution permission for the exact recording. The composition is public domain, but that does not clear the separate recording right.

This pass found one new rights-complete research candidate, one lower-confidence licensed candidate, and reverified the existing Dutch candidate. None is an approved replacement. Audio ID `23`, its runtime WAV, base sound, settings wrappers, catalogue row, and firing/playback assignment remain absent by design.

The strongest legal candidate from this pass is **The Enola Foam March** by Peet/Pete Hudson. The source page identifies it as an original composition and own recording under CC BY-SA 4.0, the embedded file metadata identifies `Peter Hudson` / `Peet Hudson`, and the creator's Commons profile grants permission to use, remix, and publicly share the listed original works with credit. Its rights evidence is clearer than the older Dutch source chain, but its WWII-style and slight Klezmer framing create a material tone risk for a pluralistic league proclamation.

The existing **Toujours en Tête** / **Defileermars van het Regiment Infanterie Johan Willem Friso** remains the best documented formal-ceremony candidate. Its Dutch Ministry of Defence VRT permission record supports CC BY-SA 4.0 worldwide redistribution, but the original Ministry music URL currently returns 404 and the regimental-march identity still requires human tone review.

The Lully / Advent Chamber Orchestra recording of **Marche pour la Cérémonie des Turcs** is preserved as a lower-confidence comparison. Its public-domain composition and Commons CC BY-SA 2.0 recording label are attractive, but the cited ibiblio source and old EFF Open Audio License page are unavailable today, so its recording-rights chain is not strong enough to clear the gate.
