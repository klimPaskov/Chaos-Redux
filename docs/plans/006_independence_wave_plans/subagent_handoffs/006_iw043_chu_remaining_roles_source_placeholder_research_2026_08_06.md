## Civic-presidium candidate: Zinnetullah Ahsen Böre

### Identity, date, and role evidence

The [English biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zinnetullah_Ahsen_B%C3%B6re) and [Finnish biography](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zinnetullah_Ahsen_B%C3%B6re) identify Zinnetullah Ahsen Böre (born 20 March 1886, died 9 November 1945) as a Finnish Tatar publisher, businessman, imam, and spokesperson for a Turkish identity among Finland's Tatars. The biographies record his Tampere textile and fur shop and his publication of the first Finnish Quran translation. He was alive in the 1936 baseline and has a defensible civic-community fit for a fictional Bolgar presidium transfer, subject to parent approval.

The local immutable master is `docs/assets/006_independence_wave/iw043_chu_portrait_source_research_2026_08_02/source_masters/zinnetullah_ahsen_bore_commons.jpg`. The retained Commons API snapshots are `source_metadata/commons_imageinfo_zinnetullah_ahsen_bore.json` and `source_metadata/commons_raw_zinnetullah_ahsen_bore.json`.
