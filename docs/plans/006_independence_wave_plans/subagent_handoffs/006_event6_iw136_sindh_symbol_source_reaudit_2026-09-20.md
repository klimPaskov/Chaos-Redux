## Source URLs and findings

### Historical and institutional context

- [Provincial Assembly of Sindh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_Assembly_of_Sindh) records the separation of Sindh from the Bombay Presidency with effect from 1 April 1936. It is contextual secondary evidence, not a flag source.
- [Sind United Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sind_United_Party) records the June 1936 party and its communal-harmony purpose. The party page is contextual secondary evidence and supplies no period symbol.
- [Pakistan, a modern history](https://archive.org/details/pakistanmodernhi00talb) is the cited Ian Talbot archival catalogue lead for the historical party context; the item is access-restricted and was not treated as a symbol source.
- [Sindh Hari Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sindh_Hari_Committee), the [ILO catalogue record](https://ilo.primo.exlibrisgroup.com/discovery/fulldisplay/alma991822883402676/41ILO_INST:41ILO_V2), and the [IISH archives entry](https://www.iisg.nl/archives/en/files/p/10817906full.php) support the historical organization/archive lead but do not attribute the 2024 flag design to 1930–1936 use.
- [Karachi Port Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karachi_Port_Trust) provides the checked secondary history for the 1887 institution. The official [KPT site](https://kpt.gov.pk/) was attempted but did not return within the bounded request, so no official mark claim is made.
- The official [Sindh Irrigation Department](https://irrigation.sindh.gov.pk/) confirms the current irrigation and Sukkur Barrage institutional surface. [Sukkur Barrage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukkur_Barrage) provides the checked secondary 1932 opening date; neither source supplies a period mark.
