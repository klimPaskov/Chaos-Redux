The NARA `Von Wrede` item (`162122497`, `242-HF-0518_001.jpg`) is an unidentified wedding group and is not a production-safe commander source.

Otto von Lossow remains blocked because the exact Bain/Commons photograph has only a `PD-US` statement and no verified exact Library of Congress object; the similarly titled LOC record is not the same photograph.

Konrad Krafft von Dellmensingen has a pre-1933, no-political-insignia portrait and passes the identity-ownership gate, but the source is a 471x593 Commons scan from Fritz Jung's 1933 book with an unknown photographer and `PD-anon-70-EU` template. It lacks an object-level institutional archive accession, so it is a documented `needs_user_review` alternative rather than a stronger clearance.

No additional candidate found in the bounded sweep is both better-provenanced than Dollmann and free of the ownership or identity defects above.
