# Event 006 FORM-12 / FORM-13 / FORM-18 Signature Architecture Handoff

## Scope and ownership

This handoff records the reusable scripted architecture produced for the
IW-043 and IW-058 signature formables and the parent resolution that connected
it to decisions, events, achievements, and cleanup. The subagent owned the
registry constants, predicates, and effects. The parent owns final gameplay
wiring, validation, and completion claims.

No advisor portraits, icons, sprites, GFX, dossiers, or other advisor assets
were created. Gameplay advisors remain asset-neutral.

## Exact member contract

FORM-12 and FORM-13 admit only sovereign, active, origin-safe countries with a
unique anchor they currently own and control:

- `IW-044/TAT`
- `IW-045/BSK`
- `IW-047/MEL`
- `IW-048/UDM`
- `IW-050/KOM`

FORM-18 admits only:

- `IW-060/KUR`
- `IW-062/CJX`

The shared CHU rows `IW-043` and `IW-046`, the ASY signature country, Event 5
origins, subjects, countries at war with the carrier, countries already in a
formable transaction, and missing or non-controlled anchors are rejected.
`IW-059` remains a vanilla overlay and is not treated as a sovereign member.

FORM-12 and FORM-13 require three members, three explicit consents, and three
unique anchors. FORM-18 requires two of each. AI consent is scored from route
compatibility, host threat, recognition, bilateral relations, the autonomy
offer, disputes, and instability. Human candidates receive exact accept or
reject events and are never auto-accepted.
