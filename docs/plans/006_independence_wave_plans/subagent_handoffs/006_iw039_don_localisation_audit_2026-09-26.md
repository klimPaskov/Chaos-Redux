## IW-039 findings and recommended wording

1. **The hearing understates a negative effect.** `independence_wave_don_petitions_complete_tt` at `localisation/english/006_independence_wave_don_l_english.yml:19` says the hearing makes disputes and obligations “visible.” The effect actually increases former-host obligations and property and population disputes at `common/scripted_effects/006_independence_wave_don_package_effects.txt:92-107`, as the system document states at `docs/events/006_independence_wave/systems/iw039_don_millerovo_host_charter.md:89`. Recommend: “The council records host and non-host claims. Legitimacy rises, but the hearing also brings property and population disputes and former-host obligations to the fore.” Keep the exact gameplay meaning when the owning agent confirms how those bilateral values are presented.

2. **The federation docket is presented as an available player action.** `independence_wave_don_charter_failed_tt`, `independence_wave_don_charter_succeeded_tt`, `independence_wave_don_emergency_review_failed_tt`, and `independence_wave_don_ratify_the_millerovo_charter_tt` at localisation lines `5-6`, `10`, and `52` say a separate Don-Kuban federation docket opens or closes. Success sets only `independence_wave_don_form11_docket_open` at `common/scripted_effects/006_independence_wave_don_package_effects.txt:260-265`; failure clears it at lines `287-295`. There is no consumer in the named Don decisions or focus file, and the system document says FORM-11 still needs a separately accepted carrier and formation decision at lines `17`, `77-79`, and `169`. This is a cross-surface promise that a player can act on a docket which is not presently available. Until a consumer is implemented, remove the docket claim from these four strings and describe the ratification, recognition, network, and failure consequences actually present. If a later accepted feature supplies a visible docket, revise those keys with its real reveal condition.

3. **The government requirement tooltip repeats gates already required for visibility and misleads when funds are short.** `independence_wave_don_government_available_tt` at localisation line `32` says the railhead receipt, petition receipt, and issue focuses are required. Each government decision is visible only after `has_independence_wave_don_government_requirements` at `common/decisions/006_independence_wave_don_decisions.txt:170-179`, `193-202`, and `220-229`; the trigger includes both receipts and focuses at `common/scripted_triggers/006_independence_wave_don_package_triggers.txt:141-150`. Once the row appears, political power is the remaining ordinary gate (`triggers:152-159`), shown in the cost row. Recommend a requirement tooltip that explains a genuine remaining condition or remove the repeated custom wrapper if the native cost display suffices. Preserve the exact route lock and payment checks in script.

4. **The two project requirement strings omit serialization.** `independence_wave_don_railhead_available_tt` and `independence_wave_don_petitions_available_tt` at localisation lines `13` and `18` list the focus, charter mission, and Millerovo control. Both project triggers additionally require no active Don timed project at `common/scripted_triggers/006_independence_wave_don_package_triggers.txt:125-139`. Recommend adding “Only one charter survey or hearing can run at a time” to the relevant blocked text, ideally as a distinct condition so the player sees the actual blocker. Their focus and mission gates remain accurate.

5. **The constitutional cost requirement points vaguely at UI placement.** `independence_wave_don_constitutional_available_tt` at localisation line `33` says the command-power commitment is “shown below.” The constitutional cost row at lines `62-64`, decision wiring at `common/decisions/006_independence_wave_don_decisions.txt:198-210`, and payment trigger at `common/scripted_triggers/006_independence_wave_don_package_triggers.txt:116-119` already state the exact dynamic cost. Recommend removing this redundant sentence or using a precise, dynamic command-power condition only if the native cost row does not make the blocked amount clear. The label should not depend on screen placement.

6. **Two focus rewards speak in implementation terms.** `independence_wave_don_survey_rail_and_remount_stores_tt` and `independence_wave_don_hear_land_and_service_claims_tt` at localisation lines `43` and `46` explain that a focus “does not grant its receipt” or “settle a government.” The focus rewards at `common/national_focus/006_independence_wave_don_focus.txt:51-53` and `74-76` unlock the paid decision sequence. Recommend short consequence-led text such as “The council can commission a paid railhead survey” and “The council can convene a paid district hearing.” The existing decision rows explain the price and the receipt after completion.

7. **The quota description exposes an internal abstraction and may imply an unimplemented troop grant.** `independence_wave_don_set_the_service_quota_desc` at localisation line `48` says “bounded mounted-service quota under the existing force pathways.” The effect at `common/scripted_effects/006_independence_wave_don_package_effects.txt:230-249` sets a quota flag and changes security, capacity, former-host obligations, and network standing. It does not add a division or expose a numeric quota. The system document explicitly says so at lines `75` and `99`. Recommend describing the actual bargain, for example: “The council can set how much mounted service the districts owe. The commitment strengthens security and capacity but adds to obligations owed to the former host.” Confirm the intended subject of “former-host obligations” with the owning agent before adopting that sentence. A visible numeric quota would require a separate design and script handoff.

8. **A few strings obscure who is included or what changes.** `don_elected_host_assembly_desc` at localisation line `75` calls non-host residents those “within and beyond the host,” which reads as geography rather than their petition status. Recommend “The elected assembly gives host and non-host residents standing to petition the district council.” `independence_wave_don_review_civilian_command` at line `30` is ambiguous beside “Review the Emergency Host Staff” at line `7`; the decision at `common/decisions/006_independence_wave_don_decisions.txt:247-265` submits the emergency staff's service mandate for civilian review. Recommend “Submit the Emergency Mandate for Civilian Review,” subject to title width in the native decision row. `independence_wave_don_register_millerovo_authorities_desc` at line `39` says offices act “in the name of the new charter” before ratification. Recommend “The provisional council records which district offices and mounted staff may act in its name.”

9. **Cost display has one unresolved blocked-state nuance.** `independence_wave_don_cost_constitutional_blocked` at localisation line `63` colors both political and command power red whenever the blocked variant is selected, even if only one resource is insufficient. The two affordability predicates are separate at `common/scripted_triggers/006_independence_wave_don_package_triggers.txt:112-119`. A native decision render is needed to establish whether the UI ever displays this `_blocked` key and whether it colors both values together. If so, the blocked display should identify the unpaid resource without marking a payable resource red. No scripted GUI for this package was identified, so the exposed scripted-GUI renderer does not provide a native decision row here.

The five cost tooltip keys at localisation lines `55`, `58`, `61`, `64`, and `67` have no explicit binding in the named Don decisions or focuses. They repeat the cost row and action title. They are not missing keys or broken bindings; they appear to be unused prose. If the owning agent confirms no implicit engine use, they can be removed or repurposed for a genuine payment consequence. The dynamic amount tokens and icon pairings should remain in all displayed cost strings.
