## Rights and date evidence

The primary VTsIK decree dated 20 October 1930 establishes the autonomy date and the fact that the oblast remained a territorial part of the West Siberian Krai; it does not establish sovereignty or an independent state flag.

The official Government of the Republic of Khakassia historical summary describes 20 October 1930 as the beginning of Khakass statehood in the form of an autonomous oblast and explicitly describes the limited rights and self-administration attached to that status.

The 1925 RSFSR Constitution, in the text applicable through the pre-1937 amendments, supports the following bounded claims: equality and protection for nationalities plus native-language use and autonomous republic/oblast formation in Article 13; VTsIK oversight of autonomous boundaries and constitutions in Article 17; Congress-of-Soviets and executive-committee formation for autonomous oblasts in Article 44; executive-committee accountability and subordination in Article 54; and local cultural, economic, and administrative competence in Article 64.

Article 88 of that Constitution defines the period RSFSR state flag as a red or scarlet field with gold `Р.С.Ф.С.Р.` letters in the upper hoist. This is a defensible neutral legal symbol only for a Soviet-autonomy route, not evidence of a separate KHA national flag.

The secondary Khakass Autonomous Oblast chronology places the region in Krasnoyarsk Krai from the end of 1934, which is consistent with the installed KHA state being a Soviet-host remnant rather than a pre-existing sovereign country.
