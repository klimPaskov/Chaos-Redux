### Source checks for strongest unresolved emblem leads

FORM-13 Idel-Ural remains blocked. The Flags of the World Idel-Ural page records a light-blue/gold tamga association but cautions that the blue flag has no records before Gayaz Iskhaki's 1933 book and that the only authentic 1918 lead is a logo on a proclamation, not proof of a flag design. The available Commons vectors are later reconstructions, one is explicitly marked fictitious, and FOTW's reuse disclaimer is not a clean redistribution grant for a modified political game asset. The exact source pages are [FOTW Idel-Ural](https://www.crwflags.com/fotw/flags/ru-idurh.html), [Commons Flag of Idel-Ural State](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Idel-Ural_State.svg), [Commons Idel-Ural flag](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Idel-Ural_flag.svg), [Commons Idel-ural ver1](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Idel-ural_ver1.svg), and [FOTW reuse disclaimer](https://www.crwflags.com/fotw/flags/disclaim.html). No candidate was downloaded or promoted.

FORM-43 Gran Colombian Federation remains blocked. The CC BY-SA 3.0/4.0 Commons vectors are historical flag renditions, not an exact formable emblem and not tied to a stable Event 006 X/UI consumer. They remain research leads only and must not be converted into an emblem.

The shared league mark remains blocked. The [League of Nations 1939 flag](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_the_League_of_Nations_(1939).svg) is CC BY-SA 4.0 but its own historical note says the League never had an official flag, logo, or emblem and that the displayed design was a New York World's Fair communication illustration. The extracted [League of Nations emblem](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Emblem_of_the_League_of_Nations_(1939).svg) therefore has the wrong institutional identity for Event 006's League of New States. No universal emblem, source, owner, or consumer is established.
