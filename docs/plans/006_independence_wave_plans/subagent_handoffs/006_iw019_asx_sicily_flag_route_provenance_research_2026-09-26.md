## Historical candidate and rights findings

The Italian Ministry of Culture catalog records the surviving 1848 Sicilian national colour S.15: vertical green/ecru/red fields and a gold embroidered Trinacria, sent under the Sicilian Parliament’s 1 April 1848 decree after Sicily declared independence from Bourbon rule ([Ministry catalog record](https://catalogo.beniculturali.it/detail/HistoricOrArtisticProperty/0100215963); local evidence summary: `docs/assets/006_independence_wave/mediterranean_danube_flag_sources_2026_07_15/historical_evidence.md:56-78`).
This supports the flag’s date, geometry, regional emblem, and revolutionary-government ownership, so it fits the constitutional route much better than the other four.

The source packet also attributes the 28 March 1848 Trinacria arms decree and the 25 April addition of the device to the national flag to the Archivio di Stato di Palermo parliamentary-decree collection ([archive collection](https://digitalibrary-saassipa.cultura.gov.it/entities/archivalmaterial/20749410-4367-4365-9f75-33392164d6ae)).
The archive link did not resolve during this pass, so those two decree details remain supported here by the existing source packet rather than a fresh page read.
The official regional history and law date the present red/yellow diagonal regional flag to 2000, so it is not a period 1936 substitute ([Regione Siciliana flag history](https://www.regione.sicilia.it/istituzioni/i-simboli-della-regione/la-bandiera), [regional law](https://www.regione.sicilia.it/istituzioni/regione/strutture-regionali/presidenza-regione/ufficio-rappresentanza-cerimoniale/normativa/legge-4)).

Historical identity and image rights are separate.
The catalog’s metadata is recorded locally as CC BY 4.0, but the attached object photographs carry the Beni Culturali Standard (BCS) reuse label, not a plain CC BY image license.
BCS permits specified creative and editorial uses with attribution and the same conditions on sharing; lucrative use requires prior authorization, so do not treat the catalog metadata license as clearance for its photographs ([BCS terms](https://docs.italia.it/italia/icdp/icdp-pnd-circolazione-riuso-docs/it/v1.0-giugno-2022/testo-etichetta-BCS.html)).
The archive scan is recorded in the source packet as all-rights-reserved and remains link-only.

The saved modern SVG layout aid was created in 2020 and is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, with later Wikimedia Commons contributors credited in its file history ([Commons file and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Sicilian_Kingdom_1848.svg); local source notes: `docs/assets/006_independence_wave/mediterranean_danube_flag_sources_2026_07_15/source_images/README.md:9,17`).
It is not the surviving object, and its flesh-and-gold Trinacria differs from S.15’s all-gold embroidery.
If retained as a design input, preserve its attribution and share-alike obligations.
The existing generated master’s package records this input but does not state separate provider/output redistribution terms, so no such status is inferred here.
