## Source-safe findings

- The outer selection is probability-proportional over the fourteen prepared region totals. The `independence_wave_calculate_candidate_allocation_weight` helper at `common/scripted_effects/006_independence_wave_package_planner_effects.txt:585` produces candidate allocation scores. Those scores are not click probabilities.
- Each nested regional selector first gates candidates through `can_plan_independence_wave_package_*`, loads package data, calculates a candidate score, and adds it to a regional total before the nested `random_list`. Positive source weight therefore does not prove that the country, host, anchor, target state, reservation group, or route is valid.
- `independence_wave_allocate_automatic_packages` retains an exact target-count check and writes `liberation_plan_reject_reason.insufficient_pool` on shortfall. The zero-total source fixture proves only that the random-list adapter has no conditional probability when all values are zero. It does not prove the complete retry, replacement, or terminal-state behavior.
- The source and accepted matrices contain multiple score ladders and conditional modifiers for decisions, missions, focuses, and package selection. The current MCP evidence does not normalize those score races into campaign probabilities.
- No current evidence proves dominance, starvation, rank reversal, repetition rate, cooldown drift, recovery behavior, unsafe snowballing, or campaign MTTH for the nested package pool or AI score surfaces.
