## Accepted design basis

The source design is accepted and was queued for implementation by D30, D31, and D33 in `docs/plans/006_independence_wave_plans/agent_decisions_2026-10-04.md`: D30 lines 779-800 accepts and queues the Indus Federation because all four named members are admitted; D31 lines 808-834 accepts and queues the Maritime and Mainland Southeast Asian Federations because their member minimums are met; D33 lines 868-884 accepts and queues the North Caucasian Federation because its three named members are admitted.

Part 6 of the accepted Event 006 specification, `docs/specs/006_independence_wave_specs/specs/006_independence_wave_spec_part_6_formables_league_and_scenario.md:78-87`, establishes the governing contract: formation sets a carrier cosmetic identity and runs two sovereignty-preserving integration stages while members keep their tags, sovereignty, Event 006 origins, territory, focus content, and units; annexation, subject creation, blanket core grants, and origin-ending shortcuts are excluded.

The lead’s 2026-10-06 G6 instruction authorizes correcting weak ready-member formable wiring within these four accepted families. The selected identity strings and tags are NCX North Caucasian Federation, IDX Indus Federation, MRX Maritime Federation, and MSX Mainland Southeast Asian Federation; these are cosmetic identities on an existing carrier, not new sovereign countries or release tags.

The concrete integration split follows the authorized regional aftermaths and the accepted two-stage model: FORM-17 funds mountain defense before the autonomy charter; FORM-32 funds a water-and-rail board before language and army settlement; FORM-36 funds shipping schedules before customs and island autonomy; FORM-37 funds a mainland transport authority before joint command and strong member autonomy. Reusing the current standard costs and 180-day project duration makes each benefit follow an explicit payment and avoids a parallel cost system or repeatable reward loop.
