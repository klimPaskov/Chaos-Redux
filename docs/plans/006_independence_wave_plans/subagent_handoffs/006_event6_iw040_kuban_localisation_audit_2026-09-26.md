## Prose findings and recommended key-level changes

1. **Misleading implementation language and cross-surface mismatch:** `independence_wave_kub_border_guards_project_paid_tt` and `independence_wave_kub_community_compacts_project_paid_tt` call the destination a “mounted-service focus docket” and “petition focus docket.” The only destination focus is **Publish the Land and Service Docket**. “Records payment” describes an internal receipt rather than what the player unlocks. Suggested wording: “Completing this project satisfies the paid requirement for Publish the Land and Service Docket.” for each key. These lines are in `localisation/english/006_independence_wave_frontier_l_english.yml:210-211`; callers are decision lines 736 and 802. The project titles at frontier lines 169 and 171 match the gate names.
2. **Vagueness and obvious explanation:** `independence_wave_kub_publish_land_and_service_docket_paid_recorded_tt` (“A completed, matching paid project is on record”) does not identify what matched. The gate line already names both options. Suggested wording: “The paid project for your chosen route is complete.” at `localisation/english/006_independence_wave_focus_l_english.yml:569`. Its caller is focus line 5173.
3. **Bloat and bureaucratic phrasing:** Both companion keys say that “this focus and the completed ... project are both recorded.” The sentence uses three completion markers to explain one condition. Suggested wording: “Complete this focus and Register the Community Compacts to gain up to [existing minor-gain token] Civic Concord once.” and the matching Border Guards/Mounted Readiness wording. Preserve the existing token and once-only meaning. These keys are focus lines 560 and 564; callers appear in both focus rewards and project success effects.
4. **Vagueness:** `independence_wave_kub_publish_land_and_service_docket_desc` ends with “the questions still owed to the former host.” The referent is unclear. Suggested wording: “The provisional record lists rights under review, mounted-service obligations, and claims still requiring talks with the former host.” at focus localisation line 566. This preserves the former-host claim without inventing a resolution.
5. **Style-rule repair:** The two branch effect tooltips at focus localisation lines 559 and 563 use semicolons to join gain and loss. Replace each semicolon with a period or “while,” preserving both dynamic tokens and the gain/loss distinction. No em dash, prompt fragment, update-history claim, or hidden route reveal was found in the scoped text.

The first two focus descriptions are concrete and distinct: public petitions precede a grain/rail register, then a hearing or service-roll audit. The branch titles and project titles name different actions. The docket gate accurately gives the route-specific sequence. The compact transition line is clear and only emits after the values cross the stability threshold. There is no sourced or attributed quotation on these surfaces; no quotation wording needs preservation.
