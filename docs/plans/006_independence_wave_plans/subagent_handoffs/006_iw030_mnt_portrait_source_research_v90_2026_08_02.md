## Selected candidate

Janko Vukotić (1866–1927) is a documented Montenegrin serdar and Army General who served as prime minister from 1913 to 1915, minister of war and foreign affairs, and chief of staff of Montenegro's Supreme Command during World War I. Role evidence is recorded from the English Wikipedia article and raw page at <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janko_Vukoti%C4%87> and <https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Janko_Vukoti%C4%87&action=raw>, with <https://www.worldstatesmen.org/Montenegro.html> retained as a secondary state-office cross-check.

The selected source is Commons [File:Serdar Janko Vukotić with his family.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Serdar_Janko_Vukoti%C4%87_with_his_family.jpg), explicitly dated circa 1910 and carrying `PD-Montenegro`. The canonical downloaded image URL is <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/65/Serdar_Janko_Vukoti%C4%87_with_his_family.jpg>. Commons gives the source as a Kurir 2018 repost and the original author as unknown. The public-domain/reuse basis is therefore explicit at the Commons-record level, while the original photographer, archive, and first-publication date remain uncertain and are not invented in the handoff.
