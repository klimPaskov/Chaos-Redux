## Source attribution, era fit, and rights

- Official Xunta de Galicia flag page: <https://www.xunta.gal/a-bandeira>. The archived source note records access on 2026-08-12 and describes the civil flag as 3:2, white, with a light/cobalt-blue diagonal from upper-left to lower-right; it also states that there is no testimony proving the current flag before the twentieth century and explains the A Coruña Naval Command and emigrant lineage.
- Official Xunta de Galicia shield page: <https://www.xunta.gal/o-escudo>. The archived source note dates the definitive shield model to 1972 and its regulation to the 5 May 1984 symbols law.
- Historical corroboration: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_of_Galicia>. The archived source note records public white-and-blue use by 1891 and distinguishes the 1936 red-star `Estreleira` as a left-nationalist route symbol rather than the default civil flag.
- Geometry reference: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Galicia.svg> and <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/64/Flag_of_Galicia.svg>. The archived source note attributes the reference to Pedro A. Gracia Fajardo, records first upload on 25 November 2005 and current revision on 26 February 2006, leaves the source date blank, marks the SVG W3C-invalid, and records Public Domain in the United States only.

- The installed vanilla TGAs are game-install source files, not a mod redistribution licence, and the official Xunta pages are identity and geometry evidence rather than permission to ship derivative binaries.
