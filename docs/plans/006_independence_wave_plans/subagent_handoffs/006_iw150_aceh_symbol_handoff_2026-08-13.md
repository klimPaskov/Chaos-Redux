## Exact accepted source

- Source page: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Aceh_Sultanate.svg>
- Source identity: `Flag of Aceh Sultanate.svg`.
- Source type: public-domain reconstruction hosted on Wikimedia Commons; the file history identifies a drawing in *Tarikh Aceh dan Nusantara* as the design basis.
- Rights/provenance: the Commons page presents the reconstruction as public domain. This is sufficient for a route-owned research candidate under the accepted ledger, but it does not convert the modern reconstruction into a contemporaneous 1936 source. Preserve the page URL, file name, reconstruction status, and underlying-book basis in any future manifest; do not claim an original 1936 flag or an unqualified period adoption.
- Historical/date fit: usable only for a traditional/sultanate route. The accepted source ledger and dossier do not establish this reconstruction as the standard of the 1936 coalition authority.
- Required attribution/provenance note for later asset work: “Modern public-domain reconstruction; design basis identified in *Tarikh Aceh dan Nusantara*; route-owned Aceh Sultanate/traditional branch; not a neutral 1936 coalition flag.”

## Route and chronology guardrails

1. `ATJ` is the reused registered tag for IW-150 and remains high-chaos-only while not living; this handoff does not change that ownership decision.
2. The 1936 opening authority is the coalition of uleebalang, ulama, merchants, district notables, veterans, schools, and port administration. No source in the accepted dossier assigns that coalition the Sultanate reconstruction as its neutral emblem.
3. PUSA content and symbols begin in 1939 or later.
4. Daud Beureueh is a later educational/religious reform figure and must not be used as the opening 1936 identity anchor.
5. Aceh, Minangkabau, and Riau share `RG-SUMATRA`; this symbol handoff does not authorize a shared or pan-Sumatran emblem.
