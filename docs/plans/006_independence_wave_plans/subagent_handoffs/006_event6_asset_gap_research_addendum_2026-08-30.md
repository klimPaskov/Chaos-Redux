## Method and rights boundary

The local source registers, package manifests, validation records, current runtime hashes, and the canonical reference root were checked against the parent asset prompt.

Exact Wikimedia Commons file pages were checked for the historical reference candidates listed below on 2026-08-30; the page timestamp is a Commons revision timestamp, not the design adoption date.

A Commons licence for a reference file does not transfer to an independently generated ImageGen master, and no provider generation date or applicable output terms are recorded for the chunk-3 or BWX masters.

The local reference PNGs remain comparison inputs only; no external reference pixels were newly downloaded, copied, traced, cropped, or promoted.

Period evidence exists for the Soviet Mordovian ASSR, not for a neutral Erzya-Moksha Federal Republic.

The [Russian Presidential Library sketch record 1417941](https://www.prlib.ru/item/1417941) and [decree record 1418019](https://www.prlib.ru/item/1418019) date the Mordovian ASSR flag and emblem approval to 27 December 1934 and identify the Central State Archive of the Republic of Mordovia as the source archive.

The [Russian Centre of Vexillology and Heraldry Mordovia record](https://vexillographia.ru/russia/subjects/mordovia.htm) describes the 1934 Soviet ASSR design and separately dates the modern Mordovia flag to 1995/2008.

The [Commons reconstruction of the 1934–1937 Mordovian ASSR flag](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Mordovian_ASSR_(1934-1937).svg) and its [variant](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Mordovian_ASSR_(1934-1937)_(Variant_2).svg) are labelled public domain on their file pages, but they are reconstructions of a Soviet ASSR design and do not establish BWX's neutral federal identity.

The [Fenno-Ugria Erzya page](https://fennougria.ee/en/peoples/mordvins/erzya/), [Fenno-Ugria Moksha page](https://fennougria.ee/en/peoples/mordvins/moksha/), and [M.A. Castrén Society clothing-symbolism page](https://www.ugri.net/in-english/cultures/mordvins/symbolism-of-mordvin-clothing/) support people, language, textile, rosette, rhombus, and protective-motif context, but do not attest a state flag owned by a 1936 federal route.

The 1934 archive material has period fit but fails current identity fit, and its public item pages do not state scan redistribution terms.

The exact release gate is a parent decision between a Soviet successor identity and an explicitly fictional civic synthesis, followed by a map anchor/capital/state-split decision; the current reservation does not authorize silently binding BWX to states 255, 256, 399, 397, 574, or 564.

If the fictional route is approved, the replacement package must identify itself as alternate-history, separate textile and ritual motifs from state ownership, record provider generation date and output terms, and reuse the unchanged `BWX` basename only after the identity and map gates close.
