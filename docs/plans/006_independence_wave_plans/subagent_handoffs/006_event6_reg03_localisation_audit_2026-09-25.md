## Actionable findings, ranked

1. **Moderate — satisfied docket conditions still say “Requires completion”.** The TRA, BOS, and MAC docket `custom_override_tooltip` blocks set both `tooltip` and `not_tooltip` to the same key at `common/national_focus/006_independence_wave_focus.txt:4408`, `:4541`, and `:4674`. The corresponding strings at `localisation/english/006_independence_wave_focus_l_english.yml:462`, `:481`, and `:500` describe only an unmet requirement. This is a confirmed wording defect in the satisfied state, though the paid-receipt OR gates themselves match the named decisions. Give the satisfied and unsatisfied states distinct wording and keys, for example “Multiethnic assembly or regulars screening completed” versus “Complete the multiethnic assembly or regulars screening”; make parallel BOS/MAC versions. The official vanilla `triggers_documentation.md` explicitly distinguishes `tooltip` and `not_tooltip` for this trigger. Runtime appearance remains unverified because imported nodes are absent from the MCP render.
2. **Moderate — reward amounts are duplicated as literal text.** All fifteen base reward tooltips and six companion tooltips at `localisation/english/006_independence_wave_focus_l_english.yml:449-501` print `+5`, `+10`, or `-5`, while focus effects use named pressure constants, for example `common/national_focus/006_independence_wave_focus.txt:4347-4357`, `:4480-4491`, and `:4613-4624`. Source, documentation, and current text express the same minor/standard tradeoffs, but the exact numeric constant definitions are outside the assigned files and were not independently inspected. Bind displayed amounts to their existing constants, or keep an explicit tuning handoff so the strings cannot drift. The clamped ledger helpers also mean these are nominal deltas, not a promise of an exact resulting ledger value at a cap.
3. **Low — several descriptions need concrete subjects and actions.** “Municipal records ... agree” gives an inanimate subject an action at `localisation/english/006_independence_wave_focus_l_english.yml:448`; make the clerks, councils, and veteran delegates agree on district authority. “The service board links farms, railheads, workshops, and guarded mountain routes” at `:470` and “Transport authorities connect harvest traffic, small workshops, and mountain depots” at `:489` do not say what is actually linked or moved; name the supply or transport arrangement. “Community, transit, and former-host questions” at `:498` is abstract; name the disputed rights or routes. These are prose-quality findings, not missing mechanics inferred from the text.
4. **Low — avoid an abstract contrast in the BOS docket.** `localisation/english/006_independence_wave_focus_l_english.yml:479` says the government submits rights “to a staged settlement rather than a unilateral map revision”. Lead with the tangible docket and the parties who will negotiate it; the current contrast is bureaucratic and does not clarify a player choice. The TRA and MAC docket descriptions at `:460` and `:498` provide the route-specific counterparts to preserve.
