## Lifecycle and category notes

The signature categories append 1 Oyo, 3 Aceh, and 3 Mon timed decision actions to categories that contain 4, 3, and 3 earlier Level 2 actions respectively, for 5/6/6 defined actions per category. The Level 3 actions remain gated behind each package’s completed Level 2 work and route state. The shared slot limits the new timed signature projects to one active project per country package.

There are no separate mission definitions in these owned files; the seven `days_remove` decisions provide the timed objectives. Their owners are the released country scopes DSX, ATJ, and IAX, and their categories are Oyo, Aceh, and Mon. Durations use shared constants: `standard = 120` days and `long = 180` days. The strategic/admin projects with `civilian_factory_use` reserve two factories during the timer; the other projects use their stated duration without that modifier.

Success is handled by each `remove_effect`, which calls a completion helper that rechecks its finish predicate, performs the accepted project or institutional change, and clears its project receipt. Cancellation occurs when the finish predicate becomes false; the matching `cancel_effect` clears the started flag and generation/package variables. Package cleanup removes all seven decisions and explicitly clears all seven active flags and their generation/package receipts, so cleanup remains covered when `remove_decision` bypasses `remove_effect`. Cancellation does not refund costs, as the existing localized cancellation text states.

No duplicate-completion loop was found: project receipts include generation and package identity, successful stages set completion flags or replace the relevant idea, and the resulting stage predicates prevent repeat completion. The one-slot trigger counts exactly the seven project-start flags; all seven are cleared both by their cancellation/completion helpers and by package cleanup.
