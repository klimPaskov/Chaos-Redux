## Role and date findings

FER was established on 1920-04-06 and liquidated on 1922-11-15 according to the archived and freshly checked FER history page.

The 1936 Event 006 opening therefore requires an explicitly documented restored, revival-era, or institution-backed interpretation if a former FER figure is used as a current leader token.

### Recommended human candidate: Pyotr Mikhailovich Nikiforov

The Russian identity page identifies Nikiforov as Chairman of the FER Council of Ministers from 1921-05-08 through 1922-10-02, with Boris Shumyatsky as predecessor and Pyotr Kobozev as successor.

The English identity page calls him the third Chairman of the Council of Ministers, but gives a shorter end date of December 1921, so the exact end-date wording remains a source discrepancy.

The source page identifies the photographed person as P. M. Nikiforov and dates the image to 1922.

He was born 1882-10-12 and died 1974-01-06, so he was alive in 1936 but was not a documented active FER officeholder after the republic's dissolution.

Identity fit: **PASS for research attribution only**.

Historical role/date fit: **CONDITIONAL HOLD** pending parent acceptance of the restored-1936 semantics and a single documented role/date wording.

Framing fit: **PASS for source-crop review**; the available crop is a centered solo male portrait with clear facial visibility and adequate head-and-shoulder framing.

Rights/provenance fit: **HOLD**; the Commons metadata asserts public domain, but project acceptance still requires the independent rights decision described below.

### Secondary human candidate: Alexander Mikhailovich Krasnoshchyokov

The English identity page identifies Krasnoshchyokov as the first Chairman of the FER Government from 1920-04-06 to December 1921 and the first Chairman of the Council of Ministers from 1920-04-06 to November 1920.

The same page lists a first Foreign Affairs Minister term from 1920-04-06 to June 1920, while the surrounding source wording does not provide a clean, reconciled institutional timeline for production use.

He was born 1880-10-10 and died 1937-11-26, so he was alive in 1936 but was not a documented active FER officeholder after the republic's dissolution.

Identity fit: **PASS for research attribution only**.

Historical role/date fit: **HOLD** pending reconciliation of the source wording and parent acceptance of restored-1936 semantics.

Framing fit: **CONDITIONAL HOLD**; the centered solo crop is usable as evidence, but the image is substantially darker and grainier than the Nikiforov source.

Rights/provenance fit: **HOLD**; the Commons metadata asserts Russian and U.S. public-domain status, but project acceptance still requires the independent rights decision described below.

### Institutional evidence: 1923 FER government passport

The passport is dated 1923 and attributed by the Commons metadata to the Government of the Far Eastern Republic.

It contains no person suitable for an attributed human portrait and cannot fill a human 156x210 leader slot.

Institutional fit: **EVIDENCE ONLY**; it may support an explicitly institution-backed token if the parent chooses that design, but it must not be wired as a human leader portrait or presented as a 1936 officeholder.
