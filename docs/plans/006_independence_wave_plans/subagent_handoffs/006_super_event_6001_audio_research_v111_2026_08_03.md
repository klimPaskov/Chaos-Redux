## Editing and conversion steps

1. Downloaded the Public Domain Project's original FLAC and Ogg mirror without modifying either source.
2. Mapped the first audio stream only and preserved stereo.
3. Selected the opening `0.000000` through `110.000000` seconds as a one-shot excerpt, staying within the super-event 1-2 minute target and avoiding a loop.
4. Applied a `1.5 s` fade-in from excerpt time `0.000` to `1.500` seconds.
5. Applied a `2.0 s` fade-out from excerpt time `108.000` to `110.000` seconds.
6. Ran two-pass EBU R128-style `loudnorm` targeting `-18 LUFS`, `-2 dBTP`, and `11 LU` maximum target range; the first-pass measurements were `input_i=-26.02`, `input_tp=-8.52`, `input_lra=5.60`, `input_thresh=-36.16`, and `target_offset=-0.03`.
7. Resampled to `44.1 kHz` and encoded as stereo PCM S16LE WAV.
8. Re-read the WAV with `ffprobe`, measured loudness and true peak with `ebur128`, and decoded the file to a null sink successfully.

No source file was overwritten or destructively edited.
