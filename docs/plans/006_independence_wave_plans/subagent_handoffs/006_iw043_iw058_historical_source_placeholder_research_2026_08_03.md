## Candidate ownership and rejection notes

The focused exact-name scan covered current Chaos Redux `common`, `gfx/leaders`, `interface`, and `localisation`, vanilla HOI4 `common/characters`, and approved workshop roots `1521695605`, `2265420196`, and `1458561226`. No exact owner was found for Sultan-Galiev, Tinchurin, Spasov, Joel E. Werda, or Haydo. The near-name `ASY_qambar_warda` records are a different person and are not an ownership match for Joel E. Werda/Warda.

Yusuf Salman Yusuf was rejected for the civic consumer because vanilla and Kaiserreich own `IRQ_yusuf_salman_yusuf`. Yacob Khoshaba Aboona was rejected for the levies consumer because the source page states all rights reserved. Daniel Ismail remained unresolved for 1936 continuity and exact group-photo identity. Farid Nuzha and Ishak Armale did not yield an attributable solo portrait. No generic, invented, modern reenactment, or wrong-era substitute was introduced.
