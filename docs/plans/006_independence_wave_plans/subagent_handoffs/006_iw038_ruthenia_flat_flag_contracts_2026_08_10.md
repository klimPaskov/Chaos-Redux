# IW-038 Ruthenia flat-flag and grounded-identity research handoff

## Handoff scope

This is a source and prompt contract for the Event 006 IW-038 Ruthenia package (`RUT`, state 73, Carpathian Ruthenia, 1936 release context). It covers the four requested runtime cosmetic basenames: `RUT_INDEPENDENCE_WAVE_CIVICX`, `RUT_INDEPENDENCE_WAVE_AGRARIANX`, `RUT_INDEPENDENCE_WAVE_SOCIALISTX`, and `RUT_INDEPENDENCE_WAVE_EMERGENCYX`.

No gameplay, GFX, flag, portrait, source-image, PNG, DDS, TGA, or manifest binary was edited or produced by this handoff. Character portraits remain owned by `chaosx_portrait_creator`; the leader table below is a research lead list only.

The package specification requires a researched period civic flag and forbids an invented flag being presented as an authentic 1936 state flag. The contracts below therefore call every generated design an alternate-history (`AH`) route synthesis unless the source is explicitly described as a historical design study or heraldic device. Do not localise any of these generated designs as an attested 1936 Ruthenian national flag.

## Four runtime cosmetic contracts

### `RUT_INDEPENDENCE_WAVE_CIVICX` — opening and constitutional autonomy

Use the shared 2:1:1 blue/white/maroon base and the compact 1920 Carpathian Ruthenian arms exactly as described above. This is the universal opening flag and the constitutional route flag; it is an AH civic synthesis grounded in a 1920 heraldic device plus later reported regional colour ordering, not a historical 1936 state flag.

The 82×52 prompt should leave a generous white margin around the shield so the red bear and blue/gold stripes are readable. The 41×26 version may reduce the shield to broad colour blocks. The 10×7 version may reduce the stripe half to three or four broad alternating bars while retaining a single red bear block.

Prompt additions: “lawful municipal civic flag, calm administrative identity, restrained heraldic shield, no political party badge.”

Prompt exclusions: no 1939 gold trident, no patriarchal cross, no green cloverleaf, no red star, no hammer and sickle, no black fascist marks, no religious text, no Ukrainian state flag claim, no modern 1990 regional emblem claim.

Date and sensitivity gate: the arms are safe as a pre-1936 regional device because the cited law dates to 30 March 1920. The colour ordering is not safe to describe as an interwar adoption and must remain “generated civic baseline” in documentation. The shield can be read by Ukrainians, Rusyns, Hungarians, Slovaks, and other communities of the interwar region; avoid exclusivist captions and avoid expanding the compact state into a maximal ethnic claim.

### `RUT_INDEPENDENCE_WAVE_AGRARIANX` — agrarian compact and restrained traditional option

Keep the shared CIVICX base and shield. Add one small solid green cloverleaf in the upper hoist portion of the blue band, no larger than 16% of flag width and 25% of flag height. Use a simple four-lobed cloverleaf with a short stem at 82×52, and a single rounded green mark at 41×26 or 10×7. This references the contemporary Czechoslovak agrarian party’s reported green/cloverleaf visual language while remaining explicitly an AH coalition synthesis; neither the IEU Ruthenian Agrarian party nor Andrii Brodii’s AZS has a sourced flag in this handoff.

The same runtime cosmetic may support the traditional-religious/Russophile branch by replacing the cloverleaf with one small gold patriarchal half-cross in the upper hoist blue band. This is a restrained use of the spring 1920 Gustav Friedrich design-study motif reported by FOTW `ua_ruth`; that study was not adopted, so the branch must be labelled “design-study-inspired AH route” and never “historical Ruthenian flag.” Do not combine both charges, and do not add a large generic Orthodox cross.

Prompt additions for agrarian: “village compact, agricultural self-government, one small green cloverleaf, no party lettering.” Prompt additions for the traditional option: “one small gold patriarchal half-cross as a 1920 design-study reference, not a church scene, no text.”

Prompt exclusions: no wheat sheaf unless a later asset-specific source is approved, no plough, no crown, no coat supporters, no rosary, no icon, no Cyrillic, no modern Rusyn UNPO flag claim, no 1939 trident, no red star, no hammer and sickle, no fascist or irredentist mark.

Date and sensitivity gate: the regional agrarian party source is dated August 1920–1924, the Czechoslovak host agrarian party operated through 1938, and the Friedrich half-cross study is spring 1920. The green cloverleaf must not be presented as a Ruthenian party emblem. The traditional option must not collapse Ukrainophile Christian People's Party figures into Russophile politics, and Brodii’s documented pro-Hungarian activity requires neutral route text and a sensitivity note.

### `RUT_INDEPENDENCE_WAVE_SOCIALISTX` — socialist councils

Keep the 2:1:1 blue/white/maroon base and compact shield. Overlay a deep-red hoist canton occupying the leftmost 1/6 of the upper blue band only, with one flat gold five-point star centred inside it at about 12% of flag width. The star must be a broad, low-detail silhouette that remains a single gold mark at 10×7. This is period socialist visual shorthand anchored by the existence of KSČ in the 1921–1938 First Czechoslovak Republic, not an attested Subcarpathian KSČ flag.

Prompt additions: “workers’ council route, one plain gold five-point star inside a small deep-red canton, retain the Carpathian shield, flat 1930s civic design.”

Prompt exclusions: no hammer and sickle, no Soviet coat of arms, no Stalin portrait, no Cyrillic slogan, no Comintern lettering, no Soviet state flag, no multiple stars, no industrial collage, no railway scene, no modern communist logo, no 1944–45 occupation imagery.

Date and sensitivity gate: KSČ and Red Trade Union activity are period-valid in 1936, but the star geometry is a declared AH synthesis because no exact local flag source was located. The route must not imply Soviet annexation or later state ownership. If Ivan Turyanytsia is selected, record his 1944–45 role in deportations of local Hungarian and German civilians as a sensitivity note and avoid celebratory retrospective wording.

### `RUT_INDEPENDENCE_WAVE_EMERGENCYX` — mountain emergency command

Use a strict-1936-safe emergency contract: retain the 2:1:1 base, enlarge the 1920 shield to about 44% of flag width, and add one narrow deep-red hoist stripe 1/8 of flag width running the full height. The enlarged shield and alert stripe are an AH emergency-command synthesis from the documented 1920 arms and the package’s sourced mountain-frontier/border-guard identity; no historical 1936 emergency flag is claimed.

The 1939 gold St Volodymyr trident is a source-supported late-branch option only, not part of the strict 1936 `EMERGENCYX` contract. If the parent deliberately date-gates a late alternate branch to 15 March 1939 or later, replace the deep-red hoist stripe with a small deep-navy canton and one solid gold three-pronged trident, using the FOTW `ua-zk` description and the IEU Carpatho-Ukraine law date. Record that back-projection explicitly in the manifest and localisation. Do not silently backdate the trident to the 1936 opening.

Prompt additions for the strict contract: “temporary mountain emergency command, enlarged bear-and-stripes shield, one narrow deep-red hoist stripe, disciplined civic flag, no military scene.” Prompt additions for a date-gated late branch: “small gold St Volodymyr trident in a deep-navy canton, historical 1939 motif intentionally used for a later alternate branch.”

Prompt exclusions: no Carpathian Sich runes, no Nazi or fascist symbols, no black sun, no skulls, no militarist eagle, no sword crossed with a rifle, no 1939 trident before the date gate, no Hungarian or Soviet state flag, no map, no camouflage, no waving fabric.

Date and sensitivity gate: the strict contract is the only safe visual for a 1936 start. The late trident branch is historically dated to 15 March 1939 and carries contemporary Ukrainian national symbolism; use it only when the route’s chronology and identity text make that counterfactual explicit. Mykhailo Kolodzinsky and Carpathian Sich imagery are not approved substitutes because their OUN/Sich association and documented ethnic violence make a celebratory emergency flag unsafe. Stepan Klochurak is the preferred grounded institutional bridge if a local emergency figure is needed.

### Institutional name leads

The most defensible named institutions for a 1936 provisional cabinet are the **Central Ruthenian People's Council**, **Ruthenian Agrarian party**, **Christian People's party**, **Prosvita society**, local municipal or district councils, and **Czechoslovak/Transcarpathian Red Trade Union** structures. These names are supported by the IEU biographies and party entry above, but no institutional flag or seal is approved by this handoff. Do not place a party name, initials, or Cyrillic text on any runtime flag.

## Blockers and parent decisions

1. There is no verified surviving 1936 Ruthenian national flag source in the reviewed references. CIVICX and all route variants must remain labelled AH generated designs.
2. The blue/white/maroon 2:1:1 ordering is a modern/uncertain report, and the blue/white/red 2:1:1 ordering is a later proposal. Neither may be described as an interwar adoption.
3. The 1920 arms are the strongest pre-1936 motif, but the cited descriptions disagree on the bear’s facing direction and on three versus seven stripes. Use the 30 March 1920 wording as the baseline, record the ambiguity, and simplify it at small sizes rather than asserting false precision.
4. `AGRARIANX` green cloverleaf is a host-country Czechoslovak agrarian reference, not a sourced Ruthenian party flag. Its traditional-religious option may use the non-adopted spring 1920 Friedrich half-cross only as a design-study-inspired AH variant.
5. `SOCIALISTX` has no located local flag source. The red-star canton is deliberately declared period shorthand and AH synthesis; do not add hammer-and-sickle or Soviet state heraldry without a new source and identity review.
6. `EMERGENCYX` strict mode is the only 1936-safe contract. The gold St Volodymyr trident is a 15 March 1939 Carpatho-Ukraine motif and requires an explicit late-route date gate; it must not appear on the opening flag.
7. All grounded leader portraits remain pending the portrait worker’s attributed source, exact-period image, rights determination, and HOI4 crop review. If no defensible source exists for the selected identity, block the leader rather than substituting a generic officeholder.
