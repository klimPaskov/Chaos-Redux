## Best source lead: King John Sigra

The named identity lead is King John Sigra, also recorded as Na II and King John of Kosrae.

The Rulers.org Micronesian traditional-polities roster is [https://rulers.org/micrtrad.html](https://rulers.org/micrtrad.html).

That roster records Kosrae as Na II, “King John,” with a 1910–1946 governing period, birth year 1875, and death year 1957.

The attributable image record is the Micronesian Seminar page [King John Sigra, Chief of Kosrae stands in front of the church](https://micsem.org/librarysearchphoto/king-john-sigra-chief-of-kosrae-stands-in-front-of-the-church/).

The Micronesian Seminar page identifies Francis X. Hezel S.J. as the poster and shows a 2019-03-29 posting date.

The exact indexed collection record is [Micronesian Seminar photo-search results, page 82](https://micsem.org/search-photos-results/page/82/).

That record reads `1936. [BM01009]. Black & White Photo, Photograph, Restricted. King John Sigra, Chief of Kosrae stands in front of the church.`

The associated collection is the Bishop Museum Collection at Lelu, Kosrae.

Some search snippets associated with the same record mention 1953, while the exact indexed record states 1936; the date discrepancy remains unresolved and requires confirmation from the collection owner.

The Micronesian Seminar footer states `© 2010-2019 all rights reserved`.

No explicit public-domain, Creative Commons, derivative, redistribution, or written mod-use permission was found in the reviewed record or page evidence.

No full-resolution BM01009 bytes were downloaded or decoded because the exact record is marked `Restricted` and the rights gate was not cleared.
