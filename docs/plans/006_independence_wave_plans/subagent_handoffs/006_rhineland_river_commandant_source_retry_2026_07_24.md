## Candidate choice

The selected candidate is Gustav-Adolf von Zangen (1892-1964), a real male German infantry officer who was alive and usable in the 1936 setting.

Zangen is a materially stronger role fit than the rejected Josef Harpe trial because he served in the 9th Rheinische Infanterie-Regiment Nr. 160 from 1911 and later held Westfront and 15th Army territorial commands that connect directly to a Rhenish river-corridor emergency command.

The Federal Archives record states that Zangen entered Wehrmacht service in 1936 and later held repeated troop commands, so the alternate RHI commandant role does not require a false claim that he historically held the fictional office.
