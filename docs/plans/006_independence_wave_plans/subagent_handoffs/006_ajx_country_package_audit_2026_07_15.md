Date: 2026-07-15

## Verdict

The bounded AJX country package **passes after one narrow cleanup fix**. Its
country shell, protected-host release contract, politics, characters, ideas,
decisions, focus branches, force mapping, AI, localisation, flags, portraits,
advisor dossiers, and distinct neutral-route focus art are coherent with the

the IW-010 runtime content attestation or SCN-008 preflight. The later FORM-04
operational re-audit and readiness promotion certify the family consumer, but
do not replace the separate IW-010 package-admission audit.

## Package audit findings

### Country identity, collision, and release safety

- `AJX` is registered once in Chaos Redux and has no tag definition collision
  in vanilla, the installed Workshop content tree, or another local mod.
- The dormant history recruits Friedrich Hoffmann and Karl Becker and supplies
  baseline laws without making AJX exist at game start.
- The frozen-plan executor temporarily masks unrelated historical cores,
  releases only the frozen package state set, transfers ownership and control,
  assigns state 42 as capital, and only then runs package setup.
- IW-010 reserves the Rhine-Saar group and releases only state 42. Shared host
  preparation selects a protected remnant state before transfer, and AJX's
  prepared proof independently requires the former host to exist, differ from
  AJX, and still own that protected state.
- The full Event 006 focus framework is loaded only for an Event 006-created
  package. AJX is such a dormant package, so no meaningful living-country focus
  tree is overwritten.

### Setup, validation, and lifecycle

- Central setup, final-validation, and cleanup dispatches all include the Saar
  adapter.
- Hidden synchronous event `chaosx.nr6.10` recruits the three AJX advisors
  before the five-character roster proof is evaluated.
- Setup establishes exact IW-010 identity, state/capital, politics, both public
  values, focus assignment, route availability and exclusions, power struggle,
  AI profile, FORM-04 carrier metadata, and P10 forces before publishing the
  package-complete result.
- Final validation runs only after active-country and network registries are
  live and proves both arrays, the network-member flag, and all prepared
  package invariants before durable origin history is committed.
- The exposed/balanced compact ideas are mutually maintained by the public
  value lifecycle, while route ideas are removed before one permanent identity
  is installed.

### Politics, routes, and characters

- All five popularity tables sum to 100: provisional, constitutional, labor,
  patron, and neutral commission.
- The four accepted permanent routes are present: constitutional municipal
  charter, labor councils, patron industrial mandate, and the distinct
  civilian Municipal Neutral Commission.
- Traditional restoration, emergency military government, and radical
  sovereignty are excluded during setup. The neutral route uses the shared
  route-lock transaction, and `neutral_commission` lies inside the shared valid
  government-route range.
- The neutral root is mutually exclusive with all six shared government roots;
  the route-lock trigger additionally prevents any later settlement from
  bypassing that exclusivity.
- Friedrich Hoffmann has the exact civilian leader roles needed by the accepted
  routes. Karl Becker is a skill-3 corps commander and is never promoted into a
  country-leader role, preserving the civilian-neutral design.
- The three advisors have distinct office identities, valid roles, centralized
  costs and AI weights, bounded visibility, correct on-add/on-remove state, and
  distinct dossier portraits.

### Decisions and balance

- Industrial Continuity and Neutrality Credibility both start at 30, stabilize
  at 60, and are clamped to the shared 0–100 range.
- The founding crisis lasts 480 days. A deterministic focus route reaches the
  threshold in 133 focus days: Keep the Mines Breathing (5 weeks), Charter the
  Coal and Rail Authority (7 weeks), and Screen the Industrial Security
  Companies (7 weeks) move the values from 30/30 to 70/60.
- The three primary serialized projects also fit inside the crisis window:
  mine/rail dispatch (75 days), factory security (120 days), and cross-border
  trade (180 days) total 375 days and move the values to 75/70 when completed
  from the opening state.
- Costs use the shared material and command-power effects and are paid on
  selection. Successful project effects resolve on removal; cancellation and
  timeout have explicit failure consequences. The mission cannot be manually
  selected and cancels cleanly on stability, package loss, or loss of capital
  control.
- The four route ideas are materially distinct and moderate. Patron and neutral
  identities include explicit political/economic tradeoffs rather than only
  bonuses.
- FORM-04 survey and congress decisions remain carrier actions. A later
  commit-readiness re-audit found and repaired the custom congress's direct-
  commit bypass: it now invokes shared preparation, grants AJX rewards only on
  `transaction_ready`, and leaves commitment to the shared proclamation gate.

### Focus tree

- AJX contributes ten bespoke focuses: four neutral-commission focuses and six
  coal, rail, security, trade, former-host-account, and delegation focuses.
- All ten focus IDs are unique in the file, and all ten absolute coordinates
  are unique within the AJX surface.
- Every prerequisite resolves, every branch is exact-package gated, and every
  completion reward calls a defined scripted effect.
- The Municipal Neutral Commission root consumes the distinct AJX focus icon;
  the remaining nodes intentionally reuse registered Event 006 semantic icons.

### Opening forces

- P10 matches the accepted force matrix: `industrial_security`, military
  tradition 50, reinforcement mask 1349, no inherited formations, no research
  sensitivity, and no navy or air inheritance.
- Mask 1349 resolves to exactly the five accepted reinforcement pathways:
  integrate militias, secure depots, mobilize factory/rail guards, accept
  foreign arms missions, and reinforce capital/border defense.
- The setup proof requires all five positive flags, rejects every other
  reinforcement pathway, verifies the expected pathway count, and verifies the
  no-navy/no-air contract.
- Force application derives division count, template, equipment, trains,
  trucks, fuel, and stockpiles from the shared dynamic force system and state,
  host, chaos, legitimacy, patron, and network inputs.

### AI

- Five exact-origin, setup-gated AI records cover industrial survival, founding
  restraint, severe former-host threat, civic routes, and the patron route.
- Threat response reads the dynamic former-host ledger; it does not assume a
  German or French host.
- AI strategy values are centralized. Targetless `avoid_starting_wars` uses
  negative additive restraint (`-200` during founding and `-400` after
  settlement); severe host threat removes the restraint while adding emergency
  army and bunker priorities.

### Localisation, assets, and documentation

- All 113 expected AJX country, character, advisor, trait, idea, category,
  decision, focus, description, and tooltip localisation keys resolve.
- Every AJX portrait and focus sprite reference resolves to a registered live
  texture. The leader and commander portraits, all three advisor dossiers, and

- Asset validation reports all four newly produced assets valid: three 65×67

- Visual review confirms the three advisors are visually distinct fictional

- The old implementation handoff's missing-advisor and missing-focus-art items
  are superseded by the dated AJX asset-completion handoff and the live updated
  package documentation.

## Defect fixed during audit

`independence_wave_setup_iw_010_saar` set
`independence_wave_radical_sovereignty_route_excluded`, but the AJX package
cleanup did not clear it. The comparable IW-008 and IW-009 cleanups do clear the
same exclusion flag. Leaving it set would leak AJX route availability state
across a generation reset.

The audit added the matching `clr_country_flag` to
`independence_wave_cleanup_iw_010_saar`. A post-fix comparison found no AJX-owned
setup flag without a cleanup counterpart.
