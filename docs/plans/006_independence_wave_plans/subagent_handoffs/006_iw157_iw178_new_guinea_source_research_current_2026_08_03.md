## IW-157 WPG: Fakfak / Sekar / Kokas lead

### Named male identity candidate

The strongest period-fit lead found is the KITLV archival photograph titled “Gezicht op Kokas met de Radja Singgaray van Sekar te Nieuw-Guinea”.

- Source page: [Wikimedia Commons file record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:KITLV_A497_-_Gezicht_op_Kokas_met_de_Radja_Singgaray_van_Sekar_te_Nieuw-Guinea,_KITLV_377510.tiff).
- Archive record: [KITLV/Leiden persistent item 745990](https://hdl.handle.net/1887.1/item:745990).
- Original source URL: `https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/13/KITLV_A497_-_Gezicht_op_Kokas_met_de_Radja_Singgaray_van_Sekar_te_Nieuw-Guinea%2C_KITLV_377510.tiff`.
- Archive/shelfmark: KITLV 377510, Leiden Digital Collections, anonymous photographer/creator.
- Captioned identity: “Radja Singgaray van Sekar” and place “Kokas”, with Commons geographic metadata for Fakfak, Indonesia, Papua Barat.
- Date/era: Commons metadata gives `[Between 1933 and 1936]`, which touches the 1936 scenario baseline but is not an exact capture date.
- Rights: the Commons record states [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- Candidate disposition: `source_ready_candidate`, not runtime-approved. “Radja” is a male ruling title in the caption, but the exact face-to-name identity and the photographer attribution still require the mandatory source-locked crop and independent identity audit.

This candidate is materially better than the earlier Silas Papare leads because it is a named local Fakfak/Sekar ruler, it is period-matched to the baseline, and it is geographically specific rather than pan-Papuan. The dated range and anonymous-creator metadata must remain explicit in any later manifest.

## IW-178 PNG: Milne Bay / Dogura / Gedulalara lead

### Named male identity and district evidence

The best archival lead is the Australian War Memorial’s public-domain Milne Bay photograph containing the person captioned Edward Guise.

- Source page: [Australian War Memorial C238294](https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/C238294).
- Direct image source: `https://s3-ap-southeast-2.amazonaws.com/awm-media/collection/014649/screen/6220405.JPG`.
- Archive accession/maker: AWM accession 014649, photographer Norman Brown, place made Milne Bay, Papua, 14 April 1943, black-and-white photograph.
- Caption/identity: the AWM description identifies the six people left-to-right as Maiogaru (Gimuleia) Taulebona, Edward Guise, John Pilacapio, Pilimomi, Ilai Dixon, and Banaba; Edward Guise is second from the left.
- Rights: the AWM record states “Copyright expired - public domain” and links the [Public Domain Mark](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/).
- Candidate disposition: `needs_user_review`, not a cleared Sir John Guise portrait. The AWM caption’s name “Edward Guise” conflicts with the [Australian Dictionary of Biography entry for Sir John Douglas Guise](https://adb.anu.edu.au/biography/guise-sir-john-15980/text35290), which identifies Edward Guise as John’s father. The AWM C33999 page separately says “Edward Guise, later Sir John Guise”, so the archive caption/ADB relationship must be reconciled before this image can be used as a leader identity.

The second AWM page is useful as a cross-check of the unresolved naming issue.

- Source page: [Australian War Memorial C33999](https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/C33999).
- Direct image source: `https://s3-ap-southeast-2.amazonaws.com/awm-media/collection/014657/screen/3885062.JPG`.
- Archive accession/maker/date: AWM accession 014657, Norman Brown, Milne Bay, 14 April 1943, black-and-white photograph, public domain under the AWM record.
- The description says the decorated people included “Edward Guise, later Sir John Guise”. This wording cannot by itself override the ADB’s explicit parentage statement, so it remains a naming-conflict source and not an approved identity master.

The authoritative textual district/biographical cross-reference is the ADB article.

- Source: [Sir John Douglas Guise (1914–1991), Australian Dictionary of Biography](https://adb.anu.edu.au/biography/guise-sir-john-15980/text35290).
- It places John Guise’s birth on 29 August 1914 at Gedulalara, near Dogura, Milne Bay, Papua, and records his early-1942 ANGAU service and later Milne Bay political representation.
- This proves a named male Milne Bay/Gedulalara/Dogura identity and adult age in 1936, but it does not prove that he held a country-leader office in the 1936 baseline.
- The ADB article has no portrait and its text is used only for identity, date, and district evidence; it is not an image source.

### PNG community and motif context

For the Motu/Koitabu/Hanuabada alternative, the strongest primary source found is C. G. Seligman’s 1910 ethnography.

- Scan: [Internet Archive, *The Melanesians of British New Guinea*](https://archive.org/details/melanesiansofbri00seli).
- OCR: `https://archive.org/download/melanesiansofbri00seli/melanesiansofbri00seli_djvu.txt`.
- Author/date: C. G. Seligman, 1910.
- The text names Hanuabada near Port Moresby, the Motu and Koita peoples, the annual Hiri trading voyage, and composite lakatoi craft used in sago-trading expeditions.
- The scan is an early public-domain-era work, but the Internet Archive record does not state a license field; rights basis must be verified before any derived art is released.
- Disposition: named community and motif evidence only. Hiri/lakatoi is not an attested historic national or community flag, and the unnamed Hanuabada men in the scan cannot supply a real leader identity.
