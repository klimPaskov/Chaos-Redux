## Ownership and transfer checks

Exact and variant-name searches across current Chaos Redux `common/characters`, `history/countries`, `gfx/leaders`, `interface`, and `localisation` found no custom character, recruitment, portrait, leader, commander, operative, or officeholder owner for Musa Dzhalil, Shamil Usmanov, Ahmet Zeki Velidi Togan, Mar Benyamin Shimun, Mar Eshai Shimun, Naum Faiq, or Agha Petros.
Vanilla has `TUR_zeki_velidi_togan` as an idea/localisation token only, not a character or portrait owner.
Vanilla `common/characters/ASY.txt` owns `ASY_shimun_eshai` and `ASY_benjamin_arsanis`, recruited by `history/countries/ASY - Assyria.txt`; their portraits are generic vanilla leader sprites (`GFX_portrait_ASY_shimun_eshai` uses `portrait_europe_generic_6.dds` and `ASY_benjamin_arsanis` uses `portrait_europe_generic_1.dds`).
No transfer contract or source-rights grant exists for those vanilla characters, so their generic portrait ownership cannot be treated as a grounded replacement for Mar Eshai or Mar Benyamin.
