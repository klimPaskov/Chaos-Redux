## Validity, dominance, starvation, repetition, and external factors

No finding of actual starvation, dominance, rank reversal, repetition, or exploit was proven for decision/mission scores because the adapters provide no selection probabilities and the complete in-scenario candidate set and external factors are absent. DM60's factors and DM47's threshold are conditional score modifiers, not probability multipliers. The rival leave-contract source has a high=25 factor at patron-capture gate 45; this is a conditional score jump worth including in a future full score race, but the empty-state probe did not resolve it and does not establish dominance. The main file's AI source inventory found 85 weighted candidates across 13 decision and 72 mission candidates, with the broad inspect reporting poolComplete=false and 89 required inputs; this is not a complete scenario race. The package AI-strategy registry inspection found no ai_strategy_factor adapter surfaces in that file, but campaign-wide external strategy factors were not declared or evaluated.

Source gates reviewed include DM45 retry activation, DM46 all-pillars visibility and proposal flags, DM47 phase/member/standing gates, DM60 target/ground/authority checks, DM61 victim and route checks, DM62 defensive route and external target checks, and DM44 mutual-defense target checks. Because MCP did not receive their scenario state, these source gates cannot support a claim that a weighted candidate is live, dead, or properly ranked in a campaign.
