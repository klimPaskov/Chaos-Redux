## Decision

Both signature packages should be expanded. The accepted Event 006 design promises modern political bridges, distinct institutions, route play, local military problems, formable ambitions, and achievement proof. The bounded base tranche now admits the exact CHU and ASY package content through the shared runtime registry, with package-owned mechanics, routes, localisation, and reviewed visual assets. The exact compatibility adapter prevents active Event 006 IW-043/IW-058 carriers from using the vanilla `form_idel_uralic_republic`, `neo_assyrian_empire_decision`, and `neo_mesopotamia_decision` shortcuts while preserving ordinary and Event 005 behavior. The signature extensions remain held because their FORM-12/13/18 adapter-attestation and achievement proof writers are absent. Leaving those extensions open is intentional and fail-closed.

This addendum is the only open improvement-loop layer for IW-043 and IW-058. No earlier addendum for either package exists. The earlier FORM-48 plan was implemented in its bounded tranche and remains separately readiness-blocked pending promotion and independent closure. It is not duplicated here.

The accepted design should remain narrow in three ways:

- No new scripted GUI is justified. Use the existing Event 006 mechanics header and normal package decision categories.
- No new super-event is justified. These packages feed existing Event 006 evolution, league, danger, formable, report, and news surfaces.
- No package-specific technology tree is justified. The installed agent package has no Technology Tree Viewer. This is a recorded tooling limitation, not a request to create another technology system.

## IW-043 Volga Bulgaria package

### Playable promise

The player begins as a modern emergency congress in Kazan, not as a restored medieval court. Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash, Mari, Udmurt, labor, municipal, rail, industrial, Muslim, secular, and other resident delegates disagree about what the new authority is for. The player must make the Volga navigable, keep the guard under law, settle religion and civic jurisdiction, and write a rights compact before choosing one of two achievement-bearing outcomes:

- a modern Volga federation built around equal civic and national chambers
- a modern Volga Bulgaria proclamation that draws legitimacy from Bolgar history and Islam while remaining constitutional and protecting non-Tatar and non-Muslim residents

An emergency river-security path exists as a failure-control and survival route. It is militarily useful but cannot write either achievement route flag until civilian law is restored and one of the two constitutional outcomes is completed.

### Territorial design

Opening territory:

- required anchor `249 Kazan`
- optional compact state `256 Cheboksary` only when the allocator safely reserves it in the same release transaction

The package may not seize a protected host capital. If `256` cannot be included, the package remains playable from `249` and uses a treaty and ferry mission to obtain Cheboksary participation later. Achievement completion still requires own and control of both `249` and `256`, matching the accepted trigger.

No opening cores or direct ownership are granted outside the committed compact. Bashkir, Mari, Udmurt, Mordvin, Komi, and other Volga-Ural anchors enter through member consent, accession, league transformation, or an actual staged military settlement under FORM-12 or FORM-13.

## Balance contract

### Reward discipline

- All authored balance rewards and penalties use round increments, normally 5, 10, 15, 20, or 25.
- A focus must produce a visible change in route, mechanic, decision access, institution, or meaningful material capacity. No node exists only to grant a small amount of political power, stability, war support, or experience.
- The package has no repeatable political-power purchase store.
- Command-power costs never exceed 60 across concurrent package commitments.
- Easy missions run at least 90 days. Normal package missions use 120 or 150 days. Institutional and settlement missions use 180 days.
- Industry rewards repair or redirect capacity over time. They do not create factories in states the actor does not own or control.
- Force rewards use paid conversion, training, or ordinary recruitment. They do not spawn free duplicate divisions.
- Formables stage cores and integration. They do not grant blanket cores at proclamation.
- External support can create patron leverage. It cannot grant costless equipment and recognition without a political or economic obligation.

### Comparative route balance

IW-043:

- Federal route has the best recognition, accession consent, and FORM-12 access. It pays in slower central authority and higher administrative cost.
- Restoration route has the best historical legitimacy, religious settlement, and FORM-13 access. It pays in harder external recognition and must spend more to prove resident equality.
- Emergency route has the best short-term anchor and river defense. It loses recognition, rights progress, and constitutional capstone access until normalized.

IW-058:

- Church-civic route has the best church and diaspora cohesion and a strong external network. It pays in slower secular administration and must clearly limit church competence through civic law.
- Civic route has the best municipal administration, public service, and broad diplomatic recognition. It pays in more difficult church settlement and local legitimacy work.
- Guardianship has the best short-term defense. It pays in cohesion, recognition, civilian control, formable access, and achievement delay.
- FORM-18 provides the strongest regional structure but requires member consent and long integration.
- Sovereign autonomy is smaller and safer but yields less federal capacity and relies on a durable treaty partner.

### Balance scenarios

The parent must run scripted or save-based scenario checks at low, medium, and high chaos where applicable:

- minimum compact with only the required anchor
- optional compact state present
- weak former host, matched former host, and strong former host
- human actor and AI actor
- no compatible formable members, exact minimum members, and surplus members
- no external guarantor, one viable guarantor, and competing guarantors
- early defensive war, peaceful host settlement, and failed host crisis
- federal or restoration route versus emergency survival route

The packages are high-chaos only in the accepted registry. Lower-chaos tests exist to prove the allocator keeps them unavailable, not to balance a hidden release.
