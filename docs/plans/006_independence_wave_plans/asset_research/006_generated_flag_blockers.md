# Event 006 generated flag production blockers

## Decision

The Mediterranean and Danube production tranche cleared three flag assets through researched official-ImageGen workflows: `IW-018` Sardinia (`ARX`) and `IW-021` Trieste (`ICX`) are handed off, while `IW-019` Sicily (`ASX`) has a completed 1848 S.015 triplet that remains locked to the constitutional-independence route pending route-ownership review. `IW-024` Banat (`AXX`) and `IW-027` Thrace (`BAX`) now have retained flat alternate-history civic ladders with researched motif provenance. The accepted asset research still forbids generic motifs, invented designs presented as historical, sacred or community-owned symbols treated as decoration, and modern flags backdated without evidence.

The complete evidence, official ImageGen sources, processed ladders, manifests, validation results, and contact sheets are under `docs/assets/006_independence_wave/mediterranean_danube_flag_sources_2026_07_15/` and `docs/assets/006_independence_wave/mediterranean_danube_generated_flags_2026_07_15/`. These asset results do not by themselves mark a country package content-ready.

The shared gate requires a final public name and political identity; a source-mode decision; an asset-specific archive, museum, official, installed, or rights-cleared source; creator or institution, date, license, and attribution; a statement identifying the historical function of the source; and an approved identity review for Groups D and E. Once cleared, every generated flag still requires a source PNG, 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7 TGA outputs, triplet readability review, route/ideology labels, and a manifest statement that calls generated work generated.

## Unblocking handoff
