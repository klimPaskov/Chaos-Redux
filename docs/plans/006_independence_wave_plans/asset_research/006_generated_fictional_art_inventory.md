## Country-level generated art

### Flags

1. Forty-four generated period civic baselines for Group B in 006_package_asset_coverage.md.
2. Restrained generated civic baselines for Group C whenever an attested standard is dynasty-owned, route-owned, disputed, or absent.
3. Exact-community civic designs for Group D only after the identity review described in the source ledger. These are alternate-history civic designs and must be labelled that way.
4. Signature designs for Volga Bulgaria, Assyria, Mesopotamia, Asante route variants, Edo Benin, Oyo, Sokoto, Aceh non-historical routes, prewar Mon, and Mapuche routes.
5. Distinct ideology and route variants for registered tags where the installed base does not represent the selected route.
6. Formable flags and league emblems from ASSET-046, after the formable identity and final registered tag are fixed.

Every final flag needs:

- Source PNG or layered source.
- Normal, medium, and small TGA outputs at 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7.
- Manual readability checks at all three sizes.
- Identity, route, and ideology label.
- A manifest statement saying whether the design is historical, reconstructed, generated civic, or generated route art.

Generated designs must not contain readable generated text. Sacred, ceremonial, royal, or community-owned motifs require the exact ownership note from the source ledger.

### Portraits

1. Provisional assemblies, cabinets, congresses, municipal councils, royal councils, regencies, and councils of houses specified in the package coverage ledger.
2. Fictional one-person leaders introduced by later route content. Each requires a gender-aligned regional name pool and a generated portrait; no real person's likeness may be used.
3. Institutional portrait sets for unresolved royal or claimant packages. These are deliberately not generic headshots: the scene must visually communicate the institution, such as a council table, regency chamber, assembly dais, or cabinet group.
4. Distinct institutional portraits for sensitive packages. A fictional individual must not visually stand in for a broad living identity.

Each final leader portrait is 156x210 DDS and needs source art, processed PNG, final DDS, identity note, name-pool note, and generated-content disclosure in the manifest.

## Super-event scene briefs

### ASSET-005: The League of New States

Create a period-authentic alternate-history congress, centered on a charter table and a crowded gallery of delegates from visibly distinct new governments. The composition must read as institution-building rather than a generic diplomatic banquet. No readable generated text, no modern conference equipment, and no flag wall made of duplicated or placeholder emblems.

### ASSET-006: Every Border a Casus Belli

Create a tense military-political assembly where several revisionist delegations coordinate mobilization. The threat should come from people, documents, guarded corridors, mobilization boards, and competing insignia rather than a red-tinted map. Avoid modern uniforms, fantasy weapons, or a single villainous ethnicity.

Audio is owned by the separate super-event audio research route.

## Frame-animation deliverables

Each of ASSET-040 through ASSET-043 requires:

- A frame plan describing the semantic change in every phase.
- Separate generated, sourced, or hand-drawn source frames.
- Static fallback.
- Numbered processed PNG frames.
- Final frame sheet or registered sequence as implementation requires.
- Contact sheet.
- GIF or video preview.
- Manifest with frame provenance.
- GFX or GUI handoff naming the intended sprite, frame count, frame order, frame duration, loop behavior, and static fallback.

The following do not count as final frame animation: moving, scaling, rotating, warping, blurring, recoloring, filtering, or fading one still image without independently designed frame content.

## Production stop conditions

Stop and return a gap instead of generating when:

- The public name or exact community is still a working label.
- A real person's portrait is requested without a rights-cleared source.
- A historical flag's ownership or date is disputed.
- A sacred or ceremonial symbol is being treated as generic decoration.
- A modern flag is being backdated without evidence.
- The animation brief would only transform one still image.
- The final filename, sprite name, or route identity is not registered.

No fallback or placeholder art is authorized by this inventory.
