# Event 006 package asset coverage

## Purpose and binding scope

This ledger covers every Event 006 country package that can enter the automatic pool, a high-chaos pool, or a scenario-only pool in the accepted candidate registry.

- Covered packages: 167.
- Automatic-pool-ready: 11.
- Automatic-pool-ready if the registered tag is not living: 44.
- Automatic-pool-ready if a unique state exists: 77.
- High-chaos-only: 32.
- Scenario-variant-only: 3.
- Deliberately excluded from this production tranche: 9 formable-or-route-only packages and 30 specific-community-variant-only packages.

The five groups below are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive. Their counts are 63 + 44 + 35 + 15 + 10 = 167.

## Asset status vocabulary

- Existing base reuse: retain the installed flag triplets; do not copy, repaint, or silently replace them. Historical or route-specific variants still require separate provenance.
- Generated civic baseline: create a visibly fictional alternate-history civic flag from researched period motifs. The manifest must never call it a historical flag.
- Attested-symbol study: begin with a documented royal, civic, regional, or institutional standard. Record who owned the symbol. If no safely attributable state flag exists, create a restrained civic baseline instead of laundering an uncertain reconstruction into history.
- Exact-community review: use only symbols and institutions belonging to the named community. No pan-identity invention, sacred-symbol repurposing, or fictional individual presented as representative of a broad living people.
- Signature dossier: follow the accepted country dossier and the package-specific source notes in the companion source ledger.

All final flag families require normal, medium, and small 32-bit uncompressed TGA files at 82x52, 41x26, and 10x7, with bottom-left origin. All sourced portraits require a provenance record and a 156x210 final DDS. Generated institutional portraits and fictional leaders are owned by the generated-art route.

## Group D: exact-community or institution review — 15

These packages require an institutional council or a sourced leader from the exact named community. A fictional personal leader cannot stand in for a broad real identity. The flag must belong to the exact named community or institution; sacred and ceremonial symbols are not generic state decoration.

IW-060 Kurdistan; IW-066 Dagestan; IW-082 Palestine; IW-115 Somaliland sultanate federation; IW-134 Punjab; IW-135 Sikh state; IW-137 Balochistan; IW-138 Pashtunistan; IW-139 Kashmir; IW-164 Arakan or Rakhine; IW-186 Cherokee state; IW-188 Lakota state; IW-189 Dine state; IW-198 Aymara state; IW-200 Guarani state.

Binding distinctions:

- Kurdistan may use a documented interwar political flag only for the movement that owned it; it is not a universal Kurdish design by default.
- Dagestan requires a multiethnic civic treatment. A generic Islamic or single-people flag is not acceptable.
- The Palestinian national movement used Arab Revolt-derived colors in the interwar period, but the route and date still control the exact ordering and attribution.
- A Somaliland federation cannot universalize one clan, dynasty, or sultanate.
- Punjab and a Sikh state are mutually exclusive package identities. A Punjab civic flag cannot be converted into a Sikh flag by adding a sacred emblem, and a religious standard cannot be treated as a neutral Punjabi state flag.
- Balochistan, Pashtunistan, and Kashmir begin compact. Cross-border or maximal symbols belong to later route content.
- Arakan or Rakhine naming and symbols depend on the chosen political settlement.
- The Cherokee, Lakota, and Dine packages must use exact nation or council institutions. No pan-Indigenous flag is permitted.
- The modern Navajo Nation flag dates to 1968 and is not a 1936 baseline.
- The familiar seven-color Wiphala configuration has a contested modern political history and cannot be presented as an uncontested 1936 Aymara national flag.
- The Guarani package cannot universalize dispersed communities across modern borders.

## Production gates

No package may receive final art until its row has:

1. A final public name and exact political identity.
2. A source-mode decision from this ledger.
3. An asset-specific source URL or local installed path.
4. Creator or institution, date, license, and attribution text.
5. A statement explaining whether the source is a flag, standard, seal, emblem, portrait, reconstruction, or contextual motif.
6. An identity review for Groups D and E.
7. A static institutional portrait plan whenever a real-person source remains unresolved.

An unresolved real-person portrait is not permission to use a generic person. The accepted opening government in those cases is the specified institutional council or regency.
