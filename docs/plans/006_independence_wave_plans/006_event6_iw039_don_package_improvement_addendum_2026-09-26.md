### Government, rights, and campaign loop

Propose a **Millerovo Host Charter** as one visible objective rather than a copied two-value Kuban pressure board.
Its 360-day founding mission starts only after successful IW-039 setup and asks the player to keep 245 owned, controlled and capitalized; finish the paid rail/remount survey; publish a civilian land-and-service hearing; and ratify one government settlement.
Loss of 245, invalidation of the Event 006 package, or the deadline closes the mission with a named failure idea and stops FORM-11 eligibility; a temporary 238 loss removes only that optional project's benefit and cannot silently move the capital.
Success removes the temporary logistics/legitimacy weakness and opens one controlled reinforcement cycle, but does not award free cores, state transfers, federation membership, or a second OOB.
Propose initial `Millerovo Emergency Stores` as a weakness to mounted supply and training and `Unratified Service Mandate` as a weakness to political stability or mobilization; the paid depot receipt should retire the former and charter ratification should retire the latter, with one success-stage idea such as `Chartered Don Service` that reflects the selected government.
The government choices should be mutually exclusive and written as a public-rights settlement, not an ideology skin: an elected host assembly with non-host district petition rights; a constitutional district compact placing the mounted command under elected civilian oversight; or an emergency host staff with a dated review and higher immediate military capacity but weaker recognition and federation consent.
Retain the vanilla DON `30 democratic / 30 neutrality / 20 communism / 20 fascism` popularity only as the carrier baseline until a paid government choice explicitly changes politics; the package should not turn a provisional railhead into a full ideological mandate on release.
For balance review, propose the elected assembly as +5 stability and no immediate war-support gain, the constitutional compact as +10 stability after its longer ratification and a small command-power cost, and emergency staff as +5 war support with -5 stability plus a dated civilian-review obligation; all are bounded one-time country-value effects through shared constants, not accepted numeric rewards.
The fourth “traditional” registry direction needs a sourced, non-duplicative office and rights rule before it becomes a selectable route; a generic royal claimant or an automatic Sidorin ataman settlement is not authorized.
The proposal gives the military route real short-term force utility but makes unresolved district representation a cost to network and FORM-11 consent, while the civic routes trade time and political power for durable civilian legitimacy.
