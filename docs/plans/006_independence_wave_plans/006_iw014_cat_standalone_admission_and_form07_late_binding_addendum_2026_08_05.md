## Decision

Admit `IW-014` Catalonia as a complete standalone Event 006 country package after a bounded source and audit pass.

Keep `FORM-07` Iberian Federation registered as CAT's future regional ambition, but keep every player-facing formable surface closed until the complete existing FORM-07 readiness contract passes.

This is a country-package admission correction.

It is not a FORM-07 completion claim.

It does not authorize a placeholder formable identity, provisional flag, generated historical portrait, generic commander, weakened member corridor, or fallback country package.

## Why this is the next tranche

The current whole-event audit records 15 attested packages across 14 compatible reservation groups, 10 adapter-only packages, and 178 unattested selectable packages.

Nine of the ten adapter-only packages still depend on grounded portraits, identity research, historical flag review, complete rosters, or incomplete formable member packages.

CAT is the one adapter-only carrier whose country package can use an installed vanilla tag, installed vanilla history, installed vanilla flags, the installed vanilla `CAT_lluis_companys` character, an implemented p14 force mapping, a complete Event 006 focus assignment, CAT-specific decisions, CAT-specific ideas, CAT-specific AI, localisation, and generation-safe cleanup.

The remaining CAT blocker is an architectural coupling between base-country validation and an optional later formable transaction.

That coupling makes a missing alternate-history X identity prevent a fully authored Catalan country package from entering the allocator.

The accepted candidate registry describes the regional ambition as a Mediterranean republic league or an Iberian federation.

It does not make successful formation of FORM-07 a prerequisite for Catalonia to exist or play.

Separating country admission from formable readiness therefore preserves the accepted country promise while keeping the unresolved formable honest.

After a successful admission, the expected boundary is 16 attested packages, 15 compatible reservation groups, and 177 unattested selectable packages.

The 14-country automatic band remains supported.

The 20-country automatic band remains fail-closed and still needs at least four more mutually compatible attested packages.
