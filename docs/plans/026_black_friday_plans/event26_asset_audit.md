## Family and reference checks

The report family was compared with the canonical `event_art/report/contact_sheet.png` and individual Vanilla report references, then checked against the installed `interface/eventpictures.gfx` report sprite and `interface/eventwindow.gui` report slot. The processed card is `210x176`, has transparent corners, and its alpha bounding box is `(6, 7, 206, 172)`; native-size and contact-sheet review show the complete tilted card and soft shadow inside the canvas with no clipped hard edge.

The idea family was compared with the canonical `icons/ideas/contact_sheet.png`, `idea_generic_constitutional_guarantee.png`, `idea_generic_deal_with_the_devil.png`, and `generic_air_bonus.png`, then checked against the installed Vanilla `interface/ideas.gfx` idea sprite pattern. The 64x64 output has transparent unused canvas and remains a single readable gear, crate, and price-tag silhouette.

The achievement family was compared with the canonical `icons/achievements/contact_sheet.png`, individual Vanilla completed/grey/not-eligible states, and the supplied achievement templates. The completed, grey, and not-eligible outputs are separate state layers over the correct templates; the idea icon was not reused. The two opaque checkerboard-like generated sources are preserved unchanged as rejected provenance, while the final grey state is deterministic grayscale of the completed layer and the final not-eligible state is that grey layer plus the unchanged canonical red-X overlay.

Every final DDS was decoded individually at native size. All five files use the strict one-level legacy header (`DDS `, header `124`, `32-bit BGRA`, flags `65`, fourCC `0`, masks `0x00FF0000/0x0000FF00/0x000000FF/0xFF000000`, `DDSCAPS_TEXTURE`), exact length, and expected dimensions. Runtime DDS bytes equal their staged copies. The achievement processor returned `AUDIT OK 026_black_friday_five_departments: strict 64x64 BGRA triplet and source-layer equality verified`.

The 2026-09-02 consumer-path audit rechecked all three achievement registrations and installed the staged triplet at the exact `gfx/achievements/` paths consumed by `interface/026_black_friday.gfx`; each runtime SHA-256 matches its staged DDS byte-for-byte.

The review evidence is retained in `docs/assets/026_black_friday/contact_sheets/black_friday_report_contact_sheet.png`, `docs/assets/026_black_friday/contact_sheets/icon_package_contact_sheet.png`, `docs/assets/026_black_friday/notes/black_friday_report_dds_roundtrip.png`, and `docs/assets/026_black_friday/decoded/`. The persistent asset manifest and GFX handoff remain the source records while Event 26 is incomplete.

## Scope exclusions and blockers

The accepted Event 26 asset rows do not include a news image, decision category icon or picture, focus icon, flag, portrait, animated sprite, scripted-GUI panel, super-event image, 3D model, counter, or audio cue. No such asset was inferred or substituted. No runtime reference points into `docs/assets/`.

The local source, processing, DDS, path, family, and visual checks are complete. The installed HOI4 MCP event render remains a structural partial route and no live HOI4 session was launched, so exact in-game texture loading and final popup presentation remain open live-validation evidence rather than an asset-production defect. The event stays disabled until the broader Event 26 cost registry, audits, and acceptance gates are complete.
