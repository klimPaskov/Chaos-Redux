# Event 26 cost-surface registry

This is the completion-gating registry for Black Friday and the shared universal cost source.

The registry is deliberately conservative: an owner-controlled custom cost is not called covered until its ordinary quote, affordability, payment, display, refund, AI, and save-state paths use the shared contract.

## Discovery method

The source review covered the complete Event 026 specification package, the offline Paradox wiki, the installed vanilla documentation, all Chaos Redux `common` scripts, and the installed vanilla `common` scripts that expose cost modifiers or purchase definitions.

The current Chaos Redux decision scan found 2,224 `custom_cost_trigger` consumer occurrences and 2,225 `custom_cost_text` references across 81 decision files. The sole aggregate offset is the non-purchase requirement disclosure at `common/decisions/020_black_plague_rat_decisions.txt:923`, which is text-only; the nuclear-bomb owner has 63 paired trigger and text declarations, and `016_brilliant_scientist_technology_actions.txt` adds eight paired rows. The Event 35, Event 32, Event 31, Event 29, Event 25, and Event 21 owner rows are recorded at their current worktree counts in Appendix A and the exact inventory.

The current Chaos Redux decision scan found 1,328 native `cost =` declarations across 62 decision files: 1,033 declarations not written as the literal `cost = 0` and 295 literal zero/free sentinels.

The vanilla modifier documentation exposes 39 technology-category cost fields, 195 equipment design cost types, 312 equipment-module design cost types, and 157 unit design cost types. The technology-category fields were audited but are deliberately not installed because they change research cost/time, which Part 4 excludes from the sale.

The rows below are the source-of-truth dispositions for those discoveries; Appendix A is the complete custom-cost owner summary, `event26_cost_surface_custom_inventory.md` is its exact line-level inventory, Appendix B is the complete native decision-owner file inventory, and `event26_cost_surface_native_inventory.md` is its exact line-level inventory.
