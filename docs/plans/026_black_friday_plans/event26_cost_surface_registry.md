# Event 26 cost-surface registry

This is the completion-gating registry for Black Friday and the shared universal cost source.

The registry is deliberately conservative: an owner-controlled custom cost is not called covered until its ordinary quote, affordability, payment, display, refund, AI, and save-state paths use the shared contract.

Decision declaration discovery was refreshed against the worktree on 2026-10-03, with exact per-file hashes and comment exclusions in the two linked inventories.
Logical transaction/component coverage remains incomplete, and neither the discovery snapshot nor the bounded adapter rows constitute a final implementation-commit audit.

## Discovery method

The source review uses the complete Event 026 specification package, the offline Paradox wiki, and installed Vanilla documentation.
The current exhaustive declaration scan covers both decision roots, while semantic decomposition and the complete audit of event options, GUI actions, focus commitments, raids, and shared debit helpers remain unresolved.

The current mod scan records 2,342 active `custom_cost_trigger` declarations and 2,343 active `custom_cost_text` declarations across 100 owner files.
The sole aggregate offset is the text-only terminal-readiness requirement at `common/decisions/020_black_plague_rat_decisions.txt:923`.
Vanilla has 368 active paired declarations across 38 owners, with three commented pairs excluded from the raw 371 matches.

The current mod scan records 1,316 active native `cost =` declarations across 55 owner files, including 343 literal zero and 973 nonliteral-zero declarations.
Of those 973 declarations, 833 are symbolic or unresolved and 140 are numeric nonzero, so the nonliteral-zero subtotal is not a positive-purchase count.
Vanilla has 2,903 active native declarations across 86 owner files, with 52 commented matches excluded.

The vanilla modifier documentation exposes 39 technology-category cost fields, 195 equipment design cost types, 312 equipment-module design cost types, and 157 unit design cost types. The technology-category fields were audited but are deliberately not installed because they change research cost/time, which Part 4 excludes from the sale.

The rows below are the source-of-truth dispositions for those discoveries; Appendix A is the complete custom-cost owner summary, `event26_cost_surface_custom_inventory.md` is its exact line-level inventory, Appendix B is the complete native decision-owner file inventory, and `event26_cost_surface_native_inventory.md` is its exact line-level inventory.
