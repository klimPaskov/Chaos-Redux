# Protective Aid payment and shipment contract

Disposition: implemented in source after parent review and the read-only decision audit; native decision scenario and visual evidence remain unresolved.

The acceptance basis is the user's approved CBRN overhaul and the later explicit choice to reserve timed protection crates when selected and refund unused crates on cancellation. Event 026's accepted specification discounts voluntary purchase costs while preserving rewards and physical outputs, and states that the sale does not add equipment. Protective Aid therefore keeps its full calculated respirator shipment as a conserved transfer. Only its 25 Political Power administrative payment uses the Event 026 sale ratio: the 50% quote is 13 and the 25% Evolution I quote is 7 after upward whole-unit rounding.

The `cbrn_deploy_protective_aid` decision targets one occupied state. The selected country and state must satisfy the existing program, civilian-factory, emergency, occupation, and stock gates. At selection, the state quote is capped to the donor's available mask stock, the five real mask models are reserved oldest first, and the current Political Power fee is paid once. A failed fee or receipt append returns the reserved models and leaves no active delivery project. A successful start retains the existing 21-day timer and two-factory native commitment. On completion, the existing state delivery consumes the needed reserved models and returns unused models to the stored donor; only a proven delivery can settle and credit the sale-time PP receipt to Event 026. On cancellation or invalid target, the state escrow refunds unused models, the Political Power fee remains spent, and no completed-purchase achievement family is credited.

The five persistent state receipts are `cbrn_external_mask_escrow_reconditioned`, `_basic`, `_improved`, `_advanced`, and `_sealed`, with the stored donor and active flag owned by `common/scripted_effects/cbrn_protection_effects.txt`.
