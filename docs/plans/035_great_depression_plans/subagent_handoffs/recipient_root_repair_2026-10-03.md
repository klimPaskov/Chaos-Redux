## Defect

The former registry loop entered each affected country and invoked continuity, mission capacity, and the weekly pulse directly.

The normal weighted selector similarly entered its selected country and called the start/deepen API directly while ROOT remained the entry sender.
This contradicted the exact selected-country contract when sender and recipient differed.
