## Guards

`great_depression_call_event34_snapshot_is_valid` binds the call to the actual frozen country, source episode, collapse receipt, evolution floor, final and peak Overheating, landing state, reserves, projects, region and shock counts, repeat history, dangerous duration, and collapse day.
The country ID is an explicit public input and is cleared with the other call inputs.

`great_depression_call_relapse_context_is_valid` requires the recipient's completed recovery, scar memory, active recovery watch, actual prior completed episode, actual completion proof, and an elapsed time inside the tuned recovery-watch window.
An active crisis or transition rejects this caller class.

`great_depression_call_worldwide_context_is_valid` requires the active global lifecycle in Coordination, the recipient's registered pressure row and episode, exact pressure stage and conversion receipt, and the real global origin target.

`great_depression_call_contagion_context_is_valid` binds the national receipt to its link receipt and requires an unresolved conversion-ready exposure.
`great_depression_call_contagion_rows_are_proven` checks the recipient's incoming source row and the source's outgoing target row against the same country pair, active source episode, receipt, and stage.
Both searches use the existing sparse registry cap.
They do not scan the world or depend on the sender's ROOT.
