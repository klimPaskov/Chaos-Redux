# Event 001 supplemental generation prompts

The accepted prompts below produced generated fictional staged period photographs through the built-in image_gen tool. These are documentary scene assets, not portrayals of identified people. The outputs are full-canvas opaque photo sources; the report-card processor supplies the finished sepia treatment, transparent card margins, tilt, and soft shadow.

## communist_state_control

ImageGen output: exec-370452cb-4f36-4672-9b00-4e70d64cc761.png (1536x1024, RGB). Generation fit because the event uses fictional alternate-history state-control imagery and does not require a real person or archive-specific object.

> Use case: historical-scene
> Asset type: HOI4 report-event photograph source, Event 001 Communism Spread; final consumer is a 210x176 sepia tilted documentary card with transparent outer margins.
> Primary request: Create a distinct fictional, period-authentic documentary press photograph showing tightened state control in an Eastern European industrial city around 1937.
> Scene/backdrop: Interior checkpoint at the entrance to a municipal office after dusk, brick walls, frosted glass door, coat hooks, practical desk lamp, modest period furnishings.
> Subject: A plain-uniformed sentry seen from behind at a wooden checkpoint desk, two distant civilians waiting near the doorway, blank papers turned face-down; the checkpoint and controlled entrance are the clear subject.
> Style/medium: Candid 1930s press photography, believable optical softness and restrained film grain, natural monochrome-neutral source tones for later sepia processing.
> Composition/framing: Medium-wide horizontal 3:2 photograph, eye-level, readable major shapes that survive a tight crop to 210x176; no close faces.
> Lighting/mood: Dim practical tungsten light, guarded and tense, realistic exposure.
> Constraints: Fictional staged scene; no real or recognizable person, no portrait, no distinctive political insignia, no readable text or numbers, no watermark.
> Avoid: Modern props, modern uniforms, readable papers or signs, flags, UI, poster layouts, cinematic color grading, gore, generic glamour faces.
