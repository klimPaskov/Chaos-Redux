# chaosx_icon_artist handoff — Event 039 Murder Mystery

## Result

Produced original visual assets for the accepted Event 039 operations kit, Assassin Forces profiles, route spirits/ideas, Veiled Compact emblem, decision categories/actions, missions, technologies, and achievements. The detailed ledger below covers every one of the 119 final runtime DDS files.

The icon artist did not edit GFX or GUI definitions, gameplay scripts, localisation, decisions, focus trees, or event files. Runtime bindings and live in-game presentation remain unverified.

## Canonical references and inspected consumer families

The only reference root used was `C:/Users/klimp/OneDrive/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/chaos_redux/.agents/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/assets/vanilla_reference`.

Before generation, the matching contact sheets and selected individual references were inspected for ideas, national focus, decisions, missions, decision categories, achievements, technologies, special projects, officer-corps spirits, factions, land/air/naval counters, division-template emblems, and equipment technology art.

Read-only vanilla/project consumer definitions inspected included `interface/ideas.gfx`, `interface/decisions.gfx`, `interface/goals.gfx`, `interface/Technologies.gfx`, `interface/factions/factions.gfx`, `interface/subuniticons.gfx`, and the project custom-counter precedent `interface/012_africa_strange_force_counters.gfx`.

Relevant dimensions were matched to the inspected families: ideas 60x68; decisions and missions 33x32; small decision categories 52x40; standard technology/profile icons 64x64; operations-kit technology art 133x55; faction logo 200x100 with two 100x100 frames plus 32x32 miniature; achievements 64x64 full-canvas triplets.
