## Strategic-depth check

The design has a concrete allocation problem without adding an unrelated punishment. A player's new offensive reach competes with the need for home protection, while every foreign service receives its own expansion. Timed specialties create short-term priorities, a full tree continues to benefit, and ordinary operation resources still constrain commitments.

The meaningful later outcomes belong to native espionage and accepted connected events. Adding a political-control meter, rival agency rivalry, covert world government, or mandatory mini-focus tree would shift the event away from the user's Low-member idea. Those additions were not needed to make the prescribed rewards playable.
