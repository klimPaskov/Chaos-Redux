## Capacity, markers, targets, and cleanup

`sov_nuclear_bombs_controlled_reactor_count` counts registered physical sites currently owned and controlled by SOV.
`sov_nuclear_bombs_reactor_count` is the effective count consumed by existing production quote, entitlement checks, and maintenance capacity calculations.
It equals the physical controlled count capped at `sov_nuclear_bombs_reactor_entitlement_count`.
The physical count is not capped, so recapturing a historical reactor after replacing it preserves the extra physical facility without creating extra production entitlement.

State flag `sov_nuclear_bombs_production_reactor_counted` prevents duplicate credits/debits.
State flag `sov_nuclear_bombs_production_reactor_lost` prevents duplicate Readiness penalties while a site remains lost.
Recapture clears the lost marker and allows a later distinct loss episode to charge once.
Readiness is clamped to zero through the existing zero and value-maximum constants.
Recapture does not refund Readiness.

Historical `sov_nuclear_bombs_production_reactor_site`, `sov_nuclear_bombs_reactor_event_owned`, the original production-owner pointer, and the vanilla reactor building remain intact.
These historical markers are intentionally persistent, allowing later recapture callbacks to recognize the site.
There are no new event targets, persistent scope pointers, temporary output variables, cleanup jobs, or periodic scans.
All reactor bookkeeping is isolated from device-accounting helpers and device buckets.
Reactor ownership cannot grant a device.
