## Result in brief

No named scenario has a completed MCP probability evaluation available for this handoff. Therefore all selection probabilities, effective MTTH distributions, rankings, dominance/starvation, rank reversals, repetition rates, and exploit/snowball risks remain unresolved. Do not read any source `ai_will_do` or `ai_chance` score as a probability, and do not infer normalized random-list odds by hand.

The source logic does express the intended Evolution IV severe-first-use gate: AI major first use without a confirmed enemy detonation requires `sov_nuclear_bombs_event_severe_first_use_gate`, whose conjunction includes Evolution IV, Chaos at least 1000, war, at least three strategic losses, capital/front/reserve crisis flags, a confirmed enemy use or verified launch preparation, no moratorium or prior Soviet major first use, a valid delivery route and crew, readiness at least 75, integrity at least 20, and a nuclear-major target. This is source-only evidence; MCP did not prove the runtime outcome. A confirmed-enemy-use path is separately controlled by the major-exchange world gate and must not be conflated with first use.

One source-level AI-score risk remains for owner review: `sov_nuclear_bombs_give_final_authorization` has base `0.10`, applies `constant:sov_nuclear_bombs_ai.severe_loss_factor` (currently `1.50`) when `sov_nuclear_bombs_event_can_authorize_first_use` is true, and applies a zero factor only below Evolution III. Absence of the severe gate does not itself zero that base term. This is a raw willingness-score observation, not proof the decision is eligible or that a major strike can execute. Test this exact context before deciding whether the AI score needs a strict severe-gate zero for unconfirmed first use.

The MTTH constants are currently 180/240/300/360 days for Evolutions I–IV. The probability scenario spec describes design centers of 120 days for Evolution I, 120 days for Evolution II, and 180 days for Evolution III. This is a source/spec discrepancy, not an MCP timing result; modifiers and gates have not been evaluated into effective timing.

## Source-only findings and recommendations

- `common/scripted_effects/023_sov_nuclear_bombs_event_effects.txt` defines one four-entry test-outcome `random_list` (success/partial/failure/accident) at line 798. Base weights are constants 60/20/12/8; source-local flags and readiness/integrity factors alter weight variables before each is clamped to at least one. These are weights, not probabilities. MCP confirms the four-entry candidate pool is structurally complete, but does not provide values for the four required variables, so no outcome distribution is established.
- The event-option adapter uses a proportional-categorical interpretation for a complete local pool but documents HOI4's d100 selection path and flags sub-one-percent outcomes. Scores across different events must not be combined. Event `.2`, command-board `.100`, coercion `.120`, and other event option pools need separate complete candidate lists and scope-valid scenarios.
- MTTH source modifiers include public/crisis acceleration, war, breakaway/collapse, major exchange/confirmed enemy use, restraint/stable-command modifiers, low-integrity delay, and moratorium delay. The caller schedules an evolution-check event from a computed timing variable, but the adapter did not recognize these reusable definitions/caller as a timing surface. Effective MTTH, cumulative chance, medians, and timing drift remain unresolved.
- `common/ai_strategy/` and `common/ai_templates/` searches found no direct `sov_nuclear_bombs`-named strategy surface. This is source-search evidence only; indirect/external factors and target-selection weights remain unresolved. No separate target-selection weighting candidate or complete target pool was proven by MCP.
- Requested follow-up: first obtain current-revision inspect coverage for the decision and mission source; then evaluate the named scenarios as separate event-local candidate pools using only documented scenario keys and complete actor/target/scope inputs. Specifically test whether an otherwise eligible AI final-authorization decision can retain its base score when a nuclear-major first-use target lacks the severe gate, while preserving legal retaliation/limited-use routes. Add or demonstrate the requested high-Chaos/no-Evolution-IV case; do not substitute the Chaos-900/Evolution-III case. Reconcile the MTTH design-center discrepancy in the accepted spec/source. Only then sweep thresholds/rank reversals, render the analyses, and compare before/after if a weighted patch is made.
- Do not describe raw `ai_will_do`/`ai_chance` as click probability; do not claim dominance, starvation, rank reversal, repetition, or safe snowball behavior until complete-pool scenario evidence exists. Decision/mission selection cadence and repeat pressure were not quantified.
