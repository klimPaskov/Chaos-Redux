# Event 033 GUI font repair, 2026-10-04

Disposition: implemented.

## Change

`interface/033_acid_rain.gui` used `hoi_10`, `hoi_12mbs` and `hoi_14mbs`, which neither vanilla `interface/core.gfx` nor the mod registers.
Line heights of the registered replacements come from the vanilla `.fnt` files: `Arial12` 14, `hoi_16mbs` 16.

| Old font | Replacement | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hoi_14mbs` (titles, card identities, coverage and preparedness values; boxes 18-20 px high) | `hoi_16mbs` | Registered heading font whose 16 px line fits every box. |
| `hoi_12mbs` (card body lines, region rows, global ledger; boxes 14-36 px high) | `Arial12` | Registered compact font whose 14 px line fits the 14 px region rows. |
| `hoi_10` (notes, legends, national strip; boxes 14-18 px high) | `Arial12` | Smallest registered readable font. |
| `buttonFont = "hoi_12mbs"` on the three 123x34 buttons | `hoi_16mbs` | Vanilla button font; "World map", "REFRESH" and "Motion: Off" fit. |
