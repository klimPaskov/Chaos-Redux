## Governing role correction

The canonical four-role taxonomy is the taxonomy in the Event 012 super-event prompt and final-text handoff:

1. Africa is one: the first completed continent-wide political settlement.

## Role 1 — Africa is one

### Exact final choice

Commission an original, instrumental, through-composed cue for Event 012. Working production reference: `Event 012 Role 1 — continental institution`. The credited work title is to be supplied by the commissioned creator and frozen in the final rights ledger; it is not a new player-facing super-event title.

Source references for the brief are the role contract in [`africa_super_event_prompt.md`](../../specs/012_africa_specs/prompts/africa_super_event_prompt.md), the completed-settlement logic in [`012_africa_spec_part_1_core_progression.md`](../../specs/012_africa_specs/specs/012_africa_spec_part_1_core_progression.md), and the route-aware final text in [`012_africa_super_event_final_text_research_handoff.md`](012_africa_super_event_final_text_research_handoff.md). There is no external recording URL because this is a new master.

### Composition and production brief

- Final duration and edit window: compose and deliver exactly `110.000 s`; use the complete master from `00:00.000` through `01:50.000`.
- Tempo and motion: a clear pulse in roughly the `76-88 BPM` range, with melodic development and counterpoint rather than a cinematic drone or unchanging ostinato.
- `00:00-00:12`: one restrained, intelligible motif establishes political gravity without a victory fanfare.
- `00:12-00:38`: independent lines enter and remain individually audible. The music should suggest institutions negotiating a shared order, not anonymous mass unanimity.
- `00:38-01:18`: the lines reach a shared theme with civic breadth. Avoid a military march, coronation cadence, trailer riser, or apocalyptic swell.
- `01:18-01:41`: broaden the harmony and orchestration while retaining at least one independent counterline, so federal, republican, royal-council, popular, military, confederal, and Covenant descriptions can all remain plausible.
- `01:41-01:50`: an earned, open cadence and natural tail. Emotional resolution may be strong, but it must not imply that every political dispute has disappeared.
- Voices and language: no lyrics, chant, anthem quotation, spoken words, or language-dependent vocal line. Wordless human voice is also excluded from this first brief so that no route inherits an unintended liturgical identity.
- Instrumentation: the commissioned African composer, producer, or composer-led ensemble chooses the palette. The project must not prescribe a generic percussion list, a pseudo-traditional sound, or a collage of supposedly representative regional instruments. Any culturally specific instrument must be used through a performer or consultant who can identify its context and approve the credit.
- Fade and loop: no loop; retain no more than `0.250 s` of pre-roll, apply only a `0.100 s` safety fade-in if required, and use the written natural ending. A maximum `3.000 s` tail fade is allowed only if documented in the edit record.
- Source-master delivery: stereo `44,100 Hz`, at least `24-bit` PCM WAV, plus stems or a lossless multitrack archive sufficient to correct a click or rebalance without recomposing.

### Rights and attribution contract

Acceptance requires a signed agreement covering the composition, arrangement, performance, recording master, performer/producer neighbouring rights, and every sample. It must grant Chaos Redux and its redistributors a worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free right to reproduce, edit, convert, synchronize, distribute, mirror, and sublicense the cue with the mod in present and future media. Where assignment or waiver is ineffective, the agreement must supply an equivalent licence and a non-assertion covenant. It must disclose collecting-society obligations and forbid uncleared third-party samples or stock-library terms that prevent redistribution of the isolated game asset.

The agreement must freeze the exact credit line: work title, composer, arranger if any, performers, producer/engineer, recording date, commissioning note, and required licence notice. Courtesy credit remains in the catalogue and Event 012 audio documentation even if the creator uses CC0. Creative Commons explains that a licensor must control all necessary rights before applying CC0; CC0 itself does not clear unrelated contributors' rights: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en>.

### Cultural and gameplay rationale

The role announces a political settlement, not an essential African sound. Composer-led original work allows institutional plurality to be audible without using one existing national or liberation anthem as a continent-wide identity. The converging but still independent lines support the federal, republican, council, popular, command, confederal, and Covenant variants while the event description names the actual route.

### Hard blocker

Audio ID `58` must not be registered or wired until the signed rights package, credited creator and performers, lossless source master, final `110.000 s` edit, and uniqueness evidence all exist. If original production cannot be secured, stop and request a new audio decision. Do not substitute the rejected anthem recording or any generic “African” library cue.

## Rejected candidates and why they remain rejected

### Liberation-hymn recording for role 1

The earlier provisional candidate was Enoch Sontonga's *Nkosi Sikelel' iAfrika*, performed at the White House in 1994 by a United States military band.

- Frozen source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File:%22Nkosi_Sikelel%27_iAfrika%22_performed_at_the_White_House_in_1994.oga&oldid=999959845>
- The composition is old enough to be public domain, but the source page's recording basis is a United States federal-government public-domain claim, not a clear worldwide recording dedication.
- The preserved provenance runs through a White House programme copied to YouTube rather than a lossless institutional master with a worldwide rights statement.
- More importantly, one South African liberation hymn would be made to represent every federal, republican, council, popular, command, confederal, and high-chaos settlement. That collapses political and cultural plurality into one inherited symbol and creates the pan-African essentialism this package must avoid.

Disposition: rejected, not a fallback. A later user decision to revive it would require both a worldwide recording-rights resolution and explicit cultural approval.

### Schubert recording for role 4

The earlier provisional candidate was Schubert's Symphony No. 8, II. *Andante con moto*, performed by the Fulda Symphonic Orchestra under Simon Schindler.

- Frozen source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File:Schubert%27s_8th_Symphony,_2nd_movement_Andante_con_moto_in_E_major.ogg&oldid=1189797410>
- The source uses EFF Open Audio License 1.0 terms requiring preservation of author attribution, licence terms, and the original file's attribution information. The Event 012 dual WAV pipeline needs an explicit, documented method for carrying those obligations outside Vorbis tags.
- Even if that operational issue were resolved, this one existing movement does not deliberately bridge peaceful union, federation, submission, and victory. Original production provides a stronger route-neutral terminal identity.

Disposition: rejected, not a fallback. Do not silently revive it if original production is delayed.
