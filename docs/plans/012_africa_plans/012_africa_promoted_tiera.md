# Event 012 Promoted Tier A High-Chaos Packages

Event 012 exposes six additive high-chaos country packages on the existing Event 006 niche carriers. The carrier tags remain EBX Aro Confederacy, EHX Ankole, DPX Fante, EEX Bunyoro, DFX Kabylia, and DHX Tripolitania outside the reveal. The six promoted Tier A visual and runtime packages, including their male fictional portraits and eight strange-unit consumers, are installed on those existing carriers. No new country tag or cosmetic carrier is created here.

The reveal trigger requires the exact carrier to own and control its surveyed state, to use the surveyed state as its capital, and to have no global claim flag from an earlier promoted package. Ancient Hosts must own and control both Tripolitania states and capital 448. If any condition is absent, the reveal decision is unavailable; there is no static or generic fallback. The state scope transfer in the reveal effect is therefore a guarded ownership handoff, not an unconditional annexation.

## Package treatment

Pan uses toolmaker council, forest republic, or war troop hierarchy politics; its mechanic advances stolen machinery and forest intelligence; its League role is a protected forest charter; refusal creates a forest resistance and optional rival bloc; post-settlement play exports engineering and reconnaissance.

Gorilla Kingdom uses silverback monarchy, guardian council, or sanctuary command politics; its mechanic is a guardian oath with supply and strength tradeoffs; its League role is a guarded charter; refusal is isolationist; post-settlement play is sanctuary defence.

The Green uses biomandate, collective, or canopy covenant politics; its mechanic grows protected biomes and ecological wrath; its League role can be autonomous federal or chartered with extraction limits; refusal is rewilding resistance; post-settlement play restores the biome.

Living Rivers uses basin council, flood law, or river guardianship politics; its mechanic advances flood bargaining and seasonal flow; its League role protects waterways and bank sovereignty; refusal is a riverbank resistance; post-settlement play builds basin works.

Stoneborn uses stone crown, ruin guardians, or silent conquest politics; its mechanic builds stone resonance and monument defence; its League role is an associate or heritage protector; refusal is a silent heritage bloc; post-settlement play restores monuments.

Ancient Hosts uses relic court, memory council, or oath host politics; its mechanic reconnects relic memory; its League role is a relic charter or autonomy; refusal is an unquiet host; post-settlement play completes the relic network.

## High-chaos actor operations

The promoted category now exposes four targeted actor operations and one distinct current-host great-power war. The four actor targets must be current-generation human members with a cooperative relationship, settled representation, and explicit integration consent. A nonhuman carrier cannot select a nonhuman country or a merely adjacent state, and target locks prevent stale duplicate entries while outcomes are recorded.

| Decision | Owning actor | Exact outcome owner | Target consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `africa_promoted_tiera_nonhuman_rampage` | Pan, Gorilla Kingdom, Living Rivers, or Ancient Hosts | Full outcome writes `africa_achievement_nonhuman_rampage`; partial outcome remains an authored target assault without opening the DQ | Full outcome makes the member resistant and lowers stability, war support, and confidence; partial outcome applies the smaller pressure band |
| `africa_promoted_tiera_forest_actor_rampage` | The Green | Accepted Event 013 call writes `africa_achievement_forest_actor_rampage`; civilian deaths remain Event 013's `africa_achievement_disaster_weaponised_against_civilians` owner | Event 013 receives a selected country, hostile-actor caller ID 12, regional severity, single sequence, meaningful report/news, normal aftermath, and all three caller proofs |
| `africa_promoted_tiera_stoneborn_rights_violation` | Stoneborn | Full or partial adjudication writes `africa_achievement_stoneborn_rights_violation` and the existing rights-preservation DQ | Full outcome clears representation and consent and moves the member to resistance; partial outcome records a smaller authored withdrawal |
| `africa_promoted_tiera_stoneborn_human_member_war` | Stoneborn | Only an engine-confirmed declaration writes `africa_achievement_stoneborn_human_member_war` | The selected member receives a war-target receipt and the Stoneborn declares `annex_everything` war |
| `africa_promoted_tiera_host_great_power_war` | Current Event 012 host | Confirmed host declaration snapshots a major enemy and opens the separate Row 35 lifecycle | The current host declares `annex_everything` war on a major nonhuman-excluded enemy; only the later capitulation/control receipt can continue Row 35 |

The four actor decisions charge political power through their normal decision cost and a command-power reserve through the custom-cost contract. The distinct host war has its own political-power, command-power, 365-day re-enable, one-use, and target-lock contract. Mature mechanic or host stages use a higher command-power band. Actor operations share a 180-day cooldown and 90-day target lock. Full and partial outcomes have distinct stability, war-support, and confidence bands; failed nonhuman or rights outcomes leave the target unharmed and do not set an achievement disqualifier.

The Forest operation calls the Event 013 public API directly from the Green actor scope. It saves the selected member as `natural_disaster_call_target_country`, supplies caller type `hostile_actor`, caller event ID `constant:africa_natural_disaster.caller_event_id`, random family/group, selected-country target mode, regional severity, one sequence, meaningful report/news, normal aftermath, family chain, Event 013 history logging, and cost/cooldown/target-legitimacy proof values. Event 013 remains authoritative for acceptance, rejection reason, sequence ID, impact scheduling, and the later civilian-death weaponisation witness.

The Event 012 disease adapter already owns accepted deliberate ordinary-pathogen seed receipts. This promoted carrier surface does not invent a second disease release, irreversible outcome, or terminal outcome. There is no valid Ancient Hosts-to-member operation that can pass the native disease actor/source contract without rewriting the disease system; `africa_achievement_irreversible_disease_outcome` and `africa_achievement_terminal_disease_outcome` remain zero-writer structural invariants and are not completion checks. No ordinary failure, cleanup, battlefield dissemination, or active-flag proxy may be promoted into either outcome.

The achievement completion predicates also omit four zero-writer negative flags whose approved owners make their paths structurally unavailable: `africa_achievement_human_caricature_used` under the four-family rights-preserving nonhuman contract, `africa_achievement_elephant_used_against_unthreatened_african` under the protected defensive-war target gate, and the two disease outcomes above under the native adapter contract. Removing those dead checks does not create a success proxy; the exact authored positive and disqualifier owners remain the only reachable receipts.

The Stoneborn human-member war remains a Row 40 disqualifier only. Row 35 has a separate host-owned lifecycle: `africa_promoted_tiera_host_great_power_war` snapshots `is_major = yes` only after the current host's engine-confirmed declaration against a major nonhuman-excluded enemy, and stores a dedicated pending target. `africa_promoted_tiera_record_nonhuman_great_power_war_victory` is available only after that target capitulates with every core controlled by the current major host, while four genuine formation families and at least four distinct current-generation actors in `global.africa_achievement_nonhuman_rights_actors` have preserved rights and obligations. The receipt also requires the existing rights-violation and extermination DQs to be clear. It calls the existing `africa_achievement_record_milestone` owner with `nonhuman_great_power_war_won`, then clears the pending flag, target snapshot, and global target. A reveal, Stoneborn member war, generic occupation, non-major target, or partial operation cannot write Row 35.

Row 40 also has a generic declaration owner in the Event 012 `on_war_relation_added` callback. The callback checks both attacker/defender orientations, routes through the current host, requires a registered active Stoneborn actor and a cooperative current-generation human member with settled representation and integration consent, and explicitly excludes actual nonhuman and special chaos countries on the member side. It writes the exact Stoneborn/human-member DQ once, so ordinary or scripted diplomacy cannot bypass the bespoke decision writer and Stoneborn/nonhuman wars cannot satisfy the row.
