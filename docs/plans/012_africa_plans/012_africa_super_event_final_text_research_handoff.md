## Source-of-truth lock

### Exactly four roles

This handoff preserves exactly these presentation roles:

1. Africa is one, for the first completed continent-wide political settlement.
2. Scramble response, for the first globally visible outside-power response to that continental political fact.
3. Continental wars, for the first terminal war between valid continent-scale actors.
4. The World, for the terminal state in which only one eligible world-scale political identity remains.

There is no route-by-route super-event grid, host-country grid, extra evolution super-event, or generic fallback presentation. Route and outcome differences belong in scripted description clauses and actor fields.

### Evolution interpretation

The text contract follows the accepted evolution structure:

| State | Logged tier | Narrative function relevant to these roles |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Baseline | `4` | The continental project begins with a host, partners, proof obligations, and a grounded constitutional route. |
| Evolution I | `4` | Wider interventions, regional consolidation, and visible foreign reaction become possible. |
| Evolution II | `5` | Continental machinery, integration, economy, identity, regnal flavour, and stronger sanctions deepen. |
| Evolution III | `6` | Africa becomes a world pole, gains external anti-colonial actions, bases, islands, sponsorship, post-unity Scramble pressure, and continent-scale rivals. |
| Post-unification world order | no new logged evolution | Terminal wars, rival resolution, and The World operate as a world-order layer after the accepted evolution chain. |

Africa is one may fire from a grounded completed settlement without Evolution III. An ordinary globally visible Scramble response may also occur before the terminal world-order layer. Continental wars and The World require Evolution III or an explicitly approved equivalent state that satisfies the same world-pole and rival-registry guarantees. A requested Evolution IV must remain a post-unification world-order layer rather than a fourth logged tier-six evolution.

## Role 1: Africa is one

### Trigger and narrative role

Fire once for the first valid continent actor that completes every required regional acceptance condition, records a settled constitutional identity, and opens the public institution that represents that settlement. Territorial occupation alone is insufficient. A temporary coalition, unresolved military occupation, or numeric integration threshold without the route's required settlement proof must not fire it.

The event announces achieved political scale and institutional standing. It does not claim that every dispute, autonomy question, cost, or coercive residue has disappeared.

### Final title, quote, and remark

- Final title: `THE CONTINENT TAKES ITS SEAT`
- Final quotation: use the exact selected Garvey fragment in the handoff result table.
- Final attribution: `Marcus Garvey, "Africa for the Africans," Negro World, 22 April 1922`
- Final button remark: `The continent will answer for itself.`

The title frames continental formation as entry into political agency and international standing. It works for an assembly, republic, crown compact, people's congress, command structure, confederation, or public Covenant because the dynamic description names the actual institution.

The selected quotation appears continuously in the 1923 collected edition scan. The [Wikisource scan page](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Marcus_Garvey_-_Philosophy_and_Opinions_of_Marcus_Garvey_%282009_printing%29.pdf/42) supplies the exact wording. The [PBS American Experience transcription](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/americanexperience/features/garvey-his-own-words/) identifies the original article as `Africa for the Africans` in the *Negro World* of 22 April 1922. The [University of Ghana record for the 1923 edition](https://ugspace.ug.edu.gh/items/2d5e716a-d723-48cd-8227-3c769cf99b1a) supplies independent edition metadata. The [Wikimedia Commons source-file record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Marcus_Garvey_-_Philosophy_and_Opinions_of_Marcus_Garvey_%282009_printing%29.pdf) identifies the underlying 1923 publication and records a United States public-domain basis for pre-1931 publication.

Text confidence is high. United States public-domain confidence is high for the 1922 article and 1923 edition. Wider-jurisdiction confidence is medium-high because Garvey died in 1940, which places the underlying authorial text beyond life plus seventy years, while the final ledger should still freeze the exact scan and distinguish the underlying text from later transcription or compilation work.

Cultural approval remains required. Garvey's wider political programme is specific and contested. The selected fragment avoids the article's racial-imperial language, yet using his words for every constitutional outcome still communicates an association. The parent or user must approve that association before localisation is wired.

## Role 2: Scramble response

### Trigger and narrative role

Fire once when a named outside power or coalition turns the continental settlement into a globally visible recognition crisis, sanctions regime, ultimatum, expedition plan, or open war. The response must cross the specification's public threshold. Private AI preference, a minor opinion modifier, an unissued plan, or routine diplomatic hostility is insufficient.

The African actor remains the central subject. The event records how it answers a renewed attempt at containment, partition, or coerced hierarchy. It must not frame the old Scramble as neutral adventure.

### Final title, quote, and remark

- Final title: `THE MAPMAKERS RETURN`
- Final quotation: use the exact selected Article 34 fragment in the handoff result table.
- Final attribution: `General Act of the Conference at Berlin, Article 34, 26 February 1885`
- Final button remark: `This time, Africa answers.`

The title recalls the political practice of partition without giving the outside powers ownership of the scene. The description names both the response and Africa's action.

The selected fragment appears in Article 34 of this [English transcription of the General Act](https://www.internationalwaterlaw.org/documents/regionaldocs/1885GeneralActBerlinConference.pdf). The [German Federal Foreign Office archive](https://archiv.diplo.de/arc-en/the-political-archive/general-act-2684414) confirms that no African representatives were consulted and that Articles 34 and 35 established the effective-occupation framework. The [French treaty text hosted by the University of Perpignan](https://mjp.univ-perp.fr/traites/1885berlin.htm) supplies the original-language Article 34 for comparison.

The underlying 1885 official treaty text is public domain by age and official-document character with high confidence. The exact English wording has medium confidence because the accessible transcription does not name its translator or edition. Before localisation ships, compare it word for word with a contemporaneous official English printing and record that edition. Until that comparison is done, the quotation remains approval-blocked. No substitute is selected by default.

## Role 3: Continental wars

### Trigger and narrative role

Fire once when two valid continent-scale actors enter the first terminal campaign war and both actors, their public identities, and the war's terminal character are known. A regional war, intervention against a minor state, colonial expedition, or war involving only one continent-scale actor is insufficient.

The event must name the rivals and the political issue that crossed into war. It must show armies and institutions at continental scale while retaining the people, ports, corridors, and member polities that bear the first consequences.

### Final title, quote, and remark

- Final title: `CONTINENTS UNDER ARMS`
- Final quotation: use the exact selected Clausewitz sentence in the handoff result table.
- Final attribution: `Carl von Clausewitz, On War, Book I, Chapter I, J. J. Graham translation`
- Final button remark: `The arguments have reached the front.`

The [Project Gutenberg edition of On War](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1946/1946-h/1946-h.htm) reproduces the exact wording as the heading of the relevant section and identifies the 1874 J. J. Project Gutenberg's own transcription terms and jurisdiction notice should remain in the research ledger even though the underlying text is public domain.

The quotation explains that the war grows out of rival political projects. It does not prejudge the winner, prescribe a route, or reduce the event to spectacle.

## Role 4: The World

### Trigger and narrative role

Fire once when one eligible continent-scale sovereignty remains and every registered terminal rival has been resolved through a recorded union, federation, submission, negotiated settlement, or victory. All terminal-war locks, pending rival states, and incompatible world-end competitions must be closed. Ordinary conquest score, temporary occupation, an empty rival pointer, or the disappearance of one tag is insufficient.

The event announces the end of plural eligible world orders. It does not announce the extinction of people, cultures, member polities, or public institutions.

### Final title, quote, and remark

- Final title: `ONE WORLD REMAINS`
- Final quotation: use the exact selected Shelley sentence in the handoff result table.
- Final attribution: `Percy Bysshe Shelley, "Ozymandias," first published 1818`
- Final button remark: `The last border is an archive.`

The [Wikisource transcription of the 1914 Hutchinson edition](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Complete_Poetical_Works_of_Percy_Bysshe_Shelley_%28ed._Hutchinson%2C_1914%29/Ozymandias) supplies the exact line and links the scanned source edition. The poem first appeared in 1818. The poem and 1914 edition are public domain by age with high confidence. The source transcription page has its own reuse terms, so production should cite or transcribe from the linked public-domain scan in the final ledger.

The line supplies finality, exhaustion, and a warning against empty triumph. The mandatory description guard keeps the subject political. What remains is one eligible world-scale order with living peoples and inherited obligations.
