# Event 012 Charter Four-Reading and Tooltip Mapping

Disposition: accepted by the parent on 2026-10-06; implementation is in progress. This mapping keeps the existing Event 012 Charter composition and hierarchy. It changes localization attached to existing elements and adds native tooltips to those same elements; it adds no panels, tabs, actions, costs, or layout positions.

## Acceptance and source basis

The parent sets the four visible mechanic readings to Authority, Pressure, Readiness, and current Objective. The selected country, region, diaspora origin/skill, proposal, and proposal family remain visible as navigation context. Every other accepted status value remains available through a two-to-four-line tooltip with a visible cue.

Event 012 Part 2, `docs/specs/012_africa_specs/specs/012_africa_spec_part_2_charter_league_integration.md`, defines Charter Authority as belief in the host's continental commitments and lists effects on invitations, emergency votes, confidence recovery, integration speed, rival formation, recognition, and late focuses. It defines Colonial Pressure as the external response to the League. Its supporting-values section places Continental War Readiness in external crises. Authority is clamped to `africa_measure.minimum..maximum` in `common/scripted_effects/012_africa_effects.txt:442`; full/failure action outcomes add `africa_action_result.full_authority` and `failure_authority` in `common/scripted_effects/012_africa_action_effects.txt:6221` and `:6321`, with values 2 and -3 in `common/script_constants/012_africa_action_constants.txt:898` and `:916`. Authority is used by the Charter League focus trigger in `common/scripted_triggers/012_africa_focus_route_triggers.txt:525`. Readiness is clamped at `common/scripted_effects/012_africa_effects.txt:451` and used by the command-route trigger at `common/scripted_triggers/012_africa_focus_route_triggers.txt:460`.

Native `pdx_tooltip` fields are documented for `iconType` in Offline Wiki `Interface modding`, lines 178-195, revision `b74afc86be4e5dbb3b0fc600820d73a8340f7dc1821fc5ef02c9ee1f028c0b1c`, and `instantTextBoxType` in lines 196-226 of the same revision. Both document `pdx_tooltip` as taking a localization key. Installed-game `common/scripted_guis/_documentation.md`, lines 1-70, revision `43ce647fc5fde27488855b060b5463c790e5e356149fceb010d36b960b40f838`, documents the decision-category scripted GUI's named-window attachment and element triggers.

The existing normal/empty/full render baseline is indexed in `docs/plans/012_africa_plans/subagent_handoffs/takeover_gui_animation_2026-10-06_evidence.json`, with visuals under `docs/assets/012_africa/animations/charter_visual_review_20261006/references/`. The matching parent-reviewed before crop remains `references/mcp_prechange_visible_cropped.png`.
