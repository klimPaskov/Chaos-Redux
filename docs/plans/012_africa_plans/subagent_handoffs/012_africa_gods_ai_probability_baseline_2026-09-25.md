Audit date: 2026-09-25. Workspace: `mod_chaos_redux_ea3b2d67c2c0`. This is a read-only baseline before completion work, not a post-change comparison. No gameplay, localisation, spreadsheet, or asset files were changed.

## Outcome and scope

The weighted sources and their raw score formulas were inventoried. The three decision/focus `probability_evaluate` calls returned `INTERNAL_ERROR`, and the sensitivity sweep returned `PROBABILITY_SWEEP_RANGE_REQUIRED`. A separate mission-adapter evaluation did return partial score-only evidence: both mission bases were exactly 1 in both attempted scenarios, but each mission’s `hidden_trigger` eligibility remained unresolved. The MCP evidence therefore supports no claim of normalized odds, scenario balance, starvation, dominance, or runtime validity.

The bounded surfaces are current participant demand-response decisions, host decisions, the 24-focus Gods overlay inside the Africa tree, the named Gods AI-strategy file, and any custom weighted selector in the Gods effects. Event 070 and the rest of the Africa tree’s AI are out of scope.

`docs/events/012_africa/gods_of_africa.md` is the authoritative implementation description. Its accepted gaps remain boundaries: guarantee, transport, intelligence, reconstruction, disaster, migration, and trade-support protection families; focus capability flags that are still witnesses; incomplete probability adapters/pools; and no live GUI/save acceptance. No live GUI or game was opened or launched.
