## Event 013 helper contract

`africa_achievement_record_natural_disaster_reconstruction_success` is a COUNTRY-scope callback invoked on the reconstructed state's controller, with PREV as the reconstructed state. Its inputs are `africa_event_active`, current-host/current-generation membership, `natural_disaster_reconstruction_success`, and `natural_disaster_sequence_id`; its side effects are one unique sequence array entry and one global containment increment. `africa_achievement_record_natural_disaster_famine_prevention` uses the same controller/PREV contract and requires `natural_disaster_chain_prevented`, `natural_disaster_stabilize_chain_prevention_taken`, a non-arrived famine chain, and the famine enum before setting the drought/famine survival flag. `africa_achievement_initialize_normal_host` clears the global reconstruction sequence array once at launch.
