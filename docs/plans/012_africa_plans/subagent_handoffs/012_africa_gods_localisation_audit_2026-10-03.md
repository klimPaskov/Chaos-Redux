## Prose quality and recommended edits

- **Vagueness:** `chaosx.nr12.604.d`, `.605.d`, `.614.d`, `gods_of_africa_focus_measure_needs_desc`, `gods_of_africa_focus_continental_reserve_desc`, and `gods_of_africa_seek_reconciliation_desc` use “relationship,” “measured,” “current,” “conduct,” or “costly” without naming the resource, action, or consequence. Replace them with the known outcome and actor; use dynamic text only where the owner has stable receipt variables.
- **Bloat and overcomplication:** `gods_of_africa_protect_partner_effect_tt` (`:151`) combines cooldown, three doctrine adjustments, multiple DLC gates, intelligence, infrastructure, reception, and optional transport in one long tooltip. Break it into a short base effect plus conditional tooltip lines already used by report `.610`; keep all applicable costs and requirements. `chaosx.nr12.610.d` and `gods_of_africa_protection_cost_tooltip` (`:154`) can also be shortened after confirming all distinct conditions remain visible.
- **Obvious explanation and repetition:** `gods_of_africa_demand_effect_tt` repeats the “Read the demand” button; `gods_of_africa_prioritize_*_effect_tt` (`:282-305`) repeats its title/description; `gods_of_africa_defensive_*_cost_tooltip` (`:344-353`) repeats the button and icon cost. Remove or use those lines for a real hidden requirement, consequence, or cooldown. `gods_of_africa_choose_strategic_ambiguity_desc` repeats “Keep the voices veiled” from its title. The `.604` response and `.608` settlement effect text repeat their reports without a new decision-relevant fact.
- **Style-rule repair:** Semicolons appear in player-facing `chaosx.nr12.610.d`, `gods_of_africa_choose_strategic_ambiguity_desc`, `gods_of_africa_choose_reciprocal_covenant_effect_tt`, and `gods_of_africa_focus_sovereign_exaction_desc` (`:38,118,145,260`). Rewrite as direct sentences. The report and tooltip families frequently use administrative words such as “recorded,” “frozen,” “debit/credit,” “capacity-checked,” and “evaluated” (`:130-144`); state the shipment, decision, and consequence in player terms while keeping any actual fixed quote and transfer amount. No sourced or attributed quotation appears in the inspected Gods text, so there is no quotation to normalize or preserve verbatim.
- **Route identity:** `gods_of_africa_focus_reciprocal_covenant_desc` and `_sovereign_exaction_desc` (`:258-260`) do distinguish mutual protection from restitution, but both read mostly as policy summaries. Keep that distinction while naming concrete African needs, partners, or demands where established mechanics support them. Avoid inventing lore.
