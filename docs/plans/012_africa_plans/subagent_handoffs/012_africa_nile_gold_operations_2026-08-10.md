# Event 012 Nile and Gold Roads operation handoff

Date: 2026-08-10. Owner scope: the Event 012 priority-member decisions, priority-member effects, priority-member triggers, priority-member constants, matching localisation, and this handoff only. No achievement core file, focus file, on-action, world-order file, model, tag, state definition, or workbook was edited. No commit was created.

## Implementation result

Nubia no longer reaches the controlled-pool achievement through the generic post-settlement progress proxy. The old Nubia post-settlement decision is hidden and the post-settlement focus callback excludes Nubia and Great Zimbabwe from `africa_priority_member_run_post_settlement_action`. Six positive player-visible and AI-usable Nile decisions now own the route: `africa_priority_nubia_nile_survey`, `africa_priority_nubia_nile_kush_recognition`, `africa_priority_nubia_nile_makuria_recognition`, `africa_priority_nubia_nile_alodia_recognition`, `africa_priority_nubia_nile_capital_consent`, and `africa_priority_nubia_shared_nile_corridor`. Two separate AI-disabled negative decisions record capital-dispute and corridor-failure receipts when live access collapses.

The Nile effects are `africa_priority_nubia_execute_nile_survey`, `africa_priority_nubia_execute_nile_kush`, `africa_priority_nubia_execute_nile_makuria`, `africa_priority_nubia_execute_nile_alodia`, `africa_priority_nubia_execute_nile_capital_consent`, `africa_priority_nubia_record_nile_capital_dispute`, `africa_priority_nubia_execute_shared_nile_corridor`, and `africa_priority_nubia_record_nile_corridor_failure`. Each identity effect calls the existing `africa_achievement_record_restoration_identity` helper with the exact Kush, Makuria, or Alodia enum. Capital consent writes a state receipt on the existing capital and requires all three local dockets plus the existing overlap disposition. Corridor success writes the shared Nile state receipt and calls the existing Nile overlap and corridor milestone helpers.

Nile hard failures are authored in the operation effect, not inferred from progress. A lost viable capital, war, or stale host generation while settling the capital writes `africa_priority_nubia_nile_capital_dispute_receipt` and global `africa_achievement_nile_capital_dispute`; the dedicated player-visible `africa_priority_nubia_record_capital_dispute` decision remains AI-disabled so the receipt is still reachable after a relationship failure. The same access break while opening the corridor writes `africa_priority_nubia_nile_corridor_failure_receipt` and global `africa_achievement_nile_corridor_failed`, with `africa_priority_nubia_record_corridor_failure` providing the explicit negative receipt path. An unresolved overlap is a separate `*_partial_receipt` path and does not falsely set either disqualifier.

Great Zimbabwe no longer reaches the Gold Roads owner gate through the generic post-settlement progress proxy. The old Great Zimbabwe post-settlement decision is hidden. Seven positive ordinary decisions now own the route: `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_plateau_survey`, `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_identity_recognition`, `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_mutapa_recognition`, `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_rozwi_recognition`, `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_processing_survey`, `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_plateau_corridor`, and `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_local_ownership_ledger`. Two AI-disabled negative choices record plateau corridor failure or foreign concession majority.

The Gold Roads effects are `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_execute_plateau_survey`, `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_execute_identity`, `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_execute_mutapa`, `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_execute_rozwi`, `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_execute_processing_survey`, `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_execute_plateau_corridor`, `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_record_plateau_corridor_failure`, `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_execute_local_ledger`, and `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_record_foreign_majority`. Great Zimbabwe, Mutapa, and Rozwi are recorded as separate restoration identities through the existing achievement identity helper. Processing survey marks three distinct existing controlled states, records the existing capital as a surveyed mine-source, activates the local processing programme, and increments the processing-zone ledger by three in one idempotent survey receipt. Mutapa and Rozwi identity dockets do not increment processing counts; they remain identity-only dockets.

Gold corridor success is separate from the processing survey and ownership ledger. It marks the surveyed mine-source and all three surveyed processing states with an explicit corridor-connected state receipt, so the route is not only a country counter or a capital flag. War, lost capital control, or stale host generation writes `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_plateau_corridor_failure_receipt` and global `africa_achievement_plateau_corridor_failed`; the dedicated `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_record_corridor_failure` decision keeps this receipt reachable when the normal corridor action is no longer available. Unresolved overlap writes a repairable partial receipt. The local ledger records global local ownership at `constant:africa_achievement_ratio.protected_local_ownership` and foreign concessions at `constant:africa_achievement_ratio.resource_review_settlement_foreign_concession_share`, starts the existing five-year survival-window refresh, and opens the Gold Roads owner gate only after the global three-polity and three-processing-zone counts are met. The human-only foreign concession choice writes `africa_priority_great_zimbabwe_foreign_resource_majority_receipt`, sets global `africa_achievement_foreign_resource_majority`, stores a 60-percent foreign-majority share at `constant:africa_priority_member_operation.foreign_majority_share`, and clears owner-ready flags. Its AI weight is the central zero constant.

## Constants and tuning

The existing generic cost and progress constants remain available to the other fourteen packages.

## Trigger map

`common/scripted_triggers/012_africa_priority_member_triggers.txt` adds the shared Nile and Gold base gates, viable-operation gates for AI, and stage gates for every decision. The base gates require the recorded Event 012 settlement, current host generation, an open post-settlement window, and a non-occupied/non-leaving relationship. They also close the positive routes after the exact global Nile or Gold failure receipts. Stage gates require the preceding authored receipt, not a mutable generic progress value.

## Localisation and assets

`localisation/english/012_africa_priority_member_l_english.yml` is UTF-8 with BOM and contains titles, descriptions, operation tooltips, and exact capital-dispute, corridor-failure, and foreign-majority receipt strings for every new decision. All decisions reuse the already registered Nubia and Great Zimbabwe post-settlement icons; no new binary asset or GFX registration is required.
