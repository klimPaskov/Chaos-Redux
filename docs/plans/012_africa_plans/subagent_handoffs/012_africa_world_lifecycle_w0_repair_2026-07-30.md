The successor inherits the live package balances but starts the package focus sequence again.

The replacement therefore receives the correct package tree with `keep_completed = yes`, begins at the independent active-package status, and must ratify its route and final settlement itself.

This is not terminal proof and cannot advance The World gate by itself.
