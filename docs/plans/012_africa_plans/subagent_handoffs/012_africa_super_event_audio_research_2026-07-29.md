## Rejected or non-approvable alternatives

- John Bartmann, *African Moon*, and *Happy African Village* were checked because their titles and instrumentation might appear relevant to role 1. The Commons pages display a CC0 deed but preserve embedded FMA metadata stating CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, so composition and recording rights are contradictory. Both are rejected.
- The United States Navy Band recording of Algeria's *Kassaman* has a public-domain federal performance, but the Commons page flags the 1955 composition as potentially restored or copyrighted in the United States and other jurisdictions. It is also a national anthem that would over-identify one country with continent-wide completion. It is rejected.
- The Ethiopian national anthem recording *Ethiopia, Ethiopia, Ethiopia be first* is identified as public domain in Ethiopia, but the 1975 composition and recording rights are not a clear worldwide grant. It is also a state anthem for one historical regime rather than a route-neutral continental cue. It is rejected.
- Bruno Walter's 1938 Mahler Symphony No. 9 IV recording is marked public domain for the European Union because phonographic rights expired there, not as a worldwide release. It is rejected for the mod's worldwide redistribution requirement.
- Generated tones, synthesized beds, generic drones, trailer stingers, modern commercial recordings without explicit rights, and anthem substitutes remain disallowed. No fallback was silently accepted.
