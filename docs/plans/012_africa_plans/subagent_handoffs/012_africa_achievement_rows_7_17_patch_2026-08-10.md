# Event 012 achievement rows 7–17 patch handoff (2026-08-10)

## Scope and status

This handoff covers the Event 012 achievement completion gates for rows 7–17, their live source ledgers, exact loss/reset owners, the Event 018 concession restoration bridge, player-facing localisation, and the structural invariants that replace definition-only technical disqualifier flags. The unregistered Event 012 forced-scenario route is retired by the rows 1–6 cleanup and is not an owner for this tranche. No commit or staging was performed.

The implementation is source-first and fail-closed. Where the repository has no authoritative writer for a named historical disqualifier, the technical proxy was removed and the trigger now requires the current constitution, relationship, live member roster, live region ledger, state settlement, or exact event-system witness that the engine can actually observe. No synthetic writer was added for an unsupported deposition, military transition, terminal-coercion, emergency, or partition flag.

## Row-by-row proof map

7. `africa_the_clause_is_the_country_is_complete` now requires Africa is One and `global.africa_achievement_live_clause_members >= 10`. `africa_achievement_refresh_live_clause_proof` loops the current relationship registry and counts only current-generation, living chartered/federal/integrated members with a resolved accession dispute, accepted continental constitution, and at least three distinct full clause receipts. If a previously complete ten-member roster falls below ten, the exact rebuild sets `africa_achievement_clause_member_loss`; untracked annexation and the Military Continentalism route remain explicit disqualifiers.

8. `africa_exit_without_war_is_complete` now depends on the exact negotiated departure start, target identity, current-generation return, no capitulation, no war with the host, and a 720-day elapsed peace interval. `africa_apply_relationship_transition` records a peaceful associate return, while `on_join_faction` records the ally return. `on_war_relation_added`, `on_capitulation`, occupied return, and the coercive annex owner write `africa_achievement_exit_war_or_coup` or `africa_achievement_exit_coerced_return` at their actual source. There is no inferred “peaceful” result from a generic relationship count.

9. `africa_no_second_capital_is_complete` requires four resolved crises, an empty live `africa_rival_bloc_countries` registry, and `africa_achievement_rival_terminal_result`. The transition owner calls `africa_achievement_record_rival_terminal_result` only after a rival member actually leaves for associate/outside and the live rival registry is empty. The old terminal-high-chaos proxy was removed; leader annexation and overrun remain exact existing disqualifiers.

10. `africa_every_region_speaks_is_complete` now requires the qualifying congress agenda, a live congress roster, nine lifetime congress-region receipts, Africa is One, nine live represented regions, and nine settled-overlap regions. `africa_achievement_record_full_action` appends only the selected target overlay for a full `convene_regional_congress` completion, with duplicate suppression; `africa_achievement_record_congress_agenda_completed` reads that lifetime ledger and never populates it from the current roster. `africa_achievement_refresh_live_regional_proofs` derives the represented-region ledger from living current-generation cooperative members and valid overlays, so `guarantee_regional_representation` and congress action receipts cannot act as live-region proxies. The former representation branch in `africa_achievement_record_target_region_for_current_action` was removed.

11. `africa_confidence_is_contagious_is_complete` requires the captured high-confidence floor and the live high-confidence count of fifteen, Integration Burden below high, and the 720-day deadline. `africa_achievement_refresh_live_duration_counts` rebuilds the live count from current-generation non-capitulated cooperative members. `africa_achievement_reset_live_member_loss_clocks` is called before and after relationship loss, on capitulation/annex, stale action-target cleanup, and the full promoted-tier disruption owner; it clears and restarts the confidence clock on the next qualifying refresh.

12. `africa_federation_by_consent_is_complete` requires Federal Union, Africa is One, captured and live autonomous-federal floors of twelve, and twelve live settled members. `africa_country_counts_as_federal_settlement` is the exact member invariant: accepted constitution, locked term, explicit integration consent, accepted autonomy statute, no civil war/war/leaving/resistant/rival state, current host generation, real federal/integrated autonomy tier, host overlord, and final integration consent for an integrated region. Representation and customs flags are required for the settled sub-count; the old military-takeover proxy was removed.

13. `africa_republic_of_many_capitals_is_complete` requires Continental Republic, five distinct entries in `global.africa_achievement_republic_institution_regions`, the authored owner-ready flag, active succession, and the five-year deadline. The only writers are the five explicit focus rewards in `012_africa_focus_route_effects.txt`; congress attendance never writes this institution ledger. Civil war remains the direct current-state gate. No authored route can suspend the distributed institution or collapse it to one region, so the dead `africa_achievement_republic_suspended` and `africa_achievement_republic_one_region_centralisation` checks were removed; the positive five-placement/owner-ready/succession contract is authoritative, and the unsupported military-transition flag was also removed.

14. `africa_crowns_at_one_table_is_complete` requires a monarchical/restored origin, Council of Crowns, Africa is One, captured and live court floors of eight, and settled crown succession. `africa_country_counts_as_current_court` requires current host generation, recognised-court receipt, preserved crown clause, accepted current continental constitution, settled succession rules, living non-capitulated/no-war/no-civil-war status, a current cooperative relationship, and a recognised monarchical/restored government identity. Unsupported court-deposition and monarchy-abolition flags are not written or checked; the live court invariant fails closed when a counted court is no longer current.

15. `africa_union_of_work_and_land_is_complete` requires a socialist/communist People’s Union host, eight live worker/peasant regions, nine live food-secure regions, twelve socialised projects, and the three-year food deadline. Worker and food arrays are rebuilt from current-generation cooperative members carrying the exact action-owned proof flags. Event 013’s failed famine-prevention mission writes `africa_achievement_preventable_famine` only for a live Event 012 host/member. The row-15 private-concession disqualifier is written only by Event 018’s real restoration effect after `resources_found_grant_selected_field_resource_rights` sets `resources_found_foreign_concession_granted` on an African field owned by the current host or a current-generation member.

16. `africa_order_without_partition_is_complete` requires the Military Continentalism constitution, Africa is One, two exact intervention victories, the authored emergency-rule reduction and representation-restored receipts, nine live represented/overlap regions, all African states currently settled by `africa_all_african_states_are_settled`, emergency debt below maximum, no civil war, no Event 013 genocide receipt, and no `africa_achievement_african_core_ceded` receipt. The latter is written by the authoritative settled Scramble core snapshot when a marked African core is owned or controlled by a foreign-capital actor; temporary control loss alone is not treated as partition. `africa_achievement_member_genocide` is written by the shared genocide system only when positive state deaths implicate a living current-generation Event 012 member. Permanent-maximum-emergency and synthetic region-partition flags were not invented; current emergency debt, the state-settlement/region ledgers, and the exact Scramble cession witness are the source-enforced invariants.

17. `africa_confederation_that_endured_is_complete` requires Continental Confederation, fifteen live sovereign confederal members, burden below the confederal ceiling, the ten-year deadline, and the Scramble settlement/win. `africa_country_counts_as_confederal_settlement` requires current generation, independent sovereign status, chartered relationship, accepted continental constitution, member-recognised confederal unity, and the host’s Confederation capstone. `africa_apply_relationship_transition` records the exact confederal-to-federal annexation when a chartered member is moved into a federal tier, and every relevant loss path resets the live confederation clock. Ceiling breach and member cascade remain their existing source-owned disqualifiers.

## Helper and lifecycle map

`africa_achievement_refresh_live_clause_proof` is host-scoped, takes the relationship registry as input, outputs the live clause-member count, and sets the sticky clause-member-loss flag when a completed roster drops below ten. `africa_achievement_refresh_live_duration_counts` is host-scoped, rebuilds high-confidence/federal-settled/current-court/confederal counts, and feeds the survival-window clocks. `africa_achievement_refresh_live_regional_proofs` is host-scoped and rebuilds representation, overlap, worker, food, and connection arrays from current live members. `africa_achievement_record_republic_institution_placement` is host-scoped and consumes the temporary authored overlay ID from the five focus rewards. `africa_achievement_record_peaceful_reassociation` is member-scoped and consumes the target ID/start day written by `prepare_member_exit_terms`. `africa_achievement_record_rival_terminal_result` is host-scoped and consumes the emptied live rival registry. `africa_achievement_reset_live_member_loss_clocks` is host-scoped and clears confidence/confederation starts and deadlines before rebuilding the live clause and regional ledgers. `africa_achievement_record_private_resource_concession` is host-scoped and has no caller outside the Event 018 rights-restoration owner.

No new shared script constants were necessary; the patch uses the existing `africa_achievement_count`, `africa_achievement_days`, `africa_overlay`, `africa_constitution`, `africa_relationship`, and `africa_measure` script-constant categories. Event targets are existing `africa_host`, `resources_found_contract_partner_target`, and action targets; no new global target lifecycle was introduced.
