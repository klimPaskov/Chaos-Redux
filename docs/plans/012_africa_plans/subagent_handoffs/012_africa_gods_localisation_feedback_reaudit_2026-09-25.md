## Prose and cross-surface findings

- **Incorrect peace condition:** `gods_of_africa_protect_partner_effect_tt` at `012_africa_gods_l_english.yml:128` says Arms Against Tyranny mutual market access applies “when both countries are at peace.” The source checks that the partner and Africa are not at war *with each other*; it does not require either to be at peace generally. Recommended wording: “With Arms Against Tyranny and the sanctuary focus, protection may grant mutual market access when Africa and the partner are not at war with each other.”
- **Intelligence condition missing in effect tooltip:** the same key says two agencies suffice; source also requires Africa at war and the security focus. `gods_of_africa_focus_security_oaths_desc` at `:195` correctly mentions Africa at war. Recommended wording: “With La Résistance, the security focus, agencies in both countries, and Africa at war, the partner gains [?constant:gods_of_africa_transaction.protection_intelligence_amount|0] points of each intelligence type on Africa's current enemies it is not fighting.”
- **Cost row is prose-heavy and likely too wide:** `gods_of_africa_protection_cost` and `_blocked` at `:129-130` place a `Cost:` label, three prices, a reserve explanation, both transport alternatives, the floor, and Event 013 separation into the native cost string. The decision skill limits inline display to three icon values and up to four normal words, with explanation in the hover tooltip. The first line meets the three-value limit, but the entire keyed string does not. The full normal and blocked rows also repeat material from `gods_of_africa_protection_cost_tooltip` at `:131` and `gods_of_africa_protect_partner_effect_tt` at `:128`. Move the reserve, conditional transport, and relief separation into the tooltip or decision description, keeping the normal and blocked cost row compact; preserve all conditions and amounts. The tooltip itself uses a semicolon, contrary to the event prose style, and can be split into two direct sentences.
- **Blocked state lacks diagnosis:** `gods_of_africa_protection_cost_blocked` turns all three inline prices red even if only one fails, and the reserve is text-only. The player cannot tell whether real equipment, fuel, political power, or the reserve blocked the decision. A resource-specific dynamic presentation or a concise blocked-reason tooltip would be clearer. This remains a display recommendation, not an assertion that the engine renders individual failures automatically.
- **Event 013 inline row is long but must keep the price:** `natural_disaster_cost_neighbor_relief` and `_blocked` each draw five amounts, beyond the skill's three-inline-value rule. `natural_disaster_cost_neighbor_relief_tooltip` adds Stability, War Support, and construction burden. Any layout repair must preserve all five material costs and the two penalties. The user explicitly preserved this choice's complete price; do not trim it to satisfy Event 012's four-type cap.
- **Repeated and obvious explanation:** `chaosx.nr12.610.d`, `gods_of_africa_protect_partner_desc`, `gods_of_africa_protect_partner_effect_tt`, `gods_of_africa_protection_cost`, and `gods_of_africa_focus_relief_beyond_desc` repeat that Relief Convoy is a separate choice “at its listed price.” One clear statement in the decision description or cost tooltip is enough for this linked action. `gods_of_africa_protect_partner_effect_tt` also repeats the basic infantry and fuel transfer and cooldown already shown elsewhere. Keep the event popup in-world and reserve mechanical detail for the decision.
- **Vagueness and overcomplication:** “real equipment” in the decision description adds little beside the explicit infantry and fuel shipment. “Shortage-checked” is implementation-style jargon; “if the partner has fewer than 20 convoys or trains” states the condition. The protection effect tooltip combines many focus-conditional outcomes into one long paragraph, making the immediate consequence hard to find. The specific actor, resource, and location clauses can remain while splitting conditional outcomes into readable sentences. No generic route-lore rewrite is needed.
- **Cross-surface match:** Event 012 text consistently presents Relief Convoy as separate, and Event 013 describes its own full foreign disaster package. The Event 012 protection report says only infantry and fuel were sent, consistent with its unconditional effect. The “both at peace” claim is the identified mismatch with the market-access script. The event catalog workbook was not inspected or edited under this bounded assignment; the spreadsheet owner should mirror any later approved final wording for `chaosx.nr12.610.d`, `gods_of_africa_protect_partner_desc`, and `gods_of_africa_protect_partner_effect_tt` if corresponding fields exist.
- **Sourced quotations:** none occur in the inspected protection and relief-price keys. No quotation was changed.
