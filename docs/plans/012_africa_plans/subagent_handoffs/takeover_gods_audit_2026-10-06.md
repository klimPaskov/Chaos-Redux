## Decision-surface and mission quality notes

The two ordinary categories are `gods_of_africa_participant_category` and `gods_of_africa_host_category` in `common/decisions/categories/012_africa_gods_categories.txt`. The host category adds the selected doctrine. No scripted GUI is attached, and the settlement delivery row uses the ordinary host category.

Mission owners are participant country scope. `gods_of_africa_demand_mission` activates for the one current demand, calls the compliance path on success, and fails the contract on timeout. `gods_of_africa_reconciliation_mission` is gated by current participant/history identity, open reconciliation, peace with Africa, and no owned/controlled host-core territory; success calls accepted reconciliation and timeout records failure. `gods_of_africa_final_ultimatum_mission` is activated only while an ultimatum receipt is current; the country receives the `.618` notice on opening, can return land or pay by decisions, and timeout/refusal runs the strength-capped punishment evaluator. Their displayed duration, category visible count, and live failure effects were not rendered here.

One active demand per participant and mutually exclusive war/defiance/reconciliation/settlement gates are source-owned. The bounded active-demand cap and staggered dispatch are centralized in Gods timing constants. The static source did not prove that each campaign state stays at or below six simultaneous visible host actions, so category density remains a concrete MCP/live-review item.
