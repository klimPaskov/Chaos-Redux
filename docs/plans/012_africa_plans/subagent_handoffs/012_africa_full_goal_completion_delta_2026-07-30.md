## Overall conclusion

Event 012 is **incomplete** and cannot be accepted as the full requested Africa goal.

The protection-first grounded runtime is materially implemented, and the world and model-dependent branches are mostly fail-closed.

The package is not safe to promote as a whole because active grounded surfaces still contain unreachable diaspora actions, an isolated South Africa civil-war package with no shared caller, incomplete or false achievement proof, unresolved focus-matrix/layout acceptance, and undisposed action simplifications.

The six external continent packages, The World, and all four super-events remain correctly dormant, but their readiness gates must remain closed.

Commit `433c22f4f` closes the four previously reported world lifecycle defects involving installed-candidate removal, breakup authority arithmetic, successor replacement, and exile/breakup terminal accounting.

Those four defects are not repeated below as open findings.

The same commit does not complete the rest of W0 or any of W1 through W5.
