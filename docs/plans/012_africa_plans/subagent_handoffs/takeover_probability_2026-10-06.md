## Focus, other Gods actions, and limits

Source review confirms `gods_of_africa_focus_reciprocal_covenant` and `gods_of_africa_focus_sovereign_exaction` are mutually exclusive siblings behind `gods_of_africa_focus_continental_proclamation`. Both require the current African host, Evolution I, active Gods, and committed activation. Reciprocal uses the shared high base and an extra preferred multiplier while the doctrine variable is zero. Exaction uses the same base and adds preferred multipliers for war and tier-IV-or-higher Chaos. The complete focus pool is not narrowed to these two IDs: the live focus inspection found 301 candidates and requires the host/capital/constitution/phase/integration/region/rival context plus complete external factors. Focus probability evaluation was therefore skipped; the focus owner can use `FOCUS-STABLE-COALITION` and `FOCUS-OCCUPIED-CONTINENT` with a complete available-focus pool.

The inspect artifact for Gods decisions also indexes host targeted actions, settlement, substitute, ultimatum, and instalment decisions. This bounded pass did not produce usable scores for `gods_of_africa_mark_offender`, `gods_of_africa_escalate_offense`, `gods_of_africa_protect_partner`, `gods_of_africa_accept_substitute`, `gods_of_africa_sign_support_agreement`, or `gods_of_africa_deliver_settlement_support`. Those require target-array scopes, frozen payment state, or stock availability not established by the participant-response fixtures. Their values remain unresolved, not zero.

The score-only invitation decision has no normalized denominator. The event-option adapter has a complete local option pool for the invitation response event. None of the analyses measures event firing likelihood, actual AI click cadence, random-seed frequencies, repeated delivery, or long-run sequence behavior.
