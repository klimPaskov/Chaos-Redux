## Protection overlay and consumer wiring

Its `target_root_trigger` and `visible` blocks repeat the same OR over shelter, reserve, security, logistics, relief, expedition, and sanctuary capability flags (`common/decisions/012_africa_gods_decisions.txt:870-908`). Shelter is the shortest route to a first capability, through `gods_of_africa_focus_shelter_faithful` setting `gods_of_africa_focus_shelter_unlocked` (`common/national_focus/012_africa_continental_focus_tree.txt:7778`), though another route can reach its own capability first. Later accepted capabilities alter what the same action delivers.

The protection target is source-gated to a current-generation registered participant, not defiant, with reliable memory applied, at peace with the host, and with the 180-day cooldown elapsed. The host must have the base equipment, fuel, political power, and common-defense reserve (`common/scripted_triggers/012_africa_gods_triggers.txt:604-634`). The decision's target trigger and availability use that package gate (`common/decisions/012_africa_gods_decisions.txt:890-916`).
