## Exact shared-runtime defects

### 1. Installed ready actor remains a closure veto

Candidates are added to `africa_world_package_candidates` at `common/scripted_effects/012_africa_world_order_effects.txt:41`, and the array is cleared only when the subsystem is initialised at `:385`.

`africa_world_install_current_package` clears `africa_world_package_candidate` and adds the actor to `africa_world_package_actors` at `common/scripted_effects/012_africa_world_order_effects.txt:1077`, but it never removes that scope from `africa_world_package_candidates`.

The Africa-only close checks every scope still in the candidate array and rejects closure if any has `africa_world_package_implementation_ready`, without checking that it still has `africa_world_package_candidate`, at `common/scripted_triggers/012_africa_world_order_triggers.txt:418`.

If one ready candidate installs while fewer than all six packages install, full ratification fails the six-package count and partial closure remains vetoed by the installed actor's stale array entry.

Recommended correction: remove the actor from the candidate array during installation, and make the close veto require both a live candidate flag and the readiness flag. Both checks should be retained for idempotence.

### 2. Breakup increases authority

`constant:africa_world_roster.breakup_authority_loss` is `-20` at `common/script_constants/012_africa_world_order_constants.txt:241`.

`africa_world_record_package_breakup` subtracts that negative constant at `common/scripted_effects/012_africa_world_order_effects.txt:1049`, which adds twenty authority.

Recommended correction: use `add_to_variable` with the negative loss constant, matching the existing negative-constant convention, or change the constant to positive and retain subtraction consistently.

### 3. Successor recognition is not a package transfer

`africa_world_commit_package_successor` marks `africa_world_package_successor_recorded` on the original actor and selected candidate at `common/scripted_effects/012_africa_world_order_effects.txt:1009`.

It does not transfer `africa_world_package_actor`, `africa_world_package_installed`, continent identity, install status, route, focus tree, mechanic state, constituent and heartland arrays, public identity, or the host's `africa_world_package_actors` entry. It also does not clear the old actor's installed and actor flags.

The targeted successor decision remains owned by the original installed actor at `common/decisions/012_africa_decisions.txt:2020`, while annex loss handling opens review on the victim scope during `on_annex` at `common/on_actions/012_africa_world_order_on_actions.txt:118`.

The terminal trigger accepts the original actor's bare successor marker at `common/scripted_triggers/012_africa_world_order_triggers.txt:174`, even though the selected successor never becomes the package actor.

Recommended correction: replace the host's actor-array entry, transfer or reinitialise all package-owned state on the selected successor, clear the original actor's active-package state, recalculate heartland and constituents, and move decision ownership to the replacement. The original successor marker must not be terminal proof.

### 4. Lifecycle resolutions do not advance the terminal ledger

`africa_world_record_terminal_resolution` is the only effect that increments `africa_world_terminal_resolved_count`, at `common/scripted_effects/012_africa_world_order_effects.txt:2256`.

The successor, exile, and breakup decisions at `common/decisions/012_africa_decisions.txt:2020` call their state-recording effects but never call the terminal recorder.

The terminal-resolution trigger accepts successor, exile, and breakup flags at `common/scripted_triggers/012_africa_world_order_triggers.txt:157`, while the terminal World gate separately requires six counted terminal resolutions at `:493`.

Recommended correction: treat successor recognition as a transfer rather than terminal resolution. Count the successor only after it reaches an accepted final state. Invoke the idempotent terminal recorder after accepted exile or breakup resolution, and remove stale actor, constituent, and successor-array entries as part of that lifecycle.
