# Parent review and disposition

This is the parent author's review. It is not the required independent subagent improvement pass.

## Changes made during the local review

The draft initially risked treating direct equipment aid as a discounted resource purchase. The material rule now explicitly conserves donor and recipient quantities and limits the supported discount to eligible administrative costs. Factory commitments also have a separate stated discount boundary.

Investment work was initially described only in five-day installments. The final design records actual funded days through a bounded active-project update, preserves partial installments, and does not infer five funded days from a single capacity check at the end of the interval.

The blocking coalition initially offered mainly completion continuity if its initiator disappeared. It now has a concrete timing benefit for funded cooperation, while retaining the common victim loss cap. Late membership cannot delay an existing due date or create an immediate unscheduled completion.

The military family now includes expanded and senior advisory routes, so later-stage participation does not depend entirely on a deliverable weapon shipment. These routes provide real training and institutional outputs without claiming to deliver nonexistent weapons.

The subject-player issue is recorded as a central engine gate, including the explicit ancestry-cycle case. The plan does not hide the issue behind a narrower roster or a reward to the existing overlord.

## Retained design choices

One headline Influence measure, three categorical institutions, and per-project work progress provide useful depth without several competing national meters. Fixed action-cap limits create a meaningful opportunity for concentrated minors. Immediate victory remains unconditional once a valid positive completion reaches the threshold.

The baseline retains a single active target. The Great Game increases capacity through later ordinary firings, not automatic batch creation. This preserves the user's repeat behavior and prevents an evolved opening from consuming multiple unrelated event selections.

## Open review concerns

The industrial output ratios are deliberately authored grant contracts and require balance testing against ordinary construction. Their attractiveness may be high in long races. The no-automatic-winner rule can leave an abandoned baseline race occupying capacity, which is an intentional tradeoff that ordinary AI and campaign tests must examine.

Source reading, runtime, native GUI, probability, multiplayer, and independent review limitations remain listed in the audit. No local formatting or arithmetic result closes those gates.
