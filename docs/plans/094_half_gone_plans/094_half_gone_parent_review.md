# Parent review

Review type: manual design review by the authoring parent. Independent subagent review: not performed.

## Corrections incorporated

The legacy report-option debit was replaced in the design with a pre-presentation transaction. Train and convoy aliases were prevented from receiving a second half-loss. Person identity was separated from multiple role slots. Population disappearance was separated from confirmed Deaths. Population/manpower and overlapping roster combinations received explicit pre-selection conflict rules.

The evolution design was resolved as per-firing intense openings rather than adding an unrelated autonomous ninety-day removal process. This is a deliberate documented exception requiring the later probability reviewer to confirm the actual implementation matches that design.

The aftermath was bounded to four response types and one active program. No focus tree, new country, 3D asset, custom investigation GUI, or extra public meter was added. Unique-owner absence keeps completed institutions and deployed creations intact unless the actual owner has a separate established reason to change them.

Source-status paragraphs were removed from the narrative design parts and retained in the reading and decision reports. Existing catalog names and numbers were not silently treated as the newer owner designs.

## Questions that remain genuinely open

Can unfinished research progress be reduced safely across all required ordinary and special systems? Can state population and current manpower be independently reconciled? What are the exact current Wu, Kruger, and Devil absence/restoration contracts?

Do the proposed family weights produce the intended rare but meaningful human losses across small and large country pools? Are the paid programs worth their real opportunity costs? Can the equipment-value recovery achievement be tracked without inventing unsupported production evidence?

Are the chosen population presentation thresholds appropriate in actual campaigns? Which researched text and licensed structured music fit the scene? These questions require the named source, probability, asset, and independent-review gates. They are not closed by this manual review.

## Anti-bloat result

The design expansion is concentrated in the category meanings, material ownership, population accounting, multi-role people, recovery choices, and named connections. It avoids an explanation meter and a second campaign-management system. The remaining reviews should improve those existing surfaces rather than append unrelated routes merely to make the package longer.

The package is ready for substantive design review and targeted implementation reconnaissance, not a claim that the mandatory full execution workflow has completed.
