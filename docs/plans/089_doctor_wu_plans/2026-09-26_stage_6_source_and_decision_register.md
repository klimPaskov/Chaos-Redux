## Findings that change the design

### K01, K02, K07, K08: rights and the canonical character

The fixed Kruger character and his valid research anchor remain Event 016-owned. Ordinary transfer removes his old roles, reconciles the old host, moves the same character, restores valid roles, and inherits carried history. This does not make every physical or national right portable.

Resolved facility and custody decisions stay with the old host and its physical state. Only unresolved facility or custody report ownership travels through the inspected recipient-copy helper. Country-specific institutional settlement receipts are also described as host-local. Part 10 therefore separates a scientist's move from transfer of a hospital, public method, patent arrangement, staff assignment, or material stock.

### K03 and K11: rivalry is not early sovereignty

Kruger's sovereignty board opens after his fourth evolution under its own contract. The board uses a causal resolution and its actual territory and institutional facts. A failed territorial plan can remain a noncountry laboratory crisis.

The current transfer trigger rejects an active scientist assignment, a pending or running cross-domain review, confinement, confirmed death, and other owner conditions. The recipient predicate also retains prior-host and rejection exclusions. Part 10 does not waive any of those conditions. It provides ordinary service protection and administrative settlements separately from owner-controlled arrest, forced departure, shutdown, or sovereignty.

### K04: native project clocks and durable learning

The project owner keeps family-and-stage receipts, capacity, predecessors, paid native prototypes, completion, and cancellation separate. A shared undertaking cannot complete either component again. Learned operational packages remain with their country, while pre-operational or service-dependent output can still require a functioning project or institution.

The cross-domain paleogenetic and xenobiological review retains separate histories and its transition lock. A domestic joint charter is not permission to repeat or bypass that review.

### K02 and K05: domestic joint work is a separate contract

The existing joint-laboratory framework is a foreign partner and site arrangement. It has its own one-time establishment, cleanup, and history. The international contest also has its own evolution access, actor locks, host limits, and outcome records.

Part 10's domestic joint institute requires a proposed bounded contract. It does not reuse a foreign partner flag, initialize the international contest early, reset a foreign operation, or redirect that operation's original actor and host. Full Wu foreign operations remain for Part 11.

### K03: hidden value semantics

Independent Capacity cannot be treated as a generic public-independence bonus from its name. The inspected containment document uses it in Kruger's resisting strength and separately accounts for nationally replicated scientific families. Part 10 proposes factual case inputs to the Kruger owner and leaves numerical mapping unapproved instead of inventing a direct hidden-meter patch.

### K09: legacy clone production remains separate

The inspected binding contract preserves ten weekly manpower per stockpiled legacy clone equipment and the accepted controlled-growth equipment output. Its bounded source acceptance is separate from live validation. Wu's finite person mode remains D03, with its separate unapproved accounting extension. A joint laboratory cannot convert either economy into the other through an institutional label.

### K10: strategic-raid discrepancy carried forward

The final resolver still selects an internal handling state and sets completed consumption to the full native reservation. Its inspected code contains no script-side refund of that completed reservation. The Stage 5 guide-versus-runtime discrepancy therefore remains current for those exact facts.

The guide was not reread in this stage. Its conflicting description is retained as a Stage 5 finding, not presented as a freshly inspected document. Native cancellation, earlier outcome helpers, and engine ordering remain unverified. No attempt was made to restore older staging or partial-refund behavior.
