### O02. Sanctions, trade, and medical access

Current sanctions are source- and participant-owned. A humanitarian carve-out reduces practical pressure, but the inspected contract does not provide a universal medicine-delivery exemption. The shared inspection system discloses its supported hidden buckets. Exact chemical action reconciliation does not establish an equivalent generic exact-biological-case API. A Wu exposure case needs its own approved contribution or disclosure interface and cannot duplicate an already recorded act.

Migration owns exact cohort identity, generation, origin debit, route deaths, survivor credit, and reception. Famine owns the positive versioned food-safety projection and invalidates unknown facts. A clinical agreement cannot supply either owner with guessed safe values.

Multi-leg and overseas routes require distinct supported owner contracts. Ordinary land-combat and bombing attribution cannot be inferred from control change alone. The corridor document retains its own pending integration and runtime evidence limits.

The current ordinary biological equipment definitions prohibit ordinary licensing and lend-lease. A copied design does not grant physical stock, and a specimen contract cannot evade those prohibitions through an unrelated equipment transfer.
