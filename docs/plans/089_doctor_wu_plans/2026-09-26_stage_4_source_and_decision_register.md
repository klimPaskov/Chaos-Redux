## Current owner findings

Famine and migration are independent sparse systems. Their current guides require exact owner-proven state, source, amount, generation, revision, and routing facts. Migration consumes famine's validated destination-food-safety projection and does not interpret an untracked state as stable. Part 8 therefore makes missing food proof block admission instead of using a medical result to certify reception.

The biological protection integration rebuilds state effects from their base values before applying the current protection stack. A repeated filter renewal must not subtract its reduction from an already reduced result. The new service adapter must let the disease owner reconcile the current strongest compatible Wu contribution and retain other owners' distinct protections.

Black Plague already owns a 420-day Population Recovery Programme, a 120-day Memorial and Biosecurity Charter, a 210-day vigilance programme, and a 360-day inspection compact. Their disease, devastation, and aftermath records remain with Event 020. L12 may add actual receiving works or adopt a matching service contribution, but cannot replay those programmes' rewards or label their historical deaths restored.

Zombie Outbreak distinguishes propagation shutdown from final defeat. Existing zombie countries can survive a stopped spread system. Its guide also describes an older capital-focused evacuation transaction. This task did not complete the full migration adapter call-chain review for that transaction. A Wu-assisted evacuation must select one proven migration-owned movement path and cannot call both the legacy evacuation mutation and a second population transfer.

The public custom technology API has seven custom operational families, separate conventional packages, permanent external knowledge and provenance, and no general revocation API. Xenobiological control selects one occupied channel. Part 8 respects this distinction and never uses machinery qualification to revoke a learned family, create Kruger history, or stack incompatible controls.

Event 019's current contract has thirteen runtime callbacks plus registration. Its source-owned presentation, equipment publication, exact payment, derivative isolation, and cleanup proofs remain mandatory. The existing natural-release recreate-prove-delete exception cannot preserve every live formation detail and does not authorize arbitrary Wu transfer. Support attachments need a real combat anchor. An animal provider cannot add generic consumer fees on top of four paid types without an explicitly accepted fee disposition.

CXT registers unseen carriers through bounded startup or tag-scoped registration. Daily registration does not grant stock or apply content. Part 8's fixtures must remain player-applied and distinguish raw debug equipment from proof that a finite human was actually produced.
