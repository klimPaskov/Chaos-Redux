## Current source findings that constrain implementation

The fresh clone equipment, effect, modifier, technology, constant, and unit reads confirm that the existing physical clone stock remains a recurring income source. The reserve helper rounds the stockpile and multiplies it by ten, then refreshes a weekly manpower modifier. It does not consume one finite person per payment. The finite mode must therefore use a genuinely separate contract, not merely a new description of the old equipment.

The current KRG maturation transaction creates 100 legacy clone equipment after its paid cycle. Its source clears reward authority before output or refund. That is a useful ownership pattern, but its output remains the legacy recurring stock and its single-active-receipt assumptions do not establish safety for overlapping Wu orders. The current KRG site rule accepts a currently valid marked site rather than proving continuity at one originally selected location. Wu's named nursery and cohort custody require their own stronger location record.

The inspected legacy battalion requires 1,000 manpower, 90 rifles, and one legacy clone equipment. Reusing that unit unchanged would retain the wrong finite-stock semantics. Its width, organisation, and strength serve only as an initial comparison profile. They do not establish approval or balance for the proposed finite unit.

The complete Kruger transfer function moves the same canonical character after input validation and role removal, preserves bounded history, and reconstructs the recipient. It does not create several simultaneous Krugers. Wu's multi-copy branch must supply its own distinct person identities while retaining the guarded transfer principle.

The current unit-family contract has thirteen runtime callbacks plus registration. Its natural-release recreate-and-delete exception explicitly cannot preserve every live equipment, manpower, experience, decoration, and order detail. Part 7 does not inherit that exception as permission for arbitrary Wu formation transfers. Older Kruger map wording with a smaller provider census or callback count remains historical.

The source-reading report and inventory give exact pinned paths, blob identities, and completed ranges. The full Kruger source-of-truth and core-runtime maps have now been read. Reading those maps does not establish that their linked historical audits were rerun or that every linked specification was read.
