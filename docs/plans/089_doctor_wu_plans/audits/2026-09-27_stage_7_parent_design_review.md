## Corrections made during this review

The first draft allowed the standing asylum institution to read as a delay before immediate safety. New Part 11 now permits provisional refuge at an already proven prepared destination without free scientific appointment or laboratory benefits.

The receiving-country section now explicitly requests factual food and reception publication from their owners. It cannot infer safety from an untracked state, and it does not permanently exclude a healthy destination merely because its facts were not yet initialized.

The nonhuman recipient paragraph now distinguishes limited medical access to owner-approved patients from hosting Wu, dissolving an independent actor, or annexing territory through a cure.

The stock example now uses a separate owner-approved material commitment. This avoids implying that a native biological payload is automatically interchangeable with clinical medicine. The conservation arithmetic is unchanged.
