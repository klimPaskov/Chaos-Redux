### Capacity and expertise are not copied

The same staff member cannot supply a full specialist benefit to two simultaneous jobs. A joint institution cannot count one machine twice or reserve a hospital place for two recipients. A qualified successor can maintain what that person actually knows. A stored archive, an unqualified copy, or an inherited technology does not become an active second scientist. Kruger remains a single canonical character with his own continuity.

### Emergency cooperation preserves obligations

The emergency overlay coordinates actual medical and technical work. It does not create medicine, reset a control deadline, provide extra receiving places, or pardon the cause of the incident. A ninety-day handover cannot fit a forty-five-day deadline without a genuine interim service or timely withdrawal.

The worked reception example allocates 5,000 proven places once among survivors, migrants, and new citizens. Another 500 new people remain unsettled with actual custody, food demand, and care.

### Authority reversal uses the actual applied amount

An early version of the new Authority paragraph could have allowed a clamped concession to be reversed at its full nominal value. The final draft removes only the amount actually applied for that concession. A value of 95 receiving a nominal ten-point concession rises to 100, and reversal of that same right removes five. It cannot fall to 90 through a ceiling exploit.

This was a correction to the new Part 10 draft. No inherited part or accepted setting was changed.

### Kruger's hidden value meanings remain owner-controlled

The source review showed that Independent Capacity participates in Kruger's resisting strength and cannot be treated as public institutional independence from its label. Part 10 therefore specifies factual case inputs and leaves their numerical mapping to an accepted owner bridge. It does not invent a hidden-meter change or repurpose legacy Event 089 callbacks.
