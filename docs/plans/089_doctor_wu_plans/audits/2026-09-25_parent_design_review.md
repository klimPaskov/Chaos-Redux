# Doctor Wu Stage 2 parent design review

## Review scope

This is the author's direct document review of Part 6 and the cumulative package. It is not an independent specialist review, an implementation audit, an HOI4 MCP result, or a live-game test. Part 6 is authored proposed design. The whole Event 089 specification remains incomplete.

The review compared the new content with the unchanged original brief, Parts 1–5, the completed supplied-source set, and the current owner evidence listed in the source register.

## Corrections made before packaging

**Four-cost admission.** An early drafting passage could have required both embedded handlers and an external service-team reservation on the same permanent or reanimated admission. That would introduce a fifth commitment beside manpower and three physical products. The final text includes the necessary embedded staff in the admission manpower. External team reservations apply to temporary courses on existing ordinary formations, whose enrolment has only packs and a service assignment as spendable types.

**Funding is not delivery.** The draft initially described prepaid service in terms that could imply that all future cover had already reached the formation. The final text distinguishes a fully funded allocation from its actual shipment or service location. Supported cover begins only after valid delivery. This keeps blockades, captured depots, and interrupted routes meaningful without charging for the same packs twice.

**Rounding.** Recovery and combined-role demand now round upward once for the complete funded cohort or formation to a multiple of five. This avoids per-battalion rounding differences becoming a split-and-merge advantage. A partial human care block still reserves one complete service block so an understrength unit cannot reinforce into unpaid cover.

**Prerequisite grouping.** H07 explicitly uses `(H02 OR C02) + M01`. The grouped form prevents the independent archive from accidentally requiring both full military and controlled-organism branches, or from omitting M01 on one route.

**Primary-lineage preservation.** C08 keeps one primary lineage and one bounded addition. It has no full-strength stack of two lineages. A failed addition can be removed without erasing the intact primary control standard.

**National presentation.** Course states and lineage variants use the existing institution and selected-force records. They do not create one national spirit per formation or evade the three-spirit limit by treating every project as a separate institution.

## Calculations actually performed

These calculations test the proposed tables, not the engine, balance, or medical reality.

An endurance care block funds 90 days of field service and 30 days of recovery at five packs per 30 days. Its initial bill is 20 packs. Ten blocks therefore require 200 packs.

A combat care block funds 60 days at ten packs per 30 days and 45 recovery days at five packs per 30 days. Ten blocks require 275 packs after rounding the complete allocation. One block requires 30 packs under the multiple-of-five rule. The allocation is not recalculated into a fresh cheaper purchase when its formation is split.

Five Command battalions require five body cohorts, 500 handler manpower, 450 infantry equipment, and 75 Control Service Packs for the first interval. Five Assault battalions require five cohorts, 750 handler manpower, and 150 Control Service Packs. These are four and three spendable types respectively.

A five-battalion Command-Guardian combination requires 95 packs per 30 days after rounding the base 75 multiplied by 1.25. This is a 20-pack increase in the actual displayed bill. It is not presented as exactly 25 percent after rounding.

The control-production table intentionally exposes an important tuning pressure. One continuous two-factory order produces 200 packs in 90 days. A full five-battalion Command formation needs 225 packs over those 90 days. The same single line can sustain Guardian's 150-pack demand, but cannot sustain Command, Hunter, Assault, or Carrier indefinitely on its own. Initial reserves, an additional real order, a smaller supported authorized force where the eventual provider permits it, or a supply partner are needed. The AI must calculate continuing capacity and may not infer sustainability from a paid first interval. This arithmetic finding is not a judgment that the proposed prices are balanced.

## Open evidence that blocks implementation completion

Exact selected-formation modifiers, persistence across split/merge, mixed-template eligibility, prepared-zone effects, conversion, and live force ownership changes have not been proved against the installed game. The design rejects a national-bonus or fresh-division fallback, so these need explicit engine evidence.

The finite shared clone mode is proposed and not approved or implemented. Part 7 must finish its person, production, transfer, and admission contract before it can be treated as an operational result. Existing legacy recurring income remains intact outside this proposal.

The new Scientific Research cluster has no reserved ID or current workbook change. Its proposed one-member behavior must be checked against the live registry. Old Event 089 queue, history, and origin-version compatibility is likewise unimplemented.

Carrier has no implemented native consumer. The actual operation's cohort fate, reservation, invalidation, cancellation, and concurrent outcome behavior remain evidence requirements. Reading the native owner does not prove those new callbacks exist.

No quantitative cross-owner comparison establishes Wu's promised medical leadership or the intended military strengths. In particular, the current Kruger conventional Biomedical package is already powerful. A later comparison must preserve its accepted full-strength API results and evaluate Wu's distinct coverage, care, and cure benefits without weakening another owner to make the comparison favorable.

No models, counters, service icons, reports, audio sources, native renders, or live animations were produced. Unit-specific selection audio and exact model/action consumers require proof. No specialist has independently audited the specification.

## Stopping point

Part 6 ends at `## What the institute carries into replication`. It is not cut off mid-section. This stage does not replace the next research family or four evolutions with a shorter summary. The current resume packet names Part 7 and retains the unresolved owner dependencies.
