## Open owner interfaces

The specification requires supported agreement generations, actual principal transfers, exact material custody, multi-leg medical routes, care-preserving provider changes, finite pre-settlement movement, and attributable evidence. Where current owners do not expose them, their design is proposed and implementation remains blocked pending the explicit amendment. The requested gameplay has not been silently replaced with a weaker generic reward.
