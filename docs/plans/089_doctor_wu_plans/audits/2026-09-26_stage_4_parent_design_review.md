# Doctor Wu Stage 4 parent design review

Date: 26 September 2026. Stage: DW-089-S4.

This review was performed by the authoring parent in this task. It is not an independent specialist audit. Part 8 is written through `What designed life leaves for the biological contest`, with fifteen projects and twenty named AI scenarios. The whole event remains unfinished.

## Design findings resolved in the new draft

The first project map covered the main designed-life families but did not yet give anti-aging and appetite treatment their own developed uses. The original brief explicitly includes those subjects. L14 and L15 now give them distinct medical prerequisites, bounded recipients, treatment and follow-up, interruption and political limits. Earlier regeneration and hibernation projects were retained instead of recreated.

The assault draft could be read as granting a permanent fort advantage and then granting the same advantage again through preparation. The current text places that advantage only in the paid prepared-assault window. Ordinary creature strengths and vulnerabilities remain distinct. Loss of the approach ends the opportunity, not the existing formation.

Camouflage fitting initially risked looking like a fifth commitment if the formation itself was counted as another payment. The current fitting contract keeps the recipient formation available for ordinary use. The four explicit cost types fund the course. A separate action that actually ties down a formation must count that commitment in its own budget.

Nonhuman clinical access is now explicitly separated from the arrival-host classifier. A compatible nonhuman actor may receive a foreign medical service under its owner contract. That does not make it a valid resident host for the original Doctor Wu arrival.

These are corrections to Part 8 before delivery. Parts 1–7 were not rewritten.

## Accounting and source-owner review

Living assemblies and actual animals have separate physical identities from their design. Human handlers remain people, while animal and colony losses do not become human Deaths. The draft does not allow a blueprint, captured empty habitat, or service renewal to generate usable stock. Custody follows real surviving output.

A fixed filter protects compatible incoming exposure. A cleanup colony works on an existing compatible burden. Their effects cannot be substituted for each other or repeatedly subtracted from already reduced data. The disease owner accepts the result and retains phase, mortality, other source contributions, and historical evidence. The new living-service adapters remain proposed.

Husbandry is a real obligation. The draft deliberately does not invent a conversion between Food Security and physical feed. An exact famine-owned quote and demand contract is required before implementing that payment. Previously funded care must not be billed again merely because the food owner also records demand. This is an unresolved shared interface, not a completed food economy.

Agricultural medicine acts on a diagnosed biological loss within production. Its worked example leaves transport and extraction losses unchanged. Famine continues to own food reserves, security, relief access and famine mortality. The receiving state must supply positive current food-safety evidence before L13 admits people.

The reconstruction design gives survivors, migrants and new citizens one common set of places. Its 5,000-place example cannot become 15,000 places by assigning one pool to each source. A real repair and current housing, food, clinical and route constraints bound capacity. The native construction budget must be coupled to the same commitment, with no free reward or second hidden factory bill.

Part 7 remains the sole proposed finite production and first-settlement basis. L13 adds a civilian programme and destination rules. It does not create another birth reward. A person who is first settled cannot become unsettled again after death or transfer. Specialist and continuity appointments use existing counted people. The new pre-settlement food projection requires the shared finite owner and famine owner to agree on exactly when external demand becomes normal population demand.

The public technology API keeps learned neutral knowledge and provenance separate from Kruger project stages and physical operation. L11's machinery uses actual teams and compatible knowledge. It does not grant Kruger's entire portfolio, add a second research slot, choose another xenobiological control, or create an attack payload.

Black Plague and Zombie Outbreak retain their independent actors and their own containment, defeat and aftermath endpoints. The current zombie documentation's older evacuation description is not treated as permission to perform two population transfers. The actual migration adapter path still needs implementation review.

## Local checks performed

The included [fixture script](2026-09-26_stage_4_design_fixture_checks.py) ran successfully and wrote [91 passing symbolic checks](2026-09-26_stage_4_design_fixture_results.json). The checks exercise authored arithmetic, 29 admission-cost inventories, stock and assignment uniqueness, bounded service work, partial observations, benefit non-compounding, husbandry rounding, shared receiving places and a finite-person accounting example.

The test run initially rejected the AI scenario table because its parser expected an ID-only first cell. The actual table includes the scenario name after each ID. The parser was corrected to recognize the stable ID prefix, and the full script was rerun. This was a test-parser correction, not a gameplay defect or an engine test.

The person fixture begins with 1,000 living unsettled people. It settles 600 into military manpower and 250 into civilian reception, leaving 150 unsettled. Five specialist appointments among those civilians add no people. Ten deaths before settlement leave 140 unsettled, 850 historically first-settled and ten pre-settlement deaths. A later death among the settled group leaves the historical first-settlement count at 850. It cannot reopen an output entitlement.

The production timing check preserves the normal Part 7 reference of 270 production and maturation days plus 90 adult-orientation days before civilian reception. The machinery timing fixture applies a four-thirds work rate once, giving a three-quarter duration, rather than compounding it with another overlapping service bonus.

These are small authored models. They do not execute Clausewitz, native AI, unit combat, a save file, an actual food conversion, or any biological process. Passing them does not prove game balance or engine safety. The scenario matrix expresses intended AI ordering and refusals, not measured probabilities.

File-preservation, local-link, heading, and ZIP checks are recorded separately by the package integrity check. The original brief and seven earlier specification parts remain unchanged. Dated earlier reports retain their original evidence boundaries. Only README, the current index, and the current resume change among existing Stage 3 files, with the old resume copied into a dated snapshot.
