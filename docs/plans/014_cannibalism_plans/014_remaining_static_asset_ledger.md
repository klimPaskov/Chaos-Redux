# Event 014 Remaining Static Asset Ledger

## Status and ownership

This is the frozen production ledger for Event 014 static assets that are already registered by live gameplay but do not yet have a runtime DDS. It excludes the separately owned 108 unified focus icons, 26 mechanic-window statics, 12 frame-led GUI animations, two animated leader portraits, 195 flags, and 18 achievement triplets.

The static-asset worker owns source art, processed PNGs, runtime DDS files, manifests, contact sheets, and a GFX handoff for every item below. It must not edit gameplay, localisation, `.gfx`, `.gui`, flags, focus icons, animated assets, portraits, or achievements.

## Production contract

- Use generated fictional source art. Never use real atrocity photographs or identifiable victims.
- Important compositions must be visibly gore-heavy and physical, with blood, butchered military spaces, damaged bodies or severed material where the target size permits.
- Use 1930s to 1940s clothing, weapons, vehicles, buildings, field equipment, prison equipment, and photographic treatment.
- Do not generate readable text, modern equipment, real political symbols, living Indigenous claims, or borrowed sacred motifs.
- Every listed base asset needs its own composition. A category, decision, idea, report, focus, achievement, or GUI composition may not be reused as another asset type.
- Decision and category icons are 32 by 32. Category panels are 114 by 101. Idea and dynamic-modifier icons are 64 by 64. Report-event cards are 210 by 176.
- Runtime DDS files must match the working Event 014 DDS conventions, preserve required alpha, and contain one mip level.
- The final handoff must prove exact path coverage, dimensions, decoded DDS readability, source-to-runtime mapping, and nonduplicate visual composition.
