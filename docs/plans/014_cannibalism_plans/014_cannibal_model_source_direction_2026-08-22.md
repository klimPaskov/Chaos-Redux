## Source rule

Every Event 014 unit model begins with separately researched modern visual artwork suited to that formation's body type, pose, silhouette, and weapon role, or with an explicitly identified user-supplied modern artwork source. Suitable families are game concept art, game unit or character art, tabletop or miniature concept art, fantasy or horror illustration, and professional character design sheets.

Archival photographs, museum paintings or drawings, historical plates, antiquities, archaeological images, and documentary references are excluded. The previous archival source batch is rejected and may not become an ImageGen or Meshy input.

The untouched source, source page, creator or publisher, license or usage status, access date, dimensions, and SHA-256 remain archived in the model job. Official artist, studio, publisher, portfolio, or asset pages and explicitly reusable artwork are preferred. Copyrighted game or commercial artwork without reusable licensing may be retained only as attributed visual-research evidence and must be labelled `reference-only`, never `licensed`.

ImageGen may be used only for faithful enhancement of the selected artwork, such as resolution improvement, alpha extraction, background cleanup, edge repair, padding, or removal of non-subject presentation noise. It must preserve the source subject's identity, pose, silhouette, equipment function, proportions, and material cues without redesigning, re-costuming, tracing into a new character, or inventing missing requirements.

Only the approved faithful input may be submitted to Meshy 7. The Internet or user-supplied source and its enhancement record must remain archived beside the input, and no substantially redesigned or generated-from-scratch descendant may become a provider input.

## Cultural boundary

All bone construction, paint geometry, trophies, clothing combinations, and weapon decoration are fictional and culture-neutral.

No model may copy living Indigenous, African, Pacific, religious, national, or ceremonial regalia, sacred symbols, body-paint traditions, ritual objects, or authenticity claims.

## Superseded work

The earlier generated-from-scratch Event 014 model references, substantially redesigned ImageGen inputs, Meshy candidates, rigs, and action previews are rejected evidence and must not be exported, synchronized, registered, or wired into runtime consumers.

The generated-from-scratch Bone Riders image is also rejected evidence and must not be sent to Meshy.

The museum, public-domain archive, historical drawing, antiquity, and documentary source batch researched before the modern-artwork correction is rejected evidence. Those images and their processed derivatives may remain in audit history, but they must be marked superseded and may not guide ImageGen, enter `refs/original/meshy_input.png`, or be submitted to Meshy.
