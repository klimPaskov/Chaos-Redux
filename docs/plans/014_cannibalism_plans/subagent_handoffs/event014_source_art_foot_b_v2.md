## Mandatory acceptance gate

An accepted source must be one actual Internet artwork or painted miniature/product image showing one coherent full-body subject with all essential anatomy and role weapons visible.

The subject must be a living, fleshy, feral fictional cannibal with conspicuous aggressive face/body paint or equivalent high-contrast markings, exposed flesh, rough hides or scavenged cloth, and skull/bone armor, helmet, or trophies.

The source must be culturally unanchored fictional horror/fantasy/tabletop design and must not claim or directly borrow living Indigenous, African, Polynesian, Mesoamerican, Aztec, Mayan, Wagadu, or other living cultural traditions or sacred motifs.

Island Reavers additionally require a readable coastal or boarding silhouette, harpoon or long spear, secondary axe or cleaver, and rope/bone identity.

Siege Eaters additionally require a massive living fleshy breacher with skull/bone armor and a clearly two-handed maul, sledge, ram, club, or similarly oversized blunt weapon.

March Predation additionally requires a light fast living runner or skirmisher with a visible bow, quiver or arrow bundle, and visible secondary machete or knife.

Blood, a red sculpt finish, or painted product color does not by itself prove deliberate body/face paint. Grayscale sculpt renders do not satisfy the paint gate. A near-match cannot be repaired by ImageGen prompt invention, compositing, or redesign.

Historical, archival, museum, archaeological, ethnographic, documentary, reenactment, modern tourism, recognizable modern-character, undead, skeleton, knightly, polished-armored, goblin, and generic-fantasy substitutes were rejected.

## Decision summary

`cannibal_island_reavers` is `blocked_exact_gate_unmet`. Bestiarum's painted Fleshmad guide is the closest living painted cannibal source, but the figure has no readable harpoon or coastal boarding weapon and the finish does not establish conspicuous deliberate body/face paint.

`cannibal_siege_eaters` is `blocked_exact_gate_unmet`. Tooth Wu's colored cannibal concepts supply living fleshy painted bodies and crude melee identity, but their cleavers or whips are not a two-handed maul or sledge and they do not provide the required massive skull/bone-armored breacher silhouette.
