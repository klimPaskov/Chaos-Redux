## Recommended final packages

### 1. Cannibal islands reveal

- Title: `The Silent Islands`
- Quote: `Hell is empty, and all the devils are here.`
- Speaker/source: Ariel, William Shakespeare, *The Tempest*, Act 1, Scene 2
- Date: c. 1610-1611
- Source URL: https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/the-tempest/read/1/2/
- Attribution confidence: High
- Copyright note: Public domain
- Button remark: `Suspend all shore leave.`
- Fit note: Best fit for an island-and-shipborne public reveal that is horrifying but still uncertain.
- Backup set: `No Harbor Safe` / `Water, water, every where, / Nor any drop to drink.` / `No landing parties.`

### 2. Hannibal network reveal

- Title: `Hannibal Ad Portas`
- Quote: `...cannot be satisfied, but gathereth unto him all nations, and heapeth unto him all people:`
- Speaker/source: Habakkuk 2:5, King James Version
- Date: Biblical text; KJV 1611
- Source URL: https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Habakkuk+2%3A5&version=KJV
- Attribution confidence: High
- Copyright note: Public domain
- Button remark: `The orders were real.`
- Fit note: Best fit when Hannibal is publicly revealed as an organizer whose appetite has become imperial and multinational.
- Title allusion source: https://atlas.perseus.tufts.edu/dictionaries/entry/urn%3Acite2%3Ascaife-viewer%3Adictionary-entries.atlas_v1%3Alat.ls.perseus-eng2-n37124/

- Backup set: `The Hungry Command` / `Yond Cassius has a lean and hungry look. He thinks too much. Such men are dangerous.` / `So there was a master.`
