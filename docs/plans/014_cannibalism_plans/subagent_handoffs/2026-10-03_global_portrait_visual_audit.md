## Result

The refreshed global contact sheet was reviewed at the requested 156x210 native tile size. The seven regional groups each contain eight readable bald adult male faces. Face shapes, expressions, visible scars and grime, gestures, props, clothing, and settings vary across the set. No exact repeated face was apparent. The closest visual resemblance is Europe CBA versus Europe CBF: both use a pale, gaunt, bald, scarred warlord look, while their visible face presentation differs. Europe CBA is more strongly angled, with an asymmetric squint and open mouth; Europe CBF faces more forward, with a different gaze and fuller cheek and jaw line. I do not treat this pair as a confirmed duplicate and do not recommend a face edit from the current evidence.

One visible brief concern remains in Asia CBD. Its torn pages carry repeated small handwriting-like marks. No words are legible at native size, but the marks read as writing. If the accepted no-text rule covers simulated writing on props, the minimal correction is to blank only the page surfaces and preserve the face, pose, torn-paper prop, clothing, and background.

The portraits use a more cinematic, textured, full-scene treatment than the quieter installed-vanilla leader examples. Their 156x210 framing and face readability fit the same leader-portrait surface. The accepted brief calls for distinct props and backdrops, so this consistent scene treatment is recorded as a style difference rather than a defect.

No recognizable real-person or actor likeness was apparent in the visual review. No external identity search was performed. I saw no obvious animal, supernatural, prison, or cultural-regalia elements. The only text-like content noticed is the Asia CBD page writing described above.
