# Event014 Regional Portrait Consumption Audit

Audit date: 2026-10-03. Scope: read-only inspection of the 56 current regional warlord DDS files, 64 stable portrait sprite identifiers, their dynamic consumer, source/crop evidence, and the accepted Part 10 portrait requirements. The parent-owned eight-European-candidate tranche is separate; this audit created no candidates and did not change runtime portrait files or wiring.

## Result

The current implementation has 64 stable warlord portrait sprite identifiers that resolve to 56 distinct texture paths: eight unsuffixed base identifiers and their eight `_europe` identifiers share the same European texture, while the remaining 48 regional identifiers each use their own file. All 56 paths exist, all 56 DDS files have distinct file hashes and decoded-pixel hashes, and all 56 DDS hashes match the corresponding row in the dated 2026-08-26 runtime hash ledger. The earlier “16 active total” claim came from treating the recovered HATE source-archive subset as the live texture set; it does not describe current wiring. No alias-count defect or runtime wiring change is indicated.

The complete slot-region-to-sprite-to-DDS matrix appears below. The current GFX rows cover Europe, Asia, Africa, Middle East, North America, South America, and Oceania for each slot CBA-CBH. `common/scripted_effects/014_cannibalism_effects.txt:4730-4738` builds the portrait identifier from the slot and region getters, while `common/scripted_localisation/014_cannibalism_scripted_localisation.txt:407-426` maps each supported slot and region token. `common/characters/014_cannibalism_characters.txt` defines Hannibal and Wendigo characters; the local warlords are created dynamically rather than through eight static character entries.

These are 56 unique source-placeholder pixel sets, not 56 accepted final portraits. The native-size review shows substantial mismatch with the accepted fictional warlord final brief: four Diablo III references are visibly female-presenting; several other Diablo III and Elden Ring rows are demonic, undead, bestial, or otherwise supernatural; many entries show full figures, poster/action scenes, headgear, or covered scalps; and the HATE miniatures retain elaborate skull, horn, mask, and trophy styling. The source families also visibly retain their Diablo III and Elden Ring character-design identities. No clear Hannibal likeness or prison/cell setting was visible in the 56-image sheet.

All 56 remain pending as source placeholders. The 16 HATE packages now present under `docs/assets/portraits/014_cannibalism/` are exact restorations of their previously selected originals, historical crops, processed PNGs, and runtime DDS files. Their co-located records explicitly say `source_placeholder`, `styled_final: not_supplied_or_accepted`, and `unresolved_final_rights`. The other 40 D/E runtime DDS files have historical source names, URLs, and hashes in the dated handoff, but their cited source packages, originals, crops, processed PNGs, source-page archives, and per-row contracts are absent from the current checkout. The August license handoff records all 56 source families as copyrighted-reference-only with no permissive redistribution basis; a current Creative Uncut information page likewise says its video-game artwork remains copyrighted by the respective game companies. Neither prior user authorization nor the dated handoff is a rights-holder grant.

The parent’s accepted native fictional candidate route for eight European originals remains independent of this audit. No candidate is installed or treated as approved here, and all existing selected source/placeholders and their GFX bindings remain intact pending parent review.
