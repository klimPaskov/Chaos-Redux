## Exact gate results

`cannibal_network_cadre` requires one living conspicuously painted culturally neutral cannibal archer with a visible bow, quiver, and bone kit.

`cannibal_bone_riders` requires one single artwork showing a living painted cannibal rider on a living horse in bone barding, visibly using or holding a sling with a stone pouch, with no spear, lance, or sword substituting the sling.

The strongest network candidates each fail at least one non-negotiable gate: QooApp Archery Clash is a painted close-up without quiver/full bone kit; Narrow One has bow/bone armor but no conspicuous paint or clear independent quiver; NEN CHANG has paint and arrows but the bow is cropped and the full bone kit is absent; AlphaCoders Bone-Helm Huntress has bow/skull styling but no conspicuous paint or quiver; Minmax has bone/tribal styling but no clear quiver and is culturally anchored; and Wagadu Black Ancestor has paint/bow/bone motifs but arrows are held rather than quivered and the character is a culturally anchored named identity.

The strongest Bone Riders candidates separate the required components: Tatyana Kupriyanova's living painted rider has two spears and no sling; Diablo IV Crystal and Bone is riderless horse armor; Wargames Atlantic is undead skeleton cavalry with a sword; Bloodstock is horse tack without a rider; the Diablo mount sheet is a multi-view/rider-incomplete game reference; Renato Fujie's sling concept is an on-foot sketch sheet; Alicia Online is riderless horse armor; and Bestiarum's otherwise strong living Man Eater is mounted on a pig/Manswine and carries melee weapons rather than a sling.

Hot Goblin's Cannibal Tribe Archer was not promoted as an exact source because the storefront was unavailable behind 403/maintenance and prior listing evidence identifies its colored reference as AI-created, which fails the actual-artwork-only and NoAI gates.
