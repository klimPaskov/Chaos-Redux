# Event 014 portrait license research handoff

date: 2026-08-26
owner: portrait-production subagent
scope: Internet-source and redistribution-license audit for the 56 Event 014 warlord portrait slots; no runtime asset or 3D/model changes

## Decision

No complete 56-slot replacement set is eligible for installation at this time. The current 56 runtime DDS files remain source placeholders backed by modern fictional game/concept-art references, and they remain marked `source_placeholder_installed_user_final_pending` with `copyrighted_reference_only_user_authorized` rights. No runtime portrait file, portrait alias, Hannibal file, or model file was changed by this audit.

The strongest follow-up candidates are Thomas Novosel's 66-portrait and 60-portrait grunge packs because each has enough distinct hand-drawn portraits, an explicit commercial-project permission after purchase, and a no-generative-AI statement. They still require user purchase or a supplied licensed archive, and the available preview evidence does not establish the requested distribution of tribal, feral, cannibal, ritual-tool, and action silhouettes across all 56 slots. They are therefore conditional leads, not installed replacements.

## Acceptance gates

An installed replacement must pass all four gates: the original page must identify the creator and an explicit CC0, CC-BY, or equivalent commercial redistribution permission; the individual source files must be obtainable and archived with source-page and checksum evidence; the artwork must be hand-made or otherwise comply with the no-ImageGen/no-photo/no-3D brief; and a manual review must confirm 56 distinct, readable 156x210 crops with the required feral or ritual differentiation.

A paywalled pack is not treated as acquired merely because its landing page is public. Until the user purchases or supplies it, no source archive, crop, PNG, DDS, alias, or manifest replacement is authorized. A license that allows commercial use does not by itself prove that the visual set satisfies the Event 014 brief.

## Follow-up search and exact blockers

### Jeff Preston: 60 Terrible Character Portraits

[The OpenGameArt source page](https://opengameart.org/content/60-terrible-character-portraits) identifies Jeff Preston as the artist, classifies the work as concept art, publishes CC-BY 3.0, and describes the drawings as hand-drawn. The downloadable [TCP.zip](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/TCP.zip) archive contains an internal CC-BY 3.0 license notice, the artist and coordinator readme, and 108 JPEG files across the advertised and stretch-goal categories. An audit-only download measured 21655015 bytes with SHA-256 `510023445589fda68579f7a96833ba62cf245fb94cd2e1301762c215c7315825`; no copy or discarded gallery was retained under the repository.

The archive is a genuine legal source lead: the 108 images are hand-drawn black-and-white concept portraits, not archival photographs, ImageGen output, or 3D renders. The source images are high enough resolution to be center-cropped to the 156x210 ratio and resized without stretching, using side or vertical crops as needed. A test selection of 56 unique crops was audited from Scary 1-7, Troll 1-9, Manimal 1-9, Vampire 1-5, Zombie 1-7, Armored 1-6, Pirate 1-7, and Dwarf 1-6; the temporary test crops were not installed or retained.

The visual gate fails for Event 014. The selected portraits are readable and disturbing in places, but they are almost entirely static headshots, with no consistent tribal or cannibal identity, no hand-held ritual actions, and only limited skull or mask presence with no reliable bone-paint or ritual-tool coverage. The set also mixes ordinary human, pirate, dwarf, vampire, and zombie faces, so choosing 56 rows would be a generic horror substitution rather than the requested regional warlord art. This source is therefore `LEGAL_GATE_PASS; VISUAL_GATE_BLOCKED` and was not installed.

### DieselShot: Fantasy Portrait & Bust Stockart Megapack

[DieselShot's source page](https://dieselshot.itch.io/fantasy-portrait-bust-stockart-megapack) describes over 85 black-and-white fantasy busts and portraits with sepia and transparent-background variants, including gobbos, 11 orcs, demonborn, dragonborn, gnolls, a cultist, a necromancer, tattooed figures, a cloaked druid, and an orc with an animal-skull adornment. The page states that purchase grants permanent, unlimited non-exclusive commercial and personal rights to the paying individual or organization, requires attribution to Patrice Long, prohibits use in generative-AI works or products promoting real-world hatred, and states no generative AI was used. The listed archive is 195 MB after a $20 minimum purchase.

This is the strongest visual lead found in the follow-up search because the public preview and item list include horns, animal skull adornment, masks, tattoos, cultic silhouettes, orcs, gobbos, and gnolls in one consistent hand-drawn fantasy family. It remains `CONDITIONAL` rather than installed: the archive has not been acquired, the 85-plus individual files have not been reviewed or hashed, and the landing page does not explicitly state whether embedding modified files in a redistributed mod is permitted beyond the purchaser's project-use grant. User purchase or a direct written redistribution clarification is required before any crop or runtime change.

### Crimson Delight Games and Penflower Ink cross-check

[Crimson Delight Games' 300-plus pack](https://crimsondelightgames.itch.io/crimsondelightgames-medieval-and-fantasy-portrait-packs) has 47 characters and over 300 variations, but its own previews show many variations are palette or costume changes rather than 56 genuinely distinct subjects, and its terms expressly forbid re-selling, re-packaging, re-distributing, or sub-licensing the assets. It therefore cannot supply this mod without separate permission.

[Penflower Ink's 50-NPC pack](https://penflower-ink.itch.io/50-fantasy-npcs), [30-character pack](https://penflower-ink.itch.io/30-fantasy-rpg-character-art-assets), and two six-character packs ([first](https://penflower-ink.itch.io/fantasy-adventurer-character-art-assets), [second](https://penflower-ink.itch.io/six-more-fantasy-adventurer-art-assets)) are hand-drawn and permit commercial projects with attribution, but they are paywalled, generic adventurer/NPC art rather than a verified feral cannibal set, and their terms prohibit resale while restricting products that promote violence toward specific ethnicities or marginalized groups. They remain visual and redistribution clarification leads only.
