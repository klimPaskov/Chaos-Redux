# Event 014 modern model-source adaptation brief

Date: 2026-08-22.

This brief translates three modern portfolio artworks into substantially original ImageGen direction for later parent review.

The source images are attributed visual research only and are `reference-only`, `not licensed`, and not cleared for direct redistribution, runtime use, or provider submission.

## Global adaptation boundary

Create one complete full-body, single-subject model-sheet image per job with the entire figure, hands, feet, and primary weapon visible.

Use a neutral light-gray or transparent presentation background with no scenery, text, logo, watermark, base, extra figures, or decorative frame.

Retain only broad pose, silhouette, equipment function, and material logic from the attributed source.

Do not reproduce a source character's name, face, identity, species, helmet or mask, exact costume, exact armor layout, distinctive silhouette marks, proprietary symbols, logos, palette, background, weapon geometry, or traced pose.

Do not reference or copy any of the user's attachments.

Do not open, use, trace, submit, or adapt `C:/Users/klimp/AppData/Local/Temp/codex-clipboard-59672c99-c6a5-4728-9ab5-71e311186bd4.png`.

Keep all bone construction, paint geometry, trophies, clothing combinations, and weapon decoration fictional and culture-neutral.

Do not use identifiable living Indigenous regalia, sacred motifs, religious ceremonial objects, national symbols, or culture-specific paint.

Use grounded dark low-saturation material logic suitable for an HOI4 3D unit model, with readable value separation between skin, bone, cloth, leather, scrap metal, and weapon.

The resulting adaptation must be a new design that does not claim to be the source artist's work or a licensed derivative.
