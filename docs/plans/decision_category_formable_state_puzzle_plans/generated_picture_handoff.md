## Canvas and format checks

Every ImageGen master is `1330x1182` RGB and is retained without destructive overwrite.

Every processed preview is `114x101` RGBA and was resized with Lanczos filtering from its matching master.

Every final DDS is `114x101`, 46,184 bytes, one-level uncompressed BGRA with a 128-byte header, DDS magic, header size 124, pixel-format size 32, flags 65, fourCC 0, 32-bit BGRA masks `0x00FF0000/0x0000FF00/0x000000FF/0xFF000000`, texture caps `0x1000`, and no mipmaps.

All six decoded DDS alpha channels are opaque (`255..255`) because decision-category pictures are painted full-canvas surfaces.

All six DDS payloads were decoded as BGRA and matched their processed PNG pixel bytes exactly.

The review-only six-up contact sheet is `docs/assets/decision_category_formable_state_puzzle/contact_sheet.png` and is not a runtime asset.
