## NC14 supplemental choices

The proposed shared charter-breach normalization is accepted.
The default actual breach is Cohesion minus 10 and Legitimacy minus 8 once, measured after clamping and attributed to one exact promise.
The former Union phrase implying at least ten Legitimacy loss is replaced, not added as another penalty.

The proposed extra Legitimacy minus 4 for an overdue administrative review with no material breach is rejected.
The accepted default instead suspends the specific dependent institutional or cooperation benefit, with no additional generic meter loss.
That is a concrete finite consequence and preserves the distinction between unfinished administration and repudiating an accepted promise.
Where a P program already states its own specific review loss, that original contract remains authoritative.
This choice does not waive the review, its price, its actual work or any true breach consequence.

The sustainable Union coordination value is accepted at 3 percent, replacing the temporary 5 percent emergency version in the same approved modifier and spirit slot.
The two versions never stack.
If the exact native scope requires the documented single-program cost binding, the same five-to-three replacement applies to that binding.

The undefined Horde legitimacy drain is replaced with an explicit unresolved tribute or emergency-mandate status.
It blocks dependent political benefit and further endorsement until the actual obligation is settled.
An actual expired promise repudiated by unilateral continuation uses the same ordinary breach owner.
This creates no unexplained recurring drain or extra reward for renegotiating the same arrears.

The parent's default postwar table groups each GP's review within one complete A20 or A21 profile.
It preserves each branch's real political objectives, and does not require a separate paid action for every component of the same national settlement.
A more specific original P contract still governs the same promise.
