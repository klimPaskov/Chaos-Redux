## Numbered findings and closure evidence

### NC01. First-charter dependency cycle and early interface contract

**Disposition: Closed.**

Part 03 sections 5.2 and 5.3 make the free provisional compact and funded civilian settlement available before a permanent charter, extra state, sponsor or offensive-readiness requirement.
The compact supplies one +5 Cohesion and +2 Legitimacy result.
One quoted 35 Political Power base payment funds the actual representation, livelihoods and guarantee checkpoints at the earliest funded days 30, 60 and 90.
The stage budgets are bounded, the ordinary first-council credit is inside the first stage, and no stage or compact resets after interruption.

The conservative 35 and 25 starting boundary reaches 50 and 50 after the compact and three stages.
The actual lowest coupled formula begins at Cohesion 40 and reaches 55 and 50 on the same path.
Both satisfy the civilian charter's political requirement without conquest or arrears rewards.
The assembly timer starts only when the player convenes it, allowing the subsequent thirty-day hold.
Delayed funding and a failed founding mission retain ordinary civilian and assembly recovery without renewing the opening grant.

Parts 04, 08, 10 and 11 carry the same bridge, fee ownership and timing.
Part 08 resolves the main interface to five ordinary primary actions plus at most one eligible emergency or status row.
The coding, decision and goal handoffs preserve that rule.
F52 through F59 and the expanded V01 provide concrete later boundary fixtures.

### NC02. Regional completion thresholds reward delayed success

**Disposition: Closed.**

Part 04 lowers full P02 Cohesion to 60, full P07 Legitimacy to 60 and full P09 Legitimacy to 60.
Merit training, actual equipped formations, valid mandates, reciprocal agreements, transport work and sustained service remain required.
Their finite completion budgets and lower partial outcomes remain distinct.
A player need not delay a limited result merely to claim its reward above ordinary passive recovery's ceiling.

Part 05 gives all basic GP08 durable independence, autonomy and refuge outcomes the 60 and 60 political baseline alongside their actual receiving, service and protection conditions.
Part 11 explicitly requires a generic one-state H12 host without external contact to reach that regional endpoint through real civilian, economic and service work.
The achievement prompt's durable-host path uses the same 60 and 60 baseline and adds its own real time and readiness objectives.
No accepted P01 through P14 proposal is removed, deferred or turned into an unsupported independent full tree.

### NC03. National high-meter gates need their own finite political proof

**Disposition: Closed.**

Part 05's finite constitutional endorsement table gives each demanding national branch its own bounded path from the ordinary baseline to its exact final political gate.
The final constitutional mission explicitly starts at that baseline once its other start requirements pass.
The higher meter is a final formation requirement, so the funded mission does not require its own later reward before it can begin.

Endorsement requires every actual mandate, physical objective, service obligation and compliance interval, with no active material breach.
It credits only the missing gap, at most ten points per meter, includes prior same-cause gains and is consumed once per project across variants and later restoration.
GP05 retains its lower Cohesion 55 gate and can earn only the needed Legitimacy gap from 60 to 65.
GP07 and GP08 receive no unnecessary endorsement increase.
No extra payment, mission, building, force or formation reward is introduced.

The coding and decision prompts preserve the start-versus-formation distinction, and Part 11 requires branch-by-branch before-and-after verification plus a variant-switch replay case.
The written national route gate no longer depends on unrelated conquest, manufactured late payment or withholding an earlier earned reward.

### NC04. Service arrears can become a repeatable positive reward

**Disposition: Closed.**

Part 03 section 6 records the actual clamped loss for each missed installment and permits repair only up to that same receipt's still-unrecovered loss.
A nominal penalty that could not reduce the meter creates no larger recoverable amount.
Earlier repair, renegotiation and positive credits from any other source consume the corresponding outstanding recovery balance.
The latter accounting does not withhold an earned positive credit, but prevents the same loss from authorizing another credit later.

The former net +4 Cohesion and +1 Legitimacy delinquency loop is removed.
F60 and F61 cover repeated intentional lateness and near-zero clamping.
Part 05's generic breach repair and the decision prompt use the same actual-loss ceiling, while specific regional first-peace contracts retain their explicit original outcomes.

### NC05. The one-state arsenal path needs preflight proof

**Disposition: Closed.**

Parts 01, 02 and 03 require an actual repairable military factory, a genuinely available factory slot or a verified available native civilian-to-military conversion route before an opening state can be selected.
A hoped-for arms supplier, later conquest, invented slot or unverified conversion is insufficient.
Part 03 distinguishes the free-slot bridge from a real conversion and preserves the native conversion's actual cost and duration.
The converted factory is the result of that route and does not also receive a second workshop factory.

The administrative minimum remains ninety days.
Existing damaged military capacity receives repair or organization, with no duplicate building.
F62 and F63 and the no-industry V01 explicitly cover invalid full-slot states and the conversion-only case.
The specification now has a finite production path or rejects the opening before mutation.
Actual installed conversion semantics remain a required implementation proof, not an assumed completed test.
