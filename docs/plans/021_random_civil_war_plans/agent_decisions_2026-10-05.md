## D37. Readable exposure and containment

The apply helper establishes exposure without incrementing it.
Only an unanswered live source advances the stage on an already-due bounded country review.
Monitoring and mediation lower the stage once and hold advancement for their existing timed response periods.
Mobilizing adds eight pressure points, while the nearby-conflict flag activates the existing four-point pressure and 60-ticket targeting inputs.
Cleanup converts that flag to a 180-day aftermath and removes the stage and humanitarian condition.
Humanitarian need follows territorial disruption at the source and is relieved separately from military support.
This fixes containment worsening exposure and fulfills the Evolution II readable-risk requirement without forcing a neighbour into war.

## D38. Campaign choices and high-chaos mobilization

One paid adaptation report offers supply escorts, prepared defenses or mobile columns for 90 days.
Each exchanges a useful military benefit for a military penalty, with training experience and rifles spent before the choice.
A once-per-crisis 60-day mobilization at Totalen Chaos or World Collapse costs arms and support equipment, strains civilian work and leaves recurrence memory.
The final four sacrifices are rifles, support equipment, temporary stability and consumer goods requirements.
There is no free division or equipment grant and no Event 006 identity change.
The choices cover the accepted operational-adaptation requirement with three distinct campaign plans rather than separate decisions for every terrain.
The general supply plan does not claim to simulate a particular railway.

## D40. One response slot
