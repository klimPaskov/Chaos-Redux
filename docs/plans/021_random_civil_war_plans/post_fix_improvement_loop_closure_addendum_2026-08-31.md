## Current playable completeness findings

### Decisions and missions

The standard decision category contains the accepted opening, exposure, settlement, reconstruction, and prevention actions without a custom scripted GUI.

The current opening layer includes securing arsenals, defending the capital, reviewing loyalty, and seizing the priority-front depot.

The exposure layer separates civilian relief from armed support and includes border monitoring, government support, opposition support, and mediation.

The three accepted missions remain distinct:

- Hold the capital.
- Secure the rail spine.
- Hold the settlement terms.

The rail mission checks the exact stored mission state, ownership and control, opening-core membership, supply, and infrastructure.

The depot action marks the exact priority-front state and applies its timed national effect rather than using an abstract country-only receipt.

Mission success and timeout paths call distinct effects.

### Exposure and sponsorship

Exposure records a source and channel but does not itself roll or grant aid.

Neighbor exposure is bounded to countries adjacent to an active side.

Civilian relief is separate from armed support.

Sponsor commitments resolve against a recorded recipient instead of treating all support as a generic crisis-wide pool.

### Settlement and recurrence

Settlement derives partition eligibility from aligned-front topology and requires at least two durable fronts and distinct anchors.

Merger requires one valid regional or legal candidate plus talks rather than functioning as a generic fallback.

The outcome family includes Event 006 independence, opposition victory, same-tag results, partition, merger, autonomy or coalition, and government victory.

Ordinary opposition actors are cleaned before annexation, while partition successors and the Event 006 actor receive preservation rules.

Postwar obligations cover recognition, sponsor repayment, coalition governance, rail security, and disarmament.

Obligations use objective validation and hold dates, and a breach can feed the recurrence path.

Generation state is seeded and copied through crisis actors and front rows, recurrence is windowed and delayed by grace, and the maximum generation is two.

### Scheduler and annex cleanup

The system uses the existing global host pulse and bounded registration samples.

Review cursors and maximum review/critical scan limits bound work over registered objects.

The scheduler calls bounded review, recurrence, secondary-front review, settlement, reconstruction, and critical-queue processing.

The annex callback scopes the annexed country through `FROM` and invokes `event021_handle_annexed_country`.

That cleanup removes the live target count, front and theater registration, critical-queue membership, registered/exposed/CXT arrays, and registry flags.
