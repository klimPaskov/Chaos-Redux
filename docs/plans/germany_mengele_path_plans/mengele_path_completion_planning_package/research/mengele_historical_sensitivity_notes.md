# Historical sensitivity notes

The Germany Mengele path touches real Nazi atrocity history, Auschwitz, forced experimentation, genocide-crisis mechanics, and fictionalized alternate-history escalation. Finish it with restraint.

## Player-facing framing

- Use sober institutional and survivor-evidence framing where exposure is public.
- Avoid gore, victim spectacle, cheap horror, or sensational experiment scenes.
- Avoid heroic or charismatic perpetrator framing.
- Avoid comedy that rewards atrocity escalation.
- Avoid text that treats Nazi racial claims as true. The current source is explicit that the Tibet and Aryan-origin flags are regime claims or manufactured beliefs.
- Make consequences legible: deaths, instability, exposure, foreign reaction, discovery, tribunal pressure, civil conflict, and world threat.

## Asset source mode

- Use sourced archival imagery only when depicting real historical material.
- Use generated art only for symbolic, fictional, or non-literal super-event presentation.
- Do not generate real historical leaders or scenes claiming to show actual victims or experiments.
- Reject modern reenactments, film stills, cosplay, modern uniforms, modern props, and unclear substitutes unless explicitly approved as a documented fallback.

## Gameplay framing

- Do not make atrocities efficient bonuses without costs.
- Any short-term regime benefit must be paired with deaths, internal instability, evidence, resistance, condemnation risk, foreign reaction, and long-term consequences.
- Discovery should matter. Hidden damage can occur before the outside world can prove responsibility, but exposure should sharply change diplomatic and war-crimes consequences.

## Achievements

If achievements are added, they should reward containment, exposure, evidence preservation, preventing the Directorate, defeating it, or stopping the world-order path. They should not reward maximizing atrocities or make the path a joke.
