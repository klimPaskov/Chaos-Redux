## Exact screening arithmetic

Let `p` be the selected-channel prior and `q` a fixed source-compromise probability. For a method with clean adverse probabilities `a` for aliens and `h` for humans, the proposed compromised table gives:

`A = (1 − q) × a + q × 0.25`

`H = (1 − q) × h + q × 0.55`

For two genuinely independent source groups under that fixed context, use `LR = A1 × A2 / (H1 × H2)` and posterior `p × LR / (1 − p + p × LR)`. The following calculations use no biography cue.

| Compromise chance | Independent adverse pair | Broad 10% prior | Ordinary 20% prior | Linked 35% prior | Human joint-adverse rate |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 5% | History and material | 43.207% | 63.123% | 78.664% | 5.76375% |
| 5% | Material and contact | 45.140% | 64.929% | 79.950% | 5.76375% |
| 15% | History and material | 34.061% | 53.752% | 71.456% | 7.44875% |
| 15% | Material and contact | 35.760% | 55.604% | 72.956% | 7.44875% |
| 25% | History and material | 26.365% | 44.617% | 63.439% | 9.34375% |
| 25% | Material and contact | 27.758% | 46.368% | 65.061% | 9.34375% |

History/contact is weaker. Its human joint-adverse rates are 7.0225%, 8.7025%, and 10.5625% at those three compromise levels. Thus no fixed independent pair alone exceeds the proposed 15% innocent-admitted wrongful-purge target. This is not a full-policy result. Audit replacement, adaptive choices, shared sources, observed new conduct, candidate selection, and finite-cohort depletion can change the eventual policy rate. “Wrongful purges divided by innocent admitted people” also differs from “innocents among everyone purged.”

At 15% compromise, the linked material/contact pair reaches the revised 70% ordinary threshold without appearance. At 25% it reaches the 65% emergency threshold but falls short of ordinary approval. At the ordinary 20% prior, the same pair reaches only 55.604%. The design therefore needs its real nonlethal and institutional alternatives. It should not manufacture stronger clues to force every case into a lethal outcome.

The old 85% ordinary threshold is reachable only in the clean idealized strongest pair with the maximum biography cue, about 85.965%, among these examples. It is not reached once even 5% independent source compromise is included. This preserves the before-and-after reason for the threshold correction.

### Correlation, appearance, and audit calibration

The clean history/contact copy rule gives `P(contact adverse | history adverse, alien) = 0.8 + 0.2 × 0.65 = 0.93`, and the human equivalent is `0.85`. For a 20% prior, two adverse shared-source reports therefore produce about 39.631%, not the 60.938% from falsely treating the two clean reports as independent. The published example is correct.

Portrait appearance alone has LR 1. The combined perfect-biography cue has LR at most 1.25. Applying that maximum alone gives posteriors 12.195%, 23.810%, and 40.230% from priors 10%, 20%, and 35%. The examples in Parts 3 and 12 are arithmetically correct. Several adjectives must not multiply this cap.

With the audit's 80% sensitivity and 10% clean-source concern rate, `P(compromised | concern)` is 29.630%, 58.537%, and 72.727% for compromise priors 5%, 15%, and 25%. These are internal model calculations, not player-visible certainty. Actual AI estimation must integrate actor-known context and any shared institutional uncertainty. Multiplying independent marginals is justified only when the stated conditional independence holds.

## Mother reachability and temporal checks

The written replacement is logically coherent as one durable state receipt. A state contributes if it is both brood-owned and brood-controlled, or after 30 continuous days of brood control with a valid supplied holding. After any control loss, a new 30-day admission is mandatory even if alien legal ownership remained. Supply interruption during admission resets that clock. Once admitted, later supply loss alone does not erase territorial weight, while loss of control removes it immediately. A supplied viable Mother host remains an independent requirement.

Original host, world region, and restoration claims remain recorded. Inter-brood mergers preserve receipts without duplicate credit. Initial allocated states and their recaptures remain excluded from recent-gain credit forever. Only a nonbaseline state's first genuine durable admission supplies positive credit, with actual durable-weight losses subtracted over the same preceding 60 days. A scripted release/return or an inter-brood transfer earns no conquest credit.

The timing example is reachable: capture on day 10, uninterrupted supplied control through day 40, first durable-holding admission and corresponding genuine conquest credit on day 40, then 45 continuous qualifying days, ending on day 85. Credit must use the defined admission event consistently. Counting the original day-10 capture while waiting 30 days for qualification would shorten the usable recent-success window in a different way.

Relevant control, ownership, Chaos, and world-end lifecycle changes must invalidate the continuous streak immediately between five-day resolution checks. Otherwise a short liberation or a brief drop to Chaos 1,000 could be missed. Exactly 1,000 fails. Values above 1,000 remain insufficient without the territorial, temporal, host, and scenario-ownership tests.

Relay requirements are arithmetically valid: actual relay counts 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 require 1, 2, 2, 3, 3 disabled relays. Full-operation success plus fixed 15% partial success cannot exceed 100% under the 85% full-success ceiling. Saved random ranks prevent interface rerolls. This does not guarantee independent successes across a live campaign or establish the average time needed for victory.

The direct total-conquest fallback, single Mother identity, finite relocation, actual lair, and prohibition on missing-guardian immortality are sound design safeguards. The funded-guardian and repair corrections were rechecked. A guardian designates an existing funded formation without refilling it, or the missing-guardian condition falls back to the actual territory. A relocation requires an existing funded escort and a real continuing controlled route.

## Small-country and new-adult arithmetic

The revised 50-deployable-adult floor requires total entitlement of at least 167, 112, or 84 under the 30%, 45%, or 60% deployment shares, assuming whole-adult flooring. An unsupported partial formation instead needs a complete 250-adult Cadre from the same committed share or resolves nonterritorially. A tiny package may consolidate its actual allocation into Cadre, avoiding the contradiction in which a nominal 50-adult commitment gave only 17–18 adults to the 35% Cadre slice.

Fifty funded Cadre adults authorize ten equipment at full proportional issue. Readiness factors 60%, 75%, and 90%, rounded down, give six, seven, and nine. No arithmetic correction authorizes extra adults or stores. Native creation must separately support those actual fill quantities.

The added Mother-era assimilation policy has a separate finite input cohort. It accepts only previously registered eligible adult humans physically held after capture, never unknown residents, minors, service cohorts, or already consumed people. A 90-day operation may consume at most 250 measured adults and produce `floor(0.50 × measured loss)` pending adults. Thus 250 gives 125, and 180 gives 90. The once-per-180-day start cadence bounds this route to at most 125 new adults per 180 days per country in sustained operation, before the shared monthly release cap and before finite captives run out. Industrial incubation and this route together have a nominal sustained ceiling of `500/90 + 125/180 = 6.25` completed adults per day per country before those further limits. Already-paid merger inheritances can produce temporary completion bursts, not a renewed steady-state entitlement.

Rescue preempts the captive reservation through the shared owner, preventing a later completion from consuming people already evacuated. Staff must be genuinely uncommitted and cannot simultaneously serve in formations, escorts, or another batch. These explicit constraints close the source-level population loops. Their native implementation and military balance remain unverified.

## Other passed reasoning checks and practical limits

- **Finite people precede sampling targets.** Part 3 explicitly treats 10%, 20%, and 35% as selected-cohort test targets. Admission consumes existing leads and cannot create aliens after a cell is exhausted. Fixed truth and shared person records prevent a foreign inquiry or duplicate accusation from spawning another alien. Calibration against a depleted campaign remains different from calibration against the nominal test cohort.
- **Rerolls are bounded.** Truth, evidence, audit findings, operation ranks, and committed network choices are saved. Reopening preserves method use, a source remedy is limited to one justified replacement, and shared provenance survives country transitions. Ordinary save determinism does not prevent a player from purging, observing truthful human Deaths, then returning to a pre-purge save. This is a residual information limitation, not a runtime guarantee of save-scumming prevention. Do not invent human deaths to hide it.
- **No simple Confidence reward loop was found.** A healthy service circuit cannot be rebuilt for more rewards. Repair restores only lost capacity. Witness rewards and cell outcomes have finite receipts. Restitution cannot exceed the Confidence actually lost to the qualifying wrongs. These are source-level safeguards, not an exhaustive proof against all implemented transaction bugs.
- **The Chaos ledger is finite and adds correctly.** Positive contributions total `5 + 30 + 60 + 30 + 20 + 30 + 15 + 50 = 240`, of which 190 precede the Mother. Reversals total `30 + 30 + 50 + 20 = 130`. Immutable original-region receipts exclude repeated release/recapture farming. Shared death, annexation, and war contributions must still remain with their real owners.
- **Strong institutions do not confer universal immunity.** At maximum surviving presence 100 and access 80, Reach 100 and protection 20 yield readiness `60 + 32 − 25 − 20 = 47`, above the 45 seizure threshold. Containment must reduce real cells. A protected one-state host may avoid its stricter 75 full-takeover test while still facing the defined local insurrection and external war.
- **Late openings are stronger without free active-event growth.** The revised 600–799 opening bands and 800+ bands keep the finite adult cohort and minimum 90-day response window. Existing active events gain no free score merely by crossing a Chaos threshold. Runtime pacing and representative outcomes remain unmeasured.
- **Industrial maturation has a throughput bound.** Five paid batches producing 100 adults each in 90 days bound its sustained completion to 500 adults per 90 days per country before release restrictions. The monthly ceiling of `min(500, floor(0.05 × opening living adults))` cannot create additional adults. Pending completions and seed staff must remain distinct from deployable reserves. This is not proof that the resulting army is balanced against human production. Multiple existing countries can run their own finite pipelines before merger.
- **Native AI scores are not normalized chances.** Part 7's production allocations are targets, while native strategy factors, template selection, and decision willingness require their own semantics. Resource starvation, valid candidate pools, cumulative vanilla/mod strategies, and role-level versus target-level template behavior remain implementation review requirements. The explicit 50 Command Power final-mission reserve avoids the former 40-point emergency-budget deadlock.
