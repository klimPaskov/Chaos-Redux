### Findings grounded in those actual files

The main Zombie helper annexes eligible neighbors into fixed ZZZ with troop transfer. Its strongest-successor scorer uses division count and controlled states. Separate weaponized paths use pairwise division counts and different equality handling. None of those inspected paths is the complete frozen-component, weighted-surviving-adult algorithm specified for Mistresses. Part 10 identifies the exact functions and their limits.

The inspected civilian-loss helper rejects a nonhuman owner or controller, which makes absorption ordering and a later occupied-human adapter material design requirements. The positive civilian helper measures actual addition and removes event-created immediate recruitment credit. Human survivors cannot be inferred to have vanished merely because the country became nonhuman.

The unit registry exposes contract version 4, reserved future-provider conventions and default callbacks that do not charge the proposed organic costs. Part 6's companion therefore preserves the three actual family rows, one provider, real payment obligations and separate CXT grouping. The world-threat owner enumerates registered source flags. Mistresses must extend that owner correctly instead of asserting or clearing another system's aggregate directly.
