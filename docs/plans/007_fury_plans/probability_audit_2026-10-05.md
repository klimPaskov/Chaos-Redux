The ticket count does not prove an exact selection share unless the complete live candidate pool and all actor values are supplied.

### Target score and direct random selectors

The next-target choice in common/scripted_effects/007_fury_effects.txt:674–700 scans eligible neighbors, computes fury_target_score, and retains only candidates meeting minimum_score 1. It then keeps the strictly highest score. That is a deterministic score selector, not probability-proportional sampling. Ties retain the first candidate visited; no explicit ID tie-break is written in the task-start helper. A numeric best-score sentinel of zero is safe for candidates that have already passed the minimum-one score gate, but valid neighbors that score below one are omitted.
