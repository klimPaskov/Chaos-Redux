## Accepted source mode

The 12 in-scope portraits depict fictional zombie hordes, impossible infected profiles, and one fictional supernatural creature. Native ImageGen is the correct source route for these subjects; no historical person, real institution, or public figure is portrayed. Each source and exact prompt is archived under docs/assets/portraits/002_zombie_outbreak/release_20261006/.

The four leader stages are an explicitly authorized staged three-member fictional group, not an institutional or historical collective. The group seed uses three invented faces, and the later stage images preserve those same faces and positions. The first living-looking stage was rejected after parent review because the outbreak opening already depicts risen dead; the selected leader_1_v2 adds fresh-death pallor, cloudy eyes, and stiff expressions without changing identity, uniforms, or composition.

The weaponized profiles use distinct fictional visual cues based on the current special-project outcomes: neurobiological infection, rage, parasitic symbiosis, mutant cunning, canonical undead reanimation, durable necrotic siege, extra-scientific demonic corruption, and a fast predatory antlered Wendigo creature. The Wendigo is explicitly fictional and is not presented as an authentic cultural or religious depiction.

The parent approved leader_1_v2, leader_2_v1, leader_3_v1, leader_4_v1, weaponized_infected_v1, weaponized_rabid_v2, weaponized_parasitic_v1, weaponized_mutant_v2, weaponized_undead_v1, weaponized_necrotic_v1, weaponized_demonic_v1, and weaponized_wendigo_v2 on 2026-10-06. The final candidate PNGs were processed to opaque 156x210; candidate DDS files were pixel-compared against those PNGs; and all 12 selected DDS files were copied to their existing runtime paths after whole-package acceptance.
