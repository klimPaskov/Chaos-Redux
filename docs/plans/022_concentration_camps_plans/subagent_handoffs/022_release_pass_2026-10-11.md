## Phase 2, evolutions I to III (implemented)

File: `common/scripted_effects/022_concentration_camps_evolution_effects.txt`, events `.201`, `.301`, `.401`, the opening option `.2.e`, evolution rows in the three Event Log surfaces and the Event Details preview (cloned from the Industrial Boom rows), constants `concentration_camps_evolution*`.
- Stage resolution and logging on the shared evolution context, due-day pacing from the monthly monitor, pre-fire evolved opening (20 percent below Evolution III, 50 percent at III with only the 2 percent loss).
- Conversion of `floor(n / 2)` concentration camps through `genocide_register_extermination_site_for_root` and the killing role, gated by `camp_admin_can_use_radical_mandate`. Shocks of 1 and 2 percent through `camp_admin_record_deaths`, once per state, clamped to the living cohort, marker set at zero. New sites after a stage take that stage's shock once.
- Deviations: the shocks run synchronously in one pass (the per-state workers of the chain map only guard idempotency, the state flags do that here); a country without the radical route skips conversion and still advances; new Evolution III extermination sites are created as concentration camps with the late cohort and then converted, so they keep the 1.0 percent cohort instead of 1.5 percent; the sweep cohort of P-022-C is not used (plain clamping, as the index describes).
- Unverified: the effect of `genocide_register_extermination_site_for_root` on a site that the shared registration already holds, and whether the shared processor kills only after the mandate is radical (read from the shared contract, not run).

## Not implemented in Phase 1, with the reason

- Exposure and Resistance are the shared values; no Event 22 meter exists by design (P-022-A).
- Regional closure mission and staged building removal: closure uses the shared national reform and the Ledger per-site dismantlement. The monitor treats "reform begun and no operational site" as closed, so relief, registration and records milestones are not separate Event 22 gates.
- Restrictive review inspection chain, source exhaustion incident, military objection, administrator corruption, disaster, underground network and restricted chemical accident incidents: deferred to Phase 3.
- Germany, Japan, Soviet and civil-kit countries (`camp_rework_country_uses_generic_kit` false) are excluded from selection. The package handoff adapter is Phase 3.
- Black Friday sale pricing is not applied to the Event 22 decision costs.
- Visible actions in the category are 2 decisions and 1 mission, below the 3 to 5 target until the Phase 3 relief decisions exist.
- Spreadsheet catalog row and CSV export are not updated (parent or spreadsheet worker).
- Countries whose reform ran keep `camp_admin_reforming` and are never selected again.
