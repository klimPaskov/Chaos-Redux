## Not implemented in Phase 1, with the reason

- Exposure and Resistance are the shared values; no Event 22 meter exists by design (P-022-A).
- Regional closure mission and staged building removal: closure uses the shared national reform and the Ledger per-site dismantlement. The monitor treats "reform begun and no operational site" as closed, so relief, registration and records milestones are not separate Event 22 gates.
- Restrictive review inspection chain, source exhaustion incident, military objection, administrator corruption, disaster, underground network and restricted chemical accident incidents: deferred to Phase 3.
- Germany, Japan, Soviet and civil-kit countries (`camp_rework_country_uses_generic_kit` false) are excluded from selection. The package handoff adapter is Phase 3.
- Black Friday sale pricing is not applied to the Event 22 decision costs.
- Visible actions in the category are 2 decisions and 1 mission, below the 3 to 5 target until the Phase 3 relief decisions exist.
- Spreadsheet catalog row and CSV export are not updated (parent or spreadsheet worker).
- Countries whose reform ran keep `camp_admin_reforming` and are never selected again.
