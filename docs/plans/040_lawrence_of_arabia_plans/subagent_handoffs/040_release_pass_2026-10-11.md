### Not implemented in Phase 1 and why

- Evolution rows in Event Details and the log: Phase 2.
- Desert Air Route and favoured-partner conference, Reinforce a Client Government: Evolution II / later.
- Achievements Promises Kept, A Desert Conference, Rails Ports and Promises, Gold Without Chains, The Uncrowned Kingdom: need Evolution II or III content.
- Decision, idea, achievement and category icons are vanilla stand-ins, the Lawrence portrait is not wired (documented in the doc page, needs the portrait workflow and the user's HOI4-style portrait).
- Independence Wave, Malta, Great Embargo, Intel Leaked, Murder Mystery, Famine and Migration bridges: the narrow reaction events and the `lawrence_requested_protection` flag exist, direct adapters do not. `is_special_chaos_country` excludes Event 38 actors only if Event 38 registers them there.
- Probability audit through the MCP, focus tools: not run in Phase 1. Weights were reviewed from the script by hand.
