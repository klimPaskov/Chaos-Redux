# Event 010 - Death

Event 010 is `Death`, a minor fire-once event rooted at `chaosx.nr10.1`. It has no cluster and uses `DTH` as the conflict-free country tag. Death is ruled by Zol with the `God of Death` leader trait, uses an all-black map color and flag set, starts from a remote low-population island, and receives no starting divisions, starting manpower, starting equipment, or public opening notification.

## Flow

The hidden root event creates or reactivates Death, consumes one eligible island state, records Death in the event log, and schedules delayed marine-office packets for the former owner/controller and nearby coastal or naval observers. Those packets are indirect, arrive months after each missing island, and open Maritime Errata decisions for recipient countries without revealing Death or Zol.

All consumption paths call `death_consume_current_state` in `common/scripted_effects/010_death_effects.txt`. The shared effect records the previous population, removes population and state value, transfers owner/controller to Death, adds a Death core, applies active wasteland state effects, records civilian deaths when the deaths system is enabled, updates consumed-state counters, per-continent mainland counters, Last Ferry evacuation credit, withers lingering divisions, and checks reveal, defeat, world-end, and whole-world-consumed gates.

Before reveal, Death is intentionally slow. Baseline Death consumes one eligible island, waits through long randomized pulses, and does not consume another island until the Second Shore evolution is recorded and Death has taken an island-spread focus. Hidden island spread then prefers nearby sub-100,000-population islands first, falls back to any eligible sub-100,000 island only when no nearby one exists, and only then widens to the broader low-population island pool. Each hidden island consumption schedules the same delayed marine-office packet for the former owner/controller and nearby coastal observers.

Death reveals itself only when it consumes a mainland coastal state above the configured population threshold, and the natural mainland route remains locked until the Mainland Hunger evolution has been recorded, Death has taken the mainland focus, and enough island pressure has built up. The reveal pans player cameras to the revealed state, fires super-event `62`, declares war on the owner or controller of the consumed state, and can pull in neighboring countries only when they directly border a Death wasteland or have already recognized a coastal warning. It does not start a world war by itself. Landlocked countries do not receive the general coastal response unless Death is physically on their border or already at war with them. The reveal also immediately consumes unguarded neighboring states from the mainland wasteland and enables containment decisions for countries that have a reason to respond. After reveal, Death runs daily public pressure from its tag-scoped on-action: living units in active wastelands are withered every day, neighboring living states receive a weaker border-withering modifier and daily unit damage, unguarded neighboring states fall immediately from any mainland Death wasteland, and scheduled pulses are shortened for stronger ghost refreshes, wither checks, and cooldown-limited coastal jumps. Death becomes a world threat source only after it consumes a continent.

World-end starts only when Death has consumed a whole continent and Chaos is above the Death world-end threshold. This fires super-event `63`, swaps Zol to the world-end portrait, creates one coastal foothold on each continent without an active Death-controlled wasteland presence, and unlocks the strongest host behavior. World-end footholds prefer empty non-capital coasts, then relax watch/quarantine and defense filters before falling back to any valid coastal state. A successful foothold pulls its owner/controller into war if needed. If every populated state is consumed, super-event `65` fires.

Defeat is custom. Death is defeated only when it controls no states, not by ordinary capitulation shortcuts. Defeat clears the world-threat source, removes Death units/templates, and leaves wastelands as recaptured dead zones. Super-event `64` fires only after public reveal and a major crisis threshold: at least 10 million consumed population, at least three mainland states consumed, or a world-end attempt.

## Decisions And AI

Maritime Errata decisions are limited to countries that receive delayed marine-office packets. They can send a survey boat, check records, issue harbor sanitation notices, or close the file under weather. Surveying can expose Death before the world learns its name and preserves quiet-defeat tracking.

The Death Country category appears after public reveal only for countries with direct border danger, active war contact, Living Compact membership, or a confirmed coastal warning. The public reveal does not make the category global, and landlocked countries outside the danger area do not receive the general coastal response. The category contains public war recognition, Living Compact coordination, coastal patrols, wasteland gear, Last Shores response, quarantine line actions, coastal-watch state preparation, wasteland surveys, dead-zone outposts, Dark Methods, Black Oath, Herald, and Black Apostolate route decisions. The forbidden route decisions display in red. Stability is consumed as a consequence instead of blocking the action when the country is below the stability spend. The human player can hide or show the state list for port-watch decisions, while AI countries still evaluate those decisions directly. Keeping a port lit costs a major fuel stockpile and command resources, applies Coastal Watch for 120 days, and blocks normal hidden coastal jumps from selecting that state. A strong global watch network can stop one normal low-pressure coastal jump before it lands, then spends part of the network strength. The No Ferry Returns focus, high spread pressure, and world-end coastal pressure bypass that interception. Strengthening a quarantine line also starts a timed hold-line mission. If the line survives the mission period, global front readiness improves and spread pressure falls, while an early break raises spread pressure. The Black Atlas is opened from the category as a transient text dashboard. It has no separate close control or decorative status icons, and its consumed-population line switches to `M` and `B` bands once the totals reach millions or billions.

The Black Ledger category is visible only to active DTH. It shows consumed states, island and mainland counters, consumed population, spread pressure, soul power, spent soul power, host counts, and status lines for island spread, mainland spread, and host availability. Soul power is generated from consumed states, consumed population, and last-shore footholds after world end begins. It has no storage cap. Available soul power is generated soul power minus what Death has already spent on spread decisions or ghost hosts. Death can spend it to force an eligible island spread, press a valid mainland route when pressure or the war bypass allows it, or raise the ghost-host tier currently unlocked by Chaos and the Death stage. Forced island spread starts at `constant:death_soul_power.island_spread_cost`, then each successful Black Ledger island consumption raises the next forced island cost by `constant:death_soul_power.island_spread_cost_step`. Each successful Black Ledger mainland consumption raises the next forced mainland cost by `constant:death_soul_power.mainland_spread_cost_step`. Each ghost host raised raises all later host costs by `constant:death_soul_power.host_cost_step`. Spread pressure unlocks the normal mainland route at `constant:death_spread.mainland_pressure_threshold`, makes withering faster at the high and extreme thresholds, shortens coastal-jump cooldowns at those thresholds, and lets Death keep seeking coastal jumps once pressure reaches `constant:death_spread.pressure_coastal_jump_threshold`. AI Death uses the same decisions. It prioritizes cheap island consumption before public reveal while island targets remain empty, then spends on mainland pressure and ghost hosts as the crisis opens.

Dark Methods are a living-country desperation route. A threatened country can open Black Book offices, bind unburied names into weak capped hosts, interrogate empty roads for Death pressure intelligence, seal names in iron, and burn the Black Book before exposure becomes a domestic scandal. If the country only opened the book and took nothing from it, the book can be closed for free. Once a forbidden method has been used, burning the book costs political power. The route uses exposure, bound names, and mourning debt variables, keeps AI use conservative, and records the hidden Book Burner predicates only when the country used a Dark Method, burned the book before the high-exposure threshold, and later helped defeat Death.

The Black Oath is a separate betrayal route. A desperate country can whisper to Zol, take the oath, leave the Living Compact, receive the Herald of Zol cosmetic identity and national idea, and gain Herald decisions. Heralds can offer a prison census, open dead ports, feed border states to Death through the shared consumption helper, or break the oath and become a direct Death target. Name debt, black favor, living disgust, sacrificed states, and dead ports control the route. If a Herald reaches the hidden thresholds after world-end, it can proclaim the Black Apostolate cosmetic identity and route culmination.

After Death is defeated, active war and compact decisions close. Recaptured wasteland decisions remain available so living countries can survey dead zones and build outposts. Dead-zone outpost tracking starts only after the custom defeat path has fired. Capitulation runs the same defeat cleanup and the winner annexes DTH instead of leaving it as a puppet. If a peace result still creates a DTH subject, the subject loses Death mechanics, receives a generic focus tree, and is assigned an emergency administrator.

AI weights are intentionally conservative before reveal, stronger for threatened countries after reveal, and strongest for compact/Last Shores responses. AI countries will not choose Death war decisions while Death has consumed only its first island. Once Death has consumed more than one island, aggressive AI countries can use the public war decisions only when they border Death, are already in contact with it, or have a confirmed coastal warning. Compact invitations and Compact war declarations use the same boundary, so distant countries and landlocked countries outside the danger area stay out. Dark Methods have low AI weights and are biased toward direct-threat or world-end desperation. Black Oath AI is heavily restricted to unstable, non-major, threatened countries in world-end conditions, and the Black Apostolate proclamation is player-only. Death itself has staged ghost behavior: no starting units, weak passive custom hosts at the First Hosts milestone, stronger but still inferior custom hosts at the Hollow Hosts milestone, and aggressive parity hosts only for world-end. `common/ai_strategy/010_death.txt` keeps public pre-world-end fronts careful and non-executing, then switches Death to rush execution after world-end starts. Host effects provision only the dummy manpower and infantry equipment needed by the locked custom template at creation time. Each ghost battalion uses one dummy manpower and one dummy infantry equipment, and Death has no normal recruitment route for these units. Natural pulses, focus rewards, manual Black Ledger decisions, and last-shore footholds spend from a shared soul budget based on consumed states, consumed population, and recorded last-shore footholds. There is no soul cap. The practical limit is rising cost: island spread, mainland spread, and host raising all become more expensive after successful uses. The Black Ledger AI spends on cheap island spread first while valid empty islands remain, raises available ghost hosts when it is not saving for a pre-reveal mainland press, and spends more aggressively after public reveal or war contact. SCN-006 directly creates its selected starting hosts and charges the same host counters afterward so later natural spawns keep the same escalating prices.

SCN-006 has one triggerable Death setup: Instant Outbreak. Manual launch consumes a hidden origin, intensity-scaled extra islands, at least one mainland reveal target, and intensity-scaled ghost hosts. It does not set the Chaos Meter, record natural evolutions by shortcut, or start world-end. After launch, Death continues through the same natural pulses and can only escalate further through ordinary crisis growth.

Achievement tracking follows the accepted route predicates rather than simple button counts. `death_not_on_my_continent` uses the player's capital-continent mainland-consumption counter, `death_last_ferry` records prepared threatened states only when Death later consumes them, `death_counted_every_name` requires census participation plus major/compact coordination before Hollow Hosts gather and below Black Book scandal exposure, and `death_black_tide_reversed` requires all recorded last-shore footholds recaptured with no surviving Herald of Zol.

The Death focus tree is a fixed-purpose 26-node lane tree, not a normal country tree. It has an opening trunk, Shroud/Hunger/Census pre-reveal lanes, a real `Public Death` convergence focus gated by public reveal, Coastal/Wasteland/Host post-reveal lanes, and a terminal Last Shores/World Consumed branch. The Shroud lane is available while Death is hidden, at peace, and no later than Gathering Storm, and it closes once a living-country survey war has started. The Island Pattern can be taken before Second Shore, but its immediate island consumption only fires when island spread is already active. If a living country declares war after the Second Shore evolution, Death can complete Mainland Smell before the natural Mainland Hunger tier and record that evolution early. This bypass never works before Second Shore. The forbidden routes live on living-country decisions instead of adding another DTH focus branch.
