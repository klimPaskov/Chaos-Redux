# Event 12 Africa evolutions

## Overview

Event 12 begins at the public Chaos tier 4. Its ordinary logged escalation therefore consists of Evolution I at public tier 4, Evolution II at public tier 5, and Evolution III at public tier 6. The shared Chaos Meter stores those bands as zero-based runtime IDs 3, 4, and 5. `africa_evolution_runtime` is the sole translation layer; the public design numbers remain unchanged in the Event 12 source specification.

The requested Evolution IV material is not discarded or written as an invalid second World Collapse row. It belongs to the post-unification world-order system after a stable Africa, the Scramble response, and the required external continent packages exist.

## Runtime sequence

1. The selected Event 12 host schedules one private `mtth:africa_evolution_interval` clock after host initialization. The clock does not use a daily, weekly, monthly, or whole-world on action.
2. The base delay is 90 days and is clamped between 45 and 180 days. War, a functioning region, Charter authority, colonial pressure, low member confidence, and integration burden modify the sampled delay.
3. Each fired clock validates the current host generation. A superseded host cannot advance the event; the one valid South African exile custodian receives a fresh clock.
4. At most one enabled, eligible stage activates on a clock tick. Enabled stages remain sequential. A stage disabled in settings receives an explicit disposition flag, no recorded flag, and does not block the next stage or the grounded path to continental settlement.
5. Every activation writes the Event Log row with the current host as actor, opens its incident, adjusts visible League values, unlocks its bounded mechanics, refreshes the focus layout, and schedules the next private clock.
6. Evolution III closes the ordinary evolution clock. Post-unification world-order play has its own settlement and terminal guards.

## Gates and effects

### Evolution I — Regional Consolidation

The host must be at the shared Chaos Tier band, possess a proven regional mandate and operational corridor, satisfy its exact opening proof, and maintain at least three cooperative relationships. It opens regional intervention, congresses, first integration projects, restoration expansion, rival regional blocs, early foreign reactions, and transport priority. Authority, reach, and burden all move visibly.

### Evolution II — Continental Machinery

Evolution I must be logged or explicitly disabled. The host must reach Totalen Chaos, declare the Charter League, maintain at least nine cooperative relationships, and reach 50 continental influence. It opens temporary preparation, claim settlement, deeper federal bargaining, resource expansion, advanced identity states, strange human formations, elephant logistics, and harder sanction pressure. Reach, central capacity, defence stockpile, and burden all move visibly.

### Evolution III — Africa as a World Pole

Evolution II must be logged or explicitly disabled. The host must reach World Collapse, complete a constitutional capstone, and either settle Africa or combine at least sixteen cooperative relationships with 75 reach and 75 authority. It opens external anti-colonial operations, continental mobilisation, overseas-base pressure, continent sponsorship, high-chaos investigations, and continent union or rivalry eligibility. It does not grant continental control or make high-chaos content a prerequisite for `Africa Is One`.
