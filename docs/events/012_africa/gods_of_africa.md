## Ownership and activation

Gods of Africa is an Event 012 subsystem owned by the African unifier created by Africa Is One. It has no normal-event pool entry, no separate Event 070 identity, and no unrestricted recurring world scan. Evolution I starts a host-owned consolidation clock; after the 180-day consolidation interval, the activation effect rechecks the Event 012 host, generation, Africa Is One proof, and evolution gate before committing the system.

The `SCN-011` Africa Is One manual scenario seeds the Evolution I chronology receipt without replaying the ordinary Evolution I popup or reward package, then calls this same owner-local initializer so its 180-day delay, generation checks, and activation gates remain identical to the normal route. The `World Is One` scenario type intentionally prepares the separate terminal-campaign package and does not start the tribute loop.

The committed host owns one bounded participant registry, the global Gods of Africa Strength value, the dispatch cursor, demand budget, doctrine, priority family, and ending lock. Participant countries own their personal Wrath, frozen demand contract, payment progress, defiance state, and hidden relationship history. A restoration generation prevents delayed work from resolving against a previous host.

Ordinary current-generation authority additionally requires the country to be in that host's live participant array and the shared host pointer to identify an existing committed unifier.
Matching flags or generation numbers on a nonmember cannot permit an ordinary transaction.
Final-settlement and victor reports use their separate frozen historical receipts, so this live-membership condition does not suppress completed presentation after the roster is cleared.

## Bounded census and host-transfer persistence

The one-time Event 012 census retains known ordinary country scopes from vanilla's `global.countries` array in `gods_of_africa_candidate_registry`, with one unique row per native country object.
The candidate pool includes minors, capitulated countries, and landless tags so later eligibility does not require a recurring world scan.
The unifier and special or actual nonhuman actors are excluded.
Actual enrollment separately requires an existing, non-capitulated major or player country.

`gods_of_africa_remember_candidate` inserts a scope once and advances the candidate count only for a new entry below the cap.
Release, puppet, peace-conference, and government-change callbacks remember the supplied countries after the census, including before Gods activation.
The government-change callback also covers the two sides created by a civil war.
`gods_of_africa_initialize_registry` enrolls eligible candidates after consolidation.
The private `.607` clock alone owns later 90-day reviews, while its due-date guard ignores early or duplicate deliveries without consuming the scheduled receipt.
Review archives invalid participants from a separate snapshot, releases their active-demand budget, then checks the maintained candidate pool for later major or player eligibility.
The snapshot is cleared after each review, and the 30-day owner tick does not run a second registry review.
These are source findings within the user's bounded-enrollment goal, while runtime cadence and enrollment remain validation items.

During host succession, `africa_host_transfer_stage_scalar_ledger` stages the old host's generation, Gods candidate count, census flag, and candidate array alongside the wider Event 012 ledger. `africa_host_transfer_restore_world_order_state` rebuilds the successor's candidate array and census state before the successor closes staging, while generation guards prevent delayed callbacks from resolving against the old host. A suspended transfer intentionally retains its inspectable staging docket until the transfer is resolved; this persistence is source evidence and remains bounded by the transfer lifecycle.

On annexation, the Event 012 world-order `on_annex` bridge calls `africa_begin_host_transfer` for the current host while the annexed country remains available as `FROM`; that effect invokes `gods_of_africa_cancel_on_unifier_loss` before changing the shared host pointer. The unifier's `on_capitulation` callback invokes the same cleanup. It removes live participant and contract state, removes their missions, withdraws owned protection services, closes the scheduled-clock receipts, clears the active registry, and sets public Strength to zero. Host-transfer staging carries the bounded candidate census for a successor but does not move the active Gods participant registry or public Strength. The initializer and activation trigger reject the old generation's loss receipt, so a delayed `.600` cannot restart its former authority. Returning participants retain Wrath, conduct counters, and aligned kind/day history; first enrollment initializes that ledger once. Permanent opt-out survives the cancellation of live authority and is restored to live defiance on re-entry.

## Public values

The player sees exactly two values:

- Gods of Africa Strength is a global value from 0 to 100. It combines African military readiness, industry and logistics, continental control, strategic reach, internal condition, Chaos, and Event 012 development. Ordinary refreshes are smoothed; major defeat or recovery may request an immediate recalculation. Its five bands set the highest punishment tier the system can credibly attempt.
- Wrath of the Gods is a 0 to 100 value stored separately for each participant. Compliance, meaningful aid, refusal, failed deadlines, war, occupation, treaty breaches, and defiance move it through one central transaction helper. War, occupied African territory, broken treaties, permanent defiance, and recent extreme punishment reapply hidden floors after every transaction.

Capacity, African needs, conduct history, demand repetition, punishment intent, and settlement history remain hidden. The interface communicates them through qualitative demand language, reports, tooltips, diplomatic memory, and outcomes rather than a third meter.

## Tribute loop

After activation, every eligible major or player in the maintained candidate pool is enrolled once, excluding the unifier and invalid or special actors.
Candidate and participant capacity use the native `global.countries^num` cardinality and their actual array sizes rather than a fixed tag-count ceiling or scalar bookkeeping count.
Only native country objects can enter either deduplicated registry, and registration requires successful durable candidate membership before creating live authority or history.
Every genuinely enrolled actor therefore remains in the final census after archival; newly allocated countries use the same bounded callback contract.
The 90-day registry clock handles later major or player changes, while transaction callbacks can enroll a newly eligible country immediately.
Each participant receives an introduction and a deterministic first-demand delay.
A participant can have only one active ordinary demand, and the owner dispatch budget staggers notices across the registry.

Recent repetition tracks actual consecutive committed demand family, with separate truck/artillery identity, rather than the number of relationship-history entries.
Matching demands within the configured 365-day gap increment the private streak; a different family/type or a longer gap starts at one.
The existing repetition threshold and percentage adjustment remain unchanged, and negotiation, aid, failed quotes, partial payments, and substitute responses cannot advance or replay that contract receipt.
The precise contract and source scenarios are in `common/scripted_effects/012_africa_gods_repetition_effects.md` and `012_africa_gods_repetition_2026-10-03.md`.

Demand creation freezes a family, readable amount, deadline, generation, contract identity, and status. `gods_of_africa_calculate_demand_amount` scales the amount and selects the contract band from current capacity, Strength, Wrath, Chaos, need, doctrine, conduct, and repetition, while `gods_of_africa_calculate_demand_deadline` resolves the matching constant-backed deadline. Reciprocal Covenant retains its 90% amount scale, Sovereign Exaction retains its 120% scale, and Strategic Ambiguity extends each frozen deadline by 30 days without changing the quoted amount. The `gods_of_africa_demand` constants include explicit ceremonial, standard, severe, emergency, and `punitive_band` values plus their deadline constants, including `deadline_punitive`; this is source evidence for conduct-aware escalation, not a balance approval. The family is selected from current African needs—land equipment, support equipment, fuel, manpower, industry, emergency relief, transport reserves, aircraft reserves, or a controlled steel-bearing state—and, after the corresponding focus capability is unlocked, can also request recognition, return of an African core state, or termination of recorded support for an African enemy. The strategic-resource lane uses `gods_of_africa_transaction.resources_amount` as a live state-output threshold and pays through the documented `give_resource_rights` effect; it does not fake a stockpile debit, and the participant's rights receipt prevents the same concession from being demanded repeatedly in the same host generation. Those diplomatic lanes require the relevant target condition and political-power capacity, and territory compliance transfers one controlled African core state to the host. When all maintained need flags and focus-gated diplomatic conditions are clear, the owner returns without forming a contract, so the default priority cannot create an arbitrary demand during a healthy interval. The emergency lane requires an initialized debt variable above the high-debt threshold, or above the medium-debt threshold after the needs-profile focus is completed; an absent debt variable is treated as a healthy zero during partial restoration. `gods_of_africa_set_payment_capacity_requirement`, `gods_of_africa_set_substitute_capacity_requirement`, and `gods_of_africa_set_partial_capacity_requirement` call `gods_of_africa_compute_capacity_requirement` to keep the public transfer amount separate from a hidden requirement that adds the family-specific operating floor for equipment, fuel, manpower, or industry. The capacity and affordability triggers consume those hidden requirements with the current-generation contract guards. Material compliance debits the participant's real stockpile, fuel, manpower, or political power and credits the same amount to the African host; the resource lane grants real steel rights, and infantry transfers also replenish the maintained Event 012 common-defence reserve used by the shortage profile. Partial payment records only the remaining amount; substitution and extension use the frozen contract rather than rerolling it.

The native demand mission is synchronized to the frozen deadline immediately after activation and after an extension.
The 180-day definition is an activation anchor, while the live countdown uses the actual ceremonial, standard, severe, emergency, or punitive duration and any approved extension.
Mission and reconciliation descriptions read the engine's remaining timeout rather than an absolute day counter.
The owner tick's deadline pass is a recovery check, while the native timeout owns ordinary expiry.
Partial shipments retain the same running deadline and do not restart the mission.

Participants can comply, negotiate, substitute a valid offer, refuse, or permanently defy. Negotiation freezes up to three deterministic, capacity-checked alternatives with the contract; the participant submits one offer, while the host explicitly accepts or rejects it. Rejection raises Wrath slightly, records the attempt, restores the original payment fields, and leaves the extended demand available; returning to the demand cancels an unsubmitted offer. Refusal raises Wrath and invokes punishment review without silently ending the relationship. Defiance stops ordinary demands, records a permanent offense, raises the Wrath floor, and leaves punishment, retaliation, protection withdrawal, and conflict available. Only the reconciliation path can restore ordinary demand eligibility, and reconciliation preserves prior-defiance history.

Reconciliation requires a live review deadline, an actual ten-political-power reparations transfer to Africa, and host acceptance before that deadline. The transfer records a separate paid-offer history entry. Archival, participant cleanup, and unifier loss remove pending review, payment permission, offer permission, and deadline state while retaining the actual completed transfer and durable history. Re-entry can open a fresh review through the existing owner clock; interrupted permission cannot accept reconciliation or block that review indefinitely. Only accepted reconciliation clears permanent opt-out.

Outside an active demand, `gods_of_africa_offer_voluntary_aid` provides one participant-side offering when the host's maintained need profile shows a land-equipment or fuel shortage. Land equipment is selected first when both needs are present; otherwise fuel is selected. The effect rechecks the current generation, host activity, need, war state, cooldown, and sender reserve before debiting 500 units, credits the host's real stores (and the common defence reserve for equipment), records an `aid` history entry, and opens the separate `chaosx.nr12.614` receipt. The first voluntary offer lowers Wrath by four and later offers by two; a 180-day participant cooldown and a 500-unit reserve floor prevent repeated tiny shipments. The host rebuilds its need profile after a successful transfer, and the existing reliable-partner memory can make the participant eligible for future protection.

If a participant enters a war with Africa while an ordinary demand is open, the owner-local tick cancels that contract once, records a treaty break, raises Wrath by the bounded war amount, and reports the pause. No ordinary demand is dispatched while the participant remains at war; after peace, the normal registry and cooldown gates may issue a fresh contract.

## Punishment and protection

Punishment intent is calculated separately from the capability ceiling. `gods_of_africa_evaluate_punishment` first writes `gods_of_africa_punishment_intent_tier` from Wrath and escalation evidence, copies that intent into `gods_of_africa_pending_punishment_tier`, and applies the first-failure and host Strength-band ceilings. `gods_of_africa_apply_punishment` then validates and prices only the capped pending tier, fails closed from catastrophe when the extreme gate is absent, records both last punishment tiers, and clears the transient intent and pending values. Wrath, failure history, proven offenses, failed-demand severity, Chaos, and hostility increase desired punishment; Strength, African capability, evolution access, target condition, cooldowns, and valid Event 013 inputs cap what can actually happen. A first ordinary refusal is capped at a low tier. High Wrath alone never opens catastrophe. The extreme route additionally requires extreme Strength, very high Wrath, high Chaos, repeated failure or a major offense, the required evolution, a valid ultimatum, no target or global extreme cooldown, and an Africa state that is not collapsing.

Weak Africa therefore produces pressure, unrest, limited disruption, failed manifestations, and bounded sabotage. Natural disasters use the Event 013 gateway and fail closed when that gateway rejects the call. Any civilian population loss uses the shared exact state-population transaction and records Deaths once. Protection remains one host-selected action over the maintained participant registry, gated by the existing reliable-partner, current-generation, host-reserve, cooldown, non-defiance, and no-war checks. Africa pays 500 infantry equipment, 500 fuel, and 10 political power, subtracts the equipment from its common-defence reserve, credits equipment and fuel to the participant, and starts the 180-day protection cooldown; the reserve focus shortens that interval. With the logistics focus, at most one shortage-checked 20-unit convoy or train transfer may add a fourth cost type when Africa can retain more than its configured 40-unit floor after shipment.

The current focus overlay adds owner-gated services to that paid compact. The Sanctuary capability creates a guarantee only when none existed and, with Arms Against Tyranny, may add mutual market access when the pair is at peace; the judgment-branch focus `gods_of_africa_focus_no_sanctuary`, localized as “Sanctuary for Reliable Partners,” supplies that capability. Security creates military access only when absent and, with La Résistance, the Security capability, valid agencies, and an active African war, shares five points of each intelligence category, or ten under Strategic Ambiguity, about Africa's current non-capitulated enemies that the participant is not already fighting. Logistics transfers 20 convoys when the participant has fewer than 20 and Africa can send them while keeping more than its configured 40-unit floor; trains use the same test if a convoy shipment cannot be made. Relief adds stability and builds one infrastructure level in an owned-and-controlled partner capital below level five. A Relief Convoy is a separate choice in Foreign Disaster Relief; for a protected partner under the relief focus, that choice uses Event 013's preflight adapter and full existing price, and Event 013 owns the donor receipt and mission lifecycle. The shared decision filters unresolved-territory cards and selects an actionable card before charging or setting recipient state. The manpower, support equipment, trucks, convoys, fuel, stability, war-support, and temporary light construction costs are not charged by the Event 012 protection action. Expedition adds war support. Shelter may install Migration-owned shelter and medical reception capacity in the participant's current capital after Migration's food-safety and empty-service checks, without moving population.

Created guarantees, access rights, and Migration capacity have participant-owned receipts, so defiance, archive, host loss, cancellation, and final settlement withdraw only recorded services whose owner-side state still matches. Pre-existing relations and rewritten or transferred Migration services are preserved. Because HOI4 diplomatic relations are binary, a relation revoked and independently re-created for the same country pair before cleanup cannot be distinguished from the original grant; cleanup still follows the stored created-by-protection receipt and current relation check. Completed equipment transfers, construction, and intelligence already learned are historical effects, and an Event 013 relief mission already dispatched is not recalled when Event 012 protection is withdrawn. The base package works without optional DLC; AAT market access and La Resistance intelligence remain gated by their respective DLC and live agency conditions.
