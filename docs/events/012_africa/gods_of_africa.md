# Gods of Africa

## Ownership and activation

Gods of Africa is an Event 012 subsystem owned by the African unifier created by Africa Is One. It has no normal-event pool entry, no separate Event 070 identity, and no unrestricted recurring world scan. Evolution I starts a host-owned consolidation clock; after the 180-day consolidation interval, the activation effect rechecks the Event 012 host, generation, Africa Is One proof, and evolution gate before committing the system.

The `SCN-011` Africa Is One manual scenario seeds the Evolution I chronology receipt without replaying the ordinary Evolution I popup or reward package, then calls this same owner-local initializer so its 180-day delay, generation checks, and activation gates remain identical to the normal route. The `World Is One` scenario type intentionally prepares the separate terminal-campaign package and does not start the tribute loop.

The committed host owns one bounded participant registry, the global Gods of Africa Strength value, the dispatch cursor, demand budget, doctrine, priority family, and ending lock. Participant countries own their personal Wrath, frozen demand contract, payment progress, defiance state, and hidden relationship history. A restoration generation prevents delayed work from resolving against a previous host.

## Bounded census and host-transfer persistence

Current source evidence separates the Event 012 candidate census from later Gods registry review. The one-time post-unification census in `common/scripted_effects/012_africa_world_order_effects.txt` clears `gods_of_africa_candidate_registry`, performs two bounded Scramble participant sweeps, then performs one capped candidate sweep that excludes the unifier, capitulated countries, actual nonhuman countries, and special Chaos countries and admits `is_major = yes` or player-controlled countries until `constant:gods_of_africa_timing.max_participants` is reached.

That sweep stores candidates on the host, increments `gods_of_africa_candidate_count`, and sets `gods_of_africa_candidate_census_complete`. `gods_of_africa_initialize_registry` consumes that frozen array once, while `gods_of_africa_review_registry` reviews only the maintained Gods participant array and archives invalid records; neither helper is an unrestricted recurring world scan. These are implementation findings, not a new approval basis for the accepted design.

During host succession, `africa_host_transfer_stage_scalar_ledger` stages the old host's generation, Gods candidate count, census flag, and candidate array alongside the wider Event 012 ledger. `africa_host_transfer_restore_world_order_state` rebuilds the successor's candidate array and census state before the successor closes staging, while generation guards prevent delayed callbacks from resolving against the old host. A suspended transfer intentionally retains its inspectable staging docket until the transfer is resolved; this persistence is source evidence and remains bounded by the transfer lifecycle.

## Public values

The player sees exactly two values:

- Gods of Africa Strength is a global value from 0 to 100. It combines African military readiness, industry and logistics, continental control, strategic reach, internal condition, Chaos, and Event 012 development. Ordinary refreshes are smoothed; major defeat or recovery may request an immediate recalculation. Its five bands set the highest punishment tier the system can credibly attempt.
- Wrath of the Gods is a 0 to 100 value stored separately for each participant. Compliance, meaningful aid, refusal, failed deadlines, war, occupation, treaty breaches, and defiance move it through one central transaction helper. War, occupied African territory, broken treaties, permanent defiance, and recent extreme punishment reapply hidden floors after every transaction.

Capacity, African needs, conduct history, demand repetition, punishment intent, and settlement history remain hidden. The interface communicates them through qualitative demand language, reports, tooltips, diplomatic memory, and outcomes rather than a third meter.

## Tribute loop

After activation, every valid major or player country in the frozen Event 012 census is enrolled once, excluding the unifier and invalid or special actors. The owner-local review archives annexed or stale records, while transaction-boundary callbacks can enroll a newly eligible country when the engine supplies that country scope. Each participant receives an introduction and a deterministic first-demand delay. A participant can have only one active ordinary demand, and the owner dispatch budget staggers notices across the registry.

Demand creation freezes a family, readable amount, deadline, generation, contract identity, and status. `gods_of_africa_calculate_demand_amount` scales the amount and selects the contract band from current capacity, Strength, Wrath, Chaos, need, doctrine, conduct, and repetition, while `gods_of_africa_calculate_demand_deadline` resolves the matching constant-backed deadline. The `gods_of_africa_demand` constants include explicit ceremonial, standard, severe, emergency, and `punitive_band` values plus their deadline constants, including `deadline_punitive`; this is source evidence for conduct-aware escalation, not a balance approval. The family is selected only from a current African need—land equipment, support equipment, fuel, manpower, industry, emergency relief, transport reserves, or aircraft reserves—and a target capacity profile above a protected operating floor. When all maintained need flags are clear, the owner returns without forming a contract, so the default priority cannot create an arbitrary demand during a healthy interval. The emergency lane requires an initialized debt variable above the high-debt threshold, or above the medium-debt threshold after the needs-profile focus is completed; an absent debt variable is treated as a healthy zero during partial restoration. `gods_of_africa_set_payment_capacity_requirement`, `gods_of_africa_set_substitute_capacity_requirement`, and `gods_of_africa_set_partial_capacity_requirement` call `gods_of_africa_compute_capacity_requirement` to keep the public transfer amount separate from a hidden requirement that adds the family-specific operating floor for equipment, fuel, manpower, or industry. The capacity and affordability triggers consume those hidden requirements with the current-generation contract guards. Material compliance debits the participant's real stockpile, fuel, manpower, or political power and credits the same amount to the African host; infantry transfers also replenish the maintained Event 012 common-defence reserve used by the shortage profile. Partial payment records only the remaining amount; substitution and extension use the frozen contract rather than rerolling it.

Participants can comply, negotiate, substitute a valid offer, refuse, or permanently defy. Negotiation freezes up to three deterministic, capacity-checked alternatives with the contract; the participant submits one offer, while the host explicitly accepts or rejects it. Rejection raises Wrath slightly, records the attempt, restores the original payment fields, and leaves the extended demand available; returning to the demand cancels an unsubmitted offer. Refusal raises Wrath and invokes punishment review without silently ending the relationship. Defiance stops ordinary demands, records a permanent offense, raises the Wrath floor, and leaves punishment, retaliation, protection withdrawal, and conflict available. Only the reconciliation path can restore ordinary demand eligibility, and reconciliation preserves prior-defiance history.

Outside an active demand, `gods_of_africa_offer_voluntary_aid` provides one participant-side offering when the host's maintained need profile shows a land-equipment or fuel shortage. Land equipment is selected first when both needs are present; otherwise fuel is selected. The effect rechecks the current generation, host activity, need, war state, cooldown, and sender reserve before debiting 500 units, credits the host's real stores (and the common defence reserve for equipment), records an `aid` history entry, and opens the separate `chaosx.nr12.614` receipt. The first voluntary offer lowers Wrath by four and later offers by two; a 180-day participant cooldown and a 500-unit reserve floor prevent repeated tiny shipments. The host rebuilds its need profile after a successful transfer, and the existing reliable-partner memory can make the participant eligible for future protection.

If a participant enters a war with Africa while an ordinary demand is open, the owner-local tick cancels that contract once, records a treaty break, raises Wrath by the bounded war amount, and reports the pause. No ordinary demand is dispatched while the participant remains at war; after peace, the normal registry and cooldown gates may issue a fresh contract.

## Punishment and protection

Punishment intent is calculated separately from the capability ceiling. `gods_of_africa_evaluate_punishment` first writes `gods_of_africa_punishment_intent_tier` from Wrath and escalation evidence, copies that intent into `gods_of_africa_pending_punishment_tier`, and applies the first-failure and host Strength-band ceilings. `gods_of_africa_apply_punishment` then validates and prices only the capped pending tier, fails closed from catastrophe when the extreme gate is absent, records both last punishment tiers, and clears the transient intent and pending values. Wrath, failure history, proven offenses, failed-demand severity, Chaos, and hostility increase desired punishment; Strength, African capability, evolution access, target condition, cooldowns, and valid Event 013 inputs cap what can actually happen. A first ordinary refusal is capped at a low tier. High Wrath alone never opens catastrophe. The extreme route additionally requires extreme Strength, very high Wrath, high Chaos, repeated failure or a major offense, the required evolution, a valid ultimatum, no target or global extreme cooldown, and an Africa state that is not collapsing.

Weak Africa therefore produces pressure, unrest, limited disruption, failed manifestations, and bounded sabotage. Natural disasters use the Event 013 gateway and fail closed when that gateway rejects the call. Any civilian population loss uses the shared exact state-population transaction and records Deaths once. Protection is also capacity-bound: equipment, fuel, access, relief, reconstruction, and disaster mitigation are paid by Africa and are available only to eligible cooperative participants.

## Africa-side agency and ending

The host category exposes the current Strength band, doctrine, one qualitative priority, and bounded target actions. Africa can choose a reciprocal, exaction, or ambiguity doctrine; grant one-off leniency; mark a proven offender; protect a reliable partner; pardon an eligible temporary floor; and escalate only a proven offense. Priority-offender marks are capped at three concurrent marked participants, and archiving a stale marked participant releases its host slot. These actions modify the same ledger and do not raise the Strength ceiling.

When Event 012 secures the continent, ordinary demands stop before settlement. The owner computes one hidden relationship result per surviving participant: Favored, Respected, Distrusted, or Enemy. Loyal support earns substantial reciprocal rewards. A defeated, capitulated, or disappearing unifier cancels active demands, punishment jobs, participant presentation, and current authority. Delayed work is generation-checked and cannot revive the old system.

## Focus, decisions, and logs

The doctrine and functional branches are an overlay in `africa_continental_focus_tree`, gated by the Event 012 Evolution I and Gods activation proofs. They cover preparation, reciprocal and exaction doctrine, provision, oaths, protection, judgment, later evolution expansion, and continental settlement without creating a detached tree. The reciprocal and exaction route flags feed the same demand scaler as the host doctrine variable; the needs-profile focus opens emergency relief at the medium debt band, the arsenals focus rewards support-equipment transfers with host stability, and the logistics focus accepts either convoy or train shortage, shortens the repeat interval, and exposes the matching priority lane. Reserve, relief, and expedition focuses alter protection timing and participant defensive rewards, while the last-sentence focus is an explicit catastrophe gate. Focus-gated host actions expose leniency, protection, offender marking, and settlement only after their corresponding branch capability exists, without making the core tribute loop depend on the overlay. The ordinary participant and host decision categories carry the two public values, one active mission, phased response actions, the voluntary aid action, selected-target controls, defiance preparation, reconciliation, and bounded protection/priority actions. A defiant participant receives a real reconciliation mission with a 60-day review window; timeout records a failed review, raises Wrath, and applies a retry cooldown, while completion clears the pending reconciliation state. Routine transactions remain in the owner ledger; activation, major punishment, defiance, protection, settlement, and termination use Event 012 event-log milestones.

The host priority surface is phased to keep the five core material lanes plus one advanced lane visible at a time. Emergency relief is the sixth lane before the provision focuses, support equipment replaces it after the arsenals focus, and aircraft reserves replace it after the logistics focus; automatic demand-family fallback still covers every maintained family.
