# IW-028 Epirus package

IW-028 is the Event 006 Epirus package. Its reusable carrier is `BBX`, its compact installed-map anchor is state `185` (Epirus), and its former host is `GRE`. State `805` (Northern Epirus) is optional extension territory and is trimmed whenever its allocation would violate the unique-state or host-survival contract. The reservation group is `RG-185`. The package is in the Balkans and Danube region, uses standard package depth, and is admitted only when state 185 is uniquely available and Greece retains a protected remnant.

## Reusable country identity

Later events can select `BBX` from `chaosx_country_independence_wave` or `chaosx_country_region_balkans_and_danube` after recording their own origin and proving their own host, anchor, and package-readiness contract. They must not call the Event 006 setup dispatcher by tag alone. Their overlay must be additive and must leave a meaningful existing tree and origin untouched.

## Package gameplay

The package gives every released BBX country the shared Event 006 focus framework and adds Epirus hooks for the civic-mandate and mountain-defence ledgers. The five government routes are constitutional republic, popular council, traditional authority, emergency mountain commission, and patron compact. The package also exposes former-host settlement, the Balkan Federation formable family, the informal Ionian network action, and the standard mountain-frontier reinforcement pathways.

The Balkan Federation route is implemented through the shared FORM-09 transaction documented in `form09_balkan_federation.md`. `BBX` can carry the X-ending `BLX` cosmetic identity after a frozen congress of at least three consenting reviewed anchors. Full-integration consent transfers only certified compact territory and military assets; autonomous members keep their country, tree, package, territory, and forces. The resulting federal carrier receives a general-staff idea and a paid border-board project.

The visible decision category contains a founding crisis mission and eleven paid projects. They consume administration, security, diplomatic, civilian-factory, and strategic capacity over time. Each project changes a ledger, route, host relation, network, league value, force pathway, or founding outcome. No political-power store or free-unit loop is used.

Lifecycle ideas distinguish divided authority from the stable Epirus Compact. Five route ideas are mutually exclusive and are removed or replaced when a new route is installed or the package generation is cleaned up. The AI layers prioritize anchor survival, former-host restraint, mountain defence, settlement, and route-specific force posture.

## Identity assets

The visible political leader is sourced male historical subject Georgios Christakis-Zografos, who headed the Autonomous Republic of Northern Epirus in 1914. The corps commander is sourced male historical subject Spyros Spyromilios, a gendarmerie officer and Northern Epirus autonomist commander. Both are deliberately used as alternate-history survivals beyond their historical deaths, and both use their original archival identities as source placeholders without an ImageGen repaint. Their original source files are flat in `docs/assets/portraits/006_independence_wave/` (`iw028_epirus_georgios_christakis_zografos_source_placeholder_2026_08_06__portrait_BBX_independence_wave_georgios_christakis_zografos_original.jpg` and `iw028_epirus_spyros_spyromilios_source_placeholder_2026_08_09__portrait_BBX_independence_wave_spyros_spyromilios.jpg`); crops, provenance, and review evidence are in the single `processed/` subfolder. Runtime wiring is in `interface/006_independence_wave_portraits_registry.gfx`.

The BBX flag is a flat ImageGen reconstruction of the historically attested 1914 Northern Epirus design: blue field, broad white Greek cross, and a centered black crowned double-headed eagle. Its surviving flag photograph, period stamp, public-domain reconstruction, ImageGen source, flattened master, processed ladder, final TGAs, and manifest are retained under `docs/assets/006_independence_wave/event006_missing_flags_2026_08_02/`, with the runtime family under `gfx/flags/`. It is presented as a historical-design reconstruction, not a painted flag artwork.

No Event 006 advisor icons, advisor portraits, dossier portraits, or `_small` portrait derivatives are required for this package.
