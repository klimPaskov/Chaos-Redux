# Event 006 Oyo, Aceh and Mon Signature Modules

This document covers the Level 3 signature mechanics for Oyo (DSX/IW-097), Aceh (ATJ/IW-150) and Mon State (IAX/IW-161). The package modules remain tied to their exact active Event 006 origin and current owned anchor; their focus groups are shared-focus additions to the parent Event 006 tree.

## Acceptance and implementation status

The D27 and D31 tranche decisions accepted these Level 3 packages while their Level 2 layers were implemented.
The task owner's 2026-10-06 G6 follow-up authorizes the detailed mechanics below.
The 2026-10-07 parent accepts the recommended Mon river-service and PUSA education payoffs under the user's standing instruction to take recommended choices.
The source imports all 31 signature focuses into the main Event 006 tree and registers the three signature achievements with their nine reviewed runtime icons.
The decision, focus, localisation and dependency handoffs record the source review; imported-focus layout, complete campaign AI evidence and native lifecycle acceptance remain separate open gates.
This is source integration of these modules, not completion of Event 006 or its country/portrait package goal.

The accepted focus architecture requires package-aware branches whose rewards change actual decisions, ideas, buildings, diplomacy or formable access, and existing vanilla trees remain additive. Oyo is grounded in the Alaafin, Oyo Mesi, Ibadan and Yoruba town authorities; Aceh distinguishes district, ulama, school and port institutions and keeps PUSA out of the 1936 start; Mon uses local councils, monasteries, language schools and Moulmein river administration without requiring foreign help for its mainland compact. See the [Part 4 focus-tree adaptation rules](../../specs/006_independence_wave_specs/specs/006_independence_wave_spec_part_4_focus_tree_architecture.md#shared-focus-tree-adaptation-rules), [Part 5 Southeast Asia signature-package themes](../../specs/006_independence_wave_specs/specs/006_independence_wave_spec_part_5_country_packages_and_regional_overlays.md#regional-overlay-13-southeast-asia-east-asia-and-oceania), and the [signature-country research dossiers](../../specs/006_independence_wave_specs/research/006_signature_country_research_dossiers.md#iw-097-oyo).

## Oyo: crown, council and city terms

DSX enters the module after its Oyo Mesi, Ibadan, horse-guard and Yoruba-town Level 2 work is complete, the signature branch is open and the stored anchor is owned and controlled. The shared `independence_wave_internal_power_balance` chooses one of three institutional settlements: the Alaafin's charter, an Oyo Mesi mandate, or a voluntary Yoruba town federation. If the generic balance is already settled, the matching choice follows that existing result instead of shifting the balance a second time. The selected signature idea replaces `independence_wave_dsx_oyo_mesi_council`.

The Alaafin route commissions the cavalry charter; the Oyo Mesi route registers a town guard under council direction, adding a small land-army defence bonus without creating a free division; and the town route guarantees Ibadan's negotiated place. Each branch's single route idea replaces the Level 2 Oyo Mesi council, and the final compact focus adds no fourth idea. The road survey authorizes a costed market-road project in DSX's current capital anchor. The project adds one infrastructure level and opens one shared building slot for follow-on market development. Its callback is withheld unless the same anchor can still legally construct the exact infrastructure level when the project expires.

The congress focus opens the existing regional-ambition and leadership actions when their own gates pass. The Oyo Compact requires a completed authority settlement, completed market roads and congress authorization. It publishes a durable completion receipt and advances the shared ambition values without adding a fourth national spirit. It does not claim that Oyo represents or annexes every Yoruba community.

## Aceh: district guarantees, school networks and a port trust

ATJ enters after the notables, madrasah and pepper-port projects are complete, the signature branch is open and the stored anchor remains owned and controlled. The uleebalang district, ulama school-board and shared-council branches are mutually exclusive and follow the current internal power-balance result. Their selected idea replaces the Level 2 Council of Notables. The later school network is a separate institution from the selected government route.

The school-network focus authorizes an optional PUSA organization decision.
PUSA is named from 5 May 1939, the dated foundation recorded by the [Indonesian Ministry of Education and Culture entry](https://esi.kemenbud.go.id/wiki/Teungku_Muhammad_Daud_Beureueh) and the [Oxford Academic account](https://academic.oup.com/book/25629/chapter/193029309).
Successful paid organization replaces the ordinary school-network idea and grants one named 50% industry research bonus, representing practical teacher training in this alternate-history program.
Its durable generation receipt prevents replenishment through repeated completion, cleanup/setup or CXT application in the same origin generation.

The port-board focus authorizes a merchant-credit decision. On completion it builds one naval-base level at Banda Aceh (province 13398 in anchor state 1050), using the current controlled anchor state, and replaces the Level 2 pepper-port idea with merchant credit. The trade modifier shifts from +10% opinion/−2% consumer-goods use to +5% opinion/+3% consumer-goods use while the debt is open. The action requires that the anchor can construct a naval base and that the exact Banda Aceh province remains controlled and below its ten-level cap, both before payment and at expiry. A separate 180-day strategic-cost project retires the credit, removes its burden and replaces the credit idea with Aceh Port Trust (+5% trade opinion/−2% consumer-goods use). The maritime congress focus enters the existing FORM-36 selection and consent path; it does not create another invitation, transfer land or form the federation itself. The existing vanilla Aceh roster remains unchanged.

## Mon: local federal council, river administration and frontier watch

IAX enters after the Moulmein council, Mon-language school and port projects are complete, the signature branch is open and the stored anchor remains owned and controlled. Mon can choose the local council route or a central Moulmein River Office. Both choices respect an earlier matching balance result and replace the Level 2 federal-council idea. The language-school focus replaces the ordinary Mon school idea with a Mon Language School Trust.

The local route's autonomy capstone requires the local charter, paid Salween transport works and paid Tenasserim border watch.
The separate FORM-37 entry also requires the accepted exact federation roster.
The transport project adds one infrastructure level and one shared building slot at the current capital anchor.
The border-watch project places one bunker level at province 1296 in Tenasserim anchor state 994, adjoining Western Siam state 289.
It requires provincial control, native construction legality and a province level below the jungle cap of three at selection and expiry.
Both construction callbacks build instantly only after their final gate passes.

The river-office route can commission river services once its charter, school trust, paid transport and paid border watch are ready.
The 120-day administration project pays 20 command power and 5,000 manpower and commits two factories.
Successful expiry replaces the plain river-office institution with the River Service Administration, providing 5% political-power gain and 10% infrastructure construction speed, and changes the country ledger by +15 capacity, +5 legitimacy and −5 instability through the shared writer and clamp.
The project snapshots package, generation and anchor, rejects changed anchors and institutions, and preserves its award-generation history through cleanup.
A federal charter replacing the plain office cancels the work without reward or refund.

The school-delegate focus enables a separate optional program. It can run only while both native BRM (Burma) and SIA (Siam) exist and are at peace with IAX. Completion adds a reciprocal +10 opinion modifier with each for 24 months. If IAX loses its capital, war starts or a counterpart disappears during the 120-day project, the attempt ends after its diplomatic cost has been paid; the authorization remains so Mon can retry when both counterparts exist and peace returns. A successful exchange is terminal and cannot reward the pair twice. This program does not gate FORM-37; IAX has a fully local path through council, school, transport, border and autonomy work.

FORM-36 and FORM-37 remain owned by the shared ready-formable contract. The package capstones select the matching family and call the common selected-family readiness wrapper only after their exact local requirements pass. Invitations, consent, shared formation cost, integration and autonomous-member compacts remain in that common contract. These focuses do not annex native Burma or Siam and never trigger formation automatically.

## Timed-project lifecycle and CXT extension

The eight timed decisions share one active project slot and pay their shared Event 006 cost when selected.
Active receipts bind the package and origin generation; river services also snapshots the anchor.
Continuing proofs retain the exact live package, current force/framework generation and controlled capital without requiring a release-time prepared flag or a living former host.
Success receipts are written before the active project is cleared and protect successful expiry from a later cancellation test.
Cleanup removes native decisions, active receipts and package ideas; it preserves the Mon and PUSA award-generation history.
Invalid work grants no reward or refund.
Construction is applied once at guarded expiry.

One modifier-free CXT carrier, `independence_wave_signature_modules_cxt_extension`, matches its `_apply` effect.
Startup registers the token through one existing country; the CXT-specific fallback registers and marks pending content for the player's debug action.
Both registration call boundaries initialize the temporary added-output storage before calling the nested helper.
Manual application visits only aligned active-country entries and reapplies exact live DSX, ATJ or IAX signature setup.
An empty selected ATJ/IAX federation contract may request country-owned relay `chaosx.nr6.8618` to refresh readiness with ROOT rebased to its actual carrier.
The relay revalidates generation, family, exact package and the absence of proposals, transaction locks, consent or commitment before registration.
This path grants no resources or ideas, sends no invitations and forms no federation.

## Signature governance API

`independence_wave_ready_formable_apply_signature_governance` runs in the active ATJ/IW-150 or IAX/IW-161 country scope.
Its input is caller-created temporary `independence_wave_ready_formable_governance_choice`, using the matching key in `constant:independence_wave_ready_formable_governance`.
The supported choices are `atj_district`, `atj_school_board`, `atj_shared_council`, `iax_local_council` and `iax_river_office`.
An absent input, mismatched package or unsupported choice does nothing; there is no granting default.
When a current-generation carrier charter or member compact owns the institution, the helper keeps that spirit and its integration stage, clears the old specific source-term receipts and records the chosen terms for exact restoration.
Otherwise it removes the other plain governance ideas from the same package and applies the chosen institution.
School and port ideas are preserved, and the helper grants no resources or country-ledger reward.
The five signature institution-completion writers use this API so signature politics may follow federation entry without adding a second government spirit.
The Mon river-service project separately requires the plain river-office institution throughout its paid work; a charter replacing that institution cancels the project without reward or refund.

```text
set_temp_variable = { independence_wave_ready_formable_governance_choice = constant:independence_wave_ready_formable_governance.iax_local_council }
independence_wave_ready_formable_apply_signature_governance = yes
```
