# Event 006 FORM-03 Post-Charter Progression

## Purpose and invariant

FORM-03 is the LCX Confederation of Low Countries settlement carried by a
successfully committed AFX or AGX origin. It is a post-formation constitutional
and industrial state machine, not a second formable transaction. BEL, HOL, and
LUX remain sovereign countries: the progression never transfers or cores their
states, changes their capitals or focus trees, or creates a subject relation.
The only formable integration remains the already audited consenting AFX/AGX
anchor transaction.

The progression starts exactly once after the shared formable transaction has
set both `independence_wave_formable_active` and
`independence_wave_formable_committed`. It never starts from an identity or
integration adapter and therefore cannot interfere with pre-commit rollback.

## Public state

The carrier owns two clamped values from 0 through 100:

- `independence_wave_form03_federal_accommodation`, initialized to 15;
- `independence_wave_form03_industrial_integration`, initialized to 15.

The stored phase moves through provisional, drafting, ratification, complete,
compromise, or rupture. The stored language model is parallel federal services,
territorial administration with federal appeal, or one working register with
protected local services. All thresholds and deltas live in
`common/script_constants/006_independence_wave_constants_registry.txt`.

## Step-by-step play

1. The post-commit opening event publishes the two values and reveals the six
   focus branch below `independence_wave_establish_integration_commission`.
2. The carrier funds a 180-day language convention and selects one structural
   service model. The model changes follow-up actions and sends an explicit
   constitutional-status event to exact sovereign BEL, HOL, and LUX members.
3. A full sovereign member must fund its own 180-day language-guarantee action.
   A member may instead remain a limited economic associate or withhold.
4. The carrier funds anchor works in state 34 and/or state 36, standardizes rail
   and customs manifests, may request Development Compact technical support,
   and can invite sovereign members to fund one bounded corridor state.
5. The convergence focus requires both values at least 50 and every living
   member's constitutional status resolved. It activates the 360-day
   ratification mission rather than relying on periodic polling.
6. Full ratification requires both values at least 70 plus a real federal
   language scope: carrier control of both anchors or one sovereign signatory.
7. A timeout selects charter without works, industrial directorate, dual
   compromise, or rupture from the stored values and member state. Compromise
   permits bounded repairs and resubmission. Rupture requires a 360-day reopening
   action before the same repair path is available.

## Sovereign corridor and territorial consequences

Carrier projects apply durable legal-operating modifiers to state 34 or state
36 and queue one non-instant infrastructure level only while a legal level
remains. Sovereign-member corridor targets are exact:

- BEL: state 6 or 977;
- HOL: state 7 or 35;
- LUX: state 8.

State 980 is excluded. A member chooses, owns, controls, and pays for its own
target. Completed physical infrastructure remains after legal cleanup, while
FORM-03 state modifiers and participation flags are removed. Player-facing
text treats state 36 as a mixed coarse game state and does not describe every
resident as Frisian.

## Existing-system integrations

- Language model, anchor work, full ratification, rupture, appeals, and service
  actions use the shared five-value effects.
- Only the carrier's saved `independence_wave_former_host` can receive bounded
  language, corridor, or ratification-window withholding ledger deltas.
- Development Compact support reserves 20 shared points at selection, settles
  carrier contribution/confidence and global cohesion/common cause on success,
  and refunds 10 once if canceled.
- A sovereign Event 006-origin member receives 10 network standing for its own
  completed corridor; an ordinary BEL, HOL, or LUX country receives no invented
  Event 006 ledger.
- State 34 works feed the existing AFX industrial-continuity value only for an
  AFX carrier. State 36 works feed existing AGX waterline and coastal-security
  values only for an AGX carrier.

## Cleanup and validation posture

Cleanup first sets a guard, settles any outstanding reserve refund, removes the
mission and timed actions, removes all six ideas and three legal state modifier
families, clears exact member state, and finally clears public variables and the
guard. Cancellation, timeout, and withdrawal penalties are suppressed during
cleanup. Dispatch is fixed to AFX, AGX, BEL, HOL, and LUX; there is no daily,
weekly, monthly, or generic country scan.

`independence_wave_form03_progression_attested` is deliberately not set by the
implementation. Automatic/scenario readiness remains closed until the parent
audits every accepted scenario and explicitly promotes the attestation.

subsystem.
