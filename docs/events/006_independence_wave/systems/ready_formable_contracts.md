# Event 006 ready-member federation contracts

FORM-17, FORM-27, FORM-29, FORM-31, FORM-32, FORM-36 and FORM-37 use the existing Event 006 invitation, consent, congress, payment and commit framework.
They place a cosmetic identity on an existing carrier and preserve every member's sovereign tag, origin, territory, focus content and forces.
They do not annex, create subjects, grant blanket cores or end member origins.
The accepted basis is Part 6 and decisions D28, D29, D30, D31 and D33; the October 6 and October 10 implementation instructions authorize their concrete two-stage institutions.

Membership requires exact active Event 006 package identity, registry generation, stored anchor, ownership/control and capital, plus peace and the accepted geographical or diplomatic connection to the actual proposing country.
Fixed-state owner queries use their enclosing country through PREV.PREV, so a recipient event querying its stored carrier retains the carrier's profile and connections.
The two Punjab alternatives contribute one FDX member, and FORM-32 requires all four packages.
FORM-27 requires all three Great Lakes packages and FORM-31 both island packages.
FORM-29 accepts three or four of its four packages: the roster check lists every three-package combination, the ledger accepts exactly three or exactly four rows, and the second stage needs at least three consents instead of every listed member.
A living vanilla AFA that Event 006 did not release is not a member, because membership needs the active registry row, the IW-114 package and anchor 908.
Buganda, Rwanda and Burundi share one reservation group, and so do Harar, the Afar state, Somaliland and Tigray, so each family forms only from countries released in distinct waves.
Maritime shipping proof requires the listed ports and the carrier's convoy reserve. FORM-31 uses the same proof for Madagascar and Mauritius, and an exact reserve passes.

Selection loads a family profile and builds bounded aligned ledgers.
The ledger builder owns the temporary consent output before invoking the evaluator.
These four families remain observers until a real sequence-bound invitation is accepted or withheld; generic AI willingness does not manufacture consent.
The carrier records its own reply when it issues the proposal.
Human members receive event `chaosx.nr6.8617`, while AI members resolve the existing deterministic family predicate.

The visible popup keeps immutable carrier, family, sequence and both-generation snapshots until it closes.
Proposal cleanup invalidates pending delivery while preserving that shown snapshot and delivery lock.
Every reply effect revalidates the snapshot against the current invitation; an initially visible option can remain visible after its conditions change, so option visibility is never the mutation authority.
The first option refuses a current invitation, or merely closes a stale one.
The documented native timeout selects that first option after thirteen days.
After closure, any current replacement invitation is delivered through carrier-owned relay `chaosx.nr6.8619`.
This serialization prevents an old popup from accepting, refusing or clearing a replacement receipt.
Native popup timing and closure remain user-owned validation.

Before mutation, each cosmetic identity must be unused.
The identity adapter records current generation, family and proposal sequence before taking its global lock.
The integration proof recognizes only that carrier's matching reservation and cosmetic; a foreign or stale reservation remains blocked.
Cleanup releases the owned lock and charter receipts even after another cosmetic has replaced it, while dropping only the cosmetic still owned by this contract.
Reciprocal access and guarantees use an explicitly captured member target, and removals require the contract's ownership receipts.

Each family has two sequential 180-day projects. FORM-27 pays a diplomatic stage then a security stage, FORM-29 a security stage then a diplomatic stage, and FORM-31 a diplomatic stage then a security stage; none reserves factories.
Selection uses the full shared price quote, and payment occurs once at startup.
FORM-32 stage 1 and FORM-36 stage 2 reserve two factories; their startup boundary rechecks CP/manpower without counting its own native reservation twice.
Both stage gates reject the opposite active stage.
Expiry revalidates exact family, generation, current roster and continuing consent before evolving the installed charter/member compacts once.
Cancellation checks the matching stage receipt, and completed-stage flags protect success before active receipts are cleared.
Failed or cancelled work grants no benefit or refund.
The shared security price shows three material/experience fields in its compact row and all four spendable resources, including manpower, in its hover quote; its payment is unchanged.

An existing package institution is replaced only when exactly one supported choice is present.
Carrier and member receipt sets distinguish ATJ's four choices and IAX's four choices, including its commissioned river administration.
Signature politics completed after federation entry update the recorded source terms within the owned charter/compact instead of adding another government spirit.
Restoration returns the exact recorded institution only while that package's current framework remains live.
Departing or generation-ended packages restore none; unrelated school and port ideas are preserved.
The [signature governance API](../signature_modules.md#signature-governance-api) records inputs, outputs and usage.

The seven map overlays reuse the Event 006 grouped window and its twenty-two-family layout.
They add twenty-three candidate pieces and forty-six unresolved/qualifying DDS assets; the FDX alternatives share a four-member denominator, and the Horn overlay shows all four candidates against a denominator of three.
The actual consumers are the ready-formable route and integration categories, attached to `independence_wave_formable_state_puzzle_scripted_gui` and group `independence_wave_formables`.
State sprites are registered in `interface/chaosx_formable_state_puzzles.gfx`, layout in `interface/chaosx_formable_state_puzzle_group_independence_wave_formables.gui`, and manifests under `docs/formables/state_puzzles/006_form17_state_puzzle/` and the corresponding 32/36/37 folders.
The accepted NCX/IDX/MRX/MSX flag art has generic and ideology variants at all three native sizes, and LKX, HLX and IOX follow the same pattern (see `docs/assets/006_independence_wave/regional_formable_flags_20261010/`).
All four ideological paths deliberately use the same fictional federation charter symbol; the aliases copy the accepted base files byte-for-byte and make cosmetic flag precedence explicit.
Their palettes live in the engine-loaded `common/countries/cosmetic.txt`; LKX, HLX and IOX were checked against every country tag, cosmetic tag and flag file in the mod and the installed game before use.
Existing integration, border arbitration, network and league decision icons are reused; no new portrait or model is introduced by these contracts.

Source review and bounded callback, reservation, relation and institution-order cases are recorded in the October 7 dependency and decision handoffs.
MCP script checking covers only its reported commands/scopes and leaves native argument semantics explicit.
Imported focus, event helper/lifecycle, production GUI before/after, full named-scenario AI and live consumer evidence remain open.
