## Millerovo charter sequence

Setup starts a 420-day `Millerovo Host Charter` mission that requires the council to keep state 245 owned, controlled, and capitalized, publish two paid receipts, set a service quota, settle one government, and complete the charter capstone.

State 238 can improve the later corridor but is never required by the founding mission or the p39 muster.

The railhead survey spends 25 political power and takes the shared 75-day short project duration.

The district-petition hearing spends 20 political power and takes the shared 45-day rapid project duration.

The projects serialize and issue their receipt only after successful completion while Millerovo remains under DON control.

No action requires or commits a civilian factory because states 245 and 238 have none.

The three government decisions each spend 30 political power after the two issue focuses and paid receipts are complete.

The constitutional district compact also spends 10 command power and grants 10 stability.

The elected host assembly grants 5 stability and records the route's petition-rights settlement.

The emergency host staff grants 5 war support and loses 5 stability, then starts a 90-day civilian-review mission.

The emergency review action spends 10 political power and grants 5 stability; missing the review deadline fails the charter.

The service-quota focus records the district's mounted-service commitment, raises the obligation owed to the former host, and improves shared security, capacity, and network standing. It does not create an additional division.

Charter success records completion of the host arrangement and sets the internal FORM-11 later-gate marker. FORM-11 remains separate, with no current decision consumer, carrier, or formation effect.

Failure adds `don_fractured_host_charter`, records lower legitimacy and higher instability and former-host hostility, and does not create or change FORM-11 membership.
