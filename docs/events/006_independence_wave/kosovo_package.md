# IW-031 Kosovo package

IW-031 reuses vanilla `KOS` and its native state 802 rather than creating a second Kosovo tag.

The package preserves vanilla country and state history, assigns state 802 as the unique Event 006 anchor, records the releasing country as the former host, and loads the full shared `independence_wave_focus_tree`.

## Public mechanics

Kosovo tracks Civic Concord and Municipal Reach from 0 to 100.

Both values must reach 60, and one of four government settlements must take office, before the 570-day Cantonal Compact crisis can resolve.

The ten paid projects cover municipal administration, community guarantees, territorial guards, the Yugoslav settlement ledger, four government routes, durable sovereignty, and the Balkan network corridor.

Only one project can run at a time. Projects consume command, manpower, equipment, transport, stability, war support, and factory commitments, and each cancelled project applies one failure consequence.

## Government routes

- The Agrarian Civic Union forms an elected assembly with explicit community protections and uses the researched Albanian civic standard.
- The Kosovo Workers' Front forms a Municipal Workers' Council to organize mines, workshops, rail crews, and municipal services.
- The Federal Cantonal League preserves municipal autonomy through a federal settlement and uses the researched Yugoslav municipal standard.
- The Territorial Defence Command places the regional guards under one emergency staff while municipal authorities continue to supply them.

Every route changes party rule, popularities, route ideas, shared Event 006 values, and the package's public ledgers.

## Forces, ambitions, and FORM-09

The p31 force package uses `mountain_frontier`, military tradition 55, reinforcement mask 1047, and no navy or air inheritance.

Its five paths are militia integration, regional guards, secure depots, volunteer corridors, and capital-border defence.

Kosovo participates in the informal network and league routes and may join FORM-09 as a sovereign member through state 802.

It is not a FORM-09 carrier and never replaces the BBX/BAX carrier identity.

## Identity and assets

The vanilla post-2008 Kosovo flag remains untouched and is not used as an interwar route identity.

`KOS_independence_wave_albanian_civicX` uses a flat ImageGen reconstruction of the attested 1928 Albanian red field and black double-headed eagle, explicitly as an Albanian-led civic standard rather than a universal Kosovo flag.

`KOS_independence_wave_yugoslav_municipalX` uses the 1931 Yugoslav blue-white-red tricolour as the provisional municipal standard retained by the federal, socialist, and emergency settlements.

Both runtime packages use native TGA flag ladders at 82×52, 41×26, and 10×7 with no `.gfx` sprite.

Ferhat Bey Draga, Miladin Popović, and Shaban Polluzha use attributed archival source placeholders with explicit head-and-shoulders crops, 156×210 runtime candidates, and dedicated DDS and sprite wiring. Draga serves the civic and federal routes, Popović serves the socialist route, and Polluzha serves the emergency route and territorial corps command.

The durable portrait archive records the exact source pages, dates, rights notes, crop boxes, and checksums. The Popović photograph dates from 1943–1944, and the Polluzha runtime source is a named-individual archival portrait reproduced in a later exhibition photograph. The earlier group-image crop is retained as rejected research evidence, and the exhibition reproduction's original photographer and date remain documented caveats. These are explicit source placeholders for the accepted grounded-portrait workflow, not claims that the photographs were taken in 1936.

No advisor, high-command, dossier, or small portrait is part of IW-031.
