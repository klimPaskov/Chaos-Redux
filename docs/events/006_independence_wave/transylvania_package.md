# IW-023 Transylvania package

IW-023 uses the registered TRA identity, compact Transylvania anchor at state 84, and optional North Transylvania state 76. Its release package keeps the former-host relationship recorded by the release transaction and the accepted staged-settlement approach for mixed communities and competing border claims.

## Focus carrier and regional callback

When TRA has `austro_hungarian_releasable_focus`, package setup preserves that meaningful tree and assigns the Event 006 additive overlay. The IW-023 prepared-package proof accepts the shared full-framework or reviewed additive-carrier contract. When the Austro-Hungarian tree is absent, the existing full Event 006 framework remains the package path.

Completing `independence_wave_overlay_address_former_host` still runs `independence_wave_tra_focus_arbitrate_danube_border` for an active IW-023 origin. It raises federal legitimacy and frontier cohesion by the centralized standard gain, clamps both values to their 0–100 range, and refreshes the settlement lifecycle.

The package also imports the five REG-03 district and frontier focuses into the full framework and the reviewed Austro-Hungarian carrier. Their four-focus route costs 24 focus units, or 168 days at normal speed, and requires state 84 to remain the controlled capital. The root raises federal legitimacy by 5 and the rail survey raises frontier cohesion by 5. The mutually exclusive civilian branch raises federal legitimacy by 10 and lowers frontier cohesion by 5; the frontier branch reverses those ledger changes. The docket raises both by 5 and remains unavailable until either the paid assembly or paid regulars-screening project succeeds.

The complete focus IDs, reward ordering, source wiring, and validation limits are documented in [REG-03 Danube Package Focus Groups](reg03_danube_focus_groups.md).

The matching paid assembly or screening project contributes one additional 5-point bonus to its branch ledger whether its receipt exists before focus completion or the paid project succeeds afterward. A cancelled project grants no companion reward. Its durable awarded marker follows completed focus history on the retained carrier; a full-framework reload clears both together. The group's focus effects do not waive depot, assembly, screening, former-host, or Danube network costs, and they do not transfer states or settle claims.

The callback does not transfer states, add claims, or make any maximal ethnic border automatic. The shared focus's generic public-settlement reward and the existing costed Transylvania decisions continue to handle host relations and negotiated integration.

## Local mechanics

The local ledgers begin at 38 frontier cohesion and 44 federal legitimacy. A stable Danube settlement requires both to reach 60. The shared change effect clamps both ledgers and refreshes the visible lifecycle ideas `tra_divided_border_authority` and `tra_danube_settlement`.

Transylvania's costed decisions continue to govern the multiethnic assembly, Carpathian depots, defecting regulars, government routes, former-host obligations, and Danube network. Route effects use the same centralized values and can improve or damage both ledgers according to the choice and outcome.

## Icons and localisation

No new icon is needed. The group reuses five existing sprites defined in `interface/006_independence_wave.gfx`; their exact texture paths and all focus and tooltip localisation keys are listed in [REG-03 Danube Package Focus Groups](reg03_danube_focus_groups.md).

The groups have no custom `ai_will_do` weights. The current probability adapter omits imported shared focuses and the recorded baseline lacks typed campaign inputs, so route-aware AI selection remains unresolved.

## Future direction

Further Transylvania focus content should connect to researched local institutions, federal rights, river and rail access, and negotiated border outcomes. Any expansion should preserve the compact release footprint and use specific package research before introducing additional territorial or community claims.
