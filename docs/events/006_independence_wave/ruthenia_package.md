# IW-038 Ruthenia Package

IW-038 reuses vanilla Ruthenia and releases its Event 006 country from state 73 without replacing the ordinary vanilla country history or roster.

The release transaction preserves the actual former host captured by the Independence Wave planner, so Czechoslovakia is an expected opening host rather than a hardcoded runtime assumption.

## Compact and founding crisis

The package exposes Civic Concord and Mountain Security as two visible values from 0 to 100.

Both values must reach 60 for the mountain compact to become stable.

The founding mission lasts 600 days and requires a selected government, both compact values at 60, and ownership and control of state 73.

Failure marks the compact crisis as lost and cancels active package projects through their ordinary failure effects.

## Projects and governments

The Ruthenian category contains ten paid projects covering depots, border guards, community guarantees, former-host ledgers, four government settlements, durable sovereignty, and the Carpathian network corridor.

Only one package project may run at a time, every project requires capital control, and project costs draw from the shared command, manpower, equipment, transport, stability, and war-support ledgers, with one civilian factory committed where shown.

The constitutional route places Augustin Voloshyn and the Ruthenian Civic Assembly in office under the civic standard.

The agrarian route places Andriy Brodiy and the Ruthenian Village Compact in office under the agrarian standard.

The socialist route places Ivan Mondok and the Carpathian Workers' Council in office under the socialist standard.

The emergency route places Dmytro Klympush and the Mountain Defence Directorate in office under the emergency standard.

Durable sovereignty and the network corridor remain unavailable until the founding crisis is resolved, and the corridor additionally requires stable ledgers and an active league route.

## Shared focus and forces

Ruthenia uses the shared full Independence Wave focus tree and does not define a bespoke or additive focus tree.

Five idempotent package hooks connect the shared administration, community, military, former-host, and network lanes to the Ruthenian compact.

The same helpers are used by the matching decisions, so completing a focus and a project cannot duplicate their rewards.

The p38 force package uses the mountain-frontier profile with tradition 60 and pathway mask 647.

It provides land and territorial-defence pathways without naval or air inheritance.

## Assets and identity

The four route flags use generated flat alternate-history designs with normal, medium, and small TGA ladders under `gfx/flags/` and evidence under `docs/assets/006_independence_wave/iw038_ruthenia_flags_2026_08_10/`.

Augustin Voloshyn, Andriy Brodiy, Ivan Mondok, and Dmytro Klympush use sourced historical portraits cropped and resized as 156 by 210 DDS placeholders.

Their original sources, exact crops, rights findings, and manifests are archived under `docs/assets/portraits/006_independence_wave/`.

Mondok has a complete public-domain publication chain, while the Voloshyn, Brodiy, and Klympush placeholders retain the rights caveats recorded in their manifests.

The Event 006 Augustin portrait uses a dedicated sprite token and cleanup restores his ordinary vanilla portrait token.

The Event 006 additions define no advisor, high-command, dossier, operative, or new small-portrait consumer. Voloshyn retains his vanilla small portrait while Event 006 overrides only his civilian-large portrait.

## UI, formables, and cleanup

The package uses the ordinary decision category and shared focus surfaces, with no dedicated scripted GUI.

No Ruthenian formable is admitted by this package, and any future Carpathian federation route must pass the shared state-puzzle, carrier, member, consent, and territory contracts before it can be exposed.

Cleanup removes Ruthenian decisions, missions, ideas, route cosmetics, package variables, completion flags, additive characters, force receipts, and Event 006 portrait overrides without rewriting ordinary vanilla Ruthenia.

## Future plans

Future work may add an admitted Carpathian formable only after its exact state set, historical identity, consent flow, and grouped state-puzzle consumer are approved.

The sourced placeholders may later be replaced by user-supplied HOI4-style portraits while preserving their stable character and sprite identifiers.
