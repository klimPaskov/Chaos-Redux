## FORM-48 boundary

All three packages select the Pacific Regional Federation family and call the
shared family registration adapter. Pacific founding invitations have their
own human ballot: HAW and FSM may authorize sovereign autonomous membership or
withhold the mandate. The generic FORM-01/02/04 ballot explicitly rejects the
Pacific family, so no Pacific invitation can expose full integration.

After the readiness-gated shared transaction has set PFX active and committed
and proved both generation-bound autonomous members, the carrier initializes
five visible ledgers: shipping coordination, industrial specialization,
defense readiness, member autonomy, and compact strain. Initialization is not
called from package setup, discovery, invitation issue, the identity adapter,
or a partial integration. It cannot run without the exact successful shared
commit proof.

The federal work program then repeats three stages:

1. California pays convoys, fuel, command attention, and factory capacity to
   commission a convoy-defense rotation. HAW and FSM each pay a bounded convoy
   obligation or explicitly withhold it before the mission deadline.
2. California pays equipment, hulls, and factory capacity for shared
   procurement. Both members fulfill or withhold a national equipment quota.
3. California pays command, support, and factory capacity to negotiate a
   limited basing convention. Both members separately grant or withhold a
   revocable national consent.

Successful missions improve the relevant PFX ledgers, former-host settlement
and fear dimensions, league cohesion/common cause/reserve/confidence, and—on
procurement—reduce patron influence and league patron capture. Withholding
raises the autonomy ledger but immediately reduces the relevant centralized
capacity, member contribution/confidence records, league trust, and compact
integrity. Deadline failures damage the relevant values, strengthen former-
host claim/hostility/fear pressure, and worsen league patron capture. Compact
strain is clamped to 0–100; high strain or a departed member exposes a timed
dissolution action, and 100 strain dissolves automatically.

Dissolution removes the PFX cosmetic identity, transaction-owned access and
guarantees, autonomous bindings, missions, decisions, ideas, and formable
runtime ledgers. It applies country, former-host, and league consequences but
does not annex a member, transfer a state, change either member's active focus
tree, or end any of the three Event 006 origins. A hidden high-chaos PFX carrier that is a
member of a durable radical-revisionist league becomes the durable league
leader and publishes the one-shot dangerous-milestone reason 4 through the
existing super-event FIFO.

`independence_wave_form48_registry_surface_registered` marker and retains the
