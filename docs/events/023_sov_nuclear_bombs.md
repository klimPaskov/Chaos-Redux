## Identity and boundaries

Event 23 uses the `chaosx.nr23.1` root and is a Minor Fire-Once event for `SOV` at Chaos level 2. Its opening option is deliberately additive: it calls the shared nuclear stockpile effect with an amount of exactly 100 and never replaces the pre-existing national stockpile.

The event owns authorization, actor identity, action context, selected target or site, device reservation, progression state, custody classification, and Event 23 records. Shared nuclear systems own the native detonation call, detonation consequences, and terminal Fallout. Every Event 23 test, demonstration, strike, accident, and demolition enters the single shared action contract in `common/scripted_effects/023_sov_nuclear_bombs_runtime_effects.txt`; there is one native `launch_nuke` adapter and no second consequence path.

Event 23 remains separate from Events 5, 32, 47, and 76, the shared Fallout system, and Final Silence. It uses neutral hooks only. It does not register an Arms-race cluster, add a triggerable scenario, or add a world-end catalog row. It has no focus tree, tag, portrait, flag, custom unit, counter, 3D model, scripted GUI, or animation.

## Progression ladder

The event-owned ledger begins at 100 devices from the opening grant. Each enabled evolution is contiguous and records only after its complete gate is satisfied.

| Evolution | Cumulative event-owned devices | Post-baseline increment | Event-owned reactor count | Additional gate | Disabled behavior |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| I | 175 | 75 | 2 | Chaos threshold and Event 23 actor validity | Grants, unlocks, and records nothing |
| II | 275 | 100 | 4 | Evolution I plus its Chaos threshold and valid world conditions | Grants, unlocks, and records nothing |
| III | 400 | 125 | 6 | Evolution II plus its world-state gate and another nuclear major | Grants, unlocks, and records nothing |
| IV | 600 | 200 | 8 | Evolution III plus its world-state gate | Grants, unlocks, and records nothing |

The progression effects are centralized in `common/scripted_effects/023_sov_nuclear_bombs_event_effects.txt` and the ladder values are in `common/script_constants/023_sov_nuclear_bombs_constants.txt`. The log tier mapping follows the actual Chaos meter tiers: Evolution I uses `tier_2`, Evolution II `tier_3`, Evolution III `tier_4`, and Evolution IV `tier_final`.

Evolution II opens ordinary minor and breakaway coercion. Evolution III opens multi-major retaliation and the nonterminal exchange route only when the world-state gate exists. Evolution IV is the only stage at which Soviet AI first use against a nuclear major can be eligible, and it additionally requires the severe strategic-loss, war, route, command, target, device, and stand-down gates. High Chaos by itself never permits first use.

## Custody, transfers, and ledger conservation

Physical custody, technical access, command formation, and delivery integration are separate state classifications. A breakaway can hold or attempt to recover devices without becoming operational immediately; the staged operationalization mission must complete its technical, command, and delivery phases before any operational claim, and breakaway actors have no native launch route in this package.

Event 5 integration uses a pre-transfer custody snapshot and post-transfer reconciliation. Immediately before each of the four Event 5 release branches, `SOV = { sov_nuclear_bombs_snapshot_event5_release_tranche = yes }` records the exact state owner, controller, site flags, and ledger buckets. Bounded `on_state_control_changed`, `on_release_as_free`, and `on_release_as_puppet` callbacks reconcile the saved snapshot after transfer. If `SOV` disappears, Soviet-only production and evolution effects stop; valid local custody and transferred ledgers remain tracked.

All reserve, release, transfer, demolition, expenditure, and native launch paths pass through the ledger helpers in `023_sov_nuclear_bombs_runtime_effects.txt`. Reservation, commit, cancellation, state-control reconciliation, and annex cleanup are idempotent and retain the event-owned audit buckets across save and reload. Native nuclear stockpile decrement is left to the verified native launch route for a detonation and is applied by the shared commit route for demolition; Event 23 never subtracts the same device twice.

## Verified nuclear action route

The installed atomic capability is the vanilla `atomic_research` plus `nukes` route. The selected delivery route accepts the installed strategic bomber capability (`strategic_bomber1` or the BBA `iw_large_airframe` route), requires deployed strategic bombers and an owned, controlled airbase, and then calls the native `launch_nuke` effect with the selected state. No missile, thermonuclear, free-aircraft, unrelated technology, or substitute equipment is granted.

The action adapter validates the Event 23 actor, authorization, action type, target or site context, terminal-state exclusion, native technology, operational device stock, and selected-state route before reservation and execution. Its shared context gate also rechecks that a combat action against a nuclear major either follows confirmed enemy nuclear use or passes the complete Evolution IV severe first-use gate. Limited non-major combat use remains a distinct Evolution II route and records generic Soviet combat use without creating a pre-Evolution-IV major first-use route. The exact native range trigger is not documented by the installed vanilla documentation, so the package uses a selected-state, ownership, controller, airbase, bomber, and native launch route and records this engine-evidence limitation instead of inventing a trigger.

## World threat, exchange, and terminal behavior

Event 23 contributes the canonical `world_threat_source_sov_nuclear_bombs` source through the shared world-threat aggregator. Terminal Fallout, `fallout_transition_active`, `fallout_active`, and Final Silence flags suppress the Event 23 source and invalidate new Event 23 actions. Shared terminal Fallout remains the sole owner of terminal consequences.

The first confirmed multi-major exchange opens exactly one nonterminal Event 23 super-event and records its one-time global exchange state. Testing, threats, failed launches, accidents, minor coercion, and a single major striking a nonnuclear minor do not qualify. The exchange super-event is separate from Fallout and Final Silence and does not itself apply terminal consequences.
