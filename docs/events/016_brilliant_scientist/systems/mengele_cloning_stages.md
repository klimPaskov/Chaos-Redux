# Directorate Cloning stages

Mengele's Cloning programme advances through Theory, Prototype, Deployment, and Weaponization in the Biological Programs decision category.
Each paid decision reserves its quoted civilian factories, debits Political Power, Support Equipment, and Fuel through the private Event 016 stage receipt, and settles or refunds that receipt on completion or cancellation.
The stage core records family history and updates the private modifier without writing Kruger's capacity or stage ledger.

## Native and paid Prototype

With Götterdämmerung, `sp_brilliant_scientist_cloning` supplies the native Prototype clock and completion callback.
Without that DLC, `mengele_event016_cloning_prototype` supplies a paid timed Prototype.
The paid path grants the dedicated Mengele cloning programme only after a successful Prototype finish, then grants shared clone operational access with Mengele provenance.
The native bridge does the same after a successful authenticated grant with private Theory and Prototype history, and guards the dedicated completion against replay.
The dedicated effect selects Mengele refinement, produces clone equipment through its established consumer, and can queue the existing revolt event when its trigger is ready.
An established German master-race claim enables the separate Aryan clone refinement and formation template if its technology is absent.

## Tuning, icons, and integration

Cloning Deployment organizes the existing biological warfare facilities into a sustained production network.
On the established weekly update, each facility-bearing state owned and controlled by the programme adds four Clone Cohorts to its normal one-cohort output.
Controlled facilities in foreign-owned states retain the normal baseline, and lost control removes that state's output.
The enhancement requires the active programme, both private Cloning stage receipts and shared clone operational knowledge.
Completion, construction and inheritance calls use the baseline-only entry point, even after a weekly call.
They cannot repeat the weekly enhancement.

The production calculator reads physical facilities and current ownership/control instead of preserving a country-only site count.
It clears its temporary mode after each calculation.
The stockpile writer adds one combined amount in the invoking country and refreshes the existing reserve-manpower consumer once.
The existing paid KRG maturation action and ordinary military-factory manufacture remain separate.
No formation or direct manpower is granted by this throughput tier.

Durations and prices use the Event 016 script constants and the private stage effects.
The additional weekly output is `germany_mengele_cloning_project.deployment_bonus_per_owned_facility_per_week` and its tooltip reads that same constant.
The four existing `GFX_decision_brilliant_scientist_project_cloning_*` sprites are defined in `interface/016_brilliant_scientist_project_icons.gfx` and use DDS files under `gfx/interface/decisions/016_brilliant_scientist/projects/`.
The programme shares the existing clone equipment, formation, and revolt consumers; this stage family adds no new bitmap.
