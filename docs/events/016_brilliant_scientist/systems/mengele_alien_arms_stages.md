# Directorate Alien Arms stages

Mengele's Alien Arms programme advances through Theory, Prototype, Deployment, and Weaponization under the Advanced Programs decision category.
Theory, Deployment, and Weaponization use paid timed decisions in either DLC state; `sp_brilliant_scientist_alien_arms` owns the Prototype clock and payment with Götterdämmerung, while a paid decision supplies that stage without the DLC.
The native and paid paths record the same private family history without writing Kruger's project receipts.

## Lifecycle and interactions

Each decision requires Directorate authority, its exact predecessor, an empty Alien Arms receipt, and its stage quote.
The timed decision reserves civilian factories through `civilian_factory_use`, while the private begin effect records and debits Political Power, Support Equipment, and Fuel; cancellation settles the saved direct payment and releases the native reservation.
On the no-DLC Prototype, a successful private finish grants Alien Infantry operational access through the existing neutral custom technology API with Mengele provenance.
The authenticated native callback supplies that operational grant with the DLC, and the later private stages use the shared operational and Alien predictive warfare upgrade effects.
Alien Infantry operational access is one of the five exact receipts required for Mengele's D'Rhondan envoy craft.

## Tuning and assets

The shared Event 016 script constants set duration and four-axis quotes, with local aliases only for native decision fields that reject `constant:` tokens.
The four existing `GFX_decision_brilliant_scientist_project_alien_arms_*` sprites are registered in `interface/016_brilliant_scientist_project_icons.gfx` and use DDS files under `gfx/interface/decisions/016_brilliant_scientist/projects/`.
The AI retains Kruger's low stage bases and wartime preference, with caution when available project factories do not exceed the current stage's quote of 2, 3, 6, or 9.

## Future plans and acceptance

The paid and native Prototype callbacks, operational consumer, D'Rhondan gate, native factory reservation, AI selection, and both DLC-state lifecycles remain subject to engine and user live acceptance.
