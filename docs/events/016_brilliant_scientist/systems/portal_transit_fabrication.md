# Portal transit fabrication

The Kruger State commits terminal capacity to a finite batch of Teleportation Equipment through its existing Portal and Temporal decision category.
The action requires active Portal operations, operational technology, recruitment authority, military industry at admission and its established AI/network access rule.
One batch supplies 180 `teleportation_equipment_1` across 90 days, with a 30-day re-enable interval and the existing four-success history limit.

## Payment and native timing

The ordinary price is 65 Political Power, 200 concrete `support_equipment_1`, 1,000 fuel and four committed civilian factories.
The three direct resources use the shared universal-cost quote, payment and receipt APIs.
The same fresh quote governs the cost row, its blocked state, each resource's colour, selection eligibility and the selection debit.
The stored actual payment remains authoritative for later refunds, including when a discount expires or the country's reserves change.
The native factory commitment is a fixed four-factory modifier; a universal-discount adapter for that native field remains an explicit integration limitation.

The native decision owns its timer, factory reservation and release.
Selection checks available factories as well as direct resources.
Its literal entry callback supplies and consumes a temporary native-entry marker, allowing the payment helper to avoid counting that reservation twice while rejecting a bare helper invocation.
The quote and entry helpers do not create a replacement timer or periodic grant.

The receipt binds the payer, action and current serial to three nominal/final/source/actual component records and one physical output quantity.
The actor's shared transaction ledger retains concrete-model payment and refund evidence.
Original equipment producer/design provenance cannot be reconstructed by the native stockpile API; exact model quantities remain separate from that engine limit.
The serial validates the stored current receipt and does not establish immutable identity for a delayed native callback.

## Completion and recovery

Expiry verifies the funded receipt and continuing operational authority without purchasing the quote again.
Depleted reserves after payment do not invalidate the promised equipment.
The neutral equipment helper consumes its explicit quantity, writes stockpile output for the invoking country and reports the measured concrete delta.
It writes no programme, facility or batch history.
Only a fully delivered paid KRG batch advances `brilliant_scientist_krg_portal_transit_batches` once.

An order with no delivered equipment may return its measured payments through the receipt.
Failed refund work remains due and replaces the normal fabrication row with a matching recovery action.
A positive partial delivery retains the funded obligation and the remaining quantity; it cannot combine retained equipment with a blind full refund.
Output recovery delivers only the outstanding portion, while settlement recovery closes the delivered order without equipment or history replay.
The recovery actions require the original proof and charge no new resource, timer or factory commitment.
The existing terminal cleanup invokes receipt cleanup before removing the native decision.
