# Mengele Strategic Singularity stages

The registered Mengele Directorate progresses through private Theory, Prototype, Deployment, and Weaponization receipts under `mengele_advanced_programs_category`.
The four paid decision wrappers use the existing private Event 016 family ledger, exact direct payment receipts, timed civilian factory reservations, and provider cleanup.
The Strategic Singularity scenario setting must remain enabled while each stage is active.

## Stage flow

Theory is the first timed stage and unlocks the Prototype route.
With Gotterdammerung, the six existing native component projects are the only Prototype route; each exact completion enters the private component array once and the sixth component settles Prototype.
Without Gotterdammerung, the paid Prototype decision follows Theory and uses the existing fallback Prototype costs and duration.
Deployment follows private Prototype, and Weaponization follows private Deployment.
Both later stages require the existing minimum of three currently owned and controlled qualifying special facilities; losing that live requirement cancels the active stage and settles its private payment receipt.
The DLC-present private Prototype predecessor continues to require all six authenticated component receipts, including when called through the generic stage helper.

Each decision's native timer reserves civilian factories, while the private stage effects debit Political Power, Support Equipment, and fuel as applicable.
The stage receipt prevents two Singularity stages from running at once, and cancellation refunds only an exact unsettled direct payment receipt.
Completion records the durable private stage flag and enables its existing dynamic modifier without granting Kruger identity, Kruger project history, or a terminal result.

The native components retain their six existing `GFX_sp_brilliant_scientist_singularity_*` sprites in the same GFX file.
No new icon or texture is required for these stage decisions.

## Interaction and future plans

The stage flags and component array are provider-owned; the existing Fallout transition remains shared and requires a separate actor-authenticated provider terminal adapter.
Weaponization alone does not fire a world-end effect or authorize a Kruger terminal.
The terminal route still needs physical command-node and power-link site receipts, a retry-safe Fallout request, shared presentation, and a no-DLC eligibility decision that does not imply nonexistent native components.
The stage AI source declares base willingness 10 and a wartime factor of 2, while the current MCP evaluation returned four unresolved scenario items and no readable effective-score result; its same-source comparison is not a historical balance comparison.
Direct payment timing, live facility loss, and stage AI choice among competing decisions remain for accepted MCP evidence and user-owned live validation.
