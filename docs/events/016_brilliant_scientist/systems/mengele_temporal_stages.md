# Directorate Temporal stages

Mengele's Temporal Mechanics programme advances through Theory, Prototype, Deployment, and Weaponization under the existing clone-army decision category.
Theory, Deployment, and Weaponization use paid timed decisions in either DLC state; `sp_brilliant_scientist_temporal_mechanics` owns the Prototype clock and payment with Götterdämmerung, while a paid decision supplies that stage without the DLC.
Both routes record private Temporal history and leave Kruger's project board intact.

## Lifecycle and interactions

Each stage requires Directorate authority, its exact predecessor, an empty Temporal receipt, and its stage quote.
The decision lifecycle reserves civilian factories; the private begin effect debits Political Power, Support Equipment, and Fuel, with saved direct costs refunded by the cancellation effect.
Only a successful no-DLC Prototype finish grants Temporal Guard operational access through the neutral custom technology API with Mengele provenance.
The authenticated native callback supplies that operational access with the DLC, and the later private stages use the shared operational and Temporal Weaponization upgrade effects.

## Tuning and assets

Duration and four-axis payment quotes come from the shared Event 016 script constants; local aliases mirror only native decision fields that reject `constant:` tokens.
The four existing `GFX_decision_brilliant_scientist_project_temporal_mechanics_*` sprites are registered in `interface/016_brilliant_scientist_project_icons.gfx` and point to matching DDS files under `gfx/interface/decisions/016_brilliant_scientist/projects/`.
The AI retains Kruger's low Theory, Deployment, and Weaponization bases and high Prototype base, wartime preference, and cautious factor when available project factories do not exceed the current stage's quote of 2, 3, 6, or 10.

## Future plans and acceptance

The native callback, both DLC-state lifecycles, Temporal Guard formation, factory reservation, and AI selection remain subject to engine and user live acceptance.
