# Event 042 Asset Provenance

All Event 042 art is original generated artwork, because the scenes are fictional and physically impossible. It was produced with the native ImageGen route (`mcp__gptimage__generate_image`) under the `chaos-redux-event-assets` workflow on 2026-09-25. No portraits, flags, focus or decision icons, counters, animations, 3D models, news images, or super-event images belong to this event.

## Report-event cards

- Source photographs: 1536x1024, high quality, opaque full-canvas documentary scenes.
- Processing: the deterministic `process_report_event_image.py` report-card treatment (black and white with sepia, slight tilt, transparent corners, soft shadow) at default parameters, output 210x176 RGBA with alpha 0 at all four corners.
- Conversion: `convert_to_dds.py` to 32-bit uncompressed B8G8R8A8 without mipmaps. The crate-field card was checked pixel for pixel against its processed PNG with zero differences, and the same command produced the other five.
- Review: every source passed the forbidden-content checklist on the first generation (no sender aircraft, no modern equipment or uniforms, no readable text, no map or manifest as the subject, no identifiable special creature, robot, clone, plague, or alien in the sealed-cargo scene). The native and 3x contact sheets were reviewed for clarity at 210x176.
