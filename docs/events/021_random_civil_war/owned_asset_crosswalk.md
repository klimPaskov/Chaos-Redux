# Event 021 owned-asset crosswalk

This table covers forty active Event 021-owned runtime textures, not the reused Event 006 package universe.
The repository also preserves fifteen tracked, unregistered legacy DDS files under `gfx/interface/achievements/021_random_civil_war/`; they have no active sprite or gameplay references and are audited below as `unused_orphan` provenance rather than counted as active consumers.
It consolidates the individual final-DDS reviews and preserved source/processed evidence in `docs/assets/021_random_civil_war/validation/visual_asset_audit_2026-09-01.md` with the September 2 achievement-consumer repair.
`pass` and `fixed` here certify the inspected asset pixels and source-to-runtime path, not full event lifecycle or live-game acceptance.
No source or image is regenerated by this crosswalk update.
Historical native-alpha failures and documented background-repair fallbacks remain reported in the linked visual audit and are not erased by a clean final DDS.
The complete event audit remains open for the reused Event 006 crosswalk and current consumer evidence.

September 2 individual-source review supersedes the clean-edge verdicts for the two v1-rejected achievement layers: Fractals of Sovereignty grey and The Terms Hold not-eligible.
Their original, processed, and decoded runtime images were opened individually, followed by explicit alpha composition on contrasting backgrounds.
Checkerboard RGB at alpha zero is not an opaque runtime checkerboard, but white fringe and isolated visible matte pixels remain in some fallback-treated high-resolution layers.
The reviewed Fractals not-eligible and War Within not-eligible candidates were promoted on September 2 from the frozen pair in `docs/plans/021_random_civil_war_plans/subagent_handoffs/achievement_alpha_edges_2026-09-02.md`.
The v2 repairs for Fractals grey and The Terms Hold not-eligible were parent-reviewed and promoted to their existing root consumers; their prior runtime DDS and processed PNG files are preserved under `docs/assets/021_random_civil_war/validation/alpha_edge_repair_2026-09-02/runtime_before_parent_promotion_v2/`.
All forty owned original/processed pairs have now been individually reviewed in addition to the previously reviewed final DDS images.

Counts: one report image, two news images, one static category picture, one category icon, eleven decision icons, three mission icons, three idea icons, and eighteen achievement states.
