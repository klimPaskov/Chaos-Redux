# Event 011 - Secret Alliance

Event 011 is `Secret Alliance`, a Minor Fire-Once event rooted at `chaosx.nr11.1`. It is not part of an event cluster. At the instant the event is accepted, the current player country is saved as the fixed target and remains the target for the entire lifecycle, including concealed operations, public reveal, coalition war, settlement, achievements, and cleanup.

## Opening and founder selection

The automatic event can fire only when the player is a valid country and at least three valid AI minors exist. It cannot restart after the fire-once history flag, while another Event 011 context is active, or after the event has reached a terminal state. Manual Coalition Unmasked launches use their own gate.

`chaosx.nr11.900` is a hidden, triggered-only AI test entry. It can be invoked only from a valid AI country scope and temporarily opts that country into the normal target initializer. The initializer records the run as forced through `achievement_secret_alliance_forced_origin`, which disqualifies all six Event 011 achievements. The entry is absent from player routes and player-facing content. SCN-009 remains the manual scenario route.

Automatic relaunch also requires `secret_alliance_scheduled_callbacks_are_drained`. A prior run's counted `.50` delayed member-call callback, counted `.51`, `.52`, or `.53` emergency, diplomacy, or offensive commitment callback, pending `.190` war pulse, `.201` scenario-composition notice, or `.202` reveal-presentation cleanup must consume its explicit pending state before another automatic run can begin. Scenario launch has the same drain gate. Annexation of a delayed-call owner releases that country's counted callback slot immediately. Delayed visible events `.4` and `.5` require the exact fixed target and their current evolution phase before displaying, while invitation and sponsor notifications require their live pending transaction and saved recipient. Cleanup or invalidation closes those pending transactions instead of allowing stale content into a later run.

The opening builds a weighted ticket pool for each founder selection. Candidates must be valid independent AI-controlled minor countries. The selector excludes the fixed target, subjects, capitulated or invalid countries, special nonhuman chaos countries, target faction partners, countries already fighting the target, and countries already selected. It strongly prefers factionless countries, then considers adjacency, continent, ideological rivalry, target threat, country size, and grievances. Three distinct founders are drawn from the pool and receive persistent motive, role, support-capability, private-commitment, and commitment-strength profiles.

Later recruitment uses a dynamic cap. The evolution ceiling is reduced against a weak minor target and against a target that has generated little tension, then clamped to the valid range and to the actual remaining candidate pool. That pool ceiling counts eligible AI minors and eligible human minors. Human capacity is therefore preserved without treating an unanswered invitation as consent.

Normal openings begin at baseline. If evolution settings already make later stages available before the event fires, the event records the open stages and starts with their corresponding membership, readiness, counter-network, and warning package. A pre-fire Evolution III opening first creates the Evolution II package and then gives the pact a short real preparation interval before the public-war stage opens.

## Concealed pact

The pact is event-owned and is never represented by a normal faction while hidden. Its durable state is held in member, founder, sponsor, suspect, confirmed-member, turned-member, and public-member arrays plus global cohesion, readiness, alertness, doctrine, war pressure, and operation state. Each member has its own motive, operational role, support capability, private bargain, commitment score, and commitment band. Successful and disrupted operations change the actor's score and immediately recalculate its band. Commitment weights future operation ownership and defection selection, governs whether a targeted member defects or merely delays its call, modifies delayed-call reliability, and contributes to Coalition Resolve at reveal.

Baseline pulses are ordinary progression. They can recruit valid minors, generate internal disputes, leak traces, lose members, or launch one operation. Baseline operations draw from diplomatic isolation, intelligence penetration, industrial and transport sabotage, and recruitment. Evolution I opens political and social pressure plus military preparation, giving the pact all six accepted families. Evolution II adds the rare political-violence branch `chaosx.nr11.21`, which depicts an attempted assassination and carries the longer severe-recovery response. The actor, target surface, risk, evidence class, and readiness layer are recorded before resolution. Adaptive recovery blocks immediate repetition after an exposed or failed operation.

Resolution consumes the stored dossier rather than rerolling its identity. On success, the stored risk scales the readiness and actor-commitment gains. On disruption, it scales the actor-commitment loss, Evidence, and suspect-confidence gains. External, intelligence, logistics, political, military, and recruitment readiness layers apply named multipliers to the exact readiness change. A successful industrial or transport operation damages infrastructure in its recorded target state according to risk. A successful military-preparation operation preserves its mapped state and adds opening coordination when the target still controls that state at reveal. A disrupted industrial or military operation preserves its exposed state and adds known weaknesses when the target still controls that state at reveal. The recorded evidence class determines the clue created by disruption.

- Evolution I opens recruitment toward the current dynamic cap and opens the full six-family operation set without guaranteeing an immediate fourth member.
- Evolution II raises the potential minor ceiling, permits one valid major sponsor, enables serious covert action including the rare `chaosx.nr11.21` attempt, and opens Foreign Interference with visible Evidence and Preparedness.
- Evolution III raises the potential ceiling again, permits a possible second major sponsor only after the first sponsor and enough concealed-operation pulses, builds War Pressure, displays the animated confrontation emblem, and permits public faction formation, preparation, and preemption.

Major sponsors must have operational reach, sufficient industrial or equipment capacity, and a political or strategic reason to oppose the target. Candidate weights also consider war, stability, losses, access dependence, theater fit, and how a second sponsor complements the first sponsor's land, maritime, or distant-support role.

Human countries are never silently forced into the hidden pact. A normal human invitation is an explicit event with join, conditional join, refuse, leak, and expose choices. Conditional acceptance preserves the recruit's private demand and begins from a lower commitment band. Scenario construction selects AI members only and keeps the launching human as the target.

## Evidence, suspects, and preparedness

Evidence is source-aware. A clue records its source country and one of six independent classes: method, communications, financial, diplomatic, military, or human. The same source and class pair cannot repeatedly farm Evidence. Each suspect maintains its own independent-class count, while the global dossier separately tracks network-wide classes. A country reaches Confirmed only when its confidence threshold and its own corroboration threshold are both satisfied. The target can see at most three suspect cards at once and selects them directly from the scripted GUI. Visibility as a suspect never exposes hidden membership.

The hidden pact also reacts to the dossier. Higher Evidence can make it clean exposed routes, plant false traffic, protect an exposed member, or accelerate disclosure. Target AI reads the full internal suspect set and uses the same investigation, protection, and offensive effects as the player.

Preparedness is the sum of seven maintained components: staff security, industrial security, transport security, border readiness, continuity, allied coordination, and known plans. Every project, emergency measure, patrol commitment, and consultation keeps a separate source contribution and clears only its own value on expiry. The five map-facing protection projects save the exact industrial, stockpile, border, port or airfield, or continuity state selected by the target. They deny or disrupt an operation only when its recorded surface matches that protected state. Protection projects are timed and carry real burdens while active. Evidence and Preparedness directly change outcome bands, operation-family weights, delayed-call pressure, and reveal conversion.

The compact counter-network panel remains spoiler-safe. It shows exact target-owned Evidence and Preparedness, but hidden foreign state appears only as broad unity and preparation bands. It does not reveal member count, motives, exact Cohesion, exact Readiness, or unconfirmed membership.

## Decision and mission matrix

`secret_alliance_foreign_interference` opens after a serious Evolution II incident. Its investigations compare diplomatic traffic, audit funding, inspect missions and access talks, compare sabotage signatures, trace couriers, question intermediaries, and reconstruct meeting circuits. Named missions then track a real map or country objective: Watch a Liaison Route, Seize a Compromised Courier, Turn a Recruited Clerk, Protect a Defecting Envoy, Break a Safehouse Network, conduct a National Manhunt, and Control a Rumor Channel. A delayed verification event checks the named state and supporting field requirements after activation and repeats while the mission remains active, preventing same-tick completion. Full and partial results register the mission's actual suspect and evidence class. Expiry can preserve partial progress rather than silently becoming failure.

Decision visibility is capacity-aware and phase-aware. The live caps are two investigations, one protection, one diplomatic action, one offensive action, one border action, and one emergency action. Incident family, suspect confidence, prepared objective state, route flags, and the current category remove irrelevant actions from view. Foreign Interference owns concealed Evolution II play, while Coalition Crisis owns Evolution III and revealed play. A live false-plan channel shows Feed False Mobilization Plans as the offensive action and hides the other seven offensive branches until that channel is consumed. This keeps those branches mutually exclusive instead of presenting the full catalog.

Protection projects compartmentalize staff plans, rotate ciphers, secure industrial choke points, disperse stockpiles, guard the cabinet, harden border communications, protect ports and airfields, and establish continuity sites. Continuity sites require elevated War Pressure in addition to their protection slot and displayed package. Diplomacy can approach a suspect, offer a security guarantee, address a grievance, expose sponsor pressure privately, request allied consultation, convene a neutral inquiry, or demand an explanation. Offensive actions feed false plans, run controlled shipments, plant a false dispute, turn a member, sabotage a forward depot, disrupt a conference, raid the border, or seize a courier aircraft or vessel. A controlled shipment can fully succeed, partly succeed, or fail, and any useful result exposes a safehouse route. Full conference success captures records and forces public disclosure, while partial success disrupts confidence among the foreign planners. Border actions prepare one named crossing backed by exact stored attacker and defender states. The rail supply gate checks those states for the required infrastructure and railway level, then checks target divisions on the crossing and suspect divisions in the named opposing state. The action also requires the displayed trains and other local commitments before it can open a limited conflict with escalation, withdrawal, or negotiated stand-down outcomes. Withdrawal cancels the border war and lowers War Pressure; it does not create a separate withdrawal record.

The pair is bound to the selected suspect as well as the exact attacker and defender states. Scripted-GUI select and clear clicks refresh the pair, and those controls are disabled while an unresolved border conflict owns it. The offensive reconnaissance route pays and activates its crossing mission only after a valid pair is built, establishes the reinforced-patrol completion state, and clears or rebuilds stale pairs when suspect, ownership, control, passability, adjacency, or conflict state changes.

Public actions require evidence thresholds and independent corroboration before the target can release a partial dossier, name a first member, present the coalition case, expose a major sponsor, or demand inspections. Naming an innocent country records a durable false-accusation consequence, strengthens the hidden pact, damages stability and relations, and disqualifies the applicable achievements. The coalition-crisis category adds emergency mobilization, fortification, overseas recall, a final warning, preemption, defensive alliance seeking, leadership evacuation, `Countdown to Open Confrontation`, and post-reveal war actions. Concealed crisis copy tells the target what can still be disrupted before the countdown expires.

Costs scale from the target's industry, field army, territorial capacity, major status, action family, and relevant Evidence band. Repeated use does not apply a separate cost multiplier. Actions can consume command power, service experience, political power, stability, war support, support equipment, trains, trucks, convoys, fuel, and manpower. Availability uses the same computed values as the displayed custom costs. Preempt the Coalition requires at least 65 percent War Support. Its separately displayed strain is 15 percent with a weak case, 10 percent with strong Evidence, and 6 percent with complete Evidence. A false case also costs 8 percent Stability. The minimum War Support is an eligibility gate and the displayed strain is the amount spent. AI countries use the same effects, caps, commitments, objectives, and outcome model.

Direct target-AI execution has one owner. The controller owns all eight protection projects, Turn Member, and the three wartime actions, while their engine `ai_will_do` weights are blocked. It selects the same valid state surfaces and pays the same resources as a player action, records which project actually fired, and applies target-owned cooldowns of 120 days for protection, 180 days for Turn Member, and 120 days for a wartime action. Cleanup resets both cooldown families and their temporary action targets.

## Reveal contract

Every reveal route calls `secret_alliance_reveal_pact`. The reusable effect refreshes member validity, snapshots the reveal state, selects the public leader in the required order of designated active major sponsor, strongest active major, strongest founder, then hostile-war trigger country, creates `secret_alliance_public_coalition`, and adds every valid active member. A second major cannot displace the designated sponsor while that sponsor remains valid. Members who must leave an unrelated faction record its current leader before transfer. When a target war already exists, the effect performs the complete member call before it commits the revealed state or fires the super-event. It retries the call transaction, restores every recorded prior faction if formation or a mandatory war call fails, and never routes a technical rollback through the achievement-bearing hidden-collapse outcome. Temporary faction-creation authority is cleared immediately after the creation attempt. The faction name is dynamically rendered as Anti-[target] Pact. Dynamic targets and targets carrying `secret_alliance_faction_name_country_exception` use the country-name form. The latter is the maintained grammatical fallback for a missing or awkward adjective. `secret_alliance_initialize_faction_name_grammar` initializes this choice during normal context creation, scenario setup, and public faction creation so the fallback is established before any faction, news, Event Log, or super-event text reads it.

If any active member enters a normal hostile war with the fixed target, `secret_alliance_handle_war_relation_added` immediately saves that war as the anchor, reveals the pact, creates the faction, adds every valid active member, and calls every valid active member into the same target war. This route deliberately ignores delayed-call and turned-member hesitation because the external war has already activated the pact.

Public conference, public dossier, captured conference, preemption, weakened, and fracture reveal routes converge on the same effect. Evolution III resolves the public crisis into four state-driven outcomes. High Cohesion and Readiness against lower target Preparedness produce the controlled conference route. Coalition-case Evidence or an exposed sponsor produces the player-forced package. Low Cohesion, a recent dispute, or a preserved turned channel produces the fractured route. The remaining state produces the weakened route and reduces Cohesion and Readiness before conversion. A recent dispute still forces the explicit fractured route for any non-hostile reveal, while the hostile-war route remains exact.

Planned reveals preserve turned-member exits, planted false-plan consequences, Preparedness-driven delayed calls, and fractured-member withdrawals. High aggregate Preparedness can delay up to two eligible non-major members, with role-matched Preparedness components increasing their selection weight. The hostile-war route clears those delays and calls every valid active member because open war has already activated the pact. Hidden cohesion becomes Coalition Resolve. Readiness becomes opening coordination. Evidence becomes known weaknesses. Preparedness becomes target defenses. The converted bands apply staged coalition and target ideas rather than flattening the hidden game into one modifier.

The reveal fires super-event 73, starts `Countdown to Open Confrontation` when no war already exists, and replaces concealed AI with target-specific coalition-war AI. Before runtime settlement can clear live faction state, the reveal copies the route, target, leader, member count, and faction-name grammar into a presentation context. News and super-event text read `secret_alliance_presentation_target`, `secret_alliance_presentation_leader`, the target-owned member-count snapshot, and `GetSecretAlliancePresentationFactionName`. The display slot remains active for 14 days; hidden event `.202` closes the audio, slot, and presentation context on day 15. Invalid-country lifecycle handling closes the same context early when either presentation country ceases to exist. There is no generic route-text fallback.

## Coalition war and settlement

Members receive role-aware AI priorities for the target front, production, logistics, naval support, intelligence, and distant support. A border member, target neighbor, or member with access receives target-specific `front_unit_request`, conquest pressure, and force concentration. A distant support member receives a negative target-front request so it does not waste divisions on an implausible land deployment. Major sponsors protect the public leader and receive stronger build and target pressure. Turned and fractured members are de-prioritized or removed. Maximum-intensity scenario members receive the most aggressive mapped posture.

During war, Coalition Resolve changes only from named facts with one-shot or state-specific guards. These facts include target objective progress, a failed offensive after enough pulses, member post-reveal casualties or surrender burden, sponsor aid, sponsor distraction, accessible route survival, credible concessions, sponsor or member collapse, conflicting promises, two-major rivalry, separate terms, and Event 011 fractures. Member casualties are snapshotted at reveal, so losses from earlier wars cannot farm the Event 011 war-burden penalty. Target starting-capital control is an achievement and settlement fact rather than a recurring automatic Resolve drain. The target can exploit liaison maps, strike known depots, offer separate terms, publicize conflicting aims, support member opposition, demand sponsor accountability, or coordinate a counteroffensive. War pulses test collapse, delayed calls, fracture exits, capital survival, and settlement eligibility.

The final outcomes are target victory, coalition victory, negotiated settlement, internal rupture, leadership split, dissolution, or a continuing regional bloc. Coalition victory subjects the faction to a separate survival evaluation. Sponsor collapse and major rivalry can split it. Spoils doctrine fractures over unresolved promises unless the coalition remains unusually coherent. Containment normally closes after terms are imposed. Strong Resolve, membership, and leadership can preserve a narrow security council. A major-led regime-pressure coalition can survive. A weak punitive coalition dissolves.

A surviving coalition is converted before runtime cleanup. Its anti-target name, manifest, doctrine goals, and wartime rules are replaced by the faction-native `Coalition Security Council`, `secret_alliance_postwar_security_manifest`, `secret_alliance_goal_postwar_security`, and four postwar rules. These surfaces no longer depend on the cleared target, doctrine, Resolve, or reveal-state facts. Narrow faction lifecycle hooks maintain the surviving-member registry, transfer its leader marker, and dismantle an invalid one-country remnant. Automatic and manual Event 011 openings remain blocked while this postwar bloc exists.

If the concealed pact collapses before reveal, `chaosx.nr11.194` retains only proven participants, innocent suspects, Evidence, and Preparedness long enough for one final choice. The target can publish the surviving case, seal the files, repair relations with innocent suspects, or keep a Preparedness-scaled counterintelligence benefit for up to two years, capped at 730 days. The choice then clears the aftermath arrays. Settlement records starting-capital control, preserves achievement snapshots, applies the appropriate country flags and ideas, and removes Event 011's runtime arrays, event targets, missions, timed burdens, border conflict, hidden variables, and AI strategies.

## Triggerable scenario

SCN-009 `Coalition Unmasked` launches the complete Evolution III package against the current player through one of five compositions:

- Regional Ring
- Ideological Front
- Great-Power Sponsor
- Unlikely Coalition
- Random Coalition

Low, Medium, High, and Maximum intensity scale member count, major count, resolve, readiness, equipment, trains, trucks, and fuel. Regional Ring requires multiple reachable approaches and assigns border or logistics roles. Ideological Front requires genuine ideological opposition for minors and majors and assigns political-arm roles. Great-Power Sponsor selects a strategically reachable major first, then minors that supply a land approach, maritime route, access agreement, or second theater. Unlikely Coalition deliberately selects both aligned and opposed governments, assigns distinct opportunist and ideological motives, stores incompatible promises, and carries a fracture reserve into Coalition Resolve. Random Coalition uses the full safe weighted pool and derives doctrine and roles from the selected members.

Manual launch bypasses normal Chaos, evolution, date, history, prior Event 011 completion, and automatic-fire requirements. It retains the active-context and postwar-bloc guards, the active `world_end` terminal-conflict gate, valid-target and viable-composition checks, and the normal human-consent rule. Scenario members are AI-only. Every opening resets per-run outcome and achievement snapshots so a previous Maximum run cannot qualify a later Low or Medium run. Maximum intensity snapshots requested membership, the safe valid pool, achieved membership, and achieved majors. Its dedicated achievement qualifies when the requested roster is reached or every safe valid candidate is used, even when the exhausted safe pool contains fewer than eight members.
