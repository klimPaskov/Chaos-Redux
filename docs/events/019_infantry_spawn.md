## Identity and status

- Event ID: `19`
- Entry event: `chaosx.nr19.1`
- Classification: `Minor Repeatable`
- Cluster: none. Event 19 remains unclustered.
- World-end route: none

uses diminishing territorial coverage, so a large country receives more units
without receiving one unit in every state. Each selected state generates a
weighted formation lot with an immutable generation, lot, unit, template,

contest between the military value of those formations and the administrative,
equipment, supply, claimant, and anomalous pressure created by keeping them.

## Runtime flow

1. `chaosx.nr19.1` calls the manifestation dispatcher and records Event 19 once
   in the shared Event Log.
2. Every valid country is evaluated by the requested global dispatch. Invalid,
   derivative, scenario-actor, and protected scopes are rejected before
   generation.
3. The country builds an eligible-state pool, calculates a continuous
   diminishing-coverage target, and selects states without replacement using
   state context and controlled weights.
4. Each selected state draws a formation family and lot size. The generator
   constructs the exact template, materializes the units, verifies the resulting
   army count, and commits the ledger only after success.
5. The ledger records component manifests, technology gates, finite equipment,
   manpower liability, weighted equipment debt, quality, coherence, command,
   supply burden, and stable unit/lot/generation identifiers.
6. The country receives Muster Control and Army Congestion pressure. Selected-lot
   decisions and the Muster Board use the same scripted effects, and AI calls the
   same actions after passing the same affordability and state checks.
7. Integration, standardization, supervised demobilization, specialist
   preservation, claimant responses, containment, and derivative cleanup settle
   or transfer only the exact selected rows. Missing units remain unaccounted
   until their obligations are explicitly settled. Disbanding, merging, or
   replacing a unit does not manufacture destruction credit or salvage.
8. A country pulse first drains exact same-tag deferred actions, then reconciles
   ledgers, refreshes ideas and pressure, resolves claimant and anomalous
   lifecycles, checks due evolution sampling, runs bounded AI on its normal
   delayed cadence, and advances at most one bounded lifetime-maintenance phase.
   A separate lightweight daily event samples only active rail,
   claimant-leadership, and scenario achievement continuity. It never runs the
   ledger, AI, pressure, family lifecycle, or compactor. No new daily, weekly,
   or monthly world iteration is used.
9. Ordinary-country annexation records history first, then locks the exact Event
   19 templates, destroys only ledger-proved unit UIDs and delete cohorts wherever
   annexation placed them, proves those units and templates absent, and clears the
   country package. A failed proof quarantines the removed country and retains its
   ledger instead of silently releasing stale identities into a restored tag.

## Formation lots and accounting

Ordinary generation uses weighted lots instead of a flat infantry loop. State
context can favor territorial infantry, line infantry, mobile reserves, mounted
formations, armor, artillery, engineers, or other compatible families. Later
stages add advanced and strange equipment only when their providers and
technology/project gates are valid.

Every committed lot owns aligned arrays for:

- stable generation, lot, unit, template, and deletion-cohort identifiers.
- origin country and state.
- exact combat and support component tokens.
- equipment obligation profile and remaining quantity.
- manpower liability distinct from equipment debt.
- training, quality, coherence, equipment state, command relation, supply
  burden, claimant susceptibility, and demobilization resistance.
- integration, demobilization, destruction, unaccounted, revolt, derivative,
  and achievement proof state.

Equipment debt is a weighted accounting value, while obligation rows retain the
actual resource family and quantity needed for exact settlement. Each ordinary
equipment row carries 45 debt, support rows 20, fuel rows 40, finite prototype
or extended-equipment rows 90, and anomalous-provider rows 100. Manpower rows
carry no equipment debt and instead contribute their exact issued quantity to a
separate manpower liability. Partial payments reduce a row's weighted debt in
the same proportion as its exact outstanding quantity. A final payment clears
the residual exactly.

An unaccounted lot can be selected and settled as one exact transaction. Every
outstanding person, weapon, vehicle, chassis, train, fuel, or anomalous-resource
row must be affordable before any debit occurs. A read-only preflight freezes
the complete obligation-UID set, proves its row count and exact debt/manpower
aggregates, and proves any terminal unaccounted disposition before country
resources are touched. The post-debit pass is therefore a deterministic commit
with no late row-validation branch and no partial-payment rollback gap. The
absent lot is archived only when both its weighted equipment debt and exact
manpower liability reach zero. This path never records destruction or
demobilization credit. Incident debt forgiveness is accounting-only and never
becomes salvageable material.

Paid formation requests snapshot the reusable generation tail, every affected
ledger length, country aggregates, generated templates and units, resource
payment, and relevant tracking flags before preparation. Cooldown, request
counts, Muster Control, board state, and success telemetry commit only after an
engine template, exact live division identity, and aligned ledger rows are all
proved. Failure deletes the newly allocated template and cohorts, truncates the
appended rows, restores the prior state, and refunds the exact quoted payment.
Monotonic UID allocators are not rewound, so a failed request can leave harmless
identity gaps but no live formation, resource loss, or history increment.

### Bounded lifetime-ledger compaction

Terminal Event 19 rows are maintained by a country-scoped state machine on the
existing pulse. A cycle is due no more often than every thirty days unless
changed row counts leave a dirty backlog. Candidate and referential scans inspect
at most twenty-four rows per pulse. A commit removes at most sixty-four aligned
logical rows, and a cycle commits at most four lot graphs plus four generation
summaries. Every multi-pulse proof freezes source-array sizes and the scenario
launch serial, revalidates stable UIDs immediately before mutation, and removes
indexes in descending order. Drift restarts the bounded proof instead of
committing against a stale row.

A lot can compact only after its exact generation is resolved or archived, its
unit, obligation, selected-state, template, component, and auxiliary records are
fully detached and terminal, and equipment debt, manpower liability, live-unit
count, and claimant identity are all zero. Shared or reusable templates,
claimant history, transfer-staged objects, and externally unresolved derivative
identity are preserved. Generation summaries retain the newest twelve Muster
Board rows and every protected last-closed, derivative-origin, or scenario-origin
identity. Open-board commits repair at most forty cached lot rows and twelve
history rows. They never rebuild the lifetime view synchronously. Order from
Noise proof UIDs stop growing at the achievement's exact distinct-lot threshold.

## Management systems

### Muster Control

Muster Control measures whether the state can identify, direct, supply, and
legally dispose of the formations. Audits, staff work, training, liaison,
controlled integration, and supervised demobilization improve it. Unresolved
lots, divided command, claimant autonomy, missing units, and breaches reduce it.

### Army Congestion

Army Congestion is recalculated from live lot burdens, supply mismatch, requests,
claimants, anomalous families, scenario floors, and durable management pressure
or relief. Each outstanding 500 manpower adds one battalion-equivalent of
congestion until its exact liability row is settled. It is not a temporary gauge
that can be erased by the next refresh.

### Integration and demobilization

Lot actions always select a stable lot UID before mutation. Standardization,
emergency integration, supervised demobilization, and specialist preservation
all require zero exact equipment debt and zero exact manpower liability. This
keeps unpaid manpower in the existing liability, Army Congestion, pressure-idea,
Muster Board accounting, and decision-availability surfaces instead of allowing
integration to erase it.

Standardization creates at most one country-level `National Muster Standard`: a
locked, nonrecruitable eight-battalion ordinary line-infantry template. Its one
template-ledger row deliberately owns sentinel lot UID 0, with one aligned
component row for each battalion. Before a conversion starts and again at
mission completion, the system proves the selected lot UID, generation UID,
unique source-template UID and lot ownership, exact active unit-UID set, and
exact live division count. Completion charges the shared 15 percent
`standardization_loss_share` against physically paid unit-backed equipment and
fuel only, never manpower or forgiven amounts, then converts every proven unit
with `change_division_template`. A failed post-conversion proof restores the
old dynamic template and refunds the exact charge. Only a successful proof
repoints the lot and unit ledgers to the canonical UID and retires the obsolete
source as an inert locked shell.

Verified supervised demobilization returns 30 percent of each physically paid,
unit-backed obligation row. Its preflight proves the same exact unit identity
set plus exclusive lot ownership of the locked source template, then performs
one `delete_units` operation for that template. Salvage and ledger retirement
are committed only after the exact division delta and complete lot-identity
absence are proven. Specialist preservation uses the same safe teardown and
returns 45 percent of paid support equipment plus 45 percent of the paid
manpower share attributable to support companies, then grants a temporary
training benefit. Technical cleanup may reconcile an already absent annexed
cohort, forfeits every remaining row, and grants no salvage.

Standardized lots are resolved (`standardized` is beyond the unresolved
selection range) and therefore cannot be selected for later single-lot
demobilization. The teardown preflight also rejects the canonical sentinel row
explicitly, so shared canonical units can never be deleted through a unique-lot
template order. Event 96's `division_template_lock_event_active` marker gates
both starts and completion-time mutations. An overlap fails and cleans up the
mission without conversion, deletion, or salvage. No new visual asset is needed
for these transactions. They reuse the existing standardization and
demobilization decision sprites documented below.

## Evolutions

Each evolution supports both an active-event migration path and a pre-fire
opening path. Evolution history is recorded once in the shared Event Log.
Scenario actors and derivative countries do not contribute to ordinary Event 19
evolution sampling or start the normal evolution timer.

### Evolution I: Organized Muster

District staffs, common rolls, training cycles, territorial assignments, and
integration offices improve organization and equipment state. Formation lots
remain identifiable and accountable throughout the management cycle.

### Evolution II: Arsenal Lottery

Serious, advanced, and strange formation families enter the weighted pool.
Technology/project gates are checked before selection, unusual materiel is
finite, and maintenance or replacement obligations follow the exact manifest.
Countries can submit costly requests for numbers, discipline, firepower,
mobility, or an unrestricted draw.

Every active advanced lot also receives an aligned prototype-disposition row.
The player can select an unmanaged lot and either preserve it through a dynamic
120-to-365-day maintenance trial or cannibalize its exact recorded cohort.
Preservation pays army experience, support equipment, trains, and fuel, then
proves the same generation, lot, template, and live unit identities at timeout.
A sound result improves training, quality, and equipment condition, keeps the
formation under a restricted-reinforcement burden, and grants one finite
technology-category insight derived from the prototype profile. A strained but
identifiable formation survives with material decay. Missing or mismatched
identity fails closed.

Cannibalization is a distinct controlled teardown disposition. It deletes only
the proved prototype cohort and its unique template, salvages the settled and
unit-backed share of its obligations, forfeits unpaid obligations without
turning them into material, lowers congestion, and may recover a smaller
profile-specific research insight. It never records supervised demobilization
or awards its achievement credit. AI countries preserve the strongest
sustainable lot when industry and fuel permit, and dismantle the least
sustainable lot under severe congestion, fuel shortage, or equipment debt.

### Evolution III: Command Fracture

Normal automatic spawning is disabled by default. New draws use the consolidated
ordinary component registry to build safe random combat and support layouts.
Quality and coherence are separate properties, so a powerful formation can
remain internally incompatible. Claimant officers gain persistent identities,
regional names, identity scene sprites, demands, takeover attempts, failed coups, and revolt
paths. One-state countries use takeover or failed-coup logic instead of an
unsafe territorial split.

For multi-state countries, an ordinary claimant release remains available as
soon as Evolution III is active. The selector uses claimant headquarters and
exact loyal-unit origins, and neither Evolution IV nor an anomalous provider is
required. Failure to prove an eligible loyal set produces the visible failed
coup and cools down the crisis rather than leaving it silently mature.

The region transaction prefers controlled, passable, non-capital mainland
states. A non-capital island can anchor the same connected transaction only when
the parent has no viable mainland base. If the claimant headquarters is the
protected parent capital or otherwise unsafe, an exact loyal formation origin
becomes the breakaway headquarters; the parent capital is never transferred.

## Claimants

Twenty fictional male claimant identities cover the planned regional male name
pools, identity scene sprites, traits, and metadata. Every generated claimant
character explicitly uses `female = no`. Claimants can request equipment,
political authority, territorial autonomy, recognition, or command. The state
can accept, refuse, counter-command, discredit, arrest, or ultimately promote a
claimant. Counter-command and discredit responses each set their own shared
officer-search lock in the response effect, so the decision, Muster Board, and

A pending demand stores the issuing claimant's immutable UID separately from
the movable Muster Board selection. Board rebuild, player decisions, scripted
GUI buttons, AI response, timeout refusal, and deferred replay must resolve that
same UID before accepting or refusing anything. The deferred record additionally
freezes the demand enum. Missing or mismatched ownership enters the ledger
invariant and clears the malformed pending context; it never substitutes another
claimant row. Every demand completion, crisis teardown, derivative conversion,
and ordinary-country cleanup clears the bound UID with the pending flag.

### Event 67 Generalissimo bridge

Event 67 records its chosen country in `global.generalissimo_country` and its
chosen character in `global.generalissimo`. Event 19 treats that record as live
only when the recorded country is the current country and the exact recorded
character remains an active army leader owned by it. This rejects stale global
values after a character dies, retires, or leaves the country.

The selected claimant's immutable character UID is compared with the recorded
Generalissimo character ID. This produces two distinct command relationships:

- When the claimant is the Generalissimo, claimant demand generation is limited
  to emergency command powers during war, a political seat when it can be paid,
  or a formal appointment. AI acceptance receives a dedicated preference for
  preserving the existing supreme command.
- When a different claimant rises beneath a living Generalissimo, strong Muster
  Control favors a formally subordinate commission. Weak control or high
  claimant influence favors a parallel command. Accepting the subordinate post
  reduces claimant influence and raises Muster Control. Accepting the parallel
  command increases claimant influence and lowers Muster Control. Refusal has
  separate, centrally tuned consequences for each relationship.

A natural one-state claimant takeover against a different living Generalissimo
uses the existing failed-coup resolver. A natural takeover by the Generalissimo,
or in a country without a valid Event 67 record, keeps the ordinary takeover
path. Direct scenario takeovers still call the original takeover effect and are
unchanged. The bridge creates no new ideas or flags, changes no parent event
stage or count, and contributes to no world-end route.

Event 127 Warlords and Event 131 Widespread Mutiny have no live top-level event
namespaces, event scripts, registrations, or event-name localisation in the
current repository. The only matching Event 127 and Event 131 identity remnants
are orphan `chaosx.event_name.127` and `chaosx.event_name.131` selectors in the
settings and debug scripted-localisation files. Other occurrences of `127`,
`131`, or the word `warlords` are unrelated subevents, provider tokens, map
identifiers, or Event 14 text. Event 19 therefore adds no runtime hook for those
two absent events and invents no replacement identifiers.

Revolt eligibility is tied to recorded loyal Event 19 formations. Because the
engine has no documented division-scoped ownership-transfer effect, the system
uses the approved recreate-then-delete transaction. It recreates only the
frozen UID and cohort set from the recorded Event 19 template/component
manifest, copies ledger identities and outstanding obligations without
consuming global allocators, proves the complete destination set and territory,
and only then deletes the matching source cohorts with `disband = no`.

The transaction also prevents a revolt from repairing damaged formations. Each
source division must have at least the larger of its recorded starting equipment
and manpower factors, while every replacement must remain at or below that same
ceiling. This proves that the recreated force is not stronger in aggregate than
the live source set. HOI4 does not expose exact live per-equipment composition,
manpower fill, organization, veterancy, decorations, officer history, army
assignment, or orders to this script path. Those live-only properties are not
claimed as preserved; the authoritative transfer contract is the immutable
Event 19 issue manifest, starting factors, identities, and obligations.

Any failure before source-accounting commit invokes exact recovery: prove and
remove destination replacements, remove provisional cores, annex the unpublished
actor with troop transfer disabled, prove the selected territory returned,
recreate only missing frozen parent UIDs, rebind their original rows, and prove
the full restored source set before either country unlocks. A recovery that had
to recreate a source division permanently disables another natural
recreate/delete revolt for that parent. A failure after source-accounting commit
locks both countries as an invariant failure instead of attempting a lossy
rollback.

The ordinary-claimant, anomalous-claimant, and claimant-independent family
preflights never use a country-wide fraction, never select an unrelated national
division, and fail closed when a stored unit scope, UID, delete cohort, template,
lot, generation, origin state, or mode-appropriate loyalty record cannot be
proved. Ordinary claimant failure resolves through the visible failed coup.
Family failure restores the source or enters quarantine, applies a bounded
containment setback, and exposes a visible deferred-breach report.
