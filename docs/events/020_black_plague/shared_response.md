# Event 020 Shared Disease Response

Event 020 uses the existing `chaosx_disease_containment_category` and the existing disease board. It does not register a second Black Plague decision category. State actions are selected from the board, check the selected state's phase and control, and resolve through a bounded project that pays equipment, fuel, civilian production, command power, or stability as appropriate.

## State actions

`common/decisions/020_black_plague_shared_response_decisions.txt` registers prevention, transport, military-route, hospital, travel, relief, burial, evacuation, containment, recovery, cured-state, and anti-rat actions. `common/scripted_triggers/020_black_plague_shared_response_triggers.txt` owns the phase and state gates. `common/scripted_effects/020_black_plague_shared_response_effects.txt` owns population-band scaling, payment, action ownership, outcomes, cooldowns, weekly pulses, cancellation, and terminal cleanup.

The action ids are centralized in `black_plague_shared_action` in `common/script_constants/020_black_plague_shared_response_constants.txt`. Durations, costs, burden, pulse reductions, recovery gains, and anti-rat results are kept in the same constants file. A state action uses the selected state's population bands (`minimum`, `standard`, `large`, and `metropolitan`) to scale support equipment, motorized equipment, and fuel. The country-level knowledge programmes use the country cost band and commit `country_program` response burden while active.

State pulse flags are cleared when the state becomes Cured or when the disease episode's terminal cleanup runs. A cancelled action pays no second outcome and releases the action burden. The terminal cleanup also clears the shared country flags and re-evaluates the shared crisis board.

## Country knowledge actions

The shared category includes Publish Findings, Restricted Alliance Exchange, Hoard Protocol, Steal Foreign Progress, International Medical Mission, Emergency Countermeasure Drive, route-crisis responses, Royal Node strikes, Crown Strike, and post-defeat Seal Royal Burrows. They require the countermeasure programme or recorded findings, use equipment and fuel rather than political power, and feed the existing 0–100 countermeasure progress producer. Preserving the Royal Basin archives in the defeat report registers the responder and exposes Publish Findings immediately; its recovered evidence remains latent until the normal countermeasure setup transfers it, so the archive cannot cure a state or skip the 0–100 progression. Intelligence theft requires a real intelligence agency and operative; it does not use a synthetic `operative_recruited` flag.

Royal Node AI willingness is urgency-aware rather than a flat emergency score. It rises when terminal preparation or RTX Dominion is high, when the country has an active Severe Crisis state, and while at war; the thresholds and factors live in `black_plague_response_ai` so balance changes do not scatter magic numbers through the decision file.

After Evolution V opens its earned route, a human country that still holds established Black Plague ground can start either native mission in the same category, and may run both concurrently when it meets both gates. Hold the Line pays support, motorized, infantry, train, fuel, manpower, command-power, factory, stability, and war-support costs and gains weekly progress from war, countermeasure readiness, and held states. Secure the Refuge uses the same payment surface at a higher cost, requires a held terminal capital, refuge node, or city, and gains an additional node bonus. Success lowers global Rat King terminal preparation, raises countermeasure progress, and improves containment without curing a state. Timeout raises terminal preparation and Rat King hunger and adds incoming exposure to the remaining human-established states. Both missions are removed by the event-owned runtime when the last eligible ground is lost or when the terminal takeover begins. Crown Strike and Seal Royal Burrows now use the same native mission surface: a state-selected zero-day launcher stores the target marker, and the country mission owns the 180-day timer, cancellation, factory reservation, and exactly-once shared-action resolution.
After a Rat defeat or full eradication, the same category exposes three optional country projects. Rebuild and Keep Vigilance runs for 210 days, advances countermeasure findings, improves containment and treatment in controlled recovering or cured states, and lowers relapse risk. The International Inspection Compact runs for 360 days with a faction partner and a controlled coastal hub; its timed country flag reduces internal transport, troop, refugee, and port exposure on both ends of those routes. Condemn Future Weaponization runs for 60 days, records the verified ban, improves stability and war support, advances the countermeasure programme, and blocks the existing Black Plague weaponization project. Each action pays the country material band, reserves civilian factories, registers Response Capacity, and resolves through a logged event without instant cure or state deletion.
The same category also exposes a 420-day Population Recovery Programme and a 120-day Memorial and Biosecurity Charter. Population Recovery applies a timed state stewardship modifier to controlled recovering or cured states with devastation, lowers that devastation, improves treatment, and lowers relapse risk. The Memorial Charter is permanent for the country, adds stability and war support, and gives recovered states a durable stewardship modifier while preserving the countermeasure record. Neither project creates a new disease category or changes a phase without the ordinary state machine.

## Integration points

`black_plague_process_current_state_persistent_response` calls `black_plague_apply_shared_action_pulse` after Black Plague-specific response deltas and before the final state delta application. National capacity recomputation counts an active shared state action and an active country programme. The response registry retains shared-action states and owners so a live project cannot disappear from burden accounting.

The decisions use existing vanilla decision sprites for generic research, civil support, intelligence operation, quarantine, faction integration, and medical missions. Event-020-specific decision art is defined in `interface/020_black_plague_response.gfx` and stored under `gfx/interface/decisions/020_black_plague/`. The shared board's Severe Crisis and Collapsed selected-state card uses `GFX_black_plague_crisis_seal_static` and `GFX_black_plague_crisis_seal_animated`, registered in `interface/020_black_plague_rat_identity.gfx` against `gfx/interface/animated/020_black_plague/crisis_seal/black_plague_crisis_seal_static.dds` and `gfx/interface/animated/020_black_plague/crisis_seal/black_plague_crisis_seal_sheet.dds`. The RTX final-order decision `black_plague_rat_king_execute_terminal_takeover` uses `GFX_black_plague_rat_king_terminal_readiness_animated` from the same GFX file, with static fallback `GFX_black_plague_rat_king_terminal_readiness_static` and textures `gfx/interface/animated/020_black_plague/world_end_readiness_seal/black_plague_rat_king_terminal_readiness_static.dds` and `gfx/interface/animated/020_black_plague/world_end_readiness_seal/black_plague_rat_king_terminal_readiness_sheet.dds`. The shared disease board now reports terminal readiness through `GetBlackPlagueTerminalReadiness`; the route-open and target-selected rows include target control ratio, surviving capital and refuge-node counts, route-focus readiness, Rat King Dominion/Sentience/Cohesion/Hunger, recorded deaths, human-established state count, and the aggregate human response-strength register. Crown Strike is also an AI-eligible shared-category action once its war, route, contribution, target, and payment gates are satisfied; its urgency weights use terminal pressure, Rat King Dominion, and war state. No separate scripted-GUI readiness window is used.

## Future depth

Future response work can add state-specific scripted tooltips for each population band and faction-level findings diplomacy without changing the shared category or action API. Any new action must reserve a new constant id, add a phase-gated trigger, define a real cost and duration, add a player-facing name/description/cost tooltip, and extend terminal cleanup. Native missions that need a state target must follow the Crown/Seal pattern: a zero-day state launcher, an explicit state marker and owner, a country mission with `days_mission_timeout`, and a resolver that clears both marker and mission during timeout, cancellation, defeat, and world-end cleanup.
