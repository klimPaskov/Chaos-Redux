# Black Plague weaponization raid

The Black Plague programme produces a real `plague_bomb_1` payload at offensive project completion and through the later stockpile-expansion decision. A country that has completed the programme, retained offensive delivery, and unlocked the plague-bomb project or delivery technology can prepare `black_plague_weaponization_air_delivery` in the existing Biological Raids category. Ordinary defensive work and payload production remain on the disease board.

The player chooses an enemy state and assigns at least 50 tactical or 20 strategic bombers from an air base. Native raid creation reserves one plague-bomb payload and 60 support equipment and allocates 35 Command Power. The fourteen-day preparation is part of the raid, so there is no attack decision, attack factory timer, or attack fuel payment. The native raid owns cancellation and its reserved resources. The project and stockpile-expansion actions retain their own production commitments and material costs.

War, target control, disease eligibility, and CBRN authorization are checked when the raid is prepared and launched. A successful or critical result carries the exact actor, victim, and state through regular event targets into hidden actor events `chaosx_nr20_weaponization.9` and `.10`. The bridge checks the same country and state again before calling the shared weaponized-exposure runtime. Only an accepted release records weaponization history, the achievement receipt, public condemnation, and the Event 020 delivery report. Failure and limited success record native raid history and aircraft effects but do not claim a confirmed Black Plague exposure. No callback debits a second payload, support equipment, Command Power, or fuel.

The raid uses the existing `GFX_raid_type_icon_plague_strike` map glyph from `interface/chaosx_raids.gfx`, `GFX_plague_bomb_equipment_medium` badge from `interface/chaosx_equipment.gfx`, and vanilla `GFX_other_target_icon` marker from `interface/buildings/landmarks.gfx`. No new sprite or DDS file is required. The production decisions keep `GFX_decision_black_plague_weapon_delivery` in `interface/020_black_plague_weaponization.gfx`, and the report keeps `GFX_report_event_020_black_plague_unbound` in `interface/020_black_plague_event_pictures.gfx`.

The offline wiki at `paradox_wiki/Data structures - Hearts of Iron 4 Wiki.md:263-327` defines regular event-target lifetime, while installed `documentation/effects_documentation.md:6496-6505` and `documentation/triggers_documentation.md:3844-3853` define the save and presence checks. Vanilla `common/raids/paratrooper_raids_custom.txt:52-67` and mod `common/raids/biological_raids.txt` provide native raid precedents. The connected HOI4 MCP exposes event inspection and rendering but no ordinary raid inspection or native raid AI probability adapter; source review and the user's live-game validation remain necessary for reservation and cancellation behavior.

Future extensions could vary release strength by outcome and add evidence from assigned bomber losses to the forensic investigation system. Those changes should reuse the same native raid and exposure bridge, with no second attack menu or payment path.
