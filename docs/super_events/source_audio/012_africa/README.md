## Production contract

Both edits use the exact source window from 00:00.000 through 01:55.000.

The production chain resamples once to stereo 44,100 Hz, applies a 0.100-second equal-power safety fade-in, applies a seven-second equal-power fade from 01:48.000 to 01:55.000, preserves the musical dynamics through a single fixed gain, and exports a 24-bit PCM production master.

Role 2 uses `volume=7.82dB`, producing -20.00 LUFS integrated loudness and -1.60 dBTP on the lossless master.

Role 3 uses `volume=-2.02dB`, producing -19.06 LUFS integrated loudness and -3.20 dBTP on the lossless master.

No dynamic compression, peak limiting, loop, repeated bar, time stretch, pitch change, denoising, or synthetic extension is used.

The Ogg Vorbis runtime files are encoded from the lossless production masters at quality 5.

The PCM WAV sound files are encoded from the lossless production masters, not decoded from the Ogg files.
