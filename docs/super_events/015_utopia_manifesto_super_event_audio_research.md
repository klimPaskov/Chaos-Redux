# Event 015 Utopia Manifesto super-event audio research

## Scope and final disposition

This package supplies one shared, route-spanning cue for the Event 15 regional proclamation super-event, whose selected title is **UTOPIA HAS NEIGHBORS**. One recording is preferable to five route tracks here: the selected passage can carry civic promise, material coordination, planning discipline, closed-state unease, and practical restraint without turning any route into a simple victory scene.

The final cue is a real orchestral performance. It is not generated audio, a MIDI render, a drone, ambience, a test waveform, or placeholder music. Composition and recording rights were checked separately. The exact source, frozen file-page evidence, CC0 deed and legal code, and final 44.1 kHz OGG are preserved in event-scoped folders.

## Musical and route fit

The opening is lyrical but not serene. Its measured pulse and recurring minor-key tension give the proclamation weight without turning it into a march, coronation, or battle victory. Warm orchestral statements support the achievement of a functioning regional order; darker returns keep assignment, hierarchy, and dependence audible as unresolved costs.

| Route | Fit |
| --- | --- |
| Voluntary commonwealth | The warm opening and patient rise support linked municipalities and public promise without declaring history finished. |
| Council union | Repeated phrases passed through the orchestra suggest coordination and response, while the restrained tempo leaves room for deliberation and delay. |
| Planned Utopia | Regular pacing and a controlled long arc fit transport, housing, schedules, and technical standards; minor-key pressure prevents the result from sounding like neutral efficiency. |
| Closed island-state | The disciplined pulse can carry mass service and rigid order, while the darker swells expose unease rather than celebrating compulsion. |
| Practical commonwealth | The movement's modest, unresolved character suits reform by aid, revision, and example instead of one sacred model. |

The same cue therefore supports all five route-sensitive descriptions. Separate tracks would add licensing and integration burden without producing a clearer dramatic distinction than the route art and text already provide.

## Candidate disposition

- **Selected:** Brahms, Symphony No. 3, III. *Poco allegretto*, Musopen Symphony Orchestra. File-specific CC0, exact checksum match, unused work, and the strongest shared balance of warmth, discipline, and unease.
- **Rejected after production test:** Holst, *First Suite in E-flat*, I. *Chaconne*, United States Marine Band. The musical fit was excellent, but the recording's affirmative public-domain basis was U.S. federal law rather than a clean worldwide dedication or licence. The downloaded test source, evidence, and derivative were removed before delivery.
- **Rejected historical cue:** Bruckner, *Ave Maria*, United States Navy Band Sea Chanters. Its U.S.-federal basis presents the same worldwide-distribution problem, and sacred choral character overweights the proclamation toward moral consecration.
- **Rejected historical cue:** Chopin, *Funeral March*, Op. 72 No. 2, Aya Higuchi. Its CC0 rights are clear, but the piece is too funereal for voluntary and practical routes and makes the regional proclamation sound like defeat.
- **Rejected:** Mussorgsky, the first *Promenade* from the Musopen/Skidmore *Pictures at an Exhibition*. The rights record is strong, but its procession is more openly triumphant and another movement from the same source album and orchestra is already used by Event 018.
- **Rejected:** Beethoven, Symphony No. 7, II. *Allegretto*, John Michel cello arrangement. The licence is compatible, but the solo arrangement is less civic and more elegiac than the selected full-orchestra passage.

The removed historical Event 15 package at commit `0bce9e9a4` and its removal in `ae2191ed1` were inspected only as evidence. Its two-cue `New Utopia` / `Marked Bounds` split is not treated as design authority over the accepted five-route proclamation specification.
