# Event 005 super-event quotation research

This note records the exact quotation text, displayed attribution, primary or archival source, translation, rights assessment, and thematic fit for Soviet Collapse super-event slots 14 through 22.

## Slot 14 — The Union Unmade

The displayed quotation is “The state is a relation of men dominating men.” It is attributed to Max Weber, *Politics as a Vocation* (1919). The wording is verified in the [Balliol College Oxford extract](https://www.balliol.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/politics_as_a_vocation_extract.pdf). Attribution confidence is high. Rights confidence is medium-high because the historical text is public domain while the extract does not identify its English translator. The line frames the collapse as the loss of an accepted relation of command.

## Slot 15 — The Black Banner Returns

The displayed quotation is “To be governed is to be watched.” It is attributed to Pierre-Joseph Proudhon, *General Idea of the Revolution in the Nineteenth Century* (1851), translated by John Beverly Robinson (1923). The wording and edition are verified through the [Fair Use Repository text](https://fair-use.org/p-j-proudhon/general-idea-of-the-revolution/) and the [Wits University archival copy](https://wiredspace.wits.ac.za/server/api/core/bitstreams/59b65e1a-d36b-4c63-acd9-3cbb3ce6ebd0/content). Attribution confidence and rights confidence are high. The quotation directly supports the Black Banner rejection of every successor government.

## Slot 16 — The Northern Signals Break

The displayed quotation is “Therefore, just as water retains no constant shape, so in warfare there are no constant conditions.” It is attributed to Sun Tzu, *The Art of War*, translated by Lionel Giles (1910), chapter VI, section 32. The exact public-domain translation is verified in [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/17405/17405-h/17405-h.htm). Attribution confidence and rights confidence are high. It replaces the unsupported invented line previously used in this slot and describes local commands adapting after the fixed chain of orders breaks.

## Slot 18 — Every Port a Council

The displayed quotation is “All power to soviets, and not parties.” It is the headline and slogan in *Kronstadt Izvestia* No. 5, dated 7 March 1921. The exact wording is verified in the [Kronstadt Izvestia archive](https://www.marxists.org/history/ussr/events/kronstadt/izvestia/05.htm). Attribution confidence is high and rights confidence is medium-high because the underlying 1921 slogan is public-domain historical material while the archived English translation is modern and the excerpt is seven words. The exact wording replaces the loose variant previously used in this slot.
