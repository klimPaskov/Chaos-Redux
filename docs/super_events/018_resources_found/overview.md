## Slot 82 — first emergence

### Original display text

- **Title:** `THE OTH-KESH HOST RISES`
- **Description:** The extraction zone has collapsed into a passage wider than its deepest surveys recorded. Mineral-armored formations advance through the ruins in ordered columns, with the cave monster Vhorruk directing them toward the border roads. The fallen field has become the capital of the Oth-Kesh Host, and its war broods are already attacking every country along its borders.
- **Button:** `The field has a sovereign.`

The text introduces an organized nonhuman polity, the literal cave-monster leader Vhorruk, the former resource field as its capital, and its immediate neighbor wars without describing the emergence as an automatic global ending.

### Quotation and provenance

> As for the earth, out of it comes bread. Underneath it is turned up as it were by fire.

- **Attribution:** Job 28:5, World English Bible.
- **Direct source:** [World English Bible British Edition, Job 28](https://ebible.org/engwebpb/JOB28.htm).
- **Rights:** The linked World English Bible edition is public domain and expressly permits copying.
- **Context:** Job 28 moves from mining and the exposure of buried materials to the contrast between the productive surface and the disturbed earth below. The line is an analogy, not Oth-Kesh religious lore or a biological claim.

## Slot 83 — cross-continent World End

### Original display text

- **Title:** `THE DEEP WAR CROSSES THE SEAS`
- **Description:** The Oth-Kesh Host has converted its first continent into a connected domain of chambers, resource basins, and marching routes. Across distant continents, mines, refineries, and industrial districts split open under stronger formations shaped by the first war. Vhorruk’s armies are emerging on several shores at once, and the ground beneath the remaining countries has become a front.
- **Button:** `Every deep has found a road.`

The copy records three visible facts: the first continent has become a connected Oth-Kesh domain, distant resource-weighted footholds have opened, and the war has become simultaneous across continents. It does not expose numeric chaos or control gates.

### Quotation and provenance

> the firmament is embroiled with the deep.

- **Attribution:** Aeschylus, *Prometheus Bound*, translated by Theodore Alois Buckley.
- **Direct source:** [Project Gutenberg, *Prometheus Bound and the Seven Against Thebes*](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/27458/27458-h/27458-h.htm); [ebook rights record](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/27458).
- **Rights:** The ancient play and Buckley’s nineteenth-century translation are public domain. Buckley died in 1856.
- **Context:** The clause closes a longer account of earth, thunder, winds, the heavens, and the deep being driven into one conflict. Its lower-case opening is preserved because it is an exact terminal clause rather than a newly capitalized sentence. It remains a historical analogy, not Oth-Kesh lore.

- **Title:** `THE LAST DEPTH IS SEALED`
- **Description:** The last territory held by the Oth-Kesh Host has been retaken, and every known foothold has been broken or sealed. Survivors return to damaged resource regions beside engineers, burial teams, and soldiers carrying weapons built for the underground war. ## Runtime trigger and presentation contract

The constants in `common/script_constants/018_resources_found_cave_constants.txt` bind display values `82`, `83`, and `84` to audio IDs `54`, `55`, and `56` for a fourteen-day super-event presentation.

- **Emergence:** the first successful Oth-Kesh country creation calls `resources_found_emit_emergence_super_event`. A one-shot global flag prevents later breach reinforcements from replaying the country-birth super-event.
- **World End:** only the final focus `DHO_the_world_opens_below` calls `resources_found_cave_begin_world_end`. The shared effect rechecks verified control of every eligible origin-continent state, chaos above the configured gate, enabled World End settings, no active World End, and the completed preparation state before setting the terminal flags, suspending incompatible field progression, transforming DHO, opening resource-weighted footholds, and emitting slot `83`.
- **Defeat:** regional defeat uses its own news path and cannot emit slot `84`. The defeat super-event requires `resources_found_cave_global_defeat_eligible`, which is set only when the cave threat became global through World End/footholds or met the near-global long-campaign condition. It fires once after complete DHO defeat and cleanup routing.

`common/scripted_localisation/chaosx_scripted_localisation_super_events.txt` maps the three display values to the three image sprites. `localisation/english/018_resources_found_system_l_english.yml` supplies `.t`, `.d`, `.q`, and `.a` for each slot. `common/scripted_effects/018_resources_found_cave_effects.txt` assigns the matching audio ID and dispatches the settings-aware sound helper. `sound/chaosx_sound.asset` retains one base sound definition and six soundeffect wrappers per cue for the runtime WAV files, while the canonical `music/chaosx_music_track_list.html` catalogue remains parent-owned.

## Technical and uniqueness disposition

All three OGG files are unique Vorbis recordings at 44.1 kHz stereo and between 60 and 120 seconds. All three WAV mirrors are unique 44.1 kHz stereo PCM s16le files. Their decoded PCM hashes are also mutually unique. The original images are three separate generated compositions at `457x328`, with separate source PNG, processed PNG, runtime DDS, and sprite records.

Rejected music candidates include reused repository recordings, provenance-unclear performances, YouTube-only sources, modern commercial recordings, generated or MIDI-rendered cues, drones, test tones, and the superseded jurisdiction-limited Debussy recording. Rejected quotation candidates and their context are retained in the detailed text research; none is live. No placeholder text, substitute super-event visual, generated music, reused final super-event image, unresolved quotation attribution, or unresolved distribution-rights caveat remains in this combined package.

## Validation boundary

Static checks prove identifier, path, file, duration, codec, checksum, source, licence, localisation, sprite, trigger, and one-shot wiring alignment. The project does not launch HOI4 for this task; therefore actual in-engine mask composition, playback loudness, display timing, and event sequencing remain live-session validation rather than claims made by this research note.
