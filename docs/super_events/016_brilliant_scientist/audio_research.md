## Rights risks rejected during selection

- The United States Marine Band recording of Handel's *Water Music: Sarabande* was rejected for Laboratory World. Section 105 establishes United States public-domain status for qualifying federal works but does not itself resolve protection abroad. The CC BY-SA Halvorsen recording removes that uncertainty.
- The 1921 Edison *Ride of the Valkyries* file rejected in Event 013 remains rejected. Event 016 uses a different 2014 performance with an explicit worldwide EFF Open Audio License.
