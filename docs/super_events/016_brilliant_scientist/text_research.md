# Event 016 Brilliant Scientist super-event text research

## Scope and authority

This note supplies a text-only research package for all six Event 016 super-events. It covers titles, displayed quotations, button remarks, source links, attribution confidence, rights status, contextual fit, risks, and final recommendations.

The following parent dispositions are binding for this research:

- International recognition remains a conditional super-event package. Recommendation R1 to remove it is rejected.
- Kruger State formation remains a full package for a viable sovereign country formed by charter, enclave, rebellion, or takeover.
- Global threat remains distinct from formation. It marks an organized expansion power, not merely an unusual new state.
- Laboratory World is administrative conquest, submission, integration, and global facility governance.
- Strategic Singularity is a vulnerable multi-year denied-victory device. Its firing raises chaos above the world-end threshold and enters the Fallout pipeline.
- Defeat aftermath remains conditional on a long global crisis. It is not a reward for a local or regional victory.

## Rights and confidence legend

- **PD-US, explicit** means the host identifies the underlying text or collection as public domain in the United States.
- **PD-US, high** means the work and consulted edition are old enough to be public domain in the United States, with a public-domain institutional or Project Gutenberg copy.
- **Federal or donated public rights, high** means an official U.S. source supplies the text and the relevant institutional record supports public reuse.
- **KJV caveat** means the King James Version is public domain in the United States, while Crown rights and permissions can matter in the United Kingdom.
- **Original** means newly proposed Chaos Redux wording in this note. It makes no quotation claim and needs no source link. This is not a trademark clearance opinion.

Rights conclusions are scoped to the supplied text and cited editions. Distribution outside the United States should still follow the law of the distribution jurisdiction. Project Gutenberg branding and license terms should not be copied into the mod. Only the underlying public-domain wording is proposed.

Attribution confidence describes confidence in the exact displayed wording and named source, not a general judgment about every edition of a work.
