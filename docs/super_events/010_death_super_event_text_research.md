## Research Notes

- `The Name in the Ledger`, `The Census of Zol`, and `No Witness Remains` are original event titles. They are not presented as direct quotations.
- Every remark and quote is backed by a public-domain primary text or public-domain scripture translation.
- The Black Oath quote uses the first clause of Isaiah 28:15. The longer clause is kept out of localisation so the event reads as a formal oath instead of a full scripture citation.
- No selected line comes from modern copyrighted fiction, games, songs, films, or unverified quote aggregators.
