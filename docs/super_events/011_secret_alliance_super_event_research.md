# Event 011 Secret Alliance Super-Event Research

## Role

- Event id: `011`
- Event slug: `secret_alliance`
- Super-event role: public reveal of the hidden compact as the visible `Anti-[target country] Pact`
- Tone target: cold diplomatic menace, coordinated betrayal, sudden clarity after repeated covert incidents

This note covers text research only. It does not propose localisation edits, event wiring, GFX, GUI, sound definitions, or spreadsheet changes.

## Title Direction

### Recommended title

- `Anti-[Target Country] Pact`

### Why this is the best title

- It matches the event's public function exactly: the hidden system has become a named bloc.
- It is colder and more diplomatic than a dramatic sentence-title.
- It aligns with the spec direction that the public faction name itself is dynamic and visible.
- It avoids generic super-event language such as "The Hidden Alliance" or "The Plot Revealed."

### Backup title directions

- `The Pact Declares Itself`
- `The Hidden Compact`
- `Encirclement, Confirmed`

Implementation note: if the faction itself is named `Anti-[Target Country] Pact`, the cleanest final localisation is to use that exact dynamic name as the super-event title as well, without adding `The`.

## Main Quote Research

### Selected quote

> "The real cause I consider to be the one which was formally most kept out of sight. The growth of the power of Athens, and the alarm which this inspired in Lacedaemon, made war inevitable."

- Selected speaker/author: Thucydides
- Source: *History of the Peloponnesian War*, Book I, Chapter 23, Richard Crawley translation
- Date or period: 5th century BCE history, 19th century translation used for wording
- Source URL: https://classics.mit.edu/Thucydides/pelopwar.1.first.html
- Attribution confidence: High
- Copyright/public-domain note: ancient source text, Crawley translation is public domain

### Why it fits

- It directly names the distinction between public pretexts and the hidden real cause.
- It frames alliance formation through fear, not friendship, which matches the mechanics spec.
- It sounds diplomatic and historical rather than mystical or melodramatic.
- It supports a reveal that can tip directly into open war.

### Exact wording recommendation for final localisation

- Quote text:
  `The real cause I consider to be the one which was formally most kept out of sight. The growth of the power of Athens, and the alarm which this inspired in Lacedaemon, made war inevitable.`
- Attribution line recommendation:
  `- Thucydides, The History of the Peloponnesian War`
