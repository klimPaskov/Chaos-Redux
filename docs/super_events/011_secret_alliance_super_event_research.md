## Why this fits

- `The Pact Made Public` is short, cold, and specific to the exact reveal: a hidden anti-target alignment crossing into formal public existence.
- Kant is the strongest main-quote fit because the event is not just about secrecy; it is about private commitments carrying a latent war inside them.
- `Openly arrived at.` works as bitter diplomatic irony. It points at the ritual of formal publication without turning the moment into comedy.
- The package stays usable with dynamic target localisation because the title and button avoid hardcoded country names, while the description can carry the Anti-[target] wording.

## Short description direction

State that several governments have converted private coordination into the open Anti-[target] Pact. If the reveal came through war, note that first open hostilities activated the hidden protocol and forced the signatories into declared alignment. Keep the tone formal, tense, and specific; stress the public shape of a prepared diplomatic machine rather than apocalypse language.

## Rejected title candidates

1. `Open Covenants`
   Why rejected: too directly tied to Wilson and sounds almost approving rather than tense

2. `The Hidden Protocol`
   Why rejected: points at secrecy, but not at the public diplomatic break that defines the super-event trigger

3. `The Secret Protocols`
   Why rejected: broader and heavier than needed; the event is about formal exposure and alignment, not document fetish or conspiracy pulp

## Uncertainty and caution

- Kant is the best fit I found, but the cited wording is an English translation. If the main agent wants stricter philological exactness, use the same source family consistently in localisation and docs.
- The Wilson fragment works well as button text because it is very short. A longer direct Wilson line should stay in docs or quote research, not in the button.
- The Luke candidate is legally safe, but tonally riskier unless the final image and audio also lean liturgical.

## Implementation recommendation

- Use `The Pact Made Public` as the super-event title.
- Use the Kant line as the main quote.
- Use `Openly arrived at.` as the button text.
- Keep the description explicitly about private coordination becoming the formal Anti-[target] Pact, with a war-trigger sentence only when the reveal was activated by first hostilities.
