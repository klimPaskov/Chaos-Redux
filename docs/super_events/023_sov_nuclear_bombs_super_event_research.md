## Editing and conversion record

## Why this fits the exchange

“Marche au supplice” is a structured orchestral movement rather than an ambience bed or stinger, and its recurring march gives the super-event a concrete sense of military process continuing after the first detonation.

The opening has immediate weight and a clear pulse for the first confirmed blast, while the movement's orchestral development supports crowded communications, retaliatory preparation, evacuation, and the shortening decision window.

The title's execution-march association reinforces the event's irreversible human cost without making the cue celebratory, mystical, or terminal.

The cue is severe and period-compatible in musical language without duplicating the existing catalogue's Berlioz-free composition set or any existing Event 23, Fallout, or Final Silence track.

The 115-second excerpt meets the repository's normal one-to-two-minute super-event preference and avoids a hard loop.
