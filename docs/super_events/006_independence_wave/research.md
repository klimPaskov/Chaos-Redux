## Qualification contract

`can_independence_wave_trigger_danger_milestone` is a one-shot OR across five
independent factual packages. The first true package in the following stable
priority order records the reason and actor:

1. **Radical offensive league.** The league is durable and
   radical-revisionist, has at least seven current members, at least 75 common
   cause, at least 60 shared reserve, and at least three completed revisionist
   actions. Its exact living Event 006 leader must also be a current member.
2. **High-chaos claim wave.** A successfully committed wave contains exactly
   ten Event 006 countries at Totalen Chaos or World Collapse. At least three
   distinct armed-or-radical countries must each receive one verified claim on
   a distinct adjacent state still owned by their surviving former host. The
   claims and actor witnesses are tied to that plan ID and are created only
   after the shared release plan commits.
3. **Synchronized former-host wars.** One Maximum-intensity Wars of Separation
   batch, or Maximum Universal Belligerence limited to former hosts, must
   produce at least three successful declarations by distinct active Event 006
   countries against their own surviving former hosts. Failed declarations
   create the scenario's regional-threat mission and never count.
4. **Hidden formable bloc center.** The exact living leader of a durable
   radical-revisionist league must be a current member and the committed
   carrier of a hidden-high-chaos formable. The league must also meet the
   seven-member, 75-common-cause, and 60-reserve gates. This package does not
   borrow the revisionist-action requirement from package 1.
5. **League-sponsored cascade.** At least three published DM-57 contracts must
   have been sponsored by an active Event 006 member while the league was
   formal, durable, or reformed, and then consumed by successful shared-plan
   commits. Ordinary sponsorships advance the general sponsorship history but
   not this dedicated counter.

Ordinary waves, consultative congresses, elections, isolated host wars,
ordinary formables, Maximum scenario selection by itself, and nonleague
sponsorships do not qualify.

Packages 1, 2, 3, and 5 have reachable runtime producers. Package 4's exact
predicate and publication branch are wired, but no currently promoted formable
can carry `hidden_high_chaos`: the only registered families with that
capability are FORM-42 and FORM-48, and both remain fail-closed. Package 4
therefore cannot fire until one of those families and its carrier country
package is fully implemented, audited, and readiness-promoted. No ordinary
formable or synthetic readiness flag substitutes for that missing carrier.

Revisionist pressure and completed revisionist actions are separate ledgers.
World Collapse multiplies the visible pressure gained from high-chaos focuses;
each completed high-chaos focus or accepted league operation records exactly
one action toward package 1's three-action gate.

## One-shot presentation and history

The selected actor immediately receives the durable reason record. A separate
Event 006 history row is appended with payload 6002; it is not a sixth Event 006
evolution and does not change the repeatable event's firing counter. Event Log
title and detail branches use the accepted milestone wording.

Presentation is submitted to the existing settings-aware FIFO through
`natural_disaster_emit_super_event`. Despite its historical subsystem name,
that queue stores generic slot, audio, and optional scope values. It prevents
slot 24 from replacing another visible super-event and dispatches the next
queued entry when the shared window closes. Playback is emitted once per human
country through the existing volume-mode helper.

Package 1 records
`independence_wave_radical_bloc_qualification_recorded` and the exact qualified
actor. It does not award an achievement. The accepted Radical Bloc achievement definition and trigger scaffolding are registered, but no currently admitted route completes the later containment-survival proof. The achievements tranche must consume this qualification only through the reviewed qualification, containment, and disqualification contract and must not award it early. Packages 2-5 do not set the qualification.

## Blocked league-formation package

The accepted 1949 London Brass Players recording for **The League of New
States** has no verified permission or waiver covering United States
redistribution. No substitute is authorized. Audio ID 6001 and display slot 23
therefore remain absent from runtime until the exact recording is cleared or
the user approves a newly researched replacement.
