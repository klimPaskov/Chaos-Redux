## Qualification contract

`can_independence_wave_trigger_danger_milestone` is a one-shot OR across five independent factual packages. The first true package in the following stable priority order records the reason and actor:

1. **Radical offensive league.** The league is durable and
radical-revisionist, has at least seven current members, at least 75 common cause, at least 60 shared reserve, and at least three completed revisionist actions. Its exact living Event 006 leader must also be a current member.
2. **High-chaos claim wave.** A successfully committed wave contains exactly
ten Event 006 countries at Totalen Chaos or World Collapse. At least three distinct armed-or-radical countries must each receive one verified claim on a distinct adjacent state still owned by their surviving former host. The claims and actor witnesses are tied to that plan ID and are created only after the shared release plan commits.
3. **Synchronized former-host wars.** One Maximum-intensity Wars of Separation
batch, or Maximum Universal Belligerence limited to former hosts, must produce at least three successful declarations by distinct active Event 006 countries against their own surviving former hosts. Failed declarations create the scenario's regional-threat mission and never count.
4. **Hidden formable bloc center.** The exact living leader of a durable
radical-revisionist league must be a current member and the committed carrier of a hidden-high-chaos formable. The league must also meet the seven-member, 75-common-cause, and 60-reserve gates. This package does not borrow the revisionist-action requirement from package 1.
5. **League-sponsored cascade.** At least three published DM-57 contracts must
have been sponsored by an active Event 006 member while the league was formal, durable, or reformed, and then consumed by successful shared-plan commits. Ordinary sponsorships advance the general sponsorship history but not this dedicated counter.

Ordinary waves, consultative congresses, elections, isolated host wars, ordinary formables, Maximum scenario selection by itself, and nonleague sponsorships do not qualify.

Packages 1, 2, 3, and 5 have reachable runtime producers. Revisionist pressure and completed revisionist actions are separate ledgers. World Collapse multiplies the visible pressure gained from high-chaos focuses; each completed high-chaos focus or accepted league operation records exactly one action toward package 1's three-action gate.
