# Historical Chaos Warfare Theorists and CBRN Advisors

## Overview

Selected historical scientists already represented in Chaos Redux can serve both as special-project scientists and as named national advisors or theorists.

Each person remains one static character with one identity and one accepted large scientist portrait.
The advisor role uses a separate 65x67 dossier card generated through the repository advisor workflow.

Medical countermeasure research by Florey and Fleming does not require Chaos Warfare.
Appointments tied to offensive, retaliatory, clandestine, or dual-use weaponization programs require the grand doctrine even though the character remains available as a scientist before adoption.

## Gameplay integration

The static trait modifiers are deliberately substantial: the theorists reduce land-doctrine costs by 25-30%, add 20-25% Army experience gain, and provide 25-35% special-project speed or 15% research speed where appropriate.
Political advisors provide strong research, project, production, stability, resistance, or compliance bonuses according to their role.

Operational effects are conditional on the character occupying the relevant advisor slot.
They are applied through `common/scripted_effects/cbrn_historical_advisor_effects.txt` and called only from the shared chemical exposure, Chemical Readiness, and biological lifecycle systems.

Chemical specialists increase delivered dose, deaths, contamination, and readiness efficiency.
Biological specialists increase seed potency, growth, spread, deaths, and medical saturation.
Defensive specialists reduce deaths and medical saturation or increase outbreak detection.
Offensive specialists reduce the Condemnation generated by their operations, but never reduce payload expenditure, evidence, attribution, or recorded deaths.

## Static-character migration

The twelve selected scientists were previously generated and renamed at startup.
They are now defined in `common/characters/cbrn_historical_specialists.txt` and recruited by the same startup country effects.
Their former identity flags are restored immediately after recruitment so the existing Ishii and Witaszek consumers continue to work.
Other named scientists remain on the existing generation path.

## Assets and wiring

The accepted large portraits remain under `gfx/leaders/scientists/` and remain registered by the existing scientist portrait file.
Advisor DDS files live under `gfx/interface/advisors/cbrn/` and are registered by `interface/cbrn_historical_advisors.gfx`.
Native and 4x review PNGs, exact transforms, source hashes, template hash, and runtime hashes are recorded under `docs/assets/chaos_warfare_historical_advisors/`.

## Future extensions

Country routes may add event-driven retirement, defection, arrest, or program-control changes for these characters when historically appropriate.
Those changes should manipulate the existing static character roles rather than create replacement identities.
