# Event 016 achievement and provenance system

## Overview

Event 016 defines exactly seventeen achievements in the shared Chaos Redux registry. The system rewards campaign history rather than appointment alone: post-arrival research, project breadth, public replication, deliberate foreign recruitment, safe separation, stable control, real project divisions, route succession, terminal outcomes, and a rights-respecting global-threat settlement.

Every completion trigger requires the human-controlled country and rejects the Event 016 progress-granting setup flag. Role flags and immutable receipts prevent tag switching, releasing a subject, or transferring Kruger out and back from manufacturing eligibility.

## Host-entry research baseline

`brilliant_scientist_achievement_record_host_entry` runs after the recipient's appointment or transferred portfolio is final. It records whether advanced electronics, industry, rocketry, atomic science, and medicine were already satisfied. It also starts one nonrenewable 2,920-day country flag. `Borrowed Century` therefore counts only categories completed after Kruger's first arrival in that country.

The atomic transfer path copies the fixed character and portfolio before the entry snapshot. A deliberate foreign-operation transfer then records the inherited Prototype count separately, allowing `The One Who Left` to require a genuinely new Prototype under the new host.

## Provenance investigation

The hidden `Not From Here` route is implemented as a six-decision chain under `brilliant_scientist_origin_investigation_category`:

1. Analyze exotic interface residue using Alien Arms and Materials prototypes.
2. Sequence xenobiological isotopes using Xenobiological Synthesis and biomedical or cloning work.
3. Triangulate a stellar vector using Rocketry, High Energy, and computation or electronics.
4. Authenticate a nonhuman archive using Temporal, Alien Arms, and Computation prototypes.
5. Convene an independent tribunal after three independent sources and two authenticated sources.
6. Publish all four sources after Extraterrestrial Provenance is locked.

Each source can complete once, consumes political power, civilian-factory capacity, equipment, and time, requires the primary facility, raises Exposure, and has route-sensitive AI weights. Two sources are independently authenticated; the other two are independent corroboration. Transformation flags never add evidence. Evidence and the locked conclusion transfer with the one Kruger identity and are inherited by a formed Kruger State.

## Operational military evidence

War achievements use event-driven on actions only. At a major war's opening, the system marks the enemy's then-current capital with that war's date epoch and counts live Kruger-owned divisions containing each exact Event 016 battalion. `Combined Arms, Redefined` requires three distinct families at both war opening and that enemy's capitulation, plus the current project-force logistics package. `Clever Girl` requires a Paleogenetic Shock Pack division physically present when the marked major capital changes control during the same still-active war; stale capital markers from an earlier conflict cannot qualify.

The same capitulation receipt records a post-transition major victory for a machine-majority network only after biological Kruger is no longer sole ruler. Nuclear and thermonuclear attacks against Kruger-controlled territory or any Event 016 laboratory state permanently disqualify the relevant former-host and global-defeat achievements for the country that launched them.

## Defeat and settlement evidence

Local, regional, and qualifying defeat recipients copy the defeated state's real project-family and Weaponization history before dismantlement. A violent former host qualifies only against a project-rich rebellion. The dismantlement effect separately records that the project arsenal was actually secured.

A qualifying global-threat custodian receives `No Second Sun` candidacy only after late Singularity construction and 540 days of world threat. `Ordinary People Won` candidacy additionally requires at least one surviving clone, machine, paleogenetic, or xenobiological population. Its final trigger requires the full treaty, inspections, reconstruction, countermeasure exchange, and rights-protecting outcome for every population that survived.

## Terminal evidence

The Singularity defeat evaluator records whether detonation came specifically from the capitulation failsafe before entering the shared Fallout pipeline. Laboratory World requires the normal world-end flag and the country-level verified-nonterminal Singularity state; simple absence of a detonation is insufficient.

## Required visual assets

Each of the seventeen achievement IDs needs one distinct completed 64x64 DDS, one grey DDS, and one not-eligible DDS under `gfx/achievements/`. The shared sprite registry already uses the exact filenames and `GFX_achievement_<id>` names. Editable source masters and the contact sheet live under `docs/assets/016_brilliant_scientist/` as specified by the asset manifest.

## Future plans and extension ideas

- Add an Event Details achievement ledger that explains already-earned causal receipts without exposing hidden-origin spoilers.
- Let foreign intelligence operations steal one provenance clue as a lead, but never count it as authenticated until the receiving country performs its own facility test.
- Add post-achievement narrative events for the public provenance dossier and the rights settlement without attaching additional mechanical rewards.

These are optional extensions. They must not change the fixed seventeen-achievement set or bypass the existing evidence requirements.
