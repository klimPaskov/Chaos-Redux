# Partner deportation orders

Germany's occupation administration can demand dated handovers from Hungary, Slovakia, Croatia, Bulgarian-held Macedonia and Thrace, and Vichy France.
Each government receives one report and chooses whether transports continue, slow down or stop.
The halt decision remains visible only while people are waiting, and it uses the current Black Friday price.
Stopping the trains permanently releases the waiting people at home during the next monthly pass.
Germany receives one notification for each government that halts its transports.

The route preserves living origins through measured population movements and counts each later death once under German responsibility.
The wider partner route uses the Lublin district as an aggregate reception host, with its geographic limitation recorded in the [implementation contract](../../common/scripted_effects/camp_occ_partner_effects.md).
The separate Hungarian Auschwitz route retains its own capacity, dates and support requirements.
German puppet administrations can keep either destination under German authority without becoming the recorded responsible country.

The wider partner reports reuse vanilla `GFX_report_event_generic_train`, and Hungary keeps its national report pictures.
The decisions reuse `GFX_decision_generic_protection`, and the existing Records window displays the halt history through `camp_admin_history_extended_partner_halt`.
No new icon file is required.

Future development could model separate historical receiving districts and more precise origin allocations, subject to the exact movement and single-attribution contract.
