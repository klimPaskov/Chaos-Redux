# Historical Chaos Warfare Theorists and CBRN Advisors

## Overview

Selected historical scientists already represented in Chaos Redux can serve both as special-project scientists and as named national advisors or theorists.

Each person remains one static character with one identity and one wired large scientist portrait.
The advisor role uses a separate wired 65x67 dossier card; its source and review package is unresolved.

Appointments tied to offensive, retaliatory, clandestine, or dual-use weaponization programs require the grand doctrine even though the character remains available as a scientist before adoption.

## Gameplay integration

The static trait modifiers are deliberately substantial: the theorists reduce land-doctrine costs by 25-30%, add 20-25% Army experience gain, and provide 25-35% special-project speed or 15% research speed where appropriate.
Political advisors provide strong research, project, production, stability, resistance, or compliance bonuses according to their role.

Operational effects are conditional on the character occupying the relevant advisor slot.
They are applied through `common/scripted_effects/cbrn_historical_advisor_effects.txt` and called only from the shared chemical exposure and biological lifecycle systems.

The accepted combined CBRN balance cap is evaluated by one legally appointable country, not by multiplying every advisor in the roster. `common/characters/cbrn_historical_specialists.txt` restricts Gerhard Schrader's chemical theorist appointment to GER and Grigory Mairanovsky's toxicological director appointment to SOV, so their 1.03 dose and 1.02 contamination factors cannot stack in one country. Neither specialist changes the derived readiness value. JAP's Ishii and Kitano can supply two compatible 1.02 biological potency factors after Kitano's date gate, as can the USA's Baldwin and Olson after Olson's date gate. The doctrine guide records separately legal conditional source scenarios. These are source appointment bounds, not engine or probability evidence.

Chemical specialists increase delivered dose, deaths, or contamination according to their appointment, while reducing resulting Condemnation without erasing evidence.
Biological specialists increase seed potency, growth, spread, deaths, and medical saturation.
Defensive specialists reduce deaths and medical saturation or increase outbreak detection.
Offensive specialists reduce the Condemnation generated by their operations, but never reduce payload expenditure, evidence, attribution, or recorded deaths.

## Static-character migration

The twelve selected scientists were previously generated and renamed at startup.
They are now defined in `common/characters/cbrn_historical_specialists.txt` and recruited by the same startup country effects.
Their former identity flags are restored immediately after recruitment so the existing Ishii and Witaszek consumers continue to work.
Other named scientists remain on the existing generation path.

## Assets and wiring

The large portraits remain under `gfx/leaders/scientists/` and remain registered by the existing scientist portrait file.
Advisor DDS files live under `gfx/interface/advisors/cbrn/` and are registered by `interface/cbrn_historical_advisors.gfx`.
Political-advisor cards are assigned through `portraits.civilian.small`; theorist cards are assigned through `portraits.army.small`; the full scientist portrait remains `portraits.army.large`.
The previously documented actual-workflow package at `docs/assets/chaos_warfare_historical_advisors/v2_actual_workflow/` is absent from the current workspace. All twelve card sprites and 65x67 DDS files resolve, but their source/template hashes and visual-review record are not independently available. The [2026-09-25 portrait audit](../../plans/chaos_warfare_system_plans/subagent_handoffs/2026-09-25_cbrn_scientist_portrait_provenance_audit.md) records this limit.

## Future extensions

Country routes may add event-driven retirement, defection, arrest, or program-control changes for these characters when historically appropriate.
Those changes should manipulate the existing static character roles rather than create replacement identities.
