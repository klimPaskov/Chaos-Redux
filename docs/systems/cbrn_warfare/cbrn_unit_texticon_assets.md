# CBRN Unit Texticon Asset Manifest

Status: all 23 current CBRN small texticons are produced, parent-reviewed, wired, and source-verified. The parent completed a 23-sprite crosswalk confirming 23 unique GFX sprites map to 23 distinct existing DDS files.

## Consumer and source contract

The current runtime consumers are the existing sprites in `interface/chaosx_texticons.gfx`; each uses `noOfFrames = 2` and its engine token is the sprite name without `GFX_`. Every final texture is a native-alpha `60x12` horizontal strip with two `30x12` frames.

The exact installed vanilla precedents are `interface/texticons.gfx` and `gfx/texticons/support_engineer_small.dds`, `support_field_hospital_small.dds`, `support_logistics_small.dds`, `support_unit_hq_icon_small.dds`, `unit_category_support_battalions_icon_small.dds`, `unit_category_recon_icon_small.dds`, and `unit_category_army_icon_small.dds`. Their matching CBRN texticon family references are the canonical vanilla `60x12` files under `.agents/skills/chaos-redux-event-assets/assets/gfx_references/units/land/map_counters/`; the contact sheet was reviewed before the individual infantry and artillery examples.

The native first frame follows the vanilla support style with a vivid-blue plus marker and muted field-green role art. The second frame uses a pale, dark-outlined counter tile. Unused canvas stays genuinely transparent; no background removal or component-catalog layer was used.

The full native source, prompts, processed PNG, DDS round-trip PNG, contact sheets, per-frame bounds, and detailed alpha/header audits are kept locally under the ignored staging package `docs/assets/chaos_warfare_cbrn/unit_texticon_replacements/`. Its `notes/processing_audit.json` and `notes/dds_roundtrip_audit.json` are the detailed machine-readable evidence. The runtime DDS and the source and DDS hashes below are the durable crosswalk.

## Validation boundary

All twenty-three DDS files were decoded and compared pixel-for-pixel to their processed `60x12` PNGs. Each is a valid one-level uncompressed BGRA DDS with a transparent alpha range of `0–255` and a `3,008`-byte file size. The parent passed all three contact sheets at original resolution, wired all 23 paths, and source-verified the unique sprite-to-DDS crosswalk. The user retains live-game visual validation.
