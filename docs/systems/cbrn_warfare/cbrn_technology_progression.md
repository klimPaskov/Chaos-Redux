# CBRN technology progression

The chemical and biological folders use a 50/75/100 base-day research scale: `research_cost = 0.5` for basic utility, `0.75` for ordinary equipment and service improvements, and `1` for advanced equipment and containment. The values are relative to the engine's 100-day baseline before year penalties and research modifiers. `common/technologies/chaosx_technologies.txt` owns the shared research nodes, while the separate headquarters and regimental technology files own their unit certifications.

## Research and grants

1. Basic Service Respirators lead to Improved Service Masks, Advanced Protective Clothing, and Sealed Assault Protection. The first three unlock their matching equipment models; Sealed Assault Protection is the 1942 advanced equipment research, requires Chaos Warfare doctrine, unlocks the fourth mask model, and awards the hidden overpressure capability. Improved masks award fitting, registration, and filter standardization; advanced masks award rapid filter replacement. The hidden capabilities remain as IDs because protection effects and compatibility consumers read them.
2. Field Detection and Weather Sets lead to Mobile Sampling Laboratories and Theater Detection and Forecasting Sets. Each visible research unlocks the next instrument model and awards the matching hidden weather capability, so reconnaissance and forecast improvements require one research step per equipment tier.
3. Field Decontamination Kits lead to Mobile Wash Columns and Rapid Decontamination Plants. Each visible research unlocks the matching decontamination equipment tier; the field kits also unlock the Gas Mask and Decontamination Detachment. The doctrine's Hazard Pioneer Formation certification upgrades that detachment directly at its mastery milestone. Its unit technology is owned by `common/technologies/cbrn_regimental_support_technologies.txt` and has no visible research node.
4. Pathogen Handling Protocols lead to Sealed Containment Laboratories and Fail-Safe Containment Facilities. Biological Surveillance Networks lead to Rapid Outbreak Response and Integrated Epidemic Control. These are ordinary biological defensive research, while the bomb rewards below are granted by projects.

The following special projects grant their matching equipment technology on completion. The technology definitions retain equipment unlocks and completion effects but have `allow = { always = no }` and no folder placement, so they cannot require a second research order. Existing event, focus, and debug grants may still award these same IDs.

They have no visible research branch. Sealed Assault Protection remains visible equipment research after Advanced Protective Clothing; possession of doctrine alone does not grant its equipment.

## Direct unit-stat balance

The two Livens Projector researches each give the Chemical Projector Battery +2.5% soft attack, +13% hard attack, +5% defense, and +7.5% breakthrough. These are fractional subunit bonuses, not flat stat points. With both researches, the legally cumulative CBRN doctrine categories give four separate +5% soft-attack rewards, four +5% defense rewards, and three +5% breakthrough rewards; the largest compatible CBRN-wide army attack sources add another +1.5% and +2.5% to soft and hard attack. The raw source sums are +29% soft attack, +30% hard attack, +30% defense, and +30% breakthrough. If every source applied as an independent factor, the corresponding upper products would be +32.860%, +32.845%, +34.010%, and +33.778%; the exact engine combination across technology, doctrine category, and country modifier layers has not been established. The hard-attack fraction is larger because the battery's native hard-attack base is 4, compared with 12 soft attack; one technology gives 0.30 native soft-attack points and 0.52 hard-attack points before equipment and other modifiers. The 1942 Chaos Assault Battalion grant gives +15% breakthrough, yielding a +30% raw sum alongside its +10% category and +5% direct doctrine rewards; even an all-independent product of those sources is +33.228%.

`max_organisation = 11` on the 1942 Battalion technology is a flat organization-point bonus, following the installed vanilla infantry-technology pattern. On the Battalion's base 40, this is a +27.5% relative gain. The compatible CBRN organization factors are +1% each from Controlled Retaliation, Mask Discipline, Hazard Assault M1, and Integrated Command M5, plus +0.5% from the Biological Security Director high-command trait. An all-independent upper product gives `(1 + 11/40) × 1.01^4 × 1.005 − 1 = 33.340%`; the previous flat 12 with five +1% factors would have reached 36.631%. The hidden weather technologies grant flat reconnaissance points of 0.2, 0.4, and 0.6, awarded cumulatively by the three visible detection tiers. The CBRN Recon Detachment starts at 4, so the full +1.2 points is a +30% relative gain; the earlier 2/3/5 grant would have been +250%. Installed vanilla `infantry.txt` uses fractional `recon = 0.10` in a hidden technology, confirming this syntax. These sums describe source values under a legal CBRN stack, not an observed engine combat result.

## Folder layout and presentation

The technology origins in `interface/countrytechtreeview.gui` use `position = { x = 110 y = 160 }` and `slotsize = { width = 90 height = 50 }`. Chemical rows are agents 0–4, projectors 6, masks 8, detection 10, and decontamination 12. The visible mask, instrument, and decontamination branches each have a clear left-to-right path to the next equipment tier. The chemical folder's 1050×900 size filler encloses its lower equipment row and preserves native scrolling at smaller viewports. At 1920×1080 and 2560×1440, both at UI scale 1, all visible source coordinates remain inside the folder canvas. This is a source-layout calculation; MCP technology render and the user's live-game check remain the visual evidence boundaries.

Player-facing names, descriptions, and the six `CBRN_TECH_YEAR_*` folder-year labels live in `localisation/english/cbrn_technology_research_l_english.yml`. They describe the combined equipment and service capability rather than displaying tuning constants. The folder and technology icon sprites are registered in `interface/chaosx_techtree.gfx` and `interface/cbrn_protection.gfx`; no new artwork is needed for this progression. The visible protection icons are `GFX_basic_gas_masks_medium`, `GFX_improved_gas_masks_medium`, `GFX_advanced_gas_masks_medium`, and `GFX_sealed_assault_protection_medium`. The visible detection icons are `GFX_chemical_detection_paper_medium`, `GFX_mobile_sampling_laboratories_medium`, and `GFX_theater_cbrn_command_set_medium`. The visible decontamination icons are `GFX_field_decontamination_kits_medium`, `GFX_mobile_wash_columns_medium`, and `GFX_rapid_decontamination_plants_medium`. The biological containment and surveillance icons are `GFX_pathogen_handling_protocols_medium`, `GFX_sealed_containment_laboratories_medium`, `GFX_fail_safe_containment_facilities_medium`, `GFX_bio_surveillance_networks_medium`, `GFX_rapid_outbreak_response_medium`, and `GFX_integrated_epidemic_control_medium`. Their DDS files live under `gfx/interface/technologies/` or `gfx/interface/technologies/stage_2_protective_equipment/`, matching the sprite `texturefile` definitions. The technology folder backgrounds are `GFX_chemical_warfare_techtree_bg` and `GFX_biowarfare_techtree_bg` in `interface/chaosx_techtree.gfx`.

## Interactions and future plans

Protection issue and maintenance consume actual mask stocks through `common/scripted_effects/cbrn_protection_effects.txt`. Doctrine grants are resolved in `common/scripted_effects/cbrn_doctrine_effects.txt`. Project completion outputs are in `common/special_projects/projects/biowarfare_main_projects.txt` and `common/special_projects/projects/chemical_warfare_nerve_projects.txt`. Research AI weights are in `common/ai_strategy/chemical_warfare_research.txt`; retired capability IDs must not receive direct research weight because they are awarded automatically.

Future work could add an in-folder project milestone indicator that shows completed bomb and nerve-agent projects without making their output technologies researchable. Any such presentation should use the installed technology GUI and preserve the one-project, one-equipment-unlock path.
