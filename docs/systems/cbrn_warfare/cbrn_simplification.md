# CBRN command and protection

The approved CBRN command model has three deliberate policy choices: Retaliation Only, Battlefield Authorization, and Unrestricted Authorization. Each direct change costs five Command Power and starts a 90-day reassessment period. Retaliation requires a live exact-target right; Battlefield Authorization supports basic battlefield chemical and biological operations even under a vanilla doctrine; Unrestricted Authorization requires terminal Chaos Warfare command and permits extreme and strategic release subject to each route's real target and equipment gates. Doctrine mastery grants its capability and dependent technologies when earned, without a claim, training-purchase, establishment, or commission card. Policy is never raised by mastery.

`cbrn_initialize_country_data` normalizes retired policy values 1 and 4 to 2 and 5 and synchronizes retained hidden technology aliases when a successor was granted by history. `cbrn_set_use_policy` accepts only 2, 3, or 5. The policy decisions use `cbrn_can_pay_policy_change` for both their cost row and availability, and `cbrn_change_chaos_warfare_use_policy` debits five Command Power only when the setter accepts the choice. `cbrn_reconcile_doctrine_capabilities` checks milestones in order and is called by `cbrn_grant_available_doctrine_technologies` from doctrine mastery and relevant technology-research callbacks. The nerve occupation authorization writer calls the same grant effect after setting its flag, so its hidden upgrade has no removed commission-card dependency. Retired policy history flags remain for compatibility; the numeric policy is the current source of truth.

`cbrn_refresh_country_mask_snapshot` derives military coverage, real shortage, and Chemical Readiness from issued masks, filter condition, field decontamination equipment, basic/improved training, and Chaos specialization. The field cleanup requirement is one kit per four thousand deployed troops as a gameplay calibration, rounded upward to a whole kit; stockpiled and army-issued kits count, while Field Decontamination Kits research is needed to use them. `cbrn_refresh_country_readiness_status` owns the derived readiness value. Protection actions refresh the snapshot after real stock or issue changes; they do not grant or spend readiness directly. The Program Management category displays a plain-language operational status, military mask and cleanup-kit coverage, issued or available equipment, shortages, and training. Its status first identifies an absent field force, missing training, equipment shortfall, or worn filters, then reports established field protection when those conditions clear. The Civil Defence category displays civilian issued crates, threatened-state cleanup-kit need, and the shortage after military priority. The readiness number remains internal to raid eligibility and hazard calculations. Real stock and state ledgers still govern protection and distribution.

The same stock-backed field cleanup coverage supplies the military decontamination layer in chemical exposure resolution. Without Field Decontamination Kits research or usable kits, that layer is zero; producing and fielding kits raises it automatically through the bounded protection snapshot. Civilians use a separate share of the same real equipment. Kits already deployed with divisions reduce the army's remaining requirement; stockpiled kits fill that gap first. The rest form one country pool spread in proportion to population-scaled kit needs across controlled states with a verified chemical alert, real contamination, or civilian masks already issued against an enemy chemical capability. The last case supports protection against a first strike in states that have prepared, without counting clean unprepared states. Civilian demand is one kit per 100,000 people, rounded upward per state as a gameplay calibration. Institutional decontamination capacity multiplies the equipped fraction, so doctrine mastery alone cannot supply civilian cleanup. The pool is derived without taking stock out of the warehouse. The existing country maintenance event, exact alert handling, control transfer, and each exposure resolution refresh this allocation; no worldwide pulse is added.

The decontamination layer can reduce contamination by at most 50% at complete coverage; partial coverage scales the reduction linearly. The other casualty and disruption protection layers retain their separate agent-specific tables.

The shared `cbrn_mask_scale` constants require ten crates per thousand deployed soldiers and one crate per thousand civilians, so the respirator archetype tooltip describes one crate as coverage for about one hundred fully fitted soldiers or one thousand civilians. Buildable models enter the ordinary military-factory production queue. Civilian conversion, reconditioning, and imports add to the same real stockpile used by automatic issue and filter service.

`cbrn_start_protection_maintenance_job` starts one country-owned `cbrn_protection.1` event chain when masks or field decontamination technology or protective equipment exists. `cbrn_run_protection_maintenance_cycle` refreshes stock and the civilian cleanup pool, services military filters and issues military masks toward uncovered need before civilian filter service, then distributes civilian masks when enemy chemical capability, chemical use, a state alert, contamination, or a qualifying historical issue exists. The Civil Defence category appears for enemy chemical capability, chemical use, a state alert, contamination, an active biological outbreak, or a continuing civilian distribution program. It remains visible under those conditions even when no chemical-response decision is available. Every twelfth 30-day cycle applies existing storage and issued-mask loss functions. The event reschedules itself only while the country retains a mask program, Field Decontamination Kits knowledge, or protective stock. This is a bounded per-country job, with no global daily, weekly, or monthly scan. Existing mask history, state transfer, and protection resolver remain in use.

The three protection procurement cards and Export Protective Equipment use one custom cost row for Political Power, equipment, and civilian-factory commitment. Their inclusive affordability predicates feed both availability and the row, and their begin effects debit the displayed Political Power once. The AI's Political Power reserve hint matches that price. Hover text explains the factory's duration rather than repeating payment amounts. Export's fixed respirator shipment uses the registered `GFX_gas_mask_equipment_1_medium` texticon and leaves real stock at completion, old models first; its larger exportable-reserve gate remains separate from the amount sent.

Occupied Mask Program and Protective Aid quote the current protection gap from state population, issued-mask models, fitting, filter condition, and local delivery conditions. Their three-icon cost rows show Political Power, the exact crates reserved from the five real mask models when selected, and civilian factories assigned for the timer. A national occupied program rounds each state's need before summing and caps its reservation at available stock; foreign aid does the same for its selected state. Respirator crates are one substitutable equipment payment shown as one cost type; the five model receipts preserve the exact real stock variants and return each unused variant to its supplier. Each state stores its original donor and five model receipts. A valid completion distributes only from those receipts and returns unused models; cancellation or control transfer refunds before clearing the state target. The in-flight validity checks do not require free stock or uncommitted factories. A donor-scoped active-project count keeps concurrent foreign-aid timers visible after their stock is reserved. `interface/cbrn_protection.gfx` owns the `GFX_gas_mask_equipment_1_medium` cost texticon and `GFX_decision_cbrn_supply_occupied_population`; `interface/cbrn_occupation.gfx` owns `GFX_decision_cbrn_deploy_protective_aid`. This transaction uses the existing state-control callback and no worldwide periodic iteration.

Emergency Masks uses the same live state-gap calculator with its own 35% target, improvised wastage, reduced fitting, and degraded filter inputs. Its two-icon cost row shows ten Political Power and the rounded crate requirement capped to the five real national mask models. The shared country affordability predicate feeds both availability and the cost row, including an exact ten-point balance. The selected state remains visible when stock is exhausted so the blocked row can explain the shortage. Selection rechecks the predicate, debits real crates through the ordinary emergency distributor, and spends Political Power once only after a positive crate delivery. A failed delivery does not use the state's one-time surge or apply congestion. `GetCbrnEmergencyMaskDistributionQuote` owns the dynamic crate value; `interface/cbrn_protection.gfx` already registers its matching cost texticon.

The policy, theater cleanup, international-response, hospital, and shelter custom-cost cards omit a native `cost` field. Their custom-cost triggers and selection effects remain responsible for payments. International-response cards rely on their target and availability gates without an empty `always = yes` visibility condition.

At both historical bookmarks, the common profile retains country knowledge and program identity but grants none of the archival fixed mask stock. It has no generic civilian distribution path; Britain's separately sourced 1939 issue owns the sole dated starting civilian transaction. The force-scaled profile uses deployed army manpower at the selected bookmark: 75% active military issue for established majors, 100% for Italy, 50% for Japan, and 25% for the remaining profile countries, with a separate 25% replacement reserve as real basic-mask equipment. `chaosx_apply_historical_eng_1936_force_scaled_profile` owns Britain's military transaction; `chaosx_apply_historical_1936_force_scaled_profile` reconciles the other profile countries. Their legacy `1936` identifiers apply to both bookmarks. No non-British country starts with civilian mask distribution without country-specific evidence. The [historical civilian-start correction](../../specs/chaos_warfare_system_specs/corrections/2026-09-26_historical_civilian_start_cleanup.md) records the removed fallback and the German 1939 source limit.

At the 1939 British bookmark, `cbrn_apply_historical_uk_1939_civilian_issue` reconciles the reported 40 million civilian respirators as a 40,000-crate game target after military starting issue. It counts any civilian masks already issued, uses only warehouse stock above the military replacement reserve, adds the remaining historical logistical shortfall as real basic masks, and distributes through the ordinary stock-debit state helper. It restores the military reserve floor if disruption makes a state's actual distribution cost exceed the estimate. Its one-time receipt prevents a repeat grant. The target is limited by controlled core population and real distribution feasibility; the resulting issued and reserve ledgers, rather than the historical flag alone, determine protection.

The same dated British startup branch adds one basic-respirator production line requesting one military factory at full starting efficiency. The line uses the already unlocked `gas_mask_equipment_1` model and competes with other military production; it does not add starting stock. The factory count is a bounded gameplay calibration of the documented prewar supply programme, not a historical plant count. Its [source and calibration note](../../specs/chaos_warfare_system_specs/corrections/2026-09-25_britain_1939_respirator_production.md) distinguishes the production line from the one-time civilian issue and records the engine validation limits.

`cbrn_policy_allows_extreme_use` is the stable unrestricted policy gate used by special projects. The native chemical land raid adapter is documented in `common/scripted_effects/cbrn_chemical_land_raid_effects.md`. HQ survivor capability triggers map retired weather, decontamination, and biological section identifiers to Operations and Intelligence, Protection and Decontamination, and Medical and Biosecurity while external consumers migrate. In `cbrn_hq.2`, each successful paid upkeep branch calls `cbrn_commander_record_completed_hq_operation` after the tick reaches zero; the start helper clears its idempotence flag for the next valid operation. Failed upkeep, preparation, and scheduled cleanup do not award service.

The tunable policy values, five-Command-Power price, readiness weights, 30-day job interval, twelve-cycle loss interval, and hazard doctrine multipliers live in shared `common/script_constants/cbrn_system_constants.txt` and `cbrn_doctrine_constants.txt`. Doctrine balance targets are coordinated across doctrine source, traits, MIOs, advisors, and chemical route constants so a complete legal stack stays within the accepted 50% hazard-specific benefit ceiling.

The changes reuse the existing `GFX_decision_category_cbrn_chemical_operations`, `GFX_decision_category_cbrn_program_management`, `GFX_decision_category_cbrn_civil_defence`, and three existing policy-card sprites. No new icon, DDS, or `.gfx` registration is needed for these shared helpers. A future extension could expose state-by-state civilian coverage in a dedicated status surface, but that is not required for the present category descriptions.
