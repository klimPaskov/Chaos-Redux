# CBRN command and protection

The approved CBRN command model has three deliberate policy choices: Retaliation Only, Battlefield Authorization, and Unrestricted Authorization. Each direct change costs five Command Power and starts a 90-day reassessment period. Retaliation requires a live exact-target right; Battlefield Authorization supports basic battlefield chemical operations even under a vanilla doctrine; Unrestricted Authorization requires terminal Chaos Warfare command and permits extreme and strategic release subject to each route's real target and equipment gates. Doctrine mastery grants its capability and dependent technologies when earned, without a claim, training-purchase, establishment, or commission card. Policy is never raised by mastery.

`cbrn_initialize_country_data` normalizes retired policy values 1 and 4 to 2 and 5 and synchronizes retained hidden technology aliases when a successor was granted by history. `cbrn_set_use_policy` accepts only 2, 3, or 5. The policy decisions use `cbrn_can_pay_policy_change` for both their cost row and availability, and `cbrn_change_chaos_warfare_use_policy` debits five Command Power only when the setter accepts the choice. `cbrn_reconcile_doctrine_capabilities` checks milestones in order and is called by `cbrn_grant_available_doctrine_technologies` from doctrine mastery and relevant technology-research callbacks. The nerve occupation authorization writer calls the same grant effect after setting its flag, so its hidden upgrade has no removed commission-card dependency. Retired policy history flags remain for compatibility; the numeric policy is the current source of truth.

`cbrn_refresh_country_mask_snapshot` derives military coverage, real shortage, and Chemical Readiness from issued masks, filter condition, decontamination capacity, basic/improved training, and Chaos specialization. `cbrn_refresh_country_readiness_status` owns the derived readiness value. Legacy readiness grant calls refresh the snapshot rather than minting a resource. Category descriptions display policy, issued military crates, requirement, shortage, coverage, training, civilian issued crates, and readiness. Real stock and state ledgers still govern protection and distribution.

`cbrn_start_protection_maintenance_job` starts one country-owned `cbrn_protection.1` event chain when basic masks or protective equipment exists. `cbrn_run_protection_maintenance_cycle` refreshes stock, services military and civilian filters from real mask equipment, issues military masks toward uncovered need, and distributes civilian masks when enemy chemical capability, chemical use, a state alert, contamination, or a qualifying historical issue exists. The Civil Defence category itself appears only for enemy chemical capability, chemical use, a state alert, contamination, or an active biological outbreak. Every twelfth 30-day cycle applies existing storage and issued-mask loss functions. The event reschedules itself only while the country retains a program or issued/reserved masks. This is a bounded per-country job, with no global daily, weekly, or monthly scan. Existing mask history, state transfer, and protection resolver remain in use.

At the 1936 British bookmark, the common profile grants no fixed civilian or warehouse masks. `chaosx_apply_historical_eng_1936_force_scaled_profile` uses deployed army manpower to supply 75% active military issue and a separate 25% replacement reserve as real basic-mask equipment, taking any prior warehouse stock into account. The 1939 bookmark uses its dated stock profile and never applies the 1936 correction.

At the 1939 British bookmark, `cbrn_apply_historical_uk_1939_civilian_issue` reconciles the reported 40 million civilian respirators as a 40,000-crate game target after military starting issue. It subtracts civilian crates already issued by the common profile, uses remaining national warehouse stock first, adds only the historical logistical shortfall as real basic masks, and distributes through the ordinary stock-debit state helper. Its one-time receipt prevents a repeat grant. The target is limited by controlled core population and real distribution feasibility; the resulting issued and reserve ledgers, rather than the historical flag alone, determine protection.

`cbrn_policy_allows_extreme_use` is the stable unrestricted policy gate used by special projects. The native chemical land raid adapter is documented in `common/scripted_effects/cbrn_chemical_land_raid_effects.md`. HQ survivor capability triggers map the retired weather, decontamination, and biological section names to Operations Section, Protective Logistics Section, and Medical Countermeasure Directorate while external consumers migrate. In `cbrn_hq.2`, each successful paid upkeep branch calls `cbrn_commander_record_completed_hq_operation` after the tick reaches zero; the start helper clears its idempotence flag for the next valid operation. Failed upkeep, preparation, and scheduled cleanup do not award service.

The tunable policy values, five-Command-Power price, readiness weights, 30-day job interval, twelve-cycle loss interval, and hazard doctrine multipliers live in shared `common/script_constants/cbrn_system_constants.txt` and `cbrn_doctrine_constants.txt`. Doctrine balance targets are coordinated across doctrine source, traits, MIOs, advisors, and chemical route constants so a complete legal stack stays within the accepted 50% hazard-specific benefit ceiling.

The changes reuse the existing `GFX_decision_category_cbrn_chemical_operations`, `GFX_decision_category_cbrn_program_management`, `GFX_decision_category_cbrn_civil_defence`, and three existing policy-card sprites. No new icon, DDS, or `.gfx` registration is needed for these shared helpers. A future extension could expose state-by-state civilian coverage in a dedicated status surface, but that is not required for the present category descriptions.

The shared localisation owner should remove obsolete name, description, requirement, effect, cost, and mission text keys for these retired decision IDs after all consumers are reconciled: `cbrn_convene_institutional_review`, `cbrn_chaos_warfare_establishment_mission`, `cbrn_complete_delayed_establishment`, `cbrn_begin_hazard_assault_training`, `cbrn_hazard_assault_training_mission`, `cbrn_claim_protective_foundation`, `cbrn_claim_delivery_integration`, `cbrn_claim_theater_exploitation`, `cbrn_claim_terminal_command`, `cbrn_set_defensive_preparation_policy`, `cbrn_set_retaliation_authority_policy`, `cbrn_set_limited_battlefield_policy`, `cbrn_set_strategic_release_policy`, `cbrn_set_unrestricted_policy`, `cbrn_commission_sealed_tank_crews`, `cbrn_commission_persistent_shell_filling`, `cbrn_commission_nerve_suppression`, `cbrn_commission_biological_security_assault`, `cbrn_establish_national_respirator_reserve`, `cbrn_register_and_fit_population`, `cbrn_issue_masks_to_field_army`, `cbrn_replace_military_mask_filters`, `cbrn_priority_state_mask_issue`, `cbrn_full_state_mask_distribution`, and `cbrn_replace_state_mask_filters`.
