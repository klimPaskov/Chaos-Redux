# Event20 generated visual prompts

All three assets were generated with the built-in official ImageGen workflow. The report source was generated as a period documentary photograph and passed through the repository report-card processor. The two super-event sources were generated as unique alternate-history scenes and resized to the native 457x328 super-event canvas.

## report_event_020_black_plague_unbound

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: Hearts of Iron IV report-event image source artwork, later processed into a 210x176 tilted sepia documentary card. Primary request: a fictional alternate-history World War II-era documentary photograph showing the Black Plague unbound in a bombed European rail district, with a swarm of dark rats pouring through a cracked quarantine doorway, two period nurses behind a canvas barrier, broken rail lines, abandoned 1930s freight cars, sandbags, and a dim winter sky. The plague should feel terrifying through scale, smoke, silence, and contamination, not gore. Style: authentic black-and-white wartime press photograph, realistic grain and imperfect exposure, no modern cinematic treatment, no color. Constraints: 1936-1945 technology, clothing, architecture, and vehicles; no gore, modern props, logos, watermark, UI, or readable text; avoid modern streets, modern hazmat suits, modern cars, sci-fi machinery, fantasy armor, neon, meme styling, cartoon rats, or maps as the main subject.

## super_event_085_rat_king_coronation

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Hearts of Iron IV super-event image, final canvas 457x328. Primary request: a colossal black rat sovereign with an ornate scavenged metal crown seated on a throne built from broken artillery and railway iron inside a bomb-damaged 1940s cathedral converted into a plague court, with a living ring of smaller rats below. Style: detailed alternate-history illustration with restrained HOI4 painted-documentary finish, believable anatomy, tactile surfaces, dramatic but grounded lighting. Palette: charcoal black, ash gray, tarnished brass, iron red, restrained crimson. Constraints: period architecture and industrial material, no gore, modern objects, readable text, logos, watermark, UI, cartoon or cute mascot styling, neon, sci-fi machinery, or title card.

## super_event_086_rat_king_takeover

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Hearts of Iron IV terminal super-event image, final canvas 457x328. Primary request: the terminal Rat King takeover of a fictional alternate-history World War II world, with an enormous crowned rat king standing on a collapsed 1940s parliament dais while a dark tide of rats floods shattered streets, ruined radio towers, factory smokestacks, rail bridges, abandoned period tanks, and barricades disappear beneath plague fog. The rat king raises one claw toward the horizon to command a final occupation. This is a world-ending takeover, not a coronation ceremony. Style: detailed painted-documentary realism with grounded wartime materials. Palette: ash gray, soot black, tarnished brass, dead ochre, muted rust red, sickly green haze. Constraints: 1936-1945 vehicles, architecture, and industrial details only; no gore, modern objects, readable text, logos, watermark, UI, cathedral interior, throne room, cute or cartoon rats, neon, sci-fi machinery, modern military gear, clean modern skyline, abstract map, or title card.
